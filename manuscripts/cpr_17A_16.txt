[TRANSLATION]
1     … by Adelph[ius] …
2     having read … the things in accordance …
3     to the … remembering the [care] of this, that is to say
4     who it might be that gathered in the [crop] or let his cattle loose upon the fodder
5     belonging to himself.
6     In the consulship of our masters
7     Licinius Augustus for the 6th time and Licinius
8     the most illustrious Caesar for the 2nd time,
9     Pharmouthi the third.
10    … [whose mother is] … [and] …
11    of Horos, wh[ose mother is] … and Horos son of Phibis, whose mother is
12    Teuoris, and Anynis son of Betos, whose mother is Thaesis,
13    the four field-guards of the boundaries of the village of Tertembythis. To you who inquire from the petition of Adelphius
14    son of Adelphius, gymnasiarch and councillor of Hermou-
15    polis, who it m[ight b]e that made the cutting, that is, the consuming,
16    of arakos-fodder over thirteen
17    arouras, necessarily we make manifest to your equity,
18    declaring that Anysion son of Theophanes, a former exactor, sent
19    his herd of cattle and that those cattle themselves made the consumption of the thirteen arouras of fodder
20    and that nothing has been falsely stated. Farewell.
21    In the aforesaid consulship, Pharmouthi the tenth. Copy also of the subscription. We the aforesaid have submitted (it). Aurelius Didymus, I wrote
22    on their behalf, they not knowing letters.
23    [From the field-gua]rds of Tertembythis.
