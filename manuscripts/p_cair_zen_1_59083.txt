[TRANSLATION]
1     Peisikles to Zenon greetings. I have written down [for you of the things purchased] through Philokrates
2     fish the prices: mackerel 5 and mullets … [drachmas] …, [bream] 1, capers 1, cuttle-fish 4, drachmas 2 obol,
3     boiled [fish] 2 obols 1 half-obol, oysters, clams smooth and rough …, fresh seasonings 1 half-obol, mulberry 1 half-obol,
4     sumac 1 half-obol, cheese quarter, salt quarter, they total drachmas 6 [obol 1 half-obol].
5     Farewell.
6     Peisikles concerning fish the purchased
7     through Philokrates drachmas 6 obol 1 ½. Year 29,
8     Daisios 21, in Arsinoe.
9     To Zenon.
