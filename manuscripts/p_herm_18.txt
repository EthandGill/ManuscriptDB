[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of those who will be consuls for the third time,] on the 8th day before the Ides of December, Choiak 9.
2     [When] Firmus [had come forward] and presented Patrikios, Klematios the orator said: To Firmus who has
3     come forward Patrikios is a house-slave; bringing him forward, he asks that he be questioned about his status.
4     The [record]-keepers [said] to Patrikios: Are you a slave or free? He answered: A slave.
5     The record-keepers said to him: Whose? He answered: Firmus's.
6     The record-keepers said to him: From whom did he acquire you? He answered: From Reskoupos.
7     The record-keepers said to him: From whom? He answered: From Neikostratos.
8     The record-keepers said to him: Is your mother a slave? He answered: Yes.
9     The record-keepers said to him: What is she called? He answered: Hesychis.
10    The record-keepers said to him: Do you have brothers? He answered: Yes, one, Eutychios.
11    The record-keepers said to him: Is he too a slave? He answered: Yes. Klematios the orator said: Since
12    the lawful purchase of the slave has been made plainly evident, order that the archives register it accordingly.
13    Elpidios, presiding record-keeper, and Anatolios, of the record-keepers, deputizing for the absence of … the presiding record-keeper, said: At the risk of the one who has brought him forward, without prejudice to the truth, the archives shall register it in accordance with the deeds of purchase, the purchaser providing for his own security.
