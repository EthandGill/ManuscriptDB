[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord landlord, Papnouthis,
2     greeting.
3     As you ordered me to go to Berky in order that
4     I might receive their grain, but having found their grain
5     to be of barley, I was not able to take it in until
6     I receive from you a ruling about this. As for the names
7     which the secretary of the city gave us,
8     they were found to pertain to Psinour and Praous.
9     Psinour did not wish to make the collection with us,
10    the names pertaining to Praous, saying that
11    "I have no business with it," nor did Praous come to collect from them with us, saying
12    that "I have no leisure." And concerning their measure
13    malam…eis, as they themselves suggest, that it is equal
14    to the public kankellos-measure. But know that their praepositus … coming with soldiers … and collects from them
15    as he wishes. For the rest, write to me whether you want me to take in the grain at the measure. Some of them
16    say that "you must measure it," but if we find
17    them to be unreasonable, we too take
18    soldiers and beat them with cudgels. If indeed you wish,
19    as also the other praepositus takes in, we too will take in. Write back to me quickly as you wish.
20    Make my master the landlord Eulogios
21    write to Psinour many threats. If indeed you are able to come here, there will be a matter to be set right. I pray for your good health, my lord landlord.
22    The assistant of Apollonios made a beginning … of the measure as Eulogios the praepositus of our … division measured it, and if you wish me
23    to take it in at the same measure so that I may deliver it to the assistant of Apollonios as he orders,
24    write back to me; for thus also the assistant of Apollonios agreed with him and gave
25    him a written undertaking on these terms. The secretary of the village of Berky distributed
26    to me the names as those paying before.
27    Deliver to my lord landlord.
