[TRANSLATION]
1     To [A]nnius Antoninus the most excellent, administering the high-priesthood,
2     from Aurelius Onnophris son of Onnophris son of Teos, his mother Sintheus, from Oxyrhynchus city, a fire-bearer and pastophoros of Athena
3     Thoeris greatest goddess, of the sanctuary which is in the same city. According to [the th]ings ordered by you, … I declare to the official sent, Aurelius Zenon, that I have been made a priest in the aforesaid sanctuary and have been circumcised by permission
4     of the high-priesthood, in accordance with the memorandum drawn up, of which I have appended the copy below, so that nothing
5     escape your attention. Year 2 of the Emperor
6     Caesar Gaius Vibius Trebonianus Gallus and Gaius
7     Vibius Afinius Gallus Veldumianus Volusianus
8     Pious Fortunate Augusti, Mesore. And it is (as follows):
9     [Year] 5, Hathyr 5. When Aurelius Onnophris son of Teos, fire-bearer
10    [and pas]tophoros, presented his son Aurelius Onno[phris] and asked that he be circumcised, having submitted
11    [als]o the letter written about him by Aurelius
12    [Harpo]kration strategos of the Oxyrhynchite, Sabinianus
13    [ord]ered it together with the appended (documents) to be read [out. When it had been re]ad, Sabinianus, ordering the boy
14    … , inquired of the hierogrammateis whether
15    [any of the] forbidden things or any other mark he has on his bo[dy]. When they said (he was) clean and unmarked, Sabei[nianu]s, succeeding to the high-priesthood, having subscri[bed] to the [le]tter, ordered him to be circumcised.
16    [I, Aurelius Onno]phris son of Onnophris, have submitted (this).
