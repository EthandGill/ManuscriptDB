[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King P]tolemaeus, gr[eetings]. … [of those] from the city of Crocodil[es]. I am being wronged by Hermogenes
2     [of those from the] same city … so that he might [me]asure out on my behalf to the roy[al]
3     … of wheat 20 artabae … but now he has not [measured] the 20 [artabae] of wheat
4     … … me … he has not [measured] the grain on my behalf …
5     … I beseech you therefore, O [King], if it seems good to you, to command
6     [To Diophanês the general] … by law, [if it should appear] that the things [in the petition are] true, to compel him
7     … [when this is] done, [through you], O [King], the savior common to all,
8     … may [you] prosper.
9     [Sent with] Nikon.
10    [Year 4, Daisios 27], Athyr 29.
