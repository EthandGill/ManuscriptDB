[TRANSLATION]
1   Kametis son of Petearpres, (grand)son of Amphiomis, paid, for
2   poll-tax of the South-and-West (quarter), year 2, 8 dr. Year 2 of Vespasian
3   the lord, Mecheir 26. Likewise Phamenoth 30,
4   8 dr.; likewise Pachon 10, 4 dr.; likewise Mesore 6,
5   for the baths, 4 dr.; likewise (in) year 3, Phaophi 4, for dike-tax
6   of year 2, 3 dr. 1½ ob., which are 2 dr. 5½ ob.
