[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelia Ptolema through me
2     of the man John gymnasiarch
3     to Aurelius Isidorus greetings.
4     I received from you the tax
5     for which you farm my plots near the village Karanida of wheat
6     artabas four and a half,
7     total artabas 4½. Aurelius John
8     wrote all.
9     year 15 and 3 Athur 21.
