[TRANSLATION]
1     From Ischyrion.
2     I wondered how even until today you have not gone up having settled the account. You having promised
3     for within three days
4     to do this you did not do.
5     Now putting everything aside
6     go up
7     so that also the matters sought concerning you you might settle. I pray that you fare well.
8     To Heroneinous, superintendent of Theadelpheia
9     year 1, Thoth 29.
