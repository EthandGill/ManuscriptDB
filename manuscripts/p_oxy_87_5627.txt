[TRANSLATION]
1     … thorn-bushes sold from Leonidas' estate
2     … to the carpenters from Heracles and to the sailors from Nike
3     … in the month of Pachon 14, of the 10th indiction, thus:
4     From the northern thorn-bushes facing east: 4 thorn-bushes for 6 nomismata
5     From the middle thorn-bushes: 6 thorn-bushes for 8 nomismata
6     From the [northern] part of the Limeniados church: 2 thorn-bushes for 2 nomismata
7     Total: 12 thorn-bushes for 16 nomismata only
8     … [thorn]-bushes sold in the [estate] …
