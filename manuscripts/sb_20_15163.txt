[TRANSLATION]
1     Agreement: Dios son of Heracles also called Pnepheres, aged
2     45, without distinguishing marks, [son] of Dios from Kerkesis, aged 31,
3     mark: small finger of the left [hand];
4     Patron, younger; Kanios [son] of [the] elder, from [the...]
5     [who has served as] cosmetes, aged 28, without distinguishing marks, [he agrees] to owe drachmas 14,
6     of which the repayment he will make with vegetable seed at one artaba, by oil-production measure
7     of Teptyneis. If moreover he does not repay,
8     [he will have] to repay drachmas 20.
