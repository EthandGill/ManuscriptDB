[TRANSLATION]
1     I will go up to you. I received from Sa[t]r[ius] 24 drachmas. Write to me
2     about rush-baskets and linen and lentils; these I have given out and not yet completed; if it is completed sooner, if I find the trustworthy [person], I will send
3     with the Julians. And two artabas of lentils I bought and
4     Satris was not willing to take them. The rush-basket five by three [at] 36 drachmas, the
5     linen [at] 10 drachmas, the lentils [at] 6 drachmas. I write this to you not so that our relatives know these [things] and cause me trouble.
6     And I am well with the 24 drachmas which you sent through Satrius. I will add
7     and I myself my [contribution]. If it is possible, send someone trustworthy
8     or Apollo to the 25th of Epip so that, if I receive the salary,
9     I will send you 900 drachmas concerning the suspended [matter] of two years. And ...
10    what is owing to my mother I will send to her if anything she wishes. And make
11    me [a] garment through her [with] nothing damaged, not so that I should buy [it] again and get encumbered. Concerning the Nile you will inform [me]
12    what [it] desires. I greet Iulas my siste[r] and
13    Hephestus. Pauni 25.
