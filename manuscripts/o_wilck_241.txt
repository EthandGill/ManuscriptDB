[TRANSLATION]
1   Ammonios and Sok(rates), the two collectors of the money-tax of Elephantine,
2   through Pachompetenephotes the assistant. Paid:
3   Panapothis son of Ammonios, mother Thinnesis, for the assess-
4   ments of the 21st year: 1 drachma 1½ obols. Year 23 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
5   [Pau]ni 13. I, Ammonios, have signed off.
