[TRANSLATION]
1     Joint reckoning, with God, of the estates of Pezoulios and Psaatis and Melanthios
2     and of Thmouei epake and of Amoeis and of Ageron, together also with gold … 14
3     of the outlying places
4     [against so-and-so on behalf of the Water-wheel of] Petnouris: 44 artabas of grain by measure, and on behalf of Megalou Leptinou, according to the quarter share, 8½¼ artabas, 6 choinikes; [t]hey come to 53½¼ artabas by measure, [6 choinikes]
5     [which by the kankellos-measure are 64½¼ artabas]; of weighing-surcharge at 11 per hundred, 7 artabas 6 choinikes; of expense reckoned in, 1 artaba; of helpers, ½ artaba; of carelessness, 4 choinikes; of grain-measurer's fee, ½¼ artaba; they come to [74¼ artabas, 2 choinikes]
6     [against so-and-so,] Matthias the elder and Onnophrios, on behalf of the Water-wheel of Kylles: 29 artabas of grain by measure, which by the kankellos are 34½¼ artabas 2 choinikes; of weighing-surcharge at 8 per hundred, 2½¼ artabas 2 choinikes
7     [of expense reckoned in,] 1 artaba; of helpers, ½ artaba; of carelessness, 4 choinikes; [they come to] 39 artabas 8 choinikes, of which 15½ artabas were measured out, remaining 24 artabas
8     against Apollos son of Poatroesios, on behalf of the Water-wheel of Hatres: 77 artabas of grain by measure, and on behalf of Megalou Leptinou, according to the other quarter share, 9½¼ artabas of grain by measure, 6 choinikes
9     they come to 86½¼ artabas by measure, 6 choinikes, which by the kankellos are 104¼ artabas 2 choinikes; [of weighing-surcharge] at 11 per hundred, 11½ artabas; of expense reckoned in, 1 artaba; of helpers, ½ artaba; of carelessness, 8 choinikes
10    of grain-measurer's fee, 1½ artaba 8 choinikes; they come to 119 artabas 8 choinikes
11    … kra… 4 and foul (lentil?) 80
