[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Appianus, having served as exegete, councilor of the br[ightest]
2     city of the Alexandrians, through Aurelius
3     Ninnus and as he is designated
4     from Aurelius Heron son of Syrus, having served as cosmete-councilor
5     of the Arsinoe city. I wish to hire[ing] … to [the] shrine of Petesoucheios in [the] …
6     … ia pigeon-houses 2 and a small-room one
7     [for a period of] three [years] from the current month
8     [Tybi] of the present first year, of rent of all
9     [according to] each year [of] equal and prepaid [amounts] …
10    … Aphrodisios and Serenus who is also Katilus
11    [of silver:] drachmas four-hundred and additional[ly]
12    … two hundred, unaccounted and without risk[.]
