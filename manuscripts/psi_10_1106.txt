[TRANSLATION]
1     in the consulship of Virius Nepotianus and Tet[tius]
2     Facundus of the most illustrious
3     to Aurelius Isidorus, prapositus of [a] pagus
4     from Aureliuses Beesa (son) of Procles, tessalararius
5     and Heracles (son) of Pamounus and Pabanus (son) of Panephius and Horusr (son) of Heracles and Dioscorus (son) of Chousetus
6     village-heads, all from [the] village
7     Thmoineopsoutheos of the same [pagus]. we give
8     with our own risk for grain-tax-collection
9     of [the] private register of the same village
10    the fortunate 10th indiction the underwritten
11    being well-off and suitable for the need.
12    they are: Aurelii
13    Patereus (son) of Procles
14    Pet[t]iris (son) of Theon
15    both from the same vill[age]
