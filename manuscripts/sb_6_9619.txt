[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twenty-fourth year of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Augustus, Armeniacus,
2     Medicus, Parthicus, Sarmaticus, Germanicus the Greatest, in the month Loios, Pauni 15, in Ptolemais Euergetis
3     of the Arsinoite nome. Heliodoros son of Sotos son of Heron of those having served as cosmetes, being of age
4     forty-two without marks, acknowledges to Aphrodisios son of Didymos as being twenty-eight, guardian of Maron son of [M]aron and Philoumene and Sarapis, the three underage children of Maron Sarapion who served as agoranomos,
5     and to the mother of the underage, being and following after the guardianship, Ammonia daughter of Apion, as being thirty-four without marks, with her lord Isidoros [Her]on as being forty without marks, [that Heliodoros] holds
6     from those around Aphrodisios from [the] account of the underage silver drachmas two thousand being [the] remaining of silver
7     drachmas six thousand being of one talent, which he owed to Heliodoros the father of the underage M[ar]on
8     the aforementioned according to a handwritten [document] double made in the eighteenth year of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar
9     the lord and god Aurelius Antoninus in the month Mecheir; but from the double handwritten [document] alone Heliodoros [states he] delivered
10    the one [copy] to those around [Aphrod]eisios so that to the underage for annulment and nullification; the other handwritten [document] he claims
11    the same Heliodoros to have mislaid, which he also concedes [to be] invalid [in] every way for himself and [all]
12    [bringing] this forward. From which drachmas two thousand … they claim … … concerning [his property of] the
13    Aphrodisios through [the] security of sale of the ar[uras] … reed-land … together with … concerning the village [S]ebennytos
14    [the] silver drachmas six hundred … [and the] [silver] drachmas four thousand for completion of the whole [principal]
15    of one talent [and] the interest … … reed-land … [the] aforementioned
16    of the underage [as] mother Ammonia from the
17    own [property of] hers [now] and still formerly [in] accordance with which he had to her of Heliodoros [his] own written letters; upon the very reed-land [not] to [come] neither him Heliodoros nor those [from] him nor against the aforementioned underage nor against any of them nor against those [from] them [about] nothing, [for] which he holds, as has been set forth, now and still formerly

19    hand nor about any other matter simply nor debt nor all by way of contract whether written nor unwritten from former times until the present day in no way, the seal fixed having been paid. Heliodoros [son of So]tos Heron I hold from those around Aphrodisios silver drachmas
