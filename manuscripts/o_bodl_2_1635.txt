[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis of the produce, year 2, of our lords
2     Gallus and Volusianus, Sebastoi
3     Pauni 20, for the receipts of the same year 2, in the name of heirs
4     Petosirius, likewise of elder Porieuthos, wheat half
5     [a] third, 24, and for the receipts of year 2, in the name
6     Petosirius Loloutus Porieuthos, wheat
7     one eighth, total together wheat artabas 2. Aurelius
8     Besophilus, decaprote, I have been sealed.
