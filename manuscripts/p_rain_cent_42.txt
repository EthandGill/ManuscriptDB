[TRANSLATION]
1     Isokrates to Horos, greetings. Well
2     you will do by showing the courtyard
3     to Petosires the cowherd so that he might have
4     the livestock until the distributing
5     the fodder, and do not do otherwise
6     for it is a necessary need
7     especially indeed also a sheltered building, so that the heifers might have and not
8     be in the open.
9     Farewell. Year 26, Hathyr 12
10    To Horos
