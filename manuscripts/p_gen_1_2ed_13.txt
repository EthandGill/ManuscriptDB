[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Potamon of the Nile, land-holder in Philadelphia, to Aurelius Isa, foreign farmer, greetings.
2     I have received from you the tax/rent of which you farmed [for] m[e]
3     [of] arourae near the village Tanis, [of] 2 arourae for [the] produce of year 19 and 7 and 5 and 3 of wheat artabas
4     five, [which] amount [to] artabas 5 full, and no [account]
5     do I have against you concerning the tax[es].
6     [in the] consulate of Ruff[ius] [Volcasius] and Petronius Annianus
7     [of] the most illustrious, Pauni [the] 15[th]. Aurelius
8     Potamon [I] have received the tax [in full].
9     Aurel[ius Alypius] fr[om] Phil[adelphia]
10    [I] wrote on behalf [of them being] illiterate.
