[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greetings. Ta[syokis] … [I am wronged by Theodotus and Demetrius …]
2     of him, of those from the [island] of Alexander …
3     and my mother having died …
4     royal land to [X] arouras, the men accused … [Apollonius]
5     the overseer and Tesenouphis the toparchus […]
6     [?] not even thus do they vacate. I therefore beg you, king, [to order Diophaneus the strategos to write to Apollonius the overseer]
7     to compel them to vacate the house, if [they say anything in opposition, send them to Diophaneus] … [to be judged] by me, so that through you, king, I may obtain justice. …
8     To Apollonius. Most of all resolve their matter; [if not,] …
9     Year 1, Gorpiaeus 28, Tybi 12.
10    Tasyokis against Theodotus and
11    Demetrius concerning a house.
