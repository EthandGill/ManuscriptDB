[TRANSLATION]
1     Syros to Heroninos and
2     Dionysios [greetings].
3     If indeed the calf
4     which I [ordered] to be given to Pala
5     [having become] pleasing to him
6     and you were with him, O Dionysios, [go] from her to Philoteris and the in place of him
7     they brought this, for thus ordered my lord Alypis.
8     for greatly he needs him here. [Farewell]
9     I pray for you
10    O dearest
11    year 5
12    Athur 18
13    to Heroninos, steward [of Theadelpheia]
