[TRANSLATION]
1     To Kleōn the architect, greetings, the ten-commanders of the quarry-workers from the
2     quarry. We are being wronged by Apollōniōs the foreman. Having cast us
3     into the hard rock, not having [separated] us toward
4     the rest, but the soft [rock] he showed to those with him. Now we are being destroyed exhausting iron [tools]. We beseech you, so that of justice
5     we might obtain, having re-measured for us [more] than which we have hewn the hard
6     rock [so that] it be re-assigned to the [...] quarry-workers, so that we not be oppressed
7     and to all it be assigned. Farewell.
8     Year 30, Phaōphi 24, to Kleōn.
9     The ten-commanders
10    those from the quarry.
