[TRANSLATION]
1     Of Tothes son of Ammonius, mother Taledith, of Hermopolis
2     To Pekosis Kaleous, greetings. I have leased [to you]
3     only the present ninth year those which I have in lease
4     in Hermatuth, eight and two-thirds arouras for [xylamē]
5     of vetches and a bed of sheep for each
6     [aroura], in silver drachmas twenty-[seven]
7     [upon] the same drachmas [two-hundred] [thirty-four]
8     of the [land] to-be-found in sowing from measurement,
9     which [you shall pay] in the Epeiph month of the present
10    [ninth] year of Hadrian Caesar the master
11    in the kleros of Chemmatis. Year ninth of the Autocrator
12    Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus
13    Thoth 20th. Tothes [son of Or]ion, I wrote on behalf of him
14    not knowing letters.
