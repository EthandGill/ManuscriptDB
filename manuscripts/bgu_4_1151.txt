1     Sheet.
2     To Protarchos.
3     From Dionysia daughter of Ariston, with her guardian her mother's [brother Aga]thinos son of Philotas, and from Alexandros son of Neikodemos. [Concerning the matters]
4     in dispute, Dionysia agrees that she has received from Alexandros, by hand from [the house],
5     one hundred drachmas of silver out of two hundred drachmas of silver which the deceased
6     Theodoros, the brother of Alexandros, assigned to her according to the will which he made through the
7     archive of the Jews; and that neither Dionysia nor anyone else on her behalf shall proceed against Alexandros
8     concerning the one hundred drachmas which she has received; and that she has apportioned to him,
9     on account of the remaining hundred drachmas which … for a period of seventeen months from … of the present
10    month Pharmouthi of the seventeenth year of Caesar, which also, having recovered them without interest … will pay
11    the security upon these things … and still the bond; and if,
12    the time having elapsed, Alexandros does not repay the
13    hundred drachmas of silver aforesaid as capital, these together with one-half in addition, and for the
14    time overrun the interest of two drachmas according to the edict, the right of execution belonging [to Dionysia]
15    both upon Alexandros himself and upon all his property as if [by legal judgment],
16    and that he shall not bring forward pledges, or [they shall be invalid]. We make this request.
17    Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 17.
18    Alexandros son of Nikodemos.
19    Dionysia daughter of Ariston.
20    Guardian, mother's brother Agatheinos son of Philotas.
21    Will from two hundred drachmas.
22    One hundred drachmas by hand.
23    The remaining hundred drachmas until Mesore 7 of the coming 18th year of Caesar.
24    Without interest.
25    To Protarchos.
26    From Gaius Julius Philios and from Agathokles [son of] Sarasinos and Lyka[rion]
27    son of Apollonios, both Persians of the epigone. Concerning the matters in dispute, Agathokl[es]
28    and Lykarion agree that they have from Gaius Julius Philios a loan through the money-changing bank of Kastor
29    of seventy drachmas of Ptolemaic silver, interest-free, which they will also repay within
30    three months from the 1st of Pachon of the pr[esent] 17th year of Caesar, giving each
31    day the falling-due five obols until the aforesaid capital is paid in full,
32    making no deficient payment, and that they will do this without [judgment]
33    and any dispute; and if not, that they shall be liable to seizure, and from whatever
34    day they default, not waiting for the time apportioned
35    to them, until they pay off the whole capital or what shall be owed together with one-half in addition,
36    and for the time overrun the interest of two drachmas according to the edict, the right of execution belonging to
37    Gaius Julius Philios both from those who are mutually liable as sureties for repayment and from any one [and from whomever he may choose]
38    and from all their property as if by legal judgment, and further also from the property belonging to one of them,
39    Agathokles, namely two timber-selling workshops in the … one [in the]
40    so-called Kampra by the Kibotos, and the other [in]

44    from every danger, void being any [pledges] which they may bring forward [, of all protection]
