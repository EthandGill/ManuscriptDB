[TRANSLATION]
1     Sosianus to Aspidoutes, greetings.
2     You sent me a letter
3     so that I should not be on your behalf in anger, departing from Maximianus.
4     I have told you: do not write me a letter. I remember your fine words
5     of you having departed from Maximianus.
6     For ten days I was able
7     to receive; you did not see me, nor I you
8     and your fine character.
9     I give thanks to Fronton, the most honored
10    [brother], greatly, and I [remember] you and the
11    graces of yours
12    [surpassing].
