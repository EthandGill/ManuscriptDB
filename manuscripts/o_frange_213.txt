[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Frange, writing, greeting [to] his monks, [the] brothers, [the] abbot and Charisius and their companions, [I] greet also the servants
2     all, according to their names, in [the] Lord
3     greetings, since [my] beloved father
4     of such measure [is the] brother, glory to this in the desert
5     like [that] of my father Israel, the holy [one], because
6     they will see a sign in heaven, which my companion gave [to me], I have come
7     his prayer, [I] gave glory to God
8     he who knows, [I] gave [a] small offering from [the] fifth which was stretched for [these] times
9     [there is] joy in [the] Lord
