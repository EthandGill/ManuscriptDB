[TRANSLATION]
1     Account of passenger fares.
2     And it is through me Aphuges the Chian
3     from Chaereum to Nicius: one talent, two drachmas.
4     Similarly the aforesaid Aphuges held
5     for the fare of other passengers
6     from the aforesaid Chaereum to Heracles: one talent, three drachmas.
7     Similarly fare of other passengers
8     from the same Chaereum to Cynos: … drachmas.
9     In total [for] the account of fare: three talents, … drachmas.
