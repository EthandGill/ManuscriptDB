[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year 15 a]nd 3 and 1, Epeiph … Isidoros son of Hera
2     and Anoupis son of Maskulle[inos] and … [A]ioneōs and Pantel
3     and Priskos and Sarapion …, the six grain-collectors of the village
4     of Karanida. Were measured to us in the treasury of the same village for the produce of the same
5     15th year and 3rd year and 1st year of the name of Didymos Proklos through Isidoros Ptolemaios
6     for the boundary-marking of Karanida and new Pto[lema]is by the public measure
7     strigle of wheat, artabae 10—equal wheat artabai 10—and the tithes.
8     Aurelius I[sí]doros, I have signed [off].
