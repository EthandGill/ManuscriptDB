[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Phoiba]mmon the page, son of Olympios, going [off] to the Arsinoite (nome) with letters, on account of expenses, in the month Pharmouthi of the 9th indiction, 3 carats.
2     … to … of the glorious house, and of the most magnificent councillors, for 1 pair of muslins bought
3     and given to the praetorium on account of the Persians, in the 9th indiction, from 6 carats, the share of the glorious house, 1 carat ½ 8.
4     [For the price of] 1 pair of muslins bought and given for the exchange of the colobium, on account of the Persians, in the month Pharmouthi of the 9th indiction, 3 carats.
5     To [Ge]orgios, instead of Peter the much-respected, on account of piety according to custom, and in the 9th indiction, Pharmouthi of the 9th indiction, 280 folleis.
6     [For ] … bought and given (for) the Persians, in the month Pharmouthi of the 9th indiction, 120 folleis.
7     To [Phoibammon] the page, son of Olympios, going off to the Arsinoite (nome), on account of expenses, Pharmouthi 24 of the 9th indiction, 120 folleis.
8     To … the key-maker, who made the key of the bolts …, 1 carat.
9     To Victor the groom, going off to (the city) of Herakles with letters to Tzimein the most glorious …
10    [For the] price of 50 mirror-tiles bought for the use of the landlord's bath of the village of Takona, and of other [80 tiles for]
11    the bath in the village of Ophis, total 130 tiles, at 3 folleis each, Pauni of the 9th indiction … 390 folleis … .
12    For the price of 6 ropes of palm-fiber bought for the use of the landlord's camels of the glorious house, Epeiph 1 of the 9th indiction …
13    To Phoibammon [the page,] going off to the Thebaid with the fine flour sent to Bottas the most glorious …
14    To Abraamios … the …, going off to Kromydion with letters, on account of expenses, Epeiph 10 of the 9th indiction …
15    To Germanos the p[age,] going off to (the) city of Theodosius for landlord's business, on account of expenses, Epeiph … [of the 9th indiction] …
16    To Peter the p[age, going off to] the Arsinoite (nome), on account of expenses …
