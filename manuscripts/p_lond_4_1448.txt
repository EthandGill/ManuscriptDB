[TRANSLATION]
1     [Account of the things [s]pent through me, Horouonch[io]s the notary, from the month Thoth until Pharmouthi,
2     as follows:
3     [Spent] to [B]asileios the sailor for [f]a[r]e as far as Babylon: 3 solidi, 3 (carats).
4     [Spent] to [Io]annes the soldier who went up, for … the accountant: 1 solidus.
5     Spent to the assistants who … e … : ⅓ of a solidus.
6     In el … [3 (carats)] of a solidus; [i]n the secretariat 3 (carats) of a solidus.
7     [S]pent for the pr[ic]e of [unwr]itten papyrus-sheets: 1 solidus 3 (carats).
8     [S]pent to the assistants of the euesche of the secretariat: 3 (carats) of a solidus.
9     Spe[n]t for the rent of the … i for 7 months at 3 (carats) of a solidus: 2 solidi 3 (carats).
10    Spent for the price of wine for 7 months at 1 (solidus) of a solidus: 7 solidi.
11    Spent for the price of small loaves for 7 months at 3 (carats) of a solidus: 2 solidi 3 (carats).
12    Spent for the price of oil for 7 months at 6 (carats) of a solidus: 1 solidus 6 (carats).
13    Spent for the price of wood for 7 months: 1 solidus.
14    Spent for the price of 1 piglet: 1 solidus.
15    Spent for the price of 2 sheep: ⅓ of a solidus.
16    Spent for the price of salt 29 (units) 3: ½ of a solidus.
17    Spent for the wage of …: 2 solidi 3 (carats).
18    [Spent for] fare from Babylon to Aphrodito: 1 solidus.
19    [Spent] for the price of small change for 7 months at 3 (carats) of a solidus: 2 solidi 3 (carats).
20    [Spent] for the price of a camel on account of the servant-lad: 1 solidus.
21    [Spent] to lord Kadis for the discharge-fee: 6 solidi.
22    [Spent] to l[o]rd Marinos, notary of the same: 1 solidus.
23    [Spent] to lord Iakob for the disch[arge-fee], [li]kewise: 3 solidi.
24    [Spent] to lord Georgios, notary of the [sa]me: ½ of a solidus.
25    Total from the month Thoth until Phar[m]outhi: 39½ solidi 3 (carats).
26    And from the month Pachon, 2nd indiction, until Thoth:
27    Spent for re[n]t for 5 months: 1⅓ solidus.
28    Spent for the price of win[e] for 5 months: 5 solidi.
29    Spent for the price of unwritten papyrus-sheets: 1 solidus.
30    Spent for the price of oil: ½ of a solidus.
31    Spent for the price of small loaves: 1 solidus.
32    Spent for the price of salt 29 (units) 2: 3 (carats) of a solidus.
33    Spent for the price of wood: ½ of a solidus 3 (carats).
34    Spent for the price of 1 piglet: ⅓ of a solidus.
35    Spent for the price of 1 sheep: ½ of a solidus.
36    Spent for the price of small change on account of other expenses: 2 solidi 3 (carats).
37    Spent for fare from Babylon to Aphrodito: 1 solidus 6 (carats).
38    Spent for porters' fees and on account of the lading: ½ of a solidus 3 (carats).
39    Total: 15½ solidi 3 (carats).
