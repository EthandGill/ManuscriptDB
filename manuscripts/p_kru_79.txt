[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Kalisthene the daughter of …, the woman of Neihbabe
2     in the district of Primide, write to the holy place of Abba Phoibamon the holy martyr of the mountain
3     of Jeme, through you, the most reverent abba
4     Sourous the deacon and the superior of that holy monastery, and through everyone
5     who shall be appointed after him forever, greeting.
6     The law of God exhorts the whole race
7     of men to do the good, to do what is pleasing to God,
8     for the benefit of their souls. Since no one has authority
9     to hinder any person from doing what is theirs … of that which is his own; I have considered, because I am a sinner
10    leaving them upon the earth, according to (the way) of all my fathers,
11    I have decreed and I have donated my beloved son
12    Merkoure to the holy oratory
13    of the saint Abba Phoibamon of the mountain of Jeme, that he should become a servant within the holy place forever, for
14    the salvation of my soul, the sinner, and my late husband. I shall inform you with accuracy
15    that, when God the compassionate and the merciful
16    gave me this little son, afterward, when the little child came
17    to … God the good brought upon him a sickness, very severe and strong,
18    so that I despaired of him, namely that he would die.
19    Thereafter we besought God and the saint
20    Abba Phoibamon, namely "if you grant him healing, I shall donate him to your altar,
21    that he become a servant of God in return for the good things that you have done with me." I dedicated this little son
22    of this kind; I brought him up in my instruction, when he came to the age of maturity he was willing and
23    consented to me to go in to the monastery
24    of the saint Apa Phoibamon of the mountain of Jeme for
25    the salvation of his soul; and we said, "It is a place
26    of the wilderness; it needs a vow of this kind." It pleased us
27    in this good thing of this kind. To set apart then this holy monastery, I have applied myself to this donation forever, I, Talitsene the daughter
28    of …, who first wrote above, being willing and persuaded, without any deceit or fear or violence
29    or fraud or trickery, there being no constraint laid
30    upon us, but in our own free purpose,
31    we acknowledge, and I have donated my beloved
32    son Merkoure to this monastery
33    the holy, the saint Abba Phoibamon of the mountain
34    of Jeme, that he may become a servant to it
35    for its sweeping and its lighting and every matter of need
36    belonging to the holy monastery, and that this holy monastery be his lord forever, with the toil
37    of his hands and the wage of his body, whether
38    he is in the monastery or outside the monastery, according to the command of the superior;
39    and it is not permitted to me, nor to any person
40    that is mine, nor to any person at all whatsoever, whether of my village, whether great or small, whether those who rule or
41    those who shall rule over us, to come and sue this holy monastery concerning this little son of this kind,
42    or to make any approach to it before us, magistracy or authority, small or great, to do this —
43    first he shall benefit no one, but God shall set him before His holy oratory with me, that I may go to law with him and he shall give account
44    at the tribunal of God concerning what he has done, namely that he undertook to destroy this vow and the offering
45    which I vowed to God; and he shall draw
46    upon himself the great curses of the scripture which are in the Deuteronomy of Moses, and he shall see the kingdom of God opened and they shall hinder him from going
47    into it. For your security then I have drawn up this donation,
48    and I consent to it according as it is written.
49    I, Magarre, the man of Sne, give witness, and I consent.
50    I, Papnoute, the man of Ermont, give witness, and I consent
51    to the donation, and I give witness.
