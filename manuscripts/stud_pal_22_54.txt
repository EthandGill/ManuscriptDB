1     … [to Cl]audius Herennius, decarch
2     [from] Posei, [son] of Matais, from [the] village
3     [Sokno]paios Nesos, Herakleidi[an]
4     division. Recently, sir, [having] wish[ed] to ent[er] the place which I have
5     being in the part of [a] house [allotted] to me in the same village
