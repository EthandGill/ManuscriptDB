[TRANSLATION]
1     to write to you in order that I [not] appear to have neglected it, but you manage as you wish, if indeed you have decided against me in other matters you ought this before
2     all to know; and if doing this is some way advantageous to you and I write to you, you ought not to neglect; for the whole discussion came about
3     with the strategos being removed, and it is reasonable what he says; for indeed
4     [to] the matter is this advantageous; I have given an account to you then about all things, in order that you not appear to have written without feeling,
5     [if] also shamefully this to you through the letters has been written
6     [un]til this letter I have been content; to condemn
7     you I wish, that a report was given to the most excellent Apollonius
8     concerning three date palms and one stone pine near Hermopolis of Themistus division in an olive-grove-paradise and date-palm-grove of the Tonaitians property lease of Ptolemaeus
9     Protarchos and associates. I write to you then, in order that you know, since long you have remembered to me about the stone pine; evident then
10    to you I have made this, in order that what you wish about this you might reply;
11    Farewell.
12    Greet all your [people] [and] Apion and Heron, the most excellent;
13    until now the administrator has not yet arrived, but is expected.
14    To Heron friend
