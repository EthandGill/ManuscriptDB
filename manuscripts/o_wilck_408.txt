1   Pasemis son of Paos, of Leo( ), and Pasemis (his) son paid,
2   for the drachma of the Memnoneia of year 4, 2 dr. Year 5 of Nero Claudius
3   Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
4   Phaophi 5. Likewise (on the) 12th, for dike-tax 1 dr. 3½ ob.; likewise for Pasemis (his) son 1 dr. [..]
5   which are also 1 dr. 4½ ob.
