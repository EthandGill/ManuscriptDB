1     [Year 23 of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aureli]us Commodus Antoninus Augustus, Armeniacus, Medicus, Parthicus, Germanicus, Sarmaticus, Greatest,
2     in the month Apellaios, Phaophi 2, in Ptolemais
3     [Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges Kyrilla,] daughter of Marcus Valerius Turbo, a citizen, through her guardian appointed for her according to
4     the guardianship-deed completed through Ammonios son of Heron, the contract-scribe, … of Claudius Vitalis, about forty-nine years old, mark 49, a scar on the calf of the right foot — to Longinia Nemesilla,
5     acting without a guardian
6     [by the right of children according to Roman custom, about … ]-four years old, a scar on the left wrist, guardian of her sons, the two Marci Valerii, Montanus
7     and Longinus also called Numissianus … [that she has received] from her, by hand, four thousand drachmas of silver, all from one talent of silver, the legacy assigned
8     to her, Kyrilla, by her deceased [father] … [Marcus V]alerius Turbo, who died, on the conditions written above, to both his and Longinia Nemesilla's
9     children and heirs of Valerius,
10    … [in] accordance with the Roman [testament] which he made … translated into Greek as far as possible, in the past twenty-second
11    [year; and the four hundred] drachmas of silver previously spent by Kyrilla on his laying-out and funeral, which [Long]inia Nemesilla
