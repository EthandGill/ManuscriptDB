[TRANSLATION]
1     Loan of wheat, 1 1/4 artabas, to Irinious daughter of Sentheus;
2     Kastor son of Mithrodates lent (it).
3     In the reign of Ptolemy and Cleopatra also called Tryphaina,
4     his sister, the Father-loving and Brother-loving gods, in the
5     fourth year, in the priesthood of Alexander and the other (priests)
6     written in Alexandria, on the sixth of the month Xandikos
7     (= ) the sixth of Mecheir, in the hamlet of Nestos in the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nome. There lent
8     Kastor son of Mithrodates, Macedonian, hipparch over men, and Hilara his wife, to Ptolemaeus also called Petesouchos
9     son of Horos, Persian of the epigone, and to his wife Apollonia also called Tasous daughter of Harchypsis, a Persian woman,
10    with her guardian, her own husband Ptolemaeus
11    aforewritten, … of wheat, one
12    and a quarter artaba, which they also acknowledge to have had measured out
13    and to have in full together with this contract,
14    which is established as valid. And this loan, the one and a quarter artaba of wheat, let the
15    borrowers repay to Kastor or to his agents in the month Pauni
16    of the same year, new wheat, pure and unadulterated,
17    free from all (impurities), by the half-artaba measure, set up (i.e. delivered) into the same (hamlet) at their own expenses; and of whatever
18    artaba they do not repay as has been written, let them pay the price of each artaba, three thousand copper drachmas, or the highest price these shall be (worth), the right of execution being both from the
19    borrowers themselves and from all their
20    belongings, to the one exacting payment with full authority as if by judgment,
21    they being sureties for one another for repayment. This contract is valid.
22    Witnesses: Ischyrion, Simias, Hermias, Menon, …ios,
23    Apelles, the six, the five of those of Kassander's (troop) …
24    keeper-of-the-contract: [Ischy]rion.
25    [Pto]lemaeus also called Petesouchos, Persian
26    of the epigone, I have the loan
27    …s.
