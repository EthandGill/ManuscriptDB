[TRANSLATION]
1     Horus to Harmaios, greetings. It has been reported to me by many of those from the no[me]
2     those who have sailed down [report] that the oil is being so[ld] at a higher price than that in the order [given]
3     clarified, but from you nothing has been announced to us nor to Imouthis [the]
4     son in the places have [you] transmitted. So then also now clarify for me, how the [oil is being so]ld
5     the oil in the places under you, so that we might report to Theogenes the manager.
6     and for the future let [care] be to you, if anything such occurs or [people] are deceived
7     the farmers and the others, or if any other wrong is done, to write to us or to Imouthis the
8     son in the places to give, so that through this it might be sent to us and we might report
9     to the manager. Farewell. Year 5, Pauni 16.
