[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklas son of Besis, his mother being Taorsenouphis: I acknowledge that I have sold to Herakleides the younger son of Maron, his mother being Arsinoe, the half part belonging to me
2     of a vacant lot, held in common and undivided with my brother Patynis, and of all that pertains to it, in Tebtynis, of which the dimensions of the whole vacant lot are:
3     from south to north, seven and two-thirds cubits; from west to east, fourteen and two-thirds cubits, or however much the full measurement may be, or whatever they may be,
4     together with the exit and entrance, held in common and undivided; of which the neighbors of the whole lot are: on the south, the house of Harmaeios son of Petesouchos and of Haryotes his brother;
5     on the north, the house of the same Herakleides and of the sons of Petesouchos; on the west likewise (the property) of the same Herakleides and, in part, of Harmiysis and Horos, both sons of Orsenouphis; on the east, the house of Orseus son of Orseus, through which is the aforewritten entrance and exit at ground level. And I receive from Herakleides the
6     agreed price, the whole, in full from hand to hand from the house, and I will guarantee against those who shall proceed concerning
7     my dwelling
8     … of the half part. And my wife Tepheros daughter of Tephersos, her mother being Taarmiysis, consents to this sale and (agrees) not to proceed [for] a[ll] time nor to make a claim, nor any other on her behalf, as is aforesaid. There wrote on their behal[f ] …
9     … [because they do not know] letters.
