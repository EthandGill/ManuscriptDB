[TRANSLATION]
1     It was given through Serenus, tenant-farmer, to Serenus, tenant-farmer, on account of [wa]ges of the
2     13th indiction, in private coinage, four nomismata minus sixteen carats; [they] total in private coinage four nomismata minus sixteen carats only.
3     [In the] year 256 [and] 225, [on] Pharmouthi 16, [of the] 13th indiction.
