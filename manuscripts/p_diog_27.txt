[TRANSLATION]
1     Marcus Lucretius Diogenes and my sister Lucretia Octavia, Antinopol[ites]
2     and as we are styled Ignatius Apolimarius, An[tinopolite]
3     and as he is designated, greetings. We acknowledge through this written document to have received from you silver, imperial coinage, four hundred drachmas in full number, and wheat, ten artabas, by the eighth measure of Apolimarius, to which nothing was added, which we shall repay to you in the month of Pauni [without delay].
4     But if we do not repay, [enforcement] being to [you]
5     from both us and from all our property, just as from a judgment.
6     This written document, written simply, apart from
7     [oil] and [subscription], shall be valid everywhere and with everyone who brings it, as if [in the public] [registered]. Lucretius Diogenes
8     and Lucretia [received] the drachmas
9     four hundred [and the] artabas of wheat, ten [and] [we will repay] as aforesaid. [Lucretius I] wrote on behalf of my sister, [she] not knowing letters.
10    Year 22 of Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus
11    Parthicus Maximus Britannicus Maximus
12    Pious Augustus, Hathyr 16.
13    of Diogenes of Paradromai
