1     Asklas and Soter, tax-collectors of silver of Charakos
2     To Pasemes, [son] of Kephalos. We received on account of rent, year 20
3     from Kephalos [the] Gymnastikos, dirty drachmas
4     four; [they] are dirty drachmas 4, year 21 of Hadrian
5     the lord, Thoth 21.
6     Asklas, I have signed, [for] drachmas 4
