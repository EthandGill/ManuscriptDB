[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Berenike, the Benefactor gods, in the fifth year, the priest of so-and-so being]
2     … of Alexander [and] of the Brother gods and of the [Benefactor gods],
3     the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos being Philinna daughter of …,
4     on the twentieth of the month Dystros, in the village of Takona in the Oxyrhynchite [nome], Amyntas son of Menedemos, a Cyrenaean of the epigone, lent to Menedemos son of Theokritos, a Cyrenaean of the epigone,
5     [of silver] visible to the eye, five hundred drachmas, together
6     [with] this contract which is sealed; and let Menedemos repay this loan
7     to Amyntas in the month Peritios in the
8     fifth [year]. And if he does not repay according to what is written,
9     let Menedemos pay to Amyntas or to the (agents) of Amyntas this loan increased by half and interest for the time overrun
10    at two drachmas per mina each month, and let the execution be
11    for Amyntas or for the (agents) of Amyntas according to the decree. And let this contract be valid wherever it is produced. Witnesses: Ptolem[aios]
12    a Macedonian, holder of thirty arouras, of those allotted in the Herakleopolite (nome), of those not yet under a commander; Demeas son of Nikodemos, a Naxian;
13    Philon son of Ptolemaios, a Mysian; Polemokrates son of Philoxenos, a Cyrenaean;
14    Archelaos son of Kallixenos, a Chalkidian; Seirambos son of Phrynichos, a Boeotian;
15    the five (being) of the epigone. Keeper of the contract: Serambos.
