[TRANSLATION]
1     Stephen with God illustrious through me Marnites deputy-proprietor
2     To Damian the most illustrious gold-receiver: provide from the revenues
3     of the sixth indiction to Kosmas iron-and-copper worker likewise for the completion of the salary of a guard
4     of the suburb Paki [for] two festivals Thoth and Tybi of the same sixth indiction
5     from Alexandria of one coin 22 carats, of the other carats 23 having already been given to him through another order
6     in the name of the same Kosmas as for a portion [of those] worn out, these
7     being owed to be reckoned to the same guard for the iron-and-copper worker, and others stolen
8     from the same suburb of the same sixth indiction it comes to through receipt of the same Kosmas Alexandria carats 23
9     it is Alexandria carats twenty-three alone. But no other subscription being brought forward, Tybi 4 of the same sixth indiction Tybi 4 sixth indiction of gold
10    carats twenty-three Alexandria carats 23 Alexandria [of] Marni[tes] …
