[TRANSLATION]
1   Year eleven of Imperator Caesar
2   Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus
3   Augustus Pius. Has worked, for the dike-works of
4   the same year 11, Pauni 25–29,
5   in the old Hexathyron of Theadelphia:
6   Teos son of Kronion, the (son) of Nepheros,
7   mother Taoueis.
8   (2nd hand) Nilos, I have signed.
