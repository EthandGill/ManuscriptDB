[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus Germanicus, in the month
2     Peritios, on the twenty-fifth, = Choiak on the twenty-fifth, in Philadelphia of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge Aunes, aged about fifty-two, with a scar on the little finger of the left hand, and Papontos, aged about
3     fifty, with a scar on the left thumb, both sons of Petermouthes, of those from
4     the Nestos hamlet of the same division, Persians of the epigone, guarantors of one another
5     for the repayment, to Marcus Antonius Aper son of Marcus, of the (tribe) Quirina, aged about
6     twenty-three, without distinguishing mark, that the acknowledging parties are under obligation to repay to
7     Marcus Antonius Aper of the (tribe) Quirina, or to his agents, the (rents) carried forward as arrears
8     upon them from the account of rents up to the past fifth
9     year, and of the fifth year itself, of Imperator Caesar Domitian
10    Augustus Germanicus, of which they cultivate of Marcus Antonius, around the aforewritten village Philadelphia, of a holding of twenty-four arouras, of wheat, by the fourth measure of Philippos, that of Marcus Antonius, one hundred eighty-one and a half artabas, and of barley eighty-seven artabas, in
11    five years from the present year, in the month Pauni, per year in equal parts, together with the rents they measure out, without delay and without pretext.
12    But if the acknowledging parties do not repay as is written, let them pay
13    to Marcus Antonius with the one-and-a-half (penalty), the right of execution belonging to Marcus
14    Antonius from the acknowledging parties both, and from one (of them), and from whichever of them
15    he may choose, and from all their belongings just as by legal judgment, apart from the other (obligations)
16    which the one, Papontos, owes to the same (man): of hay, three arouras for the fodder of sheep, the (leases) remaining valid
17    which Marcus has of them, of leases of holdings. The subscriber for the acknowledging parties (is) Dioskoros son of Kephalion, aged about 63, with a scar on the left wrist.
18    Aunes and Papontos, both sons of Petermouthes, Persians of the epigone,
19    guarantors of one another for the repayment, of those from the Nestos hamlet, we acknowledge that we are under obligation to repay to Marcus Antonius Aper son of Marcus, of the (tribe) Quirina,
20    in five years from the present sixth year, per year
21    together with the rents which we owe per year, the appropriate (portion), the (rents)
22    carried forward as arrears upon us from the account of rents of the earlier times
23    up to the past fifth year, and of the fifth year itself
24    of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus Germanicus, of which we cultivate of Marcus, around Philadelphia, of a holding of twenty-four arouras,
25    of wheat one hundred eighty-one and a half artabas, and of barley
26    eighty-seven artabas, apart from the other (obligations) which the one, Papontos, owes: of hay,
27    three arouras for the fodder of sheep, the (leases) remaining valid which
28    you have of ours, of leases of holdings, and of the tenancy of another lease which you have of me, Aunes, as is aforesaid. There wrote on their behalf
29    Dioskoros son of Kephalion, because they do not know letters. Marcus Antonius
30    Aper son of Marcus, of the (tribe) Quirina: the agreement has been made with me as is aforesaid. In the sixth year of Imperator
31    [Ca]esar [Domi]tian Augustus Germanicus, Choiak 25. It has been registered through …chos, at the record-office of Philadelphia.
32    Agreement of Aunes and his brother with Marcus
33    Antonius (Aper) of the (tribe) Quirina, of arrears of wheat 181 1/2, of barley 87 artabas.
