1     To Zenon greetings Horos. … … of the light I was in the silver-stern
2     the one-and-a-half oared, into which [the] king boards… [when I had cried out] to the king about my vineyard, as [they] had written from …, and I was to control the property of the 23rd year
3     The time had therefore passed to me … for me to recover the property. When I had cried out to the king … [order] and I recovered the vineyard
4     and the documents. But in the … he cried out to Alkides to judge
5     me against him; when [we] had been judged … in the city of Aphrodite I prevailed
6     in the judgment and the judges pronounced … it was given to me; my opponent wrote
7     a deed of discharge against… having paid a certain [amount] the deed was given and
8     the judgment to Pharates as… [I] have therefore written to you about these things, so that you know that
9     I must recover the deed [and the judgment] …
10    Farewell.
