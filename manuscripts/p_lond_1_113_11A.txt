[TRANSLATION]
1     I Apollos, son of Antonius, sponge-head, from the village of Arrabos of the Arsinoite nome
2     to you Abramios, Hebrew, son of Theodotus, from the city of Arsinoe, [I] am ready to exchange
3     with you the vinegar-jars of one hundred twenty-eight [measures] of wine of the sixth indiction
4     until the month of Tybi, and the wine in return for it, acceptable, I will give to you from
5     the seventh indiction, and you to profit from the vinegar of the sixth indiction as it seemed good between us
6     It was written in the month Thoth 19, of the sixth indiction, through me Helias, notary-scribe
