1     To Sarapion, of the successors and sub-administrator...
2     from Ptolemaios the Macedonian, the one being in [sanctuary] in the great Serapieion near Memphis...
3     [for] 11 years already. Remember, I gave to you the [document] from...
4     [the k]ing through the window [being] sealed concerning the...
5     [twins'] petition, and you said to me before Sarapis: since...
6     [you are in sanctuary], who then shall be responsible for these [matters]? I have recommended...
7     to you Apollonios, my younger brother.
8     We have no helper except you and Sarapis.
9     The two sisters are being wronged by those in charge...
10    of the [allowance] of the temple. Of the 18th year, from Mechir...
11    until Mesore and the five intercalary days they did not receive. Of the 19th year, from Mechir until Mesore...
12    [30 days] and the five intercalary days they did not receive, except the half of [the provisions]. Of the present 20th year, from Thoth 1 until Choiak 9 they did not receive...
13    [except] the half, from Choiak 10 until today they did not receive bread. They are selling their...
14    [spelt] of the artaba [for] drachmas 40, and the two sisters...
15    are wasting away from hunger. But the king has paid the allowance, [yet] it is being plundered by those in charge of the temple. We beg you, if it seems good to you, to summon back Psintas, the overseer...
16    of the temple, and to compel [him] to give to us...
17    the amounts owed of the 18th year and of the 19th year...
18    [of] spelt [artabas] 160,
19    Similarly also the [amount] having come from the mentioned...
20    Asclepieum from the [period of] mourning...
21    outstanding [allowance, that is [four] loaves [of] bread. May Sarapis...
22    repay you with gratitude and goodwill toward the king.
23    Farewell.
