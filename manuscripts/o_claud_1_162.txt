[TRANSLATION]
1     Pthaios to Peteneph the
2     father, greetings. Receive
3     from Chennamis the camel-driver a basket of meats and a liver one.
4     Greet your wife and your children. Not well
5     did you [write]
6     that I owe [you]
7     four staters, which I do not owe.
8     Reply to me
9     therefore if you have received
10    the meats.
11    Farewell.
