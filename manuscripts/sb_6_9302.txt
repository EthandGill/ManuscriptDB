[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King] Ptolemy, greetings. Philotas, torch-bearer of the
2     [in Apollo's] city, the great [of cléruchs. Since]
3     [frequent] droughts are becoming in the land now already and
4     completely, I wish to report to you, king, a device, [from] which you will suffer no harm, and the land will be saved
5     [because too] much for three years not [to have risen] the [river] [famine] ... such a famine...
6     ... will perish ...
7     ... but if you will, king, there will be great prosperity.
8     [I beseech] [therefore] you, king, if it seems good to you, to order Ariston the
9     general, giving [me] thirty days of provisions, [to] send me the quickest way to you or ...
10    petition, so that, if it seems good to you, king, from the seed
11    immediately it might come to be. And it will be, you being willing, through the whole
12    of the Thebaid in days fifty at most.
13    Prosper.
