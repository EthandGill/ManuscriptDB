3     and for distributions of water-suppliers of year 7 and for the other expenses of the care of Pachon
4     month of wine of vintage 6 of year of property of Colluthus winepress 2 jars 97, of vintage 5 of year of property Libyan winepress 4
5     double jars 5 single jars 8, of which the prices Sarapias the manager was ordered to record, on one hand from vintage 6 of year of wine as of the jar 1 drachmas 12, on the other hand from vintage 5 of year of the on one hand
6     Pachon 30
7     of jar 1 drachmas 16, of the double jars as of 1 drachmas 24. Be well and give the wine jars
8     [hundred] five double jars five, it amounts to jars 105 double jars 5.
9     Of year 7 Pachon 30.
