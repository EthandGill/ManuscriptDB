[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting, Pistos son of Leontomenes, a Persian of the epigone. I am wronged by Aristokrates,
2     a Thracian, holder of a hundred arouras of the 1st hipparchy, of those dwelling in Autodike. For in the 3rd year, on the 8th of the month Audnaios, I, in Autodike … to him by contract … Ptolemai … on condition that I should accompany him on the campaign,
3     providing him with services … to bring [him] back to Autodike, receiving from him as wages each month
4     the amount agreed between us, and although I provided him the services and was blameless and brought him back to Autodike … contract … Aristokrates owing me from the wages
5     [10] drachmas, when demanded by me he does not pay, looking down on me because of my weakness, but is such as to harass me. Therefore I beg you, king,
6     to instruct Diophanes the strategos to write to Pythiades the epistates to send Aristokrates to him,
7     and, if what I write is true, to compel him to pay me back the 10 drachmas and to take up the contract for me, so that
8     I may not be harassed by him, in order that through you, king, I may obtain help.
9     Farewell.
10    To Pythiades. Having investigated, see to it that he obtains justice. Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 28.
11    Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 28.
12    Pistos son of Leontomenes, a Persian,
13    against Aristokrates holder of a hundred arouras, concerning 10 drachmas
14    and a contract.
