[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos,
2   from Tryphon son of Ptolemaios, patron
3   of associations (eranoi), and from Lysimachos son of Theodot[os and]
4   his wife Marion daughter of Isakis and Tryphon
5   son of Theodotos, the three Persians, with the guardi[an]
6   of Marion, her husband(?). That which they pay down
7   (is) one share of a ten-drachma association (eranos) ...lēr.ou,
8   which was one hundred and 40, of which the beginning is from Pham(eno)th
9   of the 20th (year) of Caesar, they agree to bring forward,
10   and the ... through (their) hand 140 (drachmas) and pro[ ... ]
11   for the remaining 13 months from [Pharmo(uthi)]
12   of the same (year) the monthly 10 (drachmas?), n[o]
13   month being left hollow (defaulted), or in whatever mon[th]
14   they do not pay the monthly [10 (drachmas)],
15   [to pay off, within th]e time, t[he amount that will be owed]
16   [with] the half-as-much (penalty) of the time over[run]
17   the interest at (two drachmas?), the exaction being made [for]
18   Tryphon both from them themselves, being mutually-bound, for [re-]
19   [payment] and from any one and from whichever of them he chooses,
20   just as by judgment ....Fr
21   Lysimachos son of Theodotos
22   Marion daughter of Isakis
23   and Tryphon son of Theodotos
24   association (eranos), 10 (drachmas)
25   from Phame(noth) 14
26   for the remaining 13 months from Ph(a)r(mouthi).
