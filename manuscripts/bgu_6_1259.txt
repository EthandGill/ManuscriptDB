[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy surnamed Alexander and Berenike his sister, the Mother-loving gods,
2     year 15, the priests and priestesses and the kanephoros being those (in office), on the 3rd of the month Thoth, in Pathyris, under Hermias
3     the (agent) of Paniskos the agoranomos:
4     Patous son of Horos, a Persian of the epigone, about 60 years old, of medium build, honey-coloured, with straight hair, long-faced, straight-nosed, sold from the
5     grain-bearing dry land belonging to himself and his brothers
6     in the strip (of Pathyris) called
7     of Telon, undivided, the share falling to him, one aroura; the neighbours of the whole land being: on the south the land
8     of Patseous son of Phibis, on the north the land of Pboukis, on the east the enclosure of the vineyards, on the west the royal road,
9     or whoever may be the neighbours on every side. Pakoibis son of Patous, a Persian, about 30 years old, bought (it) for two talents of bronze; the warrantor and guarantor of all matters relating to this sale is Patous the vendor, whom Pakoibis accepted
10    Pakoibis the purchaser.
11    I, Hermias the (agent) of Paniskos, have transacted (this).
12    Year 15, Mecheir 12, there has been paid to the tenth (tax) of the sales-tax through Paniskos the tax-farmer:
13    Pakoibis son of Patous, for grain-bearing dry land in the strip of Pathyris
14    called of Telon, one aroura, whose neighbours have been declared through the aforesaid deed,
15    which he bought from Patous son of Horos for two talents of bronze, making 2 talents;
16    the tax: 1200(?) drachmas.
17    Paniskos the tax-farmer: 1360(?) drachmas.
18    Purchase of Pakoibis,
19    against Patous.
