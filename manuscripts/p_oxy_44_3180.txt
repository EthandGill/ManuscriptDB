[TRANSLATION]
1     one year of Gaius Messius Quintus Traianus
2     Decius Caes[ar] of the lord, Mesore eleven.
3     he wrote to Aurelius Dionysius who is also Apollonius
4     decaprotus of Apeliote toparchy Aurelius Ammonarus Panaron taxes and didrachm [fee] and zeugmatic [tax] collection
5     of the pre[sen]t one year drachmas twenty-eight,
6     total drachmas twenty-eight. Aurelius Leonides I have noted.
