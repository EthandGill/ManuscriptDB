[TRANSLATION]
1     Ischyrion to Cornelius his father, greetings.
2     I received your letter through Serenus. I learned
3     Indeed about my mother. And do you also try to write me
4     About your health and [of] ours. The clothes, if they have been made, do not [send] to me until
5     I shall inform you about them. I greet brother Horion and my mother Thaesis and
6     Everyone in the house by name. I wish you well, father.
7     Give [this] to Cornelius from Ischyrion, [his] son
