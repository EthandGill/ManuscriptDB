[TRANSLATION]
1     Longos to Rufinos, greetings. Before all things I make your obeisance...
2     before all the gods. To know...
3     you I wish, that Bellicos wrote to me...
4     saying [concerning] the [great] serving-dish, that you have it in order that you send it to me; but if you do not [have]...
5     it, write to me in order that I [write]...
6     to him. You will say to Mucrates...
7     if he makes for me the half-artabe of the breads.
8     Farewell.
