[TRANSLATION]
1     Clea[nax] to Zenon, greetings. Concerning the mules
2     which I [sent] to you, the servants, in order that [they be purchased] for us
3     and [they] be transported to you and concerning those from Apollo[nius]
4     they be sent to the gift-estate in Memphis of Apollonius; you would do well
5     if indeed you have attended, but if not, having given thought so that
6     safely with your [people] they be sent as being
7     of Apollonius, lest they be troubled at any toll,
8     and if indeed he himself arrives, but if not, having written to us concerning
9     them whether they have arrived and whether they are sent and what
10    management you have brought about, so that we might know that they are [ours]
11    and they are safely preserved; but if they have not yet arrived, so that with your [directions], as we have asked you to manage [this].
12    Farewell. Year 33, Daisios 13.
13    [Year] 33, Pauni 19.
14    [Cl]eanax.
15    To Zenon.
