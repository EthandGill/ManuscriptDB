[TRANSLATION]
1     [A] memorandum to Zenon from Alexander and
2     Ismail. We ask you, since last year [when] we worked 3½ arurae, it befalls us those [things]
3     both to work on and another plot greater than this
4     to become fallow because you did not provide for us
5     the [things] according to the agreement —, now then we ask you to do
6     just as [you] promised, so that [you] also might advance-lend to us;
7     For you yourself know that no crop has come to be
8     on account of the lack of water, and for the future time [we] have toiled. Now then tell [us]
9     us what it might seem to you, so that we not be ruined
