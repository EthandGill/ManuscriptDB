[TRANSLATION]
1   Pamonthes son of Haryothes paid,
2   for the vineyard of year 13, of 4 arouras, ten drachmas — total 10 dr.,
3   and for wine one drachma, an obol — total 1 dr. 1 ob.,
4   and the surcharges on these. Year 14 of Tiberius
5   Claudius Caesar Augustus
6   Germanicus Imperator,
7   month Sebastos 25.
8   Kephalos, [I have signed].
