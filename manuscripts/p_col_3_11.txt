1     Zenon Protogenes [Apollon]idès to Zenon greetings. We, hearing the goodwill
2     which towards all the [fellow-cit]izens you have, we praise you and would gladly
3     [have arranged beforehand] for you [wanting to converse about] both the things advantageous to the city and their own.
4     Since it has not [come about for us] ... θε ... [to be able], considering the three, it is fitting for you, just as [for the other] citizens who conduct themselves from the most excellent manner, to care for these things, we [ask] you [to return] the letter which we gave to Apollonides, being useful to all [of us], with Pyrrhus and Apollonidus
5     to Apollonius, and [if any] other thing you are able [to do], to cooperate with us so that we might obtain [consideration].
6     knowing precisely that [having gone] into our own [place], we will not forget these [things]
7     but to the [people] we will report in return so that [it becomes] clear to you
8     and we ourselves will [try] to give thanks.
9     Prosper.
10    Zenon Protogenes Apollonidès.
11    Year 29 Xanthicus. In Memphis.
12    [To Zen]on
