[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of an agreement. In the 2nd year of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
2     in the month Sebastos, the 2nd, in Tebtynis of the division of Polemon
3     of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge to one another
4     Kronion son of Kronion, about 54 years old, with a scar on the left forearm,
5     and the woman who became his wife, being also his sister by the same father
6     and the same mother, Taorsenouphis, about 50 years old, with no distinguishing mark,
7     she with her guardian, the father of them both,
8     Kronion son of Cheos, about 76 years old, with a scar on the right hand,
9     that they have dissolved the cohabitation with one another
10    which had been established between them without written contract, and that it is permitted
11    to each of them to manage their own affairs
12    as they choose, and for Taorsenouphis to live with another
13    husband, she being free from accusation in every way; and concerning what all the aforementioned persons allege, namely that Kronion carried off
14    of his sister Taorsenouphis's ornament: gold of one mina-weight and ten quarters, and of unworked (silver)
15    of the weight of twenty-eight staters, and that he had it converted to cash
16    for his own use, the acknowledging Kronion shall under compulsion
17    repay to his sister Taorsenouphis in equal ornaments
18    within sixty days from the present day, the right of execution belonging to her, Taorsenouphis, against her brother
19    Kronion and against all his property; and concerning
20    the other matters pertaining to the cohabitation, (they agree) that
21    they shall not proceed against one another on any pretext whatsoever,
22    nor Kronion against Taorsenouphis, not even concerning what he purchased
23    in any manner whatsoever, because she herself has paid out the price of these things
24    from her own funds, but not even concerning any other
25    matter at all, whether written or unwritten,
26    up to the present day. There have been born to them from one another
27    sons Sasopis and Pakebkis and a daughter Tephorsaein.
