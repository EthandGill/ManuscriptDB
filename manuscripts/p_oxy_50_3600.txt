[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of the Flavii Probus and Avienus, the most illustrious,
2     Choiak 5, of the 11th indiction.
3     Flavius Tim[o]theos son of Ioannes,
4     of the prefectural staff of the province of Arcadia,
5     originating from the village of Koba of the Herakleopolite nome, now residing in the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
6     to Aurelius Komes son of Serenos son of … n …
7     b … of the same city of the Oxyrhynchites,
8     greeting. Of my own free will I undertake to lease
9     from the present month Choiak of the [pr]esent
10    eleventh indiction, from the (property) belonging
11    to you, situated in the same city, in the quarter
12    of the lane of the lodging-house of Aollos, of a house
13    facing the east, an entire
14    dining-room looking to the north, with
15    all [its] right and appurtenances,
16    [a]nd I will pay for [the re]nt …
17    … [which] rent I shall pay
18    yearly, by [six-mon]th (instalments), the half; and whenever
19    you wish I shall hand over the same
20    dining-room with its possession unimpaired
21    as also I received it. Valid is the lease,
22    written in a single copy, [and] on being questioned I acknowledged it.
23    I, Flavius Timotheos son of Ioannes, official, the aforesaid, have made the
24    lease and shall pay the rent
25    and the things written agree with me
26    as aforesaid, and on being questioned I acknowledged it, and having subscribed with my own hand I issued (it).
27    … by me id …
28    lease … of Timotheos, offic… e … iui … an of the village of Kob[a] of the Her[a]kleopol[ite nom]e.
