[TRANSLATION]
1     To the Aurelii Dioskoros, former exegetes, and Nemesion
2     also called Liberalis, former agoranomoi, councillors, keepers of the records
3     of the public accounts of the Herakleopolite,
4     from the Aurelii Tamounis, daughter of Horos, her mother being Thenpachnoubis,
5     and Thenpachnoubis her sister on the father's side, her mother being
6     Thenphatres, of those from the village of Ankyron, through
7     Titus Aelius Sarapammon, veteran. We have extracted from the glued-together house-by-house census-returns of the 24th year
8     of Severus Antoninus, according to
9     the orders given by Valerius Datus, the former prefect, (from the returns) of Ankyron, after others, of column 19:
10    … it is as follows: to the village-scribe of Ankyron,
11    from Aurelius Pareitis, son of Horos, (his mother) Thenpachnoubis, from
12    the village of Ankyron. I register myself for the present 24th year of Marcus
13    Aurelius Severus Antoninus Caesar the lord, according to the orders given by Aurelius Antinoos, the most excellent, who has succeeded to the
14    prefecture, in the house belonging to me which is in the middle
15    of the village. And I am Aurelius Pareitis, 37 years old;
16    my wife … about 25 years old;
17    … year 1, (child of) Paritis;
18    Aurelia Tamounis, sister of me the aforementioned, her mother being the same, 33 years old;
19    Aurelia Thenpachnoubis, my sister on the father's side, her mother being Thenphatres,
20    … years old. There belongs (to us) … a … part, after others:
21    … and other appurtenances.
22    And I swear by the fortune of Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus
23    Caesar the lord and by the god of the nome,
24    Herakles, that I have soundly and truthfully submitted the aforesaid
25    declaration …
