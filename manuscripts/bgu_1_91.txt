[TRANSLATION]
1     To Megalonymos, strategos of the Arsinoite, Themistos an[d]
2     Po[lemon] divi[si]ons:
3     from P[a]sion also called Didymos, [village]-scribe
4     of Talei and Ibion of the Twenty-five-aroura-holders.
5     Being asked by you for names for the marsh-service of the sowing that is taking place under good auspices, I give the undersigned, being well-off
6     and suitable; and they are:
7     of Talei: Ptollas son of Chairemon, surnamed Heron, having means of 800 drachmas
8     Phaesis son of Nepheros, likewise having means of 700 drachmas
9     Sarapion son of Mareious, [likewise] having means [of] 800 [drachmas]
10    Chairemon the younger, son of Chairemon, likewise having means [of] … [drachmas]
11    Maron son of Heron son of Panesneus, likewise having means of 800 drachmas
12    Sarapion son of Orsenouphis, likewise having means of 800 drachmas
13    Heron son of Heron son of Orseus, likewise having means of 800 drachmas
14    Souchion son of Achtibous, likewise having means of 700 drachmas
15    Hiermas, fatherless, of the mother Tapontos, likewise 800 drachmas
16    Didas, fatherless, [of the mother] Tapontos, likewise having 700 drachmas
17    Orseus son of Heron Taskeilos, likewise having means of 800 drachmas
18    Ischyras son of Horion son of Poanor, having means of 700 drachmas
19    of Ibion of the Twenty-five-aroura-holders, likewise:
20    Panechotes son of Geminus, likewise having means of 800 drachmas
21    Heron son of Sarapion, surnamed Lasis, likewise having means of 700 drachmas
22    Horos son of Patron son of Ptolemaios, having means [of] 800 drachmas
23    Orsenouphis son of Horos son of Chairemon, having [700 drachmas]
24    I, Pasion the village-scribe, have submitted (this).
25    Year 11 of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the lord
26    Armeniacus Medicus Parthicus Maximus.
