[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Athanasios,
2     Didymos.
3     (by) your heavenly
4     God, so may you bring in marriage your male
5     children, above all,
6     since you have me as debtor
7     of this great favour, apply
8     yourself to the church. And the price
9     of the donkey and the other
10    three solidi,
11    from these spend
12    and complete the church. For already Isiaeias
13    has found the stones,
14    the stone-cutter. So he agreed with the man. And buy
15    two gold pieces,
16    and they are content. Complete
17    the columns. From Tampemou have the
18    fishing(-boat) made before the
19    disembarkation to bring. I beg you, do not
20    disregard this request of mine.
21    I pray that you be in good health
22    for many years.
23    (…) the request. For indeed on behalf of my soul and of yours you act,
24    prepare the construction of the machines of the estate.
25    Go around the estate. I have sent to you also Heortasios to be at your disposal.
26    All the memoranda of the Augustalis which you have, I intend to inspect as is needful; send them to me with haste. For I have need of them in Pelusion. Do not …
27    of my freedwoman, and in this too you have me as debtor,
28    as she herself also urged you. And I am confident that rather you do everything as if I were present, that we may be able
29    to find the public (dues). And do not neglect the columns. Press
30    the stewards. Find a free doorkeeper.
