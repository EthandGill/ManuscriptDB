[TRANSLATION]
1     Festinus to Niger
2     to the most honored, very many greetings;
3     before all things I pray you to be in good health
4     and to prosper; you will do well
5     brother, having given to Petronius the drachmas two
6     since you know that I ordered [them] for him
7     from my [account] them. And I sent to you
8     and through Petronius a new stater which they did not receive
9     in order that you exchange it. Do not
10    therefore do otherwise brother. I pray [you to be strong]
11    [and] to prosper.
