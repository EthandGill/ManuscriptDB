[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos, the one in charge of the court,
2     from Achilleus son of Theon, of Althaia, and from Lyka daughter of
3     Zoilos, a Persian woman, with her guardian her husband Dionysios son of Ptolemaios, a Persian of the epigone, and that same Dionysios. Lyka and Dionysios agree that they have from Achilleus a loan
4     hand to hand from his house, of Ptolemaic silver, three hundred
5     and ten drachmas, on which they will pay interest of eight obols on each mina
6     per month from Hathyr of the present twenty-sixth year
7     of Caesar, and they will repay the principal within three months from that same
8     month without any postponement, or else they are forthwith liable to seizure, or whichever of them is present is, and they are to be held until
9     they pay off the loan with the half again added, and the interest at the simple rate,
10    and for the time overdue the two-drachma interest according to the edict,
11    the right of execution belonging to Achilleus both from those who are jointly liable for each other for payment and from any one of them whom he
12    may choose, and from all their property as if by legal judgment,
13    all pleas being void and all securities they may bring forward,
14    and every shelter. We make our request.
15    Achilleus: I agree according to what is written above.
16    Lyka and Dionysios: I agree
17    according to what is written above. Dionysios: I have been registered as her …
18    guardian and I have written concerning
19    her, she not knowing letters.
