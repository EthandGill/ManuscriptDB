[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our masters Theodosio[s] for the 12th time and Valentinianus for the 2nd time,
2     the eternal Augusti, Phamenoth 17.
3     To the office of the princeps of the authority of my lord the most illustrious governor
4     of the province of Arcadia, the attendants Ptolemaios and Paulos being singulares
5     and executors, from the Aurelii Pausirios and Horion, sons of Horona…,
6     the same Horion making the plea on behalf of Onnophrio[s] son of Onnophr[i]os
7     according to the written commission given to him, both being from the village
8     of Senokomis of the Oxyrhynchite nome. Kyros from the metropolis,
9     agent, having come, through the presenting of a petition, to the same authority of the same
10    my lord the most illustrious governor Flavius Demetrianus Maximus, and having accused
11    us concerning a debt, and the decision having been disclosed to us which wished (us)
12    either to come to a settlement or to go to trial — relying therefore on our just claims
13    we have come to the d[ue] reply, calling upon the cognizance in your undefiled
14    court, for the conclusion of what has been declared.
15    In accordance with this, we acknowledge, on mutual surety, swearing by God the Almighty
16    and by the piety of our all-conquering masters the Flavii
17    [Th]eodosios and Valentinianus, the eternal Augusti, that we shall from here
18    now sail up to the office wherever the court may be conducting (business), and go to trial against the
19    [aforesa]id adversary, and not be left behind until the conclusion (whatever) the matters of the case may chance (to be),
20    so that we be in no way blamed. Valid is the reply, written in single copy, and on being questioned I acknowledged.
21    We, Aurelii Pausirios and Horion, sons of Horona…, fr[o]m the v[il]lage of Sen[okom]is, have made the
22    reply and we have sworn th[e] divine oath an[d] we shall act as is set forth. I, Aurelius Heraklas
23    son of Gaianos, wrote on their behalf, since they do n[ot kn]ow letters.
24    by me … meu …
25    Reply of Pausirios and Horion his brother, from Senokomis.
