[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius John, the most glorious and most eminent general,
2     Thoth 1 of the 8th indiction, in Oxyrhynchus.
3     To Flavius Apphous the most illustrious tribune, son of him of the most excellent
4     memory, Eulogius, landowning here in the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, I, Aurelius John, son of Banos and his mother Senamounis, from the village of Senokomis of the Oxyrhynchite
5     nome, greeting. Of my own free will I undertake to lease, for a four-year period from the present month Thoth, the new-moon (day), of the year
6     176 = 145, of the present eighth indiction, from the property belonging to your nobility lying at the same village,
7     to the south of it, a whole farmstead and the
8     dwellings within it, near the bakery, facing north,
9     with all the appurtenances and rights, and I will pay as rent yearly one gold nomismation, it makes [of gold] 1 nomisma, which rent I will repay each year by half-yearly (instalments), the half, and whenever
10    your nobility should wish, I will hand back the same farmstead
11    with the dwellings in it and the unimpaired possession,
12    just as I also received (it). (The lease) is valid.
13    I, Aurelius Sarapion son of Timotheos, …
14    through me, Kosmas … 6th indiction, 8th.
15    Lease of John.
