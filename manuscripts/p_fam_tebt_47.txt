[TRANSLATION]
1     To Philantinoos also called Herodes, an Antinoite, and to
2     Tanouphis and Didyme, the two (women), through the aforesaid
3     Philantinoos also called Herodes.
4     From Herakleos son of Maximos, from the metropolis. I wish to lease from you the (lands) belonging to each of you according to the right that each has
5     near the village Theogonis, of a catoecic kleros, three arouras or however many there may be, for four years from the coming fourth year, at a total rent each year, unsown, of thirteen and a half artabas of wheat, free of deductions and risks; and I will carry out each year all the agricultural works that are fitting from
6     my own (means) at the proper seasons, doing no damage, sowing the arouras with whatever crops I choose
7     except safflower, and I will pay the rent in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor of the aforesaid
8     village, by the six-choinix measure of the granary of the
9     village, (the wheat) clean, unadulterated, free of clods, free of barley,
10    sifted, the measuring being done
11    by one of your (men), the public
12    transport-charges being upon me, Herakleios, and the
13    other public (charges) all being upon
14    you. And I will also give for the donkey-driver's (charge) of wheat
15    a sixth of an artaba. And after the term I will hand over the arouras, the half part after
16    the harvesting of wheat, and the remaining half part after the resting (under) hay-crop, and clean
17    of rush, reed, dog's-tooth grass, and all dirt,
18    if it seem good to lease (them).
19    Herakleos, 52 years old, scar on the little finger of the left hand.
20    Year 3 of Lucius Septimius Severus Pius
21    Pertinax Augustus Arabicus Adiabenicus, in the month
22    Mesore 21.
