r.9    εἴς με ἡ πρᾶσις καθὼς πρόκιται.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Paponton, also called Diodorus, [son] of Pyrrhus, mother Tasokis, acknowledge [that I] have sold to Haryotes
2     the younger [Haryotes], surnamed Sakbelius, mother Taorseute, the [property] which belongs to me
3     bare land, of which the measurements [are] from south to north twenty cubits, from west to east cubits
4     ten, and [for all] of the measurements, whether [they are] more or less;
5     of which neighbors [are] on [the] south and east [are] royal streets, [on the] north [is] Paponton's bare land,
6     on [the] west [are] other bare lands of the purchaser Haryotes; and I have received from Haryotes the agreed-upon price entirely in full in hand from the [house] and I shall guarantee the sale with every
7     guarantee from [both] public and private [claims] and every [encumbrance] throughout all
8     time as aforesaid. Kronion son of Annius wrote for him because he did not know letters. Haryotes [son] of Haryotes mother Taorseute [confirms that] it has become
9     the sale to me as aforesaid.
