1     Maximus to Ophellius the
2     most esteemed, greetings.
3     You will do well [by] transferring the two minas to Heras the
4     brother of yours, since I am greatly troubled by those who have entrusted [funds]. But see that you do not neglect [it].
5     Farewell, you,
6     I pray.
