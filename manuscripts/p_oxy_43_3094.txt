[TRANSLATION]
1     Many greetings, Eutychidas. We, Sarapas and
2     Gaia, salute you and make your obeisance, and that of your brother
3     Heraclides, and of your wives
4     and children, before great Sarapis. Know that after the
5     unlawful judgement which he pronounced
6     against Gaia, Agrippas, she petitioned the former prefect Datus, asking to recover for maintenance the interest of her bounty, and he ordered her by subscription to petition the same Agrippas. And she petitioned Agrippas too, and he undertook
7     the inquiry. But later, the memorandum having been put forward and the fortunate prefect Julius Basilianus having auspiciously arrived in the city, I petitioned
8     him by means of a petition, prefacing it with the
9     ordinance blamed by Agrippas
10    and others, and the whole document of Heraclitus
11    and the subscription and
12    the memorandum of Agrippas, complaining that he had given his verdict contrary to the laws,
13    and complaining also
14    of the advocate for not having set forth
15    my just claims, and requesting that either what had been reported to me be restored or my own (property).
16    He ordered me by subscription, having referred the memorandum, to use my lawful (rights). And since the
17    subscription was ambiguous, I gave him another,
18    again setting out everything aforesaid … and requesting whether he wishes me to use the
19    subscription of Heraclitus. And again he subscribed, agreeing with her
20    who orders me (to proceed) by the procedure of the laws
21    … (to do). Therefore I write to you that you too may help me … Sarapammon salutes you. We pray that you be in good health.
22    From Sarapas and Gaia.
23    Give (this) to Ammonius the branch-bearer by the temple
24    of the god, and he will give it to him.
25    To Eutyches the branch-bearer below the gateway
26    of the Sarapeum, by the great image.
