[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: The Emperor Caesar Lucius Septimius Severus Pius Pertinax Augustus Arabicus Adiabenicus Parthicus Maximus and the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Pius Augustus to Varus son of Damasaeus. If you can claim the assistance due to immature age, the praefect of the province shall decide the suit for release. Published in Alexandria... To Procunda daughter of Hermaeus through Epagathus, freedman. If you can claim the assistance due to immature age, the praefect of the province shall decide the suit for fraud. Published in Alexandria...
1     A chapter from the gnomon (regulation) of Severus and Antoninus, the lords Augusti. … …
2     Year 8, in the month Phamenoth 24. In part, thus: to minors and to those [away on public]
3     service who are absent or have been absent, the customary assistance shall belong; [and those]
4     under twenty-five years of age, if with a view to their being defrauded they were manifestly [circumvented,]
5     it is reasonable that they too obtain assistance. The things ordained concerning those not of full age shall be observed also for the cities.
6     From the petitions of the Alexandrians. Before the 13th of the Kalends of January: certain younger persons requesting that [the]
7     lands sold by the guardians remain firmly (theirs) and that they not [unlawfully be deprived]
8     of them; among other things: Caesar said: if some younger person comes [to our tribunal]
9     and says, "under compulsion, having been circumvented, I sold my lands for much [less than their worth,"]
10    shall we not help him? Why then shall we help a private person, but not help our own treasury?
11    A chapter from the Laetorian law, interpreted as far as possible …
12    … : if he was circumvented … let him petition the most excellent epistrategos.
13    Imperator Caesar Lucius Septimius Severus [Pius] Pertinax [Augustus, Arabicus, Adiabenicus,]
14    Parthicus Maximus, and Imperator Caesar Marcus [Aurelius] Antoninus [Pius Augustus,]
15    to Varus son of Damasaeus. If you can claim the assistance due to immature age, the praefect [of the province]
16    shall decide the suit for release. Published in Alexandria …
17    To Procunda daughter of Hermaeus through Epagathus, freedman. If you can claim the assistance due to immature age,
18    the praefect of the province shall decide the suit for
19    fraud. Published in Alexandria …
20    To Claudius Julianus.
