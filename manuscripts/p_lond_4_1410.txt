[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God, Korra son of Szerich, counselor, to you, those from the village
2     Aphroditopolis. Provide by account of beautification of ships and small-ship workers and courier-workers being on the island
3     Of Babylon under Abdelale son of Abi Achim being imposed on the present indiction 8, but corvée of indiction 9
4     Four artisans, four, with expense of 3 months thus: shipwrights 2, two, at 2 coins of the month
5     Carpen[ter] [one] o[ne of the] month, coin 1⅓, ca[lk]a[phat]e one, one of the month, coin 1½, and if you give cash-release, [provide]
6     For wages and expense of them as it appears above only. It was written in month Hathur 6, in[d]iction [8]
7     Totaling shipwrights 2, coins 12, carpenter 1, coins 4, caulker 1, coins 4½
