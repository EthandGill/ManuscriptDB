[TRANSLATION]
1     Year twenty-five of Autocrator Caesar
2     Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus Parthicus
3     Maximus Britannicus Maximus Germanicus Maximus
4     Pious Augustus, of the month Hadrian, Choiak, [the] seventh,
5     in Ptolemais Evergetis of the Arsinoite nome.
6     Aurelius Sotas, [son] of Apunchus, from the village
7     Soknopaios Island, conducting [affairs] in the settlement
8     Marebes of the division of Themistos, being of age forty-two, [with a] scar on the right shin, to Gaius
9     Julius Saturnilus, [son] of Gaius Julius Saturnilus
10    veteran, [the one] acknowledging [to have] from him forthwith through hand [in] silver principal seventy-two drachmae and seven-and-one-half measures of vegetable-seed, which the acknowledger shall repay in
11    the month of Pauni of the present year without delay, execution being made … from the acknowledger or also from all [his] existing [possessions] all just as from a judgment.
12    Sotas from Soknopaios Island, drachmae seventy-two and measures of vegetable-seed seven-and-one-half.
