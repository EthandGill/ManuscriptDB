[TRANSLATION]
1     Apion, nomarch, through Heron
2     [Herakles], farmer, and associates, greetings.
3     I have received from you the produce-tax
4     of which you farm the plot concerning the village
5     Theadelpheia, of the house of the city of the queen Ptolemy the New Dionysus, [for] the produce of the twelfth year, complete.
6     Year 12, [Epeiph] 22.
7     Receipt of [Herakles] and the associates.
