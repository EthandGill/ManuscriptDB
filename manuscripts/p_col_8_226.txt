[TRANSLATION]
1     Silvanus [son] of Ammonius, Hermopolite, merchant
2     To Plutio [son] of Tapous and Dioscoros
3     [of] Hadrian, both, from Alabastrine, greets [you]; I have agreed with
4     you so that I might serve as a piper together with
5     my entire salary in the aforementioned village for 8 days from the 24
6     of the next month Epeiph, of the wages
7     of each day of silver drachmae
