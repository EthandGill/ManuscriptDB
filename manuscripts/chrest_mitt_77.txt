[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Aelius Gessius, the most illustrious prefect,
2     Aurelius Kleoboulos, town-councillor of Oasis Magna, greeting.
3     I received an order from your Illustriousness, lord, the one commanding me to send over to the court
4     the heirs of Apius and also the heirs of Heraklios Domettios and …nos Septimius, or the proxies able to answer on their behalf in the court regarding the charges to be brought against them
5     by the most illustrious house of Andromachos, and immediately, holding in my breast the fear of your Greatness,
6     I pressed upon them, harassing them, and having undertaken the journey into the Inner Oasis, where they have their abodes,
7     the able proxies handed over to me by them, capable of fulfilling their place,
8     together with guarantors, in accordance with the written bonds they had set down, I sent them off through Isidoros, beneficiarius of your, my
9     lord's, office, having appended below the single copy of their written bonds so that nothing might escape
10    your most vigilant guardianship, master. I pray for your health, most illustrious prefect lord, for many years.
11    In the consulship of our masters Valens for the … time and Valentinian for the … time.
