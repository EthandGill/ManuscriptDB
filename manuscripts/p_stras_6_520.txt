[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most reverent abba John the elder and Aphout the farmer, Asclepiadis. I have received
2     from you for the harvest-tax of the named Dionysius Polydeukes on behalf of fruits of the tenth indiction
3     this is [of wheat] artabas seven and a half and of barley artabas seven and a half, [totaling] wheat 7½ and barley 7½
4     … and for your security I have made for you this
5     [satisfactory receipt] as is aforesaid, Asclepiadis having agreed [to] the
6     receipt
7     … Pauni 13 of the indiction
