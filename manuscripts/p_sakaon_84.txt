[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 14 and year 13 and year 6, Phamenoth 19.
2     To Paneiout[is] [a] priest from Thraso: I received straw
3     three hundred pounds and [the] freight charges of Memphis.
4     Through me Sarapammon[os] the administrator I have marked.
5     The Aurelii Sarapio[n] and Leontas, the receivers
6     To Aurelius Paneioutis the priest from Theadelphia, greetings.
7     We have received from you straw
8     one hundred fifty pounds, which amount to pounds 150. We have marked.
9     Year 14 and 13 and 6, Phamenoth 25.
10    28. Similarly the same, other straw
11    four hundred fifty pounds,
12    [which amount to pounds 450. We have marked].
