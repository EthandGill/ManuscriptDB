[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Paul,
2     Pous greetings. The money which
3     I undertake from Patehnumis the camel-driver, being diligent; take from them and send to me
4     of silver, talents two thousand
5     three hundred, and send to me
6     [I being] called upon.
7     Be well for many years.
