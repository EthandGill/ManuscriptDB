[TRANSLATION]
1     Heraklides with Ammonios the one concerning the registration has been summoned, year 50 Phamenoth 1….
2     Year 50 Payni 11, it has been registered with the bank in Zeus' City the Great, over which Eirenaios [presides], for the tenth of the register
3     according to the registration by Proitus, under which Apollonios the copy-clerk signs,
4     Sachperis the younger Osoreherios [of] the completion of purchase from an [h]ouse enclosed and courtyard from the north and another from the west
5     and of all adjoining [properties] of 14 cubits from the south side, the 35th [p]art
6     that is in the north part of the Hera street that leads to the river, which he purchased from Necouthes son of Hasotus in the
7     50th year 20… drachmas 300; the tax of which exchange [rate] three hundred becomes 300.
8     Eirenaios the banker.
