1     [To Apollonios] also called Ptolemaios, strategos of the Arsinoite, H[erakleides division],
2     fr[om Sabinus son of Z]osimos, from the metro[polis but residing in the v]illage of Karanis. The …
3     … of camels from …
4     Antonios came forward wishing through insolence [to defra]ud me of what my wife Aphrodite has acquired …
5     two camels; and when I did not yield, he assaulted me with very many [bl]ows, and moreover, during my absence
6     he demanded from the son of my wife, Polydeukes, who is under
7     age, two drachmas, on the ground that they are given
8     to him by everyone per camel. But also today, when I asked him for the carriage-fee for the days I had worked, from which he demanded from
9     a[ll those f]rom the village, six dr[ach]mas for each artaba, again he committed against me the utmost outrage, threatening me even to the point of (taking my) life,
10    plotting (against me). And fearing lest he carry out in deed as he said,
11    [as far as the vi]llage of Pharbaithon of this same division [I fled, an]d I submit th[is] petition [and I ask] that this be entered in the records, and that he hear me against him,
12    [so that on the] appointed (day) I may pro[v]e on the … day
13    … him (having) very many camels, after which
14    … from which his theft is manifest. Farewell.
15    [Sabinu]s son of Zosimos, I have submitted (this).
16    [Year …] … [of Aure]lius Com[modus Antonin]us Caesar the lord, P[ach]on 11.
