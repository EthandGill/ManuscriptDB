1     [Through] the letter I greet your brotherly magnificence. Since now I was in need
2     of [briefly] examining matters by reason of the helping-you church and seeking I found four cast [down]
3     [in your vi]neyards, and without order from you these things I was not able to take. But I beseech
4     the [god-guardia]ns [you] to order these things to be given to me in use, a[nd] [other things] I leave to their places
5     of them; for you know that small [things] we are not able to [put] into the roof. And may the … supplication of mine
6     [prevail] for this, so that also on behalf of this as indeed in all things I may present [to the g]od-guardi[ans] to you. The Lord Jesus the Christ
7     may guard you with whole household.
8     to my master in all things most magnificent, worthy of all honor and adoration, genuine brother Jou[stus] the count, pag-ruler of Sam[ba]s
