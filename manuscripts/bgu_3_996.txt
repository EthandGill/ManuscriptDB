[TRANSLATION]
1     Tanemieus sold,
2     and Sales, brother and sister, the (property) belonging to them,
3     a house, built [and] furnished with doors and
4     roofed, and the adjoining
5     courtyard. Bought (it)
6     Namosesis daughter of Spemminis,
7     for 2 talents of copper.
8     In the reign of Cleopatra and King Ptolemaios, the Mother-loving Savior Gods,
9     in year 11, in the priesthood of the priest of King Ptolemaios the Mother-loving Savior God, of Alexander
10    and of the Savior Gods and the Sibling Gods and the Benefactor Gods and the Father-loving Gods
11    and the Manifest Gods and the God Eupator and the God Philometor [and the God Philopator] the new and the God Euergetes
12    and the Mother-loving Savior Gods; (in the priesthood) of the sacred-victim-bearer of Isis the greatest goddess, mother of the gods,
13    of the prize-bearer of Berenike the Benefactress, of the basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphos, of the priestess of Arsinoe
14    Philopator, of those who are in Alexandria; and in Ptolemais of the Thebaid, in the (presence of the) priests
15    and priestesses and basket-bearer of those (men and women) who are in Ptolemais; in the month of Phaophi 5,
16    in Pathyris, before Heliodoros the agoranomos. There sold Sales son of Patseous, Persian of the
17    epigone, about 18 years old, honey-colored, with straight hair, long-faced, straight-nosed, and Tanemieus
18    daughter of Patseous, Persian, about 25 years old, of middle stature, honey-colored, long-faced, straight-nosed, with the guardian
19    of both, Paamenis son of Psenneis, Persian of the epigone, about 30 years old, of middle stature,
20    honey-colored, slightly stooped, round-faced, straight-nosed, the
21    share falling to them of a house, built and roofed and furnished with doors, and of the adjoining courtyard,
22    which is in the western part of Pathyris; neighbors: on the south the house of Petosiris the priest and of Nechoutes his brother; on the north the house of Patous
23    son of Herieus; on the east the house of Petes son of Pelaios; on the west the house
24    of Paous son of Pelaios, or whoever may be the neighbors on all sides.
25    There bought Namosesis daughter of Spemminis, Persian, about 30 years old, of middle stature, dark-skinned,
26    straight-nosed, for two talents of copper. The fore-sellers and guarantors
27    of all things relating to this purchase (are) Sales and Tanemieus
28    the sellers, whom Nemesesis the buyer accepted.
29    I, Heliodoros, have transacted (this).
