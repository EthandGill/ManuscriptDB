[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of the Flavii Areobindus [and Asper],
2     the most illustrious, Mecheir 1, in the 3rd indiction, in [Herakleous]
3     [ci]ty. Aurelii Pamoun son of Ploution, his mother A…,
4     and Ammonios son of I[sak], his mother Tan[a]byous, both
5     from the village of Papa Mikra of the Herakleopolite [nome],
6     farmers, to Aurelius Anouthios,
7     [from] Herakleous city, a baker, gre[eting.]
8     We acknowledge that on mutual surety we have received [from]
9     you, hand to hand, and have been paid in full the mutually agreed price of six and a half artabas of barley
10    and four and a half artabas of chickling-vetch,
11    making 6 1/2 artabas of barley and 4 1/2 artabas of chickling-vetch, which of necessity
12    we shall repay to you in the month Pauni of the fourth harvest-season,
13    of the (same) indiction, by the just tenth measure, without delay and without
14    any dispute, the right of execution belonging to you and to those
15    acting for you, both against us on mutual surety and against
16    all our possessions. This bond
17    is valid, and, having been formally questioned, we acknowledged (it). Aurelius Pamoun
18    son of Ploution and Amm[o]nios son of Isak, the aforesaid, we have received on mutual surety the
19    price of the six
20    and a half artabas of barley and four and a half
21    artabas of chickling-vetch, and we shall repay [as] is aforesaid.
22    Aurelius Paulos son of Aion wrote on behalf of
23    them, being illiterate.
24    Written through me, Annianos.
25    Bond of Pamounios son of Ploution and Ammonios son of Isak from Papa Mikra: barley 6 1/2 and chickling-vetch 4 1/2 artabas.
