[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysius to Herakleides
2     the brother, very many
3     greetings; [I judged] necessary by [letter]
4     you to greet. You know,
5     [brother], that nothing is
6     here so that I might send to you. But if
7     something else you need, send to me and I will send
8     to you either lentils or copper.
9     Greet through me …
10    Heron, and if anything to her
11    you provide, on account of me
12    to her provide.
13    Be well.
14    Greet [Chairemon].
