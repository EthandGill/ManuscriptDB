[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting from Ptolemaios, a Macedonian, of the troop of Pythangelos and of Ptolemaios
2     his son, troop-commander, cleruch. I am wronged by Polemon, a Macedonian, holder of thirty arouras, and by Aristomachos. For though I hold a kleros from you, O king, near the village Hiera Nesos, of the Herakleides
3     division, of 82½ [arouras], in the 23rd year while I was in Alexandria for a lawsuit and no one
4     of mine was on the spot, Polemon the aforesaid, having leased from the treasury the
5     half-kleros, there being no contract between me and him, sowed the whole kleros
6     in the [23rd] year, of which the crops fell to the 24th year; and in the 24th year, neither having leased the half-kleros from the treasury, nor there being any contract between them and me, the aforesaid men sowed it
7     with sesame and grain and have carried off the produce contrary to all justice, with Philon the crop-guard also conniving with them.
8     And when I came from Alexandria I demanded from
9     each of them the rents, just as in former times too I used to lease at a rent of [4½] artabas of wheat for each aroura,
10    which makes for the two years, for the one year the rent of the half-kleros 18[5½ ⅛] artabas of wheat,
11    and for the 24th year, of which the crops fell to the 25th year, the rent of the whole kleros 371¼ artabas of wheat; they do not pay, but always promising to pay they put me off. I beg you therefore, O king, to command Diophanes the strategos to write to Menellas the epistates to send them to be judged; and, if what I write is true, let each of them be made to pay me of the aforesaid amount
12    the rent falling due upon each, at a price for each aroura of 4 silver drachmas. And concerning their having sown it by force,
13    later I shall obtain justice from them. For this being done,
14    Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach [13].
15    Ptolemaios against
16    Polemon and [Aristo]machos concerning … s
