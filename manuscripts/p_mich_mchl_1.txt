1     [Gaius Julius] Niger Osirantinean [who is] also Hermaeus, as [being] age 47 on behalf of [his] son
2     [Gaius Julius] Longinus [of] ten months [and] ten days, for the month Boedromion, Pauni the 9th of the present 18th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord.
3     [witnesses]: Gaius Domitius Clemens Nervianiean [who is] also
4     Irenius,
5     [Mark] Antonius Maximus Nervianiean [who is] also Propatoriean,
6     …us Julius Heraclanianus Hadrianiean [who is] also Zeniean.

8     [Augustus Pious], of the month Boedromion, Pauni 10,
9     [under the prytaneus] Socrates [son] of Didymus.
10    …[who is] also Marcelline, I have witnessed [it].
