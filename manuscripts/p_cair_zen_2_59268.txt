1     Year 34, account of cultivation, from
2     Pyrrhos, of (the sum) from receipt.
3     You ought to have assessed of green-(crops) 949,
4     and from cucumber 15 drachmas; total 964.
5     We have from Limnaios
6     80 drachmas of silver.
7     To Pataikion, 100 drachmas of silver.
8     To Demetrios the donkey-driver, 40 drachmas of silver.
9     To Dionysios, 20 drachmas.
10    From Pyrrhos himself, 70 drachmas.
11    To Euempolos, 20 drachmas.
12    Total 330 drachmas of silver.
13    And to Philemon, 10 drachmas of bronze.
14    To Petosiris son of Seseosis, 12 drachmas of bronze.
15    To Patis, 48 drachmas.
16    To Horos son of Peteminis, 24 drachmas.
17    To Diophantos, 20 drachmas.
18    To [A]rtemon, 12 drachmas.
19    … …
20    And from me you have, of bronze, 70 drachmas,
21    and from Polykleitos, 40 drachmas of silver,
22    and which Horos brought, 40 drachmas of silver,
23    and from Zenon you bring
24    having from the bank 50 drachmas;
25    from Maron, 10 drachmas;
26    from Eutychos, 8 drachmas;
27    other (drachmas) from Eutychos, 20 drachmas;
28    other drachmas, 17;
29    for plucking, 217 drachmas.
30    Total 430 drachmas 2-obols of silver, which are 466 drachmas 1-obol of bronze.
31    325 drachmas of bronze.
32    For the same, 791 drachmas 1-obol of bronze.
33    For this you write, having expended,
34    of which each item belongs to the
35    sowing of the green-(crops), 45 drachmas 2/14 obols;
36    for the water-channels and watering, 87, 3/14 obols;
37    for the sesame sowing
