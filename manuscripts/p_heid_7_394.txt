[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dikaios, village-guard of Moithymedon, from Pasitos, royal farmer. Having come upon the sheep of Ammenneos and Ta[...] I [assessed]
2     the [property] belonging to me in the
3     Krateus plot, which is valued at drachmae 400.
4     concerning which also
5     I have delivered to you
6     the shepherds.
7     I have written therefore
8     to you so that you may know.
9     Year 8, Mecheir 20.
10    to the scribe Apollonius
