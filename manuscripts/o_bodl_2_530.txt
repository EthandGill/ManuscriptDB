[TRANSLATION]
1     Postamos, also called Horus, son of Postamos, tax-collector of cash
2     to Porgebthe, son of Pikotes. I received for

4     twelve, total drachmas 12. Year 8 of Hadrian
5     the lord, Phamenoth twenty. Kollouthes I have attested.
