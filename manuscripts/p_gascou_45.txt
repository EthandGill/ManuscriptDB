1     [From the] [lady] Victoria to the most admirable Julian, administrator. You have given from your account in the eighth indiction
2     [For the] first payment of the canon of the same eighth indiction in gold coins thirteen, carats seventeen
3     [Half], [comes to] gold coins 13, carats 17½ by [the] scale. Written Athyr 7, indiction 8. [It] comes to coins thirteen, carats ten
4     Seventeen and half from my hand [of] Julian the administrator. [The] marks of John
