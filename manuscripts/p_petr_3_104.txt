[TRANSLATION]
1     Let [the] aforementioned yield be measured off then into the royal treasury
2     Akhoapi. Of Alketas of those from [Asia] [captives]
3     Of that which was assessed into the royal treasury after the sowing of the 4th year
4     Concerning Psinarpsenes has been brought to us
5     Apollonius the contract-keeper a contract which he said
6     to have been written Alketas to Heliodorus the
7     [farmer] of the lot of the appointed wheat-yield
8     of thirty artabae and [they have signed] the customary
9     oath of so much to have been leased. Let it be measured off then
10    [into the royal] treasury the aforementioned yield
