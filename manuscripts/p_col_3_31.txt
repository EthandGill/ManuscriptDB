[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Zenon greetings. We have sent to you Midas
2     the muleteer. Use him therefore with the mules
3     and command the care of them to be exercised. Give to him
4     both the ration and the grain-allowance that has been appointed in order.
5     For the clothing he has received from us and the ration has been brought [to him] until
6     the month of Dius.
7     Farewell. Year 30, Dius 11, Athyr 1.
8     of Midas
9     Year 30, Dius 23, Athyr 23.
10    of Midas the muleteer
11    of the ration.
12    To Zenon
