[TRANSLATION]
1     Promethion to Zenon greetings. You wrote to me about the wax that
2     it is established at 1 talent with the tax at Memphis at drachmas 44, but you [say] that
3     you hear it is with us drachmas 40. Do not therefore attend to those spouting nonsense, for it is sold
4     here at drachmas 48. You will therefore do well [by] sending to us
5     as much as you are able. I have given, just as you wrote to me, to Aigyptus the [man]
6     from you for the price of the [wax] silver drachmas 500, and the remainder which there may be
7     to whomever you assign I will settle at once. And also of honey let [5]
8     measurers [be taken for me]. I praise your goodwill and the eagerness which you always have toward us.
9     And you, if you have need of anything from among our things, do not hesitate to write.
10    Farewell. Year 33, Pharmouthi 19.
11    To Zenon.
12    [regarding] the theater to build [it].
