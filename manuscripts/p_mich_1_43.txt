[TRANSLATION]
1     Nicobius to Maimachus, greetings. I have copied out to you the letter from Diotimus
2     the copy. You will do well by writing to us an account of both
3     the grain and the sesame, how much [you have measured out] into the ... ... to him,
4     so that we might send [according as] he writes. Farewell. Year 33, Thoth 2.
5     Diotimus to Nicobius, greetings. Since we have been away, nothing proper
6     has been done by the nome-officials concerning the measuring of the sesame and the grain. When you receive the
7     letter, if they do not immediately measure out the proper amount, do not refrain;
8     and it will be measured if you yourself stand by at each location and compel them.
9     Farewell. Year 33, Mesore 8.
10    To Maimachus.
