1     [To Gaius Avidius Hel]iodoros, prefect of Egypt,
2     [from] … son of …, from the village of Karanis of the Herakleides
3     [division of the Arsinoite] nome. While I am a cultivator of public and domain land,
4     … my father, lord, happens to be dying, by which
5     [will he made he set] all his property in the name of two
6     … Lucii Antistii Gemelli
7     … did, because I was in public (service) … that they keep for me my own (property)
8     … and up to now I am in possession
9     … of the revenues, of which I also paid the public dues … me by the agents of the nome … public service and the collectorship of money-taxes …
10    … I am of the village even up to today, lord
11    … happening to be … . Since therefore, lord,
12    … having been discharged from military service and … formerly to return … together with Sabinus who is with him and
13    … …
14    … of mine which I was holding for years
15    … having maltreated with very many [blows] … of my wife … a deed of obligation for one and a half talents
16    … to Sabinus now serving as a soldier … I request, lord, if it seem good to your Fortune, concerning
17    … I have (cause) to write against him to the (strategos) of the Herakleides
18    [division] … him … to me and …
19    … of the property and concerning the outrage
20    … whatever I have to say against them
21    … is able, and I myself too, through your beneficence as benefactor, … to recover my property and
22    … Antistius Gemellus … concerning
23    … having been benefited. Farewell.
