[TRANSLATION]
1     In the seventh year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
2     Imperator, in the month Kaisareios, the 25th. Having come together, the undersigned men, salt-sellers of those from Tebtynis, resolved
3     among themselves by common decision to elect one of their number, a good man,
4     to be also superintendent and collector of the public (dues), Apynchis
5     son of Orseus, for the coming eighth year of Tiberius Claudius
6     Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, the same
7     Apynchis bringing in the public (dues) of the same trade
8     all of them for the same coming year, and that all together sell the salt in the aforesaid village of Tebtynis and
9     that Orseus alone has been allotted to sell the gypsum
10    in the aforesaid village of Tebtynis and in the adjoining villages, for which he shall pay, apart from his share of the public (dues) falling to
11    him, another sixty-
12    six silver drachmas. And likewise the same Orseus has been allotted Kerkesis, to
13    sell salt alone in it, for which he shall pay likewise another eight silver drachmas, and that there has been allotted to
14    Harmiysis also called Belles son of Harmiysis, alone, the selling of
15    salt and gypsum in the village of Tristomos also called Boukolos, for
16    which he shall pay, apart from his share of the public (dues) falling to him,
17    another five silver drachmas, on condition that they sell the good salt at
18    two and a half obols, and the fine at two obols
19    and the finer at one and a half obols, by
20    our measure or that of the granary. Whoever sells for less than these, let such a one be fined to the common (fund) of silver eight drachmas and to the treasury the same amount, and if
21    [any] one of them be found to have sold to a merchant more than a stater of salt, let such a one be fined to the common (fund) of silver
22    eight drachmas and to the treasury the same amount. But if
23    the merchant intends to buy more than 4 drachmas (worth), all are under compulsion
24    to sell to him jointly. And if anyone brings gypsum and intends to sell it abroad, under compulsion it will be left in the (hands) of Orseus son of Harmiysis until he takes it
25    abroad and sells it, on condition that they drink monthly always on the 25th
26    each one one chous of beer, whether (they pay?) the full (price?), or whether …
27    … of the (rebellion?), in the village 1 drachma, but abroad 4 drachmas, but
28    [in] the metropolis 8 drachmas. And the one who acts unjustly
29    [and does] not fulfill any of the public (dues) or any of the
30    [charges] brought against them, it shall be permitted to the same Ap[ynchis to take] them as pledges, both in the broad-street and in
31    [the hous]es and in the field, and to hand them over
32    [as has been writ]ten above.
