[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Gonatas, landholder, Antoninus [sends] many greetings. First of all I pray [for]
2     your wholeness/good health before the lord God so that [you] remaining whole
3     [I] may receive you. I greet your unenviable children.
4     I wish you to know, my lord patron, that [there is] a complaint
5     for me concerning your camel-driver that he insulted us.
6     And I insulted Tithoe with words, not with a rod
7     but with words. What then does the matter have [against] me [from] Panther
8     to insult? For I kept your friendship, the
9     ancient [one], and until today I still have the
10    … friendship. If indeed you wish [you] should tear away the friendship, let it be. Know then that nothing to Panther himself
11    [I] did because of you. For not logically [does] a fugitive and base
12    man insult the more refined of him. Because of you [these] things they do to us. If I insult Tithoe logically then I also insult him and he [insults] me, since [he is] co-heir of mine
13    is. For the base [man] not logically me insults, if not
14    [you] turn [toward] him. Know then that [I] am able him
15    [I will] discipline. I because of you nothing him [I] did until today and if you come [you] are able to learn from
16    his brother. [I] pray you to be in good health for many years,
17    my lord brother.
18    For I sought [after] my own [things], not which foreign [things] we give. [I] left the matter until [you] come.
19    And if you hear your camel-drivers, no longer [will you] show friendship … with a man which no one did.
20    I say to you rather not that you hear
21    them until you come
22    to my lord brother Gonatas
23    from Antoninus
