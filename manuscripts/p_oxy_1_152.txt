r.2    ἵπ ποις τοῦ ἱπ πικοῦ μέρους Βενέτων ὑπὲρ μηνιαίων τοῦ Μ[ε]χὶρ μηνὸς ϛ ἰνδικτίονος χρυσοῦ Ἀλεξανδρείας κεράτια

[TRANSLATION]
1     Was given through the most illustrious Georgios, chartulary, to the 2 aphetes serving with the
2     Horses of the cavalry division of the Venetoi for the monthly [payment] of Mechir month 6 indiction, gold of Alexandria keratia
3     Ten and one-half eighths; comes to gold of Alexandria keratia 10 and one-half eighth only.
4     Year 294 and 263, Phamenoth 5, sixth indiction.
