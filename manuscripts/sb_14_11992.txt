[TRANSLATION]
1     [Safe]ly to Horouchos, who himself [...]
2     [When things had been] said and spoken, he made clear to Theodosios in order that he send
3     [to him] Papnouthos the from numerarius[]. May your
4     [great]mindedness be pleased to make clear whether you wish [us] to speak with him concerning your
5     brightness or not, and how we ought to speak with him. By the holy
6     [and] consubstantial trinity, neither was I present when Horouchos spoke with him,
7     nor [after] … chose Papnouthos, but he grieved me
8     [concerning things] … grieved me favorably toward Aiolus
9     [being] … indeed the most brilliant John … [to] you perhaps,
10    [having left him to speak] and concerning you, with speed the copies
11    [may] receive today.
