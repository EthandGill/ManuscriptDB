[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Sar[apion], strategos of Oxyrhynchus
2     from Aurelii Theoneineus, also Sarapion
3     having served as archiereus [and] Theon, both
4     exegetes, coun[c]illors of the city of the Oxyrhynchites.
5     and while still living our father Sarapion, Sarapion, having served as exegete of the same
6     city, and each dwelling separately by himself, and
7     of us withdrew to his own house; when he died, as heirs to us and
8     and another of our brothers Ammonius, according to the wills which he left, which also after [his] death were opened, having come to his [affairs] we discovered thefts of books and [other] …
9     not few to have occurred. Concerning which, [safeguarding] these little books, we present them for
10    their being in deposit, so that if, having made inquiry, we discover something, let remain to us
11    the claim against those appearing to have made the theft. Year 6 of the Emperors Caesars
12    of Publius Licinius Valerianus and Publius
13    Licinius Valerianus Gallienus Germanici
14    Greatest, Pious, Fortunate, [and Pu]blius
