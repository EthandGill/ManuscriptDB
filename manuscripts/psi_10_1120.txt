[TRANSLATION]
1     hundred drachmas ... [as] has been written, [let] Herakleios [pay to] Loucius and Gaius each[thing]
2     one hand of absence from bed [or from] daytime absence which if he makes one drachma of silver
3     but the proven theft or embezzlement double, and if he [is] torn away within the time or finally abandon
4     the service and the silver immediately with one and a half [times]
5     and interest from which if he makes an agreement of time two drachmas
6     per mina the month each and [a] fine of silver drachmas
7     two hundred and to the public the same. and let the collection [be for] Loucius
8     and Gaius from both Herakleios and from all his possessions
9     as if from judgment, [all being] invalid and whatever if he bring forth documents.
10    and having remained Herakleios the annual time
11    and after this departing let him be released from the twenty-four drachmas of silver and all [matters] according to this service. Valid [is] the service.
12    Herakleios as of [about] years 45 medium dark-complexioned long-faced [with a] scar under the right eyebrow.
13    Loucius as of years 25 medium dark-complexioned somewhat stern-looking
14    round-faced [with a] scar on the right shin.
15    Gaius as of years 30 taller than medium dark-complexioned long-faced somewhat stern-looking [with a] scar on the outside of the left shin.
