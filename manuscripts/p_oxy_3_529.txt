[TRANSLATION]
1   Before [all I pray that you]
2   are in health. Rec[eiv]e by K[e]r-
3   don, for Dionysios,
4   four kotylai of unguent and
5   a basket of sweetmeats
6   containing 100 figs
7   (and) 100 nuts, and half a chous
8   of oil, of which you will give the same
9   Dionysios
10   four kotylai, and to yourself two kotý-
11   lai. Greet your
12   mother and
13   Matris and her children
14   and all who love
15   you. I myself
16   am going to Koptos with the
17   prefect.
18   [Deliver] to the house of Pausanias, former
19   [scri]be of the city, to Athenarous, by Ker-
20   don.
