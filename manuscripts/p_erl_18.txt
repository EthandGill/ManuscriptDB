[TRANSLATION]
1     From the minutes of the most excellent council of the Oxyrhynchites, under …
2     as prytanis and Apollonios as gymnasiarch.
3     Year 6 of the Emperors Caesars Marcus Julius [Philippi the Augusti],
4     Phaophi 30, the council being in session.
5     The prytanis (said): I consider that nothing else is more necessary … [than the]
6     provisioning of the city and the oil-supplies. For it is on account of [this matter]
7     that I have made this request of your council, and since you provide me with the means to do everything, nothing prevents me from making the same request of [your council. And concerning]
8     the gymnasiarchs, you know that, for nine months, [they undertook] the office of food-supply
9     … seven … and in addition gymnasiarchies. But if [these men are able to undertake the conduct] of this charge, but the majority of them …
10    with your guidance, perhaps … will also undertake these things …
11    … since I am cooperating much more in today's session. For since
12    you elected (me) prytanis, in order that you might leave [to him the affairs and]
13    that he might take thought concerning the common matters and [advise] you of what is profitable,
14    I too shall … you, in whom, trusting both in mind and in fore[sight, I now]
15    set before you the things which you know and which are before your eyes. [For you yourselves know the]
16    [scarcity] of provisions and now the difficulty …
17    he was appointed for the days of the food-supply office and not …
18    16. After other matters the prytanis (said): if as a favor [you assign this to me,]
19    for it is very much to my pleasure that you also elected him [to]
20    the aforesaid duties, again I shall request of [your council] …
21    … not at all …
