[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Theonammon
2     of Paulos, ship's captain, [to] heirs
3     of Heraklius from [the] logistai of the splendid
4     and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites
5     to Aurelius Papnouthios and Dorotheos brothers, greetings. I have measured and loaded from you
6     into the aforementioned ship
7     for the produce of the 15th indiction [of] pure grain artabas
8     thirty five, making artabas 35 only, of these the dust and the shipping fees and the sack-carrying fee [paid] in full.
9     Year 47, [day] 16 Mesore 3.
10    Aurelius Theonammon the aforementioned agrees with me as aforesaid. Aurelius Helladios [son] of Iulianus
11    wrote on behalf of him [these] letters
12    not knowing [letters/writing].
