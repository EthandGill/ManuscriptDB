[TRANSLATION]
1     … we do not know the …
2     … from among them toward
3     … … …
4     … [we made clear] to them in the same
5     … the pagarchy of the Arsinoite.
6     [Immediately therefore upon your receiving] the present letter,
7     [with all speed write] to us what you have already
8     [done] [about the matter of such] fugitives as have been mentioned,
9     [of the Arsinoite, so as to turn] back those who have remained
10    in your administration, if you have at all left in it
11    any before now our envoy reaches you,
12    who must make the search for them. For you shall be
13    aware that, if he finds in it anyone from among those about whom
14    we have written to you to turn the fugitives back to their own villages,
15    you no longer have — nor do the men of your administration — any pardon
16    with us, nor even a word of excuse. And if there have escaped
17    into your administration any of the fugitives turned back
18    from it into other pagarchies, from among the
19    men sent by us on condition of turning each one back
20    to his own place, take these and send (them) with
21    security to their own villages, yoking together
22    with them from the same villages, in which they are found, men (?)
23    for their restoration, and writing about them to
24    the officer set over their pagarchy. After which also
25    send an envoy of yours who must obtain
26    the receipt for them. And if also any of those turned back
27    into your administration from other pagarchies again fled
28    into the very places where they were before this, write to us about these
29    sending immediately also someone who must take them over;
30    for these, by God's command, we hand over to him entirely.
31    And energetically and with all accuracy conduct yourself
32    in such a matter, writing to us as has been said
33    the number of the fugitives sent by you,
34    giving no pretext against yourself in this. For if there be found in
35    your administration by our envoy sent for the turning back
36    of the fugitives, from among those about whom we had written to you
37    to turn back to their own (villages), one single soul and only,
38    there shall come upon you a requital that wipes out your substance.
39    For the rest, guard yourself and those of your administration,
40    charging them with what lies in their power, that they not be found
41    responsible in the matter of the fugitives so many times mentioned.
42    Written in the month Pharmouthi 7, of the eighth indiction.
