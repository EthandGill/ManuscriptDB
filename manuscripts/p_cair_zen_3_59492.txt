[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon, greetings. Paosis the father of Horos
2     of the kontotathou of Apollonius, who having taken me by the hand gave me to you, and you said
3     that if anyone wrongs you, report it to me.
4     Know then Heraclides, the one in charge of the ten thousand, having taken me away to the prison
5     has punished me, exacting one hundred drachmae.
6     But to me there is nothing except whatever
7     Horos left to me a certain donkey and [sheep] of that one. Do not therefore overlook me
8     in the prison perishing, but if you have the ability to help me, rescue me, until I write to that one,
9     and he will approach Apollonius concerning
10    these matters.
11    Fare well.
12    To Zenon
