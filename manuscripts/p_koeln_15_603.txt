1     Metrodorus to Apollonius greetings. Since [therefore sl]ower the [matters] with respect to year 9
2     debts have been brought in, and it has been written to us [to sail] to the accounting,
3     certainly in the Tybi month [we] made haste, so that nothing … … … [come]…
4     but at the same time [as] the 4 passing, all the money will have fallen on the bank
5     and it will be reestablished in the following [journey] to Crocodilopolis, so that having conversed with you
6     we sail down. For this having occurred, also to you well… and with good will a demonstration
7     having made of the necessary thing you will obtain… … For in the respect to particulars
8     the whole things are preserved, it happens to occur. Farewell. Year 9, as the 4th, the revenues year 10, Tybi 22
9     … Year 10, Tybi 28. Metrodorus
