[TRANSLATION]
1     In the seventeenth [year] of the Emperor Caesar
2     [Marcus] Aurelius Antoninus Augustus and
3     [the Emperor C]aesar Lucius Aurelius Commodus Augustus,
4     [Armeniac]us, Medicus, Parthicus, Germanicus,
5     [Sarmaticus] Maximus, in the month Artemisios = Phamenoth,
6     [at Ptolemais] Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge [to one another: Aphr]odisia daughter of Sabinus son of Mysthes, her mother being Ammo-…, [from the m]etropolis, aged about 31 years, with a scar on the left eyebrow,
7     [with as guardian] Achillas son of Origenes son of Herakleides, his mother being
8     …, from the quarter of the Thesmophorion, aged about 52 years, lame,
9     … and her former husband Ptolemaios
10    [son of Ptolemaios] son of Sokrates, his mother being Soeris, from the village [of Karan]is, aged about 37 years, with a scar on the left knee, that they
11    had joined together the cohabitation with one another which
12    [had been established] for them … [and that] it is permitted to each of them to manage their own affairs as each may choose; and that Aphrodisia
13    [may join herself with another] husband, being free from malicious charge in every way, and that she has received back
14    [from Ptolem]aios the dowry, in gold and silver, which she brought to him, and all the parapherna,
15    [and that they shall] not bring any charge against [one an]other concerning any of the matters
16    [pertaining to the cohabit]ation;
17    [and that Ptole]maios (shall make no claim) not even concerning what Aphrodisia alone purchased, around the plain of Psenarpsenesis,
18    … a quarter aroura of olive-grove, and
19    [another, around the village of K]erkesoucha, one aroura of grain-land,
20    [one] …
21    … from the former times
22    [up to the pre]sent day, in no way.
