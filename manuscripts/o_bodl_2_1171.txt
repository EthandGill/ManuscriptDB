1     They have measured into the sacred treasury
2     Phahērios [son] of Imouthos and associates on behalf of [the] Island of Piasaka
3     of produce of year 16, wheat in taxation artabas two-hundred [and] twenty
4     [and] seven [and a] third; of barley artabas seventy-five; of croton
5     fifty [artabas]; of seed-barley artabas ten [and a] half [and a] fourth; of croton artabas ten, [those] under epistratēgia [of a] heap
6     of barley artabas three [and a] half [and a] fourth; amounts to wheat artabas 227 [and a] third; of barley artabas 75
7     of croton artabas 50; of seed-barley artabas 10 [and a] half [and a] fourth; of croton artabas 10, [those] under epistratēgia [of a] heap of barley [and a] half [and a] fourth.
8     Year 16 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Pauni 29. I wrote
9     Epōnychos, grain-scribe.
