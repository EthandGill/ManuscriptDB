10    of the fort of Memnonion, in the year of the Saracens 132 and the year of Diocletian 466.
11    The matter is in (my) heart, that I should make the return which everyone owes, according to
12    the decree which God ordained against our father
13    Adam from the beginning, namely: Adam, you are earth, you shall return to the earth;
14    and the psalmist David said: I am a sojourner upon
15    the earth like all my fathers; I too have looked, in a reflection
16    of mankind, that it is right for me to make clear my whole affair while I am yet alive,
17    my reasoning being established, my mind firm, having come to the wish
18    of a testament, free of any guile or fear or violence
19    or deceit, with no compulsion laid upon me, but out of my own purpose and my willingness of heart, I,
20    Tbasbes daughter of the blessed Apa Biktor, my mother
21    being the blessed Thabronia, in this single fort of Djeme and
22    in this single nome of Hermont; and afterward the subscription of the one who shall subscribe on my behalf, with other trustworthy witnesses, these who shall testify
23    on my behalf to this wish of a testament while I am yet alive, that I shall be lord
24    of that which is mine according to the just laws. But if it happen, as happens to all men, that it happen to me too, so that I leave this world
25    behind me to go to my fathers, I wish and I am persuaded
26    that my father Shenoute come in and become lord over all the things which I shall arrange
27    for him, according to how I shall reveal the portion of each one,
28    in this way: my portion of the church which I possess, on behalf of my father
29    Apa Biktor — that of my father is Shenoute, with Pesynte his son,
30    that is eighteen months, on behalf of my father Apa Biktor, that they may become
31    lords over them and administer them in the fear of God; the portion
32    likewise on behalf of my mother Thebroneia — that of Trempou is in the church and the house too,
33    in which Trempou is — a third again of the house in which I am, that of my sister daughter of Leontios, because she bought it, she gave its price to me and to my mother Thebroneia,
34    and she herself drew up its deed. When she perished, these further seven months and
35    ten days, in the church, are Trempou's, with Stephanos who is her scribe, while Shenoute shall take the house in which I am — except a third — which my sister of Leontios
36    bought, giving its price to me and to my mother, taking it back; and Shenoute shall give
37    twelve years of offering on my behalf, the share which I lay up in
38    the body — namely a redemption, a share — in three shares, those of Shenoute, while
39    the abbot shall give them as offering on my behalf for the six months;
40    the rent of my whole house, which is one female pig and a pair, with two widow-(rooms?)
41    and two storerooms; simply, all that is in my whole house belongs to God; and whoever takes them shall give them as offering on my behalf. This solidus which I gave to Biktor
42    son of David for wine — Shenoute shall take it and give it as offering on my behalf.
43    I lay up three keratia for Damianos at Aplo; he shall give them for wine as offering; and that this testament of mine may become valid and having force in every
44    place where they shall produce it; that no man, whether an heir of mine
45    or any other man whatsoever, shall be able for all time
46    coming after me to come out and play tyrant, or to wish to set aside any
47    word that is written in this testament. Whoever shall dare, for all time forever,
48    coming after me to come out, wishing to set aside any word that is written in this testament — first, those persons shall profit nothing, nor shall the matter benefit them, but the first decree which shall come to pass shall be that he is a stranger to the holy oath of the Christians, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
49    and afterward he shall pay to the account of the penalty three ounces
50    of gold, and he shall come in subject to this testament of mine which I have set down,
51    immovable and unshaken by any authority and any power.
52    But whoever shall wish, or seek to destroy, any word that is written in it,
53    he shall come to the judgment-seat of God, that I may go to judgment with him, because this is what she did to me; she neglected me; I learned that it was in her power; I set it down, complete,
54    according to every (proper) procedure.
55    I, Petros son of Sonchem, give witness, to the request of Tbasbes.
56    I, Dimetrios son of Pmei, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know how, and I give witness.
57    I, Dioskoros son of Kosma, give witness.
58    I, Joannake son of Kosma, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know how.
59    I, David son of Pesynthe, give witness. I, Petros (son of) Antoni, was requested by David; I wrote on his behalf.
60    I, Isaak son of the blessed Zacharias, in this single fort of Djeme, was requested by Tbasbes; I drew up this testament with my hand.
