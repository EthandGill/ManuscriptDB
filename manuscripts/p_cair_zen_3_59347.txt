[TRANSLATION]
1     [Eudemos son of Euthygenes testifies against Antipatros, [being] thirty-five [years old], dark-complexioned, bald, Nilotic, [of] the ancient cavalry unit, cavalry-commander.
2     … [Mem]phis. And in the second year in the month of Loos [regarding] Antipatros to whom I testify
3     … [Ni]kon and [the one] saying that his son was confined by him [and was being] held [together]
4     … Nikon denied/decided, saying he had received the boy from no one
5     … [nor] had confined [nor does he] have the bo[y] by any contrivance whatsoever.
6     … [to] Antipatros against Ni… [of Eudemos]
7     … [year 2] Hyperberetaios 25.
