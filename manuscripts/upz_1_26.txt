[TRANSLATION]
1     Mennides to Theon, greetings. [Regarding] the petition presented to the king
2     by Thauetes and Tautus, the twins in the great Serapeum,
3     having been sent [to] Asclepiades the
4     chief-bodyguard and dioiketes, together with the previously separated
5     decision, and from him having been delivered to Sarapion of the
6     successors and subdioiketes with signature; the copy is placed below. Since therefore Dorian, the copyist, having taken this, brought up to Sarapion the
7     previously separated report, and Ptolemaios, [the] one standing over the twins,
8     submitted to Sarapion concerning these matters the appointed memorandum,
9     which has also been sent to us having a signature: Mennides. Having examined
10    as much as it behooves to deliver, to Demetrios, son of Sosius, a Cretan, previously [under] Eumalos,
11    the one appointed by them through the petition so as to them [to] advance
12    [the sum] due in the 18th year: sesame oil one metretes, castor-oil one metretes,
13    and in the 19th year sesame oil one metretes, castor-oil one metretes, totaling [oil] two metretae,
14    castor-oil two metretae, in exchange for which sesame oil one metretes, and all the
15    sesame oil three metretae, and [he should] make records as is proper.
16    Year 20, Athur 17.
17    Sarapion the subdioiketes signed [this]:
18    How was it possible in the 18th year to receive [the amounts due in] the 19th year,
19    I ask.
20    Year 20, Tybi 17.
21    … months to Deme[trios] [is to be] advanced
