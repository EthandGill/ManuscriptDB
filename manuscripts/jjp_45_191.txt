[TRANSLATION]
1     Zoilus scribe of-farmers Akoussilas scribe-of-grain-supply greetings.
2     Measure Herak … [to which]
3     he-farms royal land of-wheat one [and a-half],
4     [these amount-to] wheat one-and-a-half. Year 20, Hathyr 16.
5     Marres village-scribe; [me]asure [the]
6     aforesaid wheat one [and a-half]
7     [these amount-to] wheat artabas one-and-a-half. Year 20, Hathyr [16].
8     Onnōphris leader of-farmers; authenticate artabas one-and-a-half,
9     [these amount-to] artabas one-and-a-half. Year 20, Hathyr 16.
