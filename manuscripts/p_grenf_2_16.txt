[TRANSLATION]
1     In year 35, Phaophi 11, in Pathyris, under Dioscurides the agoranomus,
2     [they] willingly drew up [the document]. Patous Patouts acknowledges
3     and Takmeis Patouts [that they] have sold
4     the sycamore-fig tree that belongs to them
5     to Zmenous Psemmonthos [for] 1 talent 4 [obol] of bronze, each [at]
6     5 [obols] of bronze, and Patoun will owe
7     [to Takme]out 5 [obols], which [he will] hand over
8     in the month of Pachon of the same year. If [not]
9     [he] pays [it], [he] shall pay the one-and-a-half-fold immediately.
10    Patouts and Takmeous.
