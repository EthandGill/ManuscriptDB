[TRANSLATION]
1     Sanmous, collector. Petorzmetis of Peteuris has cleared [the debt] for the dike-tax
2     of the river-guard [tax] in the 3rd year of Hadrian
3     the lord, in silver, two drachmas, [which] amount to 2 drachmas
4     and for the 2nd year, [an additional] three-obol [coin]. Choiak, 27th [day].
5     Sarapion, scribe, [I] have written.
