[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master,
2     greatest benefactor Flavius Tiberius Mauricius,
3     the eternal Augustus and Emperor, year 6, in the consulship
4     of the same most pious master of ours, year 5, Tubi 2,
5     of the sixth indiction.
6     To the most eminent successors of the one of glorious
7     memory Apion, the former patrician of highest rank,
8     landholders here too in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
9     through Menas the house-servant who asks and
10    secures for their own masters, the same
11    all-praiseworthy persons, the right of action and liability:
12    Aurelius Phoibammon son of Papnouthios, his mother
13    being Maura, originating from the hamlet of Neos in the
14    Oxyrhynchite nome, belonging to your
15    eminence, a registered cultivator of it,
16    greeting. Since need has now arisen, the
17    estate-water-wheel under me called "Of the Fields,"
18    drawing water for those same fields, of one axle,
19    having gone up to the city, I requested your
20    eminence to command that the axle
21    be provided to me, and at once your eminence,
22    making provision for the maintenance of
23    her own affairs, this (axle) having been purchased by me
24    in the country, and reckoned to my account the price for it
25    in the receipts — a new, suitable, water-drawing axle,
26    pleasing — I received it for the replacement of all the
27    machine-implements on this present day, which is
28    Tubi the second of the present sixth indiction, for the water-supply
29    of the crops of the seventh tax-assessment, with God's help, and the same axle
30    serving the irrigations for a five-year period, and the
31    old one having been given to the doorkeeper, and as proof of the receipt
32    I have made this handwritten document, which being authoritative, written in single copy, and on being asked
33    I acknowledged. Aurelius Phoibammon son of Papnouthios — this handwritten document is satisfactory to me
34    as aforesaid. I, Papnouthios, wrote on his behalf since he is illiterate. Total: one axle only.
35    Handwritten receipt of Phoibammon son of Papnouthios from the hamlet of Neos for the receipt of one axle.
