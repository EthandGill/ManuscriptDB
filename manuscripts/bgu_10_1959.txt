[TRANSLATION]
1     [acting in accordance with the decr]ee. And let this contract
2     [be valid wherever it is produced. Witnesses.]
3     [In the reign] of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Ber[enike]
4     [the Beneficent G]ods, in the eighth year, in the priesthood of Ptolemy s[on of Ptolem]y son of Stasikrates of Alexander and of the [Savior] Gods and of the Sibling Gods and of the Beneficent Gods and of the F[ather-loving] Gods, the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos being Arsinoe d[aughter of So]sibios, in the month Hyperberetaios, at Tholthis of the Oxyrhynchite
5     nome. Nomos, [Mace]donian, holder of thirty arouras, c[leruch] of those not yet under a commander, acknowledges that he has from Aristolochos [son of Stra]tios, Thracian of the epigone, … of wheat artabas
6     for the rents of his own allotment for the tenth year, [according to the]
7     contract of the lease of the allotment which [is deposited with]
8     the contract-keeper Ptolemaios the Bithynian, [holder of seventy arouras, of those of Ni]kodemos. And let [Nom]os accept from Arist[olochos this grain for the] rents [of the elevent]h year. But if he does not [accept] (it) but Aris[tolochos] is made to pay this grain [by Nomos]
9     or by any other person in whatever manner, or some royal [hindrance]
10    arises so that this grain is not accepted by Aristolochos
11    on behalf of Nomos, let Nom[os] pay back to [Aristo]lo[ch]os the [price of the] wheat-[artaba], for each, whether it is exacted or not accepted from him, ten drachmas, and the
12    right of execution shall belong to Aristolochos against Nomos, acting in accordance with the [decree]. And let this contract be valid wherever it is produced. Wit[n]esses.
13    Diokles son of Hippolysos, Cyrenaean of the epigone
