[TRANSLATION]
1     … and I know
2     … him demand… … of him not… ancient or being… [which he sent back] … it likewise [they] vegetable-cultivate. We demand him;
3     Write to your brother.
4     But I sent you Serapas for the sake of copper.
5     You did not send for the necessity
6     For each one hangs over [me]
7     of them being insulted
8     of legionaries gave … flee …
9     … hours when he brought you other citrons four become forty-two. To fare well you I pray
