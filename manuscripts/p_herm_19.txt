[TRANSLATION]
1     … to [Apollo]n [and Theogn]ostos … … hand …
2     … to give them my allotment intact, of fifteen liturgies
3     [of arouras?, having in my breast t]he fear of the court, I gave; and certain men, after the allotment,
4     [not having been found ] … of my lord, the one who held office before, and by the most illustrious defensor
5     … to give a [pled]ge in place of those who were released, I gave, and those whom I did not find
6     … I gave; but a certain Theodoros, a lessee of the public accounting-office,
7     … [the] liturgist [who had been allott]ed and having collected wages from him, that is:
8     … of his [li]turgy and a bond from me for the security of the liturgist
9     [I issued ] … him … released him and he took refuge in the protection
10    … [wi]shing to demand from me wages on behalf of the allotted liturgist
11    … I have suffered and, being seized by illness for a whole eight months
12    … he knows, and all my very great poverty. For this reason I take refuge
13    … [to your spl]endour, most humane of judges; have pity on me, a man of modest means,
14    … [Ap]ollon the riparius and Theognostos the magistrates, that they may help
15    … not to be harassed by the stationarius on behalf of the allotted liturgist
16    … so that I may enjoy this benefaction. I shall give thanks for
17    [ever ] … may it befall you, lord.
18    [In the consulship of our master Arkadio]s eternal Augustus, for the 2nd time, and of Rufinus the most illustrious comes and magister
19    … [Ph]aophi 9. I, Aurelius Honoratos, have submitted (this).
