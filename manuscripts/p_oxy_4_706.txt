[TRANSLATION]
1     … among Egyptians …
2     [the freed persons] to their masters, and Hera[c]lides
3     … [to have receive]d from him money and [to have written a hand]writing concerning having nothing …
4     [toward him, and] when Loups read the handwriting
5     [having deliberate]d with his friends declared thus:
6     [In the laws of] Egyptians nothing concerning
7     … [and t]he authority of those who freed
8     … in accordance with the civic laws
9     [I command Damar]ion to Heraclides his master
10    … [in] accordance with law. And he said to Damarion:
11    … and I add, if he reproach you
12    … [I will command you to be] beaten with sticks.
