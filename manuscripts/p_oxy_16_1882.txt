[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Herm[i]as, scholasticus and defensor of the splendid [city of the Oxyrhynchites.]
2     On this present day, which is the sec[on]d of the … [of the] … [indiction,]
3     in my presence Theophanios, the reverend sin[g]ularis of the great]
4     Augustalian office, detained Eustochio[s …, according to]
5     a register made by Koilos, the agent …
6     on behalf of Maximos and Iulianos, the respectable tr[actatores,] [and] …
7     and the register of the said Eustochios was demanded [by Theophanios, the reverend]
8     singularis of the great Augustalian office, [of gold pounds]
9     ten, in accordance with the registe[r] given to him. [And at the same time there appeared]
10    Petros, son of Prozokios of blessed memory, … [of the]
11    [d]evoted clibanarii, and Am[m]onianos …
12    of the praesidial office, [who also] gave surety for him, [the said Eustochios,]
13    who is sought, to deliver (him) by the thirti[eth] of the present [month]
14    both on a non-business and on a business day, or to pay down the ten pounds of gold.
15    And since Theophanios the reverend man requ[ested a document of the things]
16    that had been resolved through the sealed instrument, [this attestation]
17    I have made with my own signature and issued as [aforesaid.]
