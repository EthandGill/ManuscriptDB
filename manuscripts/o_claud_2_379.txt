[TRANSLATION]
1     Julius Apolinaris
2     curator of the Tiberian [Pamos], to the curator of the Claudian
3     greetings.
4     Know that of the two soldiers
5     a lot I did not cast, since they [are] not registered here. If [you]
6     know that they are registered here,
7     you shall cast a lot for them
8     and you shall send one of them and
9     you shall report [to] me.
10    Farewell.
