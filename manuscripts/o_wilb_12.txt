1     Archybios, also called Didymos, and Eutychos, son of Eutychos, lessees of the sacred
2     gate of Soene, through me Petorzmethios of Serenos
3     helper. Pasenis, son of Pasenes, mother Senps[a]nsnōtos, paid for
4     p[o]ll-tax of year 10, drachmas four
5     obols two. Year 10, Paoni 5.
6     [Likewise], Pachon, for poll-tax
7     [of the same year dra]chmas twelve.
