[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God the almighty
2     It was written in the month of Tybi, [day] first, of indiction 6
3     We, Theophilus, son of Helias, and Suerus, son of Helias
4     men of the village of the holy one of … truly they are dwelling in the village in
5     [the] village of Hermonthis, these which give testimony of the [person who acts as] secretary
6     who acts as secretary concerning them and [they] request free men who will testify through their request
7     and their authority, they will testify truly for the right of the monastery [which is] holy, of [the] holy father Phobammōn
8     of the village on earth. Through you yourself Sōros [the] deacon and
9     the one presiding and through anyone who might manage on your behalf
10    in this monastery forever we will confess in our desire
