[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 5, Phaophi 3, in Pathyris under Heliodorus the agoranomos.
2     [loaned] Naomses Spemminio of Persian [status]
3     to [Thaesis] Harsiesis of Persian [status] wheat artabas ten without [interest].
4     And let [she who has been lent to] repay this loan.
5     Thaesis to Naomses in the month of Pachon of year 5 [wheat]
6     [new] clean restored into the house
7     to her with her own expenses by measure
8     by which she also received [it]. If she does not repay in the appointed time,
9     let [her] repay immediately
10    [the hemiolion] that will be in the marketplace
11    price, but let the right of recovery be to Naomses from
12    the one lent-to, Thaesis, and from her property, exacting as if from a legal proceeding.
13    [Repayment] year 5, [loan] of Thaesis wheat ten.
