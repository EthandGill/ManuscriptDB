[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our master Flavius Anastasius the eternal Augustus
2     and Flavius Rufus the most glorious, Pauni 23 of the 15th indiction, in Herakleopolis.
3     To the household of Apion the most glorious and most exalted, through
4     Apollos the logistes, Flavius Iakob, singularius of the governor's office,
5     son of Panneous, an Arsinoite, greeting. I acknowledge, swearing by God
6     almighty and by the victory and piety of the gloriously-triumphant
7     crown (i.e. the emperor), willingly and of my own choice, to go surety and undertake
8     for the staying and presence of Aurelius Apa Sion, son of Ioannes,
9     ostiary and gardener [of the] govern[o]r's lodging-house,
10    that he shall remain unfa[il]ingly and irreproachably and
11    serve in the p[ub]lic duties and the
12    governor's command[s], in no way being absent, for all the time of the logistia of the aforesaid household. But if he himself should default and, when sought,
13    I do not produce (him) and hand (him) over in a public p[lace of] this
14    city, outside holy precincts and di[v]ine sanctuaries
15    and every other refuge of any kind whatever — wherefore I have also received (him) —
16    I, his gua[ran]tor, the aforewritten, shall be liable
17    to make the answers on his behalf, all my property
18    being pledged for this. The surety is valid, and on being questioned I acknowledged.
19    [Flavius] Iakob, singularius, son of Panneous, the afore[writte]n, I go surety for Aurelius Apa Sion son of Ioannes,
20    ostiary and gardener of the governor's lodging-house, as is set forth above. A[ur]elius Papnouthis son of Phoibammon, private-scribe, from Herakleopolis, on being requested,
21    I wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
22    Through me, Epiphanios, contract-writer,
23    it was written.
24    Surety of Iakob son of Panneous, singularius, for Apa Sio[n], ostiary and gardener.
