[TRANSLATION]
1     Year nine ... [acknowledges] [Sarapion]
2     to have handed over Kolylos
3     of the shepherds from Muches,
4     of whom he has the value,
5     from the bank, through Onnophrios of the one from Choapis, of wool
6     one talent, it comes to talent one.
7     [Sarapion] acknowledges to have received me through [Onnophrios]
8     of the one from Choapis the [talent]
9     [one] of wool, it comes to talent one.
