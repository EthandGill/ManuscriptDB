[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aur]elii Hermodidymos son of Anoubion son of Agathos Daimon also called Epimachos, and [Tithoetion also called Sarapammon]
2     son of Herminos also called Achilleus son of Achilleus, both Hermopolites registered in [the Phrourion-Libos quarter, by their mother Eu]daimonis also called Phintys daughter of Demetrios, to each other, greeting. We acknow[ledge that we have exchanged in counter-exchange]
3     with each other henceforth, validly and irrevocably and without right of repentance, [for all ti]me, namely Aurelius Hermodidymos (gives) the dwelling and use granted to him [by written security]
4     of the place called Cheimerinon in the gateway, and of the [inner store-room of the] paternal [house]
5     of Tithoetion in Hermou polis, in the East-of-the-City quarter, in a lane called [Kalathoplokon, the whole of which]
6     the ownership and dwelling-right reverts to the same Tithoetion; while Tithoe[tion also called Sarapam]mon (gives) the sixth share belonging to him of a house and the joint-shares and ap[purtenances and all that pertains] and entrances and exits in the same quarter, opposite, which has come [to him, Ti]thoetion, by inheritance from their common mother according to (his) joint-tenancy with [another sixth share of]
7     Hermodidymos; and that henceforth each take in exchange and use and [administer and manage]
8     that for which the exchange was made, as has been declared above, for all time, an[d guaranteeing throughout];
9     and Hermodidymos has received from Tithoetion, for (equalizing the) choice, [two hundred] silver drachmas [from hand to hand, and] it shall not be permitted to any of us to transgress this counter-exchange on [no just] pretext [whatever] for all time, because we have so agreed upon these things. The coun[ter-exchange (is) valid, which] we have written in quadruplicate so as to be in duplicate with each party. Year [25 of Imperator Caesar]
10    Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus, Parthicus Maximus, Britannicus Maxim[us, Germanicus Maximus,]
11    Pius Augustus, Tybi 19. I, Aurelius Hermodidymos son of Anoubion, have counter-ex[changed and consent to all that is]
12    afore[wri]tten.
13    I, Aurelius Hermodidymos son of Anoubion, consent to the future public registration of the security.
