1     Apollonios to Apollonios [his] brother, greetings. If you are well and other things [are going] rightly with you
2     [it] would be as I wish and I myself [am] well. And during my absence I have been concerned about you [regarding] what is useful [for] your affairs, which I have not been able to explain to you through the letter because [of]
