[TRANSLATION]
1     To Herakleides the vine-dresser.
2     The contract-price of the 37 1/2 arouras is:
3     375 drachmas
4     and of the self-grown onions which
5     he himself bought: 90 drachmas
6     of the roses in the orchard
7     likewise of the 33rd year: 60 drachmas
8     of figs and pomegranates which are in the
9     sandy land of the same year: 20 drachmas
10    and of 150 artabas of wheat which he measured out
11    from the granary, which [he] must
12    [r]epay; but until [the re][payment] we reckon it as, at five obols per artaba, [making 125 drachmas]
13    but if it should be valued at more or less
14    it shall be to his (account)
15    a[nd] he has received an advance for the
16    25 [arouras] which he additionally planted
17    in the 33rd year, 40 drachmas, of which it falls to him in Phaophi to repay 20 drachmas
18    and (the amounts) which he further owes from the account
19    of the vegetable-garden of the 32nd year: 22 drachmas, 2 obols
20    and to Agathon and Menandros
21    likewise of the 33rd year: 10 drachmas, 5 obols
22    and to Stotoetis according to a contract
23    of the same year, of leeks which
24    he bought from him: 28 drachmas, 4 obols
25    Total: 751 drachmas, 5 obols
26    From these is subtracted
27    what he must receive as wages for the
28    second year, performing all
29    the works: 211 drachmas, 3 obols
