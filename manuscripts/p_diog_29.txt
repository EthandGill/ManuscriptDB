[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelia Kopreia daughter of Ammonarion, a minor, through Aurelius Sarapion son of Pasis,
2     from Aurelius Longinus son of Herminos. I wish
3     to lease from you for three years from
4     the present 5th year the (lands) belonging
5     to the minor near the village of Philadelphia,
6     eight and a half arouras of grain-land, or however many they may be, in the place of Apatisia(?), at a rent — for the first year, unsown,
7     of fourteen
8     and a third and a twenty-fourth artabas of wheat; and for the
9     remaining two years, per year, together with the (artabas) of seed-wheat which I shall receive,
10    eight and a half, of the whole, per year,
11    forty
12    two and a half artabas of wheat, by the eighth-measure of Kalomallos, exempt from deduction and free of risk, the
13    works all, and the public transport-charges,
14    being for me, the lessee;
15    and I shall deliver the yearly rents at the threshing-floor in the month Pauni, the transport-charges being
16    for the landowner; and after the term
17    I shall hand over the half of the arouras
18    from fallow, and the remaining half
19    from stubble. On being questioned I acknowledge. Longinus son of Herminos, I have lease[d]
20    as is set forth above. Dioskoros, notary, I wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
21    Year 5 of the Emperor Caesar Marcus
22    Aurelius Severus Alexander
23    Pius Felix Augustus, Hathyr 1.
