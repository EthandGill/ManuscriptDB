1     Measurement of the treasury of villages of the produce of the 23rd year
2     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Epeiph 30
3     on behalf of South, from Psaios, Pathraeus, through [a] farmer
4     Onnophrios Pamonthos, of wheat, three artabes, becomes three artabes of wheat
5     and on behalf of Charakos, named Onnophrios Pamonthos
6     of wheat, two artabes, becomes two artabes of wheat. A… I have marked.
