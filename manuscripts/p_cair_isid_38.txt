[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Year 2 of our lord Lucius Domitius Domitianus Augustus, Hathyr 11. We, Aurelius Severinus, senator of Alexandria; Aurelius Sarmates, formerly gymnasiarch; Aurelius Andrias; Aurelius Philadelpus; and Aurelius Sabinus, formerly gymnasiarch, dekaprotoi of the 4th and 5th toparchy of the Heraclides division, have received at the granary of the village of Karanis for the horiodeiktia of Kerkesoucha, for the crop of the 1st year in the name of Sarapion, son of Onnophris, acting through Isidorus ... fourteen artabas, and for the sowing ...twenty-eight artabas, equal in all 42 art, and the obols and the diagraphai. (2nd hand) I, Aurelius Philadelphus, have signed the receipt. (3rd hand) I, Andrias, have signed the receipt. (4th hand) I, Aurelius Sabinus, have signed the receipt.
