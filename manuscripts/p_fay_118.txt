[TRANSLATION]
1   […] go to Diony-
2   sias, to Psiathas the sowing-overseer,
3   until you water the olive-grove there; and buy
4   for us, for the sending to those at the fes-
5   tival to whom we are accustomed to send, espe-
6   cially the strategoi. Two days ahead buy
7   the little birds for the feast, and
8   send them; and the rest send
9   to the city with one carrying the sacks, since
10   the beasts are about to cart dung at Psennoph-
11   ris — with him bringing the slings(?) and little sie-
12   ves(?), as if for wood-cutting. I am putting six arouras
13   into Psennophris. If the beasts come up,
14   load them with cabbage and wood.
15   So do not do otherwise. Stay there until you water
16   the seven-aroura piece of the olive-grov[e]. Greet
17   all who love you in truth.
18   Farewell. Year 14 of Tra[j]an Caesar the lord,
19   Hathyr 10.
