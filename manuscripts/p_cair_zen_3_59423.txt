1     Dionysius to Artemidorus
2     greetings. I have been entrusted
3     to Criton to bring down for you
4     purple-bordered couches two, of which one [is worth] fifty minas, [the] other
5     forty minas. And we have also added
6     also the old couch, which having unraveled from the beginning we wove
7     having inserted warp new of double-weft.
