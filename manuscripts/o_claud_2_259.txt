[TRANSLATION]
1     Titianus to Serapammon
2     and to Ammonianos many greetings.
3     The obeisance of you I make before the lady Isis.
4     Titianus to Maximos the most beloved, very many greetings. Let it be of concern to you concerning the things which I commanded you. Titianus to Alexas
5     and to Orsenouphis and to Marinos and to Leon the [most beloved] ones, greetings. The obeisance of you I make before the lady
6     Isis.
7     I greet Asclepiades.
8     to fare well you
9     I pray.
