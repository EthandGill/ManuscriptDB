[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Justinian the all-praiseworthy,
2     Pachon 29, at the beginning of the 15th indiction.
3     To Flavius Ioannes son of Kolluthos, the devoted,
4     one of the draconarii of the corps of the most noble
5     Moors stationed at (the territory of) the Hermopolitans,
6     from the Aurelii Apollos son of Ares, his mother being Tieus, and
7     Ioannes son of Iakybios, his mother being Tamounis, and Lias son of Kolluthos,
8     his mother being Eus, the three farmers, from the village of Tarouthis
9     of the Hermopolite nome. We acknowledge, being mutual sureties and mutual guarantors, voluntarily and of our own free will,
10    that we have leased from you for a five-year period,
11    reckoned from the crops of the, God willing, coming
12    first indiction, the half (and) quarter of an aroura belonging to you,
13    full by the just palm-measure, of ammata sixty-four
14    each aroura, together with the share falling (to it)
15    of the cistern there and every right, from
16    … of our eastern farmland situated
17    in the plain of the same village, Ekbyth…ra, and to the east of our village, for sowing and planting
18    of crops whichever we may choose, at the rent of the same half (and)
19    [quarter aroura each year, whether flooded] and unflooded,
20    Lease of Apollos and Ioannes and Lias, 15th indiction.
