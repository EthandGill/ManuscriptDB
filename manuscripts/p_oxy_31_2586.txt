[TRANSLATION]
1     There acknowledge to [one another the Aur]elii Hermias son of Ptol[ema]i[os, ex-]market-officer of the [O]xyr[hynchite]
2     city, and Dioskoros son of Ath[enodoros]
3     from the same city, [linen]-weaver, on the one hand Hermias that he has handed over
4     to the master his own s[o]n
5     for the learning of the said craft for a period of years
6     five from the present mo[nt]h Th[o]th
7     of the present 12th year, on which terms the father shall furnish the
8     boy to the master each day from the rising
9     of the sun until its setting, fed and clothed by the
10    father, performing all that shall be entrusted to him by the master pertaining to the said
11    craft, and for the first six months for the sake of teaching without wage, but for
12    the remaining time receiving as wage, in the first
13    six-month period, daily two obols
14    and for another year daily six obols and for another year daily ten obols and for another year
15    two drachmas and for the final year daily
16    two drachmas, four obols. And the same Hermias acknowledges that he has on the spot
17    in advance from Dioskoros four hundred drachmas of silver, which
18    he shall repay at the end of the period.
19    And for whatever days the boy is idle in the period in which he receives wage, or — which may it not
20    be — falls sick, of these an equal number
21    he shall remain with the master working without wage. And the boy shall receive
22    on account of festival idle-days: Tybi
23    Pachon, at the Amesysia, seven days,
24    at the Sarapia, two days. For they consent on the[se] terms, and each thing
25    the same master shall do, and at
26    the end of the period let him hand over the boy
27    fully working the said craft as he himself
28    knows it, it not being permitted to any of the
29    parties acknowledging to transgress the
30    aforesaid terms. The acknowledgment is valid, and having questioned one another
31    they assented. Year 12 of Imperator
32    Caesar Publius Licinius
33    Gallienus Germanicus Maximus
34    Pius Felix Augustus,
35    Thoth 18. I, Aurelius Dioskoros
36    son of Athenodoros, consent as
37    is set out above, and on being questioned I assented.
