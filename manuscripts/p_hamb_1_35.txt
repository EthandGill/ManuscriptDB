[TRANSLATION]
1     To Hierax, strategus of the Arsinoan [division of] Herakleidos' portion,
2     Valerios and Sarapion and Gebinas and
3     the rest, whom you ordered to be assigned to [manage] the village Philadelphia, [greetings] to the lord.
4     [this] village being [not] easy
5     in [small] part and there being great need for us
6     of assistance both from the majority of those of the
7     vill[age] leaders not persisting in the collection, especially
8     [the] field-guards, we request
9     that they undergo examination by you,
10    so that the collection too
11    m[ight] become more diligent.
12    We pray that you fare well.
13    [To Hier]ax, strategus of the Arsinoan [Herakleidos' p]ortion.
