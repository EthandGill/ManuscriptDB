[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius, collector of bar[ley],
2     to Paminis, son of Artemis[ius], and
3     Kerybos, [son] of Nikaios, from Isidion Mountain,
4     greetings.
5     I have received from you up to now for provisions
6     of the 15th year, indiction, up to the
7     peasant-proprietor's assembly grain of own
8     artabas fifteen and [a] half,
9     [of] barley artaba one [and] two-thirds,
10    [they] make wheat artabas 15 and [a] half, barley artaba 1 two-thirds, chaff artaba one.
11    Apollonius through me … ou
12    have marked.
