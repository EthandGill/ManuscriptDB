[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sabinus, former gymnasiarch, and partners, public bankers.
2     We the undersigned cattle-raisers of the village of Oros
3     of Kerkesoucha, of the division of Herakleides, have received from you
4     the (sums) ordered for us by Ptolemaios the royal scribe
5     of the division of Polemon, who also administers the affairs of the strategia,
6     and by Ptolemaios the royal scribe of the division of Themistos, for the freight-charges
7     of the wheat-processing which we performed from the granaries
8     of the division of Polemon to the harbors, of the produce of the 17th year, of wheat 11 1/3 1/8 artabas
9     at the price of each artaba of 8 drachmas, the amounting
10    of silver ninety-one drachmas four obols, makes 91 drachmas 4 obols.
11    And we are: Ammonios son of Heron, of wheat 4 1/4 artabas.
12    Ammonios son of Mysthes, of wheat 1/3 artaba.
13    Tithoes son of Kyros, 40 years old, scar on the little finger of the left hand, of wheat 4 1/6 artabas
14    Hermias son of Hermias, 40 years old, scar in the middle of the forehead, of wheat 2 2/3 1/24 artabas
15    Drawn up through Marion the contract-writer on their behalf, they saying that the
16    persons drawn up do not know letters. Year 18 of the Emperor
17    Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus
18    Pius, Phamenoth 2. Ammonias son of Heron
19    I have received as stated above. Ammonios son of Mysthes, I jointly have received as stated above.
