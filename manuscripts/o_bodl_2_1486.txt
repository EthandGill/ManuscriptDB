[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of treasury of the metropolis of harvest, 1st year
2     Of Gaius Auidios Kassios
3     Caesar of the lord, Epeiph 1
4     On behalf of the South, name Panamés Hōros
5     Wheat artaba one one-quarter amounts to wheat artaba 1 one-quarter
6     And on behalf of Charas, name Psensénésios
7     Onnōphrios wheat artaba one-quarter amounts to wheat artaba one-quarter.
8     Amōnios I have marked.
