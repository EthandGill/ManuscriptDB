[TRANSLATION]
1   Kallea(s) to Sarapias his sis-
2   ter, greeting.
3   I want you to know that I have
4   arrived in Alexandria.
5   So do not be faint-hearted that I
6   mean to stay at Antinoou. I
7   came to Alexandria to wor-
8   [s]hip. So do not listen to peo-
9   ple (saying) that I mean to stay
10   here: I am coming to you
11   quickly, to the country.
12   Kallea(s) to Protous his sister,
13   greeting.
14   Do everything to look after
15   my wife until I ar-
16   rive; and tell Akoutas
17   my brother too that, if my wife
18   has need of anything, he should
19   supply her need
20   until I come — let her go seek-
21   ing nothing. Greet the children of
22   my sister.
23   I pray for your health.
24   Deli(ver) to Sarap[i]as f(rom) Kallea(s).
