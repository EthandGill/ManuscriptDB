1     The (monks) of the monastery [of father] [Agen]ius came giving thanks for your good reputation; and they came also now
2     asking her [to care for] [themselves] and the matters. And indeed I was compelled
3     to write to my [most magnificent] lord Count Ammonius. But meanwhile review the estates and [manage] them, namely appoint a person of yours
4     to do this. For I [appeal to] you by God, and especially on account of the common lord
5     the Count Ammonius. [Since] one farmer has requested two nomismata
6     to give (them) for sixteen artabas of wheat owed by him, may your excellency be willing

9     or toward the price of [reeds]; and to speak simply, look after this matter as your own.
