[TRANSLATION]
1     Sosstratos to Hegetor, greetings.
2     I have sent to the brother for the
3     sacrifice of the Theadelpheia, sacrificial animals
4     pigs 3, leading Erianouphis the helmsman of the small boat, if then [it] should be late
5     the tokens, you will do well having sent them, so that [it not be] hindered. Be strong.
