# HGV-EN: Claudius Germanus, steward of Claudius Munatianus the younger, to the sitologus of Ophis, greetings. Transfer, from the amount you hold on deposit for the aforesaid Claudius Munatianus, to Demetrius or to whomever he chooses, from the wheat-crop of the 6th year of Hadrianus Caesar the lord, thirty-four (and) a half (and) one-quarter artabas, total 34 art. Year 6 of Hadrianus Caesar the lord, month, day. (N. Gonis)
