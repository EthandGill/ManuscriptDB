[TRANSLATION]
1     In the second year of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajan
2     Augustus Germanicus, in the month Sebastos, on the 19th, in Karanis
3     of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges Apeis, son of Maron, a Persian of the epigone,
4     about twenty-four years old, with a scar on the left shin, to Herakleides,
5     … with a scar on the right knee, that he has leased to him, Herakleides,
6     for the present 2nd year of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajan
7     Augustus Germanicus, from the (land) which Apeis cultivates around Patsontis, of public arouras two, or however many they may be, around Hiereus,
8     from the southern part, one aroura by the ninety-six-cubit measuring-rope, for sowing with whatever he may choose; and that he (Apeis) has received
9     from Herakleides as advance payment the
10    price of the produce, fifty-six drachmas of silver, on the spot, by hand, out of the house; and that both Apeis himself and those acting for him shall guarantee (the land) to Herakleides and to those acting for him
11    with every guarantee, clear of both rents and taxes
12    of all kinds, all the agricultural labors
13    and irrigations being the responsibility of Herakleides; and if he transgress any of these, let him pay the damage and the expenses twofold. The subscriber for the acknowledging party
14    (is) Heron, son of Satyros, with a scar in the middle of the forehead.
