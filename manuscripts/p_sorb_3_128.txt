[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolemy, greeting, from Epistratos son of Ptolem]aios, a Herakleote of the epigone, of those inhabiting the village
2     [of Mouchis. I am being wronged by the inhabitants of the a]foredescribed village and by those who possess the houses
3     … to Ptolemaios at the threshing-floor, of the loads which … to me … … …
4     … [in the night] before the 25th of Pachon of the … [year] they set fire to the aforedescribed … … to Diophanes concerning the arson … with him after these things
5     … I submitted a petition. And Diophanes having written to Demetrios the epistates to exact (satisfaction) for me …
6     … the exaction. And Demetrios having written to Euen…is and Ptolemaios and Neoptolemos and Kalliphon and … …
7     … to obey in accordance with the ordinance. But of those who obeyed, some spoke against (it), saying that the … had earlier concerning
8     these matters written and given a token to the strategos … … by name … They say that in the presence of Diophanes they will pay me the sum falling to each for the arson of the seed … drachmas … having given information against no one by name. I ask you therefore, O king, to instruct Diophanes the strategos to write to Demetrios to send those disputing before him, so that … me by the royal oath … and if they do not submit to give a written undertaking, my father Ptolemaios has given … has been named and … a token concerning the arson; concerning
9     them, that Diophanes decide what penalty they deserve to receive, and (concerning) me, to compel … them to pay the portion falling
10    to them of the seed. For this having been done, through you, O king, I shall obtain my just claim.
11    Farewell.
12    To Demetrios. Best of all, reconcile them; but if not, send them to us so that before the appropriate
13    court they may be judged.
14    Year 3, Apellaios 22, Pharmouthi 26.
