[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity
2     Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. It was written in the month Thoth 1, indiction 5
3     I Tachel, daughter of Sophia, in the village of the nome
4     of the city Hermopolis, and being also of sound mind, I do this with me
5     of my own will, [calling for] the undersigner who will sign hereto
6     and [requesting] trustworthy witnesses who will bear witness
7     to this deed of gift that is not to be violated …
8     and not to be judged concerning it apart from [those] of the law
9     that is situated in the holy monastery of Apa Phibamon in the [the] mountain
10    [of] Keme, this which is under the subjection of the most glorious lord and … [of] the archon
11    the lord Psemmo and the steward of the holy monastery and the castellan
12    all, greetings. Since the law of God orders [that all should] exhort
13    the whole race of human beings to the fact that they remember the one who will benefit them
14    [to] be redeemed from their sins; because … and without sin
15    if not in God, this sinless one [himself]; there is no authority able to rule in time, to hinder any man who …
16    what is good for him in what he desires; for in this time that is coming
17    there came to be a boy, free in every respect. Now I Tachel, his wife, and … and free in his whole matter in every way, I desired him as …
18    into [the] holy monastery of Apa Phibamon in the [the] mountain of Keme
19    so that when God takes him to death, I will place him in the place
20    that is holy with myself, in which God would increase the boy-child who comes
21    forward; this [one] to whom I gave his name at the holy baptism, [namely] Athanasios
22    [the] reasoning that … dark … great … [of] sins, I plotted
23    against this boy-child so that I would place him [in the] place that is holy
24    in which God established [it] upon lawlessness, [and] I said, … he took
25    the boy-child forward [with] great joy, such that he did great things in
26    so that [they] destroyed it [and] every one who would look upon it [saying that] he died in
27    [with] the darkness [of] the thought of sin [and] boldness which I said, I held to
28    …, I besought the holy one in his place, [saying,] if you will beseech God, he will grant [pardon for] the transgression [of] this boy-child
29    I will place him into the place forever, according to my first confession
30    [for the] rest, truly if God wishes, he wishes concerning the boy
31    of child, he granted to him [pardon for] the transgression, I took him as mine [and] I placed him
32    into the holy place, indeed [it] came to pass [in] great joy
33    […] any man who saw him, they did [great] wonders. Therefore
34    [of] the holy place they rejoiced on account of this deed of gift. To my foster-son Athanasios, this [one] to whom I gave his name to whom I agreed
35    in that my mind and my reasoning [are] clear [and] there is no joy
36    [of] physical [involvement] with myself, but in my desire of [my] heart [and] [my] purpose, [of] mine, in me, without any compulsion, in fear, in shame, in constraint, in deception, in seizure, I confess, I renounce my son
37    Athanasios, into the holy monastery, this [one] I chose to commit him from now forever and toward any time
38    he will live with me forever, and the person who will dare
39    to come forth against this boy-child of this kind, he will fall
40    under the judgment [of] my offering at the throne of God which I made in his presence. Therefore in the holy place I have signed this deed of gift
41    may it be strong [and] firm in every place where they will present it [before] them
42    I Abbas, a deacon, and George, a lover of God, and Chael, John, these men from the village, the elders, the companions, they approved; I have subscribed to them.
