[TRANSLATION]
1     Ischyra to Paraboulos, greetings.
2     [We perform] your obeisance before the gods
3     all. I wish to know you
4     that your deed-document
5     I left half-finished
6     because I do not have a sharp iron-tool; you will do well
7     having sent me the small carving-tool
8     and the striking-tool. Greet
9     Marcus,
10    the curator
11    of you. Fare well.
