[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters Valentinian and Valens, eternal Augusti, for the 4th time, Epeiph 16.
2     To the Aurelii Horion son of Ptolemaios and Hesychios son of Julianus and Sarapion son of Ploutarchos
3     and Hierax son of Serenus, all town-councillors
4     of the splendid and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, superintendents of barley for Alexandria,
5     from Aurelius Isidoros son of Horion, from the same Oxyrhynchite, captain of a ship
6     of Ptolemaios son of Eulogios, town-councillor, with a guarantor for the delivery of the cargo and for
7     the bringing in full of receipt documents, of the same and aforesaid Ptolemaios, owner
8     of the same ship. We acknowledge, swearing the revered divine oath of our masters
9     Valentinian and Valens and Gratian, the eternal Augusti — I,
10    the captain, that I have received and loaded into the aforesaid ship, on account of the produce of the fortunate first new indiction, new clean unadulterated barley and …
11    … apart from every fault, by the public measure, by the measurement that has been ordered, in all … artabas, and the cargo we shall carry down to Alexandria and
12    deliver into the public granaries there, in full count, and to receive receipt documents for the delivery of these; and I, the guarantor, of my willing and free
13    will, guarantee that the same captain, having delivered this cargo of barley and bringing the receipt in your name, the superintendents, so that in no respect blame
14    may arise concerning us from this, or else we shall be liable to the divine oath.
15    I, Aurelius Ptolemynos son of Eulogios, town-councillor, guarantee the aforesaid captain Isidoros,
16    bringing the receipt for the aforesaid cargo of barley in full, as set out above.
17    I, Aurelius Isidoros son of Horion, received and loaded the clean artabas of barley,
18    two hundred and eleven, and I shall deliver in full as set out above. …
19    Through me, Jacob …
20    And it is as follows:
21    Of barley of the assessment, in all:
22    thus:
23    of the 1st indiction:
24    of the civic (account), Theodoros son of Demetrios and Pelaios and
25    Panarous, 2 artabas.
