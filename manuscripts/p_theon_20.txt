[TRANSLATION]
1     Paponton, also called Pata[s], administrator of lower [property], to M[atrea]
2     the most honored, greet[ings.]
3     If it please you, master, send word
4     to me for the expenses with me,
5     of which I will give account, drachmas [f]orty, total drachmas forty, through
6     Psenanoubes.
7     [I] wish [you] to be well.
8     Of the year twenty-three of Antoninus Caesar [the] m[aster]
9     of the month of Hadrian, twenty-two.
10    [Give] the drachmas forty.
