[TRANSLATION]
1     Heriophmois and Kephalion, embankment-(officials) of (the district) Around Thebes,
2     (to) Bechis, likewise (son of) Phaeris, and Phatres (his) son
3     and Petechespochrates, another son, greeting. You have thrown up, onto the dyke
4     of the Athenaioi(?), a naubion ½, total ½. Year 8 of Trajan
5     the lord, Pachon 12.
