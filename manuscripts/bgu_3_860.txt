[TRANSLATION]
1   To [Aure]lia Diogenis also called Heraklia, a matron stolata(?),
2   [through Harpo]kration the steward(?),
3   [from Au]relii Pethotes son of Paesis, mother Tasouka-
4   [tis, and] Harpathotes son of Saras, mother Eus, both
5   [from the vil]lage of Magdola Mire. We wish, willingly,
6   [to le]ase from you, for the present
7   [. yea]r only of our lord Gallienus Augustus,
8   [from] the (lands) belonging to you jointly with others, by shares,
9   [near] Poampinouphis, from the allotment of Agazelos,
10   [arou]ras eighteen, for sowing wheat and grass (fodder)
11   [and] whatever other crops we choose, of the fallow-
12   rotation kinds, at half(-share) rent: of the (land) in
13   […], the half … four artabas
