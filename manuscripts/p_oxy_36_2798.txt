[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Munatius
2     and Ammonis, linen-dealers
3     to Aurelius Horion, fellow-worker
4     greetings. We have received from you
5     for the price of two artabae of [wheat]
6     comes to two artabae, of [silver] two thousand [four hundred] drachmas
7     comes to two thousand four hundred drachmas.
8     in year 13 and 1 of our lords
9     Constantius and Maximianus,
10    Augustuses and Severus and Maximinus,
11    of the [most illustrious] Caesars.
12    [Aurelius] Munatius and [Ammonis] have received as stated. Aurelius Didymus
13    I wrote on behalf of them not knowing [letters].
