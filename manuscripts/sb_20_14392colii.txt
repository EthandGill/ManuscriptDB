1     delta
2     To Asclepiades, son of Asclepiades of Pharaios, [Maronean],
3     and to Didymus, son of Horion, Althaeian, but on the mother's side [Zenaean],
4     both library keepers of the library in Memphis.
5     From Demetrius, son of Haroeris
6     of Apollonius also, from Memphis.
7     in accordance with [the decrees of] the [most excellent] governor
8     Lucius Luscius Geta.
9     I register what belongs to me in Memphis
10    in the fourteenth ward
11    a third part of an ancestral house.
12    Year [13] of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus
13    Germanicus Imperator, Epeiph 21.
14    Damas, [son] of Haroeris[anus]
15    [of] and [Apollonius]
