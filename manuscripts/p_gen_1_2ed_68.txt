[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Pergamios son of Kyrillos made a declaration and acknowledged,
2     from the village of Philadelphia of the Arsinoite nome. I, the aforesaid
3     Pergamios, acknowledge: I received from the purchaser
4     Ol Koueies from the same village the share of Kyrillos
5     my brother, Ol, all the things accruing to him
6     from our father: full hoofed sheep, forty-two,
7     sheep 42, lambs eleven, lambs 11, four goats, goats 4,
8     one kid, kid 1, two small ones, small ones 2, of gold solidi
9     eight, gold coins 8, one light sack, one stool,
10    and its matching domestic couch, one,
11    a large stool, in the presence of Didymos and Paulos the elder
12    of the same village, and I have no claim against
13    Aurelius Ol, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Pergamios,
14    the aforesaid, received the share of Kyrillos from Aurelius
15    Ol, all the things accruing to him, whether sheep, or gold,
16    or clothing, or bedding, and I have no claim against you
17    concerning this. I, Aurelius Klamasios son of Sarapammon, wrote on their behalf as they are illiterate, having written the document. I, Aurelius Amaeis, wrote on their behalf as they are illiterate and I bear witness as set forth above.
18    After the consulship of Eucherios and Syagrios the most illustrious,
19    … Pachon 13 of the 11th indiction in Philadelphia.
