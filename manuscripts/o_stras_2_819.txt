[TRANSLATION]
1     Petechespocrales son of Tithoous Psenosirus has recorded
2     for the dike-tax of Opheios, 10, 5 drachmas, two-obol, bath-tax of the southern market, four-obol, half-obol,
3     total 6 drachmas, half-obol, and being additionally recorded. Year 10 of Domitian the lord
4     Epeiph 34. Sokrates I have signed.
