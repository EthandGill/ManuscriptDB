[TRANSLATION]
1     You will do well, brother, to write back to me about these matters and you will say to my foster-sister that I have not dismissed anyone
2     to send [it] yesterday nor today on account of what we hear. For I, if I do not gather in the hay within three days, am not able to leave them [behind]. Greet Kronion … and Baukale
3     garments
