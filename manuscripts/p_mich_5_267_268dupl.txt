[TRANSLATION]
1     Herodes also called Orseus the younger, son of Heroïdes son of Heroïdes also called Isidoros, a Macedonian of the catoeci, acknowledge
2     that I have ceded to Didymos the elder son of Lysimachos, so that to him and his descendants and those (acting) for hi[m]
3     for transfer, from the present day for all time, the (allotment) belonging to me aro[u]nd Tebty[nis],
4     a patrimonial catoecic allotment of three arouras, or however many they may be, in one seal-plot, of which the neighbours (are): on the south, the allotment of Didymos the purchaser; on the north, the allotment of Kronion son of Eirenaios; on the west, uncultivated land, there being in the middle an aqueduct through whic[h]
5     the allotment is watered; on the east, the allotment of Psabotos son of Apollonios and his partners; for which I will also carry out the proper (formalities) of the transfer and cession to Didymos through the cat[oe]cic record-office. And I have received from Didymos the whole established silver cession
6     principal, and I will guarantee (it) with every guarantee, free of all public debts and all registrations and dyke-taxes and artaba-tax and counting-tax and surpluses and price of land and impos[t]
7     of the village and penalty and all the other (charges) demanded, those from the earlier times unti[l]
8     the first year and the first year itself of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
9     and from private (debts) and all claim for all time as is set forth. There wrote on hi[s] behalf
10    Sokrates son of Sokrates because he does not know letters. Didymos the elder son of Lysimachos: the aforesaid cession has been made out [t]o
11    me as is set forth.
12    Heroïdes, about 37 years old, scar on the left
13    calf. Didymos, 48 years old, scar
14    on the first finger of the left hand. Sokrates,
15    35 years old, scar …
16    Cession of Didymos, village-scribe
