[TRANSLATION]
1     [To my lady mother Theonis and my lord]
2     brother Askl… … greeting.
3     Before all things I pray that you are in health, with the
4     gods among whom I am sojourning … of the
5     brother Pas[ion] … I sent off to you a small jar of oil, having bought it for
6     six hundred drachmas, for I have heard that with you
7     oil is dear; and having a basket, I too (have) dried figs. You will give to my brother Kornelianos of them 150.
8     And a half-ounce of Berenician purple, so that thus,
9     if it seems good to you, you may make the little tunics and two hoods.
10    But my dalmatic … let it
11    be for you; send me my hood, the dark-blue one, and my cloak, and shake out safely my other cloak lest it rot; and I will send you money if you send me back in return the little linen cloths which you made for me.
12    And make known to me what you received from Dioskorion
13    and from Isidoros and from Kastor or rather Polydeukes the former … so that I may know. Receive everything that I send to you
