[TRANSLATION]
1     … of Flavius Sallustius, the most illustrious praefect
2     of the sacred praetorium.
3     To Gaius Julius Leucadius, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite (nome),
4     from the Aurelii … and S… , … son of Serenus, and Serenus son of Eusebios, and
5     Ammonius son of Apion, and Sarapion son of Ploutarchos, all of them councillors of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, superintendents
6     of grain for Alexandria. We acknowledge, swearing the august divine oath of our master Julianus the eternal
7     Augustus, that we have received and loaded onto the boat listed below, from the produce
8     of the fortunate 7th new indiction, new wheat, clean, unmixed with barley and unwetted and set apart from every fault,
9     by public measure, by the measurement ordered, in all 3,750 artabas; and the cargo we will carry down to the
10    most illustrious metropolis of the Alexandrians, into the public granaries there, in full number, and of the
11    delivery … to bring in … so that in nothing we be blamed, or else we would be liable to the
12    divine oath. And it is as follows:
13    Onto a private boat of Theon, of the annona-department of my lord
14    the most illustrious duke, of which the steersman is Horos son of … , from the city of Diocletianus of the Thebaid,
15    the aforesaid 3,750 artabas of wheat, of which the guarantor is Mouses son of … , from the …
16    city … of Annianus the tribune.
17    I, Aurelius Sarapion son of Ploutarchos, have received jointly together with my partners the
18    three thousand seven hundred and fifty artabas of clean wheat, only, and will deliver them jointly as aforesaid.
19    I, Aurelius Timagenes, have received jointly together with my partners and will deliver jointly as aforesaid.
20    And it is as follows:
21    Of wheat of the assessment, [in all 3,750 artabas,]
22    thus:
23    of the 7th indiction,
24    of the villagers
25    of the 8th pagus of Palosis, through
26    Theon and … nk … i
27    k… i… on, [artabas] 3,750
28    total, the aforesaid.
29    In the consulship aforesaid, Mesore … .
