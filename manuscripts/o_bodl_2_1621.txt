[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis of the produce, year 1, of our lords the Emperors
2     Caesars Marcus Claudius Pupienus [M]aximus and Decimus
3     Cailius Caluinus Balbunus, the Fortunate, the Pious
4     Sebastoi, Epeiph 27, in the name of Aurelius Nicomachus for Notus
5     in the name of Ps… atos Anubion, wheat artabas eight, total wheat artabas 8. Gallus, I have been sealed.
6     Similarly of the treasury of the villages, Mesore 18, for Notus, in the name of Nicomachus
7     of Straton for the name of Psenaous Anubion, wheat five
8     total wheat artabas 5. Aurelius Dio, I have been sealed.
