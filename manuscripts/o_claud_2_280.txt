[TRANSLATION]
1     Heraclides and Dionysius to Paniscus and Herminus, both, many greetings. Your proskynem we make
2     before the Fortune of the guard-post
3     You wrote us then that I sent a double-necked vessel of water
4     he did not give us except a small vessel. Receive the small-basket which you sent us under
5     the storage-jars, so that, if
6     you find opportunity, send again to us from it. The
7     now not yet have happened the vegetables. Do not neglect concerning the
8     pieces so that you buy
9     we pray you farewell,
10    most beloved
