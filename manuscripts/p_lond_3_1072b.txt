r.1    ἡ ἁγία τοῦ θεοῦ ἐκκλησία Ἑρμουπόλεως διʼ ἐμοῦ Κολλούθου προνοητοῦ μερίδος Ἑρμουπόλεως τῷ ἀδελφῷ

[TRANSLATION]
1     The holy church of God of Hermopolis through me Colluthus, agent of the district of Hermopolis, to the brother
2     to Victor, stippourgus. [I] have given for the rent of planted land for the heirs of Ameiolios
3     of fruits of the twelfth indiction of gold carats twenty two and a half
4     becomes gold carats 22½ Alexandrian. [It] was written, Mesore 8, indiction 12. Colluthus, agent, [signs].
