[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most excellent council of Hermopolis the great
2     ancient and splendid and most venerable, through Aurelius
3     Corellius Alexandros, of equestrian rank, retired from military service,
4     gymnasiarch, councillor, prytanis in office
5     of the same city, and however he is styled,
6     from Aurelius Menelaos son of Pascheious, his mother being
7     Teseus, from the village of Thelbonthis; I wish
8     willingly to lease from the civic account
9     for a period of four years from the present 14th year,
10    around the same Thelbonthis, from the kleros of Philokrates,
11    six arouras for the sowing of wheat and of rest-crop kinds each year by half, at an annual fixed rent and tax of the whole
12    arouras, eighteen artabas of wheat and seventy-two drachmas of silver, which I will pay
13    and measure out in the months Pauni and Epeiph each year,
14    the silver being approved coin and the wheat into the public (granary) at the first measuring by one (measure) with the dochikon-measure instead of
15    the one of Athenaios, and I will make clean measuring for the
16    disbursing, all the public dues of the land
17    and the additional assessments being chargeable to the account of the city;
18    and if — which may it not happen — it become unwatered, from the
19    following year I will of necessity irrigate (it) and pay
20    half of the aforesaid taxes, and if an additional bid is made, it being permitted to others to sublease
21    or also to cultivate it themselves; and on being formally questioned I agreed. Year 14 of Imperator
22    Caesar Publius Licinius Gallienus
23    Germanicus Maximus Persicus Maximus Pius
24    Felix Augustus, Choiak 3.
25    Aurelius Menelaos son of Pascheious, I have leased as set out above.
26    Aurelius Kopres, I wrote on his behalf as he does not know letters.
