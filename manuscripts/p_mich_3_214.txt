[TRANSLATION]
1     Paniskos to my wife
2     Ploutogenia, mother of my daughter,
3     very many greetings.
4     Before [all things] I pray for your good health
5     each day before
6     all the gods. I want you to know therefore,
7     sister, that in Koptos
8     we remained near your sister
9     and her children,
10    so that you may not be distressed at coming
11    to Koptos; for here are
12    your brothers. Just as you also
13    by all means wish to greet her warmly, she prays
14    to the gods each day, wishing
15    to greet you together with your
16    mother. So, on receiving this letter of mine, put your affairs in order,
17    so that, if I send for you,
18    you may come quickly. And bring,
19    when you come, fleeces of wool,
20    ten, of olives six jars,
21    of liquid-extract four jars,
22    and only my new weapon,
23    my helmet. Bring also my lances.
24    Bring also the gear of the tent. If you find a good opportunity, come with good people.
25    Let Nonnos come
26    with you. Bring, when you come, all our clothes. Bring,
27    when you come, your gold ornaments, but
28    do not wear them on the boat.
29    I greet my lady daughter Heliodora. Hermias greets you (pl.).
30    Deliver to my wife and to my daughter, from Paniskos the father.
