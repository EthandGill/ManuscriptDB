[TRANSLATION]
1     To the census-officer of Pa[m]menes' Garden
2     from the Au[re]lii Pet siris and Aphynchis,
3     both sons of Aphynchis son of Pet siris, his mother being
4     Taaphyn[chi]s, from the city of Oxyrhynchos. According to the things
5     command[e]d by Aurelius Basileus, the most illustrious
6     former-governor, we register, for the house-by-house registration of the past 1st year
7     of the Marci [I]ulii Philippi, Caesars,
8     the lo[r]ds Augusti, the
9     portion of a house belonging to us and all its appurtenances, formerly belonging to Harseis who was inherited by us by succession, in which we register:
10    Pet siris son of Aphynchis son of Pet siris, his mother being
11    Taaphynchis, paying twelve-drachma tax, without trade, without distinguishing marks, 27 years old;
12    Aphynchis, his brother of the same parents, paying twelve-drachma tax, without distinguishing marks, 26 years old.
13    Silvanus, house-born slave of Aphynchis, belonging to Aphynchis
14    by the third part, without trade, without distinguishing marks, 19 years old.
15    There are added for the first time: Mouses, slave of the same
16    Aphynchis by the third part, born of the slave-woman Sinthonis,
17    without trade, without distinguishing marks, 3 years old.
18    Marcus, likewise house-born slave of the same, likewise
19    by the third part, born of the same slave-woman, without trade, without distinguishing marks, in the 1st year.
20    Marcus [the young]er, our slave, declared to have been released and set free.
21    … Sinthonis, house-born slave-woman of the same
22    Aphynchis by the third part, without trade, without distinguishing marks, 24 years old.
23    Eirene, house-born slave-woman of the same Aphynchis
24    by the third part, born of the slave-woman Sinthonis, without distinguishing marks, 5 years old.
25    And we swear the oath customary for the Romans
26    that we have not lied. Year 2 of the Imperator
27    Caesar Marcus Iulius Philippus, Pious,
28    Fortunate, and of Marcus Iulius Philippus,
29    most noble and most illustrious Caesar,
30    Augusti, Pachon. We, the Aurelii Petsiris
31    and Aphynchis, have submitted and have sworn the oath.
32    I, Aurelius Serenus, wrote on their behalf, since they do not know letters.
33    It was registered before the census-officer of Pammenes' Garden.
