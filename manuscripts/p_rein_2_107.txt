[TRANSLATION]
1     To the blessed monastery of Phel through you, abba Jacob the most god-beloved presbyter and hegoumenos of the
2     same monastery of the Coptic nome, Aurelius Allammon also called Eiot son of Praepositus
3     coppersmith from Syene, greetings. I acknowledge having received from you and owing you one gold nomisma
4     and this I have ready to provide to you and to repay you whenever you wish without dispute. And the security [is] authoritative
5     and binding with my signature as aforesaid. [It] was written Pharmouthi 1 of the sixth indiction † Iot [son of] Praepositus
6     the security agrees as aforesaid, Theophemptus of Christopher, [advocate] …
7     from Diocletianopolis, I attest to the security having heard from Eiot also known [as him] who placed [it]
8     [the] security of Allammon also called Eiot son of Praepositus coppersmith from Syene [for] one gold nomisma
