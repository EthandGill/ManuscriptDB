[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Nemesianos, [acting] strategos [of the Hermopo]lite (nome),
2     from Aurelius Hermeias son of Ammonios and as he is styled. [Having a claim to make]
3     against Aurelius Ammonios, [former] … [co-scribe(?)] of Aurelius Antonius, former strategos — the most splendid governor Valerius Firmus having ordered, concerning the matters in which he extorted from me
4     when I happened to be secretary of the praktores of money, two talents and three thousand drachmas — I request that this man be ordered
5     to be summoned through an attendant of the strategia to be present and to wait in attendance [at the tribunal] of our [most splendid] governor until the
6     [matters sought against him] reach [an end, and also] that he produce
7     [his assistants Isidoros and Gerontios and Hierakion]
8     [so that] nothing [be lacking] in the matter being argued, in accordance
9     with the subscriptions [given] to me, which are as follows — of one, namely:
10    [Year 3, Pa]chon 8: Having summoned the one you accuse, petition me when I hold court. Of rolls, 69
11    [of the volume] …, [and of another], namely: Year 4, Thoth 12: If, when you have petitioned, [your]
12    [opponent] does not obey, a ruling will be given. Of roll 1 of the volume … .
13    [Year 4 of the Emperor] Caesar Marcus Julius Philippus Pius Fortunate and Marcus
14    [Julius Philippus, most noble and most] illustrious Caesar, Augusti, Phamenoth [30].
15    [I, Aurelius Hermeias son of Ammonios, have submitted] and
16    have given notice [as is] set forth above.
