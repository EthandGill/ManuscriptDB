[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of [our] l[or]d Gr[atian]us the eternal Augustus
2     for the 2nd (time) and of Flavi[us Pro]b[us t]he most il[lustriou]s pr[ef]ect of the sacred
3     praetorium, [Me]sor[e] 29.
4     To Flavius Isidoros, official of the staff of the prefecture (gov[ernor]ship) of the Thebaid,
5     from Aurel[ius] Apo[ll]onios son of B[esap]ollon, former magistrate of Panopolis.
6     I acknowledge, swearing by the divine and h[e]avenly fortune of
7     our lords who conquer all things, the eternal
8     Augusti Valentinian[u]s and Valens and Gratianus,
9     willingly and of my own free will, that I stand surety for Paniskos
10    son of Makarios, overseer of clothing of the thirteenth
11    indiction, who has been ordered to go down to the most illustrious Alexan[d]ria, [on condition that he go down to that same Alexandria] and not be parted from you
12    until the matters concerning him reach their [con]clusion, whether at (the court) of the Alexandr[i]ans or on the return-journey before the tribunal;
13    and if he should desert and I do not produce [him], I
14    myself shall render the account on his behalf, or I shall be li[a]ble
15    to the divine oath and to the danger that hangs over
16    this (matter); and having been formally questioned, I acknowledged (it).
17    Aurelius A[po]llonios son of Besap[o]llon, the aforesaid,
18    I stand surety for the aforesaid Pani[s]ko[n a]s is aforesaid.
19    Surety-bond of [A]pol[lon]i[os ] …
