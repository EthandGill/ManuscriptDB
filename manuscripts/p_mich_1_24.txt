[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonides to Apollonios, greetings. Since the [pear-trees] with us …
2     through you to the king, I have sent so that to you from another orchard pear-plants [are provided].
3     Farewell. …
4     Apollonides, clerk, to Apollonios
5     pear-trees 200. Year 29, Panemus, to the administrator.
6     in Arsinoë of Dionysus.
