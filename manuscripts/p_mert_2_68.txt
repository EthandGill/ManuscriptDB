[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Didymarion] daughter of … and to her niece Helenous daughter of Kastor, both [with their guardian,] their kinsman Lysimachos son of Isidoros also called Herodes,
2     [from Tryphon] son of Elis and Hera daughter of Patynis, the two from the village [of Kerkeosi]ris of the Polemon division, Hera with her guardian her nephew. We wish to lease [from you,] under joint liability, the (arouras) belonging to you in common around
3     [the aforesaid] village of Kerkeosiris, of an allotment, seven and a half
4     [a quarter arouras, or however many] there may be, in two parcels, of which (parcels) the first parcel (has) arouras
5     [four and a half] a quarter, the second parcel three arouras, all previously [cultivated] by Hera's husband Patynis son of Maron, for
6     [four] years [from] the coming twenty-first year of Hadrian Caesar
7     [the lord; in the] first and third year of the lease, the three
8     [arouras and the half] part of the four and a half quarter arouras
9     [for sowing of wheat, and the] remaining half for sowing of hay as fodder for sheep [and bedding], the rent of which, for the two-year period, per year, together with the
10    five artabas of wheat with which I am supplied as seed, being of the whole, per year, … artabas of wheat, and a money-rent of eight drachmas; and (in) the second
11    [and fourth (year), the] half part of the four and a half quarter
12    [arouras for sowing] of wheat, [and the] remaining half and the three arouras for sowing
13    [of hay as fodder] for sheep and for bedding, the rent of which, for the two-year period,
14    [in each year, together] with the two and a half artabas of wheat with which I am supplied as seed,
15    [being of the whole, in each year,] twenty-four artabas of wheat, and a rent
16    [of … money-drachmas] and …, for the four-year period, of capital … of all, by the six-choinix measure of Kerkeosiris,
17    [free of deduction and free of risk,] we performing all
18    the agricultural works of the arouras [from our own (means)] at the proper seasons, doing no damage, and the
19    [yearly rents we shall pay] in the month Pauni, on the threshing-floor, new, clean,
20    unadulterated, sifted; the public freight-charges of the speeding-up being [our (concern), Hera and] Tryphon's, but the transport of the rents
21    [being yours, Helenous and] Didymarion's; and it is for me, Hera, (to pay) the [per-aroura artab(-tax) and the naubion] of the three arouras and of the whole impost … [and the seed] of the coming year's sowing of the
22    grain-bearing [arouras] … [and after] the time we shall hand over the
23    [arouras clean of rush, ] reed, dog's-tooth grass, sediment of every kind, and you shall guarantee
24    [to us with every guarantee against] all the public (dues), if it seem good (to you) to lease. …
