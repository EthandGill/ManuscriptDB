[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most illustrious lord Menas with [god] wine-receiver and administrator in glorious
2     house from Victorus son of Psai … [from] village Patanei of the Cynopolite nome. I received and was satisfied from your illustriousness
3     [for] price of light [goods] one thousand two hundred new and beautiful; [they] make light [goods] …;
4     [for] price moreover of the same light [goods] of gold coins six of proper weight;
5     makes gold coins 6 of proper weight; and for your security I have made
6     the present small receipt one, written. Menas as elder secretary of the same village, it was written in month of Phamekoth 13, indiction 10. Victor agrees with me Samia …
