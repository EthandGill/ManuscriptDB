[TRANSLATION]
1     …
2     … [the portions falling to us from] our parents …
3     … [the] fifth part of it that has come (to us) …
4     … Kyrikos son of Pam…
5     … to the benevolent …
6     … on, Faustus …
7     [of the whole house which has come] down to us from our blessed father
8     … with all its appurtenances, situated in the same village in the quarter
9     … the neighbors of the whole house being these who dwell around (it): on the south, (the property) of Joannes son of Josephios,
10    on the north a public street, on the east a public street, again on the west the house of me, Patermouthios;
11    neighbors on every side all around — that from now on you, the aforewritten, and also together with
12    our brother Aurelius Faustus, may have control, ownership, and mastery of the aforesaid
13    whole house from foundation to air, as they have stated above, to administer, dwell in,
14    manage, build upon, sell, cede, give as a gift, doing everything concerning it
15    with full authority and unhindered, and no one being able to bring action against you, neither
16    of us, nor of our heirs, nor of (our) brothers, nor of (our) nephews, nor of anyone joined with us,
17    absolutely; but whoever wishes ever at any time to bring action against you concerning this on account of
18    any pretext, on which (condition) the one daring to do these things shall pay, by way of penalty,
19    six ounces of gold, exacted in deed and in fact, and after the payment of the penalty
20    he shall be constrained to acquiesce and abide by this security, which is valid and secure, and
21    on being formally questioned we acknowledged it and released (you). The Aurelii, Patermouthis and Sophia
22    and Kalaophre and Martha, the aforewritten women, have set down this duly executed
23    security together with the aforesaid penalty; and everything contained in it is satisfactory to us
24    as stated above. Through me, Victor son of Ananias, document-writer of the Memnoneia, by (me). Paulus, his brother,
25    having been requested, wrote in their presence, on behalf of the women, they not knowing letters, which I also drew up.
26    Mathousalas son of Joannes, presbyter, I bear witness.
27    Kyrikos son of Joseph, presbyter, I bear witness.
28    Aurelius Petros also called Paulus … er, I bear witness.
