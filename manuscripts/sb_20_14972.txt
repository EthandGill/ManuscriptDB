[TRANSLATION]
1     from John the most distinguished and with god's [aid] receiver
2     to Phoeibammon ...; I received and was satisfied by you [from] produce under you
3     my arouras 4 of fruits of the fourth indiction through the order of Psates, scribe of the village of Sentowtos
4     of dirty grain apart from freight charges and surcharges and all kinds of expenses, artabas thirteen,
5     it makes grain artabas 13 alone, and for your security I have made this receipt
6     with my signature as previously set down. Flavius John agrees with me [for] grain artabas thirteen, 13.
