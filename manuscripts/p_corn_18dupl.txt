[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Diogenes also called Hermias, syndic of the glorious
2     and most glorious city of the Oxyrhynchites, for the present 7th year and 6th year, from Marcus Aurelius Apion
3     son of Philippos, his mother being Apias, of the Althaian (tribe). I wish, for the first time,
4     to register, in the quarter of the Dromos of Thoeris, the
5     (children) born to me from my (wife), now deceased, Tisois daughter of Sarapammon
6     son of Eutychides also called Sarapion, her mother being Thatoes, from
7     the glorious and most glorious city of the Oxyrhynchites:
8     the sons Aurelii — Philippos, being, for the same present 7th year and 6th year, 13 years old; and Gaius, likewise being, for
9     the same present 7th year and 6th year, 5 years old, both of them
10    twelve-drachma payers from the gymnasium; and Melanous also called Anoubiaina, being, for the same 7th year and 6th year, 15 years old; Taeous
11    likewise being, for the same present 7th year and 6th year,
12    7 years old. Therefore I submit the memorandum, requesting that they be enrolled in the class of those of the same age as is fitting, and I swear the customary Roman oath
13    that I have not lied. Year 7 of the Emperor Caesar
14    Gaius Aurelius Valerius Diocletian and year 6
15    of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Valerius
16    Maximian, Germanici Maximi, Pii, Felices, Augusti, Epeiph 3… I, Aurelius Apion son of Philippos,
17    have submitted and have sworn the oath.
18    (Quarter) of the Dromos of Thoeris.
19    Philippos, 13 years old
20    Gaius, 5 years old
21    females: Melanous also called Anoubiaina, 15 years old
22    Taeus, 7 years old
23    children of Apion son of Philippos. Steward:
24    Noukekies
