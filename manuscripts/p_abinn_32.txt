[TRANSLATION]
1     To my master and beloved
2     brother Abinnaeus the praepositius
3     Kaor, father of Hermes city, [greet]ings.
4     I salute your children greatly.
5     I wish you to know, lord,
6     con[cern]ing Paul the soldier
7     concerning his flight, grant
8     him this once
9     since I am occupied [to come] to
10    you these days. And again
11    if he does not cease [he will come]
12    into your hands another time.
13    I pray that you be strong for many years, my lord
14    brother.
