[TRANSLATION]
1     Nemesianus to Severus, greetings.
2     Give to Colouthus the potter toward an account
3     of delivery of the [present] month of Pachon
4     of wheat of the produce of year 5, artabas 18, and
5     of wine of the produce of year 6 of the [estate] of Colouthus
6     from the wine press 2, vessels 24, of which the collected price was sent
7     Sarapiades the manager to charge: on the one hand, of the wheat, as of the artaba 1, drachmas 16,
8     on the other hand, of the wine, as of the vessel 1, drachmas 12.
9     Be well, and [give] the of the wheat
10    artabas eighteen, and of wine
11    vessels twenty four,
12    of year 7, Pachon 30.
