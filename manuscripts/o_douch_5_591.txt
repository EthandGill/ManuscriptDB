r.2    Χρῆστος ἐπιμελητὴς οἴνου … ἰνδικτίωνος χαίρειν.
r.3    δὸς Ἑρμησίας οἴ[νου ξέστ]ας

[TRANSLATION]
1     to the lord brother Victor of Pekysis
2     Chrestos, overseer of wine, … of the indiction, greetings.
3     Give to Hermesias [wine measures]
4     twenty, [they] become wine measures twenty. I have signed.
5     I, Chrestos. I pray [that] you are well.
