[TRANSLATION]
1     There paid Thaesis (daughter of) Thatres, for rent
2     of Charax of year 1, base, 2½ obols; and in the name of the same
3     Thaesis, for rent, 2½ obols, total 5 obols.
4     Year 2 of Antoninus and Verus,
5     the lords, the Imperatores,
6     Phaophi 5. I, Ka( ), have signed.
