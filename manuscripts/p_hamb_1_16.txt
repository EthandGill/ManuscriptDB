[TRANSLATION]
1     [I, so-and-so, have signed. In the 18th year of the Impe]rator[s Caesars] Lucius Septimius Severus
2     Pious [Pertinax and] Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Pious,
3     August[i], and [Publius Se]ptimius Geta Caesar Augustus, Phaophi 10.
4     To Didas who served as exegetes and [Mystes who served as] cosmetes, councillors, keepers of the property-records of the Ars[inoite],
5     from Antonia Thermoutharion, transacting business without a guardian according to the customs of the Romans by the right of children. According to the public
6     registration completed on the present day through the record-office
7     here, I bought in the village of Philadelphia a half-fifth-tenth part, common and undivided, of a sixth part of a house and
8     appurtenances, at the price of one hundred drachmas of silver, from Titoleius
9     also called Isidoros, and Titoleius, both of them Antinoites Sabinians also called Harmonieis, and Kyrilla and Titania, the
10    four (children) of Lon ginus also called Sarapion son of Isidoros, who are not
11    registered, and each of the women being with her guardian,
12    Kyrilla with Sokrates son of Theon son of Ptolemaios, from the quarter of the Arabs, and Titania with Ammonios son of
13    Klaudianos also called Ptolemaios, who served as high-priest of the city of the Arsinoites. Wherefore I submit (this) in order that the registration be made
14    in accordance with the copy of the transaction which we have appended.
15    For whenever I make the declaration of it, I shall demonstrate that it exists and is unencumbered, held by no one; but if it should appear
16    to belong to another or to have been previously held through the record-office, there shall
17    be no impediment (to the seller) from this registration. I, Didas who served as exegetes,
18    councillor, have entered (it). I, Mystes who served as cosmetes,
19    councillor, have entered (it). Year 18 of Lucius Septimius
20    Severus Pious Pertinax and Marcus
21    Aurelius Antoninus Pious, August[i],
22    and Publius Septimius Geta Caesar Augustus,
23    Phaophi 10.
