[TRANSLATION]
1     Septimius Ammonios also called Dionysios,
2     strategos of the Arsinoite, of the Themistos and Polemon divisions,
3     gives notice to all who have been clearly
4     reported to me by the dekaprotoi
5     as being cultivators of the plain called Phrou
6     the Less, that they without excuse attend to the
7     cultivation, for the sake that the dues owed
8     to the most sacred treasury may unimpededly … be paid;
9     of the same … according to what
10    they requested through the documents which they submitted
11    to me.
12    Year 5 of the Imperators Caesars Marci
13    Julii Philippi, Pii,
14    Felices, Augusti, Hathyr 29.
