2     for the canon of the just-now-passed eighth indiction toward the …
3     conversion-into-money of this, and toward the security, that is freedom-from-care, of the
4     glorious house administered by them and of the administrators themselves of the same affairs
5     and as proof of their good faith toward the treasury, we have made use
6     of the present declaration, having appended to it in our own hand also
7     the statement of the public (dues) accomplished in detail, in accordance with
8     the judicial orders issued to the said glorious house
9     and with the formaria, that is …

11    proofs, no other item remaining over from the same three
12    estates and the property of Thmoun Pamoun for the eighth indiction
13    both in grain and wine-and-meat and barley-chaff and all kinds of gold-(dues) and
14    largitional and annona items belonging to the same bank
15    for comparison according to the appended statement, on behalf of the same three
16    estates and the property of Thmoun Pamoun, lying in the upper Thebaid;
17    we have made use (of it) also at the present, in all respects, most learned defensor, lord.
