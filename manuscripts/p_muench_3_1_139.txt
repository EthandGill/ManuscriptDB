1     Collections of gold (solidi): 72 coins.
2     Thus:
3     To Pabois, for the grain-collectors: 5 coins.
4     Pachymios, for the grain-collectors: 5 coins.
5     Serenos, for the grain-collectors: 3 coins.
6     Phoibammon son of Lammon: 5 coins.
7     Taurinos, for the grain-collectors: 3 coins.
8     The same, on behalf of Sois: 2 coins.
9     Phoibammon son of Pinoution: 7 coins.
10    Beres, for the grain-collectors: 5 coins.
11    Ionis son of Tyrannos: 6 coins.
12    Sois, likewise: 4 coins.
13    The same, on behalf of Pinoution: 2 coins.
14    Phoibammon son of Lilous, through Phoibammon son of Lammon: 1 coin.
15    Phoibammon son of Pinoution: 1 coin.
16    Pachymios, likewise: 2 coins.
17    1st receipt: 51 coins and 20 myriads of silver talents, 9300.
18    2nd receipt:
19    Thus:
20    Taurinos son of Bion: 2 coins.
21    Phoibammon son of Lammon: 1 coin.
22    Iones son of Tyrannos: 1 coin.
23    Tiberios: 1 coin.
24    5 coins and 3 myriads of talents, 9500.
25    3rd receipt:
26    Pabois: 1 coin.
27    Phoibammon son of Pinoution: 1 coin.
28    Phoibammon son of Lammon: 1 coin.
29    Ionis and Tiberios: 1 coin.
30    Tiberios: 1 coin, through Ionis: 1 coin.
31    Through Serenos the komarch: 1 coin.
32    Serenos the komarch, likewise: 1 coin.
33    Total together: 64 coins.
34    Phoibammon son of Pinoution: 1 coin.
