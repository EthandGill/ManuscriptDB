[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Transferred, from the wheat-crop of the past 15th year of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the lord, through the sitologi of the western toparchy, district of Seryphis, from the deposit of Philoxenus also called Philiscus, to Dionysius son of Harthoonis, as a deposit, sixty artabas, total 60 art. of wheat. I, Dionysius, sitologus, have countersigned. I, Dionysius son of Harthoonis, the aforesaid. Transfer to Dionysius son of . . . the aforementioned sixty artabas, total 60 art. (J. De Jong)
