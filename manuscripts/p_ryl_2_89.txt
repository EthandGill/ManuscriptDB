[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: ‘Socnopaei Nesus, list of officials for the 33nd year. Elders :—Sotas son of Ptolemaeus and Soteris, surnamed Posei, Plas son of Sempronius, Justus brother of Pakusis, Apunchis son of Pakusis son of Apunchis. Archephodus:— Pabous son of Pabous and Soteria. Watchmen:— Orsenouphis son of Sotas Kouis, Orsenoup his son of Horion, Soterichus son of Panesneus, Paouetis son of Kanneis. Police:— Demas son of Demas, Panephremmis son of Sempronius.’
