[TRANSLATION]
1     athas
2     Thonis son of Psenephthas, son of Psenephthas, his m[o]ther being …,
3     from the city of the Oxyrhynchites, fire-priest of Athena (and) Thoeris the greatest
4     goddess, Persian of the epigone, to my own ne[p]he[w] Thonis
5     son of Hephaistas, son of Psenephthas, his mother being Taseus, from the same
6     city, fire-priest of the same goddess, greeting. I acknowledge that I have
7     from you, through the bank at the Sarapeion by the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
8     of Hierax and the inspectors with him, the agent Martiallis attending — you having given a promise — of silver
9     of imperial coinage six hundred drachmas, making of silver
10    six hundred drachmas of capital, to which nothing whatever has been added,
11    at interest of one drachma per mina each month from the present month Pharmouthi; and I shall of necessity pay back to you the
12    interest each month and the capital on the thirtieth of Phamenoth
13    of the twentieth year of Antoninus Caesar the lord, without
14    any delay. And if I do not pay back as has been written, I agree that there remain to you, Thonis son of Hephaistas, and (your) offspring
15    and those receiving (rights) from you, in return for (the case) that, if I do not pay back
16    after the appointed term, the possession and ownership for
17    ever of all the shares falling to me, in common
18    with my siblings Tasenpothes and your father
19    Hephaistas, in the quarter of the Tenth (district), in the parts to the west, in
20    a blind alley, of a house and courtyard and appurtenances and entrances
21    and exits, of all of which the neighbors are: on the south (the property) of Sarapion, on the nor[t]h an entrance,
22    on the east (that) of Apis, on the west (that) of me and my siblings; and that you assess
23    through yourself, if you choose, the taxes for these and be master
24    of them as though a sale had taken place to you, and the surplus
25    carry off and sell to others and use as you choose,
26    no recourse being left to me; but also of necessity
27    I will furnish to you and to those (acting) from you the same shares of the real-property,
28    through everything, secure against all (claims), with all warranty, and clear
29    from every registration, and from royal cultivation and
30    estate-(cultivation) and every kind, and from anything whatsoever
31    other; and until payment-back it shall not be permitted to me to sell the same shares
32    of the real-property, nor a part, nor to mortgage, nor otherwise to alienate in any way, nor to register anyone upon them, or else all that is done to the contrary shall be void, it being permitted to you, through yourself, from
33    now whenever you choose, to make a holding of these through the
34    record-office of the property-registry until I pay back to you the capital
35    and the interest, the choice and option being with you, Thonis
36    son of Hephaistas, if you wish after the time, I not acting justly toward the capital and interest, to be master in return for these of the same shares of the house
37    on the aforesaid (terms), or to make the exaction both of the
38    same capital and of the (sums) named and, of the elapsed time, of equal interest of a drachma per mina
39    each month, both from me and from the shares of the
40    house and from all the other (property) belonging to me,
41    as though by legal judgment. Valid is the bond, produced everywhere.
42    Year 17 of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus
43    Augustus Pius, Pharmouthi 4. I, Thonis son of Psenephthas, son of Psenephthas, have from my nephew Thonis son of Hephaistas, the same, of silver six hundred drachmas of capital, at interest
44    of a drachma per mina each month
45    from the present month Pharmouthi; and
46    I shall pay back the interest each month and the
47    capital on the thirtieth of Phamenoth of the
48    twentieth year of Antoninus Caesar the lord;
49    and if not, he shall be master of all the shares
50    falling to me, in common with my siblings Tasemphothes and his father Hephaistas, in the quarter of the Tenth (district), in the
51    parts to the west, in a blind alley, of a house
52    and courtyard and appurtenances and entrances and
53    [ex]its, upon all the aforesaid (terms), as also
54    of Senpothes, brother of Thonis [and] Hephaistas,
55    through the bank, on mortgage of shares
56    of houses.
