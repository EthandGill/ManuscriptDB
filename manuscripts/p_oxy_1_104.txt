[TRANSLATION]
1   In the sixteenth year of Imperator Caesar Domitian
2   Augustus Germanicus, Choiak 30, in the city of the Oxyrhynchi in the Thebaid,
3   for good fortune.
4   Thus has Soeris, daughter of Harpochras, freedman
5   of Sarapion son of Chairemon, her mother being Tnepheros daughter of Annios,
6   of the city of the Oxyrhynchi, made her will, being sane and of sound mind, with as guardian her husband Hatres, his mother being Teraus
7   also called Thaubastis, daughter of Phatres, both of the same city, in the
8   street. So long as I live in health, may I have power over my own property, to use and ad-
9   minister it in whatever way I choose. But after my coming
10   death, I concede that there belong to my son Areotes, styled
11   as of me, Soeris, as mother — if he lives, and if not, to his issue — the house
12   and court belonging to me at the Sarapeion by the city of the Oxyrhynchi, in the quarter
13   formerly of the Cavalry Camp, with entrances
14   and exits and appurtenances, on condition that my designated husband Hatres shall have
15   the right of dwelling, and the accruing rents, of the designated
16   house and court for the span of his life, free of rent,
17   cast out by no one; to whom the same son Areotes shall furnish
18   yearly forty-eight drachmas of silver until there be paid in
19   full three hundred drachmas of silver — the sum settled between them
20   in settlement and agreement concerning what is owed by me to the
21   same husband Hatres on a bank security for the tenancy-pledge of the
22   same house and court, six hundred drachmas of silver. And the same son
23   shall give to the daughter born to me of my husband Hatres, Tnepheros,
24   after my husband’s death, within thirty days, what I be-
25   queath her: forty drachmas of silver. And she shall dwell in one room
26   on the ground floor, at the gate-house, whenever she be separated from her husband, until
27   she be once for all reconciled, free of rent. And in general it shall not be lawful for anyone
28   […] to make demand of anything else from the son or his assigns
29   [after] my husband’s death — nothing of what passed through the bank se-
30   curity of the tenancy-pledge, in any [way]; rather he is re-
31   leased from payment of what is owed through it […]. And to no one else
32   [at all] do I leave any of my property. All the fore-
33   [writt]en has the approval of [my] designated [husband Hatre]s, of the same
34   [city], in the same street […] Artemidor[os …]
