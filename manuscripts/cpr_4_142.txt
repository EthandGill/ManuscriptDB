[TRANSLATION]
1     Theodosios in the name of God the presbyter, his signature for the scribe Victor, that I took and I fulfilled [it] for you
2     at the public [storehouse] of the treasury of these months which you said to me upon the produce of the fifteenth indiction
3     which these are the carats [of gold], become 5 carats of gold, to your share therefore I wrote the proof/receipt for you
4     I subscribe to it, Theodosios in the name of God the presbyter, I compose this proof/receipt
