[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Pto]lemy, greeting, from Tetosiris. A lawsuit … against … me …
2     … [against A]pollodoros concerning a house which is in Berenikis the [Thesmophorou];
3     having need of testimonies for the lawsuit, I received from the introducer of cases …
4     a letter [to] Herakleodoros the epistates, that whatever witnesses I should bring before him should swear their testimonies for me; Apollodoros, having made a conspiracy
5     and making a greater (disturbance), frightened off all my witnesses, saying that he would beat
6     them and me to death and cast (us) out of the village; and moreover a certain Biou…an, a holder of a hundred arouras, who was about
7     to testify for me, he reviled greatly and said that he too would beat him to death, for which reason he did not testify for me; and because those who were about to testify for me were Egyptians, taking fright
8     they withdrew so as not to testify. I therefore beg you, O king, entreating, that I may not on account of this
9     cause be deprived of justice and lose … [to] order Diophanes the strategos to write [to Herakleodor]os, whatever … the … who built
10    this house, the brick-carriers and the carpenters … to send up before Diophanes
11    so that … in order that, this being done, having taken refuge with you, O king, the common benefactor of all, I may obtain from you your kindness.
12    Farewell.
13    To… through…
14    … child …
15    Year 1, Gorpiaios …, [Tybi 1] …
16    Tetosiris …
