[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign and consulship of our most divine and most pious
2     master Flavius Justinus the eternal Augustus and Imperator,
3     year 1, Phaophi 18, of the 15th indiction, in the city of the Oxyrhynchi.
4     To Flavius Apion, the all-praiseworthy and most magnificent, of the ordinary
5     consuls and patrician, landholder also here in the illustrious
6     city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas, servant, who both asks the formal question and
7     furnishes to his own master, the same all-praiseworthy man,
8     the action and liability, Aurelius Apaor son of Apollos, [his mother being]
9     … me, originating from the hamlet of Niketos of the Oxyrhynchite
10    nome, belonging to your Magnificence, a registered
11    cultivator of it, greeting. Since need has now arisen for the landholding
12    water-wheel under me called (that) of the Agroikika, which draws water
13    for a vineyard and for arable land, of one axle, having come up
14    to the city, I asked your Magnificence to order
15    this to be provided to me; and at once your Magnificence,
16    making provision for the maintenance of her own affairs,
17    the same axle, purchased by me in the fields, and the
18    price for it having been reckoned to me for … of the
19    fifteenth indiction, and the same axle serving
20    the irrigations for a seven-year period, the old axle having been given
21    by me to the doorkeeper, … a new, suitable
22    watering (axle), satisfactory, I received in fulfilment of all
23    the mechanical implements on this present day, which is
24    Phaophi the eighteenth of the present year 243/212 of the
25    present fifteenth indiction, of the water-supply of the crops
26    of the, God willing, first indiction. And for proof of the
27    receipt I have made this handwritten deed, which, being valid, drawn up in a single copy,
28    and, having been formally asked, I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Apaor son of Apollos, the aforesaid, have made this handwritten deed, having received the new
29    axle, and everything agrees with me as aforesaid. I, Daniel, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
30    Total: one axle.
31    Handwritten deed of Apaor son of Apollos, from the hamlet of Niketos, of the receipt of one axle.
