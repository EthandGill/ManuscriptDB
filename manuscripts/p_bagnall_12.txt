[TRANSLATION]
1     From Aphroditos to Boubas the
2     Brother, greetings. Just as I asked you about the documents, be eager for me to send [them], lord, safely. Having been urged, whatever you might find
3     With you, buy for me and
4     Send, through [our] being pressed here; for [there] is not
5     A conductor. Greet Komon greatly and share with him [that] not
6     Me [you] forget.
7     Farewell.
