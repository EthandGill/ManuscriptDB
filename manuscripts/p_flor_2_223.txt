[TRANSLATION]
1     Horion to Heroneiños, [his] dearest, greetings.
2     If you are well, provide
3     to Hatra, [the] ox-driver, from the lord's
4     account being in the authentic
5     canal, together with others, so that
6     [fodder] for his oxen; this
7     for [thus] [has] the lord, my [master]
8     Syros, commanded. I wish you to be well.
9     Year 4, Mesore 2
10    To Heroneiños from Horion
