1     … son of Gennadios, from Antaios, to Apollos son of Dioskoros. I have received and have been paid in full by you the rents of the arouras under you of unwatered land, situated
2     [in] the holding of Piase, in the plain of Phthla, on account of the assessment of the third indiction, in full.
3     The heirs of Kyros of illustrious memory, town-councillor, through me
4     Biktor son of Psaios, presbyter and steward of the Antaiopolitan affairs,
5     to Apollos son of Dioskoros. I have received from you and have been paid in full of the rents
6     of the unwatered arouras under you, being in various places in the plain
7     [of the village] of Phthla, of the holding of Piase, on account of the assessment of the third indiction, in full. And for [your]
8     [security] I have made this receipt of full payment in accordance with the lease,
9     as stated above, Mesore 3 of the same 3rd indiction. The heirs of Kyros, through me Biktor
10    [presbyter]: the receipt agrees with me as stated above.
11    [Flavi]us Ioannes the most illustrious, town-councillor of the Antaiopolites, through me Phieib
12    son of Dios, steward, to Apollos son of Dioskoros. I have received from you and have been paid in full
13    of the rents of the unwatered arouras under you, situated in the plain
14    of the village of Phthla, of the holding of Piase, in various places, I mean the arouras
15    … (called) Ermous and those called Ialemlim, on account of the assessment of the third indiction, in full.
16    And for your security I have made this receipt of full payment as stated above.
17    Mesore 3 of the same 3rd indiction. Flavius Ioanne, through me Phieib steward, the receipt agrees with me as stated above.
18    Psais son of Biktor, presbyter, having been asked, I wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters.
19    Flavius Megas the most illustrious, of the defensores of the city of Pan(opolis), through me Ioubinos
20    servant and tax-collector, to Apollos son of Dioskoros. I have received and have been paid in full
21    [from] you of the rents of the arouras under you of unwatered land, situated in the plain
22    [of the village of Phthla], of the holding of Piase, in full, in accordance with the lease, on account
23    [of the assessment] of the third indiction. And for your security I have made this receipt of
24    full payment as stated above. Mesore … of the same 3rd indiction. Flavius Megas the most illustrious
25    [of the] defensores of Pan(opolis), through Ioubinos servant, I have issued
26    the receipt and it agrees with me as stated above. Ioannes son of Hermeios from
27    Aphrodite, I wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters.
28    Written through me, Abraamios son of Apollos, oul…
29    Flavius Nemesianos the advocate, through me Menas steward, to Apollos son of Dioskoros. I have received
30    [and been] paid in full by you of the rent for the arouras under me of unwatered land, situated
31    in the plain of the village of Phthla called Piase, both in wheat and barley, in full, of the assessment
32    of the third indiction. And for your security I have made this receipt of full payment
33    as stated above. Menas the afore-written landholder: the receipt agrees with me as stated above. Aurelius
34    [Phoib]ammon, his son, having been entrusted, I wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters.
35    Flavius Kollouthos son of Ammonios, scriniarius, through me Apollos servant and steward,
36    to Apollos son of Dioskoros from Aphrodite. I have received and have been paid in full by you the rent
37    of the part under me of the unwatered arouras of the holding of Piase in the plain of the village
38    of Phthla, on behalf of the share of my father and of my mother, on account of the assessment of the third indiction, in full.
39    And for your security I have made this receipt of full payment as stated above.
40    Thoth 27 of the same 3rd indiction. Flavius Kollouthos son of Ammonios, scriniarius, through me Apollos servant: it agrees with me
41    the receipt as stated above. Aurelius Abraam son of Biktor, assistant, having been asked, I wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters, he being present with me
42    and having spoken. That is to say, moreover, also on behalf of the whole share of the heirs
43    of Aineias of blessed memory, I have received and been paid in full by you

45    Kollouthos son of Ammonios, scriniarius, through me Apollos servant: it agrees with me
