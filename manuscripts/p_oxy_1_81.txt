[TRANSLATION]
1     Silver (taxes) of the metropolis.
2     To Aurelius Dios, also called Pertinax, strategos of Oxyrhynchite,
3     Aurelius Apion, (son) of Dionysius,
4     (mother) Tarmaloe, from Oxyrhynchus city, having been introduced
5     by the [amphodo]grammateus
6     of the present second year, into the tax-collection of silver (taxes) of the metropolis
7     of the same second year, I swear by the
8     Marcus Julius Philippus
9     Caesar, the lord's [fortune]
