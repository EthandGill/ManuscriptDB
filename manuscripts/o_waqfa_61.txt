1     [Property] of [the] son of Poule; from [hereditary land] in Pege Sycamineia

4     Of which [for] seeds artaba 1, remaining artabas 7
5     The half to the landholders artabas 4, measures 2
6     As of [the] 1st season, measures 2, 3
