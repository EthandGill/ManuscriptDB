[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelios Apollonios, decarch,
2     from Aurelios Sakis Maximos
3     from the village Theoxenidos of Themistos
4     division, shepherd of livestock of the most honored Aurelios Apianos. On the 19th of the present
5     month of Tybi, as I was traveling up from [a] village [with] the livestock which I had with [me] to the aforementioned village Theoxenidos, and when I arrived at the tombs of the same village Theoxenidos, [certain] criminals came upon me
6     certain criminals, whom I do not know, and they drove away from me the black donkey which I had. Wherefore
7     I submit this petition hereby making this matter clear to you, sir, so that there may remain for me
8     the right against those who will appear [as]
9     guilty. Farewell.
10    Sakis, about 35 years of age, with a scar on [his] right knee.
11    Year 2 of Emperors Caesars Publius
12    Licinnios Valerianus and Publius
13    Licinnios Valerianus Gallienus, Pious
14    Fortunate Augustuses, Tybi 22.
