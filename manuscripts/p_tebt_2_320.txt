[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Sarapio]n also called Agathos Daimon and to Serenus also called Diodoros,
2     … former gymnasiarchs, examiners,
3     [from Eu]daimon, son of Heron, grandson of Souchas, his mother being Thermou[thari]on his (i.e. Souchas's) sister, and (from) his (Eudaimon's) wife Sarapias,
4     [who is] my sister on the father's and mo[ther's side], both of us from the me[tropoli]s, registered in the amphodon of Tam[e]ia, and Sarapia[s acting] with her guardian Eud[ai]mon. Of the son Heron born to us
5     [from one another], advancing in the present 21st year
6     [of Marcus] Aurelius C[o]mm[od]us Ant[onin]us Caesar the
7     [lor]d, and being due to be examined, we have appended our proofs.
8     [Bo]th of us then registered in the [house-by-house registration of the 23rd year] of the deified Aelius
9     [Antonin]us and the 14th year of the deified Aurelius Antoninus by household
10    [registration in] the aforesaid amphodon of Tam[ei]a, having jointly registered only in the (registration) of the [1]4th year our son Heron who is now being examined;
11    [wherefore] we submit (this). I, Sarapion also called Agathos Daimon, former gymnasiarch, through
12    [Anto]ninus my secretary, have signed. Year 21 of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
13    [Antonin]us Augustus, Epeiph 7.
