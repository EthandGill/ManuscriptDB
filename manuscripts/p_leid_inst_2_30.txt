[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dionysios, ro[yal scr]ibe of the Arsinoite, division of Polemon,
2     fr[om] … [also] called He…, komogrammateus
3     [of Psinteo] …
4     In the 11th year of Anton[inus Caesar th]e l[ord] …
5     belonging to the village-scribe's office, I made an inspection of the things that are in the village-scribe's district,
6     in public places, of the trees, and finding some
7     to exist in the country, more than those in the register of such things, but others to have fallen, or to be fewer,
8     these things I indicated through the register which I submitted, among which also
9     in the aforesaid village of Psinteo, having found, dried up
10    and fallen upon the canal of Arsainios, one sycamore-fig,
11    this I indicated through the proper reports in the month Pachon.
12    But of those who made the inspection of the trees in the same 11th year,
13    at the time of the inundation having made the report, the sycamore having fallen into the aforesaid canal
14    while the waters were in flood, they having written tha[t] not …
15    … but that not even upon the canal was such a condition to be seen, [nor] as to seem a fall to have happened, they took up according to these things …
16    … [on behalf] of the sycamore, 3000 drachmas, since …
17    being s[ing]le-branched, and that one being upon the b[ank] of the can[al],
18    was swept away, having dried up, into that very [canal; and those]
19    who made the inspection, at the [time of the in]undation as is stat[ed], having arrived while it was under water [and the bank]
20    had been washed away, so that the … was not visible …
21    of the sycamore, these things they wrote. But the [sycamore] itself …
22    … lies in the canal and in the sam[e] …
23    and that this is evident from its also having been assessed [by the]
24    assessors in the same 11th year and in the 12th [year] …
