[TRANSLATION]
1     … the presbyter …
2     … his lord … of him … of the one coming …
3     for the sake of the soldiers who are under your care, and the presbyter himself
4     having been called to account to pay not only the owed
5     wages to Puibes, the soldier stationed on behalf of the people from Neson, but
6     also on behalf of those from Thelbo, and through ignorance he reported it. So when he had gone to the places he paid in full to Puibes what was owed. And I make clear that the peace-officers of Thelbo too have been paid
7     the wages customarily given to them by the people from Neson, as the countrymen affirm. And this very thing I write to your diligence,
8     so that neither may the presbyter be troubled nor indeed may you suppose that he has misrepresented their account. But if the aforesaid peace-officers of Thelbo deny that the receipt was made from
9     the countrymen, I write asking to order these men to go to them with Makarios, the soldier stationed on their behalf, so that they may convict or be convicted. For this is profitable
10    to both parties. For indeed concerning this matter the same villagers wrote to my lord, our brother,
11    … the exactor. I pray that you may be in health for many times,
12    for many times I pray,
13    my beloved, incomparable lord.
14    … to my father Fronton on the expulsion of the …
15    … speculator (military officer) …
