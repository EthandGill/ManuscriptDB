[TRANSLATION]
1     You transported into the ala on behalf of the produce of the 25th year
2     [By] name Sisoitos [son] of Phthumônthes, eight payments,
3     [They] become eight [payments]. Year 26 of Aurelius Commodus
4     Antoninus Caesar the lord, Phaôphi 11.
5     Paniskos [has] marked.
