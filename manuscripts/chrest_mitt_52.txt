[TRANSLATION]
1     To Demetrios also called Harpokration, strategos of the divisions of Themistos and Polemon of the Arsinoite (nome),
2     from Stotoetis son of Stotoetis, from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos of the division of Herakleides
3     of the Arsinoite nome. Since I have (a complaint) to make against Sotas son of Theon and his son Ammonios,
4     who are from the metropolis but reside in the hamlet called (that) of Areios, which is near the village Herakleia of the division of Themistos, who clung to (their) recklessness and violence — concerning the violence which they did to me, conspiring with one another, having taken along with them Herakleides an attendant,
5     as if on the instruction of Theon who had served as strategos of the same division — to the point that, having got me into their power, they accomplished a most criminal undertaking: to compel me, with outrages
6     and blows, to issue documents of a written sale and of a mortgage and of a loan of four hundred
7     drachmas in the name of my sister, she not having consented but indeed being absent, in the
8     name of the daughter of Sotous, Satyriaine, while she was alive — despising the condition concerning my
9     feet (i.e. my lameness), behaving insolently and treating me as if in a desert,
10    thinking that their lawless and unjust deeds would proceed for them, by the fact that no disbursement of staters had taken place; concerning which, not having kept quiet about these things, I petitioned [the former] prefect Honoratus, who subscribed to me to petition Crispus the (former) epistrategos,
11    and up to now no end has yet been put to the matter; and since this matter requires the cognizance of the most illustrious prefect Munatius Felix, I request that an equal (copy) of this be delivered through an attendant to each of them, so that they may know that they are to be present
12    at the most sacred tribunal of the prefect, wherever he holds the assize or court of the nome, I being content with this notification, my plea remaining (reserved) to me concerning
13    all the written rights which I have against them.
