[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 10, Thoth 7. Of burial
2     of ibises and hawks and
3     of the other sacred animals, under
4     Pilaios, kinsman and general
5     and Pilaias, Helen and Heracles, and Pathrus and
6     Hermius, [son] of Callios, steward
7     and overseer and Callios
8     ... of divine animals
9     Ach, [they] become Ach. Year 10, Thoth 7,
10    of the 5th year and 8th year.
