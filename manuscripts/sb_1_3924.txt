1     [Germanicus Caesar, [son] of Augustus]
2     [son, of god Augustus, grandson],
3     [proconsul says: for my]
4     [presence now already hearing]
5     requis[itions] … [of vessels]
6     and of beasts to happen and
7     tent-lodgings [for soldiers] to be seized, [hospitality] by force and
8     to be greatly disturbed, [the] private citizens,
9     [I] deemed [it] necessary to make clear, that neither a vessel by anyone
10    or beast of burden to be held [do] I wish, unless according to the [orders] of Baibius
11    my friend and secretary
12    by order, nor [should] hospitality be seized. For if it should be necessary, Baibius himself
13    from [a sense of] equality and justice, the hospitality
14    [will] distribute. And for the requisitioned vessels or teams [of beasts]
15    [the] wages to be paid according to
16    my ordinance [I] command.
17    But those objecting to the
18    secretary of mine [I wish to be] brought, who [will] either himself prevent the private citizens from being wronged [or] report to me. But beasts of burden running through the city [those who] encounter [them] by force to seize [I] forbid.
19    For this [is] already [a matter of] acknowledged
20    banditry [is the] work.
21    Germanicus Caesar, [son of] Augustus,
22    of god Augustus, grandson, proconsul
23    says: your goodwill, on the one hand,
24    which you always show whenever you see me, I accept; but the envious
25    to me and equal-to-the-gods utterances
26    of yours I utterly refuse.
27    For [they are] fitting for the savior alone
28    truly and benefactor of all
29    the human race, to my
30    father and to his mother, my
31    grandmother. And our [honors] are in subordination
32    of their divinity, so that
33    if you do not obey me, you force me
34    not often to appear to you.
