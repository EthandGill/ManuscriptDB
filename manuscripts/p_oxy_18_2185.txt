[TRANSLATION]
1     Claudius Areios, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite,
2     to Papantos and his associates,
3     grain-collectors of Pakerke eastern (district), greeting. Measure out,
4     the (officials) following along in accordance with the deposit-record, both the
5     sowing-overseers and those nominated,
6     Harpoch the royal scribe jointly so ordering,
7     to Herakleides son of Herakleides son of Ptolemaios, his mother being Diogenis daughter of Sarapi(on), of those from the city of Oxyrhynchus, about
8     … years (old), with a scar on the left shin, whom you will identify
9     at your own risk, for seed from the produce of the past year, for the sowing of the
10    current 12th year of the Emperor
11    Caesar Domitian Augustus
12    Germanicus, for which … Ammonios, village-scribe of Psobthis
13    and Perothis, (declares) that he himself cultivates,
14    about Psobthis indeed, in the eastern (district),
15    of the imperial estate of Atikrates and Sostratos, arouras 30,
16    and about Perothis, in the name of Ptolemaios
17    son of Araios, an aroura ½, and of … menes, arouras 11¼, as comes
18    to the same, of imperial revenue land from Nile-flooded (ground), arouras 41½¼ of wheat, artabas 41½¼,
19    which he shall also deposit upon the land soundly,
20    nothing being reckoned to a debt or
21    anything else whatsoever, from whom also receive the appropriate deed of hand
22    concerning his restoring afresh from new (seed)
23    together with the appropriate taxes of the land. [Year 12] of the Emperor [Caesar Do]m[itian Au][gustus Germanicus] …
