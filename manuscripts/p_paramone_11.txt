[TRANSLATION]
1     Upper toparchy: Loupos having served as cosmete and Apollonios having served as agoranome
2     Western toparchy: Serapion, also called Alexandros, having served as cosmete alone
3     Eastern toparchy: Sarapion, also called Diogenes, having served as gymnasiarch and cosmete
4     Middle toparchy: Potamon and Apion …
5     Thmoisepho toparchy: Didymos, also called Didymian, having served as high priest
6     Lower toparchy: Apollonios [son] of Ptolem[ai]os, having served as gymnasiarch and
7     [Didy]mos [hav]ing served as high priest [and] Diogenes having served as agoranome
