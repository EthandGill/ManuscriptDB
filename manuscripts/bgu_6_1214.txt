[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemaios, strategos, to the epistatai
2     of the villages in the division of Herakleides, [greeting].
3     From among the Egyptian
4     schoolmaster-scribes residing with you, those accustomed to write
5     the contracts according to the law of the land, the undersigned have been selected by
6     Pasis, the epistatês of the temples in the nome,
7     and of the others, according to the matters declared to us by Protarchos,
8     one of the friends, to be suitable
9     to serve in the established need; from whom also, having taken
10    a written declaration of the royal oath, we have given instruction that
11    they shall exact no more than the established scribal fees,
12    on any pretext whatsoever, [nor] shall they permit others
13    to retain [a portion] of this revenue, neither for the
14    royal treasury, nor [for the temple, nor] for [private] use,
15    nor shall they jointly exact along with [those who have carried out the transaction], … to [report]
16    to Protarchos; and the selected one is to be,
17    just as also those who write in the Bousirite nome,
18    they are established to write, of the sale and the deed of release of claim, twenty drachmas,
19    and of the others, ten drachmas. And in order that the private persons too,
20    having followed up about these matters, may be assessed the declared amount,
21    post a notice before the most conspicuous temples in the villages,
22    and likewise also in the places in which …
