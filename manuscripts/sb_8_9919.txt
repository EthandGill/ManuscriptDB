[TRANSLATION]
1     … [of the] of the l[and] public [of which being toward you the] landholder controlling of the
2     [of fruits until the] owed things you receive,
3     assured [for us] of the pledge; necessarily
4     [we render] the rent from joint liability
5     per year [in] Pauni month barley new pure [un]mixed sifted in the tenth measure,
6     being done of the transaction as is fitting; valid receipt and having been questioned we agreed, with each artaba being measured four choenices
7     Aur[e]lii Apollas and Chaeremon
8     and Plous we have hired the land
9     and we will render the rent from joint liability as is set forth. Aurelius Theon of Heracles I wrote on behalf of them not knowing letters.
