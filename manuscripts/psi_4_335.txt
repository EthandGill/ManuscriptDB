[TRANSLATION]
1     Aristeus to Zenon greetings. We have also written to you previously about the
2     grain becoming [available to] Aleximachos the agent, in order that you write
3     to Artemidoros to measure it out to him, and now you would do well to take care about it and write so that he measure it out to him; for he does not provide us with trivial
4     needs. But Amyntas is also eager about it.
5     Farewell.
6     Year 29, Dystros 9, Phamenoth 9.
7     Aristeus [son] of Aleximachos
8     of grain.
9     To Zenon.
