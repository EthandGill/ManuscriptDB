1     Account of the month of Hadrianos from 1 to 30.
2     Wages of men
3     … Pasemios gardener, 24 drachmas
4     [wagon-driver] 2, of Hadrianos 15 to 19
5     [5 days f]rom the five-obol [rate] make 7 drachmas [and] one obol, fodder of ox 15
6     [to] 19 each day 4 artabas make 1 artaba [and] 4
7     [in the] [same amount] of silver 31 drachmas [and] one obol, of lentil 1 artaba [and] 4
