[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Father and the Son and the [Holy] Spirit
2     It was written in the month Thoth [of the first] indiction
3     [I am] Mina, son of the blessed Paia, former scholasticus
4     [In the] 28th year, a witness, the brother of Petros, former Roman [citizen], in the village of Ermont, since I begged you [and] you gave
5     a holokottinos and a trimession, which equals 1⅓ gold nomismata, these I sent [to] the public [treasury], with God, in the will of God [I] will give
6     a stole of linen to you, concerning the assistance of my daughters, when you will give his share of trimession of gold, these equals 1⅓ nomismata towards the stole
7     of linen, and in the will of God [I] will give his share of it …
8     to you, and I will do thus, if her child does thus, and [it] is not risky for you, if not something [upon which] your authority depends; therefore I committed the guardianship to you, your authority [stands] through the Lord,
9     in any place whatsoever in which they will present her to her [heart], they will take the oath which I, Mina, the one who is first, [should] observe to be proper
10    I, John, the priest of Horite, [I sign] as witness
11    I, Anoup, the brother of Mina, former scholasticus, [I sign] as witness
12    I, Philotheos, the brother of Daniel, and Christopher, the brother of Demetrius, co-scholasticus, we are at risk [this] now, you
13    [as a] witness, [towards] the agreement of the guardianship
14    by me, Christopher, [son] of Demetrius, [I] wrote
15    the guardianship of the woman, [is established] by witnesses
