[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Philomousos agent of the most excellent
2     Aurelius Ammonion
3     To Aurelius Maximus agent greetings.
4     I received from you which also
5     I loaded in Pelusium into the ship of Heraiskus the sailor wheat
6     artabas one-hundred,
7     total arta[ba]s 100.
8     in [the consulship] Phaophi 23.
