[TRANSLATION]
1     Zosilus, scribe of farmers, to Akousilaos, grain-buyer
2     greetings. measure out to Ptolemaios, son of Horos
3     from seed-grain of wheat six fourths,
4     there become wheat 6/4. year 20, Hathur 15.
5     Onnophris, leader of farmers; authorize artabas 6/4, there become 6/4;
6     year 20, Hathur 15.
7     Marres, village-scribe; measure out
8     the aforesaid of wheat six fourths,
9     there become wheat 6/4. year 20, Hathur 15.
