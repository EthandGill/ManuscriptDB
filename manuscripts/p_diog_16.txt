[TRANSLATION]
1     … Dioskorous daughter of Hierax, a townswoman, about 30 years old, with a scar on her right eyebrow, with her guardian whom she willingly chooses, through Ammonios
2     also called Soterichos, priest and exegetes, in accordance with the official communication (chrematismos) sent by him, of which a copy is appended below — (with) Sarapion son of Heras,
3     son of Harpokration, of the Neokosmion (and) Althaieus (tribe and deme), about 39 years old, with a scar on his left cheek — acknowledges to Marcus Lucretius Diogenes, and however he is styled,
4     about twenty-three years old, with a scar on his left eyebrow, that she, the acknowledging party Dioskorous, has received from him, by hand, in silver,
5     of capital, six hundred and fifty-four drachmas, being equal, which she lent to him by public official-deed (chrematismos) completed through the same record-office (grapheion) in the past year, in Phaophi, on condition that, in place of the interest on these
6     at a drachma (per mina) and of food and clothing, he has, for service, his slave-woman Pasistilla also called Couneinis,
7     and that Dioskorous shall not, nor those acting for her, proceed against Lucretius Diogenes nor against those
8     acting for him, neither concerning the things which she received of the capital, of the silver six hundred and fifty-four drachmas,
9     nor concerning the said service; but neither concerning any other matter whatsoever, written or unwritten,
10    from former times up to the present day, in any way. And the copy of the official communication (chrematismos)
11    is this: Ammonios also called Soterichos, priest, exegetes and archiprytanis, councillor, to those who manage the archives, greeting. A petition having been given to me in the name of Dioskorous daughter of Hierax, a townswoman, a copy
12    is sent to you through Harpalos, attendant of the magistracy, so that, if absolutely nothing is contrary (to it), you may jointly transact
13    with her, concerning the underlying business, her guardian being registered, as she requested, Sarapion son of Heras, son of Sarapion.
14    Attend to this as is fitting. Farewell. Year 16 of Lucius Septimius Severus Pius Pertinax and Marcus Aurelius
15    Antoninus Pius, the Augusti, and Publius Septimius Geta Caesar Augustus, Hathyr 30. To Ammonios also called Soterichos,
16    priest (and) exegetes, from Dioskorous daughter of Hierax, a townswoman, with her guardian whom she willingly chooses, through you, Sarapion son of Heras, son of Sarapion, of the Neokosmion (and) Althaieus (tribe and deme). Receiving back from Marcus Lucretius Diogenes, and however he is styled, of silver
17    six hundred and fifty-four drachmas owed to me by public official-deed completed through the local
18    record-office in the past 15th year, on condition that, in place of the interest on these, his slave-woman Pasistilla also called
19    Couneinis should serve; and, issuing a receipt, I am hindered, not having my lawful registered guardian
20    because I am without a husband and have no father present nor father's father, nor have I a brother or son. Wherefore, choosing
21    through you the aforesaid Sarapion, I submit (this) and request that it be sent to those who manage the archives, that they jointly transact with me as I issue the receipt of the aforesaid drachmas, with my guardian Sarapion, without hindrance.
22    I have read it. I, Harpalos, prytanic and exegetic attendant, have transferred (the copy). The same date.
