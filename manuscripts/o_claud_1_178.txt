[TRANSLATION]
1     Julius Capito to Gaius the
2     very dear [friend], greetings. I want you to know that I gave you
3     the three-obol [piece] in order that you make
4     a key-ornament and a raven
5     having a nail, and if not
6     the key-ornament, [you] did not send. Therefore I ask you
7     send to me the raven
8     and [the] nail.
