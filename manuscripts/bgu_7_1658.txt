[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Serenus, son of Publius
2     veteran of those honorably discharged and as I am styled
3     to Aurelius Diogenes, [son] of Turbo, from the village Philadelphia, greetings. I have received
4     from you which you owed me in writing
5     drachmas one hundred sixty,
6     the total is drachmas 160, full, and nothing to you
7     do I charge. Year 14 of Emperor Caesar
8     Marcus Aurelius Severus Alexander
