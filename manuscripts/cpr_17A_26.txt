[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the masters] my brothers Demetrius and
2     [A]delphius of Canopus, greetings.
3     [Certain ones] having accused you to
4     [the virtue] of my lord the governor
5     [as if] contrary to the order of affairs
6     having distributed to them gold,
7     his virtue ordered you
8     to be present and for this reason it was necessary
9     to write to you so that during the night you might meet at Antinous
10    [according to] the things commanded by
11    [virtue] of his. I pray you to be strong
12    for many times, brothers.
