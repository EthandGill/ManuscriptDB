[TRANSLATION]
1   Aurelius Theon alias Neikammon,
2   sacred victor from his ephebate,
3   having served as chief-priest of the sacred
4   ecumenical great syn-
5   od, to Aurelius Ammon and
6   however he is styled, his son, greeting.
7   I appoint you as my representative in place of myself,
8   as I am traveling abroad to the most illust-
9   rious Alexandria, so that you
10   obtain a deposition of Agathos
11   Daimon who became assistant
12   of my father and of me
13   ... through memoranda
14   of the strategos or also through whatever oth-
15   er witnesses he acknowledged,
16   ... the exegetes and
17   ... and Diogenes, coun-
18   cilors, and Ophellios the agorano-
19   mos and Aphynchios the sacred victor
20   and Theon, (as to) who Euporos may be
21   and of what mother, and
22   thus to make an attestation
23   and to have the witnesses themselves sub-
24   scribe and send (it) to me,
25   so that I may not seem to be disadvantaged
26   in my rights. Valid is the deed of appoint-
27   ment, being in my own hand, and
28   on being formally questioned I gave my acknowledgment.
29   Year 12 of our lord
30   Gallienus Augustus,
31   Pharmouthi 9
