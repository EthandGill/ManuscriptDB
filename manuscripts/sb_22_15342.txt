[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the twentieth year of Imperator Caesar
2     Nerva Trajan, Best, Augustus,
3     Germanicus, Dacicus, Parthicus.
4     Pauni 30. [He/she] erased through Heron, tax-collector,
5     Pakebkis of Paopais, [son] of Pakebkios,
6     mother Thaubastis, his own account of enrolled [status],
7     of priests of the twentieth year of Teptuneis,
8     drachmas fifty-two; there are drachmas 52, registered additionally: 3 obol, half-obol, [and] symbolic three-obol.
