[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Ak[am]mon, [son of] Pasutos
2     and Sois, [son of] Pamun(i)us, and Sarapammon
3     Pekys(i)us, and Paesis, [son of] Pammon
4     and Timotheos, [son of] Silvanus, all of them [public] functionaries of the village of Magdolum of Meira
5     To Aurelius Nearchides, [son of] Nearchides, [having served as] archon
6     To the [councilor] of Hermes, of the most splendid city, greetings; we declare
7     [that we] have received and been paid
8     from you the tax for the distribution of Magdolum
9     of [the] B[u]koloi, grain in artabae eight
10    [they] amount [to] artabae 8; the receipt [is] valid; and when questioned we declare [the same]; Phaophi 25, [8th] indiction
11    The aforementioned [people] have been paid by Nearchides the aforementioned tax as stated
12    Aurelius Sarapammon, village-scribe
13    I wrote documents on their behalf, [they] not knowing [how to write]
