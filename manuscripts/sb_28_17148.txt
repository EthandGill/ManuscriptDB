[TRANSLATION]
1     I heard that to afflict on account of the man Hattok
2     in (the) month, the Alexandrian, I informed him
3     (to) you so that it might be, (he) wishes, his own, which is needed
4     or his own, simply, he is to serve
5     to you, these, which you wish, only, do
6     your goods, well, do not despise
7     and not the one who seizes, his possessions, they are, and in
8     the lord, victor, the examiner, you do not perceive, and some, hundred, to you, from them
9     NOT
10    to the most splendid Senuthios, the notary, Taurinos
