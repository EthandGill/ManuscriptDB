1     Duplicate. 76
2     To [Theon], strategos of the Arsinoite nome, divisions of Themistos and Polemon,
3     [from] Soterichos son of Neilos and Didymos son of Enation, the two of them, and the remaining elders managing
4     [the affairs of] the village-secretaryship of the village of Dionysias.
5     [With reference to] a letter written by Eglektos, procurator of the
6     [lord Caesar], of which there is a copy:
7     [Eglektos] to Theon, strategos of the Arsinoite, divisions of Themistos and Polemon,
8     greeting.
9     Of the petition given to me by Auneous son of Peteuris, having noted the copy, I sent it to you. He says that
10    Valerius Niger had become a lessee of the imperial estates together with Petronius his brother, and that, Petronius having died, Niger
11    owes to pay into the imperial account the debts owed both from the lease
12    and from another lease, a fish-receiving station. See therefore whether anything is owed in good faith
13    to the imperial account and exact it. I pray for your good health.
14    Year [9] of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Hadrianus 30.
