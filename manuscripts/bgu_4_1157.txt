[TRANSLATION]
1   To Pro[tarchos]
2   f[rom Ammonios son of …, and from Pnepheros son of …,]
3   [and Piesies son of …, and Petearenphois son of P]iesies.
4   [Whereas, by three agreements completed through the tribunal — by one,] made [in the]
5   four[th (year) of Ca]esar, P[auni(?) …,] Pnepheros and
6   Piesies, and further Petearenphois son of Piesies, [have acknowledged receipt] from Ammonios of one thousand
7   and thirty-two silver drachmas
8   at interest; and by the second, Ammonios has acknowledged that, on recovering these and the
9   in[terest,]
10   he will credit them to the three — an agreement of hire-purchase of the timber-carry-
11   ing barge belonging to him, of thirty-five cubits, eleven cubits in beam; and by the third,
12   made in the nineteenth year of Caesar, Epeiph, that Ammonios, having received
13   from
14   Pnepheros and Piesies, toward the aforesaid principal, three hun-
15   dred silver drachmas, has credited them to Petearenphois according to the agreement approved by them concerning hire-pur-
16   chase of … a third share of the said barge — now we agree
17   with one another on these terms: Ammonios, having recovered from Pnepheros and Piesies the remain(ing) […]
18   of the loan — seven hundred and thirty-two silver drachmas — and the accrued
19   interest, shall render void … the said agreements of the loan [and] of the hire-purchase,
20   and forthwith has leased to them, Pnepheros and [Pie]sies,
21   for a per(iod) of
22   fifty years from Pharmouthi of the present twentieth year of Caesar, the remaining
23   [two] shares [of the same barge,] … to work it …
24   … to bring in, and whatever they may wish to contribute; and forthwith […]
25   neither of them, nor any other on his behalf, shall proceed about […]
26   … and the interest; and he shall guarantee the two shares of the [bar]ge […]
27   […] … to the two, at once, unimpeded; or — the agreed terms remaining nonetheless valid —
28   [he shall pay back the] principal with half again, [and the damages and expenses,]
29   [as if by legal judgment. …]
