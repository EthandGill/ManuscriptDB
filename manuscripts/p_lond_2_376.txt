[TRANSLATION]
1     [From] Didymos, having served as agoranomos, son of Papion
2     [having served as cosmetes], chosen by Theodo[ros]
3     [of the strategos] of the [Ar]sinoan Herakleidan division to perform enumeration of livestock of the division instead of
4     the customary one being appointed beforehand by
5     the most excellent epistrategos from
6     [another] nome enumeration of sheep and
7     [goats] and camels and other which came to be
8     [both by] me and by the same strategos
9     Theo[doro]s and Timag[e]nes, the royal secretary
10    of Arsinoan [Herak]leidan division and of those under the same
11    strategos Theodoros having been chosen for the countryside
12    on the 20th of the present month Mecheir
13    of the current 22nd year; and it is
14    Hekysis of Horos: camels 9, foals 3
