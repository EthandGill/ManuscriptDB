1     (hand 3) Recei(ved) year 3, Hathyr 9, through Ptol(lion).
2     (hand 1) Isidora to Asklepiades her brother, greeting
3     and good health [con]tinual[ly. ..]k[.....]me.... you had written;
4     I have re[ceived ... bed]spreads (peristromata)
5     vermilion-coloured [...].....onia 10, sky-blue 3,
6     bedsprea[ds] of sykanionia(?) 2. And concerning
7     Paniskos the brother, Noumenios has sent
8     Philon his brother to him
9     in [haste] concerning the administration. So you and
10    [Haramotes] play the man and bring in
11    the [price of] lentils (and) emmer. And the rest; and take care of yourself,
12    that you may be well.
13    Farewell. Year 3, Hathyr 3.

15    [if it se]ems (good), proceed until I write.

17    and Ptollion brings to you a defence(?)....
