[TRANSLATION]
1     4 auth(entic?)
2     In the eighteenth year of Imperator
3     Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus,
4     in the month Kaisareios, on the 4th intercalary day, in Theogonis of the division of Polemon of the
5     Arsinoite nome. Onnophris son of Pakebkis, priest of the temple of the god Kronos at Teptynis, about sixty years old, with a scar on the left wrist, leased to Taorseus daughter of Kronion, about thirty-eight years old, without distinguishing mark,
6     with her guardian, her kinsman Kollouthos son of Paophis, about sixty years old, with a scar on the right knee, from the public sacred lands around Teptynis registered to Onnophris, the two arouras in a single sealed parcel
7     or however many they may be, in the so-called
8     Neophyton plot. This lease is for
9     another nineteen years after the period
10    of which Onnophris had previously made to
11    Taorseus of other leases, which shall also remain valid, in return for all the public (dues) and additional-measures and freight-charges of the expediting-service on behalf of these,
12    which Taorseus shall measure out each year into the
13    public (treasury); and (Onnophris shall guarantee) that Onnophris will guarantee (it) with every guarantee, because the same Onnophris has been persuaded by
14    Taorseus by the inducement which he has received. Under-writer: Pakebkis the son, his father writing slowly.
15    … I, Onnophris son of Pakebkis, have leased the two arouras for another nineteen years. I, Onnophris son of Pakebkis, have leased to Taorseus the two arouras for another nineteen years
16    after the periods of the three leases, in return for the public (dues), and I have been persuaded [as]
17    is set out above. I, Pakebkis, wrote (this),
18    my father writing slowly.
19    Taorseus daughter of Kronion, with her guardian [her kinsman]
20    Kollouthos son of Paophis … …
21    … …
22    … It has been registered through the record-office at Theogonis.
