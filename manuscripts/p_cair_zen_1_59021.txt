[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apol[lon]ios, greeting from Demetrios.
2     It is well if you yourself are in good health and
3     your other affairs are to your mind.
4     And I too, just as you wrote to me,
5     am giving attention to it; and I have received
6     5 myriads of gold, Z (zeta?), and having processed (it)
7     I delivered (it back). We would have received even
8     many times more, but as I both
9     wrote to you before, the foreigners
10    who sail in and the merchants and the
11    factors and others bring both the
12    local coinage of full weight and
13    the trichrysa (triple-gold pieces), so that they may have new (coin) for themselves, according to the decree which orders us to take and process (it), Philaretos not allowing me to receive (it), having no (way)
14    to whom we should make the referral
15    about these matters, and we are compe[lle]d
16    not to receive [t]hese, and the me[n] are vexed, neither at
17    our banks nor onto our scales (our) receiving (it), nor (being) able to send (it) into the country
18    for the cargoes, but they say they hold
19    the gold idle and are damaged not a
20    little, having had (it) sent for from abroad
21    and not being able readily to dispose of (it) even to others at a lesser price.
22    And all those throughout the city use the worn-out gold with difficulty.
23    For none of them has (anywhere) where, having made the referral and adding something, he shall recover either fine gold or silver
24    in exchange for it. For now, these things being such, I see also the revenues of the king being damaged not a little. I have therefore written these things to you so that you may know, and if it seems good to you, to the
25    king you may write about these matters, and (write) to me
26    to whom I should make the referral about these matters
27    let me make (it). For I suppose it to be advantageous
28    i[f] even from the country abroad gold
29    is imported in as great quantity as possible, and the coinage o[f] t[h]e [k]ing is fine and
30    new continually, no expense
31    arising for him. For about
32    certain (matters), as they treat us, it was not well
33    to write, but as soon as you are present you will hear … Write to me about these matters so that I may act thus.
34    Farewell.
35    Year 28, Gorpiaios 15.
36    From Demetrios. To Apollonios.
