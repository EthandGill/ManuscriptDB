1     Ptolemaios who has [received]
2     the sixth of the fruit-crops of [Apollonopolis]
3     for the 46th year [Ha]remsyni
4     Pachumios and the associates
5     Greetings. You have assessed the proper tax
6     of what belongs to you
7     palm-grove near Apollo's city in copper
8     for silver: 400 become 400.
9     Year 46, Mesore 30.
