[TRANSLATION]
1   Petemonthes son of Pamonthes paid, for poll-tax
2   of year 12, ten drachmas, and bath-tax 1 (dr.) 1½ ob. — total 11 dr. 1½ ob. — and
3   surcharges at an obol (and) half-obol. Year 12 of Tiberius Claudius
4   Caesar <Augustus Germanicus>
5   Imperator, month Germanikeios 30.
6   (2nd hand) Bassos son of Dec(mus), banker, I have signed.
