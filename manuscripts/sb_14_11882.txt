1     to my [master], as truly in all things most honored brother
2     … John, Dorotheos.
3     Having heard, master, concerning the things about Aphyggius [I] was not little troubled, although indeed long before having this fear. Therefore, master, of your
4     alone presence it is to restore my judgment and in the
5     same to be us from the establishment of your perfection and
6     for indeed to me what happened has come and [is] happening. Many also this very thing say to me has happened. But in the power of the god who governed me
7     we are confident. Even though having nothing privately but the foreign stands
8     I beseech therefore, master, so that you might accomplish the possible things to complete for me the twenty gold-[pieces] which with him …
