[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 7, Pauni 28, they have measured
2     into the great treasury in Zeus-city
3     on behalf of the place, for the 7th year,
4     of Patseôs and Epainês
5     of wheat 33 1/2 1/3, [which] total 33 1/2 1/3.
6     Asklepiadis.
