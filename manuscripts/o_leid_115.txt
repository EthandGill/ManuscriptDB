[TRANSLATION]
1     Psansnos and Kassianos, collectors
2     of silver [taxes] of Sentfous [and] Harpmoites. We received for
3     poll-tax and bath-tax from [the] North Market of year 20, eight soiled drachmas,
4     total soiled drachmas 8. Year 20 of Hadrian the lord
5     Phamenoth 7.
6     Psansnos, I have signed.
7     another [entry], Pharmouthi 10, in the name of [...]Pikotes for poll-tax and bath-tax
8     from [the] North Market of year 20, four soiled drachmas, total soiled drachmas 4.
9     Psansnos, I have signed.
