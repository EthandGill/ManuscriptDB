[TRANSLATION]
1     (Symbol) 643 (= invocation of God)
2     [In the reig]n of our most divine and most pious master, greatest benefactor, Flavius Tiberius Mauricius
3     [the eternal] Augustus and Emperor, year 4, in the consulship of the same our most pious master, year 3,
4     Mecheir 13 of the 4th indiction, year 264 and 231.
5     [Aurelius] Petrus, son of Pamtas, his mother being Martha, originating from the village of Tammon of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
6     [I dispos]e of the following matters set down below, reckoning with human (frailty) and the common end of life
7     [and my] departure, but still more being of sound and right mind and bearing the reckoning of my own affairs well and rightly,
8     [and] having my mind sound and entire, unimpaired in my senses, knowing that it is proper
9     … of this my last will, so as not to leave to those after me occasion or quarrel
10    … for the sake of my very small inheritance, dividing (it), having come forward to the village-scribe
11    [having approach]ed him, I dictated to him this my testament or will, which also as a codicil
12    [so as to] hold by mode the force of being valid and unshaken before every magistracy and authority by the
13    [civi]c and praetorian and peregrine law, for the sake of the main provision.
