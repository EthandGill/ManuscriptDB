r.1    τῇ εὐγενεστάτῃ Ἡρᾶι Φοιβάμμωνος ἀπὸ Ἑρμοῦ πόλεως

[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most well-born Hera, [daughter] of Phoibammon, from the city of Hermes,
2     Petros, accountant of the division of Dioscorus. I have been paid by you
3     for the fifth part of coins 4 minus karats of the [coins]
4     owed to me from the late
5     your brother Sarapionis for the public [taxes]
6     of him for the past times
7     until the 11th indiction and the same itself, that is gold
8     coin 1 minus karats 6, and I have no account against you
9     concerning this matter, and for your security this
10    I have made for you the receipt with signature
11    of mine as stated above.
