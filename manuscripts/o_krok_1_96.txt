[TRANSLATION]
1     Kassianus to Anthestius the
2     brother very many greetings. Brother, do not
3     blame me; vegetables are not there
4     here; and Priscus too will tell you; it was not
5     for a great matter to send.
6     Above all write to me
7     about your welfare
8     and if anything newer
9     you hear about your successors write to me.
10    about which if you should wish
11    write to me and I will gladly do [so]. Be well.
