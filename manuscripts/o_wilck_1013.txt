[TRANSLATION]
1     Anoubion and Onnophris, collectors of the grain-tax of the islands,
2     to Decimus the elder (son of) Heraklas. You supplied
3     to the cavalrymen of the Herakl(ian) squadron, of barley, in lieu of (green) fodder,
4     for the produce of year 3, of barley, a third and a twelfth of an artaba,
5     total barley ⅓ 1/12 of an artaba. Anoub(ion).
6     I, Onnophris, have signed.
