[TRANSLATION]
1     Perdikkas to Zenon greetings. [Just] as you wrote, I questioned Aristomachos
2     the brother of Asteropaeus, with Thra[son] from you present, concerning
3     Stachys the boy, and he said [that] Asteropaeus still last year was not
4     in the nome, nor to know where he is, but [said] he has the boy, and wherever
5     he perceives him to be, [to] report [to] us. I have therefore written to you so that you might know.
6     Farewell. Year 33, Mecheir 7.
7     To Zenon.
