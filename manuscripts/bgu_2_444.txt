1     [In the … year] of [Imperator] Caesar [N]erva Trajan Augustus Germanicus, in the month
2     … in Karanis of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge
3     [to one another Petesouchos], son of Papontos, about thirty years old, with a scar under the first finger
4     [of his hand … and Thae]sis, daughter of Pasion, about thirty-two years old, without distinguishing mark, of the city, with as guardian her
5     … son of Phaesis, about …5 years old, with a scar on the right forearm, that they have divided
6     [between themselves from] now for all time the (property) belonging to them, each as he holds (it),
7     … of the olive-grove in the katoikic class, [aroura] one, or however much it may be, in three plots [around Psenarpsenesis, and from which] they have made between themselves, by a contented agreed division, that there falls to the lot, on the one hand, of
8     [Petesouchos], for the half part falling to him, the whole third plot, whose neighbors on the south
9     [is that which] Thaesis [has obtained] from the first plot, a part; on the north the olive-grove of Aphrodisias daughter of Apollodoros and of Charidemos
10    [son of Onnoph]ris; on the west the olive-grove [of the] same Charidemos; on the east the olive-grove of Therm[o]uthis; and the remainder
11    [for completion of] the half part falling to him, from the part toward the west of the first plot,
12    [whose neighbors on the] south a canal, on the north the olive-grove of Charidemos son of [Onno]phris, toward the west that of Thermouthis, an olive-grove,
13    [toward the east the part which] Thaesis [has obtained]; and (there falls to the lot) of Thaesis, for the part falling to her too, the whole
14    [second plot, whose neighbors on the south] a canal, on the north the olive-grove of Aphrodisias the aforewritten, on the west the olive-grove of Thermouthis,
15    [on the east the] olive-grove of …, and [the] remainder for completion of the half part falling to her, from the
16    [part toward the east] of the first plot, whose neighbors on the south a canal, on the north that which Petesouchos has obtained, the
17    [third plot … of the half] part, and the olive-grove of Charidemos; on the west that part which Petesouchos has obtained;
18    [on the east the olive-grove of Thermouthis. The (matters)] according to the division have been done by allotment, all the ground remaining (in common);
19    … and … and the (one) of the same (parties) in the (matters) according to this
