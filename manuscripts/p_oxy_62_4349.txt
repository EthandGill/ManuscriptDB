[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius
2     Dexicrates the most glorious, Mesore
3     27, at the beginning of the 13th [indiction.]
4     Aurelius John, son of Theodoros, whose mother is
5     Erebekka, with [a guarantor]
6     who also takes responsibility for him as one who gives and fulfils the
7     matter
8     set forth below.
9     A debt to me, Aurelius Phoibammon, son of Danielios,
10    miller, both of us subscribing below, originating from this
11    illustrious
12    city of the Oxyrhynchites, to Victor, servant of the most glorious count
13    Eudaimon, landowner in the same city of the Oxyrhynchites,
14    greeting.
15    I, the aforesaid John, acknowledge that I have received from
16    you,
17    on account of the price of the grain listed below, according to what
18    was agreed upon henceforth, three gold solidi less six carats
19    by the private standard,
20    that is, of gold
21    3 solidi less carats
22    6 by the private standard,
23    on condition that I, or also I the guarantor of this man,
24    being liable on his behalf for payment concerning these, shall give to you, of grain,
25    thirteen and a half artabas by the just measure at the
26    new moon of the following month
27    Thoth of the coming year 181=150, from the new
28    crops of the thirteenth
29    indiction. But if we should not repay the same grain
30    — either I the
31    debtor or also I the guarantor of this man — we acknowledge
32    from [mutual]
33    guarantee to give at the appointed term the same [amount of]
34    gold solidi,]
35    three less carats six of the same, of me
36    subscribed,
37    the bond of John with
38    the guarantor Phoibammon …
