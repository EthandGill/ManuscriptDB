[TRANSLATION]
1     To Pasigenes son of Sabinus also called Ninnos, kosmetes,
2     from Paopis and Orseus, both sons of Kronion
3     son of Orsenouphis, from Tebtynis. We wish to lease from you
4     for four years from the incoming nineteenth year, around the village of Theogonis, of a holding, arouras
5     thirteen, or [howe]ver many they may [be in] 2 par[c]els;
6     in the [1st and 3rd year], five arouras
7     for the sowing of [wheat, and th]e remaining 8 arouras of fodder
8     for the gra[zing of sh]eep, of rent for the
9     two-year (period) [each year], together with the seed we shall receive, 5 artabas of wheat,
10    [of the wh]ole [of wheat, artabas] 60, and of money-rent 160 drachmas; but the [2]nd
11    a[nd] 4th year, [8] arouras for the sowing of wheat, but the [remaining]
12    5 arouras for fodder for cutting, of rent
13    together with the seed we shall receive, 8 artabas of wheat, of the whole each year of wheat, artabas
14    96, [an]d of money-rent 100 drachmas, and of extra-due for the
15    four-year (period) each year [a] cock, of 4 drachmas, all
16    by your own six-choinix mea[sure, free of deduction and exempt from risk],
17    the [public] freight-charges being upon us, but the conveyance of the rents (being) upon
18    you; and [after the time] we shall hand over the arouras
19    clean [of] couch-grass and all moisture, except
20    rush (and) reed. In the 18th year of Antoninus, the 2nd of the intercalary days.
