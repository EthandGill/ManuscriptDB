[TRANSLATION]
1   Gemellus to Apollinarius,
2   his most honoured bro-
3   ther, gree[ti]ngs.
4   Very great thanks to you, broth-
5   er, for having taken thought for me;
6   your recommendation helped me
7   much. And already it had been a care
8   to your most ex-
9   cellent friend Roul-
10   lius — knowing the goodwill you have to-
11   ward me — to fill up your ab-
12   sence:
13   to Aemilianus
14   unhesitatingly and zeal-
15   ously he recommended <me> as your
16   kinsman, whom he received most gladly.
17   And I ask, brother,
18   that you give thanks in return to
19   Rufus, if you write to him.
20   I sent you the let-
21   ters of Aemilianus and Ru-
22   fus and Chariton. Write
23   to me about your welfare
24   and (about) what you wish. Greet
25   all your (folk), with whom also
26   fare well, brother.
27   To Julius Apollinarius ☓, veteran, from Gemellus.
