[TRANSLATION]
1     … I, Tribunos, son of the blessed Nilus [from] the city of Arsinoites, to you, my full brother
2     To Palatinus, I am ready with God in the month Thoth of the third indiction entering with God to nominate myself
3     [on the][joint] our receipts [from] equal portion and jointly for us to receive the taxes and the sale from equal portion; but if at the appointed time I do not nominate myself on the joint receipts, so that [I] not receive from the taxes, [namely] from the sale. But if it should happen …
4     … commonly for us to give account. Written in the month Pharmouthi 12 [?] indiction
