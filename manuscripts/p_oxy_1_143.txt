2     [i]ndiction, in the month of Tybi 15 of the same 13 indiction, of gold on private scale
3     excluding alloy, eighty coins besides four hundred forty carats,
4     of alloy forty carats, total of gold on private scale with alloy eighty coins besides ninety-six carats only.
5     In the year [numerals], month of Tybi 15, indiction 13. Philoxenus, banker.
6     Receipt of the banker of coins [numerals] besides [numerals] 1/2 with alloy of the [numerals].
7     excluding alloy, coins sixty besides three hundred carats, of alloy thirty carats, total with alloy coins sixty besides three hundred thirty carats
8     excluding alloy, coins eighty-nine besides five hundred twenty-eight and 1/4 carats, of alloy forty-four and 1/2 carats, total with alloy coins eighty-nine besides [numerals] 1/2 [and] 1/4 carats.
