[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Cleopatra and Ptolemaios, the Mother-loving
2     Saviour gods, in the eighth year, when the priest who is in Alexandria
3     of Alexander and of the Saviour gods and of the Brother gods and of the [Benefactor gods and of the]
4     Father-loving gods and of the Manifest gods and of the god Eupator and of the god
5     Philometor and of the god New Philopator and of the god Benefactor and of the
6     Mother-loving Saviour gods (was so-and-so); the sacred colt [of Isis], the great mother of the gods,
7     the stephanephoros of Queen Cleopatra, the goddess Philometor Soteira
8     Dikaiosyne Nikephoros; the athlophoros of Berenike Euergetis; the phosphoros of Queen Cleopatra, the goddess Philometor Soteira Dikaiosyne
9     Nikephoros; the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos; the priestess of Queen Cleopatra, the goddess Philometor Soteira Dikaiosyne Nikephoros; the priestess
10    of Arsinoe Philopator — those (priesthoods) being and existing in Alexandria;
11    in the month Dystros, on the twenty-ninth, (which is) Tybi the twenty-ninth, in Tenis
12    also called Akoris, of the Mochite district, in the Hermopolite nome.
13    Dionysios son of Apollonios, a Persian, file-closer (rear-guard), lent to Dionysios
14    son of Kephalas, a Persian of the epigone, and to his mother Sarapias also
15    called Senabellis, daughter of Heliodoros also called Herieus, a Persian woman, with as guardian her
16    own son Dionysios, also the aforementioned, of solid wheat
17    one hundred and sixty-six artabas, two-thirds, which they have measured out from him from his house, the interest being a third of each artaba of wheat, that is in artabas
18    eighty-three and a third; and let the borrowers repay all the wheat, two hundred
19    and fifty artabas, to Dionysios or to those
20    acting for him, in the month Loios, which is Pauni of the eighth year, wheat
21    new, solid, unadulterated, by the measure of the bronze (standard), delivered
22    to the harbour at Akoris, at their own expense, without
23    suit and judgment and any malicious trickery; and if they do not repay
24    or do not act according to what is written, let the borrowers pay to
25    Dionysios or to those acting for him, as the value of each artaba, of bronze
26    three thousand drachmas, and none the less (the debt itself); the borrowers themselves being
27    sureties for one another for the loan, for the repayment, and for all the terms of the contract;
28    and let the right of execution belong to Dionysios for the
29    loan, both from Dionysios himself and from Sarapias the aforementioned, and from one and from both, and from whichever of them
30    he may choose, and from all their property, as if by judgment; let this contract be valid wherever produced.
31    Witnesses: Ptolemaios son of Anaxagoras, [a Milesian; Agenor son of Barkaios,]
32    a Lacedaemonian; Arimmas son of Dionysios, a Charistereios; [Heroides son of Herakleides,]
33    an Alexandrian; Eumenes son of Polykrates, a Persian; [Apollonios son of …, a Persian,]
34    of those enrolled in (the cavalry-troop named after) Cleopatra. [Keeper of the contract (syngraphophylax): Ptolemaios.]
35    [Dionysios son of] Kephalas, a Persian of the epigone, [and his mother]
36    Sarapias also called Senabellis, daughter of Heliodoros also called Herieus, a Persian woman,
37    with as guardian her aforementioned son Dionysios
38    the aforementioned: we have the two hundred fifty artabas
39    of wheat as is stated above and we will do according to the other things
40    written, and we have given Ptolemaios as keeper (of the contract). I, Dionysios
41    the aforementioned, have written also on behalf of Sarapias my mother, because
42    she does not know letters.
43    I, Ptolemaios, hold (the contract) as keeper.
44    Year 8, Tybi 29: loan of Dionysios to Dionysios and Sarapias, of 250 artabas of wheat;
45    let the borrower repay (it) in Pauni of the 8th year, to Akoris, by the measure of the bronze (standard);
46    penalty per artaba, of bronze, 3000 drachmas; keeper of the contract: Ptolemaios.
47    (Witnesses:) Dionysios, Sarapias, Agenor, Eumenes,
48    Dionysios, Ptolemaios, Heroides, Apollonios,
49    Arimmas.
50    Loan of Dionysios [to Dionysios]
51    and Sarapias, of 250 artabas of wheat.
52    and Sarapias, of 250 artabas of wheat.
