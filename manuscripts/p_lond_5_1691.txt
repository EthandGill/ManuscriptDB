[TRANSLATION]
1     999 (chmg).
2     After the consulship of the Flavii Orestes and Lampadios, the most glorious,
3     Phamenoth 12, of the tenth indiction.
4     To Aurelius Apollos son of Dioskoros son of Psimanobet, from the village
5     of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, Aurelius Kyriakos
6     son of Andreas, his mother being Maria, coppersmith, from the same village
7     of Aphrodite, greeting. I acknowledge that I have leased from you,
8     for whatever time you wish from today into the time hereafter,
9     the whole ancestral share belonging to you of the
10    whole walled enclosure in the rank of a farmstead, facing the south-west, with every right belonging to it,
11    according to your ancestral rights, on the east of my
12    dwelling-house, just as your parents possessed it,
13    with a share of the common well there and of the other appurtenances,
14    [situate]d in the same village of Aphrodite on the street
15    [of] the house of the old man Psimanobet your forefather,
16    [on condition] that I have this for my every need, for whatever
17    [u]se I may wish, and that I give to you by way of rent each year
18    three thousand talents [of sil]ver without dispute. The lease
19    is valid and secure, and, having been questioned, I acknowledged. Aurelius
20    Kyriakos son of Andreas the aforesaid, have leased as set forth above.
21    … of Kyriakos son of Andreas the cop[persmith].
