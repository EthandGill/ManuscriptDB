[TRANSLATION]
1     Sertoris to Iu[lius] the Bithynian
2     very many greetings; I h[eard] that you were sick; I pray [for] you to be healthy.
3     You know also that we have nothing here
4     to send to you immediately. [With] [your] brother Longinus together we live. You have said to me concerning
5     your chariot-officer
6     to Zeus-city and I have not believed you. From when I went up, with Longinus
7     I lodge together. Against us
8     the man wages war,
9     your komono[p]laris,
10    bearing false witness.
11    Greet Klemes.
12    Longinus greets you. Greet the
13    fellow soldiers all.
