[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 41, Pauni 9
2     Agreement
3     Year 41, Pauni 9, in the city of Crocodiles of the Pathyrite, under Aniketos the agoranomos.
4     An agreement which, willingly and having agreed, Horos son of Imouthos, a Persian of [the household of] Boethos, [about] age 50, … middle dark-complexioned, ears …
5     and having transferred, according to which he agrees to have made over to Petosires son of Horos his own [son]
6     middle dark-complexioned, tanned, long-faced, straight-browed, with a scar on the right wrist, from [the property belonging to him]
7     in the field lying to the south of the city of Crocodiles, from 16 arourae or however much the fourth part is, which is [4 arourae, neighbors]
8     [of the whole] land, to the south land of Erienouphos of Portites and his brothers; to the north land …
