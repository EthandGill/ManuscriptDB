[TRANSLATION]
1     [So that] our most magnificent brotherhood may learn that yesterday, [whi]ch was the fourth of the present month, I received [let]ters
2     [from] my lord the all-praiseworthy duke through Sergios the soldier sent con[cerning the fled cau]l[kers,]
3     [(stating) that, if] you should be [foun]d [to have let pass even on]e [and o]nly (one), you should pay 1 solidus on his behalf, [a]nd you would be endangered in your soul.
4     And if there are foreign caulk[e]rs i[n y]our pagarchy, seize these too and send (them) under wooden bonds.
5     Copy of the sigillion sent to me f[rom][ ][m]y lord the most glorious amir:
6     In the name of God, Iord[an]es to all the pagarchs of the Thebaid: since the caulkers who labor
7     on the boats of Babylon [h]ave fled, and we have charged our deputy not to let even one
8     and only caulker (escape), and not to send this one to us, but whoever holds a caulker or hides (one), let him give
9     1 solidus, if he is able—we having charged him to show them the present sigillion. Henceforth, whoever does not surrender
10    himself and send you each caulker who is in his administration, after he has seen and (it has) been read
11    to him, this very sigillion, having let pass even one and only (one), we do not accept his property in place of
12    his soul. Henceforth, [as] has been [sa]id, gather together the caulkers and to our condemned (?) vessels
13    send (them). And so that [yo]u not doubt the present sigillion I have used (it). And following
14    upon my present letter [I] have [appended] the sigillion sent to me from the same most glorious amir, …
15    to my [god-guarded][ lord and brother] Papas the pagarch of Apollonos Ano, Hellad[ios]
