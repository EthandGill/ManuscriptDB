[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonius, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2     from Petaus, comogrammateus of Ptolemais Hormou and of other villages.
3     For the purpose of providing supplies for the visit of the
4     most illustrious governor Longaeus Rufus, so that
5     there may be a full supply for those wishing to buy in accordance
6     with the instructions, I give the persons written below,
7     being well-off and suitable. They are:
8     For bread:
9     Dioscorus son of Ischyrion son of Dioscorus.
10    Ischyrion son of Ammonius son of Heuremon, of the mother Heracleia.
11    Maron son of Marinus son of Maron.
12    Deios son of Neseus son of Ischyrion.
13    For the wine-sellers:
14    Pantonymus son of Ischyrion son of Pantonymus.
15    Aphrodeisius son of Aphrodeisius son of Aphrodeisius.
16    Ischyrion son of Heraklas son of Ischyrion.
17    For the oil-sellers:
18    Euangelos son of Heron son of Euangelos.
19    Apynchis son of Peteeus son of Ischyrion.
20    For the provisions (for guests):
21    Ischyrion son of Neseus son of Orsenouphis.
22    Ischyrion son of Orsenouphis son of Philammon, of the mother Thenparotis.
23    For the hay:
24    Ischyrion son of Athas son of Maron.
25    Pnepheros son of Neseus, of the mother Demos.
26    For the barley:
27    Maron son of Heron son of Maron.
28    Heracles son of Ischyrion son of Herakleus, of the mother Thaesis.
29    For the wood:
30    Ischyrion son of Hierakion son of Phanias.
31    Peebaus son of Hatres son of Onnophris.
32    For the vegetables: Akiaris son of Syrus.
33    Karos son of Papontos.
34    I, Petaus, have submitted (this).
35    The 25th year of Marcus Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Augustus
36    Pius, Tybi 15.
