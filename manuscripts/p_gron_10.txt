[TRANSLATION]
1     … and the …
2     and forthwith the half of all these (I order) to be set aside [for you, the holy church,]
3     after my death, as I said before, and the other half [part for Kosmilla, my]
4     wife, except for the slaves, both [the male and the] female [of mine, all of which]
5     I wish [and order] to be set free, with no one gainsaying or laying claim,
6     and [afterwards the] same half part of the same property of mine passing to you, the same
7     holy church, after the death of the same wife of mine.
8     This I ordain, being lord of all the things belonging to me,
9     and no one being able to annul this gift. And since my most genuine sister Seneponychos requested me to cede to her three arouras of dry land to the west of the estate,
10    this I order to take effect immediately after my death,
11    so that she has the same three arouras together with the third part of the island
12    and of the place Pkro, which was long ago ceded to her without writing, no one with any other purpose
13    or by any other document being able to overturn the present intention, but
14    all my property, as I said before, being administered in this manner — that is, the half being set aside for you the church immediately, [and]
15    my freedwoman having the other half part and enjoying its fruits until
16    her death; thereafter it shall pass up to you the church, the same
17    wife of mine not being able, after my death, any longer to give away any portion of the same
18    property. And let the gift be valid and secure, produced everywhere.
19    [And] on being formally questioned I have agreed. Written in duplicate of the same type, one for the church,
20    [and the other for my] wife Kosmilla. And I say and order and grant, after the
21    [death] of my aforesaid wife, the half part of the house
22    on the lane … the (part) granted to me by my father, to my sister
23    Sophrone and to the heirs of my sister Theotime, according to the half
24    part, and concerning this, on being formally questioned, I have agreed. Flavius Abraam, of the praepositi,
25    the aforesaid, have set down this document of my intention, that is, of my gift, and I consent to all the things
26    written as is set forth. Flavius L…p…chem, of the praepositi, was present and witness.
27    Aurelius Ktistes son of Pleneios, former magistrate, witness. Flavius Theodosios son of Eulogios,
28    advocate, was present and join in witnessing. Flavius Heliodoros, of the tribunes, witness.
29    Flavius Syros, of the tribunes, witness. Flavius Makarios, of the tribunes, witness.
