1     There measured in, Lysimachos
2     son of Apollonios, into the
3     granary of the administration (dioikesis), for the public(?) (levy),
4     of the produce of year 31, … of Caesar, of wheat, artabas
5     seventeen and a half and a third,
6     likewise Mesore 21, of wheat one artaba and two-thirds,
7     total wheat 19½ artabas.
8     Year 31 of Caesar, Mesore 21.
