[TRANSLATION]
1     After I had made the previous let[ter], there came anothe[r] courier (beredarios) [with le]tters of our master the all-praiseworthy amir (amiras),
2     in which he wro[t]e blaming m[e because] it was learned … [con]cerning the canal of Latopolis, that up to now nothing worthy of mention
3     has been done on its work. And he ordered m[e] to send a notary of mine who ought to rem[ai]n for [its dre]dging, and
4     to seek out the muster-roll (adnumen)
5     of the workmen each day, and to tig[hten the] mat[t]er of it, whe[ther for the cl]eansing or for the dredging of the
6     [re]ma[inder] for ea[c]h (one), so [that the work] may be comple[ted wit]hin ten
7     days or fifteen. And I was compelled to disregard
8     my own least matter, and to send John m[y] notary, on account of the un[av]oidable order of our master the all-praiseworthy
9     amir. And it is necessary for you, as he wrote ordering m[e], to labor either for the cl[ea]nsing or for the dredging of the remainder
10    of the new (canal). For the rest, if you wish with haste to be allowed to (go out fr]om the (works) there and to co[m]e to [your] own works at once, learn [your sh]a[re]
11    whether of the ol[d] or of the new, and com[plete the works] and dep[a]rt. For we cannot dis[regard the]
12    [orders of our master ] … Pachon 9, indiction 1[2] [ ]
13    To the in all things circumspect and most honored brother Papas, pagarch of Apollonos Ano, Elias, notary.
