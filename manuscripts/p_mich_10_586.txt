[TRANSLATION]
1     six, a scar …
2     … [Persians of the epigone], sureties for [one anoth]er for payment, in the property belonging to Herak[leides]
3     in the said village, a farmstead … and in it a mill,
4     a Theban (mill) with grinding-tables and handle, and one mortar and one oven,
5     one. The lease (is) for two years from the aforesaid time,
6     for bread-baking, if the lessees wish, on condition that the lessees pay to Herakleides on account of rent for the
7     whole (period) each year, of the aforesaid silver, eighty drachmas,
8     eighty, without any deduction of any reckoning. Let it not be permitted
9     to the lessees to abandon the lease, but let the lessees also pay the
10    indicated rent to
11    Herakleides in four equal instalments, in Tybi and Pharmouthi, Pauni, Epeiph, always on the twentieth, per instalment, of silver
12    twenty drachmas, the right of execution belonging to Herakleides both from
13    the lessees and from all their possessions, as if by legal judgment, the lease being guaranteed
14    by Herakleides. Subscriber for the lessees: Diodoros son of Ptolemaios, a scar on the right foot. Petesouchos
15    and Sochotes, the two sons of Petesouchos, Persians of the
16    epigone, sureties for one another for payment, have taken the lease on all the aforesaid terms. Diodoros son of Ptolemaios wrote on their behalf
17    [because] they do not [kno]w letters.
