[TRANSLATION]
1     Before all things I do reverence to
2     you and I greet [your]
3     God-guarded lordshi[p]. [The]n,
4     Victor having departed … m …
5     and he brought two hundred and twenty-two
6     artabas of barley and seventy-two artabas
7     of wheat and of wine 297
8     kouri,
9     and twelve sheep — but bad ones,
10    since a man cannot eat them —
11    and six piglets and twelve linen-cloths
12    and thirty-nine fowls, and
13    Victor said, "I cannot stay
14    here," and because of his saying "I cannot,"
15    and six coins, [and]
16    two flasks of radish-oil and five xestia of Spanos and 200 eggs
17    and two and a half kouri of honey and the bit of pepper,
18    to settle (it) — behold, I have sent him to you,
19    and your little horses that are there. One
20    of them, behold, it is four months
21    since it died, and another one cannot
22    labour at all; it has a month of days as of today.
23    And behold then, I have sent the said
24    Victor so that he may give it to the …
25    and bring it here together with also other
26    thirty artabas of barley.
27    These you ought to send, and if
28    I have need of other fodder-chaff, I will write to you
29    also about this. And the six coins
30    which you sent for the wage of the grooms
31    and the freight of camels and of others and
32    for the vegetables for the bikla — I know
33    that they are not sufficient. And unless I write to yo[u]
34    then, even (though) your servant-lads come,
35    do not listen to them and send
36    anything whatever there, until, as has been said,
37    I write to you. For barley and wine
38    and fowls and the whole expense
39    I know that it suffices for the two months,
40    but as for the fodder and the chaff and
41    the wage of the grooms,
42    I do not know whether they suffice or do not suffice.
43    And I have given an oath to Victor that
44    he should come up and come to me. And on account of the
45    purification (-offering) I received it and I thank you
46    for this. And the supply that you sent
47    here, the half of it I took for the
48    exchange, and the other half I put
49    into the harbour until the rising
50    of the waters, and by means of small boats
51    I am taking it up to the monastery.
52    And whatever you sent for the exchange, set in order
53    for me into one account, the what and what you sent,
54    and send it to me, since the cellarer
55    of (the monastery of) Herakleos brought four
56    months' supply with him and stole
57    them, and except for two months' supply
58    he left nothing. And unless I write to you
59    and seal my letter,
60    send nothing there, if you trust me
61    about this. And of her health
62    write to me.
63    … To the [most illustr]ious, worthy of honour, lord Marinos, scholasticus, from Reme [the saddler] …
64    … es of the saddl…
65    kos … phantia to others, of Pinarachthis.
