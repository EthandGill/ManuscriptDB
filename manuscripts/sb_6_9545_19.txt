[TRANSLATION]
1     Julius Sabinus and those with him, overseers of the sacred
2     gate of Syene, through Palachenius, assistant. Patepoueris registered
3     (son) of Papremeithos, mother Thinpa[p]remeithis, for the poll-tax of the 19th year
4     of Hadrian Caesar of the lord, impure drachmas eight, (which) comes to drachmas 8.
5     Year 20 of Hadrian Caesar of the lord, Pharmouthi 23.
