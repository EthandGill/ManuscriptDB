[TRANSLATION]
1   Petemen[ophis] son of Pamonthes paid, for dike-tax of year 5,
2   six drachmas, four obols; bath-tax four obols, a half-obol — total 7 dr. 2½ ob. — and surcharges <at an obol (and) half-obol>
3   Year 6 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar <Augustus>
4   Germanicus Imperator, Phaophi 18.
5   Pikos son of Kephalos.
