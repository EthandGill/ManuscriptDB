[TRANSLATION]
1     The eighteenth year of Imperator Caesar Trajanus
2     Hadrianus Augustus, Pharmouthi 2, at Tebtunis of the Polemon division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge
3     Sansneus son of Pasytmis, aged about fifty-six, a scar on the left shin, and Nepheros son of Herakles, aged about forty-five, a scar on the left forearm, the two of them to Deios son of Zeuxis son of Deios,
4     of the Sosikosmian (tribe) also called Althaian (deme), aged about twenty-seven, a scar on the
5     second finger of the left hand, Sansneus on the one part that Sansneus
6     will pay to Deios the rents of the present eighteenth year
7     of (the land) which this man farms near Theogonis, eight arourae,
8     free of risk and free of deduction, and (the wheat) which the same Sansneus owes
9     to Nemesas son of S…yrion under an agreement for a loan of wheat,
10    six and two-thirds artabas, in the month Pauni of the present eighteenth year,
11    without delay, at the threshing-floor of Theogonis; and Nepheros on the other part that he guarantees
12    Sansneus … of the rents and of the loan,
13    and that Sansneus, having paid these, shall go out from the
14    farming (of the land) from the coming nineteenth year, handing over
15    … the rest in accordance with the lease, and that
16    [Deios], holding fast (to it) and leasing it out to others, shall not bring a charge against Sansneus concerning the fodder which he cut on one aroura
17    … of the arourae … through the lease
18    … the present eighteenth year. Sansneus son of Pasytmis and Nepheros son of Herakles, Sansneus
19    [on the one part] of necessity shall pay to Deios, free of risk
