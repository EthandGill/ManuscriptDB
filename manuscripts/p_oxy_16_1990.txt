[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master,
2     the greatest benefactor Flavius Mauricius the New Tiberius, the
3     eternal Augustus and Emperor, year 9,
4     in the consulship of the same most pious master of ours, year 8,
5     Tybi 17, of the 9th indiction.
6     To Flavia Praeiecta and Apion her son,
7     the most eminent heirs of him of glorious
8     memory Apion the former patrician, landholders
9     here too in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas
10    the servant who puts the question and procures
11    for his own masters, the same all-praiseworthy
12    men, the action and obligation: Aurelius
13    Sarmates son of Phoibammon, his mother Anna,
14    originating from the estate of Parthenias belonging
15    to your eminence, of the Oxyrhynchite nome, registered
16    cultivator of it, greeting. A need having now arisen
17    for the irrigation-machine under me belonging to the estate, called
18    "Within the Same Estate", which draws water for a vineyard
19    and arable land, of one axle, I, having come up to the
20    city, petitioned your eminence through those
21    pertaining to her, that you should order to be provided
22    to me the said axle. And immediately, taking thought
23    for the maintenance of her own affairs,
24    this axle purchased by me in the fields, and the
25    price for it reckoned to me among my own
26    receipts — a new, suitable, well-pleasing water-drawing axle —
27    I received for the replenishment of all the machine's
28    implements on the present day, which is Tybi
29    sixteen of the current 9th indiction, for the water-supply of the crops of the 10th, with God,
30    indiction. And as proof of the receipt
31    I have made this acknowledgment in writing, which, being valid,
32    written in a single copy, and on being questioned I assented. Aurelius
33    Sarmates the aforesaid, I have made this
34    written acknowledgment of the said one axle as
35    is stated above. I, Anastasios, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate,
36    being so.
37    Written acknowledgment of Sarmates son of Phoibammon from the estate of Parthenias,
38    of one axle.
