[TRANSLATION]
1     From Apianus.
2     And if anyone sends back the very shortest, he ought to send back [with] letters and make clear
3     what was sent back through whom. But what
4     you sent back was not so much that both man and donkey could be incapacitated through four cartuls of rotten figs. But it appeared from the badness and dryness and thirst of the figs the of the field
5     neglect. But concerning these things we will sometime draw up an account
6     toward them. And he from Euhemeria also sent another back with few things. And you both are able through one to send back, having reported one to one.
7     To Heroneinus, manager.
8     Year 3, Epeiph 24
9     To Heroneinus, manager.
