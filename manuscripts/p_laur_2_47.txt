1     I-entreat our good master [by] God, help!

3     Pebichis and the Small Lane, those from Talao and the estate Tkout
4     and those from village Tholtheos and Pmochios Gole
5     [and] Loustou Alichsenenei with others and their […] and …

7     entirely to-take and the appurtenances and the […] through the […] of-the house of Arsantus of Oxyrhynchites [we] will-make through the fields.

9     your slave.
