1     [to m]y lord brother Augarus of Aphynchis
2     many greetings.
3     [before] all things I pray you to be whole with the lord god. But see, my lord
4     [my, do] not neglect … […] me quickly
5     [send]. For [it is] days [to] throw
6     [the] boats into [the] river, so that we may bring down the boat to Lo […]
7     … and I inquired if […]

9     until it comes to me. But see [that] not
10    you withhold two with you there
11    […] for expense I have and […] to arrange marks. I greet the brother
12    Alexander and the brother Macro[n]
13    [and] … and Issak and the whole house of you
14    [by] name. To be well I pray you for many times.
