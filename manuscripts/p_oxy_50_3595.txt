[TRANSLATION]
1     To the Aur[eliae Le]ontarous and Plousia, and as you are designated, through Aur[elius] …odorus, guardian, from Aurelius Paesis son of Hephaistas, [his mother] being Thaisous, residing in the village of Senepta,
2     a po[tter] of wine-jars. Of my own free will I undertake to lease
3     for a period of two years from the present month Thoth of the present
4     7th year the (workshop) belonging to you in the great farmstead of the property near Senepta, a pottery of wine-jars, with its vaults
5     and kiln and potter's stone and the other equipment, on condition that each year I mould for you and fire
6     and bake and pitch empty (vessels), the so-called Oxyrhynchite four-choes (jars), to the number of fifteen thousand, double-jars one hundred fifty, two-choes (jars) one hundred fifty, you providing porous clay
7     and sandy and black clay, and at the kiln
8     the sufficient fuel and water for the cistern
9     and for the pitching, of pitch by weight, by the measure of Aline,
10    at twenty-six talents for the (one) myriad; and I providing for myself the sufficient moulders and assistants
11    and stokers, and receiving as wage, for the single-jars only,
12    at thirty-two drachmas per hundred, and as an extra (allowance) for the myriad two keramia of wine
13    and two keramia of vinegar. And the wages collected,
14    four thousand eight hundred drachmas, I shall receive each year in these instalments: from Thoth to Pachon four hundred drachmas per month, Pauni and Epeiph for the firing five hundred drachmas per month, Mesore the remaining
15    two hundred drachmas. And if, beyond the aforesaid number, I mould other empty (vessels) and you have need of them, it shall be permitted to you to take them, I receiving from you
16    the equal wage and the pitch and the rest in conformity with the aforesaid number. And the undertaking being guaranteed to me,
17    I shall deliver the aforesaid empty (vessels) on the cooling-floors of the same pottery, from the winter moulding, well fired and pitched from base to lips, not leaking, apart from those needing treatment and the defective, each four-choes (jar) holding up to the lip twenty Maximianic kotylai; and at the end of the period I shall deliver the same pottery clean of ash and potsherds,
18    the execution being effected as is fitting, the account remaining
19    concerning whatever I may be shown to owe. The undertaking is valid, and on being questioned I acknowledged. Year 7
20    of the Imperator Caesar Marcus Antonius Gordianus
21    Pius Felix Augustus, Thoth 7.
22    I, Aurelius Paesis son of Hepha[istas], have leased the pottery and I shall do the moulding of the aforesaid
23    empty (vessels), fifteen thousand, of double-jars one hundred fifty, of two-choes (jars) one hundred fifty, at the above wage [and] extras, and I shall deliver as set forth, an[d on being questi]oned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Theon also called As[klepiades, wro]te on his behalf, since he does not know letters, [the account remaining] as above shown. …
