[TRANSLATION]
1     To the right(s) of the holy monastery of this northern rock of the city of the Antinoeans, formerly of Ap[a S]abinus a[n]d [A]pa
2     Athanasius, through you Kollouthos most reverent presbyter and superior of the same holy rock — Aurelius
3     Pheous son of Horos, his mother being Sophia, a farmer originating from the village of Thyneos of the Hermopolite nome,
4     [being present] in this city of the Antinoeans. I acknowledge through this my written security [that I owe] you and am indebted, for the arrears of the rents of the crops of the third indiction, of gold solid[i] …
5     … [le]ss six carats each, by the public standard, makes of gold solidi … less … carats, by the public standard, an[d] these
6     I am ready to fur[nish]
7     you on the new-moon day of the month Thoth of the pre[sen]t third indiction, beyond doubt; and for your sec[urity]
8     this security I have made you, with the sub[scrip]tion of the one subscribing on my behalf as is set forth above. Aurelius Ph[eous]
9     son of Horos, the farmer, the aforesaid, this security is agreeable to me [and I will pa]y the solid[i] within the appoint[ed time as is set forth above. Aurelius] …
10    son of I[si]doros, from the city of Antinoos, having been requested, wrote on [his behalf, he] [be]ing [illiterate.] It was written, Pachon 24, of indiction 3 …
