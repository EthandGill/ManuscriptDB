1     Mesore 10. Heron and his fellow corn-officers of Hiera
2     Karanis delivered, through Papees son of Isidoros, for the second new
3     indiction, on behalf of the villagers, of clean wheat two
4     and a half and a third and a twelfth artabas, 2 1/2 1/3 1/12 artabas only. Souchidas, I have signed.
5     Kyrillos, I have signed.
6     And on the 12th likewise the same men, through Papees son of Isidoros, for the second
7     new indiction, on behalf of the villagers of Hiera Karanis, of clean wheat
8     a third of an artaba, 1/3 of an artaba only. Souchidas, I have signed. Kyrillos, I have signed.
9     Mesore 10. Pannous and his fellow corn-officers of Ptolemais Karanis
10    through Papees son of Isidoros, for the second new indiction,
11    on behalf of the villagers, of clean wheat two artabas, 2 artabas only.
12    Souchidas, I have signed. Kyrillos, I have signed.
13    And on the 12th likewise the same men, through P[ap]ees son of Isidoros, for the second
14    new indiction, on behalf of the villagers of Ptolemais Karanis,
15    of clean wheat two artabas, 2 artabas only. Souchidas, I have signed. Kyrillos, I have signed.
16    Mesore 10. Apollonios and his fellow corn-officers of Kainon Karanis, through
17    Papees son of Isidoros, of clean wheat half and a third
18    and a twelfth of an artaba, 1/2 1/3 1/12 of an artaba only. Souchidas, I have signed. Kyrillos, I have signed.
19    Kyrillos, I have signed.
20    And on [the] 12th likewise the same men, through Papees son of Isidoros, for the second new
21    indiction, on behalf of the villagers of Karanis, of clean wheat
22    a third and a twelfth of an artaba, 1/3 1/12 of an artaba only. Souchidas, I have signed.
