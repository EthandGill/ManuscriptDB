[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a nursing-contract. Year 16 of Imperator Caesar
2     Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus,
3     in the month Sebastos, the 30th, in Ptolemais Euergetis of the
4     Arsinoite nome. Helene daughter of Heron, a Persian woman,
5     [about] 40 [years old], with a scar on the right forearm, with as guardian her brother
6     Deidas son of Deidas, about 20 years old, with a scar on the forehead on the left,
7     agrees with Sarapion son of Asklepiades, Phylaxithalasseios also called
8     Althaieus, about 31 years old, without distinguishing mark, to nurse and suckle
9     at her own (home) with her own milk for two years from the aforesaid
10    time, and to tend for another six months, the child born
11    from the female slave Tyche belonging to Sarapion himself,
12    a female slave-child whose name is Korinthia, receiving
13    from the contracting party, both for the milk-nursing
14    per month six drachmas and two kotylai of oil, and yearly at the Amesysia (festival) a keramion of wine for libation and four fowls, and for the six-month tending-period per month the half … [of silver] …; therefore let it not be permitted
15    to Helene [within] the time to cast off to Sarapion the slave-child, nor to suckle alongside
16    another, nor to spoil the milk, nor anything else
17    to do disadvantageously to him, but rather to take the
18    fitting care of it both by night
19    [and by day], … upon this in every respect.
20    [And if she] cast (it) off or transgress anything else of the aforesaid,
21    [she shall pay] him at once both whatever she has received and the
22    monies with the half as much again, and the damages double, and a penalty
23    of one hundred drachmas of silver, and to the public treasury the equal amount.
24    Subscriber for Helene and Deidas: Deios son of Deios, about 60 years old, with a scar on the left eyebrow.
25    … son of Heron son of Heron, through me it was submitted.
26    Year 10(?) of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Pauni 4.
