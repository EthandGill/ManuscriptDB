[TRANSLATION]
1   Year 26, Hathyr 14. Paid:
2   Petechnout( ) son of Ammonios, for the survey-tax
3   on the palm-grove of the produce of year 25, for the account of Ammonios
4   son of Psentouoris: the quarter, drachmas
5   two, total 2 dr., through me, Amenothes.
6   On the same day he paid, for the quarter-tax
7   on the palm-grove of the produce of year 25, for the account of Ammonios, Psen-
8   touoris: one drachma, three obols,
9   a half-obol — total 1 dr. 3½ ob. — through me,
10   Amenothes, assistant.
