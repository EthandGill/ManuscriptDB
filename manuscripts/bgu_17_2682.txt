[TRANSLATION]
1     In (the year) after the consulship of our master Flavius Ze[no]
2     the eternal Augustus, Emperor, for the [3rd] (time), Thoth … 5 of the in[diction].
3     To Flavius Theodoros, son of Theodoros, the venerable senator of Hermopolis the most illustrious, from Aurelius Papnouthios
4     son of Horos, his mother being Eus, from the village of Phbu of the Hermopolite nome. I acknowledge of my own free will and choice
5     that I have leased from your Wondrousness
6     for a four-year period, from the crops of the prosperous
7     sixth indiction, the one share belonging to your Excellence
8     of a vineyard plot of arouras, however many there are,
9     in cultivation together with all the plants and palm-trees, situated
10    in the plain of the village of Sesiu of the same nome, for manual
11    vine-working labour and weeding and … [and]
12    clearing and all care, on condition that you,
13    the landowner, receive the half-quarter share of all the
14    wine produced and of the [fruit of the] various plants and palm-trees, and that I,
15    the vine-worker, receive the other quarter share in return for
16    the labours and expenses I undertake … on condition that you,
17    the landowner, receive from the common jar, on the account of the first-vat, one hundred …; and at the time of the vintage one piglet
18    worth six thousand [talents of silver], and a dish
19    full of [food] worth four thousand [tale]nts of silver, and … [with] the other vine-workers, and
20    of oil … one Syrian sextarius and of scarce oil half a sextarius
21    and of soft loaves half an artaba, on condition that I dig over
22    the [plot] a [four]th (part) each year. And I am ready to perform the corvée-labours
23    … with my fellow vine-workers,
24    the embankment-works … . The lease is binding and secure, and on being questioned
25    I have acknowledged. [Aurelius] Papnouthios son of Horos, the aforesaid, have lea[s]ed [a]s s[et forth]. Aurelius Her[mi]nos son of Palladios, from Hermopolis, having been asked,
26    [wrote for him the le]tt[er]s because he does not know letters. Aur[eli]us
27    … ō … ō … on, from Hermopolis, I witness the lea[se, having hea]r[d it fr]o[m the] one who made it. Aurelius Phoibammon son of Herakleios, from Hermopolis, I witness
28    [the lease, having hea]rd it from the one who made it.
29    … Through me, Antinoos, it was written, kt…ithei.
30    Lease of Papnouthios son of Horos [from the village of Phbu of the Hermopolite]
