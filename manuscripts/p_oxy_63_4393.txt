r.2    παρὰ Αὐρηλίας Ἀηοῦ ἀπὸ τῆς αὐτῆς πόλεως.

r.13   Αὐρηλία Ἀηοῦ ἐπιδέδωκα.

[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Flavius Phoibammon], serving as father of the [city] of [Oxyrhynchus],
2     from Aurelia Ahous, from the same city.
3     My husband has departed from human affairs, owing
4     debts. And my son Aeion, [having taken] a wife to [himself], and
5     from his father's prosperity of [affairs],
6     having found, is plotting to burden me, the [mother], with a share
7     of the debt, claiming also of my parental property.
8     house. Wherefore I [beseech] your
9     firmness so that you might order this one to present himself,
10    since indeed he has even acquired for himself possessions,
11    having also his infant brothers suffering together, and to hear us and provide a
12    just form. For I appear on [his] behalf in the bank having mortgaged my daughter for four [golden] coins.
13    I, Aurelia Ahous, have submitted [this].
