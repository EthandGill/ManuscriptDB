[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most honored and beloved father Papnoutios,
2     Athanasios, greeting in the Lord God.
3     May the Almighty God and his Christ grant that your piety
4     remain with us a long time and remember us in
5     your prayers; for while your holiness continues (to do) this
6     it shall be for us to be in health everywhere. I beseech you, then, more frequently to remember us; for the prayers offered by you are taken up because of your holy love, and as you may ask
7     in your holy prayers, it shall be well with us. I shall give thanks to the Righteous One,
8     [being persuaded] that you make remembrance of us everywhere; for indeed I know that
9     you love us; but most of all my concern is for Didyme and her
10    mother; for Didyme … and my mother is unwell
11    … so there is a very great struggle concerning me, suffering
12    … and being most feeble; but I believe in the Savior of all.
13    And passing our time in these sicknesses, we are glad that, through
14    your care, it has come about that you sent to us the good son Horion.
15    Theodosios, …sth…, Antiochos, Didyme, my mother, all
16    those of our household, greatly do obeisance to you and address you,
17    most honored beloved father. May divine providence keep you in health for the longest time, ever mindful of us,
18    beloved, most honored.
19    To the most honored and beloved father Papnoutios,
20    Athanasios, in the Lord God.
