[TRANSLATION]
1     [It has been read.] Year 32, Choiak 7.
2     To Harkoiphis, controller of the granary of Agema.
3     If the agents of Leonides, the one appointed over the grain-collection (sitologia), measure out, in accordance with the
4     order from Sarapion the (officer) in charge of the revenues, for seed and loans for the
5     sowing for the 32nd year, to the farmers from (the land) of Queen Kolasouchis, for that (land) which
6     they cultivate around the village among the revenue-lands formerly (belonging to) the land of the king's mother,
7     besides what was previously sent, seventy further artabas of lentils — which have also been measured out by
8     Theris the (king's) kinsman and strategos and (officer) in charge of the revenues and private (accounts),
9     a written oath by the king having first been taken from them concerning the sowing of
10    the land registered around [the] village and all land that is in the revenue-account, and (concerning)
11    leaving none unsown and delivering from the new (crop), together with the
12    rents of the land, without deduction of any allowance, they being mutually sureties for payment, together with the additional measures, with the consent of Harimphis the agent of
13    … the topogrammateus, seventy artabas of lentils,
14    [appor]tioned to the … (farmers ?), seventy artabas of lentils, then a receipt
15    [and counter-receipt make out with them as is fitting.]
