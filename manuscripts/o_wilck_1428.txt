[TRANSLATION]
1     Psansnos and Kasianos, collectors of money-taxes,
2     (to) Horos (son of) Phatres (son of) Bechis. We have, for embankment-tax, of the fifth part,
3     year 20, impure one drachma 2½ obols, and for
4     bath-tax, impure 4½ obols, 2 chalkoi. Year 21 of Hadrian
5     the lord, Phaophi 6.
6     (2nd hand?) Kasianos, through Loukas (his) son,
7     2 dr. 1 obol, 2 chalkoi.
