[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our masters
2     Honorius for the 13th time and Theodosius for the 10th, the
3     eternal Augusti, Epeiph 30, at the beginning of the seventh
4     indiction. Aurelius Lucius (son of) Hermes,
5     his mother Aronas, from the village of Terythis
6     of the Upper Kynopolite nome, to Au[relius]
7     Pasalymios (son of) Papnouthios, monk, from the same
8     village, greeting. I acknowledge that I owe you
9     and have received from you for my own an[d]
10    necessary need two gold, tested, imperial, full-weight solidi,
11    that is 2 gold solidi, on condition that I, out of these,
12    will give you in tow (flax) at the prevailing
13    price of the future, at the season of the
14    shaking-out of the eighth indiction, the on[e (solidus)]
15    … ou, and the other one gold solidus I will give you at the festival of the new moon of the same eighth indiction;
16    and if the aforesaid term passes and I do not repay you, interest I will
17    furnish you, for each solidus per each year, in silver
18    four hundred and eighty
19    myriads, until the repayment of the aforesaid
20    principal, without any dispute or delay, the execution being yours against both [m]e and against all my property of every kind, just as by judgment. The [bo]nd is valid and se[cure,] a single (copy) written, and having been formally questioned I ackn[owl]edged. Aurelius Lu[cius]
21    (son of) Hermes the afor[e]said have received and owe
22    the two gold [so]lidi and will r[epa]y as stated above. Aurelius Flavianus
23    (son of) Elias from the village of Thmoinpella, having been requested,
24    wrote o[n his beh]alf, as he does not know letters.
25    Handwritten bond of Lucius (son of) Hermes, 2 gold solidi.
