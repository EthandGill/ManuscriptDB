1     Longeinos to Ptolem[aeus] the [brother]
2     many greetings.
3     First of all I pray [for] you to be well, and I do your obeisance before the lord Sarapis. Know [that] Apollinaris
4     has not given me except only 500 drachmas. Since then ... [you] had written, [you] will therefore do well by sending my grain [items] with
5     the ... [remaining] items from last year and [with] the difference of them, and the
6     [items] from the year-before-last with the difference of them. [D]o not delay the harvest, [but] the newly accruing ... beneficial ...
7     so that I too might be treated kindly. From my own [property]
8     apart from me, when I too had need, more than the price ... you will therefore enclose [the] whole into my house so that by god [arriving]
9     I might find them together. The works carefully ... and [all] let it become as you promised me, and of the ... works carefully [be] managed ... to Capito[n]
10    give over to Ptolemaeus.
11    from Longeinos Clemens, veteran.
