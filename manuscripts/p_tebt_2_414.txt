[TRANSLATION]
1   Receive from Tephersais fifty
2   dried figs.
3   Thenpetsokis to Thenapynchis
4   her sister, very many greet[ings].
5   Before all I pray that you are in
6   health, and your children an[d]
7   Pasis the little chief. I sent you
8   by Protas 50 dried figs;
9   had I not long been ill, I would have sent
10   to you (before); and if I do nicely, I will
11   send your daughter a kotyle of iris-perfume.
12   You will gi[ve] Tephersais the loom
13   and the …(?) and the [……]
14   of the weaver’s reeds
15   and the …(?) and the mortar
16   and the two kneading-troughs and the bas-
17   kets of the papers and the little
18   …(?) and the cup and the
19   lamp-stand and the little basket with what
20   is in it at the bottom, and the child’s feed-
21   er and the big case.
22   Kotos shall give Tephersais the
23   box that I lent him,
24   for it does not belong to the brother of
25   your mother. I greet you
26   and your brothers. And let the daugh-
27   ter of Kephalas give the measure
28   to Tephersais; let her sell it and send
29   me the money, since I am coming.
30   I greet Aphrodite
31   our mother. The wine-press is
32   Agathangelos’, so let it be given to the
33   children. You will speak to the wife of the
34   tinsmith Ameimon about
35   your son. You will give the wooden
36   stool and the little door and the little
37   lamp.
38   Del[ive]r to Thenapynchis
39   the wife of the potter.
