[TRANSLATION]
1     Asclepiades to Serapion
2     to the friend greetings most abundantly.
3     as sent to me Charis of
4     Pobleius, and if you have the
5     record, I am sending the
6     letter to you, so that
7     well then if you are able, send [it]
8     to me; but if you yourself do not have [it], buy [it] for me, and I will send to you immediately the bronze. And if you have malbothri[o]n, give to me …
9     I pray that you fare well.
