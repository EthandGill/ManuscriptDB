1     [In year 2 of Emperor Caesar Marcus Julius Phi]lippus
2     [Pius Felix Augustus, Thoth 12, outside the] suburbs. Petosiris son of Petechon, his mother being Senanouphis, and Petechon
3     [his bro]ther, his mother being Timouthis, undertakers [from] the city of the [Hi]bites,
4     to the Kysite Aurelius Marianus son of Paapis, (acknowledge) that they have appointed him,
5     when he sails down to Alexandria, to make public before the chief-justice
6     the bond of the gift made to them, of which (the following) is a copy:
7     Petechon son of Petosiris son of Katamersis, his mother being Asklatarion, undertaker from the city of the Hibites, to Petosiris son of Petechon, his mother being Senanouphis, and to Petechon his brother, his mother being Timouthis, from the same (city),
8     my sons, greeting: I acknowledge that I give to you, by gift eternal and irrevocable, for the sake of goodwill and affection, from now for all time,
