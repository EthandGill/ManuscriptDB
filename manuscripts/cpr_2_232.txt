[TRANSLATION]
1     Your brothers at the water [are] daring to go down; the lord their god, the prostates of the eme Mena, behold indeed anyone the proskynesis you [their] god, the divine lord, anyone the divine lord, knowing that the courage [by] word the divine lord came forth to the honor of the isou three gold [pieces] to the old papa Ioule [he gave it] saying god; the chief of the guards of men in the city they came to the state of the honor, disturbing the honor all
2     They took their gold of the honor all; that they did not give [it] to a man … the honor took them to the honor the shepherd of the old papa with me was I led
3     Out [of it] they took the honor, behold now [we] men are hungry [and] no one else; the shepherd being empty his condition [is] bad
4     To him that behold [we] are poor men, it is of the public [matter], and thus that boldness [was] our hunger
5     Upon the one with after we had crossed over, [they] said that we hunger and we have nothing to give [you]; may your divine lordship hear into the heart of the chief of the guards [and] they know no one, the word having [no] …
6     Behold. Rejoice in the lord, our lord
