1   Tryphon son of Dionysios, a Per[sian of the e]pigone, to Thoonis
2   son of Thoonis, greeting. I acknow[ledge that I h]ave from y[o]u, at
3   the [Sa]rapeion by Oxyrhynchus ci[ty], through the
4   bank of Ammonios and Epimachos, of silver
5   of Augustan and Pt[o]lemaic coinage, dr[a]chmas
6   one hundred and four — total 104 dr. of Pt(olemaic) silver coin(age) — of principal,
7   to which nothing at all has been ad[d]ed, which I will also repay
8   to you on the thirtieth of Tybi of the present
9   second year of Nero Claudius Caesar
10   Au[g]ustus Germanicus Imperator, without
11   an[y] postponement. And i[f] I do [n]ot repay as
12   is written, I will pay you the whole aforesaid
13   principal with half as much again, and interest for the over-
14   due time, the appropriate (rate), [the] right of execu-
15   tion belonging to you both from me [and] from my pos-
16   sessions, all of them. The bond is valid every-
17   where produced and for everyone producing it.
18   Year [sec]ond of Nero Clau[dius] Caesar Augustus
19   Ger[m]anicus Imperator, m[onth Neos Sebasto]s 4.
20   (2nd hand) I, Tryphon son of Dionysios, have the [silver] drachmas
21   one hundred and four of principal, [and] I will repay as
22   is stated above. I, Zoilos son of Zoilos, [wro]te [on his behal]f,
23   as he does not know letters. Year 2 of N[ero] Claudius
24   Caesar Augustus Germanic[us] Imperator, [mo(nth)] Neos
25   Se[bast]os 4.
26   (3rd hand) Year second of Nero Claudius Caesar
27   Augustus Germanicus Impe[rator], mo(nth) Neos Sebastos 4.
28   We, Ammonios and Epimachos, bankers, have signed.
29   (4th hand) I, Thoonis son of Thoonis, have back the aforesaid principal
30   and make no claim. I, Straton[i]kos son of Apion, wrote
31   on his behalf, as he does not know letters. Year 2 of Nero
32   Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
33   Imperator, Mecheir [1].
34   (5th hand) Year second of Nero Claudius Caesar
35   Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Mech(eir) 1. Through the
36   bank of Ammonios and Epima[ch(os)] the payment was made.
