[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion to Ptolemy and Apollonius his brothers, greeting. If you are well, I too am well. I have betrothed [myself] to the daughter of Hesperus, and I am about to conduct [the marriage]
2     in the month Mesore. You will do well to send me a half
3     of oil. I have written to you so that you might know.
4     Come then to the day,
5     Apollonius.
6     Farewell. Year 28, Epeiph 21.
