[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 8 of Emperor Caesar Titus
2     Aelius Hadrian Antoninus Augustus
3     the Pious, Thoth 6, of the census of Mesore. He/she wrote [this] down
4     Pasion son of Dioscorus, slave of Laberia, mother of
5     Alexandra, for the poll-tax of the seventh year
6     in Phremei twenty drachmas, which total twenty, with additional charge of copper ten obols.
