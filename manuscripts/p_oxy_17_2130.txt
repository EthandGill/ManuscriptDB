[TRANSLATION]
1     … 2 … opinion(?).
2     To the order of the gymnasiarchs of the
3     city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Aurelius Sarapion also called Philoxenos, former gymnasiarch, who is acting as deputy in the prytany,
4     from Aurelius Sarapion also called Serenus,
5     former gymnasiarch, former prytanis over the garlands, councillor
6     of the illustrious city of the Antinoites and however I am styled,
7     through Aurelius Gaianus, agent. With respect to whatever may have arisen at some point out of an illegality,
8     a memorandum having been read among you, as I learned, on the past 30th, the council being in session, concerning a nomination
9     arising from my designation, pertaining to the office of the gymnasiarchy, on that very account I brought before you
10    books of appeal to the most excellent
11    epistrategos Aelius Faustus the ducenarius, and these not having been admitted,
12    I dedicated, in the Sebasteion there
13    at the divine footsteps of our lord
14    the Emperor Gallienus Augustus, those documents to be forwarded by the official on duty
15    to the most illustrious prefect Iuvenius
16    Genialis, and to the very official on duty I delivered
17    the copies, and now too I request, according to what has been commanded, that the opinion be issued to me, showing the reason why
18    you did not admit the books of appeal,
19    so that the consequent steps may be carried out.
20    Year 14 of the Emperor Caesar Publius
21    Licinius Gallienus Germanicus
22    Maximus Persicus Maximus Pius
23    Felix Augustus, Phamenoth 20.
24    Aurelius Sarapion, through me
25    Gaianus, have submitted (this).
