1     [Ptolemaios son of Apion, grandson of Apion, his mother being Sarapous, from Oxyrhynchus]
2     [city, a Persian of the epigone, to Apion also called Petosorapis son of Petosorapis,]
3     [grandson of Petosorapis, of those who have served as kosmetes of the same city, greeting.]
4     [I acknowledge that I have received from you, through your hand, of imperial silver coinage]
5     six [hundr]ed [drachma]s of principal, to which has been added nothing of interest, at the drachma rate [for each mina]
6     [for] each [m]onth [from the present m]onth Ph[armouth]i. And I shall repay [to you the p]rincipal
7     [together with the] interest on the [thirtieth of Mesore of the p]resent th[ir]teenth ye[ar of An]toninus Caesar
8     [the l]ord, w[ithout dela]y. But if I do n[ot rep]ay, I agree that there shall remain with you and
9     [those who] succeed from you, in place of [the] principal and the interest, the pos[session] and ownership of [my] sla[ve girl] Isarous, about nine years old, free from challenge except for the sacred disease and epilepsy; and that you shall pay, if you release her, the taxes upon her; and
10    to be [mas]ter of h[e]r and of [the] offspring to be born of her from now on, as if a sale
11    had been made [to you], and to carry off the surplus and to sell to others and to use as you choose
12    [no] right of recourse being left [to] me. And I shall provide to you and to those from you the
13    [slave gi]rl and her offspring to be born from now on, guaranteed with every guarantee, it not being permitted to me, unless I first repay the six hundred drachmas and the interest, to sell
14    [nor] to mortgage nor otherwise to dispose of the slave girl Isarous nor her offspring to be born
15    [from h]er, the choice being yours, should I fail to act justly, if you choose after the time to take possession of the slave girl and the offspring to be born, in place of the
16    [six hun]dred drachmas and the interest, or to exact payment of all, including for the time that has elapsed,
17    [dra]chma-rate interest, both from me and from the slave girl Isarous and from her
18    [offsp]ring to be born and from all my other possessions. Flight
19    [or de]ath [or in]jury, if it befall the same Isa[ro]us or the offspring to be born,
20    [no] dam[age] shall result to [the same prin]cipal or the interest nor to any part, sin[ce] all
21    [things are] free from [all] risk. Valid is the autograph note of mine in my own hand, which
22    [in dupl]icate I [wro]te. In the thirteenth ye[ar] of the Empero[r] Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus
