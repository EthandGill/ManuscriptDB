[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ptollarion son of Ptollarion, ex-gymnasiarch, and to Geminus, ex-gymnasiarch,
2     and to Amatios also called Paulinus, both (sons)
3     of Patron, ex-gymnasiarch,
4     from Penneis son of Orsenouphis, grandson of Penneis,
5     [from the vil]lage of Theogonis, and from Syros son of Demetrios,
6     from the quarter of the Macedonians, the two farmers and mutual sureties for payment. We wish to lease from you for six years
7     from the coming eleventh year of Aurelius
8     Antoninus Caesar the lord, the (arouras) belonging to you, to Ptollarion as to the half
9     share and to those of Geminus's party as to the remaining
10    half, around the aforesaid village of Theogonis, grain-land, eight arouras in the so-called
11    [allotment of Pyrrhos], in two basins; the first
12    [and third] and fifth year for sowing of fodder-grass for the eating-down of sheep, the rent for the
13    three-year period per year, without seed, for the whole eight arouras
14    being sixty-four silver drachmas; but the second and fourth and sixth
15    [year] for sowing of wheat, the rent for the
16    [three-year period] per year together with the wheat-seed we shall receive, eight artabas, of the whole wheat being one hundred and seven artabas, all
17    free from deduction and from risk; and we shall perform the yearly works, as many as are fitting, at the proper
18    seasons, doing no damage, and we shall pay the rents each year in the month Pauni
19    on the threshing-floor, new, pure, by the six-choinix measure
20    your own, the measuring being done through
21    your stewards, and the rent in Phamenoth, all the public dues being
22    charged to you the landholders, but the yearly
23    transport-charges of the urging-on (being) charged to us; and after the time we shall hand over the arouras
24    clean from rush, reed, dog's-tooth grass,
25    (and) every weed, if it seem good to lease.
