[TRANSLATION]
1     Ctesidemus and Sarapion, contractors
2     of the estate of Jul[ius] Pollus through Apollonius
3     scribe to the grain-collectors of Singkephas, greetings; apportion from what you have with us of our [property]
4     wheat of the produce of year 11 of Trajan Hadrian Caesar
5     of the Lord, Petseirei, [son] of Horus from Petne
6     for … wheat artabae two one-quarter, [they] become wheat artabae 2 1/4
7     year eleven of Emperor Caesar Trajan
8     Hadrian Augustus, month Caesareus, 4th, Saturday
