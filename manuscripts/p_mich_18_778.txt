[TRANSLATION]
1     To Athenodoros the dioiketes, from Peteminis, one of those from Mouchis
2     of the division of Polemon. I am being wronged by Pythion, who administers that same division. For Onnophris, son of the man who farmed
3     the beer-tax of Mouchis for the 13th year …
4     having … in Phaophi, on account of the extortions of Pythion,
5     Pythion assigned to Petesouchos, who had farmed
6     the beer-tax of Aphrodites-polis and Eleusis, the
7     beer-tax of Mouchis at 42 talents 2,000 drachmas, 20 talents … for …
8     to take charge of the beer-shop in Mouchis, and at the same time to the office …
9     a clerk … through … the same Petesouchos … the registered beer-shops that had been entered, and …
10    he did not look into … of Mouchis …
11    for his own account …
12    the documents … and … of Aphrodites-polis
13    and of Eleusis … to separate …
14    … … and of the
15    … of … 4,200 drachmas and a capital sum per month …
16    and of Aphrodites-polis and Eleusis from Phaophi to Tybi, four months,
17    of Onnoph…
18    … judge … and of the … six interest charges
19    … 6 talents drachmas … before being approved …
20    … having come to Mouchis, having aimed
21    but … the said Petesouchos
22    … a fur cloak, 1,200 drachmas
23    … of Petesouchos … is set aside at the
24    present … that the clerk owes, alleging as a pretext
25    … month … and not to give, thus, having sent for me,
26    … he exacted 4,000 drachmas, against which I gave him as pledges
27    two women's robes and one man's and … other …
28    which were worth 5 talents 2,300 drachmas … having come to the
29    … of those in good order. I therefore request you, if it seem good to you,
30    since then Pythion has withdrawn, and to me who submitted a second memorandum none of my rights has been done — preferably that
31    my memorandum be dispatched to the chrematistai, but if not,
32    to write to Philon the chief of police to write me an order to the
33    village police, that wherever he be found, they may restore
34    him to you. And this having been done, I shall have obtained my
35    rights.
