[TRANSLATION]
1     Harpokras, collector of money-taxes of the South and of Ophieus,
2     to Abos the elder, (son of) Petosiris.
3     I have received for the poll-tax of Ophieus, year 5,
4     base drachmas ten, 4 obols, total base drachmas 10, 4 obols.
5     Year 5 of Antoninus Caesar
6     the lord, Pharmouthi 5. I, Amenrosis, have signed.
