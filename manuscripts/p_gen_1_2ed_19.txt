[TRANSLATION]
1     To Tyrannos also called Isidoros and to Ninnos also called Chrysippos, former gymnasiarchs, examiners,
2     from Heron son of Souchion, grandson of Diodoros, his mother being Hermione, and from
3     Thaubarion daughter of Heron, who was and has been divorced from him as his wife, daughter of Heron
4     son of Heron, both being registered, Heron in the quarter of the Hellenion,
5     and Thaubarion at the quarter of Bithynians and Other Places, with her guardian Heron himself. Their son
6     Isidoros, born of one another, having entered upon his thirteenth year in the present 11th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord
7     and being obliged to undergo the examination, we have appended the proofs. Now I, Heron,
8     registered myself in the census of the 2nd year of the god Hadrian under Phanesis, and in that of the 16th year
9     of the god Hadrian and in that of the 9th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord by the house-to-house
10    census at the Hellenion, having jointly registered in the census of the 9th year also the son Isidoros being examined.
11    And I, Thaubarion, registered myself in that of the 16th year and in that of the 9th year at the quarter of Bithynians and Other Places.
12    And the son Isidoros being examined, both of us registered him among those born afterward in the
13    6th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord at the Hellenion. And the parents of me, Thaubarion,
14    registered in the census of the 2nd year, I not yet having been born, at the quarter of Bithynians and Other Places. Ninnos
15    also called Chrysippos, former gymnasiarch, through Ammonios the secretary: I have signed off Isidoros son of Heron, grandson of
16    Souchion, his mother being Thaubarion. Year 11 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Mesore 30.
17    Examination of Isidoros.
