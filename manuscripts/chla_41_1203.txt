1     In the consulship of our lords Diocletian Augustus for the 7th time and Maximian Augustus for the 6th time.
2     To Julius Septimius Sabinus, censitor (census-officer),
3     from Aurelius Isidoros son of Ptolemaios, from the village of Karanis of the Arsinoite nome.
4     In accordance with the divine ordinance of our masters Diocletian and Maximian, the Augusti, and Constantius and Maximian,
5     the most illustrious Caesars, I make known to you that I possess, in the boundary-survey district of the village of Karanis, of the fourth (and) fifth toparchy
6     of the division of Herakleides of the same nome, and that I have received the measurements of the arouras of my property in the boundary-survey, the measurers being
