[TRANSLATION]
1     642 (chmg).
2     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Justinian, the
3     eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the thirty-ninth year,
4     and after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most glorious, in the twenty-third year,
5     Hathyr 11, of the fourteenth indiction.
6     Flavius Dioscorus son of Apollos, from Aphrodite the village of the Antaiopolite
7     nome, to the legal entity of the holy monastery of Zminos, which lies
8     by the opposite bank of Panopolis, through the most pious John, the superior,
9     and whoever shall in due time be (superior) of the same monastery, greeting. I acknowledge through this my
10    written deed of security of sale that I have sold to you and ceded, from now for all time hereafter and perpetually,
11    the three arouras of waterless land belonging to me, situated
12    in the southern plain of the same village of Aphrodite, in the holding
13    of Hieras, in the great farm of the same holy monastery of Zminos,
14    in accordance with the neighbouring boundaries of those (arouras) in that field on all four sides
15    round about; so that you, the holy monastery, that is, those who under you at any time
16    administer (it) as superiors, shall hold these (arouras) according to the demarcation of the
17    public tax-codex and shall account for their assessment toward the
18    register of the measurement made of them by John, of learned
19    memory, the late scholasticus and censitor, or toward whatever likely measurement may come about
20    in the Antaiopolite (nome), if it so happen, and shall own and possess, for
21    all (time), their tenure and ownership, in return for the urban assessment now
22    to be given on my behalf by you, according to the share falling
23    to me in the name of Apollos son of Dioscorus my father, for the (tax-)schedule
24    of the passing thirteenth indiction, of all kinds of gold titles
25    and disbursements, and likewise of the grain-shipment of the fourteenth indiction
26    and its surcharge and the freight-charges and all kinds of expenses of the underlying (land)
27    of my properties that are in the plain of Piahse of the village of Phthla, which
28    were transferred to the said village of Aphrodite,
29    being assessed for fourteen arouras more or less of pure
30    sowable land, amounting to four gold solidi without weight-deduction and eighteen artabas of grain
31    by the (tax-)schedule, and so that it shall be permitted to the aforesaid holy monastery to have and to hold the aforesaid arouras
32    with the assessment attaching to them according to the codex, and to use them
33    in every manner you may wish by right of ownership, unhindered and unimpeded
34    by anyone at all, whether of my heirs or co-heirs, and
35    those related to me by descent or by kinship; and the person who shall proceed
36    against you concerning the aforesaid arouras I will [drive off] at my own
37    expenses and costs, and I will guarantee (it) to you with every guarantee against everyone
38    and forever, against him who shall proceed against you at any time, under compulsion; and if I do not
39    attend to the guarantee, I will pay back double the aforesaid price
40    together with the (compensation for) loss and expense, in addition to this sale being valid and binding,
41    produced everywhere before [every authority — which I have issued to you for]
42    security, with my subscription and of the customary witnesses, [and on being questioned]
43    I acknowledged it. Flavius Dioscorus son of Apollos, the aforewritten, [have made this]
44    sale and [acknowledged] …
45    at the aforesaid price as stated above. Aurelius Or… [I bear witness]
46    to the sale, having heard (it) from the one who made it.
