[TRANSLATION]
1     To Fl[avius Apion the all-praiseworth]y [and most eminent, of the ex-consuls]
2     ordi[nary, landlo]rd al[so her]e in the splen[did city of the Oxy]rhynchites, through Menas
3     (his) servant [who in]terrogates [and] guarantees to his own master,
4     the same all-prais]eworthy m[an], the action and liability; Aurelius
5     Sourou[s son of] Pebes, his mother being Soueras, originating from the village of
6     Kyoth[e]os of the upper Kyn[o]polite nome, governed (as a pagarchy) by
7     yo[ur ][m]agnificence, greeting. A need having now arisen for the
8     irrigation-machine under me called Petear, drawing water
9     onto arable land, of one large worker (=cog?) and one small worker and
10    one bent wheel, having come up to the city, I requested
11    your magnificence, through her (estate-)agents, so as
12    to order that the same machine-parts be furnished to me;
13    and at once your magnificence, taking forethought for the
14    maintenance of her own property, these things having been bought
15    by her (estate-)agents in the fields, new, suitable,
16    fit for water-drawing, I received toward the completion of all
17    the machine-parts, on this present day, which is
18    the sixteenth of Thoth of the present year 229 (and) 198, of the present
19    first indiction, of the water-supply (irrigation) of the crops of the second
20    epinemesis; and as proof of the receipt, this
21    acknowledgment have I made, which being valid, single, written, and being asked,
22    I have agreed. I, Aurelius S[o]urous son of Pekysios, the aforewritten,
23    have made this acknowledgment, having received the same equipment for
24    [ ] [cheiro]graph of Sourous son of Pebes from the village of Keuotheos, of receipt of o[ne large] [worker and]
25    one small wo[r]ker and one bent w[heel]
