[TRANSLATION]
1     …[os] to Zenon, greeting. You wrote to me about Ptolemaios the cleruch, that you had introduced him to me, so that I might [set over me] Ptolemaios the produce-guard, that he might do him justice. Now it happened that I was troubled in Philadelphia
2     [so that] I had no leisure on that day. And Ptolemaios said that he had been introduced to Andron concerning the price of the 80 artabas.
3     [As for An]dron, he too was troubled, and he went off to Koite. On the next day I sought him and did not find [him in Philade]lphia. So I asked Ptolemaios the secretary and he said he did not know, and the price had not yet been collected
4     [nor had it at all] been put in writing. Having taken from you bronze for the wood-cutting, on the 2nd intercalary day I went off to the Iseion and pro[ceeded t]o Koite, learning from him that he had gone off without making a contract or collecting the price of the wheat … and at the same time that he has a matter with Iason concerning both the price and the rest of the rent. So he said that, as soon as he had recovered, he would come [to] Philadelphia for the money. So he no longer took me with him, but secretly collected (it) without my knowledge. And having gone off,
5     [according to the let]ter from you, I proceeded to Philon at Koite and met with him about these matters. So there was
6     … [and he] ordered Ptolemaios to come. For the produce is the 100 of the barley artabas and the 20 of the wheat
7     [artabas. And whatever is ow]ed there at Koite, he said he would provide him from the same produce, and at the same time also concerning the
8     … . And know that I received the yokes (of oxen) from Onnophris yesterday evening, so as to work today, and [all the yokes,] if any of them fail, are to be replaced. And he has also sent along 3 farmers, to whom he orders me to
9     [give the wages. So s]end me bronze for this; and if it seems good to you, you will do well to send 8 drachmas, so that I may give [it for the farm]ers; for they are for 12 arouras at 4 drachmas for the wage-labourer. For Onnophris gave us no fodder except [for]ty [artabas] of the 400 which you wrote to him about, so that it is more profitable to hire (fodder) than to seek provender. And send us
10    [the … artabas] of the wheat; for we have only 2 days' seed. I myself would have come to you, but I am [trouble]d with the sowing. So write to me about these matters as it seems good to you. Farewell. Year 36, Phaophi 26.
11    [Year 36,] Phaophi 26.
