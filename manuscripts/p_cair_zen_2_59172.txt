[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum to Zenon from Komoapis.
2     Of the contracting-out, from the middle road,
3     the upper parts toward the south, which Petechon previously took as contractor, the nine schoinia: first schoinion,
4     Pais son of Paneios, the Troïte, whose surety is Pasis son of Parautos,
5     the one in charge of the stone-barge, of 7 drachmas; second, Harentotes son of Harioiris, whose surety is Herieus, of 6 drachmas; 3rd, Harentotes son of Pasis,
6     the Troïte, surety Athemeus, of 5 drachmas 3 obols; 4th, Pakoïmis son of Sinomounis, self-guaranteed, of 3 drachmas; 5th, Petearmotis son of Psintaes, surety Amos the stone-cutter, of 7 drachmas; 6th, Pais son of Paneios the Troïte, surety Pasis, of 3 drachmas; 7th, Stotoetis
7     son of Oseuris, self-guaranteed, of 2 drachmas 5 obols; 8th, Pais son of Paneios
8     the Troïte, surety Pasis, of 2 drachmas 3 obols; 9th, Petobastis son of Thotoeutmis, the Aphroditopolite, surety Meïeus the keeper of the Isis-temple,
9     of 2 drachmas 5 obols; total: 39 drachmas 4 obols.
10    others toward the south, above the excavation,
11    which are 14 schoinia as far as the rock:
12    1st, Pais son of Paneios, self-guaranteed, of 7 drachmas 4 obols; 2nd, Petosiris son of Etpemounis, surety Pais the guard, of 5 drachmas 3 obols;
13    3rd, Pasis son of Polemios, Pasis son of Parautos, 5 drachmas 1 obol; 4th, Pais son of Paneios, self-guaranteed, of 5 drachmas; 5th, Petearmotis son of Psintaes,
14    surety Amos the stone-cutter, of 5 drachmas; 6th, Pasis son of Pais, surety Patis the guard, of 5 drachmas; 7th, Horos son of Paminis the builder, surety Styrax, of 4 drachmas; 8th, Agathon son of Ammonios,
15    surety Apollodoros the desert-guard, of 4 drachmas; 9th
16    Horos son of Paminis, surety Styrax, of 4 drachmas; 10th, Pakoïmis
17    son of Sinomounis, self-guaranteed, of 5 drachmas 2 obols; 11th, the same, of 4 drachmas;
18    12th, Agathon son of Ammonios, surety Apollodoros, of 3 drachmas 3 obols;
19    13th and 14th, Petearmotis son of Psintaes, surety Amos the stone-cutter, of 12 drachmas; total: 70 drachmas 1 obol.
20    and in the same, 23 schoinia; total: 109 drachmas 5 obols.
21    these we contracted out from the great canal as far as the small canal,
22    until he himself shall also inspect it.
23    Year 30.
24    From Komoapis
25    and Petechon.
