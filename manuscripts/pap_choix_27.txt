[TRANSLATION]
1     [Consulship of our lords Constantius and Maximian]
2     [of the] most illustrious Caesars the [fifth].
3     To Aurelius Apion strategus of Arsinoite
4     from Aurelius Pamutius Athioi and Arrianus of Apynchis both village-magistrates of village Philadelphia. For the benefit of the most sacred treasury and of the establishment of our [intent] we make [this petition]. Strategus best, from afar we happen to dwell our village, and very much indeed owing to the most sacred treasury for our village, nevertheless also for
5     part of Tane. For the reason therefore that immediately we might be able [to have] water and we
6     to receive and to have benefit and all the land of us
7     to farm and to be able to pay the over them public obligations and grain-tax and all manner of levies, we submit to you these
8     the documents requesting [you] order through one of the ones about you serving
9     to the most powerful council through the opening magistrate Aurelius Kastorion, so that [they] might choose an inspector the inspection of the channels
10    and stone-works of those in village Tane of [your] respective nome,
11    in order that we might be able even we of the flax-supply to enjoy
12    and drinkable water to have and the plain of our [respective] village
13    to sow and in the private to stand and benefit to have of the private things. Fare well.
14    Year twenty-one and thirteen of our lords Diocletian and Maximian Augustuses and Constantius
15    and Maximian of the most illustrious Caesars Pachon first. Aurelius
16    Pamutis and Arrianus we have submitted. Aurelius Kopres of Euporius
17    wrote for them as unlettered.
