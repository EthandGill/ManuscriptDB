1     [In the consulship of the Flavii Paulus and Moschianus] the most glorious, Epeiph 3, of the 6th indiction.
2     [Aurelius Herminos son of Phoibammon, his mother being ] …as, a farmer [fr]om the village of Onomphis
3     [of the Hermopolite nome, to the heirs] of Sarapodoros of illustrious memory
4     [from the city of the Hermopolites, greeting. I acknowledge that I o]we you and am in debt for rents
5     [of your vineyard plots which I farm, fo]r the present sixth indiction, of capital, gold solidi
6     … carats … by the public standard; and I am ready to provide
7     [you, in place of the payment of the cash ren]ts, of good and satisfactory reeds
8     [two myriads ] … … I am ready to provide to you and to deliver
9     … [in the] month Tybi, at the time of the cutting of the reed-crop
10    … [in] good and satisfactory reeds, without delay and without
11    [any dispute or judgment] or lawsuit; and if any shortfall should occur from the
12    [same reeds, I am ready] to provide it to you whenever you wish, on this condition:
13    [I will perform [all] the good cultivation and care of the same
14    [vineyard plots blamelessly and] irreproachably and unimpededly and not [contemptuously, all my present possessions being subject to yo]u for this
15    [and future proper]ty, just as by judgment; the document is valid and
16    [secure, and on being questioned I acknowledged.] Aurelius Herminos son of Phoibammon the aforesaid have made this
17    [document of the] two myriads [of reed]s and I am persuaded of all as set forth. Aurelius
18    … [hav]ing been asked, wrote on his behalf, as he does not know letters.
19    … [fr]om Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the document, having heard from the
20    [maker. ] … son of Phoibammon from Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the document
21    [having heard from the maker.] …lius Neoteros son of Phoibammon from Hermoupolis
22    [I bear witness to the document, having hear]d … [fr]om the makers.
