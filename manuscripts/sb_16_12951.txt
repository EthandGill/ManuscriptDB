1     To Julius Nominatus …
2     from Castor, [son] of Apion, [from the village]
3     of Karanida. [He] attacked … [by robber-like]
4     manner into my house [and carried off] 8 artabae of olives and lotos-seed …
5     deposit … and … [having] examined, … I found …
6     long-spear … of me …
7     when making the search, [I found]
8     one measure of oil in a house
9     of Petheos the Heraklite, whom I also suspect[ed], in the presence of the village headman and elder.
10    Therefore I request [that] he be brought before you and [obtain]
11    that [which is] fitting.
12    Castor, aged about 50, with scar on [the] forehead from [the] left.
13    Year 3 of Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan
14    the Venerable, Germanicus, Mecheir 17.
