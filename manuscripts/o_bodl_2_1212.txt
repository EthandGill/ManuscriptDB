[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis, produce of the 2nd year
2     Of Hadrian Caesar the lord.
3     Epeiph 6, under the name of Tankamitios Petosirios.
4     Through Chesthotos Maieurios, wheat artabe, (one) eighth.
5     Amounts to wheat artabe 8. An (I have) signed. Another: 9 wheat artabe, (one) twenty-fourth.
6     Amounts to wheat artabe 24. An (I have) signed.
