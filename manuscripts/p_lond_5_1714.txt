[TRANSLATION]
1     643, amen.
2     Grace of God.
3     God, be (our) guide.
4     God is with us
5     at every time, amen.
6     Jesus Christ, Son of God, Savior.
7     Jesus Christ, God, Son of God, Savior.
8     Total: 5 solidi, 12 keratia by the scale.
9     In the reign and consulship of our most divine master Flavius
10    Justin, the eternal Augustus, Imperator, in the fifth year,
11    Phamenoth on the eighteenth, of the third indiction, in the most illustrious city of Antinoopolis.
12    To Flavius Theodoros, son of the most illustrious and admirable lord
13    Menas, scriniarius of the illustrious ducal staff of the Thebaid,
14    the most illustrious exceptor of the same staff, who originates
15    from the Antaiopolite (nome), in which he is also a landowner — from me, present,
16    Aurelius Pekysios, son of Kollouthos the most pious presbyter,
17    his mother being Parthenope, a sailor originating from the village
18    of … of the Antaiopolite nome, but found here in
19    th[is] fair city of the Antinoites, providing a subscriber
20    on his behalf and the witnesses subscribing below to the present
21    lease-acknowledgment, on the following declared agreements and
22    acknowledgments, on all the conditions which it contains, greeting.
23    I, the aforesaid Pekysis the sailor, acknowledge through th[i]s my
24    written security, the lease-acknowledgment, willingly and
25    of my own accord, that I have leased from your illustriousness for
26    a four-year period, reckoned from the coming, with God,
27    new-moon of the next ensuing month Pharouthi of the present
28    third indiction, both it and the following time, year by year, until
29    the completion of the said four-year period, the boat belonging to you
30    and pertaining (to you), a skiff of a capacity of three hundred artabas,
31    more or less, with mast and yard and rigging and various ropes
32    and anchor, that is, one single-fluke (anchor), tented over with
33    rush-mats of papyrus, with a steering-rudder, planked from
34    [stern] to prow, with all the rest of the timber, that is,
35    [wo]od, in it, w[ith] all its rights and the whole, through
36    … on condition that I am to have this under me both for dwelling and
37    … of my nautical skill, and to sail in it
38    in accordance with your command, for whatever
39    work and cargo you may wish; and to carry out the conveyance
40    of all manner of your goods, serving you in all your needs
41    [and] assisting you without delay and without reproach,
42    [and if I must] go off to a foreign land for the needs of others,
43    … to do this also without delay, with consent
44    … against the wind, with
45    … to [re]store (it) to you, apart from God's
46    [violence and without] any sort of malpractice, and to bring back to you
47    … of your goods, the things accruing
48    … with the integrity of good faith
49    … goods that shall be entrusted (to me)
50    … in every way, altogether, simply
51    … without proof …
