[TRANSLATION]
1     Sheet (kollema).
2     To Protarchos,
3     from Thermoutharion daughter of Eirenaios, a citizen-woman, with her guardian Hermias
4     son of Athenodoros, and from Gaius Ignatius Maximus. Gaius
5     Ignatius Maximus agrees to provide his own slave-woman Chrotarion
6     to feed and suckle with her own milk, clean and
7     uncorrupted, outside at his house, Gaius's, in the city, for a period of
8     ten months from Hathyr of the present twenty-sixth year of Caesar,
9     the female child which Thermoutharion has entrusted to her, (the child) of her freedwoman
10    Kallityche, whose name is Pollarous, because the
11    milk of Kallityche, who was set in sickness, had been corrupted,
12    at the agreed wage both for the milk and the nursing,
13    with oil, of ten drachmas per month; and Gaius has received from
14    Thermoutharion, hand to hand from the house, the (sum) gathered together of the
15    three months from Hathyr until Tybi for the nursing, thirty drachmas;
16    Gaius keeping good order with regard to the nursing-payments from the month Mecheir,
17    the slave-woman is to provide from now on, exercising both her own
18    and the child's care, not corrupting the milk nor lying with a man
19    nor becoming pregnant nor suckling another child besides, and whatever
20    she receive or be entrusted with she will keep safe and return when it is demanded,
21    or else pay the value of each, except for evident loss
22    which also being made plain let her be released, and not to abandon the nursing
23    within the period. But if she transgress in anything, she herself is to pay back the nursing-payments she has received
24    and whatever she received, with the half again added, and the damages and expenses and another
25    two hundred silver drachmas, the execution being as if
26    by legal judgment. And Thermoutharion, while Chrotarion performs
27    each thing, is to supply from now on the monthly nursing-payments and not
28    to draw away the child within the period, or else she too is to be liable to the same
29    penalty. And Chrotarion shall bring (the child) with the child to
30    Thermoutharion in the month … . We make our request.
31    Year 26 of Caesar, Hathyr 8.
