[TRANSLATION]
1     Harmais son of …or…s and to Demetrios,
2     cultivators of … we received from Ptolemaios son of …oys, near Aphrodite of the city of Berenike.
3     When you arrived on the 19th at the cooling-floor and we were showing you the plantation, Theodotos the guard accompanying
4     us, we arranged with him, having … , to guard
5     the cooling-floor; and when he came to the cooling-floor
6     he found Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios, of the uncultivated-land men, … and Pnepheros
7     son of Horos, of those from Boubastos residing there, plundering(?) the cooling-floor together.
8     Pnepheros escaped, but the guard held fast Ptolemaios.
9     But Ptolemaios his father, having heard from Pnepheros the
10    one who had escaped, went down to the plain of the castor-bearing (land)
11    bringing with him Lyson the … and Trebios the copyist and Demetrios and Meleagros, the two of the uncultivated-land men,
12    and eisa…anth… and Euporos and Aristonomos, epigonoi,
13    and many others. They assaulted the guard and, having stripped
14    Lichnaios the additionally-appointed other guard, they gave …
15    … and a little tunic to Pnepheros, the one who had plundered
16    the cooling-floor together and was fleeing, and ou…aphil… Ptolemaios …
17    the one who had been held. Having heard Theodotos the [guard]
18    shouting, and more men having come to the place, we found
19    Theodotos being led off by them to Boubastos. Concerning which we called to witness before you and Pasis, guard of Aphrodite of the city of Berenike,
20    coming from Boubastos, who also followed after these men.
21    After some time they released Theodotos. You will do well, then,
22    to write to whom it is fitting to send up the men, so that the inquiry may take place concerning these matters and we may be able for the king
23    to do what is just without pretext. Farewell. In the 6th year, Phaophi 4.
