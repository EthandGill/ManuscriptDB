[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Isido[ros], royal scribe of the Arsinoite nome, Themistos division, from Soteles, son of Josepos, the son of Theomnas, mother Erotion, from the village of Apollonias. My son J[os]epos, mother Sarra, a minor, who had not yet ceased (to be a minor) with respect to the poll-tax, died in the month of Tybi of the present fourth year of Traianus Caesar, the lord. Therefore I request that he is included amongst those, who have died. (m. 2) To the village scribe. If he died, register him and make a report with a written sworn statement, as usual. Year 4 of Imperator Caesar Nerva Traianus Augustus Germanicus, Mecheir 16. (m. 3) I, Soteles, son of Josepos, the above mentioned, swear by Imperator Caesa[r Nerva] Traian[us Optimus] Augustus [Germanicus]
