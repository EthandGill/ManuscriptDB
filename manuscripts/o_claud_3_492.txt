[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemaeus, [son] of Irenaius, to Petronas
2     stable-master, greetings. You will do well
3     [if you] give to Florus from my allowance drachmas
4     fourteen, since I previously borrowed from him
5     for the account of food for the month of Mecheir and
6     Phamenoth. Year 4 of the Emperor Caesar
7     Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus
8     Augustus and [the] Pious, the lord
9     of the month of Tybi, [day] 6
10    Ptolemaeus: I have [it] and will repay as is set forth
