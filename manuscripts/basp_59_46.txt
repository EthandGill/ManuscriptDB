[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Kin]g Ptol[emy], greeting from Lastratos, Macedonian, holder of a thirty-aroura allotment of the 6th chiliarchy. I am being wronged by Pan[tauch]os … -arouros. For whereas my allotment has been measured out around [Ar]sinoe by Sebennytos, and that of Pantauchos has been measured out around Sebennytos,
2     and whereas a royal road has been laid out for us in the middle between the boundaries of our allotments, because also
3     our allotments have been laid out in the fields of the aforesaid villages,
4     … of the land, now Pantauchos, having trespassed upon the common
5     [of us] … road, the royal road, ploughs it up and is intent on sowing it … and passing through it. I beg you therefore, O king, to order Diophanes the strategos to write to Horos the royal scribe to send out together with me
6     a surveyor who will point out to us the boundaries of the allotments and will lay out the royal road. For when this is done, I shall not be wronged but, having taken refuge with you, O king, I shall obtain justice.
7     Farewell.
8     To Horos. Point out to each of them the appropriate boundaries. Year 2,
9     Xandikos 1 = Mesore 7.
10    greeting. Having investigated, make clear to u[s]
11    and … phalonos …
12    [L]astrato[s] to Horos
13    [against Pá]ntauchos.
