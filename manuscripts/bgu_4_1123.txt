[TRANSLATION]
1   […]os son of Ptole[maios] …, the three (of us), to ourselves, greeting. Since we have entered upon a lease for the paternal (and) grandmaternal … […]
2   [… which has come to be] on the unprofitable-land register, near the village of Syron Mesogeios of Alexarchou Island of the
3   Menelaite nome, of the (district) of Berenike, of one thousand and eighty-four arouras, and however many there may be, in which (are) a vineyard … and pastures and …[…]
4   […] Nikomedes and Alexandros gave, in their own name, the petition concerning the lease; and through this it is declared that a
5   crown(-payment) is to be paid, once, for the aforesaid (land), of one thousand silver drachmas […]
6   […] a rent of eight hundred drachmas; we agree that the three (of us) are partners and sharers and owners, each according to
7   the third part, from now for all time, of the aforesaid lease […]
8   […] of all …, on condition that Sextus or Nikomedes shall give the petition to the collector
9   of the revenues for the crown that is given, whether the aforesaid (one) or whatever (it shall be) according to the coming (assessment)
10   […] of capital, and both, or either of them, shall expend from their own (funds) the
11   future expenses, all of them, until the receiving of the documents concerning the ownership; and Sextus [and Nikomedes] shall recover […]
12   […] they shall expend, the interest of the capital, out of whatever we may find, immediately upon coming
13   to the place(s), from the lease, in whatever manner; and if anything is left over
14   from the …[…]
15   […] of the [le]ase, after the settlement of the capital, it shall be divided into thr[ee] equal and
16   alike parts, and each of us shall take one part; and there shall always be …[…]
17   […] the three parts of what shall come from the lease, whether of wine or of grain produce
18   or fish or wild (game) or timber or anything else, in whatever manner, the … using it […]
19   [… of the] expenses that shall be on account of the lease, and each is to take the third
20   part, Nikomedes and Alexandros making … the (payment) for the le[ase …]
21   […] it being permitted to none of us to set aside any of the things agreed concerning the partnership,
22   nor to do anything else to the injury of the other, in any way, or …[…]
23   […] the transgressor is to pay to the one abiding (by it) the damages following on his account
24   and expenses, double, and, as a private debt, three thousand silver drachmas; and Sext[us …]
