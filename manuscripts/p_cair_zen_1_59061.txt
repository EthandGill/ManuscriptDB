[TRANSLATION]
1     Hierokles to Zenon, greetings. If you are well and otherwise [are] getting along as you [wish], it would be well;
2     I myself am in good health. Make haste to send the mat to the [boy] and a cover
3     and pillows and two jars, one nine-cotyle, the other [of one] choius, for they are useful, and [a cleaning] rod, and especially let the leather be goat[-skin], but if not, fine calf[-skin]. You wrote to me to marvel
4     if I do not understand that a duty attends to all these matters. I understand this myself, but you are [able] to administer and send [them] as safely as possible.
5     [Farewell.]
6     [Hier]okles about bedding.
7     Year 29, Xandikos 2.
8     In Memphis.
9     To Zenon
