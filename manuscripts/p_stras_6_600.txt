[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving
2     and consubstantial Trinity, Father and Son
3     and Holy Spirit. Written on Phaophi, new-moon day of the thirteenth indiction.
4     To Aurelius Petros, the most admirable,
5     ex-… from the city of the Hermopolitans,
6     from Aurelius …nios son of Victor
7     from his mother … , key-maker,
8     from the same city. I acknowledge willingly
9     and of my own choice that I have leased from you
10    for as long a time as I hold it, reckoned
11    from the present day, which is Phaophi new-moon,
12    of the present thirteenth indiction, the
13    one hall belonging to you and one bedroom
14    being on the second floor, and the whole
15    dining-room facing west, with the appropriate [part]
16    of the roof-terrace and the gateway and cellar
17    and the courtyard and the entrance and exit and going up and going down and [every] right, from a house
18    situated in the same city of the Hermopolitans
19    at the quarter of the East Fort, the aforesaid …;
20    and the use …;
21    of these …;
22    I wrote on [his behalf, as he does not know letters.]
23    I, Aurelius Victor son of … ou, from Hermopolis, bear witness to the lease, having heard from the
24    party who made it. I, Aurelius Ioannes son of Josephios, from Hermopolis,
25    bear witness to the lease, having heard from the party who made it.
26    Through me, Victor, notary with God's help …;
27    [Lease] of a house … [key]-maker from Hermopolis, of rent 15 keratia.
