[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 7 and 6 of our lords
2     Diocletian and Maximian the Augusti,
3     Tybi 25. There paid Isidoros son of Ptolemaios
4     for the price of wheat of the produce of the 5th year and the 4th year, on behalf
5     of the seed-corn of the village of Karanis, of silver
6     eight hundred drachmas, total 800 drachmas. Aurelius
7     Masculinus, having been gymnasiarch, have signed.
8     And through Pateieus, on behalf of the sowing of the 5th year and the 4th year,
9     four hundred drachmas, total 400 drachmas. Aurelius
10    Masculinus, having been gymnasiarch, have signed. Aurelius Athanasios
11    through Kepion the assistant, I have signed for the eight hundred drachmas as is set forth above.
12    In the consulship of our lords Constantius and Maximian
13    the most illustrious Caesars, year 10 and the ninth year of our lords
14    Diocletian and Maximian the Augusti, and the 2nd year of our lords
15    Constantius and Maximian the most illustrious Caesars,
16    Mesore 28. There paid Sarapion son of Onno[ph]ris, for the price of wheat of the produce of the 9th year and the 8th year and the 1st year
17    of the village of Hiera Nikolaou, four hundred drachmas, total 400 drachmas. Aurelius Philadelphos through me
18    Melas have signed.
19    Of the 11th year and the 10th year and the 3rd year, Hathyr 22.
20    There paid Isidoros, in the name of Sarapion son of Onnophris,
21    for wine of the levied annona
22    of the produce of the 2nd year, three hundred
23    and si[xt]een drachmas, total 316, and the freight of these,
24    through me Horion the collector, and I have signed.
