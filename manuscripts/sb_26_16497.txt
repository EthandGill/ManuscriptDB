1     [Antio]chus Cassianus and those with him, inspectors

4     [the poll-tax] of the [14]th year of Hadrian Caesar the lord, drachmas seventeen [and half-obol]; [of the] jailer's-tax, half-obol; total: drachmas 17 obol; and for the special per-head tax

9     [obol half-obol; total:] drachmas 18 obol half-obol.
