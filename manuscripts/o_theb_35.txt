[TRANSLATION]
1     Petosíris and Pasḗmis, tax-collectors of silver revenues of Memnon, to Sachumni
2     Of Pamonthu, capitation-tax, year 13 of Memnon, 8 drachmas. Year 13 of Trajan
3     Caesar the lord, Phamenos 3rd. Similarly Pharmouth
4     8 drachmas 4. Similarly Pachon 1st drachmas 4.
5     Similarly 16th drachmas 4. Similarly dyke-tax bronzes 2 which is also bronze 1.
