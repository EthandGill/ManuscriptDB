[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Julius Lykarion, veteran, and however I am styled, to Longinia Nemesilla, with her guardian her brother Valerius
2     Tourbon, greeting. I have received from you,
3     in respect of what you owe me together with your brother
4     Tourbon according to a handwritten note completed
5     through the record-office here in the fourth year,
6     Mesore 30, out of joint liability, of silver
7     two hundred and forty drachmas
8     and four artabas of wheat and forty-five artabas of barley and eleven measures of vegetable-seed, on the security of the
9     (lands) belonging [to you] about the village of Philadelphia of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite
10    nome, five arouras in two seals (parcels),
11    the share falling (to you) of both the
12    two hundred and forty drachmas of silver and the
13    four artabas of wheat and eleven measures of vegetable-seed, the account remaining
14    to me concerning the forty-five artabas of barley and whatever other (claims)
15    I have from this handwritten note against
16    Tourbon. I, Julius Lykarion, have received from Nemesilla the share falling (to her) of the
17    two hundred and forty drachmas of silver and four artabas of wheat
18    and eleven measures of vegetable-seed, and
19    I have agreed, as is set forth above.
20    Year 11 of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Severus
21    Alexander Pious Fortunate Augustus, Thoth 13.
