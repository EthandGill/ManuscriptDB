[TRANSLATION]
1     To the brother Horugchios, assistant of the village I, Magistor with God, assistant of the record-office. I received from the receiving-office of the second indiction
2     gold coins six of proper weight of Alexandria given to Basilios, assistant... six
3     ... grain place of the first indiction, likewise on behalf of Victor, Dio[skoros], Isidore, carats.
4     twenty [three] of Alexandria, likewise on behalf of the monastery of apa Jacob from coins 6 carats 17½, carats seventeen
5     half [of Alexandria], the total becomes coins 7 carats 16½ of Alexandria
6     [from which also] [was] given to the brother Menas, receiver, one coin of proper weight of Alexandria; the remainder coins 6 carats 16½ of Alexandria
7     on behalf of the same second indiction. It was written Tybi 5, indiction 2.
8     ... [M]agistor with God, assistant of the record-office, I have handed over gold coins 6 carats 16½ of Alexandria
9     on order of the lord Magistor, second indiction, gold coins 7 carats 16½ [of Alexandria]
