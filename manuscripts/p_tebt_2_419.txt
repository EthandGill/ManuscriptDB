[TRANSLATION]
1   Heron to Heron the most hon(ored), greeting.
2   By all means tomorrow,
3   which is the 25th, send
4   the she-donkey so that
5   it be branded. See you do not
6   neglect it, since
7   a tribune was here
8   today
9   for this. If
10   my daughter is ready,
11   let her come
12   up on the she-donkey,
13   but the other donkey let it
14   not come up until
15   I tell you. And if
16   my daughter does not come
17   up, load it with
18   hay and by night
19   send it. Let it be your care
20   about the water-
21   ing of the colophonia
22   and the simiais(?) and about
23   the hay.
24   I pr[a]y for your health.
25   To Heron the manager.
