[TRANSLATION]
1     In the forty-first year of Caesar, Hathyr, on the twenty-fourth. There has been registered in Psinachis of the Themistos division a sale and deed of cession of a house and forecourt and courtyard and vacant spaces and all the appurtenances, in
2     Soknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite (nome), which Chairemon makes,
3     son of Herodes, his mother being Thasis, prophet, to Satabous son of Eriopsemis, his mother being Satabous; and further also the wife of Chairemon, Tomsais daughter of Chairemon, her mother being Tamesthasythmis.
4     I, Chairemon son of Herodes, my mother being Thasis, prophet, and my wife Thesmais daughter of Chairemon, her mother being Tamesthasythmis, we acknowledge — Chairemon that he has sold to Satabous son of Eriopsemis (and) of Satabous the house belonging to me and
5     forecourt on the north side and courtyard and the adjoining vacant space on the south side and all the appurtenances, in Soknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleides division, of whatever
6     measurements they are, with all that is there; neighbors: on the south the village perimeter, on the north the house of Horos son of Horos, on the west and east royal streets; and I have received the whole agreed price in full at once
7     by hand from the house, and I shall guarantee it for all time; and I, Themsais, consent to the sale and (agree) not to proceed against it, as is written above. I, Chairemon the aforesaid, prophet and chief stolistes
8     of Souchos the great great god, have written on her behalf because she does not know letters, and I am forty-three years old, with a scar in the middle of the forehead; and Themsais is forty years old, without distinguishing mark.
