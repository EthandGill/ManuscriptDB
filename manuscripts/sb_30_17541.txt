[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Serviius] Sulpicius Serenus, overseer of [Augustus]
2     [From] … supervisor of waterworks.
3     [I wish to inform] you, sir, that according to your order I have gone down into the waterworks
4     [and I have dig]ged somewhat and have come upon hard rock which has worn away the tools and the iron [implements]. Know
5     [therefore], sir, scarcely giving the third [part]
6     itself of which [was giving] water; wherefore I beg
7     you, if it seems good to your fortune, to send forth
8     for me a coppersmith and copper-sheet
9     metal [of] fifteen minas and
10    men [those] able from the
11    family, whose names [are]: Justus,
12    Cretan, Alaphthanes, Glaukon
