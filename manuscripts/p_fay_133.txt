[TRANSLATION]
1   Fr(om) Alypios [to Heroninos(?)]
2   I have sent the stew[ar]d [Hera-]
3   kleides to you, as you ask[ed],
4   to make the arrangement for the vintage.
5   Put it off two
6   or three days, so that both the empty (jars) may
7   [c]atch up with you, and also the wine
8   may turn out good — for you know that
9   the season is now rather late,
10   as I did also in the other
11   little estates. So, by your own inspection,
12   not trusting the fruit-buyers, make the vint-
13   age, and write me back accordingly.
14   I have sent you also a little release-note of wine
15   for the vintage, for you alone […]
16   I pray for your health.
17   To Heroninos, ma(nager of) Thraso.
18   Year 7, Mesore 18.
