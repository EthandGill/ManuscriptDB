[TRANSLATION]
1     [Copy] of the schedule of land-allotments of the Arsinoite nome:
2     [of catoecic land] and of land bought from the public treasury,
3     [of a male] catoec, for each aroura of grain-land 4 drachmas,
4     [of orchard-land] 8 drachmas; of a female catoec, for
5     [each] aroura of grain-land 8 drachmas, of orchard-land 16 drachmas.
6     [Of catoecic land] of one acquiring for the first time, up to the fifth year
7     [of the second], for each aroura of grain-land 8 drachmas,
8     [of orchard-land] 16 drachmas; of females likewise of grain-land,
9     [for each aroura] 16 drachmas, of orchard-land 32 drachmas.
10    [And of the] children registered and
11    … to the fathers through the books
12    [of the] land-allotments, of males indeed
13    [of grain-] lands, for each aroura 2 drachmas,
14    [of orchard-land] 4 drachmas; of females likewise of those
15    … grain-lands
16    [for each] aroura 6 drachmas, of orchard-land likewise
17    [12 drachmas]. Of registrations of those not entered,
18    [of males] of grain-lands, for each aroura
19    [4 drachmas, of orchard-]land 8 drachmas; of females likewise of those
20    [not] entered, of grain-lands, for each
21    [aroura] 12 drachmas, of orchard-land 24 drachmas.
22    [Of mortgage], for each aroura of grain-land 1 drachma,
23    [of orchard-] land 2 drachmas; of release of mortgage,
24    [of grain-land] 1 drachma, of orchard-land 2 drachmas. Of registrations,
25    [for each] name of males 2 drachmas, of females 4 drachmas.
26    [Of cessions of] the official-document sent up
27    [from Alex]andria, for each name
28    [2 drachmas]; … ios of a female ceded,
29    [for each] name 4 drachmas. Of official-documents, for each
30    [name] 12 drachmas, of an unregistered one four obols, of a seal 1 drachma,
31    … of those from land into the account
32    … for each name 4 drachmas.
