[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Soknopaiou Nesos, 3 camels. To Apollinaris, strategos, and Sarapion, royal scribe of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division, from Sotas, son of Arabas, from the quarter of Dionysios’ areas. The three camels, which I registered in the past year near Soknopaiou Nesos, I register now as well for the present 4th year of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, in Soknopaiou Nesos. (m. 2) 3 camels were registered at the office of the strategos. Year 4 of Antoninus Caesar, the [lord, Mecheir x]. (m. 3) [3 camels] were registered at the office of the royal [scribe] – – –
