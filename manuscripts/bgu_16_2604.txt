[TRANSLATION]
1     Stilbon to Athenodoros the god and
2     lord, greeting and good fortune; already
3     me having gone out and being [at]
4     the place at the tower of Thaubarios, having gone through, slave of Chrysippos and Scaliphos
5     [brought] me into the city with blows and outrages; if it seems to you
6     to [Scaliphos] to write concerning the ship
7     and me, so that you might release me together with the ship;
8     for Scaliphos blames you, saying that
9     not even of any ordinary letter have I obtained from you; therefore I ask you to write to the
10    Scaliphos so that he might release me more quickly
11    because your sister wants in
12    the ship to sail up; but concerning the cargo you have here, I said to all the captains of the ships to bring to you
13    it and what they have sold [to bring]; but let this be this letter;
14    Farewell, year 24 of Caesar, Phaophi
15    August
