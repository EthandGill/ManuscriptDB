[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Ptolemaios, councillor, and Panneus, ex-agoranomos,
2     both superintendents of barley, to Aurelius
3     Paesios from the village of Theadelphia, greeting.
4     We have measured out from you, for the annona now imposed, of barley sixty-
5     seven modii, on behalf of the same village, 67 modii,
6     and the freight-charges (naula) for these.
7     In the consulship of our masters Diocletian [the]
8     father of the emperors, for the 10th time, and Galerius Valerius
9     Maximian Augustus, for the 7th time, on the 6th day before the Ides
10    of February. I, Aurelius Agathos, wrote on
11    their behalf, as they are illiterate.
12    Aurelius Firmus, magistrate, receiver of barley of the harbor of the metropolis, to Aurelius Arion, tesserarius, greeting.
13    I have received from you, for the newly imposed additional levy (epibole),
14    on behalf of the village of Theadelphia, of barley one hundred forty-one modii, 141 modii, and the freight-charges for these.
15    In the consulship of our masters Diocletian,
16    the father of the emperors, for the 10th time, on the
17    fourteenth day before the Kalends of March.
18    I, Aurelius Firmus, have signed.
19    And likewise the same, on the 5th day before the Kalends of March, on behalf of the
20    newly imposed additional levy on behalf of the village of Theadelphia, of barley
21    forty-nine modii, 49 modii, and the freight-charges for these.
22    In the consulship of our masters Diocletian,
23    the father of the emperors, for the 10th time, and Galerius Valerius
24    Maximian Augustus, for the 7th time, on the 5th day before the Kalends
25    of March. I, Aurelius Firmus, have signed.
26    Aurelius Kyrillos, superintendent of barley,
27    to the Aurelii Esouris and Arion, greeting.
28    I have received from you, of the new additional levy on behalf of the village
29    of Theadelphia, of barley fifty-
30    four modii, total 54 modii. I, Kyrillos, have signed.
31    In the consulship of our masters Diocletian,
32    the father of the emperors, for the 10th time, and Galerius
33    Valerius Maximian, for the 7th time,
34    on the 3rd day before the Nones of February.
