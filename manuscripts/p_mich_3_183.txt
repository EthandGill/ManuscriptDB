[TRANSLATION]
1     Eirene, [daughter] of Orpheus Maketa, with the lord of herself her husband Agamemnon
2     of Chrysermu[s] Lalasse[u]s, to Leontiskos and Thymos and Tesenoufis
3     greetings. I agree with you to strike off for Nikandros of Syracuse
4     the re[nt of the] whole orchard in copper, forty-eight talents, in the times according to the lease
5     and nothing to accuse you [of] concerning these [matters], if you do these things
6     according to the contracts. Farewell. Year 23, Tybi 29.
7     I agree according to what is written above.
8     To Leontiskos and
9     Thymos and Tesenoufis.
