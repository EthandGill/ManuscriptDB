[TRANSLATION]
1     [Heras] [Her]atos son of Heratos, mother Tha[esia] of Horus, of those from Oxyrhynchus
2     [city], Persian of later generation, to Paatis … a … nios son of Horus, mother Sinthoonis, from the same city, greetings.
3     I acknowledge having received from you by hand
4     from the imperial bank of Augustan silver currency
5     drachmas one hundred, making one hundred drachmas, and measured from the bank
6     wheat artabas two, making wheat two artabas, as principal to which nothing at all
7     accrues, which I will also repay in the month Pauni of the present
8     third year of Emperor Caesar Nerva
9     [Trajan Aug]ustus Ger[man]ic[us] …
