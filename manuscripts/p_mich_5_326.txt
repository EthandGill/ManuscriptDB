1     In the eighth year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Pharmouthi eleventh. There acknowledge to one another
2     Herakleides and Maron and Heroides and Didymos and Herakleides also called Louris and Herakleia, the six children of Herakleides, with the guardian of Herakleia (being) Herakleios son of Didymos, who is her husband,
3     that they have divided among themselves, by mutual consent, by a division for the present, from the present day for all time, the (lands) belonging to them: near the village Teptynis, of cataecic allo[tme]nt
4     28½ arouras in two parcels, and near Kerkesoucha Orous likewise of cataecic allotment 25 arouras in one parcel, and near Kerkesis and the hamlet of Herakleides likewise of cata[ec]ic allotment
5     30 arouras or however many they may be in three parcels, and near Kerkesephis likewise of cataecic allotment 6 arouras in one parcel, and near Theogonis likewise of cataecic allotment 17½ arouras in t[w]o parcels, and ne[a]r the
6     same village Theogonis a vine-trained vineyard in the sixth-tax category, 3½ ⅛ arouras, all paternal, and near the same village Theogonis a maternal vine-trained vineyard
7     in the sixth-tax category, 2 arouras, and likewise the paternal slave bodies belonging to them, five, whose names are Agathous and her children Herakles and Euphrosynon and others, Epaphras and Leontas, the (slaves) for the same account,
