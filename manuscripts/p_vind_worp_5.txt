1     I, Zoilos, have signed. Year 9 of Anton[inus and Verus the]
2     lords Augusti …
3     To Didas also called Isidoros, ex-exegetes, [keeper of the property-records (bibliophylax of acquisitions)] of the Arsinoite,
4     from Ammonia daughter of Maron [her mother being Philoumene]
5     registered in the quarter of Dionysios' Places, with guardian Ammonios … [son of]
6     Ammonios. I register …
7     as an inalienable gift (prosphora) …
8     … around the village of Sebennytos, a hal[f share]
9     of a half share — that is a quarter share, held in common and undivided — of a vineyard subject to the sixth-tax, of two arouras, a quarter, a two-thirty-second (?),
10    a sixty-fourth, and … in one plot (sphragis) of a vineyard subject to the sixth-tax, of an aroura,
11    a sixth, held in common and undivided, out of an aroura
12    and a half; and around the same village
13    … a half share of a half share — that is a quarter share, held in common and undivided —
14    [of a vineyard] subject to the sixth-tax, of two arouras
15    [a quarter, a two-thirty-secon]d, a sixty-fourth
16    … in the (place) called …, and whole, of a vineyard subject to the sixth-tax, two arouras a two-thirty-second, and a small reed-plot, and two slave persons, Sarapion and Isidora,
17    fr[om my] mother Philoumene daughter of Phanios
18    son of Sarapion, from Dionysios' Places, registered through your predecessors, the whole of the
19    two arouras of the vineyard, a two-thirty-second,
20    and at [Mo]eris, through the former (records), after the appointed term, the half share of the half share
21    of the two arouras of the vineyard, a quarter
22    a two-thirty-second, a sixty-fourth, 32, 64, and the
23    rest [at] Phremei, coming (in marriage) to my full brother by the same father
24    and [same] mother, Sarapion
25    also called Maron, in marriage. I, Herakouis,
26    … have registered (this). Year 9 of Antoninus and Verus
27    the [lords], Xandikos 26.
