[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Diokles … former town-councilor, praepositus of the 12th
2     pagus of the Hermopolite nome,
3     from the Aurelii A…, …yorios and Koulos son of Paues and
4     Kopreus son of Harpaesis and Pamounis son of Silvanus, the four collectors
5     of the village of Sesonchon of the same pagus. We submit and report
6     those registered below as collectors in our place of the annona
7     of the fortunate new 8th indiction, they being well-off and suitable
8     for the duty and having a livelihood …, at our risk and that of the people of the same village by mutual surety;
9     and they are:
10    Silvanus son of Hatres …
11    Pamounis son of Silvanus …
12    Koulos son of Paues …
13    … i … r …
14    [whom we guarantee and] shall produce blamelessly, discharging the collection of the annona entrusted to them
15    with all good faith … . And if they should desert and we should not
16    produce them, we ourselves shall undergo the reckoning on their behalf, and being formally questioned we gave our consent. After the consulship of Flavius Philippus, the most illustrious prefect of the sacred praetorium, and Flavius
17    Salia, the most illustrious master of horse, Pharmouthi 5.
18    We the aforesaid have submitted (this) as set forth above. Aurelius Papnouthis
19    son of Silvanus, wrote on their behalf, they declaring that they do not know letters.
