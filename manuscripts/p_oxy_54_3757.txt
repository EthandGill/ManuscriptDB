[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year] 1[9, the] ninth, Phamenoth 17, in the consulship of Paulinus and
2     Julianus the most ill[ustrious].
3     At the Capitolium. In the presence of Horion and Heras, attend[ants],
4     and of Eulogius, assessor. The logistes said: An order of my lord the most em[inent prefect]
5     of Egypt Flavius Magnus was brought to my (office of) moderation by [Aeithales]
6     from the country of the Masti[tai, concerning matters belonging to the] girl Isis under his guardianship, and held by the pres[ent] Philammon and his brothers, heirs of Bokkas. Having therefore in my breast the … fear of the manliness of so great a ruler,
7     I transmitted [to the opposing parties the orders, and I summon]ed them, so that we might learn what are the things they transacted with [each other]
8     concerning the whole … (so that) so great an order be fulfilled. Phila[mmon]
9     answered: We were reconciled, and I have a document concerning t[his. The logistes said: Let it be read.] And it was read thus, as it con[tains] … [After the]
10    reading, Aeithales answered: From these matters he came to owe 50 talents of silver, which he also wrote that he would repay within a term,
11    and I have the document of the debt. The logistes said: The order of my lord
12    the most eminent prefect [o]f Egypt Flavius Magnus has been
13    brought to a conclusion; for the document of the reconciliation that took place between (you)
14    proves that both parties have been satisfied. Whence you shall depart upon th[e]
15    agreements made by you. But concerning that of which [Ae]ithales speaks,
16    the document of the debt of the 50 talents, in due time [he shall proceed against]
17    the debtor concerning the debt in question.
