[TRANSLATION]
1     I sent the other one coin of twenty keratia from Kalamurion to your protective august mastership
2     through the letter-carrier, and in order that my master might learn that ε … ε … τ … τ … ι
3     [she] shut again according to fine grain, not of large [portion] of ours, [she] asked me to do for her
4     in order that I might send two younger ones and guard their embankments, and I dared and wrote
5     to my own master, after god, [gap] benefactor, Justus your slave
