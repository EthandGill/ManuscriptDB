[TRANSLATION]
1     [M]enon to Hermokrates his brother, greetings.
2     [If] you are well, we ourselves are well and
3     [A]phrodisia and the daughter and the servant-girl and the
4     [d]aughter of hers. You wrote [to us about] …
5     … for us the horse and from a Jew
6     … [to transport] her, whose name we do not know.
7     [He] himself neither having returned to us nor [having given] a horse nor having given us her journey-fare, we wrote to you, so that you might know.
8     Be [well].
9     So you will do well having bought for me both purple [dyes]
10    [s]taters two and for Aphrodisia two.
11    To Hermokrates.
