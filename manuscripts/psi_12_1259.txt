[TRANSLATION]
1     Nepus to Sarapion the [lord] brother
2     [with] very many greetings.
3     And now I greet you through these letters of mine. I want you to know that Morus who was formerly the servant of the front-official came to me
4     saying about the house of Ptolemy [the]
5     [cabbage-head], [saying] that if the younger [does not yield] it
6     for purchase, I intend to give a pledge before others give [one]. For I learned that others
7     are buying it. But I said to him, saying:
8     [it] is in the middle of his [houses] and not
9     [another] is able to buy it.
10    And he was saying: if indeed others get ahead of me,
11    make known to him about this; and if anything
12    if it should seem good to him, you will tell me, but rather quickly
13    make known to him. And I was saying to him that I will write and send. Wherefore I write to you so that you might know.
14    I sent to you through Dionysius [the]
15    … a letter from the 16th of the [month]
16    [P]auni concerning the things that have come to be
17    here of suspended [matters] that belong
18    to you toward Coracium. Greet the lady Isa[ro]us and Sarapammona and Sirenus and all your [people]. [Someone] greets
19    you Plutarch and Tyrannus and
20    all of ours. Pauni 22.
21    I pray that you are well, lord brother.
22    To Sarapion son of Spar[ta] [who had been] gymnasium-director from Nepus.
