[TRANSLATION]
1     [Cephal]ion to Heraclea, the most honored, greetings.
2     [Having come] to Alexandria, I delivered a report to the lord governor; and nothing has been written to me until now. Nor does your opponent cease making petitions. Nor do I, being in suspension, so that I remain at the appointed [place], and whenever you are proclaimed, [I] will make petition. But this will not be [a matter of] a short time, as I think. For the
3     lord governor departs on the new moon of Hathyr into Hermopolis to complete the examinations, and he has issued a command about this. If then
4     it seems [good] to you to send me drachmas 40, and a [letter] to your boy so that
5     [from him] I might receive another drachmas 40, in order that

8     [To Herac]lea the elder from Cephalion.
