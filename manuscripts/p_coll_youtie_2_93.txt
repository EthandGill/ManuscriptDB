[TRANSLATION]
1     … seventh indiction in the city of Heracles
2     … -os son of apa Nilus, of mother Apias
3     [from a small farm] … -otionos of the Heracleopolis nome
4     To Aurelius Phib, son of Alifius, from the same village, greetings.
5     I acknowledge to have received from you and to have been paid
6     the agreed-upon price between each other of vegetable seed
7     [of pure] grain of three-and-a-half artabas measured by just measure
8     and of new suitable wine of double pentaxestiai of two hundred
9     [and] forty, amounting to of barley artabas 3 1/2 and of wine double [measures] 240, which
10    at the [press] necessarily I will return to you in [the] months
11    [Choiak] and Mesore of [the] produce and of the fortunate [delivery]
12    [of the] eighth indiction without delay; I will exchange for you
13    if [you find] it to have become vinegar or [a vinegary thing] regarding good wine until
14    [the month of] Pharmouthi [of] the same year. The deed [is] binding.
15    and [when questioned] I acknowledged.
