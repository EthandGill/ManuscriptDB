1     [In the cons]ulsh[ip] of the Flavii [Ma]x[imus for the 2nd time and Paterius, the]
2     most illustrious, Hathyr 16, of the [fortunate twelfth]
3     indiction, in Herakleopolis, to Flav[ius ] … [, the devo]ted magistrianus of the divine [offices, in the]
4     Herakleopolite, Aurelius [Andronikos son of Neilos]
5     fr[om th]e great city of Alex[andria],
6     [in He]rakleopo[lis], confectioner, sub[scribing below with his own]
7     [lett]ers, greeting. I ac[k]n[o]wl[edge that I have rec]eived
8     [and have been paid in full] by you the price agree[d between us with each other] of two thousand [two hundred] approved empty jars; [and I have] also and owe, on account of the delivery of the twelfth indiction, [an]o[ther] two hundred empty jars on[ly, making 2200 empty jars, which] of [necess]ity I shall pay back to you in the month Mesore at the beginning of the fortunate three
9     and tenth indiction, for the delivery of the same 13th indiction, without delay
10    and without any dispute, and it shall not be permitted to me to request another
11    term concerning the repayment, you having the right of execution and
12    those acting on your behalf, both upon me and upon all my belongings
13    of every kind, of every sort, the deed being valid, and
14    on being questioned I acknowledged it. Aurelius Andronikos son of Nilos,
15    the aforesaid, I have received the price of the empty jars,
16    two thousand two hundred approved ones; and I have also
17    on account another two hundred empty jars, and I will pay you back at the term [as set forth above.]
18    Bond of Andronikos the confectioner, 2200 empty jars.
