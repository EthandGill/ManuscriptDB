1     The receiver of chaff, to Seuthes and Isidoros, collectors of chaff
2     of Karanis, greeting. I have received from you, for the produce of the 19th year,
3     4,800 litras of chaff, total 4,800 litras. In the consulship of Maximinus for the 2nd time, Choiak 7.
4     I, Gerontios, have signed.
5     And likewise, on Choiak 7, through the same persons, 2,850 litras of chaff,
6     total 2,850 litras. I, Gerontios, have signed.
7     [And likewise], through the collector, on the same day, of chaff
8     4,800 litras, total 4,800 litras. I, Gerontios, have signed.
9     And likewise, through the same collectors, on Choiak 9, of chaff
10    5,800 litras, total 5,800 litras. I, Gerontios, have signed.
11    And likewise, on Choiak 9, through the same collectors,
12    1,650 litras of chaff, total 1,650 litras. I, Gerontios, have signed.
13    And likewise, on Choiak 11, through the same collectors, of chaff
14    8,400 litras, total 8,400 litras. I, Gerontios, have signed.
15    And likewise, on Choiak 13, through the same collectors,
