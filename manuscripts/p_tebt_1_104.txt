[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 22, Mecheir 11. Philiskos son of Apollonios, Persian of the epigone, acknowledges to Apollonia also called Kellauthis,
2     daughter of Herakleides, Persian woman, with her guardian, her [brother] Apollonios, that he has received from her, on the account of copper coinage, 2 talents and
3     4000 drachmas,
4     the dowry agreed upon with him on behalf of her, Apollonia; if … the dowry …
5     … [Keeper of the contract:] Dionysios.
6     In the reign of Ptolemy [also called Alexander], the god Philometor, in the twenty-second year,
7     the priest of Alexander and of the other (gods) being as recorded in
8     Alexandria, in the month Xandikos, the eleventh, Mecheir the eleventh,
9     in Kerkeosiris in the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. Philiskos son of Apollonios,
10    Persian of the epigone, acknowledges to Apollonia also
11    called Kellauthis, daughter of Herakleides, Persian woman, with her own
12    guardian, her brother Apollonios, that he has received from her, on the account of copper coinage, two talents and four thousand drachmas, the dowry agreed upon with him on behalf of her, Apollonia. Let
13    Apollonia remain with Philiskos, obeying him as it is fitting for
14    a wife (to obey) a husband, holding in common with him ownership of their property.
15    And let Philiskos furnish to Apollonia all necessities and clothing and the other things that are due to a wedded
16    wife, whether he is at home or abroad,
17    according to the means of their property; and let it not be permitted to Philiskos
18    to bring in another wife besides Apollonia, nor to keep a concubine nor
19    a boy-favourite, nor to father children by another woman while Apollonia
20    lives, nor to dwell in another house over which Apollonia shall not have control,
21    nor to expel nor insult nor ill-treat her, nor to alienate any of the property to the injury of Apollonia. And if
22    he be shown to be doing any of these things, or if he does not furnish to her the necessities or the clothing or the other things
23    (owed) to her as has been written, let Philiskos pay back to Apollonia
24    forthwith the dowry, the two talents and the four thousand drachmas
25    of copper. And in the same way let it not be permitted to Apollonia either to spend the night
26    or the day away from the house of Philiskos without the consent of Philiskos, nor to consort with another
27    man, nor to ruin the common household, nor to bring shame
28    on Philiskos in whatever things bring shame to a husband. And if Apollonia of her own will wishes
29    to be separated from Philiskos, let Philiskos return to her the dowry in full
30    within ten days from the day on which it is demanded back. And if
31    he does not return it as has been written,
32    let him pay back to her forthwith the dowry which he received increased by one half.
33    Witnesses: Dionysios son of Patron, Dionysios son of Hermaiskos, Theon son of Ptolemaios,
34    Didymos son of Ptolemaios, Dionysios son of Dionysios, Herakleios son of Diokles, the six Macedonians
35    of the epigone. Keeper of the contract: Dionysios.
36    I, Philiskos son of Apollonios, Persian of the epigone,
37    acknowledge that I have (received) the dowry, the two talents
38    and the four thousand drachmas of copper, as has been written above, and I have deposited
39    the contract as valid with Dionysios. Dionysios son of Hermaiskos,
40    the aforementioned, wrote on his behalf, because he does not know letters.
41    I, Dionysios, hold the valid (contract).
42    Year 22, Mecheir 11, it has fallen into registration.

44    marriage-agreement … in common.
