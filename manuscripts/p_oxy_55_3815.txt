[TRANSLATION]
1     To my master, my son Apollonius, Eusebius
2     greetings.
3     that you care for my master, my son Sabinus,
4     I am reassured. But the boy, being a child, needs
5     not to turn toward disorder, and concerning this I trust that he will receive orders from
6     you, Epagathus, so that he may persist with him. But if also those concerning Adorane persist in the same luxury, let them be checked through your, my master's,
7     turning back and under the lady
8     my [daughter] Ptolemais. For the
