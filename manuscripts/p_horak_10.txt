[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of our most divine master Flavius] [Justi]nia[n] the eternal Augustus, emperor, in the
2     [twenty-ninth year,] [after the consulship of Flavius Basilius] [th]e all-praiseworthy, in the fourteenth year,
3     … [of the fourth] [indict]ion.
4     [To the rights of the] [holy martyr-shrine of Saint John, for] five and a third arou[r]as, and of the holy martyr-shrine
5     [of the Holy Three Martyrs of Saint Serapion, for] [other] tw[o] arouras, of those situated in the
6     [plain] [of the village of Thyneos of the Hermopolite nome, through you] … …uton…ou son of Kallinikos — from Aurelius Sois
7     [son of] … [his mother being] … [from the village of] … [of the same] nome, greeting. I acknowledge willingly and of my own free will
8     [that I have leased from the same holy martyr-shrines for] a [thr]ee-year period, reckoned from the crops which
9     [are now in the fields of the prosperous, God-favored incoming] [fif]th indiction itself, the (arouras) belonging to the same
10    [holy martyr-shrines] … seven and a third arouras of sowable land in the
11    [farmstead called Tbote, with the] … [share of the cis]tern there and of this water-wheel and cattle-yard and
12    … [and every right of the same far]mstead called Tbote, of thirty arouras
13    … [un]divided, in common with the other partners
14    … and each toward the completion of the same
15    [farmstead called Tbote, under the supervision of those from] [the vil]lage of Thyneos of the Hermopolite nome, for sowing
16    [and] [the laying-down of crops, whichever I may choose, at a rent per] [yea]r — for the five and a third arouras of Saint John
17    … [per year, for each aroura, of wheat] [six art]abas and of barley two artabas and of vegetable-[seed] [a sixth of an artaba;] [for the two arouras of the holy] [Mart]yrs of Saint Serapion, per year, for each aroura,
18    [of wheat] … [artabas and of barley] … [artabas] and of vegetable-seed a sixth of an artaba, to the one
19    … [in] imitation of the same (terms) of the same farmstead
20    … [which rent, new, clean,] [s]ieved, I will pay and measure out
21    [to the same holy martyr-shrines] … at the season that will be, by the receiving
22    [Athenian measure, the rent itself being established] … without delay in the month of E[pei]ph each year
23    … [into the storehouse of the same holy martyr-shrines] in Herm[opolis with my own animals]
24    [and expenses, without delay. The lease is valid and secure, and] on being formally questioned I gave [my assent.]
