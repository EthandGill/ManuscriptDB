[TRANSLATION]
1     Imouth and partners, to Pikotes, of Hereios
2     Pikotes. We have received for the dike-tax of Charakas, 18th year, tarnished
3     drachmae four, making tarnished drachmae 4, and for the name
4     Pekysios, brother, tarnished drachmae four, making tarnished drachmae 4. Of the 18th year of Trajan
5     Aristos the lord
6     Epeiph 26. I have countersigned.
