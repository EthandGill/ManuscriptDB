[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon gr...
2     Zenon to Axates greetings. We have written to you also before
3     concerning Kollytkos the priest of Thoeris of Philadelphia [that it is] to be given back
4     to him the amount accruing from the priest of Thoeris into the temple in Philadelphia, and you wrote back to me that there should come to him for the year 12 drachmas. No one therefore pays anything. You therefore order [that it] be paid to him all the amounts owed; for from these they perform the sacrifices.
5     Farewell. Year 36,
6     Mesore.
