[TRANSLATION]
1     Six standard coins I released to Kollouthos the pork-butcher toward those from the village-chiefs, and these have been reckoned
2     … [to be] satisfied by them. He retained twenty-two standard karats of the document-keepers
3     for … [having been given to] him in the [visit of the] general. Please therefore by all [means] demand
4     them for as much as they owe to the same Kollouthos for the six coins and give to the document-keepers.
5     They owe, as has been said, [to] the same Kollouthos. But by all means demand from them, since god knows I am sending the chief gaoler
6     demanding from them with cost
7     And free them and from what they owe concerning them
