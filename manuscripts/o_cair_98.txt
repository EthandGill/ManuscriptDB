r.1    διαγέγραφε Φθομώνθης Φβούκιος Ἁρπαήσιος ὑπὲρ λαογραφίας
r.2    Βορρᾶ καὶ Ἀπηλιώτου ια ἔτους δραχμὰς ιϛ. ἔτους ια Δομιτιανοῦ τοῦ

r.6    Δωρ σεσημείωμαι.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Has written off Phthomōnthes Phboukios Harpaēsios for the poll-tax
2     of Boreas and Apheliōtou, year 11, drachmas 16. Year 11 of Domitian the
3     lord, Pachon 27. Similarly, year 12, Thoth
4     10, of tower-guards, drachmas 2 triobols. Similarly, Phaophi 25, of the dyke-tax, drachmas 8,
5     the net 7 triobols. Similarly, Tybi 1, drachmas 2 triobols, the net 2 obols, 1 and 1/2 chalkoi.
6     Dōr, [I] have signed.
