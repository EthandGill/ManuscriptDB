1     Measure of the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 18
2     of the Aurelii Antoninus (and)

4     lords, Epeiph 9, for Charax, in the name

6     (son of) Phaeris, of wheat a third, total wheat ⅓.
7     I, Amonios, have signed.
