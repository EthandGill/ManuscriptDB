[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the chief magistrate]
2     [From Eugeneia the...] ... [with a guardian] ...
3     ... [and from Marcus Julius] Felix, freedman of the
4     patron Gaius Julius Felix ...
5     Epaphroditus. Marcus agrees [to have received] from
6     Eugeneia by hand from the house maintenance and [the] other expense of twelve months which according to [the agreement] completed through the same magistrate court to the [sixth] and
7     twentieth year of Caesar, Mechir, has been given to him [of hers]
8     slave child Thallusa, so that [to be nourished]
9     by his slave woman, and that Marcus not come
10    nor anyone on his behalf to Eugeneia concerning the
11    maintenance, but also that he not come to the child [in any way nor in any] manner, or, apart from the [agreed] matters being valid, he shall pay him what [he received] [maintenance] with one-and-a-half and the damages and expenses and other silver drachmas
12    [two hundred] as if from judgment. And Eugeneia has
13    received the child Thallusa. We request good fortune.
