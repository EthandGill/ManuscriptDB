1   [...]seinos to (his) mother, very many,
2   many greetings.
3   Before all things I pray for your he-
4   alth, and I make your obeisance
5   every day before the lord
6   Sarapis and the gods who share his temple,
7   praying for your health. First
8   of all I want you to know that I am gri-
9   eved whenever someone arrives and does
10   not bring me a little letter. For if
11   you cared about me, you would
12   write to me; but indeed I have often writ-
