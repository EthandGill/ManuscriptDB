[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 15 of Lucius Sep[ti]mius Severus the Pious Pertinax
2     and Ma[rcus] Aurelius Antoninus the Pious Augusti
3     and Publius Septimius Geta Caesar the August Phamemoth 30. [He] wrote for Apion, nome-governor of the Arsinouites through Sarapion
4     manager of forage in the division of Taneos through Meleagros
5     year 15 drachmas three hundred, become drachmas 300.
6     Of year [15] of Lucius Septimius Severus the Pious
7     Pertinax and Ma[rcus] Aurelius Antoninus
8     the Pious Augusti and Publius Septimius Geta
9     Caesar the August Phamemoth 30. [He] wrote for Apion, nome-governor of the Arsinouites
10    through Sarapion, manager of the tax of the nomes of Taneos
11    through the presbyters year 15 drachmas two hundred, become drachmas 200.
