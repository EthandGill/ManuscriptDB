[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To my lord and brother Alexander the monthly president and his associates of the guild of canvas and carpet workers from Heraclius the systates greetings. I have cancelled the name of Antinous, son of Heraclides, on account of the anabolikon tax of the thirteenth indiction, because I have no claim whatsoever on him on account of the said tax of his. I Heraclius the systates have signed.
