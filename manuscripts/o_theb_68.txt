[TRANSLATION]
1     Nepheros the elder, Phthouminos and associates, supervisors of tax
2     of small cattle of the 6th year, in the name of Petemenwphis [son of] Harbechis.
3     We have received from you for the tax of the month Payni
4     six drachmai, total: 6 drachmai.
5     Year 6, Epeiph 10.
