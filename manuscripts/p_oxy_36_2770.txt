[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lo[r]ds the Emperors Diocletian for the [9th time] and Maximian
2     for the 8th time, the Augusti.
3     Aurelius Herakles son of Serapion, his mother being
4     O…dina, from the splendid and most splendid
5     city of the Oxyrhynchites, and she who was
6     and has been separated (from me), my wife Aurelia
7     Maria daughter of Herakleides, her mother being Tauonis, from the
8     same city, greeting. We acknowledge that we have been parted from o[u]r living together with one another
9     and that each of us has received back his/her own (property) i[n full] in full, and that (we will) bring no charge against you,
10    nor charge, nor proceed (against you), nor concerning the same living-together, of written [or] u[nwr]itten matter [or] [d]e[b]t or a…o…n…gm…os,
11    from the previous tim[e]s up to the
12    present day. There being no chil[d]ren … from
13    one another … (each is to) have authority,
14    whichever of us, to join (himself/herself)
15    in whatever marriage we may choose, it being unaccou[nta]ble
16    and unreproachable. Valid are the
17    [d]eeds of [t]he separation, written in duplicate
18    so that each may have a single copy, and
19    being questioned we acknowledged.
20    Year 20 of our lords Diocletian
21    and Maximian the Augusti, and the 12th of our
22    lords Constantius and
23    Maximian the most illustriou[s]
24    Caes[a]rs, Tybi 30.
25    Aurelius Herakles.
26    I have been parted from the
27    living-together with you and have received our
28    (property) all of it as is set forth, and being questioned I acknowledged.
