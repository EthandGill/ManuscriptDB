[TRANSLATION]
1     To Mark Petronius Honoratus, prefect of Egypt
2     From Ptolemaios Diodoros, also called Dioskoros, from the Arsi[n]oite [nome]. Seeking your supreme justice [and] hatred of wickedness
3     I have fled to you, the benefactor of all. A certain Ptolemaios
4     son of Pappos, who has served as gymnasiarch [of] the nome, [living] a moneylender's
5     life, arrogant in manner [and] violent because he seems to himself
6     to be a magistrate, and [undertaking] matters [suited to himself] to [such] an extent that [he] has [agents] under himself in the villages [...]
7     ... [difficult] and with insolence arrogantly to approach
8     the debtors.
