[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Valentinianus the most illustrious son of our master Valens Augustus and of Flavius Victor
2     the most renowned general, Thoth 27.
3     [Aureli]i Thonios son of Thonios and Neilammon son of Ammonios, both assistants of the office of the exactor of the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
4     to the Aurelii Sarapion son of Plutarchus and Theon son of [Ammon]ios and Eulogius son of Ptolemaeus and their associates,
5     the [super]intendents of the barley of Alexandria of the 11th indiction, greeting. Since Hermias, of the rank of former praepositi,
6     produced two [receip]ts for the (barley) delivered by him for the same 11th indiction, of barley artabas
7     one thousand two hundred forty-one, total artabas 1,241—one (receipt) for artabas 211, the other for artabas 1,030, the aforesaid (sums)—
8     … to the community for the same 11th indiction, and received these from various [shipmasters], accordingly we acknowledge that we are (responsible) for the aforesaid measure of the barley, artabas 1,241,
9     … to [enter in the accounts] for the same 11th indiction, and that it is not permitted to us to enter these in the accounts for the 13th indiction,
10    [because of (their) being accounted] for the same 11th indiction. The acknowledgment, written in single copy, is valid, and questioned we have agreed.
11    As is aforesaid.
12    [Aurelius Thon]ios the aforesaid, it accords with me as is aforesaid.
13    [Aurelius Neilammon the aforesaid,] it accords with me as is aforesaid.
