[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: … From Philemon, son of Dios, priest, from the same village, greetings. I acknowledge, through this written security of mine that I have received and been paid from you the full price of fifty pounds of wool per six (sheep), 50 pounds of wool of 6 sheep, and I am ready to pay you back in the month of Mecheir of the present first indiction, according to my weighing-machine, without any dispute, and all my fortune being mortgaged until the payment, and having been asked the formal question on all points I gave my consent. (hand 2) † Philemon, son of Dios the aforesaid priest, I agree as mentioned above … … priest …
