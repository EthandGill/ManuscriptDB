[TRANSLATION]
1     Priscus to Maximus … [to]
2     [most] honored, [greetings;] [I want you to know]
3     that he has brought to me …
4     from [my] [fatherland] [;] let this matter be of care to you and you will write to me
5     a letter about where they are and not
6     be reluctant to write me a letter; if
7     you find a donkey coming, come
8     you also with it; send me a small girl; take from the one bringing the letter a bundle
9     of basil and a bundle of cabbage. Farewell.
