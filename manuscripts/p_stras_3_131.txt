[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of our master Julian Augustus] for the 4th time and of Flavius Sallustius the most illustrious prefect
2     [of the sacred praetorium,] … in … [of the] Arsinoite nome. Aurelius Leontios son of Euboulos, from the
3     … [village] … acknowledges to [Flavius] Tiambous, veteran, of those honorably discharged from the vexillations, … residing in the Arsinoite (nome), that he has from him — he, the acknowledging Leontios —
4     … [for his daughter Mamoukia,] who is joining him in the partnership of marriage, as dowry:
5     … a white linen (garment), half-worn, striped, from madder-root purple;
6     … [a dalmatic] … of Nicaean purple, of three ounces; another dalmatic
7     … a white maphorion, half-worn, a pair, from madder-root purple;
8     … one, a bolster half-worn, of three-thread (weave), one; a bronze pot,
9     … to the dowry, and he provides, on account of the bridal gift, that is the wedding-present, …
10    … which all, being aforewritten, of the dowry …
11    [acknowledges that he has received them from Flavius] Tiambous and has them from him. Let the [married couple] therefore live together with one another [blamelessly, Leontios] providing to his own wife all that is needful and the clothing
12    [as befits a wedded wife, and Mamoukia herself] providing herself blameless and unaccusable in the cohabitation. And if … [a difference] of the marriage arises and they separate from one another,
13    … [let Aurelius Leontios repay to the aforesaid] Flavius Tiambous or to his married daughter Mamoukia
14    … [the things which he has] received, forthwith, without delay, and upon the exaction,
15    [there being to Flavius Tiambous] … or … [to] his daughter Mamoukia the right of execution both from the acknowledging Leontios [and from all his property] as by legal judgment …
