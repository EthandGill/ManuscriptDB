[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 3, Pharmouthi 24, there sold,
2     Naomsesis and Stotoetis,
3     of solid measure, 4 cubits
4     of empty ground; there bought,
5     Sensouchos son of Nechoutes, for 4000 bronze (drachmas).
6     In the reign of the queen and king Ptolemy, the gods Philometores Soteres, year [3],
7     under the priest of king Ptolemy the god Philometor Soter Alexander, and of the gods
8     Soteres and the gods Brothers and the gods Benefactors and the gods Philopatores and the gods [Mani]fest and the god Eupator and the god Philometor and the god Philopator the New and the god Be[nefactor]
9     and the gods Philometores Soteres, (under) the sacred-officiant of Isis the great mother of the gods, the prize-bearer (athlophoros)
10    of Berenike the benefactress, the basket-bearer (kanephoros) of Arsinoe Philadelphos, the priestess of Arsinoe Philopa[tor]
11    those being in Alexandria, and in Ptolemais of the Thebaid under the priests and priestess[es]
12    and basket-bearer of those being and existing in Ptolemais of the Thebaid, in the month Pharmouthi [24]
13    in Pathyris, under Heliodoros the agoranomos,
14    there sold Naomsesis daughter of Onnophris, priestess, with the guardianship of her own husband Stotoe[tis]
15    son of Petearoeris, priest of Souchos and Aphrodite, aged about … years, of medium (stature), honey-coloured, with smoo[th hair],
16    round-faced, straight-nosed, selling jointly (with him), from the empty [ground] belonging to them.
