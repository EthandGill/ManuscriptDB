[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Amois optio to Agathos Daemon the superintendent
2     of the camps greetings.
3     Give to our brother Horion the leader-of-ten for a reckoning
4     of my provisions barley modii Italian
5     thirty five, from Athyr until Tybi 3, modii 35
6     … him you shall retain
7     Heracleides [I have] signed Athyr 30.
