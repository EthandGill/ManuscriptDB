[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of Imperator
2     Caesar Gaius Aurelius
3     Valerius Diocletianus and in the fifth year
4     of Imperator Caesar Marcus
5     Aurelius Valerius Maximianus, the Germanici
6     Maximi, Pii, Felices, Augusti,
7     in the month Audnaios = Hathyr 2[.], in Ptolemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome.
8     Aurelius Cornelius Pasi, his mother being
9     Loulianē, from the village of Philadelphia, about
10    3[.] years old, with a scar above the instep of the left [foot], acknowledges
11    to A[urelius] Heron, a veteran of those honorably
12    discharged, holding land in the village of Dinnis,
13    that he, the acknowledging party, has from him
14    on loan of wheat, [with] the half-as-much-again (interest), twenty-eight artabas [and] eight measures, which he shall also of necessity
15    repay [to him], the acknowledging party …
16    of the present [year] …
17    clean, free of barley, free of earth, …
18    the exaction being made for him both from
19    the acknowledging party and from his belongings,
20    all of them, as if by legal judgment. This
21    bond, in single copy, I have issued to you
22    with the subscription of the one subscribing on my behalf, being illiterate, and let it be valid wherever produced, as if registered in a public archive,
23    and on being formally questioned he assented.
24    I, Aurelius Cornelius, acknowledge
25    that I have the twenty-eight artabas of wheat
26    [with] the half-as-much-again, and eight measures, and
27    I shall repay at the appointed term as
28    aforesaid, and on being questioned I assented. I, Aurelius Phanias son of Akousilaos, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
