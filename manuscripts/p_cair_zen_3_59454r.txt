[TRANSLATION]
1     Hippocrates to Nicanor greetings. Not now for the first time [do you attempt] to wrong me,
2     but you always continue getting drunk and insulting me, having judged me simple. If [indeed you also think]
3     what is not, from me must be the recovery of the A drachmas and not from the [guarantor],
4     surely you ought not yet into an alien house, where I [happen to be, having entered]
5     to get drunk and insult a free woman and to tamper with another's [goods of which to you]
6     nothing pertains. But regarding these matters herself Phila the mistress of [the house],
7     [if she wishes], will help herself as soon as Hermaphilus comes and [provides]
8     justice to receive from you; for my part, so that the guarantor you may not [trouble],
9     me indeed, if you are able, and insult and drag away. For I will try [to help myself].
10    Nevertheless know that you are absurd and by however much anyone cares for you …
11    by so much the more do you overstep. And this not I alone say, [but all]
12    those in the city; so loved-by-all you are.
13    Fare[well]. Of the year …
