[TRANSLATION]
1     Anubion to Matrea
2     to the lord, greetings.
3     If it seems good to you, send
4     to me through Horus for expenses of the works with me, of which I will give an accounting,
5     two hundred drachmas, [which] come to drachmas 200.
6     Be well, lord.
7     of the year 23 of Antoninus
8     Caesar of the [lord].
9     Mesore 13.
10    Give only drachmas 140.
