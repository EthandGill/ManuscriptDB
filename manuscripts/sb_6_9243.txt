[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion to [Ar]abic farmer, greetings.
2     I have received [the] produce-tax of which you farm
3     around the village New Ptolemais, of a [kler]os of homestead-land, nine arouras, [of whe]at, thirty-t[wo] artabas, for the [past year], and I lodge no complaint against you concerning anything at all, as is agreed.
4     Year 2 of Imperator Caesar Marcus
5     Aurelius Antoninus Pious Fortunate
6     Augustus, Phaophi 22.
