[TRANSLATION]
1     For a long time letters from [your] fraternal magnificence I did not
2     receive; yet with joy I have rejoiced … so that even you such
3     choice you had toward me. Please write to me through the lady
4     Eutropian, since I am very troubled in this, and I shall greet you greatly.
5     From me please greet your w[ife] … and her sweetest
6     child Syron, friend o[f] mine, and my daugh[ter] and Amarkadon.
7     And I Victor your servant many the reverend traces of my master
8     I greet.
9     Give [this] with God the sweetest [brother] Timothy with God count from Theotimas sister.
