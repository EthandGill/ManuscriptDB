[TRANSLATION]
1     Gaius Valerius Chairemonianus, veteran, Antinoite,
2     of the Neroviania (tribe), also called Hestiaios, to Marcus Sempronius Clemens,
3     veteran, greeting. I have appointed you by this handwritten document
4     to take charge of my property in the Arsinoite (nome) and
5     to demand (the dues) from the lessees, and, if there be need, to lease out
6     or to cultivate (it) yourself and to issue receipts to them in
7     my name, and to carry out everything pertaining to the management, just as is permitted to me when present, and to make a partition
8     of plots … in Karanis, on condition that
9     you shall restore to me, upon my arriving here, whatever is left over to me, on your own good faith; and I approve whatever you carry out toward
10    these ends. Let this handwritten document, written by me in duplicate, be valid. Gaius Iulius Saturnilus,
11    veteran, wrote the greater part. Year 11 of Imperator
12    Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus
13    Pius, Tybi 7. He shall also take care and demand (the dues) of (the property) of my granddaughter Longinia Tasoucharion, who is under my charge, and of her property, and (do) all the other things in accordance with the aforesaid manner on my behalf.
14    manner. I, Gaius Valerius Chairemonianus, have appointed
15    Marcus Sempronius Clemens over all the aforesaid (matters).
16    I, Marcus Sempronius Clemens, have been appointed and will do each of the aforesaid (things) as is set forth above.
17    Of the agency-deed of Sempronius … …
18    deed of appointment (apostatikon)
19    of Sempronius.
