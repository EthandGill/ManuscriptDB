[TRANSLATION]
1     Anthestianos to Psois
2     the potter, greeting.
3     Already once before, again,
4     I sent Sarapammon to you, so that at last
5     at some point you might set right the wrongs previously done by you, both once and a second time, and pay back what you owe, for both
6     the remaining price of the chaff
7     and the hire of the animals for the transport of the same chaff
8     and likewise for the transport
9     of a choous, at the present time,
10    seven hundred drachmas,
11    and likewise those which you received
12    [from] Horion the potter while you were staying with you … of wheat artabas
13    … in the first year
14    … eta … erma …
15    … ntos … eranm …
16    … chesi … taale
17    … other drachmas
18    … and of wine …
19    … one four-choous measure; and thus again, having dealt insolently
20    with the work, you withdrew, and you had no
21    reckoning with Sarapammon, but you said to him that
22    "grant me this respite now, since I have come from abroad with my pitch, so that you may know
23    the token." So it was necessary
24    for me to ask my friend Dionysios to demand of you these things which you owe, or for you
25    to give them to him in jars. I have also written to Sarapammon to come again to you, so that you may not
26    quarrel shamelessly, behaving senselessly toward the demand,
27    making excuses, but
28    by all means be reasonable,
29    lest I act otherwise in some way
30    against you, summoning
31    you through the guardian of the laws …
