[TRANSLATION]
1     [Pse]ntaēs to Zēnon, greetings. From Phaōphi I perform service—I and my [boys] and my farmer. I
2     [have] watered for you the half of the thousand arourae. Wage[s]
3     [I] did not receive from you, but [for] five [workers] …
4     [I am] being deprived; [that] you bring me to Apollonius. The land which I was farming [with] castor [oil] you sowed. I feed eleven [workers].
5     I have [from you] drachmas twelve of two-months' wage and [barley] artabas two. In Philadelphia I built a house. I spent drachmas sixteen. If [it] seems good to you, order [someone] to return to me the wage and the grain-measure so that I provision [myself],
6     [since] the grain [is] cheap, and the six drachmas. Order [someone] to return the remainder of the twenty arourae
7     and four [arourae] of the border-plot. For [they] did not give to you
8     Anothis in [the] notice. [There] were not many woods.
9     but many reeds and chicories. And in [the] memorandum I have given [this] to you. Farewell.
