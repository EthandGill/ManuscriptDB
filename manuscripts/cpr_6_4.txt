[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year 22 of Marcus Aurelius Com[m]odus
2     Antoninus Augustus. Pachon 30.
3     There were registered to Nemesion and those with him
4     Having become nomarchs for the tax of nomes of Taneis through Ammon Nemesion 21 year drachmas one hundred.
5     There come to be [drachmas 100]. Through Herm it has been marked.
