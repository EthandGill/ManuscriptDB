[TRANSLATION]
1     ...of Ammonius the Syrian, mother...
2     ...from the village Thynae
3     [residing] in Magdolus, Mire, as of age 42, scar
4     ...[left] to Eutuchides son of Sarapion, greetings.
5     [I acknowledge] I have received from you by hand
6     [from the] house a loan with interest of silver drachmas
7     [two] hundred, total silver 200, which I will repay
8     [to you in] Payni month of the present third year
9     [of Hadrian] Caesar our lord, without any
10    [postponement] and dispute, [with] the right of execution
11    [being] both from me and from all [my] property as if from a judgment.
12    [In the] third year of Imperator Caesar Trajan
13    [Hadrian] Augustus, Phaophi 22. Hermaios
14    [also called Ph]ibion son of Ammonius wrote on behalf of him [saying] not [to] know [letters].
