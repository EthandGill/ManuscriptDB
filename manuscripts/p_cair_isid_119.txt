[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Gemellion and Pasigenès through me
2     Isidore to Aurelius Isidore, son of Ptolemaeus, farmer, greetings.
3     We have received from you the tax and the share
4     of the surplus produce which you cultivated on our arouras at the [boundary-marking] of Karanis
5     in the place called Pammarès for what is in hand
6     through the past 18th year and 6th year and 4th year and 2nd year, complete,
7     and when questioned, we acknowledged.
8     In the consulate of our masters Galerius
9     Valerius Maximianus Augustus the 8th
10    and Galerius Valerius Maximinus Augustus
11    the 2nd, Mecheir 29. Isidore wrote the whole.
