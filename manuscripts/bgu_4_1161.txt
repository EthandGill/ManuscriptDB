[TRANSLATION]
1   From Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios [...]
2   and from Hermaphilos son of [..., a Persian]
3   of the epigone, and his [wife]
4   Agathonike, [the ... Per-]
5   sian woman, with her guardian [her husband. There]
6   agree Hermaphilos [and Agathonike]
7   to have from Ptolemaios [a loan]
8   from hand to hand out of the house of silver [... drachmas]
9   at nine-obol interest [for each mina]
10   for each month, which also they will re-
11   pay in six months from [... of the pres-]
12   ent 7th year of Caesar, [giving the]
13   interest by the month [duly],
14   and the capital [in the last]
15   month without any de[lay, or that they]
16   be on the spot liable to seizure
17   and held under arrest [until they pay]
18   off the loan [with the half-as-much again],
19   and the interest at the simple rate, and for the
20   time overrun the interest according to
21   the edict [at two drachmas, the]
22   execution being made for Pto-
23   lemaios from those who are liable, being
24   jointly bound [for repayment],
25   both from one and from whichever of them he chooses,
26   and from all that belongs to them,
27   as if by judgment, all (pledges of) good
28   faith they may adduce being void
29   and all protection being void.
