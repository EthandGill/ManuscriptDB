[TRANSLATION]
1     Kopres son of Trophimos and Apynchis son of Panetbeous
2     and Onnophris son of Nemesas [and] Epimachos, fatherless,
3     of the mother Koprilles, and Psaros son of Petermouthis
4     and the remaining [elders] of the village of Philadelphia
5     to Sarapion son of Tesenouphis, our partner, greeting.
6     We have received from you, for our whole term,
7     on account of expense and the salary of a secretary
8     and of all expenses, of silver, drachmas
9     seven hundred, those from Epeiph of the 1st year until Mesore
10    of the incoming year, monthly the proportionate amount in equal shares.
11    Mesore 16, drachmas one hundred twenty-eight;
12    Thoth 24, another sixty drachmas; Phaophi …
13    likewise another forty drachmas; Hathyr 1, another
14    one hundred drachmas; [Choiak] … another forty-eight drachmas; Tybi … [another] one hundred twenty drachmas;
15    Mecheir 6, another sixty drachmas; Phamenoth 4,
16    another one hundred drachmas; Pharmouthi … another twenty
17    four drachmas; [Pachon] … [another] twenty drachmas, in full. And if any
18    of us … to you the aforesaid
