[TRANSLATION]
1     … [the] [toparches] …
2     Judgments concerning the taxes or [the purchases] are proper.
3     only the nomarch with the strategos. And if anyone
4     judges contrary to these or is judged, let it be void.
5     If an ox or a beast of burden or a sheep or something else
6     … [enters] into another's plot or garden
7     or [vineyard] or damages something or injures. The owner shall pay to the one wronged the damage which he might have caused by judgment. But before judgment no one shall take security nor forcibly seize anything.
8     by any pretext. And if anyone does any of these things, let him pay [immediately] 1000 drachmas and whatever he may have pledged [or forcibly seized], let him restore to the owner immediately. And let the tax-collector compel him.
9     appointed over the [royal] revenues.
