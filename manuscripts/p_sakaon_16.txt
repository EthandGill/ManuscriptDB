[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters Valerian
2     Licinianus Licinius Augustus and Flavius Valerian
3     Constantine [son] of the Augustuses, four days before the Kalends of April.
4     Saul and Melas and the associates, grain-collectors of Theadelphia, wrote off
5     on account of uncollectable names by divine judgment
6     four talents, amounting to talents 4.
7     And three days before the Kalends of April the same men wrote off [on behalf of]
8     the same village, on account of uncollectable names by divine
9     judgment four talents, amounting to talents 4.
10    And on the Kalends of April Sakao and his associates wrote off uncollectable names.
11    three talents, amounting to talents 3.
12    And four days before [the Nones of April the same men wrote off]
13    on account of [uncollectable] names [by divine judgment]
14    one [talent] and [thousand] drachmas, [amounting to] [one talent and drachmas Α].
