[TRANSLATION]
1   [Th]eogi[ton to Ap]ollonios,
2   [greet]ing.
3   Again […] to write to you …
4   … […] your doings …
5   …, though I am not practiced
6   in letters; and now, then,
7   I have tried again to write to you
8   before I take in hand to do
9   anything more drastic — if only you
10   will deal fairly in what concerns your mo-
11   ther. For you seem to me
12   to be quite senseless, in the present
13   month(?), not keeping your pledged
14   hand — for even if there were no
15   documents, yet thanks be to the g[od]s
16   that no presump-
17   tion has arisen on our part,
18   that you should think, without legal forms,
19   to put us aside. And now,
20   then, if you do not comply and ren-
21   der the allowance to mother
22   fairly, the consequence of
23   this will follow, and your greed
24   will bring you regret
25   yet again. For do not suppose
26   that your mother keeps quiet
27   about these things. Farewell.
