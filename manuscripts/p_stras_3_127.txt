[TRANSLATION]
1     Heracleides son of Heracleides to Chairemon
2     and to Ammonius also called Nicarius sitologoi of the eastern toparchy
3     of Psobthis, places, greetings. Separate off which you have [of] me [registered] in the account for wheat [of the] harvest
4     year 19 of Hadrian Caesar the lord [give] to Diogenides son of Heracleides of Psobthis artabas
5     twenty-three and one-half and one-eighth; [total] of wheat artabas 23 1/2 1/8.
6     Year 20 of the Emperor Caesar Trajan
7     Hadrian Augustus, Thoth 12.
