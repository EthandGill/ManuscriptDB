[TRANSLATION]
1     I Frange having written of the commandment: that it became
2     You gave a vessel for the house to come forth [until you know the vessel these], need is like need is, also you give a vessel
3     Until completion said the response, these, swift
4     I spoke this man bearing [vessel]
5     That he does need [them]
6     One joy in [the Lord]
