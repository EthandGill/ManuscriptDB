[TRANSLATION]
1     Harpocratio to Heraïs his
2     sister, greetings.
3     Before all else I pray
4     that you are well together with your children and all yours. I myself am writing to you in good health and praying
5     the very best for
6     you. Greet Apollonius and Dionysius and
7     Plutogenia and Eutychia
8     and her sons and her daughter. The nurse greets you, and Dionysius and Heron
9     and all my [people] send you their regards. I pray you fare well [with all] your household.
10    To Aurelia [Heraïs] from her brother Harpocratio.
