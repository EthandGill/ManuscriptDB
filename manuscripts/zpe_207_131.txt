[TRANSLATION]
1     Tryphas to Athenodorus his son and
2     Artemis his daughter, very many
3     greetings and [to be in good health] always.
4     Know the grain which you sent me [that] I have not received it ... . [I understood] for [that] it does not benefit me to receive it; from now on therefore [order it] to be lifted; neither Thoas nor anyone [should] exert the bodies [to extricate].
5     Each day moreover I am troubled by two ... -ators S...
6     ... and Gnomon ...
7     being weighed down and being held as security. Often I have written to [you] ...
8     if you should care for the bodies; they will die in prison; and you will do well having listened to me and having locked up your grain and to no one nothing
9     giving. For on the fifteenth [day]
10    it rose to a triobol and they fear lest it become more precious. Nardos and Nike greet you and all [those] in the house. Take care moreover of yourself so that you be in good health.
11    Be strong. Year twenty-three of Caesar, Pachon fifteen.
12    Deliver to Athenodorus the son.
