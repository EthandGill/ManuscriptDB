[TRANSLATION]
1     We received it in year 4, Me[cheir 1]4.
2     Horos to the village-scribes (komogrammateis), greeting. Of the letter from Eirenaios the
3     kinsman and finance-minister (dioiketes) the copy is appended below.
4     Having therefore [registered the property] in the places under your charge, to
5     [P]rotarchos you shall se[nd] to us, through the man exhibiting the instruction, the register … so that the rest may be carried out
6     in accordance with the [inst]ructions. Farewell. Year 4, Mecheir 10.
7     Eirenaios to Horos, gree[ting. Of the] letter to A[sklep]iades
8     the copy [is appended below. See to it therefore that] it be done accordingly.
9     [Year 4, Dystro]s 21, Tybi 21.
10    To Asklepia[des. Of the letter to Apoll]onios, the copy
11    is appended below. Attend to it [therefore that it be done accordingly] to the [things] additionally enjoined.
12    To Apollonios. We have read [what] you write concerning the matters [regard]ing
13    Protarchos, how, being appointed by Asklepiades the (official) in charge of the
14    revenues, ensyn…ei…n…os, that what was owed in it, against the
15    assessment, from silver, [he should de]posit and, more decently, in
16    his own house paying-in(?) until, having given account to himself,
17    he might make provision for the matters of the audit of the moneys — instead
18    of doing this, di…eis he had made the voyage down to the city,
19    so that, the matter having caused much confusion, in the exaction of the remaining sums owed toward the assessment (he) became [neg]ligent about his own affairs.
20    In order therefore that by public notice he be summoned, and if
21    he do not appear at … he be posted up, we have instructed
22    those in charge of these matters as is [fitting], and to Asklepiades we have made proclamation … [t]he [propert]y belonging to him against what is
23    owed [in] it … oi.
