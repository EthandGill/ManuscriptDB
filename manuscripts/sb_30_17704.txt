[TRANSLATION]
1     … [… Pr]ocles says:
2     seeing … [in the] laws being made
3     to some … the form putting on … and, which also Barbarus
4     [the] governor … [, that to no one in] a cloak to walk
5     [in] the laws [except to those being soldiered] is permitted. [Who]ever if
6     [contrary to] these [one] do, [the governor] having ordered [one] will be punished. To arrest [therefore the sus]picious and to send
7     to the governor with safety [the] caught.
8     Year 18 of Caesar, Epeiph 28.
