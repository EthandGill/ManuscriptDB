[TRANSLATION]
1     [A]sklepiades (son of) Charmagon,
2     to Portis (son of) Permamis, greeting.
3     I have received from you the rent (ekphorion)
4     falling to me and the crop-surplus (epigenema)
5     of the allotment which I leased to you
6     for the sowing of year 25,
7     an[d] I make no claim against you.
8     There wrote on his behalf Eumelos (son of) Herma( ),
9     having been requested, because he
10    writes rather slowly.
11    Year 25, Phamenoth 2.
