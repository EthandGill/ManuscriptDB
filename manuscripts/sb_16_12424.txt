[TRANSLATION]
1     to my lord brother Hermodotos
2     Anubian greetings.
3     Theognōstos having gone up perhaps
4     [recounted] to you the things concerning the complaint of the praepositus. Since then
5     of the same proposal it is, I was compelled to write to the land-owning woman concerning
6     him; which to the land-owning woman
7     letters having [read] give to her,
8     but also cause [her] to make clear
9     to him the copies as I requested.
10    I pray that you fare well, lord brother,
11    for [many] times.
