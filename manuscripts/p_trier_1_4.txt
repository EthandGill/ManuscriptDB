1     [aged …], … complexion, straight-haired, a scar on the forehead
2     [in the middle.]
3     Antigonos, a Macedonian, of the (troop) of Eukrates, of the third hipparchy, holder of eighty arouras, according as I, Antigonos, a Macedonian, made the contract,
4     of … of the same hipparchy, holder of eighty arouras …
5     of the (troop) of …on, a private person; and according as … made the contract, of the (troop) of
6     …, that in the 19th year, in the month Peritios, having borrowed from me of arakos 5 artabas according to a loan-contract, of which Hegesinikos is the keeper of the contract, on which was registered as your surety for the (obligations) according to the
7     contract, for payment, Didymos, a Macedonian, of the (troop) of Automedon, a private person, and according as … made the contract, of the (troop) of Pe—, holder of … arouras,
8     and the contract of the loan providing, among other things,
9     that you should give me back this arakos in the Egyptian month …;
10    … but if you do not give (it) back as is written, that you shall pay for each artaba of the arakos 200 drachmas, or however much more it may be sold for in the market at Herakleopolis, and the
11    time according to the contract having elapsed and much more (time)
12    having supervened, though demanded by me and repeatedly, the
13    5 artabas of the arakos you have not given back to me; wherefore I bring suit against you in the
14    court chosen by lot at Herakleopolis above Memphis,
15    according to the loan-contract, for the 1,000 drachmas of the price of the arakos,
16    for each artaba, at whatever it is sold for … in the market at Herakleopolis,
17    of copper coinage 1 myriad and a half (of drachmas) [i.e. 15,000], the whole of the copper coinage being
18    8 myriads of drachmas [80,000]. The assessment of the suit: of copper coinage 8 myriads of drachmas. The wrong was committed when, being demanded by me the aforementioned
19    arakos, you did not give (it) back to me. Year 22, in the priesthood of Marsyas, son of Demetrios, of Alexander [and the] Sibling Gods and the Benefactor Gods and
20    the Father-loving Gods [and the Manifest Gods]; the athlophoros of Berenike Euergetis
21    [being Demonike, daughter of Leonnatos]; the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos
22    [being Anaxiboule, daughter of Telestes]; the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator being Eirene,
23    [daughter of Ptolemaios; in the month …]. And you have been summoned; and the complaint
24    [I have given to you in person, in year 22, in the month …]
25    Year 22, Artemisios … Hathyr … [complaint]
26    of Antigonos against … , of 8 myriads of drachmas.
