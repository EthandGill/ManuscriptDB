[TRANSLATION]
1     I Frange, the least the first who died
2     Who dwelt in the darkness of ignorance, writing he greets
3     His beloved Lord's son according to the flesh his father's death and his wife
4     I greet also my brothers
5     Who were with father Victor and his wife, a greeting
6     In the Lord
7     Holy Trinity
8     My son, my father's death those of whom the darkness is in your holy cross go every work, you take [with you?] two mournings in your grave you go
9     These, quickly
10    Quick because the need is
11    Of my test
12    Of the work
13    Which he [?]
