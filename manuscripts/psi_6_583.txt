1     Concerning Paon of Ameneios. He has seventy-one sheep of the
2     35th year, Phamenoth, each per annum drachmas 2, three-obols
3     total … 11 months drachmas 162, four-obols
4     quarter-obol
5     and of the 36th year 12 months drachmas 177, three-obols
6     and of the 37th year 12 months [drachmas 177] three-obols
7     and of the 38th year 12 months drachmas 177, three-obols
8     and of the 39th year 12 months drachmas 177, three-obols
9     and of the 2nd year 12 months drachmas 177, three-obols
10    and of the 3rd year 12 months drachmas 1[7]7, three-obols
11    and of the 4th year 12 months drachmas 177, three-obols
12    to the same [total] drachmas …95, obol, quarter-obol
13    let him produce proof of this which has been appointed
14    tokens as appointed.
