[TRANSLATION]
1     [I]n the name of God. The community of the hamlet of Apa Phoibammon …
2     … to Hermopolis(?) through us, we, Pehew son of the blessed Enoch and the …
3     … the hamlet … writes to Apa Severus son of the blessed Shenoute, the man …
4     … that: since I divided (it) with you, I, Phoibammon son of Kollouthe,
5     … of one year, of days, reckoned (counting) from today, which is the twenty-fifth, that
6     [of Paope of] this year, this tenth indiction, until the twenty-fifth of Paop[e]
7     … [of] this year that is coming, with God, the eleventh indiction, [(I) be]ing(?) beside you
8     … to you without contempt; and you yourself shall [gi]ve me
9     … per year a holokottinos (solidus) and a trimesion, without any (defect)
10    … of it according to the manner, faultless, eighth-free(?), without subtraction(?), being secure
11    [for you, I have es]tablished this account for you, I agreeing, sw[ear]ing [to] God the Pantokrator (Almighty)
12    … to you according to [his] power, in the month Phaophi 26 of the [10th] indiction; the sign of [P]iew (Pehew). I, Pehew, the af[ore]said,
13    [write, I a]gree to this account [as] it is written.
14    [ I,] Ko[s]m[a son of G]eorge, the man of Hermo[polis] …
15    … this account …
16    … [I,] Ioannes son of the blessed R…rk…, the man of Hermopolis, give as witness
17    … in the month Phaophi 26 of the 10th indiction, I received through Phoibammon son of Kollouthos, from the hamlet of Aba Phoibammon, from the assignment of the canon (tax)
18    … of the [t]enth indiction, in counted coin, 3⅓ solidi only; sign of Piew, Piew (= Pehew) son of Enoch; Kosmas son of George agrees; the notary signed below.
