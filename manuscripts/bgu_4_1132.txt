[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos,
2   from Ammonios son of Dionysios of the Zeneion (deme) and from Alexandros son of Nikodemos
3   the Macedonian. Since, according to the agreements completed through the tribunal by
4   Ammonios and also by the deceased brother of Alexandros,
5   Theodoros son of Nikodemos, four agreements are indicated:
6   according to one, in the 15th year of Caesar, in Thoth, Ammonios
7   with respect to (the sums) he lent to Theodoros under a memorandum-
8   contract and under a diagraphe through the bank of Marion and [Theo]genes,
9   (of) 500 (drachmas), and that interest had been added through the agents
10   from the property of Theodoros in the Aurinites of the
11   territory of the Alexandrians, near the village of Syron, of plot 6, of the parcel of P[ai-]
12   deros and Abrytanos, dry land overflowed (by inundation),
13   which is of arouras 9 1/4 1/16 1/32, held in common, the remaining
14   share to him (being) (a half) and in the same plot and parcel another adjacent dry (land)
15   on the side toward the south, likewise overflowed, of arouras 5,
16   and in the same plot and parcel, likewise overflowed, arouras
17   1/2 1/4 1/16, and that there had been ceded to the same
18   Ammonios by Theodoros, through the agents, an additional claim of the (properties)
19   formerly of Aristios, of the homestead of Aristion in the village of Syron, and
20   of the adjoining workshops and vacant lots and house-plots,
21   and that the same Ammonios had received from Theodoros, out of the silver
22   of 500 drach(mas), 200 (drachmas), and had been paid the interest on the 500 (drachmas(?)) up to the
23   indicated time, and further also had received
24   into the account of the additional claim, of those (sums) which he
25   ceded to him, of the (property) of Aristios, of silver (drachmas) [.], and according to another (agreement),
26   the one made in the same year, in Phaophi, are indicated both the aforesaid things
27   and that, of (the sums) remaining with Theodoros out of the silver of 500 (drachmas),
28   300 drach(mas) had been further lent by Ammonios through the same
29   bank, another 500 of silver (drachmas), so that there came to be with Theodoros 800 (drachmas),
30   of which also he had carried over to Ammonios, in the same
31   agreement, a loan-agreement, and according to the third,
32   the one made in the 16th year of Caesar, in Hathyr, it is shown that the earlier
33   agreements were carried over and that Theodoros had been further lent by
34   Ammonios, through the same bank of Marion and Theogenes,
35   in Hathyr another 200 (drachmas), so as
36   to come, within m[onths 27, the t(otal)] of(?) silver (drachmas) 1200, which also[...]
37   [...]oi[...]e[...] to pay( ) ... the aforesaid agreements to Theodoros
38   [... agree]ment( ), and according to the fourth,
39   [the agreement made] in the same 16th year of Caesar, in Pauni[...].
40   There being no guarantee of the aforesaid additional claim <against> Ammonios through the
41   agreement made through the agents[...]
