[TRANSLATION]
1     […] Apion the brother, greetings and always
2     to be well. I wished indeed while present with you the
3     arrangement of your house to have shown. For I as
4     you know more cleanly conducting [myself] am hindered
5     through the doorkeeper, whose if you hear the attacks, not as
6     you would be displeased. For I think it is no longer
7     in a house but in a slave-dealer's place or something like that …
8     in difficult circumstances sometimes falling …
9     … concerning the figs …
