[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius [Poseidonius], strategos of the Oxyrhynchite
2     Aurelii Theon also called Ammonios of Eudaimon, mother Apollonia, and Thonios of Thonios, mother
3     Diogenida, both from the city of Oxyrhynchus. We swear
4     the of our lords [Publius] Licinnius
5     [Valerianus] and [Gallienus] Augustuses
6     pious fortune willingly
7     and [voluntarily] to pledge
8     Aurelius Heras son of Diogenes
9     phylarch, whom we shall also produce whenever it shall be sought, or we are liable
10    to the oath
11    Year 1 of the Imperators Kaisares
12    of Publius Licinnius
