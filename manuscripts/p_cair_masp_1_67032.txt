1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master Flavius
2     Justinian, the eternal Augustus and Emperor,
3     in the twenty-fifth year, after the consulship of Flavius Basilius
4     the most illustrious, in the tenth year, on the fifth day before the Ides of July,
5     in the fourteenth indiction, in the splendid and glorious city of Flavius Constantine,
6     Rome. These mutual acknowledgments and
7     agreements toward one another, without deceit and force and fraud
8     and compulsion and all circumvention, by willing intent and
9     free choice: on the one part Dioskoros
10    son of Apollos, and Kallinikos son of Victor, and Apollos son of John,
11    and Kyros son of Victor, through Senouthes son of Apollos, who takes
12    his place as he is absent, all originating from the village
13    called Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome of the Thebaid
14    province, and on the other part Flavius Palladius the most illustrious
15    count of the divine consistory, son of John of blessed
16    memory, and Epigonos the most illustrious count, both of them … …
17    originating from the most illustrious city of Leontios in the First
18    province of the Cappadocians, but recently residing in this
19    imperial city and acknowledging to one another upon the following
20    declared agreements. Having arrived here in this
21    imperial city, we the aforewritten Dioskoros and
22    Apollos and Kallinikos and Kyros, through me Senouthes who takes
23    his place, obtained a divine command against
24    our adversaries, those around Herakleios son of Psaios and the rest
25    from our village, and moreover also against any person whatsoever
26    proved guilty in accordance with the divine command for us,
27    and having petitioned according to law … the executive officer
28    of the matter, we have brought entreaties to your
29    illustriousness in the month of June of the just-begun fourteenth
30    indiction, by the Egyptian reckoning the fifteenth,
31    so that she, with God arriving in the Theban province,
32    may receive the said divine command, that is the obtained
33    divine memorandum from us, and present herself to the
34    local courts, and apply all goodwill and … …
35    and urgency and vigilance and execution …
36    to our matter, until the suit is handed over to its conclusion
37    for his complete discharge according to the force of the same
38    divine command, and produce before the court all
39    the persons enrolled in the same command under sureties
40    secure, and indeed also all the persons proved guilty
41    according to the force of the said decree, and to carry through to execution
42    until the conclusion of the suit, while we provide the informant's fees
43    and the expenses of the office, and receive the fourth
44    part of all the sportulae of the persons sued,
45    while the other half-quarter part of the same sportulae
46    is remitted to your illustriousness; and of the proceeds
47    coming out of the suit or before suit it is allowed for us to take, by way of profit,
48    on behalf of our losses and theirs, the half-quarter
49    part, and to your illustriousness the other quarter, so as to be
50    this on behalf of trouble and toil and her expenses
51    or also of the servant-boys belonging to her; and that she may not
52    in any way waver or transgress the foregoing
53    agreements, but carry them to conclusion reasonably and diligently and justly
54    in every clear manner, without any betrayal whatsoever
55    and trickery and postponement and delay and procrastination
56    and mockery, we too being ready under obligation to attend
57    upon the most glorious general of the province, and the …
58    whatever she, your illustriousness, will take in advance for us from them
59    until the suit of our matter is brought to conclusion
60    blamelessly and irreproachably and … to make execution
61    and to deliver to those being sued the document,
62    we are ready, at our own risk and expense, …
63    and to plead suits. And I the aforesaid most illustrious Palladius
64    acknowledge in turn that I am ready to abide by the things predetermined for me by you,
65    the agreements and acknowledgments, and to accept all and to carry
66    to conclusion in whatever manner aforementioned by you,
67    … to attend in these two
68    acknowledgments and not in anything to transgress … these things

73    the completion of your

78    master Flavius Justinian the eternal Augustus
79    and Emperor, … to abide by the things acknowledged

81    acknowledgments. And if we do not do this, the party not abiding
