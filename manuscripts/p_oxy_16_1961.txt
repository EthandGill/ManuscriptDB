[TRANSLATION]
1     643 (Chmg).
2     After the consulship of Flavius Long inus the most illustrious,
3     Pharmouthi 19, of the 10th indiction.
4     To the Aurelii Martyrios and Apphous, full brothers
5     by the father, of blessed memory,
6     Eulogios, who was a palatinus, from the splendid
7     and most splendid city of Oxyrhynchus,
8     Aurelia Mar[ty]ria daughter of Sarmates, from the village
9     of Ib[i]chis of t[he H]erakleopolite nome, greeting.
10    Willingly I undertake to lease, further, from the new-moon of the present month Pharmouthi
11    of the present year 163 = 132, of the present
12    tenth ind[i]ction, from the (property) belonging to
13    your nobility, situated in the same city
14    in the quarter of Pammenes' Garden, from a house
15    one room, facing south, with all
16    its right; and I shall pay as rent yearly, of silver, one thousand myriads, which rent
17    I shall repa[y] each year by half-year, the half, and whenever you should wish I shall deliver the same room for occupation. Valid is the lease,
18    written in single copy, and on being questioned I acknowledged.
19    I, Aurelia Martyri[a da]ughter of Sarmates, the aforesaid,
20    have made th[e] lease and I shall pay
21    the rent, and all the things written
22    agree with me as is set forth. I, Aurelius Serenos
23    son of Philoxeno[s], who was a deacon, having been requested, wrote on her behalf, present, since she does not know letters.
24    by me Serenos … it was paid, Pharmouthi 19, of the 10th indiction.
25    Lease of Martyria daughter [of S]armates.
