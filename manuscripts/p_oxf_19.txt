[TRANSLATION]
1     Serapias to Hermine, to the son
2     Greetings. Having been asked, do
3     the favor, so that you carry my [daughter]
4     when she gives birth, so that I may thank you, on Mesore 1st
5     Do not rob me concerning the
6     fare of the donkey,
7     so that I may be friends with you.
