[TRANSLATION]
1     [F]lavius Theodorus Menas Iulianus Iaccobus, the most magnificent count and governor of the province of the Thebaid, for the 4th time.
2     The time having come at which the care of the indispensable grain-transport of the fortunate tenth
3     indiction must be undertaken, shunning the reproach of all slackness, hasten to send this off to the great city of the Alexandrians
4     with all (due) fear, loading onto small vessels the required measure straightway and
5     at once, according to the appended notice, at the risk of the office; for it is for this reason
6     that there has been dispatched from the office. (Read.)
7     To those from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite (nome).
8     [I]t is necessary that from your village, in imitation
9     of the past ninth indiction,
10    the appended measure on account of the shipment
11    of the tenth indiction be brought in [and]
12    loaded onto boats, and the president an[d the]
13    superintendent and the sailors together also with the
14    boats laden with the produce
15    be sent off to the office for the customary [transaction]
16    of the records, so that thereafter there be sen[t out]
17    the same measure to the great city of Alexandr[ia]
18    of grain, by the kankellos (measure), 6053 artabas;
19    thus:
20    account through the small vessels, of grain by the kankellos, 2000 artabas;
21    account of the great shipment, of grain by the kankellos, 4053 artabas.
