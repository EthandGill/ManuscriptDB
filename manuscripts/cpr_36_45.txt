r.1    ὁ παρὼν γραμματηφόρος ἦλθεν λέγων ὡς
r.2    συναλλαγὴν ἔχει μετά τινων ἀπὸ τῆς κώμης Σιρ …

r.5    ἐστι πέμψῃ μοι αὐτοὺ[ς] ἐνταῦθα καὶ διοικήσω τὸ πρᾶγμα
r.6    αὐτῶν.

[TRANSLATION]
1     The present letter-carrier came saying that
2     he has a transaction with certain people from the village Siro …
3     [and] of the estate Ptal.; [therefore] let the esteemed [one] take up the matter
4     your friendship and manage [it] with justice. But if anything …
5     [there] is, send them to me here and I will manage the matter
6     of them.
7     [To] the esteemed one worthy of all honor Senouthios, manager, [from] Ioannes
