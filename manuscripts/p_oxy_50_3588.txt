[TRANSLATION]
1     22 of year, Thoth 2.
2     into the record of Julians, of Theon and Theon
3     noted by Matreus. From the [purchas]ed
4     in the 20th year of dry pitch was given to [P]ata the superintendent
5     of lower property for pitching the light wine-dealers [with him], [of] weight talents 24, the talent having minas 60, and the mina having staters 40.
6     Since therefore Pata declared that [light ones] were constructed with him until Mesore 13, [in] number 7,065
7     to which he accepts, as [from] the thousand pitch
8     talents 3, [he receives] talents 21 and minas 27, as [thus] to be recorded remaining with him talents 2 minas 33, but only
9     from the weighing which took place on the 1st [of the month] …
10    of Mesore was found talent 1 mina 38, so as to be sought minas 55, the price will be set of the 55 as much
11    as in the same 20th year [it] was purchas[ed].
12    … hemi … record … of the reduction
13    of pitch mina 55.
