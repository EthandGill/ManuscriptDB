[TRANSLATION]
1   Tryphon son of Dionysios, a Persian of the epigone, to Saraeus daughter of Apion,
2   with her guardian Onnophris son of Antipatros, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received
3   from you at the Sarapeion by the city of Oxyrhynchus, through the
4   bank of Sarapion son of Kleandros, in Imperial silver
5   and Ptolemaic coin, forty drachmas, and
6   as the value of one pair of gold earrings twenty silver drachmas,
7   and of a milk-white tunic twelve silver drachmas,
8   so that the total is seventy-two silver drachmas
9   of capital, to which nothing at all has been added, concerning which also
10   I have been persuaded. And the seventy-
11   two silver drachmas I will repay to you on the thirtieth of Phaophi of the coming
12   second year of Gaius Caesar Germanicus the New Augustus
13   Imperator, without any delay. And if I do not repay
14   as has been written, I will forfeit to you the aforesaid capital
15   with the half as much again, the right of execution belonging to you both against me and against
16   all my property, just as by a legal judgment. And if
17   we be separated from one another, it shall be permitted to you to keep the pair of ear-
18   rings at the equal valuation. And since we live together
19   with one another without written contract, I further agree that if likewise from a disagreement
20   we be separated from one another, you being pregnant, I will give to you
21   [on account of the ex]pense of [childbirth] [ ... ]
22   [Valid is the recei]pt, [wherever produced and] to anyone [who produces it].
23   [Year 1 of Gaius Ca]esar Germ[anicus the Ne]w Augustu[s Impera]tor,
24   Pachon 27, the Augustan (day).
25   (hand 2) [Tryph]on son of Dionysios: I have the seventy-two silver d[rach]mas
26   [of capita]l and I will repay as is set forth. Leon son of A[p]eros, I have written on his behalf because
27   he does not know letters. Year 1 of Gai[u]s Caesar German[i]cus the New Augustus Imperator,
28   Pachon 2[7], the Augustan (day).
29   (hand 3) Onnophris son of Antipatros: I have been registered as guardian of Seraeus. Theon
30   son of Paapis, I have written on his behalf as he does not know letters. Year 1 of Gaius Caesar
31   Germanicus the New Augustus Imperator, Pachon 27, the Augustan (day).
32   (hand 4) Year one of Gaius Caesar Germanicus the New Augustus Imperator,
33   Pachon, the Augustan (day). Through the bank of Sarapion son of Kleandros
34   the payment has been made. (hand 5) Seraeus daughter of Apion: I have received the
35   aforesaid capital of the capital and
36   I make no claim against you. Didymos son of Boethos wrote on behalf of
37   me, not knowing letters, and as her guardian a[ . . . ]
38   Year 3 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus
39   Germanicus Imperator, Pauni 15.
