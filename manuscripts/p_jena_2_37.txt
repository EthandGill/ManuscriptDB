[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum of articles purchased by me
2     in Alexandria through Menas the sailor, thus:
3     There were given to him into (his) hands 4 solidi less 20 carats by the standard of Alexandria, and
4     for the freight-charges of the boat, 6 solidi less 12 carats by the standard of Alexandria likewise
5     it comes to 10 solidi of gold less 32 carats, the carats 208
6     thus:
7     for 1 male peacock, 5 carats
8     for 3 female peacocks, 15 carats
9     for iron, 24 carats
10    for small wood … 6 cubits, 37 carats
11    [for] Spanish oil, 78 sextarii, 36 carats
12    [for] sweet-oil, 30 sextarii, 12 carats
13    for … fish-sauce, 24 carats
14    for … …, 12 carats
15    for fish-sauce, 16 carats
16    for cedar-oil, 12 carats
17    for … … … … white [and] black 16, and boli 3, 12 carats
18    if ever, having been of your …
19    … … … … … … …
20    … … of all the command, but also for my own favour
21    … … … to release my sailor and without loss
22    … … and if there be need of other carats whether
23    … … [in f]ull for the purchase of my memorandum
24    … … … favouring me, lend him the great things
25    … letters to Abba Herakleides to receive from me
26    … …, and I pray that you be in health continually.
