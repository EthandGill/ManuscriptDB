[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Zenon greetings. We have need
2     so that for the feeding of birds 400 and of brood-sows 100.
3     You will do well having purchased for us and having sent to
4     Ptolemais the [city] on the two mouths to Dioscurides
5     who is with us.
6     Farewell. Year 35, Panemus 28, Epeiph 30.
7     Year 35, Mesore 7. To Zenon.
8     Apollonius.
