[TRANSLATION]
1   Lucius Bellenus Gemellus
2   to Sabinus his son, greeting.
3   Do well then: on receiv-
4   ing my letter,
5   send me Pindaros
6   to the city, the field-
7   guard of Dionysias,
8   since Hermo-
9   nax asked me that he might
10   take him to Kerkesoucha
11   to look over his
12   olive-grove, since
13   it is thick, and
14   he wants to cut
15   trees out of it, so that
16   what is to be cut
17   be cut with skill; and
18   send the fish
19   on the 24th or 25th for
20   the birthday of Gemella.
21   So do not fool away
22   your olive-shaking.
23   Farewell. Year 4 of Imperator
24   Caesar Nerva
25   Trajan Augustus
26   Germanicus, Choiak
27   18.
