[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the village [of Soknopa]ios [Island], [order] is given to the undersigned robber-[catchers to come to the] public buildings of the village
2     and to search for the [sought-after] wrongdoers.
3     But if they are negligent, bound they will be s[ent] to the most brilliant
4     our governor.
5     And they are:
6     Seleuas [son] of Pakysis … . Paemmis, engineer.
7     Eieus [son] of Alatolus. Sotas [son] of Horion.
8     Outipsemis [son] of Iamos.
