1     In the second year of Gaius Caesar Augustus Germanicus,
2     Mesore the thirtieth, in the village of Sinaru of the lower toparchy of the Oxyrhynchite. Ischyrion
3     son of Dionysios lent to Hermogenes son of Hermogenes son of Demetrios the younger, and to Hermias son of Zenodoros the younger, both Persians of the epigone, in
4     the street, of silver of [Augustan] a[nd P]tolemaic coinage, [one hundred and eight] dr[achmas] of principal, to which
5     nothing [at all has] been added. Let [the borrowers] repay
6     to [I]schyrion the
7     [one hundred and eight] drachmas of silver on the thirtieth
8     of Choiak [of the coming thi]rd year of Gaius
9     Caesa[r Augustus Germa]nicus. But if they do not
10    repay [as is written, let the bo]rrowers pay back
11    [to Ischyrion the lo]an w[ith half as much again, and the interest for the t]ime over[run, the appropriate (rate), they being sureties]
12    for one another [for the payment, the right of execution belonging to]
13    Ischyrio[n both against them and against any one and against whom]ever
14    of them [he choose, and out of all the property belonging to them]
15    all [just as by legal action, he not being prejudiced concerning]
16    those [other things which Hermias owes either to Ischyrion or to] his [wi]fe by another secur[ity. Valid (is)]
17    the contract.
18    Hermogenes son of Hermogenes and Hermias son of Zenodoros the younger
19    we have borrowed the one hundred and eight drachmas of silver
20    of principal and we shall repay through sureties of one another

29    Through Achilleus the (clerk) at the records-office of the village
