1     Apollonius, leader of the guards of the Twenty-five Arouras in Ibion, to Mestasytmei, greetings. [Concerning] Diotimus [and ...]epimius and Petoseiris [son] of Psenesis the elder and [Petoseiris the younger]
2     and Petoseiris [son] of Horus of the guards from Tebtynis, appointed [by]
3     Ptolemaios, the chief-guard of the division, [those who] also having [signed the royal]
4     oath [to be] [present] at the grain-guard duty of the 29th year of the aforesaid Ibion
5     and Xylidis, did not show up, Petoseiris [son] of Psenesis the younger [for the watch]. Since then we foresee lest perhaps also the others abandon the [watching without any]
6     attention being made, we thought [we] ought to write so that, if it seems [good], you should [bring [it] before ...] …
7     of the successors and a supervisor concerning him, and the matter of [...]
8     toward him might come about.
9     Farewell. [Year] 29 [...]
10    To Mestasytmei.
