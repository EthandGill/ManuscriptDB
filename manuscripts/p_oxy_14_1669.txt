1     Horeion to Serenos, greetings.
2     You have not sent the receipts as
3     I commanded you, but you have not even written
4     whether they have refused, so that thus I may settle,
5     [n]or whether Diogeas the oil-dealer came,
6     so that we might also know his intention.
7     I commanded you to examine concerning the partnership contract whether it is being fulfilled, and to meet with Ptolemaios the grain-collector and the
8     account to send after you received it from
9     Apollonios and Heras, so that our household may not be troubled. So then if
10    now you do these things, [and you] hasten
