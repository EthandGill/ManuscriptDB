1     Serenos also called Dionysios leased,
2     son of Dionysios, former exegetes of the city of the Oxyrhynchi
3     tes, to Ptolemaios son of Papontos, of the mother
4     Tauris, and to Antonios son of Thotsytmis, of the mother Kaipous, both residing in the village of Phokoou, for a period of two years from the present thirtieth
5     year, of the (lands) belonging to him near
6     Phokoou, on the south of the holding of Trochos
7     which the aforesaid hold in lease
8     of the same Serenos, four arouras
9     a quarter, so that these in the present (year) they are to sow and
10    crop with wheat and barley, at a fixed rent of wheat
11    twelve artabas and of bar[ley]
12    fourteen artabas, but in the coming year to crop with green (fodder) for cutting and grazing, at a rent per aroura
13    of sixty drachmas. And further
14    he has leased to the same men, on the east of these, eight arouras by
15    survey, so that these too, in the
16    present year, are to be cropped with green (fodder) at a rent per aroura of twenty
17    four drachmas, but in the coming year to sow
18    or crop with barley at a rent per aroura of eight artabas of barley. And the lessees acknowledge that they have received here from
19    the lessor for seed
20    of the land four artabas of wheat at
21    interest of a third, all (being) free of risk
22    of every risk, the public (dues) of the land being upon the lessor, who also is to have ownership of the crops until all that is owed
23    each year is collected; and if any (land)
24    becomes unwatered in the coming year, it shall be allowed to the lessees. And the lease being guaranteed, let
25    the lessees, having gathered in,
26    repay the stated crops at the threshing-floor of Phokoou,
27    the wheat new, clean, unadulterated, free of clods, free of barley, sifted, as measured for the treasury, and the barley whole
28    new, clean, free of clods, well
29    trodden, without darnel and
30    husk, all by the receiving-choinix-measure of the landowner, together with the (interest) for seed and the interest, they measuring out to themselves what is from him, the right of execution belonging to him both against them
31    being mutual sureties for payment
32    [and against one and against whomever] of them he chooses …
