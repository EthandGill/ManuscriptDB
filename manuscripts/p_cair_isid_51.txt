[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelii Kastor Pankratios and Palemones Pa[…]
2     collectors of ship-freight charges of kinds year 16 and year 4, and Kassianus and Demetrius Valerianus, collectors year 18 and year 6, of village Karanidos and boundary-district
3     To Aurelius Palemones Ptolemaios, greetings. We received from you
4     through Kopres, in silver, drachmas two-thousand five-hundred,
5     totaling drachmas 2500.
6     Year 19 and 7 and 5 and 3, Pharmouthi 6.
