[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of Go[d]. K[o]rr[a ben Szerich, governor ] … [ to the administrator]
2     of the village of Aphr[odi]to. We have made [an allocation of craftsmen for the work]
3     of the mosque of Damasc[us, under] Iezid son of Themim, appointed for the present
4     seventh indiction; and we have assigned, through your administration, one
5     sawyer with his provision for six months in money-payment, and
6     having made out the receipts for this, we have sent them to you, entering into them what he owed
7     to receive for wages and provision and maintenance. Receiving therefore
8     the pres ent letter, according to the force of the receipts, be willing this man
9     to send to us in Babylon so that he may go out by the highway
10    to the same worksite. And if you find that the craftsman has slipped away,
11    of your administration, the one who was at the same worksite in the past
12    year, send him again with his tools so that
13    he may go out to the same work. But let it not seem good to you to send the money-payment (in lieu)
14    of such a craftsman, unless indeed the person himself be useful
15    … to do the wor k which was assigned …
16    … [ of the mos]que … [ for the rest]
17    d[o no]t neglect to send this man, lest you give a ground of complaint against
18    your soul. It was written in the month Mecheir 17, indiction 7.
19    Korra so n of Szerich, gover[nor] … [ to the administrator of the village of Aphrodito]
