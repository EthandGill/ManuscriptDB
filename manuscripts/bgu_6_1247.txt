[TRANSLATION]
1     To Santobithys, chief bodyguard and strategos,
2     from Esoroëris son of Petoseiris, of the epigone.
3     I am wronged by Neoptolemos son of Neoptolemos,
4     whose native city I do not know, who holds the established (post)
5     of soldiers in Syene. There being a house belonging to me within
6     the garrison-fort in Syene, and he himself being a billet-holder
7     in former times, while I was toward him
8     blameless, but now in the month Mecheir of the 33rd year
9     having deliberately set up a quarrel with me,
10    while on the one hand he reviled me with many and unseemly things, and using a certain
11    lawlessness he broke through the upper parts of the
12    upper house, so that I am continually covered with dust.
13    But not being content with these things, using the
14    wicked conduct concerning him, laying hold of me in the
15    courtyard, they were beating me with many blows on whatever
16    part of my body chanced, certain persons being present … and
17    … . And since, on account of this
18    cause, I have departed to the Ombite (nome) in …
19    … …
20    … he insults me beyond what is fitting.
21    And since you have arrived at the places as a helper becoming
22    to the one … being wronged, I request, if it seem good,
23    … us, that from the inquiry that takes place …
24    … of the investigation I may obtain my rights.
25    Farewell.
