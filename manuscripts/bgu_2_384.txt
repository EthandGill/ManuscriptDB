[TRANSLATION]
1     Epagathus to Cleopatra the
2     sister, very many greetings.
3     First of all I pray you
4     to be well, and the obeisance
5     of you I make before the lord
6     Sarapis. Inform me about what
7     I sent to you. I wished also to send
8     to you something else, and no one [receives]
9     [so that] it might be conveyed to you. I greet
10    Socrates and Marcella and all [your] by name
11    [Deliver] to [Cleopatra] from Epagathus
