[TRANSLATION]
1     Cresilaos to Artemidorus, greetings. [We] having sent
2     to the Koites frankincense cargoes
3     for the disposition there, [someone] reported that the one who transported [it] has prevented
4     you [from being able] to produce to you [that] it is necessary [to establish] the price
5     to be set. I wondered, therefore, from where
6     such a thing came to you to be done;
7     which indeed we have sent
8     Asclepiades and Semthea to dispose [of it] and to compel
9     those assigned the quarter of [ointments] to fix [the price] of that in them...
10    Year 39, Mesore 18. To Artemidorus.
