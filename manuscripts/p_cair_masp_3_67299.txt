19    to transform the said place and the cells in it,
20    and to rebuild them …, and simply, however to him
21    it seems, whether into better or into worse appearance to bring it, since the
22    fortune of the same place is at his risk, and that it be permitted to him

24    and without impediment. And Phoibammon in turn also acknowledges,

39    according to the exchange of the city. And the parties on either side acknowledge that it shall not be permitted
40    to either party to wrestle against the aforewritten emphyteusis or to proceed contrary
41    to it, but at all times and in all ways to keep it inviolate and unshaken.
