[TRANSLATION]
1     From Seouthos through me Philantinous to Heracles, greetings.
2     Give to Ammonius [son] of Ptolemy other [quantities of] wine
3     vessels four; [that is] vessels 4.
4     And to the landowner in the month of Tybi
5     present there for him to drink wine
6     vessels three; [that is] vessels 3. To the vineyard-workers by order of the landowner toward renewal
7     [of the] newly-[planted vines a] vessel, [that is] vessel 1.
8     To Thonios the farmer [of] Apollotas
9     for the price of light vessels 60, as
10    per vessel drach[mas 1?], the 60 vessels of wine vessels
11    four; [that is] vessels 4.
12    To Apollotheon by order
13    of the landowner wine vessels ten, [that is] vessels 10.
