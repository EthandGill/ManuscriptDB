[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonios, also called Ptolemaios, strategos of the Arsinoite, Herakleides division
2     from Apynchos [son] of Apynchos and partners, tax-farmers of grain of the village of Soknopaiou Nesos. [The land]
3     [having been] apportioned to the [aforementioned] village from the fields of the village of Bacchias, [the] land, for which the owing revenues are being paid blamelessly by us,
4     Since then it is also near Soknopaiou shore—
5     land, whenever it was not sown we managed [it] from
6     our own [resources] the [account] for it in very many [—]. But now in the present 28th year, the Nile having flooded and [our land] having been sown, those from Theogenes violently seized the land, which we also made clear to you
7     requesting them to submit the [account]. Wherefore
8     we present [and request] [you] to order [them] to be brought before you and
9     to hear us against [them], [us having recorded] our portions of the shore-land with the [overseers]
10    in order that from your [aid] we [might be able] upon the
11    our own [land], remaining [to] complete [the] [obligations].
12    Year 28 of Aurelius [Commodus Antoninus Caesar]
13    [of the lord].
