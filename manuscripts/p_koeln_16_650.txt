[TRANSLATION]
1     To Antipatros, chief-bodyguard and scribe
2     of the levy,
3     from Nikandros, son of Iolaos, a Macedonian
4     of those of Neoptolemos, a private individual of those
5     from Tilothis. . of Philippos, son of
6     Sosibios, of those of Arobbos, of the kinsmen
7     catoec cavalrymen of those from the
8     same (unit), who is harassing me …
9     [before] Euphranor the strategos
10    through which he has presented to him
11    a memorandum concerning [which]
12    he lays claim, contrary to what is [proper],
13    to a part of the cavalry-billet belonging
14    to me in t …
15    … ouses …
16    … stouto …
17    from the things written …
18    by Euphranor. And since [the matter]
19    concerns a part of the billet and [it is fitting] that it be carried through [before you]
20    and the cavalry-court in accordance
21    with the existing
22    ordinances concerning these matters and the other official rulings, I request, if it seems good,
23    that you order the copy of the memorandum to be appended for Euphranor,
24    so that he, knowing that the matter concerns
25    a billet, may condemn me in nothing
26    on the basis of the memorandum presented to him
27    by Philippos,
28    but may order him, if he thinks he is
29    being wronged by me, to obtain
30    justice before you and the cavalry
31    court. And this being done
32    I shall meet with fair treatment.
33    Farewell.
34    Write as is fitting to Euphranor.
35    Year [33] …
36    Year 33, Choiach 1.
37    He appeared.
