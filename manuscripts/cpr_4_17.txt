[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God first. I, Isaac, son of the blessed
2     Abraham, the monk of the holy Apa Jeremias on the mountain of Memphis,
3     write to Theodore, son of the blessed Apa Kyre Kontidon, the monk
4     of this same place: since they have cut … my head, the head of … of his boat
5     so that I might go … them in the … indiction …, I came and entreated
6     you, and you went for me … twice. Now therefore I have agreed with you according to
7     the manner in which God … your soul; may God lead the way
8     for you, and may you go to Babylon as much as you wish, you who are a strong man; as much as you wish
9     spend of your body, you who are strong; as much as you give of expense, it is yours; as much as you give of cost, it is yours;
10    forever I shall not turn back from you for ever, nor shall you yourself turn back from me
11    for ever; for this is the manner in which I have persuaded you, and there is nothing of mine that has come to your hand
12    beyond the wage of the king, which is these 4 1/2 coins, the entagion, the diagraphon … came forth … 12, and this charter (papyrus) is bound
13    in its manner … as witness. I, Abraham, son of the blessed Helias,
14    bear witness. I, Isaac … the chartularios, bear witness.
15    I, Philotheos the notary, son of Apa Nishterou, bear witness.
16    I, Paule, son of the blessed Jeremias, wrote, I bear witness, in the month Tybi 16, of the 6th indiction.
17    The charter of Isaac (and) Abraham, through …
