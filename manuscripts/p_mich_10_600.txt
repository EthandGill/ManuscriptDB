[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 20 and year 12 of our lords
2     Diocletian and Maximian Augustuses
3     and Constantine and Maximian of the most illustrious
4     Caesars, Epeiph 6. (They) wrote off Aph and Papeeis and the associates
5     of the price of grain of year 18 and year 17 and year 10 of Philadelphia, talents twenty, (making) talents 20.
