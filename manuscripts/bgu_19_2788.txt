1     [In the name of the holy Tr]inity, Fa[the]r and [Son and Holy] Spir[it; in the reign] of our most divine master Fl[av]i[us] Phokas, the [ete]rnal Augustus, Emperor, in the sixth year
2     … of the [in]dict[ion, in He]rmou[po]lis [of the The]baïd.
3     [To the public account] of the (city) of the Hermopolites … through you, F[la]vius Magistor, the most reputable assistant of the accounting-office and apportioner of the division [of Ge]rmanos, son of the (man) of blessed memory Kallinikos
4     [from the same cit]y, Aureli[us Phoi]bammon, son of … of mother Eu…as, farmer from the hamlet of Kouta in the fields of the village of Magdola Mire of the Hermopolite nome, greeting. I acknowledge, I
5     …os, the things subjoined below. Some time ago Leontios, of blessed memory, who was a monk and had become a calligrapher, took up from Joannes son of Menas the Younger, one of the assayers,
6     … of the … situated in [the p]lain of [the sa]me village of Magdola Mire, for the payment of public (dues), yearly, of nine carat[s,] and after some time
7     … Leontios the [afore]said, [of bl]essed [me]mory, afterward fell behind concerning the payment of the same nine carats … and the said amma (plots)
8     … selling(?) … the public (dues) … of the same nine carats, and that a farmer be appointed [b]y the assistant of the accounting-office in office at the time
9     … the amma allotted to him being under cultivation, the public (revenue) falls short yearly, and I myself too
