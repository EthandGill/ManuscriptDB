[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year …] of the Emperor [Caesar] Marcus Aurelius
2     Antoninus, Parthicus Maximus, Britannicus
3     Maximus, Pius Augustus, (month) Artemisios
4     Phamenoth 8, in the city of Oxyrhynchus,
5     under the overseers of the agoranomos-office, Dorotheon and his colleague.
6     Lucius Valerius Severus, also called
7     Melas, about 44 years old, with a scar on the middle of his nose, purchased from
8     Statoria Philoxena, about 50 years old, with a scar on the wrist
9     of her right hand, acting without a guardian according to the customs of the Romans by right of children,
10    in the street, the home-bred slave belonging to her, (born) of the female slave Isidora, Thonis
11    also called Epagathos, about 19 years old, squint-eyed, with a scar
12    on his forehead, examined by the
13    strategos of the nome, Didymos, in the present month, in accordance with what the purchaser submitted to him
14    of the examination in documents; the one (document) which, under
15    the subscription of the strategos, was unique, the same purchaser kept by himself; who also there received from
16    Statoria Philoxena the slave
17    Thonis also called Epagathos, such a one, not-to-be-returned except for the sacred disease (epilepsy)
18    and leprous-touch; and the agreed-upon (sums)
19    on behalf of the price of the same slave Thonis
20    also called Epagathos, of silver Imperial coin one thousand six hundred drachmae, on the spot Statoria Philoxena received from the
21    purchaser Severus also called Melas in full. Statoria Philoxena
22    sells and guarantees the same slave as set forth above, in the same street.
