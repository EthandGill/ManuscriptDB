1     [They have measured into the] joint-purchase of the crop of year 2 of Trajan Caesar
2     [of the lord, E]peiph 28, Psenchonsis, Pete … and Pete …
3     … [of wheat] four, they become wheat 4, and of-the-name Psenmn Onnophris
4     [of wheat half], it becomes wheat ½. Another, Mesore 3, of-the-name Psenmn Onnophris
5     … of wheat one third twelfth, it becomes 1, 3, 12. Kephalos (has) been marked.
