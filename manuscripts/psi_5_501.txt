[TRANSLATION]
1     Maron to Zenon greetings. [I have sent to you the] account of the expended money
2     … write to us, so that we may know
3     if you have received [it]; and, if it seems good to you, having shown …. You would do well having written
4     to Jason to give [papyri] [five] from Memphis to us; for they do not provide to each [one]
5     accounts to give; and may it not happen to me [to receive] [nothing] and to purchase papyri.
6     Farewell. Year 29, Pachon 14.
7     Maron. Concerning the expended
8     money and papyri. To Zenon
9     Year 29, Daisios 14, in [Alexandria].
