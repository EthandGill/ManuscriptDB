1     To Aurelius Apion, having held the office of exegetes of the most splendid city
2     of the Alexandrians and as he now acts owner of a third part
3     and of the remaining half part, lessee of the estate Aponian Berenice
4     from [A]matios Sokmenes and Neophytos, [his] mother Tharmouth,
5     of the two from the village Philadelphia. We wish to lease
6     from you concerning the aforesaid village Philadelphia, from a jointly liable plot, a cultivated plot, eight arouras, being from the west of the village, for a time of four years from the present 23rd year, for the harvest
7     [of the] whole, each year of the revenues and prepayments
8     ... [illegible]... and Kronion with seeds of barley
9     in artabas eight of barley in artabas seventy-two, unreckoned and risk-free, and it will not be allowed me within the time
10    to abandon the lease in no way, but under compulsion I will perform all the yearly works of the leased [land],
11    as is fitting, from my own [resources] at the proper times, doing no damage, and I will render the yearly [rent] in the month Payni by proper measure and [I will] return the arouras from dried grass,
12    and the rest from reed stubble all clear, if [you] appear to lease ... pairs of oxen eight. Aurelius Apion
13    having held the office of exegetes of the [most] splendid city of the Alexandrians and as he now acts d…
14    owner and les[see]... m... ch... to the...
15    year 23 of Marcus Aurelius S[everus An]toninus Parthicus Maximus, Britannicus Maximus
