[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Herakleides son of Ammonios, town-councillor,
2     overseer of the craftsmen dispatched to the alabaster-works of Alexandria,
3     to the Aurelii Pinnis, komarch of the village
4     of Theadelphia, and Kanaoug his colleague, greeting.
5     I have received from you the share falling to you
6     of the builder and of the carpenter, from
7     Thoth the new moon until Phaophi the thirtieth,
8     for two months.
9     In the consulship of Constantius and Maximus the most illustrious
10    prefects, Thoth 1.
11    In the consulship of Constantius and Maximus the
12    most illustrious, Thoth 9. There were paid out
13    (by) Sakaon and his fellow grain-collectors, out of the money-conversion (exargyrismos)
14    of the 14th indiction, at the higher valuation (pleiotimia) of Theadelphia,
15    fifty-eight talents, total 58 talents.
16    I, Herakleitos, through Horion, have signed.
17    And on the 14th the same men paid out,
18    out of the money-conversion of the 14th indiction, at the higher valuation
19    of Theadelphia, forty talents, total 40 talents.
20    I, Herakleitos, through Horion, have signed.
21    The Aurelii Gerontios son of Sarapion, through Ptoleminos,
22    and Gerontios son of Demeas and Sotas son of Asinos and their colleagues …
23    overseers of the workers of the bakeries of Alexandria, to the Aurelii
24    Kanaoug and Penis, komarchs of the village of Theadelphia, greeting.
25    We have received from you the sum falling to you of the worker
26    of the bakeries, from Mesore 1 until Thoth the thirtieth,
27    for two months, in full. I, Gerontios, have signed. I, Ptoleminos, have signed.
28    In the consulship of Flavius Constantius and Valerius Maximus the most illustrious, Thoth 14.
