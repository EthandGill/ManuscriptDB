[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings, lady Atari[on],
2     from Hatres the steward.
3     I wish you to know,
4     that the cuttings in the estate of Sairenous were stolen. We are weary seeking the places which we were watching,
5     with the public officials and
6     we found no sign.
7     I have transferred the wine, as
8     you said; send therefore the
9     sailors, lest it escape.
10    I wish you to be well with everyone.
