[TRANSLATION]
1     Pheiu the sailor, the one dwelling in Antinoopolis, approached as a man of your esteemed favor [the]
2     his courtyard he took. And as I believe he ought not to have done this, unless if it be [exchanged/altered]
3     for him his courtyard, and the right be preserved for him, and the neighbors of his house [be shown]
4     [to the] [esteemed], worthy of honor, friend Pappa, pagarch of Upper Apollonopolis. Theodore [writes]
5     concerning the courtyard of Pheiu [the sailor]
