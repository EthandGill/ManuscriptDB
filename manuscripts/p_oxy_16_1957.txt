1     Af[ter the] consulship of the Flavii Flor[entius and Dionysius]
2     the most illustrious, Pharmouthi 2.
3     To Flavius Flavianus son of Agathinos di … e …
4     from the illustrious and most illustrious city of the Ox[yrhynchites,]
5     from Aurelia[s] Didyme, daught[er] [of Horos, from the]
6     same city, transacting business without a guardian. Will[ingly]
7     I undertake to lease, from the present [month Pharmouthi]
8     of the present year 106, 75, of the present thir[teenth]
9     indiction, the three places belonging to you, [being]
10    in the same city, in the quarter of the Cavalrymen's Camp,
11    whole — both a dining-room and another dining-roo[m] [situated]
12    above the arch, and in the open court a single-roo[m,]
13    together with all appurtenances; and I shall pay you, for [their] rent
14    yearly, half a gold solid[us,] which of n[ecessity]
15    I shall pay each year by [the] half-[year, the] half, and when[ever]
16    you wish I shall hand over to you the [s]ame places a[s] [I rec]eived them. Valid is the lease, written singly, and having been questioned I ackno[wledged.]
17    Aurelia Didyme, daughter of Horos, transacting bu[siness wi]thout a guardian, as she herself confirmed, the afore[men]tioned, have leased [the]
18    aforementioned places and I shall pa[y] the rent [as aforesaid.]
19    I, Aurelius Theodoros son of Theodor[os, wrote on her behalf,]
20    she being present (but) not knowing letters.
21    Lease of Didyme daughter of Horos, three places, of rent half a s[olidus.]
