[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord [and] master Jesus
2     [Christ] our God and Savior and of
3     our [Lady] the holy Mother of God and of all
4     the [saints]. Pauni 16, at the end of the third indiction, in Arsinoe.
5     The Aurelii [Na]araus the headman, son of Abraamios,
6     and Serenos son of Georgios and Naaraus, capitation-collectors
7     of the expenditure, son of Menas alias Pareus, and Kyriakos
8     son of [Apo]llo, and Apaol the left-maimed, son of Pousis,
9     hailing from the village of Arabon of the Arsinoite
10    nome, to the reverend Petros the scribe, son
11    of Laeos, from the city of the Arsinoites, greeting. We acknowledge that we have received from your reverence
12    by hand, out of the price of the seventh-part
13    of the grain of the present third indiction, of the solidus,
14    one, payable, of twenty artabas, for now,
15    five solidi of pure gold,
16    each at twenty-three carats
17    of Alexandria — five solidi of pure gold, each at 23 carats of Alexandria;
18    and for your security we have made out for you this
19    receipt, being valid, and having been formally questioned we acknowledged (it).
20    The Aurelii Naaraus the headman, son of Abraamios, and
21    Serenos son of Georgios and Naaraus, capitation-collectors of the expenditure,
22    son of Menas alias Pareus, and Kyriakos son of Apollo, and
23    Apaol son of Pousis, the aforesaid: the present receipt is satisfactory to us,
24    of the five pure solidi at twenty-
25    three carats of Alexandria, as is set forth.
26    Through me, Ioannes. Through me, Ioannes.
27    … [it was] made by the Aurelii Naaraus the headman, son of Abraamios, and Serenos
28    [son of Georgios and Naaraus] capitation-collectors of the expenditure, son of [Menas alias P]areus, and Kyriakos son of Apollo, and Apaol son of
29    Pousis, to the reverend
30    [Petros the scribe, son of Laeos.]
