[TRANSLATION]
1     To my ladies, my mother and grandmothers,
2     together also with Kyra, Eudaimon, greeting.
3     I hastened even now to address you,
4     having found an opportunity, praying
5     to the divine Providence that, being cheerful
6     and in good health, you may receive the
7     letter from me. For Heraklammon, when he came, quite alarmed us, because he says our sister Kyra fell ill,
8     but we give thanks to the divine Providence which everywhere and in all things helps us, because she too has recovered. Let it be known that the linen garments of
9     our sister Kyrilla have been cut, and if
10    I find someone trustworthy going, I send them and the purple
11    hooded cloak (caracallus) and the sandals. We have received the goods from Helene
12    the embroiderer, and only four books
13    did I find in the double sack, but you wrote that you had sent five. We received
14    also all the other things except only
15    the jar of the pork-fat. Wherefore
16    let our brother
17    Theodoros make haste to seek out … the …
18    … and to learn about it
19    … … a jar; he gave, instead of the pork-fat, a jar of eye-salves (kollyria). And make haste to send me the little bronze tablet, so that I may make other tools, not the same ones, and the vapour-bath (steamer)
20    likewise and the cupping-vessels, so that I may make a …
21    And send also three pounds of eye-salves (kollyria) mixed from all kinds …
22    astringents and … … other good ones … …
23    Deliver to the surgery from Eudaimon.
