[TRANSLATION]
1     … on the day before the Ides of November, Hathyr 16,
2     [when the petition of Eulogius had been presented:] … [from the office.] The kind of petition which Eulogius, devoted palatine, and Megas have presented to your authority, holding (it) in (my) hands, I shall read out,
3     [if your greatness should so order. The governor of the province of Arcadia said: re]ad. And he recited: To your authority, from Flavius Eulogius, devoted palatine, and Megalos.
4     [The persons subscribed below, originating from ] … [ow]ed me a clear amount of gold according to the force of the bond made out to me by them, having mortgaged for the
5     [debt ] … [Since therefore there have pas]sed fifteen years from when by their flight they were eager to evade the debt, and no one appears who on their behalf
6     [answers me ] … of a house situated in this city, which is a part of the same things mortgaged by them,
7     … n, and that the official staff and the public functionaries of the Oxyrhynchites should take care of this. And there have become liable to me from
8     [the aforesaid bond ] … Serenos a[n]d Marty[r]ios, whom I ask your greatness to be compelled to be driven to fair-dealing and
9     … [pr]aefect lord. …
10    … [the governor of the province of Arcadia said. ][if] anyone appears … keis to … s … oi hypeph … oneke
