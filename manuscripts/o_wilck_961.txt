[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammonios, collector of the grain-tax
2     of the islands, to Phatres also called Phami.a, greeting.
3     You conveyed of barley, given to the cavalrymen
4     of the Herakleian squadron (eile), for the produce of year 28,
5     of barley, a third and a twenty-fourth of an artaba, total ⅓ [1/24] artaba.
6     Year 28 of Aurelius C[o]mm[odu]s
7     Antoninus Caesar the
8     lord, Pauni 29.
