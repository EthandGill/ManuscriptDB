[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelia
2     Dionysia, daughter of Theon also called Dionysotheon, a minor, through Aurelius
3     Apollonios, guardian, has leased
4     to Claudius Apelles also called Isidoros, son of Horion, a veteran of those honourably discharged, for four years
5     from the present 2nd year, from
6     the property belonging to her near
7     Sko, from the allotment of Pedieus,
8     of royal land, one and a half and a quarter aroura, so as each year
9     to sow and plant with whatever crops
10    the lessee may choose,
11    at a fixed rent per year
12    of eight artabas of wheat, free of every risk.
13    And if in the following years,
14    which may it not be, (the land) becomes unflooded,
15    an allowance shall be made to the lessee, the annual public taxes on the land
16    each year being
17    borne by the landowner. And let him have control
18    of the crops until he recovers what is owed each year.
19    The lease being guaranteed, the lessee shall of necessity measure out the
20    wheat each year into the public granary at each year's
21    first measurement, and shall deliver these to the landowner as a clean deposit, the right of execution being against the lessee as is fitting.
22    The lease is valid, concerning which
23    the lessee, having been formally questioned, acknowledged.
24    Year 2 of the Emperors
25    Caesars Titus Fulvius
26    Junius Macrianus and Titus
27    Fulvius Junius Quietus
28    Pii Felices
29    Augusti, Phaophi 18.
30    Claudius Apelles also called
31    Isidoros: I have leased the land and shall repay everything as afore[said]
32    and having been formally questioned I acknowledged.
