[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 2 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, month of New Augustus 28.
2     Year second of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, month of New Augustus eighth and twentieth,
3     in Tebtunis of the Polemon division of the Arsinoe nome. Akousílaos lent
4     Kleanoros, [about] thirty-five, with a scar on his right hand, to Paopes, about thirty-eight, with clear marks,
5     of the right eye and a scar beside the right cheek, and to Orseutes, about thirty-five,
6     with a scar on the left eyebrow, the two sons of Phomnasius, Persians of the descendants and [their] wives,
7     of Paopes [to] Thaubaste, Peeutos, about thirty-two, with a scar on the upper lip on the left,
8     [of] Orseutes [to] Tamustas, Peeutos, about thirty-seven, with a squint and a scar
9     on the right forearm, both Persian [women], with the guardian of each of the aforesaid [herself]
10    husband, the four being each other's sureties for the payment of the new clean
11    unadulterated grain-measures twenty-nine in principal, which they are to repay in month Pauni of the
12    present second year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus established into the village
13    Tebtunis, by measure of four choinikes of the treasury of Livia Augusta, in which measure they received [it].
14    But if they do not repay [as] it is written in the fixed time, the
15    borrowers shall pay to Akousílaos immediately the price of each grain-measure of silver
16    drachmas nine, or the highest price that will be in the village, the right of [recovery]
17    [being] with Akousílaos or his agents of those [according to the contract [from both the] [bo]rrowers [and] from whichever of them he chooses and from the] property [all of theirs] as if from a judgment, apart from other things which Orseutes owes … [ to]
18    [Akousílaos] [under an]other security. The contract is valid …
19    … of them …
