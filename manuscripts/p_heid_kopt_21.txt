[TRANSLATION]
1     … [he/she] the man dwelling in the village of the city which is called … through you, you the lord George the greater, [I testify]
2     that I am a debtor … I owe in Babylon, now therefore I am ready to give to him
3     his [portion] before our lord the illustrious; if it be for me, I will give to him his [portion]
4     I am ready, I am full in wholesome silver on the debt without deception without law
5     without blame in himself without ambiguity … God almighty, that [I will not] transgress
6     [the] testimony before her strength; it was written Phaoph 10, 20 … sign … sixth hour …
7     I … Wehchor the son of Helias [I mark my sign] to the testimony below, which is written according to it, the …
8     I Djol the son of Lemahshi the man of witnesses, I heard [it] and I testified concerning him, that I saw not the writing, and I am the witness
9     sign of P… sign of Menas
