1     Of the 15th year of Emperor Caesar
2     Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus
3     Augustus Pius. He has worked for embankment
4     works of the same 15th year, from Mesore 9

6     of Karanides, Ptolemaios (son) of Phanomgeos

8     Serapion the sower [has] marked.
