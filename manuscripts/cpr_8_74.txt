[TRANSLATION]
1     [With] God, Fl[avius] Ati[as, most renowned du]x, to you [the residents from the village of Dikaion of the] [Arsin]oite [pa]garch[ia], [through Epim]ach[us]:
2     Provide to the [men] of the third portion of Modr, turma [of] Lephif, of the sons of Theoem, son of Alerth
3     man Omaura, son of Abdella, on behalf of [his] portion, roga of names 643, [for] 8 months, from grain artabas 1164
4     grain, artabas 150, one hundred fifty only, through [the] dispatch of Abdelaziz, councillor
5     [having] been brought through Kosmas, man of Rabia, from the remaining payment of the past indiction 11
6     And if you do not have grain, provide to them at 20 artabas [to] 1 small coin, one only. Mesore 27, indiction 12
7     Dispatch of grain [roga] of Moagaritoi, village Dikaion, … from payment
