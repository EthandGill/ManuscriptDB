[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum to Zenon
2     concerning the boat. The
3     tax to the king is 29 drachmas, 3 obols
4     and the wage to the sailors,
5     being 3, to each 7 drachmas, 3 obols,
6     total 22 drachmas, 3 obols; to the helmsman, 10 drachmas,
7     total 32 drachmas, 3 obols; and for the year, 390.
8     Or I shall give to the sailors
9     to each a wage of 6 drachmas
10    and of wheat 1 ½, to the helmsman 8 drachmas
11    and of wheat 1 ½; for the year, 312
12    and of wheat 72 artabas. This is
13    of silver 680, 3 obols
14    and of wheat 72 artabas.
15    Or I shall give to you a rent of 500 drachmas,
16    and the tax you shall assess,
17    and if any need of the boat
18    arises for you, from the aforesaid
19    rent and wage it shall serve (you).
20    Or I shall both feed the sailors
21    and assess to the king
22    the tax, and provide the
23    boat for your needs, and you shall
24    give me for the year 72 artabas of wheat
25    for the sailors, which they must
26    be measured-out monthly, but for myself I do not enter a wage,
27    except you shall give me of wheat 1 ½.
28    And you shall give me the sails and timber
29    for the building of the boat
30    for the repair and shipwrights,
31    this being the expenditure
32    of a sail, 12 drachmas, and to the shipwrights, 6 drachmas
33    nails for the rudder, 8.
34    To the king … tax, 4 months
35    to the sailors, 4 months
36    to you, 4 months.
