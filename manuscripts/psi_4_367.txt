[TRANSLATION]
1     Damis to Zenon greetings. You will do well especially by returning
2     the cow to the farmer; if not, send the one who took [it] out
3     to us; for they report to us that you have placed him in custody. And I have also made the pasture allowance for the Arabs, as you wrote to me,
4     [with] others already having [it]; and they too will report to you.
5     Be well. Year 36, Thoth 23.
6     To Zenon
