[TRANSLATION]
1     To the village-scribe of village B[akk]hiados
2     from Diogenes of Limnai
3     from village Karanidos.
4     According to what was ordered I declare the[se] properties belonging to me
5     concerning village Bakkhiada
6     of a residential allotment [of] 14½ arouras
7     being assigned as crop-obligation on the name of Petheos Hatrei[ou] and
8     [b]eing assessed [to]
9     … … … without-rain [toward the] present
10    11th year of Aurelius [An]tonei[nus].
11    I, Diogene[s], have submitted [this].
12    In the 11th year of Aurelius Antoninus
13    Caesar our lord, [the] Armeniakos
14    Medikos Parth[i]kos the greatest.
