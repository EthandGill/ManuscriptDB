[TRANSLATION]
1   ☧ Flavius Dorotheos, advocate (scholastikos) and defensor (ekdikos) of Hermopolis,
2   to Flavius John, the most learned advocate from the
3   same city. On the present day, which is
4   Phaophi the second of the present fourth indiction,
5   Matheias son of Banos, a farmer from the village of Sigkere,
6   deposed before me and the assisting staff that Matheias
7   son of Hatres, from the same village of Sigkere, his fellow
8   farmer, has shifted the boundary, to the injury of your splendour,
9   upon the arouras farmed by us, both mine
10   and the said Matheias' own, and has ceded to the neighbour
11   [...] from [those same] arouras
12   eight cubits in width and eighty palm-fronds(?)
13   in length. And as proof of what has been said,
14   I have issued this sealed attestation with my
15   subscription, as set out above. † ☧ I, Flavius Dorotheos, advocate and defensor
16   of Hermopolis, have issued this deposition, as set out above. †
17   † In the consulship of Flavius Philoxenos, the most glorious,
18   Phaophi 2, 4th indiction.
