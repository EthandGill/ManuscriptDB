[TRANSLATION]
1     … [for the unwatered, if it not be,] [provide]
2     the half of the aforesaid rent, which rent new [pure]
3     sifted, I will give you by measure at the proper time of gathering
4     of each year without fail in the month of Epiphi, and I will restore
5     the rent itself into your house in Hermopolis with my own animals
6     The lease binding and firm, and being questioned I acknowledged [it], Aurelius
7     Josephus [son] of Colluthus, the aforementioned, I have leased as stated
8     Apollon [son] of Kallinikos, subdeacon from Hermopolis, I testify to the lease [having heard] from the lessor
9     Phoibammon [son] of Basilides, from Hermopolis, I testify to the lease, having heard from the lessor
10    Through me, Hermes, it was written
