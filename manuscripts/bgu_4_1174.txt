[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos.
2   From Lucius Maccius Meleager, and from Herodes son of Apollo-
3   nios, of the Althaieus deme. Lucius agrees that he has received from Herodes,
4   by hand from his house, in full discharge - also on behalf of his wife Hermione
5   daughter of Dionysios - those (drachmas) which he had lent <to him> by agreement registered [through the same] court
6   in the
7   21st year of Caesar, ..., namely five hundred Ptolemaic silver drachmas,
8   together with their interest; and that the loan-agreement is to be void,
9   and that neither Lucius nor anyone on his behalf shall proceed against Herodes,
10   neither concerning the loan and the interest nor [concerning anything else]
11   at all, written or unwritten, of matters up [to the present day],
12   or he shall be liable to the [fixed] penalty, apart from [the validity of]
13   the things [agr]eed. We request (registration).
14   Year 26 of [C]aesar, Ha[thyr 8].
