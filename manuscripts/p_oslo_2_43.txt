[TRANSLATION]
1     I acknowledge to have from you by hand a price-of-wine in four-handled pottery vessels so-called, into which I will render light [wine] and will repay to you in Hathur
2     Month of the incoming 5th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord in wine-measure of Theon [the cousin] of-your, at-the Monimos estate in the sun-drying-floor of-the same
3     From the vintage [of] the present 4th year of-vineyard of-the same cousin of-you, which [I] have in-lease, from-which if you-choose [from-the] wine-press
4     Without-delay. But if not, I will pay to you the [price] at-the time
5     Price double with two-drachma interest becoming to-you of-the
6     Execution from both me and of-all my-possessions.
7     Valid [is] my own-written hand.
