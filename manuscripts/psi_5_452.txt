[TRANSLATION]
1     [From the famous Aurora] [daughter] of Apollo from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites … and now your majesty knows
2     … [enslaved] … and (a) rule to establish [by one of the partners] … how it was necessary something to be done on the description of the other part. For
3     [the authority of so-and-so] defined the document made thus towards deception neither to the one issuing [nor indeed to the one] receiving to furnish any benefit, but both to be deprived and to the partner an entry [to be.] But in addition to what was left to us by our parents, to me
4     [and to the brother] Eusthokios and enslaved persons came to both of us in equal share.
5     [Of these the] brother indeed indifferently and without writing, as is customary, held some, and I (held) others.
6     [But it has happened that] the servants being with the partner Anatolios and Makaria and her [grandchild], having planned wickedly, [or] rather scheming, were able to evade
7     [the bond] of slavery, without our knowledge to forge and perhaps persuade
8     [the] brother [a] letter of release to receive on the description of the service owed to me from [them], and then having repented, [or] rather also having known the things from the
9     [decrees] determined, the same [to] wear away to refer to Sabinianus's command
10    [the matter], as if he being able [and] without some written division of (the) holding
11    … of these given … of the auditor on records rejected
12    … at the entry … it was necessary (the) whole to be held in common and (the) right
13    … to be said to both, but it happened marked in the same
14    … concerning Anatolios … he was speaking first [that] he received freedom regarding …
15    … and concerning the others. [This being spread around,] testifying I hasten [to write the matter in] good judgment and finding fault with that stale letter on account of (it) not guarding any of my [rights]. And not even thus the auditor agrees with their ironic tricks, but, as
16    [even before,] intact the mastery for both he preserves. Since then the one partner
17    … his own rights he provided, and the servants leaning on his right hand, as they say, [deny] our service, I ask for your good conscience and to strengthen me
18    [now against them] and to order the judge to examine the truth of all these things, and by
19    [your signature] to compel the servants, who are subject by (the) bond of slavery, to hold fast to
20    [our] service, so that, having obtained justice and the legitimate benefaction, I may confess gratitude to you [forever], lord.
