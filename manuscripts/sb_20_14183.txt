[TRANSLATION]
1     To Agathos, general and cavalry commander,
2     from Sentheos son of Sochotes, royal
3     farmer and village scribe of the village
4     of Lysimachia. On the 7th of Tybi of
5     the 7th year, [when] my pair of oxen traveling on the royal road so as to
6     plow, there came upon [me] the
7     [oxen] of Antigonus, hundred-aroura [landholder], from Ph[...]chius
8     who lives in Lysimachia, [his] pair
9     and having leaped upon ... them, a bull
10    ... gores him on the
11    [7th, and at the] end of the month [he] died,
12    ... whom I value at eight thousand drachmas. I ask therefore, if it seems good to you,
13    may [you] prosper ...
