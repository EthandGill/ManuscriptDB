[TRANSLATION]
1     [because in the past year also I did not have the customary water,]
2     but now not only has he none, but neither has the village;
3     of necessity I have written to you, so that you may make these things clear to Apollonios,
4     that he may at last know (of them). Let him furnish the customary water to all the estate properties, or let him know that I shall report him to the most excellent procurator concerning this,
5     since in the year before this too, on account of this marsh, a rent for the year
6     was assessed against me by the most excellent procurator. I pray for your health.
7     Year 11 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, Thoth 30.
8     The copy of the letter was sent to Apollonios, the beach-guard, as follows.
9     To Apollonios, the beach-guard.
10    A letter of Epithymetos, assistant of Eirenaios, procurator
11    of the lord Caesar, concerning a request of Pamphilos also called Sokrates,
12    lessee of the marsh around the village of Theadelpheia, who complains of you
13    as not having furnished him the customary water, concerning which he says
14    he had written to you: I have appended the copy, so that, since through me too
15    he wished the things he wrote to be made clear to you, you may know and take care
16    that the customary water be furnished.
17    Year 11 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Phaophi 3.
18    And to Epithymetos it was sent as follows.
19    The letter sent under your name from a request of Pamphilos
20    also called Sokrates, lessee of the marsh around the village of
21    Theadelpheia, I have made known to Apollonios the
22    beach-guard, as is set out below.
23    Year 11 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Phaophi 3.
24    And there has been appended the copy of the aforementioned
25    letter.
26    Thus far the letter of Epithymetos.
27    To which reply was written to Apollonios the beach-guard as follows.
28    Theon, strategos of the Arsinoite, of the Themistos and Polemon districts, to Apollonios the beach-guard,
29    his dearest friend, greeting.
30    A letter.
31    And there has been appended the copy of the letter of Epithymetos.
32    Thus far also the (documents) by Theon of the letter to Apollonios
33    and to Epithymetos of the letter.
34    Year 12 of Antoninus Caesar
35    the lord, Pachon 25,
36    in Theadelpheia of the Themistos (district).
