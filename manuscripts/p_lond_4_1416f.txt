[TRANSLATION]
1     Month Hathyr 14, indiction 3, by a letter brought concerning 3 men on account of the Klysma, through Melee the courier …
2     Aphrodito, 5 men; Pediades, 1 man …
3     Brought in the month Hathyr 23, indiction 3, on behalf of part of the wage and expense of 1 auxiliary (named), being in the service of the …
4     5 (men) of Pediades, 3 of money, 1 artaba of grain; Pakauneos, 6 of money, 1 artaba of grain …
5     On behalf of part of the wage and expense of 2 auxiliaries (named) for the service of the overseer of your village for 12 months, … nomismata.
6     Thus:
7     Of the village of Aphrodito, 2 names, 3⅓ nomismata, 12 artabas of grain; 5 (men) of Pediades, … nomismata.
8     2 (men) of Pediades, 3 of money, 2 artabas; Pakauneos …
9     Brought in the month Hathyr 23, indiction 3, through the order of the assessor, on behalf of public taxes of indiction 1 owed to be paid …
10    Thus:
11    Of the village of Aphrodito, through Senouthios son of Enoch, 1337½⅓ nomismata; 5 of Pediades …
12    3 of Pediades, through Joannes son of Kallinikos, 189 nomismata; 2 of Pediades …
13    Of the men of Holy Mary, through Psatos son of Iakob, 9 nomismata; of the monastery …
14    Of Pakauneos, through the same man, 72½ nomismata; of the Emphyteutai …
15    Of Bounai, through the same man, 16 nomismata; of Keramion …
16    Of Poimen, through the same man, 21 nomismata; of Psyros …
17    Of (the place of) Holy Pinoution, through the same man, 33 nomismata; of Sakoore …
18    Of the mountain of Abba Hermaotos, through the same man, 11⅙ nomismata; of the monastery of Taroou …
19    Of Pharoou, through the same man, 3 nomismata; of Barbaros …
20    Of the mountain of Holy Mary, through the same man, 24 nomismata.
21    In all, 2385 nomismata.
