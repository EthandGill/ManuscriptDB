[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidoros to Heroneinios
2     to the dearest, greetings.
3     I sent Sauelos
4     in order that you [immediately] fill him with artabas of bread
5     two. But do not keep this as secondary
6     and do not be negligent in the work; very pressing, for you know
7     the trouble of
8     Dionysiad, now,
9     that not even to those present
10    are we able to listen. [be well] you,
11    I pray, dearest.
