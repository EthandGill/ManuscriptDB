[TRANSLATION]
1   † In the reign of our most divine and most pious master Flavius Justinianus
2   the eternal Augustus
3   and Imperator, in the 24th year, in the 8th (year) after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most illustrious,
4   Phaophi 22, of the 14th indiction, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites. †
5   To Flavius Apion the all-praiseworthy and most exalted, of the ordinary ex-consuls,
6   landowner also here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas the servant who interrogates
7   and procures for his own master, the same all-praiseworthy man, the right of action and
8   liability, the community of the chief villagers of the village of Takona of the Oxyrhynchite
9   nome, administered as a pagarchy by the house of your gloriousness, through us, the Aurelii
10   Phoibammon the headman, son of Pekysios, and Anoup son of Aritsi, and Menas his brother,
11   and Koulaetb son of Ioannes, and Anoup son of Priskos, and Herakleides son of Palmas, and Phib son of
12   Ioulios, and the rest of the village-officials of this (village), greetings. We acknowledge that we have received from
13   your gloriousness on loan and have had measured out to us, from the produce of the present
14   fourteenth indiction, for the seed-sowing of our arouras (with) the crops of the
15   fifteenth tax-cycle, God willing, of dirty wheat without sifting-deduction
16   by the kankellos-measure, two hundred artabas, the same also given to us through the heirs of the
17   late Menas son of Asklas, shipmaster of the same glorious house of yours, total: wheat 200 artabas by the kankellos;
18   which new wheat, sifted, by the kankellos-measure by which we received it, of necessity
19   we shall repay to your gloriousness together also with our transport-rent in the month Pauni
20   of the present year 296 (era reckoning) of the same present fourteenth indiction
21   from the new crops of the fifteenth tax-cycle, God willing, without delay, at the risk of
22   the property belonging to us pledged for this. Valid is the document, drawn up in single copy,
23   and having been asked we gave our assent. †
24   (hand 2) The community of the chief villagers of the village of Takona of the Oxyrhynchite nome, through the aforewritten
25   names in it, (issued) this document to your gloriousness for the wheat, two hundred artabas
26   of dirty (wheat) without sifting-deduction by the kankellos, total: dirty wheat 200 artabas, and we shall repay within the said term
27   and everything written is agreed by us as stated above, † and having been asked we gave our assent
28   and we discharged (it). (I,) Aurelius Herakleides scribe of the village of Takona, having been requested, wrote on their behalf,
29   they being illiterate. †
30   (hand 1) ☧ through me, Philoxenos, it was completed. Phaophi 23, of the indiction 14 .... † (verso)
31   † Document made by the chief villagers of the village of Takona
32   on account of seed-sowing of the crops of the 15th tax-cycle, of wheat by the kankellos 200 artabas.
