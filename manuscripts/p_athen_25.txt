[TRANSLATION]
1     In the seventh year of Nero C[la]udius Cae[sar]
2     Augustus Germanicus Imperator, in the month
3     Daisios 26 = Pharmouthi 26, in Karanis of the [Herakleides]
4     division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges Petesouchos
5     son of Horos, about 58 years old, with a scar on the forehead on the right,
6     to Pekmeis son of Mesoeris, about 29 years old, with a scar on the left hand,
7     that he has sold to him the Theban mill belonging to him,
8     together with its table and
9     handle, this such (mill), not to be rejected,
10    and that the acknowledging party has received from
11    Pekmeis the agreed
12    price in full, from hand to hand, out of the house,
13    and that the acknowledging party will guarantee (it) with every
14    guarantee. Subscriber: Korax son of Apollonios, 30 years old, with a scar on the right cheek,
15    and Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios, 40 years old, with a scar on the left eyebrow.
16    I, Petesouchos son of Horos, acknowledge that I have sold
17    to Pekmeis son of Mesoeris the Theban mill belonging to me,
18    together with table and handle,
19    this such (mill), not to be rejected, and I have received the
20    price and I guarantee (it) to you with every guarantee,
21    as stated above. Korax son of Apollonios wrote on his behalf,
22    since he does not know letters.
23    I, Pekmeis son of Mesoeris, have bought (it) as stated above. Ptolemaios son of
24    Ptolemaios wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters. It has been registered for record
25    through the record-office in Karanis, Pharmouthi 26.
