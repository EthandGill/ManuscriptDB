r.1    Σωτήριχος ὁ ἐξειληφὼς τὴν τῶν κασοποιῶν ὠνὴν Κοπτίτου εἰς τὸ λη ἔτους Ὥρωι χαίρειν·

[TRANSLATION]
1     Soterichos, the one having taken up the contract of the weavers of Coptos for the 38th year, to Horos, greetings;
2     has been ordained what is due to you, [the] tax
3     Pachon; [in the] 38th year, Pauni, [the] 5th.
