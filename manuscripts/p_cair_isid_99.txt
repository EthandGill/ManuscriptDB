[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Zoilos, son of Apollonios, prytanic and exegetic
2     attendant and however he is styled,
3     from Aurelius Isidoros, son of Ptolemaios, of his mother Heroeis, from the village of Karanis.
4     I wish to lease from you the (lands) belonging to you around the village of Kerkesoucha Agora,
5     being … the boundary-district of Karanis, in the place
6     called Hippa… ei, of public land four arouras,
7     and in the place called Kaleix, of private land four
8     and a half arouras, in the same place another private aroura, 1½,
9     all [10 arouras], or however many there be, for a period of
10    three years from the sowing of the present 2nd year, the rent
11    of the whole each year being of wheat
12    twenty artabas, total 20 artabas, exempt-from-deduction and free-of-risk, and it shall not be permitted to me within the term
13    to abandon the lease in any way,
14    but I shall of necessity perform all the yearly
15    works, all that are fitting, from my own (means) at the proper
16    seasons, doing not even one (bit of) damage; and the yearly rent I shall pay in the month of Pauni
17    at the threshing-floor by the four-choinix measure, all the public (dues) being upon you the
18    owner, and after the term I shall hand over
19    the arouras from fallow and stubble
20    equally, [if] it seems good to lease. And having been formally questioned I agreed.
21    Year 2 of our lord Lucius Domitius Domitianus Augustus, Thoth 1.
22    I, Aurelius Zoilos, have leased as aforesaid.
