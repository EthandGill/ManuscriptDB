1     By [the present] Ammonius and we demand him to restore to us the six talents and [was present]
2     [Witness]. [Nicantinus] said: What does the witness who was brought say? Antinous through an interpreter answered: Not once or even a second time did Gunthus entrust money to [me]. [Nicantinus] said to him: Concerning what? Through an interpreter answered: He gave me a small unnamed coin, so that this
3     Ammonius had
4     I would sell to him and I sold. Nicantinus said to him: Concerning the six talents what do you testify? Through an interpreter answered: I received
5     six talents
6     Six talents and I gave them to this Ammonius, him requesting Gunthus to have need [of these]. Ammonius said:
7     Nothing [of these]. [He lies]. [Gunthus] said: Let his wife swear to me that she did not receive the six talents, and if she swears, I will release them to him. [Ammonius said]: I will make her swear. If she does not swear, I give them to him.
