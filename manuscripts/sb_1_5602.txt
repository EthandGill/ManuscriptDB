[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit
2     It was written in the month of Pharmuthi, indiction 4, year of the Saracens 164; this irrevocable [gift]…
3     unshakeable, eternal in time, concerning which I myself, [the] joint-holder, [the] son of Panias
4     and my wife Tasia, [having] come to the fortress of Upper Egypt in the nome of the city of Hermopolis, because at this time we came [and] had one son
5     and [we] named him Panias; and God [granted] him great health; [but he then] came toward death; and we resolved from [the] heart according to God, [that] anyone on earth might give our son the boy
6     to the monastery of the holy abba Phoibammon of Upper Egypt
7     in the land of the fortress, [just as] it came to us; therefore we presented [it] in the document entirely
8     [and] we took him to the monastery there [and] we placed [certain] baptisms
9     of them in the holy place [and] laid [upon] his body [and] the relics of the martyr there; grant him a blessing; he withdrew year [by] year [and] we begged the steward overseeing the place [that] she might take our offering to the holy monastery; therefore we placed him in the holy place
10    in the holy place [with certain] boys, the steward serving him thus
11    I myself, the joint-holder, my father; when he comes to blessing in the holy place
12    [he] will withdraw year [by] year; when God thus happened, [the] appearing of the son [being] male; [he] might grant him blessing [and] we took him to the house
13    [and] we remained [for] years serving him until his body came to death therefore
14    [they] went repeatedly [for] years serving him until his body came [to] death…
15    [they] went with the steward there, which was Souros the [manager]
16    of the holy place there; for to God of the place might grant blessing
17    to the boy the male so that he might come [and] remain in the holy place
18    serving in his heart; God with you commanding him thus
19    to give his document to the holy place; when… [came to] rest concerning all the sons of the holy place, God with you commanding [us]
20    [any] ruler, [any] authority of God the Almighty to you
21    since this irrevocable [gift] is placed thus, unshakeable, so that they honor it
22    [and] not transgress against it, because we gave [as a] gift
23    the son the boy entirely into the holy place, I myself, the joint-holder
24    with my wife Tasia, [we] agree to this agreement; this charter [being] written [according]
25    [to] the Trinity, Father and Son and [the] Holy Spirit and in communion with any Christian, because we gave
26    him to the holy place
27    I Theodoros, [a] witness in Mount Sinai of [a] martyr
28    I Pachomios, [the] son of John, in my village, [who] write [as a] witness
29    I Chael, [the] son of [John] in my village
30    of Mount Sinai, [who] write [as a] martyr
