[TRANSLATION]
1     These are the (dues) paid by me, Taurinos son of Menas, from Hermopolis: 17 nomismata, 3 ½ 3 24 keratia, and 6 artabas of wheat,
2     thus:
3     for the proportional share of the 600 thousands, 7 nomismata, 18 ½ keratia;
4     for the gold-taxes of the former assessment, at 2 nomismata 16 keratia, 3 nomismata 15 4 keratia;
5     to the village of Tekerkethoth, for the gold-taxes of the former assessment, at 2 nomismata 15 keratia, 3 nomismata 22 ½ keratia, [and] 6 [ar]tabas;
6     to the same village, for the commutation into money and the embolē (grain-shipment) of the 3 artabas of the 15th (indiction), 1 nomisma 4 ½ 4 keratia …
7     and for the proportional share of the 500 thousands, 9 nomismata, [9] 3 24 [keratia];
8     for the six-keratia (tax) of the city, 1 nomisma 21 ½ 4 keratia;
9     for the six-keratia (tax) of the village of Tekerkethoth, 1 nomisma 23 ½ 8 keratia;
10    for revenues, 7 nomismata 12 keratia.
11    I, Taurinos son of Menas, from Hermopolis, have submitted the present register of the public (dues)
12    paid by me. I acknowledge, before God the Almighty and the safety of our masters the Amir-folk,
13    that if I should appear to have omitted anything in such a register, [I shall pay] in double quantity, with my being liable, together with the perjury, to a charge.
14    Cognizance (gnosis) of Taurinos son of Menas, from Hermopolis.
