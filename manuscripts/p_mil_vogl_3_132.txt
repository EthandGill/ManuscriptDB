[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ptollarion, son of Ptollarion who has served as gymnasiarch, a minor, through
2     his guardian Sabinus who has served as gymnasiarch,
3     from Ptolemaios son of Theogiton, son of Theogiton, from the quarter of Tameia, and from his son Harpokration, and from Sabinus son of Patron, son of Apion, and from Isidoros son of Areios, son of Ptolemai[os], the two from the quarter of Tharapeia, the four of them. We wish to lease from you for four years
4     from the coming sixth year of Antoninus and Verus the
5     lords Augusti, the part belonging to you of arouras,
6     in equal share, the half which you have around the village of Kerkesis, of a plot of seven arouras, and around the village Kerkeseph[is, of a plot of] fifteen arouras, of all, or however many [there ma]y be; for the first and third year, on the one hand arouras
7     three and a half for the sowing of whatever we choose, except wheat,
8     and the [remai]ning ones for the sowing of grass as fodder for oxen,
9     at a rent for the two-year period, each year, without seed, of the (arouras)
10    around Kerkesis, of one hundred forty silver drachmas,
11    and of those around Kerkesephis, of silver
12    one hundred fifty dra[chmas]; and the second
13    and fourth year, for the sowing of wheat, at a rent for the
14    two-year period, each year, together with which we shall receive of seed
15    of those around Kerkesis, three and a half artabas of wheat,
16    of all the wheat fifty-two and a half artabas, [and] of those around Kerkesephis, sev[en and a] half artabas of wheat, of all the wheat ninety-one artabas, by your own four-choinix measure,
17    and of a present each year two cheeses, forty sheep,
18    all without deduction and without risk, performing the
19    yearly works of the arouras, all that is fitting, out of our
20    own, at the proper seasons, and we shall pay the rents each year in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor, new, clean, unadulterated, the measuring being done by someone
21    of yours, and the (money-)rent in the month Pharmouthi; the
22    yearly transport-charges of the speeding-up being upon us,
23    and the [carriage of the rent]s upon you; and af[ter]
24    the ti[me we shall hand over] the arouras clean
25    from couch-gr[ass and all rubbish]. If it seems good to lease.
26    [Pt]olemaios, about 47 years old, scar on the left [shin]
27    Ha[rp]okration, about 22 years old, scar on the left knee
28    Sabinus, about 28 years old, scar on the third finger of the left hand
29    [I]si[d]oros, about 23 years old, scar on the left shin.
30    Year 6 of Antoninus and Verus the lords
31    Augusti, [Me]sore 28.
