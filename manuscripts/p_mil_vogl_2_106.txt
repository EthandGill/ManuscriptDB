[TRANSLATION]
1     A… son of Geminus,
2     from Kastor son of Sotas son of Sotas, registered in the quarter
3     of Iseion Dromou, [a Persian] of the epigone. I wish to lease from you
4     for six years [from] the coming nineteenth year of Hadrian
5     Caesar the lord the arouras belonging to you near the village of Kerkeusiris,
6     twelve and a half arouras of catoecic holding, or however many there may be, in two parcels
7     called those of Keskisis, of which the first parcel is eleven arouras
8     and the remaining one and a half arouras the second — the first [and]
9     third and fifth year of the lease for sowing
10    of wheat, the rent over the three-year period each year, together with which
11    you shall supply me each year as wheat-seed twelve and a half artabas,
12    the whole each year being one hundred seventy-eight artabas of wheat and, for the supervisor's and donkey-driver's dues, one artaba, all by the six-choinix measure of the god of Kerkeusiris; and the second and fourth year for sowing
13    of fodder-grass for the browsing of sheep and for folding, without
14    [rent]; and the sixth year, which is the last year of the
15    [lease], eight and a half arouras for sowing
16    of fodder-grass for the browsing of sheep and folding,
17    and the remaining four arouras for sowing of vegetable-seed, likewise without rent or rents, all being free of deduction and risk; and I performing the
18    yearly agricultural works of the arouras, all that
19    is fitting, at my own expense at the proper seasons, doing
20    no damage; and I will pay the rents each year
21    [in the month Pauni] at the threshing-floor, new, clean, unadulterated, the measuring being done by one of yours, with me
22    [the lessee being responsible for the] yearly freight-charges of hastening, and the conveyance of the rents being toward
23    [you the landowner; and after] the term I will hand over
24    [the arouras clean of] couch-grass and all rubbish
25    [except reed and rush]; and you shall guarantee to me the lease and clean of all public dues and not re-leased
26    [and] not encumbered … … and
27    … [if] it seems good to lease.
