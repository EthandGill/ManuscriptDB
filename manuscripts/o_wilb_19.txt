1     Pachon 19, of the 10th year, in the name of
2     Psenchonius, likewise [son] of Horus, for the dike-tax of the 10th year

4     and in the name of Psenchonius [the] son, likewise
5     drachmas three and a half-obol, copper 2, amounting to drachmas 3 and a half-obol, copper 2.
6     Ap[...], I have signed.
