[TRANSLATION]
1     To Calpurnius Isidorus also called
2     Harpocration, strategos of the Memphite nome,
3     from Theon son of Hermaion,
4     having served as gymnasiarch of the
5     city of the Memphites. Since
6     from the month Mecheir
7     of the present year, having suffered outrage
8     at the hands of a certain Apion,
9     a camel-driver of mine, who had under his charge all the equipment of
10    my beasts of burden,
11    and who had moreover received an advance,
12    he, using no pretext at all, stole some of the
13    equipment and ran off.
14    Learning of his madness, I made light of my own losses and did not proceed against him, in order that
15    I might not suffer worse things at his hands. But today, finding him here working
16    with other camels of the …
17    …nophios, having served as gymnasiarch
18    of the same city, I laid a claim against this Apion
19    both concerning my own property and concerning
20    his lawless departure, on the ground that
21    I found him beside my camel-yard, which is at the … dromos of the oracular precinct of Aphrodite
22    the greatest goddess; thinking that he could desist, he instead used the same
23    madness and makes many threats against me.
24    Being on my guard, therefore,
25    lest the substance of his threat come into
26    effect, and securing myself, I present
27    this petition. I request that Apion be brought in, so that you may examine his manner of life carefully, and that he provide a surety, so that I may suffer no harm from his
28    threat.
29    Year 21 of the Emperor Caesar
30    Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus
31    Parthicus Maximus Britannicus
32    Maximus Pius Augustus, Pharmouthi
33    28. I, Theon son of Hermaion, having served as gymnasiarch, have presented it as aforesaid.
