[TRANSLATION]
1     Demetrius son-of Diogenes to-the grain-accountant of-the Monimos district
2     Greetings. Distribute which you have
3     of-me in a theme of-wheat produce of-the 17th year
4     of Hadrian Caesar [of] the Lord
5     to Apollonius son-of Demetrius, property
6     of-Senecianus, one-and-one-half artaba, making artaba 1½.
7     Year 18 of Hadrian Caesar of-the Lord
8     Phaophi 10. To Apollonius son-of Eutychides
9     one-and-one-half [and] one-quarter artaba, choinikes nine
10    making in-total artaba 1¾, choinikes 9. Same time.
