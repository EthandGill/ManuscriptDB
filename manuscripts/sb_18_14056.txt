[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of [our] masters Constantine Augustus, [year] 7, and
2     of Constantine, the most manifest Caesar, [year] 1.
3     To Aurelius Ammoni[us], also called Canopus, and to Neilammon, also called Hiera[x], both
4     having served as councillors of Hermes [city] of the most splendid, joint-defenders of the city
5     from [Aureli]us Did[ym]us Herminus, councillor of the same city; I possess
6     a slave named Eu[tychi]us, who has been frequently enslaved by Hermes
7     Isido[rus from] the [same] city, recorded in the quarter of the Guard
8     of the East… a life of non-interference I had chosen, taking back the slave
9     nor having wished to bring [from] the enslavement a charge against him. But since
10    so far despising [my] non-interference and now he harms him
11    at night while I was sleeping, on the day before yesterday, which was the 22nd of the present month
12    Hathyr, he opened the door of my house in a robber-like manner and very many
13    movable items of mine to be plundered, as I witnessed catching the slave in the act right there with the aforementioned Hermes, and in order that the enslavement and the plundering should not go unavenged, I submit these
14    documents requesting through your tr[ust] for knowledge to my lord
15    most distinguished governor Valerius Victorinianus to be brought forward
16    so that I might be able and [for] the equal slave of the enslavement according to the
17    laws to [recover] and the plundered items and to receive the deserved vengeance
18    to obtain. Prosper.
19    of the aforementioned consulship, Hathyr 23.
20    Aurelius Didymus have submitted [this].
