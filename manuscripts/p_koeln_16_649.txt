1     Ptolemy of Pyrrhus, to the overseers in the division of Polemon, greetings. Take …

3     recorded [handwriting and] send to us carefully. Farewell. Year 29, Phaophi 2

5     and strategus, the topographers and village-scribes in the division of Polemon. We swear the King [Ptolemy god Benefactor the one descended from Ptolemy] and Cleopatra of the gods
6     [the Manifest Ones and Queen Cleopatra the sister] goddess Benefactress and [god]
7     Philometor and god [Eupator] and gods Manifest and gods Philopatores and gods [Benefactors]
8     and gods [Brothers] and [gods Saviors] and Sarapis and Isis and the others
9     gods all and [all if not] concerning all things being reported … and the
10    in the villages [being held back] and not being sent out immediately we shall report or the next day. If we swear truly, may it be well with us; if we swear falsely, may the opposite befall us.
