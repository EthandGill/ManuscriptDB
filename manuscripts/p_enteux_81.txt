[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolemy, greeting] … [On the te]nth of the month Tybi … in the 1st year, from
2     … of his own wife Hed[ist]e and of her
3     … of my husband Philon, the house which was …
4     … both [m]e and my sister …
5     … Alkimos ordered me to withdraw, alleging
6     … having entered into the house, to throw out the inhabitants, but while I … [un]less he should produce some document concerning these things, and
7     … he set upon … [Al]kimos carried off … of our [equip]ment into
8     the … of my sister … and while she cried out to people, and when more
9     had gathered together, among whom was also Aga[thokles the epist]ates, Hediste and Konnara struck …
10    … while I cried out … and when certain people had come in, Alkimos, taking up
11    in his right hand a stick, struck … with a [stic]k deliberately on the right forearm of (her) hand and broke it … while she called the bystanders to witness, he struck … [at] whatever part of my body he chanced upon, beginning unjust
12    handling against m[e] … [he was thro]wing (me) out of the aforesaid house; and when [I] did not withdraw … when my son Aristom[ach]os had come up, and after
13    these things my hus[band Philon also, leav]ing us they departed. Since therefore
14    by what has been done a[gainst me] … to be settled, I have sent my own
15    husband to give the pe[tition] … these things to the strategos, I beg you, O king,
16    entreating you, to or[der Diophanes the strategos] to write to Agathokles the epistates both to make clear
17    to him the consequence … to send (him) so that he be put under surety, and, if within the
18    days according to the law … [he flee?], but if I recover, that I receive from him
19    justice through the cou[rt] … I shall not fail, but through you, O king, may obtain your benevolence. Farewell.
20    … [Year 1], Gorpiaios 30, Tybi 13.
