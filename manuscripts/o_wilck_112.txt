[TRANSLATION]
1   Sanmous, collector. Paid:
2   Petorzmethis son of Petorzmethis,
3   mother Thipoueris, for the assessment of the river-
4   guard, 3rd year of Hadrian the lord:
5   two silver drachmas, and for the 2nd year, three obols.
6   I, Sarapion the scribe, wrote (this), Mecheir 29.
