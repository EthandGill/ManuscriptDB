[TRANSLATION]
1     Kollanthos, royal scribe of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite (nome), to the village-scribe of Pt[olem]ai[s Hormou], greeting. Of the injunction sent to me in the name of Rufus son of Pnepheros, who wishes to buy from the katoikic dry
2     unproductive (land) by demarcation, 16 arouras, I send you a copy, so that, having gone to the place and having accomplished the
3     consequent (procedure), you may declare (it) to me. I have signed.
4     Year 25 of Marcus Aur[e]lius Commodus [Anto]ninus Augustus Pius, Mesore 16.
5     Apollonius, [strategos] of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite (nome), to Kollanthos, royal scribe of the same division,
6     his most dear (friend), greeting. Of the petitions given to me
7     from Rufus son of P[nef]eros son of Pachnoubis, who wishes to buy
8     from the katoikic dry unproductive (land) in a hollow, by demarcation, for
9     a brickworks, 16 arouras, I send you the copy, most dear (friend), so that you may know [and] fill up your own parts. I pray [for your] health, most dear (friend). [Year 25]
10    of Marcus A[urelius Comm]odus
11    Antonin[us Ca]es[a]r the lord, Epeiph 30. To Apollonius, strategos
12    of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite (nome), from Rufus son of Pnepheros son of [P]achnoubis
13    from the village of P[tolemai]s Hormo[u]. I wish to buy from the katoikic
14    dry unproductive (land) [in] a hollow, by demarcation, for a brickworks, 16 arouras,
15    neighbours: on the south a dyke and [so]wn plots, on the north the perimeter of the village, on the east likewise the perimeter of the village,
16    on the west grain-plots, at a price of 28 drachmas [an]d the surcharge, on condition that, having been given the demarcation, I shall pay
17    the price with the [attendant charges] and surcharge to the public bank. And there shall remain to me and to
18    those who shall receive (it) from me the possession of this and its ownership, inalienable
19    for ever, and it shall be guaranteed to me clear of all public dues
20    and of every kind up to the time of the demarcation.
