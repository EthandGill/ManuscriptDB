[TRANSLATION]
1     To Julius Proculus, prefect, praepositius,
2     a petition from Aurelius Abidsauta,
3     of Abidierdu, councilman of Neopolis,
4     dwelling in Bethphouraia village.
5     Philotas Nisaraiabou, sir, at every turn compelled me; concerning which documents
6     to our most illustrious consul
7     I transmitted concerning what, having dared,
8     perpetrated along with Sibbaraiu, his servant.
9     His. I request and beseech you for [your]
10    [humanity], since the charges
11    with which I am charged, requiring [examination by] a greater judge, [to] refer them
12    with me before the majesty of [the one having held] the consulate Pomponius Laetianus;
13    for I am ready to accuse him concerning what he perpetrated, and forever I shall give thanks to your fortune.
14    Prosper, sir, for many years.
