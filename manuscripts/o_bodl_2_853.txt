1     Psansnos and Kasianos, tax collectors of silver [to Phateretes]
2     Krouries Maieurios. We received for distributions of watchtowers and [doubled amounts]
3     And harbor dues and other [services] of the 20th year, small change, drachmas six, obols [six, half-obol, copper coins two].
4     Total: small change, drachmas six, obols six, half-obol, copper coins two. Of the 20th year of Hadrian Caesar[s]
5     The master, Pauni 19.
