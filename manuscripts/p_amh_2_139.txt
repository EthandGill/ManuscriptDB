[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Diokles, praepositus of the 12th pagus of the Hermopolite nome,
2     from Aurelius Pesis and Pasops, both komarchs of the village of Ibion Panektyreos
3     of the pagus under you. We give and report the following inscribed men as komarchs and eirenarchs and sitologoi and collectors of the annona, being men of means, at our own risk and (that) of all
4     those from our village.
5     They are:
6     komarchs:
7     Pinoution son of Harpalos,
8     T…eis son of Sotes.
9     eirenarch:
10    …s son of Phibion.
11    sitologoi:
12    Pinoution son of Demetrios,
13    Serenos son of Sarapion,
14    Pesis son of Kopres,
15    Akoris son of Dios,
16    Iosephios son of Kollouthos,
17    Mousaios son of Kastor,
18    Herminos son of Perousis,
19    Isidoros son of Mousiaos,
20    Pelys son of Herakleios,
21    Melas son of Diogenes.
22    collectors of the annona:
23    Mousaios son of Pekysis,
24    Asklepiades son of Mousaios,
25    Horion son of Paesis,
26    Pagenes son of Dios,
27    Ibois son of Kalokairos,
28    Melas son of Pekysis.
29    Whom indeed we guarantee and will produce, by mutual surety, blamelessly, the liturgy entrusted to them
30    to be found wanting in nothing; and if any of them should default and we should not produce (him),
31    we ourselves will bear the account on his behalf, or else may we be liable to the divine oath and to the
32    risk concerning this, and being asked the formal question we agreed. After the consulship of Ulpius Limenius and Akontius
33    Catullinus, the most illustrious, Phamenoth 4. We the aforesaid Pesis and Pasotes
34    report and guarantee as is stated above. I, Aurelius Sarapion, an acquaintance, have written on their behalf,
35    they not knowing letters.
