1     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Tiberius Mauricius
2     the eternal Augustus and Imperator and greatest
3     benefactor, in the third year, Pauni 5 of the third indiction, in Syene.
4     Aurelia Tapia daughter of Tsios, her mother being Mariam, transacting business without
5     her lord and husband, wife then of Iakobos,
6     originating from Syene, who also furnishes the subscriber below
7     who signs on her behalf and the witnesses below
8     witnessing to this valid sale, to the Aurelii
9     Patermouthios son of Menas, sailor and my son-in-law, and
10    Kako his wife, my own daughter from her father the aforesaid Iakobos, originating from the city of the Syenites,
11    greeting. I the aforesaid Tapia acknowledge through this
12    my written deed of sale, willingly and
13    persuaded, free of all guile and fear and force
14    and necessity and constraint and flattery and contrivance
15    and ill-will and malice and every defect
16    and any base intention and every lawful
17    circumvention, but with willing mind and self-chosen will
18    and upright understanding and pure purpose and unturning reasoning
19    and unchangeable intent and sincere conscience, while also
20    swearing the dread and venerable oath of
21    God the almighty and of the victory and permanence of
22    our most pious masters the Flavii Tiberius Mauricius
23    and Aelia Constantina the eternal Augusti
24    and Imperators and greatest benefactors, that I have sold
25    to you today, the aforesaid Patermouthios and
26    Kako his wife, by law of sale and eternal possession
27    and every power and every fullest right of
28    ownership, and have conveyed from now for the whole
29    ever after continuous time, the half-share belonging to me
30    of the banquet-room in the house of my mother, of which also the other half-share
31    belongs to Menas and Tselet my siblings, inclining to the north
32    toward the buttress on the second story, and my share of the terrace on the fourth
33    story above the chamber of Taliphantis, together with my share
34    of all the appurtenances of the house, from which I have sold you the half-
35    [share] of the banquet-room, being in Syene near the southern part of the fortress
36    on the lane of the oratory of the holy and victorious Victor, having come
37    to me thus: a quarter-share from the inheritance of my mother,
38    and another quarter from a purchase from George my brother;
39    moreover I have also sold you my share from the house of
40    my father Tsios according to the division, the same house lying
41    in the same lane; moreover also my share from the small house
42    in the same lane, having come to me thus: a share from the inheritance
43    of my mother, and a share from paternal inheritance, of whatever
44    condition it is, from foundations to air. Simply, nothing at all
45    has been left to me unsold of the three dwellings
46    of my mother and of my father, according to the share that has fallen to me, together with the share of
47    all the appurtenances, they being common and undivided
48    between me and my other siblings. I further acknowledge that I have also
49    sold the half-share of a house falling to me, having come
50    to me from lawful purchase from John son of Patechnoumios, of which also
51    the other half-share belongs to my late husband from a purchase
52    from the same John, the same house lying in
53    Syene near the southern part of the fortress and on the lane
54    of the camp. The neighbours of the same house are: south, the house
55    of the heirs of Apa Dios … son of Abr[aamio]s,
56    or whoever may be the neighbours all round on every side, just as also by word of mouth
57    I the seller dictated. And nothing at all has been left to me
58    unsold of the aforesaid shares
59    of the dwellings, by the force of the sales and of the
60    deeds also handed over for your greater security,
61    for the price mutually agreed and pleasing
62    of ten gold solidi by the standard of the Syenites,
63    making 10 gold solidi by the standard of Syene, which full price, on the spot,
64    I have received from you the buyers, part of which I have spent
65    on my pressing need and sustenance in the city of the Antinoites,
66    and part of which I have given for the loss falling upon me in the
67    same city by reason of John my brother, on the present
68    day from hand to hand to me out of your house, in number and weight
69    in full. And I will guarantee to you the present sale with every guarantee
70    against everyone, throughout, who shall come or also lay claim;
71    and on condition that you the buyers from now own and
72    be master and administer and manage and rebuild
73    and sell and resell and bestow and to children and
74    descendants assign, to acquire, to use in every way pleasing
75    to you, unhindered and unimpeded, and that it not be permitted
76    to me nor to my heirs nor successors nor holders
77    to proceed against you nor against those after you who shall inherit
78    nor successors nor holders, nor to sue nor accuse
79    nor encroach nor transgress nor disturb this
80    my sale, now and at no time or season
81    in any way on any pretext whatsoever, because, as I said before,
82    I have received from you the full enclosed price
83    in ten solidi. And the one coming against you or also laying claim on account of the sale or a share of it I will
84    entirely remove and clear at my own expenses
85    and costs. And for your security I have
86    issued this sale, valid and firm
87    and unbreakable and unshaken and lawful, everywhere
88    produced, with the subscription of the one signing on my behalf
89    and of the witnesses below witnessing, and being formally questioned
90    face to face I acknowledged. Aurelia Tapia daughter of Tsios,
91    her mother being Mariam, transacting business without her lord and husband, originating from Syene, the aforesaid, having sold the half-share belonging to me
92    of the banquet-room on the second story and my share from the terrace on the fourth story
93    in the house of my mother, moreover also the half-share of a house
94    having come to me from lawful purchase from John son of Patechnoumios,
95    together with their share from all the appurtenances, both the fore-doors and gateways
96    and buttresses and other appurtenances belonging, and having received from you
97    the full and aforesaid price in ten gold solidi by the standard of Syene,
98    and I guarantee to you the sale with every guarantee, and everything agrees [with me] as aforesaid. (2nd hand) Flavius

103   Flavius Joseph son of Victor, physician and soldier of the legion of Syene, I bear witness.
104   Flavius Mousaios son of Abraamios, soldier of the unit of Syene, I bear witness.
