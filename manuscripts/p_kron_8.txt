1     In the year thirteen of the [Autocrator] [Caesar] Nerva Trajan the August
2     Germanicus Dacicus, [Choiaki] in Tebtunis of Polemon's [district] of the Arsinote [nome ... they agree]: Kronion of Cheos
3     as forty [nine] years, scar on small finger of hand
4     left, and the [wife Thenapychis] of Patyneos, as
5     of forty years, scar [on the kne]e right, and the children of both,
6     [Kronion as] of twenty [five] years, [scar] on right forearm, and Taorsenouphis, being also the wife of the [brother Kronion], as of twenty-two years
7     scar on right shin, the four sureties of one another for [recovery],
8     [the] women [with] their lords [each] of the aforementioned her …
9     [of husband], to Heraklides of Didymos the [younger] of Herodes
10    [As nineteen years old, scar on forehead, having] from Heraklides
11    [Immediately by hand use of silver] of principal drachmas
12    [Two hundred thirty-six with interest], of which also compulsory
13    [the repayment they will make] …
