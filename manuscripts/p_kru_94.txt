[TRANSLATION]
1     … [of Komes] s[o]n of Chael, administrator of the fortress of Memnon[i]on.
2     I, Chael the son of Mena,
3     the man of the fortress of Djeme, the nome
4     of the city of Ermont, greetings. Those who know how to write write of their own,
5     in their own writings, while those who do not know how to write urge on
6     some men to understand writing for them,
7     according to their request
8     and their eagerness.
9     The royal law commands
10    in this manner, that each one
11    do what pleases him in that which is his own.
12    I have followed, then, the sequence
13    of the laws, to donate
14    my beloved son Stephanos
15    into the venerable place
16    of the holy Apa Phoibammon of the mountain
17    of the fortress of Djeme,
18    through you, Kyriakos
19    the superior and the hegoumenos
20    and the monk and the presbyter
21    of the holy Apa Phoibammon,
22    so that Stephanos my son may become
23    a servant to the venerable place
24    of the holy Apa Phoibammon,
25    according to the manner in which you shall command
26    it for him, so that you may not find him in any disorder
27    nor disturbance, and he shall become
28    decent in everything.
29    Whoever shall annul this document
30    and stir against it shall become
31    alien to the Father
32    and the Son and the Holy Spirit, and he shall receive the curse
33    of his wife, and he shall receive the curses which are in the law
34    of Moses. To set apart, then,
35    the holy place, I have established
36    this deed of donation, and
37    I agree to it.
38    I, Chael the son of Mena, agree.
39    I, Zikeel the son of Ammone, with Markos
40    the son of Makare, bear witness.
41    I, Iohannes the son of Hilias — the persons
42    asked me, I wrote for them, since they do not know how to write,
43    and I bear witness.
44    I, Shenetom of Biktor,
45    bear witness. I, Sanhem
46    the son of Iohannes — they urged me, I wrote for them, [since]
47    they do not know how to write.
48    I, Petros the son of the late …,
49    I, Daniel the so[n of the late]
50    Theodotos, the least [presbyter] of the holy Maria of Tryga[ta] of Djeme, [I] …
51    according to the request of Chae[l].
