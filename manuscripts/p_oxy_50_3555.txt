[TRANSLATION]
1     To Asklepe(s) strategos
2     from Thermouthion daughter of Ploutarchos
3     of those from the city of Oxyrhynchos.
4     A little house-born slave-girl of mine,
5     whose name is Peina, I loved and cared for like a little daughter
6     in the hope that, when she came of age, I should have her as a support of my old age,
7     being a helpless woman
8     and alone. And this (girl), as she was passing through
9     the city on the 19th of the past month for instruction
10    in singing and other things, while Eucharion
11    a certain freedwoman of Longinus was conducting her,
12    she (the freedwoman), at the hour of her
13    arrival from my house,
14    brought Peina in
15    having her right hand bound,
16    and when I inquired
17    of her the cause she reported to me that by a certain slave-boy
18    of Polydeukes who was accompanying
19    a donkey she had been knocked down,
20    so that as a result her whole
21    hand was crushed
22    and most parts were mangled, while the others were gaping (with wounds).
23    And then, not having the one presiding over the strategia, [I did not]
24    file a petition concerning
25    this, thinking the sore to be
26    passing, but it being incurable
27    and (I) not bearing the pain concerning
28    the slave-girl, since she
29    is in danger of her
30    life, and I am gripped by despondency of
31    life — which from
32    the plain facts you too, when you look into (it),
33    will indignantly (regard). Necessarily
34    therefore having taken refuge with you
35    the protector, I request to be helped and to obtain the (relief) from
36    [y]ou …
