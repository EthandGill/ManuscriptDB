[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greetings. Epoeris daughter of Panetus, isionomos, dwelling in the village of Athena,
2     of the division of Themistos. There belongs to me an Isis-temple in the aforementioned village, which it has happened
3     to have deteriorated and on account of this to not be able to dwell in it, fearing that it might collapse.
4     I petition you therefore, O King, if it seems good to you, to order Diophanes the general to write to those to whom it is fitting,
5     if indeed that which I write be true, let the demolition permit be given to me, on condition that, having rebuilt, I will restore it to the same, and the sacrifices [be completed] both for you and for your sister and for your children.
6     For [this having occurred], I will be one who has obtained the kindness from you.
7     Farewell.
8     To Straton. Having taken the … overseer and the village scribe, examine and, if that which she writes be true, allow
9     it to be demolished, and [let it be a care to] you that it be rebuilt again not worse than the pre-existing
10    Isis-temple, but … according to the ordinance.
11    Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13.
12    [Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13.]
13    Epoeris daughter of Panetus
14    concerning the demolition of the Isis-temple.
