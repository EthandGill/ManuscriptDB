1     Account of the sheep of Eutychides son of Sarapion,
2     of Hermopolis, through Panaithios son of Harmiysis, shepherd,
3     in place of whom: receipts of sheep of the same Eutychides, year 2
4     of Hadrian Caesar the lord, in
5     Mecheir of the same [year]. Of mixed sheep together with females, in number 552,
6     and lambs of the offspring of the same year 2,
7     in number of lambs 205;
8     goats likewise 10;
9     kid 1.
10    Of which, by sale in the same year 2,
11    through the same Eutychides, so as to belong
12    to Nilas son of Tauriskion: sheep 84;
13    goats with them, in the same, goats 1;
14    and there perished in Mecheir:
15    (Mecheir) 23, in the flock of Pelatis; Thatres saw it,
16    the doorkeeper: female 1;
17    Phamenoth 1, in Pisiris; saw it
18    Eutychides: female 1;
19    (Phamenoth) 17, in the flock of Pisos, lambs; saw it
20    Eutychides, held back 10 for the house of Eutychides, lambs 1;
21    Pachon 19, adjoining Thyneus,
