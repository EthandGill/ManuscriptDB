[TRANSLATION]
1     Phaeris and … [to] Petechespocratis, (son) of Tithoeus
2     Psen … . We received for the poll-tax of Ophieus, year 15,
3     dirty drachmas ten [and] 4 obols, and for the bath-tax of South Agoras, year 15, one drachma [and] 2 obols,
4     totaling twelve drachmas. Year 15 of Trajan the lord, Pachon 31.
5     … has signed.
