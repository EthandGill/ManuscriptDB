[TRANSLATION]
1     Biennis to Prototogenes, greetings. I have sent you the account of [what has entered] in the places of Apollonides and Ptolemy in the month Thoth until the 20th and of [what has been distributed] to the elder cavalry in the
2     places of Apollonides and of salt of the same time;
3     and of Ptolemy's: the per-unit measure of wheat pentartal to the elders
4     cavalry upon the account 298; and the per-unit measure I will report to you together with
5     the day and Apollonides and Dorotheos at night.
6     I have also sent you the difference of the
7     elders and salt-makers.
8     Farewell.
9     To Prototogenes
10    must be opened.
