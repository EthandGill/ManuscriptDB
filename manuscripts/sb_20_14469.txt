[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius [Ori]genes, strategos of Hermopolis
2     from Aurelius Herm[o]s, also [called] Pinutus e[...]ou, gymnasiarch, [councilor]
3     of Herm[os c]ity, the [great] and most illustrious. being about to depart
4     toward the footsteps of our lords and all-conquering Emperors
5     Diocletian and Maximian, Augustuses, and Constantine and Maximian of the
6     most illustrious Caesars, having been appointed to this from the strongest council
7     and already having begun the journey, but certain outstanding [matters] being incumbent of my decaprotia toparchia Pasko, on account of this for the sake of these also being able to be settled,
8     [I] present to you [these] documents, requesting [that] you write to Aurelius Kopres, clerk of Ibion Chysis of the [same] toparchia … of [the] care and collection …
