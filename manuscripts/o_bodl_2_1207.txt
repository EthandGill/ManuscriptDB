[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis, produce of the 20th year
2     of Trajan Aristos the lord, Pauni 15.
3     Under the name of Petechonsius son of Horus, wheat artabe one, (one) half, (one) third.
4     Amounts to wheat artabe 1, 1/2, 1/3. An (I have) signed. And under the name of Senfabotes
5     of Pamonthos, wheat artabe, (one) half, (one) third, (one) twelfth.
6     Amounts to wheat artabe 1/2, 1/3, 1/12. An (I have) signed.
