1   Apol… to Apollinarius, [his]
2   brother, gr[eetings].
3   Having learned that you are in Bakchias,
4   I greet you, brother, and now
5   I urge (you) to send word to
6   us about your health. For I
7   have already used up a sheet of papyrus
8   writing to you, and barely one let-
9   ter of yours did I receive, in which
10   you indicated that I should receive the cloaks and
11   the young pig. The
12   pig I did not re-

15   (may you be) rejoicing. Receive four good lettuces

18   mullet, half-(dried). Theon the brother
19   greets you and urges you
20   (1st hand) Farewell. Pharmouthi 4.
21   (2nd hand) to come to us from Bakchias.
22   For watch those coming from there ar-
23   riving by the second hour.
24   (1st hand) To Apollinarius ☓, (his) friend.
