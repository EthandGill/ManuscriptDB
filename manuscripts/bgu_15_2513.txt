[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year twenty-four] [of Mark] Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Augustus …
2     [Augustus, Payni] twelve. They-recorded through Satabus and fellow-tax-collectors …
3     [of-silver-tax village] of Soknopaios Island, Tesenouphis son-of-Tesenouphis …
4     [mother of Tanaphreus] and Herieus [the] brother of the same mother, for dike-tax
5     of the [past] twenty-three year, gross drachmas fifteen obols two-and-a-half, they-amount-to drachmas fifteen obols two-and-a-half.
6     … for Pakysis of Tesenouphe, mother of Tesenouphe, for dike-tax
7     [of the past twenty-three year, gross] drachmas seven four-obol [coppers two, they-become] drachmas seven four-obol coppers two.
