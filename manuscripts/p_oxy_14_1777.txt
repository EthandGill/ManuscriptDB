[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Tyran[n]os of Chous, greetings.
2     Just as you said the laborers owe [money], trust them,
3     to them, as we know, brother, that they owe for
4     the reckoning of pounds myriads 234.
5     Make haste, brother,
6     [have them] given to my brother Eulogios, just as you
7     yourself said to trust them, and I trusted [them]. Do not neglect
8     therefore, make [it] be given
9     the money to Eulogios
10    my brother. Since
11    moreover I have [some] days here, do not [neglect]. I ... you and [your] father.
12    I pray [that] you [be] well, my lord brother.
