1     ... [and necess]ity and all description according to this agr[eement, by willing]
2     choice voluntary and unrepentant and guileless deliberation firm with right mind, and
3     and swearing an oath god almighty and the piety and victory [of] our [lord]
4     Fl[avius Ju]stinian the eternal Augustus Emperor [to have sold today to you]
5     the most wonderful Victor the purchaser [have transferred and made over and handed over] complete with every masterly
6     lawful right and good faith [with all authority and eternal] possession according to every kind of mastery
7     according to this simple [wri]tten security from now upon forever and what follows always into
8     all time the things belonging to me and come to me from lawful exchange ...
9     from the most renowned Victor the purchaser arouras ten two-third seed-bearing [land]
10    waterless inferior land and not subject to taxes the whole from aroura one half [of] seed-bearing land
11    waterless according to partnership of Hales and Thekla, my full-sister, into the remaining
12    arouras twenty one third for completing of the same half-aroura, lying under the
13    watchful-keeping of those from the village Enseu of the same Hermopolite nome, neighbors of
14    the half-aroura to the south of the one aroura north houses of the men Leontius to the east
15    houses of Hales to the west the public road or whatever are neighbors in all directions everywhere, price of
16    agreed-upon between each other and consented to and
17    agreed-upon between me [and] you
18    according to gold imperial coin approved one third beside keratia eight.
