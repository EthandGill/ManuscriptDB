1   Kametis son of Kametis, (grand)son of Petearpres, paid,
2   for poll-tax of the South-and-West (quarter), year 3, 8 dr. Year 3 of Domitian

4   Pachon 5, 4 dr.; likewise (on the) 9th, 4 dr.;
5   likewise Pauni 22, 4 (dr.); likewise Epeiph 2,
6   4 dr.; likewise Mesore 8, 2 (dr.) 2½ ob., (= ) 2 (dr.) 1½ ob. 1 chalkous,
7   likewise for the guards' pay of year 3, 1 dr.
