[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos, the one in charge of the court.
2   From Taphesies daughter of Boukolos, with her guardian ... Pro-
3   tarchos son of Dorion, of the Zeneios deme, and from Protarche daughter of ...,
4   a Persian woman, with her guardian, her husband Sen...(?)
5   son of Diomedes. Protarche agrees that she has from Taphesies
6   a loan, by hand from the house, of one hundred Ptolemaic silver drachmas; and in re-
7   turn for these and their interest, and for maintenance and clothing, that of necessity
8   Protarche, for a period of three years from Mecheir of the present twen-
9   ty-second year of Caesar, shall remain in service to Taphesies in the
10   beer-shop which Taphesies herself owns, performing [what is] enjo[ined]
11   upon her therein by Taphesies, without delay, being neither absent by night
12   nor absent by day from Taphesies without her consent;
13   and whatever she receives or is entrusted with she shall keep safe and return
14   to Taphesies, or pay the value of each, except for evident lo[ss],
15   of which, once made clear, let her be released; and she shall not abandon the (service)
16   within the period; and if she transgresses anything, she shall pay the
17   one hundred silver drachmas with half as much again, and interest at two drachmas (per mina), and the
18   damage and expense, and another hundred silver drachmas, the right of [exe]cution
19   belonging to Taphesies, against Protarche herself and against [her] property,
20   as if by legal judgment; and she shall bring no pleas, or they shall be void. And
21   [Ta]phesies, while Protarche performs each duty accordingly,
22   (is) to feed and clothe her for the period, and not to turn her away wi[thi]n
23   [this (period)]; and when it has elapsed, having received nothing further, she shall release [her]
24   from the repayment ... of the hundred silver drachmas, and shall give [her] in addition
25   from her own (funds) another forty silver drachmas, and shall make a re-
26   lease of this agreement, or be liable to the same penalty.
27   We request (registration).
28   ...
29   (2nd hand) I, Taphesies, agree as writ-
30   ten. I, Protarchos, am registered as her guard-
31   ian and have writ[ten on] her behalf, since
32   she does not know letters.
33   (3rd hand) I, Protarche, agree according to what is written above. ...
34   ...ros, am registered as guardian and have writt[en on her behalf,]
35   since she does not know letters.
36   Year 22 of Caesar, [Hathy]r 17.
