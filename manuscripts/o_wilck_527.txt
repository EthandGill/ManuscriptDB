[TRANSLATION]
1   Postumus also called Horos, (to) Panas(?),
2   mother Thatres. He received, for the trade-tax (cheironaxion)
3   of the bath-keepers, of the fifth year,
4   in base (coin), seven drachmas, three obols, two chalk[oi]
5   — total, base, 7 dr. 3 ob. 2 chalkoi. Year 6 of Hadrian Caesar
6   the lord, Thoth 28.
