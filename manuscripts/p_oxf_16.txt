1     of Taur[inos the assistant, the part]ner [and] his part[n]ers,
2     for the remaining [a]rourai, and in another place the remaining knots
3     forty-two, from four and a half arourai which are
4     to the north of the canal, in partnership with the same Taurinos
5     the assistant and his partners, for the remaining arourai,
6     for sowing and laying-down of crops, whichever I may choose, at a rent
7     for the one aroura and fifty knots, per year,
8     of wheat-barley at half-and-half, twelve and a quarter artabas.
9     But for the unflooded land, which may it not be, I shall furnish the third of the aforesaid rent.
10    Which rent, new, clean, sifted, by the Athenian measure,
11    I shall render and measure out in the month Epeiph each year without delay, delivering the rent itself to the same workshop with my own
12    animals and at my own expense. And he shall furnish each year also of chaff-fuel
13    one basket, and of milk one pot, and of ears of grain ten bundles,
14    and at the feast of Thynis one donkey for the going-away, apart from freight-charges. The lease
15    is valid and secure, and having been formally questioned I acknowledged it. Aurelius Horion son of Noubas,
16    the aforesaid, have leased it as set forth. [Aurelius] Phoibammon son of Sarapion from Hermoupolis, I witness the lease, having heard it fro[m the contractor.] Hermias son of Paisas,
