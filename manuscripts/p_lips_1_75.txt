[TRANSLATION]
1     account of the granary of the metropolis of the produce of year 5 of Antoninus
2     and Verus of the Lords Augustuses
3     Pauni 16, on behalf of Charax, by name Amenrosios
4     Psenmonthos, wheat one-third, becomes wheat 1/3. …
5     another: Epeiph 9, likewise by name of the same, wheat one-eighth
6     become wheat 1/8, and by name Psenmonthos
7     Amenrosios, wheat, half one-third
8     becomes wheat 1/6. Amonios has been recorded.
