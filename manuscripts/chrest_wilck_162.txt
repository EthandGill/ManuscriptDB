[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 19, Choiak 5, there has been paid into the bank at Hermonthis
2     over which Teos presides, to the king, into the private account, in accordance with the payment-order from Protarchos
3     who is in charge of the revenues throughout the Thebaid, the order written in year 15,
4     Phaophi 29, beneath which Harendotes the royal scribe of the Thebaid subscribes —
5     Proitos son of Sosikrates, the price of dry land, which Harendotes the royal
6     scribe declares to have been taken over into the treasury, but which was formerly belonging to Myron son of
7     Moschos in Pathyris, 35 arouras at 4½ each, of those put up for sale
8     and proclaimed in Diospolis the Great in year 15, Phaophi,
9     from the 1st to the 6th, and confirmed on the 7th, there being present at both the
10    proclamation and the confirmation Ptolemaios the successor who is in charge of the
11    strategia, Megisthenes the garrison-commander, Harendotes the royal scribe
12    of the Thebaid, Lysimachos the sitologos and banker of the Thebaid,
13    Ptolemaios the oikonomos, Horos and Psenamounis the topogrammateis,
14    Imouthes the komogrammateus of Diospolis and many others,
15    through the herald Archelaos of the army-heralds, the price amounting to
16    in copper at the silver standard 2,000 drachmas, on condition that he shall be owner of the assigned land
17    just as the former owners also possessed it, paying regularly into the treasury
18    the imposed rents and paying to the temples …
19    the dues given until the 16th year, but of the price he shall pay in the 20th year
20    and the 21st year 1,333 drachmas 2 obols, the remainder, and there has now been paid
21    in copper at the silver standard six hundred sixty-six drachmas four obols
22    and the one-twentieth of the transfer-tax and the other appropriate dues. …
