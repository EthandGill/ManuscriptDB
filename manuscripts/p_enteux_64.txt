[TRANSLATION]
1     [To king Ptolemy, greeting], Ptolema. I am wronged by Neilos. For when Theodotos, the …
2     … took on lease the produce of the date-grove belonging to him [near] Samareia for …
3     … the price of the produce to Theodotos, concerning which he also forwarded a letter,
4     … and Theodotos having died before he carried off … the produce, while I …
5     … of the things left behind by Theodotos and keeping … the produce
6     … sustenance, and to the creditors on behalf of Theodotos I gave …, Neilos having come upon … in Mesore expelled me from the aforesaid date-grove …
7     … since therefore I am unable to bring suit against him … of a contract,
8     … and my affairs require [the investigation before the strategos],
9     [I beg you, O king, to] order Diophanes the strategos to write … to the epistates to send
10    [Neilos] … and if it is as I write, to compel [him to give back] to me the value
11    … of 100 drachmas, and concerning the expulsion that Diophanes [decide as] seems good to him.
12    [And this being done, through you], O king, I shall obtain justice. Farewell.
13    … [above all reconcile them]; but if not, [send them] so that according to the laws they may receive justice.
14    Year 1, Gorpiaios 30, Tybi 13.
