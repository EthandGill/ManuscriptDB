[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Heron], strategos of the divisions of Themistes and Polemon of the Arsinoite nome,
2     [from Isidor]a daughter of Pakebkis son of Marsisouchos,
3     [her mother being] …eus daughter of Marsisouchos, priestess and
4     [prophetess] of the recognized temple which is [in] the village of Teptynis,
5     [through] … son of Kronion, an exempt priest and
6     stolistes [of the] same temple. Wishing to circumcise
7     my son Pakebkis, whose father is Kronion son of Pakebkis son of Harpokration, an exempt priest and successor to the prophetship of the same temple, and the son of my maternal
8     uncle, the deceased Marepsemis son of Marsisouchos, (namely) Panesis, whose mother is Thenpakebkis
9     daughter of Panesis, likewise priests of the same temple,
10    they being boys … anap… and
11    of the temple … and …
12    of priests, through whom she …
13    I request that, according to custom, a letter …
14    be written by you to [the most excellent high-priest, so that]
15    with his consent [the boys may] be able
16    to be circumcised and to perform the incumbent [sacred rites]. The boys are: Pakebkis
17    son of Kronion son of Pakebkis, whose mother is Isidora daughter of Pakebkis, aged 7 with reference to the 30th year; Panesis son of Marepsemis
18    son of Marsisouchos, whose mother is Thenpakebkis daughter of Panesis, likewise aged 11 with reference to the 30th year, whom my aforesaid
19    husband Kronion son of Pakebkis, being now in Alexandria,
20    will present to the most excellent high-priest.
