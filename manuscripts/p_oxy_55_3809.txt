[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aga]thangelus to Panarelus the barber
2     very many greetings.
3     [I gre]et and Heliodor[a]. the
4     [pros]kynema of you I make
5     [be]fore the gods here and
6     [the] prosk[y]nema of you each [day] I make. of the gods
7     [be]ing willing already the master [I] cut hair, and the household members
8     [all] I cut hair. whatever day I cut hair, the proskyn[e]ma I am accustomed to make. greet
9     [the] fellow-students all.
