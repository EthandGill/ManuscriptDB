[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidora to Sarapias her daughter, many greetings.
2     Before all things I pray
3     that you are in health together with your own people,
4     and I make obeisance for you
5     before the lord Sarapis
6     together with your child.
7     You know that I am distressed in my mind because of
8     your brother. I did not receive his deposits because I have been ill; they have days to receive them.
9     Concerning what you said, that I should send
10    a tunic for the little girl: if I find someone trustworthy I will send it.
11    And if god wills, I have overtaken you.
12    Whatever I have is yours and your brother's. I heard that you have had a quarrel with your husband on account of
13    your father. He has not remained … of mine because it is winter. Send him to himself; if I go up
14    I will steer him again. I remain
15    Apollon. Perhaps he will serve as a soldier;
16    whether he serves or does not serve, I must
17    go up. Write to me about
18    your welfare quickly. If you wish
19    to write a letter … of Serenos the brother of Skambys.
20    I remain his obeisance
21    in the bosom of Sarapis.
22    Greet the father of the
23    children and the children. Greet Thausaris, greet your children.
24    Greet Peeous and Pemen.
25    Do not compel me to blame you, Onnophris, over … the displeasure
26    of her. … Apollos greets you and your children.
27    Deliver to Onnophris the priest … a … es … to
28    From Apollos son of Salibotas.
