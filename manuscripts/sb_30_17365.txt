[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] year, … [in the month of Th]oth, 9, in the city of Crocodilopolis
2     [under Paniscus], agoranomos, of the upper toparchy
3     [of the Pathyrite.]
4     [acknowledges], Petesouchos, [son] of Peteartemtheos
5     [Tortominne], [son] of Schos, Persian, with [her] guardian
6     [of] her own son, Panobchouni, [son] of Harpaesis
7     [from the] guards, concerning, if he should confirm
8     [it] to him, the one who bought, from her, a half portion
9     [of a house], in the outer district, according to a written agreement
10    [of the sale, the one placed], through the same archive …
