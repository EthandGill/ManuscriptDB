[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion to Peteimouthes, greeting. The copy of the financial-order to
2     Leonides the grain-collector
3     is appended below. Farewell. Year 31, Pharmouthi 16.
4     To Le[onides: the] copy of the financial-order from Kastor the kinsman and administrator
5     of the affairs [of the admini]stration is appended below: Measure out — Peteimouthes the royal scribe joining in the instruction —
6     to the men designated to accompany Kastor, the Arab archers,
7     twenty men, for Phamenoth, to ea[ch] 2 artabai of wheat, which amount to
8     forty artabai of wheat from the produce of the 31st year, [by the] consumption [measure], total: wheat [by the] consumption measure 40 artabai, and make
9     a receipt and counter-receipt with them as is fitting.
10    Kastor to Sarapion, greeting. Measure out — Peteimouthes the royal scribe joining in the instructio[n] —
11    to the Arab archers accompanying
12    us, 20 men, for Phamenoth, to ea[c]h
13    2 artabai [of whea]t, amounting to forty artaba[s] of wheat
14    [total]: wheat [by the] consumption measure 40 artabai, and make a receipt and
15    [counter-receipt] with them as is fitting.
16    [To the scribes ] year [31], Ph[ar]mouthi 16.
