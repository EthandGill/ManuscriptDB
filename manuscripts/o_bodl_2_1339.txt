[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been measured in the treasury of villages, produce of year 2 of Antoninus
2     Caesar, the lord, Pauni 19, on behalf of Kerameians, by the name
3     [of Quintus] Apolaeus Atterius, through Marcus Clodius
4     [Alexander] ... ios Nech wheat: artaba one, half; it amounts to wheat artaba 1 1/2. Pekysis I have signed.
