[TRANSLATION]
1     To the village-scribe of Seruphaios
2     from Aurelius Paridos, who is also called Zeuxianos
3     Nerouianaios, who is also called Genealarchios, appointed
4     high priest of the illustrious
5     city of the Antinoites, through
6     Aurelius Ptolemaios of Herakiaian from Pela.
7     I register according to the things commanded
8     by Aurelius Antoninus
9     the most mighty, concerning the inspections
10    to Chairemon the strategos
