[TRANSLATION]
1     [In] the name of the holy and life-giving and consubstantial Trinity, of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
2     it was written in the month of Hathyr, on the 3rd, of the fourth indiction.
3     Aurelia Eudoxia, daughter of Georgios, her mother being Routh …, with the consent
4     and [agreement] to this present sale of mine of my husband, [Anouphios, son of]
5     [Isakios, tax-payer, from the village of Aphrodite of the]
6     Antaiopolite nome, to Aurelius [Menas, son of] the most blessed Christophoros, originating
7     from the same village of Aphrodite, greeting. I, the aforewritten Eudoxia, daughter
8     of Georgios, declare, having stated, and with the consent of my husband Anouphios, son of Isakios, tax-payer, through this
9     written security of mine, that is, a sale, of my own free will, by self-chosen wish
10    and with unrepentant and unchangeable and unalterable resolve, with most pure intention
11    and clean conscience and guileless purpose, with upright mind, and also swearing by both the
12    Almighty God and the victory of the imperial salvation, that I have sold to [you, the] aforesaid
13    Menas, son of the most blessed Christophoros, agent, and have conveyed by deed to you and
14    have ceded and made over to you from now for the ever-succeeding whole
15    and perpetual time, with every fullest right of ownership and in good faith
16    and with full authority and for eternal lordly possession, the part belonging to me and that came
17    to me by lawful inheritance from my most blessed father — my whole
18    share of the old, ruined house called (the house) of Jonathan the builder,
19    situated in the northern part of the said village of Aphrodite, on the street
20    of the holy catholic church, [op]ened toward the west into the
21    public street, with all its right, from the ground up to the air above,
22    in a word just as I possessed it while my most blessed father was still living, leaving nothing
23    out of my afore-agreed whole share of the aforesaid old
24    ruined house and of any right whatever, from the ground up to the air above as has been set out above; and the neighbors of the aforesaid old house
25    on the four sides are these: to the south the pier of the house of Menas, son of Chichois, the coppersmith;
26    to the east the great farmstead of the heirs of Promauos; to the north the house
27    of the heirs of Petros, or whoever the neighbors may be on every side, all round;
28    at the price mutually agreed and approved between us, full
29    and worthy, of two-thirds of a lawful imperial gold solidus
30    by exchange against an extra-weight of two on the standard of the same village, that is, 2/3 of a gold solidus, in carats by exchange 2,
31    which, this gold of the price, I have received in full, having stated it, I the seller Eudoxia, daughter
32    of Georgios, and with the consent of my husband Anouphios, son of Isakios, from you,
33    the buyer Menas, son of Christophoros, from your hand into my hands
34    in the sight of the lawful witnesses subscribing below, for the time from
35    now forever, in number and in weight full and complete, together with the sale being made, on which
36    price, being also persuaded, so that from this point you, the same buyer Menas,
37    son of Christophoros, may hold and own and be master, from my afore-agreed whole share of the aforesaid ruined
38    old house called (the house) of Jonathan the builder, and from any right whatever
39    from the ground up to the air above, according to the above specification, and may dwell
40    and administer and manage and build or also build upon, and lease
41    and re-lease, and pass it on to your own heirs and successors and possessors,
42    and use and enjoy it according to whatever lordly manner seems good to you, without hindrance
43    and unimpeded, with no one disputing with you or about to dispute, neither of
44    my heirs or co-heirs nor of those belonging to me by descent
45    or kinship; and the one who shall come against (you) at any time I will of necessity [turn away]; and if
46    I take too little care of the guarantee, I will repay the price threefold
47    together with the costs and expenses, in addition to this sale being
48    secure and valid, produced wherever; this sale, written in single copy, which willingly
49    and being persuaded I have made for you without force and deceit and compulsion and error
50    of any kind and fear and any guile, in a public archive and according to the laws,
51    completed with the subscription of him who signs on my behalf and of those who, according to
52    the customary practice, at my request bore witness as witnesses; and I have issued (it) to you for
53    security, and concerning all that is written herein and concerning the guarantee and
54    clearing of this sale, there being pledged to you for this
55    all my present and future property, both movable
56    and immovable and self-moving, generally and specifically, in every kind [and] sort,
57    by way of pledge and by right of mortgage, just as if by legal judgment; and concerning
58    all that is written herein, having been formally questioned, that these things are thus, that I will give, do,
59    and keep (them), I have agreed. Aurelia Eudoxia, daughter of Georgios, her mother being
60    Routh, and with the consent of her husband Anouphios, son of Isakios, the aforesaid,
61    have made this sale [and] have received the aforesaid full price, namely 2/3 of a gold solidus,
62    and I will guarantee with every guarantee against everyone and forever, and all
63    that is written herein is agreeable to me, and I have sworn the divine oath as aforesaid. Aurelius Ioannes,
64    son of Apollos, having been requested, wrote on her behalf. Aurelius Anouphios, son of Isakios,
65    the aforewritten, have made this sale and I receive the aforesaid
66    full price, namely 2/3 of a gold solidus, [and] we will guarantee
67    with every guarantee against everyone and (everyone?), and all that is written herein is agreeable to us,
68    and I have sworn the divine oath as aforesaid. Aurelius Ioannes, son of Hermauos,
69    I bear witness to this sale, having heard from the one who made it; I bear witness also to the
70    payment of the gold of the solidus of 2/3 of the price.
71    Aurelius Daueit, son of Phoibammon, I bear witness to this sale, having heard
72    from those who made it; I bear witness also to the payment of the gold
73    of the 2/3 solidus of the price. Aurelius Komasios, son of Biktor,
74    I bear witness to this sale, having heard from those who made it; I bear witness also [to] the payment
75    of the gold of the solidus, 2/3, of the price.
76    Through me, Apa Rasios the notary, [it was written], who also draw it up.
