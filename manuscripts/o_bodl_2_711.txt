[TRANSLATION]
1     Heras and associates, overseers of the sacred treasury
2     to Phatretes [Abotos]. We have received the bath-tax
3     of Charax of the 20th year of Hadrian Caesar
4     of the lord, Phamenoth 16. Asklas, I have witnessed.
5     Heriophymois, I have witnessed.
