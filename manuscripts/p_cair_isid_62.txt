[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Leontios also called Isidoros, … stationed in the Arsinoite,
2     from the Aurelii Thaesion and Kyrillous, both daughters of Kopres, whose mother is Sarapias,
3     from the village of Karanis, through their advocate and brother-in-law (?), their maternal
4     uncle Aurelius Ammonios son of Isidoros, from the same village
5     of Karanis. The greedy acts of men are of many sorts, but in this case,
6     when they are convicted, it remains for the attention of the higher authority
7     to redress what has been ventured. Our aforesaid father, after the death of our mother,
8     having joined himself to another woman, having ventured (this), remained a wretched time.
9     And, when he was about to depart life, we summoned the aforesaid wife of his and inquired of her whether our father owed anything to her
10    or to any other; and she acknowledged that he owed nothing. But after his death, the utterly wicked woman, first, put forward her own
11    father and compelled (him) to make off, from our father's livestock,
12    twenty-seven sheep; and, this having succeeded for her, she then
13    put forward (a claim) that there is owed to her by (our) father one gold mina-piece. And, having alleged this through mediators and not having proved this well as demonstrable, now, employing another method, she has brought against us a document as having been made by our aforesaid father, through which she registered that he, our father, had mortgaged to her
14    a half share of a slave-girl, in return for which she made use of her own dowry; but the proofs she
15    will not be able to make. For this is laid down by the laws, that the registered
16    dowries be assessed-together both through a goldsmith and through a tailor (?), and neither indeed will she
17    prove their dowry-items, nor (will she prove) the document of the mortgage which she alleged was drawn up for the half share
18    of the slave-girl — perhaps foreseeing that it was not rightly done. And being about, concerning this, to inform the greatness of the most eminent corrector Aurelius Achilleus,
19    we make the submission of these petitions, attesting this very thing, requesting
20    that through you she be compelled to exhibit to us copies of the securities she has put forward,
21    that is, both the dowry-document and the mortgage, so that thus, through the archives, having sought these out,
22    we may be able to speak against her before the higher authority. For we pay not a few and have discharged, on behalf of our aforesaid father, public and
23    private burdens, on behalf of the very large amount of public and private land registered (to him). Farewell.
24    In the 2nd year of our lord Lucius Domitius Domitianus Augustus, Thoth 8.
