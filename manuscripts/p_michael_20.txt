[TRANSLATION]
1     … [I] sent
2     to my brothers Borianos and
3     Hermogenes so that there might belong to them those things of prosperity.
4     But know well that if improperly concerning
5     this you should conduct yourself, you will be in danger not of a small kind. I wish you to be strong.
6     Year 2 of our lord Marcus
7     Aurelius Probus Augustus
8     Pauni, the twenty-third.
