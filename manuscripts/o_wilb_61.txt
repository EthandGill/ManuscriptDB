[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured from the threshing-floor into a boat through grain-collectors of the metropolis, produce
2     of 22nd year of Hadrian the lord, Pauni
3     8, on behalf of Charakas, in the name of Paouphtheious son of Paoutus through Psenchonsios
4     Phaerios and associates, of wheat artabae three plus two-thirds, which become wheat artabae 3 2/3. Petekhespokhrates has signed it.
