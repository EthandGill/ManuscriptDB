[TRANSLATION]
1     … [in Tholthis in] the [Oxyrhynchite nome.]
2     [Eupolis has leased for one] year, sowing [and harvest, one (crop) from the]
3     [sowing] in the ninth year, [whose produce is for the] tenth
4     [year]: Eupolis, an Athenian of the (men) of [Philon, a private person], to Alexandros son of Krates, a Cyrenaean of [the epigone, and] to Horos,
5     … an Oxyrhynchite, hierodoulos of Thoeris, the half
6     of the fallow land in his own allotment, which are arouras
7     twenty-two, together with seed, free of risk, at a rent of one hundred forty-six artabas of wheat; and let Eupolis give them
8     seed for each aroura, one artaba of wheat, at the
9     time of need. And if Eupolis find any land more than the twenty-two
10    arouras by survey of the same portion, let there be assigned
11    to Eupolis as rent for each aroura [eight] artabas of wheat together
12    with seed. And let Alexandros and Horos pay the rents that have been contracted to Eupolis in the month Dystros of the tenth year,
13    clean and unadulterated grain, produced in the land, by the measure,
14    the chous, of Eupolis, by just measurement, and let them carry it off to
15    Eupolis' (premises) at their own expense. And if they do not pay according to
16    what is written, [let them pay to Eupolis the price] …
