[TRANSLATION]
1     Athenodoros to Eurylokhos, the dearest, greetings
2     [and] to be well. You will do well by giving to Antonios in place of
3     the equal [sums] which we held from him on the Herakleopolite [territory]
4     from an account of registered settlers, of silver drachmas two thousand
5     of silver drachmas two thousand, total drachmas 2000
