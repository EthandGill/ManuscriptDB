[TRANSLATION]
1   Has paid: (the son) of Herieus son of Paapis, for poll-tax of year 20,
2   ten drachmas and bath-tax one, an obol, a half-obol — total 11 dr. 1½ ob. — and the surcharges on these
3   at an obol (and) half-obol. Year 20 of Tiberius Caesar
4   Augustus, Pharmouthi 31. Petemenophis son of Pikos.
