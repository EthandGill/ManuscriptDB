[TRANSLATION]
1     [Pylades] ... ou, Macedonian of the later generation
2     [loaned to Mene]sthes, [son] of Apollodotus, Persian
3     [of the later generation] of copper drachmas nine hundred [thirty] [with interest]. And the loan
4     [this] and the interest, let Mene[sthe]us repay to Pylades in one year from Dystrou[s]
5     of the sixteenth year. guarantor for collection: Diotimos, [son] of Ap[ollodo]tus, Persian of the later generation; document-keeper: Megas, [son] of Moschio[n], Ka[r]dian of the [later] generation.
6     Pylades [was] about forty years old, of medium build, fair-complexioned, long-faced, with a mole on his left eyebrow.
7     Mene[sthe]us [was] about thirty years old, of medium build, dark-complexioned, round-faced, with a scar in the middle of the forehead.
8     Diotimos [was] about forty-two years old, of medium build, dark-complexioned, long-faced, with a scar on the middle of the nose.
9     [Megas was] about forty-one years of good size, dark-complexioned
10    [round]-faced, with a scar conspicuous above both eyebrows.
11    [was written] in the year 16, Dystrous.
