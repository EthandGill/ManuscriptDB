[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ptollarion [son of] Ptollarion, former gymnasiarch, and
2     to Geminus, former gymnasiarch, and to Amatios also called Paulinos,
3     both sons of Patron, former gymnasiarch, and to Geminus, being absent, through Amatios also called Paulinos,
4     from Satyros son of Herakleides son of Heraklas,
5     [registered in the] quarter of T[am]e[ia, and]
6     from his son P[olion], the two (of us). [We wish] to lease from you for four years from the present tenth year the
7     [arourae] belonging [to you, to] P[tollarion]
8     as to the half part, and to the two (of you) as to
9     [the] remaining half part, around the village of Theogonis, of the holding, eight arourae, or as many as there may be,
10    in the first and third year for sowing
11    of fodder-grass for the eating of the sheep, without
12    rent, and in the second and fourth year
13    for [sowing] of wheat, the rent for the two-year period
14    [yearly together with what] I shall receive of seed
15    [of wheat, eight artabas], for the whole, yearly [for the two-year period,] of wheat, [one hundred and twenty artabas, by the] six-choinix measure [of the storehouse of Ptollarion and] of others, all [without deduction and free of risk, we doing all the] work of the arourae as is fitting from our own resources at the proper [seasons],
16    and we shall deliver the rents yearly in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor, new, clean, unadulterated, the measuring being done through one of your men,
17    the yearly transport-costs being upon you,
18    the (cost of) urging on; but the conveyance of the rents being upon us; and after the (lease) period we shall hand back the arourae clean of rush, reed, dog's-tooth grass, and all undergrowth. If it seems good to lease.
19    Year 10 of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the lord, Hathyr 11.
