[TRANSLATION]
1     … [to Athendoros] …
2     most greetings and to be well always; Philip of Ecrates registered in the accounting office from the
3     old account wheat artabae seven and Dionys
4     Proarchos wheat artabae twenty-one and [the]
5     village-scribe of the year I have reconciled and I have them in custody;
6     If therefore it seems to you, send me two dagger-bearers, so that I [send out] [them] to you; take care of yourself so that you are in good health.
7     Farewell. Year twenty-one of [Caesar] … day twenty-five.
8     To the administrator [Athendoros]
