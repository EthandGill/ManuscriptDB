[TRANSLATION]
1     It was given through the monks of the monastery of abba Andrew Joseph having remained for the need of the
2     machine place of Helios outside the gate in the month of Mecheir 24 of the fourth indiction one rope namely one ring [item],
3     it makes one rope namely one ring 1 only.
4     of year 232 and 201 Mecheir 24 of the fourth indiction.
5     Pikos
