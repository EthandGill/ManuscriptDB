[TRANSLATION]
1   Register of private embankments
2   of the 3rd year of the Imperator
3   Caesar Domitianus
4   Augustus Germanicus,
5   and these are (the items):
6   Tychios son of Nekos, embankment called
7   Papsis, that maintained according to the assessment-allotment
8   of which each holds (in) arouras
9   by the men listed below,
10   (measuring by) cord: ½ + ¼ + 1/16;
11   Horion son of Harpaesis: 16 arouras,
12   Demetris and Theon, both
13   sons of Didymos in equal shares: 50 1/3,
14   Didyme daughter of Horion and Harthoonis
15   son of Thoonis son of Harthoonis and Tanechotes
16   (daughter) of Horion: 18,
17   Sambous (daughter) of Dionysios: 3,
18   Sarapion and Chairemon a[nd] Di[o]nysia,
19   the three children of Dionysios son of Sarapion son of Athenaios: 91,
20   of those from the household, (through) Horion
21   the overseer: 200,
22   Petsiris also called Antit( ) son of Petsiris: paid in full,
23   (through) Totoeus son of Onnophris,
24   completing(?): 1,
25   Tasentheus (daughter) of Onnophris: 2,
26   Tsenyris (daughter) of Ergeus: 3,
27   Strouthes son of Strouthos son of Petsiris: 1,
28   Herakleides(?) son of Herakleides, completing: 1,
29   (of) Tiberius Claudius son of Theon
30   son of Sarapion: 6,
31   Petsiris also called Aniketos, Inaro( )
32   for the sons: 3½,
33   total: 401½ + 1/3 arouras.
34   And from the west of the public embankment
35   [...]oi[..]. public( )
