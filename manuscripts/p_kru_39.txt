[TRANSLATION]
1     With Theutourou, the daughter [of Hymai, and] with Sanagape, the son
2     of the blessed Isaak — his [mother] was the sister — all of us belonging to
3     our family in the fortress of Djeme, the nome of the city of Hermonth,
4     having afterwards a subscriber to subscribe on our behalf
5     to this written deed of partition which is a settlement, not to be transgressed
6     and not to be shaken out of them by the laws,
7     and moreover to confirm it through some further trustworthy witnesses
8     who shall testify to it on our behalf, according to our request,
9     our own, of ourselves, we who write to our brother Shenoute
10    the priest, and the son of the blessed Germanos,
11    the man of this same fortress of Djeme, the nome of this same city,
12    greeting: that, since at this present time which is laid
13    before us now, the 3rd indiction, we sued one another in agreement
14    before the most glorious Komes, the administrator of the fortress of Djeme,
15    concerning the whole house which is on the street of Koulol, that we might divide it,
16    so that what fell to each one's lot he should take of it and become
17    its lord — that is, two for the male, one for the female —
18    according to the orders of our lords; and after the partition,
19    then, of the legal claim which we made with one another, before his most-glorious
20    self, Komes the administrator, who commanded us according to the manner in which we were persuaded, we chose certain great sons of the fortress.
21    He cast them into the house of our blessed father Germanos,
22    this which is on the street of Koulol, and they divided it among us
23    of what is just, with the fear of God, according to the manner in which it pleased us,
24    into two parts; he cast lots up; the lot
25    came up upon you, you our brother Shenoute the priest.
26    You took the whole upper room (?) which is in the granary; you took the fourth part
27    of the dining-hall (symposion) which is in front of the door of the masters, from
