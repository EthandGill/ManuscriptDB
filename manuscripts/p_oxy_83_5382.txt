[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of our most divine and] most piou[s] [lord, greatest benefactor,]
2     [Flavius Tiberius] [Cons]t[ant]ine [the eternal Augustus and Imperator, year 8, in the consulship of the same]
3     [most pious lord] of ours, the four[th] year … [indiction] …
4     [To the most eminen]t heir[s] [of Apion of glorious memory, who became]
5     [protopatricius,] [landho]l[d]ers he[re too in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas]
6     the servant, who as[k]s and pro[vide]s to his [ow]n master[s], the s[ame] [all-praiseworthy]
7     men, the ac[tio]n and liability, (I) [P]etros, monk and a[rch]imandri[te]
8     of the monastery of Pa[mou]thios, son of the b[les]sed John, subscribing below in [my own] [hand],
9     from the same cit[y]. I acknowledge, of [my ow]n free wi[ll] and by volu[nta]ry ch[oi]ce,
10    swearing the divine and revered oath, that I stand su[re]ty and guarantee before
11    your magnificence the Aurelii Herminos and Souroun, full brothers
12    by their father, originating from the estate of Megale Tarouthinos of the
13    Oxyrhynchite nome, belonging to your magnificence, registered farmers,
14    on condition that they shall remain without interruption and pass their life in the same estate
15    and, fulfilling the produce-rents owed by them, (that) your
16    magnificence, that is I, from my own means on their behalf shall give and pay in full
17    (to) your magnificence on their behalf. And when they are sought
18    from me, I shall present them and hand them over without any recourse, where
19    I also received them, in the custody of your glorious house.
20    And if I do not do this, I acknowledge that I am liable in all things to answer for what is sought against them.
21    The surety is valid, written singly, and having been formally questioned I acknowledged.
22    I, Petros, monk, son of John, this
23    surety is agreeable to me as aforesaid.
24    [ ] [Surety] made by Petros the monk, son of John, from (the city) of the Oxyrhynchites, guaranteeing Herminos
25    [an]d S[ou]roun fro[m] Megale Tar[outhinos].
