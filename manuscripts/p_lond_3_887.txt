[TRANSLATION]
1     … [from] him I have leased out the [upper] parts, but he himself has moved into two rooms in the
2     courtyard, and has built in the courtyard a stair[case] carrying to[…]
3     another's house existing beside the house up[on] the staircase, and then
4     having disposed of my [things] to another Egyptian for gain's sake, and now on
5     which [places] I hold, having come upon [them], he has cast me out from my portions of the
6     courtyard, using force. I beseech you therefore to look upon [this] and, having recalled him[self], to examine concerning these [matters], and if these are [true]
7     to compel him to yield up to me [my] portions,
8     so that through you, the just [one], I might obtain [relief]. Farewell.
