r.9    χρείαν ποιήσῃ καί, ἐὰν συνορᾷ, καὶ προτέλειαν ἀγοράσῃ.

[TRANSLATION]
1     [this …] letter send immediately to the lord George in Antinoopolis through one of our [people].
2     But I received [the things] sent through Stephanacius, once the boy of the lord Theodore, and truly the thrice-accursed
3     builder Senouthes greatly neglected [it]. Moreover, in another place shall you cause the kiln of lime to be built and prepare
4     it thoroughly, quickly clean out that entire space so that we may find [it necessary] to fill it, as I wrote previously,
5     in the coming fasts, God commanding. Write me of the health of Theodosius, since I heard that he is ailing
6     and I was very concerned. And besides having Zacharias struck together with us here, I wish to heal him. Moreover,
7     rather he has reeds, [up to] three small myriads in the plain of Thynes. These cause by every means
8     to be transported to his estate. He wrote me that he needs reeds and, as I learned, [the] reed is nothing there. Moreover, the
9     need you shall provide and, if he sees fit, even the preliminary payment purchase [it].
