[TRANSLATION]
1     Pamouthes son of Pthomouthes and Pmenches, elder of Athas
2     chaff-collectors of the Memnoneia, in the name of Quintus, second
3     of the daughters of Quintus, through the farmer Aurelius
4     Psenmouthes son of Horus. We have received for produce of [the] 22nd year
5     of chaff, measure 24, 48.
6     Of [the] 23rd year of Antoninus Caesar
7     the lord, Pauni 1st.
