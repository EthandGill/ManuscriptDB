[TRANSLATION]
1     Since the time has come at which the care of the inexorable grain-transport of the fortunate … must take place,
2     of the second indiction, avoiding any blame of negligence, take pains to send this off to the great city of the Alexandrians
3     with all fear, loading into small vessels the required measure
4     directly and at once, according to the appended notice, [at the risk] of the office; for it is for this very reason
5     that he has been sent from the office. (Latin: legi — "I have read.")
6     To those from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite (nome).
7     [It is necessary, from your village, in imitation of the past]
8     [first indiction, to bring in the appended measure on account of the shipment of the second]
9     [indiction and to load (it) onto boats, and]
10    the head-man [and the supervisor and the sailors together with the]
11    boats laden with the produce, to send off to
12    the office for the customary drawing-up of the memoranda,
13    so that after this the same measure be sent off to
14    the great city of Alexandria.
15    Grain: 21,100 artabas.
16    Of which, in the meantime, of the grain by small vessels: 2,000 artabas, and the twentieths 1 and 2;
17    remaining for the great shipment: grain 4,100 artabas.
18    [To those from the] village of Aphrodite [of the Antaiopolite (nome)].
