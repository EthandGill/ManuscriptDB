[TRANSLATION]
1     Philammon to Tekose
2     my sister, many greetings.
3     I am astonished that you did not write me a single letter about
4     anything at all. I wrote to you
5     before this, saying: Keep
6     my place, which is
7     under lease. So then you will do well: take men and estimate the olives. And if
8     God orders you to release
9     us and we live, I
10    will repay you your
11    favour; but if God does not (so order),
12    I give you the favour.
13    For surely you too have heard
14    the things that happened concerning us here. But simply, concerning everything
15    I shall entrust my place to you, that you may keep it. And the little jar of oil, if you take it to yourself, sell it and quickly
16    send the price through a trustworthy
17    man, since I [have] need of it here, because much loss
18    has been inflicted on me here. And
19    the colt, sell
20    it and send the
21    price. And I am astonished:
22    I wrote to you, saying: Send my
23    cloak and
24    the tunic, and you took no care; send them to me. You know
25    too that I remember (you). Why did you not remember us either, but you are forgetful? I greet your
26    son Psais and his
27    brothers. I greet Cholos, I greet
28    Ammonios.
29    So then, look after my
30    mother. Whatever you have of mine,
31    keep her, because she has not yet
32    learned the things concerning us here.
33    I pray that you
34    be in health for many
35    years.
36    To Tekose my sister
37    from Philammon.
