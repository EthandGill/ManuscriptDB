[TRANSLATION]
1     Diogenes to Dorian the brother
2     Greetings.
3     I considered it necessary through a letter before all things to greet you.
4     You [wrote] to me concerning the price of the
5     oil, that now it is of much price
6     with you, and it does not seem to me to buy at much. And previously I wrote to you concerning the
7     wooden items, in order that you might send them to me. If
8     then it be brought to me, I will give to them the carrying-frame. And you will send to me a metretes
9     of oil of the existing price [only] fair.
10    Farewell.
11    [Greet] Polydan the friend
12    and all yours.
13    In year 8 of Autocrat Caesar [Domitian]
14    Augustus Germanicus. Choiach 6.
15    From Diogenes to Dorian the brother
