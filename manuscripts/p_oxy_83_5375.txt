[TRANSLATION]
1     … [Pau]ni 24, in the 5th indiction, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites.
2     [To Flavius Apion] the all-praiseworthy and most eminent, former ordinary consul, landholde[r]
3     [also here in the splendid] city of the [Oxy]rhynchites, through Menas his servant who is interrogating and securing
4     [for his own master, the] s[a]me all-praiseworthy man, the action and liability, Georgios
5     [son of] the (man) of [bl]essed memory Apollos, from the same city, subscribing below in his own
6     hand, appointed also as rent-collector of the estate of the most glorious Apion.
7     I acknowledge, of my own free will, swearing by God the Almighty and by the piety
8     of the ever-victorious fortune, that I guarantee and undertake before your eminence the Aurelii
9     Anouthios and Phileas, full brothers, from their father Phoibammon, their mother being Maura,
10    registered cultivators of your eminence, originating from the hamlet of …
11    … [belon]ging to it, of the Ox[yr]hynchite nome, on condition that they remain in
12    [the same] [estat]e with their same dearest ones and wives and cattle and all
13    [their baggage] … all the things that pertain to their person
14    [that is, to the status of the registered cultivator,] whom, when they are sought from me, I shall produce
15    [and hand over into the public prison] of this city, outside holy precincts
16    [and divine images and] ev[ery] place of refuge, where I also received them,
17    [or, if I do not do this,] I shall be liable to answer for all things sought against them,
18    [pledging] into the security of this surety all my
19    [possess]ions bo[t]h [pres]ent and [futu]re, specifically and generally, by way of pledge and by right of mortgage.
20    [Binding is] the surety, written singly, and on being questioned I acknowledged.
21    [ ] [Georgi]os so[n] of the bles[sed] Ap[o]llos, the aforewritten,
22    … e … the af[or]esaid Anouth[io]n and Phileas
23    … [as] [af]or[esai]d …
