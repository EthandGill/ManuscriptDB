[TRANSLATION]
1     Trapbos to Heracl[e]us his father, very many greetings. Before all
2     else, I pray that you are well.
3     I have made [myself] troublesome to my relatives
4     [unpleasant], and I wish to depart from them. If indeed you are able
5     to come, come quickly. But if indeed I do not depart from them
6     then every day they beat me. Greet all those
7     in the house.
8     Year 11 of Imperator Caesar Tr[aian]
9     Hadrian Augustus, on Payni 5.
10    [From] Trapbos. Deliver to Heracl[e]us.
