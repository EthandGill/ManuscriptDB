[TRANSLATION]
1     [grain-collectors] of the village of Magdala Bucolus
2     [patrimonial] Hermes of Phibion and Bion
3     [P]asion account of silver-payment, 3rd indiction
4     [account] of patrimonial Demetria also called Am[monia], twenty talents; [it] becomes talents twenty
5     [for] the same silver-payment of the same 3rd indiction
6     Mecheir 10, of the consulship of Cecina Sabinus and Oe[ti]us Rufinus of the most illustrious. Aurelius Apollo, notary, I wrote the document.
7     on behalf of them, not [knowing] letters.
