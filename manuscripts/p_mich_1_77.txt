1     Apollonius
2     to Zenon greetings. Concerning the
3     boy whom
4     Philo the teacher brought to you, if to you
5     [it is] against [your] judgment because
6     of time, until the indeed
7     so-called
8     gray hairs [he] may get and
9     the rest, nothing
10    may you not have to him
11    to complain.
