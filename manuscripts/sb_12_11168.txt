1     Theon of Diogenes of Theon
2     eirenaophylakios, also Althaieus
3     to the grain-collectors of the Middle toparchy
4     of Kerkeuraosis district
5     greetings. Distribute what you have of mine
6     remaining amounts of [wheat] for Hephaestion, of Harpocration, artabas 3, 4, 8; they amount to artabas 3, 4, 8. Of the year
7     second and twentieth of the Emperor
8     Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrian Antonie[n]us
9     Augustus Pious, Phaophi eighth.
