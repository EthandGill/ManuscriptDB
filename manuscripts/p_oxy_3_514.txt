[TRANSLATION]
1   To Neilos, styled (son of his) mother Soeris, and the collector of grain-(taxes) with him,
2   of those owing for ... and
3   the rents of (the lands) in Talao near Sinaru,
4   of the produce of the past 30th year.
5   Greeting. I have received from you, for salary (opsonion), 400 silver drachmas,
6   the registration of the accounts being my responsibility.
