[TRANSLATION]
1     In the first year of Imperator Caesar Domitian
2     Augustus, in the month Dystros the seventh (= Tybi 7), in Theadelph[ia]
3     of the Themistos division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge,
4     Dios, about twenty-seven years old, with a scar on the first finger
5     of his left hand, and Osis, about twenty-four years old,
6     with a scar on his left foot, and Herakles, about
7     twenty years old, with a scar on his left shin — the three (sons) of Herakles
8     the younger, Persians of the epigone, mutual sureties
9     for payment, to Theon also called Sokrates, son of Heron,
10    about thirty-one years old, with no distinguishing mark — that they have from him
11    on the spot, from hand to hand, out of (his) house, the price of new wine
12    of the vat, from the produce which they will harvest about the
13    aforesaid village of Theadelphia, of the so-called
14    vineyard of Dionysios: sixty-six Arsinoite jars (keramia),
15    in bulk from that same vineyard,
16    of which the acknowledging parties shall also make delivery
17    to Theon in the month Kaisareios of the present
18    first year, at the vat, without any delay
19    or pretext-finding, Theon supplying the (storage-)jars.
20    And if they do not deliver as has been written, let them forfeit
21    on the spot, with the half-as-much-again (penalty) and interest, the right of
22    execution accruing to Theon both from the acknowledging parties
23    and from whichever of them he chooses, and from all the things belonging
24    to them, just as by legal judgment. The subscriber for the
25    acknowledging parties is Phronton son of Papos, about 40 years old, with a scar on his neck on the right.
26    Dios and Osis and Herakles, the three (sons) of Her[akles, Persians of the]
27    epigone, mutual sureties for [payment, we acknowledge] that we have from Theon also called [Sokrates, son of Hero]n, the price of new wine of the vat …
