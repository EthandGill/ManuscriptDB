[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Herminus the notary, Flavius Menas son of Phoib[ammon].
2     I received from you [concerning] my grain-money and interest through Maus the weigher
3     … of Philadelphia, and other manifest carats of gold [coins]
4     five minus seven carats, [which] makes gold coins five minus seven carats, and these readily
5     I [am ready to settle] for you from my grain-money and interest account
6     [as it is being] done and for your security I have made [you] this receipt
7     under [my] signature as stated. Flavius Menas [son of Phoib]ammon agrees with me [regarding] the receipt
8     of the coins five minus seven carats as stated. Written in Mecheir 14th, 9th indiction.
