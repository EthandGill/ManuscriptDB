[TRANSLATION]
1     Esthiadas to the father and to the mother, to greet
2     and to be well. Since many times I write to you concerning the one who acted nobly,
3     yourself to care for until the
4     matters to be restored,
5     even now you will do well by encouraging yourself and those with us.
6     For Paon is sailing up in the month Tybi with forces
7     sufficient to suppress the
8     crowds in Hermouthis, and to treat them
9     as rebels; [and] supervise
10    and the sisters and Pelops
11    and Stachys and Senathyris.
12    Be well. Year 40, Choiac 23.
13    Deliver [to]
14    Pathyris
15    to the father.
