[TRANSLATION]
1     Tasoucharis to Nilus the brother
2     [most] greetings. Before all things
3     I pray you to be well. To know
4     I want you, that there has come to me
5     Suchas saying [that] buy for me
6     the share of the olive-grove. He gave to me
7     Nilus four minas. Examine concerning it
8     and learn, if it is clear, and send to me the same the copy,
9     so that I may learn, what I shall do. I greet
10    Serapion and his children
11    and Heron and [the] children of him
12    and Ammoun and the children
13    of her and your wife and
14    your child.
15    Deliver to Nilus from Tasoucharis sister of Tasachos.
