1     28
2     To Aurelius Plution, secretary of the grain-dole,
3     from Aurelius Kopreus, son of Th… [whose mother is]
4     Didyme, from the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites. Being an Oxyrhynchite and having been scrutinized in the quarter
5     of the Dromos of Thoeris, and having become, in
6     the present 2nd year, of … years of age, [from the lot]
7     I introduce myself in place of …
8     …klas son of Theon, whose mother is …
9     who was of the same quarter,
10    now deceased, [and I request, demonstrating]
11    that I am a citizen, to [receive a] share in the distribution of the grain-dole. Year 2 of the Emperor
12    Caesar Marcus Aurelius
13    Claudius Pius [Felix]
14    Augustus, Thoth …
15    I, Aurelius Kopreus son of Th… [have submitted this.]
16    I, Aurelius Herakleides, wrote [on his behalf,]
17    he being illiterate.
18    I, Aurelius Didymus, having been [phylarch: thus]
19    it stands and he himself is [the aforesaid one,]
20    who, having been proclaimed, [responded.]
21    I, Aurelius Serenus, scrutineer: this [is]
22    the one scrutinized, who, having been proclaimed, responded.
23    I, Aurelius Theon, verifier: this is the one examined,
24    whom [also] I identify. I, Aurelius Saras also [called Isidoros, verifier:]
25    this is the one examined, whom [also I identify.]
26    Registered in year 2, Phaophi.
27    Of the Dromos of Thoeris.
