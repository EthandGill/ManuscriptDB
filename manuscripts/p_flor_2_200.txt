1     From Ischyrion.
2     to the descended bulls upon malaga [...]
3     making room for the construction of a fountain, provide the customary food. I pray that you are in good health.
4     To Heroneinous, the manager of Thea[delphia].
5     Year 7, Hathyr 16.
