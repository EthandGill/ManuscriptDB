[TRANSLATION]
1     To Cyriacus the oil-seller: furnish to Augarus the caulker for the expense
2     of working on the boats of the canal, oil sextarii 3 three only. Written in month
3     Mesore 9, indiction 6, by me Pettarius [deacon] and notary [of him] it was completed
