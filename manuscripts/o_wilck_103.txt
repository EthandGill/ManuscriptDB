[TRANSLATION]
1   Didymion, collector of the money-tax.
2   Paid: Harpaesis son of Phano-
3   phis, mother Tachomtbekis,
4   for the poll-tax of the nineteenth year
5   of Trajan Optimus the lord:
6   eight drachmas — total 8 dr., through Sarapion,
7   Phamenoth 21.
8   (2nd hand) likewise another eight drachmas
9   — total 8 dr.
