[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Flavius Sisinnius the ] … [ most eminent rationalis (katholikos)]
2     [from Aurel]ius [Ammon son of Petear]besch[inis, advocate from Panopolis]
3     [of] the Thebaid. Since [Eugene]ios the memorialis (memorarios) [and I, Ammon,] having become friend[s]
4     between ourselves, in the city of the Panopolitans, before Panisk]os ex-
5     ju[r]idicus, and Apollo[n] the poet [an]d another, Horion, [a w]ritten [agreement]
6     in common we made con[cerning] slaves now [being] in Alexandri[a], this
7     splendid city, [le]ft behind by [Ha]rpokration my [brot]her.
8     But in this agreement I do not [know] for what reason I was led to ac[cept] such a fixed [time-limit]
9     as this, that within twenty days from the seven[th and tw]entieth
10    of the past month Hathyr I should arr[ive] in Alexandria, so that an end
11    might be set to the matter. But having now come here two [days bef]ore (the term), the
12    aforesaid Eugeneios I did not meet, nor have I [been able] to find him; for this reason
13    today I publicly protest, (today) which is Choiak the thir[teenth, fo]ur (days) before the time-limit, securing myself and requesting that this [my] safeguarding-declaration be deposited i[n the of]fice, until he arrives, so [that] it may [no]t be possible for him to put forward any pretext; and these very [things set forth I requ]est, before
14    your Greatness, master, to be referred through the city['s tabu]larius
15    on this present day.
16    In the consulship of Flavius Philippus the most illustrious prefect of the [sacred prae]torium
17    and of Flavius Salia the most illustrious master of cavalry, [Choiak] [the thir]teenth.
18    I, Aurelius Ammon, have presented (this) as is set forth.
19    Advocate (scholasticus).
