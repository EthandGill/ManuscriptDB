[TRANSLATION]
1     … [grain-collector] the [workshop] around Oxyrhynchus Asclepia[des] greetings. I have embarked
2     [with the accompany]ing [ones] upon the at the New harbor so as for arrangement
3     th… to the … a by Asclepiades and Nicarchos
4     … hs unmarked whose lessee… ios the ship-owner Apollonius from the [products of the] 28th year wheat tax artabas one thousand six hundred total, Ach measure, in the measuring vessel
5     agreed [according] to the bronze and just scale, but the sample having been jointly sealed
6     to the ship-owner I sent for the sample [inspection]. with [the] ring with which also the messenger has been sealed.
7     Farewell. Year 29 Thoth 12
8     Apollonius and Apollonius the [ones] counter-written by the royal secretary we have embarked as also has been written before wheat [artabas] one thousand six hundred total, wheat artabas Ach.
9     Farewell. Year 29 Thoth 12
10    to Asclepiades
