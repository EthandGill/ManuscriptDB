[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 3rd year, from Menches, village-scr[ib]e
2     of Kerkeosiris. Register of temples and pr[oph]ets and
3     of liturgical days [and] of the (property) belonging around
4     the village. Of Souchieion and the crocodile-burial-place,
5     through Marres son of Petosiris and his brothers,
6     of liturgical days 30,
7     and the fifth part the same men hold,
8     having bought (it) from the treasury according to the
9     underlying schedule of which the date is the 3rd year,
10    Phaophi 18; and there is given to the crocodile-burial-place
11    from those from the same (village),
12    from the royal cultivators, both for the sacrifices and for lightings
13    of lamps and cedar-oil, 5 artabas of wheat, and in sacred land
14    of lesser temples in the unproductive (list) 5 ¼ ⅛ arouras,
15    other revenue they have none.
16    Of the Thogerieion, there hold in possession the undersigned,
17    of liturgical days 30, through Ka[ty]tis
18    son of Katytis and Onnophris son of Nektenib[is a]nd
19    Harmachores son of Harmachores and Tothoes son of … and
20    Pasis son of Panetbeus, of liturgical day[s] 30,
21    and the fifth part the sam[e] men hold,
22    other revenue they have none.
23    Of another Thogerieion, through Kenteisis son of H[o]ros and his brothers
24    and Harmiysis son of Petosiris,
25    of liturgical days 30,

27    from the father,
28    revenue they have none.
