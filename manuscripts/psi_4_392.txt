[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermocrates to Zenon, greetings. If you are in good health and everything else is turning out well for you, that would be as it should be; but I myself am well.
2     Since our examination has been conducted properly and submitted to the court, and while we were waiting, being under no small expense, [we considered it] not to trouble any of our friends, thinking we would soon visit the king; but since through other matters that had arisen necessarily it became prolonged, and what [small amount] we had [kept aside] for those protecting our safety, we spent on daily necessities, so that [it came to pass]
3     the king having become gracious and having prescribed the pardon, which together with a god I am persuaded [happened],
4     [it] seems [to] others [that we are] changed; our matters toward you still from [the] beginning placed in the most necessary [concerns], we thought [it] good
5     to write how, if it seems [good] to you, supposing [something] of the proposed [matter] to have been expended, giving to Ammonius
6     who brought you the letter whatever [amount] whenever you are able, and know well that whatever contributes to my salvation, if, as we write, [the things] from the king might come to us according to [reason], [you will] receive [them] doubled;
7     but if not, [you will] be among all not-turned-away [as] a man unblamed by you having become in disgrace
8     from misfortune. You would do me a favor writing more earnestly here those [things] you suppose
9     suitable for to stand by us in what we might happen to need harmlessly, and to me
10    write how much you agree with Ammonius.
11    Farewell. Year 7, Tybi 12.
12    To Zenon
