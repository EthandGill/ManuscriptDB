[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sefoi.
2     To Hie[ra]x the strategos
3     from Amatokus son of Dikaiogenes;
4     I declare for the present seventh year
5     of Tiberius Caesar Sebas[t]us the livestock belonging to us sheep twenty
6     eight, goats two, making sheep twenty-eight,
7     accompanying lambs kids,
8     which will be pastured around Sefoi of the Thmoisefo toparchia and through the whole nome
9     through [the shepherd] … of Mosios of Deios
10    being registered for the
11    Nesmemis, of which I shall also arrange the due
12    tax.
