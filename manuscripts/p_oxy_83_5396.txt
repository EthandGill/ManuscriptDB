[TRANSLATION]
1     Phaophi 24 of the fortunate fourteenth indiction, in Herakleopolis. To Flavius Apion, the most glorious
2     and most magnificent consul, son of the … of illustrious memory Strategios and Praeiecta, through Elias your
3     household servant who is present and acts as questioner and provides to his own master the rights of action from this
4     interrogation and the liabilities and every right, Ploutinos, chief-symmachus of your glorious
5     house, son of Elias, from Herakleopolis, knowing letters but unable to subscribe
6     on account of his eye-disease, and he entrusted his own brother Chrestos, your riparius, on his behalf
7     to subscribe, greeting. I acknowledge willingly, through this my written surety, without deceit and
8     without fear and force and fraud and compulsion and without extortion, that I stand surety before your magnificence
9     [through] those pertaining to it, and undertake for Makarios the presbyter, son of Papnouthios, and
10    … Kerekon son of Menas and Abraamios son of Anoup, agents from your village
11    of …eine of the Herakleopolite nome, whom, being sought at any time by
12    your magnificence through those pertaining to it, on condition that I produce and deliver them
13    into the public prison of this city or into your glorious house, without any detention
14    or pretext, just as I have received them. And if I do not do this, I acknowledge that I hold myself liable
15    to present myself for their production, that is, to provide to you, for each person
16    of default, half a pound of gold, total 1 1/2 pounds of gold, at my risk and that of my property
17    and future property. And this I, the aforewritten Ploution, chief-symmachus, acknowledge readily
18    to hold, God willing, while I am in health, to subscribe to this surety without hindrance.
19    The surety is valid, and being asked the formal question about all these things I gave my consent. Ploutinos, chief-symmachus of
20    your glorious house, son of Elias, the aforewritten, I have made this
21    surety and everything agrees with me as aforesaid. I, Chrestos, riparius of the same
22    glorious house, wrote on his behalf because he suffers from eye-disease, having been entrusted by him.
23    Surety made by Ploutinos, chief-symmachus, son of Elias, from Herakleopolis, standing surety for Makarios and …
24    and Kerekon and Abraamios, agents from …eine.
