[TRANSLATION]
1     Andromachos to Harchebis, greeting. A copy of the [transactions with] Leonides the grain-collector
2     is appended below. … Farewell. Year 4, Hathyr 11.
3     To Leonides. For Petosiris, son of Semtheus, [who farms] around Bousiris, measure out
4     — with Harchebis the [royal] scribe joining in the instruction — for seed-loans
5     for the seed for the 4th year, he having previously written by hand-pledge
6     the royal oath that he will sow all the land and will deliver
7     from the new (crops) the loans together with the rents, exempt from any deduction,
8     with the opinion of the topogrammateus and of the others accustomed (to it).
9     The (sums) which in the 3rd year he also transacted: three hundred
10    forty-nine and a third artabas of wheat, total 349 1/3 artabas of wheat; and a receipt
11    and counter-receipt make out for them as is fitting.
12    And another. For Hasphis and Teos and those with them from Thmoiobasis
13    [of the area] around Bousiris, who farm around the village around Mouchinakon, from the
14    … into which they farm
15    [royal land around the village] … and around Bousiris … …
16    … of wheat 490 artabas …
17    … [royal] land to the farmers, of wheat 500 artabas …
18    … into which they farm [around the village royal land] … with the [opinion of the]
19    [topogrammateus, of wheat … artabas] … and around Tebetnoi, of wheat 70 artabas …
20    … to the scribes. Year 4, Choiak 16.
21    … to Herakleodoros, year 4, Choiak 16.
