1     Year 6, Pachon 7
2     He has measured into the treasury of Apollo's city into the
3     same year of the Apollonopolitan, Haremsyni[s]

5     Payni 14, wheat, 4 artabas [and] 16; similarly, wheat, 1 artaba [and one-half]
6     Kastor
