[TRANSLATION]
1     Dioskoros and Pamonthes, collectors of the money-tax of the South,
2     (to) Arynchis (son of) Psentkys (son of) Petosiris, his mother being ...( ).
3     We have, for poll-tax of year 13, impure eight drachmas, total impure 8 dr. Year 13
4     of Antoninus the lord, Phamenoth 9. Psenminis.
5     (2nd hand) Amonios, I have signed.
