[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Panares to Heraklas his son, greetings. I, your mother, your sister and your child and the little Tapsais, we greet you. I visited Mnesitheos and got 300 drachmas, of which I gave Dionysios 200 drachmas for the sheaves, to the public donkey-drivers on your behalf for the two associations 12 drachmas. I pray for your health. Try to buy on your way about … artabs of lentils … if you find … From Panares to Heraklas his son.
