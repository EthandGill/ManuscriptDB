[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, harvest of year 3 of Lucius Septimius
2     Severus Pertinax Caesar of the lord
3     Pauni 24 for Agorae, in the name of Tchiôtos (son) of Imouthes
4     through the heirs through Bêsios, Bêsios, of wheat, artabae one-half
5     there is of wheat, artabae one-half. Ba, grain-collector, I have signed.
