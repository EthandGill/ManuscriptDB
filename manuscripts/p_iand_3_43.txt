[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Philoxenos the
2     most illustrious, Phaophi 17, of the 4th indiction.
3     To Flavius Serenus the most illustrious, of (the rank of)
4     tribunes, son of the man of illustrious memory
5     Martyrios, from the illustrious
6     city of the Oxyrhynchites — (from) Aurelia Rachel
7     daughter of Auan, her mother being Irgine,
8     a fuller-woman from the [s]ame city,
9     greeting. I acknowledge that I have received and
10    have been paid in full from your Illustriousness my wage, which I earned
11    working at my own cr[af]t
12    of the fullers, for the man of g[lori]ous
13    memory Pamouthios, who was the rec[eiver],
14    son of the blessed Papnouthios who
15    was also (called) Aba Koutos, from this city,
16    for the one year — the (sums) due
17    to me, in full, th[at] is, two artabas of wheat
18    (and) a half, makes 2½ (artabas) of wheat; and [fo]r the security
19    of your Illustriousness I have made this
20    receipt, which, being valid,
21    written in single copy, and on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
22    I, Aurelia Rachel daughter of Au[a]n, the
23    aforewritten, have made this
24    receipt [as is set] forth above, having been paid in full
25    [by] way of wage for the on[e] year, two artabas of wheat
26    (and) a half, and everything agrees with me as is set forth above, and
27    on being formally questioned I gave my assent and released (her). I, Au[reli]us Victor, son
28    of the blessed Ioannes, having been requested, wrote on
29    her behalf, she being illiterate.
30    Receipt of Rachel the fuller-woman, daughter of Auan, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites, by way of wage,
31    of wheat 2½ artabas.
