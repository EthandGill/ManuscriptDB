[TRANSLATION]
1     Our father, the one called by his son, the father Peter, [since] you entreated me, I gave the woman to you forth this day and tomorrow, the excellence of children in the heart and their oath to me … he took
2     … their word of our Lord therefore may they come down to the earth and
3     they may judge, go forth being sent and come to her to turn
4     to them, a joy in the Lord. Month of Phaophi 24, indiction 15.
