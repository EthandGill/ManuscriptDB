[TRANSLATION]
1     [Melas] … to Sarapion and Silvanus
2     … greeting. I have sent to you
3     [through the] undertaker the body of
4     [our brother] Phibion, and I paid
5     him the wages for the conveyance of the body, being three hundred and forty drachmas
6     forty, of the old
7     coinage; and I am very much astonished
8     [that] without reason you departed without taking up
9     [the b]ody of your brother, but
10    having collected whatever he had, and thus
11    you departed. And from this I learned
12    that you did not go up for the sake of the dead man
13    but for the sake of his belongings.
14    See to it, therefore, to prepare what was spent. The expenses are:
15    price of drugs, 60 old drachmas
16    price of wine on the first
17    day, 2 choes, 32 old drachmas
18    for expenditure on bread and side-dishes, 16 drachmas
19    to the undertaker for the desert (cemetery),
20    after the recorded
21    wage, a chous of wine, 20 drachmas
22    of oil, 1/2 chous, 12 drachmas
23    of barley, 1 artaba, 20 drachmas
24    price of a linen cloth, 20 drachmas
25    and the wage as set forth, 340 drachmas
26    total for the
27    whole expenditure, of the old
28    coinage, drachmas
29    five hundred and twenty,
30    total 520 drachmas.
31    So you will do everything to assist the one
32    who is going to bring the body,
33    with bread and a little wine and oil
34    and whatever is possible for you, so that he may bear witness to me. But do not do anything
35    … of the … in silver, because I have not … (be able)
36    … ed … p … and … ta … Pachon 28
37    … pi … you e … I pray for your good health.
38    [To Sar]api[on] and
39    [Si]lvanus, brothers
40    of Phibion.
41    Melas, decurion.
