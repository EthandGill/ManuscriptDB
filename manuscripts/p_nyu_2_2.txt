1     24th, through the two-drachma (tax) …
2     The Aureliai Stratonike also called Sosipatra and Apollonia who
3     is also Dieus, both (daughters) of Saras, who served as gymnasiarch,
4     councillor of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians
5     and however she is styled, to Aurelius Theon
6     son of Didymos, exegetes and councillor, and to the one
7     with him, public bankers of the Oxyrhynchite, greeting.
8     We have paid over to you on the present
9     day, for the reckoning of Thoth,
10    the seventh, through the two-drachma and four-drachma (tax) of the vineyards,
11    of the receipts of the past 1st year; and it is as follows:
12    of the eastern toparchy, 2 drachmas,
13    of the western toparchy, 17 drachmas,
14    of the middle toparchy, district of Alexandros,
15    26 drachmas;
16    they make of the assessment 45 drachmas.
