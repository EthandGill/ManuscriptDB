[TRANSLATION]
1     [Eirenius to Apolinarius] [to the sweetest] [brother], [many] [greetings].
2     And through [all] I pray you to be well.
3     And [I] myself am well. I want you to know that to the land
4     I came on the 6th of Epeiph
5     of the month and we unloaded on the
6     18th of the same month. I went up
7     indeed to Rome on the 25th of the same month and the place received us as god willed,
8     and each day [we await] two-and-a-half hours so that until
9     today no one of those with grain has been released.
10    I greet your wife
11    much and Seren and all those who love you by name.
12    Farewell. Mesore the 9th.
13    To Apolinarius from Eirenius the brother.
