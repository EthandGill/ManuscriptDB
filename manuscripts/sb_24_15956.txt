[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Johannes, notary of the register of Antaeus, sixth indiction, through me Psat[es]
2     [of the] village-chief of the village of Aphrod[it]e D… nn… I received
3     from you in account of wheat in silver-conversions and of the wine-[keeper]…
4     by decree of the same sixth indiction wheat artabae one hundred seventy[…]
5     in gold and [in] … of pounds eight thousand seven-[hundred]
6     and for your security I have made the receipt as is aforesaid…
7     of artabae … [of] coin [by] standard weight [by] public scales. Flavius J[ohannes through me]
8     Psat[es], father of him, the receipt, that is the record, is proper to me, as aforesaid.
