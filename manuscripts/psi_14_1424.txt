[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Theonas and Dionysodorus, both ship-captains of the harbor of Memphis.
2     Since a protest having been lodged in the same Memphis
3     before the house of the praepositus of the soldiers here
4     [soldiers] E … deos on account of the name of …
5     expenses. On account of this we delivered this
6     document to the overseer Taurinus and … of this Paul
7     … for safety as aforesaid. Aurelius Theonas
8     and Dionysodorus, ship-captains of Memphis, we delivered this document and we witness
9     to all [those] written [above] as aforesaid.
10    [It] was written through me Marcelinus, tribune, assistant
11    [of] the document of ship-captains.
