[TRANSLATION]
1     Senpikas Kamhetios has written off for surveying of the Upper toparchy, year 7
2     drachmas seven, obol three; [makes] drachmas seven, obol three, and in the name of the sons of Pamonthus Psenchonsios
3     through Pamonthus Psenchonsios for surveying of the Upper toparchy
4     year 7, drachmas seven, obol three; [makes] drachmas seven, obol three, and further recorded. Year 7 of Domitian
5     Caesar our lord, Mesore 30, 28.
6     Dekmos has noted [this].
