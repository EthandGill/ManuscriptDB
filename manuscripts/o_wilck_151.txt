[TRANSLATION]
1   Antiochos Cassianus and those with him,
2   overseers of the Sacred Gate of Syene, through Phanophis the assistant.
3   Paid: Bienchis son of Zmethis, mother Sen…, for the poll-tax
4   of the 14th year of Hadrian the lord: seventeen drachmas (and) a half-obol, (and) ½ obol (keepers' fee)
5   — total 17 dr. 1 ob.; and for the assessment of the head-tax of those who had withdrawn, of the
6   13th year of Hadrian the lord, one drachma (and) a half-obol — total 1 dr. ½ ob. Year 14
7   of Hadrian the lord.
