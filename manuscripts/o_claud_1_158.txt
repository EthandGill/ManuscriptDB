[TRANSLATION]
1     Domitius Capito to Ballion the
2     brother, greetings.
3     Valentinus the lord of mine
4     and brother I have asked
5     that he may have you in hand for what you wish
6     and you to him become obedient
7     for what he wishes. I wrote to you through
8     Octavius another letter, greeting you
9     and you will send the five staters to the eranarch. Farewell.
