r.3    καὶ τὰ [ἐκ]φ[ό]ρ[ια ἀποδώ]σω τοῖς περὶ τὸν Οὐαρ[έ]λιον κατὰ τὸ ἥμισυ [μ]έ[ρ]ος καὶ τοῖς περὶ τὴν

[TRANSLATION]
1     arou[ras, agricultural work all that is befitting]
2     from [my own at the ap]propriate times, doing no damage
3     and the [revenues I will] give to those [around] Ouar[elius] according to the half share and to those [around]
4     Herakleia according to the remaining half part in the month Pauni
5     at the threshing floor of [the] Cercesouchoi [with] the four-quart measure of the village treasury, [with] the measurement
6     being made through [some] of yours, at my [charge] those yearly carriage fees, delivery service and beast-labor, [and] of the
7     transport of the revenues to you. And after the time I will hand over the arouras from harvest of wheat, if it should appear [I should] lease [them].
8     Pisaeis, as if of 32 years, scar on small finger of left hand.
9     Year 31 of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus
10    Caesar our lord, Hathur 14.
