[TRANSLATION]
1     Postumus, collector of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis (son of) Amenothes.
3     I have received for watchtower (skopelos) and guard-duty and other (taxes) of year 7, base
4     drachmas four, [3] obols, total base drachmas 4, 3 obols.
5     Year 7 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
6     Pharmouthi 25. I, Postumus, through Karo( ) the assistant, have signed.
