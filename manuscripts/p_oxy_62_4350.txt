1     In the reign of the most divine and most pious
2     our master,
3     greatest benefactor Flavius Justinus, the eternal
4     Augustus and Emperor, in the 11th year, in the consulship of their own
5     serenity for the 2nd time, and of Flavius Tiberius also called New
6     Constantine, our most fortunate
7     Caesar, Epeiph
8     28, in the 9th indiction.
9     To Flavius Apion, the all-praiseworthy and
10    most eminent, former ordinary consul
11    and patrician, landholder
12    also here in the city of Nea Justinou, through Menas
13    his servant, who is
14    interrogating and securing for his own
15    master, the same all-praiseworthy man, the action and
16    liability, we, the Aurelii:
17    Anoup, steward, son
18    of Jeremias, and Pamouthios son of Anoup and Pamouthios
19    another,
20    secretary, son of Praous, all originating
21    from the hamlet of Sasou Kato, belonging to your
22    eminence,
23    registered cultivators of it, greeting.
24    We acknowledge, through this present written surety of ours,
25    without deceit
26    and without fear and force and
27    trickery an[d] compulsion, that we are ready to collect all the
28    public dues of the tenth
29    indiction,
30    at our own risk and that of the property of each of us,
31    and of the property, and
32    to pay them in to the
33    glorious house, and to leave nothing at all of them
34    outstanding. And if it should happen
35    that any remain
36    of the said public dues of the tenth indiction
37    uncollected,
38    we acknowledge,
39    being liable for one another, to give these to
40    your eminence, without
41    any delay,
42    at the risk of [all our] possessions.
43    Binding is the pre[sent]
44    acknowledgment, written singly, [and]
45    on being questioned we [ac]knowledged.
46    We, the Aurelii, Ano[up so]n of Elias an[d
47    Pa]mouthis s[on of] Anoup [and
48    P]amouth[is] son of Praous,
49    the aforewritten, this present acknowledgment concerning the
50    public dues satisfies us
51    as aforesaid. I, the same Pamouthis, wrote with my own
52    hand for my own
53    name
54    and for the [othe]rs who are illiter[ate].
55    Through me, Menas,
56    it was completed.
57    Acknowledgment
58    made by
59    those from the haml[et] of Sasou Kato.
