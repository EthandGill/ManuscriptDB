[TRANSLATION]
1     Greeting to Zenon from Massichos. I encountered you saying that I will [go] to Apollonius
2     concerning the money which Philiskos exacted from me, and you [said] to me that also [Dio]timos
3     will listen to you. If then it seems good to you,
4     Diotimos being here,
5     listen to me and [Philiskos]
6     that he exacted from me [unjustly]
7     266 drachmas and four obols. [You would do] well assisting [me,]
8     so that this [does] not happen [henceforth]; ... [not] even would I have been here, but I [would have] gone to my own [property] ...
9     Farewell.
