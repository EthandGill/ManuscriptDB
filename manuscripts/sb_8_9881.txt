[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Horion [and] as he is designated
2     to Aurelia Isidora a woman
3     Paritus, greetings.
4     the [remaining] in you
5     from the preceding
6     of year wheat, seven artabas
7     I received, and the portion falling to you
8     the share of the according-to-[you] share of the past [year], three artabas,
9     again I received
10    of year 9 and 7 being [brought forward], 4
