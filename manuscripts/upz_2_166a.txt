1     [Year] 29, Phamenoth 9, [it] has been deposited at the bank in Hermouthis, of which [is] Dionysios, of the twentieth circulation according to [the agreement] of Asklepiades
2     and Kratos [concerning] the purchase [agreement], under which Ptolemaios the copyist writes,
3     [Ha]sus [son] of Horos of [the] purchase of bare land, [of] cubits 2½ being from [the] south [of] Zeus' city the Great, whose neighbors are made clear

6     Amenothes, of bronze, [of] talent 1 [and] ... [the] change [of] five hundred, [it] becomes [500].
