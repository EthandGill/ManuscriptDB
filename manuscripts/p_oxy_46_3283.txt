1     93 (col. number).
2     Fro[m] Theon son of Theon son of Theon, his mother being
3     [Diog]enous (daughter) of Dionysios, from Oxyrhynchus c[i]t[y].
4     [In accord]ance with the orders concerning the scrutiny of those coming of age into the gymnasial class, whether they are of this descent, there was enrolled in the quarter of Cavalry Camp my son
5     [P]ausiris, his mother being Thaesis (daughter) of Didymos, who became thirteen years old in the
6     past 11th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord. Wherefore, having come forward for his scrutiny, I declare, in accordance with the (scrutiny)
7     held in the 5th year of the deified Vespasian under Sutorius Sosibi[us]
8     who had been strategos, and Nikandros who had been royal secretary, and the others to whom the [scrutiny] pertains,
9     that my grandfather Theon son of Ammonios had been scrutinized in the quarter
10    [Pa]mmenes' Garden, in accordance with the proofs which he brought,
11    th[at] his grandfather Theon son of Ammonios is in the register of the 34th year
12    of the deified Caesar, and that my father Theon, having come of age, had been scrutinized in the 11th year of Domitian on the aforesaid
