# HGV-EN: Year twenty of the Aurelii, Antoninus and Commodus, the lords Augusti, Pachon 11. Paid to Sotas and his associates, collectors of money taxes of the village of Soknopaiou Nesos (by) Onnophris, son of Stotoetis, for dike taxes of the past 19th year, seven dirty drachmas 4 obols 2 chalkoi, = 7 dirty drachmas 4 obols 2 chalkoi.
