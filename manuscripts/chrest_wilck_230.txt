[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Clodius Culcianus the most eminent prefect of Egypt
2     from … and … gathos, both councillors of the city of the Arsinoites. In the census which
3     was carried out by Septimius Sabinus the censitor, much mischief has been committed against the treasury,
4     … of the decemprimi, not by the censitor but by certain malefactors —
5     … a boundary-surveyor, having authority, with everything not being consistent
6     … in the registrations of the arouras which he himself farms and harvests, a certain Horion
7     … and a certain Souchammon, himself also having land there, falsely entered, not
8     … his brother Serenos and Manon, and likewise also Sara… and Taesis and Panetbeous, and registered against them the land belonging to him,
9     … they have been able both to commit crime and to dare it, having the jurors … as accomplices in the things done by them, so that it has not been possible
10    … for those collecting the imperial taxes to find the registered properties
11    … to be compelled to pay in the dues on their behalf. Having therefore the means to show to the … in that the names have falsely entered this land, for this reason
12    … we ask you to command that, through whomever you may approve, those men be compelled to pay the dues on behalf of the
13    land … you may be able … all hatred of wickedness
