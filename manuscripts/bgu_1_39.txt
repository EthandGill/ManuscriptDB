[TRANSLATION]
1    To Longinia Sempronia, through (her) man-
2    ager, the one also called Ninnaros, also called
3    Ptolemaios, (son) of Isidoros,
4    from Pnepheros son of Petaus, from
5    the village of Karanis. I wish to
6    lease, for three years (and) three
7    sowings, from the present 26th year,
8    the (land) belonging to you near the village
9    of Bakchias, of a catoecic allotment,
10   in one parcel, 5 arouras, in the place called Ke-
11   tekem, at a rent per
12   year, for all the arouras, of wheat,
13   twenty-two and a half artabas — total 22½ art. of wheat,
14   by the six-choinix measure of Sarapous son of
15   Tabei; the seed being
16   (charged) to Pnepheros, and all the pub-
17   lic taxes (charged) to Longi-
18   nia. And all the farming works
19   I shall carry out at the proper season,
20   and after the term I shall give back
21   the allotment (cleared) of rushes and reed,
22   if it seems good to grant the lease
23   on the aforesaid terms. I, Pnepheros
24   son of Petaus, have taken the lease as
25   stated above. I, Neilos also called Achillas,
26   wrote on his behalf, as he is illiterate.
27   Year 26 of Marcus Aurelius
28   Commodus Antoninus Caesar
29   the lord. Mesore 26.
