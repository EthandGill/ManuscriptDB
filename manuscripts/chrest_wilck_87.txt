[TRANSLATION]
1     To Archias, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2     from Pakysis son of Pakysis son of Herieus, his mother being
3     …sis, from the village of Soknopaiu Nesos. With regard to the matter
4     referred for investigation by the former acting-strategos Protarchos —
5     … us having been declared to have sacrificed calves,
6     … inquiry was made … the [calf-]sealers
7     were accustomed, so that those producing receipts
8     or another proof might bring it, according to which the calves were inspected by the calf-sealers …
9     … in the 5th year of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
10    in the month then Choiak, one calf, I report,
11    swearing by the fortune of Imperator Caesar Trajan
12    Hadrianus Augustus, that I sacrificed the said
13    calf at the indicated time in the aforesaid
14    village, and that it was inspected by Marreis
15    son of Apynchis, then being calf-sealer,
16    as being clean according to custom, and that it was sealed; concerning which no documents were issued to me
17    because at that time it was not the custom; for from the 7th year
18    of Hadrian the lord, Julius Pardalas, who was in charge of the private account, on the basis of a report submitted
19    to him by Pekmeis son of Marreis and the calf-sealers with him,
20    they having requested, after the customary … receipt …
21    … from the 7th year …
22    … ordered that documents
23    be issued, or that I be liable to the oath.
24    Year … [of Imperator Caesar Trajan] Hadrian
25    [Augustus] …
