[TRANSLATION]
1     Akytes Haronnophius through Theon, friend of Theon, from
2     Oxyrhynchus city to the grain-measurers of the eastern toparchy of Ophes places, greetings.
3     Distribute from what you have of mine as [a land-]plot [for] wheat of the production year 20 of Antoninus
4     Caesar, our lord, to the heirs of Theon also called
5     Gaius Peteurios through Akytes, [of] Akytes farmer, [for] [the] plot:
6     artabas seventy-two, and to Taonnophre[is]
7     also called Chaeremonidis of Dionysius through the same Akytes farmer
8     [for] [the] plot artabas seventy-four; total 74 artabas; grand total for the same
9     artabas one hundred forty-six. Year 21
10    of Antoninus Caesar, our lord, Phaophi 17.
