[TRANSLATION]
1     Theoktistos to Apo[llonios] his dearest, greeting.
2     Now at last, having declined everything,
3     go up to Chairemon; for, by the
4     gods, [I] have henceforth nothing to write to you
5     in reply to the letters he writes me; the (contents) of his letters bring me exceedingly into [grief] and distress.
6     So from every quarter
7     take from him [your] money,
8     so that he may not have it for spending, and to repay …
9     … to him to wage war (against me?) and
10    that I may not be so very worn out. Yes, and he has written you the monthly accounts. For, by the gods,
11    … the public affairs press me …
12    … on account of this besides
13    … to go down, so that I may urge you on,
14    for there will be no delivery; therefore he says that you should go up to him … their accounts
15    … I know … not being worthy … toward the accounts …
16    therefore, so that before the third (of the month) you may sail down concerning the pitch,
17    let it be your care to go to Horion, so that you may send me to Sebennytos
18    the ten talents of pitch
19    as soon as … of the jar
20    and if not … to …
21    to the people the … to lose; but if you do [not] have … the ten
22    talents for pitch into …
23    to send me … (six) talents I sent
24    so that I may altogether have a jar to take
25    Saras Horion told me in the
26    city that I have to give you talents …, and I wrote also to
27    Horion a letter on account of
28    this, since it presses me. I detained Sabinos until
29    I go down. Do not
30    therefore do otherwise.
31    Farewell.
32    Pauni 21.
33    So before you go off to Chairemon,
34    come up to me, so that I may take my leave of you.
35    To Ap[ollonios] (my) dearest.
