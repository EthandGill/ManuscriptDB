[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord in all things most wonderful
2     patron Herodes, son of Hera.
3     I learned that those from the village, having come
4     to your wonderfulness, blamed me,
5     as the scale being heavier, by which I receive
6     the gold of the demand and of these keraia
7     being lighter. And having wished to go up
8     and to inform your magnificence, I was not permitted [to do so without] her order; I write therefore,
9     thus, that neither a scale did I take with me from
10    the city, nor [anything else], but [the] scale I received from Dionysius, the secretary of the village,
11    in which [he] had held previously the demand, and
12    upon it I stood and I received the gold. If therefore
13    [it] seems to your virtue, since without [his] will
14    nothing am I able to do, [I ask you] to take [for me] a record
15    from each official of the gold taking [his] share
16    to his portion, namely to make Dionysius the secretary again receive [it]. And [he ordered me] to provide [that which] stands before your virtue. [You] will order me
17    to be written; I [will] toward [the things ordered] me
18    follow through, [master].
19    to be well [to] you
20    I pray for many
21    times, [master].
22    [at] hour 10, Tybi.
23    To my lord in all things most wonderful and patron Herodes, Dionysius.
