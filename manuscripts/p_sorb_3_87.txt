[TRANSLATION]
1     Aristarchus to Chrysippus, greetings. When …
2     the grain-buyer from Nicon not meeting [it], but it happens, fifty beasts of burden
3     standing there and the grain being clean on the threshing floors, [they] do not receive [it]. You will do well therefore
4     to send for Nicon and having ordered him to send the attendant
5     and so that this might not happen to me in the future. Farewell. Year 4, Pharmuthi 17.
6     To Chrysippus.
