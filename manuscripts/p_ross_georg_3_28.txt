[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our master Constantius, the eternal
2     Augustus, [for the …th time], Mecheir 30. This is a copy:
3     There agreed with one another the Aurelii: Asep son of Hol, his mother being
4     Termouthis, from the village of Taurinos of the Arsinoite nome,
5     and Aron son of Kounias, from the hamlet of Psetra of the same nome,
6     on condition that Aurelius Asep join with the
7     daughter of Aron, Apia, in the community of marriage. But Aron
8     out of good faith(?) received Asep, being destitute,
9     from the present first indiction, and Aron provided
10    to his own daughter, on account of the wedding-gift, three dalmatic-cloaks and one mantle. From here, then, let the married pair live together; moreover also a face-cloth and
11    … and an earring; and there being no power
12    for [Ase]p to abandon Sophia after this,
13    the one transgressing after this shall give to the one abiding (by the agreement)
14    for a penalty and turning-away and outrage, of gold
15    ten solidi; and moreover her father Aron shall not
16    abandon (him), since it has thus been agreed and
17    consented to; and, having been questioned, they acknowledged.
18    I, Aurelius Asep, the aforesaid, have agreed
19    to all the things written, as is set forth.
20    I, Aurelius Aron son of Kounias, have agreed to all
21    the things written, as is set forth. Aurelius
22    Ammonios, the priest, wrote on their behalf, they being present, being illiterate.
