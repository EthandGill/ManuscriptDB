[TRANSLATION]
1     Aphrodito.
2     [Concerning] goods and the expense of Klysma, indiction 8. (Arabic:) to the companion of Ishquh concerning the hastening of the supplies of al-Qulzum (Klysma).
3     In the name of God, Korra son of Szerich, governor,
4     to Basileios administrator of the village of Aphrodito. We had
5     assigned, through your administration, various kinds (of goods)
6     for the matter of the fitting-out and equipping of the ships of Klysma,
7     and moreover the expense of the sailors of the ships that are in the same
8     Klysma, sending (orders) to you, and the warrants for these
9     many days ago, having written these in brief
10    to dispatch before the going-down of the waters of the Trajan (canal) should occur;
11    and up to now you have not sent anything of these worth mentioning.
12    Therefore, on receiving the present letter, immediately and at that very
13    hour, send, if there is anything, through your administration,
14    from these, falling short in nothing at all, nor indeed needing
15    further letters from us about this, if indeed you understand
16    and have sense. For you shall know that [if] you fall short
17    in anything whatsoever of these same goods and expenses, and
18    the going-down of the waters occurs, you are going to convey these by road
19    as far as the same Klysma, supplying the freight-charge
20    for them from your own substance. Written in the month Tybi 8, indiction 8.
21    Korra son of Szerich, governor, to Basileios administrator of the village of Aph[rodito].
22    In the month Mecheir 15, indiction 8, brought through Saeid, concerning the dispatch of the distributions of Klysma.
