1     Both [indeed] being in danger through him to abandon
2     the household, being in need to the treasury we beseech peace, existing of the deepest and of a[ll] men
3     having conducted piously in the most fortunate [matters] of the lords
4     of us Emperors in time, if to you by fortune it should seem good, to hear us toward him, so that upon the aforesaid manifest us
5     doing [these things] we obtain the just [things] from you.
6     … toward the of the little-book
7     presentation we have sent. Sogenes [wrote on] behalf of them.

11    [in the] treas[ury]. Of year 4 of Antoninus and Verus of the lords.
