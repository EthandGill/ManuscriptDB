[TRANSLATION]
1     Wheat, 28 1/4 artabas.
2     Aelius Aphrodisios, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
3     to the grain-collectors of the eastern toparchy of the district of Phoboou, greeting.
4     Measure out from the finest sample,
5     with the joint authorization of Serе[nus also called]
6     Sarapion the royal sec[retary, for]
7     seed-lo[a]ns for the [present]
8     12th year of Antoninus Caes[ar the lord],
9     for the sowing from the produce of t[he past 11th year],
10    to Kleochares son of Chairemon al[s]o called Antonius [son of] Kleochares, his mother … rio …
11    aged about 70, with a scar on the right forearm, and to another, Kleochare[s] son of Chairemon also called Antonius,
12    [his mother] … aged 36 …,
13    of whe[a]t arta[bas] … [se]ven,
14    making in all of pu[re wheat],
15    unadulterated, free of clods, free of barley, sifted,
16    by the pu[b]lic half-artaba measure, 28 1/4 artabas, on
17    condition that they put it into the ground soundly, the customary officials supervising,
18    having given security at your own risk, on condition that they repay them from the new (crop) the equal amounts with the additions together with the
19    pub[li]c (dues) of the land, from whom
20    receive the proper double receipt,
21    of which give over the one to me. Year 12
22    of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius
23    Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius,
24    Tyb[i] 1. I, Aelius Aphrodeisios,
25    thr[oug]h Antoninus my assistant, measure out as is stated above
26    the 28 1/4 artabas of wheat,
27    making 28 1/4 artabas of wheat.
28    I, Serenus also called Sarapion, measure out as is stated above
29    the twenty-eight
30    and a quarter artabas of wheat, making 28 1/4 artabas.
