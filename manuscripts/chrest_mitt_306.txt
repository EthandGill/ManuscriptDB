[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eighteenth year of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus
2     Pius, Phamenoth 25, in Soknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite
3     nome. Stotoetis son of Horos son of Panephremmis, from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, priest of the fourth tribe of Soknopaios the great great god, aged about forty-three, with a scar on the second finger
4     of the right hand, acknowledges that he concedes, after his own death, to the children born to him from his wife who lives with him,
5     Thases daughter of Satabous, namely the two minor sons Horos and Pabous: to Horos the acknowledging party concedes, after his own death, a two-thirds share of whatever property he may leave, and of buildings of every kind
6     and of movable goods and gear and all household furnishings; to the same Horos the acknowledging party concedes, after his own
7     death, a two-thirds share of what his aforesaid wife Thases lent, in the time of their cohabitation,
8     to Horos son of Satabous, in the past seventeenth year of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
9     in the month Mecheir, on the new moon, through the same record-office, of two thousand five hundred drachmas of silver,
10    on the security of an allotment of eight arouras; and to Pabous the same acknowledging party concedes, after his own death, the remaining third share of whatever property he may leave, and of buildings of every kind
11    and of movable goods and gear and all household furnishings; the same acknowledging party concedes, after his own
12    death, to his aforesaid wife Thases, for as long a time as she remains unmarried, each month half and a tenth of an artaba of wheat by the four-choinix dromos measure and two cotylae of oil, and yearly, on account of clothing, twenty drachmas of silver; the same acknowledging party appoints, after his
13    own death, as guardian for his minor children Horos and Pabous and to act as their guardian, until they reach the lawful age, his genuine friend Pabous son of Satabous son of Harpagathes, from the aforesaid village, priest of the same god; and the guardian shall provide to the
14    minor children Horos and Pabous the fitting nourishment and the oil and the clothing and the other things,
15    as many as are fitting, Pabous rendering to the minors, whenever they reach the lawful age, the
16    account about everything, with full faith concerning all matters being upon Pabous; and for as long a time as
17    the acknowledging party survives, he shall have full authority over all his own property to sell, to mortgage,
18    to cede to others; the witnesses being present at the (signing) at the magistracy and sealing together
19    this concession: Horos son of Panephremmis son of Horos, aged about twenty-eight, with a scar … o …
20    on the left side; and Stotoetis the elder, son of Stotoetis son of Stotoetis, aged about … p …
21    on the left side; and Tesenouphis son of Tesenouphis son of Tesenouphis, aged about thirty … ,
22    on the left side; and Stotoetis the elder, son of Stotoetis son of Stotoetis, aged about seventy … , on the left side; and Stotoetis son of Tesenouphis son of Tesenouphis, aged about
23    sixty-six, with a scar … ; and Herakleides …
24    to both (wives) who lived with (him). Subscriber for the acknowledging party: Harpagathes son of Pakysis son of Panephremmis, aged about 35, with a scar on the left hand.
25    I, Stotoetis son of Horos, acknowledge that I have conceded, after my death,
26    to my children Horos and Pabous, to Horos a two-thirds share of all the
27    aforesaid property, and to Pabous the remaining third share of the same,
28    and to my wife Thases, for as long a time as she remains unmarried, each month
29    half and a tenth of an artaba of wheat and two cotylae of oil, and yearly
30    on account of clothing twenty drachmas of silver, and I will seal it, as is set forth above.
31    I, Harpagathes son of Pakysis, have written on his behalf as he does not know letters, being illiterate, having deposited (it) on the …
32    Pabous … I, Horos son of Panephremmis, witness together and seal together. I, Stotoetis the elder son of Stotoetis, witness together and seal together. I, Tesenouphis
33    son of Tesenouphis, witness together and seal together. I, Stotoetis the elder son of Stotoetis, witness together and seal together.
34    I witness together and seal together. I, Stotoetis son of Tesenouphis, witness together and seal together. I, Herakleides … son of …, witness together and seal together.
