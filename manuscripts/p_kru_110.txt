[TRANSLATION]
1     With God. I, Philotheos, the son of the late
2     Psmo, from the village of Pankame in the nome of the city of Ermont, write, donating my gold (?) into the place of Apa
3     Phoibammon the martyr of Christ, through
4     the clergy: Apa Sourous the superior of the place and Matthaios his brother, who did not compel me to do this, but [in]
5     my own free will I have done this, for
6     the salvation of my soul, it being upon the portion of the land which is called the [l]and of my house on the east, that of the king,
7     [s]o that its boundary may be straightened, together with his brother Isaac,
8     and that which comes up under my Askour as well,
9     it being likewise — he shall again be its master —
10    and that no man at all is mine,
11    neither [a son] nor [a daughter,] generation upon generation, neither child nor daughter, nor a townsman, who shall come
12    out against it forever; whoever shall dare
13    to come out against it, the h[oly] saint Apa Phoibammon shall take vengeance on him, and he shall become a stranger to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit in this age and the one to come,
14    and he shall not see the kingdom of God
15    opened, and he shall not be worthy to enter into it, but he shall be
16    in the pit of the abyss forever.
17    I, Philotheos, the son of Psmou, agree to this written deed,
18    according to the manner in which it is written. I, Isaac, his brother,
19    have written this written deed with my own hand, according to
20    the request of my brother Philotheos, and I bear witness, and I agree to this document, according to
21    the manner in which it is written, and they questioned me, and I agreed.
