[TRANSLATION]
1     … as in the (list) of the customary dues
2     … to Onnophris, Achoapis, son of Psenephthas,
3     … nebnēis, and prophet of the Benefactor Gods (Theoi Euergetai)
4     … [with] pressing necessities the matters concerning the
5     … [Well then, you will do] well to give orders to hand over to the
6     … [and] to make out receipts (symbola) as is fitting. And in order that
7     … how(?) they may be set in order, having taken forethought
8     … And [we have appen]ded also the copy of the instruction delivered by the king
9     [and the queens] concerning the things due
10    [to the temples to be received], so that, having attended with the greatest diligence, you may omit nothing of care.
11    [King Ptolemy and Que]en Cleopatra the sister, and Queen
12    [Cleopatra the wife, to the strate]goi and the phrourarchs and the
13    [epistatai of the phylakitai an]d the archiphylakitai and the epimeletai
14    [and the oikonomoi and the royal sc]ribes and the others who
15    [transact the royal business], greeting. The priests have written to us,
16    [ … ] … and of the Brother Gods (Theoi Adelphoi) and
17    [of the Benefactor Gods and of the Phi]lopator Gods and of the Manifest Gods (Theoi Epiphaneis)
18    [and of the God Eupator and of the Phi]lometor Gods and of the Benefactor Gods,
19    [concerning the sacred land … ] together with that consecrated by the cleruchs, [and of the (proceeds) from] perquisites and prophetships and
20    [scribeships and] all [liturgies] of the produce bought for the temple and … from properties, and of the things (levied) according to decrees,
21    [on behalf of … of wh]ich also, of the several associations by division,
22    and of the temple-slaves, from trading and workshops and wages
23    that are assessed, and of the sums collected in Alexandria
24    and in the countryside for treasuries and bowls and cups by
25    the men and women and from those in the so-called shrines of Aphrodite, and in general of the receipts that fall
26    into … is registered as revenue — (they wrote) that some, renting
27    land and other things for a longer period, and some even using force, without agreements, do not pay the appropriate taxes nor render in full the proceeds
28    of the [perquisites] and prophetships and scribeships;
29    and that others withhold (a portion) from the dues and
30    collected sums, and, being appointed without their
31    consent, receive rather the shrines of Aphrodite for the sake of
32    collecting the things due to the goddess; and that others attempt
33    to entangle themselves in the revenues and to lay hands (on them) [and to] administer contrary to custom. Just as, therefore, we also
34    previously ordered concerning the things due to be received by the
35    temples, so long as the specified revenues of the goddess
36    remain … undisturbed, entrust to no one in any
37    way to do any of the aforesaid things, nor to compel by force those sent out by the priests to convey the specified goods; but those who disobey, compel them in orderly fashion
38    to render each thing, so that, receiving all in full,
39    they may be able unhindered to perform the customary rites for the
40    gods on behalf of us and our children.
41    Farewell. [Year] 31, Panemos 1…
