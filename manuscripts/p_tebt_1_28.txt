[TRANSLATION]
1     Dioskourides and Amenneus to Ptolemaios, greeting.
2     As for the details of the hindrances that have attended our
3     occupation, owing to the harassment caused by the village-scribes in the [no]me, and in what manner already in the month Mesore of the … [year],
4     when we entered the nome to the localities, having been appointed
5     to the service, choosing to inspect the matters reported from the nome, the arrears-account and the other things had collapsed, these men not lending a hand, we happen also, through the appended
6     letter, to have been enrolled together with Eirenaios. And at present, through the reports,
7     the same course being followed, and at the same time, the sowing …
8     … so that the several matters should not be brought under scrutiny,
9     … if we should enter the villages …
10    … these … thoroughly … lightly(?) …
11    and the voyage down to the city having been made as is indicated, we ask you, having looked into what has been shown, and
12    if it be also possible, from the inspections that are to take place, to make a gain for the royal treasury,
13    to give orders that provision be made, so that those who are named
14    may be secured, so that the rest too, having foreseen (it), receiving the
15    scrutiny of wrongdoing that will be made by you … being roused up by these very men,
16    may apply a readier diligence in the matters concerning the
17    inspections, and that [nothing of what is useful for] the royal treasury …
18    be omitted.
19    Farewell. [Year] …
