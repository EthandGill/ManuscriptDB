1     … .ai … n …
2     … and since at that time [our (claim)] also [passed away from us]
3     [well and necessarily it (was needful) to] a[d]d to the deed this very thing, [namely] [unless]
4     … died … nor anything else concerning [the] slaves did their master determine,
5     the (slaves)
6     who were guarded and
7     by him kept shut up
8     when he was about to die; but now, having considered this by [my]self, I have taken thought
9     and have grasped the er[ror] that occurred; [for I,] as though stru[ck dumb] a[t the unex]pe[ct]edness of the matter, and scarcely seeming to be in my own (mind), [was handing over the slaves], seeing those who wished thus carrying off and bearing away our property as in (a case) of confiscation of men's (goods) or some such other thing [they had suffer]ed; for this reason now I make safeguard, requesting
10    and beseeching the precision of your (court), O master, in accordance with the laws, if indeed it is necessary for me to bear the
11    deception concerning the slaves, that at least a correction of the error that occurred
12    be made upon the records and this be added: if there be discovered afterwards
13    a testament made by that man, that this man too be by necessity made subject to the things that shall be shown through the testaments, and that there be no prejudgment, if this appears to have been left out in (the matter) which seems not to have been done according to (proper) manner … and (that) it is necessary
14    for him to furnish a surety, a man in this city or in the Thebaid having his hearth (home),
15    so that, if anything newer afterwards concerning these slaves be shown as from
16    the intention of their master, that man may become a surety, answerable for what shall be shown
17    and shall be, this man not being present, b[u]t residing in other lands. And since
18    these slaves were guarded [wi]th the office of the catholicus (financial minister) unt[il] my
19    arrival, and Eugeneios both arres[ted] (them) and requested them to be under guard, an[d toward]s
20    me Eugeneios has nothing further to say in opposition, I request, master, that these be handed over to me and
21    [fro]m this guard be released, for now the laws call me to the
22    lordship of them, being the brother of that Harp[o]kration, [of the same father and]
23    of the same mother, and as the things written in the divine [re]s[c]rip[t] given to Eugeneios contain,
24    keeping the consequence and favour of the laws.
