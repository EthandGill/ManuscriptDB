[TRANSLATION]
1     Norbanus Orestes to Hermias son of Sabourion, greetings. I acknowledge [that I will] not accuse you nor [will] accuse you
2     concerning no matter
3     simply regarding written [and] unwritten [matters] over the former
4     times until the present day, nor [shall] you [accuse] me. Year nine of the Emperor
5     Caesar Trajan Hadrian
6     Augustus, Hathyr, new moon.
