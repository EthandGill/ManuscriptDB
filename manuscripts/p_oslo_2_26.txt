[TRANSLATION]
1     x arouras 5, of wheat 3.
2     To Onnophris, topogrammateus of the
3     toparchy toward the south-west,
4     from Leon son of Herakleides,
5     of those from the city of Oxyrhynchus,
6     of the public farmers. Of the royal lands farmed by me
7     around the Ibion of Antilochos, opposite Pelle, in the present 26th year of Caesar, sown by me at the proper season with the greatest expense and hardship, these
8     having become entirely worm-eaten — which are of those from Dionysios
9     the exegetes — sown with lentil
10    five arouras, and with wheat three arouras,
11    the neighbors of the first
12    plot, which is of the five arouras sown with lentil,
13    on the part from the south-west being royal land, on the north
14    fallow land, on the south the land of Theon son of Sarapion, on the west that of Vibius
15    the centurion, [on the east] that of Berenike
16    daughter of Herak[leides; and of the plot of the]
17    three arouras, on the part from the
18    south, on the north …
19    of Artemidoros, on the south …
20    and of Teos, on the west that of Theon
21    aforewritten, on the east that of the same Berenike,
22    I submit this memorandum
23    and request, if it seem good, that [the aforesaid] be registered
24    so that I may be [protected].
25    I, Leon son of Herakleides, have submitted the foregoing memorandum
26    and I swear by Caesar Imperator,
27    son of God, Zeus the Liberator, Augustus,
28    that the foregoing things are [true] and …
