[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 26th year, Mesore
2     28. There sold,
3     Peteesis
4     son of Pates,
5     to Pelaias son of Eunous,
6     the (property) belonging to him
7     in the part toward
8     the east
9     of Pathyris,
10    a house built
11    and roofed and fitted with doors,
12    of which the neighbouring boundaries
13    have been declared
14    through the deed of sale.
15    In the reign of Ptolemy who is called Alexander and Cleopatra his sister, the Philometor
16    Soter gods, in the twenty-sixth year, under the priests and priestesses and kanephoros those being
17    and existing, in the month Mesore 28, in Pathyris, under Hermias, agoranomos of the upper toparchy of the Pathyrites.
18    Peteesis son of Pates sold, a Persian, about 40 years old, of middle height, honey-skinned, with straight hair, long-faced,
19    straight-nosed, with a scar under the left eyebrow, the house belonging to him, built and roofed and fitted with doors, which is in the eastern part of Pathyris
20    in the so-called (district) of the spring, of which the neighbours are: to the south the house of Pelaias the buyer, to the north the house
21    of Taenoutis daughter of Psenpoeris, which Totoes son of Panechates holds, to the east and west
22    the royal street, or whoever the neighbours may be on all sides. Pelaias son of Eunous bought it, for 1 talent of copper.
23    The vendor's agent and guarantor of all things pertaining to this sale is Peteesis
24    the vendor, whom Pelaias the buyer accepted.
25    I, Hermias, have transacted the deed.
