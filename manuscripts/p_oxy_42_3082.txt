[TRANSLATION]
1     Agathos to Phanias, the
2     brother, [very] many greetings.
3     Well indeed you did
4     by informing me about [the] mother,
5     [but] it would be better if you did [so] by standing [by her]
6     until she be not further troubled; but I have written about this to the leader. And I myself
7     wishing to go up
8     was not able alone
9     being in the assembly.
10    I wish you to be well.
11    To Phanias … written by Agathos [the] brother.
