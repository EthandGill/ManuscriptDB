[TRANSLATION]
1     In a letter, first indeed [in] your word of the least, I prostrate [myself], I lift up, I kiss the footstool of your beloved [ones] of your majesty, my father the holy one, I know your manner of mercy
2     of times throughout month[s] in my heart all of them and my soul all of them, the great [one of] bishop of the country all of it
3     the great [one of] Helias [who] is Thesbite
4     and Apa Helesaios [who] is spirit-bearer, your student [the] holy [one], you are the great [one of] light
5     man within toward the reasonable [substance]
6     the holy [one], you are the pillar
7     of a light which stands upon the inhabited-world all of it, you are our glory and
8     our boast and our joy of the least [one]
9     to beseech through Christ on behalf of you that he
10    is the wise [one] of those that shine, bishop of the souls, helper of those that labor, protector standing before all those that shine, each one on account of conscience
11    each [one] to grant to you a crown and a palm of the Lord forever of all what we have seen to you [in] every time that the witness of the devil has been cast out
12    of us within, [like] a judge
13    in the Lord
