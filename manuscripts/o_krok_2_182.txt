[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: … (?) to his father Sotas, greetings. You should know that I have rented Prokla to Simiou for sixty drachmas, including the quintana. Write me a letter to give me guardianship so that I have power to negotiate about her. And if you have a chiton send it to her since hers was mislaid (as she arrived here,) so she had to put on something of mine. And write to me through whom I can send you the money. Greet Arrianos. Farewell.
