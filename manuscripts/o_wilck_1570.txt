[TRANSLATION]
1     Chesphmois, collector of the money-tax of the metropolis,
2     (to) Onnophris (son of) Abos. I have, for embankment-tax of year 4,
3     impure 7 dr. 5 obols, 2 chalkoi. Year 5 of Hadrian Caesar
4     the lord, Phaophi 20. Paniskos, I have signed.
5     Others, Pauni 29, for watchtowers and others of year 5, impure
6     3 dr. 2 obols. Paniskos, I have signed.
