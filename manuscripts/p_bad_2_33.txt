1     Ptolemaeus to Deios the
2     dearest, greetings.
3     Your reverence
4     I make before the lord
5     Sarapis. If it is not burdensome to you,
6     brother, exert yourself and, if you are able,
7     our little olive-grove
8     water and you will do a great
9     [kindness]. Perhaps
10    but I will come down in the meanwhile
