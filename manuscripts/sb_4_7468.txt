[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Aurelius] Hierax, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoites,
2     [from] Lucious Nonius Cassianus:
3     I leased long ago from Aurelia Lucretia around the village
4     Philadelphia, grain-bearing arurae eleven; but I having paid the year-by-year
5     the produce-taxes up to
6     of the past year, and of the
7     arurae for the present year having remained without water through lack of waters, and not being able any longer to endure the farming, I present and I request word be sent to her through one of the
8     attendants about you, this equal matter,
9     so that she may know me withdrawing from the allotment,
10    I having testified before
11    her then that not through my
12    negligence the allotment lacked water, being satisfied I with this arrangement.
13    Of the fourth year of Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
14    Antoninus Pius, Fortunate, Augustus, Mecheir 30.
