[TRANSLATION]
1     Lawyer. We speak on behalf of Sambathion and Heras from the village of Karanis.
2     Much and varied land the clients registered in the censuses that have been taken
3     in the district of the village of Karanis of the Arsinoite (nome): Sambathion, of royal (land)
4     ninety arouras and of private (land) ten, and Heras, of royal (land) ninety.
5     But, the water-supply having collapsed and the lands being no longer irrigated, it came about
6     that they remained unsown and brought a very great loss upon those who are being assisted.
7     Since, then, they did not hold out about this part, the exactors bring a greater oppression:
8     for, Alexandros and Heraklas (whose heirs stand here) having registered a very great deal of land in that plain, and this being [already] cultivated
9     by certain fellow-villagers, and those (men) having gone into withdrawal, the exactors
10    improperly come upon these (clients) and compel them, who are neither owners nor registrants nor holders, to discharge the assessments, though it is due from those
11    who registered and are owners, and that too while they are established (as such); but they (the exactors) have crossed over against the rustics
12    and the simple folk. We beseech this Magnitude, since we have also
13    the censuses, where it is proved that the lands belong to no other than Heraklas
14    and Alexandros, that they (Heraklas and Alexandros), being in the city, be compelled
15    to acknowledge the assessments of the lands, and that those who are being assisted be left undisturbed, since
16    those men, being able to do much in the localities and being violent, fasten their own assessments upon the rustics.
