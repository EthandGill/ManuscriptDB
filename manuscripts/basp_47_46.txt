1     (of the quarter of) Herakleous Topon …
2     … [and]
3     To Athenaios also called Apollonios,
4     keepers of the archive (bibliophylakes),
5     [f]rom Zenas son of Zenas, grandson of Heras,
6     his mother being Areia daughter of Hypatikos, of those
7     from the city of Oxyrhynchos, of the quarter
8     [of Her]akleous Topon. In accordance with the orders concerning the scrutiny (epikrisis)
9     of those advancing into (the class) of those from the
10    gymnasium, whether they are of this descent,
11    I declare that I myself have advanced
12    into the class of the thirteen-year-olds from the gymnasium in the
13    15th year of Domitian, and that (the record) was transmitted in the same year for scrutiny
14    among the thirteen-year-olds in the quarter [of the Dr]omos of the Gymnasium;
15    whence, having come forward for
16    my own scrutiny … I declare that
17    my indicated father is
18    Zenas son of Heras, grandson of Zenas, from
19    the same city, [who], when he was alive, was registered
20    at the Dromos of the Gymnasium in the (register) under Suto[rios]
21    Sosibios who served as strategos, and Nikandros who became royal scribe (basilikos grammateus), and the o[t]hers, the list having been recorded i[n]
22    the 5th year of the god Vespasian,
23    of those approved from the [gymna]sium,
24    in the same qua[rt]er, in the cla[s]s
25    of those scrutinized under Cou[rtius P]aulinus the trib[une]… ea …
26    … of a young pig: 16 drachmas
27    … of o[l]ive(oil): 7 kotylai, into Senepta: 7 drachmas
