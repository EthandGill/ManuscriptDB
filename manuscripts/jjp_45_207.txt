[TRANSLATION]
1     Zoilus, secretary of farmers, to Akoussilaos, grain-buyer, greetings.
2     measure for Damon of Heracles for
3     [which] he works royal land, wheat six and a half,
4     total wheat 6½. Year 20, Hathyr 16.
5     memorandum on … wheat 3/4, given wheat 3/4.
6     Marres, village-secretary; measure the
7     aforementioned wheat six and a half, total wheat 6½.
8     memorandum on … wheat 3/4, given wheat [3/4].
9     Year 20, Hathyr 16
10    Onnophris, leader of farmers; reckon artabas 6½,
11    total artabas 6½. Year 20, Hathyr 16.
