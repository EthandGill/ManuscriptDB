[TRANSLATION]
1   To Herakleides, s[tra]tegos and (official) over the revenues:
2   from Kastor, [g]ymn[asi]arch of Koma, and the
3   other [of the] catoecs (settlers) in the village, and the
4   other cultivators and the royal cultivators
5   [of those] dwelling in the village. Already from the
6   16th (year) of Caesar, of (the dyke) maintained along the village,
7   the public embankment (dyke) to. ... , and on account of those (which arise) from this,
8   the occurring breaches, there exists for us in the
9   village a ferry-boat, by means of which we carry out both the summer works an[d] the
10   whole rest of the sowing, ferrying across by this means.
11   And while the ferryman Patouons was paying
12   into the public treasury on its behalf three hundred drachmas of silver,
13   clear ... of the accounts, having made use of (this), Apollos ..
14   ...having arrived at the village with a soldier,
15   lodged in his house and the cloaks he found there,
16   two new ones, he took up and carried off, although he (the ferryman) owed nothing,
17   and he himself, being worn down by the tax-farmers, a report
18   to us [he submitted ]
