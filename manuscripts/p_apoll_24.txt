[TRANSLATION]
1     And at another time, as I think, I wrote to your esteemed [genuine] friendship about [the] present letter-bearing sailor
2     on account of his debt, namely that he owes one or two pieces of money, and the debtor died,
3     and his father-in-law inherited him, and would not tolerate him being released from the debt of the deceased. Receiving then
4     your esteemed friendship my present letter, from the heir of the deceased demand the
5     debt of his. For he has, as he said, witnesses knowing that the deceased owes him the same two pieces of money.
6     Furthermore, do not let there be need of another letter of mine about him, so that he himself might be able to pay his obligation
7     In the month Athyr ... [indiction] [...]
8     To the esteemed worthy-of-honor [friend] Pappa, village-elder of Upper Apollonia, Theodore
9     about the household of Phis the sailor
