[TRANSLATION]
1     Year [15] of the Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus
2     Germanicus Dacicus. [He] has worked in the Epagathian canal for five days for
3     dyke-works of the same fifteenth year of Soknopaiou Nesos
4     Stototis, Papeitus son of Papeitus, mother Thasete.
5     Apollos, [I] have marked [it].
6     Mysthos, [I] have marked [it].
