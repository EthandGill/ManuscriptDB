r.1    μεμέτρηται εἰς τὸν τῆς διοικήσεως θησαυρὸν Ἄνω τοπαρχίας γενήματος θ ἔτους

r.7    Κρι σεσημείωμαι.

[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the treasury of the administration of Upper toparchy, produce of the 9th year
2     of Trajan the lord, Hathyr 26, of the 10th year
3     for back-reckoned times, the two sons of Horos, Esminis
4     through Horos Porieythos and two brothers, year
5     of Trajan the lord, grain agreements of administration
6     from ungathered wheat, one and a half [and] a third artabae, it totals wheat artabae 1 ½ ⅓
7     Barley: [it] has been marked.
