[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, [fare]well. Tryphon, [son of] Antigonus …
2     I am wronged by a certain Teôs. Having received from me …
3     which he himself obtained, on the condition that we work it …
4     and he will make a lease contract, common …
5     the dry land, and my demanding that he establish for me [the] …
6     he has excluded me from the partnership and [from] the work[s. I beseech you therefore, King, if it seems good to you, [order] Diophanès the general to write [to Rhodon the overseer, to send him before] Diophanès so that it might be decided for me against him [and if the matters through the petition are true, compel Teôs to establish for me the contract …
7     For this having happened, I will be through you, King, [the one who has obtained justice. Farewell.]
8     To Rhodon: above all reconcile them; but if not, send …
9     they be decided.
10    Year 4, Daisios 27, Athur 28.
11    Tryphon, son of Antigonus, against Teôs,
12    concerning the contract and work[s].
