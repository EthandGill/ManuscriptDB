[TRANSLATION]
1     ... of the holy church to the most magnificent lord Phoibammon, magistros. I have from
2     of [your] magnificence concerning the remainder of the gold of the night-commanders of Hermoupolis, coin
3     one k[eratia] nineteen, there amounts to coin 1 keratia 19 alone. It was written
4     in the month Mechir 23, indiction 6, the holy church through me Isaac
5     deacon, [agrees to] coin one keratia nineteen as aforesaid.
