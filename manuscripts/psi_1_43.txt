[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Zacharias, son of Pebetos, undersigning in order, and Silvanus, son of Ammonios, both village-chiefs
2     from the settlement of Demetrius of the Hermopolite nome, undertaking
3     the opinion of the [fellow-villagers]
4     To Tyrannus, deacon of the church of Hermoupolis, greetings. We have been satisfied by you for [all]
5     public whether canonical titles or annona-contributions on behalf of the twelfth indiction in full of the [for]
6     arouras, that is to say in silver myriads five and a thousand talents, amounts to in silver myriads five one alone and for this reason
7     we have issued to you this receipt for your security, and having been questioned we agreed. Aurelius Zachari[as]
8     Pebetos and Silvanus son of Ammonios, the aforementioned, we issued the
9     receipt as stated; I, the same Zacharias, wrote [letters] on behalf of [Sil]van[us], [he] not [knowing]
