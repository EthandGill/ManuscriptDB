[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a petition. To Aquilius Capitolinus, the most excellent epistrategos,
2     from Julius Apolinarius, veteran, advocate of Apolinarius and
3     Valerius … ranus, minors, Antinoites. It is fitting for you, greatest of procurators, to render to all men their own, but especially
4     to those underage who are without resources. The matter is as follows:
5     Thatres … from the village of Karanis in the Herakleides division
6     of the Arsinoite nome, alleging that she is the sister of the grandfather of the
7     minors, took possession of all the property belonging to the minors, both slave persons and
8     of the household effects left by their parents,
9     which were very many; against her I brought suit before Aelius Eudaemon
10    the former strategos, who, handing over to me the slave persons,
11    ordered that the household effects be restored to me and
12    that I take up the property. But Thatres, learning of the departure of Eudaemon, took as her accomplice Julius
13    Valerius, who, learning of the departure of Eudaemon, did not give back the household effects, nor indeed did he hand over the property; and as a result it became necessary for me, at your former
14    visit, to petition you by memoranda, to which you also subscribed that I should appeal to the basilikos (grammateus) who was administering the affairs of
15    the strategia, before whom I brought suit on the 30th of the past month Hathyr against Valerius, and there was a ruling
16    [for me by] Serenos the basilikos who was administering also the affairs of
17    the strategia; and having examined the matter he said that the things stated by each party
18    were entered in the records. I therefore refer
19    the matter to the most excellent epistrategos,
20    the slaves being in the possession of those with whom they are. Wherefore I request,
21    [if it seem good] to your fortune, to hear me against them,
22    [so that] at some point at last, through your beneficence, the minors may be able to recover their own property, that I may be, together with them, benefited by you. Farewell.
