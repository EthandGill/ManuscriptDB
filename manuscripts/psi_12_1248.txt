[TRANSLATION]
1     Menesthianos to Apollonianos and Spartiates: be cheerful
2     [The] gods [are] witnesses that having learned about my master, your son, thus I was grieved and
3     mourned as my own child; for he was worthy of affection; and having [a] desire in me to go forth to you, Pinoutios held [me] back, saying you my master Apollonianos
4     had instructed him [to tell me] not to come up [to you]
5     as you were going to the Arsinoite [nome]. But
6     bear [it] nobly; for this indeed [is] also for the gods
7     [to decide]. In all things to you, my master Apollonianos, I acknowledge gratitude, because you protect me thus as [your] self. And I too now being able
8     [to] repay myself to Chairemon
9     for those [things] in which he treated me badly, I endured until [I could] tell you. For he acted not humanely, and he himself having done things with others
10    not yet [claims he] owes nothing of those [arising] from men
11    [having treated us [badly]. Whatever then may seem [good] to you, make [it] clear to me; for I do not remain silent [about] it myself. Yet equally of the
12    handwritten [document] by the acquaintance sent through [I] sent [it] to you, so that you know. And I
13    [myself] T … [for] a home-born child lost
14    [worth] two talents. I pray you farewell, master
15    mine, together with my master Spartiates [to] all gods
16    being cheerful.
17    If the messages [have] come to be [delivered], send
18    to us.
19    And of the handwritten [document] is a copy:
20    To Aurelius Paulinus, strategos of the Hermopolite [nome]
21    from Marcus Aurelius Dionysius and Aurelius Ammonius
22    from Oxyrhynchos city. We have been sent together by Aurelius Leonides
23    strategos of the Oxyrhynchite [nome], from those [things] written to him by Julius Julianus the most mighty epistrategos, to which you reported [them]
24    to him, to be here and identify the men declared by Titus Flavius Chairemon, village-scribe of Oxyrhynchos city, [in] number 5. We swear by the
25    [fortune] of Gaius Julius Verus Maximinus Caesar the lord
26    [that] we, having been present in the territory, [have] learned
27    these [men] to have gone away into their own [places] [to] take care of the necessities falling to them;
28    or we would be bound by the oath. Year 2, Choiak 17
29    so-and-so and so-and-so through [the hand of] someone [like] me we swore the oath
30    as stated before.
31    To Aurelius Apollonianos … [having held the office of] gymnasiarch [and] strategos of nomes.
