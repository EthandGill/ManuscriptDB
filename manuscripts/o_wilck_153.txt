[TRANSLATION]
1   Antiochos Casianus and Arrius Pupl(ianus) and Julius Maximus and those
2   with them, overseers of the Sacred Gate of Syene, through Phanophis the assistant. Paid:
3   Petechnoubis
4   son of Pachnoubis, mother Tachnoubis, for the trade-tax of the sixteenth year of Hadrian
5   the lord: twenty drachmas (and) two obols — total 20 dr. 2 ob. Year 16 of Hadrian the lord,
6   Pachon 9.
