[TRANSLATION]
1     From the record-office of the village of Neilopolis. Year 8
2     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Mesore 25.
3     Segathis daughter of Pakysis son of Stotoetis the elder acknowledges,
4     her mother being Thaesis, priestess of the first tribe of Soknopaios the great, great god, about 37 years old, with a scar on the shin, with her guardian, her own kinsman
5     Pakysis son of Satabous son of Pakysis the priest,
6     from the same first tribe, about 33 years old, with a scar on the instep of the right foot, to her own husband Horos son of P…s son of
7     Onnophris, a priest, from the same first tribe, about
8     58 years old, with a scar on the left eyebrow, since Horos acted as guardian and clothed and supplied everything to
9     Segathis' son Horos from the first year
10    of Antoninus Caesar the lord up to the present year, that she will exact nothing under this
11    acknowledgement nor proceed against him, nor shall those
12    acting for Segathis in any way against Horos himself
13    nor against those acting for him concerning the things for which … as
14    he was appointed, to her aforewritten son Horos and to another son …, whether in utensils of every kind or all household goods, because
15    Horos has maintained …;
16    … the things owed by him to the aforewritten Horos, further from …;
17    and to the aforewritten Segathis …;
18    … it is fitting, because …;
19    … up to the present day …;
20    … ge … ai.
