[TRANSLATION]
1   To Ploutogenes, royal scribe acting in the matters of
2   the strategia,
3   Antonius Vestinus, duplicarius, dispatched
4   by Valerius Frontinus, prefect of the
5   Heraklian cavalry-wing at Koptos. I have had measured out
6   to me from Asies son of Phibis son of Asies, and Inaro-
7   ous son of Kollouthos son of Cornelius, elders of Magdo-
8   la of Petechon the Kousseite, Upper, the
9   (barley) levied on their village out of what was order-
10   ed by the former prefect Longaeus
11   Rufus to be requisitioned from the produce of the 24th (year)
12   for the use of the aforesaid wing — of barley, two
13   myriads of artabas, by the public receiving-measure,
14   by the ordered measurement, of barley fifteen artabas,
15   in accordance with the apportionment made
16   by the procurators of the nome.
17   And I have issued this receipt in quadruplicate.
