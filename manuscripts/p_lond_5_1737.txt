[TRANSLATION]
1     … of our most divine and most serene
2     master Flavius Heraclius the eternal Augustus and Emperor,
3     in the third year, Mecheir 15, of the first indiction.
4     Flavius Patermouthis son of Menas, soldier of the unit of Elephantine, sailor
5     from Syene, to Aurelius Joannes son of Pityron, sailor
6     from the same Syene, greeting. I, Patermouthis, acknowledge through this
7     my written security that I have had and borrowed from you today, for my necessary need, of gold
8     three and one-third nomismata by the standard of Syene — total, 3⅓ gold nomismata by the standard of Syene —
9     and these, with God('s help), I am ready to furnish to you whenever you should wish, with the interest on them, of each nomisma
10    per month, one-quarter of a gold keration, from the aforewritten day,
11    without any dispute; and I have pledged to you, by way of security, a brazen scale-beam(?) … of pounds,
12    24½ pounds, with the balance of mine and …
13    weighing 100 pounds, on the condition that these be under
14    your authority until the repayment and completion
15    of the enclosed gold, as a pledge …
16    and for security I have made this bond valid and firm,
17    produced everywhere, and being formally questioned I acknowledged (it). Flavius Kollouthos son of Alexandros, centurion of the unit
18    of Syene, I bear witness. Flavius Theodosios son of Apa Deios, soldier of the unit of Syene, I bear witness. Flavius
19    Faustinos son of Pachymios, soldier
20    of the unit of Syene, I bear witness.
21    Through me, Dios son of Th…
22    Security of Patermouthis son of Menas, sailor from Syene; total, 3⅓ gold nomismata by the standard of Syene.
