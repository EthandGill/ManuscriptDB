[TRANSLATION]
1     [without delay and whenever you wish]
2     to have, I will deliver to you the use
3     of the same places with two
4     doors and keys as I received them; the rental
5     is valid and secure, and when questioned I acknowledged.
6     Aurelius Sarapion, [son] of Taurinus from Hermu city
7     I testify [to the rental,] having heard from the
8     one who placed [it]. Aurelius Victor, [son] of Kollouthos from Hermu city
9     I testify to the rental, having hear[d from] the one who placed [it].
10    [by] me Ptolemaios
