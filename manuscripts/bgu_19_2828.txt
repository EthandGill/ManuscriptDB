[TRANSLATION]
1     for the crops … …
2     to plough these (lands) a third time and to irrigate them a third time [through my own]
3     animals, and to guard their crops until the
4     carrying away of them; for I acknowledge that I have been paid by you from now
5     the full wage-payment for these, and if I do not give the
6     third irrigation of them, I will furnish you on its behalf eighteen keratia; and if it should happen that I or some other person hinder you from receiving
7     their crops, I acknowledge that I will give you on their behalf of gold eight
8     gold solidi, without judgement or lawsuit, at my own risk and from the resource of
9     my entire property; the present receipt of wages is valid and
10    secure, and on being formally questioned I gave my assent. I, Aurelius Victor … oulous the aforesaid, drew up
11    this receipt of wages as set forth above. I, Kollouthos son of Isakios, least of deacons,
12    of the … wrote on his behalf, he being unlettered. Aurelius
13    Elisaios son of Phoibammon from Hermopolis bear witness to the receipt of wages
14    having heard (it) from the one who drew it up. Aurelius Petros Kol …
15    from Hermopolis bear witness to the aforesaid receipt of wages having heard (it)
16    from the one who drew it up.
17    Through me, Kollouthos, with God, notary … …
