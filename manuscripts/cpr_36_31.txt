[TRANSLATION]
1     the fugitive Oxyrhynchites [whom you found]
2     to Ibion and you arrested; arrest [them]
3     them immediately and send to me. If not, and
4     send me the [boys] whom I sent to you
5     on behalf of those separated from them, Oxyrhynchites. But all, both the Oxyrhynchites
6     both [the] others, send to me immediately
7     some of them
8     [let be given to Senouthios] the [no]tary by Athanasios the illustrious
