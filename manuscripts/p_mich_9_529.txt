[TRANSLATION]
1     To Maevius Honoratianus, prefect of E[gypt],
2     [from Aur]elius … son of Chairemon, from the village [of Karanis]
3     [of the Herakl]eides division of the Arsinoite nome. The d[ivine and]
4     [imperial] ordinances expressly command that, in [the following]
5     [year], one is not to perform any other liturgy but to be passed over [and rest]. Since therefore, most illustrious prefect, those now from [the village]
6     [of Karanis], the men of affairs, disregarding what is customary,
7     … [for] the safety of the nation and of us the farm[ers] …
8     … me, who am a grain-collector of the produce of the past
9     [2nd year, for the guard]ing of the wheat in the granary of the vill[age of Ka]ranis, they have appointed; and now, contrary to all the law[s, I have been given over to] the liturgy mentioned, the grain-collectorship of the prod[uce]
10    [of the present] 3rd year, I have fled to your feet, beg[ging you]
11    [by your most] illustrious [fortune], if it please you, to hear me against [them],
12    [so that] by your help, my lord, I may be able [to obtain]
13    [that, from this] unlawful appointment thus [being released]
14    [I may be able blame]lessly to fulfill the burden of the previously en[trusted]
15    [to me liturg]y, and, remaining in my own place [forever]
16    [to give thanks to your fortune]. Farewell.
17    … Epeiph 8
18    [] … the things … to you
19    … see to it. Sheet 22 of roll 1.
