[TRANSLATION]
1     [… of Theo]dosius, procurator, through Ponnitus
2     tax-collector, to Phoibammon, … [greetings]. I have received on behalf of [you] from
3     the exports of the fields under you, [of] us, of the canon of the eighth indiction, the tax of the full
4     grain according to the lease, that is, grain, artabas twenty-four and in account of [additional]
5     artabas two with expenses and for your security I have made this receipt as stated. Epeiph
6     fourth [day] of the eighth indiction. Ponis [son of] Isaakios, administrator, it accords with me as stated.
