[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our master Flavius Basiliscus, for the 2nd time,
2     and of Armatus, the most illustrious, Mecheir 3, 15th indiction, in the city of the Arsinoites.
3     To Eirene also called Eulogia, called the catholic church
4     [of the martyrs] of the city of the Ars[inoit]es, under the most holy
5     Helladios the bishop, through the most devout Iohannes
6     presbyter and steward, Aurelius Iohannes
7     son of Apphous, cultivator of this same holy church,
8     from the village of Psenyris of the Arsinoite nome, greetings.
9     I acknowled[g]e that I have had from you, through hand,
10    three solidi of gold less ten and a half
11    keratia, t[otal] g[old] s[olidi] 3 less keratia 10½, as capital,
12    [be]ing held b[y] m[e] as the pric[e] of green-stuff and
13    [h]ay and ve[t]ch [and pul]ses, at the pre[v]ailing price … … to you
14    [whi]ch I will pay in full from my cultivator's share
15    [o]f your arouras: the green-stuff, on the one hand,
16    [in] this present month Mecheir, the hay
17    in the month Pharmouthi, and the pulses in the mo[nth Pauni] …
18    by your measure, in the village, from the crops
19    [o]f the first in[diction, with]out delay, from all my
20    property, an[d being asked the formal question, I acknowledged it. I, Aurelius Iohan]nes [son] of Apphous, the a[foresaid, have had the so]lidi three
21    … pulses … [Aur]elius Iohannes son of Paulos wrote [on his behalf] as he is illiterate,
22    the bond of Iohannes son of Apphous. Gold solidi 3 less keratia 10½. To the holy church.
