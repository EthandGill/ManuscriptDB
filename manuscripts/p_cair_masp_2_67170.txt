[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Basilius
2     the most glorious, in the twenty-[third] year,
3     Pharmouthi 21, of the thirteenth (indiction).
4     To the community of the most pious monks
5     of the holy monastery of Zmin, [through] the
6     most God-beloved Apa Ioannes the monk
7     and superior of the same [monastery], which holds property
8     about the far bank of the (territory) of the Panopolites,
9     from Aurelii Senouthes, of his father Nouthos
10    of his mother Stephanous, [and Iou]lios
11    son of Abrahamios, of his mother Tamine, plant-grower(?),
12    both from the said village of Zmin
13    of the same Panopolite nome, greeting.
14    We acknowledge willingly and of our own choice,
15    with joint surety and mutual guarantee,
16    that we have leased from you for whatever
17    time you wish, the property belonging to the
18    holy monastery under you, an entire orchard
19    situated to the west about the same village,
20    called Psaiou Jine, with an entire cistern
21    and olive-trees and date-palms and all the rights
22    of its whole circuit and enclosure,
23    of the part within only, of made-up land,
24    on condition that we have this under us for
25    cultivation and for the planting of vegetables
26    unceasingly with our own hands and
27    beasts and at our own expense, and to give
28    you always vegetables of the
29    various vegetables growing from it
30    and of that which, if it happen, is sown from it … from
