[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Heracleides, epistropus
2     from Aurelius Arthonius, agent of Stephanitis. I request that there be sent
3     to the embankment of the property of Stephanitis
4     of the owing first-year share, 400 drachmas,
5     and for the price of [this] reed [of the] share likewise
6     on account 300 drachmas, and to me [for]
7     wages from Choiak until
8     Mechir, 3 months, 180 drachmas.
9     The total of the request amounts to 880 drachmas.
10    Of which set off
11    for the price of wine, 2 jars, each [one]
12    of 9 drachmas and 1 obol, total 18 drachmas and 2 obols.
13    Year 2, Mechir 5.
14    Aurelius Arthonius has presented this.
15    Aurelius Heracleides has [noted] it.
