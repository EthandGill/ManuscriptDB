[TRANSLATION]
1     Polemis and Petes[ouc]hos, the two [sons] of Phemnas, and the daughters of our deceased brother of (the) same father and mother, Siphon (the) elder,
2     daughters Aresis and Thenapollôs, with [their] guardians [who are] their own relatives, [of] the one
3     of Aresis (is) Pelôis of Patyneios, and
4     of Thenapollôs (is) Nepherôs of Peteeutos, we agree to have sold to Hestia [daughter] of Akousilaus, with her guardian, her own
5     brother Sotos (the) younger of Akousilaus, the things belonging to us and pertaining to our share from the southern part of what belongs to you, Hestia, in (the) village Kerkesouchoi Oros, a garden-park of bare land, and all attending (things) held in common and undivided
6     towards the same Hestia in the same Kerkesouchoi Oros, with measurements and measurements (by cubits); of which the neighbors to the south …
7     and of the partners bare land, and to some part of the same Hestia
8     bare land, to the north of the same Hestia [the aforementioned garden-park,]
9     to the west of me, the undersigned Phemnas, and of the partners, a courtyard, to the east (the) royal road; and we have received from Hestia the
10    agreed price all in full immediately by hand from home, and
11    we will guarantee this sale with every guarantee from both public (charges) and private (ones) and all
12    imposition for all time, as stated, in no way
13    of the same being diminished for (the debts) which the aforementioned
14    Pelôis and Nepherôs owe to him according to agreed security, as
15    [is stated]. I wrote for them, Phemnas [son] of Phemnasios, because they do not know letters. Aresis and Thenapollôs both
16    [of Siph]on (the) elder, with their own guardian, each [of them, of] Aresis (is) I, Pelôis of Patyneios, and of Thenapol[lôs is] Nepherôs of Peteeutos, we, the two, agree to have sold the things belonging and pertaining to us [a] share
17    [of bare] land at Kerkesouchoi Oros of (the) Polemon division, and we have jointly received the price, and we will jointly guarantee with every guaran[tee, as] is stated. Pelôis the aforementioned, I wrote, and for those not knowing letters.
18    [Polemis, about] 58 years old, (has) a scar on (the) left shin. Petesouchos about 55 years old, (has) a scar below the calf
19    on (the) right. Aresis about 26 years old, (has) a scar on (the) left knee. Thenapollôs about 22 years old, (has) a scar on (the) right elbow.
20    Pelôis about 53 years old, (has) a scar on (the) left forearm. Nepherôs about 52 years old, (has) a scar on (the) left shin.
