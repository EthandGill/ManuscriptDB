[TRANSLATION]
1     The document of three notaries of the most glorious emir
2     That is: send 150 four-pound measures immediately
3     And send a good dish so that [it] not turn away.
4     But by all means send it immediately.
5     [It was] released Pharmouthi 13 at hour 9
6     Let it be delivered to Senouthios the notary from Athanasios
7     the illustrious
