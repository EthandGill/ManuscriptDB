[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysios to Diogenes, greetings and health. Since not knowing
2     to the greatest god Amonrasonther we sent according to the
3     registers where the feathered adornment was purchased, the expenditure of moneys
4     of the purchase, 250 talents, from which also was imposed on Harsiesis the god-hated
5     in account 90 talents, and there are for the priests revenues from the sacred ship
6     of Phaophi and Pauni of year 40, 50 talents, and from the anchors of the
7     Papoherieion other 40 talents, becomes 90 talents, so as to be equal to those assessed,
8     we choose to run over again the impiety that happened and to make
9     the god propitious, who both from the beginning and now is saving us, for indeed
10    what we have done concerning the surveying of his islands
11    to all is clear, apart from the other things that have come about through us.
12    Well will you do using these revenues instead of the
13    expended 90 talents, and having written out the payment from both the accounts and having sent back the document with this letter.
14    For we have persuaded the priests to yield the moneys, on condition that
15    again from our own [funds] we shall correct [it] because of us being cautious
16    [regarding] Amenwphis and Osiris. Wherefore also having recalled
17    [those things] in which we have benefited you, in exchange for which you have disappointed us, destroying together
18    with the [affairs] of Paiouts, whatever in a few days will be changed to the end
19    and [he] will completely lose the private [things], do the things being shown and
20    our payment send with the letter. For well
21    we shall meet with you being ungrateful. Take account and however you may accomplish [it],
22    make clear. Fare well. Year 40, Phaophi 17.
