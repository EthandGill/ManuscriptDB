[TRANSLATION]
1     to one another the things written below. I, the aforesaid
2     Phoibammon son of Triadelphos, landholder, acknowledge that I give the public tax on behalf of the
3     below-written holding of the same holy monastery called (that) of Psentouses, situated
4     in the western plain of the same village, in the allotment of Tchious, to the west of my holding
5     called (that) of the dyers, to the right of the aforesaid holy monastery
6     of Apa Sourous, through the most God-loving Kollouchios, monk and superior,
7     [through you, the most] reverend John son of Samuel, priest and administrator, in grain
8     and gold-payments and freight-charges and all kinds of annona-titles,
9     in respect of the registration of the public codex of the (man) of glorious memory,
10    John, the deceased scholasticus and censitor, if it should so happen,
11    another assessment in the Antaiopolite nome, beginning from the crops
12    of the prosperous fifteenth indiction that shall be, God willing, and of itself
13    and of the time following, in perpetuity, after the public tax of the aforesaid
14    holding, so that I give to you each year of grain,
15    pure, twenty artabas by your tax-measure, and of sweet wine
16    fifty jars of six xestai each per jar, the empty vessels being supplied
17    by you, making 20 artabas of grain and 50 jars of wine only, the grain at the time of the threshing,
18    and the wine at the time of the vintage. And if anyone of us should attempt
19    to transgress and not abide by this eternal and written agreement,
20    the party not abiding shall pay to the abiding party, by way of penalty,
21    from formal interrogation and transgression, three ounces of gold, exacted by deed and force,
22    in addition to this written agreement remaining valid
23    and secure, produced everywhere, which, drawn up in duplicate
24    of identical wording, [we have established], pledging to one another for all the
25    aforesaid matters and for the exaction of the aforesaid penalty
26    the possessions of the aforementioned monastery and the possessions
27    of the aforesaid Phoibammon, both movable and immovable
28    and self-moving, of every kind and class, by way of pledge
29    and right of mortgage, as if by legal judgment; and concerning all that has been written
30    having interrogated one another and having been interrogated by one another,
31    we have agreed that these things are so, to give, to do, to keep, to abide, continually to carry through to the end, with the subscription of ourselves
32    and of the customary witnesses following. I, the aforesaid party of the
33    holy monastery of Apa Sourous, through the most God-loving Kollouchios, monk
34    and superior of the same holy monastery, through me John son of Samuel,
35    priest and deacon of the aforesaid (monastery), have established this agreement, and
36    all that has been written satisfies me as aforesaid, and having subscribed
37    with my own hand by commission of the aforesaid superior. Aurelius Phoibammon son of Triadelphos, the aforesaid,
38    have established this agreement, and all that has been written satisfies me
39    as aforesaid, and having subscribed and read it I issued it. Victor
40    son of Phoibammon, priest of the holy catholic church of Apa Mousaios,
41    [I] witness this agreement, having heard from the contracting parties, and I witness
42    that I heard from the aforesaid superior committing
43    to the most reverend John, priest, to make this agreement. Daueid son of Andreas,
44    lector of the same holy catholic church, I witness this agreement, having heard
45    from the contracting parties, and I witness that I heard from the aforesaid superior committing to John the most reverend priest to make this
46    agreement. John son of Promaos, lector of the holy place of Apa Promaos
47    the martyr, I witness this agreement, having heard from the contracting parties,
48    and I witness that I heard from the aforesaid superior committing to John, most reverend priest, to make this agreement.
49    Aurelius John son of Kyriakos, I witness this agreement, having heard from the contracting parties.
