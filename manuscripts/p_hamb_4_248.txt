[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the eighth year of Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrian
2     Antoninus Augustus Pious, Phamenoth 28. They wrote off
3     To Theon, tax collector of the market of the nome and of other purchases through
4     Heracl, Horus son of Satabutus and the other priests of Soknopaiu Nesos
5     For the appointed [amount] of boats [sieves?] of the past
6     Seventh year, silver, one hundred drachmas, [they] total 100 drachmas, likewise the same for
7     Permit of which they hold of donkeys of the past seventh year
8     Silver, one hundred drachmas, [they] total 100 drachmas.
