[TRANSLATION]
1     Aristeides to Zenon, greetings. If you are well and the other things for you are all according to
2     judgment, I would have much thanks to the gods; I [am well] also myself. It has come to pass that I have been nominated by the citizens as a grain-receiver, not yet being of age nor this service applying to me, but through some envy certain [people nominated me]. We have therefore sent both myself and my brother Theronides Dromon so that he might make clear these things to Apo[llo]nius, in order that he might help us and release me
3     from this receivership. You would do a favor therefore, having brought Dromon to Apollonius with speed
4     and having taken part in his getting before Apollonius [the swiftest], and so that he might send him to us with speed, having arranged all things. Write also yourself, if you have need of anything of the
5     from us, in order that we might do all things for you.
6     Farewell.
7     Aristeides concerning himself to Zenon.
8     of grain receivership. Year 29,
9     Panemos 1, in Arsinoe.
