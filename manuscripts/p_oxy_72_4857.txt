[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Achilles, secretary of Iulius Theon, to Didymus, sitologus of the district of Sco, greetings. Transfer, from the amount you hold on deposit for Iulius Theon, from the wheat-crop of the 2nd year, to Isarus daughter of Diogenes, (for her account) at . . . , four and a half artabas, at Suis, two and a half and a quarter artabas, at Sento, one and a quarter artaba, grand total 8 ½ art. of wheat. Year 2 of Hadrianus Caesar the lord, Mesore epagomenal 3. (N. Gonis)
