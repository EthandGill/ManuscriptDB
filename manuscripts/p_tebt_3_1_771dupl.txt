1     To king Ptolemy [and queen Cleo]patra the sister, gods … [greeting],
2     Petesouchos son of Pet[o]s, royal [cultivator]
3     of the men from Oxyrhyncha of the [division of Polemon]
4     of the Arsinoite nome. [I dwell in Ker]keusiris of the same no[me, and there belongs to me]
5     18
6     it has been left
7     an ancestral house in the [aforewritten village]
8     of Oxyrhyncha, and this, my [father] having held it as long as he lived, [and I after]
9     his death un[til now, no]
10    dispute arising; but [Stratonike]
11    daughter of Ptolemaios, of those dwelling in [Kroko]dilon polis of the aforedeclared [nome],
12    making trouble and [wishing] to extort from [me],
13    coming with [oth]ers against the declared [house, forces her way in] before judgment and ek…
14    in the village concerning the …
15    the house, coming up to and [laying claim] to it contrary to what is [fitting. I beg you],
16    the greatest gods, if [it seem good to you, to send] my petition to [Menekrat]es the
17    chief-bodyguard and s[trategos, so that]
18    he may enjoin Stratonike [not to force her way into]
19    the house, and, if she thinks she is being wronged, [to receive from me]
20    justice as is fitting. And this [being done],
21    I shall have been helped. …
