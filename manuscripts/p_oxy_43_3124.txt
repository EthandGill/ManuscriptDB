[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Aur]elius F[lavi]anus, most distinguished, and
2     Dionyso[d]orus, most excellent, presiding
3     over the public bakeries at Ptolemais,
4     [S]eptimius Crater, strategos of Lycopolis,
5     greetings.
6     [have] been conveyed and now by beasts [of burden]
7     [to] the military baking [that takes place] with you through Lycarios, son of Anubis, as transporter
8     wheat three hundred twelve artabae
9     and in order that you might know I write to you, most beloved. I pray for your health. Through the assistant.
10    To Aurelius Flavianus and D[ion]ysodorus.
