[TRANSLATION]
1     of [the] seventh year of the Emperor
2     Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
3     Augustus and of the Emperor
4     Caesar Lucius Aurelius Verus
5     Augustus, Thoth 13, through the bank of Sarapion [at] Tameion. Didymos
6     of Didymos and also of Demetrius
7     Sosikosmos [also called] Althaieus
8     and Pabous of Satabous
9     to Agathos Daemon also [called] Sosikrates, who has served as cosmetes, son of Mysthos, who served [as] cosmetes, to acknowledge receipt
10    from him from the aforesaid
11    equal [to those] which they owed to him according to cancellation of the same bank
12    which also he has given back to them for cancellation [and] nullification [of] silver, drachmas three hundred sixty, totaling drachmas 360,
13    and [he will] make no claim against them until the present
14    day. Agathos Daemon [also called] Sokrates, I have received and signed.
