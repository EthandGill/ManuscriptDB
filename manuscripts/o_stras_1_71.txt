[TRANSLATION]
1     Onnophrios Pikotos has been written on behalf of
2     Dikes: year 6, 6 drachmas 4-obol and bath[-tax]
3     4-obol [and] half: total 7 drachmas 2½ obols and the additional amounts
4     From 1½ obol of the stater. Year 7 of Tiberius
5     Claudius Caesar Augustus
6     Germanicus Imperator, of the month
7     Augustus 25. Kephalos Pikotos.
