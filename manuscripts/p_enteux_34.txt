[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting, Sopatros son of Melas and Dionysios son of Alkainetos and Ptolemaios
2     son of Peritas, retail wine-dealers from Kerkesoucha. We are wronged by Petenenteris. For in year 4, in the month Mecheir,
3     having sold to us 126 jars of wine, of which 72 were six-chous jars and 54 were five-chous jars,
4     each six-chous measure at 14 drachmas, and having received from us for this an earnest-money of 80 drachmas, on the terms that, also
5     adjusting the price of what was delivered, we should draw it from store; and when we had drawn from store
6     30 six-chous jars and 6 five-chous jars, and when we had given the price of these to
7     the man from Nikon, to whom … Petenenteris, 490 drachmas, and besides 110 drachmas more, so that this too might stand to our credit beyond the earnest-money given, so that the earnest-money should be 190 drachmas; and when we wished
8     to draw the remainder also from store, we found from the quantity a shortfall of 6 six-chous jars
9     and 8 five-chous jars. We beg you therefore, O king, if it seems good to you, to order Diophanes the strategos to summon
10    Petenenteris and investigate concerning these matters, and if it is true as we declare through this petition,
11    to compel him to give back to us the value of the 14 missing jars, as much as
12    we may swear to, so that, having taken refuge with you, O king, we may obtain justice.
13    Farewell.
14    … Year 4, Dios 3, Phamenoth 27.
15    Year 4, Dios 3, Phamenoth 27.
16    Sopatros and those with him,
17    retail-dealers, against Petenenteris
18    concerning the difference in wine.
