1     Year twenty, Epeiph two. Give an account.
2     of Julian, Theon, and Theon
3     to Theon the carpenter for a price
4     of castings of one yoke having been conveyed during winter to
5     [machine] [common] [toward]
6     to Theogenes from thirty drachmas; drachmas [fifteen]; [...] the
7     [the] selecting to Theo[genes]
8     … drachmas fifteen, the [remaining];
9     Give the drachmas fifteen.
