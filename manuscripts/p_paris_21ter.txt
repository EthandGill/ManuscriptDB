[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ our God and Saviour,
2     in the reign of our most serene master Flavius Mauricius Tiberius the eternal
3     Augustus and Emperor, in the seventeenth year,
4     Epeiph 19, of the third indiction.
5     Aurelius Arsenius son of Callinicus, his mother being Eugenia, goldsmith, from the
6     … providing on his own behalf a subscriber and witnesses of this
7     eternal sale, the ones subscribing below, I set down this
8     conveyance by a simple written security to you, the Aurelii
9     Pachymius son of Psas, purple-seller, and Maria his sister.
10    I the aforewritten Arsenius, from … this town, but now having my
11    dwelling in this city, … sell and acknowledge, by my free will
12    and self-chosen resolve and guileless conscience and unalterable
13    reckoning and unchanging purpose and unregretted intention, with firm
14    and right mind, with every fullest right of ownership and with all authority and eternal possession, from now and for all the continuous time,
15    … swearing by the holy and consubstantial Trinity and by the victory
16    and endurance of the God-guarded masters of the world, the Flavii Mauricius
17    Tiberius and Aelia Constantina, the eternal Augusti and emperors,
18    that I have sold to you and conveyed in good faith and choice today, being persuaded,
19    my share, a third of a whole three-storeyed house and of all the upper rooms,
20    with all its rights, the same house being held in common
21    between me and Maria and Joanna, another sister of ours, as to the remaining
22    third part,
23    it having also come to us from our paternal succession,
24    situated in this Panopolis, on the street of the Great Church; neighbours of the
25    whole house: to the south a narrow lane; to the north the house of the heirs of
26    …; to the east a public street onto which the door opens; to the west
27    … or whoever the neighbours may be, on every side all around,
