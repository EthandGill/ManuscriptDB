[TRANSLATION]
1     [A] son of the blessed Anoup from Herakles-city, from you Damianus the tarsikarios, son of the blessed Pamoun
2     … the need [for] one pure gold coin, which makes one pure gold coin, which I will repay to you gladly
3     [Month] of the present fifth indiction, unambiguously; [and] it was written [on] the month Thoth 4, indiction 5, year of Diocletian 323
4     [Through me] Peter [the notary]
