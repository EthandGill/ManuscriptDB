[TRANSLATION]
1     To Juennius Genealius the most brilliant prefect of Egypt
2     from Aurelia Sarapiades of Antinoopolis, on behalf of an orphaned daughter
3     and quite needy in age, master governor, placing a supplication upon your greatness I take refuge. For our husband Aurelius Paulus of Antinoopolis died intestate having ended [his] life [leaving] the daughter born of both
4     quite an infant, to whom the name
5     is Paulina. On behalf therefore of guardianship and management of the [possessions] of the child [I propose] my own brother Aurelius Sarapion, also called Alexander, himself an Antinoean,
6     [whom] I have tested for goodwill and faith and family affinity, [he] will care genuinely for the child's revenue, [so] I ask
7     that with his own risk [you] declare [him] guardian to the child,
8     so that if it should seem right to you with your most gracious fortune you command
9     by letter to the general of the Arsinoite nome where we hold land
10    that the appointment of the aforesaid Sarapion be made,
11    so that we may be benefited. Farewell.
