[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Soknopaiou Nesos, one camel. To Theodoros the strategos and to Timagenes the royal scribe of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite Nome, from Harpagathes son of Pakysis, grandson of Pneferos, from the town of Soknopaiou Nesos. That one camel which I declared at my town during the 20th year I am also declaring now in the 21st year of our lord Antoninus Caesar at my town. One camel was registered at the office of the strategos, by his assistant Anoubion, 4 Mecheir. One camel was registered at the office of the royal scribe, 4 Mecheir. It was counted at the office of the strategos accordingly. By the office of the royal scribe accordingly. I accept the tally(?).
