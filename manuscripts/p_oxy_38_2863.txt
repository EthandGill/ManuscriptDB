[TRANSLATION]
1     Leontas son of Didymus to Diogenes, grain-buyer of the eastern [section]
2     toparchy of Phobion, places, greetings. Set out from what you have of mine
3     in the [category] of wheat grain of the present
4     7th year of Hadrian Caesar the lord, artabas one hundred
5     thirty, [they] amount to wheat artabas 130, to Sara and Theogenes both
6     of Theogenes. 7th year of the autocrator Caesar Trajan
7     Hadrian Augustus, Mesore 29
