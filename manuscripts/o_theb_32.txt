[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been registered Psemmontis, [son] of Patefmois, mother Tachoule
2     Pamounius, for [the] poll-tax of Memnoneians, year 14, drachmas 8. Year 14 of Nero
3     the lord, Mecheir 26. Similarly, Phamenoth 25, drachmas 4.
4     Similarly, Pharmouthi 20, drachmas 4. Similarly, Pachon 23, drachmas 4. Similarly,
5     Pauni 29, drachmas 4. Similarly, year 1, Mesore 5, for [the] dike-tax, year 1
6     drachmas 3, obol, [which also equal] 2 five-obols and 1/2 obol.
