[TRANSLATION]
1     … …
2     … has written … …
3     … Eutychos concerning these things …
4     to be and to have spent on the building
5     and to prove concerning these things, and Agenor [having ordered]
6     until Eutych[os] should prove …
7     that those judged be detained, Eutychos, having been seduced
8     by Arsinoe, because formerly he had consorted with her
9     and children of his exist by her,
10    moved some of the property in the house
11    on the following night, which (property) the guards too
12    seized,
13    and he himself withdrew, and is even still now in the
14    Sarapieion at Memphis, being fed (with grain) by Arsinoe, who
15    later submitted a petition saying
16    that I had been appointed to furnish both the
17    builders and those from whom Eu[tychos] bought the brick and the timber, and the donkey-drivers
18    making written depositions,
19    though I had not been appointed; wherefore Agenor, deceived, and …
20    by the statement of Arsinoe, writes to Kleitorios that he summon me
21    (and) order that, in the matters for which I have been appointed, I furnish, making written depositions, the
22    men, and if they do not make written depositions, that he permit
23    no one to use force against Arsinoe. [I beg you therefore, O king],
24    since the man
25    [seduc]ed by Arsinoe has withdrawn so that he may not prove
26    [that] the house is his own
27    … may summon (?)
28    and she herself has not proved … to hear us, and if …
29    [Arsinoe should not] prove that the house is hers, as he wro[te]
30    he writes through the first petition, that the house is mine,
31    so that I too, through you, O king, may obtain justice.
32    Farewell.
33    Of Arsinoe. (endorsement)
