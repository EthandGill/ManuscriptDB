[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Justinian
2     the eternal Augustus and Imperator, in the twenty-seventh year,
3     after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most glorious, in the twelfth year,
4     Epeiph 30, of the second indiction.
5     To Flavius Julianus the most magnificent, of consular rank, and to Menas the most illustrious
6     scriniarius and pagarchs of the Antaiopolite (nome), through you Flavii Dioskoros son of Apollos
7     and Apollos son of Ioannes, most esteemed chief-villagers of the village of Aphrodite
8     of the same Antaiopolite nome, from Aurelii Pesalous son of Isidoros, from his mother
9     Maria, and Menas son of Hermauos, from his mother Tsone,
10    collectors of the public services
11    of the same village of Aphrodite, greeting. [We acknow]le[d]ge through this our written
12    security that we owe you and are in debt, in a clear and indubitable (manner), on behalf
13    of the same public services, that is, of the guards of the same vil[la]ge of Aphrodite, on behalf of the present
14    se[c]ond ind[iction], twelve gold coins of full weight by public standard, total
15    12 gold coins of full weight by public standar[d], and these we are ready to provide to you
16    in three installments of the public-(treasury), each in[s]tallment four gold coins
17    of full weight as stated, without any dispute or postponement,
18    of necessity, all our resources being held by you until
19    the repayment and completion of all the said gold; and
20    when questioned we acknowledged, manifestly, that (we do so) under mutual liability and as mutual guarantors and on behalf of
21    one another undertaking … and we have pledged to you, by way of pledge and by right of mortgage,
22    all things belonging to us and that shall belong (to us), generally and specifically, for the aforesaid (debt), under any obligation whatsoever, until the discharge of the aforesaid gold of the
23    same twelve coins of full weight by public standard; and upon this and all (terms), when questioned, we acknowledged (it). From Aurelii Pisalous son of Isidoros and Menas son of Hermauos, as aforesaid, (it) agrees,
24    all as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Psais son of Biktor, having been asked, wrote on their behalf, since they do not know letters.
25    Bik[tor] son of Phoibammon, elder, I bear witness to the debt, having heard (it) fr[o]m the
26    contracting parties. Fl[avius] Biktor, I bear wit[n]ess to the debt, having heard (it) from
27    the contracting parties.
28    Through me, Pilatos the notary, it was wri[t]ten.
29    docume[nt] … from P[isalous son of Is]idoros and Menas son of He[rma]uos
30    of gold, 12 coins of full weight (by) pub[lic standard].
