[TRANSLATION]
1     Dekos and partners, overseers of the market-tax (agoranomia),
2     to Panameus (son of) Pikos (son of) Nepheros. We have received
3     from you for the tax on the dealings of fishermen,
4     four drachmas, total drachmas 4. Year 23 of Antoninus
5     Caesar the lord, Pachon 16.
6     Another two drachmas, total drachmas 2. I, Dekos, have signed.
