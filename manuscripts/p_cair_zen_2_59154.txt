[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Zenon, greetings. Of the dry woods,
2     having loaded into a ship logs as many as possible and
3     the thickest, send the quickest [way] into [Alexandria],
4     so that we might be able to make use of [them] for the festival of the [Isiae].
5     Farewell. Year 30, Dius 3, Phaophi 23.
6     Year 30, Dius 18, Hathur 18.
7     Apollonius woods
8     [into] the Isiae.
9     To Zenon.
