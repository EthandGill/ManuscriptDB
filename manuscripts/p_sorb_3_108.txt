[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greetings, Nikandros Aenios of the third hipparchy, centurion […] [I am wronged by Petesou]chos son of Paitos and Pnepherôs son of Peteûrios from Mu[khis] …
2     [of my] allotment, and watering they left the allotment …
3     my [allotment], and because of this cause my new embankment was destroyed, on which I spent drachm[as] … [to the beside]
4     Demetrios the overseer I showed the damage so that … [I ask therefore you, King,]
5     if it please you, to order Diophanes the strategos to write to D[emetrios the overseer and, if what I write is tr]ue, to compel them both to pay me the damage [and] …
6     to be sent to Diophanes in order that by him to me [justice may be done concerning which through the peti]tion I call upon them. For this having happened, I will be through you, King, having obtained justice.
7     [Fare well.]
8     To Demetrios: having examined, explain [to us concerning these matters.]
9     [In year] …
10    Nikandros
11    against Petesouchus
12    and Pnepherôs
13    To Demetrios
