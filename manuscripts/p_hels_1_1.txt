[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Pt]olemy and Queen [Cleopatra his sister,]
2     [the Manifest] and Beneficent Gods, [greeting.]
3     From Asklepiades. I am wronged [by Petaus] … For when I, with others, had taken the contract for the sixth [of the gardens of]
4     Dikaiou in the Arsinoite nome in year 1 … [and Petaus the]
5     aforesaid sharing with me in [the same purchase to the fourth] part, concerning which we also wrote an oath [and] each of us subscribed in his own hand, and when [this man]
6     would not undertake to give surety for the fourth part [falling to him,]
7     but ordered [that one borrow from him]
8     whatever he might guarantee, and it was exacted on [our] behalf — [Asynchis my wife —]
9     concerning which I also wrote for him a contract [of loan of bronze]
10    of 7 talents, and when this had been completed we set up his surety … and of others instead …
11    … together with his apomoira (share-tax) …
12    … 5 talents of bronze, 1023 (?), so as to discharge
13    [this]. But now, harassing [us] and acting as a sycophant, he has made an outcry before the laokritai (native judges) against Asynchis my
14    wife, alleging that she had become surety, through the
15    contract of loan which I wrote for him together with my partners,
16    for the third part falling to me. Since therefore the loan
17    which he demands from my woman, instead of the surety which I gave on our behalf, is one which he has also recovered, but acting further as a sycophant
18    he has set her before the laokritai, I request — being one pressing and entitled — that before the chrematistai who judge revenue cases
19    I take and give what is just, and the
20    matters aforesaid being from the revenue of Philadelphos,
21    that you send my memorandum to them, so that, having dealt with it for judgement, they may summon Petaus,
22    and if we prove that he shared with us in the sixth
23    and that he received the surety on behalf of which we also wrote for him the aforesaid contract of loan,
24    the chrematistai may decide that he pay …
25    … [the] fourth part falling to him …
26    … they admit …
27    … [the] contract [of] loan …
