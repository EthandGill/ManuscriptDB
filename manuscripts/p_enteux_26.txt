[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting from Ktesikles. I am wronged by Dionysios and by Nike, my daughter.
2     For though I raised my own daughter and educated her and brought her to maturity, when I became disabled in my own body and unable to see with my eyes, she was not willing
3     to supply me with any of the necessities. When I wished to obtain from her my just due
4     in Alexandria, she begged me off, and in the 18th year she wrote me a royal oath
5     at the temple of Arsinoe Aktia, that she would give me twenty drachmas each month, working
6     for herself with her own body; but if she should not do so, or transgress any of the (terms) of the agreement,
7     she should pay me 500 drachmas or be liable to the oath. [But now, corrupted by Dionys]ios, who is
8     a debauchee, she does for me nothing of (the terms) of the agreement, despising me because of my old age and my present disability. I beg you, therefore, O king, not to allow me to be wronged by
9     my daughter and by Dionysios, the debauchee who corrupted her, but to order
10    Diophanes the strategos, having summoned them, to give us a hearing …
11    and to deal with the … who corrupted her, that Diophanes (treat him) as may seem good to him, and [Nike,]
12    my daughter, to compel her to do me justice in … [for if these things are done,] I shall not be wronged, but, having taken refuge with you, O king, [I shall obtain justice].
13    There was sent along Euphor…
14    Year 1, Gorpiaios 30, Tybi 13.
15    Ktesikles against Dionysios and
16    Nike his daughter, concerning a written agreement.
