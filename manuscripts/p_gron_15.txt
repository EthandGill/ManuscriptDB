[TRANSLATION]
1     Turbo to Apitis the most honored, many greetings.
2     You will do well having received through Onnophres, Onnophres, sailor, the bur[ial] of my mother which I convey to you, … perhaps you will do [this] with yourself until …
3     I myself arrive.
4     I pray you to fare well.
5     Turbo to Kolanthos the most honored, many greetings.
6     You will do well having received through [Onnophres, Onnophres]
7     sailor, [the] burial of my mother …
8     do until I [arrive]. Fare well
9     you I pray.
10    Deliver in Psonis to Apitis the veteran.
11    From Turbo the veteran.
12    To Psonis to Kolanthos son of Titus Aisatos.
13    From Turbo the veteran.
