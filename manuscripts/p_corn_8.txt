1     A … p … [moth]er
2     Aphr[o]disia of Ammonios, greetings. Which we leased from the public [treasury] concerning a village
3     Hephaistad[a] from those of the [adm]inistration's gene[m]tograp[h]ed holdings previously [Her]akl[eide]s
4     At the rate of wheat one-and-a-half, arouras two, and likewise concerning the same village from wasteland [a]rouras two, [for] the same [ar]ouras four we agree according to this hand[writ]ten [document] in common equally each according to the hal[f] [p]ortion to perform
5     The sowing of these for what time we leased, of the works all and seeds being toward us in common according to the half [p]ortion, on which we shall bear fruit
6     The fruit of the aforementioned arouras
7     Four in common equally according to the half.
