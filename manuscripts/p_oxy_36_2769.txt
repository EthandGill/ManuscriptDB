[TRANSLATION]
1     There acknowledge to one another Aurelius Philosarapis
2     son of Zoilos, his mother being Thaesis, from the city of Oxyrhynchus, nominated by the
3     district-secretary of the same city for the grain-collectorship of public wheat of the village of Tanais of the present
4     5th year, and Aurelius Sarapion also called Horion son of Sarapion, his mother being Eudaimonis, from the same
5     city: Aurelius Philosarapis, on the one hand, that he has made over to Aurelius Sarapion also called Horion the aforesaid grain-collectorship, on the terms that
6     he shall take over the public half-artaba measure and the public wheat measured at the collectorship, and shall issue to
7     those who measure the receipt-tokens, and shall deliver to the
8     strategos of the nome the customary collations and the
9     monthly accounts and the other requisitioned books
10    of the collectorship, and shall hand over to the shippers detailed [to] the same collectorship all the public wheat measured at it,
11    full, soundly and faithfully,
12    and shall take, in the name of the grain-collector, the customary receipts; and Philosarapis shall keep (him) unmolested and free from exaction and untroubled with regard to absolutely everything pertaining to the same collectorship; and Aurelius Sarapion also called Horion, on the other hand, being compliant to
13    Philosarapis, that he will do each thing concerning all the expenses of the collectorship as has been declared above. The agreement is valid, written in duplicate, so that each may have a single copy; and on questioning one another they acknowledged.
14    The 5th year of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Antonius
15    Gordianus, Pious, Fortunate, Augustus,
16    Pauni 9. I, Aurelius Sarapion also called
17    Horion son of Sarapion, have made the agreement
18    as set forth, and am [satisfied] concerning the
19    expenses … at the collectorship.
20    And each thing I will do …
21    … so …
22    Agreement of grain-collectorship.
