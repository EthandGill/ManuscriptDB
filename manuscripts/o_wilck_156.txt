[TRANSLATION]
1   Antiochos Kasianos and Arrius Pupli(anus) and Jo[ulius] M[a-]
2   ximos and those with them, overseers of the Sacred Gate of Syene, through
3   Phanophis the assistant. Paid: Petorzmethis son of Poueris,
4   mother Tisatis, for the poll-tax of the sixteenth year of Hadrian
5   the lord: seventeen drachmas (and) a half-obol, (and) ½ obol (keepers' fee) — total 17 dr. 1 ob.; and for
6   the 15th year, one drachma
7   [(and) an obol (and) 3 chalkoi]. Year 16 of Hadrian the lord.
