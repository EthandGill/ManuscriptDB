[TRANSLATION]
1     Choiak 11, a debt.
2     In the second year of Imperator Caesar
3     Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus,
4     Choiach 11, in Tebtynis of the Polemon
5     division of the Arsinoite nome.
6     Petesouchos son of Onnophris,
7     one of those from Talei, a Persian of the epigone,
8     about forty-five years old, with a scar
9     on the little finger of the left hand,
10    acknowledges to Zoilos son of Harmiysis, about forty-eight years old, with a scar on the right forearm,
11    that he has from him, Zoilos, from hand
12    from the house, as a loan, of new pure
13    unadulterated wheat one and one-sixth artaba, and
14    eight silver drachmas and
15    a third of an artaba of lentils
16    by the six-choinix measure of Talei, and that the acknowledger is under compulsion
17    to repay to
18    Zoilos all the aforesaid
19    in the month of Pauni of the present
20    second year, without any postponement
21    and pretext, the right of execution belonging
22    to Zoilos both against the acknowledger
23    and against all his belongings. Subscriber:
24    [L]ysimachos son of Kronion, about 58 years old, with a scar on the little finger of the left hand.
25    Petesouchos son of Onnophris, one of those from Talei,
26    I acknowledge that I have from Zoilos son of
27    Harmiysis a loan of wh[ea]t, one artaba
28    and a sixth, by the six-choinix measure
29    of Theogonis, and eight silver drachmas
30    and a third of an artaba of lentils,
31    which I [wi]ll [also repa]y in the month of Pauni of the
32    present second year, as
33    is set forth. Lys[imachos] wrote on his behalf,
34    son of Kronion, he not knowing letters. Zo[ilos]
35    son of Harmiysis: the acknowledgment has been made to me
36    as is set forth.
37    Registered through the record-office in Tebtynis.
