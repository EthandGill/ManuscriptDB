[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of Claudius] Mamertinus and Flavius Nevitta [the most illustrious.]
2     [To Gaius Julius] Leukadios, strategos [of the Oxyrhynchite,]
3     [from the Aurelii] Petros son of Agathinos and Ammonios son of Apion [and ] …
4     [and Sarapion] son of Ploutarchos and Dorotheos son of Arsinoos, [all councillors of the city of the Oxyrhynchites,]
5     [overseers of barley] of Alexandria. We acknowledge, [swearing the august divine oath of the]
6     [our master Julian], the eternal Augustus, to have received [and to have loaded into the subjoined boat, from] the produce of the 6th indiction, of barley, [new, clean, unadulterated and]
7     [unwetted and free of every] charge whatsoever, by the public measure [by the measurement that was ordered,]
8     [in all 701 artabas; and the cargo] we will carry down to most illustrious Alexandria … and to bring receipt letters of the delivery [so that we be blamed in nothing]
9     [or be liable to the] divine oath.
10    [Into] a private [boat] …
11    … of the pilot of the same …
12    … [the aforesaid of the] barley, 701 artabas, whose guarantor …
13    [Aurelius] Sarapion son of Ploutarchos, I have jointly received …
14    … seven hundred and one and …
15    701 artabas;
16    of which of townspeople, 1 artaba;
17    of villagers, 700 artabas.
