[TRANSLATION]
1     John, count, to Philoxenus, overseer of Peribleptus
2     Provide for the purchase of reeds for reed-working of the estate, 13th indiction, gold coins
3     twelve, amount to gold coins 12 only; I have noted gold coins twelve, amount to coins 12, to Philoxenus, overseer of Peribleptus.
4     Of year 150, 119, Mecheir 6, 12th indiction.
5     A memorandum concerning the price of reeds, coins 12, and concerning the price of bulls, coins 4.
