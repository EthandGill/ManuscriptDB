[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: ‘Signed by me, Telephus, the … year of Domitian the lord, Pharmouthi 5. ‘To Socrates, ex-gymnasiarch, and Antipater, keepers of the property record-office of the Arsinoite nome, from Satabous son of Pakusis son of Herieus, of Soknopaei Nesus in the division of Heraclides. I register up to the present time three-quarters of a half share of a house and yard situated in the same village, which I have bought from my mother Tabous daughter of Orsenouphis son of Satabous, of the said village, with her guardian her kinsman Papeis son of Papeis, at the price of 200 dr. of silver; I accordingly present this return. ‘I, Socrates, through Telephus, scribe, have entered it on the register. The … year of the Emperor Caesar Domitianus Augustus, Pharmouthi 5.’
