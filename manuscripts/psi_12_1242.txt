[TRANSLATION]
1     We concerning the festivals
2     of the birthdays of Dionysios we are arriving, and we hope them most pleasantly to celebrate, the
3     indeed as for external matters; for what is in our power certainly
4     pleasantly we will dispose of. Well then
5     would it be for you also to celebrate fitly the first birthday of Dionysios first-born child. We however
6     already toward sailing we were.
7     Greet the mother and Dionysios and Claudia and
8     Serapous.
9     Be well.
10    [T]o Antonia Tekoseis from Her…
