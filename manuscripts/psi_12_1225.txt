[TRANSLATION]
1     To Marcus Ulpius Pasion of those having served as market-overseers
2     priest-exegete and the Caesarean and the other prytaneis
3     from Didymus of Hierax of Protarchos
4     of Sosikosmeios also called Althaios, of those having served as epheboi in the sixteenth year of god Hadrian, and of his
5     wife Plutarout of Harpokration
6     a citizen with guardian the same Didymus. desiring
7     to enroll into those in the twentieth year of [the autocrat]
8     [Caesar] Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus, the pious
9     the son born to us from one another for ephebate
10    Menodoros also called Hierax, we ask you
11    to arrange the ones assigned to these matters, having received from us
12    a written declaration concerning the truth of the matters presented … to have authority for us [the things relating to the firstfruits] and ephebate of my aforementioned son Menodoros also called Hierax, and to order the kosm[etes] and the … siarch to receive him into those of the same year
13    having served as epheboi
14    a memorandum concerning ephebate of Menodoros also called Hierax
