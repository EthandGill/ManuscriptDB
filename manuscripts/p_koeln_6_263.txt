[TRANSLATION]
1     [Apollonius] to Metrodorus [greetings]. And previously to you I happen
2     having written [concerning] those crossing into Memphis beer-makers
3     according to custom, that … they-are-prevented by Nikheratos [having-ordered]
4     use… and through this reason having-additionally-owed
5     … the beer-maker to-withdraw still in Choiak, similarly moreover
6     [and] … standing she-the-one-having-received the sale on the transaction, concerning moreover these
7     [nothing] to me having-been-said, well to-be I-took again
8     [to-remind] you, in-order that not even in year ten [difference] might-come-to-be.
9     If moreover otherwise it-has-seemed, as also in beginning I-requested, if to you it-seems,
10    well you-will-do having-ordered to Nikheratos to-undertake-again
11    the sale.
12    Be-well. Year ten, Tybi nine.
13    to Metrodorus.
