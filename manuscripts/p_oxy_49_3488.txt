1     Dionysios who is also Petseiris [leased]
2     Sara… of those from the city of Oxyrhynchos … i (son) of Philiskos, of those from Pe… e … s of the Kynopolite, a Persian of the epigone, for two years, two soakings (waterings)
3     two, from the present third year
4     of the Emperor Caesar Vespasian
5     Augus[t]us, the (lands) belonging to him around
6     Thm[oith]oth[i]s of the upper toparchy:
7     from the holding of Pitholaos, ten arouras, so that in the present
8     year he sow these with vetch, at a fixed rent of wheat of
9     seven and a half artabas; and in the coming
10    year sow with wheat, at a fixed (rent) of wheat of
11    fifty-five a[rtab]as; and
12    … of the holding, another five arouras and an eighth — in the present year to sow these with wheat, at a fixed rent of wheat of
13    twenty artabas; and in the coming year to plant with vetch, at a fixed (rent) of wheat of
14    ten artab[a]s. And the lessee acknowledges that he has received from Dionysios
15    of wheat-seed ten artabas,
16    which he shall repay to him together with the rents of the coming year, the yearly
17    working of the embankments of the
18    ten arouras and their guarding being
19    (the responsibility) of the lessee, who shall also
20    hand these over worked
21    and sound in the coming year, or pay to Dionysios the agreed eighty drachmas of silver
22    eighty. And (let) all (be) free of risk,
23    of every risk, the public (taxes) on the land being
24    (the responsibility) of Dionysios,
25    who shall also be lord of the yearly crops
26    until he recovers his own. And the
27    lease being guaranteed, let the lessee repay to Dionysios
28    the yearly rents always in the month Pauni, and together with those of the coming year also the seed,
29    at the threshing-floors around the hamlet of Nigron, new wheat, clean, unadulterated, unmixed with barley, by the
30    four-choinix measure … oerios
31    or pay the price of each
32    artaba which he does not repay,
33    of copper drachmas … . And [let] the right of execution be
34    to Dion[ysios both] from the [les]see [and] from …
