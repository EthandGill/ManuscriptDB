[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the Aurelii] … [to] and to Serenus, eparchus, prytanis, and to [Ammoneinu]s
2     … of Agatheinus, both syndics, councillors
3     of the [splendid and] [most splendid] Oxyrhynch[ite] city
4     Aur[e]lii Eudaemon, also called Helladius, gymnasiarch, councillor of the same city
5     [and the] with me record-keepers of the same nome to the dearest, greetings.
6     You sent word to us [that you] had ordered Valerius Asterius, prefect
7     [p]rivate of Egypt, the belongings of Theothorus, exarch, promotus of the third legion, by decision of the most distinguished [du]ke, to be recovered in the treasury accounts. From which
8     having proceeded to examination through the recorded [documents] [in the]
9     record-keeper offices of the books, we declare [concerning] Theothorus
10    nothing … rho[n] to have possessed, nor at all to have been dispersed
11    through the books in the archives.
12    I pray you fare well, most beloved.
13    consul[ate] of our masters [Vale]rius Licinnianus
14    Licinnius Augustus and Flavius V[a]lerius Constantine
15    … … before the 6th of the Nones of Ju[ly]
