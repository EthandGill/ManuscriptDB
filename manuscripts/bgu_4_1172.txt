[TRANSLATION]
1   [To Protarchos.]
2   [From Princeps, and from Dionysios son of ...]
3   [a Persian of the epigone, and his wife Dionysia daughter of ...]
4   ...nios, with [her husband as guardian. There agree] Diony-
5   sios and Dionysia that they have [from] Princeps a loan, by
6   hand from his house, of seventy [Ptole]maic silver drachmas,
7   interest-free, which they will also repay within five months
8   from Hathyr of the present twenty-second year
9   of Caesar, without any delay; or they shall be im-
10   mediately subject to seizure and held until they pay
11   the [loan with the half-as-much-again penalty], and for the time overrun
12   the interest fixed by the edict, two drachmas (per mina); the right of ex-
13   ecution belonging to Princeps against both, who are
14   mutually surety for payment, and against either one, whichever
15   of them he chooses, and against all their
16   property, as if by legal judgment; and they shall bring no pleas
17   or (such pleas) shall be void. We request (registration). Farewell.
