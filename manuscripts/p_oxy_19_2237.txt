[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our master Flavius Anastasius the eternal
2     Augustus, for the 2nd time, Tybi 20, of the 6th indiction, in the city of Oxyrhynchus.
3     To Flavius Didymos the reverend prefect's-officer, son of him of the most excellent memory
4     Timotheos, from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, Aurelius
5     Theodoros son of him of the blessed memory Megas also called Didymos, notary, from the
6     same city, subscribing below in his own hand, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received
7     from your reverence on loan from hand to hand from her house, for my own
8     and necessary need, of gold plain imperial approved solidi
9     six in number by the private standard of the Oxyrhynchites at six keratia, that is, of gold 6 solidi at 6 keratia,
10    which, being free of all risk, I will of necessity repay
11    to your admirableness in the month Pachon of the present year 174 143 of the
12    present sixth indiction, without postponement. And if, the aforesaid
13    term being at hand, … I act in bad faith with respect to the
14    said debt, should you wish to give me another at our own request, I acknowledge
15    that for the time running on after the term I will furnish the lawful one-percent
16    interest until the complete repayment of the same debt, repaid to you by me
17    after the term, if I should so happen … or, the exaction being made by you from both me and from all my possessions, which are pledged
18    to the payment of this debt by way of security and right of mortgage. The bond, single,
19    written, is valid, and on being questioned I assented. I, Aurelius Theodoros son of M[egas, the] aforesaid notary, have received in principal of gold solidi six [at] six [ker]atia and I will repay at the [te]rm
20    [and] all that is written agrees with me [as is set out above, and] having subscribed in my own hand, on being [questioned, I as]sented.
21    I, Aurelius Philoxenos from his mother Heraeis, [witness] the bond as is set out above.
22    Bond of Theodoros the notary from the Oxyrhynchite, of gold 6 solidi at 6 keratia.
