[TRANSLATION]
1     Artemidorus to Zenon greetings. With Dionysodorus, the son of Dionysius, we left
2     two batiacium vessels in a case because we had obtained small change from him for travel expenses. Since then
3     we have sent the tokens, let them be given to them and retrieve the two [ba]tiacium vessels from
4     Dionysodorus. I have also written to him so that he may give [them] to you. Having received [them], if anyone
5     suitable comes by, give [them] so that he might carry [them] to us. Farewell.
6     Year 29, Audnaios 4, Choiak 4.
7     Artemidorus [regarding] batiacium vessels.
8     To Zenon.
