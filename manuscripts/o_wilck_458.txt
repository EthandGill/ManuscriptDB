1   Nepheros son of Pechoites paid,
2   for dike-tax of the Charax (quarter), year 2, 6 dr. 4 ob.; of others, 4½ ob. — total 7 dr. 2 ob. — and surcharges.
3   Year 2 of Domitian the lord, (month) Kaisareios 25.
4   Apollodoros son of H(erakleides), I have signed.
