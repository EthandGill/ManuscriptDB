[TRANSLATION]
1     Pichus and partners, chaff-receivers of the [metropolis], year 1
2     Psaiti [Senphthomonthos]. We received
3     from you your chaff of the produce of year 1 for the countryside
4     of the metropolis. Year 3 of Antoninus
5     Caesar the lord, of the month of Hadrian,
6     [day] 1. Horus I have signed.
7     Psenamounis Pautos I have signed.
