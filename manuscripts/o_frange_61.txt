[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Frange, who write concerning his matter
2     [to] Phibamon, saying
3     Since you came here to take
4     the Nile and your son, I have given him together with a [haircut/tonsure] to you; you said that I will … the water that was lacking
5     [I gave] him to you thus, wrongly to you, because you took
6     the word thus which my father Victor [said/says] to you
7     and the … I the water …
8     that will take him, him here, he said …
9     water here, we will take it, and your heart …, because you [neglected]
