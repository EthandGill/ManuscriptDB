r.2    Ἐπεὶφ ιϛ [ὀ]νόματος Σενθοτσύτου

[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into [the treasury] of villages, of the produce of the 15th year of Hadrian the lord
2     Epeiph 16, [in the name] of Senthotsytou
3     Maieurios, through Psenosírios Paonnōphrios, [for]
4     Agoroi, wheat artabas four, become wheat artabas 4. Marked, Pikōs.
