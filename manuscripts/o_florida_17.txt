[TRANSLATION]
1     Sentis to Proklos his brother, greetings; well
2     you did [this], brother, having given to Anchubi
3     the two [shipments], and write to me about the freight and
4     immediately I will send to you. I did not send to you, brother, meat so that
5     I might not take leave of you.
6     For the rest I ask you, lord,
7     honor me and come
8     with the Ethiopian.
9     let us be glad.
10    be well
11    not then otherwise will you do but if you love
12    me come
13    let us be glad.
