[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 18 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius,
3     Mecheir 27. Eudaimon son of Sarapion and partners, overseers, made (payment) at the bank of Titus Flavius Eutychides,
4     of the property of the house of the city of the Alexandrians,
5     formerly of Julius Asklepiades the philosopher, being around
6     the village of Euhemeria, for the rent of the 17th year, one talent
7     and four thousand drachmas, on condition that the
8     equivalent be given at Alexandria to the one
9     appointed over the garlands, total 1 talent 4000 drachmas.
10    Epeiph 8, the same (man) and partners likewise, for the rent of the 17th year,
11    one talent and one thousand four hundred drachmas, total as aforesaid 1 talent 1400 drachmas.
12    19th year, Phaophi 10, the same (man) and partners likewise, for the
13    rent of the 18th year, one thousand drachmas, total as aforesaid 1000 drachmas.
14    Hadrianos 8, the same (man) and partners likewise, for the rent
15    [of the 18th year,] one thousand eight hundred drachmas, total as aforesaid [1800 drachmas].
16    [Phame]noth 7, [the same (man) and] partners, for the rent
17    of the 18th year, two thousand drachmas, [total] as aforesaid 2000 drachmas.
18    Epeiph 5, the same (man) and partners, for the rent of the 18th year, two thousand drachmas,
19    (2000), total as aforesaid 2000 drachmas.
20    20th year, Phaophi 5, the same (man) and partners, for the rent
21    of the 18th year, one thousand … hundred drachmas, total as
22    aforesaid 1… drachmas.
