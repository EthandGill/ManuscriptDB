[TRANSLATION]
1     Amyntas to Zenon greetings. Zopyrus who is delivering to you the letter [is]
2     son of Sosigenes of Caunus; and we have also written to Apollo[nius]
3     concerning him, [we having been] requested by someone. You would do well, whenever you get Apollonius [at a] convenient [time], having introduced him, so that he might petition [concerning what he has gone away].
4     Farewell.
5     Of Amyntas concerning Zopyrus the
6     son of Sosigenes. Year 28,
7     Peritius 22, in Bubastis.
8     To Zenon.
