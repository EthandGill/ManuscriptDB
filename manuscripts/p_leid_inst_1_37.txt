# HGV-EN: (Translation: P. L. Bat. 25.37) Isidorus alias Asclepiades, ex-gymnasiarch, represented by Aelianus, greets Papus and his brothers. I have received from you on account of rents as partial payment eighty-four artabas of wheat = 84 art. of wheat, without prejudice to my claim on the other artabas of rents you owe me. In the 19th year of the Aurelii Antoninus and Commodus, the imperial lords, (month day).
