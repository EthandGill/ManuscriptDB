[TRANSLATION]
1     … Arsenios [son of Kallinikos]
2     … on which condition I was of course persuaded
3     … according to the laws of this sale
4     … of my heirs and successors
5     [and assigns] … [with every] guarantee against everyone
6     who may come forward … to (my) heirs or successors or assigns
7     … in my name or that of any other person whatsoever,
8     [at every time under compulsion, and if it should happen], which may it not, that any dispossession or dispute
9     arise concerning the said one-third [share] of the house, or concerning a part of it …
10    or ownership or right of any kind whatsoever, on the part of any person whatsoever, in the courts
11    or outside the courts, on this condition, that I the seller and my heirs
12    and successors of every kind, at my own expenses and outlays, will hold off
13    and drive away all [who come forward] against me and my heirs
14    or successors or assigns, or (else) forfeit the aforesaid price twofold
15    and all the expenses and the outlays that shall be expended both for improvement
16    and for building and for other [handiwork] and for a lawsuit, [if any should happen to] arise,
17    likewise twofold I will [restore], together with this sale being unbreakable
18    and unshaken forever, which I made with you for security,
19    [being valid and guaranteed everywhere] when produced, at my risk
20    and that of my heirs [and successors, from the resource of our] entire substance,
21    which is also pledged to you for this as if by legal judgment; and upon all these terms,
22    having been formally questioned, I acknowledged (it), I, Aurelius Arsenios son of Kallinikos, the aforewritten;
23    I made the present sale of the said one-third share of the house with all
24    its right, and I received from the aforesaid (parties) the price, that is,
25    two gold solidi less … carats, [two solidi less … carats] by the standard of Alexandria, and I will guarantee
26    this sale with every guarantee; and everything is agreeable to me as aforesaid.
27    I wrote on his behalf, having been requested, I, Flavius Ph…si…aios son of …y…k…om, from Panopolis.
28    I, Flavius Heuresios son of Victor, landholder, from Panopolis, bear witness to the present sale, having been asked by the party who made it.
29    I, Flavius Ioannes son of Megalos, from Panopolis, bear witness to the [present] sale, having been asked by the party who made it.
30    I, Flavius Zacharias son of Pannes, by God's help secretary, from Panopolis, bear witness to the present sale, having been asked by the
31    party who made it.
32    Written through the record-office of Marcus, by God's help notary.
