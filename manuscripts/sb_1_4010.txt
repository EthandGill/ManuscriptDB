[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 33, Epeiph 15. [It has been assigned] to the bank in the city of Crocodiles, presided over by Paniskos, of the twentieth encyclios
2     according to the document of Sarapion concerning the purchase written [by],
3     under which Apollonios the notary subscribes, Psennesis son of Senthotes of a bare place in the southern part of Pathyris, to whom [belongs] Thotoroutes Totoroutes […]
4     […], whose boundaries are set forth through the agreement, which they bought from Patoutes son of Harsiesis [for] one talent of bronze, tax […]
5     Paniskos banker.
