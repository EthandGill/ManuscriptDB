1     I the one Mena the monk of the place of the holy apa Apollo behold to the one George the monk of the place of ours because I entreated you and you gave these sums
2     Of whole gold I gave upon
3     A trust I entrusted to you of God almighty and their greeting of the lords they are many so that not to transgress against you to the strength of the safety to keep the safety there which is hidden in him
4     […] his peace of the portion of the days which are to come by Mena
