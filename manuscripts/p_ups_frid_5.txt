[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Apion also called Ptollion,
2     kosmetes (and) councillor of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, to Aurelius Hierax also called … and however you are styled, greeting. I acknowledge that even [now]
3     I have received from you, from hand
4     out of your house, the full price of wheat placed in deposit from the produce of the
5     present 1st year, of six hundred artabas — makes 600 artabas of wheat; which wheat
6     I am under compulsion to measure out in whatever
7     names you choose, during the summer
8     of the same present year, into
9     the public granary, and (to deliver) the deposit
10    clean of all (charges), giving it to you at the first measuring without delay; and if not,
11    I will pay you the price, at the time then current,
12    of the wheat in deposit, double, the right of execution being yours both against me and against
13    all my property, valid remaining the other agreement of mine which you hold for another five hundred artabas
14    of wheat, on all the terms it contains. Valid also is this handwritten note,
15    written in duplicate, produced anywhere and by anyone
16    producing it on your behalf. And having been formally questioned
17    by you, I gave my assent. Year 1
18    of the Emperors Caesars Titus
19    Fulvius Iunius Macrianus and
20    Titus Fulvius Iunius Quietus,
