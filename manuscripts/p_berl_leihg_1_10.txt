[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Eudemos, str]ategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2     [from Marcus An]tonius Titanianus, absent, through his agent Lucius Valerius Expeditus. The copy of the legal ruling (chrematismos) of Boukolos [the priest and] chief-justice, which I have delivered to you, is appended below, and I ask that an identical copy of it be conveyed to the
3     lawful guardian of [Terentia Geme]lla, Ignatius Niger, through an attendant, so that he may know that he is debarred from the property of Terentia Gemella that has been assigned [to me] and registered to me, just as the legal ruling
4     [contains. Year 5] of the Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Hathyr 29. Boukolos the priest and chief-justice
5     to the strategos [of the Herakleides] division [of the Arsinoite nome], greeting. A copy of the subscription made at the adjudication of the petitions
6     [is appended together with the] petition; I have therefore written to you, so that you may know. Farewell.
7     I noted it. Year 5 of the Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus,
8     … [Year 5] of the Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Apellaios 22, Phaophi 22. The judges-of-petitions,
9     [Titi Flavii Apo]llodoros and Poplianus, who adjudicated the petitions, the petition of Marcus Anton[ius Titanianus] having been read out and the conveyance indicated through it having been adduced, and the petitioner having sworn
10    [by the Emperor Caes]ar Trajan Hadrian Augustus that the matters declared through the petition are true and that nothing [contrary] has been transacted, we judged that a copy of the petition should be written and sent to the strategos of the Herakleides division
11    [of the Arsinoite nome], so that, having first given notice to those in possession of the registered property to withdraw
12    [from it within ten] days after these, he may carry out the entry-into-possession, in the manner appropriate to what has been ordered.
13    [Accordingly.] To Titus Haterius Nepos. Marcus Antonius Titanianus, in the current month,
14    [have had registered to me k]according to what was ordered, by concession through the record-office, the pledge-seizure and assignment of the property of the
15    … [foreign] col[lection of the tax-farmers of the] Arsi[noite nome] and the things mortgaged [to me] by it through the …
