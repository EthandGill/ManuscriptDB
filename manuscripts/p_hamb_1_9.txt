[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, Pachon
3     1 … . There paid to Theon, lessee of the license-tax on donkeys of the nome and
4     other taxes, through Maximos his assistant, Sotas son of Ptolemaios
5     the machinist of Prinkips, village-scribe of Theadelphia, for the license of the same
6     year, from the three horses which he has, on account of two horses, drachmas
7     sixteen, obols sixteen, total drachmas 16, obols 16; and on Mesore 9, other amounts
8     likewise for one horse, eight drachmas, eight obols, total drachmas 8, obols 8.
9     And in the 7th year, in the month Hadrianos, on the 7th, for the present 7th year,
10    from the three horses which he has, on account of one horse, eight drachmas
11    eight obols, total drachmas 8, obols 8; and on Mecheir 8 others likewise for one horse,
12    eight drachmas, eight obols, total drachmas 8, obols 8; and on Mesore 21 others
13    likewise for one horse, eight drachmas, eight obols, total drachmas 8, obols 8.
14    And in the 8th year, Pachon 30, the same man for the present eighth
15    year, from the three horses which he has, on account of one horse,
16    eight drachmas, eight obols, total drachmas 8, obols 8; and on Mesore 23
17    others likewise, on account of one horse, eight drachmas, eight obols
18    total drachmas 8, obols 8; and the same man on Phaophi 20 others for the past eighth
19    year likewise for one horse, eight drachmas, eight obols, total drachmas 8, obols 8.
20    In the ninth year of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius
21    Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, in the month
22    …, 20th. There paid to Theon, lessee of the license-tax on donkeys
23    of the nome and other taxes, through Maximos his assistant, Sotas son of Ptolemaios
24    the machinist of Chare…s, village-scribe of Theadelphia, for the license of the same
25    year, from the three horses which he has, on account of one horse, drachmas
26    eight, obols eight, total drachmas 8, obols 8; and on Pachon 10 others likewise
27    for one horse, eight drachmas, eight obols, total drachmas 8, obols 8; and on Epeiph 9
28    others likewise for one horse, eight drachmas, eight obols, total drachmas 8, obols 8.
