[TRANSLATION]
1   Harmiysis
2   from the Aphrodision of the Small Oasis
3   to Diogenes son of Sarapion, ...
4   former agoranomos, gymnasiarch-in-office
5   of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, temple-
6   warden of Tyche, greeting. I ac-
7   knowledge that I have received from you of sil-
8   ver two hundred and sixty drachmas
9   of capital, to which nothing has been added, at interest
10   of one drachma per each mina each
11   month from the present month
12   Pharmouthi. And I will repay the capi-
13   tal together with the interest on the thirtieth
14   of Phamenoth of the coming
15   tenth year of Aurelius Antoninus
16   Caesar the lord, without delay;
17   but if I do not repay you, I will pay (it) back with the
18   half-as-much-again, together with the interest equal to the dues for the over-
19   time, the same one-drachma interest for each
20   mina per month, the right of execution
21   belonging to you against both me and
22   all my property. And the
23   aforesaid two hundred
24   and sixty drachmas I have deposited for the joint-
25   purchase of hay to be stored
26   in the camel-stable which I hold on lease,
27   (that) of Similis in the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
28   which very hay it will not be permitted to me
29   to carry off nor to sell nor to mort-
30   gage until I repay you the capi-
31   tal and the interest, because it is pledged
32   to you. And if any peril
33   should befall the said hay, no
34   harm shall follow upon you, because
35   the money together with the interest
36   is free of all peril. Valid
37   is the handwritten document, written in duplicate,
38   producible everywhere.
39   Year 9 of the Emperor Caesar
40   Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus
41   Armeniacus Medicus Parthicus Maximus,
42   [Pharmouthi ..]
