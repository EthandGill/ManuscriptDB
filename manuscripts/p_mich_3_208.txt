[TRANSLATION]
1     ... [to Pan]esnuit the brother very many greetings.
2     [First of all] I pray you are in good health and I do your obeisance [to you] each day before the lord Sarapis.
3     [I want you] to know, brother, that I wrote to you concerning the toparch and you wrote back no reply to me. And if
4     [even if you are] unwilling, make [it] clear to me concerning all things pertaining to me. I greet my mother much and Thain
5     [your wife] and your unblemished children. All greet you [by name] — Heraiskos and his mother and
6     [...and] Theodorus and Kalathus. All greet you ...
7     I pray you are well.
