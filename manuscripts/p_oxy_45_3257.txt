[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters [Licin]nius Augustus for the 5th time and of Crispus
2     [the] most illustrious [Caesar] for the 1st time.
3     To the heirs of …us Valerius, through [M]atrinus their guardian,
4     from the Aurelii [Leon]ides son of Theon and Ammonios son of Kopreus, both
5     from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites. Of our own free will we undertake
6     to lease, for only the present 13th year = 11th year = [3rd] year, from the property
7     belonging to them around Ision Panga in the embankment-district of Nesla, by survey, five arouras formerly of Thonios the Younger so-called, for the sowing of flax-stalk, and we shall pay you as rent for [ea]ch aroura three silver talents and a thousand drachmas, all free of every risk, the public dues of the land and the [annonae] resting upon you the
8     landlords, you being masters of the crops [un]til you recover what is [ow]ed.
9     The lease being secured to us, of necessity
10    we shall pay the money rent in two months, Epeiph and …
11    of the same year, without delay, the right of execution being yours
12    against us, being mutually responsible for the repayment as is fitting. Valid is
13    the lease, and on being questioned we acknowledged.
14    In the consulship aforesaid, Hathyr 14. [O]ualerios Matrinos:
15    through me, Dionysios, I received the copy [of this].
