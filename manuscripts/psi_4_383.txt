[TRANSLATION]
1     Aristandros to Zenon, greetings. Theron, the one who has given you the letter, is one of our [people].
2     household member; and farming a certain estate and having arranged the things coming to the royal [treasury]
3     has been wronged by the ones working at the tax-office, about which you will learn from the petition written below to you. Well then would [you] do, since the peti[tion] has been transferred
4     to Apollonios, [requesting] him that he [will] make haste to [give him] justice as quickly as possible [and] reasonably
5     to be handled/settled. Farewell.
6     To king Ptolemy greetings, Theron. I am one of the farmers; and I having paid the remainder
7     of the tax produced into completion of the 38th year, having taken the ones from the bank the
8     from the tax-collectors writ[e], which the wine-seller having bought the wine carried to them, [are] reporting against me the deposit of the tax having ordered [it to be recorded] i[nto] the 37th year, me owing nothing
9     but having paid everything properly. Since therefore having erred the ones from the tax-office wrote the entry
10    into the 37th year, I request you, king, [to send] my petition to Apollonios the
11    dioiketes, so that, if I appear owing nothing into the 37th year, but having paid all the things produced, and this silver having arranged into the 38th year, as I write, he might order to transfer my
12    deposit into the 38th year, in order that having fled for refuge to you, the savior of all, I not be wronged,
13    but [may] obtain justice.
14    Farewell.
15    To Zenon
