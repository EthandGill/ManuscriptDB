[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lord Flavius Anastasius, the eternal Augustus and Emperor, and of Flavius Rufus the most illustrious, Thoth 5, of the first indiction.
2     Aurelius Achilleus son of Aphous, mother Aseneth, from the hamlet of Photinos
3     of the Hermopolite nome, to Flavius Achilleus, the devoted soldier
4     from the (city) of the Hermopolites — my previous mortgage [deed] being valid and secure,
5     the one in your possession, which also has its own strength and force until repayment — greeting. I, the aforewritten Achilleus, acknowledge through this
6     my written security, willingly and persuaded, without deceit and fear
7     and force and trickery and compulsion, that I have had and have borrowed from you,
8     the said Achilleus the soldier, for my own and necessary need,
9     from hand to hand out of [your] house, as capital, gold imperial approved solidi
10    six in number less fifteen carats, by the standard of Hermopolis, [total of gold 6 solidi less 15 carats]
11    which I shall repay [t]o you whenever you wish, without any [delay]
12    and dispute and litigation and lawsuit and every lawful [demurrer]
13    and pretext and pretense whatsoever, on condition that I [pay, on]
14    account of interest, for each solidus per month [a fourth] of a gold
15    carat, the time of the (interest-bearing) loan being reckoned [from this present]
16    day, which is Thoth the fifth of the [presen]t fir[st indiction]
17    until repayment of the same [six gold solidi less fif]teen [ca]rats without delay, [all] my present and future [property being pledged to you for this contract]
18    and my future [property] until repayment, generally [and]
19    particularly, as if by court judgment. [This] document [is valid and secure]
20    and, having been formally questioned, I assented. Aurelius [Achilleus, from the hamlet]
21    of Photinos, the aforesaid, have received the gold solid[i six less fif]teen [carats] as is set out. I, Aurelius Lilous … from the Hermo[polite],
22    having been requested, wrote on his behalf the writing, he not knowing (letters). [ ]
23    I, Aurelius Kollouthos son of Phoibammon, from the Hermopolite, [bear witness]
24    to the document and to the giving of the gold of the solid[i six.]
25    I, Aurelius Phoibam[m]on son of Aurelius, from the Hermopolite, [bear witness]
26    to the document and to the [giv]ing of the gold of the sol[idi]
27    six. I, Aurelius Ioannes son of Phoiba[m]mon, from the Hermopolite, bear witness to the document and [to the giving]
28    of the gold of the solidi [si]x.
29    Through me, Phoibammon, it was writte[n] …
