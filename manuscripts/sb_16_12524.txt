[TRANSLATION]
1     To Pappus, overseer and chief-guard of Theogonida,
2     from Sosinnikos son of Kallinikos, [a Macedonian].
3     Since there belongs to my brother Kallinikos a grandfather's vineyard
4     around the aforesaid Theogonida, with Kephalon son of Ammonios having a share in the water-tank, on the 12th of Epeiph of the 13th year, Kallias having attacked
5     from [the] [Kephalon] against the pumping-station of Kallinikos … and [grandfather's property] he tore down, preventing [water] [to pump] into the vineyard through which [pumping] was [agreed], knowing that my brother had
6     departed from life and that his children are orphans, attempting to rule over others' [property] improperly and contrary to what is [fitting], so that from
7     thirst [it] was dried up and [damage] followed
8     to the vineyard [not] [small] [damage]. wishing
9     and [to the] [sons] of my [older] [brother] … I petition, if it seems good,
10    …
11    a copy of a memorandum concerning the in Theogonida
12    vineyard of water-pumping.
