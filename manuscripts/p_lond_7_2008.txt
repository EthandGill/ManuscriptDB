[TRANSLATION]
1     Jason to Zenon, greeting. I have written to you
2     many times about the pasture-taxes
3     and the guard-tax on the swine victims,
4     and you have made no arrangement
5     nor requested a time within which we might pay.
6     But here we are being maltreated both by
7     the stewards and the collectors.
8     I have therefore resolved to sail down to
9     you bringing the accounts, so that, when Theophilos arrives, I may not be maltreated because of having given him a written undertaking. I have been assessed, since I made the reckoning, at 400 drachmas. But the debts are
10    impossible (to collect). I handed over the garlic-growers in Hephaistias for the pasture-tax, and
11    their loads, to Aristandros the steward. But Etearchos said that the things in
12    the land were his and that he himself had supplied (them), and that, as soon as
13    I should recover, he said, both the rent and the outlay,
14    if anything is left over, you may recover it. And we
15    therefore released them.
16    The sowing of the sesame I inspected on the 9th
17    of Phamenoth, and the growth was sparse.
18    When I rebuked Eudemos, he said that you
19    had written to him to do what was ordered
20    by Herodotos, but that Herodotos
21    would not include
22    him in the sowing
23    nor allow (him) to follow up
24    what was being done, and likewise also Menitas.
25    Eudemos said that there had been sown into each aroura 4½ choinikes of seed. The rest
26    Satyros will report to you;
27    for he has inspected everything.
28    Timokles, one of the elders, reported to us
29    that the rest of the elders in the Quarters of Dinneas
30    owe you
31    [what] you spent on the canal,
32    amounting to 56 drachmas 1 obol; on the understanding that, if we reckon with them and it comes to an agreement, there shall be remitted
33    the share falling to him
34    of the 56 drachmas 1 obol, (namely) 6 drachmas 4 obols. They have therefore reckoned and agreed in writing to pay it toward the
35    money-taxes of the 36th year. But if
36    we pay (it), we shall set it off against them toward the rent of the 39th year.
37    Farewell. Year 39, Phamenoth 10.
38    To Zenon.
