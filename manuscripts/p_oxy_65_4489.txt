[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Imperator Maximianus Augustus
2     for the 5th time and Maximianus the most illustrious Caesar for the 2nd time.
3     To Aurelius Horion son of Theon, representative of the illustrious and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites for the present 13th year and 12th year and 5th year,
4     from Aurelia Helene daughter of Horion, her mother being Thermouthion, from the
5     same city, acting without a guardian by the right of children. I wish to have registered for the first time, at the census-post of the quarter of Poimenike, the (son) born to me from my late husband, deceased,
6     Aurelius Silvanus son of Besammon, his mother being
7     Isis, from the same city, my son Aurelius Ammon,
8     of the twelve-drachma class, of gymnasial rank, being toward the present 13th year and 12th year and 5th year 13 years of age;
9     wherefore I hand in the memorandum, requesting that he be enrolled
10    through the register of minors registered by you
11    in the class of those of the same age, as is proper; and I swear
12    the oath customary to the Romans that I have not lied. Year 13 and year 12
13    of our lords the Imperatores Diocletianus
14    and Maximianus, the Augusti, and year 5 of our lords
15    Constantius and Maximianus, the most illustrious
16    Caesars, Mesore 12.
17    I, Aurelia Helene, have handed in and have sworn the oath
18    as aforesaid. Aurelius … , I wrote on her behalf, since she does not
19    [know letters.]
