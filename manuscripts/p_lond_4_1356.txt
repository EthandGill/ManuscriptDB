[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name] of God. Qurra son of Sharik, governor,
2     to [Basi]l[ei]os administrator of the village [of A]phr[od]it[o. We give th]a[n]k[s]
3     [to God,] and after these things. The constitution and correction and
4     [comp]l[et]ion of the public (taxes) of the district, after the service of God,
5     [is (a matter) of] the overseer of the pagarchy being
6     … untiring, receiving without delay the approaches
7     [of all those of] his administration and apportioning to each one
8     [with] the fear of God his (just) right; nay rather also
9     [trying to ma]ke equal the apportionment by which are distributed
10    [the ext]raordinary (levies) and the requisitions of the public (treasury). On receiving
11    [therefore the pre]sent letter, devote yourself to the (matters) of your administration
12    [in order to] hear the things said by them and to judge to each one
13    [the (just) thin]g, not shutting yourself off and … them
14    … . And appoint the headmen and chief men
15    [of each] estate and command them to choose
16    [tru]stworthy and knowledgeable [men], and these being chosen
17    [on oath,] entrust to them the apportioning of the apportionment
18    [of each es]tate according to (its) capacity, contriving to be found
19    [yourself a t]rustworthy overseer of the pagarchy and on oath [to ma]ke
20    [such an] apportionment; and this being completed, send (it)
21    [to u]s, making indeed a copy of such and keeping (it) by yourself,
22    [writing] to us the name and patronymic and by estate
23    [of those who ma]de such an apportionment. — For you shall be
24    [aw]are that, if we find any estate at all weighted down
25    [beyond (its) cap]acity, or even lightened beyond what was just for them to be assessed,
26    [or even (if)] an estate is unable to give (according) to the assessment
27    … in the apportionment now made by them
28    [we lay upon] those who apportioned, together also with the overseer,
29    [a penal]ty which they are not able to bear, fining them, if any
30    [such] estate is [unab]le and does not find (means) to give. Moreover,
31    [read] to them this present letter of ours, attesting beforehand
32    [to them, so a]s to have the fear of God before (their) eyes and
33    [without partia]lity to apportion the said apportionment.
34    [Do not se]nd it to us until you have looked closely into
35    [it], and if indeed they kept the (just) right and the equality
36    [you shall write] to us about this. But if again you find them
37    [havi]ng lightened or even weighted down, likewise again
38    [you shall write] to us, (telling) how they conducted (themselves). Written the month Pharmouthi 20, 8th indiction.
39    [Qurra son of Sha]rik, governor, to Basileios administrator of the village of Aphrodito.
40    [ Month ][P]achon … 9th indiction; it was brought through Ubai[d] the courier [concerning] the … [ap]portionment.
