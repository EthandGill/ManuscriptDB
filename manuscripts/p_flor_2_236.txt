[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapammon to Heron[i]nus the
2     most honored, greetings.
3     I sent Apollonius
4     and Claudius to supervise the vintage of Chrysochus. Others follow with
5     these and come down from night. Therefore harvest carefully. I pray that you fare well, most honored one.
6     Year 13, Mesore 23
