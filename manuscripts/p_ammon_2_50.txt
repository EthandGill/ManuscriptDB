1     In the consulship of our lords Diocle[t]ian for the sev[ent]h time
2     and Maximian for the sixt[h time], the Augusti.
3     To Aurelius Chrysippus the most eminent censitor of the Panopolite nome,
4     from Aurelius Horion son of Petearbeschinis, chief-prophet of the temples
5     first-in-rank (protologima) of Panopolis and of the subordinate temples.
6     According to the divine [o]rder of our masters Diocletian
7     and Maximian the Augusti, and of Constantius and Maximian
8     the most illustrious Caesars, I declare with you that I possess
9     about the metropolis and the nome, from common land of the priests of the five tribes, on behalf of (my) share of the prophet-office, the under-written (parcels). They are as follows:
10    Of the metropolis:
11    bed (koite) 243, of the allotment of … of an olive-and-date plantation,
