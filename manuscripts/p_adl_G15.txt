[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year 15] … [in Pathyris, in the office of Hermias the agent]
2     [a]ct[ing for Paniskos the ago]r[anomos. Horos lent,]
3     son of Horos, to Horos son of Nechoutes, about 50 years old, [of medium build, honey-skinned,]
4     somewhat bandy-legged, balding in front, long-faced,
5     straight-nosed, with the left ear pierced, of wheat forty-
6     six artabas, of barley sixty-nine, of equal (measure),
7     interest-free, the repayment of which [let him make] in the month Pachon of the same
8     15th year, [and] let him pay back for each [artaba] of wheat which he does not repa[y]
9     two thousand four hundred drachmas of copper, of barle[y]
10    one thousand four hundred, and a seventh part of all the produce; and let the borrower repay this loan
11    to Horos in the month
12    Pachon of the same year, [free] from everything and
13    delivered to (his) house [in] the fields, at [his own]
14    expense, by the measure with which he also received it;
15    and if he does not repay within the appointed
16    time, or does not act as has been written, let him pay back in the following month forthwi[th]
17    one-and-a-half times, or the price that shall be
18    in the market, and let the right of execution belong to Horos against the
19    borrower and against any one and each
20    of them and against whomever he may choose and out of the
21    property belonging to him, all of it, for the one exacting payment
22    just as by legal judgment.
23    Hermias the agent of Paniskos, I have transacted (this).
24    Horos son of Horos lent to Horos son of Nechoutes.
