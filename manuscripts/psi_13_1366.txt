r.2    εἰς ἀννώνας τοῦ βικαρίου

r.11   καὶ τὰ ἄλλα τῆς ιε ἰνδικτίονος.

[TRANSLATION]
1     … you displayed to him what he declared, namely the price of the given barley
2     into the rations of the vicar
3     and the wine and the silver payments from double and the
4     tirocinium and the allowance
5     and the other things that he declared.
6     but only do not neglect,
7     my lord son, and Diogenes
8     [the son] of Rufus, demand [payment from]
9     if anything he owes from the remaining [amounts],
10    as he declared, and the linen
11    and the other things of the 15th indiction.
12    … I wish you to be well
13    … [for] many years.
