[TRANSLATION]
1     Consulate of Flavius John the most illustrious, Choiak 6, 2nd indiction
2     John [son] of Collouthus, mother Archontia, deacon, from village Enseu
3     of the Hermopolite district to Flavius Silvanus [son] of Phoebamuon, from soldiers
4     of the formerly in Hermu city number of the Mauri, greetings. I agree
5     that I, the aforementioned John, have received and been paid by your
6     excellency, the mutually agreed upon complete and worthy
7     payment of price of new wine, must, of measures eighty, it amounts to wine measures eighty
8     [which measures eighty] I shall repay to you in the time of the vintage
9     [in Mesore month fresh excellent] produce of the third distribution in wine new fine
10    [and pleasing each] measure from quarts five of these
11    [all light, provided by] me. And if vinegar or unfinished
12    [or soured [wine be found from the] same wine until Phamenoth month and itself
13    [of the same indiction] so that I exchange for you instead of them in fine
14    [wine without] any postponement and objection and judgment and
15    [suit and pretext. And] until the same deadline if not I repay
16    [you the aforementioned meas]ures eighty ready I have to provide to you
17    [for their] price [in] gold currency [two] minus twelve carats
18    holograph John Collouth[us] …
