[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios to Panakestor greetings. [Concerning the wood]
2     we are not able to send [it] to you, but you also
3     from the nome itself purchase-together and to the
4     Memphis [district] send, likewise also [to the] Aphrodito[politan] and Herakleopolis[tan]; and from wherever else
5     you are able, purchase-together, of-which you have need.
6     Farewell. Year 29, Hyperberetaios 20, Thoth 21.
7     25
8     wood.
9     To Panakestor.
