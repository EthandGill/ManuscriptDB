r.7    [ἀρούρης ἑκ]άστης τῷ αὐτῷ τρόπῳ. εἰ δὲ εὑρεθῇ παραινα ἀναδώσομέν σοι τὸ δημόσιον … ττοντος. ἐγράφη μηνὶ Μεχεὶρ κε ια ἰνδικτίονος.
r.8    διʼ ἐμοῦ Σεργίου συμβολαι[ογ]ράφου ἐσημειώθη.

[TRANSLATION]
1     We received and were paid, we George son of Jeremiah and Abramios son of Apphous from village Tin of the Arsinoite nome, from you Ampeloudos
2     wife of the blessed John, who was chartulary, the public tax owed by you in the field of the same village
3     of one and one-half aroura, which is to say concerning the produce of the present eleventh indiction as complete, according to the survey made
4     at your village, for the present survey, which is for each one aroura of wheat one and one-third artaba, and
5     … of gold ke[ratin] eight and one-half … and … of us so that it be surveyed
6     … separate from the same one and one-half [ar]oura and [you] give back to us the public
7     [aroura each] in the same manner. But if there be found a [default(?)] we will give back to you the public … being. It was written in the month Mechir 25 of the 11th indiction.
8     By me Sergius, notary, [it] was attested.
