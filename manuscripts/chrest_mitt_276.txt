[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of our lords Flavius Valerius]
2     [Constantine Au]gustus for the [6th time and Con]stant[ine the] most Eminent [Caesar.]
3     [To Aurelius He]rmeias also called Silvanus, son of Olympios, [councillor] of the city of Hermes,
4     [from the Aurelii] Hales son of Horion, his mother being Isidora,
5     [and Pino]ution son of Pieus, his mother being Sophia,
6     [and …]os son of Ammonios, his mother being Tanyris,
7     [and] Amonios son of Pieus, through us the
8     present (parties), Hales and Pinoution, of
9     all from the most splendid city of Hermes.
10    We wish, willingly and of our own choice, to lease from you, for the 9th indiction only,
11    the five arouras belonging to you around Thynis, from the allotment of Asklepiades, for the sowing
12    of wheat, at a rent for each aroura of the (land) under
13    sowing, according to the survey that shall appear,
14    of four artabas of wheat; and the rent collected we shall pay and measure out from our joint liability or from whomever of us you may choose,
15    for the discharge of the same [four] artabas,
16    new wheat, [purified, unadulterated, free of refuse,]
17    sifted, by [the Athenian] measure,
18    at the threshing-floors of the metropolis, [all the public dues]
19    and apportionments of every kind
20    and impositions and corn-levies being upon you
21    the landlord. The lease is valid, and on being asked the formal question we agreed.
22    In the aforesaid consulship, Phaophi 28.
23    I, Aurelius Hermeias also called Silvanus, have leased (it) out as is set out above.
