[TRANSLATION]
1     To Phanias, one of the First Friends
2     and strategos and (official) in charge of the revenues,
3     from Onnophris, son of Petemounis, a royal cultivator (basilikos georgos) of those from Oxyrhyncha.
4     While I was cultivating jointly with Mestasytmis
5     also called Pates, son of Petemounis, my
6     brother, 12 arouras of royal land
7     around the village — but in the 32nd year,
8     (in) Epeiph, my aforementioned brother
9     having departed this life, neither seed
10    nor anything else at all having left behind,
11    whence I was compelled to take over all the land,
12    and while I was engaged in the irrigation
13    and the labors, the woman who was the
14    wife of Mestasytmis,
15    Aunchis, being mischievous and wishing to shake me down (extort)
16    contrary to your p[o]licy
17    and to what is proper, has submitted
18    against me memoranda (claiming) that I have carried off from the (property) of Mestasytmis
19    10 artabas of wheat and 10 artabas of fenugreek,
20    there being no such thing; and through the miscalculation which she has made it has come about that I am unreasonably being drawn away from
21    the royal land; and further also,
22    having come upon (it), she has sealed up a house of mine
23    in which (there is) fenugreek to (the amount of) 2 artabas. For which reason,
24    having made my refuge to you,
25    I ask, if it seem good, to instruct
26    (that it be) written to Sarapion the overseer (epistates)
27    to send her off to you, so that I
28    may obtain justice and she be reprimanded. And when this has come about, I shall be
29    helped.
30    Farewell.
