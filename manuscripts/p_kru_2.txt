[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity, the Father and the Son
2     and the Holy Spirit. Written in the month of Phamenoth 10, of the 3rd indiction, in the presence of the comes
3     son of Chael the administrator of the fort of Djeme.
4     We, Tagape daughter of Solomon and Esther daughter of Taphane the daughter,
5     she too (daughter) of Solomon, acting as representative of all her brethren according to their names, men of the fort of Djeme of the nome of the city of Hermonthis, providing afterward
6     a subscriber to subscribe for us to this written deed of sale,
7     inviolable and unshakeable by the laws, and moreover confirming it through trustworthy witnesses,
8     these who shall testify to it for us according to our own request, of ourselves —
9     we write to you, son of the blessed Senouthios, the man of our fort, persuaded, without any guile or fear or violence or deceit or fraud or compulsion or extortion, with no compulsion laid upon us, but
10    with all good and fair purpose; and we swear afterward by the holy Trinity,
11    consubstantial, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
12    and by the welfare of our sovereigns the kings, these who rule over us.
13    Now we sell to you, you yourself, son of Senouthios,
14    that which exists, from today's day and forever, it coming after us, the portions of ground which (belonged) to Solomon our father, until they
15    are in the hands of Daniel son of Saul and his heirs;
16    that you may become the holder of their lordship, you and your children and those who come after you, according to the way I will reveal their boundaries to you, surrounding (it) on every side: the first ground and the second, these are their boundaries — south Senouthios son of Phoibammon, the north … na …
17    the west, the corner up to Taf son of David; the east, the door of the master.
18    Behold these are the boundaries of the one portion of ground, the limit of it; likewise the east portion
19    of the further ground; these — the west, Iakob son of Andreas, the east, the door of the master; the south, Esidre the tribune; the north, Solomon (son of) Moses.
20    Behold these are the boundaries on its four sides. And the price upon which we agreed
21    with one another, and it pleased us, of the portions two — that is three solidi
22    of approved pure gold by the weight of the fort of Djeme,
23    which comes to 3 of gold, the complete price; it came to our hands, hand into hand, full
24    by the weight of the fort of Djeme. So therefore it is to you, you yourself,
25    son of Senouthios, that you shall come in and take possession and dispose of those portions of the places which I revealed to you above, when I begat them
26    for you, to beget them again for you, to manage them, to administer them,
27    to beautify them, to grant them, to bestow them,
28    to give them as a gift, to give them away, to take them back, to do with them in every manner that is in your hand, to leave them to your children and your heirs; that no man shall be able to come out against you ever, neither we
29    nor son nor son's son nor brother nor brother's son
30    nor son of one nor offspring of offspring. And whoever shall dare to come out
31    against you — first, that one shall profit nothing, primarily,
32    being a stranger to the holy oath of the Christians, the Father
33    and the Son and the Holy Spirit; and he shall pay the account of a penalty
34    of twenty solidi of gold; and afterward he shall come in subject
35    to this deed of sale which we drew up, it being secured for you; we have drawn up this written deed, valid, having force in every place where it shall be produced,
36    and let them not question us; and we agree.
37    I, Merkourios son of Phoibammon, consent to the deed of sale
38    according to the way it is written.
39    I, Mena (son of) Samuel, give witness to the request of Agape.
40    I, Petros the least, the south one, son of the blessed Abriam,
41    give witness. Makare son of the blessed Kosma, gives witness. I, Petros, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know how to write.
42    We, Mase son of the blessed Pesnte and Stephanos,
43    and David son of the blessed Karakos, we give witness.
44    Petros the least, the south one, I wrote on their behalf, since they do not know how to write.
45    I, Senouthes son of the blessed Chmntsneu, least presbyter,
46    from the fort of Memnonion, wrote.
