[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonios, strategos of the Arsinoite nome, division of Herakleides,
2     from Petaus, village scribe of Ptolemais Hormou
3     and other villages.
4     Being requested by you for names for the
5     guarding of the produce brought in
6     from the fields and to compel it to be carried to the threshing-floors
7     and to watch over it while it is on the threshing-floors
8     until what is owed to the treasury
9     be measured out, and to make provision for the
10    wheat to be selected of barley, and (to oversee) the
11    winnowing and the collection of the wheat,
12    and to seal up the granaries and
13    to press on the shipments and to attend to the transport down, I give
14    the men subscribed below, who are men of means and
15    suitable, on condition that they also make provision for the
16    peace. And they are:
17    Of Ptolemais Hormou:
18    Petheus son of Hatres, his mother being Didyme, called Pkanpesoul, having means of 800 drachmas
19    Apynchis son of Pabos, his mother being … , likewise of … drachmas
20    … as, called Alektoras, likewise of 800 drachmas
21    Phaseis, fatherless, his mother being Taureis, called the carpenter, [likewise of … drachmas]
22    … his mother being Ischyriaine, likewise of 800 drachmas
23    Ischyrion son of A…s son of …y…, likewise of 700 drachmas
24    Of the village of Syron, likewise:
25    Sexstillis son of Miysis, likewise of 700 drachmas
26    … likewise of 800 drachmas
27    Pathynis … likewise of … drachmas
28    Maximos son of Sokmetis, called Tene, likewise of 800 drachmas
29    Sokmas son of Paaus (son of) Ilas, likewise of 500 drachmas
30    Of Kerkesoucha Orous, likewise:
31    Ischeis son of Horos, his mother being Thaseis, likewise of 800 drachmas
32    Marres son of Phaneses, his mother being Taonnophris, likewise of 700 drachmas
33    Phanesis son of Horos, his mother being Charitous, likewise of 800 drachmas
34    Orsenouphis son of Orsenouphis, his mother being Sambous, likewise of 800 drachmas
