[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavii Zeno and Postumianus,
2     the most illustrious, Mesore 19, of the 3rd indiction.
3     Aurelia Lia, daughter of Dioskoros, mother Lia, from the hamlet of Iliou of the Hermopolite nome, to Aurelius Herakleon son of Hierokles, the venerable, councillor, riparios of Hermoupolis the most illustrious. Whereas before this [of the]
4     year … having become (in need) I borrowed from you four gold solidi,
5     [for] security of the debt I mortgaged to you as visible … pledges
6     … eron … from all the restitution of the same debt for the sake
7     [and n]ow I acknowledge, swearing by the Almighty God and by the
8     [piet]y and victory of our masters Theodosius and Valentini[an]
9     the eternal Augusti, … (that I shall) have, on the twenty-fifth
10    of … Mesore of the present third indiction, the
11    four gold solidi to furnish to you and to take back from you
12    … the pledges; but if I do not do this, on condition that I no longer seek out
13    … the pledges and (that I) shall give, for condemnation and transgression,
14    one gold solidus, and (that) nothing has been falsely sworn. The acknowledgement is valid and sec[ure],
15    and on being formally questioned I gave my assent. I, Aurelia Lia daughter of Dioskoros, the aforesaid, made the
16    acknowledgement and I consent and am persuaded by all the (terms) written herein and I swore
17    the divine oath, as is set forth above. I, Aurelius P[i]noution son of Annes, from Antinoopol[is the most illustri]ous, wrote for her, since she does not know letters.
18    Through me, Biktor, it was written by S…ion the assistant.
