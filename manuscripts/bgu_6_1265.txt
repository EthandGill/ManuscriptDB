1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Berenike, the Benefactor Gods, in the ninth year, the priest being he who is in Alexandria of Alexander and of the Savior Gods and the Sibling Gods and the Benefactor Gods and
2     the Father-loving Gods, the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos being she who is
3     in Alexandria, in the month Dystros, in Tholthis of the Oxyrhynchite (nome).
4     Menonides, a Persian of those of Philon, a private person, acknowledges that he has from
5     Aristolochos son of Stratios, a Thracian of the epigone, and from Straton, a Macedonian, a thirty-aroura cleruch of those not yet under a commander, one hundred artabas of wheat for the rents of his allotment
6     of the eleventh year, according to the contract of the lease of the allotment which is deposited with the contract-keeper
7     Theokl…on … of those of Philon, a private person; and let Menonides hand over to Aristolochos and Straton this grain for the
8     rents of …; but if Menonides does not hand over to Aristolochos and Straton this grain
9     … in whatever manner, or some royal
10    hindrance arise so that this grain is not handed over to Aristolochos and Straton
11    by Menonides, let Menonides pay to Aristolochos and Straton, as the price of each artaba of the wheat,
12    ten drachmas, and let the right of execution belong to Aristolochos and Straton against Menonides, exacting according to the edict. And let this contract be valid wherever it is produced. Witnesses.
