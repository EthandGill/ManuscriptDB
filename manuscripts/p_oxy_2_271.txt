1   Copy. Year second of Nero Cla[udius C]aesar Augustus Germ[anic]us Imperator,

9   se[c]o[n]d year of Nero Claudius Caesa[r Augus]tus Germanicus Im[pe]rator,

12   through th[e] same record-office, in the ep[agom]enal (days) of the Kaisareios month of the
13   first year of Nero Claudius Cae[sar] Augustus Germanicus Imperator;
14   and that she has further ceded to him likewise the right which she herself had ce-

16   of the two hundred silver (drachmas), … Pa[pon]tos …
