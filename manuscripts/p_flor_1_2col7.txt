1     [α]να …
2     [Flavius Pa]ni[sk]os also called Lo[ngo]s, strategos of the Hermopolite (nome).
3     [Of the re]port given to me by the village-headmen
4     [of the village of En]seu through the brigand-hunters who hand in those registered through it (the report) for the
5     headmanship [in] their stead, a copy is publicly
6     posted so that all may know and that those reported may keep hold of the things entrusted to them
7     soundly and faithfully, so that in no respect may they be blamed.
8     I have noted it.
9     Year 12 of our lord Gal[li]enus Augustus,
10    Epeiph 3.
11    To Flavius Paniskos also called Longos, strategos of the Hermopolite (nome),
12    fr[om] the Aurelii Tyrannos son of Herma[po]llon and Pathotes,
13    … both village-headmen of the village of Enseu, through us
14    the Aurelii Pollon son of Patho[tes a]nd Horos son of Hatres, both brigand-hunters, [an]d the [res]t through us who are present. We give and re[po]rt the undersigned (as) village-headmen, on condition that they take up the
15    duty from today, which is [the 3rd] of Epeiph of the pres[en]t
16    12th year, being well-off and suitable, at the ris[k] of us
17    and of all those remaining in the same village, on the basis of mutual security, for whom we ourselves also stand surety. They are:
18    Paesis son of Kollouthos, his mother … es,
19    aged about 45, having means [of] 2000 [drachmas].
20    Potamon son of Paesis, his moth[er] … esios,
21    aged about 38, having means [of] 2000 drachmas.
22    Farewell.
23    Year 12 of Imperator Caesar [Publi]us Licinius
24    Gallienus Germanicus Maximus, [Piou]s, [Fortu]nate,
25    Augustus, Epeiph 3. The a[foresaid vi]llage-headmen
26    through us who are present, [the brigand-hu]nters,
27    have handed (this) in. A[urelius] …
28    … os wrote on their behalf, [being illiterate].
29    I have noted it.
30    I, Tyrann[os the attendant, having publicly posted (it), reg]istered (it).
