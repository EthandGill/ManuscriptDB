[TRANSLATION]
1     In the opening of the letter I greatly do obeisance to and salute t[he]
2     your admirable Brotherhood, praying to the Lord God to preserve
3     it and (grant it) to fare well, and that I may be deemed worthy to do obeisance to it in person h[ere].
4     For the Lord God knows how I give thanks and pray for it, and how [moreo]ver … the holy and good name into the midst of meeting, the prayers …
5     of it, I tell to the common master, the most glorious illustris and anti-landlord, … …
6     of the (woman) found in the same, remaining … by your good zeal
7     to make known to it the truth, this is easier … and the Lord God knows,
8     master. And I beseech it, the … … to show toward me
9     and to prepare the customary little (gifts) for the defensor in office at the time
10    and at present to be given to Basilei[os] the tabularius, so that even if a few of them
11    he may send to me here, and I shall as usual give thanks to your admirable Brotherhood.
12    Pardon me, master, since your slave, my son, wrote this present
13    letter. Having written these things I greatly do obeisance to a[nd] salute your
14    admirable Brotherhood until (we) meet.
15    [ ][T]o my master, in all things admirable, worthy of all honour and obeisance, genuine friend and brother George, comes, chartularius and administrator, Menas, with God,
16    defensor of the Kyno[p]o[lite]. …
