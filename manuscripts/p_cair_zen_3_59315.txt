[TRANSLATION]
1     Pekyusis to Zenon, greetings. [He has written] to us Aristandros from the city so that I might gather for the tax-collector drachmas 490. [You know] that I have spoken with you concerning 160 copper obols for
2     the soldiers whose allotments [Onnophris] farms. If then it seems good to you, you will do well
3     by writing to [him] and [helping] us to cross off the 160 copper obols [as] 1 drachma into the
4     490 drachmas as you granted. As for sesame, they do not wish to divide
5     those from Heraclides unless Heraclides writes. Give orders to Heraclides
6     so that he might order the handlers [to measure] according as Onnophris provides the division.
7     Farewell. Year 36, Thoth 20.
8     To Zenon.
