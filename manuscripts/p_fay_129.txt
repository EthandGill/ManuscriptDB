[TRANSLATION]
1   Greetings, my most honored lord.
2   I met with Apollos,
3   and he undertook by all means
4   to come down on the elev-
5   enth and to make the de-
6   livery. And he asks that it
7   take place in your presence.
8   So that you may know, I report
9   to you. I pray for the health of all your house.
10   Deli(ver) to Serenos.
