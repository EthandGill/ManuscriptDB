[TRANSLATION]
1     V[a]lerius Palmas
2     To V[a]lerius Long[g]us, the brother, [very many] greetings.
3     I want you to know that
4     Diocles took away from me the
5     copper of mine, seventeen staters, and the whetstone.
6     If you are able, keep the whetstone of Fourius unharmed until I come and
7     settle with him.
8     For no one entered
9     except himself. And I,
10    having risen early, having searched, and did not find it. No one gave me κομειᾶτον as an opportunity
11    so I might obtain it. Greet
12    the sister
13    of yours. He sends greetings,
14    Maximus.
