[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord Cyril [fath]er
2     Timagenes, river-boat owner.
3     There is owing to you on account of a registered [tax of a]
4     river-boat owner for the twelfth and thirteenth indiction
5     of gold nomismata three, making nomismata 3.
6     and to provide these to you whenever
7     you wish without delay. I pray that you be strong
8     for many times.
9     in the month Pauni 20.
10    [To Cyril father ]
11    from Timagenes, river-boat owner.
