[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Apion, the most illustrious and most supereminent, from [the consuls and of patrician] rank, landholder, and here in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites. Account of [receipts] and expenditures which occurred through me Stephen, overseer of Pangouleion, together with the other districts and Margarite and Ambiotus and Maeuma and other foreign places, and in the fifth indiction of year 293 and 262. Receipts thus: from the heirs of Apphoutus Epimachus, from the estate of Pangouleion, wheat in the granary, artabae fifty-five and currency two-hundred ninety.
2     From the commons of the farmers on behalf of private land.
3     Senamun, [the] tapestry-[worker].
