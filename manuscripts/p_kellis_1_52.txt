[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aurelius] …sop from village Tpake of the Mothites …
2     [city Aurelius] to Horos Mersios the camel-driver from village Ke[llis of the] same city, greetings. I acknowledge …
3     [that I have received] from you here in the city of Hermes donkey-loads [of] fresh olives [and] cucumbers in order to provide [to my lord] the land-owner. I have given you the receipt [for security] and being questioned I have acknowledged.
4     [Under the consulate of our] lords Constantine Augustus …
5     [the 6th and Constantine] the most illustrious Caesar the 1st …
6     Phaophi 4.
7     [Aurelius] …sop the aforesaid I have received the …
8     [aforementioned load]s as aforesaid. I wrote on his behalf …
9     [the letters, [he being] not [knowing how to write], Aurelius Psenamounis Petosirious …
10    [from the city of Hermes.]
