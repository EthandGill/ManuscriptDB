1     Ischyras to Maxima
2     greetings.
3     I want you to know that the two
4     drachmas of Parabolas I received;
5     moreover to you concerning them
6     I write not in order
7     coming you might say to me
8     that I gave [it back],
9     if indeed you are about to speak.
