[TRANSLATION]
1     Tybi 28. Valerius son of Antiourios delivered, at the harbor of Leukogion,
2     on behalf of … of the … indiction, of the villagers of Karanis, of wheat
3     clean, ninety-one artabas, artabas 91 only.
4     I, Ouranios, have signed.
5     Phaophi 24. Aion son of Sarapion delivered,
6     at the harbor of Leukogion, on behalf of the ninth
7     indiction, of the villagers of Karanis,
8     of clean wheat twenty-eight artabas, artabas 28 only.
9     I, Souchidas, through me Timotheos, have signed.
10    Hathyr 6. Aeion son of Sarapion, of the villagers of Karanis, delivered,
11    at the harbor of Leukogion, on behalf of the ninth indiction, of wheat
12    clean, three artabas, artabas 3 only. I, Timotheos, have signed.
13    Phaophi 15. Aion son of Sarapion delivered, at the harbor of Leukogion,
14    into the boat of Papnouthios son of Hermias, on behalf of the eighth indiction,
15    of the villagers of Karanis, of clean wheat six artabas, artabas 6 only.
16    And through Ptollas son of Aion, of clean wheat two and a half
17    and a third, artabas 2, ½, ⅓ only. I, Ision the grain-measurer, have signed.
18    Phaophi 24. Aion son of Sarapion delivered, at the harbor of Leukogion,
19    on behalf of the ninth indiction, into the boat of Ammonios
20    from Leukogion, of the villagers of Karanis, of wheat
21    clean, two and a half, a third, a twelfth, artabas 2, ½, ⅓, 1/12.
22    I, Timotheos the grain-measurer, have signed.
23    Likewise the same man, on behalf of the same indiction, of wheat
24    clean, four artabas, artabas 4 only. I, Timotheos, have signed.
