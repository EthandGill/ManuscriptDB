1     To Dionysios strategos of Arsinoe, Herakleides division,
2     from Hereios son of Stotoetes the elder and Pabous [son] of Pabous, mother Segathios, archer-chief, and Hereios [son] of Pakuses and Apugchis son of Horion and Esouris son of Paouites
3     and Demas son of Demas and Orsenouphis son of Hereios and Petesouchos son of Sotos and Horus, mother Thaisates, and Soterichos [without father], mother Thermouthes, and Pkates son of Pekuses and Pathes son of Satanous and Pabous son of Pabous and Kannites son of Pathes and Sotas son of Pabous and Paites son of Satanous and Pekuses son of Psenesis and Apugchis son of Apugchis and Aboutos son of Satanous and P[akuses] son of Hereios [and] Pousetis son of Mataites and Pakuses son of Apugchis and Melas son of Pakuses and Aeietos son of Kan[eites] and Apugchis son of Sarapion, [of the] 25th and [of the] other public farmers of [the] village Soknopaiou Nesos. Our lords, the most divine and most august
4     Emperors Severus and Antoninus [having arisen] in [their] Egypt, with whom [very many] goods they gave, [they] wished also those
5     in foreign [lands] living [to go] all into their own house [having broken] the violent [and unlawful], and according to their sacred
6     [orders] we entered [in]. Holding therefore us [to the] workings
7     [which was] revealed shore-land, each as [one has] the power, a certain Orseus [being] a violent and obstinate man happened
8     to attack us with four brothers of his, preventing the working and making [a] sowing and frightening us, in order that from this according to the former [course] into [the] foreign [land] we flee and alone they claim [the] land; [we] declare [to] you, lord, the violence of these. For neither are they fellow-contributors of the monthly occurring in the village distributions and imposed [taxes of] grain and silver payments, but also [there] is [a] property [for which] yearly we are inscribed alone [for] drachmas two thousand four hundred; and alone
9     of these the four-footed [animals], [being] very many, [make] the pastures. And never did they perform [a] liturgy, [they being] frightening
10    the village-recorders [over] time. Whence according to what is necessary we make [the] refuge to you and we ask, if it seems good to your fortune, to command [them] to be brought before you and to hear us against him in order that from your aid
11    having been punished [we] might be able to idle with [the] land and to the imposed [matters] on us to devote ourselves, [and] Orseus and the brothers [to be] fellow-contributors to the public payments [and]
12    to perform [the] fitting to them liturgies and to have [from] equal us all [of] the sowing of [the]
13    [revealed] land, so that we may be in our own [place] remaining in your fortune
14    to give thanks. May you prosper.
