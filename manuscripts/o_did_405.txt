[TRANSLATION]
1     Theanus and Panisinaes
2     to Papelthos the brother
3     greetings. Since we departed from you, we seek you. Do not grieve
4     that we departed from
5     you, for I hope that
6     and I will help you so that
7     you might cross here. Be friends, you and Potamon. Greet Hartosis and Ankyra.
8     Be well.
