[TRANSLATION]
1     To Alkibiades, [chief] of the [guards],
2     from Kallistratos son of Kallistratos, [overseer]
3     of the herds of the estate of Livia [and]
4     of Germanicus Caesar. In the (month of)
5     Choiach of the [past]
6     34th year [of Caesar], I took on
7     Pe…nios as donkey-driver
8     [to take care] of the donkeys,
9     [my pack-animals, and that] through the
10    [summer] he should [stay] until
11    the Choiach [of the] 35th year of Caesar
12    and carry out everything
13    whatever is fitting for a donkey-driver. The one indicated agreed.
14    But having spent the winter of the 34th year
15    of Caesar and stayed through the
16    summer, he was found
17    snatching from the freight-loads
18    the donkey-equipment underneath,
19    despising what was right. First, having neglected the donkeys, they grew weak, and having wounded (one)
20    he destroyed a donkey of mine worth
21    silver drachmas … ,
22    the … and having brought in … this
23    … the donkey having collapsed … and despising … the rest of my
24    animals … it has been … . Still from
25    the [month] Thouth of the 35th year
26    of Caesar, [holding] my three sacks
27    … [, not] having handed over to me
28    either the pack-saddles or the rest of the tools of the donkeys, and
29    because of this my donkeys
30    have stood idle. Therefore I submit to you this memorandum so that,
31    having secured the accused, you may send him off to those whom
32    it concerns, and that before them he may be compelled to pay me the
33    price of the donkey and the
34    (compensation for) the idleness of the donkeys and the sacks
35    and the pack-saddles and the tools,
36    so that I may obtain what is fitting.
37    Farewell.
