1     [I] Maria, [the daughter of the blessed Isidorus] from the [village/city of] the fortress on earth
2     I greet you in Christ, O Daniel, from this [same] fortress. Greetings, saying I confess
3     [that …] of the solidy [cloth] which Kollouthos, my son, [I acknowledge]
4     that I took [it] …, truly I confess that I received the solidy [cloth] of Kollouthos
5     my son there, and no security [exists] which they might come against
6     you, which they will be null, because not …
7     is righteous [with] you [regarding] the solidy [cloth] there, [according to] an [agreement], therefore
8     I sealed this document and I will conform to it wherever they might [make public]
9     it in [my] heart
10    by me [Aristophanes]
