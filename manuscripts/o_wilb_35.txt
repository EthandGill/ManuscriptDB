1     Asklas and Soter, tax collectors of silver [tax] of Charas
2     Chatabouti Phthouminiou through
3     Pachnoumios Paouftheioutos. We have received
4     on behalf of land tax of the 19th year of Charas dirty drachmas
5     four, making dirty drachmas four. Year 20
6     of Hadrian, of the lord, Tybi 1.
