r.1    διαγεγράφασι Ψανσνῶς Καμήτιος Φθουσνέως μητρὸς Σενχώνσιος καὶ Φθουσνεῦς υἱὸς

[TRANSLATION]
1     Have been charged: Psansnos Kametius [of] Pthousnes, mother Senchonsius, and Pthousnes [the] son
2     for the dyke-tax of Charakos, 14th year: thirteen drachmas [and a] two-obol piece, bathing-tax: one three-obol piece, totaling fourteen five-obol pieces
3     and of these are additionally charged. Of the 14th year of Nero the
4     master, Epeiph 26. Apion [I] have signed.
