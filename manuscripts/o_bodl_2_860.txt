[TRANSLATION]
1     Pamonthes and Postomos, tax collectors of silver-tax, Charakos
2     to Antiphilos, [of] Kronios, Psenetym[us].
3     we received on behalf of watchtowers and guards and others, 6th year, drachmas
4     four, [which] amounts to 4 drachmas. Year 6 of Antoninus
5     Caesar, the lord, Epeiph 25.
6     Postomos, through Karouries, assistant, I have signed.
7     Pamonthes, I have signed.
