[TRANSLATION]
1     Tiberius Claudius Alexander, freedman of Tiberius … , made a will.
2     Let Claudia Theanous, my fellow-freedwoman, of all the property belonging
3     to me, be my heir, and let all the rest be disinherited
4     by me; and let her be bound to give and to do all that in
5     this will shall be written, without evil fraud,
6     and let her declare my inheritance within the 100 days next
7     after those on which she shall know and be able to attest that she is my heir.
8     But if she neither declare nor attest, let her be disinherited.
9     … Let Tiberius Claudius Ptolemaios, her son, in the second place
10    be my heir, and let all the [rest] be disinherited; and let him be bound to give and to do all that
11    … my heir, and his mother to give
12    … and let him declare my inheritance within the days
13    … [next] without evil fraud. Whoever is my heir
14    … [I] enjoin (him) to give. To Tiberius
15    [Claud]ius Theonas, my fellow-freedman, I give and bequeath one hundred drachmas;
16    To Tiberius Claudius Demetrios, my fellow-freedman, I give and bequeath one hundred drachmas. The care of my body
17    I leave to my heir, on which I wish to be spent
18    not less than one hundred drachmas. And if, after this my
19    will, on tablets, codicils, papyrus, or any other (material)
20    … written by me or subscribed and sealed I leave, by which something is to be given or done, and I entrust and forbid (something) to be provided, I wish it to be equally valid
21    as also this my will. Of this will
22    let evil fraud be absent. The household and goods of the will
23    being made, he bought (it) for one sesterce coin,
24    the weigher being …
25    he attested in turn …
26    The will was made in the city of Oxyrhynchus of the Thebaid, on the 16th day before the Kalends
27    of June, in the consulship of Lucius Julius Ursus Servianus for the 3rd time and Titus Vibius
28    Varus, in the 18th year of the Emperor Caesar Trajan
29    Hadrian Augustus, Pachon 22. Tiberius Claudius
30    Alexander, I read out my will, in accordance with
31    [which] I wished my (will) to be written in Latin.
32    For everything is agreeable to me as aforesaid.
