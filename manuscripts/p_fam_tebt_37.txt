[TRANSLATION]
1     To Lucceius Ophellianus, the most excellent epistrategos,
2     [fro]m Lysimachos also called Didymos and Philosarapis,
3     both sons of Herakleides also called Valerius, of the Antinoites,
4     of the Matideian (tribe) and the Kalliteknean (deme). There belongs to us,
5     lord, against our other brother Philantinoos also called
6     Neilammon a common maternal female slave by the name of Martilla,
7     who, being in the Arsinoite, where we hold land,
8     sends the necessaries to us at Antinoou (city).
9     This (slave), then, while she was busy, a certain Sarapamon
10    son of Pyrrhion, and his brother Deios, from the Arsinoite, residing in the village of Theptunis of the Polemon
11    division, having dragged off by force without a strategos, kept
12    in their own keeping, whom up to now they hold, on the pretext
13    of a dispute which they say they have against our other
14    brother Philantinoos also called Neilammon. According to
15    necessity, then, lord, we petition you, requesting,
16    if it seem good to your fortune, to order to be written to the strategos of the
17    Themistes and Polemon divisions to send them up to where, to the city of Antinoou,
18    before your jurisdiction, lord, concerning the
19    violence and abduction of the slave which they committed, [whom] also now
20    still they have in their keeping, so that we may be benefited.
21    Farewell.
22    I, Lysimachos also called Didymos, have submitted (it). I, Philoserapis
23    son of Herakleides, have jointly submitted (it).
24    Year 7, Phamenoth …
25    [The] strategos will send from here
26    those named in (the petition) to me.
