[TRANSLATION]
1     of the year 10, Pachon 18
2     To Heracleides, secretary
3     Asclepiadis the one in charge of the disposition of grain in Memphis [to Theodore]
4     Greetings. On the 6th of Pachon [the grain] was disposed in Memphis from …
5     [grain] brought from the … [citizen] white wheat [artabas] …
6     at 170 drachmas per artaba being sold of similar [grain] in the market at 180 drachmas, and on the [7th]
7     [the grain] was disposed of the same wheat [artabas] … at 170 drachmas being sold
8     of similar [grain] at 180 drachmas and on the 8th of the [same] 52 artabas at 170 drachmas
9     being sold of similar [grain] at 180 drachmas and on [the 9th of the same artabas] …
10    at 170 drachmas being sold of the [similar grain] at [180 drachmas and on the 10th]
11    of the same artabas … 6 at 170 drachmas being sold [of the similar grain at 180 drachmas].
12    To Theodore.
