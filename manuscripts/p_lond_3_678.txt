[TRANSLATION]
1     [During the reign] of Ptolemy called Alexander and Berenice [his] sister, [the] divine
2     [Philometor], year 16, under [the priests and priestesses and] canephorus [of those] being and existing, month Choiak ..., in Crocodile-city [under Paniskos, agoranomos of the]
3     upper toparchy of the Pathyrite [nome].
4     [Sold] Petesouchos son of Schotos, priest of Soukhos and Aphrodite, about 54 years, shorter, [of] honey-colored [complexion],
5     long-faced, straight-nosed, [impaired in] sight, [the land] belonging to him in the upper plain
6     near Pathyra, land [and] mainland grain-bearing [of] ... arouras, whose neighbors [are]
7     [on] the south [broad] road, [on] the north ... dike ... …
8     ..., [aroura]e, [on] the west [a] mountain [or whoever] might be neighbors [on] all sides.
9     [Purchased by Harsiesis] son of Schotos, one of the same priests, [about 47 years, medium height, honey-colored,] long-faced, straight-nosed,
10    ears standing out, [for] one copper talent. [The] previous seller and guarantor of [those] according to
11    this sale [is] Petesouchos the one who [made the] sale, whom Harsiesis
12    [the buyer] received.
