[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master, greatest benefactor, Flavius Justinus the eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the year 10, in the consulship of their serenity for the 2nd time,
2     Pauni 21, of the 8th indiction, in the year 251 = 220, on the 9th beginning.
3     The Flaviae Megalo and Anastasia, the most illustrious daughters of Ptolemaios of glorious memory, to the exactor's office and to the assistants
4     of the accounting-office of this new city of Justinus. We send the appended matters. You are to know that of Tmoue and
5     of Dorkon, the irrigation-machines which came to us, lying in the village of Thammou, we have sold by written purchase-deed to the
6     most learned advocate Theodoros, and we have been paid their price. Since therefore we agreed with him
7     that he should discharge, on behalf of the same machines and of all their obligation, on the one hand by way of the grain-embole of the (fiscal) rule
8     eighteen artabas, into dirty (grain) by the cancellus with the surcharge, twenty-five artabas, four choinikes; and on behalf of the fiscal
9     government (dues), twenty carats, to the treasury for Alexandria twenty and a half carats, and on behalf of the freight-charge to Alexandria and transport
10    and other things, seven and three-quarters carats: be willing, on receiving the present order, to relieve our name
11    of the said embole, which amounts to eighteen artabas of grain by the (fiscal) rule, and into dirty (grain) by the cancellus with the surcharge
12    [twenty-five artabas, four choin]i[kes], and [on behalf] of the fiscal government (dues), twenty carats, to the treasury for Alexandria
13    [twenty and a half carats] … in[to] the designation
14    an order of the relief …
