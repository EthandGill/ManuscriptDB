[TRANSLATION]
1     Eirene to Parammon, greetings.
2     I wrote to Philon to give you
3     I gave to Kalocairos drachmas 900 and
4     whatever else if you wish. Well then
5     you will do well having-taken from him
6     and the clothing-pouch mine
7     which I gave to Kolocairos in which
8     I wrote to be-sent to-me cosmetics of-two-drachmas. And quickly
9     send the cosmetics since need me is
10    to Philon. Farewell. Athur 30.
