[TRANSLATION]
1     I Sle owe you Ose, phylarch, gold coins
2     fourteen, making coins 14 only, and these with God, I Sle
3     shall provide to you whenever you wish, and it is settled
4     for me as aforesaid. Noubal, brother, I witness.
5     Amatepioi, I witness. Sentasao, I witness.
6     Krouahe, I witness. Pasapip, I witness.
7     through me Sansnotes. It was written in the month
8     Pharmouthi, 17,
9     indiction 5.
