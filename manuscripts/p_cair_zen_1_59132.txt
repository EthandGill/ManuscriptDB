[TRANSLATION]
1     Mys to Zenon [greetings]. Concerning Symbotus, I have also sent word to you previously, because [repeatedly]
2     to him and [we write and] we meet, requesting to receive the land, and
3     and now I thought it [good to] remind you; because it was entrusted
4     to him the land … [to be measured], we do not approach toward the products
5     from [this land], but it happens [they] are being destroyed. And con[cerning] these things to
6     Symbotus [revealing] we continue, but he does not attend, but argues against the
7     royal [secretary], thinking it necessary with the just rope to be measured
8     to him, [of which] is the [difference] beside the hundred arouras of ten arouras. However, as it might seem to you, and concerning [these] matters you would do well to write to us most speedily.
9     Fare well. Year 29, Mecheir 30.
10    Year 29, Dystrus 1, Phamenoth 1.
11    Mys of Symbotus.
12    [To] Zenon.
