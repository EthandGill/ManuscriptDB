[TRANSLATION]
1   Korbolon to [Herakleid(es)] his l[o]rd,
2   g[reeting].
3   I sent you by Hor[ion] the key and
4   by Onnophris, camel-man of Apol(lonios), the press-lid. I folded up
5   with that letter a pattern of white-violet;
6   so, prithee, you will do
7   well to buy me 2 drachmas’ worth, and send it
8   to me quickly by whomever you find, since the tu-
9   nic is about to be woven. I received
10   everything you wrote that I would receive,
11   from Onnophris, intact. I sent you
12   by the same Onnophris six choinikes of fine
13   apples. I give thanks to all the gods, knowing
14   that I learned Ploution had turned up
15   in the Oxyrhynchite nome.
16   Do not think I have been careless of the key;
17   the reason is rather this: that the
18   smith is far from us.
19   As for the things I had written you to send
20   me by Korbolon, I wonder how
21   you did not see fit to send them — and
22   that when I needed them for a feast.
23   Prithee, buy me a silver
24   seal-ring, and send it to me quickly. See to it,
25   until Onnophris buys
26   for me what Eirene’s mother told him. I told him that Syntrophos said to give nothing
27   more
28   to Amarantos on my account from now on. Let me know what you gave him, that I may settle
29   accounts with him; and if not, I am coming out with my son on this very account.
30   I had from Korbolon the big cheeses — but I did not want big ones;
31   little ones I wanted. And whatever you want, let me know — I’ll gladly do it. Farewell.
32   Pauni 1. Send me one obol’s worth of cakes for my sister’s boy.
33   To Herakleid(es) son of Ammo(nios), my lord.
