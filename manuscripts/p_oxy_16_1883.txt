[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Hermia the most distinguished scholastic advocate of the [Oxyrhynchite] city
2     From Flavius Ala, vicarius of the fort of Psoptheos, from [Oxyrhynchite] [city]
3     Menas, saddle-maker, setting out from [the] [same] city, owes me in writing and being ordered
4     by me the gratification [to make] delays. Awaiting necessity [I beseech] your
5     education through the delivery [of this] [the] petition
6     having come under security [to be compelled] the
7     gratification for me to make, [most brilliant] advocate,
8     most wise master.
9     Flavius Ala, vicarius, I have delivered.
10    In the consulship of Flavius Cetheghus the most [renowned], Choiak 25th.
