[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollodorus and Psenamounis, collectors of taxes [from the] market-places, [no.] 2
2     [To] Petechespokhrates of the Phatres [tribe], [from] Psenasoukhios: we have received for the land-survey
3     year 11, dirty drachmas four, total drachmas 4. Year 12 of Antoninus
4     Caesar our lord, Thoth 2. Onnophris [witnessed]. I have signed, Psenamounis.
5     Other [receipt], Thoth 28, in the name of Senmouthos
6     [From] Psenamounios [son] of Kesthotes, through Maierius
7     [of] Harpmoites, dirty drachmas twelve, total dirty drachmas 12.
