[TRANSLATION]
1     To Boethus, kinsman and epistrategos
2     from Euthaeus son of Ammonius, a Libyan
3     [concerning the period after him]. In the 26th year I lent
4     to Zenon [son] of Thaliarch[us], a Libyan, and to Polemon
5     [son] of Dionysius, a Persian, both of Theo[...]
6     [who were stationed] in Phabis of Coptic [region], soldier[s] receiving regular pay, jointly
7     [I lent] to them solid wheat, ten and one-half artabas, according to a contract
8     of a loan, by which they stand surety for each other
9     and they allow me to make recovery from both and from whichever I
10    of them I choose; and [although] the time for
11    repayment has passed and [even] more time has been added and they have not
12    repaid, and one of them,
13    Polemon, having [departed] this
14    life before repayment, I ask, if
15    it seems [right], since [I] wish to make recovery from Zenon in accordance with the contract, [to] order [to be] written
16    to Euphranor, of those equal in rank to the first [friends] and strategos, to compel him to repay
17    the ten and one-half artabas of [grain]; if he disobeys, place [him] in your [custody]. When this is done I shall obtain
18    what is just.
19    Farewell.
20    To Euphranor: compel [him to do]
21    what is just [immediately]
22    or place [in your custody]. ...
23    Year 33, Payni 14
24    To Agnesimenes: compel [...]
25    [him to do] what is just or place [the one accused] in your custody.
26    Year 33, Pay[ni 1]4
27    Of Euthaeus to Euphranor
28    [son] of Ammonius
