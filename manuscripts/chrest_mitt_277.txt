[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of] our [master] Theodosius the eternal
2     [Augustus for the] 2nd time [and] Flavius Cynegius the most illustrious.
3     [To Flavius Isidoros], of the beneficiarii of the staff of the prefecture of the Thebaid,
4     [from Aurelius] Bekis son of Hatres, from the city of Hermopolis
5     [the most] illustrious, residing in the village of Timonthis
6     of the Hermopolite nome, greeting. I wish, willingly
7     and of my own choice, to lease from you, for one year only, [from] the crops of the fortunate third new indiction, your arouras, however many they may be,
8     to the west of the Tomis river, of the farmstead called
9     Pkol…eno…, for sowing and planting of grain
10    and of fallow-crop kinds, at the half-share; and in return for the
11    rents I shall provide to you the half of all the
12    crops and chaff that come out, I being responsible for both the seed and all expenses of every kind from sowing up to and including the harvest,
13    the rent agreed upon between one another (being) … for the wooden
14    machine, eight artabas of grain, as indeed to be taken
15    from my own portion, eight artabas of grain,
16    on condition that I, on the portion in fallow, sow in fodder … of stunted growth six arouras, of which to you the
17    landlord two arouras, and to me the cultivator four arouras,
18    I providing to you the landlord, on account of rent, from the
19    aforesaid half-share of yours, … eight artabas of grain,
20    the public taxes of every kind being upon you
21    the landlord. The lease is valid, and having been formally questioned I gave acknowledgement. Phaophi 4.
22    I, Aurelius Bekis son of Hatres, the aforesaid, have leased as aforesaid.
23    I, …ios Ploution son of Apion, having been requested, wrote on behalf of him
24    who does not know letters.
