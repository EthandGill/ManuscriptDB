[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymus to Apollonius
2     to the most honored
3     greetings.
4     You will do well, having come together
5     with [A]ilourion, the one carrying to you the [letter], in order that
6     for the festival pigeons for us he may purchase,
7     and having been asked, having come down, you may [dine]
8     with us. [This] therefore having done, you will render to me great
9     kindness [having been placed upon yourself].
10    Greet all your people.
11    Farewell.
12    In the third year of the Emperor
13    Caesar Domitian
14    Augustus Germanicus, Pachon 15.
15    To Bacchiada, [deliver to Apollonius], the most honor[ed]
