[TRANSLATION]
1     … io…
2     mother Isis, hailing from the village of Episemos of the Oxyrhynchite
3     nome, administered as a pagarchy by your Eminence. We acknowledge
4     of willing mind and voluntary choice, swearing
5     the divine and revered oath, that we guarantee and undertake
6     toward your Eminence the Aurelii Pamouthios son
7     of Phoibammon, mother Maria, from the hamlet of Sekoundos, and
8     Chekoul son of Apollo, moth[er] Herais, from the same
9     village of Episemos, as guards, on the terms that they without ceasing
10    remain and dwell in their own places and in no way
11    absent themselves or move
12    to another place, but that (if) they are sought from us,
13    we shall bring them forward [and] shall deliver (them) with[out] argument where [we r]eceived them
14    also, [in] the pri[s]on of your glorious
15    house. But if we do not do this, [we] acknowledge that we are liable
16    to answer for all things sought against them.
17    The guarantee is valid, single, written, and on being questioned we acknowledged. The commonalty of the
18    aforewritten names consents for us, this guarantee, as set forth.
19    Markos I wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
20    … [Pa]mouthios and Chekoul as guards …
