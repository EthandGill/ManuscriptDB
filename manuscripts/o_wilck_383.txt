1   Has paid: Pakybis son of Pach[noumis(?), for]
2   poll-tax of year 4, ten drachmas and bath-tax on[e,]
3   an obol, a half-obol — total 11 dr. 1½ ob. — and surcharges at an obol (and) half-obol.
4   Year 4 of Gaius Caesar Imperator
5   Augustus, Pachon 26.
