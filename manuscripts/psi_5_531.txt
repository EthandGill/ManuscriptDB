[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon greetings, the priests of Astart[h of those of the Phoinikian-Egyptians in Me]mphis. We pray for you
2     by Astarte to give you fav[or towards] the king. Heros[t]ratos sent
3     to you the letter-scribe concerning the … [of A]starte who gave you the [enc]ounter
4     in the Serapeum, requesting you to care [for] the encounter. If then it seems good to you,
5     let oil and castor-oil be given to us … in the way that at value oil and castor-oil
6     to the temples of the Carians and Hellenomemphites in Memphis. For oil and castor-oil are given
7     to the temples of the Carians and Helleno[mem]phi[tes] at value the [amount] of oil at drachmas 2 triobol,
8     and of the castor-oil drachma 1 obols 4½. The temple of Astarte is similar to the temples of the Carians
9     and Hellenomemphites; as therefore it is done for them, let it be done for our temple.
10    [Fare]well.
