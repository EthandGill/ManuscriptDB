[TRANSLATION]
1     Eurylochus to Athenodorus the dearest, greetings and being strong
2     [to prosper]; if you are able to help
3     … by ship having been entreated, do not send away; and I
4     on the 29th having bailed out I come to you; if you are able to benefit us [with] a small chain
5     or something newer … [to] send to me; I will give to you if I hire
6     to Memphis; it will not at all hinder [the] very pressing; take care of yourself so that you might be healthy; farewell
7     year 28 of Caesar
8     Mesore …
9     [To] Athenodor[us] the dearest
