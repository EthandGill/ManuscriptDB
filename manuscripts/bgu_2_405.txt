[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Philip the most illustrious
2     prefect of the sacred praetorium and Flavius Salias
3     the most illustrious master of the horse, Phamenoth 10.
4     Aurelia Valeria, daughter of Alupios, to Aurelia
5     Nemesilla, my sister of the same womb, from the village Philadelphia of the Arsinoite nome, greetings.
6     Since a millstone and a mill-machine, [which] ancestral property of ours, we inherited, and I alone took the millstone and sold [it] for the portion seeming good to me, accordingly I acknowledge
7     not to have a share in the aforementioned mill-machine, but [that] I am foreign [to it]
8     and alienated from it, and no one will come against you concerning it, neither I Valeria nor [my] children nor [my] heirs nor anyone else
9     and I have given to you my hand for [the purpose of]
10    security. The acknowledgement [is] valid, and being asked I acknowledged. Aurelia Valeria
11    the aforementioned gave [the] security
12    of not having a share in the machine and
13    I concur with the things written, as stated above.
14    Aurelius Sarapion [son] of Iulianus from Alexandria, dwelling in the same village, wrote
15    on her behalf [these] letters, [she] not knowing [letters].
