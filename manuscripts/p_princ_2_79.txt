1     In the consulate of our lords [K]onstantinus Augustus [the 7th] and Constantine [the most distinguished Caesar the 1st,] …
2     The Aurelii Horion and Eudaimon [and] Eulogius, the three from the father [D]ionysios Dioscouridos … [the aforementioned] our father Dion[ysios] has departed this life, and we his children … [to prepare] us to refrain from [the] contention and division between one another …
3     It seemed [good to u]s three, upon [our] own portions, to make a division among ourselves … [to Horion]
4     and to Eudaimon [of] silver talents [seven]ty-five, so that I may have my [sha]re …

6     of the [share] being claimed by us [a] half portion, in whatever amount also on the arourae …
