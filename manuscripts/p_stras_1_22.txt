[TRANSLATION]
1     The deified Severus and Antoninus (Caracalla), to Iuliana
2     daughter of Sosthenes, through Sosthenes her husband.
3     The plea of long possession (praescriptio longae possessionis) benefits those who have had a just
4     cause and have been in possession without any dispute: against
5     those who have resided in another city, they are helped by twenty years'
6     count; but against those in the same (city),
7     ten. Posted at Alexandria,
8     in the 8th year, Pharmouthi 24.
9     When Subatianus Aquila was prefect.
10    In the 15th year, Phamenoth 17, Sabinus
11    and Maximus son of Dionysios having been summoned and having answered,
12    among other things: Aquila said: what do you reply
13    concerning the time of the possession, since he says that after the purchase from Tausorapis about fourteen years (have passed), and (what of) the silence during this?
14    Asklepiades the advocate said: it has occurred. Aquila said: there are ordinances of the lords concerning
15    those dwelling in the provinces: if elsewhere possession follows when someone has
16    an occasion and even a brief just (title) of holding,
17    when the one who thinks (the property) belongs to him has kept silent
18    and has acquiesced for over ten years, those who have held it have the security (of title).
19    When Mettius Rufus was prefect, in the 10th year,
20    Hathyr 24, Sallustius Capito having been summoned against Iulia Pytharous and (he) having said
21    that the recorder (hypomnematographos) Maecianus had heard about the
22    matter, among other things: Mettius Rufus
23    said to Capito: you have no loophole (of entry),
24    for the woman has been in possession for a long time. But with us, behold, possession through a (single) year is sufficient
25    for ownership.
