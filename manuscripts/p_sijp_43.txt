1     In the fourth year of Imperator Caesar [Trajan] Hadrian Augustus, [in the month] …, in … .
2     The following did she dispose, being sound and of sound mind, Th… daughter of Harpaesis son of Herakleos, her mother being …, from the village of …, with as guardian … son of Panesneus, whose mother is …, dot… son of Panomgeus son of Panomgeus: [as long as I live, I am to have authority over my own property]
3     to carry out whatever I may wish, and to alter and annul this will; but whatever I carry out is to be valid. And if I die under this will having carried out nothing, I leave (my property):
4     to my husband Ploution son of Pekysis son of D…, from the same village, if he lives; but if not, to the adopted sons …nios son of Harpalos, from the city of Oxyrhynchos, in equal shares to each, if he lives; but if not, to their children …, to the aforewritten adopted sons Melanion and Sarapion in equal shares to each, if he lives; but if not, to their children …
5     and other utensils. And concerning the … slave-girl Demetrous …
6     … is to belong to my aforewritten husband Ploution, as long as he lives, the use and all the revenues …
7     to whom it shall also be permitted to sell these same things, which are to pass to the adopted sons, in my name, and to borrow upon them [and to use them for his own debt with the proceeds arising from sale]
8     or also of loans of money. And after one year from the death of me and of my husband Ploution … to Ploution's natural daughter Leontous, to whom alone I bequeath a hundred silver drachmas. And if something [should happen] to anyone …
9     … my … I allotted to him for the … him …
10    are to be undivided for both of them of my daughter Lezatous …, if she lives; but if not, of her children … [and it is to be permitted to no one to transgress any of the things ordained by me,]
11    and the one who transgresses is to pay for each attempt both the damage and a penalty of [ … ] silver [drachmas and the same to the treasury, and none the less the aforesaid provisions are to remain valid. The will is valid.]
12    I, Th… daughter of Harpaesis son of Herakleos, [have made] the will [and I leave, after my death, to my husband Ploution, if]
