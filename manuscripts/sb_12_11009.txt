[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings lord my Agenor from
2     Xenophon.
3     Through Harpokration the most excellent [it] was necessary, lord,
4     letters [of] you to have received from me
5     [so that at once] you might know me as [one] granting
6     [favor to him] acknowledging most eagerly the absence
7     of you filling-up in which
8     if I should have need of him: nor nothing
9     else foreign did [he] do of the
10    disposition towards you. The estates, as also another time to you I wrote, lord, happen to be according-to ability both of the works and of the
11    irrigations. I pray [for you to be] strong.
12    [I pray].
13    [To] Claude [Agen]or [from Xenoph]on
