# HGV-EN: Year twenty two of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, Thoth 28. Paid through He[r]ieus and his associates, tax collectors, (by) Stotoetis, son of Apynchis, for the rent on boats of the Antonian estate in Soknopaiou Nesos of year twenty fifty one dirty drachmas 3 obols, = 51 drachmas 3 obols.
