[TRANSLATION]
1     Eutyches to Ametrion my mother, very many greetings.
2     Before all things I pray to God that you may be received
3     in good health. I wish you to know that from the thirtieth of Tybi I came to Tyrannin and
4     did not find how to come to you,
5     since the camel-drivers were unwilling to come to the Oxyrhynchite.
6     Not only (that), but I also
7     went up to Antinoou for a boat and did not find (one). So now I have decided
8     to take up my loads to Antinoou and to stay there until I find a boat and sail down. You will do well
9     to give to those who deliver to you these my letters, of new
10    coinage two and a half talents, they make 2 talents 3000 drachmas, [in return for which I asked] and received from them [in the Ty]rannion on account of fr[eight], and
11    do not detain them (even) one hour,
12    as if … to the Theban nome. And receive from them
13    of old (coinage) two thousand and ninety-two drachmas, they make 2092 drachmas, in full,
14    and my donkey with the saddle-cloth.
15    But if you know that you do not have it in
16    your hands, borrow from … ita
17    and pay them in full, since they cannot
18    wait one hour.
19    And see that you do not do wrong and ensnare the men who have done me a kindness. [Greet]
20    for me all the … [and]
21    all the [friends]; [and] I shall come to you quickly.
22    … I pray for your health …
23    Notice: to the Camp, where … [ ]
24    … a, examine the (things) from Amaso…
25    e … no … gin.
26    [To Amet]rion the
27    … mother,
