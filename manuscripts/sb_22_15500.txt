[TRANSLATION]
1     To Julius Marinus, centurion, the one in charge of the order of Sphorakene, from Bathsabbatha of Arsinoe,
2     of the village Magdala of Sphorakene.
3     Since, lord, [you] having been in [A]ppada[na], I presented to you Aurelius Abilaas, soldier of legion 16
4     Flavia Firma and Barsemaias, a veteran, men
5     worthy, who testified before you
6     Nisraiabos, my brother, who had been freed by me,
7     having been murdered by some evildoers, whose the possessions to me belonging,
8     Iabathanaia holds, and
9     I am ready to accuse her concerning the same matter
10    murder, from this therefore I ask you to make a note
11    for you from me by this note for the sake of testimony.
