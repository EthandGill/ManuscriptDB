[TRANSLATION]
1   Harpaesis son of Phenophis, Thrakidas, paid,
2   for the trade-tax of the seventh year of Trajan
3   Caesar the lord, on account eight drachmas — total 8 dr.
4   I, Hermogenes, the collector, wrote (this),
5   year 9 of Trajan Caesar the lord,
6   Pharmouthi 11.
7   The same, the remaining four drachmas — total 4 dr.,
8   year 10 of Trajan the lord, Thoth 23.
