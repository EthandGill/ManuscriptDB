# HGV-EN: Order to Achillis. Buy half a cotyle of dry precipitate (?) of good perfume at 16 drachmae, some trodden grapes (?) at 8 drachmae, sweet gum (?) to the weight of 4 drachmae, onyx-shell to the weight of 4 denarii, incense to the weight of 1 denarius, some large cones at 1 denarius, dry powder at 12 drachmae, thread at 20 drachmae, 2 hair-combs at 1 denarius, sauce at 1denarius. Horion the baker says, buy me 4 obols of marjoram. Diogenes says, buy a sheet of papyrus. Buy us a bedstead... buy us 1 (?) sextarius of oil, buy us ... some pomegranate wine. I pray for your health.
