[TRANSLATION]
1     [After] the consulship of Flavius Dexicrates the most illustrious, Thoth 15, of the 13th indiction, in Arsinoe.
2     [To Flavius] Barius the most magnificent and most glorious count of the devoted domestici,
3     [from the] city of the Memphites, landholder also over the city of the Arsinoites, Aurelius Phoibammon
4     [son of Sam]bas, coming from the village of Tristomon of the Theodosioupolite nome, greeting. I acknowledge
5     [that I have leased] from your Magnificence, of the right of the same estate, in the plain
6     [of your] village, in various allotments and localities, as many arouras as there may be, for as long a time
7     [as] your Greatness [wishes], from the crops of the fortunate coming fourteenth indiction,
8     [I, the lessee] Phoibammon, [providing] for the fixed rent of each aroura
9     … [of the … hundred] arouras in the same allotments and localities, at the measurement by the schoinion … , by the measure customarily provided
10    … [which] rent I shall provide yearly in the month Pauni in the village
11    … [the lease being] valid, all my (property) being pledged to your Greatness for this
12    [both present] and future, specifically and generally, by way of security and mortgage
13    [by right as] from a judgment. And on being questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius Phibamon son of Sambas the aforesaid, I have leased
14    [your arou]ras and I shall pay the rent yearly as set forth. Aurelius Ioanis son of Sambas …
15    [I wrote on behalf of him, present, being illiterate] …
