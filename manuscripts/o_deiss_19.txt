[TRANSLATION]
1     Patsobthis and Miyeios, straw-contractors of the metropolis
2     To Phatretes, Pekousios and Pasemios, [son] of Petenouphis:
3     you conveyed to Ophion for the tenth-year harvest [a] gomol
4     one and half and third, making a gomol 1-½-⅓, of the 10th year
5     of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar
6     our Lord, [in the month of] Pauni, [day] 26
7     Miyeios, I have signed [for] a gomol one and half and third.
