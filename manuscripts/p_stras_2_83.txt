[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of the queen and King Ptolemy, the Gods Philo[metor]es Soteres, in the 3rd year, [under] the priest of King Ptolemy the god
2     [Philom]etor Soter Alexander, and of the Saviour Gods and of the [Brother]
3     Gods and of the Benefactor Gods and of the Father-loving Gods and of the
4     [Manife]st Gods and of the god Eupator and of the god Philometor and of the god Philopat[o]r
5     [Neos] and of the god Euergetes and of the Gods Philometores Soteres, (under) the sacred colt
6     of [Isis] the great mother of the gods, (under) the prize-bearer of Berenike the Benefactress,
7     [the bas]ket-bearer of Arsin[o]e Philadelphos, the priestess of Arsin[o]e Philopator, those being
8     [in Al]exandria; and in Ptolemais of the Thebaid, under the priests and
9     [priest]esses and the basket-bearer, those men and women being in Ptolemais of the
10    [Thebai]d, in the month Phamenoth 19. In Pathyris, before Heliodoros the agoranomos.
11    [Willin]gly they made a contract. Pates son of Totoes, a Persian of the epigone, and Taelilous
12    [and] Siepmous, (daughters) of Totoes, Persian women, with the guardian, the aforewritten Pates, acknowledge that they withdraw from the land of Takmeis daughter of Patous,
13    [their] mother, of dry grain-bearing land, of three parcels,
14    [in] the field around Pathyris, which she conveyed to them by a deed of gift in the 45th year,
15    [of which] one is in the upper field, another in the middle
16    [str]ip called Tiebothe, another in Temrauthis lower,
17    [of which] the neighbors have been declared through the deed of sale, which also
18    they [have] handed back, the deed, to Takmeis willingly,
19    [and] that neither Pates nor Taelilous nor Siepmous,
20    (children) of Totoes, nor anyone else on their behalf, shall proceed against
21    [Tak]meis nor against anyone else of those acting for her concerning
22    [the] specified contract nor concerning the three
23    [parc]els of land in any manner; and if not, the claim, both against him
24    [and] against the one proceeding on their behalf, shall be void, and the one proceeding shall further pay as a penalty forthwith
25    [to Tak]meis fifteen talents of bronze and, as sacred (dues)
26    [to the kin]gs, 300 drachmas of coined silver, and nonetheless
27    [it shall be com]pulsory for them to act according to the aforewritten (terms).
