[TRANSLATION]
1   Copy. In the second year of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
2   month Audnaios-S[e]basto[s 6, in the city of the Oxyrhynchi]
3   in the Thebaid, before the agoranomoi Andromachos and Diogenes. Tryphon son of Dionysios,
4   [of the city of the Oxyrhynchi],
5   of middle height, honey-skinned, long-faced, somewhat squinting, a scar on his right wrist, has bought from his mother Thamounis’ cou[sin Pnepheros]
6   son of Pap[o]ntos, of the same city, aged [about] 65, of middle height, honey-skinned, long-faced, a scar above his [eye]brow [… and]
7   another on his right knee, in the street: the half share of the three-storey house belonging to him from his mother,
8   with [all] its en[trances]
9   and exits [and] appurtenances, situated at the Sarapeion by the city of the Oxyrhynchi,
10   in the southern [parts of the quarter]
11   Temgemou[thi]s, west of the street leading to the quarter called “of the Shepherds”. Neighbors of the whole [house: on the south]
12   and east, public streets; on the north, that of the aforesaid buyer Tryphon’s
13   mother Thamounis; [on the west, the house of the]
14   seller Pnepheros’ sister Tausiris, a blind alley lying between. (Price:) 32 talents
15   of bronze. And [the seller] will gua[rantee the]
16   half share of the house under disposition, for ever, against all comers, with every guarantee, in the
17   sa[me] street.
18   In the second year of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, month
19   Sebastos 6, executed through And[romachos and Diogenes]
20   the agoranomoi.
21   Payment of the conveyance-tax, year 2 of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Ger[manicu]s Imperator, month [Sebastos 6. Paid through the]
22   bank in the city of the Oxyrhynchi headed by Sara[pi]on and partners …[…] Tryphon son of Dionysios […]
23   tax on the half share of the three-storey house belonging from his mother to the [se]ller,
24   with [all its entrances]
25   and exits and appurtenances, situated at the Sarapeion by the city of the Oxyrhynchi in the [southern parts]
26   of the quarter Temgenouthis, in the parts west of the street leading to the quarter called
27   [“of the Shepherds”,]
28   which he bought from his mother Thamounis’ cousin Pnephe[ros] son of Papontos
29   [of the same]
30   city by agoranomic deed, for 32 bronze talents — bronze reckoned in silver, 3 talents 1200 dr. — the one-tenth tax with transport charge, […] drachmas.
