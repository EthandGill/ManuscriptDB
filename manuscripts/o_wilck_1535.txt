[TRANSLATION]
1     Lysimachos to Alexandros,
2     greeting. You have paid, for the holding which
3     you farm, (the holding) of Herieus son of
4     Herieus, to his fellow-soldiers,
5     for the requisition-order, together with
6     Platon the kinsman and general,
7     from the price of wheat, of copper,
8     three thousand drachmas, total [3000…]
