1     … to …beios, the most renowned praefect of Egypt,
2     [from] … son of Didaros, who has served as chief-priest of the city of the Arsinoites. That the
3     [recorders] who are appointed by the councils to attend upon your immaculate [court], governor, my lord, ought to be both skilled in letters
4     [and] drawn [from the council], has not escaped your manliness. As for me, then,
