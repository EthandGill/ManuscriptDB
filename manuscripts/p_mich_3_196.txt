[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of Imperator Caesar Trajan
2     Hadrian Augustus, in the month of Germanikeios, on the twenty-third,
3     Pachons 23, in Bakchias of the Herakleides
4     div[isio]n of the Arsinoite nome. Ha[r]y[otes],
5     son of Alexas, aged about 60, with a scar on the left foot, acknowledges to Horos, son of Horos, surnamed Imesbailis, farmer, aged about 48, with a scar
6     on the left eyebrow, that the acknowledger has received from him the rent of the present sixth [year]
7     of Hadrian Caesar the lord for the (land) which the Horos farms of his,
8     of [Hary]otes, around the aforewritten
9     village, of the holding of four and a half arouras, sixteen artabas of wheat by the six-choinix measure, the same Horos having reserved
10    with himself the seed for the sowing of the coming 7th year,
11    four artabas of wheat, the lease remaining valid.
12    Subscribers: of the acknowledger, Heliodoros, son of Heliodoros, aged about 49,
13    with a scar on the right cheek; of the other, Ammonios,
14    son of Dioskoros, aged about 45, with a scar on the forehead on the right.
15    I, Haryotes, son of Alexas, acknowledge that I have received from
16    Horos the rent of the present year
17    for which he farms of mine around the village, of four
18    and a half arouras, sixteen artabas of wheat, he having the seed of the coming
19    seventh year, four artabas of wheat,
20    the lease being valid as aforesaid. There wrote on
21    his behalf Heliodoros, son of Heliodoros, since he does not know letters.
22    I, Horos, son of Horos: the receipt has been made out to me as
23    aforesaid. Ammonios, son of Dioskoros, wrote on
24    his behalf since he does not know letter[s]. It has been registered
25    through the records-office in Bakchias.
26    Acknowledgment of Haryotes to Horos of receipt of rent.
