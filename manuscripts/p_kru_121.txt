[TRANSLATION]
1     The fort of Egypt that in my heart I have sent to you with [...]
2     To lead you with two men as servants; these are whom we have gifted to them
3     [To come] to the place of the holy abba Phibamon I took, I filled [your body] in the company this which I have sent to lead you until the twenty-[eighth ...]
4     You will accomplish the custom indeed which we sent with [the] servants which being a portion full beside a ceratium they owe; then I enrolled for you the deed that is complete
5     And to correspond with us Kosmos the son of Petros and Demetre[...]
6     The son of Stratike and Mamtre, Basile, the son of Senouthios, I greet [upon] them
