[TRANSLATION]
1     One [they say] they speak
2     which is evil, which brings forth
3     from their heart in one
4     the one of the men to speak of
5     him, these, that it came to pass
6     a son, a youth, and he spoke it
7     these which his father [gave] upon him, to him thus
8     which came to pass, the one who understands this father not, except [something] upon they speak the son saying that I will give
9     a gift to him upon him, a sin ...
10    ... is [that] which they spoke, the youth which ... the ... word concerning shining ...
11    in the earth which ... the darkness of the people
12    not entirely is [the] servant
13    because of the works of Satan, the other works which will shine forth which [is in] the house
14    of the Lord, the son says [this]
