[TRANSLATION]
1     Onnophris … [of those from the] village of Magdol
2     Mire to Eutychides [Sarapion] greetings. I acknowledge
3     to have received from y[ou through hand from house use]
4     [bearing] interest of silver [drachmas one-hundred] twenty-four
5     four-obol [making] of silver drachmas 124 four-obol, [which I will also repay] to y[ou] in the
6     Epeiph month [of the present] year 17 of Trajan Caesar
7     the lord without [all] delay and evasion. Year 17 of the Emp[eror] Caesar Nerva
8     Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus Mechir 6.
9     Demetrius son of Herodes wrote on behalf of him not knowing letters.
