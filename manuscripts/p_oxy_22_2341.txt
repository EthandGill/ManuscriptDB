[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 16, Pharmouthi 16.
2     From the minutes of Subatianus Aquila.
3     In the Oxyrhynchite. After other matters.
4     Aelius Ammonios, president, said: This our river, lying alongside the rising (flood), has an inflow
5     and abundance of water. We request that then the boats
6     be sent and the riverside villages first
7     be loaded along with this river; after this, the (loading) by stake is the custom and the lawful practice,
8     to begin from the start from the upper (region) and each granary be emptied and (the grain) be driven (out) according to custom. Aquila said: What is the (loading) by stake?
9     He answered: Each district begins from the south.
10    Aquila said: From the upper toparchy? He answered: Yes. For this has always been the custom and observed, that (the loading) does not pass over from village to village
11    …, but they are emptied together with the rising (flood) so that the riverside (villages) of this T[o]mis river may f[irst] be lo[aded]. Aquila said to [Didy]mos the strategos: For
12    [what reason did this not] hap[pen? He an]swered: poia … me
13    … said: be … let him say s…
14    … said to [Ammon]ios … te him …
15    … eis … [Aqui]la[s said] …
16    and to Didymos the strategos he said: the now
17    remainder, where is it, the (grain) not yet sent? In what places? Didymos the strategos said: In the lower (region). Aquila said to Ammonios: If you were proving some fault committed, I would even have rebuked (him). But there is scarcely any question that this must be observed.
