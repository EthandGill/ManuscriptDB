[TRANSLATION]
1     in [the] month …
2     Justus [wagon-maker], son of John
3     Abra[ham], from Heracles city, to you
4     Appa Sion, chief of the
5     dyers, [son] of Kalous, from the same city.
6     I confess with willing mind and
7     not from any compulsion to guarantee and
8     to accept from you John
9     and him [who is] a dyer, son of Heraklides
10    from the same city, standing
11    [and] making the distribution [of the]
12    [w]ork from both the profit
13    and from [whatever] loss
14    which might arise from all his craft
15    according to the pattern of his colleagues
16    without hindrance; if he does not do this
17    so that I should bring forth this one and
18    hand [him] over to you without [excuse]
19    in [the place of] detention where I
20    even received [him], namely the
21    defenses to make to you
22    [regarding] all [matters] sought from you
23    concerning his production.
24    The present guarantee [is] valid, and being questioned I agreed
25    and [I] released [it]. Justus the aforesaid is satisfactory to me
26    as is set forth. Anoup [son] of Peter, I wrote
27    on behalf of him, [he] being illiterate.
28    …Anoup by me
29    Anoup, notary and legal [agent]
30    guarantee made by Justus, wagon-maker, guaranteeing
31    John, dyer, from Heracles city to Appa Sion
32    chief.
