1     To Theris. What was given to you by [Artemidoros son of Kleitos] …
2     with the guardian Artemidoros, brother of his father,
3     a memorandum through which, having put forward that there belong to him around Pera (the further side)
4     in catoecic (land) 27 arouras, and in vineyards sown with grain
5     around Nisea, registered to Berenike daughter of Eubios, his mother,
6     18 1/2 arouras, total 45 1/2, which he is unable to sow
7     for the 32nd year on account of being in a weak state, he requested that an order be drawn up
8     for him for seed, for each aroura one artaba of wheat, I have transferred (the matter),
9     it having a subscription.
10    The royal scribe registered …
11    … of the scribes … [registered to]
12    Kleitos the father of Artemidoros around [Pera in catoecic (land)]
13    [arouras] 27, and to the mother Berenike daughter of Eubios [in vineyards]
14    [sown] with grain around Nisea, arouras [18 1/2, total 45 1/2,]
15    of which the (amount) due of the 32nd year, one artaba [for each aroura, from the]
16    31st year of those sparsely-sown, of wheat artabas 15, 1050 (?), [of the] 30th year [of wheat artabas 39, of copper talents 1050,]
17    total of wheat 54, talents 2,775 (?), …
18    of those also shown in the 25th year [of wheat artabas] …

21    revenues, of wheat 15, and he has subscribed … [the]
22    guardian of the orphan Artemidoros …
23    of copper talents 1,630, he will deliver in the …
