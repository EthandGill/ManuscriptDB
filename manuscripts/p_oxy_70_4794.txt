[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious lord, greatest benefactor, Flavius Tiberius Constantine
2     the eternal Augustus and Imperator, year 5, in the consulship of the same most pious lord of ours, year 2,
3     Phamenoth 3, of the 13th indiction.
4     To the most eminent heirs of Apion of glorious memory, who became
5     protopatricius, landholders here too in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas the servant,
6     who asks and provides on behalf of his own masters, the same all-praiseworthy men,
7     the action and liability, (I) [Aurelius J]eremias, of the elders' rank, son of Prokopios, mother Eudoxia,
8     originating from the [estat]e of Palosis of the Oxyrhynchite nome belonging to your
9     magnificence, a registered farmer of it. I acknowledge, of my own free will and by voluntary choice,
10    swearing the [divine and] revered oath, that I stand surety and guarantee before your
11    magnificence, through those attached to it, the Aurelii Julius, brother of me Jeremias, and
12    Enoch the businessman, [son of] Patase, both of them likewise originating from the same estate
13    and [reg]istered themselves, that they shall remain without interruption and pass their life in the same
14    [estate and] in no way absent themselves or transfer to another
15    [place, but also,] when they are sought from me by your magnificence through those
16    [attached to it] on whatever day, for whatsoever reason of pretext, these men
17    [I shall present an]d hand over, without any recourse, where I received them,
18    [in the custody of] your glorious house. And if I do not do this, I acknowledge that I am liable
19    [to answer for all things] sought against them, having pledged for the security
20    [of this suret]y all my belongings present and future, specifically and generally, by way of pledge
21    [and by right of mort]gage. The surety is valid, written singly, and having been formally questioned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Jeremias,
22    [of the elders' rank, so]n of Prokopios, the aforewritten, have made the present
23    [surety as] afore[said]. I, Papnouthios, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
24    Surety made by Jerem[ias] …
25    …o…ion…eth …
