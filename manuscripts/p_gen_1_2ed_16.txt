[TRANSLATION]
1     To Julius Julianus, centurion,
2     fr[om] Herieus son of Stotoetis, stone-cutter, and Pabukas son of Pabous and Herieus son of Pakysis
3     an[d Ap]ynchis son of Horion and Esouris son of Paouites and Demas son of Demas and Orsenouphis
4     son of [Herie]u[s] and Petesouchos son of Sotes and Horos whose mother is Thaisas and Soterichos, fatherless, whose mother is
5     Tha[e]sis, and Teikas son of Pakysis and Pates son of Satabous and Pabous son of Pabous and Kannis son [of P]ates and Sostos son of Pabous and Pais son of Satabous and Pakysis son of Psenesis
6     an[d] Apynchis son of Apynchis and Abous son of Satabous and Pakysis son of Herieus and Pousis
7     son of Mataïs and Pakysis son of Apynchis and Satabous son of Pakysis and Aeïs son of Kannes and Melas son of Areus, all from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos in the Herakleides division. We bring before you, lord, a petition that requires your protection, which is of this kind: there is among us a beach-land registered around our village, comprising very many arouras; and whenever such land is uncovered, it is leased and sown according to the customary rent per aroura, and this is measured out to the most sacred treasury, and on account
8     of this the share that supports the village—being very large—is paid, because the village has neither private
9     land nor royal land nor any other category. But in order that all
10    may be able to remain in their own place—especially since the most illustrious
11    governor Subatianus Aquila has ordered all who are abroad to come back into their own (place),
12    keeping to their customary work—since therefore Orsenouphis
13    son of Stotoetis and his brothers, being five
14    in number, have attacked us, preventing us from sowing
15    such land, we necessarily submit (this), requesting,
16    if it seem good to you, that you order them to be brought before you to render an account
17    concerning this. Farewell.
18    Year 16, Phaophi 14.
