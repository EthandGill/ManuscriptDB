[TRANSLATION]
1   Sabinianus to Apollinarius,
2   his brother and lord, very many
3   greetings.
4   Each day I too, for your
5   welfare, make prayers before the
6   gods here, for your being preserved
7   for long times. More, rather, do you
8   provide, and have provided, for (my) sister,
9   wherefore I acknowledge all thanks
10   to you before all the gods; for it was witnessed
11   to me both by (my) sister and (my) mother.
12   Often I have written to you, and the negligence of those
13   carrying (the letters) misrepresented us
14   as negligent. As you take everything as binding for me,
15   I urge (you) to attend to (my)
16   sister in whatever she may need of you. I gre-
17   et Abaskantos the most useful (one)
18   and all your (folk) by name.
19   I pray for your health,
20   with all your household.
21   Mecheir 18.
22   To Julius Apollinarius from Sabinianus, brother
23   of Apollinarius the (…).
