[TRANSLATION]
1     To King [Pto]lemaeus [and Queen Cleopatra]
2     [his] [sister], gods Philometer[-lovers, greetings]
3     Ptol[e]maeus, [son] of Glaucias, [Macedonian, who] [is] in the
4     at Memphis, great Sarapiei[o]n, in seclusion
5     in the Astartieon until now. I am wronged
6     by Argeios, attendant of Dexhelos's station,
7     for he deprives me of the grain-allowances, which you gave me,
8     O king. For of the 24th [year], when you granted to enroll my brother Apollonios and you
9     permitted and [he was] added to Dexilaos's
10    station, [the] aforementioned Argeios drags away
11    him away from me to the liturgies and the grain-allowances [he] does not return, [I] having
12    no other [protector] because of the wicked in that place,
13    both the priests and shrine-attendants and certain others, whenever
14    they learn that [he] is separated for service
15    missing the whole [place], forcibly pledge and outrage me, so that through the window
16    [I] am beaten with stones. About which, when Poseidonios went up
17    the chief body-guard and general
18    in Pharmouthi, I fell in with him through the window
19    and having recalled them [he] rebuked [them].
20    Therefore I beseech [you], O Hel[ios], king, not to overlook me
21    being in seclusion, but, if it seems good to you, [order]
22    to write to Poseidonios, to allow him
23    to be exempt from service, so that he may be with me, and compel Argeios to return to me the [grain]-allowances of the past time,
24    and for the future to be regularly returned
25    rightly and justly with the … [?]sia.
26    And may Isis and Sarapis and the twelve gods who [are] in
27    Herakles's city grant [you] to rule over all
28    land which Helios watches over and [to] your childr[en]
29    for all time.
30    Farewell.
