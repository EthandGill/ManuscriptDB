[TRANSLATION]
1     Many greetings, Hierax, from Asklep[i]ades
2     your brother. [Before] all I pray that you are well
3     and I make your obeisance each
4     day before the goddess Isis of the myriad names and
5     before the lord Apollo and the gods who share his temple.
6     I want you to know that the letters which you indicated through your letter, and the document (biblion),
7     which you gave to the (man) of Pat-tape, I have received all. You will do well, brother,
8     to send off everything and come up; if everything
9     turns out well again, we are able to come down. First, I am alive, but I was about to send you money; I was cautious lest they overtake you in
10    [A]lexandria; for I met Aphrodisios,
11    the one who gave the additional payment (epitheke) to Isido[ros], and he said
12    that whatever need he has, I shall give him; and Isidoros the (son) of Pat-tape said to me that
13    you have gone to Syros the strategos, and he arranged with him
14    to give (it); these things I write to you in duplicate at once, two (copies), lest they overtake you in Alexandria;
15    but I was not able to give him the tunic
16    and the other things, lest you had gone up; for
17    Petasos was saying that he is embarking with Koprilla
18    into the boat of Palaus. We are questioned each day about you and Isidoros. I greet
19    warmly [Isidor]os and Koprilla and al[l]
20    those who [love] us by name. All our (people) greet
21    y[ou].
22    [I pray for your] health. Phamenoth [10].
23    Deliver to Hierax, from As[kl]ep[iades].
