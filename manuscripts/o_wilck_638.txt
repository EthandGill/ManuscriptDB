1     Didymos, collector of money-taxes of Charax, to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis.
2     I have received for the assessment for cushions(?) (tyloi) and other (taxes) of year 12, one base drachma, 3 obols, total base drachma 1, 3 obols.
3     Year 12 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Tybi 23. Pha( ).
