[TRANSLATION]
1     Horos and partners in the allotment of the collection of silver-tax of Charakos
2     to Porieuthos [son] of Herakleos. We received for
3     reefs and doubles year 16 drachmas 2 [and] 3-obols; and for 1
4     year 5 dike-tax year 16 drachma 1 obols 2 [and a half]. Year 17
5     of Antoninus Caesar
6     the master Phaophi 20. Dioskoros the scribe [I] have signed.
