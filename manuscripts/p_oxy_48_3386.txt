[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of the Fl[a]vii Ursus and Polemius,
2     the most illustrious.
3     To the Fl[avi]i Aphthonios (son) of Flavianus, gymnasiarch, councillor,
4     an[d Asy]nkrithion daughter of Achilleus, former
5     vir perfectissimus, and to Amm[o]nion and Helladios, sons
6     of Ammonios, former co[u]ncillor of the most illustrious
7     Alexandria,
8     from Aurelius Aphynchios (son) of Horos, from the splendid
9     and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites. Of my own free will
10    I undertake to lease for a period of
11    two years from the present month Pharmouthi
12    of the present 32nd = 22nd = 14th = 5th year, the (property) belonging
13    to you, according to the share each one holds, in the
14    same city of the Oxyrhynchites, in the quarter
15    [N]emesion, under the eastern colonnade of the
16    city, … the half-share of a hou[se]
17    and basement with all the appurtenances, at a rent
18    per year of twenty [tal]ents of silver,
19    that is, 20 talents only; and the undertaking being guaranteed
20    to me, I shall of necessity pay
21    the rent yearly without arrears, and after
22    the period I shall hand over the half-share of the
23    house free from dung and all filth, and
24    the doors and keys that I may receive,
25    or, whatever I do not hand over, I shall pay its proper value.
26    Valid is the undertaking, and on being questioned I acknowledged it.
27    In the consulship aforesaid, Pharmouthi 2.
28    Aurelius Aphynch[is], I have leased the half-
29    share of the house and shall [pa]y the rent
30    as aforesaid. Aurelius Sarapammon, I wrote
31    on his behalf, he being illiterate.
