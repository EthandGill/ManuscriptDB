1     In the month Neos Sebastos, the 6th,
2     in the third year of Gaius Caesar Augustus
3     Germanicus, in the month Neos Sebastos, the sixth,
4     in Theadelphia of the division of Themistes
5     of the Arsinoite nome. Areios
6     son of Didymos, about thirty-three years old,
7     with a scar on the right forearm, acknowledges to Taphaunis daughter of Harthotes,
8     about thirty-eight years old, with a scar below the right temple, with as guardian her son Aunes
9     son of Harthotes, about twenty-two years old, with a scar
10    above the left eyebrow, that he has received from her eig[hty] drachmas of silver,
11    which Taphaunis owed, together with
12    her own brother Harpathothoes, who has died, to Areios' father Didy[mos]
13    son of Euemeros, who likewise has died,
14    according to two contracts, one indeed
15    of an apprenticeship-bond of sixty drachmas,
16    and the other of a loan, pa…
17    of twenty drachmas, and both
18    completed through the same record-office
19    in former times, which Areios has also returned to Taphaunis
20    for cancellation and annulment, and
21    that neither he, Areios,
22    nor another on his behalf, shall bring a charge or proceed against
