[TRANSLATION]
1     To Quintus Aemilius [Saturninus, prefect of Egypt]
2     from Gemellos [and Horion, son] of Gaius Apolinarius of [Antinoopolis, and as] he is [styled a landowner in Karanidis]
3     of the Arsinoite [nome of the Herakleid division. Long ago,]
4     lord, our [father died and] with me
5     my sister [as heirs, and we took possession of the] property with nothing [coming [against us]. Similarly indeed it happened]
6     also that my uncle G[aius Julius Longinus died]
7     eight years ago [and of this I took control]
8     and [I] collected [the revenue with no one] hinder[ing me]. Now [Julius and Sotas, both sons of Eudatos]
9     improperly, forcibly [and arrogantly came upon my land after I] had [done the sowing, and]
10    they hindered me [in these matters by force at the]
11    places, despising [the weakness concerning my appearance]
12    weakness. Wherefore [I have fled to you, the savior,]
13    asking [if it seem good to your] fortune [to hear me against them,]
14    so that I may be [able to take possession of my own things]
15    and [be] benefited by you [the] lord. Farewell.
