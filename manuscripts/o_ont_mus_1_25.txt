[TRANSLATION]
1     [Ka]rouris and Petechespochrates Phatreous and partners, overseers of properties being registered for taxation
2     [of the metropolis] [Pe]tepsaiti Pankametes Phthumonthu, we received for addition
3     [for the price] of dates, produce of year 7, unrefined drachmas 8 and three obols, total drachmas 8 and three obols
4     year 8 of Antoninus Caesar the lord
5     Hathyr 16. Karouris [I] have signed. Other [entries] 26, likewise unrefined drachmas 8 and three obols, total drachmas 8 and three obols. Karouris [I] have signed.
