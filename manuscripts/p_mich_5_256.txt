[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Akes son of Pisais, acknowledge that I have ceded to Orsenouphis son of Pr…, from the property belonging to me about the hamlet of Herakleides
2     of the division of Polemon, of sacred land of the god Souchos, five arouras, or however many they may be, in one sealed (plot), apart from the supplementary assessment of the
3     village, of which the neighbours of the whole holding are: on the south the holding of Ptolemaios son of …; on the north the holding of Psenobastis; on the west the canal through which the holding is watered; on the east the canal through which the holding is drained. And I receive from
4     Orsenouphis the agreed
5     monetary cession-price, the whole sum, and I shall guarantee the sale with every guarantee, free on the one hand from all public
6     debts and all imposts and embankment-dues and the artaba-tax and land-price, except the supplementary assessment of the village, and all the other
7     things demanded from the times above up to the past fifteenth
8     year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus and that same fifteenth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, and on the other hand from private claims and every encumbrance for all time as set out above. Marsisouchos son of Marepsemis wrote on his behalf
9     because he does not know letters.
