[TRANSLATION]
1     [..] to Epaphroditus [you] not worthy, greetings […]
2     [..] our faith. You agreed with me. You received the price through Skenus our brother
3     being ours. But I have gratitude to the gods that …
4     ours became Skenus; but if not, you denied both the
5     [copper] through [..] you did not take my opinion. Concerning the young dolphins you imposed other [things].
6     [I] handed over to you concerning them drachmas […] and not […]
7     […] of Proklos. But I know my fortune;
8     [but] you think me still in the pit
9     to be, and so [I] had luxury in the pit; here I do not have [it]. Or does the agreement which you have not please you
10    with me? You will do well by giving
11    […] the copper.
