[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Ammonios,
2     son of Ammonios, shipmaster
3     of the administration of Nea Polis, of three ships
4     of a capacity of 11,000 artabas, to Aurelius
5     Sarapion, grain-collector of the upper toparchy
6     of the district of Sko, greeting. I have received and have had measured out to me from
7     you the (grain) ordered for me by both
8     the strategos Aurelius Harpokration and Aurelius Nemesion
9     also called Dionysios, royal scribe,
10    those appointed over
11    the lading following along,
12    and the others whose presence is required, from
13    the public granaries of the aforesaid grain-collectorship, to the harbor
14    of Satyros on the great river, of wheat of the produce of the
15    past 3rd year, pure, unadulterated,
16    free of clods, free of barley, untrodden,
17    sifted, by the public half-artaba measure,
18    by the measurement ordered, with
19    the one-hundredth additional 1 and the half-artaba, artabas
20    seventy-seven, total artabas 77,
21    which I shall also bring down to Alexandria and deliver to the
22    administration at Nea Polis, the cargo full and unhandled.
23    The receipt is valid, written in triplicate, to you
24    the grain-collector single, but to the strategos
25    double, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged.
26    Year 4 of the Emperor Caesar
27    Marcus Aurelius Severus
28    Alexander Pius Felix
29    Augustus, Pharmouthi 11.
30    Aurelius Ammonios son of Ammonios,
31    shipmaster of the administration of Nea
32    Polis, I have measured out and laden the (artabas) of wheat
33    with the half-artaba [and] the one [hundredth, artabas seventy-seven] …
