1     Alexander the gold-receiver to the most wonderful [ones] from Acanthus: you have paid through
2     Abraham, agent of Butu, from the gold-[dues] of the eleventh indiction, in the month Pachon of the twelfth indiction
3     of gold coins one, keratia twenty-one and a half, by the standard of Memphis, total gold coins 1, keratia
4     21 and a half, only, Pharmouthi 30th, indiction 12; likewise the same [persons] have paid from the gold-[dues] of the same eleventh indiction
5     through the same Abraham, in the month Phauni 3rd, indiction 12, gold coins 1, keratia 7, total gold coins 1, keratia 7, only, Pharmouthi 30th, indiction 12.
6     Pharmouthi 30th, indiction 12, coins 3, keratia 4 [½]
7     of those from Acanthus
