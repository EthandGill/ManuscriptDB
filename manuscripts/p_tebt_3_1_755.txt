[TRANSLATION]
1     Heliodorus to Epidoros
2     greetings. If you are well
3     and the rest for you goes according to reason
4     it is good,
5     and I myself also moderately
6     am faring. And formerly
7     I happen to have written to you how I
8     should talk with you before
9     I sail down,
10    and now, if it seems so, you will do well
11    arranging a meeting with me
12    especially on the 20th
13    to the grain-collector Epidoros.
