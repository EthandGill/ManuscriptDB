[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, harvest of year 31
2     of Commodus Antoninus Caesar
3     of the lord Epeiph 18 for South
4     in the name of Psenamounios (son) of Herakleous, of wheat, artabae one-half
5     one-third, 12; there is of wheat, artabae one-half, one-third, 12. Paniskos, I have signed, of wheat, artabae one-half, one-third, 12.
