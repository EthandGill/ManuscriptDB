[TRANSLATION]
1     of which [for fixed export grain] grain
2     six and a half artabas; of pulse [six] artabas
3     six and a half [   ] which collected taxes
4     I will measure out to you in Epeiph month each year
5     without delay in new, pure [produce]
6     winnowed by Athenian measure
7     on the threshing floor of the same village of public [lands]
8     of all sorts of embankments being toward you[r]
9     the landholders. The lease is valid and when questioned I agreed.
10    Phamenoth 30.
11    Aurelius Manos son of Pahes[ius] I have leased as aforesaid. Aurelius
12    Silvanus son of Kopreus having been requested wrote on his behalf letters though not knowing [how to write].
13    by me Serenous it was written.
