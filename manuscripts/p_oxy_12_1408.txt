1     … 2.
2     … give. Sopatros [said:] "Fifteen will be enough for you." Tryphon said: "On this condition, however, that if I am sued within the appointed time, I have the right of action against him."
3     [Sopa]tros said: "This, even apart from the petition and the memoranda, the declaration and the assumption of the guarantee and the bond on behalf of the father, the trustworthy (one),
4     furnish [to you]." Asklepiades said: "According to the share that falls to me." Tryphon said: "The
5     … [are mu]tual guarantors. For there is one appeal and one penalty." Sopatro[s]
6     said to [Asklepi]ades: "Take care to come down within the fifteen days
7     [and] to deposit the [pen]alty in full, for it is a mutual guarantee of yours, so that the most estimable Tryphon may have security."
8     [Baibius] Iuncinus, to the strategoi of the Seven nomes and of the Arsinoite, greeting.
9     I have indeed, also through other letters of mine, given orders carefully
10    to make the search for the brigands, holding over you the danger if you are negligent; and now too by edict I wished to confirm my intention, so that all those throughout Egypt may know that I do not lay down this
11    duty as a thing of small account, but also set rewards before you who help in arresting (them), and a danger
12    [for those who] choose to be disobedient, held over them. Which edict I wish
13    to be posted [both in the me]tropoleis and in the most conspicuous [places] of the nomes,
14    [a penalty being laid upon you] together with danger, if any criminal escapes notice and is able to use [vio]lence. … I wish you good health.
15    … [in the year ] … Phaophi 28.
16    [Lucius Ba]ibius Aurelius Iuncinus, prefect of Egypt, declares:
17    "[That brigands can]not be destroyed apart from those who harbor them is clear to [all];
18    [but] when they are stripped of those who surround them, we shall quickly [punish them] … there are many ways of harboring. For some, [sharing in the crimes,] harbor them, while others, though not participating, … …
