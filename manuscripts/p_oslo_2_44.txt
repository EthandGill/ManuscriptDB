[TRANSLATION]
1     Lollia[ne] also Plutiena through me
2     Thoni[us] superintendent to Papnutius farmer, greetings.
3     I received from you for what you farm
4     [produce] of the 12th indiction of wheat artabas two quarter choenicea six; total wheat artabas two quarter choenicea six, and silver
5     [drachmas] eight hundred and the meat and the ch[aff].
6     Of the year 19 [and] 17 and 9, Thoth 6. The same Thonios I have signed.
7     The same [and] for the produce of the 13th indiction.
8     [Provided] Papnutius wheat artabas
9     [two] [quarter] choenicea six, total wheat artabas two quarter choenicea six
10    and the [silver] tax drachmas eight hundred and the
11    selection [yours] of the meat and chaff.
12    Of the year 19 and ninth and 1 Epeiph 30
13    The same Thonios superintendent I have signed
