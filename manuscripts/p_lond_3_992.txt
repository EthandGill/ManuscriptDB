[TRANSLATION]
1     In the [consul]ship of our master Flavius [An]astasius the eternal Augustus, Imperator,
2     for the 3rd time, Epeiph …, of the first indiction.
3     [This] agreement of compromise (the parties) make with one another:
4     [on] the one part the Flavii Silvanus and Sarapion, soldiers of the regiment
5     of Hermopolis, and Aurelius Isakos, full brothers from their father
6     Phoib[am]mon, from the same city — Isakos making the declaration on behalf both of himself
7     and on behalf of [the heir]s of Tyrannos, our deceased brother;
8     and on [the] other part Flavius Bes (son) of Nikon, soldier of the same regiment, and
9     his wife Archontia, sister of the aforewritten (parties) from the same
10    parents, from the same city — to one another, greeting. Since we have disputed
11    with one another about manifest sums of money, and before litigation and contention
12    it seemed good to us by common consent to resort to arbitration before Kardemeos and
13    Zenodotos, the most eloquent advocates of the court of the Thebaid, and to abide by
14    and obey what shall be decided by their learning — accordingly we acknowledge,
15    swearing both by Almighty God and by the divine and heavenly fortune of our all-
16    conquering master Flavius Anastasius the eternal Augustus, Imperator,
17    that we shall appear before the aforesaid arbitrators and submit to their arbitration
18    until the eighth [of the present] month Epeiph of the present first indiction,
19    [and obey all the things] that shall be decided by them; and whoever does not appear
20    [before them or also does not obey] the things decided by them shall provide,
21    [for breach of the agreement,] to the party abiding (by it), by way of penalty, of gold solidi
22    [twelve, without dispute. This agree]ment is valid and secure, and on being questioned we have acknowledged it.
23    … the [a]foresaid (agreement) is satisfactory to me as set out above. Aurelia Archontia (daughter) of Phoibammon —
24    [I have made this] [compro]mise as set out above. Asterios (son) of Kollouthos, from Antinoopolis, wrote on her behalf,
25    [she not knowing letters] …
26    [Through] me, Horos, it was written, o …
