[TRANSLATION]
1     Were given through Menas the pork-butcher to the 10 soldiers remaining in the glorious house, as an account of expenses
2     of the month Pachon of the 14th indiction, meat: three hundred pounds. And to Faustina remaining in the same
3     glorious house, as an account of expenses from the month Pachon 15 through 30, for 16 days, meat: sixteen pounds; there are of meat [sixteen] pounds alone.
4     There are of meat three hundred sixteen pounds Pachon 15
5     14th indiction.
6     Year 327, 196, Pachon 15, of the 14th indiction.
7     [to the] soldiers.
