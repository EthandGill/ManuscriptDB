[GREEK]
r.1    Φιλάδελφος Νεμεσίωνος ἀπὸ Ἐροάθεως
r.2    εἰδὼς γράμματα ἐτῶν με
r.3    γενόμενος γραμματεὺς νομῶν τινων ἰδίου λόγου καὶ εἰσαγωγεὺς στρατηγοῦ Ἀμμωνιακῆς καὶ σεκκ χωρατα

[TRANSLATION]
1     Philadelphos of Nemesion from Eroatheos
2     knowing letters, [of] 45 years
3     having become scribe of some nomes [under] his own account and introducer to the strategos of Ammoniacae and sekke chorata
4     of [the territory] Cabaseitou and boundary and revenues
5     Methelite[s], now being scribe of the royal [treasury] of Libya,
6     to whom belong around Eroathi arouras 11 [worth] drachmae 500
7     and in it house-plots [worth] drachmae 2500
8     and around Aurineitae arouras 30 [of] talent 1,
9     Total talents 2 drachmae 1500.
10    in the cleansing of the public wheat [storage] place around Syria
