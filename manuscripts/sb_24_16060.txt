[TRANSLATION]
1     Artemias [of Artemidorus] to Besarion, Besa …
2     and Apolinaris, to the kibariatae, of the month Epeiph, [greetings].
3     To Harbeschain of Pachnoumieus: drachmas 42 triobol
4     To Sansnot P … gheti: drachmas 8
5     Sell the wheat and pay … for the replenishment
6     of the wages [my], which I gave
7     orders the imposition to the kibariatae.
