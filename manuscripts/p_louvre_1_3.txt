[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Calvisius Maximus, centurion,
2     from Aurelius Pakysis son of Tesenouphis,
3     priest and stolistes of the renowned temple of the village
4     of Soknopaiou Nesos in the division of Herakleides.
5     I have a place in the house of the wife of my son Aurelius
6     Tesenouphis, in the hamlet called Pisai
7     in the division of Themistes, in which are
8     the grain-supplies stored away for my sustenance.
9     Recently, then, when my household members entered the place — because I was in Alexandria — the grain-supplies were not found, having been lightened (diminished). The cause of the
10    theft appeared (to be) that, the place being an upper room, through the floor being pierced through
11    the crime was committed. And those dwelling inside, being convicted that the
12    deed had been done through their insolence,
13    promised, through both the
14    village chief-constable and through [oth]ers, to give
15    on account of the the[f]t seven artabas of wheat; but since to the promise
16    they agreed, yet to the repayment until n[ow]
17    they have not made good, of necessity I make
18    my recourse to you, and I ask that the accused, Panouphis son of Stotoetis and Pakysis son of Kanneis, be brought, so that
19    from your authority I may be able, in place of
20    the larger amount stolen, to recover the seven artabas of wheat assessed for me. Farewell. Aurelius
21    Pakysis, I have submitted (this).
22    In the 24th year of Marcus Aurelius Severus
23    Antoninus Parthicus Maximus Britannicus
24    Maximus Germanicus Maximus Pious
25    Augustus, Pharmouthi 12.
