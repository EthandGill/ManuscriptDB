[TRANSLATION]
1     … the … to have received from him according to the right
2     of this agreement, as dowry, twelve minae-weights of assayed gold in
3     the ornaments specified through the contract, and the garments through it …
4     … of them … the valuation of the eight hundred drachmas
5     and all her own parapherna, and to have recovered all the things
6     brought to her by her own mother, belonging (to her) …
7     … and that Syros has received the share falling to (him) of
8     their revenues, of which Syra also called Isarion remained with him for (so many) months,
9     and that they shall not proceed against one another, neither of them, nor
10    those of theirs, neither concerning the things pertaining to the cohabitation, nor concerning those for which
11    Syros was registered as her guardian in the times of the cohabitation, in whatever
12    way, because she has received everything, nor concerning the aforementioned items, nor concerning
13    any other thing whatever at all, written or unwritten, up to the present
14    day, on any pretext whatsoever. Let the things mutually agreed be valid as they agreed.
15    Syra also called Isarion, daughter of Aphrodeisios: I have dissolved the
16    cohabitation with Syros and I have (back) the dowry, the twelve minae-weights of gold
17    and the garments at a valuation of eight hundred drachmas and all the parapherna,
18    and I have recovered the things brought to me by
19    my mother, belonging (to me); I shall not proceed against him concerning any part nor concerning the things for which he was registered as my guardian in the times of the
20    cohabitation in any way whatever, as is stated above. Syros
21    son of Dios: I have dissolved the cohabitation and I have received the share falling (to me) of the revenues, of which
22    she remained with me for (so many) months, and I shall not proceed concerning any other thing,
23    as is stated above.
