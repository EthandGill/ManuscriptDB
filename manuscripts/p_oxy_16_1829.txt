[TRANSLATION]
1     On the tenth of [the present month it was written] to me by the
2     [m]ost magnificent governor that the schedules of the
3     pagarchy of your father of glorious memory had been presented, and there were discussed
4     [upon] them the proper matters; and so that your Authority may know that
5     [the] command of yours was not neglected, but was at once fulfilled by the most magnificent (governor) himself
6     (in) the Herakleopolite (nome); and except for as much as my master commanded
7     [the gov]ernor having arrived,
8     [your all]-praiseworthy brother — it was not given to the collectors —
9     [up]on the Lord God; that, also the most illustrious tribune Paulos
10    [arriv]ing at the Oxyrhynchite (nome), there are fulfilled the things commanded
11    [b]y your [A]u[th]ority on behalf of his most illustrious sister.
12    On the tenth of the present month it was written to me by the
13    [most ma]gnif[icent] governor that, the schedules of (the affairs) of your father-in-law of glorious
14    [mem]ory having been presented, and a proper discussion
15    [h]aving come forth — and so that your Eminence may know
16    [th]at, the most magnificent governor having arrived, at once your command
17    was carried out; and except that, as you wished and directed, the
18    collector was not answered against, I made use of the letters. I hope, then, in the
19    [Lo]rd God, that, the most illustrious Paulos the tribune also arriving at
20    [the] places here, both your command and that of my mistress the glorious Kyra
21    [c]oncerning the few matters that ought to be divided
22    [be]tween you and the most illustrious lady Theognosia, will be fulfilled.
23    And as the main point of the letter: I greatly do obeisance to your
24    Authority and to the sweetest children.
25    [To Fl]avius Strategios, the most glorious and most eminent and most eminent and all-praiseworthy.
