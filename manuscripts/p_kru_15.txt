r.99   αἰτεῖν ⲙⲙⲟⲓ ⲁⲓⲥϩⲁⲓ ϩⲁⲣⲟⲟⲩ ϫⲉ ⲙⲉⲩνοεῖν πρός ⲧⲉⲩαἴτησις
r.100  [ δ]ιʼ ἐμοῦ Ἀριστοφάνους υἱοῦ Ἰωάννου ἐγένετο

[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity,
2     Father and Son and Holy Spirit. Written in the month Hathyr 12, of the 10th indiction,
3     from Diocletian, the years to this day being 473.
4     Under our master, through Flavius Kometos son of Chael, administrator of the fortress of Memnonion.
5     I, Damianos, the son of the blessed Kosma — his mother
6     was the blessed Taounan — the man of the fortress of Djeme, the nome of the city
7     of Hermonth — having afterwards a subscriber to subscribe
8     on my behalf, and calling some further trustworthy witnesses
9     who shall testify to it on my behalf, according to my request —
10    write this deed of sale, not to be transgressed
11    and not to be shaken out of the laws.
12    I write to Aaron, the son of the blessed Shenoute — his mother
13    was Talek — the man of this same fortress of Djeme, the nome
14    of this same city of Hermonth, greeting: that I acknowledge,
15    I, Damianos, the son of the blessed Kosma, the one who has first
16    written to you, you Aaron, son of Shenoute, the buyer
17    and the one to whom I have first written, according to this sale,
18    which is in writing, to be guaranteed and valid in one matter,
19    by it. I have inclined toward it of my own wish and
20    being persuaded without any deceit or fear or violence
21    or fraud or extortion or coercion, with no single compulsion laid upon me, but in all
22    good and fair purpose. And I swear afterwards
23    by God the Almighty and by the salvation
24    of our lords who rule over us now, through
25    the command of God, that I give to you, you,
26    Aaron, son of the blessed Shenoute, the buyer, and
27    the one to whom I have first named it many times, in every lordship of a just law of an outright sale,
28    and I write to you from today and from now on,
29    forever, the whole house which is on the street of the men of Pshoumare,
30    this which I have donated to the athlophoros (prize-bearer)
31    and the nikophoros (victory-bearer) and stephanophoros (crown-bearer), the gloriously victorious and the martyr who shines forth, the holy
32    Abba Victor of the fortress of Djeme, for the salvation
33    of his soul, that of Pahom son of Georgios, so that
34    he may stand in mercy before God who is exalted
35    above everyone — that you may become lord of that house, this
36    which is set on the street of the heirs of the men of
37    Pshoumare, according to its boundaries, which surround it, which are these:
38    the south, Katharon, the daughter of Phet;
39    Joseph the Cripple, the west; Ananias the son,
40    the east; Pannh son of Petros, the son of Petale, the boundaries
41    these are of the house, which is enclosed on every side
42    — every side. The price is this,
43    which we agreed upon together: it is four holokottinos (solidi)
44    of refined gold of the measure of the fortress of Djeme, that is, four nomismata,
45    by the weight of the fortress of Memnonion, the complete price, which came into my hand, from you, you Aaron, son of the blessed Shenoute,
46    the buyer and the one to whom I first wrote, from hand to hand,
47    from the house, in approved gold, and as a capital sum,
48    for every guarantee. From now on, then, to you,
49    Aaron, son of Shenoute, to whom I have explained
50    it, you shall come in and take possession, and you shall become
51    the lord of that house,
52    from its foundations up to its appurtenances, with the other utensils
53    belonging to it; because I have received and have been paid in full by you
54    of the complete price, by you, Aaron, son of Shenoute, the buyer, and the one to whom I first wrote,
55    according to the agreement which I first declared,
56    from above, to beget today, or to beget again, or to administer
57    it, to manage it, to adorn
58    it, to dwell in it, to give it to a stranger, to donate
59    it, to cede it, to make it an exchange, to give it forth, to grant it, to give it away,
60    to give it away, to leave it to your children
61    and to your heirs, to use it in
62    every manner that pleases you, according to every possession and
63    lordship and tenancy eternally, properly
64    and without hindrance. At whatever time anyone shall come
65    forth against you ever, neither I nor a brother nor a sister
66    nor son of one nor son of two, nor on behalf of my father
67    and my mother, who shall sue you in a tribunal
68    or outside a tribunal, within the district or outside
69    the district, or who shall establish any proceeding
70    against you, anyone in authority, exalted or in glory,
71    who shall bring a charge against you, or shall bring a charge against you, or a part
72    of it as a whole, or who shall move against you — at the hand of
73    any disturbance of the praetorium —
74    first he shall not profit in anything,
75    primarily he shall be a stranger to the Father
76    and the Son and the Holy Spirit, and
77    he shall pay to the account of a fine thirty-three
78    holokottinos of gold, of the authority which exacts in this very time,
79    apart from the penalty which our law
80    determines against the one who shall dare and transgress.
81    And on top of all [these] he shall come in and confirm this sale
82    which is in writing. For its security, then, I have established it, firm,
83    valid in every place where they shall produce
84    it, through any authority and any power, and they shall question (us), and we shall acknowledge it; and after all these,
85    it was read to me; I approved it; I declared, "It stands
86    in this manner." I have confirmed it with a witness and a confirmation;
87    I have let it go forth in every regular form.
88    I, Tanos, son of Kosma,
89    agree to this sale.
90    I, Papnoute, son of the blessed Stephanos, give witness.
91    I, Stratike, son of the blessed Ilias, with Komete,
92    the son of the blessed Apa Victor, we give witness.
93    I, Petros, the least priest, have written on their behalf, because
94    they do not know how to write, and I give witness.
95    I, Mena Chamoul, give witness.
96    I, Abraham Aron, give witness.
97    Mena Chamoul: I have written on his behalf, because he does not know (how to write),
98    the son of the blessed Iohannes; he asked me in person,
99    asking me; I have written on their behalf, because they do not know (how to write), according to their request.
100   [Through] me, Aristophanes son of Iohannes, it was drawn up.
