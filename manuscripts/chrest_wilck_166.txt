[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ptolemaios the epimeletes, from Theophilos
2     the agent of Antikles in charge of the exportation of
3     the royal grain in the Arsinoite, of the ships through him.
4     Pinyris and Erianoupis and
5     the shipwrights who are in the Arsinoite engaged in
6     the repair of the exporting barges (carrying) the royal grain,
7     having received in advance from Ptolemaios son of
8     Asklepiades … for works of the new ships
9     and … for the repair of (the ships) through Antikles,
10    went off to Herakleopolis, and Herakleides the chief of police detained them.
11    But Ptolemaios having appealed to Herakleides the oikonomos and pleading the need,
12    he wrote to Herakleides the chief of police to release them,
13    since he too refrained from making the appeal (himself).
14    But now I learn that Herakleides has not heeded the letter of Herakleides the oikonomos about releasing (them), unless
15    either you or the dioiketes write to him.
16    In order, therefore, that the exportation of the grain not be held up,
17    the ships not being repaired forthwith, and this when a considerable
18    multitude (of grain) has fallen due, both from the purchased (grain)
19    and from the freight-grain, but … to a ship, or because it is permitted (that) it be delivered, I ask you, if it seems good to you, to give order
20    to write to Herakleides the chief of police in the Herakleopolite to release them,
21    so that they may attend to the need and the ships not lie idle.
22    For I think that not even you are unaware that of the …
23    grain at the … and the storehouse of Boukoloi village, [if] it
24    be exported by pack-animals, the difference would be …
25    over the 100 artabas, 5 drachmas.
