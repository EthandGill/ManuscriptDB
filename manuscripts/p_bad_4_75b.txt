[TRANSLATION]
1     To Julius Saturninus,
2     strategos of the Herakleopolite nome,
3     from Petesouchos son of Pisois,
4     his mother being Thenamenneus, of those fro[m]
5     the village of Ankyron. I register
6     for the 9th year of Antoninus Caesar
7     the lord, in accordance with the orders given
8     by Valerius Proclus the
9     prefect, for the half-share belonging to me of
10    a house and a vacant lot.
11    And I am as follows:
12    I, Petesouchos, aged 42, without distinguishing mark,
13    my wife Tausiris daughter of Pareis, aged 34,
14    Pnephoros my son, aged 17,
15    Psenamounis another son of mine,
16    aged 5, without distinguishing mark.
17    Pnephoros, his son who reached 17 years of age, [abroad].
18    And there belong to me in the same village
19    other buildings, and to my wife Tausiris other buildings.
20    And I swear by the fortune of the Emperor
21    Caesar Titus Aelius
22    Hadrianus Antoninus
23    Augustus Pius,
24    and by the god of the nome,
25    Herakles, that soundly and
26    truthfully I have submitted the foregoing registration,
27    and that I have in no way given false statement nor
28    omitted any person owed
29    to be registered by me, nor that any namesake
30    has transacted business, nor that I have set up anyone in opposition to the scrutiny; or may I be liable
31    to the oath.
32    Year 10 of the Emperor Caesar
33    [Titu]s [Aeli]us Hadrianus
34    Antoninus Augustus
35    Pius, Phamenoth 15.
36    Mareis through Alphynchis, Phib, Oue …
37    Harpsemis and Pachnoubis,
38    Ep…ros … iospho … ssion
39    I, Isidoros the village-scribe, through Panas, have signed off.
