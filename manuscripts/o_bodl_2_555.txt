1     Pauni 3 of the 10th year on behalf of Philon Claudius
2     Besarion on account of head-tax and other [levies] of the 9th year drachmas sixteen
3     [they] total drachmas 16. Harpokras [I] have signed-off
4     Pauni 3 of the 10th year on behalf of heirs Karouerios younger Lenoulow …
5     on account of geometry of South and West drachmas two pentobols, [they] total drachmas 2 pentobols. Ammonios [I] have signed-off
