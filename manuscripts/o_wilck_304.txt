[TRANSLATION]
1   Antonis Malchais, the one occupied with
2   the harbor-guard of Syene, to Harpaesis
3   the goose-keeper, greeting. I have received from you
4   the mooring-due for the freight-loads which you made, fr-
5   om Choiak 30 to Pharmouthi 30 of year 18
6   of Trajan Optimus Caesar the lord.
7   Pharmouthi 30. (2nd hand) Antonios Malchaios,
8   I have (it).
