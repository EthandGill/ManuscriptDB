[TRANSLATION]
1     From Chairemon greetings to [my] lord
2     Theon. Know that I sought
3     all of Sentow and found not
4     from anyone in Sentow
5     modii 4 of drachmas 24, and I brought modii 2 of drachmas 20
6     [to] the oxen of Sentow. For not
7     do they have fodder nor chaff
8     in Sentow, [nor] in Tholthe is fodder. The son of Horeigenes went out and I complained
9     him because he is angry toward you
10    the respectable one. I then exhorted
11    them to give us modii 15 from drachmas 17 obol. Send to me then
12    for the expenses a small coin
13    and barley for the sowing.
14    I wish you to be well, lord …
