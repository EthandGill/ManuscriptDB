[TRANSLATION]
1     To Quintus Aemilius Saturninus,
2     prefect of Egypt,
3     from Ammonius son of Horos and Orsenouphis son of Pa… and Peteuris son of Peteuris,
4     from the village of Bakchias in the Arsinoite nome, division of Herakleides:
5     it is the custom of the priests of Souchos,
6     the great god, to pay an eight-drachma (tax) for
7     poll-taxes. We pay (it)
8     to the collectors of the village, from whom
9     we also obtain receipts. Nothing
10    else … the worship toward the
11    gods. But of those who covet (gain), most mighty prefect, … … …
12    an old (custom?) in the village … …
13    [we] wish … …
14    of the canals … himself,
15    having entered into the house … ‡1 …
16    ordering them to work in the [canals]
17    … and if [not], … …
18    120 drachmas … … help …
19    … to command, through your sacred
20    subscription, the strategos of the nome, [s]o that he may compel the
21    aforew[ri]tten (men) to restore the (sums) which they unduly
22    extorted from us, one hundred twenty drachmas, until
23    we obtain justice, so that, through your fortune,
24    we may be able to attend to
25    the work, not being exploited
26    by anyone, paying
27    to the treasury the customary eight-drachma (tax) for poll-taxes, so that we may be benefited. Farewell.
28    Ammonis [and] Orsenouphis [and] Peteuris have submitted (this);
29    they came themselves …
30    In the 7th year, Hathyr 12.
31    No one being detained (?), petition the
32    most excellent epistrategos.
33    Deliver (it).
