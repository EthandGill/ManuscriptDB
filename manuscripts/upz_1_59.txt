[TRANSLATION]
1     Isias to Hephaistion, her brother, [greetings.]
2     If you are [in good] health [and] everything else with you proceeds as is fitting,
3     it is as I continually pray to the gods [that it should be], and I myself am also in good health
4     and the child and all those in the house
5     of you, making constant mention [of you].
6     Having received the letter from you
7     from Horos, in which you explained that you are
8     in custody in the Sarapieum
9     in Memphis, on the one hand, at your being in good health
10    I immediately gave thanks to the gods,
11    but on the other hand, at [your] not coming, [while all]
12    of the things there, having been cut off, have [come to pass]
13    I am distressed on account of such a
14    time, [both myself and your child]
15    [I] have been managing and in every respect
16    [I] have gone through on account of the price of grain
17    and [I] was seeming now that you having come
18    [I would] obtain some relief, but you
19    have not even considered coming
20    or concerned yourself with our situation, as if [still] you being present [I would] need all,
21    not to mention so much time having passed
22    and such times and [you] having sent nothing.
23    Still moreover, Horos, the one who brought the letter, reported [that] you have been released
24    from custody, [and] I am completely distressed.
25    Nevertheless, since also your mother happens
26    to be ill, [you] will do well both on account of her
27    and on account of us, coming to the city, if indeed [it is] not
28    something more pressing [that] claims on you. [You] will do well also
29    taking care of [your] body so that you stay healthy.
30    Farewell. Year 2, Epiphi 30.
31    To Hephaistion.
