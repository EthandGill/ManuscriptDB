[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy and Queen Cleopatra,
2     the sister, gods Philometors, greetings.
3     Ptolemy, son of Glaucius, Macedonian, of those
4     in possession [of office], this year
5     the thirteenth in the [great sanctuary] toward Memphis
6     great Sarapieion. [There] being for me
7     an ancestral house about the village Psichis of the
8     Herakleopolis, and this [house] being destroyed,
9     and the goods from it having been carried off,
10    being worth twenty talents of bronze, by the
11    neighbors of mine Hesperus and Ataeus the
12    son of him and the brother of him Polemon,
13    the aforementioned, not having been satisfied with what they
14    had transacted, furthermore also the adjoining
15    courtyard and the place of-the house bare
16    having enclosed [it], use as their own,
17    despising [on the ground] that I am unable
18    having gone out from the temple and having come
19    to the places to make against them
20    a charge. Since then, greatest king and
21    queen, on the one hand concerning the carried-off
22    goods I am unable at the present time a charge
23    to make against them, but concerning the [house] which they enclosed, the things of-the father of-me
24    having been left, and use as-they might choose
25    manner, I beseech you to send of-me the
26    petition to Cydias the of-the district
27    strategos, so that having summoned the
28    aforementioned [men] he might order them no longer
29    to force [their way] into the aforementioned places,
30    but to hand over to those with me, and concerning
31    the [act] of violence [which] has been done, [he] might consider with hatred-of-wrong,
32    so that I having also shared of-the of-you
33    for life support.
34    Fare well.
