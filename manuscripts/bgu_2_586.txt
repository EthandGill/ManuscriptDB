[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ouranios, son of Theo[do]ros, councillor of the city of the Ars[i]noi[t]es,
2     fro[m] Pau[los], son of Theod[o]ros, [who was] a coun[cil]lor of the city of the [Arsi]noi[tes], and
3     Athepation, mother Kopr[i]a, from the qu[arte]r of the Other Chenoboskoi. We wish to lease from yo[u the] arable land belonging (to you)
4     around the village of Boukoloi,
5     th[i]rteen arouras or however many there may be, by measurement with the cord, for the sowing only of the present year, in partnership,
6     for a half share, the rent of each aroura
7     (being) of common, unadulterated wheat, one and
8     a half artabas, free of deduction and at our own risk,
9     and it shall not be permitted to us within the period to abandon the lease in any way, but also of necessity we shall perform all the year's work
10    as much as is due, from our own (resources) at the proper seasons, with us doing no
11    damage, and you the owner furnishing all the four-footed beasts and the see[d] … for the
12    use, all the public (taxes) being
13    on you the owner, and after a little (while) [to you]
14    we shall hand over the arouras …
15    … which … . The l[ease is va]lid, and on being qu[estion]ed we ack[nowledged].
16    [To the future] consuls [the fo]urth (time).
17    [The …] I, Paulos, have leased as aforesaid.
18    I, A[th]e[pation], have leased as aforesaid.
