1     Paorpkes [son] of Sakhomnetos has recorded
2     for poll-tax of the 3rd year Memnonian sixteen drachmas; likewise for
3     enkyklion and other [taxes] two drachmas; likewise for bath-tax
4     two drachmas of the 3rd year of Tiberius Claudius
5     Caesar Augustus Germanicus
6     Imperator, Mesore 22nd
7     likewise of the month Soter 13th for
8     dike-tax three drachmas four obols half-obol; likewise 17th for
9     dike-tax two drachmas five obols half-obol
