1     … inheritance … [has come to be]. …
2     … [undertaking] of Callinicus for sale. [Liberalis]
3     [having considered] with [those in the] council said: if those around Achonaros ... [should] wish the gymnasium[y] to give back, one of them [let be gymnasia]rch [and] this [one let] be named gymnasi[arch]. [He] shall purchase the estate [from the others]
4     heirs [so that if] there are certain voluntary [ones] having undertaken, after [those] gymnasiarch[y, if] not, after the present gymnasi arch [to be] archon.
5     another upon the dead;
6     Artemidorus having been brought forward, Sarapion the younger rhetor said: our
7     father Cassius bore the gymnasiarchy of this city from the sixteenth
8     year into the seventeenth year. Having been unable and he to complete the
9     gymnasiarchy, and still dying in the eighteenth year, his son was seized and
10    [acted as] gymnasiarch in the same eighteenth year and requests decrees all
11    honors which [he] bore through a year these himself preserve. L[iberalis]
12    inquired: whether anyone speaks against? With no one having objected, Liberalis [said: he will]
13    the honors.
14    year nineteen of God Aelius [Antoninus] … sixteen.
