[TRANSLATION]
1     … [greetin]gs. And being present with you, I remembered concerning Sokmhenios
2     … of the deceased [man's] lot in the Pseur inside
3     … [I went] to Pakysis the village-headman, so that he might hand it over to him.
4     Having received then, Pakysis, [the] letters from you, he says [they] must be inscribed to Aristander
5     and to Philon the secretary epi …, so that he might register [it] into this.
6     You will do well then, just as you promised, ordering Philon also and [ordering] Aristander
7     having written to hand over to him the land.
