[TRANSLATION]
1     [It is] laid down …
2     arouras two. To them [I] leave whatever [I leave and which as]
3     [it] is laid down [they] left to them house-lots furnishings and fur[niture and household-contents]
4     and all other things in whatever manner. But as for other things I leave [them] unmanaged being and all other things in whatever manner simi[larly]
5     unmanaged to be I wish: of the daughter my Tsensarapion [according to]
6     the half part, but of those around Hermes from equal according to the other half part
7     and if each [one] leaves [it], but if not, of his children. But the other thing I had
8     concerning Theia from Kallicrates' share, a vineyard property [I] managed to regis[ter]
