[TRANSLATION]
1     In the [name] of God
2     These: Father Kyrin redeemed from Alasuad ben Adah his son
3     For fifty coins; from them twenty coins [he granted]
4     Alasuad to Father Kyrin them, and thirty coins [he completed]
5     them Father Kyrin to Alasuad ben Adah, and agreed Alasuad ben Adah [Father]
6     Kyrin; because of his son he has [authority] [where] he wishes; he has [authority] to depart, and Alasuad has nothing against him because of the son [of the Father]
7     Kyrin concerning small or great; and the boy himself has authority over his soul [wherever] he goes, so that no one has against him a word, neither I Alasu[ad] [nor]
8     my [heirs] and my successors. It was written by my hand, Father George Vict[o]r[o]s, on the third of the month Peritius, indiction 15, of the year according to Elephantine φπα
9     Sergios of George the Patrician, I testify as was stated above
