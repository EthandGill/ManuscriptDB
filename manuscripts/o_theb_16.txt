[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 51, Pauni, has measured into the [treasury] in Zeus-city
2     the great treasury, [to] the half-artaba of year 51, Seloulis Lolhenios,
3     the one written before not to use; but the one in the 24th year
4     into the assessment of the same year for completion of Seloulis, not to use
5     wheat: artabas 13 [one-half one-fourth], total wheat artabas 13 [one-half one-fourth] … .
