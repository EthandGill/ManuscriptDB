[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos, one of the Friends, from Amenneus son of Horos, embalmer, from the village
2     of Tanis in the Arsinoite nome. Being wronged, both I and Onnophris my partner,
3     by Abouchis son of Horos and Kelechon son of Pasis and Pasis son of Psenephmous,
4     embalmers from Philadelphia in the same nome, we submitted to you a memorandum
5     in the 16th year, Phamenoth …, in Memphis, by which, having explained in detail the wrongs one by one and the manner in which the aforesaid men had, from the royal (revenues),
6     embezzled what it was necessary for them to pay each year into the treasury from the 2nd year
7     until now; and you having written to the financial officials (chrematistai) who judge revenue cases, of whom
8     the introducer is Melas, to carry through the proceedings concerning these matters, and we having submitted to them
9     the (documents) written by you of the same year …, and having subscribed with a written oath,
10    the royal oath, to remain (present) until the matters of the judgment were carried through,
11    and having given to the opposing parties the copy of the memorandum,
12    they having come down to us … being present …
13    … having been judged … to us … the …
14    of the day of the hearing; but the opposing parties did not appear, but taking
15    the guards they harassed us, wishing to injure us so that we might abandon
16    the trial; and we having submitted a memorandum to the chrematistai concerning
17    these matters and (having received) nothing … one of us, having sailed down,
18    Onnophris, submitted [to you a memorandum] on the 1st of Mesore, in which,
19    having explained concerning each of the events that had occurred …

21    the opposing parties, having learned of each thing, taking [with them] …

25    of them, into suspicion …
26    and being compelled …
