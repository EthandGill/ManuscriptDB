[TRANSLATION]
1     Quintus, son of Zenas, son of Quintus, to Gaius
2     Iulius Zosimus, greetings. I acknowledge receiving from
3     you the amounts left according to a Roman will
4     bequeathed to me by Gaius Maecena Gratus in Ptolemaic silver
5     ten drachmas, amounting to drachmas 10
6     Year 1 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Epeiph 14
