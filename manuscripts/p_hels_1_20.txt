r.1    Διονυσίωι

r.6    εἰς τὸ αὐτὸ ἔτος τοὺς

r.12   ἀξιοῦμεν ἐὰν φαίνηται κατατάξαι
r.13   ἡμῶν τὴν ἀπογ[ραφὴν παρὰ σοὶ]
r.14   [ἐν χρηματισμῶι]

[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dionysios
2     steward from Kalliklatos and Agathokles and Dorotheus
3     and of the brothers.
4     We register and
5     We jointly-assess toward you
6     into the same year the
7     fruits of the possessing to us pigeon-house in village
8     Tnei copper toward silver talents five
9     of-which the third we shall arrange upon the
10    royal bank/table
11    in the fitting time.
12    We deem if it appears to put-down/register
13    our the regist[ration beside you]
14    [in a decision/judgment]
