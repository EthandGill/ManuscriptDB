[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship] of Flavius Basilius the most renowned, Phaophi 21, of the 5th indiction.
2     [To Fl]avius John, the venerable scriniarius of the military office of the Thebaid, son
3     [of] Taurinos [of mos]t excellent memory, from the (city) of the Hermopolitans, from the Aurelii Herakleides son of Pinoution
4     [his mother being ] …sia, and Bictor his full brother from the same parents, both from the village
5     … [of the Her]mopol[ite no]me. We acknowledge, being mutually surety, willingly and of our own accord that we have lea[sed fro]m [your e]xcellence for a se[v]en-year period from the crops of the prosperous sixth indiction
6     [the (arourai) belongin]g to your wondrousness, eight arourai lying in the marsh of the same village
7     … in one place four arourai, and in another place two other arourai,
8     [and in a third pl]ace the remaining two arourai, for the sowing and planting of whatever cr[ops we may] choose, at a fixed [rent] for these per year of six gold solidi of the lords, approved, less
9     [carats ] … to the public standard of the same city, which gold rent we will pay
10    [to your excellence in the month Paun]i of each year without delay. The deed of lease is valid
11    [and secure, and on being formally questioned ][we acknow]ledged it. We, the Aurelii Herakleides son of Pinoution and Bictor his brother, the aforesaid, have leased
12    [under mutual surety as aforesaid. ] [Au]relius Aphous son of Horos from Hermopolis, having been requested, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
13    … son of …doros, from Hermopolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the lessee. … son of Didymos, from Hermopolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the lessees.
14    … son of …u, from Hermopolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the lessees.
15    … through me, Sarapion, it was written …
