[TRANSLATION]
1     [These things were disposed, being of sound mind and] sensible, by Peisias the Lycian, of the troop of Leontiscus,
2     … [cleruch] of those settled in the Arsinoite, about … years old, honey-coloured, with hair up, of middle height, long-faced, with a scar under the chin. May it be mine, while in health,
3     to manage my own affairs. But if I suffer anything mortal, I leave
4     my property in Alexandria to Pisicrates my son
5     by Niko, the tenement-house and all the property I have there,
6     all the furniture, and the slaves Dionysius and Eutychus, Syrians,
7     and the slave-girl Abisila and her daughter Eirene,
8     a Syrian; and to Axiothea daughter of Hippias, Lycidian, my own wife,
9     the slave-girl Syra, a Libyan, and the house belonging to me in the village of Boubastos in the Arsinoite, and the
10    remaining furnishing in Boubastos in common to Pisicrates
11    and Axiothea. Whatever Axiothea brought in as dowry and
12    survives, let her have it and let Peisicrates have no share in it; but whatever
13    does not survive or is worn out, let Pisicrates pay back to Axiothea the values written below, whatever less they are found to be:
14    for a woollen woman's tunic, 40 drachmas; for a summer-wrap, 6 drachmas; for a man's tunic,
15    12 drachmas; for a worn summer-wrap, 10 drachmas; for a new tunic, 10 drachmas 2 obols; for a
16    man's belt, 1 drachma; for a new summer-wrap, 32 drachmas; for a light garment, 8 drachmas; for a bed-cover, 12 drachmas; for a bronze bowl, 4 drachmas; for a bronze cooler, 6 drachmas;
17    for 2 linen cloths, 4 drachmas; for women's shoes, 4 drachmas; for …
18    30 drachmas; for a knife and skewer, 1 drachma; for soft wool, … drachmas …
19    for an armlet, 18 drachmas; ear-rings, 12 drachmas; for a gold bracelet, 20 drachmas; of wool
20    In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Arsinoe,
21    the Sibling Gods, in the 10th year, in the priesthood of Apollonides son of Moschion,
22    the 2nd year of Alexander and the Sibling Gods and the Benefactor Gods,
23    the basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphus being Menecrateia daughter of Philammon …
