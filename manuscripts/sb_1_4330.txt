[TRANSLATION]
1     Chemtsneus Pelelis has been recorded
2     for the capitation-tax of the 10th year, fifteen drachmas, which make 15 drachmas,
3     and additionally recorded: one obol and half-obol. Of the 10th year of Tiberius
4     Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
5     Imperator, Pharmuthi 35. Bassus [son] of Dekmus, banker.
