[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum from Herakleotes to Zenon and Nestos, [my chos]en guardians.
2     I gave you before, too, a memorand[um asking concer]ning the instrument
3     left to me by testament [b]y Demeas [my teach]er, requesting that, when it was lost, you, finding it inside (the house), giv[e it back to me, or another] not worse, that, while practicing, I might compete, [so that I might not] fall behind in many things through being unpracticed; and another memorandum concerning the instrument, asking yo[u], since Hieron acknowledged that he held the lost instrument as a pledge inside (the house) for one hundr[ed and] five drachmas, that, paying (that off), you give it back to me, or [another] not at all worse than that, buy [and give] it to me, so that
4     I may compete while practicing and not fall behind in many things, being [unpracti]ced — you have paid no
5     attention to these matters. And I gave you a third othe[r memor]andum, asking,
6     since Demeas my teacher left to me by testam[ent that I be re]ared
7     having all the things needful, as many as are fitting for a free man for the practice of the kithara-playing ar[t], until I should enter into a contest; but you provide me for m[eat each] month three drachmas, four obols, a half-obol; for oil, three drachm[as,] [three] chal[koi]; for relish
8     two drachmas, a half-obol, seven and a half choes of wine — I declaring these things to be n[ot enou]gh for me
9     for the practice, but asking you, both for the sake of Demeas and of what is fitting, to give me each month, for meat seven drachmas, three obols; for oil, six drachmas
10    six chalkoi; for relish, seven drachmas, three obols; fif[tee]n choes of wine — you have done nothing for me
11    of the things according to the memoranda. I ask you, therefore, even n[ow], that my instrument
12    be given back to me, mine, which Hieron says he holds, the one left to me by testament; or that another not at all worse than that one be bought and given to me, so that, practicing, I may compete, and not,
13    on account of this cause, fall behind in many things through not having an instrument; and that the things needful
14    be provided to me, as many as I write to you through the memorandum, just [as] the testament also orders that all the things needful be provided to me, as many as are [fitting for a free man] for the practice of the kitha[ra-playing] art, until
15    I enter into a contest. But if these things are not (a thing) you choose to provide m[e, I as]k you to give me back the value accruing per month for two years,
16    so that, taking care of myself and obtaining a trainer, I may enter [into the] contests which the
17    king sets up, and not perish here, but be able [to hel]p myself.
18    Far[ewell.] Year 6, in the month (of) …
