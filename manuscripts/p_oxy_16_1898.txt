[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious
2     master, greatest benefactor,
3     Flavius Tiberius Mauricius the eternal
4     Augustus and Imperator, in the 5th year, in the consulship
5     of the same our most pious master, in the 4th year, Phamenoth 17, of the 5th indiction.
6     To the most eminent successors of him
7     of glorious memory, Apion who was
8     ex-first-patrician, landowners
9     here too in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
10    through Menas the household servant
11    who puts the formal question and provides surety
12    to his own masters, the same
13    all-honourable persons, for the conduct
14    and liability: I, Menas notary of
15    your eminence, son of the late
16    John, subscribing below in my own
17    hand, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites
18    and steward of the holy hospital
19    of abba Elias by the (shrine of) holy Neilos.
20    I acknowledge that I have received and been paid in full
21    from your eminence the holy
22    contribution of the sixth indiction-cycle, with God,
23    that is to say of wheat by the kankellos (measure), artabas
24    three hundred seventy-one,
25    they make of wheat by the kankellos 371 artabas, the same also given to me
26    through Justus the venerable chartularius
27    of your eminence; and for
28    proof of the receipt I have made this
29    receipt, which,
30    being valid, simply written, and having been formally questioned
31    I acknowledged it. I, Menas notary
32    of the glorious house, son of the late John,
33    this present receipt of the 6th indiction satisfies me,
34    of the three hundred seventy-one artabas,
35    as is set forth above.
36    Through me, Papnouthios.
37    Receipt of Menas, the most esteemed notary of the glorious house, steward of the hospital
38    of abba Elias, of wheat, 371 artabas by the kankellos.
