[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most [esteemed] [Aphous], weigher, from Pkylios, helper of the village Pōes. I have and [am indebted].
2     Purely and absolutely for a necessary need of gold [coins] seven well-weighed,
3     It amounts to gold coins seven well-weighed, and these readily I have [to provide] [to you] in the Phaophi [of the] tenth indiction,
4     And for the security of gold keratia one and one-half and one-fourth per month,
5     And for your security [I have made] this security as is laid out unambiguously.
6     Aurelius Pkyllis, helper of the village Pōes, stands surety to me for the security of the
7     of coins seven well-weighed as laid out. Written Epeiph 7 of the eighth indiction.
