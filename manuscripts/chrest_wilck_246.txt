[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Soknopaiou Nesos, 7 camels, 2 foals. To Herakleides, <ex> royal scribe, administering the office of the strategos and Di[o]phantos, royal scribe of the Herakleides division, from Tesenouphis, the elder, nicknamed Seiphon and Tesenouphis, the younger, nicknamed Kiales, both sons of Tesenouphis, the elder, the son of Tesenouphis, from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos. From the eight camels and one foal, which we registered in year 13 near the village, two camels have died, and I now register the remaining 7 fully grown camels for the present 14th year and the present two foals from their lineage, in the village of Soknopaiou Nesos. (m. 2) 7 camels were registered at the office of Herakleides, Mecheir 5. (m. 3) At the office of the royal scribe likewise 7 camels, Mecheir 5. I, Maron, gymnasiarch, have counted 7 camels, 2 foals. At the office of the royal scribe likewise, as aforesaid. (m. 6) I, Dionysios, have counted through Loukios and having counted, I confirm.
