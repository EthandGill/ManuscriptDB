[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 52, Pachon 15, [it] has been registered at the bank in Hermouthis, at which Ammonios [is the officer], of the tenth circle according to the agreement of Ptolemaios
2     the tax-collector, under which [he] signs
3     Ammonios the counter-scribe, [regarding the plot of] Esohris [son] of Horos, empty ground of 10 cubits in Pakenei, which [he] purchased from Imouthis [son] of Thotsyton, [payment] of talents 2, [the] tax [is] As.
4     Ammonios the banker.
