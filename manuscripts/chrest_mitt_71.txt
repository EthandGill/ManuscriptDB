[TRANSLATION]
1     … to my lord, the most illustrious and most learned advocate and [defensor] of Hermopolis,
2     from Aurelius Sarapion son of Euthymios, his mother being Chias, from the same city.
3     By unwritten sureties I undertook to guarantee a certain person for the repayment of declared debts to Sarapion … [from] the same city,
4     but I do not know from where; though the one guaranteed by me was present and had not resorted to flight, [he pressed] me on
5     his behalf as though that man were acting in bad faith, and I was summoned and shut up in the public prison
6     on account of the debts, and many … which is forbidden by the laws, I was even tortured … my own hearth.
7     Then also in the public prison, while your Illustriousness was unaware of these things. But since everywhere [the] laws command that Romans keep their body free and unmolested, I have made use of this cession …
8     … in place of [the] debts, that all our property and [gold] be taken in full, and I ask to have at least the sixth and that I myself be released from the imprisonments and tortures and have my [body] free
9     [and unmolested] according to the laws; and I adjure the public counter-scribe Apalos by [God] Almighty and by the piety and victory of our [master] Flavius Leo the eternal [Augustus] and
10    [Emperor], so that having taken up … [in] this … cession … public … through …
11    … [sub]scrib[ing] … so that I … of the public …
12    [And on being questioned I ack]nowledged it to your Illustriousness, most illustrious defensor.
13    … [Aurelius] [Sar]apion son of Euthymios, the aforesaid, have made the cession as [is set forth above].
14    … Aurelius Paulinos son of Hermammon, the aforesaid, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters.
15    Aurelius Apalos son of Papnouthios … of Hermopolis, have taken up the cession, posted publicly …
16    [In the consulship of] our [master] Flavius Leo the eternal Augustus for the 2nd time and of Flavius … [on] the twentieth of [Epei]ph.
