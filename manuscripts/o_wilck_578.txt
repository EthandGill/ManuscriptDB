[TRANSLATION]
1     Psansnos and Kasianos, collectors of money-taxes,
2     to Psenamounis (son of) Apollodoros (son of) Pikos. We have received
3     for the doubles (dipla) of year 21, four drachmas,
4     total 4 dr. Year 21 of Ha[dr]ian Caesar the
5     lord, Mesore 12.
6     (2nd hand) I, Kasianos, have signed [for four drachmas, total] 4 dr.
