[TRANSLATION]
1     inasmuch as you tended me in my old age, just as you have tended (me),
2     in no way harming me nor being in want, I have therefore considered with myself,
3     for this reason, (because of) the things you did for me, I order from now on already,
4     from today's day, that the same former gift be void
5     [and invalid, wherever it is] p[ro]duced, [whence I have come to this]
6     [second] gift, through which I, this same singularis, acknowledge, through this my
7     written gift, that immediately after my death all my
8     possessions and property to be possessed, both movable and immovable
9     and self-moving, from the most costly to the cheapest item and a single as,
10    are to be conveyed to you, this same aforementioned daughter of mine, which aforesaid things, [with] all the [rights] of them, having been gifted to you [by me]
11    immediately after my death, being previously pledged to no one else for [any]
12    capital or contract or matter whatsoever, so that you may administer and manage
13    and dispose and alter and give as a gift and
14    grant as a favor and exchange
15    and give as a dowry and transmit to your heirs and successors
16    and possessors, I the donor and my heirs
17    and successors and possessors guaranteeing with every guarantee and clearing of title
18    forever, against everyone who shall come forward or lay claim,
19    since you provided me with every repose and care
20    in my illness and old age; and the oath and the customary clauses.
