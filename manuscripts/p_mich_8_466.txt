[TRANSLATION]
1   Julius Ap[ollinarius to Ju]lius Sabinus, his
2   sweet[est fa]ther, very many greetings.
3   Before all things [I pra]y for your health, which is my wish,
4   [since I revere] you after the gods. This
5   has troubled m[e], that, though I have very often
6   written [through] Saturninus the
7   standard-bearer, likewise through Julianus son of
8   Longinus, you have not yet
9   written back to me about your welfare. But still,
10   being asked, of necessity bring yourself before all
11   things to write to me about your (pl.) welfare.
12   Though I often asked Longinus,
13   the one bringing you this letter, to
14   carry something to you, he refused, saying he was not
15   able to take it. I want you to know
16   that Domitius the armourer(?) forwarded a basket
17   in which [...] was inside for you.
18   Things are going well for me. After Sarapis
19   brought me here prosperously, while others all
20   [...]
21   [...] the whole
22   [day] cutting stones and
23   [doing other things], I, up to to-
24   [day,] suffered none of these things, but
25   [also,] when I asked Claudius
26   Severus the consular to make me
27   his own clerk (librarius), and he said
28   that 'there is no vacancy, but
29   meanwhile I will make you clerk of the legion'
30   with prospects — so by lot I
31   was assigned, from the consular of the
32   legion, to the cornicularius.
33   So if you love me, set to at once
34   to write to me about your welfare, and
35   if you are concerned for me, to send linen through
36   Sempronius; for from Pelusium every
37   day merchants come to us.
38   And I will set to at once, when the
39   governor begins to grant furlough,
40   to come to you at once. There greets
41   you Volussius Proclus, Longinus, Paccius
42   Valerius, Sempronius, Valerius Herma(s),
43   Julius Priscus, Apollinarios …ion [and] all the tent-mates. Greet Julia my lady
44   sister, likewise Sarapias and the mother, the grandmother Sambathion, Thermouthis and
45   her children, the father of Paccius and all your colleagues by name and those in
46   the house.
47   I pray for your health. Year 10 of Trajan the lord, Phamenoth 30.
48   I thank Volussius and Longinus the Barbarian. You will pass on to those of Aphrodas the spice-seller's son
49   that they enrolled <me> into a cohort at Bostra. It lies eight days' (journey) before Petra; and …
