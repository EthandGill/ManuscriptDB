[TRANSLATION]
1   Aibutius Niger and Pachnoubis son of Petorz(methes),
2   through Antonios son of Ioulas, scribe. Paid: Psenpetes
3   son of Psais, mother Tisatis, for the expense of recruits and [transport]
4   and provisions of the guard(?) and other expenses, year 13: 1 dr. 5½ ob. 2 chalkoi. Year 14,
5   Hadrianos (month) 16. (2nd hand) I, Aibutis Niger, have signed for the account …: 1 dr. 5½ ob. 2 chalkoi.
