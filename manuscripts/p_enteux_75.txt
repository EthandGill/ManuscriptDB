[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting from Krateuas, a Macedonian, holder of thirty arouras. I am wronged by Ha[rmiesis and] …
2     … from Alabanthis, shepherds. For when I was in charge of certain allotments of the …
3     [and in] the 25th year, Phaophi 13, when I went down into the field and found the sheep of the [aforementioned] shepherds having grazed down the pastures of one allotment, that of Asteropaios …
4     when I rebuked them and called witnesses, they, taking no account (of it), rushed upon me holding
5     [rod]s and struck blows upon me and stripped off the cloak which I was wearing. And when I came
6     … and [showed] my blows to Herodotos the epistates and (how) I was naked through them … [there appear]ed Chales, one of the shepherds, bringing my cloak, which I also recovered
7     … I submitted a petition to Diophanes the strategos, which, having dealt with it, he sent [to Herodotos the epistates; but Hero]dotos, acting negligently, has put me off until now. I beg, therefore,
8     [you, O king, to order Diophanes th]e strategos not to allow me to be lawlessly outraged by the shepherds
9     [but to write to Herodotos the epistat]es not to act negligently, but to send them before Diophanes
10    [and, if they be shown to have] grazed down [the pastures] and to have outraged me and stripped me, of the pastures …
11    … and concerning the outrage that Diophanes adjudicate. For when this is done,
12    [through you, O king, the common sa]viour of all, I shall obtain justice and assistance. Farewell.
13    [To Herodotos.] … so that he may not petition repeatedly about the same matters.
14    … Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13
15    [Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13].
16    [Krateuas against Ha]rmiesis
17    … concerning allotments
