[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God, from me Soubeheit amir to Papan the pagarch of Apollonis Upper:
2     receiving this my letter, your majesty, look [upon] t[he one delivering]
3     this my letter and prepare him to depart, and taking [pledges]
4     do not allow him to come to me concerning this; and peace to you from [God]
5     to the pagarch of Apollonis Upper from Soubeheit son of Chedez amir
