[TRANSLATION]
1     [Psan]sus and Cassianus, collectors
2     of silver [taxes] of the Market of the North. Phibius, [son] of Petemenophes
3     we received on account of the distribution of those who have withdrawn, year 22, drachmas
4     eight, making drachmas 8. Year 22 of Hadrian
5     Caesar, our lord, Pauni 24.
6     Cassianus through Lucas, I have confirmed drachmas 8.
7     Other [payments] Mesore 9, in the name of the same Phibius
8     likewise on account of [locks/barriers] and others, year 22, one drachma
9     four-obol, one drachma four-obol, and for embankment-tax and bath-tax, year 22
10    seven drachmas three-obol half-obol two-chalkoi, making in total drachmas 9 obol half-obol two-chalkoi.
11    Cassian[us] through L[uc]as, dirty drachmas, drachmas 9 obol half-obol two-chalkoi.
