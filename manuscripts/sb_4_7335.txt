[TRANSLATION]
1     Claudius Agath[us] Daemon to Sarapion the
2     dearest, greetings.
3     Passing through to the Thebaid, I greet you most sweetly, [s]weetest Sarapion, and I beseech you to do the same. And if there should be need [of anything]
4     for you from the Thebaid, I urge you to write to me, [which] I shall do most sweetly.
5     I pray [for] you to be in good health
6     always with the children.
7     Farewell
8     To Sarapion, kosmetes of the Hermopolite.
