[TRANSLATION]
1     643
2     I, Apollo of Pathon, and [Abraham] of [Phibammon], and Anoup, have received
3     from Ammonius, the superintendent of Thaesis
4     on behalf of brick-makers and builders for the fifth indiction until the eighth
5     indiction, that is each year three artabas of grain and of gold
6     one nomisma minus [four] and one-half karats, weighed by private scale, it totals grain artabas 3, nomisma 1 minus 4 1/2
7     by private scale. It was written in month Epeiph, indiction 8, for
8     5 and 6 and 7 and 8 indictions, complete. through me
9     Apollo, I wrote on behalf of him being [illiterate].
