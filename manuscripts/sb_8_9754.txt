[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Christopher, the most magnificent place-inspector of this Heracles city; Iuliana, wife of Helias
2     the younger, from the same city. I acknowledge
3     to have received and taken over from you from here already from the meager possessions of my own partner Helias the younger [some] finger-rings
4     silver, three, and old shirts, two, and camel-hair garments, two, and one old linen cloth and one dyed papyrus-box; total nine types.
5     and for your security I have made for you this present receipt written in the month Paun, [on the] tenth, fourth indiction
6     through me Anoup [the] notary.
