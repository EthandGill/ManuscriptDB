[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Apollonius who [also]
2     Dionysios, gymnasiarch having served as councilman
3     arch-magistrate, prytanis [of] the Oxyrhynchites
4     city to Eudaimon son of Ammonios, kapsarius, greetings. I received from you
5     the tax of the month of Mecheis drachmas
6     five hundred thirty-five,
7     total drachmas 535. Year 5 Phamenos the 3rd.
8     Aurelius Dionysios, collector of civic [taxes], I have noted.
