[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name [of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ, our God and Savior],
2     in the reign [of our most divine master Fl]avius Mauri[cius Tiberius]
3     the eternal Augustus and Imperator, in the twelfth year, [and the consulship]
4     of their serenity, in the eleventh year, Phamenoth 10, of the indiction [12],
5     at Syene.
6     Aurelia Tapia, daughter of Tsios, her mother being Maria, a widow,
7     originating from Syene, who also provides the subscriber below, the one subscribing on [her behalf]
8     and the witnesses who below bear witness, to this [secure]
9     and lawful sale, to Flavius Apa Dios, son of Sourous, sol[dier]
10    of the regiment of Syene, greeting. I, the aforewritten [Tapia], acknowledge
11    through this my written security, willingly and being persuaded,
12    without any deceit and fear and force and trickery and compulsion and
13    extortion, and apart from any malice and ill-will and
14    every base intention, but with a willing and unchanging mind
15    and guileless purpose and sincere conscience, according to this
16    simple written security, that I have sold to you today and conveyed, from now on for the ever and henceforth all time, the (property) belonging to me
17    and falling to me, the half-share of the dining-room (sumposion) which is on the second floor
18    and the fourth part of the airspace above the alcove (akkoubiton) which is
19    above the dining-room, which is also undivided between me and my brothers
20    Menas and Tselet, and my share of all the appurtenances —
21    of the vestibule and the gateway and the pier of the side-passage and
22    the oven — with entrance and exit and ascent and descent, of the same house
23    situated in the same Syene about the southern part of the Fortress
24    and about the quarter of the holy victorious Apa Victor the martyr,
25    the same half-share of the dining-room and the fourth part having come to me
26    from the roof-terrace thus: a fourth part from the dining-room
27    and the eighth part from the inheritance of my mother Maria,
28    and the other fourth part and the eighth part from the aforementioned
29    places as said above, from a lawful purchase, by written
30    deed of sale, from Georgios my brother, (they) being common
31    and undivided between me and my brothers Menas and Tselet
32    and the same aforewritten Mariam, from a lawful inheritance
33    of her parents Papnouthios and Thekla, according to the force of
34    their lot. The neighbors of the whole house: to the south the house of Thekla
35    and Dios son of Keloulios, to the north (the property) of the holy place of Apa Victor the martyr
36    and into which its principal door opens from the lane, after which (comes)
37    (the property) of the same holy place; to the west the courtyard of Gounthos son of Makarios, to the east
38    (the property) of the same place of holy Apa Victor, or whoever the neighbors may be on every
39    side all around, as I have dictated; and nothing has been left to me
40    unsold from the same half-share of the dining-room
41    on the second floor, facing north, to the width of the pier,
42    and the fourth part from the roof-terrace up to the airspace, with a share
43    of all the appurtenances in proportion to me, for the price agreed
44    between one another and mutually pleasing, of gold coins
45    three by the weight of Syene, total gold coins 3 by the weight of Syene, which full price here
46    I, the seller, have received from you, the buyer, by
47    hand into my hand, from your house, in number and weight full; on
48    the condition that you, the buyer, from now on (be master) of the aforementioned
49    half-share of the dining-room which is also undivided,
50    on the second floor, and the fourth part from the roof-terrace up to
51    the airspace, with all the appurtenances, together with our other co-sharers,
52    are to be lord and master and to administer and manage and build and sell and bestow and dispose to children and descendants
53    in whatever way you choose, unhindered and unimpeded; and (that) I shall not bring suit against you
54    nor shall sue, nor shall any other in my name,
55    at any time, in any way, on any pretext whatsoever,
56    because, as I said before, I have received from you the full
57    aforewritten price; and the one who shall bring suit against you or lay claim
58    concerning the aforementioned half-share of the dining-room
59    and the fourth part from the roof-terrace, with a share of the appurtenances
60    in proportion, I the seller shall remove and clear for you
61    at my own costs and expenses; and if I should not be able (to do so),
62    on condition that I pay to you the aforewritten price in double
63    amount; and for your security I have made for you this sale,
64    being valid and secure and lawful, producible everywhere,
65    with the subscription of the one subscribing on my behalf and of the following
66    witnesses; and on being questioned I acknowledged and released (it).
67    I, Aurelia Tapia, daughter of Tsios, her mother being Maria, the aforewritten, have sold the half-share of the dining-room and the fourth part of the airspace
68    above the alcove, and I have received from you the contained price
69    in three coins, and I guarantee to you the sale with every guarantee
70    as set forth. I, Theophilos, least deacon, being requested, wrote on her behalf
71    she being illiterate. I, Komes, least presbyter of Syene, bear witness.
72    I, Flavius Joannes son of Abraamios, registrar of the regiment of Syene, bear witness.
73    I, Flavius Komes son of Paamios, augustalis of the regiment of Syene, bear witness.
74    I, Flavius Ananias son of Georgios, soldier of the regiment of Syene, bear witness.
75    I, Apa Joseph son of Apa Dios, deacon of Syene, bear witness.
76    I, Flavius Dios son of Psachei, of the vicars of the regiment of Syene, bear witness.
77    I, Flavius Hermias son of Joannes, soldier of the regiment of Syene, bear witness.
78    Through me, Flavius Lazaros son of Peter, adjutant of the regiment of Syene, it was made.
