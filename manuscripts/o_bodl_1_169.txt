[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year thirty-one, Pachon 28, have measured into the in Dios city
2     the great treasury into the inscription of year 31 on behalf of the place, Diphilos of Alexander and the brothers, of wheat forty-five
3     total wheat 45. Ptolemaios, grain-scribe.
4     Epeiph 20, the same, of wheat twenty-five, total wheat 25. Ptolemaios, grain-scribe.
5     Herakleides, of wheat seventy, total wheat 70. Of year 31, Epeiph 20.
6     … n … kles, of artabas of wheat seventy, total wheat 70. Of year 31, Epeiph 20.
