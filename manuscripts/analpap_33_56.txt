[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ our God and Saviour
2     and of our Lady the holy Mother of God and ever-virgin Mary
3     and of all the saints; in the year of Diocletian 391, Thoth 26, of the 3rd indiction, in Arsinoe.
4     The present compromise (arbitration agreement) they make toward one another, of their own free will,
5     on the one part Aurelius Apa Hol, orchard-keeper, son
6     of Apa Ision, and on the other part Aurelius
7     Phoibammon, sandal-maker, son of Ptollion, from the
8     city of the Arsinoites, from the quarter — Apa Hol of (the quarter of) Psappalios
9     and Phoibammon of (the quarter of) Kleopatrios — greeting. We acknowledge
10    that we will go to law against one another before the judges
11    of the public guard of the same city,
12    and that we will be content and abide by their judgment.
13    And if either of us is not content with their decision,
14    the transgressing party shall be liable to give to the one
15    abiding (by it), by way of penalty, gold solidi
16    twelve, gold solidi 12. The compromise is valid.
17    And on being formally questioned we acknowledged, Apa Hol and Phoibammon, it is agreeable to us as set forth above.
18    Phoibammon, with God, presbyter of the church of Saint
19    Naraau, I bear witness to this compromise
20    as set forth above. Aristomachos, deacon, I bear witness
21    to this compromise as set forth above.
22    God help us; through me, Kosmas Phib, 99.
23    Bond of Phoibammon the sandal-maker and of Apa Hol the orchard-keeper.
24    … … of the lease-sale …
