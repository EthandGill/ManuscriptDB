[TRANSLATION]
1     [Lampon] … [his moth]er being Isidora, I acknowledge that I have so[l]d to [S]at[a]bous daughter of [H]oros, her mother being Tamo[u]ni[s], the share falling to me of a house and courtyard, from the part toward the north, held in common and undivided, and
2     [of all the appurtenances in] the (village of) Soknopaiou [N]esos of the Her[ak]leides di[vi]sion, of which the neighbors of the whole house and the courtyard are: to south and east, a royal street; to north, a royal street and, in part,
3     [the house and courtyard of Harsiesis], to (the) [w]est the house and courtyard of Herieus son of Papais and the house and courtyard of Harsiesis; and I have received forthwith, from hand to hand, out of the house, the agreed-upon price, all of it, in fu[ll, and I will guarantee (it) with every guarantee] from the present day for all time, and I will do the other things as is written above. We have instructed the witnesses to wri[t]e, and the one at the record-office
4     who has reg[istered] … . Pap[a]is son of …tosarchos wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters. I, Satabous daughter of Horos, my mother being Tamounis, have bought as is set forth. There wrote on her behalf
5     [Soilos son of Leonidas, since she does not know] letters. Sale and cession of the share falling to him of a two-storied house and courtyard, from the part toward the north, held in common and undivided, and of all the appurtenan[ces in the (village of) Soknopaiou] Nesos of the Herakleides div[isio]n, of which the neighbors of the whole house and the courtyard, which are set forth above; and he receives
6     the price [and] guarantees (it), which Lampon makes (the sale).
7     … [aged for]ty, [with a scar] … of the left, in the middle; and S[at]abous daughter of Horos, her mother being Tamounis, aged 35, with a scar on the left eyebrow. Subscriber of the seller: Papais
8     … son of …tosarchos, aged …, [with a scar] … [an]d for the other (party), Zoi[los son of Leoni]das, aged 37, without distinguishing mark. Y[ea]r 8 of Tiberius Claudius Caesa[r]s Augustus Germanicus Imperator, in the month …
9     … [. It has been registered through the (record-office) in the (village of) Soknop]aio[u] Nes[os.]
