[TRANSLATION]
1   Timokrates son of Timokrates, to Strobilos
2   son of Heron, and to Philemon son of Strobilos, greeting. I acknowledge that nei[ther]
3   I myself nor anyone else on my behalf shall proceed against you
4   concerning anything at all, written or unwritten, whether debt or [claim]
5   or payments, in any manner whatsoever, from former times up to the present
6   day [...]
7   on no pretext; or, apart from the things agr[eed] remaining valid,
8   I will pay to you, for whatever I transgress, both the damages and expenses, and as a private debt 3000 silver drachmas;
9   and let this bond be valid.
