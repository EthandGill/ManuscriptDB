[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Kastorion, ex-gymnasiarch, ex-prytanis,
2     town-councillor, curator of gold,
3     to Aurelius Arion, tesserarius of Theadelphia, greeting.
4     I received from you, on account of the
5     imposition made by divine command, on behalf of the property of Theadelphia, of pure gold
6     six grammata and a quarter, 6¼ grammata.
7     [Year] 6 and 4, the Nones of June.
8     I, Aurelius Kastorion, have signed for grammata
9     six and a quarter.
10    And likewise the same Arion, tesserarius,
11    on behalf of the same village of Theadelphia,
12    of pure gold, ten grammata, 10 grammata.
13    On the 8th (day) before the Ides of June.
14    I, Aurelius Kastorion, have signed.
15    And likewise the same Arion, tesserarius,
16    on behalf of the same property of Theadelphia,
17    of pure gold, one grammon and a quarter, making 1¼ grammon,
18    and of unstamped (silver) likewise the same, on behalf of the same village of Theadelphia, of the property,
19    of pure unstamped silver, four ounces, 4 ounces.
20    On the 15th (day) before the Kalends of July.
21    I, Aurelius Kastorion, have signed.
22    And likewise the same Arion, tesserarius,
23    on behalf of the same village of Theadelphia, of pure unstamped silver, two ounces, making 2 ounces.
24    On the 14th (day) before the Kalends of July.
25    I, Aurelius Kastorion, have signed.
26    And likewise the same Arion, tesserarius,
27    on behalf of the same property, of pure unstamped silver, two ounces and sixteen grammata, making 2 ounces 16 grammata.
28    I, Aurelius Kastorion, have signed.
29    An[d] likewise, in the name of
30    Sakaon son of Satabous, of pure unstamped silver, one ounce,
31    making 1 ounce. On the 12th (day) before the Kalends
32    of July.
33    I, Aurelius Kastorion, have signed.
34    And likewise the same Arion, tesserarius,
35    on behalf of the same village of Theadelphia,
36    of pure unstamped silver, one ounce and
37    eight grammata, making 1 ounce 8 grammata. On the
38    11th (day) before the Kalends of July.
39    I, Aurelius Kastorion, have signed.
