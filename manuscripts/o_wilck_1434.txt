1     Postomos, collector of the money-tax of Charax (and) Kleopatris,
2     (to) Dekmos, through ...( ) (son of) Basos. I have, for land-survey tax of year 4,
3     impure twelve drachmas, total impure 12 dr. Year 5 of Antoninus
4     Caesar the lord, Hathyr 13. Postomos, through Phatres, I have signed.
