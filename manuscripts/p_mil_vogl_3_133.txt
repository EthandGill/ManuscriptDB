1     [To Ptollar]ion son of Ptollarion, ex-gymnasiarch, a minor,
2     [through] his guardian Sabinus, ex-gymnasiarch,
3     f[rom Pto]lemaios son of Theogiton, grandson of Theogiton,
4     fro[m the quart]er of the Storehouses, and his son Harpokration, [and S]abinus son of Patron, grandson of Apion, and
5     Is[idoros son of A]reos, grandson of Ptolemaios, the two from
6     the qu[arter of Thara]peia, the four (of us). We wish [to lease] from you for four years
7     fro[m the] coming sixth year of Antoninus and
8     Ver[us t]he lords Augusti, the half share belonging [to you] around the village
9     of Ker[ke]sis, an allotment of seven arouras, and around
10    the village of Kerkesephis, an allotment of fifteen
11    arouras in all, or however many they may be; the first
12    a[nd third] year three and a half arouras for sowing
13    of whatever [we may] choose except wheat, but the rest
14    [for sow]ing of fodder-grass for the eating-down of cattle, the rent for the
15    [two-year period] per year, without seed, for those around
16    [Kerkesis] one hundred [for]ty silver drachmas, but for those around Kerkesephis one hundred fifty silver [drachmas]; and the
17    [secon]d and fourth year for sowing of wheat,
18    [the rent] for the two-year period per year together with what
19    [we shall receive] as seed, for those around Ker[kesis] of wheat three and a half artabas, the whole
20    [wheat being] fifty-two and a half [arta]bas,
21    [and for those aro]und Kerkesephis of wheat seven
22    [and a half] artabas, of the whole wheat ninety-
23    [one artabas], by the four-choinix measure your own, and besides, of garden-produce [per yea]r, two cheeses, forty eggs, all [free from de]duction and from risk, performing
24    a[ll the yearly] works of the arouras, as many as
25    [are fitting,] at our own (expense) at the proper seasons, and
26    [we shall p]ay [the rents] each year in the month Pauni
27    [on the threshing-floor, new,] pure, unadulterated, the measuring
28    being d[one] through one of your (men), and the rent
29    in the month [Phar]mouthi, the yearly transport-charges of the urging-on being charged to us, but the conv[eyance of the] rents charged to you; and after the
30    ti[me we shall hand] over the arouras clean from
31    [dog's-tooth gras]s (and) every weed, except rush (and) reed.
