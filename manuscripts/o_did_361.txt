[TRANSLATION]
1     [?] to Areios the dearest, very many greetings: I ask you, use a flask and give
2     to Marcus the donkey-driver, the one carrying to you
3     this ostrakon, until you return from Berenice and recover it. A sign that you arrive calling me and
4     the son-in-law, and I tell you that he has ophthalmia. Do not then punish the donkeys
5     of us, because they lag behind. Thermouthis greets you. Farewell.
6     Phamenoth
7     fifth.
