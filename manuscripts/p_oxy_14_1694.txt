[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius leased,
2     Heraklas son of Zoilos, his mother being
3     Thermouthion, from the splendid
4     and most splendid (city) of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     to Aurelia Chairemon
6     daughter of Heraklammon also called Agathos
7     Daimon, who served as chief-priest of
8     the Small Oasis, for a period of
9     six years from the present month of Phamenoth of the current 5th year, the
10    house belonging to him at the quarter of
11    the Cavalrymen's Camp
12    and a courtyard and an enclosure in which (is)
13    a we[l]l, and all its appurtenances,
14    at a rent per year of one thousand drachmas of silver.
15    The lease being confirmed, let the lessee use all the leased (premises)
16    for the period without hindrance,
17    and let her pay the yearly rent in two installments, half every six months, and at the end of the
18    period let her hand over the house clean from dung
19    and all filth, and (with) such doors and keys as she
20    received,
21    or let her forfeit, for whatever she does not hand over, the worth (in) value, the
22    execution resting also against the l[e]ssee as is fitting.
23    Valid is the lease, concerning which the lessee, being formally questioned,
24    [a]cknowledged. Year 5
25    of the Emperor Caesar Marcus
26    Aurelius Probus Gothicus Maximus
27    Persicus Maximus Germanicus [Maximus]
28    P[io]u[s] Felix A[ugustus],
29    Phamenoth 11. I, Aurelius
30    Epimacho[s], who served as chief-priest,
31    have leased the aforesaid (premises)
32    and I shall pay the rent
33    as is aforesaid, and being formally question[ed] I acknowledged.
