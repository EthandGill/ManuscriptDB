[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of God, Korra ben Szerich, governor, to Basileios administrator]
2     of the village of Aphrodito. [We give thanks] to [God, and after] these things:
3     Often we appear to have employed our letters [to you]
4     concerning the two-thirds portion of the gold public taxes of your administration,
5     and we supposed that you had already paid this in. So when we
6     had instructed the notaries to look into the papers of the treasury (sakelle),
7     so as to learn what you had paid into the treasury, we found your work
8     incompetent and worthless, and you in this matter acting badly.
9     For we did not send you to be at leisure for eating, but rather
10    we sent you to fear God and to keep your faith
11    and to accomplish the just due of the Commander of the Faithful. For you have no excuse
12    of any kind, nor do those of your administration. For the
13    fruit of the land has turned out well, and God has blessed this and doubled it
14    beyond what it was before this. And the grain too has been abundant, and this
15    has been sold by those of the district. So, as has been said, you have
16    no excuse of any kind. Look therefore to what remains owing
17    through your administration from the two-thirds portion of the gold
18    public taxes of it. With all dispatch accomplish this,
19    not falling short of it by a single miliaresion, for God knows
20    that it has not pleased us, how you have acted
21    in the matter of these public taxes. For we wished to give you
22    a recompense on this account. If, then, there is any good in you,
23    as has been said, accomplish with all zeal what remains
24    through your administration from this same two-thirds portion
25    of the gold public taxes, and send (it). For it is profitable
26    for those of the district to give, as it falls due, the (dues) through them,
27    and not to leave them until they pile up upon them
28    and they are hard-pressed to pay. We know that, carried away by incompetence and
29    uselessness, the official seeks pretexts toward an excuse
30    for falling short in the (dues) through him. Do not, then, become such, and give
31    a pretext against your own life. Behold, we testify to you.
32    Written in the month Pauni 7, indiction 9.
33    [In the month] … [in]diction 9, through Muslim the courier, concerning the dispatch of the completion of the two-thirds portion of the gold public taxes.
