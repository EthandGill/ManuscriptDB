1     [To King Ptolemaios, greetings,] … [is called] of those dwelling in Mouche
2     [of the Polemon division. I am being wronged by An]tilokhos, [son] of Antigonus, Macedonian. [Having loaned me …] drachmas [of] interest [at] two drachmas per mna each month … [according to a] contrac[t] before Calliphon. And in year 1, [month …] he cast me out; [something] of ours which was worth twenty drachmas he removed and is able
3     … I gave the 600 drachmas for this warehouse. I beseech [you therefore, king, not to overlook me being wronged] by a violent man and being deprived
4     … [but to order] Diophan[es] the strategos to write to Demetrius
5     [the overseer to send him to Diophan]es so that he may investigate and, if what I write be true,
6     [to compel the accused to repay] me the garment and to order him not to s[ell] … [the] contract of the loan from his possessions
7     … [For if this comes to pass, k]ing, I shall have received help from you.
8     Farewell.
9     [To Demetrius: above all reconcile th]em; but if not, send them
