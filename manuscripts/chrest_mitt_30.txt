1     Argaios to Aphthonetos. Of the subscription that has been made by the
2     chrematistai who judge the cases that come up,
3     we have appended for you [the copy].
4     Year 19, Hyperberetaios 25 …
5     through Zoilos son of Hephaistion.
6     From Alkidemos … [and] … [and]
7     Artemidoros, of those who have transacted [the cases] that come up.
8     When Apollonios son of
9     Sokles had been put on trial, kal…
10    gra…ame…
11    en…okos…
12    of the priest …
13    that … was in prison, that he was unable, having appeared in the
14    days according to the diagramma,
15    to repay [that which (was due)] from the
16    collector of the (revenues) of the localities … ma
17    to Aristippos and the collectors,
18    … and that it was possible for Ammonios
19    to have appeared, and that, since he requested
20    that it be written to Aphthonetos so that
21    he might order Ammonios
22    to appear, [and the] sealed document having been brought against him, he gave surety … this man
23    … [and] he wrote out … an oath concerning the whole matter
24    … to the trial … and Apollonios consenting that, if upon Ammonios appearing the
25    sealed document be accepted from him,
26    of the suit …
27    the suit … to him,
28    and if he does not appear (and)
29    the sealed document be not accepted from him … o…o, g…
30    write … to Aphthonetos to instruct
31    those acting through him …
32    Ammonios in …
33    … t…osi instruct … ma…
34    … from Apollonios a copy
35    of the petition that has been sent
36    to those who judge the cases
37    that come up,
38    to give it to him, and to ask leave
39    to sail down to [Alexandria]
40    in the days according to the diagramma,
41    having also the documents-of-right toward
42    the trial;
43    [and] if he does not appear,
44    the claim will be given to Apollonios;
45    but if, upon his appearing, Apollonios
46    does not obtain the claim, he shall pay to him the expenses incurred
47    [for the] voyage down, and concerning these things, in whatever
48    way it be settled, he shall write back to him. Year 19, Hyperberetaios 25,
49    through Zoilos son of Hephaistion, introducer (of the case).
