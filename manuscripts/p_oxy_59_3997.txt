[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakles to [Kerdo]n his brother, very many greetings. Before
2     all things I pra[y] that you keep entirely well together
3     with the sweetes[t] Taphynchis your daughter. I marvel how you wrote me no letter. But I
4     have often written to you, seeking your
5     friendship; but you wrote me only one, nor again did you write to me, but you wrote to my father, threatening me not a little. But God alone
6     knows that every single day I make your obeisance, whether among men or before
7     God. For I have desired to make obeisance to you also
8     through letters, until God grants the
9     good road and we make obeisance to each other. For Kranas knows how I question him about you, and I was begging him from the first journey that he bring you something, and he could not, because there was
10    Heron the master of the camels there. And
11    now I have begged him and he has promised me.
12    If therefore I receive (it), I send you something. And so urge him on, that he may be unhesitating toward us
13    and write me what you need
14    and through whom you wish me to send (it). No one …
15    … from me; and write
16    … of you all
17    … we heard
18    … of (your) daughter concerning
19    … [Salute Am]monios the bro[ther and] … and Heras and
20    … [and] all our (people)
21    … the quarter(?) our(?) …
22    … . Salute
23    … -pion and all
24    [those in the house, and w]rite about
25    [your] [well-being.] I pray that you keep
26    [well.] [ ]
27    Send us a few good saggatha (saddle-cloths?), as many as you find.
28    Kopres the son of Ammonous salutes you, …
29    Topastas salutes you, and Aphous and Ammonous and Helene
30    and the father and the mother. Helene salutes your daughter.
31    Do not be neglectful about the saggatha. Salute Chenamoun the
32    old woman and her children and the reader.
33    We pray that [yo]u keep well.
34    Deliver to Kerdon (my) brother, from Herak[les.]
