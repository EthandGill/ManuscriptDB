1     [After the consulship of Flavius Longinus the most glorious, Pach]on 6, of the 11th indiction.
2     [To Flavius Ioannes son of Taurinos the reverend, of the former primicerii of the] unit (arithmos) of the Mauri of Hermopolis
3     [from Aurelius] … [from the vill]age of Ptemenkyrkeos of Poimenon
4     [of the Hermopolite nome; I acknowledge willingly and of my own free wil]l that I have leased from your
5     [virtue, for] … [a period, from the crops of the fortunate twel]fth indiction, the arou[r]as belonging to you
6     [arouras] … [situated about the collectorate (praktoria) of the aforewri]tten village of mine, for sowing of whatever
7     [I may choose, as fixed rent for these per year, of gold no]mismatia three less keratia nine
8     … [and for the unwatered (land), which m]ay not be, I will furnish of gold nomismatia
9     … [which gold rent I will deliver] to you in the Mesore
10    [month of each year without delay; the lease (is) valid and secure, and on being questioned I acknowledged it.] Aurelius
11    … [the aforesaid have leased as aforesaid. Aurelius] … from Hermopolis, having been requested, wrote
12    [on his behalf, he being illiterate. Aurelius] … [from Hermopolis I bear wit]ness to the lease, having heard from
13    [the (one) who made it. Aurelius] … [from Hermopolis I bear witness to the lease, having hea]rd from the (one) who made it.
14    … [(one) who] made (it).
15    … through me, Phoibammon, it was written.
