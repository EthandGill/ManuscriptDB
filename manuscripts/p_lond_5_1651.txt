[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our master Julian the eternal Augustus,
2     for the 4th time, and of Flavius Sallustius the most illustrious prefect
3     of the sacred praetorium.
4     To Aurelius Hermeias son of Heliodoros, ex-magistrate, councillor, strategos in office of the most illustrious Hermopolis,
5     from Aurelius Dios son of Apollon from the same
6     city. Already since
7     the month Mesore my wife Hermione, watching for
8     my absence while I was staying in the village, stole away all the contents
9     in our house, in which were also the necessary documents, and made an unlawful departure; and often
10    I sent to … then indeed it appeared, when
11    … they were shameless …
12    she has not returned my property, especially the deeds of sale
13    of my buildings. On account of this, being unable
14    to keep quiet, I submit to [your] discernment these
15    documents, asking that she be brought [from] every quarter
16    and be kept in safekeeping until the fortunate
17    arrival of my lord the most eminent prefect
18    Cerealius Telephius Hierocles, when I intend
19    to make my petition concerning this. Farewell.
20    In the aforesaid consulship, Pharmouthi 25.
21    I, Aurelius Dios son of Apollon, have submitted (this). I, Aurelius
22    Olkoueis son of Pathermouthios, [wrote] on his behalf, since he does not
23    [kno]w letters.
