[TRANSLATION]
1     Under the consulship of our lords
2     Diocletian [for his] 6th [time] and Constantius [for his] 2nd [time].
3     Aurelius Didymus, son of Sarapion, having held office of chief of the most splendid city of
4     the Alexandrians, leased to Aurelius Petechon, [son] of Ammonius, mother Tsenpsoit, from the splendid and most splendid Oxyrhynchite city, for this year 13 and 12
5     and 5, from his possessions around the Iseum Panga in the embankment [area] of Pekty, six arouras, so as to gather wood [with] hay, [at] fixed [rent] of silver, one talent and one thousand eight hundred drachmae, of which the landholder acknowledges to have received from the lessee five thousand [drachmae], the remaining rent [being] free from risk
6     of all risk, the public [taxes] being upon the landholder holding
7     the crops until he receives [what is] owed. The lease having been secured, let the lessee repay the remaining
8     [of the] rent drachmae two thousand eight hundred.
