[TRANSLATION]
1     ... imis ... n ... r ...
2     an arura and the things of-your father's brother
3     yours again in-the-same-way; but always
4     the things of parents [are] sweeter, if
5     not in private [they are] worthy and fine [to] me;
6     when I-learned [this] from the secretary
7     about these things, if you-spend again a great expense, you-are-able
8     [for] them to-be-removed in this
9     time, [they] do everything and I-blame
10    Zoidas, that she-went-out and did-not signal me, so-that I-give her a letter;
11    for I did-not have-leisure to-go to her and I-blame you greatly, because
12    ... you did-not write-back to-me in-person
13    ... [you-will] show ... to-me and
14    ... Apollin[ar]ius from Didymus.
