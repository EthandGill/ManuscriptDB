r.1    ἐν τῇ βασιλικῇ θερισμός. λέγει
r.2    Θοσῶτ[ο]μ τεθερίσθαι

r.5    τεθερίσθ[α]ι χωρὶς ὧν ἐθέρισαν οἱ Ἡφαιστιῶται, ἀρτάβας λζ,

[TRANSLATION]
1     In the royal harvest. [He] says
2     [that] Thosotom has harvested
3     35 artabas at 160 drachmas [per artaba]
4     and in the allotment [he] says
5     [it] has been harvested apart from what the Hephaestians harvested, 37 artabas
6     and the Hephaestians, [he] says
7     Thosotom, have harvested
8     18 artabas
9     and Esormosius' son
10    harvested 4 artabas
11    and to those from Theophilus
12    [that] daily harvesting, Thosotom gave 2 artabas
