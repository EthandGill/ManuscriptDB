[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eighth year of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, in the month Kaisareios, on the 5th of the intercalary days, in Herakleia of the division of Themistos of the Arsinoite nome. Stotoetis son of Stotoetis son of Horos, [collector] of money-taxes of the
3     aforesaid village of Herakleia, about 26 years old, with a scar on the left shin, acknowledges to Saturnilus son of Apion son of Didymos, about 50 years old,
4     with a scar in the middle of the forehead, that he, the acknowledger, has appointed Saturnilus as collector from the coming
5     9th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord for two years,
6     and that he shall pay into the public treasury the portion falling
7     to Stotoetis, a third part of the aforesaid collectorship, Saturnilus likewise discharging by reckoning the third part falling to him,
8     and further Saturnilus shall also register the books of the office at the customary deadlines, with
9     Saturnilus furnishing the papyrus and the other
10    expense being at his charge, while he receives
11    from Stotoetis yearly, on account of salary,
12    two hundred and fifty-two drachmas of silver,
13    the repayment of which he shall make to him in four installments, at three-month intervals, the due amount in equal portions.
14    And Stotoetis shall serve as joint-collector with him, whenever
15    there is need, since the agreement has been made on these terms.
