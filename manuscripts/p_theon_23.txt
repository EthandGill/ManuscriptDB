[TRANSLATION]
1     Papodontus, also called Patas, [guardian] of the lower estate, to Matres
2     to the most honored
3     greetings.
4     If it seem good to you, lord,
5     send me for wage[s] of deferment
6     of [shipwrights], of whom reckoning I-will-give,
7     drachmas [forty],
8     Drachmas 40 become, through Thonatus.
9     [I-pray] you to-be-well.
10    Year 23 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
11    Mesore 10.
12    Give the drachmas 40.
