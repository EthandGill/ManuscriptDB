[TRANSLATION]
1     Postumus, collector of money-taxes of Charax, to Pikos the younger, (son of) Archis.
2     I have received for the doubles (dipla) of year 5, base drachmas two, total base drachmas 2.
3     Year 5 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
4     Tybi 14. I, …, Postumus, through Phatres, have signed.
