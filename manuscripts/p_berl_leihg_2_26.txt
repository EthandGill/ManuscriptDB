[TRANSLATION]
1     Wheat, 34 1/3 1/12 artabas.
2     Phokion, strategos of the Arsinoite, divisions of Themistos and Polemon, to the sitologoi
3     of Theadelphia, greeting. Measure out, with the concurrence of Leonides, royal scribe of the division of Themistos, from the produce
4     of the 7th year of Antoninus and Verus, the lords
5     Emperors, for seed-loans of the present
6     8th year, for a prosperous sowing, those which the same royal scribe, to whom
7     the decision concerning the seeds belongs, wrote ought to
8     be disbursed to the public cultivators of the village of Kameinoi
9     and of Kerkesephis, who requested it through Chilas son of Kronion and Peteeus son of Peteeus, the two elders of Kerkesephis, and Harmieus
10    son of Souchas and Orsenouphis son of Chaireus, the two elders of the village
11    of Kameinoi, through the under-cultivator Demokrates son of Neon, for the
12    plots additionally apportioned to them from the fields of the village of Argias:
13    to those from Kameinoi, of royal land, 10 1/2 arouras, of seed of wheat
14    with the supplement, 15 1/6 artabas; of revenue-land, 5 [1/3] arouras, of seed of wheat with the supplement, 7 1/2 1/3 1/12 artabas;
15    to those from Kerkesephis, of royal land, 5 1/3 arouras, of seed of wheat with
16    the supplement, 7 1/2 1/24 artabas; of revenue-land, 2 1/2 arouras, of seed of wheat with the supplement, 3 1/2 1/8 artabas;
17    the same all together, of seed of wheat, [pure], unadulterated, free of clods,
18    free of barley, sifted, by the public measure, by the measurement
19    that has been ordered, thirty-four artabas, third, twelfth, those who attend at the disbursement and at the deposition into the
20    land — the beach-guard and sowers being present, and the dyke-overseers and the others who are customarily present according to usage — nothing being reckoned in the name of a public or private debt, on condition that the equal amounts be restored from the new crops together with the public dues of the land and all the additional measurements; and having received the proper double receipts written to me,
21    deliver them up. Year 8 of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
22    Antoninus Augustus, Armeniacus, Medicus, Parthicus Maximus, [and] of the Emperor
23    Caesar Lucius Aurelius Verus Augustus, Armeniacus, Medicus, Parthicus Maximus, Hadrianos 28.
24    Phokion, strategos: measure out the thirty-four artabas of wheat,
25    third, twelfth, as is set forth above.
26    Leonides, royal scribe: measure out the 34 1/3 1/12 artabas of wheat, as is set forth above.
27    Harmieus son of Souchas and Orsenouphis son of Chaireus, the two public and
28    estate and revenue cultivators
29    of the village of Kameinoi, and
30    Chilas son of Kronion and
31    Peteeus son of Peteeus, the two
32    public and estate and revenue
33    cultivators of the village
34    of Kerkesephis, for the
35    plots additionally apportioned to them
36    from the fields of the village of Argias, through the under-cultivator
37    Demokrates son of Neon:
38    of royal land of Kameinoi, 10 1/2 arouras, [of seed with] the supplement
39    15 1/6 artabas; of revenue-land, 5 1/3 arouras of seed with the supplement
40    7 1/2 1/3 1/12; of royal land of Kerkesephis
41    5 1/3 arouras of seed with the supplement 7 1/2 1/24
42    of revenue-land 2 1/2 arouras of seed with the supplement 3 1/2 1/8;
43    they come, all together, by the measurement that has been ordered,
44    of seed with the supplement,
45    wheat, 34 1/3 1/12 artabas;
46    we the four elders have received our share.
47    Kasas son of Peko, scribe: I have submitted (this).
