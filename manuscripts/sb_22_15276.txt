[TRANSLATION]
1     Choirine to Zenon the oeconomist, greetings. Know that the fulled-cloth has been destroyed …
2     …drew one… [three] stateres with harpe-dons. Be concerned […]…
3     [of a] six-month… for I am troubled. If then it seems [good] to you, let the half wool be given […]…
4     Be concerned… [about] the slave-girl or wages be given or wool. For I am naked […]…
5     [became] wetter… for I do not have how [I might] reproach her […]
6     You then do not neglect […] of the slave-girl.
7     … Fare well.
8     To Zenon.
9     Choirine.
