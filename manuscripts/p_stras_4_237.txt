[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of the Emperor Caesar Titus [Aelius Hadrianus]
2     Antoninus Augustus Pius, in the month Apellaios …
3     Phaophi …, in Ptolemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite [nome],
4     in (the temple of) Julia Augusta. [Maron] … acknowledges,
5     son of …mon, a Persian of the epigone, about forty years old, with a scar on his right eyebrow, to the woman formerly and now living with him,
6     his wife Boubastis daughter of Heliodoros, about twenty years old …
7     and to her mother Alexandra also called Sempronia,
8     about seventy-four years old, without distinguishing mark, to the two (women) with the guardian, their kinsman Ptolemaios son of Souchion, about sixty years old, with a scar on his left shin, that the acknowledging party Maron has [from them],
9     upon (marriage with) Boubastis, [from]
10    the same Boubastous, together with the marriage-cohabitation, [drachmas]
11    seventy-two, being garments valued (thus) …
12    a pair of tested gold (ornaments) of two quarters (weight), and a bronze figure of Aphrodite, a folding mirror, jars, oil-flasks, a wooden case for the Aphrodite, a perfume-box … [from]
13    the same Sempronia also called Alexandra, twenty-four drachmas, which are rings … [so let them live together]
14    with one another, both Maron and Boubastous.
15    …astou Eu…
16    … of Maron …
17    … [seven]ty-four …
18    … livestock …
19    … camels …
