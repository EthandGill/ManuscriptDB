[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Apianus, having served as exegete [and] councillor of the most brilliant city
2     of the Alexandrians, and as he is designated,
3     from Aurelius Heroninus, superintendent of Theadelphia;
4     account of receipts and expenditures of the month of Pauni of the
5     current 3rd year; it is as follows:
6     receipts: from the price of wine, single-chous [jars] …
7     as the single one from 12 drachmas, drachmas …
