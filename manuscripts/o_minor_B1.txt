r.1    διέγραψε Πικῶς Πετεχώνσιος ὑπὲρ λαογραφίας
r.2    γ ἔτους δραχμὰς δέκα βαλανευτικοῦ δραχμὴν α ὀβολὸν α 𐅵 γίνονται δραχμαὶ ια ὀβολὸς α 𐅵

[TRANSLATION]
1     Pikos son of Petehonsis has paid regarding poll-tax
2     year 3: ten drachmas; for bathing-tax: one drachma, one obol, one half-obol; total: eleven drachmas, one obol, one half-obol
3     and of these additionally entered from [the] obol half-obol. Year 3 of Nero
4     Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
5     Emperor, month Germaniceus 28. Bassos, I have signed.
