[TRANSLATION]
1     I [am] Pason, [son of] Apollo … [concerning the judgment].
2     Of the monastery of the holy apa Apollo, through [him] … [the archimandrite].
3     And the father of the place [says] that since …
4     … [the] Athenian, to me concerning sid[eros] … and the holokottin …
5     … For wheat, artabae 5, by Athenian measure, and of gold coins, reckoning 23.
