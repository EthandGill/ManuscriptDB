[TRANSLATION]
1     Year twenty-two Mecheir four. It has been registered through the office in Ptolemais Euergetis registered property share one of [portions] five as portion of dead embalmers which are
2     around Hauereis and Ptolemais Hormos of the outer places of the Herakleian division and one-half share [o]f a house and courtyard in the same Hauereis, which is held
3     Harmais Petesouchos. Three are giving orders; others [are] Harmais and Phyllos. Through secretary Marreus of the … choai.
4     Year twenty-two Mecheir four. It has been registered in the library.
5     [registered property] five thirtieths of one-thirtieth of dead embalmers of the same vill[ages a]nd [one-half share of a] house
6     [a]nd courtyard in Hauereis, which is held Harmais the aforementioned Petesouchos the son of mother Terpots.
7     [o]rders the elder son Harmais Harmaios of mother Tamarreios.
