[TRANSLATION]
1     Hōros [son] of Pam[onthes…Hō]ros has written off for the poll-tax of the Memnoneia of the 13th year
2     8 drachmas. Year 13 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus
3     Germanicus Autocrat, Pharmouthi 23. Similarly, the 30th
4     8 drachmas. Similarly, Pachon 25: 8 drachmas. Similarly, 14th year, Thoth 28, for the dyke-tax: 2 drachmas, 5 obols 𐅵.
