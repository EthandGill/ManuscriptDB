# HGV-EN: Second year of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Pius Felix Augustus, Epeiph 8. Horion and his associates, (collectors) of crown taxes of the village of Herakleia. Paid for the crown taxes twenty four drachmas, = 24 drachmas. (m. 2) a further 8 drachmas. Mesore a further 8 drachmas. Thoth of year 3 a further 4 8 drachmas: Phaophi 8 drachmas: Hathyr four drachmas, = 4 drachmas: Choiak four drachmas, 4 drachmas: Tybi four drachmas, 4 drachmas: Mecheir four drachmas, 4 drachmas: Phamenoth 4 drachmas: Pachon four drachmas, = 4 drachmas: a further 4 drachmas: Payni four drachmas, = 4 drachmas: Epeiph four drachmas, = 4 drachmas: Mesore four drachmas,= 4 drachmas; Phaophi four drachmas, = 4 drachmas.
