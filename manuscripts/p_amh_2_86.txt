[TRANSLATION]
1     To Hermaios, [son] of Apollonios, Philoclaudian,
2     the one also called Althaios, exegetes of the Hermopolite [nome],
3     from Hermias son of Hermias, a Hermopolite. I wish to lease for five years from the present tenth year
4     of the Emperor Caesar Vespasian Augustus the [property] left behind by Didyme of Hermias, sister of mine, [now] dead, [to] two orphan children Hermophilos and Salion, both of Areios, [consisting of] land and possessions and whatever else I find of theirs
5     in whatever manner as a lease, per year
6     of silver drachmas two hundred sixty, apart from genuine public [taxes], which also I will pay per year, twice yearly in [the] month of Phaophi to those to whom you, the exegetes, may order. If any [year] should become rainless, [a reduction] will be conceded
7     to me according to [the] proportion of the lease. And if other [goods] are sold apart from genuine public [taxes], similarly
8     [a reduction] will be conceded to me. And if [a] levy having been made,
9     it will be possible for you [either] to lease to others if you wish, [or]
10    to lease to me on these [terms]. Year ten of the Emperor
11    Caesar Vespasian Augustus, Phamenoth
12    seventh.
