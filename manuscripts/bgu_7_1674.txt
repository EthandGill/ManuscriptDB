[TRANSLATION]
1     … and you did not write back to me what you want me to do. If then you want to have something from them, the
2     quantity write [for me] and I will receive it for you, since, as I wrote you, receive from Borius my farmer
3     the twenty drachmas, take [them] from Heron
4     from me two ropes of garlic. For then there was not yet passage for them, since I would have sent them to you. And
5     the tablet to you I will send [the one on] astronomy,
6     since I was burdened in other kinds [but] the nautical [one]
7     and no longer received anything. And concerning the others
8     I hold [these] in mind. When there is and among the friends, whom I have instructed, I will send [it] to you.
9     Farewell.
10    … from Heraiscus the physician.
