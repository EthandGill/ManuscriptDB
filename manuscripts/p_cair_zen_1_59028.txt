[TRANSLATION]
1     Satyra to Zenon gr[eetin]gs. Apollonius having ordered clothing
2     to give to us, both to me and to my mother, and you will find the memorandum which he wrote
3     Apollonius concerning these things; for from him we have not received [it], and now it is [a] year
4     this second. You would do well having examined and made known to Apolloni[us] … [something] concerning us to make mention, so that we not be naked. And
5     this let him give to have privately from you. [And] concerning the provision-ration look after; altogether we have not received
6     except once, and this which you sent to the Demetrians to give to us. Well
7     you would do well having also looked after these things, if it seems good to you, [do it] as quickly as possible.
8     Farewell.
9     Satyra['s]
10    of the [maiden].
11    To Zenon.
