[TRANSLATION]
1     ... through Adrastus, protector of Tilothees, if ... be judged
2     ... (you) indicated to me how we might meet with them
3     Since ... [Sot]erichos [went] away, it will [be] to take counsel somehow if he is able to send forward someone to Iaseron the ... linen for the sake of the plots of
4     the house; the tenant with me is on [the trespass]; I fear
5     him because the time of his lease has ended;
6     for his lease is until the twenty-second year; and if he is able
7     to seize beforehand and concerning the against-Kerke before the and
8     ... to be seized, because it has been leased in parts; concerning the
9     Isidos Lochiad, nothing you have indicated to me; what has happened to you
10    toward Antigonon; but the other things, take care of yourself so that you might be well, which is indeed greatest
11    I consider; farewell; of the year twenty-second of Caesar, Choiach twenty-fourth.
