1     it was registered [before] … Augustuses.
2     [In the consulate of] our lords [Licinius] Augustus [and Constantine] son of Augustuses, on the Kalends of August, in Ptolemais Euergetis of the
3     Arsinoite nome.
4     [acknowledges Aur]elia Ptolemais, [daughter of] Dioskoron of Her[a]cl[ion], former councillor of the city of Arsinoe, mother Demetrias
5     from the quarter of the Bithynians [of] Ision,
6     [approximately aged] … without a scar, not having [without a guardian] acting as a minor, one who is present and consenting and co-signing in this
7     sale of the one living with her
8     [to] her [husband] of Aurelius Gerontius, former chief priest, councillor of the same city, son of Heron, he too having been councillor of the
9     same city of Arsinoe,
10    to Aurelius Philippus, [son of] Isidore, mother Thaisarios, from the quarter of the Bithynians, [of] Ision, [that] Ptolemais has sold to Philippus from now for all time
11    the house belonging to her in the metropolis in the aforesaid quarter of the Bithynians [of] Ision, in which [is] an atrium and courtyard on her existing measurements
12    and cubit-measures and foundations and walls and light-wells and entrances and exits and all other uses and rights according to the ancient and up to now customary [practice],
13    whose neighbors, as from agreement they stated, [to the] south [are the] heirs of Theodorus from the registers, [to the] north [is] a
14    house that was sold, another house, to the east [is] a public street through which [is] entrance and exit, to the west [is] Kyrillutos' house, and [Ptolemais] the one acknowledging has received from the purchaser Philippus the agreed [sum] of the price in silver
15    thirteen talents, which is talents thirteen, in full by hand, and she shall guarantee herself, the one acknowledging,
16    Ptolemais and [those] from her to the purchaser Philippus and [those] from him [as to] the [matters] according to the sale of the aforesaid house and atrium and courtyard with full guarantee, which also she shall provide unencumbered and
17    without pledge and without borrowed [interest] and clear of every debt, both public and private, and of all claims in perpetuity, and from now the purchaser Philippus shall hold the house and be master and carry away the remainder into his own [possession] and manage and administer it as he wishes,
18    to which also shall not come [to claim it] the one acknowledging
19    Ptolemais, nor [shall] any other on her behalf [in any] manner, but also she shall remove any one coming upon [it] or making a claim in whatever manner immediately at her own [expenses] and moreover [she shall] pay the [price] with one-and-a-half-fold and the fees and the
20    expenses double. Concerning that these [things are] so [correctly]
21    [done] well and having been questioned she [acknowledged]. Aurelia Ptolemais, acting without a guardian, acknowledge [that] I have sold to Philippus the
22    house belonging to me in the metropolis in the quarter of the Bithynians [of] Ision, in which [is] an atrium and courtyard, whose neighbors [are] set forth, and I have received the price
23    of silver talents thirteen in full by hand, and I guarantee [it] clear as set forth, and having been questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius Euporion, veteran,
24    I wrote for her, [she being] illiterate. Aurelius Gerontius, former chief priest, councillor, husband of Ptolemais, I consent to the sale as set forth, and having been questioned I acknowledged.
25    Aurelius Nepos, veteran, I have acted [as I should] as set forth and delivered [the document].
