[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious [master, greatest benefactor, Flavius Justinus]
2     the eternal Augustus and [Imperator, year 13, in the consulship of their serenity]
3     for the 2nd time, and of Flavius Tiberius also called New Constantine, our most fortunate Caesar, year 3,
4     Phamenoth 29, indiction 11, year 254 = 223.
5     To Flavius Apion, the all-praiseworthy and most magnificent, ex-consul
6     ordinarius and patrician, landowner also here in the city of Justinus-Nea,
7     through Menas his household servant who acts as questioner and provides to his own master,
8     the same all-praiseworthy man, the right of action and liability, Aurelius
9     Philoxenos son of Ap… his mother being Maria, from the hamlet of Kyamon
10    of the Justinopolite nome, belonging to your magnificence,
11    a registered farmer of it, greeting. I acknowledge, of my own free will
12    and by voluntary choice, swearing the divine and venerable
13    oath, that I stand surety and undertake before your magnificence for
14    [Phi]… son of …, his mother being Maria, and Phoibammon his brother,
15    they too originating from the same estate, and being themselves
16    registered farmers, on condition that they remain uninterruptedly
17    and dwell on the same estate with their belongings
18    and dear ones and wives and cattle, answerable for everything.
19    Surety made by Philoxenos son of Ap… from the hamlet of Kyamon,
20    undertaking for Phi… and Phoibammon his brother …
