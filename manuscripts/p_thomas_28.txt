1     I cede …
2     from the small building-plot, in front …
3     ceded to me by you. And if …
4     of the eighth part of the cell and the measu[re] …
5     of the cell from my building-plot, that is …
6     of the wall, from th[e] southern party-wall of the …
7     toward the south, where it reaches the same measure …
8     … o … u … of the sam[e] …
9     of the cell, to cede to you and not to have autho[rity] …
10    of the building-plot, but that it be under me, the afore[said Victor,] … [ceded to me]
11    by you in perpetuity, of which things we have duly made [this exchange, so that each part goes into]
12    the portion of each and goes out [from the portion of each, and] …
13    from now on to have and to be master of the po[rtion of each, and] …
14    and to enjoy and to pass on to one's own heirs and successors and to use
15    in whatever manner one wishes, without hindrance; of which (properties) willingly and being persuaded, and (we agree)
16    to guarantee to one another with every guarantee [against everyone and through] every person who shall come forward
17    or make a claim, at every time, under compul[sion; and] that nothing [contrary] ever go against
18    what has been written above; and that the one of us transgressing what has been written above, the party not abiding, shall give
19    to the party abiding one ounce of gold, and even against his will he shall abide by all that has been written herein,
20    besides this exchange being unbroken and secure, valid wherever it is produced,
21    written in triplicate, of identical form, each side to have one copy, with the subscription
22    of me Victor and of the one subscribing on behalf of Hermauos and of the customary witnesses. And having been formally questioned
23    about all these things, we acknowledged them. Aurelius Victor son of Besarion, the aforesaid, have made this
24    exchange and have sworn the divine oath as stated above. Aurelius Hermaos son of Psaios, the aforesaid, have made
25    this exchange and have sworn the div[in]e oath as stated above. Flavius Theoteknos son of Psaios, ex-praepositus,
26    having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters. Aurelius Ioannes son of Abraamios, I bear witness to
27    the exchange, having heard it from the contracting parties. Aurelius Apollos son of Dioskoros, I bear witness to
28    the exchange, having heard it from the contracting parties, subscribing with my own hand. Aurelius Senoethes son of Apollos,
29    I bear witness to the exchange, having heard it from the contracting parties.
30    Written by me, Victor, from the same village.
