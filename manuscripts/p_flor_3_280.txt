[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Senator the most illustrious,
2     Tybi 3, eighth indiction.
3     Aurelius David, son of Viktor, son of Kostantios,
4     of the chief villagers of the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, subscribing below, to Aurelius
5     Apollos the most admirable chief villager,
6     son of the (man) of blessed memory Dioskoros,
7     from the same village, greeting. I acknowledge through this
8     written security of mine that I owe
9     and am indebted to your admirableness, on account of the surety
10    and pure guarantee for the embole (grain-delivery)
11    of the most admirable Hadrianos the soldier
12    and collector of barley, thirty artabas of barley,
13    that makes 30 artabas of barley, which I shall repay to you
14    at the time of the harvest
15    of the coming ninth indiction, God willing,
16    by the measure of the most admirable Saul
17    the actuarius of Antaiou, and I shall make
18    the transport as far as the harbor
19    of the navigable river without any dispute
20    or delay, of necessity, all my
21    means being held by you until
22    the discharge of the aforesaid debt. This bond
23    I have established for you as security upon my subscription, and on being questioned I acknowledged.
24    I, Aur[elius Da]vid son of Viktor, the aforesaid, agree as aforesaid.
25    I, Aurelius Bot[tos] son of Promaos, witness the debt. I, Aurelius
26    Apollos son of Besios, witness the debt, having heard (it)
27    from the one who established (it).
28    It was written through me, Viktor son of Apoll(os).
