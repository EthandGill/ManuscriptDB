[TRANSLATION]
1     … [nomarch of] Antinoöpolis … to the [royal scribe] of the Arsinoite
2     [nome,] of the Polemon meris, [his dearest friend, greeting.]
3     … to those allotted to Antinoöpolis and … allotment … of poll-taxes exempt
4     … for fourteen years … single only.
5     … that the wives be Antinoïdes, the inquiry
6     … having taken place, Magnus the former prefect, having considered
7     [the matter in] his judicial session, through records, on the 1st of Phame[noth, among other things con]cerning the (taxes) of this kind, judged thus: Magnus
8     … the Egyptians toward the Antinoïdes … but if they remain, (saying) that on the one hand they make themselves Antinoeans
9     … but that on the other hand … they are liable to the poll-taxes … toward those …
10    … paying single they bear the poll-taxes; but I
11    consider them like the others … to become … thus he granted, permitting them to remain here where they reside … other poll-taxes, single indeed of one …
12    … the accountant of the nomarchy …
13    … to him who set out, concerning the examination of such matters
14    … the single poll-taxes, from those
15    nomes to be transferred here so as to pay them at Antinoöpolis, where indeed they reside, in accordance with what was judged in
16    the register submitted by those in charge of the examination of this part, … the undersigned being from the nome under you,
17    that they may have the right of intermarriage with the Antinoïdes, and through the petitions which they submitted to me, declaring that they had paid to you the poll-taxes
18    up to the (present), and that, as they say, they have receipts, requesting that it be written to you that, according to what was judged, they be relieved and transferred
19    here, I write to you, dearest friend, so that you may examine whether they pay poll-taxes with you, and may take care, in accordance with what was judged, to relieve them so that
20    here they
21    (may), where indeed they were declared to reside, pay, and that you make it clear to me.
22    I pray for your health, dearest friend.
23    Year 6, Mesore 1. And they are:
24    Didymos son of Posidonios, son of Chrates, from the village
25    of Tebtynis of the Polemon meris,
26    Orsenouphis son of Orsenouphis, from the same Tebtynis,
27    Akes son of Hor, son of Tryphon, whose mother is Arees,
28    from Narmouthis,
29    Dioskoros son of Eirenaios, son of Dioskoros, from the
30    same Narmouthis.
