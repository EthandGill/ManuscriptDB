[TRANSLATION]
1   In the consulship of Tus[c]us and A[n]ulinus,
2   Aurelius Patron son of Sabinus, of foreign (non-citizen) status,
3   of the village of Narmouthis,
4   to Aurelius Hatres son of Abok, likewise
5   of foreign status, of the village of Arsinoe
6   of the Herakleides division, greeting.
7   I have received back from you what is [yo]ur debt(?),
8   of silve[r, fi]ve thou[sa]nd
9   [d]rachmas, [concerning which] also no(thing).
10   I [have] no [claim against you] about [a]ny[thi]ng
11   at al[l, (any) ma]tter, writt[e]n
12   or unwritten, …, and on being formally
13   question[ed] I acknowledged. —
14   I, Aureli(us) Patron, have received back the whole of
15   your debt(?), of silver, five thousand drachmas,
16   and I have no claim against you, as is stated above.
17   I, Aur(elius) Sarapion son of Theon, wrote on behalf of
18   him, as he is il[lit]erate.
19   Year 12 and year 11 and year [4] of
20   our lords Diocletian a[nd Max]imian,
21   Augusti, and Constantius and [Max]imian,
22   the most illustrious Caesars, Phaoph(i) 13.
