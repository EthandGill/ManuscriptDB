[TRANSLATION]
1     To Gaius [Turran]ius
2     From So[teric]hus of Noukios, priest, appointed and by the fellow-priests Aruotos and Arsiesios and Soterichous, priests of Isis and Sarapis and Arpsenesius and
3     of Asklepios of those in Busiris of above-Memphis
4     of Herakleopolis. Greatest hegemon, from before and from the times of the queen until
5     of the fifth and twentieth [god]
6     and [Augustus] Caesar, of the year, the four priests being from priests serving the sacred [rites] and
7     performing purifications and sacrifices two
8     apart from [two] undisturbed we have been regarding the poll-tax, but in the present 26th year of Caesar
9     of Epiodhoros the royal [scribe]
10    of the nome, having brought us into demand regarding the poll-tax, we were pres[sed]
11    for four years, each of us …
12    [Silver] drachmae 64 and dike-tax [silver]
13    drachmae 108. Wherefore we request.
