[TRANSLATION]
1     Pabelleous son of Onnophris, Persian of the epigone,
2     about forty years old, with a scar on the forehead on the left side, to Harmiysis son of Onnophris, about twenty-six years old,
3     with a scar on the left calf: that he has received from him,
4     on the spot, hand to hand, out of the house, of stamped silver
5     forty-eight drachmas, and that, in lieu of the interest on these and of necessities and of clothing, the acknowledging party shall provide his own daughter
6     Kolleuthis, remaining with Harmiysis and
7     those acting for him for one year from the aforesaid
8     time, abiding and doing everything enjoined
9     upon her, not staying away by night nor absent by day from the house of Harmiysis without
10    his consent, but also following him
11    everywhere throughout Egypt; and it shall not be permitted to him,
12    Pabelleous, within the time to take the daughter away; and if he takes her away or if she herself willingly
13    departs, or otherwise they transgress any of the things written above,
14    or … or doing damage or embezzling
15    is caught (in respect) of the property of Harmiysis or of those acting for him, or, having received anything to guard, does not render it safe, unless
16    something be taken from her by force, let the acknowledging party pay
17    to Harmiysis on the spot both the sums which he has received from him, as
18    aforesaid, forty-eight drachmas of silver with the addition of one half
19    and the interest, and as a penalty one hundred drachmas of silver, and to the treasury [an equal sum],
20    and the damage, and for each day on which she shall be in default
21    of the … three obols of copper, and the capital
22    and fivefold the amount stolen, but for the embezzlement
23    one-and-a-half times, and the interest at the simple rate. And if she does not
24    hand over (the property) or does not render it safe, the value according to
25    the assessment of Harmiysis, Harmiysis having
26    the right of execution both upon the debtor and upon his property as if by legal judgment. And when the time is fulfilled, let
27    the acknowledging party repay the aforesaid forty-eight drachmas of silver,
28    and if he does not repay, let him pay with the addition of one half and interest
29    and as a penalty another one hundred drachmas of silver and to the treasury [an equal sum].
30    Subscriber for the acknowledging party: Kollouthos son of Mieus, about 20 years old, with a scar on the right wrist.
31    Pabelleous son of Onnophris, Persian of the epigone:
32    I acknowledge that I have received from Harmiysis forty-eight drachmas of silver …
