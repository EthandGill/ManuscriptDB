[TRANSLATION]
1     Pikos, tax collector of silver payments,
2     to Petechespokhrates, also called Apollos,
3     of Apollos, through the agents of Panameus, Senthotes,
4     and of Pathreios, Bechis.
5     I received for the geometria-tax of Charakos, 11th year, dirty drachmai twenty,
6     total drachmai 20. Of the 11th year of Hadrian
7     Caesar, the lord, Mesore 22.
