1     [It] has been measured into the granary of the metropolis, produce of year 16
2     of Hadrian the lord, Pauni 29, in the name
3     of Senchonios Kametios, through Senfatrous
4     the younger, for the Agora district, wheat, artaba one [and] one-quarter; [it] totals wheat artaba 1 1/4. Another
5     Epeiph 5, wheat [of an] artaba, half [and] one-quarter
6     [it] totals wheat [of an] artaba 1/2 1/4. Pikos I have signed.
7     A measurement [of] wheat [of an] artaba 1/2 1/4.
