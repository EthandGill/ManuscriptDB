[TRANSLATION]
1     [Yea]r 17 and 16 of our lo[rds Diocletian] and Maximian the Augusti
2     [and the] ninth [year] of our lords Co[nstantius and Maximi]an the most noble
3     [Cae]sars, Pauni 12. In the consulsh[ip of Postumius Titianus for the 2nd time and Viri]us Nepotianus, in the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites
4     Aurelius Di[dymos also called Sarapion son of P]yrrhos, from the splendid
5     and most splendid (city) of the Oxyrhynchit[es] … bears witness (against) the bondsman (systates) Isidoros
6     also called Apollonios … from the same city,
7     not worthily of the [scrupulousness] of the laws [and of the f]ear of the most eminent
8     [pr]efect of Egypt Clod[ius Culcianus] … and since
9     … the (woman) from the … necessarily
10    … here in the deposition [of witness] … he placed. Therefore the …
11    … neither account … to have made
12    … of evasion of [a litur]gy … nor …
13    … having appointed him, in what manner I do not know [h]ow it came to pass
14    against his own wife Aurelia Thermouthion, and that he deceived
15    her and the female white donkey foal which she has, that for use
16    he had taken (it), and having taken (it) on such a charge he departed,
17    [an]d today three days holding control of her (the donkey), neither having again made declaration nor having restored the donkey to its proper station, and
18    since the audacious act belongs to no other authority but the manliness
19    of the most eminent prefect of Egypt Clodius Culcianus, this
20    deposition of witness he set forth publicly, so that all may know, and through the
21    good faith of the stationed beneficiarius the greatness of the prefecture may not escape notice,
22    before whom it is unhesitating to impose against his unlawful undertaking the due and fitting penalty.
23    I, Aurelius Didymos also called Sarapion, have made the deposition of witness as is set forth above.
