[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the sixth year of the [Emperor], the third
2     Caesar M[ar]cus Aurelius
3     Antoninus Augu[stus] and Emperor
4     Caesar Lucius Aurelius Verus
5     Augustus. Phaophi 23. Origen
6     and the partners, grain-collectors of the village of Theadelphia
7     we have measured out from the produce of the past
8     fifth year to Sabein[a], daughter of Sabeinus
9     of Ptolemais of Drymus, vineyard-land, of wheat an artaba
10    one-sixth, of residents artabas eight
11    one-third one-twelfth, of surcharge an artaba one-half
12    one-fourth; these become of the contribution of wheat in measure
13    public measure, [upon demand,] artabas ten one-third;
14    become of wheat 10 3, and the additionally-measured and the difference.
