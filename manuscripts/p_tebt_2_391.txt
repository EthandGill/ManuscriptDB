[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakles and Athenodoros and Heron and
2     Zoilos, the four collectors (praktores) of the poll-tax (laographia)
3     of the village of Tebtynis, we acknowledge
4     voluntarily and of our own accord to have divided (the duties),
5     further from the month Hathyr 15, which is the reckoning of Phaophi of the third year of Trajan
6     Caesar the lord, for only the presen[t]
7     third year of Imperator Caesar Nerva
8     Trajan Augustus Germanicus: that Athenodoros
9     and Herakles have been allotted the men residing and settled in the village,
10    (namely) those residing and settled in the village,
11    but Heron and Zoilos, they too have been allotted all those residing and settled in other villages and
12    in the metropolis, on condition that those [allotted] the outside (population)
13    shall pay over each month of silver
14    one thousand one hundred drachmas, but those allotted the village shall make good each month
15    the remainder of the assessment of the poll-tax; and the wage of the swordbearer (guard)
16    is to be against those allotted the village. And if any of us the four
17    should transgress against the aforewritten (terms),
18    he shall pay to the one abiding (by them) of silver drachm[as]
19    five hundred and to the treasury the same.
20    Let this instrument (cheir) be valid as if
21    deposited in a public office. And the future
22    additional apportionment of the present 3rd year each shall demand from the men whom he has been allotted.
23    Year third of Imperator Caesar
24    Nerv[a] Trajan Augustus Germanicus
25    [Hathyr] …
