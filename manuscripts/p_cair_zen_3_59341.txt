[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonios the dioiketes, greeting, Theopropos
2     theoros, from Kalyndoi. In the 38th year
3     my farmer Theron contracted from
4     the city to supply wine for the
5     festival held in Kyprandoi each year,
6     for which I supplied the wine, 84 metretai, the metretes at 10 drachmas each,
7     which comes to 840 drachmas.
8     And toward this the treasurers Diophantos and Akrisios paid me back
9     600 drachmas, but the remaining
10    250 drachmas, because not all the contributions had come in,
11    they did not pay; so I summoned the treasurers
12    before both the strategos Motes and the oikonomos
13    Diodotos, demanding the 250 drachmas.
14    But the treasurers Diophantos and Akrisios required
15    that a decree be written for them, saying they were not authorized to pay without a decree; but the prytaneis
16    and the secretary delayed and did not write
17    the decree, until, having been appointed theoros by the city,
18    along with Diophantos, one of the treasurers, I arrived here before the king. If, then, it
19    seems good to you, you would do well to write both to our
20    city and to the strategos and to the oikonomos
21    that the 250 drachmas be paid to me,
22    [so that] I be not wronged,
23    but that I too may have enjoyed the kindness
24    from you.
25    Farewell.
26    Neon to Damonikos, greeting. If you are well and your other affairs
27    are to your liking, it would be well; I myself was in good health
28    and all the rest. You would do well
29    if, having reminded Zenon about the billet and the
30    fodder and the green-forage of the sixty-day (horses),
31    so that it be entered in my name; for now
32    we have both billeted-soldiers and pay the fodder
33    and the green-forage to the cavalryman, since
34    they paid no heed to the first letter;
35    but, as he writes, let him write to the council and
36    to the people. I am undertaking to come myself
37    at all events. You, then, until I arrive, remind
38    him, taking along also Ariston and Epharmostos
39    and Apollonios. I have written also to Apollonios and Epharmostos that they too remind on your behalf
40    Zenon.
41    Farewell.
42    From Zenon.
43    Concerning Therarchos, who has the sister of (my)
44    father, who dwells in Kalyndoi, you wrote that she should be exempt from billeting
45    [and] released from the fodder and green-forage to be given;
46    but Therarchos having died, they are being billeted (upon her)
47    [and] they exact both the fodder and the green-forage.
48    [You would do we]ll, then, to write to Diodotos the oikonomos and to the council
49    [and] to the people that for Neon the son
50    the same things be done.
51    … write to …kles to give back the mare of Satyros, my agent,
52    which he took, it being in foal,
53    and the young mule born from it.
