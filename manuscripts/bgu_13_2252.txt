[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius [A]mmonius, praepositus of the 12th [district]
2     From Aur[e]lii Ammonius Thiarchas and Le[o]ntius Apianos, both village-magistrates of the village Cynopolis; we give and declare
3     at our own risk laborer... [being]... for the need
4     [blamelessly] being performed
5     for the public [service]; may it prosper
6     ... [is]... Le[...]tos from the
7     same village of Cynopolis
8     in the consulship of Flavius Gallicanus
9     and of Valerius Tullianus of the most eminent
10    Mecheir 22
11    We Aurelii Ammonius and Leontius have submitted; Aurelius Polydorus
12    I wrote for them, they being illiterate
