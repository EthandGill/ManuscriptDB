[TRANSLATION]
1     Sokonopis, the one in charge of the disposition of the royal [documents]
2     [of] the Arsinoite in the 23rd year to the epistat[e and chief-]guard [and] guards and desert-guards and [village-chief]
3     and village-scribe, greetings. Petous, the [one presenting the] [order]
4     has taken from [us] the disposition [of the]
5     royal documents in Tali for the same year, [and it is possible]
6     for him to take handwritten-agreements of the royal oath from the
7     monograph-holders concerning not using private freight
8     of those coinciding with the purchase neither from the [unauthorized traders] conducting jointly, but from the [royal]
9     selling-places. In whatever you have need of those [toward]
10    these [matters], well you will do taking in hand willingly, and if anyone to you delivers one selling something or conducting unauthorized trade,
11    [the such ones] taking in hand together with whatever if
12    [with whatever cargo] they [have], restore upon Zopyron
13    [the overseer], so that [the proper] penalties be collected. Be well. Year 23, Thoth 26.
