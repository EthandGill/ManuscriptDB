[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign and consulship of our most divine master
2     Flavius Mauricius Tiberius the New, the eternal Augustus Imperator,
3     in the fourth year, on Mesore the twentieth, of the fourth indiction,
4     To Aurelia Aphthonia the most noble, from her father
5     the blessed Hypsistos, from the (city) of the Hermopolites; from the Aurelii Papnouthios
6     son of Ioannes, his mother being …as, and Pinoutios his brother, tow-workers (stippourgoi)
7     from the village of Ares of the Hermopolite (nome). We acknowledge, being mutually liable
8     and undividedly sureties for one another, in the framing of this
9     lease, willingly and of our own free will, that we have leased from you
10    for a ten-year period, reckoned from the crops of the, with God, fifth indiction
11    and it (itself), the two arouras belonging to you of sowable waterless
12    land, being in the eastern (part) of the same city, south of the
13    embankment, eastward toward the south of the (church of the) holy Virtue,
14    under the guard of the (people) from the village of Enseu of the Hermopolite (nome)
15    of which the neighbors: to the south …, to the north …
16    to the east …
17    to the west, for sowing
18    and planting of crops whichever we may choose, as rent for these
19    per year twelve artabas of grain, makes of grain artabas 12, which
20    rent, new, clean, sifted, by the
21    Athenian measure, we will deliver and restore to your house
22    in Hermopolis with our own animals and at (our own) expenses, in the
23    month Epeiph, per year, without delay. And we will furnish
24    per year, by way of custom, eight bundles of ears (of grain) and
25    of grain-chaff a basket [and] of milk one pot, and
26    of bean(s) … a bundle of one, and if you wish that we do not withdraw
27    from the cult[ivation of] the same two arouras before the completion
28    of the said t[en]-year period, on (the condition) that we …
29    paying the rent fixed, with the help of the laws and …
30    tē … ei … and it is not permitted to us to withdraw … ento …
31    The lease (is) val[id and se]cure, and on being questioned we acknowledged it. Papnouthios and Pinoutios sons of Ioan[nes] [the aforesaid have leased as aforesaid. ] … [ ] …
32    … from Hermopolis I bear witness to the lease
33    having heard from [th]ose who made it; Epiphanios …
34    from Hermopolis I bear witness to the lease, having heard from those who made it.
35    Through me, Iou… notary, it was written.
