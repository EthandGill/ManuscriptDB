[TRANSLATION]
1     Theon, also called Anthos, [son] of Ammōnianos, leased
2     [former] gymnasiarch [and] agoranomos of Alexandria through Prokōpianos, [son] of Philos, to Petosirēs, son of Hōros, [son] of Petosirēs, [his] mother Chēnpsoitē, of [the] village Sinagērē of the Hermopolitan [nome], for four years [four harvests] from the present 21st year of Trajan Hadrian
3     Caesar, our lord, the property belonging to him from
4     the Hippalan lot near Pounīn, [of] arurae
5     nineteen [and a] quarter, so that in the present 21st year and 23rd year to gather [green fodder] as tax
6     of each aruron from surveying, from [the standard] measure
7     at silver drachmae twenty-two, and in the incoming 22nd year
8     [and 24th year] to sow wheat for rent of each aruron as aforesaid at wheat measure, public, [in]
9     total, artabae seven. But the aforementioned grain
10    rents yearly shall measure the lessee
11    into public treasuries of the Oxyrhynchite, [of which he shall] deliver [a] clean total at the proper time …
12    … and the yearly silver rent he shall pay
13    the same lessee to the lessor [or to the]
14    [agents] of him in [the month of] Pauni yearly without delay,
15    of the yearly water-guarding and working of the embankments of the aforementioned fields being
16    towards the lessee, who agrees to have received the embankments from the start waterproofed,
17    which he shall also deliver in the final year as he received [them] … …
