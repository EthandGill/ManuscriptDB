[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting, Thasos son of Petathymis. I am wronged by Herakleides,
2     one of the Jews settled in Muchis of the division of Polemon. For, there being a maternal house and courtyard belonging to me in the aforewritten village,
3     on the 1st day of Choiach of the 1st year Herakleides came, taking with him also
4     several men whose names I do not know, and … bringing brick, began to build up
5     my house. Being prevented by my people, he did not desist but even laid
6     his hands upon them. And when I petitioned the laokritai against
7     the builder, they wrote to the guards to bring him before them
8     … he was brought up because they were maltreated by Herakleides.
9     I beg you therefore, O king, to order Diophanes the strategos to write
10    to Demetrios his agent to send Herakleides before him,
11    likewise also the builder, that they may receive correction concerning these things. For this being done, I shall obtain help from you.
12    Farewell.
13    To Demetrios: best of all, settle them; but if not, send them
14    that they may be judged before the proper tribunal. Year 1, Panemos 12, Choiach 7.
15    Thasos to Demetrios.
