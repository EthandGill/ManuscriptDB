[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Licinius
2     Augustus for the 5th time and Crispus the most illustrious
3     Caesar for the 1st time.
4     Aurelius [Choous] son of Kames, mother Tallos,
5     from the village of Taampemou of the 5th pagus of the
6     Oxyrhynchite nome, to Aurelius Ammon
7     son of Theon, from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, greeting. I acknowledge
8     that I have received from you on loan through hand
9     from your house, of silver of the Augusti's coinage, three talents and three thousand drachmas,
10    total 3 talents, 3,000 drachmas, which I shall of necessity repay to you
11    in the present month Pachon, without interest and without
12    any delay or subterfuge, and if not,
13    I shall pay you for the time overrun
14    the established interest on each mina
15    for each month, the right of execution belonging to you.
16    In the consulship of our lords Licinius
17    Augustus for the 5th time and Crispus the most illustrious
18    Caesar for the 1st time.
19    Aurelius Choous son of Kames, mother Tallos,
20    from the village of Taampemou of the 5th pagus of the
21    Oxyrhynchite nome, to Aurelius Ammon
22    son of Theon, from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
23    greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from you
24    on loan through hand from your house, of silver
25    of the Augusti's coinage, talents
26    three and three thousand drachmas, total 3 talents, 3,000 drachmas,
27    which I shall of necessity repay to you in the present month Pachon, without interest and without any delay and subterfuge,
28    and if not, I shall pay you for the time overrun
29    the established interest on the mina
30    for each, for each month, the right of execution belonging (to you).
31    Bond of Choous from Tampemou for 3 talents, 3,000 drachmas.
