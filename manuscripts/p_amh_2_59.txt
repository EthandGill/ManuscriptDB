[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 30, Pachon 29. [agrees/acknowledges] …
2     the grain-collector around the Philopator workshop [has measured] upon the following
3     in Soknopaiou Isle on behalf of him from products of the same year
4     from Marreious Sisouchos and his partners in the lease of wheat artabas
5     sixty eight and one-quarter [artaba], [which] amount to 68 and one-quarter [artaba].
6     the same Payni 4, wheat artabas twenty five and one-quarter [and] 1/12, [which] amount to 25 and one-quarter [and] 1/12.
7     19, wheat artabas ten and one-half and one-quarter [and] 1/12, [which] amount to 10 and one-half [and one-quarter] 1/12.
8     29, wheat artabas ten and one-quarter [and] 1/12, [which] amount to 10 and one-quarter 1/12.
9     Epeiph 9, ten [and] 1/12, [which] amount to 10 [and] 1/12. Total 125 and one-sixth.
10    Herodes the secretary has jointly measured as previously written wheat artabas one hundred twenty five [and] one-sixth,
11    [which] amount to wheat artabas 125 [and] one-sixth.
