[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aelius Publius, the most eminent prefect of Egypt,
2     from Gaius Julius …n Serenus, victor in sacred games, victor in many contests, extraordinary athlete, of the Oxyrhynchite,
3     and citizen of Antinoe [and of ma]ny other cities. To these very universal laws
4     and to your experience in [all thing]s I make this petition. I well know that from your
5     benevolence I shall obtain what I supplicate. That victors in the sacred games are not to be subjected to vexations or to any
6     public services, as you know, has been decreed by the laws and likewise by our lords
7     Diocletian and Maximian the Augusti and Constantius and Maximian
8     the most illustrious Caesars, that those who have reached sixty years of life's span be released from every kind of public service and vexation, so that of the rights belonging to me
9     from either side, nothing else is lacking than that your greatness should
10    become concurring. Now I happen to be a victor in the sacred games, having, gloriously and becomingly, the sacred
11    contests which (were held) for the victory and eternal continuance of our l[o]rds Diocletian
12    and Maximian the Augusti and Constantius and Maximi[a]n the most illustrious
13    Caesars been crowned. But now, having come to old age after my prime
14    and after the training therein by me and a time of … life of sixty-four years, so that in this respect I may be benefited by the divine decree of the
15    greatest our kings and most illust[rious Caesa]rs, I re[qu]est, being a moderate man,
16    and (I) beg, if it seems good to your Fortune, … in both these respects
17    to grant me your assent and by your sacred subscri[ption to command that I hav]e freedom from molestation
18    in some manner, so that I too, having obtained the [benefaction from you], may render the greatest thanks
19    to your unsurpassable Fortune. [Farewell.] (May you) [prosp]er.
20    Gaius Julius …
21    Year 15 …
