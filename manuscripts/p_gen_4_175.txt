r.3    ὑφ’ ὑμῶν στίππια ὑπὲρ φόρω[ν τὴς] ἐνάτης ἰνδικτίονος ἀριθμῷ δεσμιδίων ρν 𐅸,

[TRANSLATION]
1     From [A]pollonius to the [hippo-]workers of Taanpe[mou], greeting.
2     Provide to Thonios of Pathatres through Apollonius the merchant the things being taxed
3     by you hippo-things for taxes [of the] ninth indiction in number of bundles 150 …,
4     by the customary weight.
5     Year 30 and 20 and 12 and 3, Thoth … I noted.
