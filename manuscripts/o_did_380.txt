1     Receive then from the horseman.
2     bundles of cabbage, 3, and a bundle of [vegetables]; [for the rest] then.
3     You will write back to me about what to you,
4     I sent, whether you received [or] did not receive. I sent to you a pallet
5     [and] sandals and very many vegetables, [and] [you do not] write to me.
6     You know that I [did not] send Scnipes for an [uneducated] matter, but for your sake; to the gods, for
7     grace we have, of nothing
8     we lack. Just now we [are grateful].
