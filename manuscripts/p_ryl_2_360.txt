[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 24 of the Emperor Caesar Titus
2     Aelius Hadri[anus] Antoninus Augustus
3     Pius, Thoth 7, reckoning of Mesore, [he] recorded/struck off
4     Satyros son of Asclepiades, son of Sōt, mother Tamystha
5     of the poll-tax, 23rd year of Boutaphius, drachmas twenty, [which] amount to 20,
6     with additionally recorded bronze obols ten.
