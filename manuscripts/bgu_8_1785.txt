[TRANSLATION]
1     To Cleon
2     I received what you had written.
3     I wished then, just as [the]
4     gods know, altogether
5     not to give you the subscription.
6     Of the inhabitants indeed the
7     appearance through themselves having managed [on their own] I was hindered;
8     still not at all
9     am I inhuman,
10    for instead of more … [of the]
11    [things] being brought [to you] into few [things]
12    I reduced, just as
13    to Diophantus [it] became visible
14    [it] has come about.
