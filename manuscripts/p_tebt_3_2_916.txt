[TRANSLATION]
1     Petesis to Ptolemaeus, greetings.
2     If Herakleidus [contracts]
3     for the same month(s), wheat 150, [of the] four-month [period] 600,
4     of the artaba drachmas 120, talents 12; everything of bronze talents seventeen; total of bronze talents 17, together [as above]
5     accordingly. But if you have advanced anything, [charge] it to this
6     account.
7     Farewell. Year 21, Choiak 30.
8     of bronze talents 17.
