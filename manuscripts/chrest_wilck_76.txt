[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: From the minutes of Ulpius Serenianus, vir egregius, high-priest. Year 11 of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar, the lord, Tyb[i] 28. In Memphis. He saluted the prefect, vir clarissimus, and after that, as he held audience at the temple of Apis Panephremmis, son of Stotoetis also called Satabous, elder priest, having presented his own son Panephremmis and having requested that permission be given to circumcise him and having submitted a letter written concerning him by [Sa]rapion, strategos of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division, through Alexandros, ex-gymnasiarch, dated to the past year 10 Phaophi 6, Serenia[nus] enquired of the coryphaei and sub-coryphaei and keepers of the sacred records who were present, whether the boy had any blemish. Upon their saying that he was without a blemish [Ulpius] Seren[i]a[nu]s, high-priest and minister for the temple administration, by appending his signature to the letter gave permission for the boy to be circumcised in accordance with custom. I have read this.
