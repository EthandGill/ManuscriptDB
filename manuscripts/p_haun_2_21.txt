[TRANSLATION]
1     … is and …
2     … the price of wine. Here it is sold
3     four denarii and a nummus the sambaton into a boat. Learn also the spent
4     expense for them and the tax. And if you wish I will send you as much as you want, I am able
5     for indeed, and if I do not have the price to pay, having given as pledge as much as I have
6     in hand. And if again you are not willing to send me more quickly a letter so that
7     also I may live without worry, for at every hour
8     ready [I] am awaiting you
9     letters. But do not neglect me, not at all
10    for I am grieved, you having written nothing
11    to me. I pray you to be well
