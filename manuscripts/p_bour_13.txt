[TRANSLATION]
1     Petosiris son of Iranoupis and Petermouthes son of Apynchis, both of the lentil-dealers from Memphis, acknowledge that they have established between themselves a partnership and association from the
2     … of the month Kaisareios, the 5 intercalary days,
3     of the present second year … Petosiris having in the same year taken over from Isidoros and partners,
4     the two contractors of the market-supervision and other categories … the vetch-selling business of the Memphite (nome) …
5     … of the same Memphite (nome), so that they shall jointly, by the half share, manage and produce vetch and
6     sell at their own expense from … out of the existing, as aforesaid, vetch-selling business and all the produce; first of all they shall pay off the amounts promised by Petosiris for the rent of the vetch-shop, one hundred sixty silver (drachmas), and on account of rent … four silver drachmas …
7     … drachmas … eight silver drachmas, and to the market-supervisors a half-artaba of whole lentils, and on account of rent …
8     ten drachmas of Petosiris …
9     … for the expense; and the net surplus profit remaining thereafter shall be in two equal and like parts, and
10    each of them shall receive one part and …
11    … to contain the … account of the silver toll-tax …, the right of execution in all matters
12    belonging to Petermouthes against Petosiris himself and against all his other property
13    [belonging to him], as if by a court judgment. Year two of Emperor Nerva Caesar Augustus Germanicus, Phamenoth 3 …
14    Petermouthes son of Apynchis … the partnership …
15    Petosiris son of Iranoupis, as aforesaid. Dinon son of Artemidoros wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
