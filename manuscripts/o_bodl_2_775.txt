[TRANSLATION]
1     [Collector] of Ailouria, collector of monetary [matters] of Charakos, to Pikotos
2     [son] of Pekousios, Faherius. I have received on account of a distribution of taxes
3     of the 4th year [in] tainted drachmas, four. [This] makes drachmas four. Of the 4th year
4     of Antoninus Caesar, the lord,
5     Mesore 15. [Of] Ailouria, through Mencheus, I have signed.
