[TRANSLATION]
1     Gaius Julius Diogenes, soldier of legion II Traiana Fortis, century of Cornelius Priscus, to Aurelii Dionysios and Maximina, bankers,
2     greetings. Pay to Saturninus, imperial steward, Aurelius Felix the most eminent prefect following along, the price for which I was confirmed in the nome of Arsinoe[te]
3     concerning the village Hephaestias of the Heraklidou division, of one and [one-half] arourae of vineyard land
4     formerly [of Tiberius] Gemellus, [now] of the most sacred treasury … [of the] proclamation [of] the same [prefect] in the present [9th year] of silver drach[mas]
5     one thousand two hundred and for the hundredth[s] [four eight and for]ty
6     and for [guarantee] drachmas two hundred fifty, total drachmas 1450.
7     Year 9 of the Emperors Caesars Lucius Septimius Severus P[ious] Pertinax [Parthian-conquering]
8     Adiabene-conquering Parthian-conquering Greatest and of Marcus Aurelius Anton[inus] Pious [Augustuses]
9     Epeiph.
