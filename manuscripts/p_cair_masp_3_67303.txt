[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Justinian the eternal Augustus, Emperor,
2     in the twenty-sixth year, after the consulate of Flavius Basilius the most glorious, the twelfth year,
3     Pachon the second, of the ending first indiction.
4     Aurelius Menas son of Psais, of the mother Rebekka, a hired farmer, from the plain of the village
5     of Tanyaitheos of the lesser Apollonopolite nome, now passing his time here in the village of Aphrodite of the
6     Antaiopolite nome, with a surety who also guarantees him for all payment and restoration
7     of the rent set out below, of the haulage-wagon, and of the unimpaired return of the same,
8     namely Aurelius Palos son of Hermauos, of the mother Thekla, a carpenter from the same village
9     of Aphrodite, to Flavius Dioskoros, son of Apollos son of Dioskoros, of blessed memory,
10    the most admirable tax-collector from the same village of Aphrodite, greetings. I acknowledge, willingly
11    and convinced, that I have leased from you, for as many days as the work of the sole
12    gathering of my produce of the three estates under me on the present sole second indiction (requires),
13    the haulage-wagon belonging to you, fitted with iron and equipped, that is, the two
14    wheels with iron casings and naves and felloes and pyrinthos with rings and pole
15    and yoke with chains and axle and a wooden support, that is, the pkom(?), on condition that I have this under me
16    for the work of my produce of the said sole estates until the gathering now present
17    in the field, and that I give to you, by way of rent for this work, ten artabas of clean wheat, by my
18    six-measure of each artaba, and that I make the transport of these as far as your house at
19    my risk and resource, with all my property liable, generally and individually. And we are ready
20    to restore to you the said wagon equipped, unimpaired in every respect, just as we received it now.
21    The lease agreement shall be valid and secure, and having been asked the formal question on all points, we acknowledged. Aurelius Menas
22    son of Psais, with our sureties Aurelii Palos son of Hermauos, and David son of Hermauos, carpenters, the aforesaid, I have leased
23    and all is agreeable to me as aforesaid. Aurelius Psellos son of Isakios, having been requested, I wrote for them, they being illiterate … Kallinikos son of Viktor
24    I witness the lease, having heard from the contracting parties present and speaking to me. Aurelius Petros son of Hermauos
25    I witness the lease, having heard from the contracting parties present and speaking to me.
26    Written through me, Pilatos the notary … is(?)
