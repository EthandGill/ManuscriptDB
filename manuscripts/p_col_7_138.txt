[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Sarapammon and Aurelius Potammon,
2     superintendents of bullion, to Aurelius Isidoros
3     son of Ptolemaios, greeting. We have received from you, in accordance with the divine ordinance, on behalf of the possession (?) of your village,
4     of the boundary-survey of Karanis, of pure unwrought silver
5     four ounces, six grammata.
6     I, Potammon, have signed.
7     In the consulship of our lord Maximinus Caesar,
8     nine days before the Kalends of January.
9     Aurelius Eudaimon, former high priest, councillor,
10    superintendent of gold, to Isidoros, greeting.
11    I have received from you, on account of the
12    levy imposed in accordance with the divine command, on behalf of
13    the boundary-survey of Karanis, of pure gold,
14    six and a half grammata, total grammata 6 1/2. I, Eudaimon, have written the whole.
15    Ten days before the Kalends of May.
16    Aurelius Heron, councillor, superintendent of gold and bullion,
17    to Aurelius Isidoros son of Ptolemaios, greeting.
18    I have received from you, of the levy imposed in accordance with the divine ordinance,
19    of the village of Karanis, of the boundary-survey, of pure gold,
20    four and an eighth grammata, grammata 4 1/8. I, Heron, have signed.
21    Year 16 and 4 of our lords Galerius Valerius Maximianus
22    Augustus and Galerius Valerius Maximinus the most noble
23    Caesar, six days before the Kalends of May.
24    Aurelius Heron, councillor, superintendent, to Aurelius Heras son of Ptolemaios,
25    from the village of Karanis, greeting.
26    I have received from you, on account of the levy imposed,
27    on behalf of the village, of the boundary-survey of Karanis, of gold,
28    pure, four grammata; total of gold, grammata 4.
29    I, Heron, have signed.
30    Year 16 and 4 of our lords Galerius Valerius Maximianus
31    Augustus and Galerius Valerius Maximinus Caesar, five days before
32    the Kalends of May.
33    Aurelius Euporos, former magistrate, councillor, superintendent
34    of gold, to Aurelius Heras and Taesis, greeting.
35    I have received from you, on account of the levy
36    imposed in accordance with the divine ordinance, of the village,
37    of the boundary-survey of Karanis, three quarter eighth
38    sixteenth grammata, total grammata 3 1/4 1/8 1/16.
39    I, Gerontios, have signed.
40    Year 16 and 4 of our lords Galerius Valerius Maximianus
41    Augustus and Maximinus the most noble Caesar, two days before
42    the Kalends of May. I, son of Neilos, have signed.
