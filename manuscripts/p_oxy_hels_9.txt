[TRANSLATION]
1     From Apollonius, toparch
2     of certain villages of the lower toparchy.
3     Debts in the name of Gaius Julius
4     Socrates for the 11th year
5     of Tiberius Caesar Augustus:
6     for a plot of 9 artabas, a half-aroura holding bought in the 15th year from the (property) of Artemidorus, a half-aroura, 15;
7     and for Zosarion — I, Apollonius, have signed —
8     bought in the 43rd year of Discus, of dry land 1;
9     … of Moschion and Sogenes,
10    bought [from among the vineyards] in the 41st year, of dry-irrigated land 2; of wheat 3 artabas, [ ]
11    of the single-artaba (rate) 16, in vineyards 2, of wheat 3 artabas, total of wheat 19 artabas, … 1/2 6 choinikes, total 19 (artabas) 6 choinikes,
12    and for the 10th year likewise of wheat 19 artabas 1/2 6 choinikes,
13    and for the 9th year likewise of wheat 19 artabas 1/2 6 choinikes, total of wheat 59 artabas [a half-artaba 8 choinikes].
14    Of dyke-tax likewise for the 11th year 9 drachmas 3 obols, … [1 obols 5] 1/2,
15    of exchange-fee obol(s), total 11 (drachmas?) obols 3 1/2,
16    and for the 10th year likewise 11 (drachmas) obols 3 1/2, 9th year likewise 11 [obols 3 1/2, [ ]]
17    total 34 drachmas obols 4 1/2.
18    I, Dionysius, secretary, have presented (it).
19    Year 13 of Tiberius Caesar
20    Augustus, in the month Sebastos, the 19th.
