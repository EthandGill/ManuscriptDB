[TRANSLATION]
1     Mnesitheios to Apollophanes, greetings.
2     Receive from Philiskos, son of Tryphon,
3     Macedonian cavalry officer over men,
4     the price of the slave-girl Thermouthia, as of 30 years,
5     who is in flight, whom having searched out,
6     [Philiskos] will lead back to himself, whom he had purchased
7     from Lucius Rutilius Philomousos,
8     of copper talents 13; [of copper] [for] silver talents 2, [drachmas] 693, [and] 3 half-obol.
9     Fare[well]. Year … 20.
10    To Nikolaos: to you from the price
11    Provide drachmas twelve, [they] total drachmas 12.
