1     ☧ 99 (chi-mu-gamma; cipher for "amen")
2     I have received the letter of your Holiness; I have learned of your welfare from it, and I [re]joiced greatly. So then, according as your Reverence wrote to me concerning the [little] son, that I should write to him in a separate document, out of
3     the letter — I did not think that I had written to him
4     outside the letter, since he came to us.
5     My Littleness … unless it be for two days
6     more … let no occasions arise
7     for this … for the present, unless
8     in a [docu]ment … the holy, our father
9     A[pa] … the archbishop of Rakote (Alexandria), interpreting … the prophet who wrote, of the foundation
10    … this document, that … this [docu]ment … again, according as they first declared
11    … the … our … that men are wont to write
12    … a writing; and again, that you say, "I am a … the (one) of Christ"; I think … that I said
13    … unless she be a learner in the way that you wrote, lest perhaps
14    … [for] I am truly a learner, since again you also … to Kyrikos the lord, knowing that, since the day … I do not cease entreating him and instructing Ja[cob] … that profits him according to God; rather, rejoice,
15    … that your Piety command me some matter that … not to be reluctant; you heard up to this place, O brother. Forgive
16    [me] that I am one without care; I am not worthy that I … to worship the footprint [of your] feet, your Holiness. Forgive me that [I am] a babbler;
17    I have written many words, beyond my measure it is.
18    Do the charity (of love) and remember me in your holy prayers, that the God of … may make me
19    worthy to do his will of us.
20    I am one careless; I worship (you).
21    Your Reverence, through my writings, the least one, until the hour
22    when God shall forgive me, that I may worship you face to face.
