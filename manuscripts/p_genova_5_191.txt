1     In the fifth year of the Autocrator Caesar [Titus Aelius
2     Hadrian Antoninus] the Most August Pious [work was done]

6     Mesoheris son of Heracles of Phasot
7     mother Taonnophrios
8     Apollonios, [I have] noted
