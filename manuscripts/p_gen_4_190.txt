[TRANSLATION]
1     chmg (= 643) 99
2     In the consulship of the Flavii Symmachus and Boethius the most glorious …
3     Phaophi 26, 2nd indiction.
4     Aurelius Ioannes son of Phoibammon, his mother being Maria, from the hamlet of
5     Elias in the tax-collectorate of the village of Sinarchebis of the Hermopolite
6     nome, to Aurelius Ioannes the most admirable and steward
7     of the hamlet of Sinkere of the territory of Hermopolis, greeting.
8     I acknowledge that I have received and been paid in full from your
9     admirableness the agreed-upon between one another
10    complete and worthy price of a capital sum of new must wine, knidia
11    one hundred and twenty-three, total wine knidia 123, which I shall render to you
12    the most admirable at the time of the vintage in the month Mesore of the harvests of the fortunate, God willing, third indiction, in finest and most pleasing wine, by the measure of the most noble lady Dioskourias,
13    you providing the empty jars for them. And if it be found to be vinegar
14    or unfinished or stinking from that same wine up to the month Tybi
15    of the same indiction, it being incumbent on me to exchange it for you with good wine, without delay
16    and without dispute and without doubt and without pretext,
17    all my property being subject to you for this debt
18    as if by legal judgment. The document is authoritative and secure, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged.
19    Aurelius Ioannes son of Phoibammon, the aforesaid, have been paid in full the complete
20    price of the wine knidia 123, and I shall render it at the appointed term as set forth above.
21    Phoibammon son of Petros, deacon, I bear witness to the deed and to the giving of the gold of the price
22    of the wine. The Aurelii Ales son of Ammon and Taurinos son of Ioannes from Hermou-
23    polis, we bear witness to the deed and to the giving of the gold of the price of the wine.
24    Aurelius Elias, surety of the village of Sinarchebis, having been requested, wrote on his behalf and on behalf of those
25    present who do not know letters.
26    Through me, Hatres, notary, it was written.
27    Cheirograph: Ioannes son of Phoibammon from the hamlet of Elias …
28    … of the Hermopolite, of a capital sum of wine knidia 123.
