[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonios, strategos of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division, through
2     Heraklios also called Liberalis, who is succeeding to the strategia,
3     from Petaus, village-scribe of Ptolemais [Hor]mou [and] other villages.
4     In place of Philotas son of Ischyrion and of Phaesis
5     son of Pnepheros and of Gaion son of Ischyrion and of Heron
6     son of Konnos, the four collectors of grain-dues
7     for the public farmers of Ptolemais Hormou, and
8     of Petesouchos son of Phanesis and of Sarapion son of Horos,
9     the two, likewise, collectors of grain-dues
10    for the public farmers of the village of Kerkesoucha Orous,
11    the 6 men in all who are most nearly
12    completing [the appointed time], I give (as nominees) the undersigned,
13    being well-off and suitable, on my own judgement
14    and surety, of all who farm from the villages and
15    those around them,
16    to be sent according to custom by lot
17    to the most excellent epistrategos. And it is:
18    Of Ptolemais Hormou:
19    Heron son of Ischyrion, whose mother is Sambous, [having a resource of] 800 [drachmas]
20    Ptollas son of Ptollas (son) of Ischyrion, whose mother is Herais, likewise 900 drachmas
21    Ischyrion son of Heron (son) of Philammon, whose mother is Heraklous, [likewise] 800 drachmas
22    Aphrodas son of Theon, whose mother is Soumeis, likewise 800 drachmas
23    Heron son of Heron (son) of Ischyrion, whose mother is Julias, likewise 900 drachmas
24    Asklas, fatherless, whose mother is Sarapias, likewise 800 drachmas
25    Pnepheros son of Theon, whose mother is Sietous, likewise 800 drachmas
26    Hermas son of Heron, whose mother is Berous, likewise 900 drachmas
27    Of Kerkesoucha Orous, likewise:
