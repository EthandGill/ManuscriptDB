[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving
2     and consubstantial Trinity, of the Father
3     and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.
4     In the reign of our most divine
5     and most serene and God-crowned masters, the Flavii
6     Heraclius and Heraclius
7     New Constantine, the
8     eternal Augusti, Imperators
9     and greatest benefactors,
10    in the twenty-third year, on Thoth
11    the tenth, of the seventh indiction.
12    To Aurelius Danielios, farmer,
13    son of the late Christodoros,
14    from the (city) of the Hermopolites, from Aurelius Iosephios
15    son of Paulos, his mother being
16    Martha, farmer, from the
17    same (city) of the Hermopolites. I acknowledge that, willingly
18    and of my own free will, I have leased
19    from you, for only one year,
20    that is the crops of the, God willing,
21    coming eighth indiction and only it,
22    the arouras belonging to you,
23    three of sowable land, the
24    sixty-four cords being fulfilled
25    by the just measuring-rope, each
26    aroura, situated in the farmstead
27    called Tbote in the plain
28    of the village of Thyneos and under
29    the protection of this
30    Hermopolite nome, together with the share
31    belonging to them of the cistern there and
32    the water-wheel and cattle-yard
33    and every right, for sowing
34    and laying-down of crops
35    of whatever I may choose, at a rent of grain,
36    fifteen artabas — total, grain 15 artabas —
37    which rent, new, good,
38    pure, sifted,
39    by your measure, the Athenaion, I shall pay and deliver
40    to you, into your house in Her[mopolis],
41    by my own animals and at my own expenses,
42    in the month Epeiph of the same
43    indiction. The lease is valid and secure,
44    and being formally questioned I gave my assent. Aurelius
45    Iosephios son [of Paulos] …
46    an…or… (subscriber for) Iosephios son of Paulos, farmer, from euph…
