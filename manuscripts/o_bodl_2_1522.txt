1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, produce of the 26th year of Commodus Antoninus
2     Caesar our Lord, Pauni 14, for the Agora, in the name of Thacsius
3     Kametius, wheat artaba one-quarter, it becomes wheat artaba [one-quarter], and in the name of Senpamontus Collouthus, wheat artaba 24, and in the name of [the] heirs
4     Of Petosirius Psensenurus
5     Wheat artaba 12. Horus.
