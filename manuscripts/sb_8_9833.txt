1     [Under the consulship of our lords the Emperors Dio][cletian VII and Maximian] VI, Augu[st]i.
2     [Aurelia Taapammon]... daughter of mother Tapausirius
3     [from a village]... [of the] lower toparchy [and] the nephew Theodore [son of] Romaeus [mother]... konos
4     [both] [through me] Pausiri[us s]on of Sarapion, mother Soerius
5     [from the same] [village] to Aurelius Heraklion also [Dionys]ius [who has] served as gymnasiarch, [pryta]nis, councillor of the splendid and most splendid

7     [for the] time from 16 Pharmouthi until 15 Pharm[outhi of the] present 15th year and 14th year and 7th year complete, and the said loom
8     [to have received] from you in sound condition, and nothing to you to claim [nor] to go on claiming about anything whatsoever altogether. [The receipt is valid], and being questioned I agreed.

11    [Const]antius and Maximian of the most illustrious
12    [Caes]ars, Pachon 7. Aurelius Theodore [son of] Romaeus and

15    [I] agreed. Aurelius Sarapammon wrote for them
