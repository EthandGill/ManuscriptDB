[TRANSLATION]
1     … distinguishing moreover I say not even in the Agathinus … …
2     … [to] say, the opposite moreover, as I said, very many and of which …
3     … with him is. Year 6 of Emperor Caesar Marcus
4     [Aurelius A]ntoninus Augustus, and of Emperor Caesar Lucius
5     [Aurelius] Verus Augustus, Pauni 9. Diogenes have submitted. After the
6     [reading] Agathos Daimon steward of Agathinus
7     answered; [I] did not establish case to speak, but only [a distinction] to communicate. I demand moreover [that] you learn from the servant
8     [of Diophant]us how the maiden went out. The strategos of Diophantus
9     [servant] being present inquired; how did you see the maiden having gone out [when it was] not [possible for her to have gone out]? [She was wearing] a subrica pallium purple-[colored] and a small ring with stone. Claudius Demetrius also called
10    [Hermias strat]egus said [that] the things stated be recorded.
11    [so-and-so have] submitted. [May you] fare well.
