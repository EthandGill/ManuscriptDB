[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius, who has taken [t]he sixth of the
2     fruit-lands of Tentyrite for the 36th year Thermouthi
3     and Pachon, Psennesias. I have granted
4     to you to harvest and dispose [of] the produce of the date-palm area belonging to you, one [place], you
5     recording onto the royal bank the king's humanitarian bronze being deducted
6     against silver [at the rate of] drachmas four hundred. [They] amount to 400.
7     Year 36, Pauni 22.
8     Antipatros the counter-scribe I have endorsed.
9     Year 36, Pauni 22.
