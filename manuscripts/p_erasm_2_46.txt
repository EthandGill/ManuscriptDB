[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 30, Epeiph 12, of tributary wheat artabas
2     thousand eight-hundred five, there are 1805.
3     Year 30, Epeiph 12. Tothoes confesses
4     ship-owner of the Dionysius's light merchant vessel [240]
5     to have loaded on the [vessel] at the New
6     harbor so to Alexandria
7     to the royal [treasury] from the produce
8     of the same year from Dionysius the one collecting grain for the Oxyrhynchus workshop
9     [w]heat tributary artabas thousand
10    eight-hundred five, there are 1805
11    by dochic measure which has been agreed
12    [toward the bronze [standard] and by [rod] just]
13    [and nothing do I have to complain of to you.]
