1     Marcus Annius Nemonianus
2     tax-collector of silver-taxes of Elephantine and Ammonius
3     Savior appointed debt-collector.
4     Senesendis Bieghios for the price of public date-palm
5     of produce year 30 by name… [name]
6     Onnophres 4 drachmas and four-obols and by-name Senpsansnutos Peteureus
7     4 drachmas and four-obols and by-name Tekosis Petorzmethios 4 drachmas, total 13 drachmas [and two-obols].

9     Antoninus Caesar the lord, Phamenoth 10.
