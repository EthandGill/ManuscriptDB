[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy also called Alexander, the god Philometor, and Berenike [his sister, goddess Philadelphos,]
2     in the sixteenth year, when the priests and priestesses and the kanephoros who are in office [are those in office, in the month Pharmouthi] …
3     in Poinkoris of the lower toparchy of the Libyan district of the Diopolite nome, [before] … [the agent of] …
4     the agoranomos. Horos son of Hermias, about twelve years old, sold, with [his kinswoman acting as joint-seller, Hor]os'
5     [Arsi]noe daughter of Belleus, about 22 years old, of middle height, honey-coloured, round-fac[ed, straight-nosed, with a scar on the forehead]
6     on the right, with her guardian Ptolemaios son of Psenmenchis, about 35 years old, of middle [height,] … [all]
7     of those from the Pathyrite nome — the land belonging to the father of Horos, of … arouras [in the upper]
8     plain of Krokodilonpolis, from one seal-plot registered to Ps[en]m…
9     of the five-aroura plots: to the south the land of Petesouchos son of Hermias, to the north the land of Belleus and of [his brothers(?), to the east]
10    the rest of the seal-plot, to the west the land of Horos son of Nechouthes, the buyer; likewi[s]e also [from the vacant lots belonging]
11    to them, in the part on the west of the village of Mouchis, of the seven-aroura plots, [solid] cub[its by the measure]
12    royal, three hundred, of which the neighbours are: to the south the house of Pkois, which … holds [, to the north]
13    the rest of the lots, to the east the royal street, to the west the rest of the lots, or whoever [the neighbours may be on all sides].
