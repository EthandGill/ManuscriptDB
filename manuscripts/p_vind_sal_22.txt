r.1    τῷ δεσπότῃ μου ὡς ἀληθῶς διὰ πάντα θαυμασιωτάτῳ κ[αὶ] ἐναρέτῳ

[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord as truly through everything most marvelous [and] virtuous
2     To Origen of Panis
3     The paper of Pakome which you have, your excellency, deign [to]
4     to me send [it] through Apollonius the peace-officer
5     me responding to all the matters having been set [forth]
6     [to] the paper. For your perfection knows,
7     and even if I do not write, the foolishness of the man. To fare well
8     you I pray [for] many times.
9     To my lord [as trul]y through everything [most marvelous and virtuous] to Origen
