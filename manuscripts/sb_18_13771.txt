[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of] God. Abdurrahman [son] of Abiaouf and Abdurrahman [son] of Szou … you [the]
2     [from village] Apion of the pagarchy of Herakleopolite; you have laid [a tax demand] upon us
3     [through] … and associates for a portion of gold public [taxes] of indiction 4 possessing coins 108 carats 19
4     [numbered coins 118 sixth] it comes to possessing coins one hundred eight carats nineteen
5     [it comes to numbered coins one hundred eighte]en sixth only. In the month Pharmouthi 22 of the fifth indiction. Daniel notary
6     … it comes to Pharmouthi 22 of indiction 5 for indiction 4 through coins 108 carats 19 numbered coins 118 sixth
7     Stephanos [son] of Helios concerning artabas 100.
