[TRANSLATION]
1     To Stotoetis and Pakysis, both sons of Apynchis son of
2     Stotoetis,
3     from Hera[klei]des son of Horion also called Papeirios,
4     son of Hera[kl]eides, registered in the quarter of Pha…os, a Persian
5     of the epigone. I wish to lease from you,
6     for the present thirteenth year only
7     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, from those (lands) which you also
8     happen to hold in lease from the people of the village of
9     Soknopaiou Nesos, seven arouras of public land,
10    or however many there may be, in one parcel, in the area of Bakchias, at a total rent
11    of the whole, unsown, of wheat by the six-choinix measure
12    of fourteen artabas, apart from which I shall also perform all the agricultural works as are fitting
13    sowing from my own (seed), five arouras with wheat
14    and the rem[aining] two ar[our]as on the south with fallow-recovery crops; and the fourteen art[abas]
15    of wheat as rent I shall deliver to you at the threshing-floor of the
16    fields, the donkey-driver's freight up to Hiera being at my charge, all the rents on their behalf
17    and (those) of every kind being
18    at your charge, you (the parties) around Stotoetis, if it seem good to lease. The neighbors of the leased
19    seven arouras (are): on the south, that which Ptollas holds in lease,
20    son of Sataboo[s]; on the north, the sown fields of Aleinos;
21    on the west, the plain road; on the east, a canal.
22    Herakleides, about 35 years old, a scar on the left eyebrow.
23    Year 13 of the Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
24    Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, (month of) Phaophi 22.
