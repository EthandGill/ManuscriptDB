[TRANSLATION]
1     Theon to Sambas the oil-worker: two and a half
2     xesta of oil provide to the stone-carriers θ
3     and [carpenter] one for four days going
4     to the Skeleton to take up the doors
5     and to fill the stones, oil xesta two and a half only
6     Of the epagomenes, 2nd, 14th indiction. I recorded oil xesta two and a half only.
7     of the epagomenes, 14th indiction.
