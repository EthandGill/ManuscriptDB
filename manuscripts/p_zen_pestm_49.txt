1     Eukles to Apollonios greetings. I have sent you the
2     letters that were written both to Orsikleides
3     the chief grain-measurer about the grain measurement which must
4     be transacted by him according to the [letter] from Hermaphilos
5     sent to him. Then also the
6     [letters] to Chrysippos, [namely] the one about the talent
7     how he might remove [it] from the order that was written
8     to Python and correct [it], and make a symbol/token instead of [one] to me to
9     those being withdrawn from exemption and the one about
10    grain measurement so that he might remind Orsikleides.
11    So having carefully delivered [them] to them, take care
12    to bring into administration [matters] about what we have written and concerning
13    Orsikleides, receive a letter to Nikon and from
14    Nikon to Herakleides and to us briefly
15    send [it], and remind Chrysippos how he should arrange
16    for Python to send word to Skymmos to transact [business] so that
17    the works may not be under supervision. And take along for this
18    also Lykophron. And we have also assigned to Proitos
19    to seize together some of those owing [money] and the existing [property]
20    to take as security. Having taken along therefore Ktesikles and of
21    those with Lykophron, [of] the armed men, some, go through together with him
22    and whoever he might show to you, hand [it] over to them and the
23    existing [property] place [it] alongside with Lykophron. And write
24    to us, however you may administer [things].
25    Farewell, year 4, Phamenoth 10.
26    And about the 19 diobol [matters]
27    which you write [that] I need to receive from the income of the bath of the [place] through Pathiophios
28    lease, send word to me from where I should receive [it]; for
29    the guarantee through the accounts you have raised up.
30    To Apollonios
31    From Eukles.
