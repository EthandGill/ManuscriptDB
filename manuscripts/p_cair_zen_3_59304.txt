[TRANSLATION]
1     Protarchos to Zenon greetings. In view of the fact that you promised to send us
2     the ... [illegible] ...
3     [for] going up we did not send; so it has happened, since [it] no longer exists in the
4     nome, going about we are being supplied from other nomes, so that the boiling not fail. Still therefore even now you will do well, if you have not sent for it, now at least
5     send and to us a letter write ...
6     ... [have] decided, in order that we might be concerned and not the linen-boilers be idle not having natron for the boiling.
7     Farewell. Year 36, Epeiphi 11.
8     To Zenon.
