1     Account of millet:
2     I received from Theogeitus the miller, 164 [and] 1/2 artabae
3     and through Eutyches from Eleusis 31 [and] 1/5 artabae …
4     [these] amount in total to 195 [and] 1/2 [and] 1/5 artabae, as the measure
5     [is] 9 artabae; measurers 21 [and] 1/2 [and] 1/4,
6     of which I delivered in the 1st transport through Melan[os] the donkey-driver, measurers 5
7     2nd transport through Didymus, measurers 8
8     3rd transport through [A]lexand[e]r the donkey-driver, measurers 4
9     4th transport [through] the same likewise, measurers 2 [and] 1/4
10    5th transport th[rough th]e same likewise, measurers 2 [and] 1/2 [and] 1/4
11    [these] amount to measurers 22, of which I delivered to Pan [one] ceramic [vessel].
