[TRANSLATION]
1     The things written to me by your magnificent brotherhood I have received … [to be deli]vered to the canal of Laton, I have written …
2     possible to concede the whole, even (if only) the half; but if [not] …
3     I know the narrowness and shortage of men of your pag[archy] …
4     he may permit; and our master the all-praised [amir] …
5     to do everything for the constitution of the public (works) … [to the]
6     aforesaid all-praised man …
7     … [whether for the cleaning-out or for the digging-up]
8     of the remainder, so that with haste, before the p[ublic (works), thi]s work may be completed, [and each may withdraw]
9     to his own labor. For the rest, do not trouble [yourselves to learn] exactly his intention, that one must not over[turn]
10    a word coming forth from his mouth con[cerning the constitut]ion of the public (works). For the rest, if you wish to learn
11    your portion, then gather many me[n and complet]e this, and let each withdraw to his own (affairs).
12    See, I told Menasios this: prep[are ] … [and] I marvel how until now there has not arrived
13    your payment. For the rest, if not yet … [im]mediately send it and begin on the
14    second (day) in the month Mecheir 7, in the 11th indiction.
15    [To my brother distinguished in all things and most honored,] Papas, by God's (grace) pagarch of Apollonos Ano, Helias notarius.
