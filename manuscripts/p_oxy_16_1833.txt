[TRANSLATION]
1     From
2     … [and even if] I do not write to your disposition, [you] do not fail to know that such as are [being] sufficient and well-off
3     [toward you], but that your wonderfulness does not bring the wine with itself, and this is clear.
4     Let your disposition therefore learn the quantity of the wine of the whole estate and
5     thus let [it] mark and let [it] remain with the vineyard-workers until we know
6     of whom the land happens to be. For neither do I think those men to be so reckless as even
7     to [disclose] land or even estate belonging to the count.
8     … [give] to my lord, most wonderful brother
9     … to Heraclius, notary, from John.
