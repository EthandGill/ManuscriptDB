[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Ap[oll]onios …
2     of the great Sarapis and however he is styled.
3     from the Aurelii Pto[l]as son of Ision …
4     Sarapammon, brother of ou… ,
5     and Herieus son of Onesimos, whose mother is Son… ,
6     the three, through Aurelius Chairemon … amysthas, from the village of Ptolemais Hor[mou. We wish]
7     to lease from you by mutual guarantee, from
8     the (lands) belonging (to you) near the same village of Pol[y]deukia,
9     10 arouras lying (there), or however many they may be, for two years from the present 10th year, at a rent each year, without seed (supplied), of twenty-five artabas of wheat, not subject to deduction
10    and free of risk; and it shall not be permitted to us within
11    the (lease-)time to abandon the lease in any way whatsoever, but rather we shall also of necessity
12    perform each year all the works, as many as …
13    and at the due seasons, causing no damage; and the yearly rent
14    we shall pay in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor, pure,
15    unadulterated, free of barley, by the four-choinix running-measure of the (landlord) of good standing, all the public dues
16    apart from transport-charges being upon the (landlord) of good standing, but the transport-charges being upon us the lessees; and after the (lease-)time I shall hand over the arouras,
17    the half part from fallow(-ground) and the
18    remainder from stubble, and clear of rush, reed, dog's-tooth grass, and all (weeds), if it
19    seem good to lease …
20    year. Of Imperator Caesar Mar[cu]s [A]urelius [Seve]rus
21    Alexander Pious Fortunate Augustus, Ph[a]o[ph]i 11.
