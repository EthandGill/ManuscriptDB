[TRANSLATION]
1     To Artemidoros, archidikastes and in charge of the supervision of the chrematistai and of the other courts,
2     from Eirene daughter of Tryphon, with her guardian
3     Dionysios son of Dionysios, and from Gnaeus Octavius Damas. Concerning the security to be brought forward that has been established, Gnaeus
4     [Octa]vius Damas agrees that he has received from
5     Eirene, from hand to hand out of the house, the maintenance-payments for the nursing by him of Eirene's little slave-body whose name is Renata, and that neither Gnaeus himself
6     Octavius Damas nor anyone else on his behalf shall proceed against Eirene, either concerning maintenance-payments or anything
7     else [written] or unwritten, but neither shall (anyone) proceed against the slave-body itself
8     Renata, but the one who does proceed (against her),
9     him Gnaeus himself
10    [Octa]vius [Damas] shall drive off from
11    [Eirene at his] own expense; or, apart from the agreed terms being valid, he shall further pay in addition both what he received of the maintenance-capital with the half as much again, at once, and as a fine other drachmas of silver
12    five hundred and the damages and expenses and the fixed
13    penalty, as if by legal judgment;
14    and Eirene has received from
15    Gnaeus the little slave-child
16    Renata, who is about three years
17    and six months old. We request.
18    Year 15 of Caesar, Tybi 6.
