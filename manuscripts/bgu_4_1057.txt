[TRANSLATION]
1   Sheet (kollema)
2   To Protarchos,
3   from Chairemon son of Isidoros and from Thermouthis daughter of Padallos,
4   Persian woman, with her guardian her son Philoxenos son of Philoxenos, and (from) the same
5   Philoxenos and [...] standing surety for the repayment, Heliodoros son of Kandanos, both being Persians
6   of the epigone.
7   Concerning the matters at issue: Thermouthis and Philoxenos agree
8   to have from [Ch]airemon a loan, by hand from the house, of silver,
9   Ptolemaic, one hundred drachmas, at interest of two drachmas per month each,
10   which they will also repay with the interest within thirty days from the four-
11   teenth of the present month Pharmouthi of the seventeenth year
12   of Caesar, without any deferral, or they shall be at once subject to seizure and
13   be held until they pay the loan with half as much again, and the interest plainly,
14   and for the time overrun the equal (interest), the execution being made for Chairemon
15   from those liable, Thermouthis and Philoxenos, and from the surety Heliodoros
16   and from any one and from whichever he chooses and from all their possessions, just as by a court judgment,
17   (the documents) being void, and of all pledges whatsoever they may bring forward, and of all protection.
18   Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 14, z(?)( )
19   To Protarchos,
20   from Heliodoros son of Kandanos and from Thermouthis daughter of Kandanos, Per-
21   sian woman, with her guardian her son Philoxenos, Persian of the epigone, and
22   the same Philoxenos. Concerning the matters at issue: Thermouthis and Philoxenos agree to pro-
23   vide Heliodoros undisturbed and untroubled concerning those for whom he stands surety,
24   (namely) themselves, borrowing from Chaire(mon) son of Isidoros according to the agreement completed from this
25   having been comple-
26   ted through the same tribunal, of silver (drachmas) one hundred, of interest
27   at two drach[mas],
28   for thirty days from the present month Pharm(outhi) of the 17th year of Caesar, and being under com-
29   pulsion both to pay to Chaire(mon) the (drachmas) of silver and
30   the interest on the aforesaid
31   day, so that Heliodoros be released; and if they transgress anything, that they pay
32   them,
33   whatever may be exacted from or Heliodoros be damaged of the loan, [...] of silver (drachmas(?)) one hundred
34   and of interest, the execution being made for Heliodoros from both, mutually liable,
35   for repayment, and from any one and from whichever he chooses and from their possessions, just as
36   by a court judgment, (the documents) being void, and
37   of all pledges whatsoever they may bring forward, of all protection.
38   Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 14
