[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 12 of Emperor Caesar
2     Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus
3     Augustus Pious, Pauni 5 toward
4     reckoning of Pachon. Antonius wrote through
5     Agathopos to the grain-tax collectors of Philadelphia for the proper duties
6     for security-deposit account [so] that it might hold toward
7     purchase of wheat produce of the eleventh year [drachmas]
8     two hundred, [they] amount to drachmas 200.
