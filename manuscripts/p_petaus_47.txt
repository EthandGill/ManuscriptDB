[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonius, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2     from Petaus, village-scribe of Ptolemais Hormou and other villages.
3     For the provision of supplies for the visit
4     of the most illustrious governor Longaeus Rufus, so that
5     there may be an abundance for those wishing to buy, according to the
6     regulations, I give the persons listed below, being
7     well-off and fit. They are:
8     For bread:
9     Dioscorus son of Ischyrion grandson of Dioscorus, having means of 1,000 drachmas.
10    Ischyrion son of Ammonius grandson of Heuremon, his mother being
11    Heraclea, likewise 900 drachmas.
12    Maron son of Marinus grandson of Maron, likewise 1,000 drachmas.
13    Deios son of Neseus grandson of Ischyrion, likewise 900 drachmas.
14    For wine-sellers:
15    Pantonymus son of Ischyrion grandson of Pantonymus, likewise 1,000 drachmas.
16    Aphrodeisios son of Aphrodeisios grandson of Aphrodeisios, likewise 800 drachmas.
17    Ischyrion son of Heraklas grandson of Ischyrion, likewise 1,000 drachmas.
18    And for oil:
19    Euangelos son of Heron grandson of Euangelos, likewise … drachmas.
20    Apynchis son of Peteeus grandson of Ischyrion, likewise … drachmas.
21    Zosimus, and as he is styled, landholding in …, likewise … drachmas.
22    And for the guest-provisions:
23    Ischyrion son of Neseus grandson of Orsenouphis, likewise … drachmas.
24    Ischyrion son of Orsenouphis grandson of Philammon, likewise … drachmas.
25    And for the barley:
26    Ischyrion son of Athas grandson of Maron, likewise 600 drachmas.
27    Pnepheros son of Neseus, his mother being Demos, likewise … drachmas.
28    Maron son of Heron grandson of Maron, likewise … drachmas.
29    Sokras son of Petepnous, likewise 500 drachmas.
30    Pamounis son of Kennas, likewise 500 drachmas.
31    Ptollis son of Pathynis grandson of Ptollis, likewise 600 drachmas.
32    Paaus son of Paaus grandson of Theabennis, his mother being Thasos, likewise 600 drachmas.
33    Paaus son of Ptollas grandson of P…, his mother being Tamarreis, likewise 600 drachmas.
34    And for timber:
35    Ischyrion son of Hierakion grandson of Phanias, likewise 600 drachmas.
36    Peebaus son of Hatres grandson of Onnophris, likewise 500 drachmas.
37    And for vegetables and the other supplies:
38    Akiaris son of Syros, likewise 600 drachmas.
39    Karos son of Papontos, likewise 500 drachmas.
40    Siasis surnamed Silbas, likewise 500 drachmas.
41    Aphrodas son of Heraklas, likewise 600 drachmas.
42    Onnophris son of Akous, likewise 500 drachmas.
43    Sarapion son of Ischyrion surnamed Pithiaithis, likewise 600 drachmas.
44    And for the registration of those in the praetorium:
45    Sarapion son of Ischyrion, likewise 600 drachmas.
46    Harpalos son of Harpaesion, likewise 500 drachmas.
47    Pnepheros son of Theon, likewise 600 drachmas.
48    And for fishing-boats:
49    Ischeis son of Ischeis, likewise 500 drachmas.
50    Phaseis son of Neseus, likewise 600 drachmas.
51    Hatres son of Neseus, likewise 700 drachmas.
52    Sarapion son of Petenouphis, likewise 600 drachmas.
53    Heracles son of Paaipis, likewise 500 drachmas.
54    I, Petaus, have submitted it.
55    Year 25 of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
56    Antoninus Augustus, Tybi 30.
