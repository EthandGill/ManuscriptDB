2     … [the curator(?) logistes of the Oxyrhynchite] through Eutolmios his deputy, to Sotas the attendant. Of the books given to me by the registered party, a report having been inserted,
3     [together with the subscription which I obtained from] my lord the most distinguished prefect of Egypt Clodius Culcianus, a copy is sent to you, that you may make manifest what she requested. Signed by me.
4     [Year 20 of our lords Diocletian and] Maximian Augusti and 12 of our lords Constantius and Maximian the most noble Caesars, Tybi 17.
5     … to the logistes of the illustrious and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Aurelius Eutolmios, exegetes, councillor of the same city, his deputy,
6     [from Septimius Aristion, exegetes, councillor of the sam]e city. A suit concerning the inheritance of Asklatarion, daughter of my brother Amyntianos, lay before the majesty of my lord the most distinguished prefect of Egypt Clodius
7     [Culcianus] … in accordance with the proceedings drawn up before his excellency from the cross-examination against Thaesis, the former wife of my aforesaid brother, but concerning
8     … [he] ordered us to come before the appointed auditors, namely the then officer on duty, the beneficiarius Marcellinus, and Berenikianos and Dioscourides, both former magistrates,
9     [councillors of the same city] … [of the things] expended by me, and this matter having been in no way concluded, but while I was offering myself ready to hand over the possession, the auditing being done together,
10    … she carried off [the produce], reckoning my absence a windfall, while I was in attendance on my lord the most distinguished catholicus Valerius Euethios; and these things did not happen without witnesses,
11    … [of the rep]ort, and his providence in all things, through the subscription by which he assented to my appending [these things], and that I might not seem to misinterpret, having appended a copy I submit (it) to you, requesting … that the prefectorial [decision] be carried to completion, and that Thaesis come to the auditing and lawfully receive [the possession] and act according to what has been decided; thus I wish [in accordance with what has been ordained to come] before the immaculate prefectorial [tribunal] on account of the inheritance, I not being diminished in the other rights of every kind that I [have], concerning which an account will be established for me [before the] greatness of the prefecture.
12    [Farewell. Year 20 and year 12 of our lords Diocletian and] Maximian Augusti and of Co[nstantius and] Maximian the most noble Caesars, Tybi … . And the copy is as follows.
13    [To Clodius Culcianus the most distinguished] prefect of Egypt [from] Septimius Aristion, exegetes, councillor of t[he illustrious and] most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites. A proceeding took place before me, before you my lord,

17    [I subjoined] … . When we had come to the spot, before the billeting-officer I called upon her in writing to do both things, namely both to make the audit and to receive the possession, but I know not what she thought
18    [she was unwilling to receive the possession nor to make the audit]ing. Upon these things, further, what also happened while the matter remained pending, when my lord your brother the most distinguished catholicus Valerius Euethios was present on the spot,
19    … [of the pub]lic ships, that I was shut up together with others on the spot from then until now. Having now myself enjoyed the good fortune of the vicennalia of our masters the Emperors Diocletian and Maximian
20    [of the largesse, I necessarily make my recourse to] your majesty and request [you], most distinguished prefect, that according to what was previously decided by you concerning the audit, as I said before, having taken place, the possession be handed over to her
