[TRANSLATION]
1     [Jul]ius Apollinaris to Marcus and Apollinarius
2     [to the] [both of you] very many greetings; first
3     [before all] [we wish] you to be well
4     [you received] from Antonius the cavalry soldier
5     … [and] from Heracleides the con[ductor] a package of salt; if we have any salt we sent [it]
6     to you the half; greet all the fellow-soldiers. Farewell.
