[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a house-by-house census of Antinoopolis. To Nikostratos,
2     also called Demaliōn, monarchs of Antinoo and Sarapion
3     of Isidoras son of Sarapammon of Antinoo and Sarapammon
4     of Aspidas son of Hermias Parrhasiōs and Ammonius of Origen... ...
5     of the 3 tribes of Os[iranti]noeids chosen for the house-by-house census,
6     from Valerius also called Philantinoos, Philantinoos also called Nilammon
7     Herakleidus Matidaeus also called Kalliteknius. I register, as
8     ordered by Aurelius [O]uerianus the most brilliant hegemon, for the
9     [year] 28 of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar of
10    the lord, [in the] house-by-house census for the property to me paternal fourth part of
11    a house and courtyard [and a]ula in the first letter brick seven southerly, and I am for
12    the present [year] of 24 years; and my wife Didymē of 21 years daughter of Lysimachos
13    also called Didymus Herakle[i]d[u]s [M]atidaeus also called Kal[litecn]eius, [and] slave
14    body come to me from inheritance of the
15    deceased my [m]other Kroniēnē also called Heracleias, of Antinoeid
16    Diodora of 42 years, [and] her descendant Kopria of 15 years, and freedman of
17    my father Sarapion of 38 years; and to my wife
18    Didymē Lysimachos belongs a fourth part of the aforesaid house
19    in the first letter brick seven souther[ly]. Year 28 of Aurelius
20    Commodus Antoninus Caesar of the lord, Mesore 19.
