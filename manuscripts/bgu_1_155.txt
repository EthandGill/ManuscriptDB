[TRANSLATION]
1     [Of] Soerus of-Nile with the lord
2     the according-to-mother uncle Satorneilus Deida to Syrus of-Heron farmer
3     Greetings. I-have-received from you on account Soherus Stotoutes for which he-owes me [the] principal and the interest for the sixteenth year
4     of Antoninus Caesar the lord.
5     I-acknowledge of-the same sixteenth
6     year the interest not [being] diminished
7     of-the principal and the accruing interest and of-the mortgage and the
8     hand-to-hand interest and the accruing. Year sixteenth of Antoninus
9     Caesar the lord, Mesore 7.
