[TRANSLATION]
1     Nemesianus to Severus, greetings.
2     Of the [items] sold by you from harvest of year 5 of the estate
3     Libyan wine-vat, 4 [items]; jars, 25; and double jars, 6 — transfer the price to Sarapias
4     the administrator of the property: of the jars, 25, at the rate of one [each]
5     sixteen drachmas; of the double jars, at the rate of one [each]
6     twenty-four drachmas. Farewell, and transfer the
7     price as set forth. Year 7, Pachon 30.
