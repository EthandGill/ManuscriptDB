[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a memorandum. Year 11 of Autokrator Caesar Nerva
2     Trajan Augustus German Dacian, Mecheir 15. Ptolemaeus
3     village-secretary of Nile city and Soknopaiou Nesos, having presented
4     Pekysis of Pekysis and Tryphon of Panephremis and those with him from
5     Soknopaiou Nesos, priests of the 5th phyle. A certain Harpagathes has filed a complaint against
6     these concerning arson and [he] became absent. The present [ones] say that man
7     rather did this; for indeed others, as struck by him, [filed] a report
8     gave. The strategus: Concerning the arson, I ask [when] [it] happened and
9     what [it] accomplished. Ptolemaeus: Nothing serious happened; for private [fixtures]
10    alone were burned, and the [ones] accused Hermaios [the also] … of Achaios. The strategus:
11    [whether] did [they] wrong the aforementioned, or [did you present?] Ptolemaeus: No. The strategus: What do you suspect [with this] [was] done[?] Ptolemaeus: [Which has] become absent.
12    [The strategus]: Concerning what you [know], you yourselves [speak to] me, and likewise also the
13    [public officials] and the chief [of the roads, concerning] what they know, [let them speak]. Ptolemaeus:
14    Grant me [until the] twentieth so that after [this I may] speak. Claud[ius]
15    Asclepiades the strategus: You have five days in [which also this] [one] to [be] sought
16    make. I read [this].
