[TRANSLATION]
1     What is exigible, man by man,
2     of grain-dues, through the public
3     cultivators of the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, of the shore-land, 93 arouras,
4     of dirty wheat, 217 and a half and a quarter artabas, of which each item:
5     Abous son of Satabous, 5 arouras, of wheat 12 and a half artabas
6     Apynchis son of Apynchis, 2 and a half arouras, of wheat 6 and a quarter artabas
7     Pabous son of Pakysis, 1 and a half arouras, of wheat 3 and a half and a quarter artabas
8     Abous son of Toualēs, 2 arouras, of wheat 5 artabas
9     Herieus son of Apynchis, 3 arouras, of wheat 7 and a half artabas
10    Ekysis son of Stotoetis, 3 arouras, of wheat 7 and a half artabas
11    Melas son of Asmalēs, 2 arouras, of wheat 5 artabas
12    Stotoetis son of Pabous, 7 arouras, of wheat 17 and a half artabas
13    Orsenoupheis son of Stotoetis, 7 arouras, of wheat 17 and a half artabas
14    Paboukas son of Pabous, 8 arouras, of wheat 20 artabas
15    Paeis son of Pasoknopaios, 3 arouras, of wheat 7 and a half artabas
16    Paemeis son of Sempronios, 2 and a half arouras, of wheat 6 and a quarter artabas
17    Posis son of Mataeis, 2 arouras, of wheat 5 artabas
18    Stotoetis son of Herieus, 9 arouras, of wheat 22 and a half artabas
19    Satabous son of Satabous, 2 arouras, of wheat 5 artabas
20    Sotas son of Pareous, 2 arouras, of wheat 5 artabas
21    Horion, his mother being Thasias, 5 arouras, of wheat 12 and a half artabas
22    Damas son of Damas, 4 arouras, of wheat 10 artabas
23    and through those from Philopator:
24    Petesouchos son of Sambas and Aiōn
25    son of Syros and the rest, 9 arouras, of wheat 22 and a half artabas
26    Year 23, Pharmouthi
