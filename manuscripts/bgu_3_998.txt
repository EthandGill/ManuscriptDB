[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy also surnamed Alexander, god Philometor, year 14,
2     the priests and priestesses and kanephoros being those who are in office, in the month of Choiak, the 2nd, in Pathyris,
3     under Hermias, the agent of Paniskos, agoranomos. Psenmegches, son of Panechates, a Persian
4     [of the] epigone, aged about 55, of middle height, honey-skinned, with straight hair, somewhat bald, long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on his
5     [left] cheek, limping on his right foot — with the joint consent of his son Harpaesis,
6     [son of] Psenmegches, a Persian, aged about 30, of middle height, honey-skinned, somewhat stooping, long-faced, straight-nosed, without distinguishing mark —
7     sold [the] existing house, built and vaulted and furnished with a door, and
8     [the] adjoining [courtyard] which is in the northern part of Pathyris, whose neighbors are: on the south the house of Nechoutes
9     [son of] Panechates, on the north the house of Kephalon, on the east a street, on the west the house of Portis, son of Petesouchos,
10    [whose] between (them) is a street, or whoever the neighbors may be on all sides. Peteesis, son of Onoes, a Persian, aged about 40,
11    [of middle height], honey-skinned, somewhat stooping, with a scar on his forehead, bought (it for) four talents of bronze. The seller-in-advance and
12    [guaran]tor of all the terms of this purchase is Psenmegches the seller, whom
13    [Petee]sis the buyer accepted.
14    Hermias, the agent of Paniskos, I have transacted (this).
15    Year 14, Choiak 2, in Pathyris, under Hermias, the agent of Paniskos, agoranomos.
16    Psenmegches, son of Panechates, a Persian of the epigone, acknowledges that he withdraws from
17    the house that has been sold, built and furnished with a door, and from the adjoining
18    courtyard which is in the northern part of Pathyris, whose neighbors are: on the south the house
19    of Nechoutes, son of Panechates, on the north the house of Kephalon, on the east a street, on the west the house
20    of Portis, son of Petesouchos, whose between (them) is a street, or whoever the neighbors may be — which sale he made
21    to Peteesis at the same record-office for four talents of bronze; and that neither Psenmegches himself nor any other on his behalf shall proceed against
22    Peteesis nor against any other on his (Peteesis') behalf; and if he does, the proceeding of the one who proceeds shall be invalid, and the one who proceeds shall in addition pay forthwith as penalty
23    eight talents of bronze and to the kings, as sacred (fine), one hundred and sixty drachmas of coined silver,
24    and none the less it shall be compulsory for him to act according to [the] aforesaid.
25    And let this acknowledgment be valid everywhere it may be produced.
26    Hermias, the agent of Paniskos, I have transacted (this).
