[TRANSLATION]
1     Provided [through] the Saracen from the village of Phanu
2     from the public [revenues] of my arouras, crops,
3     of the first indiction: gold coin one and wheat
4     artabas three; become gold coin 1 and wheat artabas 3 only.
5     Written month Choiak 18, first indiction, on behalf of the same first indiction.
6     Through me, Lord Patrick.
