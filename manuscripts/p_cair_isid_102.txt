[TRANSLATION]
1     … I wish to lease
2     [from you the property belonging to you] near the village of Kerke[soucha] … of Karanis, of private
3     arable [land, five arouras], and near the village of
4     [Pto]lemais [Nea], of private arable land
5     two [arouras], making [in] all of private
6     arable land [7] arouras, [or] however many they may be, for a period
7     of three [years] from the sowing of the present year,
8     without rent, on condition that I the lessee am answerable for all the dues demanded for the
9     arouras, all the public charges, that is the grain-measure and the annonae and all the impositions
10    and the divine assessment from
11    the 20th year and the 19th year and the 12th year, for the aforesaid three-year period, free of deduction and free of risk, and
12    it shall not be permitted to me within the period to abandon
13    the lease in any manner whatsoever, but I shall also of necessity perform all the yearly works, as many as are fitting,
14    [at my own] expense at the proper seasons, doing no damage,
15    [all the public charges], as aforesaid, [both the grain-measure] and the ann[onae and all] the impositions [and] the divine assess[ment being upon me the lessee] …
