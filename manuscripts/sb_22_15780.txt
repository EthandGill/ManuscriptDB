[TRANSLATION]
1     [Of the Bithynians] [Ision's] sheep and two goats
2     [To Aelius] Sarapion, strategos of Arsinoe of Heracleides division
3     [And to Sarapion], the royal scribe of the same division
4     [From] … [Horion] of Heron, from the district of the Bithynians
5     [Of Ision.] I register for the present 8th year of Antoninus
6     [Caesar] the lord, which I also registered in the past 7th year
7     [In the] same district pastoral sheep …[mixed] twenty … goats two
8     [Which I also will] assign to whichever I choose
9     [Of the] nome villages pastures watering places
