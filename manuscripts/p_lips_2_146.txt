1     To Vibius Alex[ander], the most excellent epistrategos
2     from Ant[onius D]omnus, also called Philanti[nus]
3     of Antonio[us Romana]nus Traianaeus, also [called Strat]aeus
4     of Antinoopolis. [I would not] have been brought forth into this matter, O greatest [prefect], being [moderate] and non-activist
5     a man, if not [the outrage] the greatest
6     [I had suffered] by Horion the [co]mogramm[a]teus
7     of Philadelph[ia] of the Herakleiden divis[ion]
8     of Arsinoe. [For which] reason I [report] he pushed [himself] against [what was forbidden] into the village secretariat, [neither] having assessed grain nor
9     [having collected] taxes, being utterly destitute.
10    For which reason, also before I have not ceased petitioning, and now I request, if [it seems good] to your fortune,
11    to hear me [against] him, so that I might obtain
12    justice [against wrongdoers] from you, so that I might be by [you]
13    in all respects helped. Fare well.
14    Antonius Domnus, I have presented [this].
