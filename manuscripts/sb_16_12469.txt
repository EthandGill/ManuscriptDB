1     Six
2     In the year fifth of the Caesar's rule, son of god, month of Dystrus, new moon, Tybi, new moon, in the Soknopaiou Nese of the
3     Herakleide division. [Acknowledges] Papus, also called Papais, [son] of Melanos, also called Pekysios, Persian of the conquered race, [that he] has leased from Anteros, Gaius Pompeiios Poros, a bull
4     one [reddish], to which the name is Taurus, from Hathyr of the fifth
5     year of Caesar, until Mesore the 30th, revenue of the whole wheat artabas ten [having been] established into Nile
6     city by measure, running-measure, wheat [new] clean, from all and unmixed. And after the time [he] will hand over
7     Papus the bull healthy and unharmed; if [he] should not hand over,
8     [he] will pay immediately from [his] own the of the bull
9     price in silver, of official coinage, drachmas
10    one hundred eighty seven, of the execution being
11    to the Anteros and to those from him from both the
12    [one] having leased, and from [the] all [of] these [things] all, and as if from judgment. The contract
13    is binding. Witnesses:
14    In the year five of Caesar, Tybi, the 1st. [It] has fallen into registration through
15    Ptolemaios, the one at the office Soknopaiou Nese.
16    Papus, also called Papais, [son] of Melanos, also called Pekysios, Persian
17    of the conquered race, [that I] have leased from Anteros, Gaius Pompeiios Poros, a bull one reddish, to which the name is Taurus, from Hathyr

19    same fifth year, revenue of the whole [by] measure, [running-measure,]

22    [if I] should not hand over, [I will] pay from [my] own silver
