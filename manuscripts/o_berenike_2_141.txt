[TRANSLATION]
1     Albis quintan[arius] of the fourth year of the Autocrat[or]
2     of Caesar Vespasian Augustus
3     to Pakoîbi and Psenapâthei
4     both of Psenmîn[iôs]
5     greetings. I have from you the quintan [tax]
6     Pauni Epeiph [of] silver drachmas thirty-two, amounts to drachmas thirty-two.
7     Albis I have drachmas [thirty-two].
