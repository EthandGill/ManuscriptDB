[TRANSLATION]
1     Nechthosiris to Leon his
2     brother, greeting. Fare you well, and
3     Apollonios the brother, and Epiodoros
4     and all those with you. And I too am
5     well myself. Though I have written you rather many
6     letters and nothing has been
7     communicated to me from you, being the more anxious
8     because of not having heard, even up to now,
9     the things concerning you, I kept beseeching the god often.
10    But when Protolaos reported to us the
11    things concerning you, I rejoiced greatly. Since then I have sent to Dionysios our (agent) to send me a cloak an[d] a tunic from Thoy[th]
12    he has neither sent (them), nor has he sent to the boys even a little gr[ai]n up to now
13    for their sustenance. Wherefore I ask you yourself
14    to take the trouble, when they arrive at
15    the city up to the 15th of Choiach, and giving yourself
16    (to it) until (it is) sent to me and
17    he gives the boys a little grain. But if he does not
18    give (it), write to me if anything befalls you,
19    concealing nothing. And concerning
20    the things concerning me, do not be anxious. Everything has happened quite
21    according to reason. They have been
22    condemned by the chrematistai, and
23    for this reason I was detained by the dioiketes, lest, having been asked, he should report to the
24    dioiketes that (he) cannot be brought (to court).
25    For the king himself, sitting (in session),
26    hears (the case). And the greatest thing of all is with
27    the gods. And concerning your brother
28    [I j]udge that he should come to
29    [the] cit[y] in the present circumstances,
30    for he will be released as soon as we
31    make him clear in the trial against
32    [t]hese men. You will therefore do me a favor
33    by taking care of your body so that you may be well.
34    Fa[re well]. Year [1]8, Hathyr 25.
35    To the toparch Leon
36    … [of A]pollonios, a request
37    … on and of the 1 talent.
38    … [imme]diately to come
39    … still those from me
40    … anton the (things) agreed upon.
