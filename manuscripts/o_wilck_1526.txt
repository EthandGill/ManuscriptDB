[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 48, Pachon 29: there has been paid to the
2     bank in Hermonthis, of which Ammonios (is in charge), (for) the apomoira of year 48:
3     Harsiesis (son of) Nechthmonthes,
4     one thousand 300, total 1300.
5     (For) year 47, the same, (of) apomoira, one thousand
6     three hundred, total 1300 — but
7     do not use the previously-written (receipt) for the same amount —
8     total 2600.
