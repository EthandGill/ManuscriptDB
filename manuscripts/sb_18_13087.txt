[TRANSLATION]
1     a ...
2     To Gaius Iulius ... prefect of the watchmen
3     from [I]mouths son of Ptolemaios; (concerning) Popontas son of
4     Andron of those from the Attinos Isieion of the
5     Herakleidos division, having received from me the price
6     of hay owed to me, and (also) lying under (a) bathhouse
7     which I have in the village of Iulia and Libia under lease
8     (of the) bathhouse, wheat, three artabae, and (having) not repaid
9     neither the hay nor yet the wheat, but also
10    together with his sons and his wife having committed outrage against me
11    I filed the proper petition to Solon
12    who had been made prefect of the watchmen, from which time
13    my servants having come together and having gone into
14    the village for the recovery both of the hay
15    and of the wheat, (of) the wife of Popontas, Herakleias, having given me
16    a cloak as a pledge, both through the headman of the village
17    and other elders, the (wife) of Popontas
18    aforementioned woman Herakleias and the (son) of both
19    (their) son Andron, having attacked upon my house
20    wounded (me) with great outrage and blows
21    [and] snatched away what I had in pledge, a cloak, and
22    [our] other cloak worth silver 20 drachmai; wherefore I request
23    [you] to order (that someone) write to the village prefect to establish
24    both Popontas and the others (against) you so that
25    I may recover the hay and the wheat and the
26    our cloak, and (that) they may meet with what is fitting
27    Farewell
28    To the watchman-prefect
29    ... to do ... ... ... ...
30    of the year 26 of Caesar, Epeiph ...
