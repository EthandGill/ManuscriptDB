[TRANSLATION]
1     to [the masters] and on account of all things most wonderful brothers Nemesianus [and]
2     Flavianus ...
3     [having found] an opportunity of our common brother and ... [...]
4     [he writes] first [in form], so that you might deign to report to us matters concerning your health
5     to [us]; and if anything is needed, without hesitation deign to order us. Concerning the storeroom he has written wonderful things and I received [them] in full, just as you sent me perfume through Antonius the ally. Complete favor and moreover
6     show zeal toward me and send to me also the donkey, first-rate and excellent, because I have need of it for service; and also one garment to the one loving
7     you, Theophobius, send [it] along also with the little donkey, worth as if only half [value]
8     [with] me reckoning its price, and two pots of cheese and jars of olives
9     [of] honey-color three. And I have taken an oath [from] Heraclides, so that he not neglect concerning
10    this. And care [for] the property as genuine brothers.
11    May divine providence preserve your wonderful majesty in good health.
12    Give [this] to my lord brother Nemesianus from Etaeon
