[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymos, collector of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis. I have received for embankment-tax
3     and bath-tax of year 11, base drachmas seven, 5 obols, 2 chalkoi, total base drachmas 7, 5 obols, 2 chalkoi.
4     Year 11 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
5     Mesore 20. I, Didymos, through Phatres the scribe, have signed.
