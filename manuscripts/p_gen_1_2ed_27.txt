[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklepiades I have marked, 15th year, Mesore 7
2     To Dius and also Apollonios and
3     Herodes and also Diogenes, having served as gymnasiarchs, keepers of records
4     of property-holdings of Arsinoe[ite]
5     from Pakysios [son] of Pakysios
6     [son] of Herieus, from village Soknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleid district.
7     According to what was ordered I register the property which I have, which is
8     in the aforesaid village, a sixth
9     part of a house and courtyard, and another
10    ... a twentieth part [of a] house ..., and if something concerning them
11    I dispose of, I will show how it is
