[TRANSLATION]
1     [through Menas, slave, who asks and pro]vide[s for his own master, the same all-praiseworthy]
2     man, t[he right of acti]on a[nd liability] … [through you, the]
3     most admirab[l]e Kyriakos, st[ewar]d,
4     Aurelius Anoup, son of Petronios, mother Maxima, supervisor,
5     gardener, from the hamlet of Nesos Lachanias across (the river) from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
6     of an estate of your Magnificence, registered tenant
7     of it, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from your
8     Magnificence, herewith already, by way of an advance for the
9     small garden under me, of your Magnificence, two solidi of gold by private standard less eight keratia,
10    given to me through the same most admirable Kyriakos,
11    steward — that is, 2 solidi of gold less 8 keratia by private standard — which, being free of risk from every risk, I shall of necessity
12    repay to your Magnificence or to the one acting lawfully
13    on its behalf in making the recovery against me
14    whenever (it) might wish to take (it) back, without delay,
15    without any contradiction, at the risk of all my belongings.
16    Valid is this bond of the advance, written singly, and on being formally questioned
17    I acknowledged it.
18    Aurelius Anoup, supervisor, son of Petronios,
19    the aforesaid, have received by way of advance the
20    two solidi of gold less eight keratia by private standard, as capital,
21    and I shall repay whenever you might wish, and all agrees with me
22    as is set out above. Aurelius Serenos, son of the late
23    Ptolemios, being requested, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
