[TRANSLATION]
1     [Hatre]s son of Harpaesis [an]d Ladamas son of Pelasis
2     and Harsiesis son of Horos and Peteuris son of Petosiris
3     and Hatres son of Semtheus and Semthonaes son of Mencheus
4     and Harsisois son of Harpaesis, elders of those
5     from the village of Korphotoi, we swear by the god
6     Imperator Caesar, son of god, that we will truly bring up
7     and make watertight and drain off (the water around) the
8     three public dykes around the village, of which are
9     the one called (the dyke) of Zaneris and the one called
10    (the dyke) of the Great Road and the one to the west of the … -onios
11    garden, and we will dig them out and keep watch
12    by night and by day … -meno…
13    unharmed, unbroken and uncut until the
14    receding of the river, being [mutually liable];
15    e… and if we keep the oath, may it be well with us … a;
16    written through Theon the single-scribe
17    [because] they say they do not know [le]tters.
18    Year … [Me]sore 8.
19    Hatres son of Harpaesis a[nd his] brother
20    Harsisois and Ladamas son of Pelasis and
21    Harsiesis son of Horos and Hatres son of Semtheus
22    and Semthonaes son of Mencheus, of those from
23    the village of Korphotoi, to Herakleios, toparch of Agema. We acknowledge
24    that we have received from you, through the public bank
25    of Areios, for the working
26    of the dykes for which we have given our handwritten undertaking,
27    three, around the village, of silver
28    one hundred and twenty drachmas, total: drachmas 120 …
29    which we will also work out of (the money) in sound condition, and
30    we make no claim.
31    Written through Theon the single-scribe, because
32    they say they do not know letters.
33    Year 5, Mesore 8.
