[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammonios to Apollonios the
2     brother, greetings. I received your
3     letter through which I learned you are well
4     and was very glad. The money I gave to Amo
5     so that your father not have burden. You will give
6     him from mine a chous of wine
7     [and o]n behalf of you another. This too to him
8     ... I have said [he should] receive from you two choes of wine,
9     [so] that [you are] owing. Concerning the feet of the bed
10    ... and I have the wood of it, [the wood] for the feet.
11    Farewell.
