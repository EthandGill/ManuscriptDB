[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Constantinus [and]
2     Licinius, the Augusti, for the fourth time, Pauni on the new moon.
3     Aurelius Theotimos son of Hierakion also called Theophanes, ex-magistrate of the city of Panopolis
4     the most illustrious, to Aurelius Gounthos son of Mikkalos, of the [same] city,
5     greeting. I acknowledge that I have today received and borrowed from
6     you, for my own and necessary need, of Imperial silver
7     coinage eleven talents and three hundred denarii, total 11 talents 300 denarii,
8     which I shall repay to you in the month Hathyr on the thirtieth of the [present consulship,] without interest, without any postponement and evasion, under compulsion,
9     [and it shall not be lawful for me] to demand another term outside the [aforesaid,] and, for the security of the same money, to mortgage
10    to you the grain-bearing arourai belonging to me, six, near the metropolis,
11    in the plain of Pmouchis, of the cleruchic class, on holding 136; of which grain-bearing arourai
12    two I obtained by right of inheritance, and the remaining four arourai by right of an exchange made with Hierakion
13    my brother, in the past consulship of Volusianus and Annianus,
14    in the month Epeiph 22, [through Arte]midoros, contract-writer
15    of the city, in accordance with the securities which [I hold] and their neighbours appearing in the field, which [I have set forth] to you today, being persuaded;
16    and it shall not be lawful for me to mortgage further or to sell or to make any other disposition of them to anyone else except you until I pay you in full,
17    or, if you are not paid, that you shall be master of them by right of purchase and
18    have control and hold all the ownership for the perpetual
19    time, the guarantee following upon me continually against everyone entirely, against him who shall come forward, either in my name
20    or of anyone else, at every occasion, under compulsion; and the deed of the
21    mortgage being valid … under … under signature …
