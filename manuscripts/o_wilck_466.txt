1   Psensentithoes son of Pabouchis,
2   grandson of Peteesis, paid, for poll-tax of the South-and-West (quarter),
3   year 5, 8 dr. Year 5 of Domitian Caesar the lord,
4   Pachon 6. Likewise Pachon 6, 4 dr.; likewise
5   Pachon 8, 4 dr.; likewise (in) year 6, Phaophi 5, dike-tax 4 dr.,
6   (= ) 3 dr. 4½ ob.; likewise Phaophi 28, dike-tax 6 dr. 3 ob.
7   (2nd hand) Likewise Pachon 6, for the guards' pay of year 5, 1 dr.
