[TRANSLATION]
1     May God fulfill your brotherly disposition. I bear [wit]ness
2     concerning the matter between Paos and the clergy of holy Mary [that] not by necessity
3     they made for him the [compens]ation, and not only that they made the compensation;
4     with a guarantor the agreement came to be, and I am the guarantor. If therefore [to manage]
5     [they have] not authority over their lands; understand further; let [me be entreated]
6     therefore let your brotherhood [be urged to] command to guard for him the right… [the aforementioned] these fields; for the clergy did not do anything unreasonable, master
7     to my master, the most brilliant in all things and most esteemed lord Dioscorus, Ana[s]ta[s]ios, notary
