[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion and Exagon, both [sons] of Heros,
2     [Son] of Exakous from the city of Oxyrhynchus to Herat son of Pausyrion,
3     [Son] of Pausyrios, mother Teteuria, from the village of Psobthis of the
4     Middle toparchy, the oil-working equipment belonging to them in the southern parts of the village in their own house, [set up for] oil,
5     [Oil-processing] machine together with all the stone and wooden equipment arranged around it,
6     For two years from the present thirteenth year of Trajan
7     Caesar the lord until the entering fourteenth
8     Year of Trajan Caesar the lord, Mesore the 30th, [at a] rent [of]
9     Per year of Augustus silver currency drachmas one hundred
10    And forty, and for the harvest of oil one chous and one hundred pressings and earthen vessels of oil and [for] oil-processing per year without charge eight artabas of vegetable-seed; and if the lessees wish, they shall take oil at their own
11    Measure of the [price] being at the time up to five choai.
12    If any [part] breaks of the machine, so much shall the [lessees] give to [him], the carpentry wages being
13    [To the lessee], the [charges concerning] the machine being public [charges]
14    [Being] [toward the lessees]. Let the lessee pay the rent from Pharmouthi up to Mesore per
15    [Month] [twenty-eight drachmas], and after the time let the lessee deliver the [machine] and [all the things] around it in good [condition]
16    [And unharmed, the collection] of the rent being [from] both
17    [The lessee] and from all his [possessions]
18    [Just as from] a court judgment. The lease is valid. In the year 13 of the Imperator
19    [Caesar Nerva] Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus,
20    [Thoth] [the] 12th. Heras son of Pausyrion, I myself having written
21    [I have] leased [the aforementioned machine for] the rent [of] drachmas [of] one hundred [forty] and for the [harvests at/for]
22    [All] the aforementioned [terms]. In the year 13 of Trajan Caesar
23    [The] lord, Thoth the 12th.
