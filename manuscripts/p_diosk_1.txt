[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dioskourides, commander and
2     garrison-commander,
3     from Theon son of Theon,
4     a Cyrenaean, of the (company) of Hermotimos
5     and Meleagros, of the infantry.
6     On the night before the 16th of
7     Thoth of the 28th year, Iason son of Iason,
8     a Jew, of the same (company), came
9     to my house which is in the
10    fort at the harbour, and watching for the moment when
11    my slave-girl was opening the courtyard
12    door so as to pour out a basin, having upset
13    this, suddenly burst in
14    and dragging Theodosios, who was dining with me,
15    down from the couch
16    drunkenly assaulted (us) not moderately; and at once,
17    when I had got control of my door so that no others might run in together, and a greater uproar having arisen and more people having come up besides, among whom also Herakleotes,
18    I sent this man (to fetch you?) to you;
19    and so, Herakleides, the one from you, having come and entered
20    and having found the aforesaid man
21    pressing upon Theodosios on the
22    ground, and having led both away, I also gave as surety in addition and to you
23    Iason forthwith, whom indeed also
24    on the following day Herakleides brought before
25    the strategos.
26    Having therefore submitted, concerning these same matters,
27    both to the others, the customary ones,
28    I ask that my
29    report be registered, and with you
30    in an official record.
31    Farewell.
32    Year 28, Thoth …
