[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship o]f our lords Diocletianus
2     A[ugustus for the 6th time] and Constantius the most illustrious
3     Cae[sar for the] 2nd time.
4     To Aurelius Plotinus son of Eudai[mo]n, exegetes, councillor
5     of the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
6     from Aurelius Akoueis son of Apis, his mother being Tsenthonis, from the same city, residing
7     in the hamlet of Pseneuar near Senepta. Of [my own free will]
8     I undertake to lease for on[ly the]
9     present 13th and 12th and 5th year, from the [property belonging]
10    to you near Senepta …
11    … from arouras 5 … [one aroura]
12    and a half, so as to plant this with fodder,
13    at a rent for the same one and a half arouras of one thousand silver drachmas, total 1000 drachmas, free of every risk, the public dues of the land
14    resting upon you the landlord, who is master of the
15    crops until you recover the rent. The lease being secur[ed to me] I shall of necessity pay the
16    re[nt in the month P]achon without delay, the exe[cution being] yours against me as is fitting. Valid is the [lease, and] on being questioned by you I acknowledged.
17    Year 13 [and 12] of our lords Diocletianus
18    and Ma[ximianu]s Augusti and the 5th year of our lords
19    Constantius and Maximianus
20    the most illustrious Caesars, Hathyr 4.
21    I, Aurelius Akoueis, have undertaken in lease one and a half arouras and I shall pay
22    the rent as stated above and on being questioned
23    I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Silvanus
24    also called Thonios, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
