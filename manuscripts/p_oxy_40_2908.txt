1     13. It was decided.
2     To Calpurnius Horion, the most excellent recorder (hypomnematographos),
3     from Aurelius Apion son of Plution also called Horion, son of Diogenes, his mother being Chairemonis, from
4     the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites.
5     Being registered in the quarter of South Crepis and having been scrutinized
6     in the 13th year, so that I am, with respect to the present 1st year, 19 years of age,
7     I did not answer at the proclamation that was held, because I was
8     abroad. But now,
9     having come to reside (here), I submit
10    these documents and I request to share, together with the others, in the
11    grant of the grain-dole.
12    Farewell.
