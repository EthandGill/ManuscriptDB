[TRANSLATION]
1     on the condition that I give by account of a fine gold
2     one ounce and to remain as
3     is set forth. Aurelius Lilou of Pekysios agrees with me as is set forth. Flavius Georgios
4     scribe of the Memnonians who also incorporated [me]
5     I wrote on his behalf.
6     Agathe of Theodotus, elder, I witness.
7     Pathermouthis of Elia, deacon, I witness.
8     Abraham of Paminkel, elder, I witness.
9     Peter of Pene[utus], elder, I witness.
10    Elias of Joseph of Penutus, deacon, I witness.
