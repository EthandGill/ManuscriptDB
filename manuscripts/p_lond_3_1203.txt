[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] year 4, Mecheir 27, in Pathyris, under Heliodoros the agoranomos,
2     [they] willingly drew up [the contract] together. Totoes son of Pelaios acknowledges
3     that he owes Panobchounis son of Totoes copper talents 8
4     and wheat artabae 13 1/2 without interest. Now this debt let [him] repay
5     Totoes to Panobchounis in [the] month Mesore of the 5th year
6     But if he does not repay in the appointed time,
7     let him pay immediately one and a half [times the amount] and for the [time] overpassed interest [of] two drachmas per mina per month
8     each [month], and the wheat [at] the price in the marketplace.
9     And the right of execution shall be [that of] Panobchounis from Totoes
10    and from all [the property] belonging to him, just as
11    from [a] judgment.
12    Areios, [acting] for Heliodoros, [has] verified [this].
