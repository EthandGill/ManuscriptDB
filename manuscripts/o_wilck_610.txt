[TRANSLATION]
1     Pamouthes and Postomos, tax-collectors of the silver [tax] of Charax
2     To Amenothes son of Harbechis. We have received on account of the apportionment …
3     of the 5th year [drachma] one obol six, totaling drachma 1 obol 6, and on account of the doubles
4     of the 5th year [drachma] one obol four, totaling drachma 1 obol 4, and on account of the skopelon
5     and of others of the 5th year obols 4½. Of the 6th year of Antoninus
6     Caesar our lord, Phaophi 18.
