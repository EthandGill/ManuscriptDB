[TRANSLATION]
1     … Sotelis my father
2     [very much] greetings and [to one] strong
3     [to prosper], having excused myself
4     … come! For the farmers [do] not tolerate me; if not
5     … 400 measures; you do not have favor from Stoites and Naarautus often; but before all things [care for] the body so that [you] are well;
6     … farewell; year 14, Epeiph 30.
