r.1    Παταικίων Ζήνωνι χαίρειν. ἀκηκόαμεν παρὰ

[TRANSLATION]
1     Pataicion to Zenon, greetings. We have heard from
2     Pyrrhus [that] the goatherds are withdrawing. The Limnean indeed he said has already gone away, [and] Demetrius [is]
3     in addition to this. And concerning Hermias too he said he had heard. I have written you in order that
4     you might know. You then take thought
5     concerning these as it seems to you.
6     Farewell. [In the] fourth year, Choiak 24.
7     To Zenon.
