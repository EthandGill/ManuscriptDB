[TRANSLATION]
1     [...] to Dioskoros, scribe of the Blemmyan people, from the customs-(offices) [...]
2     [...]...., full brothers, from [...]
3     [from the village of ...]saimis of the Latopolite [no]me, greet(ing). We acknowledge that we [...]
4     [... and] have borrowed from your good-repute today [...]
5     [... of wine, four] hundred koeis, total 400 koeis of wine, and these, God willing, we are read[y ...]
6     [...]n me to you ...[...] of the, God willing, beginning [...]
7     [... on the fif]teenth, at four lak each, in the lak, of each, koeis,
8     and ...ma to you ... written, to bring the same empty (jars); and when the said term has passed
9     and we do not repay you the aforesaid four hundred koeis of wine, at four lak each koeis,
10    on condition that you take all our cattle, female and male, small and large, until
11    (repay-)
12    ment and completion of the aforesaid wine, indisputably; and for security we have made this bond
13    as aforesaid. Aurelia Asaneth, daughter of Pamouthes, and her sons, the Aurelii (sons) of Pamouthes and Midios, the aforesaid, have placed
14    this security for the four hundred koeis of wine, and all the things written are agreeable to us as aforesaid.
15    Flavius Pelitos (son) of Danielios, having been requested by the aforewritten, I wrote on their behalf,
16    they not knowing letters,
17    which also I registered. (It) was written in the month Epeiph 30, indiction 15. (verso)
18    † Security made concerning Asaneth.
