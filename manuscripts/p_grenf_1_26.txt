[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 9, Athur 13, in Pathyris, under Sosus the agoranomos,
2     Psenenuphis Onnophrios [repaid] a loan
3     of wheat, 56 artabas, which Erianouphis lent to him
4     of Pathot in year 3, Thoth, according to a loan contract
5     [which had been] deposited in the [archive] in Pathyris,
6     [who] moreover being present at the archive, Erianouphis
7     [acknowledged] to have received, and not to [make a claim] concerning all matters relating to the loan
8     in no manner, but to remit the [interest].
9     Hermias, the one from Sosus, received the payment.
10    Settlement of Psenenuphis.
