[TRANSLATION]
1     We received. Year 5, Pha[men]oth …
2     [Marres] to Menches, greeting. Of the letter [from Horos the royal] [scribe]
3     concerning which he indicates
4     a cession has taken place to D[ionysios son of Dionysios by] [Menandros son of Pantauchos]
5     of a kleros near Kerkeosiris, the copy [is appended below.]
6     [Year 5, Phamenoth] …
7     Horos to Marres, greeting. Of the letter from Aristip[pos, who is in charge of the assignment of the katoikoi]
8     cavalry, concerning which he indicates that [a cession has taken place by Menandros]
9     to Dionysios son of Dionysios, the copy is appended below.
10    [Year 5, Phamenoth] 22.
11    Aristippos to Horos, greeting. Of the memorandum given to us by Dionysios
12    son of Dionysios, [a Mace]donian, concerning what he indicates has been ceded to him by
13    Menandros son of Pantauchos, a kleros near Kerkeosiris in the division of Polemon,
14    of 34 1/16 1/32 arouras, we have subjoined the copy. You will do well, therefore, to give instructions
15    for the indicated land to be registered to him in your office as well. Year 5, Phamenoth 22.
16    To Aristippos, of the First Friends and in charge of the assignment, from Dionysios son of
17    Dionysios, a Macedonian. Having had ceded to me the kleros of Menandros son of Pantauchos
18    near Kerkeosiris in the division of Polemon, of 34 1/16 1/32 arouras, but since those
19    in charge of the registers are unaware of the transaction that has taken place and still
20    register the kleros to Menandros, I request that instructions be given to write
21    to the royal scribe so that, knowing (it), he may register to me the indicated
22    arouras 34 1/16 1/32, that I may be treated with favor.
23    Farewell.
