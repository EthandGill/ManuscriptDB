[TRANSLATION]
1     … [under the] guard of those
2     from the village of Phbou of the Hermopolite nome. I acknowledge willingly and
3     of my own accord that I have leased from you for a ten-year period,
4     reckoned from the crops now in the fields of the, with God,
5     incoming eleventh indiction and thereafter consecutively, the property belonging
6     to the same holy monastery, a vineyard plot
7     planted with vines, in extent of however many arouras it is, together with a complete cistern
8     and a water-wheel and a cattle-stall and a wooden machine and
9     a reed-bed and every right, situated in the place called Siklous
10    under the guard of those from the same village of Phbou of the Hermopolite
11    nome, for my own manual labour and weeding
12    and reed-setting and reed-working and the fences
13    of squared (stones),
14    and digging-around five times per year,
15    [and to exercise all] diligence and goodwill in the same
16    property blamelessly and irreproachably, on condition that the same holy
17    monastery receives the half part of all the wine to be produced
18    and that I together with the farmer receive the other
19    half part in return for the labours we perform, and that I sow
20    the small plots of land belonging to the same vineyard from the east … and that I receive the
21    crop of them apart [from the rents]. And I will provide each year the customary
22    jars … of the vintage. And that I cannot withdraw from
23    the … service before the completion of the same ten-year
24    [period] … with the help of the laws as helpers of us
25    … not to transgress … The (lease is) valid.
