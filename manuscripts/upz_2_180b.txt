[TRANSLATION]
1     [Copy] of a sale.
2     [Year] 4, Epeiph 9, after the public [holidays] in Hermontis
3     [of] Pathyrite of the Thebaid under Hermias as agoranomos.
4     Horos son of Horos, one of the Memnonean tomb-workers, sold
5     as of sixty-nine years, of medium [build], honey-colored, sturdy, [bald], long-faced, straight-nosed, ears
6     large and standing out, weak in the eyes, from the
7     [appertain]ing to him and to [his] partners [a] built house and of the
8     adjoining bare [ground] of the being in [the] southern part
9     of Zeus-city, the Great, the share falling to him, one-seventh
10    of which the neighbors of the whole house: south house of Paitus
11    the flute-player, north house of Tachleutos, east royal street,
12    west [canal], and the house toward Tagē built
13    of three and one-quarter cubits of which [neighbors]: south house of Harpahēsios, north bare places
14    of the crowds, east house of Pechytos the tomb-worker, west
15    house of Hasōtos, and in Pmonemoûne[i] [from] a demolished house
16    the one-half, of which the neighbors of the whole house: south royal street,
17    n[orth] house of P[o]ēbios, east ho[use] of Petearpreous son of Menneus,
18    w[est] house of Zmanreous son of Psemneus, of which [through the] middle [is a] passageway,
19    or whoever may be neighbors [in all directions] from all sides. [Persons] purchased Osorohe[ris] son of Horos as of fifty years,
20    of medium [build], honey-colored, sturdy, long-faced, straight-nosed, and Nechthmonthes and
21    Petosîris, each according to the one-half of one-quarter, Tagē's the other quarter
22    apart from the house toward it of three and one-quarter cubits and a house
23    built of the being within the in the Memnonean fortress, of which neighbors;
24    south royal street, north house of Petosîrios pastophoros
25    of Aphrodite, east house of Poērios fuller, west street
26    royal or whoever may be neighbors from all sides, each of the same according to the quarter,
27    of bronze two talents and the custody of the
28    appertaining to him bodies
29    of those being transferred to the tombs
30    and of these accounts and [collections],
31    of which the per-capita [share] through this document is declared.
32    seller and warrantor of the according to the
33    sale Horos the one who sold, whom received
34    Osoroheris and his brothers the purchasers.
35    Year 4, Mesore 28, [it] has been deposited with the bank in Hermontis,
36    which [bank] Ammonios [manages], tenth of the cycle, by draft [of Ptolemaios] tax-collector, under which
37    signs Asklepiades the copy-scribe, Osoroheris
38    son of Horos and his brothers, payment [transfer] of the property
39    to Horos the father, of bronze two talents, complete.
40    Ammonios the banker.
