[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord, landholder Valerianus,
2     Petrus, assistant.
3     I received from the lady for account
4     of barley, year 13 of the indiction,
5     gold coins three; they come to coins three,
6     for me to reckon these for you
7     on account of [your] name of the same
8     year 13 of the indiction of the [account] [being made]
9     year 6, year 45, year Cho[i]ak 11.
10    Petrus, assistant; I agree: gold coins three only.
