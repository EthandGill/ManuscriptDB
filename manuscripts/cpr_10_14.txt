1     Damianos
2     To John the tax-collector of Pto[lemais].
3     Furnish Chael the notary of the
4     most glorious village-scribe the goods that came to be

6     Mesore 15, indiction 13.
7     [T]otal: [w]heat [artabas] 50 [Mesore] 15 indiction 13.
8     [Give to John] from lord Damianos wheat, artabas 50, indiction 13, Mesore 15
