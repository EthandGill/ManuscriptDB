1     May your splendidness, together with all the other benefactions, even this favor
2     complete; since I have requested the most glorious regional inspector, the illustrious Theodosios
3     on account of this, and [the inspector] wrote to your splendidness so that you might write to the private citizens of the village and
4     Tlethmes and [they] transport from their camels the few reeds which I purchased
5     near them until the [of] y[our] estate the ol…, very clearly marked; for [this] alone
6     to my master, with god all things, most splendid and most renowned lord Theodosios, Kyriakos inspector of the most glorious
