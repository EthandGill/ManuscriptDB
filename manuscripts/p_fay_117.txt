[TRANSLATION]
1     Lucius Bellenus Gemellus
2     to Sabinus his son, greeting and continual good health. Know that Eluras the
3     basilikos (grammateus) is succeeding to the strategia of Erasos, by a letter of the most excellent prefect. If it seem good to you, send him 1 artaba of olives and a little fish, since
4     we have need of him. You shall send us
5     to the house seasonings and olive(s), since they
6     do not have new olive(s) in the house. Send the workmen (?), since Erasos will perhaps
7     hold the Harpokrateia here on the 14th, and send him the cabbages. Load all the animals with cabbage and we shall send
8     him … five (loads of) cabbage and the same to the house. Know that … has received the three-… of Erasos for 300 drachmae, and … of it has been made.
9     Wherefore I write to you, so that you may … the horses
10    which, having taken, you should exchange, so that he
11    may receive them. Shake out the … so that
12    you may be free from care. As to what you wrote me,
13    not to leave the builder at rest, it has been written superfluously, and you write me very much
14    that "I give thanks to the village" when
15    four staters against you (pl.)
16    they have written. I pray for your good health
17    for all time. Year 11 of Trajan
18    Caesar the lord, Tybi 12.
19    Deliver to Sabinus the son from Lucius
20    Bellenus Gemellus.
