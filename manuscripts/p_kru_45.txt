[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity,
2     of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. It was written in the month Pharmouthi 29, the 8th indiction,
3     under Flavius Saal son of Abdella, the most glorious amir, of the pagarchs from Diospolis to Latopolis.
4     I, Abesa daughter of the blessed Zacharias, my mother being
5     Abegea, the woman of the fortress of Djeme of the nome of the city
6     of Hermonthis, I give afterward (the subscription) of a subscriber
7     to subscribe on my behalf, regarding the written deed of settlement,
8     of division and of freedom-from-care, that cannot transgress
9     it and cannot shake it, through them,
10    the laws — rather, moreover, establishing it firmly through some other trustworthy witnesses, those who shall bear witness
11    to it on my behalf, according to my own request, my own.
12    I write to Takoum my genuine sister, from
13    the same father and the same mother, of the nome of this same city,
14    greeting, that I acknowledge that, since in this present time, now, in the eighth year,
15    we brought suit against one another concerning the house of our blessed father
16    Zacharias, which is up above (the house) of Andreas of Sikeinta, that we might divide it between us with one another, so that each one of us might become lord
17    of his portion. After the agreement, therefore, of a legal argument, having made it
18    with one another, we chose some great men and the builder
19    in the fortress, we cast them into that house,
20    they mediated it and they cast a lot upon that whole house;
21    when the lot came up upon me, you Takoum my genuine sister, so that I might become lord of the whole portion
22    of that house, from the share of the vault toward the south
23    upon it, of that house, from its foundations up to the roof,
24    with all its appurtenances that pertain to it, and again,
25    that the door should turn toward your portion, you Takoum my sister,
26    out toward the street under the vault, that the door of your portion
27    might open into our heart — because I have set up the whole southern portion
28    from the side of the vault toward the south upon that house,
29    according to the way that I shall reveal its boundary out,
30    these (limits) which are: the south side belongs to Antone, the heart (court). I, Abesa,
31    who first wrote to you, Takoum, and that the door should turn to the south.
32    East: the house of the children of Andreas of Sekeinta. West:
33    the house of Aristophanes son of Iohannes. The boundary of that house
34    is these (limits); and the portion of you Takoum my sister.
35    So that, from now on, you Takoum may come into it and become lord of the whole share of that house, from its foundations up to the roof with all its appurtenances that pertain
36    to it, according to the way that we shall reveal them out, by this settlement
37    of division, according to the partition above — to beget it for you for ever,
38    to beget it for you, to administer it, to manage it, to embellish
39    it, to turn it, to give it for repair, to give (it) as a gift,
40    to cede it, to leave it to your sons and your daughters,
41    to beget it for an heir, to use it in any
42    fashion that pleases you at any time. Whoever shall dare, at any
43    time, to come out against you ever, neither I nor my children
44    nor brother nor sister nor (one born) of one (parent) nor (one born) of two, nor
45    on my father's side nor on my mother's side, to bring suit against you, nor near any
46    authority however exalted and glorious, concerning the portion of mine which I set up, or another portion. Firstly, indeed, that that one shall profit
47    nothing; first of all, indeed, he is estranged from the Father and
48    the Son and the Holy Spirit; afterward he shall give the account
49    of the fine of twelve gold solidi to the authority that collects against us at that time;
50    afterward he shall come in (and) confirm the force of this settlement of division
51    and freedom-from-care, it being settled for you in every place where it shall be produced, and they shall ask me what I acknowledged.
52    I, Abesa daughter of the blessed Zacharias, who first wrote above, I agree to it.
53    I, Philochristos, the least deacon,
54    son of … ol, wrote on her behalf.
55    I, Aristophanes son of the blessed Iohannes, give witness.
56    I, Zegeel son of the blessed Mathias,
57    I give witness. I, Peshate son of Ioseph, give witness.
58    Thomas son of the blessed Biktor — they asked me; I wrote on their behalf,
59    because they do not know (how to write); and I give witness.
60    I, Nohe the presbyter and the hegoumenos,
61    I give witness according to the request of Abesa.
