[TRANSLATION]
1     [From] my lord Alypios
2     from his order.
3     Sarapion to Heroninos
4     manager Thraso, greetings.
5     [Hand over] to Paesis a fisherman
6     to Apollonios a horseman, so that
7     he may carry him to
8     my lord Alypios.
9     For he himself ordered this. I wish you well
10    Year 12, Choiak 19.
