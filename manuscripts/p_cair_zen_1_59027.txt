[TRANSLATION]
1     Aristeus to Apollonius, greetings. Amyntas having assigned [it] to us, we have given wage to the persons left [behind] for two months, and to Kallianax the carpenter indeed for three months. I have therefore written also to Zenon so that he may know.
2     Farewell. year 28, Gorpiaios 17.
3     of Aristeus.
4     to Apollonius
5     administrator.
6     year 28, Hyperberetaios.
7     [w]ages to the persons.
