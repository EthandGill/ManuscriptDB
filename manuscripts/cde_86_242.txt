[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 22 of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrianus Augustus, Pauni 18. The following have made (this disposition): the Mesobeitai, Tithoes son of Psenamounis son of Psais, his mother being Tasechthis,
3     about 45 years old, with a scar under the left knee, and Talaeis
4     daughter of Horos son of Patchalmis, her mother being Tapsais, about 40 years old, with a scar on the right shin, his wife, mutual sureties for payment,
5     agreeing with Mesobeites Tithoes son of Bekis son of Psais,
6     his mother being Tbekis: we owe you in silver
7     one hundred and forty drachmas, total 140 drachmas, which we will also repay to you in the coming 23rd year of Hadrianus Caesar
8     the lord, Pauni 18, without postponement; but if
9     we do not pay you in full at the stated deadline,
10    we will pay you interest for the time overdue,
11    the one-drachma interest, and it shall be permitted to you to be paid out of all that we have and whatever we may acquire in addition, and not to sell or mortgage
12    until we first pay you. Harpokras, contract-writer of Mesobe…, I have transacted through …athos also called Philetos, secretary.
13    Tithoes son of Psenamounis son of Psais and Talaeis daughter of Horos
14    his wife, with her guardian Tithoes,
15    the aforewritten persons, to Tithoes son of Bekis: we have placed with you the aforesaid loan of
16    one hundred and forty silver drachmas as is stated above. I wrote on behalf of
17    them, Epagathos, freedman of Katenos, having been asked.
