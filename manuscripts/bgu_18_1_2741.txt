[TRANSLATION]
1     Read year 31 Mecheis 18, copied year 31 Mecheis 18.
2     To Tothoeis, scribe of the treasury of Peri Koma.
3     If [the men] from Leonides, who has been assigned to the
4     grain collection, should be deposited so that to Alexandria into the royal [storehouse] according to [the order] from Sarapionis
5     of the one over the revenues, [issue] the response
6     the [shipments] sent [to be] falling short
7     to which we have sent into Peene[...]
8     from the cohort into the boat of Diognetus for transport of wheat artabas
9     five hundred, [they] total wheat artabas 500, let [them] be deposited together as is customary with token and counter-token
10    [act] toward them as is fitting.
11    … to the auditors.
