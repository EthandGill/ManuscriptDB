r.2    καὶ μέτοχοι τελῶναι ἑταιρικοῦ νομοῦ Ἑρμωνθίτου

[TRANSLATION]
1     Phlenis, elder, son of Phthomouthes
2     and associates, tax-farmers of the trade-tax of the Hermontthite nome
3     through Karourus, assistant, to Artemidorus, [whose] mother [is] Sambe
4     Greetings. [I] received for the trade-tax, year 10, four drachmas.
5     Year 10 of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar
6     our lord, Pachon 8.
