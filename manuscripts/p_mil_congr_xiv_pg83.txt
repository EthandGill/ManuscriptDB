[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of [Flavius] Ho[noriu]s the most renowned
2     son and [of] Flavius Euodius the most illustrious, Payni 3.
3     Aurelius Pityron [son] of Pityron the aforesaid stand surety for the aforesaid Didymus
4     [son] of Demetrius, whom I will also produce as stipulated.
5     Aurelius … [?] … [?] … of Theonius I wrote on behalf of
6     him documents, he not knowing [how to write].
