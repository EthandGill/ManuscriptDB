[TRANSLATION]
1     We bear witness to these things — we, Sergios son of Georgios, by the mercy of God presbyter, and Georgios
2     son of Victor, by the mercy of God archdeacon, and Sergios son of Palladios, deacon, and Ioannes
3     son of Stephanos of Azaeiath, and Zacharias son of Georgios, and Stephanos son of Oedos, and Georgis
4     son of Elias — that Ioannes son of Oualios, presbyter, brought us together, and his wife
5     Nonna daughter of Ioannes son of Kotemos, and with her her mother Thekemis,
6     and many things having been stirred up between them and disputed concerning capital sums;
7     and we all the aforenamed men remained that they might be reconciled to
8     one another, and we said much between them that they might be brought together with one another,
9     and they would not be brought to agreement. Then the same Ioannes the presbyter said to his wife
10    "I give you a choice: if you wish a judge, and if you wish anyone from
11    our village, even wherever you wish, I will take with you a judge."
12    Then the same Nonna answered together with her mother and they said "We wish nothing
13    from you, nor do we take any judge with you, nor have we taken from you anything whatever,
14    neither from the dowry nor from any movable or self-moving property, but only that you release me." Then the same Ioannes answered
15    in the presence of us the aforenamed men, "You are released."
16    "We have no claim against one another." And these things we bear witness,
17    we the aforenamed men, before God. Neither I
18    Nonna nor my heirs nor successors, nor you Ioannes nor
19    your heirs nor successors, has any claim, neither about dowry nor about any matter small or great, written or unwritten, whether having come
20    to mind or not having come. It was written in the month of Gorpiaios, of the 3rd indiction, in the year by Elousa,
21    five hundred and eighty-fourth, by my hand, of Sergios son of Georgios; and I bear witness —
22    Sergios son of Georgios, by the mercy of God presbyter, I was present and
23    I bear witness to these things.
24    [ ]Georgios son of Victor, by the mercy of God archdeacon, I was present and
25    I bear witness — Sergis son of Palladios, by the mercy of God deacon, and
26    I bear witness — Ioannes son of Stephanos of Azzaeiath, I was present
27    and I bear witness to these things together with my companions.
28    son of Oedos, I was present and I bear witness to these things together with my companions —
29    Zacharias son of Georgios, I was present and I bear witness together with my
30    companions — Georgios son of Elias, I was present and I bear witness.
