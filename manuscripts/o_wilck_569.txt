[TRANSLATION]
1     Kametis, collector of money-taxes, to Pachnoumis (son of) Paouphtheus.
2     I have received for poll-tax and bath-tax of year 18, base drachmas eight, total base drachmas 8. Year 19
3     of Hadrian Caesar the lord, Phamenoth …
4     Another (payment), the 29th, in the name of the same, for poll-tax and bath-tax of year 19,
5     four drachmas, total base drachmas 4.
6     I, Herieus, have signed.
