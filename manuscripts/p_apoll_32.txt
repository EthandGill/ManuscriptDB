[TRANSLATION]
1     … the letter [I received] after I came to Antinoou on the fifth of the month,
2     containing (the request) for cypress-wood … and I marveled that it came laden to …; after I
3     sent the cy[pres]s-wood … nor again did he write just now what he wants again, but …
4     that man wishing to wait at his house [wi]th his brot[hers] … and (they) wishing
5     to reckon to me the days for the rent, considering that: while we are still writing down to him there pass
6     twenty days, and while they are still coming up another mo[nt]h passes. Then for the writing and replying there pass two
7     months, and another two month[s] they want for the refitting, so that four months are reckoned, which indeed does not happen,
8     God willing. And on this account I said to him, when he tol[d m]e [th]a[t he wi]shes to refit the boat, that he should let it (go)
9     laden to my house, and at … he would find his need im[medi]ately, and he should not delay at his house;
10    bu[t] he would not endure to listen to me, expecting to remain at [his house] and to reckon to me the days;
11    so that I may not write much: henceforth I do not send him cypr[es]s-wood, nor do I undertake to send (it); but if he finds (it possible) to buy
12    the other planks which he wants, let him buy (them) wherever he finds (them) here … from the cargo; and if not, let him put into it
13    the other plank[s of ac]acia, and let your [es]teemed brotherhood make him send down to me the same boat
14    immediately, having been refitted. And I wrote to her the day before yesterday through the son of Enoch [the] soldier, and if she received (it),
15    To my esteemed brother Papas the pagarch of Apollonos Ano, Helias notary.
