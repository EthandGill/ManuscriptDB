[TRANSLATION]
1   Account of the apostoleus (dispatcher) Triadelphos.
2   3,400 artabas of wheat at 21 drachmas (each): 712 drachmas.
3   To the same, as on the 1,000 (artabas) at 4 drachmas: 12 drachmas.
4   For freight of 171 artabas of wheat carried out at the unloading of the dried-out (grain),
5   as on the 100 at 4 drachmas: 6 drachmas.
6   Price of a jar sent to the assistants and a soldier: 8 drachmas 1 obol.
7   To the elders of Ophis for the wage of 11 workers
8   loading the cargo: 6 drachmas 2 obols.
9   To Aphynchis the granary-guard for the provisions of those
10   from (the month) Tybi, another 18 drachmas.
11   Total of expenditure: 762 drachmas 3 obols, of which the half...
12   Of the consignment of Horion son of Ammonios: 1,500 artabas
13   and of the consignment of Pausiris son of Apollonios: 500 artabas, total: 2,000 artabas,
14   at 21 drachmas: 420 drachmas.
15   For the sailors' (fee) of Horion the helmsman, the (sums) given: 8 drachmas.
16   Price of a jar sent to the assistants and a soldier: 8 drachmas 1 obol.
17   Price of 2 other jars sent to the sailors: 16 drachmas 2 obols.
18   Price(?) of vegetables for the same men, apart from bread(?): 4 obols.
19   To the elders of Ophis for the wage of 7 workers
20   loading the cargo: 4 drachmas.
21   To 1 worker assisting at the half-(artaba?) and
22   loading up the grain, for 3½ days: 5 drachmas.
23   Total of expenditure: 466 drachmas, of which the half...
24   Grand total of expenditure altogether: 1,228 drachmas 3 obols, of which the half...
25   614 drachmas 2 obols.
26   To Dioskoras, of which he gives account: 3 obols.
27   Price of 2 jars consumed by us alone
28   from Phamenoth 27 until Pharmouthi 15: 16 drachmas 2 obols.
29   Small(?) expenses together with the price of oil for the same
30   days: 8 drachmas 4 obols.
