[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 15th year of our lord Gallienus
2     Augustus, Phaofi 27, in the metropolis
3     of the Arsinoite [nome]. [He] acknowledges [to have] sold
4     Aurelius Julianus son of Octaeus, of Hippo, of Palestine, aged 36,
5     [with a] scar on the right shin, to Aurelius Ptolemaeus son of Pancrates from the village Karanis [a] male black ass foal
6     unbroke, at the price of silver drachmas five hundred,
7     comes to drachmas 500,
8     and when questioned [they] agreed.
