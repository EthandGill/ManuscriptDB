[TRANSLATION]
1     Chairemon, son of E[u]daimon, to Anubion, son of Sarapion,
2     greetings. I have leased to you for only the present
3     year, the 8th year of Hadrian Caesar the lord, the existing
4     to me in the Peri-p[o]lin below, from Xenophantus,
5     [a] plot of four [a]rouras for wood-gathering and fodder
6     and fenugreek and cutting—the rent of eac[h] arou[ra]
7     of that which in sowing will appear [per] [each]
8     aroura, silver thirty-one drachmas,
9     becoming per each aroura silver thirty-one drachmas, which you shall give
10    to me in the Epeiph month of the same year,
11    without delay, of all public charges
12    bei[ng] towards me, the resident. Year 8
13    of the Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus,
14    Thoth, 20th.
