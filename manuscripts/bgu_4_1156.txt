[TRANSLATION]
1     … year …
2     To Achaios, the one in charge of the court in the courtyard,
3     from Gaius Julius Philios and from Nilos, son of …
4     and his wife Zois, daughter of … and the
5     son of both of them, Eirenaios also called Dorotheos,
6     with the guardian of Zois, her own husband. Concerning the matters
7     at variance, Nilos and Zois agree, and also
8     Eirenaios, that they have from Gaius Philios a loan both by
9     hand and through the money-changing bank of Kastor,
10    one hundred and eighty drachmas of Ptolemaic silver,
11    interest-free, which they will repay in six months from the new moon of
12    Mecheir of the present fifteenth year of Caesar, giving
13    each day toward the aforesaid principal the proper one drachma punctually, making no default,
14    until Gaius Philios is paid in full with the
15    one hundred and eighty drachmas of silver, and they will do this
16    without trial and any dispute; and if they default in any of the daily
17    payments, they shall be liable to seizure, not having awaited
18    the term allotted to them, and shall be held until they have paid in full
19    the whole principal or whatever remains owing with the addition of one half
20    at once, and for the time overrun the interest of two drachmas according to the diagramma,
21    the exaction being made for Gaius Philios both from those three men, who are mutually liable
22    for the payment, and from one and from whomever he chooses and from the property
23    of them all just as by legal judgment, and until full settlement it shall not be permitted
24    to them to bring forward pledges nor to flee for refuge to …
25    nor to a place of asylum nor to an ordinance of the benevolences or of the (imperial) administration
26    nor to any other protection whatsoever; or whatever they may resort to,
27    let them be void. And the (loan) which the two
28    of the aforesaid, Nilos and Zois, together with Sarapion, son of
29    Sosos, have additionally brought forward, another loan-agreement through the same court
30    in Hathyr of the same fifteenth year, to Gaius Philios, of one hundred
31    twenty drachmas, is to be void … through …
32    … and the further …
33    that he will not proceed against the principal of the one hundred twenty drachmas of silver
34    one hundred and eighty …
