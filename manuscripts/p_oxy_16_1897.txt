[TRANSLATION]
1     To the illustrious house of the most-renowned patrician, through you the most-magnificent
2     Count Antronicus, landholder, Philoxenus the weigher, son of the blessed
3     Paul. I agree [that I] am ready firmly to give to the same
4     illustrious house through your magnificence on behalf of those from the
5     village Popano [as] pretext of the reason of the removed from
6     Pakerkis of the estate by those from Popano gold [co]ins
7     eighteen, full [weight] of Alexandria, on the new moon of Thoth month
8     of the with-god eleventh indiction. It was written in the month Mesore
9     23rd, indiction 10. The same
10    Philoxenus agrees with me this in-his-own-hand [document] and I will settle what is in it
11    without delay and without any contradiction and for the security
12    of the same illustrious house and of those from Pakerkis I have made
13    the present [document] in-my-own-hand written wholly by my hand as stated.
14    In-own-hand [document] of Andronicous, [of] coins 18 of Alexandria until Thoth 1, indiction 11.
