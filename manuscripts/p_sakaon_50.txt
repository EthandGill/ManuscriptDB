[TRANSLATION]
1     while tending his [sheep]. being unable to easily carry his body, and not
2     to examine his death,
3     not knowing how he died, without assurance,
4     [for which reason] I [submit] to you these documents providing security about [this] in order that you can know.
5     Prosper.
6     of the consulship of Gallicanus and [Bassus], the
7     most illustrious, Tybi 13.
8     I, Aurelius Sakaon, have submitted it as aforementioned.
9     I, Aurelius A...ios, wrote on behalf of him [being] illiterate.
