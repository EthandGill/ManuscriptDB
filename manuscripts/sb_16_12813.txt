[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Year …, Phamenoth 18. Notification to Horos, archiphylakitês of Arsinoë near Sebennytos, and to the epistatai of Karanis. On the 8th of the same month, I arrived with my attendants in the aforementioned village so as to perform the grain-measurement for Posidonius the sitologos as ordered. Inspecting the corn which was in the sacks, we found that a plunder had occurred to a considerable amount of wheat. I told him to meet (me). He wrote the same thing to Timokrates the epistates.
