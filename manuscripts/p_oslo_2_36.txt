1     Alexas [son] of Herieus of Aphrodisios, from
2     the village Theadelphia, having become [a] hired [worker] of palm groves around the village
3     of Helen [daughter] of Harpokyration, [concerning] arurae
4     half of one-quarter of one-eighth to He[r]on
5     of Dioskoros, now [the] hired [worker] of [the]
6     aforementioned palm grove, greetings.
7     I acknowledge [by] necessity [the] reconstruction
8     to have made of the aforementioned palm grove [wooden] posts, which also I will hand over
9     completed until Mesore, [the] nineteenth of the present year,
10    because concerning these things then [I have been] hired
11    me. Alexas, as approximately fifty years old, [with a] scar on [the] left eyebrow,
12    was portrayed through Didymos, [the] legal-scribe
13    of Euhemerias. [Year] 8 of Autocrator Caesar
14    Titus Ailius Hadrianos Antoninos Augustus
15    Pious, Epeiph 25th.
