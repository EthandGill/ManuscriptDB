[TRANSLATION]
1   Fl(avius) Souchtas(?), soldier of the le-
2   gion, the fifth Macedonian,
3   of those stationed at Memphis
4   under the tribune ..., to Atrius son of Apio-
5   n, peace-officer of the village of Philadelphia
6   of the Arsinoite nome, greeting.
7   I acknowledge that I have received from you
8   your donkey, and
9   I have no claim against you,
10   neither I nor those who might raise
11   a pretext on its behalf, that is, Aga-
12   thokles; and I have given you my
13   pledge for security.
14   I, Fl(avius) Souchidas, the afore-
15   said: it is agreeable to me
16   as aforesaid, and those who might raise a pretext;
17   having subscribed.
