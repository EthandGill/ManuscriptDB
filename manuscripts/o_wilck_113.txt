[TRANSLATION]
1   Vallarion and the other lessees
2   of the Sacred Gate of Syene,
3   through Sarapion the scribe. Paid:
4   Pachnoubis son of Petorzmethis,
5   mother Thinpoueris, for the poll-tax of the 2nd year
6   of Hadrian the lord:
7   eight drachmas — total 8 dr. Year 3
8   of Hadrian the lord,
9   Pharmouthi 8.
