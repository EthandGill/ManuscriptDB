[TRANSLATION]
1     To Thaisarion daughter of Longinus, with her guardian, her kinsman
2     Apollonios son of Herakleides,
3     from Sotas son of Ision son of Sambas, his mother being Isarous,
4     registered in the Thesmophorion quarter: I wish to lease from you for six years from the
5     coming seventeenth year of Antoninus Caesar
6     the lord, the catoecic-holding land belonging to you around the village of Arsinoe in the Herakleides division,
7     three arouras or however many there may be, in two seals: in
8     the first seal two arouras in the place called …teleis, and in the second
9     seal the remaining aroura is in the place called …elmoni…, at a rent for the whole, each year, unsown, of ten artabas of wheat
10    by the dromos four-choinix measure of the granary formerly of Pasion and others, each year
11    twenty choinikes of bread, free of deduction and
12    free of risk; and it shall not be permitted me to abandon
13    the lease within the term, nor …
14    … within the term, but
15    … all the works of the arouras, all the works of the arouras from my
16    own resources, doing no damage, sowing each year whatever crops I choose except safflower, in this last year the same arouras …
17    reap… of wheat; and the yearly rents I will pay to you in the month Pauni … of the holding,
18    of wheat … and to the public treasury … …
19    … … … …
20    … being chargeable to Thaisarion, and after the term I will hand over the soil as also
21    I received it, if it seems good to lease on the
22    aforesaid terms.
23    Sotas, about 32 years old, with a scar on the right knee.
24    Year 16 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
25    Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius,
26    Mesore …
