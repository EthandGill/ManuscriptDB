15    ta … of the commanded …

17    of the month Phaophi, to the royal secretary
18    … of the 24th year, of silver talents 500 (?) …
19    do. But if not clearly and within the appointed time the necessary things …
20    that man, for the exaction from you …
21    handed over, and there is nothing that is
22    called belatedly circumscribed by him, he would have withheld the giving
23    of the silver previously disbursed to him by you. And take care
24    for the future that nothing be done contrary to what has been agreed.
25    The 25th year, Choiak 20.
26    Of another. Plautius Italus to the strategoi and
27    royal secretaries of the nomes written below,
28    greeting. In examining the silver
29    accounts I found that some of the strategoi
30    and royal secretaries had for some time taken up
31    salaries (salaria) for themselves through their own hands,
32    using rashness rather than obedience to what had been ordered; and to those I sent the necessary instructions,
33    but to all in common I proclaim this a second time:
34    without being permitted,
35    not to lay hold of the imperial funds.
