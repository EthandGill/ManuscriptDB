[TRANSLATION]
1     Just as your magnificence deigned to write to me concerning the little
2     opium, God is my witness, I sought very hard from the physician and scarcely
3     after many labors I found two ounces and quickly I sent
4     to your magnificence through the guard letter-carrier.
5     And I salute and bow down to your greatness,
6     to the most magnificent and renowned count Eulogius, from Isaac, the least monk
