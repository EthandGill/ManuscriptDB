[TRANSLATION]
1     I Frange who am [doing] penance greeting sending to my beloved of the Lord
2     Brothers that they may know him Abba Ananias I truly speak it
3     In my heart entirely that [this] is need
4     It happened in heaven you say to me
5     Before one work of you, your command all—shall grant you now
6     [Since] the work please you, you say
7     I say to my brother toward Napoulo indeed [I] gave you sight
8     Say my brother toward Lapouke indeed you gave me hope to see
9     [And] he would give them [those] who die indeed a greeting in
10    The Lord
