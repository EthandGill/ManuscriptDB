[TRANSLATION]
1   […] … month … therefore I write to you,
2   so you may know. As yet we have done nothing.
3   And if the adversary comes up, keep watch on him;
4   for I fear he may slip away, for he has grown sick of it.
5   About Sarapas our son: he has not lodged
6   with me at all, but went off to the camp
7   to enlist. You did not do well
8   to advise him to enlist;
9   for when I tell him not to enlist, he says to me:
10   “My father told me to enlist.”
11   About Epaphroditos: he is here with me.
12   Now please — I have, in the middle room,
13   lentils; send them to me, and a chous of radish
14   oil, so that I have my month’s provisions here; for I
15   am not downhearted, but keep my courage and stay on.
16   And if the allotment is flooded, be quick … and
17   sow it well. I gave you word about the
18   produce … once …
19   … a letter to the prefect … us, so that
20   it be measured into the public granary and given
21   for seed …
22   [Year … of Clau]dius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Mesore 22.
23   […] so greet his mother and Demetris and his children
24   […] and Aparosi… …
25   [… and] his mother and Dionyseia and her children and […]
26   […] her … and the mother …
27   [N.N., daughter of Dem]etrios, to Sarapion her father.
