[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermaios to Aelius Apollonios
2     to the most honored, greetings.
3     and in this and in the
4     all other things, lord, the goodwill fitting to you [you] provide to me
5     and friendship [you provide]; and small though I am to know [your] grace, the gods will repay you.
6     The greeting, as you ordered and showed, I sent to you and I would have come, lord, if
7     not you, having anticipated, and concerning
8     the model you wrote, and to the
9     god me I dedicated, by whom the
10    obeisance of you I make with all [of] yours. Fare
11    you I pray, lord
12    mine.
13    To Aelius Apollonios, gymnasiarch
