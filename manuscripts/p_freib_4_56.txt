[TRANSLATION]
1     Heracleides to Helena greetings
2     Since on the eighth from you I became ill and I am unable to come down,
3     You will do well, having taken care for the reed-pipe to be worked, having supplied the
4     wages until whenever I come down. And send me the
5     carriers so that they may carry down to you the sufficient ropes. From Alexandria …
6     To Helena from Heracleides
