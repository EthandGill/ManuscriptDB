1     Truly, with God commanding, lord Papas, [your God-protected brotherliness] cannot cast us … …
2     because I wrote to her to send me the adversaries (at law) of Sabinos who [ought to come down to me;]
3     she went and cast them into a fishing-boat without a morsel of bread, an[d] …
4     and they fled and went up and did not come to me at all. But …
5     almost (?) let her not wait for one moment unless she cast [them] … [(and) send]
6     them to me wherever I am, (they) being obliged to come down …
7     her passage; and, with God commanding, henceforth above let (her) lea[rn] …
8     thus it holds us, for not even whenever she also wishes to cast us …
9     let her immediately send to me, through a soldier of (the place) of Apollon, the one ob[liged] …
10    but let her cast their wives into prison until he r[eceives a letter from my lord the most glorious]
11    locum-tenens (topoteretes) that they have settled their affair; and t…
12    if he himself also wishes to come, let him come, since it is hers, the …
13    but the persons toward the letter already sent …
14    but at all events until they overtake me wherever I am ei-… [concerning the]
15    their evil appearance, it was written to me again yesterday …
16    that he expel these (men) before he himself goes up. …
17    [To my God-protected lord and brother] Papas the pagarch, (from) Helladios, notary.
18    Concerning the adversaries (at law) of Sabinos.
