[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Arsinoe, the Brother-Gods, year 10, the priest of Alexander and the Brother-Gods and the Benefactor-Gods being Apollonides son of Moschion, the basket-bearer
2     of Arsinoe Philadelphos being Menekrateia daughter of Philammon, the 2nd year,
3     in the month … , in Krokodilon polis of the Arsinoite (nome),
4     the following did Philon son of Herakleides, a Cyrenaean of the epigone, being sound in mind and understanding, dispose, being about 45 years old, honey-coloured, of middle size,
5     … having a scar on the forehead above the right eyebrow.
6     May it be mine, while in health, to administer my own affairs; but if I suffer anything human (i.e. die), I leave all my possessions
7     to my own wife Eirene daughter of Asklepiades, a Cyrenaean,
8     and to my own daughter Demo, the daughter by Eirene, and the
9     slave-persons, female: Parthenion, Myrine, male:
10    … and, according to the subscription in public record, Apollonion
11    … a Syrian, a resident alien, who in Syrian is called Jonathan,
12    who owes me 150 drachmas of silver. To no one else do I leave anything.
13    As guardians I choose King Ptolemy
14    the son of Ptolemy and Arsinoe the Brother-Gods, and Queen
15    Berenice the sister and wife of King Ptolemy
16    and their children. Witnesses: Tlepolemos son of Kleodemos
17    … of the epigone, about 56 years old, white-skinned, with straight hair …
18    … a scar above the right eye; Telesarchos son of Aleximachos
19    … of the epigone, about 53 years old, white-skinned, with curly hair, bald in front, a scar on the tip of the nose and others beneath the chin …
20    … of the epigone, about … years old …
21    … somewhat short-sighted …
22    … th … ach …
