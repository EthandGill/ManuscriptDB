[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year after the consulship of our lord
2     [Flavius] Justin the eternal Augustus, year
3     … [the] tenth, and of Flavius Tiberius New Constantine the most gentle Caesar, year the third,
4     Mecheir 14, of the tenth indiction, in Arsinoiton Polis.
5     [Aurelius] Serenos son of Kyrikos, with the guarantor and surety of the repayment declared hereafter, namely of me, Aurelius
6     Petros son of Neilos, maker of light pottery,
7     [from] Arsinoiton Polis, from the quarter of Moeris, to Aurelius Palatinos
8     son of Kalos, from the same city, greeting.
9     I acknowledge that I have received from you
10    [by hand and that I have been paid] the
11    [price of new vessels] …
12    … [and the] repayment of these I shall make in the
13    month Epeiph, at the beginning of the (with God)
14    incoming eleventh indiction,
15    [at] the light-pottery workshop under me
16    [which is] in the plain of the hamlet
17    … of the Arsinoite nome,
18    [without] delay, out of all my
19    property; and having been asked the formal question, I assented.
20    Aurelius Serenos son of Kyrikos, with the guarantor Aurelius Petros son of Neilos, the aforesaid,
21    agrees to the things written, as is set forth. Aurelius
22    Ioustos son of Petros, I wrote on his behalf, he being present and illiterate.
23    … [of] Serenos son of Kyrikos, [with the guarantor and surety Petros son of Neilos], maker of light pottery, of new vessels
24    … of the … kouri … chn.
25    … isth … tab.
