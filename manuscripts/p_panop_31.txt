[TRANSLATION]
1     He wrongs the same aged man.
2     A certain Hierakapollon, a councillor
3     of this city,
4     being his neighbour in the fields,
5     tore away 5 arouras of the man being aided,
6     in the present year,
7     in the month before, using violence;
8     since he himself pays in full
9     for the land,
10    he asks that it be restored to him,
11    and further also the rents,
12    with the aid of Hesychius, one of the ex-exactors,
13    and, should he be shameless, that he be sent to the court.
14    Narratio (statement): you speak on behalf of Sansnos from Panos.
15    This old man was once in need of money
16    for his own private needs, and he offered as security to one Heron, a councillor of this
17    city, 9 arouras, having borrowed from him
18    money. A little later he paid off
19    the debt and recovered the security,
20    and out of these 9 arouras, four
21    he sold to Hierakapollon the
22    former procurator; and the possession
23    of the remaining 5 arouras he kept,
24    … paying all the imperial dues.
25    But a certain other Hierakapollon, being a neighbour in the fields
26    to the old man, in the
27    present year attempted to tear away the land of this man who pays
28    all the imperial dues. Therefore he asks
29    your Illustriousness to give orders that the
30    land be restored to him, with the aid
31    of Hesychius, one of the ex-exactors, and the
32    rents owed from the same man;
33    and, should he be shameless, that he be sent
34    to the court.
