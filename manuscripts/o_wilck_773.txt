1     There measured into the granary of the administration,
2     of the produce of year 1, Panameus (son of) Hermias,
3     of wheat two and a half and a third and a twelfth artabas, total wheat 2½⅓ 1/12.
4     Year 1 of Vespasian Caesar the lord,
5     Mesore 3; others, the 22nd, of wheat a quarter, total wheat ¼,
6     total wheat 3 1/6. (2nd hand) …, the 29th, of wheat 1/12, total wheat 3¼.
