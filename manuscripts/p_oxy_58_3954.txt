[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of our master Jesus
2     Christ, God and Savior. In the reign
3     of our most divine and most pious master
4     Flavius Heraclius, the eternal Augustus
5     and Emperor, in the first year,
6     Mecheir 18, in the 14th indiction.
7     To Flavius Georgios, the most illustrious
8     administrator of the glorious house
9     of the all-praiseworthy patrician
10    Apion, of this Upper city of the Kynopolites,
11    on his father's side, of the blessed
12    from the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
13    we, the Aurelii: Makaris
14    the elder, son of Jeremias, whose mother is
15    Maria, and Joannes son of
16    Origenes, whose mother is Maria, and
17    Menas son of Anoup, whose mother is Martyria,
18    and Abraham son of Phounsios, whose mother is
19    Rebecca, and Menas son of Georgios,
20    whose mother is Sophia, and Origenes son of
21    Stephanos, whose mother is Rebecca,
22    all from the village of Apsempsis
23    of the Upper Kynopolite nome, greeting.
24    We acknowledge that we have received and
25    obtained and been paid in full
26    from your illustriousness
27    the price of the wine sold
28    to her by us, to the (last) single obol,
29    that is, two hundred Samian (jars) of wine,
30    upon the crops of the present
31    fourteenth indiction,
32    to the (last) single obol, that is, of wine, Samian (jars)
33    two hundred. We acknowledge that henceforth we have no
34    claim against your magnificence, because
35    we have received and obtained
36    and been paid in full by her
37    the price of the wine sold
38    to her by us, to the (last) single obol,
39    and as proof of the payment of the price
40    of the said wine
41    we have issued to her this discharging
42    receipt, which …
43    … and, having been formally questioned, we acknowledged it. There comes to be
44    year 287, 256, in the 14th indiction. We, the Aurelii: Makaris
45    the elder and Joannes and Menas and Abraham
46    [and Menas] and Origenes, the aforewritten.
47    Receipt of those from Apsempsis of the Upper Kynopolite nome.
