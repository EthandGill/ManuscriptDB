[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting, Libys, shipmaster of (the boat) of Archidamos and Metrophanes,
2     a kerkouros of a capacity of 10,000 (artabas). When I was carrying letters into the Thebaid, it happened,
3     a storm having arisen off Aphrodite's city, that the yard-arm of the boat was damaged,
4     so that I could no longer be brought up to where I was conveying the letters; and since the Arsinoite nome was close by, we, having had
5     much trouble, barely dragging the boat, brought it to the harbour of
6     the Arsinoite (nome), since it was no longer possible to use the sails. Now in order that we may not be ruined here, it being customary, if some such thing happens to any of the shipmasters,
7     to report it to the strategoi in charge of the districts,
8     so that the boats may not be ruined in the districts and detriment arise to the conveyance of the grain,
9     but that it may be loaded under the letters given to them from the
10    city, I beg you, king, to give orders to Diophanes the strategos to investigate concerning these matters and, if what I write is true, to direct Euphranor the sitologos
11    of the lower division to load the boat under the existing letters from the districts in his charge as quickly as possible,
12    since the boat is large and, the water receding, it may not be possible, even empty, for the boat
13    to be brought to the city, but that through you,
14    king, we may obtain (relief) …
15    Farewell.
16    … I swear by king Ptolemy and queen Berenike and Sarapis and Isis and …
17    Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13.
18    [Libys], shipmaster, concerning
19    the loading of his boat
20    from the nome.
