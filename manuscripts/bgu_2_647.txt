[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a report.
2     To Protarchos, strategos of the Arsinoite, Herakleides division.
3     Gaius Menikios Valerianus, having a surgery in the village
4     of Karanis, and Phaesis son of Zenas and Esouris son of Kastor, the two
5     elders of the village. It was enjoined upon us by Herakleides
6     the assistant to inspect the condition of (the man) under Mystharion son of Kameis, in the presence of him and of his brother Petesouchos, and
7     having appeared before you we reported as follows, swearing the fortune of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus: on the one hand
8     Gaius Menikios Valerianus (declared) that on the fifth day of
9     Mystharion's having been struck, he had given wound-treatment over
10    the left temple of the head, a wound, a fracture
11    in depth, in which I found small fragments of stone; and on the other hand
12    Phaesis and Esouris, together with Menikios, (declared) that they had inspected the aforesaid wound, or may we be liable to the
13    oath. It was written through Aphrodisios, law-scribe of Karanis, Herakleides the assistant attending.
14    … a … i … k … i
15    Phaesis, about 80 years old, with a scar on his left shin.
16    E[s]ouris, about 45 years old, with a scar on his head …
17    Gaius Menikios Valerianus, having the surgery in the village of Karanis, swearing the aforesaid oath that on the fifth
18    day of Mystharion's having been struck, he had given wound-treatment
19    over the left temple of the head, a wound,
20    a fracture in depth, in which I found small fragments of stones, or may I be liable to the oath.
21    Year 14 of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus,
22    Mes[or]e the twenty-ninth. I, Herakleides son of Herakleides, the assistant, have attended.
