[TRANSLATION]
1     … to …
2     [From] … son of … of the Oxyrhynchite
3     [nome. I wish to purchase from] the public (treasury), free of any deficit … of holdings (kleroi) that have been taken over, of those up to
4     [the present] fourth year of [Nero] Claudius Caesar that have become dry,
5     near … of the [middle] toparchy of the same nome: from
6     dry corn-bearing land into corn-bearing land, one aroura; and near the
7     hamlet of Hermokrates of the … toparchy of the same nome, three arouras,
8     and near the hamlet of Istros of the same toparchy, likewise four arouras,
9     and near N…a likewise three arouras, and near Sinaru and Talao likewise
10    seven arouras, and near Epie likewise four arouras, and near Thmoinepsobthis
11    likewise three arouras; total in all 25 arouras, on condition that, when these have been pointed out to me, I shall pay through the
12    local public bank the determined price for each aroura
13    of 12 silver drachmas, and I shall have, for the bringing-up and working of these, exemption for three years
14    from the fifth year of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
15    after which I shall pay, of each aroura that proves to be under crop, one artaba of wheat
16    and the embankment-tax; and I shall not be troubled at all for anything else,
17    and there shall remain to me and my descendants and those who receive from me the holding and ownership of these things, inalienable forever. If then it seems good, it will be ordered to those to whom it pertains to carry out each thing in the manner that is fitting.
