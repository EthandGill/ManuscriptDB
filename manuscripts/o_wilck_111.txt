[TRANSLATION]
1   Sanmous, collector. Petorzmetis paid,
2   son of Pachompabienchis (grandson) of Psyros, deacon(?), for the price
3   of the state palm(-dates), of the produce of the 2nd year of Hadrian
4   the lord: fifteen silver drachmas, four
5   and a half obols — total 15 dr. 4½ ob. Year 3
6   of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
7   Hathyr 15.
