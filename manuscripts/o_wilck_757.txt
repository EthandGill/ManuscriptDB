[TRANSLATION]
1     Damon son of Bakchios to Didymos son of Aphrodi[sios],
2     greeting. I have received from you of wine
3     sixteen keramia, which I gave you
4     together with the surcharge of year 12 which
5     is also year 9, and I make no claim against you
6     concerning the aforesaid
7     sixteen keramia of wine. There wrote
8     on his behalf Argaias son of Archios, having been requested (to do so),
9     because he (Damon) writes rather slowly.
10    (2nd hand) I, Damon, consent to the
11    aforesaid.
