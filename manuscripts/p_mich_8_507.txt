[TRANSLATION]
1   Artemis to Sokrates, greetings.
2   Before all things I pray <for you> to be in health.
3   I arrived at the city on the 9th.
4   I ask you, if you are able, sen-
5   d me one of you (pl.), because I have ne-
6   ed that you become my legal representative (ekdikos);
7   for it is not permitted for a woman without
8   a representative to go to law. Know
9   that, if you (pl.) help, the matter has a sa-
10   fe outcome. So that it may come quickly,
11   learn, then, that if
12   I am harmed, you too will
13   be harmed; but if we win,
14   the matter is yours (to your benefit).
15   We greet your mother
16   Thatres and Achillas and Sarapion
17   and Ptolemais and Kastor and
18   Tasoucharion. I pray for your health.
19   Deliver [...] ☓ from Harpakysis(?).
