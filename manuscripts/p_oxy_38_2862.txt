r.1    Λουκιλλιανὸς Διδύμῳ καὶ Ἡρακλείδῃ
r.2    τοῖς τιμιωτάτοις χαίρειν.

r.4    τὸ πρᾶγμα αἰτησάμενος. μαρτυρήσει δὲ ὑμῖν καὶ ὁ ὑφʼ ὑμῶν διαπεμφθεὶς ὅτι

r.7    Διδύμῳ ἐξηγητ … παρὰ Λουκιλλιανοῦ.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Lucillianus to Didymos and Herakleides
2     to the most honored, greetings.
3     The matter concerning which you wrote to me I completed, having spent nothing on it, but rather
4     having requested the matter. And there will bear witness to you also the one sent by you, that
5     I did not hesitate. Greet the good Ptolemais and your children.
6     With whom I pray that you might be strong in all the gods.
7     To Didymos ... from Lucillianus.
