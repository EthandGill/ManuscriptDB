1     Of year 1 of Autocrator Caesar
2     Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus
3     and Autocrator Caesar Lucius
4     Aurelius Uerus Augustus, he worked for
5     dikes of the same year 1, Mesore 13, 17, in the
6     Bubastis canal (of) Soknopaiou, Sotas, slave
7     of Tapakyses
8     [D]idymos, I have signed.
