[TRANSLATION]
1   Harpaesis son of Phenophis, Thrakidas, paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the eighth year of Trajan
3   Caesar the lord: seventeen silver drachmas,
4   total 17 dr., together with those I had received before.
5   I, Hermogenes son of Ammonas,
6   the collector, wrote (this), Mesore 20.
