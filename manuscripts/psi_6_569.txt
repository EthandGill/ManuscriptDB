1     [Phili]nos to Zenon greetings. It would be well if you were well; [I am well]
2     [and] myself. I have sent you cages, which […]
3     and 60 cones, 22 pomegranates, and a male red-colored [bird],
4     [and] his sister reddish-black, [and] another phoenix-bird […]
5     [of] the Nausinician [breed], and his sister black eagle-eyed […]
6     […], of these one ashen, another male ashen sharp-eyed,
7     [and] his sister white reddish, another male ashen and two black females reddish, one black female with the [tail]
8     [br]oken, another large bronze-winged one with all-white [eye]
9     having, [and] 4 black females and one sprig. You would do well [to]
10    give the double-cloaks to Moschos [who is] from me, and the travel goods to Libanos [who is]
11    from Telestus. Farewell. Year 33, Tybi […].
12    [Year 33,] [Tybi] 5 Philinos to Zenon
13    […] [concerning] the things he sent.
