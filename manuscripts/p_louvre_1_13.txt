[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fifteenth year of Tiber[ius]
2     [Caesa]r [Aug]ustus, in the month Xandikos the thirteenth, Mecheir 13, in Soknopaiou Nesos
3     of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite
4     nome. Satabous also called
5     Sisois son of Satabous, about thirty years old, with a scar on the right cheek, acknowledges to Stotoetis son of Horos,
6     about thirty years old, [with a scar] on the right wrist,
7     that he has sold to him the
8     donkey foal belonging to him, not yet having cast its teeth, white in colour, such as it is, not to be returned,
9     and that Satabous has received from
10    Stotoetis the agreed
11    price in full, complete, and that Satabous will warrant (it) to Stotoetis
12    with every warranty just as by
13    legal judgement. Subscriber for the one acknowledging is Laomedon son of Petesouchos,
14    about thirty years old, with a scar on
15    the right shin. The contract is valid, being produced (in court).
16    I, Satabous also called Sisois son of Satabous, acknowledge that I have sold to Stotoetis son of Horos the donkey belonging to me,
17    a foal not yet having cast its teeth, white in colour, such as it is,
18    not to be returned, and I have received the price and will warr[ant] (it) as set forth above. There wrote [on his] behalf
19    [Lao]medon son of Petesouchos, because he does not know letters.
20    Mecheir 13. Registered through the record-office in Soknopaiou Nesos.
21    Agreement of sale of a donkey, of Satabous
22    to Stotoetis.
