1     … [account] of me, Menas, scribe of the village Teritthos, through the lord
2     [M]en[as] shipowner from Koma. I received from John, the superintendent of Ko[ma]
3     … for the assessment of the fourteenth indiction of dirty grain
4     of the public new [vessel …] one hundred thirty artabas alone, becoming artabas 130 alone
5     written in the month of Mesore, [day] 5, indiction 14, thus: Menas, scribe of the lord Menas
6     shipowner from Koma, I have recorded likewise Mesore 6, dirty grain [standard […]]
7     artabas forty-five, measures three, becoming altogether all artabas 175, measures 3, alone.
8     [through] me Menas, scribe of the lord Menas shipowner, the [account] is verified to me of one hundred
9     seventy-five and measures 3, public new [standard]. It is verified to me as it stands.
10    [Account] of Menas, scribe of Teritthos.
