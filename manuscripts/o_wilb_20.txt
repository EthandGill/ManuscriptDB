[TRANSLATION]
1     Kalaseir, a tax-collector. Pemphas, also …, erased.
2     Phamais, son of Herieutes, mother Soheris, division
3     of the fishery and river-guard station, year 15 of Hadrian
4     of the lord, base drachmas 3, tetrobol, Phamenoth 19
5     Hikkienos wrote for him, (he) not knowing letters.
