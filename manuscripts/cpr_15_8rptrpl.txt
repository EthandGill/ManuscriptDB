[TRANSLATION]
1     From Satabous son of Erges the younger,
2     of the priests from the island of Soknopaios:
3     In the 41st year of Caesar I lent to Harpagathes son of Panephrommis, a Persian of the epigone, a priest, according to a deed of loan,
4     325 drachmas of silver as principal. And through
5     the same deed Harpagathes
6     declared that all the privileges falling
7     to him from the temple of the
8     great great god Soknopaios
9     he would not lay hold of these,
10    but that they should be mine, Satabous's, until
11    he repays me the aforesaid principal
12    and the interest. So, since Harpagathes
13    is not willing voluntarily [to repay]
14    the aforesaid principal [and the interest],
15    but is putting me off … [through]
16    your … … [and]
17    I [request], my memorandum having been registered,
18    that the copy [be communicated to the] head of the priests of the god
19    Soknopaios, Satabous
20    son of Horos, so that he may know that, in accordance
21    with the said deed, there remains to me
22    the share falling to Harpagathes
23    of the privileges, as also in other cases
24    is customary, with me being in no way diminished in my right of execution against Harpagathes.
25    execution.
26    Farewell.
