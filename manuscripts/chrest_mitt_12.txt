[TRANSLATION]
1     To the unfailing general from Philip
2     and Ptolemy. We have submitted to you a petition
3     against Pausanias concerning
4     of which we accused him.
5     Since you wrote to the
6     superintendent [to forward him] …
7     [therefore] you to have called in
8     us and having examined what through the petition against him we brought charges to compel
9     him justice
10    to us to promise. For this having occurred
11    not the more time
12    we will be destroyed, but
13    through you of all [kindness we will obtain].
14    [Farewell.]
