[TRANSLATION]
1     if Petros had not come with the letters of the lord Agathemeros, I would not have learned
2     that our held farmer is staying in the estate of the lord
3     Menas the chief physician. Go therefore with the most reverent Apa Horoongchios to
4     Apa Nakios and have him released; for there will be no inquiry
5     from me concerning any damage to the place either to the field-guards or to Apa Nakios
6     himself, whether he is responsible as they say or [someone] else. But if you know him reluctant
7     to release him (which I do not believe [will happen]), receive him and release [him] until I come out
8     and meet with him; [as for] the chaff, see that it be transported. For behold, even now [I have] two
9     baskets sent. But if there is need for you to go [to/toward …-]as Leu[…]
10    [go to] the procurator
