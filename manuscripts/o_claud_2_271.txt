r.1    Πατρεμπαβάθης Ἀπολλινάρι τῷ φιλτάτῳ

[TRANSLATION]
1     Patrempabathe[s] to Apollinaris the dearest
2     very many greetings and your obeisance I make before
3     the gods here and I heard
4     that you are about to come down.
5     You will do well, brother,
6     [having] done [something] for me regarding what
7     you were commissioned [to do]. Bring
8     a small bundle of vegetables
9     from Rufus. Didymus
10    greets you. Make effort to
11    send the piglet to him.
12    I wish you to be well.
