r.1    Αὐρήλιοι Ἑρμοδίδυ[μος Ἀνουβίωνος Ἀγα]θοῦ Δαίμονος τοῦ [καὶ Ἐπι]μάχου καὶ Τιθοητίω[ν ὁ καὶ Σαρα]π[ά]μμων Ἑρμίνου το[ῦ καὶ Ἀχιλλέως] Ἀχιλλέως ἀμφότεροι Ἑρμοπολεῖται ἀναγραφόμενοι ἐπὶ Φρουρίου λιβὸς ἐκ μητρὸς
r.2    Εὐδαιμον[ί]δ[ος] τῆς κα[ὶ Φίντυς] Δημητρίο[υ] ἀλλήλοις χαίρειν. ὁμολογοῦμεν ἀντικατηλλάχεναι πρὸς ἀλλήλους ἐ[ντεῦθ]εν κυρίως καὶ ἀναφαιρέτως καὶ ἀμετανοήτως ἐπὶ τὸν ἅπαντα χρόνον

r.6    ἧ[ς] ὅλης ἡ κυρεία καὶ ἡ ο[ἴ]κησις ὑποστέλλει τῷ αὐτῷ Τιθοητίωνι· τὸν δὲ Τιθοητίωνα τὸν καὶ Σαρ[α]πάμμωνα τὸ ὑπάρ[χον α]ὐτῷ ἕκτον μέρος οἰκίας καὶ συνκυρόντων καὶ χρηστηρίων καὶ ἀνηκόντων πάντων [καὶ εἰσ]όδων καὶ ἐξόδων ἐπὶ το[ῦ αὐτοῦ] ἀμφόδου καταντικρὺ κ[ατ]ηντηκὸς εἰς αὐτὸν Τιθοητίωνα ἀπὸ κληρονομίας τῆς κοινῆς μητρὸς κατὰ μετοχὴν ἄλλου ἕκτου μέρους

[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Hermodidy[mos son of Anoubion son of Aga]thos Daimon also called Epimachos, and Tithoetion also called Sarapammon, son of Herminos also called Achilleus, son of Achilleus, both Hermopolites registered in (the quarter of) the Fort, west, born of the mother
2     Eudaimonis also called Phintys, daughter of Demetrios, to each other, greeting. We acknowledge that we have exchanged with one another from now on, validly and irrevocably and without right of withdrawal, for all time,
3     the (one), Aurelius Hermodidymos, the dwelling and use, granted to him by written security,
4     of the place called the Winter-room in the gateway, and of the inner storeroom of the paternal house
5     of Tithoetion in Hermopolis, in (the quarter of) the City, east, in the lane called of the Basket-weavers,
6     of all of which the ownership and the dwelling-right reverts to the same Tithoetion; and the (other), Tithoetion also called Sarapammon, the sixth share of a house belonging to him and of the appurtenances and amenities and all that pertains and [of the entr]ances and exits in [the same] quarter opposite, which came to him, Tithoetion, by inheritance from their common mother by way of a portion of another sixth share
7     of Hermodidymos; and from now on each of them is to take possession and to use and to administer and to manage that which was exchanged, as has been declared above, for all time, and warranting it perpetually; and Hermodidymos has received from Tithoetion, for the difference of value, two hundred drachmas of silver, from
8     hand to hand, and it shall not be permitted to any of us to transgress this exchange on any
9     just pretext whatever for all time, because we have agreed to these terms. The
10    exchange is valid, which we also wrote in quadruplicate, so that there should be two copies with each party.
11    Year 25 of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus, Parthicus maximus, Britannicus
12    m[aximus, Ger]manicus maximus, Pius Augustus, Tybi 19. I, Aurelius Hermydidymos son of Anoubion, have made the exchange and I consent to all the aforesaid.
13    [I, Aurelius Hermodidymos son of Anoubion, consent] to the publication of the security.
