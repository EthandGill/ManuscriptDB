[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eighth year of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Choiak 22, in
2     Tebtynis of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. Thaësis acknowledges,
3     daughter of Orsenouphis, grandson of Onnophris, her mother being Thenobastis, from the aforementioned village
4     of Tebtynis, about seventy-eight years old, a scar on the right forearm, with her guardian,
5     her own kinsman Kronion son of Ameis, about twenty-seven years old, a scar on the mid-brow,
6     that she, the acknowledging Thaësis, after her own death,
7     has agreed that there shall belong to the daughter born to her from her deceased former husband
8     Pomsais, (namely) Thenpetesouchos, and further also to the son of her deceased
9     other daughter Taorseus, (namely) Sansneus son of Tephersos, to the two: to
10    Thenpetesouchos alone, the (property) belonging to her, Thaësis, in the aforementioned village
11    of Tebtynis, purchased from Thenpetesouchos daughter of Petesouchos, (namely) a house and
12    courtyard and all the appurtenances, and the movables to be left behind by her, Thaësis, and furnishings and household-goods and clothing and
13    (things) owed to her or also others in whatever manner soever; but to Sansneus she has assigned eight silver drachmas, which Sansneus shall recover from [Thenpetesouchos]
14    after the death of Thaësis, on condition that the daughter Thenpetesouchos shall perform the funeral and laying-out of the mother
15    as is fitting and shall discharge whatever private debts Thaësis may appear to owe;
16    and for the time during which the mother Thaësis survives, she is to have it (herself) …
