[TRANSLATION]
1     Horos and the associates demanding division of purchase tax
2     Market 2, from Thaubasthes, Papystios through Senamounios. We received for
3     the division of those withdrawn, one filthy drachma, two and a half obols
4     become one filthy drachma, two and a half obols. Of year 6 of Antoninus
5     Caesar the lord, Pharmouthi 15
6     Pasemis, I have signed
