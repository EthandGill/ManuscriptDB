[TRANSLATION]
1     Either monastic or in the same pagan form ...
2     him not I shall produce to her, I the guarantor from home shall acknowledge concerning him [to make] answer
3     of gold ounces two at my own risk and of my substance, and nothing [to be] falsified,
4     or liable [I] be to the divine oath and to the risk dependent upon this matter, and for all [matters] I have agreed.
5     [J]oseph the elder son of Jacobius the aforementioned I guarantee the aforementioned Colluthus
6     [K]yriacus and I have sworn the divine oath as aforesaid Anupis [son] of Senthahesy the elder
7     [I] testify to the guarantee having heard from the one providing [it]. Aurelius John of Hermopolis [I] testify to the guarantee
8     having heard from the one providing [it].
9     [It] was written through me Victor Apollo[nius]
