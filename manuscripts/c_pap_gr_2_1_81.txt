[TRANSLATION]
1     in [the consul]ships of our lords Constantine
2     and Maximianus of the most illustrious Caesars, the third
3     to Aurelius Sarapion, advocate of the bright and brightest city of the Oxyrhynchites, (in the) region of Pammenes Paradise
4     and others,
5     from Aurelia Tineuto daughter of Lykarion from the same city. The former husband of my daughter Aurelius Eudaimon (son) of Eudaimon, (his) mother Taaphychis from the
6     same city, a wool-merchant, happening to be registered in the region of Pammenes Paradise, up to the 11th of the month Phaophi of the present
7     16th year and 15th year and 8th year departed from life. Therefore,
8     I present this petition requesting that he be released and be received into
9     the rank of the deceased, in order that I might have
10    freedom from disturbance regarding what pertains to the need
11    of the wool-merchants and regarding the poll-tax
12    and I swear the oath customary to Romans not to have lied.
13    In the 16th year and the 15th year of our lord Diocletian
14    [and Maximian]nus the [August]i, and in the [8th year of our lords]
