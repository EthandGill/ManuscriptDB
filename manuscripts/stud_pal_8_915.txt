[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Kyrikas,] oil-seller: provide to Sisinnius pal[likarios] …
2     … on behalf of debts owed to be given to the lord
3     Georgios, of oil four xestes:
4     [it was written in the month of] Pachon the 29th, indiction 5, by me Petterios, deacon and notary of him, it was completed.
