[TRANSLATION]
1     [of the consulate of our lords]
2     Con[stantine Augustus the fifth] and
3     Lic[inius Caesar the first]
4     to Valerius Ammonianos, the also
5     Gerontios, [administering] the accounts of Oxyrhynchus
6     from [the] public [of the] white clothiers of the
7     splendid city of Oxyrhynchus through us Aurelius Maximus Sarmata and Pathermouthios Serapion, monthly magistrates. In accordance with what has been commanded, with our own valuation we declare the following written
8     valuation of which we handle commodities and
9     we swear the divine oath to have falsified nothing. And it is:
10    of linens of all kinds white, of the
11    fine, pound 1, talent 1, denarii 125.
12    of the other common, pound 1, denarii 875.
13    inferior country, pound 1, denarii 500.
14    of the aforesaid consulate, Phamenoth 30.
15    We, Aurelius Maximus and Pathermouthis, have submitted, declaring as is set forth. I, Aurelius Sarmata, wrote on their behalf, they not knowing letters.
