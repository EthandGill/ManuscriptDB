[TRANSLATION]
1     To Antonius Sarapammon, strategos of the Ar[sino]ite (nome),
2     from Aurelii Dion[ysio]s son of Arion and Alypios son of Herodes, both of them
3     grain-collectors of the village of Theadelphia, for the produce of the 8th year and the 6th year and the 4th year,
4     of the landholders, through me Arion, father of Dionysios.
5     We acknowledge, swearing by the fortune of our lords the Emperors,
6     the Augusti, that we have received from those enrolled below
7     and have brought into the granaries of the village, from the produce
8     of the 8th year and the 6th year and the 4th year, of wheat together with the ten percent surcharges, of the landholders, 363 5/6 artabas,
9     of which, of townsmen 33 1/2 1/12 artabas, of villagers 330 1/12 artabas — these make the aforesaid (total) — and of barley
10    likewise from the same 8th year, of the landholders (and) villagers, 61 1/3 1/12 artabas,
11    the transport of all of which we shall make to the river-harbor
12    places; and, having been formally questioned, we acknowledged (it).
13    And it is as follows: of grain,
14    from the produce of the 8th year and the 6th year and the 4th year,
15    of the townsmen:
16    of the village of Theadelphia:
17    Rouphina daughter of Rouphos, through Sakaon a farmer, 18 artabas
18    (the) tax-ticket of Maskoulinos, through Kanis a farmer, 15 1/2 1/12 artabas
19    make (a total) of townsmen 33 1/2 1/12 artabas.
20    Of villagers, likewise of the same 8th year:
21    Paneous, priest, 34 1/2 1/3 artabas
22    Sarapion son of Herakleides, 25 1/2 1/12 artabas
23    Esouris son of Horion, through his son, 28 1/2 1/3 artabas
24    Allion son of Satabous, 22 1/3 artabas
25    Arion son of Dioskoros, through Dioskoros, 2 5/6 artabas
26    Melas son of Herakleides, 26 artabas
27    Sakaon son of Satabous, 23 1/2 artabas
28    Paneous, priest, 19 1/6 artabas
29    Arios son of Dioskoros, 15 artabas
30    Sarapion son of Herakleides, 18 1/12 artabas
31    Ptoleminos son of Euporos, 3 1/12 artabas
32    Esouris son of Horion, 14 1/3 artabas
33    Aeous daughter of Ketis, through Keletous, 3 artabas
34    Arios son of Dioskoros, 1 1/2 artaba
35    Arios son of Dioskoros, through Dioskoros, 1 1/2 1/3 artaba
36    Aeous son of Heron, through Alypios, 16 1/2 artabas
37    Paneous, priest, 6 1/3 1/12 artabas
38    Aunes son of Satabous, 1 1/3 1/12 artaba
39    Paesis son of Satabous, 10 1/2 1/12 artabas
40    Atia, through the heirs of Herakleides, 7 1/4 artabas
41    Paesis son of Satabous, 7 1/2 1/12 artabas
42    Esouris son of Horion, 2 artabas
43    Melas son of Herakleides, 1 1/2 artaba
44    Atia daughter of Dioskoros, through Hatres, 5 artabas
45    Paneous, priest, 3 1/2 artabas
46    Kaet son of Kaetis, 3 1/12 artabas
47    Atiaina, through Dioskoros, 2 1/4 artabas
48    Atia, through Melas a farmer, 6 artabas
49    Zakaon son of Satabous, 10 1/2 1/12 artabas
50    Ptoleminos son of Euporos, 3 1/3 artabas
51    Arios son of Dioskoros, 3 artabas
52    make (a total) of villagers 330 1/12 artabas.
53    They make together of grain 363 5/6 artabas.
54    Of barley, likewise from the same 8th year, of villagers:
55    Atiaina daughter of Dioskoros, through the heirs of Sarapion a farmer, 4 5/6 artabas
56    Sarapion son of Herakleides, 16 3/4 artabas
57    Melas son of Herakleides, 11 artabas
58    Esouris son of Horion, 15 artabas
59    Paneous, priest, 15 artabas
60    they make together of barley 61 1/3 1/12 artabas.
61    In the consulship of our lords Constantine
62    and Licinius, the Augusti, for the 2nd time, Mesore 29.
63    We, Aurelii Dionysios and Alypios, have received the measure of the wheat and barley written above, as is aforesaid. Aurelius
64    Demetrios, I wrote on behalf of them, being illiterate.
