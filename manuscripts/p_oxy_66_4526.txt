[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy. Claudius Herodes, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
2     to Theon and his associates, public bankers of the same nome, greeting. Make payment, Hermaios the royal scribe jointly instructing, to the under-written ferry-men from
3     the city of the Oxyrhynchites, on a joint-liability basis, the half-freight of the
4     freight originally fixed for the hundred artabas, 35 drachmas,
5     on condition that they deliver to Neapolis at their own
6     risk the grain that is loaded on them,
7     the domanial grain, from Claudius Theon, clean and free from
8     all fraud; the totals of the half-freight of what they load, apart from the monthly charges
9     customarily … added:
10    To Kilikas son of P…: of a cargo of 530 artabas, 500 artabas, 87 drachmas, 3 obols
11    To Herakleios son of Herakleios: of a cargo of 530 artabas, 500 artabas, 87 drachmas, 3 obols
12    To Harpalos son of Harpalos: of 318 artabas, 300 artabas, 52 drachmas, 3 obols
13    To Amois son of …: of 318 artabas, 300 artabas, 52 drachmas, 3 obols
14    To Sarapion son of …: of 371 artabas, 350 artabas, 61 drachmas, 1 obol
15    To Papontos son of Syros: of 318 artabas, 300 artabas, 52 drachmas, 3 obols
16    To Horos son of …chos: of 212 artabas, 200 artabas, 35 drachmas
17    To Hera…: of 212 artabas, 200 artabas, 35 drachmas
18    To Lo…: of 318 artabas, 300 artabas, 52 drachmas, 3 obols
19    To Hermous son of Damarion: of 424 artabas, 400 artabas, 70 drachmas
20    To Dionysios son of Dionysios Sapron: of 318 artabas, 300 artabas, 52 drachmas, 3 obols
21    In all, 752 drachmas, 3 obols, from which deduct the
22    hundredths customarily added to the imperial account, and take from them the appropriate receipt. Year 2 of Imperator Caesar Vespasian
23    Augustus, Tybi … Sebaste.
