[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother John S... [cartulary]; I reckon to you concerning the first
2     order of grain in exchange [through] [G]erontius gold coins
3     twenty-six [full weight], being gold coins 26 [full weight;] … cartulary, agrees the coins
4     twenty-six [full weight] as aforesaid; likewise through … [gold] coins twenty-six except karats
5     four and a half, being gold coins 55 except karats [4 1/2; was noted] 12th indiction.
