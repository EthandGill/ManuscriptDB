[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Abinnaeus, prefect of the squadron
2     of soldiers of the camp of Dionysias, from Aurelia
3     Maria, sister-in-law of Akiar, of the soldiers who are
4     stationed in Memphis under Kollouthos the praepositus,
5     a landholder in the village of Theoxenis. On the second of the present month Epeiph, [lor]d, I do not know by what [reaso]n and in robber-like fashion some malefactors, making use of [the nigh]t, came upon [my] field of
6     [s]heep, and after shearing my [she]ep nine in number, carried off another three sheep
7     alive. And the field-guards of the village of Theoxenis, searching out the shearing and driving-off of the sheep,
8     found in the field of Narmouthis [Io]annes and Eleias as the ones who had done the
9     shearin[g an]d driving-off. For this very reason I request and beg of your benevolence that, having arrested these men, you compel them to confess the shearing of the sheep
10    and the driving-off, then that the things written by
11    me you may bring up to the knowledge of my lord the duke;
12    for it is his (duty) [those] who dare such things
13    to pun[ish]; [and having obtained this], I shall acknowledge gratitude to you,
14    [lord]. Farewell.
15    I, Aurelia Maria, sister-in-law of Akiar the soldier,
16    have submitted (this).
17    In the consulship of our masters Constantius
18    for the 4th time and of Constans for the 3rd time, the Augusti,
19    Epeiph 11.
