[TRANSLATION]
1     Glaukias to Apollonios greetings. Concerning which [things] you ordered me
2     to announce to Nikanor and Antiochus, we announced [them]. Know that
3     them [as being] beneficial. The remaining [things], as whenever we come,
4     we will announce to you. Having come and [going] into Baitanota
5     and having taken Melana, I went [over] the plants and all the other [things].
6     Sufficiently thus it seems to me [that things] have been worked, and he said [that] the vineyard [was]
7     eighty thousand [vines]. He had also constructed both a well and [a] sufficient dwelling.
8     He gave me also to taste of the wine, which I did not discern whether it is Chian
9     or local. [You] do well thus, having [a] good lot in all respects.
10    Farewell. Year 29, Xanthicus 7.
11    From Glaukias, concerning which [things] he had been ordered to announce to Antiochus and Nikanor,
12    and concerning the wine in Baitanata.
13    To Apollonios.
14    Year 29, Xanthicus, in Alexandria.
