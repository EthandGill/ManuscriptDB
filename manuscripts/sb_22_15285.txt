[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Philoxen[us]
2     the most splendid, Choiak 7, indiction 5, in the village Leu[kios]
3     Aurelius Horigenes son of Ioannes
4     from mother Martha with a guarantor who also
5     undertakes him for delivery
6     and complete good will of the follo[wing]
7     of the declared grain of me Aurelius
8     apa Ioannes presbyter son
9     of Xenophilos having become presbyter
10    coming from village Leukios
11    of the Oxyrhynchite nome to the most admirable Pamouthios son of blessed memory apa Ioannes having become presbyter from the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, greetings; I confess
