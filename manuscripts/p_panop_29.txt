[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Serenus, exactor of the Panopolite nome,
2     from Aurelius Pasnos son of Alopex from the same city of Panopolis. Recently,
3     having been nominated by Besous, priestess of the same city, to the office of cattle-superintendent, I took refuge in the excellence of my lord the most eminent governor Flavius Quintillinianus
4     concerning this, and I obtained a self-executing decision by memorandum, through which his greatness ordered that another be put forward in my place on account of my old age
5     and advanced years, with the assistance of the defensor.
6     … I do not know how, from the sixth of the present month Epeiph until today, that same man
7     … being powerful over the localities, has shut me up in the public accounts-office
8     and, not obeying the order of my same lord the governor, has compelled me, wishing me
9     to carry out the burden of the cattle-superintendency. Let therefore the courts of my same lord
10    the most eminent governor be convened before your moderation, of both me and you, and concerning the rights belonging to me
11    I have appealed from you, calling upon the greatness of my same lord
12    the most eminent governor; I present to you this appeal, requesting that there be given to me
13    the customary subscription, and that the required apostoli be given, so that I may be able to plead the appeal before the tribunal of his greatness and obtain vindication;
14    and if you should be willing to grant me the customary subscription, that you know from
15    the posting of the sheets that the contest of this appeal of mine is not to be hindered,
16    this contest of mine before his undefiled court, requesting the scribe of the council
17    to make the posting of the appeal.
18    In the consulship of Papius Pacatianus, the most illustrious prefect of the sacred praetorium, and of Maecilius Hilarianus, the most illustrious,
19    [Epeiph] …
