[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apa Apollos son of Dioskoros Psimanobet,
2     from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, Aurelius Phoibammon
3     son of Viktor of Tagape, residing
4     in the hamlet of Pseketis near the
5     same village, and a shepherd, greetings.
6     I acknowledge that I have leased from your
7     reverence, for a yearly
8     term, of the produce, God willing, of the coming
9     ninth, of the new indiction, the
10    pasture-animal(?) belonging to you, situated upon …
11    of the (land) being under me …
12    of the allotment of Pasikorios, with all its
13    right, according to the boundaries, on condition that I have this
14    and pasture (it) for the feeding of my
15    flocks, and that I give the rent
16    to your holiness …
17    according to grazing, of gold, … solidus … according to the
18    exchange-rate of the village(?), total comes to … solidus … minus a carat …
19    to the goldsmith …
20    … the lease being valid
21    and secure; and, having been asked the formal question, I acknowledged. Aurelius
22    Phoibammon son of Viktor of Tagape,
23    I have leased as aforesaid. Aurelius
24    Kyrikos … having been requested,
25    wrote for him the document,
26    he being illiterate …
27    I witness the lease, having heard
28    from the contracting party.
29    Written through me, Abraamios son of Apollos, notary.
30    Lease of Phoibammon son of Viktor of Tagape concerning the pasture-animal of Pasikorios, of the produce of the 9th indiction.
