[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Didymos, strategos of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite,
2     from Marcus Lucretius Diogenes, Antinoite. Of the petition which I submitted to Claudius Herennianus, the most excellent ju[risdiction-judge admin]istering also the affairs of the prefecture,
3     and the subscription which I obtained, a copy is appended below, and I ask that the consequent things be done, that you appoint me guardian to the un[derwritten] minors
4     so that nothing of theirs be lost, in accordance with the laws. And it is: To Tiberius Claudius Herennianus, the most excell[ent jurisdiction-judge] administering also [the affairs of] the
5     prefecture by divine command, from Marcus Lucretius Diogenes, Antinoite. Being about to bring a petition befitt[ing] your nobility, [I am in hope] of obtaining the justice from you. I will define the mat[ter] in brief. In the past month Phaoph[i my sis]te[r] Octavia Lucre[tia di]ed intestate, leaving as heirs three sons from different marriages, Marci Aurelii Iulas and [Lucretius and Ru]fus, minors. The
6     one of them, then, Iulas, is under the guardianship of [his] own father Marcus Aurelius Heron. He has rec[eived, then,]
7     the share falling to him of the things left.
8     But the others—Lucretius has be[co]me fatherless, and the father of Rufus has died, and there is no one for them nearer in kin than I,
9     [Lucreti]us Diogenes, on whose account I make this petition, and I entreat, if it seem good to your fortune, to comm[and that it be written by su]bscription to the strategos of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nom[e, where] their affairs are, [to appo]int me guardian of the orphanhood of the children, so that I may be able to make the administration of the things left to them, in order that these may be preserved for them until their coming-of-age.
10    Farewell. I, Marcus Lucretius Di[og]en[es, son of Marcus] Aurelius Sarapion, have submitted it. The strategos, having before his eyes his own risk, will take care to appoint a guardian for the minors. Of sheets 68. Lucreti[us] …
11    In the 4th year of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Severus Alexander Pius Felix [Augustus, T]ybi 17.
