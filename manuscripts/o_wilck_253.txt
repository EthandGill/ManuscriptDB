1   Valerius Serenos and Patachemis,
2   the two collectors of the money-tax of Elephantine, through Petorzm(ethes) the assistant.
3   Paid: Patsibtis son of Petorzmethes, mother Sen-
4   petorzm(ethes), [and P]achnoubis his son, mother
5   Taplilauthis(?), for the assessments of the 3rd year, each
6   man at 1 dr. 1½ ob. — total 2 dr. 3 ob. Year 3
7   of Antoninus and Verus, the Caesars, the
8   lords, Pauni 28.
