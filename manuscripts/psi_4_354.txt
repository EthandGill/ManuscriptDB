[TRANSLATION]
1     Bobalos to Zenon greetings.
2     Athenagoras wrote to Hermolaus that the king is to arrive,
3     and to prepare a market and
4     fodder. I then
5     have distributed the hay
6     [that was] gathered through us
7     so [it is available] to you, so it may not be carried away; it is moreover in passage. You will thus do well
8     sending the mules
9     so that [it] may be gathered together to Moithym
10    quickly, so that not in the
11    passing by the king [it] be troubled by
12    the force.
13    Farewell.
14    Year 32, Pachons 23.
15    To Zenon.
