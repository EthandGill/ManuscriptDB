[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis of the harvest, year 13 of Antoninus
2     Caesar our lord, Epeiph 11, on behalf of Charakos
3     by name Asklatos Paoutos, through the farmer, wheat, one-sixth [of] an artaba
4     total: wheat 6 artabas; Paei; [it] has been noted
