[TRANSLATION]
1     So that your god-guarded [brotherhood] might learn [that] the day before yesterday, which [is] the seventh of the present month, sailed down
2     My master, the most illustrious [toparch], and he commanded me to write to you so that you might write to me the matter
3     Of the ship—what [you did] to it; immediately [then] upon the reading of the present letter your god-guarded
4     Your brotherhood write to me the [circumstances] of the matter, and what is needed again in word regarding it. [And] thereafter
5     Of the present letters I send to you the dried sycamore; but send your man to the
6     Lord Aristophanes, bound to select the acanthus according to the word of the so-called κερκίς, since he himself it
7     Supplies, and I greet you through the letter. But if your man comes down, write to me again through him, and [write]
8     To Lord Aristophanes regarding this again, Choiak the ninth, indiction the fourth
9     To my god-guarded master and brother Papas, the village-chief of Apollo Upper Helladios
10    Concerning the ship
