[TRANSLATION]
1     Eudaimon to Sarapion
2     the brother, greetings.
3     Before all, I greet you; then, brother,
4     remember to go
5     toward the landholders
6     of the within-embankment
7     fields so that they may go out with you toward the remeasurement of the places.
8     Do not therefore be neglectful, brother. Know that we
9     are worn out in every way.
10    Everything therefore, brother, having set everything aside, go out with them, since
11    there is need of [you] and your presence will be delightful to us. Oversee
12    Appan and bring us the reports of the review.
13    Farewell. Pharmouthi 17.
14    From Eudaimon to Sarapion the dutiful.
