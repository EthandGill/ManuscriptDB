[TRANSLATION]
1     My eagerness is always …
2     [to] reverence your [magnificence] …
3     for the present scarcity … is he who is able
4     to inform my master that from each one going up and going down I seek
5     [information] about your desired health [and] about your most magnificent brother and [about] your sweetest
6     and most longed-for children … I myself rejoice as if
7     I had personally reverenced my master. Since then, master, a need has arisen for some straw
8     for feeding the unreasoning animals, and I sent [word] beseeching my master through the brother and esteemed
9     sir aba John, and you ordered Daniel your attendant to send the [boat]
10    and behold, this such boat I hired and sent to receive such straw for
11    your order. Therefore I beseech you to order [it] to be sent to me, so that also in this [matter]
12    I may pray abundantly for the good living of my [master]; for it is your custom to be well-disposed towards all [and]
13    especially those who genuinely love [you] and pray abundantly for the good living and [the]
14    preservation of your blessed and generous house. Moreover I add another burden, [a request for] a little wood;
15    and this too order to be sent through the same boat. But let these things be preceded by [the]
16    owed reverence [which] I direct from myself to my master and through him from me …
17    your most magnificent brother. May the Lord Jesus Christ protect you. Praying for [the]
18    personal reverence, together with me, Origen your brother does this.
