[TRANSLATION]
1     You buy for me a thousand bundles of chaff from barley
2     for the sake of my beasts of burden from two hundred pounds
3     each bundle for three nomismata
4     as others buy
5     to the most illustrious in all things Senouthes the notary Athanasios
6     [with] God [illustrious]
