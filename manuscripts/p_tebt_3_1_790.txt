[TRANSLATION]
1     To Arkas, one of the First Friends and chief-doorkeepers,
2     administering the affairs of the strategia, from
3     Petosir[i]s son of Spagis and his associates,
4     the overseers of the precinct in Oxyrhyncha
5     of the division of Polemon, (the precinct)
6     of Arsinoe. Since certain of the tax-payers from the village
7     and others forcing their way
8     into the said precinct not only
9     assail him but also blackmail (us)
10    and take pledges contrary to what is proper,
11    and this with insolence and harassment …
12    … having sailed down to Alexandria
13    we sub[m]itted a pe[t]ition to the king and to the
14    q[uee]n, in which we set forth things similar to those [afore]said; and this
15    having been [s]ent to …ollides, who had been strategos, containing the order
16    … …that neither the precinct
17    nor the [oversee]rs should be harassed, but should be left [undis]tur[be]d as they request,
18    and …ollis having written
19    to the epistates of the village accordingly,
20    likewi[se also] concerning these same matters, when we submitte[d] to you many memoranda, to which
21    you wro[t]e to Pt[ol]emaios the epistates of the village
22    to permit no one to do anything contrary to what is
23    proper, and to bring in the persons indicated,
24    and no correction having taken place even until now, we further request that from here
25    there belong to the precinct the (order)
26    ordered by the king and the queen, and
27    that a copy of this be inscribed on the
28    outer gateway of the [precin]ct, beneath the [ex]isting slab, so that no one against it
29    may force his way in.
30    We ask you, if it seem g[ood],
31    to grant us to carry out the or[dered ] …
