[TRANSLATION]
1     To Theon, strategos of the Arsinoite nome, divisions of Themistos and Polemon,
2     from Ptolemaios son of Diodoros, also called Dioskoros, lessee of the imperial marsh near the village
3     of Theadelpheia in the division of Themistos, and of others. Often, when I approached Apollonios
4     the shore-guard and the seed-officer of the localities, having made many representations to him while he was present in the village, the Nile still being at its height, before the waters should pass over into the fields, to supply to the aforesaid marsh the
5     customary and sufficient waters — and being put off by them down to now, they were unwilling to supply (them),
6     the most sacred Nile having brought a perfect rise and being bound to bring forth all the nome … , the marsh therefore being down to today most deficient; for it has had altogether little
7     water and was barren of outflows and had not received even one (channel) from a main inlet; whence, being unable to keep quiet, I ask that a copy of this notice be conveyed both to the shore-guard and to the seed-officer of the localities, Tyrannos, through one of the assistants about you, in order that the
8     aforesaid marsh be filled, or that they know that, if any damage result from this, they will render account
9     before the most illustrious prefect and the most excellent procurator; for, the aforesaid marsh being dried up to the ground and the spawn and the breeding-channels being diminished, not only of this year's
10    rent does damage ensue, but also for the next three years, until the marsh gets spawn and breeding-channels into increase,
11    does damage ensue. But let there remain to me, against the shore-guard, the claim concerning which I have
12    … the notice concerning the same marsh for the past eighth year before the
13    most excellent epistrategos.
