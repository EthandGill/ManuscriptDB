[TRANSLATION]
1     Sostrates to Zenon, greetings. On the 26th as I was going up
2     from the plain, the dog handlers brought the female dog,
3     the one sent from Apollonius, having died. I have written therefore to you
4     so that you might know.
5     Farewell. Year 32, Tybi 26.
6     [To] Z[enon].
