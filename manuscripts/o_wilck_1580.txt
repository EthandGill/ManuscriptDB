[TRANSLATION]
1     Ailourion, collector of the money-tax of Charax, (to) Philammon
2     (son of) Pikos (son of) Paous. I have, for rent of year 3, from Pikos
3     (son of) Pachrates, impure four drachmas, total 4 dr. Year 3 of Antoninus
4     Caesar the lord, Mesore 2. Ailourion (son of) Mencheus, I have signed.
5     Others, Mesore 12, in the name of the same Philammon, I have (received), for rent, from
6     Pikos (son of) Pachrates, impure two drachmas 2 obols, total 2 dr. 2 obols.
7     Ailourion (son of) Mencheus, I have signed.
