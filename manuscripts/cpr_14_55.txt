[TRANSLATION]
1     Since, after which I went out from your [m]agnificence, I found a certain brother of mine having come
2     through me, for the sake of the brothers wishing to depart to [the church] of the Precious Cross.
3     I shall go to Zinnei and I shall see … for God willing at the fourth hour tomorrow,
4     if something is on the fifth [hour], you will find me in Berenikis, if you command before daybreak
5     tomorrow you go out with one ally. And we do the command of the lord. I bow down
6     [before] your brightness.
7     to my master, the lord Helias, with God, notary, from … … …
