[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolemy, greetings] … [of those] in the island of Alexander. I am wronged by
2     [Dorotheos] … [a] village. For in year 5, as the revenue records [show], Phamenoth […]
3     … to my co-slave [he] took care of my garment
4     … he went off having it. But when I perceived […]
5     … [the garment] in the synagogue of the Jews […] .; moreover Lezelmis, holder of one hundred arouras, appeared
6     … [the garment] to Nicomachus the temple-keeper until [the] judgment
7     … I therefore beg you, king, to order Diophaneus
8     [the strategos to write] … [to the] [over]seer to send Dorotheos and Ni[comachus] … [the garment] before him, and if what I write is true,
9     [to compel him to restore to me the garment] or its price, and concerning the carelessness
10    … for if this happens, I shall be through you, king,
11    [a recipient of justice]. Farewell.
