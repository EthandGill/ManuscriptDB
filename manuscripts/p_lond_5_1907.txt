[TRANSLATION]
1     With God, account of receipts of the property of the lord Adrian, count …
2     through the farmers of the vegetable-garden of Hermopolis for precious-metal [payments]
3     through the farmers of Psilamonthos
4     through the farmers of the seed-beds of Hermopolis
5     through the farmers of the two arouras of Thasagarl of Hermopolis
6     to the advocate from grain 115 artabas by the dochs measure for the silver-value of grain 72 artabas at 6 nomismata each and grain 43 artabas
7     through the farmers of Adelphos from grain 82½ artabas by the dochs measure for the silver-value of grain 60 artabas at 4 nomismata [each] and grain 22½ artabas
8     for the price of the pomarium-place of New … in [account] 4 nomismata, 3 nomismata and 8 carats
