[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and master Jesus Christ the God
2     and [savior] of us, [under the] reign of the most divine and most pious
3     our master greatest benefactor Flavius Her[a]clius the eternal
4     Augustus and autocrat, year 3, Thoth 30, indiction 2.
5     To Flavius Apion the most-renowned and most-eminent, of consular [rank] and
6     patrician landholder, and here in the brilliant city of the Oxyrhynchites through Menes
7     [ser]v[an]t of the one questioning and furnishing to his own master
8     the same most-renowned man, the guardianship and liability,
9     Aurelius [J]ustus son of Cyriacus, mother Tiseout, from
10    estate Pilasia of the Oxyrhynchite nome. I declare
11    with willing mind and free choice, swearing
12    [the] divine and venerable oath, to pledge and undertake
13    before [your] eminence Aurelius Peter son of Jeremiah
14    mother Martha, from estate Actuarium of the Oxyrhynchite nome
15    registered of your eminence, [as] registered of her
16    farmer, on which condition he should remain continuously and
17    dwell in the same estate and sow upon the
18    present [second] indiction [of] crops [of the] third indiction and
19    fulfill their public [obligations] and [whatever is] demanded
20    him [before] me I present and will deliver
21    without [recourse] where him and I have received, in the custody
22    of the [glorious] your house; but if not this I do, I declare
23    [myself] liable to answer for all [claims] against him [that are] demanded
24    Valid [is] the guarantee, written simply, and questioned I acknowledged.
25    Justus son of Cyriacus, [this] guarantee agrees with me
26    as stated. John wrote on behalf of him [he being] illiterate
27    Guarantee of Justus son of Cyriacus from estate Pilasia, undertaking
28    Peter from estate Actuarium.
