[TRANSLATION]
1     for a period of … years, [from the seventeenth and sixteenth] and ninth year, at a rent [each year] for [the]
2     aroura of … and a half artabas of wheat, exempt-from-deduction [and free-of-risk], and I shall of necessity perform [all] the yearly [works],
3     [embankings], waterings, under-cuttings, double-hoeing,
4     [and] the throwings-up [of dykes] and all the other (works) that are fitting
5     from my own (means) at the proper seasons, doing not even one (bit of) [damage],
6     and the [rent] I shall pay each year in the month of Pauni
7     in the same village by the four-choinix measure, all the public (dues)
8     being upon [you] the owners, and after the
9     term I shall hand over [the] arouras from stubble just as
10    I too received (them), if it seems good to lease. And having been formally questioned [I] agreed. [Aur]elia Serenilla, through me
11    Zoilos my son, have leased as aforesaid. The Aurelii
12    Gemellus and Pasigenes, through me Isidoros, and Ptollas and Oualas, have leased as aforesaid. I, Isidoros, wrote also on behalf of Ptollas and Oualas, who are illiterate.
13    Year 17 and [16] and ninth year of our lords Diocletian
14    and Maximian Augusti and Constantius and Maximian,
15    most noble Caesars, Phaophi 7.
