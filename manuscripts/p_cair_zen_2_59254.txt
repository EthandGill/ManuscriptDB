[TRANSLATION]
1     Phanias to Zenon greetings. The allotment-holders in the
2     Arsinoi[te]
3     nome, young men, I have decided to count all and I will swear in Philadelp[hia].
4     You will do well therefore having prepared lodging for me;
5     for in my body I happened to be in poor condition,
6     and at the same time I wish to see you as much as the time permits
7     time. Farewell.
8     [Year] 3[4], Pachons 22. Phanias
9     [con]cerning his coming to Philadelphia.
10    To Zenon.
