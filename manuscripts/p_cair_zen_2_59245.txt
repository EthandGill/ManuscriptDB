[TRANSLATION]
1     Kolluthes to Zenon, greetings. When I departed from you I found the farmers [from]
2     the measured-out land to the soldiers having withdrawn to the Isieion the in the Memphite [region].
3     [When] therefore I received the letter from you, I went to the city of Crocodiles to Maim[achos],
4     [so] that he might rouse them; and when this happens we will come to you; he was not resident [for]
5     [in] the village Psenomous. I have therefore written to you so that you may know.
6     Farewell. Year 33, Choiach….
7     [Year 33], Tybi 2. Kolluthes.
8     [To]
9     Zenon.
