[TRANSLATION]
1     From Flavius Herminus, from the accountants
2     to the most admirable John, the assistant. I have received from you the compensation
3     of fifty nomismata from Pharmouthi, new moon, of the tenth
4     indiction until Payni, new moon of the eleventh indiction. Flavius Herminus
5     from the accountants I agree [to] the compensation from Pharmouthi, new moon
6     of the tenth indiction until Payni, new moon of the eleventh indiction as aforesaid.
