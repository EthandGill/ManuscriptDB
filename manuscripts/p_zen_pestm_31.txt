[TRANSLATION]
1     Dorion to Zenon greetings. We learned that from a certain time the pledge [cups] and cosmetic vessels through Charmous have been lying [there]; the principal [is] 600 drachmas of silver and the interest on each mina of silver [is drachmas] ….
2     time [is] 2 years and 11 months. Similarly also the other [items]; the principal [is] 900 drachmas of silver, from which
3     [are received] from Petalius in the city [600 drachmas] of silver and from Callon in Memphis 300 drachmas of silver, [making silver] [900 drachmas];
4     and the time [is] one year and 5 months. But concerning the 900 drachmas of interest, do not worry, but as [seems good to you]
5     so do. You will do well also [to collect] from Sostratus 32 drachmas of silver which [I have]
6     through the bank in behalf of Dionysius, taking care that [it] is sent to me. And [he] agreed [to repay] 1 talent
7     to be repaid in Memphis, which he ordered to be given for the provisions of Pais and Phalout and
8     other 2 talents, [making] 3 talents. And he wrote me to Thrason a note to account for [the things] of which
9     no one has paid the price.
10    Farewell. Year 36, Mecheir 2.
11    To Zenon.
12    Year 36, Mecheir 12. Dorion
13    of cups, of Sostratus
14    of silver 32 drachmas.
