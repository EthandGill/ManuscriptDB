[TRANSLATION]
1     ap… ll… to do… I have borne [him] until today and to whom he did not desire to care-for but also was displeased …
2     he holds, and if indeed he is able there to demand from him the hundred coins, well and fine, [but if not], …
3     sends to me here and finds his matter. Very much indeed I was troubled on account of the small ch[ild] …
4     of the lady Chrysanthes. And what are we able to do to this if not [alon]e to glorify the philanthropic [God?]
5     Through the letter I greet her and through her the God-guarded common children and the mothe[r] …
