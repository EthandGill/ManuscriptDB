[TRANSLATION]
1     To Herakleides, of the chief bodyguards
2     and hipparch over men and epistates [of the] Region of Thebes,
3     from Hermias son of Ptolemaios, of the leaders
4     from Ombos. Having been wronged not moderately
5     and being in danger of being deprived of my own property
6     by Horos [s]on of Horos and Chonnonpreus
7     son of Harsies[is], Psenchonsis son of Teephibis,
8     Panas [son of Pe]chytis, choachytes (funerary priests),
9     and those wi[th] them: for, since there belong [to me]
10    in Diospolis ancestral houses,
11    of which my ancestors too held ownership for as long a time as they lived,
12    the accused, having their dwelling
13    in the Memnoneia
14    still from the beginning, because it was not permitted
15    to them to dwell in Diospolis,
16    but in the disturbance of the times
17    having formed a low opinion (of me) as (holding) another's,
18    that I hold the dwelling, having attacked
19    [on]e [of my] aforewritten houses, which is on
20    the sou[th] and [we]st of Dios-
21    polis, [from the nor]th of the dromos
22    of the greatest [go]dd[es]s Hera, the (one) leading to the river, of which the walls were
23    built, having built up the
24    demolished parts of it, they dwell in it, laying claim
25    unjustly. Concerning these things, having taken up (the matter) in the 46th year,
26    when I had come to Diospolis, I submitted
27    a memorandum against them to Her[m]ias [t]he strategos; and when they had been summoned to come to the court, being conscious to themselves of having nothing secure,
28    they turned aside. Likewise also in the 49th year, when
29    Hermias had set out to Diospolis and, concerning
30    the same matters, when I had appealed and they had removed themselves,
31    he ordered Hermogenes the
32    then chief guard to hand over to me the house.
33    But (though ordered) to hand over the house to me: after I had departed
34    to the Ombite (nome), attacking again
35    this house, they dwell in it. Therefore I request, if
36    it seem good, to order that the accused be brought to trial, so that, if I prove
37    these things to be so, they may be compelled to withdraw fr[om the hou]se
38    … for I shall do …
39    … written …
40    an[d ] corpses [depos]ited
41    in the said ho[u]se, to determine
42    concerning it … ; and this being done,
43    I shall have obtained [j]ustice.
44    Farewell.
