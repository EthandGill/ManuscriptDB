1     To the sitologoi …
2     of the 1st year of A[ntoninus Caesar] the lord;
3     we the undersigned camel-herders of the village
4     of Soknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleides division;
5     we have received from you the (sums) ordered
6     for us by Ael[ius] Numis[ianus, strategos]
7     of the Themistos a[n]d Polemon divisions, and
8     by Artemidoros, royal scribe of the Th[emis]tos di[vision],
9     for transport-charges of public wheat for the processing
10    of the produce of the 1st year of [An]toninus Caesar the
11    lord, and of the 21[st year] of the deified Hadrian, carried out in the Herakleides division, from public wheat
12    [wheat: artabas] 551½, and after the [from the] produce of the 16th year
13    [ord]ered for us, artabas 3 … ½ …, the remaining (artabas) from the produce of the 1st year of Antoninus Caesar
14    [the] lord, at the granary of Dionysias,
15    [artabas] 519, of which, for the processing of the produce of the 1st year,
16    [artabas] 516½ ¼, of the produce of the 21st year [artabas] 2¼, total: the aforesaid amounts.
17    And we are:
18    Stotoetis son of Stotoetis: of wheat artabas 460
19    Ouetis the elder, son of … : of wheat artabas 59
20    total: the aforesaid amounts.
21    Year 1 of [Imperator Caesar Ti]tus Aelius
