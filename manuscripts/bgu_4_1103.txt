1   Sheet (kollema).
2   To Protarchos,
3   from Zois daughter of Heraklides, with (her) guar-
4   dian, her brother Eirenaios son of Heraklides,
5   and from Antipatros [s]on of Zenon. They a[gree,]
6   Zois and Antipatros, that they have separated
7   from one another from the [co-]
8   habitation established for them by agreement through the [sa-]
9   me tribunal in the present year 17 of Caesar,
10   Hathyr; and that Zois has received from
11   Antipatros, from hand to hand out of the house, what she had as
12   a little dowry — garments, in [silv]er dra[chmas]
13   one hundred and twenty, and of [g]old earrings
14   a pair; (and that) the marriage-agreement is from this moment void,
15   and that Zois shall not pro-
16   ceed, nor an-
17   other on her behalf, against Antipatros
18   concerning the demand for the dowry; and that nei-
19   ther (shall proceed) against the other, neither concerning
20   the cohabitation nor concerning anything
21   else at all, of (matters) up to the present
22   day — from which it is also permitted to
23   Zois to join with another man
24   and to Antipatros (to join) with another wo-
25   man, both being free of liability;
26   and, apart from the things agreed being
27   valid, the transgressor is also to be liable
28   both to the damages
29   and to the fixed penalty.
30   Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 2.
