[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of treasury of the metropolis of produce, year 23 of Marcus Aurelius Severus
2     Antoninus Caesar the lord, Pauni 22
3     On behalf of Notus, name of Panámés Petechónsios Psenchónsios
4     Of wheat two ... other Epeiph 20 name of the same
5     ... makes wheat 3 1/2 1/8. Pol marked wheat three
6     Half eighth, makes wheat 3 1/2 1/8. Others Mesore 4
7     On behalf of Notus, name of Panámés Petechónsios the younger Psenchónsios
8     Of wheat one-half third and on behalf of Charakos Pasémis of wheat two-thirds, makes wheat 1 1/2 1/24. Pol marked.
