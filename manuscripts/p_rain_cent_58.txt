[TRANSLATION]
1     … … …
2     … [of Soknopaios]
3     the great, great god, and of the temple-sharing gods, of the registered temple which is [in the village of Soknopaiou Nesos],
4     he submitted to me a petition, [wishing] in priestly fashion
5     to circumcise his two sons, Pakysis and Stotoetis,
6     from his mother Taphiomis, priestess of the same gods. For proof of being of priestly descent, by a special (return) (?)
7     he appended a copy of the house-by-house census of the 9th year
8     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, and of the registration-by-portrait of the 16th year of [the deified]
9     Hadrian, upon the subscription of the public
10    record-keepers of the nome, and a copy of the sheet
11    of the after-birth (registration) of the children, drawn up together [with the]
12    proofs, of Petesouchos also called Ptolemaios, son of Onnophris, stolistes of Souchos the great, great god,
13    and of the temple-sharing gods, and successor to the prophecy [of the]
14    gods in the Arsinoite (nome), and of Pakysis son of Panephremmis, son [of Horos]
15    and of Sataboys son of Horos, son of Sataboys, and of Panephremmis
16    son of Anchophis, and of Tesenouphis son of Tesenouphis, [and of Harpagathos] son of Stotoetis … [of the] five elder priests [of the village]. I write to you, most honoured one, having instructed [Onno]phris to come [to you with the children;] they are indeed [toward]
17    the present … [year of our lord] Antoninus Caesar
18    according to their after-birth (registration) [made as set forth above]
19    Pakysis being … [years old, and Stoto]etis … [years old] …
20    I pray for your health.
