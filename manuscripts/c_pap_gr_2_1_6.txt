[TRANSLATION]
1     To Maron village scribe of Philadelphia
2     from Stratius of Titanus of Philadelphia. My son Nemesion
3     [being] registered about the
4     village, died on the fifth
5     of Choiak of the present
6     ninth year of Tiberius
7     Claudius Caesar
8     Augustus Germanicus
9     Autocrator. Wherefore I present to you the petition so that
10    you shall refer [it] to those to whom it is proper and his name shall be removed and reported
11    in the list of the dead so that I may be without blame
12    just as I also am.
13    Farewell.
