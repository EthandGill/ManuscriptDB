1   (hand 2) [In year] 16, Phamenoth 9
2   (hand 1) [To Nearchos, deputy-general] and chief[...]
3   [...] of success
4   [...]asiai[...]
5   [...]en of the
6   [...the] royal
7   [... of] them out of
8   [...of the catoec]ic cavalry, the
9   [...]athos in no
10   [reckoning, holding] what is fair and
11   [...]ous through many
12   [...]na to whom death
13   [...]mon, holding the
14   allotment which neighbors on the side toward the
15   east of the four-aroura plot
16   in the fields around Tilothis(?),
17   by force has taken in addition the said
18   four-aroura plot, and he has driven (us) out
19   also from the aforementioned allotment of ours,
20   (a) quarter over the twenty
21   schoinia, since the 8th year, and he carries
22   off the yearly produce, (of wheat) 5 (artabas),
23   while it falls to us to settle the dues
24   upon them, wherefore, worn down,
25   we have been driven to threats.
26   We ask that in no detriment to
27   us provision be made, so that the
28   land may be restored to us, and there shall be exacted
29   the rents of the times of the 2 years
30   [in]to the royal treasury, the matter concerning it being further
31   settled fittingly.
32   Farewell.
33   (hand 2) Write to Theon the chief-of-police that the man
34   who has appropriated the ground
35   appear. Year 16, Phamenoth 9
