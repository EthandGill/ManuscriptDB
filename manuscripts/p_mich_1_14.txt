r.1    Νίκων Ζήνωνι χαίρειν. γεγράφαμεν Ἀρτεμιδώρωι τῶι ἐλεάτρωι τὴν τιμὴν τῶν ἀβακίων, ὧν παρεθέμεθ[α]

[TRANSLATION]
1     Nikon to Zenon greetings. We have written to Artemidorus the oil-dealer the price of the counting-boards which we have [deposited]
2     with him, to return to you, if we have not sold [them], the the counting-boards themselves. If therefore he brings [them], [send]
3     to us, if [they] are not useful to you.
4     Be well!
5     Nikon, concerning the price of the counting-boards, to Zenon.
6     to bring from Artemidorus.
7     Year 29, Xanthicus 28, in Alexandria.
