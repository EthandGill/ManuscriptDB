[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Zo[ilos] prytanis's servant
2     To Aurelius Isidorus of Ptolemy
3     to the farmer, greetings.
4     I received from you the export-tax of the arouras which
5     you cultivated for me concerning the [boundary-delineation]
6     for the produce of the 19th year and the 6th year and the 5th year and the 3rd year
7     of wheat nine artabas, they come to artabas 9,
8     complete. Aurelius Zoislos I wrote
9     the whole.
10    of the consulship of our lord Galerius
11    Valerius Maximinus Augustus the 2nd
12    of the intercalated 3.
