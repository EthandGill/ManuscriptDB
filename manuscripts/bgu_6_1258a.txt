[TRANSLATION]
1     There was registered … Ptolemaios son of Protarchos, hipparch over men of those from the Thebaid, about 54 years old, of medium height,
2     honey-coloured, with straight hair, broad-faced, lightly tattooed, to Horos;
3     … son of Petenouris, of those from the Thebaid … of the (men) of Peteuris son of Hareus, concerning his receiving from him in bronze: talents
4     for the rent of the wool which he sold to him in the 27th year, and that he will not proceed against him; through Harpaesis.
5     … Harpebekis son of Onnophris, gardener, of Hermoupolis, about 34 years old, of medium height, honey-coloured, with straight hair,
6     long-faced, straight-nosed, a scar on the left jaw, to Petosiris also called Pchotis son of Petenaithis;
7     … concerning working a water-wheel in the … bath-house of Am… from Thoth of the 28th year until Mesore of the same year, at a daily wage
8     of bronze: drachmas … 7, three obols; and if he does not stay, a fine of bronze: 2 [talents], and let him give … the wage … through Harpaesis.
9     … Seephchemis son of Sephibis, about 21 years old, of medium height, honey-coloured … long-faced, straight-nosed,
10    slightly squinting, freckles on both cheeks, to the same Petosiris;
11    of the same wage, the same penalty; through Harpaesis.
12    There was registered a loan: Inarous the elder son of Katytis, farmer, of Hermoupolis, about 37 years old … of medium height, honey-coloured,
13    with straight hair, round-faced … to A…yranis son of Nikaios: [drachmas] 2,000, repayment in Pachon of the 28th
14    year, or a penalty of half as much again; through Haronnetis.
15    There was registered a lease: Amalalis son of Amalalis, pigeon-seller, of Hermoupolis, about 49 years old, of medium height, honey-coloured,
16    … scars on the feet … left, … 6, to Diades son of Patrokles, commander and hipparch over men,
17    of the fifth part belonging to him of a dovecote … of another in the part to the south and west
18    of Hermoupolis, for the 28th year: bronze 4 talents.
19    Repayment by the instalments indicated through the lease: bronze …

21    for 5 months, 2 talents per month; and if he does not repay, let him pay half as much again; through Haronnetis.
22    There was registered a … Thoteus son of Haronnetis, of Hermoupolis, about 26 years old, of medium height, honey-coloured, with curly hair,
23    long-faced, lightly tattooed … to … son of Senatoumis son of Neschatoumis: bronze 1 talent, … through Harei…s.
24    There was registered a loan: Menepasis son of Petosiris, farmer, of those from the village of Apollonos, about 26 years old, of medium height, honey-coloured,

26    of the (men) of Pasinos … of wheat 3 artabas, repayment in Choiak of the 28th year … to Hermoupolis;
27    the price of each artaba bronze 1,200; through Harpaesis.
