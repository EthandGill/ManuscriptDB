1     Heraclides [to] … the
2     most beloved, greetings.
3     what you sent to me to present,
4     I delivered to the secretary of the

7     For the rest, therefore, order the
8     elders not to have any matter [towards] this man. And the one who was delivered in his place for the need [is] Apollo [son] of Nemesion, [son] of Heracles, mother Taarpasis.
