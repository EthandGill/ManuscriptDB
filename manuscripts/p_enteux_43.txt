[TRANSLATION]
1     … makes no account, but has utterly scorn[ed] me a … o … [being] in old age …
2     I be[seech] you therefore, King, if it please you, to order Diophanes the [str]ateg[us] to wr[ite]
3     to Menander the ἐπιστάτης to recall Simias and Krups, through whom he received the [drachmas]
4     and to send [forth] what was attested to Diophanes and Diogenes himself with
5     the documents, so that Diophanes, having acted against him, if these things are true, may restore to me and I shall have [for]
6     [old] age the necessities, so that I may be, King, through you the just and merciful [one], having obtained [for the remain]der of life. Fare well.
7     To Menander. By all means reconcile them; if not, send Diogenes so that they may be judged before the proper tribunal.
8     Year 1, Gorpiaios 30, Tybi 13.
