[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 15 of the Emperor Caesar Titus
2     Aeli[us Ha]drian Antoninus Augustus
3     Euse[b]ous, Mesore 7, reckoning of Epeiph. [He] paid
4     He[ra]k Didymus son of Didymus, mother Sarap
5     for the poll-tax of the fifteenth year of the Syrian [district]
6     twenty drachmas, making 20, with the addition of bronze obols ten.
