2   by Artemidoros son of Amenneus son of Artemas, his mother being Thakoris daughter of Amenneus, from the same city,
3   by a deed of appointment made through the record-office in the same city in the present month,
4   a copy of which is appended - to Eudaimonis also called Ploutarche, styled as daughter of Sinthonis
5   daughter of Pekysis,
6   and to her mother Sinthonis daughter of Pekysis, her mother being Teenkegous, both from Tanais,
7   in equal shares to each, with guardians - for Eudaimonis also called Ploutarche, (her guardian) Ammonios also called
8   Dio-
9   nysios, son of Ammonios, his mother being Harasis, from the city of Oxyrhynchos; and for Sinthonis, her full
10   brother Pachnoubis, greeting. I acknowledge that I have sold to you, out of the property be-
11   longing to the same Artemidoros who appointed me, in the same city in the quarter of Pammenes' Garden - a paternal house and forecourt
12   and the adjoining courtyard - from that same courtyard, from the
13   eastern part of it, fifty square cubits of area, extending from north to
14   south to the end of the whole courtyard, and from east to west until the same
15   fifty square cubits of
16   area are made up. Neighbours of the same (plot) sold to you by me from the courtyard, at the site of fifty cubits: south, (property) of Diogenes and others;
17   north, of Sarapion styl-

19   the remaining areas of the same courtyard. The (sum) agreed between us as the price

24   [on]
25   the condition that the same ... his ... in the eastern and southern parts of the same

29   every [claim, following upon] the same Artemidoros who appointed [me];
