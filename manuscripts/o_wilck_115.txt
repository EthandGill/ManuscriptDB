[TRANSLATION]
1   Valerion and the other lessees of the Sacred
2   Gate of Syene, through Sarapion the scribe.
3   Paid: Pachnoubis son of Phanophis,
4   mother Tachomtbekis, for the poll-tax of the third
5   year of Hadrian the lord,
6   in part: seventeen drachmas (and) a half-obol,
7   (and) ½ obol (keepers' fee) — total 17 dr. 1 ob. Year 4
8   of Hadrian the lord,
9   Mecheir 17.
