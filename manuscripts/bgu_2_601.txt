[TRANSLATION]
1     Tasoucharion to Neilos her
2     brother, many greetings.
3     Before all things I pray that you
4     are in good health, and I make your obeisance before the lord Sarapis. Know that I have given
5     to Ptolemaios the reed-merchant the securities of the house, into the Demetrieion. So do well to write to me
6     about the house, as to what you have done,
7     and the earnest-money of Sarapion the advocate I have given to him,
8     and write to me about the registration.
9     If you are making the registration for me …
10    you do well … [and]
11    write me a note, so that I may prepare and sail up to you.
12    And concerning the little grain-stores, do not sell
13    them. I greet my sister
14    Taonnophris and the daughter of Bellaios.
15    Didymos and Heliodoros greet you.
16    Ptolemaios and Tiberinos and Sarapion greet you. I greet
17    Sarapio[n] son of Imouthes and his children
18    and Soma and his children and his wife
19    and Heron and Tabous and Ischyriaina.
20    Satorneilos greets you. I pray
21    for your good health. Tasoucharion greets Pe…in
22    and her children. Helene greets
23    my mother many times and the brothers.
24    Chairemon son of …nos greets you.
25    Deliver to Neilos, from Tasoucharion, sister of Tasachos.
