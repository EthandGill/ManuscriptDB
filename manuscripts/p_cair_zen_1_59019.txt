[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Zenon, greeting. If you are well, it would be good; I myself am also in health. After you sailed off I brought in the
2     [man who] tends the gazelle-skins (?), and after examining them he said they had been spoiled by raw flesh, and that for this reason they had been damaged. He said it was not possible to make them wax-like, for he said that after a year they would change; but he said he would make them …, yet with much labour, so that he said they were not worth such trouble.
3     [Concerning] the treatment we shall try copper ones, but if not, two-chalkous ones; for he himself says that for a half-obol
4     [he treats] the knucklebone (?) at the courtyard; and he said it is possible to ask Antipater the Etesias, for he said he had
5     [treated] him. You then, as soon as you receive the letter, write to me about these things …
6     [before] the season runs out. Know also that Patron is unwilling to take back Apollophanes, but has caused us much trouble. I approached Melas, and he was ready to be registered as guarantor, both himself [and]
7     [another] of the citizens. But that man, seeing this, that even so he would not be left behind, and while we were fighting on his behalf, took him back. The rest I will write to you more precisely, for just now I have not had the leisure. Try to write to me as quickly as possible [about]
8     [everything].
9     Farewell.
10    To Zenon.
