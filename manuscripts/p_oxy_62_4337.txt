[TRANSLATION]
1     To Theon […][stra]tegos,
2     from Heraclea of Theon of Sarapattos, mother of Hera[clea], from the city of Oxyrhynchus, with guardian Hierakion, also called Phanius son of Phanius, from the same city. I wish to purchase from the public [treasury] from those exposed for sale previously of Marcus Salvius Justus, whose affairs
3     passed into the treasury, concerning the village Pela, from
4     on one hand [the property] of Seuth[os kler]os grain [land] of one-artaba [measure]
5     nineteen ar[o]uras and private soil on which
6     thorns forty-o[n]e, of which arouras [the] neighbors [are]
7     south: earth, north: royal [land], east: flank, west: the follo[wing se]al; [value] of the appraisal: three talents and
8     thousand[s] and of the succeeding [items]; but from the Krotos [plot] grain [land] of one-artaba arouras
9     thre[e], of which [the] neighbors [are] south: earth, north: royal [land]…
