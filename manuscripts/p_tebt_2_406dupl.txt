[TRANSLATION]
1     … iko, of Paulos and the
2     brothers, 5 slave-persons:
3     Nikois, Lakonis,
4     Kophe, Belles,
5     and of the latter, Sarapias,
6     children.
7     To the village of Teptynis.
8     Account of what Paulos, my late husband, father
9     of my under-age daughter Paulina, left behind, which were also handed over
10    to Pasigenes, my husband's brother, through Souchammon the mediator. It is as follows:
11    a complete lamp-stand with an Eros and a lamp, valued together at … 6 drachmas,
12    a bronze basin, a frying-pan, … t…ape,
13    and among garments: a tunic of first-quality fulling with a Laconian stripe,
14    worth a stater,
15    a maphortes (shawl), likewise white with a Laconian stripe, worth a didrachm,
16    another tunic, half-worn, a linen kolobion (sleeveless tunic) with a double stripe, new,
17    a linen face-cloth, new, white linen cloths, 12 in number, at 8 drachmas each, 96 drachmas,
18    a small adze for pruning, a plough-share likewise complete, a wooden bed complete.
19    And what he has in common with the brothers: a complete kettle
20    of lead for boiling linen, a complete bowl, a small
21    bronze vessel, a small jar.
22    And of the slaves which he controls, held in common, of the father of my daughter, 5 in number:
23    Neikois, Kophe another, and of the latter the offspring
24    Lakonis, Belles, Sarapias.
25    And of the many arouras which he again controls,
26    being.
