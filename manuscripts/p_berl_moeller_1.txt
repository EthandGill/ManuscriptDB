[TRANSLATION]
1     In Euergetis by Lykon, the illustrious city.
2     In the sixteenth year of Imperator Caesar Gaius Aurelius Valerius Diocletianus and the fifteenth of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Valerius Maximianus, Greatest Germanici, Greatest Sarmatici,
3     Greatest Persici, Pious, Fortunate Augusti, and the eighth (year) of Flavius Valerius Constantius and Galerius Valerius Maximianus, the most noble Caesars, in the consulship of our lords Constantius and Maximianus,
4     the most noble Caesars, for the third time, on Gorpiaios … under the chief-priest, of the Kaisareion Marcus Aurelius Besarion son of Didymos, of the councillor-magistrates of Lykopolis, and of the Sebasteion Marcus Aurelius Besarion also called Pantonymos, of the councillor-magistrates, and however they are styled. An agreement, which Aurelia Didyme also called … daughter of Hierax, acting without a guardian by the right of children according to Roman custom, with the concurrence of her own husband Didymarchos
5     also called Dioskorides, of those in Lykopolis the illustrious city, [and Aurelia Herakleia] … likewise acting without a guardian by the right of children, with the concurrence of … made; according to which
6     they acknowledge: since Aurelia Didyme petitioned by a report to the most eminent prefect of Egypt, complaining against Aurelia Herakleia that, concerning a deposit of three thousand drachmas of old coinage, she wronged her,
7     the matter was referred to the president by subscription of the prefect; she petitioned the president, and the president, having come to a decision, will determine what is fitting; and, the suit being about to be pleaded before the appointed judge,
8     they decided rather to settle it without trouble, and, with the consent of … relatives and of Didyme's husband Didymarchos, they resolved their dispute with one another without a court,
9     and they agreed with one another according to Roman custom: Aurelia Didyme, with the concurrence of her husband Dioskorides, neither to make claim nor to make any future claim, nor any other on her behalf, against Aurelia Herakleia, neither concerning
10    the deposit nor concerning the documents … nor concerning anything whatsoever, written or unwritten, in any way, on any pretext; and Herakleia likewise not to make claim against Aurelia
11    Didyme concerning anything whatsoever, written or unwritten, so that their dispute is dissolved and annulled by this agreement of settlement and by the pledge of the oaths; and that this
12    agreement of settlement shall remain valid for all time, and it shall not be permitted to anyone to contradict or to transgress any of the things written in it, or the one contradicting shall pay, as
13    penalty to the most sacred treasury, … drachmas of silver, and the same amount sacred (to the fisc), in addition to the agreed terms remaining valid; on all which terms, on being formally questioned, the Aureliae Didyme, with the concurrence of
14    her husband Dioskorides, and Herakleia, with the concurrence of …, acknowledged their consent. And declaring themselves not to know letters, they presented for themselves a reader, Aurelius
15    of the record-office of Euergetis … the things of her, and three witnesses, Aurelii.
16    The Aureliae jointly agreed …
