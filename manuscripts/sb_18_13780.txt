[TRANSLATION]
1     [I acknowledge] gratitude to you. Farewell.
2     Before the 6th of the Ides of February. Concerning the land and its taxes.
3     [It] will be settled between you [by] the exactor. [Of] wax-sheet 98 [of] volume 4. Up to these [items].
4     The same. Farewell.
5     In the aforementioned consulship, Mecheir 20, I, Origen, have presented [this].
6     I made known [it] to the inscribed [record] through Neket the servant, Mecheir, the 20th.
