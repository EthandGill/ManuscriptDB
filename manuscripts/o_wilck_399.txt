1   Antiphilos son of Antiphilos paid, for
2   poll-tax of year 11, ten drachmas — total 10 dr. — and surcharges at an obol (and) half-obol.
3   Year 11 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
4   Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
5   Pharmouthi 33. Bassos son of Decmus, banker, I have signed.
6   (2nd hand) Pharmouthi 33, bath-tax 1 drachma 1½ obols — total 1 (dr.) 1½ ob. Apol( ) signed.
