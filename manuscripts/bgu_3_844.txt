[TRANSLATION]
1   Herodes to Apollonios,
2   his dearest (friend), greetings.
3   I want you to know that,
4   having gone up at once, I was much
5   storm-tossed (i.e. afflicted) by the priest
6   [...] in Arsinoe
7   by an illness, from which he causes me (trouble).
8   So you will do well to send
9   me, if you have anything in
10   hand, so that I may give (it) …; for he causes
11   me troubles —
12   (me) being ill. So do not
13   do otherwise; you will
14   send me the heather-
15   wood stick, since I have need
16   of it. And you will do well
17   to furnish
18   Dioskoros (with) ten geese(?) for
19   my account. So do not
20   do otherwise concerning the things about which
21   I write you. (2nd hand) Farewell.
22   Year 3 of Imperator Caesar Domitian
23   Augustus, Thoth 22.
24   (1st hand) To Apollonios from Herodes.
