[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God I declare I am Chael, son of Abraham, the bull-driver
2     The one called Tbuonaalaali, by the one called Theodore, son of Joseph; the one called Kalanqehy
3     Thus the aforesaid says: "I have need of you; I have [taken] ... three (pieces of) currency, three only"
4     and [taken] ... (pieces of) currency, two only; and [taken] ...
5     according to vegetable-seed six artabas; [whereas] vegetable-seed twelve artabas [together]
6     one alone; and [taken] according to ... fifteen
7     the one [being] whole-linen the one [being] in readiness, complete
8     ... in the grace of God by the fruit, eleventh indiction
9     without ambiguity, written in [the month of] Mecheir, 21st, indiction 11
10    … deacon Menas from the village of Ampelium I testify under …
11    I am the deacon …
12    son of Phobammon [by the] meter, I am the deacon Johannes, son of Menas, by the meter
13    [by] me Menas, least deacon, secretary, [this] was written
