[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon greetings, Dionysios the grain-measurer. I beseech you and supp[licate, praying for yo]u the
2     paternal gods and the health of [Apollonius], not to ignore me being wronged,
3     but to visit [him] yourself most of all concerning me, but if not, [to] Ar[te]midoros
4     [to] order [him] to take sureties for [my] stay until Apollonius might come and
5     [concerning these things] might inspect; for I [through the] affairs [have] nothing carried away,
6     [but] I provided the necessaries to those [from him], Nikon a[nd Add]aeus, receiving wages and grain-measu[rement]; if not, [order] to accept for me the measure[d grain] by the measure, and of the remain[ing we will] make complete the ten […]
7     [hund]red, as Apollonius wri[tes. I then] take refuge with you, judging [you] equal
8     [to Ap]ollonius; for whatever you judge concerning me, [this] I will do according to
9     [what] falls to me [as my] share.
10    Farewell.
11    [Dionys]ios the grain-measurer.
