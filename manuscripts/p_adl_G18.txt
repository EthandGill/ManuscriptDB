[TRANSLATION]
1     While Ptolemy the so-called Alexander and Bere[nice the sis]ter
2     [Were reigning,] gods Philometors, year 16, under the priests and priestesses and canephorus [of those] being and exist[ing]
3     Month Athur 5, in Pathyreoi, before Hermias of [the supervision of] Paniskus market-overseer[.]
4     Psenonupis, [son of] Portitus, Persian, as [if] fifty years old, less than middle [height], dark-skinned, round-fa[ced]
5     An urban property from the belonging to him and to his partners undividedly, his allotted [share,] of aruras 1/2.
6     Neighbors of the whole land: to the south Patseoutus of Phibius; to the north the remaining land of his; to the east[ern]
7     [The] river; to the west the road of the crowds; or whoever are neighbors on all sides. [The purchaser was] Kalib[is]
8     Petemarsnoufios, Persian [woman], as [if] twenty years old, middle, dark-skinned, downturned-[faced], long-fa[ced]
9     An urban property of bronze coin of one talent. [The] pre-selle[r and] guarantor
10    Of all things according to this purchase [is] Psenenup[is who has so]ld [it],
11    Whom Kalib[is] the [woman] purchaser received.
12    Hermias confirmed [the transaction as] banker.
