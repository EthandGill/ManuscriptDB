[TRANSLATION]
1     [Petesouchos], also called Peteurus, of Selebutus
2     Persian of the later generation, to Hermia, also called Ergus of Petesouchos, greetings. I acknowledge that I have
3     from you wheat, twenty-four artabas
4     one-and-a-half, with the sesquialter, which I will also repay to you in the [month] of Payni of the twenty-second year
5     [established] to [Kerke]usiris by dromic measure.
6     If [indeed] I do not repay to you, I will pay you immediately
7     a price of each artaba of copper drachmae
8     three thousand, or the highest price that will be, and [penalty] of stamped silver drachmae
9     [sixty], and the damage, and to the royal treasury the same. Let this hand be valid everywhere brought forward.
10    Fare well. Year twenty-two, Mecheir twenty-three.
