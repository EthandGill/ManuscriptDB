[TRANSLATION]
1     [Cle]on greetings, the quarrymen at Pastos
2     [work]ing. We received from you
3     the stones at Titnoueis and [they] have been quarried already
4     But now we are idle because we do not have bod[ies/workers]
5     so as to clean up the sand on top
6     of the remaining stone [fr]om the month Epeiph until Thoth 9. And the days which they have worked
7     ten [will] follow the two-month [period]. And you know that [the] place
8     is desolate and we do not have grain. We wish therefore
9     to complete the works so that as quickly as possible
10    we might depart. Send therefore workers to us
11    speedily so that we might not [be] abandoned as well. Fare well.
12    Year 30, Thoth 9
13    To Cleon. Year 30, Thoth 9 from the [quarry]men
14    petition
