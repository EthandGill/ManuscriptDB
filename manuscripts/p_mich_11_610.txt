[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius leased,
2     namely Plutarchos also called Ammonios, former gymnasiarch,
3     councillor of the splendid
4     and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, to Aurelius
5     Kopreus son of Papontos, his mother being Theonis, from the
6     same city, komastes (parade-bearer)
7     of the busts of our lord
8     Probus Augustus
9     and of his Victory, for only the present 8th year, from
10    the property belonging to him
11    around the Iseion of Pangas, the
12    plots on the east of the holding
13    of Nepos, in the place called Peklbth, in
14    two places, … arouras
15    by survey, so as to plant these with flax, at a rent per aroura of one thousand six hundred drachmas. And, the lease being guaranteed,
16    the rent is to be free of risk
17    from all risk, [the]
18    public taxes on the land [be]ing the responsibility of the landholder,
19    who is to be owner of the [cr]ops until he recovers the rent.
20    Of necessity let the lessee pay back
21    the rent in the month Epeiph of the present 8th year without delay, the right of execution
22    being against the lessee [and against all his property] as is fitting.
23    The lease is valid; concerning
24    which the lessee, on being formally questioned, gave his consent.
25    Year 8 of Imperator Caesar
26    Marcus Aurelius Probus
27    Gothicus Maximus, Persicus
28    Maximus, Germanicus Maximus,
29    Pius Felix Augustus,
30    Phaophi. I, Aurelius Plutarchos, have a
31    copy of this.
