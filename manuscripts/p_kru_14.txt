3     of the years from Diocletian to this present day 472,
4     under our master, through Flavius Kometos son of Chael, dioiketes
5     of the fortress of Memnonia.
6     I, Papas son of the blessed Theodoros, his mother
7     being Joudein, of the fortress of Djeme of the nome of the city of
8     Hermonthis, asking afterward for a subscriber to subscribe for me to this document of sale, not transgressing
9     it and not setting it aside and not removing it
10    from the laws, the more to confirm it through other trustworthy witnesses, these who [shall] witness to it according to
11    my request of my own kind, and my readiness:
12    I write to Aaron son of the blessed Shenoute, his mother
13    being Elisabeth daughter of the blessed Joseph, of this same fortress
14    of Djeme of the nome of this same city, Hermonthis, greeting,
15    that I acknowledge, I, Papas son of the blessed Theodoros,
16    the one writing first to you, you Aaron son of Shenoute, the buyer, and the one I have first drawn up, according to this sale that is written,
17    confirming and being valid in a single matter; I have arisen, I have given
18    my attention to it, willing in a single understanding and an
19    undivided reckoning, with a settled faith and a thinking
20    that is upright, without any deceit or fear or extortion,
21    or fraud or deception or circumvention, with no compulsion
22    set upon me, in every good and
23    fair purpose, swearing afterward by God
24    the Almighty and the welfare of our rulers who reign
25    over us now through God, I give it to you out,
26    you Aaron son of the blessed Shenoute, the buyer,
27    and the one whom I have first specified, in every righteous lordship of the law of sale,
28    being fulfilled; and I write to you from this present day, and from now forever, every time,
29    forever, what shall come after you of my whole house
30    which is in this courtyard, this one that is below the house of Philotheos,
31    to the east, this one that came to me on behalf of my blessed
32    father Theodoros, he himself, who had bought it from
33    Trakote daughter of Kyriakos son of …, through a righteous purchase,
34    through a document of sale, this one that I shall give
35    to you now, it being secured for you and those coming after you,
36    according to its ancient placements and in the old reading thus
37    they contain, which are these: its borders, to the south the rear of Paham; to the north the public street, and the door of the chiefs;
38    to the east Thania daughter of the comes; to the west the house of Philotheos.
39    These are the borders of that house on every side,
40    its price being this on which we agreed with one another,
41    namely six gold solidi of refined gold of the measure of the fortress
42    of Djeme, that is 6 nomismata, six, refined, by the weight of the fortress of Memnonia, the full price of this kind,
43    came to my hand from you, you Aaron son of Shenoute,
44    the buyer and the one whom I first named above,
45    many times, through hand into hand, from the house, of approved gold and as capital, so that you, you
46    Aaron son of the blessed Shenoute, the buyer and
47    the one whom I first drew up, may come in and take hold, and
48    be lord, and become the master of the whole house
49    in this courtyard, according to its borders which I have revealed to you,
50    over the whole house foursquare, from its foundations to its walls,
51    with the other appurtenances reckoned to it, that you become its master,
52    you with your children and your heirs and
53    your kindred relations, according to the disposition that I first
54    declared above, to acquire it for you, to acquire it again
55    for you, to administer it, to manage it, to administer it, to beautify it, to dwell in it, to make it for security, to donate it, to cede
56    it, to make it an exchange, to give it out, to grant it,
57    to bestow it, to leave it to your children, to give it
58    to your heirs, to use it in any
59    fashion that pleases you, according to every possession and lordship
60    and eternal holding, lawfully and unhinderedly,
61    that at no season shall anyone come forth against you ever, neither I
62    nor brother nor sister nor first cousin
63    nor second cousin nor any man belonging to my family, who shall raise any pretext
64    against you beside any authority, …
65    for glory … or authority … against this sale,
66    this one, or a portion of it, the whole of it. First, may that one profit nothing primarily; first he shall be a stranger
67    to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, and he shall give to the account a penalty of thirty-six gold solidi to the authority that exacts,
68    in that season, apart from the punishment that the laws ordain against the one who shall dare
69    and transgress against all these things, and shall come in
70    and oppose this sale that is written and confirmed,
71    [it being secu]red for you … secured, having force
72    every … and authority, and they shall not interrogate me, what I acknowledge.
73    I, Konstantine son of …,
74    indeed Papas son of Athanase,
75    we are witness that the sale according
76    to the way it is written. I, Isaac
77    son … of Konstantine,
78    write for them, since they do not understand how to write.
79    I, Papnoute son of the blessed Stephanos,
80    am witness according to the request of the sale.
81    I, Komes the dioiketes, with Athanasios son of Georgios and Apa Piktor son of Komes, we are witness.
82    I, Aristoph[anes] [so]n of Joha[nnes], [the] persons
83    requested me, I wrote for them, the confir …
84    I, Papnoute son of Stephanos, wrote for him,
85    since he does not understand how to write.
86    Through me, Aristophanes son of Johannes, it was made.
