[TRANSLATION]
1     Let it be valid … having carried out the term … the [five] hundred drachmas, the rent, and the expense [that] has been incurred, let him recover … Daimach[os] … and let [Daimachos] guarantee
2     to Diodoros [and to those of Diod]oros’ party what he has leased according to this contract for the contracted period, in accordance with what he has leased. And if he does [not] guarantee it, but Daimachos, or one of Daimachos’ party, or any other on Daimachos’ behalf, ejects
3     Diodoros [in any manner whatsoever,] or those of Diodoros’ party, from what has been leased, whether before the term or after the term has passed, before they have recovered the five hundred drachmas — the rent — and the expense that has been incurred: then the (right of) approach to the one who proceeds (against them) shall be void, and let Daimachos, or the one proceeding on his behalf, pay in addition
4     to Diodoros, or to those of Diodoros’ party, for each approach a penalty of five hundred drachmas of silver, and the five hundred drachmas — the rent — and the expense that has been incurred, forthwith; and none the less let Diodoros be master,
5     and those of Diodoros’ party, of what has been leased, until the period of the lease has passed and they have recovered the five hundred drachmas — the rent —
6     and the expense that has been incurred, according to what has been written; and let the right of execution belong to Diodoros and to those of Diodoros’ party against Daimachos and against the (property) of Daimachos, for the one proceeding according to the ordinance. Surety for the repayment of the five hundred drachmas and the expense that has been incurred and of all the terms of this contract: Agenor son of Daimachos, Mace[donian, of the epigone.] And let this contract be valid wherever it is produced. Witnesses.
