[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelia Herakleidiaina, matrona stolata, daughter of Aurelius Herakleides, former town-councillor
2     of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians, from …
3     through …
4     … [voluntarily]
5     I undertake to lease, for only the present
6     6th year and the 5th year, from the property belonging to you in the embankment-district of Pekty of this nome, in the basin called
7     Psenerotos, thirty-four arouras or however many
8     there may be, in which there are rocky places, at a fixed rent
9     of two talents of silver and five thousand drachmas,
10    free of all risk, the public dues of the land
11    being the responsibility of you the landowner, you being master of the crops until you recover what is owed. And, the undertaking being guaranteed to me, I will pay the rent within three months, month by month,
12    on the fifteenth, in the month Tybi one talent of silver,
13    and in the following month Mecheir another one talent, and in Pharmouthi the remaining
14    five thousand drachmas of the rent without delay, you having the right of execution against
15    me and against all my property.
16    The undertaking is valid, and having been formally questioned by you
17    I gave my assent. Year 6 of the Emperor Caesar Gaius
18    Aurelius Valerius Diocletian and year 5
19    of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Valerius
20    Maximian, Germanici Maximi, Pii,
21    Felices, Augusti … I, Aurelia
22    Herakleidiaina, have received a copy of this.
