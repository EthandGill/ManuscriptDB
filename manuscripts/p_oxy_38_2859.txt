[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Sarapammon son of Heras, his mother being Isidora, chosen decanus, and
2     Paulos son of Horion, his mother being Helene, both from the splendid and most splendid
3     city of the Oxyrhynchites, with a surety for staying and appearance for the following
4     service, Aurelius Horion son of Horion from the same city, to one another
5     greeting. We agree, I Paulos for my part, in your stead, Sarapammon,
6     to go down wherever I be ordered, with the donkey- and camel-beasts and beadles handed over to me, and to take on your place as
7     decanus and discharge it and remain at the
8     public command until I be released, discharging all
9     the things enjoined upon me, so that no complaint or charge may follow. And I shall receive for wages daily, from the following day
10    which is Hathyr 15 until I return, two hundred
11    and fifty drachmas of silver. And on the spot I acknowledge that I have received for the two months
12    two talents and three thousand drachmas of silver. And if I return
13    within the two months I shall restore the monies that shall appear with me. And … the two months being completed, with me attending
14    on the public service, you shall supply me wages until I return.
15    And (that) you are to keep
16    [S]a[r]apammon free from disturbance and trouble bodily concerning absolutely all
17    things pertaining to the service of the decanus. And I, Sarapammon,
18    (agree) to consent to these things and to pay the wages that shall appear without delay. And if any inquiry arise in whatever way concerning …
19    … to the decani, me Sarapammon
20    … according to the part falling to me, and that you, Paulos, are
21    to be free from disturbance concerning this. The documents are valid, written in duplicate
22    so that each may hold one copy. And having formally questioned one another, to one another
23    we acknowledged. Year 18 and year 17 of our sovereigns
24    Diocletian and Maximian Augusti, and year 10 of our sovereigns
25    Constantius and Maximian the most illustrious
26    Caesars, Hathyr 14, in the consulship of Titianus and Nepotianus.
27    I, Aurelius Sarapammon, consent to all the aforesaid and shall pay the wages that shall appear as is set forth above, and on being formally questioned
28    I acknowledged.
29    Theon to Dionysodoros his brother
30    greeting.
31    I was instructed concerning beasts, 60,
32    of the Oxyrhynchite, through Isidoros
33    and Patermouthios, decani,
34    of chaff.
