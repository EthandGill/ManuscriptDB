[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ptollarion son of Ptollarion, former gymnasiarch, and to Gemeinos,
2     former gymnasiarch, and to Amatios also called Pauleinos, both sons of Patron, former gymnasiarch — Gemeinos being absent, through Amatios also called
3     Pauleinos —
4     from Sat[yros] son of Herakleides son of Heraklas, registered in the quarter of [Ta]m[eia], and his own [son P]olion, the two.
5     We wish to lease from you for four years
6     from the present tenth year the lands belonging
7     to you — to Ptollarion as to the half share,
8     [and to the two] as to the re[maining half] share — the
9     … about the village [of Theogon]is, of the holding,
10    [arou]ras [eight, or however many there may be], for the [f]ir[st]
11    and third ye[ar for the s]owing of fodder-[grass] for the gra[zing]
12    [of sheep, without re]nt; and for the [second and]
13    [fourth year for the s]owing of wheat, the rent for the
14    [two-year period together with what I] shall receive of seed
15    [of wheat eigh]t [artabas], for the wh[ole], yearly
16    [for the two-year period], of wheat one hundred and tw[enty arta]bas … by the six-choinix measure [of the granary]
17    [of Ptollari]on and of othe[rs, al]l free of deduction
18    [and risk-free], performing the works of the arouras,
19    [all that is] fitting, at my own cost at the proper sea[sons; and] we shall pay the rents yearly in the month
20    P[auni at the thresh]ing-floor, new, pure, unadulterated, the measuring being done by one of yours, the
21    yearly transport-charges of the urging-on being on you,
22    but the conveyance of the rents on us,
23    and after the time we shall hand over the arouras clean of rush, reed, dog's-tooth grass,
24    and all marsh-weed. If it seem good to lease.
25    Satyros, about 40 years old, scar beside the left ankle
26    Polion, about 20 years old, scar on the little finger of the left hand.
27    The 10th year of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the lord, Hathyr 11.
