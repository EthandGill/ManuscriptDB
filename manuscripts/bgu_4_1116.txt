[TRANSLATION]
1     Sheet (kollema)
2     To Protarchos,
3     from Antonia Philematis, freedwoman of Antonius, wi[th her guardi]an
4     Gaius Iulius Felix, and from Sarapion, the son of Aristeus, a Persian of the ep[igone. Co]ncerning [the points at iss]ue, we agree on these terms, namely that, since Sarapion has leased from
5     Antonia Philematis, for a period of two years from Pharmouthi of the present 1[7th] year of Caesar,
6     the house belonging to her in the Delta together with the appurtenant
7     workshops attached to it, at the fixed rent each month of Ptolemaic
8     sixty silver drachmas, which he will also pay to Antonia Philematis fully each (month), free of any deduction and expense …
9     … of the passing month up to the 29th of the following month, the repairs and the (repairs) of the public … and the … and the roofings, and
10    the imposition for the public conduits and the cutting and the clearing all being
11    on Antonia Philematis, but the rubbish inside (being) on
12    Sarapion, who, having received the house (with its) doors and windows fitted and locked and clean of refuse, (shall) hold to the
13    lease for the agreed period and carry off the surplus produce
14    to his own (account), having the authority to settle in and evict whom
15    he wishes, and to sublet the house to others together with the appurtenant
16    workshops, it not being permitted to him to abandon the lease within
17    the period nor to withhold the monthly rent, but, when the period
18    [has passed], to hand over the house with the standing
19    doors and windows and locks, and moreover with the things
20    being in the house: a millstone and a wooden pulley and a wooden well-sweep in the wall and two chamber-pot stools in the privy, all
21    just as he received them, except for wear and decay; and if he transgresses any of these (terms),
22    he is immediately liable to seizure and can be held under arrest [un]til he pays off
23    whatever he owes toward the rent, with half as much again, and (the value of) whatever he does not hand over, and the damages and expenses, and as a penalty 100 drachmas
24    and the fixed fine, the right of execution belonging to Antonia Philematis both from Sarapion and from
25    [all] his belongings just as if by virtue of a legal decision, (any safe-conducts) being void, and whatever safe-conducts he may adduce, (and) every protection (being ineffective), none the
26    less the authority belonging to Antonia Philematis, if Sarapion violates the agreement,
27    to remove (him) from the lease within the period and to sublet to others
28    and to exact the surplus that shall arise upon the re-letting, but, with him
29    doing each (term) accordingly, Antonia Philematis (is) to furnish him a guaranteed
30    lease for the period
31    or else herself to pay the equal penalty; and Sarapion shall deduct for himself
32    out of the … of the rent 60 drachmas, on account of … ,
33    eight drachmas; and Sarapion, paying the monthly rent to Antonia Philematis, shall receive from her slave Philargyros the
34    … receipt, and Antonia Philematis is to be in no way anxious about the …
35    … Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi … 6.
