[TRANSLATION]
1     Serapion, I have noted [it]. In the 13th year of Hadrian Caesar
2     the lord, Mecheir 7.
3     To Dionysius and Theon, having served as gymnasiarch, keepers of documents
4     of acquisitions of Arsinoe
5     From Gaius Valerius Maior
6     which I have registered through you on the present
7     day concerning the estate of Nestos in the Herakleid division, a homestead allotment of arouras
8     four I wish to transfer
9     to Heraklides, [son] of Diogenes of Ammonieus
10    Sosikosmeios, also called Althaios
11    registered in the earlier [records], clear from
12    debt of brokerage, every guaranty of transfer, [of drachmae] two thousand
13    four hundred. Wherefore I petition in order that
14    you may send [instructions] as is fitting.
