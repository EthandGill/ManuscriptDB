[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Psempnoutis
2     Timothy the carpenter greets.
3     Before all else I greet you many times
4     with all your [people]. I marvel
5     that after so much time you were unwilling to give the money. Therefore, if
6     Pakhoumis comes to you, provide
7     him [with] the three-thousand talents; already
8     for I have received [something] from him. Learn for
9     that if you are unwilling to give him, I make you be demanded by
10    soldiers. Take care lest you neglect [it].
11    Fare well I pray for you
12    for many times.
13    to my lord
14    Psempnoutis
15    [Ti]mothy the carpenter
