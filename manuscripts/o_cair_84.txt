[TRANSLATION]
1     Through Theodore and Psansnōtos and Chesthōtos, tax collectors of Gerdiacus
2     Of year 36 of Caesar, Onnōphris Pedō, 8 drachmas 1 obol 1/2 obol reckoning, Athur Choiach.
3     Drakōn, the one from Chesthōtos the tax collector, countersigned.
4     Theodōrīōn, the one from Theodore the tax collector, countersigned.
5     And for Epōnyuchos, Onnōphris, 8 drachmas 1 obol 1/2 obol reckoning, Thoth Phaōphi Athur Choiach.
6     Drakōn, the one from [...]χ the tax collector, countersigned.
7     Theodōrīōn, the one from Theodore the tax collector, countersigned.
