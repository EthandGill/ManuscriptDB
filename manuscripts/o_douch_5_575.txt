[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother
2     Isokrates [of] Horos, Pappollon, greetings.
3     If [there] come[s] to you
4     The brother Apollon
5     [Of] Hermammon, provide!
6     To him grain [by] measure
7     [Of] iron [measure], artabas
8     Four, [they] total [as] grain
9     [Of] artabas, 4 only.
10    [I,] Pappollon, have signed.
