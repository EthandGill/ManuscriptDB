[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Potamon son of Pecheroutes, mother Taopis, from the city Bubastis
2     To Aurelius Sarapion son of Heron, mother Tachireina, from the city Oxyrhynchus, greetings.
3     I acknowledge that I have exchanged with you a female donkey
4     dark-brown colored foal and I have received from you
5     an equal donkey white complete pregnant marked with a defect on the right eye
6     and I have received from you Sarapion the
7     standing additional assessment of silver
8     drachmas eighty. Drawn in duplicate and having been questioned by one another we acknowledged year 2
9     of the Emperor Caesar Gaius Julius Verus
10    Maximinus Pious Fortunate Augustus and Gaius
11    Julius Verus Maximus the most sacred
12    [Caesar Aug]ustus son of Augustus Payni 30.
