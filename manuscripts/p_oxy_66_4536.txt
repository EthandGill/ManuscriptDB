[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ
2     our God and Savior. In the reign of our
3     most divine and most pious master, greatest
4     benefactor Flavius Heraclius the eternal Augustus
5     and Emperor, in the second year, Phaophi 30,
6     of the 1st indiction.
7     To Flavius Apion the all-praiseworthy and most eminent
8     ex-consul and patrician, landowner
9     also here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,
10    through Menas his servant who puts the question
11    and provides for his own master
12    the same all-praiseworthy man the right of action
13    and liability, the Aurelii Apol[l]os
14    son of Anoup and Erkot son of Apollo,
15    from the village of Pleein of the Oxyrhynchite
16    nome, administered as a pagarchy by
17    your Eminence. We acknowledge
18    to your Eminence that if ever at any
19    time or season we appear
20    to have stolen mechanical
21    implements or oxen or any whatsoever
22    theft made … [or] to have harbored
23    robbers, on the terms that w[e] provide
24    to your Eminence, through those belonging to it,
25    for each
26    offense, of gold nomismatia
27    twenty-four, exacted in fact and by force
28    at the risk
29    of us and of our property.
30    The acknowledgment is valid, single,
31    written, and on being questioned we acknowledged.
32    The commonalty of the names
33    consents for us, this acknowledgment,
34    as set forth. Ioannes
35    I wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate
36    (persons).
37    Acknowledgment of Apollo son of Anoup and Erkot son of Apollo, chief-guards
38    hailing from the village of Pleein of the Oxyrhynchite nome.
