[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and [most piou]s [master,] greatest
2     benefactor Flavius Justinus the eternal Augustus and Emperor, year 12,
3     in the consulship of their serenity for the 2nd time, and of Flavius Tiberius who is also called the New
4     Constantinus our most fortunate Caesar, Pachon 22, in the 10th indiction.
5     To Flavius Apion, the all-praiseworthy and most magnificent, of consular rank
6     ordinary, and patrician, landholder here too in the New City of Justinus,
7     through Menas his servant, who asks and procures
8     for his own master, the same all-praiseworthy man, the right of action and obligation,
9     the Aurelii Phoibammon the steward, son of Praous, and Phib son of Papnouthios
10    and Abraamios son of Philippos and Makaris son of Anoup and Phoibammon
11    son of Papoe and Papnouthios son of Anoup and Abraamios son of Papnouthios
12    and Paulos son of Horos, all originating from the hamlet
13    of Leon, belonging to your magnificence, registered cultivators of it,
14    greeting. We acknowledge, through this our written security,
15    that we are ready, being bound for one another, to give to your
16    magnificence, on account of the landlord's due of the landlord's
17    vines of our property, at the vintage of the month Mesore of the present
18    tenth indiction, of the wine-due of the eleventh indiction, God willing,
19    of wine, three thousand landlord's eight-sextarius measures, together with the remainder of the
20    [arrear of the] present tenth indiction, making landlord's eight-sextarius measures of wine 3,000, and these
21    [we acknowled]ge to deliver up to the cellar in full, without diminution.
22    [And if] we do [not] do this, we acknowledge to give for each jar
23    … at the risk of the things belonging to us. Valid is the bond, written singly, and
24    [having been questioned we acknowledged.] The community of the aforewritten names agrees.
25    … mit … e … s as afore[said.] Ioannes
26    Bond of those from the property of Leon, of acceptable wine, of [landlord's] [m]easures 3,000.
