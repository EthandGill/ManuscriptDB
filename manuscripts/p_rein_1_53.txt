1     From Alupius.
2     From the more timely ones
3     The sent and being-sent camels
4     Load with wine and send to the city to be delivered
5     To Maximus with arrangement for provisions, of which he will account
6     I pray that you are well
7     I have noted
8     To Heroninus, steward, Thrasas
9     Year twelve, Athyr seven
