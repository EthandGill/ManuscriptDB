[TRANSLATION]
1     I received, I Gerontios, cook, son of Philoxenos, from the city of the Arsinites, from the quarter of Moeris
2     from you, Ioustos, the most illustrious administrator of the holy church of Saint Theodore, from my wage,
3     which I make for you [for] the need of the cook, that is to say of the present year, reckoned from
4     the new moon of the past month Phaophi of the present eighth indiction, [one and] half gold coin dirty,
5     [one and] half gold coin dirty. It was written in month Pharmouthi 13 eighth indiction. Through me Ioustos, notary
6     receipt of Gerontios cook, [one and] half coin
