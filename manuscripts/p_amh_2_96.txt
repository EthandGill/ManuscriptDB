[TRANSLATION]
1     …os, her mother being … … registered in the amphodon of the city west, with the guardian her husband Hermes son of Diogenes, who served as kosmetes of the same city, I have followed the
2     [aforesaid] notification [and] have [so]ld from now for all time to Apollonia daughter of Hermione … registered at the West Fort with the guardian Sarapammon son of Ploutas, and however he is styled, the (lands) belonging to me in the Upper Peri Polin (district) near the village of Monkaneis, from the (holding) of Aischrion, together with the … catoecic (allotment), free from claim, four arouras, which I shall deliver in the field, sown, complete, by the right of the catoecate, by rope-measure, clear of the royal (tax) and of every kind whatsoever, out of the common
3     [and undi]vided eighteen arouras by portion, of all of which the neighbors as I dictated are … on the east, domain land and private grounds, on the other (winds), private grounds and other things; of the price agreed upon, of silver
4     four thousand [dra]chmas, total: 4000 drachmas, which I have here received from the buyer [according to the] [aforesai]d notification, and that there belong to her and to her assigns the ownership and possession of the four sold [as af]oresaid arouras,
5     they [usi]ng and managing them in whatever way they choose, and the (persons) from [these] … [cultiv]ating and leasing out and reaping and carrying off the produce arising from them year by year and all that accrues
6     [all] to her own (account) from the present twenty-first year … on [their] behalf the public (dues) yearly being incomplete (?), those of the [pre]vious times up to the past twentieth year and of the twentieth
7     year itself, all public (dues) and supplementary assessments (being) upon me the seller, the warranty throughout against every (claim) following along for me the sel[ler, and that] I shall not bring suit, nor others on my behalf, against the buyer
8     [n]or against her assigns concerning anything of this sale in any way; and if I do bring suit or do not warrant (it), let the action be void and let me pay in addition, or he who shall bring suit on my behalf, to the buyer or to her assigns, both the damages and expenses and as penalty, as a private debt, double the price, and to the treasury an equal sum, and none the less let the sale be valid. Year 21 of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Severus
9     [An]toninus Parthicus Maximus Britannicus Maximus Pius Augustus, Pharmouthi.
