r.1    τὴν δὲ σεσολοικισμένην ἐπισ[τολὴ]ν αὐτῆς ἐδεξάμην καὶ ἐφύλαξα ἵνα πάλιν μὴ ἀρνήσηται

r.5    τῷ τὰ πάντα [θ]ε[ο]φ[ι]λεστάτῳ τιμῆς ἀξίῳ Φοιβάμμωνι διακόνῳ καὶ χαρτουλαρίῳ

[TRANSLATION]
1     I received her worn letter and guarded it so that she might not again deny it
2     according to her custom, and as for the timbers which have been sawn with also two other large pieces from one
3     cubit in length from the branches stored in the stable, send to me at once to Sinela,
4     and the grain provisions and the stalk-provisions, send to my son the master Ioannes
5     to the most [divinely] beloved worthy of honor Phoibammon the deacon and document-keeper
6     Hypatius
