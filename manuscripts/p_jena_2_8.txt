[TRANSLATION]
1     Macarius measured and loaded
2     of Pabion the soldier into the ship of Phoiammôn
3     ... of which the steersman is Kopres
4     of Horos [from] the village Muchis of the Antinoopolitan [district] ... of the [twelfth] indiction grain [pure] with transport fees and one-hundredth
5     [artabas] six become artabas 6 only and for
6     ... twentieth eleventh indiction
7     [artaba] one becomes artaba 1. Aurelius
8     Kopres of Horos the aforementioned through me
9     Phias of Horos the scribe of the ship
10    I issued the receipt as aforesaid
11    having been satisfied with these the surcharges and the
12    sack-carrying fee.
