# HGV-EN: Didymus son of Ptolemaeus, grandson of Ptolemaeus, to the sitologus of the eastern(?) toparchy, district of Ophis(?), greetings. Transfer, from the amount you hold on deposit for me, from the wheat-crop of the past sixth year of Hadrianus Caesar the lord, to Demetrius son of Diogenes(?), . . . (for his account) at Phoboou, thirty . . . artabas of wheat. Year seventh of Hadrianus Caesar the lord, (month, day). (N. Gonis)
