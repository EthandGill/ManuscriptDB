[TRANSLATION]
1   Copy of an agreement. Year 9 of the Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian
2   Augustus, in the month Kaisareios, 2nd intercalary day, in Boubastos of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge
3   Orsenouphis son of Petesouchos, grandson of Herieus, about 28 years old, with a scar on his left foot, and Sotas son of Panephremmis, grandson of Har-
4   pagathes, about 37 years old, with a scar on his right foot, and Sotas son of Psenatymis, grandson of Stotoetis, about 40 years old, without distinguishing marks, and Tese-
5   nouphis son of Stotoetis, grandson of Papeis, about 32 years old, with a scar on the little finger of his left hand, and Hatres son of Satabous, grandson of
6   Tesenouphis, about 32 years old, with a scar on his left knee, and Tesenouphis son of Papeis, grandson of Papeis, about 30 years old, with a scar on his right
7   shin, and Apynchis son of Panephremmis, grandson of Apynchis, about 23 years old, with a scar on the little finger
8   of his left hand, and Pakysis son of Pakysis, grandson of Pakysis, [about ... years old,] with a scar on the little finger of his [left]
9   hand, and Sotas son of Satabous, grandson of Panokneus, about 26 years old, with a scar on his right shin, and Sto-
10   [toetis] son of Stotoetis, grandson of Papeis, about 50 years old, with a scar on the little finger of his left hand — the ten ... 
11   (forming) a decania (collective of ten), to Athenion, village-scribe of Soknopaiou
12   Nesos, about 30 years old, with a white film over his right eye, (acknowledge) that they have received and have had measured out, of the (sums)
13   assigned to the acknowledging parties from the harvest of the 4th year and 5th [year]
14   of Hadrian Caesar the lord, from the granary of Herakleia of the Themistes
15   division, of wheat by the public measure: that Apynchis son of Panephremmis
16   and Stotoetis son of Stotoetis each (received) fifteen artabas, and the rest
17   each five artabas, apart from what was assigned and which they previously received — namely that
18   Apynchis son of Panephremmis and Stotoetis son of Stotoetis (received) from the granary of Boubas-
19   tos, harvest of the 4th year, thirty artabas, and the rest all (received)
20   from the granary of Boubastos, harvest of the 4th year, thirty artabas, and from the granary
21   of Herakleia, harvest of the 4th year and 5th year, ten artabas — so that for the same two each
22   cultivator (received) forty-five artabas, the price of which, at the rate of seven drachmas one obol per artaba,
23   Athenion declares that he has paid them in full.
24   Panephremmis (called) Demas, about 50 years old, with a scar on the little finger of his left hand, I consent
25   with the cultivator Apynchis. Subscriber: Kephalon son of Diodoros. ―
