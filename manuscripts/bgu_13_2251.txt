[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: And by the (village secretary) of Apias: Dios the Younger, son of Ision ... from the metropolis. He was assigned in Apias. For the superintending of the marshes in Theadelphia: Abus, son of Ision and Tapsois, from the metropolis, owning land in Euhemeria. Pachon. For the drawing for grain-tax collector of Ptolemais Melissurgon (were proposed) Thonis, also called Labe.as, son of Thonis and Thanus, from the village; Orseus, son of Orseus and Herais, and grandson of Heron. From Tebetny, etc.
