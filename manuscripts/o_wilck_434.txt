[TRANSLATION]
1     Kametis [son] of Petearpheus registered
2     Amphiomios for poll-tax of Notos and Libos year 5
3     drachmae 8. Year 5 of Vespasian the lord
4     Mechir 23, similarly Phamenoth 4 drachmae 4,
5     similarly Phamenoth 29 drachmae 4,
6     [similarly] Pharmouthi 22 drachmae 4,
7     similarly Mesore 5 drachmae 4, similarly
8     6th year Thoth 29 drachmae 3 obol, which are also drachmae 2 obols 5 [and a half].
