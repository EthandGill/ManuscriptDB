[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Dionysios and those with him,
2     overseers of the sacred gate of Syene, through me, Marcus
3     Annius Nemonianus, collector. There has measured,
4     Tameat, for the third estimate of myrobalanus (behen-nuts),
5     of the produce of year 26, in the name of Peteptauet, of (his) own
6     basket, from three matia,
7     the share falling to him.
8     Year 27, Hathyr
9     8.
