[TRANSLATION]
1   Triadelphos, collector. Paid:
2   Harpaesis son of Phenophis, pastoph-
3   oros of Ammon the greatest god,
4   for the admission-fee (eiskritikon): eight drachmas
5   (and) three obols — total 8 dr. 3 ob.
6   Year 10 of Hadrian Caesar
7   the lord, Mesore, 5th intercalary day.
8   (2nd hand) through Pachnoubis, his bro-
9   ther.
