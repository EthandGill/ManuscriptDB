[TRANSLATION]
1   …, the agreed [artabas of wheat,]
2   one hundred and fifty, [which]
3   Apollophanes is [to de]liver, [from]
4   the present twenty-<sixth> year,
5   at the harbour of the village of Denneus …,
6   handing over new wheat, clean,
7   unadulterated, by the measure of Hermes and a
8   [ju]st strickle, ready for loading, into the (ship) to be
9   provided by the agents of
10   […], a ship, without any postpone-
11   ment; or else [Apo]llophanes is liable to seizure,
12   as one having transacted as a Persian
13   of the epigone, and to be held until
14   he delivers the one hundred
15   fifty artabas of wheat with half as much again, or the price
16   of each artaba, the highest prevailing
17   at the time in Alexandria,
18   and the damage, the right of exe[cution]
19   being both from him and from all his proper-
20   ty, as if by legal judgement,
21   [void being (the pledges)] and whatever (pledges) of trust he may bring
22   [forward — all of them — (and) every protection].
