[TRANSLATION]
1     Horos to Khesilaos, greetings. You wrote to us on the 5th of Pachon
2     [to withdraw] from the bank for the embankments copper drachmas 1000
3     and when we had sent for the village-chiefs according to village
4     [and ce]rtain of the people, in order that we might distribute to them and [it] might be worked
5     [the embankments,] and when we had delivered the from-Platon
6     [letter] to the banker in Phus, he said [it] did not [exist]
7     [Well] then you will do, having taken thought, in order that [the embankments] [may be completed]
8     Farewell. Year 9, Pachon 16
9     [Year 9,] Pachon 16
10    Horos, wage for [the] works. To Khesilaos
