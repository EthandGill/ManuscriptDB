[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarcho[s],
2   from Gaius [.....]uten[o]s and from Zmyrna daughter of Asklepiades,
3   a Persian woman, with as guardian her husband Helenos son of Caesar's freedman, [a Persian of the epigo(ne), and] he
4   Helenos himself. Zmyrna and Heleno[s] agree to have from Gaius a loan by hand
5   from house to house of Ptolemaic silver one hun[dred] drach[mas, of interest]
6   of two drachm(as?) per mina by the month, which also they will repay
7   within two months from Tybi of the present sixth and
8   twentieth year of Caesar without any postponement, or to pay
9   the loan with a half as much again, and the interest in single amount,
10   and for the over[due time] the like, the exaction [being made]
11   for Gaius from both the persons below
12   and they not bringing forward pledges [or]
13   being liable jointly to each other for repayment, both from one of them, whichever he chooses,
14   and from the
15   property belonging to them [..]..meso( ) as if by judgement.
16   (Year) 26 of Caesar, Tybi 1.
