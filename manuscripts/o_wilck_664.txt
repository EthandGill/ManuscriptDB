1     Premaos and partners, overseers of the weavers'-tax,
2     to Horos (son of) Diogenes, greeting.
3     We have received for the tax of the month Mecheir of year 31,

5     and likewise in the name of Besis (his) son …, total drachmas 7.
6     Year 31 […].
