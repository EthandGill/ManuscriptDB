[TRANSLATION]
1     It was given through George the notary … to [wo]rkers concerning the embankments from the south of the estate of Kotyleion
2     In the 8th indiction fortification 9th indiction … for numismata two, one-third twenty-fourth of the numismation 1 double-measures 31, double-measures 74, double-measures 66, there results wine double-measures 66 alone.
3     With the signature of the comes Diogenes.
4     Year 221 190, month Mesore 21, indiction 8th.
