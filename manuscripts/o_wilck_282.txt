[TRANSLATION]
1   Pachnoubis son of Psenchnoubis, mother Thinau( ),
2   to Bienchis son of Papremithes, greeting.
3   I acknowledge that I have received from you, out of what
4   you owe me by the note of hand, for in-
5   terest, 16 drachmas, and for the principal
6   another 40 drachmas. Year 6 of Imperator
7   Caesar Marcus Aurelius
8   [Sev]erus the lord, Epeiph 20.
