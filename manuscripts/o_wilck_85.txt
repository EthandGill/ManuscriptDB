[TRANSLATION]
1   Pachnoumis (son of)
2   Phanophis, mother Tachoumtbekis, paid,
3   for the poll-tax of the 15th year of Trajan
4   the lord: seventeen drachmas, and
5   for the surcharge (diplon), two obols (and) two chalkoi — total 17 dr. 2 ob. 2 ch.
6   Year 16 of Trajan the lord, Mecheir 19,
7   through Lucius Aurelius the overseer.
