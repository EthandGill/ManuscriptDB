[TRANSLATION]
1     [A]pollonius the general to Akoute
2     The toparch of Tebtynis, greetings.
3     Immediately send to me a report
4     Of those [matters] up until today having been written
5     Item by item, for thus I will know
6     Whether I will let you [remain] in place
7     Doing something or having summoned
8     (I) will send to the gover[nor] as [neglecting the collection].
9     Farewell.
10    Year 9 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus
