[TRANSLATION]
1     [of the reign] and consulship of the most divine and
2     most pious our master Flavius Justinus the eternal
3     Augustus and Autocrator, year 1, Hathur 23, indiction 15.
4     To the most noble Heraïs, daughter of the blessed
5     Theon, from the renowned Oxyrhynchites
6     [of the] city, Heraïs, servant-girl of the renowned house of the
7     most excellent [person] from consuls ordinarii
8     Apion and patrician, daughter of Hyacinthos
9     [of] mother Constantina from the same
10    city, greetings. I willingly take upon myself to lease
11    from the new moon of Phamenoth, the present month
12    of the fifteenth indiction, the distinguished
13    to your nobility, whole house facing
14    toward the east situated in this
15    city, on the neighborhood of the alley of the inn
16    of Apollo, with all the spaces in it
17    [which are] level and upper-story and courtyard and
18    well and all the whole of it
19    surrounding, both material and incorporeal, and
20    I will pay to her for the rent of this [house] yearly
21    gold coins two; [there are] gold coins 2, in private weight
22    [being] reckoned; which rent I will pay per year through six-months
23    the hal[f], and whenever you wish I agree
24    the occupancy of the same house unharmed
25    undamaged to hand over as I also received [it]. Valid
26    [is] the lease simply written; and being asked I agreed.
27    Heraïs, servant-girl, daughter of Hyacinthos
28    this lease agrees with me
29    concerning the aforementioned rent
30    as aforesaid. … [I wrote] for
31    her being [illiterate.]
