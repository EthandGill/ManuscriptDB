[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of our most divine and most pious mas]ter Flavius Justinianus the eternal
2     [Augustus and Emperor, in the ]17th [year], in the 4th (year) after [the] consulship of Flavius Basilius the most illustrious, Mecheir 12, of the 7th indiction.
3     [To Flavius Apion the most ma]gnificent ordinary [con]sul, landlord, also here in the splendid
4     [city of the Oxyrhynchites, through M]enas [his] servant who puts the question and provides to his own
5     [master the same most ma]gni[f]icent man the action and obligation, Aurelius Serenos,
6     [a millstone-cutter by tra]de, son of Helias, [o]rig[ina]ting from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
7     [greeting. I acknowled]ge through this my written security that I am ready to work
8     from … for the time of my life on all the millstones and round-millstones
9     and baskets that are in each place of your estate, both in the bakehouse
10    and in all the properties and in the oil-presses of your magnificence, unhesitatingly
11    and unblamably, so that no fault or neglect or censure or slackness
12    arise concerning me, apart from sickness and some affliction; (and) that I receive from
13    your magnificence, for each new round-millstone and new basket,
14    two and a half gold solidi reckoned by the private standard, I taking
15    the old stones, and … (that I) not withdraw from your [service]
16    or from the works that are to be done. But if I do this, I am to give
17    to your magnificence by way of penalty twenty-four gold solidi. And if
18    your magnificence, through its administrators, casts me out without censure
19    or slackness or any cause whatever, it too is to be subject to the same penalty. Having pledged
20    for the right of this agreement all my property and future property, both what I now have
21    and [whatever else] I shall acquire hereafter, specifically and generally, by way of pledge and right of mortgage.
22    Valid is [the] agreement, written in duplicate, and on being questioned I acknowledged it. Aurelius Serenos,
23    millsto[ne-cu]tter by trade, son of Helias the aforesaid, have made this agreement
24    [accor]ding to the foregoing manner and all things agree with me as aforesaid. Aurelius
25    … [son] of the late Isak, having been asked, I wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
26    Through me, Iustus the subdeacon, it was completed, 12, of the 7th indiction.
27    … of [Sere]no[s] the millstone-cutter by trade, son of Helias, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites.
