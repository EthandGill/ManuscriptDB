[TRANSLATION]
1     Marcus Antonius, Autocrator
2     of three men [for the management] of public affairs
3     of restoration to the community of the Greeks from Asia, greetings. And
4     when previously he met with me in Ephesus
5     Mark Antonius Artemidorus, who
6     my friend and anointer, together with the eponymous [president] of the synod of those from
7     the inhabited world, sacred-victors and crown-wearers, priest Charopenus of Ephesus,
8     concerning [that] what pre-existed for the synod to remain inalienable, and concerning the
9     rest which he was requesting from me, honors
10    and benefices of exemption from military service
11    and exemption from all liturgies and lack of station-masters and of truces regarding the festival and asylum and
12    purple, in order that he might agree to write
13    immediately to you, I agree
14    wishing and for my friend Artemidorus and the eponymous priest of theirs also for the order of the
15    synod and its increase to grant a favor. And now again when Artemidorus met with me, how
16    it might be possible for them to dedicate a bronze tablet and to inscribe into it concerning
17    the aforementioned benefices,
18    I, having resolved to delay Artemidorus in nothing concerning s[om]e
19    when he met with me, I granted the [ded]ication of the tablet as he beseech[es me].
20    But I have written to you concerning these things.
