[TRANSLATION]
1     of the present … [the half-share belonging]
2     to you, half-share of one exedra
3     in the second story facing south,
4     and half-share of one vaulted chamber in the
5     ground floor, with the portion falling (to it) of the well and of the
6     courtyard and of the roof-terrace and of all the
7     appurtenances and rights, of the house situated
8     in the (city) of the Hermopolites, in the quarter of the City
9     eastward, in the street of the Cobblers, facing
10    east, in common with your
11    sister Maria for the other half-share,
12    to the completion of these (shares), for my own use
13    and dwelling, at a rent for the [same] half-
14    share of the two places per year of
15    two gold keratia, total 2 gold keratia, which rent I shall pay to you at the close of each year without delay,
16    and whenever you should wish to have (them back), I shall hand over to you
17    the same half-share of the exedra and of the
18    vaulted chamber, with doors and keys, as I received (them).
19    The lease is valid and secure. And on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
20    I, Aurelius Iordanes son of Ophis, the aforesaid,
21    have leased as is set forth above. I, Aurelius
22    Theodoros son of Ptolemaios, from Hermopolis, wrote for him,
23    since he does not know letters. I, Theophilos son of Hypatios,
24    from Hermopolis, bear witness to the lease, having heard (it)
25    from the contracting party. I, Aurelius Theodoros
26    son of Christodoros, from Hermopolis,
27    bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the contracting party.
28    Through me, Koullouthos, by the grace of God notary.
29    … (lease of) [Iordan]es son of Ophis the fuller, from Hermopolis, for a rent of 2 gold keratia.
30    Iordanes.
