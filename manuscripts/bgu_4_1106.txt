1   (1st hand) [To Protarcho]s, president of the tribunal,
2   (2nd hand) [from] Marcus Aemilius, son of Marcus, of the tribe Callidia(?), a[nd from]
3   Th[eod]ote daughter of Dositheos, Persian, wi[th as guardian]
4   a[nd s]urety for what is set out in this agree[ment]
5   her husband Sophron, [son of]
6   […]…archos, Per[s]ian of the [epi-]
7   [gone.] Concerning the matters agreed, [Theo-]
8   dote [consents,] for a period of [eighteen] months [from Pha-]
9   menoth of the current 17th <year> of Cae[sar, to]
10   [nurse and suckle, outside,] at her own home [in the city,]
11   [with her own milk, pure and unspoiled,]
12   [the foundling slave infant at the breast of his, Tyche,]
13   [that Marcus has entrusted to her,]
14   [receiving from] him each m[onth as]
15   [wage for the milk and the nursing, with]
16   [oil, 8 silver drachmas. And Theo-]
17   [dote] has received, through her surety S[o]phron, from M[arcus,]
18   [from hand to hand ou]t of the house, for the aforesaid [eighteen] mon[ths,]
19   the nursing-wages of nine months gathered to[gether —]
20   seventy-two [drachma]s; and if it happen within [these]
21   that the child suffer the human lot, [Theo-]
22   [dote,] taking up another child, [shall nurse]
23   [and suck]le it and present it to Marc[us for]
24   [the] same nine months, taking nothing at
25   [all,] because she has undertaken to nurse a-
26   gainst death; and, being kept in good order with the re[maining]
27   monthly nursing-wages, she is to exercise the proper care
28   both of herself [and of the] child, not
29   [spoili]ng her milk, nor sleeping with a man, nor con-
30   ceiving, nor suckling another child be-
31   sides; and whatever she receives or is entrusted with, to keep safe
32   and return whenever demanded, or to pay the
33   value of each item, except manifest loss, on which, hav-
34   ing also been made evident, she shall be released; and not to qu[it]
35   the nursing within the period. And [i]f she trans-
36   gresses anything, she and Sophron are to be liable to seizure
37   and held until they pay back both the nursing-wages she has re-
38   ceived and whatever she may receive, with half again, and the
39   damages and expenses and a further 300 silver drachmas, the
40   right of execution belonging <to Marcus> upon both — being
41   mutual sureties for the payment — and upon one, and upon whichever
42   of them he chooses, and upon all their belong-
43   ings, as if by legal judgment; void being also
44   whatever pleas they may bring forward, every shel-
45   ter; and, she performing each thing, that [Mar-]
46   [cu]s Aemilius furnish her the monthly
47   nursing-wages for the remaining nine months, and not [take]
48   the child away within the period, or him-
49   self pay the same penalty. And Theodote shall come over
50   to Marcus … of the month …
51   willingly, with the child too, for it to be inspected
52   by him.
