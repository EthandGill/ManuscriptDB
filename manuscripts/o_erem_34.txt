r.1    Ἰοῦστος Λ[ο]λλίῳ χαίρειν. τοὺς ἥλους,

[TRANSLATION]
1     Justus to L[o]llius greetings. The nails,
2     which you bought, keep carefully, so that
3     Tyrannus the helmsman might testify to me, that
4     through him together they were spent. The wood
5     the … kinon the one in the courtyard, if you need,
6     [for the] [c]hemata take and cavities,
7     if you need, from the roof[s] take,
8     I sent you other drachmas 12; [these] amount to drachmas 40.
