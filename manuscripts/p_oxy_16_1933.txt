[TRANSLATION]
1     through me then [he] beseeches you to send his garments on account of
2     the holy festival. Many [times I] greet the feet of your [bright] protection
3     I greet, and I beseech on my behalf reverence-paying to my master
4     the most magnificent [one] of yours, [your] brother, and the lady Indarus.
5     But the garments of brother Theodore the goldsmith, having been found, they gave
6     to the archsymmachus of the church, [and] if not entirely he received,
7     [but] all the same part. But nothing completely is harmed
8     , since [some] part of them was found.
