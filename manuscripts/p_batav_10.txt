[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a cession (deed of withdrawal).
2     In the twentieth year of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus,
3     in the month Daisios, Pharmouthi 6, in Bakchias of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite
4     nome. Horaine daughter of Pasion son of Pasion, about 39 years old, with a scar on her right foot,
5     with as guardian the husband of her daughter Thaisarion daughter of Chairemon, namely
6     Apollinarios son of the other Chairemon, about 28 years old, with a scar on the right foot, acknowledges to Thaisarion
7     daughter of Didymos son of Herodes, about 30 years old, with no distinguishing mark, with as guardian her kinsman Anoubas son of
8     Maron, about 71 years old, with a scar on the little finger of the left hand — in return for which the acknowledging woman says
9     that she owes to Thaisarion daughter of Didymos, from hand to hand without written bond, two hundred drachmas of silver,
10    eighty (more), that she cedes to her, in accordance with this agreement, from
11    now for all time, of the property belonging to her, the acknowledging woman,
12    in one portion (sphragis) of a purchased catoecic allotment, a half aroura,
13    of which the neighbours, as they jointly declared, are: on the south the formerly
14    allotment of Alitaios son of Apollonios, on the north a canal through which the allotment is watered, on the east the allotment of the aforewritten Thaisarion daughter of Didymos,
15    on the west the allotment formerly of Thermoutharion daughter of Heron and of others,
16    and that the acknowledging woman and her agents shall guarantee to Thaisarion
17    and her agents the (property) ceded according to this agreement
18    with every guarantee, and shall furnish it free from all public dues up to the past 19th year and the
19    19th year itself of Hadrian Caesar the lord, and free from private claims for all time and from
20    every kind of claim and from all catoecic registry-offices and every kind whatsoever; and if the acknowledging woman does not guarantee as stated above, let her pay the price double, and as a penalty one hundred and forty drachmas of silver, and to the treasury the
21    same amount; and whenever Thaisarion may wish, she herself shall register
22    through the property-record library the aforesaid half
23    aroura, being content with a copy of this agreement and not requiring the presence of the acknowledging woman. Subscriber: …
