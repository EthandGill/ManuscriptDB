1     Aurelius Epimachos to Demetrius
2     to the companion, do well.
3     I marvel how through Cornelius you did not write
4     to me, I having sent word to you through Criton, but to me inquiring from
5     him it became clear that you habitually
6     conduct yourself. Your son I have sent for many times and I have given orders
