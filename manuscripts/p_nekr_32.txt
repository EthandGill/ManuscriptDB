[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Heron son of Kastor, cavalry soldier of the promoti
2     secundi from the second Trajanic legion stationed
3     in Tentyra under Makrobios the commander, to Aurelius
4     Apianos son of Ka…os, gate-guard, from the village of Kysis
5     of the Hibite nome, greeting.
6     I acknowledge that I have sold you in Tentyra a camel,
7     a white female, in the present 18th and 17th and 10th (year),
8     for the price mutually agreed, of silver,
9     new imperial coinage, nine talents,
10    making 9 talents, which I have here received from you in number, in full,
11    on which I was also persuaded, and that you the buyer shall have
12    valid ownership and inalienable possession of it from now
13    for ever, using and managing
14    it in whatever way you choose, both you and all who shall inherit after you. I will guarantee to you this sale with every
15    guarantee against everyone who shall come against it both from me
16    in name or also from any other, simply, at every time, without delay.
17    And this sale, written simple, shall be valid and
18    firm; and being formally questioned I acknowledged.
19    In the 18th and 17th [and 10th] year of our lords Diocletian
20    and Maximian the Augusti and Constantius and Maximian
21    the most noble Caesars, Pharmouthi 30.
22    Aurelius Heron son of Kastor, the aforesaid
23    soldier, I have sold the aforesaid camel and have received the price
24    as aforesaid.
