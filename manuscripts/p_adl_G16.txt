[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy surnamed Alexander and Berenike his sister, the Philometor gods,
2     in the 15th year, with the priests and priestesses and kanephoros being those in office, in the month Pachon 8, in Pathyris, before Hermias the
3     deputy of Paniskos the agoranomos.
4     Pates son of Poeris, Persian of the epigone, about 45 years old, of medium height, honey-colored, with straight hair, long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on the forehead on the right, has sold the dry grain-bearing land belonging to him, undivided, lying in the plain north of Pathyris,
5     of three parcels, which he himself also purchased from Pa-nas son of Pates; of the first, neighbors: on the south the land of Patephis son of Pemsais,
6     on the north the land of Pakoibis, which the sons of Phibis son of Panechates hold; on the east a royal road; on the west the land of Psemmonthes son of Panechates,
7     of the other called (the plot) of Phamounis: on the south the land of Pathotes son of Patous; on the north the land of Patseous son of Phibis; on the east the river; on the west the land
8     of the (plot) called (that) of Haroeris, between which is a road; of the other called Choma, neighbors: on the south the land of Onnophris son of Imouthes, which Petosiris holds,
9     a priest, and Eunous son of Patseous; on the north Chenaibis son of Portis; on the east a canal called (that) of Telon; on the west a mountain; or whoever the neighbors may be
10    on all sides. Horos son of Nechoutes, about 50 years old, bought it for four talents of copper. Guarantor of all matters relating to this sale
11    is Pates the seller, whom Horos the buyer accepted.
12    Hermias the deputy of Paniskos: I have transacted (this).
