[TRANSLATION]
1     Since the most holy bishop commanded me through Peter the
2     younger one, the one delivering to her my present letter[,]
3     do well by the command of the most holy man to give [him]
4     the rations of the same hallowed man. Rather, for α …
5     he has many children, and does not find to support them. But as is the custom
6     by the command of the same most holy man, you will do this. And if
7     seized Kōma and [learned] what somehow about the most magnificent
8     charulary, write to me.
9     To my master, most shining in all respects, worthy of all honor, genuine brother [Ge]orgios, [count], administrator Victor, with God, [anti-geouchos][.]
