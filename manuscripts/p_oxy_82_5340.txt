[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master, greatest benefactor,
2     Flavius Justinus the eternal Augustus and Imperator, in the seventh year, in the consulship of
3     their Serenity for the 2nd time, Pachon 15, of the fifth indiction, year 248 = 217.
4     To the office of the exactor of the division and the house of Theon of respected memory, through you,
5     Kyros the reverend administrator of this new city of Justinus, Flavius Ioannes,
6     scholastikos, tax-collector of this province of the Arcadians, son of Serenus of splendid
7     memory. May your Reverence be willing, from the public rolls stored with it,
8     to lighten my name and to burden the name of Stephanous, my most decorous daughter,
9     with the consent of Markos her most learned husband, for each year, from the embole (grain-levy)
10    and gold-taxes of the God-willing coming sixth assessment, both of it and for all the
11    time hereafter: for the embole, of the standard grain sixty-three artabas together with the
12    freight-charges of these to Alexandria and transport and all kinds of expenses, and for the canonical (dues)
13    the twenty-two carats of gold, by the public standard, that are also paid to the national gold-buyer for the time being,
14    and for the arca-dues, paid to the arca-keeper for the time being
15    or embolator, twenty-two and a half carats of refined gold, making by the public (standard) carats
16    twenty-four. For these it seemed good that they should discharge (them) on behalf of the immovable
17    dowry-property in the country given to the same most decorous daughter of mine, Stephanous,
18    property, for each year; and so that your Reverence may know
19    and for the security of the public account, I have made the present notice of the
20    lightening with my subscription as aforesaid. I, Flavius Ioannes, scholastikos, son of Serenus of
21    splendid memory, the aforewritten, have made the present notice of the lightening of the yearly discharge of the
22    sixty-three artabas of the standard grain together with the
23    freight-charges of these to Alexandria and transport and all kinds of expenses
24    and of the twenty-two carats of gold by the public standard for the canonical (dues) and of the other twenty-two and a half carats of refined gold
25    into twenty-four carats public, to you, Kyros the reverend administrator of the house of Theon, and, being in agreement with all the aforewritten,
26    I subscribed as aforesaid.
27    Notice of lightening made by Ioannes, scholastikos, son of Serenus of splendid
28    memory, from the new city of Justinus.
