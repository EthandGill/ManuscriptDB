[TRANSLATION]
1     [To so-and-so] priest enarch exegete councillor of the Oxyrhynchite city
2     [From Aurelia Techosarion] also called Isidora, daughter of Panaretius also called Theon from the same city.
3     [I request] … [at my own] risk [a guardian] of my underage children
4     … of their paternal inheritance Aurelius Achilles
5     [of Theon] … … the uncle on their father's side being well-off
6     … [present] and consenting to the assumption of guardianship on the condition that he
7     … to me because of necessity and [I being a follower] … [little document] in order that the subsequent affairs of these might come about and
8     [their rights might not] be lost. Year 2 of Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
9     [Antoninus Pius] Fortunate Augustus Phao(phi). Aurelia Techosarion
10    [also called Isidora has presented (this petition)] Aurelius Serenus I have subscribed as her guardian, having been asked
11    [and I have written on her behalf] [she] not knowing letters. Aurelius Achilles son of Theon I consent
12    … [servant through] Aurelius Origenes son I presented.
13    … servant I presented (it) Hathyr 3.
