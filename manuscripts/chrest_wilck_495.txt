[TRANSLATION]
1     To Cosmos, chief of the entry …
2     from Satyros, [son] of Satyros …
3     from the Island. I wish …
4     to take you along with the aulos-players …
5     and … three [maidens?] and four girls to serve in the aforesaid village for six days from …
6     the 18th of the present month …
7     Phaophi, and you receiving …
8     each day in silver drachmas …
9     forty, and we shall hand over whatever we might receive …
10    and we shall provide care [and] attention and shall give …
11    further, four donkeys [for the] descent and [on the] ascent …
12    the same [number].
13    Year 6 of Antoninus and Verus …
14    of the lords Augusti, Phaophi …
15    the 4th.
