[TRANSLATION]
1     [of the consulship of the] lords
2     our Constantine [the] Augustus the
3     fifth and of Licinius Caesar the first.
4     To Valerius Ammonianus, who also
5     Gerontius, administering the logistical office of Oxyrhynchus
6     from the [association] of the oil-sellers of the splendid Oxyrhynchite city
7     through me Aurelius Ammonius Tryphon. In accordance with the things commanded with [particular] assessment I declare the following written [price] of the purchased goods which I handle, and I swear the divine oath
8     that nothing has been falsified. It is:
9     of oil, the κνίδιον, one, denarii five hundred …
10    of the aforesaid consulship, Phamenoth 30.
11    Aurelius Ammonius I have submitted declaring as aforesaid. Aurelius
12    Horion I wrote letters on behalf of him, not knowing [how to write].
