[TRANSLATION]
1     [Chaeremon] to Apollonius the most beloved, greetings.
2     And through another letter I asked you to send
3     to me what from your account is lacking, as I myself
4     have need. I ask you then through Daeus to give them, who will testify to you something under oath. And I think that you will certainly do this.
5     Send then to me through Daeus wine, four two-quart measures. And of gods
6     being willing, and I myself soon will greet you … the
7     children of yours, with whom also be well.
8     Phaophi 16
9     To Apollonius the most beloved …
