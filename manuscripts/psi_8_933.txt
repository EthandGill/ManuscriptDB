[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the consulate of Flavius Iōannēs the most glorious, Mesore intercalary [days], second indiction.
2     To the [admini]strator of the holy monastery of Apa Agēnios [the] mountain of the Apollōnopolite
3     sma[ll] nome, under the administration of the most glorious count Ammōnios of the divine council
4     th[rough] the most venerable Apa Apollōs, Dioskoros, Psimānōbet, from Aphroditē
5     of the village of the Antaiopoli[t]e nome from Aurelius Georgios, from [the] father
6     Iō[sēph], [of] Herakle[i]ou, from mother Nonna, remaining in the same village Aphroditē, greetings.
7     [willing]ly and of my own choice ready I have [myself] for four years
