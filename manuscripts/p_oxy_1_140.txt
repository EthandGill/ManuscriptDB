[TRANSLATION]
1   † In the reign of our most divine and most pious master Flavius Justinian the
2   eternal Augustus and Emperor, year 24, in the 8th year after the consulship of Flavius
3   Basilios the most illustrious, Pachon 1, indiction 13, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites. †
4   To Flavius Serenos the most magnificent and most distinguished comes of the divine
5   consistory, son of Martyrios of illustrious memory, landholder here
6   in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, Aurelius Serenos alias Kortibos, son
7   of Ioustos, his mother being Maria, a stableman of the express post (cursus velox) from the same
8   city, greeting. I acknowledge of my own free will and self-chosen purpose that I have made an agreement
9   with your magnificence for one year, reckoned from this present
10   day, which is Pachon, the new-moon (first) day of the current year 295/226 of the present
11   thirteenth indiction, at the beginning and with the harvest of the, God willing, fourteenth indiction-cycle,
12   on the condition that I fulfill the office of stableman of the same express post on behalf of the household of your magnificence
13   throughout this city, and I am ready to ... and ...
14   in its (your) own stable, and to discharge all the duties pertaining to the same service of the stableman
15   blamelessly and without slackness and irreproachably with all zeal
16   and genuineness and free goodwill and good faith, so that no blame
17   or negligence or any reproach concerning me may arise, apart from sickness and any pain;
18   and that I receive from your magnificence by way of wages for myself and the same
19   grooms,
20   for the whole of the same single year, of wheat by the kankellon measure eighty artabas, and for
21   barley and vegetables nine gold solidi reckoned by the private standard, and of wine
22   eighty estate-jars (knidia), and for the expense of green-fodder another half solidus,
23   this too being current (legal tender), together also with my own animal called Perissos. And I further
24   acknowledge that I have already received from your magnificence by way of earnest-money (arrabon)
25   four and a half gold solidi, and that it is not permitted to me within the same single year
26   to withdraw from your service and attendance. And if I do this, I acknowledge that the
27   same four and a half solidi I will restore to her (it) double. But if your
28   magnificence should wish to dismiss me from the service of the stableman before the completion of
29   the
30   same single year without any cause, on the condition that she too forfeit the same four and a half
31   solidi of the earnest-money. The contract is valid, written in single copy, and on being formally questioned
32   I gave my acknowledgment. (hand 2) † Aurelius Serenos son
33   of Ioustos the aforewritten, I have made this contract according to the foregoing
34   manner, and it is satisfactory to me
35   as stated above. I, Apollon son of Herakleides, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
36   † Through me, Philoxenos, it was completed, (Pachon?) ..., indiction 13 ... † (verso)
37   † Contract of Serenos the stableman, son of Ioustos, from the (city) of the Oxyrhynchites.
