[TRANSLATION]
1     From
2     A petition was just now brought here by the people from the village of Aphrodite, since they are being demanded
3     for gold of recruits, thirty-six solidi, and on account of those dispatched, twelve solidi.
4     But behold then, since they wrote here many piteous things, as being destitute and unable
5     already to pay even their own public taxes, let your brotherhood deign to be content, on account of the gold of recruits, with thirty solidi, and on account of the one dispatched
6     single recruit, with six solidi; for it is a concern to discharge each matter to the possible
7     measure. Postpone the payment entirely until the new moon or the second of the month.
8     … then indeed deign to make (them) be found in this city, and to write to me
9     … of the settlers of the Hermopolites. And thus
10    … discharging the matter. Deign not to trouble them to come down
11    to this city on account of this matter, nor to allow them to be troubled over another (matter)
12    to come here into the magistrate's office, as is likely, until they receive my letters.
13    To the most esteemed lord Taurinos the singularis. † (God is my help) †
