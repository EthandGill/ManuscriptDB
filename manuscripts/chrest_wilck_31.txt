[TRANSLATION]
1     Year twenty of Autocrat Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pious, Thoth, fifth [day]
3     through the in Memphis of Sarapion of Areios, having served as chief priest of the Hadrianeion of the city of the Memphites, bank,
4     Anubian, of Anubian, Memphite from gymnasium,
5     son of colonist of Heliopolis, to Labaites, of Anubian, mother Tnepherota, Memphite, tax-farmer, remaining of price
6     which he purchased from him and received, of ship dung-transporting, river, with one pole, of which the Labaites had
7     from Anubian according to record through the in Memphis
8     of Apollonios of Asklepiad, bank, in the passed [year] …
9     acquittance of Labaites.
