[TRANSLATION]
1     [Ag]... Philotheos, Peton to Zenon greetings. [We have sent to you] ...
2     [a copy] of the [petition] sent to us from Theon [which he gave]
3     Onnophris [with respect to] Neoptolemos. Give then to him the [copy and]
4     [send] to us, in order that [it] may be resolved against him ...
5     if [he does not] meet [with us], we will condemn him [for the things] through [the petition]
6     [being] charged. And write to us.
7     Farewell. Year 32, of Daisios ...
8     Year 32, Epeiph 20.
9     Concerning Neoptol[emos].
10    To Zenon.
