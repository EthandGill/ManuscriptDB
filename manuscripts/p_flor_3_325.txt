[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the consul]ship of Flavius Longi[n]us the most illustrious, Pachon 25, of the 12th indiction, in the city of Oxyrhynchos.
2     [To Flavius Strategios the most ill]ustrious [a]nd a counci[l]lor of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, son of the most exalted [ex-consul A]pion, landholding in it through Theodoros his agent, (we), Ioannes
3     [a deacon][ of the h]oly church, son of Aneous, his mother being Apia, and Aurelius Phoibammon, headman (meizon)
4     … his mother being Peieous, from the estate of Pakerke of the Oxyrhynchite nome, greeting. Of our own [free will]
5     [we under]take to lease in mutual surety from the present year, the 165th = 134th, of the gathering of the crops
6     [of the thir]teenth [i]ndiction, from the (property) belonging to your Illustriousness and devolving
7     [upon it] by right of a quarter-share of (the property) of Serenus, of illustrious memory, formerly a councillor
8     … of the same Oxyrhynchites, by order of the all-(magnanimous) Meizon and Exalted (lord),
9     … [situated] in the plains of the same estate, one whole water-wheel called
10    … [equip]ped with all wooden gear and iron-work, together also with the adjoining
11    [whole cistern and ] … and the twenty-one arouras farmed together with it
12    [or however many there may be, for the sowing of whatever] crops we may choose; and we shall pay, by way of fixed
13    [rent,] [both for Nile-flooded] [and un]flooded (land), each year, of wheat by the kankellon measure, artabas
14    … [the ren]t being free [of ris]k from every ri[s]k, the public dues of the land
15    [being chargeable to ] …
16    … [F]lavius Ioannes …
