[TRANSLATION]
1     Asclas and Soter, collectors of monetary taxes, to Kephalon
2     Pasemius through Eponychus. We have received for the house-tax of administration of Charakos,
3     year 21, from Kephalon P[…] inferior drachmas
4     eight; total inferior drachmas [8]. Year 21 of Hadrian, the lord,
5     Payni 12. Asclas: I have recorded drachmas 8.
6     Soter: I have recorded drachmas 8.
7     Another: Payni 30, in the name of the same, for the house-tax in the name of the same,
8     inferior drachmas four; total inferior drachmas 4.
9     Asclas: I have recorded drachmas 4.
10    Another: Epeiph 1, in the name of the same, for the house-tax
11    of the same, year 21, Charakos, inferior drachmas four:
12    total inferior drachmas 4. Asclas: I have recorded drachmas 4.
