[TRANSLATION]
1     … until today he has not reminded
2     the most illustrious lord Elias, the curator, concerning the twelve
3     solidi, and received (them). For I wrote to his Illustriousness about this. May
4     your Brotherhood deign to remind his Illustriousness and to receive the twelve
5     gold pieces and write me [his] answer. For God knows if he will hesitate and
6     give nothing. And write me the matter … [the most magnificent] tribune
7     Komitas; and I believe that, if I bring … …
8     against me, but he makes me altogether if these things with you it is necessary … therefore entreat
9     him, so that no grievance may arise and I be forced to grieve him and to bring the matter
10    to the tribune. For God knows, if he ordered me to pawn(?) the cloak
11    for his answers, I could not have hesitated; and I greet him many times.
12    And the letters of the lord Nemesion, the son of the most illustrious advocate
13    Petechon, I sent to you inside our letters, so that you may deliver (them)
14    to the lord Menas and to his brother. And having come here and having learned,
15    he said that no one had given me letters except [one], which was during those same
16    days. How, up to the present day …
17    but I was even blamed by her …
18    Deliver to the in all things most God-loving …
