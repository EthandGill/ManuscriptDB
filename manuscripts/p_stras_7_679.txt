1     In the preamble I greet much and I bow to the
2     blessed footprints of [your] [holiness]. Since then
3     it is fitting for your god-loving character and for the pure to bring together
4     their own brothers and friends; now you yourself are [not] ignorant
5     Constantine my notary. He must manage a little
6     matter so that bodily needs may be found. Deem worthy then
7     the matters to give to him so that he may manage them. For this
8     doing your reverence accomplishes a worthy matter. [Be well], most holy father … i … h … a abba Taurinus concerning Horion
9     … ete … en … i him so that … n …
10    Hand over to the all-holy most holy father [a]bba Heracleides the archimandrite
11    from Dioscurides the son and Taurinus the least
