[TRANSLATION]
1     Theano to Dionysius [the] brother
2     greetings most. Concerning the man you made clear to me, we did not find what he accomplished, and he himself moreover went out to you.
3     Be well, greeting from me [to] the
4     children. Again be well. Pharmouthi 26.
5     To Dionysius brother.
