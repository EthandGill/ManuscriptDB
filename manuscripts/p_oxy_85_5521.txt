[TRANSLATION]
1     … (symbol)
2     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master, the greatest
3     benefactor Flavius Justin the eternal Augustus and Emperor, year 4,
4     in the consulship of their serenity for the 2nd time, Mecheir 23, of the 2nd indiction.
5     To Flavius Apion the all-praiseworthy and most magnificent, ex-consul ordinarius and patrician,
6     landowner also here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas
7     his servant, who both acts as questioner and provides for his own master,
8     the same all-praiseworthy man, the right of action and liability, Menas
9     doorkeeper of the glorious house, son of the most admirable Serenos, with a guarantor
10    who also undertakes for him in this contract,
11    myself, John, notary (nomikarios) and riparius, son of the late
12    Philoxenos, both originating from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
13    subscribing below in our own hand, greeting. I acknowledge, I
14    the principal Menas, of my own free will and voluntary
15    choice, that I have contracted myself to the glorious house of
16    your magnificence, that is, through those belonging to it, for one
17    year, reckoned from the twenty-third of the present
18    month Mecheir of the present second indiction, on condition that I
19    fulfil the post of doorkeeper of its suburban estate, unhesitatingly
20    and blamelessly, receiving as wages for the whole one
21    year two solidi of gold and twelve artabas of grain by the kankellos (measure)
22    and twenty-five estate-jars (knidia) of wine, and that I not be able
23    before the end of the one year to withdraw from such
24    service without delay and any trouble; and if I do this
25    before the end of the same one year, I acknowledge that I forfeit
26    my wages, or I too, his guarantor. The
27    contract, written single, is valid, and having been formally questioned we gave our assent.
28    I, Menas son of the most admirable Serenos, the aforesaid,
29    have made this contract …
30    Contract of Menas … of … of the city of the Oxyrhynchites
