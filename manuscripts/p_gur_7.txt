[TRANSLATION]
1     … greetings. I undertake [to]
2     furnish to you Sosibios son of Sosibios
3     [named] Ainias of the [younger] generation, written [down]
4     to you [by] written agreement of receipt of five hundred drachmas of copper toward
5     silver which [he] pledged [as security] for you in the sixth [regnal year] of Philadelphos for the properties until the year 11 until Pachon 30
6     of [those arrangements] in the same year. And if [I do] not
7     furnish it to you written [down]
8     as has been written before, I will repay
9     to you in the following month
10    one thousand drachmas, and enforcement [of the agreement] shall be to you according to the ordinances and the laws.
