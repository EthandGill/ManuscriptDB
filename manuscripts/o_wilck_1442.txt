[TRANSLATION]
1     Thoteus, collector of the apportionment of the arrears of the tax-farms of Charax: Kleopas (son of) Bassos
2     (son of) Dekmos, through Theon (son of) Bassos and Heraklas (his) brother.
3     I have, for the apportionment of year 12, seven drachmas, total 7 dr. Year 13 of Antoninus
4     Caesar the lord, Mesore 30.
