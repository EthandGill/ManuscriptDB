[TRANSLATION]
1     I [am] Frange being present of his being monk, yours, sister, [that] perform love of greeting toward a community of men who lives, either a male either or a female of us, a child of the beloved of the holy [one], monk, [who is] Peter
2     He will say behold he lives; I saw him, my small one, of them is according to God David
3     We knew him, this [is] your glory; I am he, we [are] one in
4     The Lord glory [of] your life forever
5     These of him of David [are] blessed
6     In the Lord greetings
7     In him
