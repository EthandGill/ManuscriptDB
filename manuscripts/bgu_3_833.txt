[TRANSLATION]
1   To Heron, royal scribe
2   of the Memphite (nome),
3   from Melas son of Areios, his mother
4   being Kaleis, of those from Memph-
5   is without a trade, registered in the 2nd
6   quarter. I register myself
7   and my people, as lodgers,
8   in (the house) I dwell in at Memphis
9   in the same 2nd quarter,
10   the house of Isidoros son of Anou-
11   bion also called Pankrates,
12   for the house-by-house
13   census of the past 14th year of Aurelius
14   Antoninus Caesar
15   the lord —
16   (this) registration:
17   myself, Melas, for the sa-
18   me 14th year, aged 51,
19   and the (children) born of
20   different women,
21   (my) sons: from Tesagris,
22   a Memphite woman, Melas,
23   aged 1;
24   and from Herieus a [dau]ghter, Tne-
25   phremphis, aged 16.
26   And the aforesaid house-owner, present,
27   Isidoros, goes surety for us
28   for the poll-taxes.
29   And I swear by the
30   Fortune of the lord Aurelius Antoninus Caesar
31   that the foregoing is true.
32   Year 15 of Imperator Caesar
33   Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
34   Augustus Armeniacus Medicus
35   Parthicus Germanicus Greatest,
36   Phaophi 4.
