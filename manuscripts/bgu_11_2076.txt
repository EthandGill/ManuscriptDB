[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Dionysios, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite (nome),
2     from the Aurelii: Longinus also called Zosimos
3     son of Leonides, and Horion son of Maron, and Heron son of Kopres,
4     and Harpalos son of Sarapion, and Harp… son of A…on, and the
5     remaining grain-collectors of the village of Karanis. Monthly
6     summary account for the month Hathyr of the present 25th year,
7     from the harvest of the past 24th year. These are the (artabas) measured out
8     to us in the granary [of the] aforesaid village
9     in this month: of wheat, 3,91[7] artabas; of surcharge-wheat, 21 and a half (and) an eighth artabas,
10    total with surcharge, of wheat, [3,938 and a half (and) an eighth] artabas … of which
11    of Karanis, of public (land), [ … artabas of wheat … of catoeci …]
12    total of the village, of wheat, [3,634 artabas, on behalf of the other villages]
13    [of Ker]kesoucha, of public (land), 24 …
14    … of catoeci 1 …
15    total of the village, of wheat, 283 artabas, of surcharge-wheat, 21 and a half (and) an eighth artabas,
16    total also [on behalf] of other villages, of wheat, 283 artabas, of surcharge …
17    and [in addition there are taken up with these] on behalf [of the philanthropon (subsidy) and others]
18    of wheat … (and) an eighth artabas, total with the phi[lanthropon], of wheat, 39 artabas …
19    and of the selection of the previous month …
20    of wheat, Era… artabas, and with the [selection]
21    of wheat, 7,100 and a half artabas
22    of Karanis, of public (land), of wheat … artabas
23    total of the village, of wheat, 3,634 artabas …
24    of Kerkesoucha, of public (land), of wheat, 24 artabas …
25    total of Kerkesoucha … of wheat … artabas
26    … 3,924 and a half (and) an eighth
