[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 10, enumeration Phaobi. [He] has discharged
2     to Ptolemaeus and those with him in a clerouchy of the silver-tax collection
3     of the village Tebtynis, Lautanis of Petesouchos
4     other drachmas eight, which total drachmas eight.
