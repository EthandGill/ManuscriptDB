[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Theodoros the most magnificent comes, through me [Ariston son of Phoibammon]
2     the boy, to Aurelius Herakleios son of Pauomthos, wine-seller from Aphrodite, greeting. I have received
3     from you, on account of the wine owed to us by you, [three] solidi of gold less
4     six keratia, to the goldsmith of the same village, from various persons; [and]
5     of these I accepted from Pheous and Anouphios the shepherds [two] solidi of gold
6     and I have given them the cancellation of these; total 3 solidi of gold less 6, and for your security
7     I have made this security-document of the aforesaid three solidi, with the signature of him who on
8     my behalf signs; and on being formally questioned [I agreed], Thoth 22 of the fourth indiction. I, Ariston son of Phoibammon,
9     boy of the most magnificent comes Theodoros, the aforesaid, this security-document is satisfactory to me,
10    of the aforesaid three solidi less six keratia, to the goldsmith of the village of Aphrodite,
11    as is set forth above. I, Flavius Phoibammon son of Dorantinoos, the ordinarius of the governor's office,
12    wrote on his behalf, he having told me face to face, since he does not know letters.
13    Written through me, Abraamios son of Apoll(o)…
14    … apa Theodoros on behalf of Herakleios …
