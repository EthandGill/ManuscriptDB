[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 54, Phaophi 15. It has been appointed to the bank in the city of Crocodiles, on which Ptolemy [is registered] as the tenth of the circle according to the [agreement] from Paniscus, the [one] concerning the purchase [record] under which
2     [there] signs Apollonius the grapheus as witness to the purchase [of] Maithōtios of Phsenenoupios, bare land 1½, of which the neighbors have been indicated through the present agreement, which [he] purchased
3     from Phsenenoupios son of Horus for a bronze talent 1, the tax of which [is] in exchange drachmas 20,
4     [which] amount to drachmas 20.
5     Ptolemy, banker.
6     year
