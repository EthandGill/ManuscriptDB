[TRANSLATION]
1   Copy(?).
2   The most excellent council of the illustrious and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,
3   through Aurelius Euporos also called Agathos Dai-
4   mon, former kosmetes, exegetes, hypomnemato-
5   graph of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians, former prytanis,
6   and however he is styled, councillor, prytanis in office,
7   to the keepers of the property-archives, their dearest, greeting:
8   Aurelius Apollodidy-
9   mos son of Ploution presented to us his rights, depending
10   on universal laws, concerning his having been enrolled
11   in the assembly of the sacred synod,
12   and, having according to custom done reverence to
13   the divine, we have all the more confirmed
14   these to him. Since, then, we judged it conse-
15   quent to report this openly to you, that you may
16   know the immunity belonging to him by the
17   laws and may make the due an-
18   notation against his name,
19   this is sent to you, dearest friends.
20   I pray for your health, dearest friends.
21   Year 5 of our lord Aurelian
22   Augustus, Mecheir.
