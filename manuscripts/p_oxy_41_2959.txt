[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dios, strategos,
2     from Flavia Dionysia, and as
3     she is styled, as she has been permitted
4     to conduct business without a guardian.
5     I request that [you] send orders from the
6     public [treasury] the sums owed to me
7     for the price of wheat [from] joint-purchase produce of the past
8     2nd year, which I measured in accordance
9     with the orders given by Pompeius Planta, the most excellent governor, through the grain-collectors of Kerkemuni[s],
10    in the present month of Neo
11    Sebastos, 17 artabas, 69 [and a half],
12    24, artabas 38 [and a half], of the [at]
13    the same, of artabas 108, as [of]
