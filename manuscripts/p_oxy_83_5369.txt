2     Flavius Justinian the eternal Augustus and Emperor, year twenty-two, on the

4     Pachon first, indiction eleven, in the city of Oxyrhynchus.

7     city of the Oxyrhynchites, through [Menas] the servant who also asks
8     [and provides] to his [own] master the same most honored
9     [man] the claim and [obligation], Aurelius Phib son
