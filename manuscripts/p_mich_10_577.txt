[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakleides to Diogenes, greetings and perpetually be well.
2     The young men written alongside
3     against [the] years 8 of [the] year
4     of poll-tax of so many years
5     drachmas these.
6     No one has given to the tax-collectors a dagger-bearer. Until
7     the reckoning make effort [as] much as
8     you are able to do and demand. All
9     the young men demand [payment] and bring [in amount] to a small [sum] into the
10    reckoning. If you have Bellees with you, keep him. You will send up to Petaisis [a] son …
