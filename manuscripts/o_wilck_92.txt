[TRANSLATION]
1   Ammonios and Petorzmethis, [collectors of the money-taxes of Syene].
2   Paid: Zmet[pos son of Harpaesis],
3   mother Sentithoeous, for the Dacian-river (tax) and
4   the rent of the river-guard, 17th year of Trajan Caesar
5   the lord: six silver drachmas, five obols — total 6 dr. 5 ob.
6   Year 17 of Trajan Caesar the lord,
7   Phamenoth 25.
8   (2nd hand) I, Nigrinus, have signed off.
9   (3rd hand) I, Pachompretis, have signed
10   off.
