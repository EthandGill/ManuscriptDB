1     Apphous and Isidorianus to Apollonios the cook, greetings.
2     Give to Pamouthios from Alkyon[eis], recruited into the now present new recruits on behalf of the property of my masters
3     landholders of the 7th indiction for [the] expense
4     of 5 days from …
5     … of meat, pounds 2½. I pray [you] be well for many
6     times. Give meat, pounds 2½.
7     Of year 49, Pauni 26. I pray [you] be well for many times. Give pounds 2½.
