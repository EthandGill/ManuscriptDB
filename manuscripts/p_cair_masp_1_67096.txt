[TRANSLATION]
1     † (God is my help) †
2     In the reign and consulship of our most divine master Flavius
3     Justin the eternal Augustus, Emperor, in the eighth year,
4     … the twenty-third day of the present seventh indiction.
5     To the right of the holy diaconia of the newly-founded mountain of the holy and
6     Christ-bearing Apostles, named after Apa Apollos,
7     after the name of the founder, through Enoch son of Ioannes, the most pious
8     monk and steward of the same, through you Flavius Dioskoros
9     son of the principal, and administrator-curator of the same holy
10    diaconia by command of your same father, situated in
11    the mountain of the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome; from me, Psa also called
12    Psates son of Apollos, of the mother Sibyla, a wretched monk
13    and priest, hailing from the village of Temseu Skordon
14    of the Hermopolite nome, but residing now in your
15    mountain of the same diaconia of the hermit monks, greetings.
16    Formerly, by a written mortgage-security, for a guarantee of two
17    unweighed solidi, there mortgaged to me, while he was alive, the late Phoibammon
18    the brother of you the aforesaid Enoch, who was then himself also administering
19    the holy diaconia, a cell, that is, a simple monastic dwelling,
20    … of your cell called
21    Mousaiou … belonging … to the
22    most holy diaconia under you, as this too belongs to it, standing open
23    … to the east, having come into the same diaconia by
24    a lawful gift from the late Mousaios the deceased
25    monk, who hailed formerly from a village called
26    …obesante of the Oxyrhynchite nome, but who now has his dwelling
27    before the cell(?); from then until now I have asked you, beseeching you to cede to me the small cell in front, that is,
28    the kabane(?), so that I may make it into … for the need of
29    housing some poor one of the hermit foreign monks who
30    happen to come from time to time, who wish, on account of the
31    narrowness of the dwelling, to dwell in it, for the fruitfulness
32    and increase of you and of all the community. And you disputed
33    with me, since the mountain is sealed and closed off by the tradition of the first founder. And from this I was able
34    to entreat you toward what seemed to me right upon this
35    condition set out hereafter, on account of which I acknowledge, swearing the
36    most dread oath, that this cell which is to be built
37    by me, and moreover the mortgage held by me of the
38    two solidi, after my death, these together
39    both, with also the kabane in front, shall revert
40    properly to the same holy diaconia, just as I command and
41    wish, by every lawful gift holding the inter vivos
42    manner, so that these shall be without hindrance and unimpeded by anyone
43    altogether to the aforesaid holy place, by ownership-right, and
44    that none of those belonging to me may lay claim to these
45    in any manner whatsoever, a penalty being set upon this of gold,
46    twelve solidi, and again likewise for the mere transgression; and
47    upon all these things I have acknowledged and signed.
