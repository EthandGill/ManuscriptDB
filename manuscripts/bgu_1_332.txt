1     Serapias to the children Ptolemy and Apollinaria and
2     Ptolemy very much greeting
3     Before all things I pray you to be well, which for me all things
4     it is more necessary. The pro[s]kynema of you I make before the
5     lord Serapis, praying you being well to recover

7     because well you were saved. Greet Ammo[n]ous with children and
