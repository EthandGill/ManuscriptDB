[TRANSLATION]
1   To my lord brother Apphynchios
2   and Didymos the carpet-weaver:
3   Petysis son of Pantebes.
4   Just as I received from you in
5   the village the half holokottinos,
6   so now provide to my brother
7   Pettirios the scribe, for the security-receipt
8   of the ninth indiction, another half
9   gold nomismation. Do not neg-
10   lect it, lord brothers. (2nd hand) We pray for your
11   health for many years, lord
12   brothers.
