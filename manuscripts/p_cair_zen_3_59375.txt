1     Addaios to Z[enon, greeting. You wrote to us that] the fattened fowl [should be d]ispatched.
2     I therefore asked … boros the one fattening
3     the birds. And concerning [the wine] I also wrote to you for how much I sold (it). Know then that the
4     tax-farmers have depri[ved] us. I then we[n]t to Apollonios the brother of Pythangelos,
5     and having sum[moned th]em he [order]ed (them) to [p]ay; but they did not heed him.
6     Pythangelos then is w[ith you], as is Epikrates the one who has bought the tax. Meet then
7     with him so that he may w[rite a l]etter to Alexarchos his brother, that he may pay us
8     the wine. And concerning [the ] … ron about which I wrote to you that the order should be sent to us, take care
9     that it be sent to us. … neri … I wrote to y[o]u, do not neglect that it be bought up together
10    for us now into the ko…; for having sold beforehand more cheaply we shall receive (it). And concerning the oil,
11    seal for Zopyr[os with] the tax-farmers the token, so that he may bring down to us the oil;
12    for I have nothing for t … n into the coming (month). If then he does not seal (it) for him, know
13    that we are buyin[g ] … timie … n. And concerning the things I have written to you in the
14    letters which Zopyros conveyed to you, sen[d (back) t]he copies, so that I may act thus.
