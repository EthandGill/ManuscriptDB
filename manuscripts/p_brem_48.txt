[TRANSLATION]
1     Herodes to Herakleios the most honored,
2     greeting.
3     Know that on the third of the month I arrived at Alexandria, although I was in much danger from a violent storm
4     for my life, and that I delivered to her,
5     on the third, to Apia, the letter from your friend
6     Apollos, in which I said that as
7     he passed by, Achilleus, who is about to become my friend, having heard about the
8     sailing down, near as one having heard that the
9     son was ill — and now, thank God, I report that he is in good health. I ask you therefore
10    … to Achilleus, whatever may seem good to you,
11    … that I delivered the letter
12    … to me, and after some days …
13    to wait upon her, so that I may get an answer. And I also met my brother
14    Hierakion, as I wrote to you through Kastor
15    the friend. If Claudia Apia had discharged me
16    by an answer, I would not have had a pretext to wander about here. And I remind you concerning the timber
17    for the building, whether you might somehow be able to buy a finished (lot) from the Oxyrhynchite, so that we may not fall later
18    into the hands of the craftsmen. And know that
19    out of the ferry-boats I came down in haste
20    at much freight-cost. Before all else, tomorrow
21    I will make your act of obeisance in the Sarapeion, since today I did not go up because of
22    severe troubles and dangers.
23    Farewell, dearest one. Hathyr 3,
24    at the 8th hour.
25    I, Hierakion, greet you, sweetest one.
26    To Herakleios, procurator of Apollonios the strategos.
