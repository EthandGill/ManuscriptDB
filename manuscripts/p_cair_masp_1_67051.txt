[TRANSLATION]
1     [From Flavius Psatus, notary of the district of Antaeus, through me John Psatus]
2     [his successor to the most admirable village-officials of the village of Aphrodite: I received]
3     [and was satisfied by you] on behalf of [annona soldiers of Antaeus]
4     [canon of the] eighth indiction toward the [authority of the judicial]
5     decree, that is wheat in gold [one hundred]
6     [sev]enty, and wine-meat eight thousand seven [hundred]
7     fifty, of which in gold five thousand; total [wheat in gold]
8     170 of wine-meat 8,750 of which in gold 5,000. and for your [security]
9     [and of the public treasury] I have made this receipt, that is [a formalis]
10    as stated. Flavius Psatus notary of the district of Antaeus, [eighth] canon [of the indiction]
11    through me John Psatus his successor; this [receipt] is established for me
12    that is wheat in gold one hundred seventy and wine-meat [eight thousand]
13    seven hundred fift[y], of which in gold five thousand;
14    total wheat in gold 170 and wine-meat 8,750 of which in gold 5,000, as aforesaid.
