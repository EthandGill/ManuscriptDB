r.3    ἱερείων· καλῶς ἂν οὖν ποιήσαις συναποστείλας

r.5    Ζήνωνι.

[TRANSLATION]
1     A memorandum to Zenon from Heraclides. Concerning
2     the announcement that I have given you concerning Thotis'
3     priests; well then you would do [if you would] send along
4     someone with me, so that I may lead [them] down to him before he himself sells [them]. For I am being pursued by the pig-herders here. Farewell
5     To Zenon.
