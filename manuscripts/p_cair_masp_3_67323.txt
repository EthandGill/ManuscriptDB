[TRANSLATION]
1     From
2     My master, the most glorious comes Ammonios, [did not neglect],
3     for God's sake, the constitution of your village. He spoke …
4     to seek the settlement of the same village, and [he ordered this]
5     to be written to all those of the village and to the exactors, both through
6     your admirableness and Senouthis son of Apollos and [the other]
7     protokometai of the same village, and to those willing—you, however,
8     and the others, I mean Botton and his son and those [around him]—
9     to withdraw and devote themselves to their own affairs, attending to [their own business].
10    And such letters I have sent through the letter-carrier …
11    to your brotherhood. Having therefore received these, prepare to …
12    to gather those of the village and to deliver to them and …
13    of the exactors, to give him the letters that are for them …
14    to the city, and he brings them up, putting them in order; and you, [with]
15    the exactors, by yourselves perform the duty [concerning]
16    the settlement, but do not turn back the [matters] …
17    from my said master the comes and …
18    but altogether follow the order. And [your grandfather]
19    Psimanobet send forth at once hither …
20    … of the most glorious comes
21    [Deliver] to the brother Apollos the protokometes, from Psais the [numerarius].
