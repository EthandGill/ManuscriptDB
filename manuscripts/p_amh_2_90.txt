[TRANSLATION]
1     … [We wish to lease, under mutual surety,] the eight aro[uras of] catoecic allotment land [belonging to you near] the village of Theadelphia,
2     eight, or however many they may be, in 1 plot, for two years
3     from the present 23rd year of Antoninus Caesar
4     the lord, the rent for the whole, unsown,
5     in the first year being thirty-two artabas of wheat, and in the second year likewise
6     forty artabas of wheat, and a gratuity each year likewise of one artaba of wheat, all by the dromos measure of four choinikes, free of deductions and free of risk,
7     and we will perform the yearly works, all
8     that are fitting, sowing at our own (cost), in the last year half the portion with wheat and the remaining half portion
9     with grass for cutting, and the yearly rents
10    we will pay always in the month Pauni in the village,
11    the yearly transport-charges and expediting on behalf of the arouras being
12    upon us the lessees, and the other public charges
13    all being upon you, Dideis. And
14    after the time we will hand over the arouras clean of reed, rush, dog's-tooth grass, and all silt,
15    if it seems good to lease. I, Dideis also called Ptollarous
16    daughter of Ptolemaios, through Herakleides the manager,
17    have leased on all the foregoing conditions.
18    Year 23 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
19    Phaophi 25.
