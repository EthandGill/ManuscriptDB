[TRANSLATION]
1     With God [fighting alongside] and the saints we seized Alexandria
2     very greatly [having suffered] and [we found] Belisarius the magister
3     sent with replies [from] our common master, and
4     we waited so that in the same time we might depart toward our God-protected
5     master, and we hope again our seizing
6     Babylon again we write to my master. For all things
7     the things set in motion into the great expedition and in [Constantine's] city
8     already we wrote to you. May the Lord of glory deem me worthy and them
9     in their sight to greet the footprints of my master.
10    to my own good master, [the] all [most splendid] [Theodore] …
