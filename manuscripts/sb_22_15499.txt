[TRANSLATION]
1     To Julius Proclos, prefect praepositus praetenturae, from Aurelius
2     Abidsauta Abidierdu, councillor
3     of Neopolis dwelling in Bethfouraea
4     village. Philotas Nisaraiabu,
5     my fellow-villager, lord, at every turn
6     has forced me, regarding which documents to the most splendid of us, the consul I sent, regarding which, having dared, he accomplished along with Sibbaraios, his slave.
7     I request and beseech you for humanity since the charges which I am charged with
8     needing [examination by] a greater judge
9     to be examined, send them back together
10    with me [upon] the matters of the one conducting the consulship [Po]mponius Laitianus.
11    For I am ready to accuse him
12    regarding what he has accomplished and always at your
13    fortune I will give thanks.
14    Prosper, lord, many [years].
