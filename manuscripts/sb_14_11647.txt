1     To Julius Claudianus, the most excellent epistrategos
2     Aur[elius] Harpocration, strategos of Memphis, greets
3     The [in]spection of embankment and drainage works [given] to me by Isidorus, public surveyor
4     of the present sixth year of our lord M[arcus]
