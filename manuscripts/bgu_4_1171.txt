[TRANSLATION]
1   (Sheet.)
2   To Protarchos.
3   From Stephanos son of Kaisar, and from
4   Zamanos [son of ...]tos, with the presence and con-
5   sent to this agreement of He[rodes]
6   son of Isi[doros], a Persian of the epigone. We a-
7   gree with one another on these terms:
8   Zamanos (agrees) that void is (the cession) which
9   Stephanos brought in to him, with the consent
10   of Herodes - an agreement made through the
11   same court in the 19th year of Caesar,
12   Pachon - a cession of a loan
13   ...which Stephanos had lent
14   to Herodes by another agree-
15   ment through the [sa]me court in the
16   17th year of Caesar, Mesore, (a loan) of
17   one thousand drachmas - because this (cession) had been made
18   to Zamanos in trust;
19   and that it is permitted to Stephanos to ex-
20   act from the debtor the loan and
21   the interest owed, just as
22   before. And Stephanos, having been
23   paid in full by Herodes,
24   through the months after the cession,
25   of the capital - at the new moon(?) - the inter-
26   est up to Pharmouthi of the present
27   year - has allotted for the
28   repayment eight months from the 1st of
29   Pachon of the same year, within which,
30   having recovered his own (money), he shall can-
31   cel the loan-agreement;
32   and that both shall not proceed
33   against Herodes concerning anything
34   at all, written or unwritten,
35   (of matters) up to the present day,
36   except (the recourse of) Stephanos for the
37   (claim) concerning the one thousand
38   silver drachmas and the interest from
39   Pachon; or, apart from the agreed terms
40   remaining valid, the transgressor shall be
41   liable both to the
42   damages and to the fixed
43   penalty. We request (registration). Farewell.
44   Year 20 of Caesar, Pachon 1.
