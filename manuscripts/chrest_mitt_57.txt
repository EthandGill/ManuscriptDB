[TRANSLATION]
1     To Gaius Vitrasius Pollio,
2     from Versenouphis son of Manrres, of those from the village of Bakchias of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite
3     nome, a royal cultivator. In the 4th year of Gaius Caesar
4     Imperator Augustus, when my aforesaid father died, he left by will his property to me and to my brothers Orsenouphis and Sisois and Petobastis
5     and Marres, and the gear in his house and
6     other things to our mother Taonnophris for the time of
7     her life. But in the meantime, our
8     mother also having died, and we being bound to take over her property
9     in accordance with the will of our father,
10    our elder sister, having been dowered
11    by our father back in the 35th year of Caesar
12    the god, and nothing of the
13    paternal and maternal (property) falling to her in accordance with our father's
14    will, dared, together with
15    her husband Ptolemaios son of Horion, to carry off our paternal movables and gear
16    left by our father to our mother
17    and falling to us, to the value of 1500 drachmas of silver. Therefore
18    I ask you, the savior and benefactor of all,
19    if it seem good, to take cognizance so that I may obtain my rights, in order that we may be benefited. Farewell.
