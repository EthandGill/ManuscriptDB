[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting from Ptolemaios, a Macedonian … [ I am wronged]
2     by Horos the keeper of the Isis-temple and Thotortaios and Pathei… and Palout… [dwell]ing … in … [of the Arsi]noite no[me]. Having watched for me on the 3rd of … emi…
3     an oath … … …
4     pasturing … having joined in the pursuit … …
5     … they hamstrung these things … …
6     to certain people passing by toward Oxyrhyncha they gave … to cut down the … … …
7     in the plain; but afterwards we found it (a beast) gnawed at by the [dogs and t]he ravens,
8     which also we showed to the guards and to many others. So I beg you, king, if it seems good to you,
9     to command Diophanes the strategos to write to Mos[chion] the epistates at [O]xyrhyncha to send the persons accused by me to him, so that he may investigate concerning these things and, if
10    the matters in the petition are true, that they be made to pay forthwith and to pay back to me
11    the aforestated price. For when this has been done, king, having taken refuge with you,
12    the common benefactor and helper, I shall obtain your help.
13    Farewell.
14    To Moschion. Best of all reconcile [them; but if not, send (them)] so that on the common court they may be judged.
15    Year 1, Gorpiaios 28, Tybi 12.
16    Year 1, G[o]r[piaios 28, Tybi 12].
17    Ptole[maios against Horos the keeper of the Isis-temple] and the … [ concerning]
18    sacrificial beasts.
