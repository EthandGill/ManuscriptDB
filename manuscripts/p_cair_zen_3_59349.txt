[TRANSLATION]
1     Hierokles to Zenon greetings.
2     You will do well giving to Antipater
3     the brother ceramic jars of wine 4
4     so that for use and, if there is of the old, one jar of vinegar,
5     but if [there] is not of the old,
6     of the new, if it is already usable.
7     Farewell. Year 3,
8     Mesore 7.
9     To Zenon
