[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklas and Soter, collectors
2     of the money-tax of Charax, (to) Thaminis (daughter) of Thasies.
3     We have, for land-survey tax of Charax, year 19, impure one drachma 2 obols,
4     total 1 dr. 2 obols. Year 20 of Hadrian the lord,
5     Tybi 29. (2nd hand) Asklas, I have signed, 1 dr. 2 obols.
6     (3rd hand) Soter, I have signed, 1 dr. 2 obols.
