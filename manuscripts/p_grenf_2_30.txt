[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 16th year which is also the 13th, Choiak 4, in Pathyris, before Hermias the agent of Paniskos
2     the agoranomos.
3     Petearsemtheus discharged,
4     and Petesouchos son of Panobchounis
5     son of Totoes, and their brothers,
6     a loan of 2 talents of copper which was lent
7     to them by Petearsemtheus son of Almapheus
8     according to a deed of loan deposited at
9     the archive in Pathyris in the
10    15th year which is also the 12th; who, being present
11    at the archive, Petearsemtheus
12    son of Almapheus, acknowledged
13    [that he has back] from Petearsemtheus son of
14    Panobchounis and from his brothers
15    the 2 talents of the indicated copper,
16    and that neither Petearsemtheus nor any other of
17    those on his behalf shall proceed against Petearsemtheus
18    and his brothers, nor against any
19    other of those on their behalf. But if not,
20    let the prosecution be void, and let the one proceeding additionally pay as a penalty forthwith 5 talents of copper and, sacred to the kings, of stamped silver
21    100 drachmas, and nonetheless
22    let it be valid according to [the] aforewritten terms.
23    This is the debt
24    which Harpos son of Pabus and his
25    wife Tareesis owed.
26    I, Hermias the agent of Paniskos, have transacted it.
27    Discharge [to] Petearsemtheus
28    and his brothers,
29    of a loan of 2 talents of copper which Petearsemtheus son of Almapheus lent to him.
