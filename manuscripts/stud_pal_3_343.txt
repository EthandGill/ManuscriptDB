[TRANSLATION]
1     … son of G[eorg]e from the village Thelbo of the Heracleopolitan nome [ ] to you Paulos, the most glorious riparios, son of the blessed Elias …
2     … I have brought suit against you on account of certain matters of mine, in that some days ago I placed these things with you as a pledge, and you …
3     … from you which indeed I have given … are not pledges … and from this to order …
4     … which ordered you to deposit with me a solemn oath in the holy church of God that you purchased these things and they are not pledges
5     … of the most glorious authority; from here already you have sworn to me such an oath, and the [remainder] [no one] discussion to have, neither myself nor another on behalf of me toward you
6     … [eterr] concerning this; but if also it should seem good to me at another time to bring suit against you concerning this, so that I provide to you an account of a fine in golden coins
7     twelve pure gold [coins]; it was written in the month of Pharmouthi
8     … Paommas son of the blessed Loullus from Heracleapolis witnesses
9     [Seoureros] by the mercy of God deacon of the holy great church from Heracleapolis witnesses
10    [ ] … deacon son of the blessed Senouthios from Heracleapolis witnesses; through me Paul, the notary
