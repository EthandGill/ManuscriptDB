1     Aurelius Plution son of Silvanus
2     mother Eudaimonis from the
3     splendid and most splendid city of Oxyrhynchos to Aurelius Philoxenos son of Philoxenos mother Demetrous from village Nesmeimes greetings. I acknowledge [I have sold] to you whom I have donkeys male two complete, one indeed white, the other black,
4     which donkeys there you have received from me these such without defect(?), [at a] price
5     of the agreed [upon]
6     between one another of the same donkeys two in silver of Augustus
7     currency of talents four [which] I there [have received from you]
8     through hand in full and [I will guarantee] to you the [same donkeys]
9     [with] all guarantee, [and that which will come] to you [for these things]
10    [I will] remove me [immediately]
11    [with my] own expenses [as if] from judgment …
