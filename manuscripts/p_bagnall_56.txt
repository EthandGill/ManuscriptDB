[TRANSLATION]
1     To the amphodogrammateis of Polis Apeliotou (East City),
2     from Hermaios also called Pathotes, son of Achilleus
3     son of Hermaios, his mother being Eudaimonis also called Tisois,
4     daughter of Nearchos, Hermopolite, of the gymnasium class, registered in Polis Apeliotou. I register, according to the things commanded by Aurelius Verianus,
5     the former prefect, for the house-by-house
6     census of the 28th year of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus
7     Caesar the lord, at the lodging of Ammonios
8     son of Anoubion son of Petes son of Psenamounis, and however
9     he is styled, registered in Polis Apeliotou, a 1/12 part of a house and courtyard and appurtenances in Polis Apeliotou, the 17th quarter, there being present and consenting to this registration both myself and my people:
10    Hermaios also called Pathotes, of the gymnasium class, aged 48;
11    Hermeinos also called Moros, my son, by his mother
12    Soueros daughter of Kastor, of the gymnasium class, aged 21;
13    Isidoros, another son, of the same mother, of the gymnasium class,
14    a minor [in the 28th] year, aged 13;
15    Theognostos also called Moros, another, of the same mother,
16    of the gymnasium class, a minor in the 28th year, aged 8;
17    of the women: Soueros daughter of Kastor, of the gymnasium class, a woman aged 52;
18    …dora, our daughter, of the gymnasium class, one year old in the 28th year, aged 2;
19    and I swear by the fortune of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus
20    Caesar the lord that these things are so. Year 29
21    of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
22    Commodus Antoninus Pius Felix
23    Augustus, Armeniacus, Medicus, Parthicus,
24    Sarmaticus, Germanicus Maximus,
25    Britannicus, M… I, Hermaios also called Pathotes,
26    son of Achilleus, have presented (this) and have sworn the oath. I, Achilleus …,
27    wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters. I, Ammonios son of Anoubion, consent. I, Ploution, have received a copy.
