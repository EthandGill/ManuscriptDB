[TRANSLATION]
1     … year 15
2     To Asklep[iades] strategos and to [Hie]r[a]x royal scribe (basilikos grammateus)
3     from Herakles son of Mencheus, his mother being Tatas, and
4     Ptollis son of Amois, his mother being Thae[s]is, both from the village of Talao,
5     priests of Herakles the god
6     most great. With regard to the type referred for examination
7     of the special account (idios logos), from the accounts of year 15,
8     of which (this) is a copy: the undersigned minors,
9     sons of priests and of those of sacred descent,
10    having advanced to fourteen-year-olds
11    in the 15th year of Hadrian Caesar
12    the lord, beyond the appointed time, paid the entrance-fee (eiskritikon). So that it [may] be examined
13    whether anything is incumbent on their orders from the public (treasury)
14    or from outside, and who they are
15    those who, in the customary way, are scrutinized by the chief-prophet, and
16    received the deed from him, (the matter) is referred: lower toparchy of Talao, priests
17    of Herakles the god most great
18    Panemgeus son of Herakles son of
19    Mencheus, his mother being Tathonas (daughter) of Panemgeus; Am[o]is
20    son of Ptol[lis son of Amois], his mother being Ta…llas: we declare, swearing by
21    Imperator Caesar
22    Trajan Hadrian Augustus
23    that nothing is incumbent on the orders of our aforesaid sons either
24    from the public (treasury) or from outside
25    nor is it the custom for such persons to be scrutinized by the
26    chief-prophet, or may we be liable
27    to the oath. Year 16 of Imperator
28    Caesar Trajan Hadrian
29    Augustus, the 25th of the month Hadrianos.
30    Herakles son of Mencheus
31    [and] Ptollis son of Amois, we have submitted (this) and have sworn the oath.
32    …is son of Alexandros
33    [I wro]te on their behalf, they not
34    [knowing] letters.
