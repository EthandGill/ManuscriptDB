[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
2     Germanicus Imperator, in the month Neos Sebastos 30,
3     before Eirenaios the agoranomos of Parembole by Babylon of the Heliopolite nome. Petos son of Chelkias
4     and h[i]s sons Chelkias and Dor … koas, the three,
5     Jews, Persians of the epigone, of those from the village of Syron, to Lucius Vettius, cavalryman of the squadron of the Vocontii,
6     of the troop of Longinus. We acknowledge that we have from you
7     by hand, by mutual gua[r]antee for repayment, as a deposit, of imperial and Ptolemaic silver, drachmas
8     six hundred, total silver drachmas 600, which also we will repay to you
9     by Tybi the thirtieth of the present sixth year
10    of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
11    Imperator, without interest, paying out approved, tested, acceptable silver coin, free of all
12    deduction. And if we do not repay as is set forth above, we will pay back the deposit, both the silver drachmas
13    six hundred and the interest for the time overrun and as penalty another hundred
14    and twenty silver drachmas, the right of execution belonging to you both from us
15    the three and from any one of us whom you may choose, and
16    from all our property in every way,
17    you exacting it with full authority. And let the depo[sit be valid]
18    everywhere. Pe[t]os, about … 6 years old, dark-com[plexioned] …
19    [Chel]kia[s, about … years old] … en … with a [s]car on his shin …
