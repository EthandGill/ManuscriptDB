r.2    ἔλαβεν ἀπὸ τοῦ κνάφεως, πέμψ[α]ι μοι δ[ι]ὰ Θεοδώρου τοῦ λαμπροτάτου μειζοτέρου,

[TRANSLATION]
1     I ask your genuine brotherhood the old oil-fleece garment, which
2     [it] took from the fuller, send [to] me th[rough] Theodore the most illustrious superior,
3     since, as he knows, the seasons changed. The health of her and of the
4     children I ask her to signal to me and what [the] common master
5     the most glorious anti-geouchos on account of [my being] sent in Heracl[e]us,
6     since very I am concerned on account of him writing me [co]ntinually to settle
7     my account here and to seize Oxyrhynchus and if there are
8     still the Heracleopolitans.
9     to the master my own and brother Theodore count gre[ate]r George subscribing.
