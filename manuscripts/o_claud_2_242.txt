[TRANSLATION]
1     Antammon to Poseitas the
2     brother, greetings
3     if you are willing, brother, eagerly fresh fish, as many as
4     you are able, cause to be brought
5     and even one or two, since [it is] necessary
6     it is. I wish you to be well,
7     brother, in good fortune
