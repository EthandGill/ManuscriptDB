[TRANSLATION]
1     Papaeis son of Atisios, of the receipt-note of Hol: talents, myriads 2 (20,000).
2     Papaeis son of Atisios: 4000 talents.
3     … : 5050 talents.
4     Aurelius Alypios, veteran,
5     of the city of the Arsinoites, from the quarter
6     of Phremi, to Aurelius Papees son of Atisios, from
7     the village of Karanis, greeting.
8     Since I hold a bond against you for grain,
9     of twenty-four artabas, artabas 24,
10    out of the twenty-four I have supplied
11    to you — I, Papees — fifteen artabas of grain,
12    a third (and) a twelfth, artabas 15 1/3 1/12.
13    There remain from me — I, Papees — of grain artabas
14    eight and a half, a twelfth, artabas 8 1/2 1/12,
15    which I myself have supplied to you. I, Aurelius Hol, have written
16    the whole.
17    Epeiph 10. Ammonas son of Atisios delivered at the harbor of the city,
18    on behalf of the third new indiction, of the villagers of Karanis,
19    of pure wheat, fifty-one artabas, artabas 51 only.
20    I, Elias, through me Romanos, have signed for fifty-one artabas of grain only.
21    Epeiph 13. Ammonas son of Atisios delivered at the harbor of the city, on behalf of the third
22    new indiction, of the villagers of Karanis, of pure wheat, eight artabas
23    and a half, artabas 8 1/2 only. I, Elias, through me Romanos, have signed
24    for eight and a half artabas of grain only.
25    Epeiph 18. Ammonas son of Atisios delivered at the harbor of the city, on behalf of
26    the third new indiction, of the villagers of Karanis,
27    of pure wheat, five and a half and a third artabas, artabas 5 1/2 1/3 only.
28    I, Elias, through me Romanos, have signed for five and a half and a third artabas of grain only.
29    Epeiph 10. Ammonas son of Atisios delivered at the harbor of the city, on behalf of the third
30    new indiction, of the villagers of Karanis, of barley artabas
31    twenty and two-thirds, artabas 20 2/3 only. I, Elias, through me Romanos,
32    have signed for twenty and two-thirds artabas of barley only.
