1     There acknowledge to one another Platonis also called Ophe[lia,] daughter of Horion,
2     of Oxyrhynchus, with as [guard]ian her full brother
3     Platon, and Lucius son of Ision, his mother being Tisasis, from
4     the Aphrodision of the Small
5     Oasis, a weaver — Platonis also called Ophelia (acknowledging) that she has apprenticed to Lucius the under-age slave-girl whom she has, Thermouthion,
6     for learning the weaver's craft for a period of four years from the new moon
7     of the following month Tubi of the
8     present year, on which terms (he agrees) to feed
9     and clothe the girl
10    and to furnish her to the
11    teacher each day
12    from sunrise until sunset, performing
13    all the tasks that shall be assigned to her by him pertaining to the aforesaid craft, at a wage for the
14    first year, per
15    month, of eight drachmas,
16    and for the second likewise per month of twelve drachmas, and for the third likewise
17    per month of
18    sixteen drachmas, and for the fourth year likewise
19    per month of
20    twenty drachmas; and (he agrees) that
21    the girl shall receive each year, [on]
