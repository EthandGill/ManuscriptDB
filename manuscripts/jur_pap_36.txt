1     In the reign of the king of kings Arsakes Euergetes, the Just, the Manifest and Philhellene, and of the queens Siake his
2     full sister by the same father and wife, and of Aryazate called
3     Automa, daughter of the great king Tigranes and his wife,
4     and of Azate his full sister by the same father and wife, in the year 226,
5     in the month Apellaios, in the province of Baiseira at the staging-post of Baithabarta, in the village of Kopanis, before the witnesses subscribed below. Barakes and Sobenes, the sons of Maiphorros, acknowledged and contracted in writing that they had received from Gathakes son of Oipates thirty drachmas of stamped silver as the price of a vine that is in the village of
6     Kopanis, called Dadbakanra — his own share, the share falling to him from the co-heirs, with water
7     and with fruit-trees both bearing and barren, and with entrance and exit
8     and all that pertains to it. Let the one share be Barakes's and
9     the one share Gathakes's, on condition that the said Gathakes, having taken it over,
10    shall hold the silver-bought vine for all time, both he himself and
11    his descendants, paying yearly in common all the things written in
12    the old contract in full. And let it not be permitted to Barakes nor to his brother nor to their descendants nor to anyone else on their behalf to eject Gathakes from the silver-bought vine, neither him nor his descendants. And whoever ejects him,
13    or, when another ejects him, does not take a stand to defend the case and does not make it clear (of claims), shall be void and shall in addition pay double the price which he received and another 200 drachmas as a fine, and to the king the
14    like sum. And if Gathakes too neglects the vine and does not keep it free from claim, let him pay the same fine. And
15    the water, every eighth day, the half, and of the supply-channel
16    by night the half. Witnesses: Chosstroes son of Orkadates, Apakes
