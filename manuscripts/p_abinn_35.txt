[TRANSLATION]
1     To my master and my lord
2     the praepositus, (from) Paesios.
3     You know my disposition, what sort of
4     disposition I have, and you know my mind, what sort of mind it is.
5     God is witness that not for the sake of profit (lemma)
6     do I fight, but I fight for you. Since
7     you are doing (well), I want you always to fare
8     well; but we want you to gain a little profit,
9     for this is, beyond
10    all things, good, and (good) before God.
11    Examine Heron who came
12    with your letter, since I was
13    not found in our own
14    village but in Karanis; and my son
15    who serves in the office (officium)
16    of the prefect of Egypt came with himself
17    to me in (the village of) [A]grarion; for we spent three days in Karanis
18    in common, and we were not able
19    to detach a single man from there.
20    We detained the keeper-of-the-peace (eirenarch) wishing to detach him; … came the whole
21    village (saying) that we did [n]ot permit anyone
22    to go out from the village. They gave
23    you, if it seems good [to y]ou, two solidi (nomismatia)
24    and fifty talents of silver.
25    I salute you together with your children
26    of your household, free from the evil eye,
27    and if you arrive successfully, examine concerning this matter, that there is no difference at all.
28    And when we had gone off there we found the praepositus of the district (pagus) there, and we did not wish
29    to stir up the matter; and if God wills, I am with you.
30    To my master and patron Ameneos the praepositus, (from) Paesios.
