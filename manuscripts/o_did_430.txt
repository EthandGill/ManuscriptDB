[TRANSLATION]
1     Longginos curator
2     of Aphrodite of Mount Kilikati
3     to the brother greetings;
4     have asked me those from the
5     praeesidium so that I write to you
6     concerning your slave-girl
7     who to you carries of the sixty drachmas. Send and to me it is
8     the bronze or send to me
9     [they] say and I will send to you and
10    the [debt] of the conductor
11    is with us.
12    Farewell.
