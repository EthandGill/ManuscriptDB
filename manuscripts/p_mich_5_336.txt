[TRANSLATION]
1     13 Year, Pharmouti 3
2     [Orseues son] of Haruotes, Persian of the epigone, I hold the loan from Galates
3     [of Lysimachos], the drachmas of stamped silver four hundred fifty [with] interest at one drachma three obols per mina per month
4     [each,] which I will also repay in the month of Tybi of the coming fourteenth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus as aforesaid. [Someone] wrote
5     [on his behalf Dio]nysios son of Maron, because he writes more slowly.
6     [Orseues son of] Haruotes, I have ordered [it] written.
