[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelii Ammon[ios] … [and as he] serves as councillor of the Oxyrhynchite [city]
2     and Heliodôros, both [librarians]
3     from Aurelii Thônis Ar[sates, whose mother is]
4     Taosirapios, and Didymos Thôn[os, whose mother is]
5     Thatrêtos, and Pekysios Seouêr[os], whose mother is Thaisates, and Thônis Didymos,
6     whose mother is Soêrios, the four from the Oxyrhynchite city, as priests of Thoerion, the double-share holders and [another one] called Sintanôs; through whom they sold to us Au[r]elios
7     Thônis Pekysios, whose mother is Thatrêtos,
8     from the same city, joint-priest
9     of the same double-share holdings, together with curator Aurelios Sarapiakos freedman of Kanôpos and Dêmêtrios
10    both having served as high-priests
11    of the same city through [the]
12    bank of money-changers in the city
13    in the month of Apochê itself, which [sole]ly
14    on the written authorization being in place [is to be] sent to the bank [we shall provide]
15    [to you], he confessed [to us that he] has received
16    [from us, which] he also [lent] the one who passed away in regard to him as heir
17    his father Pekys Thônis, whose mother is Kronôutos, from the same
18    city, who was a priest of the same
19    double-share holdings, according to a handwritten [document] made in the 2nd year of this most fortunate
20    reign, in the month of Thôth, of a principal sum of silver
21    drachmas four thousand five hundred, and the remaining interest owed in monthly instalments, with the deficient [amounts] having been repaid by us … to
22    his father, whenever it remained [in]
23    a share, as the receipt contains, through which
24    he ratified the said handwritten [document] and the single publication of [it] made publicly that remained un[changed] … and the [other one]
25    made through the same registry of the
26    holding of our [names] toward the debt; which
27    wishing to release we present
28    the petition, as is proper, and we swear [by] the Fortune of Marcus Julius Philippus
29    Caesars, the lords
30    not to have sworn falsely. Year [6] of Emperors
31    Caesars Marcus Julius Philippus
32    Carpicians Greatest, Germanics
33    Greatest, Pious, Fortunate
34    Augustuses, Mesore, the fifth epagomenal [day].
35    Aurelii Thônis Arsates and Didymos
36    Thônis and Pekysios Seouêrou and Thôni[s]
37    Didymos, the four; through me Aurelios, and I presented the petition and swore the oath.
38    [Th]ônis Arsates.
