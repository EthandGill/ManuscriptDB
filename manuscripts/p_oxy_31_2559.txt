1     Arrius Eudaemon to Dionysius
2     to the brother, greetings.
3     this first and only
4     I received from you [a] letter through
5     Sarapiacus. [That] you yourself indeed
6     are well and [are] occupied with the works and with me saved you rejoice,
7     you do well. I expect moreover
8     you will come according to that which also by
9     toward the private account recently about the agreement sent.

11    mother Demetron.
12    I wish you to be well, brother
13    most honored.
14    To Dionysius brother, strategos of Apollonopolis
15    from Arrius Eudaemon
