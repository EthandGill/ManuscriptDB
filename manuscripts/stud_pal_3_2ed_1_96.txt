[TRANSLATION]
1     [I received and I was satisfied, I Io]annes groom son of Menas from the city of the Arsinoitae from the district
2     … [from you] … [of] the most illustrious record-keeper of the most holy universal church here
3     [of the holy three mart]yrs my wages of grain and gold and customary [allowances] for which
4     [I perform the groom's] service, I mean from earlier times until the past month
5     [Phaophi] [fourteen]th indiction in full. It was written in the month of Hathur on the thirtieth of the same fourteenth indiction.
6     Through me Iou(stos) notary.
7     Receipt of Ioannes son of Menas the groom.
