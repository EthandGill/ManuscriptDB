[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelia Ptolemas daughter of Apollonios, mother Serenilla,
2     from the quarter of Phremi, to Aurelius Isidoros son of Ptolemaios, cultivator,
3     greeting. I have received from you the rent of the arouras which you cultivated for me around the boundary-marking district of Karanis, for the produce of the 21st year, nine artabas of wheat, total 9 artabas, in full,
4     [the lease being valid], and on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
5     In the consulship of Rufius Volusianus and Petronius Annianus,
6     the most illustrious, Hathyr 9. Aurelia Ptolemas
7     have received the rent in full as is set out.
8     Aurelius Serenos wrote on behalf of my mother, who is illiterate.
9     Aurelia Ptolemas daughter of Apollonios, mother Serenilla,
10    from the quarter of Phremi, to Aurelius Isidoros son of Ptolemaios, cultivator, greeting.
11    I have received from you the rent of the arouras which you cultivate for me
12    around the boundary-marking district of Karanis, for the produce
13    of the 2nd indiction, which is the 22nd year, and of the 3rd indiction,
14    which is the 23rd year, eighteen artabas of wheat, total 18 artabas,
15    in full as is set out.
16    In the consulship of our masters Constantinus and
17    Licinius Augusti, for the 4th time, Mesore 4.
18    Aurelia Ptolemas have received the rent
19    as is set out. Aurelius Serenos wrote on behalf of my
20    mother, who is illiterate.
