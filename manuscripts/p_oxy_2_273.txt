1   Year fourteenth of Imperator Caes[ar]
2   Domitian Augustus Germanicus, month [P]aun[i]
3   (2nd hand) 30, (1st hand) in the city of Oxyrhynchus of the Thebaïd.
4   Julia [H]erak[l]as, with the guardian gi[v]en
5   to her according to [Ro]man cus[t]om by Gaius Se[pt]im[iu]s
6   V[e]ge[t]us the former [pre]fect, in accord[an]ce with
7   the executed tablet (tabella) of Lucius Ofellius son of Lucius,
8   of the [Ou]fentine (tribe), Anthestius, to Theon son of Nikippos
9   son of Nikippos, of the Phylaxithalassios (deme), also called Altheus,
10   in the street, acknowledges that she has ceded to her own daughter
11   Gaia also called Sarapias, (daughter) of Pausanias also called Diony-
12   sios son of Astyanax son of Tryphon, of the Phylaxithalas-
13   sios (deme) also called Herakleios, not yet being of
14   age — from now for all time, as a gift
15   irrevocable — from the (lands) belonging to her
16   near Seryphis of the toparchy toward the west, from the
17   allotment of Nikandros, fifteen arouras,
18   out of which, whichever of these she chooses, of the principal (parcel) of [katoi-]
19   kic land, five arouras, which it shall also be permitt[ed]
20   for Gaia also called Sarapias, from this [agree-]
21   ment, to have re-registered through herself [through the]
22   land-registries, without further needing [the]
23   joint-subscription of her mother Julia Heraklas.
24   So Gaia also called Sarapias is to hold and own (it)
25   together with offspring an[d those]
