[TRANSLATION]
1   Year 2 of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
2   Germanicus Imperator, Pauni 20, dies Augusta. Has paid for the weaver-tax of the 1st year,
3   (quarter of) Teumenouthis: Apion son of Tryphon, apprentice - 1st year, through Theon, ten
4   (and) five, total 15. Epeiph 10, the same Apion son of Tryphon, apprentice, through
5   Dionysios, fifteen drachmas, total 15; month Kaisareios 3, the same,
6   through Apollonios, six drachmas, total 6.
