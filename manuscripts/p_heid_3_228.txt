1     Apollonides to Demonax
2     to the brother greetings. If you are in good health with
3     your father and the brothers [and]
4     the other things are in order, it would be
5     as I wish. Be well and
6     you yourself and all those with me.

9     for you to receive. I did not decide to receive [it] until you arrived. For indeed even now other things
