[TRANSLATION]
1     I write to your honor, with no disagreement existing, so that
2     by means of the packer the goods that are with the cook. For you know, even if I do not write, that with God it is the season
3     of the pig-sacrifices and we have need of the arbellaria and of the kormion and of the mortar. All
4     for he has. And the cook said to me that if we go up to the city we will buy and we will send
5     you these things, and he did none of these things. Deign therefore to cause these to be brought to me. Very
6     for I need them. But likewise see to it that with good deliberation you might bring out these things,
7     I mean arbellaria, the kormion, the mortar, the grater.
8     Deliver to my lord [most honored] brother John
9     from Samuel.
