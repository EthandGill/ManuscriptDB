[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of an agreement.
2     In the nineteenth year of Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan
3     Best Augustus Germanicus Dacicus Parthicus, Epeiph 25, in Tebtynis of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. Kronion son of Cheos, about fifty-six years old, with a scar on the little finger of the left hand, acknowledges to Helene daughter of Herodes,
4     about sixty-five years old, without distinguishing marks, with her guardian her own
5     son Herakleides son of Didymos, about thirty-six years old, with a scar
6     on the middle of his forehead, that he has leased to her, Helene, for the coming
7     twentieth year, the lands which Kronion happens to cultivate around
8     Tebtynis, on the so-called allotment of Apollonios,
9     six and a quarter arouras, called those of Kategoros, and those of the
10    daughter of the same Kronion, Taorsenouphis, by
11    the so-called canal of Chelon, five and a half arouras,
12    or however many there may be, for the sowing of grass and the cutting and drying
13    around Tebtynis, from the nine arouras out of the northern part
14    of the allotment of Horotynes, the provision of the seed and the
15    cutting of the grass being the responsibility of Helene, but the agricultural
16    works and all the taxes being the responsibility of
17    Kronion, who also has received from Helene as an advance payment
18    the price of the grass in full, and he shall guarantee it against everyone,
19    Helene not being diminished concerning what Kronion owes her
20    without written record, twenty artabas of wheat by the four-choinix measure of Tebtynis.
21    Subscriber … son of Deios, about 50 years old, with a scar on the middle of his forehead. Kronion
22    son of Cheos, I acknowledge that I have leased to
23    Helene for the coming twentieth year the
