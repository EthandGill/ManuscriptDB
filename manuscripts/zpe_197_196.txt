[TRANSLATION]
1     To Asclepiades, strategus of the Polem[o]n division,
2     from Marepshemis son of Marepshemis and Marsisoukhos son of Sighereus and Pachis son of Marepshemis, the three hegemones, and Petosirios
3     [son of] Pakekis secretary, and Panesis
4     son of Pak[he]bkis and Marepkamis son of Marepkamis and Marepshemis son of Marepshemis Pesourios and Onnophrios son of Psypheis and Marsisoukhos son of Marsisoukhos, the nine hegemones
5     and secretary and from the tribes of the
6     same priests from Tebtynis
7     of Kronos mighty god great greatest. Since
8     we have leased already long ago in former times until the present year
9     and day from the deceased treasurer Thenapynchis and from his son
10    Apynchis son of Harmiousios and from the woman Thaesis the [treasury] in the aforementioned village
11    Tebtynis of Kronos mighty god great greatest, a functioning treasury in which
12    a tower and an adjoining other tower and gate, we and the other priests of us taking over
13    from the aforementioned Apynchis
14    and the woman Thaesis the treasury
15    in which certain of the equipment which he had received
16    and doors—we found in the present month Pharmouthi of the present year 23
17    of Tiberius Caesar Augustus the standing
18    doors removed by them and unsheathed, not as had been received, so that in whole
19    we are punished [by being] unable to bring into the same trea[sur]y
20    what we measure out to the public, wheat and
21    and barley public. Therefore we ask you to write to the supervisor of Tebtynis
22    having gone to the indicated treasury and seen how it stands and sent to you those accused
23    toward the punishment to come.
24    Onnophris son of Psoipheis and Marepshemis [son of] Marepshemios
25    and Marsisoukhos [son of] Sighereus and Pachis son of Marepshemios and Petosiris [son of] Pakekios and Panisis [son of] Pakebkios and Marepakamis and Marepshemis [son of] Pesourios
26    and the remaining priests we have presented the aforementioned petition. Onnophris wrote [it], they not knowing letters. …
