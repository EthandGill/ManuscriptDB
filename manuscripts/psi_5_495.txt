[TRANSLATION]
1     Heraclitus to Criton, greetings. … [we are in good health] and we ourselves also.
2     Zoilus who (is) in Ptolemai[s] came to us … having come
3     to you having gossiped about him/it … announcing to Zoilus
4     from [you] [con]cerning the small money and that to no one … but to Apollonius only.
5     Having sworn to us by all the gods, he … [Callianak]ta neither to have said
6     nothing of such sort, but even the opposite he said … that you wrote to him such (a thing)
7     neither before nor when Zenon came … for Criton it was not fitting
8     to write to you concerning th[ese things]; but me myself it was necessary to … hesitate. But he said to be cautious
9     lest somehow … … Zenon follow … he says to me [unclear]. Now then he has repented because of having been rebuked by each …, he said to wish to repay
10    … … … [some] he was giving … to arrange if anyone wishes to serve.
11    I have written to you concerning these things. And Zoilus [also] … and Nicanor having come to
12    Ptolemais, met Ammonius and asked … to Tripoli for the registration of the
13    soldiers to introduce to Spintharos the cavalry[commander] … [so] it becomes sufficient; concerning the horse he himself
14    said to care, so that for me it be provided and so that … Now after this, nothing has happened to us
15    up to now; for both Ammonius is being dragged along … to Tripoli, and the provisions [for us]
16    have given out, and borrowing we manage our livelihood. You would do well [to write] to him concerning us, so that quickly he may register us; for I know that if you write that … quickly he will settle us in place.
17    And you would do me a favor also with Iatrocles and … letters to him so that he may be engaged.
18    For you would not know what great a change has come about … [You would do] well to care for yourself so that
19    you are in good health; for nothing is greater than this … [if you are in good] health, so that we may not be anxious.
20    For we want you both in body to be strong … as you yourself choose; for this
21    I know that you differ greatly from the rest …, … [according to] … ity and according to character. May it be to you
22    to prosper and succeed in all things.
23    Farewell. Year 28, Hyperberetaios 23.
24    To Criton …
