[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of the Flavii Orestes and Avienus the mo]st illustrious, Phamenoth 25, of the 10th indiction, in the city of Oxyrhynchos.
2     … [of thi]s splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, from the guild
3     [of the bleachers of the same city of the Oxyrhynchite]s, through us who are present and subscribing below
4     … [undertaking also for] our fellow-workers who remain with us, the persons listed below … [by ord]er of the in all things most magnificent and most wise common
5     governor of ours, which was made known to u[s] … urging also us who fulfil
6     the work of the bleachers to make a nomination of the one of us who ought to undertake the office of the chief-citizen
7     (protodemotes) for the fortunate eleventh indiction; the time being at hand, being in fear, subject to the things
8     fearfully ordained concerning this, having become as one and prevailing in a single resolve, having chosen, we name and report
9     at our own risk and liability Aurelius Theodoros son of Maximos, of the same craft as us, from our city,
10    originating, as chief-citizen (protodemotes), being competent (and) able to accomplish such an office entrusted to him by us, of the chief-citize[n,]
11    the liturgy, in the mentioned eleventh indiction, fittingly; whom also, guaranteeing
12    for (his) competence (when) sought … we will … a[nd] we will hand over, by mutual surety, in a public place. Valid is the nomination,
13    [written in a single (copy), and on being questioned we acknowledged it. A]urelius Isak son of Ioannes, bleacher …
14    … nomination of Theod[oros ] …
15    … Oxyrhynch having become …
