[TRANSLATION]
1   [...]...[...]...aros, son of Dionysios son of [Sar]a[pi-]
2   on, from the city of Oxyrhynchus, to Horion,
3   clerk of the record-office (agoranomeion) of the same
4   city, greeting. I have received back from you,
5   for annulment, the will which I deposited through the
6   same record-office under seal,
7   (the will) of the sixteenth
8   year of the deified Trajan, in the month Kaisareios, on the
9   fifth of the intercalary days - this at my own re-
10   quest. In the seventh year of Imperator
11   Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus,
12   Phamenoth the third.
