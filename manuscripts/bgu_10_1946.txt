[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of Ptolemy] son of Ptolemy and Berenice, the Benefactor Gods, in the tenth year, the priest being Pythangelos son of
2     [Philokleitos of Alexander] and of the Savior Gods and the Brother Gods and the Benefactor Gods and the Father-loving Gods, the kanephoros [of Arsinoe Philadelphos being A]gathokleia daughter of Diognetos, in the month Gorpiaios, at Tholthis of the Oxyrhynchite nome. [Eupolis the At]henian, of the (corps of) Philon, a private person, [leased] to Theokles the Cyrenaean, of the (corps of) Philon, a private person …
3     … of the tenth year …
4     … [the] share of Onnophris, on condition that Theokles shall provide to [Eupoli]s, on behalf of Onnophris, [of the wheat ar]tabas … two, the share of the rents falling to him according to the contract [of the lease] which is deposited with the contract-keeper Herakleitos the Cyrenaean, of the (corps of) Philon, a private person. [And let Theokles repay]
5     [to Eupolis this grain] in the month Dystros of the eleventh year, clean [and unadulterated] grain [produced in the al]lotment, by the just choenix-measure of Eupolis, by just measurement, and [let him deliver it to Eupolis's (granary) at his own] expense. And if he does not repay according to what is written, [let Theokles pay to Eupolis as the price] of each artaba of the wheat ten bronze drachmas, and let the right of exaction [belong to Eupolis, exacting from Theokle]s according to the ordinance. And let this contract be valid wherever
6     [it is produced. Witnesses: Poly]ainos the Cyrenaean, Kallikrates the Hesperite, Diomedon
7     … [the three, of (the corps of) Philo]n, private persons; Agathinos son of Hermias the Mytilenean …
8     … os son of Demetrios the Cyrenaean, the three of the epigone.
