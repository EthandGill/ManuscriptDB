1     Posthomos, collector of silver [tax] of Charakos to Kottarion
2     also to Tewt[os] of Amenothos. I received for lookout posts and guards
3     and others, year 7, dirty drachmas four obols 6, in total drachmas 4 obols 6
4     and for rent, year 7, dirty obol …
5     dirty drachmas three chalkoi 2, in total drachmas 3 chalkoi 2, and others [for] river guard-duty
6     and other dirty drachmas two half-obol chalkoi 2, in total drachmas 2 half-obol chalkoi 2, and others …
7     through Horion and others, dirty obol one, in total dirty obol
8     of year 8 of Antoninus Caesar the lord
9     P[os]thomas through Karourios [the] assistant I have been marked
