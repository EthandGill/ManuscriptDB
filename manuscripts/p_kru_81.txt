[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Written in the month Paoni 1,
2     of the 9th indiction. I, Staurou [the dau]ghter of the blessed Pesate,
3     the (woman) of the hamlet of the soldiers in the district of Psoi, she who dwells
4     in the monastery of Apa Sergios in the fortress of Ape;
5     I give, and afterwards a subscriber to subscribe on my behalf, together with witnesses
6     trustworthy and worthy of note, to witness to the deed of donation, not to be transgressed,
7     and not to be dissolved, and not to be undone through the laws
8     which are laid down; I write to the holy place, the prize-winner,
9     martyr, the holy Apa Phobamon, this which is laid down in the mountain
10    of the fortress of Djeme, through you, you, the most pious and the deacon, Apa Syrous,
11    and through everyone who comes after you forever; greeting; that, since
12    the law of God commands and exhorts everyone, that each one
13    do the good and that which is fine for the salvation of his soul, and afterwards (that) no one
14    have authority, according to the time, to prevent any man from doing what is in his (power), in what is his own; that, when God the Good commanded, and there was begotten to me a son,
15    a male, I called his name Andreas; God commanded, he brought
16    upon him a sickness according to the deserving of our sins; I took him into the place,
17    holy, Abba Phobamon the honoured martyr, who is laid down in the mountain
18    of Djeme; I laid him before the altar, I entreated him with a supplication
19    and with petitions, that, if you receive favour over him before Christ, and his mercy
20    raise him up and have mercy on him, I will make him a servant unto your holy place, that he may serve the holy monastery, either in the servitude of his body, or
21    that he pay his tax(-due) in to the lamp of the holy place, through you, Sourous the deacon,
22    and the head of that holy place; so that no man
23    shall become lord over my beloved son Andreas, except only the holy place,
24    that he become a servant unto the place all the days of his life,
25    after the manner of all the servants of the monastery, that he become a contributor of his tax(-due)
26    in to the holy monastery yearly. Since I have considered well
27    that I am a sinner, I have determined and I have donated my beloved son
28    Andreas to the holy monastery for the salvation of my soul; I have set my intent in to this deed of donation forever. I, Staurou the daughter of the blessed Pesate, she who first wrote above, willing and persuaded,
29    without any deceit or fear or extortion or fraud
30    or coercion or imposition; there is no compulsion existing for me;
31    I will that the holy monastery become the lord of my beloved
32    son, in the labour of his hands and in the servitude [of] his body;
33    and it is not permitted to me, nor to any man (acting) for me, whether great
34    or small, whether those who rule or those who are ruled over them,
35    to be able to come against the holy monastery concerning the little child of this kind,
36    nor to establish any purpose against him near us, beginning or authority,
37    neither small nor great; and whoever shall dare to do this, in the first place that person shall gain no profit, but rather God shall make him stand
38    on his feet at his holy oratory, and he (God) shall pursue judgment with him, and he shall render account
39    at the judgment-seat of God, concerning what he did, exacting, since he laid his hand upon (it), to the destruction
40    of our vow and our offering, this which we vowed
41    with God; and he shall draw upon himself the curses of the scripture which is in the Deuteronomy of Moses, that he shall not see the kingdom of God,
42    it being open, and they shall prevent him from going in to it, established firm. So
43    I have set down this deed of donation of love, that it shall become firm,
44    valid, guaranteed in every place, before every authority,
