[TRANSLATION]
1     Indeed the word of the least have I heard
2     I have remembered concerning my beloved son
3     In peace I am the first of this
4     With him [vessels] of frankincense
5     I have given to you of his feet
6     Concerning my son Daniel, I will make brotherhood, and you will receive love
7     Which of these concerning my son Daniel, with me, some hairs of him
8     To the sole one of the child which
9     From which I have sent you, thus you say to him one of these
10    That it will come from which we have sent from
11    The days which we have placed in the heart
12    Which I have filled these
13    Of son in peace through
14    He will send forth
15    Completely toward me
