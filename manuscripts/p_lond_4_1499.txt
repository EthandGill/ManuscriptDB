[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God
2     the merciful.
3     There is no god
4     but God alone.
5     … [of the Ho]ly Spirit, of the holy and life-giving and
6     … written in the month Phamenoth 19, of the seventh indiction.
7     … (Coptic:) [the district] of Psoi and Kollouthos the son of Oualentios the lasha[ne (headman)]
8     of Enetos … to bend (submit); but if anyone be over them, to bend, the lord, the most illustrious, the governor, will impose it upon us for the …
9     I, Korthos the son of [O]uarentios, the headman of [S]akoo[re], I undertake to (give) a surety-acknowledgment according to its (his?) power, and I take the risk before the person of Mouses son of Makare the steersman (?), that he may carry through his journey up … so that I may convey (?) (it), under any penalty, just as our lord the most illustrious governor (commands) …
10    I, Apa Victor the son of Theodosios, he said to me, [I wrote on] his (behalf), because he does not know how to write.
11    I, Athanasios the son of the blessed Makare, I bear witness to this acknowledgment according to its power.
12    David, the least presbyter, the son of the blessed Jacob, I bear witness to this surety-acknowledgment
13    according to its power …
14    I, Dioskoros the son of the blessed Isaak, the man of Tjkoou, I bear witness to this acknowledgment.
15    By the request of the one who drew it up: I, Phoibammon the son of the blessed Theodoro[s],
16    I bear witness to this acknowledgment according to its power.
17    Apollo, the least presbyter, the son of the blessed Thomas, I bear witness to this surety-acknowledgment
18    according to its power.
19    Surety record (gnosis):
20    of Patermouthios (son of) Menas the headman from the hamlet of Bounon, (and) of Kollouthos (son of) Oualentios the headman fro[m the hamlet of Sakoore],
21    through me, Theodoros, with God, [the notary].
22    Surety-acknowledgment made by Patermouthios (son of) Menas the headman from Bounon and Kollouthos the headman from Sak[oore]
23    on account of the sending-out of 3 sailors, for the matter of the sailors of the boats of the raid of Egypt at … of the raid of indiction 8, written in the month Phamenoth [19, of the seventh indiction].
