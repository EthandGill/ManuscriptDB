[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother [Pamour,]
2     Pekysis your brother.
3     Before all things I greet you warmly, praying for your good health. Your wife greets you, and your son Horos
4     and his little sister. Your brother Horos greets you
5     and Theognostos and Psais and all our people, and we are
6     all in good health by the grace of God.
7     Perhaps you have heard about
8     our son Horos, that
9     he is now serving (in the liturgy), and surely because of this you did not wish to come to us,
10    foreseeing the
11    fulfillment of the liturgy. Come yourself, and he has no need of you for this. I am amazed how you did not write to me even if
12    one fleece, knowing that we have nothing else in hand. Neither to me
13    did you send, nor to your son.
14    For not only is he occupied
15    with the service of the liturgy, but I myself have
16    not leisure for a single hour from such matters; and I am astonished
17    at your lack of concern about
18    us. For if you bought the
19    little purple, even just for a sack, you did not send it. Astonishing! For you could have
20    bought a linen cloth and put
21    it into it and sent it
22    to me. And, if requested, buy me the good-colored
23    little wool. Greet for me brother Philammon, if he is
24    with you. Do what you do. I pray for your health for many
25    times, prospering.
26    And I will overtake you quickly upon this, for you appeared heavy-headed (slow).
27    We were greatly grieved … not
28    having received (anyone?) through those who have now come to us, I mean Nestorios and Besas son of Syros.
29    For the whole day on which they came in, it was still uncertain … of the arrangement
30    Be well.
31    To my lord brother
32    Pamour
33    Pekysis
