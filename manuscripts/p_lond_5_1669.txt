1     [Payment] to John the deputy.
2     [Provide] from the lodging
3     of the [canon] of the first indiction
4     [Gold] coins six, it amounts to gold coins six
5     beside [keratia] [fifteen] quarter gold-coinage, Thoth eighteen
6     of the same [indiction,] what was given to
7     Antinoopolis account of expenditures.
8     Bottos the village-headman assents.
9     [Charisios] the village-headman assents.
