[TRANSLATION]
1     … about the same [vill]age, [g]reeting. I acknowledge [voluntar]i[ly]
2     and of my own free will that I have leased from you, for a five-year period reckoned from the crops, with God, of the tenth indiction
3     and of it (onward), the estate of the most noble Sibylla and Heraeis,
4     daughters of Mousaios (son) of Syrion, the deceased tax-collector,
5     situated on the parts toward the west of the
6     same village, of the allotment of Piah Peto, with cisterns, both a spring-fed
7     and a flood-fed (one), and a dwelling and plantings and palm-trees and every right
8     within an[d] without according to the boundaries, on condition that I cultivate this
9     with my o[w]n animal[s] and farm-workers, and in return for the
10    rents I am ready, in full and (allowing for) lack-of-flooding —
11    which may it not be — to provide what is owed upon
12    the estate itself in grain and in money-taxes and in other things,
13    and on the account of the landlords themselves, apart from the tax-assessment,
14    in the upper [in]stalment and the west, to give them, of grain,
15    twenty-six artaba[s] by the measure of the most glorious count Ammonios,
16    and for the [pa]sture-animals to give them, of clean washed
17    wool, twelve pounds; and I have without hindrance (the right)
18    to take [wat]er from the water-channels onto outside arouras
19    without ch[arge. The] lease is in duplicate, of identical form, and on being questioned [I acknowledged]. Aurelius
20    Psacho[s (son) of Vict]or, the aforesaid, have leased as set out above. Aurelius
21    Joann[es (son) of Ab]raamios, having been asked, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing
22    letters. … for the embo[l]e (grain-shipment) with the fr[ei]ght-charges, and the money-tax
23    and oth[er] … And I shall give also seventy cheeses and of lepsane
24    six short (loaves) … os, John … ostou, through [me] … it was writt[en].
25    … (of) Psakos (son) of Victor, the estate of Piah Peto, of the canon of the tenth [indiction].
