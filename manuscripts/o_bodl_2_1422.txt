[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of villages of the produce of the 15th year of Antoninus
2     Caesar the lord, Epeiph 5, on behalf of
3     Charakos, named Porieuthos of Herakleios
4     of wheat, one-eighth of an artabe, becomes eight artabes of wheat, and named Tabos
5     … of Herakleios, wheat, one-twenty-fourth of an artabe becomes 24 artabes of wheat
6     P… I have marked.
