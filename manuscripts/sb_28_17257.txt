[TRANSLATION]
1     With God. Sale son of Abirasdzid to you Apollos of Apakurou
2     from the rock abba Poulei. [Something] befell you from the public [and]
3     [of the] distribution 7th indiction and of expenditure 8th indiction, numismata 2 thus: distribution numisma 1 third,
4     [of expenditure] months 12 numisma 3, exed numisma 3, and for grain-supplement artabas 1/2. And you
5     [these things] having paid nothing else provide and take from the
6     deputy [the] order being given by you.
7     total [numismata] 2 grain artabas 1/2
