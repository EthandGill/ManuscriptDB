[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting from Neandros, an Oitaian, of the … hipparchy, holder of a hundred arouras. I am wronged by Paos, of those
2     who dwell in Topsais. For when I gave Pete… his father an Egyptian contract
3     in security, which Pechysis drew up with me concerning 75 artabas of wheat as rent, which he owed me from the 3rd [year], on condition that, if he should not
4     repay them to me, I should recover the contract from him; and Pete…os having died,
5     the father of Paos, before I had recovered the … , Paos [his son] holding
6     the contract, being asked by me … the grain …
7     he does not repay (it). I beg you, therefore, O king, [to order] Diophanes the strategos [to write]
8     to Kallikles the epistates to send Paos [before D]iophanes, in order that, if what I write be true,
9     he may compel him to give back to me the [contr]act, that through you, O king, I may obtain
10    justice. Farewell.
11    To Kallikles. Above all, reconcile them; but if not, [send them to us before the 10th of Choiach, so that
12    they may be judged before the proper court].
13    [Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 28].
14    Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 28.
15    Neandros the Oitaian, holder of a hundred arouras, against
16    Paos, concerning a contract.
