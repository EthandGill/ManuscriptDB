[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammonios and partners, embankment-superintendents of (the district) Around Thebes,
2     (to) Eponychos (son of) Horos, greeting. You have thrown up (completed)
3     the naubion falling to you, ⅔ 1/12, on the dyke of Kerameia,
4     of year 4 of Domitian Caesar
5     the lord, Pharmouthi 21.
6     E[p]onychos(?), clerk, I have signed.
