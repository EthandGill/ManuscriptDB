[TRANSLATION]
1     To Heron son of Sarapion, a minor, with his guardian Ischyrion
2     son of Herodes, the minor's mother Heroïs attending,
3     from Aphrodisios son of Akousilaos from the metropolis.
4     I wish to lease the (lands) belonging to Heron
5     about the village Euhemereia, of vineyard land eleven arouras
6     or however many there may be, in one parcel, for two years from the present
7     23rd year of Antoninus Caesar the lord, the total rent each year
8     of the whole arouras, without seed-supply (i.e. unsown), of forty artabas of wheat by the dromos-measure of four choinikes, free of deductions
9     and free of risk; and I shall perform the yearly works: embankments,
10    irrigations, hoeings, double-diggings, raisings (of soil) of the canals,
11    sowings, weedings, and the other (works) as are fitting,
12    at my own (expense) at the needful times, doing no damage,
13    sowing the arouras in the first year with whatever
14    crops I may choose except safflower, and in the second year
15    the half part with wheat and the remaining half with crops of fallow(-restoration), the yearly public transport-charges being
16    against me, Aphrodisios, but all the other public (charges)
17    against Heron. And the yearly rent I shall pay
18    in the month Pauni, new, pure, unadulterated, free of barley, in
19    the village Theadelpheia, and after the term I shall hand over
20    the arouras, from the harvest of what is to be sown,
21    as is set forth, clean from rush, reed, dog's-tooth grass, sedge,
22    of every (weed), if it seem good to lease (them).
23    Aphrodisios, about 40 years old, scar on the middle of the forehead.
24    Year 23 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Hathyr 12.
