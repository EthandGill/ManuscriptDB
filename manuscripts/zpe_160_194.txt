1     From the estate of Theon at Pakoul, sheet 12.
2     Achilles of Plutio to the grain-collectors of Tholthe
3     of the middle [district], greetings. Distribute the things you have of mine
4     In the estate [claim] for wheat of the yield of year 1 of the Aurelii,
5     of Marcus Antoninus and Lucius Verus,
6     of the lords Aut[ocrat]ors, to Dionysius
7     of Sarapion's mother Pl[utarch], artabas
8     twelve, total of wheat, artabas 12. Achilles, the aforementioned
9     I have noted the wheat artabas, twelve, [total] artabas 12.
10    Dionysius, the aforementioned, distributed to me, [of] Peteno [district], artabas 4, one-fourth, choinikes 4,
11    [of the] Istr[us district], artabas 6, one-half, one-fourth, choinikes 8,
12    …lou…i Harpaesis, Peenno, [half] artabe, choinikes 8,
13    [Total of wheat] artabas twelve.
