r.1    ἄγοντ … μὲν
r.2    γὰρ οὐ πρ[ο]σέσχεν, ἀλλʼ οὔτε πρὸς σὲ ἠβουλήθη βαδίσαι οὔτε ἐπʼ ἔργον.

r.5    καὶ γέγραφέν σοι ἐ
r.6    πιστολὴν ὥρας δευτέρας, ἣν

[TRANSLATION]
1     [bringing/conducting] … indeed
2     for he did not attend, but neither did he wish to go to you nor to work.
3     Moreover, he has also come,
4     Metrodorus to us
5     and he has written to you [a]
6     letter at the second hour, which
7     giving to the guard toward you
8     we sent, requesting you to wait for him. Fare well. Year 32, Pauni 23.
