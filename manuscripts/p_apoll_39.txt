[TRANSLATION]
1     The one delivering to your God-protected and most magnificent Lordship this present letter of mine has aske[d] …
2     you on behalf of his companion, that [i]s, Theodosios the sailor, [a]nd i[f it be] possible I beseec[h] my mas[ter to order] …
3     for I did not wish to wr[i]te to my master, since he came and laid out an exp[e]ns[e] as through some …
4     to the Latopolis (district) you detained his sailor; and it seemed incredib[le to m]e, and neither did I wish to give …
5     that the master of mine does not endure to do any such thing; so when the common
6     brother came, Kyr[os] …
7     having inquired closely I learned the cause, and from this I did not wish to write to him to rele[ase] …
8     he came beseeching that I write to my master. So, as I said before, I bese[ech] my master to ord[er] …
9     and concerning the assessment-register of his man, whatever you order, write to me …
10    unless that Kyros Christophoros enrolled him into the register of the asses[sment] …
11    … into the register of Latopolis, unless Kyros Christophor[os] himself …
12    … him from the re[g]is[ter] …
13    of my mas[t]er. I greet you, the God-[pro]tected, [through the lett]er.
14    To Kyros Papas, the greatest count and pagarch, Platon.
15    Concerning Philotheos son of Sampson, sailor.
