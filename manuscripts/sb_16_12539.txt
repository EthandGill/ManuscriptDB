[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year thirteen of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, in the month of Augustus
2     on the twenty-fifth, in Tebtunis of the Polemon division of the Arsinuite nome. Sokrates son of Ptolemaios, about sixty-two years old,
3     scar on the left cheek below the temple, leased from Theogonides [to Horos son of Horos] a Persian
4     of the later-born, about fifty-five years old, scar on the left shin, the plot of land belonging to him near Theogonides, a residential allotment of
5     four arouras, or however many are in the two seals, for four years from the thirteenth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus; the first year of the lease
6     the sown crops of Sokrates remaining without tax and share-payments; the
7     second and last year of the lease for wheat sowing, rent together with wheat seed each year of the two-year period wheat at four artabas of the whole each year wheat at six artabas; and the third
8     year of the lease for sowing of crops other than wheat, rent together with seeds of whatever crops he chooses, of all the crops at twenty-eight artabas and of hay and wine for libation at one earthenware jar; of the three years
9     all free from all risk and unaccountable for all reckoning.
10    It shall not be allowed then to the lessee within the time to abandon the
11    lease. All the works of this allotment let him complete
12    each year [the] lessee from his own resources and the care of the green crops
13    providing. And the aforesaid rents of the three years each year shall receive
14    Sokrates holding [them] in the month of Payni each year in Theogonides in a treasury measure
15    measured by treasury measure. But if the [lessee] does not deliver or owes anything according to what is written herein, let him pay to Sokrates all the annual rents of the three-year period
16    [and] a penalty [of one hundred twenty-two drachmas of silver] and to the public [the same amount], the recovery of payment
17    being for Sokrates from both the lessee and from all his possessions just as
18    from a judgment confirmed, the lease against
19    all the years by Sokrates
20    I Horos son of Horos a Persian of the later-born have leased the aforementioned four arouras on all the aforementioned [terms]. He wrote for
21    him Horos his son because he did not know letters.
