[TRANSLATION]
1     … [Ka]lybes of the Oxyrhynchite nome
2     [belonging to the] same illustrious household, enrolled
3     his farmer, on [which condition] he continuously
4     to remain and to dwell in the same
5     [estate] and not at all him to be abandoned
6     nor indeed to be transferred to another
7     place, but also if sought him
8     to me from the same illustrious household
9     through the things pertaining to him on whatever [day] whatsoever for whatever reason
10    of pretext him I transfer and [I will deliver without any place] of refuge
11    [surety] … from the city of the Oxyrhynchites
12    … eion of Onnophrios …
