[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, produce of the 28th year of Commodus
2     Antoninus Caesar our Lord, Epeiph 27
3     For Charax, in the name of Senpamontus
4     Of Dematus, wheat artaba one one-third
5     It becomes wheat artaba 1 [one-third]. Horus.
