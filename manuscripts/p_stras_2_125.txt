[TRANSLATION]
1     Damion of Philon Sk… to Pachoutus greetings.
2     I acknowledge from you [the] rent of the ninth year …
3     of Arabia of the field, which I bought from Thrakides, [for] silver seven drachmas. Year 26 of Caesar. And nothing
4     do I charge you with. Moschas of Kallites wrote the
5     acknowledgment, this [one] of Damion, having arranged [it] for me.
