1     From Taugest and Taustos tw[in] [brothers] those in […] in Memphis
2     great Sarapieion. We are wronged by Neph[o]ritus
3     our mother. Having abandoned our fa[ther]
4     and having lived with Philip son of Sōgenes a soldier from the
5     unit of Py…rōtos, and Philip, according to
6     distrust ordered him to kill our father
7     … [a wo]man/… and drawing the sword he pursued him. And the house of our father near

9     and swimming down until he came to an island in the ri[ver]
