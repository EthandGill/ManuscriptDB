[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Eudoxios son of Athanasios, distributor of Syene,
2     to Aurelius Philammon son of Hermes, from Hermou polis,
3     superintendent of the 3rd indiction, greeting.
4     I received from you in Hermou polis of meat three hundred
5     thirty-four pounds, on the condition that I credit them to you
6     on the spot and issue receipt documents
7     of this transaction. I, Aurelius Eudoxios the aforesaid,
8     have signed, and it is in my own hand. And likewise
9     you credited to Pamounis the centurion from Erkousis
10    of meat fifteen pounds and of chaff three hundred pounds, on the condition that I credit them to you in Syene.
11    I, Eudoxios, have signed. I received the copy.
12    I, Silbanos, have signed.
13    Aurelius Eudoxios son of Athanasios, councillor of Hermou polis
14    the most splendid, distributor of Syene, to Aurelius
15    Philammon son of Hermes, councillor of Hermou polis,
16    superintendent of the 3rd indiction. I received from you
17    in Hermou polis of wine five hundred seventy-seven xestai and of meat one hundred seventy-five pounds and of chaff three thousand pounds, on the condition that I credit them to you in Syene and give you receipt documents. I, Eudoxios, have signed.
18    And likewise I have from you on account of … of wine one hundred thirty-five xestai,
19    on the condition that I credit them to you on the spot.
20    I received the copy. I, Silbanos, have signed.
