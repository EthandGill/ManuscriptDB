[TRANSLATION]
1     To Asklepiades, kinsman (of the king) and strategos,
2     from Dionysios son of Kephalas, royal farmer,
3     of those from the village of Tenis also called Akoris
4     of the Mochites. On account of the reasons to be shown in the matter — when I and
5     my mother Senabollous had drawn up with Admetos also called Chesthotes, one of those from the same
6     village, a contract of loan through the record-office of 150 artabas of wheat in the 9th year, not only that,
7     but I also deposited with him in trust, on (the security of) the bare plots which
8     I have, a contract of mortgage;
9     the accused, having got control
10    of the agreements, brought to completion none of the matters arranged
11    between me and him, on account of which
12    no small harm has befallen me through him; and now too, taking his stand in an unjust dispute,
13    seeing that I am occupied about the sowing of the land which I farm,
14    making attacks on me, he does not allow [me] to attend
15    to the farming, contrary to the things ordered concerning us
16    the farmers in many (instructions); whence, the land being in danger of going out of cultivation, I have been compelled, since at present
17    I cannot litigate with him about the agreements, to make my refuge with you. I ask, if it seem good,
18    to give order, before all else, to write to the
19    epistates of Akoris not to permit the
20    accused to harass either me or
21    my mother, and to give
22    me the assurances in writing, until,
23    when I have come away from the sowing,
24    I may set up with him the
25    account about each matter; and this being done, none of the things
26    useful to the king will fall through, and I shall have received help.
27    Farewell.
28    To Bias: if he is a royal
29    farmer, let provision be made that he be set up
30    undisturbed
31    until [from] the sowing
32    he is finished.
33    Year 10, Thoth 24.
34    Of Dionysios
35    To Bias.
