[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Pam[our son of Pam]our[i]o[s, whose mother is]
2     Takose, from the vill[a]ge of Kellis of the city of the [Mo]th[i]tai of the Great Oasis,
3     now dwelling in the village of Aphrodite of the
4     Antaeo[p]olite, dark-haired, stunted,
5     to Aurelia Sophia daughter of Besas, whose mother is Ta…s,
6     from the same village of Kellis, now
7     residing in the same village of Aphrodite, greetings.
8     I acknowledge that I have had and have borrowed
9     from you, here, for my own and
10    necessary need, and have had counted out through
11    hand, of silver of the Augusti's coinage,
12    five thousand talents, total 5,000 talents of silver,
13    as capital; and I will provide you, as interest
14    on these, fixed per each month,
15    five hundred talents of silver, total 500 talents,
16    from the present day of the current
17    month Mecheir 20 of the present 7th
18    indiction, until the repayment of the capital;
19    and whenever you wish to recover from
20    me the capital, I will restore it to you
21    together with the accrued fixed interest, that is
22    the interest, without delay and without dispute,
23    all my means being pledged to you until I repay the debt. And this
24    bond, written in a single copy, I have issued to you
25    for security, valid and secure, and
26    being asked the formal question, I ack[n]owledged it. After [the] consulship
27    of Jo[vian] for the 4th time and of Flavius Sallustius
28    the m[o]st illustrious prefect of the sacred praetorium,
29    Mecheir 20. I, Aurelius Pamour son of Pamourios,
30    the aforesaid, have had and owe the
31    five thousand talents of the silver capital
32    and will repay together with the accrued interest
33    per month of five hundred talents of silver
34    and I conse[nt to all] the things written as set out above. I, Aurelius Pebos son of Tithoes, from the same
35    village of Kellis, wrote on his behalf the letters
36    as he does not know letters.
37    Pamour son of Pamour, from Kellis, 5,000 talents of silver.
