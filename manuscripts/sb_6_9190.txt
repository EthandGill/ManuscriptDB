[TRANSLATION]
1     SM
2     [In the year] sixteenth of Emperor Caesar Trajan
3     [Hadrian's, month] New Sebaste, 24, in the village [Tala]w of the lower toparchy of the Oxyrhynchite [nome].
4     [made a loan] Horos son of Horos son of Herakles, [his] mother Nephersout,
5     [to] Panoufis son of Dieuchos son of Panoufis, [his] mother Tetheut,
6     [a Persian woman] of later [generation], both from the aforesaid village [Tala]w, in the street, apart from the other [loans] which he had loaned to him
7     [under a] separate loan through the same bank office in the same month Sebaste
8     [of the four]teenth year of Hadrian Caesar the lord
9     [silver] 408 drachmas, as a guarantee concerning
10    [the said] Talw, from the Konon and the other plot
11    [cultivated] of one and a half arouras, which he in error had [declared] as dwellers the whole one [and a] half, but by nature [reed] indeed one aroura and of fallow-vineyard indeed half an aroura
12    ... through which Panoufis brought [a petition] against Horos
13    [through a] bank's handwritten [document] in the same fourteenth [year]
14    [year, in the month] Epeiph it was declared, and of the whole aroura of one and a half
15    [possession] Horos registered into the [acquisition] property registry, and now other silver drachmas two hundred ten,
16    [those added to the said] silver drachmas six hundred eighteen
