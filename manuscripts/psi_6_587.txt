[TRANSLATION]
1     Etearch to Zenon greetings. Diotimus ordered me to write to you that
2     He will be present in Philadelphia as
3     From Onneus and Moiethymius, and
4     In order that, if to Alexandria you wish
5     To sail down together with him, you may prepare.
6     Fare [well. Year] … Phaophi 26.
7     To Zenon
