[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of treasury of the metropolis of grain, year 7 of Antoninus and
2     Verus of the lords Augustuses, Pauni 22
3     for Charax, name Senchatbut of Horus, through Thionchonsis Sachomnaios, artabas one-fourth 24, [it] amounts to artabas 4 24
4     and of name Petemmenophius … for villages through the same, artabas one-sixth 24
5     [it] amounts to artabas 6 24. Amonius, I have marked.
