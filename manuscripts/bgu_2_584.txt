# HGV-EN: Apollonios, son of Kephalon, to Paneph[remmis], son of Tesenouphis. I acknowledge that I have sold to you the white-mouse coloured donkey belonging to me, which has cast its first teeth, on the spot, just as it is, unreturnable. And I have received the whole price agreed upon in full and I shall guarantee with every guarantee. Year 5 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, T[y]bi 3.
