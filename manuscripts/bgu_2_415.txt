[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a payment-order from the … and
2     [Is]idoros bank, in the [six]th year
3     of Imperator Caesar Nerva [Trajan Augustus]
4     Germanicus Dacicus, in the month Neos Seb[astos], … :
5     Horion son of Satabous and Sarapi[on]
6     son of Ammonios and Publius Fabius D[ionysi]os
7     and Pa[nom]geus son of [S]totoetis and St[otoetis]
8     son of Erieus and Pakysis son of Erieus, Stotoe[tis]
9     son of Petesouchos and Stotoetis son of Psene[sis],
10    the eight (acknowledge) to Ammonios son of Heron that he has been paid in full
11    the equivalent of what they owed
12    by payment-order of the bank of Philos,
13    which he has also returned to them for cancellation, namely four hundred and forty silver drachmas, making 440 drachmas,
14    and that they make no [claim against] them concerning
15    any matter at all, written or unwritten, up to the present day, in any way whatever,
16    it being void, namely (the receipt) which
17    the aforesaid father of Ammonios,
18    Heron, issued to them, another receipt.
19    I, Ammonios son of Heron, have been paid in full by
20    the aforesaid persons the
21    four hundred and forty silver drachmas,
22    which they gave me by payment-order of the bank of Philos
23    of the Clothes-Market, which I have also returned for cancellation, and I make no
24    claim against them concerning any matter at all up to the present day,
25    it being void, namely (the receipt) which
26    my aforesaid father issued to them, a written receipt, as is set forth above.
