[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakleides son of Akous, his mother being Diogenis, from Aphrodision in the Little
2     Oasis, acknowledges that he has received from Ammonianos son of Sarammon, his mother being Helene,
3     from the office of village-scribes of the
4     Ammoniake, through Herakleides son of Chairemon, his mother being Dionysia, from the city of Oxyrhynchos, a friend of Ammonianos,
5     those (sums) which he deposited with him when he was in the Ammoniake in the fourth year, namely in silver
6     of the Augusti coinage three hundred and sixty drachmas, total 360 drachmas, and he makes no
7     claim nor shall he make a claim concerning this matter
8     nor concerning anything else whatsoever, written or unwritten, up to the present
9     day, on account of his having received it
10    in full. And void and not to be brought forward are the documents of security for these (sums) which at that time he happened to have issued to him.
11    valid are the letters of the surety of them.
12    The acknowledgment, written in duplicate, is valid.
13    Year seven of the Emperors Caesars
14    Lucius Septimius Severus Pius
15    Pertinax, Arabicus, Adiabenicus, Parthicus
16    Maximus, and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
17    Augusti, Phaophi 4. I, Herakleides son of Akous, the aforesaid, have received from Ammonianos through Herakleides those (sums) which I deposited with him in the Ammoniake,
18    being in the fourth year, the three hundred and
19    sixty drachmas, and I make no claim against you, but all is as
20    stated above.
21    Sent to Ammonianos.
