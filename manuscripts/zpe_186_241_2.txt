# HGV-EN: Year 3, Pachons 13, for the beer tax twenty drachmae. Year 3, Pachons 13. There has been paid into the tax office at Phebichis to Nikolaos the banker and Stotoetis the controller from Petosiris the (amount) due from Taembes from Talae for the beer tax on the account of Pharmouthi eleven drachmae and on the account of Pachons nine drachmae, total 20 drachmae. In the presence of Dorion, twenty drachmae in total. In front of Petosiris son of Nechtyris.
