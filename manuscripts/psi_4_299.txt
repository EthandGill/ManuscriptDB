[TRANSLATION]
1     To the lady [sister], Titianus wishes well-being.
2     Having obtained [the one] going up to you, I was moved
3     to write to you the things that have happened to me, that I was seized
4     by illness for a long time … so as not to be able even to move.
5     And when the illness was lightened for me, my eye troubled me and I had trachoma and terrible [afflictions]
6     I have suffered, and moreover other p[arts] of the body
7     so that I nearly came to [need] surgery, but thanks to god. But my father …, for whom also being sick I remained, is sick; and because of him
8     I am still here. Be patient, therefore, sister, until
9     god should prosper [to] you. And continually on account of this [I pray t]o god until
10    he should prosper me again to you. But all those in the house fell sick, both the mother and all the children, so that we do not have even a servant, but
11    we continually need everything from god. And I myself
12    try, whenever I obtain passage by ship, to reach you. My lord
13    father and mother greet you; all those in the house greet you; I greet my lord [brother] and … [and] Cyrilla
14    A letter of the governor was sent to me; and if indeed it was brought to you, it would be well [to] … [to] receive or let Morus the friend messenger receive and let him take …
15    very many greetings.
16    Before all things I pray
17    greet … greet …
