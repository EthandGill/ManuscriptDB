[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Arsinoe, the Brother-and-Sister Gods,
2     year …, Epeiph 28. Oath which Semtheus son of Teos swore and subscribed in his own hand,
3     a Herakleopolite of the laborers (?), who is also called Herakleodoros. I swear by
4     King Ptolemy, the (son) descended from King Ptolemy, and by Queen Berenike
5     and the Brother-and-Sister Gods and the Benefactor Gods their parents and by
6     Isis and Sarapis and all the other native gods and all the goddesses,
7     that I have indeed conducted business under Klitarchos, the agent of Asklepiades the banker at the collection-office in Phebichis of (the toparchy of) Koites, and
8     that I will deliver all that falls (due) into the royal (treasury) rightly and justly, and whatever
9     copper money I receive from Klitarchos, apart from what I may … ,
10    I will restore … to the bank in Herakleopolis, and if
11    [any] expenditure be instructed, which I shall expend upon the localities, I will also give
12    an [account] of all that falls (due) to Klitarchos, both of the receipt and
13    of the expenditure, and the vouchers, of whatever I shall expend. And if I owe anything
14    in addition with respect to the management, I will pay (it) to the royal bank
15    within 5 days, and let the right of execution be both from me and from [all my property],
16    and (I swear) to alienate none of my property, or (else) let this
17    contract (be valid) against me, and that I will be answerable to Klitarchos [and to those]
18    acting for him, outside temple and altar and precinct and every shelter.
19    If I keep my oath, may it be well with me; but if I forswear it, may I be liable to impiety.
