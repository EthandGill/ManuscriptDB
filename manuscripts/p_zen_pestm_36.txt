[TRANSLATION]
1     Menes to Zenon greetings. [After sending the previously written letter,]
2     a little later I seized Stachys … having led [him] to the
3     [property] of Amyntas I handed over [him] with [the other boys. And this now] I have written to you
4     so that knowing [you] might not be distressed.
5     [Farewell. Year 29, Daision] …
6     To Zenon.
7     Menes concerning Stachys.
8     Year 29, Daision 21,
9     in Arsinoe.
