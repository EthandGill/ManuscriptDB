[TRANSLATION]
1   Thaisarion to Serenos and [...] the brothers, very many greetings.
2   Before all things I pray for your (pl.) health, and our obeisance
3   I make before the lord Sarapis. I want you to know that
4   I received from Nilos [...]. Receive, you too, from him
5   a bundle of reeds and four sandals for Herais and two for Serenos and
6   one for Ammonios and one for Ch[...], and a fou-
7   kation(?). When the sister [...] and … well, quickly
8   send me a little letter. [I greet] Ammonion and Sampous.
9   Ptolemaios and Alexandros greet you all.
10   Thaisarion to Serapous her [sister and] the brothers, very many greetings.
11   Before all things I pray for your (pl.) health, and our obeisance
12   I make before the lord Sarapis. I want you to know that our brother
13   Ptolemaios went up in the morning, at the hour, Epeiph 9. And I spent
14   on his dinner what you (pl.) sent me. And you, the half
15   send me, of two jars of radish-oil, as of the price of what I have
16   spent. For I too have need of them when I give birth. And
17   indeed he is your (pl.) brother. For Ammonios sailed up with
18   the prefect, being one of his mess; and wherever he may be, he caught up with
19   us. If it seems good to my mother to come with him, let her get
20   herself ready. But you did not even deign to write one letter. And send me a small jar
21   of eye-salve(?) … . Farewell.
22   Deliver to Serenos [...] ☓ [...]
