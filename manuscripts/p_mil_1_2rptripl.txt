[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Cleopatra and King Ptolemy called Alexander, the son of the Mother-loving Gods,
2     year 13 which is also year 10, under the priests and priestesses and kanephoros of those who are and exist, in the month Pauni 18, in Pathyris,
3     before Hermias, deputy of Paniskos the agoranomos.
4     Thaibis daughter of Phibis, Persian, about 58 years old, of middling height, honey-colored, long-faced, straight-nosed, somewhat short-sighted, with her guardian her own brother
5     Paous the elder son of Patous son of Phagonis, Persian, about 35 years old, of middling height, honey-colored, somewhat bent, long-faced, straight-nosed, without distinguishing mark, sold
6     from the ground of a palm-grove belonging to her, that planted with growth, which is in the eastern part of Pathyris, the sixth share falling to Pasemis son of Nechoutes her own son, of which the neighbors are: to the south, the portion of the palm-grove of Panereus,
7     to the north and east, that of Horos son of Nechoutes; to the west, a canal, or whoever may be the neighbors on all sides. There bought it Phibis and Horos, the two
8     sons of Nechoutes, born of Thaibis daughter of Phibis, an equal third share, for two talents of bronze, four thousand drachmas.
9     The seller-in-advance and guarantor of all matters concerning this purchase is Thaibis, who selling them, which Phibis
10    and Horos the buyers received.
11    Hermias, deputy of Paniskos, I have transacted.
