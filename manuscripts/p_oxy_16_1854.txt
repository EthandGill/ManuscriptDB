[TRANSLATION]
1     I have [delivered] the stable-master to the younger excavators so that they may give to him the horse which they took
2     they may give it to him and [may return] … not having brought it, and I think that the mystery already
3     has obscured and those people. Will you therefore by all means before the waters take possession
4     of the land send to me him, since the lord lives, if anything should happen to him, you will bear
5     his value. But by all means release Serenus the [cartulary] and the fan-bearer
6     and the greater one, for I do not want you to [ever] accomplish [such] a thing. For I was able
7     and I could have made those coming from Cynopolis to Oxyrhynchus not even appear,
8     and yet I did nothing of the sort. But send here one of the cartularies
9     owing to prepare an account with Agrippan the notable, so that we may learn
10    what we owe from him, because we are engaged in the matter. By all means therefore through today
11    send him.
12    To the master, most illustrious in all things and worthy of all honor, genuine [brother] George, administrator, illustrious Victor,
13    with God, landholder.
