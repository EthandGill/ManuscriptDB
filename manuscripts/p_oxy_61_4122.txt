[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Constantius and
2     Maximian, the most noble Caesars, for the 5th time;
3     To Aurelius Seuthes also called Horion, logistes of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
4     from Aurelius Hierax son of Asklepiades, from the same city. Concerning all that has been lawlessly dared against
5     our wife, Sarapias by name, by Psois
6     and Tiron his son-in-law and their wives
7     and sons, I have already made the whole account
8     through the deposition of witnesses which I published publicly. Since
9     therefore my aforesaid wife was maltreated with
10    blows and they struck her on the arm, of necessity I submit these documents, requesting that through one of
11    the attendants about you a public physician be sent to inspect, together with that same attendant,
12    the condition of my wife and to report to you in writing, and that these things be in the class of testimony and security for our sake, so that the right of redress may be preserved for me
13    before the greatness of the prefecture.
14    Year 21 and 13 of our lords Diocletian
15    and Maximian, Augusti, and of Constantius and Maximian,
16    the most noble Caesars, Payni 28.
17    I, Aurelius Hierax son of Asklepiades, have submitted this.
18    I, Aurelius Horion, attendant of the logistes,
19    have brought it in.
20    Year 21 and 13, Payni 28.
