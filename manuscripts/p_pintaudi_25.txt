[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the second year of Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus
2     Germanicus. [He] worked in the mountain canal of Polemon for five days for
3     embankment works of the same second year of Narmouthes;
4     Petermouthis of Trypho[n] of Orseus, [his] mother [of] Tamieus.
5     Neilos, I have sealed.
6     Aphrodisios, I have sealed.
7     Sarapei[o]n, I have sealed Petermouthis.
8     Ptolemaios, I have sealed.
