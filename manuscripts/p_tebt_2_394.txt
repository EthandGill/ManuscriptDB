[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymos, also called Louris, son of Lysimachos, to Lysimachos son of Pasion,
2     greetings. I received from you
3     the value of jointly-purchased wheat
4     which you withdrew from the public bank in the name
5     of Ptolemaios son of Didymos
6     of Kerkeseiphos, of wheat, artabas
7     five [and a] quarter, and Tamysthas son of Lysimachos of Tebtunis,
8     [of] one [and a] half artaba, and Ker[seiphos]
9     likewise [of] one [and a] half, [these] together amount to
10    artabas eight [and a] quarter, at [the rate of] each
11    artaba [of] drachmae
12    seven, the total drachmae fifty-seven
13    four-obol [and] half-obol,
14    [which] amount to drachmae 57 four-obol [and] half-obol. [In] year 12 of Antoninus
15    Caesar the lord, Phamenoth 27.
