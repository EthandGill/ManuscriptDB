[TRANSLATION]
1     … [to] be in danger from …
2     Of Menophilus a garment worth in silver 32 drachmae …
3     Of Sitalkos a robe worth in silver 5 drachmae …, we request
4     to summon those from Nikippos,
5     [so that] when the inspection has been made
6     we indeed may receive, but they
7     may obtain the fitting punishment,
8     in order that we may be helped.
9     farewell
