[TRANSLATION]
1     Karpos son of Markus Lailius [H]ymenaeus to Petearpokhrates son of Nikanor
2     Greetings. I have from you on account of Berenice what you deposited at Koptos for Phnas son of Pamineus for the account of Markus Lailius
3     Hymenaeus: of Amnean wine, six jars, three gomoi
4     Total: jars 6, gomoi 3. Year 21 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Athur 19.
