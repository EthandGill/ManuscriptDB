1   Peteuris son of Peteuris, linen-seller, paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the fifth year
3   of Trajan Caesar the
4   lord, on account eight drachmas — total 8 dr.

6   I, Hermogenes, the collector,
7   wrote (this).
