[TRANSLATION]
1     Aur[elius] Hie[rax also called Melas] … and as I am registered, an Alexandrian
2     [land-]holder in [the Oxyrhyn]chite nome in the village of Sespta,
3     To Aur[elius] Sarapion [also called] Apollo[nian], strategos of the Hermopolite [nome], greetings.
4     Since I helped by accomplishing the grain [tax] of the [nome] that [is] under you [administered by] Apollonius the
5     your leader, I swear by the [fortune] of our lords the Emperors Caesar Marcus
6     Aurelius Antoninus Pious Fortunate and Marcus Aurelius Alexander Caesar
7     [the] Augustuses [that] their fortune [may] remain with you and [may not] depart from the city of Hermes until I [shall have] presented all
8     my accomplishment to you soundly and with all faithfulness [having] accomplished [it], or [may] I be liable to the oath. Aurelius Hierax also called Melas the aforementioned [wrote this] in my own hand
9     [I] wrote and swore the oath. Year 5 [of Emperor] Caesar Marcus Aurelius
10    Antoninus Pious Fortunate and [Marcus Aurelius Ale]xand[er] Caesar Augu[stuses],
11    Choiak 29.
