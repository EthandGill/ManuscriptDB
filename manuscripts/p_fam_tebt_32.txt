[TRANSLATION]
1     [From the] approval of epheboi, ninth year of Antoninus
2     [Caesar], the master: Philosarapis, [son of] Heracleides,
3     [the one] also [called] Valerius Heracleides, mother Heracleas, [daughter of] Hermios,
4     [aged] fourteen. Witnesses: Nemesamnon, [son of] Demetrius,
5     [an] Athenian, the one also [called] Artemisios, Poseidonios, the one also [called] Turbon,
6     [son of] Harpok[ratiom of Oseirantinoe, the one also called Parrasios,]
7     … [son of] Kastor, Aelius, the one also [called] Polieus. Copy
8     [of the] signature of the mightiest strategist Minikios
9     [Korel]lianos thus being: [it was] received. Apollonios,
10    [council]man and keeper of civic records: … is in registration.
11    Philosarapis, [son of] Heracleides, the
12    one also [called] Valerius Heracleides, [son of] Matidios,
13    the one also [called] Kallitekn[i]os.
