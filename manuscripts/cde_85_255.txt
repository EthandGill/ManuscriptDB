[TRANSLATION]
1     30(?).
2     In the twelfth year of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus
3     Germanicus, in the month Neos Sebastos, the 30th, in Ptolemais Euergetis of the
4     Arsinoite nome. Taeuemeros daughter of Markos, about fifty-five years old, with a scar by the right thumb, with as guardian her son Sarapion son of Sarapion, about twenty-five years old, with a scar in the middle of the forehead
5     under the hair, agrees with Markos Anthestios Gemellos, a soldier of the third cohort of Itouraeans, of the century of Titius, about thirty-five years old, with a scar on the chin
6     on the left, that the contracting Taeuemeros shall under compulsion repay to Markos Anthestios Gemellos, within (the days) which she requested of him
7     for a respite of the repayment, five days from the aforesaid (date), the (sums) owed to the mother of Anthestios, Thaesis daughter of Sisois,
8     [still] surviving, by Herakleides son of Horion, according to the agreement drawn up through the
9     [grapheion in the village of Karanis], completed in the sixth year of the deified Vespasian, in the month Pharmouthi,
10    [a thousand drachmas of silver] … the Taeuemeros (mortgaged?) of Herakleides [around Karanis] … [of an olive-grove of arouras]
11    [three and a half, against the] … [as it is stated]
12    [by the same Herakleides] four hundred forty [drachmas] of silver [, the … the] Taeuemeros [to Markos of the]
13    [aforesaid] thousand [drachmas] of silver [and of what the same] [Herakleides] owes
14    [Herakleides by another] document, of other [drachmas] of silver, forty(?)- … [forty …] … [of the present year …] …
