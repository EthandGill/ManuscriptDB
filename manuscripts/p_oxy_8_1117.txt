[TRANSLATION]
1     From so-and-so and so-and-so, who became curators of the golden statue of Athe[na also called Thoeris,]
2     the greatest goddess. Recently, my lord prefect, while you were visiting [in our]
3     city, you decided between us and the magistrates an appeal …
4     concerning which the most excellent Magnus ordered to be brought into the city … [18 talents by those]
5     who made the Thoeris (statue) and the crowned magistrates of the year; [concerning which you the]
6     lord, according to what seemed good to you, gave your decision thus: he who gave the undertaking and he who
7     received the summary and the magistrates who disbursed and the curators shall restore to your native city the deficit of the gold, according to the … [We therefore],
8     lord, being ourselves men of moderate means, request, in accordance also with the [decision] …
9     and the two who disbursed, being gymnasiarchs of the year, and the …
10    Theon, who himself also disbursed throughout the whole year, and a choice … having received … from the goldsmith craftsmen, and having received documents of the hand[ing-over from the goldsmi]ths who took it over, and on the spot for the …
11    having registered, in accordance with the memoranda which he also registered, to contribute
12    for taxes to us, the curators, 6 in number … …
13    being (obliged to pay) the 18 talents to the city, from two talents may we contribute …
14    and thus what was ordered by you the lord, in accordance also with what you decided,
15    may come about, so that we may be benefited. For we …
16    possess moderate means, from which we barely live. Wherefore it is [just that other]
17    time-limits be given to us for the repayment, so that … [by each]
18    of us the curators, yearly, may be contributed to the city …
19    2000 drachmas, and thus we too may be able to remain in our own (place) …
20    and not, being besieged all at once, toward the …
21    become subject to sale.
