[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year] … and twentieth of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Augustus Armeniacus Medicus Parthicus Sarmaticus Germanicus Maximus, Epeiph 2, in the city of Oxyrhynchos of the Thebaid, with good fortune.
2     [These dispositions were made,] being of sound mind and understanding, by Petosorapis son of Petosorapis, grandson of Epineikos, his mother being Sarapias, from the city of Oxyrhynchos, in the street. For as long as I survive, I am to have authority over my own property, to do with it whatever I wish and to take it back or annul this will without hindrance; and whatever I do is to be valid. But if I should die under this will, having done nothing concerning it, I leave as heir my son Epineikos, his mother being …chero…ys, [from] the same city, if he lives; but if not, the children he may have and the other children that shall be born to me, or, if no other children are born to me,
3     … all … of the property formerly belonging to me, of Hermias as he is styled, his mother being Isi…sio…, in Antipera Pela, from the …
4     … to the future descendants … and of whatever furniture and utensils and household goods and debts I may leave …
5     … to my sister [Apollonous] if she survives, but if not, to the same son of mine Epineikos; and the utensils and furniture and other things being in three …
6     … niece … and the same son of mine Epineikos, both in the exedra and the cell above the gateway and in the whole …
7     … and … [from what] I left to her, to repay whatever I may appear to owe, and she is to have authority to settle everything as may seem good to her … the same son of mine … living with her until he becomes twenty years old, taking care … to succeed in my name … the proceeds arising from them and supporting him from them …
8     … to him with motherly affection, who shall restore to him, when he reaches the aforesaid
9     [age] … of whatever he succeeds to in my name, after whatever she may have spent on him, from whom neither accounts nor …
10    … to him. And I wish my same sister Apollonous to take care of the one under guardianship … and whatever, under the published copy of this will, I may write in my own hand, whether removing something of the aforesaid or adding further dispositions [or favouring others or even wishing anything else — let these too be valid] as … written in the will, and let it not be permitted to anyone at all to interfere with the things ordered by me … [for the one who abides by it] a penalty of three thousand drachmas, and to the public treasury the
11    like sum, and none the less are the things ordered by me to remain valid.
12    [The will is valid.] …
