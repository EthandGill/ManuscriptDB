[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklas and partners, collectors of the assessment
2     for the customs-arrears (telonikon deficit), …,
3     to Psendokim( ) (son of) Aos, also called Pa…pe…:
4     we have received for the tax two drachmas, total 2 dr. Year 3
5     of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
6     month Hadrianos 27. I, Patebes, have signed. I, Dioskoros, have signed.
7     Others, Tybi 10, in the name of the same, two drachmas, total 2 dr. I, Asklas, have signed.
8     I, Dioskoros, have signed. Patebes.
9     Others, Pharmouthi 6, in the name of the same, two obols. I, Asklas, have signed.
10    I, Dioskoros, have signed.
