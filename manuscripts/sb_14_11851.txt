[TRANSLATION]
1     Lucretianus to Lucretius the
2     father, greetings. I do obeisance to you
3     before Sarapis. I remain until the proclamation becomes [known], since it did not occur on the twentieth, since the nomarch will go out and my work not yet completed on account of this,
4     [I] remained so that it might occur perhaps the proclamation. Receive from Plosius
5     the dia[---]kian of Valerius and small coins
6     five. I have received from Valerius
7     drachmae 7 and a dalmatic and a mantle
8     and a small basket of bread and dates
9     and an onion. Greet Vetia
10    and Valerianus and the
11    lodgers all. Farewell you all.
12    Give [this] to Lucretius from Lucretianus his son.
