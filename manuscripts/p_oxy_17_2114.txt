1     Aurelius Apollonius, also called Eudaimon, prefect of Oxyrhynchus through
2     Plou... successor
3     To Aurelius Hera, prior-apparent of... the village, the dearest, greetings.
4     Through what [he] wrote, the propriety of my lord, [the] administrator of the Heptanomia Aurelius Gregorius, ordered of the delegated wine of the Thebaid in the 5th indiction the half
5     to be sent in old [measure] or the value of each xestes from denarii 65, [those] having met together
6     for this of wine-tasters having been chosen and
7     from the council of the superintendent. Take care therefore, having received this letter, the
8     the [falling due] measurement in xestes to the village under you
9     to deliver to the superintendent, so that no ambush concerning the military rations might occur. I wish you to be well,
10    dearest.
11    [of the consulship of Kaici]nius Sabinus and Vettius Rufinus
12    [of the most il]lustrious, Mesore 17.
13    [Aurelius Dio]scorus, servant of the [prefect], I brought
14    [on the same] day.
