[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 12th year of the Autocrator Caesar
2     Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus
3     Augustus Pious, Phamenothos 9th
4     Petosiries of Marreios, sacred calf-sealer, I inspected one calf
5     being sacrificed in Soknopaiou Nesos by Pausieres Pan[e]phremphis from the same
6     village and having approved, I sealed [it] as
7     [it] is pure. …
