1     He measured out to us [grain-collectors] of the treasury
2     of the village of Terythes, Papnouthis of [Kollouthos]
3     for the allotment and [Massalinian]
4     of dirty wheat, six one-fourth artabae
5     and twenty-fourth, the wheat artabae total 6 4 24 for
6     the 16th of the indiction, and the freight-charges and
7     the sack-carriage charges in full. Papoes
8     by the father Horus I have marked.

10    through [me] Papnouthis they have marked.
