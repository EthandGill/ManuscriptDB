[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapion, one of the successors and sub-administrator, from Ptolemy [a] Macedonian, of those being in military possession in the
2     great Serapieion, this year the eleventh. I gave to you the one from the king
3     and of the queen appointed
4     of the twins petition for
5     what is owed to them per year,
6     and you ordered to give [the portion] of the 18th year
7     and for the 19th year oil and castor-oil, each
8     year of oil one metretes and of castor-oil one metretes.
9     Concerning these things indeed, Sarapis and Isis give to you
10    grace granted by Aphrodite, form, with the king
11    and the queen through which you have toward
12    the divine piety. Seeking still to obtain and in these things cooperation, I ask you,
13    since I foresee, lest, having been separated
14    you to the upper regions, the twins should not receive [the portion] of the twentieth year
15    oil and castor-oil, thinking [that] you in private
16    deliver [to] them, write to Mennides,
17    the overseer, to deliver [it] to me
18    and
19    of this year the owed oil,
20    one metretes and castor-oil one metretes, with nothing offset. And to you may it be in return for which toward
21    the divine you are piously disposed and of the
22    temple-servants [and] all of those in the sanctuary
23    you care for, may you have grace granted by Aphrodite,
24    form, prosperity, and [in] other matters good fortune.
25    Farewell. To the secretaries: having examined [it] to submit.
26    Year 20, Choiach 23. To Dorion:
27    having examined [it] to submit. Year 20, Choiach 29.
