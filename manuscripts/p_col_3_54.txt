[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy Soter, year 30, under the priest
2     Alexandros son of Leonidas, of Alexander and the Brother-Gods, the basket-bearer of Arsinoe
3     Philadelphos being Prepousa daughter of Demetrios, in the month Panemos, and by the Egyptians Epeiph 10,
4     Hegesarchos son of Theopompos and Theopompos son of Hegesarchos and Nikodemos son of Hegesarchos
5     the three Macedonians of the epigone took on lease from Zenon son of Agreophon, a Caunian, of the staff of
6     Apollonios the dioiketes, from the lands given by the king in the Arsinoite
7     nome near Philadelphia to Apollonios the dioiketes — of ten thousand arouras of arable land — in the
8     third embankment-section, from north to south, 100 arouras for one year, each aroura at 7 1/2 artabas of wheat, free of risk
9     and exempt from every deduction. There shall be given for the wheat-bearing land as seed 1/2 artaba per aroura,
10    and for the barley-bearing land in proportion, and for upkeep 1 artaba of barley per aroura, and for weeding 1/2 artaba of barley,
11    and for wood-cutting, if cutting falls due, as much as may be reckoned sufficient to be given for each
12    aroura. And Hegesarchos and Theopompos and Nikodemos shall measure out the grain for the
13    rent into the granary at Philadelphia according to the corn-officer's regulation in the month
14    Daisios in year 31, and likewise also whatever grain they take for seed and
15    weeding and upkeep, and whatever silver they borrow for the cutting, or grain
16    instead of the silver — of wheat at 1 drachma 2 obols of copper per artaba, and of barley in proportion.
17    And Hegesarchos and Theopompos and Nikodemos shall sow of the land they have leased the
18    two parts with wheat and the third part with barley. And if they do not act as they have contracted,
19    let it be permitted to Zenon to lease out the land to others. And the damage by which they harm
20    the revenue of Apollonios, or whatever they may owe in addition with respect to the rent and the loans,
21    let them pay to Zenon forthwith with the half added. And let the right of execution belong to Zenon, or to another acting on his behalf,
22    against themselves and the sureties and all their possessions
23    and against one and against all, as in royal cases. Sureties for the terms of the contract
24    for payment are the contracting parties for one another, and Ammonios son of Theon, a Cyrenaean, of the overseers.
25    And let this contract be valid wherever it is produced. Witnesses: Damis son of Kleon, Sostratos
26    son of Kleon, the two Heleneans; Theopompos son of Aristion, a Thessalian, physician; Diodoros son of Zopyros,
27    a Magnesian, of the staff of Apollonios the dioiketes; Agathinos son of Pyrrhos, a Cyrenaean, of the overseers.
28    Anosis son of Totorchoes, a Saite, village-scribe of Philadelphia.
29    Keeper of the contract: Damis.
30    The rent of the 100 arouras is, in wheat, 712 1/2 artabas
31    and for seed they have, in wheat, 73 [4] artabas
32    and what was given for the sowing and the remaining works,
33    100 drachmas of copper, which in wheat, according to the contract
34    of the lease, reckoning wheat at [1] artaba per [1 drachma] 2 obols, are 75 artabas.
35    Total: 860 1/2 1/4 artabas of wheat.

40    5 years, each year 347 1/3 artabas of wheat,
41    and for the 5 years, 1736 2/3 artabas of wheat.
