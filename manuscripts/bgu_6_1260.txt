[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 16 which is also year 13, Mecheir 5, in Pathyris, before Hermias
2     the (agent) of Paniskos the agoranomos.
3     Nechthanoupis son of Patseous, a Persian, about 55 years old,
4     and his sons withdraw from the (claim to the) land sold to them
5     by Petearsemtheia son of Panobchounis, grain-bearing land
6     in the plain around Pathyris, one aroura, whose neighbours are:
7     to the south the land of Patous son of Horos and of his brothers, to the north
8     the land of Chensthotes son of Harpaesis, to the east the land of Leos
9     and his associates, to the west an embankment, or whoever the neighbours may be,
10    for three talents of bronze; and they, being present at the
11    record-office, agreed, Nechthanoupis and his sons, that they have received the redemption-price of the said
12    one aroura, and that Nechthanoupis
13    and his sons and no one else on their behalf shall not proceed
14    against Petearsemtheia nor against any other on his behalf concerning the said talents; and if not,
15    let the (right of) proceeding be void for the one proceeding, and let the one who proceeds in addition pay forthwith as a penalty
16    six talents of bronze and, as sacred to the sovereigns, of coined silver
17    120 drachmas, and none the less let the things written above be
18    valid.
19    I, Hermias the (agent) of Paniskos, have transacted (this).
20    Discharge (of claim).
21    Petearsemtheia
22    son of Panobchounis;
23    of land, 1 aroura, which has been sold;
24    Nechthanoupis son of Patseous
25    and his sons.
