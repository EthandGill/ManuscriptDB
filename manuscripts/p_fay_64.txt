[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 9, Pachon 27, through Dioskoros and Toutetos and [their] colleagues, silver tax collectors of the village
2     of Euhemeria. You have recorded [that] I have received for an account
3     of recording oil goods of year 8, year 7 … year 8
4     fifty-six drachmas, total 56 drachmas, which
5     we will also record to the treasury on [your] account of Apollonius, and we will deliver to you the treasury receipt.
