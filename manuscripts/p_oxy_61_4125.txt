1     [After the consulship of] our lords Licinius [Augustus for the 6th time and Licinius the most] illustrious
2     [Caesar for the 2nd time, in the consulship of those to be designated] consuls for the 2nd time,
3     [Pharmouthi] 3.
4     [Aurelius Apphous son of Theon,] his mother being Thermouthis,
5     [from the splendid and most splendid] city of the Oxyrhynchites,
6     [priest of Zeus and Hera and komast] of the divine
7     [busts and of their Victory, to Au]relius
8     [Apphthonios son of Stratonikos, who had served as] councillor of the (same city),
9     [after the consulship] of our lords Licinius
10    [Augustus for the 6th time] and Licinius the most illustrious
11    Caesar for the 2nd time, in the consulship of those to be designated consuls for the 2nd time, Pharmouthi 3.
12    Aurelius Apphous son of Theon, his mother being Thermouthis,
13    from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, priest
14    of Zeus and Hera and komast of the divine busts
15    and of their Victory, to Aurelius Aphthonios son of Stratonikos,
16    who had served as councillor of the same city, greeting. I acknowledge
17    that I have received from you as a loan, from hand to hand, out of your house,
18    two talents of imperial silver coinage
19    and four thousand drachmas, total 2 talents, 4,000 drachmas, which I shall of necessity repay to you by the second of Pachon
20    of the present 16th, 14th, and 6th year, without any delay or subterfuge; but if not, I shall pay you for
21    the time overrun the interest fixed between
22    us at each mina for each month, you having the right of execution both against me and out of
23    all my property. This bond of hand is valid,
24    written in duplicate, valid wherever produced
25    and by anyone producing it on your behalf, and, on being formally questioned, I gave my assent.
26    I, Aurelius Apphous, have received as a loan the
27    two talents of silver and four thousand drachmas, and I shall repay as aforesaid, and, on being formally questioned, I gave my assent. I, Aurelius Zoilos, wrote on his behalf as he does not
28    [know] letters.
29    Through me, Sotas, it was written.
