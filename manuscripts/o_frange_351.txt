[TRANSLATION]
1     on the one hand, of my [word]
2     of the least [one I greet] you, Father of the holy ones, and the mighty [ones], Abba Prange and my Brother of the brothers who will know Moses
3     since you have the spirit with us, since you have received the mark [of] love also when you went among the tombs
4     I give you a belt also, because the Lord loves you
5     I know that I gave it to you
6     but you ... you ...
7     make love that you may be at peace
8     with you among all your choice of the holy ones, the man of the faith, a joy in the Lord
9     thanks of the brothers through Matthew, this least one
