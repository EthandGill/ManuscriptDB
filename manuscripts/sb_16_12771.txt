1     In year 20, Mesore 30, [he/she] has measured in for the record of the same year

3     of the Phatreus [wheat] artabas 7, [total] wheat artabas 7. Mesore 30
4     Apollonius has measured in as previously stated, year 20 of the lower place
5     wheat artabas 7, [total] wheat artabas 7. year 20 Mesore 30
