[TRANSLATION]
1     John, a surgeon, who in some manner gave a donation … concerning a portion
2     in the whole property of the Balonian[s] with men belonging to you generally, as pertaining, as the superior said, made in the holy church of Ravenna [by] the honest Sisibera, a donor, which [I?] present signed
3     [with] the sign of the holy cross, and [it] was left with us, of this
4     subscription, and shall be preserved by all [things?] for the gospel
5     corporally provides sacraments, and also [this] donation
6     [by] Sisibera Palombus, a deacon and made vicar, handed … [I] saw.
