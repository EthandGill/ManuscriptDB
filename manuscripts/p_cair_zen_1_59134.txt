[TRANSLATION]
1     Agathinos to Zenon greetings.
2     Theudoros came to me
3     concerning money, saying that you
4     had written to give [it] to him,
5     but Krotos having appeared said not to give [it].
6     As then it seems to you, write to us.
7     Farewell. Year 29, Phamenoth
8     11.
9     To Zenon.
