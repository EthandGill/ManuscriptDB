[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lord[s] Constantius and Maximian
2     the most illustrious Caesars, for the 5th time.
3     To Aurelius Horion, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite,
4     from Aurelius Tryphon son of Thonius, ex-exegete, councillor of the splendid and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, superintendent of the grain-and-barley being conveyed up to the Small
5     Oasis. I swear by the fortune of our lords the Emperors
6     Diocletian and Maximian, [A]ugusti, and Constantius and
7     Maximian, the most illustrious Caesars, that I have received, from the Aurelii Sabinus and his partners, receivers and sealers of the granaries of the village of Kerkeura of the middle
8     toparchy of the Oxyrhynchite nome, from the produce of the nineteenth and 18th year and 11th year, of wheat
9     of the purest, by the public measure, three hundred artabas, making 300 artabas, and from
10    the Aurelii Kiarourios and Louteus and their partners, receivers
11    and sealers of the granaries of the city of Oxyrhynchus, from the exchange
12    of grain of the produce of the past 20th year [and 19th year] and 12th year, of barley of the purest
13    likewise by the public measure, one hundred fifty artabas,
14    making 150 artabas, which grain-and-barley I shall convey up to the Small Oasis
15    [and] shall hand over to the receivers there in full number, and
16    [of the] delivery I shall produce written receipts, so that
17    [in nothing] may I [be blam]ed, or may I [be] liable [to the divine oath ] …
