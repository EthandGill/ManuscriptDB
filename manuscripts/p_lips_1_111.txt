[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord and brother
2     Isidoros, Sion, greeting.
3     Before all things I pray to the Most High God concerning
4     your health and well-being, that this letter from me may find you healthy and
5     in good spirits when you receive it.
6     I wish you to know, my lord brother, that Eusebios went off to the village, to the farm, and did not find
7     the calves working in the farm, neither those of Gennadios
8     nor those of Dikaiarchos; but I also said to Didymos, "Pay the wages
9     to others, so that they may work." He said to me, "We have no money,
10    and if you wish me to go off there and hire workers,
11    then I will work along with them and we shall clear the farm.
12    Write back to me, but see that you do not neglect to write back to me
13    concerning this. For you know that it is the (right) season. And know, my lord brother, that your brother has come from the imperial court (komitatos) holding a praepositura, straight to the Oasis,
14    and is present with me; and he arrived at … on the thirtieth of the month Epeiph. I greet you (and) all those in the
15    household, small and great, by name. And know, my lord
16    brother, why you unreasonably (treated?) him without cause. Write back to me concerning the things you wish, and do not be anxious about us,
17    for no one troubles us, nor (does anyone trouble) Zosimos concerning public taxes
18    … n …
19    I pray for your health
20    for many years,
21    my lord brother.
22    … to my lord brother Isidoros the kephalaiotes, from Nemesion.
