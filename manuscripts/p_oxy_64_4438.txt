[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Agathos Daimon, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
2     from the Aurelii Theon also called Kastor and Dioskourides, both sons of Apollophanes, exegetes and bouleutes
3     of the city of the Oxyrhynchites. According to a holograph sale
4     in the year of Messius, in Choiak, our mother, while she was still living and has since died,
5     purchased for us, her only two sons and heirs, from Aurelius Sarapion also called Dionysotheon, gymnasiarch and bouleutes of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, son of Septimius Epimachos and as
6     he is styled, having served as eutheniarch of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians,
7     in the quarter of the Gymnasium Road, a house
8     with three towers and a courtyard, beneath which is a cellar,
9     and two courts and all their appurtenances,
10    together with entrances and exits, with the consent of his full sister
11    Aurelia Kasiane also called Herais, as the
12    holograph sale contains, which, having been made public by consent, having subjoined to these documents the notarial deed sent back to the
13    keepers of the property records there, we submit
14    the documents, requesting
15    that instructions be sent to the record-keepers to make the requisite
16    entry, as is proper, in the registers kept by them, for our
17    security. Year 3, Phaophi.
18    The Aurelii Theon also called Kastor and Dioskourides,
19    through me their father Aurelius Apollophanes, have submitted this.
