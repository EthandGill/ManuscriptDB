[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Ulkakios Rufinus and Flavius Eusebius
2     of the most splendid [ones], Tybi 4.
3     [The] Aurelii Neilos son of Sansne[us]
4     mother Asitos and Apollon [son] of Mida,
5     mother Alaunios, both from the village
6     Euhemerias [agree] to Aurelius Petros son of Zakawon,
7     deacon of the catholic church of Arsinoe,
8     to have from him the [ones] agreeing, which also
9     they previously agreed to be fulfilled, the price
10    of wheat [indeed] arurae two and vegetable-seed
11    by measure oilary arurae two, and necessarily the [ones] agreeing repay
12    to Petros from mutual liability in [the] month Pauni
13    of the incoming 7th year of [the] new indiction in the village
14    by measure four-choinix without delay,
15    and upon [the] demand, occurring [to you]
16    of the execution from both of the agreeing
17    or also from the possessions of all of them, just as from [a] legal suit, and having been questioned we agreed. Aurelii Neilos
18    and Apollon we agree [we] have and
19    fulfilled the price of the wheat
20    arurae two and vegetable-seed by measure
21    oilary arurae two, which also we will repay from mutual liability at the set time, as
22    [is] provided. Aurelius Heron [son] of Apollonios
23    I wrote on their behalf, [they being] illiterate.
24    [The] handwriting of Neilos and Apollon [for] wheat arurae 2 and vegetable-seed arurae 2.
