[TRANSLATION]
1     I[n the nam]e of [G]o[d]. K[orra son of Szerich, governo]r,
2     [to Basil administrator of the village of Aphrodito. We give thanks to God, and]
3     [aft]e[r th]i[s. One hundred si]x[ty-si]x [and] t[wo-third]s [coi]n[s]
4     we [as]s[essed] th[r]ough your district for the price of the unders[pecified]
5     items of our own expenditure and (that) of the with-us serving men,
6     both Arabs and Christians and various persons,
7     for twelve months of the present ninth indiction; and their
8     receipts having been made, we sent (them) to you for those of the districts. — Receiving therefor[e]
9     the present letter, this gold being collected with guidance(?)
10    to the amount of our receipts, send (it) out at the levy(?)
11    through your trustworthy men, who must pay (it) into the treasury
12    and bring back the receipt for it. Written in the month Pachon 6, indiction 9.
13    Statement of coins: 166, ⅔.
14    thus:
15    sheep 155 at ½ coin, 77 coins ½; fowls 170 at 20 (per coin), 8 coins ½;
16    oil 47 measures at ½ coin, 23 coins ½; vinegar 48 measures at 72 xestai per coin, 6 coins, ⅔;
17    boiled-wine(?) 102 measures at 4 (per) coin, 28 coins; wine 165 jars at 30 (per) coin, 5 coins ½;
18    dates 1 artaba at 12 (per) coin; raisins 2 artabas at ½ coin, 1 coin;
19    onions 5 artabas at 10 (per) coin, ½ coin; wood 240 hundredweights at 16 (per) coin, 15 coins;
20    vegetables 250 bundles at 600 (per) coin, ⅓ 1/12 coin.
