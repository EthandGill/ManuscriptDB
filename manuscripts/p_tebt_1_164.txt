13    … in the … part in
14    the (village) also called Kerkeosiris, also called Ke[rkeosiris], of the
15    division of Polemon, to the south of the Argaitis
16    canal of Euergetes, of the Arsinoite nome.
17    The neighbors of these two parcels of ground (are):
18    on the south, the entrance-way of Horos and Polemon, which is 2 cubits along
19    the length of the entrance and exit; (that) of Tothis son of Sentheus
