[TRANSLATION]
1     Kamēs, collector of money-taxes
2     to Paoukalē Pachnumius
3     Paouftheiutos. I received for the dike-tax and bath-tax
4     of Charakos, year 17, [in] dirty drachmas seven and a half-obol, copper 2, amounting to dirty
5     drachmas 7 and a half-obol, copper 2. Year 18 of Hadrian our lord,
6     Thoth 1. Kamēs, I have signed.
