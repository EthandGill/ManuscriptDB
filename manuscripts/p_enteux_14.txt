[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolem]aios, greetings, [from] Dizaporis, Thracian … [cavalry] commander of seventy arouras. I am wronged
2     [by] Dei[nius the over]seer. For [a woman] … having pledged …
3     … which [he] had in Ker[k]esouchis and when the time specified by the pledge had passed …
4     [Instead] of [renewing] it, he [made] a deposit [according] to the ordinance, and while I was dwelling [in this] stable, [having gone to] Denius [ejected the] horse without trial and installed …
5     … so that [my] horse [is] under the open sky. I beseech therefore you, king, if it seems good to you, to order …
6     Diophanês the [stra]tegus to write to Denius the over[see]r to relocate the Egyptian and return to me the [hou]se; and if he raises any objection, [for him] to be tried [for me], in order that …
7     with the horse exposed [to] the sky, some accident might not occur and …
8     For when this comes to pass, having fled to you, king, I will obtain the ju[stice.]
9     To Denius. Make clear to us concerning these matters how … . Farewell.
10    Year 25, [month] Loios 26, Choiach 13.
