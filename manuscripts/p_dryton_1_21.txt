1     Zois, also called Onchassis, [daughter] of Isidorus,
2     to Patouti, the priest, the [one] from the
3     priests who are in Crocodilopolis
4     city and Pathyris, [of the] priests,

8     [and] three thousand drachmas, and I accuse [you of] nothing,
