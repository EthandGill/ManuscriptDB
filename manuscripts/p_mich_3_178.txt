[TRANSLATION]
1     To Eudemos, strategos, and to Hermaios also
2     called Dryton, royal scribe of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite (nome),
3     and to Ptolemaios, village-scribe, and to Aunes
4     son of Auneous and Orsenouphis son of Orsenouphis,
5     poll-tax registrars of the village of Bakchias of the same division,
6     from Horos, son of Horos, son of Horos, his mother being
7     Heries daughter of Mencheus, from the
8     aforewritten village of Bakchias.
9     There belongs to me and to my brother
10    Horion, in the village, a fourth
11    part of a house and courtyard in which we reside, and I register myself
12    and my household for the house-by-house census of the past second year of Hadrian
13    Caesar the lord.
14    And I am
15    Horos the aforewritten, public farmer, 48 years old, with a scar on the l[eft] eyebrow,
16    and my wife Tapekysis daughter of Horos, 45 years old, of no distinguishing marks,
17    and the children of us both, Horos my son, … years old, of no distinguishing marks,
18    and Horion, another son, 1 year old, of no distinguishing marks,
19    and my aforewritten brother Horion, 35 years old, with a scar in the m[iddle] of the forehead,
20    and his wife Thenatymis
21    daughter of Chariton, about 39 years old, of no distinguishing marks,
22    to whom belongs a half part of the house and courtyard in which we reside,
23    and the son of them both, Horos, 1 year old, of no distinguishing marks.
24    I, Heras, registrar of Bakchias, have written it.
25    I, Orsenouphis, poll-tax registrar, have registered it
26    through Me[la]nas, secretary.
27    I, Aunes son of Auneous, poll-tax registrar, have registered it
28    through Epimachos, secretary. In the 3rd year of Hadrian
29    the lord, Pachon 10.
30    I, the village-scribe, have registered it. Pachon 10.
