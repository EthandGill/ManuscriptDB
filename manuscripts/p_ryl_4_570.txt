[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon, greetings. [Apo...]... I am being wronged by the guards
2     of those in Kerkeosukhis. For robbery [...]... places they brought me by force to Crocodiles'
3     city before Timotheus the nome[arch]... [I] was not released. I beseech you then, since
4     by force by them I was brought and outraged [...]... to me of the village
5     the [plantation] be-in-possession [?]... so that you might be able to do just [things].
6     I wish moreover to provide to you and myself... so that you
7     have nothing to complain of, so that I too [...]... and to write to you
8     I wish concerning these [things] and to... you the brothers. Farewell.
