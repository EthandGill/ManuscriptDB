9     you may take care, and Kraton the collector
10    you may request to deal accommodatingly with Dionysodoros concerning the 3000 drachmas
11    for which he stood surety for the (associates) of Hippokrates and Demetrios.
12    We learn that Zenodoros has sailed up to Sebennytos
13    and we conjecture that Demetrios
14    has not found Zenodoros in the city. If, then,
15    you have spoken to Kraton and he has consented, it would be
