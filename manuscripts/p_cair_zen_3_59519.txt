[TRANSLATION]
1     Phanesis, grain-measurer
2     To Zenon greetings. Having seen me
3     Dionysodoros ordered
4     to take me away already three days.
5     You will do well then, having sent someone from your side
6     to me with the one delivering [you] the letter,
7     because I have no one
8     in the city, and having sent to me a cloak or bronzes,
9     as many as you wish, until someone might sail down to me.
10    Be well.
11    To Zenon.
