[TRANSLATION]
1   Valerion and the other lessees of the Sacred
2   Gate of Syene, through Sarapion the scribe.
3   Paid: Harpaesis son of Phanophis,
4   mother Tachomtbekis, for the trade-
5   tax of the 2nd year of Hadrian the lord:
6   twelve drachmas — total 12 dr. Year 3
7   of Hadrian the lord, Thoth 17.
