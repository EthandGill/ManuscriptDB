[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters Constantius
2     Augustus for the 4th time and Constans Augustus for the 3rd time.
3     To Aurelius Demeas son of Demeas, praepositus of the 7th pagus of the Hermopolite (nome),
4     from the Aurelii: Hatres son of Pamon, his mother being Helene, and
5     Pamounis son of Horos, his mother being Tnaphersois, and Rouphos son of Proous the subscriber, and Petobastis son of Pathermouthios,
6     his mother being Lousia, and Leus son of Pheys, his mother being Tinoutia, and Sneous son of Achillas, his mother being Rhia, all of them from the village of Bousiris. We acknowledge, swearing
7     by the divine fortune of our all-conquering
8     masters the Augusti, willingly and of our own free will, that we have stood surety for the registered public officials nominated
9     by those of the village for the registered
10    services, whom also we will produce, (they) remaining
11    and performing the public service entrusted to each
12    [pu]blic duty. But if any of them should desert
13    [and] we do not produce (him), we ourselves will undertake the
14    [acc]ount on his behalf, or may we be liable to the holy
15    [oa]th and to the peril concerning this matter; and on being formally questioned
16    [we ackn]owledged it. They are:
17    Silvanus son of P[e]tereus, komarch;
18    Leus son of Elias, exactor of the annona (corn-supply).
19    [In the con]sulship of the same, Epeiph 26. Aurelius Rouphos …
20    [I have sw]orn [a]nd I will produce (him) as is set forth above. The others,
21    we who are likewise aforementioned, have sworn and will produce (them) as is set forth above. … I wrote
22    [on] their behalf, they not knowing letters.
