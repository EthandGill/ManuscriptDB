[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionyso[dorus] to Asclepiades greetings. It is a custom for the farmers from Oxyrhynchos to sojourn abroad
2     into the O[xyrhyn]chite [nome] and to perform sacred service because they are from the Oxyrhynchite. [In order that] then
3     having come [briefly], [they] should be ready in relation to the sesam[ic] crop [it seemed] to me [if also] to you [to go] abroad with them. I thought it right to write to you in order that [you might know and what] it seems [you should] write. Be well. Year 17, Tybi 8.
4     If indeed [it is necessary] [quickly] [to be absent] they … well and … [?]
5     [Be well.]
6     To Dionysodorus.
7     To Asclepiades.
