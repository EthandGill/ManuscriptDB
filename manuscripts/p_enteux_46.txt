[TRANSLATION]
1     To King [Ptolemy] greetings [from] The[o]nides from Ammonias. I am wronged [by] Seuthos
2     a cavalry soldier owning [a hundred arouras], who dwells in the same [village]. For owing me directly
3     of barley 15 artabae, which [he pledged to me to] repay from the crops of the 26th year and the time having passed, demanded many times by me, he does not repay to me. I beseech
4     you therefore, king, if it seems good to you, to order Diophanes the general to write to Agathocles
5     the administrator to send Seuthos [for him to] and, if these things are true, to compel him to repay to me, but if he protests in any way, claiming under oath that he does not owe, let him be released,
6     so that I may not be wronged, but having fled to you, king, the benefactor of all,
7     may obtain justice. Fare well.
8     To Agathocles. Having investigated, take care that he may obtain [justice]. Year 1, Gorpiaios 30, Tybi 13.
9     Year 1, Gorpiaios 30, Tybi [13].
10    Theonides against Seuthos
11    concerning barley 15 artabae.
