[TRANSLATION]
1     It was filed.
2     To Theon and Heradion, keepers of the archives,
3     from Apollonides son of Leonides, his mother being
4     Thaesis, from the city of Oxyrhynchos.
5     In accordance with a loan made through the registry-office of Talao
6     in the present fourth year, (in the month) Phaophi,
7     I lent to Myrtilos, freedman of Heliodoros
8     (son) of Diodoros, from the same city, of silver
9     a hundred and sixty drachmas of principal,
10    at interest of a drachma per mina each month, from the same Phaophi to the appointed term, the thirtieth of Phamenoth of the same fourth year,
11    it being stipulated that the right of execution be mine out of
12    all his property, and that it be permitted
13    to me, whenever I wish, to make a (legal) seizure
14    through the same archive, of (the property in) the name of Myrtilos, until I recover (my loan); which seizure, wishing it to take place, I submit
15    this memorandum as is fitting, and I declare that there has previously been seized, through the same archive,
16    in the intercalary days of the ninth year
17    of the deified Aelius Antoninus, by Demetria
18    (daughter) of Artemon son of … esis, against drachmas
19    two hundred of principal at a drachma interest, in
20    Talao, in the parts to the north of the dromos of Isis, a small house with appurtenances; and in (the month) Pauni
21    of the twelfth year of the deified Aelius Antoninu[s]
22    [b]y Apollonios son of Amois son of Harpaesion …
