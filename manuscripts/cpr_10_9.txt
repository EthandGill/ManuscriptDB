[TRANSLATION]
1     [...] Stephanos, slave of the Mother of God, to John, official collector of Ptolemais. Provide for sustenance
2     of bulls, three, and oxen, ..., given per year
3     through Samoun, pre-farmer, to each, of days seven, three, becoming, for days thirty of the month of Thoth, [of] vetch
4     artabas six, quarter, become, of vetch, artabai six, quarter only.
5     of month Thoth 3, indiction 13.
6     become Thoth 3, indiction 13, of vetch, artabai six, quarter
7     Thoth 3, indiction 12, portion of vetch of lord Stephanos, of vetch, artabai six, fourth
