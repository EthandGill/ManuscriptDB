[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollos Mikkalou[ti]
2     to the sister, greetings. I deemed [it] [necessary] to Raeima
3     having come to greet you. As I have enjoined you, observe [it]. Against no one
4     dispute [with anyone], and I also care for you. As I bade [you] farewell, let nothing trouble [you].
5     Greet Aplonous,
6     greets you
7     Phestus.
8     Farewell.
