[TRANSLATION]
1     From the memoranda of Rutillius Achilles, epistrategus.
2     Year 2 of Lucius Septimius Severus Pertinax
3     Pachon 30. When Gaius approached
4     Julius Ptolemaeus, Julianus the rhetor
5     said: this is an Antinoite. violence
6     and [he] suffered and outrage and contract-labor [abuse]
7     appealed to you against Chae[i]remon
8     of Maron. Since you ordered through servant Ptolemaeus, since therefore he is not present, we request according to [the] … the [proper]
9     which you also preserved [by ordering] him sent to Antinopolis to be tried
10    Achilles said: where is the opponent? [He] replied: from a village
11    of Kar[anid]os of this nome.
12    [Achilles said]: the strategos
13    [let arrange the anti]dikos your
14    [to Antinopolis s]end.
