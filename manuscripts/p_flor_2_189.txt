[TRANSLATION]
1     Heras to Heroneinous the dearest, greetings.
2     I remind you of the insult which we suffered last year on account of
3     of the animals and of the [baskets].
4     Since therefore our lord Alypis
5     is not in the city since I was going [to] him and I was making
6     him write to you, but since necessity presses, as indeed you yourself are not ignorant,
7     I write to you, dearest, so that
8     on the 13th from night you [send]
9     the animals and the [baskets towards]
10    me so that not as last year we [are harassed] and now the same [we suffer]. To be well [I pray] for you.
11    Year 14, Mesore 12
