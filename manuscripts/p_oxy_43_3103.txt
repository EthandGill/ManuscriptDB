r.1    Αὐρηλίῳ Ἀντύλλῳ στρατηγῷ Ὀξυρυγχίτου
r.2    παρὰ Αὐρηλίας Σαραπιάδος τῆς καὶ Ἡρακλείας καὶ Αὐρηλίας Τσεντούρβωνος καὶ Αὐρηλίας Τσενοσείρεως διὰ Αὐρηλίου Ἡρακλειανοῦ.

[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Antyllus, strategos of Oxyrhynchus
2     from Aurelia Sarapiada, also called Heraclea, and Aurelia Tsenturbonos and Aurelia Tsenoseireōs, through Aurelius Heraklianos
3     We declare in accordance with what was ordered by Aurelius Terpsilaos, the former administrator
4     [of the o]usiacal properties us to be registered as heirs according to the will of Aurelius Moros, a freedman, and the things bequeathed by [him] are worth eight hundred drachmas, and we swear by Marcus
5     of Aurelius Severus Alexander Caesar
6     of the lord's fortune [not] to be false. Year 5
7     of Imperator Caesar Marcus
8     Aurelius Severus Alexander Pius
9     Fortunate Augustus, Pachon
10    Aurelius Heraklianos presented this
11    and swore the [oath]
