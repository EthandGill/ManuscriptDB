[TRANSLATION]
1     He has measured it into the treasury of the metropolis, produce of 7th year
2     of Hadrian the lord, Epeiph 1,
3     Pachnumis son of Paouphtheiotus, of wheat two
4     plus a half and a twelfth, which become wheat artabae 2 1/2 1/12. Amo has signed it.
