1     Year 17 of Emperor Caesar
2     Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus
3     Augustus Pious. Work has been done for dike works
4     [of] the 17th year, Epeiph 12 through 16
5     in the Epagathian canal
6     from the month [Epeiph 12 for five days] of the 17th year, Soknopaiou Nesos, Stotoetis,
7     [son] of Apynches, son of Stotoetios, mother Tekiasios.
8     Diomedes, I have been marked.
