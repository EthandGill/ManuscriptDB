[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master Flavius Justinus the Emperor Augustus, year one, in the consulship of the same our master, in the year four hundred and sixty-first 461 of the reckoning, in the fifteenth indiction, in the month
2     of Dios, on the sixteenth, in the village of Nessana of the territory of the city of Elusa. These things …
3     … they make of their own divisions with one another: on the one part
4     Flavius Anmos son of Stephanos son of Oualens, soldier of the camp here; and on the
5     [oth]er part Ania daughter of Stephanos son of [Oualens], his full sister, widow, wife of the blessed
6     [Ph]anes son of Abraamios, of buildings belonging to them and devolving by inheritance from Anmos Ouaelos, the blessed grandfather of them on the mother's
7     side. A casting of lots, therefore, and a throwing of dice having taken place between them
8     [in the presence] of their friends and kinsmen present with them, and having bound one another by oaths upon the Holy Trinity and the imperial safety, to abide
9     by this division and to disturb nothing of the things contained in it.
10    [I]f any of them should attempt to transgress, the transgressing party shall give to the abiding
11    party, as a fine and penalty, eight gold pieces, eight gold coins 8, and besides
12    even after the payment of the fine this division shall be valid and secure and
13    [s]trong, with all the force with which it is brought forward.
14    There fell, then, to Anmos the most devoted, as his share, half — the two
15    northern dwellings joined together, being three-vaulted, … roofed, set apart toward the south, with
16    all their right, of which the neighbours: on the east-north, a public street;
17    on the [w]est, the heirs of Alaphallos Zonainos the blessed physician;
18    on the [s]outh, the courtyard-passage of the court and of the portico held in common by them.
