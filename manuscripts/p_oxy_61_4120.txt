[TRANSLATION]
1     It was filed.
2     To the Aurelii Asklepiades, former recorder,
3     and, however he is styled, and his colleague, keepers of the archives,
4     from Aurelius Apollonios, freedman
5     of Antonius, and however he is styled. In accordance with a cheirographon (bond)
6     drawn up in duplicate in the past year 2 and 1,
7     in the month Pachon, Aurelius Asklepiades son of Didymos, his mother being Berenike, from the
8     illustrious and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, acknowledged that he had received from me on loan, from hand to hand, of silver,
9     of the coinage of the Augusti, one talent
10    and two thousand two hundred drachmas of principal,
11    on the security of the property belonging to him and having devolved upon him by right of inheritance, previously belonging to his said father
12    Didymos, namely a third share of a house and courtyard and
13    all the appurtenances thereof, apart from the banqueting-room, situated in the quarter of the Cretan (quarter), by the appointed date of repayment which had been set and had passed,
14    the appointed time of the repayment, as the
15    cheirographon contains; which having been made public by me with the consent of the debtor in the present year 3 and 2, in the month Choiak, the
16    business transaction sent back to us from the assize I bring to you together with
17    equal copies, I hand in the memorandum and declare that the same debtor of mine
18    has not registered the third share of the
19    aforesaid house-properties, and that this belongs to him
20    just as aforesaid by right of inheritance, previously belonging to his said father
21    Aurelius Didymos, as is proper; and I swear
22    the oath customary to the Romans that I have not lied.
23    Year 3 of Imperator Caesar Gaius Aurelius Valerius
24    Diocletianus Germanicus Maximus, and year 2
25    of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Valerius
26    Maximianus, the Pious, the Fortunate, the Augusti,
27    Tybi 6. Aurelius Apollonios
28    have handed in and have sworn the oath
29    as aforesaid. Aurelius Sarapion
30    wrote on his behalf, since he does not know
31    letters.
32    Aurelius Asklepiades, keeper of the archives, I have signed.
33    Security of Sarapion the coppersmith.
