[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of our master] Flavius Justinianus the eternal and Emperor Augustus, in the twelfth year, in the consulship of Flavius Johannes the most glorious, on the ninth day before the Kalends of September, in the year of the province
2     [the four hundred and thirtieth th]ird, in the month Gorpiaios, on the sixth, in the times of the first indiction, in the metropolis Petra of the Third Palestine Salutaris. I, Flavius Patricius son of Diphilus,
3     … [from th]is metropolis, to the most reverend Euthenios son of Dousarios, the most reverend councillor and receiver of the same (city), direct the things set out below:
4     [inasmuch as in the previous] memoranda between me and the most illustrious Panolbios it was resolved that I should furnish to the same most illustrious Panolbios, on account of his expenses, each year the
5     … twelve solidi of gold, but in place of these both I and the most illustrious Panolbios himself agreed that I should lighten the estate and property-group of the same most illustrious Panolbios
6     [from the third part that fell] to him: from the group of Petra, four freehold koriaiai, and from the group of Augustopolis, one and a sixth freehold koriaia. I direct
7     [your] reverence and the most reverend receivers for the time being to lighten the account and the person of the same most illustrious Panolbios from the third
8     [part] of the group of Augustopolis by one and a sixth freehold koriaia, but to charge my own person and account, from the second (indiction) about to enter with God's help
9     [and it itself] and for all the ensuing time of the life of the same most illustrious Panolbios, with the same one and a sixth freehold koriaia, there being licence
10    for [your] reverence and the most reverend receivers for the time being to charge the heirs of the said most illustrious Panolbios, without a second direction,
11    [from the group of Augustopolis with the freeh]old koriaia, one and a sixth, according to … …
