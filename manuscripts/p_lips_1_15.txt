[TRANSLATION]
1     […]reus Chinychios of Hermopolis
2     registered in the quarter of the city south as [aged] forty-nine
3     scar on the little finger of the left hand, to Hermammon [son] of Hermaios from the same
4     city, greetings. I received from you
5     [as] payment for half an aroura from which
6     I [have] sold to Chinychios Porementhios
7     of the produce of the present year
8     in the Around-City above, concerning Thoteus,
9     after which the same Chinychios received from you by hand
10    the same Chinychios silver drachmas
11    eight, and now the remaining [amount] of the payment
12    silver drachmas twenty-eight, of public [affairs]
