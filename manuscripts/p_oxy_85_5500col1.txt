[TRANSLATION]
1     To Isidoros, strategos, and Didymos, basilikogrammateus of the same nome,
2     the most dear, greeting.
3     Of the memorandum of Ulpius Serenianus, the most excellent high-priest, made in the assize … and …
4     … pertaining to the … a copy, as we were instructed
5     by the eclogistes of that nome, I have appended, most dear ones,
6     so that you may know. I pray for your health, most dear.
7     Year 11 of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
8     Augustus, Armeniacus, Medicus, Parthicus, Greatest, Payni.
9     It is as follows:
10    Ulpius Serenianus, the high-priest and superintendent of the sacred things, said:
11    Let the eclogistae and the basilikogrammateis make the adjudications which they have postponed
12    within three months, and thenceforth each year, in the same term, let them make those of the past year, or they shall not be unpunished. And this
13    I necessarily ordain, so that at the time of each man's registration,
14    when it appears, the exaction from the wealthy may also become
15    easier, so that from this negligence not
16    something hard to collect come to be and they themselves endure the loss of necessity. And this too, concerning the nomes under assize, I ordain universally; and let the
17    strategoi and the basilikogrammateis, if they do not examine each matter within the terms I have set, know that they too shall not be free of liability. And in matters for which I have not given a term, I ordain that these too be completed within three months. But if any local accusers, being present and appearing in the registers,
18    concerning which, having been summoned to the reading, they did not obey,
19    concerning which I did not specifically judge about a penalty, let the prescribed penalties be exacted.
20    To Isidoros, strategos, year 11 …
21    … e …
22    … to the strategos and Didymos, basilikogrammateus of the Diopolite of the Thebaid …
