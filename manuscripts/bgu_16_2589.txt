[TRANSLATION]
1     [...] ... Techtho 5 4.
2     Soterichos of Aphrodisios, of those from village Tecth[o]
3     I swear by Caesar Emperor, Son of God, truly:
4     to go away to Heracles of Heracleides, those in the
5     Poncheion plain, of-those-belonging to me, [going] to Herodes
6     of Herodes, arourae ten; and neither shall advance [upon]
7     nor [any] of the sons of me, nor other
8     on behalf of us, anyone, or I shall remove with my own
9     expenses. Should I swear truly, may it be well for me;
10    but should I swear falsely, the opposite. Year 2 of Caesar, Mesore 12
