[TRANSLATION]
1     Do not wish to have any concern about our sister Maria, your
2     wife. For we do not allow, God willing, her to need anything, nor do we think
3     to do [her] harm. And as you have written about the scarlet garment, I have given [it].
4     to her, and I went to your steward wanting to take one gold [coin]
5     from your salary, having made a note for him with my own hand, and he was unwilling to
6     give [it to] me. A small cosmetic box please deign to buy for your sister Phoebadia and four
7     combs of the [Gordian] type. If you are able to buy an iron [instrument] of one mina [weight], do not hesitate to do this
8     [deed]. Abramius, your father-in-law, has permitted me to write to you so that if you receive gold from
9     your steward, [you should] purchase for him one gold [coin's worth of] scales. And we greet [you] much from
10    small to great in your [thoughts], and we are well with God.
11    Give [this] to the master brother Theodore pr[...]
12    from ... brother.
