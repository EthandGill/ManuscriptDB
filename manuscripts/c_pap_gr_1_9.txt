[TRANSLATION]
1     To Artemidoros, chief-justice and in charge of the supervision of the chrematistai and the other courts,
2     from Marcus Sempronius, son of M[arcus,] of the Aemilian tribe, a soldier of the
3     twenty-sec[ond] le[gi]on, of the coh[ort] … ka… rei… [and] from
4     E[rotarion daughter of …] … komei … [with the guardian and gu]arantor, her kinsman [L]u[c]ius … omysios
5     son of Lucius. Erotarion agrees, for fifteen months from
6     Phaophi of the present twenty-sixth year of Caesar, to rear and suckle, outside, at her own place in the city,
7     with her own milk, pure and unspoiled,
8     the (child) which Marcus has entrusted to her, ever since Epeiph of the past year, a slave infant
9     whose name is Primus, for a wage both of the milk and of the rearing, per month, of ten drachmas
10    and two cotylae of olive-oil; and forthwith Erotarion has received from Marcus
11    for both Epeiph and Mesore and Thoth the rearing-wages [and] the oil … and further also for the sixteen months from Pha[op]hi
12    likewise the rearing-wages and the oil for six months; and if — which may it not be — it happen that the infant suffer something that befalls a human (i.e. die)
13    ed … lein …
14    within the six months Erotarion is of necessity
15    to take up another infant and rear and suckle it for the six months, not
16    receiving more than the aforesaid ten months …
17    Erotarion being duly maintained in the rearing-wages and the oil, (she is) to make
18    the care both of herself and of the infant, not spoiling the milk nor lying with a man nor becoming pregnant nor suckling another infant alongside, fo[r] the months
19    six from Phaophi; and if after the six months Erotarion wishes another infant …
20    to suckle alongside, it shall be permitted to her; and whatever she may have received or been entrusted with of the property of the infant,
21    whenever it is demanded back, (she is) either to pay (it)
22    the value of each, except for evident loss, which, when it has also become manifest,
23    let her be released; and (she is) not to abandon the rearing within [the] time; and if she transgress anything,
24    let her pay (back) the rearing-wages she has received and whatever she may receive in addition, with the half as much again,
25    and the damages and expenses and another two hundred silver drachmas, the right of execution being made
26    out of Erotarion and
27    out of all that belongs to her just as if by a legal decision; and (she is) not to bring in pledges, or let them be invalid;
28    and Erotarion carrying out each thing, Marcus too is to supply her the monthly
29    remaining rearing-wages as aforesaid, and not to take the infant away within [the] time … or else he himself also is to be liable to the equal fine. And Erotarion shall present herself to Marcus together with the
30    infant always thrice a month, to be inspected by him. And
31    Lucius is to be entered in the action against Erotarion for what she owes him, together with
32    her mother Cleopatra, according to the agreement. We request. Farewell.
33    Year 26 of Caesar, Phaophi 8.
