[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the treasury of the village boundary-marking of Karanis
2     Of produce of the seventh year and sixth year, in the name of Ammonios
3     Of Papeitos, via Neilos, via the beasts
4     Of Dionysias, via Didas, ten donkeys,
5     [there] come to be ten.
6     Of year eight and seven, Epiphi four.
