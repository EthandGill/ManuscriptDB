[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Horion, Didymus.
2     As many things as I have spoken to you concerning the sons of Matrin[us] …
3     you bring forward in deed; therefore show that you remembered
4     which I deemed you worthy of. F[or] you are not ignorant that I am able
5     to render to you an equal favor, in respect to which
6     if you should deem me worthy.
7     Fare well
8     [I pray]
9     [Lord] bro[ther]
10    to m[y] lord brother.
