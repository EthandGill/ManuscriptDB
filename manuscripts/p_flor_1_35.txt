[TRANSLATION]
1     [From Aun]es Heron [and his] associates, [the] grain-measurers of the village Taleii, [they were] measured to us on the
2     12th [of] the present month Phaophi
3     of the present eighth year
4     of Antoninus and Verus, [our] lords
5     August [greatest rulers], from the yield
6     of [the] 7th year for [those] of the Twenty-Five-Arourae [-lots]
7     [the] land-grant-holders' grain by measure
8     public [standard], [the] due [amount of] six artabae [and] one-third, [with] grain additionally measured [of] one artaba [and] 1/12,
9     [and these] become [by the] reckoning
10    of grain artabae seven [and one] third
11    [and one] twelfth, [and they] become of grain artabae 7 3 12.
12    Aunes Heron, grain-measurer, through Dionysios
13    the scribe, [I] have measured
14    the [artabae] of grain, seven [and one] third
15    [and one] twelfth, [and they] become of grain artabae 7 3 12.
