[TRANSLATION]
1     To Pankrates, chief bodyguard and in charge of [the assessment(?)],
2     from Ptolemaios the Macedonian, holder of eighty arouras, who is in charge
3     of the hundred-aroura allotment of Menoites son of Olympiodoros, an orphan,
4     near Oxyrhyncha of the division of Polemon. In the
5     local apportionments of the registration it was determined by the
6     men that those sowing up to 50 arouras should be charged at the full-seed rate, and those farther than that according to the reckoning
7     of what is to be sown. But in the allotment here detailed,
8     since nothing was sown except 30 arouras, [whose] produce is 90 artabas,
9     out of which it is fitting to assign to the registration of the
10    29th year [1]5 artabas, in place of which, contrary to reckoning, the scribes
11    have registered, contrary to the established rates, at the full-seed rate, 50 artabas, by which there is an excess of 35 artabas, which it is necessary
12    to be entered, into the registered (account) of the 28th year, for the cavalry-deficiency and the king's crown(-tax), 18 (artabas);
13    total 68; and for the scribal (dues) 18;
14    total 86; and the remaining 4 artabas for the freight and the handling-charge and the rest, so that all the gathered 90 artabas should be [enter]ed and nothing at all
15    be [left over. Therefore] I request you, since nothing has been gathered
16    [in the] year by reason of the [allotment] being sown, that you order
17    a letter to be written to Koinos the collector, that, having measured out the
18    86 artabas of wheat as aforesaid, he hold back the rest
19    against the new (crops), him himself the ch …
20    is needed. For this being done …
21    it will be, and through you I shall obtain [justice] …
