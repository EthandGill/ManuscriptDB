[TRANSLATION]
1     Hierokles to Artemidoros, greetings. If you are well and are managing in other respects according to your mind, it would be good. For I myself, having sailed down from above...
2     [was] greatly troubled, but now I am on the point of recovering. And Epharmost is well, and your little children. Concerning Ptolemaeus...
3     and previously indeed I wrote to you … …
4     Zenon, thinking [he] has the [palaestra] …
5     … brought the living creatures …
6     now therefore it happens that he is in a shameful position, unless you apply yourself...
7     concerning him, as is right [for] those who have promised...
8     to carry out. And the cause of all the evils for you is Metrodorus; for he has replied to those who meet [him] concerning Ptolemaeus in...
9     the court, saying such things, that I previously was zealous...
10    concerning Ptolemaeus, for the matter escaped me; but now...
11    having perceived what it is, I speak against it saying these things. Apollonius on the one hand...
12    it happens that he spends the greater time in the country, but Amyntas both living outside and having married and [having] a child...
13    already belonging to him, [so that] no suspicion [falls] on him at least...
14    falls; remaining, the arrow is aimed at me [who am] in the house...
15    living. For if the king perceives the palaestra...
16    [having been] opened, I shall have the greatest suspicion [for it to be] opened through me, because...
17    I am ambitious. Therefore I also ask Amyntas to hasten with us for [the] not...
18    [the palaestra being] opened; but if even Amyntas is forced...
19    it will befall me to withdraw from the house, if I am not able...
20    to persuade Hegemon to write to Apollonius. You therefore both we ask and beseech to make all zeal concerning Ptolemaeus...
21    [in order] to receive the palaestra; for we are persuaded that if you wish all things will come to be. And further also that: will you not be defeated by a man...
22    [who is] not free? But write also to Artemidoros so that he the…[garment]...
23    give to the little children; for he does not give unless you write. Send...
24    also to us the little child which you showed me, so that we may bring even this one to learning.
25    Farewell. Year 29, Dios 19.
26    To the physician Artemidoros.
