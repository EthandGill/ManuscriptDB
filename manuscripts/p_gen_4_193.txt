[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollos son of Dioskoros has given through his heirs as payment for a delivery
2     of the fifteenth indiction for the village taxes of the village of Phthla through the deputy Timotheos, secretary
3     of the village of Aphrodite, grain according to the standard, artabas twenty, twenty-fourth, twenty-fourth,
4     forty-eighth, the sum amounts to grain according to the standard [artabas] 20 4 24 48. The most illustrious pagarch Julianus
5     Illustris, through me Pninnus, assistant, [agrees with me as] aforesaid. The holy trinity. Grain, artabas 20 4 [24] [48]. …
