[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, produce of year 10 of Antoninus
2     Caesar, the lord, Epeiph 10, on behalf of Notos
3     by the name Porieutha Petechespocratus Phthomontha
4     winter wheat: artaba half-third; it amounts to wheat artaba 1/2 1/3. Karuris I have signed.
