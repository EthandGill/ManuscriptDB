1     Menandros and Demetrios
2     To Didymos and Cappares, both brothers, greetings.
3     There is a crowd here now and [everything] alone here. So well
4     you will do, having come here with the girl, and for that [one's] sake.
5     Greet Philotera the
6     daughter of you much, and Maximos. [Receive(?)] a bunch
7     of cabbage …
8     … of what quality …
9     Greet He[gemon]ida the
10    daughter of mine and Skneips
11    and Philokles. It will be a care to you then to come, so that
12    also you [may] take the oregano.
13    I received bread
14    five.
15    Fare well.
