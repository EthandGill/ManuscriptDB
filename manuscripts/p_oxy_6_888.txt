[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Proclamation of his excellency Flavius Valerius Pompeianus, praefect of Egypt. Orphans for whom no guardians have been assigned shall have guardians in accordance with their age created for them by those competent to make the appointment ...; for it will thus result that they receive proper attention, whereas at present much business concerning orphans and depending upon their guardians is delayed because the orphans are unattended by tutores or curatores. The 4th which = the 3rd year of our lords the Augusti Diocletianus and Maximianus, Phaophi... Published in Oxyrhynchus on the 247th of the same month Phaophi. To ..., exegetes in office of the Oxyrhynchite nome and the Small Oasis, senator of the illustrious and most illustrious city of Oxyrhynchus, from ... and Apollonia, both children of Origenes and Thaësis, of the illustrious and most illustrious city of Oxyrhynchus. We were given the guardianship of our nephews (or nieces), the children of our dead sister ... daughter of Origenes, of the said city...
