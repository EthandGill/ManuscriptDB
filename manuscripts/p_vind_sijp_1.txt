[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Adelphios, superintendent of charcoal
2     of the Hermopolite nome, from Aurelius
3     Besas and Moros my brother, ship-owners,
4     helmsmen of a private boat with a wicker rudder(?),
5     of a capacity of three hundred artabas, from Panopolis.
6     We acknowledge, swearing by the divine and
7     heavenly fortune of our all-conquering lords the emperors, that we have received and had loaded into the boat under us,
8     for the past fortunate eleventh indiction, of pure sifted charcoal
9     free from every fault, 150 hundredweight and their tenths, that is hundredths, which we shall carry down
10    to most glorious Alexandria and deliver to whomever you may command, under Eudaimon, centurion, the procurator of the department of the
11    privata (imperial estate), and when their delivery
12    has taken place we shall produce receipt documents, and on being formally questioned
13    we acknowledged. Thus: one basket, one hundredweight, total 1.
14    In the consulship of Ursus and Polemius, the most illustrious,
15    Choiak 28. We, Aurelius Besas … and Moros the brother, the aforementioned
16    … our own boat, of pure charcoal
17    To Aurelius Adelphios, superintendent of charcoal of the Hermopolite
18    nome, from Aurelius Anoubion, ship-owner,
19    helmsman of a private boat with a wicker rudder(?), of a capacity of four hundred artabas, from Mouchis of the
20    Lykopolite nome, greeting. I acknowledge, swearing by the
21    divine and heavenly fortune of our all-conquering
22    lords the emperors, that I have received and had loaded into the boat under us, for the past fortunate eleventh indiction, of pure and sifted charcoal free from every
23    fault, two hundred hundredweight, total 200 hundredweight, and their
24    tenths, that is hundredths, which I shall carry down to
25    most glorious Alexandria and deliver
26    to whomever you may command there, under Eudaimon, centurion, procurator of the department of the privata, and when their delivery has taken place I shall produce receipt documents, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged. Thus:
27    when their delivery has taken place I shall produce receipt documents, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged. Thus:
28    one basket, one hundredweight, total 1.
29    In the consulship of Ursus and Polemius, the most illustrious,
30    Choiak 28. I, Aurelius Anoubion, the aforementioned, had loaded into
31    the aforementioned my own boat, of pure sifted charcoal, 200 hundredweight and their tenths, that is
32    hundredths, and I shall deliver as aforesaid. I wrote on
33    his behalf, he not knowing letters: Aurelius Pagenes, physician.
34    Receipt for charcoal.
