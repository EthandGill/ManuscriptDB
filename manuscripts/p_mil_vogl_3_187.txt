[TRANSLATION]
1     …[for] sowing of wheat as rent for the three-year period together with [which we shall receive seeds] of wheat in three artabae, wheat in eighteen artabae, and the
2     [remaining] arouras three for sowing of hay and pasture [for] sheep and
3     [stalling] [and apportionment] of [ox]en for the three-year period annually without seeds in silver
4     [drachmas of] one hundred twenty, all in measure of four-choinix of ancient treasury
5     …rents unaccountable and without risk, and I will complete the things annually
6     [of the arouras] all agricultural works as many as are fitting [from] the same, and the proposed
7     [rents] [we will deliver] annually in the month of Pauni on [the] threshing-floor, and the rent in the month of Phamenoth, being at us the things annually of the expedited rent-carts
8     [a]nd after the time we shall deliver the arouras clean from [w]eeds of all kinds
9     [a]nd from [non-perfor]mance of the proposed. If it seems [good] to rent, I have noted [it].
