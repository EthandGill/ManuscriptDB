[TRANSLATION]
1     Phthoumonthes and Amenothes,
2     collectors of money-taxes of the Potteries (Kerameia), to Psenchonsis
3     (son of) Pikos (son of) Petobasthis. We have received
4     for poll-tax of year 12, base drachmas five, 2 obols,
5     total base drachmas 5, 2 obols. Year 12 of Antoninus
6     Caesar the lord, Pachon 27.
7     I, Horos, have signed.
8     (2nd hand) I, Phthoumonthes, have signed.
