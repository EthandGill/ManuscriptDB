[TRANSLATION]
1     From Horion son of Apollon[ios,]
2     grandson of Horion also called L …
3     and of his cousin Horion also
4     called Myros … ei …
5     nos, of those who have been exegetai of the city of the Oxyrhynchites. We register, according to what was commanded by Dionysios Papirius the most illustrious prefect,
6     for the house-by-house census of the past 28th year of Aurelius
7     Commodus Antoninus Caesar
8     the lord, the property
9     belonging to us from
10    our fathers … na
11    of the mother Dionysia also called Taamois daughter of Achillion, about the village
12    Ision Panga of the upper toparchy,
13    a house, and about the same village another bare plot, and about the same
14    other bare plots, and about the same a house and courtyard, and about the same a bare plot,
15    in which no one is registered nor resides;
16    and we swear by the fortune of Aurelius
17    Commodus Antoninus
18    Caesar the lord
19    that we have not lied. In the 29th year of Imperat[or]
20    Caesar Marcus Aurelius
21    Commodus Antoninus
22    pius, fortunate, Augustus,
23    Armeniacus, Medicus, Parthicus,
24    Sarmaticus, Germanicus,
25    Maximus, Britannicus,
26    Mesore 30. Horion son of Apollonios, grandson of Horion, also [called]
27    Li … and his cousin Horion
28    also called … we have submitted and have sworn the oath.
