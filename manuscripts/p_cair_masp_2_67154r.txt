[TRANSLATION]
1     [This written deed of dissolution, having the force]
2     also of a repudium, they set down and make toward each other, [willingly]
3     and persuaded: on the one part, F[lavius K]a[ll]inik[o]s
4     son of Kollouthos, notary by trade, [and on] the [oth]er [part]
5     Aurelia Kyra daughter of Menas, of the mother Mariam, both
6     originating from the (city) of the Antinoites, and they acknowledge to each other con[cerning]
7     the matters set forth. Since formerly they were joi[ned]
8     to each other in lawful marriage, but now, a discord
9     having arisen between them through an evil demon who grudged
10    their common cohabitation, they were sundered from their
11    life together; and on this account they have [come] to this
12    written deed of dissolution, [having the for]ce
13    also of a repudium, through which they acknowledge, and each [of them] acknowledges
14    that the (other) party has had and received in full all the [things] brought in by them at the time of their union,
15    from costly to cheap article and one as.
16    And that for the rest neither party has nor shall have any claim
17    against the other, neither about wedding-gifts nor about dowry nor about the expenses
18    of the marriage, nor about utensils or articles, nor about any other matter whatsoever
19    pertaining to their common cohabitation,
20    in sum, small or great, written or unwritten, thought of
21    or not thought of, come to mind or not come [to] mind, entered
22    in this dissolution or not entered, but also that it is permitted
23    to each party to enter into another marriage if they wish, unhindered
24    and unimpeded. And concerning Anastasios, who was born
25    of their common cohabitation, it was decided that he be between
26    them both. And that they will keep all the aforewritten things
27    secure forever, they swore by the holy and consubstantial
28    Trinity and by the victory and permanence of our triumphant
29    lord Flavius Justinian, the eternal Augustus and Emperor, and after the oath they again acknowledged to abide
30    perpetually by this dissolution and not to transgress it in any way,
31    the transgressing party providing to the abiding
32    party, by way of penalty, thirty-six gold nomismatia
33    exacted by execution, with this dissolution thus remaining valid
34    forever, which they set down for each other
35    for security, it being valid and secure. And on all points
36    having been formally questioned they acknowledged, mortgaging to each other for these things all
37    the property now belonging and to belong to them. I, Aurelia Kyra
38    daughter of Menas, the aforewritten, set down this
39    dissolution, and I [sw]ore the divine oath, upon the aforesaid
40    penalty, and I assent to all that is written as set forth.
41    I, Aurelius Christophoros son of Victor from Antinoou, having been requested, wrote on her behalf
42    the documents, she being illiterate. I, Megas son of Psyros, bear witness to this
43    dissolution, having heard from those who made it. I, Aurelius Zacharias
44    son of Menas, assistant from Antinoou, bear witness to this dissolution, having heard
45    from those who made it. I, Aurelius Peter son of Kyros, from Antinoou,
46    bear witness to this dissolution, having heard from those who made it.
47    Through me, Elias the notary, it was completed.
