[TRANSLATION]
1     From Kronion son of Pakebkis of Harpokration, successor to the priesthood, and
2     Maron son of Kronion of [Harpokration],
3     and Maron son of Maron of Marepsheme,
4     and Pakebkis son of Kronion of Psoiphe,
5     of the three priests of the four of the [legitimate] exempt temple of the village of Teptuneis. Concerning the
6     petition submitted to you by Marepsheme son of Marsisouchos of Harpokration,
7     priest of the same temple, requesting the
8     his son [Panesis], of mother [Thenpakebkis] of [Panesios], [to be circumcised], [inquiring] with you whether he is of [priestly]
9     [lineage] and ought to [be circumcised]. We [declare], swearing by the fortune of
10    Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Augustus,
11    [fortune] truly that he is of priestly
12    [lineage] and those presented by him
13    [securities are genuine], and he must [be circumcised] because he is unable to perform the [sacred rites] unless [this happens], or [we should be] liable to the oath. Kronion [of Pakebkis] swore the aforesaid oath [as] it is laid down. [Maron] son of Maron concurred in the oath as
14    [it is laid down.] Maron son of Kronion
15    concurred in the oath [as] it is laid down.
