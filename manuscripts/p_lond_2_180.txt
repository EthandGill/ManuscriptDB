[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the 7th year of the Empe]ror C[aesar] Mar[c]us [Au]reli[us]
2     [Severu]s Alexand[er Pi]ous For[t]unate
3     [Augus]tus, Pachon 9, [from] Aureli[us Melan]os
4     [son of Diosc]orus, and Philadelph[us] son of Polion, [the two]
5     [si]tologoi of the village of Neilopolis:
6     we [have had measured] out to us, of wheat, by the public measure,
7     the smoothed (measure), from the produce of the same year,
8     in the granary of the aforesaid village,
9     on behalf of the public dues of Soknopaios, of wheat, artabas
10    twenty-five, in the name of Melas son of Pousis;
11    total: of wheat, artabas 25; and on the 9th, other of wheat, artaba[s]
12    nineteen and a sixth: total, of wheat, artabas 19 1/6; and on the
13    18th, other of wheat, artabas eleven and a half
14    and a quarter: total, of wheat, artabas 11 3/4; and on Pauni 4, other
15    of wheat, artabas twenty-one: total, of wheat, artabas 21; of the
16    grand total, of wheat, artabas 77. Pauni 21,
17    likewise other of wheat, artabas four and a quarter
18    and a twenty-fourth: total, of wheat, artabas 4 1/4 1/24; Epeiph 10, likewise other
19    of wheat, artabas thirty and two-thirds:
20    total, of wheat, artabas 30 2/3.
21    In the 7th year of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
22    Severus Alexander Pious Fortunate
23    Augus[t]us, Pachon 9, from the Aurelii Melanos
24    son of Dioscorus, and Philadelphus son of Polion, the two
25    sitologoi of the village of Neilopolis: we have had measured out to us, of wheat, by the public smoothed measure, from the produce of the same year, in the granary of the
26    aforesaid village, on behalf of the public dues of Soknopaios
27    Nesos, in the name of Pikiolis the priest,
28    of wheat, artabas nineteen and a sixth, of wheat, artabas 19 1/6,
29    and on Pauni 4, other of wheat, artabas
30    twenty-one, of wheat, artabas 21; making, of the grand total,
31    of wheat, artabas 40 1/6.
