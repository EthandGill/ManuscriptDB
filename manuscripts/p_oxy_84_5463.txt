1     Account of the measurement of the catholicus (finance-official) of the 13th indiction, of it:
2     the measurement is, of dirty wheat by the kankellos-measure, 6 myriads (60,000) and 768 artabas, converting to great-kankellos artabas 5 myriads and 4,462;
3     of which there was sent, for Oxyrhynchos, by the great kankellos, [4 myriads (of) artabas] and 7,375 1/4,
4     [and] there was expended by the great kankellos [artabas] 4,358
5     … by the great kankellos [artabas] 2,133
6     … so that there remain of wheat by the great kankellos [2,757] artabas.
7     Account of the gold-taxes of the same 13th indiction thus:
8     receipts thus:
9     for the conversion into money of various places, 2,189 solidi, 21 carats;
10    for freight, 779 solidi, 21 1/2 carats;
11    total of the money-conversion and of the freights and other items of Alexandria, 2,969 solidi, 18 1/2 carats,
12    of which there was given for expenditures, for freight, as the account has, of Alexandria, 6 hundred(?) solidi, … carats
13    21 [and there was sent to] Alexandria … carats
14    Receipt of the catholicus of the 14th indiction, of dirty wheat by the kankellos, 9 myriads (90,000) and 2,000 artabas,
15    converting to great-kankellos artabas 8 myriads and 2,338,
16    other great-kankellos artabas, with the remainder of the 13th indiction of wheat 2,757, in all great-kankellos artabas 8 myriads and 5,095,
17    of which there was sent, for the glorious House of Oxyrhynchos, of wheat by the great kankellos, 7 myriads and 9,305 artabas,
18    and there was expended, as the account has, of wheat by the great kankellos, 5,785 artabas,
19    total of the sent and given measurement of wheat by the great kankellos, 8 myriads and 5,090 artabas. Complete [is the account].
20    Account of the gold-taxes [of the same 14th indiction] thus:
21    Receipt of the 15th indiction, of dirty wheat by the kankellos, 10 myriads (100,000) and 9,457 artabas 3 choinikes, converting to great-kankellos artabas 9 myriads [and] 8,325,
