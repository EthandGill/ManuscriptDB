[TRANSLATION]
1     Aunes, topogrammateus of the (region) around Bousiris, to the priests from Onneus
2     of Mendes and Ammon and Chonsis and
3     Harpochrates, the greatest gods, greeting. I agree to measure out
4     to you, from the 12th year, yearly, from the account of (my) office (ascholia),
5     the (emmer) dedicated by me to the bakery of the
6     aforesaid temple: thirteen artabas of emmer (olyra), of which, per
7     each month, one artaba, and for the five Epagomenal (days)
8     the remaining one [artaba], so that the aforesaid
9     thirteen artabas may belong to the temple for
10    all time, the indicated benefaction; on which (terms) you shall dis-
11    tribute (it), at each new-moon, to the topogrammateus in Thal....
12    ...kar(?), as a kyllestis-(loaf), for all time.
13    Year 12, Pachon 21.
