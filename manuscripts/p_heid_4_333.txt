[TRANSLATION]
1     (chi-rho symbol)
2     To my most admirable and virtuous sons Marcellinus and Anatolius,
3     from Makarios your father and Maximinus your brother, greeting.
4     Know that I received everything, both the little grains and the beans and your hooded cloak
5     and the tunic from Zosimos's place. And I received the copies
6     of all that I sent to you up to the 11th of [Hathy]r. I received also from Apa Dai of Severinus the wrappings of the consular garments. Know that I sent you through Hor the sailor a small bag and visible things. I sent you also through Isak a box
7     of linens. I received also the letters th[r]ough Sabin[us] and Dionysios the servant of Germanos … .
8     Know that I sen[t] … … … … to Antoninus 40 coins, forty. And I sent [you through P]amoutios the sailor the goods I have.
9     Know that the lady … gave birth … on the fourth (day) to a female, Kyrapanto.
10    Write me quickly about … … his …, because I am very anxious.
11    By Argarizin, … … no longer … Sinderichos is here.
12    And concerning St…ourios, P…ia said that: If the boy comes out
13    from you, do not let him go. But i[f the] boy does [not come] out, take the three letters.
14    But when … a great order … the three letters.
15    Hathyr 30, of the indiction …
16    By the truth, I did not drink with Euphrasios until … .
17    As God knows, I reminded even … Euphrasios, and he swore that, by God, of his
18    I have nothing. So then, with him swearing, what am I to do to him? Send a little gold
19    to the children, because they are very grieved, wearing nothing. For you know how my hands
20    are. And concerning the consular garments—of the ones I know the only truth, according to
21    the mysteries he reveres he swore that, (by) my head, I gave them to you, and I do not know how you grumbled. And concerning the consular garments of the Rhodian … Paulos came with me
22    and stood by at the purchase … that ever he should prosecute you further.
23    But, with God's help, through an acquaintance I send y[ou the] order which you wrote to me.
24    Write me whether the flax garments, the shirts, are good. And when Theoktistos came, the
25    cowl … said … … Anatolius, write me through Petinos.
26    … to the lord Marcellinus, Anatolius
27    from Makarios, peace.
