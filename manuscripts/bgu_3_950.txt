[TRANSLATION]
1     … of house the …
2     … and your mother and … and me, but deign
3     to buy for me a leek-bed one and a small bed one
4     very first-rate and [something] such as at some time …
5     to do this. If therefore now you arrive with god's will in Alexandria,
6     yourself bring them. But if you delay in Arcadia, I beseech
7     at once send them to the house and write to us
8     concerning your health. I pray that you be well and …
9     for many years, most honored lord brother.
