[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord and esteemed b[roth]er Ioannes
2     son of Akindynos, the reverend notary from the (city) of the Apollonopolites, from Flavius Psenoserios son of Anoubion, soldier
3     from the same city, greeting. I acknowledge through this my
4     written security that I owe and am in debt
5     to your reverence, repayable, for my own and
6     necessary use, of gold as principal, nomismatia
7     imperial, plain, approved, in number six
8     less fourteen and three-quarters keratia by your standard, that is of gold 6 nomismatia less 14½ 1/4 keratia
9     and these I am ready, God willing, to provide to your
10    excellence whenever you wish, together with the consolation (interest) of them,
11    that is, each year, for each nomismation
12    five kolobon (measures) of pure and unadulterated wine, by the measure
13    of the holy monastery of Abba Agenes, until the repayment
14    and [com]pletion of the af[ore]wri[t]ten six
15    nomismatia, without any pretext whatsoever
16    and postponement, at my risk and on the security of
17    my substance; and for your security I have made for you
18    this security, and it is in my own handwriting, autograph;
19    I have issued (it) as is aforesaid. Flavius Psensoerios, soldier, the aforewritten,
20    the security of the six nomismata less 14½ 1/4 keratia is satisfactory to me
21    and these I shall repay together with the consolation as is aforesaid.
22    Kyriakos, presbyter and superior of the holy
23    monastery of Abba Agenes, I bear witness to the security, having heard (it) from the one who made it; namely
24    the former pittakion of mine being valid, that of the one nomisma.
25    Likewise Psensoerios, it is satisfactory to me.
26    Written, Phaophi 3, 4th indiction.
27    … of Psensoerios …
