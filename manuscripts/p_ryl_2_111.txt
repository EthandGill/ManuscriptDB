[TRANSLATION]
1     … [in which I dwell] and register myself for the house-by-house census of the past twenty-third year, the 2nd–3rd(?) year, of the deified Aelius Antoninus,
2     [myself] … [and my] full sister on the father's and mother's side, … marion, aged 10, without distinguishing mark, and my present wi[fe] … [together with the] children of both, Tryphaine
3     … Nikephoros surnamed Harpalos
4     … [aged] … [without di]stinguishing mark, and Isiakos also called Amoules, not re[gistered among those subsequently born, aged] … [without] distinguishing mark, and Eudaimonis, aged 9, without distinguishing mark
5     [and] … [registered in the 9th year of the deified Aelius An]toninus in the quarter of Sekennebtynis,
6     [but now making a transfer to the quarter of Di]onysios Topon, and his wife
7     … [registered in the 9th year of the deified Aeliu]s Antoninus in the quarter of Apollonios
8     … [and children of both] … [re]gistered among those subsequently born, aged 8, and Dioskoros
9     … [Ka]lokairos, slave of Heroïs daughter of Harpokration
10    … [registered in] the quarter of Dionysios Topon, and of both
11    … [li]kewise, aged 4, and Heron son of Heron
12    … ran, aged 29, and Tasoucharion, another
13    … [but now] making a transfer to the aforesaid
14    [quarter] … [and] … s, aged 51, without distinguishing mark, registered in the same
15    [quarter, and his wife] … [regi]stered in the house-by-house census of the 9th year of the deified Aelius Antoninus
16    [in the quarter of Dionysios] Topon, and daughters of both
