[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Flavius] Marcellus, tribu[ne appointe]d over the peace,
2     [from Aurel]ia Attiena from [the] city [of the Oxyrhynchit]es. A certain Paulos, originating
3     [from the] same city, behaving recklessly, by force and under compulsion carried [me off and] cohabited with me in marriage, and I bore from him a female child;
4     … having brought (him) into [our] hou[se], … nothing … the ungentlemanly
5     disposition of his, and all my prop erty … … he … leaving me
6     together with my infant daughter at a time … he united with another woman and left me
7     widowed. And after some time he again deceived (me) through elders until again I brought him into our house, he having written to me to continue cohabitation,
8     but if he should wish to do the same ungentlemanly things, that he would pay two ounces of gold
9     and his father undertook for him in writing. And having brought him [into] our house, he undertook to do worse than his former offences, despising my orphanhood,
10    not only in that he wrought desolation against my house, but also, soldiers having been billeted in my house, he plundered them and departed, and I suffered insults and damages until
11    they should grant me my life. Fearing lest I should again undergo danger on his account, I sent him a notice of divorce through a tabularius, through the tabularius of the city, according to the imperial
12    law. Again, behaving recklessly, having his wife at his house,
13    taking along with himself a multitude of disorderly men, he carried me off and shut (me) up
14    at his house for [not] a few days, and when I became pregnant again, leaving me,
15    he united with the same so-called wife of his, and now he announces to me that he is stirring up some malice
16    against me. Wherefore I beseech your firmness of mine to order him to present himself
17    and that he be required, according to his written agreement, (to pay) the two ounces of gold and
18    whatever I was damaged on his account, and that he be punished for what he has dared against me.
19    I, Aurelia Attiena, have submitted (this).
