[TRANSLATION]
1     [M]arcus [Sempronius] Liber[alis], prefect
2     of Egypt, says:
3     I learn that some people have abandoned their own homes on account of the hardship that has occurred, still elsewhere obtaining their livelihood, and [others] having evaded certain compulsory services on account of the weakness that then surrounded them still remain away in foreign lands now out of fear of the immediate proscriptions that were made. Therefore I exhort all to return
4     to their own places; and they shall reap the first and greatest
5     fruit of good fortune and [benefit from] the care of our lord
6     the Emperor's care for all people being brought about, and [let them] not wander as homeless and houseless in foreign lands. And so that they may do this more eagerly and willingly, let them know the resource
7     arising from this cause will still be released by the goodwill and kindness of the greatest Emperor permitting this, and there will be no inquiry against them, but neither
8     against the others who for whatever reason by
9     the strategoi have been proscribed; and these too
10    … to descend into the places … having been … after the …
11    … so that not even … on account of the innate [quality] in
12    … cowardice …
13    …
14    … more …
15    … away …
16    …
17    … those who once did not obey … as acknowledged
18    [criminals] … to be searched out
19    … but from
20    …
21    … but those who willingly have fled and chosen an evil and bandit-ridden life to mingle with. So that not only these [people], but also others
22    may learn that these [things] I counsel and carry out,
23    let them know that to the most excellent epistrategi
24    and to the strategoi and to those sent by me
25    for the safety and peace of the land
26    I have commanded the soldiers to prevent the rising
27    raids by foreseeing and meeting them, and immediately to pursue those that have occurred and the criminals caught in the act to examine nothing further than [what happens] in the very
28    banditry that occurred; but others of those who were once proscribed, who keep quiet and persist in their own farming, not to trouble.
29    Let them descend therefore without anxiety, and let there be
30    a time limit for them, from when this edict of mine is posted in each nome, [of] three months.
31    But if anyone after this great clemency of mine is found wandering in foreign lands,
32    this one no longer as a suspect but as an admitted
33    criminal, when apprehended, will be sent to me.
34    Year 18 of Antoninus, our lord,
35    Thoth 1.
