[TRANSLATION]
1     Gaios to Horion, his most dear, greetings.
2     And at another time I wrote to you through your cameleer
3     that no one has given me [any] of the cedar oil.
4     Therefore if you would lean back [on my request], [that is] good and fine;
5     but if not, instruct your cameleer so that
6     [he] gives me either the price or grain of them.
7     But do not neglect and write to us concerning the
8     equipment of Ammonios, bare [items], and … he gave
9     and the letter of his son.
10    Farewell
11    Phamenoth 28
12    To [Hori]on, cameleer, [to] his cameleer
13    from Gaios
