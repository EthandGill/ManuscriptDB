[TRANSLATION]
1   [To Protarch]os,
2   from Platon son of Theodoros and from Pto-
3   lemaios son of Zenon, a Persian of the epigone,
4   and from this man's wife Selene daughter of Pto-
5   lemaios, a Persian woman, with her guardian, her
6   husband. Concerning the matters at issue, they agree —
7   Ptolemaios and Selene — that they have from
8   Platon a loan, from hand to hand out of the house,
9   of Ptolemaic silver, 112 (drachmas), at interest of two drach-
10   mas on the 112 (drachmas) each month,
11   which they will also repay in six months from Pachon
12   of the present year 17 of Caesar, paying
13   the interest monthly and punctually, and the
14   principal in the last month, without any
15   postponement; or else they are at once
16   liable to seizure and to be held until
17   they pay the loan with half as much again, and the
18   interest at the simple rate, and, for the time
19   elapsed, the equal (interest), the right of execution be-
20   longing to Platon, both from the debtors,
21   who are mutual sureties for the payment, and
22   from one and from whichever of them he chooses, and
23   from all their property,
24   as if by legal judgement, void being whatever
25   pledges of trust they may bring forward,
26   (and) every protection. We request (this).
27   Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 22.
