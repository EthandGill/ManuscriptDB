[TRANSLATION]
1     Sotas to Sotas, the dearest and most honored, many
2     greetings.
3     You will do well, having examined
4     the matters concerning the caretaker
5     Stotoetes, the friend, and
6     [having examined]
7     you will grant him relief, since indeed also
8     he [himself] …
9     never to have rented
10    to him the fields. But examine those of last year and
11    [the year before last] [tax-collectors],
12    and, if you are persuaded [about] this, without disturbance
13    him I will make.
14    I pray you be well,
15    dearest.
