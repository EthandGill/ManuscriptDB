[TRANSLATION]
1     The common master, the most holy bishop Abba Makarios, wrote
2     to my humble self, that I should write to you concerning the grain and barley given to the [one of holy memory]
3     Abba Senouthes, on behalf of the lady among the saints … .
4     For as he wrote, your circumspect brotherhood said to him that, unless
5     I write to you signed letters about this — but [I]
6     wondered at you, knowing that not only in this matter, but [also concerning]
7     any matter whatsoever pertaining to (the church of) Apollo. Nor do I venture to write,
8     unless indeed I shall entreat the common master, the most glorious, the illustrious,
9     to write himself; and as in truth I wished to entreat [his glory]
10    about this, but since I knew that other monasteries … and clearly he arranged everything in the memorandum given to me by you, that I should entreat him
11    also to subscribe, for this reason I did not wish to make a difference, but that I might continue
12    entreating him to write only in the memorandum. For I know that you too do not make
13    a difference. He wrote to me, and to the most glorious common master himself, that your circumspect brotherhood was in doubt
14    about the 1200 artabas of grain, that you do not provide them unless you receive the orders
15    for them; and you did well to seek your security. And I conversed …
16    … and since I cannot but speak the truth to every man, whatever
17    he said to me I wrote to the same most holy bishop. For indeed, as I think, out of
18    shame he found no way to reply to him at all. These things I write, greeting
19    your brotherly circumspection. And this also he said to me, that unless [I come]
20    with my most holy father-in-law to Abba Paulos the [most holy]
21    archimandrite, and he settles the matter and judges who ought to receive the
22    gold, you provide nothing … of them. So, not finding how to persuade him to reply to him,
23    I wrote the things of the truth. Besides, he wrote something else to me about the guest-house
24    in Naboo, that someone wishes to make an innovation in that same guest-house contrary to what [he said] in the time of
25    the holy memory of Abba Senouthes, that I should entreat his glory and
26    that he write to you about this. And I think it is superfluous that his glory
27    should write to you about this; for he does not wish any innovation whatsoever to be made
28    in any matter whatsoever, especially in ecclesiastical affairs. Nor ought you
29    to permit it. And I entreat you to release at once the boat of Victor the
30    son of Kos with the chickling-vetch, and the other boat with the same vetch.
31    [To my master,] circumspect in [all things,] worthy of all honour [and] reverence, my genuine friend and brother Plousammon, count and … sch
32    Kolluthos, with God, chartularius.
