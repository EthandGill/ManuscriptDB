[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios to Kleon, greeting. I wrote to you on the 17th
2     of the settlement made by me with the decarchs
3     before Diotimos concerning the number of the workmen and the delivery
4     of the stones, and in what manner they agreed before Diotimos
5     both to supply in addition the deficient workmen and to fulfil the whole delivery of the stones by the new moon,
6     if iron be furnished to them; and I wrote to you what must be given for
7     each of the rough(-work) and the finished-work. And now too you would do well
8     to order that wedges be assigned for each (of) the undersigned, so that we may remove their excuses: to Techesthes, for the (tasks) for which
9     we have counted out 18 workmen with the boys, 4 wedges;
10    to Berothes, with the boys 15 workmen, 3 wedges; to Achomneus
11    with the boys 18 workmen, 4 wedges; to Paous with the boys
12    16 workmen, 3 wedges; to Pemsais with the boys 17 workmen,
