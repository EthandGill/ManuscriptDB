[TRANSLATION]
1     The kings having [commanded] no one of those over the
2     courts and the [other] for the public service
3     to receive accusations against those subordinate
4     to the administration, nor to divert, nor to permit others to conduct against them; but the accusations brought against them and those against them
5     pending to [send] to Eirenaio the
6     kinsman and [administrator].
7     Year 3, Phamenoth 23.
