[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aelius Publius, the most eminent prefect of Egypt,
2     from Aurelius Isidorus son of Ptolemaios, from the village of Karanis
3     of the Arsinoite nome. A matter requiring your investigation,
4     by means of this my petition, lord prefect, I lay before
5     your excellence, being of good hope to obtain help from you. The matter is thus. As my threshing-floor lay full from the harvest
6     of eleven arouras around the plain of the same village of Karanis,
7     I myself, having gone for the inspection of it, in order to make
8     the discharge of the agricultural works, on the 27th of the month Mesore
9     of the past year, found the aforesaid threshing-floor as
10    set on fire by malefactors and … using the proper procedure, (drew up) documents with witnesses … these
11    [I sub]mitted [to the strategos], requesting that an officer [be sent] in order that,
12    having gone down [to where the] outrage occurred and having submitted (a report), he might make a formal report, as he did report this. And as I made inquiry, I learned
13    [from indications those who] dared this, namely Akotas son of
14    Germanos and Chairemon son of Ptolemaios son of Harba…, from the same
15    village of Karanis, and Heron son of Mouranos, from the village of Ptolemais Nea,
16    inquiring about this from Sotas and Leonides, the public men of the village, who might be those who on that day
17    [appeared at this place, who supplied these very names,]
18    [and concerning this I called other documents to witness] …
