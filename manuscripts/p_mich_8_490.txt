[TRANSLATION]
1   Apollinaris to Taesion, (his) mother,
2   many greetings.
3   Before everything, farewell to me, being in health,
4   (while) I make your obeisance before all
5   the gods. And from Cyrene, having found
6   the one coming to you, I had need
7   to make clear to you about my welfare; and you,
8   more quickly make clear to me about your freedom-from-harm
9   and that of my brothers. And now, from
10   Portus, I make (it) clear to you, for I have not yet gone up to Rome
11   and been assigned. When I am assigned and learn into which (fleet)
12   I go, I will at once make (it) clear to you; and you too, do not hesitate to wri-
13   te about your welfare and that of my broth-
14   ers. And if you do not find the one coming
15   to me, write to Socrates and he
16   forwards (it) to me. I greet warmly the little brothers
17   and Apollinaris and his children and Kalalas
18   and his children and all those who love you.
19   Asklepiades greets you.
20   Farewell to me, being in health.
21   I arrived at Portus on Pachon 25.
22   (2nd hand) Know that I was assigned to Misenum, for later I
23   learned (it).
24   (1st hand) Deliver at Karanis ☓ to Taesion, from Apollinaris
25   ☓ (her) son.
