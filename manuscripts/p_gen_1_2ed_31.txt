[TRANSLATION]
1     To Minicius Corelianus, the most excellent epistrategos
2     from Tete, (daughter) of Tryphon, from those of Hermu, the great city
3     Dioscorus son of Hermaios, husband of my daughter Tieutos, [who] always troubled you during his visits and petitioned you, was referred [by] you before Judge Dioscorus son of Musaeus in the 9th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord
4     the lord, who having examined concerning the disputed matters
5     to us gave a decision, preserving what we had agreed with each other,
6     [the] letters. [When] after the decision with the same arrogance,
7     using [it], Dioscorus, despising my widowhood, unreasonably leased [my] property to farmers
8     and from the [portion] annual rents [he] received in [the] 9th year, of the
9     remaining [he-withheld] through the strategos from the farmers until
10    decision, having been ordered and Dioscorus through the [strategos-officer]
11    sword-bearer to come for his examin[ation was-abandoned], I request you the benefactor, if you [think it good to do what is proper] regarding him and put an end to the [wrong-doings]
12    so that not sti[ll] afterwards I suffer ill-treatment, [that I may be] [benefited]. Fare well.
