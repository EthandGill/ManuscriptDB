[TRANSLATION]
1     Constantinus
2     to John the collector of Ptolemais.
3     provide the things placed [in]
4     into the sacred pool of our lord the holy Alexander
5     and sent in the Arsinoite and owing to be sold through Jeremiah
6     boy … of lentils artabas 132 only;
7     total of lentils artabas one hundred
8     thirty two only
9     of the month Choiak 11 of the twelfth indiction.
10    Choiak 1[1] [of the twelfth indiction] to John from the lord Constantinus lenti[ls] artabas 132.
