[TRANSLATION]
1     Hierokles to Zenon greetings. [I reminded you also previously in the] city on behalf of some Hermogenes of the
2     cleruchs, but I thought [it was necessary to remind you again. You would do well therefore,] when [he] delivers to you
3     the letter, having made [yourself more familiar to him and, if he] should have any need of you,
4     [you will] do for him whatever is [possible for you, for we are firmly recommended] to us by certain friends
5     with whom it happens [to associate frequently, and if the cleruchs] are not stationed in the
6     places around you, having written [carefully] to the [official who is] there, so that through you [they] not
7     be overlooked, but [they] may obtain [all] kindness.
8     [Be well.] Year 35, Gorpiaios 25.
9     Year 35, Tybi 22.
10    Hierokles
11    Of Hermogenes.
12    To Ze[non].
