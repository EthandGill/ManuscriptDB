[TRANSLATION]
1     … on behalf of Olympiane, [daugh]ter of Dionysios, of the rank of former primipilarii, with Kastor the curator, councillor of the illustrious
2     city of the Hermo[p]olites. The one being assisted (i.e. the girl) belongs to both her parents as a ward(?). So, when her aforementioned curator Kast[o]r had been requested [by] a certain [Be]sarion, of the rank of beneficiarii of this same honourable order, upon certain bridal-gifts, since he wished to marry the girl, the c[u]rator, the consent of the one being [assisted] having been given, accepted the marriage upon agreed [bridal-gif]ts, without a written contract; but since the one who wished to marry did not have ready to provide the bridal-gifts, he made a bond to the one now being assisted, together with the curator, and they supposed that, according to the pledge of the bond,
3     the bridal-gifts would [be pr]ovided, wherefore also the marriage was contracted. These things having been so done, and the cohabitation
4     having been arranged according to these terms, together not long after, since the bridal-gifts were about to be demanded both from the curato[r and from] th[e] (woman) who had been married, we do not know in what manner he, wishing (it), did not return them,
5     but withdrew from the household, that in which the marriage had been celebrated, carrying off not his own things only,
6     but also certain things of the (woman) who had been married. Since, then, he has not returned the bridal-gift, but even now still owes it,
7     but still retains in money what he plundered of the girl, and, having left (her) to live by herself, departed so as
8     to send (a petition?) according to the laws, to her, together with the curator, demanding that these things be made available to her: first, that the owed bridal-gift be returned to her in writing, and then also the things which he carried off
9     … him to return to its proper place. For these things having been so done, fittingly also the
10    harmony [of the] marriage will be perfect.
