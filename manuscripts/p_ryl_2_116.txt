[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy from the will of Saprion.
2     To Heraclides, strategos of Hermopolis,
3     From Saprion, also called Hermaios, son of Sarapion, who served as cosmete and gymnasiarch of the great city of Hermes. Me disputing
4     with my mother Eudaimonides, daughter of Eudaimon, elder of Sotas, concerning the things left to me by my father Sarapion in accordance
5     with the will which he made, and being troubled in conscience concerning what she embezzled both in household goods and in deposits and in very many other things, not few being, she came against me with Serenus, also called
6     Tiberinus, a man having been gymnasiarch
7     of the sister, and not only did they outrage me but also tore my clothing around, wishing to deprive me of my property. Therefore
8     I submit this little document, requesting it to be
9     in registration for testimony until
10    of the [approach] against them, account being preserved for me
11    concerning the things I have of rights
12    of all. Year 2 of Imperator Caesar
13    [Lucius] Septimius Severus Pertinax
14    Augustus, the 20th of Pachon. Saprion, also called [Hermaios], submitted.
