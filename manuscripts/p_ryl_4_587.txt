7     Paos son of Onnophris, Arsinoite, to Psillous and P[et]eesis
8     also called Petesouchos, the two sons of Agathinos also called Harmais, Persians
9     of the Epigone, and to their mother Meth… [also called]

11    Psillous, her aforewritten son, of copper [coin]age two talents and two thousand five [hundred] drachmas,
12    at interest of two drachmas per mina each month. This [is]
13    the loan which the borrowers received from Paos in the …
14    …blabamm…n through … the … And this loan [and the]
