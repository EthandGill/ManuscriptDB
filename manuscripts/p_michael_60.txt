1     … [I acknowledge that I have leased] from you for a two-year period [reckoning from produce]
2     [of what with god enters] … [indiction] the existing wholly property of-substance to-you
3     … of the arranged in the eastern plain
4     … Talo with whole ditch and dwelling and tower
5     … and chaff-house and vegetable-garden and
6     … of buildings and other different bearing plants
7     [both and non-bearing] … and all rights within and without according-to-the
8     [ancient boundaries of the property, on condition that] I this have under me for farming and for storage
9     [of whatever produce I may wish to-produce] … beyond fixed rent by-year in full and rain-free, which not-be, wheat pure grain measures five each of-aroura
