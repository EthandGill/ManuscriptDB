1     Measure of the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 31 of Commodus
2     Antoninus Caesar the lord, Pauni 28,
3     for Charax, in the name of Decimus the elder (son of) Heraklas
4     and Panameus (his) brother, of wheat one and a third and a twenty-fourth,
5     total wheat 1⅓ 1/24. I, Pan( ), have signed.
