[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious
2     master, greatest benefactor, Flavius Tiberius Mauricius the
3     eternal Augustus and Emperor, year 5, in the consulship
4     of the same most pious our master, year 4, Mecheir
5     23, in the 5th indiction.
6     To the most eminent successors of him of glorious
7     memory Apion, the late protopatrician,
8     landholders here too in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,
9     through Menas the house-servant who puts the formal question
10    and who provides on behalf of his own masters, the
11    same all-renowned persons, the conduct
12    and obligation: Phoibammon, advocate,
13    son of the blessed Serenus, subscribing below
14    in his own hand, from the same city, greeting.
15    A need having now arisen for the [my]
16    landholding waterwheel called Pit…
17    Eutar on the estate of Pangouleeion the great,
18    of one workman, and at once your Eminence, taking forethought for the maintenance
19    of her own affairs, provided this for me
20    through Zacharias the local machine-maker,
21    a new, serviceable, satisfactory drawing-wheel, which I received
22    for the supply of all the machine-implements
23    on the present day, which is Mecheir 23 of the
24    present fifth indiction, for the water-supply of the crops
25    of the sixth assessment, with God's help; and as proof
26    of the receipt I have made this written acknowledgment,
27    which, being valid, single, having been written, and on being formally questioned I assented.
28    Phoibammon, son of the blessed
29    Serenus: this written acknowledgment is agreeable to me
30    [as] aforesaid. Total: one great workman only.
31    Written acknowledgment of Phoibammon the reverend advocate, son of the blessed
32    Serenus, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites: one great workman.
