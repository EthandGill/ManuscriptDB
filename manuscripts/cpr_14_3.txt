[TRANSLATION]
1     … owed to be given by you to the … [for]
2     the service of all fish …
3     … and what (sort) on your behalf …
4     … of the customary due, the …
5     … of the customary due we owe you whatsoever …
6     … to be given by you. And in addition to these things, that we provide
7     also to your Piety monthly, for the whole aforesaid time, of gold half a solidus less three and a half and a quarter
8     and an eighth carats, of gold 1/2 solidus less carats 3 1/2 1/4 1/8, for your need of the fish…
9     whenever you come to us in our storehouse of the same city, and that it not be permitted to us to withdraw from this agreement
10    before the completion of the time, nor for you to expel us within it. But if we withdraw within the time, that we be liable
11    to pay forthwith by way of penalty six gold solidi,
12    6 solidi, out of all my property. And if you too expel us before the completion of this (time), that you be liable and (must) pay forthwith to us
13    by way of penalty the equal sum of six gold solidi, 6 gold solidi, and on being formally questioned we so acknowledged.
14    We, Aurelii Serenos and Amaios, sons of Apollos, fish-sellers,
15    the aforesaid, the contract is satisfactory to us as
16    stated above. I, Aurelius Herakleios son of Ioannes, wrote on behalf of them, being present,
17    they being illiterate.
18    through me, Elias (in unliterary script)
19    through me, Elias.
20    Agreement made by the Aurelii Amaios son of Apollos
21    and Serenos son of Apollos to the most pious Kyrikos, deacon.
