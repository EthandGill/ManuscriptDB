[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis of produce, 25th year of Commodus
2     Antoninus Caesar the lord, Epeiph 3
3     on behalf of Characos, of the name of Marcus Satrius, of wheat twenty-fourth, [they] become wheat 24. Ammonios [I] have sealed wheat 24.
4     other [entries], Pauni 15, similarly of wheat eighth, [they] become wheat 8.
