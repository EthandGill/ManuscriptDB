[TRANSLATION]
1     [...]...sipp...siou, with as guardian (my) brother,
2     [the same-father] and same-mother brother Apollon[ios]
3     [... (son) of Orsen]ouphis (son) of Satabous, a Persian
4     [of the epigone.] I wish to lease from you
5     for four years from the present fourteen-
6     th [year] the (arouras) belonging to you around the vill[age]
7     of H[ephaistias(?)], of katoikic holding, eight arouras
8     [or however many there may be] in two parcels, of rent of the
9     [whole per yea]r I will take up jointly from you
10    [of seed] per aroura one artaba of wheat,
11    of the whole, thirty-four artabas of wheat,
12    by the six-choinix measure of the granary of sa.mou(?),
13    [free of deduction] and free of risk; and I will perform the
14    ...n ka...on, all the agricultural works,
15    the dyke-works and waterings and weed-
16    ings and siphon-channelings, all from (my) own (resources),
17    and at the [proper] season, doing no harm,
18    [sowing the arouras] year by year — for the first
19    [three] years with whatever (crops) I choose, but in the last year
20    [...]; and the rents year by year I will pay over
21    [to you] in the month Pa[u]ni at the threshing-floor of the lands;
22    and after the period I will hand back the arouras
23    [...] as (they were given) to me by you
24    [...] on the aforesaid (terms);
25    [the] public (dues) of all (being)... apart from the public
26    [dues ...] and being against me.
27    [If it seems good, (I ask) to lea]se on these (terms).
28    ...nou, about 32 years (old), scar on the left hand.
29    [Year 14 of Imperat]or Caesar Nerva
30    [Trajan] Augustus Germanicus Dacicus.
31    d...athou(?)
