[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eighth year of Imperator Caesar Trajan
2     Hadrian[us] Augustus, in the month Gor[piaios, on the twelfth],
3     Epeiph 12, in Bakchias of the division of Herakleides
4     of the Arsinoite nome. Haryotes agrees,
5     son of Alexas, aged about 63, with a scar on his left foot, to Horos son of Horos,
6     aged about 50, with a scar on his left eyebrow, that the one agreeing has received from him
7     the rent and the seed-corn
8     of the present eighth year of Hadrianus Caesar the
9     lord, of the holding which he (Horos) cultivates around Bakchias,
10    of four and a half arouras, twenty artabas of wheat
11    by the six-choinix measure, because the time of the lease
12    has been fulfilled and the one agreeing
13    will not proceed, nor those acting for him, against Horos
14    nor against those acting for him, neither concerning these things, as is
15    set out above, nor concerning any other matter whatsoever
16    written or unwritten, of any transaction
17    up to the present day, on any pretext whatever.
18    Sub[scribers]: for the one agreeing, Heleiodoros son of Heleiodoros, aged about 50, with a scar on his right cheek,
19    and for the other, Souchas son of Sochotes, aged about 44, with a scar on his right forearm.
20    I, Haryo[t]es son of Alexas, agree that I have received from
21    [Horos] the rent and the seed-corn of the presen[t eighth ye]ar, which he cultivates of mine around Bakchia[s], of fo[ur a]nd a half arou[ras], twen[ty] artabas of wheat,
22    bec[ause the t]ime of the lease has been fulfilled
23    [and I shall not] pro[cee]d concerning anything, as is set out above.
24    I, [Heliod]oros son of Hel[iodor]os, wrote on behalf of Haryotes
25    … not k[now]ing letters. I, Horos son of Horos, the receipt has co[me to] me as is set out above. Souchas
26    [son of Sochot]os, wro[te] on his behalf, he not knowing le[t]ters.
27    Registered through the record-office in Bakchias.
