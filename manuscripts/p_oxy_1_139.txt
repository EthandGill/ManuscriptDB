[TRANSLATION]
1   [† In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus]
2   [Christ our God and Savior,]
3   [(in the reign of) our greatest lord and benefactor Fl(avius) Heraclius,]
4   [the eternal Augustus] and Imperator,
5   ye[ar 3], Ph[ao]phi 29, indiction 1.
6   To Fl(avius) Apion, the all-praiseworthy and most magnificent,
7   ex-consul and patrician, landholder also
8   here in the illustrious city of the Oxyr(hynchites), through Menas
9   the servant, who puts the question and secures
10   for his own master, the same all-praiseworthy
11   man, the right of action and obligation —
12   Aurelius Menas, chief-guard, son
13   of Hor, his mother being Herais, from the village
14   of Adaios in the Oxyr(hynchite) nome, governed as a pagarchy
15   by your magnificence. I acknowledge
16   to your magnificence, through those belonging to it,
17   that if ever, at any season
18   or time, we be found to have stolen
19   machine-parts or oxen,
20   or to have committed any theft whatever,
21   or to have harbored
22   brigands — on condition that I furnish
23   to your magnificence, for
24   each offense, of gold
25   twenty-four solidi, in deed and
26   in effect exacted, at my own risk and
27   (that) of my property. Valid is
28   the acknowledgment, written in single copy, and on question I acknowledged it. †
29   I, Menas son of Hor — it is agreeable to me,
30   this acknowledgment, as aforesaid. I, Joh[n],
31   wrote on his behalf, (he) being illiterate.
32   (Bond) of Menas chief-guard, son of Hor, from the village of Adaios of the Oxyrhynch(ite)
33   nome. †
