[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious
2     lord Flavius Justinian
3     the eternal Augustus and Emperor, year 27, in the 13th (year) after the
4     consulship of Flavius Basilius the most illustrious,
5     Pauni 14, of the 2nd indiction.
6     To Flavius Anastasius the
7     respectable comes and
8     banker of the glorious house, from
9     the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,
10    Aurelii George and Anoup,
11    full brothers, from the father
12    Andreas, the mother being Stephanous,
13    machine-makers of the same glorious house,
14    from the same city, greeting.
15    We acknowledge that we have received from
16    your Magnificence, at our own
17    request, in loan through hand
18    from her house, for our own and
19    necessary needs, of gold solidi
20    four, current by private standard, making 4 gold solidi
21    current, and we acknowledge that we shall repay these to your Magnificence with all
22    thanks at the first payment of
23    our wages from the glorious house, in the month Hathyr
24    of the coming third indiction,
25    without delay, at the risk of our belongings
26    pledged to this. The bond is valid, written
27    singly, and having been asked the formal question we gave our consent. We, Aurelii George and
28    Anoup sons of Andreas, have made the bond
29    of the 4 solidi as aforesaid. I, Pamouthios son of George,
30    having been requested, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
31    Bond of George and Anoup, brothers, from the father Andreas, machine-makers of the glorious house,
32    from the city of the Oxyrhynchites, of 4 gold solidi current by private standard.
