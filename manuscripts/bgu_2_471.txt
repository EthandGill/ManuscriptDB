[TRANSLATION]
1     From the weavers, through Akeious, son of Psenobastis, and the rest, 20 drachmas;
2     additional charges, 1 drachma 1½ obol 3 chalkoi; total, 21 drachmas 1½ obol 3 chalkoi.
3     From the public farmers, through the archephodoi of the village,
4     20 drachmas, additional charges 1 drachma 1½ obol 3 chalkoi; total, 21 drachmas 1½ obol 3 chalkoi.
5     Total, 220 drachmas; additional charges, 14 drachmas 2 obols; total, 234 drachmas 2 obols.
6     For the priests' tax: from the priests of the god Phemnoeris of the village,
7     demanded through Pet…s, son of Akousilaos, and the rest, 216 drachmas; additional charges,
8     additional charges 30 drachmas 1½ obol; total, 246 drachmas 1½ obol.
9     And from the fines of the 27th year, under Kephalas, collector of the same,
10    40 drachmas, additional charges and receipt-fees 5 drachmas 3½ obols 2 chalkoi; total, 45 drachmas 3½ obols 2 chalkoi.
11    Total, 256 drachmas; additional charges 35 drachmas 5 obols 2 chalkoi; total, 291 drachmas 5 obols 2 chalkoi.
12    Of the priests of Demeter, through Tryphon, son of Tryphon, 32 drachmas;
13    additional charges 2 drachmas ½ obol; total, 34 drachmas ½ obol; collector's fee 16 drachmas, additional charges 1 drachma
14    2 chalkoi; total, 17 drachmas 2 chalkoi.
15    Total, 48 drachmas; additional charges 3 drachmas 1½ obol 2 chalkoi; total, 51 drachmas ½ obol 2 chalkoi.
16    Of the gypsum-tax: through Erieus, son of Orses, his mother being Tageleus, 8 drachmas; additional charges 1 obol 1 chalkous.
17    Total, 8 drachmas 1 obol 1 chalkous.
18    Collector's fee, through the same, 4 drachmas; additional charges ½ obol 1 chalkous; total, 4 drachmas ½ obol 1 chalkous.
19    Total, 12 drachmas; additional charges 4½ obols 2 chalkoi; total, 12 drachmas 4½ obols 2 chalkoi.
20    Grand total from the dues demanded of Hexapotamos, 1623 drachmas; additional charges 124 drachmas 5½ obols 2 chalkoi.
21    Total, 1747 drachmas 5½ obols 2 chalkoi.
