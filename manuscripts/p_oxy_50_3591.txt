[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius leased,
2     Dioscurides also called Castor, former gymnasiarch,
3     councillor of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, to Aurelius Sarapion son of Athenodoros
4     son of Sarapion, from the
5     same city, for
6     four years from the present 3rd year, the (lands) belonging
7     to him and to his partners
8     around Mermertha, from
9     (the allotment) of Metokos and Metokos, ten arouras,
10    so as each year to sow and
11    plant with green wheat-crops,
12    on (terms of) the half of the produce and a fixed rent each year of wheat to the amount of twenty-seven artabas and of two hundred and eight drachmas, all free of every
13    risk, the yearly public dues of the land being
14    the concern of the landlords, who
15    shall also be owners of the crops
16    [un]til they recover the (sums) owed each year. And if, in
17    the following years—may it not be—it become unflooded, allowance shall be made to the lessee. And the lease being confirmed, let the lessee repay the
18    rent in the month Pauni, and the
19    wheat let him measure out by compulsion
20    into the public granary at his
21    own expense, and let him give over a deposit thereof, clean from all (impurities), to the lessor under the
22    first measuring of each year without delay. Valid is the lease. Year 3 of the Emperor
23    Cae[s]ar Marcus Aurelius
24    Antoninus Pius
25    Felix Augustus, Thoth 20.
26    … I, Aurelius Dioscurides, former gymnasiarch and however I am styled, have leased as is aforesaid.
