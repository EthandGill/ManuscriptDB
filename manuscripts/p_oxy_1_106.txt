[TRANSLATION]
1   To the agoranomoi of Oxyryn-
2   chos, (from) Apollon-
3   ios son of Ptolemaios, servant.
4   I have reported to you
5   that the former strategos of the nome, Deme-
6   trios, has given orders
7   to hand over to Ptolema
8   daughter of Straton, mother being Diony-
9   sia, from the city of Oxyrhynchos,
10   the will which she deposited through you
11   in the 9th year of the deified Trajan,
12   (in the month) Mecheir, under seals,
13   the will, this being what she re-
14   quested herself, which (will) also through
15   me she took back. In the year
16   the nineteenth
17   of the Emperor Caesar
18   Trajan Hadrian
19   Augustus, Pharmouthi 25.
20   (hand 2) I, Ptolema daughter of Straton, have taken back my
21   aforementioned will under the
22   same seals. I, Pedon son of Calli-
23   stratos, have been registered as her guardian
24   and have written on her behalf, since she does not know letters.
25   The date is the same.
