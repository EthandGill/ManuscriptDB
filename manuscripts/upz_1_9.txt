[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy and Queen Cleopatra the sister
2     O gods Philometors, greetings. Ptolemaeus of Glaucias, Macedonian,
3     of those in occupation in the great Sarapion at Memphis
4     Year this one 12. Of the 18th year in Thoth having completed his life my father
5     whose fatherland was in Psich of the Heracleopolis, leaving
6     me indeed being in the aforementioned temple, but my other siblings three
7     in the aforementioned village Hippalon Sarapion Apollo,
8     these being shaken by the in the village rulers,
9     as befalls to me, I beseech you with supplication, O gods
10    Savior Benefactors, having looked upon both me, that I am not able having gone out
11    from the temple to seize them, and upon their orphanhood
12    to order to write to Cudias the strategus to take care as so that for the future
13    no one either shakes nor disturbs them from the things concerning the operations because those furnishing to me the breads me to maintain.
14    To you indeed may it be to hold all whatever you might wish of land and to grow old
15    with your children the both the land of you to be in which you choose
16    [a] dispositio[n]. Fare well.
