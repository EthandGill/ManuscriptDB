[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the] masters of land and [s]ea and of every [n]ation and race of men, the Flavii, Valens and
2     Gratian[us a]nd Valentinian[us], the eternal Augusti, a plea and supplication
3     from Flavius [I]sidoros, officialis of the staff of the governorship of your Thebaid. At the first
4     indiction, [most divin]e emperors, I was entrusted by Ammonas, of the assessors of our staff,
5     two hundred and thirty-eight gold solidi, so as to convey and hand them over to a certain Dioskourides, a Hermopolite,
6     being at the divine comitatus; and having arrived before the aforesaid (man), some I have paid to him,
7     sixty-one gold solidi, having received from him a receipt; but there were left with me other solidi,
8     one hundred and seven[ty]-seven, the need not being pressing. And it came to pass, through some wicked demon, that a theft occurred of this [le]ft-over gold of mine, as many Thebans who were present at that time have also testified for me,
9     Z[enag]enes the defensor and those who served as envoys on behalf of the province, Makarios and Philapollon,
10    and other trustw[orth]y men. And having fallen into this misfortune, I returned to the staff. But the city-councillors
11    of Hermo[u]p[olis, t]hose who had given [the g]old to Ammonas, came before the most illustrious Pergamios, the [g]overnor of the [province], cl[aim]ing that I was withholding the gold; and an examination having been held, and the
12    witnesses [having deposed] in the records, I was ordered to provide them a certain stated sum of gold
13    of the remainder [reckon]ed to them[selves] toward the completion of the account in the investigative records.
14    And having sold off all [my own goods], and almost, so to speak, having come to my very dearest ones,
15    very barely was I [abl]e to pay it in. Because of this I beg your heavenly fortune to assent
16    that there remain [f]irm and unshaken the records done about this case from the opposing parties,
17    the laws so commanding that there must not be disturbed the things once decided by the magistrate
18    from the opposing parties, the magistrate of the province assisting. And having obtained this,
19    I shall acknowledge ever-memorable thanks to your most illustrious piety through all (time).
20    Copy of the receipt which Dioskourides gave
21    to Ammonas, of the assessors, when
22    the matter of the gold was demanded.
23    To Flavius Eutychios, the most illustrious go[v]ernor,
24    from Aurelius Dioskourides son of Silvanus, councillor
25    of Hermopolis the most illustrious, receiver
26    of the gold of the recruits. I have received from Isidoros
27    the officialis, in Hierapolis, on the account of the clothing of the recruits
28    of the 11th and 14th indictions, sixty-one gold solidi, that is 61 gold solidi; and so that nothing
29    escape your magnificence, I report (it), governor
30    lord.
31    In the consulship of our masters Valentinianus
32    and Valens, the eternal Augusti, for the 4th time.
33    Authentic plea to the divine (emperors) concerning the house.
