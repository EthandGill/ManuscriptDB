[TRANSLATION]
1     Just as I found you yesterday in the four-columned [structure] and asked you about the monachre
2     and you said that it is in Hermopolis with my mother. Behold then, I sent
3     my boy with the five carats in advance-payment; be willing then
4     to give to him the same monachre and allow me the female phakion
5     until I go up to you
6     Give [it] to Priscus the clothes-merchant from Phoibammon the singularius
