[TRANSLATION]
1     Caulkers near you, write in order that I may send to you the cypress-[wood] …
2     So that you [may] care-beautifully-for this; but if not, arrange for the thorny-[things] to be bought for me …
3     And [for it] to be sawed and sent to me through them. Behold I have caulkers in my house …
4     And in truth I wish [it] to be cared-beautifully-for in my house. Behold for I entrusted him to give
5     The prices of them. But by the Lord do not allow him to be hindered in order that I may give-thanks to you,
6     And I greet your most-magnificent [ones] through the letter.
