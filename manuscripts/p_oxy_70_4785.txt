[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of the Flavii [Orestes] and L[ampa]dius, the most illustrious,
2     [ Phaoph]i 22, in the 9th indiction.
3     To Flao[u]ius Strategios, the most glorious and most eminent
4     general, most renowned patrician, holding the first rank
5     both in the Heracleopolite (nome) and in this
6     splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas his servant, who is
7     interrogating and securing for his own master,
8     the same all-praiseworthy man, the action and
9     liability, we, the Aureli[i]: Pamouthios son of Sitta, his mother being Aria,
10    and Menas son of Isaac, his mother being Tabes, originating from
11    the village of Tampeti of the Oxyrhynchite nome, being under a pagarch
12    also (subject) to your eminence, greeting. A need having now also
13    arisen for the estate-machine under us, called
14    Sitta, which draws water for a vineyard and for arable land, of one
15    axle, having gone up to the city we requested
16    your eminence to order this to be furnished to us.
17    And immediately your magnificence, taking thought for the
18    maintenance of her own property, that same axle
19    furnished to us from the new (ones) brought from the
20    grea[t] city of Alexandria, in place of the worn-out one,
21    which new one, serviceable, suitable for drawing water, satisfactory,
22    we received for the compl[e]tion of all the mechanical
23    apparatus on the pre[sent d]ay, which is Phaophi the sec[on]d, the twentieth,
24    of the present yea[r] [207, 176] of the pres[en]t ninth [i]ndiction,
25    for water-supply [of the crops of the tenth indiction. And in]
26    proof of the re[ceipt, we have made this handwritten document,]
27    which, being binding, wri[tten singly, and on being questioned we acknowledged. We, the Aurelii,]
28    Pa[m]ou[thios son of Sitta and Menas son of Isaac, the] [aforew]ritten, [have made this handwritten document.]
29    Handwritten document of Pamouthios son of Sitta and of Me[nas] son of [Isaac, from the village of Tampeti.]
