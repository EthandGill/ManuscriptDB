[TRANSLATION]
1     … [the] city [of Her]mont, asking afterward [for a] subscriber
2     [to] subscribe for me to this document of sale,
3     not transgressing it and not shaking it
4     from the laws, the more to confirm it [through] trustworthy [witnesses], these who shall witness to it
5     [for] me according to my request of my own kind, and my commission. I write, I, Abessa daughter of the blessed Zacharia, of this same fortress of Djeme of the nome of this same city
6     of Her[mont], …, that: since in this season …
7     of the affair now of this sixth year I bought the house of your blessed
8     father Zacharia, with the courtyard, these which are below the house of the children
9     of Konstantinos, and the workshop of Antonios son
10    of Paulos, having given twelve gold solidi
11    of refined gold for them, having approached me now
12    before the most glorious lord, beloved-of-the-amir, the person
13    of our lord the most renowned amir, concerning the dividing of the house
14    of the vaulted-chamber to the north and the dividing of the courtyard, after the partition by pleading which we made with
15    one another, the most glorious lord, the amir, commanding,
16    so that I give the share of the house to you, Abessa, of the north
17    and the share of the courtyard that is in front of it. Now
18    I acknowledge that, behold, seven solidi less
19    one-quarter of a trimesion came to my hand from you, Abessa,
20    for the share of the house of the north and the share of the courtyard,
21    according to the way that you gave them to me through hand into hand. From
22    now then you, Abessa, daughter of Zacharia,
23    whom I have first written, may come in and take hold
24    and be lord, and become the master
25    of the share of the house of the vaulted-chamber of the north,
26    from its foundations to its walls, with the other appurtenances,
27    all of them that are reckoned to it, and the share
28    of the courtyard, because their price came to my hand
29    from you, according to the way that I first wrote above,
30    according to the disposition that I first declared
31    above, to acquire it, again to acquire it, to administer
32    it, to manage it, to leave it to your sons and your daughters, to give it to your heirs,
33    to use it in any fashion that pleases you, that at no season
34    shall anyone come forth against you ever, neither I nor my children
35    nor my daughters nor my heirs, to bring suit against you
36    in a court or outside a court, or to set up any approach against you beside any
37    authority concerning this sale, this one. First, he shall be a stranger
38    to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, and he shall give
39    to the account a penalty of ten gold solidi to the authority,
40    and afterward he shall come in and oppose this sale; for the securing
41    then I have established it, secured, having force in every place
42    where it shall be produced.
43    I, Johannes, the least presbyter, am witness.
44    I, Isak son of Konstantinos,
45    am witness.
46    I, Phibamon the least presbyter, am witness.
47    Through me, Aristophanes son of Johannes, it was made.
