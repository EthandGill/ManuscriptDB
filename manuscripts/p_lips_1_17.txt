[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consul[s]hip of our master Gratianus
2     th[e ete]rnal Augus[t]us for the 4th time, an[d] of Flavius Merobaudes
3     the most illustrious.
4     To Fl[avi]us Isidoros, officialis of the staff of the governorship
5     of the Thebaid,
6     from [A]urelius Phibaleo[n] son of Neilos, his mother being Hermione,
7     from Hermou polis the most illustrious,
8     registered in the quarter of the city
9     of the east. I wish willingly and of my own free choice to lease from you for a two-year
10    period, reckoned from the next month
11    Ph[aoph]i of the present sixth indiction, from the whole house belonging to you, two-storied and with a basement, in
12    the upper storey one room, in
13    the s[a]me Her[m]ou polis, in the same quarter
14    of the city of the east, for dwelling and use,
15    at a rent each year for this room alone of two thousand five hundred talents of silver,
16    which rent I shall pay to you each year
17    at the close of the year's time; and at
18    the en[d] of the time of the lease I shall hand over
19    [t]o you [the sam]e room together with the door fitted
20    [to it], as I received it, having damaged it
21    in nothing except by use. The le[ase is val]id, and, having been questioned, I have agreed. Thoth 13.
22    [Aurelius Ph]ibaleon son of Neilos, the aforesaid,
23    have leased as is aforesaid. Flavius Hermap[oll]on son of Apolinarios, of the officials of the procuratorial office,
24    having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he being present and not knowing letters.
25    Through [m]e, Philosarapis, it was written.
