r.1    Μῦς Ζήνωνι χαίρειν. εἰ ἔρρωσαι κ[αὶ τὰ ἄλλα] ἐστὶν κατὰ λόγον, εἴη ἂν ὡς ἡμεῖς

[TRANSLATION]
1     Mys to Zenon greetings. If you are well and [the other things] are in accordance with reason, it would be as we
2     wish; we are healthy ourselves too, and remember you constantly,
3     and we are concerned hearing that you are straining yourself. Would you do me a favor
4     then and taking care at the same time of yourself so that you are healthy
5     and writing to us how you are, so that knowing we may not be anxious.
6     Farewell. Year 29, Phamenoth 17.
7     To Zenon.
