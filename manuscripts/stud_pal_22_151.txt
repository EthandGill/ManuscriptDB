r.2    τῆς καὶ Θεογένους ἑκατοστῆς καὶ πεντηκοστῆς Σωτᾶς ἐξάγων ἐπὶ καμήλῳ ἑνὶ ὀρόβου

[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been taxed through the gate of Philopator
2     and Theodorus and the hundredth and fiftieth [customs-point] of Sotas, exporting on one camel [of] bitter vetch
3     artabas six, (they) amount to artabas 6
4     of year 8, Choiak, on the seventeenth 17, without a mark
