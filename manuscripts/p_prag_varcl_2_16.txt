[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidorus to Heroninus
2     to [the] dearest, greetings.
3     wished my lord
4     Alypis [with] the camels
5     going up to fill
6     them [with] firewood.
7     Prepare therefore them,
8     so that the [ones] going up, ready [things] having become,
9     they-might-fill them.
10    [to be] well you I-pray, dearest.
