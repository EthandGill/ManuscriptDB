1     Year 28, Tybi 11: there has measured into the
2     (granary) in Diospolis Magna, the granary, year 28:
3     Simon (son of) Izaros, who has farmed (the tax), of wheat
4     ninety, total wheat 90. Bryon.
5     (2nd hand) Apollonios: I have received by measure, of wheat ninety, total wheat 90.
6     (3rd hand) Hermokrates: of wheat ninety, total wheat 90.
