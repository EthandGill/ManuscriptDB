[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 22, Mecheir 4. [It] has been registered through the office in Ptolemais Euergetes, [a] 1/5 share of [a] part from 1/2 [and] 1/10 of embalmed dead-bodies
2     of those near Auheris and Ptolemais Hormos of the outer regions of the Heracleides division, and [of] 1/2 of [a] house and [a] courtyard in th[e] same Auheris,
3     which Harmais [makes to] Psyllos. [The] three order: [oth]ers Harmais and Petesouchos. Through [a] scribe-teacher Marreios
4     of Akeios. Year 22, Mecheir 4. [It] has been registered in the library.
