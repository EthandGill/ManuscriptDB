[TRANSLATION]
1     Valerius to Julius, greetings.
2     I find fault with you because concerning which things
3     I ordered you, you did not write back to me about Antonius.
4     And I also wrote to Balathon so that he might cooperate with you. If the payment is displayed
5     you (both) act and guard until I
6     am present. If you have sold the barley, but if not
7     you will give it to Albanus so that he may bring it to me; here
8     [for] sixteen drachmas is the artaba. If you are
9     having sold the barley you will send
10    to me the copper through Albanus the
11    horseman. Greet Antonius and find fault with him about the leather and say to him I pray that never
12    may he have need of me any longer.
13    Farewell.
