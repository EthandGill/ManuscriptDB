1     of the reign of our most divine lord Flavius
2     Tiberius New Constantine of eternal Augustus, emperor, year first, Thoth 13, [of the] indiction.
3     To Aurelia Aphthonia Hypsistou, the most noble
4     from [the city of] the Hermopolitans, from Aurelius Proupochios
5     Isaakios, mother Hera, farmer, from the same city.
6     I acknowledge willingly and voluntarily to have rented from
7     you for a three-year period, reckoned from the produce
8     of the - with God - fourteenth indiction, and [for]
9     the fields belonging to you, two [arouras] of sown land
10    waterless, being in the east of the same city
11    from [the] south of the embankment, from the east of the [area called] Arete
12    under the guardianship of [those] from [the] village [En]seus of the
13    Hermopolitan, for sowing and depositing of produce which if [I] choose, [as] rent
14    of these two fields, per year, of wheat, [in] artabae
15    twelve, which rent [must be] new, pure, sifted
16    measured by the Athenian measure, I shall deliver in Epiphi
17    [the] month, per year, and I shall restore the same rent
18    into your house in Hermoupolis, [at] my own [expense with] beasts and
19    expenses, and I shall provide per year, as is customary, [with] stalks
20    eight bundles, and of wheat chaff one sack
21    and of milk one pot, and of beans a bundle, and
22    if you do not wish [me] not to be able to withdraw from the farming of the same
23    [fields before] the completion of the same three-year [period]
24    [time] … the rent of these [fields]
25    … from
26    … [the] freedom/permission of the farmers
27    … to stand/remain
