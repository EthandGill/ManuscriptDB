[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemaios son of Asklepiades, a Persian of the epigone,
2     … is called, leased to Jonathas
3     son of Jonathes and to Apollonios son of Philippos, the two Jews of the epigone, the vineyard belonging to him
4     around Teptynis for one year from
5     the month Artemisios of the 16th year, at a fixed rent
6     of eighty-five measures of Arsinoite
7     wine. And all the dues to the royal treasury
8     let Ptolemaios pay. And let Jonathas and Apollonios deliver to Ptolemaios
9     from the vineyard the eighty-[five] measures of the wine in the Egyptian month Mesore,
10    he providing him a jar. Let Jonathas and Apollonios work the vineyard with this year's labors. And Ptolemaios has
11    from Jonathes and Apollonios four hundred
12    drachmas of copper, which let him repay at the vat
13    of the same year. Keeper of the contract: Philopator son of Teros, a Jew of the epigone.
14    Ptolemaios was about 34 years old, very tall …
15    … a scar by the right eyebrow.
16    [Jonathas, about … years old,] of middle (height), honey-colored,
17    [Apollonios] about 38 years old, of middle (height), honey-colored …
18    [a scar] …
19    [Philo]pator, about 53 years old, … honey-colored, [long]-faced,
20    [a scar] … bald in front.
21    Written in the 16th year, (month) Artemisios.
