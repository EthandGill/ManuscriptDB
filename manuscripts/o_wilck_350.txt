1   Year 44, Mesore.
2   There has been paid into the bank at Hermonthis, at which Hera(kleides) (presides):
3   of the land-surtax (eparourion) of year 44, Haren-

5   of copper, drachmas one thousand five hundred
6   — total 1500 dr. Hera(kleides) banker.
