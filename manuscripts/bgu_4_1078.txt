[TRANSLATION]
1   Sarapion to Sarapias his sister, very many greetings
2   and continual good health. Know that I have sold
3   at the right moment; but you did not do well —
4   though many friends were setting out — to send
5   me not one word, though you knew that I am anxious
6   about you. Well then, if I get the bits of money
7   I shall see what I must do; for I must not
8   sit idle. And I could wish you had given me word about the works,
9   whether they have gone cheap or not.
10   And know that Hegemon came in
11   on the twenty-third. For the rest, take care of
12   yourselves, that you stay well. Greet the children
13   and all those in the house, and Panechotes.
14   Farewell.
15   Year 3 of Gaius Caesar Augustus Germanicus,
16   month Soter 23.
17   Deliver — from Sarapion the merchant
18   to Sarapion his younger son, at the house.
