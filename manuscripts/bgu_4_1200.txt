[TRANSLATION]
1   To Publius Octavius,
2   from Horos son of Teos and the [other p]riests [of (the gods) ... and]
3   Apollo, the greatest gods, [from] the v[illage of Busiris]
4   of the Herakleopolite (nome) above Memphis. [Near the village ... we happened to]
5   hold lands consecrated [(to the temple) and a revenue, by succession from our]
6   parents, of [one hundred] artabas of wheat. [But when the lands were taken over]
7   into the public domain, ... [an allowance(?)]
8   of one hundred artabas of wheat, and from the [revenue ...]
9   one hundred artabas of wheat, so that the [total was two hundred per year,]
10   from the [11]th [year] of Caesar until the 1[9th year ...; and for the]
11   [due] (sacrifices) on behalf of the god and lord, Imperator Ca[esar],
12   the sacrifices and libations, and for the [public services] performed by [us]
13   each year, two hundred and eighty silver drachmas. [But from the 19th year,]
14   Asklepiades, who had charge of the revenue, having gone abroad to Italy, the
15   managers and grain-officers Herakleides and Dionysios, [both being]
16   sons of Asklepiades' brother, and being from the [village of Line, having been bribed]
17   with no small sum by the men of their own village, [deprived]
18   us of the hundred artabas of wheat which we have from the revenue, [... until]
19   the 19th year of Caesar. And the aforesaid Herakleides and Diony[sios ...],
20   having authority over Asklepiades' affairs, have unlawfully given (the wheat) to th[ose of Line],
21   though they had not yet received ... from the revenue ... ours, toward completion of the 175 (artabas) - the 100 (artabas) of wheat (that are) ours; and up to now they have obstructed (us), [being pow-]
22   erfully backed by them. But now, having examined the public (records) [and found]
23   the aforesaid wheat being assigned out to our temple,
24   one hund[red] artabas, [and]
25   (those) of the men of Line, seventy-five artabas of wheat - we have ta[ken refuge]
26   with you, and we ask you, the savior of [all]
27   and benefactor, to order [that they be summoned]
28   before you, and to decide concerning the tru[th - that] they deprived [us ...]
29   up to the 28th year, of nine hund[red] artabas of wheat, [and] for this 29th year of [Cae]sar, [100] ar[tabas] of wheat;
30   [and] to restore to us the revenue, [the hundred] artabas of wheat [due each] year,
31   [so that we may] be benefited [by] you.
