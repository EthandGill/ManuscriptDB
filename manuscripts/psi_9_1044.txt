[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fourth year of the Emperor Caesar
2     Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus
3     Augustus Pius. [He] has worked for embankment
4     Works of the same 4th year for seven days, in accordance
5     With [the things] ordered, Pachon 15 through 21
6     To the lower embankment of the grove of the Arabs, of Soknopaiou island
7     Pakysis son of Pakysio[s] of P… mother Thasaitos.
8     Amois, farmer, [I] have marked.
