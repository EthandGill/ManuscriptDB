[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 15, which is also year 12, Hathyr 24, in Pathyris,
2     under Hermias, deputy of Paniskos the agoranomos.
3     There lent Petearsemtheus son of Nechoutes,
4     a Persian, to Petearsemtheus and Petesouchos, the (sons)
5     of Panobchounis son of Totoes, the two
6     Persians of the epigone, of bronze coinage five thousand one hundred drachmas
7     interest-free. And let this loan be repaid
8     by the borrowers to Petearsemtheus in the month
9     Pharmouthi of the 15th, which is also the 12th, year. But if they do not
10    repay within the appointed time
11    or do not act in accordance with what is written, let them forfeit in the following month forthwith the [drachmas] of bronze, 5100, plus one half, and of the
12    time exceeded interest, 2 drachmas
13    per mina each month. And this
14    is the loan which Horos
15    son of Paous guaranteed against Pakoibis son of Petearsemtheus
16    from one talent of bronze, 5200 drachmas. Sureties for one another for
17    the payment of all the (sums) under this loan are the borrowers themselves. And let the right of execution belong to Petearsemtheus, against the borrowers
18    and against each one and either of them, and against whichever (of them)
19    he may choose, and against all the property
20    belonging to them, exacting payment just as
21    by judgment.
22    I, Hermias, deputy of Paniskos, have transacted (it).
23    Loan of Petearsemtheus against
24    Petearsemtheus and
25    Petesouchos, the (sons) of Panobchounis,
26    of bronze 5100 drachmas.
