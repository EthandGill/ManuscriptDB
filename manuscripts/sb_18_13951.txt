1     After the consulship of Fl[avius] ...
2     To Flavius Eustochius, the [most magnificent] [and most glorious count of the consecrated domestics and]
3     presiding over [the city of the Arsinoites, Aurelius Joseph Karalas, peace-officer]
4     still also Paul ... setting out from k... [greetings; I agree willingly, swearing]
5     god the almighty [and the divine piety of the fair-conquering crown]
6     to guarantee and to [receive from your eminence Aurelius]
7     Samban son of Aris and [Pellen son of] ... [setting out from] ... [but also]
8     being sought [by your eminence I shall bring and hand over them]
9     in the public [prison of this city whence I received these]
10    received, [but if I do not hand these over as stated, being sought so that I]
11    the surety of these [liable to be to your eminence for all the being sought] by [it against these and to make the answers]
12    subjected [to it for this of all my property, present and future]
13    individually and gener[ally by way of pledge and by the right of mortgage as if from judgment.]
14    valid the surety [and] [being questioned I agreed. Aurelius Joseph Kefalas, peace-officer, I guarantee willingly]
15    of will and mani[fest]ation [of Aurelius Samban son of Aris and Pellen son of] ...
16    as stated and I released. [So-and-so] son of [so-and-so] I wrote for [him being] illiterate. ...
17    Surety for Samban and Pellen by Aurelius Joseph Kefalas, peace-officer, to Flavius
18    Eustochius, count.
