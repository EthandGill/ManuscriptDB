[TRANSLATION]
1     643 (chmg), Amen.
2     In the name of the holy and life-giving Trinity, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit; in the reign
3     of our most serene and God-crowned master Flavius Heraclius, the eternal Augustus, Imperator,
4     in the sixth year and in the consulship of the same our most pious master, in the fifth year, Mesore 12,
5     of the fifth indiction. This written and inviolable agreement of emphyteusis (perpetual lease) is established and made between themselves, willingly and being persuaded,
6     by the two parties; on the one part,
7     the legal right of the holy monastery of the abba Patois of God-beloved memory,
8     of the hill of the village of Tanyaithis of the Apollonopolite nome, through me Menas son of Paneus the
9     most reverend superior and monk of the same monastery, and through others who in due time shall receive
10    from him the headship of the same monastery; and on the other part, Aurelius Joannes, son of his father
11    Phoibammon, of his mother Hypatia, originating from a hamlet called Bespaiom of the hill of the
12    same village of Tanyaithis of the same Apollonopolite nome; and they agree mutually as to the matters below
13    subjoined, greeting. The aforesaid Joannes son of Phoibammon has brought forward proposals to the same monastery
14    through the aforewritten Menas the superior, at his own request and entreaty, wishing to take on perpetual lease
15    twelve and a half arouras of sowable plots and further also five arouras of dry land, out of the property belonging
16    to it of sowable plots and dry plots, tax-free land and waterless land, according to the type prescribed
17    by the laws concerning emphyteusis, which lie also in the same hill of the same village,
18    round about the same monastery, and which have come to the same monastery from the legal rights
19    of it; and the terms concerning these having been agreed by each party, willingly and being persuaded,
20    free of all guile and force and fear and deceit and compulsion and error and any legal
21    objection or circumvention whatsoever, they have come to this written and inviolable emphyteutic
22    agreement, by which they agree: the aforesaid right of the same holy monastery of abba Patois,
23    through me the aforewritten Menas the superior, from now already to have leased out and ceded to the
24    aforesaid Joannes son of Phoibammon, and to have registered, from now for all ever and continuing time
25    in perpetuity, according to the right of emphyteusis, for the time of his life and that of his
26    heirs and successors and possessors, the aforesaid twelve and a half sowable arouras
27    and five arouras of dry land, by the just measuring-cord, out of the property belonging to it of sowable plots
28    and dry plots, tax-free land and waterless; the neighbours of the same sowable plots
29    of twelve and a half arouras are thus: to the south the arouras of the same monastery, to the north the arouras of Paul son of
30    Senouthes and of the children of Joannes son of Palos, having come to them from a just emphyteusis from
31    the same monastery; to the east the lot, that is the arouras of the same monastery formerly under
32    Pantak the farmer; to the west the hill of the same village; and if they should not be found to fill out the same
33    sowable plots of twelve and a half arouras, to make up the deficiency, that is the completion
34    of them, from the south of the aforesaid five arouras of dry plots, and from the boundary of the common (land),
35    that is Psai, towards the east, until making up the completion of the same sowable plots
36    of twelve and a half arouras; the neighbours of the same five arouras of dry plots are thus:
