1     of dyke-tax, 16th year: Achillas son of Dios … ina, 1 obol, 2 chalkoi.

3     of dyke-tax, 16th year: Hermione … 4, and Eudaimonis daughter of Herodes, 8, three obols.
4     and 16th [year]: Hermaios son of H … [through] the secretary,
5     Sept(ember), 1 drachma.
6     and 16th year: Hermaios also called … on son of Dion …
7     of the assessment of the Had[riane]ion, drachm …
8     [of the a]ssessment, 16th year: Tene … eus … two obols, half-obol.

10    13 drachmas, three obols; of the assessment 6 drachmas, four obols, half-obol, 2 chalkoi.
11    Comes to be, of the quarter,
12    [40] drachmas, two obols, 2 chalkoi; 44 drachmas, two obols, 2 chalkoi; [94] drachmas, three obols; 20 drachmas, 1 obol, half-obol, 2 chalkoi; there come to be 99 drachmas, 3 obols, 2 chalkoi, of which
13    of dyke-tax 30 drachmas, five obols, 2 chalkoi; [24] drachmas, two obols, half-obol, [2 chalkoi]; 71 drachmas, two obols; 13 drachmas, three obols; comes to be 40, 1 obol.
14    of the assessment 9 drachmas, three obols; 1[9 drachmas, five obols, half-obol, 1 chalkous; 23 drachmas, 1 obol; 6] drachmas, four obols, half-obol, 2 chalkoi; comes to be 59 drachmas, two obols, [3 ch]alkoi.
