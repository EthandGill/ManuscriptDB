[TRANSLATION]
1     Aphunchis to Apeiti, greetings and
2     be well. Since Horos my son
3     having come down from the city said to me that you said to him to remember me concerning the wood-collection which you have to give entirely [to me] in the year, [if] therefore you have leisure
4     to come down and to hire for me
5     either you or your child, because I am occupied concerning the [animals]
6     and not to have free time. Therefore do not do otherwise, since others too are at hand to me, but by choice
7     I prefer you. Having deliberated therefore about this, answer back to me most quickly
8     in order that I not fall away. And take care of [yourself] in order that you be well. Farewell.
9     Year 28 of Caesar, Phaophi ...
10    To Apeiti
