[TRANSLATION]
1     Phoibammon of Kastus has given through the lord
2     Kallinikos Horinikos to the account of public dues of the city
3     of Antinoopolis gold karats four, equals karats 4 only.
4     Isaac of Eustathios recipient; the requisition accounts [to me]
5     of the 2nd indiction as aforesaid.
6     requisition of Peter the recipient
