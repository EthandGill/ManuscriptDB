[TRANSLATION]
1   Year 11: Sarapion paid, and
2   partners, collectors of money-taxes of the village
3   of Herakleia, to Tixis(?), for the nabion-tax of the katoikoi,
4   four drachmas, total 4 dr.
5   Year 12, likewise the same, for the registration of the oil-press,
6   twenty-four drachmas, total 24 dr.
