[TRANSLATION]
1     Hero[des] … to Apollonios his
2     lord, greeting.
3     It is surely not unknown to you, the urgent
4     woodwork-tasks both of the temples and of the guest-house,
5     for the sake of which we have with difficulty set in hand, on the 2nd
6     of the intercalary days, the matter of the contract for the
7     door-frames of the bedchamber in the open court;
8     and your steward has agreed,
9     on my urging, to contract for the
10    two doors of both the dining-room and the
11    fore-stages (?), so that it may be done at no extra cost and
12    not by daily wages, for the squared timber has been sawn for these purposes. But
13    because his
14    daughter has died, he has held back from the work, until
15    he lays aside his mourning, and also (until) the
16    lightening of the public works.
17    I therefore beg you, lord, to permit me,
18    during the spare days, to go down to your (?) brother Hierakion
19    by boat — for at another time I shall not be able to go on foot through the (those) places …
20    on account of the devastation of the places
21    and the scarcity …
22    … the officials having been [summoned] to go off to Memphis for the audit (dialogismos), so that, taking the opportunity, I may learn what Hierakion is doing. For the distance is (only) of two days. I have made your obeisance (proskynema)
23    at the sacrifices of Isis on the night
24    of her birthday-festival, and the more I prayed that you make the most abundant advancement.
25    Farewell, lord. Thoth 1.
26    To Apollonios, (my) lord.
