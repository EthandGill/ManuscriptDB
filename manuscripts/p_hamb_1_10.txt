1     [To Ant]onius Longus, decurion,
2     fr[o]m Herais daughter of Dioskoros, with her guardian, he[r] kinsman Ptolemaios son of Papos. On the night leading into the 28th of the present month
3     Hathyr, a band of robbers came up against my houses which are
4     in the village of Theadelpheia of the Themistos division of the Arsinoite (nome), and
5     when I had got out of the way together also
6     with my [d]aughter Heroïs, they mur[d]e[re]d
7     my steward Dioskoros [and] a brother
8     nicknamed Ptolemaios and [the]
9     servant-girl of my daughter, Isarion,
10    and moreover they carried off my outfits,
11    finished, white, thirteen in number, among which
12    broad-striped women's (garments) ten, and
13    two children's and other colored (ones), a white-Spanish one and another
14    Spanish one, a rose-colored one and a milk-colored one, and a hooded cloak, white-Spanish, finished, with Laconian stripe, one,
15    and women's outfits, of purple
16    two, among which one with a girdle,
