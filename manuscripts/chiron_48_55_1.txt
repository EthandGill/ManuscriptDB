[TRANSLATION]
1     pa... e...
2     would rule the magistrates by a[utho]rity self-chosen under their compulsion ...
3     your incomparable leadership, which they set-against with their writings concerning ...
4     in both votes by show of hand and archairesias and the arrangement of our lords the Emperors, which ...
5     you do not ignore, which from all force and harassment frees the veterans and us the sons of [them] ...
6     undisturbed the benefit from you the master, so that I am aided. [I may prosper.] Quintus Mari[nus] [Claudianus I submitted.]
7     And concerning the maintaining the time-limits and the ranks according to the original promise on ...
8     [those] promising and [those] being-elected I submitted to you the ordinance of Marcus Aure[lius] [Antoninus]
9     and Lucius Aurelius Verus the ordinance and of Sempronius Liberalis the one-having-governed a-memo[randum].
10    I submitted moreover to you the ordinance of the Emperors and of Sempronius Liberalis the one-having-governed
11    a memorandum concerning the observing the time-limits and the ranks according to the original promises
12    both of those being-elected and those promising, so that, if your fortune seems good, also
13    you might order me to be observed. And it is thus:
