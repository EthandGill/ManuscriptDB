[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most excellent council of the Antinoites,
2     New Hellenes,
3     from Lucius Valerius Lucretianus of the Matidian (tribe), also called Plotinius, and Lucius Longinus Herennius
4     Paulinius, also called Megaleisius. You are not unaware, most excellent men, that from all
5     liturgies elsewhere we have been exempted
6     according to the ordinance of the god Hadrian, the founder also
7     of our city. Since therefore, having come to Philadelphia, a village of the Arsinoite nome, of the division of Herakleides, where we own land, at the collection of the public
8     taxes, the
9     village-scribe of the aforesaid village, Aphrodas son of Theon, out of spite presented us for the conveyance
10    of the grain, contrary to what has been ordained. By necessity, lords, we make our approach to you, requesting, if it seems good to you, to refer the matter to the most excellent epistrategos
11    Calpurnius Concessus concerning this, so that
12    according to the rights belonging to us he may order
13    that others be appointed in our stead and
14    that he himself render an account of the things rashly done, and that for the future we be kept free from harassment. Farewell. Year 4 of the Emperor
15    Caesar Lucius Septimius Severus Pius Pertinax
16    Augustus Arabicus Adiabenicus, Mesore 20. I, Lucius
17    Valerius Lucretianus, have submitted
18    and have written on behalf of Herennius, who does not know
19    letters.
