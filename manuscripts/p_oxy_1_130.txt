[TRANSLATION]
1   † To my good lord, lover of Christ, lover of the poor, all-
2   praiseworthy, most magnificent patrician and dux of the Thebaid,
3   Apion, fr(om) Anup, your pitiable slave, from the property belonging to
4   it called Phakra.
5   Nothing unjust or impious does the glorious house of my good
6   lord possess; rather it is ever full of mercy, pouring out upon the needy
7   their necessities. Wherefore I too, the pitiable slave of my good lord,
8   through this my present petition wish to be shown mercy,
9   (and) that your lordship know how, from fathers and forefathers, I have served
10   my good lord (and) paid yearly the public dues; and, by God’s
11   will, in the past eleventh indiction and the past
12   tenth my beasts died, and I borrowed no little gold,
13   15 sol(idi), until I could buy the same beasts. But when I came
14   to my good lord and (when) he came here to have mercy on me, the agents
15   of my lord would not act according to the command of my good
16   lord. For if, master, your mercy does not reach me, I cannot stand
17   on my property and be of use for the landlord’s affairs. And
18   I beg and entreat your lordship to order that I be shown mercy,
19   since I have come to great ruin. For I have no other ref-
20   uge except that of the Master Christ and of your eminence.
21   And I will send up undying hymns to the Master Christ for the
22   life of your lordship and of her most magnificent son
23   Strategios the lord. †
