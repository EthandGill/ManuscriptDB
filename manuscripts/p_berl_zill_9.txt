[TRANSLATION]
1     Heracleides to Satabbute the most dear
2     greetings.
3     I ordered for myself before the 1 of Phaophi
4     to come upon the threads and the warp of the garments, and you did not come.
5     well therefore you will do if from now on you come to me. for it is necessary them until
6     of the tenth to be cut out,
7     lest perhaps need become for me to sail down into city. but if not
8     you intend to come, again for me
9     from now on word send, so that
10    here them I delay. look
11    therefore, do not otherwise act.
12    be well.
13    of year 2 of Servius Galba Imperator
14    Caesar Augustus, Phaophi 9.
15    To Satabbute Ibianes the dyer.
