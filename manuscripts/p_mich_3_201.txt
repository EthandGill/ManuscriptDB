[TRANSLATION]
1     Antonis to Apolheios and Valerianus both, greetings and to be well always. First of all you
2     greet [by] [a] [letter]. Well then, you will manage and care for us concerning the two purple-workers, so that not another carry them out
3     and the garments, the subricae, and
4     the palliolum of them. And ask Apina about the phenols, and ask him how much they will spend on weavers.
5     And let Atolissia the Isota [descend]. I will write a letter. Let one of you descend. He/she greets you
6     Thermoutas greets you very much and very much complains to you because you are not sending her a letter and the antiphonesis.
7     Year 2 of Emperor Nerva Trajan Augustus
8     [Germanicus], Mechir 17. And the pallium of Daphne, send [it] to her.
9     Return it to [Apolheios]
10    [Return] it to Valerianus.
