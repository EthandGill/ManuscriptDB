1     Account of expenditure.
2     From Macrinus, gardener, drachmas eight.
3     From Phankeim, drachmas eight.
4     From Abinous, basket-maker, drachmas twenty.
5     From Heras, son of Demas, drachmas four.
6     From Besammon[s], dyer, drachmas five, a half-drachma.
7     From Ptolemaeus, porter, drachmas twenty.
8     From Heracleus, merchant, drachmas eight.
9     From Charmeinus, wine-seller, drachmas thirty-two.
10    Rents of house-plots.
11    Meleteia, drachmas …
12    Cornelio[s], drachmas …
13    Antoni[o]s, drachmas …
14    Thonis, embroiderer, drachmas.
15    Account of expenditure of the present five year of the ky[ri]on.
