[TRANSLATION]
1     to my lord father Aminnaeus
2     Alupios.
3     not having found clean papyrus
4     at this time for this I wrote. You will do well, lord
5     [father], to demand [Asklepiadēs] that which he owes me
6     [coins] two, and send (them)
7     [me] in the city; and
8     give to Zakāōn
9     grain artabas two, and when
10    I winnow the same, you receive from the same
11    Zakāōn. [be] well
12    I pray
13    for many years.
