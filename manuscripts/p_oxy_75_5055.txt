[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Sarapammon to Achilleus and Severus, the most honoured, greetings. Before all I pray for your health, and I make your obeisance before the ancestral gods. Please, my lords, since we stated our case before the epistrategus regarding the house of the painter on the 14th of Phaophi of the current 2nd(?) year, and we did not have enough time(?) to get the minutes (of the hearing), I beg you without fail to excerpt them for me. The names are: Zois daughter of Zoilus and Thaesis daughter of Zenarium and Tiberius Claudius . . . To Achilleus, advocate, and Severus . . . (R. Hatzilambrou)
