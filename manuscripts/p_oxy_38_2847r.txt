[TRANSLATION]
1     productive (land) …
2     Of assessment: there is a certain land whose rent, according to the year's
3     yield of the crops, is brought in.
4     of the year's own (crops). Into this inspection many
5     categories fall, such as: when a vine is cut down, for which the taxes are reduced; that which is replanted in its stead; private land that is ceded; that which
6     is bought from waste, and other such things.
7     Of riverside land: all the public and private plots beside the river are … inspected.
8     Of the reckoning-deduction (hypologos): of all the land that is unproductive, of the imperial domain, an inspection is made every three years, and it is called hypologos because it is deducted from the measure of the land in the plain, so that the remaining productive part is left.
9     Of geometria (land-survey): of the vineyards and the other orchard-lands a remeasurement is made every four years.
10    Remeasurement of every plot: whenever imperial land is sought, those fields
11    in which the inquiry lies are remeasured.
12    Remeasurement of the dykes: each of the local people digs out five naubia …
13    according to the assignment of the dykes, and less
14    as the need requires. A naubion is a measure
15    of dug earth having three cubits in breadth and length and depth.
16    Remeasurement of the canals: the canals too each year
17    are dug out, for from the … of the river …
