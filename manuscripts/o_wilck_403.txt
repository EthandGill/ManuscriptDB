[TRANSLATION]
1     Amenothes Petechonsius has been written off
2     for poll-tax year 13 ten drachmae, and for bath-tax one obol and one and a half, they total eleven drachmae [and] one obol and a half
3     and of these additionally written. Of year 13 of Tiberius Claudius
4     Caesar Augustus Germanicus Emperor
5     Pharmouthi 15th. Sekes of Pamonthus have confirmed.
