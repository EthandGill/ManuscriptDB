[TRANSLATION]
1     From the registry-office of the village of Teptynis.
2     In the year … of [Hadria]n Caesar the lord, Mesore 23.
3     There acknowledge Sarapas son of Charidemos, aged about 38,
4     scar on the left eyebrow, and Protas son of Tephersos, aged about 32,
5     [scar] [on] the left thumb, and Orseus son of Hareotes, aged about 50,
6     scar on the left eyebrow, and Poueris son of Amation,
7     aged about 25, scar on the forehead on the left, and Heron
8     son of Kronion, aged about 85, scar on the little finger of the left hand,
9     and Heron freedman of Herakleia, aged about 30, unmarked,
10    and P[r]otas son of Kronion, aged about 25, scar on the left hand,
11    and … t … on son of Aphrodisi[os], aged about 30, unmarked, and Heron
12    son of Harp[o]kration, aged about 30, [scar] on the left shin, and Maron
13    son of Neilos, aged about 35, [scar] on the right knee, and Orses the elder son of Orseus,
14    aged about 64, [scar] on the left eyebrow, and Protas son of Kronion
15    son of Nekpheraus, aged about 30, on the left knee — the twelve elders of the village of Tebtynis and the remaining cultivators,
16    that they have leased to him, Didymos, for eight years from
17    the 17th year of Hadrian Caesar the lord, the existing
18    and future plots and pasturages, both winter and
19    summer, of the land below the village belonging to the marsh
20    called "God's," registered in the account of the one-third (tax), for the grazing of whatever Didymos may choose
21    of sheep and goats; and that the acknowledging parties have received
22    from Didymos in advance the rents
23    of the eight years from the 17th year by hand, and
24    that they will guarantee (it) from public (claims) and every kind, the leases of the acknowledging parties which Didymos holds remaining valid.
