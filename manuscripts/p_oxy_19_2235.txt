[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Eulogios, ex-logistes, riparius of the Oxyrhynchite (nome),
2     from Aurelius Horos son of Theodoros, from the village of Adaiou
3     of the 4th pagus of the same nome. There belongs to the children of my own daughter, Peteuris and Patas, from the succession
4     of their father Penephis, in the fields of the
5     same village, of private grain-bearing land, one aroura,
6     registered in the name of their father
7     and aforesaid Penephis, and falling under
8     the plain of the same Adaiou, as also is entered yearly through the books
9     for collection. But since
10    Pausiris, scribe from the village of Terythis of the same pagus, at every point plotting against the orphan children, attacks them, on the pretext
11    of public taxes of the same one aroura,
12    as if it falls under the plain of Terythis, wishing to carry out an innovation
13    to the defrauding of the children of my daughter — for never has any payment been made to the tax-collectors of Terythis for this land, especially since the
14    boundary-surveyor Aphynchis of our village has issued to us a written declaration that this aroura is entered in the plain of Adaiou — therefore I approach, asking that the aforesaid Pausiris be summoned and ordered to cease henceforth from plotting against the orphan children, lest on account of his action they become fugitives, lord. Farewell.
15    I, Aurelius Horos, have submitted (this) as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Paulos
16    son of Petros, wrote on his behalf as he is illiterate.
