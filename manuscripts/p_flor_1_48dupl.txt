[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Apoll[oni]os [also called Eudai]m[on], son of Demetrios son of Eudaimon, Hermopolite, registered in Polis,
2     the eastern (quarter), have followed this transfer-deed and have received from my nephew Aurelius Tithoetion also called Sarapammon, son of Herminos also called Achilleus, registered in the Western Fort, for the discharge of what his deceased mother Eudaimonis also called Phintys owed me according to a bank transfer-deed
3     in the 29th year of the deified Commodus, in the month Mecheir on the new moon, of principal of silver one talent, at interest
4     of three obols, of bare silver one thousand two hundred drachmas, total drachmas (1200), besides which I have received in part by hand and besides which I have made a gift; and I will not proceed in any way to the dissolution of the same transfer-deed
5     and of the stipulations through it and of the pledge which I produced in the past year, and we make no claim against one another, the agreement-document being valid which both I and his mother made in
6     the same 29th year, Tybi, as it contains. Year 5 of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
7     Pius Felix and Marcus Aurelius Alexander Caesar, Augusti, Tybi 9. I, Aurelius
8     Apollonios also called Eudaimon, son of Demetrios, have received and have released and [make no claim] as aforesaid.
9     I, Aurelius Tithoetion also called Sarapammon, have paid out as aforesaid.
