2     to fare well.
3     Many fellow-citizens having visited us without
4     letters (from you), we were greatly grieved and are disposed to such
5     suspicion as that you are ill, along with
6     our own troubles; not even in this do you comfort us so as

8     how much you have suffered; but the same things (have befallen) us too, or perhaps even worse.
