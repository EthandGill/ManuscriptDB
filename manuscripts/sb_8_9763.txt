1     ... [placed/delivered] the documents [and] also the goods, whatever [was brought] into the [...]

5     has come to this settlement, since [all the]
6     golden ornaments from Auxonia, the mother [of] [T]aurinus
7     of Abraham, the most celebrated scholasticus, has [been] returned
8     to the aforementioned Auxonia through the aforesaid
9     [Achilles]. And Achilles himself sought security
10    that no one should bear risk of the service both
11    first and second. To this [agrees] Theo[ni]lla
12    the aforementioned, swearing by almighty God and the
13    victory and permanence of our lord Flavius Leo the eternal
14    Augustus Emperor, [that she] has received the documents and the goods,
15    [and has] entrusted to Achilles the service of distribution
16    of the golden ornaments to the same Auxonia, and likewise also again
17    to renounce the service of the settlement and not to bring action against him
18    in any court local or foreign
19    [neither] by imperial rescript nor concerning any matter
20    [concerning] the aforementioned principal matters neither financially
21    nor by charge, neither against him nor against his son Phoibam[mon],
22    as if she had suffered something from him, when she happened to be in his household or in
23    another place; for doing a [pious] deed [when] Phoibamm[on] [was] sick
24    she ministered to him from beginning to end; and in accordance with this the same
25    [Theon]illa, willing and [persuaded], settled [matters] with Achilles and with
26    his son Phoibammon. And the same Achille[s] also agrees
27    [that he] has handed over the documents and the goods that were brought
28    into his household, and for this reason each party has sworn
29    the same most fearsome oath, [not] to bring charges [against] each other concerning any matter raised or not raised,
30    said or not said, understood or not understood,
31    brought to court or not brought to court; and not
32    able neither one of them either by rescript nor
33    [by] imperial notation to litigate against [each] other because
34    to be extinguished for them all seed of suit and action both for matter
35    and for person; and not able none of them [in] any

37    [it should] happen [that] some of them [suffer] any suit or harassment
38    to endure from anyone differing with another of them
39    concerning any matter of the aforementioned principal points,

41    [losses] that will accrue to the one adhering [to be] known
42    and from all sides for him the unharmed and [the] [un]damag[ed]

45    [five] pounds of gold; nonetheless also unbroken and immovable

48    power; and nothing to be gainsaid or [they] would be liable to the aforesaid penalty and the divine oath and concerning this
49    jeopardy; and both, when interrogated, agreed.
50    [A]urelia Theonilla, daughter of Theodore, the aforementioned, have placed the settlement and assent and am persuaded by all the written things as aforesaid. Flavius A[chil]les, citizen, the aforementioned, have placed
51    the settlement and assent and am persuaded by all the [written things]
52    as aforesaid. Flavius Herodorus, citizen, [is] present for the settlement.
