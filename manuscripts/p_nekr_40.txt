[TRANSLATION]
1     Tapaoûs daughter of Pia[oûs], undertaker of the city of the Mothites, to Ka[sia]n[os] son of Kasianos, undertaker
2     of the toparchy of K[ysis], greeting. I acknowledge that I have served you (for board) for the fourth part of the service. I received from you the maintenance and the clothing-allowance, in silver of the Augusti, in coinage, in
3     n[u]mmi, twenty talents, which he paid me in full
4     on the 1st day before the Kalends of October, fully,
5     at the beginning. Again, before the … Kalends of October
6     by way of confirmation … a … the maintenance and
7     the clothing-allowance of the [four]th part
8     of the servi[ce] … you will [pro]vide. Valid is this security, this handwritten document, bearing the subscription of the subscriber; let it be valid and
9     firm as lawful, as deposited in a public (archive),
10    and having been asked the formal question I have agreed.
11    In the consulship of our lords
12    Diocletianus, father of the Augusti, for the 10th time,
13    and Gal[erius] Valerius Maximianus Augustus for the [7th] time, on the 8th day before the Ides of Ja[nu]ary.
14    Tapaoûs daughter of Piaoûs, undertaker, the aforesaid, to [Kas]ianos son of Kasianos, undertaker,
15    the aforesaid. I received from you the maintenance and the clothing-allowance of the fourth
16    part of the service, in silver of the Augusti,
17    in nummi, 20 talents, as is set forth,
18    and having been asked the formal question I have agreed. I wrote
19    on her behalf, she being illiterate, (I) Aurelius Philosar[api]s, from the city of the Mothites.
