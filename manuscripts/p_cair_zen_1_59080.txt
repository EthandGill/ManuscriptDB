[TRANSLATION]
1     [Iolla] to Zenon, greetings. I gave [you] … me and to hear me badly [about] Zenodoros. [And when]
2     I heard that he is angry with me; I went … [the] knees of him I grasped; for not he allowed me …
3     Zenodoros to him. Now therefore the things ordered Zenodor[os] … something [heard?] … Zenodoros …
4     five Egyptian [scourgers] … with scourges me [arrogantly]
5     [So that] and my skin has been cut through and now … still worse [I] have been outraged by [him].
6     I was about to flee and come to you … [Z]enodoros prevented me, lest the work, he said, [might be hindered],
7     but he asked me to write to you. Now therefore I have [written] … when he arrives, everything will be reported [to you]
8     the things that have happened. Force for is [she who commits outrage] … she masters both us and him.
9     Be prosperous.
10    [Iolla] weaver concerning Addaios
11    and [how] they have outraged him.
12    [Year 29, month D]aisios 21, in Arsinoe.
13    To Zenon.
