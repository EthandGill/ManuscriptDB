[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the granary of the metropolis of produce, 4th year of Antoninus
2     of the lord, Epeiph 9, for Kerameion
3     [for] a man named Antiphilos [son of] Kronios, Psenetymos, of wheat, artabae 1 1/12, and for South [for] a man named
4     Senpiktos [son of] Antiphilos through Antiphilos
5     [his] son, of wheat, artabae 1/3 1/12. Total wheat, artabae one and a half.
6     Horos I have signed.
