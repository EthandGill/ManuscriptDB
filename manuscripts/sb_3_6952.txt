[TRANSLATION]
1     To Servillius Pudens, centurion
2     From Horion, son of Mystus; night having passed, an attack having occurred of the
3     courtyard of me, two piglets were carried off.
4     I, wandering about together with the official [the] [search] making,
5     I came to Men…tin…[son]
6     of Ammonut…[to have]…ow…[a].
7     and he did not allow me [to enter] together with the
8     official; wherefore I request him [to be brought] before you, him about-to-give an account of the things [dared] by him, in order that I be able
9     to recover the things of me and to obtain justice.
10    Farewell.
11    Year 3 of Lucius Septimius Severus Pertinax
12    Augustus, Phamenoth.
