[TRANSLATION]
1   Apollinaris to Taesis, (his) mother and lady,
2   many greetings. Before all things I pray for your
3   health, and I myself am in health, and your obeisance
4   I make before the gods here. I want you to
5   know, mother, that I arrived safe at Rome
6   in the month Pachon, the 25th, and was allotted to Misenum.
7   But I have not yet learned my century; for I had not
8   gone off to Misenum when I was writing you this letter.
9   So I ask you, mother, attend to yourself,
10   do not be anxious about me; for I have come to a good
11   place. You will do well to write me a letter
12   about your welfare and (that) of my brothers and
13   of all your (folk). And I, if I find anyone, write
14   to you; I will not hesitate to write to you. I greet my bro-
15   thers warmly, and Apollinaris and his chil-
16   dren and Karalas and his children.
17   I greet Ptolemaios and Ptolemais and her
18   children and Heraklous and her children.
19   I greet all those who love you by
20   name.
21   I pray for your health.
22   Deliver at Karanis ☓ to Taesis, from Apollinaris (her) son,
23   ☓ man of Misenum.
