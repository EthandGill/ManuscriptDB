[TRANSLATION]
1     In the ninth year of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius,
3     in the month Sebastos the 16th, Thoth 16, in Herakleia of the
4     Themistos division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges
5     Apollonios son of Kastor, son of Sambas, from the quarter
6     of the Bithynians of other places, a Persian of the epigone,
7     about 32 years old, with a scar on the first finger of the left hand,
8     to Stotoetis son of Horos, son of Panephremmis, priest, about 31 years old,
9     with a scar on the second finger of the right hand, that he has
10    received from him, the acknowledger, forthwith from hand to hand, an interest-bearing loan of capital in silver, two hundred drachmas, and of wheat in kind, new, clean, unadulterated, fifteen artabas by the dromos four-choinix measure; of which let the acknowledger also make repayment to Stotoetis in the month
11    Pauni of the present 9th year of Antoninus Caesar
12    the lord, without delay, the right of execution being granted
13    to Stotoetis both from the acknowledger
14    and from all his property, as if by legal judgment. I, Apollonios son of Kastor, have from
15    Stotoetis the aforesaid two hundred drachmas of silver
16    and fifteen artabas of wheat, and
17    I will repay them in the month Pauni of the present year
18    as is set forth above. Alkimos, secretary of the aforesaid record-office,
19    wrote on his behalf, since he says he does not know letters.
20    It has been registered through the record-office in Herakleia.
