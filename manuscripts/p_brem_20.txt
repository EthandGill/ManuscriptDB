[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Apollonios the
2     most honored, greetings.
3     Having been in Lykos city the second
4     and having received your letters, I was pleased,
5     brother, at that you are strong with
6     your people, for this is for me through prayer
7     is. Plutios staying as visitor I did not find, but it is said your commission
8     has detained [him]. This therefore immediately,
9     if he comes, [it] will be sent back to you. Concerning my not having come to you I ask you to forgive me, for pending matters held me up. After a few days, if the
10    gods preserve, I will be present to you.
11    [I pray] you to be strong,
12    my lord Apollonius,
13    with your un-envied
14    children. Be strong.
15    To Apollonios, strategos of the seven villages of Apollonopolis.
