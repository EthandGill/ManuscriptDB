[TRANSLATION]
1     To Callistratus [son] of Callistratus
2     greetings. I have received from you the
3     tax-revenue which [you] have farmed concerning indeed
4     Herakleia [of the] lesser plots
5     of Hermuthes Psephigides, as many as
6     were sown in the
7     24th year of Caesar, and nothing to you
8     do I complain. Year 24 of Caesar, Mesore 25
