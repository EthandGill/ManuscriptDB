[TRANSLATION]
1     Ailourion and Horos, collectors of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Abos the elder, (son of) Petosiris. We have received for bath-tax of year 3,
3     base drachmas 2, ½ obol, 2 chalkoi, total drachmas 2, ½ obol, 2 chalkoi. Year 3 of Antoninus
4     Caesar the lord, Pharmouthi 18.
5     I, Ailourion, through Kales, have signed.
