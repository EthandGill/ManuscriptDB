[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidoros to Isidoros and Paniskos
2     to the sons, greetings.
3     I wrote to you through another ostracon that you send me the pillow
4     the small one since I suffer sleeping and you did not send (it). Send then it and
5     the ink and barley-loaves two and of the small (ones)
6     the half into a rag, having taken, send, since your ink has a blemish …
7     to knead into the pot since I stand writing and instead of it I will send to you. But concerning the
8     copper I wrote at another time that, as soon as if
9     I sell barley, I will send. Send whatever
10    was brought to me letters from Egypt. It seemed good to me not to trouble you because of your being in the
11    mountain to be. Do not then [neglect]
12    which I write to you… [to pro]vide.
13    Be well.
14    Phaophi
15    22
