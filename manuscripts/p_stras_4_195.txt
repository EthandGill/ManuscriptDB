[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 14 of Aurelius Antoninus
2     Caesar our lord; for reckoning, Mechir 3
3     he credited Asklepias, collector of revenue of Memphis, of the second
4     quarter Horos of the Nile, poll-tax, drachmas eight, total drachmas 8
5     and for two workers working at the bath, as for the one, drachmas two, total drachmas 4
6     from Lysimachus?
