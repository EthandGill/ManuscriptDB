[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the spotless and consubstantial Trinity father
2     And son and holy spirit. Epeiph first, indiction thirteen.
3     To Flavius Apion the most illustrious and most magnificent from consuls and
4     Patrician landholder and here in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites
5     Through Menas [the] servant of the questioning and providing to his own master
6     To the same most illustrious man the complaint and liability.
7     Aurelius Petros son of Joseph [of] mother Irene from [the] village
8     Of Nekonthos [of] the Oxyrhynchite n[ome] differing from your
