[TRANSLATION]
1     After the [consulship] of Flavius Eusebius, the most splendid, second, Choiak 17, third indiction.
2     To Flavius Taurinus, [respectable,] son of John, record-keeper of the military unit in the Thebaid,
3     from the [Hermopolites,] from Aurelius Victor, Apalictos, mother Kyra, farmer from the
4     same city. I agree willingly [and] deliberately that I have leased from your excellence
5     for a four-year period from the [harvest] of the fortunate fourth indiction the fields that are yours, [amounting to] arourae
6     twenty-five [together with] the [share belonging] to them of the [canal] there from a farm of arourae
7     thirty-one according to [a] joint [ownership] … [and] Theodora and Theod[osius,] sons of Phoibammon,
8     your paternal uncle, [of] the remaining [arourae] six for the completion of the same farm [which is] situated
9     in the east of the [same] [city for sowing] and storage of fruits which if I should choose, [as] rent
10    [of the same] twenty-five [arourae of] grain artabae one hundred and beans artabae
11    … [and vetch artabae] thirty-two and a half and lentils artabae five.
