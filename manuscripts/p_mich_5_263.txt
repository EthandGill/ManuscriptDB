[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twenty-second year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
2     in the month …, in Tebtynis
3     of the Polemon division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges Psenkebkis son of Psyphis, about 31 years old, with a scar on the outside of his right hand, to Pnebtynis son of Psyphis, about 45 years old, with a scar on the first finger
4     of his left hand, that he has sold to him the (land) belonging to him, Psenkebkis, around the aforesaid village of Tebtynis, sacred
5     land of Soknebtynis, of land four arouras together with the village's impost
6     of a half-aroura, or however many there may be, in one parcel, of which
7     of which the neighbours are, on the south a public dyke through which the allotment is watered and drained, on the north the embankment of the Chomeniac canal, on the west the
8     allotment of the buyer Pnebtynis, on the east the allotment of Marepsemis
9     son of Marepsemis; and that he has received from Pnebtynis the whole agreed price in full
10    through hand from house, and that he will guarantee, both Psenkebkis himself
11    and those acting through him, to Pnebtynis and to those acting through him, the (matters) concerning
12    this sale with every guarantee, from public debts — all of them — and every kind, from the earlier times up to the past twenty-first
13    year and of the twenty-first year itself of Tiberius
14    Caesar Augustus, and from private claims and every
15    imposition for all time. Subscriber, Marepsemis
16    son of Marepsemis, about 36 years old, with a scar under his right shin.
17    I, Psenkebkis son of Psoiphis, acknowledge that I have sold to Pnebtynis son of Psoiphis, from
18    the present day for all time, the (land) belonging to me
19    around the aforesaid village of Tebtynis, sacred land of Soknebtynis,
20    of land four arouras together with the village's impost of a half-aroura, or however many
21    there may be, in one parcel, of which the neighbours are, on the south a public dyke
22    through which the allotment is watered and drained, on the north the embankment of the Chomeniac canal, on the west the allotment of the buyer Pnebtynis,
23    on the east the allotment of Marepsemis son of Marepsemis; and I have received
24    from Pnebtynis the whole agreed price
25    in full through hand from house, and I will guarantee the
26    sale with every guarantee, from public and private (claims) and every kind, from the earlier times up to the past twenty-first year and of it
27    itself, the past twenty-first year, of Tiberius
28    Caesar Augustus, and from private claims and every imposition for all time, as stated above. I, Pnebtynis son of Psoiphis,
29    the sale has come to me as stated above. There wrote on his behalf
30    Marepsemis son of Marepsemis, because he does not know letters.
31    Sale of Pnebtynis
32    against Alkippos, of sacred land
