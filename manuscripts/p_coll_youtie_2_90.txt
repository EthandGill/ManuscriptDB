[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of the Flavii Clementinus and Probus, the
2     most glorious, Thoth the seventh of the seventh indiction.
3     To Flavius Ioannes, the most esteemed son of the pious
4     memory of Taurinus, from the Hermopolite (city), from Aurelius Phoibammon
5     son of Paesios, whose mother is Troilis, from the hamlet of Syntapouo, in the
6     tax-collectorship of the village of Ptemmenkyrkis of the Hermopolite nome:
7     I acknowledge that willingly and of my own accord I have leased from your
8     esteemedness, for a four-year period reckoned from the crops
9     of the eighth indiction, coming with God, and including it, the share belonging to you,
10    the half part, of the dry arouras, as many as there are, together with a half share
11    of a cistern and all the right of it and of the great and the small
12    drum (water-wheel), lying in the plain of the same village of Ptemmenkyrkis, for the sowing and planting of crops which I may choose,
13    on condition that your wondrousness, if I am able to sow the
14    same dry land, shall take a half share of all the produce that comes to be
15    on it, according to the agreements made between me and her,
16    and shall demand from me rent for the same half
17    share of the drums, while I have the other half share in return for the
18    labors I perform; but if I am not able to clear and sow the dry land, I will furnish [to you] the rent for the same half share of the two drums yearly, in the manner of all the cultivators there.
19    The lease is valid and secure, and being formally questioned I have so agreed. Aurelius Phoibammon
20    son of Paesios, cultivator, the aforesaid, have leased as is set forth above. Aurelius Victor son of Basileides,
21    from … having been requested, [I wrote] on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
22    … skor … ou … th … from Hermopolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard
23    [from the contracting party.] Aurelius Theon son of Dioskoros, from Hermopolis, I bear witness to the lease
24    having heard from the contracting party. Aurelius Serenos son of Kyros, from Hermopolis, I bear witness
25    to the lease, having heard from the contracting party.
26    Through me, Hermogenes son of Z…, it was completed.
27    A[u]relius Phoib[ammon] …
