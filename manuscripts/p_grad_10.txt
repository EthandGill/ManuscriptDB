[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Berenike, the Benefactor Gods, in the eighth year, in the priesthood of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy
2     son of Stasikrates, of Alexander and [the Saviour Gods and the]
3     Sibling Gods and the Benefactor Gods and the Father-loving Gods, the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos being Arsinoe daughter of Sosibios,
4     in the month Dios, in Tholthis of the Oxyrhynchite nome. Nikandros … the Cyrenaean, of the Epigone, acknowledges that he has received from Sogenes the Macedonian, chiliarch of the men of Endios,
5     of lawful copper, visible to the eye, in the presence of the registered witnesses,
6     four hundred drachmas, the price of the she-asses belonging to him,
7     three in number, one black, two white. And these four hundred drachmas are the
8     remainder of the rent of the bath at Tholthis, which Nikandros owed for the
9     eighth year, and it is not permitted either to Nikandros or to another on his behalf to proceed against Sogenes concerning these asses nor concerning the offspring born from them on any pretext whatsoever. And if Nikandros should proceed,
10    or another on his behalf, against Sogenes in any way whatsoever, the proceeding of the one proceeding shall be invalid, and Nikandros or the one proceeding on his behalf shall pay in addition to Sogenes for each proceeding a penalty of silver drachmas
11    one thousand, and the right of execution shall belong to Sogenes against Nikandros and the one proceeding on his behalf, levying it according to the decree.
