[TRANSLATION]
1   Aibutius Niger and Iulius Serenus, collectors of the mon[ey-taxes <of Elephantine>].
2   Paid: Thinpsench( ), for the price of [state]
3   date-palm of the produce <of year 14, for> the account of Kat…,
4   son of Pamonthes: two drachmas, three obols — total 2 dr. 3 ob. <Year 14>
5   of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the lor[d].
6   I, Iulius Serenus, have jointly received.
