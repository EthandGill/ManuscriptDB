[TRANSLATION]
1     … ei …
2     and caulking.
3     previously the administrator(?) 80 drachmas, on condition that he provide the stone-barge seaworthy and watertight
4     for 12 months. But now they say again that it needs caulking anew because … it is already rather old. Pay out to him therefore another 40 drachmas of copper
5     so that on this account the stone-barge may not
6     be idle. And as soon as we refer (it) to the dioiketes, if he directs, the 40 drachmas will be exacted from the
7     freight-charges given to him.
8     To Dikaios. Having directed that security be given, pay out to Dionysius son of Apollonius,
9     who has contracted to demolish the existing royal lodging in Ptolemais
10    because it has fallen into disrepair, and to transfer whatever brick is sound
11    to the foundations of the new lodging, in accordance with the contract, at 15 drachmas per myriad of brick; for the present,
12    for 3 myriads, [drachmas] 45.
13    … guarantors have stood: Paos son of Horus, Nechythes son of Semphtheus, Harphichoumis
14    … Petesouchos son of Nechthentbes, Psenobastis son of Pasis, Paos son of Petesis,
15    … Pamounios, brick-carriers who have contracted to haul
16    2 myriads of [brick] for the royal lodging being completed in Ptolemais, at 10 drachmas per myriad, 20 drachmas of copper.
17    If Nikanor son of Parmenon and Philippus son of Limnaios, who have contracted along with Sokeus son of Harphonychos for the brushwood-facing of the great dyke by Psenaryos, have stood as guarantors,
18    pay out to them the second instalment from 700 drachmas according to the portion falling
19    to them, 116 drachmas 4 obols.
