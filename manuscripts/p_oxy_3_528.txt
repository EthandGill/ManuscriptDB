[TRANSLATION]
1   Serenos to Isidora [his sis-]
2   ter and lady, very many [greetings].
3   Before all I pray [for your heal-]
4   th, and each [day] and
5   evening I make obeisance for you
6   before Thoeris who loves you. I want you
7   to know that since you went out from me
8   I have kept mourning, weeping by night
9   and lamenting by day. Since the 12th of Phaophi, when
10   I bathed with you, I did not bathe
11   nor anoint myself until the 12th of Hathyr; and you sent
12   me letters that could move a
13   stone — so much have your words stir-
14   red me. That very hour I wrote
15   back to you, and gave it on the 12th, sealed up
16   together with your letters.
17   But apart from your words and let-
18   ters — “Kolobos has made me a whore!” —
19   he said: “Your wife sent me word
20   that he himself sold the little
21   chain and himself set me on the
22   boat.” You say these words so that
23   I be no longer trusted for my lading.
24   See how many times I have sent to you! Whether you come
25   or do not come, let me know.
26   Deliver to Isidora f(rom) Serenos.
