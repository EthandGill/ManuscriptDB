[TRANSLATION]
1     [A] memorandum to Zenon
2     from Nikon. For the
3     digging and [palisading], just as
4     you have given [these], I shall accept. And there are also
5     ditches three at four ropes [of] one-eighth [measure], at two obols per rope.
6     [They] total drachmae 4. And iron for the
7     digging you give for three digging-tools.
8     Farewell.
