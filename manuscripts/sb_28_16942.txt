[TRANSLATION]
1     A … [not to be] separated from the need … he wrote …
2     together with Timaeus to Ptolemais of the beekeepers at the winnowing of the sesame.
3     whenever I stop I will transfer to Oxyrhynchus as you wrote. And I will begin
4     to winnow in Oxyrhynchus on the 25th of Choiak. [And] send Marsyas to us.
5     Farewell. Year 12, Choiak [1]6.
