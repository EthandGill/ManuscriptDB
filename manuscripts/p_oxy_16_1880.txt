[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our masters Theodosius for the 12th time and
2     Valentinianus for the 2nd time, the eternal Augusti, Phamenoth 1.
3     To the office of the princeps of the authority of my lord the most magnificent
4     governor of the province, Flavius Demetrianus [Maxi]mus, accompanied by
5     Paulus the singularis: I, Aurelius Cyrus son of Leontius, agent, from the
6     great city of Alexandria, now resident here
7     in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites. Having approached the same authority by
8     a petition of presentation, that is to say an entreaty, I accused Nestorius son of
9     Nestorius, himself also an Alexandrian agent, concerning a debt,
10    and he having been produced and having given me satisfaction in this respect,
11    henceforth from now I have no claim against him, nor do I make accusation, nor
12    will I make accusation, on account of this matter; on which account I acknowledge,
13    swearing by God the Almighty and by the piety
14    of our masters Theodosius and Valentinianus, who conquer all things, the eternal Augusti, that I shall abide
15    by all the things written and shall transgress them in nothing,
16    and for security I have made this settlement, simply
17    written, and having been formally questioned I acknowledged it. I, Aurelius Cyrus son of Leontius, … have made the settlement and have sworn the divine oath, and I shall act as is set forth above.
18    Settlement of Cyrus, the Alexandrian agent, towards Ne[storius.]
