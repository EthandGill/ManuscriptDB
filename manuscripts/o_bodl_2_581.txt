[TRANSLATION]
1     Imouth, tax collector, to Esmines, of Horus, Porieuthos
2     younger. I received for the dike-tax of Agorai Borra, 19th year, tarnished drachmae four, making drachmae 4.
3     Of the 19th year of Trajan the lord, Epeiph 15.
4     […] I have countersigned.
5     another, Mesore 22, likewise, tarnished drachmae three, pentobols, coppers 2, making drachmae 3, pentobols, coppers 2
6     […] I have countersigned.
