[TRANSLATION]
1     Account of the bailing of Dionysius.
2     Mecheir 24: they make [a] water-conduit
3     in the plot for workers 2, [at] 10 obols,
4     25th: 2 [at] 10 obols, 26th: 2 [at] 10 obols,
5     27th: 1 [at] 5 obols, 28th: they bail for workers 4, [at] 36 obols,
6     29th: they bail and convey [the] water-conduit [for] 4 [at] 36 obols,
7     30th: 4 [at] 36 obols, and [for] the expense of the rental
8     of the crane-house [at] 18 obols; [total] 161 obols.
9     And [for] the price of oil [at] 2 obols, [total] 163 obols,
10    which [equals] drachmas 27 1/2.
