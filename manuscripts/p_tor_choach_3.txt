[TRANSLATION]
1     To Hermocles of the chief bodyguards and overseer of the Pathurite [district]
2     from Tasemis of Seleion
3     of the choachyte. I am wronged by
4     Thennesis, of [she] who was
5     of my father's wife. For this [woman], after I was born
6     to him from another married woman
7     [my father] having united with the aforementioned [woman] and having made from her
8     other children, [and he] having passed away
9     his life in the sixth year, [while] I was absent, she has appropriated [to herself]
10    all [things] left behind by him: two houses,
11    one on the one hand in Dios-polis the
12    Great, another on the other hand in the Memnonia, and the furnishings
13    being adequate and livestock,
14    and furthermore also of those from the paternal property according to their [respective] needs falling …
15    [to] Hermocles.
