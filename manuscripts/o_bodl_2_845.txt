[TRANSLATION]
1     Heriophmos, tax-collector of silver
2     to Panates the younger [son] of Ptollides Pikotes.
3     I have received for watch-towers and other [taxes from] Charax, year 14, tarnished drachmas
4     4, five-obols, and in the name of Pikotes Ptollides
5     similarly, five-obols. Year 14 of Hadrian
6     the lord, Pauni, 23rd.
