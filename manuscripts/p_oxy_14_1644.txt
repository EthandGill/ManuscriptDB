[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy, god, the New Dionysos, Phil[opa]tor Philadelphus,
2     in the nineteen[th] year, and as for the other common (formulae) as is written in Al[ex]andria,
3     in the month Peritios and (= Egyptian) Choiak, in the city of Oxyrhynchus
4     in th[e Theb]aïs. There acknowledge: Pasion and Ptolemaios and
5     Ap[o]ll[o]nios, the three (sons) of Dionysios, Macedonians of those among the cleruch
6     cavalry, to the son of their late sister Berenike,
7     who has died, namely Moschion, … os, Macedonian [of the c]leruch cavalry, all of the quarter of Cleopatra
8     Aphrodi[t]e, that they bring no charge against them, nor any other on their behalf,
9     nor shall they bring charge nor proceed against Moschion nor those
10    on hi[s sid]e concerning that which Moschion deposited with the mother of the acknowledging parties,
11    she being grandmother of Moschion himself on the mother's side, namely Arsinoe
12    daughter of P[tol]emaios, and she having departed life,
13    of a loan at interest of silver principal otherwise transacted by contract through the
14    record-office in the aforesaid city in former times, nor concerning anything
15    else whatever at all of the matters relating to the said contract of the
16    loan, on the ground that Moschion, for certain reasons, the
17    fresh transfer of the aforesaid silver transaction
18    into (the name of) Arsinoe in trust, on account of the aforewritten
19    particular circumstance, made. And if any of us, contrary to the aforewritten (terms), should transgress the contract or proceed against Moschion, apart from
20    the proceeding being invalid, let him also pay in addition,
21    he the (one) proceeding or the (one) who shall proceed on his behalf, to Moschion
22    or to [whom]ever of those on his side he proceeds against, as a pe[nal]ty of silver
23    five hu[nd]red drachmas and to the royal (treasury) the equal (sum), and none the
24    less (let the contract be valid). And upon a[l]l [t]he aforewritten (terms) there consent [the] wit[ne]sses of the acknow[led]ging parties, of [Pasion]
25    [Dion]ysios …
26    … lod …
27    … t …
28    Year 19, Choiak, agree[men]t
29    of Pasion and his brothers
30    t[oward] Moschion.
