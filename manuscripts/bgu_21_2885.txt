1     You have given to the most admirable Georgios for [the Thebaid] for the village-dwellers of the village Nagogis of the eighth indiction
2     for the name of Sophia Taurinos carats four and for the name of Menas Cheidou carats six and a third; [this] comes to gold carats 10 3 only.
3     Phoibadios … 5 through me Taurinos … .
