[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum from Otearmotus.
2     Order Zenon to survey the land at Tapeptia
3     with the rope with which they also measure the Memphis [land].
4     Concerning the boat with mulberry wood
5     Order so that an order may be given
6     as you agreed.
7     Year 31, Thoth 20.
8     From Otearmotus.
