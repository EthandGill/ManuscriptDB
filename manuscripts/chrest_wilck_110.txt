[TRANSLATION]
1     Theotimus, son of Euphron, … [I swear by King Ptolemy the one from Ptolemy and]
2     Arsinoe, gods Philopators, [and] gods [Philopators], [and gods], Brethren [and gods], [Benefactors]
3     and gods Saviors, and Sarapis, and [Isis], and the other gods all, and all [this]
4     the pledge which I have pledged for [two tal]ents [to be] mine clear, and not to be liable
5     to anything else, but only the previously written [mortga]ge. To me swearing truly, may it be well; to me forswearing, may the opposite [befall].
6     Theotimus, son of Euphron, Thracian, of those not yet under the hipparch, eighty-aroura, I acknowledge to guarantee for the [settlement]
7     Philip, the one who has received the apomorya for the second year of the lands around Philadelphia
8     of vineyards and parks of one talent and three thousand drachmas
9     and the same for the [receipt] of the areas around Bubastus vineyards and parks
10    [of] three thousand drachmas, so that it be the [whole] talents [2], for which I pledge the existing
11    to me house and courtyard and the [appurtenances] in Euergitis, [and] I have sworn [the] written royal oath
12    according to this agreement.
