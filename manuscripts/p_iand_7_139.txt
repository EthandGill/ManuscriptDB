[TRANSLATION]
1     … year
2     … to the strategos
3     [of the Oxyrhynchite nome] …
4     [and to Serenus also called] Sarapion
5     [the royal] scribe, from Theon son of Ammonios
6     [son of Theo]n, from the city of Oxyrhynchos, former
7     dike-overseer together with another Theon
8     son of Ammonios, his mother being Eisidote, son of Deios,
9     now deceased. With respect to the document referred
10    for examination, a record of the idios logos (private account) from the conventus of year 11, of which there is a copy in the 19th
11    roll, sheet 119: through the relevant officials in the month
12    Mecheir of the same 3rd year: Papontos also called Diodoros surnamed Longinus, village-scribe
13    of Teis and other villages of the Thmoisepho toparchy,
14    reported that mulberry-trees standing on a dike,
15    formerly of Dionysios son of Phmois, near Tein, had through the force
16    of wind fallen down, [2 trees,] worth 246 drachmas each,
17    and from a great embankment likewise other
18    mulberry-trees, 2, near the same village, from
19    a breach that had occurred, had fallen down,
20    likewise worth 277 drachmas each. So that it might be examined according to the aforesaid manner, it was referred, and
21    those who examined it declared that the village-scribe, having on oath reported all the aforesaid matters
22    first — at which time it was indicated that they had fallen through force of wind — confirmed 840 drachmas
23    and, of the following, had added the ones from the
24    great embankment to have been shaken loose by the breach. And so that it might be made clear for what reason it was not reported by the dike-overseers, he is brought in.
25    I report, swearing by the fortune of the Emperor Caesar
26    Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus
27    Pius
28    that it is not the custom for such things to be reported by dike-overseers, for which reason [I did not]
29    report, or may I be liable to the oath.
30    [Year 11,] Mesore 28.
31    [The referral that took] place has been examined.
32    [Year 11, roll 19.]
