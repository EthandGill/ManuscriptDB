1     To Tiber[ius] Claudius He[ro]des, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite,
2     Diogen[es son of D]iogenes son of Sarapion, of those [fr]om the city of Oxyrhynchos,
3     by the lett[e]r of Julius Lysimachos the (official) appointed over the special account (idios logos), having been established superintendent of dykes of the public embankments
4     of the middle toparchy in place of Theogenes son of Theogenes, released by the same Julius Lysimach[o]s, I swear by the
5     fortune of [I]mperator Caesar Vespasian Augustus and
6     [t]he ancestral gods that I will take charge of the [su]pervision of the embankments
7     and with all care (will see to it that) the men assigned to these works for
8     [t]he completion, or those who shall be assigned,
9     will accomplish each the share of digging (naubia) falling to him, with the proper measuring-rod, making the throwing-up far from the embankments, before (i.e. set back by) eighteen cubits
10    so that the embankments may be completed and made watertight as is [fi]tting, and likewise (that) of the water-guard
11    [t]he fitting supervision (will) be made, and (that) I will compel the assigned men to complete the proper works of the
12    [w]ater-[gua]rd … each embankment
13    … each embankment
14    … …
15    … …
16    … …
17    [o]f the dyke-[superintenden]cy …
18    … of all my administration
19    [a]nd of the spec[ial account], completing all the duties of the dyke-superintendency in the manner that is fitting, (with the security of) the property belonging to me, of which the item-by-item (list) is set out below with the conjectural valuation appended,
20    [t]hese being pledged, and whatever I may acquire in addition; and the item-by-item (list)
21    [o]f those (properties) belonging to me up to the present day (is):
22    around the village of Senekeleu, from the holding of Sosos and Agathon,
23    of grain[-l]and 15 arouras: 1 talent; and from (the holding of) Kapistoteles and Diophantos
24    [f]rom an out-of-time contract, 8 arouras: 2,500 drachmas; and I have 2 talents 3,000 drachmas
25    [u]pon the property of Sarapion and Ptolemaios, both sons of Sarapion,
26    [m]inors, formerly of their father, and of Theon his brother;
27    total in all 3 talents of silver and 5,500 drachmas. If I keep my oath well may it be well with me, but if I forswear it the opposite. Year 2 of Imperator
28    [C]aesar Vespasian Augustus, Pharmouthi 21, on the Sebaste day.
