# HGV-EN: [... I have submitted for registration in the past, 13th year of Antoninus Caesar] our Lord, in the village of Talao [of the lower toparchy (?) ...] 13 sheep, 1 goat, of which, in the same past year, after the registration and counting, 4 sheep have perished, remaining are 9 sheep, 1 goat, which I submit for registration in the present 14th year of Antoninus Caesar our Lord, and, following the birth, 1 lamb, which will graze in the neighborhood of the same village and through the entire nome, the shepherd being [...]. And I swear by the Emperor, Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, that I have not lied. (hand 2) Year 14 of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, Mecheir 3rd. (hand 3) I Sarapion son of [] have submitted for registration with [...].
