[TRANSLATION]
1     Euterpe to Didyma the
2     brother, greetings. Your obeisance I make beside the gods [here], praying for you the finest things in life. More carefree
3     I want you to be, knowing that if you come you will know how your storehouse managed itself diligently. Greet many [times]
4     Dionysion and those with you
5     [all, ] … [ and]
6     Good Daemon. Greets
7     you Aphrodite. Buy me a fine sack of the price.
8     To be well I pray for you, with the whole household.
9     Deliver to Didyma the brother.
