[TRANSLATION]
1     Soter, tax collector of money, to Phthuminei and
2     Isidore, chief guardians. I have received for the dependent [matters]
3     [of] farmers of the Islands in the accounting of month Epiphi
4     of the 18th year of Hadrian Caesar, the lord
5     [of] rough silver, 14 drachmas. Year 18 of Hadrian Caesar
6     of the lord, Mesore 6. Asclas, I have signed.
