[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of the Imperator Caesar Vespasian Augustus,
2     Pharmouthi, the 4th of the month. There paid: [A]phrodisios son of Pasion, scribe of the farmers,
3     the price of wheat of the fifth year carried as remainder among the farmers of Philadelphia,
4     of the estate of Maecenas, three hundred dirty (debased) drachmas, that is 300 dirty drachmas; and of Tapatias daughter of Ammonios … with Sarpous son of Akousilaos following up,
5     assistant of the strategos, likewise one thousand fifty-five dirty [drachmas]
6     five obols, that is 1055 dirty drachmas five obols; of Iucundus son of Grypianus, likewise three thousand
7     eight hundred seventy-five dirty drachmas, one obol, a half-obol, that is 3875 dirty drachmas, one obol, a half-obol,
8     of the estate of Seneca, likewise fifty-five dirty drachmas, four obols, a half-obol, that is 55 dirty drachmas, four obols, a half-obol,
9     of the estate of Lurius, likewise ninety-two dirty drachmas, that is 92 dirty drachmas; of (the estate) of Germanicus
10    likewise nine hundred twenty-one dirty drachmas, one obol, that is 921 dirty drachmas, one obol.
11    And on Pauni 30 the same man paid likewise, of the estate of Germanicus of Philadelphia, dirty
12    one hundred ninety-five drachmas, that is 195 dirty drachmas.
13    [Pri]ce of [whea]t carried as remainder among the farmers of the 5th year of Philadelphia,
14    of silver, 195 drachmas, three obols; extra-charges (prosdiagraphomena),
15    one drachma; symbolic-fees, three obols, a half-obol; that is 197 drachmas.
