[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolemaios, greetings] from … [one] Horos, royal farmer, from Alexander's island. I am wronged by …
2     … [and] … and Sobchanopis, of [his] brothers who are indeed younger brothers of …
3     [my father] … us Horos having died leaves behind a house and a palm-grove and …
4     … [from which there belongs to] my father Horos two shares, because the elder of them [is] a son …
5     … of the possessions, but now I am being cast out by [the] aforementioned and …
6     … Horos, using violence. I beseech therefore you, king, [if] it seems good to you, to order Diophanês …
7     [the strategus to write] … [the] overseer there to send them to Diophanês and if this …
8     [is true, to compel them to return to m]e the share belonging to my father, in order that through you, king, the common sav[iour of all, I may obtain justice.] Farewell.
9     … [at least settle] them; if not, [send] them to us so that we may examine.
10    Year 25, [month] Loios 26, Choiach 13.
