[TRANSLATION]
1     Demophon to Ptolemaios, greeting.
2     I have written below for you (the names) of those from Tholthis
3     [who] have grazed off the royal land in the lower toparchy — the names and
4     the amounts and of which holdings
5     they have used up the pasturage. So you,
6     try as securely as possible
7     to take security, so that no loss may arise afterwards,
8     for I think you … … …
9     … Barkaios, a private person, likewise 17 drachmas 4½ obols,
10    P…ias, a Cyrenaean of the epigone, 9 drachmas 2 obols,
11    Demetrios son of Philon, a Cyrenaean of the
12    [epi]gone, 8 drachmas 5¼ obols,
13    … Teos a shepherd and Petermouthis son of Komoapis, 9 drachmas a quarter-obol,
14    Horos son of Pnas, priest of a sorcerer, 17 (drachmas), half an obol,
15    others, the same man, 2 arouras, 3 drachmas 2 obols,
16    Petosiris son of Phaues and Petosiris
17    son of Pasigonis and Hippolysos, 2¼ arouras, 3 drachmas 5 obols,
18    others, Petosiris son of Auphmoÿs, of bitter-vetch,
19    5 arouras, 5 drachmas 3 obols,
20    … [of the e]pigone
21    [of bitter-vetch, ] 15 [arouras], 2 drachmas …
22    [from the (holding) of Ptol]emaios, Praxias son of Kallidromos, a Cyrenaean of the epigone, of bitter-vetch, 6 arouras at 1 (drachma) 4 obols, 10 drachmas,
23    … o…s, of bitter-vetch, 14 (arouras) at 1 (drachma) 4 obols, 23 drachmas 2 obols,
24    …tos and Harmiysis, shepherds,
25    … of bitter-vetch, 10 arouras, 16 drachmas 4 obols,
