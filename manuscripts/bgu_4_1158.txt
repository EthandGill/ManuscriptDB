[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos,
2   from Olos son of Cornelius Idaios and from Cornelia Tatia daughter of Cor-
3   nelius, with her guardian her maternal uncle Leukios Kallios Paion. There agrees
4   Cornelia, having received from Olos son of Cornelius Idaios, within eight months —
5   from Choiak of the present 22nd year of Caesar — the silver Ptolemaic 80 drachmas which he owes her, interest-free, that she will at once
6   cede back to him, Olos, or to whomever he may direct, through the public archive, that which
7   Cornelia has had ceded to her by him by agreement through the regis-
8   try in the 21st year of Caesar, in Pauni: dry vineyard land of five arouras,
9   or however many they may be, whether more or less, in the lands of Ares
10   the greatest god near the village of Tholthis of the walled district of the Heptako-
11   mia of the Sebennyte (nome), whose neighbors are set out through the agreement,
12   deferring in nothing; and if, when the time has come, Olos does not repay
13   the 80 drachmas of silver, that there remain to Cornelia herself the right and choice either to exact
14   the capital from him or, instead of this, to hold and own the things ceded
15   to her as is set out above, needing no further
16   notice or summons; and if in the meantime it should happen that concerning the
17   land some claim arise, it is nothing against Cornelia, but the
18   debt shall remain free of all risk, and the dues paid to the treasury
19   from now until the repayment of the silver shall be
20   the responsibility of Olos, because the damages fall upon him; and if, Olos being ready
21   having
22   within the aforesaid time to pay down the 80 drachmas of silver, Cornelia does not cede
23   the land back, that it be permitted to him, having deposited at a lawful bank,
24   the 80 drachmas of silver in her own name, to hold and own the five (arouras) as
25   before, it being unencumbered, and apart from the things agreed being valid,
26   the things granted ... (and?) ... 80 ... for the cost that will be incurred, and another
27   200 drachmas of silver as if by judgment. Year 22 of Caesar, Choiak 11.
