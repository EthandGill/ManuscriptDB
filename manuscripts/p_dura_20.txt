1     In the reign of the king of kings Arsakes the benefactor, just, manifest and philhellene, in the year 368 as the king of king[s]
2     reckons, but as formerly 432, in the month Daisios on the twenty-sixth, in Paliga, a village of the hyparchy about Iardas, in the presence of Metolbaissa son of Men…
3     tosde…ou son of Menarnaios, the garrison-commander, and of the foremost and most-honored friends and of the bodyguards, and of t[he]
4     undersigned wit[ness]es. Phraates, eunuch, arkapates, of those (subject) to Manesos son of Phraates, of the batesa and of the
5     freed(men), receiver and strategos of Mesopotamia and Parapotamia and Arabarch, lent to Barlaas son of Thathaios son of Ablaios, of those [from]
6     [the] …erakon village … charaisi, four hundred drachmas of good Tyrian-stamp silver, on the security of all
7     [the propert]y belonging to him … [which] shall also be with the lord. And in place of the interest of the same silver, Barlaas, [remaining together] with [Phraates until the time] of repayment, shall provide him slave-services, doing all the things ordered
8     [him], not [being absent by day nor absent by night without] the consent of Phraates; and if he is absent by day or absent by nig[ht from Phraates],
9     [he] shall pay each [day one drachma] … [bu]t if he falls ill more than seven days, [he, Bar]laas, [shall pay each day on which]
10    [he is] idle [one] drach[ma]; and if he takes [refuge in some sanct]uary, he shall be cast out of the sanctuary and Phra[ate]s shall use h[im] …
11    …, but Ph[raates] shall provide him the daily food and clothing; and of flight and dea[t]h and in[jury and]
12    other dangers the cre[d]itor shall have no part. [And] the aforewritten silver, and if anything else be added from the idle-days, Ba[rl]aas shall repay to Phraates in the month Dais[i]os in the year reckoned as formerly 433; and if he does not repay within the appointed
13    ti[me], the[n e]ven so Barlaas shall be providing to Phraates the same services on the aforesaid terms until the rep[ayme]nt of the s[ilver]; if however, the time having [p]a[s]sed, Phraates wishes to recover the silver, and Barlaas, being asked
14    … to repay the silver, the right of execu[t]ion shall belong to Phraates and to ever[y] one bearing this con[trac]t, from both
15    [Ba]rlao[s and t]he property pledged to him and whatever [o]th[e]r things he may acquire, [the one e]x[a]cting authoritatively the whole except
16    … [wit]h the … . And Barlaas has contracted to renew this contract through the registry-office in Europos whenever he is ordered to [by] Phraates within another five days; [and i]f [he do]es n[o]t renew it, he shall pay to Phraates a penalty of four hundred silver drach[mas, an]d into the royal treasury the like; and this con[tr]act shall even so be valid.
