[TRANSLATION]
1   Sempronius Clemens to Apollinarius,
2   his most honoured (friend), greetings.
3   I received your letters from your (man) Julius
4   Sabinus, from which I learned that you were declaring,
5   not that I was unable to settle the things belonging
6   to you, but that, as you say, I was unwilling. And indeed
7   I felt (the reproach) in this; but nevertheless I will defend myself.
8   For the holdup came about in that Valerianus,
9   being unwilling — his wife having made a counter-claim against him —
10   (refused) to be brought at once before her.
11   Since, this being so, in order that thus in future
12   no other pretext for delay be left,
13   you wrote to me, instructing rather more attentively,
14   I say that, a need pressing, I shall be about to go up
15   to Antinoou (Antinoopolis), and from there
16   I will write to you concerning these things. And if somehow
17   Fortune permits me again to set out hither from
18   Antinoou, it causes no delay. For I will write
19   to you at once after this distraction,
20   so that you may not be anxious about the things belonging to you.
21   For thus I will show you how
22   I will be second to none in attentiveness. —
23   (2nd hand) I pray for your health, with all your household.
