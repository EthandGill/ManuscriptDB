[TRANSLATION]
1     Horos and Ailourion, tax collectors of silver tax of Charakos
2     To Senphaeri of Panatos. We received for the 5th geometric tax of year 2
3     soiled drachmas two, obols three and a half, chalks two: they make drachmas 2, obols 3 and a half, chalks 2; and by name of Petenbdoios
4     Psenamounios through the same, a half-obol, chalks 2: they make a half-obol, chalks 2. Year 3 of Antoninus
5     Caesar our lord, Pachon 29. Heraklas, I have marked [this].
6     Ailourion through Kaleos, I have marked [this].
