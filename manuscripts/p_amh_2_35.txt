[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonios, of the first friends
2     and strategos and in charge of the revenues,
3     from the priests of Soknopaios
4     the great god and of Isis Nepherses
5     the greatest goddess and of the temple-sharing
6     gods, and the royal cultivators
7     of those from Soknopaiou Nesos.
8     On the 18th of Epeiph of the 38th year, watching for his chance when we were occupied in
9     Krokodilopolis, Petesouchos
10    the lesonis of Soknopaios the great god,
11    having gone down to Dionysias and having defrauded our cultivators
12    Teos and Stotoetis, as though sent by us for the receipt of the rents of the sacred
13    land of Soknopaios the great god which they cultivate, having compelled them, at the threshing-floor carried off 225 artabas of wheat to the
14    house of Paalas, concerning which, having petitioned
15    you at Premit on the 22nd of the same
16    month and having summoned
17    Paalas, you have placed under guarantee the
18    wheat of Soknopaios the great god,
19    concerning which Petesouchos the lesonis has given a written declaration on the royal oath that he
20    will not lay hold of the rents of the land
21    in any way, in the agreement that took place
22    between us and him for the
23    office of lesonis of the 38th year, and having transgressed the terms of the written declaration. Since therefore
24    you have been preserved in your illness by
25    Soknopaios the great god and Isis Nepherses the greatest goddess and the temple-sharing
26    gods, we request, if it seem good, that you order our memorandum to be filed
27    with you in the record-office, with a view to the suit that we shall have against Petesouchos the lesonis,
28    so that it may not be permitted to him to lay hold
29    of the wheat, and that you write to Apollonios the epistates to bring him before you for the settlement of these matters,
30    so that, if these things are
31    thus, we may recover the 225 artabas of wheat for the
32    god's account, and concerning the violence and written undertaking that he has committed, to take cognizance
33    concerning him
34    in hatred of evildoers, for
35    the attention of others,
36    so that we may be able to perform
37    the customary rites to the gods on behalf
38    both of the king and
39    of the royal children,
40    having obtained
41    the assistance from y[o]u.
42    Farewell.
43    Farewell.
44    Year 38,
45    Epeiph 22.
