[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemaios [son] of Ptolemaios, Macedonian of the succeeding [generation]
2     [To D]emetrios [son] of Sos, Cretan of the former [generation]
3     Eumelos [the] regular-salaried [employee], greetings. I acknowledge
4     [that I have] received from you the assigned salary from the royal [treasury] to the twins …
5     sesame oil measures 2 of both the 18th year
6     and 19th year, on which [I am satisfied and will not] come against you and neither another [on] behalf of me nor on behalf of the
7     twins; [and] if anyone [should] come against you,
8     I shall turn him away forcibly and mercilessly and my recourse shall be void and I shall pay back to you both the amount which I have from you
9     with one and a half [times the amount] and sacred [obligations] to the king and
10    to the queen silver drachmas twenty.
11    [Of] year 20 Hathyr 27.
12    To Ptolemaios [his] brother, greetings.
