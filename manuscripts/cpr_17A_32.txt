[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Asklepiades son of Adelphios, former magistrate,
2     councillor and [praepositus] of the 15th pagus of the Hermopolite (nome),
3     from the Aurelii Horos son of Ammonianos, his mother being Tisois, about
4     51 years old, with a scar on the little finger of the left hand, from
5     the village of Admentha, and Klomis son of Achilleus,
6     his mother being Kollauchis, about …2 years old, with a scar on the forefinger
7     of the right hand, from the hamlet of Tiberius in the Hermopolite (nome).
8     We acknowledge, swearing by the divine fortune of
9     our all-conquering lords the Augusti, that voluntarily
10    and of our own accord we have given surety for the Aurelii Horos son of Kastor and Nenis son of Paesis, from the village of Prektis
11    of your pagus, that we will produce the recruit assigned to them for presentation before the
12    higher authority, without any delay or contradiction.
13    And if they fall short in the matter of his presentation,
14    we ourselves will assume the responsibility on their behalf, or else we shall be liable to the divine oath and to the penalty
15    concerning this. [And on being formally questioned we acknowledged it. In the consulship] of Septimius Acindynus
16    and Po[plonius Proculus, the] most illustrious, Choiak 14.
17    We, the aforesaid, [give surety and] will produce (him) as set forth.
18    I, Aurelius … [wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.]
