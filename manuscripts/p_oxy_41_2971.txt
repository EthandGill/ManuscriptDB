[TRANSLATION]
1     Taseus daughter of Heraklas, with her guardian her own full brother Seuthes, and Seuthes son of Diogenes, agree with one another,
2     son of Dionysius, a weaver — on the one hand Taseus,
3     that she has apprenticed to Seuthes her own son
4     Heraklas son of Apollo…, who is not yet
5     of (full) age, for the learning of the weaver's craft, for two years and
6     six months from the present day, doing all the things assigned to him
7     by Seuthes according to the weaver's craft. And Seuthes himself will also teach
8     him as he himself knows, the boy
9     being fed and clothed by
10    the teacher Seuthes; and the same Seuthes will settle on the boy's behalf the
11    craftsman's tax (cheironaxion) together with the assessment for the
12    two and a half years. And if also during the
13    same period the boy is demanded for poll-tax or the embankment-tax or the pig-tax, it will be
14    the responsibility of the same Seuthes. And the same
15    Seuthes, if the boy does not wish to be fed by
16    the teacher, shall supply to Taseus for his maintenance each month, of silver,
17    five drachmas; and after the period
18    he shall give to the boy when he departs a tunic
19    worth twelve drachmas, or those very
20    [twelve drachmas]. And the boy shall be idle
21    … at the Amesysia festival.
22    … but for as many days as the boy is idle beyond these, for the equal (number) Taseus shall provide him after the period, or let her pay one drachma for each day;
23    and for being withdrawn before the period
24    a penalty of one hundred drachmas of silver, and to the public treasury
25    the like sum. And if the same Seuthes does not teach the boy, let him be liable to the equal
26    penalty. The apprenticeship-contract is valid. Year 12
27    of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
28    Germanicus Imperator, Phamenoth 15.
29    Seuthes son of Diogenes: I will teach
30    the boy and will do as
31    is set forth. Year 12 of Nero Claudius
32    Caesar Augustus Germanicus
33    Imperator, Phamenoth 15.
34    Apprenticeship-contract.
