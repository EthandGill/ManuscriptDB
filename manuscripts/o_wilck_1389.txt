[TRANSLATION]
1     There has paid, Pamonthes (son of) Nechthauis,
2     for date-groves of year 4, (assessment) 16 32 64´, two dr. 1 obol,
3     total 2 dr. 1 obol and the surcharges on these. Year 5 of Nero
4     Claudius Caesar Augustus
5     Germanicus Imperator,
6     in the month Neos Sebastos 9. Apion, I have signed.
