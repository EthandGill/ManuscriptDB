[TRANSLATION]
1     … we [acknowledge that we have leased] from you the …
2     forty arouras, more or less, around Memphis, for as long a time
3     as you wish, from the crops of the auspicious incoming ninth
4     indiction, on condition that of the produce arising from these very arouras,
5     of the pulse, to you the landlord two parts and to us the
6     farmers one part, and of the hay, to you the landlord five parts
7     and to us one part; and that we shall of necessity perform all the works toward the
8     good cultivation of the arouras irreproachably, in addition to our
9     also making the binding of all the hay and the cutting of the
10    hay, each according to the parts, the all-kinds of four-footed beasts and the advance of seed being charged to you the landlord, the seed being set down (to your account), and the threshing and sheaf-carrying being from common (expense). And we shall give
11    eight kids for the milk according to the Lord's (day) … the lease (being) valid, and having been formally questioned we acknowledged (it). Aurelii Amaeios, son of Dioskoros,
12    and his son Philoxenos, vine-dressers, the aforesaid, have leased
13    the aforewritten arouras as set forth. Aurelius Paulos, son of Philippos, from the
14    [city] of the Arsinoites, wrote on behalf of them, they being illiterate.
