[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of] our masters Constantine Augustus the fourth and Constantius Augustus the third.
2     To Aurelius Nilus, [son] of Hermus, councilor of the great
3     Hermoupolis,
4     from Aurelius Silvanus, [son] of Castor, from the village
5     of Thoteis. I wish willingly and of my own accord
6     [to] lease from you for the present year alone the fruits of the fortunate [incoming sixth indiction] the fields
7     four in total at the place called Penapaste, for
8     [the] deposit of linen-reed, [for] a fixed fee of each
9     [field] of [a] sheaf [of grain] suitable grain-measure of one and a half [measures], which [having been] collected,
10    the fee I will deliver to you and will weigh [it]
11    [in the month of] Phaophi in the same year without delay in [a] sheaf [of] suitable weight, [in the] public weight without delay, [with] all public [things] and annona and temporary impositions being [yours] toward you, the land-holder. The lease is valid and having been questioned I agreed.
12    [In the] consulship aforementioned, Choiak [26].
13    Aurelius Nilus of Hermu[s, the] aforementioned [have] leased
14    as stated above.
