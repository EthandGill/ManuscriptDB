1     And before the letters of your distinguished wisdom [I was moved]
2     to [my] coming to you at night and planning with you
3     and when I reached the [Serapeum] came the administrator of
4     there and said to me some [message] from the most-magnificent ruler
5     and prevented me from coming into the city. And immediately I went up to a place
6     Poureh, the most-glorious illu[strious] sent someone to me a manager
7     Sarapammon and some [things] he wrote to me the things having compelled me to go up [to]
8     him. I went up so that I might do reverence to him and immediately return. He did not
9     release me until now saying that if he does not hold the celebration near me, he will by no means
10    release you, and I was not able to grieve him. I beseech you therefore, if the most-magnificent [one]
11    [asks], press him until I hold the celebration, and after the celebration I come so that
12    in person I may do reverence to you. But I wrote to Philoxenus so that he gives you the
13    [Kalends-payment] as [a reckoning?] of your household slaves. Deign therefore to receive these without any …
14    and to you many times and well. These I write with the respect owed to you from me
15    in reverence.
16    To the master, all-glorious … Theodorus
