[TRANSLATION]
1     Having been scrutinized: the phylarch. Man by man of those scrutinized, as they are also in the public library
2     they being the ones examined and answering at the proclamation
3     under whom (are) Romans and Alexandrians.
4     The phylarch. Man by man of the admitted who have been registered, and of those who have advanced from the register of minors
5     … accordingly with the public library, they being the ones examined
6     and answering at the proclamation.
7     The vagrants (rhemboi). Man by man of those who have performed liturgies
8     and of the freedmen who have performed liturgies, accordingly with the public library, they being the ones examined and at
9     the proclamation answering.
10    A certain Aurelius, scrutineer. The aforesaid were scrutinized, and they are themselves the very ones examined
11    and answering at the proclamation.
12    A certain Aurelius, witness (gnoster). I certify that the aforesaid are so circumstanced
13    and that they themselves are the ones also answering at the proclamation.
14    … phylarch, liturgy …
15    [A certain Aurelius,] phylarch. The aforesaid were scrutinized, and they are themselves the very ones examined
16    [and at] the proclamation answering.
17    [A certain Aurelius,] phylarch. The aforesaid are admitted, and having advanced from the register of minors
18    [as they] are also in the library, and they themselves are the ones examined and at the
19    [proclama]tion they answer.
20    The vagrants (rhemboi). A certain Aurelius, phylarch. The aforesaid performed liturgies and are so circumstanced
21    and (are) in the library, and they themselves are the ones examined and answering at the proclamation.
