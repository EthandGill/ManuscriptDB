[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapiōn, also called Apollōnianos, stratēgos
2     of [the] Arsinōite [nome], of the divisions of Themistus and Polemon
3     from Ptollas, called Philadelphos, from [the] village
4     Dionusiados, being also a presbyter
5     of the same village and [a] public [officer]
6     and estate-farmer. Since the village-secretary of Philōterida has now assigned me to oversight of [the] property being registered
7     of the aforementioned village Philōterida, [I being] entirely destitute and possessing nothing concerning the village
8     and not farming anything concerning it, I request,
9     not being able to fulfill both tasks according as also to our lords
10    our Autocrats seemed [best], not [to perform] two liturgies at one time, I request, [as] a poor man barely able to fulfill the presbytery and the farming, which is most heavy, to be relieved of the
11    oversight and to order another
12    to the village-secretary of Philōterida to enter [in place of] me, so that I might be able unobstructed to direct also the results of the farming.
13    Ptollas, as [being] about 53 years old, scar on the left shinbone.
14    Year 16 of Lucius Septimius Severus, pious
15    Pertinax and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
16    pious Sebastoi and Publius Septimius
17    Geta Caesar Augustus, Phamenōth 19.
