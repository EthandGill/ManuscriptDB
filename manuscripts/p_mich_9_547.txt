[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Sempronius and
2     Agathinus and the remaining administrators of palm-fronds to Aurelius Ptollarion, village-chief of Karanidus, greetings. We have received from you on behalf of the same
3     village, one palm-frond; [which] comes to palm-frond 1.
4     Aurelius Sempronius wrote this entire [document].
5     year 14 and 13 and 6, Payni 21.
