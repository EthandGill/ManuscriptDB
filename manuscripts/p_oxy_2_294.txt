[TRANSLATION]
1   The inquiry […]
2   Sarapion to Dorion [his brother, greet-]
3   ing and continual good health. [Since I ar-]
4   rived in Alexandria on the [… of the under-]
5   written month, I learned [from certain]
6   fishermen … to Alexandria […] that
7   Sa…illa(?) … […]
8   from me in the court, and the house [of]
9   Sekonda has been searched, and […]
10   my house has been searched, and […]
11   and … whether these things are really so.
12   You will do well, then, to write me a reply
13   about this, so that I myself may present a pe-
14   tition to the prefect. Do not do otherwise. I
15   myself have not even anointed myself until I hear
16   word from you about everything. And I am being
17   pressed by friends to become a household-man of the chief
18   usher Apollonios, so that I may come with him to the in-
19   quiry. The deputy of the stra-
20   tegos and Justus the sword-bearer are in cus-
21   tody, as the prefect ordered, until
22   the inquiry — unless they persuade the chief
23   usher to give security for them until the in-
24   quiry. About Phalakros, write me how
25   he is again being coddled up there. Do not do other-
26   wise. And I told Diogenes your friend not to wrong
27   me … regarding the expense of what he has of mine;
28   for … with the chief usher. And I ask you
29   and entreat you, write me a reply about
30   what has happened. Before all, take care of
31   yourself, that you stay well. Look after Demetrous
32   and Dorion our father. Farewell.
33   Year 9 of Tiberius Caesar [Augustus, Cho]iak 15.
34   Deliver to Dorion my brother.
