[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelii Dioscorus of Chairemon and Philadelph[us]
2     and Serenos and Ischyrion to Aurelius Aune of Mikkalo, greetings. Pnas Anoufios has measured on behalf of year 17 and year 16 and year 9
3     on behalf of public [dues] of the village of Karanis
4     of chaff, pounds 150.
5     year 18 and 17 and 10, Thoth 9.
