[TRANSLATION]
1     Harmaïs to Zenon, greetings. You do not fail to realize that we provide for needs, receiving neither grain-measure …
2     nor provisions receiving, but being sustained at … [you would do] well, if also it seems good to you, coming to help us concerning …
3     … may record you in no (list). For it happens in the other … [us]
4     to provide needs for the embankments and the rest. [I have written] [to Apollonius]
5     a memorandum, of which a copy is written below. And don't blame m[e] …
6     to arrive; for I am with Lysimach about the registration being made of the bodi[es].
7     Farewell. [In the year 28] …
8     [To A]pollonius, manager, greetings, Harmaïs. Throughout the city of Memphis are the sections [divided]
9     embankments [of] rope 100. Of these, Syrian-Persian [of] rope 12, Paasu 7, those above [of]
10    Hephaestus rampart and those below 4, those in-city with the royal(s) 23, Karikon …, …
11    Hellenion 3, beyond Memphis those toward southwest of the royal garden 20 and toward [the east] …
12    and toward north [rope] 5, cubits 30. And for the filling of these embankments was given in th[e 28th year]
13    talent 1, drachmas 295, [the] rise having occurred, cubits 10, palms 3, finger 1 [6], and in the 27th year was given
14    talent 1, drachmas 180 [of/to]
15    (the) river having risen, cubits 10, palms 6, fingers 2 [5/6]. Now then I undertake to you to fill the same embankments …
16    … from the foundation of the embankments up to the height of cubits 12, pleasing to the manager …
17    and to the architect, receiving from (the) royal [treasury] talent 1. And there will be provided to us
18    according to the usual (practice) vessels which again we will restore.
19    Farewell.
20    Harmaïs concerning the things according to
21    Memphis concerning embankments.
22    Of the year 28, Peritios 18,
23    in Bubastis.
24    To Zenon
