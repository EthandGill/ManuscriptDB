[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Thomas son of Anoups, from the village Embolos of the Arsinoite nome, [do acknowledge receipt from] you Paul, goldsmith, son of Theodore, from the city of the Arsinoites
2     city, and [I have] satisfied the price of acceptable grain [of] four artabas, [of] grain [of] four artabas. And I will make the return of them to you
3     in the month Epeiph, [by] just measure in the village from the current crops of the tenth indiction; but if I deny [this obligation], you have authority
4     to demand in exchange for the price of crops [in] gold coins, half as is customary, it comes to gold coins [half] as is customary. It was written in the month Tybi [of the ... ] ninth indiction. By me Elias, notary, it was completed.
