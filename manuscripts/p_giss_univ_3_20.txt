[TRANSLATION]
1     … letters of recommendation to the epistrategos, lest
2     it ever happen to come about in your presence. And I gave instructions
3     al[so to A]ntoninus the soldier, who was in the epis[t]rategate of Vindex with us, and now has sailed up also with the epistrategos, so that in the whole
4     [m]atter he may stand by you. None the less even now I have
5     [se]nt you to him a little letter. Of the sla[ve]s I have not found the contracts up to now.
6     For absolutely n[eit]her was the purchase from you to Achilleus found w[it]h m[e]
7     nor the one from Achilleus to Eumoiron.
8     And Achill[eu]s is away abroad, and over the matter
9     I am at a loss, not being accustomed to throw away any
10    document at all, especially the more necessary ones. But
11    also the one who bought from you the other little slave, a certain petty orator before the jurisdiction-officer, having often appointed the time to tell us
12    of the pur[cha]se, has behaved unfairly. If then you ar[e ab]le
13    to remember the t[im]e well and through what office it was registered, make it clear to me
14    and se[ar]ch in your documents whether it is the purchase to
15    Apol[lo]narion. And if you know
16    through what scribe the registration to Achilleus from you
17    has been [made], write to me; for it was not found w[ith P]otamon the scribe. And so that
18    in the meantime no one trouble you, I have written
19    to the scribe of the strategos commending
20    little Herakleides and making clear about the
21    matter, so that he may devise something else
22    until I send the contracts
23    or come in person. The letter about Sarapion declare, as your own success, to those who inquire of you and to Sarapion
24    himself, taking up also the magnitude of the matter. And if for good
25    you sail down, bring an excellent
26    couch, so that we may send (it) to Philos, who has eagerly
27    presented himself to us, or whatever
28    else you approve. I pray that you be in good health,
29    sister. Embrace
30    Herakleides. Ptolemaios greets all.
31    Mecheir 28.
