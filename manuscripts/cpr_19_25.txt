[TRANSLATION]
1     From Mena son of Damianus, Phageb, for public and other [taxes], canon 11, indiction, month Phamenoth 16, through Cosmas
2     Papheout, currency 1/2, 3, 24, one-half, third, twenty-fourth, only; Bartholomaeus deacon; another Phamenoth 24, through Zacharius Syncritius pall[akia]
3     currency 3, 24, 48, third, twenty-fourth, forty-eighth, only; another Pachon 5, through the wife of Zacharius Syncritius pall[akia], currency 24, 48, twenty-fourth, forty-eighth, [eighth] [only] [] …
4     another Pachon 9, through Aret, currency 1/2, only; another Pachon 21, through Aret, currency 3, third, only; another Pachon 28, through Aret, currency 4, fourth, only
5     From the same for a share of payment of sailors, canon 11, indiction, Pachon 5, currency 8, eighth, only; Bartholomaeus deacon
6     From the same likewise for the 8th part of the price of order, canon 11, indiction, month Pachon 5, currency 8, 48, eighth, forty-eighth, eighth, only
