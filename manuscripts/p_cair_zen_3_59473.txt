[TRANSLATION]
1     From Neon. Look after
2     concerning Seuthes son of Teres, a Thracian,
3     who owes 30 artabas of wheat
4     and is in Onnis,
5     and Amphia, a Thracian,
6     who owes 300 artabas of barley.
7     He indeed has died, there are
8     his capable sons
9     and they are in Komae.
10    Neon's letter.
