[TRANSLATION]
1     To Soterichos, the god [and lord],
2     from Horos and ...os, both
3     (sons) of Theos, and Pet[e]ar..., priests of the village
4     of Bousiris. Quite early, on the .. [o]f the pre-
5     sent month Pauni of the 31st year of Caes[ar],
6     as the priests in purity were passing
7     to the liturgies and
8     sacrifices of the gods, we found the
9     doors of the temple of Sarapis, greatest god,
10    set alight with fire. And when we
11    cried out, into the village,
12    for help, there ran down
13    the gymnasiarch of the village
14    and the elders and all the rest,
15    and they found, in part, the
16    doors burnt down, and they
17    found the pivot of the one
18    leaf [o]f the door lifted off by ha-
19    nd. There[fore] we report (it) by a re-
20    port, that you may know. Farewell.
