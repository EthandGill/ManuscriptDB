[TRANSLATION]
1     … the aforementioned quantity. And if anything is lacking, he shall accept of the same
2     price; and on these terms, when we had agreed and I was giving my deposit,
3     he ordered 200 bronze drachmas to be counted out to Glauke his wife, which also
4     I immediately gave. And as security for the remainder of the price I appointed as surety
5     a certain Amennes, one of the merchants there, whom he himself also chose; who undertook to repay him the balance by the 30th of Mesore, when I had carried off the wine,
6     and from this wine, having at once taken out 2 jars, I sealed up both the
7     storeroom and the key. But when the time was at hand and we were paying him the rest, so that having received the cargo we might dispose of it, he paid no heed, at one moment saying that I was to make the contract first, at another that I had bought it by registration, doing these things to ruin me and being unwilling
8     to deliver the wine. I beg you, therefore, O king, if it seems good to you, to order Peitholaos
9     the strategos, having summoned Kleitos, to investigate, and if indeed I appear
10    to have purchased on these terms, to compel him to deliver the wine to me. But if he disputes that I bought by registration, to give me the pledges in the Herakleion
11    and to deliver the wine, so that, having taken refuge with you, O king, the helper and benefactor of all,
12    I may obtain justice. Farewell.
13    To Zoilos they said that Kleitos was not in residence.
