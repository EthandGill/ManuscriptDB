[TRANSLATION]
1   Hatres to Heras his brother, greeting.
2   As you instructed me about Serenus
3   the fuller, the one working with
4   Phileas: if you have need of him, send
5   an attendant for him today, that
6   is the 19th. {For I am holding him} But
7   see that you do not neglect it, for I am hold-
8   ing him.
9   I pray that you fare well and prosper.
10   Deli(ver) to [He]ras.
