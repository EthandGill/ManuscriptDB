[TRANSLATION]
1     to Aurelius Heron, strategos of Arsinoe
2     from Aurelii Taesis and Kyrillutos both
3     Kopres from village of Karanides. our [own]
4     father, best of the strategoi, having ended life
5     left movable property. but his brother Chairemon all that [he] left [he] embezzled
6     and provided us the women [with] grain-lands public
7     arouras to those not being able to meet the taxes
8     of the arouras. we approached also then the former village-chief of the village Serenus also called
9     Harpocrates and [he] ordered him all the [things] left behind by our father these to us
10    to provide. but he [shows] no concern for us
11    shows, and on account of this very thing [concerning] the left-behind [things we] beseech and entreat your benevolence so that you may order to him Chairemon
12    these to us to provide so that we may be able [to have] benefit
13    to have of our [own]. prosper well. but the property [items] are:
14    [sheep] perfect 61, goats perfect 40, grinding-machine 1 and of silver talents 3 and of grain
15    artabae 2 and slave-bodies two from which [he] sold [a] slave [one].
