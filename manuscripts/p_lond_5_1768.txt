[TRANSLATION]
1     … indiction, the two whole sitting-rooms (exedrai) belonging to her, the one within the other, the
2     one outward, looking toward the north onto the open court (aithra),
3     the other inward, looking toward the east, with
4     their share of the well and the courtyard and
5     the terrace, and one vaulted room in the ground floor
6     looking toward the east, and the chaff-store
7     and all manner of appurtenances and rights, from
8     a house lying in this (city) of the Hermopolites, in the quarter of Phrourion Libos, in the lane
9     of Pakouk, looking toward the north, for my use
10    and dwelling, the rent of these
11    per year being six carats, total 6 carats, which
12    rent I will pay to you at the close
13    of each year without delay, after the manner
14    of the other tenants; and whenever you wish
15    to have (them back), I will hand over to you the same places with their doors
16    in the same [condition] as I received them. The lease
17    is valid and se[cure, an]d on being formally questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius Elias
18    son of Pkylios the af[oresaid, I] have leased as is stated above.
19    Aurelius Herm…, son of Menas, from Hermopolis, having been requested,
20    I wrote on [his behalf the lett]er[s], since he does not know (them). Aurelius
21    Ioannes son of Menas, [from Hermopolis, I bear witness to] the lease, having heard from the
22    one making it. [Aurelius] Phoibammon son of Agathos,
23    [from Hermopolis,] I bear wi[tness to the leas]e, having heard from the one making it.
24    … son of Pkylios, cultivator(?), from Hermopolis, for rent of [6] carats.
