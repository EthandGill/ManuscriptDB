[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Opportunus the m[o]st illustrious, Thoth the twenty-eighth, of the third indiction.
2     To Aurelia Eucharistia daughter of Theodoros, the most noble, from the Hermopolite (city), from Sarapion son of Hermogenes,
3     subdeacon of the catholic church of the same city. I agree, willingly and of my own free will,
4     to have leased from your nobility for only a single year, of the crops of the coming with God
5     fourth indiction, the one share of newly-planted land belonging to your nobility, that is, the (one)
6     lying to the west of the hamlet in the plain of the village of Ibion Sesembytheos of the Hermopolite
7     nome, for water-supply through our ox-animals and their fodder and herdsmen,
8     receiving, however, from your nobility, on account of the wages for the same share for the same year, seven gold solidi;
9     and I shall water the same plot,
10    in winter every twelve days, and in summer every eight days.
11    On condition that I lease from your nobility also of arable land three arourai
12    from the w[es]t of the same hamlet, [fo]r sowing and de[posi]t of crops, [of which]ever I choose, the rent for these of the same year (being)
13    [of go]ld a half so[lid]us, [according to] the exchange-rate [of th]e same cit[y]. [The] lease is valid and secure, and, questioned,
14    I agreed.
