[TRANSLATION]
1     To Luckius O[phell]ianus
2     most excellent strategos
3     from Marcus Anthestius Gemellus Nervianing [one] of the out-of-district grain-storage district
4     being proclaimed. having arrived I, lord, here toward
5     [the] harvest collection [toward] [so as] to direct the public [business], but until now having completed nothing, I request, if it
6     [seems good] to you, to allot additionally to me with
7     … [departure] the being away
8     [me and] other thirty days [so that] I may be benefited. fare well.
