[TRANSLATION]
1     [The neighbours are made cl]ear through the securities concerning it, and likewise in [the same city, one aroura]
2     in the plain of Marion, on the west side, belonging to the daughter of Dioskoros, son of Kalykas, [purchas]ed by me
3     while it was [dry] land and planted at my own expenses, of which also the neighbours, through
4     the securities concerning it, are made clear; and the aforesaid I held by right of prior purchase
5     [in the] 5th year of Probus Augustus, Pachon 3, from Aurelius Philapollon [son of Phi]lapollon, through the
6     [public] notary in most glorious Alexandria, Aurelius Didymos, by means of Aurelius Isidoros, public notary. I have sol[d] to you, by purchase and sale, at the price agreed upon with you for each of the two, of silver, of Ptolemaic coinage, two talents, which [I rece]ived from yo[u through] me,
7     [the] aforesaid father, Aurelius Eudaimon, in full, through the hand, so that from now on
8     [you] may own them and have control and manage them as you may choose, gathering in the
9     [reve]nues and paying the public dues; and I will guarantee them to you with every guarantee at every time
10    [against] everyone who shall come against them. And this deed of the sale, written in duplicate in my own hand, I have given out to you, and let it be valid and secure as if registered in a public office; and whenever you wish I will produce it for you according to a public transaction; and being asked whether these things have rightly and well been done thus, I agreed.
11    Year 6 of our lord Probus Augustus, Pharmouthi.
12    [Aurel]ius Hierax also called Eustephios, councillor of the most glorious city of the Alexandrians, through me, Aurelius Eudaimon, son of Serenos, councillor of the same, have sold the aforesaid tenth and thirtieth share of a house and one aroura, and I have received the price, two talents of silver … these things, and I have been formally asked, as is set forth above.
