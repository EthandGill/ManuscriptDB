[TRANSLATION]
1     … s. For I thought that [he] did not depart to Lykon, but remained
2     … and knowing the intention of the villagers, that they wished to bargain, I remained there
3     oppressed, until I received from her letters, how [he] lives in Lykon. Do not seem to you then
4     to withdraw [for the] most illustrious commander, but if it seems [right] to [him] to go up, go up with [him]. Which I think,
5     [cannot] bear now to go up, until complete settlement happens. Concerning your writing,
6     that the son of the kadis Kolotse went up to Munaei and was fought there and many of the
7     [followers] of him they slaughtered and he returned to [An]tinoopolis and did not dare still to go up.
