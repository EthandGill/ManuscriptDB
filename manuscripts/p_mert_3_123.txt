[TRANSLATION]
1     I will repay … in the month Pauni on [the threshing-floor]
2     new, pure, unmixed, the measurement having been made through one of yours, and after
3     the time I will hand over the fields clean from reed-trash [and] wild grass, [having been] stripped
4     all the reed [stalk] from cutting
5     if it appears [that one is] to rent.
6     Herakleia of Achilleus with [her] lord husband
7     [A]pollonius of Lourios, I have rented upon the
8     aforementioned [terms], as it is set forth. Apollonius, I wrote, and on behalf of the
9     wife of mine [who is] illiterate. Year 14 of Antoninus Caesar[s]
10    of the lord, Epeiph 3.
