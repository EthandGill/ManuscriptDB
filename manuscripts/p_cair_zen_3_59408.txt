[TRANSLATION]
1     [A] memorandum
2     To Zenon from [Asklepiades].
3     You will do well,
4     concerning which Erenaeus commanded to you,
5     giving to me the travel-fund,
6     so that I might be carried back toward him
7     and [so that it] not seem that I
8     [am] losing courage about him. And
9     [he] sent a messenger to me calling me.
10    What then do you order to me?
11    Farewell.
