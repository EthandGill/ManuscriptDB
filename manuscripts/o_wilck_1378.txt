[TRANSLATION]
1     There has paid, Paorpkos (son of) Sachomneus,
2     for poll-tax of year 3, of the Memnoneia, 16 dr.; likewise for
3     the enkyklion and other (taxes), 2 dr.; likewise for baths,
4     2 dr. Year 3 of Tiberius Claudius
5     Caesar Augustus Germanicus
6     Imperator, Mesore 22.
7     (2nd hand) Likewise, month Soter 13, for
8     embankment-tax, 3 dr. 4½ obols; likewise (the) 17th, for
9     embankment-tax, 2 dr. 5½ obols.
