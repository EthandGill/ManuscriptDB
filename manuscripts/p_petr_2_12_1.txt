[TRANSLATION]
1     Agenor to Theodoros, greeting. Of the letter written to me by Aphthon[eto]s, under which also
2     [the memorandum from Andronikos to Aphthon]etos [is sub]scribed, I have sent you the copy
3     … osto … un … sus …
4     … mos … t … n … unt … house, altar…
5     … tos ka … tis … Aphthonetos, since kli…
6     … i … i … n … s … ioi … i … ap … oiai … e … asao … e … as of the altars, so as not to be billeted upon
7     … write to us. Farewell. Year 6, Artemisios 22.
8     Aphthonetos. Of the memorandum brought forward from Andronikos I have sent you the copy.
9     Having examined, then, whether these things are so, accomplish (matters) accordingly. Year 6, Artemisios 2 …
10    Memorandum to Aphthonetos the strategos from Andronikos. We find in Krokodilon polis that [some]
11    of the houses formerly billeted upon have had their roofs demolished by the owners,
12    and likewise, having also built up the doors of the houses, they have built altars against (them). And this
13    they have done so as not to be billeted upon. If, then, it seems good to you, since we are cramped for billets,
14    write to Agenor to compel the owners of the houses to move the altars onto
15    the most convenient and most conspicuous places on the roofs and to rebuild
16    (them) better than the pre-existing altars, so that we may be able to assign (billets) to the overseers of the works now arriving.
17    Year 6, Choiach 9, we received (it).
18    From A[g]e[nor]
19    to [Theodo]ros.
