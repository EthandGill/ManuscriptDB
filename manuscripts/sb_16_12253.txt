1     Account from the village of Anoup ...
2     Thus.
3     ... from which through the various person[s] ...
4     Through Naaraus [as] deacon ...
5     There come to be through Pousi Alli arouras 3 ammata 35 12.
6     Together arouras 8 ammata 37 cubits 7.

12    There come to be of Athious Phobammōn Kanp for [his] wife.
13    Arouras 3 ammata 50 6.
14    Together the same arouras 18 58 [half] 4.
