[TRANSLATION]
1   Has paid: Apollos son of Petemenophis,
2   for poll-tax of year 21, ten drachmas and bath-tax one,
3   an obol, a half-obol — total 11 dr. 1½ ob. — and the surcharges at an obol (and) half-obol.
4   Year 21 of Tiberius Caesar
5   Augustus, Pharmouthi 28.
6   Petemenophis son of Pikos.
