1   Apollodoros, collector,
2   to Pamonthes son of Harbechis.
3   He received, for dike-tax and bath-tax
4   of the Charax (quarter), year 3, in base (coin), seven drachmas, 3 obols, 2 chalkoi.
5   Year 3 of Hadrian Caesar
6   the lord, Hathyr 28.
7   Porieuthes(?), I have signed.
