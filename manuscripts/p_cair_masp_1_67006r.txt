[TRANSLATION]
1     A peti[ti]on and supplicat[ion] from Maria from the village [of S]abbes of the Theodosiopolite nome.
2     [The greatest benefacti]on lies before all who have been wronged: the protection of your gl[o]rious
3     surpassing-eminence. I am wronged beyond all telling, and I, the wretched widow, supporting m[a]ny orphan minor sons, and never … according to the sowing of wheat
4     … neither [f]rom my parents and ances[t]ors having this innate (skill), and so the leading men of my village of Sabbes have proceeded against me by force, wishing to impose upon my widowhood arou[r]as that are turned over … to the resourceless (woman)
5     … together with my orphan children, who also lack the [neces]sary food, to give account for these not being sown ever, the public (dues) and
6     all burdens; wherefore I beseech the [ph]ilanthropic height of you[rs]
7     [if it should be poss]ib[le] to order that I be not, contrary to custom and the usage of my parents and of all my kind, dragged into such an agricultural duty and care of slavery, of which I am inexperienced and in which I am incapable, the part … nai
8     …poro of the unceasing (toil), so that I may not even find (myself forced) to mortgage my small plots, so that, having obtained this benefaction, I may send up prayers of thanksgiving and hymns to the all-creator God and [s]av[i]our Christ on [be]half of the [continuance] as … alone
9     … [of the phi]lanthropic [h]eight of yours an[d] of your sweetest children continually, master
10    a petition and supplic[a]tion from Maria
