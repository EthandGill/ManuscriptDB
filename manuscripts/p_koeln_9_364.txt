[TRANSLATION]
1     … Ctesipp[o] Ptolemy, to all upon the scene, greetings. You do well if
2     you fare well. And I too fare well. Having come to Androteles, I found
3     all in good health and Berenice the daughter having given birth to a female and
4     everything according to reason has come[to pass] for her. I say then, just as you asked me to announce most quickly, so that you and Ptolemy [might] prepare the good news which you promised in Sacred [Island] to give, so that coming I might bring [them].
5     Take care [also] of Androteles the father, and doing this you will gratify me.
6     Year 16, Artemisios 6.
7     To Ctesippo.
