[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the year …] of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus and of the Emperor Caesar Lucius Aurelius Verus Augustus, Tybi the second.
2     In Euhemeria of the Themistes division of the Arsinoite nome. Thaisas, daughter of Kastor son of Paion, her mother being
3     …s, registered at the house-by-house census of the 23rd year of the deified Aelius Antoninus in the quarter of the Places of Lysanias, about fifty-six years old,
4     with scars on her left shin and by her left eyebrow, with her guardian, her own husband Herodes … about … years old, with a scar on his right shin, acknowledges that the declarant
5     Thaisas has agreed that, after her own death, there shall belong to the daughter of them both, Diodora also called Koprous, and to their other son Mystharion … the (children) of her husband Herodes: to the daughter Diodora also called Koprous, from the property belonging to her, as she says maternal, near the village of
6     Philoteris, purchased … [near the village of Dionysias] a fourth part of a vegetable-garden of two arouras, together with all that is in it,
7     according to the same fourth part, and in the same village of Dionysias, in the quarter of Herminos, a fourth part of a house … and two slave-persons, Sarapous and Sotas, ten months old,
8     house-born, from the mother Ammonilla, on condition that Ammonilla, after the weaning of the
9     … for another six months, shall also hand over (them) to Diodora; and to the son Mystharion, who was not well-disposed toward her during her life and did not
10    stand by her in her illness, but in order that …
11    … the village of the Themistes division, a half share of a catoecic allotment of five and a half and an eighth and a sixteenth arouras, 5½ 1/8 1/16,
12    in the place called Tameous, and a half share of the …
13    … a half share, and slave-persons, Boubastarion and her children Deion also called Epagathos and Helene also called Tyche, on condition that Mystharion shall repay … if it be shown …
14    … as aforesaid, of the slave-persons, and in all cases at the aroura-rate by seals, or however much it may be. Furthermore
15    Thaisas also agrees, after her own death, … under (the pledge of) Zeus, Earth, Sun, and through the sales-tax and whatever else is fitting, and to consent to her future (transaction) through the
16    course appointed in the metropolis,
17    [by the herald's proclamation] … six, and all the taxes and expenses of the manumission being (chargeable) to
18    Ammonilla; and the same Ammonilla (to be) a servant of the …
19    … account … neither … nor to bring a claim nor to raise a dispute nor to proceed against;
20    …, and if she should proceed against or bring a claim, the attack and claim (shall be) void in every part.
