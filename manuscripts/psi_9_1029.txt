[TRANSLATION]
1     Tausorapis [daughter] of Komons, with [her] guardian
2     her brother Pekysios of the same Komons
3     to Plou[tas/tês, son] of Ptolemaios, a Persian of (the) later class, for the
4     present thirteenth year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
5     Augustus Germanicus Emperor, the lands belonging to her around Seruphis of (the) southwestern toparchy,
6     from the (land) of Sisinos, eight arourae, so that these
7     [are] sown with wheat; and from (the land) of Nikanoros, with (the) royal [land], arourae fifteen and a half quarter
8     so [that they] sow with wheat arourae eight quarter
9     and barley one and a half aroura, and [to fodder with]
10    vetch the remaining six arourae; (for the) yield of each
11    aroura of those in rain, from (the) geometry of (the) rectangle, for the wheat of the eight arourae
12    per (aroura) wheat seven artabae, and [the] eight quarter arourae, per (aroura) wheat six artabae, and [the]
13    barley per (aroura) barley five artabae, and [the] vetch, per (aroura) wheat two artabae; and the lessee will give
14    to Tausorapis of all [kinds of] fine things one artaba
15    one; and the yield [is] safe from all risk.
16    And if the lessee owes anything to the government or to another on behalf of Tausorapis or of the land, he will account for it from the yield. And [of] the fruits [let] Tausorapis and those with her have authority until
17    she receives her (share). And of the rental
18    Apollonios
19    Hekaton [son] of Xenôn
20    in Isios Pagga, drachmas 400
