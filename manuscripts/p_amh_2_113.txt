[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year twenty-first of the Emperor
2     [C]ae[s]ar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus
3     Au[gustus the Piou]s, Phaophi 22, in [the village of] Soknopaiou
4     N[esos of the Herak]leides division of the [Arsi]noite
5     n[ome. Stotoetis the eld]er son of Stotoetis [son of St]otoetis acknowledges, priest of the fourth
6     tribe of Soknopaios the g[r]eat god, about 64 years old, scar on the middle of the forehead,
7     to Erieus daughter of Panephremmis son of Stotoetis,
8     priestess from the same village, about 28 years old, scar on the forehead,
9     with her guardian her husband Stotoetis son of Pakysis, about 35 years old, scar on the second finger of the left hand, that he has received
10    from her, the acknowledging party, forthwith from hand to hand,
11    out of three hundred drachmas of silver, of silver
12    one hundred seventy-eight drachmas and the interest, which her deceased mother owed,
13    Sega[this daughter of P]akysis, according to an acknowledgment complet[ed] through the same record-office in the 10th year of Antoninus
14    Caesar the lord, in the month Hathyr on the twelfth,
15    which acknowledgment he has also given back to her for
16    annulment and cancellation, and that the acknowledging party makes no claim concerning the drachmas which he has received
17    as is set forth above, apart from the other (drachmas) which the deceased brother owes, of silver drachmas
18    one hundred. Under-signer (is) Stotoetis the younger,
19    about 40 years old, scar on the middle finger of the left hand.
20    I, Sto[to]etis the el[der] son of Stotoetis, acknowledge
21    that I have received from Eria the aforesaid (sums), out of three hundred
22    fifty-six drachmas of silver, of silver
23    one hundred seventy-eight dr[ach]mas and the interest, which there owed
24    me the mother of Eria, Sega[t]hi[s] daughter of Pakysis, according to an acknowledgment completed through the same record-office, and nothing
25    at all do I claim concerning the mother,
26    apart from the others [which] this woman's brother owed me,
27    Stotoetis the elder, of silver
28    one hundred drachmas, as is set forth. I, Stotoetis, wrote on behalf of my
29    father.
