[TRANSLATION]
1     [Report] to Kollouthes [the village] scribe of Ammonias [from] the farmers [of Ammonias], whose seed [of them] was measured for the foot-soldiers. [We are wronged] by Nestos and [Torrimmos].
2     [And] Zen[on], [being judges themselves], and our land they took away. [The] [foot-soldiers] wishing to [give] us back the land
3     [to the] farmers [who have sown, so that we might not] be ruined.
4     [Being farmers] they hindered—[Nestos] and [Torrimm]as and [Zen]on took [away].
5     [the land]. Since indeed the land [they took away] of [ours], we ask for [the seed].
6     [to be given back] to us.
7     … ηι … according to the assessment. We have written therefore to you [so that]
8     you know, that [we will give in, being wronged], and Philisk[os having written the]
9     steward concerning [us] [to review] in year 33, Choiak [21].
