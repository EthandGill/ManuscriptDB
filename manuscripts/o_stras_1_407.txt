[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, produce of the 5th year of our lord
2     Imperator Caesar Marcus Antonius Gordianus Pious, Fortunate, August
3     Mesore, 11th; for the tax-farming of Charakos, name Bekios Pe… Patomekutos, wheat one artaba; it amounts to wheat 1. Aurelius
4     Herakleios [I] have signed.
