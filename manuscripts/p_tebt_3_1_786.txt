[TRANSLATION]
1     To Phani[as, of the] first friends
2     and stra[teg]os and (superintendent) over the revenues,
3     from [t]he royal cultivators
4     from Oxyrhyncha. We having acted valiantly
5     according to your exhortations
6     and having sown the royal land which we cultivate,
7     and having borrowed
8     no small (sums of) money so that no
9     deficiency might arise, and having
10    brought up the produce from the land to the royal
11    threshing-floor, and having presented
12    (it) to be handed over into the royal treasury, but foreseeing
13    the exactions being made by some of those who do not conduct themselves
14    from the best (motives)
15    and who transgress the standing decrees
16    concerning the cultivators
17    and the instructions that have reached us from you concerning the like matters (given) to the epistatai,
18    we ask you, if it seem good, taking up (the cause) of us and of the
19    royal (cultivators), to give orders to write
20    to Demetrios and Stephanos the
21    epistatai to follow the aforewritten (rules),
22    to permit no one in any way whatever
23    to exact from us unjustly, nor to force their way into the threshing-floors,
24    but to send such (persons)
25    to you, in no way carried off (with them), so that you may decide
26    concerning them openly.
27    And this being done, we shall be able to hand over the rents in full,
28    having obtained
29    your protection, and no
30    loss [to the] king will arise.
31    [Farewell.]
