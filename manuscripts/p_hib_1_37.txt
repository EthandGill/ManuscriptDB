[TRANSLATION]
1     Year [twelve], [of the month of Pharm]outh [the 8th].
2     reports Stratios son of Straton, Thracian of foreign descent, to Ptolemaios the village guard of [Taleos].
3     have been lost at night in the
4     [Herakleitos's] plot: hairy goats two, male and female, whose value is four drachmas.
5     Year 12, [of the] month of Pharmouthis, the 8th. [reports] Stratios son of Straton
6     Thracian of foreign descent, to Ptolemaios, the [village] guard of the village of [Taleous,] have been lost at night
7     [in the He]rakleitos plot
8     [hairy goats] two, male
9     and female, whose value drachmas
10    four.
