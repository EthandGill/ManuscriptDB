[TRANSLATION]
1     Pemnas to Zenon greetings. Concerning the amounts owed in the
2     hog-herds from previous times together with the tax of year 37, Heraklides having made an agreement with Thoteas they themselves without us have reckoned and we are not able to follow what they have done until
3     now, nor asking for the account did they dare to give it to us.
4     And concerning these things then repeatedly I testified to Jason that not correctly
5     are they doing [having] agreed. And [he] has also the contracts [with respect] to the
6     hog-herds all, Heraklides. I have written therefore to you concerning these things so that you might know.
7     Farewell. Year 38, Pachon 10.
8     2
9     To Zenon
