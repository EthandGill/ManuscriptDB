[TRANSLATION]
1     A[m]enneus to Asclepiadeus, greetings. [Since] you [wrote], we have made ready
2     For the arrival of Chrysippos, [the chief body-guard] and administrator
3     White-faced [cattle?] ten, day-geese five, birds fifty,
4     … [geese] fifty, birds two-hundred, pigeon-[birds] one-hundred. We have provided donkeys, road-travellers five, and of these the …, we have made ready
5     And the forty donkeys [the bag-carrying ones], and we proceed to the road-making.
6     [Farewell. Year] 22, Choiak 4.
7     Year 22, Choiak 7, Amneus to Asclepiadeus
8     Of the guest-gifts that were made ready.
