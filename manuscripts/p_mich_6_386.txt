[TRANSLATION]
1     The twenty-sixth year of Marcus Aurelius
2     Commodus Antoninus Augustus, the Fortunate,
3     the Pious, Phamenoth 24: there paid to Syros and partners, collectors
4     of money-taxes in the holding of Karanis, Horion: for orchard, for apomoira of the twenty-fifth year, one drachma, one obol, a half-obol; total: 1 drachma, 1 obol, a half-obol; for transport of olives, an obol, 2 chalkoi; for naubion, an obol, 2 chalkoi;
5     extra charges, two obols; for the per-aroura tax, five obols, a half-obol; extra charges, a half-obol; exchange-fee, a half-obol; Pharmouthi 16:
6     for orchard, for apomoira of the twenty-fifth year,
7     five drachmas; total: 5 drachmas; for transport of olives, four obols, 2 chalkoi; for naubion, an obol, a half-obol; extra charges, one drachma,
8     one obol, a half-obol; total: 1 drachma, 1 obol, a half-obol; for the per-aroura tax, three drachmas, two obols; total:
9     [3 drachmas, two obols]; extra charges, an obol, a half-obol, 2 chalkoi; exchange-fee, an obol, 2 chalkoi; Pachon 16:
10    [the same man], for orchard, for apomoira of the twenty-fifth year,
11    [two] drachmas, three obols; total: 2 drachmas, three obols; for transport of olives, two obols, 2 chalkoi; for naubion, an obol, a half-obol; extra charges, three obols, a half-obol, 2 chalkoi; for the per-aroura tax,
12    [one drachma, four obols]; total: 1 drachma, 4 obols; extra charges, an obol; exchange-fee, a half-obol, 2 chalkoi.
