[TRANSLATION]
1     To Philoxenus, former kosmete, strategus
2     of the Arsinoite Themist division
3     from Her[o]us son of Satbous of those from Soknepaion Island. I leased still from [the]
4     13th year of the god Claudius from the previously Narcissus
5     property [an] olive-press at [a rent] of 200 drachmas and oil
6     of three measures. From that time the presiding [magistrates]
7     not having given into the equipment neither measuring-vessels
8     nor the other [things] which [were required], I myself was forced from
9     my own [funds] having purchased [them] to supply and [a] machine
10    similarly both wedges and the other things that were needed
11    again from my own, [they] having made no recompense for me. Since therefore also the olive-press itself collapsed and I was forced to place beams and supports
12    and [for] two years I am forced outside the olive-press
13    being [I have] to record the rents from [my] house, I ask
14    no longer [being] able to remain [bearing] the losses
15    to compel the presiding [magistrates] to make me free from trouble concerning the rents.
