[TRANSLATION]
1     Satornilus to Sempronius his brother
2     and lord, very many greetings.
3     Before all things I pray that you are in good health and
4     prospering; and at the same time I make your obeisance
5     daily before the ancestral gods. I am astonished, brother—this is the second letter that I have sent you since I came
6     home—and you have sent me no answer. I ask you then, brother, to write to me
7     more quickly about your welfare, so that I too may pass my time more free of care. For you know, brother, that I hold you not only as a brother but
8     also as a father and lord and god. For when I came home, at the same hour I of necessity wrote you the first letter,
9     having come home safely on the twenty-first, and I found everything well and our lady
10    faring well. Our lady greets you,
11    and Maximus and his wife and
12    the children, and Gemellus and Julius and his wife and Helene and all the little ones by name. Greet Valerius warmly and Celler and his (household) and Julius Serenus
13    warmly, and Serenus son of Sabinus and Sarapion
14    and all those who love you by name.
15    I pray for your health, most honored brother.
16    … from
17    Saturnilus your brother.
18    To Sempronius Maximus.
