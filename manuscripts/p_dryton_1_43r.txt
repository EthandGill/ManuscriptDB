[TRANSLATION]
1     From the 15th to the animals I have given artabas from the remainder 4…, Athur from the 21st
2     through Myrsine other artabas 20, Choiach 10, 2 making 4
3     making until Choiach 10, artabas 20, remainder 24 of which
4     I being away to Ptolemais the 2
5     Myrsine so that to Hermokrates the banker he has given artabas 1/2
6     Pachon 2, through me artabas 4, making 4 1/2
7     remainder 19 1/2, and Pachon 28, through me other 2, making 6 1/2, remainder 17 1/2 of which
