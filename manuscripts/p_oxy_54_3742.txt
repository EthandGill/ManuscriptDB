[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Valerius] Ammonian[us also called]
2     [Geront]ius, accountant of Oxyrhynch[ites],
3     [from] the guild of glass-workers
4     [of the] illustrious and most illustrious city of Oxyrhynchites,
5     [through] me Aurelius Areion.
6     … us. In accordance [with]
7     [the] instructions [I] with my own valuation [re]port the following recorded price of the goods I handle, and I swear by the
8     divine oath that I have lied in nothing. And it is:
9     of glass by weight per centenary 1, talents 4.
10    In the consulship of [Aquilius?] Gallicanus
11    and Caesonius Bassus the
12    most illustrious, Athyr 30.
13    Aurelius Areion I have submitted [this petition],
14    declaring as aforesaid. Aurelius Patermouthis I wrote the letter on his behalf, not knowing [how to write].
