[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, harvest of the 13th year of Antoninus Caesa[r]
2     the lord, Payni 21, on behalf of Noto, [the] name Taoutes
3     Petemempis and [his] brothers through Herieus Teotes, wheat, two-thirds [and a] twenty-fourth
4     [it] comes to wheat, artabas 2/3 [and] 1/24, and [the] name Thaesis Petemempis wheat, one-sixth [it] comes to wheat, artabas 1/6.
