[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 10 of Emperor Caesar
2     Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus
3     Conqueror of Armenia, Conqueror of Media, Conqueror of Parthia, greatest.
4     Work has been done on dikes for year 9, from Thoth 9 to 10, the ordered
5     2 five-day periods in the Arab village; (you) shall not use another token because of the claim that [it] has lapsed for Soknopaiou [son of] Stotoetis Apygches, of Stotetius, mother Tekiasios.
