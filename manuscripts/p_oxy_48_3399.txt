[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Dorotheos,
2     Papnouthis.
3     I wish [to know] you that I received
4     from Isidoros at the city silver
5     of denaries one myriad of myriads
6     and myriads thousand two hundred fifty, total of denaries myriads of myriads … and through the guardian
7     coins six and of silver of denaries myriads of myriads … .
8     I pray that you be well for many times,
9     Lord, brother.
