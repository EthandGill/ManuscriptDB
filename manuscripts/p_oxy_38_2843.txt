[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fifth year of the Emperor Caesar Domitian
2     [A]ugust[u]s Germanicus, in the intercalary (days) of Hyperb[er]etaios,
3     of the month Caesare[u]s in the intercalary (days), in the city of Oxyrhynchus of the
4     Thebaid, in the term of the agorano[m]oi Theon and Dius and Dionysius:
5     Aline daughter of Komon set free under Zeus, Earth, Sun
6     son of Dionysius, her mother being Kleopatra daughter of Dionysius, of those
7     from the city of Oxyrhynchus, about … years old, of medium height, honey-skinned, long-faced, with a scar, with the guardianship of her own
8     son Komon, s[on] of Mnesitheus, [so]n of P[e]tosouchos, of those [from]
9     the same city, about … years old, of medium height, honey-skinned, long-faced, with a scar on the left eyebrow, in the street, the slave-woman Euphro[s]yne belonging to her, about 35 years old, of medium height, honey-skinned, long-faced, with a scar,
10    house-born from the slave-woman Dem[e]tr[o]us, for ten drachmas of stamped silver, and (the sums) which were paid on her (the slave's) behalf to
11    her aforewritten mistress Aline (by) Theon son of Dionysius son of Leon, his mother being Isione, of those from the same
12    city, about 43 years old, of medium height, honey-skinned, long-faced,
13    with a scar on the right calf, of ransom-money, eight hundred drachmas of imperial silver coinage, equal to ten talents three thousand (drachmas) of copper, it not being permitted to
14    Theon nor to any other concerning him to make demand from the freed[woman Eu]phro[sy]ne nor from her (representatives), of the [ransom]
15    nor of any kind in any manner. Witness o[f the manu]mission
