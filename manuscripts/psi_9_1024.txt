r.12   Ἑρμόδωρος κεχρημάτικα.

[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Cleopatra and Ptolemy called Alexander the son of gods loving their mother, [in] the thirteenth year which was also the tenth,
2     with priests and priestesses and the basket-bearer of those being and existing, [in the] month Pachon the nineteenth, in Hermouthis of the Pathyrite [nome] of the Thebaid, with Hermodorus as market-overseer.
3     Sale. Sold Picos Psemminius of the [sacred officials] from the Memnoneia, as of [age] thirty-two, medium complexion, shorn, long-faced, straight-nosed
4     [with a] scar on the left eyebrow, [another] on the middle of [the] nose
5     the portion accruing to him [of] days of abstinence and their produce and the sacred services of the great Iseum in the Memnoneia and open spaces
6     and all the other [things] of the month [for] one day, of the year [for] twelve days. [Bought by] Tateathyris Psemminius Persine
7     as of [age] twenty-five, medium, medium-complexioned, long-faced, snub-nosed with [a] scar on the nose from [the] left, together with the authority of her own husband Toteous of Zmanreus
8     of the [sacred officials] from the Memnoneia
9     as of [age] thirty-two, medium-complexioned, long-faced, straight-nosed with [a] scar on the right wrist [in] bronze coinage [of] talents four
10    drachmas thousand five hundred. [Agent/Broker]
11    and guarantor of the matters according to the sale Picos the one having sold, whom Tateathyris the [one] having bought received.
12    Hermodorus has [made official record].
