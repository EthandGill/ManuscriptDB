[TRANSLATION]
1     To Posidonios, the manager of the (affairs) of Theanous daughter of Alexandros, from Ammonios son of Arche[de]mos and Do[r]ion son of
2     Dorion, the two of them. We wish [to lease] the olive-grove belonging [to Theanous],
3     newly planted, in the area of Karani[s],
4     the crops of the twenty-second year of Caesar, falling due
5     in the twenty-third year of Caesar,
6     for a total rent of [fo]rty silver drachmas, which we shall also pay in
7     the month Hathyr by the thirtieth, we [being] sureties for one another for payment without any [post]ponement or quibbling; and it shall be permitted [to you]
8     to re-lease to the one offering more, and
9     we shall carry out the pruning in
10    the month Hathyr of the twenty-third [year]
11    of Caesar. The 23rd year of Caesar, Phaophi 20.
12    I, Dorion son of Dorion, acknowledge that I have joined in leasing
13    in accordance with all the aforesaid, and I shall jointly pay
14    the rent [to which] I have also agreed. The 23rd year of Caesar,
15    Phaophi, on the twentieth.
16    I, Posidonios, the manager of the (affairs) of Theani[ous]
17    daughter of Alexandros, have granted the le[ase]
18    on all the aforesaid terms, and it shall be [permit]ted to me to re-lease to the one [offering] more.
19    The twenty-third year of Caesar, Hathyr 2.
20    … the 23rd year of Caesar, P[hao]ph[i] 20.
