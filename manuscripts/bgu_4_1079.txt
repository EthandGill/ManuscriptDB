[TRANSLATION]
1   Sarapion to Herakleides our
2   own, greeting. I sent you
3   two other letters,
4   one by Nedymos, one by
5   Kronios the sword-bearer.
6   Well then, I re-
7   ceived from the Arab the
8   letter, and I read it
9   and was grieved.
10   Stick close to Ptoll-
11   arion at every hour; per-
12   haps he can set you
13   free. Tell him: “I am
14   one thing, everyone else another;
15   I am a mere boy. I have sold you
16   my wares for a talent too
17   little; I do not know
18   what my patron will do to me;
19   many creditors we
20   have. Do not ruin
21   us.” Ask him
22   every day; perhaps he can
23   take pity on you. If not, then like
24   everyone else, you too watch
25   yourself against the Jews(?).
26   Rather, by sticking close to
27   him you may become his friend.
28   See whether the tablet can
29   be signed through Diodoros,
30   through the wife of the Pre-
31   fect. If you do your
32   part, you are not to blame.
33   Greet Diodoros warmly.
34   Farewell. Greet Harpochration.
35   Year 1 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
36   Augustus Germanicus Imperator, month
37   Kaisareios 11.
38   [Deliver at] Alexandria,
39   at the Augustan Market, at the …
40   …storehouse, for Herakleides from Sarapion
41   …on, son of Sosipatros.
