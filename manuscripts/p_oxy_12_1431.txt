# HGV-EN: Athanasius to Sarapion, agent, greeting. Give Gennadius the carpet-maker for the price of a carpet on the occasion of the visit of the dux 2,250,000 denarii, equivalent to 1,500 talents. I pray for your health. The 45th, 27th, and 11th year, Tubi 8.

1     Athanasius to Sarapion, agent, greetings.
2     Give to Gennadius the carpet-maker for the price of a carpet on the occasion of the visit of the dux 2,250,000 silver denarii.
3     Which equals 1,500 talents. I pray for your health.
4     The 45th, 27th, and 11th year, Tybi 8.
