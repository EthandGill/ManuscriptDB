[TRANSLATION]
1     Prophet
2     Along with private individuals to come about [and] from [all] activity and zeal and friendship [to be?] … universal in all things
3     [that] it is permitted [to] all in pure places to come about, and they showed nothing … of the charges laid, but also removed them from the purchase [made] by Marsisouchos.
4     I will write [in] … to my friend concerning this matter. Therefore complete [the matters] with us, and moreover they say send to Ibion so that also
5     [someone] may take care of [the] property [for] the necessary works. Greet [him/them].
