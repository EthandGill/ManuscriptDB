[TRANSLATION]
1     ⳨ (chi-mu-gamma)
2     In the consulship of Flavius Justin the most glorious,
3     on the fourth … of the fourth indiction.
4     John presbyter, son of John of pious memory,
5     through me Senouthes son of Apollos, to Aurelius Besarion
6     son of Dioskoros Psimanobet, both from the village of Aphrodite
7     of the Antaiopolite nome. I have leased to you for a two-year period
8     reckoned from the crops, with God, of the coming fifth indiction
9     and itself, my existing farm, of however many arouras
10    it is, situated in the southern plain of the same village of Aphrodite
11    in the holding of Nempkes, with cistern and dwelling and all rights
12    according to the boundaries, on condition that you do all the agricultural
13    work on it from your own expenses
14    and animals, and give me the rent at a fixed rate, in full
15    and free of flood-failure (which may not be), each year, of wheat
16    artabas … by the rent-measure of the most magnificent
17    count Ammonios son of Theodosios, and one gold solidus
18    less … carats, by the goldsmith's standard, and you shall provide me
19    five cheeses and six small leeks(?), without dispute. The counter-lease
20    is valid and secure; and having been questioned I acknowledged. Indeed you shall also provide me …
21    Counter-lease of John son of Makarios presbyter, through Senouthes son of Apollos, of the farm …
