[TRANSLATION]
1     … and of the [So]n and of [the Holy Sp]irit, of the …
2     and consubstantial in the unity of the Trinity. Written in the month Phamenoth 20, of the sixth indiction.
3     [I, Shenou]te the soldier, the son of the blessed Patermoute, the man of the village of Jkō[ou],
4     … [public] account, by your hands, the all-praiseworthy [l]ord Abdallah the son,
5     … governor, by your hands, also from you, the most glorious lord …
6     … the single [vill]age (of) Jkōou with its hamlets and with its plains, greeting.
7     … [the fore]going. I write, I wish, I settle (the account) with your lordship; I acknowledge … without any [c]ompulsion or deceit …
8     … without any extortion or fraud or any legal (defect) …
9     [I] acknowledge that, behold, fifteen of these boats, fitted out and turned over to the account
10    of the expedition of the East, which were brought south against the administration of the village of Jkōou,
11    in this year, the sixth indiction, for the expedition of the seventh indiction, according to the force of the order to the most glorious
12    all-praiseworthy governor — these fifteen boats, therefore, I have ready,
13    ready, that I might (deliver) th[em] …
14    [for]ward, by the will of God, without negligence and without [shortfall] …
15    … [in] every place, so that our lords will command me to give a receipt …
16    [for their] expense …
17    [Acknow]ledgment made by Senouthes son of Patermouthios the soldier from Aphr[odito] …
18    of the East, 6th indiction, of the expedition, 7th indiction, and 19 solidi for the commutation in cash of his expense, 2 months …
19    on the road as far as the East, and the wage of Senouthios himself, the agent …
