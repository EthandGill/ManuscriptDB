[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year twelve of the Emperor
2     Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrian
3     Antoninus Aug[ustus] Pious
4     Epeiph 4. Ptollas, Heracles and the partners, grain-collectors
5     of Bakcchiades, [of the] Hephaestiad [division], we have measured from the produce of
6     the same year of wheat in artabae with public measure, cleaned, [poured] into
7     Theon son of Theon of Bakcchiades, of the ampelitis [division]
8     of wheat three and one-eighth artabae, [with additional measurement of] [one-half and] 24, total of wheat artabae three [and 7/8].
