# HGV-EN: To [Hi]er[a]x, strategos of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division, and Timagenes, [royal scribe] of the same division and to Harphaesis, village scribe of Karanis, and to Didymos and the other laographoi of the village of Karanis [from S]is[oi]s, son of Petheus, the son of Akousilaos, mother Thermouthis, daughter of [..]is from the same (village of) Karanis. I register myself and my family for the house-by-house registration of the past year 23 of the God Antoninus for the house and courtyard belonging to me in the village. I am Sisois, the above mentioned, 29 years old and (I register) my wife Themouthis, daughter of [P]asok[nopa]ios, 24 years old, without a distinguishing mark, and the daughter of both of us, Thermouthi[s][, x years old, without a distinguishing mark], and my brother of the same father and [of the same mother], Bes[a]s, 2[. years old, without a distinguishing mark][ and] (my) sister Teonchonsis, 14 years old, without a distinguishing mark [and] the son of my uncle on my father’s side, [Si]sois, son of Onnophris, mother this, daughter of Apolloni[os, x years old] and my mother [The]rmouthis, 54 years old.
