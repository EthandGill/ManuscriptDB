[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Neilammon … ammis also called Hierax, councillor of the most illustrious Hermopolis,
2     from Aurelius Diskas son of Pinoution, his mother being Tathotis, with a scar on the
3     index finger of the left hand, from the village of Temenkyrkis of the Shepherds.
4     I wish willingly to lease from you for a period of 4 years reckoned
5     from the present 8th year and 7th year the belonging to you near the same Temenkyrkis
6     … out of the allotment of Menelaos, to the south of the plot of Anoubion, from common and undivided property, … arourae, and to the south of the orchard of Kyrillous
7     [and] of Domittius, … arourae … and out of the same allotment likewise from common and undivided property, 3 … arourae [for the sowing of wheat] and planting
8     of [fodder each] year, at the half rent per aroura, the one part
9     [in wheat] … and the other half in fodder, at … drachmas of silver;
10    … drach…;
11    … u … u … o … lou from common and undivided property … other
12    3 1/2 1/4 arourae likewise for the sowing of wheat and planting of fodder
13    each year at the half rent per aroura, the half in wheat
14    at eight artabae of wheat, and the other half in fodder at forty drachmas; which total rent I shall pay you and measure out in the
15    months Pauni and Epeiph of each year without delay; the silver
16    we shall give in old Ptolemaic coin, and the wheat new, clean, unadulterated,
17    unlumped, unmixed-with-barley, sifted, by your wooden receiving-measure at the threshing-floors of the same village-secretariat, the public dues and all the additional assessments
18    of the land being upon you, the landholder. The lease is valid
19    and being questioned I acknowledged. In the 8th year of our lord Diocletian and the 7th year
20    of our lord Maximian, the Augusti, Phaophi 9. I, Aurelius
21    Diskas son of Pinoution, have leased … I, Aurelius Hermias
22    also called Olympios, and as he transacts, wrote on his behalf, as he does not know letters,
23    and I stand surety for the payment and repayment through the manager for everything.
