[TRANSLATION]
1    In the ninth year of Imperator
2    Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus
3    Antoninus Augustus Pius, Tybi
4    3, through the bank of Sabinus.
5    Tameia(?): Ischyrion son of Aphrodisios and the
6    overseers with him of the alum(-tax) of the Arsinoite —
7    Panouphis son of Tesenouphis, mother Stotoetis,
8    from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, Herakleides
9    division, camel-driver — (acknowledge) that he has paid in full
10   the toll on what he transported from the Small
11   Oasis through the gate of Nynpos into the Arsinoite:
12   of crude alum, thirty talents,
13   at 1 drachma 3 obols per talent = 45 drachmas; the resulting
14   metallic talents, 12; for their freight,
15   at 7 drachmas 3 obols = 90 drachmas; total collected, 135 silver drachmas.
16   And Ischyrion has received from the overseers
17   of the Small Oasis, through the aforesaid camel-driver,
18   the … of the alum, 12 metallic talents,
19   from … the customarily given six and a half percent(?).
20   (2nd hand) I, Ischyrion, overseer, together with
21   others, have received the … of the
22   alum, twelve metallic
23   talents, the resulting
24   thirty crude talents,
25   of which the toll and the freight charges
26   we have paid in full.
