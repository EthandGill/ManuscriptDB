[TRANSLATION]
1     Subatianus Aquila to Theon the strategos
2     of the Arsinoe [nome], greetings.
3     Nigera [son] of Papyrius, condemned to the alabaster-[quarry] for five years by Claudius Julianus the most distinguished, having completed
4     the time of condemnation I released. I wish you to be well.
5     Mauricianus Mennius read [this].
6     Year 18 of the Emperors Caesars Lucius Septimius Severus
7     Pious Pertinax Arabicus
8     Adiabenic[us] Parthic[us] Maximus and Marcus Aurelius
9     Antoninus Pious Augustuses.
10    Tybi, new moon [day].
