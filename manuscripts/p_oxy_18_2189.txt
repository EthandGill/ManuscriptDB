[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Sarapion leased,
2     also called Apollonianos, having been gymnasiarch of the city of the Oxyrhynchites
3     and however he is styled, through
4     Aurelius Ammon his agent,
5     to Aurelius Podas son of Psenamounis,
6     his mother being Tapetsiris, from Sko,
7     for two years from the present
8     3rd year, from the property belonging to him
9     near Sko from the holding of Apollonios
10    … called ro…os, eleven and a quarter arouras, so as each year to sow with lentil, of fixed rent
11    each year by the fourth-measure of lentil
12    fifty-one artabas,
13    all (rent) free from all risk, the public dues of the land
14    being the responsibility of the lessor,
15    who also is to have ownership of the crops
16    until he recovers what is owed each year. And if
17    in the following years (the land) becomes unwatered,
18    allowance will be made
19    to the lessee, the right of execution belonging to you
20    against me and against all my property
21    as if by legal decision. Valid is
22    the lease. Year 3 of the Emperor
23    Caesar Marcus Aurelius
24    Antoninus Pius
25    Felix Augustus, Phaophi 13. I, Aurelius Podas son of Psenamounis, the aforesaid, have leased
26    the land for the two years
27    and I will pay each year a fixed rent of lentil by the fourth-measure of fifty
28    one artabas, and I will do all things as aforesaid.
29    I, Aurelius Diogenes, wrote
30    on his behalf since he does not know letters.
