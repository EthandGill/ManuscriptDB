[TRANSLATION]
1     … [The Aurelii Gelasios and however he is styled] and Eus daughter of Pelaios, former councilor of Hermoupolis,
2     [the great, ancient,] and most august and most illustrious, she knowing letters,
3     [the agent being] … Aurelius Silvanus also called Agathodaimon, former gymnasiarch, councilor of the same city,
4     [greeting to one another. We have brought charges] against one another through petitions, both before the
5     … before the most estimable strategoi in office, and moreover
6     [also] … before the same strategoi without writing against one another, and
7     … the things said they ordered likewise without writing to be arbitrated … having employed these things, and matters being set before them by us … no longer to plead lawsuits, but rather in friendship
8     … to settle the disputes, and on these terms we agree
9     … that we have each received back our own and do not make claims against one another … on behalf of us against us, nor against those acting for one another [concerning any matter,] whether undecided or decided, nor concerning anything [else]
10    … [on condition] that we have no claim against one another in [any]
11    [way] … from the present day for all
12    [time] …
13    … and that we do not make further use of the petitions submitted by each of us … . The release is valid, written in duplicate, of the same form, so that it may be a single (copy) in the hands of each party, and having been questioned in turn
14    by one another we gave our consent. Year 3 of Imperator Caesar
15    Lucius Domitius Aurelianus Gothicus Maximus Pius Felix
16    Augustus, Mesore, the 3rd intercalary day. Aurelius Gelasios,
17    and however I am styled, … nor will I make claim, as set forth above. Aurelia Eus will jointly submit (this); I make no claim, as
18    set forth above. Aurelius Silvanus the aforesaid, I present myself, as set forth above.
