1     Eutyches, son of Eutyches, of Sōterichōs, to Paōpeis the farmer, greetings. You will do well [by] giving to my brothers Sōterichōs
2     and Hermēs, from what you owe me of the transportation charges of the present nineteenth year of Trajan, Best Caesar, of the
3     lord, to account for the remaining maintenance-costs, up to the present nineteenth, and of that nineteenth
4     year, of wheat, by the measure as declared through the lease, sixty artabae
