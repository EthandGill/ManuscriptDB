[TRANSLATION]
1     To Heron, strategos of the Themistos and [Pol]emon divisions of the Arsinoite (nome), and to Dionysammon
2     royal scribe of the Polemon division of the Arsinoite (nome), and to Panesne[u]s village-scribe of Teptynis, and to Didymos and Dios poll-tax officials of the same village
3     from Isidora daughter of Orses the elder son of Orses, her mother being Esas
4     daughter of Paeus, from the village of Teptynis, with her guardian [P]akebkis son of Auneus grandson of Ornophris. There belong to me in the same village
5     of Teptynis paternal and maternal house-properties: paternal m[e]n two houses,
6     in which are vaulted rooms and a courtyard and yards and other appurtenances
7     and entrance and exit, and to the north of these another house
8     and a yard and other appurtenances and entrance and exit; and maternal in another place two vaulted rooms with the upper storeys and an open court and a yard and bare plots, in which is a small house and entrance
9     and exit; in which (properties) I register for the house-by-house census of the past 28th year
10    of Aurelius Comm[o]dus Antoninus Caesar the lord
11    in the aforesaid village.
12    And I am Isidora, aged 6…, and (these are) my slave persons: Philoumene aged 45 and her offspring Dioskorous
13    aged 8 [and] Athenarion aged 4, and another slave-woman Elephantine
14    offspring of Demet[ri]as aged 20, and offspring Eudaimonis aged 5
15    and Isarous aged 1, [an]d another slave-woman Helene being in flight, aged 68, and Am[m]onarion aged 42 and Herakleia aged 38
16    likewise in fl[igh]t. Therefore I submit (this declaration). Year 29 of Aurelius
17    Com[mo]dus Antoninus Caesar the lord,
18    Mesore 29. D[i]dymos and Deios, poll-tax officials, through me Didymos,
19    have received the copy of this until examination.
