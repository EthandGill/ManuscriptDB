[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To [Kro]kos, village scribe of the village of So[knopaiou] Nesos [from Ha]tres, son of Satabous, the son of [Pan]e[phre]mmis, mother [Segathi]s, [from] the village of Soknop[aiou Nesos]. I register myself and [my family] for the house-by-house registration of the past [year 23 of the God Aelius] Antoninus, [in the house], in which I live, which is in the [village] and is inherited from my mother. And I am the above mentioned Ha[tres], 2[5] years old, without a distinguishing mark and (I register) my wife Isarion, father unknown, whose [mother] is [T]anephremmis, [daughter of Panomieus, 13 years old, without a distinguishing mark. There belongs to her in the village] two houses and her [grandmother’s] quarter (inheritance). And (I register) Tapet[s]ir[is, daughter of Stotoetis, the son of Paneph]remmis, [mother T]apetsiris, 12 years old, without a distinguishing mark. There belongs to Tapet[siris] two houses and a courtyard and her grandfather’s quarter and her grandmother’s quarter. [Therefore] I submit this registration. [Year 1] of Imperator Caesar [Marcus Au]rel[ius] Antoninus [Augustus and] Imperator Caesar [Lucius] Aurelius Verus Augustus, [Mesore 4].
