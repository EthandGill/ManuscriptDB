[TRANSLATION]
1     There has been measured into the granary of the administration,
2     of the Upper toparchy, of the produce of year 6 of Domitian
3     the lord, Mesore 15, in the name
4     of Pachrates (son of) Amenrosis,
5     of wheat, in heap, 1⅔ artaba; and in the name
6     of Psenapathes also called Cha-
7     ponchonsis (son of) Pikos,
8     of wheat 1⅔, total together of wheat 3⅓.
9     I, Arch( ), have signed.
