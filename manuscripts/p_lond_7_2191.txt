2     a seventh share of all the property belonging to her,
3     both land and movables and whatever she may further acquire, and of the land of Telon
4     ½ aroura.
5     In the reign of Ptolemy the god Euergetes, son of Ptolemy and Cleopatra the Manifest gods, and of Queen
6     Cleopatra the sister and Queen Cleopatra the wife, the Benefactor gods, year 2,
7     the priest who is in Alexandria of Alexander and of the Saviour gods and the Brother gods and the
8     Benefactor gods and the Father-loving gods and the Manifest gods and the god Eupator and the Mother-loving god
9     and the Benefactor gods being (in office); the prize-bearer of Berenike the Benefactress, the basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphos, the priestess
10    of Arsinoe Philopator being those who are in Alexandria,
11    and in Ptolemais of the Thebaid, the priests and priestesses and basket-bearer being those who are and exist
12    in Ptolemais of the Thebaid; in the month Hathyr 8, in Pathyris, before Heliodoros the agoranomos.
13    Agreement which Tathotis daughter of Phibis, Persian, about 50 years old, honey-skinned,
14    broad-faced, straight-nosed, willingly and with consent made, with her guardian, her brother Panobchounis son of Nechoutes, Persian, of the
15    registered (settlers), according to which she agrees that she has ceded to Kobaetesis daughter of Phagonis, her own daughter,
16    Persian, with her guardian, her husband Panobchounis son of Totoes, Persian of the epigone; namely from
17    the so-called grain-bearing land of Telon, from the 8th part belonging to her, half an aroura; neighbours
18    of the whole land: on the south the land of Patseous, on the north the land of Nechoutes son of Bouchis, on the east the vineyard of the crowds(?),
19    on the west the canal of Pmoes;
20    and from the other property belonging to her, both land and movables and whatever she may further acquire, a seventh share, apart from the dowry given to her,

23    parcel called (that) of Senamounis, from the 8th part appertaining to her, the seventh share; neighbours of the whole land: on the south the land of Patous son of Phibis and his brothers,
24    on the north the land of Psenthotes son of Kaleous and his brothers and of Patous son of Pathotes and his brothers, on the east the embankment, on the west the desert-hill; and from another parcel of Zminis son of Harsiesis, of 4½ 8 32 arouras,

26    the same seventh share; neighbours of the two parcels: on the south fallow land, on the north the land of Nechoutes son of Zmin, on the east a road, on the west the desert-hill; and from another parcel, 1½ ¼ 8 arouras
27    at 6½, from the (share) appertaining to her, the same seventh share; neighbours of the whole: on the south and east the sacred land of Aphrodite, on the north the land of Nechoutes son of Zmin,
28    on the west the desert-hill; and from another parcel called (that) of Tmonaree, from the eighth part falling to her, the same seventh share; neighbours of the whole land:
29    on the south the land of Kallias, on the east the [sacred] land of Aphrodite and a hollow, on the north the sacred land of the temple in Pathyris, [on the west the desert-hill]; and from another parcel, in which Tisytmis daughter of Phrasis has a share,
30    her own shares, from the part falling to her the same seventh share; neighbours of the whole land: on the south the land of Senamounis, on the north the land
31    of the ibis-keepers, on the east the embankment, on the west the land of Senamounis; and from another parcel in the lower strip, in which the same Tisytmis daughter of Phrasis has
32    a share, from the (share) appertaining to her the same seventh share; neighbours of the whole land: on the south the land of Kollouthos son of Phibis, on the north the land of Kales,

36    in which Thaibis has the ½, from the ½ of it the seventh share; neighbours: on the south the house of Thrason,
