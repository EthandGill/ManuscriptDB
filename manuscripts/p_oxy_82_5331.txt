[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of] our master New Leo, the eternal Augustus, for the 1st time, Thoth 30, indiction 13.
2     To [Flavius] John, the admirable comes of the divine consistory, landowner here in the illustrious
3     [city of the Oxyrhynchites], the Aurelii Anysios son of Paulus, his mother being Thekla, and …enesios son of Germanos,
4     [his mother being] …, both from the hamlet of Choulchoupeg, an estate of your magnificence, of the
5     [Oxyrhynchite nome], registered farmers, greeting. Willingly we undertake to lease on a joint-liability basis, [from the] present year 151 = 120, the sowing of the coming fourteenth
6     [indiction, from] the property belonging to your greatness, situated in the fields of our
7     [hamlet, the whole] half-share of a water-wheel called the South (wheel), fitted out with all
8     [wooden gear and] iron-work, together with the adjoining cistern and all appurtenances, and of the
9     [land farmed] with it, however many arouras there may be, for sowing of whatever crops we choose,
10    [and we will pay on] a joint-liability basis the rent, both on Nile-flooded and on unwatered (land), for each year
11    [in accordance with your] demand-register. And we agree also to lease jointly the parcel that consists of vineyard,
12    [of however many arouras] there may be, according to the half-share, the plot, having agreed also … of the embankment
13    … and the drawings of water and the water-supplies … blamelessly … was assigned … sow …
14    … … then …
