[TRANSLATION]
1     [I have received and] have been paid I Wenaphrius son of Jeremiah ally from you Andrew [secretary] [of the village of Tamaue]
2     [the customary things] given to me for the wages of Apollo son of Joseph and his ally, [that is to say for an apportionment]
3     … tenth indiction gold coins two less ten carats, gold coins two less ten carats. it was written [in the month]
4     … [indiction]. through me Strategius.
