[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 4, from Mencheus, village-scribe
2     of Kerkeosiris. Sheet by sheet, the register of the total
3     of the sowing of the same year.
4     There have been sown in the 3rd year, together with pastures,
5     of land, 1,193¾ arouras, of which the rent, reckoned in wheat, is 4,665 + ⅓ + 1/12.
6     And for the 4th year to have been sown:
7     with wheat, 600 arouras, 2,600 artabas,
8     of which 25½ arouras at 5 (artabas each) = 127½; 382½
9     at 4 + ⅓ + 1/12 = 1,876 artabas; 46 at 4½ = 207 artabas;
10    8½ at 4 = 34; 5 at 3 + ¼ + 1/16 + ¼(?) = ; 75¾ + ¼
11    at 3 = 227½ + ¼; 61½ at 2½ = 153½ + ¼ artabas;
12    7 at 2 = 14;
13    with barley, 91 arouras, of which the rent is 384 + ⅙,
14    of which 52 arouras at 4 + ½ + ⅓ + 1/12 = 255 + ⅔ artabas; 12½ at 4 = 50;
15    24½ at 3 = 73½; 2 at 2½ = 5;
16    with lentil, 193½ arouras, of which the rent is 844½,
17    of which 6 arouras at 5 = 30; 93 at 4 + ½ + ⅓ + 1/12 = 457 + ¼;
18    34 at 4½ = 143; 23 at 4 = 92; 37½
19    at 3 = 122½.
20    Total of the grain-land, arouras (…), artabas 3,000(?).
21    And with other kinds administered along with wheat:
22    with phaselos (cowpea), 12½ arouras, of which the rent is 51 + ⅓ + 1/12,
23    of which 7½ arouras at 4 + ½ + ⅓ + 1/12 = 36½ + ⅓ + 1/12; 5 at 3 = 15;
24    with fenugreek, 2 arouras at 3 = 6 artabas;
25    with black-cumin (melanthion), 1 (aroura), 4 artabas.
26    Total with other kinds, 15½ arouras, 61 + ⅓ + 1/12 artabas.
27    With green crops, whose administration is set last:
28    with vetch (arakos), 109 arouras, of which the rent is 436 + ½ + 1/12,
29    of which 47 arouras at 4 + ½ + ⅓ + 1/12 = 201 + 1/12 artabas; 27 at 4 = 108;
30    2 at 3½ = 7; 16 at 3 = 18(?); 17 at 2½ = 42½;
31    with hay (chortos), 31 (arouras), of which the rent is 141½ + 1/12,
32    of which 17 arouras at 4 + ½ + ⅓ + 1/12 = 83½ + 1/12; 13 at 4 = 52;
33    1 at 3 = 3 artabas;
34    of fodder-crops (chortonomon), 81 (arouras) at 1 = 81.
35    Total with green crops, 221 (arouras), 658 + ⅙ artabas.
36    Of the sown (land), 1,133¾ arouras, of which 4,605 + ⅓ + 1/12 artabas.
37    And for that on which no seed is charged, of pastures, 60 at 1 = 60,
38    so with the sown (land), 1,000(?) arouras, 4,600 artabas.
