[TRANSLATION]
1     To the Flavii: Praeiecta, the most exalted consul's wife, and Apion and
2     Georgius, the most exalted consuls, the landholders, also
3     here in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites: an account of receipts and
4     expenditures made through me, Serenus, deacon and administrator, of Matreu and
5     in the village of Episemos and in the village of Adaios and of other outlying
6     places, for the 5th indiction, as follows:
7     The receipt of the same 5th indiction is, of grain by the kankellos-measure, 1201 artabas 5 choinikes, and with the surcharge 239 solidi ½ less 96,
8     and apart from the surcharge 16 solidi
9     out of which there was expended in the s[ame 5th indiction] as follows:
10    to the holy churches of the estate according to custom, of grain by the kankellos-measure, 3 artabas, ½ solidus less ½;
11    for the (days) from the 22nd of the month Epeiph to the 29th, 8 days, daily: of bread 150 pounds, of wine 17 double-jars, of meat 50 pounds, in folles 150,
12    of oil 6 4 sextarii — making for the 8 days: of bread 1200 pounds, of wine 136 jars, of meat 400 pounds, in folles 1200,
13    of oil 50 sextarii;
14    … for the receipt of the villages, of artabas, myriads 5 3000 …
15    … making receipts of grain by the kankellos-measure, of artabas, myriads 16 2313
16    making receipts of the administrators of Alexandria, of solidi, myriad 1 8512 … 4 20 191 ½ 4.
