[TRANSLATION]
1     summary of treasury of Memnoneion [temple] of product of 20th year on behalf [of]
2     receipts of the same 20th year of Aurelii Antoninus
3     and Commodus Caesar our lords
4     Pachon 16 until 20 in name of Psenmor Pmenche[s] Memnoneion wheat artabas 12 and in name of Tellon Harpae[s]
5     wheat artabas 12, Pauni 1 until 5 in name of Te[...] Tellonios
6     wheat artabas 24; they total for the same wheat artabas 6 [and] 24 as was set forth
7     similarly Pauni 1 until 5 in name of Senphoph Harpaesimos wheat artabas 12 and in name of Psenmor Pmenche[s]
8     wheat one-twelfth; it totals wheat artabas 12
9     for the same wheat artabas 6. [I have marked].
