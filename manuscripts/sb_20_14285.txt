1     In the sixth year of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, in the month Neronaios Augustus, the 7th
2     in Oxyrhynchus [cit]y of the Thebaid
3     they acknowledge to one another Dioscorus and [Api]on and Menas and Pet[ri]... chos and Saral[lio]n and Atharous, the six of Komos of the [from Oxy]rhynchus city, the women each with her [lord] Saral[lio]n indeed of the husb[and] herself
4     ... Atharous indeed of the belonging to her Boxos of Heracleides [to be] divided in [the] street
5     [amongst themselves] ... [the] belonging to them[selves] paternal shares four of parts
6     [five of houses two and] ... [and the] belonging ancient fortified post and what falls to them
7     [maternal fifth share of the houses] two, one indeed four-storied and of the belonging courtyard
8     ... and of the being air-chamber in which ...
9     ... house and of the belonging courtyard [and] of the into the aforesaid paternal [and mat]ernal [entries] and [exits] and of the concurrent common and undivided things being in the same city in the of the Lycians called Parembole and maternal
10    slave Nike as of [about] years twelve and the by both of the parents left-behind furnishings and equipment, and from here to have received by lot the one indeed Dioscorus instead of
11    falling to him share of all the aforesaid from the paternal shares four of parts seven of the house and courtyard and of the fortified post shares …
