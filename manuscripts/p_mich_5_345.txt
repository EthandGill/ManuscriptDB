[TRANSLATION]
1     Choiak [13].
2     In the thirty-seventh year of Caesar's
3     dominion, son of god, in the month Pereitios on the thirteenth, Choiak the thirteenth, in
4     Tebtynis of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome.
5     Year 37 of Caesar, Choiak 13. It has been registered
6     through Apion, scribe of Tebtynis.
7     Thenarmaeis daughter of Petesouchos, with my guardian,
8     my own husband Stothoetis son of Stothoetis, acknowledge that I do not and will not bring suit
9     against my brothers Harmaeis and Petesouchos, of the same father and same mother, nor any other
10    on my behalf, neither concerning paternal property nor
11    concerning any matter at all, written or unwritten, from the earlier
12    times up to the aforementioned day. But if I proceed or bring suit or anyone else on my behalf against the aforementioned Harmaeis and Petesouchos, the
13    proceeding and the suit shall be invalid [for me]
14    and for the one proceeding on my behalf, and I shall further pay in addition both the damages
15    and double the expenses, and if I transgress, [the] penalty of two hundred silver drachmas
16    and the damage, and nonetheless, or apart from this,
17    the things mutually agreed shall be valid
18    for all time as has been written above. Maron
19    son of Euhemeros wrote on their behalf, having been requested, because they do not know
20    letters.
21    … Harmaeis and
22    Petesouchos.
