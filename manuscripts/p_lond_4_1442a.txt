[TRANSLATION]
1     … [item]s on account
2     of the building and repair and embellishment and equipping of karaboi (boats) and akatenaria and dromonaria and others that are within the island of Babylon:
3     … of the village of Aphrodito, 42½ solidi, thus: acacias 20, 20 solidi; of sycamores … 604, 1 solidus + ⅓; oil, 7 measures, 2 solidi ½ + ⅓ + 1/12; cables of sebennytic-fibre 3, 3½ solidi; thin sebennytic (ropes) 2, 1 solidus + ⅓.
4     fine tow 1, 1 solidus; small cords 50, 2 solidi + 1/12; masert 1, 1 solidus ½ + ⅓.
5     mats 28, 7 solidi; of tow, 2 centenaria, 1 solidus ½.
6     of 5 Pediades, 15 solidi; acacias 4, 4 solidi; split palm-beams 3, 1 solidus; mats 40, 10 solidi …
7     of 3 Pediades, 8 solidi; acacias 2, 2 solidi; mats 24, 6 solidi.
8     of 2 Pediades, 5½ + ⅓ solidi; 1, 1 solidus; split (beam) 1, ⅓ of a solidus; mats 18, 4½ solidi …
9     [of the people of Holy Maria, 1 solidus + ⅔;] 2, ⅔ + ¼ of a solidus, 1 solidus.
10    [Of Pakaunis, 22 solidi;] 6, 6 solidi; split (beams) 3, 1 solidus; 56, 14 solidi; stakes 6, 1 solidus.
11    … 11, 2 solidi ½ + ¼.
12    Of Keramion, 1 solidus + ⅓; [1 solidus.]
13    Of Poimen, ⅔ of a solidus; 2, [⅔ of a solidus.]
14    Of Holy Pinoution, 1 solidus ½; of saggathos, 6 centenaria, 1 solidus ½.
15    … [1 solidus + ¼,] 1, ¼ of a solidus; 4 centenaria, 1 solidus.
16    … 2 centenaria, ½ of a solidus.
17    … [1,] ⅓ of a solidus; 6, 1 solidus ½.
18    Of Holy Maria, ½ of a solidus; 2, ½ of a solidus.
19    total, 105 solidi: acacias 33, 33 solidi; of sycamores … 604, 1 solidus + ⅓; oil, 7 measures, 2 solidi ½ + ⅓ + 1/12; cables of sebennytic-fibre 3, 3½ solidi; thin sebennytic (ropes) 2, 1 solidus + ⅓; fine tow, 1 solidus; small cords 50, 2 solidi + 1/12; masert 1, 1 solidus ½ + ⅓; mats 182, 45½ solidi; of tow, 2 centenaria, 1 solidus ½.
20    split palm-beams 15, 5 solidi; stakes 6, 1 solidus; of saggathos, 20 centenaria, 5 solidi.
