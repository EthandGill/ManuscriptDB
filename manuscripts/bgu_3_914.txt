[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixteenth year of Imperator Caesar Nerva
2     Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, in the month Kaisareios 26.
3     There has paid, to the sales-tax (enkyklion) bank in Ptolemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite (nome),
4     to the account of Demetrios also called Philotas
5     and partners, the sales-tax (collectors): Tachnoubis (daughter) of Orsenouphis
6     (daughter of) ...oros, from the village of Karanis, the tax on the cession
7     of an empty, walled-round plot of two hundred twenty-five cubits,
8     ...by ...phris (son?) of Maritios
9     ...pheri.thos and ... of Helene and [...]
10    ...os, in return for which she owed, one hundred drachmas,
11    ...of the agreed thirty-two drachmas:
12    Demetrios also called Philotas (received) the price of the empty walled-round plot,
13    of silver thirty-two drachmas. (hand 2) I, Andronikos, have signed.
14    (hand 3) ...s, I have signed. (hand 4) Athenodoros, I have signed.
