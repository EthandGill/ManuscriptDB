[TRANSLATION]
1   Zosimos to Sarapion,
2   his dearest (friend), greetings.
3   I have sent to you my
4   man, so that you may
5   do well to settle (the matter)
6   about the olive-grove that belongs
7   to him here; for recently,
8   being harassed
9   by the tax-collectors on account of an impost,
10   his fruit-contractor(?)
11   sent me, through Soterichos
12   the camel-driver, word: 'At-
13   tend to it, until I come to you'; and
14   I promised them the
15   matter, as (if) he were coming;
16   but just now I heard from the fruit-
17   contractor that it is being sold to the chief
18   of the royal (estate). So one must pre-
19   pare and take (it) in hand, so that he may have (means) of
20   selling; for I have the prior (claim?),
21   since I am his neighbour.
22   So you will do <well> to make known
23   to me how things stand; for, knowing
24   to whom I am writing, also our
25   guards (will be) a trouble against
26   …her(?). I pray for your health.
