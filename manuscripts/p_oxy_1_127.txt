[TRANSLATION]
1   † The glorious house of the Oxyrhynchites contributes, for the grain-shipment (embole), by the cancellus-measure 8 myriads of artabas of grain and
2   7018 1/2; of these, for the mint-levy,
3   at 55 solidi 12 carats per myriad, total 487 solidi 8 carats, Alexandrian standard;
4   of which there was sent to Alexandria, through Menas notary and escort,
5   and paid to the most distinguished bankers Ioannes
6   and Theodoros, 484 solidi 22 carats, Alexandrian standard. Remainder: 2 solidi 10 carats, Alexandrian standard.
7   † The glorious house of the Kynopolites contributes, for the grain-shipment,
8   by the cancellus-measure 5 myriads and 2,8[..] of grain; of these,
9   for the mint-levy, at 55 solidi 12 carats per myriad,
10   total 293 solidi, Alexandrian standard; of which there was sent to Alexandria through Menas
11   notary and escort, and paid to the most distinguished
12   bankers Ioannes and Theodoros, 285 solidi 1 carat, Alexandrian standard.
13   Remainder: [7] solidi [23 carats], Alexandrian standard.
