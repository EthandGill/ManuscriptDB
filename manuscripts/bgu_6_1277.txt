[TRANSLATION]
1     … Sosib[ios]
2     of the month Xandicos in Thol[thi] of the Oxyrhynchite nome. [So-and-so] lent
3     [Theog]onos Ainian of Philon, pentakosiarch, to Praxo of Proxenos, Cyrenaean, with her guardian Menon of Kallios, [a] Persian
4     of the produce of spelt artabas fifty, let [he/she] repay
5     Praxo to Theogonos this grain in the month Dystros
6     [of] the ninth year [spel]t clean [and unadulterated] by measure choinix in fair measure with fair measuring and let [it] be carried away
7     to Tholthi wherever Theogonos may direct at [his] own expense,
8     but if [he/she] does not repay according to what has been written, let Praxo pay to Theog[onos] the price for each artaba of the spelt
9     [in silv]er … drachmas four, and let the right of distress be [Theog]ono's against Praxo [distrain]ing according to the ordinance, but the
10    [contract] this [valid] shall be wherever it may be presented. Witnesses
