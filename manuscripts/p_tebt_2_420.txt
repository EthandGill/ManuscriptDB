[TRANSLATION]
1   Aure[liu]s Sarapion to Polion
2   his brother and Diogenes
3   his father, very many greetings.
4   You know that I am out of pocket, and
5   without reason you went off from me with-
6   out giving me coppers. I bought
7   2 rolls for the person-
8   lists of the grain-dues of the royal
9   (scribe), and 28 dr. for the agreement
10   of the …, again for the royal
11   (scribe). So do everything — as
12   there is necessity — send me
13   my 52 dr.; and collect for me too,
14   on my account, another 60 dr., for I have
15   need: the … is half done.
16   Without fail then, my lord brother
17   Polion, remember me — what I too
18   have done for you from beginning to
19   end, and I serve you still. They have urged
20   my brother to
21   come to you about the bar-
22   ley; and tell Diogenes that he too
23   should give an artaba of barley on the ac-
24   count of supplies. So give your con-
25   sent, that it be fetched up without fail,
26   for I am shut up till
27   today in the account-bureau;
28   and let me know without fail
29   what you have done about me. I pray for your health.
30   [To …]on my brother — Sarapi(on).
