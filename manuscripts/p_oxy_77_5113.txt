[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings, child Phanius,
2     from Dorion.
3     Gladly I learned you safe
4     you to have returned and gratitude
5     I have to the Polieus Sarapis that strong he restored you. Many
6     me the troubling things were, child
7     Phanius, with [myself] so that me
8     not to be at rest, which not even you
9     perhaps are ignorant; I pray moreover
10    all of us according to mind [to do]; perhaps for [better] [fortune]
11    having obtained [we shall be able]
12    again [to do] as [we wish].
