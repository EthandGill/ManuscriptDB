[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the] holy and [life-giving and]
2     [consubstantial Trinity, of the] Father and Son [and] holy [Spirit]
3     [of the kingdom of the most] divine [of] our masters Fl[avius]
4     [Heraclius and] Heraclius the New Constantine of the eternal
5     [A]ugustuses [A]utocrators, in the twenty-eighth year, Phamenoth
6     on the twentieth of the eleventh indiction, in the city of Hermes of the
7     Thebaid.
8     Aurelius Danielis, son of Enoch from the mother Susanna,
9     farmer from the Hermopolitan [district], to Aurelius Samuel
10    son of Phoibammon from the same city,
11    greetings. I acknowledge having received and been fully paid
12    by you the complete and fitting price of wine,
13    new must, of two hundred fifty measures—
14    totaling wine measures 250 [which] I will provide you in the month Mesore
15    of the twelfth indiction, God willing, in wine
16    new, finest and pleasing, by the measure of the
17    jar, without any postponement and dispute
18    and judgment and lawsuit, and if there should be found vinegar or
19    unripe or foul-smelling from it until the month Tybi
20    of the same indiction, I acknowledge [I will] exchange for you
21    in good wine without postponement. But if I [do not] give you
22    the same wine within the same deadline
23    I acknowledge [I will] provide you in place of its price
24    gold coins, four, immediately after the
25    deadline [at] my risk and expense of
26    all my property. The present
27    document [is] binding and firm and being questioned
28    I acknowledged. Aurelius Danielis, son of Enoch,
29    placed this document as aforesaid.
30    Aurelius Jacob, [son] of Kollouthos, from the city of Hermes
31    having been asked, I wrote on his behalf letters, [because he was] not
32    knowing [how to write]. Aurelius John, son of the blessed
33    Theodore, from Hermopolis, I witness to the document
34    having heard [it] from the one who placed [it]. Aurelius
35    Christodorus, [son] of Tiberius, from the city of Hermes, I witness
36    to the document having heard [it] from the one who placed [it].
37    Through me [L]eontius, [with God's will], notary, it was written, Phamenoth twenty-second
38    [handwritten document] of wine, measures 250, made by Danielius Enoch, farmer from the city of Hermes
