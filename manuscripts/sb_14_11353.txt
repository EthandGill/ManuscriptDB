[TRANSLATION]
1     from Kallinikos the most illustrious.
2     to Papnouthis the chief guard. I received one whole nummus at ker[a]tia four
3     from the public [funds] of village Tanemoe[sis], from Oros of Taurinus, equals of gold coin 1 at 4 only.
4     Mechir 26, of 5 indiction. Kallinikos I agree.
