[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most excellent council of Hermopolis the great
2     ancient, splendid and most august,
3     through Aurelius Alexandros also called Korellios,
4     who has served as eutheniarch, gymnasiarch, councillor, prytanis in office,
5     from Aurelius Arachthes son of Peionis, his mother being
6     Telytis, from the village of Thelbonthis. I wish willingly to lease from the civic account
7     for four years from the present 14th year
8     around the same Thelbonthis, from the
9     allotment of Philokrates, six arouras for the sowing of wheat
10    and fodder each year, at the rate of half, as rent and fixed payment each year
11    of the whole, fifteen artabas of wheat and sixty silver drachmas
12    and as additional charge upon the whole twenty-eight
13    drachmas, which rents and payments
14    I shall render and measure out in the months of Pauni
15    (and) Epeiph each year, the silver being approved and the wheat into the public granary, one (measure)
16    by the receiving-measure instead of one Athenian measure, of which
17    I shall also make a clean measuring-out in your name, the public dues and additional levies being charged to the account of the estate,
18    and on being formally questioned I acknowledged. Year 14 of the Emperor
19    Caesar Publius Licinius
20    Gallienus Germanicus Maximus
21    Persicus Maximus Pius
22    Felix Augustus, Hathyr 30.
