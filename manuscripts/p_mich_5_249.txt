[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year] 4 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Mesore, supplementary days 5. [This is] a sale [document].
2     [Tasoucho]s of Sekonopeia, mother Souherios, [I] acknowledge [that I] have sold according to Egyptian contracts to Patyni[s] Harapeios, mother Tapetesouchos, [that which] belongs to me
3     [the third] maternal share of a house and courtyard, [both] common and [indivisible] and all [that is] connected in Talei of the Polemon division, of which [are the] neighbors of the whole maternal
4     [share on the south] royal street, on the north [the] places of Horos of Horos and [his] brothers, on the west [the] house of Nektonnephios of Sekonopeia, on the east [the house of] Akouslaos of Nei…, and I have received the entire agreed price in full and I will guarantee [it] with every guarantee for all time, and I will do the other things as
5     [is set forth]. [Paponto]s [son of] Petosouches wrote on her behalf … because she does not know letters. Patynis Harapeios, the sale has come to me of the third
6     [maternal] share of house and courtyard, and I have given the price as is set forth. Eutychos [son of] Eutychos wrote on his behalf because he does not know letters.
