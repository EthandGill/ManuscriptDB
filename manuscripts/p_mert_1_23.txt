1     Paraïs to [Dionysius] the [beloved] greetings.
2     Before all things [I pray] you to be well with the
3     of [all] your things. The one carrying to you Taphorsis, [T]apasiries, mother of Horos, from Palôs, this
4     Provide this [letter] to her for two arourae
5     concerning the village Netrô, five hundred [drachmas] of silver; and if you wish in the name [of me] the
6     transaction [make], or if you wish in your name indifferent. [And] take up
7     for interest of each mina, per month drachmas
8     four; [less] than a year do not recover
9     the interest, but the whole year recover
10    the interest. I pray you be well.
