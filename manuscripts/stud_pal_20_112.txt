1     I, Sergios, grain-measurer of the village of Twenty-Five-Aruras, ninth indiction, [owe] to the lord Philoxenos
2     to the overseer of the grain-measurers, in one [sum], one nomisma, seven [and] half [and] quarter
3     [of] golden nomisma, one [and] half [and] quarter, and with god I shall return this in [the] month of Thoth, [at the] end of the entering ninth indiction
4     and what for wages I measure [in] nomismata, [one-]half, as is reckoned, [of] golden nomisma, [one-]half, as
5     is reckoned, together, [in] nomisma, [one-]half, as is reckoned
6     [it] was written in [the] month of Pauni, [the] 26th, eighth indiction, [in] my hand, Georgios
