[TRANSLATION]
1     agrees Philotera, daughter of Philon, a Cretan woman, to Akesandros
2     of Nikanor, [a] Macedonian. If I run away from you
3     or am reconciled with Peron, I will pay to Akesandros
4     forty drachmas; with Akesandros minded of my things and thinking of my things, I will bring in to him all [my]
5     private [property] as much as belongs [to Philotera]. Year 39 Choiak 22.
6     And [Philotera] will enter to Akesandros on 1 Tybi.
7     agrees [Akesandros] of Nikanor, [a] Macedonian, to Philotera
8     [daughter] of Philon [Cretan] … to have you as wife and to provide … I have clothing, shoes … she will provide subsistence, but [Akesandros] …
