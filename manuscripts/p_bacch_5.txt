1     [From Peteüris son of Peteüris, the el]der, of the priests
2     [of Soknobraisis the great god,] of the great village
3     [of Bakchias. A register of the priests an]d of the inventory of the
4     present 28th year. [And it is, of] the things in the temple of So[k]nobraisis, a wooden shrine overlaid with gold, and of Pnepheros
5     a sh[ri]ne over[l]aid with gol[d]; lampstands of bronze, trumpet-shaped, 15;
6     [a]n incense-burner of bronze, 1; drinking-cups of bronze, 10; a small trumpet
7     of bronze, 1; two wooden rods having seven bronze leaves;
8     [wooden] discs, 2, overl[aid with gold]; a bronze vessel of bronze, 1.
9     And it is, of [t]he priests, [man by ma]n, all having been assessed at 12 drachmas
10    and having paid the entrance-fee:
