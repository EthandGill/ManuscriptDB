[TRANSLATION]
1     Theon to Sambas the oil-maker:
2     a quarter of a xestes of oil provide
3     to Helladios, in the service of(?) Eustathios the magistrate (archon),
4     of oil, a quarter of a xestes. Pharmouthi 24(?), 1st(?) indiction.
5     (hand 2) † I have signed for a quarter of a xestes of oil only †.
