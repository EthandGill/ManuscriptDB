[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Amyntas to Zenon, greetings. Kallianax the builder, the catamite, got drunk and … the couches which he has readied for Apollonios. And he, receiving in advance from us for … money, they are altering … by us … the debt collector, he has sailed up, as it seems, to have an audience with Apollonios against the … the man who tracked him down. If, therefore, you meet someplace, it is good that you not be unaware … to explain to Apollonios not to trust him. Farewell … (Letter) of Amyntas concerning Kallianax. Year 28, Audnaios 25, in the harbor. To Zenon.
