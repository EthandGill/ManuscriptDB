[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most glorious] in the eleventh year, Mechir 17, first indiction
2     ... [to the most admirable one] from the Hermopolites
3     [By] ... [mother] Nonnas, farmer and carpenter, from
4     [the same city. I acknowledge willingly] and voluntarily to have rented from you
5     [for] ... [years, time reckoned] from the crops now in fields
6     [of the present first indiction, and] from it the arable land belonging to you
7     [one aroura from the farm] of five arouras in common [sharing]
8     [of] ... [the remaining] arouras for completion [of the same five arouras] situated in the east
9     [of the city, in place] called Tkonelikon fields
10    [of village] ... [of the Hermopolite nome] for sowing and [depositing]
11    [of crops which I might choose, rent] of the land, to the watered [portion] per year
12    ... and [to] the unwatered, which not [be], I will [provide]
