[TRANSLATION]
1   Horos, to Apion the most honoured, greetings.
2   To Lampon the mouse-catcher I gave, through you, a de-
3   posit of 8 (drachmas), so that he may catch mice at Toka. You will do well
4   to send them (the drachmas) to me. And to Dionysios, president of Nemerai,
5   I have lent 8 (drachmas), and these he has not sent — that you may know (it).
6   Farewell. Pauni 24.
