[TRANSLATION]
1     (Christian symbol) 29
2     After the consulship of Flavius [Basil] the most glorious, in the twenty-first year … of the eleventh indiction.
3     To Aurelia Archontia, daughter of A …, and Vanthia, daughter of John, from the (city) of the Hermopolites, from A[u]relius Horitos son of Isidorus,
4     his mother being Heraeïs, [both] from the village of Sinakyreos in the nome of Hermoupolis, greeting. I, the aforewritten
5     Horitos, acknowledge through this my [written] surety that of my own free will and choice I have leased from you, for as long a time as you may wish, reckoned [from] the crops of the prosperous coming twelfth indiction and that very (year), the three arouras belonging to you,
6     dry land … [in a vine]yard, more or less, the reed-stubble being cleared away, out of the … from the …
7     accordingly, together with the whole cistern and the water-supply and the wooden (irrigation) machine and all the appurtenances and every
8     right, situated in the plain of the village of Pesla in the nome of Hermoupolis, from the so-called machine of Aphous,
9     on condition that the watering be done by means of my own animals which are also fed (by me), up to the vine-tending
10    work, and (with) all care and good keeping and five diggings in the year … and …
11    … shall carry out for you, and the produce remaining over in your … to be apportioned, the things being managed …
