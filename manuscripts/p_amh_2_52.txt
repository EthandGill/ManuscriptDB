r.1    ἔτους λα Φαμενὼθ ια. τέτακται ἐπὶ τὴν ἐν Ἑρμώνθει τράπεζαν ἐφʼ ἧς Ἀπολλώνιος εἰκοστῆς ἐγκυκλίου κατὰ τὴν παρὰ Ζμῖνος καὶ τῶν μετόχων τῶν πρὸς τῆι ὠνῆι

[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 31, Phamenōth 11: [it] has been set up upon the bank in Hermopolis, of which [is] Apollonios of the twentieth circle, according to the [agreement] from Zmines and the associates, those concerning the purchase
2     contract under which Ammonios the scribe subscribed, [for] the purchase [of] Pechytes of Arsiesis, [of] the fourth share of all which Arsiesis the father of him transferred
3     [for] Senthote of Panates; and [it] has been valued at 2 talents, of which the exchange [is] six hundred, totaling 600. Apollonios, banker.
