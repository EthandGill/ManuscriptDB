[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 15, Phaophi 13, in Pathyris, before Hermias [the (agent)]
2     of Paniskos the agoranomos.
3     Kollouthes son of Ph[age]ris, Persian of the
4     epigone, acknowledges to Horos son of N[echou]thes, Persian, that he withdraws from
5     and will not proceed against the dry (i.e. non-flooded) grain-bearing land belonging to him which is
6     in the plain about Pathyris, three (3) arouras
7     of the seven arouras, and the adjoining waste-plot, in two parcels, of which the neighbors
8     of the one (parcel): on the south the land of Patephis, on the north the land
9     of Pakoibis through the sons of Phibis, on the east the road
10    royal, on the west the land of Psemmonthes son of Panechat[es]
11    and of his associates; and of the other: on the south the land
12    of Pakoibis, on the north the land of Tathotis, on the east the royal road,
13    on the west the land of Poregebthis and of Paos, or whoever
14    may be the neighbors of the two parcels, the deed
15    of cession of which was completed in the 14th year, Phaophi 10,
16    for the (land) remaining to me unsold, of land of 3 arouras,
17    and (he acknowledges) that neither he himself,
18    Kollouthes, nor anyone else on his behalf,
19    will proceed against Horos, nor against any other of those
20    on his side, concerning the 3 arouras; but if not, the recourse for the one proceeding (against) shall be invalid,
21    and the one who proceeds shall pay in addition as a penalty
22    forthwith of bronze 6 talents, and sacred to the kings of coined silver 120 drachmas, and none the less it shall be compulsory for him to act according to [the] aforewritten (terms).
23    Hermias the (agent) of Paniskos: I have transacted (this).
24    Acknowledgment of Horos son of Nechouthes
25    from Kollouthes son of Phageris,
26    of land of 3 arouras.
