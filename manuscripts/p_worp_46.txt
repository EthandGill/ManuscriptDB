# HGV-EN: Herakleides and Isidoros, tax-farmers of the Gate of Syene, through the assistant Pachompsachis. Petorzmethis, son of Harpaesis, whose mother is Marcia, paid for trade-tax of the xth year twelve drachmas, makes 12 drachmas. Year x of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, on the 12th of (month). The same paid for trade-tax of the xth year eight drachmas, two obols, makes 8 drachmas 2 obols. (F. A. J. Hoogendijk)
