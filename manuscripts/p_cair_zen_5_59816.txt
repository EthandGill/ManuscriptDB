[TRANSLATION]
1     Artemidoros to Panakestor, greeting. When I was coming from Boubastos to Mem[phis]
2     Apollonios was instructing (me) preferably to go through to you myself, but if not, to send
3     someone from me who would report to you the (matters) from him. For he had heard that not all
4     the land, the ten thousand arourai, is being sown over. He therefore directed (me) to report to you so that all of it might be cleared of wood and watered
5     and [especiall]y so that you sow all of it over, but if not, as much as you are not abl[e]
6     to sow. … let it be sown with sesame and let no part of the land lie idle. Since [then] I myse[lf]
7     have not been ab[le to a]rrive because of being hindered, having written I have sent off
8     [to] you, so that you may kno[w]
9     and do thu[s]. For he was directing (you) to hire and to throw in en masse …
10    the sowers, the hoers, and those who will serve these. While, then, it is still the seas[on]
11    for sow[ing], do thus. And I have reported also to Zenon and to Artemidoros [th]e (one) in M[emphis],
12    just as Apo[llo]nios instructed me, so that they may supply you with bronze, as much as you have need [of]
13    for these (things). Take it, then; for it will be given to you. And they said also no[w ] …
14    to Maron 1 dra(chma).
15    Farewell. Year 29, A[pel]laios 2.
16    To Philadelphia. To Panakestor.
17    Year 29, Hathyr 9. Artemidoros.
