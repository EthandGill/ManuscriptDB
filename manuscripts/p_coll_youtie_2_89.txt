[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year after the consulship of Flavius Theodorichus the most illustrious, Epeiph 4,
2     [9th] indiction.
3     To Flavius Ioannes son of Taurinos, the venerable, of the rank of former primicerii of the unit
4     of the Moors stationed in the territory of Hermopolis,
5     from Aurelius Isakios son of Papnouthios, on the mother's side of Sophia, from
6     the hamlet called Sentapao in the district of the praktoria of the village
7     of Ptemenkyrkis of the Shepherds in the Hermopolite nome.
8     I acknowledge that I have willingly and of my own accord leased
9     from your Admirableness for a three-year period
10    [the] vineyard-plot belonging to your Admirableness,
11    of however many arouras it consists, with all its plantings, [from the produce] of the fortunate
12    tenth indiction, situated in the district of the praktoria of the same village, for
13    manual vine-dressing labor and all care and good cultivation
14    and tending and reed-cutting and weeding and irreproachable digging-round, on condition that you, the landowner, receive three-quarters of all the wine that shall be produced, and from the common vat one hundred jars of wine, while to me the lessee, that is the vine-dresser, belongs the other quarter-share in return for the labors I perform; and I shall dig the same vineyard a fourth (annually) each year, and I shall furnish at the time of the vintage a piglet worth eight thousand talents of silver and a platter worth talents of silver
15    four thousand; and I shall furnish each year, on account of the rent of the peach-trees(?) there, … of gold solidi … or, if in wine, ninety jars of wine, without delay. The lease is valid
16    and secure, and having been asked the formal question I assented. I, Aurelius Isakos son of Papnouthios, the aforesaid, have leased as is set forth. I, Aurelius Phoibammon
17    son of Taurinos, from Hermopolis, having been requested, wrote on his behalf since he does not know letters.
18    I, Aurelius Theodoros son of L … ou, bear witness to the lease, having been asked by the lessee.
19    I, Aurelius Do … from Hermopolis, bear witness to the lease, having been asked by the lessee.
20    I, Aurelius … s son of Theodoros, [from] Hermopolis, bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the contracting party.
21    Through me … [it] was written.
22    Lease of Isakos son of Papnouthios from …
