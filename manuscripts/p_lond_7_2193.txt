[TRANSLATION]
1     Horion son of Haryotes, of the flock of goats, drachmas
2     thirty-two, total, 32.
3     Good fortune.
4     The law which they who are of the association of Zeus the Highest established in common, that this be valid,
5     and acting as it directs: first, having chosen for themselves
6     as president Petesouchos son of Teephbennis, a man of repute, worthy of the place and of the men,
7     for a year from the aforewritten month and day; and all the contributors to make each month one drinking-session in the (temple) of Zeus,
8     in his shrine, in which, in the common men's-hall, pouring libations let them pray and (perform) the other customary rites both for the god and the lord king; and that all shall obey both the president and his attendant in the matters pertaining to the association; and they shall be present at the
9     instructions given to them and the assemblies and gatherings and journeys abroad;
10    and let it not be permitted to any of them to … to form factions nor to set up schisms,
11    nor to withdraw from the president's fraternity into another fraternity,
12    and not … one (against) the other in the banquet, nor that one revile the other in the banquet, nor speak (against him), nor accuse (him), nor bring a charge against the other, nor give renunciation
13    during the year; and not to defile the banquets nor to press always from place to place, nor from one couch to the other; and not to hinder the
14    … in public, and instead of … both toward the contributions and the other (things) toward
15    … and each of them to bring in
