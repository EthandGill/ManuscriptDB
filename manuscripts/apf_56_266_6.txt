[TRANSLATION]
1     Pamonthes [son] of Haruothes has recorded on behalf of poll-tax [of year ten],
2     ten drachmas and of bath-tax one drachma and half-obol, [amount to drachmas 11, obol and half-obol, and additionally recorded]
3     Year ten of Tiberius Claudius Caesar [Augustus]
4     Germanicus, Autocrator, Phamemoth 33, Alexandros
5     of Kephalos.
