[TRANSLATION]
1     To Demetrius, strategos, and to the others to whom it pertains,
2     [f]rom Petosiris son of Dionysius the son of Petosiris, his mother being
3     Mieus daughter of Horos, of those from the city of Oxyrhynchus. In accordance with the orders given by Rammius Martialis the most excellent prefect
4     it was filed.
5     I register, for the house-by-house census of the past 2nd year of Hadrianus Caesar
6     the lord, the property belonging to me at the quarter
7     of the North Dromos, a half share of a house, and to my wife Tetoeus
8     daughter of Th[oo]nas, formerly belonging to her father, of the remainder a half (and) the third, and
9     [li]kewise there belongs to her, formerly belonging to Petosiris her brother,
10    a third part of the same half share, with whom is also the remaining
11    third part for the completion of the half share, which Papontos her other brother, in a security made overdue,
12    in place of the share falling due, of the (sums owed) accord[ing to a loan-contract]
13    ow[ed] to Tetoeus by Papon[tos] …
14    …laxos, with a scar on the left foot, 74 years old.
15    Ti… his mother being Tetoeus daughter of Thoonas, stonecutter(?),
16    a scar on the right foot, 45 years old.
17    Apollonides son of Petosiris the son of Thoonios, his mother being Taps[ois] …
18    of Paulos, stonecutter, without distinguishing marks, 21 years old,
19    residing in a share of a house at the same quarter.
20    Papontos son of Thoonas the son of Petosiris, his mother being Thaisous daughter of Papontos, stonecutter,
21    now being abroad, 57 years old.
22    Total: 4 persons.
23    … son of Thoonas the son of Petosiris, his mother being Thaisous …
24    … without distinguishing marks, about 3 years old …
25    Thaesis daughter of Tho…, her slave, bought, without distinguishing marks, about 30 years old.
26    Total: 2 persons.
27    And likewise there belong to me at th[e] same quarter other shares of ho[uses]
28    held in common also with Petosiris son of Tho[onas], in which no one re[sides][,]
29    but in my own house I make declaration.
30    Theon, his mother being Thermo[u]th[ion] [daughter of Ne]chthenibis, e … d …
31    in the 10th year of the god Vesp[as]ia[n by the ho]use-by-house [census] …
32    … s a … ap …
33    those up to the pres[ent] …
34    and I declare also a right …
35    and of Petosiris son of Thoonas, of shares of a house …
