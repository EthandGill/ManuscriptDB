[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulate of Rufius Volusianus and Petronius
2     Annianus, [of] the most illustrious
3     To Aurelius Adelphius, also [called] Dionysodorus, and as you are styled
4     From Aurelius Silvanus [son] of Ibis, mother Tanenther[ia], from the village of Selilae. I wish willingly to lease from you for the present year of the 3rd indiction from the lands belonging to you around the same [Selilae] called Phreatōs for sowing of barley
5     [at fixed rent] of barley, thirty artabae, which
6     I shall measure [out] to you in the Pauni and Epeiph months of the same
7     [3rd indiction] without fail, of new barley, clean, unadulterated
8     [undeducted], sifted, measured by the measure of the estate, [six-part measure] … …
