[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Dositheus of Dionysodorus, having served as councillor
2     of Panosopolis
3     from Aurelius Besapollon surnamed Horkatetes from Theos.
4     I have leased from you for the present [year]
5     your share from ships, other [portions] [according to] the individual
6     of grain crops, a rent according to
7     the partners, of all the public
8     to you the landholder.
9     of the consulate of Flavius Gallicanus and
10    Aurelius Symmachus of the most illustrious,
11    Phaophi 30.
12    It was written through Theodore.
