1     … …

9     half an aroura, being in the catoecic category, whose neighbours of the first parcel are: to south and west, (the land) of Longeinia Gemella; to north, the canal called Thelamoui; to east, the olive-grove of the (heirs) of Kastol, in which there is a watch-tower; and of the second parcel: to south, the olive-grove of Didymarion who is conveying (it); to north, the aforewritten canal; to east and west, the olive-grove of the aforesaid Sempronia Gemella; and in the same plain, in the place called Chalkologos, of an olive-grove in the catoecic category, half part of one aroura from the eastern side, extending from south to north, of which whole the neighbours are: to south, a phalanx (ridge); to north and west,

11    in the place of the house of Kann…, of the (land) called, two-thirds part of a catoecic allotment of three arouras,
12    of which whole the neighbours are: to south and west, (the land) of the aforewritten Sempronius Longeinos; to north, (the land) of the heirs of Magna his sister; to east, an aqueduct, from which the allotment is watered, … public land; and in the same plain, in the place of the house of Sa…
