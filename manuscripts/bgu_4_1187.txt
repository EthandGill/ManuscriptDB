[TRANSLATION]
1   To Andromachos, (royal) kinsman and strategos and
2   in charge of the revenues,
3   from Kastor son of Polydeukes, of
4   the village of Tokoeos. There belonging to me
5   in the same village, by maternal (inheritance),
6   bare plots of one and a half bikos,
7   of which the possession and ownership
8   belonged to my mother, and
9   for the time she lived holding and own-
10   ing (them) unhindered, with no one
11   at al[l h]in[dering, she continued;]
12   and [... after her]
13   death, lord, [... the]
14   aforesaid …[...]
15   it has befallen me (that) Sen[...]
16   daughter of Semtheus, and Stotoe daughter of Pne[...]
17   (son) of …ion, of the sam[e village,]
18   have alienated the desig-
19   nated plots, with no
20   right to lay claim, but
21   using the violence and willfulness that is theirs,
22   thinking
23   to get away with it and not to render
24   the account concerning these things. Being in dan-
25   ger, then, of being deprived of my own,
26   if I do not obtain your
27   assistance, I ask, if it seem good,
28   to order them brought before you
29   and that I obtain my rights;
30   and (that) they, for what is fitting, with regard to
31   the attention of others — there remaining to me
32   the ownership and lordship
33   of the said plots,
34   inasmuch as they are indeed ours, that I may be
35   [a]ssisted.
36   Farewell.
