[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Zenon [greetings]. I have sent to you
2     the copies of the [letter] to Nikerate.
3     whenever [the] wood is unloaded into the …,
4     come and arrange [to transport] [through the]
5     canal to [Philadelphia], and this carefully
6     let it be done.
7     Farewell. Year 32, Hyperberetaeus 25, Phaophi 25.
8     To Nikerate. The wood which was bought for us through Phanesis
9     we have arranged to load into ships and to send
10    to you. Whenever [it] arrives, arrange to unload
11    and let it be a care to you that it be preserved;
12    for we have arranged with Zenon to transport through the canal
13    to Philadelphia. Farewell.
14    To Zenon.
