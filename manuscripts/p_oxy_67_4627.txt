[TRANSLATION]
1     to my lord brother Hierapollon
2     Serenus, greetings.
3     I wonder how until now you have remained with yourself. No small
4     certainly into me anxiety you have cast [with] this
5     having done. And if now then either quickly reach me or write
6     to me what is the delay, before all [other things]
7     but of all things about your safety and about what you need there. I greet the
8     my lady sister and my lady mother and all the
9     ones of ours. Be well you I pray
10    for many times.
11    Pachon 20.
12    to my lord brother Hierapollon, Sarammou.
