[TRANSLATION]
1   Dionysia daughter of Chairemon, with
2   her guardian, her son Apion also called Dionysios son of Dioge-
3   nes, both from the city of Oxyrhynchus, leased to Psena-
4   mounis son of Thonis, mother Seoeris, from Paker-
5   ke of the eastern toparchy, a Persian of the epigone,
6   for six years from the present sixth ye[ar]
7   of Antoninus Caesar the lord, the (land) belong-
8   ing to her and previously farmed by Psena-
9   mounis under another lease: thirty-
10   eight arouras — so that, for the fixed
11   five years, he may sow and cultivate with whatever he
12   chooses, apart from woad and ochomenion(?),
13   and in the last year likewise sow
14   and cultivate with the crops indicated through the previous
15   lease, he being required to produce them
16   in the last year; at a rent,
17   for all the arouras, over the six-year period, per
18   [y]ear, of a fixed quantity of wheat: one hun-
19   dre[d and] ninety artabas; and likewise of a libation pe[r]
20   [yea]r of twelve drachmas — everything free of risk,
21   of every risk, the public charges on the land be-
22   ing (charged) to the lessor, who also is to own
23   the crops until she rec-
24   overs what is owed each year. And if in the following years any (land)
25   goes unwatered, an allowance shall be made to the
26   lessee; and he, the lease being guaranteed,
27   (2nd hand) shall measure out, from the
28   yearly fixed quantity, into the public
29   granary the yearly grain-charges
30   on the holdings, at his own
31   expense, and the receipt for them let him hand over to the les-
32   sor, clear of everything, each ye-
33   ar at the first measuring, an allow-
34   ance being made to him of one (artaba) for one; and the
35   rest of the yearly rents and
36   the libation let him pay to her always in the mo-
37   nth Pauni, at the threshing-floor of Pakerke: new wheat,
38   clean, unadulterated, free of barley, sift-
39   ed, as measured into the publ[ic] (granary),
40   by the four-choinix bronze-rimmed
41   receiving-measure of the lessor [or]
42   of those measuring for her. And whatever the les-
43   see may additionally owe, let him pay
44   with half as much again; and let the right of execution belong to the
45   lessor against both the les-
46   see and a[l]l his belong-
47   ings — the lessor not being permitted
48   to lease (it) out to others nor to farm it herself with-
49   in the term. The lease is valid. Year 6
50   of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
51   Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius,
52   Phaophi 5. (3rd hand) I, Psenamounis son of Thonis, have
53   leased the land for the six years
54   at a fixed quantity per year of wheat: one hundred
55   and ninety artabas, and a libation of
56   twelve drachmas, and I shall
57   pay everything as stated above. I, Theon
58   son of Theon, mother Ophelia, wro-
59   te on his behalf, as he does not know let-
60   ters. The date is the same.
