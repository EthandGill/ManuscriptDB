[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy, in the twelfth year, when Leontiskos son of
2     Kallimedes was priest, in the month Peritios, in Pitos of the
3     Memphite nome. Dionysios son of Apollonios, a Gazaean of those about Deinon, lent to Isidoros, a Thracian, a forty-aroura holder of those of Lykophron, thirty-four drachmas of silver, this being the price of the royal
4     grain, at interest of two drachmas on each mina each month. Let Isidoros repay to Dionysios the
5     loan in the month Artemisios in the thirteenth year, and the interest. And if he does not
6     repay within the written time, let him pay back the loan one and a half times, and let the right of execution belong
7     to Dionysios from the property of Isidoros
8     and of the guarantor, exacting in whatever manner
9     Dionysios wishes, as for royal (debts). Guarantor
10    of the loan of Isidoros and of the things according to the contract
11    for payment to Dionysios: Demetrios son of Damon,
12    a Thracian, fellow-holder. And let this contract be valid wherever
13    Dionysios produces it or to whomever Dionysios delivers it. Witnesses: Terepes, a Thracian forty-aroura holder; Hebryzelmis, a Thracian forty-aroura holder; Poseidonios, a Thracian
14    seventy-aroura holder; Bithys, a Thracian hundred-and-ten-aroura holder;
15    Dionysios, a Thracian sixty-aroura holder; Zipyros son of Bithys, a Thracian fellow-holder; these of those of Lykophron.
