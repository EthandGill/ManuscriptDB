[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of God, from me Subeheit the emir] to Papas the pagarch of Apollonos Ano;
2     … [the things sent] to us by you, garments; behold I sent
3     … from Kois, to which I spoke to you in the
4     … [step by] step I carry the gold, and I am going up
5     … of you how [it/he] is; and peace to you.
6     To the pagarch of Apollonos Ano, [from Subeheit son of Chedez the emir].
