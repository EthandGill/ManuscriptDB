[TRANSLATION]
1     Metocus to Somptutus greetings. I permit to you the [lands] in your plot,
2     which by Pachnes, on which having guarded,
3     five arouras, to leave them until
4     the surveying and having harvested
5     you apart from them to one-third [of the crop] for you.
6     Farewell. Year 31, Mechir 16.
7     Somptutus.
