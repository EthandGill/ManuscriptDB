[TRANSLATION]
1     [a] statement to Epimachius who served as gymnasiarch [and is] son of Philosopus.
2     it appears [that] it was given to him in the 4th year of our lord
3     [our] Probus Augustus from the pitch which had been purchased by Didymus himself the landowner
4     having been handed over to him by the same Didymus [the amount of] 12 talents in weight
5     the price of the [items] aforesaid as 1 talent, as through a daily record of the same
6     4th year it is indicated, drachmas Γυ, total [is] 6 talents drachmas Δω.
7     and from the price of light [goods] having been provided to him by Theon
8     the potter of Pela from the prescription of the same Theon
9     … of him the … ω native/local …
