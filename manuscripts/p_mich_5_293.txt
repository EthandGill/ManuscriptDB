[TRANSLATION]
1     [Heraklides] and Papontos and Taorsenouphis and Tasooukis, the four children of Papontos, whose mother is Kolleuthis, and the husband of Tasooukis herself, Harmiysis son of Panesneous, whose mother is Taorphaesis, we acknowledge, on the one hand
2     [Heraklides] and Papontos and Taorsenouphis and Tasooukis, that we have sold according to Egyptian contracts to Kouthion son of Menchoueis, whose mother is Tapetsiris, the house and courtyard belonging to us and all the appurtenances in Theogonis of the Polemon division, of which the measurements are: south to north eight cubits, west to east twenty-five cubits, on both
3     of the measurements, or however many there may be, of which the neighbours are, on the south the house of Apynchis son of Apynchis, on the north the house and courtyard of the so-called Apynchis the cropped, on the west the house of Leontiskos, on the east
4     [a royal] street; and we have received the whole agreed price in full, and we will guarantee with every guarantee for all time. And Harmiysis the afore[written], the husband of Tasooukis, I consent to the aforesaid sale and will not proceed against it for all time nor make claim, and I shall do the other things
5     [as] stated above. There wrote on their behalf Herodes son of Herodes, because they do not know letters.
6     Heraklides, 27 years old, with a scar
7     on the right of his face.
8     Papontos, 23 years old,
9     with a scar on the left of his chin.
10    Taorsenouphis, 21 years old, with a scar
11    on her right cheek.
12    Tasooukis, 21 years old, with a scar
13    over her left eyebrow.

15    on his forearm …
