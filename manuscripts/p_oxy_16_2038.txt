[TRANSLATION]
1     [Account] of remission for unirrigated land [and] of relief of the measures of the 12th indiction:
2     of the hamlet of Orth[onion]:
3     [Orig]enes the presbyter, for unirrigated land, of grain by the kankellon 2¼ artabas, 6 choinikes,
4     [the same] Origenes the presbyter, for unirrigated land, of grain by the kankellon 2¼ artabas, 6 choinikes,
5     … [for unirrigated land] … 1, and of barley 306¼ artabas, 7 choinikes, and on account of relief of grain by the kankellon 60½ artabas, 2 choinikes,
6     … [for unirrigated land] … and of barley 13¼ artabas, and on account of relief by the kankellon 9 artabas, 9 choinikes, …
7     of the hamlet of Perouen:
8     Apollos and Apphous, for unirrigated land, of grain by the kankellon 70¼ artabas, 6 choinikes, and on account of relief of grain by the kankellon 16 artabas,
9     Heras son of Mousaios, for unirrigated land, of grain by the kankellon 54½¼ artabas, 3 choinikes, and on account of relief of grain by the kankellon 15½ artabas, 6 choinikes,
10    Pekysis the deacon and Kyriakos, for unirrigated land, of grain by the kankellon 27¼ artabas, 3 choinikes, and on account of relief of grain by the kankellon 7½¼ artabas, 2 choinikes,
11    the guild of the vine-dressers, on behalf of the outside land, for unirrigated land, of grain by the kankellon 24½¼ artabas,
12    the same and aforesaid cultivators, on behalf of the outside land, for unirrigated land, of grain by the kankellon 12 artabas.
