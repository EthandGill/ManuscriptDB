[TRANSLATION]
1     Kalliklēs to Harimouthēs,
2     Greetings. Arrange [to measure]
3     the sesame in Pelai
4     Protomachus and the [sitologus], for there is not
5     sesame in the city. In order then
6     nothing may delay the [oil-works],
7     Take care so that you may not have blame
8     and send the [oil-workers] to me.
9     Farewell. Year 24, Epiphi 20.
10    Year 24, Epiphi 20, from
11    Kalliklēs, concerning sesame, for Protomachus.
12    to Harimouthēs.
