r.1    ἔτους ϛ … ιη, ἔχει Ἀμμών[ιος]
r.2    παρὰ Χενσνεῦτος τοῦ Τετνούφιος

r.4    τοῦ ϛ ἔτους τῆς ἄνω τοπαρχίας μέτροις

r.6    οἰνολογίας χόας τρεῖς κοτύλας τέσσαρας γίνονται χόες μη κοτύλαι ζ 𐅵 δ. ὁμολογεῖ Ἀμμώνιος ἔχειν

[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 6 ... 18, Ammo[nios] has
2     from Chensneutos, [son] of Tetnouphis
3     for the apportionment made to them
4     of the 6th year of the upper toparchy by measures
5     Attic, with dregs, of wine, measures forty-five, cotylae three [and a half and a quarter]
6     of wine-reckoning, measures three, cotylae four, make measures 48, cotylae 7 [and a half]. Ammonios agrees to have
7     the aforementioned quantity.
8     Moschion, I have witnessed the delivery
9     of wine, Attic measures, with dregs
10    forty-eight
11    cotylae 7 [and a half], make measures 48, cotylae 7 [and a half].
