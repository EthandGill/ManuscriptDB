[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the] most admirable Just… of the
2     [n]ew city of Justinus, Aurelius Agenis son of Abaron,
3     his mother being Thraesis, elder in religious office, from the hamlet
4     of Eunouchos of the Justinopolite [n]ome. I acknowledge that I have received
5     from you from now the price agreed upon by me,
6     in full, for wine, three hundred and sixty
7     six-sextarius measures, total: 360 six-sextarius measures of wine, which wine
8     at the vat, from unadulterated must, you providing
9     the empty jars, I shall by necessity render to you at the vintage
10    of the month Mesore of the present fourth indiction,
11    of the wine-flow of the, God willing, fifth taxation-year,
12    without delay; and I undertake the good quality of the wine
13    and its keeping until the whole of the month Tybi, and if indeed
14    there should be found in it parts or all defective, that I
15    shall exchange these and provide to you first-quality wine; and the
16    same wine I acknowledge that I give you from the irrigation-machine
17    called Tzeras and reckon … …
18    4 knidia and 4 must-jars; and the transport
19    of the empty jars of the wine I am to make at my own (cost), without
20    loss, without delay. The present single document is valid,
21    written, and being formally questioned I acknowledged.
22    I, Aurelius Agenis son of Abaron, agree to
23    this docu[ment as a]foresaid.
24    I, Papnouthios, [having been requested,] wrote on behalf
25    of him, he being illiterate.
26    … of Agenis son of Arabon, from the hamlet of Eunouchos, 360 six-sextarius measures of wine.
