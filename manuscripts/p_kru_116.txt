[TRANSLATION]
1     I Papnouthe the son of Mathaeus on the one hand
2     Maria his wife of our father Daniel from the fortress
3     on behalf of that we confess and we conform that we trust in you toward God whom God praised
4     because you gave-as-gift your house to us and [we will] give you forever when I give [you]
5     You O Lord of your house I Papnouthios
6     I have come to your door and I conform
7     I Victor the son of the father-deity [give] [witness] …
8     the son of John asked me I came to him that he might understand the coming
9     and I give witness I Aristophanes [John] I give witness
10    written document by Papnouthios son of Mathaeus
11    before Daniel
