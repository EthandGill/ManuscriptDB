[TRANSLATION]
1     Aulenos to Capito the brother greetings.
2     You will do well giving to Tithoetes barley
3     [measures] two and a half and of chaff mnas
4     ten since I found carrying them
5     to Crocodilópolis and I said to him: I
6     will write to Capito the friend so that
7     he give to you there; and write to me to whom
8     you wish I give here and you will be doing me great favor; greet
9     all those loving you
10    Do not do otherwise.
11    Farewell.
12    Hathyr 2.
