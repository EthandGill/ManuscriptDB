# HGV-EN: – – – crown tax anchos, son of Pates, through his sister sixty drachmas on account, 60 drachmas: (m. 2) For the reckoning for Mesore, the same (person) a further twenty eight drachmas, = 28 drachmas. (m. 3) For the reckoning for Thoth, the same (person) a further eight drachmas, = 8 drachmas, three (drachmas), 3 drachmas, *** six drachmas to Ammonianos, the soldier. (m. 6) For the reckoning for Choiak twenty four drachmas, = 2[4] drachmas. For Pharmouthi eight drachmas, = 8 drachmas. For Payni eight drachmas, 8 drachmas.
