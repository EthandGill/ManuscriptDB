[TRANSLATION]
1     … the lord Ioannes together with … Flavius Askos …
2     … the lord Ioannes …
3     … to the children not …
4     … being occupied, and not being found at once
5     … so as to plunder the … … …
6     … the things belonging to … and the …
7     … as Arsenios the headman explained, that Flavius Askos
8     … child … they brought to me in your house at a late hour, God knows,
9     without wine; for one must report the truth. But I was able at that
10    hour to entreat Sophronios the chief physician to take charge of him, and he treated him,
11    stanching the blood, since indeed a very great wound surrounds his head, and his whole
12    body has been broken. It is therefore of God to help, that he may be saved.
13    I spoke to my master the grammarian about my taking action, lest somehow
14    he be endangered, and I was not ordered; but the matter ordered me to leave it to the lord
15    Ioannes, and he himself … of the … Ioannes has many wounds about the face.
16    And Kyriakos the boy is here, master, and I have given him an account if you order; order
17    that he too take his bread-ration, for I shall make use of him here. Your most illustrious children much
18    do reverence to you, together with the lord Zacharias their teacher, and I your servant; therefore
19    write quickly what seems good, master.
