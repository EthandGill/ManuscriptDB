1     They acknowledge to each other, Aurelius Diogenes son of Heras, his mother being
2     Sarapias, from the city of Oxyrhynchos, and Thonis freed[ma]n
3     of Harpokration also called Herminos, a comb-maker — Diogenes on the one part
4     that he has apprenticed his own slave
5     Didymos, born in the house of the slave-woman
6     Taias, for the learning [of the]
7     comb-maker's craft for a period of five years from the 1st of T[ybi]
8     of the present 3rd year, he being fed and clothed for
9     the whole period by [the]
10    master, remaining with him, on condition that for the fir]st six months he receive nothing for wages; after which
11    he shall receive, for the second six-month period, daily, ob[ol]s
12    two; and for the second year, daily, obols
13    four; and for the third
14    year, daily, obols
15    six; and for the fourth year, daily, obols [e]ight;
16    and for the fifth year,
17    daily, obols ten.
18    And if during the period the boy is idle, or — may it not happen — falls sick
19    for some days, the wages for these
20    shall be deducted. On these terms,
21    therefore, let Diogenes provide
22    his own slave, remaining with the instructor for the period,
23    each day from sunrise
24    until sunset, not idle, doing all
25    the things that shall be entrusted to him
26    by the instructor pertaining to the craft, as in the case of [similar] apprentices. And the instructor (agrees) to be content on
27    these terms and to teach th[e] apprentice within
28    the period the said craft and to furnish the wag[es].
29    And it shall not be permitted to either of them to transgress the foregoing, or the transgressor
30    shall pay to the party abiding (by it), by way of
31    penalty, one hundred drachmas. Valid
32    is the acknowled[g]ement, and on questioning each other they acknowledged it. [In the third year]
33    of the Emperor Caesa[r Marcus]
34    Antonius Gordian[us Pius]
35    Fortunate Augustus, …
36    [Aurel]ius Diog[enes son of]
37    [Her]as, I cons[ent] …
