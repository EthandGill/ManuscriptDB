[TRANSLATION]
1     A measuring into the treasury of the metropolis, grain, year 16 of Hadrian, our lord
2     Payni 20, name [of] Senphabotos Peophios
3     through Pthominio[s] son of Dioskorou, for Charakos, wheat, artabas thirty
4     [and] eight, [which] total wheat artabas 38. Pikos has signed.
