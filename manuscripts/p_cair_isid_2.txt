[TRANSLATION]
1     To Julius Septimius Sabinus, censitor,
2     from Aurelius Pankratios son of Ptolemaios, from
3     the village of Karanis of the Arsinoite, on behalf of
4     my mother Heroeis daughter of Chairemon, making the declaration by her instruction.
5     In accordance with the divine ordinance of our
6     masters, the unconquered kings Diocletianus
7     and Maximianus the Augusti, and Constantius
8     and Maximianus the most noble Caesars,
9     I make known to you that the aforesaid Heroeis possesses, about the aforesaid village of Karanis, in the fourth-fifth toparchy
10    of the division of Herakleides, on
11    the 16th portion (sphragis), in the place called Kiamoul, of private land, twenty-four olive-trees, olive-trees 24,
12    of which the neighbours are: on the east, ownerless
13    dry-land, after which (is) the property of Atolas, and on
14    the west, likewise, ownerless dry-land, after which (is)
15    the property of Tauelma.
16    And I swear the august oath that I have made
17    the declaration truly.
18    In the 15th and 14th and 7th year of our lords Diocletianus
19    and Maximianus the Augusti and Constantius and
20    Maximianus the most noble Caesars,
21    in the consulship of Faustus and Gallus, Choiak 5.
22    Aurelius Pankratios, on behalf of my mother Heroeis, I swore
23    the august oath that I have made the declaration truly, and I submitted (it).
24    Aurelius Heliodoros son of Isidoros, I wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
25    They are olive-trees from private land, olive-trees 24.
