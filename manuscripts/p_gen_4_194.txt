[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Basilius the
2     most illustrious, Pachon 16, of the 4th indiction, in Arsinoe.
3     Aurelius Menas son of Piseph, with a guarantor and surety for the repayment of the debt of me, Aurelius Paulus
4     son of Elias, from the village of Dikaiou of the Arsinoite nome, to Aurelius Menas the respected,
5     son of the blessed Thomas, now trading here in the Arsinoite, greeting.
6     I acknowledge that I have now received,
7     from you, by hand, for my own need,
8     five gold solidi at the rate of exchange — gold solidi 5 at the rate of exchange — and of tow
9     thirteen loads and seven and a half
10    small bundles — of tow 13 loads and 7 and a half small bundles,
11    capital by the standard weight of the Arsinoite.
12    And I will make the repayment of these to you
13    in the month Thoth of the coming fifth in[diction] without delay, out of all my property, and on being asked the formal question I assented. Aurelius
14    Menas son of Piseph, the aforesaid, have received and [owe the] solidi and will repay at the appointed term, with the
15    interest as is set forth above. Aurelius Ne[il]os son of Paulus
16    wrote on his behalf, [he being illiterate.]
17    And now from Menas son of Piseph, with the guarantor Aurelius Paulus from Dikaiou, of gold
18    5 solidi at the rate of exchange
19    and of tow 13 loads, 7 and a half small bundles, to Aurelius
20    Menas the respected.
