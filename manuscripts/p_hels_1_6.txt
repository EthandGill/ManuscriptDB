[TRANSLATION]
1     Received in year 6, Epeiph 20. To Erasychis, royal scribe. Of the letter which Heroides and Alexandros, acting as sitologoi,
2     have written to us concerning certain places of the Upper Agema, we have appended the copy. If it seems good, you will do well to give order
3     to write to those under you to transact for them the appropriate transport-charges, as they write, before the
4     inundation of the waters into the plains.
5     Heroides and Alexandros to Philippos, greeting. Having brought in
6     to the fortress on the Sacred Island, for the measurings-out of the
7     soldiers, from the granary at Alilai a certain portion of the surplus wheat and barley and olyra, because it is customary
8     and moreover because the place is hard to guard. And Horos, the man from
9     Erasychis the royal scribe, who copies records,
10    that we do not allow him to have come to write to those to whom it has been written
11    to transact the transport-charges of these, alleging that unless
12    it be communicated to him from Erasychis (he will not act). Therefore we have considered it well
13    to make the report to you, so that, having looked to the security of the management, and ju[st a]s Heroides
14    the chief bodyguard and dioiketes makes [let]ters
15    concerning the grain to be delivered in the villages … of the fortresses,
16    to convey (it) into the fortresses. If [it seems good to you], give ord[er]
17    to make the fitting record to you. [Farewell.] …
18    grain … to Philippos.
