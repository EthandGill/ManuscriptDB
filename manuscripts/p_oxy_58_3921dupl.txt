[TRANSLATION]
1     [F]rom [A]urelius Hierax son of Sarap[i]on,
2     his mother being Tausoreous, guardian of the minor
3     [child]ren of Isidoros son of Achillas, the Aurelii
4     [Bere]nikianos also called A[g]a[thos] Daimon
5     [and] Sarapiades also called Pos[ei]donios,
6     a[n]d from Neikarous, mother and ass[isting-]guardian (parakolouthetria)
7     of the same minors, in accordance with
8     the will, which was also opened in the elapsed 2nd year, Mecheir.
9     Account of receipts and expenses
10    [for those] from Phamenoth of the elapsed 1st year
11    [until M]echeir of the present 2nd year.
12    Of rec[eipt]s, then:
13    of interest of silver, two thousand
14    fifty-six drachmas, at twenty
15    drach[m]as per month, from Phamenoth of the
16    elapsed [1st] year until Mecheir of the present 2nd year,
17    12 months, drachmas 240;
18    of house-rent, likewise for 12 months, drachmas 120;
19    of wages of a slave-girl weaver, from Thoth of the present
20    2nd year until Mecheir, 6 months:
21    for Thoth, drachmas 20,
22    and from Phaophi until Mecheir,
23    5 months, at 24 drachmas per month, drachmas 120;
24    total altogether of receipts, drachmas 500.
25    Out of which, of expenses:
26    of maintenance for the minors, from Phamenoth
27    of the elapsed 1st year until Mecheir of the present 2nd year,
28    those from Phamenoth until Hathyr,
29    9 months, at 24 drachmas per month, drachmas 216;
30    and those of Choiak (and) Tybi, 2 months,
31    at 32 drachmas per month, drachmas 64;
32    and for Mecheir, drachmas 40;
33    of clothing for the present 2nd year, drachmas 60;
34    for rent of one place
35    in their house, from Thoth
36    of the present year until Mecheir, 6 months, drachmas 16;
37    total altogether of expenses, drachmas 396.
38    Remaining of the aforesaid receipts, added to the capital, from Phamenoth
39    of the present 2nd year, drachmas 104;
40    so that the capital altogether is two thousand one hundred sixty drachmas, total drachmas 2,160,
41    which are also carried forward as a balance with me,
42    Hierax.
43    Year 2 of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
44    Antoninus Pius Felix Augustus, Phamenoth.
45    I, Aurelius Hierax son of Sarapion, have registered (this).
46    I, Aurelia Neikarous, assistant-guardian, have jointly registered (it).
47    I, Aurelius Ammonianus son of Apollonios, am enrolled
48    as her guardian, having been requested by her, and wrote on
49    her behalf as she does not know letters.
