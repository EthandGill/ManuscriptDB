[TRANSLATION]
1   (…, year 16, Tybi 30.)
2   To Nearchos, deputy-strategos,
3   from Pnephoros son of Herakleios, of those from Sinaru on the
4   far side. I report to you how, in the
5   past <year>, Patellis, of those from Sinaru,
6   son of Mesthasys, attacking together with others, and taking up
7   a tool — other men, 15, of those from the same (village) —
8   the doorkeeper assigned to the watch at the chap-
9   el of Hellanikos in the temple at Busiris,
10   the wife of Malephis,
11   they murdered on the spot. And further, this man's
12   younger brother Ambesis, together
13   with Pnephoros son of Petesouchos, who was traveling in the
14   settlement — setting upon, in brigand fashion,
15   a certain merchant of those from the Oxyrhynchite —
16   they stripped and stole from him fleeces
17   of wool, 150, and 1500 silver drachmas; concerning which, even in
18   Sinaru itself they were handed over, and, so
19   that the matter not be paraded in public, were re-
20   leased.
