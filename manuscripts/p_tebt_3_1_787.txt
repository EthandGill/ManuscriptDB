1     [To Phanias, of the First Friends] and strategos,

14    [from the a]foresaid cause; and in the
15    … [year, having hope] of your assistance,

17    hardship, we took up the land again and s[owed (it) i]n the proper seasons. But the har[vest of t]he produce being at hand,
18    [and we bei]ng engaged about this, so as to [duly measure out] the rents into the treasury, Apollon[io]s
19    [the ] … having arrived and having ins[pected] … we are ignorant … …
20    … t[he el]ders of us …
21    … to sow over two arouras, which also …

24    [village, but] we draw water from the springs;
25    [so that, foresee]ing lest the seed

30    [if it seems good], taking our part, to give orders
31    [to write] … concerning the sowing …

33    … of the produce …
