r.1    Ποριεύθης καὶ μέτοχοι ἀπαιτηταὶ μερισμοῦ τελωνικῶν Ἀγορῶν β
r.2    Σενπαμώνθῃ Ἰμούθου διὰ Τεκώσιος θ[υγατρός].

[TRANSLATION]
1     Porieuthos and partners [are] claimants of the distribution of taxes of Market 2,
2     (to) Senpamouthes (son of) Imouthes, through Tekosios his [daughter].
3     We received for the distribution of year 22 four drachmas; [this] makes four drachmas. Year 23
4     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Pachon 28. Porieuthos, I have sealed.
5     Others, Pauni 29, in the name of the same [person], likewise one drachma and one obol. Isidoros, I have sealed.
