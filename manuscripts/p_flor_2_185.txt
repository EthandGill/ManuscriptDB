[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakleidēs to Hērōneinos
2     to the dearest, greetings.
3     Since a helper was sent by
4     our lord Ischyrion of the grain-harvesting
5     for the sake of, but the pack-saddles
6     [found] broken and unusable, necessary for you
7     then to become, having taken
8     of me one of the letters
9     of the carpenters with you
10    to hand over Myda to the boy so that he may lead him
11    toward us, having and [of him] and the tools so that
12    of them being accomplished
13    we might be able to harvest grain.
14    I wish you to be strong.
15    Year 1, Pachōn 27
