[TRANSLATION]
1     Diogenes to Asclepiades and Gemellus and the Syrian and Dionysius the brothers, very many
2     greetings
3     Before all things I pray
4     [that] you be healthy and I make your obeisance beside the
5     lady Isis according to each day
6     and here came Petronis the horseman [letter restored]
7     legionary saying that I received drachmas 500 from the mother Tarx
