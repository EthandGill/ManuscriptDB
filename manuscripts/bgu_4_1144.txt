[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos,
2     from Tryphon son of Tryphon and from Pompeius son of Pompeius and
3     Ptolemaios son of Philokles, both Persians of the epigone. Since
4     we three have borrowed from Ar…os son of …, the one also called
5     Pamphilos, according to a memorandum contract, in the present seventeenth year of Caesar, in Phamenoth, in Ptolemaic silver,
6     and according to a bank-order through the
7     exchange bank of Kastor,
8     six hundred drachmas at interest, and from these there have been used up,
9     by Pompeius, five hundred twenty silver drachmas,
10    and by Ptolemaios, the remaining eighty drachmas; we agree
11    on these terms: that each repay the capital that is in his own charge to the…,
12    together with the interest, within the time indicated in the contract,
13    and that both shall present Tryphon as exacting nothing on account of the loan, and each shall present the
14    other for the capital that he has used up, and shall pay out whatever may be exacted or be exacted from the other on account of these things, both the interest and
15    the damages and expenses, the exaction being made from the party violating the contract and from all his belongings, just as
16    by legal judgment, with these (pledges) being void and with all assurances which one may bring forward,
17    and all protection.
18    Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 5.
