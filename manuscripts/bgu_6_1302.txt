[TRANSLATION]
1     Kolloutes to Nektērōis, potter
2     greetings. The one mediated by
3     Ptolemaios of Ornēus, empty vessels
4     eight-hundred and half-measures
5     fifty, I agree to give you the […]
6     of the one and 20th year, because of the Ptolemaian
7     having said to me to deliver to you.
8     Alexandros wrote on his behalf
9     of Herakleides, deemed worthy because he says
10    he does not know [letters]. Year 20, Epiphi 16
