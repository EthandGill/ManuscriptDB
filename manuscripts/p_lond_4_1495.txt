[TRANSLATION]
1     … to be liable to them until they come,
2     … to turn aside, that we draw you back, under any penalty,
3     … of him against us, against their person.
4     … through your excellency, the lord. We have drawn up this written guarantee-agreement.
5     [And] we are at risk to you, with all our property, on account of this matter; we swear by the name
6     [of God Almighty and the] welfare of the sovereigns who hold power, that we will keep guard and watch
7     [according to the force of this agreement; and they questioned us] and we acknowledged.
8     [Statement thus:] Athanasios son of Theodosios, sailor, 1; Senouthios son of Athanasios, sailor, 1.
9     We, Kollouthos the small one(?) son of the younger and Ouerschenoube son of Pamias, agree to this guarantee-
10    agreement according to its force; and we are at risk on behalf of the persons whom we shall produce, from above,
11    so that they go up and draw their voyage upward against turning aside; and if anyone among them turns aside,
12    that we give account, under any penalty, the lord all-praiseworthy councillor determining
13    it against us. I, Andreas son of Solomon, the least, the elder, having been asked, wrote on [their behalf].
14    … Phoib(ammon) bear witness; the blessed Biktor bears witness;
15    … bears witness. I, Philotheos son of the blessed Mena, give testimony.
16    … the abbot son of the blessed Mena, give testimony to this agreement according to its [force].
17    … give testimony to this guarantee-agreement according to its force.
18    … Ouersenouphis son of Pamias, receiver.
19    … with God, of the notary.
