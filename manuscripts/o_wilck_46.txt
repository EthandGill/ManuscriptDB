[TRANSLATION]
1   Petroszmethis son of Ammonios,
2   grandson of Tisas, paid for the poll-tax of the first
3   year of Nerva Caesar the
4   lord, on account eight drachmas — total 8 dr.
5   I, Hermogenes, the collector, wrote (this), Epeiph 8.
6   The same Petroszmethis, the remaining
7   of the 1st year of Nerva the lord: nine drachmas
8   total 9 dr. I, Hermogenes son of Ammonas, the collector, wrote (this), Mesore 30.
