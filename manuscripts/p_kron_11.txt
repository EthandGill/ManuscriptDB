[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the fifth year of Imperator Caesar Trai]anus Hadrianus the lord, Pauni …
2     [in Tebtynis of the Polemon] division of the Arsinoite nome. [Herakleides son of Did]ymos the younger, grandson of Herodes, acting in office as [cosmetes,] acknowledges … about thirty years old, with a scar on [both] eyebrows, [to Kronion son of Ch]eos, about sixty years old, with a scar [on the little finger of the left hand,] and to his [wife] Thenap[ynch]is
3     [daughter of Patynis, about fif]ty-one years old, with a scar on the right knee,
4     [and to the children of both,] Kr[oni]on, about thirty-six years old,
5     [with a scar on the right forearm, and Taorse]nouphis, being the wife of the son Kronion,
6     [a woman about thirty]-three years old, with a scar on the right shin,
7     [the women each with the] guardianship of her husband — that he has received from them
8     [from hand to hand out of the house the] two hundred and thirty[-six drachmas] of silver [and the interest, which] the four owed to Herakleides
9     [through a] contract of loan completed through the same record-office
10    in the thirteenth year of the deified Trajan, in the month Choiak, on the eleventh,
11    which he has also handed back to them on the spot for cancellation and annulment,
12    and that neither Herakleides nor his agents shall proceed against the
13    four nor against each one of them nor against their agents, neither concerning these matters nor concerning any other matter whatsoever, written or unwritten,
14    [nor] debt or contract up to the present day in any way whatsoever.
15    [I, Her]akleides son of Didymos the younger, acknowledge
16    [that I have receiv]ed from Kronion son of Cheos
17    [and from] the wife and the children
18    [the dra]chmas, two hundred and thirty-six,
19    [and the] interest, and I shall not proceed
20    [against Kro]nion nor the wife
21    [and the childr]en. Kronion and the … the quittance has been made to us as is stated above.
22    … [ wrote on be]half of them since they do not know letters. Registered through Lour[i]os the
23    [clerk in the record-]office of Teptynis and Kerkesoucha Orous of the Polemon (division).
