[TRANSLATION]
1     [I] had the [freedom of speech] to write to your brotherhood [by] prior greeting so that, having been urged,
2     [you would procure] for me one three-month and [one for] the needy lady Patricia one
3     whole-cottine wool. I heard her saying to Abba Roman that [he] departed
4     to Teruthi, and I besought her so that [she would] release me with the one going away and [she] promised to release me with Abba Roman, and having gone
5     there we did not find [it] in readiness, but [they] gave us a postponement
6     for tomorrow [in order to do] bringing it to him. Since then I myself also need one three-month wool and [they] agreed with me [to] bring to me and of my one three-month
7     may your brotherhood deign to send to me [from] there one three-month
8     so that I may not be found doing otherwise [from] those from Teruthi, and with God
9     coming [when I come] to the city I shall give account to my master.
10    Please seek from the letter-carrier six gourds
11    indifferently. Do not therefore neglect to send me the reply. And I greet you greatly and prostrate myself before my brother.
12    Do not then hold [it] as an insult that I write to you as to a brother but as
13    my master I write to you. Greet then greatly the common
14    sister my Theodothe especially the lady the mother yours.
15    Send me then the [little one] and take care of the kell…
16    Give to the sweetest brother Philoxenos …
17    from Hyacinth brother.
