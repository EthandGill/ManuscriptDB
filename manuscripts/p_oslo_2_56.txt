[TRANSLATION]
1     Justus to Plutio, the most dear
2     many greetings
3     You will do well [having bought] for me in Busir tunics
4     linen two solid pure fine
5     up to drachmas forty, cross-striped pair of Diospolis fine, bathing-vessel single, half-mattress fine; if you see something larger then not
6     do not neglect other things inferior to the Saite
7     deliver to Plutio[n] …
