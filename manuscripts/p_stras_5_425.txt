1     Year nineteen of Imperator Caesar
2     Traianus Hadrianus Augustus, Pharmouthi 17. Petesouchos the Heliotian recorded through [Areotes] the collector
3     Of mother Taayneiou on behalf of the beer of the same nineteenth year of Bacchiadae
4     Drachmas [three] become drachmas 3, 18, similarly [drachmas]
5     Three become drachmas 3; Mesore 26: [similarly] [drachma] one four-obol becomes drachma one four-obol

7     Drachmas three five-obol become drachmas 3 five-obol
8     The same, of property, of the same nineteenth year
9     Bacchiadae, [drachmas] 2 two-obol become drachmas 2 two-obol
