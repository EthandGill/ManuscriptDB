1     Psansnos and Kasianos, collectors
2     of money-taxes, to Horos the elder (son of) Psenthatres. We have received
3     for the g( ) e( ) embankment-tax of year 20, one base drachma, 2½ obols,
4     and for the hunt of gazelles of year 20, in small coin, 6 obols,
5     total together drachmas 2, 2½ obols. Year 22 of Hadrian
6     Caesar the lord, Pauni 16.
7     (2nd hand) I, Kasianos, have signed ...nou as..
