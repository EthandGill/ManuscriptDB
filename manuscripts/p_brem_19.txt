[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings, my lord Apollonius.
2     Concerning that which I besought you face to face,
3     most honored one, and by chance Harpocration having come to
4     Deius, I conferred with him and referred [it] to your
5     judgment, in order that what you approve, this may come to be. What has appeared to you, lord, you will make clear [to] me.
6     I wish you to fare well, Apollonius
7     most honored one.
8     To Apollonius, strategos of Heptacomia
