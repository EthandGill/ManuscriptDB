[TRANSLATION]
1     It was given through Serenus the wine-dispenser to Theodore the one over the [stablehands] as an account of expenditure for Thoth and
2     Phaophi from command from letters of our master the [patrician] toward the illustrious count Menas
3     Each month wine double-measures six, they total of the two months wine double-measures twelve, they total wine double-measures 12 only.
4     Year 241 210, month Phaophi 5, indiction thirteenth. Wine double-measures twelve.
