1     Paion son of Paios, tax-farmer of the granary
2     of the temples, to Nepheros (son of) Pechois
3     and Pekysis (his) son, and Panameus (his) other son,
4     greeting. I have received in full the bath-tax of year 9
5     of Domitian the lord, Pauni 20.
6     I, Esoueris(?), have signed.
