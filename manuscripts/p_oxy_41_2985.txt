[TRANSLATION]
1     From Theon, greetings, Chairemon.
2     If you learn that you need hay-trampled [fodder]
3     to Tholthis [trans]port the of Hekys there.
4     but let much straw be mixed in so that [it is] not
5     quickly consumed; for when the wine [?] of the wine-shop are sold, then you will be able to buy hay-fodder there. Perhaps
6     it will be cheaper when the water is coming [up]. Don't neglect the transport
7     of the straw of Tholthis. Now
8     wages do not give for the possession on account of manure
9     until the rise [in water]. so that [you] not [pay] wages
10    [you] pay. Fare well. Send back by order 6 jars of wine from the wine-shop [by means of] the donkey
11    of Kopreus, but [do so] now, and [one] of his sons.
