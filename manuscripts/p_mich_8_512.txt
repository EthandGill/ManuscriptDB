[TRANSLATION]
1   Ptolemaios to Heron, his son, greetings.
2   Up to today we have not found a buyer. I cede
3   everything to Didymos, on (his) taking a third part
4   of the loan and of the rest. Had I not disputed
5   with Ptolemaios, we would long ago have been quit; for he was angered
6   on coming down. So I write to you that you may know. Greet
7   your [chil]dren and a[l]l your (folk) and Tais and her children.
8   I [pray for your health].
9   Deliver to Heron the land-sur[veyor].
