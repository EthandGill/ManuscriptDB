1     Aurelius Silvanus also called …eō …
2     son of Theon, who served as cosmete …
3     of the bright and most [bright] city of the Oxyrhynchites and Sarapion …
4     of Horation, mother of Herate, from

7     that we shall partner with each other

11    on condition that we both
