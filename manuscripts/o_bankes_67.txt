[TRANSLATION]
1     Sabeinius, in the month of Thoth: eighteen drachmae in chalkoi,
2     of which were spent 3 … T drachmae 14 in chalkoi
3     remainder in it four drachmae, and for
4     Syros the farmer two drachmae and a diobol, making six drachmae and a diobol
