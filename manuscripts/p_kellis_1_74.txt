[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord father Aron, Psais
2     the potter, his son, greetings.
3     I greet you much, before all else,
4     and pray that you are well. My mother
5     and the brothers by name greet you
6     much, and I greet much the mother Agape and her daughter; having been asked, in accordance with the
7     arrangement, send me the little (bit) of purple for a tunic of
8     my mother and of my sister,
9     since there is need and the
10    materials are lying (ready) until you send quickly
11    the purple. And learn that the
12    father Klodianos has taken up
13    the olives and has given us our share of the produce thereon, and he has given the olives to Psenpnouthes, and as yet up to now
14    we have not had wages. So make clear to us whether you wi[sh] to take (them) or n[ot.]
15    And learn that …
16    … to us the …
17    … on account of the [purp]le …
18    Do not hesitate to write to me, to the
19    mother Tagape, indicating
20    that: Petem[inios] has departed from the house
21    and harasses me continually as though you
22    owed him (something). If then
23    you owe him anything, indicate (it) to me and I will get rid of him. And having been asked,
24    O brother Aron, make haste to come to us, since we have very much need of you.
25    I pray that you are in good health, my lord master.
26    To my lord father brother
27    Aron in Kel[lis], (from) Psais the potter.
