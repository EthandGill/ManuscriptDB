[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the Aurelii …] and Kronios, ex-exegete, procession-leader in office, [both] councillors of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, those in charge of the scrutiny,
2     [from Aurelius Sarapion, his mother being …]lia, of the city of the Oxyrhynchites. In accordance with the orders concerning the scrutiny of those approaching admission among those from the gymnasium, whether they are of this class, he was registered in the quarter of …
3     … [my son] … [his mother being] … being, with respect to the present 7th year, 14 years old, having no scar, a runner in training. Wherefore, having come forward for his scrutiny, I declare, in accordance with the scrutiny which took place in the 5th year of the deified
4     [Vespasian, of those from the gymnasium, that the great-grandfather of my grandfather, Dionysios] son of Philon, was scrutinized in the quarter of Metroon, in accordance with the proofs which he produced, namely that his father too, Dionysios son of Philon, is in the register of the 34th year of the deified Caesar,
5     [and that the great-grandfather of my father …] having been admitted, was scrutinized in the … [year] of [D]omitian in the quarter of Kretikos, and that my great-grandfather Kornelios, having been admitted to scrutiny in the 17th year of the deified Trajan, was scrutinized in the quarter of Metroon, and that
6     [my grandfather …] having been admitted, was scrutinized in the … [year of the deified Antoninus, in the quarter] of the same, and that my father Sarapion, having been admitted in the 6th year of the deified Aurelii Antoninus and Verus, was scrutinized in the quarter of Anamphodarchoi,
7     [and that I, having been admitted, was scrutinized in the … year of the deified …] in the quarter of Kretikos, and that the great-grandfather of the grandfather of the mother of my son, Apollonios son of Apollonios, was registered through the scrutiny of the same 5th year of the deified Vespasian
8     [in the quarter of … and that the great-grandfather of her father], Apollonios, having been admitted in the 2nd year of the deified Titus, was scrutinized in the quarter of the same, and that her great-grandfather Apollonios also called Zoilos, having been admitted in the 7th year
9     [of the deified Hadrian, in the quarter of … and that her grandfather …] having been admitted, was scrutinized in the 11th year of the deified Aelius Antoninus, in the quarter of the same, and that her father Chosion, having been admitted, was scrutinized in the 22nd year of the deified Commodus
10    [in the quarter of … and I have provided] witnesses [of the scrutiny] and I swear by the Fortune of Valerian and Gallienus and Cornelius Saloninus Valerianus, the Caesars, the lords Augusti,
11    [that the foregoing is true and that the … is] the son by nature of me and of …tyche, and neither by adoption nor supposititious, nor that I have made use of others' securities or of homonymies — or may I be liable to the oath.
12    [Year 7 of the Emperors Caesars Publius Licinius Valerianus and Pu]blius Licinius Valerianus Gallienus, Germanici Maximi, Pii, Felices, and Publius Licinius
13    [Cornelius Saloninus Valerianus the most illustrious Caesar, Augusti], Mesore, of the intercalary days the 5th. Witnesses: Marci Aurelii Makreinos son of Maron, his mother being Aline also called Apia; Theon also called Kronion;
14    … the three of the same city.
