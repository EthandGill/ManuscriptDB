[TRANSLATION]
1     Mechir 18, indiction 12. I have received from Sion, son of Peter
2     From the receptors, principal of the southern part of the city of Hermes,
3     Levied upon us for public [dues] and other [obligations]
4     Of the eleventh indiction, year 189, amount: one numisma, one sole. Severus verifies [this].
