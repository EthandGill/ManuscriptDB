[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon greeting, Petobastis, dove-keeper. Well
2     would you do, if also it seems good to you,
3     having ordered [it] to be given to me
4     the salary; for I am in distress; and the
5     salary is owed to me of four months.
6     And in the grain-ration I have
7     barley-wheat which is useless; we are not able to use [it].
8     I provide services and my children. I ask then you,
9     if also it seems good to you, having ordered [it] to be given to me the
10    grain-ration and the salary
11    conveniently, so that to you the services
12    I may provide.
13    Fare well.
