[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Ioannes, with God, pagarch, to those from the village Ampelius
2     you deposited from the freight of payment of the eleventh indiction through a receipt of Elias
3     of the weigher, nomismata 5, keratia 6, one-half; nomismata five, keratia six, half only. Mechir 22, indiction of the same, 11
4     through me, Dorotheos, chartularius
