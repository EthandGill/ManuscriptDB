r.1    τῷ εὐλαβεστάτῳ Παμουθίῳ προεστῶτι μ[ο]νῆς Μουσαίου.
r.2    παράσχου τοῖς ἀπὸ Θεαγένους ὀνόμασι κβ ψωμία

[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most cautious Pamuthius, presiding [over the] m[onastic] community of Musaeus.
2     Furnish to those from Theagenes by name, twenty-two loaves
3     Sixty-six, [they] become loaves sixty-six only. Pachon 20 …, of the twelfth indiction.
