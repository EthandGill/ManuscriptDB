[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Osarapis, chief prophet and first-stolistes of those in
2     the city of Oxyrhynchus, [of the] first-approved and approved sacred [officials/temples]
3     from Aurelius Petoseris, from the village
4     of Th[a]es, sacred psalmist of Ammon and Kronos and Hera and Heracles and Sarapis and of the associated great gods [of the] approved sacred [temple] being in
5     the same village called [Imbien]ophres, son of Horus [and] mother Tsenouris
6     of [the] priests of the same gods. As was written to you and to the strategos of the nome by Aurelius
7     Timages of the most eminent high priest for life, concerning [the matter of] making clear to him which sacred ranks there were and which were controlled by whom and which
8     of the revenues they claimed, [and to] register those who had been ordained as
9     sons born to them, [I] declare [in order]
10    that it not escape [notice that] Aurelii Amenothe and Ammonia are theagi [priests] from father Horus Ameneus a theagus, whom they inherited
11    and of his [property] they hold [jointly], [and they] have been registered under the one according to mother
12    their grandfather Onnophris [as] priest of
13    the same gods; and Psenamunis and Ammonia [his] brother as pastophori
14    to be from father Ameneus a pastophoros, whom they inherited, [from] mother Artemeis
15    a pastophorissa, [and they] have been registered under
16    another Amenothe [as] priest; and Tamenea [as] hierissa and Onnophris [as] son
17    of Horus, whom he inherited, [and he] has been registered under Onnophris [as] priest …
18    [He] has given an excuse regarding memoranda
19    of the high priesthood [concerning] not having [a] son
20    male. And regarding the circumcision of their children, [that] of
21    Amenothe [named] Onn[o]phris from mother
22    Di[u]n[...]us from [the family of] Isiou Tryphos
23    a pastophorissa, and of Psenamunis
24    sons Ameneus and Horus … from mother Taamennota daughter of Apollonius a theagissa, and [the] son of Ammonia [named] Ameneus from Hieramousa a theagissa, and Onnophris
25    wishing to register under himself Horoouchis a son being of Origen a pastophoros from mother Herouthis a pastophorissa, [was] stopped, [and it] was accomplished through
26    you to the same most eminent
27    Aurelius Timages.
28    Year 25 …
