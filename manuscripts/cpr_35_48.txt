[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the most excellent council of Hermopolis the great,]
2     [ancient and most venerable and most splendid,]
3     [from] Aurelius Leukad[ios, the Hermopolite, sacred victor]
4     pancratiast, [through Aurelius Appianos, also]
5     called Demetrios, [the Hermopolite], appointed [guardian]: I ask that an order be sent from the civic account [for] my prize-money which I won and was crowned for in the sacred triumphal-entry
6     games from the month Phamenoth 10 of the year until
7     30 Mecheir of the 14th year, 48 [months], at the rate per month
8     of 180 drachmas, total 1 talent and 2640 drachmas, [and] for that for which I first
9     won and [was crowned], the sacred triumphal-entry, ecumenical, purple-bordered, equal-to-Olympic [contest] in the colony of the Sidonians, a city,
10    from the 6th of Phamenoth of the 11th year until Mecheir
11    and the 14th year itself, 35 [months] and 25 days, at the rate per
12    month of 180 drachmas, 1 talent and 450 [drachmas], total on the same account of the
13    request, silver two talents and three thousand ninety drachmas,
14    total silver 2 talents and 3090 drachmas, the account being reserved to the city
15    and to the council concerning every right which they have:
16    14th year of the Emperor Caesar Publius
17    Licinius Gallienus Germanicus Maximus
18    Persicus Maximus Pius Fortunate
19    Augustus, Phamenoth.
