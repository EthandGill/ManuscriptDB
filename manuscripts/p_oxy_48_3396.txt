[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord father Aphynchios and to my lady mother
2     Maria, Papnouthis, greeting.
3     First of all I pray to the divine providence that you, being in health and entire
4     and of good cheer, may receive the letter from me. How often
5     have I written to you concerning those pledges, and there is neither
6     word nor footstep (of reply). Concerning the same pledges I now write
7     to you also; for the landlord of Sarapammon wrote to Eudaimon son of Theodoros the banker that the money be provided to you.
8     If you know that you received the money from Eudaimon, write back to me; if again you know that he did not give you
9     the money, write to me, and he will make one solidus
10    already be brought to you in silver; but see that you do not neglect
11    to write to me. And I wish you to know that in Alexandria
12    I am alone, with denarii to the amount of twenty-five
13    myriad talents. The governor wrote to the landlord
14    of Sarapammon and to his partner, that there be levied at
15    Alexandria of silver denarii myriads of myriads
16    seven and a half, and they sent me with our portion
17    and I shall remain at Alexandria until I pay them out
18    and again I depart for the city of the Athribites.
19    I salute my lady wife Apias, I salute my
20    lady sister Tereous, I salute my lord brother
21    Gerontios, I salute my master brother Dorotheos,
22    I salute my sister Euethis, I salute my
23    lady sister Mikke, I salute Choous and his wife
24    Herakleia and her children, free of the evil eye, I salute
25    my father Ammonios, I salute Tasilbaneis
26    and her children, free of the evil eye, I salute my father
27    Petemounis and my mother Taeseis
28    and her children, free of the evil eye, I salute my lady and sister Palladie; I salute all
29    by name those who love us.
30    Deliver to my lord father Aphynchios, from Papnouthis.
