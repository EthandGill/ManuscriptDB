[TRANSLATION]
1     Antonios Malchaios, he who is occupied with
2     the harbor guard [duty] of Syene, to Pantiotes Marcianus, greetings. I have from you
3     the anchorage fees of [boats/goods] which I conducted
4     [those] from Tybi through Phamenoth 30
5     on account according to the agreement
6     of year 3 of Hadrian Caesar
7     of the lord, Phamenoth 30. Antonios Malchaios, on account.
