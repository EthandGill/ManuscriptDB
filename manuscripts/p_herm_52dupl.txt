1     After the consulship of Flavius Caesarius and Atticus the most illustrious.
2     To Aurelius Cyrus son of Philammon, serving as prefect

4     From Aurelius Anna son of Joseph, a Jew, purple-dyer
5     from the same city. During the night going into
