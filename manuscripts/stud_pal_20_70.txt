1     To Aurelia Thermutharis the Antinoëte mother
2     rightfully of children from Aurelii Julius
3     son of Parmenos vet[e]ran of the honorably
4     discharged and Ammonias slave of you
5     of the two. We wish to lease from
6     you the existing to you near village Dionysias of olive groves in three places in one place called Epikharu acres five and

11    of the lease[d] all of the acres
12    for time three years from the present first year of rent of the date palm grove each
13    year silver drachmas one hundred and emmer measures three and Syrian measures half, of the
14    and of the olive groves fruits one-third part to us
15    to the lessees, and the each [year] rent
16    we shall pay in the month Athur, and of the public [taxes] all to you the owner, and of the work
17    all of the olive groves and date palm grove of embankment irrigation channeling, ditch-digging
18    dry-cleaning, wood-gathering, of dikes and water channels
19    delays and the other whatever belongs from the [private] to the
20    due times damage none making and dung-gathering
21    you providing the livestock and after the time
22    we shall return from gathering of fruit as also
23    we received of herbage having remained all
24    if it appears [satisfactory] to lease. Aurelii Julius and A[mm]onias we have leased
25    as stated.
26    Year 1 of our lords Macrinus and Quietus
