[TRANSLATION]
1     Heroninus to Heron his son
2     greetings.
3     You-will-do well, giving to the one bringing to you these, my letters
4     wine, two gommaria, which are
5     two-choes eight from the
6     Chrysochoos, having-taken from
7     him what [if] he-gives you
8     change. Be-well, I-wish you.
9     Year 13, Thoth 19.
10    If but another gommarion they-wish
11    give to them, having-taken the price.
