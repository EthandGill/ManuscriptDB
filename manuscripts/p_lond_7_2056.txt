1     Philinus to Zenon
2     greeting. Well would
3     you do giving
4     to Poseidonios the earthen jar of the sweet wine
5     which you promised,
6     and likewise to us
7     send the and
8     pickled-relish and the honey.

11    so that not we might be delayed
12    for the of the king
