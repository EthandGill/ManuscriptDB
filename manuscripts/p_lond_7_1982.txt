[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermolaus to Zenon, greetings. If you are well, [it] would be well.
2     I am also in good health. We have sent Korragos to receive the croton that is with you and to convey [it]. You will do well therefore, having ordered [him] to hire pack-animals for him
3     pack-animals so that he may convey [it] quickly.
4     Farewell. Year 34, Mecheir 15.
5     To Zenon.
