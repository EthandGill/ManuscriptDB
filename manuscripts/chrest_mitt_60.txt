1     … to [Kal]purnianus, juridicus.
2     [From Gaius Iulius Agrippinus,] soldier [of the legion]
3     [II Traiana Fortis, of the century of] Publicius Severus …
4     … …

10    … e.
11    To Lucius [Valerius Proclus], prefect of Egypt,
12    from Gaius [Iulius Agr]ippinus, soldier of the legion II
13    Traiana [Fortis, of the century of Sulpi]cius Severus. Having been wronged, lord,
14    by … [Iu]lius Saturninus, my cousin,
15    I appeal to you. … being heir of my father
16    Iulius Agrippinianus. Maliciously Saturninus
17    brought against me a suit before Claudius Neokydes,
18    who was juridicus, demanding of me (the sum) which he said he had given
19    to my father as a deposit, producing against me a bond … of
20    eight gold mnae, which he compelled me to write by force against my will, for I happen to have written this while still within (the protection of) the Laetorian law; and moreover, having in the meantime fraudulently obtained by miscalculation a letter [of the]
21    most excellent juridicus Kalpurnianus to the strategos of the Arsinoite (nome), so that I might be put in possession of the property belonging to me, being in valuation of ten talents and more, on account of the aforesaid
22    eight gold mnae, Saturninus, having sent
23    the letter through two soldiers of the most excellent juridicus.
24    Year 10, Pharmouthi 2 … .
