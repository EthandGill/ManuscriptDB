[TRANSLATION]
1     Having found an opportunity of the letter-carrier, I deemed it necessary to write,
2     bowing down and greeting the blessed footprints of the blessed
3     feet to my good master, together with all of the
4     most God-loving brothers with my good master, and
5     I beseech my master to remember my humility in
6     the all-holy and well-received prayers to my good master,
7     until God deems me worthy to bow down face to face to
8     my good master. For I do not cease night and day
9     praying toward the master Chr[i]st on behalf of the salvation and
10    perseverance to my good master, and all of those loving
11    my master in the Lord, master. Phamenoth, second, second indiction
12    with God. To my good master, presiding after God, Psois, most humble
13    slave.
