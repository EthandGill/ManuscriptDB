[TRANSLATION]
1     153
2     [Fl]avius Paniskos also called Longus, strategos of the Hermopolite (nome).
3     Of the nominations given to me by the village-heads of the village of Monyris and of Damaras,
4     who present in their own stead for the same village-headship, an equal copy is posted publicly, so that
5     all may know, and that those nominated may keep to the (duties) entrusted to them at the [needed]
6     time soundly and faithfully, so that they be blamed in nothing. I have noted (it).
7     Year 12 of our lord Galli[en]us Augustus, Epeiph 22.
8     To Flavius Paniskos also called Longus, strategos of the Hermopolite (nome),
9     from the Aureli[i] Dioskoros son of Serenos and Apion
10    son of Kolluthos, both village-heads of the hamlet
11    of Monyris for the present 12th year. We give and nominate the undersigned in our stead for the
12    same village-headship, who are also in [the same off]ice from the 1st
13    of the present month Epeiph, being [men of mean]s and suitable, at our risk and (that) of a[ll] those residing in the same hamlet [by mutual] surety, whom also
14    we ourselves guarantee for (their) appearance. They are:
15    Pamounis son of Pinoution, [mother Ter]eus,
16    aged about 32, [having] means of 1000 drachmas.
17    So… son of Horion, mother Dioskoro[us],
18    aged about 35, having means of 1000 drachmas.
19    Farewell.
20    Year 12 of Imperator Caesar [Publius Li]cinius
21    [G]allienus, Germanicus Maximus, Pius, Felix,
22    Augustus, Epeiph 22. I, Aurelius Dios[ko]ros son of Serenos,
23    have submitted (this). I, Aurelius Apion son of K[olluthos, have submitted (this).]
24    [I, Aur]elius Neilos, wrote for (him) who does not know (letters).
25    [I, Aurelius] Tyr[a]nnos, attendant, [having posted (it) publi]cly, [registered (it)].
