[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eighth year of Imperator C[aesar]
2     Domitian Augustus Germanicus, [in the month]
3     Neos Sebastos, on the eighth, in Theadel[pheia of the]
4     division of Themistes of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge Sambas son of Mysthes, aged about …
5     two, with a scar on the right shin, [and Heron]
6     son of Aphrodisios, aged about forty …
7     … in his sight, to Soterichos son of Ly[kos, aged] about forty-five, with a scar on the little
8     finger of the left hand, that they have received [from him]
9     the rent and the extra dues of the (palm-grove) which he holds of theirs [around]
10    the aforesaid village of Theadelpheia [in]
11    lease of a palm-grove, (arrears) of the past
12    seventh year carried over into the present
13    eighth year, and that they make no claim against him,
14    the lease of the palm-grove remaining valid on all the terms it contains. The contract is valid.
15    We, Sambas son of Mysthes and Heron son of Aphrodisios,
16    the two, acknowledge that we have received from
17    Soterichos the rent and the extra dues of the (palm-grove) which he holds in lease from us around Theadelphia, a palm-grove,
18    (arrears) of the past seventh year,
19    and that we make no claim against him, the lease of the palm-grove
20    remaining valid as stated above. Sarapion
21    son of Theogeiton wrote for them because they do not know
22    letters. Soterichos son of Lykos.
