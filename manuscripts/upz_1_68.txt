[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios to Ptolemaios his father greetings. If you are well, I myself am well too,
2     [it] would be as I wish and other things [are] going well for you. Know that I have traveled to Herakles' city on account of the house. The bronze [coins] which you have loaned to Petosires and Semphthae and Sarapion I have received.
3     As for the drachmas which you have given to Petetis to buy a goose, what do you command about these things? Send me a letter having Polydeukes quickly and if you wish to say something else, say [it]. For I [am having] dreams
4     [I] see [that are] evil, I see Menedemus running down upon me. D[escribe] to me the things concerning Apollonios
5     and [the things] concerning yourself accurately, so that I may know, for I am anxious concerning you. Farewell. Year 29, Epeiph 8.
6     to the father
7     Ptolemaios.
