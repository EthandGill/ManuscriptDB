[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysius and someone from him the things signified, for 5 years and for so much time as the loan ought, with all confirmation;
2     after the 5 years, let Olympias repay to Dionysius the 10 talents of copper in other days 30; and if not
3     he does according to the things written before, [let] Olympias pay Dionysius for the not confirming a fine of copper coin of talents 20, and the loan
4     with a half-share and of the overdue time interest 2 drachmae to the mina, each month, each one
5     and the legal action, let it be to Dionysius from both Ol[ympia]s and from the things belonging to her, all, just as
6     from judgment; and until the same, having recovered, let him dwell together with those from him in her
7     places without rent, but if Dionysius, having recovered his own, does not leave
8     after those from him from the house in other [days] 30, from which if he recovers,
9     having handed over to Olympias or to those from her, the places clean from all uncleanness
10    [and] which he received, standing doors 5, keys 5, let him pay Olympias for the not leaving, a fine
11    [of copper] talents 20, for each of which doors, if he does not hand over, a price of copper drachmae 3; of the side-door talents 2
12    …[of not] clearing, a wage of copper drachmae 2, and nonetheless let him move out
13    …six, then there are the 7 talents, remaining of the 10 talents of copper, which he lent
14    [Dionysius to Olympias, accordingly the] contract, the…an diou…ou of the…h…
15    …in…to…the a…rian tame-par…
16    …s Dionysius kek…beside Olympias, just as together they wrote.
