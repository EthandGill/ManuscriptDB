[TRANSLATION]
1     ... of mother ... [from] the village of Alabastrine of the [Hermopolite no]me, [priest] of the [sacred] first-listed [temples of] Apollo and Isis ...
2     ... cavalry soldier, sigil-bearer of the Mauretanian troop of Heracleides, greetings. I acknowledge having received from you a loan through hand from home, silver of Augustan [currency], one hundred sixteen drachmas being [a] complete count...
3     [of principal], interest at drachmas per mina each month, which I will also of necessity [repay] to you in four months from the month Epeiph of the present 13th year without delay. But if, when the time for repayment arrives...
4     [I do not repay, I am] actionable until [I] repay the principal together with one-and-a-half times [the interest]...
5     [the interest being] simple, and for the time that passes, [equal drachmic] interest, [with] execution for you from both me and from all my [possessions, as from a judgment. This] handwritten [document], written singly and signed...
6     [by me, is to be] valid as if [in] a public office registered. Of the thirteenth year...
7     [of the Emperors Caesars Lucius] Septimius Severus, Pious, Pertinax, Arabian...
8     [Adiabenic, Parthic, Greatest], and of Marcus Aurelius Antoninus, Pious, Augustuses...

10    ... [I acknowledge having] received one hundred sixteen drachmas in full as is set forth.
