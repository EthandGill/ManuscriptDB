[TRANSLATION]
1     to [those] appointed over the sacrifices
2     from Aurelia Demotion, fatherless
3     of mother Helen, wife of Aurelius Ireneaus
4     from the district of Helleneion. And always sacrificing to the
5     gods I have persisted, and now in your presence
6     according to the [prescribed] things, and I sacrificed, and I poured a libation, and I tasted of the sacrifices, and I ask
7     you to sign for me. May it go well.
8     Aurelia Demotion I have submitted. Aurelius
9     [Ireneaus], I wrote on behalf of her, illiterate.
10    [Aurelius] Sabinus, magistrate, [I saw] you sacrificing.
11    of year 1 of Emperor Caesar Gaius Messius
12    Quintus Traianus Decius Pious Fortunate
13    [Augustus], Pauni 20.
