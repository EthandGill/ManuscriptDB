[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ptolemaios son of Nemesion,
2     from Stotoetis son of Soïs, from the village of Dionysias, and Petesouchos, of unknown father, his mother being Soiteris,
3     from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, the two being mutual
4     sureties for payment. We wish to lease
5     from you, from the property belonging to you around
6     the village of Boubastos, in the plain of Argeias,
7     of palm-grove sub-sown [four] arouras, or however many there may be, and
8     of vineyard, in which are bare patches, one and a third aroura,
9     or however many there may be, for five years, five crops,
10    the palms (reckoned) from the standing
11    crops of the past 17th year, in the first year,
12    falling due
13    in the sixteenth year, and the soil from the
14    18th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord, the total rent in the first year being one hundred and forty-five drachmas of silver, and for the other years,
15    four, [each year] two hundred drachmas of silver,
16    [and as] extras each year
17    of date-palm dates one artaba by the measure
18    of the village, and … one and a half artaba, and
19    … five … unreckoned
20    and risk-free. [And] we shall perform all the yearly works, watering, irrigation, pruning, the dams from our own (resources), and at the proper seasons doing no [damage], the (responsibility) being to you
21    (for) manure … as is fitting. And the rent
22    of the first [year] we shall pay in the month Pauni,
23    and for the [remaining] four years in the month
24    Hathyr one hundred drachmas, the remaining 100 drachmas with the extras at the time of harvest
25    in the holding. And after the term we shall hand over (the land) clean of rushes, reed,
26    couch-grass, all rubbish, the public (dues)
27    being (the responsibility) of you the [lessor], and the
28    public freight-charges (the responsibility) of the
29    lessees, [if] it seems good to lease.
30    Ptolemaios son of [Nem]esion, I have leased
31    as [stated above.]
