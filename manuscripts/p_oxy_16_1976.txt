[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine [a]nd most pious lord Flavius
2     Tiberius Mauricius the eternal Augustus and Imperator,
3     year 1, after the consulship of him of divine lot, our late
4     lord Tiberius Constantine, year 4, Hathyr 27, in the 1st indiction.
5     To the most exalted heirs of him of glorious memory,
6     Apion the former protopatricius, landowners also here
7     in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas the servant,
8     who puts the formal question and assumes for his own masters, the
9     same all-praiseworthy men, the prosecution and the obligation: the Aurelii
10    Samuel son of Biktor, mother Sosanne, and Hareotes son of
11    Phoibammon, mother Tsaeie, originating from the hamlet
12    of Melitas of the Oxyrhynchite nome, with a guarantor Georgios their steward,
13    son of the late Apollos, from the (city) of the Oxyrhynchites.
14    We acknowledge that we have received from your exaltedness
15    from now already, by way of seed-sowing of the crops of the second
16    indiction, of wheat by the cancellus-measure eleven artabas, thus:
17    I, Hareotes, six artabas, I, Samuel, the
18    other five artabas, total of wheat 11 artabas, and these we acknowledge
19    to furnish to your exaltedness at the time of the threshing-season
20    of the second indiction, or else I, the guarantor of these, from my own (means) on their behalf
21    to give. Valid (is) the note, written in single copy, and being formally questioned we acknowledged. The Aurelii
22    Hareotes and Samuel, it agrees with us as is set forth. I, Papnouthios, wrote
23    on behalf of (them) being illiterate. I, Georgios the steward, son of the late Apollos, the aforewritten,
24    stand surety for the same Samuel and Hareotes for all things as is set forth.
25    By me, Papnouthios, it was completed.
26    Note of Hareotes and Samuel, from the hamlet of Melitas,
27    with a guarantor Georgios the steward, son of [A]po[ll]os: of wheat by the cancellus-measure 1[1] artabas.
