# HGV-EN: Copy of receipt. 17th year of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajanus Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, Pachon 27th. Herakleides, son of Maron, ex-keeper of the public archives, has paid as a penalty on account of his administration in the district of Herakleides during the 16th year, two hundred eighty two drachmae and two obols, say 282 dr. 2 ob., extra 17 dr. 4 ob., and according to another receipt of the same 17th year of Trajanus Caesar the lord, in the month Epeiph after other payments on the 7th: the same through his children, Herakleides also called Valerius and Lysimachos, for penalty on account of his administration during the aforesaid keeping of the public archives one thousand two hundred - - - drachmae, extra - - - 1 obol - - -
