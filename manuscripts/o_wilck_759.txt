1     Haryothes son of Pamonthes to Bion, greeting.
2     I have from you the price of wheat, seven artabas,
3     from the (share) that falls to you
4     from the lease, total wheat 7 artabas.
5     Year [16] which is also 13, Mecheir 4.
6     Haryothes son of Pamonthes wrote (this).
