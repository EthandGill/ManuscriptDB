1     [To Diosk]ourid[es, strategos of the Hermopolite nome ] …
2     [from] A[urelia Demetria ] … daughter of Horio[n, her mother being] Eudaimonis, of the city of Her[m]opolis,
3     [in the] western quarter … [ with] my attending Au[r]elius E… [son of] Ag[a]thos Daimon Theonermeinos, his mother being … [ of the Hermopolite nome, my kinsman.] Apart from the house I have registered … [ and] all the things appurtenant on the west side
4     … [ I regist]er, according to the things commanded by V]aleri[u]s [Dato]s the most illustrious governor, for
5     [the house-by-house regist]ration of the [2]4th year of Marcus Aurelius [Severus] Antoninus Caesar the lord,
6     [for my belongings, mys]elf and my own:
7     … [ ] my [slave], from a mother (named) Plotine, of the city, western quarter, 34 years old
8     … (the slave) named also Beryllos, surnamed Xenophon, another, from a mother
9     … [ also called] Hermione, my slave, of the city, western quarter, 28 years old
10    [and myself, Aur]elia Demetria the aforewritten, of gymnasium class, 72 years old
