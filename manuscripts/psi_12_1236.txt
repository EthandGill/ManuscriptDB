[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Fl]avius Ptolemaeus and to Marion, the same as Dioscorus
2     And to Apion, [having been] designated by Claudius
3     Quintianus, the most excellent epistrategos, towards
4     Examination of accounts
5     From Zosimu and Mysthos and Eirenaios, heirs of Eirenaios Zosimu, [who] had become a collector of money of the fourth toparchy of Themistos division, of those concerning
6     [Philagrid]a. [They] seek from you receipts of which [they] deposited money through the administration to the public bank of the places
7     Of [the accounts] from the fifth year until the sixth year, of the fifth year the accounting in Pachon, of the
8     [Sixth year] the accounting in Hathur [and] Choiak, I presented before you, swearing by the
9     [Emperor] Caesar Trajan Hadrian, August [one's] fortune
10    That the authentic [documents] are with you, which also we showed, and
11    Whenever if need should arise, [we] will bring [them]. It is of the
12    [Fifth year] the accounting Pachon, which [he] administered, the [one who] had died,
13    Our father Eirenaios, of the villages Andromachis [and] Theoxenis
14    Of the city of Hermu, [a] copy; in the fifth year of Emperor Caesar Trajan
15    Hadrian the August, Eirenaios and Heron, collectors, recorded [it] through payment
16    With other [things], and on Epeiph the 13th, towards the accounting of Pachon, silver drachmae three thousand;
17    [Total:] drachmae three thousand; and on Mesore the sixth, towards the accounting of Pachon, silver drachmae one thousand
18    Three hundred [and] fifty-six; [total:] drachmae one thousand three hundred fifty-six; and [of the] sixth year, which [he] administered
19    Our father Eirenaios, of the village Philagrid; in the sixth year of the Emperor
20    Caesar Trajan Hadrian the August, Eirenaios collector of Philagrid
21    With other [things], and on Tubi the 7th, towards the accounting of Hathur, silver drachmae three thousand; [total:] drachmae three thousand.
22    [And] on Tubi the 19th, towards the accounting of Hathur, silver drachmae one thousand four hundred; [total:] drachmae one thousand four hundred; and
23    [On T]ubi the 24th, towards the accounting of Hathur, silver drachmae one hundred seventy-five two-obols; [total:] drachmae one hundred seventy-five two-obols.
24    [A] copy of signature; the above-written [I] have presented. And we swear
25    The foregoing oath as it lies [above]. In the year 12, on Tubi the 12th.
