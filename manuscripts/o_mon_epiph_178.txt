[TRANSLATION]
1     Behold, here [is] coming out from
2     The least, not humble, the monk, the son
3     The son of Daniel, writing, beseeching
4     And bowing, and prostrating himself before the god
5     Without his knowledge, prostrating himself before your lordship
6     Son, which is in Christ, the service all, from
7     The little [things], to the great, so that it be pleasing to your lordship, that you do the work with my humility, [that] you will
8     As if you were captive slaves
9     And when you gather together for work, do this for the lord
10    Of us, god, know that I
11    A miserable [one] of wretchedness
