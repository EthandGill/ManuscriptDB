[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fif[t]eenth year of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus
2     [G]ermanicus, in the month P[eri]tios 1 = Choiach 1, in Karanis of the division of Herakleides
3     of the Arsinoit[e] nome. [M]arcus Sempronius
4     Gem[e]l[l]us, sol[d]ier of the third legion Cyrenaica, of the century of Po[m]ponius
5     Severus, about forty years old, with a scar on the ankle of the left foot, acknowledges to Mikkalos
6     son of Ptolemaios, about forty-five years old, with a scar in the middle of the forehead under the hair,
7     that he has received from him forth[wit]h, through hand, from the house, of silver one thousand
8     two hundred and forty drachmas, and the interest on these, which the
9     Mikkalos owed to him according to a loan complet[e]d through the record-office in Alexandria
10    in the thirteenth year of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus
11    Germanicus, on Pha[rmouth]i 9; and Marcus Sempronius
12    Gemellus has handed it over to him for cancellation, and that neither he, Marcus Sempronius
13    Gemellus, nor his agents shall proceed against Mikkalos nor against his agents, neither
14    concerning the things which he has received, as is stated above, of silver one thousand two hundred
15    and forty drachmas, nor concerning their interest, nor concerning any other
16    matter whatsoever, nor debt, nor any agreement at all, written or unwritten, from the earlier
17    times up to the present day, in any manner. Let the contract
18    be valid everywhere, just as the things agreed are also valid.
19    I, Marcus Sempronius [Ge]mellus, soldier of the third legion Cyrenaica, of the century of Pomponius Se[ve]rus, acknowledge that I have receiv[ed] from Mikkalos son of Ptolemaios the
20    one thousand two hundred and forty drachmas of silver, and the interest, which they owed me according to a loan completed through the record-office in Alexandria in the thirteenth year of Domitian
21    the lord, in the month Pharmouthi; which I have also handed over for annulment and cancellation, and
22    neither shall I proceed concerning any other matter whatsoever, written or unwritten, as
23    is sta[ted]. Mikkalos son of Pto[lema]ios: the receipt has been made out to me, as is stated above. It [has been re]gistered th[rough the] record-office in Karanis.
