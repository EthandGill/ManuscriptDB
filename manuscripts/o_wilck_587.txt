1     Soter and Ailour[ion, collectors of money-taxes of Charax],
2     to Senphaeris (daughter of) Pana[mes. We have received for land-survey tax of year 22,]
3     which is also year 1, base drachmas eig[ht, total base drachmas 8. Year 2 of Titus]
4     Aelius Anton[inus Caesar the lord,]
5     Thoth 2. (2nd hand) I, A[menothes, have signed].
6     (3rd hand) I, Ailourion, have signed, drachmas 8.
7     (1st hand) Others, Phaophi 10, in the name of the same, likewise for land-survey tax of year 1, base
8     drachmas four, total drachmas 4. I, Ailourion, through Horos the assistant, have signed.
9     (2nd hand) I, Amenothes, have signed.
10    (1st hand) Others, the 21st, in the name of [the same], likewise base drachma 1, 1 obol; and in the name
11    of Petenob( ) (son of) Ps[enamounis], for land-survey tax of year 1, base, 4 obols.
12    I, Ailourion, through Horos the assistant, have signed. (2nd hand) I, Amenothes, have signed.
