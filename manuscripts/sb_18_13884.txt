1     … for [the sake of need] to be present
2     … we certainly speak of optiones
3     … for we have been taught by the responsaries
4     [of] the same roll the wrongful need of the optio; but if also the
5     [optio]nes think [it is] a need of the presence in accordance with Antinoopolis
6     [of other certain] per[sons of the] number, it will also be necessary [for] those with them
7     to be sent here so that other causes not be placed on the matter
8     of the fixed to them grain-ration bread. Immediately therefore with the letters
9     receiving immediately your brightness prepare the mentioned
10    Theodore and John and also [those] whom they think need to have other
11    persons from their number to come to us; for if they delay,
12    they are about to suffer themselves and the whole of their noble roll the greatest
13    harm. And behold also to the prior-commanders of their number
14    we have written so that immediately they send them forth here and not
15    [suffer] danger themselves from the neglecting of our
16    on this orders, and the aforementioned optiones
17    under reliable sureties here to be sent prepare,
18    [so that not] through their pretext might be found the public damaged
19    and from this this number of them into troubles might fall.
