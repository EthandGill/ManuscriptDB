1     [ ] After the consulship of our lord Flavius Novus
2     [Leo] the eternal Augustus for the 1st time, (month of) Tybi 30, of the fortu[na]te thirteenth indiction, in Herakleopolis. To Flavius

5     [through] Julius Anou[b]ion your wine-dealer, Aurelius

8     [of the wine which I bought fro]m you, approved by me,
