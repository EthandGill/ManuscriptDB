[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Probus and Abie[n]us of the most distinguished
2     Phamenth 12, 11th indiction, in Arsinoe.
3     Aurelius Pamoun, son of Aeion
4     farmer from the city of the Arsinoites
5     from the district of New Church to Aurelius
6     Heraclides, son, and himself a farmer
7     from the same city, greetings. I acknowledge to have received
8     from you by hand into my own and necessary
9     need, [of] gold coins, half valued as per gold currency ½ valued as per principal, and I have been paid also the price of grain
10    of satisfactory [grain], artabas three and a half
11    [loan of] Pamoun, son of Aeion, far[m]er …
