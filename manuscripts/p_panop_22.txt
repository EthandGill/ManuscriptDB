[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Julius Constantius, patrician, brother of our master Constantine Augustus,
2     and of Rufius Albinus, the most illustrious, Phamenoth 29. I, Aurelius Heron son of Hierakapollon, former magistrate and councillor of the city of Panopolis, signing
3     in my own hand, have set down this release to the Aurelii Panodoros son of Pasnos and Orphanos his brother and Senpetemeine his sister, from the same city, whereby I acknowledge today, being satisfied, that I release the eight and a quarter arourae which I purchased from you,
4     of sown and … privately-owned and dry land, in the plain of Pmouchis, of the cleruchic class, on plot 164, in
5     the consulship of Papius
6     Pacatianus, the most illustrious prefect of the sacred praetorium, and of Mecilius Hilarianus, the most illustrious, Phamenoth 21, through the bank of Aurelius
7     Apollonios, … in accordance with the rights and securities which you declared you held, whose securities were declared through the deed of conveyance, having been paid by you today the moneys declared through the conveyance for the price of these, and (I acknowledge) that I will bring no charge against you
8     concerning the same conveyance, because I have been paid in full the moneys of the price, so that from now on you shall own and control
9     and hold full ownership in whatever manner … if you wish, you gathering in the resulting
10    produce and paying the taxes of every kind, public and additional, that shall be assessed,
11    all of them, from the present fortunate tenth indiction
12    and this same one, for all time, the former conveyance being void and not liable to be brought forward at any time; and being questioned concerning
13    this release, that it has thus been rightly and well done, I acknowledged. I, Theonas son of Theodoros, appointed representative, wrote the body of the document. I, Aurelius Heron son of Hierakapollon, former magistrate and councillor, have released, being satisfied, the aforesaid eight and a quarter arourae of sown and privately-owned and dry land, and I have received
14    the moneys declared through the conveyance, as aforesaid.
15    Completed through me, Charisios son of Ktistes, notary of the city of Panopolis. …
16    Theonas son of Theodoros having been appointed representative …
