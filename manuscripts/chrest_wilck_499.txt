[TRANSLATION]
1     Senpamonth to Pamonth
2     to his brother, greetings.
3     I sent to you the body of Senyrios
4     of my mother, having been buried, having a board on
5     the neck through Gallis
6     [the] father of Hierax in a boat
7     private, the boat-fare having been given
8     by me in full. And it is
9     [the] mark of the burial: there is a linen cloth outside having a rose-scented unguent, inscribed on the belly [is] her name. I wish you to be well,
10    brother, I pray.
11    Year 3, Thoth 11.
12    To Pamonth of Morus from Senpamonth [her] sister.
