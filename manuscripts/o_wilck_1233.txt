[TRANSLATION]
1     (2nd hand) I have received (a copy).
2     (1st hand) Simon (son of) Iazaros, who has
3     farmed the quarter-tax
4     on the fishermen for the 28th year,
5     to Mesoueris, greeting. I have from you,
6     toward the tax of you and (your) sons,
7     in the month Tybi, of copper, four
8     thousand (dr.), total 4000. There wrote
9     Dellous, Simon
10    having requested (it), because
11    he does not know
12    how to write.
