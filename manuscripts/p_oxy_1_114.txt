[TRANSLATION]
1   the …(?) …[…]… Now
2   let it be your care to redeem my things from Sara-
3   pion. They lie pledged for 2 minas; I have paid the
4   interest up to Epeiph, at a stater per mina:
5   a frankincense-colored leather hooded cape, an onyx-colored
6   leather hooded cape, a tunic and a white hooded cape
7   of true purple, a kerchief with Laconian stripe,
8   a purple-bordered linen cloth, 2 bracelets, a necklace,
9   a coverlet, an Aphrodite, a bowl, a tin
10   flask, a big one, and a jar. From Onetor
11   fetch the 2 armlets. They lie pledged for eight “hands”
12   since Tybi of last year, at a stater per mina. If
13   the cash does not suffice — through the negligence of our lady
14   Theagenis — if then the cash does not suf-
15   fice, sell the bracelets to make up the
16   sum. Greet warmly Aia and Eutychia
17   — I pray for [y]our health — and Alexandra. Xanthilla greets
18   Aia and all
19   hers.
20   […] ✗ from Eunoi[a].
