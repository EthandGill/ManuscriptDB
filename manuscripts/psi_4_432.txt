[TRANSLATION]
1     […]os to Zenon, greetings. Dionysios the farmer and Philon, sailing down, have ordered me to work
2     the one hundred twenty arouras. If then you think fit to have them sown with vetch, we will sow with vetch
3     indeed ninety arouras, or it may also become sesame, and with lotus herbage thirty arouras.
4     But if you want [a crop to be] wheat-bearing, [see that] the seed [be supplied] in time. Whenever then you think fit, let it be supplied promptly, so that we not fall short of the seasons.
5     Farewell.
6     of the month Mesore
7     the 2nd.
