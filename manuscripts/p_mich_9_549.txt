[TRANSLATION]
1     … in the second year of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus
2     [I made the following dispositions, being of sound mind and] understanding, Sambathion daughter of Neilos son of Neilos, of those from the metropolis, [about … years old,] weak in eyesight, with
3     [her guardian] … Socrates son of Sarapion, of those from the same metropolis, sub… the recorded
4     [before] … the memoranda of the former archidikastes, of which the date is of the present [year] … and that they remain
5     … by Apollonios the younger son of Sarapion, of those from the record-office, of Sabinus son of …
6     … of the registry. May it be, while I am in health, for me to have authority over my own [property] … in whatever manner I may choose; but if I suffer anything human (i.e. die), the slave belonging to me, Abaskantos, house-born … of the deceased
7     … of Apion, according to a contract drawn up through the registry-office in the village of [Karanis,] for those who wish
8     … being in the Caesareum in one thousand one hundred drachmas, and the public taxes … and whatever else
9     … of my brother Neilos son of Julius Sabinus, of those from the legion, as before his military service
10    … also called Sabinus son of Neilos son of Gaius Julius Apollinarius … …
11    … my laying-out and funeral I urge to be carried out …
12    … in the property belonging to me in the aforesaid Karanis … …
