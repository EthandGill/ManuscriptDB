[TRANSLATION]
1     During the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and his son [Ptolemy]
2     In year 26, under the priesthood of Taurine son of Alexander, and Alexander [...] and of the [gods Broth]ren, kanephore of Arsinoe Philadelphus, Arsinoe of [Poseidon]ios
3     Of the month Choiak, Ptolemy holds P... [...]
4     From Petosirius son of Amasios, the [one] from Apollonidus, the
5     Nomarch from the upper toparchy, the [writ]ten account and expenditure of seed for the 27th year of the ...
6     Of a cavalry allotment
7     Of Spartakos ... vetch ... wheat ...
8     Pure, unadulterated grain from all shall be measured in a just measure
9     In accordance with the bronze [standard]; let Ptolemy measure out into the
10    Royal [treasuries], from Thoth of the 27th year, the seed which he received
11    Together with the exports from new [grain]; but if he does not measure, let him pay back four drachmas per artabe, and the legal action toward the royal treasuries.
