[TRANSLATION]
1     [From] the Aurelii Sarmates son of Silvanus and
2     Ionios son of Ammonios, [village-heads,] and Kastor son of Teukes, overseer,
3     and Choous son of Herakles and Phibis son of Herakles and
4     Ammonios son of Ionios and Silvanus son of Pkylis, grain-officers of the 13th indiction,
5     all of them from the village of Prektis of the same nome,
6     greeting. We deliver up and nominate
7     the following registered [grain-officers] of our
8     village, being men of means and suitable, able
9     to carry out the liturgy entrusted to them
10    soundly and faithfully and blamelessly, at our risk
11    and (that) of all those from the village. And they are:
12    grain-officers of the assessment and of the patrimonium:
13    Ammonas son of Choous,
14    Herakles son of Phibis,
15    K… ki…
16    [To Aurelius Asklepiades, praepositus]
17    [of the 15th] pagus of the nome [of Hermopolis,]
18    from the Aurelii Sarmates son of Silvanus and Ionios
19    son of Ammonios, village-heads, and Kastor son of Teukes,
20    overseer, and Choous son of Herakles and Phibis son of Herakles
21    and Ammonios son of Ionios and Silvanus son of Pkylis,
22    grain-officers of the 13th indiction, all of them from
23    the village of Prektis of the same nome, greeting.
24    We deliver up and nominate the following registered grain-officers of our village
25    being men of means and suitable, able
26    to carry out the liturgy entrusted to them
27    soundly and faithfully and blamelessly, at our risk
28    and (that) of all those from our village.
29    And they are: grain-officers of the assessment [and of the] patrimonium of the division of Hermopolis:
30    Ammonas son of Choous,
31    Herakles son of Phibis.
