[TRANSLATION]
1     [ ] After the consulship of the Flavii Monaxius and
2     Plinta, the most illustrious, Mecheir 20.
3     To Flavius Isak, ex-praepositi, landholder in the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
4     procurator of the most divine House, through
5     Didymos the wine-seller, Aurelius Phoibamm[on]
6     son of Ammonios, from the same city, greet[ing.]
7     I acknowledge that I owe and am in debt to you on behalf of the price
8     of wine which I have bought from you and carried off,
9     according to the agreements between us, of gold one simple lordly coin, full-weight, approved,
10    one, that is 1 coin, which, being free from all
11    risk, I shall of necessity render to you in the
12    month Pauni of the present year 96 = 65, at the beginning
13    of the fourth indiction, without delay, the exaction belonging to you both from me and from
14    all my property. The bond is valid,
15    written in single copy, and having been formally questioned I acknowledged it.
16    I Aurelius Phoibammon son of Ammonios, the aforesaid,
17    have and owe from the price of wine the one gold coin and I shall render it at the appointed term as set forth.
18    I Aurelius Ammonios son of Mauros wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
19    (Through me, Nadu(?), Nikios(?)).
20    Bond of Phoibammon son of Ammonios, attendant, son of Ammon, of 1 coin.
