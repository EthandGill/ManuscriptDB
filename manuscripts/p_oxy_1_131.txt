[TRANSLATION]
1   † To my good lord, (next) after God, a petition (and) supplication † from me,
2   Susneus, your pitiable slave, from Patani. I inform
3   my good lord of the matter concerning me, which is in this
4   way. While my father lived he called me and
5   my brothers, saying that one of you shall hold
6   the property of your mother Io[..]raphe, and the others are maintained from my property;
7   and he raised up David my younger
8   brother, and gave (him) into the holding of my mother.
9   And as he was about to die, my father ordered that there be given
10   to that David from his own [pr]operty a half-aroura, saying
11   that the half-aroura suffices him, since he also has the holding of
12   his mother. And behold, it is three years today since he died;
13   and as soon as he died I went to Abraamios the
14   headman of Klaudianos, and he brought forward the witnesses
15   who had been present over my father — that is, Julius the elder
16   and Apollon — and he caused it to be done according to the word of my father;
17   and year by year I sow my property, and
18   David my brother sows the property of my mother and
19   his half-aroura. And today Abraamios, suborned
20   by the same David, has warned me, saying that unless
21   my brother takes the property of the mother — save one (aroura) — and the half-aroura
22   which my father gave him, then again there be re-divided between me and
23   him whatever my father left me; for my father gave to my mother
24   110 sol(idi) to divide between me and m[y] brothers,
25   and these she gave to Elisabet my elder sister. And I beg
26   my good lord to make provision that, according to what my father said,
27   the right be safeguarded for me. †
