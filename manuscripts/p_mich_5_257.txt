[TRANSLATION]
1     16th year, Pharmouthi 29
2     Didyme of Petesouchos, [wife] of Chambouchis, together with the lord, [my] husband Harpoones of Orseutes, I acknowledge [that] I have sold to Marites [son] of Dionysios Kamouchis my share—[namely the] third from [a] fourth part of [a] three-story house and courtyard and of the things joining-with all [of them] in Tale, common and [undivided],
3     of which the neighbors of the whole house and courtyard [are], of-the-house to-the-south Petermouthios of Orseutes, bronze-worker, house;
4     of-the-courtyard Zenon of Ptolemaios, courtyard; to-the-north Anubion and [of] the brother, fuller, house; to-the-west Zenon of Ptolemaios, courtyard; to-the-east royal road. And I have received from Marites
5     the conceded and fixed price of the [portion] having-been-sold
6     in-full by hand from [the] house, and I will warrant the sale with every warranty from
7     all public and private [debts] and all types, as is set forth. He-wrote
8     in behalf of them Herodes [son] of Herodes because they do not know letters. Marites [son] of Dionysios Kamouchis [the sale] has come to me as is set forth. He-wrote in behalf of him Diodoros
9     [son] of Ptolemaios, because he does not know letters.
