[TRANSLATION]
1     As your Reverence wrote to me concerning the granary, behold, Petros the servant has gone up to the city.
2     Have the comes tell him together with the steward. But I have not found where to put grain, because
3     the two kyphai (jars/holds) have been loaded. Make haste to come up here on account of the
4     remainder of the grain. And when you come, your Reverence, bring a katenarion (small chain?), so that I may put … …
5     … and to put grain there. For, God willing, the matter of the grain has progressed and the
6     persuasion(?) is very good. And if you are ready to send my provision-pay up before the going-up,
7     inform me. For I have grown weary here this year more than others. For God alone knows that, if you
8     were here, you would not do what I do. And God bears me witness as to how I labor for you. As
9     your Reverence informed me concerning the wine, by God, I have already had in use another four
10    little (jars of) wine. For I did not wish to give grain in exchange for the little (jars of) wine I took in use. And write me the …
11    of the letters. And I greet you all, from small to great. And send us
12    a little (jar of) oil, since I have not found (any) here, nor did Menas the eirenarch wish to give me for
13    the nomismation. And let your Reverence know that I did not receive gold from Justus your son.
14    [Deliver to] the most God-fearing Martyrios the presbyter.
15    From Esaeios the deacon.
