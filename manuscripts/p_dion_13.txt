1     In the reign of Cleopatra and Ptolemy, the gods Philometores
2     Soteres, in the sixth year, the priest who is in Alexandria being of Alexander and
3     of the Soter gods and the Brother-gods and the Benefactor-[gods an]d the Father-loving gods and [the Manif]est-gods and the god Eupator and [the] god Philome[t]or

5     the sacred [colt of Isis the moth]er of the gods being great, the garland-bearer of Queen Cleo[patra the goddess Philometor Soteira Dikaiosyne Nikephor]os, the prize-bearer
6     [of Berenike Euergetis,] the light-bearer of Queen C[leopatra] the goddess Philometor Soteira Dikaiosyne Nikephoros, the basket-bearer of Ar[sinoe Phil]a[delphos,] the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator, those being in [Alexandria,]
7     in the [month of Audnaios] on the sixteenth, Hathyr the sixteenth, [in the village]
8     of Te[nis] also called Akoris of the Hermopolite (nome), [Dionysios]
9     [son of Apoll]onios, a Persian of the command of Artemidoros, of the A[korites,] lent
10    [to Dionysios son of Kephalas,] a Persian of the epigone, of solid wheat artabas
11    [fi]fty-five, which have been disbursed by him, at interest of half-as-much-again, [in arta]bas twenty-seven and a half; and the wheat and the
12    interest, the whole eighty-two and a half [wheat] artabas, let Dionysios son of Kephalas pay back to Dionysios son of Apollonios or to those
13    from him in the month of Loios, which is Pauni of the sixth year,
14    solid new clean unadulterated wheat, by the measure that goes to the
15    bronze (standard), and let him deliver it to the
16    harbor by Akoris at his own expenses; and if he does not pay back according to what
17    is written, let Dionysios son of Kephalas pay to Dionysios
18    son of Apollonios or to those from him as the price of each artaba of [b]ronz[e]
19    three thousand drachmas, and for the half the corresponding amount;
20    and let the right of execution belong to Dionysios son of Apollonios and to those from him, for the things
21    according to the contract, both upon Dionysios son of Kephalas himself and
22    upon all his property, as by legal judgment;
23    let this contract be valid. Witnesses: Alexandros son of Pasis,
