[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of treasury of the metropolis of harvest, 22nd year
2     Of Commodus Antoninus
3     Caesar of the lord, Epeiph 5
4     On behalf of the Agorae, name Senesminios Hōros
5     Phahérios wheat four
6     Total wheat artabae 4 and on behalf of the Agorae, name Petechespochrátou
7     Hériés wheat one-half one-quarter amounts to wheat artaba one-half one-quarter, total to the same wheat artabae 4 one-half one-quarter.
8     Amōnios I have marked, wheat artabae 4 one-half one-quarter.
