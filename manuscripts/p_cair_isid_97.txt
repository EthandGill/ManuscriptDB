1     Aurelius Ptolem[aios] son of Dio[skoro]s, his mother being …, about 32 years old, with a scar

9     one hundred, which under com[pul]sion we shall rep[ay] in the month Pauni in the

13    as [if by legal judgment. And on being formally que]stioned we have acknowledged.
14    In the consulship of our lord [Diocl]etian the father of the emperors for the [10th time] and
15    of Galerius Valerius Maximianus [Augustus for the 7th time], before … the Kalends of May.
16    Aurelius Ptolemaios and Ptollas and Sabinos and Herodianos and Ol
17    and Melas and Isidoros and the rest, we acknowledge that we have the
18    bean (phaselos) artabas, one hundred, and we shall repay at the appointed term as is set out above,
19    and on being formally questioned we have acknowledged. Aurelius Demetrios son of Besarion
20    wrote on their behalf, as they declare that they do not know letters.
