[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of receipt which I also issued at another time. Petechwnsis, tax-collector of silver
2     Charakos, Porieuthi of Herakleios Apollodoros. I received for laography and bathing [tax]
3     Year 15, drachmas four; [they] amount to drachmas 4; year 15 of Antoninus Caesar
4     the lord, Pachon 6. Dioskoros, scribe, I have signed.
