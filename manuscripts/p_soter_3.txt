[TRANSLATION]
1     To Tamystha daughter of Antigonos, with her guardian, her kinsman Achillas
2     son of Aphrodisios,
3     from Soterichos son of Lykas, a Persian of the epigone. I wish
4     to lease from you the vineyard belonging to you near the village of Theadelphia of the Themistos division, of one and a half arourae or however many there may be, and the castor-oil plants and fruit-trees on the unirrigated land, and the belonging to you near the same village, of the
5     cleruchic allotment, eight arourae or however many there may be, in two plots;
6     and this offer for ten years, the vineyard from
7     the present ninth year, and the arourae from the coming
8     tenth year, and the vineyard at a third share
9     for the lessee and to you, Tamystha, the remaining
10    two-thirds share of all the wine-produce
11    that shall come forth, and further also another tenth share of the yield that shall come forth each year, and from the common stock all
12    the first-fruits, and of the arourae a rent each year per
13    aroura, together with the seed of wheat which I shall receive, one artaba
14    of wheat, eight artabae of wheat; and I shall carry out for the vineyard all the
15    proper works, the embankments and irrigations and double-hoeings, and all the agricultural works of the arourae, the embankments and irrigations and sowings and weedings and all the other agricultural service, [all]
16    at the proper seasons at my own expense, doing no damage; and
17    I shall sow the arourae with wheat and whatever crops I may choose,
18    except safflower; and the aforesaid yearly rents I shall pay in the month Pauni by the dromos-measure of four choinikes
19    in the same village of Theadelphia; and the supply of reeds
20    and cords for the vine-props newly made and other repair of the reed-work being
21    upon you, Tamystha, while I supply … ;
22    and … the nomarchic transport-charges …;
23    … ;
24    …; and after the term I shall hand over the arourae as
25    I too received them, and the vineyard after the vintage; and I shall plant each year in the vineyard vine-shoots …
26    you supplying mattocks, the fruit-trees and
27    all the plants on the unirrigated land being mine, Soterichos', of the …;
28    … each year being upon me, Soterichos, not to be re-leased and not to be worked further …, if it seem good to lease to me on all the aforesaid terms. I, Tamystha daughter of Antigonos, with my guardian, my kinsman Achillas son of Aphrodisios, have leased the vineyard and the arourae on all the aforesaid terms, not to be re-leased and
29    not to be worked further …;
30    …;
31    … in the [9th] year of the Emperor Caesar Domitian Augustus Germanicus …
