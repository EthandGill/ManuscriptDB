[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and master Jesus Christ our God and saviour.
2     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master, the greatest benefactor [Flavius]
3     Heraclius, the eternal Augustus and Imperator, year 4, Pachon [13, of the 2nd indiction.]
4     To Flavius Apion, the all-praiseworthy and most magnificent [former consul and]
5     patrician, landholder also here in the illustrious [city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas]
6     the slave who questions and provides surety for his own master, the same
7     all-praiseworthy man, the (right of) action and obligation: I, Joseph, psaltes [of the guild]
8     of Saint Theodore and rent-collector of your glorious house, son of the [pious]
9     memory Victor, former deacon and evangelist, [subscribing below]
10    with my own hand, from the same city, greeting. I acknowledge [of my own free will]
11    and by deliberate choice that I have agreed with your [magnificence]
12    through those belonging to it, for one year reckoned from the beginning [of the]
13    rents of the present second indiction, on (the terms) that I [fill the office of the]
14    rent-collector with it, in the …
15    properties situated in this city of the Oxyrhynchites,
16    namely from houses and outbuildings and other (properties) belonging [to the]
17    glorious house of yours and pertaining to the rent-collectorship and … [to the]
18    your magnificence, through those belonging to it, by way of pact …
19    of the same one year, one hundred twenty-five gold solidi [by the private]
20    standard of this city; and the small change supplied by me [to those]
21    belonging to the glorious house of yours, in accordance with the …
22    receipts (issued) to me, at thirty-two thousand for [each solidus]
23    by the private standard, is given. The oil supplied by me … [in accordance with the]
24    prevailing custom, receiving the requisitioners for …
25    And I shall render my accounts to your magnificence …
26    of the pact of the one hundred twenty-five solidi by number,
27    and I shall pay in full the (sums) from the audits at my own risk and that [of my]
28    substance, having pledged, by the right of this contract,
29    all my property, present and future, specifically and generally, [by way of security]
30    and the right of mortgage. The contract is valid, written in duplicate, [and when questioned, I acknowledged it.]
31    I, Joseph, with God psaltes and rent-collector, son of the pious memory Victor, [former deacon]
32    and evangelist, the aforewritten, have made this contract of …
33    from the beginning of the rents of the second indiction, and I shall pay the …
34    one hundred twenty-five gold solidi by the private standard, in the afore[said manner]
35    as set out above. I have subscribed with my own hand and have issued (it).
36    Through me, Joannes the notary, it was completed.
37    … (Pachon?) 13 of the 2nd indiction, year 290 2[59.]
38    [Contract of Joseph], the most pious psaltes of the guild of Saint Theodore and rent-collector,
39    [son of the pious mem]ory of Victor, former deacon and evangelist, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites.
