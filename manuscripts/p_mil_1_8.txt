[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eighth year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
2     Augustus Germanicus Imperator, in the month
3     … 1 … in Theadelphia
4     of the Themistos division of the Arsinoite
5     nome. There acknowledges Aunes son of Harthotes,
6     a Persian of the epigone, aged about twenty-
7     eight, scar above the left eyebrow,
8     to Sotas son of Psenesis, aged about forty-five, scar on the middle of the forehead,
9     that he has from him, from what he handles from
10    Claudius Epimachos, the price of new
11    clean unadulterated barley, of artabas
12    two and a half, by the dromos four-choinix measure of the granary of Eisis, of which let the acknowledging party make the repayment to
13    Sotas in the month Pauni of the present eighth year of Tiberius Claudius
14    Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator
15    in the village; but if he does not [repay]
16    according to what has been written, let him pay (it) with [the half as much again],
17    the right of execution belonging to S[ot]as
18    both against the acknowledging party and against all his property as if by legal judgment.
19    I, Aunes son of Harthotes, a Persian of the
20    epigone, acknowledge that I have
21    from Sotas son of Psenesis
22    the price of barley, of artabas two
23    and a half, by the dromos four-choinix measure of the granary of Isis,
24    which I shall also repay in the month Pauni
25    of the present year, as is set forth. There wrote
26    on his behalf Sarapion son of Zenon,
27    because he does not know letters.
28    I, Sotas son of Psenesis — there has come into being for me
29    the acknowledgment, as is set forth.
30    There wrote on his behalf Dorion
31    son of Aphrodisios, because he does not know letters.
32    In the eighth year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
33    Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
34    … 14 … wrote
35    I, Sotas son of Psenesis, [have] received the aforesaid bar[ley, artabas 2 ½.] There wrote on
36    his behalf Dorion son of Aphrodisios, because
37    he does not know letters.
38    In the 8th year of Tiberius Germanicus Imperator,
39    in the month Germanikeios 28.
