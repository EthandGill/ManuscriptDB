[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of God Korra son of Szerich] to you from [the] village of Aphrodito.
2     [Provide on account of wages and expense for timber] for the labor of the mosque of Damascus
3     [of six months on the present eighth indiction] in numerical coins 4 four alone. It was written in the month Athur 7 indiction [8]
