16    Augustus, Phaophi 30, in Ptolemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome. Segathis, daughter of Satabous, son of Stotoetis, about twen[ty-]
17    nine years old, with a scar on the ankle of her left foot, and her [mother] Thases,
18    daughter of Stotoetis, son of Mesonoeus, about 53 years old, without distinguishing mark, both [with] their guardian,
19    the son of Thases and (likewise) the brother of Segathis by the same father,
20    Stotoetis, about 32 years old, with a scar on his left eyebrow, (acknowledge) to Theano, daughter of Sarapion, son of [De]m[e]trios, citizeness, about 33 years old, without distinguishing mark, with her guardian, her kinsman Ptolemaios, son of Mari[on],
21    son of Ptolemaios, of the Sosicosmian tribe and the Althaean deme, about 48 years old, without distinguishing mark, namely
22    that Segathis has sold to Theano, by deed of sale, through the record-office in the aforementioned city,
23    from now for all time, the slave-child belonging to

26    seven hundred dr[ach]mas, through the … bank of Phremei, and that Segathis and her agents will [guar]antee the sale, [as]
27    aforesaid, of the slave-[ch]ild [Sot]as [with every] guara[n]tee, [and will furnish (him)]
28    free from claim and unpledged and unmortgaged and [clean (free)]
29    from every public debt up to now …, and from
30    [private (debts)] and every claim through[out], with no one hindering Theano nor her agents from being owners [of the slave-]child Sotas and disposing of him …
