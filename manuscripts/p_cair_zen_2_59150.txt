[TRANSLATION]
1     Theodoros to Zenon, greeting. In the
2     28th year there were brought down for us to Alexandria
3     ninety artabas of wheat,
4     which we measured out from the countryside.
5     Of these we have measured out, by your instruction,
6     from Artemidoros, the one in Alexandria,
7     on the 11th of Xandikos 1 artaba, on the 20th 4 artabas,
8     on the 30th 10 artabas, on the 26th of Artemisios 10 artabas,
9     on the 5th of Loios 30 artabas, total: 55 artabas of wheat, and
10    there remain owed to us with you 35 artabas of wheat.
11    You would do well, then, to write (telling us) from whom
12    we shall collect (it) in Alexandria. For having borrowed grain in advance from Artemidoros the
13    secretary, we are eager to pay (it) back.
14    And now too, having come to Alexandria, we demanded of Artemidoros, the one in charge of the house,
15    saying that you still owe (us),
16    but he said it was not up to him concerning these matters,
17    nor would he take heed even if you write. So see to it
18    concerning the collection, so that we do not charge the price
19    to you according to what is just, unless
20    by Zeus you sort out that the rest, and at last those
21    around Simylos, have received it, but that what is due to us
22    is lacking from you. And Artemidoros
23    the secretary too says that, though you wrote that it should be measured out,
24    no one took any heed.
25    Farewell.
26    Year 30, [Loi]os 2, Mesore 2.
27    [Theo]doros, concerning grain.
28    To Zenon.
