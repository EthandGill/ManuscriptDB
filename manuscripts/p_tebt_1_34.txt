[TRANSLATION]
1     Philoxenus to Apollonius the
2     brother, greetings and be well.
3     At the same time as receiving your letter
4     I came together with Horus of Kotys
5     to Hermias the village-scribe
6     on account of what was reported by him
7     and to Chairemon the
8     agent. But let [him] be released
9     and not [be troubled] by anyone
10    on account of having written to us
11    De[me]trius concerning him, and he being
12    under protection and a farmer.
13    But I write to you instructions for them
14    to give.
15    Athenion of Archias
