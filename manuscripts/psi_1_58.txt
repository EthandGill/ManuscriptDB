[TRANSLATION]
1     [of the reign and consulship of the most divine and] most pious our master Flavius Justinian, the eternal Augustus and emperor
2     [of the year] ... in the city of Oxyrhynchus
3     [to Flavius Apion, the most renowned and most illustrious] man of consular rank and patrician, landholder, and here in the illustrious
4     [city of Oxyrhynchus, through Menas, servant,] the one petitioning and bringing to his own master the same most renowned
5     [man the petition and submission, so-and-so,] presbyter, son of Patates, mother Mary, and Patas, son of Ion, mother
6     … [setting out from] the estate of Pestetos, belonging to your excellency, of the Oxyrhynchite
7     [nome, greetings. We] acknowledge the following. For since from parents and ancestors
8     … your excellency, dwelling in the aforementioned estate and
