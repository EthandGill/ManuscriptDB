[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the na]me of the Lord and Master Jesus
2     [Christ] our God and Savior, in the reign
3     [of the most pi]ous and man-loving our master
4     [and great]est benefactor [Flavius] Herakleios the eternal
5     Aug[ust]us and Emperor, in the twentieth year,
6     [Ph]amenoth … [of the fourth indiction,] in Ar[sinoe].
7     A[u]relius Menas, son [of Apollos], from the farmstead of Kyra
8     M[ari]a, with a guarantor, A[pa Apol]los son of Biktor
9     from the farmstead of the Lord God, to the most illustrious Appa Kyros,
10    ostiarius (doorkeeper), son of the blessed Theodoros, from the
11    city of the Arsinoites, greeting. I acknowledge that I have leased
12    from you the (property) belonging to you, in the field of the same
13    farmstead of Kyra Maria, [in the holding] called
14    (that) of the son of Ioulos, a vine-plot of a [quarter of an] arura,
15    more or less, with all its rights,
16    for as long a time as you wish, from the crops of the fourth indiction, God willing,
17    on condition that I work the same plot and display all
18    vine-dressing labor in it duly
19    and blamelessly and without contempt, and from the
20    yield of the wine coming from it, God willing, yearly
21    two parts fall to you and one part to me. And I have received
22    for the work one hoe (xoida). But if I should be negligent as to the
23    working of the same plot, or be judged (at fault) to the damage
24    of its crop, on condition that you may remove me from it without
25    (compensation for) cultivation, even if I should reach the [time] of the vintage.
26    The lease is valid and, being questioned, [I acknowledged.] Aurelius
27    Menas son of Apollos: all is in agreement with me as stated.
28    Through me, Ioustos.
29    [Lease of a] vine-plot of 4 aruras in the field of the farmstead of Kyra Maria, made by Aurelius Menas son of
30    Apollos, with a guarantor Apollos son of Biktor, to Appa Kyros, ostiarius.
