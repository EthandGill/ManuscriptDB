[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the year 12 which is also 9, Audnaios 7, Hathyr 7,]
2     [in Hermopolis of the Thebaid.]
3     [Hipponikos son of Pt]olemaios lent,
4     [a Macedonian, centu]rion of the [command] of Arte[midoros], of the Akoritai, to Diony[sios son of Kephalas, a Persi]an of the epigone
5     [and to his mother Sen]abellis daughter of Heliodoros
6     [and to his wife Isidora] daughter of Pachois, both [Persian-women, with as guard]ian Dionysios the
7     [aforewritten, of solid wheat] 40 artabas, which they have measured [out from him at one-and-]a-half interest, [twenty] artabas of wheat; and the
8     [wheat and the inter]est, the whole [60 artabas of wheat, let the borrow]ers repay to Hip[ponikos or to those]
9     [from him, in the month Loios which is Pauni of the]
10    [aforesaid year, without lawsuit or judgment]
11    [and any malice, new solid wheat]
12    [clean, unadulterated, by the measure that is for the bronze (standard), and let them de]liver it to the harbor by Akoris
13    [at their own expense, to Hipponikos or]
14    [to those from him; and if they do not repay according to what is writ]ten, let the borrowers pay
15    [to Hipponikos or to those from him as the price of each artaba of bronze three thou]sand drachmas; and let the right of execution belong to Hipponikos and to those
16    [from him for the matters of the contract, both from them]
17    [the borrowers, and from one and from whichever]
18    [of them he chooses, and from their belongings]
19    all, [as if by legal judgment; (being) sureties for one ano]ther for the wh[eat or the aforewritten]
20    price for full payment, the borrowers;
21    let this contract [be valid].
