1     There has paid, Hermias (son of) Eponychos, for poll-tax
2     of year 3, ten dr., (for) bath-tax 1 dr. 1½ obols, total 11 dr. ½-obol and surcharges.
3     Year 3 of Imperator Caesar Vespasian
4     Augustus, Pharmouthi 28. Pasemis, I have signed.
