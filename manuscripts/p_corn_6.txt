[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakleios, 35 years old, a wart in the middle of the nose.
2     In the third year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, in the month Xanthikos on the tenth, Mecheir tenth,
3     [in Oxyr]hyncha of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. Herakleios
4     son of [Herak]leios, about thirty-five years old, a wart in the middle of the nose, lent to Dionysios son of Maron,
5     [Pers]ian of the epigone, about forty-two years old, a scar on the wrist of the right hand,
6     an[d t]o his wife Thasos daughter of Psoiphis, Persian-woman, about forty years old, a scar
7     on the [r]i[ght] eyebrow, with as guardian the aforewritten husband Dionysios, (the two of them being) of one another
8     sureties for repayment, of silver of stamped coinage, as capital, dr[ach]mas
9     [four] hundred eighty, which they also received from him at once [by] hand
10    from (his) house, at interest of one drachma and three obols per mina for each month. And this
11    loan, the four hundred and eighty drachmas of stamped coinage silver,
12    let the borrowers repay to Herakleios in the month Tybi
13    of the coming fourth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus. And if they do not repay, let them pay back the loan at one-and-a-half, and the interest simple, the right of execution belonging to Herakleios both against the borrowers and against all their property. And I, Herakleios son of Herakleios, have lent the aforesaid four hundred and eighty silver drach[mas] on all the aforewritten terms.
14    [Di]onysios son of Maron, Persian of the epigone, and my wife Thasos daughter of Psoiphis, Persian-woman, with as guardian me, the aforewritten husband Dionysios, (the two being) sureties of one another for repayment, we have the loan from
15    [Her]akleos son of Herakleos, the steward of (the property of) Herakleos
16    [son of] Herakleos son of Herodes, the four hundred eighty silver drachmas of stamped coinage
17    [of sil]ver, four hundred eighty, at interest as
18    of one drachma and three obols per mina for each month,
19    which we will also repay in the month Tybi of the coming fourth
20    year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, as is set out above. I, Dionysios the aforewritten, have written also on behalf of my wife
21    Thasos, who does not know letters, and I am registered as
22    her guardian. I am about forty-two years old, a scar on the wrist of the right hand. In the third year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
23    Mecheir tenth, through Hermias, law-scribe of Oxyrhyncha of the Arsinoite (nome).
24    Loan of 480 silver drachmas of Herakleios
25    against Dionysios and his wife.
