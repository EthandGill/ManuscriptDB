[TRANSLATION]
1   The [pri]est Haryotes to Pe...arios(?), prophet,
2   says: if you would kindly inquire
3   about me ... I have received from the ...[...]
4   [...] for the eighteenth
5   [year] of Caesar, of vegetable(-seed), for the twelve
6   months, one hundred and eleven and a half artabas,
7   with a surplus of five and a half artabas; and for Epeiph,
8   as the price of thirty-five artabas of vegetable(-seed),
9   one hundred and thirty-six silver drachmas,
10   instead of one hundred and fifty
11   six (drachmas) three obols; there remaining (owed)
12   twenty drachmas three obols, total 20 dr. 3 ob., 5 1/2 art.
13   And for the nineteenth year of Caesar,
14   for five months, of which forty-
15   six and a half artabas, with a surplus of two and ... artabas,
16   there remaining a surplus of 7 3/4 artabas, and in silver
17   twenty drachmas three obols, and as freight-charge
18   ten silver drachmas; total 30 dr. 3 ob., 7 3/4 art.
19   and there is deducted from the 46 1/2 artabas, for oil,
20   230 kotylai at a price of 60 drachmas; total 90 dr. 3 ob., 7 3/4 art.
