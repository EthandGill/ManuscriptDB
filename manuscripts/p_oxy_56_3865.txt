[TRANSLATION]
1     999 (= Amen, isopsephism)
2     To my most honoured lord
3     brother Martyrios,
4     Samuel, greeting.
5     [Before] all things I pray to the all-merciful God for
6     your health.
7     I wish you to know
8     that by the grace
9     of God we were able to do quite a (good) work in Senokome, and many
10    of them have given
11    their principal. Only the
12    yearly interest
13    have we remitted
14    to them, and what they owe for the current
15    time we have demanded back from them down to a single
16    little bundle. So do not at all
17    be anxious concerning us.
18    Do the work for yourself. For I hear from
19    the one carrying your letter that you are still idling about the
20    … of Akous and have spoken with no one
21    in Pakerke about the collection. Do you not
22    know that if
23    the water (?) recedes
24    and they are found
25    I pray that you
26    be well
27    for many
28    times.
29    (so as) to do the work in the field[s],
30    no one lays claim to
31    you for the rest; what you are doing,
32    do. For, God willing,
33    if we clean
34    our fields, after
35    three days I shall go up to the city
36    together with Faustinianos
37    and I shall make the same
38    Faustinianos catch
39    you at Pakerke. I wish you to know concerning
40    the mill of Orthonios
41    that I made his wife lease it with
42    all thankfulness,
43    and how much we did with that
44    crazy
45    Horos until
46    we could expel him from
47    there, he saying that my
48    father too has the half of it. And the pagarch of the village in the
49    same (matter) sent one tax-collector so as to demand back …
50    and I was required, on account of
51    the same mill, of denarii myriads 2,250
52    and I received the receipt
53    e…mon from
54    … chief … … grace …
