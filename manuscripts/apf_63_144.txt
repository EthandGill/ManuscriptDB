[TRANSLATION]
1     …they bring, you made known. And concerning the little ox,
2     if it has turned out well, sacrifice it. As long as
3     you dispatch the cook; and altogether, if
4     you require it, you have it with you at the ready, and
5     do not impute it to me as a favor in this matter. You sent me, through Miysis the sailor, two storage jars
6     and two pitchers … of an obol, and another two
7     of obols, and a little flask for Techosous,
8     and a staff for Heron my father. You made known to me
9     that he was shut up in the treasury of Atianus for
10    three days. For what reason? For the sailors took it away.
11    Eutyches having come to me, I gave to him, instead of 22 drachmas,
12    24 drachmas. Give thanks to him, to Eutyches and to Ptolemaios, because I guard you all continually
13    in the estate (lands). But you so guarded me — they took away not even one — the vessel
14    of the oil which I sent you, 12 and a half kotylai … 4 … 8 drachmas,
15    and now at 5 obols, 10 kotylai … which is 8 drachmas 2 obols.
16    You will send me some little salt-fish. And concerning
17    the little (girl), you … will be … if also among us
18    I was acting toward your satisfaction. And to whomever
19    you wish, only give him your right hand; but not even one thing
20    receive from him, until you make known to me
21    to whom you gave your right hand. If father (appa)
22    Demetrios goes out, you shall give to him the bundle of
23    the sebennia … e … ou. Thoth 30.
24    Greet the children and all those in the house.
