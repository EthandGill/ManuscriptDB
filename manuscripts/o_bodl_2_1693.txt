[TRANSLATION]
1     Karouris and partners, overseers of estates, produce-recorded, of the metropolis
2     of the heirs of Papustius Pamonthus.
3     You have delivered to Phthumenius and partners
4     wine-sellers, for which you owe of the public
5     wine, [containers/measures] two, of the produce
6     year 12, through us, the overseers.
7     Year 13 of Antoninus Caesar, our lord
8     Tybi 21.
