[TRANSLATION]
1     From Alupiōs.
2     Since I ordered the ox-cart
3     The one under Dēmeas to remain with you,
4     Provide feed for it of green fodder,
5     For the one day
6     Which he spends there, since [there is] work
7     I ordered him to do something.
8     Give to the ox-driver the customary expense. I pray you to be well, most dear [one].
9     To Herōneinaios, steward, Thrasō.
10    Year 10, Tybi 29.
