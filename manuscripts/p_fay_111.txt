[TRANSLATION]
1   Lucius Bellenus Gemellus
2   to Epagathos his own, greeting.
3   I blame you greatly: you
4   lost two piglets from the
5   jolting of the road, though you have
6   ten working beasts
7   in the village. Heraklidas the don-
8   key-driver spread the blame a-
9   round, saying that you told him
10   to drive the piglets on foot.
11   Moreover, I ordered you
12   to stay at Dio[nys]ias two
13   days, until you bought
14   20 artabas of lotus. They say the
15   lotus is to be had at Diony-
16   sia[s] at 18 dr. At whatever
17   price you find it, by all
18   means buy the 20 artabas of lotus —
19   count it urgent.
20   Push on the flooding
21   of all the olive-
22   groves, [and] assign at Sen-
23   [theus’] a workman for the green-
24   stuff, to flood; and water
25   the row of plants
26   at the prophet’s.
27   So do not do otherwise.
28   Farewell. Year 15 of Imperator
29   Caesar Domitian Augus[tus]
30   Germanicus, month Germanik(os).
31   [To Epagathos] his own,
32   [from Lucius Bellen]us Gemellus.
