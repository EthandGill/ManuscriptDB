[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Herculanus to Aplonarion,
2     the sweetest and most honored,
3     very many greetings.
4     I rejoiced greatly on receiving your letter, the
5     cutler having given it to me. But what
6     you write that you sent me through Plato
7     the dancer's (son), I did not receive.
8     But I was very grieved that
9     you did not come to the birthday-feast
10    of my child, you and your husband; for you could for many
11    days have rejoiced together with
12    him. But no doubt you had better (things). For this reason you have looked down on us. I want you always to be
13    well as (I want) for myself, but
14    again I am grieved that
15    you are away from me. But if, being away from me, you are not unwell,
16    I rejoice that you are well indeed,
17    and yet again I am vexed
18    not seeing you. Do what is
19    advantageous for you. For whenever you wish
20    to see us at any time, most gladly will we receive you.
21    You will do well, then, to come in (the month) Mesore to
22    [us], so that we may indeed see you.
23    [Gre]et your mother
24    [and] your father and Cal[lias.] My son greets you,
25    [and] his mother
26    [and Di]onysius my fellow-contestant, who serves me in
27    [the] record-office. Greet
28    all who [love] you.
29    I pray for your health.
30    Deliver to Aplonarion
31    from Herculanus …
32    from Flavius Herculanus
33    the patron.
