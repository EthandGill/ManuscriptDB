[TRANSLATION]
1     Tiberia to Pompeios [the]
2     brother and lord
3     most greetings. The
4     obeisance of you I do before the lady Athena. I pray to all the gods that once that day
5     may it become so that I
6     [may] your
7     face [may] worship.
8     But you have forgotten me.
9     And I am not able
10    to forget you. Someone has said
11    to me that you are about to
12    buy the abrikion. Buy it. I sent you fifty and five
13    dates through Petron[atos]
14    give to the boy
15    of mine and Hegemoni[da].
