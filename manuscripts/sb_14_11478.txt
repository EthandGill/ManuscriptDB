[TRANSLATION]
1     To Antonius Colonianus, the
2     most excellent epistrategus,
3     from Gellius Serenus and Gemellus Horion and the
4     remaining landholders and public farmers of the village
5     Kerkesoukha. Before all else, most excellent administrator,
6     the governors according to circumstances, taking care of
7     the land, write concerning the construction of embankments and canals,
8     [their] completion to be accomplished. And we, wishing to
9     and always most eagerly to complete the works
10    pertaining to the land, the field-supervisors of the Argaitid basin
11    of the present 19th year, I know not how, whether through negligence
12    did not bring the timber passed down yearly through
13    them for the reconstruction of the thorny structure around the same village called Kormos; nor did they accomplish the work, as if ... suspicion the ditch to make the difference. The land being in danger through this
14    of remaining unwatered and [to] damage the most sacred treasury,
15    the public revenues measured for these things, not [being] in
16    small myriads [of drachmas], of the most sacred Nile, most eagerly having favored himself toward us for good, we ask that if it seem good to your most kind fortune
17    order through your strength the work
18    to be accomplished, [so that] we might be able [we] the sowing
19    about to happen for good, blamelessly to increase for the most [part] and to the most sacred treasury nothing
20    to be lost.
