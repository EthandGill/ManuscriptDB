[TRANSLATION]
1     Pikos and partners, receivers of chaff for the metropolis of
2     year 1 of Antoninus the lord, (to) Bassos
3     (son of) Psenamounis and partners. We have from you,
4     for brick-haulage(?), one and a half loads of chaff, and for
5     the mainland(?), in the name of Theon (son of) Bassos, of a load,
6     a quarter, total ¼ load. Year 5 of Antoninus
7     the lord, Thoth 14. Horos, I have signed.
