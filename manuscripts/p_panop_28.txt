[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Gregorius, the most distinguished prefect,
2     from Aurelia Senmoros daughter of Sansnos, through me Aurelius Sansnos son of Besas, her father,
3     who also holds her under his authority, from the city of Panopolis. Twelve years ago, as it happens,
4     my lord prefect, she was given in marriage as a virgin to a certain Paniskos son of …outhes from the same
5     city, by whom she had a child, and a son survives to her. Then she was cast out
6     a year ago, and until today she is supported by me together with her son. Moreover, her husband also pledged
7     my securities, those given to her by me in the character of a dowry at the time of the marriage, to a certain
8     creditor, and until today he has not recovered them. Later, after a long time, he tore away the child. Therefore I have been led to beseech your kindness, my lord prefect, through
9     this my petition, asking you to order, through your command, whomever your greatness may approve, to compel this man to redeem her securities and have them restored to me, and that I not be troubled concerning his debt, so that out of your ordinance,
10    [being able to recover my own property] and to be supported from these, I may acknowledge thanks to you. Farewell.
11    [In the consulship of our masters] Constantine Augustus for the 8th time and Constantine the most illustrious
12    [Caesar for the 4th time], Epeiph 15. I Aurelius Sansnos the aforesaid have submitted this as aforesaid. I Aurelius Hierakion wrote on his behalf.
