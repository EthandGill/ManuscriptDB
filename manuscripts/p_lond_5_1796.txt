[TRANSLATION]
1     Thus … on …
2     … p…os … yse … sen … lei …
3     … ti … of the now-expiring … indiction …
4     furnishing … of seed … this … according to your
5     order, of one year's crop of the same arouras, namely on condition that I plough
6     and re-plough the aforesaid arouras by means of my own animals, without
7     wages, as is aforewritten, apart from the present ploughing which is also … of arouras, and that I water the same arouras by means of my own animals, without wages, likewise as is aforewritten, whenever you should wish, until the completion of the aforewritten year. However, having received from you
8     on behalf of the rent of these, twelve gold nomismatia in accordance with the
9     lease made to me by you, and from here I have received from you
10    two gold nomismatia from the aforesaid rent of the twelve nomismatia,
11    remaining … to you, ten gold nomismatia …
12    Nonetheless I am to furnish you my two camels with their cameleer for twelve days for work and … of the same arouras
13    without wages. The agreement is valid and firm, and being formally questioned I acknowledged (it). Namely, the beginning …
14    of the work of the same arouras (is) from the new-moon of the following month Mesore …
15    Phoibammon son of Pebes … on, the aforesaid, set down this agreement as is aforesaid. Aur(elius) … es
16    son of Isakios, having been requested, I wrote [on his behalf], he not knowing letters. Aurelius Joannes son of Basileides, from Hermoupolis,
17    I bear witness to the agreement, having heard (it) from those who set it down. Aurelius Biktor son of Taurinos, [from] Hermoupolis,
18    I bear witness to the agreement, having heard (it) from those who set it down.
19    [Through me] … it was written through Josephios.
