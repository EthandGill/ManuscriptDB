r.5   το σο ἀδελφ[ῷ] περὶ τῶν ποταμιτο͂ν δήλοσον , ἐπιδὴ οὐκ ἔμαθεν
r.6   τὸ πρᾶγμα.

[TRANSLATION]
1   † As your Excellence has written concerning the canal, it has been written to Pousis son of Ella
2   to join (forces) with those from Syron(?), until they make it; for whom also the letters
3   I have sent. Deem it worthy, then, to have Pousis make haste, until
4   they make it; and as for the matter of the two gold pieces, about which it has been written
5   to your brother concerning the rivermen, make (it) known, since he did not learn
6   the matter.
