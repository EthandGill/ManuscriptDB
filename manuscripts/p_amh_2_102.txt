[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twentieth year of the Imperatores Caesares Marcus
2     Aurelius Antoninus and Lucius Aurelius
3     Commodus, the Augusti, conquerors of the Armenians, the Medes,
4     the Parthians, the Germans, the Sarmatians, the Greatest,
5     in the month Dystros, Tybi 30, in Ptolemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges Thases
6     daughter of Anchophis, about 35 years old, with a distinguishing mark on the right eye,
7     acting with her guardian her husband Stotoetis son of Stotoetis, about 50 years old, a scar on the right forearm, to Stotoetis son of Anchophis, about thir[ty]-two years old, a scar on the left cheek — all from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos — that she, Thases, has sold to Stotoetis,
8     from the camels which she has in the registration in the village, six
9     camels, one camel, and that she has received the price,
10    of silver, … hundred drachmas, the guarantee
11    following for her, [and] that there is a necessity for Stotoetis to register (it) in the registration of the livestock.
12    I, Stotoetis, have been registered as guardian.
13    Thases daughter of Anchophis, acting with my guardian my husband Stotoetis son of Stotoetis, have sold and have received from Stotoetis son of Anchophis drachmas … hundred as is set forth above. Tar…s son of D… [wro]te on behalf of her, Thases,
14    being illiterate, since he himself, Stotoetis, writes slowly.
15    … In the year 20, Tybi 30.
