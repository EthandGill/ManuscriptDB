[TRANSLATION]
1     Cyriacus to Theon, greetings. Give to Hermogenes
2     and to others horse-keepers four and to Cyril
3     driver and to Ammon, horse-pursuer
4     for six days wine jars eleven, jars eleven only. Pharmouthi one
5     until six. Cyriacus.
