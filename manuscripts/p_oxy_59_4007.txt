[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammon the boy arrived here bringing twenty-five artabas
2     of grain by the measure of lord Pamouthios. [He] reported to me that
3     the two [items], some … that were agreed to me by your
4     magnificence were demanded for the [space] … and your magnificence did well
5     in allowing them [space] … to have more beyond
6     last year [by] thirty carats …, and, God alone knows,
7     if I had been [in possession] according to my patrimony, I could not [give] preliminary [payment for] the
8     one karat. But tell Apollo the boy that [for] the [remainder]
9     of the barley send to me, since, God knows, the lord of all,
10    if it happens [for me] to come, [I] will demand from him four-fold. and furthermore with good
11    [terms,] give or send me the equivalent in the account of the grain so that understanding [I] may write to you
12    to give to Theodore and to whomever …
13    give [this] to Cyriacus the most magnificent manager from Julius.
