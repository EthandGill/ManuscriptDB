[TRANSLATION]
1     To God.
2     Learn, master father, that behold
3     I have agreed with Theodosios
4     about the coin; [I owe] jars 57.
5     If you wish, give to him what you wish
6     and make your agreements with him, in order that he may care for the wine.
7     and look concerning the donkey, that the
8     [parts] beneath his foot pain;
9     bring the doctor and make it [well]
