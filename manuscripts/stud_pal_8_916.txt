[TRANSLATION]
1     To Kyrikas, deacon: provide to Menas, [shepherd]
2     of the village of Psineuris, for wages, indiction 6, of oil twenty
3     four only; it was written in the month of Tubi the 21st, indiction 6, by me
4     Petterios, deacon and notary, it was completed.
