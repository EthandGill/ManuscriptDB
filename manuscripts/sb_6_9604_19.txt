[TRANSLATION]
1     Antiochos Kassianus and Arrius Publianus
2     and Julius Maximus and those with them, overseers of the sacred
3     gate of Syene, through Phanophes the assistant. Harpaesis
4     son of Phanophes, mother Tachomtbekis, on account of the capitation tax
5     year fifteen of Hadrian the lord, seventeen drachmas [and] a half-obol for prison-keeping [tax], a half-obol,
6     amounting to seventeen drachmas [and] an obol, and on account of distribution of per-capita [tax] … of the
7     year fourteen, one drachma, one obol, two chalkoi; year sixteen of Hadrian
8     the lord, Athyr, the twenty-fifth.
