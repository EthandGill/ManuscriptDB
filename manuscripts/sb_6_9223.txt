[TRANSLATION]
1     of the Oxyrhynchite district.
2     Ammonius son of Ammonius, helmsman of a public boat whose official mark [is] a…s, by the command of Lucius Uklatius, soldier
3     of legion 22, cohort 2, century of Maximus Stoltius, and Hermias son of Petal, helmsman of another boat
4     whose official mark [is] Egypt, by the command of Lucius Castricius, soldier of legion 22, cohort 4, century
5     of Titus Pompeius. There is [a sample] which we have deposited from the produce of the 28th year of Caesar, the one [from] Ammonius into a sluice-gate
6     of wheat, artabas 543 and 1/4; the one [from] Hermias likewise of wheat, artabas 543 and 1/4; [there] are all those [that have been] deposited through Leonidas and Apollonius
7     of grain-collectors of the east wind
8     of the division of the lower toparchy, wheat, artabas 486 and 1/2; and we have additionally measured with the hundred artabas of wheat, artaba 1/2; and the deposit we have made from the 2nd of Hathyr until the 4th of the same month; and we have sealed together with the seal of both, the one of Ammonius
9     whose [image is] of Ammon, and the one of Hermius whose [image is] of Harpocrates. In the 29th year of Caesar, Hathyr 4.
10    Hermias and Amonis [sealed] the [samples]. In the year [29] of Caesar, Hathyr 19.
