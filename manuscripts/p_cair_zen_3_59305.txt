[TRANSLATION]
1     Paramonos to Zenon greetings. It would be well if you are in good health; I myself am also in good health. We wrote to you also before concerning the thirty drachmae: send to me either a bedding or the money,
2     you would do me a favor having sent a bedding; and, if you expend anything, to Epharm[ostos] I will set right
3     or to whomever you [may] write. And concerning the sacrificial animal make an effort so that I have for the festival one worthy of you and
4     Epharm[ostos].
5     Be well. Year 36, Panemos 4.
6     Paramonos to Zenon.
