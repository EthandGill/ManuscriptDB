[TRANSLATION]
1     … metretes
2     … you have loaned [to] me metretes six.
3     … [on behalf of the] general one metrete, and on behalf of He[ra…] Athenaeus two metretes, and on behalf of the temple two metretes,
4     total five metretes. [There] remains one metrete. Write to me where
5     the other metrete is, and do not [do] otherwise
6     [do]. And concerning whatever of anyone, if you should use
7     write to me and [I] will do [it] most gladly. Be well.
8     of year 3 of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
9     Germanicus Imperator, Payni 21.
