[TRANSLATION]
1   [Claudius Ter]entianus to Claudius Tiberianus, his father,
2   very many greetings.
3   [Before] all things I pray for your health, and I myself am well too.
4   [I] make your obeisance every day before the
5   [gods here]. I want you to know, my lord father, that until
6   today, from when you left me, I have been attending upon Aemilius,
7   asking an account about those matters, and he has brought me a counter-plea,
8   saying that the bonds (cheirographa) of that man are dissimilar
9   and cannot be registered without the archidikas-
10   [tes ...] they were written. So I gave him
11   [...] them another thirty-two drachmas,
12   [...] saying that 'if you condemn me, either you or your father,'
13   [...] 'recover from me what you gave me.' For I
14   [gave another ...] my … a memorandum to the archidi-
15   [kastes,] through which he gave [...] a memorandum. So I hope quickly
16   [from him] to recover them completed, and to send (them) off
17   [...] you deemed me worthy …
