[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master
2     Jesus Christ, our God and Savior,
3     Mesore 13, at the beginning of the 13th indiction, at Arsinoe.
4     Aurelius Menas the painter,
5     son of the blessed Phoibammon,
6     from the city of the Arsinoites, from
7     the quarter of Therapeia, subscribing below in his own hand, to Aurelius Amaios
8     the collector, son of Kyrikos, from the
9     same city, greeting. I acknowledge that I have had
10    and received and been paid
11    by you through (your) hand henceforth already
12    the price of the house previously sold to you by me,
13    situated in the same quarter,
14    according to the force of the sale made to you by me,
15    which (sale) is also valid and secure
16    and has its own credit
17    and force in full and in entirety; and that for the future no one has any claim, neither I nor
18    my heirs nor any other
19    person on my behalf, against you
20    or against your heirs,
21    concerning the said and sold
22    house, sold to you by me, according to the force
23    of the same sale, nor concerning
24    any other price of it at all,
25    because I have henceforth already been paid
26    by you, as has been said. Valid
27    is this quittance, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged.
28    Aurelius Mena[s], son of the
29    blessed Phoibammon, the
30    aforesaid, it is agreeable to me, this quittance, as is stated above, and having subscribed in my own hand I released (it).
31    Through me, Phib…
32    A discharging quittance made by Aurelius Menas the painter, son of Phoibammon,
33    to Aurelius Amaios the collector.
