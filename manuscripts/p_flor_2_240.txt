1     Suchammon to Heroninus
2     to the dearest [friend], greetings
3     as the most worthy dignified [man] in Talei of …
4     during the vintage sent for me
5     and instructions I received to send to you
6     wine 120 chous, from which I have sent
7     to you on account via the master's
8     beasts four thirty-two chous, but via the transportable
9     donkeys eight other sixty-four chous of wine, [total] makes 96 chous
10    send me therefore a document
11    of the receipt as the most worthy dignified [man] wished, you will give the freight charges for the transportable
12    donkeys 8 the freightage. I shall arrive
13    then today at Talei to the
14    dignified [man]. I wish you to be well
15    I wish you [well], dearest. year 3, Thoth 9
