[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consul]ship of Flavius Modestus, the most illustrious prefect of the sacred
2     praetorium, and of Flavius Arintheus, the most illustrious, Thoth … .
3     To the Aurelii Dioskoros son of Eulogios and Petros son of Agathinos
4     and Agathinos son of Didymos and Sarapion son of Ploutarchos
5     and Hierax son of Serenos and Timagenes son of Serenos, all
6     councillors of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, supervisors of the grain of Alexandria,
7     from Aurelius Elias son of Thonios, from the Oxyrhynchite,
8     pilot of the ship of Theon, of the annonary rank
9     of the most illustrious dux: in accordance with the bond which I made, of which I undertook a cargo of grain of the fortunate
10    first new indiction from the same Oxyrhynchite
11    [nome, I acknowledge,] swearing the august
12    divine oath of our masters
13    Valentinian and Valens and Gratian
14    the eternal Augusti, that I have received the
15    barley-measurement from the landowners who loaded it,
16    on condition that, if it should happen — which may not be — that some disturbance
17    should occur, or any other foul-play, by the
18    greater authority on the pretext of the barley-measurement of the
19    same cargo, I the pilot shall undergo
20    whatever befalls concerning this, so that I be blamed in nothing, or may I be liable to the divine oath.
21    I, Aurelius Elias son of Thonios, have sworn the divine oath
22    and have received the barley-measurement as stated above.
23    I, Aurelius Thonios son of Ammonios, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters.
