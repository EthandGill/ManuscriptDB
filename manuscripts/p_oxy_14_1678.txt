[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lady mother, Theon, greeting.
2     Before all things I pray that you are whole
3     and in health before the lord God. Do not think, my lady mother, that I neglec[ted] not to go up [t]o the Oxyrhynchite. [Y]ou know that, if it [mi]sses the prime season, after that I see
4     no other hope. So you will do everything, my lady mother, not to neglect your daughter. For my brother has thrown off
5     the colt. You must look after it,
6     and I was not able to go up.
7     But if it seems good to you for me to go up, write to me
8     and I come. And if it seems good to you to go off to the
9     most splendid Alexandria, write
10    to me, and whatever seems good to you, what to bring, whether purple-(cloth),
11    write to me, of what kind you want me to bring, write
12    to me; whether oil, how much I should bring, write
13    to me; whether[ anything els]e you wish, [wri]te to me. I gr[ee]t m[y sister]s and my father Barbarion [and] my mother Herakleia
14    and Euterpe and her children and Annis and her children and Eisis and Eutyche and my mother Sophronis
15    and Neilos and Pymenis and our father
16    Choous and our mother and
17    Aphous and his brother and his sisters. I pray you are in health and whole.
18    Deliver from Theon. Direction: in Teumenoutis in the
19    lane
20    opposite the well.
