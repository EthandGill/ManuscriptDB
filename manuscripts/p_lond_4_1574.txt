[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name] of the Father [and the Son and the Ho]ly Spirit, the holy, life-giving and consubstantial Trinity in unity. Written in the month Epeiph 24 of the ninth indiction.
2     [We], the representatives of the men of Tjkoou, whether those in Tjk[oo]u or [those in]
3     [Babyl]on, write to the public treasury, that is to our lord all-praiseworthy …
4     … through you, you, the most glorious lord, the master Basileios,
5     [by the will of] God the illustris and the pagarch, greeting. [That] we acknowledge …
6     … one account of the men of this single village of Tjko[ou], its boundary …
7     … [the] hamlet and the men of the fields, sixty(?) and four solidi …
8     … you are to pay over thirty solidi for it, with the others(?) being solidi which …
9     … 60, and the other forty-[four] solidi, no land, taking three [solidi]
10    … and three of the fields take the other forty solidi …
11    … of the sixty and four solidi according to the force of the account …
12    … having made a partition …
13    to make clear concerning it, from above …
14    to sue you concerning this matter for ever …
15    whether those of the north or those of the south, because we are the men of Babylon
16    … to make our own person, on account of … to make the person
17    … [the men] of Babylon and we … the men of Tjkoou
18    … [our] own person …
19    … we will not be persuaded …
20    [An ack]nowledgement made by various persons from Aphrodito on account of …
21    of the men brought into the … into 30 solidi …
