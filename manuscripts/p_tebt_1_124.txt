[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolemy and Queen Cleopatra the sister and Queen ][Cl]eopatra the wife, the Benefactor gods, greeting.
2     … the catoec[ic ][cavalr]y who are in the Arsinoite nome. The …
3     … from the beginning … we have … goodwill toward affairs … and, following the loyalty of our forebears, we did not think it right to enumerate (our services), being well aware that far
4     rather for the increase … the property …
5     catoecic (holdings) and the acts of benevolence, for which we hold you in the greatest gratitude,
6     and … devoting ourselves more … to every … thing that is expedient,
7     we did not cease, having fallen in … from you the men appointed … that it be discussed with them
8     … allotments … by you, O king, and having received … of those registered to those from the … land … of silver, four drachmas, with also
9     … likewise, and the (dues) of those … who had been enumerated, the existing …
10    … and this, that they had brought in golden crowns and wheat, and after that
11    … these matters, that they called upon us of the …
12    … in the goodwill which we hold from the beginning
13    doing good service and afflicting ourselves on behalf of … us
14    of wheat … of the produce of the 48th year we ask … thus
15    also to Eirenaios the accountant and to the others …
16    of the kinds we ask … and to Ptolemaios the … and
17    to make report, so that in these things [we may hold toward you the] greatest gratitude.
18    Farewell.
19    That there be released those … the … of established indictments, charges,
20    errors, offences, condemnations, all accusations, up to the 53rd year.
21    That the allotments which they hold, of whatever kind they are, remain securely to us and to our descendants
22    in whatever manner, up to the 52nd year, free from malicious prosecution and undisturbed
23    from every charge.
24    Likewise also that those who in whatever manner have been brought to seizure
25    or have become involved in defaults or additional reckonings hold (them) securely in the same way.
26    And since some, having grown weak … being in straits, have ceded (their holdings) to the …,
27    and others have exchanged the allotment, and some have divided (it) with their fellow-soldiers,
28    and others from private-owners and from other categories have transferred into the catoecic class according to
29    the ordinances, that these too and their descendants remain (in possession) for all time, and that
30    those who administer be not carried away among the cleruchs (assessed) by rank …
31    nor that the extra charges [and] levies made from time to time be exacted, [in accordance]
32    with the previous acts of benevolence.
33    Likewise also that those hitherto registered on the cleruchy through the successive
34    epistatai and our scribes according to the ordinances, [both from private-owners]
35    and other categories, who have transferred into the catoecic class, be not distracted into [ot]her
36    liturgies except the catoecic ones.
37    And since … it was decided in the 44th year, in Pachon, that something of the revenue be measured out in addition … half of the wheat, of that which will be flooded from the produce of the same year,
38    and that of that which will be unwatered in the following year the exaction be made …
39    and of the … land the exaction be made from the 48th year, the half-artaba, and toward
40    the busi[ness] the things decided from this henceforth (be treated) otherwise.
