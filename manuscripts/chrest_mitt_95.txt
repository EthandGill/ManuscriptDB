[TRANSLATION]
1     On behalf of Sallu[stia …] …
2     and of Horion the steward. She has acquired much land in the Her[mo]polite nome, and as much as …
3     when they come together they may demonstrate that they themselves possess (it). She needs the … and many of the … some guardian[ship]s, who will look after her. For the woman is incapable, on account of the weakness of her nature, …
4     it completes. You therefore, my lord, even formerly ordered that none of her agents should in any way be molested, since …
5     in no way having the helpers, however many the size of the estate requires. The former president, then, plotted against Pet[er]m[uthis, also called Dius, her] helper, who is neither a councillor nor established as one of councillor descent. For this reason that man too asks of your virtue to be released … [according to]
6     the things formerly declared by you, that he be in no way harassed.
7     In what did the president plot against him? He replied: he wished him to be[come] supervisor of barley.
8     A non-councillor? He replied: a non-councillor.
9     He will not be able to. He replied: order the remaining helpers to have immunity from harassment.
10    If any wish to molest the cultivators or the helpers concerning the administration, …
11    but first they shall petition me. For by drawing some away from the necessary administration they will cause themselves to be fined …
12    of dues. Provide therefore that another be named in place of Dius and that the molestation against that man be stopped.
13    if he happens to be of farming … or otherwise is established as a public person and … [toward]
14    he has been named to an office. For the non-councillor cannot undertake such a liturgy. Land
15    he has long cultivated, having received from his father land free of encumbrance or otherwise inherited it; since therefore also from the boundary-markers …
16    and even today he contributes on her behalf; for this reason he asks of your virtue both to take over the land and the dues …
17    Which judge does he want? [P:] the praepositus of the pagus who also collects the public (taxes).
18    The praepositus of the pagus shall decide between them concerning the justice …
