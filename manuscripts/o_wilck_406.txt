1   Pamonthes son of Hermias paid, for
2   dike-tax of year 13, six drachmas, four obols, and bath-tax 4½ obols,
3   total 7 dr. 2½ ob., and the surcharges on these. Year 14
4   of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus
5   Germanicus Imperator, Phaophi 21.
6   Kephalos, I have signed.
