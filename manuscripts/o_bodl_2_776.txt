[TRANSLATION]
1     Lucanus and associates, collectors of the division of customs of Charakos
2     To Psenmouthes, [son] of Petosirius, [of] Psentkerebi
3     We received for the division, year 11, two drachmas, [they] become 2 drachmas
4     Year 12 of Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius
5     Hadrian Antoninus Augustus Pious, Phao­phi 30
6     Lucanus [I] have signed. Apathes [I] have signed
