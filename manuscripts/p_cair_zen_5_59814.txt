r.2    τοῦ χόρτου ἃς γέγραφεν Ἀπολλώνιος μετρῆσαί σοι διαγράψηι παρὰ τοῦ

r.7    ἵνα τὴν τιμὴν ἀποστείλωμεν καὶ λαβόντες ἀποχρήσησθε.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Zotilos to Panaktor greetings. We have written to Kraton in order that he measure out to you the 100 artabas of the seed
2     of the fodder which Apollonios has written [to] measure out to you [and] he will strike [from] the
3     grain-keeper. Send therefore someone to [him] who having received the token will measure out.
4     As for vetch, in the nome there is none at all, nevertheless we have written to our [people]
5     to search whether it exists anywhere among private [persons], so that you might obtain [some]. And you too having sent [someone]
6     into the Memphite [district] search and if it is anywhere being sold send word to us,
7     in order that we may send the price and having received [it] you will use it.
8     Farewell. Year 29, Mesore 12.
9     Year 29, Mesore 16. To Panaktor [regarding] fodder seed.
10    Zotilos [regarding] fodder seed.
