1     Year 28, Choiach 9. It has been assigned to the bank in Hermouthis, on which [is] Hermophilus, [as] twentieth of the encyclion according to the [agreement] of Apollonius and
2     Ptolemy, of those concerning the sale, [in] the record,
3     under which Ammonius the counterscribe writes, Aganouphis Psemminus of the constructed house and the adjoining courtyard in that [area] from the south of the Memnonia,

5     which he purchased from Zmanreus son of Psemnius [for] 2 talents, [as the] tax [of copper], of which [the] exchange [is] 600; [making] 600.
