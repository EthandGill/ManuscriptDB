[TRANSLATION]
1     Thonios to Alexandros and Antinoos, the
2     …, very many greetings.
3     I received your letter through which
4     you made known, first, that you had learned of the
5     prefect's visit, perhaps from what had been
6     written, and secondly also
7     … that such a thing … had been done. The things, then,
8     … ordered either through memoranda or from imperial ordinances
9     … with you, so that thus we may proceed by way of analogy; for I too
10    found similar memoranda in the Pharbaithite (nome)
11    of the high priest, how he released a priest of the Koreion there
12    from the liturgies,
13    condemning the then phylarch to a fine for having wrongly nominated him.
14    Whenever, then, it seems good to you, write to me
15    and I will send you the copies (of the similar cases).
16    And this one thing too I remind you of,
17    that so much time has elapsed
18    … you … …
19    … …
20    … he has imposed an additional charge
21    in your name on account of tax;
22    and yet often Hermon has claimed
23    to me, saying that "Your brother,
24    having been confirmed in the office, has paid nothing
25    up to today," and I daily accuse him. And this other thing too I foretell to you and make plain: the
26    tabularius of the office has leased an estate in the upper Hermopolite (nome) and now wishes to depart for those parts;
27    for he has already received leave (commeatus) from the
28    procurator, of two months …
29    and the procurator ordered him to take
30    the assessment-lists and to make the demand. Certainly
31    then he is going up to the Oxyrhynchite (nome)
32    to his colleague-assessor; for while the assessment-list was being written I made inquiry concerning you
33    so that he might not assess you, and the account concerning you
34    up to today, for as long as I attend on the procurator, I have made and am making, [and]
35    … so that I may … , since Serenos too was changed from the office
36    … the assessment … of the lease … …
37    … I pray for your health.
38    To Alexandros, priest of the Thesmophoreion, from
39    Thonios his friend.
