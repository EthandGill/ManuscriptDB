[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of [our] masters [Constantine and Licinius]
2     the Augusti for the 4th time …
3     To Aurelia Eutropia, daughter of [Th]eodoros also called Chair[emo]n, ex-gymnasiarch, ex-prytanis, former councillor of the splendid and most splendid (city) of the Oxyrhynchites
4     city,
5     from Aurelius Dioskoros son of Ammonios, from the same [city.]
6     Willingly I undertake to lease for only [the]
7     present 10 [a]nd 8 year, from the property belonging to you around the I[seion]
8     of Panga, in the embankment-district of Pekty, in the place called Telke,
9     out of seventeen arouras held jointly with Panarous, namely the f[ourth]
10    and eighth share, which is six arouras and a quarter and an eighth [by survey], for sowing of flax-stalk, and to pay as rent for the h[alf]
11    of each [aroura] at the rate of four talents of silver, and instead of [the re]nt for [the remaining] half (I shall give) the half share of the produce
12    … of the fla[x-stal]k, and I the lessee, in return [for what I provide]
13    of seed[s] and the cultivation which I perform, (shall have) the remaining half [share]
14    and entire[ly t]he linseed; all (this) being free of all risk [of every risk,]
15    the public dues of [the la]nd being borne by you the landlady, who are mistress
16    of the cro[ps] until you recover the [debts] owed; and the undertaking being guaranteed [to me, I shall of] necessity pay the rents
17    and the dues, the money rent in the month Pauni, and the
18    re[nt of the] flax-stalk on the marsh when it has been soaked
19    … of the present year without delay, the right of execution belonging to you
20    against me, as is fitting. The undertaking is valid
21    and on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
22    In the consulship aforesaid, Hathyr 1 …
23    Aurelia Eutropion, through me Ptol…
24    I have received the copy of this.
