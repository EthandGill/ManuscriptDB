[TRANSLATION]
1     worth at drachmas pē …
2     and two sacks, from which
3     one indeed new drachmas A-psi,
4     another indeed half-worn drachmas O,
5     and a half-sack of hair
6     of drachmas t, total drachmas D-tau-zeta.
7     I present therefore to you
8     the announcement in order that
9     having sought out
10    the ones responsible you send forth against whom
11    [is fitting].
