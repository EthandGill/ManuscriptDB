[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolemaios greetings] Theon of Philippos, [Macedonian] of the succeeding [generation], from Pharbaithes. [Since] there is for us in common … [our] brother … a workman my brother [held] the place partially,
2     [I beseech you, king, if it seems good to you,] order [Diophanes the] strategos to write to Philotas the epistates
3     … to mark the place and the built structures to give to us Philotas … [when] a written contract [is] found … [to draw up] an agreement with each other for us; if not
4     … [illegible] … [shown] to me by Philotas
5     … [may I] obtain.
6     … year 4, Daisios 27, Athur 29.
