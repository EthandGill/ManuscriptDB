1     [As]klepiades, I have noted (it), (year) 3.
2     To Asklepiades, village-scribe (komogrammateus) of Peptaucha and
3     of other villages of the B[e]renike nome,
4     from Niktathymis son of Pap[e]iris son of Niktathymis, of those
5     from the [same vi]lla[ge of Pe]ptaucha. I register,
6     according to the things ordered b[y] Titus Fl[aviu]s Titianus the most excellent
7     prefe[ct, fo]r the house-by-house census of the present
8     16th year of Hadri[anus] Caesar [t]he lord, the [house] belonging to m[e] in the aforesaid village of Peptaucha in which we dwell:
9     Ni[kt]athy[m]is son of Papeir[e]os son of Niktathymis, his mother Apollonia
10    … seiradochim, of middling (stature), honey-colored, a scar on the left thumb, 71 years old,
11    … ris … in … (son of?) Aspiesies, his wife Klo(dia?)
12    … 60 years old,
13    [Nikta]th[y]mis his son from both (parents), priest, honey-colored, without distinguishing mark, 40 years old,
14    … s Amounis son of Archidemos, his mother Ta … se …
15    … (daughter of?) Paesis, his wife Klod(ia?) … 65 years old.
16    [And I sw]e[a]r by the fortune of the Imperator Caesar
17    [Trajan]us Hadrianus Augustus that the
18    [things w]ritten above are true.
19    Year 16 of the [Imp]erator Caesar Trajanus Hadrianus
20    Augus[tu]s, Epeiph 6.
21    Niktathymis son of Papeiris, I have submitted (this) and
22    have sworn the aforewritten oath as
23    is aforesaid. Petosiris son of Pathenouphis, I wrote
24    [on his behalf, since he sai]d that he does not know letters.
