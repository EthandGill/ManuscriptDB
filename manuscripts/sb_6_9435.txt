[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 9 of the Emperor Caesar
2     Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus Augustus
3     Pious, Mesore 12. Didymus and the [partners] in the lot
4     of grain-collection of Karanis have measured from the produce of the same year wheat, artabas by measure
5     public brushwood, one for Amasis, Pasoxites of Kerkesoucha, inhabitants, one twelfth
6     becomes wheat one artaba one twelfth, and the additionally-measured [things]
