[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Menche]s, village-scribe of Kerkeosiris,
2     [from] Pasis son of [Pe]tesouchos, royal cultivator of those
3     [from] the same (village). Whereas, from earlier times,
4     it being customary that the royal land belonging
5     [to me] around the same village be irrigated
6     through the intervening (channel), through the adjoining (dyke) of Lykos
7     son of Zopyrion and others, of both royal and
8     sacred land, by the royal aqueduct, but in the 2nd year,
9     when I had departed abroad concerning urgent business of Ask[l]epiades my
10    kinsman, the aforewritten Lykos, thinking
11    he had a fitting opportunity, set to and dammed up
12    the parts of the aforesaid aqueduct that were in his own land,
13    so that, as a result of this, I, having missed the proper season for the irrigation of the land,
14    suffered damage amounting to 30 artabas of wheat
15    and, for the cost of other irrigation works,
16    3 talents of copper; concerning which, at the time,
17    taking along both you and Horos the village-head and
18    the elders of the cultivators, I pointed out both the damage and the aqueduct dammed up, by whom too the
19    aforewritten Lykos was adjudged, on his having dug out
20    the parts, to let me irrigate through it in accordance with
21    the customs, as is just. But (Lykos)
22    having not even up to now dug it out
23    but keeping putting it off — on account of which
24    we keep missing, always, year by year, the proper
25    season of irrigation, and the aforesaid damage of both the
26    wheat and the copper accruing to me item by item, which
27    amounts, from the 2nd
28    to the 6th year, to 150 artabas of wheat and 15 talents of copper.
29    For which reason, being enfeebled
30    from the crop not coming up according to expectation,
31    I submit (this) to you in order that concerning each matter
32    you may make a subscription, having appended below
33    also a copy of the memorandum for those to whom it pertains,
34    so that the accused Lykos,
35    being brought forward, may be compelled, before all else,
36    to dig out the aqueduct as it previously was and to pay me the aforesaid
37    damage, the 150 artabas of the wheat and the 15 talents of the copper,
38    so that I, being unhindered, may be able
39    to pay in full the rents of the land, and that nothing
40    [to the k]ing be lost.
41    Farewell.
