[TRANSLATION]
1     … Tybi 4, in the Hadrianeion. In the presence
2     [of Serenos and Petr]onios and Choous and Horion, of all the former eirenarchs,
3     … and Tithoous and Sarapas, attendants, and of Euangelos, assessor,
4     [Didymos said:] … I referred (the matter) to my lord the most eminent prefect of
5     [Egypt,] Fl[a]vius Magnilianus, concerning our property, and he secured
6     [for me my rights;] I recovered therefore our (property). The logistes said: rea[d wh]at
7     [my lo]rd the most eminent prefect of Egypt Flav[ius Magnilianus ordered.]
8     A[nd it was re]ad as follows: Fla[viu]s Magnilianus to the curator of the Oxyrh[ynchite,]
9     greeting. That a removal had been suffered of goods belonging [to himself]
10    by certain persons, Didymos, having submitted a petition to my …
11    of which the copy I have ordered to be appended to these my [letters,]
12    … now … … … …
13    … … see to it … to provide, if he should furnish the proofs of the
14    removal made … before judgment. Farewell.
15    After the reading the logistes said: what do the present rustics say,
16    Serenos and Petronios and Choous and Horion? You have heard
17    [th]e order of [my] lord [the most] eminent prefect of Egypt Flavius Magnilianus
18    [and] the things deposited by Didymos upon the memoranda,
19    that he had been satisfied in respect of his goods; depose therefore you too
20    what you did in these matters; or did Didymos exact from you beyond (measure)?
21    The aforesaid answered: whatever we were adjudged in the imperial (court), we have given;
22    and we were reconciled among ourselves. The lo[gi]st[es] [s]aid: both the statement of Didymos
23    and that of the rustics is entered in the me[m]oranda; for the order
24    of my lord the most eminent prefect ordering that the things truly owed be restored, this has been done according to the things reported,
25    as I said, by both parties upon the memoranda.
