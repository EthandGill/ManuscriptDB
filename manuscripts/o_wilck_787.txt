[TRANSLATION]
1     Alexas and partners, tax-farmers of the vegetable-tax (lachanikon)
2     of year 15 of Domitian Caesar
3     the lord, to Pekysis (son of) Nepheros, greeting.
4     We have received in full the tax of the vegetable-plot which you cultivate,
5     called 'of the Island', of which the lease (is held)
6     from Akos (son of) Pamonthes, by order(?),
7     and we make no claim against you. Year 15
8     of Imperator Caesar
9     Domitian Augustus
10    Germanicus,
11    Tybi 6.
