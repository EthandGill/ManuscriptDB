[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidoros and partners, collectors of the apportionment of customs taxes of Characos
2     Pete Phthoumonthos, second Phthoumonthos. We received for
3     apportionments of others 2 obols and for apportionment of customs taxes of 15th year
4     2 drachmas, making 2 drachmas and two obols. Year 16 of Antoninus Caesar
5     the lord, Pharmuthi 3.
