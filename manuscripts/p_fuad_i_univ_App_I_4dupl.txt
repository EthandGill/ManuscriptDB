[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Arsinoe, the Sibling Gods,
2     year 18, Epeiph 28. Oath which Semtheus son of Teos, Herakleopolite of the labourers, who is also called Herakleodoros, swore and subscribed in his own hand: I swear by king
3     Ptolemy, the son of king Ptolemy, and queen Berenike, and the Sibling
4     Gods, and the Benefactor Gods their parents, and Isis and
5     Sarapis, and all the other native gods and all the goddesses,
6     that I will truly act as agent under Klitarchos, the man from Asklepiades
7     the banker at the collection-office of Koites in Phebichis, and will remit
8     all that falls due into the royal treasury rightly and justly, and whatever bronze I receive from Klitarchos, apart from what I shall withhold, I will restore to the bank in Herakleopolis, unless some expenditure be enjoined which I shall spend on
9     the spot; and I will render the account of all that falls due to Klitarchos, both of the
10    receipt and of the expenditure, and the vouchers for whatever I shall have spent.
11    And if I owe anything in respect of the administration, I will pay it to the royal bank within [5] days,
12    and the execution shall be from me and from all my possessions, and that I will alienate nothing
13    of my possessions, or the contract shall be valid against me, and that I will be manifest to Klitarchos and to those acting for him,
14    outside temple and altar and sacred precinct and every shelter. If I keep the oath,
15    [may it be well with me, but if I forswear myself] may I be liable to impiety.
