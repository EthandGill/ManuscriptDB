[TRANSLATION]
1     I inform your glorious excellency that, while
2     my father of blessed memory was still living, he left
3     my wretched self being of small age, and
4     he chose the solitary life, being one of the public defenders of the Antaiopolite (nome),
5     and from that time he left me to my uncle on my mother's side and
6     my most meager affairs; and afterwards, when I came of age,
7     this same father-in-law of mine joined me to his daughter, and
8     visible property, in keeping with the standing of his substance,
9     by a dowry contract, he granted to me along with his
10    daughter, having set these things apart a long time before;
11    and (the property) came into my possession and ownership, with
12    its revenue; I withdrew homeward together with his daughter.
13    But this same aforementioned father-in-law of mine, Kollouthos
14    son of Biktor son of Ision by name, having fallen into a most grievous disease — I mean inflamed gout —
15    in which he became half-dead and wholly incapable, so that
16    he is in want of necessary food, together with his
17    little children, having no kind of caretaker or
18    guardian for his most meager affairs; it is likely that
19    the very persistent harassment of these (taxes) lies excessively
20    upon him unceasingly, with the insolence and perversity of the
21    public tax-collectors, so that he, before due time,
22    calls upon death, looking to the straits everywhere
23    both of his body, from the most fearful disorder afflicting him,
24    and of his soul, from his suffocating affliction and
25    his starvation; and besides these, from the outward
26    strife of the harassment of the public (collectors), and
27    from his destitution of these things, finally, having considered (it), he was compelled
28    to register and to alienate most of his properties
29    to Petros the most illustrious and admirable scriniarius
30    for a price agreed and approved between them,
31    the aforesaid (Petros), being bound — he having purchased (them) — to pay in
32    the imperial and public tax-contribution falling upon the properties sold to him,
33    without fail, to the
34    public account, year by year, in accordance with the
35    deeds of sale made for him by my father-in-law
36    and by the force of his order, that is, the tax-relief, with the subscription of … touchos.
37    For which (relief) he also gave instruction, while he was still living …
38    to the public account, and … gladly furnished …
39    for six years, a clear time, already elapsed. But just now,
40    I do not know from where, having watched for the opportune moment, the agreement …
41    … having occurred and being most unrelated,
42    the aforesaid admirable man came on, relying on tyrannical power,
43    bearing down upon the most meager and (long) separated
44    dowry-property (separated) for me a long time before,
45    wishing to impose another tax-contribution upon me, to my
46    complete overthrow and want of bread, after which (he had) the tax-relief in due order
47    arranged in writing for my father-in-law, which I also administer, by which
48    he agreed to pay in without fail to the public account, each
49    year, the public imperial taxes of the said properties sold to him,
50    in accordance with the deeds of sale
51    made for him in writing; and since I have no other hope
52    or refuge, after God, the Lord of my
53    soul, I approach your glorious footsteps, rolling
54    and beseeching your surpassing benevolence,
55    which the all-merciful God and our most divine and ecumenical
56    master the emperor granted to this most wretched land
57    and to its inhabitants as a very great gift, since you are well-counselled, in order to
58    check the bitter wrongs of the Thebans, if it should come about
59    that you order, sufficiently, that no advance be made by the aforesaid
60    admirable man against me concerning this, nor
61    any unjust innovation which he plots to add upon me,
62    contrary to what is fitting, out of power, by violence; so that in this
63    I may enjoy your justice and always
64    pray on your behalf to the all-seeing God and the only just
65    judge, who is the father of orphans, that with a rich hand he repay your (kindnesses)
66    to you with ten-thousandfold requitals on behalf of this
67    assistance rendered to me in this matter;
68    concerning which I adjure you by the immortal head, and by our master himself,
69    the emperor, and by your salvation.
