[TRANSLATION]
1     has measured into the public treasury grain of produce of the past 19th year
2     of Aurelius Antoninus and Commodus
3     Caesars the lords through grain-collectors of Libo toparchy
4     [of] Seryphis district, Claros [son] of Didymus having been agoranomos
5     [the] measure artabas eight choinikes 4; totals grain artabas 8 choinikes 4. Diogenes grain-collector, I have noted.
