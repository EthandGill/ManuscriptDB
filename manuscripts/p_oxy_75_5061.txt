# HGV-EN: Aurelius Heras son of Papintos, to Theo—, landowner, greetings. Year 2 of our lord Aurelius Probus Augustus, Hathyr 1. I, Aurelius Silvanus, wrote for him who does not know letters. (O. Pelcer)
