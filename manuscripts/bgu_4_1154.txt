[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos.
2   From Heliodoros son of Heli[od]oros
3   also called Aphrodisios, of the Althaieus deme,
4   and from Didymos and Seuthes, bo-
5   th sons of Didymos, and of
6   Didymos' wife Theodote daughter of
7   Theodoros, with her husband as guardian.
8   Heliodoros agrees that he has received back
9   from Didymos and Seuthes and Theo-
10   dote, by hand from his house, the loans
11   which he had lent them by two
12   agreements made through the same
13   court in the 13th year of Caesar,
14   Phamenoth - the one, six hundred
15   and sixty drachmas with their
16   interest, and the other, one
17   hundred drachmas; and that
18   both agreements are void, and
19   the payment-orders made under them
20   through the bank of Zoilos,
21   together with the (paramone) indicated by the agreement of the hundred dra-
22   chmas - the bond-service (paramone)
23   of the son of Didymos
24   and Theodote, (named) Didymos; and
25   that Heliodoros shall not proceed,
26   nor anyone on his behalf, against Didy-
27   mos and Seuthes and Theodote,
28   neither concerning these matters nor
29   concerning any other debt at all
30   or claim, written
31   or unwritten, from former
32   times up to the present
33   day; nor even against the son
34   Didymos shall he proceed concerning the things
35   relating to the bond-service, in any
36   way; or, apart from the agreed terms remaining valid,
37   he shall be liable,
38   if he transgresses, both to the dama-
39   ges and to the fixed penalty.
40   Year 20 of Caesar, Pachon 9.
41   (2nd hand) Of both, Seuthes and Didymos,
42   and Didymos,
43   and Theodote daughter of Theo[doros],
44   to [Heliodoros]:
45   760 silver [drachmas].
