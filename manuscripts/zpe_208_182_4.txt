[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Agreement of the village of Soknopaiou Nesos. (To) Akas son of […] and Abous son of Aionis, the 2 elders, and Stotoetes son of Herieus and Sinas son of Paoueites, the 2 notables, and Pabous son of Pabous, chief(s) of the thief-catchers of the same village, likewise, (from) Paemis son of Kanis and Petesouchos [...] We acknowledge that we have received from the elders and notables of the same village six artabas of vegetable-seed(?), total 6 art., and I shall repay (them) in the month of Payni of the current 24th year. Year 24, Thoth 25.
