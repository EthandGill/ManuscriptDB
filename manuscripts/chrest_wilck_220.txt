[TRANSLATION]
1     … on …
2     of the same mother, a katoikos who has passed the scrutiny …
3     Theon, another brother, of the mother …
4     … another, Bernike … sister,
5     daughter of a katoikos, … years old.
6     From the iconistic register of the 7th year of the god Trajan, of Tharapeia at Seknebtynis,
7     of kollema 35, a house with two towers and a courtyard, 6 24, the sixth, the fourth and the twentieth part,
8     Dioskoros son of Teboulos son of Teboulos, his mother being Bernike daughter of Teboulos,
9     a katoikos who has passed the scrutiny, 20 years old, in the 6th year 19 years old,
10    Euboulos, brother of the same mother, having passed the scrutiny, a katoikos, 22 years old, in the 6th year 21, without distinguishing mark.
11    Theon, another brother of the same mother, a katoikos who has passed the scrutiny, 14 years old. Females.
12    Apollonarion, sister by the same father and same mother, daughter of a katoikos, 17 years old. Bernike daughter of Teboulos,
13    her mother being Helene, daughter of a katoikos, her mother being the same, 37 years old.
14    From the scrutinies of sons of katoikoi of the 8th year of the god Trajan, the scrutineers having been Sotos and
15    Papos who had served as gymnasiarchs, of kollema 11. Concerning another minor son of a katoikos,
16    taken up from those not scrutinized into those liable to poll-tax and reported by the same
17    royal scribe in order that it be examined … and how … in the village of Trano
18    and the examination having taken place in his presence and from the proofs which he brought forward,
19    it having become evident, it appeared to us that he preserves his rights with respect to the katoikoi
20    and that there ought to be admitted Tharapeia, a fourteen-year-old, of the 7th year of Trajan Caesar the lord.
21    Theon son of Teboulos son of Teboulos, his mother being Bernike, sister of the father. I, Sabeinos, took up
22    the foregoing items as agreeing with those in the registration.
