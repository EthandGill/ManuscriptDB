[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Patron] son of Paulinus
2     [from] Geminus son of Hormous son of Theogeiton, from the quarter of Gymnasion, a Persian of the epigone. I wish to lease
3     [from you for] six years from the present day the goose-pasture belonging
4     [to you in the village] of Tebtynis, fitted out with doors
5     [and locks], together with which I have received on the spot from you one hundred laying hens and seven laying geese and … geese in good condition, as rent for the whole each year of one hundred sixty silver drachmas and, as choice extras, ten hens and three hundred hen's eggs and two fattened geese, you giving
6     me for their feeding each year four artabas of barley
7     by the public measure of Tebtynis; and I will give you
8     at the birthday-festivals two geese in good condition and at the Souchia-festival two geese in good condition, you giving me toward their price
9     [silver] ninety-six drachmas, the duty toward the nomarchy account being paid by you, Patron, under
10    … the duty on the livestock; and the rent
11    I will hand over to you each year in two equal instalments,
12    [Thoth and] Phamenoth, and the choice extras whenever you wish; and after the term I will hand over to you the goose-pasture clean of all uncleanness, together with the existing doors and locks, and the hens worth two hundred drachmas, the laying geese …, the geese in good condition 250.
13    [If] it seems good to lease.
14    Geminus, about 37 years old, with a scar on the left shin.
15    Year 15 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
16    [Hadrian] Antoninus Augustus Pius, Pachon the fourth, 4. Written in copy through
17    [the records-office of Teb]tynis through Kronion the scribe.
