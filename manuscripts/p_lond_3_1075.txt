[TRANSLATION]
1     The letter written by your magnificent and
2     God-protected patrician lordship I received; and even if I do not
3     write, you know that just as I wish to attend to my own health,
4     so too the most magnificent and God-protected
5     yourselves do I wish in all things to attend to; but your son
6     my brother told me certain things, that to his face there said
7     Josephios, your farmer, things which he cannot say
8     before your servants, and indeed I was amazed;
9     for you know, even if I do not write, that it is needful that there be honoured
10    the pagarchs especially in the (local) places. However
11    that I may not use many words, God commanding,
12    tomorrow I depart to do obeisance to you together with
13    my aforesaid brother; and if anything was demanded of
14    your farmers, whether sheep or whether apples …
15    we render up to you here. But you also, masters,
16    ought to write to this your same farmer Josephios
17    that henceforth he not overstep your purpose;
18    for I have been clearly assured that you do not wish him
19    to be so brainless. For if he thus
20    persists, he harms himself. However, master,
21    as I wrote before, if all things should be turned about,
22    nothing whatsoever of the things that grieve the God-protected and
23    most magnificent yourselves comes about. But everything
24    whatsoever was demanded of your farmers we render up
25    to you here, on the credit of your ally
26    through the document as was … . I salute
27    your magnificent [and God]-protected
28    patrician lordship.
