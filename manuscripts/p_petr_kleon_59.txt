[TRANSLATION]
1     Archestratos to Kleon, greeting. If you are well [and everything else is]
2     according to reason with you, much thanks be to the [gods; and we too are]
3     well. I, wishing … to Callid[omos] … I ordered one of those from Ptolemais …
4     to inquire about the contract which [Apol]lonios, the one (in charge of) the stones, [had contracted for]. And when this man reported to me
5     that the contract was profitable and …
6     he is completing the work for him, I undertook [to come to you]
7     so that I might speak to you about these matters, but being detained [by]
8     a certain …tos I was no longer [able] to come to you …
9     in the place myself, but I have written to you. For [it seemed to us (best)] to take you as [ad]viser in the matter and to do noth[ing without you],
10    but to do so as may seem good [to you] when you have deliberated [and]
11    as you may arrange for us. So you would do [well]
12    if you took care both that the counting-stones be drawn (through) [and that a sufficient quantity]
13    be removed from the stone-quarry [for the work]
14    so that it be not delayed, and thus, as also [it has been contracted, the]
15    share being (secured). If, then, you write to me quickly, [I]
16    will press him, both by putting up notices [and] protesting, and I will approach Amadokos, [saying that we]
17    are lightening (the burden on) the king, and …
18    we will take up (the matter) with you concerning these things. … [Farewell.]
19    From Archestratos. To Kleon.
20    It came (i.e. delivered) Phaophi 12.
