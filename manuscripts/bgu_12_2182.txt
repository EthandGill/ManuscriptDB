[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Opportunus the most renowned, Pauni 8, of the 4th indiction.
2     To Taurinos the most pious presbyter of the [cat]holic church
3     of the (city) of the Her[mopolit]ans, son of John of best memory,
4     from Aurelius Ph[oi]bammon son of Pinoution, his mo[th]er being …othea,
5     a leatherworker, from the same city. I acknowledge willingly and of my own accord that I have leased from your piety, for whatever period you wish
6     reckoned from the crops of the present fourth indiction,
7     the whole crop of the acacia-groves belonging to you
8     which are in the place [c]a[ll]ed …ale and in the place called
9     Tarix, of whatever condition they are … for our
10    use, at a rent for these per year of seven gold carats,
11    which gold rent I will pay to your … piety
12    in the month Thoth of each year without delay;
13    the lease is valid and secure, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged it.
14    I, Aurelius Phoibammon son of Pinoution, the aforesaid, have leased
15    as aforesaid. Aurelius Theodotos son of Papnouthios from Hermopolis,
16    having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
17    Aurelius …s son of Pinoution from Hermopolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the contractor. Aurelius John son of Phoibammon from Hermopolis
18    I bear wit[nes]s to the leas[e], having he[ard it from the contract]or.
19    Lease made by Pho[i]bammon son of Pinoutio[n].
