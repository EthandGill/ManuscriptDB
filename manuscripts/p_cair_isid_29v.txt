1     Aurelius Ptollas
2     To Aurelius Isidōros, greetings.
3     I set before you the names
4     which I demanded [to be credited] to the account of the
5     grain [tax]. They are indeed:
6     from Priskos, drachmas 500
7     Isidōros [son] of Papeitos, drachmas 200
8     Arabikos, drachmas 360
9     Pakiāt[os], drachmas 500
10    […]ta, drachmas 200
11    Alexandros, drachmas 500
12    and for my property/estate
