[TRANSLATION]
1     The things written through the most reverend deacon by your God-guarded [brotherhood] I received and …
2     to him the apokrisiarios … his …, having charged h[i]m … of someone, until the common mother;
3     and behold, I made the [com]mon mother release the deacon, and I do not de[la]y, with God's help, to release him to you
4     with what is to be done; and since I gave her my word that I will by all means write to my brother Kyros Papas, in order that
5     your God-guarded brotherhood may settle her affair with her adversary on account of the slaves; behold
6     then, Ananias the pallix has come up; if it please you, release her, for I do not think that she seeks unjustly;
7     a letter [of yo]u[r] G[od]-guarded brotherhood was shown to me to the effect that the affair belongs to Kyros
8     Christophoros; and who ought [to …] if not your God-guarded brotherhood?
9     after I went out from Kontralata, I received other responses of our master the all-praiseworthy amir
10    through four Saracens of the Amir of the Faithful concerning the purchase of many things; and he did not permit
11    of them today; let him taste water, and truly Satan brought upon me …
12    because I was never grieved, nor afflicted, except at the present. Genuine one, I [gre]et [you,] the [God-guarded]
13    and most magnificent, through this letter
14    To my God-guarded master and brother Papas, the greatest comes, citizen-official and pagarch — Platon.
