[TRANSLATION]
1     Koitōnikos to Hērōn: greetings.
2     If you return what you have in deposit,
3     as I told you in person, thus do [it];
4     and demand back my tunics,
5     or place [them] as he says until she buys clothes and you recover mine; hand [them] over to Hermeas when he comes down,
6     until she sells five hundred
7     bundles of hay that is [there],
8     [price]; concerning the carpenters, if
9     they go up, send me word
10    so that I may know; take care therefore that you do not let
11    her be moved until you recover my tunics; for I do not wish to speak of what she has done against me.
12    Farewell. Hadrian[os] 25.
13    ε … δ …
14    Deliver to H[er]on from Koit[onikos]
