[TRANSLATION]
1     Ninnus to Heroninos the
2     dearest, greetings.
3     The things being sent to you
4     from the city through pack-animals of Memphite wine, do not receive, and let them remain with you
5     until he recalls
6     them, the superintendent of Dionysiades.
7     And make a claim through
8     the accounts of yours toward the fortunate one. When having obtained beforehand
9     he recalls them, the superintendent
10    of Dionysiades, receive from him the
11    letters. Be well, I pray, dearest.
12    Through Syrus, who served as cosmete. Year 1, Tybi 29
