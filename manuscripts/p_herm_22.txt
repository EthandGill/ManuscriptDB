[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters Arcadius for the 3rd time
2     and Honorius for the 2nd time, the eternal Augusti.
3     To Aurelia Horigenia daughter of Phibion, from Hermoupolis
4     the most illustrious,
5     from Aurelius Phibion son of Heliodoros, who signs below, and Heron son of Lytios, his mother being Thekla, both from the same city. We wish
6     of our own free will and choice to lease
7     from you for one single year, of the crops
8     of the fortunate ninth ind[iction], the (land) belonging
9     to you in the marsh near the praktoria of [O]phis, an aroura,
10    one and a quarter and a sixteenth and a sixty-fourth,
11    for the sowing of flax-stalk, the rent being for each
12    aroura two hundredweight of uncombed tow,
13    which tow we shall render to you in the
14    month of Phaophi of the same indiction, clean,
15    satisfactory, by the public weight, without delay, the public dues being upon you the
16    landowner. The lease is valid and, having been questioned,
17    I acknowledged it. I, Aurelius Phibion, have leased
18    as set forth above. I, Aurelius Heron son of Lytios, the aforesaid, have leased as set forth above. I, Aurelius Pantagathos, wrote
19    on his behalf, he being illiterate.
20    Through me, Silvanus, it was written.
21    Lease of an aroura, 1 ¼ 1/16 1/64, in the marsh near the [praktoria] of Ophis, of which the lessees are
22    Phibion son of Heliodoros
23    and Heron son of Lytios, from Hermoupolis.
