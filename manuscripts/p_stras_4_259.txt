1     Dios to Panarotis the
2     dearest, greetings.
3     As I commanded you
4     concerning the three [thirds]
5     of arouras which also the allotment I gave you, go
6     for the inspection with
7     the ones pressing toward you, so that either you lease out or whatever [is needed]
8     you do so that not …
9     into uncultivated [become diverted]
10    the land; of which if [need]
11    you have of seeds and
12    of expenses in haste …
13    to send; previously …
14    thus you asked to lease out.
15    I wish [you] to be well.
