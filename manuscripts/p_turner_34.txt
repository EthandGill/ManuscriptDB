[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy.
2     To Vettius Galianus, the most excellent prefect of the desert, acting also in the office of epistrategos,
3     from Aurelius Apollonios also called Sarapion, son of Drakon, of the Althaieus deme, holding land in Diospolis Parva of the Thebaid.
4     You know, greatest of procurators, the aforementioned and divine ordinance,
5     which I have set forth above, annulling the unlawful intent of
6     my father, who left behind out-of-date testaments, but confirming
7     if a son has lawfully and legally acquired his own share, having registered (it)
8     and at the local record-office of properties. So, having lawfully
9     come into possession, and having paid to the most sacred treasury the public dues falling upon me,
10    and the sums owed for the customary rents
11    and the things useful for the sacred grain-supply, so that no debt any longer entangles me, that I may prosper, I well know your virtue that hates wrongdoing
12    and your care for those who are defrauded. For the wife of my father, Oseaus,
13    Philemon by name, after the deification of my father, inasmuch as
14    she received by the unlawful testaments a bequest, at that time having seized possession, as was clear to all, of the household furnishings and contracts and pulse
15    and money, of which with difficulty, by much inquiry, I obtained the itemized account, she alone determined to hold possession of these, undertaking at first
16    to take away the possession of the property and to use them
17    in whatever way she chooses, having sold … and out of them …
18    … according to the aforementioned and divine ordinance, before you …
19    by the fortune of our lord the invincible emperor
20    Antoninus, and to your just right hand for ever, I acknowledge that I am able to give thanks, having received in full from my paternal (property)
21    the lawful portion falling to me, of which there is to sons nothing sweeter.
22    And I have appended also a copy of the testaments.
23    Year 25, Choiak 29.
24    Having given notice to the opposing party, you are able to petition me.
25    Deliver.
26    From Aurelius Apollonios also called Sarapion, son of Drakon.
