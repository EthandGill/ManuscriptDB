1     [In the consuls]hip [of Flavii Eu]sebius and Hypatius of the [most illustrious]
2     … to Ap[o]llonius, who is also called Theodulos
3     to the logistes of Oxyrhynchus
4     from Aurelius Mouses [son] of Theon, from
5     the same city, representative of the tribe Dromos
6     of the Gymnasium and other neighborhoods. I give
7     and I report at my own risk
8     into the region of Haliadites, that is [of the record-bearer] [of] the swift dromos for a year
