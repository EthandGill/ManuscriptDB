[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Year sixteen of the Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, Phaophi 30. Paid through Apynchis and and his associates, collectors of money taxes of Soknopaiou Nesos, (by) Stotoetis, son of Apynchis, and Papeis, son of Sotas, both elders of the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, for the dike tax of year 14 forty dirty silver drachmas, = 40 drachmas, and they (Stotoetis and Papeis) did not avail themselves of another receipt because they say that it has been lost.
