[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion, son of Apollonides
2     To Philammon greetings. Having come, deliver the wine-jars which you sent to us.
3     For we appeared to you speaking as to a stranger.
4     For if we wished to buy provisions apart
5     from [the] drinks being …
6     we would not have troubled you.
7     Know therefore [what is] lying
8     to you the [wine-jars].
9     Farewell.
10    To Philammon
