[TRANSLATION]
1     Euclés to Zenon greetings. You will oblige [me]
2     the twenty jars of vinegar concerning which
3     many times I reminded you, giving
4     to Moschion the boy. And the price
5     as soon as we possibly arrive
6     we will arrange for you. And concerning the small change
7     which you ordered me, they asked us
8     those having the most days
9     to them to delay ten days in which [they reap].
10    therefore as soon as [they deliver]
11    if you have need we shall advance for you.
12    Farewell. Year 6, Thoth 15.
13    Concerning vinegar jars, twenty, to Zenon
14    Euclés, year 6.
