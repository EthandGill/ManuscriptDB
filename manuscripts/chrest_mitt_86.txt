[TRANSLATION]
1     [Copy] of a record of proceedings. From the records of proceedings
2     of … Claudius Philoxenos, archidikastes. Year 19 of the Emperor
3     [Caesar] Trajan Hadrianus Augustus, Phamenoth 28, in Memphis,
4     [at the] tribunal. Tapontos, through her advocate Pasion her husband, against Phanomgeus
5     [and] …theas. Berneikianos the orator having stated that Petheas, the grandfather of the
6     [woman being represented], and father of the opponents, having an eldest son of the same name as himself, the father of this woman, had died,
7     [and that, in accordance with] the laws concerning the two-thirds portion of the property, that man (the grandfather), taking it up into (the name of) the woman being represented, she being a minor, had drawn up deeds-of-transfer of property; but that, when he had died, the opponents,
8     [(being) uncles] of the girl, had thrust themselves into the guardianship and, taking everything over, had neither rendered accounts nor entered a register
9     [of the things] left behind, nor had they supplied maintenance to the girl under guardianship. Dionysios and Harpokration, the orators (for the other side),
10    [having replied] that the father of the (girl who is) the opponent, being a public donkey-driver,
11    [had left] behind many liabilities outstanding, on account of which the persons being represented
12    [had been compelled] to take over the affairs, and that, since they incurred losses, they ask on this account, if the opposing party wishes
13    [to retain] the paternal (property), that acceptance be made of their expenses, while Pasion maintained that the father
14    [of the woman being] represented had not been a donkey-driver: Claudius Philoxenos, neokoros (temple-warden)
15    [of the great] Sarapis, having been prefect of the first cohort
16    [of the Damas]cenes, of those maintained tax-free in the Museum,
17    [priest] and archidikastes (decreed): the strategos of the nome shall investigate
18    [concerning this], and if those around Phanomgeus appear to have, on this
19    [account], taken over the paternal (property) of the woman bringing the appeal,
20    [he shall cause it to be restored] to her, doing what is fitting. I have read (this).
