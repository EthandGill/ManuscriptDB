[TRANSLATION]
1     [Tothes Am]monius the Hermopolite to Horion son of Heracles [from the] same city greetings. I have leased from you for one year from the new moon
2     [of the entering] month of Hadrian of the present [12th] year of Antoninus Caesar [the]
3     [lord your] half-share of a [private] courtyard and of the [room] on the same courtyard one and entrance and exit [leading] under the southern portico [of the] Dacic courtyard for rent
4     [on behalf of the] half-share of both the courtyard and [the] room in full
5     [of one twelve]-month [period] in silver drachmas
6     …, from which I have accepted from you on behalf of the half-share as a price, which I assessed at six drachmas, and the remaining drachmas … I will repay to you in the month of Pauni of the present year. And after the
7     [aforementioned] year I shall restore to you your half-share of the
8     [courtyard and] the key, such as they shall come out from [the] use and most pure
9     [you shall keep me]. Year 12 of the Emperor Caesar
10    [Titus Ael]ius Hadrian Antoninus
11    [Augustus] the Pious, Athur 11. Tothes Am[monius I have leased as [afore]said.
