1     [price] [of barley artabae] 2, as of one drachmas 160, they become drachmas 320,
2     Price of sacks of sippian drachmas 200,
3     To Philoxenos for expenses drachmas 500,
4     For a she-mule drachmas 20,
5     Workers 2 for winnowing drachmas 400,
6     To the donkey barley 1 artaba drachmas 160,
7     To the workers wine
8     1 jar drachmas 160,
9     To Teipolis for expenses drachmas 180,
10    [For] expenses drachmas 100,
11    Price of other barley 4 artabae
12    as of one drachmas 140, they become drachmas 560,
13    For cedar drachmas 200.
14    [They] become altogether drachmas 2366.
