[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy called Alexander and Berenice his sister, gods Philometors,
2     year 15, in the priesthood of priests and priestesses and basket-bearer of those existing and [those] being, month Thouth 5, in Pathyris before Hermias
3     of [the one] beside Paniskos, agoranomos. Petearsmethes Panebchounios of Totoes, Persian of [the]
4     descent, as about 45 years old, olive-skinned, curly-haired, bald, long-faced, [having] a scar on a tooth, from the [existing]
5     to him and to his brothers, a fourth portion of the three seals, one indeed called Harmonthos, neighbors
6     of the whole land south Chesthotos of Harpahesis, north Pathotos of Panemgeutos, southeast Patoos of Phibios and
7     of the brothers, southwest embankment; another called Slepios, neighbors of the whole land south and north Panatos, southeast river,
8     southwest Arahemites; another called Telon, south and north the remainder of the land, southeast road and house, of which three
9     the one falling to me [is] a fourth, of which the boundaries are set forth. Hetpesouchos Nechthanouphis and his brothers [purchased them]
10    for ten talents of copper. The seller and warrantor of all things according to this sale
11    [is] Petearsmethes the seller, whom Hetpesouchos and those with him, the ones buying, the purchasers, received.
12    Hermias the one beside Paniskos [has been] in charge [of proceedings].
