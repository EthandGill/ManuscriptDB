1     To Harpokration, strategos of the Arsinoite, of the divisions of Themistos and
2     Polemon,
3     from Isidoros, village-scribe of Lagis and
4     Trikomia.
5     Man by man, of those cultivating in place of those who have withdrawn and become destitute, for the sowing
6     of the present 3rd year of Antoninus
7     and Verus, the lords Augusti. It is as follows:
8     in place of Protion son of Ammonios, 2 1/2 arouras,
9     I assign Aphrodisios son of Sarapion son of Aphrod(isios);
10    and in place of Papontos son of Pakeis, 22 arouras,
11    I assign Pisais son of Kollouthos and
12    Sarapammon son of Heron, 2 1/2 arouras;
13    Tithoes son of Pontos, 8 1/2 arouras;
14    Chairemon son of Onnophris, 4 1/4 1/16 arouras;
15    Anoubas son of Deios, 3 arouras;
16    Onesimos son of Straton, 2 arouras;
17    Papontos and Panouphis,
18    both sons of Amais, 1 aroura 1/2 1/8 1/16;
19    and in place of Didas son of Sotes, 8 arouras,
20    I assign Pasion son of Charmos son of Areios;
21    and in place of Protion son of Longinus, 5 1/2 arouras,
22    I assign Harpoch(ras?) son of Bassos, 2 1/2 arouras;
23    Peteeus son of Petesouchos, 2 1/2 arouras;
