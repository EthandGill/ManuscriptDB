1     Year 23, Pachon 14.
2     Damon son of Apollonios, for the price
3     of the house of Marsous,
4     1300 drachmas.
5     Year 23, Pachon 14. There has been paid to the bank in Diospolis Magna,
6     over which Hermokrates (presides), to the king on his own account, according to the
7     payment-order from Dionysios the oikonomos, under which Arnouphis the topogrammateus subscribes, the one written in year 23 Pharmouthi 20, in which he made clear that
8     Ptolemaios, the one in charge of affairs in the Thebaid, and Theon
9     the royal scribe, having received (it), had ordered you, at the (bank) in Diospolis Magna
10    that the bank should enter as receipt to the king (the following): Damon son of Apollonios, (for the property) of Ptolemaios
11    son of Pausanias, the price of a house which Arnouphis the topogrammateus had registered, (that) Imouthes the village-scribe had delivered to him (a statement)
12    that it exists in Chrysopolis among the ownerless (properties), which was (the house) of Marasas
13    son of Mageiros, put up for sale in Diospolis Magna in year 23 Choiak 28
14    through Ptolemaios the one in charge of affairs in the Thebaid and Theon the royal scribe,
15    there being present also Dionysios himself and Arnouphis the topogrammateus and Imouthes the village-scribe,
16    Megisthenes the garrison-commander, Lichas the chief of police, Aristogenes of the commanders under Hippalos, Iasibis
17    overseer of the cavalry-squadron, and many others, through Timarchos the herald of the soldiers,
18    and on the 29th and 30th, after it was proclaimed beforehand, the same persons being present, through the same herald,
19    and confirmed on the 1st of Tybi, the same persons being present, and Chorchonsis and Chonophris
20    priests of Zeus, through the same herald, and after the departure of Ptolemaios the one in charge of affairs in the Thebaid
21    and Theon the royal scribe, after it was proclaimed beforehand on the 5th of the same month
22    the customary persons also being present, through the same herald, and no one undertaking (to pay) more, (the house) was confirmed to the one declared (purchaser), on condition that he pay over in 2 years of copper against silver 1300 (drachmas)
23    in the 24th year 650, forthwith 650. And he has paid the whole price
24    forthwith, of copper against silver, one thousand three hundred drachmas, 1300,
25    on condition that he shall be owner of the house just as also the former owners possessed (it).
26    And he has paid also the five-percent tax and the other due charges.
27    Hermokrates the banker. He has paid, as is aforesaid, the
28    of the 23rd and 24th year, the 2 installments, forthwith, of copper against silver, one thousand three hundred
29    1300, and the other due charges.
30    Herakleides son of Ptolemaios, the one in the place of Dionysios the oikonomos. He has paid, as is aforesaid, of copper against
31    silver, one thousand three hundred 1300, and the other due charges.
32    Apollonios the one in the place of Arnouphis the topogrammateus. He has paid the (sums) also entered
33    in Tybi of the 23rd year, of copper against silver, one thousand three hundred 1300.
