[TRANSLATION]
1     Heracleides to Athenodorus the brother, [very many]
2     greetings, and always to be well, as I [pray];
3     I went to Tilothin; the beer-makers did not grind the barley if not [illegible] ... [illegible] them
4     of the stater into Pauni; therefore if it seems good to you
5     signal; but know [that I] have worked upon the barley
6     willingly; for if [you] fail, the whole matter is at a loss; therefore
7     I write to you in order that [you] know; signal then about what you wish
8     and take care of yourself in order that you be well; farewell.
9     year 2[0?] of Caesar, Phamenoth 18
10    to Athenodorus the [administrator]
