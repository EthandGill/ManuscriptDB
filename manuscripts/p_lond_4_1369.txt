[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of God. Qurra son of Sharik, governor,]
2     to Basileios administrator of the village [of Aphrodito. We give thanks to God]
3     and after these things. We have dispatched to you …
4     on account of the making of the boats(?), of iron
5     four hundredweight, for which we have remitted a fee(?) of the
6     distribution at present … on behalf of last year's (account),
7     six pounds per hundredweight; and behold, the division of the same
8     iron we have appended for you in the present letter, making clear
9     what we reckon to each account on account of the deficit. Receiving then
10    the present letter, take over this iron and distribute
11    it through the estates according to the proportion of the
12    tax-demand-notes sent to you by us, not favouring or being prejudiced against anyone
13    in such distribution, but assigning to each estate according to
14    proportion the share falling to it from each account. Instruct
15    them to work this up with all dispatch,
16    made well and fashioned according to the specimen
17    sent to you. Written in the month Hathyr 3, of the 9th indiction.
18    Statement
19    of the division: 4 hundredweight;
20    of the bundle(?): 2 hundredweight, lightened by a third;
21    of the collection(?): 2 hundredweight, thus:
22    1 hundredweight lightened by a third and 1 hundredweight lightened by a fourth;
23    … concerning iron, 4 hundredweight.
