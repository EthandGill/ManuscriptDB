[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters Flavius Valerius
2     Constantinus and Licinianus Licinius, the Augusti, for the 2nd time.
3     To Valerius Heron also called Sarapion, logistes of the Oxyrhynchite,
4     from the guild of the perfume-sellers of the same city,
5     through Isidoros son of Sarapion. In accordance with the orders,
6     at my own valuation I declare the following price of the wares which I handle,
7     and I swear the divine oath that I have not lied. It is as follows:
8     of pepper, 1 pound: 1 talent
9     of frankincense, 1 pound: 200 denarii
10    of malabathron, 1 pound: 2 talents
11    of high-grade storax, 1 pound: 1,250 denarii
12    of light storax, 1 pound: 750 denarii
13    of costus, 1 pound: 1 talent
14    of mastic, 1 pound: …
15    of amomum, 1 pound: … denarii
16    of bdellium, 1 pound: 250 denarii
17    of cassia, 1 pound: 500 denarii
18    of kasamon, 1 pound: 450 denarii
19    of patema, 1 pound: 250 denarii
20    of camel-thorn, 1 pound: 75 denarii
21    of arna…
22    1 pound: 150 denarii
23    In the same consulship, Pachon 30.
24    [Aurelius Is]idoros, I declare as above. I, Aurelius Neilos, wrote on his behalf,
25    he being illiterate.
26    of saseleos, 1 pound: 105 denarii
27    of sphagnion, 1 pound: 75 denarii
28    of eirona, 1 pound: 150 denarii
29    of elenidia, 1 pound: 175 denarii
30    of alkeotides, 1 pound: 75 denarii
31    of onychia, 1 pound: 200 denarii
32    of wood-mastic, 1 pound: 75 denarii
33    of alimastos, 1 pound: 75 denarii
