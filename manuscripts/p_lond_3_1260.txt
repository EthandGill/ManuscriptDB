[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aurelius Hier]ax, procurator of the lower Thebaid, to the strategos of the Hermopolite nome,
2     … greeting.
3     … through the letter by which I informed you, my lord the most eminent rationalis, having been instructed by me, …
4     [Sera]podoros, that it is most necessary that thirty boats of five hundred [artabas each] be built for the sake of the transport of the … , that there be … : therefore take care, as soon as you receive my letter, that in the nome under you, ten boats …
5     … of five hundred artabas capacity be constructed, having collected …
6     … about which it was written to you long ago, and acacia and persea timber, and the workmen
7     [in the ci]ty having gathered, and for the construction having them help …
8     … from the various labor … of bark …
9     … as you were entirely ordered before; and you yourself not being ignorant that overseers, that is, taskmasters, must …
10    … most suitable and most well-off men, at the risk of the council, be appointed for this, and that the expenses, as is advantageous to the treasury, be met from public funds. And so that, when
11    [the boats] have been built, no obstacle may follow regarding [the hauling] of them and their becoming serviceable,
12    let provision now also be made for their fitting-out and for those who are to sail
13    [in them], both sailors and steersmen. And a deadline was set,
14    [of these matters], two months; therefore, using all energy and zeal, apply your mind to each of the things commanded, or, in addition to undergoing forthwith a penalty if you should be caught having been negligent,
15    [you will also] be in danger concerning your very life. [I pray] for your health. In the 20th and 12th year
16    [of our lords] Diocletian and Maximian Augusti [and of Constantius and Maximian the] most illustrious Caesars, Mecheir 1.
