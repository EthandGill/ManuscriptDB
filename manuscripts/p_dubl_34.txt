[TRANSLATION]
1     To my brother Eulogios, a Melitian monk on [the] mountain of Labla, son of Pousi from the village
2     of Theagenis,
3     from Aioulios, a monk of the same nome, son of Aranthios from the village of Psinol of the Arsinoite nome, greeting.
4     I acknowledge that, since I wrote at another time to Isak son of Sabinos concerning my monastery,
5     in whatever way my document may come forth, it is to be invalid, but after my death the monastery
6     is to be Eulogios's, and whatever I have of my own, whether of loss or of gain, is to be
7     Eulogios's. And if I depart from Eulogios while living, my monastery is to be Eulogios's, or (if) I bring any
8     person or monk over (the head of) Eulogios inside into my monastery apart from Eulogios, the monastery is to be Eulogios's.
9     And I for my part acknowledge — I, Eulogios — to my brother Aioulios, that it is not permitted to me
10    to cast you off from me while living until you also die, as is set forth. We bear witness, we the most reverent Apa Hol and Tourbos, presbyters of the holy catholic church, Melitians on [the] mountain of Labla,
11    and Elias, deacon of the same no[m]e; and we the most reverent Anoup and Pamoutios and Sambas, presbyters,
12    orthodox, on [the] mountain of Labla,
13    concerning this document, as is set forth.
14    Written on the intercalary day 1, of the 5th indiction. Through me, Apollo, it was written.
15    Acknowledgement of settlement of Aioulios to Aurelius Eulogios, monks, Me[li]tians on [the] mountain of
16    Labla.
17    concerning this document, as is set forth.
18    Written on the intercalary day 1, of the 5th indiction. Through me, Apollo, it was written.
19    Acknowledgement of settlement of Aioulios to Aurelius Eulogios, monks, Me[li]tians on [the] mountain of
20    Labla.
