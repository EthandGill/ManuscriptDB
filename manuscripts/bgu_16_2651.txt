[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklepiades to Heraklides his brother
2     greetings and [always] to be well as
3     I pray;
4     know that concerning which you sent a letter
5     the toparch gave us no reply
6     about this; but since the toparch himself
7     comes to the city of Herakles with you
8     about the matter do not neglect so that
9     you bring the matter [before him]; but on account of the
10    eye disease I have been prevented from going up
11    to you; and take care of yourself that
12    you be well;
13    Farewell, year 21 of Caesar, Mesore 29
14    To Herakl[ides] his brother
