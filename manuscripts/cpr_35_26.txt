[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Nemesianos son of Kratistos, to the most excellent
2     council of Hermou polis the great, ancient and illustrious and most august, through Marcus Aurelius Corellius
3     Alexander, of equestrian rank, from the army-rolls, gymnasiarch, councillor, prytanis in office of the same
4     city, to the dearest (friends), greeting. I wish
5     to purchase from the civic account a house and the
6     building-plots around it and vacant places in which
7     there are dung-heaps, all being in a state of collapse and now in useless condition, formerly (the property) of Hoplon, in Hermou polis in the quarter of the city
8     (called) "East," under the southern colonnade of the Antinoite Broadway, from which nothing accrues
9     to the civic account because of their having been wholly damaged in the events that befell formerly throughout
10    the city in the accursed disturbances; whose neighbors are: south, the Domitian Broadway, through which
11    is the entrance and exit; north, the Antinoite
12    Broadway, through which likewise is the entrance and exit;
13    east, (the property) of Hermes the pastophoros and partners; west, (the property) of Claudius Dikaiarchos, former magistrate of the illustrious Antinoopolis, having been added to the city in accordance with what was ordered by him of most distinguished memory,
14    Claudius Theodoros, at a price of two thousand silver drachmas, total silver drachmas 2,000; which,
15    on being confirmed (in the purchase), I will pay to the civic account.
16    And there shall remain to me and to my descendants and to those who take over from me
17    the ownership of these,
18    and a valid, secure title, the city guaranteeing it to me always against every claim;
19    but if I be not confirmed, I shall not be held
20    to this offer. I pray for your health,
21    most honored (friends).
22    Year 14 of the Imperator Caesar Publius
23    L[icinn]ius G[a]llienus Germanic[us Maximus]
24    P[ersicus] Maximu[s, Pius, Felix,]
25    A[ugustu]s, Hathyr 16 …
