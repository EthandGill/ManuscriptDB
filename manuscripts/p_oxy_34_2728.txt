[TRANSLATION]
1     Kapitolinos to Sarapammon (his) brother,
2     greeting. Before all things
3     I pray for your good health together with
4     your children and with your wife
5     before the lord god. I marvel
6     how even now, when Horigenes came
7     to me, you made nothing known to me about anything;
8     I know that I burden you much; in part it is renunciation;
9     for among philosophers silence is an answer. Can I too then
10    not answer? But nevertheless I e…
11    writing … m … men … when your letters
12    are brought … asso … ; and I think that
13    it is good for you to write down what he receives and
14    what the man spends; and before this
15    I wrote to you through Didymos item by item, and now I write;
16    and it is this: when they came to Egypt … of Horigenes, 10 spathia of wine as I wrote, having been sold
17    at 1800 drachmas, total 3 talents. And in the month Pachon 17 spathia
18    of old (wine), and in the month Pauni another 3 spathia;
19    and of these 20 spathia were sold at the same rate
20    as all, at 1600 drachmas, total 5 talents 2000 drachmas. And in the month
21    Mesore 6, another 10 spathia at 1700 drachmas, total 2 talents 5000 drachmas.
22    Total of these 40 spathia, 11 talents 1000 drachmas, apart from the balance previously declared to you by me; and now receive through the same Horigenes in the same month
23    Mesore another 10 spathia of wine; let these too be sold
24    at 1700 drachmas; the price of these then send
25    me in a sealed money-bag in small change,
26    for there is need of silver; for of the commission-goods
27    there is deducted for you from the previous balance;
28    and receive through the same (man), for your eating, a box of oil, 1 1/2 chous; if you can, send 2 ounces of pepper and a sambathon of boridia together with
29    the previous commission-goods; send also a half-chous
30    of honey, all at the price. Do not hesitate again
31    to write to me about everything; if oil sells better with you than wine, make it known …
32    all our (people) by name. I pray that you are in health.
