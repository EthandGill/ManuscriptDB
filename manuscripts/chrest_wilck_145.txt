[TRANSLATION]
1     To Hermaiskos … of the Hermopolite nome,
2     from Hermaios son of Horion, his mother being
3     Demetria also called Tereus, of those who in the
4     5th year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus have served as ephebes.
5     My son Eudaimon the younger,
6     his mother being Eudaimonis, a Hermopolite woman,
7     from his father Ploutogenes also called Tithoes,
8     son of Metrodoros, having reached the age for
9     enrollment among the ephebes, I request that the son be admitted among those who in the 7th year of Nero
10    Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator
11    are being enrolled as ephebes, he being on the side-list
12    … and of Epimachos, both sons of Pytheas
13    son of Pytheas, and of Nikostratos also called Kopreus,
14    and of Ptolemaios and of Epimachos, the three sons
15    of Epimachos. We are registered in the
16    western parts of the Phrourion,
17    and I swear by Nero Claudius Caesar
18    Augustus Germanicus Imperator that truly
19    I have served as ephebe in the aforesaid year,
20    and that the aforesaid is my legitimate son
21    from my above-named wife, who is
22    a Hermopolite woman, free, of free
23    parents, and that I am among those received
24    from the gymnasium. If I swear truly, may it be well with me,
25    but if I swear falsely, the opposite. Year 6
26    of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
27    Imperator, in the month Kaisareios, the 3rd intercalary day.
28    Enrollment of Eudaimon the younger, son of Hermaios.
