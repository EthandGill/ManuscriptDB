[TRANSLATION]
1     Zenon greetings, Pyron. You will do well
2     concerning the grain which we have in the 32nd year the
3     500 artabas of barley for which we worked construction-labor
4     through Petechons having entered a counter-entry, so that not
5     do we owe having paid back, and concerning the in the
6     34th year wheat artabas 40 and barley artabas 150, concerning which Menodoros the brother
7     having served as ambassador announced to us [that you] released us,
8     similarly having brought [accounting] management, so that we not owe.
9     And for the rest too, so that we not trouble you concerning grain-matters,
10    you will do well having taken care [that] from at least this
11    year let be obtained for us a plot of land through you into which you will provide
12    for us the first seed, so that we might be able to be well-arranged; for from the 2 artabas which I receive, nothing for me
13    is left over. For besides the scribes given to me by you another one I support
14    and I give [him] wheat artaba 1½ bronze drachmas 3 oil cotyles 2 clothing drachmas 10;
15    and to Hermolaus I also give monthly oil cotyles 2
16    and for clothing which not even you yourself are ignorant of, apart
17    from the daily expenses.
18    And for the arrival back too, so that not completely shamefully
19    [we] sail back, if it seems good to you, [for] giving thanks to us
20    and having provided in advance for a joint purchase of poppy [seed] artabas 150 which you
21    will dispose to us with his own, and for grain-deposit drachmas 100 which will be collected from the price
22    of the poppy [seed].
23    have good fortune.
