[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Op[tatus the pa]tri[c]ian and of Anicius Paulinus the most illustrious, Phamenoth 23.
2     [Aurel]ius Pageus son of Horos, from the village of Hippona of the Herakleopolite nome, presbyter, to the superiors of the monastery of monks
3     [cal]led Hathor, which is on the eastern mountain of the upper Kynopolite. Divine imperial letters having been sent down by the most pious emperor Constantine, ordering certain bishops and presbyters of Egypt and many others, and me with them, …
4     [to] meet at Caesarea of Palestinian Syria for a judgment concerning the purification [of the] holy Christian
5     [m]ultitude, I being pressed to make such a departure to the afore[wri]tten Caesarea for the fulfilment of what has been commanded, necessity has come upon me to appoint a deputy in my stead until my presence; [on account of which] I gathered the monks of our monastery in the presence of Patabaeis presbyter of Hippona
6     [an]d of Papnoutios deacon from Paminpesla and of Proous the senior monk and of m[any other]s, and …
7     and they were content, out of unanimity, willingly and of their own accord and with unrepenting mind, (to appoint) Aurelius Gerontios my full brother, as one able to fulfil my place until my presence for a time
8     [and] to provide for and administer and manage all the affairs of the monastery, concerning indeed …
9     … and … and … to choose stewards of the monastery according to my likeness, no innovation being made without the superiors of the monastery concerning the … and the monks wishing to depart
10    … … … The deed of appointment is valid, produced everywhere, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged.
11    [Aurelius Pageus the ]af[oresaid] I have signed. The af[oresaid] … and … and Kollouthos and Dioskorides
12    … Kollouthos, I wrote on behalf of the others the letters, since they do not
13    [know (them).] … [an]d Proous … your …
14    … Papn[outios] … I gave my consent.
