[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been agreed.
2     In the second year of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustu[s Pius, on the … of the intercalary days, in the city of Oxyrhynchos of the Thebaid.]
3     Sarapion son of Adrastos son of Dionysios, his mother being Arsinoe daughter of Theon, from Oxyrhynchos cit[y, acknowledges to his own full brother Theon, in the street, that there is witnessed (?)]
4     through this acknowledgment, which he has made to Theon, on the new-moon day of the intercalary days of the pre[sent second year, according to a handwritten sale of the property that had belonged to him],
5     formerly to the deceased among them, their full brother Dios the younger, a fourth part which he ha[d … of a half part of a homestead in which (there is) a tower and dovecotes]
6     two unfinished, and of empty plots, the same being of a half
7     part, as the declarant indicates, shared with … [as to the half, and with] …
8     as to the other quarter, and the whole real-estate being likewise, as the declarant indicates, shared with [the others as to the other parts with] …, [whose neighbours are:]
9     on the south, formerly empty plots of Diadelphos son of Harpalos; on the north, on the western side (the plots) of Apollonios and [Kronios both sons of Adrastos, and on the eastern (side)] … [, on the east]
10    a party-wall; on the west, on the southern side a lane of which the southern part is on the water, and on the [northern] (side) … [, the price of the fourth part of the half part]
11    of the homestead in which (there is) a tower and two unfinished dovecotes and of empty plots being two hundred drachma[s of silver, which Sarapion has received thereupon, and for which Theon also paid the dues for the trans]fer-tax on the intercalary days of the present year; the guarantee of the same fourth part of the half part [of the homestead and] of the e[mpty plots following upon Sarapion; and he shall provi]de to Theon and to his (heirs) this clear from registration of men and from royal cultivation and from im[perial-estate land and from every category and from any whatsoever other thing]
12    forever, just as the handwritten sale also contains. Which (sale), if it should happen to be lost or otherwise to be destroy[ed, Theon shall not need any further consent, being content with this contract.]
13    Valid is the acknowledgment. In the second year of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antonin[us Augustus Pius, on the … of the intercalary days.]
