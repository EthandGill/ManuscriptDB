[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Aurelius An]ubion, strategos of the Oxyrhynchites,
2     [from Aurelius] Pasion and Sarapion
3     [and those] with them, grain-measurers of the middle toparchy of Nemera
4     [places, account of grain] which has been measured and
5     [distributed by us of the] harvest of the past 24th year
6     [of Marcus Aur]elius Severus Antoninus
7     [Caesar the lo]rd. And it is [as follows]:
8     … of the levies, Mesore, artabas 203 [and] 8/48.
9     [By person] of these … [was listed]
10    as of the city [and] as of the village.
