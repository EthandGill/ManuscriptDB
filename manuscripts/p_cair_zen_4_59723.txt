[TRANSLATION]
1     The remaining (arouras) which will be sown with grass: 1354¾, of which
2     for the royal (treasury) according to the lease
3     1000 arouras, from which they will deliver
4     from the aroura two-mina [bundles]: 1200,
5     so that there are 120 myriads (of bundles), which they will deliver,
6     of price the myriad at 66 (drachmas) 4 obols: total 8000 drachmas.
7     And of the remaining 354¾ arouras,
8     if it is advantageous for the farmers
9     to sell the aroura for more than 8 drachmas,
10    it will be permitted to them, and the price
11    they will place on the bank
12    jointly with the oikonomos
13    until they buy in barley
14    and measure it out toward the rent;
15    but if they wish to deliver
16    to the king, he will receive
17    the aroura producing two-mina bundles: 1200, so that there are 42 myriads 6900 (bundles),
18    of price the myriad at 58 (drachmas) 2 obols: total 2490 drachmas 1 obol ½.
19    The price of the grass results: 10490 drachmas 1 obol ½.
20    For barley they will reckon it at 25 artabas
21    for the 20 drachmas: there results of barley 1 myriad 3112¾ (artabas), which (equal) of wheat 7867¾.
22    To the same, together with that from the grain-bearing (land), in wheat: 2 myriads 5362.
23    And what we now reduce in respect of the
24    price of the grass of the
25    120 myriads, as you allowed for Dionysios,
26    per myriad of bundles 33 drachmas 2 obols
27    total 4000 drachmas
28    likewise too, if they deliver to the royal (treasury) the
29    (produce) from the 354¾ arouras, which results
30    42 myriads 6900, of the registered
31    prices, there will be a difference per
32    myriad of 41 drachmas 4 obols: total 1758 drachmas 2 obols.
33    The whole: 5758 drachmas 2 obols.
