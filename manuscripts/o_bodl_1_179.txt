[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 38, Pauni 5, have measured into the in Dios city the great treasury
2     year 38, on behalf of the place, from counter-registration, Hermophilos of Herakleides
3     who also Thotimē and Herakleios of Herakleios, of wheat fifteen
4     [one-]twelfth, total wheat 15 1/12. Herakleides.
5     Herakleides, of wheat 15 1/12, total 15 1/12.
6     Ptolemaios, of wheat 15 1/12. Of year 38, Pauni 5.
