[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dionysios, [one] of the friends and generals,
2     from Harmaios, [one] of those in the great Serapeion
3     being under refuge, fifth year, living
4     and from what I beg for in the sacred place. I am wronged by
5     Nefporites, [one] of those from Memphis. For of this
6     [her] daughter Tathmis residing together in the
7     sacred place, living from also what she gained-through-begging
8     distributed gifts, [she] having collected copper …
9     and having-given me them [as] deposit, after some
10    time Nefporites having-deceived me
11    and having-come-forward [that] Tathmis [was of] age
12    to have, as it is [the] custom [for] the Egyptians to circumcise, [she] having-demanded me to give to her
13    the copper, on [which condition] [she] having-accomplished this would-clothe her
14    and if she [were to] give in marriage her to a man [would] provide-a-dowry; if indeed
15    [she] not do each [of] these things or also not circumcise
16    Tathmis in [the] Mecheir month of the eighteenth year,
17    [she] shall pay [to] me straightaway copper [of] …, on which [my] having-consented and having-given to her in the Thoth month
18    the copper drachmas, [she] having-done none of the things agreed-upon,
19    for which reason being-troubled by Tathmis
20    and being-demanded-back the copper, it happens [that] I not-am-able
21    to go-down to Memphis toward necessary needs.
22    I request therefore you not to overlook me being-troubled
23    and to hate-the-evil and for which [it] has-been-made-difficult
24    through deception, if it seems-good to you, [to] command
25    to recall her to you, and if [she] is as I write,
26    to compel immediately the just-things to me to do,
27    so that I myself having-paid to Tathmis not-be-troubled.
28    When this happens I will obtain aid.
29    Farewell.
