[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Origenes, strategos of the Hermopolite (nome),
2     from the Aurelii Paesis son of Stephanos, whose mother is A…,
3     and Silvanus son of Pakymis, whose mother is Helene, both komarchs of the village of Senombo. We hand over and report the workmen registered below
4     as those going off to the
5     quarry at Alabastrine, in exchange for the workmen previously sent there,
6     being suitable and fit, at our own risk and (that) of all those from the village
7     under mutual surety. They are: the Aurelii
8     Eutis son of Silvanus
9     I…t… son of Tasytis
10    whom we guarantee and shall produce blamelessly, fulfilling the
11    work-service entrusted to them and not deserting
12    the place there until orders or, that is, relief (arrives). And if
13    they fall short and we ourselves do not produce them,
14    we will undergo the account on their behalf.
15    And being asked the formal question, we acknowledged. Year 17 and year 16
16    of our lords Diocletian and Maximian,
17    [Augusti, and the] 9th [year] of our lords
18    [Constantius and] Maximian, the most noble
19    [Caesars, in the consulship] of Postumius Titianus [and]
20    Virius Nepotianus. Mesore …
21    we have presented (it). I, Aurelius Horion, wrote on their behalf, they declaring that they do not know letters.
