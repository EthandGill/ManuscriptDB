[TRANSLATION]
1     Registered, Pauni 19.
2     [Year fourth] of Imperator Caesar Lucius S[eptimius] Severus Pius Pertinax Augustus Arabicus Adiabenicus, in the month Loios,
3     Pauni 18,
4     [at Ptolemais Euergetis] of the Arsinoite nome. [There acknowledges] Tasoucharion (daughter) of Ischyrion son of Herodes, from the metropolis, registered in the Myeris quarter, aged about …-seven, with a scar on the left brow, with [her guardian] her husband Dios son of Kronion son of Philadelphos, from the Moeris quarter, aged about
5     thirty, with a scar on the right knee,
6     [to her own former female slave] Zosime, aged forty-[four], with a scar on the right eye-corner: that the acknowledging party Tasoucharion has effected the
7     [manumission] of Zosime [through] … and the sales-tax and whatever else [is needed], and that she hereby consents to the (manumission) of Zosime through the herald appointed here at the course
8     … [herald] … by his proclamation toward the manumission and proclamation, (and) that neither the acknowledging party Tasoucharion nor those acting for her shall make any claim nor
9     [proceed against Z]osime nor against the offspring that shall be born from her from now on, on any pretext whatever; and if she does proceed or make claim, the proceeding and claim [shall be void, and moreover] she shall pay to Zosime the damages and the expenses double and a penalty of three thousand silver drachmas and to the public (treasury) the like (sum), the equivalent having been paid
10    [to the acknowledging party as ransom]. Signatory for Tasoucharion and her guardian, who say they do not know letters, (is) Zenor son of Sarapion son of Mamertinos, from the … quarter
11    … [aged about] …, with a scar on the left shin. [The] guarantor of the manumission: Chairemon son of Didymos son of Sarapion, from the Moeris quarter, aged about twenty-eight, without distinguishing mark. (I,) Tasoucharion (daughter) of Ischyrion, with my guardian my husband Dios son of Kronion, have effected the manumission of Zosime and consent to the proclamation and make no claim against her concerning anything on any pretext whatever, as aforesaid. (I,) Zenor [son of Sarapion], wrote on behalf of them, as they are illiterate. (I,) Chairemon son of Didymos son of Sarapion, attest the manumission of Zosime.
12    [the manumission]. (I,) Apollonios, agoranomos … have transacted upon …
