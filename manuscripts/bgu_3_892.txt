[TRANSLATION]
1   To my lord the landholder: Perousis,
2   very many greetings. …
3   I heard from Onnophris and
4   [Th]eon of Pake about your
5   visit to Pake, and I
6   sat waiting for two days expect-
7   ing you, and because of this I could
8   not come to Toou Pa-
9   sko. Now, if you are busy with the
10   wedding — since you could not return
11   to me — let me know through the
12   […]… about the day
13   on which the wedding takes place, that I may come
14   up to you; and write to Kollouthos
15   […] the fishermen of Kirka, that
16   […] to them before I go
17   up. (I sent?) you forty half-
18   kadia by Pachymis the archepho-
19   dos from […], and sixty little
20   pigeons, safe and sound; and send
21   me quickly the half-kadia
22   of Eu…tos the oil-maker, the one from Thallou;
23   and about whatever you wish done with me out here,
24   let me know, that I may do it
25   quickly before I go up.
26   I pray for your health,
27   my lord, for many years,
28   in good spirits and pros-
29   pering.
30   To my brother: Perousis.
