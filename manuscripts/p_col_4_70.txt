[TRANSLATION]
1     Sosus to Zenon greeting.
2     Know [that] we are held up in Memphis days
3     five because of the smooth [ones] and because of the birds. And we found in Memphis Menippus and he reported that
4     the birthday celebration has been postponed eight days. And know
5     also Dionysius the farmer [that he has] the emmer which he measured for fodder-cutting [having] sold and [having] applied toward the same debt. And the stones which
6     you sent to Apollonius
7     he brought in to the king
8     and [they] greatly pleased the
9     king.
10    Farewell. Mecheir 27.
11    To Zenon.
