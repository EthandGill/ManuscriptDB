[TRANSLATION]
1     Paul, presbyter and official resident of the city
2     to the brother Abraham, tax-collector.
3     Provide from the harvest, of which you received
4     from superiors Pi[narch and] various
5     [persons from] Pokheis
6     to the sailors of the ships at the money-changing [place] of Babylon
7     for their wages [for] four months from Pa[chon]
8     15 until Mesore 15, grain, artabas
9     seventeen, amounting to grain artabas 17 only.
10    Epiphi 28, indiction 2.
11    grain artabas 17 [from] Paul, presbyter and official resident, for the wages of sailors of ships at the money-changing [place].
