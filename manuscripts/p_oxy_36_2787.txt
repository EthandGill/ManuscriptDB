[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysi[us] T... [to the]
2     sister, [to rejoice],
3     Receive from father Chairemon a mirror and the
4     tablets and chiton of
5     Tapsois. Greet the children
6     and those in the house; [he] greets
7     you, Chairemon and the children
8     and Agathos greets you
9     [to the one] coming, grapes...
10    I wish you to be well.
11    Since the strategos pressed me about this, Ariston to you
12    [read] the little tablet sent to him.
13    I wish you to be well.
14    From Dionysius, [inspector] of Heracleopolis.
