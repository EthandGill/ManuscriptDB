[TRANSLATION]
1     Aelius Didymos and Herm[o]doros, son of Soter, tax collectors of silver [tax]
2     of Elephantine. Papreszmithes, [son] of Papelias, mother Tanapotis, paid [assessed] for the apportionment of the nineteenth year, two obols and a half-obol,
3     [and] for freightage and provisions of the nineteenth year, one obol.
4     [of the nineteenth year] Epeiph [2]. Hermodorios [collected].
