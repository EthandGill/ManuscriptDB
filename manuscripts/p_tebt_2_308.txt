[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 15 of Aurelius Antoninus
2     Caesar, the lord, Hathyr 13.
3     submitted to Ammonius and Theon
4     [lessees] of woods and desert
5     of the shore of Polemon's region
6     Petesouchos of Petesouchos, priest
7     of Tebtynis, price of a book
8     of two ten-thousands in Ibion
9     of Argaeus to Tebtynis, having brought
10    through Herakleides, brother of Ammonius.
