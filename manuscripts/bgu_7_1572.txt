[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dion, strategus of Arsinöite [of Heracleides' meris]
2     from craftsmen of the village Philadelphi[a. Since we have received] from the public treasury silver [drachmas …, in order that] we might construct [public] clothing …
3     [we] being bereft from the [work] men …
4     broken, [unable] to complete the commanded [task] …
5     of these four were given to …
6     on account of the need [we were deprived, and to Alexa]ndria [we] went and still more [we became distressed], necessarily to you [we] have fled [and we request]
7     if it seems good to you, to permit [us] to be allowed [us] remaining from the craft undistracted [from all] needs, so that the given clothing [we might] complete
8     and deliver and especially because another public clothing [provision] is laid upon us, which again we ourselves shall need to complete, in order that we be treated with goodwill. We were [formerly] men
9     twelve, of which four were separated, so that [now] [we be]
10    eight men
11    [Of the] year 3 of Autocrat Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus Augustus
12    [the] Pious, month Hadrian, the 20th
