[TRANSLATION]
1     Potamon, administering the accountancy of the Antaiopolite,
2     to Artemidoros the strategos and to Polykles the royal secretary of the same nome,
3     to my dearest ones, greeting.
4     From the examination and investigation made by me at the order of Claudius Iu[lianus]
5     the most eminent,
6     so far as was possible from the registers sent …
7     when fitting to the additions imposed at various times
8     upon imperial land, whether on the mainland
9     or on islands, it was learned [that] the surcharges given at various times
10    from the 19th year of the deified Marcus were not once deducted
11    or withheld by the royal secretaries, but as often as each
12    was imposed, it was charged against each of those who [had leased],
13    through the undertaking of those registering, on condition of not
14    being liable to surcharges of former years. And again,
15    when the time of the lease was completed,
16    it being necessary to be withheld once when it was imposed …
17    by those who made the deduction,
18    the royal secretaries, with respect to the … in suspension, and if more
19    by them, the amount later imposed to be reckoned among the exactible sums,
20    and if less, this too to be reckoned as a surplus. Whatever from this cause
21    was collected against me, more than was due, being added to the taxes, and whatever else there is from a single
22    surcharge,
23    imposed in the years above, and
24    of which Diognetos the most excellent has now made the determination,
25    and in part the … according to … ,
26    and in part the … according to the additions … ,
27    again to be deducted; of necessity, having appended it,
28    I write, dearest ones, that you may know and that each
29    may fill in his own parts. However, in the case of those
