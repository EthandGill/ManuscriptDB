[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ptolemae[us (my) brother], Ammonius, [greetings].
2     The earnestness of your intention we have [communicated] to our son Asclepiades,
3     so that he may by all means be zealous in his instruction,
4     [in that] now [he wished] and through us [to be deemed worthy, so] that through letters your intention
5     ... to willingly show [your eagerness]. On account of [these things] we write to your
6     [noble] character, in order that you may be zealous for the man.
7     [He said indeed] and [he himself] to have entrusted (it) to you concerning [this]. (He) is not anxious about your house and about
8     [the] matters pertaining to the business. Be well [I pray].
9     for many years, lord brother.
10    Be well [I pray for many years,]
11    lord [brother].
