[TRANSLATION]
1     … in the unity of the Trinity. Written in the month 9 (Pachon?).
2     [I, Athanasios, son of the] blessed Isaak, the headman (lashane) of the hamlet of Nisekate, in
3     … [I wr]ite to the public revenue, that is, our lord Korra,
4     … through you, also the most glorious lord, the master
5     [Basil], the illustrious one, and the pagarch of the village of Djkoou with all its [hamlets], greeting. Since they brought a portion of laborers
6     [upon] … so that we might give it as a poll-tax assessment … 3 of the courtyard
7     … as they settle it in Babylon for this year. This
8     … poll-tax assessment, that is, its money-commutation, which is one portion
9     [of a solidus] … per month. When we came, therefore, we beseech
10    … the poll-tax assessment which came due to you. You received the money-commutation [for this] work, you gave it to Theodosios the weigher, the agent … [at] the settlement which is set against us; you also received a receipt.
11    … [g]ive them in for the laborers of this kind. When the Saracen … [came north] against us, that is, Tsarah, he made inquiry through you.
12    … [he] found it (recorded) that you had sent their money-commutation northward; he stood up for it.
13    … not, unless the poll-tax assessment compelled us.
14    … We came, therefore, to beseech you concerning the money-commutation,
15    … the settlement against Theodosios the weigher; you returned (it).
16    … [you] paid it over to us in like manner, in like manner. We acknowledge
17    [I, Athan]asios, that I (received?) the portion of solidi and the third part (trimesion) of gold.
18    [It came to me, being] lacking by a single follis. And there is nothing else
19    … [that] we should ever at any time bring suit against you for
20    … any man at all, since what I gave came to me in full.
21    … therefore I drew up this acknowledgement, agreeing to it, swearing
22    … (by God) the Almighty and the salvation of those who rule over us, that we will keep
23    … and they questioned us. I acknowledge — I, Athanasios, [son of the blessed Isaak, agree to this] acknowledgement according to its force. I, Hezekias, son of the blessed [Chamoul] … I wrote on its behalf, that he does not understand.
24    … am a witness, that the acknowledgement (stands) according to its force.
25    … I am a witness, that the acknowledgement (stands) according to its force.
26    … [a wit]ness, that the acknowledgement (stands) according to its force.
27    … the blessed Menas the deacon, by whom this acknowledgement was made.
