[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twenty-second year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
2     in the month Peritios 30, Choiak 30, at Tebtynis of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. Cheos son of Harmiysis acknowledges,
3     about twenty-five years old, with a scar on his left thumb, to Mieus son of Phomsais, about 40 years old, with a scar on his right shin, that he has sold to him the paternal sacred land belonging to him, three arouras or however many there may be in all, of which the neighbours are: on the south
4     the allotment of Orsenouphis son of Horouanchious, on the north the allotment of Marcus Julius
5     Hyllos, on the west the so-called channel, on the east the allotment formerly of Psosneus son of Tapesouris; and (he acknowledges) that Cheos has received from Mieus the agreed price
6     in full, all of it, hand to hand from the house, and that he guarantees,
7     both Cheos himself and those acting for him, to Mieus and
8     to those acting for him, the matters concerning the sale, with every guarantee,
9     against all public debts and
10    all imposts and dyke-taxes (naubia) and artaba-tax
11    and surcharges and the village levy and
12    all the other things demanded
13    from the previous times up to
14    the past twenty-first year, and the past twenty-first year itself
15    of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, and against private claims and
16    every counter-claim for all time.
17    And the mother Thaesis daughter of Psosneus consents, about 40 years old, with a scar
18    on her right knee, to the sale, and (agrees) not to proceed (against it). The subscribers
19    of the acknowledging parties: Herakles son of Herodes, about 50 years old, with a scar on his right wrist, and of the other,
20    Eischyrion son of Patron, about 25 years old, with a scar in the middle of his forehead under the hair.
21    I, Cheos son of Harmiysis, acknowledge that I have sold to Mieus son of Pomsais the maternal sacred land belonging to me, three arouras or however many there may be,
22    and all the appurtenances, at Tebtynis,
23    of which the neighbours are: on the south the allotment of Orsenouphis son of Anchious,
24    on the north Hyllos, on the west a channel, on the east the allotment formerly of Psosneus son of Tapesouris. And
25    I have received the price in full, and I will guarantee against all public dues and
26    artaba-tax and dyke-tax and every kind
27    of the previous times up to the past twenty-first year and the
28    twenty-first year itself of Tiberius Caesar
29    Augustus. My mother Thaesis daughter of Psosneus consents as is stated above. Orseus wrote on their behalf
30    because they do not know letters.
31    I, Mieus son of Pomsais; the sale has been made to me as is stated above. Ischyrion son of Patron wrote on his behalf because
32    he does not know letters.
