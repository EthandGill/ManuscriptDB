1     Lycomedes to Hippodamus, greetings. To Hegesarchus concerning the things of which he accuses Theodore [of] Philagrus …
2     in the city of Hermu; but he says that for the examination of the matters accused, certain bodies are present for him; which [so that]
3     he might show, send along with him to the city of Hermu and thus he will not be d[rawn away]. For …
4     are present for him.
5     Farewell. Year 18 [Artemisios] …

7     Theodore of Philagrus
