[TRANSLATION]
1     … the dregs …
2     … at one drachma each, and …
3     … to be for Chrysippos and Nikanor the collector of the potters, and Amosis the priest, with respect to the price of the pottery
4     … and the sale of the vinegar which he made,
5     … so that you may know. Farewell. Year 4, Phamenoth 26.
6     Eukles to Anositis, greeting. We learn that you
7     have lodged the account of the pottery in the accounting-office, having reported neither the breakage that occurred through
8     the donkey-drivers, nor — of what is still owed among the potters in place of the 6 drachmas of the wage given to them
9     for the 100 jars — to enter 8 drachmas; and these things, when we are not even able to recover from them what they admit;
10    and the account of the swine sacrificial-victims up to now
11    you have not delivered into the accounting-office;
12    and that in general you are beginning to act maliciously or …
13    … if then any of these things is true,
14    you do not appear to be the one at fault, but I myself (am at fault) to myself.
15    Nevertheless, concerning these matters I have written to Lykophron
16    and to Apollonios, that if they are levying any extra charges
17    contrary to the accounts submitted by you, to write
18    to us at once, so that having come we may settle the dispute
19    with you. For it is just that you, who falsify the reckoning and do not report rightly the proceeds
20    through the accounts, should pay the differences, not we.
21    For in future we shall try, although often
22    deceived by you, having reconsidered,
23    to deliberate better. Farewell.
24    To Dionysios. You will oblige us by compelling
25    Anosis (to render) all the accounts which Apollonios,
26    our secretary, may submit, and (compelling) him
27    to lodge the same, so that if any extra charges
28    be recorded, at once we may settle the dispute
29    with him.
