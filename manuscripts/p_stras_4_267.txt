[TRANSLATION]
1     … to the one in charge of the recovery
2     … and of other properties;
3     from Stotoetis and Onnophris, both sons of Stotoetis son of Stotoetis, of those from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos [of the division of Herakleides] … . We wish to lease
4     from those you oversee, of the formerly Antoninus-Vespasianus estate, [near the village of] Psenyris of the division of Herakleides,
5     for seven years, sixty-six arouras, from the present [1st] … year
6     of Imperator Caesar [Trajan] Hadrian Augustus,
7     a palm-grove … belonging to you, in intercultivation, through public farmers, … seven and a half
8     quarter [eighth] arouras, [or however] many there may be, more or less, in which
9     … a drying-place, having fallen out, and a drying-place and … …
10    and … …
11    … dry land … in dry land, the rent for all
12    per year being eighty [drachmas] of silver and the additional charges; and we shall do the dry-cleaning of the palms
13    and the treadings and the pruning-downs and the fork-cleanings, and
14    of the other palms the clearings, and of the ground the under-diggings
15    and the double-hoeings and [the bank-works] and the waterings at our own (cost) at the
16    proper seasons, doing no damage, sowing the ground
17    with whatever we may wish, we carrying off to our own (account) … and
18    the yearly rent we shall pay in alternate years in the month Tybi, and we shall give toward the … in the 13th year the equivalent of the aforesaid rent
19    … … toward the (rent) of the final year
20    … of the rent; and when the term is completed, of necessity we shall hand over the palms after the gathering of the crops, and the ground after the gathering of what shall have been sown, and the wheat… if it seem good to lease on these terms.
