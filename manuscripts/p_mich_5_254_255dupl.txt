[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy issued. Year [1]7, Pachon 1.
2     I, Kronion son of Herodes, agree that I have sold to Didymos son of Didymos, according to this agreement, from the present day for all time, the sacred land belonging to me around Ptolemais of the Bee-keepers, after working it, in one seal, five arouras or however many they may be, whose neighbours are: on the south, dry land; on the north, formerly the land of Petermouthis but now the land of Herakleios; on the west, royal land and, for some part, dry land that has been purchased; on the east, royal land;
3     and I have received the agreed price in full, from hand to hand, out of the house, and I shall guarantee the sale with every guarantee, both from all public debts and all surcharges and every kind (of impost) of the times above up to the past fifteenth year and the
4     fifteenth year itself of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, and from private claims and every encroachment for all time, together with the rent of the present sixteenth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, the lease of the same year remaining with the cultivator. The wife lawfully living with me consents,
5     Thermouthis daughter of Alkimos, to this sale and (agrees) not to proceed for all time nor to bring a charge, nor any other on her behalf, as is set out above.
6     I, Kronion, have written on behalf of my wife, who does not know letters, and I am registered as her guardian.
7     Kr[o]nion, aged about 57, with a scar on his lef[t]
8     forearm.
9     Thermouth[is], aged about 45, with a scar
10    … in the middle.
