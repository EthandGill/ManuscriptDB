[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Joannes the most glorious
2     and most exalted, Mecheir 22, of the indiction.
3     Aurelius Kollouthos, son of Lythios, his mother being Tsa…,
4     vine-dresser from the hamlet called
5     Teyparat, of the fields of the village of Enseu of the Hermopolite nome, to Flavius Georgios, son of Silbanos,
6     of the soldiers, from the city of the Hermopolites,
7     greeting. I acknowledge that I have received and have been paid
8     from you the full price, agreed between one another, of one and
9     a third artabas of wheat and of ten knidia of new wine, fresh must,
10    that is, of wheat one and a third artaba and of wine ten knidia, which … I shall render to you thus: the wheat in the
11    month Pauni, at the season of the gathering of the crops
12    of the coming third indiction, by God's help,
13    in new clean sifted wheat,
14    by your Athenian measure, and the wine in the
15    month Mesore, at the season of the vintage of the crops of the same
16    indiction, without delay, in new most excellent
17    and well-pleasing wine, by the measure of the vineyard plot under me,
18    you providing the empty vessels for these. But if I am not
19    able to give you the same wheat and wine
20    at the same appointed term, I shall provide you, for the
21    price of these, six carats of gold, immediately after the …
22    of the same appointed term, at my own risk and (that) of all
23    my property. This document is valid [and secure],
24    and on being questioned I acknowledged it. I, Aurelius Kollouthos son of Lythios, the aforesaid, have made this document as set forth above. I, Aurelius Apaleus, of Hermopolis,
25    being requested, wrote on his behalf, he [being illiterate].
26    I, Flavius Isakos son of Phoibammon, from Mo… [I bear witness to the]
27    document, having heard from the one who made it …
28    from He[rmopolis], I bear witness to the document, having heard from the one who made it.
29    Through me, Victor, it was written …
30    Bond of Kollouthos son of Lythios, vine-dresser from the hamlet called T[eyparat] … of wheat [one and a third] artaba and of [wine ten knidia].
