[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Marcellus the most distinguished administering the [depart]m[ents of] the governorship from Rostam[us]
2     … [of] those from Birtha [of the] Okbans.
3     [Suffering mis]ery, master, I flee to you and ask to receive me favorably
4     [my request]; for my indicated father dying, lord, left me a vineyard
5     [in] [B]irtha [being] very young; and there happened the [invasion] of the Persians and [it] came to pass
6     … [hardly] escaping and coming to be in Appadna the daily food provide… [after] these things I being in Birtha and wishing the same [left] to me vineyard to enjoy, a certain Barsemaias of Thaimus, a man very violent and willful [ex]isting and powerful too in the places, I do not know on what pretext or what right
7     [using] he has retained the same vineyard as from a six-year time until
8     [today], despising me for my modesty in that I have no one and having care neither of the [proceedings] [made] by me petitions to the centurion by places.
9     [Since] then, lor[d], I have nowhere to obtain justice unless your fortune may approve it for me,
10    for I am troubled and by Bathsabbathas, who has become the creditor of the same father of mine, to whom the same vineyard is also subject, I ask and beseech, if it seems good to your fortune, through your subscription to order
11    to the procurator in Ap[padna] Claudius Ariston, or to whomever your fortune shall see fit, [to order] regarding the same Barsemaias …, and to order him in whatever time [he] enjoyed it to return to me the revenues. Thus
12    for through [your] fortune a moderate man of mine not being bereft and remaining unharmed
13    [I] shall have to you [forever] to give thanks.
14    Clatios … being asked I wrote on behalf of [Rostam]us illiterate, having sent the notices.
15    …
