[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the keepers-of-the-records] of acquisitions of the Hermopolite (nome),
2     from Chenepeis daughter of [H]ermias also called Antimachos,
3     her mo[the]r being Hermis daughter of Her[mod]oros, Hermopolite, registered at Phrourion-libos (West-Fort),
4     with [her guardian] Hermeinos … I declare for the present 19th year
5     of the Aureli[i] Antoninus and Commodus, the Caesars
6     the lords, the (property) brought to me by my
7     maternal grandfather Hermodoros son of Herakleides
8     according to a marriage-contract, the one (made) with my husband Neoptolemos son of Areios, completed through the supervisors of the record-office in the 6th year of the god
9     A[e]lius Antoninus, in the month Epeiph, in the Leukopyrites
10    upper (toparchy) about Tochnoubis, from the allotment of Ariston,
11    three arouras, of which the usufruct of the half part
12    has been reserved to my aforewritten mother Hermis
13    for whatever time she survives; whom I declare to have died, and I swear by the fortune of the Aurelii
14    Antoninus and Comm[od]us the Caesars the lords
15    that it is so. Year 19 of the Emperors
16    Caesars Marcus Au[reli]us Antoninus and
17    Lucius Aureli[u]s [Co]mmodus, the Augusti, Armeniaci
18    Medici Parth[i]ci Germanici
19    Sarmatici Greatest. I, Chenepeis daughter of Pausis,
20    have presented (this) and have sworn the o[ath]. I, Hermeinos, am registered as her guardian
21    and have written on her behalf since she does not know letters.
