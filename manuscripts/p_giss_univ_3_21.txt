[TRANSLATION]
1     Heron to Heracles the
2     brother, greetings.
3     urged, lord, remember me of the petition which
4     face to face I have made to you. If
5     even [if] you do not spare me, nevertheless respect your boy and your daughter; and
6     Sato[rnilu]s indeed and his wife I have brought up.
7     I pray that you may be well, lord.
8     To Heracles the brother
