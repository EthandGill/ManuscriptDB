1     … To …ros of the Parrasian deme, and Isidoros son of Didymos, of the Hermaean deme …
2     … son of Herminos son of Anoubias, of the Hermaean deme, the three of the tribe [Osirantinois, chosen] for the house-by-house census of the 2nd [quarter].
3     [From Dioskourides son of Zoi]los son of Dioskourides, of the Hadrianeum also called Ol[ympion] …
4     … having conquered in the 24th year of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus [Caesar]
5     [the lord] … in full armour. I register, according [to] what was ordered by Aurelius [Verianus]
6     [the most illustrio]us prefect, for the [present] 28th year of Aurelius Commodus Anto[ninus Caesar] the lord, the house-by-house census … to the existing …
7     … to …os, of Sabinus also called Harmonius, a house … in the 2nd quarter,
8     [in the brickyard] … northern, in which I dwell, and I am, for the present year, … years old …
9     … Zoilos son of Zoilos, having been gymnasiarch of the city of the Oxyrhynchites …
10    … [slave] persons of me Dioskourides, having come [to me] …
11    …os, [aged] … , [Na]rkissos, purchased, aged 28,
12    Horos likewise …
13    … [Pa]monthis, aged 22, Paulemis also called Pauli[nos ] …
14    … [aged] … 6, Ploution, aged 22, Didymos now …
15    … [house-]born, from the slave-woman Sarapias, aged 4 …
16    … tos, aged 19, Sarapias likewise a slave-woman …
17    … einarous also called Peina, aged 12, privately owned …
18    … tai, aged 24, these formerly …
19    … [and I swear the fortune of Aurelius Commodus An]toninus Cae[sar the lord]
20    [and the greatest god Osirantinoos, that I have,] from a sound state and in [truth, submitted the aforesaid census] and have lied in nothing.
