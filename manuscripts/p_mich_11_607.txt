[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign and [consulship of our most divine]
2     master Flavius Justinus the eternal
3     Augustus, Imperator, in the fourth year,
4     on the fifth of Phamenoth, of the second (indiction?),
5     of the present indiction, in the illustrious city of Antinoopolis.
6     Flavius Christodoros, son of Theodoros, herald (praikon) of the
7     governor's office of this city of the Antinoites,
8     from which he also originates, to Aurelia
9     Euphemia, daughter of Theodoros, widowed
10    of her husband, from the same city of the Antinoites,
11    greeting. I, the aforewritten
12    Christodoros, herald, acknowledge through this present
13    written security that I have had and have borrowed
14    from you, hand to hand into my hands, [for my own] and necessary
15    needs, of gold capital one solidus
16    less six carats by the public standard of Antinoopolis,
17    that is, one gold solidus less 6 carats by the same public standard; and this
18    I am ready to provide to you, both I and
19    all my heirs, whenever
20    you wish, through those who duly act
21    on your behalf, together with its interest per month,
22    that is, of silver
23    two hundred and fifty talents, without any
24    bad faith and postponement, there being pledged
25    to you for this all my present and
26    future property; and for your security
27    I have made for you this signed document,
28    being valid and secure wherever produced, and concerning
29    everything, having been asked, I have agreed; namely, in respect of this
30    [I have] deposited with you, by way of pledge, a Tarsian Egyptian linen cloth
31    and a sub-Delian (?) shirt. (Cross-mark) of Christodoros. Flavius Christodoros, son of Theodoros,
32    herald of the governor's office, the aforewritten: it is agreeable to me, and I shall repay the aforesaid solidus with its interest whenever you wish,
33    [so that] I may recover the pledges as aforesaid. Aurelius Magistor, son of Horouonchios, from Antinoopolis, having been requested,
34    wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters; he having put down three cross-marks with his own hand.
