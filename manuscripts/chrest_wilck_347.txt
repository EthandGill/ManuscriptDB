[TRANSLATION]
1     … son of Ammonios, Hermopolite, to Hermes son of Diogenes, from the same
2     city, greeting. I acknowledge that I have taken you as partner according to
3     … which I too leased from the public treasury
4     … for five years from the present 17th year of Severus
5     and Antoninus and Geta, the Caesars our lords, in the Lower Peri-polin (district)
6     concerning … from the Dionysios (holding) of dry-fodder land of arouras
7     … one half, one quarter, one eighth, one sixteenth
8     … we may use yearly, of rent and tax
9     … of that which is declared through my lease
10    … according to the third part of the rents (declared) through the lease
11    … of impositions you shall pay back and measure out yearly … and we shall each lay claim to the cultivation … and all the surplus produce yearly
12    … out of the third part after all the public dues …
13    … the portion and all the expenses that arise
14    … you shall pay according to the same portions. And if after the above-written period I am detained at the cultivation, you shall cultivate together with me
15    likewise according to the same third part, until I am released from the cultivation. The agreement is valid, my claim remaining to me concerning those things which I have
16    against you … of the just dues. Year 17 of the Imperators
17    Caesars Lucius Septimius Severus Pius Pertinax
18    Arabicus Adiabenicus Parthicus Maximus, and Marcus Aurelius
19    Antoninus Pius, Augusti, and Publius Septimius Geta
20    Caesar Augustus, Thoth 1. … of Ammonios Patem…
21    … Hermopol(ite)
22    … of Ammonios, from the same city, I wish willingly
23    … the present 17th year of Severus
