[TRANSLATION]
1   Peteuris son of Peteuris, linen-seller, paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the eighth year
3   of Trajan Caesar the lord:
4   seventeen silver drachmas — total 17 dr., together with those I had received
5   earlier. Hermogenes son of Ammonas.
