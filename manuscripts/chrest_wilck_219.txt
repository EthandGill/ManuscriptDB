[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Alexandros also called …
2     … and to Alexandros
3     … the one in charge of …
4     … the designated gymnasiarch,
5     to Serenos also called Eros and to Alexandros also called Souchammon, who have served as gymnasiarchs
6     in charge of the epicrisis, and to Alexandros also called Souchammon, a minor, through his guardian
7     his maternal uncle Kastor also called Sarapion, the designated gymnasiarch,
8     from Theanous daughter of Origenes son of Dioskoros, her mother being
9     Taotion, registered from the metropolis,
10    in the quarter of the Linyphion (weavers), with her guardian Molesion also called
11    Sarapokanopos son of Molesion. My slaves Bakchylos
12    and Chiliarchos having come of age — Bakchylos
13    into the fourteen-year-olds, and Cheiliarchos into the thirteen-year-olds, in the present 7th year
14    of Antoninus and Verus the lords Augusti,
15    who must be examined according to what has been ordered,
16    I have submitted my proofs of right. I therefore registered, in the
17    house-by-house census-returns made at the proper time, in both the 9th year and the 23rd year
18    of the deified Aelius Antoninus, in the aforesaid
19    quarter of the Linyphion, registering together in the return of the 23rd year
20    also the slaves to be examined,
21    Bakchylos and Chiliarchos; and I appended also a copy of the epicrisis of another slave of mine, Thallos,
22    who was examined in the 2nd year of Antoninus and Verus the lords Augusti.
