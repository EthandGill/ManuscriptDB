[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy the god Euergetes, son of Ptolemy and Cleopatra the Manifest Gods, and of Queen Cleopatra the sister
2     and Queen Cleopatra the wife, the Benefactor Gods, in the 36th year, in the priesthood
3     of the one who is in Alexandria of Alexander and of the Saviour Gods and of the Brother Gods and of the Benefactor Gods [and of the Father-loving Gods]
4     and of the Manifest Gods [and of the Mother-loving God] and of the God Eupator and of the Benefactor Gods; the athlophoros of [Be]renice Euergetis,
5     the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos, the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator, those being and existing in Alexandria; and in Ptolemais of the
6     Thebaid, in the priesthood of priests, of Ptolemy Soter and of King Ptolemy
7     the god Euergetes, and Soter himself the Gracious, and of Ptolemy Philadelphos and Ptolemy Euergetes and Ptolemy Philopator
8     and Ptolemy the god Epiphanes [the Gracious] and
9     Ptolemy the god Philometor and Dikaiosyne [and of Ptolemy the god Philometor and] Ptolemy the god Eupator and
10    of the priestesses, of Queen Cleopatra the sister, and Queen Cleopatra the wife, and Quee[n]
11    Cleopatra the daughter, and Cleopatra the mother the manifest goddess, and the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos, those being and existing in
12    Ptolemais of the Thebaid; in the month Mesore, on the twenty-
13    seventh, in Krokodilon polis of the Pathyrites, before Dioskourides the agoranomos.
14    Ptollis son of Hermokrates, a Persian of the (corps) of Boethos, on regular pay, about
15    45 years old, tall, honey-coloured, with somewhat curly hair, long face, straight nose, a scar
16    over the left eyebrow, another on the left wrist, sold — and Nechouthis daughter of Pekysis, a Persian woman, about 45 years old, of middle height, dark-skinned, long-faced,
17    straight-nosed, acting with her guardian, her own son Chaleis son of Moschion, a Persian of the (corps) of Boethos, on regular pay, about 35 years old, of middle (height), honey-coloured,
18    with somewhat curly hair, long face, straight nose (?), and Chaleis himself,
19    and Harpaesis son of Krourios, a Persian of the epigone, about nineteen years old, of middle (height), honey-coloured, with straight hair, long face, straight nose,
20    somewhat bald in front, calm — [from what belongs to them],
21    in the part to the north of Pathyris, of an empty plot, seven solid cubits, of which there belong to Ptollis th[ree] solid cubits, [and to N]echouthis [and Chaleis]
22    three solid cubits, and to Harpaesis one solid cubit, to fill up the aforewritten seven solid cubits; [neighbours] of [the whole plot: on the south, the house]
23    of Psennesis son of Katytis; on the north, the dovecote of Herakleides and his brothers; on the east, the royal road; on the west, a thicket … [or whoever the neighbours may be]
24    on every side. Isidoros son of Theon, a Persian of the (corps) of Boethos, on regular pay, about 35 years old, of middle (height), honey-coloured, with somewhat curly hair,
25    long face, stra[ight nose, somewhat grey-eyed,] bought (it) [for copper coinage]
26    of one talent, two thousand drachmas. Fore-sellers and guarantors of all matters pertaining to this sale: Ptollis
27    and Nechouthis and Chaleis and Har[pa]esis the vendors, [whom Isidoros]
28    the buyer accepted.
