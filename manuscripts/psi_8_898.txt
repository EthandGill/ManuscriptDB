[TRANSLATION]
1     If they say [they] have given and [given]…
2     … and paid down to the children
3     of Klēmatiēs, we shall be knowing that [they] have allotted a plot of land
4     to them in the name of the debt
5     of Seuthos, differing by [the amount] on the mother's [side]
6     grandmother; for from other marriages they appear
7     to have been born, and the maternal [portion] they have received, and that for which debt [they] have not restituted; but which by nature was owed, this they have handed over.
