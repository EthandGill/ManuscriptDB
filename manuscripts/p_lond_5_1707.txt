[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Justinus the eternal Augustus, Emperor, in the first year, on the eighth of Phaophi, in the fifteenth indiction, in Antinoopolis the most splendid.
2     This security of compromise (i.e. arbitration agreement) they set down and make toward one another, willingly and being persuaded, without any deceit and fear and force and trickery and compulsion and any circumvention, on the one part
3     the Aurelii Theodoros son of Psoios the steward, and his nephew Psoios son of Kosmas the notary; and on the other part Aurelius Konon son of Epanakios the agent, acting and lawfully transacting on behalf of Athanasia
4     his most noble wife — the three of them originating from the Lykopolite (nome) but now residing in this (city) of the Antinoites — and they agree with one another concerning the matters subjoined. A dispute having arisen
5     between the parties concerning manifest capital matters pertaining to them, it seemed good to them to make use of arbitration, and they chose by common consent Konstantinos and Joannes the most learned advocates
6     and counsellors of the court of the Thebaid, men claiming what is just. According to this, then, those of either party agree, swearing by the holy and consubstantial Trinity and by the victory and permanence of our triumphant
7     master Flavius Justinus the eternal Augustus, Emperor, to present themselves before the aforesaid most learned men within four days reckoned from today and the aforewritten day, which is Phaophi
8     the eighth of the present fifteenth indiction, and to lay before them the rights belonging to them, and to comply and to obey the things that shall be determined or judged by them
