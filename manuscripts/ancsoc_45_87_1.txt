r.1    Ζήνωνι χαίρειν Ψενόβαστις.
r.2    εἴ σοι δοκεῖ, καλῶς ποιήσεις γράψας
r.3    Παυῆι τῶι βασιλικῶι γραμματεῖ
r.4    ὅπως ἂν πολυωρῆι ἐάν τι εὔγνωμον αὐτὸν ἀξιῶμεν. γίνωσκε δὲ

[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon greetings, Psenobackis.
2     If it seems good to you, you will do well having written
3     to Pauei, the royal secretary
4     so that he might care for us if we ask anything favorable of him. But know
5     us and those ambiguous-ones
6     taking up those from hand
7     casting around so that no one
8     other might travel to the
9     islands. Write then to Pauei so that not
10    might oppose us.
11    Farewell.
12    Psenobackis.
