[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollos to Priscus [the brother, greetings]
2     and good health always; I [received]
3     the measure of oil and immediately [I gave]
4     it to Procles; I did not send you co[pper because]
5     I do not know the donkey-driver; since you a[sked]
6     nine drachmas and four obols immediately if I find a trustworthy [person] I
7     will send you the price; I received
8     three kollyra from Pebotus and I gave
9     one to Maximus; bring a bundle of beets;
10    Greet Maximus the recruit and Domitian
11    and Apollinarius; Valeris greets you
12    and Valerianus and Chaiemon.
13    Fare well.
