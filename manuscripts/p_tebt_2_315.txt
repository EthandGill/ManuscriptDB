[TRANSLATION]
1     … to … the most dear
2     … [greet]ing.
3     [I sent] you also other letters [through] which I asked you concerning
4     [the] 6 [garments] of Pyrrhos and
5     … 2 cloaks, so that
6     [you may send] me (them) at whatever price they may be; and now too
7     [with hast]e I write so that
8     [you may] not [be anxious], for I will make you untroubled. For know that
9     an inspector of the administrations
10    of the (revenues) in the temples has entered (office) and is about to come also into your division. Therefore
11    do not be [disturbed] at all, for I will
12    [set] you free. If then you have leisure,
13    having written up your accounts, come up to me; for the man
14    is exceedingly harsh. But if
15    something detains you, send them to me and I will extricate you; for
16    he has become my friend. And if he has (a claim) against you concerning the expenditure and you do not have (it)
17    at present, write to me and
18    I, as also on the former occasion,
19    will [set] you free now. I hastened to write to you
20    [so] that you yourself may not appear; for before
21    he comes to you I will make him extricate you. For he has letters of recommendation, so that the one who disobeys
22    he may send under guard to the high-priest. But
23    do not neglect yourself, nor (fail) in the (things) which I
24    wrote to you to buy for me. And if you also
25    have any … as many as you have,
26    bring up to me, for I have need (of them).
27    Farewell (for my sake), most honoured one.
