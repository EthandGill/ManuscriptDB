[TRANSLATION]
1     … [Papnouthis] the
2     son of Horion you shame, and the
3     sixteen artabae of wheat you receive from him. Very indeed I wonder, how neither
4     the appearance which he wears is ashamed lying,
5     nor you, the one worthy of all honor, does he shame,
6     nor the principal through so much time
7     willing to give. [I greet] my lord Ophellius and the most honored mother of us
8     Tienor and Pinan and Horion; I [address]
9     [and the remaining brothers of us all]
10    [by nam]e. Healthy you in soul and
11    body the lord will keep.
12    … Nepher
13    Paul.
