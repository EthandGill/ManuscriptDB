[TRANSLATION]
1   […] to do all the works [t]oward the good cultivation of the same arouras, irre-
2   [proachably]; and (that) I (do) also the binding of all the hay, while the various
3   [four-footed beasts] and the advance of seed and the plucking of the hay look
4   [to y]ou the landlord; and (that) you deduct yearly from the common (account)
5   [the see]d given by you, and for the wage of sheaf-carrying, accord-
6   [ing to …] … (that) you deduct from the
7   [commo]n (account) also the wage of the threshing, and for the landlord's customary due
8   […, a]t one and a half artabas per artaba. The lease is valid, and on being questioned I agreed.
9   (2nd hand) [I, …]…, the aforesaid farmer, have leased the aforewritten arouras
10   and I will do and give and pay everything as is stated above. I, Aurelius Elias son of Paul(os),
11   [wrote on his behalf, as he is] present (but) illiterate. ⳨
