[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of the most illustrious Flavius Basileus, year
2     twenty-fourth, Thoth eighteenth, fourteenth indiction.
3     To Aurelia Aphthonia the most noble daughter of the of blessed memory the Highest
4     from the Hermopolitan nome, by Aurelius Bonus son of Gennadius
5     mother Eucharistia, farmer, from the same city. I acknowledge
6     willingly and of my own choice to have leased from your nobility
7     for a four-year period, reckoned from the produce of the coming with god
8     fifteenth indiction, and the existing to you two arouras
9     of seed-bearing land with all their due [rent], one of them the [one]… amen
10    [... to] your nobility to your most noble father according to written gift lying in the east of the same city under the watch of
11    Enseu of the Hermopolitan nome from the east of the holy place called of virtue
12    for sowing and storing of produce which I shall choose, with rent each year of wheat
13    eight artabas and lentils four artabas and peas one artaba
14    [... ] and beans one artaba, which rent new pure winnowed [measure by] Athenian I shall render [and] measure to the month Epeiph annually un[delayably] and I shall restore the same rent into your house in Hermopolis
15    [with my own] animals and expenses, and I shall furnish each year according to custom
16    [wheat] straw sacks two and green beans plants one and
17    [milk] jar one and grain stalks bundles twelve and turnips
18    [containers] … [the lease] valid and binding, and being questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius
19    [Bonus son of Gen]nadius farmer the aforementioned have leased as aforesaid.
20    [... A]braham deemed worthy wrote for him letters not knowing. … I testify
21    [to the lease having heard from the one producing.] …
22    [I testify to the lease having heard] from [the one producing.]
