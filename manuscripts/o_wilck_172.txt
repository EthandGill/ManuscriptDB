[TRANSLATION]
1   Annius Ammonianus and <Sarapamon(?)>
2   son of Pachnoubis, collectors. Paid:
3   Thipsenos son of Chemsneis,
4   through Psinp[etorz]methis
5   son of Bienchis, for the price of the state palm(-dates),
6   of the produce of the 2nd year of Antoninus
7   Caesar the lord:
8   3 dr. 4 ob. (worn). Year 3 of Antoninus
9   Caesar the lord, Hathyr
10   26. (2nd hand) I, Annius Ammonianus,
11   have signed off: 3 dr. 4 ob.
