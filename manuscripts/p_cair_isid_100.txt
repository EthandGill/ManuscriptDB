1     To Aurelius Nemesinus, [having served as] exegete, councillor of [the city of] Arsinoe,
2     from Aurelius Isidor[us] of Ptolem[aeus], [of the] mother
3     Heroeis from the village Karan[ides]. I wish to lease from you [the lands] belonging to you around the
4     same village [bordering] Kerkesucha,
5     grain [fields], four [arouras] or as many as there may be, for a term of three [years], from the sowing of the present year, [the] annual [rent] of everything, each year of wheat nine and [one-]half [artabs], and
6     I will complete [the work] of the ar[our]as [each year]
7     all that is fitting from my [own] [resources] at the fitting
8     [times], causing no harm whatever, and [the] annual
9     rent I will pay in the month of Pauni, on public measure, the four-[choinix] measure, of [the public]
10    all things being toward you the land-owner, and
11    after the period I will hand over the [fields]
12    from [rest-land] and [straw] alike. The lease [is] valid, and [being] questioned [I] acknowledged.
13    Aurelius Nemesinus [has] leased [this] as aforesaid.
14    Year 2 of our lord Domitian
15    Augustus, Phaophi 23.
