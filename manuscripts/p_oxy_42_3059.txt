[TRANSLATION]
1     Didyme to Apollonios, her brother
2     and sun, greetings.
3     Know me not seeing the sun
4     because of your not being seen by me.
5     For I have no other sun except you. But I thank Theonas, your brother
6     … which I sent to your father
7     … of Theon the Athenian, the friend
