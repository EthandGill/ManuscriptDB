[TRANSLATION]
1     To Statilius Maximus the
2     most excellent prefect,
3     from Soedutus of Mes…[o] from
4     of the [metr]opolis of the Arsinoe nome.
5     In the 12th year of [An]toninus Caesar, [the] lord ap…ed regarding tax-collection by the tax-collectors at that time regarding
6     inheritance having accrued
7     to me from the will of Julius Chairemonianus, what he owed on behalf of the inheritance in money, in accordance-with which I have a contract. But a certain Capitolinus and those with him come upon me continuously and oppressively, [wishing] [me] to work-for-contract [and] to exact what I do not owe
8     regarding tax-levy charges.
9     Of the contract being with me. Since then these do not [refrain] from the against-me harassment, [despising] the non-interference regarding me and the things concerning-such-matters by all governors
10    having been prescribed, so that [one] should not act wrongly, necessarily to your
11    aid I took refuge and I request
12    these finally to desist
13    from the against-me harassment, in order that I [may be able] as-a-woman alone to manage under you
14    having been benefitted. [May you prosper].
