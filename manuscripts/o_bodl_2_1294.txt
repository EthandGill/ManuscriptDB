[TRANSLATION]
1     [It] has been measured into the treasury of villages, [of the] produce of the fifteen year of Hadrian the lord
2     Thoth [the] twenty-third of the sixteen year, [under the] name of Tasemius
3     Abotos through Pachnumius … for Charax: one artabe of wheat
4     [it] comes to one artabe of wheat, and [under the] name of Senpshenamounis Petearouherios
5     [a] twelfth of an artabe of wheat [it] comes to 1/12 artabe of wheat. Another [on the] twenty-fourth [under the] name of Tasemius
6     Abotos for Notus: … [wheat] half [plus] sixth [plus] twenty-fourth of an artabe
7     [it] comes to 1/2 + 1/6 + 1/24 artabe of wheat. Another [on] Thoth [the] thirtieth of the sixteen year
8     [under the] name of Senpshenamounis Petearouherios for Notus: … [a] twelfth of an artabe of wheat
9     [it] comes to 1/12 artabe of wheat. Pikos has verified [this].
