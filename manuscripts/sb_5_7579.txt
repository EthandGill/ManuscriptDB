1     Gaius Julius Maximus and associates
2     [collectors of] the fiftieth [tax] of the harbor of Syene, Ammo…uthios [of] Hermogenes, greetings. I received from you the tax that was produced,
3     which you reported during Pharmouthi 20 [of] flax-reed
4     bundles 4. Similarly, which you reported …, drachmas 4.
5     Similarly [in the case of] 24 [units of] flax-reed bundle 1. Similarly in Payni 25
6     of barley artabas 4. Similarly in Mesore 15 of wine flagons 4.
7     Similarly, which you reported myrobalan
8     artaba 1. Year 3 of Trajan the lord
9     Mesore 29.
