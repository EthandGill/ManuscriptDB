[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God. Alaaref son of Abd to those from
2     Nestanon district of Elusian territory Gaza. Provide quickly …
3     son of Zaid of the bani Saad son of Malech for months 2 Rajab and Sha[ban]
4     for the crops of indiction 4 ninety-six modii of grain, [and oil] [measures ninety]
5     six. It was written in the month of August indiction 3, according to Arabs year 55, by the hand of Steph[anus]
6     Mai[o]umites. Total: grain modii 96, oil measures 96
7     Total: grain modii 96, oil measures 96
