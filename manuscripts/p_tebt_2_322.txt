[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios also called Diogenes, I have signed.
2     To [Am]monios, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite (nome),
3     [an]d to Harpokration also called Hierax, royal
4     scribe of the same division, and to Mystes and Heron, former scribes of the metropolis,
5     from Achilleus son of Apollonios son of Lourios also
6     called Apollonios, a settler (katoikos) registered and enrolled
7     through another memorandum. There belongs to me, in the quarter of Moeris, a part of a house and a courtyard (aithrion) and a court
8     and an exedra, in which I additionally register the undersigned lodgers for the house-by-house census of the past
9     28th year of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus
10    Caesar the lord, they being from the metropolis, registered in the quarter
11    of Syriake, in which also in the house-by-house census of the 14th year
12    they were registered; and they are Pasigenes son of Theon son of
13    Eutyches, a poll-tax-payer, donkey-driver, aged 61, and his son Eutychos, his mother being Apollonous
14    daughter of Herodes, aged 30, and the wife of Pasigenes, Herakleia daughter of Kronion, freedwoman of Didymos son of Heron
15    from Tameia, aged 40, and from both of them a daughter Thasis, aged 5,
16    and the children of Herakleia: Sabinos son of Sabinos son of Kronion, a poll-tax-payer, hairdresser, aged 18, and
17    Sarapias, aged 22, registered
18    in the earlier census at
19    Tameia, and the wife of Eutychos, being a sister on the father's side,
20    Tapesouris, her mother being Isidora, aged 18. Wherefore I submit (this).
21    And there belongs to Tapesouris, in the quarter of Moeris, a maternal one-sixth part of a house.
22    Year 29 of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar
23    the lord, Mesore, of the intercalary days 4.
24    Filed with the strategos in the 29th year, Mesore, of the intercalary days 4.
25    Filed with the royal scribe on the same day. Filed with the scribes of the city on the same (day).
