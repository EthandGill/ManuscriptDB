[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 16, of Lucius Septimius
2     Severus the Pious, Pertinax,
3     and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
4     the Pious Augustuses.
5     Hathur 3. Ptolemaeus [son] of Ptolem[aeus, and Dios]koros [son] of Mysthos, and [their] partners in a land-lot
6     of the grain-tax of the village Karanis, we have measured in the treasury of the aforementioned village [with] public measure
7     [with the] xyston [measure], exacted from the produce of the
8     elapsed 15th year, [of] Karanis, [of the] inhabitants, Horians, [of] Apollinaris, wheat, one-and-a-half, one-third,
9     one-twelfth, [which] makes wheat ... one-half, 3, 12, [month] 11, similarly,
10    the same through Julius Eudas,
11    wheat, one artaba, [which] makes wheat, one artaba.
