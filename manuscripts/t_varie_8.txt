[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the lord and master Jesus Christ of God and our savior — written in the month Phamenoth 26 — of the twelfth indiction.
2     Aurelius [S]erne, vinedresser, son of the blessed Pachome, mother Kairapantou, proceeding from the estate of Palosis
3     of the Oxyrhynchite nome — to Aurelius Sergius, son of Papnouthius, from the village of Syron of the same nome, greetings. I acknowledge
4     the aforementioned [S]erne to have received from you already hand to hand from house into my own and necessary
5     need gold coins two and a half each coin from karats twenty-two and a half by pagan weight — […],
6     [they] amount to gold coins 2½ each coin from karats 22½, [having received] fr[om] you in the mutually agreed price [from] full wine must one hundred sixty-eight,
7     [there] amount wine musts 168, which wine [I will necessarily] rep[ay] to you in the month of Mesore of the coming year [in]
8     twelfth indiction into [the] outflow of the divine prosperous [thir]teenth [and fourteenth] distribution [in] wine
9     fresh suitable of each must
10    of five xesta, you providing the light [portions] without delay and without any objection. But if [it] is found to be vinegar or
11    spoiled in the same wine until the month of Tybi of the same [thir]teenth [and fourteenth] indiction, the aforesaid [things]
12    I will exchange and [I will provide] the equal in approved wine
13    at the time of the exch[ange] being from [the] outflow [of the] fourte[enth] [indiction]. [Let it be] binding
