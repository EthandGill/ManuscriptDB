[TRANSLATION]
1   In the eighth year of Imperator Caesar
2   Trajan Hadrian Augustus, in the month Peritios 5,
3   Choiach 5, in Euhemeria of the Themistos division
4   of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge: Herakleides
5   son of Philadelphos, registered in the quarter of the Sacred
6   Gate, about thirty-six years old, scar on the left knee,
7   and Neilos son of Heron, registered in the quarter
8   of Dionysios Topon, about twenty-five years old, without distinguishing mark,
9   to Tauris daughter of Hatres, about fifty years old, without distinguishing mark,
10   with as guardian her husband Pnepheros son of Pnephe-
11   ros, about fifty-five years old, scar on the left forearm,
12   that the acknowledging parties have received from
13   Tauris the rent of the past seventh
14   year of Hadrian Caesar the lord, of the jointly-owned
15   house of theirs which she inhabits, in the village of Euhemeria,
16   and that neither they nor their agents make any claim against Tauris
17   nor against her agents, nor will proceed
18   concerning the matters of which they have received (payment), as aforesaid. Subscriber for the acknowledging parties, Mysthes
19   son of Heron, about 20 years old, without distinguishing mark. (hand 2) We, Herakleides son of Philadel-
20   phos and Neilos son of Heron, acknowledge
21   that we have received from Tauris the rent
22   of the past seventh year
23   of Hadrian Caesar the lord, of the jointly-owned house which she inhabits,
24   of ours, in the village of Eu-
25   hemeria, and we make no claim, as
26   aforesaid. I wrote on their behalf, Mys-
27   thes son of Heron, since they do not know letters.
28   (hand 1) In the eighth year, in the month Choiach 5, through Heron of the
29   record-office of Euhemeria. (verso)
30   Receipt for rent of the 7th year, of Tauris.
