1   Petechon son of Charon, the (son) of Pamonthes,
2   mother Senamenothis, paid, for poll-tax of the Charax (quarter), year 8,
3   ten drachmas; bath-tax 1 dr. 1½ ob. — total 11 (dr.) 1½ ob. — and surcharges on these.
4   Year 8 of Domitian the lord,
5   Mecheir 13(?). Herakleides, I have signed.
