1     In the consulship of Flavius Longinus the most-respected … [9 indiction in Arsinoe]
2     To Flavius Julius the most-respected son of [the blessed memory]
3     John holding land over the Arsinoe[ites city from Aurelius]
4     Akoos son of … sia [vine]worker of [your brightness from the]
5     same city from the royal quarter. Greetings. I acknowledge having leased
6     [from] your brightness what belongs to her in what belongs
7     [to] her in the estate Si[bina] of the Arsinoe[ite nome a vine-bearing]
8     [land having been worked] [and walled around, of arouras]
9     [however many there may] be, with the date-palms in it and various plants and
10    machinery and machinery-apparatus furnished and mechanical
11    tools for as long as [you wish from the crops of the with god entering] tenth indiction having received from her for work one waterschrew of pure iron, [and with god from the flowing]
12    [of wine] from the same land the being-produced yearly [to divide to you] three parts and to me one part, [and under obligation I shall complete]
13    [the things toward] good cultivation of the same vine-bearing land all necessary works. And I shall give in rental payment of …
14    … [forty] date-palms, gold coins of that amount by carats
15    … [, you providing] oxen and their food. And I shall do, if [drought] comes, toward the pumping of the [same land the] digging [toward] the vineyard ditch [whenever you wish to] reject me [from] the same land, I [shall hand over the vineyard and the]
16    [oxen and] the one waterschrew of the mentioned [weight. The lease]
17    [is valid and] having been questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius Ako[os] [son of] …sia vineworker the
18    [aforementioned] have leased and [I shall] repay and all agrees with me as
