[TRANSLATION]
1     Menches, village-scribe of Kerkeosiris in the division of Polemon, [to Horos, greeting. While I was]
2     in the [council] convened at Ptolemais Euergetis … [for the]
3     survey by holding, it was reported to me concerning [the fact that Apollodoros, the oil-]seller in the village, had found on the 11th of the [present] month [a certain Thracian who had been selling oil in violation]
4     in (the house) in which Petesouchos the shoemaker lodges …
5     and that, having handed him over to Apollonios, the one [conducting the affairs of the overseer's office] of the same (village),
6     together with the penalty, he should be brought before those to whom it is fitting; [concerning which Apollodoros has submitted to me]
7     the appended report. I judged that it was well [to report it].
8     [Farewell. Year] 4, Mecheir 14.
9     From Apollodoros, the one who has taken up the sale [and the] tax on oil
10    of the same (village) for the 4th year. Since the tax-farm has been left in arrears on account of those
11    bringing in and selling on the side smuggled(?) oil and castor-oil, whence
12    on the 11th of Mecheir it having been reported to me that a certain Thracian, whose [name] I do not know, of those from Kerkesephis, had brought in oil into the house in which Petesouchos the shoemaker lodges and was selling it on the side to Thaësis, who lodges in the same house, and to … the goose-keeper
13    and to his daughter, of those from the same village, immediately having [taken] the overseer and the (man)
14    from the chief of police—because you too were not present in the [district]—to the aforesaid house of the shoemaker, I caught the Thracian inside, but the penalty (goods) had been removed; and making a search of it together with them I found in …
15    in it … having been moved … of oil … and together …
16    and another … the
17    Thracian … [of the] shoemaker, in a rug and in sheepskins
18    … had set off in flight, and the
19    penalty (goods) … the aforewritten Thracian did not …
20    … so that a loss of 15 talents of copper has come upon me.
21    Therefore I submit (this) to you [so that you may make a subscription about each matter], appending
22    a copy [to whom it is fitting] …
23    Farewell.
24    To Horos.
