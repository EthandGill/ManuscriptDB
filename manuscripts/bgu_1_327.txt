1     To Gaius Caikilios Saluianus the most mighty judge, succeeding
2     and [the matters] according to the governorship,
3     from Aitetae Phrontis. According to which he left, master, a Roman will
4     Gaius Fab[i]ou[ll]ius Macer, veteran from the fleet of the praetorian [Misen]ians, left heir Gaius Loingeinos Kastor fellow-veteran of himself and
5     according to the same will [he] wished to be given to me legacy of silver
6     drachmas [two]-thousand and a purple-pallium of his-own color. This one indeed leaning [upon] the inheritance and all the things left behind by him in himself
7     having made, [the bequest] to me up to now he does not wish to give back
8     Therefore I ask, [if to you by] fortune it seems [good], [to hear me] toward him, [how] I might be able [the bequest to re]ceive through your [fortune] for always [to give] thanks. Prosper well.
