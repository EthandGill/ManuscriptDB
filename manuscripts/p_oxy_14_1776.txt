[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Demetrius, Paul.
2     I have sent the riverworkers
3     so that you may make them
4     work on the new
5     canal. Be willing [to supply] as much as
6     they need both in grain or
7     vinegar, provide to them;
8     and the farmers of the
9     three water-wheels of
10    Papsau demand two ceramic vessels of cheese
11    and provide
12    to them. I pray for you to be well for many years, my lord
13    brother.
