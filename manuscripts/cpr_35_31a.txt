[TRANSLATION]
1     64
2     To the most excellent council of Hermou polis the great, ancient, and splendid, through Marcus Aurelius Asklepiades also called Hermodoros, temple-warden of the great Sarapis, all-conqueror, victor in the circuit-games, anointer, unforced, unimpeachable, xystarch for life, prytanis in office, through
3     the Marci Aurelii: Ammonios also called Polydeukes, who has served as kosmetes, and Dioskoros son of Dioskoros
4     son of Hermaios, who has served as agoranomos, both councillors of the same city. Since we were chosen in the council, in accordance with the things minuted before the most excellent council, to make an inspection of plots
5     and orchards and a register of the plants found, to declare to you
6     and the condition of the formerly … now of the most holy Sarapeion, of the priestly account,
7     of which the lessee is Aurelius Paniskos also called Melas, who has served as gymnasiarch and prytanis and however he is styled, we declare to you,
8     as is set out below; and it is thus:
9     having come to be in the village of Boou, with the most worthy prytanis in office also being with us,
10    Aurelius Asklepiades, with Mauros the steward of the estate attending, we inspected
11    of the (lands) from the holding of Kallimachos a vineyard plot of 22 arouras, or however many, with a little vine living and this … through neglect and full of rushes, and round about
12    the plot much dry land and rushes; and the cistern and the water-reservoir to the south
13    of the plot in a state of collapse in part; and there stood upon the water-wheel a living mulberry-fig
14    and sycamores 20, demolished and scattered about the plot; small mulberry-figs
15    in number 7; and by the southern wall of the property, in the (part) to the south toward the west, a wine-press with a jar likewise in a state of collapse; and the northern water-wheel and the cistern in
16    a state of collapse in part, and by them … a living mulberry-fig 1; and near the water-wheel an old ruined house … with the wine-press; after this
17    inspection, having come … in number 26 of the (lands) from the
18    holding of Philiskos, a palm-orchard … this we found, in the
19    eastern part of the orchard, by the southern wall, standing together …
20    and the other part toward the eastern wall up to the western wall, and there a great body of persea-trees … in number 35; and further …
21    a living persea-tree … a sycamore cut down …
22    and up to the orchard … living trees from the east of the same cistern, persea-trees
23    … on dry land … a magnitude from the heaped-up plot of theirs
24    … attending us the steward of Melas
25    … and to the north up to … of the remaining wall …
26    … cut down in number … other palm-trees, robust, unsawn or sawn
27    … cut down in number … palm-trees 151, and two beams; and toward the …
28    … northern water-wheel standing together, of a sycamore one branch, and another branch …
29    of a sycamore cut down 1, and olive-trees scattered, living, 7, and
30    other olive-trees cut down two, a living mulberry-fig 1, palm-trees standing together …
