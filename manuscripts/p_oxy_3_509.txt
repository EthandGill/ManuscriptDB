[TRANSLATION]
1   So-and-so to so-and-so, greeting. On the present
2   day I appointed you, by a public
3   instrument through the record-office here, to de-
4   mand from Gaius Memmius Kornou-
5   thos also called Polydeukes, who is in Alexan-
6   dria, the half-share belonging to me of the (sums)
7   owed by him to the heir
8   of my same father, his neph-
9   ew Herakleides also called Amoitas;
10   but I happen to have been fully satisfied
11   as to what is owed to me. I acknowled[ge]
12   that the letter of appointment was made for you
13   only so that the receipt
14   might be issued by you to the public (officials),
15   you taking nothing, and so as to re-
16   lease the mortgage, since I,
17   as set out above, have already re-
18   ceived (payment) according to the autograph
19   documents I issued, and (I acknowledge) that I make no
20   claim about anything at all.
