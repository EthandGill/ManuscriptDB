[TRANSLATION]
1     From Thonis also called Moros, freedman of Harthonis, from Oxyrhynchus
2     city. According to the orders concerning the scrutiny of those coming of age into the thirteen-year-olds, whether
3     they are of the twelve-drachma class from both metropolite parents, there was registered in the quarter of Temgenouthis the son of my deceased friend Ischyrion son of Sibas also called D…s son of Ischyrion also called Sibas, whose mother is …sarous, from the same city, the son Harpokration also called Ischyrion, whose mother is Sarapias daughter of Sarapion, having come of age into the thirteen-year-olds
4     in the past 28th year; whence, having presented myself for his scrutiny, I declare that he is [of the twelve-drachma class and that] his
5     father Ischyrion has died …
6     being of the twelve-drachma class through the poll-tax of the
7     10th year of the deified Aelius Antoninus, of the same quarter, and that the father of the mother
8     of the said person, Sarapion son of Sarapion, has died, formerly being of the twelve-drachma class through the poll-tax of the quarter of the amphodarch-less district; and
9     I swear the fortune of the Emperor Commodus
10    Antoninus Caesar the lord
11    that I have not lied. Year 29 of the Emperor
12    Caesar Marcus Aurelius Commodus
13    Antoninus Pius, Fortunate,
14    Augustus, Armeniacus, Medicus,
15    Parthicus, Sarmaticus, Germanicus,
16    Greatest, Britannicus, Mecheir … .
17    I, Thonis also called Moros, freedman of Harthonis,
18    have submitted and sworn
19    the oath.
