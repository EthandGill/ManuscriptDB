[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most glorious, in the ninth year,
2     Mecheir the nineteenth, of the fourteenth indiction.
3     To the Aureliae Amalleet and Herais, full sisters,
4     adopted daughters of Anoubion of pious memory,
5     from Aurelius Theodosius son of Petros, subscribing below, from the
6     same city. I, the aforewritten Theodosius, acknowledge
7     that I have, voluntarily and of my own free will, leased from you
8     for as long a period as I shall hold it, reckoned from the new moon
9     of the coming month Phamenoth of the present fourteenth indiction, the half (and) quarter
10    share belonging to you of a whole house, of whatever disposition it is, with all
11    the appurtenance and right of whatever kind belonging to it,
12    both contractual and automatic, and with all its whole
13    extent, situated in this (city) of the Hermopolitans, in the quarter
14    of the East City, for my use and habitation, at a rent
15    for the same half (and) quarter share, each year,
16    of twenty-one carats of gold by the public standard, making 21 carats of gold,
17    and I shall pay you the rent at the end of each year without delay; and after whatever period you wish to hold it, I shall hand over to you this
18    same half (and) quarter share of the aforesaid house
19    with doors and keys and every right, as I received it.
20    The lease is valid and secure, and being formally questioned I gave my consent.
21    I, Aurelius Theodosius son of Petros, from the city of Hermes, the aforewritten, have leased as set forth above.
22    I, Aurelius Kyros son of Proklos, from Hermoupolis, bear witness to the lease, having heard it from the party making it. Aurelius Aloïos … ch…ios,
23    from Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard it from the party making it. Flavius Phaerios son of Pinoution,
24    from Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard it from the party making it.
25    Through me, Menas the notary, it was written, Mecheir 19, of the 14th indiction.
