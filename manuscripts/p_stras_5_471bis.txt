[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Cethenus the most illustrious, Epeiph 22, 14th indiction.
2     To the (legal) right of the holy church of the Hermopolites, called
3     Anastasia, through Serenus the most pious presbyter and steward (oikonomos)
4     of the same holy church, from Aurelius Horouonchios son of Kollouthos,
5     his mother being Nonna, a fine-pottery-maker (leptokerameus) from the same city.
6     I acknowledge that I have voluntarily and of my own choice leased from
7     your piety, for whatever time you wish, reckoned from the new moon
8     of the month Thoth of the present fourteenth indiction,
9     the half share belonging to the (legal) right of the same holy church
10    of a whole house of whatever condition it is, with the ground-floor room and
11    all appurtenances and rights, in common with me on account of the
12    remaining half share, making up the whole house,
13    situated in the same Hermopolites, in the quarter of Polis
14    Libos (West), for my dwelling and use, at a rent for the same
15    half share each year of three gold keratia, which
16    rent I shall pay you at the close of each year without delay; and whenever you wish to have (it back) I shall hand over to you the same
17    half share of the house with the doors set upon it and
18    keys, in the same condition as I received it. The lease is valid
19    and secure, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Horouonchios son of Kollouthos, the aforesaid,
20    have leased as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Pinoution son of Kyros, from Hermopolis, having been requested, wrote for him
21    since he does not know [letters]. I, Aurelius Phoibammon son of Athanasios, from Hermopolis, witness the
22    lease, having heard (it) from the party who made it. I, Aurelius Kollouthos son of Phoibammon,
23    from Hermopolis, witness the lease, having heard (it) from the party who made it.
24    Through me, Pinoution, it was written. Phaophi, month, 22, indiction 14.
25    Lease of Horouonchios son of Kollouthos, fine-pottery-maker …
