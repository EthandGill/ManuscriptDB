[TRANSLATION]
1     106
2     [To Valerius] Ammonianus also called Gerontios, curator (logistes) of the Oxyrhynchite,
3     [from Aurelius] Sarapion son of Herodotos, from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, public physician.
4     [I was instructed] yesterday, which was Mecheir 26, from a petition [submitted] to you by Valerius Nundinarius,
5     … so as to go to his farmstead P…eio and inspect the condition of the [beaten] … and … of Moueis the guard, and to report to you in writing. Whence, having come to the farmstead, I inspected the same Moueis lying bedridden, having on the front of the head an incision with laying-bare
6     [of the] bone, and on the crown two wounds with laying-bare of the bone, and below these wounds
7     … on the right side of the head and on the left temple … of swelling, and on the …
8     [of the] left ear a swelling with bruising, and on the right shoulder-[blade] and shoulder a swelling with bruising,
9     [and] on the right hand, on the greatest of the fingers, a blow with swelling, and on the forearm of the right
10    hand a swelling with bruising, and on the left thigh a wound … and above the knee a wound,
11    and on the right thigh two wounds, from end to end, and on [all] the left side a wound; whence I make my report.
12    Year 10 [and] 8 of our lords … Constantine and Licinius Augusti, in the consulship
13    of Caecinius Sabinus and Vettius Rufinus, the most illustrious, Mecheir [27].
14    I, Aurelius Sarapion, have submitted (this), reporting as aforesaid.
