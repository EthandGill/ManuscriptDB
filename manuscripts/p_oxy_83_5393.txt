[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master, the greatest benefactor,
2     Flavius Mauricius Novus Tiberius the eternal Augustus and Imperator, in the year
3     ninth, in the consulship of the same most pious master of ours, year 8, Tybi 14, of the 9th indiction.
4     To the Flavii Praeiecta and Apion her son, the most magnificent
5     consuls, landholders also here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,
6     through Menas, servant, who asks the formal question and furnishes to
7     his own masters, the same all-praiseworthy persons, the
8     action and liability, Aurelius Phileas son of Paleous, his mother being
9     … oe, from the hamlet of Aspidas Borrinou of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
10    [belonging] to your Magnificence, a registered cultivator of it,
11    [greeting. Since] need has now arisen for the landholding
12    water-wheel under me [called] "That of … uetos," which draws water for arable
13    [land, of one axle,] having come up to the city, I asked your
14    [Magnificence to] order that the same axle be provided to me.
15    [And at once] your Magnificence, making provision for the
16    [maintenance] of her own affairs,
17    [this (axle),] purchased by me in the fields, and the price for it
18    [reckoned] to me on my accounts, a new,
19    [suitable] watering (axle), satisfactory, I received in fulfilment [of all the mechanical] implements on this present
20    [day, which is Tybi] the fourteenth of the [present]
21    [ninth indiction, of the water-supply of the crops] of the, God willing, [tenth indiction,]
22    [the same axle] serving [the irrigations]
23    [for] a seven-year [period, and the] old [axle having been given by me]
24    [to the doorkeeper; and for] proof [of the receipt of the same axle]
25    [I have made this] handwritten deed …
26    Handwritten deed of Phileas [son of Paleous] …
