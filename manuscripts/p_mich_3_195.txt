[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fifth year of Imperator Caesar
2     Trajan Hadrian Augustus, in the month Gorp[iaios]
3     on the seventeenth = Epeiph 17, in Bakchias of the Herakleides
4     division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges
5     Hareotes son of Alexas, about 60 years old, scar on the left foot,
6     to Horos son of Horos, about 48 years old, scar on the right eyebrow, that he,
7     the acknowledging party, has received from him sixteen artabas of wheat
8     by the six-choinix measure, being the rent
9     of the present fifth year of Hadrian Caesar
10    the lord, of (the land) which he cultivates belonging to him around the aforesaid village, an allotment of four and
11    a half arouras, Horos having with himself the seed
12    for the sowing of the coming sixth year of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
13    four artabas of wheat by the
14    aforesaid measure, the lease of the allotment remaining valid
15    on all the terms it contains. Subscribers:
16    for the acknowledging party, Isidoros son of Eubios, 45 years old, scar on the right side of the neck,
17    and for the other, Maron son of Maron, 24 years old, scar on the left foot.
18    I, Haryotes son of Alexas, acknowledge that I have received from
19    Horos sixteen artabas of wheat by the six-choinix measure, being the rent of the present
20    year of (the land) which I cultivate of mine around Bakchias, an allotment
21    of four and a half arouras, he himself having the
22    seed for the sowing of the coming year,
23    the lease remaining valid as
24    is set forth. Isidoros son of Eubios wrote on his behalf, he not
25    knowing letters. Horos son of Horos: the receipt has been made out to me as is set forth.
26    Maron son of Maron wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
27    It has been entered through the record-office in Bakchias.
