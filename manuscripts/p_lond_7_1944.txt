[TRANSLATION]
1     Python to Zenon, greetings. As you wrote to me, I [send forward] to Theodoros
2     the [funds] from you for pack-animals, drachmas 1000, because it is not yet [possible] to give Ammonion to him. But I have sent Leon [carrying] a letter from Amyntas to Apollonios. You would therefore do [me] a favor by giving
3     at a convenient time and eagerly, so that he may arrange a receipt [to inscribe]
4     each one. But if not, however it seems to him, so that the documents
5     be given to us. And you write of whatever you need. [Farewell].
6     Python the banker to Zenon.
7     of which he gave to Theodoros
8     for the joint-purchase of the
9     pack-animals drachmas 1000. Year 29,
10    of Xanthicos 3
