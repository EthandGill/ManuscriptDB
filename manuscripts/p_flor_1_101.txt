1     … ei …
2     of Caesar [the] lord's fifty-four arouras;
3     they amount to [fifty-four] arouras, six arouras each; and you will give us for wages
4     [of the harvest of] each aroura of wheat half [and] a third; there becomes each aroura of wheat 1/2 [and] 1/3;
5     and you will measure out to us after the harvest of the aforesaid [arouras] the aforementioned wages, on condition that you gather up
6     you, [Eudaemon, the] sheaves; and you will provide us water
7     for drinking [until] we leave from the harvest of the same arouras; on condition that, if [ever] one of us should be idle, we shall necessarily set [another] in his stead
8     on the same [day]. But the aforementioned wheat you will measure out to us by measure
9     [by the Athenian sixth-] measure, and you will give us on the last day one [jar] of beer. But we shall necessarily begin the harvest
10    on the 30th [of] the present month Pharmouthi of the present [tenth year]. And concerning what shall be found in the sown [field] you will measure out to us, [as aforesaid, of each aroura] of wheat 1/2 [and] 1/3. [Of the tenth year]
