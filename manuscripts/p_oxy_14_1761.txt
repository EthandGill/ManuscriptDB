[TRANSLATION]
1     [Callirrhoe] to [Serapias]
2     to the mistress [greetings]. I make your obeisance every day beside
3     the lord Sarapis.
4     Since you departed we long for you
5     wishing to see you. Greet Thermouthin and Heliada and Plution [and]
6     Aphrodite and [Nemesian]. They greet [you]
7     Karabos and Harpocration and those in the house
8     all. I pray that you prosper.
9     Deliver
10    to Serapias
11    from Callirrhoe.
