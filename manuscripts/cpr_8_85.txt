[TRANSLATION]
1     [Re]cord of the items given from various e[states to the most renowned]
2     Abd-er-rahman the amir, through Abba Kyros the ag[ent],
3     as follows:
4     from the estate of Ares: of bread, 1 centenarium and 20 pounds; of oil, sextarii …
5     from the estate of Ophis: of bread, 2½ centenaria, and of cumin …
6     [fr]om the estate of Sinarchebis: of bread, 1½ centenaria, and of oil, sextarii …
7     [from the] estate of Pouampoimenis: of bread, 6 centenaria; of poor-quality oil, 3 sextarii; of oil, 5½
8     [from] the estate of Sinkere: of bread, 2 centenaria and 20 pounds; of wine, 10 knidia; of mustard, 1/6 of an artaba
9     [from] the estate of Tlethmis: of wood, 10 centenaria; of wine, 10 knidia; of vinegar, 9 sextarii; eggs, 36
10    [from] the estate of Telaeis: of wood, 5 centenaria; of wine, 12 knidia; of vinegar, 10 sextarii; eggs, 13
11    [from the estate of T]emseu Moron: … of oil, sextarii …
12    from the estate of Sebaemphis: of wood, 10 centenaria
13    from the estate of Lenaios: of wood, 5½ centenaria
14    from the estate of Ibion Sesembythis: of wood, 15 centenaria, and of wine, 6 knidia, and of vinegar, 10 sextarii, and of dates, ½ + 1/4 of an artaba, and of grapes, 1/3 of an artaba, [and] eggs, 16
15    from the hamlet of Neilos: of wood, 5½ centenaria
16    from the estate of Timonthis: of bread, 2 centenaria
17    from the hamlet of Krere: of salt-fish, 12 knidia; of fish-sauce, 18 sextarii
18    from the hamlet of Peros: of fish-sauce, 12 sextarii
19    from the estate of Bousiris: of onions, 3 artabas, and of oil, 11 sextarii; of poor-quality oil, 3 sextarii
20    from the hamlet of Dikaiarchos: of salt-fish, 1 kollathon and 1 lakon
21    from the estate of Sempitesis: … of vinegar, 5 sextarii
22    total: of radish-oil, 40 sextarii; of poor-quality (oil), 6 sextarii; of fish-sauce, 30 sextarii; of vinegar, 34 sextarii; of honey, 15 sextarii; and
23    of wood, 51 centenaria; (wine) 38 knidia; of salt-fish, 5 kollatha; eggs, 110; of mustard, 1 + 1/12 of an artaba
24    of cumin, 1½ mattia
