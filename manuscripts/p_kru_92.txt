[TRANSLATION]
1     … [an] only-begotten (monogenes) son; we donate (dorizein) him
2     [into the] holy monastery (monasterion), the holy martyr (martyros), that he may become
3     a servant (chauon) in every servitude (slavery), lawfully, in the holy place (topos),
4     the holy Abba Phoibammon, this one whose name we have revealed
5     many times in this deed of donation (doreastikon); but now to you, you
6     the holy martyr (martyros), with his stewards (oikonomoi) and his superiors (proestotes),
7     those who are in it now, in its stewardship (oikonomia), Apa Syros
8     the deacon (diakonos) and this superior (proestos) and the steward (oikonomos) of the holy martyr (martyros),
9     you who shall become and be lords of this little child,
10    this one who is called Markos son of Papas, with those who shall come
11    after you in the holy monastery (monasterion), from now
12    forever, that he may be for you a servant (chauon) in every servitude (slavery),
13    and like all the children of the monastery (monasterion), that he may be
14    under your subjection (hypotage), with the recitation of his holy office of lamps (lychnikon)
15    and his offering (prosphora) — be blessed, you too, you superiors (proestotes) of this holy monastery (monasterion), Apa Souros, this one who receives now,
16    with all those who come after him, forever — so that you cause
17    this little only-begotten (monogenes) son, this one who is called Markos,
18    to be in this monastery (monasterion), being a servant (chauon) in his whole life,
19    or rather, if he shall come to the perfection of (full) age (helikia), he shall contribute (syntelein) to you
20    his public tax (demosion) according as you shall agree with him, and according to his ability
21    he shall … what you pay out in the holy place (topos), the holy martyr (martyros),
22    Apa Phoibammon of the mountain of the fortress (kastron) of Djeme, this one whose name we have named many times above, since the law (nomos)
23    commands in this way, that "it is permitted for each one to do this which pleases him in his things, that they be his," and especially the things of God, through you, Papas
24    and Anna his wife — we write to you, you the martyr (martyros),
25    with his superiors (proestotes) and his stewards (oikonomoi), those who are
26    in it now, Apa Souros the deacon (diakonos), with all those who come
27    after you, from now forever — whoever shall dare,
28    one of us, whether brother or sister, or son of one, or son of two,
29    or kinsman or kinsman of a kinsman, or stranger, or man of my house, or
30    on the part of my father, or on the part of my mother, to accuse (enkalein) you, or boundaries (claims), whether in the village
31    or in the city or in the praetorium, or through a magistrate (archon), or
32    through a magistracy (archontikon), or (any) authority (exousia) that is exalted and glorious,
33    and they set up any claim (proselusis) against the holy martyr (martyros)
34    or his monastery (monasterion) or his stewards (oikonomoi)
35    in any manner — and I swear by God the Almighty
36    and by the safety of our lords, the God-protected (theophylaktoi), who rule over us
37    now, and those who shall rule forever — that we keep for you the force
38    of this deed of donation (doreastikon), without transgressing (parabainein) it, without removing it,
39    without shaking it aside the more, but rather dwelling in the confirmation
40    of it through the laws (nomoi) — whoever shall wish to transgress (parabainein) it,
41    first, those very ones shall benefit no one; and second, that one
42    shall give to the account of this fine (prostimon) thirty-six solidi (holokottinoi) of gold
43    from his substance (hypostasis), willingly (?), apart from the bodily correction (diastrophe somatike)
44    which the law (nomos) defines, and they shall apply compulsion (anagke) to him, and he shall come in
45    and be bound to this written deed of donation (doreastikon), an eternal written (deed); clear it is,
46    that — which may not happen — he shall become a stranger to the holy invocation
47    of the Christians, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
48    and this holy martyr (martyros) shall take vengeance on him at his resurrection (anastasis).
49    For your security, we established this deed of donation (doreastikon) for you, secured, in force,
50    in every place where it shall be produced before any authority (exousia)
51    whatever, and they shall question us; we acknowledge (homologein) according to our own request
52    of ourselves; one read it, being established through the lawyer (nomikos); we heard it;
53    it pleased us; we secured it with a subscriber (hypographeus) and witnesses,
54    trustworthy ones; we let it go forth according to the rule (akolouthia) of our laws (nomoi).
55    We, Souai son of Phebamou, and Petros son of Epiphane, we bear witness. I, Isak, wrote on behalf of the parties.
56    I, Isak son of Ananias, the least, presbyter, I am a witness.
57    We, Klautes son of Mathias, and Amone son of Senouthe, in the fortress (kastron) of Djeme, we bear witness. I, Isak, wrote.
