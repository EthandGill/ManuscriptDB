[TRANSLATION]
1     Philinus to Zenon
2     greeting. Well would
3     it be if you were well; I am well
4     and so am I. Well
5     would you do, if it is convenient for you, [if you send] to the
6     new [market] …
7     of wheat artabas 1.
8     And the price write
9     to us, in order that we might send [it] to you.
10    Farewell.
11    Year 35, Tybi 23. To Zenon.
12    Philinus, [regarding] wheat, artabas 1.
