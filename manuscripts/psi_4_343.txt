[TRANSLATION]
1     Nicaeus to Zenon greetings.
2     Do not be amazed at Nicolaus
3     having departed before or the discourse
4     to compose; for on account of Themistocles being at the [reasoning]
5     concerning servants we were occupied with these very same things.
6     But nevertheless I am appointed
7     to him before … to compose. So it concerns me; as if
8     and you being present the discourse will be composed.
9     Farewell. Year 30, Choiach 11.
10    To Zenon
