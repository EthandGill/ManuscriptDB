[TRANSLATION]
1   Heron also called Petosiris and partners,
2   requisitioners of assessments, (to) Tikos son of Pamonthes,
3   (grand)son of Harbechis. We have received from you, for the assessment,
4   in base (coin), nine drachmas, two-and-a-half obols, 2 chalkoi
5   — total, base, 9 dr. 2½ ob. 2 chalkoi. Year 16 of Hadrian Caesar
6   the lord, Pachon 22.
