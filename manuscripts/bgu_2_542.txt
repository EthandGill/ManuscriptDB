[TRANSLATION]
1     … Anoubion (acknowledges that he has received) from Valeria Sabina …
2     … of the record-office of Athenas (Kome) of the Themistos [division] …
3     … of the arouras … on the south a canal,
4     … and on the south the grain-lands of Herais, on the east the grain-lands of Herodaine,
5     … of the fourth (year?), on the south a canal, … the olive-yard of Petronianus, on the north
6     … and of others an olive-yard, and of the two arouras existing, in which is a tower-fort … on the north the olive-yard of the aforementioned Isiaine, on the west (the olive-yard) of Apolinarius and of others;
7     … royal land; and that Valeria Sabina also called Ptollarous has received from Anoubion … me … of the cession in silver, one thousand eight hundred drachmas, of which … into the stated (account) … one thousand six hundred silver drachmas, and now the remaining two hundred drachmas forthwith by hand
8     [out of the house. That Anoubion shall therefore hold and] possess the arouras ceded to him … [and] the produce from them … carry off the surplus for his own use and have authority to sell to others and to administer
9     [and to do concerning] them whatever he may choose, against which (arouras) neither Valeria Sabina also called (Ptollarous) shall proceed
10    [nor anyone else] on her behalf, but everyone who shall proceed against her she and the guarantor
11    … Apolinarius shall remove forthwith at their own expense, the guarantee following upon them throughout; and they shall furnish the same aforesaid arouras free from …
12    [and from public] dues, all of them, up to the past fourth year and the current year itself, and from
13    [private claims] and every adverse claim throughout, or else, apart from the aforesaid remaining valid, that Valeria Sabina also called Ptollarous shall pay to Anoubion both the cession-capital which he gave as aforesaid
14    [with] half as much again, and the damages and the expenses and the fixed penalty as if by judgment,
15    Anoubion being in no way prejudiced against Valeria Sabina also called Ptollarous by other lawful means. He requests (this). [In the fifth year of] Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus and of Imperator
16    Caesar Lucius Aurelius Verus Augustus, Mecheir, the twenty-sixth.
