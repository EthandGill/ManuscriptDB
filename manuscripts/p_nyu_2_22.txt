[TRANSLATION]
1     After leaving the city I will arrive at Takona and go up to the monastery of the holy
2     George and I will lodge [there]; and I sought Mousai[us], child of Staurophanis, for the sake of the payment of what [was entrusted] …
3     to him; and I found [whe]re he fled, and behold also his letter of those [sent] to me by him
4     after which he fled, I se[nt] to you so that you might read it too, and [regarding] the lord Bacchus the scholasticus; you know
5     for that to him I replied to him and I have his guarantee; and behold I wrote one letter to the same
6     lord Bacchus so that he might come to Takona and inspect his property; hold therefore
7     the letter of Mousaeus and send it to me, so that I might keep it as evidence.
8     to my God-protected master [and] brother … Phoibammon 25 … … to the chartulary George, deacon …
