[TRANSLATION]
1     … those exacting the joint (dues), and that Artemidoros, the (man) hateful to the gods,
2     had struck off (from the account), out of what we had measured out of the last half-artaba into the joint (dues), twenty-five artabas of wheat. And
3     for the last half-artaba nothing did
4     Ptolemaios son of Harpsaleis accept from us, except
5     for 25 artabas, on account of your not having taken a receipt-token, for
6     you put everything as a side-matter. And when they wished to seize the cattle as a pledge, we did not submit to give in to the violence (done) to him
7     after he had brought against himself sufficient (persons),
8     and he was unwilling to hold off until you should arrive in good health. But by chance,
9     Demetrios having arrived in the village, not even so did they let up, but he is persuaded
10    to have (them) seized on the spot; but when I extricated the pledge, they let up into
11    other villages. Know that I did not give in to
12    the violence until you should arrive as
13    quickly as possible. And concerning the matters in the Herakleopolite (nome) which you enjoined me, I have accomplished, I [and]
14    Ptolemaios. All those in the house send their regards to you
15    and your sister and Paraibates [and]
16    Phileas and Demetria the younger. And concerning the
17    allotment, it is safe from the rain. Take care
18    also of yourself, so that we may greet you in good health
19    as quickly as possible. Farewell. Year 2, Choiak 25.
20    diok(…)… 500 drachmas.
