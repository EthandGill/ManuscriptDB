[TRANSLATION]
1     In the [reig]n of our most divine master Flavius
2     [Justi]nian the eternal Augustus, Emperor,
3     in the [t]hirteenth [year] after the consulship of Flavius
4     [Apion] the most glorious, on the first of Pharmouthi, in the 3rd indiction.
5     To [Flav]ius Josephios son of Petros, the most admirable, from
6     [the city of the Her]mopolites, from Flavius Ammonios son of Kolluthos
7     subscribing [hereafter], from the soldiers, from the same
8     [cit]y. I acknowledge willingly and of my own choice
9     that I have leased from you, for whatever time you wish, reckoned
10    from the present day, which is Pharmouthi
11    the first of the present third indiction, one bed[room]
12    on the first floor facing south, with [th]e
13    share falling to it of the well and the inner court
14    and the entrance and exit and the way up and the way down and
15    the remaining appurtenances and rights, from the
16    share falling to you of an entire house situated
17    in the same (city) of the Hermopolites, in the East ward
18    of the C[i]ty, for my dwelling and use, at a rent
19    for the same bedroom, per year, of three and a half gold
20    carats, which rent I shall pay you at the close
21    of each year without delay, and whenever you should wish
22    to have (it), I shall hand over to you the same bedroom with [its do]or
23    and key as I received it. The lease is valid and
24    secure, and on being formally questioned I gave my assent. Flavius Ammonios
25    son of Kolluthos, the aforesaid, have leased as set forth above. Flavius Ai…kos son of Isakios
26    from the city of the Hermopolites, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the contracting party.
27    Aurelius Men…ko…ios from the city of the Hermopolites, I bear witness to the lease,
28    having h[eard (it) from the contracting party.] Flavius son of Aurelius, I bear witness to the lease.
29    To [Jos]ephios son of Petros, from Ammonios son of Kolluthos, from the city of the Her[mopolites]
