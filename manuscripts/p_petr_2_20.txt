[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Hephaestio]n. If you are well and those whom you prefer [are well], it would be good;
2     k... . When Theogenes sailed up, when [Antick]les sailed up, to Apollonius the grain-buyer, [that] the Zephyron was being sent [...]
3     While sailing [from] where, and he himself having sailed down to Memp[his]
4     Having fallen before Apollonius, whom I inquired about …
5     Having heard in Theogenes' sailing [that] the Zephyron was being sent …
6     I sailed down to Memphis. And when I was inquiring
7     Of the [men] beside him, [whether] you had made any agreement with him …
8     According to the memorandum which we left for you, they said not. From there, having approached Euphron,
9     And having reminded ... [them] of the letter which you wrote
10    I requested [him to stand with me. But] he did nothing [...]
11    It happened [to be held] up concerning the Zephyron. I request
12    Therefore you to take care concerning me.
13    For not reasonable is [it for this] for me having [it] already in hope unconcerned
14    And even of others ... to be thus ...
