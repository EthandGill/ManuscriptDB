1     In the seventh year of Imperator Caesar Vespasianus
2     Augustus, in the month Loios 25, Pauni 25, in Soknopaiou Nesos of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite
3     nome. There acknowledge: Satabous son of Panephrymmis, about
4     65 years old, with a scar on the left eyebrow, and his son Satabous,
5     about 35 years old, with a scar on the middle of the forehead, to Pisais son of Stotoetis,

8     son of Onnophris, about 20 years old, with a scar on the middle of the forehead, the
9     eight ferrymen of the village of Soknopaiou Nesos
10    of the present year, that the acknowledging parties have received in full from them
