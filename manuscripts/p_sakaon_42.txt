[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Sabinia]nus, the most eminent prefect of Mercurian Egypt,
2     [from S]akaon and Esoures and Areion, from the village of Theadel[phia.] We petitioned your Excellency, lord, in a plain statement, that, since we do not receive water for the irrigation of the lands — because also
3     the village is short of water, so that from this it has become weak — while paying for so many unwatered lands for a long time, we have come to poverty, asking to obtain your benefaction.
4     You [ordered] the embankment-overseers, together with an official and the
5     [prae]positus of the district, to be present on the spot
6     [and] to inspect the situation, and they, coming down, found
7     [us] not receiving the water, not only this year
8     [but] for a long time, because the [village] lies much lower.
9     We ask therefore your manliness to bene[fit us] according to the laws and the orders, prefect,
10    [of you and] other magistrates, that the weaker villages
11    be joined to the well-favored villages, and that we
12    be given a share of the assessments with the well-favored village
13    of the [plain], we mean Hermoupolis, to which also formerly … we were assigned among the decaprotoi.
14    [Since] also with respect to the waters we were originally …
15    …, and from them the contribution should be made,
16    [that is, of the payments of the] taxes of the lands of our village,
17    so that, obtaining this, we may be able in our own place
18    to hold together, and through
19    everything to give thanks to you. Farewell.
