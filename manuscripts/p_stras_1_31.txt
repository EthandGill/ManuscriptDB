[TRANSLATION]
1     … (son) of …, also called Apollonios, son of Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios, and of Apollo[nio]s the other son, and of … it is, on the [part] toward the south
2     [and] east, of floor-area 78 cubits, and of entrance and ex[it,] all held in common in equal shares: drachmas 36.
3     … (son) of a fatherless man, his mother being Stotoetis: half a share of a house, drachmas 38.
4     (Property) of [Apol]lonios son of Hermaiskos son of Hermaiskos, formerly a sample-collector:
5     … a share of a house, drachmas 37.
6     … (of) …ytios son of Titakios son of Tyrannos: an eighth share of a house, drachmas 60.
7     Of [the sam]e: of a collapsed house, drachmas 24.
8     Of [the sa]me, in the name of his mother Apollonia daughter of Didy[m]e: a quarter share of a house and of a courtyard joined to one another,
9     [which,] by the inspection of the boundary-survey, were added on: drachmas 8.
10    Total: drachmas 313.
11    And in the same quarter, of a common court: drachmas 4.
12    Formerly (property) of Kalpurnius Theon, formerly weigher of the Letopolite nome:
13    a new house and courtyard and court, of which those indicated by the village-scribe: drachmas 100,
14    and those added on by the inspection of the boundary-survey: drachmas 20; total drachmas 120.
15    … 4.
16    Total for the quarter: drachmas 618.
17    Total: drachmas 307.
18    (Of) Dionysios of Topoi: (property) of Arabion son of D[io]nysios son of Arabion, of a [n]ew house
19    and court and entrance and exit: drachmas 50.
20    (Of) Publius Aelius Isidoros also called Maximus: a third share of an eighth share
21    of a two-storied house and courtyard and court: drachmas [1]2.
22    (Of) Ammonios son of Ammonios: an eighth share of a house and of another house: drachmas 12.
23    [Total:] drachmas 74.
