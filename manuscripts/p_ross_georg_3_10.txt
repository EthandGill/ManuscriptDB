[TRANSLATION]
1     To my very sweetest brother Paphnoutios,
2     from your brother Hierax, augustalis
3     of the numerus of the Quintani, greeting.
4     Before all else I pray to the all-merciful God that,
5     while you are in health and good cheer, you may receive my greeting. Many greetings to Harphat,
6     many greetings to Gounthos the son of my sister Sophia; I greet my son Ph…
7     and Psois and Tao the brother of my wife,
8     many greetings to Pasen the elder and … [Ne]phrimmin the wife of Paphnoutios and her children.
9     We heard here that Phyph has already married Helen.
10    [Many] greetings to the freedwoman Imi and [the]
11    children of his(?), many greetings to Anne the village-headman and Phileas the village-headman and R…
12    … and to your freedwoman and all
13    those in the village of Tieio. Many greetings to
14    those in the village of Psosengyphto, each by name; many greetings to Isakos
15    and Atillis his brother, of the village of Psosengypto, and to all those in the village. With God's help,
16    behold, I am also on furlough from the augustales, and
17    after the Passover I shall come to you presently,
18    [if] God [wills]. Be so kind, then, even if to send me one letter about your health; do not put on airs; the one who brings you the letter — receive him in my stead. This favor
19    do for me.
20    I pray for your health
21    for many years.
22    Deliver the letter to Paphnoutios and Harph[at] of the village [of Tieio, from] Pserax, augustalis on furlough
23    of the numerus of the Quintani.
