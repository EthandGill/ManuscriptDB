[TRANSLATION]
1     Distributed wheat of the harvest of the past
2     27th year of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
3     Antoninus Caesar the lord
4     through grain-collectors of the middle toparchia of Pakerkh places from the plot
5     of Pasion Achillion the gymnasiarch
6     to the city through his plot: artabae one hundred thirteen and one-half, total wheat artabae 113½
7     Amm[o]nius I have marked
8     Distributed wheat of the harvest of the past
9     27th year of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
10    Antoninus Caesar the lord
11    through grain-collectors of the middle toparchia of Pakerkh places from
12    the own plot of Tho[o]nius of Panarus
13    to the grain-collectors of middle Pakerkh: artaba one choinices eight,
14    total artaba 1 choinices 8. Ammonius I have marked.
