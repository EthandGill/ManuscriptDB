[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fourth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, on the [seventeen]th of the month Apell[aios], on the seventeenth of Ph[a]ophi, in Dionysias which is by the [copper-mines]
2     of the Themistos division of the Arsinoite nome. [Thermouthis] leased,
3     daughter of Stotoetis, about fifty years old, with a scar on the right eyebrow, w[ith as guardian]
4     her own son Polydeukes, son of Euphrosynos, about thir[ty] years old …
5     with a scar on the left cheek, to Soterichos, son of Petesouchos, about … year[s old]
6     with a scar on the middle of the nose, and to his son Petesouchos, about … years old, [with a sc]ar on the forehead on the left, the two Persians of the epigo[ne, of one another]
7     sureties for repayment, the (land) belonging to her
8     around the copper-mi[nes, formerly]
9     the holding of Kephalon, of two arourai or however many there may be …
10    for two years from the present fourth year of Tiberius [Caesar],
11    on (terms of) a third part for the lessees of the produce yielded
12    each year from the holding, both crops and after-crops, [it being]
13    clean of public dues and of every kind; let it not be permitted [to the les]sees to abandon the lease wi[th]in the period, and [the] yearly works of all the holding, both the dyke-works [and irrigations]
14    and weedings and the other agricultural labor [let] the lessees [perfo]rm in season at [their own expense]
15    and sowing each year, with whatever (seed) Thermouthis chooses, sh[e herself]
16    furnishing the two-thirds part of the (seed) [sown] into the land,
17    the lessees furnishing the remainder …
18    providing for themselves … …
19    toward the lessee[s] …
20    … of those choosing …
21    there shall be each year … [hol]ding's part, and the les[sees] … [ and after]
22    the period let the lessees hand over …
23    of dry land, of grass, of marsh-growth all, and of rush and reed …
24    and if the lessee transgresses any of the aforesaid [things] …
25    … Thermouthis …
