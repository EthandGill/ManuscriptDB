[TRANSLATION]
1     Philonides the priest of Asklepios, to Zenōn greetings. You do well if you are well; we [are] well also. Ptolemaios the one carrying to you [the letter] is a friend to us [and]
2     necessary. Well then you would do in helping [with him if] he should ask you.
3     This then doing you would gratify both me and the god. Write then to us about whatever [you might wish]
4     as being about to be for you. Be well. Tybi 24.
5     To Zenōn.
6     Philonides priest
7     of Asklepios about Ptolemaios.
8     Year 28, Peritios 19.
