1     George
2     Elisaeus of Peter from Rem …
3     to Mizael and Rebecca
4     sons of Elisaeus [and] other of Patermouth a fine-potter.

7     I have released [a claim] and concerning approach.
8     Mizael and Rebecca sons of Elisaeus of Patermouth toward George
9     Elisaeus of Peter, that we have released [claims] toward you concerning the security of the coins two

13    Jonathan of John.
14    Esdra of Isaac.
