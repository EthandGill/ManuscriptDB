[TRANSLATION]
1     to Serapion, royal secretary of Arsinoe, Heracleides
2     of [the] division
3     from Tawetis [daughter] of Harpagathus from village Soknopaiou
4     Nese, with [my] guardian [my] kinsman Stotoetis [son] of Stotoetis, whom I registered in the past sixth year of Antoninus
5     Caesar the lord, camels
6     five I sold in the past month of Tybi to Harpagathe [son] of Satabus from the village,
7     camels three, and to Satabbutis [son] of Satabus likewise from
8     the village the remaining camels two. Since then I do not have
9     other camels, I submit the accounting. Year seven
10    of Antoninus Caesar
11    of the lord, Mecheir 5
