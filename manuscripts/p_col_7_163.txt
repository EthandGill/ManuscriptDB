1     Mesore 5. Brought Valerius [son] of Antio[urius in] [harbor] of Leucogios on behalf of [seventh indiction]
2     of villagers of Karanidos of clean wheat artabas thirty-eight and half, artabas 38 1/2 [alone].
3     Melas the grain-measurer [has] measured.
4     Similarly 9. To Amatai the grain-receiver artabas 13 [and] 1/3.
5     Seuthas I have signed.
6     Mesore 27. Brought Aeion [son] of Sarapion in harbor of the city on behalf of [the] seventh
7     indiction of villagers of Karanidos of clean wheat artabas twenty, artabas 20 alone[.]
8     Elias through me Paul [I have signed].
