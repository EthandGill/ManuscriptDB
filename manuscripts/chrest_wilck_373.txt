[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Iason, son of Abok, his mother being Thermouthis,
2     about 25 years old, with a scar on his left shoulder, a foreigner, from
3     the village of Philadelphia in the Arsinoite nome, to Aurelius
4     Hatres, his brother by the same mother and the same father, greeting.
5     I acknowledge that I have received from you the share falling to me of the things left by our father,
6     the bronze vessels and the clothes and the four-footed beasts and
7     the wooden objects and all kinds (of goods), in the presence of the mediators Neilos son of Psakatarios and Lemesas from the same village,
8     apart from the share of the mountain falling to me of the
9     aforesaid one (item). And it shall remain with you until I come to my own village, and the resulting profits and loss shall be your concern, and I shall not proceed against (you) concerning these very things, nor shall any of my (heirs), nor child of child, because I have received the share falling to me of all kinds (of goods). And this security
10    was written in Philadelphia in the presence
11    of the mediators, secure and valid everywhere
12    when produced; and on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
13    Year [two] of our lord Probus Augustus.
14    I, Aurelius Iason, consent to the things written
15    as set forth. I, Aurelius Dionysios, wrote on his behalf,
16    he being illiterate.
