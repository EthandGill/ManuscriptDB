[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious
2     master Flavius Justinian the
3     eternal Augustus and Emperor, year 27,
4     in the 12th (year) after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the
5     most illustrious, Epeiph 22 of the 1st indiction, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites.
6     To Flavia Gabrielia the most glorious and most eminent
7     patrician, who has obtained the office of logistes and
8     presidency and patronage of this
9     splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, on behalf of
10    the household of the one of revered memory
11    Timagenes, in the fortunate office of logistes
12    of the second indiction, through you the venerable
13    Christophoros her deputy,
14    (from) Aurelius Timotheos, water-supplier
15    of the public bath of the same city,
16    son of Paulos. I acknowledge that I have received
17    from your glory hereby
18    already on account of my wage on behalf of the
19    first installment of the fortunate office of logistes
20    of the second indiction two solidi
21    of gold by the private standard, also …, through
22    the most admirable Ioannes its assayer,
23    total: 2 solidi of gold by the private standard, and for the security of
24    your glory I have made this for her,
25    the receipt, which is valid, written in single copy,
26    and on being asked I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Timotheos son
27    of Paulos, the aforewritten, have made this
28    receipt, having received the same two solidi by the private standard
29    on account of my wage of the first installment of the office of logistes
30    of the second indiction, and everything is satisfactory to me as aforesaid. …
31    … having been asked, I wrote on his behalf since he is illiterate.
32    Receipt made by Timotheos the water-supplier, son of Paulos,
33    for wage of 2 gold solidi by the private standard.
