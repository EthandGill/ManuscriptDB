[TRANSLATION]
1     [ ] 643 (= ΧΜΓ, a Christian symbol)
2     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Tiberius
3     Mauricius the eternal Augustus emperor, in the
4     first year, Pauni the twenty-ninth, of the second indiction, in Antinoo-
5     polis the most illustrious.
6     These counterpart settlements they set down and make
7     toward one another willingly and persuaded, without all deceit
8     and force: on the one part Aurelius John,
9     son of Jacob, his mother being Tapia, soldier of the unit of Syene,
10    and on the other part Aurelius Patermouthis son of Menas, his mother being
11    Tsia, sailor from the same city of the Syenites, making
12    the negotiations and acting with authority and settling on his own
13    authority, both on his own behalf and on behalf of K[a]kos the most noble,
14    his own wife, and sister of the aforewritten John,
15    both now residing here in the (city) of the Antinoites.
16    And it is as set out below. Jacob, now of blessed
17    memory, father of the aforewritten John,
18    and father-in-law of the aforewritten Patermouthis, departed
19    this life, leaving (his estate) to John and Kakos his aforesaid children and heirs. And after
20    his death they divided among themselves all the property of every kind left to them, from the valuable to
21    the least kind, each (taking) the half portion, except for securities
22    of various persons owed to their most blessed
23    father, and concerning which it was resolved that these securities
24    should be (held) in common between them until their realization, and that the
25    gold realized from them be divided among themselves,
26    each (taking) the half portion. And these things having so come about,
27    the aforesaid John brought a suit against the aforewritten
28    Patermouthis concerning a portion of a house which had come to him
29    from a purchase-right from Isakios and Tsone, and furthermore
30    also concerning a half portion of a boat purchased from Jacob
31    his most blessed father-in-law. And many things having been said
32    between them concerning this, in the end, through the mediation of certain friends
33    it was resolved that John should have a quarter portion of the
34    purchased half portion of the same boat, and besides
35    that he should have, also for the inheritance of his father,
36    a quarter portion, and that Patermouthis and Kakos (should have) the
37    other half portion of the same boat, and furthermore also all the purchased
38    portion of the house belonging to them, according to the force of its sale. And of
39    such mediation, that is, settlement, being satisfactory to each
40    of their parts, it reasonably became necessary that there be made by them these present
41    general and comprehensive settlements, through which they acknowledge, and each
42    of their parts acknowledges, that they have first received and obtained
43    in full all that falls to each of their parts both from the inheritance
44    of Jacob the most blessed and from the same boat and the same house,
45    according to the above narration; and thereafter that each
46    of their parts has and shall have no claim against the other on account of the (property) left
47    by the same most blessed Jacob and of the same boat and of the
48    portion of the house, because satisfaction has been made in all things to each part
49    by the other part; and it was resolved that the aforewritten
50    John, at the time of his journey up to his own city, should give back
51    to the aforewritten Patermouthis the sale made by him
52    to the same Jacob of the portion of the same house, that is,
53    that it should be void and stale and invalid, both from the same
54    Jacob and from any person whatsoever, (though) produced anywhere,
55    and that henceforth they should not bring a charge against one another nor ever shall bring a charge, neither
56    through themselves nor through their heirs or successors or those holding by succession,
57    nor through an intermediary or any person whatsoever in the middle, neither in
58    a court or in a higher jurisdiction or outside a court, on account of this
59    matter and of any other cause whatsoever, both pecuniary
60    and criminal. And that they will keep all the aforewritten things,
61    those of each part swore by the holy
62    and consubstantial Trinity and by the victory and continuance of
63    our most pious master Flavius Tiberius Mauricius
64    the eternal Augustus emperor. And if in any way
65    whatsoever either of their parts should transgress all the things written
66    and come contrary to them, he shall in no way be benefited
67    from the attempt of the transgression, but the transgressing part acknowledges
68    to give to the part abiding (by it), by way of penalty, twelve gold
69    solidi, exacted in fact and effect,
70    along with these settlements being thus also valid forever.
71    And that the same John may not bring a suit against the same Patermouthis
72    concerning the gold necklace of Helene his daughter,
73    but that she may have it by right of ownership in perpetuity.
74    I, the aforewritten Patermouthis, acknowledge in addition, making
75    the negotiations and acting with authority both on my own behalf and on behalf of Kakos
76    his wife, that I am ready, at my own risk and from the resource
77    of all my substance, to procure that his
78    free (wife) shall be content and abide perpetually by the force
79    of the present settlements and shall in no way disturb
80    anything of them at all. And as to all the aforewritten things
81    and as to each part and head of them,
82    being formally questioned that these things so hold, to give, to do, to keep,
83    to fulfill, to abide, they acknowledged. Sign: John, soldier of the unit of Syene. I, Aurelius
84    John son of Jacob, soldier of the unit of Syene, the aforewritten, have set down
85    this settlement and obey all the things written
86    on the aforesaid penalty, having sworn also the divine oath as aforesaid
87    and released (it). I, Christophoros son of Victor, … assistant from Antinoopolis, having been requested, wrote
88    on his behalf, since he does not know letters, but he having put with his own
89    hand the three honored crosses. I, Josephis son of Isakios, with God notary from Antinoopolis,
90    witness this settlement, having heard from those who made the deed.
91    I, Flavius Philippus son of John, singularis of the governor's office, witness this
92    settlement, having heard from those who made the deed. I, Kosmas son of Theodoros,
93    with God notary from Antinoopolis, witness this settlement, having heard
94    from those who made the deed.
95    Through me, Dios son of Elias, notary.
