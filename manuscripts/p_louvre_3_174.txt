[TRANSLATION]
1     Theodoros, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite. From a letter
2     written to me by the most illustrious prefect Sempronius Liberalis
3     concerning land leased from the (account of) fallow-land, I have publicly posted a copy, so that all may know what
4     has been ordained. I have signed. Year 19, Epeiph 14.
5     Sempronius Liberalis to the strategoi of the 7 nomes and of the Arsinoite, greeting.
6     While holding assizes (in) the lower country, I was petitioned by many who said that, having leased
7     from the so-called fallow-land, they were aggrieved by certain persons who imposed increases (in rent) after the first
8     or even the second year of the lease,
9     and that thus they were ejected from the cultivation,
10    having already spent considerable (sums) on the working
11    of the land, in hope of being able
12    in the following years to recover also what had been spent in advance.
13    Moved, therefore, by this
14    just plea of theirs, I considered it fitting to make some general
15    ordinance concerning this, by which I think both those who wish to
16    be industrious will be benefited in their rights,
17    and those who approach the leases for the purpose of vexation will be stopped; and especially
18    … it is necessary that much public land, of that which is in dry/fallow (state) in …
19    … those in dry/fallow (state) let them bring back (into cultivation) …
20    on the … choice it be handed over; for the (land)
21    which admits of a lesser (rent) it is consequent
22    that it be both proclaimed yearly and fixed (in rent) just as was done before. With good
23    fortune, therefore, let those who wish come forward
24    giving the undertakings which, posting them publicly … to make the proclamations, so that the industriousness may be able to come about at the fitting
25    season. Of this my
26    letter I wish a copy to be posted both in the metropoleis and in each
27    village, so that all may know what has been ordained. I wish you health. Year 19, Pauni 20.
