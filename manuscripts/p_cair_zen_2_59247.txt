[TRANSLATION]
1     Philiscos to Zenon greetings. When I was about to come to you came
2     a letter [saying] to meet in Ptolemais Ariston the [one] from
3     the king having sailed up for inspection of the nome; and I will be present
4     at your place, as I suppose, on the 17th. Well therefore you will do by asking
5     Hedylos to come to Crocodilopolis so that the
6     sale having been made we might proclaim [it] in Philadelphia. Write but to me
7     also concerning the boats from Heracleopolitans if you have conveyed them.
8     Farewell. Year 33, Tybi 8.
9     [Year 33], Tybi 10. Philiscos
10    [to s]end Hedylos
11    [to Cro]codilopolis.
12    To Zenon.
13    … iurgios
