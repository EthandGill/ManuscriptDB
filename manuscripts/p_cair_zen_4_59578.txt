[TRANSLATION]
1     … [to Zen]on greetings. Mikkalion wrote to us that you have [greatly benefited]
2     [him and] treated him kindly and that he is eager to send [him]. You would do well then
3     [if you think fit, to send] him to us into his home, even if something must be [spent]
4     [on him so that] he does not suffer shame from hunger. By doing these things you will do us a favor and [the city].
5     Farewell.
6     To Zenon.
