8     … to summon accordingly. And I gave to you also [for]merly a [petiti]on into your …
9     … that I should sail down to the city for the appearance which
10    took place of ours …
11    … and I requested him to give (it) back to me; but he paid no heed but … to wrong …
12    I beg you, therefore, O king, to order Diophanes the strategos to write to Pyrrhos the epi[states] to send Autoboulos and Semthea the shepherd before Diophanes and, if it be true as I write, to compel
