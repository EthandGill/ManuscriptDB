[TRANSLATION]
1     I have spent many days since I reached Herakleous, and thinking
2     that in these (days) I should arrive before you, I held back the two letters to you,
3     which I carry with me. But again, having learned that you are still lingering there, I have now sent these
4     through the most esteemed Menas, lest … a necessary reply.
5     And concerning the two pairs of fine linen (cloths) I want you to know that
6     I have handed these over to Menas the colle[ctor, and through hi]m they were delivered
7     to the lord Asokas the registrar. … and …
8     the same lord Menas affirmed that he had de[livered] the [tw]o
9     pairs, and, being importuned by [me], promised to send] you the copies
10    whenever I should pass him as he was going out from Alexandria; and since it happened that he
11    fell into unpleasantness (illness) and was not found in Perone, for this reason
12    I did not get his letters to you, but the lord Menas the collector
13    said: I receive his letters [and] send (them) to the lord Andronikos
14    enclosed within my own letters; and so that [you] should no longer be neglected I wrote.
15    And the collector says: Asokas, having brought down certain g[oods], left (them)
16    with the unloaders, and did not take care … to take and give them to me,
17    and the unloaders have them until now. And since you did not write
18    to him to give anything and to take them, he too left them; and so that
19    you may know this also I have indicated it. Throughout the whole letter much
20    do I make obeisance to you. The brother Menas greets you much through me,
21    the chamberlain.
22    Deliver to my master, in all things most esteemed, genuine friend and [b]rother Andronikos, from Eleias the notary.
