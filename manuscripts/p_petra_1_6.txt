[TRANSLATION]
1     Report of the things which I, Epiphanios, lost
2     and I suspect the most cautious Hierion [son of Patrophilus concerning] the key
3     the great [key] of the upper room,
4     two cypress [pieces] from
5     the two-storied house,
6     six birds, a table.
7     When he emptied his house
8     I gave to him
9     two rooms so that he might bring [things] into them and he did not return them to me.
10    Epiphanios [son] of Damianos, elder
11    I received the oath
12    from him [most cautious]
13    [Hier]ion.
