[TRANSLATION]
1   In the third year of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus,
2   Phaophi (2nd hand) 28, (1st hand) in the city of the Oxyrhynchi in the Thebaid.
3   Marcus Antonius Ptolemaios, son of Ptolemaios, of the Sergian tribe,
4   and however he is styled, acknowledges to Dionysios the el-
5   der, son of Theon son of Dionysios, of the city of the Oxyrhynchi,
6   in the street, that he has appointed him, by this
7   acknowledgment, as agent to carry off, for alienation,
8   the slave bodies belonging to him, Antonius Ptolemaios, by paternal
9   inheritance - Diogas also called Nilos, about 40 years old,
10   and another Diogas, about 30 years old - these such, not to be re-
11   turned, except for (legal) claim and the sacred disease, to those who come forward
12   for the purchase, whether all together or one by one,
13   at whatever price he finds, and (2nd hand) about them to manage all else
14   (1st hand) just as it was open to Marcus Ptolemaios himself
15   [w]hen present; for he approves of these
16   thing[s], on condition that the price to be given (2nd hand) him for these
17   (1st hand) - or for what shall be sold of them - he shall re-
18   store to Antonius Ptolemaios, the trust
19   resting upon Dionys[i]os, and the guarantee
20   of owner[ship] falling to Anto-
21   nius Ptolemaios on the aforesaid terms.
22   The deed of agency is valid.
23   Deed of agency of Anto(nius) Ptol(emaios).
