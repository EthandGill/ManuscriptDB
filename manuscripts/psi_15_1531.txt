1     To Patron, having been exegetes, son of Gemeinus, having been gymnasiarch of the Arsinoltes cit[y]
2     And to Heracles, the same as Philantinu, of the priests-victors and tax-exempt, and to Polis, sister, through the
3     Paternal uncle Munatius, the same as Sarapammon,
4     Of Antinoe
5     From Papontot Orsey and Herod, [his] son,
6     Of the two from the village Kerkesouch of the Mount of Polemou
7     Division. [We] desire to rent from you
8     Under joint guarantee the [fields] subsisting to you concerning the
9     Foregoing village Kerkesouch, a dwelling lot
10    [Of] fourteen arous, or however many if they be, in one parcel
11    In the [place] call[ed] of Timon, for six years from the entering
12    Fourth year, the first and third and
13    Fifth year towards sowing of wheat, with export-tax upon the
14    Triennium according to year, together with which [we] will receive ten artabai of seeds
15    Of all, according to year, wheat [of] one hundred
16    [And] thirty-six, but the second and fourth and
17    Sixth year towards sowing of grass [and] fodder
18    Of sheep, with tax upon the triennium, according to year
19    [And] without seed, silver drachmae two hundred [and] sixty
20    And of provisions according to year, each one artaba of bread
