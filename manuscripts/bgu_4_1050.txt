[TRANSLATION]
1    To Protarchos,
2    from Isidora daughter of Herakleides also called Dionysios, with
3    her guardian, her brother Bakchios son of Herakleides
4    also called Dionysios, of the Althaian deme; and from Dionysios son of
5    Dionysios, of the Isideios deme. Isidora and
6    Dionysios agree that they have come together with one another in marriage,
7    and that Dionysios has also received from Isidora,
8    from hand to hand out of the house, as a dowry: women's garments to the value of
9    one hundred silver drachmas, a pair of gold earrings of two
10   quarter-weights, and of stamped Ptolemaic silver
11   sixty drachmas; (and that) Dionysios, having re-
12   ceived the aforesaid dowry, shall feed and
13   clothe Isidora as his wedded wife
14   according to his means, and shall not mistreat her nor
15   insult her nor cast her out nor bring in another
16   woman — or (else) repay the dowry with half
17   as much again, the right of execution belonging to Isidora against both
18   Dionysios himself and all his property,
19   as if by legal judgment; and that Isidora shall neither spend the night away
20   nor be absent by day from
21   Dionysios' house without Dionysios' consent,
22   nor ruin the household, nor be with another man,
23   or else she too, if she does any of these things,
24   on being judged (guilty), shall be deprived of the dowry; and that they
25   shall also deposit the marriage
26   contract before the hierothytai within five business days
27   from when they give notice to one another, in which shall be registered
28   both the dowry and the other customary matters, and
29   the provisions concerning the death of either of the spouses,
30   as may be jointly decided at the time. We request (registration).
