1     To my lord and brother Sarapammon,
2     Origenes, very many greetings.
3     Before all things I pray to divine providence concerning your well-being, that you may be in health and good spirits and also receive the letter from me, my lord brother.
4     As you sent Papnouthis to me, through your letter
5     you indicated to me that I should come to meet you in the Oxyrhynchite (nome), and on the next day I was detained by Plutarchus, soldier of the prefect, on behalf of Origenes,
6     on the pretext of the horses, and after God I found no one
7     to help me except Horion the steward of Annianus.
