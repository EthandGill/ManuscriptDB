1     There were conveyed, for the heating (hypokausis) of the bath of the cohort, of the produce
2     of year 21 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, in the name of Panameus
3     [(son of) Diosko]urides and partners, of chaff, loads three,
4     [total chaff loads] 3. Year 21 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
5     Epeiph 22. I, Antoninus, have signed.
