[TRANSLATION]
1     Year three of the Autocrator Caesar
2     Titus Aelius Hadrian Antonei[n]us
3     Augustus Eusebes Epei[ph] 6.
4     Dioskotos and his associates, grain-tax-collectors of Boubastus [have me]asured from the produce of the same [year]
5     of wheat in public measure with xy[s]ton […]
6     through those from Soknopaiou Nesos Paky[sei]
7     Pakyseos artabes one-hund[red thirty-]seven, and Mesore 1 [day]
8     of bar[ley] artabes forty
9     nine-and-one-fourth, and on the 18 [day]
10    of the same Mesore month other
11    artabes seventeen-and-one-half one-twelfth.
12    totals of all wheat 363 ½ ⅓.
13    Dioskotos [I] have jointly-measured the aforesaid wheat 363 ½ ⅓.
