1     … [on] the 22nd of the same Phaophi, the same collectors, through Bekis
2     [Aurelius] …, receiver, have signed off as set forth above.
3     [Aurelius] … son of Hermodoros, of lentil-chaff, 8500 pounds,
4     [in accordance with the] receipt [issued] by him, of which the copy is as follows:
5     [Aurelius] … [son of Hermodoros,] superintendent, that is, conveyor,
6     [of the provisioning goods conveyed] up to the Upper Thebaid,
7     [to Aurelii Iboïs and Pion and Herm]eias and Bekis, collectors of the village [of Kirka, greeting. I have received from] you and loaded into a [public] vessel … of Maximianopolis, of carrying-capacity 500 artabas,
8     [under Aurelius] … [the helmsman, from] the Antaiopolite nome, of the produce of the 19th
9     [indiction, of lentil-chaff] 8500 pounds, and of these the hundredths and the fixed [freight-charges as far as the same] Maximianopolis, and being questioned
10    [I have agreed. In the consulship of] our lord Maximinus Augustus for the 2nd time.
11    … [Aurelius]
12    Ammonas [son of Besarion, of chaff, ten thousand and 2500 pounds] …
13    in accordance with the receipt issued by him, [of which the copy is as follows:]
