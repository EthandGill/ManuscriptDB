[TRANSLATION]
1     Pasis to Zenon, greetings. I received from Peteminis the Arab the
2     letter from you and the money, the price of the papyri. Very
3     […] pleased me the matter, [in] that you sent the price beforehand,
4     because we send carefully just as you write. I have sent therefore to you
5     ten papyri.
6     Farewell. Year 36, Thoth 24.
7     To Zenon
