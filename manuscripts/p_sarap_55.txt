[TRANSLATION]
1     Account of the month Epeiph.
2     Remaining from the previous account: 183 drachmas 3 obols.
3     Through Saras son of Pekysis: 4 drachmas.
4     Through the baker Sesiys: 20 [drachmas].
5     Through the baker …: 12 [drachmas].
6     Through Kastor son of Ko…: 85 drachmas 1 obol.
7     Through Barosis: 112 drachmas.
8     Through Selene the mother: 12 drachmas.
9     Through Hermas son of Achilleus: 8 drachmas.
10    Through Mirees son of Sokrates: 4 drachmas.
11    Wage for donkeys: 4 drachmas 3 obols.
12    Through Phibion from Ibion Tanoupeos: 160 drachmas.
13    Through Ophis son of Mirees: 40 drachmas.
14    Total together: 644 drachmas 4 obols.
15    From which, expenditures of Epeiph:
16    To Hermophilos: … 1 obol.
17    For festivals: 80 drachmas 2 obols.
18    To Beles, price of cushions: 20 drachmas.
19    On the 12th, to Eudaimonis, price of …: 8 drachmas.
20    To the tax-farmer, for over-time (arrears)
21    in the name of Mirees son of Phibion: 34 drachmas.
22    To Horion son of Chairemon, keeper of the threshing-floor
23    at Sinkere: 8 drachmas.
24    To Sarapion son of Psen…: 8 drachmas.
25    To Chichoïs son of Koulo, as a payment: 8 drachmas.
26    To Pekysis, as a payment: 8 drachmas.
27    To Anoubion, for the expense of Eudaimonis: 8 drachmas.
28    To Taïbis the nurse: 8 drachmas.
29    Oil: 1 obol.
30    For a little pot: 1 obol.
31    Salt: 1 obol.
32    Gratuity, for finger(-rings): 20 obols.
33    To Kornelios from Thnephis: 2 drachmas.
34    To Prooüs son of Salatas, for the price
35    of vetch: 28 drachmas.
36    To Anoubion, price of papyrus: 8 drachmas.
37    To Apollinarios, for the expense of the storehouse: 12 drachmas.
38    Price of a small tablet: 1 drachma 2 obols.
39    Price of cheeses: 6 obols.
40    For seasonings: 1 obol.
41    To Demetrios, interest: 6 drachmas 4 obols.
42    Surcharge (?) of the storehouse of Kleopatra: 23 drachmas.
43    To Koulos the brick-maker: 32 drachmas.
44    To Hermaios son of Achillion: 116 drachmas.
45    Price of a bath-attendant (?): 4 drachmas.
46    Price of 3 choes of oil: 28 drachmas 1 obol.
47    Total together, expenditures: 465 drachmas 5 obols.
48    Remaining: 178 drachmas 6 obols.
49    From Marsip…: 600 drachmas.
50    From which, expenditures: to Hexakon son of Achillion: 200 drachmas.
51    To Ammonas, for the price
52    of green produce: 100 drachmas.
53    To Kornelios from Thnephis: 140 drachmas.
54    To Parodion: 100 drachmas.
55    For a small ring: 60 drachmas.
56    Total, the aforesaid amounts.
