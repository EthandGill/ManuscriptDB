[TRANSLATION]
1     Imouthés and partners, collectors of silver [taxes]
2     To Psáiti of Horos Pikótos, mother Senkhónsios
3     We have received on behalf of embankment-tax of Agoras north, year 16, dirty drachmas seven tetrówbolon
4     Makes dirty drachmas 7 tetrówbolon. Year 16 of Trajan the lord
5     Epeiph 36. Ar marked.
6     Others, Thot 27, of year 17, name of Horos Pikótos
7     Petechespokhratrou, we have received on behalf of embankment-tax of Agoras north, year 16, dirty drachmas seven tetrówbolon
8     Makes drachmas 7 tetrówbolon. Ar marked.
