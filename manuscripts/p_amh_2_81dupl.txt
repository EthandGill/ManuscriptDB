[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Nem[esianos], holding the office of strategos [of the Her]m[opolite (nome)],
2     from Aurel[ius Herm]eias son of Ammonios, however he is styled. Having (matter) to state [against A]urelius Ammonios, formerly [pina]cogr[a]pher, [of Aureli]us Antonios who had served as strategos, our most illustrious governor Vale[rius] Fir[m]us having ordered (it),
3     [concerning the things in which he] extorted from [me] when I happened to be secretary of the collectors, of silver, two talents and three thousand drach[mas],
4     I request that he order it to be announced
5     through an underling [of the] office of strategos that he be present and attend at the tribunal [of] our most illustrious governor
6     until the matters sought against him reach their conclusion,
7     but also that he bring forward his assistants
8     Isidoros and Gerontios and Hierakion, so that there be nothing lacking in the matter under discussion, in accordance with the subscriptions given to me, which read thus: one, of the year 3, Pachon 8, having given notice to him whom you charge, petition
9     me when I am judging, sheet 69 of the roll … and another, of the year 4, Thoth 12.
10    If, when you petition, the [oppon]ent does not obey, a [rul]ing shall be given. [Of sheet 1 of the roll ] …
11    In the year 4 [of Im]perator Caesar Marcus Ju[li]us Ph[ilippus][Pius]
12    Fel[i]x and Mar[cus] Julius Philippus the most noble [and most illustrious]
13    C[ae]s[ar, the A]ug[us]ti, Phamenoth 30.
14    I, Au[r]eli[us Herm]e[i]as son of A[m]monios, have submitted (this) a[nd given notice as][is set forth above.]
