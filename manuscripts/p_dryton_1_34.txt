[TRANSLATION]
1     To Phommous, kinsman and epistrategos and strategos
2     of the Thebaid,
3     from Apollonia also called Senmouthis and Aphrodisia
4     also called Tachratis, both daughters of Dryton,
5     residing in Pathyris. There belonging to us and to our
6     own sisters Aristo also called Senmonthis and Nikarion
7     also called Thermouthis and Apollonia the younger also called Senpelaia,
8     of a half share of the paternal landed property, which is in both the
9     Peritheban and the Pathyrite (district), and likewise also of the household slaves, among which also on the Kochlax of Arabia of the said Pathyrite nome, of a half share from a vineyard plot
10    of 2 1/2 arouras or however much more it may be, and of the orchard to the east
11    of it and of the wells and of the hamlets and of the wine-press
12    and of dry land and of other land outside taxation and of all the appurtenances, which our father held
13    for as long a time as he survived, and we, after his
14    [death,] [holding the rest], Aristo[n son of A]thenodotos
15    of those f[rom] Diospolis the [Gr]eat, having more violently
16    entered into the [stat]ed plot of the vineyard
17    and into the appurtenances [of] this, in the times of the disorder,
18    unjustly lays claim to the half share falling to us
19    and has planted some part with vines, having recognized
20    that we are women and dwelling in another place
21    and cannot easily attack the indicated
22    possession. Wherefore having taken refuge with you, we request, if it seem good, that having summoned him you investigate, and if matters are such as we write, that you compel him to withdraw from the declared half share of the vineyard plot and of the things planted on it and of the appurtenant places
23    and to pay back the produce which he has carried off from them, and concerning the
24    violence which he has done, that you deal (with it) with hatred of evil, that we may be assisted.
25    Farewell.
