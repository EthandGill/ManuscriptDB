[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 6 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrian An[t]oninus Augustus Pious, on Epeiph 18 of the reckoning of Pauni. [He] registered
3     Harpalos, [son of] Amo Ker[atis], mother Berenice
4     [on behalf of] poll-tax of the sixth year of Apollonius Parembole
5     twenty drachmas, [it] totals 20 with ten copper obols further recorded.
