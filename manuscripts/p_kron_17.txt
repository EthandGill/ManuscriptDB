[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fourth year of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus
2     Antoninus Augustus Pius, Thoth 28, in Tebtynis of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge: Taorsenouphis, about forty-five years old, with a scar on the left shin,
3     and Tephorsais, about thirty-five years old, with a scar on the left hand, both daughters of Kronion son of Cheos, each with her guardian — Taorsenouphis with her father Kronion son of Cheos, about seventy-eight years old, with a scar on the little finger of the left hand, and Tephorsais
4     with her husband, who is also her brother, Harphaesis, about forty-eight years old, with a scar on the left thumb, the two being Persians of the epigone, sureties for one another for repayment, (acknowledge) to Didyme daughter of Heron son of Amais, about
5     forty-six years old, with a scar on the left shin, with her guardian,
6     her cousin on the mother's side Harpaesis son of Herakleios, about … years old,
7     with a scar on the left hand, that they have from her, Didyme,
8     [on the spot] through hand to hand an interest-bearing loan of principal money,
9     [three hundred] seventy-[two drachmas], of which also the repayment
10    they shall make under compulsion in the month Thoth of the coming fifth
11    year without delay; and if they do not repay as has been written by the aforesaid term, it shall be permitted to Didyme, or to whoever acts for her, if she chooses,
12    to take the fruits for her own benefit from the same fifth year from the
13    properties belonging to her, Tephorsais, around the village of Tebtynis in the so-called district of Stasikrates, of a catoecic allotment of four arouras, two arouras,
14    from whichever Didyme chooses, whether the head or a part, with all the public charges of the two arouras falling upon Tephorsais; and if
15    she does not wish Didyme to take the fruits, the right of execution shall be hers both from
16    [the acknowledging women] or from the one and from the property belonging to them,
17    [all of it, as if by] legal judgment. Subscriber: Hippalos son of Chrates, about
18    [seventy] … years old, with a scar on the right forearm. Taorsenouphis and
19    [Tephorsais], both daughters of Kronion, each with her guardian,
20    [Taorsenouphis] with her father, and Tephorsais with her husband Harphaesis, we acknowledge that we have from Didyme, on joint liability, the
21    [three hundred] eighty-two drachmas as a loan, which
22    we shall repay in the month Thoth of the fifth year, and if
23    we do not, that she may take the fruits of the two arouras around Tebtynis as
24    [is stated above. There wrote] on their behalf Hippalos son of Chrates, as they do not know
25    [letters. Didyme] daughter of Heron, with her guardian, her cousin on the mother's side,
26    [Harpaesis] son of Herakleios: the acknowledgment has been made to me
27    [as is stated above]. There wrote on their behalf Maron son of Maron, as they do not know letters.
