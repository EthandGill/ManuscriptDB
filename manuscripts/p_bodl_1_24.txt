1     Of year 3 of Autocrator Caesar
2     Marcus
3     Aurelius Antoninus Fortunate,
4     Pious Augustus, Thoth 25, he recorded
5     To Aurelius Melanis, secretary of silver-tax collectors of Karanides, Longinus
6     of Heracles, of the paradise, of the same [year], drach[mae]
7     twelve, drachmae 12; Phao­phi 1, other drachmae ten
8     two, drachmae 12; Hathyr 1, other drachmae ten two, drachmae 12;
9     Choiak 29, other drachmae ten two, drachmae 12; Tybi,
10    other drachmae ten two, drachmae 12; Mechir 8, other drachmae ten two, drachmae 12, and through me Aurelius Dioscorus,
11    Phamenoth 9, for geometry of the same year, drachmae twe[nty,]
12    total drachmae 20; Pharmouthi 8, other drachmae ten two, total drachmae 12; Pachon 6, other drachmae twenty, total drachmae 20, Pauni;
13    other drachmae ten six, total drachmae 16.
