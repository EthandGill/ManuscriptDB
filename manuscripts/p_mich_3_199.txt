[TRANSLATION]
1     In the tenth year of Imperator Caesar Trajan
2     Hadrian Augustus, in the month Gorpiaios on the second,
3     Epeiph 2, in Bakchias of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges Haryotes son of Alexas son of Alexas, about 64 years old, with a mole on the right of his forehead and on his
4     left foot, to Horos son of Horos son of Horos, about 52 years old, with a scar on his left eyebrow, that he, the acknowledging party, has received from him
5     the rent of the present tenth year of Hadrian
6     Caesar the lord, of what he, Haryotes, farms about the aforementioned village of Bakchias, of a holding of four and a half arouras, or however many they may be, in one sealed (plot), in the field called …lon,
7     Horos having reckoned to his own account the seed for the sowing of the coming eleventh year of Hadrian Caesar
8     the lord, and that the acknowledging party brings no charge against Horos concerning any matter whatsoever up to the present day, in any way
9     on any pretext whatever, the lease of the holding
10    remaining valid on all the terms it contains. Subscriber of the
11    acknowledging party: Heliodoros son of Heliodoros, about 52 years old, with a scar on his right cheek.
12    I, Haryotes son of Alexas son of Alexas, acknowledge that I have received from
13    Horos son of Horos the rent of the present tenth
14    year of Hadrian the lord, of what he farms of mine about Bakchias, of a holding
15    of four and a half arouras, Horos having
16    with himself the seed of the coming year, the lease remaining valid as set out above.
17    I, Heliodoros son of Heliodoros, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
18    Registered through the records-office in Bakchias.
