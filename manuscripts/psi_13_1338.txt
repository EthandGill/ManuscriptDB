[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of the lords Emperor Diocletian [and Maximian] augus[ti]
2     To Aurelia Diogenida also called Turbianae, respectable matron [wearing] the stole
3     From Aurelius Theon [son] of Theon, mother Helena, from the [bright Oxy]rhync[h]ite city.
4     Willingly I accept to lease for one [year from] [the new-moon]
5     of the next month Athur of the present 16th year and 15th year [all the work] and irrigation
6     and tending of the laid down new vine [in your vineyard] estate
7     [around] village … … called … [plot], on condition that …
8     … during winter [vegetables] edible [and] during summer cu[cu]mbers and [mel]ons for the … [nourishment] of the vine irrigation … from new-moon Pharmuthi until … 11 Phaophi … four[fold?] and complete for me
9     for rent of each aroura [from] measuring [in] silver drachmas thousand
10    two-hundred, me doing and the service of [those] being laid down in season
11    from which if [it] becomes until it fails. [The] lease confirmed, necessarily I will fulfill all the vine work in season and the irrigation and tending and I will return the work [each] of them at the appropriate time both the rent
12    in the month Payni and after the time I will hand over the [vine] well-cared-for with all vineyarding care and flourishing and life-giving [with] the cuttings being provided to me
13    in place of the failing [ones], me making the deposit payment without hire having become to you the enforcement from me as is proper. Valid [is] the acceptance and when questioned I agreed. And 16th year and 15th year
14    of our lords Diocletian and Maximian augustii and 8th year of our lords Constantius
15    and Maximian of the most distinguished Caesars Phaophi 13.
16    Aurelius Horion procurator …
