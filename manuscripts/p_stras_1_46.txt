[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of our most divine master Flavius]
2     [Justin the eternal Augustus Imperator,]
3     [in the] first [year] after the consulship
4     of Flavius Basilius the most glorious, in the twenty-
5     fifth year, Phamenoth, the twenty-third, of the fourteenth indiction,
6     in the public market, through you, Philemmon, chief attendant
7     of the city of the Antinoites: Flavius Sarapammon son of Kollouthos, from the city of the Antinoites.
8     I acknowledge, willingly and of my own choice, that I stand surety
9     and have undertaken before you for Aurelius Ph…b…iō…,
10    of the city of the Antinoites, on condition that he shall be here in the city of the Antinoites
11    performing the service of the sausage-makers (isikiarioi)
12    blamelessly and without contempt,
13    from the holy Anastasis of the present
14    fourteenth indiction until the taking-over
15    of the, God willing, fifteenth indiction, and
16    that he in no wise absent himself. And if he should be absent, and if he be sought and I do not produce (him), I shall also hand him over to you in a public place
17    outside the holy precincts and divine sanctuaries and
18    the holy Lord's church, on a working day —
19    (I), his surety, to provide to you, on account of
20    his absence, … gold coins. [The surety being]
21    valid and secure, and in all respects, being questioned, I acknowledged. [Flavius]
22    Sarapammon son of Kollouthos, I stand surety, and all is agreeable to me as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Stephanos son of Horouonchis, from
23    the city of the Antinoites, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he not
24    knowing letters.
25    Through me, Kosmas, notary …
