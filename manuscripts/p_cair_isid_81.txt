[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulshi[p] of our lord[s] M[a]ximian Augustus for the 5th time and
2     [M]aximian [the] most illustrious Caesar for [the] 2nd time.
3     We, Aur[elii] Polion [son of P]etros, his m[o]ther being A…e…, fr[o]m the hamlet of Phanous, about 30 years old,
4     with a scar on the [m]iddle of the forehead and on the left shin, and Isidoros son of Ptolemaio[s], his mother being Heroeis, [f]rom the village of Karan[i]d[e]s, about 35 years old, with a scar on the shin
5     on the right, to one another, greeting. Since the brother of [me] Isidoros, Aurelius
6     Peras, was nominated by those from the [af]oresaid village of Karanis
7     to go abroad to the Trajan river (canal), and as my brother Peras
8     is occupied about the property belonging to him, we urged you, Polion, to go abroad in his stead
9     to the same Trajan river (canal) and there to work and to
10    discharge the public service on his behalf blamelessly, you,
11    Polion, receiving from me, Isidoros, on behalf of wages and
12    persuasion-money, the sum agreed between us, two talents of silver, and provisions for two months, on the likeness of all
13    the m[e]n sent from the nome, of which (sum) hereupon
14    I, Polion, have received from you, Isi[d]oros, on account, one talent of silver and the provisions of the [sa]me two months, and the
15    remaining one talent of the silv[e]r I shall receive from you,
16    Isidoros, in the month Pauni of the present yea[r], the two months being reckoned to me
17    from the day on which I should g[o] abroad from the Arsinoite (nome). And if I, Polion, should be detained there more than sixty days
18    in the sa[m]e T[r]ai[a]n riv[e]r (canal)
19    I shall receive from you, Isidoros, on behalf of wages, fo[r eac]h day
20    two hundred drachmas of silver, and there being no power for either of us
21    to alter any of these (terms) or to transgress an[y] of the (terms) ent[ered (in the document)] in any way, because for these we have, with willing and [o]f-our-own-choice intent,
22    made the agreement with one another. And on being questioned we gave our assent.
23    Year 13 and 12 and 5 of our lords Diocletian and Maximian the Augusti
24    and Constantius and Maximian the most illustrio[us] Caes[ars], Pharmouthi 14.
25    I, Aurelius Polion son of Petros, have agreed to all the aforesaid (terms) and have received on account one talent of silver, and the remaining
26    one talent of the silver I shall receive at the appointed time
27    as is set forth above. I, A[u]relius Silvanus also called Heron, wrote on behalf of him
28    being requested, he being illiterate.
