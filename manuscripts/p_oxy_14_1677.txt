[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings, Aphrodite—Agathos embraces you.
2     First of all I pray that you are well and
3     your obeisance I make before the lord Sarapis.
4     You will receive from Doxa, who brings to you the small letter, the order-letter of Ptolemaide, which I received from
5     Chairhemon the embroiderer to send to her as he g[ave]
6     to me sealed. Quickly make known to us through
7     report [that] you have received it, so that you may not burden people to write to you
8     a letter, so that it may not become troublesome with people.
9     And concerning the former order-letters make known to me.
10    Greet your mother and your father and Agathos
11    and Heraklammona and Didyme and Alexandra
12    and Si…trian with her husband. Let [them] know that I embrace her. Greet all yours by name
13    [on] my behalf. Doxa has again certain other things that she has received
14    [to Aphrodite] from Agathos.
