[TRANSLATION]
1     … of Asklepiades, member of the city-council
2     … of this matter … having employed … concerning the audit, he did … being carried away by much inhumanity
3     …dros … the rendering of the account with good faith and all accuracy to undertake jointly with him, trusting that not …
4     … of him but … I … power … of Ptolemaios and Ambrosios the city-councillors … of me and … that the aforementioned Asklepiades be compelled to come forward to the audit and with all good faith and conscience to render the account together with me, to the end that, free from oppression
5     … to me security, through your guardianship, lord prefect; and further indeed through the same assistance to restore to me the house which before judgment and by force he took from me, or, since even now he is behaving shamelessly, that this man be sent
6     to your greatness, and I shall give thanks to your splendor for all time.
7     Junius, the most illustrious prefect, said: those petitioned, between the parties, shall render the account, and, when what is truly owed has been paid, you shall not be troubled beyond the just debt, but, acknowledging the
8     things determined of the liability of the management of the affairs, you shall be released according to the laws.
