[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] year eleventh
2     of the Autocrator
3     Caesar Titus Aelius
4     Hadrian Antoninus
5     Augustus Pius,
6     in the month of Daisios, Pharmouthi 26, in Kerkesouchis in the Heracleides division of the Arsinoite nome; Pasion [son] of Phaseites acknowledges
7     as [being] of age 25, having a scar on [the] arm
8     left, from Phnebios
9     to Stotoes [son] of Horos, as [being] of age 35
10    [with] no distinguishing mark, [that he] has sold
11    to him a female donkey
12    mature, mouse-colored
13    [not] to be returned, [and he has received payment]
14    [the] price in silver, sixty drachmae
15    four. [He confirms it].
