[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting, Herakleides, manager of the bank in the division of Polemon. I am wronged by
2     Athenogenes, one of the donkey-drivers at Kaminoi. For when I had hired from him 73 donkeys for the transport
3     of the royal monies, and had given him a hand-towel in which … a tunic and a cloak … pledged of the peoples … the appropriate … the returns; but Athenogenes
4     … did not give back to us … if it be proved
5     that he has received from me from the …
6     … a letter to us has put forward … concerning these very things. I beg therefore you, king, if it seem good to you,
7     to give order to Diophanes the strategos to write to Hephaistion the epistates at Kaminoi, having summoned
8     Athenogenes, to investigate concerning these things and, if we prove that we gave to him the cloak and [the]
9     tunic in the hand-towel, to compel him to give back to us the …
10    the letter he has put forward to us. For this being done, through you, king, the [common savior of all and]
11    benefactor, I shall obtain justice. Farewell.
12    To Hephaistion. By all means reconcile (them); but if not, send (them) so that they may be judged before the appropriate tribunal.
13    Year 1, Gorpiaios 30, Tybi [13].
14    Year 1, Gorpiaios 30, Tybi 13.
15    Herakleides, manager, against
16    Athenogenes, concerning garments.
