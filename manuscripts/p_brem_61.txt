[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Apollonios
2     the brother, many greetings.
3     I greet you. From the day I went out
4     from you and came to be in the Hermopolite, [I cau]ght the strategos.
5     in … [t]he thieves wished
6     me [to seal up] the contents
7     of the c[hest], swearing concerning the
8     pri[ce. But I] did not wish, before
9     swearing, [than to] receive the silver,
10    knowing [that with]out a strategic threat
11    nothing … [o]usi, but only this raging madly … as. But how are you? And I
12    am anx[ious] every day, lest again
13    you be sluggish. Wr[ite] to me about your
14    safety. For above all I reckon your
15    safe[ty]
16    rather than all the things I seek. For I know how
17    you honour me, and I often bear witness
18    to all the things you have done for me. Greet
19    Aline as a sister, and Eudaimonis
20    the mother, and your children unharmed by the evil eye.
21    I pray that you may be well.
22    Pharmouthi 16.
23    You are not unaware how again the fool
24    troubles me on account of his mother,
25    playing the fool, and not having you
26    to shake off his folly.
27    Let it be a care to you, how, if I send to you
28    the children Pausan and Kotterous,
29    you may admonish (them?) concerning that matter and bring it to an end.
30    Chairas to Apollonios
31    the brother, greeting.
32    I greet you many times, and I always
33    look for your safety,
34    you having written to me how
35    you are. For I am anxious always, until you write to me
36    about your health. Herodes the brother
37    greets you.
38    I greet all those who love you. I wish
39    that you may be well.
40    Diskas to Apolonios the lord,
41    many greetings. Before all things
42    I greet you and make your obeisance
43    hourly before the lord Hermes. As you know, concerning
44    you being ill, I am anx[ious],
45    until I come to you when you are recovered.
46    You kn[o]w
47    that I remain away from you
48    on account of our pledges, whose (i.e. your) safety
49    I reckon before my own.
50    Greet all those who love you.
51    Farewell, lord.
52    [To Apollonios] strategos of the Apollonopolite
53    Heptakomia.
