1     Has been measured into treasury of metropolis, produce of year 9
2     of Hadrian Caesar of the lord
3     Mesore 13, of Petechespokhrates also [by name]
4     Apollotos, likewise through Panamaios Psenzhotos
5     and Phathraios Bēkhios, overseers
6     for Cholmis Nekhtatymis, wheat, three [artabas,] one-sixth; yield wheat artabas three, one-sixth

8     and [for] the same, wheat, one-third [and] one-twelfth of an artaba, another

10    and of Psenzhotos Psansnōtos by name
11    through the same, wheat, one-third of an artaba; yields wheat, one-half [and] one-third
