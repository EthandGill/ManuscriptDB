[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Heracleios, also called Liberalis, having served as exegetes, counselor, keeper of records of property-[holdings] in the Heracleopolitan [nome]
2     from Marcus Aurelius Orseos
3     a Roman; … I wish to manage in order
4     which I have now registered from the name of the
5     full brother of mine Julius
6     Marcus Ptolemaeus, having died without children
7     and without having made a will, [me being] sole heir,
8     which has been ceded according to a public [financial] transaction
9     completed in the 12th year of the deified Severus and
10    Antoninus, Pharmouthi, through superintendents
11    of the market-regulation [office] in Tekme, of which the right was registered
12    in the present year, Tybi, from Asklatarios, freedwoman Posnautos
13    from the village of Taemseos, which she herself ceded in the 5th year of the deified
14    Severus, Thoth, through superintendents
15    of the market-regulation [office] in the city by Souerios Orsenoupios, mother Thaesis, from
16    village of Tamasow, which she registered in the 3rd year
17    from the name of her [own] mother Thaesis,
18    mother of Souerios, [having died]
19    without having made a will, [she being sole] heiress,
20    which [Soueris] has ceded [to the]
21    16th year of the deified Aelius Antoninus
22    through the market-regulation office in Tekme by Aphygchios Alexatos, mother Eiratos
23    from the village of Tabokleow, which she registered from the grandmother Thenkollouthos, Achilles, Alexander
24    mother Thaesis, [around the village of] Tamasow, from the [Theodmetos] lot
25    from 2 arurae, along with which [she] ceded
26    Thenkollouthos 1 aroura, [the] remaining 1 aroura, and at the [then]
27    registration it was shown that [the property of] Asklatarios was distributed
28    and laid out in her name in agreement.
29    Wherefore I submit this petition so that [word be sent] to the market-regulation [office] of the [nome]
30    to complete the [financial] transaction as [is] fitting.
31    Year 3 of Imperator Caesar
32    Marcus Aurelius Severus
33    Alexander, Pious
34    Fortunate, August, Tybi 20.
35    Aurelius Orseus, as [being] about 26 years old, scar on left cheek and on right shin.
36    Aurel[ios] … [nine?], as [is] fitting.
37    Aurelius Heracleios, also called Libera[lis], having served as exegetes
38    counselor, keeper of records, [superintendents of the] market-regulation [office of the nome],
39    the treasury … [heir]
40    Heracleios, also called
41    Liberalis, having served as exegetes, counselor, keeper of records
42    [I] have verified.
43    Tybi 20.
