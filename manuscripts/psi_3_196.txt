1     the imperial treasury. To Victor, the prefect Cineas. Provide to Theodore, the clerk
2     for the distribution of silk-workers … month … 15th indiction in coined monies: two nomismata [and] kerate
3     eighteen, [and] one half, [and] one quarter, total:
4     nomismata 2, kerate 18, 1/2, 1/4 only. It was written in the month Pharmouthi, indiction 15; total in coined monies:
