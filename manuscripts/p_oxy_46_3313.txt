[TRANSLATION]
1     Apol[loni]os and Sarapias to Dionysia,
2     greeting.
3     You filled us with joy by announcing the good news of
4     the wed[ding] of the most excellent Sarapion, and at once
5     we would have come to wait upon him as on a most longed-for
6     day for us, and to rejoice together, but on account of the
7     as[siz]e and because we are recovering from sluggishness (illness)
8     we were unable to come. Many roses have not yet appeared here, but they are scarce, and from all
9     the estates and from all the garland-weavers we could scarcely gather the thousand which we sent you through Sarapas, although having harvested even
10    those which ought to have been harvested tomorrow. Narcissus, as much as you wanted, we had, so that instead of the two thousand you wrote for
11    we sent four thousand. But we do not want you
12    to think us so mean-minded
13    as to write, in mockery, that we have sent
14    the price, when indeed we too hold the children
15    as our own offspring and esteem them more than our own
16    and love them, and so rejoice equally
17    with you and [t]heir father. About whatever else you want
18    wr[i]te to us. Greet Alexandros the
19    most excellent and his (children) — bless them — Sarapion
20    and Theon and Aristokleia and the children
21    of Aristokleia. Sarapas will testify to you about the roses, that I have done everything towards
22    sending you as many as you wanted, but we did not find (them).
23    We pray for your health, lady.
24    To Dionysia, wife of Alexandros.
