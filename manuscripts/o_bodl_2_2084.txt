[TRANSLATION]
1     We have received from Pankratios [son of] Paniskos for the 16th year and
2     the 15th year and the 8th year, pork
3     fourteen pounds through the
4     dekaprotoi of the city
5     Mesore, intercalary [day],
6     4th. Ammonios [son of] Paniskos I have signed.
