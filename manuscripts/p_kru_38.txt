1     … in the name …

7     In the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
8     the consubstantial Trinity, and the power and the firmness
9     and the continuance of our masters the kings, these who hold sway
10    over the whole earth, by the ordinance of God
11    the Almighty. On this day, which is [the] second
12    of the month Parmhat of this year …, indiction …, before
13    the most honored and esteemed Johannes son of the blessed
14    Biktor, and Isak son of the blessed Kostantinos,
15    the lashanes of the fortress of Djeme in the nome of the city
16    of Hermonthis: I, Georgios son of the blessed
17    Loule, my mother being the blessed Lisabet, of the fortress
18    of Djeme in the nome of the city of Hermonthis, write to my beloved brothers Isak and Kyra and Abraam their father,
19    of the city of Souan, drawing up this settlement, asking
20    of the one who shall subscribe for me and the trustworthy witnesses,
21    worthy of credit, these who shall witness to the settlement according to my own request, since
22    in this season, this, I brought suit against you, you Isak and
23    Kyra and Abraam your father, concerning the inheritance
24    of my blessed mother Lisabet; great men,
25    God-fearing according to God, came into our midst with
26    one another, concerning the whole inheritance,
27    in every matter—in gold, in silver, in bronze, in iron, in tin,
28    from that which moves to that which does not move, whether small or great, in every matter of the whole property of the inheritance
29    of my blessed mother Lisabet; I separated myself
30    from you, according to the way that the great men separated me
31    with her, according to the way that we persuaded one another in every
32    manner; I took and I filled my hand from you concerning
33    the whole inheritance of my blessed mother Lisabet,
34    that I shall not be able to bring suit against you, you Isak and Kyria and
35    Abraam, concerning any matter forever, and forever and ever; if I shall appear in a court or outside a court, in a city or in a district or
36    in any place at all of any God-fearing man, neither I
37    nor brother nor sister nor son nor son's son
38    nor relation nor relation of relation nor anyone at all
39    who pertains to me shall bring suit against you, you,
40    concerning the whole property of the inheritance of my blessed mother
41    Lisabet, and any matter that pertains to me;
42    he shall give thirty-six gold solidi as a penalty
43    to the ruler, that is 36 nomismata of gold, refined, by the weight of the Fortress
44    of Memnonia. For I took, I filled my hand from you, of my whole portion
45    concerning the inheritance of my blessed mother Lisabet
46    in every matter; and after these things which I have come in to ratify
47    according to the force of this settlement, and every matter that is written
48    in it. Concerning all these things I swear by the holy Trinity,
49    the consubstantial, and the power and the firmness and
50    the continuance of our masters the kings, these who hold sway
51    over us through God, that no transgression shall arise in it; for the securing then I have established this settlement, secured, being lord in every place where it shall be produced. I, Georgios son
52    of the blessed Loulou, my mother being the blessed Lisabet, the one who wrote first above, assent to [this] settlement and my oath
53    and the penalty and every matter that is written in it, as stated above.
54    I, Pamphilos son of the blessed Petros, with
55    Joseph son of the blessed Pinis, and Theodoros
56    son of the blessed Kostantinos, we are witness.
57    Johannes son of Lazaros, I wrote for them.
58    I, the son in the Spirit of God, this least presbyter and the hegumen of the holy church of Djeme, am witness according to the request of Georgios.
59    I, Joseph son of the blessed Petros, witness according to
60    the request of Georgi. I, Papnouthi[os], the least
61    deacon of the apostle, am witness … according to
62    the request. I, Ephranke son of the blessed
63    Daueid, am witness to the settlement according to the request
64    [of] the one who drew it up. I, Anastasios, this least deacon of the Theotokos Maria, wrote for these witnesses and am witness. Johannes
65    son of Lazaros, with God, I drew it up by my hand
66    as stated above. Jesus Christ.
