1     [In the time] of the consulship of our lords Constantine
2     and Maximianus, the most eminent Kaisars, the third [time].

4     from Aurelius Horion, [son] of Theon, and Chosion,
5     also [called] Amyntianus, and Didymus, also [called] Sarapion,

7     searching for [you] by order of the [most distinguished]
8     our governor Claudius Cleopat[ras] …
9     if Ammonius [son] of Stephanus of the local …
10    knowledge of him such a thing [to report] …
11    examine of our books each …
12    of the tribe having become this one nowhere …
13    appearing … [and neither one nor the other?] …
14    and on account of this we address [you]. …
