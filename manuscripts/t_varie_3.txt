1     In the name of the Lord and master Jesus Christ the god and savior of us, month Pharmouthis 24, indiction 9.
2     Aurelius Serenus son of Phoibammon, mother Nonna, writing slowly, ship-owner of Tubius Apa Sion
3     [from] the Herakleopolite nome [to] Aurelius Jeremiah son of Herminus, merchant from the village Kirka of the Theodosiopolis nome, greetings. I acknowledge myself, the aforesaid Serenus, through this security written by me [to] [yo]u
4     [to receive] and load into the ship under me, [also] aforementioned in the harbor
5     of the same village Kirka wine, nidian vessels five thousand eight hundred twenty-six, of which [there are] wine, pure, nidian vessels …,
