[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ammonios, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2     and to Harpokration also called
3     Hierax, royal scribe of the same division, and
4     to the village-scribe of the village of Karanis, and
5     to the poll-tax registrars of the same village,
6     from Sarapion son of Esoureus, from
7     the village of Karanis. There belongs to those
8     under my care: to Tasoucharion, fatherless, whose mother is Sarapias,
9     an Antinoite, mother of those registered below,
10    in the village a house and courtyard and a third part
11    of another house; and to Gaia Apolinaria and
12    to Gemellus Horion, sons, Antinoites,
13    in common, in equal shares, a house and two courtyards, formerly
14    of Valeria Diodora, and a third part
15    of two houses and two courtyards, and in another place
16    a half part of a house and courtyard and of another courtyard, and in
17    another place a house and courtyard, formerly of Gaius
18    Longinus Apolinarius, a veteran;
19    two courtyards, and, formerly of Ptolemais, a house and
20    courtyard, and in another place a third part of a house and courtyard,
21    which I register for the
22    house-by-house census of the past 28th year.
23    Wherefore I submit (this declaration).
24    I, Ptolemaios, have received a copy of this for
25    examination.
26    I, Isidoros, have likewise received it.
27    I, Souchammon, have likewise received it.
28    I, Ptolemaios, have likewise received it.
29    Year 29 of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus
30    Caesar the lord, Mesore 16.
