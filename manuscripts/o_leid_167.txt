[TRANSLATION]
1     Hathur 15 of the 17th year
2     in the name of Psentphutus [son] of Petesuchos
3     for surveying of the 16th year in the South, four [and] three-obol,
4     total: drachmas 4 [and] three-obol. A I have signed.
5     Another: Petechonsius [son] of Picos for surveying, drachmas 4 [and] three-obol.
