[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakleides to Dioskourides, greeting. If you are well, both you and your children and all those with you, and everything else is for you all
2     according to your wish, it would be as I wish; and I too was in good health. On the 16th of the present month, when I had fallen in with Philotas
3     who is delivering to you the letter, in the Demetreion, he wishing to lodge against you all that he has knowledge of concerning you in
4     his whole life, to the king, and being entangled with him along with others, I esteemed and undertook for him in each matter that he should not
5     be wronged, but that everything fitting should be his. Thus he was persuaded, and with much sweat we drew (him) off; whence also
6     he alleged that he had been wronged by you in many things and deprived of certain favors which you had set up for him, and that many times
7     he had further sent word to you, but had not even gained against the man, but you kept sending threats; wherefore he was led to this point,
8     to act against you. I therefore wondered, if you … the present time, you let him sail down … nevertheless, after all, even brothers quarrel.
9     Even now you will do well, inasmuch as also … to an account, especially indeed in private
10    and, having received the matters from him, let him be persuaded … concerning the same things; greet one another kindly, considering that I have come to you
11    and you have contributed something to me. For indeed concerning the same things … to you. And most especially you will do a favor by taking care
12    of your body, that I may greet you in good health as quickly as possible. Farewell. Year 30, Tybi 18.
13    To the commander over men
14    and garrison-commander Dioskourides.
