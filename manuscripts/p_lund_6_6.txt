1     Copy [of a will]. Year 31, … before … [the agora]nomos. These (things) disposed, being of sound mind and understanding, … daughter

4     according to the marriage-contract drawn up for me … and the cession … the village of Theogonis, of a catoecic allotment of … arouras … and in Teptynis …
5     to the two, Didyme… and Thenapynchis and Z…a; but if … my [daughters] perform the burial of my body and the laying-out,

7     and now the aforesaid building-plots and the … Kalliope …
8     and on the same terms as also from the reading … as is set forth above.
