[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Heras son of Sarapion from a village
2     of Karanidos, Aurelii Ptollas [and] Mela
3     and Apion son of Isidorus from the same
4     village. We received from you for embankment work, wage-labor two, navalia 2, of the
5     fourth new indiction and
6     being questioned, we agreed. I, Aurelius Melas, wrote [this].
