[TRANSLATION]
1     [Theon to Sambas the oil-maker: provide] two and one-half quarter xestai of oil to Neilos the builder for salary
2     of coin 1 3, for the beautification of the hay-store of Centaur and of pit and of machine-platform and machine
3     to the brick-yard and [of] 2 doors of stables of Syrians and of hay-store of the settlement of Panse, … of blocking
4     [the work] having been made through Makarios the deacon and Apa Horus the builder, wage-earners, and Apa Neilos the chief builder
5     of oil xestai 2 one-half quarter only. Pauni 5, at the completion of the 13th indiction. I noted oil xestai two and one-half quarter only. By me, Poosis.
