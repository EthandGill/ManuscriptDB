[TRANSLATION]
1     Helen [daughter] of Hermaeus, together with [the authority of] my own husband Sarapion, [son of] Antas, to Eutychides, [son of] Eudaemon the elder, greetings.
2     I have leased to you for six years from the present 2nd year of Do[mitian] Caesar Augustus, [the lands with] rain-watered crops and fruit[-crops]...
3     six, [the lands] belonging to me from the allotment of Menelaos near ... Patre, upriver, [of] fourteen arouras...
4     with fixed rent of the aforesaid arouras, each year wheat, [of] seventy-five artabas, making wheat artabas 75...
5     in Athenian measure, sixth-part, Paubastius, [son] of Horos, from the village of Terton Samou. And from that place I have been measured [the grain] from you through Sarapion my husband [and] through Eudaemon your father all the aforesaid...
6     rents of the six-year [period], which is from the present 2nd year until the seventh year and even the 7th year...
7     [I am] satisfied and I have no claim against you about anything whatsoever, all the public [taxes], both grain and money-[taxes] being [on me]. And in the seventh year, the last [year], in the month...
8     Epeiph, I will measure from you other wheat, artabas [of] eighteen, making wheat artabas 18, in the sixth-part measure of the same Paubastius...
9     Horos. [In the] second year of the Emperor Caesar Domitian Augustus, [in] the month of August, [on the] thirtieth. Sarapion...
10    [of] Helen [I] have signed [as guardian] of my wife, and [I] have received all the rents, and I have written on behalf of...
11    her [who does] not know letters.
