[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master
2     Jesus Christ, the God and Saviour
3     [of us] and of our Lady
4     the holy Mother of God and of [all the]
5     saints. Year 397 of Diocletian, Epeiph 19, [of the indiction, in] the [city of the] Arsinoites.
6     Aurelius Georgios, son of Apollos, from
7     the village of Ptolemais Peristereonos
8     of the Arsinoite nome, to the most reverent
9     Sergios, deacon, son of Paulos, from the
10    city of the Arsinoites, greeting. I acknowledge
11    that I have leased from you the (plots) belonging
12    to you in the plain of the same village
13    in the place called Paratot…
14    five arouras of sowable land, more or less,
15    with all the right pertaining to them, for
16    a three-year period, counted from
17    the crops of the tenth indiction, God willing, and I shall furnish
18    to you, by way of rent for them, yearly
19    one and a third gold solidus
20    by count, one and a third gold solidus by count, at the month of
21    Pachon of each year. The lease
22    is valid, and on being formally questioned I gave my assent. Georgios, son of Apollos,
23    the aforesaid, [it is in order with me] as is set forth above. Konon, son of the
24    blessed Hor, I bear witness to this lease as is set forth above.
25    Georgios, son of Kyrikos, I bear witness to this
26    lease as is set forth above.
27    Through me, Kallinikos. Through me, Kallinikos.
28    Lease of five arouras of sowable land, more or less … … …
