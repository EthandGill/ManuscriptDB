1     Biegis, mother Jemouthes, has written off
2     Tesauros, master of hand-workers, linen-workers, of private persons of Elephantine, year 14
3     of silver drachmas twelve, comes to drachmas 12. through Leon
4     scribe. Year 15 of Trajan Caesar
5     of the lord, Phaophi 21.
6     Gellius Capito, I have been noted through
7     Pachompretes of Ammonates, scribe
