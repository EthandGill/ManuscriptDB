1     … of [y]ou[r] administration …
2     such (men) defining their (own) people who take refuge into [ea]c[h]
3     pagar chy, with respect to which we have subjoined to you in the pres ent letter
4     sending to them some competent and faith ful registered (man), who must
5     together with them be found in company with those set over the fugitives

10    to learn full knowledge, ordering them to stand vigorously
11    to their wor k and to take from no one anything whatsoever at all
12    for a gratuity. For you shall be aware that, if anyone
13    of those sent by you is convicted of having received from any person whatever
14    a gra tuity, the danger looks toward you, and you will, together with [the] one convicted,
