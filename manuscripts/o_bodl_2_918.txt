1     Phareis and Petemenofis, tax collectors of silver tax
2     of the Potteries, to Pamonthos of Psenamounios. We have received
3     for geometric tax of year 3, soiled drachmas four, obols 3 and a half
4     they make drachmas 4, obols 3 and a half. Year 5 of Antoninus
5     and Verus, of [our] lords the Augustuses
6     Thoth 8…, I have marked [this].
