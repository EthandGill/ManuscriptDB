[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the keepers of the property-archive of the Hermopolite (nome)]
2     [from Aurelius Theognostos] also called M[o]ros, son of Hermaios also called Pathotes
3     son of Achilleu[s, of the Hermopolite (nome), registered i]n the (quarter of the) Eastern City, [I reg]ister for the present 6th year
4     of M[arcus Aurelius Severus] Alexander Caesar the lord
5     with the consent of Isidoros my brother, of the same parents,
6     registered in [the same] quarter, (the property) which I bought from him according to the bank-register in the same
7     6th year in the month [Mes]ore, a third share of a house and courtyard and appurtenances
8     and all [ap]purtenances and entrance and exit, in Hermoupolis
9     in the (quarter of the) Western Fort; and of the register I have presented to you the copy in a separate document.
10    [Wherefore] I report and swear by the Fortune of Marcus Aurelius
11    Severus Alexander Pius Felix Augustus
12    that this is so. Year 6 of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius
13    Severus Alexander Pius Felix Augustus,
14    Mesore 7. I, Aurelius Theognostos also called Moros,
15    son of Hermaios, [have pre]sented (this) and have sworn the
16    oath. I, [Aure]lius Isidoros son of Hermeos,
17    [con]sent to him. (I), [Aur]elius Chairemon also called Dioskoros, councillor,
18    keeper-of-the-archives, through … the secretary, have received a copy.
