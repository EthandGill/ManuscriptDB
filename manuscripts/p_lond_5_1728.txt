[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] reign of our most divine and most pious master
2     [Flavius Ma]urice Tiberius, the eternal Augustus and Emperor
3     [and] greatest benefactor, in the third year, on Phamenoth the twelfth
4     of the second indiction, in Syene.
5     [Flavius] John son of Jacob, his mother being Tapias, a soldier, a recruit, of the legion
6     [of Syen]e, to Aurelia Kako, my own full-blood sister,
7     [origina]ting from the same Syene, greeting. A quarrel having arisen
8     [betw]een us concerning property held in common, it seemed good to us
9     … to me oaths of the holy places of prayer, and this …
10    … accordingly I acknowledge that I no longer have any claim against you
11    concerning the property held in common, except for the documents
12    … and of the house and of the timbers of the boat
13    … of the mast. Having therefore sought from me a written
14    [se]curity concerning this, accordingly I acknowledge, swearing
15    [by] the consubstantial Trinity, that it shall not be permitted to me ever, at any time
16    [or sea]son, to bring suit against you concerning the same inheritance and
17    [proper]ty; and if it should seem good to us to bring suit against you, (we agree) that we shall in nothing
18    profit, but shall furnish, by way of penalty for the transgression,
19    three gold [solidi] by the scale of Syene, exacted by deed and by force
20    [ex]acted from my substance; and for your security
21    [I have made] the security, valid and binding, with the subscription of him who subscribes on my behalf, and on being questioned
22    [I] acknowledged it. Flavius John son of Jacob, the aforewritten, have made this release of claim, and all
23    [the] things written are agreeable to me, as aforesaid. Theophilos son of Paeion,
24    [the least] deacon, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
25    [ ] Phib son of Phaustsan … …
26    [in] public I bear witness.
27    Through me, Markos son of Apa Dios, it was written.
