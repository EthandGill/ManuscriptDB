[TRANSLATION]
1     [I ano]k Frange [I s]alute [and] greet his father David and his mother Mesiane, I greet also my children
2     day by day I greet to my brother Ananias
3     and Sabek, a joy
4     in the Lord
5     [do] not forget the gladness
6     of the sons of rest, you
7     who are sleepy, come here [in]
8     a village, you hold a place
9     in the place of the village
