[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the … year of Imperator] Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, in the month Apellaios 6, Phaophi 6, in Nilopolis of the [Herakleides division of the Arsinoite] nome. Papeis, son of Papeis, about 20 years old, with a scar on his left arm, acknowledges to Tanephremmis alias Thenyris, daughter of Pane[phremmis, about … years old, without distinguishing mark], with as guardian her own husband Onnophris, son of Chairas, about 56 years old, with a scar on the forehead on the left, that [the acknowledging party] has sold [according to this] agreement, from now for all time, the (property) belonging to him in the village of Soknopaiou Nesos by [inheritance from his deceased] full brother Stotoetis, the third part of a house and courtyard and light-well, which by registration is a fourth part of the
2     [house] … and all the appurtenances; neighbors: on the south the house of Stotoetis the elder, son of Stotoetis the carpenter, on the north
3     the houses [of Onnophris husband of Tanephremmis the purchaser], on the west the royal street; and that the acknowledging party has received from Tanephremmis the
4     [agreed price in full,] the whole, at once, by hand, from house, of silver, in capital five hundred drachmas; and that the acknowledging party shall guarantee
5     [the (matters) of this sale forever] with every guarantee, against both public and private (claims) of all kinds, against all poll-taxes from the
6     [former times up to the future] other survey by house-by-house registration, and against private (claims) and every claim for all time,
7     [and that no one shall come against nor lay] claim to the sold third part of the house nor a part of it in any way, as is set forth, [and no one shall hinder Tanephremmis] nor those acting for her, Tanephremmis being mistress of the sold third part of the house, entering [and going out, demolishing] and rebuilding and repairing and selling and mortgaging and conveying to others … and [using it as she may] choose; and that with regard to the foregoing neither the acknowledging party nor those acting for him shall bring any claim nor shall bring
8     [nor dispute in any way; but] whatever of the aforewritten the acknowledging party or he acting for him shall transgress, let him pay in addition to
9     [Tanephremmis both the expenses] doubled and the price he received doubled, and as penalty two hundred and fifty silver drachmas [and to the treasury] the same, and none the less let the agreed (matters) be valid. Subscribers: for the acknowledging party, Dios[koros …] about …3 years old, with a scar on the right knee; for the other (party), the registered guardian. I, Papeis son of Papeis, acknowledge that I have sold to Tanephre[mmis alias Thenyris, from now] for all time the (property) belonging to me in the village of Soknopaiou Nesos by inheritance from my deceased brother [Stotoetis, the third part] of a house and courtyard and light-well, which by registration is a fourth part, common and undivided, and all the appurtenances; neighbors: on the south
10    the house [of Stotoetis the carpenter], on the north the houses of Onnophris son of Cheiras, husband [of Tanephremmis] the purchaser, on the west the royal street; and I receive the
11    [agreed price] in full at once by hand from house, of silver five hundred drachmas, and I shall guarantee with every guarantee as set forth. There wrote
12    [on his behalf Dioskoros because] he does not know letters. I, Tanephrymis alias Thenyris, daughter of Panephrymis, with as guardian [my] husband [Onnophris son] of Cheiras, have purchased as set forth. I, Onnophris, wrote on behalf of Tanephremmis [because she does not know] letters.
13    It has been entered through the record-office of the village of Nilopolis.
