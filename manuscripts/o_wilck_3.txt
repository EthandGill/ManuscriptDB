1   Has paid:
2   Zmeuthis son of Pachnoubis,
3   for the poll-tax of the fifth year
4   of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
5   Pharmouthi 27: eight silver drachmas — total 8 dr.
6   (2nd hand) I, Apollonios, have checked it.
