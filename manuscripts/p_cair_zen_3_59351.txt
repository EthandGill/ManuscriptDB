[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greetings, Zenon. I am wronged by Herakleides; for I gave to him in the third year
2     through Demeus gold drachmas 400, so that he should strike off on my behalf into the royal [account] copper drachmas 400, on the condition that having received [this] from me he would repay me,
3     and though I have repaid him and demanded it from him, he has not repaid [me], but has put me off until now. I beseech
4     then you, if it seems good to you, to order Agenor the strategos to call him back, and if it is true, to compel him
5     having received the 400 drachmas of copper to repay to me the 400 drachmas of gold, and may I through you obtain justice.
6     Farewell.
