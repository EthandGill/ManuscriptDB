[TRANSLATION]
1     [Pete]sokhos to Alexander, gree[tings. A messenger] has been sent by us …
2     … [stationed] at the import[ation of wine] and the hundredth [tax] of the places in
3     Philadelphia. If then some wine-vessels or any other thing [as] security is held
4     through you toward the [completion] of the imported [wine], through this let it be held
5     until [it] falls on the bank. And [the] account of Pauni present
6     to him so that he [may] follow up. Farewell. Year 8, Pauni 7.
7     [To] Alexander.
