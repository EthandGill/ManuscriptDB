[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Belisarius the most glorious, month Mesore 4, of the 13th indiction.
2     To Flavius Strategius, the all-praised, ex-consul, general,
3     most renowned patrician, leading citizen both in the Herakleopolite
4     and in this splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas
5     his servant who puts the formal question and supplies to his own master,
6     the same all-praised man, the right of action and obligation: Aurelius
7     Victor, son of Anouthios, his mother being Tekrampe, originating from the hamlet
8     of Little Terytheos of Aetios in the Oxyrhynchite nome, an estate of
9     your Eminence, a registered cultivator and brother of the steward,
10    greeting. A need having now arisen as well, regarding the landlord's mill that is under me and the other
11    fellow-tenant cultivators with me,
12    which mill is situated on our estate, of one basket(-capacity),
13    having gone up to the city I petitioned your Eminence
14    through the most illustrious persons attending upon it, to order to be given
15    for that same aforesaid mill the same basket(-stone), for
16    the refurbishment of the same mill; and indeed this he ordered and
17    he provided me the spindle and basket(-stone), through Helios the local
18    millstone-cutter, new and fit for use, on the present day, which
19    is Mesore the fourth of the current year 211/180, of the present
20    thirteenth indiction, of the fourteenth bread-baking of the fourteenth assessment,
21    and for the security and proof of your house this
22    deed of hand I have made, which, being valid, written in single copy, and on being formally questioned,
23    I acknowledged. Aurelius Victor, son of Anouthios, the aforewritten,
24    have made this deed of hand, having received the
25    aforesaid spindle and basket(-stone) of the mill, and everything agrees with me
26    as aforesaid. Aurelius Anoup, son of Joseph, having been requested, wrote
27    on his behalf, he being illiterate.
28    Through me, Theon, it was completed. di emu Theonos eteliothe.
29    Deed of hand of Victor, son of Anouthios, from the hamlet of Little Terytheos
30    of Aetios, of receipt
31    of one basket(-stone).
