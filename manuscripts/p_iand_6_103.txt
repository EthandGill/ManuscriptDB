r.17   ἐπίδος τῷ ἁγιωτάτῳ καὶ θεοσεβεστάτῳ [ἀ]ββᾷ Κορνηλίῳ διάκονι παρὰ Σαβίνου ἐλαχίστου.

[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Cornelius the deacon, from Sabinos the least. Before
2     all things I pray to the all-merciful God that in health you may receive the greeting from me;
3     and deign to greet, as from me, the brother abba Dorotheos and the lady
4     his mother and the lords Makarios and Eusebes. Know that
5     I have sent you four vessels of oil and one small one, of two solidi and two carats
6     in fullness, concerning which you wrote to me; and deign to send me the
7     little account of these. And likewise I have se[nt you] nine new [j]ars of oil; and deign
8     to provide to mother Elias a j[ar ] … and to her who lives as a virgin one jar, and to the brother
9     Dorotheos one jar, and [the rest] deign to keep for yourself. And learn
10    whether abba Makarios has any need, [and wri]te back to me; and if he wishes, the solidus
11    of the iron implement, deign to provide it, which I sent you. And deign from every side
12    to receive solidi and send them to me. And since you wrote to me about the
13    little ounce(-weight), I have not yet found it, and, God willing, I send it to your holiness. And remember
14    about the silver sextarius, and send it to me from the house of lord Victor,
15    for I forgot it. Your servant Lykatos greets you, he who also wrote this.
16    And pray for me.
17    Deliver to the most holy and most God-fearing [a]bba Cornelius the deacon, from Sabinos the least.
