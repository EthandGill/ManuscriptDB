1     from me — of which property already agreed upon …
2     the boundaries, inside and out, on all four sides, are these: on the south,
3     on the western side, the boundary of the arouras of my father
4     Isakios; on the eastern side, a lane running …
5     toward the east, to the hamlet called Psinios; on the east,
6     [on the southern side] …
7     … [and] on the
8     northern side, arouras of the same my father Isakios; on the north,
9     the property of the heirs of Apollos son of Dioskoros son of Pharatops;
10    on the west, arouras of the same my father Isakios — or whatever the
11    neighbors may be on every side all around of the aforesaid property, in the southern
12    plain of the same village of Aphrodite, in the holding called
13    [ … ] … of the price mutually agreed upon between us and
14    found satisfactory, complete and worthy, in gold imperial approved
15    solidi, ten in number, each less two carats, by the
16    goldsmith's standard of the same village of Aphrodite,
17    making 10 gold solidi less 20 carats, which gold of the price I have received, I the seller
18    John son of Isakios surnamed Beskouis, from you the purchaser
19    Apollos son of Joseph, from your hand into my hands, in the sight of the
20    lawful witnesses subscribing below, from now for
21    perpetuity, full in number and in weight, together with the sale that has taken place,
22    with which price I am also satisfied; on condition that you pay the
23    various public dues on its account — both embole and gold-tax and freight
24    and all sorts of annona-charges — according to the registration of the public
25    code of John of pious memory, the deceased
26    advocate and census-officer, [or], should it so happen, another assessment
27    that may be ratified in the Antaiopolite district, from the tax-roll
28    of the now begun eighth indiction, both of it and of the following time
29    forever to the uttermost; so that from now you yourself the purchaser
30    Apollon, son of Joseph, most admirable, of the assistants, may possess and
31    own and be master of the aforesaid half of my
32    share of the third share of property of my mother's, with all its
33    right, according to all the distinctions stated above, and may dwell in
34    and administer and manage and farm and reap and let out for rent,
35    and sell or also alter and transmit to your own
36    heirs and successors and possessors, and use and enjoy
37    in whatever manner of ownership seems good to you, unhindered and unimpeded,
38    with no one laying claim or about to lay claim against you of those after me,
39    heirs or co-heirs or those belonging to me by family
40    or kinship; and whoever shall hereafter come against you concerning
41    the aforewritten half of my share of the third
42    share of property of my mother's, with all its right according to the above
43    distinction, I myself will turn him away from you at my own expense and
44    cost; and I will warrant it to you with every warranty against everyone and
45    forever against him who comes or shall lay claim, in every way and of necessity;
46    and if I take less care of the warranty, I shall pay back the price threefold,
47    together with the loss and the expense, so that
48    this sale be secure and valid wherever it is produced,
49    which, written in duplicate, written in identical form, willingly and persuaded
50    I have made for you, without force and constraint and deceit and any error
51    and fear and any fraud, in the public archive and completed according to the laws
52    with my subscription and that of the customary witnesses below who, at my
53    invitation, have borne witness, I have issued to you for security;
54    and for all that has been written, and for the warranty and clearing
55    of this sale, there are pledged to you all my belongings
56    present and future, both movable and immovable and self-moving, generally and particularly, in every kind and class, by way of pledge
57    and by right of mortgage, as by legal judgment. And to all that has been written,
58    on being questioned I gave my acknowledgment. It being clear that, of the aforewritten
59    third share of property of my mother's, which is held in common with you the aforesaid Apollon
60    son of Joseph, who has now purchased the other half share which came
61    to you by lawful sale from Phoibammon my brother
62    on the mother's side, through his father Mathias son of Psempnouthios son of Psyros,
63    the sale being secure and valid wherever it is produced
64    in accordance with its force, and upon all these things …
65    it being clear that I have sold to you my sixth share of the dwelling and of the house
66    and of all rights. I, Aurelius John son of Isakios, the aforesaid, have made
67    this sale and received the aforesaid full price of ten
68    solidi, we will warrant it to you with every warranty against everyone and
69    forever, and I have sworn the divine oath as set forth above. Aurelius
70    Makarios son of Phoibammon, I bear witness to the sale, having heard from the maker;
71    and I bear witness also to the payment of the gold in ten solidi of the
72    price. Aurelius Onnophris son of Thomas, I bear witness to the sale, having heard from
73    the maker; and I bear witness also to the payment of the gold of the ten solidi
74    of the price. Flavius Pneis son of Joseph, I bear witness to the sale, having heard from the maker;
75    and I bear witness also to the payment of the gold of the ten solidi of the price.
76    Aurelius John son of Victor, I bear witness to the sale, having heard from the
77    maker; and I bear witness also to the payment of the ten solidi of the price.
78    Aurelius Promauos son of Hermauos, I bear witness to the sale and to the payment
79    of the gold of the ten solidi of the price, having heard from the maker.
80    Written by me, Pilatos, notary.
