[TRANSLATION]
1     [P]sonaeus, about thirty-eight years old, of medium build, honey-complexioned, long-faced, with a straight nose, a scar under the right [knee].
2     [P]somsnaeus [son] of Praxias, of those from Philadelphia, [of the Herac]leidan division of the Arsinoite nome, to those [beside]…
3     [Ca]ttius Catylos, centurion; I swear by Tiberiu[m].
4     [C]laudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Empero[r].
5     [If] I have indeed willingly pledged Praxias [son] of Diodor[us].
6     My father, and [Tha]e[sis] [daughter] of Anubion, my mother, whom I shall also present to Nemesius [the collector] [of].
7     [The] same village in the public record office [of Herac]leides within twenty-five days from [the present] day; but if I do not present [them], I shall pay [the debt with].
8     [Interest] to the public [treasury]. If I swear truly, on my [behalf].
9     May [it] go well; but if I swear falsely, on the other hand, may the opposite [befall me]. Nicanor [son] of Nicano[r].
10    [I wrote] on behalf of him, having been deemed worthy, because of [his] claiming [him] not to [kn]ow [letters]. Year 6 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar [Aug]ustus Germanicus Emperor, Epeiph thirtieth.
11    Dositheos [I have confirmed]. Year 6 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar.
12    Augustus Germanicus Emperor, Epeiph thirtieth.
