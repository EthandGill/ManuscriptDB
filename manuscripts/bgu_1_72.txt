[TRANSLATION]
1     To Didymos, strategus of [the] Arsinouites, [the] Herakleid division
2     from [S]abeinos [Z]osimos, tax-collector
3     of the public village of Karaneidios
4     at [the] night [the one] leading into the 27th
5     of the present month of Mechir, there came some [men], whose names I do not know, [and] my vegetable seeds around the present village, and [they] cut down [a] large area
6     in five arouras not little harm followed· whe[refore] I ask this the equivalent
7     in [my] record become
8     to the [one] responsible to me appearing· farewell.
9     Of year 31 of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
10    Antoninus Caesar the master
11    Mechir 27
