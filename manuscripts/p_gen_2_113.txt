1     [Year] … of [Lucius] Septimius Severus
2     [Pius Pertinax] and Marcus Aurelius
3     [Antoninus Pius, Augusti, and Publius
4     Septimius Geta Caesar Augustus], Pachon 22. Longinus also called Zosimos and Sarapion son of Heron and
5     … son of Sokrates and partners, sitologoi (grain-officials) in the allotment
6     [of the village of] Karanis, we have measured out in the granary of the
7     [same] village by the public smoothed measure the levy [from the produce of the] same year: of the katoikoi (settlers) of Kerkesoucha,
8     … Heraïskos, with the additional measures, [of wheat]
9     [twelve and a quarter artabas], total of wheat 12¼ artabas, and the surcharge.
10    … through the wife. Of Karanis,
11    [of the katoikoi, the same man], of wheat twelve artabas, total of wheat 12 artabas.
12    … [with] the additional measures, [of wheat] five artabas,
13    total of wheat [5] artabas … of the katoikoi, the same man, of wheat
14    [fifteen] artabas, [with] the additional measures, total of wheat 15 artabas. Of Kerkesoucha
15    katoikoi, [the same man] … with the additional measures, [of wheat]
16    [thirty]-nine artabas, total of wheat 39 artabas and the surcharge.
17    Pauni 7, likewise Hieras of the katoikoi, with the additional measures, of wheat
18    eleven and a half and a third artabas, total of wheat 11½ ⅓ artabas. The 9th, likewise,
19    of the katoikoi of Kerkesoucha, the same man, with the additional measures, of wheat 29⅓ artabas and the surcharge.
20    Hieras of the katoikoi, the same man, of wheat five and two-thirds artabas,
21    total of wheat 5⅔ artabas with the additional measures. Of the katoikoi of Ptolemais, the same man, of wheat
22    ½ ⅓ 1/24 artabas, total of wheat ½ ⅓ 1/24 artaba and the surcharge. Of the katoikoi of Karanis, 1 1/6 1/24 artaba of wheat,
23    total of wheat 1 1/6 1/24 artaba. I, Sarapion, have measured out as is set forth above.
