[TRANSLATION]
1     at the same time [as] Serenus the goldsmith
2     the friend coming to us, having [heard] … matters concerning you …
3     ... [on the] day of the gymnasiarchs [I] was ready to come
4     to you and to serve [you].
5     the gods indeed giving [it] well,
6     you will have. For Serenus the friend says
7     that he is free from fever. And this
8     [is] good hope. [He] greets
9     you, Sarapion my son, and the mother
10    of him. Your children are well.
11    I pray that you be well, brother.
12    Epeiph 2
13    To Sarapion the brother from Abaskantus.
