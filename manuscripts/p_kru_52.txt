[TRANSLATION]
1     [I,] Petros [the s]on of the late Komes, the man of the [f]ortress [of Dj]eme,
2     [the] nome of the city of Ermont, write to Daniel the son of the late
3     [Pach]om, and Tanope the daughter of Abraham, the wife of the late Solom[on],
4     [the man] of this same fortress, Djem[e], [the nome] of this same city, greetings.
5     [Since] I sued [yo]u before our … lord [S]ouleima[n]
6     [the] most glorious amir, concerning some vessels which you took by [thef]t
7     from within [our house] … … … we prepared
8     against you Demetr[ios] and Cha[el] the administrators, that they should exact from you
9     the whole booty which you took from within our house in a theft,
10    [you] came, you, [D]aniel, and T[ano]pe, with some other great children
11    in the fortress, they asked me on your behalf, because you were not able to be solvent and give the booty to us which you took from within our house. We came to terms with you
12    for ten holokottinoi and two trimesia. Now we acknowledge
13    that we have nothing reasonable against you ever, you,
14    Daniel and [Ta]no[pe], over the whole booty which you took from within our house, because we have settled with you over this whole booty. Whoever, at any time, shall come
15    out against you ever, neither I, nor a brother, nor [our] son,
16    nor any man who belongs to my family, su[in]g
17    you concerning these vessels of this kind, because I have settled
18    with you — we shall sue you before any authority
19    that is exalted and glorious, or we shall set an attack against him
20    in any court — whoever shall dare to do this, first
21    he is alien to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, afterward
22    he shall give 36 nomismata of gold to the authority as a penalty, afterward he shall come in
23    and confirm this quittance, valid for you, which we have established, valid,
24    unshakable in every place where they manifest it therein.
25    I, Markos the son of Patermouthios, bear witness.
26    Papnouthios the son of Kyriakos, bear witness.
27    We, Demetrios the son of the late Le[on]ti[os] and Chael the son of Psmo, bear w[itness].
28    I, Demetrios, wrote for Chael, since he does not know how.
29    I, Israel the son of Paulos, bear witness.
30    I, Sam[uel the s]on of the late …, [bear] witness.
31    Through me, Aristophanes son of Iohannes, it was done.
32    I, Heleias the son of the late Komes, bear witness.
33    I, Isaak [the son] of the late Zacharias, they asked me,
34    I wrote for them, since they do not know how to write.
