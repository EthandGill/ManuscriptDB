[TRANSLATION]
1   Petorzmethis son of Petrozmethis paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the 12th year of Domitian
3   the lord, on account 12 drachmas. (2nd hand) likewise 4 drachmas,
4   total 16 dr. I, Socrates, the collector, wrote (this).
