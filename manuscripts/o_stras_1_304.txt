[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 51, Pachon 1, [he] has measured into the
2     declaration of the same year, [of] Ara Petearsmethes.
3     Pekoibios, of wheat artabas eight and one-half and one-quarter; [they] total of wheat artabas eight and one-eighth.
4     The same … of wheat artabas nine and one-eighth; [they] total of wheat artabas nine and one-eighth.
5     [They] total of wheat artabas eighteen and one-half.
6     Zeta.
7     Paniskos, of wheat artabas eighteen and one-half.
