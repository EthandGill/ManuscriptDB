[TRANSLATION]
1     To Gaius Tyrranios.
2     From Satabous son of Pisois and from his son Pisois, of those from [the] Arsinoite (nome),
3     public farmers and lessees of certain sacred lands, paying for the public lands which we hold always, year by year, on account of wheat 800 artabas and 1500 drachmas of silver. And in the payment
4     having been hitherto blameless, but because we owe nothing, having been envied [by]
5     certain unjust men of the nome, who are always disposed rather rebelliously …
6     … against our affliction, having been sufficiently wronged, (we have recourse) to the jurisdiction (which comes) from you …
7     … that we may obtain help. For Opis and Opis, both sons of Petosiris …
8     … ventured, having handed over to Kordos who superintended the guards …
9     … asserting that their father had departed from life toward …
10    … in custody. But having presented ourselves and made our defence, we appeared to Kordos [guiltless]
11    [of the] false accusation, so that, had it not been for the very [great entreaty] which they made of both us and Kordos,
12    … according to the laws (we would have been) in danger of our lives, because they had not established (the charge) …
13    … and they wrote that this had been settled with him. But before we were [released] from the prison …
14    Kordos having been removed, Breison came to be in his place, whom also …
15    … by the renewal of the proceeding will defraud (us); others, brothers of these, …
16    against him by an accusation concerning the same matters, although the laws forbid being tried twice concerning the same (matter).
17    [And] when we had come before Breison, with Diodoros also present, of those in the
18    nome who had served as gymnasiarch, and a sufficient contest of our advocates having taken place,
19    they did not even thus establish that we had done any such thing, on account of which they themselves, contrary to all
20    [justice], being in the prison, and for this reason the farmlands being ruined …
21    … I foresaw that it had become unmanageable, both of the gathering-in
22    … of the season, we ask you to write to the strategos of the nome
23    [having released] us from the confinement and having investigated each matter accurately, if also to him according to the
24    … and to the … we appear (such as) to release, so that, when we are engaged in the
25    farming of the lands, no loss may follow for the public revenues, and that he may determine
26    … wicked, they having fallen under the punishment according to the [laws],
27    that we may be benefited. Farewell.
