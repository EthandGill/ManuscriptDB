[TRANSLATION]
1     Andromachos to Harchebis, greeting. Of the official document addressed to Leonides the sitologos
2     a copy is appended below. Farewell. Year 4, Choiach 2.
3     To Leonides. To the crown farmers from … of the Lower toparchy
4     measure out, Harchebis the royal scribe joining in the order,
5     for the seed-loans of the sowing for the 4th year, from the (grain) deducted
6     through the previous orders into the reduced portion
7     of that (assigned) by the dioiketes, 400 artabas, those measured out on account of the
8     weakness affecting them — concerning which also Tothoes the topogrammateus
9     has many times testified beforehand — to those who have previously given written undertaking,
10    swearing the royal oath, to sow over all the crown
11    land around the village, and that of the queen and of the other revenues,
12    and to leave none unsown, and to deliver these
13    and the rest of the previously-ordered loans and the rents in
14    Pachon and Pauni of the fourth year, exempt from all
15    deduction, being mutual sureties for one another for the payment of the
16    distribution made man by man through the (decision) of Tothoes and
17    of the others customarily (involved), four hundred artabas of
18    olyra (emmer); total, 400 artabas of olyra; and make out a receipt and
19    counter-receipt with them as is fitting.
20    To the scribes, year 4, Choiach 2.
21    To Herakleodoros, year 4, Choiach 2.
22    To Harchebis.
