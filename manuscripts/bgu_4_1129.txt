[TRANSLATION]
1     Sheet (kollema).
2     To Protarchos.
3     From Tryphon son of Simon, Roman citizen of Caesar [and from] Protarchos son of Zeneios, [in the presence and with the consent]
4     of the woman Opora daughter of Protarchos, designated in the agreement,
5     a citizen, with her guardian Protarchos himself. Since, according to the agreement completed before the same tribunal
6     in the present 17th year of Caesar, in the month Mecheir, Protarchos acknowledged, with the consent also of the woman,
7     that, apart from the capital sum which he received in advance from Tryphon, having paid in the remainder of the
8     established price, namely 1,400 drachmas of silver, by the 5th of Pharmouthi of the same year, he will cede to Tryphon, or to whomever he may direct, by another agreement, from the property belonging to him, Protarchos, in the Busirite (nome), in the toparchy of Phtheurie, around
9     the village of Thonophoi, of catoecic land of two hundred arouras, forty-nine and a half, an eighth, a sixteenth arouras, of which there are, in one parcel,
10    3, an eighth, a sixteenth arouras, whose neighbours are: to the south the remaining lands which Tryphon has bought, of arouras …
11    to the north the (lands) by Naro, to the east a hill, to the west … of the Alexandrians; and in another
12    parcel the remaining 46 and a half arouras, whose neighbours are: to the south
13    the land of Chelkias, to the north a dyke, to the east hills, to the west (the land) of Heron and Chelkias,
14    land [and] under whatever other conditions the agreement contains; that now,
15    in accordance with the foregoing, Protarchos has received from Tryphon
16    through the bank of Castor the 1,400 drachmas of silver, and he agrees
17    that he has ceded to Tryphon, from now for
18    all time, the aforesaid 49 and a half, an eighth, a sixteenth arouras, and that it shall be permitted
19    to Tryphon to hold and own them and to cede them to others and
20    to lease them out and to carry off the produce from them
21    that shall be, year by year, from the coming 18th year of Caesar, since the produce of the present year
22    belongs to Protarchos, and to manage and to carry out concerning them whatever he wishes,
23    and to complete also whatever other deeds he may wish, transferring (the title)
24    from this
25    agreement and the copies pertaining to it
26    onto the places … the allotment-registers … and …
27    and to have the records re-registered … without needing the presence of Protarchos
28    for the duplicate copy …
29    that he (Protarchos) shall lay no claim against any of these, and that, should he transgress, neither
30    Protarchos nor anyone on his behalf shall carry out any of the duplicate measures, [and that he shall furnish] the sale as guaranteed and
31    unencumbered and clear of all public [and other] [charges] up to the same
32    17th year of Caesar, [and from … ] of private (charges) for the time before,
33    and that he shall ward off, at his own expense, anyone who shall come against (it); and if he
34    transgresses any of these things, besides the agreed terms remaining valid he shall in addition pay
35    that which he received, [the capital ceded, together with the half] as well [and other] as a penalty 1,000 drachmas of silver,
36    and the fixed fine, as if by judgement; … and if … Protarchos
37    to [Tryphon] and … owes, of the two hundred arouras belonging (to him) … after his own …
38    … [year] 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 12.
