[TRANSLATION]
1     … of Hyperberetaios 2. Acknowledgment concerning a deposit.
2     In the consulship for the 3rd time and for the 1st, on the 6th day before the Nones of October, and in the year, according to the former reckoning, the 562nd of the second (era), in the month Hyperberetaios on the second, in … Aurelia
3     Gaia daughter of Saturnilus, of Dura, dwelling here, having the right of children,
4     acknowledged to Amaththabeile, of Dura, dwelling likewise here,
5     that there had been counted out and that she holds from her on deposit, free of all
6     risk, on the present day, of good approved silver, one hundred denarii
7     unchecked and without addition, which she shall keep with herself and on her own property
8     and shall restore to Amaththabeile whenever … it is demanded back, without delay or postponement; and if, on being demanded, she does not repay, she shall be liable to the prescribed penalties of those who transgress the pledges of deposits, and the right of execution shall belong to Amaththabeile and to anyone else
9     duly producing this acknowledgment on her behalf … [fro]m her,
10    from Gaia, and from her property altogether and in every way. … [in good] faith
11    there has been counted out and Gaia holds on deposit from Amaththabeile
12    the said one hundred denarii, and (that she shall) repay whenever it be dem[an]ded, [and she swo]re the Augustan oath that these things had thus been rightly done … (and that) these things
13    were given; in pledge Amaththabeile questioned [and] in ple[dge Gaia acknowledged.]
14    Aurelius Theodoros son of Bernikianos, of Zeugma, dw[el]ling here, on being requested, I wrote
15    on behalf of Aure[li]a Gaia, illiterate, acknowledging that she ha[s] on deposit denarii
16    one hundred, which she shall also re[pay wh]en it is dem[and]ed.
17    Antonius Polykrates.
18    Ph[lav]ius Valerius, I bear witness.
19    Aurelius Oniakes, I bear witness.
