[TRANSLATION]
1     … you ceded …
2     [it] is not for the cession to be made to our treasury but to the one (appointed) to the liturgy …
3     … you, [who, taking over your] property, will furnish your assessment,
4     [the] civic (assessment), and will
5     fulfil the liturgy. For our treasury does not
6     permit such cessions. And
7     your civic standing will in no way be harmed by this,
8     nor will your person be subjected to outrage. Posted in Alexandria in year 8, Mecheir.
9     To Aurelius Leonides, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
10    [fr]om Aurelius Thonis son of Ptolemaios, his mother being
11    … [from the sam]e city. Since Aurel[ius Heliodoros, the quarter-]scribe, through the
12    [list which he submitted of those performing litur]gies in the coming
13    … [year], [on the el]apsed day nominated me to the collectorship of grain-dues at Thmoi[sephō] of the toparchy of Tholthis, of the settlers,
14    [be]ing utterly unable to bear
15    [the] burden, I withdraw from whatever of mine may be found,
16    [and I request,] submitting to you concerning this these
17    little documents to which I have prefixed the divine
18    ordinance, that you instruct, through one of the
19    attendants about you, the copy to Aurelius Heliodoros,
20    so that, in accordance with the risk of the nomination,
21    following the indisputable things proclaimed, he may take cognizance on my behalf to take over the burden through our (resources) whatever may hold me … . [Year] … of the Emperor Caesar
22    [Marcus Aureliu]s Seve[r]us [Alexande]r.
