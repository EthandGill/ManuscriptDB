[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Pausanias, strategos or rather exactor [and] riparius
2     of the Great Oasis, from Aurelia Sozomene, daughter of M…atios, former magistrate of the city of the Hibites. When my parents had paid the debt of nature while I was still an infant, Makarios, who happened to be older than me
3     in age, whom I would not name a brother, took charge
4     on his own authority, to no benefit ever, of my property and that of my other brother, he too being a minor. For instead of preserving good faith toward us
5     the minors, on the contrary he showed himself an enemy to us,
6     in one respect appropriating to himself all the movable goods of the paternal and maternal
7     inheritance, for which an account is kept by us against him
8     reserved for every occasion before my lord the prefect, and in another respect, being asked for an account … of the affairs which he had taken in hand for the periods, and
9     not having given it, and no less also in the registers of the imperial
10    payments attempting to do us harm because of our being underage and exempt. For a measure of the public oil was being registered by
11    our parents while they were still alive, but it being necessary that through us
12    … that oil, a good measure for each of us … shall be administered equally, all on an equal footing …
13    … to me
14    … himself …
15    Give instructions to Sozomene …
