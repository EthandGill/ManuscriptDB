[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolem]y greeting Philiskos, Cyrenean, holder of one hundred arourae, of those dwelling
2     [in Arsinoe the] at-the-embankment, of Themistos' division. I am wronged by Hede[ia]
3     … [of those dwel]ling the same village. For she took from my wife
4     [me] … to weave two garments on the same loom, of which also the woven threads
5     … [at once] and the work of the two garments, and the one of them
6     [having been woven by] her not being such as to be able to weave the other garment additionally, crafting poorly.
7     [I beseech you therefore,] King, if it seems good to you, to order Poseidon the strategus
8     [to write to Onesand]ros the epistates to send Hedeia to Poseid[ion]
9     … through this petition being true, to compel her
10    … For this having been done, I will not be [wrong]ed, but to you
11    [having fled, the] [common] benefactor of all, I will obtain justice.
12    Farewell.
13    [To Onesand]ros. Having examined, see to it that [she] obtain justice.
