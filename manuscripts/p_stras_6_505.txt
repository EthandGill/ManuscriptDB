[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy … of the record-office of the village
2     of Tebtynis. In the year 1 … of the Emperor Caesar Nerva
3     Trajanus Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, Phaophi 1 …
4     There acknowledge Ischyrion son of Areios son of Maron,
5     about 67 years old, with a scar on his forehead on the right, and his sister by the same father and same mother, who is (his)
6     wife, Herois, about 64 years old, with a scar at the corner of her right eye, opposite
7     … … Herois with the guardian,
8     the son of them both, Didas, about 36 years old, with a scar on his left knee …
9     to Marcus Julius Gemellus the elder …
10    about 20 years old, with a scar on his right knee, that they have sold …
11    to Marcus the house-born slave-boy belonging to them, the acknowledging parties, whose name is
12    Zmaragdos … without distinguishing mark, being from the house-born … of the slave-woman Thaubarion …
13    the … slave Zmaragdos
14    … [that] Marcus Julius [Gemellus] has received
15    [this one], such (a one), not liable to return
16    [except for epilepsy] and the sacred disease, and
17    that the acknowledging parties have received from Gemellus the agreed price
18    of five hundred drachmas of silver in full, from hand, [out] of the house; and they shall guarantee (it)
19    with every guarantee and (free) from every debt
20    up to the present year, forever,
21    except for running-away (of the slave); the rest accordingly.
