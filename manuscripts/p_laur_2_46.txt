[TRANSLATION]
1     I received the letter of your illustrious friendship, and learning from it of the
2     health of you (pl.), I rejoiced greatly, since you wrote to me that "Behold, I have sent
3     those from Thelao in full," and the lord being (present), did you not send the third part (only)?
4     For why did you send me twenty names? For I have there together sixty names
5     of those from Thelao, empty (in vain). If not twenty names, you did not send to me. I am astonished.
6     Send me, then, the remainder and the tax(-list?), so that the others — both the
7     tax(payers?) — do not flee. And I did them no (harm), lord brother. But since, then,
8     Pasimepi fled, and those with him, if (it is) possible, seize him
9     and send him to me with security, so that another not flee. For perhaps he despised me.
10    Our lord intended to proceed (against them), and the lord being (present), until now I have not proceeded
11    against them, (not) one. Do not send me that which the expense lacks; come to me, the lord being (present), brother.
12    If not, I shall find the magister John, and he summoned me, saying that they were not finding the lordly (revenues),
13    whence to eat.
14    So that he may learn, then, that having found the one called … it … and … tiou Iust … ilisas beside our donkey

16    … e … e … ato … e … ta … ios
17    To my master, the … magnificent, honoured … de … k … illustrious … ilom, son. Of Biktor, brother …
