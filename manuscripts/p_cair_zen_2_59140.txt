[TRANSLATION]
1     Alexander to Zenon, greetings, you wrote
2     to me about Critias that he was accusing Charidumus.
3     having sent for
4     then Charidumus, when asked, said
5     none of these [things to be true], but if Critias accuses me of anything, I am ready
6     to be adjudicated with him. Know
7     then [that] Critias writes falsities to you, who even
8     of the soldiers from you
9     spared [me] not, but even against
10    these [soldiers] he has written [complaints] to you.
11    Farewell.
12    Year 30, Pharmouthi
13    3.
14    To Philadelphia.
15    To Zenon.
