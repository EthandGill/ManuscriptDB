[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of our mas]ters [Licinius Au]gu[stus for the 6th time] and Licinius the most illustrious Caesar for the 2nd time.
2     [Copy of the memo]randa of So[st]ratos Aelianus, strategos or rather exactor of the Hermopolite. Epeiph 30, at the northern gate on the public road
3     … Theophanes, [gy]mnasiarch, councillor of Hermopolis, said: while I was away, Adelphios the president made use of dispatches against my son
4     [Anysios, wish]ing to be able to bring suit against him concerning arouras. He was ordered by the excellence of the lord, the most eminent governor, having referred
5     [the documents to your] care … as I think, today is the appointed day. But the land is not Anysios', but his mother's, just as
6     indeed the deed of sale [proves ] … [ I,] being also her husband [and] having a representative authorization for her person, undertake for this reason, her person being subject to pleas of exception,
7     … [because it is not] permitted for him without a dis[pat]ch to plead a suit against the mother. And even if our rights to the arouras are most strongly established, this very
8     [document which I p]roduce [will prove it]. S[ostrato]s said to him: I would have heard the suit, having summoned Adelphios, had not my journey abroad to the
9     [aforewritten] most eminent governor been pressing me. But I see that you have caught me close by the very gates of the road, where, since it is not possible to abandon the dis[patch], the second [appointed] times shall be granted to the arbitration. Theophanes said: what arbitration? Adelphios now has a suit against Anysios
10    … [nor against the m]other, but it is not permitted for him to bring suit against her without the concession from the edict. The s[tra]tegos …
11    [said: ] … plea of exception … he may be able to make use of … of the second appointed times …
