[TRANSLATION]
1     pushed … [letter] to-me will-write
2     For already I understand you, not [by] Zeus, for why did I wait such great time here by detention.
3     Of a certain man, [on-account-of] money do!
4     my brother his small-coin to-prepare
5     until I come. And let mafortium become for me
6     quickly, since I don't have. And take!
7     from Tithoetus the-one bringing to-you
8     these my documents of money
9     two-hundred eighty talents.
10    whenever you wish I shall-give.
11    I greet you all by-name.
12    I pray you to-be-strong
13    for many times.
