[TRANSLATION]
1     π
2     may your excellency deem [it] worthy to send for Jacob the farmer of Graidos and [you will] make
3     him present together with those sent from me, the river-men, to[to] his apparatus, and
4     show them the place of the dike of the newly-planted [vineyard], in order that they work there. [you] must fill
5     the place of the vineyard for the four arouras apart from the dike.
6     I have also written to Theodore the administrator to provide them with [a sum]
7     as earnest-money, that is, of grain artabs two and of silver myriads four hundred fifty.
8     Give [this] to my lord [of] excellency … from Theodore the most illustrious […]
