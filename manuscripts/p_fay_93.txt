[TRANSLATION]
1   To Kastor son of Antiphilos, of the Sosikosmian tribe
2   and Althaian deme,
3   from Sarapion son of Artemidoros
4   son of Ptolemaios, of the quarter
5   of the Other Goose-herds. I wish
6   to lease from you the busi-
7   ness of perfume-selling and unguent-making —
8   wishing (to take), of the half
9   share falling to you, a fourth part —
10   in the Themistes division, (2nd hand) apart from mar-
11   kets together with fairs,
12   for the current
13   2nd year only, at a rent for the whole of
14   forty-five sil-
15   ver drachmas, of which I will
16   make the payment
17   monthly, in equal portions,
18   if it please you to
19   lease it. (3rd hand) I, Kastor son of Antiphilos, have
20   leased as stated above.
21   Year (2) of Antoninus and Verus
22   the lords Augusti,
23   Thoth 5.
