[TRANSLATION]
1     Panakestor to Ze[non gree]tings. I received the letter from you,
2     in which you wrote [you] had sent the plants. Know then immediately on the 29th [they were] brought by pack-animals 20, and the remaining [things] we shall bring on the [… all].
3     You will do well [therefore] and send copper; for it has left us, and we do not
4     have [the means] to provide either for [the] planting of castor-bean and sesame
5     nor to wood-cutting [nor] to the transport of wheat. Make haste also to send cuttings of figs,
6     so that we might graft. Come yourself also and do not act otherwise.
7     Farewell. Year 29, Choiak 30.
8     Year 29, Peritius.
9     Panakestor. Of plants.
10    To Zenon
