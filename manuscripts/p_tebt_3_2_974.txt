[TRANSLATION]
1     … [let there be] Myrtalē
2     [beside] … [obeying him as is fitting, ruling with him in common the property] which is theirs,
3     [all necessary things and the furniture and the clothing and the other things as many as] befit a married woman
4     [let him provide] … [to Myrtalē when at home and when away according to the capacity of the property which belongs] to him, and it shall not be [allowed to him to bring in another woman alongside] Myrtalē nor a concubine nor a [male lover to have nor to father children from another woman nor] another house to inhabit which Myrtalē shall not rule
5     [nor of the property anything to alienate nor to] cast out nor to outrage nor to maltreat
6     [her, if any of these things should be shown that he does or the furniture] or the clothing or the other things does not provide as
7     [is written, let him repay immediately the dowry and one-half] … [and] also for Myrtalē it shall not be [permitted] to be away overnight nor to [be away for a day] …
