[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord and patron, the praepositus
2     of the camp of Dionysias,
3     Thareotes, greeting in God.
4     First of all I pray that you, in good cheer,
5     may receive the letter from me; for I pray also to the Lord
6     for the wholeness (good health) of Konstantios and Domnus.
7     I beseech you, my lord, commending
8     to you the son of my brother, Syrion. He asks you
9     and
10    requests that you give heed to him as to me; for thus I
11    said, that of whatever you have need, my lord
12    provides for you there. I have sent to you, through Ammonios
13    the cobbler, 2 jars of chennion-(wine?), 1 (jar) of dark fish-sauce (garum),
14    1 (jar) of unripe-grape sweet-wine; but a letter to him
15    I did not make, and I gave the sparrows as a pledge (arrabon). Take courage, the sparrows are among them. Since therefore they are still
16    small and white, leid-… , let them grow
17    big, and I will come with you to (deal with) Phamenoth;
18    and have the tow ready, for I am bringing the
19    huntsmen when I come, so that we may make the nets. And I am sending
20    to you also the camels from the middle (station) with letters, instructing you (about) whatever need they have, those who (keep) the
21    sparrows, so that you may report to them; for many do them harm, but to receive (them) back unimpaired; and I will not let them
22    … ar … n
23    -lysome … I pray that you fare well …
24    To my lord the praepositus, (from) Thareotes.
