[TRANSLATION]
1     To Megalonymous, strategos of Arsinoe
2     of Themistos and Polemon divisions
3     from Samba, Isa, and Sarapion
4     of Apollonius [and] Isidoros
5     of Nikandros of the three public
6     farmers of the middle plain
7     of Euhemeria through those from Theadelphia
8     we register according to what was commanded by the most eminent prefect Calvisius Statianus concerning royal land
9     arouras 19 1/2 1/4 which have not been watered in the [present] year of Aurelius
10    Antoninus Caesar
11    our lord. Therefore we present.
12    Isidoros the aforementioned [I] have presented.
13    in the tenth year of Aurelius
14    Antoninus Caesar
15    our lord, Mecheir 30.
