[TRANSLATION]
1     Bisas the goldsmith to Eidoš
2     many greetings. I greet
3     you greatly with the children
4     of you. Fetch the father
5     of mine, the dead [man], and keep [it] safe until when with God
6     I sail back for the [funeral procession].
7     You shall give appropriately. Not again
8     are you careless concerning property. On another [day] the body you shall bury.
9     Eidoš from Bisas the goldsmith
