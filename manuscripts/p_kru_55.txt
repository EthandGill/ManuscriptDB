[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Georgios the s[on] …
2     … of Djeme, the nome of the city
3     [of Herm]ont, write to Petros son of the blessed
4     comes, in this single fort,
5     namely: since concerning the matter of the business which we
6     did about it with one another, we settled [with]
7     one another in respect of the two parties in every matter
8     only — concerning the matter of the loss which I cast
9     upon you, that which I had given you, you rendered account of
10    it to me. Neither I nor a brother, that it (claim) is mine,
11    nor any man acting on my behalf,
12    shall be able to proceed against you, neither you nor
13    your children nor any man, that it (claim) is yours, because
14    you have rendered me account of that which I (would) demand; and
15    we have settled with one another in gold, in
16    silver, in any kind of thing belonging to our business — except if anything
17    concerning the matter of our securities (made) with one another, and
18    with the outlay that is outside (?). We swear by God
19    the Almighty and by the welfare of our sovereigns
20    who rule over us, through the command of God,
21    that I will keep to the force of this written deed, not transgressing
22    it; and the one who shall transgress it shall pay
23    a litra of gold, it being secured for you. I drew up this written deed, valid. Written in the month of Phaophi 1…, of the fourth indiction.
24    I, Georgios son of the blessed Mathios,
25    I consent to the written deed with everything written
26    on it, with my hand. I, Daniel son of the blessed
27    Zacharias, give witness. I, Senouthios (son of) Helias, wrote on his behalf.
28    I, Mena son of Paham, give witness.
29    I, Sergios, wrote on his behalf.
