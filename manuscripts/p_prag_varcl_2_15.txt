[TRANSLATION]
1     From [Alypios].
2     Arsinoe the tower-guard of Kalamaina immediately
3     [having taken] my letter[s]
4     [send] to me,
5     [since necessarily] I [need] him, having added to him also the boy from those with you.
6     I pray [that you be strong], dearest.
7     To Heroninus the manager, Thraseus.
8     Of the year … Mecheir 8.
