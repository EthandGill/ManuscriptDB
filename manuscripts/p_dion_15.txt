[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Cleopatra and Ptolemy, the gods Philometores
2     Soteres, in the eighth year, in the priesthood of the one being in [Alexandria], priest of Alexander a[nd the gods So]teres and the gods Adelphoi and the gods Euergetai and the gods Ph[ilopatores]
3     and the gods Epiphaneis and the god Eupator and the god Philometor an[d the god Neos]
4     Philopator and the god Euergetes and the gods Philometores Soteres, [sacred c]olt of Isis the great mother of the gods, the stephanephoros being queen Cleo[patra]
5     goddess Philometor Soteira Dikaiosyne Nikephoros, the athlophoros of Bere[nike]
6     Euergetis, the phosphoros being queen Cleopatra goddess Philometor [Soteira]
7     Dikaiosyne Nikephoros, the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos, the priestess of q[ueen]
8     Cleopatra goddess Philometor Soteira Dikaiosyne Nikephoros, the pr[iestess of Arsi]noe Philopator — those being and existing in Alexandria; in the mo[nth Dystros]
9     on the sixteenth, Tybi the sixteenth, in Tenis also called Akoris, of t[he Mochites]
10    district, in the Hermopolite nome. [Dionysios] son of Asklepiades, [a Persian]
11    of the (men) of Apollophanes and Exak[on, catoecic ca]valry, lent to Dion[ysios son of Kepha]las, a Persian of the epigone, twenty-six and two-thirds artabas of wheat, [which]
12    he has been administered from him out of his house, at interest of one-and-a-half, in artaba[s thirteen]
13    and a third; and let the borrower repay all the forty artabas of wheat
14    to Dionysios in the month Loios, which is Pauni, of t[he eighth]
15    year, new pure unadulterated wheat, by the measure (calibrated) to the bronze (standard),
16    delivered at the harbor by Akor[is,]
17    without lawsuit or judgment or any pretext. But if he does not r[epay]
18    according to what is written, let the borrower pay to Dionysios the pri[ce of eac]h artaba, three thousand bronze drachmas, and into the royal treasury sacred [silver]
19    of the Ptolemaic coinage, drachmas, none the less;
20    and let Dionysios have the right of execution for the wheat and for all the things in the [deed]
21    both from Dionysios himself the borrower and from all his pro[perty], as by judgment. Let this deed be valid.
22    Witnesses: Ptolemaios son of Anaxagoras, Milesian; Arimmas son of Diony[sios, Charis]terios; Agenor son of Barkaios, Lacedaemonian; Dionysios son of Ptolemaios, [Macedonian,]
23    attendant of the unit; Eumenes son of Polykrates, Persian; [Apollonios]
24    son of Polykrates, Persian. Keeper of the deed: Pto[lemaios].
25    I, Dionysios son of Kephalas, Persian of the epigone, have [the]
26    forty artabas of wheat as is set forth above,
27    and I will do according to the other things written, and I have giv[en] (it) [as a valid (deed)]
28    [to Ptolemaios].
29    [I, Ptolemaios, have (it) as a valid (deed)].
30    Year 8, Tybi 16 …
31    fine: 3 (?). Keeper of the deed: Ptolemaios.
32    (Of) Dionysios, Ptolemaios, Agenor, Arimmas,
33    Dionysios, Eumenes, Dionysios, Apollonios.
