[TRANSLATION]
1     Chrysermos, priest and archidicastes of the Oxyrhynchite, to the strategos,
2     greeting. Let a copy of the petition which has been presented be served as follows.
3     Good-bye. Year tenth of the Emperor Caesar Trajan
4     Hadrian Augustus …
5     To Chrysermos, former priest of Alexander founder of the city and of the age-classes, priest, archidicastes and superintendent of the chrematistae and the other courts,
6     from Eud[a]imon the younger son of Pet…yris also called Apollonios, of those from the city of Oxyrhynchos. Whereas there is owed to me by Apollonios son of Sarapion, son of
7     Apollonios, his mother being Di[d]yme, from the
8     same city of Oxyrhynchos, by written security made in the third year of Hadrian the lord in the month Neos Sebastos, three hundred silver drachmas …
9     and eight of principal, the repayment of which he acknowledged he would make on the thirtieth of Tybi
10    of the same year or pay drachma-interest for the overdue time; and the repayment not having been made, I request that you order a letter to be written to the strategos of the Oxyrhynchite
11    to serve on him a copy of this,
12    so that he may repay [to m]e the aforesaid principal and the interest of the overdue time,
13    or know that I shall make the execution
14    as is fitting from his property,
15    forwarded … … .
