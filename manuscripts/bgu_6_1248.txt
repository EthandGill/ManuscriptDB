[TRANSLATION]
1     Asclepiades to Philinus, the one bringing to those in [the territory] of Panopolis and of the divided [places],
2     of places, judges, greetings. You wrote to us, having received a copy of a petition which [was submitted] by Isias, to give to the accused Esorohere and Tagotes and [to order]
3     to appear at the court within five days, having also the things pertaining to [the]
4     establishment, proofs. And in whatever manner we arranged, having given orders [...]
5     To Tagotes indeed in person, to Esorohere [indeed] from [his] house through Eponychus the guard [it was long]
6     to explain [clearly].
7     Farewell.
8     Year 34, Choiak 5.
9     To Philinus.
