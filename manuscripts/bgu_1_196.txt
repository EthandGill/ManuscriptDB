[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy. … [Year] … [of the Emperor]
2     Caesar Nerva [Trajan] [Au]gust[us Germanicus]
3     in the month Sebastos … in Ptolema[is Euerg]et[is of the]
4     Arsinoite nome, [Hera]kl[eides] di[vision]. There acknowledges [Then]ap[ynch]is daughter of Zosimos [with her guardian] Julius
5     son of Petheus, about … years old, with a scar on …
6     to Herakl[o]us daughter of Maron, about 33 years old, … …
7     and to Thermouthis daughter of Ptolemaios, called … about 28 years
8     old, with a scar on the forehead on the right and on the right forearm, each (woman) with the guardian her husband: for Heraklous, Chrates son of Perpesis, about 18 years old, scar between the eyebrows,
9     and for Thermouthis, Didymos son of … about 35 years old,
10    scar on the right shin: that Thenapynchis has received from them at once through the
11    bank of Achilleus of the Clothes-market, of silver
12    four hundred drachmas owed
13    to her by Heraklous and Thermouthis
14    according to a contract of deposit, completed
15    through the same record-office in the 12th year of the Emperor
16    Caesar the lord, in the month Mecheir … 5, which (contract) also
17    she has returned to those concerning Herak[l]ous for cancellation and annulment, and that neither she, Thenapynchis, nor those acting for her, shall proceed
18    against Heraklous and Thermouthis nor against either of them nor against those acting for them, neither
19    concerning what she has received, as is stated above, the four hundred drachmas, nor concerning anything else whatsoever
20    of any matter at all, nor any debt nor
21    any agreement, written or unwritten,
22    from former times up to the present day, in no manner. The subscribers: for
23    Thenapynchis and her guardian Julius: Zoilos
24    son of Didymos, about 55 years old, scar on the middle of the nose; for the others …
25    son of Theon, about 31 years old, without distinguishing mark.
