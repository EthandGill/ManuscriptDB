[TRANSLATION]
1     … to the most illustrious count and duke
2     … [serving as] magistrate, archon of Heracleoupolis city, greetings.
3     … [to your] magnanimity the conveyance of military rations
4     … from the council-chamber of our city the majority
5     … I mean both in village Tokoi and in other villages are thwarted
6     … council duties by not standing in the city and since
7     … [of rations] dispatch him, superintendent, troubled
8     … I am held in the public accounting office, I beseech your majesty
9     … [to Hera]klid[us] the of in Arsinoite vexillation
10    … now upon our nome all those in the nome councilors
11    … [both] in village Tokoi and [in] the other villages to stand in the city
12    … other beside them council duties toward nothing obstacle becoming
13    [of military rations] dispatch, lord. I pray you be well,
14    counts and duke, lord.
