[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, on this day which is the twenty-fourth of Mesore, of the year of the twelfth indiction, before the most honourable
2     Leontios and Mena, the lashanes of the fortress
3     of Djeme. I, Tsible the daughter of Engapatios: since I happened to fall into a grievous sickness, I was afraid lest God should seek after me and I depart from this life, and my affair come to ruin, being uncared-for,
4     together with my offering, I gave my single (decision) to a testament, undisturbable to the laws, unremovable forever, and rather firm
5     to it, through some trustworthy witnesses,
6     and with a scribe to write for them,
7     while my heart is steadfast, as I sit upon my bed of sleeping, while my mind is steadfast,
8     while my reason is firm. I was afraid lest
9     the sentence overtake me too, like
10    every man, according to the manner in which God the Word
11    appointed to it, upon our father of us all, Adam,
12    that "thou art earth, and thou shalt return to the earth." I gave
13    my single (decision), my testament, out, since I happened
14    upon my husband, he being attentive to me in all my labour,
15    and embellishing me in all honour,
16    cherishing me with all his strength.
17    I said: Lest, when God questions me
18    at his tribunal which is fearful, concerning his labour
19    and offering — now, then, I command beforehand
20    concerning his four trimesia, that my husband should take them, he shall give them as an offering for me; and again
21    my share in the houses, which came up to me on behalf of my father,
22    and my share in the field of Berbote — that my husband
23    shall become master over them, he shall receive their price from my brethren, and shall give them as an offering for me; and
24    again, concerning the vessels which came u[pon me on b]ehalf of my father, I swear by God Almighty that I gave nothing of my husband's from among them to anyone, that no man may be able to come o-
25    -ut against you ever, over any pretext of this kind, neither a brother nor a sister,
26    nor any man at all, who is mine, neither
27    you nor those who come after you all.
28    Whether now or at any time, whoever shall dare to transgress this testament,
29    whether a stranger or a man who is mine, concerning
30    the offering and the document which I have established,
31    but my husband shall become master
32    over the whole offering, in the fear
33    of God; he shall administer it, that no man may be able to administer it
34    except him. I swear by God Almighty that no ruler nor
35    lashane nor any type that bears glory,
36    being worshipped, shall annul this testament so as to guard it according to the manner in which it is written: that "it is permitted to me to do what is mine in that which is mine." Whoever shall dare
37    to transgress it, first that one shall profit nothing, but first
38    the type shall be alien to the holy altar which they serve, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, and again he shall give to the account
39    three ounces of gold, and they shall demand
40    it from his substance, afterward they shall prepare him so that he confirms, powerless against this testament, with the loss
41    of the lashane of that time. This testament then shall be, being firm.
42    I, Tsible, the one who first wrote it above,
43    agree to this testament and to everything that is written to it, since they have read it out.
44    And again I summoned a scribe
45    and some little witnesses to bear witness
46    for me; I struck it (signed) as is aforesaid.
47    I, Antreas the son of the late Phobamon, bear witness.
48    I, [Ge]orgios the son of the late
49    Sa[mou]el, bear witness. I, …,
50    the son of the late Psan, bear witness.
51    I, Theopistos the reader
52    of Apa Kyriakos, wrote for them, since
53    they do not know how to write.
54    I, Seueros the son of the late Samouel, wrote the testament with my hand according to the request of the one who established it.
