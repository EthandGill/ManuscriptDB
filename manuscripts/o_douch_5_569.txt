r.2    Σαράπει Ἰσοκράτης
r.3    χαίρειν. δὸς τῷ Ἀγῆς σίτου

[TRANSLATION]
1     to my lord brother
2     Sarapeis, Isokrates
3     greetings. Give to Ages grain
4     by land-holding measure artaba
5     one; there is artaba 1 for tax
6     of the Balaneion of the 3rd indiction.
7     I have marked, Isokrates overseer.
