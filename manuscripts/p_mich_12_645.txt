[TRANSLATION]
1     And on the 21st the same men paid for the price of wheat of the 17th and 16th and ninth (year)
2     of Philadelphia, two talents; total 2 talents
3     and from the gold disbursement of the month Pauni
4     in the name of Sarapammon, who served as prytanis, 1 talent, 100 drachmas
5     for the supplementary assessment
6     And on the 28th the same men paid for the price of wheat of the 17th and 16th and ninth year
7     of Philadelphia, two talents; total 2 talents
8     and on Pauni 4 Aphon and Ptolemaios and the partner sitologoi paid for the price of wheat of the 17th year
9     and 16th year and ninth year, of Philadelphia
10    thirteen talents and three thousand drachmas; total 13 talents, 3000 drachmas
11    and on the 7th the same men paid likewise for the price
12    of wheat of the 17th year and 16th year and ninth year
13    of Philadelphia, six talents and three thousand drachmas; total 6 talents, 3000 drachmas
14    Total 4 talents, 1500 drachmas
15    Mesore 2, from the account of unstamped (bullion)
16    of Eugenios, two talents; total 2 talents
17    Year 20 and 12th year of our lords
18    Diocletian and Maximian
19    Augusti and Constantius and Maximian
20    the most illustrious Caesars, Epeiph 2.
21    Aphon and Pasis paid for the price of wheat
22    of the 17th year and 16th year and ninth year of exchange, of Philadelphia, fifteen talents and three thousand drachmas; total 15 talents, 3000 drachmas
23    and on the 4th the same men paid likewise
24    for the price of wheat of the 17th year and 16th year and ninth
25    year of exchange, of Philadelphia
26    four talents and four thousand drachmas; total 4 talents, 4000 drachmas
27    likewise other two thousand drachmas; total 2000 drachmas
28    Total 20 talents, 3000 drachmas
