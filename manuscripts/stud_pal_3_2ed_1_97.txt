[TRANSLATION]
1     From the lord Ioustos from the city of the Oxyrhynchites
2     […] to Phokas administrator from Hermes city I have been paid by you the fifth part
3     [of] the revenue of my blessed mother-in-law Theodote for the eleventh indiction
4     [and] I have no claim against you concerning the same revenue of the eleventh indiction both for [receipt]
5     [and] expense and for your security I have made this payment receipt
6     [as] aforesaid. It was written Choiak eleventh of indiction eleven. Flavius Ioustos agrees. The quitclaim as aforesaid.
7     Receipt from the lord Ioustos from the [Oxyrhynchite] [city].
