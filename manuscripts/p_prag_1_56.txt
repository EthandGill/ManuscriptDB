[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 11 of Lucius Septimius [Severus] Pious Pertinax
2     and Mark[us] Aurelius Antoninus Pious Augustuses
3     and Publius Septimi[u]s Geta [Cae]sar Augustus. Kastor
4     of Heron and [partners] [grain-measurers] of the village Herakleias have measured
5     on the 13th of Pauni month of the present 11 from the produce of the same [year] to Seg[ath]a [Harpagath]us [resident]
6     of wheat artabas seven, [which] total wheat artabas 7. Kastor have measured
7     as is set forth. Archonias have measured as is set [forth].
