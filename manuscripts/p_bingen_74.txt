[TRANSLATION]
1     Herais to Lukretia her sister, greetings.
2     Before all things I pray that you are in good health together with
3     your unenvied children; and I am in good health with
4     my [children], making obeisance to you before the lord Sarapis.
5     Be unconcerned concerning Antonius. As for [matters] up to
6     now he has not yet served as a soldier. I want you to know that those dwelling in Egypt are oppressed concerning the strategos. [If] therefore you go up to Antinoopolis, write to me more quickly so that
7     … I come to you. But if you are not going, write to me again,
8     [not] through poverty do I go from here to there. Know that I came down
9     and I found Eros being held and I went to your brother. How many times did I ask him, and he was not willing to release him, saying that if he wishes to go up, he will [provide] for the orphans. On the fourth day, when I came to the city, came also
10    Nephotianos. And write to us whether I brought the purple from Petehios the sailor. Greet Lukretios
11    and Eutychos and Appun. Greets
12    you: Antonis and Koprous and Nephotianos.
13    Deliver to [Lukretia] from [Herais]
