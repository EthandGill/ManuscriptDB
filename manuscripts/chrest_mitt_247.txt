[TRANSLATION]
1     … (son) of Eudaimon the [elder], of both, from Hermoupolis above Memphis. Whereas there are owed to my deceased son Eudaimon, who died childless and intestate, of whom I am heir, by Didyme daughter of Ammonios, from [the sa]me city, according to (the deeds) which
2     the same Eudaimon directed to be made out, in trust, to Sarapias daughter of Hermaios, from the [sa]me city: three loan-[con]tracts on the security of the catoecic (land) of Didyme, sixteen and a quarter arouras, from the allotment of Dionysios, [around] …
3     … of Patemis Upper — the first on the first year of Domitian Caesar the lord, in (the month) Phamenoth, of one thousand six hundred and twenty-eight drachmas of silver, [for] repayment in Mecheir of the third year of Domitian Caesar the lord; the second in the month Pharmouthi of the said first year, of another one thousand six hundred drachmas of silver, for repayment on the thirtieth of Phamenoth of the fourth year; and the third on the fifth of the epagomenal (days) of the same fourth year, of another five hundred and forty drachmas, for repayment in Pharmouthi of the following fifth year, in accordance with (the deed) which Sarapias referred to my son Eudaimon while living, by public document through the same agoranomeion in the same fifth year, in the month Neos Sebastos, acknowledging the trust of the same three contracts and declaring also that the dues and the expenses of the overdue (sums) had been paid by him. And whereas there are owed to the same son
4     (text continues; cf. r.1)
5     [my (son)] Eudaimon by the same debtor Didyme, according to another loan-contract through the same agoranomeion in the
6     [sa]me fifth year, in the month Choiak, of another nine hundred and fifty drachmas, for repayment in the month Kaisareios of the [same] fifth year, on the aforesaid arouras; and according to a bank-order through the bank of Horion son of Syros at the place,
7     in the name of the same Eudaimon, in the same fifth year, of another four hundred and thirteen drachmas,
8     for repayment in the month Kaisareios of the same fifth year — it being added to the order that it should not be permitted to Didyme to release the mortgages unless she should also repay the (sums) through the order; and whereas no repayment of any of all the owed (sums)
9     [as] is set out above has been made up to now either to my son while living or to me after his death, I have necessarily come
10    [before] your authority and request that you order it written to the strategos of the Hermopolite (nome) to deliver to the debtor Didyme a copy of this
11    [memo]randum, so that she may repay to me both all the aforesaid principal sums and the interest of the overdue (sums) up [to no]w and the dues and expenses likewise with interest, or know that, with respect to the (sums) owed through the contracts,
12    I shall enter upon the mortgaged arouras as set out above, and shall hold them with full title and carry off all the produce that comes from them, and further shall manage concerning them as I may choose, no claim concerning these being left to her [or to any]one else on her behalf hereafter at all; but also that the guarantee shall follow to her
13    in accordance with the conditions declared through the contracts; and with respect to the (sums) owed through the order, that I shall make the execution from whatever other property of Didyme I may find; and that of the other rights pertaining to
14    myself and my aforementioned son I hold fast and shall hold fast in every way, being lessened in nothing; and that, for the completion of the document, I have been dispatched …
