[TRANSLATION]
1     [Prepare] Kyriakos son of Bamos to go to arbitration with the letter-bearer
2     [woman], but certainly prepare to make [it]. As the Lord lives, if he still comes to me
3     [and not] separated, you are not able to bear from me. Send immediately through today
4     [day] [John] and Parsakis with their swords and shields, since I wish
5     [these] near me. But certainly through today send to me.
6     To the most reverent Philoxenes prudent one of Paker[ky] Phoibamm[on].
