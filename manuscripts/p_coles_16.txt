[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 2 of Servius Galba, Emperor
2     of Caesar Augustus, Tybi 30, after account 5. [It] has been written off.
3     Masartas of Nile arithmetician, inhabitant of Bernicide Thesmophore.
4     of the first year [sixteen obol half-obol] drachmas, total 16 obol half-obol, further-written [drachma] 1 half-obol, symbolic three-obol.
