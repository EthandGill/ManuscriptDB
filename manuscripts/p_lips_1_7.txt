[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 10th year, Choiak 26, in Krokodilopolis,
2     before Paniskos, agoranomos of the upper toparchy of the Pathyrites. There paid down
3     Panebchounis son of Totoes and Petearsemtheus his son, of those from Pathyris, to Chairemon son of Paniskos
4     also called Erienouphis son of Psemminis, a Persian
5     of the mercenary cavalry, of copper
6     four thousand five hundred drachmas,
7     those imposed upon them [from] the contract
8     of loan which Patous son of Horos, the
9     maternal grandfather of the aforewritten Panebchounis,
10    made with Neandros son of Neandros,
11    the maternal grandfather of Chairemon,
12    through the record-office in Krokodilopolis
13    in the 33rd year, Choiak 1, before Ptolemaios
14    the agoranomos; and likewise also from the share falling to Sennesis, the daughter of
15    Patous, of the said
16    loan, which is of copper three thousand six hundred drachmas; and the whole, of copper, one talent
17    two thousand one hundred drachmas; and being present at the
18    record-office, Chairemon acknowledged
19    that he had received the one talent of copper 2100 drachmas, and that he makes no
20    claim against them concerning the said
21    contract of the loan, on any pretext
22    whatsoever.
23    I, Paniskos, have transacted (it). In the 10th year, Choiak 26.
24    In the 10th year, Choiak 26.
25    There paid down Panebchounis and
26    Petearsemtheus the son
27    to Cha[i]remon, of copper one talent 2100 [drachmas].
