[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 6, Pachon 21, [he] has measured into the storehouse of Apollo's city.
2     Into the same year, Kato, Psennes, Paletios [and]
3     Daimachus [son] of Psennes, of wheat 3 and one-half and one-twelfth; [they] total of wheat 3 and one-half and one-twelfth.
4     Paniskos, [it] has been measured in, as is stated, of wheat 3 and one-half and one-twelfth; [they] total 3 and one-half and one-twelfth.
5     Of year 6, Pachon 21.
6     Isatous, [it] has been measured in, as is stated, of wheat 3 and one-half and one-twelfth; [they] total 3 and one-half and one-twelfth.
7     Of year 6, Pachon 21.
