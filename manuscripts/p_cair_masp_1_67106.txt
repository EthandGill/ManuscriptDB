[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Apion the most glorious,
2     Phaophi 12 of the third indiction.
3     To the public account of the village of Aphrodite
4     of the Antaiopolite nome, through the most admirable assistant,
5     from Aurelius Apollos son of Kyros and Pekysios
6     son of Hermauos, shepherd from the hamlet
7     of Sakkos near the same village, greeting.
8     We acknowledge that we have jointly and severally leased
9     from you, for only the present year,
10    of the crops, God willing, of the coming fourth indiction,
11    the farmland lying fallow in the unwatered (district),
12    of however many arouras it is, situated in the plain
13    of the same village of Aphrodite and as far as
14    (the place) called Pherko of Psanch, together with the cistern
15    and every right according to the boundaries, on condition that we
16    cultivate this and sow it
17    and give you the rent at a fixed rate, two gold solidi
18    less one third, less three carats, by the goldsmith's standard
19    of weight, and two artabas of wheat
20    without dispute. The lease is valid and secure; and
21    being formally questioned [we so acknowledged]. We, Aurelii Apollos
22    son of Kyros and Pekysios, the aforesaid,
23    have leased as stated above.
24    Flavius Menas son of Iosephios, having been asked,
25    [wrote] on [their] behalf, [since they do not know letters] …
