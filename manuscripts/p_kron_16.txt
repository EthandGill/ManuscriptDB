[TRANSLATION]
1     From the registry office of the village of Tebtynis:
2     in the year 2 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
3     Phaophi 27. Kronion son of Kronion,
4     son of Cheos, from the village of Tebtynis,
5     a Persian of the epigone, about 54 years old, with a scar on his left
6     hand, acknowledges to Diogenis daughter of Lysimachos
7     also called Loupos, about 45 years old, with a scar on her left eyebrow,
8     with her guardian, her kinsman Didymos
9     son of Eutyches, about … years old, with a scar on his right hand,
10    that the acknowledging party
11    Kronion is under compulsion to repay to Diogenis
12    the sum which both women allege has been left owing with this same Kronion on account
13    of a reckoning of what they have made over to the property
14    of the things under her management, on hand
15    for the periods up to the year 21 and including the year 21 of the god Hadrian, namely one thousand silver drachmas … together with interest at one drachma per mina per month from the
16    current month, in the month Pachon
17    of the present year 2, without any delay or pretext. But if Kronion does not repay as has been written, and not
18    simply (with excuse), he is under compulsion to remain in service
19    with Diogenis, serving and doing everything that is enjoined upon him
20    in the field and in the village, in lieu of interest
21    and of the one thousand drachmas, being fed and clothed by Diogenis and paying the poll-taxes due on his own behalf to the public treasury.
22    But if Diogenis is unwilling, she shall have
23    the right of exaction of the capital and interest both from
24    Kronion himself and from all his property
25    in full. Subscriber: Hippalos son of Chrates.
