[TRANSLATION]
1     [First of all] I bow down and greet your longed-for lordship which also concerning …
2     … testifying to my soul that as often as [we] send up prayers
3     [to God and] of our wretched soul [we] make remembrance …
4     … [the] our father … in holy memory …
5     in offspring to … in this world and grace will be to your good [soul]
6     of desire(s) … we sent to your love Abraham our brother
7     with the brothers, knowing that all things are possible from your [preciousness]
8     so that [he] may come together [with] them to the matter of the holy monastery. For being bold
9     in your good soul, just as we trust in yourselves, we sent to you
10    so that [he] may come together with them until they gather the small offering of the holy [monastery.]
11    For I am persuaded precisely that all that God and our holy father does with them
12    [he] will repay to you both in the present age and in the heavenly [things]. Being well, may you pass through.
