[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis produce of the 10th year
2     Of Hadrian Caesar
3     Of the lord Epeiph 30 in the name
4     Psenchnumios Pekysios
5     Of wheat artabae one-half one-quarter
6     And in the name of Phamimios Psenchnumios for Charax
7     Of wheat artabae two-thirds one-eighth
8     Total of wheat artabae seven-eighths. … I have sealed.
