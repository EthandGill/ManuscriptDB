[TRANSLATION]
1     To Anubion son of Isidoros … son of …arous, his mother being Kleopatra, [in the vil]lage
2     of Phnebieis Lower, in the plain of Apollonopolis and Narmoutis,
3     being absent, through Chairemon son of Chairemon, his agent,
4     from Harphaesis son of Kronion son of Teos, [from]
5     the village of Tebtynis, a Persian of the epigone.
6     I wish to lease from you for two years,
7     from the present 17th [year], around Tebtynis, from
8     the ten arouras of the kleros under personal cultivation, the
9     five arouras of these from the eastern part, in return for the freight-charges for them yearly
10    and the grain-rent and money-rent, sowing
11    yearly with whatever crops I choose. And I, Harphaesis, have received [from you through Ch]airemon, as an advance
12    [account, forty]-eight [drachmas], and for
13    the [price of the crops to be sown, ano]ther forty-[eight]
14    drachmas, [which together amount] to ninety-six drachmas of silver,
15    which I shall repay in Pauni of the present 17th year without interest, while I perform all the agricultural labors of the arouras
16    that are fitting, at my own expense,
17    [at the proper] seasons, doing no damage; and when
18    [the term] is completed, I shall hand over [the] arouras
19    [clean of] rushes, reeds, coarse grass, and all undergrowth;
20    [if it seems] good to lease them. I, Anubion
21    [son of Isidoros], of the Sosikosmian tribe also Althaiean deme, [through] Chairemon,
22    [have lea]sed on the aforesaid terms [the aforesaid] arouras for two years from the present
23    17th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord.
24    Hathyr 10.
