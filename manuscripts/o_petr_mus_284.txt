[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been paid through the bank of Hermios on behalf of
2     the price of wheat artabas of the administration 25 year of the lower toparchy of Harmachis
3     Petarouerion artabas 18 drachmae 63
4     and of barley artabas 7 … croton-seed artabas …
5     [Others] of barley artaba half drachma 1 four-obol [and] half-obol and of croton-seed artaba 1 drachmae 3 [and] three-obol
6     year 26 of Caesar Tybi 30.
