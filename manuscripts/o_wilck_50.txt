[TRANSLATION]
1   Peteuris son of Peteuris paid, for the trade-tax
2   of the 1st year of Trajan Caesar the lord:
3   12 silver drachmas, because the (previous) receipt had been lost.
4   I, Hermogenes, the collector, wrote (this).
