[TRANSLATION]
1     Horigenes of [Ischyrion], scribe of farmers of the village Theadelphia
2     to Eudaimon of Theogeitos, overseer
3     of the same village, greetings. I have received from you which you had received
4     from public and private
5     of farmers of the [village] in silver
6     two thousand five hundred drachmas, thirteen obols—which total drachmas 2500 obols 13
7     which [were handed over] in the [presence]
8     of Sokrates [and] [Theon] of Herodes [the ...]
9     and [Lourios] sword-bearer, Isidoros
10    of [Horion], overseer of [Apiad], and
11    [grain-collectors] as for honor of lentils
12    the aforementioned drachmas 2500 obols 13. I, Horigenes, the aforementioned, wrote in the third year
