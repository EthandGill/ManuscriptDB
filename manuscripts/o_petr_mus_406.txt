[TRANSLATION]
1     Pikos and associates of straw received in the metropolis
2     of year two of Antoninus of the lord
3     to Antiphilos son of Kronios. We have received from you
4     into a boat a load of straw one-quarter becomes of a load of straw
5     four for the mainland of the metropolis. Of year five of Antoninus
6     of the lord Thoth fifteen. Horos I-have-signed
