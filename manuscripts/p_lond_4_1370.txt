[TRANSLATION]
1     Concerning the 1,300 artabas of grain remaining in arrears of the granaries.
2     [In] the name of God. Korra [son of Sherik, govern]or,
3     to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito. We give thanks
4     to God, and after this. We found you in arrears from the
5     grain assessed from the cargo-shipment of the produce of the ninth indiction,
6     on account of the granaries, thirteen hundred artabas.
7     On receiving therefore the present letter, immediately and at that very hour,
8     loading it, send off this grain, not neglecting it, if indeed
9     you have it ready. And if there is also any part of it through the districts of your administration,
10    write to us, by district, where it is and what is in arrears through them.
11    But do not be negligent, as has been said, until the orders of the public treasury
12    surround you all at once and you are found unable. For this
13    brings great loss upon you and upon those of your administration. And you shall be
14    aware that if you should discover the matters concerning us, and there be found in arrears
15    through your administration, from this grain, a single artaba, even
16    one only, we shall proceed to seize and constrain you until from your own resources you
17    [complete] it and deliver it, God commanding. Written in the month Hathyr 7, in[diction] 9.
18    Korra son of Sherik, govern[or, to Basileios, admini]strator of the village of Aphrodito.
19    In the month Hathyr 24, indiction 9, brought through Suleiman the courier concerning the arrears of the cargo-shipment of the 9th indiction.
