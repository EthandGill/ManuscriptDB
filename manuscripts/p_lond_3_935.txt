[TRANSLATION]
1     To the amphodo-grammateis of the West Fort (quarter),
2     from Aurelius Herminos also called Moros, son of Hermaios also called Pathotes, son of Achilleus,
3     his mother being Soueirous daughter of Kastor, a Hermopolite from the gymnasium, registered at the East Town (quarter). I register, according to what was ordered by the most illustrious prefect Valerius Datus, for the house-by-house
4     census of the 24th year of Aurelius Severus Antoninus Caesar the lord, into the property-station of my paternal uncle Herminos son of Achilleus, deceased, with
5     my father as heir, of a whole house and courtyard and all appurtenances at the West Fort (quarter) 14, myself,
6     Aurelius Herminos also called Moros, the aforewritten, from the gymnasium of the East Town (quarter), 49 years old,
7     (and) Aurelius Theognostos also called Moros, my brother, of the same parents, from the gymnasium of the same amphodon,
8     of the East Town (quarter), 36 years old;
9     and I swear by the fortune of Aurelius Severus Antoninus Caesar the lord
10    that it is thus. Year 25 of Imperator Caesar Marcus
11    Aurelius Severus Antoninus Parthicus Maximus Britannicus
12    Maximus Germanicus Maximus Pius Augustus, Phamenoth 16.
13    Aurelius Origenes: I have received in the apportionment an equal copy.
