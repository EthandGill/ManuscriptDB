[TRANSLATION]
1     [c]opy. Aurelius Petronius …
2     to Diophantus the royal scribe [of Arsinoe of Heraclides]
3     division greetings. Achillas [son] of Harpokrates
4     [h]orseman of the eile of Wokuntoi before [military ser]vice called Oronnous Rapalio…
5     mother Tamestremphi from the village Syngnai
6     [w]as reported to have served [more than twenty]-five years; it is fitting then to write according to
7     the grace of the greatest Autocrator
8     [that] he be removed from the giving of head-taxes.
9     I wish you to be strong.
10    Year 13 of the Autocrator Caesar Titus Aelius
11    Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus the Pious, Phaofi 4.
