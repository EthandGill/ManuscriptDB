[TRANSLATION]
1     Harpa[esius] overseer to-Matreas the most-honored
2     greetings.
3     if to-you it-seems good, send m[e] for grain-forage being-measured into treasuries, of-which I-will-give account, drachmas
4     one-hundred sixty, become drachmas 160.
