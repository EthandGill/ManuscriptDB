[TRANSLATION]
1     of the consulship of our lords
2     [Constantine] Augustus [year 5] and
3     [Licinius] Caesar the [first].
4     To Valerius Ammoniaus, also called Gerontius, managing the accounts of the Oxyrhynchite[s]
5     [from] the association of wool-merchants
6     of the brilliant city of the Oxyrhynchites through me Aurelius Pecyllus Stephanus. In accordance with what has been ordered at my own valuation I declare the following recorded price [of which] I handle wools [and] I swear the [divine]
7     oath [that I have] not [lied].
8     And it is:
9     of white local wool, one pound: 150 denarii.
10    of self-colored and other
11    colored [wools], one pound: 105 denarii.
12    of the aforementioned consulship, Phamenoth 30.
13    I, Aurelius Pecyllus, have submitted, declaring
14    as set out above.
