[TRANSLATION]
1     … having handed over to the (woman) being represented in court
2     … we ask that he himself give an account. Dioskoros the advocate
3     answered: Pnepheros died twelve years ago, and all the sons took up his (estate)
4     according to the fitting parts; for ours is not the only one,
5     but also others, now being in military service, according to the will which that man left. For the opposing party, knowing that … was previously settled … and there is no share for
6     [her] of that man's (estate), held her peace. Didymos
7     the elder, the advocate, said: the will is a forgery … nor … against
8     … they carry off
9     … it (being) sealed
10    … it not yet having been opened, how can …
11    … the things written in (it)
12    … unlawful
13    it being. Dioskoros: the written (documents) have been brought down. Ptolemaios, son of Sarapion, of those who have served as gymnasiarch of the city of the Arsinoites, the judge, having considered, dictated a decision, which was also read out,
14    word for word as follows: both parties shall give written bonds
15    that within thirty days there shall be (a hearing) before the strategos, so that the will may be opened and the heirs may be known. But if within these (days) the opening does not take place, the
16    produce shall be in sequestration. [A]pollonios, the prefect's assistant, went out. Subscription: I have read (it).
