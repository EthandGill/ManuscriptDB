[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonios, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2     from Petaus, village-scribe of Ptolemais Hormou.
3     A register of the public officials for the second half-year,
4     which is submitted according to custom in the
5     present 25th year of Marcus Aurelius
6     Commodus Antoninus Augustus
7     Pius. It is as follows:
8     Elders:
9     Aeion son of Ischyrion, his mother being Sambous,
10    called Teripis, having means of 600 drachmae;
11    Sambathion son of Pnepheros, called Areeis, likewise 600 drachmae;
12    Pouaris son of Ischyrion, his mother being Ischyriaine, likewise 500 drachmae;
13    Sitoous son of Heraklas, his mother being Heraklous, likewise 700 drachmae;
14    Apynchis son of Apynchis, called Teetaris, likewise 600 drachmae;
15    Piterous son of Elei, his mother being Artemis, likewise 600 drachmae;
16    Phaseis, his mother being Tauris, called "carpenter," likewise 600 drachmae;
17    Phaseis son of Hatres, his mother being Soumeeis, likewise 600 drachmae;
18    Ischyrion son of Athas, his mother being Thermouthis, likewise 700 drachmae;
19    Ischyrion son of Ammonios, his mother being Herakleia, 600 drachmae.
20    Chief-guards of the village:
21    Penneis son of Ischyrion, his mother being Heraklous;
22    Chief-warden: Bassos son of Antonios;
23    Night-warden: Phaseis son of Ischyrion, his mother being Thenapynchis;
24    Guards, likewise:
25    Harpaesis son of Ischeis, his mother being Ischyriaine, likewise 200 drachmae;
26    Heraklas son of Pesouris, his mother being Tabreseis, likewise 200 drachmae;
27    Onnophris son of Aphrodas, his mother being I…u, likewise 200 drachmae;
28    Pouaris son of Pouaris, baker, likewise 200 drachmae;
29    Souchammon son of Komon, likewise 200 drachmae;
30    Heron son of Deios, his mother being Segathis, likewise 200 drachmae;
31    Deios son of Deios, his mother being Ischyriaine, likewise 200 drachmae;
32    Pankras son of Sambas, likewise 200 drachmae;
33    Orsenouphis son of Pathyneus, likewise 200 drachmae.
34    In the 25th year of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
35    Antoninus Augustus Pius, Phamenoth.
