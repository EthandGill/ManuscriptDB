[TRANSLATION]
1     Postomos, also called Horos, tax collector of money taxes
2     to Pthomonth of Panameus. I received for the poll tax
3     and bath-tax of market 2 of the 8th year dirty drachmas twelve,
4     making dirty drachmas 12. Of the year 8 of Hadrian the lord Phamenoth
5     27 A … I have signaled.
