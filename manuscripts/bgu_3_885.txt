[TRANSLATION]
1   Theoktist[os, to Apol(lonios) his dearest (friend), greetings.]
2   Concerning all things, [I pray that you are in health.]
3   Send …[..]… into …[.]…,
4   so that he may guard it as you wrote; and let it be
5   your care to learn whose are the
6   sheep that have grazed over (the land) there,
7   so that you may send the names; and concern[ing]
8   the t[un]ics, let it be your care to learn
9   from Orsenouphis the weaver, whether they have
10   been made, and send them up,
