[TRANSLATION]
1   To Theon, strategos and in charge of the revenues,
2   from Antaios son of Onnophris, gymnasiarch of the vil-
3   lage of Busiris in the 30th year of Caesar. I became surety
4   for Theoxenos son of Lykos and Panetbeuis son of Pete-
5   chon, of the same village, decani
6   of the embankment near Koma; and, the aforesaid
7   men having defaulted on (their) appearance, I was com-
8   pelled by Apollonios, the toparch of the (parts)
9   around Busi[ris, to p]ay into the treasury on their
10   behalf 421 silver [d]rachmas and the additional charges
11   on them. Si[nce], then, the said men have means —
12   [hous]es and allotments and cattle and
13   crops — I ask, if it seem good, to instruct the sa(me)
14   toparch to assist me as I exact (it).
