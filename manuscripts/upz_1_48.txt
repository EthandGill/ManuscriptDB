[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapion of the diadochoi and subadministrator
2     from Thaes and Thautes, twins, those who serve in the great Serapeum before Memphis.
3     And previously indeed we presented to you a memorandum concerning [not] having received from the [...]
4     [priests] the [proper for us] to be given
5     per [year] for the necessary barley,
6     from which you also wrote to Mennides the overseer to return (it) to us. And of this
7     having instructed Psinthes the superintendent of the priests, he makes no account,
8     we [meanwhile] being ruined
9     in the famine
