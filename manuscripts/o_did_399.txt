[TRANSLATION]
1     Mokotralis to Gell[i]us the
2     brother, very many greetings
3     and always be well with your unbegrudged [horse]; and I myself am well.
4     It was not a great matter if to me
5     you wrote a letter. Whenever I write to the children,
6     I write greeting
7     your mother;
8     will testify to you
9     and she herself.
10    Send me [if] you have a small peppercorn.
