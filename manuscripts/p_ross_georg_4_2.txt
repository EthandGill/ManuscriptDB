[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name] of [the] G[od. Korra ben Szerich, governor,]
2     to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito. We give thanks
3     to God, and after these things we have dispatched into the lower country
4     those who are to bring back into your administrative district those men who departed
5     from it, from twenty years ago and up to now.
6     Now then, on [rec]eiving the present [letter], se[nd] thr[ee] capable men
7     who know the places to which they flee for refuge,
8     setting over them a trustworthy and capable man who is to
9     take charge of them and record with himself the number
10    of those found and reported to you, permitting
11    the (people) of their villages to give to each one a month's provision;
12    for we intend to permit their wages to be given to them,
13    by the command of God. And if there is anyone from your district who fled last year
14    and the (people) of his village knew his whereabouts, write to us
15    concerning him, making clear to us about him from whose (village) he is,
16    and we will send this man to you at once, giving also to the one who finds
17    him having (arrested) him a requital that harms him, by the command of God.
18    Written in the month Pauni 23, of the 9th indiction.
19    [Korra son of Szerich, governor, to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito.]
20    In the month Epeiph 5, of the 9th indiction, it was delivered through … pe the courier concerning … fugitives.
