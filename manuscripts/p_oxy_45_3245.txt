[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords the Imperator Maximianus
2     Augustus for the 5th time and of Maximianus the most illustrious Caesar for the 2nd time.
3     To Aurelius Ailurion also called Hesychius, former recorder (hypomnematographos),
4     councillor of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians, gymnasiarch, councillor in office,
5     prytanis of the illustrious and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,
6     from Aurelius Thonius, from the same city,
7     public physician. I was instructed by you, through Aurelius
8     Eirenaeus, attendant of the office, on the basis of a petition submitted to you by Aurelii Didymus and Ptolemaeus,
9     sons of Dionysius son of Artemidorus, both [from]
10    the illustrious and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, to inspect the man named in their petition, Patareus,
11    and whatever condition I find, to report in writing.
12    Whence I inspected this man in the same city in the presence
13    of the same attendant, having on the forearm [of the right]
14    hand a wound, and of the left a blow,
15    which things I report.
16    Year 13 and year 12 of our lords [Diocletian]
17    and Maximianus the Augusti, and year 5 of our lords [Constantius]
18    and Maximianus the most illustrious Caesars …
19    In the consulship of the …
20    Aureli… …
21    attend(ant) …
22    copy concerning … of the …
23    daughter of Dionysius.
