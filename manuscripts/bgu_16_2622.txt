[TRANSLATION]
1     Mnaseas to Athenodoros his brother
2     and dioiketes, very many greetings, and to be in good health
3     and to prosper excellently always, as I pray.
4     I was greatly astonished that Hermas, on arriving, had brought nothing of what he wrote
5     that he had received from you; for he wrote to me that … he had received of silver
6     four hundred drachmas
7     eighty, and in place of the … made
8     … two hundred drachmas
9     total 680 drachmas; for the rest then,
10    dioiketes, having taken thought for Thermouthas
11    more quickly
12    send (it) to her, so that she may not at the Demetria become unholy; for you too should know that not yet
13    no longer am I strong; so do not on account of the distractions
14    neglect (it), but as you always did
15    now also
16    take thought for her in good time, so that the
17    favor may be double; for we are not unmindful; Hermas will surely not neglect (it) but will detain you
18    … but now, having cast us into hopes through the
19    letter, he has mocked (us); and the
20    wool: doing well and rightly, send the 2 (cloths?) more quickly
21    so that she may have (them) for the winter; but if you do [not have wool],
22    send also whatever fleeces [you have], being so kind; above
23    all, take care of you[r]self so that
24    you may be in good health,
25    which indeed is for us first among the things in our prayer;
26    farewell. Year …
27    From Mnaseas to Athenodoros
