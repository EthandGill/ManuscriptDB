1     To Pythion the steward, from Ptolem[ae]us also called Petemini[us]
2     named [thus]: I undertake to [register] the [property] in Mouch [for]
3     for the 13th year
4     a brew[ery] from Tybi 30 until
5     eight months, eight talents. I will furnish sureties
6     [which] I will establish for permanence and
7     and payment in accordance with the decree.
