[TRANSLATION]
1   Sabinus to [...]naria, his mo-
2   ther, and to Demetrous,
3   to both, greetings.
4   I want you to know that,
5   from when I sailed down to Alexandria,
6   I found the greater number of days
7   without business (court not sitting), and
8   up to today I have been held up
9   on account of the lawsuit.
10   It happened that the archidi-
11   kastes too, the former one, was changed,
12   and we await the one to come.
13   And as soon as
14   he is brought in, with
15   God (helping) I shall quickly be quit.
16   And from the day I came
17   I am with our (friend) Antonius,
18   and everything is well.
19   Greet Aphrodisia and Thermouth(is)
20   and Tasoucharion and Ptol-
21   las my friend. There greet
22   you Antonius — all (of you) — and the
23   children. —
24   I pray for your health,
25   (all of you).
26   To Karanis, to the house of Sabinus son of Demetrous.
