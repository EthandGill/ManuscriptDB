[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ophelli[os and the rest of the be]loved
2     brothers, P[a]ul[os] and Tapiam
3     greeting in the Lord.
4     First of all we pray, night
5     and day, that, you being healthy in soul and
6     body, our letter may be delivered to you,
7     and then also now to remind your kindness, that in your prayers
8     you may remember to name us, because of
9     our sojourning abroad, and our children.
10    For I, Tapiam, fell ill and am still confined to bed. We beseech you therefore, pray for our
11    well-being; for before this too our children fell ill
12    and through your prayers they ceased (to be ill). For we believe that the Lord, you being righteous,
13    will hear (you). For we too in our own house
14    pray to have our end, and very near
15    to our own (people) we wish to be released from the misery
16    of the world, if indeed the Master deems us worthy
17    to be saved in our sojourning abroad.
18    And you, lord brother Nephero, a few
19    little loaves for us from the grain which
20    Papnouthis owes, make and send to us
21    so that I may have (some) with my children; for I wish,
22    God willing, to come up to you
23    and to have (it) in the boat. We greet the
24    beloved brothers all by name and the
25    virgins of God and our father
26    Horion and the mother Tienor and Pinas
27    and the rest of our brothers all
28    by name; may the divine providence keep you ever in good health in soul and body.
29    may the divine providence keep (you).
30    To my lords, beloved brothers Nephero and Ophellios and the rest,
31    Paul[os ] …
