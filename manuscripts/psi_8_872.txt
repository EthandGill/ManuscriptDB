[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Menas, the most honored prefect and magistrate of the city of Oxyrhynchus
2     From Flavius John from the same [city].
3     I report the lawlessness that has been committed against me to your honor and what I have suffered
4     before this. Having suffered assault from Philoxenos, I presented to him a private acknowledgment, and indeed the matters of the
5     private acknowledgment I did not fulfill, but from some tyranny and by force [he] prepared me to present to him a bond
6     to present a manifest quantity of gold, as your honor saw, [with] foreign slaves and servants
7     and allies in my house; which I also complained about to your honor as this [matter]
8     being stale and weak in his case. Since therefore also now with the same design and wrongdoing
9     using [it] he has kept me in private walls and has cast me into underground [chambers] and intending [the]
10    people to seize [according to] his method [things] which he did not furnish me, concerning which indeed a bond
11    I presented to him [I resisted], but this I complained about to your honor, as has been said, intending
12    to approach courts against him concerning this. For this reason this
13    petition I submit [to] your honor asking [to compel] him
