[TRANSLATION]
1     To the village-scribes of Phroura Libos
2     From Aurelia Dioskoroutos (daughter) of Hermaios also called Pathotes
3     mother Sueroutos (daughter of) Kastor of Hermopolis being registered in the district
4     of Phroura Libos, [with] my brother being present and my husband
5     Theoknustos [also called] Moros. I register according to what was commanded
6     by the [most illustrious] governor Valerius Datus for the house-by-house
7     census of the 24th year of Aurelius Severus Antoninus Caesar the lord
8     for which I [previously registered] [station] [at] Phroura Libos 14 myself
9     Aurelia [Dioskorou]n the aforementioned from the gymnasium, aged 30
10    and I swear by the [Aurelius] Severus Antoninus Caesar
11    the lord's fortune [that it is] thus. In the 25th year of the Imperator
12    Caesar Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus Parthicus
13    Maximus [Britannicus] Maximus Germanicus Maximus
14    Pious [Augustus], Phamenoth 20. Aurelius Nikostrates in
15    the lot I received equal.
