[TRANSLATION]
1     … from him, that he might bring them down(?) northward
2     … —e, when the Saracen
3     … that is, Sarah(?) he is(?); he inquired
4     [ … of thi]s [s]ort, he found it, that "you sent down
5     [their] commutation-into-money northward"; he, Sarah the Saracen, stood [before(?)] you,
6     sayi[ng], "I will not take commutation-into-money for them of this sort, except only a body(?) (slave?)"
7     of this sort, having compelled us; [we] went, we hired the workers
8     of this sort. We came, then, we beseech you to give the commutation-into-money
9     which we have given, in writing, northward, to be re[ckoned] before Theodosios the weigher(?),
10    that which you gave gold for; and that he being weighed-out, and that he
11    the one who is the weigher (in) that place, having paid back the amount of gold (to) the workers
12    of this sort, to you, you having handed them over to us, into our hands.
13    Now, then, I acknowledge that, behold, one trimesion and two of the kas(?)
14    has come to me, he being(?) for those nights, that we are not able to come forth against you,
15    whether I myself or my heirs or any person at all who shall dare
16    to give three holokottinoi as a penalty for this matter. The public record being secured, then,
17    through your most-glorious lordship, I have drawn up this acknowledgment for you, agreeing to it,
18    being answerable to you with all my substance for this matter,
19    swearing by the name of God Almighty and the safety of the lords
20    who rule, that we will guard (and) keep watch according as we wrote before.
21    I was asked, I acknowledged. I, Kollouthos, son of Oualente,
22    agree to this acknowledgment according to its force; and I agree that, behold, one trimesion
23    and two keratia has come to me. Markos, son of Kyrillos, with God a priest, having been requested, wrote
24    and bear witness. Hermauo(?), Theodosios, with God a deacon, I bear witness to this acknowledgment
25    according to its force. I, Petros, son of the blessed Makare, bear witness to this acknowledgment according to its force. I, Ezekias,
26    son of Gamoul, bear witness to this acknowledgment according to its force.
27    Through me, Theodoros, with God notary.
