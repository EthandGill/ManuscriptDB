[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Phoeibammon son of abba Sion from the village Ibion and Magdalon
2     of the Theodosiopolis nome to Aurelius Anoup son of Mousaios from the Arsinoite
3     city. I agree [that I] have leased from you from the fields belonging to you in the plain
4     of the same village in a place called Ara, five arourae
5     [with all their right for as long as you wish], and I am to provide
6     the leasing on behalf of [the] fixed tax of them yearly from [the] produce
7     of the entering first indiction gold coins two, gold coins two, in Pauni
8     month from my existing [all, as if under compulsion], and being questioned I agreed.
