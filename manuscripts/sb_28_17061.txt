1     Year 23 of Marcus Aurelius Severus Anto[ninus] Parthicus [Maximus Britannicus]
2     Maximus Germanicus Greatest Pious Se[ba]stus, Choiak 14. [there were recorded to Aurelius Apion and]
3     as he functions as nomarch of Arsinoe through Aurelius Heron assistant of beer-tax per man [of the] village ... twenty-five-arourae, y[ear 23] [through elders] [drachmas] … [they are drachmas] …
4     and on the 30th likewise other beer-tax year 23 drachmas one hundred, they are drachmas 100, and Mecheir 30 likewise [… drachmas eight, obol]
5     they are drachmas 8 obol, of one-bundle silver-currency likewise year 23 drachmas one hundred eighty e[ight] … [they are drachmas 188] … [and]
6     likewise Tybi 30 likewise beer-tax per man year 23 drachmas one hundred fifty[,] [they are drachmas 150, and Phamenoth]
7     30 likewise of one-bundle silver-currency year 23 drachmas two hundred, they are drachmas 200, [and Pharmouti 30 likewise]
8     of district-commander-tax year 23 drachmas ninety-two[,] half-obol they are [drachmas] 92 half-obol, of one-bundle … [year 23 drachmas] [thirty-] two, they are drachmas 32, of crown-tax likewise year 23 drachmas eighty [… they are drachmas 80…] and likewise
9     Pachon 30 of bronze twenty-drachma year 23 drachmas one hundred seventy, they are drachmas [170, and Pauni 30]
10    likewise of litter of the same year 23 drachmas one hundred fifty[…] [they are drachmas 150…]
11    and Epeiph 30 likewise of bronze twenty-drachma year 23 drachmas ninety … [they are drachmas 90…]
