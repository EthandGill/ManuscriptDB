[TRANSLATION]
1     Statilius Ammianus to Septimius Eudaemon
2     who served as cosmete of the Alexandrians, son of Septimius
3     Serenus, greetings.
4     I have appointed you embolarches of the Oxyrhynchites.
5     knowing then the danger of the service
6     take care [that] all the public livestock [should be made]
7     to serve in the dike, so that the ships being sent
8     for the grain-transport should not be delayed. Farewell …
9     [in the year] … of our lord Aurelianus the Revered, Paone 4.
