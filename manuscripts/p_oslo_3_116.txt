[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammonis and the partners
2     tax collectors of [silver] of Apias to Ptolemaeus, son of Didymos, through Akous the farmer, greetings. We have from you into account
3     of the reckoning of the seventh year of Apias silver drachmas eighteen,
4     become drachmas 18. The same boat-tax
5     of bronze obols thirteen, obols 13.
6     Of which we shall make for you public [token] bearing this hand-written [document].
7     Year 7 of Antoninus [C]aesar
8     [of the lord] Pachon 30.
