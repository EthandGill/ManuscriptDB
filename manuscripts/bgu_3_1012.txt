[TRANSLATION]
1     To Antaeus, [epistates] of Philadelphia,
2     from [Marrhaeus] [of] [Petoseirius],
3     merchant. [On the] 28th of Epeiph of the
4     11th year, Dorians, chief phylakite
5     of the same Philadelphia, having brought up from the field the belonging [to me] tax-liable herd
6     of sheep … has enclosed [them]
7     in the house of [Eubius,] phylakite's house,
8     having handed [them] over to Cosmus, phylakite,
9     for which reason I am ignorant. In order therefore
10    that it not happen for them to be dispersed,
11    I request … [to recover] [having brought] back [according to] me, and if [you do not] …
12    … I choose, and if Dorians does not [remain] that they be scattered,
13    to [submit] my [petition] to Ptolemaeus, the chief-bodyguard and general,
14    [that] I might obtain [the just things].
15    Farewell.
