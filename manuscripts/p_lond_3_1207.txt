[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 16, Hathyr 15.
2     Galates,
3     son of Pelaios, one of the
4     ironworkers from Pathyris, has sold from the
5     ground belonging to him
6     a vineyard not yet planted
7     in Temrauthis,
8     in the northern
9     part of Pathyris.
10    Petearsemtheus son of Panebchounis has bought (it)
11    for a price of 4,000 drachmas of copper.
12    In the reign of Ptolemy surnamed Alexander and Berenike his sist[e]r, the Philo[metor] gods,
13    in the sixteenth year, the priests and priestesses and kanephoros being those who are in office, in the month Hathyr 15, in Path[yris],
14    before Hermias the deputy of Paniskos, agoranomos.
15    Galates son of Pelaios, a Persian, one of the ironworkers from Pathyris, about 55 years old, middle height, reddish, long-[faced],
16    straight-nosed, with a scar on the forehead to the right, has sold from the ground belonging to him a vineyard not yet planted in [Te]mrauthis in the northern part of Pathyris, of which the neighbours are: to the south (the land) of Psenosiris son of Kanopos and his bro[thers],
17    to the north (that) of Tamouthis daughter of Pelaios, to the east an island called Tmonpiteb, to the west (that) of Patou[s son of]
18    Horos and his brothers, or whoever the neighbours may be on every side. Petearsemtheus son of Pane[bchounis] has bought (it),
19    a Persian, one of those from Pathyris, about 40 years old, middle height, honey-coloured, with smooth hair, round-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on [the forehead]
20    to the right, for a price of four thousand drachmas of copper. The seller and guarantor of the things concerning t[his sale]
21    is Galates the seller, whom Petearsemtheus the buyer has accepted.
22    I, Hermias the deputy of Panisk[os, have registered (this).]
