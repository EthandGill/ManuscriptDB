[TRANSLATION]
1   [To So-and-so,] priest, prytanis in office, exeg(etes), council(lor) of the city of the Ox(yrhynchites),
2   [from Aurel(ia) Techosarion] also called Isidora, daughter of Panaretos also called Theon, of the same city.
3   [I request,] at my own risk, as guardian of my under-age children
4   [over] their paternal inheritance, Aurelius Achilles
5   [son of Theon ...], their paternal uncle, who is well-off,
6   [... being present] and consenting to the choice of the guardianship, on condition that he
7   [...] to mine, since I too must necessarily be a co-
8   [overseer ...] this petition, so that what follows for them may come about and
9   [their rights not be l]ost. Year 2 of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius
10   [Antoninus Piou]s Felix Augustus, Phaophi. I, Aurelia Techosarion
11   [also called Isidora, have submitted it.] I, Aurelius Serenos, am registered as her guardian, having been asked,
12   [and wrote on her behalf,] as she does [not] know letters. I, Aurelius Achilles son of Theon, consent.
13   [...]…, assistant, through Aurelius Origenes (his) son, I delivered (it).
14   [...], assistant, I delivered (it). Hathyr 3.
