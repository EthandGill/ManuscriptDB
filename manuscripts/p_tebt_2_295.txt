1     Of Kronion, son of Psoiphis.
2     Of the priestly ranks; agreement of the 13th year, royal (scribe's roll), volume 1.
3     Of the (offices) that were declared by Claudius Dionysios the former deputy-strategos
4     to have been transmitted to him by Charisios his predecessor, as also to Charisios himself (they had been) transmitted by Apollonides his predecessor, as being liable to be sold:
5     the prophetship of the temple of Soknebtynis also (called) Kronos, of the village of Teptynis, of which it was declared first that Harpochration, son of Marepsemis, priest and stolistes of the same temple, promised (to pay) 100 drachmas, which he also paid over in the … year of Hadrian Caesar the lord, and in the
6     8th year
