[TRANSLATION]
1     [Declaration] … [unclear] … year … of Alexander; Phamenoth;
2     Claudius Herennius [to the] strategoi of seven nomes and [Arsinoe]ite, greetings.
3     What [Calpurnius] Petronius, the most distinguished procurator, chief judge, sent to me.
4     With the [attached] memoranda [concerning] him made [, which] I ordered to be submitted. You [should] attend [to it] according to what has been declared, [so that] the transactions be completed lawfully — for thus … the contracts [will be] — and what is owed for them to the.
5     Most distinguished city [of the] Alexandrians to be paid, [with] those over the.
6     Previously [offended,] fixed [penalties]; and the even greater.
7     Similar [prosecution] upon those also after this [memorandum] [of those] disobeying;
8     Of this my [letter] copies as it contains to be published in clear writing, in.
9     In the metropolis and [the notable] places of the nome, [you should see to it], so that no one [may be ignorant of] [what has been proclaimed]; and these [things] I write [making clear] [matters concerning the governorship] from divine [command].
10    … Year, Mecheir, 1 …
11    Calpurnio[s].
