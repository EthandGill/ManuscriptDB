[TRANSLATION]
1     Sinpsanseus to Leonides the
2     son, [I] greet [you].
3     You will do well having transferred
4     beside you in Oxyrhynchus to Aurelius Herakleides son of Kephalon bird-keeper partner of Leonides the brother
5     of us [the sum of] silver of the
6     coinage nine-hundred drachmas, in return for which I have received the equal
7     full nine-hundred drachmas
8     here in the village Septa, but not
9     may you hold him. As a token
10    for the reason that I deposited
11    to you linen bolts three-hundred
12    when you entered with Alexander the linen-merchant. The deposit [is] valid and being questioned
13    I acknowledged.
14    Year 14 of our lord [Gallien]us [Augustus] Thoth 29.
