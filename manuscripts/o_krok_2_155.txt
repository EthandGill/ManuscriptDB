[TRANSLATION]
1     Philocles to Capparion and
2     Didymus both of you
3     brothers [the] greatest
4     greetings. I beseech you
5     to take care of my child
6     and its mother
7     and all things in the house as also always so that even now
8     you know that we have no one
9     except yourselves. I think that
10    I am there.
11    Bring a bundle of cabbage from Maximus. Write back to me concerning
12    which I write to Sciphis immediately.
13    Fare well.
