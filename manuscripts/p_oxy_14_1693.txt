[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Apion [son] of Apollonius, who was also called Heraklas, gymnasiarch [and] councillor of the illustrious and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, leased to Aurelius
2     Apollotheon [son] of Diogenes, from the [same]
3     city for a period of 4 years from [the] 1st [of] Phamenoth of the present 4th year which he holds in
4     the same city in the quarter of Pammenes
5     [of] Paradise a [courtyard] in which
