[TRANSLATION]
1   In the second year of the Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Epeiph 17, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites of the Thebaid.
2   Epimachos son of Harsiesis son of Epimachos, his mother being Demes, and the cousins,
3   Epimachos
4   and Petosiris, both sons of Dionysios son of Epimachos, their mother being Thatres, and the paternal aunt of them all,
5   Se-
6   psarion daughter of Epimachos son of Dionysios, her mother being Thatres, all from the city of the Oxyrhynchites, and
7   Sepsarion with as guardian ...
8   Hermas son of Theon, in the street, acknowledge to one another that they have divided among themselves by mutual consent for the present the
9   house and courtyard belonging to them
10   in the village of Kerketheyris, in which there are four palm-trees, of all which there belongs to
11   Epimachos son of Harsiesis
12   two parts out of five parts, and to Epimachos and Petosiris, both sons of Dionysios, in equal share two parts, and to Sepsarion
13   daughter of Epimachos one part, and that there has been allotted by lot to Epimachos son of Harsiesis his own two parts from the
14   north part,
15   [...] extending from west to east to the end of the whole grounds, and to Sepsarion daughter of Epi-
16   machos, adjoining, after Epimachos son of Harsiesis, on the south, extending from west to east
17   to the end of the whole
18   grounds, and to Petosiris, adjoining, after Sepsarion, on the south, his own one part
19   extending from west to east
20   to the end of the whole grounds, and to Epimachos son of Dionysios, adjoining, after his brother Peto-
21   siris son of Dionysios, likewise his own one part extending likewise from west to east to
22   the end of the whole grounds;
23   and that each one of them, together with the loads (rubble) falling in upon them, into what has been allotted, of the four palm-trees together with
24   [...] into the parts of Epimachos and Petosiris both sons of Dionysios ... of both ...
25   And the acknowledging parties agree, each according to his aforesaid parts, an entrance [...]
26   [...] on the east of the whole house and courtyard, of a part of a width from west to east of cubits ...
27   [...] to the end of the whole grounds, into which entrance it shall be permitted to them to leave open whatever (doors) they may choose ...
28   [...] through which each of them will enter into only the (parts) which he has been allotted as stated above, and to manage
29   and (that)
30   [each of them] hold and own what he has obtained by lot for ever ... each (of the two?)
31   [... nor] make any claim against one another in any way.
