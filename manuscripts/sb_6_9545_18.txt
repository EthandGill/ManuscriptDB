[TRANSLATION]
1     Through Sarapion the assistant.
2     Kalaseris son of Patramethis, mother Thinpachnubis, has paid off on account of the capitation tax of the
3     eleventh year of Hadrian
4     Caesar the lord, in parts,
5     seventeen drachmas [and] a half-obol for prison-keeping [tax], a half-obol,
6     amounting to seventeen drachmas [and] an obol. Of year twelve of Hadrian
7     Caesar the lord
8     Phaophi, the twenty-fourth.
