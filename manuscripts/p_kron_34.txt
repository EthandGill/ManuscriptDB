[TRANSLATION]
1     To Asklepiades son of Asklepiades,
2     from Kronion son of Kronion son of Cheos, from the village of Tebtynis, a Persian of the epigone. I wish [to lease] for seven years
3     from the coming twentieth year of [Trajan Hadrian]
4     the (lands) belonging to you around the village of Ker[ke]sis [in the] place [called] … [of] grain-bearing
5     [land] twelve and a half arouras, and in pasture-lands twel[ve]
6     and a half arouras, in all twenty-[fi]ve arouras in catoecic category,
7     for the first and third and fifth and seventh year the
8     twelve and a half arouras to be sown for sowing of grass and fodder
9     for sheep and for grazing, and these same and the twenty-five arouras in the pasture-lands
10    over the four-year (period) without rent, in return for the
11    (charges) due upon these same twenty-five arouras over the four-year (period),
12    the artaba-tax and naubion-tax, which I, Kronion, shall measure out and pay into the public (treasury) each year [as is due]; and [the] second and fourth
13    and sixth year, those to be sown for sowing of wheat, at a rent over the three-year (period), each year [of seedless] wheat, eighty artabas by the four-choinix measure of the [granary] of Tebtynis, and a rent
14    for the pasture-land over the [same three-year (period)] each year of two hundred and [forty] silver drachmas, and (a charge) of shoots over the [seven]-year (period), each year of two cocks, [each worth] four [drachmas], being toward you, Asklepiades — the (charges) of the arouras
15    over the three-year (period), the artaba-tax and naubion-tax and all the other public dues that shall arise being toward you the landholder, Asklepiades; the urgings (for) the freight-charges being toward me, Kronion,
16    and the pasture-land for grazing of whatever beasts they choose;
17    and the rents and dues (being) free of deduction and free of risk; and I shall perform
18    each year all the agricultural works of both the lot and the pasture-land,
19    as many as are fitting, at my own (cost) at the proper seasons, doing no damage; and I shall pay the [rents] in the month of Pauni, new, pure, unadulterated, each year, and the rent in [Phamenoth]; and I shall give you each year over the
20    seven-year (period) ten artabas of cumin; and after the period
21    I shall hand over the sown (lands) [free] from grazing of sheep and clean from couch-grass and all undergrowth except rush and reed; and I shall guarantee the lease to you [on] the aforesaid (terms). Asklepiades:
22    I have leased out in return for the aforesaid (terms) twenty-five arouras
23    for seven years, four years in return for all the public dues,
24    both grain and silver, and for the remaining three years for the
25    aforesaid eighty artabas of wheat and two hundred
26    and forty drachmas and (the charge) of shoots over the seven-year (period) of two
27    cocks (worth) eight drachmas, and one measure of cumin. Year 20
28    of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Thoth 15.
