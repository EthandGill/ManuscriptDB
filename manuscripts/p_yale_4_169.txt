[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammonas and Tyrannus and those with them, renters of the desert-toll of [the] Mareiotic [region]
2     and [of] Libya and [of] Prosopites and [of] Letopolis and [of] other places, through
3     Claudius Anoub, agent of Theres, Panoupheus Sempronios, from [the island of] Nesos,
4     greetings. [Receipt] has been recorded by you regarding two camels [at] twelve drachmas,
5     [totaling] twelve drachmas. Year 23 of Marcus Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Augustus
6     [the] Greatest, Mesore [the] fifteenth.
