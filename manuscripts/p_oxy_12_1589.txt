[TRANSLATION]
1     … [greet].
2     … [I] wrote to you as
3     you wrote to me. Know then that from me
4     your registration came about, having persuaded him thus to register so that I too might be able to register equal [amounts], but also
5     the [toward] part land of yours in that very
6     Egypt. Do everything since I am coming
7     to Egypt, and if you carry to me wine, and write to me what I should give to him
8     [as] carriage-fee. But do not neglect. Farewell to me, lord.
