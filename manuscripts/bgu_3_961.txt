r.2   ἐπικ(ειμένοις) χαρακόνων κοπῆς δ ἰν(δικτίονος) ὑπὲρ τροφ(ῆς) μη(νὸς) α καὶ προν(οηταῖς) β ἐλ(αίου) ξ(έστας) τέσσαρας ,

[TRANSLATION]
1     (Chi-Rho) Theon to Sambas the oil-maker: provide twelve xestai of oil to the 12 assistants (symmachoi)
2     set over the cutting of stakes (of the) 4th indiction, for maintenance of 1 month, and to 2 overseers (pronoetai), 4 xestai of oil,
3     total, of oil, 16 xestai only. Choiak 10, [4]th indiction. (hand 2) † I have signed for sixteen xestai of oil only †.
