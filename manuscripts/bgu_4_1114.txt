[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos
2     from Himeros son of Ptolemaios, of the Zeneios (deme), and from
3     Quintus Caecilius Castor. Since it happens that
4     Quintus has received from his own patron
5     Quintus Caecilius Oenogenes, in the parts abroad,
6     to convey here to Himeros
7     as a gift the slaves Philetairos and Phaithros
8     and Bassos and Vitlos, and the voyage down was made by mistake into the name of Quintus
9     Caecilius Castor himself, and he has registered
10    to Himeros, without receiving a price, in the
11    twenty-third year of Caesar, through the
12    a[g]oranomoi, the aforenamed slaves, 4; and further also another slave, Optatos, having been paid for on behalf of … Optatos … through the bank of Asklepiades,
13    in silver, drachmas
14    one thousand two hundred—now Himeros agrees
15    that he has received … from Quintus Caecilius Casto[r]
16    through the same bank of Asklepiades the (sums)
17    paid out to him on behalf of Optatos … one thousand two hundred drachmas; (and) that neither he himself nor any other on his behalf shall proceed against Quintus
18    Caecilius Castor concerning these things or the guarantee
19    of the five slaves in any way
20    —apart from the things agreed remaining valid—
21    (and that) he, Himeros, transgressing, shall be liable both to the damages and to the appointed penalty,
22    because also Optatos was sent to Himeros by
23    Oenogenes as a gift.
24    To be settled. Year 26 of Caesar, Phaophi 26.
