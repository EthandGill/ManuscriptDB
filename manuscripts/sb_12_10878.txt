[TRANSLATION]
1     Choiak 16. Antiouries son of Abos paid for the fourteenth indiction
2     of the village of Karanidis wheat
3     artabas two and a half and a third
4     and a twelfth, totaling artabas 2 1/2 1/3 1/12. Ammōnios, I have signed.
5     Choiak 10. Antiouries paid for the village of Kainu of Karanidis fourteenth indiction wheat artabas two and a twelfth, totaling artabas 2 1/12. Ammōnios, I have signed.
6     And on the 22nd the same Antioury[ies]
7     for the fourteenth indiction of the village of Karanidis wheat
8     one artaba and a half and a third
9     and a twelfth, totaling artaba 1 1/2 1/3 1/12. Ammōnios, I have signed.
