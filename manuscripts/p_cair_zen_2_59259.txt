[TRANSLATION]
1     Maimachus to Zenon greetings. … compel Kallippos
2     to repay to Zenon the … [for I] neither to Kallippos nor
3     to Sostratos I gave … [I have] written off the money. So that
4     the grain be brought down, [take] care … you said to me concerning the grain.
5     For I hasten with speed … Farewell. Year 34, Hathyr 12.
