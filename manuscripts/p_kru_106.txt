[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving
2     consubstantial Trinity, the Father and the Son
3     and the Holy Spirit.
4     Written in the month Pauni 6, of the third indiction,
5     under Mamet, most glorious amir, amir of the pagarchy
6     of Hermonthis, and Chael son of Psmo, most illustrious dioiketes from the fortress of Memnonia,
7     in the year of Diocletian the emperor 451, and in the year
8     of the Saracens 114.
9     I, Anna, the daughter of the blessed John,
10    her mother being the blessed Taham, of the fortress of Djeme
11    under the nome of the city of Hermonthis, who, after the subscription by the hand of him who subscribed for me,
12    am writing to the God-honored and esteemed brother Abba Zacharias, the great superior, and Abba Philotheos and
13    Abba Mena, his assessors, and the great monks of the holy place, which is the congregation of the holy monasteries of holy Apa Paulos of the Rock
14    of the mountain of Djeme: that I rejoice and acknowledge, I,
15    now this wretched Anna, the one writing first, by this deed of donation, an Egyptian document, unbreakable and unshakable forever, I establish it
16    by the laws that are set down, with no compulsion set down
17    upon me, nor any deceit through fear, nor extortion through guile, nor any fraud, deception, or circumvention whatsoever, but
18    these things being of my own will, without scruple, and with unchanging reckoning,
19    with a settled heart and an untroubled conscience, having no deceit within it,
20    and with an upright heart and a firm faith,
21    fulfilled in every righteous lordship, without
22    force, without deceit, without extortion,
23    without onset out of any peril; but these things of
24    my own will, with an unwavering purpose
25    and a vigilant mind, while I am alive, walking in and out, while I have strength in my body, my mind standing firm, having now requested the scribe to confirm these things for me by
26    my own will and the resolve of the donation, by the hand
27    also of those who shall witness to this deed of donation, not transgressing it and not altering it,
28    according to the laws. I write from now to forever,
29    forever; I write now and I donate
30    to the holy monastery, this one now which gives forth light and
31    shines in its rays of light through its prayers
32    and its holy way of life, these who ascended up to the heavens, the anchorite and citizen-saint and ascetic, the blessed holy Apa Paulos, through
33    your God-honored brotherhood, Abba Zacharias the great
34    superior, and his other brethren, the assessors who are with him, Philotheos and Mena, and through those who shall come after them according to the season, one after another, of the administration.
35    Since in these times now in which we have come to them
36    God the Good has been merciful to me, opening my heart to cast
37    my little mite, this small thing, toward the word of my Lord
38    which he spoke in the holy gospels concerning the mite,
39    the two of the widow which she cast into the treasury, having more than those who put in more had to put in,
40    except only two mites; but the Lord rejoiced over them. I
41    also now, this wretched woman who looks forth from her hour, have understood through the great sickness which has fallen upon me
42    that I have approached toward the end, according to what the holy prophet said: "Make me to know, O Lord, my end," in accordance with the will
43    therefore of the Good God in my falling into this sickness,
44    this one now into which I am to go, according to the way of all my fathers,
45    having been afraid as I am upon the bier, looking to this side of me and to the side
46    the second, not seeing that to which I shall move, that I should be raised up over it, to reach
47    a small repose in the place to which I have need, since God
48    has cast it into my heart to donate this small thing for the remembrance
49    into the holy monastery, this one now which I have first
50    named with its holy stele-inscription above, of this deed of donation, holy Apa Paulos of the Rock, the great
51    anchorite—first because his entreaties
52    and his holy intercessions receive favor for me before
53    the true Judge, and because my little thing shall be for the remembrance, continuing on, because of the great love which exists now
54    toward the poor who pass by the holy monastery,
55    and because of those whom the brethren send out to the poor and
56    those in need, according to the way that the tongue that gives forth sweetness, the holy
57    Paulos the apostle, spoke, that "love never fails,"
58    and that "mercy boasts itself over judgment." In
59    my going then toward this, I made remembrance also of what our fathers
60    the apostles said in the holy catholic epistles, that "the entreaty
