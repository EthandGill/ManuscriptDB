[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Alexander, strategus of Arsinoe
2     of Themistos and Polemon divisions and to Casian
3     royal scribe of Themistos division and village scribe
4     of the village of Apiad
5     from Anchophis Stotoetes
6     priest of the gods of the village of Soknopaiou
7     Nesu. I register in accordance with what was ordered by Pompeius Kelearis, the most excellent prefect
8     the properties belonging to me near the village Apiad
9     of a residential allotment of 5 arourae in a place
10    called fifteen-arourae, unwatered in the present
11    9 year. Therefore I submit.
12    Papontus village scribe received [this]
13    copy until examination Phamenoth 24.
