[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Dioskoras his own (friend)
2     … [very many] greetings.
3     … having considered, and … eu …
4     … to repay now to me
5     … you having the whole
6     account, of which you also have a [copy]. Cross over
7     then to Heptakomia with him, together also with
8     Eutychos, whom you made master of
9     my affairs. Play along with him, and bring him too
10    with yourself there. And remember
11    that of all those things whose price you wrote you would add,
12    you must repay them according to what you wrote,
13    … the price of the wheat and the
14    lentils … and the other things … for I neither had the plough nor told you to give him anything. And even if I had appointed (it), you would not have had (the right) to give to him
15    apart from me or (my) authorization, if you bear me goodwill,
16    nor did I instruct you to give, having followed me … but you have already lingered, staying on since I sailed down,
17    so that you may receive the price of the wine at
18    five drachmas of silver per portion. For you ought from then on to have sent the price. But you wrote to me that … Androkles, after a few days I will disclose to you the price.
19    On top of everything you wrote that Eutychos did not give me the price
20    and that he (says) that he does not give it apart from you.
21    Know then that I will charge against you the price of the wine
22    at five drachmas. And if I do not receive (it) now, I have written
23    also to him to cross over to Heptakomia,
24    until the matter is settled, so that the
25    silver may be given to Apollonios, the strategos of Heptakomia, and he may write to you that he has received the silver.
26    On top of everything, however, know that, having behaved thus in all your dealings toward me, you are not going to
27    escape me, I who cultivate all my lands. By the gods, I thought, when I was following
28    the prefect, that in Hermopolis I would find
29    a talent toward the … brought in by you from the flax that was measured out
30    in the granary.
31    Farewell and … that you do not
32    escape me.
33    Farewell.
34    In the 17th year of the Imperator Caesar the lord, Pauni
35    18.
36    [To Dios]koros [the] friend
