[TRANSLATION]
1     Zoilos, [scribe of farmers], to Akousilaus, grain-measurer, [greetings. Measure]
2     For Ptolemaeus … into which [he farms] [royal land]
3     Of wheat five, [it] becomes wheat 5 artabae, of year [20 Hathyr 15.]
4     Marres, village-scribe; measure the foregoing [wheat]
5     Five, [they] become wheat artabae 5. Of year 20 Hathyr [15].
6     Onnophris, leader of farmers; settle artabae 5, [they] become artabae 5
7     Of year 20 Hathyr 15.
