10    headman, in the local civil court concerning the debt

13    [the son] for the property mortgaged to him by my parents
14    and sold to you, the aforewritten Phoibammon and
15    Anastasia his wife, by my nephews/nieces a short
16    time ago, namely Eudoxia and Antonia and Kollouthos, in accordance
17    with the force of the sales of the same property made to you by them,

19    moved between me, Nikantinoos, and Apollos the headman
20    over the matters here, who also fills the office of you the aforewritten, before trial and before adjudicatory contests, finally good friends

25    less two carats by the goldsmith's standard of the said village
