[TRANSLATION]
1     … ph …
2     To [Bakchon] the [dioiketes], from Amennes, [son] of Horos,
3     [and O]nnophris, son of Teos, undertakers (embalmers) of those
4     [from T]anis of the Arsinoite nome. We denounce
5     [Aby]kis, son of Horos, and [Kele]chon, son of Pasis, and Pasis,
6     son of [Psenephm]ous, undertakers from the village of Phil[a]delphia
7     of [the s]ame nome, that, whereas there is a decree that the
8     … those … to those who apostatized in madness,
9     their property is to be taken over into the royal treasury,
10    and whereas Psene[phmou]s, son of Paos, had become one of the apostates — a man who had no kinsman whatsoever, but who owned both landed property and other things, and likewise
11    also the embalmers' perquisite of the salting-houses (taricheiai) of those in Philadelphia —
12    these having been collected, and it having been
13    written that his property should be sold into the royal treasury, the house belonging to him in the
14    aforewritten village was sold, which Hakoris, son of Paches,
15    a royal cultivator from the same village, bought and holds until now; but the other things belonging to him and the
16    aforesaid perquisite, the party of Abykis, against whom
17    I lodge this information, have shared out among themselves contrary to what is proper,
18    not [pay]ing over the price as is customary. And there accrue from the perquisite yearly of copper 4,000 (drachmas), which they carry off from the 2nd year until [the pre]sent,
19    for 19 years, making [12] talents 4,000 drachmas. [So that nothing may be over]looked,
