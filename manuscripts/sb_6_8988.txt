1     In the name of the holy consubstantial and life-giving Trinity, Father and [Son] and [Holy]
2     [Spirit, it was] written in the month Epeiph, on the twenty-second of the sixth indiction in the city of Apollo[nia]. The present Aculianan and comprehensive [and]
3     dissolution-exemption, having the force of all other security,
4     is placed and made in the market of this upper Apollonia
5     by John the most pious deacon, son of the blessed Abraham,
6     towards Philemon and Thecla [his co]njoint-spouse, daughter of apa Dius, both
7     hailing from this upper Apollonia. And the matter is
8     as the following discourse clarifies: before a considerable time, that is before the
9     [Persians'] arrival, Thecla, the blessed daughter of Jacob Cana
10    [pledged] to Philemon and Thecla his spouse through a written hypothecary
11    security, having force after the appointed deadline set in it,
12    complete renunciation of her whole portion from the house of the heirs
13    Karaune situated in the height of the city in partnership
14    of Philemon and his spouse [to] the remaining portion of such
15    up[on] … house. Of then the aforesaid Philemon and Thecla his spouse
16    upon management and mastership having come from then of the same portion of Thecla the
17    blessed towards the remaining portion appertaining to them for completion of the
18    [whole] house, in accordance with the management and mastership by them of the often
19    [afore]said portion of Thecla the blessed until her death, and after
20    her Thecla's death, as it seemed [good], to John the most pious deacon,
21    he wrested away the management and mastership of the same portion of house
22    of Thecla the blessed through the hand of Menas Musaeus the collector,
23    [so as to] force them all to leave everything and to go down in the
24    [Oxyrhyn]chite city and to obtain from the same Thecla's heirs dwelling
25    there complete sale of such pledged to them portion of house for
26    [more secure] confirmation for them. And so having gone up they approached the aforesaid
27    [most] pious man wishing to come into management of the house in accordance with
28    the ancient form. And many things having been spoken, at last it seemed [good] so that they
29    should be heard towards one another before Marinus and Marcianus, the most esteemed
30    administrators of this city, whom both were commonly selected
31    by both of them, and many arguments having occurred for
32    sufficient days and they both renouncing
33    their arguments with subscription of both of them,
34    [c]oming to himself John the most pious deacon having learned
35    especially that he had no just argument against them from the
36    arguments subscribed by him; but rather having considered it profitable
37    for him to settle with them, being assured especially from the force
38    of the clear sale having been shown to the same esteemed judges,
39    and he took counsel to renounce and to be freed from
40    such a suit, fearing lest towards such
41    a breaking of the case he be harmed and [pay] the fees of the judges.
42    [R]ightly therefore and willingly to this he has come
43    to the written Aculianan exemption, the same most pious
44    John, through which he acknowledges and has acknowledged, being present in public
45    place, having first in awareness renounced and renouncing
46    every assistance from laws and every prerogative and decree and privilege

48    him or that might befit, but willingly and persuaded, by self-chosen will and unwavering judgment, without any guile
49    and mockery and force and deception and compulsion and
50    extortion, having no longer any argument or motion or suit
51    to have or will have against Philem[on] and Thecla his spouse, not John
52    himself, the most pious deacon, nor his heirs, nor successors, nor deputies,
53    neither against them nor against their heirs, nor successors, nor deputies, concerning the often-said whole portion of house
54    of the same blessed Thecla, that is of the small dining-room
55    above the gateway of the same house and its air-space
56    up to and including it, and of the small room or rather open courtyard
57    facing east towards the pool, and the sleeping-room above it
58    and its air-space, with everything in whatever manner
59    theirs by right and appurtenances, that is all kinds of communal
60    places above and below of the same whole house, because
61    completely to be released him towards them, as especially

63    them concerning which he brought against them, that is of the portion house

67    and all those with him, that is his heirs of every sort,

69    dissolution, and nothing of all things contained in it

75    it should seem good to him or to any of his in whatever manner or time
