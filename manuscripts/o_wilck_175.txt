[TRANSLATION]
1   Valerius Marion and those
2   with him, overseers of the Sacred Gate of Syene,
3   through Pachapsachis the assistant. Paid: Pa-
4   tesibdis son of Petorzmethes, for

6   (and) two obols — total 20 dr. 2 ob. Year 3
7   of Antoninus Caesar
