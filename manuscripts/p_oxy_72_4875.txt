[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Dius son of Acusilaus, through Acusilaus, (my) son, to the sitologi of Cercethyris, greetings. Transfer, from the amount you hold on deposit for me, from the wheat-crop of the nineteenth year of Hadrianus Caesar the lord, to Syntyche, freedwoman of Sarapias daughter of Dionysius, (for her account) at Seryphis, eight (and) one-quarter (artabas) (and) seven choenices, (and) to Heracles alias Apion son of Apion, (for his account) at Sepho, two (and) one half (and) one-quarter (artabas) (and) three choenices, grand total 11 ¼ art. Year 19 of Imperator Caesar Traianus Hadrianus Augustus, Mesore 30. (N. Gonis)
