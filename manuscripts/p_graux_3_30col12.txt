[TRANSLATION]
1     To Deios and Sabinus, ex-gymnasiarchs, and partners,
2     public bankers,
3     Neilos son of Akes and Heron also called Peter
4     and Neilos son of Heron and Neilos son of Herakles,
5     the four cattle-breeders of the village
6     of Narmouthis, through Primos, appointed
7     by them: we have received from you
8     the sums assigned to us by Ptolemaios
9     the royal secretary of the Polemon division, deputizing for the affairs of [the] strategia, for
10    transport-charges of the wheat [which we brought down]
11    from the granaries of the Pole[mon]
12    division to the harbours,
13    the work being on the authority of the royal secretary, of the produce
14    of the 17th year, of wheat twenty-eight artabas
15    and a half and a twenty-fourth,
16    at the price of the artaba according to [what was] commanded, of eight drachmas,
17    the sum amounting to two hundred and twenty-eight silver drachmas
18    and two obols, total 228 drachmas two obols,
19    from the account of administration: Neilos artabas 6 ½ ⅓, and
20    Heron also called Peter artabas 6 ½ ⅓ 1/12, and Neilos artabas 7 1/6,
21    and Neilos artabas 7 1/8. Total the aforesaid sums.
22    I, Primos, appointed by the aforewritten men, have received the silver drachmas, 228 two obols, as
23    is set forth above. In the 18th year of Antoninus Caesar
24    the lord, Mecheir 25.
