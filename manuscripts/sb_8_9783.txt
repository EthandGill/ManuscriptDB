[TRANSLATION]
1     Ailius of Arsinoe through Kyrillus
2     steward, to Aieuti the farmer
3     Greetings. I have from you the export-duty of the 17th year for which you have
4     my arourae in lease around the village Euhemeria of the
5     Themiste's meris, the leases being valid. Year 17
6     of Lucius Septimius Seve[rus the] Pious Pertinax and M[arcus]
7     [A]urelius Antoninus the Pious
8     [August]us and Pub[lius] Septimius
9     Geta Caesar Augustus, Payni 20.
