[TRANSLATION]
1     To Chairemōn, village-scribe of Bacchiades,
2     from Taorsenouphios, [daughter] of Psenaturmis, with [her] guardian, [her] full-brother
3     and full-sister-uterine brother Kamelos
4     of those from the aforesaid village.
5     My son Onnōphris, [son] of Psenamounēs
6     of Zōilos, having been underage
7     of those from the same village
8     has died in the Germaniceion
9     month of the present second year
10    of Emperor Caesar Vespa[sian Au]g[ustus.] …
11    notice concerning the deceased
