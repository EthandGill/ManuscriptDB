[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolem[aios and Queen Cleopatra], the sister [the gods]
2     Philometors, greetings
3     From [Ptolem]ai[os, son of Gl]aukios, [a] M[acedonian, being held in]
4     the Sarap[i]e[ion Astartieia for te]n years, not [having left] the inner sanctu[m,]
5     in which I am [e]nclose[d until this] day. On the 16th of Thoth Ptolemaios
6     who was appoint[ed through] the in the Anubieion chief-guard and Amosios
7     of by the high-priest taking [guards] and having entered into
8     the in the great Sarapieion Astarteion, where I am also held, as indeed I said,
9     until now, [the people] claiming [that] weapons [were] in the [place], and having searched all the place, [finding] nothing, and the guards at the exit having shaken off [their garments] and doing nothing improper
10    Amosios, the one from the high-priest, return[ing] with Imouthes, [the one by the] over the inner-sanctum keepers and Harendotos [of Katy]tos and Harendotos [of Nic]chthnibia
11    and Erbelletas of Harimouthos [and Ps]enchonsios of Amosios [his] follower
12    late in the hour and having [a lantern], having forced me and plundered the shrine
13    he brought out everything that was unattached, but the rest he sealed [up]
14    and on the [seven]teenth of the [same] month, returning in the morning, he brought out
15    the rest of the sealed items, but in addition to these also [items] of the others
16    being held [in the] deposits he plundered. Now when a certain Harmaios recognized
17    his own jar in which also were bronzes and having seized [it], disregarding [it]
18    Amos[ios], sealing this too, placed [it] with a certain Theon, [son] of Paes.
19    Not only [that] but also entering into the inner sanctum of the goddess he pl[und]ered the temple such that
20    [it] was in danger and to shatter it. When [again] [Amosios returned with] Harimouthos
21    of by the overseer and having entered and having found a bronze basin
22    being of the goddess and […] her and having brought [it] out, in addition [to this]
23    Pabelfis [son] of Petearendotos and Nicchthnibia [son] of Imouthos, having missed [the proper course], having entered and having searched the place
24    and finding nothing useful, finding certain lead cups nonetheless not even these did they leave us. When we asked for what reason they had entered, they answered us saying [they had] come under pledge
25    and [had been] sent by Psulin, the [one over] the inner-sanctum keepers, and they placed
26    the cups with Kephala, the door-keeper. Therefore I beseech you, king, not to overlook
27    me in every respect being besieged ungratefully by the aforesaid
28    and being outraged and being treated unlawfully, but if it seems good to you, order
29    to appoint before you defending on my behalf Demetrius [son] of Sosos
30    a Cretan, because I being in confinement am unable, and to appoint also the
31    aforesaid, so that they might undertake justice to me before you. When this has happened I shall have obtained aid from you.
32    Fare well.
