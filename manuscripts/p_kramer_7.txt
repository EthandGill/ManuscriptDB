1     To Aurelius Hermaiscos the
2     and to Hermaiōn royal secretary
3     of Arsinoite Themistus and Polemon districts.
4     From Aurelius Heron son of Orses from village Tebtunis. Not being an [accuser],
5     but defending myself against him for which I was wronged, I report Aurelius Heraklas fatherless
6     of mother Heraklia being called
7     Abaskanton unregistered in the household [registrations] … -atates
8     … -ephas
9     …an Aurelius Heron [I] submitted. Aurelius Heroneinos [I] wrote on behalf of him

13    [Aug]ustus, Pauni.
