r.2    τοῦ κυρίου Παῦνι α ὑπὲρ Κεραμείων ὀνόματος κληρονόμων Πικῶτος ὁμοίως

[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of villages, the harvest of year 18 of Antoninus Caesar
2     of the lord, Pauni 1, for the Kerameion [district] on behalf of the heirs of Picotus, likewise
3     Abotos through the farmer Petobeis of Picotus, year 16, grain one twenty-fourth
4     Total grain 1 and 24th. P[…] I have signed.
