r.8    αὐ[λ]ὴ μεθʼ ἣν οἰκία παλαιὰ, καὶ ἀπέχειν τὸν ὁμολογοῦντα Μάρωνα παρὰ τοῦ Ἑρμᾶτος τὰς συνπεφωνημένας τ[ῆς] τιμῆς ἀ[ργυ]ρίου δραχμὰς ἑκατὸν πάσας ἐκ πλήρους παραχρῆμα διὰ χειρὸς ἐξ οἴκου, καὶ βεβαιώσειν αὐτόν τε τὸν ὁμολογοῦντα Μάρων[α καὶ]

r.10   χρ[ό]νον καὶ παρέξεσθαι αὐτὰ ἀνέπαφα καὶ ἀνενεχύραστα καὶ ἀνεπιδάνειστα καὶ ἀνεξαλλοτρίωτα καὶ καθαρὰ ἀ[πὸ]
r.11   δημοσίου τε καὶ ἰδιωτικοῦ ὀφειλήματος καὶ μήτε προυποκείμενα μηδὲ προπεπραμένα ἑτέροις μηδὲ μέρος τ[ρόπῳ ][μη][δε]νὶ μηδὲ κωλύοντας τὸν πριάμενον Ἑρμᾶν μηδὲ τοὺς παρʼ μεταλημψομένους κυριεύο[ντας καὶ δεσ][πό]ζοντας ὧν ἐώνηται καθὼς π[ρ]όκειται καὶ εἰσοδεύοντας καὶ ἐξοδεύοντας καὶ κατασπῶντας καὶ ἀ[νοικοδο][μο]ῦντας ἐν αὐτοῖς ὡς ἐὰν αἱρῶνται ἔτι πωλοῦντας ὑποτιθέντας ἑτέροις μεταδιοικοῦντας χρω[μένους]

[TRANSLATION]
1     intercalary day 3.
2     In the tenth year of Imperator Caesar Vespasian Augustus, in the month Kaisareios, on the third intercalary day, in Bakchias of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nome. Maron son of Hermas son of Maron, about forty-seven years old, with a scar in the middle of his forehead, acknowledges to Hermas son of Ptolemaios son of Ammonios, about forty-four years old, with a scar in the middle of his forehead, that he has sold to him, according to this present agreement, from the present day for all time,
3     the property belonging to him, the acknowledging party Maron, in the aforewritten village of Bakchias — a maternal third part and a purchased ninth part, so as to be a third-and-ninth part of a jointly-owned house and an old courtyard that have partly collapsed,
4     of whatever number are the total measurements and cubit-areas, upon the existing ancient foundations and outer-walls in [their]
5     existing arrangement, of all which the neighbors are: on the south, the lots of Katoites son of Mengcheus and his associates, and for some
6     part a common entrance and exit into which a door opens; on the north, a royal street; on the west, of the aforewritten Katoites
7     son of Mengcheus and his associates an old house; on the east, of Horos son of Katoites son of Belleus and his brothers
8     a courtyard, after which an old house; and that the acknowledging party Maron has received from Hermas the agreed-upon price of the silver, one hundred drachmas in full, all of it, forthwith from hand to hand out of the house; and that he, the acknowledging party Maron, [and]
9     those acting for him shall guarantee to Hermas and to those acting for him the things sold under this present sale, with every guarantee, for all
10    time, and shall provide them free from claim, free from pledge, free from loan, free from alienation, and clear
11    from public and private debt, and neither previously mortgaged nor previously sold to others nor any part in any manner, nor hindering the purchaser Hermas nor those who will take over from him, they being owners and masters of what he has bought as aforesaid, and entering and exiting and demolishing and rebuilding upon them as they may choose, further selling, mortgaging to others, transferring, using
12    them in whatever manner they may wish without hindrance, and that no one shall bring suit nor dispute nor proceed against the purchaser Hermas nor against those acting for him in any manner on any pretext
13    whatsoever. But whatever of the aforewritten things the acknowledging party Maron or those acting for him shall transgress, or shall not [guarantee or shall not]
14    provide as has been written, apart from the contravening act being void and inadmissible in [every]
15    way, he shall further pay to Hermas forthwith the damages and the price double and as a penalty another [drachmas of silver]
16    three hundred, and to the public treasury an equal sum, and nonetheless let the sale be valid. Both the parties registered …
17    I, Maron son of Hermas son of Maron, acknowledge that I have sold to Hermas son of Ptolemaios son of Ammonios according to this present agreement, from the present day for all time, the maternal third [part belonging to me and]
18    the purchased ninth part, so as to be a third-and-ninth part of a jointly-owned house and an old courtyard that have collapsed
19    in part, of whatever number are the total measurements and cubit-areas, upon the existing ancient foundations and outer-walls [in their existing]
20    arrangement, of all which the neighbors are: on the south, of Katoites son of
21    Mengcheus and his associates the lots, and for some part [a common]
22    entrance and exit into which a door opens; on the north, a royal street; on the west, of the aforewritten Katoites [son of Mengcheus]
23    and his associates an old house; on the east, of Horos son of Katoites son of Belleus and his brothers a courtyard, after [which an old] house; and I have received the agreed price of the silver, one hundred drachmas in full, all of it, [forthwith]
24    from hand to hand out of the house, and I shall guarantee the sale with every guarantee for all time as aforesaid. Herma[s son of Ptolemaios]
25    son of Ammonios: the sale of the third and ninth part of the old house and courtyard that have collapsed in [part has come to me, as]
26    aforesaid. In the tenth year of Imperator Caesar Vespasian Augustus, in the month Kaisareios, on the 3rd intercalary day. It was registered through
27    the record-office of Bakchias and Hephaistias.
