[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Charition daughter of Herakleides, acting with her son Skylax son of Skylax as guardian, to Onesimos the woodworker, greetings. I have sold to you the various willow trees and the two acacias that I have on embankments of my two reservoirs near Theadelphia, which you are to cut down and carry off before the month of Phamenoth. I have received the price of forty-four silver drachmas and I will guarantee (the sale) with full guarantee. Year ten of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, 29th of the month Hadrianos.
