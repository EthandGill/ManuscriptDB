[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Dioscoros, having served as exegete, councilor
2     of the city of the Arsinoites, superintendent of the sacred [place], to Aurelius Ptollas farmer greetings. I have leased to you the arouras around the village
3     Polydeucia, [with] wheat, two artabas per aroura
4     of the public measurements
5     being toward the account of the gods
