1     Papnouthes guard through the lord Phoibammon of Leontios to the brother Phoibammon of Triadelphus
2     I received from you from the grain-scribe artabas thirty, making 30 artabas, of the produce of the third indiction
3     … for your security I have made pet… as is stated. Epiphi 15 (of the) third indiction. Papnouthes guard [the presented [agrees with me]
4     the order as is stated. Theologius deemed worthy of Mousaios I wrote on behalf [of him] letters not knowing the k[and] body[…]
5     John of Hermeios I witness to the order as is stated
