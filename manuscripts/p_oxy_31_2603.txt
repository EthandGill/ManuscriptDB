[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Sarapion,
2     Paulos, to fare well.
3     For one who has acquired a mirror, or some other such thing
4     holding in his hand, in which faces are reflected,
5     has no need of one who tells him or testifies
6     concerning the appearance set upon him,
7     and his complexion and his form, how it is.
8     For he himself by his own self has become a witness and can speak concerning his own likeness.
9     And it is not when someone tells him or makes clear to him concerning the beauty and the comeliness about him that he then believes. For he is not like the others who are in ignorance
10    and stand far from the mirror that displays the likenesses of all. And the same is true with regard to you also,
11    most beloved. For as through a mirror you have beheld my inborn
12    affection towards you and the love that is ever new.
13    And concerning our acquaintances
14    who are bringing the letter to you,
15    it is not necessary for me to write … to one who knows your friendship towards all
16    and your affection, especially towards our
17    brothers. Receive them therefore in love
18    as friends, for they are not catechumens
19    but belong to the circle of Ision and Nikolaos,
20    and whatever you do for them you have done for me. All the brothers here greet you. Greet also all the
21    brothers with you, both the elect and the catechumens.
22    Farewell; I pray for you.
23    And if it is possible for you to write to the others concerning [these things], do not hesitate, so that they may receive them
24    in each place.
25    Paulos to the brother …ion.
