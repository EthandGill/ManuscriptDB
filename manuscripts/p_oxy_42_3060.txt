[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemaios to Horeites [the] brother, greetings.
2     I received [saddle-gear] from Sarapatos and
3     a rare [composition], a half-worn [garment that had been treated]
4     and an [over-garment], likewise a [half-worn garment], and to my boy
5     a purple, undyed [hood], and [another] emerald-colored tunic, [all] in a new wrapper
6     in a linen napkin and a glass [small basket] and a pottery jar of pickled fish and a bed and three tin [vessels]
7     in a salt-bag …, women's garments, and a white tunic
8     children's garment and a couch and a rod. I did not receive the mina of the lotus-colored carded wool.
9     Send the [Syrian garment] to Tryphonates because he
10    serves me. I have pledged the white garments for the price of grain so that we may eat.
11    I wish you to be well.
12    I have borrowed eight drachmas from Tryphonates.
13    Repay to Horeites from Ptolemais of the sister …
