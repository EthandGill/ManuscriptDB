[TRANSLATION]
1     … [in the vintage/harvest]
2     [of Meso]r[e month] of the present thirteenth
3     indiction of wine, [of the] outflow of the with-God fourteenth distribution, without delay I undertake moreover
4     the beauty/purity of the wine and preservation until [the] entire
5     of the Tybi month; and if there should be found in it sourness
6     that is bad, [it is for] me to exchange these and to provide you with the equivalent
7     in first-quality wine. [Let the document be] valid, [the document] simple
8     written, and having been questioned I acknowledged [this]. The community of the aforementioned
9     names [agrees with us] this document
10    as it is set forth. Papnouthios I wrote on behalf of them
11    [being] absent.
