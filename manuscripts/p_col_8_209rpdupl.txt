[TRANSLATION]
1     To Asklepiades, royal
2     secretary,
3     from Marsisouchos son of Marreis, of those from Theadelphia
4     of the division of Themistos, public cultivator. In the present 33rd year of Caesar I have been ceded by Pnepheros son of
5     Tithoues, public cultivator, the lands which in his
6     name about the village, of public land, 24 arouras, which
7     having both taken over and dyked and partly
8     irrigated for the sowing into the
9     same year, by some
10    pretext of accounts Soterichos
11    secretary of the village-secretary of the village, having sent the collector and the guard into
12    the fields, dragged me up
13    and handed me over into the
14    account of Soterichos, although I simply owed nothing, and I was not released earlier except with a surety. And the
15    same Soterichos carried off a garment of mine and hindered me in matters concerning
16    the sowing of which I
17    cultivate of the public fields,
18    the season pressing, so that no small damage
19    follows upon me. Since therefore Soterichos has of mine in his own keeping a receipt of poll-tax of six years,
20    I request that you order that
21    Soterichos be brought before you, so that
22    he may receive instructions to keep away
23    from me, and be compelled
24    to give back to me what he has of mine,
25    so that none of the public dues fall through.
26    Farewell.
27    Year 33 of Caesar, Phaophi 13.
