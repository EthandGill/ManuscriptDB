[TRANSLATION]
1     Eubulos, son of Ptolemy also called Petesouchos, and Anubuon who is also Nekthnibis, son of Polemon also called Pollulios, the two
2     Persians of later descent, have leased for three years from the thirtieth year, together with Akousilaos and his son Nikaios, from Noumenios, the one presiding over Asclepiadis, the one over the accounts-bureau of the lord
3     and god king, of land arourae twenty or as many as there may be more, in five sealed sections, around Theogonida in the so-called
4     Koile, of revenue of the whole each year of wheat artabae one hundred seventy, together with seed artabae fifteen,
5     according to the measure of great god of the same village Hermes, being established for Tali, accounted as having no accounting and exempt each year of linseed one and a half; and we will replant for linseed the third
6     part each year and we pledge each other
7     for payment and the other things we consent as to what has been written before and we have ordered to the contract-writer and
8     the witnesses and to the one at the registry-office to write. He wrote on behalf of them Ptolemy son of Ptolemy having been deemed worthy, because they claim
9     themselves not to know writing.
10    Akousilaos, son of Lykophron, Persian of later descent, have jointly leased and pledged each other [for] payment as to what has been written before.
11    Nikaios, son of Akousilaos, Persian of later descent, have jointly leased and pledged each other for payment as to what has been written before.
