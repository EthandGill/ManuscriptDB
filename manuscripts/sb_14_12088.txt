1     after the consulship of the Flavii Amantius
2     and Albinus of the most illustrious [ones].
3     To Flavius Nilus, official of the order of the governorship
4     of the Augustanica
5     Aurelius Ammonios, son of Ammonius, from
6     the brilliant and most illustrious [Oxyrhyn]chite city, greetings. I acknowledge to have had
7     from you in use through hand from your house
8     of silver [in] Augustan currency
9     [tale]nts fifty of [principal and] in place of accounting profit
10    [of these] to pay you daily
11    [and] [unceasingly] for service of
12    [your] house vegetables edible
13    … one and a half. But the of principal silver talents fifty necessarily I shall repay to you in [the month] Pauni of the present year 40 [on the] 22nd [day], 13th [indiction] without all postponement and excuse;
14    but if not, I shall pay you of the exceeded time interest the fixed
15    between each other, becoming for you
16    the execution both from me and from the
17    possessions of-me all.
18    valid the chirograph simple
19    having been written, and having been questioned I acknowledged.
20    of the consulship aforesaid, Phamenōth 9.
21    Aurelius Ammonius I have in use the of silver talents fifty and I shall repay with and the
22    daily service as is aforesaid,
23    and having been questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius
24    Satornilus I wrote on behalf of him, saying
25    letters not to know. By me Chaeremōn.
