[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy]
2     Soter, the 29th year, of the epag[omenal days] … in the priesthood [of Ant]i[ochos son of Kebbas, of Ale]xander and the Brother Gods, the basket-bearer [of Arsinoe Phila]delphos being Demodike daughter of Philon. An oath [Apollo]nios son of Parmenion, a Cretan [of the] ep[igone, sub]scribed
3     to Alexandros and Archedemos the prison-keepers …
4     The oath: I swear by King Ptolemy, son of Ptol[e]my and Berenike, and Arsinoe the goddess Philade[lphos]
5     and the Saviour Gods, their parents …
6     (that I will be) manifest to you in the royal prison in Herakleopolis … to you every day
7     until my release takes place by the one who arrested (me)
8     and that I will not withdraw on any pretext whatsoever.
9     And if I do not act according to the things written, I shall pa[y the] … [drachmas,]
10    in respect of which I was arrested, and the damage, and I am liab[le to the]
11    impiety of the oath.
12    In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolema[ios So]ter, the 29th year, of the epagomenal days … in the priesthood of Ant[iochos]
13    son of Kebb[a], of Alexander and the Brother Gods, the basket-be[arer]
14    of Arsinoe [Philadel]phos being Demodike daughter of Philon. The o[ath,]
15    which Apol[lo]nios son of Parmenion[, a Cretan,] sub[scribed]
16    [of the epigone, to Alexandros] and Arch[edemos] …
