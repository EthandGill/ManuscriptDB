[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign and consulship of our most divine master Flavius Justin
2     the eternal Augustus, Emperor, in the fifth year, on the sixteenth of Hathyr, of the third indiction. In the most illustrious city of [Antinoopolis].
3     … a written proclamation of disowning and of renunciation, having my mind unaffected
4     and my thoughts, with right and exact reasoning, apart from all guile and fear and force
5     and necessity and deceit, in a public and official place. And this I send
6     to my father-slaying sons in name and only that, namely to Dionysia and
7     John and Paulina and Andreas, the [rejected ones] …
8     thinking to find you helpers in all things and supporters of my old age and obedient
9     and submissive; but on the contrary you have, when grown up, become my adversaries, as
10    even hateful, since by experience I have known your pitiless father-killing and
11    rejecting purpose, in that I became gravely ill at your hands …
12    … in quality and quantity, from the costly down to a single as
13    and a single obol, except only the falcidian portion fixed by the laws, that is, the [twelfth]
14    share of your disinheritance, and it is no longer permitted to you for the future to name [me]
15    as father, inasmuch as I too have renounced and abhorred you from [now]
16    and for the whole ever-following complete time, as rejected and [bastard]
17    and worse than slaves …
18    … crows'-food to come about, and the gouging out of the eyes in this
19    manner I stamp upon you, to receive nothing nor indeed to give [on behalf of]
20    me, whether living or also dying, because it has been rightly and justly
21    resolved by me. And I adjure every judge and magistrate and every [throne]
22    and office and authority ever to keep what is laid upon such uneducated
23    sons … the renunciation and disowning on my behalf written …
24    … place. And I adjure also at the same time both the public scribe and the tabularius
25    and the public advocate of the illustrious city of the Antinoites,
26    by God before all things and by [the victory and continuance of]
27    our universal masters and kings Flavius [Justin]
28    and Aelia Sophia the eternal Emperors, to add the customary
29    confirmation to the determinations set forth by me …
30    ever to award judgment and to proclaim, everywhere unconquered, for the consternation especially
31    of those about to do the like to these impious ones. For it behooved, according to the divine law, to honor one's own parents in the highest degree. But the contrary
32    these most wicked ones did: they reviled and despised the paternal
33    ordinance, and this I have set out, together with the aforesaid falcidian portion, in my
34    testament, also for the knowledge of all, being valid and secure, [everywhere]
35    [presented] …
