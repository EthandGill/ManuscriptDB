[TRANSLATION]
1     … and to the receivers of registers with him
2     of the accountant,
3     [from] Horion, village-scribe of Dinnis of the Onneitai.
4     Register of the documents which I have filed with you of the account
5     of the present 10th year of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the
6     lord. And it is as follows:
7     Sowing, in a summary account by folio, of the administration
8     of the estate-lands, likewise;
9     the amount collectable of the subject (?)… and of other kinds;
10    the amount collectable of the subject (register) of the royal scribe;
11    the amount collectable of the register of the guards;
12    the amount collectable of the tax on oil-works;
13    the amount collectable of the four-drachma tax and … of the money-taxes on estate-lands;
14    the amount collectable of the rent of unproductive land and of unwatered (land);
15    the amount collectable of the eight-drachma tax of the libation of Dionysus;
16    the amount collectable … of lands sold out of the estate
17    account;
18    the amount collectable of the money-taxes on estate-lands
19    reckoned among rents;
20    the amount collectable of rents … and of the fines;
21    poll-tax, in a summary account;
22    per capita, of the rents … ;
23    the statement of account of the minors;
24    the statement of account of the minor sons of priests;
25    the amount collectable of perquisites;
26    the amount collectable of the third(-tax) of the baths;
27    sowing, in a summary account of the administration.
28    I, Horion, village-scribe, through Philodemus the scribe, have submitted (this).
29    [Year 10] of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the lord.
