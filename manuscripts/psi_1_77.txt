[TRANSLATION]
1     … [met]a the consulship of Flavius Basilius the
2     most brilliant [Me]sore 7 of indiction 14.
3     To Flavius Philoxenus, the most brilliant son of the
4     reverent [one] of memory Ision who became elder of the pious clergy of the brilliant Oxyrhynchite [city], Aurelius Gerontius
5     son of Pamonios from the village of Spania of the Oxyrhynchite province, greetings. Willingly I accept
6     [to] leas[e] for a two-year period from
7     [the so]wing of the blessed with God
8     [first] indiction from the property belonging
9     [to] your brightness [and] situated
10    [near] the land of the same village at Phaei
11    a complete mill called [that] of the
12    mother of Germanos, equipped with all wooden
13    equipment and iron fittings, together with the complete overlying pit and water-supply and
14    well-wheel and garden and mechanical
15    apparatus and all kinds of plants and the
16    land worked jointly with it …
17    being more for sowing whatever I may choo[se]
18    as produce and I shall pay in the name of a fixed rent
19    both on inundated and rainless land each
20    year of the … artabae of wheat from good
21    produce sifted, fifty-five artabae and coins of gold four
22    minus sixteenths by the standard of Spania risk-free,
23    the rent [being] free from all risk, public taxes on the
24    land being [owed] to your brightness the owner
25    … to provide the rent annually
26    the grain at the top of the threshing-floor, but the golden [coins]
27    with correct weight in the month Hathyr of the with God first indiction
28    without delay. Valid is the lease simply written and when asked I acknowledged.
29    Aurelius G[erontius] son of Pamonios the aforementioned
30    I accept [the] leas[e] and all the things written agree with me
31    [as written as] aforesaid. John son of Sarapammon
32    [I wrote for] him being illiterate.
