r.67   διʼ [ἐμοῦ] Ψάτε Πισραὴλ ἐγράφη

[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity, of the Father
2     and the Son and the Holy Spirit. In the month Pauni 10, of the indiction
3     the seventh, at its beginning. In the fortress of Memnonia of the nome of the city of the Hermonthites.
4     We, Stephanos and Chareb and Abigaia — Samuel son of the blessed
5     Chareb our father consenting with us, our mother being Tshenoute —
6     Daniel son of the komes my husband consenting with me, I Abigaia,
7     concerning every matter written in this written deed of confirmation, of release, of freedom-from-care, of the man of the fortress of Djeme of the nome
8     of the city of Hermonthis, giving afterward (the subscription) of the subscriber who shall subscribe,
9     and beseeching some trustworthy witnesses to bear witness on their behalf
10    according to our own request, with our readiness; and moreover
11    establishing it through some trustworthy witnesses, those who shall bear witness
12    to it on our behalf, according to our own request — we write to Elisabet
13    daughter of the blessed Epiphaneios, her mother being Maria, and Abraham her husband,
14    he too being the son of Theodoros, in this same fortress, greeting.
15    Since, in this sixth year that has passed, the most honored Athanasios
16    son of the blessed Georgios, and Biktor son of the blessed
17    Ioseph, the lashanes of the fortress, brought suit against you concerning this inheritance of the blessed Epiphaneios and the blessed Maria, our fathers,
18    whose names we have named above, and we took counsel with one another
19    before Psate son of the blessed Israel, the man of this same fortress;
20    and after we took counsel, the most honored Athanasios and Biktor, the lashanes,
21    came to terms with one another concerning this inheritance of the blessed ones,
22    whose names we have named above, and we drew up a written deed of settlement
23    for one another; and you came to terms concerning every kind, from great to small,
24    down to a kind of worthless and very small thing and a strip of measured land; and
25    we drew up a settlement for one another before the notary of the fortress, according to the law of the fortress, that we should not bring suit against one another over any matter pertaining to this inheritance of the blessed Epiphaneios and the blessed
26    Maria. And in this seventh year, which is upon us now, you brought suit, saying:
27    "Do not destroy the oath toward you, but you should be willing to satisfy
28    us by the oath, with your wife who is at the boundary." After we came,
29    therefore, out to you concerning this matter of this oath of this kind, we resumed the case before the most honored Athanasios and Biktor, the lashanes,
30    with some other great men. You were willing to satisfy us by the oath
31    according to the way we said, without any hindrance, with great joy,
32    without any blame; and when you were willing, therefore, to persuade us
33    in this, according to the way we said above, and when we saw that you
34    were willing to render the oath to us according to the way we said,
35    after you, therefore, went into (the church of) the holy Apa Biktor and began to swear, some great men came into our midst, with one another,
36    they persuaded us with one another, we set aside the oath for you,
37    we besought you, you gave a share of one gold solidus
38    to us, with a tremissis — making half a gold solidus, a third part — from hand to hand; after you rendered to us this half-solidus, a third part, justly, you sought to receive from us
39    this written deed of freedom-from-care. We came, therefore, to it, willing and persuaded,
40    without any deceit or fear or violence or deception or any circumvention
41    and aggrandizement, no necessity being laid upon us, but rather, swearing by God the Almighty and the safety of our God-given kings who hold sway over us
42    through God, that we received and were fully satisfied at your hands regarding the oath, according to the power
43    of the boundary. Yourselves (being) Elisabet and Abraham your husband, and we agree
44    not to come out against you henceforth concerning any matter pertaining to this inheritance of the blessed Epiphaneios and Maria, regarding anything …
45    … nor those who shall come after you, ever, at any time; neither we,
46    Stephanos and Chareb and Abigaia, nor I Samuel their father, with
47    Daniel, nor any man who is on (our) behalf. But if anyone, moreover,
48    shall dare on (our) behalf, nor any child nor any heir — firstly, indeed,
49    that that one shall gain profit from nothing, but the first type, indeed, he shall become
50    estranged from the holy oath which is sworn to it, the Holy Trinity,
51    and he shall give the account of the fine of twenty-four gold solidi of pure gold,
52    making 24 gold solidi; and after the fine we confirm the force of this written deed of freedom-from-care,
53    this which we have drawn up for your assurance, it being settled, being lordly in every place where it shall be produced.
54    They read it to us, we heard it, it pleased us, we besought a subscriber,
55    he subscribed to it on our behalf, we cast witnesses upon it, we released it, it being completed,
56    before the notary, according to all the proper procedure.
57    Samuel our father … consenting with us, those who wrote above, we agree to this freedom-from-care, the type of settlement, as is stated above. I,
58    Abraham son of the blessed David, I wrote on their behalf, with my own hand, according to
59    their request.
60    I, Biktor the lashane, son of the blessed Ioseph, give witness.
61    I, Psate, wrote on his behalf.
62    Stephanos son of the blessed Moyses gives witness. I, Psate, wrote on his behalf.
63    I, Antonios son of the blessed Paulos, give witness. I, Kyriakos son of the blessed Andreas, give witness.
64    I, Shenoute, this least presbyter of the holy church of Djeme, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know how to write.
65    I, Hamos son of the blessed Petros, give witness.
66    I, Pshere, this least deacon of the Apostles, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know how to write. Athanasios son of the blessed Georgios gives witness.
67    Through me, Psate son of Israel, it was written.
