[TRANSLATION]
1     23 Mesore; to Apollonios and Ptolemaeus, hides-collectors
2     [hides] going into the construction
3     of arms of the invincible armies: Aurelius Zosimos
4     before the divine gift, named Zosimos son of Leonides, for the price of hide, [at]
5     [the] account [of] eight drachmas, [which] total drachmas 8.
