[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Iulius Sabinus and his colleagues, former supervisors of the Sacred Gate of Syene through Palachemis, the assistant. Petorzmethis, son of Patsebtis alias Kasios, whose mother is Thinmeaus, paid for poll-tax of the 19th year of Hadrianus Caesar, the lord: gross four drachmas, 3½ obols, makes 4 drachmas, 3½ obols. The 21st year of Hadrianus Caesar, the lord, on the 4th of Phamenoth, through his brother Kasios.
