[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius John, with God, ruler and pagarch of the Hermopolite, to the most admirable Zacharias, [assistant] of the village of Sinalabeh.
2     You have given
3     from the benevolence of the same village of the fourteenth indiction, [of] gold, nomismata five and karatia six; comes [to be] nomismata 5, karatia 6 only. [It] was written
4     Epeiph 4, indiction 14. Comes [to be] nomismata five, karatia six. Flavius John … through me, Phoibammon, the notary.
5     Order of benevolence of the assistant, of nomismata 5, karatia 6.
