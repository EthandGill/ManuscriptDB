[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year] of the fourteenth Auto[crat] Caesar of Trajan
2     Hadrian Augustus Pachōn … in Tebtunis of Polemon's
3     division of the Arsinō[i]te nome. There agrees [Isi]dōra
4     [daughter] of Athēniōn, son of Harphaēsēs, from the aforementioned village
5     Tebtunis, Persian as [being] about forty-eight years [old]
6     … both, with [her] guardian, [the] relative Hermēn [son] of Arētiōnos as [being] about forty
7     four, scar on the left thumb to Herakleidēs, [son] of Didymos, the younger, son of Hermōdos, [one] of the [former] gymnasiarchs as [being] about forty years [old], scars on both eyebrows
8     to have from him Herakleidēs, on the spot by hand
9     [from the] house [use of] silver principal drachmae six-thousand two-hundred, [being] [one] talent [and] drachmae two-hundred [for] three [months] from the present month Pachōn
10    interest of them [at] the [mina] per month of one drachme,
11    of which she shall make [the rep]ay[ment] [Isidō]ra to Herakleidēs
12    of the aforementioned principal drachmae six-thousand two-hundred and the interest in month Epiphi of the present fourteenth year without delay,
13    with recovery being to Herakleidēs from both Isidōra and from all her property as if from a judgment. Signatory of Isidōra, Hermēn the aforementioned
14    Isidōra [daughter] of Athēniōn, son of Harphaēsēs, with [my] guardian [of the] relative Hermēn, son of Arētiōnos, I agree [that I] have from Heraklēidēs the principal silver use drachmae six-thousand two-hundred, interest at the mina per month of one drachme, which I shall also repay in month
15    Epiphi of the present year as aforesaid.
16    Hermēn wrote and on behalf of her, [she being] illiterate.
17    … nomographer of Tebtunis through Areios [I] have been witnessed
