[TRANSLATION]
1     … k … ail …
2     Aurelii Mathias son of Ponnis and Ibeis son of Apollos,
3     both from the village of Aphrodite in the nome of Antaiopolis,
4     to Aurelius Besarion son of Dioscorus from the same village, greeting.
5     We acknowledge, on the basis of mutual surety, that we have leased from you,
6     for the present year only, of the crops of the eleventh indiction, with God's help,
7     the farm-plot leased out by you to the holy New
8     Church, of however many arouras it is, lying in the plain
9     of the same village of Aphrodite, in the holding of Hieras, with cistern and
10    reservoir and plants and palm-trees and every right, according to the boundaries,
11    on condition that we cultivate it and perform all the agricultural work
12    from our own expenses and animals,
13    not contemptuously and [without] negligence, and we shall enclose (?) the whole;
14    and the sowing-seed being given by you, you also take from the common (produce)
15    …; and the sowing-seed of the hay being given by us,
16    we also take from the common (produce); and when the season comes, the surplus of all kinds of produce and chaff shall, for us and you,
17    be divided in half: to us for the labours,
18    and to you for the rents; and you are to have from the common (produce),
19    before the division of the hay, for all the cuttings, half an aroura;
20    we shall provide you fifty dried, good, large, estate cheeses
21    and six measures of mustard; and we provide
22    from the common (fund) the price of the pens for the flocks
23    without dispute. The lease is valid and secure, and, having been asked the formal question, we acknowledged; and of the wage of the wooden
24    machine and the expenses, given from the common (fund); and we shall do the transport of the produce of your estate-share and the chaff up to the village
25    and the threshing-floor, from your threshing-floor. I, Aurelius Math[ei]as
26    son of Ponnis, the aforesaid, have leased as aforesaid. I, Aurelius …bais son of Apollos, the aforesaid, have leased as
27    afore[said]. I, A[ur]el[ius Phoi]bammon …
28    … son of Apollos …
