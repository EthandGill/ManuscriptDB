[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 16, donkeys provide-fodder, 3
2     15th loads 12; 16th loads 3; 17th loads 16, total 31.
3     Into the Ammonion fodder has been brought: 23rd loads 12; 24th 3;
4     25th loads 9; 26th loads 9; 27th loads 6;
5     28th loads 11; 29th 12; 30th loads 13, total loads 75
6     total fodder loads 106,
7     total our donkeys which have brought fodder, loads 33.
8     Harpichysis has of the fodder
9     for the binding, loads 108, at drachmas 2 [and] three-obols [each],
10    but there are not other loads 106,
11    he has drachmas 270.
