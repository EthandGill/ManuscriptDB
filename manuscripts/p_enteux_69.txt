[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greetings, Hediste of Nikànor, Maketa. I am being wronged by a certain Demetrius, a doctor,
2     of those from Karanidòs. For inasmuch as I have a certain part of bare land from the north of the properties
3     that have been built by purchase in the aforementioned village, the aforementioned, proceeding toward the
4     place, forces me bringing brick and digging a foundation so as to build. I therefore beseech you, King,
5     to order Diophanes [the] strategos to write to Lysimachos the epistates to send him to Diophanes
6     so that, [when he has examined] it, he may not permit him to proceed with things not befitting him, and until
7     [the case is] decided on the merits, not to permit anyone to [build]. For if this happens, having fled [to you], King, I [shall obtain] all assistance. [Fare well].
8     To Lysimachos. By all means settle them; but if not, send them to us so that they may be judged in the proper court.
9     Year 4, Daisios 27, Athur 29.
10    Year 4, Daisios 27, Athur 29.
11    Hediste of Nikànor, of Maketa, to
12    Demetrius, concerning land.
