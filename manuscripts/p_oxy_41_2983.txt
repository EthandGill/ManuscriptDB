[TRANSLATION]
1     Harpalos to Heras his brother,
2     greeting.
3     I wrote … through Herakleid[es] …
4     the matters of your administration. I bound together
5     my letter and another from the legal expert here to the
6     legal expert with you. I received from Ammon[io]s the things sent [to me. And I s]ent you through him a Knidian jar of good fish-sauce,
7     he having received from me on account of freight
8     and carriage 6 drachmas. The little letter,
9     the one about the consignment, the one forwarded to you from Alexandria, send to me by a reliable person.
10    I received from Herakleides a flask of wine. And do not be burdened; for whatever
11    need I have, I will write to you. It was written to me
12    from the Oasis by Leonides, saying that the friend
13    had transferred to him the price of the hides. Inquire from your end how much was transferred to him on my account. And you will sa[y]
14    to Patr… that Herakleides owes me [no]thing
15    on account of the Great Oasis,
16    and it was not necessary to detain the camel.
17    For I, restraining myself, until
18    now have done nothing, and until now
19    let it be. For the 1000 drachmas which Herakleides transferred to his own people in the Great
20    Oasis, these he had in the Oxyrhynchite nome
21    and transferred to me in the same Oxyrhynchite nome, owed to me by him.
22    In vain, then, did they detain the camel.
23    You shall communicate everything to him. Concerning whatever you wish, write to me without hesitation.
24    [I pray] that [you] be in good health.
25    I pray.
26    To Heras from Harpalos his brother.
