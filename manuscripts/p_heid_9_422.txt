[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dionysios, royal
2     scribe,
3     from Pachas,
4     royal farmer
5     from the village of Ti[nteris],
6     farming a [portion]
7     of Eu…i… of the cleruchy …
8     concerning Pôis the [Great].
9     When I had gone down,
10    write to Kydias so that he may give orders
11    concerning the portion which I farm
12    to [bring (him) before the court],
13    out of the aforewritten (land),
14    Pharmouthi 20…,
15    of the cleruchy on the 25th of
16    Pharmouthi of the 23rd year
17    I find that the sheep of Seleukos,
18    the chief of police, from
19    Tinteris
20    are grazing in it,
21    onto 16 arouras of grass …, which they had also previously grazed over during the winter.
22    Whence, when I had taken along
23    Psenobastis and Erasythis,
24    policemen of the same (village),
25    wishing to hand over
26    to them the sheep,
27    Seleukos and his
28    son Ptolemaios and
29    his son-in-law Herieus, of the men with
30    Asphês, having drawn
31    a sword, struck
32    us until we
33    rushed off into flight. I therefore ask you
34    to make the fitting
35    reckoning.
36    Farewell.
37    To Kydias, strategos.
38    Of the memorandum given to us by
39    Pachas, royal farmer of those from the village
40    of Tinteris, concerning which he alleges
41    that acts of violence have been committed by Seleukos
42    and the others named,
43    the copy is appended below. If then it seem good, give orders
44    to bring him (before the court).
45    Year 23, Pharmouthi 27, memorandum of Machatas, royal farmer,
46    concerning 16 arouras grazed over
47    by the sheep of Seleukos.
