[TRANSLATION]
1     Karourus and Petechespokhrates and associates, supervisors of properties
2     of which produce is registered, of the metropolis, [to the] heirs of Papystios [son of] Pamonth.
3     We have received as addition for the price of wine, produce of the 7th year
4     dirty drachmas eight and three-obols, becoming drachmas 8 and three-obols. Year 8
5     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Athyr 21.
6     Karourus I have signed.
