[TRANSLATION]
1     … brother … [concerning] Isaac
2     … property of Akte and to him surpassing
3     … land [lease] there paying out
4     … [from] new moon of Thoth month of the entering
5     [year] … [tenth] indiction and the supervision
6     … with livestock from [your] excellence
7     … to all [the things] that pertain; whom also we will [present] … [sought] for by your splendidness;
8     and if this [one] sought we do not present, necessarily us
9     the sureties liable to be to all those towards him sought to answer. Valid the surety simple written and asked we acknowledged. Aurelii Isaac son of Koproueus and Joseph same-born
10    bro[ther] and Daniel son of Horus the aforesaid we made the surety and we swore the divine oath
11    and agrees with us all the [things] written as aforesaid. Flavius Sarapion of Horion deemed worthy wrote on their behalf
12    [in the presence of] those not knowing letters.
