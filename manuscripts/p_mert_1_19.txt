[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios [son] of Sarapion, of the mother of Harencotus
2     Tanechis from Oxyrhynchus city to Valerius Diodorus having become the hypomnematographus from the Museum, and as
3     you are known - greetings. I have sold you a paktona, river vessel, of willow wood, of length twelve and a half cubits, with rudder and oars of willow wood
4     of wood two, and there I received from you the agreed upon sums for the price in silver, drachmas [two hundred in] full, and I will guarantee as aforementioned.
5     Year 13 of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the lord,
6     Pharmouthi 5. Apollonios the aforementioned received the agreed upon sums for
7     the price of the [paktona], drachmas two hundred
8     and [I will] guarantee as [is set forth]. [Time] the same.
