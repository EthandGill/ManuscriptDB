1     In the consulship of our lord Flavius Justinus
2     the eternal Augustus for the 1st time, Pauni 20, of the 12th indiction.
3     Aurelius Isak, son of Phoibammon, his mother being
4     Eirene, residing here in the illustrious
5     city of the Oxyrhynchites, taskmaster of the estates
6     of Neophytos and Anousia, to Aurelius Menas, son,
7     from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
8     administrator of the said estates,
9     greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from you
10    and have been paid in full my wages
11    for the service which I perform as
12    taskmaster, namely … two solidi
13    also for [the present] twelfth indiction,
14    [that is, in gold] two [solidi], making in gold [solidi] 2;
15    and as proof of the payment made to me
16    by you of the same
17    two solidi of my wages
18    of the same twelfth indiction,
19    I have made this receipt, which,
20    being valid, [in a single copy] written and, when questioned, I acknowledged (it).
21    I, Aurelius Isak, son of Phoibammon, the aforewritten,
22    have received and have been paid in full my wages of the two
23    solidi of the twelfth indiction, and everything agrees with me
24    as set out above. I, Aurelius Serenus son of Apphous, being requested, wrote on behalf
