[TRANSLATION]
1     To Julius Ammonios also called Euangelos,
2     strategos of the Thinite,
3     from Aurelia Senpatous daughter of Panouris son of Titoes, from This. About evening of the past day, a crowd
4     of malefactors came upon my house
5     in the village of This and struck
6     my husband Titoes son of Kortas
7     the cobbler, on the left shoulder
8     [a]nd the left hand, with swords,
9     and my son Psekes also, and
10    struck (him) on the head, and
11    whatever they found in the house they carried off, having broken through all
12    the doors. The malefactors therefore being unknown
13    to me, I submit these documents, requesting
14    that you assign an attendant who shall look into the condition concerning them
15    so that they may be able to obtain the needed treatment
16    to obtain.
17    Year 3 of Imperator Caesar Marcus
18    Julius Philippus Pius
19    Fortunate, and of Marcus Julius
20    Philippus most noble
21    and most illustrious Caesar,
22    Augusti, Mecheir 15.
23    I, Aurelia Senpatous daughter of Panour-
24    son of Titoes, have submitted (this).
25    I wrote on her behalf, Aurelius Soter
26    son of Soter, she not knowing
27    letters.
28    Sarapion, attendant, was assigned.
29    A copy of the document of Senpatous is sent to you, so that having taken along with (you)
30    a public physician and having inspected
31    the condition concerning her husband
32    and her son, you may report in writing.
33    [Year 4] Mech[ei]r 15.
