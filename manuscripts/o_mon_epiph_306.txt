1     Behold, thus I speak of the least, I, David, presbyter
2     Who writes and is eager
3     To his beloved father, Apa
4     Isaac and those with him
5     All, the man who bears
6     Christ in a name, having made [the] love
7     Come down, [so that] you might take [the] bond
8     And truly I heard that you came
9     … in the heart, having made the love perfect, coming to me, I did
