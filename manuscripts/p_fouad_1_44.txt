[TRANSLATION]
1     … to the archidikastes and (the official) in charge of the supervision of the chrematistai
2     [and of the other] tribunals,
3     from Didyme daughter of Dionysios, of those from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
4     with her guardian Epimachos son of Lucius, of those from the same city of the Oxyrhynchites, and from Lucius Pompeius, son of Lucius, of the tribe
5     [P]ollia, of the discharged soldiers from the second
6     and twentieth le[gio]n. We agree with one another on these terms, that … of Didyme a loan
7     … [of silver] … [of the] Augustan and Ptolemaic (coinage), drachmas …
8     … him, that instead of the interest of these
9     [he shall allow Didym]e and those of her party to dwell in the part of the paternal house falling [to Lucius], which is on the (street?) of the
10    [Sarapeion in the city of the Oxyrhynchites], in the quarter of Hermaion, for a period of years
11    … [from the month] … [Se]bastos of the 5th year of Tiberius Claudius
12    [Caesar Augustus] Germanicus Imperator, the public (dues) of the house, according to the part falling to Lucius, being
13    [against Didyme; and that it shall not be permitted] to Lucius to evict
14    [Didyme and those of her party] within the period; and after
15    [this Lucius shall give back] to Didyme the loan without interest;
16    [b]ut if in any respect he transgresses, that he shall pay forthwith the
17    loan, the 400 [drachmas] of [silver] together with the half-as-much-again and interest of a drachma
18    [for] each mina [per] month, and a penalty of 100 drachmas of silver, the exaction
19    being both from him and from all his property
20    [as] from a legal judgment. And if, when the period has come, Didyme does not recover the aforesaid principal, that she shall hold and own the indicated part of the house and administer it as she may choose, without notification and summons, no claim being left thereafter to Lucius,
21    the risk concerning the house not being against Didyme
22    nor the creditor. Neighbors … of the whole house: on the south,
23    Amois; on the north, Ischyras; on the west, a public street; on the east, a street in which
24    is the temple of Hermaion. We request (this).
25    Year 4 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
26    [Imperat]or, in the month Kaisareios, the 5th of the intercalary days.
