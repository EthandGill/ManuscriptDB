[TRANSLATION]
1     Peteroueris and Pamonthes, collectors of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis, (whose) mother is Senchesthotis.
3     We have received for watchtower and other (taxes) of year 8, base drachmas five, 1 obol,
4     total drachmas 5, 1 obol. Year 8 of Antoninus Caesar
5     the lord, (month) Hadrianos 10. (2nd hand) I, Pao( ), have signed.
