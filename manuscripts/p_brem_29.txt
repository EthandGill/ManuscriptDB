1     … …
2     from Pathotes son of Ares and Pathotes son of Tithoesais
3     and Phatres son of Phatres and Pollous son of Psais and Psensenpententaios his brother and Patomtous son of Orsenouphis and
4     Hermias son of Pathotes and Patomtous son of Enthieus and Psentapnychis the elder son of Orsenouphis and Apollonios son of Portious and
5     Psais son of Onnophris, overseers of the flooding (irrigation) of Ibion. We swear by the fortune of Emperor Caesar
6     Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus
7     that no aroura shall go unirrigated in
8     our village-secretaryship, except fifty-five arouras in the place toward the north, of which
9     those that can be watered we shall compel to be watered; or may we be liable to the oath.
