1     Year 843
2     In the consulship of Fl[a]v[ius Pa]ulus and Moschianus, Thoth …, [sixth] indiction.
3     To Taurinus, [the] most [pious] presbyter of the catholic church of Hermoupolis, the most illustrious,
4     son of [the] [blessed memory] Jo[ha]nnes from Apollos Isaios, mother Ione, vineyard-worker
5     from the village [Temen]kyr[k]es [of] the Hermoupolitan [nom]arch, and Apollos Isidoros, mother
6     Irene from the [same] village, and [he] a vineyard-[worker]. We declare mutually liable for repayment
7     and mutual sureties willingly and voluntarily to have leased from your
8     piety for a two-year period, reckoned from the harvest of the fortunate seventh indiction,
9     the vineyard land existing to [her], planted with living plants in the composition of arouras, as many as there are,
10    together with all plants [and] date-palms, situated in the plain of our same village
11    for our vineyard work and all care and beauty. We shall dig
12    and the same land fifth every year, on the condition that your piety shall receive half the share
13    of all [wine] that will go to pressing and all kinds of plants and date-palms and we together with
14    water-supplier the other half share, in return for which we do labors, and your [piety shall]
15    piety yearly no less than by reckoning [as] first-pressing from the common jar of wine
16    eighty knidias. But we shall provide yearly in the time of the vintage a small tripod worth
17    silver talents six thousand and a small platter full of various foods worth of silver
18    talents four thousand, that is of the fruits of the date-palms being packed, provided by us to your piety for the transport-tax of them yearly a third of golden currency according to your exchange.
