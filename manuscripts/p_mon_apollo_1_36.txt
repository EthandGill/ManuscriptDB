[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Victor, the son of Mathias, the man who is in the village of Shemoun, I greet Abba Enoch
2     the monk of the [place] of Abba Apollo, saying: I am indebted to you [for] eight [xestai] of oil of your fields; these [are] ready; I have sent them to you in the produce of the twelfth indiction
3     without ambiguity. I, Victor, the son of Mathias, [I agree to] sign the receipt
4     I, Proou, the son of Papyre, I have written [this]. I, Serene, the son of Victor, and Jacob
5     the son of Keworke, [I attest] the receipt. I, Paul, the son of Joseph, I greet [you]
6     It was written in the month of Choiach, 15th, 12th indiction.
7     [A] receipt [was] made [of]: Victor, Mathias, of the village of Isou, of oil, 30 xestai, [delivered]
