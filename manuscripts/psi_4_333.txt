[TRANSLATION]
1     Promethion to Zenon greetings. [I]t befell us to be distressed, having heard [that] for a considerable time you were dragged along in sickness; now hearing [that] you are well and already recovering, we rejoiced;
2     and we too are well. We gave previously to Herakleides the
3     from you from your account, [just as you] wrote to us, silver drachmas 150,
4     and he brings now myrrh units 10 in [ala]baster jars 20, which are sealed
5     with my ring. For Apollonius ordered me, [having] purchased
6     to give to him also garlands of pomegranate-blossom. We did not have time
7     then to deliver these [to] him [because] they were not ready. But to
8     Naukratis they will be brought to him; [since before the] 30th they will be completed.
9     And the price of these and of the myrrh from your account
10    we have charged, just as Apollonius wrote. And we have given
11    also for the small ship in which he sails the fare in bronze drachmas 10. It has been charged
12    also to Iatrokles for papyri being completed at Tanis so
13    [it will be] to Apollonius silver drachmas 400. These things then let (you) know thus
14    managed. And you would do well to write to (us), if you have need of any of
15    the things concerning us.
16    Farewell. Year 29, Choiak 28.
17    Year 29, Peritios 3.
18    Promethion of which
19    has given.
20    To Zenon.
