# HGV-EN: (m. 3) 62 (m. 1) To [Harpokratio]n alias Hierax, [royal scribe] of the [Arsinoite nome], Herakleides division, [from] Ho[rige]nes, son of Isid[o]ro[s of those from] the metropolis, registered [in the quarter M]eron The[.]mi . There belongs to me in the quarter of the Bithynians of Ision two thirds of a house and courtyard, in which I register for the house-by-house registration [of the past year 28] in the same Gymnasium quarter, in which I registered in the registration of year 14 as well. And there is Melas, son of Hera[kl]as, the son of Zenas, mother NN workman subject to poll-tax, [.]8 years old. (m. 2) I, Horigenes, have submitted (this registration) (m. 1) Year 29 of Aurelius Commodus [Antoninu]s Caesar, the lord, Mesore intercalary day 5.
