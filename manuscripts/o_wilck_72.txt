[TRANSLATION]
1   Petroszmethis son of Phenophis, Thrakidas, paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the seventh year
3   of Trajan Caesar the
4   lord, on account eight drachmas — total 8 dr., Mesore 30.
5   The same, the remaining nine drachmas — total 9 dr.,
6   and for the statue, three obols. I, Hermogenes, the collector,
7   wrote (this).
