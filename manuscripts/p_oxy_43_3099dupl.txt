[TRANSLATION]
1     [illustrious (city)]. Having already become, with respect to the
2     present 5th year, of [fifty]-
3     eight years, and having, according to the qualifications approved by the most estimable
4     Petronianus, the age-status of those maintained (on the grain-dole), I declare that I am
5     registered in the quarter of the Dromos
6     of the Gymnasium and that I was scrutinized at the (census) of the
7     24th year of the deified Severus Antoninus,
8     by the house-by-house census-return, in the quarter
9     of the same (name), being 48 years (then); of which scrutiny the relevant portion,
10    having extracted (it) from the local
11    public library,
12    I have subjoined, and I request that I be enrolled
13    among those who are to be maintained (on the dole).
14    And I swear by the Fortune of Marcus Aurelius
15    Severus Alexander Caesar
16    the lord, that I am of
17    the aforesaid 58 years, and that I have
18    in no way given false witness. Year 5 of the Imperator
19    Caesar Marcus Aurelius
20    Severus Alexander Pius
21    Felix Augustus, Choiak.
