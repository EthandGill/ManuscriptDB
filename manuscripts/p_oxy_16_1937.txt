[TRANSLATION]
1     I received the things written by your genuine brotherhood, and before
2     it [goes] up tomorrow prepare the [ones] from Terythae to give to the [ones] from
3     Leonidas concerning Tampetus …
4     … bring the same greater ones or prepare
5     them to return the foreign farmers. [rather] they go up
6     and the greater [ones] of Pheretnoue so that we might know the former [state] of the property
7     of Hierax. But make haste through tomorrow's reconciliation.
8     To my master, [most] illustrious, worthy of honor, genuine brother George, count and [administrator], Victor with god, counterlandholder.
