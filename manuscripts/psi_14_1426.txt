[TRANSLATION]
1     to the brother Alexander [from] Gerontius. according to the order
2     that was ordered by me of the two …, two from each
3     of three months, and of the other … purple, for which
4     and you yourself received for one gold coin the wheat, or the orders
5     send, that is to say for the same gold coin wish to take purple
6     good; but if you do not wish to be useful to me, that is to say
7     the same gold coin wish to send to me with the
8     letter-carrier or Maxinus the baker or of [this] boy Chrysaphius. … …
