[TRANSLATION]
1     To Achaeus.
2     From Herakleides son of Pammenes and Taurinos son of Dorion, and from Nilos son of Nilos.
3     Concerning the matters in dispute, Nilos agrees
4     that henceforth the agreement is void which they have referred
5     to him, both Herakleides and Taurinos, the settlement through the same tribunal in Pachon
6     of the present 12th year of Caesar, concerning the instruction … of Nilos, the son of Herakleides, Hermaiskos, who is not yet
7     of age, in the nail-smith's craft;
8     and Nilos … to have received from
9     Herakleides and Taurinos by hand
10    out of the house the sums which they advanced to them according to
11    the same agreement, 100 silver drachmas, still in …
12    … the interest, and that no claim against Herakleides and Taurinos be left
13    to Nilos nor to any other on his behalf
14    concerning these matters, but that it be permitted to Herakleides
15    also to put out his own son Hermaiskos to learn the nail-smith's
16    craft with another, and apart from the agreed terms being
17    valid … those agreed upon … (illegible) …
18    and the appointed penalty being unhindered; and the settlement which the one
19    of them, Taurinos, has referred against the aforewritten Nilos through the record-office,
20    the agreement in Epeiph of the same
21    12th year of Caesar concerning the advanced loan
22    of 700 silver drachmas and interest, shall remain valid
23    for Nilos, he being in no way diminished. We request (this).
24    Year 12 of Caesar … …
