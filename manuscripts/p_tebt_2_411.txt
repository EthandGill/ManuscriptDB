[TRANSLATION]
1   Pauleinos to Heron his
2   son, greeting.
3   The moment you receive my
4   letter, that very hour
5   come up, for his excellency the
6   epistrategos has been asking for
7   you insistently. He was even ready
8   to post a proclamation, had I not
9   promised that today
10   you would present yourself. So do not
11   do otherwise — yet do not
12   be at all alarmed:
13   when you arrive you will know
14   what it is about.
15   I pray for your health.
16   Pach(on) 3.
