1     After the consulship of Flavius Merobaudes the second and Saturninus, the most illustrious,
2     Phaophi 26.
3     To Aurelius Sarmates, son of Ptolemy,
4     serving as magistrate of the splendid and most illustrious Oxyrhynchite
5     city:
6     from [Aurelius] Phobammon, [of Peh]out, from the settlement of Konkon in the first district.
7     [Voluntarily] I agree to lease
8     [for] the sole year of the fortunate 14th indiction
9     [from] of the properties existing to you concerning the village
