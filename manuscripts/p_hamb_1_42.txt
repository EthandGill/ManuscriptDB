[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 24 of Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus
2     Parthicus Maximus, Britannicus Maximus
3     Germanicus Maximus, Pious Augu[stus]. [He recorded]
4     Lucius Julius Serenus from the ten-man rulers for the reckoning
5     of the month Tybi on behalf of the lawful [tax] on sheep of year 22 through Gaius
6     of Aurelius Melanos, secretary of silver tax-collectors of the village
7     of Karanidos, of silver eighty drachmae
8     total eighty drachmae, which I will also record against the public bank and bring to you a public receipt.
