[TRANSLATION]
1     [In] the name of God. [Korra son of Szerich, the cou]n[sellor,]
2     to Basileios, admini[st]r[ator of the village of A]ph[rodito. A certain amount of thorn-wood]
3     having been assigned to us through your administration,
4     … l … kt … [to the account] of the embellishment and equipping
5     of skiffs and small boats and runner-ships of the fleet,
6     of the ninth indiction, having sent to you also their
7     receipts twenty days ago — and up to this point you have not sent
8     anything of these; and we, having already instructed the overseer
9     of the work of these same skiffs to begin upon the building
10    and embellishment of them through the same craftsmen,
11    … e … sdan, we assigned, by the command of God,
12    t[he subjoi]n[ed as]signment of the same craftsmen.
13    Receiving, th[en,] the present letter, send at once
14    and [im]mediately whatever there is of such kinds (of goods) through
15    your administration, dispatching the first thing first,
16    before everything the thorn-wood; if however you have not yet
17    sent these, do not send anything of them
18    beyond the measure made clear to you in our former
19    letters. And you shall be aware that if
20    we be compelled to write to you another letter
21    after the present letter, because of your having delayed and not
22    sent such thorn-wood, there is going to overtake you, together with
23    those same letters, a requital
24    [b]ound to afflict your soul. Furthermore,
25    do not give against yourself any occasion whatsoever
26    and neglect to send the said
27    thorn-wood, and not only that but the rest of the kinds (of goods) too. Written in the month of Thoth 22, of the 8th indiction.
28    Account of the village of Aphrodito.
29    Thus:
30    price of thorn-wood, 1, for the account of the skiffs, 1 nomisma;
31    price of goods of the Klysma, 10 nomismata;
32    sibenine anchors 8, nomismata 9⅓; one sibenine cable, ½ of a nomisma;
33    [price of the dap]anē (provisioning) of the Klysma and the boats of the children of the Amir al-Mu'minin, 1½ nomismata, 8;
34    of loaves (?), 8 artabas, 1 nomisma; of pulse, 1⅓ artabas, of a nomisma 8;
35    of olive-oil, 8 xestai, of a nomisma 3; of vinegar, 8 xestai, of a nomisma 12;
36    of salt-fish, 2 kollatha, of a nomisma 12;
37    for the cash-conversion of milk (and) butter, of a nomisma 6;
38    price of goat-skins, m … m, for the account of … i … m of Abdella son of Abdelmelech, 1½ nomismata, 4;
39    price of split date-palm beams, 32, for the account of the court of the Amir al-Mu'minin by the river, 8 nomismata;
40    wage of 1 sailor of the African fleet, of the one advancing, 12 nomismata, 3, 12;
41    comes to: nomismata 34½, 3, 8.
42    And if anything be left over from the expenditures of the fleet of the 8th indiction, send (it) in cash-conversion against the valuation of the receipts.
43    … [ Ko]rra son of Szer[i]ch [the counsellor],
44    [to Basileios administrator of the village of Aphrodito] Phaophi 21, of the 8th indiction. Brought [through ] …
