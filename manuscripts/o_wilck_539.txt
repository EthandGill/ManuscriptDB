1     Heriophmoeis and partners tax-collectors of Charakos
2     Pachnumius [son] of Paouphtheious. We have received for
3     dike-tax and bath-tax of Charakos year 13 foul drachmae seven five-obol coppers 2.
4     Year 13 of Hadrian Caesar the lord
5     Epeiph 23.
6     but Epiphi 10 of the name of the same Pachnumius
7     I received for dike-tax and bath-tax of Charakos year 14
8     foul drachmae se[ven five-obol coppers 2 ][Pete]chespocrates I have noted.
