[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius from the proktatores
2     to Pamintias son of Ptolemy and
3     Kurbas son of Nicaeus from the Isis mountain
4     greetings.
5     I received from you the [amount] and in addition for my annual tax
6     of the 15th indiction up to the
7     of the landowner's joint-assessment wheat of special kind
8     fifteen and one-half artabas,
9     one and two-thirds artabas of barley,
10    there result wheat artabas 15 1/2, barley artaba 1 2/3, chaff one artaba 1/6.
11    Apollonius through me Eirenaios
12    have been marked/attested.
