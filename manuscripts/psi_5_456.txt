[TRANSLATION]
1     Report
2     [To Aurelius] ... Ammon, also called Kopres, strategos of Oxyrhynchus...
3     [from Aurelius Ty]rannus, [son of] Ammonius, mother Thatretes, and Cornelius Silvanus, mother Tereutus...
4     [both] [month]-magistrates, builders of the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites. Being questioned by you through Aurelius Amo[i]tus, assistant of the strategy, concerning what [has come to pass] from...
5     ... from the counterstatement of Aurelius Sarapammon, Olympic victor, [remarkable, merchant, mother Theon]idus, friend of Sarapias, against [someone], Antiochos, bronze-worker, on account of building-plots which he says [have been damaged] by him, and he [has] formally petitioned you about what disposition we [might find] in respect of them, wherefore having gone along with the same assistant to the house of the aforesaid Sarapamm[on], [which is] at [the] Hermaeum [street], we observed in the western part of it...
6     ... and on the tower, in the western part, cracks, and ... having leapt out, and inside the tower, in the wes[tern part] ..., and in the eastern part new building-works ... ... opposite the house of Sarapammon, within the wall, between...
7     ... and width of four cubits, [four] palms, and...
8     ... [wa]lls two, newly built ... cubits [thre]e, the aforesaid bronze-work ... newly built, which...
9     [we petition] ... to be especially ... [year] ... [of the Emperor Caes]ar Marcus Aurelius...
10    [Probus, Gothicus Maximus, Persicus Maximus, Germanicus Maximus, P]ious, Fortunate...
