[TRANSLATION]
1     artaba[s] …
2     of four and a half artabas, and of wine two hundred five-xestes measures,
3     amounting to four gold solidi, for eighteen artabas of barley and eighteen artabas of grain and four and a half artabas of vegetable-seed and two hundred five-xestes measures of wine.
4     Which grain and barley and vegetable-seed — new, clean,
5     sifted, by the measure of Phib Mosch — I shall by compulsion
6     pay back to you in the month Pauni of the 1st indiction;
7     and which wine, fresh from the press, from the must, unadulterated,
8     you providing the empty jars, I shall by compulsion pay back
9     at the vintage of the month Mesore, of the wine-flow
10    of the 1st indiction, of the crops of the 2nd assessment-cycle, without delay;
11    and I take upon myself the good quality of the wine and
12    its keeping until the whole of the month Tybi; and if indeed
13    there be found in it any sourness or spoilage, I am to exchange these
14    and to supply you the equivalent in first-quality wine, at the risk
15    of my belongings, which are pledged for this. The deed is valid,
16    written in single copy, and on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
17    Aurelius Onophrios son of Rachel, the aforewritten,
18    I have received the price in full, and there agree with me
19    all the things written, as aforesaid.
20    … of …nidos … [of barley] artabas 18 and
21    of grain artabas 18 and of vegetable-seed
22    artabas 4 and a half a[nd] …
