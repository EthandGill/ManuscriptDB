[TRANSLATION]
1     The holy church of God of Hermopolis through me
2     Theodosios, collector of the gold of the portion
3     of Hermopolis, father Daniel. I received from you
4     from the tax of produce of the seventh indiction
5     gold carats twenty-three, become gold carats 23
6     which [things] having been given to Ph[o]ibammon, overseer of the gymnasium
7     for the entagion [in the month] Pharmouthi, indiction 7, only. It was written Pachon 11, indiction 7.
8     Theodosios the collector admits the entagion
