[TRANSLATION]
1     Antas Pathreius and those with him overseers
2     of sacred gate of Syene through Sarapion helper.
3     erased Kalaseiris [son] of Patefautos mother
4     Senpamonthos for poll-tax of year 20 [of] Hadrian Caesar.
5     of the lord on account [in] filthy drachmas three three-obol, amount to drachmas 3 three-obol.
6     Year 21 [of] Hadrian of the lord, Thoth 16.
