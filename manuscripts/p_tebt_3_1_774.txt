1     Ammonios, son of Dionysios, royal
2     farmer and workman, dwelling in
3     the City of the Crocodiles of the Arsinoite
4     nome, I was registered as counter-clerk in the time of the
5     grandfather of the king (for) the grain
6     workshop of (the district) about Philoteri[s]
7     of the division of Themistos (in) the 25th year,
8     and I have jointly served as sitologos with Herakleides (over) the workshop in the time of
9     the father (of the king), for the 2nd year, and with Theo[n]
10    son of Dionysios (over) the (workshop) about Pelousi[on]
11    workshop and Apias of the Them[istos]
12    divisio[n] likewise (in) the 14th year, and in the time [of the]
13    king … the (places) a[bout]
14    Kaminoi of the P[o]lem[on]
15    division (in) the 3rd year, and those about Me[mphis]
16    [like]wise (in) the 4th and 5th year, and (in) the [ … year the]
17    [work]shop of the embankment of Arsinoe, the place… …
18    and I have served as sitologos with Isidoros
19    (over) the workshop about Boubastos (in) the … [year]
20    and I set forth (a total of) wheat 1500 artabas, and wi[th]
21    Heliodoros (over) the same workshop (in) the 17th [year and]
22    I set forth of wheat ar[tabas] 300, and (in) the 18th year …
23    alone Boubastos itself by itself,
24    and according to the decision of the chrematistae concerning the (matters) about which Heliodoros
25    demanded, of bronze 1 talent 700 (drachmas). And concerning all these (matters) … …
26    … to the great Serapeum in [Me]mphis.
27    Farewell.
