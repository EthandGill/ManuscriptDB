[TRANSLATION]
1     99
2     To Apion, royal scribe of the Prosopite,
3     from Pantbeus son of Petos
4     and Tithoennesis and Haronnesis
5     and Phalakres, the four, from Thelbonthon Siphtha. We register, according to the things
6     ordered by Calvisius Statianus,
7     the most illustrious prefect, for the house-by-house census taking place for good fortune,
8     the property belonging to us in the village,
9     a house and vacant plots of Pantbeus and
10    Tithoennesis and Phalakres
11    and Haronnesis, the four (sons) of Petos
12    son of Pnepheros, in which:
13    Pantbeus son of Petos son of Pnepheros,
14    whose mother is Thaeious daughter of Psenamounis,
15    49 years old, with no distinguishing mark;
16    Theros son of Ammonios son of Hephaistion,
17    whose mother is Taphibichis, the wife, 21 years old;
18    Petos son of Pantbeus, whose mother is Thapsathis
19    daughter of Petos, 10 years old, with a white eye;
20    Ammonios the brother, whose mother is Theros,
21    5 years old, with no distinguishing mark;
22    Taphibichis the sister, of the same parents,
23    4 years old;
24    Thaeies, another sister, 2 years old;
25    Tithoennesis son of Petos, whose mother is
26    Thaeious, 44 years old, with no distinguishing mark;
27    Serathes the sister and wife, 40 years old;
28    Theros the daughter of both,
29    12 years old;
30    Thapsathis the sister of the same
31    parents, 8 years old;
32    Phalakres son of Petos son of Pnepheros,
33    whose mother is Thaeious daughter of Psenamounis,
34    30 years old, with no distinguishing mark;
35    Taartysis daughter of Saiphis son of Pnepherontithoes, whose mother is Achillis,
36    the wife, 30 years old;
37    Nouatis the daughter, whose mother is Thermouthis daughter of Pnepheros, 5 years old;
38    Haronnesis son of Petos
39    son of Pnepheros, whose mother is Thaeious
40    daughter of Psenamounis, 21 years old, with no distinguishing mark;
41    Thanibichis daughter of Herpaesis, whose mother is
42    Thsenamounis, the wife, 19 years old;
43    Herpaesis, of both parents,
44    2 years old, with no distinguishing mark;
45    Thsenamounis the sister of the same
46    parents, 1 year old.
47    In the 14th year of Imperator Caesar
48    Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
49    Augustus Armeniacus
50    Medicus Parthicus
51    Germanicus Maximus,
52    Epeiph 25.
53    Pantbeus son of Petos son of Pnepheros and his brothers
54    have submitted (this) as stated above. Hierakion
55    son of Pantbeus, I wrote on their behalf, they not knowing letters.
56    Through Harpok(…), page 42.
