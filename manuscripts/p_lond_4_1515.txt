1     … under Abu Aun the overseer, of the raiding-fleet of the 7th indiction, at ½ ⅓ solidus
2     to each per month, 2½ solidi
3     … [on account] of the need of the building of the public (works), under Abderam son of Salma the overseer, at 2 solidi per month, 6 solidi
4     … e … under Elal the overseer, to each ½ ⅓ solidus per month, 2½ solidi
5     … in Palestine, at 1 solidus per month with a third solidus for board (and) maintenance, a sixth ⅓ solidus; 48½ solidi 1/12 1/24
6     … wage of 1 workman on account of the same, 3 solidi; the price of 1 small wineskin, a third solidus
7     … concerning oil, of ½ sextarius, a 24th solidus; rush-mats and baskets 2, a 12th solidus
8     … 1½ ⅓ solidus; 32½ ⅓ solidi
9     … of the boat of Klysma, under Muhammad son of Abu Habiba the overseer, of 8 months, 4½ ⅓ solidi 1/12 1/24
10    … [concerning] oil, sextarii 24, 1 solidus; vinegar, sextarii 24, a 4th solidus; of salt-fish, kollatha 6, 4 solidi 1/24
11    … with a two-thirds solidus for the exchange of the gold; of butter, a sixth-of-2 solidus (?)
12    … ½ … 1/12
13    … to confirm that the …, five solidi, a whole solidus and a peshe (Copt.), indeed
14    … [the] assessment, that which is above
15    [the agreement] … without any harm whatsoever, the … governor commanding (?), I have given the receipt (proof) to [you] … [the son of the] blessed Menas; I (am) a witness who agrees according to his ability (Copt.)
16    … [I] (am) a witness who agrees through the request of him who set it in order (Copt.)
17    … [J]acob, I (am) a witness who agrees through the request (Copt.)
18    … [by the will of Go]d, I, this least (one), the elder, am a witness who agrees according to his ability (Copt.)
19    … [Mak]are, I (am) a witness who agrees according to his ability (Copt.)
20    … I (am) a witness who agrees according to his ability (Copt.)
21    [ Through me, Theodoro]s, with God, the notary, it was written.
