1     [In the …] year of the reign [and consulship] of our most divine
2     [lord Fla]vius Justin the et[er]nal [Augustus,] Emperor, in the year
3     … the n[inth, Ep]eiph [1], of the 8th indiction.
4     To Apoll… the most pious deacon
5     … of …yridou, from the (city) of the Hermopolites,
6     from Aurelius Anouthios son of Taurinus, his mother being
7     …deias … osistou, from the same city.
8     [I a]cknowledge of my own free will and choice that I have leased from you for as long a time as
9     I have the use of the place set out below,
10    reckoned from today's
11    day, which is Epeiph, the new moon
12    of the present eighth indiction, the one place belonging
13    to you, which is in the second
14    storey, facing north, together with a share
15    both of the courtyard and of the well and of all the other
16    appurtenances and rights, of
17    a house situated in the same Hermopolis
18    in this Eastern quarter, for my use
19    and dwelling, the rent for this each
20    month being three hundred talents of silver, which
21    [rent] I shall pay you at the end of each
22    [month with]out delay; and whenever you may wish
23    [to have it, I shall hand] over to you the same place with its door
24    [just as I rec]eived it; the lease is binding and secure, and
25    [on being questioned I have so ack]nowledged. Aurelius Anouthis son of Taurinus, the aforesaid,
26    [have lea]sed as afore[said.] Aurelius Phoibammon son of Victor
27    [from Hermopolis,] having been asked, wrote for him because he does not know letters.
28    [F]lavius Victor son of Kollouthos, I witness the lease, having heard it from the one who made it. Aurelius
29    Zacharias son of Menas, from Hermopolis, I witness the
30    lease, having heard it from the one who made it.
31    [Through me …]i, with God, the notary, it was written.
