[TRANSLATION]
1     … …
2     To Gaius Statilius Maximus the
3     most excellent epistrategos,
4     from Gaius Julius Apollinarius, a soldier
5     of the first cohort of the Apamenians, of the century of Herakleides. I bought, [lord], by public proclamation in the past … [year] of our lord Antoninus, in Phamenoth,
6     [in] the territory of the village of Kerkesoucha of the Arsinoite nome of the division of Herakleides
7     six grain-bearing arouras which formerly belonged to
8     my father Mystes son of Aphrodisios and
9     to Ninos my brother, taken over
10    into the treasury, they being partners in the grain-collectorship entrusted to them,
11    of which I also paid the price on the spot. Since therefore those who previously cultivated the aforesaid arouras, Kastor son of H… and his son …des, holding on to them up to now, are unwilling to pay me the rents, I beg, [if it seem good to you],
12    to order a letter to be written to the strategos of the Arsinoite,
13    of the division of Herakleides, to compel them to pay me the rents and to withdraw henceforth from the arouras confirmed to me by you, the lord, so that I may be benefited.
