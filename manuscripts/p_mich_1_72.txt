[TRANSLATION]
1     Aigyptos to Zenon
2     Greetings. You would do well, if it is convenient for you, having sent to me
3     castor-oil, choes three, and alabaster vessels ten, and thrissae if any fresh ones exist with you,
4     and mustard, half an artaba,
5     and of the garlic,
6     the large ones, so that we might also send to Artemidoros; for you have laughed sufficiently. And
7     of the pitch the ten metretai you will do well
8     having received from Diotime.
9     These things indeed, if you send,
10    send to Ptolemais right away;
11    but if not, in vain [you send] if
12    you delay. Therefore do not [otherwise] do.
13    Send moreover to me [absolutely] linen cloths, two,
14    and cushions, four. [Farewell.]
15    Of year ... Pa ... [Aigyptos]. To Zenon.
