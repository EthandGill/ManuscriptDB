[TRANSLATION]
1     Pamonthes and Postumus, collectors of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Pikos the younger, (son of) Archis (son of) Pete( ). We have received
3     for the assessment for the destitute (aporoi) of year 5, two drachmas, 3 obols,
4     total drachmas 2, 3 obols. Year 6 of Antoninus Caesar
5     the lord, Pachon 26. I, Paniskos, have signed.
6     (2nd hand) Others, Tybi 2, in the name of the same,
7     for the doubles (dipla) of year 6, one base drachma,
8     2 obols, 2 chalkoi, total drachma 1, 2 obols, 2 chalkoi. P…, <have signed>.
