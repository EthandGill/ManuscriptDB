[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammonius to Apollonianus and Spartiates, to my lords and sweetest
2     father and brother, many greetings.
3     I write [greeting] you and praying to you that the good things in life may be yours and encouraging you to write to me constantly about your welfare, knowing that if I receive letters from you, a festival
4     I celebrate. Greet my sweetest
5     children and the sister Dioscouraina; the children
6     of yours. [Greet] Isidora and the children
7     of hers. I [pray] for you to be well
8     prospering through life always,
9     long ago the soldier [troubles] us as
10    for the sake of … I [ordered] him. [He will say]
11    therefore to you the matter Diogenes.
