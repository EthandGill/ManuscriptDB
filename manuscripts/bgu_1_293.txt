[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Copy of a receipt. Year twelve of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, Hathyr 21. Pasion paid to Artemidoros and his associates, overseers, for the income of year 11 of Ptolemais Nea for rent on houses twelve [drachmas], 12 drachmas. Year 13, Tybi 22. The same person paid likewise for the income of the past year twelve, eight drachmas.
