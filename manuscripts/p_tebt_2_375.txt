1     To Herakl[ei]des also called N[in]nos, son of Mysthes, and to his mother
2     I[si]doras, daughter of Herakleides,
3     from [I]sion, son of Heron, from the metropolis,
4     from the qu[a]rter of the Macedonians. I wish to lease
5     voluntarily and of my own accord fr[o]m you for two years,
6     from the [pr]esent fourth [y]ea[r] of Antoninus Caes[a]r
7     the l[or]d, the (lands) belonging to yo[u] around the village of Bernikis
8     (of) Thesm[opho]ros, of the Polemon division, of catoecic allotment (land),
9     arour[as si]x and a half; and around the village of Ibion Argaiou
10    of the s[a]me division, of catoecic allotment (land), arouras four, the (lands) together being arouras ten and a half, or however many they may
11    be — the [fi]rst year of the lease, the (arouras)
12    around Bernikis (of) Thesmophoros, arouras s[i]x and a half, f[or] sowing an[d] re-sowing (with) whatever I choose each year, exce[p]t
13    barley and safflower, I sowing in the present fourth year (in) grass and in the following (year in) sown-crop; but the (arouras) around Ibion each year, without seed, (in) barl[ey,] arta bas twe[nty] fi[ve];
14    an[d] of the (arouras) around Bernikis, on behalf of the fourth year
15    of silver drachmas one hundred sixty; but in the fifth
16    year, which is the last year of the lease, of wheat art abas [one] hundred six and a half, of which I shall receive on behalf of seed of wheat six and a half artabas, and the remaining wheat
17    one hundred artabas I shall deliver in the village, in the month
18    Kaisareios, by the six-choinix measure of the treasury (thesauros) of Basyllos, pure (clean)
19    from freight-charges (and) sackage; but of the public
20    freight-charges on behalf of the aforesaid arouras being upon
21    me, Ision; and I shall give you each year of leavened, seasoned dough two-thirds of an artaba, you t…s apol…to,
22    and after the (lease-)time I shall deliver all the arouras
23    clea[n] from rush, reed, (and) all fil[t]h, leveled,
24    if it seem good to lease (them) on the aforesaid (terms), on which
25    you shall guarantee to me (freedom) from public (dues) of the arouras. Ision, son of He[r]on,
26    I have written the body (of the deed) and I have leased on all the aforesaid (terms). Year fourth of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus
27    Antoni[n]us Augustus Pius, Thoth 11.
