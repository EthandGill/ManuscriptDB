[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelii Horos son of Paesis, mother
2     Techosis, and Asklas son of Apollonios, mother Taaphynchis, both from the village of Senepta, to Septimius Aurelius Eudaimon son of Serenus,
3     gymnasiarch, councillor of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, greeting.
4     We have received from you five artabas of barley of the crop of the past year, which we shall repay
5     to you with the additional charges from the third (?) within
6     the thirtieth of Pauni of the present
7     6th year at the threshing-floor of the same village
8     Senepta, new, pure, unadulterated,
9     free of clods, sifted and
10    well trodden, by the tenth measure
11    by which we also received it from
12    those measuring out on your behalf, with the additional charges. And if we do not repay
13    to you within the aforesaid
14    term, we shall pay you with the half as much extra …of the (overrun) time.
15    The bond is valid, the [ch]irograph
16    written in duplicate, produced everywhere and to anyone (producing it) on
17    your behalf, the (right of execution) arising
18    for you, the right of execution being from us
19    who are bound by mutual surety for the
20    payment and from whichever of us
21    you choose and from all
22    our belongings; and having been formally questioned by you we consented. [Year 6]
