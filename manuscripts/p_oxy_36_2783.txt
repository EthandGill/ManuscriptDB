[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios to Artemas his brother, greeting.
2     Before everything I pray to all the gods that the finest things in life
3     may be yours; and now I gave your brother 500 drachmas, and to Ptolemaios I have already
4     transferred a part, as he wished. But I wonder that on oil
5     you spent so much money. Twice over you put in six; for here the Ammoniac (oil costs) 220 drachmas,
6     and the Auastic 200 drachmas; so that if for another
7     use you take it, one able to
8     benefit us, you do well. You wrote to me
9     as though I were cutting you down in your absence,
10    and this is worse than the former things. For if
11    you know my mind from now on,
12    you ought not to be judged a (mere) man. And another time I wrote to you that neither did I detain the camel
13    of Hermias nor of anyone else. But if
14    you wish me to write these things, I write to you. For perhaps
15    you do not read what I write to you. But how many things
16    I suffered here on account of the camels
17    of Koptos, both Ammonas and the automataris Anthropas and all the others you can hear from your brother. And
18    your accursed brothers-in-law are running riot
19    and on their behalf I made many advances for
20    your sake. If …
21    … I have a plan, I will tell you. That I may not seem
22    to write much, I pray that you be in health.
23    The blind man your brother compelled me to sell;
24    To Artemas from Apollonios.
