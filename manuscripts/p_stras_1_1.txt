[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the consulship of the reign of] the Flavii Ariobindus and Asper, the most magnificent and greatest, in the year …
2     Mesore 27, of the 4th indiction.
3     [Aurelia Tabesis, daughter of Heron], her mother being Tauris, wine-merchant, with a scar on a finger of her left hand,
4     [from] … the hamlet of Demetrios of the Hermopolite [nome],
5     [to Aureli-] … from the city, greeting. I have been paid in full
6     by you and have received of the complete (price) agreed between one another
7     [as the price] of new wine, of must, five hundred knidia, that is 500 knidia, which
8     I shall repay at the time of the vintage, in the month Mesore, of the fortunate
9     fifth indiction, in wine of the finest and most acceptable quality, by the measure of Dioskourides,
10    … [you providing] their empty jars, the execution being made without delay
11    [for you] both from me and from all my belongings, as if by legal judgment; the document (being) valid and secure, which I have also issued to you for security; and having been formally questioned, I gave my assent. I, Aurelia Tabesis, daughter of Heron,
12    the aforesaid, have been paid in full and have received of the complete price of the said wine, five hundred knidia,
13    and I shall repay as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Herminos, notary,
14    wrote on her behalf, she not knowing letters,
15    and through me, Herminos, it was written.
