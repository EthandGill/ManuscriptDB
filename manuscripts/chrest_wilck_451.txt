[TRANSLATION]
1     Mnesarchos [to Ant]ipater, greetings. I have sent [a letter]
2     to Paniskos to cancel from the bank in Arsinoe
3     to Demetr[í]os, scribe of the [hunters] under Andronikós
4     so that to those departing with Peitholàos [the] men
5     salary from Artemisium until Panemos, three months, talents 2 A…,
6     with deduction made for those sent ahead to month Artemi[síon], drachmas 60, the remainder talents 2 A…. [Please] coordinate [this]
7     then as has been written.
8     Farewell. Year 25, Thoth 21.
9     To Apollonides: coordinate [this] as has been written.
10    Farewell. Year 25, Thoth 21.
