[TRANSLATION]
1   Pachnoubis son of Thrakidas paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the fifth year
3   of Trajan the lord,
4   on account eight drachmas — total 8 dr.; for the cross-weave tax(?)
5   likewise three drachmas — total 3 dr. The same,
6   the remaining 3 drachmas. I, Hermogenes,
7   the collector, wrote (this).
