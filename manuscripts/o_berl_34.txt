r.2    Ἀρίστου Καίσαρος τοῦ κυρίου κατὰ μέρος δεκαεπτὰ ἡμιωβέλιον , δεσμοῦ ἡμιωβέλιον ,

[TRANSLATION]
1     Pachnoubis son of Phanophes, mother Tisatis, has paid off the poll-tax of the 19th year of Trajan
2     of Caesar Aristus the lord, in portions 17 half-obols, of binding half-obol,
3     amounts to 17 drachmas [and] 1 obol, of provisions-money
4     river-guard [tax] 1 drachma [and] 3 obols, through Sarapion
5     Epeiph 26
