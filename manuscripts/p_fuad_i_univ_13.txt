[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Horeion of Theon assistant of the illustrious
2     and most illustrious Oxyrhynchite city
3     of the present year 14 and year 13 and year 6
4     from Marcus Aurelius Silvanus Sarapion
5     also of Sarapias also of Antiochus mother
6     Tapsyis from the same city. I wish
7     first to be registered the [one] born to me
8     and of a deceased woman Aurelia [Tapsyis]
9     [a] son Aurelius Ammon
