[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a request.
2     To Aelius Herakleitos, assistant of Aelius
3     Eglektos, procurator of the lord Caesar,
4     from Ptolemaios son of Diodoros, also called
5     Dioskoros, lessee of the marsh of the village
6     of Theadelph[e]ia. Whereas the customary waters
7     which ought to come down into the aforesaid marsh until it is filled have, up to today, not (done so), but it is in danger of being dried up, wherefore I ask you to send a letter to the shore-guard, (stating) that,
8     if any negligence occurs concerning the waters
9     and it is not fill[ed] as is the custom,
10    not only does harm [ens]ue for the ne[x]t year,
11    for, the marsh having been leased,
12    but it becomes unproductiv[e] for three years. I ask
13    therefore, lord, to make (it) clear to the shore-guard,
14    so that, if because of negligence any harm
15    occurs, I may have grounds (for redress) before the most excellent epistrategos. Year 8, Hathyr 7.
16    Copy of the dispatch-letter.
17    Aelius Herakleitos to Apollonios
18    the shore-guard, his dearest, greeting.
19    I did not expect that I would be asked also concerning the
20    marsh, so that he might furnish it with the sufficient waters, as
21    its lessee Ptolemaios
22    requested; for it was consequent
23    upon your care to take thought for this too before
24    anyone made a request. But if anything
25    up to now has been an obstacle,
26    or anything else of the matters seeming to you more pressing, has caused a delay to the
27    filling of the marsh, yet
28    now too, brother, take thought that the customary waters be furnished to it. I pray that you fare well, dearest.
29    Year 8, Hathyr 7.
