[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos,
2     from Ptolemaios son of Antaios, of the Temenos (district), and Leon son [of]
3     Theodotos, an Alexandrian, and from Apollonios son of Achilleus,
4     a Persian of the descent. Apollonios consents that he has lea[sed]
5     from Ptolemaios and Leon for a period of five years from
6     Thoth of the incoming twenty-sixth year of Caesar, from Ptolemaios the third share belonging to his wife Tryphaina daughter of Theon, a citizeness,
7     and from Leon likewise the third shar[e] belonging to him
8     out of (land) common and undivided, of marsh-land … of four and a half arouras
9     or however many there may be, in which there are now vines and
10    a rose-garden and bean-fields, being in two parcels on the spit by Canobus, by the temple, in the (place) called Ta synechomena (the adjoining lands) of Pamyches, at a rent of the conceded (land) per each year of eighty drachmas of silver, which also
11    Apollonios shall pay, giving (it) to Ptolemaios
12    and Leon each year in three equal installments through
13    a four-month period, free of all deduction and expense,
14    on these terms: that Apollonios, [holdin]g to the leased (property), shall accomplish all the fitting works of the vi[n]es and of the bean-fields in season and
15    at the proper time satisfactorily, neither … working badly nor
16    damaging nor omitting any work
17    of those pertaining to good husbandry, di[gg]ing the common (land)
18    properly and watering with the necessary waterings,
19    attending to the vegetable-cultivation in season, and if any of the
20    vine or of the rose-garden [fails, re]planting the same kinds in the same year, [and not abandoning]
21    the lease within the per[i]od; [and when this has elapsed]
22    that he hand over the lease[d (property)] full-planted
23    and flourishing and well-… [and clea]n of all mud and in no respect [worse] …
24    and the fence sound, just as he received it, or in whatever way he
25    transgresses, that he be at once [liable to seizure and]
26    held bound until of necessity … …
27    he accomplish and pay whatever rents he may owe, with the half in addition, and the damages and expenses and [other things,] as a penalty, five hundred drachmas of silver, [there being authority]
28    to the lessors, if in anything Apollonios transgresses the cont[ract,]
29    to cast him out within [the period of the]
30    lease and to others … and to exact also the (loss) that shall come about beyond the re-lease,
31    the damage occurring to the lessors, the right of execution being both from
32    Apollonios and from all his belongings
33    just as if by [legal judgment], invalid being also (any) pledges of credit which he may bring forward, and of all protections of decrees;
34    and (provided that) he acts according to the aforewritten things, that
35    Ptolemaios and Leon also guarantee to him the lease for the per[io]d … with the …
36    within … to set aside for the rent, or also
37    Pt[olemaios and Leo]n be liable to the [sa]me damages
38    … [the con]cession, on condition that
39    they shall allow to [Apollonios, of the] produce that comes to be,
40    the joint val[uation.]
