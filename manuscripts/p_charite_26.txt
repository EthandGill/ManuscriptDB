[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Silvanus [district-]administrator of the 3rd village
2     To Aurelia Charite of Amazo[n]ius, greetings.
3     I acknowledge: the farmers of your Senoabe
4     of your fields have not given meat
5     nor grain and barley and chaff and freight-fee
6     of the [fourteenth] and thirteenth
7     indictions in accordance with the covenant with
8     Hermos, witness of the same village, and nothing [to be] false. The acknowledgment [is] binding and
9     when questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius Silvanus: [this] is
10    my [own handwriting] and I swore the sacred oath.
11    in the consulate of Antonius Marcellinus and Petronius Probinus, of the most illustrious, Pachon 9th.
