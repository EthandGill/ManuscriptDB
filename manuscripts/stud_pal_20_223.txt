[TRANSLATION]
1     I received the things written by my mistress containing gratitude regarding the lime
2     Behold, I have it, and so that my mistress might learn that I am not [sending] [to] Babylon [unseasonably]
3     [But] [behold] I wrote two other letters to my mistress that send to me the
4     man, and I [send] the lime with him to Babylon for the whole, and my mistress is not willing to send me the man. Therefore my mistress commands: send to me […]
5     your workers so that they might put the lime into the ship, and I send one
6     of them aboard the ship to my mistress.
7     … to my good mistress, Joseph your servant
