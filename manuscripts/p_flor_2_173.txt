[TRANSLATION]
1     From Apianus.
2     My lord son Primus the
3     most worthy let him have all service and suitable wine-allowances and [small gift/provision] and small loaves and the other
4     everything, but so that he may testify for me and the hospitality indeed
5     let him have and if anything else is possible.
6     I pray that you may be well
7     To Heroninus manager
8     of Theadelphia.
9     Year 3, Tybi 17
