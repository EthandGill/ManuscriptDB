[TRANSLATION]
1     Concerning a letter to be made to Hypatios the town-councillor of the metropolis of the Pelusians,
2     so that he take with him Theodosios the town-councillor and father, and Photios the
3     town-councillor and superintendent over the olive-oil title, and the leaders of the people,
4     and the Tyrians, both those in the Pelusian (district) and those residing in the village of Thylax, and
5     in the conspicuous place of the city, to make an exact determination, as to how
6     the oil ought to be disbursed to those dwelling in the metropolis of the Pelusians,
7     so that they may blamelessly have what pertains to good supply, and … other things,
8     to make this clear to the court, a penalty being fixed against him,
9     an officialis being dispatched.
10    Concerning a letter to be made to Theodosios the town-councillor and father of the metropolis of the Pelusians,
11    so that he bring all the Tyrians residing in the Pelusian (district)
12    and the village of Thylax before Hypatios the town-councillor, so that,
13    with all the appointed persons present, an exact determination be made
14    concerning the sale of the oil, as to how this ought to be disbursed to those
15    dwelling in the metropolis of the Pelusians, so that they may blamelessly
16    have what pertains to good supply, a penalty being fixed against him.
17    The fiscal bureau.
18    Memorandum.
