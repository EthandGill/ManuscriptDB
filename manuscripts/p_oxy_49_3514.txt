[TRANSLATION]
1     Nemesianos to [Se]ouer[os], greeting.
2     Give to those listed below, for the maintenance-allowance
3     of the present month Pachon: to Doras the boy-slave
4     of wheat 1 artaba, of wine 1 jar; to Threptos another, of wheat 1 artaba,
5     of wine 1 jar; to Nepheros of wheat 1 artaba, of wine 1 jar;
6     to Hermeros of wheat 1 artaba, of wine 1 jar; to Neikes of wheat 1 artaba,
7     of wine 1 jar; to Tychanos the collector of wheat 1 artaba,
8     of wine 1 jar, and for the festival of Pachon the 1 jar customarily given to him; to Psenamounis the overseer of vine-dressers
9     of wheat 1 artaba, of wine 1 jar; to Apion the steward of wheat 1 artaba, of wine 1 jar; to Ammonios likewise
10    of wheat 1 artaba, of wine 1 jar; to Silvanos of wheat 1 artaba, of wine 1 jar;
11    to yourself and your assistant of wheat 2 artabas, of wine
12    3 jars, of vinegar 1 jar, and for the festival of Pachon according to
13    custom 1 jar; the totals for these of wheat of the produce of the 5th year, 12 artabas,
14    of wine of the produce of the 6th year, from what was left by the
15    merchants, of the estate of Magdolon, 15 jars, of vinegar 1 jar.
16    Farewell. And give the artabas of wheat,
17    twelve, and of wine fifteen jars, and of vinegar one jar; total, of wheat 12 artabas,
18    15 jars, of vinegar 1. In the 7th year, Pachon 30.
