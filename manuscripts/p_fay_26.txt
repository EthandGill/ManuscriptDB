[TRANSLATION]
1     To Diogenes, strategos of the Arsinoite, of the Themistos and Polemon [divisions,]
2     from Heraiskos also called Herakleides and Dioskoros, the secretaries [of the metropolis]
3     and the village-secretaries of the Themistos and Polemon divisions.
4     In response to the instruction sent to us by you, of which there is [a copy]:
5     Diogenes, strategos of the Arsinoite, of the Themistos and Polemon [divisions,]
6     to the secretaries of the metropolis and the village-secretaries, [greeting.]
7     In the present 13th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord …
8     of the properties and house-plots registered as crops in the divisions …
9     of the administration and of the estate (lands) and of the [office] of the special account
10    by both me and the royal secretary [of the Herakleides] division
11    and by the one appointed for this by the most excellent [epistrategos]
12    Herennius Philotas, Sementhion also called Selsia … ou …
13    dwelling in the Herakleopolite, so that those who are to survey the …
14    … possession of the (properties) that have appeared from the inspection …
15    … since also the tax diminished on account of this …
16    … of taxes it makes a difference, you shall assign … solvent (persons) [to] the liturgy
17    [at your] risk, we have instructed. I have signed.
18    Year [13 of Imp]erator Caesar Titus [Ae]lius Hadrianus Antoninus
19    [Augustus] P[i]ous, Phamenoth 2.
20    Sarapion the attendant: I have delivered it, Phamenoth 2.
