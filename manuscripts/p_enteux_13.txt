1     To King Ptolemy greetings, Asia. I am being wronged by [Poori] the stable-master. For my husband
2     Machatos, [being appointed as station-master] in the village of Pelousion and having divided off from Poori and having built a shrine of the Syrian god and Aphrodite Berenice in his own place, and there being a certain
3     unfinished wall between Poori's property and my husband's, but I wishing to complete
4     the wall, so that it might not be passable into our property, Poori has hindered building, nothing pertaining
5     to him of the wall, but scorning that my husband has died. I beseech you therefore, King, to order
6     Diophanēs the strategos to write to Menandros the epistates, if the wall [should appear] to be ours, not to allow Poori to hinder us from building, so that, having taken refuge [to] you, King, I might obtain justice.
7     Fare well.
8     To Menandros. [Especially] resolve [them]; if [not,] send to [us] so that we might examine.
9     Year [25, Loios] 26, Choiach 13.
10    Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13.
11    Asia against Poori concerning
12    the demolition of a wall.
