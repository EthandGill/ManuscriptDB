1     Acknowledge [the] Aurelius Satabus of Pahesius, of mother
2     [Heros], about fifty-five years old, [with a] scar under [the ankle bone] of the left foot
3     and Horeios of Aheutos, of mother Heros, about forty years old, [with a] scar
4     beside the left eyebrow, both from the village
5     Thraso, [owe to] Aurelius Didymus of [Ptolemaeus], wrestling-school keeper, and as he is registered to have from [the] [acknowledging ones] through hand silver [drachmae] [three thousand], being the remaining [portion of the] price of barley of thirty artabas, of which also
6     the repayment [the acknowledging parties] Satabus and Horeios will necessarily make [from] mutual responsibility
7     [in Pauni], [month] of the present second year to
8     [the metropolis] by measure … [four-choinix] eleven… [without postponement]; and upon the demand
9     [to occur for] Aurelius Didymus of the enforcement
10    [from both those] acknowledging Satabus and [Horeios and from the property] [possessing] them all as [from judgment; and when questioned] they acknowledged.
11    [of year 2 of our lords Marc]us Aurelii
12    [Carinus and Numerianus Augus]ti, Phanenoth twenty-eight.
13    [Aurelius Satabus rece]ived the [drachmae] of
14    [silver drachmae three thousand] …
15    … artabas thirty toward Didymus the [wrestling-school keeper].
