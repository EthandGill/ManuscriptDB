[TRANSLATION]
1     In the seventh year of Imperator [Caesar]
2     Trajan Hadrian Augustus, in the month
3     Germanikeios, on the twenty-seventh, (= ) Pachon 27, in
4     Bakchias of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite
5     nome. Hareotes son of Alexas, about 62 years old,
6     with a scar on his left foot, acknowledges to Horos son of Horos, farmer, [about 49 years old, with a scar]
7     on his right eyebrow, that he, the acknowledging party, has received from him
8     the rent and the seed of the pre[sent]
9     seventh year of Hadrian Caesar the [lord, of the land which]
10    he farms belonging to him around Bakchias, of a hol[ding of] four and a half [arouras],
11    twenty artabas of wheat [by the measure]
12    (specified) in the lease, which is to remain valid on all the terms it contains. As subscribers — for the acknowledging party, Petsiris
13    son of Papeis, about 52 years old, with a scar on his nose on the left, and for the other, Souchas
14    son of Sochotes, about 43 years old, with a scar on his right forearm. I, Haryodes son of Alexas,
15    acknowledge that I have received from Horos the rent and the seed of the present seventh year of Hadrian Caesar the lord, of the (land) which he farms belonging to me
16    around Bakchias, of four and a half arouras, twenty artabas of wheat by the measure (specified) through the lease,
17    which is to remain valid as aforesaid. Petsiris [son of Papei]s wrote on his behalf, as he does not know letters. I, Horos
18    son of Horos, the receipt has come to me as aforesaid. I, Souchas
19    son of Sochotes, wrote on his behalf, as he does not know let[ters].
20    It was registered through the record-office in Bakchias.
