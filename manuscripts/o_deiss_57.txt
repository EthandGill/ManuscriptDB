[TRANSLATION]
1     Harpocrates to Phthomothes, greetings.
2     [Necessarily] be diligent with
3     Senmothes Paus, and to Plenis of Paouosios from Phmau to the farmers of the Lake, of wheat 5
4     for the payment of wheat 35, [they] come to of wheat 35.
5     Year 33, Tybi 3.
6     and already sometime give to my maidservant
7     the 3 2/3 of wheat.
