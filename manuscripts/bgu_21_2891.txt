[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our master
2     Flavius Anastasius the eternal Augustus
3     and of Flavius Rufus the most illustrious,
4     Phamenoth 1 of the 1st indiction, in the city of the Arsinoites.
5     To Eirene also called Eulogia, the holy ca[tholi]c church
6     of the holy Martyrs of the city of the Arsinoites
7     through Sambas the most reverend
8     presbyter and steward, Aurelius Akoous
9     son of Esour, vine-dresser of the holy catholic
10    church, from the village of Syron of the
11    Arsinoite nome, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received
12    from you and have had measured out to me, in kind
13    of wheat, three artabas of principal, of wheat artabas 3, which to you
14    I will rep[a]y in the month Pauni by the measure of the holy
15    church, in which (measure) also I received (it), in the city,
16    from the crops of the prosperous second indiction,
17    without delay, out of all my property, and
18    being formally questioned I gave my assent. Aurelius Akoous, son of [E]sour, vine-dresser, the aforesaid, I received of wheat as principal
19    three artabas and I will repay to the …
20    as is aforesaid. Aurelius Kyros son of Ioannes, I wrote on his behalf
21    he being present (but) illiterate.
22    Through me Agathokleas, through me Agathokleas (in Latin and Greek).
23    Bond of Akoous son of Esour, vine-dresser, of wheat artabas 3, to the holy church of the Martyrs through Sambas
24    presbyter and steward.
