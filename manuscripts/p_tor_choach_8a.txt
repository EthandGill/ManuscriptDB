[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolemaios and] Queen Kleopatra [t]he wife, gods Benefactors, greeting.
2     Apollonios, also called Psemmonthes, son of Hermias
3     also called Petenephotes, of the mercenary [c]avalrymen from Diospolis the Great of the Thebaid. I am being wronged by Psenchonsis and Chonompreus, the (sons) of Teephibi[s],
4     and Haratreus and Pechytes, the (sons) of Horos,
5     and Mentemes, whose father I do not know — or if they have any other names —
6     choachytai (embalmers) inhabiting the same
7     city as I. For there being to me an ancestral house in the part to the south, toward the west,
8     of the aforesaid Diospolis,
9     of sixteen cubits, of whi[ch there] fall [to me]
10    seven cubits, be[cause] my [father]
11    Petenephotes was the [el]der son,
12    of which my [an]cestors and my father held [poss]ession,
13    for as long a time as they were alive,
14    indisputably — the accused, having entered into the aforesaid
15    house and built around themselves
16    dwelling-quarters, inhabit (it) by [forc]e, holding to nothing
17    just. And in the 44th year,
18    Thoth, while [t]hey were building
19    by many hands with[out builder]s,
20    and I having made [wor]ds to them (i.e. protested),
21    using no decency, but with intolerable
22    lawlessness being carried away, rushing out upon me and defiling (me), [insulti]ng me,
23    they gave (me) blows. [Therefore], having made
24    my [refuge to you,]
25    I beg you, [the greate]st gods and
26    victory-bringing, [if it seems good to you, to re]fer
27    my [petition to the] chre[matistai from the]
28    Panopoli[te (nome) as far as Syene], of whom Am[monio]s is the [introdu]cer,
29    so that, having taken it up
30    for judgment, and having summoned the
31    accused through Antiphanes the garrison-commander,
32    they may investigate, so that, if it is as I [al]lege, they may judge the disputed
33    seven cubits of the house to be mine, as indeed
34    they are, and that they (the accused) be made to pay me, for the wrong, according to the ordinance, 5 talents of copper. For concerning
35    the outrage and the blows and what they have committed against me, hereafter
36    I shall obtain from them, through another petition, justice as is fitting. And when this has been done I shall have been helped.
37    Fare you well.
