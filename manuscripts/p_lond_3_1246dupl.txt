[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Flavius Olympiodoros also called Asynkritios and to Achilleus,]
2     [son of Eudaimon, embankment-overseers of the nome of Hermo]polis, of the southern parts,
3     [fr]om the Aur[elii ] … [ Phibis and P ] …
4     [b]oth [komarchs from the ham]let [of Patelkios of the]
5     same nome. [We give and pre]sen[t for the] water-[guar]d-duty of the cut-channel … [71 ] … [—os ] … and of the bo[undaries ] …
6     at our own ri[sk and that] of all those [from the v]illag[e], on the basis of joint surety, the persons enrolled below,
7     bei[n]g well-off [and ][suita]ble for [thi]s, [on the condition that]
8     they henceforth … and undertake the
9     [water-]guard-duty [entr]usted [to them] blamelessly
10    and without absenting themselves. They are as follows:
11    The Aurelii: Serenos, son of Py… —itos; Horion, son of Taurinos;
12    Leus, son of [A]kes; Serenos, son of Chonsis;
13    A[l]ex[an]dros, son of Silb[an]os; Ph[i]bis, son of Mel[a]s;
14    Elias, son of Salamo[n]; Aroous, son of Proous;
15    Soeis, son of Kastor; Apollon, son of Premmeotes;
16    whom we guarantee as blamelessly fulfilling
17    the water-guard-duty entrusted to them. But if
18    any of them defaults and we do not produce (him),
19    we ourselves will undertake the account on his behalf;
20    a[nd] being formally questioned we acknowledged as is stated above.
21    In the consulship of the Flavii Amantius and (his colleague), the most illustrious,
22    Mesore 27.
23    We, the aforesaid komarchs, have submitted (this) as is stated above.
24    Aurelius Anoubas, secretary of the same hamlet,
25    wrote on behalf of them, who do not know letters.
