[TRANSLATION]
1     To Herodes and also Tiberius [the strategist] [of the Themisto portion of Arsinoe]
2     From Serapouta of Theo… [from the village] of Theadelphia. [The one …] in deeds and manner [established as ultimate master] by you in [the following]
3     him [using self-will and boldness] each time attacks me and [now in my own]
4     house insults irreparable [to me he brings about],
5     not only reviling, [but also with blows]
6     maltreating. Therefore, [not being able to remain] quiet, for a long time [throughout each day]
7     in danger, to you I [take refuge helpless]
8     and helpless being [and I request to be brought]
9     him before you for [the fitting redress]
10    so that I might be able through your [aid and] relief in my own [with peace to live]
11    and I [having been shown kindness] by you. [Fare well.]
12    In the twelfth year of the Emperor [Caesar Trajan]
13    Hadrian Augustus. Athyr 7.
