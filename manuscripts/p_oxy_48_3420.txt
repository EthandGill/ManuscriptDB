[TRANSLATION]
1     To my master and patron
2     Sarapammon,
3     Ammonios.
4     I am amazed how you write to me thus
5     without disclosing through your letter even the price, so that before
6     the emergency the country-folk too may make their own (purchase) ready. Hold off,
7     lest later we burn the whole; for if
8     you write thus again, the fault will not lie with me. Just now Ammonianos has been writing into Nesmimis vaguely. Thus you write. You disclosed
9     then through Amois, a man of your own, as concerning
10    meat, 600 pounds; you did not disclose
11    at what price per pound. And concerning chaff, 10,000 pounds, again the price you did not disclose, or whether you wish the
12    commodity, I mean the chaff,
13    to be sent to the city. Thus too
14    the country-folk say the same things,
15    because of your letters all the obstruction arises. Write to me therefore the
16    price of each commodity. I shall cease
17    being afflicted along with my own troubles. Perhaps the flood
18    is coming, so that in a single moment we may pay everything in full. I have sent to you Saraeous daughter of Pausirios holding
19    a receipt for 22 pounds of meat of Ammonianos; see whether it was credited; if
20    not, have it credited and write to me. I have sent to you Ammonas the field-guard with
21    letters. If you receive your own (dues),
22    have him likewise pay in full. The wool was apportioned among the sheep so that the field-guard may pay in full, and that not by a little. As for what you write
23    me about Zoilas, you did not send
24    their statement. I pray for your
25    health for many years.
26    Do not neglect to write to me the price of the meat, namely how much they demand per pound; and moreover write to me also about the chaff. Perhaps
27    you do not even read my letters. Have the president give 1 1/2 1/4 of a gold solidus on behalf of the oil-press of the heirs of Syros;
28    for already I too am demanding the other oil-presses. Write to us today the price of each, and the price
29    of the mule, namely how much per aroura, and make haste so that before the emergency they may help again.
30    To my master and patron Sarapammon,
31    Ammonios.
