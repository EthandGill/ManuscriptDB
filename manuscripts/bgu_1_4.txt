[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Severus Iustus, centurio, from Aurelius Abous, veteran. When I was serving in the army, lord, in Pelusium, I deposited with Petesouchos, a certain fellow soldier and friend of mine objects at a value of eight hundred drachmas. After I had been transferred to the ala Vocontiorum and as I was settling accounts with him concerning these things after our discharge, he took no account of me. Therefore I request, since I have a witness, Syrion, son of Is[i]doros from K[arani]s, who agrees (with me), [that they be brought before you]
