[TRANSLATION]
1     To those [who have received the tax on] donkeys of the Hermopolite
2     from [Eu]daim[on the elder] Hermaios
3     [Hermopolite] and Nearchus of Eud[aemon] [Her]mopolite. I register for the present twelfth year of Domitian Caesar
4     the lord which I hold in the name
5     of Eudaemon [donkey]s female three and a foal
6     male one and [foal] female one, and of
7     Nearchus [donkey] female o[ne] not for hire, but for the own
8     use. And I am resident on the amphod
9     of the Fort west where I also own land.
10    Tax of six drachmas of donkeys of the twelfth year
11    of Domitian the lord, Mechir
12    29. I register for Eudaemon female donkeys
13    three, a foal male one [and] [fo]al female one, and for Nearchus
14    son a donkey female o[ne]. Through Hermaios.
