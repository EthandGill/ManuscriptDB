[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the year thirteen of the Emperor Caesar
2     Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Phamenoth 3rd. has written
3     To Anubion and associates, collectors of silver-money, Petesouchus the Elites and his associates
4     gerdioi of the village of Bacchias subject to the epistrategy of the same
5     year, Bacchias, eight drachmas, make eight, and the [items] recorded above
6     the same and the … philanthropist village-secretary
7     similarly of the same year [Bacchias] forty drachmas
8     make forty drachmas, and the [items recorded] above, symbolic
