1     [Year] … [of Imperator Caesar] … [Augustus P]ious, Mecheir 11, in Tebtynis of Polemon's division of the Arsinoe district.

3     [to consent after his own death to his children Harmiusis and] … born to him from his deceased wife Tapeinata[s] … Tephorsai(s) from the … of Pasion's son, of the aforementioned Harmiusis and

6     … [ar]oura quarter and whatever other things Pasion leaves or also [left] … apart from which [what] the father Pasion brought to each of them according to
7     [the present declaration] … to the afore[said] his Pasion's son Harmiusis to consent
8     … [and I wish the] heirs [to be Harmiusis] and Tephorsae and the [her] son Tephors[ai(s) Heracles] … [and for the …] Pasion's fu[neral and] clothing and good-burial of which whatever they [ex][pend] … [sacred-offerings] of the [in mutual] reckoning drachmas twenty-four … … …
