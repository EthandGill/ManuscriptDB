[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting from Parates, an Ara[b, a barbe]r, of those dwelling in Ptolemais of the Arabs. I am wronged by Malichos son of … sazaios. For although I provided him my services and tended to all his relatives
2     blamelessly with my craft … custom and for many years toward an allowance, just as had been agreed between me and Ma[lich]os, but now … of the preceding year, the 26th, and a dispute (arose) for me
3     against … concerning these things, and he led me to the temple of Athena and made me swear that I had indeed not received (payment)
4     … I beg you, king, not to overlook … me being wronged, being a craftsman,
5     [but to command Diophane]s the strategos to wr[ite to Pt]olem[y] the epistates, if indeed I have sworn to him
6     … [if in]deed he is even now [willing] to pay back to me what I claim … [but i]f he disputes anything, [to send him] to Diophanes so that [the matter] may be decided [for me]. For when this has been done, king, I shall have obtained [from you] all benevolence. Farewell.
7     [To Ptolem]y. Best of all reconcile them; but if not, send (them) so that on the proper [tribunal they may be judged].
8     Year 1, Gorpiaios 28, Tybi [12].
9     Year 1, Gorpiaios 28, Tybi 12.
10    Parates, an Arab, a barber,
11    against Malichos concerning wages.
