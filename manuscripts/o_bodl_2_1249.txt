[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis produce of the 9th year
2     Of Hadrian Caesar the lord
3     Epeiph 27 in the name of Petehespokhrates and
4     Apollonius likewise through Panameus
5     Psenthotes and Phatreous
6     Bekhios for the Island Ptolemais Cholmes Nekhtatymeios
7     Wheat artabae one. Another wheat artabae one and one-half
8     Total on the same wheat artabae 2½. I Antiphilos have sealed.
