[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 33 of Lucius Aelius Aurelius
2     Commodus Caesar the lord, Hathyr 14.
3     There paid to Aphrodas also called Mnemon, son of Petesouchos, collector of money-taxes of the village-scribeship of Peensakoi,
4     Harmiysis son of Ploution, (grandson) of Thenteos,
5     having come back in from withdrawal (flight),
6     for the 32nd year: of beer-tax four drachmas half-obol 2 chalki, of poll-tax
7     sixteen drachmas, of pig-tax one drachma four obols,
8     of epch(?) an obol, of provision-money for the watchtower-guards and other assessments seven drachmas, of dike-tax
9     two drachmas four obols. For the 31st year: of beer-tax
10    four drachmas half-obol 2 chalki, of poll-tax sixteen drachmas,
11    of pig-tax one drachma four obols, of epch(?) an obol,
12    of provision-money for the watchtower-guards and other
13    assessments six drachmas half-obol, of dike-tax seven drachmas half-obol.
14    For the 30th year: of beer-tax four drachmas half-obol 2 chalki,
15    of poll-tax sixteen drachmas, of pig-tax one
16    drachma four obols, of epch(?) an obol, of provision-money
17    for the watchtower-guards and other assessments
18    six drachmas half-obol, of dike-tax seven drachmas half-obol.
19    For the 29th year: of beer-tax four drachmas half-obol 2 chalki,
20    of poll-tax sixteen drachmas, of pig-tax
21    one drachma four obols, of epch(?) an obol,
22    of provision-money for the watchtower-guards and other assessments
23    six drachmas half-obol, of crown-money from an advance five obols
24    half-obol 1 chalkon, of dike-tax seven drachmas half-obol.
25    Aphrodas,
26    I have signed.
