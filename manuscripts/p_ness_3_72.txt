[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God almighty: Abou Rased counselor
2     to Georgios of Nesanoi
3     I give thanks to God. Then [when] coming
4     to you Abou Almuger maule of the Eria
5     ben Abi Sufian, [you will] be willing to provide
6     [to] him a man from Nesanoi owing
7     to guide him the way of the holy
8     mountain and provide to him his wages
9     His. It was written in [the] month of Martios, indiction 12, by [the] hand of Theodore
