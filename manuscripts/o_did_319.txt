[TRANSLATION]
1     Iulis to Valerius the brother greetings. I sent
2     to you letters four
3     through the [helmsman] of that one from Didyma and
4     through Cornelius the helmsman others three so [that] six. If you received, write back how many you received; and I sent to you one. a stater
5     an exchangeable stater send in order that I give back, and the pen-case since alien
6     it is. You will forgive, I do not have frankincense, of Sertorious
7     it is. Greet Antonia and Klemes.
8     if you depart, write to me.
9     Tybi twenty-nine.
