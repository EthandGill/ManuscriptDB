[TRANSLATION]
1     Dioscorus son of Hermaeus, Hermopolite, to Mitokos and
2     Ho[ri]on both sons of Hermaeus and Asclepiades son of Callstratus
3     Hermopolites, greetings. I acknowledge willingly
4     to have received and [to have stood surety for] Menander son of Dionysius, village-scribe of Senkirkeos and Tachoi and other villages of the Leucopyrgeites below, whom I will also present
5     whenever you should wish; but if I should not [pre]sent [him]
6     I …, the [matters] concerning hi[m] with me standing surety [to] you, I will receive the guarantee. Year 2 …
7     Emperor [Caesar] of Trajan Ha[dri]a[n]
8     [August]us Mecheis 15.
