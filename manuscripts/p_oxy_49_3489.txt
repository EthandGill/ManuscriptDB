[TRANSLATION]
1     Theon son of Theon son of Theon, of those from
2     [Oxy]rhynchus city, leased to Iollas son of Pnepheros
3     son of Iollas, of those from the vil[la]ge of Peretnoueus
4     of the [K]ynopolite (nome), a Persian of the descent, for
5     two y[e]ars, two waterings, from the present
6     fifth year of the Emperor Caesar
7     Vespasian Augustus, the (land) belonging to his
8     wife Apollonous daughter of Sarapion around Thmoithothis of the upper toparchy,
9     out of nine arouras, from the northern part
10    five arouras, so that these in the present year are to be sown with vetch, the rent for each
11    aroura being two and a quarter artabas of wheat,
12    and in the coming year to be sow[n] with wheat, the rent
13    for each aroura being seven artabas of [whea]t.
14    And the lessee shall give to Theon yearly
15    a quarter and an eighth of an artaba of loaves, the yearly water-guarding of the arouras being
16    the responsibility of the lessee, the yearly rents being free from all risk. And if any (land)
17    in the coming year becomes unwatered, there shall be allowed to the lessee the
18    rent of that (land); and if anything is exacted from the lessee for the public treasury or for anything else on behalf of Theon or of the wife or of the land, let it be reckoned against
19    the rents; and of the yearly crops
20    let Theon be lord until he recovers the rents.
21    The lease being guaranteed, let
22    the lessee pay to Theon the yearly rents always in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor around the hamlet of Archibios, wheat new, clean, unadulterated,
23    …, by the four-choinix measure of Sarapion …
24    … [, let him p]ay him each …
25    Year 5: lease of Apollonous against Iollas.
