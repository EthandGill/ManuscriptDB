[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ammonios, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome.
2     From Longinia Nemesilla, a Roman and an Antinoite, through Herakleides son of Diogenes. Still
3     long ago I divided, by public deed through the record-office, with my sister Longinia Lucia the
4     property belonging to us jointly in equal shares, around the village Philadelphia, of cleruchic allotment, two and a half arouras,
5     and in the village a house and courtyard and an upper-storey vaulted room, which is a gateway, the entrance and exit of the house and courtyard, and around the village Hephaistias, of allotment
6     cleruchic, one aroura; and from the division that took place
7     it was allotted to me both all the building-plots in the village an[d] the one aroura around Hephaistias,
8     and to Longinia Lucia likewise was allotted the two and a half arouras of cleruchic allotment around Philadelphia. Since therefore, wishing to build some part of the
9     building-plots of mine through … I was not
10    allowed by the aforewritten Lo[ngi]nia Lucia … to take away the care … the
11    just rights of the [divisio]n, I submit (this petition) and req[uest] …
12    … if it seem good, to send instructions to the village-scribe of the village,
13    so that, having received from each the just dues, he may restore to me my own
14    and I may be helped …
15    Year 30 of Aurelius [Com]modus Antoninus Caesar the lord.
