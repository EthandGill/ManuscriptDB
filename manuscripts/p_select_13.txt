[TRANSLATION]
1     To Septimius Flavianus, chief magistrate of the Herakleopolite nome,
2     from Aurelia Maura, daughter of Theodoros, from the village of
3     Peensamoi of the 13th pagus of the same nome. Since Julianus,
4     son of Nemesion, my fellow-villager and cohabitant, has approached
5     your diligence, accusing me concerning outrage, and your [fortune],
6     acting in accordance with the laws, having summoned me,
7     exhorted (me) no longer to be in dispute with him, therefore
8     I acknowledge, swearing the divine and revered oath
9     of our lords Honorius and Theodosius, the eternal
10    Augusti, that from henceforth I shall not be at variance with my aforementioned
11    cohabitant Julianus, nor dispute over
12    any chance pretext; but if indeed, he having begun (it) first, I should suffer outrage, that I of necessity shall come up
13    and make it known to the laws and to your equity, without delay;
14    and if I should be shown to have sworn falsely, I shall be liable to the payment of two …
15    and to the peril hanging over me on this account; and having been formally questioned,
16    I gave my assent.
17    After the consulship of our lord Theodosius the eternal
18    Augustus for the 9th time and of Flavius Constantius the most illustrious for the 3rd time, Epeiph 1.
19    I, Aurelia Maura, daughter of Theodoros, the aforesaid, have sworn the divine oath
20    no longer to dispute with Julianus concerning any single matter,
21    as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Ioannes son of Timotheos, wine-seller,
22    having been requested, wrote on her behalf, she being present and illiterate.
23    (She) being (illiterate).
24    Through me, Artemidoros, it was written.
25    Bond of Maura, from Peensamoi.
