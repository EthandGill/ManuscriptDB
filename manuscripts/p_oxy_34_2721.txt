[TRANSLATION]
1     There acknowledge to one another Aurelii Ptollion son of Barbaros and Heras son of Heras, both presidents of the men feasting
2     in the village of Nesmeimis, and Antinoos son of Hermias, chief piper and director for this purpose of three pipers and one
3     castanet-dancing girl: those around Ptollion, that they have received Antinoos with all
4     the band (orchestra), to perform service for the
5     feasting men for four festival days from the eleventh of the next month Hathyr of the present year,
6     at a wage each day of fifty drachmas
7     and twelve pairs of loaves
8     and two kotylai of radish-oil,
9     apart from what is required for the
10    lampadary, and one portion (?), and the
11    customary attendance, and for all the
12    days one keramion of wine, all things
13    being pure; and from the wages
14    Antinoos has received there, on account of earnest-money, twenty drachmas; and
15    this man together with the others they shall take, barring divine compulsion, from the Oxyrhynchite nome by three donkeys, and shall restore (him) to the village, and
16    shall furnish them also lodging
17    that is secure and free from molestation;
18    and after the four days,
19    having paid them all the wages and all extras in full reckoning,
20    they shall restore (them) on the 15th, fully complete (?), to the same Oxyrhynchite by the same
21    three donkeys, safely and faithfully; and Antinoos consents to all the aforesaid; the agreement, written in duplicate, is valid. The 14th year
22    of the Emperor Caesar
23    Marcus Aurelius Severus
24    Alexander, pious, fortunate,
25    Augustus; Phaophi 13.
26    I, Aurelius Antinoos, consent
27    to all the aforesaid.
