[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity, the Father
2     and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Written in the month Hathyr 23, indiction 3.
3     Before the most illustrious and honoured lord Komes
4     son of the blessed Chael, the administrator (dioiketes) of the fortress (kastron)
5     of Djeme.
6     I, Talia daughter of the blessed Paham — her mother is Archontia — of the fortress of Djeme of the nome of the city of Hermonthis, acting on behalf
7     of all my brethren according to their names, and managing their affairs in all matters, I am to give afterwards a subscriber to subscribe on my behalf
8     to this written deed of sale, which is not to be transgressed and
9     not to be shaken aside from the laws,
10    and moreover to confirm it through trustworthy witnesses, those who shall testify to it on my behalf according to my request of my own self and my commission. I write to you,
11    you Aaron son of the blessed Senouthios, the man
