4     of the city, one hundred and fifty-six artabas of wheat, total 156 artabas.
5     Aurelius … alias Alexandros,
6     I have signed for the one hundred and fifty-six artabas of wheat.
7     There has been measured out and delivered, of wheat, artabas of the produce of the present 10th year

10    Hierakapollon alias Epimachos, ex-gymnasiarch, through Dionysios his son,
11    the (artabas) through Pisbk the farmer, of Kerkethyris, of the city,
12    thirty-two artabas of wheat, total 32 artabas.
13    Aurelius Antonios the grain-collector, through Aurelius Tryphon alias Diogenes, I have signed.
14    There has been measured out and delivered, of wheat, artabas of the produce of the
15    present 10th year of Marcus Aurelius Severus
16    Alexander Caesar the lord, through the grain-collectors of the upper
17    toparchy, of the district of Sko, Herakleia daughter of Diogenes, of Sko,

20    eighty-one and a half, total 81½ artabas,
