[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy, life-giving, consubstantial Trinity,
2     of the Father and Son and Holy Spirit. Written in the month Pachon 13, indiction 3.
3     Before the most honored and honored Samuel the son
4     of the late Ienoch, and Zekiel the son of the late Matthias,
5     the administrators of the fortress (of) Djeme, the nome of the city
6     of Ermont (Hermonthis): I, Daniel the son of the late Pachom,
7     the man of the fortress (of) Djeme, the nome of the city of Ermont,
8     will provide afterwards a subscriber to subscribe
9     for me, with witnesses who are trustworthy, that they may witness
10    to it for me, according to my own request, and (of) my will of heart; and afterwards I searched in my heart, I made the remembrance
11    of all my fathers; I said that men do not remain
12    upon the earth; and I said that "I do not know the day
13    of my death," according to the way they said to Adam: "Adam,
14    you are earth (and) to earth you shall return." Afterwards
15    I gave myself to this testament and deed of sale of a will,
16    this which is set down, which is written, not to transgress against it and not to alter it, rather it being (made) and confirmed, and
17    not to annul it, because I gave myself to it,
18    being sober (and) walking upon the earth, my mind established,
19    my reasoning confirmed. I write to you, Christotote my beloved
20    son, true (legitimate) son: you shall command my house, this which came
21    upon me from my blessed father Pachom, from its foundations to its tops,
22    this which is set down on the lane of the holy Apa Victor,
23    according to its ancient positions. The boundaries of that same house are these:
24    north, the house of Athanasios the husband (of) Katharon your sister;
25    south, the new house which I, Daniel, built;
26    east, the house of the sons of (?) Patape; west, the ancient gate,
27    and the public lane: behold, these are the boundaries of that same house
28    from its foundations to its tops; from now, then, (it is) to you, you,
29    Christotoros my son, that no (other) of (my) children among my children may come forth against you concerning this house of this sort for ever,
30    because it came upon me too, from my blessed father;
31    and I said too that I do not have another male child
32    besides this one only; I gave it to him. And if Marehom your sister or Papnoute should produce a document
33    against you, you, Christotote my son, it shall be invalid.
34    And afterwards you shall bring suit against them concerning the house which I built for them
35    with the (?) in my labors, because they brought nothing
36    to me concerning them ever.
37    From now, then, (it is) to you, Christotote my beloved son,
38    that you shall come in and become lord of the whole house which I formerly declared to you above, and you shall beget it for yourself
39    and you shall again beget it for yourself, you shall leave it to your children
40    and your children's children, simply, you shall use it in every manner that is yours, (at) any time.
41    The one who shall come forth against you for ever (at any) time, he shall become
42    a stranger to the holy creed of the Christians, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit; and
43    afterwards let him give the reckoning of the fine which the law
44    fixes for it, which is eighty-two solidi;
45    and let them be made to come in
46    and confirm this testament of this sort, because
47    they read it to me, (and) I heard it, it became (pleasing) to me, I confirmed it
48    with a subscription and witnesses; I turn (with) my feet (?)
49    (and) I issued it forth complete according to every procedure,
50    it being clear that the door which opens southward to the house shall open southward, not closing (it) up. I, Daniel the son of Pachom,
51    he who formerly wrote on top, consent to this document
52    according to the way it is written.
53    I, Gennadios the son of Konstantinos, give witness, and I wrote for Daniel because he does not (know how) to write.
54    I, Antonios the son of (?), give witness;
55    Gennadios — I wrote for him because he does not (know how) to write.
56    I, Athanasios the son of the late Ananias, give witness. I, Phoibammon his son, wrote for him because he does not (know how) to write,
57    (and) I give witness.
58    I, Aristophanios the son of the late Philotheos,
59    give witness, at the request of Daniel.
60    Drawn up through me, Souai (son) of the blessed Philotheos, I wrote (it).
