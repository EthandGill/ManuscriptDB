4     of the 44th (sic) year, 30 drachmas; and the provision-money for Phamenoth, Pharmouthi
5     (and) Pachons, having paid it to him, enter as a balance of wheat, 60 artabas, 62 drachmas 3 obols; of wool, 15 minas, 20 drachmas,

8     8 choes, 5 drachmas; to Sostratos, for 1 (jar?) of banotos, 5 drachmas; makes 103 drachmas 3 obols.
