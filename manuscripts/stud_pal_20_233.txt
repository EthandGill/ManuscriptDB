[TRANSLATION]
1     Four artabae of clean wheat you-will-prepare for me from evening, your god-loved (one), and ten fine grain-fed (animals)
2     and fifty [items] and ten [items]; for I want these through tomorrow by the account of a most necessary friend; but tell the
3     lady that she prepare for herself another place; for the lady Marous is coming now and has to seek the places which she knows
4     thus therefore tell to her.
5     to Phoibammon, the most god-loved [one] in all things worthy of honor, deacon and chartulary,
6     Hypatios
