[TRANSLATION]
1     [copy, year 31 ] …
2     To Tothoes, controller (antigrapheus) of the granary about [the City ]
3     If [the (agents)] of Leonides, the one appointed over the grain-collection (sitologia),
4     load (grain) so as (to send) to Alexandria, to the royal (treasury),
5     from the produce of the [3]1st year, according to the dispatch from Sarapion the (official) over the
6     revenues, into the boats of th[em] subscribed below
7     of (carrying-)capacity 4000, sent up from the City by Herakleides
8     and Herodes, the (officials) over the pulse, for the receiving of wheat clean
9     unadulterated, sifted, by the receiving-measure (metron dochikon) which (corresponds) [to] the bronze (standard)
10    and by a just leveling-rod (skytalē) … guards being put aboard, holding cleruchies, those especially having trust, to whom also the sealed sample (deigma)
11    will be put on, in earthen unbaked vessels,
12    and they will convey, from the (official) over the grain(-account) of the selection,
13    the appropriate dispatches, together with the (consent) of [Her]mias and
14    Syrion, the dagger-bearers (machaira-phoroi) and furnishers about the administration (dioikēsis),
15    of judg[m]ent: artabas of wheat 4000, makes of wheat artabas 4000; compute as is customary,
16    and make a token (symbolon) and counter-token (antisymbolon) with them a[s] is fitting;
17    … in (the boat) of Mareotic (type) of Hermias, of capacity 2400, of wheat [artabas] 2000, in another of Syrio[n]
18    of capacity 2400, of wheat [artabas] 2000, makes 2 Mareotic (boats) of capacity 4000, of wheat [artabas] 4000;
19    artab[as] …
