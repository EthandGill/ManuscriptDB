[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord father
2     Annumerios, Petauris
3     Overseer of the fortresses of Hibeos
4     [Of the] thirteenth indiction, greetings.
5     Give to [the] brother Josephios the prince
6     [Of] grain, one Italian modius, and
7     [Of] barley, six Italian modia
8     [They] total [as] grain-and-barley, seven modia. I have signed.
9     The same Petauris
10    Overseer of the 13th indiction.
