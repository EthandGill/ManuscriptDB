r.2    διὰ Μηνᾶ σοι Θεοδώρῳ

r.5    ἀφόβως καὶ θεοῦ κελευόντος

[TRANSLATION]
1     With god, Flavius Atias, most glorious dux,
2     through Mena, to you Theodore,
3     conducting [yourself]; holding the authority
4     enter into your own city
5     fearlessly and, god commanding,
6     I will not allow you to be wronged
7     but I ensure you your right
8     … do not doubt.
