[TRANSLATION]
1     Leon to Apollonius greetings. [Know the] … [of the]
2     royal land being in the pre... … of the treasuries. We are delayed therefore in the receipt because [neither]
3     Demetrius the grain-buyer neither those with him [having met] having the measures. So that therefore you not [be] under blame
4     I have written to you so that you know. I have also sent [to Demetrius]
5     concerning these things. You will do well [having sent] [to us the]
6     land-survey according to the leaf so that from [the complete] [we reckon]
7     towards the farmers. Be well!
8     Year 7, Pharmouthi 7. To Apollonius.
9     From Le[on].
