[TRANSLATION]
1     Amatios [and] Statianus son of Heron having served as exegete
2     having served as gymnasiarch and … to Arsites Apollinarius, greetings.
3     I received which Petesouchos deposited with you
4     to my account on behalf of which the father [of him] Zosimus Soucha of Samba owes
5     for [philanthropic] on account of money
6     220 drachmas become drachmas 220, [remaining] to me the account concerning both the [principal] and the remaining philanthropic contributions
7     year 4 of Lucius Septimius Severus
8     Pious Pertinax Augustus
9     [Ar]a[bi]c Adiabeni[c] Phamenoth 30
