r.1    Ἄρχων Πλουτάρχου Πέρσης τῆς ἐπιγονῆς· ὁμολογῶ

[TRANSLATION]
1     Archon [of] Plutarch, Persian of the epigone: I acknowledge
2     to have purchased from the royal allotment of Apollonius
3     of bad-growing chickling-vetch six-and-a-half arouras
4     at four drachmas per aroura, which comes to altogether drachmas
5     twenty-six, which I will place upon [a] table when the accounting
6     of the green [vegetables] comes to pass
