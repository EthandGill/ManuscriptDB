[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Didymos also called Isidoros,
2     Antinoite,
3     from the Aurelii Protas son of Philadelphos son of P[at]ron, his mother being Taesis,
4     and Fronto son of Sisois son of Akous,
5     his mother being Tisais, both
6     from the village of Kerkesephis
7     of the division of Polemon. We wish willingly to lease the lands belonging to you and to
8     others around the aforesaid
9     village of Kerkesephis, of the catoecic
10    allotment, six arouras or however many
11    there may be, for four years from the
12    present 5th year, at a rent each year, unsown, of wheat
13    twenty-four artabas by the four-choinix measure, by the dromos
14    of Akous the cultivator, and on behalf of young shoots each year one keramion of cheese, free of deduction and free of risk, and it shall not be permitted to us within the time
15    to abandon the lease in any manner, and we shall perform all the yearly works at
16    our own expense at the proper seasons, and we shall render the rent to you in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor of the village of Kerkesephis, the measurement
17    being made by you the
18    landlords, all the public dues being
19    to you the landlords, and
20    the transport-charge of the public dues being to
21    us the lessees, and after
22    the time we shall hand over
23    the arouras clean of rush,
24    reed, dog's-tooth grass, dirt
25    of every kind, if it seems good to lease. We, Aurelius Didymos and the rest,
26    have leased, as is set forth above. Year 5
27    of the Emperor …
