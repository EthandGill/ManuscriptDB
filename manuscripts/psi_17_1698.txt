[TRANSLATION]
1     Augustus and Emperor Caesar [Lucius Aurelius]
2     Verus Augustus, Phaophi 4. Harsiesis, son of Panechotes, I have leased the land for
3     four years of rent; of the first [year]
4     and third year, [I will pay] assessed per year in grain
5     of five artabas, but of the other two years
6     [I will pay] assessed per year in grain of artabas …
7     sixteen and I will pay as set forth. Plution, also called Eu[tych]es, freedman of Sarapion
8     and [Diogenes wrote] for [him], [he] not knowing
