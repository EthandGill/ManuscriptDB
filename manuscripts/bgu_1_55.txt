[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Col. I of [NN (woman)] alias Am[– – – of – – –]onios, katoikos [– – – A]phrodite [– – – of – – –]melis and of Zosimos and Herakleides the house-by-house registration of Mysthes of Pasion (Remains of three lines) Col. II from my deceased wife Aphrodite, known also amongst some as Aphroditous, freedwoman of Herakleides and Sabinus and the other children, who live there, Mysthes alias Ninnos, 33 years old, etc. and the wife of my son Mysthes alias Ninnos, Zosime, freedwoman of Ammonarion, who is the daughter of Marion, son of Geoum , daughter of a katoikos, 22 years old, registered with her mistress in the registration of year 9, still being in the register in the Helleneionquarter and now transferred to the quarter of the Hawk shrine of Apollonios [and] the children of both of them Ammonios 5 years old and Didymos 4 years old and [– – –] who have not been registered in the (list of) those born since then. Year 1 of Imperator Caesar M[arcus] Aurelius Antoninus Augustus and Imperator Caesar L[ucius] Aurelius Verus Augustus, Payni the thirtieth. From another (registration). To Potamon, strategos of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division, and Asklepiades, royal [scribe], and Agathos Daimon and Dioskoros, scribes of the metropolis, from Mysthes alias [Ninnos], son of Mysthes, the son of Philon, mother Herais, daughter of Ammonios, katoikos of the [6475]] registered in the quarter of Hawk shrine of Apollonios. There belongs to me in the Ammonios quarter the twelfth part of the place called of Nekpherotios, in which I register myself and my (family) for the house-by-house registration of the past 14th year, in which I registered in the house-by-house registration of year 23 of the God An[toninus] as well. And I am My[s]thes alias Ninnos, the aforementioned, [47] years old and] (I register) my wife Zo[s]ime, freedwoman of A[mmon]arion, daughter of Mario[n, registered] in the house-by-house registration [of year 23] in the same quarter, 38 years old, and the children of both of us [NN] not registered in the (list of) those born since then, 11 years old and Dioskoros likewise, [10 years old and NN] likewise, 9 years old, and our daughter Isidora, 8 years old. Therefore I submit (this registration). Year 15 of Au[relius] Antoninus Caesar, the lord. Mesore intercalary day 2. (m. 2) we make to the state scribe of the above mentioned (m. 3) of the above mentioned parents. (m. 4) Ammonios, son of Ammonios, the son of St[otoetis][].
