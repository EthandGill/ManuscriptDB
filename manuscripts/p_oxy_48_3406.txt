r.1    παρὰ Κληματίης γεούχου
r.2    Παπνουτίῳ προνοητῇ Σαδάλου χαίρειν.

r.6    ἀνένεγκον διὰ τοῦ πάκτωνος καὶ μὴ ἀμελήσητε διὰ τὴν ὀπτόπλινθον.

r.8    καὶ τὰ μελικηρίδια καὶ Παγᾶν τὸ ἔριον.

[TRANSLATION]
1     From Klematios landholder
2     to Papnutios, manager of Sadalos, greetings.
3     Measure out into the factoryship of Pagas
4     of grain-sacks six so that we may have it here,
5     and help Pagas so that we may have there [things] exceptional of the vintage, and rope and wool
6     bring [them] up through the factory and do not neglect [them] because of the baked-brick.
7     and demand from Paumin the two jars of honey because of the feast
8     and the honeycomb-disks and from Pagas the wool.
