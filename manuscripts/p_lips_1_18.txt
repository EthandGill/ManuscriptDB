[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Paes[i]s and Olympios son of Silvanus and
2     Ausonios also called Kleanthes, town-councillor, beyond the
3     number, victor in the sacred games, and however he is styled, to one another
4     greeting. We agree, willingly and of our own free choice, to cultivate jointly with one another, in common, in respect of one-third, the (lands) belonging to me,
5     Ausonios, around Thrage and Bounon of Kleopatra, twelve and a quarter arouras, and of Paulos son of Plokamos, three arouras, all of them,
6     in respect of one-half, for two years, the rent of the twelve (arouras) being, for the (land) in wheat, of each aroura, [six] arta[b]as
7     of wheat, and for the (land) in fodder-crops and other (crops), of each
8     aroura, twelve hundred drachmas; and of the
9     three arouras, for the (land) in wheat, five artabas and
10    of silver, … one thousand drach[mas]; and the rent that is collected we shall [necessar]ily measure out and re[store in] the months Pauni and Epeiph
11    at the threshing-floors [of the me]tropolis, by the Athenian m[ea]sure,
12    [acknowledging in com]mon, equally, both the [ex]penses
13    [and all the public-taxe]s; and likewise we shall take [in respect of one-th]ird the produce
14    [in respect of one-half.] The lease-agreement is valid, and
15    [having been formally questioned, we acknowledged (it).] In the consulship of our lords,
16    [Diocletian]us the father of the
