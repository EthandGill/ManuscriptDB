[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Phoibammon, son of blessed Kyros, [declare to] you, Ouennaphrios, son of Ple, from Kalophanos, concerning the four arouras
2     which you brought [upon me for] the produce(s) of the 7th indiction, and only
3     from nomismata three [and] carats twelve of Alexandria for now
4     already nomismata three, carats seven. Of the month Phaophi
