[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Maximos, diastoleus (distributor),
2     through me Aunes, assistant, I have received
3     from you, Heroninos, steward of Theadelphia, 142 Oxyrhynchite (jars) of wine, and to Palas,
4     steward, 80 Oxyrhynchite (jars).
5     Year 13 of our lord Gallienus
6     Augustus, Thoth 30.
7     Likewise, Phaophi 14, by camels,
8     there was hauled up to the city, of wine,
9     one hundred and two Oxyrhynchite (jars),
10    total 102, forty two-choos (jars), total 40 two-choos (jars).
11    Likewise, (Phaophi) 16, by camels, of the good-quality wine, one hundred
12    fourteen Oxyrhynchite (jars), total 114, six two-choos (jars), total 6.
13    Likewise, (Phaophi) 18, by camels, of the good-quality
14    wine, seventy-three Oxyrhynchite (jars),
15    twenty-six two-choos (jars), total 73 Oxyrhynchite (jars), 26 two-choos (jars).
16    Likewise, (Phaophi) 21, of wine 42 Oxyrhynchite (jars), 34 two-choos (jars).
17    Likewise, (Phaophi) 23, by camels,
18    of the good-quality wine, two-choos (jars)
19    26, Oxyrhynchite (jars) 46, one-choos (jars) 14,
20    and on the account of Maximos to Isidoros, of wine, fifty two-choos (jars), fifty one-choos (jars), total 50 two-choos (jars), 50 one-choos (jars).
21    Likewise, (Phaophi) 29, of wine, one hundred Oxyrhynchite (jars), six two-choos (jars), total 100 Oxyrhynchite (jars), 6 two-choos (jars).
22    Likewise, (Phaophi) 28, 82 Oxyrhynchite (jars), one one-choos (jar),
23    total 82, one one-choos (jar).
24    There amount to the same (grand total) of the wine hauled up to the city, plain, one thousand five hundred and four one-choos (jars)
25    and a half, total 1,504½.
26    Likewise, (Phaophi) 30, by camels,
27    of wine, one hundred and eight two-choos (jars),
28    total 108 two-choos (jars).
29    Likewise, [Hathyr] 2, of wine,
30    sixty-two two-choos (jars),
31    total 62 two-choos (jars).
