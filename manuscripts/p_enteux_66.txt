[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting, from Maron son of Euktos, an Argive of the epigone. I am being wronged by Theodosios son of Xanthos, a Lycian of the epigone. For my son Euktos having leased jointly with him a vacant plot in Autodike for the 99th year from Artemon, and
2     having died before the common plot was divided, Theodosios encroached and built for himself the walls of a dwelling,
3     having picked out the best and outermost spot; and after this, when a division was made and a contract was deposited that it had been divided equally and alike, it has not been divided fairly to me, but he has cheated me and a plot
4     useless and narrow lengthwise he has given me, while he himself has his own part in a square shape. I therefore beg you,
5     O king, if it please you, to order Diophanes the strategos to write to Pythiades the epistates that, going
6     to the place, he divide it for me equally and alike, and the door which he built in my part, half-finished,
7     to compel him to turn it back into his own part, and, since he has encroached upon me seventy cubits according to
8     area, that there be given me in return for them an entrance and exit up to our common road, because I have been thrown back to the inner part. For this being done, through you, O king, I shall have obtained justice.
9     Farewell.
10    To Pythiades. Best of all, settle them; but if not, send them to us by the 10th of Choiach so that before the proper
11    court they may be adjudged. Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 29.
12    Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 29.
13    Maron son of Euktos against Theodosios
14    concerning entrance and exit.
