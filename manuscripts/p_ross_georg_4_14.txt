[TRANSLATION]
1     concerning accounts of various assigned [items] of Aphrodito …
2     In the name of God. Korrah ben Szerich, councillor
3     to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodit[o]. We give thanks
4     to God and then, we having examined the things assigned
5     through your administration by our orders in the eighth indiction
6     in the account of various public items and others we found it/her
7     remaining/defaulting to the subordinate knowledge …
8     Korrah ben Szerich, councillor, to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito.
9     month Phaophi […] of [the indiction] was brought through Abdella, son of Nafe, concerning remaining accounts.
