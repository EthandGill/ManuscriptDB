[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonios, strategos of the Apollonopolite (nome)
2     of the seven villages,
3     from Psais, village-scribe of Naboo. Being questioned by you concerning the fields within the embankment registered around
4     my village-scribe's district which have been flooded
5     and those which can be furrowed,
6     I report as set out below.
7     There are collected, of those within the embankment
8     registered to Naboo around
9     my village-scribe's district, 668½ ¹⁄₁₆ ¹⁄₃₂ ¹⁄₆₄ arouras,
10    of which of royal (land) at 5 12 (artabas?), 2½ ¹⁄₁₆ ¹⁄₃₂ arouras, at 4 12,
11    1¾ ⅛ ¹⁄₃₂ aroura, at 3 12 [arouras] 55½ ¹⁄₁₆, at 2½ 12 [arouras] 35 ¹⁄₁₆ ¹⁄₃₂ ¹⁄₆₄,
12    at 2 12 arouras 127 ¹⁄₁₆, total of royal (land) 222 ⅛ ¹⁄₁₆ ¹⁄₃₂ ¹⁄₆₄.
13    Of other royal (land) registered in the class of privately owned,
14    at 1½ 12 arouras 13½, of privately owned at 1 12 arouras 15½ ⅛ ¹⁄₁₆, ¾ 6 arouras 87 ¹⁄₁₆,
15    1 4 pol(?) arouras 29½ ⅛, of catoecic arouras 237 ¾ ⅛ ¹⁄₁₆ ¹⁄₆₄,
16    of purchased arouras 38 4 ¹⁄₁₆, total of administered private land arouras 422 ⅛ ¹⁄₆₄
17    total of administered royal and private land arouras 644 ¼ ⅛.
18    Of sacred (land) leased out arouras 8,
19    of sacred (land) of the Ibis-shrine arouras 13 ⅛ ¹⁄₁₆ ¹⁄₃₂ ¹⁄₆₄, at 2½ (of) barley 2½ arouras [3]
20    total of sacred (land) arouras 24 ⅛ ¹⁄₁₆ ¹⁄₃₂ ¹⁄₆₄ total of administered and sacred (land)
21    the aforesaid arouras 668½ ¹⁄₁₆ ¹⁄₃₂ ¹⁄₆₄,
22    of which flooded arouras 402 ¼ ¹⁄₃₂,
23    able to be furrowed arouras 260 ¼ ¹⁄₆₄,
24    unwatered arouras 6 ¹⁄₁₆, total the aforesaid.
25    Of which the (account) by individual is as follows:
26    beginning from the west of the village:
27    Thatres son of Onnophris, pol(?) 3½, at 2 12 1½,
28    of catoecic ½, total arouras 5½, of which flooded 1, able
29    to be furrowed 4½.
30    Haronnophris son of Pachompsais and Puoris
31    his brother, at 3 12 ⅛, at 2 12 ¾, of catoecic ⅛, total 1,
32    of which flooded ¼, able to be furrowed ¾.
33    Panesneus son of Pekysis, at 2½ 12 ¾, flooded.
34    Pkames son of Orsenouphis, of catoecic 2 ¼,
35    of which flooded ½, able to be furrowed 1¾.
