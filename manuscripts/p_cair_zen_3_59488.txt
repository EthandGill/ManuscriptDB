[TRANSLATION]
1     Paramonos to Zenon, greetings. I did not remember to instruct you while you were at home concerning scrapers.
2     Buy then for me, for they are cheap in Memphis—adult ones 6, and child ones 6 of the
3     Sikyonian-made [ones] because [they are] most pure; and of the caper three-and-a-half artabas of the dried, not old,
4     but [of] whatever [is] newest.
5     Fare well.
6     To Zenon from
7     Paramonos.
