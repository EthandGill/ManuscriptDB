1     When Ptolemy son of Ptolemy was reigning and Queen Cleopatra [sister] and Queen Cleopatra [wife]...
2     Gods Benefactors, year 38; Mechir 1. A plot was measured in the newly-founded city Euergeti[s, by Boethus of the]...
3     kinsman and commander-of-troops and general of the Thebaid and founder, through Paniscus, land-measurer and [scribe]...
4     Tanoupei Theophios, the [plot] arranged in the rectangular wine-shop as [a] dwelling, [the area] not being assessed [nor] for the circular tax, nor for any other matter whatsoever, but [to have rightfully the]...
5     [private] possession; and it will be permitted to him to leave [it] to descendants and [his] own wife and to those nearest in kinship [or whomever he may choose]...
6     in the city, [those] being carried [in military service] similarly, nothing being assessed either for those receiving [it] or for those transferring [it], according to [no provision]...
7     contrivance, in accordance with [those] allowed by the founder, [at] two solid cubits, [making] cubits 2, of which the measure[ments from south to north, cubits 20,]...
8     west to east, cubits 10; neighbors to the south Petosieris, goldsmith, to the north the remaining places, to the east [the stoa, west] street of 10 cubits.
9     Paniscus has measured in accordance with what is set forth, [the plot] to Tanoupei Theophios the wine-shop [in the]...
10    rectangular marketplace, cubits, [making] cubits 2. Year 38, Mech[ir 1.]
