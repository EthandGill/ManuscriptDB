1     … and of the Holy Spirit, of the holy life-giving and
2     [con]substantial Trinity in unity. Written in the month of the Epagomenal (days), 1st, of the seventh indiction.
3     [Eno]ch the soldier, the man of the village of Tjkooou, writes:
4     … the all-praiseworthy [lo]rd Abdalla son of Abdelmelech
5     … the most glorious lord, the lord Epimachos the illustrious …
6     … with his hamlets and with his plains (fields) …
7     … a solidus (holokottinos) of gold and with the contra[ct] …
8     … that which shall come to pass … to us
9     … but now I acknowledge (homologein) that the amount (poson)
10    … the monetization (apargyrismos) for the workers of this village, one share
11    … the monetization (apargyrismos)
12    … the third-solidus (trimesion) which you gave into my hands, and the share
13    … [they] sent them up north for the share of workers
14    … is able to come out from this work, this (which)
15    … with them according to the strength (terms) of the order (epistalma)
16    … the governor (symboulos), these (things) which he sent to us in this year, this
17    … [the] amount (poson) of gold therefore, this village, I acknowledge (homologein)
18    … ready therefore
19    … nor again …
20    … to go to the outer country, after the men
21    … [Athana]sios the man of the same village of Tjk[ooou]
22    … the public account (demosios logos) is established therefore
23    … to agree (stoichein) to it through the one who writes
24    … [the] whole [su]bstance (hypostasis) for this work, I swearing by the name
25    … of the lords who hold (power), that I will keep guard
26    … [they] questioned me, I acknowledged (homologein)
27    … the acknowledgment (homologia)
28    … that the two, one cos (?) of a solidus (holokottinos), come to solidi 2½ of gold
29    … which you gave to me into my hands, and
30    … for the share of workers which you sent
31    … the least (elachistos) of the elders (presbyteros), what I said to him, I wrote concerning it
32    … [I] bear witness that this acknowledgment (homologia)
33    … I, Phoibammon son of the blessed (makarios) one
34    … [the] way I heard it from those who draw it up
35    … this acknowledgment (homologia), according to its strength
36    … [Mak]are, I bear witness that this acknowledgment (homologia)
37    … the fifty third-solidi (trimesia) which came to the hands of Menas
38    … [they] sent it up north for the share of workers
39    … the practor (?); I bear witness that this acknowledgment (homologia), according to its stren[gth]
