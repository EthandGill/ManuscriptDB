[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Petermouthes, chief farmer
2     of Aurelius Paniscus the elder, to Aurelius
3     Psenpes … thi, poultry-raiser, greetings
4     Give to Callimachus, gardener, [for] 5 arouras for
5     harvest [work for] 2 days, hens two
6     total hens 2, and eggs twenty, total eggs 20
7     year 7, Mesore 15, I wrote on his behalf
