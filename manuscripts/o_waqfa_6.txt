[TRANSLATION]
1     To the beloved brother
2     To Victor, [son of] Panos, Victor
3     Greetings. Give to the brother
4     To Dios, [of] barley [by] land-measure, one artaba
5     [one], it amounts to [of] barley [by] land-measure artaba 1, I pray you be well.
6     I have signed, I [myself], 8th indiction.
