[TRANSLATION]
1   (Sheet.)
2   To Protarchos.
3   From Thermoutharion daughter of Eirenaios, a citizen, with her guardian Hermias
4   son of Athenodoros, and from Gaius Ignatius Maximus. Gaius
5   [I]gnatius Maximus agrees to provide his slave-woman Chrotarion
6   to rear and suckle, with her own milk, pure and
7   untainted, outside at Gaius' own (house) in the city, for a period of
8   [te]n months from Hathyr of the present twenty-sixth year of Caesar,
9   (to nurse the child) which Thermoutharion has entrusted to her - (the child) of her freedwoman
10   Kallityche, a female infant named Pollarous - because
11   Kallityche, having fallen ill, her
12   milk has spoiled; at the fixed wage for the milk and rearing,
13   with oil, of ten drachmas a month; and Gaius has received from
14   Thermoutharion, by hand from the house, the sum amounting for the (months) from
15   Hathyr to Tybi, three months' nursing-fees, thirty drachmas;
16   Gaius paying regularly the [nursing-fees] from the month Mecheir,
17   (he is) to provide the slave-woman from now on, taking [both her own]
18   and the infant's care, not spoiling the milk, [nor lying with a man,]
19   nor becoming pregnant, nor suckling another infant alongside; [and whatever]
20   she receives or is entrusted with she shall keep safe and re[turn when demanded,]
21   or pay the value of each, except for evident [loss],
22   of which, once made clear, let her be released; and she shall not abandon [the nursing]
23   within the period. And if she transgresses anything, she shall repay [the nursing-fees she has received]
24   and whatever she received, with half as much again, and the damages and expenses, and another
25   [two hundred] silver drachmas, the right of execution being exercised as
26   by legal judgment. And Therm[outharion], while Ch[rotarion] performs
27   each duty, (is) to supply from [now] the monthly nursing-fees, and not
28   to take the infant away <within the> peri[od, or] she too shall be liable to the same
29   penalty. And [Ch]rotarion shall present herself with the infant before
30   Thermoutharion (each) month(?). We request (registration).
31   Year 26 of Caesar, Hathyr 8.
