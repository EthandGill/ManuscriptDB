[TRANSLATION]
1     To Deios and his associates, public bankers.
2     We, the undersigned public cattle-breeders
3     of the village of Memphis, through Herakleides
4     who has been appointed agent, have received from you the sums ordered to be paid to us
5     by Ptolemaios, royal scribe of the division of Polemon, who is administering the affairs concerning
6     the strategia of the two divisions, which we requested to be ordered paid for the freight-charges
7     of the wheat which we declared
8     we had carried down from the granaries of the division of Polemon to the harbours,
9     the processing of it having been done at the produce of the 17th year:
10    of wheat, 25 1/12 artabas, of barley 1 1/2 artaba, at a price, as
11    of the artaba of wheat each at 8 drachmas, 200 drachmas 4 obols,
12    and of the barley at the rate of 480 drachmas per 100 artabas, 8 drachmas,
13    making, to the same account, of silver 208 drachmas 4 obols. And they are:
14    Theantron … sios, of wheat 7 1/3 1/12 artabas,
15    of barley 1/2 artaba. Alypios son of Tesenouphis, of wheat 3 1/4 1/24 artabas.
16    Aphrodisios son of Sokrates, of wheat 3 1/3 1/8 artabas,
17    of barley 1/3 artaba. Phanias son of Sarapion,
18    of wheat 3 1/2 1/8 artabas, of barley 1/3 artaba. Aphrodisios
19    son of Aphrodisios, of wheat [3] 1/4 1/8 artabas, of barley 1/3 artaba.
20    Horos son of Herakleos, of wheat 3 1/4 artabas. The aforesaid amounts come to,
21    in accordance with the order.
22    Herakleides, the appointed law-scribe,
23    have received the aforesaid two hundred and eight drachmas four obols;
24    making 208 drachmas 4 obols.
25    [Year 1]8 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
26    Phamenoth 2.
