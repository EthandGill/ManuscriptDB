[TRANSLATION]
1     Antinous to Dioskorous his mother
2     many greetings;
3     You will do well delivering the attached
4     small letter to Antipater the
5     councillor of Antinooupolis the son Thalaua[…]
6     who became my acquaintance in the
7     assessment or to his son Didymus
8     since he has something there advantageous to him;
9     but immediately give it to him. I greet
10    my brothers and all my
11    household. I wish you to be well.
12    [To] Dioskorous [from An]tinous son
