[TRANSLATION]
1     To Menches, village-scribe of Kerkeosiris,
2     from Apollodoros, who has farmed the
3     sale and the tax of oil of the same (village)
4     for the 4th year. I happen previously
5     also to have submitted (a petition) to Polemon the
6     epistates of the village concerning what befell me on the 27th of Phaophi, namely that in the
7     house of Sisois son of Senapynchis,
8     which is in the local Thoerieion, there was smuggled
9     oil, liable to penalty. When I at once brought
10    Trychambos, the man sent by the oikonomos
11    for the confiscation,
12    since you and those in charge of affairs were unwilling to accompany me to the said house, and when I had gone there, thus the aforesaid man and his wife Tausiris,
13    grappling with me and giving me many
14    blows, by force shut
15    both the door of the temple a[nd] of the house.
16    Hence on the 4th [of] Hathyr, when I fell in with Sisois beside the local
17    temple of Zeus and wished to make an arrest,
18    in the presence of Ineilos the sword-bearer
19    and of Trychambos, there rushed upon us Pausiris his brother,
20    a sack-bearer, and Belles and Demas
21    and Maron son of Takonnos, with others
22    whose names I do not know, and having got
23    the mastery of us, laying
24    upon us many blows with the
25    helmets which they had, they wounded my wife on the right hand and me
26    likewise, so that damage has been caused to the
27    purchase-contract to the amount of 10 talents of copper. I submit to you
28    this report, that you may give orders
29    to those whom it concerns, so that, having been exacted (from them),
