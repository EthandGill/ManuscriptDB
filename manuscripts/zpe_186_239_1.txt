# HGV-EN: Year 2, Hathyr 24. Year 2, Hathyr 24. There has been paid into the tax office at Phebichis of the Koites, to Pason the banker and Stotoetis the controller from Harendotes the amount due from Taembes from Talae for the beer tax on the account of Hathyr eight drachmae of bronze at 24 1/4 obols (for a stater), total 8 drachmae. In the presence of Dorion.
