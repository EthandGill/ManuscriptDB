[TRANSLATION]
1     [From] the records of Oue[d]ius Fau[s]tus [the] most excellent epistrategos,
2     [and] others; Faustus, having deliberated with those sitting together [regarding] Harpocratio[n], strategus of the divisions of Themistus and Polemon, said: according to what [by me]
3     [was] examined and what was ordered by the most brilliant hegemon, [it will be necessary] to come to his judgment, to which I will make known all things that under me [have been done]. But they will be sufficient when going to the judgment, both
4     the nomophylax Dios and those who jointly took Harphaesis, Demetrios and Epim[a]chos, and also the wrestling-supervisors Herakleideus and Mysthos. Dios, n[o]mophylax, said: both Hermeias and Dios, dagger-bearers, jointly took with us [the]
5     Harphaesis. Faustus ordered them to be summoned, and when they did not obey
6     Faustus said: if they do not happen to appear, the following will be [the case], and Areios
7     the scribe of Harpocratio[n] the strategus will provide [something] sufficient, until Harpocrās
8     the assistant [is] found; for he too is necessary. Harpocration, strategus,
9     said: 92 [artabas] of wheat I have in treasuries and barley 19½ and lentils 81 and
10    also other types, and the measuring out of these is of concern to me. And I fear
11    lest under my [authority] these be carried and be lost to the treasury [office].
12    Faustus asked: by whom [will] it be carried? [He] answered: by the sit[o]logoi and others. If then it [seems good] to you, appoint some [supervisors]. Faustus said: ... ... ... what [have you] done concerning these?
13    [He] answered: I will not rest [having things] done at [this] time until
14    these are administered. Faustus asked: how much time is needed for the measuring? [He] answered: the most brilliant hegemon ordered
15    [it to be done] in 30 days, but also [and] other [things] will be needed. Faustus said: as I said before, I will make known all these to the most brilliant
16    hegemon; and this likewise I will make known, which is, [that it is] most necessary to be
17    the measuring, in order that whatever seems good to him [he will] order to be done. Harpocration, strategus, said: meanwhile then [do] you order me to wait?
18    Faustus said: wait doing what is necessary; for I will write to him,
19    in order that, if he wishes, [he will come] directly to me, [he will] make [it] known to me.
20    Year 2 of Antoninus and Verus, our lords Augustuses, month [of] Hadrianos 28.
