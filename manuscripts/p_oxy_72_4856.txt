# HGV-EN: (2nd hand?) Sitolog– of . . . (1st hand) Dionysius son of Pausir— to Sarapion, sitologus of the western toparchy, district of Seryphis, greetings. Transfer, from the amount you hold on deposit in your granary, from the wheat-crop of the current second year of Hadrianus the lord, to Dioscorus son of Diogenes, as a deposit, sixty-two artabas of wheat, total 62 art. of wheat. Year 2 of Hadrianus the lord, Mesore 9. (N. Gonis)
