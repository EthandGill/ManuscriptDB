[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 3, Phaophi 18. It has been assigned to the bank in Hermouthis on which Ammonios [collects] a tenth of the encyclios according to a record
2     of Memmon and Hermias [those] concerning the sale under which Ammonios the scribe subscribes
3     tax of sale of Totohe Zmanreous of purification days in the Ramessnoufieion in the
4     Memnoneia and the coinciding things [which] he bought from Psenapathus of Arsihesios
5     of bronze talents 3 tax of which change thousand eight-hundred; comes to 1800.
6     Aganises has been provided with money
7     Ammonios the banker
