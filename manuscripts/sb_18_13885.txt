1     After the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most brilliant
2     Thoth, twelfth, [of the] eleventh indiction in Arsinoe.
3     Aurelius, father Sois, son of father Hol from village Dikaios
4     of the Arsinoite nome to Aurelius Ioannes son of Paulus carpenter
5     from the city of the Arsinoi[tes] greetings. I agree to have leased from you the land-plots belonging to you in field Psabathoti
6     next to the s[uburbs] of this city in the place of an [allotment]
7     called Istopo[na]chbon, arourae two more [or] less
8     arourae 2 more or less with all their [right upon]
9     time [such as] you [wish] from the harvest of the entering
10    twelfth [indiction and] of itself and from the surpl[uses arising] from them annually from the
11    legumes to [yield] to you the landholder, two parts
12    and to me [one part], and from the [straw] five parts
13    and to me [one part, a]nd I will necessarily complete
14    all works toward [their] cultivation
15    unimpeded[ly], [and] of all kinds of quadrupeds
16    and of advance [seeds] looking toward you
17    the landholder and [for you] to be withdrawn annually from the
18    community the seeds and the wages of the
19    threshing and reaping, and I will give you according to
20    custom annually acceptable cheeses twenty
21    five and of [strained] milk four.
22    The lease [is] valid and questioned I agreed. Aurelius, father
