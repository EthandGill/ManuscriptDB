1     In the 37th year of Caesar, Pachon 11.
2     In the thirty-seventh year of Caesar's domination, in the month Panemos 11, Pachon 11, in the village
3     of Kerkeesis of the division of Polemon of the
4     Arsinoite nome. Sigesis son of Paopis leased
5     to Zoilos son of Horos, a Persian of the epigone, the seven arouras belonging
6     to him in two parcels in the (field)
7     called that of Kriton, and from the holding (hypodochion)
8     called that of Kolophonios, another two arouras, so as to be
9     in total nine arouras, for five years from
10    the coming 38th year of Caesar; and in return for these
11    (he is to pay) all the public dues, and the twenty-drachma (tax) and the
12    single-bundle (tax) of hay. And of the lease,
13    in the last year he shall hand over the additional
14    two arouras in hay for the grazing of sheep, and the
15    land clean of rush, of all weeds (?),
16    of …, ap… ōte
17    In the 37th year of Caesar, Pachon 11. It has been registered through
18    Ptolemaios, notary (nomographos) of Kerkeesis.
19    I, Zoilos son of Horos, have leased from Siouēris son of Paotis
20    the (arouras) belonging to him in two parcels around Tebtynis, which I myself also farmed,
21    around Tebtynis, public land in the
22    (field) called that of Kriton, seven arouras,
