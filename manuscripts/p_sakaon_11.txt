[TRANSLATION]
1     Year second of our lord Domitius
2     Domitianus Augustus, Thoth 16.
3     The Aurelii Heroninus, and Athanasios and Philadelphos, and Serenion, both having served as exegetes
4     of Alexandria, dekaprotoi 6 and 8 of the toparchy
5     of Themistus' division. [There] were measured for us
6     in the storeroom of the village Theadelphia with public measure, xyston, for the produce
7     of 1 of the year, [named] Thermoutharios, through heirs
8     Satabouts: [of] wheat, pure [artabas] twelve, [equaling] wheat artabas 12.
9     Aurelius Heroninus dekaprotos, through me Iulianus, [I] have marked the obols and the freight-charges of the
10    ferryings. Aurelius Philadelphos dekaprotos: [I] have marked
11    the obols and the freight-charges of the ferryings.
12    Aurelius Serenion dekaprotos, through me Sarmates: [I] have marked.
