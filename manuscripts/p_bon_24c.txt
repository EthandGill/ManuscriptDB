[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios, I have signed. Year 19 of Hadrianus Caesar the
2     Lord, Mecheir 16.
3     To Dios and Herakleides and Origenes and Ptolemaios,
4     former gymnasiarchs, appointed to the record-office of property-acquisitions of the Arsinoite (nome) for (questions of) heirs.
5     From Tephorsais daughter of Pausis son of
6     Pausis, her mother being Kollauthis, from the village of
7     Tebtynis, with her guardian, her husband Herakleides
8     son of Akousilaos. I register for the first time
9     through you, up to the present day, the
10    place belonging to me in the village of Tebtynis,
11    a wool-comber's workshop, not having registered it within the appointed term, it having come to me by inheritance from my full brother by the same father [and] the same mother, Epimachos, from [the same] village, who had registered (it) through the earlier (declarations) and, through ignorance,
12    had not had it transcribed (into the records); he having died childless and intestate
13    in the seventh year
14    of the deified Tra[ianus], in the house-by-house census, (the property being) clear
15    of debt [and] of surety. Therefore I submit
16    the regist[ration, in ac]cordance with the
17    copies I presented to you, of the house-by-house registration [of the]
18    ninth ye[ar of the deified Traian]us …
19    apopl… …mo (?) of the wor[ksh]op
20    of the place di… enoth (illegible)
