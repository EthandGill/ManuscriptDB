[TRANSLATION]
1     … to the most brilliant [of you] brotherhood … the letters, to release
2     … [the] descendant of Heraclianus Gesios the blessed from Psinomouneus, being in custody
3     of … some surety [of] free men
4     of the same village Psinomouneus or of the shepherds of the same, so that [he] might find the gathering
5     to make of the same fields, and [the] seed might not be destroyed to the harm of the state,
6     not having [anyone to] manage her affairs. For many … about this I was urged
7     by [those] who customarily bow to us, whose [request] according to God I did not find [it right] to refuse,
8     but do not neglect this and [let] the produce be damaged and [let] the state be found lacking.
