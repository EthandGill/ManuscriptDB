[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the consulship of] Flavius Theodoricus the most illustrious, Thoth 24, of the ninth in[di]ction.
2     [To Aurelia Eucharist]ia daughter of Hermogenes, the most noble, [from] the city of the Hermopolites,
3     [from Aurelius An]tinoos son of Petros, slow at writing, a baker by trade,
4     [from the same] city. I acknowledge of my own free will and choice that I have leased
5     [from] your nobility for only one year, of the crops of the prosperous
6     [coming] tenth indiction, the existing third part falling to you
7     [of arouras] however many there are, situated in the place [call]ed Tarix, on the west
8     … with two cisterns and reservoirs, for sowing and
9     [deposit]ing of crops whichever I may choose, at a rent for each aroura of land surveyed,
10    [as may] appear, of wheat-barley by half, five artabas, which rent
11    [new, clean,] sifted, by the Athenian measure, I will pay
12    in the month Epeiph yearly without delay; and I will … to you also the third part falling to you of the trees there, that is acacias,
13    olive-groves and date-palms, and … [for] rent of each [date-palm]
14    female bearing fruit, of every three bearing fruit, silver tal[ents]
15    a thousand, and a third part from the cr[op] of the [ol]ive-groves, in partnership
16    with your brother Sar[a]podoros, to the completion of the same arouras an[d of the]
17    trees, to his own two-thirds share; the lease is valid and secure, an[d on being questioned] I ack[nowledged].
18    Aur[e]lius A[n]tin[o]os son of Petros the aforesaid, I have leased as set forth.
19    Aurelius [Ph]oibam[m]on son of Ser…os, councillor of Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard from the maker.
20    Do…arios, d[eacon, from] Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lea[se], having heard from the maker.
21    Through me Sarapion it was wri[t]ten …
22    Lease of Antino[os son of Petros, the b]aker, from Hermoupolis.
