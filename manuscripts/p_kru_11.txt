[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity, Father and
2     [Son] and Holy Spirit. Written in the month Hathyr 11, of the 7th indiction.
3     [In the reign of] our master, through Flavius Kometos son of Chael, administrator of the fortress of Memnoneia.
4     [I, Ari]stophane, the son of the late John, the man of the fortress of
5     Jeme, the nome of the city of Er[mont] … I …
6     of this kind, and I appeal to certain trustworthy witnesses,
7     these who shall bear witness for him concerning me, according to my request, I of this kind
8     write to Kometos the son of the late Damianos, the man of this same fortress of Jeme, the nome of this same city, greeting.
9     Namely, I acknowledge — I, Aristophane the son of John,
10    who first write to you, you Komes the son of Daianos, the one who buys,
11    and the one to whom I first wrote — according to this sale which is written, confirming
12    and valid, in one and the same matter through me. I have applied myself to it,
13    willing and persuaded, without any deceit or fear or violence or fraud or trickery or constraint, there being no compulsion
14    whatsoever moving me to these things, in all good and fair purpose.
15    And I swear by God the Almighty and by the salvation of our masters
16    who rule over us now, through the command of God,
17    that I sell to you, you Komes the son of Damiane, the one who buys,
18    and the one to whom I have made clear from above many times, in all lordship of right and of law, of a complete sale fully accomplished,
19    according to the force of this document; and I write to you from today
20    onward and forever, my third part of land,
21    this which is below the house of Abysa the daughter of the late Zacharias,
22    the part of land which came to me through Tan… and
23    Marihahm the daughter of the late Papnoute of my mother, through
24    a lawful purchase, through a written sale,
25    according to the ancient and old dispositions and they contain as follows:
26    south, the sons of the late Ioannakios, and the door of the proprietors;
27    north, Abyssa the daughter of Zacharias; east, Abyssa the daughter
28    of Zacharias; west, the blind passage. These are the boundaries of my third
29    part of land on every side. This is the price which we agreed upon
30    with one another, and it pleased us, the two parts; this is the two
31    holokottinoi of refined gold, of the standard of the fortress of Jeme,
32    making coins, 2, two, of refined gold of the weight of the fortress of Memnoneia, the complete price. It has come to my hand from your hand,
33    you Komes the son of Damianos, the one who buys, and the one to whom I first wrote, hand to hand from your house, of approved gold
34    and full sum, so that you, Komes, to whom I first wrote, may name it as your own, may enter into it and take hold of it and be its lord,
35    and may become master of it according to the force of this sale; since its price
36    has come to my hand from your hand in full according to the agreement of which I first declared,
37    naming it above, that I have sold it to you, again I have sold it to you, for you to administer
38    it, manage it, beautify it, make use of it
39    in every manner that is yours, according to every possession and
40    lordship and eternal holding, lawfully and unhinderedly,
41    nor at any season or time shall any come out against you, neither I
42    nor a brother nor a sister nor son of one nor son of two
43    nor on the part of my father and my mother, to sue you in a court of law
44    or outside a court of law, nor to make any approach
45    against you. There is no power, however exalted or honored,
46    which can benefit those persons, primarily in the first place;
47    he is a stranger to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
48    and he shall pay as a fine thirty-six holokottinoi of gold
49    to the existing authority, apart from the penalty which our law
50    decrees against the one who dares to transgress; and over and above all these things,
51    he shall come in and assent to this sale which is written; for your security therefore
52    I have drawn it up, valid and binding in every place where it shall be produced,
53    and they shall ask me about it. Namely I acknowledge,
54    I Aristophane the son of John, that I consent to this sale
55    and to everything written in it; and the people of the lady Hannake are in common (with it).
56    I, Isidoros the son of Daniel, give witness.
57    I, Antone (son of) Mathaios, consent.
58    I, Stephanos the son of Ousep, and Panis the son of Piam, we give witness.
59    Through me, Aristophanes son of John, it was made.
60    Receipt (epagone) made by Aristophanes (son of) John, man of the village (?),
61    concerning the third part of land.
