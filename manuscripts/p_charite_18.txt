[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Cyrillus [son] of Hermeius, superintendent
2     of wine being conveyed to the Thebaid through me
3     [son] of the father Hermeius to Aurelia Charite [daughter] of Amasonios
4     greetings. I have loaded into the vessel lying [nearby]
5     boat for the sufficiency of the most noble [men]
6     soldiers wine of account private measure …
7     of the village of Sinarchybos containers
8     Italian one hundred forty-four only. Payni …
9     Aurelius Cyrillus [son] of Hermeius the aforesaid through me the father [Hermeius]
10    I have signed.
