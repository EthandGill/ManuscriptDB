r.2    Φαμίνιος Παναμέως Φμόιτος
r.3    ὑπὲρ μερισμῶν κ ἔτους Χάρακος δραχμὰς τέσσαρας
r.4    γίνονται δραχμαὶ δ. ἄλλας Φαῶφι κ ὁμοίως

[TRANSLATION]
1     Thoth 20 of the 21st year, by name
2     Phaminnios [son] of Panameus, Phmoïtos
3     for the divisions of year 20 of Charakon, four drachmas
4     become four drachmas. Others on Phaophi 20, likewise
5     two drachmas one obol, become two drachmas one obol
6     In total six drachmas one obol …, I have signed
