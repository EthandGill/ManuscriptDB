[TRANSLATION]
1     (Christian symbol) 643 (numerical sign)
2     In the reign and consulship of our most divine lord Flavius
3     Justinus the eternal Augustus, Emperor, in the
4     fourth year, Thoth the thirtieth, of the second indiction, in the most illustrious city of Antinoopolis.
5     To Flavius Phoibammon, the most illustrious and most learned
6     scholasticus and advocate [of the treasury] of the Thebaid, son of the man of blessed
7     and revered memory,
8     [Thomas] the count, holding property in
9     this city of the Antinoites, from Aurelius Kollouthos,
10    son of Victor, from Thmounkrekis, the village of the Antaiopolite
11    nome, having become … of the most illustrious (city) of the Antinoites,
12    and wishing to establish himself as a settled household servant
13    to your benevolent house through the present written
14    lease-agreement upon the conditions set forth below,
15    upon all the stipulations it contains, with his guarantor, myself,
16    Aurelius Kollouthos son of Sergios, whose mother is Dionysia, landholder,
17    originating from the same village of Thmounkrekis, but dwelling
18    for a long time here in the same city of the Antinoites, who both
19    guarantees and takes upon himself the person and the whole good faith
20    and intention and purpose and confirmation, for security of the
21    present lease-agreement, at the risk and expense and valuation
22    of all my property and of the principal person himself,
23    which is pledged for the fulfilment of all the things set forth below,
24    the agreements inviolably. Greeting. I, the aforewritten
25    Kollouthos son of Victor, settled household servant, bond-slave, acknowledge
26    through this my written lease-agreement, with my guarantor
27    myself, Aurelius Kollouthos son of Sergios, landholder, being present and taking upon himself,
28    upon all the heads and agreements set forth for him,
29    the person of the principal, both the remaining and the presence and
30    his freedom, without any blame or censure or
31    knavery, on condition that he shall remain with your illustrious wisdom and
32    attend purely and without guile and … and for the daily
33    services genuinely and diligently, and shall press on with all the servile
34    service to it, whether on foreign soil or at home, unhesitatingly
35    and without contempt, and shall in no way withdraw from his servile
36    attendance, being confined for a four-year period reckoned
37    from the new moon of the coming month Phaophi of the presently running
38    second indiction, both itself and the following time, year by year, until
39    the completion of the same four-year term, I his guarantor
40    being ready to arrange that the same principal shall show good will
41    and be well-disposed toward you in all things enjoined and entrusted to him, with the utmost virtue and obedience in all the
42    profitable works and words, blamelessly, and shall restore
43    to you everything unharmed and carry out the (matters) of your command, without
44    theft or flight, receiving, however, from you by way
45    of wage or daily allowance yearly ten artabas of wheat by the small receiving-measure,
46    and for clothing one gold solidus less five carats, and of barley by the
47    same measure four artabas, and twelve xestai of oil and twenty-four knidia of wine, all these yearly, and the small (extras) given by custom …
48    and the festival and vintage (perquisites), in imitation of all that has been granted
49    to the tenant … of your favor; and I acknowledge that I shall bring to completion all the previously agreed
50    stipulations, or else restore these to you in double quantity,
51    with sound good faith … to be fully paid and be held liable to the …
52    and to the attendance … in payment, or (pay) on his behalf twelve gold solidi …
53    The lease (is) valid and secure; and having been formally questioned, I gave my assent.
