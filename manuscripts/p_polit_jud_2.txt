1     To Alexander the politarch and the
2     body-politic
3     from Petaus son of Philippus
4     a Jew who is held
5     in prison. Since
6     I happen to be worthily
7     admonished,
8     and [I] have experienced confinement in prison
9     [having] endured sufficiently and
10    days [while] being corrupted on foreign [soil],
11    and not having the necessities,
12    I request requesting with supplication [that you] not overlook me
13    but if it seems [good, having] taken up my cause to arrange
14    to recall me from the
