[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most excellent council of the Antinoites, New Greeks, of the splendid city,
2     [Marcus] Aurelius Besammon son of Serenus, of the dēm… sm… lo…
3     [aged about] 37, dwelling in the 3rd letter-quarter, brick-block 10, and his wife Aurelia Petronia
4     … aged about 27, daughter of Eudaimon Pe…s son of Paulinius also called …
5     … acting without a guardian by the right of children according to Roman custom,
6     [we swear] by the fortune of our lords Titus Fulvius Junius Macrianus
7     and Titus Fulvius Junius Quietus, the Augusti, and by the greatest god
8     [Osirantinoos], that we register the offering of the first-fruits for the children born to us from each other,
9     [twin-born] children, Marcus Aurelius Aemilianus and Eudaimon,
10    25 days old, presenting as verifiers those who subscribe below.
11    Year 1 of the Emperors Caesars Titus Fulvius Junius Macrianus
12    and Titus Fulvius Junius Quietus, the Pious, the Fortunate, the Augusti, Thoth 20.
13    I, Aurelius Besammon, have registered the first-fruits of
14    my sons as above. I, Aurelia Petronia, have registered
15    the first-fruits of my sons as above. I, Aurelius Sarapammon son of Ammonarion, Sabinius also called Harmonieus, wrote
16    on her behalf, she being illiterate. I, Aurelius Sarapammon
17    … son of Ammonios, Neruianios also called Genearchios
18    … the children Aurelii Aemilianus
19    [and Eudaimon] 25 days old, as above. I, Aurelius Sara… son of [Pau]linius also called Homognios
20    … [the children Aurelii Aem]ilianus
