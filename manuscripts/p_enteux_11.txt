[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greeting, Stotoës son of Pasitos, farmer from Polydeukeía. I am wronged by Gerors, holder of seventy arouras. Since there is
2     for me a house in the village, I have been evicted from it by him and my livestock is exposed to the open air, he using force and since he has with respect to the village … given … to him in a fodder-station. I beseech you, king, if [it] seems good to you, to order Diophanes the strategos to write to Sosibios the overseer to send the man against it, and [if these] matters are true, not to permit him to evict me from my house, so that I may be able by farming to [be and]
3     through you, king, the savior common to all, to obtain justice. [Farewell.]
4     To Sosibios. Especially reconcile them; if not, send [them] so that they may be tried before the common court.
5     Year 1, Gorpiaios 28, Tybi 12.
6     Year 1, Gorpiaios 28, Tybi 12.
7     From Stotoës to Gerors, holder of seventy arouras
8     concerning a house.
