[TRANSLATION]
1     provided Father Hor from the rents of the Great
2     Church from Pharmouthi 1 of the 14th indiction to Thoth 30 of the 15th indiction first half-year lit karats fifteen.
3     it makes karats fifteen alone.
4     Phaophi 20 of the same 15th indiction. through me Theodore the elder.
