[TRANSLATION]
1     … In the reign of our most divine and most pious
2     master, the greatest benefactor Flavius Justin the eternal
3     Augustus and Emperor, year 13, after the second
4     consulship of their serenity, year 11, and of Flavius
5     Tiberius, also called New Constantine, our most fortunate
6     Caesar, year 3, Tybi 16, of the eleventh indiction.
7     To Flavius Apion the all-praiseworthy, ex-consul ordinarius
8     and patrician, landowner also in this city of New Justin,
9     through Menas his servant, who acts as questioner and provides for his own master, the same all-praiseworthy
10    man, the right of action and liability, we, Aurelius Serenos
11    son of …nos, his mother being Theodora, originating
12    from the same city, subscribing below in my own hand,
13    and … son of Victor, his mother being Thekla, originating
14    from the estate of Megale Tarouthinou of the Justinopolite
15    nome, belonging to your magnificence, registered
16    farmer of it, greeting. We acknowledge, voluntarily and
17    of our own free will, swearing the divine and revered
18    oath, that we stand surety for and undertake, before your magnificence
19    through those belonging to it, for Aurelius Sourous son
20    of Phib, his mother being Ama Eous, he too originating from the
21    same estate, registered farmer of it likewise,
22    on condition that he continually remain and dwell
23    in the same estate together with his dear ones and
24    wife and cattle and all his baggage,
25    answerable for everything that concerns his person,
26    that is, the status of registered farmer; and that he in no way
27    abandon the same estate nor indeed move
28    to another place, but also that, when he is sought from
29    us by your magnificence through those belonging to it …
30    … Phib from the same estate …
