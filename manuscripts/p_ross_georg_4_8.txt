[TRANSLATION]
1     ... ent ...
2     Having received then the present [letter]s, [I ordered to the]
3     [of your administration] not to sell anything [whatsoever] of the ...
4     through our letters to buy in your administration [above/before]
5     of the cutting which through previous letters of ours we determined
6     not hindering those of the estates to sell them at the said
7     price, permitting them and bearing witness in advance concerning th[is].
8     For you will know that if we find anyone who bought
9     [from] before the said cutting [before] our letters [reached] you
10    we give you back a repayment harming you. Moreover
11    eagerly be brought to the matter of such date-storage-facilities
12    following without leaving out our mandate
13    in these things not giving against you any pretext.
14    It was written in the month Pachon 7, indiction 9.
