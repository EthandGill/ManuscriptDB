[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God. Korra ibn Szerich, governor (symboulos),
2     to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito.
3     Continually we require carpenters and caulkers, for the sake
4     of the maintenance of the boats and skiffs and dromon-ships and
5     other various levies of the public service. For there is no
6     way for such levies to be completed without them,
7     and they are also profitable to those of the district.
8     Receiving therefore the present letter, dispatch,
9     throughout the city and the recommending estates and the notable
10    persons of your administration, boys for instruction in the crafts,
11    handing them over to skilled and approved craftsmen who properly understand
12    the craft, and inspecting and mustering (them)
13    each month, reviewing also their work
14    so that it be not neglected or slighted, writing to us
15    the name and patronymic, by estate and by craft,
16    of each of them, arranging for half their number
17    to be assigned to the ship-building and caulking work,
18    writing to us likewise also the boys of your administration who were released
19    during the past year by the same register, and permitting
20    those of your administration to plant a quantity of trees,
21    both in vineyard and in thorn(-trees) and the remaining trees,
22    neglecting nothing in any of the things enjoined upon you in the present
23    letter, but going about through yourself and inspecting
24    as has been said for the sake of all these things, and writing to us how
25    you have fared. For you shall know that we intend to send, by command
26    of God, the one who ought, as he ought, to go around and
27    inspect concerning these things, and (who) shall report to us in full
28    whether you have followed our command, or otherwise how
29    you have acted. Written in the month Tybi 28, indiction 8.
30    Korra son of Szerich, governor, to Basileios administrator of the village of Aphrodito.
31    … carpenters and caulkers and …
