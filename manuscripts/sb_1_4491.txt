[TRANSLATION]
1     … completely open
2     likewise to the north with
3     all [of] them of the right/claim
4     for a time, as much as you wish,
5     from the new moon of the past month Choiak
6     of the present ninth indiction
7     and [I] the lessee provide for the rent
8     of these annually in gold
9     one coin, soiled,
10    of gold one coin soiled. The lease [is] valid,
11    and being questioned I agreed. Kosmas, donkey-driver
12    agrees with me, as is set forth. …
13    through me, Helias.
