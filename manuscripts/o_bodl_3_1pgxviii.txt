[TRANSLATION]
1     Antíochos Kassíanos and those with him,
2     overseers of the sacred gate of Syene,
3     through Phanóphis, assistant. Petorzméthis wrote out,
4     of Peteureos, mother of Tisátis, concerning the poll-tax of
5     year 14 of Hadrian the lord, drachmas twelve.
6     Making drachmas 12. Year 14 of Hadrian the lord,
7     Pachon 5. Antíochos
8     Kassíanos I have signed.
9     drachmas ten two, making drachmas 12.
