1     To [Apo]llonides, [strategos of the Arsinoite, division of Polemon,]
2     from Areios also called … [son of Apion,]
3     from Linypheia. With regard to …
4     the petition lodged with you by Ta[mystha …]
5     who became wife of my deceased brother Hermous, son of Apion,
6     as though a dowry in gold pieces were owed to her
7     by him, and these having been recovered
8     from the intermediary, my lord, registered
9     for Tamystha, I declare that the
10    gold pieces I have given back to Tamystha through
11    the hand, in the presence of Thais the intermediary,
12    but that I was compelled, the sowing-season of
13    the 5th year of Hadrian Caesar the lord pressing on, by
14    Thrakides the sowing-officer, to cultivate
15    the kleros of Hermous of 1 ½ aroura around Tebtynis
