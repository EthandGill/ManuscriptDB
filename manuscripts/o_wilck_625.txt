[TRANSLATION]
1     Petearoueris, collector of money-taxes of Charax, to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis.
2     I have received for watchtower and other (taxes) of year 9, base drachmas 3, 4 obols. Year 9
3     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Hathyr 12.
4     (2nd hand) Others, Tybi 5, in the name of the same, for watchtower and other (taxes) of year 9, base
5     1 drachma, 3 obols; for doubles, drachmas 2, 2 obols. I, …, have signed.
