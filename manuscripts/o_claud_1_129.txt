[TRANSLATION]
1     Leontas to Sukcessus: greetings. I wish you to know that my flasks have all become useless and the water is running short. Well then you will do well if you are willing [to send] me new flasks and write to me.
2     But if you are not willing, select for me from the [...]
3     old [ones] until Epaphroditus [arrives].
4     And send me a rope of [linen] and
5     a double-cloak and the ladder ... [if]
6     you are willing or descend, send to me [and] ...
7     wedges and [counter-wedges] ... Farewell.
