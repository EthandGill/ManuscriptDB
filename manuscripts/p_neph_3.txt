[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most honored father of ours, beloved
2     Nepheros. Paul.
3     I remember the blessing which we have from
4     your piety, lord father. But truly not even to ourselves
5     oil have we yet received; but when with God
6     I receive—for concerning these [days] I am concerned to have provided for the children, from which I receive, with great joy I send to you. This
7     for us is sustenance of the soul and of the coming
8     hope it is. But I sent through the brother
9     of ours Horion the [monastic] monk of the monastery of Ankyrōns
10    … e Taese the from
11    To the most honored father of ours [Nepheros]
