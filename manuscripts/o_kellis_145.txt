[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelii Horos of Eudaimon
2     and Psais potter priest of the sanctuary
3     of Tithoeos God greatest Kelles
4     both from the same [place] greetings.
5     we acknowledge by means of this petition having purchased in common a heifer
6     black-colored for drachmas one hundred
7     twenty and having left with the same
8     Horos of Eudaimon on [his] share according to the
9     custom towards the proposed ancient silver
10    of Trajan and Antoninus drachmas one hundred twenty and being questioned we agreed.
11    year 11 and year 10 and year 3
12    Tybi 3 according to ancient [custom].
