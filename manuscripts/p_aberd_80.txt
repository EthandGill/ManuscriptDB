[TRANSLATION]
1     Pindaros son of Senamounios and
2     Pabouchis son of Senpleniios, the two chaff-cutters of Memnoneia at Phthomonnth
3     To Longinus, greetings. We received from you on account of the name
4     Pleniios Pseneterô cargo of chaff 24 measures from
5     harvest 16 [of the] year of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar
6     the lord Mesore 11. Pabouchis received
7     Pindaros received.
