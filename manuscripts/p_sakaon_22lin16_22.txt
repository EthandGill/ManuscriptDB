[TRANSLATION]
1     Annios and Makrobios, receivers of sacred linen
2     bearing interest to Sakaon from [the] village
3     of Theadelphia, greetings.
4     We received from you for the twelfth
5     indiction [of] linen, pounds fifty, lbs 50.
6     [for] the coming consuls [on] the 4th, Thoth 9. Sarmates [I] have sealed.
7     Makrobios [I] have sealed.
