[GREEK]
r.1    Ἀβραὰμ καὶ Νικήτην τοὺς γραμματηφόρους θελήσατε
r.2    τάξαι βουκελλαρίους ἀπὸ ἀρχῆ[ς] τοῦ Φαρμοῦθι μηνὸς καὶ ἀπολῦσαι

[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Please appoint Abraham and Nicetes, the letter-carriers, bucellarii from the beginning of the month Pharmouthi, and pay them their allowance of corn, for you know that we require bucellarii. Be sure to do this without delay. To the most illustrious and honourable secretaries and overseers from Theodorus, secretary and by the grace of God land-agent.
