[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 6, Mesore [15].
2     Tathotis sold,
3     daughter of Phibis, two cubits of solid ground,
4     Tatapes bought,
5     daughter of Totoes, for 2,000 (drachmas) of copper.
6     In the reign of the queen and King Ptolemy, the Mother-loving Saviour Gods,
7     year 6, in the priesthood of the king Ptolemy the Mother-loving Saviour God Alexander and of the Saviour
8     Gods and the Brother Gods and the Benefactor Gods and the Father-loving Gods and the Manifest Gods
9     and the god Eupator and the god Philometor and the god Philopator Neos and the god Euergetes
10    and the Mother-loving Saviour Gods; the sacred colt of Isis the great mother of the gods,
11    the athlophoros of Berenike Euergetis, the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos, the priestess of Arsinoe
12    Philopator, those being in Alexandria,
13    and in Ptolemais of the Thebaid, in the priesthood of the priests and priestesses and kanephoros of those existing
14    and being, in the month Mesore 15.
15    Before Sosos, agoranomos.
16    Tathotis daughter of Phibis sold, a Persian, about 50 years old, of middle height, honey-coloured, round-faced,
17    straight-nosed, with her guardian, her own son Patseous son of Pates, a Persian of the epigone,
18    from the vacant lot belonging to her situated in the western part of Pathyris,
19    two cubits of solid ground; neighbours: to the north, the vacant lot of Petearsemtheus son of Totoes,
20    to the south, that of Psinyris son of Aratis, to the east a street, to the west the vacant lots of the temple,
21    or whoever the neighbours may be on all sides.
22    Tatapes daughter of Totoes bought
23    for 2,000 drachmas of copper. Guarantor: Tathotis the seller, whom Tatapes
24    the buyer accepted.
25    I, Ammonios the agent of Sosos, have transacted this.
26    Year 6, Mesore 29. There has been paid to the bank in Crocodilopolis, of which Apollonios is in charge, deposit 10, of the sales-tax, Tatapes daughter of Totoes, 200 (drachmas) of copper, total 200.
27    Apollonios, banker.
