1     In the tenth year of Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Phaophi 20, in Tebtunis of the P[olemon]
2     division of the Arsinoe nome. Acknowledges Sueris [son] of Onnophres [son] of Labesis, mother Suerios, from the
3     aforementioned village of Tebtunis, as of fifty-two years, with a scar on the right shin, with [her lord]
4     her husband Orseus [son] of Tryphon, as of sixty-two years, with a scar on the small finger of the left hand
5     has granted the woman acknowledging Sueris after her own death to those who have become of her [children] of the
6     aforementioned man Orseus [being] children Onnophres and Tephersaites and Thermouthes, the three, to the
7     two, Onnophres and Tephersaites together equally each to one-half, if any of them not surv[ive]
8     to his [children], but the paternal house belonging to her Sueris and what belongs to her
9     purchased one-half one-ninth part of another house and courtyard and all things attached to all
10    in the aforementioned village Tebtunis and the things that will be left behind by her, furniture and equipment and
11    household equipment and clothing and moneys owed to her or also other things in whatever manner, but to the
12    Thermouthes [it] has been arranged in silver drachmas sixty; with the two Onnophres and Tephersaites
13    together equally being of the mother's funeral and shrouding as seems to them, and of the
14    ... [the] arranged to Thermouthes drachmas [sixty] [and] if [produce...] of her
