[TRANSLATION]
1     from Korkodeilos, the secretary, president of the former Tigellios revenue.
2     Having received the letter from you
3     concerning the cattle I brought [it] to Amyntas; since then
4     the sufficient [amount] from him I have obtained, and
5     I report [it], in order that you may know.
