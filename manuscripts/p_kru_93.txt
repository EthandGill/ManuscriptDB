[TRANSLATION]
1     I, John the son of the late Zacharias, the man of the village of Napotei
2     in the district of Ermont, write to the holy monastery
3     of the victorious one and the commander, the saint Abba Phoibamo[n]
4     of the mountain of the fortress of Jeme, through you, the most reverent Sourou[s]
5     the deacon and the superior of this monastery, and through everyone
6     who shall be superior after you forever, greeting. Since the law
7     of God commands, and they exhort every person to bearing witness to do good, there being no authority to hinder any person
8     also from doing what is theirs in what is their own; since at the begetting
9     of Shenoute my beloved son, God commanded, and he fell into a bodily sickness, so that we counted him to despair many times, namely that he would die.
10    Thereafter I vowed to God and to the saint Phoibamon, namely "if you grant him healing I shall give him into this holy monastery as a servant forever, that he be under the obedience of the holy place."
11    Thereafter God granted him the healing; and when he found
12    his heart, he fled from me secretly, he went to the district of Tanhet from place
13    to place, until he came to Babylon. I did not find his trace for many years.
14    We continued beseeching God, namely "if he returns him to us I shall fulfill my vow into the holy place." Afterward God
15    returned him by the command of God. Behold, I have renounced Shenoute
16    my beloved son into the holy monastery of Apa Phoibamon
17    of the mountain of the fortress of Jeme, with every child he shall beget, that he may become for it a servant forever. To set apart then the holy place
18    I have drawn up this donation, this to which I have applied myself, willing
19    and persuaded, without any deceit or fear or violence or fraud
20    or trickery, there being no constraint upon me, but in my own free purpose, swearing by God the Almighty and by the salvation of our masters who rule (us), I acknowledge, donating Shenoute
21    my beloved son into that holy monastery,
22    that it may take hold and be the lord of Shenoute my son forever,
23    that it may beget him and again beget him, that it may administer him, manage
24    him, sweep, throw (?) him, with the administration of the water of the laver
25    and the care of the lamp of the altar, and the administration of the bread of the strangers who shall pass by, and every matter of need belonging to the monastery within and without, whether in the monastery or
26    outside, whether in Egypt or in any matter that the superior shall command
27    him; and it is not permitted to me, nor to my wife, nor to any
28    child that is mine, nor an heir that is mine, to come out against the holy monastery forever, concerning Shenoute my beloved son; nor
29    to make any approach against it before us, any magistracy
30    or authority, small or great. Whoever shall wish to come out against the holy place
31    concerning him shall draw upon himself the judgment of my vow and
32    the tribunal that is dreadful of God, that I may go to law with him, namely that he wished to destroy my offering and my vow to God. And afterward
33    the authority that rules at that time shall lay constraint upon him,
34    and he shall come in and assent to the whole force of this donation, this which I have drawn up valid for the holy monastery, valid and binding
35    in every place where they shall produce it. It has been read to me.
36    I, John his father, it pleased me, I consented to it, I cast
37    witnesses upon it, I have released it.
38    I, John the son of the late Zacharias, consent to this donation
39    according as it is written. The archpresbyter Zacharias in Ermont asked me,
40    I wrote on his behalf, namely that he cannot write, and I give witness.
41    I, John the son of the late Kosma in Ermont, give witness.
42    I, Petros the son of the late Protogenes of Ermont, give witness.
43    I, Origene the son of the late Theochristos in Ermont, give witness.
44    I, Stephanos the son of Senouthe in Ermont, give witness.
45    Through me, Papa son of Kleonikon the deacon, it was made.
