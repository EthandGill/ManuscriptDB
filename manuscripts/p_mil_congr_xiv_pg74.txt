[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the …
2     … [near a] village …
3     … of a holding, arouras …
4     [in which there is] a water-wheel and a well …
5     … five and …
6     so that the lessees shall sow and cultivate each year the one half-part
7     and the other half-part with whatever crops they
8     may choose, except woad and pondweed, as rent each year for the whole
9     of the arouras and the house, of wheat artabas
10    forty, of silver drachmas
11    nine hundred thirty-two; the rent is free of all risk each year,
12    the public dues on the land being each year the responsibility of the lessors;
13    and the lease being guaranteed,
14    we will pay the money rent
15    each year in the months Epeiph and Mesore, and the
16    wheat we will measure out into the public granary and
17    deliver clean as a deposit at the
18    first measuring in the month Epeiph; and if
19    in the following year any part becomes unflooded, it shall be allowed to the lessees on
20    condition that the lessors set up
21    for us each year, fitted out with all
22    wooden and iron equipment, free of rent.
23    And if any part of the machine breaks or
24    grows old, the landowners shall provide its equivalent; and
25    the right of execution shall be theirs both against the lessees and against all their property.
26    The lease is valid. Year 13
27    of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
28    Augustus Armeniacus Medicus Parthicus Maximus.
