1     From Alupiōs.
2     The ox-cart together with a plow
3     And the sow and the ox-driver, hand over to Sarapiōn, my steward,
4     In order that [what] is being transported in
5     Bubastis, [a] small property,
6     May be cleared.
7     I pray you to be well.
8     I have marked [it].
9     To Herōneinaios, steward.
