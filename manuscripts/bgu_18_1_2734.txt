[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion to Peteimouthes, greeting. A copy of the official memorandum addressed to Leonides the granary-keeper concerning [the matters about which he writes that it is necessary]
2     to be officially recorded is subjoined below. Farewell. Year 32, Choiak …
3     To Leonides: of the letter from Peteimouthes the royal scribe concerning the things [about which he writes that there must]
4     be officially recorded, as loans for the sowing of the 32nd year, to the [farmers] of Kolasouchis of the queen
5     for the (land) which they farm around the vil[lage in] the revenue (-department), formerly belonging to the mother of the king, land, ap[art from those]
6     which he makes clear were previously ordered … of the capital (sums), another seventy artabas of lentil, [a copy]
7     is subjoined below. Having therefore followed (the instruction), measure out, with Theris the king's kinsm[an and strategos] co-subscribing,
8     and (with) Peteimouthes, a written oath of theirs by the king having first been taken c[oncerning]
9     sowing all the registered land around the village in the revenue (-department) [and leaving none]
10    unsown, and delivering from the new (harvest), together with the rents of the land, together [with the additional]
11    measured (amounts), with the agreement of the topo[gramma]teus, seventy artabas of lentil; [from]
12    these you shall officially record [and make a receipt and a counter-receipt] toward him as is fit[ting.]
13    [Year 32,] Choiak …
14    Peteimouthe[s to Sarapion, greeting. A copy of the letter which we have written to Theris the kinsman and strategos]
15    is subjoined below, [so that, knowing it, you may direct it to be enjoined upon the granary-keeper to advance, as is fitting, the indicated seventy artabas of lentil, they make seventy artabas of lentil.]
16    To Peteimouthes
