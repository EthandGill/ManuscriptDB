[TRANSLATION]
1     to my lord son [Pap]nouthios
2     his mother Maria [sends] very many greetings.
3     Again you persist in your negligence and
4     you did not send me [word] concerning which [things]
5     we instructed you. You will do well then to send me the [silver]. And your
6     wife says also that you send
7     her silver for wages of the
8     wool-workers. But do not be negligent and
9     send them speedily. Be eager to write to us concerning the
10    work. [I pray] that you be well
11    for many times
12    I pray.
