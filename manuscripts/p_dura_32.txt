[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year 565, in the month Xanthikos, on the twenty-ninth … eraiko… a deed of separation of Marcus Julius Antiochos, soldier of the legion IV Scythica
2     … ul… uster… and of Amimme daughter of Onouk…, [his wife.]
3     Under the consulship of our lords the Imperators Va[leria]n for the 2nd time and Gallienus the Augusti, before
4     two days of the Kalends of May … fifth (day?) of the 565th year, in the Colony of the Europ[aeans of Seleucus]
5     Nicator, the sacred and inviolable and autonomous (city). [Julius Antiochos] soldier of the vexilla[ti]on here [of the legion IV]
6     Scy[thica Valeriana Gal]liena, of the century of Alexander, and Aurelia Amimma … [Do]ur[ene, his wife,]
7     … (largely illegible)
8     … and to be released from their cohabitation with one another, and [now] they acknowledge to one another, [Antiochos on the one hand]
9     to Amimma to have provided all [that] he held from her, from small to great …
10    … of the cohabitation itself [with An]tiochos, and to allow her to contract marriage [with another] man, and neither [to make claim]
11    [nor to be going to make c]laim against Amimma, neither concerning a written or unwritten (matter), nor concerning any[thing else at all]
12    in the whole in any way; … and likewise also Amimma … [to Antiochos]
13    not to make claim nor to be going to make claim, but to have received from him all that Antiochos held of hers, and
14    not to make [claim] … nor to be going to make claim now, neither concerning a written or unwritten (matter), nor [concerning anything else]
15    in the whole at all in any way. And if anyone does not … and someone, having interfered, prevails (in a suit), the interference or the claim shall be void and he shall pay in addition, without account [or judgment,]
16    to the [oth]er a penalty of silver one thousand denarii, and to the most-sacred treas[ury the equal sum; and this de]ed of separation shall be and thus be vali[d]; … as the pledge having been formally [questioned and a]cknowledged. And there is also a counterpart.
17    I, J[u]lius [An]t[io]chos, soldier,
18    the afo[r]ewritt[e]n, acknowledge to have made the de[ed of] separati[on in accord]ance with the aforewritten (terms) …
19    I, Aurelius Va[l]e[nt]inus son of Antoninus, a Dourene, having been requested, wrote [on behalf of Amimme]
20    daughter of [Ono]uk…, being illiterate, who acknowledges to have made … the deed of separation.
21    I, Patrok[le]ianos, soldier (witness).
22    I, Abbouis son of Ab… ei… ieio, witness.
23    I, Ask[l]epi[o]dot[os son of A]thenodoros, councillor, witness.
