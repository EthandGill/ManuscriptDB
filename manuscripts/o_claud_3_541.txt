[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammonius of Vergilius [of the] numerus
2     of Porphyrites to Hareo, soldier of the Augustan class of Alexandria,
3     greetings. I acknowledge [that I] have borrowed from you four drachmas, which I will indeed repay to you, paying to you each month drachmas
4     two for [the period of] two months, [namely] Phaophi and
5     Hathyr, without delay. In the 5th year of Antoninus
6     our lord, Thoth 21.
7     I, Ammonius, [confirm] as aforesaid.
