1     To Aurelius [… strategos of Oxyrhynchus]
2     From Aurelia Apollonaria [daughter of Zoilus, former gymnasiarch] of the city of Oxyrhynchites
3     [of …] mother of … [acting] without a [kyrios], having authority
4     According to Roman [custom for the declaration] of children through Aurelius Horion the scribe.

9     Year in the month of Epeiph. And I request this my declaration to be made [known to those] to whom it concerns. Year 4, Pauni 19
