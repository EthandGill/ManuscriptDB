[TRANSLATION]
1     In the seventh year of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian
2     Augustus, in the month Xandikos on the seventh, Mecheir 7, in Bakchias
3     of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nome. Tauris
4     daughter of Ninis, about forty years old, with a scar on the left forearm by the wrist,
5     with as her guardian her kinsman on the father's side Horos son of Orsenouphis, about … years old,
6     five, with a scar on the right shin, acknowledges to Tapekysis daughter of Horos, about 48 years old, with a scar
7     on the upper lip on the left side, with as her guardian her husband Horos son of Horos, about 53 years old, with a scar
8     on the left eyebrow, that she, the acknowledging party, has received from her of stamped silver of the Augusti
9     coinage as capital sixty drachmas, on the spot, hand to hand, out of the house, and
10    that in lieu of the interest on these the acknowledging party has agreed with Tapekysis
11    that she may dwell, she and those acting for her and whomever she wishes, for the time during which
12    the acknowledging party owes her the aforesaid money, in the property belonging to her, the acknowledging party,
13    in the village, a paternal ninth-and-twenty-seventh share, held in common and undivided,
14    of sites of building-plots, of which the neighbours, as by agreement they declared, are:
15    on the south, the building-plots of Katoites son of Mengcheus and partners, and on a part, of common entrance and exit; on the north, a royal street; on the west, of Katoites son of Mengcheus and partners,
16    building-plots; on the east, the court of Horos son of Katoites son of Belleus and his brothers; and (that she will) guarantee
17    her, the acknowledging party, with every guarantee against everyone who shall come against (it) in whatever
18    manner. And if the acknowledging party wishes to repay the aforesaid money, let her repay
19    … if Tapekysis appears to have expended outlay on the repair of the dwelling,
20    the credit concerning it resting with Tapekysis. Subscribers: for the acknowledging party
21    and her guardian, Deios son of Kraton, about 25 years old, with no distinguishing mark; for the others, Souchas son of Sochotes, about 43 years old, with a scar on the left wrist …
22    Tauris daughter of Ninis, with as guardian my kinsman Horos son of Orsenouphis, I acknowledge that I have received
23    from Tapekysis the sixty drachmas of silver hand to hand, and that in lieu of the interest
24    on these I will grant her to dwell, she and those acting for her and whomever she wishes, for the time during which
25    I owe the aforesaid money, in the property belonging to me in the village, a paternal ninth
26    and twenty-seventh share, held in common and undivided, of sites of building-plots, of which the neighbours (are): on the south,
27    of Katoites son of Mengcheus and partners … building-plots, and on a part of entrance and exit,
28    on the north a royal street, on the west of Katoites son of Mengcheus and partners building-plots, on the east
29    the court of Horos son of Katoites son of Belleus and his brothers; and I will guarantee with every guarantee against everyone who
30    shall come against (it) in every way. And if I wish to repay to Tapekysis the aforesaid money, I will repay … if Tapekysis appears to have expended outlay on the repair of the
31    dwelling, the credit concerning it resting with Tapekysis, as is set forth.
32    Deios son of Kraton, I wrote on their behalf, they not knowing letters. Tapekysis
33    daughter of Horos, with as guardian my husband Horos son of Horos: the
34    acknowledgement has been made to me as is set forth. Souchas son of Sochotes, I wrote on their behalf, they not knowing letters.
35    Registered through the record-office in Bakchias.
36    Acknowledgement of loan and tenancy …
37    … of the record-office …
