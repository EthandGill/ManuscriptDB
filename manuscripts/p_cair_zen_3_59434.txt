[TRANSLATION]
1     Zenodorus to Zenon greetings. You wrote about [the]
2     boats to give to Eudemos.
3     I therefore do not follow [the] boats where they lie, because of
4     [the fact that] Euarchos is not resident, Horus
5     but they say [he] follows where
6     they lie. But I will bring him to you
7     leading [him] tomorrow;
8     and if he gives the
9     boats, I myself
10    will carry [them] to Eudemos.
11    Farewell.
