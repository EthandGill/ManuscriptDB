[TRANSLATION]
1     has made a record: Phthomonthes [son] of Horus, mother Senarapahesis
2     for the poll-tax of the South and West, 10th year, drachmas 8. 10th year of Trajan
3     Caesar the lord, Pharmouthi 4
4     Pachon 8, drachmas 4. Similarly Pachon 28, drachmas 4
5     Similarly Pauni 18, through Psenamounios
6     drachmas 4. Similarly Mesore 2, drachmas 4
7     Similarly 11th year, Phaophi 20, for
8     dike-tax, drachmas 4, which [is also] 3 obols [and] 4 ½. Similarly
9     Athur 19, obols 4 [and] ½, chalks 2, which [is also a] four-obol piece.
