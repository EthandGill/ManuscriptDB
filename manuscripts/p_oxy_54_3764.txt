[TRANSLATION]
1     … …
2     having furnished house-rents to the girl: Hierax on the one hand maintained
3     that he had dwelt (there) for a time, and that he had furnished house-rents
4     to a certain Thaesis; but Thonios appears to have stood in the capacity of guardian.
5     Ammonios the advocate said: We bought the building-plot; let him show forc[e].
6     But
7     nothing was shown of what they instructed the eminence of my lord the most eminent prefect of Egypt.
8     … [s]aid: Since my lord the most eminent prefect
9     of Egypt Flavius Laetus ordered that, if the girl should be found by agreement
10    expelled from this house, (she) be brought into possession, since …
11    this … having previously occurred … and certain other suits concerning ownership
12    both parties deposited, while the times still permitting, you are able
13    … … to instruct anew and obtain a ruling
14    on the same day, at the Hadrianeion. In the presence of Paulus the door-keeper,
15    before Asklep[ia]des son of Dionysodoros, councillor, arbitrator.
16    Ammonios the advocate said: Origenes against the opponents Artemidora and the heirs of E…us. That you are arbitrator chosen by us and by the opponents,
17    this not only the present speech teaches but also the dispatches of the most estimable p[ry]tanis … But since from that day
18    [on] which you were dispatched (as arbitrator), the times are today completed, on this account we are pres[ent]
19    [r]equesting that the default of the oppon[ents] be noted,
20    and that we be permitted to state the grounds of the case. The arbitrator said: Show when I was dispatched to become your arbitrator.
21    [Ammoni]os the advocate said: On the 23rd of the month Choiak you were dispatched
