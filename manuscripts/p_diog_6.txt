1     From a roll of scrutinies of Gaius Valerius Eudaimon, the former prefect, of which the heading is: scrutinies of Valerius Eudaimon, the former prefect, through Varianus Julianus
2     the tribune, from Pauni 1 until Mesore the tenth of the 6th year of Ant[oninus]
3     [Ca]esar the lord. After other matters, of the 20 columns, of the Arsinoite.
4     [Qui]ntus Julius Gaianus, aged …: the afore-written Iu[lius]
5     … eta … a thus: Flavia Primigeneia,
6     [having declar]ed that she wished to reside in the no[me Arsi]noite, requested that her son be granted
7     … in … scrutiny. And it is: Quintus
8     [Julius Gaia]nus, for the present [6th year], 7 years old. The mother of the chi[ld], Flavia Primigeneia, brought it, having registered as guardian
9     [Fla]vius Julianus, having declared that the father of [the child]
10    [Quintus] Julius Gaianus had died; the scrutiny …
11    … of [G]aianus, made by Rutilius Lupus the former pre[fect], according to which the father of the one being scrutinized, Quin[tus Juliu]s [G]aianus, was scrutinized; and of his patroness [Rufilla a tablet] of declaration on
12    seal[s] [in the 3rd year of the deified Titus, and of hers]elf a tabella on seals
13    [in the 7th year of the deified Hadrian, through which Flavia] Rufilla, before Haterius
14    [Nepos the former prefect,] by the vindicta freed her own slave-girl [Primigeneia, and of the one being scru]tinized a tablet of decla[ratio]n on seals in t[h]e 21st year of the deified Hadrian, through which Quin[tus Juliu]s Gaianus registered that there was a son to him, Quintus [Julius Gai]anus, from Flavia Primigeneia. And he declared [that] there was
15    to [him a dau]ghter, Julia Gaiana, of whom also he submitted a tablet [of dec]lara[tio]n on seals in the
16    following 22nd year. He also gave as [g]uarant[ors O]ualerius Bernikianus, Fla[v]ius Julianus, Pe[tr]o[nius K]eler, signing together with him that he had used
17    no one else's. And of the notation of Verginius Julianus, the former
18    tribune, against the aforesaid name:
19    Of Iulius Gaianus, seven, unmarked.
20    Scrutiny of Qui[ntus Julius Gaianus.]
