[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Souchos also called Agathos Daimon, have signed. Year 19 of Antoninus Caesar
2     the lord, in the month Hadrianos, 6th.
3     To Isidoros and M… of the Tanite and Arsinoite nomes,
4     keepers of the property-records (bibliophylakes of acquisitions),
5     from Protous son of Heraklas son of Herodes, his mother being Taorseus, from the village of Tebtynis in the Polemon division,
6     with her guardian and surety for payment, her kinsman Eutychos son of Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios. (The plots) which I registered
7     through the former record-keepers after the appointed term,
8     around the village of Tebtynis: of catoecic allotment-land two arouras
9     and of machimos-land one aroura, together three arouras,
10    held in common out of five arouras, I wish, voluntarily and
11    of my own choice, to mortgage to Lamprotyche, freedwoman of Heraklous also called Thermoutharion daughter of Didymos, against the
12    (sums) advanced for my use by her under a public loan
13    of a principal of one thousand three hundred sixty silver drachmas, and interest at a drachma per mina
14    each month. Therefore I submit (this) so that, toward not being (further) contracted,
15    through the oikonomos of Tebtynis, to the superintendent of the record-office, this registration (be made) against the aforesaid jointly-liable (properties); and I have brought
16    a receipt, without prejudice to Lamprotyche as regards the other sums I owe her under another loan of a principal of nine hundred silver drachmas and the interest from the present month.
17    I, Protous son of Heraklas son of Herodes, with my guardian and surety for payment, my kinsman Eutychos son of Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios,
18    have submitted the memorandum of mortgage of the (plots) of the
19    catoecic allotment, two arouras, and of machimos-land one aroura, together three arouras, around Tebtynis, all of the principal against the one thousand three hundred sixty drachmas, at interest of a drachma per mina
20    per month, without prejudice to Lamprotyche
21    as regards the other sums I owe her, a principal of nine hundred
22    drachmas and the interest from the present month, as
23    aforesaid. I, Menches, wrote also on behalf of Protous,
24    who is illiterate. Year 19 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
25    in the month Hadrianos, 6th. A copy of the registry of the Tanite and Arsinoite nomes,
26    of property (records), is subjoined. Year 19 of Antoninus Caesar the
27    lord, in the month Hadrianos, 6th.
