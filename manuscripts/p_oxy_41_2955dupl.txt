[TRANSLATION]
1     From the minutes of Basilianus, former prefect.
2     In year 1 of Marcus Aurelius Antoninus, Pharmouthi 11. Theon the advocate said: … I speak of the Sarapiac matter. Nemesion the advocate said: I enter a demurrer if you speak of the appealed suit …
3     in that case the archidikastes handed over to us the property. He did not appeal to the archidikastes in accordance with what he declared. After other matters, Theon the advocate said: a public … the keepers of the records issued a joint response to me … having seized the …
4     … these things came about. Sarapiakos said: he did not register it. Kleanthes [the advocate said:] …
5     declare these properties. Sarapiakos said: I purchased them. Nemesion [the advocate said: the …] …
6     … order it to be registered in the localities so that they may know. Basilianus said:
7     … you wish … [the advocate said: I read out the judgments.] …
8     …potinos. Kleanthes [the advocate said: that there first I seized] …
9     … this man must be the owner. Theon [the advocate] said: if [I obtained beforehand …] …
10    … in the city. Basilianus said: in the eleventh year Ch…oulos purchased it … Nemesion the advocate said: as he prays, it was not registered in the 11th year. Basilianus, having deliberated with those in
11    the council, said to Sarapiakos: they registered it in the most splendid …
12    suits, if you have any, against the heirs of Ch…oulos … rightly he appealed. Kleanthes the advocate said: order the surety-bond to be released. Basilianus
13    said: he rightly appealed. The surety-bond is released.
