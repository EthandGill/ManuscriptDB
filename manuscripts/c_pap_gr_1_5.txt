[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos, the one in charge of the court,
2     from Marcus Aemilius son of Marcus, of (the deme) Kallidia, and from
3     Theodote daughter of Dositheos, Persian, with her guardian
4     and surety for the matters signified in this agreement,
5     her husband Sophron, son of
6     …archos, Persian of the epigone. Concerning the matters at variance, Theodote agrees, for a time of eighteen months from Phamenoth of the present 17th year of Caesar, to rear and suckle, outside, at her own place in the city,
7     with her own milk, clean and uncorrupted,
8     the (child) which Marcus has entrusted to her, a foundling,
9     his own slave infant at the breast, Tyche,
10    receiving from him each month
11    as wages both for the milk and for the nursing, with
12    oil, 8 drachmas of silver. And Theodote has received in advance, through the surety Sophron, from Marcus
13    by hand from his house, for the aforesaid months
14    eighteen, the nursing-fees of nine months, amounting
15    to seventy-two drachmas; and if it happen within these (months) that the child suffer some human fatality, Theodote, having taken up another child, shall rear
16    and suckle it and present it to Marcus for
17    the equal nine months, receiving nothing at all, because she undertook to nurse without (extra payment for) immortality. While she is paid regularly for the remaining
18    monthly nursing-fees, she shall make for herself
19    and for the child the fitting care, not
20    spoiling the milk nor lying with a man nor becoming pregnant nor suckling another child besides; and whatever she may receive or be entrusted with she shall keep safe
21    and give back when it is demanded, or pay
22    the value of each, except for an evident loss, which,
23    being also made manifest, she shall be released from; and (she shall) not abandon
24    the nursing within the term; and if she transgress anything, she and Sophron shall be liable to seizure
25    and held until she pays the nursing-fees she has received and whatever she may receive, with the addition of one half, and the
26    damages and expenses and another 300 drachmas of silver, the
27    exaction being made for Marcus from both, who are
28    mutually liable for the payment, and from one and from whichever
29    of them he chooses and from all their property
30    just as by legal judgment, any (pledges) being void and
31    any pledges that they bring forward and all protection
32    of every kind; and as she fulfills each thing,
33    Marcus Aemilius shall provide her the monthly
34    nursing-fees for the remaining nine months and not take away the child within the term, or else himself pay the equal penalty. And Theodote shall present herself
35    to Marcus in the month …
36    willingly, and the child for inspection
37    by him.
38    Securing (clause) …
