[TRANSLATION]
1     (Year) 22. Chairemon and another to Apollonios, a cheirographia (handwritten declaration).
2     In the tenth year, in the month Dios, Egyptian Thoth, on the twenty-second, in Herakleopolis above Memphis. We swear by King Ptolemy
3     and Queen Cleopatra also called Tryphaina, the Father-loving and Brother-loving gods, and the Mother-loving goddess and the Savior god
4     and the Benefactress goddess and the Benefactor god and the New Father-loving god and the Eupator god and the Manifest gods and the
5     Father-loving gods and the Benefactor gods and the Brother gods and the
6     Savior gods and Sarapis and Isis and all the other gods
7     and goddesses:
8     We, Chairemon son of Herakleides, Macedonian of the settler cavalry,
9     and the son Herakleides, Macedonian, successor to the paternal
10    allotment, to Apollonios son of Euboulos, Macedonian of the settler
11    cavalry, (swear) to abide by and to perform for you and for those acting for you
12    all the terms of the contract of agreement which we have deposited for you through the same agoranomeion together with this cheirographia
13    here submitted, according to which we have agreed: the one
14    of us, Chairemon, concerning the property belonging to him around …
15    of the station of the places … to consent to the transfer (of registration),
16    and the other to consent to the cession and to all that
17    is set forth through the contract, and neither to violate the contract nor to act fraudulently in any of the things written through the contract or in this cheirographia,
18    nor (that) any of us shall bring a suit or lawsuit against you or those acting for you before any magistrate
19    nor before any tribunal concerning anything whatsoever of the matters about the
20    places. And if we transgress any of the things (written) through the contract
21    or of those written in this cheirographia, then the
22    cession shall be valid and the right of exaction shall belong to you and
23    to those acting for you of the penalties against us in accordance with the contract, and we ourselves would be liable to the oath
24    whenever we are called to account.
25    Chairemon.
26    To Apollonios, a cheirographia.
