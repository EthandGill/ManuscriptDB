[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius John Theodoros Menas Narses Chnoubammon Horion Hephaistos, the most glorious quaestor, duke and augustalis of the province of the Thebans, [declares]:
2     … …
3     one (thing): from the beginning, from when by God's help we took up the office, (we have shown) zeal and …
4     and here, with the benevolent aim of our most pious and most serene [masters Flavius Justin and Aelia Sophia],
5     of (our) office. Having learned therefore from the approaches made to us, that the …
6     under the name of sportulae are being oppressed by those of the staff, on the basis of unwritten [orders of those who give reminders] …
7     we judged it well, especially for the service of God, to …
8     things being done under an unwritten pretext; for upon written petitions …
9     of our mighty and gloriously-victorious emperor. Let it be known therefore … …
10    that the one owing to pay no more than two keratia to the reminding [provincial executor, or of whatever]
11    fortune he may be, let him pay over four keratia upon the reminder …
12    the one who has become the cause of the reminder having been accused, and of the exaction of the said sportulae …
13    he shall be compelled (to pay) to the one unreasonably charged; and if also a member of the staff …
14    of those withdrawing he shall be brought (to account), so that each of the …
15    to send up for the safety [and] continuance of the divine power of the most serene [emperor] …
16    and to be translated into the local dialect and posted up …
17    for understanding. Let it be posted.
