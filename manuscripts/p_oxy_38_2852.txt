[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apion the strategos,
2     from Sambous daughter of Amois,
3     with (her) guardian Saras son of Pesouris, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites.
4     Of the (claims) standing for me against
5     Apion son of Demetrios and
6     Dionysis son of Dionysios, his mother being
7     Demetria, from the same
8     city, concerning (the property) which they violently hold, of my cousin
9     Demetrios also called Diogenes,
10    the property being worth
11    ten talents, of which a share falls
12    to me by right, from the
13    deposited (will) of my grandfather Demetrios son of Dionysios,
14    through the public will; and further
15    also against the other of them,
16    Apion, concerning what he owes to
17    my deceased mother
18    Arsinoe, through the will of the same
19    grandfather of mine, of silver one talent — all (these claims)
20    needing the
21    cognizance of the most excellent governor
22    Vibius Maximus — I request that you order … your attendants that there be delivered to each of them
23    a copy of this, so that, having a written summons, they may present themselves wherever
24    the nome holds assize
25    or administers justice, and remain in attendance at the
26    tribunal of the same governor until
27    what I have against
28    them is brought to completion. For of the rights
29    belonging to me I lay hold.
30    Year 8 of the Emperor Caesar
31    Nerva Trajan Augustus
32    Germanicus Dacicus, the 4th (of)
33    Sebastos.
34    Citation-document.
35    Of Sambous.
