1     In the consulship of our lords Valens for the 5th time and Valentinian the younger [for the 1st, the eternal Augusti] …
2     The Aurelii Ioannes son of …os, aged about …, having a scar on the forefinger of the lef[t hand, and so-and-so]
3     his full brother (by the same father), aged about 29, having a scar on the middle finger of the left hand, [and] Eiren…
4     m…os, aged [about] 24, having a scar on the little finger of the left hand, and Sere[nos] … [ having a scar]
5     on the forefinger of the left hand, and Ara…tes son of Pa…, his mother Eirene, [aged about] … [ having a scar] …
6     and Silvanos son of Pachymios, his mother Helene, aged about 33, having a scar on the [fo]refinger of the lef[t hand, and so-and-so son of so-and-so]
7     his mother Thaesis, aged about …, having a scar on the thu[m]b of the [left] hand, and Isidoros son of Peue[s, son of so-and-so, aged about] … [ having a scar]
8     on the forefinger of the left hand, and Phibis, aged about …, having a scar on the … finger [ and so-and-so]
9     aged about …, having a scar on the ring finger of the left hand, [and] … [ his mother]
10    Diodora, aged about 46, having a scar on the forefinger of the left ha[nd], and Ibois, aged about … [ and] …
11    … both sons of Pathotes, their mother Tbesis, and …os son of A… [ having a scar]
12    on the ring finger of the left hand, presiding (over) …, and …
13    aged about 35, having a scar on the forefinger of the left hand, all of them from the village of … [ of the Hermo]p[o]lite nome, to Aurelius Dorotheos son of Silvanos, councillor of Hermou p[olis, greeting.]
14    We acknowledge, being mutually liable for payment, that we owe you and are indebted … [ for the damage that has occurred]
15    (and) the damage of the burning of your grain crops near Sesiu, of whe[at three hundred artabas and of chaff]
16    of wheat-chaff three hundred baskets, that makes 300 artabas of wheat and 300 baskets of chaff; which we will hand over …
17    of the fortunate 6th new indiction without delay in new whe[at, pure, unadulterated, undefiled]
18    … and the chaff new, the right of execution accruing to you fro[m us and from] all
19    our [property] as if in accordance with a legal decision; the bond is valid and, on being questioned, we ack[nowledged it.] …
20    And we owe you the damage that has occurred of the burning of [your grain crops near Sesiu]
21    of wheat three hundred artabas and of wheat-chaff three hundred baskets, and we will h[and over] …
22    as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Eulogios, wrote on their behalf, since they do not know letters,
23    (and) it was written through me, Dorotheos.
