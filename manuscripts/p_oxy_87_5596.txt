[TRANSLATION]
1     Philoxenus, count of Chara, tax-collector
2     provide to Nilammon from the account of rent
3     of the house under you for the first semester of the 3rd indiction
4     gold coin one and carat one,
5     totaling gold coin 1 and carat 1 only.
6     Year 256 125, Mechir, 3rd indiction.
7     I have noted gold coin
8     one and carat one only.
