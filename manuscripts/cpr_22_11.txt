1     With God, Auphe son of Nafe to you …
2     … [he] obtained [the task] to give you on behalf of public land, a plot of Oxyrhynchus
3     of indiction 7, for 4 arouras, 10 ammata of the aroura, 1 kerat 7½, numerical coin 1, 1/3, 1/12, one-third
4     one-twelfth only
5     It was written in the month of Phamonuthe … of indiction 8
6     It amounts to coin 1, 1/3, 1/12
