[TRANSLATION]
1     From Alypios.
2     I sent Dioskoriōn the assistant to you so that he might remain with the
3     harvest. But also provide him all the [expense]
4     according to custom. But also a pack-animal
5     for him provide running about
6     the [threshing-floors].
7     I pray that you fare well.
8     Sealed.
9     To Herōneïnos, manager of Theadelphia.
10    Year 1, Epei[ph] 9
