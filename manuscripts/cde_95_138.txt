[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Philoxenos the [most glorious],
2     Phaophi 29, of the fifth indiction.
3     Aurelii [Victor] son of Hermaous and Phoibammon
4     son of Psaios, Lart…, [and] Victor son of Psempnouthes, the
5     whole (group), field-guards over the plain of the village
6     of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, to Aurelius
7     Apollos son of Dioskoros, from the same village,
8     greeting. We agree that we have leased from [you]
9     for a three-year period, from the crops, with [God],
10    of the sixth indiction and of itself (?), the half belonging to you
11    of the whole arouras, which are waterless,
12    held in common with the heirs of Mousaios
13    son of Syrion, with respect to the other half part,
14    that is, the southern share, with all the rights,
15    according to the boundaries of the mound all around about the hill,
16    lying in the plain of the same village,
17    on condition that we cultivate this and we shall provide to you
18    the rent at a fixed rate, in the rising (of the Nile) and (?)
19    of the west, six artabas of grain and six artabas of barley by your
20    measure; nevertheless, if the Nile did not come
21    onto the aforesaid allotment, you shall remit to us
22    from the aforesaid rent three artabas of barley, [indisputably.]
23    The lease is valid and secure, and on being formally questioned we have agreed. Namely, of the allotment of Tcheneste, to the [north]
24    of the arouras of me, [Victor] son of Hermaous. Aurelii [Victor]
25    son of Hermaous and [Phoibammon] son of Psaios and Victor son of Psempnouthes, the af[oresaid, have leased] as is set out above. Josephis son of Hermaous,
26    elder, [having been asked, I wrote] for them the writing,
27    since they do not know (letters). …
28    Written [through me], Abraamios, from …
29    [Lease] of Victor son of Hermaous and Phoibammon son of Psaios and Victor son of Psempnouthes …
