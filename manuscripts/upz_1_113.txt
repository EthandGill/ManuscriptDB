[TRANSLATION]
1     Dioskourides to Dorion greetings. Of the letter to Dorion
2     the copy is below. Having thought then that the same things were sent also to you, see to it, that nothing be done contrary to these matters and [that] we not
3     of bad things about to be done escape [notice]. Farewell. Year 26, Xanthikos 1, Thoth 25.
4     To Dorion. The king and queen having long held [the view] that
5     all those under the kingdom be treated justly, but into the
6     city sailing down not a few, both [those] against you and [those]
7     and against those ordered under you, especially against those
8     encountering at the custom-houses concerning both violent seizures
9     and deceptions, some indeed also alleging to be slandered,
10    we wish you not to be unaware that all these things are
11    alien both to our conduct, no less also to your safety, whenever someone is convicted having wronged some
12    of the particulars. Therefore also so that nothing still such should happen
13    and [that] no one be wronged by anyone, especially of those
14    of the customs-officials attempting to slander, be yourselves on guard and to all the subordinates give orders concerning
15    these matters not carelessly.
