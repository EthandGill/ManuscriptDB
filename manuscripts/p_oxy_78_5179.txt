[TRANSLATION]
1     To Attius.
2     Heliodorus the friend begs you
3     to send to him his monthly payments, since [he] did not [send them] to his father-in-law
4     his. If then as he writes, [they] have not yet [been sent] to his father-in-law
5     [them], [send them] immediately to Heliodorus
6     send them to Alexandria.
7     But if [they] have been delivered to our [people], they will return them.
8     Farewell.
9     To Attius, scribe of the hundred-and-fiftieth [division] of Ptolemais of Hormus
