[TRANSLATION]
1     From Christodorus, administrator
2     … I received and I was paid by you on behalf of the rents of the holy John
3     newly-built for the crops of the third indiction, wheat artabae thirty-two and barley artabae
4     ten two-thirds and vegetable-seed artaba one, they amount to wheat artabae 32, barley artabae 10 2/3, and vegetable-seed artaba 1 only. It was written
5     Thoth 6 [of the] 3rd indiction and for your security this receipt as aforesaid. Christodorus, [it] confirms me
6     the receipt as aforesaid.
