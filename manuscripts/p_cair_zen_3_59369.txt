[TRANSLATION]
1     Philemon to Zenon, greetings. When I came before Heracl[ides]
2     my father-in-law concerning a share of the garden, having set aside [the matter of]
3     speaking, he accused me [of] being a slave and [accused me] to you …
4     to the general that you know me as a [household slave?]. [Therefore]
5     you will do well by writing to Aghenor so that I might be released for [work]
6     and not be destroyed in prison.
7     Farewell. Year 7, Epeiph …
8     To Zenon.
