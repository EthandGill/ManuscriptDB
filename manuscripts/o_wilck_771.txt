[TRANSLATION]
1     There measured into the granary of the temples,
2     of the produce of year 12, Psenamounis (son of) Kametis,
3     of wheat, in heap, three, total heaps of wheat 3. Year 12 of Nero
4     the lord, month Kaisareios 17. I, Apion, have signed.
5     Copy of a receipt in the name of Phthouminis (son of) Pharath( ), …, of wheat 3,
6     and a third, total heaps of wheat 3⅓.
