[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 44, Mesore 25, in Diospolis the Great of the Thebaid,
2     before Herakleides, agoranomos of the (district) Around Thebes.
3     Apollonios, also called Psenmonthes, son of Hermias also called
4     Petenephotes, of those from Diospolis the Great
5     of the Thebaid, of the mercenary cavalry, acknowledges to Psenchonsis son of Teephibis
6     and to Chonompres son of Harsiesis and to Aratres and to Pechytes,
7     the sons of Horos, and to Montometes son of Herieus — the five,
8     choachytai (libation-pourers) dwelling in the same city —
9     that he has settled with them concerning the (matter) which he brought against them by
10    a petition into the box set up in Ptolemais
11    by the chrematistai (judges), of whom the introducer is Ammonios,
12    in Epeiph of the 44th year, both concerning a house — from sixteen
13    cubits, seven cubits — for which it happens that
14    a local deed of sale had been made out to them before this acknowledgment,
15    and concerning outrage and blows and unlawful injury, (to the amount of) five talents of copper
16    and other (things) which he specified — (namely) that after this he will obtain
17    through another petition his right as is proper, and that he will not
18    proceed, neither he, Apollonios, himself, nor any other
19    on his behalf, against Psenchonsis and his associates,
20    nor against any other of their party, concerning anything
21    at all of the things indicated through the petition,
22    in whatever manner. But if he does proceed, or any other person
23    on his behalf, let the proceeding be void, and let him further pay to Psenchonsis and his associates, or to whomever of
24    their party he proceeds against, as a penalty forthwith, twenty talents of copper coin,
25    and, sacred to the sovereigns, four hundred drachmas of stamped silver,
26    and none the less let him be compelled to act according to what is written above.
27    To the choachytai.
