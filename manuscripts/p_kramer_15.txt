[TRANSLATION]
1     [the period] for which I hold (it), reckoned from today,
2     the day which is the second of Epeiph of the present
3     third indiction, the half-share belonging to you
4     of the whole hous[e] situated in
5     the (quarter) of the Hermopolites, in the quarter of the City of Apeliotes,
6     in the street of Saint Euphemia, facing toward
7     the south-west, of whatever position it is, with all its
8     legal right(s), for my use and dwelling; and
9     I agree to give you by way of rent
10    for this, each year, four keratia of the (current) exchange,
11    that is, four keratia only, which rent I shall pay to you
12    at the end of each year without delay;
13    and after the period of this lease
14    I shall hand over to you the same half-share of all
15    the same house, with all its
16    legal right(s), as I received (it). The lease (is)
17    valid and secure, and being formally questioned on all (points) I gave my assent.
18    I, Onnophris son of Petros, farmer, the aforewritten,
19    have leased (it) as set forth above. I, Aristophanes
20    son of Phoibammon, from Hermopolis, having been requested, wrote on
21    his behalf, since he does not know letters. I, Palous, son
22    of the late Poerios, from Hermopolis, bear witness
23    to the present lease, having heard (it) from the c[ontracting party].
24    I, Theodoros son of Paninos, from Hermopolis, bear witness to the
25    lease, having heard (it) from t[he contracting party].
26    Drawn up through me, Ioustos, by God's help, notary.
