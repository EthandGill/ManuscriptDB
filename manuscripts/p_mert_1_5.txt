[TRANSLATION]
1     To Boethos, of the first friends and strategos [of the Thebaid],
2     from Berenike daughter of Dorotheos, a citizen[ess of those from Pto]lemais. Being wronged by Androniko[s, of the same]
3     city's catoecic cavalrymen, I submit[ted to the king a petition]
4     concerning the fact that, there being city land belonging to me, [bought from]
5     Panas son of Aratos the Panopolite, that man, being a neighbor, [encroached] upon part of my land; [and he wrote back ackno]wledging that it had been surveyed by him … [in the presence]
6     of the city surveyor, and that there came out, on account of which there was hesitation, a part amounting to 6 arouras. After this [I found the]
7     boundaries shifted, and I requested that he be sent [to you];
8     in response to which, a letter having been written to Athamas the epistates [to investigate concerning]
9     our case, I appeared before him, [and he ordered Ty]chon to mark off the land for me; but the
10    adversary having dodged the voluntary delimitation [to be made], Athamas, [wish]ing to learn the matters declared in the ex[amination],
11    wrote to Tychon to report; which that man, scarcely-belie[vably,] declining as though the boundaries were not according to it …
12    upon the land and (the) shifted (boundaries), [although]
13    it being customary in the record-office [to examine the boundaries]
14    and the quantity of the depressions (uncultivated land), if any seem[s] … [but the ad]versary has no neighboring royal lan[d] …
15    such to occur, but in another mound from the land
16    of him, being of 20 arouras, neighboring m[e he holds on lease]
17    the royal land adjoining mine ab[out] … [. This man, therefore,]
18    transgressing the judgment given [to me as has been said]
19    and over-bounding the royal lan[d, paid to the god]
20    Soter the customary first-fruits, con[trary to the purchase registered]
21    by me in the public (record). I request y[ou therefore, since you ever]
22    continue assigning justice to all, [to give order to bring] us up before you, and if matters be as I write, when [examined to be true,]
23    taking the same Panas along with me, [to compel that man]
24    to fulfill the (measures) [shown] according to the rope-survey,
25    or to mark off for me my city land [shown] according to the [judgment];
26    for this happening, I shall be
27    helped.
