[TRANSLATION]
1   and the surplus from the 11th to the 20th of ferriage; and it is as follows:
2   11th: Antinoos, exporting in a small wooden boat 40 bundles, of expenditure 20 drachmas;
3   Heron, exporting in a male barge two sacks of wool, of expenditure 80 drachmas;
4   and from the 12th to the 13th: of profits, Hermes (i.e. lucky gain).
5   14th: Phesis, exporting offic( ) in a Greek ship seven mats of barley, of weight 14 pounds, without expenditure.
6   15th: Drakon, exporting in his own male barge 200 bundles, of expenditure 100 drachmas;
7   16th: of profits, Hermes.
8   17th: Aithiopous, exporting in a Greek skiff two mats of barley, of weight 4 pounds, of expenditure 80 drachmas;
9   and from the 18th to the 20th: of profits, Hermes;
10   the total for the ten-day period comes to 280 drachmas.
11   [...] of expenditure(?) [ ]
12   27th: Sarapion, lik[ewise, exporting in] a boat(?) with a hollow prow [.....] ...nomou 1, (of which) of expenditure 80 drachmas;
13   28th: of profits, He[rm]es.
14   29th: Herodianos, exporting in a boat with a hollow prow baskets [......]on[.. of] expenditure — 20 drachmas;
15   ...r Paouesis export[ing] in a [Gr]eek ship isto.[....]..atikon [drachmas] 20;
16   30th: of profits, Hermes;
17   the total for the five-day period comes to 260 drachmas.
18   and of the eklogos (selection-account) likewise 888 drachmas;
19   altogether the to[t]al / Arme(?)
20   which were also paid out at the bank of e( ) 8.
