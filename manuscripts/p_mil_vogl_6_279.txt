[TRANSLATION]
1     Patron to Laches the
2     dearest greetings.
3     a donkey for bed tomorrow
4     send and a shepherd
5     and hay, so that … Dorus may come down, since [he] much troubled me
6     because of [the barley of] Akkatios; straightway then remember him that he should enter; do not hold him from me.
7     supply him [a] sun-cup of wine from Taley
