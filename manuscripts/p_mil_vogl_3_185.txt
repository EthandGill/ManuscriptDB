[TRANSLATION]
1     In the second year of Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrian Antoninus Augustus Pius, Mecheir 1, in Tebtynis of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. There mutually acknowledge: Tephersais daughter of Heron, about twenty years old, without distinguishing mark, and her mother
3     Taorsenouphis daughter of Orseus, [about] forty-three [years old],
4     with a scar on the left hand, both with the guardian, the father of
5     Taorsenouphis, Orseus son of Orseus, about
6     sixty-seven years old, with a scar on the right forearm, and the (man) who had become
7     the husband of Tephorsais, Protas, about twenty-one years old,
8     with a scar on the forehead, and his father Aunes son of Onnophris,
9     about forty-[s]ix years old, with a scar on the left thumb,
10    the four (of them): Tephers[ai]s and Protas (acknowledge) that they have dissolved
11    the cohabitation with each other, which had been established for them
12    by a written contract of agreement of marriage completed through
13    the same record-office in the [tw]enty-first year of the god
14    Hadrian, in the mo[nth] Pachon 14, which they also agree to be invalid,
15    and that it be permitted to each of them to manage his own affairs
16    as he may choose, and that Tephorsais may
17    live with another man, she being free from malicious accusation in every way.
18    And Taorsenouphis the mother (acknowledges) that she has received from Aunes
19    the dowry through the contract, one hundred sixty silver drachmas
20    and the parapherna, and that all (of them) will not
21    proceed against one another concerning [the other things pertaining to] the
22    cohabitation nor concerning any matter at all up to now. [Signatories: Hippalo]s son of Chrates, 69 years old, with a scar …
23    on the right (hand), and Chaire[mon son of Bastos,] … [years old, with a] scar …
24    Protas and Au[nes] … we [acknowledge]
25    that Tepher[s]ais has dissolved the cohabitation, and not
26    to bring suit, as set forth above. I wrote on
27    their behalf, they not knowing letters, Chairemon
28    son of Bastos. Tephersais daughter of Heron and her mother
29    Taorsenouphis, with the guardian Orseus son of Orseus —
30    Tephersais (acknowledging) that she has dissolved the cohabitation with
31    Protas, and T[aorse]nouphis
32    that she has received from Aunes the dowry and
33    the parapher[na], and not to bring suit, as
34    set forth above. I wrote on their behalf, Hippalos son of Chrates, they not knowing letters. It has been registered
35    through the record-office in Tebtynis.
