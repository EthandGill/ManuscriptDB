[TRANSLATION]
1     … and son and holy [spirit] […] [was written] in month Epeiph 8, indiction 15
2     I Seueros the son of the blessed Peter the doctor of the castle
3     of Kheme the nome of the city of Hermonthis [I went] the prize-bearer of the martyr [and the] victor of those weeping for the sake of the saint
4     Aba Phoibamon the community of Kheme through […] Kyriakos the archpresbyter and the [steward of the] community of the castle
5     of Kheme, that since in this season this one who [has in his heart]
6     15 indiction the god the good in great abundance […] on my […] beloved son […] towards the forgiveness of sins I have prayed
