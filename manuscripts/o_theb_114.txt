[TRANSLATION]
1     Serenus, having become tax-collector of grain of Charax
2     received for addition [to the] harvest of year 32, [in the] name of
3     Senapathus, Plenios [son] of Arsiesouheus the younger
4     wheat, 1½ artabae; and [in the] name of Esouerios Pachomis
5     wheat 6 artabae; and [in the] name of Pachomis the elder, wheat 6 artabae
6     and [in the] name of Esouerios [son of] Atrheos, wheat 24 artabae; total 24 artabae
7     total together wheat artabae 224. Year 33
8     Thoth 20. Serenus, I have signed.
