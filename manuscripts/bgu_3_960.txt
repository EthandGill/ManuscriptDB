[TRANSLATION]
1     (Chi-Rho) Theon to Sambas the oil-maker: provide two xestai of oil to the 9 stone-carriers, together with Neilammon the potter and
2     one carpenter, departing to (the) Skelos to put in good order the great doors, for
3     [the maintenance of] 3 days,
4     of oil, 2 xestai [o]nly. Mesore 4, beginning of the 5th indiction. (hand 2) † I have signed for two xestai of oil only †.
