[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aur]elius Pausanias, son of Valerius, former magistrate of the city of the Mothites,
2     to Aurelius Psais, son of Pamouris, from the village of Kellis of the same
3     city of the Mothites, greeting.
4     [I acknowledge that I have] granted to you, by a gift eternal and irrevocable, from
5     [now] for all time, from the properties belonging
6     [to me], lands on the east of the village of Kellis, a plot
7     [for] building, on the south and north fifteen carpenter's
8     cubits, on the east and west twenty-five cubits.
9     [Neighbours]: on the east the lands of me, Pausanias; on the west the camel-stable of Horos, son of Mersis; on the south the house of you and your son and your … ;
10    on the north a public street, or whoever the neighbours may be on every side,
11    so that it may be possible for you to take control and [be lord of it] and
12    to have authority to manage and administer concerning the place [granted]
13    to you, by your own free will, in whatever manner you may [choose, the guarantee] following upon me through everything against everyone who shall come against or lay claim. The [gift], written in a single copy, shall be valid,
14    on my subscription, and secure [as if] deposited in a public archive, and being questioned I agreed.
15    In the consulship of Flavius Dalmatius, brother of our [master]
16    Constantine Augustus, and Domitius Zenophilus, [the] most illustrious men.
17    [Ha]thyr 1.
18    Aurelius Pausanias, son of Valerius, former magistrate, the aforesaid, have granted the aforesaid bare plot as aforesaid, and being questioned I agreed.
