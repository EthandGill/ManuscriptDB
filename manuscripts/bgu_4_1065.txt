[TRANSLATION]
1   Copy of a bank-payment through the
2   bank of Pappion of the Macedonians.
3   Year one of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajan
4   Augustus Germanicus, month Kaisareios, 2nd intercalary day.
5   Herodes son of Leon, to Mysthas son of Menelaos, goldsmith,
6   (acknowledges) that he has received the price of assayed gold,
7   of eight minaiai, which he made for him
8   into a pair of Magian bracelets, double-curved,
9   serpent-headed, by the Arsinoite standard,
10   which Hero[des] also received — of the agreed
11   charge: two thousand silver [d]rachmas
12   eight hundred and sixteen, total 2816 dr. And if
13   Herodes wishes to remake the afore-
14   said articles, he shall give, for each minaion,
15   for wastage one quarter. And if also
16   he should dispose (of them), likewise he shall give, for each minaion,
17   one quarter, Mysthas repaying the price current at the time …
18   I, Mystharion son of Menelaos, gold-
19   smith, have received from [He]rodes
20   the price of assayed gold, of
21   eight minaiai, which I made
22   for him into a pair of Magian
23   bracelets: silver drachmas
24   two thousand eight hundred sixteen,
25   [total 2]816 dr. And if he wishes to
26   remake them or dispose (of them), I will re-
27   pay the price current at the time,
28   taking back the articles, and
29   for each minaion one quar-
30   ter, as aforesaid.
