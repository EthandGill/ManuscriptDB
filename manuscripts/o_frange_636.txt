[TRANSLATION]
1     the least ... the God
2     he fell, he did obeisance to his beloved father
3     saying and ... made the spirit
4     you are angry with me, with us. I took suffering
5     now on account of the place of trial ... you cast me out, saying: I took him, I said to the man who is behind his power
6     do love, you worship towards the two of flesh
7     sins, these which are commandment with us, [that] I take them from above which I ... behold, and for
8     he rested, saying: [the one] which will rest, he gives it
9     truly of God, a trial in
10    the Lord, my beloved father
11    who is complete of all my things
