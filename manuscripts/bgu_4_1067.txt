1   […]on, the (son) of Papous,
2   […]s, the (son) of Onnophris, of those from the village of Eu(hemeria),
3   [a Persi]an of the epigone. I wish to lease
4   the working mill belonging to you in the village of Theadelphia, in which (there are)
5   five Theban millstones with tables and …(?),
6   and four stone mortars, and the two adjoining rooms,
7   … and the [rema]ining equipment of the mill, for
8   two years from the present fifth year of Imperator

12   four full-grown cocks, free of deduction and of risk;

14   eleven (artabas of) bread-grain, free of grain-charge, and
15   I will pound and grind, free of wages, per year, three
16   artabas of fine flour and one-and-a-half artabas of white wheat,
17   the public dues on the mill per year, of …,
