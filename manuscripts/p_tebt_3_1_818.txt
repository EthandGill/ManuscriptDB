[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios and Cleopatra]
2     [gods Manifest, in the seventh year, in the priesthood of Philostratos]
3     [son of … son of Alexandros, and of the Saviour] gods and the
4     [Brother] gods and the [Benefactor] gods and the Father-loving gods and
5     the gods Manifest and [the] Mother-loving [gods], the athlophoros of Berenike Euergetis being Aspasia daughter of Chryserms, the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos
6     being Isidora daughter of Apollonios, the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator being Eirene
7     daughter of Ptolemaios, in the month Gorpiaios, on the thirteenth = Phamenoth
8     the thirteenth, in Trikomia of the division of Themistos of the
9     Arsinoite nome. Ioudas son of Iosephos, a Jew of the
10    epigone, lent to Agathokles son of Ptolemaios, a Jew of those of Molossos
11    stationed in the Herakleopolite (nome), an infantryman on fixed pay,
12    two talents of copper coinage and five hundred drachmas, for twelve months from the aforesaid date, at interest
13    of two drachmas per mina each month.
14    This is the loan which Agathokles additionally owed to Ioudas
15    from the five talents which he had received from Ioudas for the setting-up of a joint exchange-trading business, according to a written contract of agreement of which the keeper of the contract is Ananias son of Ionathes, a Jew
16    of the epigone. Let Agathokles repay to Ioudas the aforesaid loan and the interest in the month Mecheir of the
17    eighth year; and if he does not repay as has been written, let him forfeit one-and-a-half times (the amount). The contract is valid. Witnesses: Deinias son of Aineas,
18    Thraseas son of Sosibios, Thebon son of Phanokles, Samaelos son of Ioanes, the four Jews of the epigone, Theodoros son of Theodoros who is also
19    called Samaelos, Nikanor son of Iason, the two Jews of those through Dositheos
20    of the first hipparchy, eighty-aroura men.
21    … [through] …
22    I, Agathokles, have the two talents [and the five hundred drachmas]
23    of copper, the aforesaid loan; I have deposited the contract
24    (as) valid with the contract-keeper Deinias.
