[TRANSLATION]
1   Harpaesis son of Phenophis, a minor, paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the 1st year of Trajan Caesar
3   the lord, on account 8 drachmas.
4   The same, the remaining 9 drachmas.
5   I, Hermogenes, the collector, wrote (this).
