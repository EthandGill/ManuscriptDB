[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis for the produce of the 2nd year
2     Of Antoninus Caesar the lord
3     Pauni 4, for Charakos, named Chatabutos [son of] Heriophmoites
4     Wheat one-quarter artaba, makes wheat one-quarter artaba. Pete has been marked.
