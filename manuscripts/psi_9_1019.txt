[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 8, Hathur 16, under Apollonius, [one] concerning the agoranomic [office] of the Memnonia of the Pathyrite [nome].
2     Imouthes Thotsutu of those from the Memnonia, priest of Pan the greatest god, [states] as [being] of 40 years
3     of middle build, honey-colored, sinewy, long-faced, straight-nosed to Pikeites Psemminis and to Tateathurei Persinei, with the lord of her own brother Pikeites the aforementioned
4     to have received from them bronze coin(s) thirteen talents and to withdraw
5     from ten days sacred abstinence and their fruits and liturgy service
6     and the things co-occurring and coming beside all annually each one and the impending
7     to him share of the added [days] and the things co-occurring to these all in the sanctuary of Aphrodite
8     called Hathor in the [places] according to the tombs of the Memnonia and not to make a disturbance
9     neither to come against neither himself nor any other on behalf of him against the Pikeites and
10    Tateathurei nor any other of those from him concerning the ten days. But if not, the action/entrance
11    let it be null/void and let the one going against pay in addition to whomever he goes against concerning these [things]
12    immediately a fine of bronze coin(s) ten talents and the harm according to the law and into the
13    royal [treasury] of marked silver one hundred drachmae [and] no [less] let the agreed-upon [things] be binding.
14    Apollonius.
