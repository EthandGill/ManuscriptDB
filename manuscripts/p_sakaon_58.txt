[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aurelius Ouená]phris, whose mother is Soueles, [with as surety his own] father Aurelius Aki…s, [came to an agreement with one another]
2     whose [mother] is Arous, from the village of Theoxenis,
3     with the [Aureli]i Melas son of Her[akleides and P]aesis son of Sata[bou]s, both village-heads (komarchs) of the village of Theadelphia,
4     on condition that I, Ouenaphris, go up to Babylon and work on behalf of the village of Theadelphia
5     for the period of one month from tomorrow's day,
6     which is the twelfth of Epeiph of the present
7     15th year and 14th year and 7th year, he receiving as wages daily two hundred and forty drachmas of silver;
8     and thereupon (from here) I have received for the
9     month one talent of silver and one thousand
10    two hundred drachmas …
11    … from him, when the
12    thirty days are completed, to send an equal
13    workman on behalf of the aforesaid man; and if
14    they do not send an equal (workman) on his behalf within the fixed time, then Melas and Paesis shall furnish
15    to him, for the time that lapses, [daily] two hundred
16    and forty drachmas of silver, [just as] they agreed with one another;
17    and on being formally questioned they consented. I, Aurelius Ouenaphris, with as surety our father Aki…s, have agreed to all the [aforesaid conditions] as [stated above. I, Aurelius] Sophron, [wrote] on his behalf as he is illiterate. …
18    The 15th year and 14th year and 7th year of our lords Diocletian
19    and Maximian the Augusti and [Constantius] and Maximian [the]
20    most illustrious Caesars, [Epeiph 11].
