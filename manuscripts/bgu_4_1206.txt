[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidora to Asklas her brother,
2     greeting and continually good health(?)
3     just as I pray. I have received
4     what you wrote. The brother Panis-
5     kos has written that Noumenios has sent
6     Philon the steward (oikonomos)
7     to him in haste con-
8     cerning the administration; he has not yet sig-
9     nalled what has come about. And concerning
10    Areios, Patr... himself writes
11    to him for the sake of registering
12    (the) wheat. And you and Hara-
13    motes, play the man in
14    the bringing-in of the price [of] the
15    lentils and emmer. If anything
16    else befalls, I will signal to you,
17    and take care of yourself, that you may be
18    well.
19    Farewell. Year 3, Hathyr 6.
20    early(?).. (verso)
21    To Asklas her brother.
