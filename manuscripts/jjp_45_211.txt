[TRANSLATION]
1     Zosilus, scribe of farmers, to Akousilaos, grain-[buyer]
2     greetings. measure out to Pamēs, son of Arnophios
3     for the royal [land] which he farms [wh]eat
4     two, there become wheat 2. year 20, Hathur [16.]
5     Marres, village-scribe; measure out
6     the aforesaid artabas of wheat two, there become wheat artabas 2.
7     year 20, Hathur 16.
8     Onnophris, lea[d]er of farmers; aut[h]orize artabas 2, there become [artabas 2].
9     year 20, Hathur 16.
