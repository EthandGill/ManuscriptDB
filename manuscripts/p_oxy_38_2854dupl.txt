[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Pasion also called Apollonios, who served as gymnasiarch, prytanis-in-office, councillor of the
2     city of the Oxyrhynchites.
3     From Septimius Eudaimon, gymn[asiarch],
4     councillor of the same city, son of Sep[timius]
5     Serenus, who served as exegetes and as pryta[nis] of the same city. Since, contrary to
6     the decrees made for me by the most excellent c[ouncil],
7     on the terms by which I undertook the acceptance of the gymnasiarchy with limits according to my resources, you named me today, which
8     is the 16th, in the most excellent council for the days of the euthen[iarc]hy, immediately
9     upon the records of the council
10    I tendered to you my withdrawal; and now too,
11    through these documents, my withdraw[al]
12    I offer to you of all the property
13    of resource belonging to me, on condition that you, taking over
14    the property belonging to me, fulfill
15    on my behalf both the gymnasiar[chy]
16    and the euthenarchy and all the
17    consequent expenses upon it, an[d further]
18    also the grain dues owed by me
19    and the money dues, both on [the (property)]
20    which I have acquired as belonging (to me) and that which I hol[d]
21    in lease, a share of an estate formerly
22    of Claudius Syrion, eternal gymnasiarch of most illustrious Alexandria. Against all which dues
23    the wine that came out from the lands has been distrained by the dekaprotoi.
24    And on being formally questioned I acknowledged.
25    Year 6 of the Emperors Caesars Marci
26    Julii Philippi, Pious, Fortunate,
27    Augusti, Hathyr the sixteenth.
28    … apia to the sister
