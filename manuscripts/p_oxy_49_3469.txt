[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings to you, my lord Heraclides.
2     [F]rom when I departed from Phenebytos the tax collectors have not profited from what was handed over to me in Pson, for they did not demand any of the suspected [amounts], but they alleged
3     and [that] the helpers had not exacted [them] together with them. The one from Prosopite, Doras Pibeke[ios … artabas] ἐneb[aleto]
4     from Bompah today, and I made the
5     [do]nkeys to carry [loads] from Bombah to Pson.
6     And the one sent to Pson
7     Ammonius Panetbeutos [... artabas] was assessed today in the farmstead of the overseer
8     in Thomnhei. And of necessity
9     I detained one of the tax collectors of
10    Phenebytos, since he is their scribe, so that being in Phenebytos I might see the account of the suspected [amounts] and demand [them].
11    I pray that you are in good health. Epeiph 5.
12    To Heraclides …
