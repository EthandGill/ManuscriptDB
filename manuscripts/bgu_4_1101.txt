[TRANSLATION]
1   Sheet.
2   To Protarchos,
3   from Dionysia daughter of Dionysios, a citizen, with her guardian Eu...sion son of the same (i.e. of Dionysios)
4   and [from Ammonios son of Diony]sios, an Alexandrian. Since, having lived together
5   for [...] years, we were separated from one another, but now have come together into the same
6   life again, we agree on these terms: that, since
7   [Am]monios has received from Dionysia from hand to hand out of the house as dowry women's garments
8   and of earrings
9   two pairs of gold quarter-pieces, and four silver bracelets each of 4 drachmas weight,
10   and stamped (silver?) — the whole on account of silver coming to two hundred drachmas — from now on
11   Ammonios shall provide her, Dionysia, with all necessities and the
12   clothing
13   [as] for a wedded wife according to the means of his property, and not maltreat
14   her nor cast (her) out
15   nor abuse (her) nor bring in another woman, or (else) pay back the dowry
16   with half as much again,
17   the execution being made for Dionysia both from Ammonios himself and
18   from all his property
19   as if by judgment; and that Dionysia for her part keep her duties toward her husband and
20   the
21   common life justly, neither sleeping away nor staying away by day from the house
22   without Ammonios' consent, nor ruining nor damaging the common
23   household, nor consorting with another man; or, if she herself does any of these things,
24   being condemned, she shall lose her dowry, apart from the transgressor being liable
25   to the fixed penalty;
26   and within five business days from when we give notice to one another, we shall draw up
27   also through the sacrificers the contract of cohabitation [...] and the appropriate things,
28   and (regarding) the female child born to us from one another whose name is Ammonarion
29   [...] for the child, as we may at the time jointly de-
30   cide [...] we request [...]
