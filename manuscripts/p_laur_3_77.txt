[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the Lord and Master Je]sus Christ our God and Saviour, in the reign of our most divine master Flavius
2     [Heraclius the eternal Augustus and Em]peror, in the ninth year, Epeiph the twelfth, of the seventh indiction, in Hermopolis of the Thebaid.
3     … [to the public account of the city] of the Hermopolites, [through] you, Flavius Magistor son of Kallinikos, assistant of the accounting-office and apportioner (diastoleus)
4     … [of the division of Dioskorides, from the sa]me city, Aurelius Enoch son of Pkalios, by his mother a farmer, from the village of …
5     … [greet]ing. May your Excellency be willing to relieve Theophile, the most noble daughter of Victor also called …
6     … [from the sa]me city, from the public registers handled by you, of the …
7     … of [pure] wheat, with freight-charges and one-percent (charges) and surcharges and all kinds of expenses, of an artaba
8     … [one] and a half and a quarter, and contributions, and of gold ten keratia, on account of the desert-land (?) of Kopreus
9     … [from the same city; and the] public assessment in both wheat and gold to impose upon me and my heirs
10    [and successors and assigns, on behalf of the public] (dues) of (the land) sold to me by the aforesaid Theophile, by written
11    … [security, of a quarter] aroura of sowable and unwatered land lying in the marsh of the village of Thynis in the …
12    … I found. The successors and tenants acknowledge that they shall pay in and contribute to the same public
13    … [account the public assessmen]t, with [the] future contributions of the public (dues) of the same division of Dioskorides.
