[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ our God and Savior, in the month Pharmouthi …
2     in the village of Papa megale. Aurelius Joseph [son of] Phib, originating from the village of Papa megale of the [Hera]kleopolite nome, to Aurelius Georgios son of the late …ios from the same village, greeting. I a[cknowledge]
3     through this my written security, by my own good faith and warranty, without any guile or fear
4     or force or deceit or compulsion or any circumvention or pleading of exception of law,
5     of my own free will and self-chosen wish and unchangeable purpose, that I have sold to you and
6     ceded to you and conveyed to you, the same Georgios, in fullest ownership, …
7     from now and for all subsequent time forever, from the things belonging to me and that came to me
8     from the lawful inheritance of my father, the aforewritten Phib, in accordance with the … that have come to me
9     and the legal titles and securities all, and the long-standing possession,
10    the whole single house situated in the same village in the lane called the southern one, and
11    … with entrance and exit and going-up and going-down and likewise
12    and air … from the foundations up to the air and the
13    courtyard within it and a well — total: one whole house and one courtyard and [one] well;
14    the neighbors being: to the south and east, a public street;
15    to the north … ; to the west, the empty plot of Anoup … the
16    locations of these, at the price agreed and mutually pleasing and approved between one another,
17    … which, of the price,
18    twenty-two gold solidi, I, Joseph the seller, have already herewith received from you
19    [the purchaser, G]eorgios, in full, by hand, concerning which counting of the whole price, and
20    … so that from now and for all subsequent time forever you may hold
21    [and own and pos]sess (it), [with children and] descendants and [those who receive from] you,
22    … to sell.
