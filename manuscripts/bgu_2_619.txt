1     … the [fix]ed revenue (prosodos) on account of the property which was his to have received, and afterward, as the village-scribe of the village
2     in which the plots are, having declared that the property of Deios
3     had been registered in error [b]y the village-scribe before him, because [it] had been sold before the (period of) seizure of the supervision (epiteresis), and that the registered [re]venue ought to be relieved, the (sum) accruing
4     until it be examined whether, before the seizure for the supervision,
5     it was sold, and by whom, and to what owners it was delivered
6     into the (state) service, that it had been withheld; and, at the examination, Nikarion
7     also called Ta[sou]charion, daughter of Heras son of Ap[o]llonios, registered in the quarter
8     of the Gymnasiu[m], with as guardian her kinsman Isidoros son of Sokrates,
9     swore on oath that her predecessors-in-title, Isidora daughter of Isido[ros] son of Eu]dorion, and the latter's daughter
10    D[io]nysia,
11    had purchased from the [afore]written Deios, Ptolem[a]i[os] son of Chairemon,
12    something from the [...]th year of the deified Hadrian, in the month Hadrianos, on the 30th, [acc]ording to a handwritten document also attested through a public acknowledgment,
13    in the same [yea]r, on Tybi 21, from Bernikis: a half part of beach-land (aigialos),
14    of a vineyard — arouras … 2 8(?) — and another half part of a vineyard, 2 arouras,
15    and likewise a half part of vine-land, 1 aroura, and of Greek reed, an eighth of an aroura,
16    before Deios was delivered into the supervision; for afterward,
17    after the declared property was sold, it was seized for
18    the supervision of estate (lands), of the lease of Ptolemaios son of Kronios, in the month Pauni
19    of the 18th year; and for the proof of these (matters) she had appended
