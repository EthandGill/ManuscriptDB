[TRANSLATION]
1     … of … …
2     of the three (officials?) from the village of Karanis,
3     from the Aurelii Kapiton son of Heliodoros, from the quarter
4     of Apollonios' Camp, and Ptolemaios son of Ptollarion,
5     from the village of Karanis. We wish to lease
6     from you, out of the holdings belonging to the village, from the name of
7     Neilos son of Kasianos, in the surveyed district of Karanis, on the 7th sealed-plot,
8     in the locality Pannipoi, five arouras of private sowable land,
9     or however many there may be, whether more or less,
10    for the sowing of the present year alone,
11    of the 8th year and 6th year of the present consulship, without rent, on condition that
12    we, the group of Kapiton and Ptolemaios, shall pay all
13    the dues belonging to the arouras, both grain-dues and barley-dues
14    and money-dues and annona-levies in full, on behalf of the same
15    arouras for the aforesaid yearly term; and
16    all the work of the arouras we the lessees shall do, and after the yearly term we shall hand back
17    the arouras after the harvesting. The lease is valid, and
18    on being formally questioned we acknowledged it.
19    In the consulship of our masters Constantine and
20    Licinius the Augusti for the 3rd time, Thoth 16. Aurelius
21    Kapiton, I have leased as aforesaid.
22    Aurelius Ptolemaios, I have leased as aforesaid.
23    Aurelius Seueros, I wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
