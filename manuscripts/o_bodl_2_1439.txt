r.2    τοῦ κυρίου Παῦνι ιθ ὑπὲρ Χάρακος ὀνόματος Ἰσιδώρου Ὥρου διὰ γεωργῶν Ὥρου Ποριεύθου

[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, produce of year 22 of Antoninus Caesar of
2     the lord, Payni 19, for Charax, name of Isidorus [son] of Horus, through farmers Horus [and] Porieuthos
3     and partners, of wheat artabas two-thirds, become wheat artabas 2 [and] 1/3. Sabinus, I have confirmed.
