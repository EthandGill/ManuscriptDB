[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos.
2     From Herakleides son of Herakleides and from his wife Thermouthis daughter of Pankrates,
3     with her guardian her husband himself, and from Spintheros son of Philammon, an Alexandrian. Concerning the matters in dispute, Spintheros agrees that he has received back from Herakleides
4     and Thermouthis, by hand, out of the house, the (sums) which
5     he had lent to them under the agreement
6     completed before the same tribunal in the eleventh year of Caesar,
7     in (the month) Tybi, namely 200 drachmas of silver and the interest on them; and that the same Spintheros has been paid in full by the same Herakleides [and Thermouthis] for that which he undertook [by the] contract as a loan-club (eranos) and [the interest, by hand]
8     out of the house, and that no (right of) [proceeding shall be left] to Spintheros
9     nor to anyone on [his] behalf against Herakleides and
10    Thermouthis, neither concerning that same loan-club nor its interest nor any other debt or claim or anything else
11    whatsoever, whether a written or an unwritten matter, in general, of those from the
12    times before up to the present day, on any pretext whatever; and that void from this moment shall be
13    the aforesaid loan-[agreement] together with the eranos-contract indicated in it
14    and the other loan-agreement,
15    through which the son of Herakleides
16    and Thermouthis is named, Herakleides;
17    and that contrary to these things nothing …
18    shall be carried out; or else, besides the agreed terms
19    remaining valid,
20    he, the transgressor, shall in addition be liable
21    both for the damages and for the fixed penalty.
