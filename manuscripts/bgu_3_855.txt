[TRANSLATION]
1   Year 21 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadria[nus]
2   Antoninus Augustus Pius, Hathyr 11. There acknowledges
3   Paouetis the younger, son of Onnophris son of Onnophris, priest,
4   from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, to Herieus son of Harpagathes,
5   weaver, ... that my slave-girl Tasous (is given) for one
6   year and six months.
