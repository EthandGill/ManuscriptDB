[TRANSLATION]
1     [After t]he consulship of the Fla[viu]s Antiocho[s] and Bás[s]us, the most illustrious,
2     … Pacho[n] … [1]5th indiction.
3     To [Flau]ius Taurinos son of Plousammon, biarch of the most illustrious
4     most noble Moors, the shield-bearers stationed in Herm[o]u[p]olis,
5     from Flavius Eudaimon son of Phoibammon, who subscribes below, former soldier of the same unit. I wish, willingly and of my own free choice, to lease from you for only one single year, reckoned from the new moon
6     of the next month Pauni of the present fifteenth indiction, the farmstead belonging to [yo]u, together with chaff-store and well and appurtenances and all that pertains to it, in the same Hermoupolis, in the quarter of the Fort, on the east, in the lane called Aphaisis, for our own use and the housing of my animals,
7     the rent of this for the whole year being one thousand eight hundred myriads of silver,
8     which rent I shall pay to you at the close of the yearly
9     period, on condition that you raise one she-goat in the green-feeding of crops
10    of the [auspiciously] incoming second indiction; and at the end of the yearly period
11    I shall hand over to you the same farmstead as I received it. The lease is valid and,
12    being formally questioned, I gave my consent. [F]lavius Eudaimon son of Phoibammon, the aforesaid,
13    have leased as aforesaid.
14    Through me, Biktor, it was written.
15    … of the farm[stead] in Herm[oupolis ] … the lease of Eudaimon son of Phoibammon.
