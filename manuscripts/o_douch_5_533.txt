[TRANSLATION]
1     To the beloved brother Alonus, farmer
2     Victor Petechon, greetings. Give to the father
3     Plotein[a], [of] grain in geometric measure [for tax]
4     [of] one artaba [and] five matia
5     it amounts to barley in geometric measure: 1 artaba [and] 5 matia for tax
6     13th indiction. I have signed, I the same.
7     13th indiction.
