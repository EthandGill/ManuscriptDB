[TRANSLATION]
1   To Herakleides, priest and archidi-
2   kastes and superintendent of the
3   chrematistai and of the
4   other tribunals,
5   from Syra daughter of Theon.
6   I lived with Sarapion, having given him a dowry
7   by agreement, on
8   account of two hundred
9   silver drachmas. So I, having taken him
10   into my parents’
11   dwelling, though he was utterly
12   penniless, kept myself blameless
13   toward him in all
14   things. But Sarapion, having
15   spent the dowry on whatever
16   purpose he wished, did not cease
17   ill-treating me and insult-
18   [in]g me and laying
19   his hands (on me), and reducing me to want
20   of the necessar-
21   ies, and later
22   abandoned me, leav-
23   ing me destitute. Therefore I ask you to order
24   that he be brought before you
25   so that he may be compelled,
26   being held, to repay
27   me the [d]owry with half
28   again. For of the
29   other things [I have against him]
30   I lay claim [and will lay claim].
