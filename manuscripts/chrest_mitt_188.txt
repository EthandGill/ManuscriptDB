[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: The so-called accounting scribes up to now active in the Catalogue should according to the old usage keep track of the agreements, including the names of the nomographoi and the contracting parties and the values of the transactions and the types of contracts and they should deposit them in both libraries. The so-called eikonistai shall, when they check the so-called composite roll for deposition, add a marginal note when something has been expunged or anything else added. And after a copy has been made on a single papyrus roll they will deposit it in both libraries. For I order that what is now observed in the Arsinoite and [...]polite nomes shall happen in the rest of Egypt as well. They shall also add the number of the (pasted) sheets and the names of the contracting parties. (lines 7-15: M. Depauw, ZPE 176 (2011), p. 197).
1     … …
2     But (it being) burdensome for those who trade at fixed rates, I subscribe, in
3     the place of (granting) immunity, that what is customarily paid into the Nanaion (treasury) be given also into
4     the other library … as quickly (as possible); and let the overseers register the accounts of the revenue into the thirty-couch (hall) every
5     five days, not only so that the revenue may become evident but also so that
6     this security too may be added to the others. The so-called accounting scribes (apologistai) up to now (active) in the Catalogue (katalogeion)
7     should according to the old usage keep track of the agreements, including (the names) of the nomographoi
8     and the names of the contracting parties and the number of the transactions and [the types] of the contracts, and they should deposit them in both libraries. The so-called eikonistai, when (they check) the composite roll

10    that stands otherwise; and after a copy has been made on a single papyrus roll they will deposit (it)
11    in the two libraries. For I order that what is now observed in the Arsinoite and …polite (nomes) shall happen in the rest of Egypt as well. They shall
12    also add the number of the (pasted) sheets and
