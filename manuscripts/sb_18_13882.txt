1     [In the consulship of Flavius Eu]stathius and Agricola of the most illustrious, Choiak 24]
2     [The most illustrious guild] of the goldsmiths of the splendid and most illustrious city of Oxyrhynchus
3     [Through us the undersigned] to Aurelius Chairemon son of Serenos from the same city, [of] the same guild
4     [greetings; for the sake of the title] of the administrator sought from our guild of the gold [and] silver [tax] of the fifth
5     [indiction] … we have chosen you Chairemon, or rather urged [you]
6     [we have entrusted to you] and trusted you to conduct the administration, we having promised
7     [all the] … under pretext of this administration becoming expenditures whether on places
8     [whether in the order us] to recognize and pay down, wherefore also this written security
9     [we have given you and we agree to all] manner of expenditures [sought] for our
