[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Phasos, son of Phasos, son of Pious, my mother being Thasos, acknowledge that I have sold according to Egyptian
2     deeds to Orsenouphis, son of Horouanchious, his mother being Tacheos, the
3     single-storey house belonging to me and the courtyard and all the appurtenances in Tebtynis, of which the measurements (are):
4     on the south toward the north seventeen cubits, on the west toward the east nine cubits, over the whole
5     of the measurements, or however many they may be, more or less; of which the neighbors of the house
6     and courtyard (are): on the south the house of Nepheros the tin-worker; on the north the royal street; on the west the house of Orsenouphis, son of Pios;
7     on the east the house of Anesouris, son of Phasos, and on part the house of Harmiysis, son of Alimes.
8     And I receive from Orsenouphis the whole agreed price in full
9     hand to hand out of the house, and I will guarantee the sale with every guarantee. My sons consent,
10    Harmiysis and Thaesis and Thenpasos, the three sons of Phasos, with the guardian of Thaesis being the aforewritten Harmiysis, and of Thenphasos her own husband Apsemis, son of Psonsneus,
11    and the wife of my deceased son Phasos, Thaubastis, daughter of …opis, with the guardian
12    being her own son Ors[eno]uphis, son of Phasos, and (they consent) not to come against (it) for the whole time
13    nor to bring any claim nor (shall) another on their behalf for the whole time. There wrote on their behalf Harmiysis
14    … [because they do not] know letters. Orsenouphis, son of Horouanchious, his mother being Tacheos: there has been made out to [me]
