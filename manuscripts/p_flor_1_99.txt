[TRANSLATION]
1     [Co]py of [a] petition.
2     … and to Heracleides, strategos of Hermopo[lis] …
3     From [A]mmonius, elder, of Hermaeus, and
4     Of his former wife A… pasis of Are[us]
5     With [the] of the cohabiting man Callistratus
6     Of A… aeos of Hermopolitans. Since the son of ours
7     Castor with others squandering dissipated
8     His all things and having moved to ours, wishes to destroy for which reason we foresee lest
9     [he] harm us or others or [do] something improper
10    [we ask] [for it to be] written forward … [to him]
