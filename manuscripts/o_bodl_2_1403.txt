r.1    μέτρημα θησαυροῦ μητροπόλεως γενήματος ιβ ἔτους Ἀντωνίνου
r.2    Καίσαρος τοῦ κυρίου Μεσορὴ α ὑπὲρ Χάρακος
r.3    ὀνόματος Περμάμιος Πετεχώνσιος πυροῦ ἀρτάβης δίμοιρον

[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, harvest of the 12th year of Antoninus
2     Caesar the lord, Mesore 1, on behalf of Charakos
3     name Permamios Petekhonsios, wheat of an artaba, two-thirds
4     totals wheat of an artaba 2/3, and on behalf of Notos, name Phatrêous Teôtos
5     through [the] farmer of the same, Permamios, wheat, one artaba [and a] fourth
6     totals wheat artaba 1 1/4, in total wheat artaba 1 1/2 1/3 [12]. [P...] I have noted.
