[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Phanis.
2     You did well going to Theadelphia and
3     [having] received from Zakaon
4     wheat, artabas 2 instead of 1.
5     One you have from the account of Choiak
6     month, and you having gone
7     received artabas [2 instead of 1].
8     And another time you went to
9     [the place of] Taurinus and you received
10    artabas 2 instead of 1 through [Za]kaon. So give the artaba of wheat to Zakaon,
11    which badly [you] received from h[im]; otherwise, you will have to regret [it].
