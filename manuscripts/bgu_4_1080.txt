[TRANSLATION]
1   Herakleides to Heras his son, greeting.
2   Before all else I embrace you, rejoicing with you
3   over the good, pious and
4   fortunate married life granted you, in accord with our common
5   vows and prayers, which the gods, hearing
6   in full, have brought to pass. And we, though
7   absent, were gladdened in spirit at the news as if present,
8   praying blessings over what is to come, and that
9   coming to you we may share together a
10   double feast in full bloom. So, just as
11   your brother Ammonas has discussed with me
12   concerning you and your af-
13   fairs, so, as is right, it shall be done. And about
14   this be confident and untroubled. And you, be eager
15   to honor us with let-
16   ters in equal measure. And about whatever you wish, write to me —
17   it gives me pleasure. And if it is no burden to you
18   and possible, send along to me ten
19   pounds of soft tow, total 10 lb., well
20   prepared, at the price current where you are,
21   yourself taking no loss in
22   this. Give many greetings from me to
23   your dearest consort, with whom
24   I pray that you fare well and flourish,
25   my lord son.
26   Oxypogon to Heras his son.
