[TRANSLATION]
1     The holy church of God of the city of Hermopolis
2     through me Kollouthos, administrator of the district
3     of Hermopolis to Kollouthos, fodder-seller: you have paid for the obligation
4     of produce of the twelfth indiction
5     of gold: seven carats
6     [and] half, making carats 7½. It was written in Thoth
7     [the] 6th of indiction 12. Kollouthos
8     administrator [has] verified [this] with me.
