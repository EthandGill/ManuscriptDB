[TRANSLATION]
1     And through the present letter I write, doing obeisance to your
2     [inner] friendship, praying [the most] high and merciful [god]
3     to hear about your health and prosperity. I wish you to know [that]
4     sincerely and earnestly I have written to you; and while I, on account of you, remain quiet, to receive
5     your letters—not a single letter of yours have I received from the time you left here.
6     May your virtue then be willing and also now to make clear to me, above all things,
7     about your health and all matters concerning you, and whether you received
8     my letters sent through the lord Theophilus, together with the things sent
9     from me, fifty apples. I beseech [you], master, do obeisance on my behalf to all
10    those worthy of being loved, especially my master, the lord Phosphoros. …
11    Give [to my lord brother] in God Iakkobus, [son] of Simon, from Faustinus
12    … in God Iakkobus, [son] of Simon, from Faustinus.
