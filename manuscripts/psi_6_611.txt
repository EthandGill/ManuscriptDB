[TRANSLATION]
1     Euclides, greetings, Senobastis.
2     The wheat cakes which you ordered
3     to the boy so that he might give [them],
4     he has not given [them] to me.
5     If therefore it seems good to you, order
6     [someone] to give to me so that I might have [them].
7     Farewell.
8     To Euclides
