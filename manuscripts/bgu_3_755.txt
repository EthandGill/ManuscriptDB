[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Year two of Imperator Caesar Traianos Hadrianos Augustus, 11th of the month of Kaisareios. We, Herakleides and associates, sitologoi of the areas around Herakleia, have had measured to us from the produce of the same year with respect to Stotoetis, son of Apynchis, for the dues on katoikoi of Herakleia by levelled public measure epaiton seventeen and a half and a twenty fourth artabas of wheat = 17 1/2 1/24 artabas of wheat and the extra charges.
