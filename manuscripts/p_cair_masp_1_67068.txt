[TRANSLATION]
1     Before all else, I do reverence to and greet your brotherly excellence.
2     First of all I blame her greatly, because the same brotherhood passed by
3     to Euphrosynos, and did not seek me out … toward whom not yet …
4     to do reverence to her. Yet indeed … I am vexed at that time; but I heard what happened
5     concerning your brotherhood in Antinoopolis, that you brought to nothing your
6     enemies. And I beg her to receive from the letter-carrier two keratia
7     in small coin, so that she may complete the need, and … since there is need.
8     For with God I wish to sail down to … our own. May I not fail
9     in this, taking courage I wrote to you … as the occasion calls. When you come down
10    to Antinoopolis to our most illustrious lords the pagarchs,
11    seek me out at Hermopolis. Since I was not deemed worthy of you at Euphrosynos,
12    come also to my city, so that my house may be deemed worthy of your brotherhood.
13    Again I greet her especially out of all my soul, until with God
14    I behold you face to face, good lord, friend.
15    Given to the most excellent Dioskoros, headman of the village …
16    Phoibammon …
