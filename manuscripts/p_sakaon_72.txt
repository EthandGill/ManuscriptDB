[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Constantius and Valerius Maximus,
2     the most illustrious praefects, Payni 30.
3     Aurelius Sakaon son of Satabous, his mother being Thermouthion, from the village of
4     Theadelphia of the Arsinoite nome, 58 years old, with a scar on his right shin,
5     to Aurelius Uranius son of Lampadius, and however he is styled, greeting.
6     I acknowledge that I have from you and owe, from a remaining balance, white sheep,
7     female, from the shearing, twelve in number, of which I have provided
8     from these two sheep, which I paid off, he having received from me the same two sheep; and the remaining ten sheep from the shearing I shall of necessity
9     repay to you in the month Thoth of the present consulship, in the present
10    fifteenth indiction, without delay; and upon the demand for payment
11    execution shall belong to Uranius son of Lampadius, both from me,
12    Sakaon the acknowledging party, and from all my belongings, as if by legal judgment; and having been formally questioned, I gave my assent.
13    I, Aurelius Sakaon son of Satabous, acknowledge that I owe sheep,
14    white, female in number, from the shearing, ten, and I shall repay
15    at the appointed time as aforesaid; and I paid off, of which he received from me two sheep, as aforesaid.
16    I, Aurelius Salakenos, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
17    Through me, …
18    Bond of Sakaon son of Satabous, from the village of Theadelphia, concerning sheep …
