[TRANSLATION]
1     I asked your brotherhood on behalf of the husband of the daughter of John, my man,
2     [that he] not be demanded of him; and having come now he said to me that this one the first [ones] demanded,
3     and be willing for me not to permit him to be demanded; for he ordered me [that] I yield nothing
4     you permit him to be demanded, nor be displeased at my response; and bring it coming
