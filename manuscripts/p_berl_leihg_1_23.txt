[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Papeeis, son of Thithiois, from the village of Kerkesoucha of the Herakleides division,
2     from Aurelius Soules, son of Herakleides, from the village of Theadelpheia.
3     I wish to lease from you what you also happen to hold
4     under lease — the trellised vineyard of Aurelius Apollonides, former cosmetes, market-overseer, councillor of the most splendid city of the Alexandrians, of 2½ arouras, called newly-planted, or however many there may be, for a period of two years, I providing
5     three hoeings, on a fruit-share of one half part, while I,
6     Papeeis, provide annually half a myriad of reeds and
7     5 bundles of cords. The sand-carriage and the dung-carriage
8     we shall both do; the repair of the machinery
9     (falling) on me the lessee, but that from new (falling) on me,
10    Papeeis; and I shall set into the nurturing land
11    annually 50 layer-shoots, I Soules feeding the oxen for the time of the watering; and I shall provide at the vintage
12    a piglet worth 20 drachmas, and after the term I shall hand over as
13    I received, if it seems good to lease; and having been formally questioned I agreed.
14    Year 2 of the Emperors Caesars Gaius Vibius Trebonianus
15    Gallus and Gaius Vibius Afinius Gallus Veldumnianus
16    Volusianus, Pious, Fortunate, Augusti, Epeiph 16.
