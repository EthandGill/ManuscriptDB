[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos,
2   from Gaius Julius Philos and from Helenos, Persian of
3   Caesar's epigone, and (from) his wife Tryphaina daughter of Ptolemaios, Persian woman,
4   with her guardian her husband. Concerning the matters in dispute, Helenos
5   and Tryphaina agree that they have from Gaius Julius Philos a loan, through the
6   exchange-bank of Kastor, of one hundred drachmas of Ptolemaic silver, at interest of two drachmas
7   (per mina)
8   for each month, which they will also repay within [...] months
9   from Phamenoth of the present 17th year of Caesar, paying the
10   interest each month punctually, and the principal in the last month without any
11   delay; or else they shall be immediately liable to seizure and held until
12   the repayment;
13   (repaying) the loan together with half as much again, and the interest at the simple rate, and for the time overrun
14   an equal (interest), the right of execution belonging to Gaius Julius Philos both from the
15   debtors, being mutually liable for the repayment, and from any one of them, and from whichever (of them) he chooses,
16   [and from] all [their possessions]
17   as if by legal judgment, any (counter-claims) being invalid, and whatever pledges they may bring forward [(being) all of no protection].
18   And this is the loan apart from that which the same Helenos owes,
19   together with Harpokration son of Areios and Tryphaina daughter of Areios, to the same
20   Gaius Julius Philos under another agreement (synchoresis) through the same court.
21   Year 17 of Caesar, Phamenoth 29.
