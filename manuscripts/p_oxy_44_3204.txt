[TRANSLATION]
1     … [In the reign of our most divine] and most pious master, greatest benefactor,
2     [Flavius Mauricius Ti]berius the eternal Augustus and Emperor, year 6,
3     [in the consulship of the same] most pious our master, year 5, Tybi 6, indiction 6, year 264 = 232.
4     [To Flavia Anastasia] the most glorious illustris, daughter of the man of glorious memory
5     [Menas son of Eudaim]on, landholding here in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through you,
6     [Flavius Phoibammo]n her most splendid administrator: I, Apollos, assistant of
7     [your] glory, son of the blessed Phoibammon, originating from the
8     [same city of the Oxyrhynchites,] subscribing below in my own hand, greeting. I acknowledge, of free
9     [will and] self-chosen purpose, swearing the divine and revered oath,
10    [to stand surety and to gua]rantee before your glory, through those belonging to it,
11    [Aurelius …]-on son of Ioannes, his mother being Tabithas, originating from
12    … [paga]rched by your glory, of the Oxyrhynchite
13    [nome; on condition that] he shall remain without interruption and dwell in his own
14    [village, with his] dearest ones and wife and cattle and all
15    [his belonging]s, answering in all things that concern his person
16    [or] the fortune of a villager, and that he shall in no way leave the same
17    [village] … nor indeed remove to another place; but also,
18    [when he is sought] by me [by] your eminence through those
19    [belonging to it,] on whatever day, on whatever account,
20    I shall produce him and hand him over in a public place
21    [out]side every place of refuge and pretext, where I also received him,
22    in the prison of the same city. Or, if I do not do this, I acknowledge
23    [that I shall pa]y on account of his absconding two ounces of gold of Alexandria,
24    [in deed and] in effect when demanded, having pledged to the right of this
25    [guarantee al]l my present and future possessions, specifically and generally,
26    [by way of pledge and by] the right of mortgage. Valid is this guarantee, written singly, and
27    [having been questioned, I ac]knowledged. I, Apollos, son of the blessed [Phoi]bammon …
28    [Guarantee of Apollos] son of the blessed [Phoibam]mon from the city of the Oxyrhynchites, undertaking … son …
