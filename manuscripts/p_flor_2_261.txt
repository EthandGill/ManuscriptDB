[TRANSLATION]
1     Tryphon to Irenaios
2     to the most-beloved, greetings.
3     the [ones] released to me by
4     Heron the cosmete
5     of wheat, artabas twenty, since
6     in the Taurine they-were-given, account-for them, and
7     spend for me on the
8     third of Phamenoth,
9     if [they] also were-given.
10    Be-well, I-wish you, most-beloved.
11    Year 5, Phamenoth 3
