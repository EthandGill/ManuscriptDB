[TRANSLATION]
1     A judgment we have given, we Theophilus son of Theon
2     and Paphnutius son of Joseph Pouaal of those concerning
3     Heracleios the elder with Pechysius son of Naraos of Chone, coming from the Leucogion estate of the Heracleopolitan nome. We heard the words between them
4     on account of the vegetable-seeds which were purchased
5     from them in common with indeed fifty
6     coins amounting and in so far as we understood
7     and God gave into our souls
8     thus them we judged so that the
9     aforesaid Pechysius should settle [the debt] of
10    the aforesaid Heracleios the elder, the stated fifty [talents] whole until [one] coin
11    and only because old are the same fifty coins. The judgment was written in the month
12    Phamenoth 26 of the 4th indiction. I Theophilus son of Theon have submitted.
13    … [I Pap]hnutius son of Jos[eph] Pouaa[l]
14    [I have submitted.] …
