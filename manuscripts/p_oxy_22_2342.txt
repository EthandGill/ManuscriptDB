1     To Gaius Minicius Italus, the lord prefect,
2     from Apion son of Apion, of those from the city of Oxyrhynchus,
3     wine-merchant. In the 2nd year of Trajan Caesar
4     the lord, Pasion son of Sarapion, who was a partner
5     and a creditor, having died in Alexandria,
6     having disowned his children upon condemnation, got as his heir his wife Berenike. She,
7     having the cargo of wine in her own keeping
8     under lock and key of the sale, pocketed the whole price, having learned of her husband's
9     death in a foreign land. She is being harassed by the
10    ill-natured sons (so as) not to tell the truth about what he managed, nor to come to a settlement of it, nor,
11    discharging the deposit which she held by way of account,
12    nor handing over the written documents, having the
13    entire matter, deceiving me every
14    day. We came before Dios the strategos,
15    she saying: "There are written (debts) … of three thousand
16    and others on wine, five thousand." Seeing
17    her lying, [he asked for] the daybook of her dead husband P[asion],
18    which he had often produced [himself, there being] full
19    account of written (debts), in number … rion, and of unwritten (debts)
20    to the same amount, five thousand two hundred forty-nine, four obols. Having been instructed
21    not to produce it because of the proof, she hid it,
22    having contrived against the strategos some pretext of haste,
23    on account of which, being wronged, I flee to you
24    … he sent (it) on. I request that, if it seem (good),
25    … it be demonstrated to you that she, having watched over us,
26    … the deprivation, and to give an account
27    [of what she] managed, returning the deposit of mine which she holds
