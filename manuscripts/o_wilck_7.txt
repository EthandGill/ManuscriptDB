[TRANSLATION]
1   Has paid:
2   Zmethis son of Pachnoubis,
3   for the poll-tax of the 13th year
4   of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
5   Pharmouthi 21, on account:
6   eight silver drachmas — total 8 dr.
7   (2nd hand) I, Herakleides son of Demetrios, have checked it, eight drachmas.
