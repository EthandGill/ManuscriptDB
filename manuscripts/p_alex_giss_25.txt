[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Apollonius, strategus of Apollonopolites Heptacomia, from Pcheris, son of Pouoris, and Orsenouphis, son of Peteminis, overseers of irrigation for the village of Naboö. We swear by the fortune of the Emperor Caesar Traianus Hadrianus Optimus Augustus Germanicus Dacicus Parthicus that we will make the lands of those named below ready for irrigation within three days. Else may we be liable to our oath. The names are as follows: Orsenouphis son of Peteminis 1 aroura Mersius, son of Horus 1 aroura The sons of Petosiris, son of Phatres 6 aroura Hierax, son of Apollonius 1 aroura The sons of Petosiris 3 3/4 aroura Mersius, son of Horus 31/32 of an aroura Pachoumis, son of Pseathyris 15/16 of an aroura Pachomatres and Orsenouphis, sons of Petosiris 2 1/4 aroura Pachoumis, son of Petemenecysis 19 arouras The 2nd year of the Emperor Caesar Traianus Hadrianus Optimus Augustus Germanicus Dacicus Parthicus - - -.
