[TRANSLATION]
1   Harpaesis son of Phanophis, Thrakidas, paid,
2   for the trade-tax of the eleventh year
3   of Trajan Caesar the lord:
4   twelve silver drachmas — total 12 dr. I, Hermogenes
5   son of Ammonas, the collector, wrote (this).
