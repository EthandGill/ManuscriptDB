[TRANSLATION]
1     243
2     To the most reverend elder Taurinus, greetings. I have been paid by your reverence
3     of the rent of the place of my house from the tenth of the past month Pachon of the
4     third indiction until Pachon the tenth of the present fourth indiction and I have no claim
5     against your reverence concerning the same said rent of the same
6     third indiction, that is from the tenth of the past month Pachon of the same present third
7     indiction until the tenth of the month Pachon of the fourth indiction as has been said because I have been paid
8     by you as I said before, and for your security I have made to you the complete receipt
9     as aforesaid. [This was] written in the month Mesore, 30th, 4th indiction.
