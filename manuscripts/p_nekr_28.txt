r.10   ὅταν ἔλθῃ σὺν θεῷ μαρτυρήσει σοι περὶ ὧν αὐτὴν πεποιήκασιν. δήλωσον δέ μοι

[TRANSLATION]
1     Xenosyris [presbyter] to Apollonius
2     to the presbyter, the beloved brother
3     in the Lord, greeting
4     Before all greatly I greet you and all beside you
5     brothers in God. to know
6     you, I wish, brother, that the grave-diggers have brought here
7     to Toeto the civic …
8     sent to Oasis by the …
9     magistracy. And this I have handed over to the good and faithful from the grave-diggers themselves for keeping, until her son Nilus comes. And
10    when he comes with God he will testify to you concerning what they have done to her. But make clear to me
11    and concerning what you wish here willingly doing
12    I pray you to be well
13    in the Lord God
14    To Apollonius from Xenosyrio[s]
15    presbyter of the presbyter in the Lord
