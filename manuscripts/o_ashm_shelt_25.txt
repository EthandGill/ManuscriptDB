[TRANSLATION]
1     Measuring of treasury of metropolis, produce of year 2 of Antoninus
2     and Verus, the lords Augustus, Epeiph 2,
3     on behalf of Notus by name Nephotes, of god the greatest
4     through the shrine-keepers, those through farmer Phathrous likewise
5     Phaminios of wheat fourteen [artabas]
6     [and one-]sixth, there are wheat 14 [and] 1/6. Panaeus [I] have noted wheat 14 [and] 1/6.
