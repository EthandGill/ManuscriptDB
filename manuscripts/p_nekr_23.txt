[TRANSLATION]
1     To Titius Honoratus the most distinguished prefect,
2     from Petechon son of Mersis, his mother being Talabon, from the village of Chosis of the
3     city of the Ib[i]tai of the [great] Oasis. To your just judgment, master
4     prefect, taking courage, this petition to you, the savior of all, I make,
5     dependent on the laws. My affairs in brief I shall set before you. There belongs to my fat[h]er an embalmer's office, which he himself
6     received from his parents, and while living enjoyed for the
7     time of his life; but after his death — pure are your ears — likewise also I myself
8     enjoyed the revenues of the office by succession from our fath[er; bu]t I do not know by what reasoning Peious and Peious the younger and Apolinarios
9     and … chillos an[d] Tapaaus wife of Parepsais, all of them Exopylitai,
10    as if rising from a [lo]ng sleep, having no action
11    against me nor share (with me), in a tyrannical manner wish to thrust me ou[t]
12    [of the] aforesaid office, disobeying the strictness of the laws and … a
