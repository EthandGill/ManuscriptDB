[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy. The eighth year of Tiberius C[la]udius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Tybi 25, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites
2     of the Thebaid. There acknowledges [Thaesi]s daughter of Herakleios, with the guardian, the son of her sister Thaesis, Petsiris
3     son of Sarapion, to her own hus[band] Ptollion son of Ptolemaios, his mother being Sarapias, in the street, that she has appointed him, according to this [acknowledgment], to exact and demand both the debts owed to her in the Oxyrhynchite and Oasite and [Ar]sinoite nomes, and the crops belonging to her, of which she has guardianship, namely (those) of the daughter of her deceased son Sarapion, Tathoonas, who is not yet
4     of age, of the fields in the same Oasite nome; and further also to sell to those who approach
5     for the purchase, at the price found, the things belonging to Thaesis, all or part
6     of them, and to make a prior division … and to manage concerning them as he may choose, just as also
7     to Thaesis herself, were she present, it was permitted; for she consents to all things that the husband Ptollion concerning the matters of
8     the appointment may manage, on condition that he restore to Thaesis everything, the trust
9     being in him; and further also to defend the things pending for her against Thoonis son of Thoonis
10    and others, no loss being incurred by Thaesis in the exaction of what Petsiris owes her by
11    a bond of [eigh]ty drachmas. The contract is valid.
12    Copy of the signature. [The eighth year] of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus German[icus]
13    Imperator, Tybi 25, thr[ough] …atos, who is with Theon the one appointed by the
14    associate agoranomoi, it has been transacted.
