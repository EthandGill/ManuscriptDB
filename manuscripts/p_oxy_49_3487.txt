[TRANSLATION]
1     6 (year 6)
2     Tausoreus daughter of Panemgeus, of those from the city of Oxyrhynchos,
3     with her guardian, her relative [K]ephalas
4     son of Kephalas, to my lawful cohabiting
5     husband Sarapion son of Ptolemaios, of those from the
6     same city, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received
7     from you at the Sarapeion by the city of Oxyrhynchos, through the bank of Dionysios — as in some (records) of Faustus — and his associates, in silver
8     of the Augustan coinage thirty-
9     two drachmas of principal, which are (part) of the seventy-two silver drachmas
10    which I lent you according to the handwritten note and the bank-order through
11    the bank of Apion son of Andromachos established at the same Sarapeion,
12    the bank of Apion son of Andromachos, which was established by the
13    aforewritten Dionysios, in the
14    month Neroneios Sebastos of the tenth
15    year of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
16    Germanicus Imperator, without my being diminished in the right of execution for the remaining
17    forty drachmas of principal and the interest
18    on these from now on, and further also in the right of the contract of cohabitation which you owe me, it being valid in all the things declared through it. Valid is the bond. Year twelfth
19    of Nero Claudius Ca[esa]r Augustus
20    Germanicus Imperator, Phaophi 4.
21    I, Tausoreus daughter of Panemgeus, receive the
22    thirty-two silver drachmas of principal
23    out of the seventy-two silver drachmas,
24    without being diminished in the right of execution for the
25    remaining forty drachmas and in the
26    right of the contract which you owe me, as stated above.
27    I, Zoilos son of Horos, wrote on her behalf, she not knowing
28    letters. Year twelfth of Nero
29    Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
30    Imperator, Phaophi 4. I, Kephalas, am registered as guardian. Year twelfth of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
31    Germanicus Imperator, Phaophi 4, through the bank of Faustus
32    and his associates the bank-order was made.
