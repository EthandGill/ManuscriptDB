[TRANSLATION]
1     From Hierakion, scribe of the city.
2     Arrangement of the guarding of the broad-streets and
3     alleys that are in the metropolis:
4     from the alley of the women's bath as far as
5     the house of Phanais, son of Sisois, which is in the quarter
6     of Onnophris the oil-seller, 123 houses,
7     through Antaios, son of Heliodoros, archon,
8     and Paaretos, son of Pachir…os, and
9     Hergeus the younger, son of Thotsytmis, men of standing;
10    and from the house of Orsenouphis, son of Tapaois,
11    as far as the broad-street of the Gate, 126 houses,
12    through Esminis, son of Harptuomthes, archon,
13    and Onnophris the younger, son of Senpuonsis, and
14    Pachompsais, son of Spagietes, men of standing;
15    and from the dromos of Isis, of Psenpaous,
16    as far as the Hermaion and the house of Harpokras,
17    son of Aphrodisios, of Pankollauthes, 129 houses,
18    through Aphrodisios, son of Ouenophris, archon,
19    and Pachompaos, son of Peteesis, and
20    … -phris the younger, son of Pseathyris, men of standing;
21    and from the house of … -os, of Pau…ophis
22    and the house of Pseathyris, son of Pankollauthes,
23    as far as the house of … -r…tos, son of Archibios, and
24    turning to the south, from the house of
25    Onnophris … -rios, son of Pachombekis,
26    as far as the house of Pachompsais the younger, son of Pseathyris,
27    priest, 130 houses,
28    through Hierakion, son of Psenosiris, archon,
29    and Petosiris, son of Hartbos, and Pachoumis
30    the younger, son of Pseathyris, men of standing;
31    and from the alley of Petemenou as far as
32    the house of Phatres, son of Peteesis, of Pitharmos, and
33    turning into the broad-street of the Leatherworkers
34    as far as the alley that is on the west of the temple,
35    128 houses,
36    through Bekis, son of Pyt…des, archon, and Sarapas, son of
37    … -otos, and Pangorsauis, son of Pachompsais, men of standing;
38    and from the Serapeum as far as the alley of Orsenoupis, of Petosiris,
39    and the house of Chairas the painter, 129 houses,
40    through Pachomos, son of Hierakion, archon, and Phmoulilous, son of
41    Seias, and Pangorsauis, son of Psachis, men of standing;
42    and from the house of Pepaes as far as the broad-street leading to Zbechthei, 129 houses,
43    through Pantbeuis, son of Psenthotes, archon, and Pseathyris, son of Psenosiris, and Pnaetios, son of E…, men of standing;
44    and from the dromos of Apollo and Aphrodite,
45    greatest gods, as far as the festal-hall (komasterion),
46    132 houses,
47    through Antaios, son of Psentarpsais, archon, and Hasies,
48    son of Kolloutchemis, elder, and Peteimouthes, son of Miysis, men of standing.
49    At Zbechthei likewise:
50    from the dromos of Aphrodite the greatest goddess called
51    Tazbes, on the northern side, as far as the broad-street of the sons of
52    Pachymis, of Paanouphis, 124 houses,
53    through Pachompsais, son of Panthophis, archon, and
54    Pachompsais, son of Papethous, and Peteimouthes, son of Psenenouphis, men of standing;
55    and from the alley of Lucius the fisherman to the south and
56    east as far as the boundary of Zbechthei, 123 houses,
57    through Pseathyris the younger, son of Psenosiris, archon, and
58    Hierakion, son of Artemidoros, and …
59    son of Hartbos, men of standing.
60    Total to the same: 1273 houses.
61    Year 20 of the Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan, the Best,
62    Augustus, Germanicus, Dacicus, Parthicus, Hathyr 11.
