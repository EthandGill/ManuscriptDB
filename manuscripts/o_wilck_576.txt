[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklas and Soter, collectors of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Bechis (son of) Bechis, through Senmonthes. We have received
3     for the land-survey tax of year 20, a fifth part, base, one obol, 2 chalkoi,
4     total base, 1 obol, 2 chalkoi. Year 21 of Hadrian the lord,
5     Phaophi 2.
6     I, Karouris the younger, have signed.
