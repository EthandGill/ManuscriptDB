[TRANSLATION]
1     Horion to Heroninus the most beloved, greetings.
2     I received from you and now from
3     [the] account of the respectable one
4     from the mandate of Syros [who served as] kosmetes for my
5     works, for which I will give account: wine
6     single-chous [measures] thirty-two
7     [they] total single-chous thirty-two.
8     [May you] be well, I pray, most beloved.
9     Year 3, Choiak 30
