1     After the consulship of …
2     The Aurelii Horion an[d P-] … [sons of Pamoun from the splendid and]
3     most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, [to our brother]
4     [by the same father], Aurelius … [from the same city, greeting.]
5     We acknowledge that by mutual surety [we have received from you]
6     in loan by hand from your house [for our own and]
7     pressing need [one sound, approved]
8     full-weight gold [solidus, total 1 gold solidus of principal,]
9     on condition that we furnish [to you, for the interest on it,]
10    each month [from the coming month]
11    Tybi of the present year … [of the present]
12    [in]diction, of silver myriads …
13    and the aforesaid [gold] solidus of the principal, being free from risk [from every risk,]
14    we shall by necessity repay [to you] …
15    without delay, the right of execution belonging to you [from both]
16    of us, being mutual sureties [for repayment, and from whichever of]
17    us you choose, and from [all our property.]
18    The bond is valid, [written singly, and on being questioned]
19    [we gave our assent.]
20    The Aurelii Horion and P… [sons of]
21    Pamoun the aforesaid, we have received in loan the [one gold] solidus and we shall repay [by mutual surety as aforesaid. Aurelius]
22    Martyrios son of John, having been requested, [wrote on their behalf, they being present,]
23    as they do not know letters.
