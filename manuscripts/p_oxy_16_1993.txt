[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of the most divine and most pious
2     our master, the greatest benefactor,
3     Flavius Tiberius Mauricius, the eternal
4     Augustus and Emperor, year 5,
5     in the consulship of the same most pious our
6     master, year 4, Phamenoth 13,
7     of the fifth indiction.
8     To the most exalted successors
9     of the one of glorious memory,
10    Apion, who was
11    first patrician, landholders
12    also here in the splendid
13    city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas
14    the servant who asks and
15    provides for his own
16    masters, the same all-praiseworthy persons, the
17    legal conduct and liability — I, Ioannes,
18    deacon and steward
19    of Saint Thecla, son of the
20    blessed Petros, from the
21    same city. I acknowledge
22    that I have received and have been paid in full
23    from your exaltedness
24    the holy contribution of the
25    sixth, with God's help, indiction,
26    that is, of wheat by the cancellus measure
27    four artabas, which were also
28    given to me through Ioustos
29    the most reverend chartularius
30    of your glorious house;
31    and as proof
32    of the receipt I have made
33    this receipt.
34    The receipt is valid, written in single copy,
35    and on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
36    I, Ioannes, deacon and steward of Saint Thecla — this
37    receipt is satisfactory to me as aforesaid.
38    Receipt of the most devout Ioannes, deacon and steward of Saint Thecla, of wheat by the cancellus measure, 4 artabas.
