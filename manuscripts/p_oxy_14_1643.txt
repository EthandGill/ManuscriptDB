1     [Aurelius Sarap]ammon also called [D]idymos, Oxyrhynchi[te] … and Athenaios, periodonikes, most excellent,
2     [xystarch fo]r life and however I am styled,
3     [to Aurelius] … and however you are styled, from the same Oxyrhyn[chites' cit]y, greeting. I commission you according to this
4     [letter so] that you, travelling away to the most splendid [Alexandri]a, may seek out a slave of mine by the nam[e of] …n, about 35 years old, whom you yourself also recognize
5     … , whom having found you shall hand over,
6     [you having authority] to do whatever is permitted to me when present
7     … and to imprison and to flog and to bring [an action] against those who have receiv[ed him] and to demand legal redress. And this comm[ission] I have issued to you in single copy, which let it be vali[d] wherever it is produced, and having been formally que[stioned I ackno]wledged it. Year 14 and year 13 of our lords
8     [Diocl]etian and Maximian Augusti, and year 6
9     [of our lord]s Constantius and Maximian the
10    [most illustrio]us Caesars, Pachon 16, in the consulship of Faustus
11    [and Gallus.]
12    [Aureliu]s Sarapammon also called Didymos,
13    [most excellen]t xystarch for life, I commissio[ned al]l the aforesaid as set [forth.]
