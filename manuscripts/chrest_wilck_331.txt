[TRANSLATION]
1     We received it in year 4, Mecheir 10.
2     Horos to the topogrammateis and komogrammateis, greeting.
3     A copy of the letter from Eirenaios the kinsman and dioiketes
4     concerning those who are to be drafted into the produce-guard duties
5     is appended below. Of those, therefore, in the places under you
6     who are reliable for trust and security, and who are placed in (sufficient) circumstances,
7     send to us the lists man by man, with note of the kleros each one holds and of the other property they have, so that the other matters may be administered as has been enjoined.
8     Farewell. Year 4, Mecheir 10.
9     Eirenaios to Horos, greeting. A copy of the letter to Asklepiades
10    is appended below. Having therefore handed over to him the list
11    of those able to be drafted into the produce-guard duties, apply the appropriate diligence so that the other matters
12    may come about in accordance with what has been indicated, since the account concerning the things
13    overlooked in these matters will also be brought to bear against you.
14    Year 4, Peritios 23, Choiak 23.
15    To Asklepiades. A copy of the letter to Hermias, who is in charge of the revenues
16    of the … division, is appended below.
17    Since, then, the same instructions have been given to you also,
18    take care that … capable men be appointed to the administrations and chief-guard-posts, and of the things concerning the
19    keeping of the crops being administered in the manner indicated, and of the collection of the dues into the treasury
20    being made earlier, nothing should
21    fall into arrears.
22    To Hermias son of Harnektotes, who is in charge under Horos, the royal scribe.
23    Having appended for us what I had written to you, having filed also the letters to
24    Theodotos who is in charge of the administration and the chief-guard-post, through which
25    it was made clear that it is necessary, the village deacons of the guards being summoned to a common
26    council,
27    that there be received, with his own opinion, the written oath-declarations which he submitted, of the royal oath concerning the
28    keeping of the annual crops — I both wondered and was greatly aggrieved if, having suffered the most dreadful things
29    at the inquiry held against you, on account of
30    no provision having been made at the proper time either for the management of the green crops
31    and of the other extra-sowings, nor for the guarding of the
32    crops, but also not employing for the administrations
33    and chief-guard-posts men of worth, but all
34    bad men and worth nothing, you remain in the same wretchedness,
35    in no way having corrected the things mismanaged contrary to what is proper.
36    Know, however, that you are liable to accusation, and even now, having determined
37    that you will obtain no pardon, if anything be neglected,
38    so that on the one hand suitable men be appointed for the aforesaid duties
39    … , and having determined the unceasing diligence to be applied in the things pertaining to the increase of the taxation,
40    and having received from the komogrammateis
41    the list of those able to be drafted into the produce-guard duties, both from those serving in the army and the others
42    inhabiting the places and placed in (sufficient) circumstances
43    and reliable for trust and security, and having appointed the suitable men
44    for the duties village by village, and from these
45    and from the guards in each village having received double written oath-declarations of the royal oath
46    that they will preside over the guarding to the best advantage, and that none of those farming the
47    royal and the released land shall touch the green crops and the other
48    extra-sowings except those for the feeding of the agricultural animals, which also together with
49    the komogrammateis shall be supplied, and of the things to be managed
50    of which the prices and the securities of these given shall be deposited at the banks
51    for the dues into the treasury in accordance with the previously issued ordinances,
52    take care that the other matters too come about for the harvest from a sound basis, and to convey
53    to the appointed places, and to let none of these slip away, but
54    when the proclamation concerning the release is published, unless all
55    of what must be handed over and of the arrears carried over to the earlier times
56    be fulfilled … , from you or from those appointed to the administrations
57    and the royal scribes, to the effect that he who comes to the choosing of the additional crops owes nothing
58    nor is liable to be held for other accusations
59    or charges, and that provision be made that all as quickly as possible may pay
60    the things due to the treasury, and from those in charge of the chief-guard-posts
61    provision shall be made that it be carried out in the same way; one (copy) deposit
62    at the royal bank, and the other, sending it to us, see to it that
63    in the villages too proclamations be published declaring that none let loose
64    animals into the land sown with grass and the like produce,
65    nor touch the rest of the extra-sowings without giving the
66    security as is set out, and that of those who act contrary to these things both the
67    animals shall be taken under forfeiture toward the rents. And above all be vigilant that, the collection of everything having been arranged earlier, it shall not befall you to fall into the like troubles, and considering altogether how great a weight the attention to the things indicated
68    contributes to the affairs, in matters of necessity regard it as your aim that nothing whatsoever of the
69    useful things be omitted, but, with something always being further devised toward the better,
70    that each thing proceed according to our purpose. For we will accept neither force nor any other
71    pretext whatsoever, and the collection of the things to be set out
72    by you shall be by force. On whatever day you receive the letter, make it clear.
