[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been measured for the treasury of the metropolis, produce of year 8 of Antoninus
2     of the lord, Pauni 15, for Agoras, in the name of
3     Petechontos Sensenourios, of wheat, in artabae
4     one-half, a forty-eighth; it amounts to 1/2, 48. Paniskos has marked.
