[TRANSLATION]
1     Having approached, priests and pastophori
2     from the village Bacchias of the Arsinoite,
3     Apollophilos the rhetor, having stood beside them, said:
4     The magistrates force those represented on behalf of them bodily to perform the labors
5     apart from what you have ordered. They ask therefore
6     that you order, just as you have also benefited others,
7     them to be undisturbed. Ulpios
8     Serenianus the high priest and [on] the holy [matters]
9     said: The general will take care that force does not happen.
10    Year 12 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, month
11    Thoth 28
