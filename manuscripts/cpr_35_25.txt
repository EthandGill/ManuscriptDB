[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most excellent council of Hermopolis
2     the great, ancient and splendid
3     and most venerable, through Aurelius Corellius Alexander, of equestrian rank, from
4     military service, gymnasiarch, councillor, prytanis-in-office of the [same]
5     city, and however he is styled,
6     from Aurelius Hermas son of Tatēris,
7     a Hermopolite registered
8     in the eastern quarter of the city: I wish
9     willingly to lease from the civic account for two years from
10    the current month Hathyr of the present
11    14th year a single-storey new house
12    in Hermopolis, in the eastern part of the city,
13    by the vault, under the [southern] portico of the Antinoite broad-street, for the dwelling
14    of myself and of those with me, at a rent
15    each month of eight drachmas,
16    which I will pay every
17    month without delay, and at the end of the term I will hand it over with
18    its doors as I received it; and if a notice is given, it shall be permitted to lease it out anew to others. On being formally questioned I gave my assent. [In the 14th year]
19    of the Emperor C[aesar]
20    Publius Licinius [Gallienus]
21    Germanicus Maximus [Persicus]
22    Maximus Pius For[tunate]
23    Augustus, Hathyr 13. A[urelius]
24    Hermes son of Tatēris, I have leased
25    as set forth above. Aurelius Kopres, I wrote on his behalf
26    since he declares he does not know letters.
