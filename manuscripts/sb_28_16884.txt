r.7    δηναρίων μυριάδας τ.

[TRANSLATION]
1     John, count, to Philoxenus, manager of Peribleptos.
2     Provide for your expenditure account charged to the foundation on account of expenditure of gold
3     one nomisma, and for freight of ship having conveyed gravel, myriads three hundred, making one nomisma and
4     myriads three hundred alone. I have noted one nomisma and myriads three hundred, making … one nomisma and myriads three hundred to you Philo[x]enus, manager of Peribleptos.
5     Of year one hundred fifty, …, Thoth eleventh, twelfth, indiction.
6     To me in expenditure account for the foundation one nomisma and for freight of ship under gravel
7     of denarii myriads three hundred.
