[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of Bere]nike the goddess Manifest, in the
2     [first year, but the rest of the] common (formulas) as is written in Alexandria,
3     [in the month Hyperberetaio]s = Mesore,
4     [in Oxyrhynchus city] of the Thebaid. I swear by queen
5     [Berenike the goddess ][Mani]fest and her ancestors
6     [and all the other god]s—I, Straton son of Nikomachos,
7     … [one of the f]irst friends and chiliarchs of the swords-bearers and of the s[ettler cav]alry, to Theon … iou
8     P[e]rsian of the settl[ers'] cavalry and successor to the
9     patrimonial allotment, the two of us of the quarter of Cleopatra Aphrodite,
10    that I will abide in all the terms according to the deed of
11    the agreement which I have made for you through the
12    record-office of the memorialists in Oxyrhynchus city on the present day,
13    by [which] I have ceded to you from my allotment
14    those (arouras) which I hold from the allotment of Phain[i]ppos around the village
15    P[e]ennō, of grain-bearing arable land, of rectangular (shape),
16    five ar[our]as, of which the neighbours are de[clar]ed through the agreement,
17    and that Straton will not act fraudulently
18    [conc]erning the cession of the land, nor concerning anything
19    e[ls]e of the matters declared through the agreement,
20    no[r concerning thi]s [oa]th, by any pretext whatsoever.
21    If … (I transgress) …, then whenever I am call[ed to account] … from you, apart from the cession
22    of the land remaining valid for you, just as it indeed is,
23    I agree that my allotment too be taken up into the
24    royal (treasury) and that I be liable to (the penalties) of the oath.
25    I, Straton, have sworn the aforewritten oath.
