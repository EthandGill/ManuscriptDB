[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolem[aios, greetings, Petesouchos]. ... [I am being wronged by Ptol]emaios of the descendants from Kerkesoucha,
2     of the Herakleide[s' division] ... ... me sitting, working ... [whatever] might be my portion
3     of the body ... ... blows
4     he inflicted ... [these things] he did using force/violence. I therefore beseech you,
5     O King, order [Diophanēs the general to write to Deinia the super]intendent to send Ptolemaios to Diophanēs
6     [to be judged for me and if what I write is true] ... [if] Diophanēs decides, so that through you, O King,
7     I might obtain justice.
8     Farewell.
9     To Deini[a.] ... [Year 1, Gorpiaios 30, Tyb]i 13.

11    Petesouchos against Pt[o]lem[aios]
