[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Flavius Oly]mpiodoros also called Asynkr[itios],
2     [praepositus, an]d to Achilleus son of E[u]daimon, both
3     dyke-[overseers] of the southern parts of the nome of Hermopoli[te, from Aureli]i Pebes son of Kollouthos and Painis
4     … [bo]th village-heads of the village Ta… [of the same] nome. We give and report
5     [the] following registered water-guards
6     for the watch over the dyke-cuttings of the boundary embankments belonging to us, (men) being well-off and
7     [s]uitable for the duty, at the risk of u[s] and all those of our village, on the basis of mutual surety. They are:
8     Si[lva]nos son of …nychis, Bekis son of Pamonios
9     …ion son of Tithoes, Marinos son of Tax…
10    Ta[uri]nos son of Silvanos, [B]akkis son of Rhanios
11    … son of [Onn]ophris, Peues son of Hatres
12    … son of [P]athotes, Horos son of Plousios
13    [wh]om we stand surety for and shall produce,
14    [we (will produce)] them, fulfilling the duty entrusted to them up to the relief (=change of shift)
15    [blame]lessly. And if [any] of them should desert [and] we ourselves do not produce
16    [him, the account on] their [behalf] we shall undergo
17    [and] on being [question]ed [we acknowledged (it)]. In the consulship of the Flavii
18    [Amantius and] Alb[inus], the most illustrious.
19    Epeiph … we, the aforesaid village-heads, re[port] the following registered (men)
20    [as is set fo]rth. Aurelius Besarion, attestor,
21    [I wrote on] their behalf, they not knowing letters.
