1     [Aurelius Hermophi]los, [son] of Horion, [who has] served as cosmete of Hermopolis the great ancient and splendid and most venerable [city]
2     To [Aurelius Eudaim]on, also called Theodotus, [who has] served as gymnasiarch and high priest, councillor, [who] assumes the prytaneia of the same city, most honorable, greetings; having beforehand written, I sent to the most excellent council through you
3     [the matters through] the dispatch of the strategos of the nome Aurelius Hieron through Aurelius He[r]mus, the strategic [officer]
4     [servant, which was] copied to me by our most illustrious governor Appius Sabinus, [to which] I had requested [from] him, standing aside from all [that] I have, [and] nominating our son
5     [Aurelius] Horion, also called Hermaios, for the office of cosmete of the same city, after which he previously
6     [fulfilled on behalf of] himself, having been exhausted, through which he was willing [that] the risk of nomination be for
7     [those who had nominated], and [that] the strategos of the nome prevent any violence occurring, if it should occur contrary to [what he had]
8     [salubriously prescribed], which have been presented in accordance with the signature issued to me by the servant [commissioned] to this [on the] twenty-first of the present month Epeiph; and the day before, which was the 22nd
9     [of Epeiph it was delivered to me] through a council [servant] your dispatch, O Eudaim[on], also Theodotus, [who himself] [sent back] a reply concerning the same office with amazement and [having been] set aside from the laws
10    [and] of the [signature], [someone] having indicated [this] enviously in some [way] according to necessity. And now I send back a reply to you
11    since to one standing aside [and] withdrawing from his own property there exists from the laws and divine ordinances this [help], [namely] to suffer no violence, [with regard to] which the most illustrious governor, agreeing, and
12    [h]aving been hallowed [and] willing [the] strategos to prevent violence, having added [that] the risk of nomination is concerning
13    [t]hose who nominated. But if you yourself think [that] having taken all my [possessions] in place of the customary third, [you would] make good the [items] concerning the office
14    [wholly] and neither to lay a snare for neither the city nor the [m]ost excellent council. For I shall be satisfied with this reply-letter in the greatest justice. Aurelius Hermophilos, [son] of Horion, [who has] served as cosmete
15    [I pray that you will be in] good health, [my] most dear [friend].
