[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Constantine
2     Augustus the seventh and Costantius the most valiant
3     Caesar the first, Thoth 10, in the Arsinoite [nome]
4     acknowledges Aurelius Pemes, [son of] Pata, mother Tanuphis
5     from the village of Karanides of the same nome, aged 45, [with a] scar
6     on the right leg, to have from Antonius Sarapammon, [who has] served as prytanis, counselor of the same city
7     [a sum of] capital silver of [imperial] currency [of] talents
8     forty-six and drachmas four thousand, [which] amount to
9     talents 46, drachmas 4000
10    [for] use for eight months without [interest]; which monies
11    shall necessarily repay the acknowledging Pemes to Sarapammon on the ninth of the month Pachon of the coming [consulship]
12    but if when the appointed time arrives the repayment [not]
13    the acknowledging [one] makes [it], [interest] fixed for these [terms] is to follow for him, and upon the demand
14    being made to Sarapammon, [the] exaction from both
15    [the one] acknowledging Pemes and from his [possessions]
16    all as from a judgment, and when asked [thus] I acknowledged
17    Aurelius Pemes Pata I acknowledge [having] and owing the [sums] of the money
18    talents forty-six and drachmas four thousand, which I shall also repay at the appointed time without interest; but if I do not repay, I shall provide
19    for the extended time the interest as stated [above]. Aurelius Nilus [son] of Heron wrote
20    on behalf of him [who is] illiterate
21    document [of] Pemes Pata from Karanides, principal of talents 35, interest [for] months 8, talents 11, drachmas 4000
22    repayment Pachon 9
