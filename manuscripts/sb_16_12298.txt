[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the ninth year of Emperor Caesar Vespasian Augustus
2     Tybi 24. [He paid] to Heron and … and Horos son of Horos
3     of Orseuta, mother Taouethaios, of the same [year]
4     of Narmouthis: twelve drachmas, they amount to 12 drachmas, and Mecheir with interest (at rate) 4,
5     eight drachmas, they amount to 8 drachmas, and Phamenoth with interest … four drachmas, they amount to 4 drachmas, and
6     Germaniceion with interest (at rate) 8, eight drachmas, they amount to 8 drachmas, and Pauni with interest (at rate) 1,
7     four drachmas, they amount to 4 drachmas, and Caesarison with interest (at rate) 6, nine drachmas and two obols,
8     they amount to 9 drachmas and two obols.
