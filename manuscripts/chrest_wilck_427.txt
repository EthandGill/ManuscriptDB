[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: – – – (m. 4) of Titus (m. 1) , since you inquire, [how many] pigs I have at present, I declare, swearing by the Fortune of Marcus Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar, the lord that I have 165 pigs, which I am feeding to take (them) to the markets of Psenbelleichis. And whenever you inquire, I shall provide (them), otherwise may I be liable to the oath. Written by Harbaithion, son of [Dionysi]os. Year 28 of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Pius Felix Augustus Arm[en]iacus Medicus Parthicus Sarmaticus Germanicus Maximus Britannicus, Phaôphi x. (m. 2) I, Nepheros, son of Kapha, herdsman, have sworn [the] above mentioned oath, that I have one hundred and sixty five pigs 165, which I shall provide, whenever you inquire, as aforesaid. I, Neikias, son of Isidoros, wrote on his behalf because he does not know letters. (m. 3) I, Titus, assistant, supervised.
