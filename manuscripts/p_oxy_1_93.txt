[TRANSLATION]
1   [† T]o the lord Timotheos, intermediary
2   [of the granaries of the] citizens, Phoibammon the weigh-
3   [er(?). Furn]ish to Theodoros, son of Am(...)si(...),
4   [from] the produce of the twelfth indiction,
5   of grain by the kankellos-measure two and one-sixth artabas only,
6   of grain (artabas) 2 1/6 by the kankellos(-measure). Mesore 1.
7   12th indiction. †
8   From Eutrygios to Dioskoros the assistant, greeting:
9   give to Gorgonios the water-organ player, by order, of grain
10   two (artabas).
11   Year 38, 7th (indiction), Tybi 21. I have signed off.
12   † Ioannes the count, to Phoibammon the wine-steward:
13   furnish to the men from Sepho who brought (it) cakes, 2 double(-loaves); to Didymos the carpenter of Alexandria, 1 double; and to the men from Kesmouchis who brought (it),
14   cakes(?), 2 double; to Megas, one of the stationarii, 1 double; to the fishermen of Kesmouchis through Kaleonistos, 1 double; to the doorkeeper of Saint
15   John, through Phib the soldier, 1 double; to the country-folk of the Martyr (shrine), through Phib the soldier, 1 double; to the field-guards of the great
16   peace-office who guard the opposite (bank?), 6 double; to the country laborers, 2 double; total of the whole assigned, eighteen double(-loaves).
17   Year 149(?), Choiak 22, 12th(?) indiction; total of the whole, 18 double only. † I have signed off. Of wine eighteen double, total of wine 18 double, to Phoibammon the wine-steward.
18   In the month Choiak 22, 12th indiction.
19   From Aphthonios to Ophellios Ochin(?), greeting:
20   furnish for the service of the landlord's house of new wine ten kerameia, and
21   to Amesystos the horse-doctor, by order, one keramion of new wine; total 11 kerameia.
22   Year 31, 21, 13, 4, 2(?), Phaophi 18.
