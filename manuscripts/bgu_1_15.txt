[TRANSLATION]
1     Aemilius Satorninus to the strategoi of the Seven Nomes
2     and of the Arsinoite, apart from the Oasis, greeting.
3     I see the shipment is being greatly neglected by you. For each one of you, when reporting that grain
4     is in the granaries, fails to take note, and that there is no other cause than the
5     scarcity of those who are accustomed to convey the grain.
6     I think, therefore — many times indeed have I written to
7     bring it now to its (full) count, but you have never seen fit
8     to attend to my letters,
9     but, conniving with the donkey-drivers, on the one hand
10    take them up onto the count, but on the other
11    the so-called trionia they are not compelled to provide, whence then it remains that they enjoy
12    the profit accruing to them from the donkey-driving,
13    but the (revenue) from these does not come to the treasury. I said,
14    therefore, this, lest it advance too much for them, lest afterwards
15    too readily it come about, both for those holding the full count
16    and for those falling short — I order each single one
17    of the donkey-drivers to be compelled to provide the owed
18    trionia by him, and you to set a seal upon each donkey. For thus both the
19    donkey-drivers will be compelled to feed (it for) the full count, and you may be able
20    to detect the donkey-drivers (in fraud).
21    I pray for your good health.
22    Year 5, Epeiph 17.
