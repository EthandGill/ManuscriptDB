[TRANSLATION]
1     Deinon to Arimouthes greeting. We wrote to you previously concerning the soldiers
2     that are in the places under you, so that they might be sent with Bithelmeinios the governor just as Apollonios the dioiketes writes, and likewise also the aforesaid reapers according to the order given to you; but seeing that you are neglecting [this]
3     I thought I ought also now to write to you. When therefore you receive the letter, having set aside all [other] business
4     send to us the soldiers now; and the [reap]ers when
5     you have made them ready send word to us. For not casually concerning these matters does the dioiketes
6     take care. Farewell. Year 32, Mecheir 13.
7     To the toparch Arimouthes
8     of the Lower [region]
9     Mecheir 14
10    concerning soldiers
11    and reapers
