[TRANSLATION]
1     [one] half; I will measure all and mark off without any alteration and dissembling. But if I do not act as aforesaid,
2     I will pay the damages and expenses and penalty in silver
3     five hundred drachmas and the same to the public [treasury], and nevertheless let this hand remain valid wherever produced; and there shall remain
4     to me, Heron, the transfer that was made to me of three arouras alone, valid and binding. Year 1 of Tiberius
5     Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, month
6     of Kaisareus 1.
