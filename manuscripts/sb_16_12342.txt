1     Year 8, Tybi 19. Socrates agrees that [it] has been registered at the bank of Python which is in Crocodile city
2     to the king Augchis of Marres
3     fee of hypothec of a third part
4     of a house and rooms
5     which are in Auhere[i]
6     of the Heracleides division
7     [witnessed by] Achomneuis
8     of Petenenteries
9     of the one who pledged [it]
10    drachmae 40 copper tetrobol of change hemiobel copper 1.
