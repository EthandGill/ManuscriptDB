1     25. Thersandros to Chares, an agreement.
2     In the fourteenth year, in the month … on the twenty-fifth, at Herakl[eopolis] above Mem[phis].

4     of the catoecic cavalry, that Thersandros consents to the transfer of registration that has been made to Chares of what [he] has [ceded]
5     to him from the allotment belonging to him, the ten arouras toward … in two allotments …
6     of Polemon five arouras, and in that of Eloios … five arouras, [of which] the survey is south to north …
7     west to east … royal … on the west royal land, on the east …
8     … in return for [which] Chares has made for Thersandros services both for the crown-taxes [and the other royal dues]
9     [of the all]otment in accordance with the administrative arrangements [through the] cavalry accounting-office, on condition that Thersandros shall provide [the declared]
10    [arou]ras free from every royal [and] private debt an[d all] other charges from the previous
11    periods up to the thirtieth of Mes[or]e of the four[te]enth year, and from other ces[sions for]
12    [a]ll time, guaranteeing with every guarantee … And there consents to the cession [of the]
13    arouras the wife of Thersa[ndr]os, He[ra]kleia [daughter of so-and-so], with [the guardianship] of the same man, her husband and br[other].
