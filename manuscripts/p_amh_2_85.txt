[TRANSLATION]
1     To Hermaios son of Apollonios, (also called) Philoklaudios and Althaieus, exegete of the Hermopolite,
2     from Apollonios son of Ammonios and Horion son of Horion,
3     Hermopolites. We wish, of our own free will, to lease for five years, from the present tenth year of Vespasian the lord,
4     all that has been left in whatever manner to the
5     orphaned sons of (him), by Sarapion son of Kastor, from Koussa, and whatever else we may find in the name of the orphaned sons
6     of Sarapion himself, for a rent per year — apart from the per-aroura artaba-tax and the naubion — of six hundred silver drachmas. And if [anything] else should be required or exacted from us, apart from the per-aroura artaba-tax and the naubion, it shall be credited to us from the aforesaid rent. The
7     rent we shall pay yearly in the month Phaophi to whomever
8     you, the exegete, shall instruct; and if any (land) becomes unwatered (abrochos)
9     or river-carried or sand-covered or scraped-bare,
10    from the coming eleventh year it shall be credited to us
11    from the aforesaid rent in proportion, if it [seems good]
12    that a copy of this lease be posted up for the
13    appropriate ten days, so that, no one bringing
14    a higher bid, there shall remain to us a secure lease for the five-year period, unalterable. In the tenth year of Imperator
15    Caesar Vespasian Augustus, Phamenoth 23. I, Horion son of Horion,
16    have submitted (this). I, Apollonios son of Ammonios, have jointly submitted (it),
17    as is aforesaid.
18    chou-… [lease] of Apollonios son of Ammonios, ech
