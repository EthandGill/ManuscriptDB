1     Year fourth of [the] Emperor
2     Caesar Domitian Augustus
3     Germanicus, Epeiph twenty-seventh. Has been measured
4     into the public [treasury] produce of the first year through Hera[tus]
5     and associates, grain-collectors [of] Libos toparchy of Senoko[m]es, from Alin[e]s Komonos through Komoapio[s] farmer of the village Syron
6     of wheat, three-month [wheat], all in public measure, swept, [sealed with] cancellation
7     nine become wheat artabas 9. Hatres, who is also Didymos of Horos, [I] have noted the wheat
8     artabas ni[ne] become wheat artabas 9.
9     Horos, the seal-officer, [I] have noted the
10    wheat artabas nine become wheat artabas 9
