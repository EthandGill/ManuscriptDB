[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of [the God Ko]rr[a, son of] Szerich, counselor
2     To Basileus, administrator of the village of Aphrodito. We give thanks
3     To God, and after these things. Zeid our envoy
4     We sent to you in order that he might bring the sailors and craftsmen
5     With their expenses and of the battles those distributed
6     Through your administration by the course of Egypt, ninth indiction
7     Receiving therefore these present letters, be willing
8     To give him a vessel for transport of artabs one hundred and below
9     As it was said, by the account of his descent, not providing passage-money
10    For such passage-money we [endure] here
11    And let it not seem good to you to give him beyond such transport
12    Of artabs one hundred, since [from home] we demand from you the passage-money
13    Making upon you; and the [necessary] investigation. It was written in month Mechir 5, indiction 8
14    Korra ben Szerich [counselor] to Basileus, administrator of the village of Aphrodito
15    Mechir 15, indiction 8, it was brought through Saeid, porter, concerning the ship, transport of artabs 100 until the Fossatum
