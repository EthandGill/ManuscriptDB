[TRANSLATION]
1     Antiochos Kasian[os] and the
2     others, overseers of the sacred gate of Syene. [he] registered
3     Petorzmethis, [son] of Poueres, mother Tisatios
4     for poll-tax of year 13, drachmas seventeen and one-half-obol; for prison-keeping, one-half-obol; total drachmas 17, 1 obol.
5     of year 14 of Hadrian the lord, Thoth, 8th [day].
6     Antiochos Kaspianos, I have noted, drachmas ten
7     seven and 1 obol; total drachmas 17, 1 obol. Year 14 of Ha[drian]
8     of the lord, Thoth, 8th [day].
