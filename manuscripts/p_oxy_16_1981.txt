[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and master Jesus
2     Christ our God and savior,
3     in the reign of our most divine and most pious
4     master, greatest benefactor, Flavius Heracleius
5     the eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the year 3,
6     Phaophi 28, of the 1st indiction.
7     To Flavius Apion, the all-praiseworthy and most exalted
8     ex-consul and patrician, landlord also
9     here in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
10    through Menas his servant, who asks and
11    stipulates on behalf of his own master, the
12    same all-praiseworthy man, the action
13    and obligation: we, the Aurelii Phib [so]n of Dioscorus
14    and Apollos son of Anoup, his mother being Ph[i]loxene,
15    from the village of Ophis in the Oxyrhynchite nome
16    administered as a pagarchy by your glorious house,
17    house. I acknowledge to your exalt[ed]ness,
18    through those belonging to it, that if
19    ever at any time or season [we should appear] to have stolen
20    either mechanical implements or cattle,
21    or to have committed any theft whatsoever,
22    or to have harbored bandits, we [a]cknowled[g]e
23    that we shall provide to your exaltedness,
24    for [e]ach undertaking,
25    twenty-four solidi of go[ld],
26    at the risk of us and of our
27    estate. Valid is the agreement, written
28    singly, and being formally questioned I acknowledged. The community
29    of the names agrees with us
30    as is aforesaid. I, John, wrote on their behalf,
31    they being illiterate.
32    Agreement of those from the village of Ophis in th[e] Ox[y]rhynchite nome.
