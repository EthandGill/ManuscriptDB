1     … of him … of the part out of the whole
2     … which was planted with vines by me on his behalf, by account
3     [of the agreement made] between you, having entrusted (it)
4     … [to your] splendour, each year the (sum) contained in it,
5     … [of] three full-weight gold [s]olidi each year, for consolation
6     … accordingly I have made for you the present
7     [wri]tten and inviolable acknowledgement of mine, through which I acknowledge that I am ready
8     to give to you each year, both in flooded and unflooded (land), without delay,
9     all (the dues) of the [perp]etual rent, three full-weight gold solidi,
10    in the month Phaophi, from the crops of the [pre]sent tenth indiction and that (year) thereafter inclusive, for as long a
11    time as the present written acknowledgement of mine remains in force with yo[u].
12    Moreover I will also pay you another six full-weight solidi,
13    once for all, for the just-passed ninth indiction, at my risk
14    and from the resource of my entire substance. The present acknowledgement
15    is valid and secure, and on being questioned I gave my assent. I, Aurelius Abraam, son of Phoibammon, assent;
16    the present acknowledgement is as aforesaid. I, Philippos the notary, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing
17    letters. I, Aurelius Petros, son of Phoibammon, from Hermoupolis, bear witness
18    to the present acknowledgement, having heard (it) from the one who made it. Phoibammon, son of Biktor,
19    from Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the one who made it.
20    Through me, Philippos, with God's help the contract-writer, it was written …
