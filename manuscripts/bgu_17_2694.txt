[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving
2     trinity, father and son and holy spirit,
3     of the reign of our most divine master
4     Flavius Phocas the eternal Augustus,
5     Imperator, in the sixth year, Tybi the twentieth,
6     of the eleventh indiction.
7     Aurelius Patous, son of Isakios,
8     from (his) mother Nonna … eus,
9     from the village of Enseu of the Hermoupolite (nome),
10    To Aurelius Matheias the most marvelous master, t[ow-]worker (stippourgos), son
11    of the late Papn[outh]ios, from
12    the Hermoupolite (city), greeting. I acknowledge
13    that I have received and been paid in full from
14    you the complete and worthy price
15    of light new large vessels,
16    five hundred thirty-
17    two, and four kouri (small jars),
18    that is, light new large vessels 532, and kouri 4,
19    which I shall furnish you in the month Epeiph
20    of the, with God, twelfth indiction, in vessels
21    new, fine, pitched,
22    untarred(?), unsoured(?), unbroken,
23    and I acknowledge … of them
24    to give; moreover also tale… omen
25    of them to the notary further two
26    of the vessels kata… to exchange for you
27    with fine vessels. But if I do not give you
28    the same vessels [in the] same term,
29    I shall furnish you for the[ir] price
30    gold solid[i] two, at risk
31    of myself and (on) the security of my entire
32    property. The [pre]sent document
33    (is) valid and secur[e, an]d on being asked,
34    I acknowledged. Au[reli]us Patous
35    son of Isakios the aforesa[id] have made this
36    document a[s] aforesaid.
37    Aurelius Zacharias … from Hermopolis
38    wrote (on his behalf), he not knowi[ng] his letters. …
39    (son) of Georgios, least deacon, from Hermopolis, I bear witness
40    to the document, having heard from the contracting party.
41    Aurelius Kollouthos (son of) Apollos, from Hermopolis,
42    I bear witness to the document, having heard from the contracting party.
43    Written through me, Isakos, with God, notary (symbolaiographos). idith
44    … [of Patous]
45    son of Isakios, from the village of Enseu of the Hermoupolite (nome).
