# HGV-EN: Year 33 of Lucius Aelius Aurelius Commodus Caesar, the lord, Tybi 3. Paid to S and his associates, tax collectors, (by) Satabous, son of Pakysis, and Stotoetis, son of Onnophris, principal of the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, for the tax of year 31 four hundred silver drachmas, = 400 drachmas.
