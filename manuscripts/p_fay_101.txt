[TRANSLATION]
1     Year …, Pauni 23.
2     … of 37 yokes (of oxen),
3     at a tithe of wheat each, they come to 3⅗ artabas of wheat by the dromos-measure.
4     Winnowers of wheat, 1⅔ artaba,
5     ratokopai (workers), 3,
6     freight-charge of 53 pack-animals,
7     at 6 (?) of wheat (each), they come to 7⅚ artabas of wheat.
8     And as rent of wheat, 6: 154 artabas.
9     And of cumin, with
10    the surcharges, 6: 33 artabas,
11    and to Ptollas the scribe, ½,
12    they come to 33½ artabas of cumin.
13    And freight-charge of 5 pack-animals
14    at 6 of cumin (each), which is ⅚ of cumin,
15    they come, the cumin, to 34⅓ artabas.
16    And of lentil, 6: 15 artabas,
17    freight-charge of 5 donkeys at 6 each, they come to ⅚,
18    and to the sword-bearers (guards)
19    and to Agenor, 21, a tithe,
20    and of safflower, 6: 50 artabas,
21    which is 60 artabas of wheat, they come, the rent,
22    to 229 artabas of wheat, and to the gardener 1 artaba of wheat, they come to 230 artabas.
23    Against which 247½ artabas of wheat,
24    there is left over 17½ artabas of wheat,
25    and 60 silver drachmas.
26    Another account. Safflower: 28th (Pauni),
27    to Tasys 9 workers,
28    29th, 8; 30th, 5;
29    Epeiph 1st, 7; 2nd, 4;
30    they come to 33 workers at a tithe of wheat (each), they come to 3 1/5 1/10 artabas of wheat.
31    Epeiph 3rd, ratokopoi 8,
32    4th, 9, they come to 17 workers at a tithe of wheat (each),
33    they come to 1⅗ artabas of wheat.
34    Another account. The allotment of Petaus:
35    2nd, 9 workers; 3rd, 8;
36    likewise in the same allotment
37    ratokopoi 9,
38    they come to 26 workers,
39    at a tithe of wheat (each), they come to 2 1/2 1/10 artabas of wheat.
40    Of which, of barley-wheat 1 artaba,
41    and of lentil 2 artabas,
42    and of safflower 1 artaba.
43    Account of silver.
44    To the workers, 10 drachmas,
45    to the ratokopoi, of cumin, 4 drachmas,
46    to the sword-bearers (guards), 6 drachmas,
47    for expenses, 4 drachmas,
48    likewise of copper 1500, likewise of copper 350,
49    they come to 1750 of copper, which is at 1 drachma, they come to 25 drachmas.
50    And as price of beer, 4 drachmas,
51    they come to 29 drachmas. And as price of oil, 1 choinix, 5 drachmas.
52    They come to 34 drachmas. Of which as price of cumin, 1 artaba, 7 drachmas.
53    And on behalf of Aphraesis, 4 drachmas,
54    and on behalf of Mesthas, 4 drachmas,
55    and as price of safflower, 4 artabas, 16 drachmas, they come to 24 drachmas.
56    Against which 29 drachmas. There is left over 3 drachmas.
57    Above: Pauni 4th to Epeiph 15th.
58    Account. Of Medresis, 150 artabas of wheat,
59    to the gardener 1 artaba,
60    of safflower, which is 60 artabas of wheat,
61    of lentil 15 artabas,
62    of barley-wheat 4 artabas,
63    they come to 230 artabas.
