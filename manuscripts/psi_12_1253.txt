[TRANSLATION]
1     [Spar]tas son of Pausanias of Sarapion, mother Didyme
2     having served as gymnasiarch of the city of Oxyrhynchus, to Marcus [Aurelius] Ktesidemos, also called Dionysios, having served as exegetes of the same city, and as he is styled
3     greetings. I acknowledge having from you from your house silver
4     of Augustan currency drachmas ten thousand
5     two thousand, [which] are of silver two talents, which I will repay to you
6     until the 30th of Phaophi of the entering eighth
7     and twenty-sixth year, with interest of one tetrobol for each mina per month from the present month Athyr of the current seventh and twenty-sixth
8     year of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar
9     of the lord. But if I do not repay as written,
10    I will pay you both the principal with interest [at the rate of] one and a half, and interest for the overdue time at the same tetrobol rate, the enforcement being
11    to you of enforcement from both me and from all my possessions as if from judgment.
12    Valid is my own handwriting of Spartas
13    written in duplicate, carried everywhere and to everyone carrying [it] on your behalf. Year seventh
14    and twenty of the Emperor Caesar Marcus
15    [Aurelius] Commodus Antoninus pious
16    fortunate Augustus, Armeniacus Medicus
17    [Parthicus] Sarmaticus Germanicus, greatest
18    Britannicus, Athyr 15th.
