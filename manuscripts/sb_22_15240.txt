1     17
2     [During the reign] of Ptolemy [and] Cleopatra, [children] of Ptolemy and [Cleopatra]
3     [the gods] Manifest, in the twenty-sixth year, under the priest who was in [Alexandria]
4     [of Alexander] and of the Savior Gods and of the Brother Gods and of the Benefactor [Gods and of the]
5     [Father-lo]ving Gods and of the Manifest Gods and of the Mother-loving Gods, [prize-bearing Beren][ice the Benefactress] basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphus priestess of Arsinoe Philo[pator of those]
6     [being in A]lexandria, in the month Dystros the seventeenth, Thoth the seventeenth, in
7     [Ammoni]a of the Arsinoit nome. Lysimachus son of Timarchos, a Bithynian of [the descend][ants], loaned to A[g]asia, a Macedonian [of the descendants] of Arateos of the second hippar[chy], a hundred-aroura [holder], two hundred artabas of [grain at] one and a half [times the interest] on the produce [of the] sixth [and twenty-sixth]
8     year of his allotment which was near Ammoniada, which he [also] privately [sows. Aga]sia acknowledges to have been measured and to have [received] from Lysimachus for the [seeding]
9     of this land. Agasia shall repay to Lysimachus the aforementioned [loan] and the one and a half times its interest, all [artabas] of grain three hundred, [from the]
10    aforementioned produce of the allotment in the month of Ap[ellaeus, Egyptian] Pauni of the same twenty-sixth year, grain seed [new pure]
11    and unadulterated from all and not black, of that born in [the allotment,]
12    by measure of choes of Timarchos, measured and [with] the just rod. [He] shall restore [it to Am]moni[ada] wherever Lysimachus directs with his own expenses [but if]
13    he does not [repay] as it has been written, he shall pay immediately a fine for [each ar]tab of coined copper drachmas one thousand, but in the [current season when]
14    it is sold in Ammoniada the greatest [pri]ce [one and a half times,]
15    and if he should not obtain the grain, it shall be permitted to Lysimachus and [to those with him]
16    to control all the produce of the [aforementioned]
17    allotment, and expelling himself both Lysimachus and those with him [and descend]ants [choose equally to]
18    this land or on the produce from it [unaccountable]
19    they shall be from all fine and damage. Guarantor for Agasia of the a[foresaid] loan of two hundred artabas of grain or [the price]
20    of them [is] to settle and guarantor of the fruits [is] his son Di[ophantos]
21    of Agasia, a Macedonian of the descendants, but the execution shall be to Lysimachus pressing [Aga]sia or his guarantor, and against one of them whichever he chooses and against both
22    and from all [their] possessions just as from judgment, but according to this [document]. And Agasia shall sign a handwritten acknowledgment to Lysimachus immediately
23    before those to whom it is customary a royal oath, in which he shall clarify also the things made clear through this document [evident], and neither shall Lysimachus bring against him neither suit [nor]
24    judgment nor anything else similar to these nor anyone having brought [himself]
25    to Lysimachus such a thing, nor [to] bring exception regarding the allotment. The document [is]
26    valid everywhere wherever it [is] brought.
