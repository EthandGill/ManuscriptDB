[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii acknowledge to one another: Sar[apammon also called]
2     Dionysios, son of Theon also called Demetrios, and Aphynchis
3     also called Cornelius, son of Aphynchis, son of Aphynchis,
4     both from the city of Oxyrhynchos, having been nominated
5     in the present year by the amphodogrammateus of the 5th tribe, 3rd period,
6     to the grain-collectorship of the eastern toparchy of the village of Phthochis,
7     through their guarantor Aurelius Satornilos also called
8     Theon, son of Theon, from the same city, and Aurelius Valerius, son of Sarapammon, his mother being Markia Eudaimonis, from the same
9     city — the party of Sarapammon also called Dionysios,
10    through their aforementioned guarantor Satorninos also called
11    Theon, (acknowledge) that they have deputized Valerius for the secretaryship of the aforesaid grain-collectorship, on condition that the same
12    Valerius shall make the receipt of all the
13    public wheat measured at the same grain-collectorship,
14    issuing to those who measure the dispatch-notes and the receipts, and shall obey the strategos of the nome, submitting each month the totals of the wheat received by him,
15    and further shall hand over to the shippers who are commissioned all the
16    wheat indicated by him, taking the customary receipts; and Valerius, being henceforth compliant concerning both salary and all expenses, consents to these things and will do each thing as has been declared above,
17    it not being permitted to any of the acknowledging parties to transgress the foregoing terms. And as to whether these things are done rightly and well,
18    questioning themselves, they acknowledged it.
19    In the 2nd year of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius
20    Severus Alexander Pius Felix Augustus,
21    Pauni 6. Sarapammon also called Dionysios,
22    son of Theon, and Cornelius also called Aphynchis Aeitos,
23    through me, Aurelius Satorneilos, we have deputized Aurelius Valerius
24    upon all the foregoing terms, he consenting.
