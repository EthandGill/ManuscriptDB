1     [Hor]os, collector of money-taxes of Charax, to Petepsais (son of) Pankametis
2     [(son of) Phatres(?)]. I have received for the doubles (dipla) of year 4, base drachmas two, total 2 dr.
3     [Year 4 of A]ntoninus Caesar the lord,
4     [Me]cheir 13. I, Heraklas, have signed.
