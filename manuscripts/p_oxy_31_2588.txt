[TRANSLATION]
1     Diogenes of those having-been [agoranomoi] and [gymnasiarchs]
2     through Hermias the oikonomos
3     to the grain-collectors above the toparchia of Skow [places]. Distribute from which you have
4     of me in the lot of wheat
5     of produce year eleven of Antoninus
6     Caesar of the lord at Thonei
7     Thonios of Thonsius from Oxyrhynchus city
8     of wheat artabai nineteen and half, (they) become of wheat artabai ten nine and half. Year twelve
9     of Antoninus the lord
10    Thoth twenty-seven.
