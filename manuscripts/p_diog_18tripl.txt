[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Didymos, strategos of the Arsinoite, division of Herakleides,
2     from [M]arcus Lucretius Diogenes, of Antinoopolis. A copy of the petition which I submitted to Claud[ius] Her[e]nnianus, the most excellent juridicus, also administering the
3     affairs of the prefecture, and of the subscription which I received, is appended below; and I ask that the consequent things be done, namely that you appoint a guardian to the undersigned minors, that nothing may be lost to [the]m, in accordance with the laws. It is as follows:
4     To Tiberius Claudius Herennianus, the most excellent juridicus, also administering [the affairs c]oncerning the prefecture by divine command,
5     from Marcus Lucretius Diogenes, of Antinoopolis. Bringing a petition befitting your nobility, I am hopeful of obtaining the
6     just things from you. I shall define the matter in brief. In the past month Phaophi my sister Octavia Lucretia [died] intestate, leaving as heirs three sons from different marriages, the Marci Aurelii Iulias and Lucretius and Rufus, minors. Now one of them, Iulias, being under the authority of his own father Marc[us] Aurelius Heron, has received
7     the share falling to him of the things left behind. But the others—Lucretius happens to be fatherless, and of Rufus
8     the father has died, and no one is in nearer kinship to them [than] I, [Lu]cretius Dio[g]enes, on whose account I make this …
9     petition, and I request, if it seem good to your Fortune, that you order by your sacred subscription the strategos of the division of Herakleides
10    of the Arsinoite nome, where their affairs are, to appoint me guardian of the orphanhood
11    of the children, so that I may be able to make the administration of the things left to th[e]m, that these may be preserved for them [unto] majority. Farewell. I, Marcus Lucretius Diogenes, through me, Au[reli]us Sarapion, have submitted it. The strategos, having his own risk before his eyes, will see to appointing a guardian for the minors. Of sheets, 68.
