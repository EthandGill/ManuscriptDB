[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: In the consulship of our lords Constantius and Maximianus, most renowned Caesars, for the fifth time. To Aurelius Seuthes also called Horion, logistes of the Oxyrhynchite nome, from Aurelius Sakaon son of Petiris, and Aurelius Psois son of Patabes, both comarchs of the village of Tampeti. In reply to your request in the present 21st which = the 13th year, in accordance with the order of his excellency the praefect Clodius Culcianus, for the village-accounts of our village in the two months Pharmouthi and Pachon, we, regarding this as a necessary duty, present them for your information, as follows :— For the price of papyrus and writing-materials ... of three workmen sent to Babylon 120 drachmae; for the price of papyrus and writing-materials ... of one workman sent to ... 1[..] drachmae; total together 2[..] drachmae...
