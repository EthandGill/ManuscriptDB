[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 10 of Emperor Caesar Domitian Augustus
2     Germanicus. [He] worked in new embankment of canal for 5 days for
3     embankment-works of the same tenth year of Socnopaeus Isle
4     Panephremis elder of Papitus [son] of Papitus
5     mother Segathis.
6     Asclepiades [I] have verified.
7     Hephaestion [I] have verified Panephremis elder of Papitus.
8     Hierakion scribe [I] have verified [the] five-day work of the embankment-works.
