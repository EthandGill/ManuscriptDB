[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 2 of Gaius Pescennius Niger
2     Iustus Augustus, Choiak 9, [it] was recorded
3     to Thon and Demetrius, tax-farmers
4     hair-tax and hand-weaving-tax of the Polioi of Pauetis
5     of the village of Soknopaiou of the Island for
6     payment of the same year on account
7     drachmas twenty, [they] become drachmas 20, and Phamenoth
