[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemaeus, Macedonian of Metrophanes, of the second
2     cavalry unit, seventy-arura [holder], to Demetrius, an assistant of the
3     second cavalry unit, sixty-arura [holder], greetings. I have from you the rents of the fourth year which I leased to you before the record-keepers, along with what you have measured on my behalf for the royal [treasury] of the
4     fourth year.
5     Be well. Year 5
6     Pauni 23.
