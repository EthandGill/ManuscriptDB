r.1    Σαραπίωνι τῶν δι[α]δόχων καὶ ὑποδιοικητῆι παρὰ Πτολε[μαί]ου Μακεδώνος τοῦ ὄντος ἐν κατοχῇ ἐν τῷ πρὸς Μέμφει

[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapeion of the successors and to the sub-administrator from Ptolemaeus the Macedonian, the one under detention near Memphis,
2     at the great Serapeum and themselves of the twins each Tauetes and Tautus, twins.
3     you, according to which ancestral choice you hold, in many ways taking hold of them
