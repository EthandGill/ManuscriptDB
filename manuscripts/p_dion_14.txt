1     In the reign of Cleopatra and Ptolemy, the Mother-loving
2     Saviour gods, in the eighth year, in the priesthood of the (priest) who is in Alexandria
3     of Alexander and the Saviour gods and the Sibling gods and the
4     Benefactor gods and the Father-loving gods and the Manifest [gods and]
5     the god Eupator and the god Philometor and the god New Father-loving (god)
6     and the god Benefactor and the Mother-loving Saviour gods, (in the priesthood) of the [sacred]
7     foal of Isis mother of the gods, the great one, of the wreath-bearer of Queen
8     Cleopatra goddess Mother-loving Saviour, Justice-(bearing), Victory-bearing,
9     of the prize-bearer of Berenice the Benefactress, of the light-bearer of Queen Cleopatra
10    goddess Mother-loving Saviour, Justice-(bearing), Victory-bearing, of the basket-bearer
11    of Arsinoe Brother-loving, of the priestess of Queen Cleopatra goddess Mother-loving Saviour, Justice-(bearing), Victory-bearing, of the priestess of Arsinoe
12    Father-loving, those being and existing in Alexandria, in the month
13    Dios, on the twenty-sixth, Thoth on the twenty-sixth, in Tenis also (called)
14    Akoris of the Hermopolite (nome): Dionysios son of Asklepiades,
15    a Persian of the (corps) of Apollophanes and Exakon, of the cleruch cavalry,
16    lent to Dionysios son of Kephalas, a Persian of the epigone, twenty artabas of wheat,
17    which he has made his own from him, at one-and-a-half interest, ten artabas; and the
18    whole thirty artabas of wheat let the borrower repay
19    to Dionysios in the month Loios, which is Pauni of the eighth year,
20    new wheat, clean and unadulterated, by the measure that is for the bronze (standard),
21    delivered to the harbor by Akoris, without lawsuit
22    or judgment or any quibbling; and if he does not repay according to what
23    is written, let the borrower pay to Dionysios as the price
24    of each artaba three thousand drachmas of bronze, and to the royal (treasury)
25    sacred (dues) of silver of the old coinage sixty drachmas
26    and none the less; and let the right of execution belong to Dionysios for the wheat and
27    for all the matters of the contract, both from Dionysios himself
28    the borrower and from all his belongings
29    as if by legal judgment; let this contract be valid. Witnesses: Dionysios son of Ptolemaios, a Macedonian, attendant of the company, Apollonios …,
30    Dionysios son of Apollonios, Herakleides son of Her…, Hermophilos son of Ammonios,
31    the four (being) Persians of the cleruchs from (the troop of) Cleopatra, Ptolemaios
32    son of Anaxagoras, a Milesian. Keeper of the contract: Dionysios.
33    I, Dionysios son of Kephalas, a Persian of the epigone, have
34    the thirty artabas of wheat as is aforesaid
35    and I have given it as valid to Dionysios.
36    I, Dionysios, have (it) as valid.
37    Year 8, Thoth 26, in the village Tenis of the Mochite (toparchy); registered through Ptolemaios.
38    Year 8, Thoth 26: a loan of Dionysios son of Asklepiades against Dionysios, of 30 artabas of wheat;
39    let him repay in Pauni of the 8th year; penalty per artaba 3000 drachmas, and to the royal (treasury) of silver 60 drachmas.
40    (of) Dionysios; (of) Dionysios; (of) Dionysios; (of) Hermophilos
41    (of) Dionysios son of Apollonios; (of) Herakleides; (of) Ptolemaios
42    a loan of Dionysios …
