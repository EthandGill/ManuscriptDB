[TRANSLATION]
1     Artemis to Theodoros, my lord husband, greeting in God.
2     Before all else I pray
3     to the Lord God that we may receive you back in good health. I sent you, through
4     Apon your fellow-soldier, a letter and a hooded cloak. I pray
5     for your health. Your children greet you, and Allous threatens you much, since
6     although you wrote often and
7     greeted everyone, her alone you did not greet. Allous greets you.
8     Artemis to Sarapion also called Isidoros. She writes:
9     if you are a soldier and bear arrogance — but the governor quickly humbles arrogance. You write to
10    us as though to terrify us, saying that the governor does not want home-wreckers. But if you wish to condone the fornications of your daughters, do not examine me, but the
11    elders of the church,
12    how the two ran off
13    saying, "We want husbands,"
14    and how Loukra was found
15    with her adulterer, making herself out to be a Gaditanian, from which
16    they are envious that we have attached them to Soucharos. But if it were
17    a matter of naming concerning lineage,
18    these things too we are again ready
19    to prove — who is the better-born. For we were not [born] from a slave-woman,
20    being born. These things I write to you, Theodoros, that you do everything for what is at stake, and one must make matters known to him by letter.
21    Deliver to Theodoros the soldier, from
22    his son.
