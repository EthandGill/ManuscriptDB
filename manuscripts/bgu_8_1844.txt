[TRANSLATION]
1     … holding fast to no just claim … hinder … concerning the …
2     … of this hinder … …
3     they themselves, because of being brought down upon the
4     … we decided to do what he wished, having set down for one another an agreement of the
5     things written by hand, that we hold, from the wall,
6     two-thirds part, but he the remaining third,
7     having guaranteed and on condition of not hindering
8     nor bringing a charge in any way whatsoever, with penalties fixed against the transgressor,
9     … having despaired of what is right
10    according to the deed of the agreement, that he would hinder,
11    employing devices, until it remains to us
12    instead of the third of the wall the half, being all but seized … … having to do with …, that we be involved not only in damages but also in charges,
13    let us be overturned; but now, the occasion having presented itself, we have been led to make against these (men)
14    an attack. We request, if it seem good,
15    that you direct a letter to be written to Adrastos the garrison-commander (ordering) the
16    accused to be brought before you, so that he may be compelled to restore to us in fuller measure [the]
17    part forcibly taken contrary to the contract,
18    a sixth, to its original condition, and pay the damages and the equivalents and obtain what is fitting, that we may be
19    assisted. Farewell.
20    To the scribes, year 3 …
21    Write to the garrison-commander that order be given to bring (him) before (you).
