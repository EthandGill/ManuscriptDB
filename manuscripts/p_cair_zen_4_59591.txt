[TRANSLATION]
1     Dioskourides to Zenon greetings. A certain farmer of [those]
2     in Pharbaetho is selling three notable female donkeys. If it seems [good] to you, he will bring them to you; but if you do not [see] them within 2 or 3 [days], he will give [them]
3     to others; for he is about to travel into Egypt.
4     And concerning the fodder, if you do not send someone into
5     the city of Akanthos in haste, no longer will we find [it],
6     but we will be compelled to buy again from the market
7     six obols the bundle. Farewell. Phamēnōth the 1st.
8     To Zenon.
