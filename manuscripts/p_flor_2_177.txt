[TRANSLATION]
1     From Apianu.
2     And beforehand I sent [orders] that fine small breads be made and sent to us, and you did not make [them], and yesterday, which is the 15th
3     having met with the donkey-driver
4     Sokra, I gave the same orders to him. But since I know that you need
5     both necessity and reminder, for this reason
6     I have also sent to you now
7     concerning this. And there is need of fenugreek. And if [even] a little, [send] fenugreek
8     you ought to contribute; for [it is] absurd
9     for them to buy [it]
10    when you all provide [it]. For the doctors need
11    it, both for wafers and for other medical needs. I wish you to be well.
12    To Eirenius, manager.
13    Year 5, Hathyr 16, 26th
