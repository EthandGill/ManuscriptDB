[TRANSLATION]
1     Philocles to Capparas the brother greetings most;
2     I sent to you through the
3     emerald-merchant
4     a basket full of dainties whe[re] there are apples twenty
5     and melons two and through Herennius the horse-dealer lettuces sixteen
6     and apples ten and
7     onions ten [and] mint and melon.
8     Write back.
9     Write back to me if you received and to Proclos
10    a bunch of cabbage.
11    Sknips greets y[ou]
12    much.
13    Be strong.
