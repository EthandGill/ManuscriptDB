[TRANSLATION]
1     To Gaius Julius Asinianus, strategos
2     of the Arsinoite (nome),
3     from Mnesitheus son of Theon. From the (suit) which I brought
4     in the fourteenth year of the deified Claudius — a charge
5     and a service by way of notification both against other men
6     and against Heration son of Maron, on the ground that they were unlawfully holding the property of my wife Maronis,
7     [of] Heration on the one hand, near Leto's city in the Herakleides division,
8     of vine-land six arouras, and of the other men, others
9     of the property of Maronis — I obtained, concerning all (these matters),
10    from Lucius Lusius Geta, the former prefect, as judge
11    Gaius Julius Iollas, priest and gymnasiarch of Alexandria,
12    who, having heard both me and the others, decided that
13    the ownership should remain to my wife in accordance with what I hold
14    by way of memoranda. But Heration having become invisible (i.e. absent)
15    and not having appeared at the trial, he was summoned by proclamation
16    by Geta through an edict, and even so not
17    having been seen up to now, he has deliberately remained abroad,
18    as though the proper time for the trial had not arrived.
19    But now, since I have arrived and have undertaken on behalf of
20    Mys… to lay claim, of the vine-land
21    six arouras, three arouras, and (since) Heration himself
22    (lays claim to) the other three arouras, I myself, not keeping quiet,
23    but rather all the more clinging to the charge against those accused,
24    submit this memorandum and request
25    that, when it has been registered with you, the copy be transmitted
26    to the accused through an attendant, so that, being informed, they may present themselves
27    wherever the lord prefect Tiberius Claudius Balbillus
28    holds the nearest assize, in order that through his
29    hearing I may obtain my just rights, and they
30    what befits (them). Farewell.
