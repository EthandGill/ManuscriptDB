r.1    Παναμεῦς καὶ μέτοχοι ἀπαιτηταὶ μερισμοῦ τελωνικῶν Νότου καὶ Ὠφιήου

r.7    [Πε]τεχεσποχράτης σεσημείωμαι.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Panameus and partners, collectors of customs assessment of South and Ophieus
2     to Philamun, similarly [of] mother Tatriphdos. We have received for the assessment
3     22 drachmas two, making drachmas 2. 23rd year of Antoninus Caesar
4     our lord, Pachon 27.
5     [Other entries]: Pauni 9 in the name of the same, similarly obols 6 chalki 2
6     [and] in the name of Herieus Chemtsnaeus obol. Panameus, I have signed.
7     [Pe]techespocratus, I have signed.
