[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the epistates of the Thebaid, … there being present Phi… of the cavalry,
2     Eutychides, Apollonios, …molos, Hermokles, all of (the troop of) Ammonios
3     son of Apollonios, cavalry settlers, Athenodoros son of …ylos,
4     Herakleides son of Bion, and several others.
5     When Amenothes son of Horos, paraschistes (embalmer's cutter), came before us against Petenephotes
6     son of Petenephotes, one of the same (guild), he alleged that the accused was organizing an exaction (levy)
7     of things not proper to him, and that too contrary to the common agreement
8     made through him in the 51st year, on Payni 13, by which it is indicated
9     — among other things — that in Diospolis (Thebes) itself the priests of Ammon and
10    their slaves were to have authority over him in matters concerning the
11    embalmer's cutter's service, with penalties for the one who transgresses the agreement;
12    but now he was laying claim also to those freed by them
13    and to those born of priests and priestesses and to illegitimate children, transgressing
14    the things declared through it. When that (agreement) had been read out, and
15    Petenephotes was unable to put forward anything against this,
16    we gave order that he attempt no longer to carry out any such thing,
17    but that they abide by the terms to which they willingly agreed with one another,
18    on which they departed reconciled.
