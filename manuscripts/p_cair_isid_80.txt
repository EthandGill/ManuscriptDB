[TRANSLATION]
1     [From the qua]rt[er of the Bithynians of Ision, to Au]re[lius Hero]n son of Pto[lemaios, from the vil]la[g]e of Ka[rani]s,
2     [greeting]. Since … o … [in] place of you
3     … [th]e service [of the] auxiliary (soldier),
4     [and I having] re[ceived fr]om you, for the wages
5     [an]d the inducement-payment, the three talents of silver agreed between us, therefore from here I acknowledge that I have received these same and
6     aforesaid three talents of silver, and the sword,
7     and (that I shall) blamelessly fulfil the service in your place,
8     and I shall remain there in the same auxiliary-service
9     until I obtain discharge, being content
10    with this aforesaid inducement-payment, and neither of us having authority to change any of these things or to transgress any of the things written herein
11    in any manner; and from here I have provided
12    [as] guarantor Aurelius Dios son of Polion, from the
13    same quarter of the Bithynians of Ision, who being present
14    consented to this agreement made,
15    on account of our having mutually composed with one another
16    on these terms. Having questioned one another, we agreed.
17    Year 2 of our lord Lucius Domittius
18    Domittianus, Pious, Fortunate, Augustus, Phaophi
19    22. I, Aurelius Heron son of Ptolemaios, from the village of Karanis,
20    have agreed to all the aforesaid and shall transgress nothing as is set out above,
21    and having been questioned I agreed. I, Aurelius Chairemon, wrote on his behalf at his request, he being illiterate.
22    I, Aurelius Deios, have stood surety for Heron as is set out [above].
