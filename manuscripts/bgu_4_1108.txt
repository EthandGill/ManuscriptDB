[TRANSLATION]
1   To Artemidoros, archidikastes and superintendent of the chrematistai and the other tribunals,
2   from Marcus Sempronius, son of [Marcus], of the tribe Aemilia, soldier of the
3   twen[ty-]second legion, of the coh[ort …] … [… and] from
4   E[rotarion daughter of …]komei…, [with as guardian and su]rety her kinsman [L]ucius …omysius,
5   son of Lucius. Erotarion consents, for fifteen months from
6   Phaophi of the current twenty-sixth year of Caesar, to nurse and suckle,
7   outside, at her own home in the city,
8   (inserted:) with her own milk, pure and unspoiled,
9   the slave child that Marcus has entrusted to her already since Epeiph of the past year,
10   whose name is Primus, at a wage for the milk and the nursing of ten drachmas a month
11   and two kotylai of oil; and forthwith Erotarion has received from Marcus
12   the nursing-wages [and] the oil for Epeiph and Mesore and Thoth …, and further for the sixteen months from [Pha]ophi
13   (inserted:) likewise the nursing-wages and the oil of six months; and if — may it not happen —
14   it befall the child to suffer the human lot
15   …
16   (inserted:) within the six months, Erotarion is bound, taking up another child, to nurse and suckle it for the
17   6 months, not
18   (inserted:) taking more than the aforesaid ten … months […]
19   Erotarion, kept in good order with the nursing-wages and oil, is to exercise
20   the care both of herself and of the child, not spoiling her milk, nor sleep-
21   ing with a man, nor conceiving, nor suckling another child besides, for the months —
22   and whatever she receives —
23   (inserted:) six from Phaophi; and if after the six months Erotarion wishes another child …
24   (inserted:) to suckle besides, it shall be permitted her; and whatever she receives or is entrusted with of the child’s belongings, (to return) whenever demanded, or to pay
25   the value of each item, except manifest loss, on which, having also been made evi-
26   dent, she shall be released; and not to quit the nursing within <the> period. And if she transgresses anything,
27   she is to pay back the nursing-wages she has received and whatever she may receive in addition, with half again,
28   and the damages and expenses, and a further two hundred silver drachmas, the right of execution being
29   from Erotarion and from either and from each and from whichever of them
30   [Ma]rcus chooses, and
31   from all her belongings, as if by legal judgment; and (she is) not to bring forward pleas, or they are void.
32   And Erotarion performing each thing, Marcus is to furnish her the remaining monthly
33   nursing-wages as aforesaid, and not to take the child away within <the> period …, or himself be li-
34   able to the same penalty. And Erotarion shall come over to Marcus with the
35   child always three times a month, for it to be inspected by hi[m.] And Lucius is to be regis-
36   tered in the right of approach against Erotarion for what she owes him together with her
37   mother Kleopatra, according to an agreement. We request (registration). Farewell.
38   (1st hand) Year 26 of Caesar, Phaophi 8.
