1     Of the Aphrodi[topolite (nome)], for the month of Thoth, Phaophi,
2     Hathyr, Choiak, thus:
3     of gold, the coin (solidus): 1, denarii 3,900 myriads;
4     of silver, the pound: 1, coins (solidi) 5;
5     of unworked silver, the pound: 1, coins (solidi) 4½ 4;
6     of grain, the artaba: 1, denarii 450 myriads;
7     of barley, the artaba: 1, denarii 225 myriads;
8     of lentils, the artaba: 1, denarii 360 myriads;
9     of chaff, the pound: 1, denarii 1½ myriads;
10    of radish-oil, the xestes: 1, denarii 105 myriads;
11    of meat, the pound: 1, denarii 30 myriads;
12    of salt, the artaba: 1, denarii 150 myriads;
13    of wine, by 7 xestai, denarii 200 myriads.
