[TRANSLATION]
1     To Achilleus, royal scribe,
2     from Didymus son of Spartalas
3     and his brother Callias, and
4     Pasion son of Pasion, and
5     his brother Amois, and Callias son of Callias, and Cephalas
6     son of Plution, and Plution
7     son of Syrus, and all their sons, from the city of Oxyrhynchus, and Zoilus son of Ptolemaeus, and Pasion
8     son of Thonis, and Panetbeus son of Harmiysis, from Nemera. We request that there be issued from
9     the public (treasury) the (sums) owed
10    to each of us as is set out below, and assigned through
11    the agents of Julius Hermias, former strategos, for the price
12    of wheat compulsorily purchased, of the produce of the first year of the deified
13    Nerva, which we measured out
14    into the public granary from the wheat allotted to the village of Nemera, at the rate per artaba
15    of 8 drachmas: Didymus for 2 artabas, 16 drachmas;
16    and his brother Callias for 1/2 artaba, 4 drachmas;
17    and Pasion for 2 artabas, 16 drachmas; and Amois
18    for 2 artabas, 16 drachmas; and the other Callias
19    for 7 1/2 1/4 artabas, 4 choinikes, 62 drachmas 4 obols; and Cephalas
20    for 3 artabas, 24 drachmas; and Plution and his
21    sons for 4 artabas, 32 drachmas; and Zoilus
22    for 1 artaba, 8 drachmas; and Pasion for 1/2 artaba, 4 drachmas;
23    and Panetbeus for 3 artabas, 24 drachmas,
24    making in all 206 drachmas 4 obols, that we may be benefited. Year 3
25    of the Emperor Caesar Nerva
26    Trajan Augustus Germanicus,
27    Tybi 27, Augustan day. Didymus and
28    Callias, both sons of Spartalas,
29    and Zoilus son of Ptolemaeus, have submitted (this). I, Didymus son of Spartalas,
30    wrote also on behalf of the others who do not
31    know letters. I, Pasion
32    son of Pasion, have submitted (this). I, Plution
33    son of Syrus, and the sons, and Panetbeus
34    son of Harmiysis, have submitted (this).
35    I, Pausiris son of Plution, wrote
36    also on behalf of the others who do not know letters. I, Cephalas son of Plution, have submitted (this). We, Amoitas son of Pasion and Pasion son of Thonis, have submitted (this). I, Amoitas son of Pasion, wrote on behalf of Pasion, who does not know letters. I, Callias son of Callias, have submitted (this).
