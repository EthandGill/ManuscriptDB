[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum of me, John, to my master
2     and brother Anastasios, namely that, God willing, on arriving in Antinoou (he should) entreat my master
3     the most magnificent count Menas concerning the
4     advance for the estate of Apammon and concerning the
5     island of the estate; and if his magnificence is willing to (give) me also two hundred xestai of radish-oil
6     so as to discuss also with the lord Theophilos concerning Leilos
7     and to hand over to him the 4 gold coins (nomismata) and to receive the
8     deeds of sale [from L]eilos; and if he is willing (to go) beyond the 4 coins,
9     let the … of Kollouthos swear before Victor the
10    brother of the aforementioned Leilos, and let him receive the remaining (sums).
11    Likewise, to cast out Papnouthis the farmer from my
12    estate and to seek from him all the things contained
13    in his lease and the advance which he holds
14    and the reeds from the eighth to the eleventh (indiction).
15    Likewise, God willing, to buy for me three hundred flat cheeses
16    and two artabas of … so as to seek the lord
17    … of the sister of the lord Neilos
18    the scriniarius of the count's office and to give him
19    the letters of his mother; and if there be need for him
20    up to ten coins, deign for him
21    to provide for him and to take an autograph (receipt) from him and
22    to send (it) to me so that he may receive them and I may send to you so as
23    to dispatch to me the one artaba of the kidney-bean for the fasts, and good charlock, and the
24    honey, and good sandals.
