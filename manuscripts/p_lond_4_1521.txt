[TRANSLATION]
1     [I]n the name of the Father an[d] …
2     and the life-giving and consubstan[tial] …
3     We, Pnei the son of George the lashane (village headman), with …
4     [the] east[ern (district) of] Tjkoou, who write to the public office (demosios logos) …
5     [Abdel]la the son of Abd-el-Melek, the symboulos (governor) …
6     the lord Epimachos, the illustrious and pagarch of the distri[ct] …
7     and its pediades (sub-districts), greeting. We acknowledge [that:] behold, Palau and Zacharias,
8     [m]en of the fields with their wives and their children, came to us as those who are listed below in this guarantee-[agreement which] they have produced —
9     in the nome of Antinoe, in this year, this seventh indiction, through Zoure son of
10    Aluasel the Saracen and the overseer (epikeimenos) of the Thebaid, the persons therefore
11    of this kind. We acknowledge that they came to us being comp[le]te [and] complete (in number); these therefore we ar[e] ready that we shall keep them, we shall give satisfaction for them according as you have commanded us; and to whomever your lordship shall seek out
12    their person from us, we are ready, we shall hand [them] over
13    to you without dispute, without cross (-examination), without de[lay] of (a single) day; but if not, that we shall give account for every penalty [whi]ch your lordship shall fix
14    upon us. The public office therefore being secured through your most glorious lordships, we have drawn up
15    this guarantee-agreement for you, we being liable to you, both we and that which is ours,
16    the whole, for this [matter]; and we swear by the name of [G]od the Almighty and
17    by the salvation of the masters who rule, [that we shall guar]d, we shall watch, according as we [previously]
18    wrote; they questioned us, we acknowledge.
19    Schedule, thus: Palau (son) of Mias and Stephanos …
20    Zacharias (son) of John, and Theophania his wife, [and Eli]sabeth his daughter, and Tsenouthis his daughter; total souls 4.
21    total souls 8.
22    I, Pnei the son of the blessed George the lashane, with the consent of his brother Chamoul, we agree to this guarantee-agreement
23    according to its content. I, Phoib[amon] the son of Patose, who was asked
24    by them, wrote on their behalf because they do not know (letters); and I bear witness, I (myself).
25    I, Joseph the son of Victor, bear [wit]ness to this guarantee-agreement,
26    according to its content. I, Peter the son of the blessed Makare, bear witness
27    to this guarantee-agreement, according to its content.
28    I, Apa Kyros the son of the blessed Andr[ea]s, bear witness to this guarantee-agreement,
29    according to [its content.]
30    Through me, Theodore, with God notary, it was written.
