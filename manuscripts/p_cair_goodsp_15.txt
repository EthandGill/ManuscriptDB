[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Mamertinus and Nevitta, the most illustrious.
2     To the Aurelii Nilos son of Gennadios and Theodoros son of Komasios, riparii of the Hermopolite nome,
3     from Aurelia Eus daughter of Phibis, from the hamlet of Paploou of the same Hermopolite nome: the outrages committed
4     against me, in such a peace administered by our master the king Flavius Julianus the eternal
5     Augustus, ought not to be overlooked unavenged. Since then I possess a farmstead by written security
6     in the same village 16 years ago, and to the north of the same farmstead I have bought a bare plot
7     together with the well in it, from Tasous daughter of Taurinos at the same time, and of the farmstead and the
8     bare plot I have held possession from the same time, along with the deeds of sale; I have made the construction of the bare
9     plot and, the cistern having been silted up, I have made the clearing-out, and I used to do the watering from the
10    well for my cattle of the herd up to the past year; and all the people of the village know
11    both the matter of the ownership and of the possession. But now I began to make the rebuilding
12    of the fallen wall. I do not know how Isakis son of Ammonion and his sister Tapiomis
13    and Helene daughter of Panitis and Helene wife of Petersos and Ta…en…s came upon me, wishing
14    to do away with me utterly, as they threaten me in every detail, had I not happened to get help, [and] also Taesis and
15    her sister Sophia; the one, Taesis, being pregnant, miscarried the baby from their blows, and they snatched away the onydion (?) of Sophia, and now it is with them; and the
16    wall of the bare plot they threw down in tyrannical fashion, wishing to snatch from me
17    the building-plots and the collapsed house of my mother, very near to the farmstead, holding
18    in hand the deeds of sale; and I made this known to the monastery (?) and to the assistant of the praepositus. Taking courage,
19    the same Isakis, in his money and his wealth, wishes to drive me out of the village
20    just as he banished the father of my children, who now is making his sojourn in foreign lands,
21    and today I pay (tax) for fifty arouras of unwatered land. Casting a greedy eye,
22    the registered man, upon my labors, just as many … from the village because of the
23    might of money; whence, not being able to keep quiet in any way, I was driven to submit
24    to your piety [these peti]tions, requesting … by your care
25    to … release the … e … peuton (?) o … en the
26    … a … may you prosper.
