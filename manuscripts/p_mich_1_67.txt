[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum from Menodoros
2     to Zenon. I had to render account
3     … from Pauni until Pachon
4     of the 5th year, of 12 months: of goats
5     120 — the tax at one kid each — that makes kids 150;
6     … 30 [she-]kids which I took on in addition in the 4th year, untaxed;
7     and from Zabdis, from the month Phamenoth
8     until Pauni, 4 months, 63 goats, of which
9     Epharmostos has 2 goats, remaining 61;
10    and I have 43 kids;
11    and on the 30th of Mecheir, from Pyrrhos, 55 goats
12    [and] 17 kids, apart from the 10 which I receiv[ed] …
13    [from] Hermias, 90 goats, 60 kids:
14    that makes 356 goats; from these
15    you have those which you handed over to the
16    Arabs, to Xenokles from Epeiph, 100,
17    [and] to the 3 Arabs another 146 goats; that makes 246;
18    remaining, which I myself have, 110 goats, of which there are
19    30 she-kids;
20    and of kids the total 270, apart from the 10
21    young ones of these; Iason has 40 kids
22    which he sold to the dealers, and Nikon those which
23    he received from Ammonia, 149; and there died of those of Hermias 2, of Zabdis 5, of Pyrrhos 5, making 12, of which Doxaios took the hides; others
24    you yourself have, 12; to Glaukias by letter 2,
25    another to Glaukias 1, and the son of Glaukias has 1,
26    and another Sostratos has 1, and of Epeiph
27    you have 32; that makes 250, remaining 20.
28    Year 5, month Tybi.
