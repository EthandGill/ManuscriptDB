[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting, from Amenneus, keeper of the Isis-shrine, and Stotoetis my wife, dwelling in
2     Arsinoe-on-the-embankment, of the division of Themistos. We are being wronged by Tnepherous daughter of
3     Petosiris, dwelling in the same village as ourselves. For in the 6th year, on Mesore 22, as the revenue records (show),
4     having taken with her Thasis and Thason and Bari…n and Thanesis and Tathripis and Aximama and
5     Hermippos and Poseidonios, the sons of Aximama, and having entered into the Isis-shrine belonging
6     to us, which is in the aforesaid village, they laid blows and more upon us,
7     striking whatever part of the body they happened to hit; and when my wife Stotoetis the
8     aforesaid had leaped out and entered into one of the chambers in the Isis-shrine, [and]
9     was being pursued by them, the aforesaid (sons) of Aximama, she shut herself in …
10    in a conical (vessel?), and Tnepherous threw the two children of Stotoetis into a chest; and I [took refuge]
11    upon the altar and cried out to people; and when certain people arrived and [rebuked] them, they left them and departed. And these things they did …
12    in the Isis-shrine, … and performing the sacrifices and the libations …
13    Amenneus against
14    Tnephoros.
15    Year 5, Mesore
16    25.
17    To Onesandros.
