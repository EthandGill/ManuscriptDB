[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios son of Herakleides, his mother being Herakleia, from Sophthis of the Herakleopolite nome above Memphis, and my associates, to Sarapion son of Sarapion son of Chairemon, of the Sosikosmian tribe and the
2     Zeneian deme, shipmaster and pilot of a cabin-boat of a carrying capacity of 800 artabas, whose emblem is …, greeting. I acknowledge that I have hired from you the aforesaid boat for the loading of two hundred empty Caesarean light-vessels, which, having loaded them from the harbour of …, you shall convey to a harbour of the aforesaid Herakleopolite nome above Memphis,
3     the agreed freight being two hundred and sixty drachmas of silver, which I have also paid you on the spot, the tolls and expenses and the expenditures along the river
4     and the mooring-dues and the timber-charge being chargeable to me the hirer. You shall furnish the boat fitted out for the voyage together with a sufficient crew, and you shall make the departure from the
5     city on the present day and sail the voyage in the customary manner, neither sailing by night nor while it is stormy, but mooring each day at the appointed and safest harbours at the fitting hours, except if — which may it not happen — some cause befall from the violence of Zeus, or from fire from the land, or from storm, or from evildoers attacking, which,
6     having made it manifest, you shall be free of liability together with the crew. You shall remain at the unloading one day, apart from any on which you moor, after which, if I detain you further, I shall give you for each day … … I shall transgress none of the aforesaid terms. …
7     … reckoning, on the ground that the hire is made on these terms. In the 16th year of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus, victor over the Armenians, the Medes, the Parthians, the Germans,
8     the greatest victor over the Sarmatians, on the twentieth of Mesore.
