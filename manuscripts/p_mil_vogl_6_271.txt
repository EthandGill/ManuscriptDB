[TRANSLATION]
1     … [and we the lessees will perform the yearly] agricultural works [of the arouras, all that are fitting,] at
2     our own expense at the proper seasons, doing no damage, sowing
3     each year the [one half] part of the ten arouras with wheat [and barley, and the] remaining half in the first five years with whatever we choose, and in the
4     last year with grass for cutting and drying and distribution to whatever
5     animals they choose; and we will pay you the rents each year in [the month Pauni at] the threshing-floor, new pure unadulterated, and the rent in the month [Phamenoth, the yearly] freight-charges being upon you,
6     of the supervision, but those of the carriage of the rents upon you; …
7     … of the rent of the present fifth
8     year … of the … lease … all being free of deduction and without risk; and after the term we will deliver the holding
9     clear of coarse grass and all weeds; the lease being clear
10    of all public dues; if it seems good to lease.
11    Ptollarion son of Paulinus, I have leased on the aforesaid terms.
12    Sarapion son of Amatios, I have leased on the nineteen and a half artabas of wheat
13    and five artabas of barley and one hundred thirty drachmas of silver, as set forth.
