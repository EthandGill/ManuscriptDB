[TRANSLATION]
1     [aged] …, [of medi]um (height), fair-skinned, with straight (hair)
2     … [a scar under the] hair in the middle of the forehead.
3     [Apollodoros] son of Nikanor, Macedonian of the epigone, against B[o]t[richos, ilarch t]hen of the (troop) of Automedon, lochagos, according as they contracted. Whereas, in the nine[teenth] year, in the month Daisios, on the eight[h, having borrowed] from me one hundred [and twenty] artabas of wheat
4     according to a contract of which the contract-keeper (is) Herak[lei]… [and] the contract stipulating that [you repay]
5     [me] this grain in the Egyptian month
6     … [of the] nineteenth y[e]ar, and, if [you do]
7     [not repay a]s is written, that you pay me as penalty
8     [for each ar]taba three hun[dred and fif]ty drachmas, [according as] the wheat is s[old] in the market at [Herakleopolis;] and, [the] time of the [loan according to the con]tract having elapsed, and much
9     [more (time) having super]ven[ed], and I having demanded of you the grain, you did not repay me according as
10    [you had cont]racted, therefore I bring suit against you in the
11    [allotted court at Herak]leopolis [above] Memphis, according to the contract, of the
12    [pri]ce of the one hundred and twenty artabas of the
13    wheat, of each artaba, of copper coin
14    [dr]achmas three hundred and fifty, according as the wheat is s[o]ld in the market at Herakleopolis,
15    of all the copper coin, seven talents.
16    [assessment of the suit:] cop[per coin, 7 talents.]
17    [year] [22] … complaint
18    of Apollodoros against Botrichos, of 7 talents.
