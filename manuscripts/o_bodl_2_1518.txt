[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, produce of the 24th year
2     Of Commodus Antoninus Caesar
3     Our Lord, Mesore 4, for the South
4     In the name of Peteprehus Lolouthus and associates, wheat artaba two-thirds
5     It becomes wheat artaba [two-thirds]. Amonius has signed.
