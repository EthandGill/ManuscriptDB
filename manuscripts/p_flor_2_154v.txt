[TRANSLATION]
1     From Alypius.
2     As soon as I receive the [letters], show me [how many]
3     artabas you measured for [the]
4     dekaphrotoi for [measured grain], and how many [you leave outstanding] [so that]
5     I may know. But if something is owed [without]
6     my letters, do not measure.
7     And if they need anything from you, report this too. I wish you well.
8     I have marked.
9     To Heroninus, manager of Theadelphia.
10    Year 15, Choiak 15.
11    From Alypius to [Heroninus], manager of Theadelphia.
