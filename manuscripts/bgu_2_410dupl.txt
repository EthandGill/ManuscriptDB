[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Hierax, strategos of the Arsinoite nom, Herakleides division from Hatre[s], son of [S]atabous, the son of Panephre[mmis], mother Segathis, from the village of Soknopaiou [N]esos. I register myself and my family for the house-by-house registration of the past year 23 of the God Aelius Antoninus, in the house, in which I live, which is in the village and is inherited from my mother. And I am the above mentioned Hatres, 25 years old, without a distinguishing mark and (I register) my wife Isari[o]n, father unknown, mother T[an]eph[r]emmis, daughter of Panomieus, 13 years old, without a distinguishing mark. There belong to her in the village two houses and her grandmother’s quarter (inheritance). [And] (there is) Tape[ts]iris, daughter of Stotoetis, the son of Pan[ephremmis], mother Ta[petsiris], 12 years old, without a distinguishing mark. There belongs to Tapets[i]ris two houses and a courtyard and her grandfather’s quarter and here grandmother’s quarter. Therefore I submit this registration. Year 1 of Imperator Caesa[r] Marcus Aur[el]ius Antoninus Augustus and Imperator Caesar Lucius Aurelius Verus Augustus, Mesor[e] 4.
