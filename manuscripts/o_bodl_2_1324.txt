[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured from the threshing-floor into a boat through grain-collectors
2     of the metropolis of the produce, 21st year of Hadrian
3     of the lord, Pachon 29, on behalf of Charax
4     in the name of Senphthoumonthos Psansnutos
5     through the farmer Hartophnahthos of Panamese
6     of wheat in artabae five two-thirds, [it] totals of wheat in artabae 5 two-thirds, and
7     on behalf of Charax, in the name of Heriophmoitos Petechonsios, through
8     Senphthoumonthos Psansnutos, which through
9     [the] farmer Sekes Hartophnahthos, of wheat in artabae three
10    two-thirds eighth, [it] totals of wheat in artabae 3 two-thirds eighth, [it] totals of wheat in artabae 9 two-thirds eighth. P.A.S. Kal, I have marked.
