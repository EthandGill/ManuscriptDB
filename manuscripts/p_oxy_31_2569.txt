[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Munatius, ex-agoranomos and ex-exegete, gymnasiarch, councillor, prytanis in office of the
2     city of the Oxyrhynchites,
3     from Aurelius Didymos son of Didymos, ex-councillor, lessee of the raising of public hot waters for the baths, for nine months
4     of the past 12th year. Scarcely, and
5     beyond my power, on account of the particular circumstance especially, did I in the past year also accomplish the matters of the service; I made it my chief concern that even
6     during the months in question I did not
7     fall short, though urged, nor indeed
8     fail in the supply for this month, although this lies outside the lease.
9     I think that you too perceive the present slowness of the rising (of the Nile) up to this point, on account of which at present the fodder of the animals seems to run short, so that you ought to
10    those put in charge by the exegetai of the water-drawings for the coming month of Phaophi — this too likewise not being included in the lease — to make it clear to Septimius …n and Agathos Daimon son of Potamon, so that of the water-drawings
11    of the same following month they may take over the service, since I, on account of the aforesaid cause, acknowledge that I have refused. Year 13 of the Emperor
12    Caesar Publius Licinius
13    Gallienus Germanicus Maximus
14    Pius Felix Augustus.
15    Thoth 27. I, Aurelius Didymos, councillor, have submitted (this).
