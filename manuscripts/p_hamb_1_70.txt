[TRANSLATION]
1     [Titus] Flavius Capito to Titus
2     [Flavius] Sarapion, fellow-guardian
3     … of Longinus Valens, minor, having become [heir] by will
4     of Flavia Ptolema, deceased
5     [in the … month] of the eighth year of Antoninus
6     [Caesar] the lord, (the deceased) being the grandmother of the aforewritten minor, greeting.
7     Since Flavia Ptolema left to me, according to the will (made) before you,
8     three arouras of katoikic allotment, which
9     also, after her death, I cultivate for fruit, and the proceeds from them
10    I carry off for my own (use), I paying
11    all the public dues concerning them
12    in the name of Flavia Ptolema,
13    I acknowledge that, if any inquiry arises
14    concerning the proceeds of them
15    or of any other (matter) of them, whenever we render the account
16    to the minor, I myself will
17    carry through the (matters) concerning these at my own expense, and whatever shall be adjudged concerning this,
18    if a trial should occur, I will settle.
19    And this chirograph is
20    my own autograph, written
21    in duplicate, without erasure and superscription,
22    which shall also be valid everywhere and for everyone
23    presenting it, as if deposited in a public (registry). And if any necessity
24    arises, I will report a copy so that it may be made (public) through a public deed without delay.
25    [Chirograph, which] I wrote to Sarapion concerning the plots from (the property of) Ptolema,
26    [of 3 arouras], that is, of the legacy.
27    Letter of Malatos concerning the bulls, to the Heliopolite (nome).
