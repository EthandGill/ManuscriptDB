[TRANSLATION]
1   Valerius Marion and those
2   with him, overseers of the Sacred Gate
3   of Syene, through Pachompsachis
4   the assistant. Paid: Zmenpos son of Pe-
5   torzmethes, also called Dakos, for the trade-tax
6   of the 22nd year of Hadrian Caesar
7   the lord, on account, twelve drachmas (worn)
8   — total 12 dr. Year 22 of Hadrian
9   Caesar the lord,
10   Mesore 14.
11   (2nd hand) In the 2nd year of Antoninus Caesar
12   the lord, the same paid for the trade-tax
13   of the 1st year, 8 dr. 2 ob. (worn); the same, for poll-tax
14   of the 20th year, 3 dr. 3 ob. (worn).
