[TRANSLATION]
1     [Try]phon and another …
2     In the sixteenth year, in the month Xandikos = Mecheir, on the eighth, in Herakleopolis …
3     I swear by King Ptolemy also called Alexander, the Mother-loving god, and by Queen
4     Berenike, his sister, the Brother-loving goddess, and by the Benefactor goddess and the Benefactor god,
5     and by the New Father-loving god and the Mother-loving god and the Eupator god and the Manifest god,
6     and by the Father-loving gods and the Sibling gods and the Savior gods and Sarapis and Isis [and the]
7     other gods all, both male and female.
8     I, Tryphon son of …, a Persian of the … among the … cleruch cavalrymen …
9     to a Persian of the same cleruch cavalrymen, also enrolled …
10    that I will abide by and perform for you and those acting through you [everything according to the contract],
11    which I have made with you together with this written undertaking, [by which I have agreed to consent to the]
12    transfer-of-registration made to you [from my allotment concerning] … [the] eighteen arouras [of which the surveying-out and the neighbors]
13    are shown through the contract, [in return for which you have rendered useful service for the]
14    royal (dues) of the allotment … cut (?) … of …
15    and I will furnish (them) clean [from all royal and private]
16    debts and from the other (debts) [from the earlier times up to the times of the]
17    transfer-of-registration, [and from other cessions for the perpetual]
18    time guaranteeing [with every guarantee] …
19    … petition.
