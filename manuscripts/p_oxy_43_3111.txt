[TRANSLATION]
1     In the splendid city of Antinoopolis of the new Hellenes. Aurelius Dionysios son of Diadelphos, his mother being
2     Thaseous, from the city of Oxyrhynchos, captain of a punt-boat belonging to Dionysammon of Alexandria, flax-importer, and however he is styled, leased it out to the Aurelii Dioskourides also called Apollonios and Iustus son of Apollonios, both archpriests and councillors, and to the supervisors of wine with you of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, the annona being furnished
3     to the most noble soldiers together with Ulpius Pasion, the most eminent corrector of Egypt, of the legion
4     II Traiana Fortis Valeriana and Gallieniana, and to the others with them — (he leased out) the said punt-boat for the loading of eight hundred jars of wine furnished to the same soldiers from the harbour of the
5     Oxyrhynchite nome as far as the harbour of Kleopatra of the great Hermopolite nome, in accordance with the orders given by
6     the most eminent Ulpius Pasion, at the freight agreed … at … (the rate) of 80 silver drachmas per 100 jars … six hundred and forty, which have here been counted out … the loading fully equipped with every fitting … not sailing by night nor in stormy weather but mooring … …
7     receiving … per hundred jars of wine, a ja[r] … and he shall deliver the wine safe and undamaged, [and he shall receive]
8     for a libation of wine one jar. The freight-contract is valid, and on being questioned he acknowledged. Year 4 of the Emperors [Caesars Publius]
9     Licinnius Valerianus and Publius Licinnius Valerianus Gallienus, Germanici Maximi, Pii, Felices, [and of Publius]
10    Licinnius Cornelius Valerianus, the most illustrious Caesar, the Augusti, Pachon 20. I, Aurelius Dioskourides also called Apollonios, have leased and paid out the freight in full as stated above. I, Aurelius Iustus, the aforesaid …
11    have joined in the lease as stated above.
