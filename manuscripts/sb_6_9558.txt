[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Asc[lepiadés, logistés of the most-illustrious Hermoupolis]
2     I testify … liturgy
3     that I, not having fled the trust [entrusted to me] of the logiste-ship by the masters of the
4     oikoumene, Constantine [the etern]al king and Crispus and Constantine and
5     Constantius, the most-manifest and most-renowned Caesars, I withdrew
6     from the city, but being cautious of the danger [which] unjustly previously I suffered, from which
7     some have brought against me slanders and conspiracies, and now, summoned to
8     Antinooupolis through Sarapion, the official, who cast greater suspicion on me, saying [I] had been ordered to seize me [and me] not be permitted to arrange anything in hand. Wherefore, being cautious from [what] previously suddenly I suffered, I fled, to wherever
9     I am able to secure the danger concerning [my] soul. A successor moreover I appoint
10    of the liturgy of the logiste-ship, if [the authorities] are willing, with my own contribution
11    Nemesion [son] of Silvanos from the prohedri, to whom I entrust [to transact] all things on behalf of the benefit of the city, until [it] seems [appropriate] to the higher [authorities] about our
12    moderation. Flavius Asclepiadés, I testify publicly, Choiak 15.
