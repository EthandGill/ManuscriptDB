[TRANSLATION]
1     … overseership, I suffered many losses;
2     [for from the former (office), without] having had a rest, I was appointed to [another duty], the collectorship of the river-guards in Alexandria, … having a resource of [ … ] hundred drachmae for it,
3     [into which therefore having been appointed I suffered loss] to the point that I even mortgaged
4     [the house, so that] I might complete the liturgy, because [I had nothing else; but now], being in danger from creditors
5     [of abandoning the mortgaged property], I take refuge with you, the
6     [saviour of all], denouncing Petosouchos as being
7     [responsible, in that, being] compelled by the village-scribes, [I suffered loss for] the following ten years and beyond, having served as scribe [for the] elders who served under Paapis;
8     … [wherefore I ask that, if] it seems good to your fortune, you order to be written
9     [to the strategos of the nome] to hear me against him, and if he finds [these things being] so, to send him to you to give
10    [account of the things ventured]. And to keep me free from harm, [so that I may be able], remaining in my own (place) with wife and children, [to farm the] royal land [which] I am assigned, or to pay [I shall keep up] the public dues [incumbent on me] and to perform liturgies according to my ability, [so that I may be] benefited. Fare well. I will write to the strategos.
