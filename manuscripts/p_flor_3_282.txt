[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Vitalianus the most glorious,
2     Thoth 3, of the 14th indiction.
3     To the most honoured Apollos, son
4     of Dioskoros Psimanobet, and …
5     [Bi]ktor (?), from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, from Aurelius Sansneous
6     son of Psais Ortabane, from the same village, greeting.
7     I have leased from you, for the whole
8     period which you choose, for my own kleros and
9     … of the land hereinafter indicated,
10    from the crops, God willing, of the coming fifteenth indiction, and (the lease) of itself, the existing share
11    of the kleros of waterless land, south
12    of my arouras, north of (the land) from [the]
13    village of Pakeskis, of the [kl]eros of Pherko, on condition that I
14    cultivate these and perform all the
15    labour and receive (it) with my own
16    animals and farmer, God willing; to give
17    the seed-corn from the (common) fund (?)
18    as it comes. And at all times let not
19    the whole kleros be flooded but, the part having been flooded,
20    that I work this myself in proportion to
21    my arouras and water (it) from the cistern
22    of my own holding; and the proportion of the
23    irrigation-machine and [of the] expenses (to be) from the
24    (common) fund; and [of the] remaining produce of every kind
25    [and chaff], (it) shall be divided between us
26    [by hal]ves: [to m]e one share for the
27    la[bours], [and to you] one share for the rents.
28    The le[ase is valid] and on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
29    In [propo]rtio[n to my] ar[ou]ras, at rents
30    a[s] I shall be able …
31    … three me… …
32    half …
