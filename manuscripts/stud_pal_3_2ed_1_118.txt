[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Basileus, with god presiding over the handwriting, submission of the third indiction, to the lord
2     Magister, with god as helper of the accounts office and [of] the division of Dioscorus of the same
3     third indiction. I received and was paid by your esteemed [person] for the customary [payment]
4     of the handwriting of the same third in[diction], that is gold nomisma one and
5     carats twenty-two quarter only, gold nomisma 1 carats 22 [1/4 only thus]; for the helper of the accounts office
6     gold nomisma one and carats twelve, for [the] division of Dioscorus gold carats
7     ten quarter, making together gold nomisma 1 carats 22 1/4, and for your security I have made the present
8     receipt as aforesaid. Mark of Basileus. Flavius Basileus the aforesaid, agrees with me [the] rece[ipt] as aforesaid.
9     Ischarion son of Ioannes, I wrote in his stead letters not knowing, but he put in his own hand the
10    three holy crosses.
