[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord Eumathius,
2     Diogenes.
3     Though I send many letters, only with much difficulty do I receive the letters sent from you,
4     which you dispatch rarely.
5     I have now sent, through the outstanding assistant, to Melas the
6     speculator, another three letters. So that you may receive these,
7     I write. Send me the water-jars and pots and coolers
8     by the boat that carries the monthly provisions. About Philadelphus
9     you wrote me nothing. How does he fare? Have Salious, the man
10    from Toka, give you the cloak of Athanasius the assistant, so that
11    you may send it to us here. And let a jar of cheeses
12    be sent to us. Send olives and a little
13    peeled lentil and a little peeled grain,
14    as much as was sent last year too. Send me the
15    cords of the bed. I have written to you often that you should make clear to me
16    about Theodore, whether he has ceased suffering with his tooth. Whether you received the fodder
17    of Adeas, that of Cornelius, make clear to me, and about the cistern and about
18    the summer works at the water-machines and whether the taxes have been paid in full.
19    How does Dionysia fare? I pray for your health for many
20    years,
21    my lord.
22    To my lord in Oxyrhynchus, Eumathius, from Diogenes, scholasticus.
