1     Year 22, Choiak 9. Dionysios also called Pet[osiris], son of Theon also called Thonis,
2     Persian of the epigone, and his wife Athenais also called Athermouthis, daughter of Apollonios also called
3     Pres…retis, Persian woman, with her guardian Dionysios her husband, the aforewritten, (sold) 3 artabas of wheat, the price of each artaba
4     being 2000 (drachmas) of copper …
5     … Sureties … [keeper of the contract] Dionysios.
6     In the reign of Ptolemy surnamed Alexander,
7     the god Philometor, the twenty-second year, when the priest
8     of Alexander and of the others who are written in Alexandria
9     was (as recorded), in the month Peritios on the ninth, Choiak on the ninth, in Kerkeosiris in the
10    division of Polemon of the Arsinoite [nome], there sold
11    Dionysios also called Petosiris, son of Theon also called Thonis, Persian of the epigone,
12    and his wife Athenais also called Athermouthis, daughter of Apollonios also called Pres…retis, Persian woman, with her guardian Dionysios her husband and the aforewritten,
13    to Petesouchos son of Marreis, an Arsinoite, three artabas of wheat, at a price for
14    each artaba of two thousand drachmas of copper, the whole price
15    of the three artabas of wheat being one talent
16    of copper coinage, which the aforewritten persons have received from Petesouchos forthwith
17    from hand to hand out of the house. And let Dionysios and Athenais deliver to Petesouchos
18    or to his agents in the month Pauni of the twenty-second year
19    new, pure, unadulterated wheat, free from everything, [by the] six-choinix measure of the
20    dromos which is in the aforewritten village of Souchieion, by just measurement,
21    having delivered it into the same (granary?) to Petesouchos at their own
22    expense. And if they do not deliver as has been written, let the aforewritten persons pay [the price that shall obtain in the market],
23    increased by half. Sureties for one another …
24    the aforewritten persons themselves. And [let the right of execution belong] to Petesouchos
25    … [and] against any one
26    [of them] … [and against whichever] he may choose and
27    [out of all their property, as] in accordance with a legal judgment.
