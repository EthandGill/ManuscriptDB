[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the … …
2     Epeiph 26 of the second indiction, in Arsinoe.
3     To Flavius Theodorakios, the most glorious military commander (stratelates)
4     and pagarch of this city of the Arsinoites,
5     Aurelius Kosmas, groom (stablites)
6     of the relay-post of the hamlet of Pantikos, son of Pousi,
7     from the same city, greeting. I acknowledge
8     that I have received and been paid in full by you,
9     through Zacharias and Ioannakios the most illustrious
10    your chartularies, the fodder
11    of the animals under my charge, seven horses
12    and two donkeys, of the same
13    relay-post, for two months, Mesore and Thoth,
14    of the present second indiction, in full,
15    which is: of barley, forty
16    two artabas — of barley, 42 artabas — and for your
17    security I myself have made this
18    receipt, being valid, and having been formally asked, I acknowledged it.
19    I, Kosmas the groom, am satisfied as aforesaid.
20    Ioannakios, son of the marikites Menas, I bear witness
21    to this receipt as aforesaid. John
22    the tabularius, I bear witness to this
23    receipt as aforesaid. Philoxenos, son of the man of
24    blessed memory Theodorakios,
25    I bear witness to this receipt as aforesaid.
26    … y(?)
