1     I [am] Frange being present
2     Greeting my beloved brothers David and our sister, I also greet my child Moses of whose little children you know that I found a monastery of monks
3     God knows that you give on behalf of my heart …
4     John of the monastery
5     And the sister who gives on behalf
6     Now I shall go to rest
7     [May we] see each other
