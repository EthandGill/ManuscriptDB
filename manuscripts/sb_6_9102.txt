[TRANSLATION]
1     Your eminence has remembered
2     how even while she sojourned through
3     this royal city the servants
4     of Apollo the most blessed ones
5     from the village of Aphrodite
6     setting out through the same
7     having become a blessed city
8     have come to us through the crowd saying
9     [that] Theodosius the most magnificent
10    [regarding] those [concerned with] the eleventh apportionment
11    [has paid] public taxes in full
12    [that] nothing concerning them [should be] deposited to the public treasury
13    but [that] the village-governor
14    [should] collect from them the pious
15    offerings, and [that] it [is] not right
16    to pay twice concerning the same
17    periods. Therefore we urge
18    your eminence, this on one hand
19    by way of justice and the law,
20    and on the other hand by way of the regard
21    for the aforementioned most magnificent
22    man, [that you] fulfill his own promises
23    and prepare
24    that sufficient [means should] come to be for them.
25    For necessity compels [that] Theodosius the most magnificent [...]
26    making appeals concerning them
27    to avert from the collection impending
28    upon them or to place
29    upon these [people] whatever
30    he happened to have received from them.
31    If indeed these matters should not also come
32    to the divine hearing from them,
33    [then] let your eminence take pains
34    to settle these [matters]. And this very thing
35    we have also entrusted to Thomas the most illustrious chartulary
36    of the care contemplated from us,
37    [a] most divine household [official],
38    to accomplish [it].
