[TRANSLATION]
1     From Heraklides. Go to Ataiin
2     the shepherd and receive from him drachmas two hundred, and hold
3     them [for] both dike-repair
4     or also [for] account of rations. As/however
5     you wish, administer them until
6     Diodoros comes down and [settles] such [accounts]. I pray [you] to be well.
7     To Heroninos, [the] father
8     Year 2, Thoth 18
