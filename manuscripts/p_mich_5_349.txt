[TRANSLATION]
1     Year twenty-[...]
2     from a glued [sheet]
3     Harpaesis son of Panorshous, a Persian of the descendants, I acknowledge under compulsion
4     to plant in the mounds around three-hundred arouras in a wood from the
5     present month of Augustus of the seventeenth year of Tiberius Caesar
6     Augustus, because I have been written concerning these [matters], and I have received immediately
7     from the fixed pay to be given of silver drachmas one hundred forty-four as a credit I have obtained immediately by hand from Phileros the slave sixty drachmas of silver; but the remaining silver drachmas eighty-four I shall receive month by month
8     as required from the month Mechir, and I shall deliver as newly-planted mulberries
9     one hundred fifty in the aforementioned mounds which I shall also water in the month
10    five times. If then I do not deliver as is prescribed, under compulsion [I shall pay] the aforementioned silver drachmas one hundred forty-four and the
11    interest as is prescribed. [There] wrote for him Marepshemis [son of] Marepshemios
12    [a scribe] because he does not know letters.
