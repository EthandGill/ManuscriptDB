[TRANSLATION]
1     Hesties, son of Pomsais, of Alexandria: 56 drachmas
2     Hatres, his brother: 56 drachmas
3     Apollonios, son of Mandrobos: 56 drachmas
4     Phaesis, son of Apollonios: 104
5     Petesouchos, son of Araus: 56 drachmas
6     Nekpheros, his brother: 56 drachmas
7     Petsiris, son of Herakleios: 56
8     Netnephis, son of Herakleios: 56
9     Papontos, son of Heliodoros: 56 drachmas
10    Phasis, son of Haryotes: 56 drachmas
11    Dionysios, son of Kephalon: 56 drachmas
12    Anchorimphis, son of Petesouchos: 56 drachmas
13    Sontois, son of Horion: 56 drachmas
14    Maron, son of Herakleios: 56 drachmas
15    Ptolion, son of Panaus: 20 drachmas
16    Herakles, son of Peouris: 28 drachmas
17    Dikranes, son of Dikranes: 20 drachmas
18    900 drachmas
19    From Nemesion, son of Zoilos,
20    collector of money-taxes from Philadelphia in the Heraklei[des]
21    division of the Arsinoite nome.
22    Horion, son of Petosiris, who was brought in along with me
23    in the tenth year of Tiberius Claudius
24    Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator
25    for the collection of the public dues,
26    and having served as collector for four assessments, showed contempt, no longer holding to the collection,
27    although he is neither an exempt
28    cultivator nor on the white-list (leukoma).
29    Therefore I ask you, the savior
30    of all, to write
31    to Cattius, the centurion in charge of the locality,
32    to compel
33    Horion to hold fast to
34    the collectorship of the
35    public dues, so that the public account suffer
36    no loss,
37    that we may be benefited.
