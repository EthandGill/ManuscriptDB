[TRANSLATION]
1     92
2     to my brother Theodosios from Alexander: as I said to you, give the reckoning
3     to Psa Phoibammon Patehho: behold, the man Psa has not gone up: give to the scribe:
4     take him to the lord Phoibammon and he will give to him twelve stathma
5     and you take a receipt from Phoibammon and he will send the remainder to Genades: but you [keep] the receipt of the nineteen stathma from Phoibammon.
