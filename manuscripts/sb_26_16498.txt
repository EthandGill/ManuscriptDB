[TRANSLATION]
1     Aibutios Niger and Pachnum[is Palamchis]
2     tax collectors of the silver tax of Elephantine; they recorded Harpaes[is]
3     of Cornelius, of mother Senarps and Pachom[p]retis son on behalf of portions of [the] 15th year
4     and 1st year each one drachma
5     two and a half obols, [which] amount to 2 drachmas five-obol; of year 1st
6     of Gaius Auidius Cassius
7     Augustus, Payni 25.
