[TRANSLATION]
1   Zenon son of Herakleides, lessee of the Sacred Gate
2   of Syene, through Sarapion the assistant.
3   Paid: Patibptis son of Petorzmethes,
4   mother Senpelaias, for the poll-tax of the
5   8th year of Antoninus Caesar
6   the lord: seventeen drachmas (and) a half-obol (worn),
7   (and) ½ obol (keepers') — total 17 dr. 1 ob. The same, the assessment
8   of the men who had withdrawn, 7th year: 1 dr. 4 ob.
9   Year 9 of Antoninus
10   the lord, Thoth 9.
