[TRANSLATION]
1     There has paid, Chempneus (son of) Inaros,
2     for the account of the month Thoth of year 18,
3     for ...( ) and the canal, (in the name of) Petechonsis,
4     of silver, impure, twenty-nine drachmas,
5     total 29 dr. Year 18 of Antoninus
6     Caesar the lord, Phaophi 2. Karo( ), I have signed.
