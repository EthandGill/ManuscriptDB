[TRANSLATION]
1     Philadelphos to Philippos
2     to [my] dearest, greetings.
3     to those delivering these to you
4     my letters, [as a] recommendation
5     to Herona, so that
6     he be persuaded; having made him willing, with them you may be
7     until they measure out the land.
8     I wish you to be well, dearest.
