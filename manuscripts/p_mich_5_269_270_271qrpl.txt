r.2    Ὀρσεῦς Ἁρμιύσιος μητρὸς Ταυσίριος συνπέπρακα τὸ ἐπιβάλλον μοι μέρος ἀπὸ ἡμίσους μέρους πατρικῆς οἰκίας παλαιᾶς συμπεπτωκυίας καὶ αὐλῆς καὶ τῶν συνκυρόντων πά[ν]των κοινῶν καὶ [ἀ]διαιρέτων πρὸς αὐτὸν τὸν πριάμενον Ψονσνεῦν
r.3    καὶ ἑτέρους ἐν Τεβτύνει, καὶ συναπέχω τὴν τιμὴν πᾶσαν ἐκ πλήρου παραχρῆμα διὰ χειρὸς ἐξ οἴκου καὶ συμβεβαιώσω τὴν πρᾶσιν πάσῃ βεβαιώσει καθὼς πρόκειται. καὶ εὐδοκεῖ ἡ γυνή μου Ταμιεῦς Πατύνις τῇ πράσει ταύτῃ καὶ

r.5    τῆς γυναικός μου μὴ εἰδυίας γράμματα.

r.7    ἀπὸ ἡμίσους μέρους πατρικῆς οἰκίας παλαιᾶς

r.11   διὰ τὸ μὴ εἰδέναι αὐτοὺς γράμ[μ]ατα. Πακῆβκις καὶ Παπνεβτῦνις οἱ δύο Ἁρμιύσιος μητρὸς Ταυσίριος συνπεπράκαμεν τὰ

[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 2nd [year] of Claudius, in the month of Kaisareios, on the 5th of the intercalary days.
2     I, Orseus, son of Harmiysis, my mother being Tausiris, have joined in selling the share falling to me from the half part of an old paternal house that has collapsed, and of the courtyard and of all the appurtenances, held in common and undivided, to the buyer himself, Psonsneus
3     and others, in Tebtynis, and I jointly receive the whole price in full forthwith hand to hand out of the house, and I will jointly guarantee the sale with every guarantee as aforesaid. And my wife Tamieus, daughter of Patynis, consents to this sale and
4     (consents) not to come against (it) for all time, nor to bring any claim, nor (shall) another on her behalf, as aforesaid. I, Orseus, have written also on behalf of
5     my wife, who does not know letters.
6     I, Herodes, son of Herodes, my mother being Taarmiysis, have joined in selling the share falling to me
7     from the half part of an old paternal house that has collapsed
8     and of the courtyard and of all the appurtenances held in common
9     and undivided, to the buyer Psonneus and others, in Tebtynis, formerly called (the house) of Opeus, and I jointly receive the whole price in full forthwith hand to hand out of the house, and I will jointly guarantee the sale with every
10    guarantee. And my wife Tamares, daughter of Aunes, consents to this sale and (consents) not to come against (it) for all time, nor to bring any claim, nor (shall) another on her behalf, as aforesaid. There wrote on their behalf Melantas, son of Kronion,
11    because they do not know letters. We, Pakebkis and Papnebtynis, the two sons of Harmiysis, our mother being Tausiris, have joined in selling the
12    shares falling to us from the half part of an old paternal house that has collapsed and of the courtyard and of all the appurtenances
13    held in common and undivided, to you the buyer and others, in Tebtynis, formerly called (the house) of Opeus; of which the neighbors of the whole
14    house and courtyard are: on the south, formerly the house of Orseus, son of Labaeis, but now of Herakleios, son of Orsenouphis, and on part the house and courtyard of the sons
15    of Hatreponsis; on the north, formerly the house of Orseus, son of Prious, and others, but now of Eutyches, son of Eutyches, and on
16    part the lots of Haratytis and others, but now of Eutyches the elder, son of Eutyches; on the west and east the royal streets. And we jointly receive from Psonsneus the whole agreed pri[ce] in full forthwith hand to hand out of the house,
17    and we will jointly guarantee the sale with every guarantee, against both public and private (claims) and every encumbrance, for the
18    whole time. And the wife of Papnebtynis, [Thenmi]eus, daughter of Mieus, consents to this sa[le] and (consents) not to come against (it)
