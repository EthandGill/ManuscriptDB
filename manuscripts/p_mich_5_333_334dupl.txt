[TRANSLATION]
1     Registered on Mecheir 24: a loan by Horion to Herakleides and his mother of 760 silver drachmas.
2     In the twelfth year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
3     Imperator, on the 24th of the month Xandikos, Mecheir 24, in Tebtynis of the
4     Polemon division of the Arsinoite nome. Horion
5     son of Kronion, about 30 years old, a scar on the middle of the forehead, lent to Herakleides
6     son of Ischyrion also called Nestnephis, a Persian of the epigone, about 25 years old,
7     a scar on the first finger of the left hand, and to his mother Thaubarion
8     daughter of Petemounis, a Persian woman, about 50 years old, a scar on the right thumb, with her guardian Herakleides, the two being mutual sureties for payment,
9     of silver stamped coinage as principal seven hundred and sixty drachmas, which they also received from him
10    forthwith from hand to hand from the house, at interest of one drachma
11    per mina each month; and this loan, the
12    seven hundred and sixty drachmas of silver, and the
13    interest, let the borrowers repay to Horion in the month
14    Tybi of the coming thirteenth year of Tiberius Claudius
15    Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator; and if they do not repay
16    according to what has been written, let the borrowers pay to Horion forthwith the loan with one-half (penalty) added, and the interest unaugmented,
17    the right of execution belonging to Horion or to those acting through him both upon the
18    borrowers and upon all their belongings, as if by legal judgment,
19    Horion being in no way prejudiced concerning what
20    the debtors owe him, according to the agreement of mortgage of what belongs to Herakleides,
21    of a catoecic holding around Theogonis of 3 1/2 1/4 arouras, against 1,000 drachmas and the interest; and it shall remain valid, which the borrowers shall not redeem sooner unless they first repay the aforesaid principal and the
22    [interest]. The subscriber, Sarapion son of Ptolemaios, about 40 years old, a scar on the neck on the left side.
