[TRANSLATION]
1     To Machatas, epistates of Tanis, from Onn[ophris]
2     embalmer, of those from the same villa[ge. When] certain kittens had been born in m[y dwell-]
3     ing and my mother not com[ing] to them, having gone to th[e] Boubasteion I asked the sompheis to come and carry (them) over to the Boubasteion. But they not com-
4     ing, but putting it off, it happened that, of those being reared by me with milk in (the) hou[se]
5     one was snatched away by a male cat and was carried off from the dwelling into the street.
6     And when I had run out after (it) and was shouting to those
7     present and hearing to come to the rescue, thus, gathering round, we with difficulty took away one kitten,
8     in the presence of those who helped, and of Phasis the
9     village-secretary, to whom also I made it known, calling (him) to witness, and (related) the things that happened. But not having
10    what I should do, because you too were learned to be away from home, and that I cannot be seen in th[is] village because of the practice (profession) concerning me, on the 22nd of the present mon[th] …
11    au … utath … ma … a …
12    and having brought (it) before the door of the Boubasteion I made a gift (of it), requesting the somphe[i]s to carry (it) in,
13    not being able to enter. In order, therefore, that I may not af[t]erward be slandered, certain men having acted maliciously, contrary to what is right, I ask you, begging,
14    having subscribed each of these things …
15    ka … up … l …
16    … a … as … s … eou … . And this
17    having been done, I shall obtain the assistance from you.
18    Year 3, Pharmouthi 22.
