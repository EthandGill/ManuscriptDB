[TRANSLATION]
1     We received, we Apa Julius son of Kyriakos from the village of Syron of the Arsinoite nome and Menas son of Kosmas from the Arsinoi[tan] city from
2     the district of the Tax-collectors, namely the Lower [district], both of us donkey-drivers, from you George, the most pious chief-deacon of the [holy great]
3     church, son of [the one] of glorious memory Petteries, formerly village head of the same city, for a portion of our [wage owed]
4     to be given to us from you for which [we] perform the donkey-driver service of your [beasts] of the present fifteenth indiction in gold coins
5     two gold coins, two in number, that is, each of us one gold coin, one gold coin in number, written in the month
6     Pharmouthi 9, fifteenth indiction, Aristomachos deacon, [I] witness to this
7     wage receipt as set forth, George deacon, [I] witness to this wage receipt as set forth, through me Paul with God notary wrote.
