[TRANSLATION]
1     Chairemon to Apollonios his brother,
2     very many greetings and (wishes for) good health.
3     You will do well, on meeting Pe[t]aus the
4     deacon, to set the calf before
5     the sheep, or Saseis son of Petesouchos
6     son of Paseis; and if they say "bring
7     it," send Hatres having it
8     to the field, and let Hatres measure out
9     the sack of wheat and carry it to Hephaistias and let Pasion exchange it for you for good seed. Do not therefore do otherwise. And meet Panesneus the lessee concerning
10    cattle, on my account, until I come down
11    and give him the money; and guard yourself, since you are still a soldier
12    with the grain-collector of the …
13    on account of the freight-charges. If … have become … to a sacred … …
14    to Chairemon by nig[ht]. Do not therefore
15    do otherwise. And if you wish, meet also Saras son of … Papontos concerning
16    the calf, since he too has told me
17    to bring it to the field. And you will say
18    that they (are) to guard them at home, let them not leave
19    the door, when it was unlocked, you will tell Hieranouphis
20    Kalasaris and Orsenouphis son of Pitheus to come up quickly. Farewell.
21    Year 8 of the Emperor Caesar Vespasianus Augustus,
22    Choiak 8.
23    Deliver to Apollonios (my) brother.
24    Ptoll[a]s to Chairemon …
25    Light-bringer, light-bearing, of friends light, light, bear, O torch!
26    From …
