[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord Papollonios [from] Chrestos
2     [wine] distributor, 1st indiction, greetings. Give to the brother Haruotes, wine, 200 sextarii
3     [total] wine, 200 sextarii. I have marked, I myself, through me
4     Olbios, his son.
