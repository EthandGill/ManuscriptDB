1   In the eleventh year of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Ph[a]rm[ou]thi 24, in the city of the Oxyrhynchi in the Thebaid, with good fortune, in the priesthood of [Ju]lia A[ugu]sta.
2   Sarapion son of Sarapion son of Sarapion son of Sarapion, his mother being Tha[ï]s daughter of Sarapion,
3   of the city of the [O]xyrhynchi, has given his own daughter Thaïs, her mother being […], in marriage to Sarap[i]on son of [Eud]aimon son of Theon, [his mother bei]ng Heras daughter of […, her mo]ther being D[ido]us; [and the bridegroom acknowledges receipt from Sarapion the father]
4   [an]d giver: a pair of … of three minaiai and fourteen and a half quarters, a brooch of eight quarters, a clasp of six quarters, a little chain with green … stones — the gold weighing […] and a half [quart]ers — so that in all there is, by the Oxyrhynchite stan[dard, gold of five minaiai and … quarters,]
5   [an]d two sets of clothing, two girdles, one flame-colored, one rose-colored, a … mantle — all at a joint valuation of five hundred and sixty silver drachmas — and one thousand eight hundred and sixty silver drachmas, so that the whole dowry together is [four thousand one hundred drachmas of] Imperial [silver] coin; [and the]
6   grandmother [of the] bride, Thaïs daughter of Sarapion, her mother being Heraklou[s], of the same c[ity], with as guardian her own
7   other son — full brother of the giver — Sarapion son of [Sa]rapi[o]n, acknowledges, in
8   the same stree[t], that she has given the Thaï[s] in marriage, and gives to the [sa]me Thaïs [… the slave Kal-]
9   [lit]yche and the offspring to be born of her; <but> her service and earnings
10   the bridegroom shall hold together … with the bride so long as they live with one another, it not being lawful for the [bridegr]oo[m]
11   to […] the slav[e] with[out the …] nor anything brought in [… Also a house]
12   [and] light-well a[nd] court and its fixtures, and the slave bodies Sarapous [and]
13   Nikarous and the offspring of N[ika]rous — Sarapous and Kerdon and [Epich]armos — and
14   what offspring shall be born of them or of others, and whatever she may acquire [besides these] or …[… — these he may not sell nor pledge nor otherwise dis-]
15   [po]se of without the consent of the bride. So let the married pair live together with one another blamelessly,
16   and let the bridegroom furnish the bride with necessaries according to his means; and if
17   they differ with one another a[nd] the bride [wishes to separate from
18   the bridegroom …, then,]
19   [when] the separation has taken place, let the bride take away the s[l]ave Kallityche
20   and the offspring born of her, and let the bridegroom repay — to the giver if he survives, otherwise to the bride — the four thou[sand] one hundred drachmas of the dowry within [… days from demand, or let him pay it with half again;]
21   […] each … and … be done. And if the bride is pregnant when she separates, the bridegroom shall give her another sixty drachmas toward
22   the expenses of her confinement. While they live together, may there be health; but if it happen that one of the married pair [die …, let the bride-]
23   [groom] have power over his own property, to bestow whatever he chooses and to share out to whom he
24   will; and if he bestow nothing, these things too shall after his death belong to the children born of them
25   both. And if [the] bridegroom have died first, let the bride have […]
26   […] let the bride be (guardian) as to the ha[lf], or the next-of-kin, and the man to be appointed by the bridegroo[m]
27   as to the other half — both guardians — the children being maintained with their mother until they come of age. And if the bridegroom appoint no one [as guardian] of the half [guardianship, let the bride alone, or]
28   the [n]ext-of-kin, (be guardian), it being lawful for no one to ca[st] her out of the guardianship or any part of it. And if the bride
29   die first, there being no children of them both, or those born having passed away
30   childless, let the bridegroom give back what is [in the dowry …: the four thou-]
31   sand one hundred [silver drachmas] within six[t]y days, and let all her other property be remitted to the same kin of the bride. And if likewise
32   the bridegroom die first, there being no children of them both, or those born having
33   after[wards passed away childless,]
34   (the bride shall recover it,) taking away the slave Kallityche and the offspring to be born of her — and until she recover it,
35   let her be mistress of everything; and in all the stipulations the choice rests with the bride, if
36   she choose, to have the gold objects named in the dowry [at the same weight, or their equal valuation,]
37   the right of execution belonging to the bride and hers both upon the bridegroom and upon all his
38   property, according as they agreed with one another. Identifier of both parties: (2nd hand) D[i]ogenes
39   son of Hierax, scri[be, o]f the same city, in [the same street].
