[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermias son of Hermias, Persian of the
2     offspring, to Leon [Orsenouphis], greetings. I acknowledge having [from] you the three metretes
3     of wine, with a half-surcharge, which
4     and [I will repay] you [in] the month of Epiphi
5     of the third year from the
6     my property, new wine acceptable in the measure by which I perform the drawing. But if I do not repay as has been written,
7     I will pay you for each jar
8     of bronze drachmas
9     three thousand. And this hand shall be valid everywhere produced.
10    Year 3, Pachon 9
