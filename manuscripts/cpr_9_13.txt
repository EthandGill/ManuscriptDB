1     To Theodore, officer. Provide for Theodore
2     [for the] grain-measurer [of] wheaten [grain], twelve charagmae
3     [it] totals: twelve charagmae only. It was written Phauni 16, indiction 2, [to one] departing
4     to the district of Magdals [of the] herdsmen, on condition of receiving
5     the measure of the grain of the district
6     [it] totals: twelve charagmae. Demetriu[s] signed
