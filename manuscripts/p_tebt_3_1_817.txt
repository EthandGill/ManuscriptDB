[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Arsinoe, the Father-loving Gods,
2     year twenty-fourth, the priest being he who is
3     in Alexandria of Alexander and of the Sibling Gods and of the Benefactor Gods and of the Father-loving Gods and of the Manifest Gods, the athlophoros
4     of Berenike Euergetis being she who is in Alexandria, the kanephoros
5     of Arsinoe Philadelphos being she who is in Alexandria, the priestess of Arsinoe
6     Philopator being she who is in Alexandria, the month being Dystros, on the twenty-eighth,
7     Thoth twenty-eighth, in Krokodilon polis of the Arsinoite nome. Apollonios son of Protogenes, a Jew of the epigone, lent to Sostratos son of Neoptolemos, a Jew of the epigone, of copper
8     coinage two talents and three thousand drachmas, interest-free, for
9     a year from the aforewritten time, on the security of the
10    house and courtyard belonging to him and all appurtenances that
11    are in Apias of the division of Themistos, of which the measurements (are): south to north
12    twenty cubits, west to east twenty cubits, and the neighbors (are):
13    [on the s]outh the house of Sopatra, on the north and east lanes, on the west the house of Harpalos
14    [an]d Sostratos, [ ] to them at the aforewritten time. And this
15    [l]oan let Sostratos repay to Apollonios within the yea[r];
16    [b]ut if he does not repay as has been written, let it be permitted to Apollonios to make additional payment(-seizure) of the security in accordance with the ordinance.
17    And let Sostratos guarantee this security to Apollonios
18    and furnish it free from claim and free from pledge and free from further-loan of another loan and clear of royal (dues). But if he does not
19    guarantee (it) or does not furnish (it) as has been written, or some risk arises concerning this security, whether concerning the whole or a part
20    of it in any manner whatsoever, let Sostratos repay to Apollonios
21    this loan within the year forthwith. But if he does not repay as has been written, let Sostratos pay to Apollonios
22    forthwith the loan increased by half, and of the elapsed
23    time interest at the rate of two drachmas per mina for each month.
24    Let this contract be valid everywhere.
25    Through Boubakos, also called Stheneus.
26    Apollonios, about 35 years old, tall, honey-colored,
27    somewhat gray-eyed, with ears standing apart.
28    Sostratos, about 35 years old, of middle (height), honey-colored, ep…
29    … [with a sca]r above the right eyebrow.
30    … [Pe]tosiris son of Pekysis
31    … and Pebrichis.
32    4.
