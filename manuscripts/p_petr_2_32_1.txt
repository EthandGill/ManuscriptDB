[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 8, Mesore 17.
2     To Dorotheus the epimeletes, from Petosiris, tanner, of those dwelling in Krokodilon polis. While I was working
3     in the royal treasury of hides and while I had a dwelling, just as also
4     the rest, in which I live, and the tax-collector …
5     Philippos having granted (me) leave to work
6     fleeces, which we pluck … the wool to the felt-makers, and the proceeds from them we deliver to the royal treasury,
7     to the tax-collector, and this even earlier, in the
8     times before, has been granted to us also under the other tax-collectors; but now a certain Dionysios, having leapt in among us, who neither collects taxes but drags himself along
9     with Philippos, having burst into the dwelling
10    where I lived, on the 2nd of Epeiph departed carrying off my garments,
11    Egyptian ones, which were worth 800 drachmas, and of bronze
12    560 drachmas, cushions 2, new, of wool, worth 100 drachmas …
13    5 minas of worked (wool) worth 300 drachmas, worn-out tunics 2 of 30 drachmas, of fine warp-thread
14    12 staters of 40 drachmas, a cup, a drinking-vessel, of 10 drachmas, and
15    a contract which I held for grain lent
16    for 12 artabas, which having gone down he has carried off, which was —
17    as the contract specifies, for each artaba [60 drachmas]
18    so as to be 720 drachmas — of household goods a lumber-heap of 100 drachmas and
19    the fleeces which Philippos granted to all
20    of us to work, he has carried off, which were … at 17 each, so that the total of what he has carried off is
21    2600 drachmas. I ask you therefore not to overlook me, wronged in the utmost, but summoning
22    Dionysios, to question him
23    on what account he has carried off the … …
24    … of those who have neglected toward Philippos
25    the tax-collector, and if the things I write are [true],
26    to compel him to give back to me [all]
27    the things laid to his charge. For this having been done,
28    I shall have obtained your acts of benevolence.
29    Farewell.
30    The matters concerning the leather-worker,
31    to summon the man in charge
32    of the leather-yard.
