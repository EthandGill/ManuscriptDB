[TRANSLATION]
1     Concerning the expense [of] amali [workers'] names, three, being for the service of the [built] building [of] the Fossat.
2     In the name of God, Korra ben Szerich, counselor
3     To Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito. We give thanks
4     [to] God and after that. Six [pieces of] currency we ordered through your administration
5     for the price of the undermentioned kinds of expense of amali [workers'] names
6     [of three] months, two being for service of the built building
7     in the Fossat, by [account of] the delivered produce from the lower
8     country under Sufian ben Haiian and Soraig ben Themim, being imposed
9     [on] the present indiction, the ninth, and these orders, having been made,
10    [for the] fields, we sent [to] you. — Receiving then the present letters
11    [according] to the capacity of our orders, the such gold
12    [accomplish] and [send it] to [your] representative. It was written [in the month of] Pauni, the 1st, indiction 9.
13    [K]orra, [son] of Szerich, counselor, to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito.
14    [In the] month of Epiphi, the 30th, [it was] conveyed through Matoi, a soldier, concerning the expense of amali [workers'] names, three, for service of the building of the Fossat
