[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Justinian the eternal
2     Augustus Emperor, year twenty-seventh, after the consulship of Flavius
3     Basilius the most glorious, year twelfth, Phaophi 14, second indiction.
4     To the holy church of the market-men of the city of the Hermopolites through Basilides
5     steward of the same holy church, Aurelius Josephus
6     of Antinoopolis, mother Maria, farmer from the same city. I acknowledge
7     to have received and accepted from the same church through
8     the aforementioned Basilides steward on account of an advance
9     of the [irrigation] of the [estate] [called] the Banu as capital
10    [gold] coins three short carats eighteen by weight
11    [the] same [city] amounts to gold coins [three] short carats eighteen, [which] to [you] I will repay,
12    whenever I withdraw from the [irrigation] of the [same] estate, without interest,
13    apart from [the] procrastination and [dispute] and judgment and
14    [litigation] … of the church …
