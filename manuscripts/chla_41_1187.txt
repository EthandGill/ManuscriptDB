[TRANSLATION]
1     … and them … ordered
2     a guardian to be appointed for the child, the daughter of Claudian[us], as one of guardians …
3     of the m[ale] Timagenes and Helladius, from then [until] today, [when they] brought in
4     the [case].
5     Athenodorus, most perfect man, prefect of Thebes, said to him: Since also a guardian was named for the minor,
6     and furthermore the court is able to be lawfully established within fifteen
7     days, hear the matter. Demetria said: Unless he first brings forward
8     proof of how it came to him, let it have no force.
9     The appointed judge knows how him
10    it is necessary to follow the laws.
