1     In the consulship of our master Flavius [Justinianus]
2     the eternal Augustus [for the 2nd time], Phaophi 27, of the 7th indiction, at Oxy[rhynchus].
3     To the holy catholic church of God which is under
4     our most holy and most God-beloved father
5     abba Petros, bishop of this splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through you the most pious Phib,
6     presbyter and catholic steward, Aurelius
7     Pseeis son of Phoibammon, his mother being Thekla, from
8     the hamlet of Kolotou, an estate of the same catholic church of the Oxyrhynchite nome, a registered farmer, greeting. A need having now also arisen
9     for the ecclesiastical machine under me called (that) of the Agroikika, which draws water for the vineyard and for
10    arable land, of one axle, having come up to the
11    [ci]ty, I petitioned your God-loving-ness so as
12    [to o]rder the same axle to be furnished to me,
13    [and] at once your God-loving-ness, taking thought
14    [for] the maintenance of the church's
15    [affai]rs, furnished it to me through the most pious Loukas, monk, a new [suitable]
16    [water-dra]wing (axle), satisfactory. I received (it) for [the comple]tion of all the mechanical eq[uipment]
