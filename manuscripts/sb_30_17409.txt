[TRANSLATION]
1     the claim of the holy place, father Michael of the Antaiopoles, through me Haraiutos [the] presbyter
2     and steward, to the most wonderful Phibammon of Triadelph: I have received and I have been paid
3     from you [the] rent of the arouras under you of the aforesaid holy place's lease
4     ... [indiction], both in wheat and in barley according to the ability of your lease
5     [in full and for your security I have] made for you this payment receipt
6     [receipt as aforesaid. The claim] of the aforesaid holy place through me Haraiutos
7     [presbyter and steward I agree] with me the receipt as aforesaid. Constantine [son] of Pinutios
8     [deacon of the same place I wit]ness to this document as aforesaid.
