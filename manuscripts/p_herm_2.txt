[TRANSLATION]
1     To my master Sarapion,
2     [A]natolios, greeting.
3     You know how I pray, my lor[d], both to behold you in person and to address you, since you are my patron and a most admirable protector of patrons; often indeed, eager as I am and (busy) in work,
4     unavoidable hindrances held back my arrival to you, on the one hand the
5     illnesses of my daughters; for some god inflicted these
6     upon me by way of malice, may he nonetheless release them; and on the other hand
7     the unavoidable obligation of the worship of the guardian god Hermes;
8     I was therefore on the road, and now having a fine and fitting
9     escort suited to your unfailing character,
10    since my lord brother The[oph]anes the wise was about to travel abroad,
11    in whom I trust … much … everywhere … [ma]ster … el … r … ti
12    … …eos … sche… of the worship of the holy month of Ph[ar]mouthi having begun, in which
13    many and continual festal processions, in due course,
14    take place, at which it is necessary to be present on both accounts, both for the service concerning the divine and for having at the proper time the best of the prayers on behalf of your health
15    and glory. I will come, the gods cooperating
16    and granting the ability, after the
17    season [of] the worship [of Ph]armouthi. May you be happy throughout,
18    my lor[d], and
19    may the gods grant whatever
20    is dear to you.
21    To the [ma]ster Sarapion, Anatolio[s ] …
