[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 47th year, Phameno[th] 2, in Pathyra before Heliodoros the agoranomos of the upper district of the Pathyrite.
2     Thus did Pachnoubis Tascou dispose: May I, in good health, be master of my own [property],
3     But if I suffer some human fate, I leave and give my property to me, both lands
4     and household goods and cattle and whatever I shall further acquire to Tathote of Haryotes, Persian woman,
5     whom I live with as wife according to law, except one bed and one embroidered couch
6     Pat… ei and Petesorrathi, of Pachnoubis, to my sons from me and another
7     woman; but all the remaining [property] of which each [item] is today listed, both lands
8     and livestock to the aforesaid Tathote: sheep indeed eight, cattle two, and the
9     [property] coming after these, and a house built and roofed and furnished, the one being in Ta[mei] of the Latopolite, neighbors to the south a house of Sennesias of the
10    Paoutes [to the] north [a] street royal, [to the] east a house of Thaesias of the Paoutes, to the west
11    [a] house of Patcormios, and of mainland grain-bearing earth, arouras twelve, the one in the part from the west
12    of the village [of Ta]meis, in which … [it agrees] … a well and an upper story for [firing of] bricks
13    brick[s], neighbors of the whole land: to the south [a] house of Psamminos of Kallias, to the north the same
14    to the east hills of the vill[age, to the west] … [c]alled Ammon's, and from another
15    seal of Kalebellaios of the … i, a third part, neighbors of the whole
16    [to the south Sar]endotes, to the north …, [to the] east [a] road, to the west a mountain or whoever are neighbors
17    [from all sides. Not] let it be allowed [to anyone at all to] proceed against this will [to] anyone
18    [to proceed], but if not, let it be invalid and let him who brings [it] later pay in addition
19    … other of the … fine of penalty immediately of bronze talents
20    … and sacred to the kings of silver [coined dra]chmas 250. Now the one disposing [was]
21    [Pachnoubis] of about 50 years, well-built, olive-complexioned, ta[unt]-… bald-headed, long-faced
22    [straight-nosed], scarred.
23    [Witness]es: Hermias [son] of Asklepiadis, Persian of the salaried cavalry, of about 25 years
24    well-built, olive-complexioned, wavy [haired], long-faced, straight-nosed, scarred, right ear pierced,
25    [and Ammo]nios [son] of Areios, Persian of the salaried cavalry, of about 30 years, average, [olive-]complexioned, wavy-haired, long-faced
26    [straight-nosed] scarred on the forehead, and Esthladas [son] of Drytan, Ptolemaian, of about 35 years
27    [well-built] olive-complexioned, wavy-haired, long-faced, straight-nosed, and Ptolemaios [son] of Asklepiadis, P[ersian] of the salaried cavalry, of about 35 years, average, olive-complexioned, long-faced, straight-nosed,
28    [and] … etos [son] of Meneклeos, Persian of the foot-soldiers, of about 35 years, average, dark-complexioned, wavy-haired,
29    [long-faced] straight-nosed, the six salaried [ones]. [Areios, the one] from Heliodoros, has witnessed.
