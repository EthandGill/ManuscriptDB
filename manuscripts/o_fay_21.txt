[TRANSLATION]
1   Has delivered, to the fort being built in the village
2   of Dionysias, Annianos son of Apollon, from the village
3   of Taurinos, one basket of burning-chaff (fuel).
4   Year 14 and year 2 and year 1, Mesore, 2nd epagomenal day.
5   Skammeiphos(?), I have signed.
