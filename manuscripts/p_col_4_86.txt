[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum to Eukles
2     from Horos and Pasites
3     concerning the Cynopolite
4     land that was brought to us earlier, having mixed it with the land from Kerkeus
5     land, we have worked [it]
6     already all of [it]. But now another Cynopolite land comes to us.
7     it will be necessary to mix
8     this [land] again
9     with the land from Kerkeus
10    land. We entreat you
11    to order [that it] be brought from Kerkeus land so that we may work all the land.
12    Farewell.
