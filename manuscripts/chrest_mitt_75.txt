[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Aurelius Herakleios also called] Asterios, [strategos] of the Oxyrhynchite (nome),
2     [from Menodora] … [surnamed] Syra, through her paternal grandfather Aurelius Sabinus, senior councillor of the most illustrious city of the Alexan[drians. A copy of the letter of Aurelius Didy]mos also called Sarapion, the chief-justice in office, which I procured, together with the appended copies of the memoranda made before him … [after] the originals, is set out below, and I request to do the things in accordance with what has been written. Year 13 of Imperator Caesar Publius Licinius Gallienus Germanicus
3     [Greatest, Pious, Fortunate, Augustus, Phaô]phi 14. There is, of the letter (the following): Aurelius Didymos also called Sarapion, chief-justice in office, to the strategos of the Oxyrhyn[chite (nome), greeting. Ammoninos also called] …kianos and his maidservant, see to it that you send to the court within twenty days, in accordance with what Menodo[ra surnamed Syra requested through her grandfather Sab]inus, fellow-councillor, nothing being altered until the trial before me. Let it be, however, that if he does not arrive with the
4     [maidservant, I shall annul what she brought] against the minor (girl), namely the notification-document and the distraint. For indeed Sabinus deposited (the claim) that you would repay to Ammoninos most
5     [of … and] … . I pray for your health. Year 13 of our lord Gallienus Augustus, Pachon 28. And of the memorandum (the following):
6     [So-and-so, on behalf of Aurelius Sabinus,] said: Aurelia Mēn[odora surn]amed Syra, through her paternal grand father Sabinus, senior councillor of the most illust[rious city of the Alexandrians, appeals (against the fact that) Ammoninos also called] …kianos, having resolved to work no ordinary violence against the lawful (rights) of the minor, began
7     … [for, the] father having owed … on account of wine, one hundred and sixty-three Oxyrhynchite (jars), and this number
8     [having long ago been paid in full,] he gave memoranda (for) [one hundred and sixty]-three [Oxyrhynchite (jars)] to the guardian of the present (man's) granddaughter, and he used not only a notification-document but [also]
9     … [he completed the] legal (formalities) concerning them … the distraint in the notification-document … and to insert the number; the notification-document itself is annulled
10    … for one hundred … and the remaining sixty-[three] being (the amount) owed, it being necessary to acknowledge
11    … to have made … so he completed the legal (formalities).
