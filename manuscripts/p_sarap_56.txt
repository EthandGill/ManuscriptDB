[TRANSLATION]
1     Account of Phaophi of the 13th year.
2     Kalas the cook: 8 drachmas, 23 obols.
3     Mires son of Pegeus: 8 drachmas.
4     Pepeious the farmer: 28 drachmas.
5     Sarapion from Artemid(-): 40 drachmas.
6     Horion son of Kalleus: 12 drachmas.
7     Horion son of Hermaios: 8 drachmas.
8     Pascheis son of Panesneus: 8 drachmas, 17 obols.
9     Hermaios son of Horion: 8 drachmas.
10    Pepeious the farmer: 60 drachmas.
11    Oryntes, price of a jar: 8 drachmas.
12    Chairemon son of Apiteus: 8 drachmas.
13    Prous son of Petosiris: 4 drachmas.
14    Total of the aforesaid: 204 drachmas, 11 obols.
15    From which, expenses of Phaophi of the 13th year:
16    to Prous, on account: 8 drachmas, 11 obols.
17    to Anoubion, for expense of the storehouse: 4 drachmas.
18    to Pekysis, Ibi… op(-), for cutting: 20 drachmas.
19    to Paesis the gardener: 40 drachmas.
20    price of papyrus: three obols.
21    (day) 6, oil: 1 obol.
22    (day) 7, oil: 1½ obols.
23    cabbage: 1 obol.
24    to Eudaimonis the wife, for expense: 12 obols.
25    (day) 8, to Anoubion through Thatres, for expense: 13 obols.
26    price of a scraper: 7 obols.
27    freight of jars carried up from the drying-floor
28    to a boat: 6 obols.
29    price of barley for donkeys: four obols.
30    to Anoubion through Taibis the nurse: 40 drachmas.
31    …, expense of Pascheis son of Panesneus: 4 drachmas, 12 obols.
32    …, to the carpenter: three obols.
33    …, Kleopha(-): three obols.
34    to Paesis the gardener, … fodder for the water-wheel: 60 drachmas.
35    rent of the drying-floor … expended …: … drachmas.
36    8 drachmas, 18½ obols.
37    to Papounis the sailor, for wages
38    of the boat: 12 drachmas.
39    to Pascheis the shepherd, for reception of the flock: 12 drachmas.
40    Total of the aforesaid: 220 drachmas, 9 obols.
41    Surplus: 12 drachmas, 27 obols. (?)
