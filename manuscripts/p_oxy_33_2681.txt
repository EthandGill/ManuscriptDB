[TRANSLATION]
1     Eudaemon to Zoilus the superintendent.
2     Greetings.
3     The letter through Eleutherus I sent to the.
4     At the embole. Know.
5     That the Goniates have obtained soldiers from.
6     The governor for the search of the Mastites.
7     Whether then barley or.
8     Lentils or grass.
9     Gather together and tread.
10    Before [any] disturbance arises.
11    Farewell.
