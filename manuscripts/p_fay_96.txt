[TRANSLATION]
1   In the seventh year of Imperator
2   Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus
3   Augustus Pius, Hathyr 29, through the bank of Sara-
4   pion (son of) Pterouit…kos:
5   Syros son of Alexandros son of Alexandros,
6   oil-make[r], to Nemesas son of Heli[od]oros son of
7   Eudaimon, of the quarter of …kon, aged about
8   forty, without distinguishing mark - that
9   Nemesas has received from Syr[o]s the rent
10   of the past sixth year of Antoninus
11   Caesar the lord, which Syros holds
12   in lease, of the oil-mill belonging
13   to the one in his charge,
14   Pompeius Ptolemaios, prytanizing gymna-
15   siarch: of oil, five metretes,
16   namely two and a half metretes of olive oil
17   and the remaining two and a half metretes
18   of radish oil; and that he makes no claim against him
19   about these things, the lease remaining
20   valid on the terms it contains.
