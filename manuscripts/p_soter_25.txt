[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twelfth year of Imperator Caesar Nerva
2     Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, in the month
3     Kaisareios, on the thirtieth, at Theadelphia of the Themistos
4     division of the Arsinoite nome. Artemeis acknowledges,
5     daughter of Apollonios, about thirty-four years old, with a scar
6     on the upper middle of her lip, with her guardian, her own kinsman Ptolemaios son of Isidoros, about forty-
7     five years old, without distinguishing mark, to Thaisarion daughter of Chares, about
8     fifty-five years old, with a scar between the eyebrows, with the guardian,
9     her own son Chares son of Soterichos, about
10    thirty-five years old, with a scar on the left shin,
11    that she has received from her (Thaisarion) of silver two hundred drachmas
12    and four artabas of wheat, which were owed by the
13    husband of Thaisarion and the father of Chares, Soterichos
14    son of Lykos, who has died, while still living, to Artemeis,
15    according to an agreement completed through the record-office at Ptolemais Euergetis
16    in the fifteenth year
17    of Domitian, in the month Neos Sebastos, on the fourth, in which (drachmas)
18    are (included) on the mortgage of the dwelling belonging to Soterichos,
19    a little house and little courtyard, one hundred drachmas of silver; and that
20    Artemeis has handed back the said and cancelled agreement
21    to Thaisarion for annulment and invalidation,
22    and that neither Artemeis nor those (acting) for her shall bring,
23    nor shall bring a claim against Thaisarion nor those (acting) for her, either
24    concerning the things she has received as aforesaid, or concerning any other
25    matter whatsoever, written or unwritten, from times past until the present day, in any way. The subscriber
26    for Artemeis and for her registered guardian is Horeion son of Horeion, 28 years old, with a scar on the left hand.
27    I, Artemeis daughter of Apollonios, with the guardian, my
28    kinsman Ptolemaios son of Isidoros, [acknowledge]
29    that I have received from Thaisarion daughter of Chares two hundred [silver]
30    drachmas and four artabas of wheat,
31    which were owed to me by the late
32    and deceased husband of Thaisarion, Soterichos
33    son of Lykos, according to an agreement which I have also handed back
34    to her for annulment and invalidation, and (that I shall) bring
35    no claim as aforesaid. There wrote for her
36    Horion son of Horion, because they do not know
37    letters. I, Thaisarion daughter of Chares, with the guardian, my son
38    Chares son of Soterichos: the receipt has been made over to me as aforesaid. There wrote [for] her Ision son of Her…
39    In the 12th year of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus,
40    in the month Kaisareios, 30, registered through the record-office at Theadelphia.
41    Agreement of Artemeis with
42    Thaisarion, of a receipt.
