[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonides to Zenon, greetings. it would be well if Apollonios and you [are in good health and we are in good health too]
2     we. I received from Iatrokleios wool weight two talents. write then to me if forty-mina [pieces 3 or talent-weight]
3     become two coverlets, or [should we make it] talent-weight and [keep the other] talent preserved [until you arrive];
4     and do this as quickly [as possible]. and write also when [we should] receive [Apollonios], so that in good time the lodging [may be prepared] for him.
5     [farewell]. year 28, Phamonoth […].
6     Apollonides clerk to Zenon
7     concerning wool from Iatrokleios [of] two talents.
8     year 29, Xandikos 2, in Memphis.
