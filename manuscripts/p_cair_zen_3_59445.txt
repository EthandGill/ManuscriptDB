[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum [to Zenon] from Theophilus the … concerning the works
2     in the [Diot]imus[house]. Of the portico
3     … in order that be painted [wa]ve-border with purple and throne varied and
4     coat-of-mail grain-like and the substructure
5     vein-surrounded, [providing] myself everything
6     providing, 30 drachmas, and of the seven-couch [room]
7     the vault to make such as the example you will have seen and the coat-of-mail
8     with desirable oil and the Lesbian to paint,
9     [my]self everything providing, 20 drachmas,
10    and [of] the five-couch [rooms] I will paint the wave-borders, myself everything providing, 3 drachmas.
11    To the same it becomes 53 drachmas.
12    If but you provide everything, [he] will make
13    30 drachmas. Farewell.
