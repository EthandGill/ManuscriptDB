# HGV-EN: ‘Account of barley owing for the 12th year on account of arrears of the Upper Leucopurgite Toparchy. Of barley in arrears for the 1th year 5 1/24 artabae for Terton Petochnoubeos (are to be deducted) from the 8 23/24 owing for Bousiris Sinarchebeos, leaving 3 11/12; formerly paid 2 1/2 artabae. For the 12th year, Poampimenis 39/48 artaba, Sinkere 4 7/16 artabae, Terton 10 1/24 artabae.’
