[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Longinus the most illustrious for the 2nd time, Hathyr
2     … of the 15th indiction, in Oxyrhynchos.
3     To Flavius … the reverend nauarch, son of the … of blessed
4     memory, former nauarch, landowner here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, Aurelius John son of Melas,
5     his mother being …, from the hamlet … of the estate …ilous of your excellence, of the Oxyrhynchite
6     nome, registered vine-dresser, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received
7     from your admirableness hereby the price agreed between one another and pleasing to me, in full, of wine from the estate vat, thirty vat-measures, total 30 vat-measures of wine, which wine at the wine-press
8     from unadulterated new must, you providing the empty jars, I will of necessity pay
9     to your excellence at the vintage of the month Mesore of the present year 168=137
10    from the outflow of wine of the fortunate first indiction, without delay. And
11    for security of the repayment of the debt, I have hereby already mortgaged to you
12    by right of mortgage, from our own privately-owned arouras lying in the fields
13    of the same estate, on the west of it, five plots (ammata), so that you hold these until the repayment of the same debt, and that it not be permitted to me before repayment
