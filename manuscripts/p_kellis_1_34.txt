[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius [Tou son of Besapo]nychos, from the village of Keramia of the city of the Mothites, to Aurelius Horos son of Mersis, from the village of Kellis of the
2     same city, greeting. I acknowledge that I have sold to you from now
3     for all [time] the half-share of a colt belonging to me,
4     a white one, this such (animal), not to be cast back and not liable to claim, having come to me [from] … my begetter, at the price agreed upon [between us] of silver of imperial coinage in coin, two [talents 3,000 drachmas, that is] 2 talents 3,000 drachmas, which I have received from you today
5     by hand [in full]. I am content and persuaded and will guarantee
6     with every guarantee, that from now you shall hold sway and be lord,
7     you the purchaser of the half-colt sold to you by me …
8     … … on behalf of your share, the aforesaid
9     share, and [have authority] to administer and to dispose concerning it …
10    [in whatever] way you choose, the guarantee following upon me the seller [thro]ughout against everything that shall come up. Valid is the s[ale] … [written in single copy] upon the subscription of the one subscribing on my behalf, [let it be]
11    and secure as if laid up in a public archive, and on being questioned
12    I acknowledged (it).
13    In the consulship of our masters Constantine and Licinius
14    Augusti for the 4th time, Mecheir 25 by the Egyptians.
15    Aurelius Tou son of Besaponychos to Aurelius Horos the aforesaid:
16    I have sold to you the half-share of the colt and have received the price, two and a half talents in coin of currency, that is 2 talents 3,000 drachmas, and I will guarantee it to you as is set forth, and on being questioned I acknowledged as is set forth. Aurelius Psais … d…ios wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate
17    [from the villag]e of Kellis.
18    Sale of a half-colt, of Tou son of Besaponychos.
