r.1    διέγραψεν Πετεμενῶφις Ψ … θι … Πάσιτος

[TRANSLATION]
1     Petemenophes son of Psi... thi... Pasitus has inscribed
2     for the poll-tax of Pakerkehe[sis] [month] 14th year, drachmas 4. Of the 14th year of Nero
3     the lord, Pachon 5. Similarly [month] 14 drachmas 4.
4     Similarly Epip 1 for [the 2nd... tax] drachmas 4 ...
5     Mesore 28 for the embankment-tax drachmas 7; the net [drachmas] 6 tetrobol.
6     Similarly 28 for the wages of the guards drachma 1 tetrobol.
