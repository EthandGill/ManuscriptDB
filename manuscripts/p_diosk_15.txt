[TRANSLATION]
1     Sosos to Dioskourides, my brother, my friend, my father, my hope,
2     greeting. I tell you, if you are well …
3     … and everything else is according to
4     reckoning. They thought that Rhodokles …
5     had recovered his sight. For the one thing,
6     namely, that I am in good health, even to write to you myself
7     seemed to me foolish, for I am not in good health,
8     but I am in the greatest misfortune,
9     even though by your exhortations
10    I have been much recalled (to spirits), O Dioskourides; yet I am still not able to cease
11    pros … r … from the misfortune around me,
12    so that … may be separated
13    from all … and from the precious things
14    … u … which last year did; but from Kassandra everything sufficient comes to me, with
15    freedom from want; but I have not been able to provide you any service whatsoever,
16    nor even to myself — by your safety! — except some one
17    thing or another. For, since Rhodoklea is in the Thesmophorion,
18    I am compelled to serve her there, for we have no one (else). But so much for these things.
19    But you will do me a great favor, and not only
20    me, but the whole city, if you take care of yourself,
21    so that you may come in good health both to your sister
22    and the children and all your friends;
23    for of this to us, by Herakles, nothing
24    is greater.
25    Year 2…, farewell. Mesore 4.
26    Sosos.
27    To Dioskourides.
28    Year 2…, Mesore 16.
29    Let him carry it to Hieron.
30    To Paotes.
