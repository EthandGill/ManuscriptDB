[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of o[ur] most divine and most pious master, greatest benefactor,
2     Flavius Ma[ur]icius Novus Tiberius, the eternal Augustus and Emperor,
3     year 14, in the consulship of the same our most pious master, year 13, Hathyr 30 of the 14th indiction.
4     To Flavius Apion, the all-praiseworthy and most exalted ex-consul,
5     landholder also here in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas,
6     household-servant, who formally interrogates and procures for his own master, the
7     same all-praiseworthy man, the right of action and liability: Zacharias, steward
8     of the church of the holy Resurrection, son of (the man) of admirable
9     memory Anastasius, originating from this (city) of the Oxyrhynchites,
10    subscribing below in his own hand, greeting. I acknowledge by willing
11    intention and free choice, swearing the divine and
12    venerable oath, that I stand surety for and undertake before your Exaltedness,
13    through those belonging to it, Aurelius Pambechios, son of Paulus,
14    his mother being Thekla, originating from the hamlet of Athlitos, an estate
15    of your Exaltedness of the Oxyrhynchite nome, a registered
16    orchard-keeper of it, on condition that he without interruption remain and
17    abide in the … their landholding-orchard, and exercise all good care
18    and good cultivation upon it, blamelessly and unhesitatingly
19    and irreproachably, and conscientiously (render) the rent due for it
20    each year an[d a]ll the customarily-given landholding
21    services [an]d, when he is required of me by your
22    Exaltedness through those belonging to it, on any
23    day for whatsoever cause on any pretext, I shall bring him forth and
24    hand (him) over i[n] a public place, outside every place of refuge
25    and account, where also I have received him in the prison of your glorious
26    house; or, if I do not do this, I acknowledge that of my own (means) [on] his behalf I shall pay
27    the rents of the same landholding-orchard at my own risk and that of
28    my whole substance. The surety, written in single copy, is authoritative, and on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
29    Zacharias, son of the blessed Anastasius, the aforewritten.
30    This surety is satisfactory to me as set forth above.
31    A surety of Zacharias, the … from the city of O[xyrhynchus],
32    undertaking for P…, from the hamlet of Athlitos, an es[tate. ] …
