1     14, year of Severus and Antoninus
2     and Geta Caesar Augustus. [He] charged [it] to Theon also called Demetrius and
3     to those with him supervisors
4     of alum being brought from Oasis and subjected camels
5     and donkeys of Heliammon Lamyrous
6     camels 3, donkeys 3, drachmas thirty
7     eight tetrobols, total drachmas 38 …
8     Theon
9     also called Demetrius I have signed.
