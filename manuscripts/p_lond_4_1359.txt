[TRANSLATION]
1     Concerning the fine of the pagarch and assistants. (Arabic: To the lord of Ishquh concerning the fine of the workers.)
2     In the name of God. Qurra son of Sharik, governor (symboulos),
3     to Basileios administrator of the village of Aphrodito. We give thanks
4     to God, and after these things: when we looked into the register
5     of the gold paid into the treasury (sakelle) for the fine
6     of the assistants, we did not find that you had paid for the fine assessed
7     through you, and (that) of the assistants of your administrative district,
8     anything worth mentioning; and God knows, we resolved
9     to repay you for such contempt
10    beyond what you suppose. Receiving, therefore, the present letters,
11    send with all speed into the treasury the assessed
12    gold, through you and the assistants of your administrative district,
13    for the fine as has been said, if indeed you have any good (sense)
14    and understand the things written to you. For if you delay
15    and we are compelled to use other letters,
16    there are about to reach you, together with the same letters, the things that afflict [you].
17    For there is known to you no excuse in this, inasmuch as
18    you have already harvested the produce, and the
19    appointed term has passed, and you have no excuse in anything.
20    For the rest, if you love your life, do not show contempt
21    in this. Written in the month Pauni 24, of the 9th indiction.
22    Qurra son of Sharik, governor, to Basileios administrator of the village of Aphrodito.
23    Epeiph 7, of the 9th indiction, it was brought concerning sending the fine [of the pagarch and the] assistants.
