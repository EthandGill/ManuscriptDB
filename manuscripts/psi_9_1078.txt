1     In the consulship of our lords Constantius Augustus [the] 8th and Julian the
2     Most courageous and most illustrious Caesar [the] 1st, Hathyr 29th.
3     To Flavius Kronion, official of rank
4     Of the prefect of Egypt
5     From Aurelius Horus [son] of Pasalymos from the village of Lenoi of [the] 4th pagus. I willingly agree to lease for only the present 33rd year and 2nd [year] from the land belonging to you around the same village

11    Eight artabas and of vegetable-seed two-and-a-half artabas,
12    All [of them measured] by your measure, the landholder …
13    … [illegible] in them [illegible] …
14    [I will] pay for them the [previously paid] rent. And I also acknowledge [that I have] received
15    From you the landholder on account of advance [payment] of silver

20    Land in which [are] water-works and a device and equipment
