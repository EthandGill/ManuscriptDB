[TRANSLATION]
1     Basile[, the] least of deacons … the son of the late
2     Petros, the man of Shmoun (Hermopolis): then [I make known] my word, and behold, I [m]ake my testament according to law while [my] heart is sound in me and my senses are unimpaired,
3     and my reasonings firm. I am ill indeed, as a man, with my heart sound within me,
4     and my mind unimpaired, my reasonings established. I do not transgress in
5     my word, but since I am [afraid] tha[t] the thing which befalls every
6     person should befall me too — [tha]t is, death — for this reason I have considered that
7     it is necessary that … with my testament …
8     … testament out, or a part of it as a whole, with
9     … ever. I wish, and I command,
10    … [that t]he holy altar of the [s]aint be served through
11    [Apa K]yrik[os], according to time, as lord over it, with an ar[t]aba of wheat
12    … les, namely my fifteen artabas of wheat, I taking them … in my third (part) and they shall pay it until … hidden in the threshing-floor
13    … rame … oshe of … find the produce
14    of the tenth indiction, and … [fo]rever, for a repose [of] my soul and a remission of my sins on
15    the day of … the receiving … of my portion … …
16    my mother, movable property …
17    whether field or rent or house or house…
18    or any of the things, small or great …
19    up to a sum, down to a single obol …
20    my beloved brethren A[n]astasia and Shenoute [shall be lords]
21    over them each by the half part, after my [great day]
22    that is to come forever, that my [si]ster nor a child of hers shall ever
23    be lord over any of the things in all my in[heritance] forever …
24    … goige of my brethren Ana[s]tasia and Shenoute …
25    … a debt that I owe them, namely these: from lady Maria
26    … one solidus; from Kolthe, the man of Thermo[us], one solidus;
27    … a keration, sunte; from Markos the smith, one solidus and
28    [fif]teen lakoote-measures of wine; from Abraham the man of An[t]inoou,
29    three, one chos of solidi — these which I gave to my children, with
30    those of my brethren — and they have power over each of them in deeds or among those reckoned
31    to me, by every degree of kinship, to dispute or to contest concerning this testament at any time or at any
32    time, whether in the court or outside the court,
33    after the great day [to com]e forever; and if anyone wish
34    to transgress the force of this testament, or a part of it as a whole,
35    at any time or at any time forever, upon the
36    first indeed let him take …
