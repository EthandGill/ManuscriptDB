[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermogenes to Theodoros greetings. I have [submitted] to you the copy of the letter from Harmachoron.
2     the copy. It is well if [you wish what was] requested to arrange well with them.
3     For having worked for considerable time [in] the copper mines and having received
4     hope until the 30th of Pachon, having worked the tasks which they had in their hands
5     inasmuch as it was decided under Logbases [and Her]maphilus for them to depart to Alabanthida on account of the lack of water of [these] places, and you having required of the scribe
6     to wait until the 10th of Pauni [and not] anyone objecting, I think it is good that in the manner which we [agreed] with them we do, namely that we send the surveyors with speed [so that] the work [having been surveyed] for them
7     they do not sit for the longer time.
8     Farewell. Year 7, Pauni 9.
9     Year 7, Pauni 11. To Hermogenes
10    Copy of the letter from Harmachoron. To Theodoros
