[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flav[ius] … the most eminent praeses …
2     from A[ure]lia [Ta]pais, daughter of Heras, [mother Allous, from the villa]ge of Karanis
3     of the Arsinoite nome. Casting a most just plea before your Greatness, lord praeses, I am hopeful of obtaining help from your Greatness. I testified to this, that An[tio]urios, [son of Abous,] and Atisios, son
4     of Le[on]ides, from the same village of Ka[ra]ni[s], committed a most lawl[ess]
5     deed against me, which the laws bid your Greatness to annul.
6     … against me, a child under age and an orphan as I am … my maternal four arouras … to a certain scribe Didymos and … being liable to tax. For it is necessary
7     for … signs … [to sub]scribe, … of them lawfully … having transacted, she was able to do … of my maternal
8     nine and a half arouras lying around the [same] village Karanis. And the
9     other deed and … he made in this sale, which he did not pay out,
10    laying claim to the ownership of them by lawsuit. … therefore these documents,
11    especially according to the fraud of persons under age … framed together, as
12    has been said before, the laws annul, and the … For this reason, having been fortunate to obtain your beneficent magistracy, I make my refuge [to] your feet,
13    an orphan child making my plea, and … of you, while I speak the truth
14    in these matters, so that you may order Ptole[maios] … this man from
15    … … pro… that the
16    unlawful sale that took place be void, and that to me be given over the ownership of the small plots,
17    so that, having obtained this, I may be able to have enjoyment of them and
18    forever give thanks to you. Farewell. I, Aure[li]a [Ta]pais, have submitted it. Aurelius
19    Dioskoros, I wrote on her behalf, as she is illiterate.
