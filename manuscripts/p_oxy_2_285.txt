[TRANSLATION]
1   To Tiberius Claudius Pasion, strate(gos),
2   from Sarapion son of Theon,
3   of the city of the Oxyrhynchi,
4   weaver, of the quarter of the Gymna-
5   sium street. Apollophanes, having been
6   collector of the trade-tax of the weav-
7   ers in the 1st year of Tiberius Claud[ius]
8   Caesar Augustus Germanicus
9   Imperator, using much force,
10   snatched away the (tunic) I was
11   wearing, a linen tunic
12   worth eight drachmas, and ex-
13   torted from me another four
14   drachmas, and from the month Neos Sebas-
15   tos of the ninth year of Tiberius
16   Claudius Caesar Augustus
17   Germanicus Imperator until
18   Pharmouthi, six months, each month
19   two drachmas, which come to 24 dr.
20   Therefore I ask that proceedings be taken against him
21   as may seem good to you. Farewell.
