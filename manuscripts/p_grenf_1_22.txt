[TRANSLATION]
1     Moschion to Petearsenthi
2     greetings. I have for (the account of) Hermios
3     an account concerning the topography-office of the 53rd year, bronze talents
4     ten. (It) totals talents 10. And concerning
5     the village-secretary-office similarly bronze talents ten. (It) totals talents 10.
6     and from the price of wheat of 150 artabas of drachmas talents 30,
7     becomes bronze talents thirty. (It) totals talents 30.
8     (It) totals talents 50.
9     Year 53, Hathyr 1.
