[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aur]elii Pasion, councillor, and Sarap[i]on Helladikos
2     both receivers of sealed grain to Aurelii
3     Antonios and Isidoros, grain-collectors, of year 17 and year 5, greetings.
4     We received from you sealed grain, pounds seven-hundred fifty, totaling pounds 750.
5     Pasion has signed.
6     Year 17 and 5, Mesore 21.
