[TRANSLATION]
1     Menches, komogrammateus of Kerkeosiris in the division of Polemon,
2     to Ptolemaios, greeting. On the 16th
3     of Epeiph of the 3rd year, while I was making an inspection
4     together with Horos the komarch and Patanis and
5     other elders of the cultivators, concerning the
6     dyke-works around the village
7     … was … brought to … ou,
8     and when we came to the outlet, the assessment (of dyke-work)
9     of Soknebtynis the great god
10    being …
11    lying between the fields around the village,
12    of the embankment, we found certain persons,
13    men of Philonautes son of Leon, of the katoikoi cavalry from Berenikis Thesmophorou, having dug under
14    … the … part of the outlet mentioned,
15    … of the dykes of the so-called embankment of Themistes,
16    to the extent of eight schoinia, and having thrown up
17    the soil from it onto the dykes of the allotment of the said Philonautes.
18    Straightway, having identified from among
19    the aforesaid persons one,
20    … we handed him over to Polemon who administers the affairs of the epistateia
21    of the village … to bring before you those …
22    … . Wherefore I report it, so that, if it seems good,
23    … before everything else … the dykes may first be secured,
24    … the assessment of dyke-work of the embankment, and that Philonautes and the
25    … appointed over the dyke
26    may meet with the fitting rebuke
27    … .
