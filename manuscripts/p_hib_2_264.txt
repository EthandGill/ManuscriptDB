1     Year 37, Hathyr 10. [a]greement
2     concerning Ptolemaeus' beer-supplies
3     which he owed. [O]phis of Teos Pse[nnes], Apollodorus of Potam[on], 25 artabas of spelt wheat, drachmas
4     eight. Guarantor: Demetrius of Chalcis. Remaining, I owe additionally from Mecheir until
5     Mesore, 100 drachmas.
