1     To my lord brother Apollonios
2     the recorder, Iustus, greeting.
3     My son Dioscurides wrote to me
4     concerning Diogenes, the son of Markella, as having died, and it was incumbent
5     on you, my lord brother, knowing that
6     for your sake I am staying here,
7     to take care and be concerned
8     for the matter, for the sake of what is
9     to our advantage. For you know, even if
10    I do not write to you, how the affairs of the
11    freedmen revert to their
12    patrons. You will do well therefore,
13    taking up my person (representing me),
14    to make my son Dioscurides have on register
15    [both the] cattle and the harvest-gathering and whatever else to him
16    … was (?), first making … converse (?) … him
17    … for the sake of the books lying there
18    … all the documents
19    … lie. Dioscurides
20    [knows] of what I speak
21    … rent … and
22    … according to the laws
23    … having come (?)
24    … of mine … private (?)
25    … inclined (?)
26    … may request a[ccording to] the la[ws] … … boys (?)
27    … fear (?) … illness (?)
28    … has been left of the … (?)
29    to the matter. For the idle talk of certain persons no [lo]nger has any
30    force at the arrival of
31    my lord the governor. And if
32    Horos talks nonsense, if indeed he survives,
33    asserting that he is (the son) of the father whose name he bears, let him learn
34    that it shall have no force
35    against justice. And I say
36    that Kalokeros, whom he too does not fail to know, that he will side
37    with us rather than with the
38    aforesaid Horos. I declare to you, so that nothing escape
39    your notice, that the petition given by your brother against certain colleagues in office, as a certain disturbance had arisen in the
40    gymnasium, was sent here to the most illustrious Alexandria and
41    in the whole praetorium
42    was heard, and, as one may say,
43    came to the ears of the authority. So that you may consider their interest,
44    this very thing I have declared to you.
45    Concerning then the ischarosimon (?),
46    just as I said before,
47    having stirred up everything against
48    him, until he grants me leave to depart, Alexander (?) … nor again do I wait so that I may accomplish the matter of the 6
49    talen[t]s before
50    [I] go [ou]t. If therefore
51    [he] come[s] by the 1[5]th, the whole
52    of the 15th. [But i]f indeed not, then I too
53    am going ou[t]. For this man is never going to bring suit. For
54    it is a matter of nonsense for him, as
55    you know. Concerning the pending matter
56    of the freedman I am confident that
57    you are not going to keep quiet,
58    so that we be not mocked even by private persons. There is no need
59    of an order, both concerning the vintage
60    and the creditors. And let him declare to me.
61    And the flow (?) and the … . I have enjoined you.
62    Farewell,
63    to me (?).
64    To my lord brother Apollonios the recorder, former prytanis,
65    and to Dioscurides his son, (from) Iustus.
