[TRANSLATION]
1     From Ischyrion.
2     Since my lord
3     my Alupis, that tomorrow
4     with the dioiketes
5     coming [here]
6     let there be care to you
7     from night the ones with
8     you fishermen to send, having fish
9     most abundant and most beautiful.
10    But at dawn [let them go up].
11    I wish you to be in good health.
12    To Heroninos the steward, Thraso.
13    Year 7, Phamenoth 3.
