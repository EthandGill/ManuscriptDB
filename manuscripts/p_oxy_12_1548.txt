[TRANSLATION]
1     For issue.
2     From Ploution son of Ploution
3     grandson of Ploution, his mother being Tapsois,
4     of Oxyrhynchus. According to the orders given by Maecius
5     Laetus, the most illustrious praefect,
6     I register for the census of the past 10th year
7     of the Caesars our lords Severus
8     and Antoninus,
9     in the house-by-house census, the property belonging to me in the North Quay quarter, a fourth part of a house and courtyard
10    and of its appurtenances, in which I register:
11    I myself, the said Ploution, of no trade, without distinguishing mark, aged 48,
12    Dioskoros my slave, of no trade, without distinguishing mark, aged … 8.
13    The women: Tapsois also called Eudaimonis, my daughter,
14    living with her husband Apollonios, of no trade, without distinguishing mark, about 20 years old,
15    Gaiane her full sister, of no trade,
16    without distinguishing mark, about 15 years old,
17    Aphrodite surnamed Isidora,
18    a purchased slave-girl of my daughter Tapsois
19    also called Eudaimonis, of the Oasis by birth,
20    being with her mistress, of no trade, without distinguishing mark, about 13 years old,
21    Taeros, a slave-girl of mine and of my brothers
22    and of others, of no trade, without distinguishing mark, about 35 years old,
23    Taeipimachos, another slave-girl of mine
24    and of my brothers and others, born from the slave-woman
25    Taeros, of no trade, without distinguishing mark, about 9 years old.
26    And I swear by the Fortune of Lucius
27    Septimius Severus Pius Pertinax
28    and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
29    Pius, the Augusti, and Publius Sep[timius Geta Caesar Augustus]
