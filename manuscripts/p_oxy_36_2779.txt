[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavii Orestes and Lampadius the most illustrious, Tybi 4 of the 9th indiction.
2     To Flavius Strategios the all-renowned and most glorious ex-consul,
3     general and patrician, holding first place both in the Herakleopolite
4     and in this illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas
5     the house-servant who also puts the formal question and provides on behalf of his own master,
6     the same most eminent man: Aurelius Epimachos also called Apima son
7     of Phoibammon, his mother being Termouthis, originating from the hamlet of
8     Pangouleeion, an estate of your Eminence, of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
9     a registered farmer, greeting. A need having now arisen for the
10    landholding machine called of Pentaarouraia under me,
11    drawing water for vineyard and for arable land, of one axle, having come up
12    to the city I requested your Eminence to
13    command that the same axle be provided to [me], and at once
14    your Eminence, taking forethought for the maintenance
15    of her own affairs, provided me the same axle through
16    Moison, house-servant of the same most eminent man,
17    a new, serviceable, satisfactory drawing-(axle), [which] I received for
18    the supply of all the machine-implements on the present
19    day, which is Tybi the fourth of the present year 207–186(?)
20    of the present ninth indiction, the water-supply being of the tenth
21    assessment, and the old axle having been brought down and
22    given over to the great landholding house; and as proof
23    of the receipt I have made this written acknowledgment which is valid,
24    single, [having been written, a]nd on being formally questioned I assented. Aurelius Epimachos son of Phoibammon
25    the aforewritten, I have made the written acknowledgment, having received the
26    new axle, and everything is agreeable to me as aforesaid. Aurelius Menas son
27    of the blessed Theodoros, having been requested, I wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
28    Through me, Philoxenos, it was completed … Tybi 4 of the 9th indiction.
29    423
30    Written acknowledgment of Epimachos son of Phoibammon, from the hamlet of Pangouleeion, of the receipt of one axle.
