[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelia Demetria, stolata,
2     matrona, daughter of Aurelius Posidonios,
3     hypomnematographos, through Aurelius
4     Marinus, ex-gymnasiarch, councillor of the city of the Arsinoites,
5     from Aurelius Ploutammon, whose mother is
6     Helene, residing in the village of Euhemeria.
7     I wish to lease, near the
8     same village of Euhemeria, 1 aroura of garden cultivated for vegetables, or however many there may be, for
9     a period of 3 years, in which there is a well and a water-wheel,
10    from the present month Mecheir of the current 2nd year, at a rent for the same (garden)
11    each year of 100 silver drachmae, free of deduction and free of risk; and it shall not be permitted to me [to abandon the lease] within the term in any way, but I shall also of necessity [do]
12    each year all the works, as much as is fitting, at the proper times, doing not even one damage; and from here I received from the lady of rank
13    for drawing water one cow
14    of 100 drachmae; and (the cost) of whatever is further required for new
15    construction or the supply of timber
16    or nails or stones or pitch or carpenters' wages, both for the well and the water-wheel and the yokes
17    and the public (charges), falling upon the
18    lady of rank and the … (counter-irrigation?)
19    and … of the kinds (of works), being toward me the
20    lessee; and the rent each year
21    I shall pay every six months
22    in equal parts; and after the term I shall hand over the garden under every kind of
23    vegetable, just as I also received it, if it seems good to lease.
24    Year 2 of Imperator Caesar Marcus
25    Antonius Gordianus Pius Felix
26    Augustus, Mecheir 29; without subscription.
