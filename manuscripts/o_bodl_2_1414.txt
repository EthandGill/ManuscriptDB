1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, harvest of the 13th year of Antoninus
2     Caesar the lord, Tybi 1 of the 14th year, on behalf
3     Agoras, name Porieuthos, similarly Petepsaïtos, on behalf of [a] co-buyer
4     wheat, one artaba [and a] third, totals wheat artaba 1 1/3. Another, Tybi 19, name
5     of the same, wheat of an artaba, [a] sixth, wheat of an artaba 6, [it] totals in total wheat artaba 1 1/2.
