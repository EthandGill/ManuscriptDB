[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Arsinoe, gods] brothers, in the second year, [under the priesthood of Telipolemus]
2     [of Artapatos] Alexandros and gods brothers, basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphous, Ptolemais [of Thuion]
3     [of the month] … [, in Philadelphia of] the Arsinoe nome. Philon Beryllios leased …
4     … [to Jason] [son of Kerki]on from Calynda, sojourning [there], his plot …
5     … [arouras] … or however many may result from surveying, except [the fallow land] …
6     [… the export dues per each] year, each arura of wheat [artabas] …
7     … [and] … [o]lyre, artabas two; and Jason shall deliver [to Philon the export dues that have been] [written in the month] … [of the] … [year,] grain pure and unadulterated which has come from the [land, measured …]
8     [just, and let him place it] to Philon in Philadelphia at his own expense; and if he does not deliver …
9     … [a receipt for] money, four drachmas.
