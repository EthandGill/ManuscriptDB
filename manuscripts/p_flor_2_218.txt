[TRANSLATION]
1     From Ophellius.
2     Have the carpenters with you
3     cut an arch
4     one and send [it] back
5     to the city through Anouphis the ox-driver, [it being] fit
6     for the construction
7     of one wheel of the
8     cart under him.
9     But in any case [see] that
10    the cart is not disabled. You do not ignore
11    for the urgent works of carts.
12    I wish you health.
13    To Heroneinog the manager.
14    Year 4, Mesore 3
