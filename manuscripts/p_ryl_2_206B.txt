[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: ‘Aurelius Sarapion, ex-cosmetes, senator, to his most esteemed Aurelius Thonis and Aurelius Apion, greeting. Please transfer from the price of my wheat on account of the monthly payments of Hathur for Phaophi to Dionysius, collector of Ammonas, decemprimus, 252 drachmae, total 252 dr. I pray for your health.’ The 1st year, Hathur 12.
