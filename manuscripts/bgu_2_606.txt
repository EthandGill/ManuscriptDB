[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the quarter of Apollonios Hierakion, from A[urelius Sarapion, whose mother is]
2     …apeas, from the [quarter] of the Bithynians of the Other Places. [I wish to lease] from you the (property) belonging to you in the metropolis in the quarter of
3     Phremei, a cattle-yard, in which (are) two stalls for the storage of chaff
4     and fodder, for a period of five years from the present month Phamenoth,
5     at a rent for each month of one hundred silver drachmas, drachmas 100,
6     not subject to deduction and free from risk; and it shall not be permitted to me within the
7     period to abandon the lease in any way whatsoever,
8     the new repair or rebuilding of the cattle-yard and the stalls being the responsibility of you the owner, but the partial upkeep [and] care being my responsibility, the lessee;
9     and I will pay the rent each month on the fifth without delay, and after the period I will hand over the cattle-yard and the
10    stalls together with the fitted doors and the outer key, just as
11    I also received them, if it seems good to lease (them); and on being formally questioned
12    I gave my assent. Year 14 and 2 of our lords
13    Constantius and Maximianus Augusti and of Severus and Maximinus
14    the most noble Caesars, Phamenoth 19.
15    I, Aurelius Sarapion, have leased
16    as set forth above. I, Aurelius Polion,
17    wrote on his behalf, he being unlettered.
