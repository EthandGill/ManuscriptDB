[TRANSLATION]
1     Somenos and Dionysius to Athenodor[us]
2     the brother, very much greetings and to be well
3     concerning what we wrote to you about the [present]
4     the share [of] the children of Dionysius …
5     but we shared in [those that were] suspended
6     shoots; if therefore it is not burdensome for you to make them, and to leave trunks …
7     … us having seized; well
8     then you will do, administrator, …
9     To Athenodorus the brother
