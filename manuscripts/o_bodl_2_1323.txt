1     Has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis, of produce, [year] 20
2     of Hadrian the lord, Mesore, [the] complementary days 3

5     similarly, wheat one-twelfth of an artaba, becomes wheat artabae 12. [Mark] … has been marked.
