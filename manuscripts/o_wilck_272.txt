[TRANSLATION]
1   Iu[lius S]erenus, collector of money-taxes of Ele(phantine), and Va-
2   leria and Apollonius, appointed to the office
3   as collectors(?) in year 20. Paid: Thine-
4   septis daughter of Tisatis, for the price of state
5   date-palm of the produce of year 18, for the account
6   of Annius Bassus, 1 dr. 5 ob.; and for the account
7   of Agathodoros and Bassus, on his own behalf:
8   his share, 5 obols. Year 19, Pachon 5.
9   (2nd hand) I, Iulius Serenus, have jointly received.
