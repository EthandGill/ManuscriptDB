[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 15, Phaophi 19. Acknowledges Psenenupes [son of] Portites, Persian, as if of age 55, of medium
2     honey-complexion, firm, long-faced, straight-nosed, ears standing-apart, to Harsieseis [son of] Schoutos,
3     priest of Suchos and Aphrodite, [that he has] ceded to him [Harsieseis] the part falling to him of the [land] which [he] leased together with Pakobis [son of] Schoutos and the associates
4     [of] cultivable land called Nectharaut, [belonging to] the great god, from the
5     priests, [for] ten years, [occupying] the remaining other [years] for him, four
6     [years] for the completion of [five] years, so that he might work [the land] of which they had set the lease
7     from the aforementioned year, and [that] it was agreed with him concerning the [agreed-upon]
8     price in bronze two talents [of] three thousand three hundred drachmas; and [neither]
9     [he himself] Psenenupes shall come against Harsieseis nor [shall] any
10    one on his behalf concerning the described part of land in any
11    way. And if [he fails], let him pay to Harsieseis a penalty immediately in bronze five talents
12    and sacred to the kings [stamped] silver drachmas one hundred, and nothing the less
13    shall be valid the agreed-upon [things].
14    Hermias beside Paniscos the holder of funds.
