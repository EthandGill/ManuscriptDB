[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been acknowledged.
2     In the second [ye]ar of the Emperor Caesar Titus [A]elius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, Pauni 7, in Oxyrhynchus city of the Thebaïd.
3     Sarapion son of Adrastos son of Dionysios, his mother Ars[i]noe (daughter) of Theon, from O[x]yrhynchus city, acknowledges to his own full brother Theon, from the same city, in the street, that he has ceded to him
4     from those (arouras) which he holds, registered in addition by him at the property-registry on the fourth of the present month Paun[i] as paternal (lots) allotted by him, out of (the division) which he made together with the same brother of his Theo[n] and their other full brothers Dios and Adrastos and another Dios and Eudaimon and Zoïs also called Nemesous and Didyme through the same record-office in the thirteenth year
5     of the deified Trajan, in Ph[a]menoth, a division around Taamire of the Thmoisepho toparchy, from the cleruchy of Alexon, out of which they came into the division from the same cleruchy nineteen arouras, both those formerly of his aunt
6     Zoïs als[o] called Nemesous, as also they were taken up through the division, five arouras being cleruchic and bought into cleruchy, which the acknowledging party declares to be in one parcel, and those formerly of his paternal grandfather Dionysios son of Dios, being two, bought into cleruchy, which likewise the acknowledging party declares to be in one parcel — these together being grain-bearing arable land of rectangular shape,
7     seven arouras, the annual rents of these being Sarapion's; neighbours of the five arouras in one parcel: to the south, on the eastern part Apollonios and Kronios both sons of Adras[tos], and on the western part public land, to the north Demetria daughter of Diogenes also called Mnesitheos, to the east a side-strip, to the west the aforementioned Demetria and others; of the other (arouras)
8     [i]n the other parc[el, of] two arouras, to the south on the eastern part a furrow, and on the western part the aforementioned Demetria and her nephew Sarapion, to the north the aforementioned Apollo[nios an]d Kronios, to the east the said side-strip, to the west the aforementioned Demetria and her nephew Sarapion — so that there may belong to Theon and to those who shall succeed from him the seven arouras ceded to him as aforesaid, together with the other dues and benefits, with full right for all time; and that the acknowledging Sarapion has forthwith received from his brother Theon the agreed (price) for the cession of the same seven arouras, four thousand five hundred drachmas of imperial silver coinage in full, and that the acknowledging party shall transgress nothing of the contract, nor another on his behalf concerning this cession in any way, but he shall also of necessity guarantee to his brother Theon and to those from him the same seven arouras
9     forever g[ua]ranteed against all (claims) with every guarantee and clear from royal and imperial-estate cultivation of [lan]d and every kind (of due) and labour and water-guarding of dykes and from any other whatsoever,
10    likewise forever from public dues and all taxes of the former times up to the present and of the present second year itself of Antoninus Caesar the lord. And if the acknowledging party transgress any of these, let it be void, and let him pay in addition to the ceded-to Theon and to those from him, in respect of whatever kind he transgress, both the damage and a penalty of one thousand silver drachmas and
11    to the public treasury an equal sum, and nonetheless let the agreed terms be valid. Valid is the receipt. In the second year of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus
12    Pius, Pauni 7. Through Chairemon the overseer with others it has been transacted. I, Sarapion who promised with others, have followed along.
