[TRANSLATION]
1   (Sheet.)
2   To Protarchos.
3   From Harpochration son of Kaisar, and from Apollonia daughter of
4   Nanos, with her guardian and surety, her husband Chairemon son of
5   Petesis, and of Chairemon himself. Since Harpochration happens to have given out to
6   [Apollonia his slave infant, a foundling at the breast,]
7   [...] named [Ag]almation, to be reared
8   by Apollonia herself, with her own milk, for the agreed
9   period; but, no
10   security yet having been made about the rearing of the slave infant named Agalmation,
11   it has come
12   about that Apollonia's milk has spoiled; we agree
13   with one another on these terms: Apollonia (acknowledges) that she has been paid in full by
14   Harpochration, both by hand from the house and through the bank, in installments, the monthly
15   ...
16   nursing-fees for the eight months, and that she makes no claim against
17   Harpochration concerning these matters in any way; and ...
18   and
19   Harpochration too (acknowledges) that he has received back from Apollonia the
20   aforesaid slave infant of his, Agalmation, and that he makes no claim either against Apollonia or against
21   Chairemon concerning [all] the matters of the rearing; or, apart from the agreed terms
22   remaining valid, (the transgressor) shall be liable,
23   if he transgresses, both to the damages and to the [fixed penalty]; and Apollonia and
24   Chairemon (are) to pay
25   ...
26   the nursing-fees Apollonia received, with [half as much again]
27   as penalty, and another
28   three hundred silver drachmas, as if by legal judgment;
29   nor (shall claim be made) concerning anything else at all up to the present day, in any
30   way.
31   Year 26 of Caesar, [Ha]thyr 25.
