[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Constantius
2     [an]d Maximian, the most noble Caesars, for the 3rd time.
3     To Aurelia Thermoutharion, daughter of Sextus the legionary, acting without a guardian by the right of children,
4     fro[m] Aurelius Isidoros son of Ptolemaios, his mother being
5     [Heroïs], from the village of Karanis. I wish to lease from you, in partnership for a half share, the olive-grove belonging to you around the same
6     village of Karanis, two arouras
7     or however many they may be, for five years from the crops
8     of the present 16th year and 15th year and 8th year, falling due in the coming year, I giving from
9     my share each year, of olives,
10    one artaba by the dromos measure
11    of four choinikes. And it shall not be permitted to me within the time
12    to abandon the lease in any way,
13    and I shall perform the yearly work, all of it:
14    dry-pruning, trunk-clearing, basin-trenching,
15    the (olive-)shaking being my responsibility, the lessee. And I shall also do the under-digging and the watering, and we shall divide
16    the crops that arise according to the half
17    share, all the public dues being your responsibility,
18    the owner; and after the time I shall hand over the olive-grove furnished with all the … as I also received it,
19    if it seems good to lease. And having been asked the formal question, I gave my assent.
20    [Aurelia] Thermoutharis: I have leased out for
21    [a half share] as aforesaid. Aurelius Mikkalos
22    [wrote] on her behalf, she not knowing letters.
23    In the year 16 and 15 and 8 of our lords Diocletian
24    and Maximian, the Augusti, and (of) Constantius and
25    Maximian, the [most] noble [Caesa]rs,
26    Mecheir 10.
