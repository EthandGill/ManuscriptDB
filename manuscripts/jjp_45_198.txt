[TRANSLATION]
1     Zoilos, secretary of the farmers, to Akousialos, grain collector, greetings. Measure
2     for Kroure of Pnepheros into which he farms royal land
3     of wheat five, it comes to wheat 5. Year 20, Athyr 15.
4     Marres, village secretary; measure the aforesaid wheat
5     five, it comes to wheat 5. Year 20, Athyr 15.
6     [Onn]ophris, leader of the farmers; settle 5 artabas, it comes to 5 artabas.
7     Year 20, Athyr 15.
8     wheat [given] 5
