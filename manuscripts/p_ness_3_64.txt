[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God; Alaarath son of Abd to those from At Ab …
2     of the region of Sycomaze, land of Gaza. Provide quickly Abdella son of Alkama
3     of Bani Saad son of Zeir for 2 months Saraain Rabi for the [remainder]
4     indiction 4 of grain modii two hundred seventy nine,
5     of oil xestai two hundred seventy nine,
6     and for the price of grain modii [two hundred] seventy [nine],
7     of oil xestai two hundred seventy [nine]
8     nomismata eighteen two-thirds. It was written in February in [indiction] [4,]
9     according to Arabs year 56, by hand of George of Maiuma.
10    total: grain modii 279, oil xestai 279 and for the price of grain modii 279 oil xestai 27[9]
11    nomismata 18 two-thirds
12    total: grain modii 279, oil xestai 279 and for the price
13    of grain modii 279, of oil xestai 279 nomismata 18 two-thirds
