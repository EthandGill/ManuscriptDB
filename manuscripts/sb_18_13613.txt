1     To [my lord H]ermēs, the f[a]th[er] of Copres, [greetings]: [before] indeed [all] things [I pray you] to be in good health, and the [obeisance to you I make] according to [each]
2     day[s] by the ancestral gods.
3     Therefore then [I] wrote to you at another time concerning the
4     [having come about] sorrow of all for the sake of the overseer
