[TRANSLATION]
1     To Marcus Caninius the decurion, Pallas the curator [of the praesidium]
2     Rahemai, greetings.
3     I was not diligent about the …
4     Demetrius the camelarius. For I do not [find]
5     where to place them because of the shortage
6     of animals. For the rest, keep them as we find for you and other camels
7     and horses. Do not …
8     Farewell.
