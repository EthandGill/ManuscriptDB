# HGV-EN: Year fifteen of Imperator Caesar Nerva Traianus Augustus [G]ermanicus Dacicus, Payni 2. Paid to Mystharion, tax farmer of the licence on donkeys of Karanis, (by) Aplonous, son of Serapion, mechanarios, for the licence of year 15 for one donkey eight silver drachmas, = 8 drachmas; and in year 16 of Traianus Caesar, the lord, Phamenoth 2 for one donkey eight silver drachmas, = 8 drachmas.
