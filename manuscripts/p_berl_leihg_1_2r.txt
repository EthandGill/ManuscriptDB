[TRANSLATION]
1     And through another (document), whose date is year 8, Tybi 26:
2     to the cattle-breeders of the village of Sobthis of the Herakleopolite nome,
3     for the freight-charges of conveyances, which were declared to have been performed under the former strategos Heliodoros, from the granaries of (the division of) Themistos, of the produce of the 3rd year, 29 artabas of wheat,
4     of the 4th year, 160 artabas of wheat, of the 5th year, 198 artabas of wheat, of the 6th year, 33 artabas of wheat; total, 420 artabas of wheat; and under
5     Ptolemaios, who had become royal scribe of the division of Themistos, having taken over
6     the affairs of the strategia from Pharmouthi of the 4th year until Epeiph,
7     in accordance with the instruction sent
8     to Heliodoros, the one after him, and now brought to account:
9     of the produce of the 2nd year, 81¼ artabas of wheat, of the produce of the 3rd year, 119¼ artabas of wheat; total, 200½ artabas of wheat,
10    total of the instruction, 620½ artabas of wheat;
11    and through another (document), whose date is year 8, Mecheir 11:
12    to the cattle-breeders of the villages of the Kynopolite nome, through
13    Sarapion (of) Heleis, appointed by them,
14    for freight-charges, of which they performed the conveyance under Harpokration,
15    from the granaries of the division of Polemon, of the produce of the 3rd year of Antoninus
16    and Verus the lords, the Emperors, 558½ ⅓ artabas of wheat,
17    total of the instruction, 558½ ⅓ artabas of wheat,
18    total of those carried in the expenditures, 5,626⅔ ⅛ artabas of wheat,
19    remaining on the spot, of administration and domain-land, 1 myriad (10,000) 984½ ½ artabas of wheat,
20    9 artabas of barley, 2,163 artabas of lentils.
21    Year 8 of Antoninus and Verus the lords,
22    Augusti, conquerors of Armenia, Media, Parthia,
23    the Greatest.
