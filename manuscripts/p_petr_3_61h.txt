1     Of (the) 21st year, Hathyr 2, Apollonius acknowledges
2     of Menelaus, Libyan, of the lower ranks
3     to have for fifteen horses fodder
4     and 200, of barley 6, of semolina bread 3 choinices, wine
5     jars 44, of oil jars 2½¼, of wheat bread 36 choinices,
6     and for ten mules, each [barley] 5 choinices, fodder bundles 8,
7     [for] 2 days fodder bundles [160 barley 2½] and for
8     drivers 10, bread [of wheat 2 choinices, for 2 days]
9     40 fodder and for expenditure each [copper obol 1 four-obol piece]
10    [for 2 days 3 two-obol pieces]
