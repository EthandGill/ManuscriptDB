1     [Aurelius] Serenos also called Sarapion, son of Agatheinos
2     [and of his mother T]aposeirias, of Oxyrhynchus city,
3     [to Aureli]us Panesneus son of Ptollis and of his mother An…s, from the village of Seryphis, greeting.
4     [I acknowledge] that I have ceded from now
5     [for] all [time], from what belonged to me by right of purch[ase] around the same Seryphis, from the
6     … allotment, a two-thirds part of one aroura of grain-land
7     [in th]ryois, after which I sold to the sons
8     [of Aurelius S]otion, former gymnasiarch of the same city,
9     [and] … emi (son) of Stephanos, former gymnasiar[ch] of the same city,
10    [a third p]art — the remaining third part formerly
11    [of Katillian]us also called Varus, of which whole (plot) the neighbours on the south
12    … [,] on the north a mound, on the east other private (land),
13    [on the we]st [of you], the (party) to whom (it) is ceded; and the (sums) agre[e]d between one another for the cession-price
14    of the same third [pa]rt, of silver of the Augustan coinage, four hundred drachmas, I have here received
15    [f]rom you on the spot, through [hand] (in cash); and of necessity
16    I will guarant[ee to yo]u and to your descendants [a]nd to those who succeed from you the third of the aroura as [s]ecure
17    from my name and from everyone who shall come against you in my n[a]me, you being content
18    with the guarantee to me of the same aroura's third from my afore-mentioned predecessor in title Katillian[u]s also called Varus, which (guarantee) I will also provide to you,
19    [(it being free) f]rom the public dues paid on its behalf
20    and all kinds of additional assessments up to the
21    pas[t] 6th year and the past 6th year itself, because
22    the (dues) from [t]he present 1st year of this (land) belong
23    to you, the (party) to whom (it) is ceded, against whom also are
24    the public dues of all kinds from the same 1st year. Valid (is)
25    [the] cession, [w]ritten in duplicate, which whenever
26    you choose you shall make public through the registry, not
27    requiring my concurrence, because from now I consent to the future publication by you;
28    and concerning the doing of these things thus rightly
29    and well, questioned by you, I have agreed.
30    The 1st year of Imperator Caesar Gai[u]s Messius
31    Quintu[s] Decius Trajanus, Pius, Felix,
32    Augustus, Choiak 1.
33    [Aur]elius Panesneus son of Ptollis, I have received the ca[ption (deed)] as is set out, and questioned
34    [I have agree]d. Aurelius Theon also called Asklepia[des, surn]amed Zoilos, I wrote on his behalf,
35    [he not knowing l]etters.
