[TRANSLATION]
1:25   …if you are neither the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?
1:26   John answered them: I baptise with water, but among you stands one you do not know,
1:27   even he who comes after me, the strap of whose sandal I am not worthy to untie.
1:28   These things took place in Bethany across the Jordan…
1:33   …the one who sent me to baptise with water said to me: He on whom you see the Spirit descend and remain, this is he who baptises with the Holy Spirit.
1:34   And I have seen and have borne witness that this is the Son of God.
