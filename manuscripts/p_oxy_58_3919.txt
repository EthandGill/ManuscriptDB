[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion to Sarapion his son, greetings.
2     I sent to you through
3     your brother drachmas 16, so that you might complete for me
4     the 16. If it should be possible
5     and to your brother
6     [take] from your mother the same drachmas 16,
7     and I will send
8     to her. If you should take
9     the drachmas 100, give to her.
10    But if not, send
11    to me, so that having gone up I myself will ask. Farewell. … .
