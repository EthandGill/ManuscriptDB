[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aur[elius] Abinas [son] of Hesichius from village B[ubastos, from] Aurelius Aeil Sakaon from village Thrasho. [I wish] to lease from you the lands [belonging to you concern]ing village
2     Bubastos, grain-producing aruras ten, or whatever they are, [toward] remeasurement...
3     measurement by rope in place Capnus for a time...
4     [from] the fortunate 13th indiction ... grain-tax, of each arura wheat one artaba, y[ou the]
5     Abi[nas], providing seeds from advance [and also]
6     b[easts], four-footed [animals], and I, Aeil, will [provide all] the
7     [work of] workers whatever fits, and waterings...
8     [removal] of embankments and public [all fitting], and seeds [and] grain-taxes I carry
9     all from common threshing-floor, and the remaining..., and after
10    the time [I] will hand over [the ar]uras as also I [rece]ived.
11    The lease is valid [and] questioned I agreed.
12    consulship of Flavius Ursus and Flavius Polemai[os] of the
13    illustrious [people], Athyr ... Aurelius Abinas the aforesaid leased as aforesaid. Aurelius Aeil I wrote on behalf of him [being] unable to write and I witnessed [it].
