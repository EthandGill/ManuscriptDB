[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords the emp[erors]
2     Diocletian for the ninth time and Max[imian]
3     for the 8th, Augusti,
4     to the Aurelii Neilos also called Ammonios, gym(nasiarch), [councillor],
5     prytanis in office, and Sarmates and Matrinos, bo[th]
6     gym(nasiarchs), councillors, syndics, all of the illustrious and most illustrious
7     city of the Oxyrhynchites,
8     Aurelius Ammonios son of Kopreus, reader of the former church of the village of Chysis.
9     You having enjoined upon me, in accordance with
10    the things written by Aurelius Athanasios, procurator of the privy purse, as by command of the most distinguished magister of the privy purse Neratius
11    Apollonides, concerning the producing of all
12    the items in the same former church, and I
13    having declared that the [same] church has neither gold nor unstamped (metal)
14    nor silver nor clothing nor quadrupeds nor slaves nor building-plots
15    nor property nor (anything) from gifts
16    nor again from bequests, except only
17    the [bro]nze gate that was found and handed over to the logistes for transport
18    to most illustrious Alexandria in accordance with the things written by our most distinguished governor Clodius
19    Culcianus; and I swear by the Fortune of our lords
20    the emperors Diocletian and Maximian Augusti
21    and Constantius and Maximian the most noble
22    Caesars that these things are thus and that I have in no way lied, or may I be liable to the divine oath.
23    Year 20 and 12 of our lords Diocletian and Maximian
24    Augusti and Constantius and Maximian the most noble Caesars.
25    Mecheir [10.] …
26    I, Aurelius Ammonios, have sworn the oath
27    as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Serenos, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
