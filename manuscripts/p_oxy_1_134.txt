[TRANSLATION]
1   † In the reign of our most divine and most pious
2   master, greatest benefactor,
3   Flavius Justinus the eternal Augustus
4   and Imperator, in the 4th year, in the consulship of
5   their serenity for the 2nd time, Pauni 15,
6   in the 2nd indiction.
7   To Flavius Apion the all-praiseworthy
8   and most exalted, of the ordinary ex-consuls
9   and patrician, landowner also
10   here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,
11   through Menas his servant who also
12   interrogates and procures
13   for his own master, the same all-praiseworthy
14   man, the right of action and liability,
15   (I,) Ioannes head of the
16   stonecutters, son of Menas, of the mother
17   Sara, originating from the hamlet
18   of Nesos Leukadiou of the Oxyrhynchite nome
19   belonging to your exaltedness.
20   I acknowledge that I have received from her (the estate)
21   from now already of gold one solidus
22   by the private standard, that is gold 1 solidus by the private (standard),
23   and in return for this I acknowledge
24   that I will bring to the cistern
25   of her same property
26   at Tarouseb large stones,
27   two hundred, without any postponement
28   whatsoever. Valid is the acknowledgement, drawn up in single
29   copy, and having been asked I gave my assent.
30   (I,) Ioannes, it is agreeable to me. (I,) Ioustos
31   wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
32   Acknowledgement of Ioannes head of the stonecutters, son of Menas, from the hamlet of Nesos
33   L[eu]kadiou, for gold 1 solidus by the private (standard).
