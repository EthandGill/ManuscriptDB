[TRANSLATION]
1     Horion to Apollonios his lord greetings.
2     Before all else I pray that you are well with Alis the lady and Heracles [of] Apollo, whose children you carry upon good fortune, for whom I do not cease the
3     obeisance making beside the lord Hermes. You do not know, lord, how I leased the banquet halls
4     and the [cells] for [a] year one; the year therefore has been completed until [Phar]mouthi. By your health and Hermes the lord, two hundred drachmas I was fined in [them] because of not being in operation
5     them. And Herakleios also knows and sees how it is
6     idle because [by] no one approaching neither for renting nor for … [dining] … … but I do not have favorable opportunity for them. I ask therefore you to write to Herakleios
7     so that he may take from me and [lease] them to another,
8     since it did not seem good to him apart from your judgment
9     to do nothing; for scarcely also I am able to do the work of the bathhouse. Do not therefore, lord, act otherwise; for those places consume one [person]
10    attending to them whole time; [they] have need
11    for of cleanliness. Heraidous greets you.
12    I pray that you are well, lord,
13    and see as it is proposed. Pauni
14    15.
15    To Apollonios the strategos
