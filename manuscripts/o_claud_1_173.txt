[TRANSLATION]
1     Anikeitos and Heraklides to-Soteriches
2     the father greetings. You-know that Neophytus having-taken staters two went-off, and
3     because of this you not I-was-able to-send. I-ask
4     therefore to-receive me
5     until barley-cakes may-come.
6     If but you-doubt being-in-debt I-will-send you.
7     [Fare]well.
