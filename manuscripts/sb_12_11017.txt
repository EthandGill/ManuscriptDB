[TRANSLATION]
1     Mysthes to Ammonios, greetings
2     I write to you concerning the donkeys that if they no longer have to work for the royal service in the Taurine region, having asked Horion the grain-official carefully, the...
3     safe situation having learned, I transferred from there
4     the livestock into the city and going up I [brought] three sacks and three packsaddles and the two spherical objects and the trough and the [trappings] of your donkeys and your choinix; and the...
5     remaining sacks having received from Kastorates and the...
6     packsaddles and whatever else remains, leave in the Taurine region in the house and having locked with the key I brought
7     the key; for I will pay the rent. And if...
8     you have hay or barley, bring them with yourself into...
9     the city; and having received these, bring them through Smetus; but if you have a downriver boat, when going up, send your livestock by boat; but if...
10    [again] you do not have, go up [again]; farewell. Year 42 of Caesar
11    Choiak 9
12    Deliver to Ammonios
