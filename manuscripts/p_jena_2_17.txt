1     [In the consul]ship of Flavius … the most glorious,
2     Pauni … of the 9th indiction.
3     Flavius Taur[i]nos son of John, registered
4     in the unit of the most noble Moors
5     stationed in the (territory) of the Hermoupolites,

7     [and] am in debt to you for …
8     … of annonae … …
9     [ind]ictions, of my debt …

11    approved, three, less [eighteen] carat[ia],

13    to provide to you in three instalments, thus,
14    that is, from the month of Epeiph of the present
15    nin[th] indiction until the fourth …
16    … …
17    without [interest, without de]lay and wit[ho]ut a[ny dispu]te or judgment or lawsuit or pretext-finding,
