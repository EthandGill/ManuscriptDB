[TRANSLATION]
1     Ataieis and Herakles
2     village-chiefs of settlement Leukogion
3     to Sakaon and the colleague village-chiefs of settlement Theadelphia greetings
4     we received from you for
5     provisions of Leukogion for the 12 indiction
6     of fodder bundles simple three hundred
7     become bundles 300
8     to the going-to-be consuls the 4, Thoth 8
9     Ataieis I have sealed/signed
