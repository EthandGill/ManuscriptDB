[TRANSLATION]
1     … [in] Philadelphia of the (division) of Herakleides
2     of the division of the Arsinoite nome. There lent Marcus Petronius Longinus, through his agent Marcus Petronius Valerianus, aged about
3     forty-five, with a scar on his right forearm,
4     to Alexandros son of Heras, aged about … -two,
5     with a scar on his left jaw, and to his wife Tenaus daughter of Purtious, aged about …-ty, with a scar
6     above the right elbow, with as guardian her aforementioned husband, to the two, Persians of the
7     epigone and each other's guarantors, for repayment, sixty-four silver drachmas, at interest of one drachma per mina per month,
8     … as principal, of barley, by the
9     eighth public measure, eight artabas, which
10    let the borrowers repay in the Pauni
11    month of the coming eighth year of Antoninus
12    Caesar the lord, he having the right of execution both against the borrowers and against whichever
13    of them he may choose and against all their belongings
14    by process of law … Apollo(-) Euem(-)
15    Alexandros son of Heras and his wife Tenaus daughter of Purtious,
16    Persians of the epigone and each other's
17    guarantors for repayment,
18    have borrowed from Petronius sixty-four silver drachmas
19    of interest one drachma per mina per
20    month, as principal, of barley by the eighth measure of the public granary eight artabas, and we shall repay in the month
21    Pauni of the coming year as set forth above.
22    I, Apollonios son of Neileus, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
23    I, Marcus Petronius Valerianus, hold back (have received?) all the aforesaid
24    in full.
