r.1    Ἀσκληπιάδει νομάρχηι παρὰ Νεχεμβέους. ἐγλαβόντος μου τὴν ἕκτην τῆς Φιλαδέλφου
r.2    τῆς Ἡρακλείδου μερίδος εἰς τὸ ι ἔτος ἡ ἀκρὶς ἐμπεσοῦσα κατέφθειρεν πάντα, ὅσα δὲ

r.5    ἀντιγραφεῖ καὶ τῶι στρατηγῶι ὅπως διακριθῶ τοῖς κυρίοις τῶν κτημάτων
r.6    ἕως τοῦ Θεόδωρον παραγενέσθαι, ἔστι γὰρ τὸ πλῆθος τοῦ ἀργυρίου οὐκ ὀλίγον, ὅπως
r.7    μηθὲν διαφωνήσηι τούτων καὶ σὺ δὲ συντάξηις καὶ Θεοκλῆι τῶι παρὰ σοῦ

[TRANSLATION]
1     To Asklepiades the nomarch, from Nechembeus. After I took the sixth of Philadelphia
2     of the Herakleid division in the tenth year, the locust having fallen destroyed everything, and as many things as
3     were saved, the masters carried off the sixth without having assessed [it]. I am therefore treated unjustly in this matter.
4     You would do well therefore, if it seems to you, consulting together with Asklepiades and the
5     recorder and the strategist, so that I may be judged with the masters of the properties
6     until Theodore arrives, for the amount of the money is not small, so that
7     nothing may be disputed concerning these things; and you will also order Theokles the [one] from you
8     to hold back the produce of Dion's vineyard which Tisikrates holds near Tana. For this [person]
9     I also appointed formerly to the strategist and it has been registered by him;
10    he wrote to hold back the produce of this property completely, and [it] has been held back
11    until now. I therefore ask you, if it seems to you, to write to hold back … so that nothing may slip away
12    to the king.
13    [Farewell]. Year 11, Pauni 5.
14    To Asklepiades.
