1     Kleon to Zenon, greetings. It would be well if you are well; we too are well. Being [about to]
2     [send] some sacrificial animals [and had] announced [this] to Damis, on account of [that] he himself at night [was about to]
3     [depart] and [did] not have so many as [to be able] to send someone to you, so that Damis would write to you
4     [asking] if [you would] buy [them] for you; but he said [he would] wait until I arrived and [would] buy
5     [the] sacrificial animals for both you and himself together. Having returned, [I purchased them] at [a rate of]
6     drachmas 4½ per animal, [total] 59 sacrificial animals. I have written to you so that you [would] follow along [with this].
7     Farewell. Year 30, Athur 22.
8     [Year 30, Di]os 23, Athur 23.
9     [Kleon …] sacrificial animals.
10    To Zenon.
