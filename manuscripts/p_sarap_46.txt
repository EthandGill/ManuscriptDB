[TRANSLATION]
1     To Eutychides son of Sarapion,
2     from Kastor son of Panechotes, of those from
3     the village of Akoreus,
4     residing in the village of Mnachis. I wish
5     to lease from you for two years from
6     the present 13th year of Hadrian Caesar
7     the lord, of those which you hold on lease
8     from Ulpia Eudaimonis in the
9     Paskos near Mnachis, in two plots, seven arourae, of which one is of four arourae and the other of three
10    arourae, all at a fixed rent,
11    of the four arourae, for the
12    13th year of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
13    each aroura at nine artabae of
14    wheat, and for the next 14th year, each
15    aroura at seven artabae of barley, and of the
16    remaining three arourae, at a rent
17    for the present 13th year of eight
18    artabae of barley each, and for the coming
19    14th year eight artabae of wheat each. And all
20    I shall measure out yearly on your behalf into
21    the public granary by the public
22    receiving-measure, of which I shall also render you the measurement at the first measuring; and I shall give
23    yearly four drachmas as rent,
24    and … slave(s) … in the present year
25    half an artaba of wheat and in the coming year
26    half of barley, the public taxes each year being upon the landholder.
27    In the thirteenth year of the Emperor
28    Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus,
29    Thoth 11. I, Eutychides, have leased as is stated above.
30    … ar … s … epik … of the …
