[TRANSLATION]
1     From Alupius.
2     And at another time indeed I sent to you
3     So that of fodder for me half-dry
4     Care you provide
5     Since after tomorrow
6     I come into the district observing the works with you. I pray you be strong.
7     It has been noted
8     To Heroninus, manager of Thraso
9     Year 7 Phamenoth 12
10    From Alupius to Heroninus, manager of Thraso
