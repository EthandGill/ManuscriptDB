[TRANSLATION]
1     Pausanias to Herakleides his brother, greeting.
2     I think that our brother Sarapammon has informed you
3     for what reason I went down to Alexandria; but I had already written to you
4     earlier about little Pausanias,
5     that he had enlisted in a legion. So when he wished no longer (to be) in a legion but in a cavalry-squadron, learning this I was obliged
6     to go to him, although I did not wish to. Being begged
7     much, then, by his mother and his sister to transfer him to Koptos, having gone down to Alexandria
8     I used many pretexts until he was transferred
9     to the squadron at Koptos. Desiring, then, to come to you
10    on the up-voyage, we were shut off by the
11    leave [grant]ed [to the chi]ld by the most illustrious prefect,
12    an[d for t]hi[s] reason I was not able to come to
13    y[o]u. So, the gods willing, for the
14    [fes]tival [of the] Amesysia I shall try to come to you. So let it be your care, brother, concerning the document of the
15    [m]ortgag[e], so that it be completed as is the custom.
16    I urge yo[u,] then, brother, to write to me about your sa[f]ety, since I heard in Antinoöpolis that among you a plague
17    [oc]curred. So do not neglect (it), so that I too about you may pass my time more cheerfully.
18    Greet warmly my lady mother and [my sis]ter and our unbewitched childr[en. Pausa]nias greets you. I [pray] for your [health] together with your whole household.
19    To the Oxyrhynchite (nome), to Herakl[e]i[des] … d … mn …
20    from Pausanias his brother.
