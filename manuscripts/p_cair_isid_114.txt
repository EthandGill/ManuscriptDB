[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aurelia] Ptolema through me
2     of the husband Aurelius [Ioannes]
3     gymnasiarch Aurelius Isidorus, greetings.
4     I received from you the portion falling to me of the arouras which you cultivated concerning
5     the boundary of Karanides for the produce of the past
6     20th year and 12th year of wheat: four and a half artabas,
7     they amount to 4 1/2 artabas,
8     remaining to me of the
9     account concerning the previous 19th year and 18th year and
10    11th year. Aurelius Ioannes I wrote all.
11    year 21 and 13
12    Hathur 17.
