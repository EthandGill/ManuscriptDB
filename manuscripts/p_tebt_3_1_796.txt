[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 21, Phaophi 11. To Phil[ō]n, chief-guard.
2     From Dionysios and Adam, thirty-aroura holders, and Poseidonios. Some having crossed on the night of the 10th into the 11th of the aforementioned month our house and having come
3     into the entry, while those with us [were sleeping] in the houses with the doors locked, have gone away [having] in the entry a basket of grain in which were
4     barley, half an artaba, of which the value [is] 90 drachmas, and of the basket 20 drachmas, and two torn linen cloths being in
5     the basket worth 60 drachmas, and a sack of goat hair
6     worth 300 drachmas, two sweat-cloths worth 120 drachmas, a four-choinix measure of 50 drachmas, so as to be
7     of the plunder in copper one thousand one [hundred] eighty drachmas,
8     total drachmas ... Since then we happen to have reported concerning these matters
9     on the same day to Hōros, village-chief of the Satyre quarter, we [request], if [to you] it seems good, [to order] [to write] to [those] to whom it [is proper] …
10    through … rou a copy [so that] kat … with you
11    so that it may exist for us in our case against Hōros.
12    Farewell.
