[TRANSLATION]
1     This third letter I have written … to my good and god-protected master
2     and of which one [I sent] through the stable-boy who was carrying to him the jujubes, [and] a second [letter]
3     likewise with Abba Kyros the consecrated [one], and now this [one], as I said,
4     [third] on account of the basket, so that it might be sent to me, and I place it in the
5     … […]. I ask therefore [you] to send this to me through the one delivering
6     [to] him my humble letters, so that I might also acknowledge thanks to him in this.
7     [Having] written this, I do obeisance many times and I greet the footsteps of my master
8     [up] to [your] presence, especially the shared mistress.
9     [From] me [greet] Faustus, the most magnificent common brother, and all those [of the] blessed
10    [your] household, from small to great, [up] to [your] presence.
11    To my own good and god-protected master Theodore, count of the greater [rank], Christopher, your servant and brother.
12    Deliver in Oxyrhynchus to the good Theodore the greater of the illustrious house of Apion.
