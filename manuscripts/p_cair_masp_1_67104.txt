[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of] the Flavii Orestes and Lampadius, the most glorious, Mesore 26, of the ninth indiction.
2     [To Flavia] …, the most magnificent daughter of the most magnificent and most glorious count Ioannes, through Flavius Menas
3     [the respectable] count and procurator, Aurelius Ioannes, son of Hermeios, of mother Rachel, from the village of Aphrodite
4     [of the Antaiopol]ite nome, greeting. I acknowledge that of my own free will and choice I have leased from your magnificence,
5     [through the] aforementioned Menas, respectable count and procurator, for a ten-year term reckoned from the crops
6     [of the auspiciously] coming, with God, tenth indiction, the vineyard plot belonging to her, full of vines, with a complete cistern
7     [and a reservoir] and date-palms and plants and all appurtenances, together with the dry-vine within it and the adjoining
8     [plots of ground and] every right, situated in the plain of the same village of Aphrodite, to the north of the holy place of Apa Victor,
9     [on condition that I] water this plot unceasingly both in winter and in summer, and perform all vine-dressing work from my
10    [own] expenses and animals. And when the time of this same, auspiciously coming with God, tenth indiction arrives
11    [and of the ensuing] time year by year, I shall pay, for the account of fixed rent for each aroura of the vineyard plot now under agreement,
12    [by the measur]ement of the cord, one hundred and twenty jars of wine, of seven xestai each jar, with the empty vessels; and I shall cultivate also the adjoining
13    [plots of ground and t]he dry-vine within, and I shall give the rent according to the terms of the lease of Ioannes the previous cultivator.
14    [And I shall give also t]he rent of the date-palms and the other customary dues according to the terms of the same lease. And I shall raise up into vineyard
15    [with wood]en fences land thus: the one half at the time of the planting of the present ninth indiction, and the other [half]
16    [at the time of t]he planting of the tenth indiction, with God, the material and the expenses being supplied by your
17    [magnificence], and the vineyard being raised up, on condition that its produce be remitted to me indisputably. The lease
18    [is valid and secu]re; and having been formally questioned, I have acknowledged. I, Aurelius Ioannes, son of Hermeios, the aforementioned, have leased
19    [as] stated above.
20    Written through me, Psatos. …
