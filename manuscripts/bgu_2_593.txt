# HGV-EN: Year 21 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius. Work has been done on the dike works of the same year 21, Payni 11 (to) 15, five days in the Epagathiane (canal) for Soknopaiou (Nesos) by (m. 2) Apynchis, son of Stotoetis, the son of Apynchis, mother Tolis. (m. 3) I, Phanias, have signed.
