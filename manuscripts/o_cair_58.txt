[TRANSLATION]
1     Herieus, son of Pamouthes, tax-collector of silver [taxes] of the Memnoneia
2     [To] Psenmouthes, son of Phthomouthes, [grandson of] Psenmouthes. For the poll-tax
3     of the Memnoneia, year 17, one obol, half an obol. Year 17 of Trajan
4     Caesar, our lord, Tybi 28.
5     Year 18, Hathyr 27, poll-tax, half an obol, two chalkoi, likewise
6     for surveying [tax] of the Memnoneia, year 17, two drachmas, [of which] the clear [amount is] one drachma, five obols, half an obol.
