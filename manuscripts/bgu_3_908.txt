[TRANSLATION]
1     [To ...] Messius Audax, centurion,
2     [from ...]...[...] (son) of Apollonios, and
3     A[pol]lon[i]os (son) of Chairas, and A[m]monios (son) of
4     Arabion, and ...arion (son) of Chairas, and Euangelos
5     (son) of Euangelos, and Dios another (son) of Euangelos,
6     and [P]tolemaios (son) of Apollonios, and Ptolem(aios)
7     (son) of Sambas, and Maximos (son) of Apollonios,
8     those from the metropolis. In the present
9     5th year of Trajan Caesar the lord, we were giv-
10    en, by way of (compulsory) service, together with others who are
11    themselves also from the metropolis, for the
12    grain-collectorship (sitologia) of the village of Bacchias. Concerning which,
13    [n]ot staying quiet, we petitioned you,
14    and you permitted us to go down to Alexan-
15    dria ...[...]; but not even so did (it) suffice ...
16    [...]...; we petitioned, at Alexandr[ia],
17    the most excellent prefect Minicius
18    Italus, (and) he referred us up to the
19    court-session (dialogismos) that will be held by him here;
20    but the public grain-collectors (sitologoi) of the village,
21    not ceasing from their harassment against us,
22    without any authority, having come
23    with the village chief-policeman (archephodos) to the
24    houses belonging to us in the village,
25    broke down the doors of some houses, and of some
26    also (sealed) the storerooms under seals, at-
27    tempting to exact from us the arrears(?) of a grain-collectorship
28    which we did not undertake. Therefore we ask, if it seems good to you,
29    to permit them to keep away from us, until
30    [...] the most excellent prefect, arriving in the district, may hear us.
