[TRANSLATION]
1     Sheet (kollema)
2     To Protarchos
3     from Thermion daughter of Apion, with as guardian
4     Apollonios son of Chaireas, and from Apollonios son of Ptolemaios. Thermion
5     and Apollonios son of Ptolemaios consent that they have come together with one another for a partnership of life, and that the same Apollonios son of Ptolemaios has received from Thermion by hand from the household a little dowry: a pair of gold earrings of three quarters (weight) and of silver drachmas … ;
6     that from now on Apollonios son of Ptolemaios
7     shall sup[pl]y to Thermion all that is needful
8     and the clothing, as for a wedded wife,
9     according to the [m]eans of his property, and shall not
10    maltreat her nor cast (her) out nor
11    insult (her), nor bring in another woman,
12    or (else) he shall pay back forthwith the little dowry
13    with one-and-a-[half], the right of execution accruing [to Thermion]
14    both from [hi]mself, Apollonios son of Ptolemaios,
15    [and] from all his property, as if by a legal decision; and that Thér[mion shall] [pre]serve the duties toward her husband and
16    [the com]mon life, justly, and shall not be a[bsent at night] nor absent by day
17    [from th]e house without the consent of Apolloni[os] so[n of P]tolemaios, nor ruin
18    [nor] damage the common household,
19    [nor] consort with [an]other man; or else she herself,
20    having been judged to have done any of these (things),
21    shall be deprived of the little dowry, apart from
22    the transgressor being liable also to the
23    appointed fine.
24    Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 20.
