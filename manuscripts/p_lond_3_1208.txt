[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 17, Mecheir 4.
2     Patous son of
3     Pelaios sold 1 aroura
4     for 1 talent of copper; there bought (it)
5     Takoibis daughter of Patous.
6     In the reign of Ptolemy surnamed Alexander and Berenike the siste[r],
7     the Mother-loving Gods, year 17, under the priests and priestesses and kanephoros of those who are and exi[st],
8     in the month Mecheir 4, in Pathyris, before Ammonios the agoranomos. Patous son of Pelaios, a Pers[ian]
9     of the descent, about 60 years old, well-grown, honey-colored, with smooth hair, long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on the middle of his nose, sold the [land] belonging
10    to him, dry grain-bearing land in Themrauthis of Pathyris, which he had bought from Petearsemtheus son
11    of Panebchounis and Psennesis his brother, one aroura, neighbors of the whole land: (on the) sou[th]
12    the land of Panebchounis son of Teos, (on the) east the river, (on the) west the land of the populace, or whoever the neighbors may be
13    on all sides. There bought (it) Takoibis daughter of Patous, a Persian woman, about 30 years old, of medium (height), honey-colored, somewhat broken (in appearance?), long-fa[ced],
14    straight-nosed, with her guardian Petearsemtheus son of Pakoibis, priest of Souchos and Aphrodite,
15    for one talent of copper coinage. The seller and guarantor of all matters concerning
16    this sale (is) Patous the seller, whom Takoibis
17    the buyer accepted.
18    I, Ammonios, have transacted (this).
