[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulate of Flavius Florentius and
2     Dionysius the most illustrious, Hathyr 20.
3     To Flavius Taurinus [son] of Plusammon, biarch
4     of the numeri of the Mauri [of] Hermoupolis the most illustrious
5     from Aurelius Dyskolos … [and] … mother Helen
6     from the village Temenkyrkeios of the same nome. I wish
7     willingly and of my own accord to lease from
8     you for whatever time you wish, reckoned from the crops
9     of the fortunately entering 15 … [your] existing arourae one [being] from [the] south of the … vineyard agency [of the] same [village,] from [the] west …
