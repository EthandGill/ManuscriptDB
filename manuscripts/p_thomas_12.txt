[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy.
2     From Dioskoros, village-scribe of Ision Panga and of other villages of the upper toparchy:
3     of the instruction sent (to me) concerning the demonstration
4     of 1½ arouras, the copy is set out below; and it is as follows:
5     Herakleides, royal scribe of the Oxyrhynchite (nome), to the village-scribe of Ision Panga [and of the other villages, greeting.]
6     Of the petitions given to me [by] Z[enarion daughter of Hierax, requesting]
7     that there be demonstrated (to her) (the land) which she bought and which was confirmed to her in the [7th year] from the unproductive land, the equivalent (copy) is sent to you, so that, having gone to the indicated
8     plot and having examined whether the boundaries agree with the disposition on the ground, and that nothing has previously been laid claim to and nothing is contrary,
9     you may demonstrate (it) as is fitting and report, as the responsibility will rest with you
10    [if] anything be done improperly. I have signed off.
11    Extract from the register of (properties) confirmed in the 8th year of the Aurelii
12    Antoninus and Verus, the lords Emperors,
13    under Claudius Demetrios also called Hermias, strategos. Upper toparchy,
14    Ision Panga … Zenarion daughter of Hierax son of Zenon, with (her) guardian
15    her father …, of the unproductive land … from the (holding) of Herakleides, from the bed(-land) of 28⅔ arouras
16    trans…, 4½ arouras; from the remaining 24¼ arouras, ½ aroura, of which the neighbors: on the south
17    and on the east, the unproductive land of the bed(-land); on the north, land bought; on the west, a strip;
18    and from the same bed(-land) another aroura, of which the neighbors on every side are unproductive land, tax-free for three years from the time of the confirmation; makes 1½ arouras.
19    7th year, Mecheir 11: I confirmed (it) to the aforewritten (woman); of the price of the produce …
20    … [an]d of others, per aroura, 4 drachmas, and of all the following …
21    … through …
