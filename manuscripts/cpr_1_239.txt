[TRANSLATION]
1     To Theon, also called Philosarapis, strategos
2     of the Arsinohite nome, Heracleides division
3     from Posis, [son] of Mataeus, and Syros, [son] of Paouites
4     the two from the village of Soknopaiou
5     Nesou. I wish to lease from reclaimed dry beach land around the aforementioned village five arouras
6     for one year and I will pay for each
7     aroura at two artabas of wheat, if
8     something unwatered or underwater should occur
9     it will be accepted for us from the revenues
10    year 20 of Marcus Aurelius [Severus] Antoninus Parthicus
11    the Great and Publius Septimius Geta Britannicus
12    the Great, Pious, August, Mecheir 5
