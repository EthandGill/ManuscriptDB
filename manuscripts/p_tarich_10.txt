1     [To the] director Argeios from Amenneus son of Horus and Onnophrius son of Teotos
2     salt-fish dealers of those [from] Tanais. Psenepmutus son of Paotus
3     salt-fish dealer of those [in Philadelphia] of the Arsinoeite nome
4     having been taken up of the [property] because having been removed
5     him among the rebels [with] relative none
6     having belonged to him, [still] still belonging to him unsold
7     privilege in the [salt-fish] factories, as was customary,
8     still even now this [is] unsold. [this] but the privilege produces fruit
9     Abykhis and K[elekh]on and Pasis salt-fish dealers
10    [of] the same village from the second year. We undertake then to arrange this forthwith to the royal treasury bronze talent one [which] we will credit
11    [upon the] royal bank, if it seems good to you to ratify [this] for us
12    [upon these] terms. Farewell.
