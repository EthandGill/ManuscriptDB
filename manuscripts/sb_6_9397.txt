[TRANSLATION]
1     through the present [letter] of mine I write venerating the God-guarded of-you God-love. Know therefore the your
2     God-lov[ing-ness], that I went out unto visiting of the common of-you sister and believe, until now [she] not has had discord.
3     and I heard, that the philanthropic God visited … [gap] …
4     I beseech therefore the your God-love to show-love to him from me and, lest [he/she] seek the misery [of] mine,
5     [de]fend to him; after God for other protection not I-have, if not the your God-love. I beseech therefore
6     and this; to go you unto the God-guarded Kyra and to defend to her on-behalf-of me; lest therefore
7     need may-become of response of the deacon Danielios, I beseech therefore to come [to] him. The lord Jesus Christ
8     may save and may guard the your God-love for longest time.
9     to the God-guarded of-me brother Jo[ann]is most-God-loving archdeacon Basil[ei]a
