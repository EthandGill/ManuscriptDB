[TRANSLATION]
1     from Alypius.
2     Forty animals I sent, in order that one hundred
3     artabas you [might] send up, from which
4     [he] haggled through
5     you my helper
6     Philippus wheat artabas 200
7     [to] be given to Eudaimon,
8     of which [he] will give account; and
9     the other therefore wheat artabas 100
10    keep ready,
11    for tomorrow animals
12    I will send for them. I wish you to be well.
13    To Heronaeus [the] manager
14    Thrasos.
15    Year 13 Thoth 29.
