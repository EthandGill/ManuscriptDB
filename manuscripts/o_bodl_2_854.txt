[TRANSLATION]
1     A copy of a receipt which I issued at another time.
2     Gaios Longinos Valerianus and Kointos [Nemonius Dioskoros]
3     tax collectors of silver-tax, South, to Petehensebai, [of] Petehespochratou.
4     we received on behalf of watchtowers and doubles and harbor-fees, 20th year, South,
5     six drachmas, 6 obols, half-obol, 2 chalkoi, [which] amounts to 6 drachmas, 6 obols, 1/2 [obol], 2 chalkoi. Year 20
6     of Hadrian, the lord, Mesore 20.
7     Amonios, I have signed.
