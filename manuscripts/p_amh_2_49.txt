1     … [let them res]tore [into the house]
2     at their own expense. And if [they do not] give back as it is set forth, let them pay in the following mon[th] one-and-a-half
3     immediately, and the execution shall be
4     against Philoumenia both from the borrowed [funds] from one and each and from which
5     she chooses and from the [property] existing of them
6     all, executing as if from a judgment.
7     Apollonios has made [this arrangement].
