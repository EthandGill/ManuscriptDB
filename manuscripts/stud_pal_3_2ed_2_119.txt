[TRANSLATION]
1     I have, I Papnouthios, overseer of saddle-makers of this [city],
2     of the city of the Arsinoites, to you Horion, trustworthy son of Apollo,
3     from the estate of Phanameth for the distribution of the present
4     fourteenth indiction—saddles of camels nine,
5     saddles of camels 9, and I am ready to pay these
6     for your distribution. It was written in the month of Pauni, [day] 21 of the same [indiction]
7     14th indiction through me Sergios, the notary.
