[TRANSLATION]
1     Year twentieth of Lucius
2     Aurelius Commodus Augustus
3     Mesore, tenth. It has been written to [Apion]
4     and to Anubion, also called Colosius,
5     to collectors of money-tax of the third and
6     fifteenth districts
7     of Memphis, Erieüs of [Anubion],
8     on behalf of inhabitants registered to him for [poll-tax]
9     and guard-tax of the same [year],
10    altogether four hundred drachmas, totaling four hundred drachmas
