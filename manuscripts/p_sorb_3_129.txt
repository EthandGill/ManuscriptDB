[TRANSLATION]
1     Demetrius to Diophanes, greetings. Epistrates, a descend[ant],
2     of those dwelling in Muchis brought me a petition against the farmers concerning burning
3     in which you authorize [me] to settle them, preferably indeed, if not, to send them to you so that they might be judged before the proper tribunal; having summoned the farmers dwelling in the village, I read to them the
4     petition; but since they were unwilling to settle before me, I have sent them to you. I have written to you therefore so that you might know.
5     [Far]ewell. Year 3, Pachon 2.
6     [To] Diophanes.
