[TRANSLATION]
1     Ploution son of Ploution and all his brothers, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites, have leased
2     to Apolinarios son of Ploutogenes
3     and to Gaius son of Paesis, both
4     from the same city, for
5     two years from the present 11th year,
6     from the property belonging to them
7     near Seryphis in the holding of Ammonas,
8     one aroura, on condition that
9     the lessees, in the present
10    11th year, shall plant it as green-crop
11    at a fixed rent
12    of sixty-four drachmas of silver, and in the coming
13    12th year shall sow it with wheat at a fixed
14    produce-rent of eight artabas
15    of wheat, all free from risk
16    of every risk, the yearly
17    public taxes on the land being the responsibility of the lessors, who shall also have ownership of the
18    crops until the yearly
19    dues are received.
20    And if in the coming year any (land)
21    becomes unwatered, it shall be allowed to the lessees.
22    The lease being guaranteed, the lessees shall pay to the lessors
23    the yearly dues in the month Payni, and the wheat of the coming 12th year at the threshing-floor of Seryphis, new, clean, unadulterated, free of clods, free of barley, sifted, as for the public granary,
24    measured by the four-choenix receiving-measure of Kapparas, those on behalf of
25    the lessors measuring it out for them; and let the right of execution belong (to the lessors) against the lessees, who are jointly liable for payment, and against one
26    and whichever of them they choose
27    and against all their
28    property. The lease is valid.
29    Year 11 of the Imperatores Caesares
30    Lucius Septimius Severus Pius
31    Pertinax, victor over Arabia, over Adiabene,
32    over Parthia, Greatest, and Marcus Aurelius
33    Antoninus Pius, Augusti,
34    …, Phaophi 20.
35    Apolinarios son of Ploutogenes
36    and Gaius son of Paesis, we have leased the land on a joint-liability basis as aforesaid. Pasion
37    son of Paesis also called Isidoros, I wrote on their behalf, they not knowing
38    letters.
