[TRANSLATION]
1     145
2     [Flavius Pa]niskos also called Longos,
3     strategos of the Hermopolite (nome).
4     Of the nomination given to me by
5     the village-chiefs of Poampimenis, presenting the man enrolled by it as toparch of the
6     toparchy, a public copy
7     is posted up, so that all may know and the one nominated may take up the office entrusted
8     to him soundly and faithfully. I have signed.
9     Year 12 of our lord Gallienus Augustus, [Pauni]
10    5.
11    To Flavius Paniskos also called Longos, strategos [of the Hermopolite (nome)],
12    from the Aurelii Hermapollon …
13    his mother being Teeus, aged about 48, with a scar on the right calf, and Kouphio…
14    his mother being Tachonsis, aged about 41, [with a scar]
15    on the right shin, both of them village-chiefs of the village of Paompimenis.
16    We give and nominate the undersigned toparch, on condition that he
17    discharge blamelessly all matters pertaining to the office, being
18    well-resourced and suitable, at the risk
19    of us and of all those residing in the same village by mutual surety, whom we also guarantee.
20    He is as follows:
21    Chonsis son of Pathotes, his mother being Th…,
22    aged about 45, having a property of 2000 drachmas.
23    Farewell.
24    Year 12 of Imperator Caesar Publius
25    Licinius Gallienus Germanicus Maximus
26    Pius Felix Augustus, Pauni 4.
27    (We,) the aforesaid village-chiefs, have presented (this). (I,) Aurelios Hermesion, wrote on their behalf, as they do not know letters.
28    (I,) [Aurelios Ser]enos, public assistant,
29    having posted (it) up, registered (it).
30    … Pauni
31    … I have signed.
