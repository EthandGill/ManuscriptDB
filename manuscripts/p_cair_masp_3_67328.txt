310   To the most estimable Apollos, riparius of the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, from Aurelius Victor son of Makarios, of the mother Maria,
311   shepherd from the same village of the same nome, greetings. I acknowledge willingly and
312   voluntarily, swearing by God Almighty and the
313   divine and heavenly fortune of the all-conquering master
314   our Flavius Justin the eternal Augustus Emperor,
315   that I guarantee and have undertaken for the staying and appearance Aurelius
316   Menas son of Psais, shepherd and field-guard of the decania …,
317   for a yearly period, reckoned from today, the
318   aforewritten day, which is the 20th of the month Epeiph of the present
319   fifteenth indiction, on condition that he remain and attend and serve all the public needs, and the guarding of peaceful matters make without delay and
320   irreproachably, and in no way that he be absent. And if
321   he should be absent, and being sought not [be found], I will bring and hand over
322   outside the holy precincts and divine effigies and the holy
323   Lord's day in a public place, I will provide, I the aforewritten
324   guarantor, on behalf of his absence, six gold nomismata.
325   And when the time is completed, on condition that I take back this
326   my guarantee, that is, remaining with her, void
327   it be and invalid wherever produced, or may I be liable to the divine oath and to the danger hanging over this matter. And
328   having been questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius Victor son of Makarios
329   the aforesaid I guarantee the aforesaid Menas [and I swore]
330   the divine oath as above. Aurelius K…
331   son of Mousaios, having been requested, I wrote on his behalf letters, he not knowing. Josephis son of Abraamios presbyter
332   I bear witness to the guarantee, having heard it from the one who made it. Phoibammon son of Psais presbyter I bear witness to the guarantee
333   having heard it from the one who made it.
