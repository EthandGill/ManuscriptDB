[TRANSLATION]
1   Chaireas to Tyrannos his dearest,
2   very many greetings.
3   [The] statement of the 12th year of Tiberius
4   Caesar Augustus, in grain and
5   in silver, write up at once,
6   since Severus has charged me
7   with the collection; and I have already writ-
8   ten [you] to act the man and to collect
9   until I arrive in good health.
10   Do [not] neglect it, then, and make ready
11   also those from the [.. year] up to the 11th year
12   [for th]e collection, in grain and
13   [in silver].
14   Farewell.
15   To Tyrannos, dioiketes.
