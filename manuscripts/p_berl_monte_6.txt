[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy also called Alexander, god Philometor, and of Queen
2     Berenike, his sister, goddess Philadelphos, in the sixteenth year, when the priest who is in Alexandria is the priest of Alexander and of those recorded in Alexandria, in the month
3     Audnaios = Hathyr, on the sixth, in the village of Tanaso of the Herakleopolite
4     nome. Philotas son of Herakleides, Persian of the epigone,
5     and his wife Agathokleia also called Tnephoros, daughter of Nechthatymis,
6     a Persian woman, with her guardian, her own husband, acknowledge to Dionysios son of Herakleides,
7     an Argive, successor to the paternal holding, that they will remain in the vineyard belonging to him, tending the vines and irrigating, from Mecheir
8     the new moon until Thoth the thirtieth of the seventeenth
9     year, neither absenting themselves from the said vineyard,
10    and they acknowledge that they take in advance, toward the threshing-floor produce to be constituted …, the threshing-rent
11    of six artabas of wheat. The vine-working tasks … they shall perform
12    as is fitting. And for whatever day they default they shall pay
13    for each day five hundred drachmas of copper, receiving … and …
14    … the threshing-rent, as much as Herakleios also gives to the …
15    vine-worker; but if in all matters and as to the vine…
16    they shall pay
17    five copper talents [and the damage and]
18    to the royal treasury twenty drachmas of consecrated silver. They are guarantors of [one another]
19    for payment, and let the right of execution belong to Dionysios [and] those [acting for him]
20    both against themselves and against their belongings. Let the contract be valid. Witnesses: Nysios, Sotas, Archonas, … nem…
21    Dionysios, the five required (witnesses), and Pnepheros …
22    keeper of the contract: Mithrodates.
23    I, Herakleides, have transacted the business. Year 16, Hathyr 6.
24    Philotas son of Herakleides, Persian of the epigone, and his wife Agathokleia also called Tnephoros, daughter of Nechthatymis, a Persian woman, with her guardian, her own
25    husband, acknowledge that we will remain tending the vines and irrigating
26    from the new moon of Mecheir until Thoth the thirtieth of the seventeenth year, and we take in advance, toward the threshing-rent, six [artabas] of wheat
27    and we will do as has been written above, and we have given the valid (copy) to Mithrodates. … Ptolemaios … teiou … [I wrote]
28    concerning them, having been asked, because they say that they do not know letters.
29    I, Mithrodates son of Temarchides, have received the valid (copy).
30    Acknowledgement of Philotas.
31    Contract.
