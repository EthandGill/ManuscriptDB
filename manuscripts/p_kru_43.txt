8     now, of the 2nd indiction, we pleaded with one another before
9     the most reputable comes, son of Chael the administrator,
10    concerning the whole inheritance of our blessed father
11    Kosma — in houses, in lands, in clothing of every kind, from
12    a valuable item down to a cheap item,
13    that we might divide them among ourselves, that which falls to each one

15    according to how we were persuaded, he commanded us; we chose
16    free men in the fort; they cast them
17    into the houses and the other lands of our blessed father
18    Kosma; they mediated them for us with the fear
19    of God; they cast a lot up; the lot
20    came up upon you, you sisters, whom we mentioned before
21    above. You took possession of these portions
22    that are in the house in which your large (room) is, the one that is on the street of the children of Konon; you took possession of all that is in [this lower part]
23    of that house, with the (room) of the daughter,
24    namely: the side of the gateway and the cistern and the disturbed-area
25    they became held in common — two-thirds to the male
26    among them, one-third to the female; and again you took possession of the portions in the house in the (place of) Setioh Kelm,
27    namely these — you took possession of the exedra in the lower part,
28    in the west, the one that is up … p … o … the storehouse
29    that is in the front-part with the daughter, all [of it], that is in this east,
30    with the basement in the south, those in the lower-part
31    of the front-part with the disturbed-area and the cistern; they became
32    held in common, that is two to the male among them
33    one to the female; so that it is to you, whom we mentioned before above, that you shall come in and take possession,
34    and that you shall become lord, and that you shall become master
35    of these portions of houses which we mentioned before, having revealed them to you;
36    the boundary of the house that is on the street of the children of Konon, these:
37    south the public street, and the door of the masters,
38    north the saint George, west the children of Japjipe,

42    Thirhire … [the door] of the masters; their boundaries

44    of houses … which I revealed to you, according to the … im … s
45    [which I] revealed before to you, when I begat them for you;
46    in [your] security … of …

48    it (?), in every manner that is in your (hands), in every possession
