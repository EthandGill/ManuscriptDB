[TRANSLATION]
1     Tauron, collector of money-taxes of Charax, to Pikos the younger, (son of) Archis.
2     I have received for the doubles (dipla) of year 11, one base drachma, total base drachma 1. Year 11
3     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Epeiph 2. I, …, have signed.
4     Others, Thoth 22 of year 12, in the name of the same, for the assessment of year 11,
5     one base drachma, total base drachma 1. I, P…, have signed.
