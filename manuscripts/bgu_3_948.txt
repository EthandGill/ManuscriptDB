[TRANSLATION]
1     ΧΜΓ ΧΜΓ ΧΜΓ
2     To my son Theodoulos, from your mother Kophae-
3     na and Zenon, greeting. Before all things I pray
4     to God the Almighty concerning your health
5     and your well-being, greeting. I want you to know that the
6     agent told you that 'your mother Kophaena
7     is ill'; behold, (it is) thirteen months, and not even a letter
8     have you dared to write to me, though you yourself know
9     that I did better for you than for the ...,
10    and you did not dare — having heard that
11    I am ill, you did not dare to send me even a little,
12    anything at all. Be willing, then, to send me ten po-
13    unds of linen (yarn), and I will make you clothes as best I
14    can, for I have nothing with which to provide for you. Be willing, then,
15    to make me a small provision (of food), and I will send
16    you ...; (she) greets you,
17    your mother — both I, Kophena, and your son
18    Zeno[n, and] your sister Kyrilla and her chil-
19    dren. Be willing then, my son Theodoulos, to buy
20    me 6 pounds of black wool, that I may make myself a ma-
21    phorion (veil), and I will send you the money, whatever they cost.
22    I pray for your health for many years. (verso)
23    Deliver this (letter) to Theodoulos
24    from Kophaena.
