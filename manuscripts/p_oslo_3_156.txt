[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammonius to Theon the teacher
2     greetings.
3     Before all things I pray [that] you are well
4     together with your [people] and [you] permit to me
5     concerning what you wish. The price to me
6     of the pottery vessel make known, in order that through
7     the [one] receiving [it] I send. Receive through Achilles the camel-driver Harpalus' basket of grapes, which alone he carried.
8     Be well.
9     To Theon the teacher of Tatoe[is].
