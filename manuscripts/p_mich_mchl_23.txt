[TRANSLATION]
1     Nilos to Nemesion
2     to the brother [greetings].
3     I rejoiced greatly hearing [that]
4     you were released. Know the
5     woman the in Kallonios'
6     to have come to Karanida
7     and I was asked [by] friends. Therefore
8     I write to you so that by hand her
9     you might have. Unshaken her you will make.
10    Do not therefore do otherwise.
11    Be well.
12    Year 12, in the month Augustus, 5
13    Deliver to Nemesion
