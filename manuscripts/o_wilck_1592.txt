[TRANSLATION]
1     Measure of the treasury of the villages, produce of the 3rd year
2     of Marcus Antonius Gordianus
3     Pius, Fortunate, Augustus
4     Thoth 15, in the name Kamétios [son] of Archios
5     Psena, on behalf of the South, wheat, three and a half artabas
6     amounts to wheat 3 and a half. Aurelius Nilus, I have signed.
