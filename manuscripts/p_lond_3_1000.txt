[TRANSLATION]
1     … iano, the most [illustrious] and …
2     From Flavius Georgius Silbanos of the same city.
3     [The following] subjects setting out from this
4     [fair] city owing me clear debt and
5     repeatedly reminded by me, were unwilling to show [goodwill] toward me. Wherefore
6     [compiling] these my petitions I submit
7     [to] your magnificence, asking that they be brought [to trial] and
8     [be] compelled to furnish their replies to me according to law
9     to provide. Flavius Georgius Silbanos aforesaid
10    I have submitted as mentioned.
11    It is as follows:
12    Phibe, called Patao, of Hermoupolis, being indicated.
13    [In the] consulship of Flavius Iohannes, the most glorious and most distinguished
14    Choiak 2, indiction 2
