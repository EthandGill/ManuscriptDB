[TRANSLATION]
1     Andronicus, the priest and chief justice, to the
2     strategos of the division of Heracleides of the Arsinoite nome,
3     greeting. Of the memorandum that has been submitted
4     let a copy be transmitted, as is fitting, in the presence (of the parties).
5     Year nine of Imperator Caesar Trajanus
6     Hadrianus Augustus, Tubi 17.
7     To Andronicus, neokoros of the great Sarapis, of those who are fed in the
8     Museum free of charge, having become strategos of the city and antexegetes, priest and chief justice and in charge of the supervision
9     of the chrematistai and the other courts,
10    from Heron son of Ischyrion. Of the order issued to me
11    a copy is appended below.
12    Philippus son of Aphrodisius to Ptolemaeus the banker, greeting.
13    Pay out, on the thirtieth of the month Pauni
14    of the present year, to Heron son of Ischyrion the same sum
15    of which I have made use … [in the … year of Hadrianus] the lord,
16    two hundred and forty-eight drachmas of silver,
17    total two hundred and forty-eight drachmas.
18    Year six of Imperator Caesar Trajanus
19    Hadrianus Augustus, on the twentieth of Choiak.
20    This being so, and no repayment having been made, but Philippus having died, leaving as heir
21    his son Aphrodisius, I wish the
22    authentic order to be placed in the public record-office, and I request that you take up
23    it, subscribed by the man dispatched by me, Ptolemaeus son of Epimachus, also
24    called Hermias son of Didymus, of the Sosicosmian tribe also
25    called Althaean, to the effect that it is in the handwriting of Philippus,
26    and register it together with this memorandum, and write
27    to the strategos of the division of Heracleides of the Arsinoite nome to deliver a copy of it to Aphrodisius in the presence of two friends, so that he may know that the order
28    has been placed in the public record, and may make to me
29    the repayment.
30    As is fitting. Year nine
31    of Imperator Caesar Trajanus
32    Hadrianus Augustus, Tubi 17.
33    I have signed.
