[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Didymus son of Didymus, superintendent of the district
2     Aurelius Didymus, legal expert of the nome, greeting.
3     Since camel-drivers are being sought, camels are being sent to the sacred palace in Memphis city,
4     [necessarily] I am writing to you, dearest, so that
5     [those] from village Thee according to the list you provide
6     a camel-driver you make.
7     In the consulship of [our lords]
8     [Valerius] Licinnianus
9     Licin[nius] August[us and] Flavius Valerius Constantine
10    [son] of king[s], on the fifteenth before the Kalends of May.
11    Aurelius Didymus, legal expert of the nome, submitted this.
