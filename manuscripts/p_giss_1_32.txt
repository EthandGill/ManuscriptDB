[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year 29 of the Emperor Caesar] Marcus Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Pius Felix Augustus Armeniacus Medicus
2     [Parthicus Sarmaticus Germanicus] Maximus Britannicus Hadrianus: discharge through the bank of Hermaios and his partners, the bankers, for Eudaimonis daughter of Demetrios,
3     [grandson of Eudaimon, her mother being Aphrodisia also called Tereu]s daughter of Leon, of the city of Hermopolis, in the Apeliotes quarter, with her guardian her brother Apollonios also called Eudaimon, of the first ancestors, to the heirs of Theon [also called Eudaimon son of Herodes,] former [gym]nasiarch of the city of Hermes and as he was styled, according to the shares falling to each, which she owed them according to the bank's deed of payment in the month Pharmouthi
4     [of the 22nd year of Commodus the lord, of principal of silver 1500 drachmas, and the] accruing interest thereon of four obols of silver for each mina per month, each month from the aforesaid month Pharmouthi
5     [until Hathyr and Hathyr itself of the present 2nd] … [year], of silver 800, the total amounting to 2300 of silver, for the release of what she had mortgaged to her father himself according to the same deed of payment in the Patemites Lower
6     [near Peentalis from the Pleisthenes] holding, of 6 arouras, of which of catoecic land 4¾¼ arouras, and of land of the area about the courtyard 1¼ aroura; but to the deed of payment a seizure of pledges did not follow. I, Hermaios, have signed.
7     [The heirs of Theon] also called Eudaimon son of Herodes, former gymnasiarch of Hermes the great city and as he was styled, [have followed] this discharge and have received from Eudaimonis daughter of Demetrios, grandson of Eudaimon, [her mother being Aphrodisia also] called Tereus daughter of Leon, of the same city, registered at the Apeliotes quarter, with her guardian her brother Apollo[nios also called Eudaimon, according to] the shares falling to each, the sums owed to our deceased father by her according to the bank's [deed of payment in the month] Pharmouthi of the 22nd year of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar the lord,
8     [of prin]cipal of silver one thousand five hundred drachmas, and the accruing interest thereon of four obols of silver,
9     [for each] mina per month, each month from the aforesaid month Pharmouthi until Hathyr and Hathyr itself of the present
10    … [29th year of Aureli]us Commod[us A]ntoninus Caesar the lord, eight hundred drachmas, the total amounting to two thousand three hundred drachmas of silver, for the release of what the same Eudaimonis had mortgaged to our same father according to the same
11    [deed of payment in the P]atemites Lower near Peentalis from the Pleisthenes holding, of six arouras, of which of catoecic [land four and a] half quarter arouras, and of land of the area about the courtyard one quarter aroura; but to the deed of payment a seizure of pledges did not follow.
