[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolemy, greeting] … -enes, a Persian of the Epigone. I am being wronged by
2     … [and by] … his (wife?) Theudote, who dwell in Pelusium … Mesore. For when we both had been living
3     … with them, and in the aforementioned year
4     … (she) having ended her life, and there being among my possessions household goods … a reaping-sickle whose value is 2 drachmas, an axe [whose value is] 2 drachmas 3 obols,
5     … full of wool whose value is 5 drachmas, a chest
6     … and of bronze coinage 20 drachmas, which I exhibited … with these things I went off to Bacchias in order to report … the ending of her life; but after this, when I had returned … despising me because I am a foreigner … [to defraud me]. I beg you therefore, O king, not to overlook [me who am being wronged, but to command Dio]phanes the strategos to write to Menander
7     [the epistates to compel them to do me jus]tice. For when this is done,
8     [through you, O king, the common benefac]tor of all, I shall obtain [justice].
9     [Farewell].
10    [To Menander. By all means settle them; but if not, send them to us from the 10th of Choiak, so that
11    they may be judged] before the proper tribunal.
12    [Year] 4, Daisios 26, Hath[yr 28].
