[TRANSLATION]
1   (Soknopaiou Nesos: camels [4].)
2   [To So-and-so,] to Herminos [...,]
3   through Pappos, former gymnasiarch, depu[ty],
4   and to Serenos, royal scribe of the same (division),
5   through Eudaimon, deputy,
6   from Tesenouphis son of Teseno-
7   uphis, elder, called
8   Seiphon, and Pakysis son
9   of Tesenouphis, younger, called
10   Kiales, both from the village
11   of Soknopaiou Nesos, through the manager
12   Tesenouphis son of Tesenouphis.
13   The camels I registered in the past 6th year,
14   4, I register also for the pres[ent]
15   7th year. Wherefore I submit (this).
16   Registered before the strategos: camels 4.
17   Mecheir 2. Registered before the royal (scribe):
18   camels 4. Mecheir 3.
19   Counted, in agreement.
20   Counted, in agreement.
