r.2    κεφαλαιωτῇ Πανευεί. Πέτρον Παύλου,
r.3    ὃν συν[έ]λα[β]ες ἐν ἐποικίῳ Πεγουλίου Ἀφθονί[ο]υ, σπούδασον

[TRANSLATION]
1     From Eulogios the riparios
2     To the chief Paneues. Petron, [son] of Paulos,
3     Whom you arrested in the dependent settlement of Pegoulios Aphthonios, make haste
4     At this hour to bring [him] before the city. Farewell.
