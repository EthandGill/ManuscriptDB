[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship [of our lords Con]stantius and Maximian, the most [illustrious] Caesars.
2     The Aurelii, P[tolemis also called Sar]mates, exegetes and councillor of the splendid and most splendid (city) of the Oxyrhynchites
3     and [Demetrammo]n son of Patermouthios, whose mother was Aristous, from the same
4     city, acknowledge to one another, Ptolemis also called Sarmates, chaff-conveyor, together with another,
5     that he has agreed [that] the district under him is (assigned) [to] Demetrammon for the receiving of the chaff
6     and its transport up to the Thebaid and its distribution there to
7     the horses of the [most noble] soldiers; and Demetrammon (agrees) to discharge blamelessly [on behalf of] Ptolemis also called Sarmates the district of the aforesaid [receiving and] transport and distribution of the chaff with all faith [and care], at a monthly wage of the amounts agreed
8     between one another for each month, of four thousand drachmas of silver;
9     and from this Demetrammon acknowledges that he has received and been paid by Ptolemis also called Sarmates for one month four thousand drachmas of silver,
10    and that for the remaining coming months until the delivery he will receive salaries as has been stated for each month, and further
11    also, for the whole time, as extras, three jars of wine and one jar of vinegar
12    and two artabas of bread, which too he acknowledges to have received; and both
13    consent to these things, and Ptolemis also called Sarmates (agrees) to pay
14    for the coming months the remaining salaries, and Demetrammon (agrees) to discharge
15    the service and to produce receipt-documents of the delivery, the coming
16    deficiencies being (chargeable) to Ptolemis also called Sarmates.
17    The agreement is valid, written in duplicate so that each party may have a single copy, and, having questioned one another, they gave their mutual assent.
18    In the 11th and 10th year of our lords Diocletian and Maximian Augusti,
19    and the 3rd year of our lords Constantius and Maximian, the
20    most illustrious Caesars-Augusti, Hathyr 23.
21    I, Aurelius Ptolemis also called Sarmates, consent to all the aforesaid
22    and … the distribution up to the Thebaid …
