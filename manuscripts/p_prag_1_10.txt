[TRANSLATION]
1     [Flavius] … Apollonius, most distinguished prefect
2     prefect of Egypt
3     … Apollo, exactor of Kabaseia, greetings.
4     [the] monthly general account of the month of Payni,
5     lord, having established the present consulate without prejudice and according to custom
6     having sent to your office and so that you might know
7     I write, lord. I pray that you remain well, prefect, lord.
