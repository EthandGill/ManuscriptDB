[TRANSLATION]
1     he has recorded in the accounting of month Pauni
2     of year 15, [of] name Thaesias of Horos, and
3     Lolutos of Patomuthis, through Porieuthos of Horos
4     Psenchounsios, for the price of date palms of harvest year 14
5     of the metropolis [,] drachmas eight, totals drachmas 8. Dionysios [I] have marked.
