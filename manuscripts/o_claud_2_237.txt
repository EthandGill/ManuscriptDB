[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymus to Draco
2     and Hermiasis and Ammonianos both many greetings
3     and your obeisance I [make]
4     before Fortune Campetes. brother
5     Draco, write to me about
6     your welfare. whatever he may say to you
7     Paniskos, deal with [it] as you do for yourself in order that thus I send to you it. whatever may
8     you spend.
