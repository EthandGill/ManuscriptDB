[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To the very great and mighty god Soknopaios, from Asklepiades son of Areios. If it is not granted to me to marry Tapetheus the daughter of Marres – and she may not become my wife – show it and confirm to me this writing. In the past Tapetheus was the wife of Horion. Year 35 of Caesar, 1 Pachon.
