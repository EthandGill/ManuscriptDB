[TRANSLATION]
1     [From those of the village of Aphrodite] … to Victor deacon and to the most admirable … and Victor our …
2     [we instruct our most pious mistress that Julian the most illustrious wishes, contrary to] custom, to drag us into the pagarchy of the Antaiopolites, which he has held [from the beginning of the …] indiction, although we are men [belonging to] the ducal authority and to the imperial estates; and we do not know whence
3     [such a man came upon us, under pretext of the public taxes; for] the local appropriate office knows that we have never been in arrears
4     [of their payment; but this man, overrunning our properties, did not hesitate to plunder] them more than the places ravaged by barbarians, on occasion
5     [of the public taxes. And his followers plundered all the property around our village,] as has been said, badly and indescribably; and moreover also its field
6     … [so that it is not possible for us, in our want, to pay] the customary public contributions and to live moderately with the animals and
7     [tools and all appurtenances, but for our maintenance] they left us nothing in [the field] and in the village of any utensil of our good living.
8     [God knows all the things done by them against the laws, and] the murders committed of the such plague that befell and bringing destructions,
9     [and all the unspeakable losses and wrongs] that came upon us then, which a page cannot contain except to recount with an unbroken
10    [voice] … the plunderer … Whence, on account of our being utterly at a loss on every side
11    [concerning our salvation, and one and only hope is left,] to have leisure for this most-earnest matter for us, to approach the most pious
12    [our mistress …] of the God-fearing [bishop of the Antaiopolites], so that she may oversee our humiliation and turn away from us
13    [the wretched the said pagarch, and we may find easily] to live [and quietly to] work at the pious payments and our extraordinary
14    [shipment, so that having obtained this benefaction we may once] be made whole [by the hand] of our most pious mistress and of your renown,
15    [through which also those evildoers will be punished, who made the dwellings] manifest and the properties [of] your belongings useless by diggings and excavations
16    [useless].
17    Victor, pitiable presbyter of the holy catholic church of the village of Aphrodite, entreating I have delivered to you this instruction for the information of our mistress.
18    Promaos son of John, pitiable presbyter of the holy Apa Promaos martyr of the village of Aphrodite, entreating I have delivered to you this instruction for the information of our mistress.
