[TRANSLATION]
1     To my master and lord, my
2     father Ach…, Hermapollon
3     … to fare well.
4     I pray to the Most High God that you may be in health and
5     of good repute continually. I continue (to write), since I have fallen into such extortionate treatment through your servant Agathos,
6     being unable to bear
7     the insults inflicted by him when things are said, and he is always
8     continually accustomed to set himself against my words. And this is the second (matter): unless you send Eucharistos to me, lest somehow I
9     fall into sickness, being unable to draw water for myself or (do) any other
10    of such matters,
11    I shall go up to my homeland, having received my greetings. Let them bring me a cloak, since indeed my own was lost at the fuller's.
12    And all the kinds of goods you brought to me, I have received
13    them exact in number. From
14    Theodoros I did not receive everything in full.
15    For I have not received jars from Theodoros.
16    I have received the price of the jars at
17    thirteen nummi, and at
18    fourteen; but I have not received the price of all of them. And fifty
19    little bundles I received from Theodoros. I received from my lord father
20    Agathos Daimon all that you sent to me.
21    And from the other men I received from them all the kinds of goods that I listed
22    in your letter. Do not neglect (this):
23    send little loaves and the other kinds of goods to
24    a better choice (place?) of the village. And
25    the livelihood having changed … ,
26    father, master, … x …
27    … ai …
28    Deliver to my master, my father A[gathos Daimon].
