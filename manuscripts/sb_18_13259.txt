1     Year 6 of Antoninus [and V]erus, the lords
2     Augustuses, Phamenoth 3. Registered to Heraclides, tax-collector [of the offices] and market-tax and
3     others of Alexandria and Nesos through Alcimus the secretary of Pakysis son of Horus, the tax on a donkey

5     drachmas one hundred eighty
6     eight, amounting to drachmas 188
