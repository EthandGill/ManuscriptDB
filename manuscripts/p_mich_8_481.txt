[TRANSLATION]
1   Terentianus to Tasoucharion,
2   his sister, very many greetings.
3   Before all things I pray for your
4   health, together with all your (folk).
5   Receive from the one deli-
6   vering to you the letter a bas-
7   ket, in which whatever you find,
8   write back to me (about) it. And through
9   Valerius the goldsmith another
10   I have sent you. So you will do well,
11   on receiving (it), to write
12   back to me both about your
13   welfare and (about) whatever you have need of.
14   For I too hope quick-
15   ly to come up to you. Do every-
16   thing to take thought for us (about) two
17   very large jars of em-
18   mer-wheat and an artaba of radish-
19   oil. I sent you oregano,
20   the (parcel) together with the oil. And if
21   you received from Ptolemaios
22   what I wrote [...] write
23   to me [...] the two ar-
24   tabas [...] and have (it)
25   so that [...] going up there.
26   Our children greet you,
27   [...] and …
28   [...] Greet P…krates with his whole household
29   [and] Ptolemaios son of Arius with (his) wife and
30   [chil]dren. Greet Ptolemaios and Tiberinus and Tiep…
31   [...] … to send fresh asparagus,
32   [...] through Melas, since we ask you, father, send
33   [… on]e. All those in the tenement greet you,
34   [...] by name. I sent you a papyrus sheet so that you may write
35   to me about your (pl.) health. I pray for your health forever.
36   Deliver to Tasoucharion ☓ from Terentianus.
