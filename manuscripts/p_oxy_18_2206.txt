[TRANSLATION]
1     In the month Pauni 16, of the … indiction … 3 solidi
2     by the bond of Georgios the headman, son of Phib, and of Petros the village-head, son of Mathios, from the village of Ision Panga, made
3     in the month Epeiph 10, of the 13th indiction, 3 solidi.
4     By the bond of the most reverend Pambachios, presbyter and steward of the holy church of Abba Hierakion, made
5     in the month Pharmouthi 7, of the 13th indiction, 3 solidi.
6     By the bond of Kyriakos and Aeion, stone-cutter of Trymis, for the price of stone purchased from them
7     for the use of the embankment of the Southern Paroria, 2 solidi.
8     To John the brick-maker, brick-making for the use of the embankment of the same Southern Paroria, by way of wage, 1 solidus.
9     By the bond of Victor from Polemon and of Georgios from Heraklas, for the price of burnt-brick (?) purchased from them
10    for the use of the brick thrown into the same embankment, Mesore 6, of the 13th indiction, 2 solidi.
11    Of the month given through them, 24 solidi; total 97⅔ solidi.
12    Total deducted on behalf of those (estates) in the following villages and properties
13    of the glorious house and others, 83 solidi.
14    Through those from Takona, 20 solidi. Through those from Spania, 10 solidi.
15    Through those from Tampeti, 12 solidi. Through those from Sepho, 2 solidi.
16    Through those from Meskanounis, 2 solidi. Through the lordly (estates), 37 solidi.
17    Total of the aforesaid, 83 solidi. Remaining, 14⅔.
