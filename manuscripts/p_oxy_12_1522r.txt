[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 5 of Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Pius,
2     and the Fortunate and Marcus Aurelius Alex[ander]
3     Caesar of the Augusti, Choiac 3, [they paid] [t]o collectors of crown-taxes
4     for 2 portions of the middle toparchy of levies of the upper toparchy of Isium [P]agga places.
5     Septimius Alexander, councillor, and his grandson, [for] crown-taxes on account,
6     Septimius Alexander [paid] forty drachmas, [totaling] 40 drachmas.
7     Aurelius Besammon, also called Saras, [I have] sealed [this].
8     And on the 10th of the following month, Tybi, the same [persons made] another [payment] on account
9     of forty drachmas, [totaling] 40 drachmas. Aurelius Besammon
10    also called Saras, clerk, [I have] sealed [this].
