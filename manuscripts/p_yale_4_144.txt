[TRANSLATION]
1     has gone through […]
2     being repeatedly demanded […]
3     [...] does not endure to pay back, but misleads me. Therefore I request, if it seems right, [bring] him [into the judgment] here
4     him; if it is as I write, compel [him] immediately to repay to me nine talents and 500 drachmas of bronze
5     and the interest [accrued] up to now.
6     [When] this [is done], I will obtain assistance.
7     Farewell.
8     To Euphranor: if he does not do
9     what is just, immediately
10    set [him] down.
11    Year 33, Payni 15.
