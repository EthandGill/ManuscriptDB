1     Severus to Limnaeus the most honored, greetings.
2     I want you to know that having arrived in the city of the Arsinoite [nome] you knew that I came from Alexandria and you did not come to visit me. I did not hope to have this from you. For I, considering you [a] brother, have had [you] here as [an] enemy ... and
