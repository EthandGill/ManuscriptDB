1     … we acknowledge
2     through this present written
3     security of ours, without deceit and fear
4     and force and
5     fraud and compulsion and every circumvention, to take the risk with you the
6     aforesaid Ioannes, steward, and to discharge the
7     public taxes of our
8     estate for the present eighth indiction
9     and to pay to the
10    glorious House,
11    and moreover also to collect the grain of the
12    embole (grain-shipment) of our, God willing,
13    ninth indiction and to give it to the
14    same glorious House and
15    to leave nothing in arrears,
16    whether from the gold public taxes of the present eighth
17    indiction, or also
18    from the grain
19    of the embole of the, God willing, ninth
20    indiction, at the risk of us and of you
21    the same Ioannes;
22    and if anything should remain in arrears, whether from the
23    gold-taxes or also from the grain,
24    we acknowledge with you to give these from our own house and from our own
25    (resources) to the
26    same glorious House.
27    And I too, Ieremias son of Horos, acknowledge to take the risk together with the others
