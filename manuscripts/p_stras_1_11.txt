1     Athenodorus through Hermes to those from
2     Cleopatra, greetings.
3     the taxes, of which you farm [arurae]
4     in wheat artabas twenty [five],

6     Year 1, Phamenoth 7
7     Aurelius Hermes, I have signed
8     [A]thenodorus landholder through Hermes, greetings.
9     the taxes, of which you farm arurae of the harvest
10    of the past 15th year in wheat artabas ten two-thirds, make wheat artabas 10 2/3,
11    measure [with] decaprotoi
12    near Polis below. Year 1
13    Phamenoth 14, Aurelius Hermes, I have signed.
