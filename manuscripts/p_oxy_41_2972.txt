[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy. In the fifth year of the Emperor Caesar Vespasian
2     Augustus, [in the month Sebastos] the 2nd, in the village of Nemera of the middle toparchy of the Oxyrhynchite nome.
3     Dionysios and Mnesithes, both sons of Theon son of
4     Theon, their mother being Sinthoonis also called Ophelous daughter of Sarapion, of those from the city of the Oxyrhynchites, acknowledge to Aristion son of Promachos
5     son of Aristion, his mother being Tauris daughter of Heniochos, of those from the
6     same city, and to his wife Hagne daughter of Ptolemaios
7     son of Ptolemaios, her mother being Senamounis daughter of Dionysios, of those
8     from the aforesaid village of Nemera, with her guardian being the
9     same Aristion, in the street, that the acknowledging parties have sold
10    to Promachos, the son of Aristion and Hagne, now being
11    abroad, and to Hagne, the maternal property belonging to them, namely
12    a half share of a house partly fallen down, and of the
13    places to the east of it called the proneision (forecourt),
14    and of the entrance and exit to it, and of the appurtenances
15    that are in the said village of Nemera; of which
16    to Promachos a half, fourth, eighth, sixteenth
17    share of the said half share of the house and places,
18    and to Hagne the remaining sixteenth share of these.
19    Neighbours of the whole house and the places: on the south and
20    east, formerly of Promachos son of Aristion the younger; on the north, of the aforewritten Aristion and of his
21    brother Amyntas; on the west, on the
22    side from the north, of Aristion and Amyntas and of another brother Heniochos and of Akoris son of
23    Aristion; and on the side from the south, of Aparous daughter of Aristion. And that the acknowledging parties have received on the spot, from
24    Aristion to the account of his son Promachos,
25    and from Hagne to her own account, the price of this agreed between one another, according as
26    each holds shares, in all the price in silver,
27    seven hundred fifty-two drachmas in full,
28    [from] hand to hand out of the house. That therefore each
29    of the buyers, both Promachos and Hagne, shall own and have control
30    [according as] each of them holds shares, and shall use and admin[ister] concerning it in whatever manner he chooses; and that of necessity the acknowledging parties shall guarantee to
31    [Prom]achos and Hagne the same half share
32    of the house and of the places, which is on the side from the south
