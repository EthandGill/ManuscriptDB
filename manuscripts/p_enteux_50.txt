[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greetings, Pasis, son of Hareutes, ibis-herdsman. I am wronged by Horus, son of Horus, of the
2     [people] from Philoteris, of the Heracleides division. For [he] claims [that] I have been registered in [a] contract with his daughter
3     Tzenamon, [for] an Egyptian contract [of] 400 drachmae, though I have not been registered. I beseech you then,
4     O king, to order Diophaneus the strategos to write to Neferotus the archiphylakite of the village to send Horus to Diophaneus [to be judged] with me, and if
5     I appear not to have been registered [in] the contract [for him] to give [it] back to me; but concerning those things [for] which
6     [the] matter has been accomplished, [for him] to receive the appropriate punishment; but if [he] denies [it],
7     [for him] to make acknowledgment before Diophaneus, so that, through you, O king, I may obtain justice.
8     Farewell.
9     To Sosibes. Most especially resolve [them]; if not, send so that before the laocrites [they may be judged].
10    Year 1, Gorpiaeus 30, Tybi [13].
11    Year 1, Gorpiaeus 30, Tybi 13.
12    Pasis, ibis-herdsman, to Horus concerning
13    those things by which he is wronged.
