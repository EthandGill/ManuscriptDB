[TRANSLATION]
1     Alexander to Zenon greeting. If you are well and in the other matters [according to mind]
2     you are faring, it would be as we wish. And we ourselves are well. When [Myus comes]
3     toward you, Myus, it seemed to us better to send to him and to Myus a memorandum [in which]
4     he might remind Apollonius about the arrangement which Menetus [ordered us. By] this [one moreover not even] [the]
5     owed amount have we received, nor from the time when that one [has died no one] [to us]
6     gives. It seemed good to us and we wrote to you so that if the [affairs have any need], he might help generously both for our sake and for Menetus's sake. For I know [that you]
7     have chosen, all the generous [acts] will be ours from Apollonius, and we [will be able to]
8     through you be saved and be restored. About which we ourselves will take care as much as [is possible for us]
9     the favors to repay to you.
10    Farewell.
11    Alexander concerning the having come about
12    [arrangement] to him. Year 28, Dystrus 24.
13    In Memphis.
14    To Zenon
