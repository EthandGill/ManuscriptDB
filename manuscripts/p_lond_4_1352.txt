[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God, Korra ben [Szerich, counselor]
2     To Basileios, administrator of the village [of Aphrodito. We give thanks]
3     [to] God and after that. [We have distributed through your administration]
4     [seventy shirts from currency of the fourth rank by order]
5     of the Amir al-Mu'minin, being done by custom through you, and these orders
6     [having been made] for the fields, we sent [to] you. Receiving then
7     the present letters, having accomplished from the fields of your administration
8     the [share] falling to them, according to the capacity of
9     our orders, procure [that] such
10    shirts [become] fine and choice, owing to please us. These
11    sending by your man owing to deliver
12    so that if God will, [if] something be turned away from them [to] whomever he gives these. It was written in the month of Mecheir, the 5th, indiction 8.
13    Korra be[n] Szerich, counselor [to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito].
14    [In the month of Mecheir, the 15th, indiction 8, it was conveyed through Saeid, concerning] shirts, [some] from currency of [the fourth rank] by order of the Amir al-Mu'minin
