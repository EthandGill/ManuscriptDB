[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Besarion and Anaxagoras son of Hermias, councillors,
2     keepers of the property-records and public accounts of the city of Antinoopolis,
3     [from Anthes]tius also called Gemellus, son of Marcus Julius Anthestius
4     [of the Athenian (deme), also] called Marathonian. I register with you (the property) which
5     [I have purchased] in the month Phaophi 15 according to the bank-transfer of the
6     … of Matidius also called Demetrieus in Antinoopolis,
7     of the [money-changing] bank: a two-storey house in which there is a courtyard [and a court, together with] all appurtenances, in the city of Antinoopolis in the
8     … 6th (quarter?) of Noteios, from Thermoutharion also called Sara[pous], of Antinoite (tribe), daughter of Protarchos, of (the deme) Ptolemaios Sebasteios
9     also called Apollonieus, at a price of three thousand silver drachmas, according to …
10    an earnest-money (agreement) … he had received in advance in the Arsinoite (nome) two thousand
11    four hundred silver drachmas, and the remaining (sum), for completion of the
12    price, six hundred silver drachmas, through the aforementioned bank-transfer in
13    the city of Antinoopolis, a copy of which, bearing the subscription of the banker, I have submitted to you separately by special document; and I swear
14    by the fortune of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar the lord
15    and by the greatest god Osirantinoos that the foregoing is true.
16    Year 23 of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Commodus
17    Antoninus [Augustus], Fortunate, Pious, Armeniacus, Medicus, Parthicus,
18    Sarmaticus, Germanicus, Britannicus, Phaophi 19. I, Anthestios also called Gemellus, have submitted (this) and have sworn the oath as set out above.
19    … We, Besarion and Anaxagoras, councillors, keepers of the records,
20    of the property-registrations and public accounts of the city of Antinoopolis, have received a copy, at the risk of the registrant, the right of prior claim being reserved for the treasury and for the civic account of the Antinoites and for whomever else it may concern.
21    Year 23 of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar the lord,
22    Phaophi 19. I, Besarion, through Didymos, have signed. I, Anaxagoras, have signed.
