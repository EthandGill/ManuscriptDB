[TRANSLATION]
1     ...so that you may know and it be read out. After the read-
2     ing, the council shouted: 'Let those who have not paid
3     be required (to pay); there is a deficit in
4     our number.' The prytanis said: 'There shall be read
5     to you the words spoken by Aurelius ..., the
6     most excellent dioiketes, that you may know what has been ordered.'
7     When these had been read, after the reading
8     the council shouted: 'There is a deficit ...[...]
9     [...] let those who have not paid be required.'
10    'Let the strategos be instructed.' The prytanis sa[id]:
11    'The memoranda shall be made public.'
