[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Year 1[1] of our lord, Gallienus Augustus, Hathyr 4. We, the Aurelii Agathos Daimon, ex-gymnasiarch, and Athanasios, ex-gymnasiarch, and Sarap[am]mon and Ko[p]res, both ex-gymnasiarchs, (both) in the capacity of one (dekaprotos), and Soucha[mm]on, ex-kosmetes, the dekaprotoi of toparchies 2 and 3 of the Herakleides division, have had measured to us at the granary of the village of Psenyris from the produce of the past 10th year by the public measure levelled by laying on the strickle for the tax on the katoikoi of the village of Psenyris in the name of Aurelius Tameios, ex-eutheniarches, through Dios, son of Prou , tenant farmer of public land, twenty artabas of wheat = 20 artabas of wheat. (m. 2) I, Aurelius Agathos Daimon, ex-gymnasiarch, councillor, have signed.
