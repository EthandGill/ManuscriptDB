[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapion of the successors and sub-administrator, from
2     Thautes and Thautes, twins serving
3     in the great Serapeum near Memphis. And previously indeed
4     we presented to you a petition concerning our not having received
5     from the priests what ought to be given to us
6     annually for the required emmer-wheat, whereupon you also wrote
7     to Mennides the overseer to repay us. But when he
8     sent word to Psintaes the supervisor of the priests,
9     he pays no attention, and we in the meantime,
10    exhausted by hunger, are in danger of abandoning the sacred service.
11    Since then it has been arranged from the royal treasury for those
12    in charge of the priests, and to us the emmer-wheat
13    from year 18 through year 19, yet we neither the
14    emmer-wheat nor what ought to be given to us in exchange for it
15    from the temple, the ration of the oil-workers,
16    have received, nor [the one] from the Asclepieum of the oil-workers from year 6
17    we beseech you with supplication,
18    seeing that [you do] not [cease] helping us,
19    and in these matters, having been our helper, recall [Psi]ntaes the supervisor of the priests
20    and compel
21    to repay us immediately what is owed to us of year 18 and year 19 in emmer-wheat
22    160 artabas and the ration from the Asclepieum taken into account. For once we have received these, we will serve
23    for you willingly in the sacred service.
24    Farewell.
25    To Mennides the overseer: decide in their favor,
26    the one in the great Serapeum near Memphis.
