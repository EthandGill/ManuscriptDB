[TRANSLATION]
1     To Tiberius Claudius Potamon, priest, recorder
2     and former exegetes, archidicastes and strategos of the city,
3     and to the Caesarei and to the other prytaneis,
4     from Heraclides son of Heraclides, of the Althaian (tribe), of those who in the twelfth year
5     of Tiberius Caesar Augustus served as ephebes. My son Theon, born to me from Posidonia daughter of … ollos, a townswoman, cut his lock of hair according to
6     the honour of the city on the fifteenth of Tybi of the present
7     year in the great Serapeum, in the presence of the priest and recorder and exegetes. And in accordance with your recorded decisions, and wishing to carry out the matters of his admission
8     among the ephebes of the present fourth year of Nero Claudius Caesar
9     Augustus Germanicus Imperator, I request that instruction be given
10    to the kosmetes and the gymnasiarch in office to receive
11    him among the ephebes of the same year, and, having examined the
12    aforesaid decisions, to declare to the …
13    to subscribe for me the documents relating to his admission and ephebate, he having been scrutinized by the reader who delivers this
14    memorandum, the men not impeding (it) inasmuch as he was not
15    presented at the inspections that were held, nor is he
16    in the registers of the boys, nor inasmuch as there is lacking
17    certain time(s) from the … of the … of those from …
18    … ap … on … phes I have been enrolled, his first-fruits …
19    … his … of the kosmeion, that is the Althaian (tribe), that I may be
20    treated with benevolence.
