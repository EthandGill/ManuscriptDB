[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting from Alexandros son of Peith…, from Pharbaithos,
2     of the Herakleides division. I am wronged
3     by Apollo[n]ios … For I, having leased a garden, in the 22nd year, from … as rent of this garden 20 drachmas, in order that he might pay (it) into the bank, of these he has paid in 10 drachmas, but the (other) ten drachmas he has dragged out still
4     even now, not having paid (them) into the bank; and I am being exacted by Theugenes, the lord of the
5     property, in accordance with the contract which I have made with him, both the interest and the half-as-much penalty, contrary to
6     the fact that Theugenes had been appointed (to pay) into the bank the 10 drachmas. So I beg you, king, if it seems good to you,
7     to command Diophanes the strategos to write to … on the epistates to send Apollonios
8     to be judged with me before him and, if these things are true, that he be made to pay the 10 drachmas together with the surcharges which I too am being exacted, so that, having taken refuge with you, king, I may obtain justice. Farewell.
9     To …on. After investigating, see to it that he obtains his rights. Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13.
10    Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13.
11    Alexandros against Apollonios
12    concerning the lease of a garden.
