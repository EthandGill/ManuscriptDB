1     Panameus son of Nepheros has receipted
2     for the poll-tax of Charakos, year 7, drachmas 10,
3     another 1 obol [and] 1 half, totaling drachmas 11 obol 1 half.
4     Year 7 of Domitian our lord
5     Payni 19.
6     Heraclides [has] witnessed.
