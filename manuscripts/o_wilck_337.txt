[TRANSLATION]
1   Year 28, Mesore 20. There has been paid into the
2   bank at Diospolis Magna, at which Ptolemaios (presides):
3   of the quarter-tax on fisher-folk of year 28, Simon —
4   whose exchange-charge is two talents — three thousand six hundred
5   forty-five
6   — total (with) exchange-charge 2 tal. 3645.
7   P(tolemaios) banker, 3 talents.
