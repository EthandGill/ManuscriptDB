[TRANSLATION]
1     Sotas to Athenodorus the superintendent
2     greeting and perpetual good health;
3     you know that in the ship I am
4     from Phamenoth; but from Pharmouthi I reckon the wage; and I have received
5     Pharmouthi and of Pachon, [for] the wage of [the months] and
6     Pauni and Epeiph; well will you do
7     on account of the [difficulty that] holds me and
8     on account of [my] discomfort, that I am shut up
9     into the temple, [having] ordered for the two-month [period]; I supply a man with wages
10    until 1 of Mesore; I ask you, begging [you] to hand over the ship which if you wish; for you are master; and the other [things] take care of yourself so that
11    you are well; be strong;
12    year 23 of Caesar [E]peiph 29
