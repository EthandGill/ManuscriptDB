[TRANSLATION]
1     Paniskos greets the wife greatly.
2     I greet Heliod[ora] my daughter greatly, and [pay-attention] to her. I want to see if you have need of anything. I wrote
3     to Aiōn, and if you have left anything in the city of Hēlios, send [it] there. And I have left the drachmas
4     for yourself, so that if you want anything, you may spend [it] for yourself. And pay-attention also to your possessions,
5     and make the three wholly-linen things foot-sandals for my daughter, and prepare the materials of your tunic
6     and of the garment. And if the god wills
7     and I come, I place there Achaon. And prepare my leather-bag, and if anything
8     they want, follow them. Know [therefore] that as [a brother to you] I write, not as
9     … of you. [I pray] you [to be-well].
10    I greet your mother [greatly]
11    and Nonnos with children and [Sarapion].
12    Deliver to the wife
13    from Paniskos
14    in Mopsarion.
