[TRANSLATION]
1     To Petesoutis, Phomnaio[s],
2     [from] Kronion, son of [Kronion]
3     of Te[os], [from the village] Teb[ty]n[eus]. I [happen] [voluntarily]
4     and willingly [to have withdrawn]
5     from the present [year]
6     from the cultivation of the lands belonging to you
7     around the village Kerkeisis, formerly
8     of Asclepiades' lot of arourae
9     twelve and a half, and ktamion-land
10    of arourae twelve and a half, [to have sown] with wheat and hay,
11    and you holding it and renting to others
12    [having rented out] from the present year; if it should appear good to release.
13    Kronion, aged 66, with a scar on his left hand.
14    Year 14 of Antoninus Caesar the
15    lord.
