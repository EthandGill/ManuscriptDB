[TRANSLATION]
1     before
2     of year 25 of Caesar, Mecheir 29, treasurers
3     in the city of Oxyrhynchus of the Thebaid. Ptolemaeus of Ammonius, a Macedonian cavalry-commander, lent to Sarapion of Demetrius, a Persian of the epigone,
4     [the] two in the street, of silver currency
5     five hundred drachmas of principal,
6     to which nothing at all attaches,
7     of nine-obol interest per mina each month
8     each, and let [him] repay
9     Sarapion to Ptolemaeus the silver
10    five hundred drachmas and their interest in the month of Mesore
11    Apollonius of Apollonius
12    of the same year without [trial] and
13    judgment and any excuse.
14    But if he does not repay as [has been written]
