[TRANSLATION]
1     Measure of the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 11 of Aure(lius)
2     Antoninus Caesar the lord,
3     Epeiph 12, for the South, in the name of Kephal( ) (son of) Apollodoros,
4     through the cultivator Psenchonsis (son of) Kametis, of wheat a half
5     and a twelfth, total wheat ½ 1/12. I, Amonios, have signed. (2nd hand) Another,
6     Epeiph 5, likewise, of wheat a sixth, total wheat 1/6. (1st hand) I, Amonios, have signed.
