[TRANSLATION]
1     Theodoros of Callicratus, [man] of Espereia of the
2     lineage; I guarantee to Pasis and Petoseiris, tanners of the village Tholthis
3     for [the] surety toward year twenty which came to be; but if to you
4     I do not provide them, I will pay to you the claimed [amount]
5     within five days according to the above [things]
6     written.
7     [From] Sonton son of Ammenes, [a] rag-worker-seller.
