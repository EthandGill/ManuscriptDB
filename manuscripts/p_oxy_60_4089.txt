[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of the Flavii Sergius and Nigrinianus, the most illustrious.
2     To Flavius Paeanius, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite.
3     From Aurelius Kolobos son of Theodoros, councillor of the same city,
4     distributor of the fortress of the camps of Psobthis of the Oxyrhynchite nome. Since you seek the account of the receipt made by me
5     and the delivery in this month of Thoth
6     and Phaophi, of necessity, having inserted it in order below, I submit it, so that
7     your diligence may know it. It is as follows. Of the 10th indiction:
8     remaining in arrears from the account of the month of Mesore, of grain, artabas 236 1/2 1/4;
9     and there was added to this account, artabas 92 2/3;
10    of which:
11    of townspeople, artabas 11;
12    of which:
13    Ploutianos son of Eudaimon, artabas 9;
14    …aina through Aphynchios, artabas 2;
15    the aforesaid amounts are made up.
16    Of villagers, likewise, artabas 51 2/3;
17    of which:
18    of the 2nd pagus of Sadalos, through Isak son of Amois, artabas 24;
19    of the 3rd pagus of Syron, through Papnoutios son of Papontos, artaba 2/3;
20    of the 7th pagus of Istros, through Kopreus, soldier, artabas 3;
21    of the 8th pagus of Teis, through Anoubion, princeps, artabas 24;
22    the aforesaid amounts are made up.
23    Of the treasury (?), of the 6th pagus of Pakerke Senopothis,
24    through Diogenes, ferryman, artabas 30;
25    together with the arrears there are altogether artabas 329 1/3 1/12.
26    Of barley likewise, of receipts:
27    remaining in arrears from the account of the month of Mesore, artabas 272 1/3;
28    and there was added to this account, artabas 270;
29    of which:
30    of townspeople, artabas 210 1/2;
31    of which:
32    Makrobios of praetorian rank and Flavius Crescentius, artabas 140;
33    Hierakous also called Demetria, artabas 19 1/2;
34    Origenia daughter of Philoxenos, artabas 8;
35    Achilleus son of Philoserapis, artabas 18;
36    Apollonios son of Demetrios, artabas 6;
37    Sarapion also called Kteson and Euethion his sister, artabas 2;
38    Paulos son of Serenos, through Horion, scribe, artabas 5;
39    Euporos son of Euporion, artabas 3;
40    Ploutianos son of Eudaimon, artabas 5;
41    Sophia daughter of Demetrios, through Theodoros, assessor, artabas 4;
42    the aforesaid amounts are made up.
43    Of villagers likewise, of receipts, artabas 53 1/2;
44    of which:
45    of the 2nd pagus of Sadalos, through Isak son of Amois, artabas 9;
46    of the 3rd pagus, artabas 32 1/2;
47    of which:
48    of Episemos, through Horos son of A…ys, artabas 20;
49    of Syron, through Apollon son of Strouthos, artabas 12 1/2;
50    of the 7th pagus of Istros, through Pa… the reed-cutter, artabas 12;
51    the aforesaid amounts are made up.
52    Of the treasury (?), of the 6th pagus of Pakerke Senopothis, through Diogenes, artabas 6;
53    together with the arrears there are altogether artabas 542 1/3.
