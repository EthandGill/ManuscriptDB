[TRANSLATION]
1     ... certain men who when she was reported ...
2     set up an attack against us and inflicted upon us many blows so that
3     my wife Sara[eut]a, being pregnant, [had to lie down] and [was] in danger of [her] life.
4     therefore I ask [her] to be brought before you, bringing [them]
5     the othe[rs] to you and decide against [them],
6     as seems [best] to you. Farewell.
7     I Tryphon [son] of Dionysios have submitted the petition. Zoslos
8     [I] Zoslos wrote on his behalf
9     [of him] not knowing letters. Of the year 11
10    of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
11    Augustus Germanicus
12    Emperor, of the month of New Augustus 11.
