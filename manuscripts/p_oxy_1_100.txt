[TRANSLATION]
1   To the agoranomoi, Marcus Antonius Deios - and however I am styled -
2   former strategos of Alexandria, temple-warden of the great Sarapis.
3   I swear the oath customary among the Romans that I have sold to Adrastos son of Chai-
4   remon son of Adrastos, of the Neilanabateios (deme) also called Althaieus, his mother being
5   Philotera daughter of Origenes, a citizen-woman; and to Dionysios also called Papontos,
6   his mother being Taarpaesis, son of Pertas, from the city of Oxyrhynchus, a min-
7   or, his father Horos being styled (guardian), his mother being Tapsois -
8   [...to the] two jointly and in equal shares, out of the property
9   belonging to me in the quarter of the Cretan and the Jewish (quarter): the remaining vacant
10   lots, four vici(?), whose situation and orientation by the compass have been shown in the
11   deed of cession; (I swear) that they are mine and are neither mortgaged
12   nor alienated to others in any way, and that I have received
13   the price, two thousand two hundred silver drachmas, and
14   that I will warrant (them) with every guarantee and provide them free fr[om] any reg-
15   istration whatsoever and from everything else of any kind. Year 17 of Imp[erator]
16   Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Pharmouthi 13.
17   I, Marcus Antonius, have sw[orn]
18   the oath.
19   Pharmouthi 13: Adrastos and the other (party) have purchased from Marcus.
