[TRANSLATION]
1     to the grain-receivers of the village Karanidos, greetings.
2     I have received from you for account
3     of seeds of the present 5th year from the harvest
4     of the year 4 which has passed
5     apart from allotment-land
6     of Kerkesoukhos public [land]: Kastor son of Paesis, of 3 arourai of wheat, 3 artabae.
7     Kastor son of Paesis has received the seeds as stated above.
8     Year 5, Hathyr.
