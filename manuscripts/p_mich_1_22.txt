[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysodoros to Zenon, greetings. we hear the fast-boat has sailed past having the papyri. well [would you do]
2     then if you would send us the
3     ten papyri which Apollonios put together. give them to Apollonides the
4     one bringing [to] you the letter, so that [we] not
5     be delayed for the need.
6     farewell. year 29, Daisios 29.
7     Dionysodoros
8     of papyri to Zenon
