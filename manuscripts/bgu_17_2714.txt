[TRANSLATION]
1     From the heirs of Phoibammon from Hermes city through me illustrious John
2     to Apollos deacon from [the] Hermopolites. I have been paid from you on behalf of the third part [of] the thirteen arouras of produce of the present third indiction
3     and I have no account against you concerning the same third part of the same thirteen [arouras] of the same indiction and for your security this
4     [for] you I have made the … receipt. It was written Pauni 2 (of) the third indiction. … heir [of] [Phoiba]mmon through me illustrious John agrees with me the receipt as is stated
5     Cyrus [of] Jacob from Hermes city deemed worthy I wrote on behalf them letters not knowing
