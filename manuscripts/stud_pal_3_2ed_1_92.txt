[TRANSLATION]
1     I received and I was satisfied, I Naara(u)os ally
2     son of Papnouthios from you Andreas secretary of the village
3     of Tamaues the per-year [allowances] given to me from you as
4     [wages for] the month of Phaophi, that is, concerning the past
5     [fi]fteenth indiction gold coins two each
6     at karats five, gold coins two each at karats five. It was written in the month
7     Epeiph twenty-seventh at the beginning of the first indiction. Through me Iou(stos) notary.
