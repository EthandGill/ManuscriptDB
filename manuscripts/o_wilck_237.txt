[TRANSLATION]
1   Stlakkios, lessee of the Sacred Gate of Syene,
2   through Serenos the assistant. Paid: Menopheilos the elder,
3   son of Orbaeis, mother Tisatis, for the poll-tax of the 21st year: eight drachmas — total 8 dr.
4   Year 21 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Pauni 4.
