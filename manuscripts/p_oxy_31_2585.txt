[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our sovereigns Const[antine]
2     and Licinius the Augusti, for the 4th time,
3     to Aurelius Dioskourides also called Julianus, gymnasiarch, former prytanis, councillor of the splendid and mo[st splendid] city of the Oxyrhynchites,
4     from Aurelius Leonides son of Theon from the same city.
5     I willingly undertake to lease for the present 10th and 8th year only, from the properties in the embankment-area of Pektu, of the holding called Karabos, of the southern ten arouras, in the parts from west to south, the two and a half arouras lying fallow by survey, or however many they be, for the sowing of flax-stalk, at a rent for each aroura of four talents of silver, all free from every risk, the public dues of the land being upon you the landowner, who own the crops until you receive back what is owed. The undertaking
6     being guaranteed to me, I shall of necessity pay the money-rent in the months Pauni and Epeiph [of the] same [yea]r
7     without delay, the exaction being made by you fro[m]
8     me as is fitting. The undertaking is valid, and on being formally questioned
9     I acknowledged.
10    Year 10 and 8 of the same our sovereigns [Cons]tantine
11    and Licinius the Augusti, Hathy[r] …
12    Aurelius Dioskourides also called Ju[lianus],
13    through me Morianos assistant, I have re[ceiv]ed this
14    copy.
