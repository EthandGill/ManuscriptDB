1   Harpaesis son of Phenophis, Thrakidas, paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the seventh year
3   of Trajan Caesar the
4   lord, on account 8 drachmas.
5   The same, the remaining 9 drachmas.
6   I, Hermogenes, the collector, wrote (this).
