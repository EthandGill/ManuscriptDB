[TRANSLATION]
1     Imouthes tax-collector of silver of the metropolis
2     To Psenminei, Onnophrius Psenminius. I have received
3     On behalf of poll-tax of Market North 19[th] year filthy drachmas eight
4     Become filthy drachmas 8. Year 19 of Trajan Best
5     Of the lord Phamenoth 14.
6     Other 15 filthy drachmas 4.
