1     50 years old, of good stature, fair-skinned, straight-haired, with a mole in the middle of the forehead.
2     Amyntas son of Machatas, an Alexandrian of the epigone, of those not yet enrolled
3     into a deme, of Herakleion, heir of Protion, deceased,
4     against Archepolis son of Thessalos, of the (deme) of Ptolemaios, a private individual,
5     [in accor]dance with the contract he made with (the man) of Chares. That in the seventeenth year,
6     in the [mon]th Audna[ios], on the … and twentieth, having borrowed from Protion
7     [the] aforementioned, three thousand drachmas of bronze coin, clear-eyed (i.e. genuine),
8     at [inter]est of two drachmas per mina per month, each
9     [according to a] contra[ct, of which] Ptolemaios is contract-keeper, on which as surety
10    [there] was registered [for the repay]ment of the [lo]an Diokles, a Persian of those not yet
11    [un]der a commander, a thirty-[aroura] holder; and the contract stipulating that you (were to) repay to P[ro]tion this [lo]an [and the interest]
12    within ten days [from the (day) on which] you should be demanded; and [if you should not repay as]
13    is written, that you [should pay in addition] one and a half times [the] lo[an, and the interest]
14    simple; but since Pr[otio]n has died …
15    from you, you, being deman[de]d by m[e for the 3000 drachmas and the interest]
16    have not [r]epaid me. Wherefore I bring suit [against you] as h[eir of the (property) of]
17    Protion's (possessions) which are in the [property] at [Herakleopolis]
18    u[p]on Mem[ph]is, in the allotted court, in ac[cordance with the contract, for the]
19    [3000] drachmas [of the lo]a[n], [with] the one-and-a-half (penalty), of bronze c[oin]
