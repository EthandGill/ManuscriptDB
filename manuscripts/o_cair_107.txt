[TRANSLATION]
1     Pthomônthês Piouêrios has measured into the treasury
2     of the administration of the lower toparchy of produce year 2 on behalf of [Memnoneia]
3     of wheat from a pile three and one-half and one-third artabas
4     twelfth, amounts to 3 and 1/2 and 1/3 and 1/12. year 2 of Trajan
5     of the lord, Pauni 22nd. similarly
6     Epeiph 6th of name of the same on behalf of Memnoneia artaba
7     one and one-half and one-third,
8     total artaba 1 and 1/2 and 1/3.
