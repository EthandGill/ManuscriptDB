[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion, administrator of Apollonius
2     of the praesidium Raeim, to Aelius Serenus
3     to the administrator of the Claudian mine, greetings.
4     I released Valerius, Choiax 10
5     At the 2nd hour and as for the things just now, we have not yet [heard] about [Egypt]. You will do well
6     Send us the two water-carriers
7     those able to [draw]. At once
8     send them lest suddenly
9     come upon us the shortage
10    or embargo and about the chaff
11    what you wish, since
12    I pray you to be well.
