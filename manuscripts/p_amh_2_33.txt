[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy and Queen Cleopatra his sister,
2     the Philometor gods, greeting.
3     Marepathis son of Sisouchos and P[a]tkos son of Onnophris [and T]esenouphis
4     son of Marres and Phatres son of Tho[to]es and Harp… [son of Ama]rantos,
5     royal cultivators of those [from] Soknopaiou Nesos of the
6     Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome. There being instituted
7     for us a hearing before [Zo]pyros the epimeletes and Petearpsenesis the royal scribe sitting in council
8     and the chrematistai who judge the royal and revenue and private (cases) in the aforesaid nome, of whom the introducer is Dexios, against Tesenouphis who served as komarch of the
9     aforesaid village, on the (matters) about which we submitted to them
10    informations concerning certain wrongs and frauds
11    both of grain and of copper, and now, our (cases) being brought in,
12    we learn that the accused Tesenouphis is appearing in court together with advocates, your forefathers having ordained
13    through the subjoined decree that those advocates who come forward to the revenue-cases
14    to the detriment of the revenues should pay to the treasury double
15    the tithe, and that it should no longer be permitted to these to act as advocates.
16    We beseech you, the greatest gods, if it seem good to you,
17    to send our petition before the same chrematistai, so that at the sorting of the petitions they may order Tesenouphis not to appear in court with an advocate.
18    For this being done, none of the things advantageous to you
19    will fail.
20    Farewell.
21    King Ptolemy to Apollonios, greeting. Since certain
22    of the undersigned advocates come forward to the
23    revenue-cases, damaging the revenues, give orders
24    that the advocates be made to pay double to the treasury
25    the tithe, and that it no longer be permitted to these to act as advocate concerning any matter. And if any of those damaging the revenues
26    be convicted of having acted as advocate concerning any matter, both send him
27    to us under guard, and his belongings
28    register to the treasury.
29    Year 27, Gorpiaios 15.
