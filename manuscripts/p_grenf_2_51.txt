[TRANSLATION]
1     In year seven of the Autokrator [Kai]s[aros]
2     Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus August[us]
3     the Pious, Phaophi 14, through the [bank of] Hermes' table
4     of [the] treasuries. The same. Antonius Sab[i]n[os]
5     dupliciarius from […o]uetran[es] Ga[l]lic squadron of Anti[llian]ou, [Pa]oueti [son] of Paoutes, as if of age 20, marked
6     on the neck from the left, and Satabouti [son of] Sataboutes as if of age 35, [with a] white spot on the left eye, and Stot[oeti [son of] Sto]toetes as if of age 50, [with a] scar on the right shin, elders of the village of So[kn]opaiou Nesos [of the] Heracleides division, as
7     they say, [though] illiterate, [to] have received the value of goat hides
8     of four, which also Antonius received [in] silver drachmas sixt[een], obols sixteen.
