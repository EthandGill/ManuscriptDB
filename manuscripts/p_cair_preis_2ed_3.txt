[TRANSLATION]
1     [Of the consulship of Mame]rtinus and E[i]uvianos of the most illustrious
2     [To Aurelius] Nilus [son] of Gennadius and Theodorus [son] of Komasios
3     [riparian [landowners] of the Hermopolite district
4     [From Aure]lius Serenus [son] of Pinutius from the village of Enseus
5     [of the same] Hermopolite. Six years ago
6     [I married] a [wom]an named Tamonin, [daughter] of [D]emetrius, and I paid the bride-gifts given by custom
7     [to the same] [w]oman and [I] fulfilled the laws of marriage
8     [according to the custom] of marriages and we lived together for three years
9     [with her. But] the mother of the aforesaid [woman] mocked me
10    [saying that my] wife had fallen victim to a demon.
11    [When I therefore] departed for my own work, in order
12    [to find] [a way to] earn a living, to another man apart [from]
13    [divorce/release], [he] gave the same woman [named Peeuti from the village of Achilles of the same] [Hermopo]lite.
