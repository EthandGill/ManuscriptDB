[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 3 of Emperor Caesar Titus
2     Aelius Hadrian Antoninus Augustus
3     the Pious, Thoth 17, of the census of Mesore, year 2. He/she wrote [this] down
4     Pasion son of Dioscorus, slave of Laberia
5     [mother of] Alexandra, for the poll-tax of the second [year]
6     in Phremei twenty drachmas, which total twenty, with additional charge of copper ten obols.
