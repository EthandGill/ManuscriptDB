[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy.
2     In the 2nd year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, in the month Gorpiaios, Epeiph 30, in Hermoupolis of the Thebaid. There acknow[ledge]:
3     Horio[n] son of He[lio]doros, about 32 years old, of medium height, honey-colored, long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar in the middle of his forehead; and Tothes son of Tothes, about 40 years old, of medium height, honey-colored, long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on his right shin — both from Hermoupolis, Persians o[f the descen]t —
4     to Dios son of He[r]m[odo]ros also called Salion, gymnasiarch-elect, about 20 years old, of medium (height), honey-colored, long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on his left cheek, that they have agreed with him to furnish (heat), namely
5     in the 4th year of Ti[beri]us Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, from the 1st of the month Sebastos until the month Kaisareios with the 5 intercalary (days) of the same year, for the [in the]
6     gymn[asi]u[m ba]th the sufficient heat each day uninterruptedly, from whatever hour Dios may choose until the bathers leave,
7     setting in place for the stoker the heat unfailingly for the furnace-room of the bath each day at their own expen[se];
8     and from the (sums) agreed upon for their wages and the price of heat for the aforewritten year, in silver of Augustus and Ptolemaic coinage,
9     2,000 drachmas, the acknowledging parties have on the spot received fr[om] Dios through the private bank of Hermonikos 500 drachmas, and of the remaining 1,500 drachmas Dios shall give them on the 30th of the month …
10    of the coming [3rd year] of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, for the purchase of donkeys, 200 drachmas, and the remaining drachmas he shall pay back [in]
11    the afore[writ]ten 4th year in thr[ee] installments: on the 30th of Choiak 400 drachmas, on the thirtieth of Mecheir 400 drachmas, and on the 30th of Epeiph the remaining 500 drachmas; and there shall give
12    the acknowled[gin]g parties to Dios in the same 4th year, for his brickworks, 150 bundles of chaff delivered to Ibion Tetachthi, and …
13    Dio[s shall pay] them in the village Sinkere and (in) Lenneus and Ibion Tetaphthi 150 cartloads of chaff; and if the bath fall short of heat
14    on a[ny da]y or in part during the year, apart from the acknowledging parties furnishing the heating as aforesaid, they shall further also pay
15    … of the resulting damage and shall pay back to Dios the aforesaid 2,000 drachmas double, and execution shall belong to Dios or his agents
16    from both the acknowledging parties and from whichever of them he may choose, they being mutually liable for the repayment, and from their property
17    all of it as if by legal judgment; and whatever the acknowledging parties have and whatever they may further acquire
18    shall be (held as) security for
19    the aforesaid (terms) concerning which it shall not be permitted to them to dispose of anything until they fulfill each (obligation), or (else) the things done contrary to this (shall be) invalid.
20    Let this agreement be valid.
