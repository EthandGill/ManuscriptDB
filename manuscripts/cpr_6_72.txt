[TRANSLATION]
1     … m …
2     [from the village of P]esla of the [Her]m[opolite] no[me], … …
3     [about] 40 years old … long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on the right calf. May it be (granted) to me, while in health,
4     to be master of all my own (property), to manage and administer concerning them in whatever way I may choose. But if I
5     suffer some human fate, I leave to my son Phibis, born to me from the woman living with me, Tinyris, about 28 years old,
6     of middle (stature), honey-complexioned, long-faced, straight-nosed, with (a scar) on the elbow of the left hand, the house belonging to me
7     and, on the east of the house, an empty plot, and
8     of their appurtenances and all that belongs to them, and entrances
9     and exits, of those (things) which are among the (buildings) on the east of the festival-court
10    of Hermes Thoth, the greatest god, of the village of Pesla. The neighbors of the
11    whole: on the south a public lane, on the north (the property of) Psois son of Chenyris, on the east
12    (the property) of the aforesaid son Phibis son of Phibis, on the west the avenue
13    of Hermes Thoth the greatest, of the same village of Pesla.
14    The [testa]ment is valid. These six (are) witnesses. Psois son of Chenyris, about 29 years old,
15    of middle (stature), honey-complexioned, long-faced, straight-nosed, with no distinguishing mark; Patophibis son of Pascheis, about 45 years old, of middle (stature), honey-complexioned, long-faced, straight-nosed,
16    with no distinguishing mark; Pathoutes, brother, about 35 years old, of middle (stature), honey-complexioned, long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on the left shin;
17    Paesis son of Chenamounis, about 45 years old, with a scar on the right shin;
