1     … 20
2     [To Aurelia Dem]etria, matron stolata,
3     [daughter of Aurelius Posid]onius, former hypomnematographos,
4     [through E]uporos, manager of the epoikion of Pset.
5     [Money accoun]t of the month Mecheir of the present 5th year. It is as follows:
6     of [recei]pts through Heronas the handler, so as
7     [for the outsi]de account given on behalf of
8     … to the … of Bakchias, 80 drachmas
9     [from the price] of 20 artabas sold to the
10    [workmen] … 10, and of others by the public measure at 12 drachmas, 240 drachmas
11    [from the account of the previous] month there were carried over as remainder 136 drachmas in copper
12    [total] of receipts 457 drachmas in copper
13    [of which, expenditure:] those weeding in the wheat
14    … 31 workmen at 12 obols, 53 drachmas 1 obol
15    … [those wate]ring in another kleros, 24 arouras
16    … 16 workmen, 27 drachmas 3 obols
17    … [those wat]ering in the vegetable-plot, 2 arouras, 6 workmen
18    … [ those wat]ering in another kleros, 4 arouras, 8
19    … called …, 5 workmen
20    … [those wate]ring in the barley, 4 other workmen
21    [those watering for] foliage in the wheat of the kleros, 16 arouras
22    … 7 workmen
23    21

26    likewise those watering hay, 11 arouras, 2 workmen
