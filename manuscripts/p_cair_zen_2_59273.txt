[TRANSLATION]
1     Iphimedon to Zenon greetings. Well you do if you are well;
2     well am I too. You wrote to me concerning the furnaces
3     so that to the calves they be. You then send ahead someone, so that
4     [I] send a boy who will hand them over to him. There are furnaces, large indeed two, small indeed three. Write to me
5     and concerning the allotment how you have managed it.
6     Farewell. Year 35,
7     Epeiph 4.
8     Year 35, Epeiph 9. Iphimedon
9     [concerning] furnaces.
10    To Zenon.
11    Iphimedon.
