[TRANSLATION]
1     In year 2, Phaophi 6; he transferred for the value of barley …
2     Libyan by arurae, Sokonnophris
3     priest through heirs of Hermes city, drachmas …
4     seventy-five, total drachmas 75,
5     and for the crown-tax drachmas twenty …
6     five, total drachmas 25.
7     Aurelius Cyr[i]llus have signed.
