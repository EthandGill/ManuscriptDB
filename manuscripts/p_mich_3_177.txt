[TRANSLATION]
1     To Locretius Cerialis, strategos,
2     and to Pasion, royal scribe of the Arsinoite,
3     of the division of Herakleides, and to Areios,
4     village scribe, and to Apollonios
5     and Patron, poll-tax registrars of the village of Bakchias.
6     From Peteuris son of Horos
7     son of Horos, whose mother is Herieus
8     daughter of Mencheis, of those from
9     the aforesaid village. There belongs
10    to me and to my brothers,
11    in the village, a quarter share
12    of a house and courtyard in which we dwell,
13    and I register both myself and
14    my (people) for the
15    7th year of Imperator Caesar that has passed
16    Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus,
17    according to the house-by-house registration. And I am
18    Peteuris the aforewritten, public
19    [farmer], 44 years old, without distinguishing mark,
20    and (I register) the brother Horos, 34 years old, without distinguishing mark,
21    and Horion, another brother, 21 years old, without distinguishing mark,
22    and this one's wife Thenatymis
23    daughter of Chariton, whose mother is Tapetosiris, 25 years old,
24    and in the same village a share
25    of house-plots.
26    Apollonios, poll-tax registrar of the village of Bakchias,
27    Patron, poll-tax registrar, through Epimachos
28    the scribe: we have registered (it). Year 8 of Imperator
29    Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus
30    Germanicus Dacicus, Choiak 5.
31    Areios, village scribe: in the
32    eighth year I have signed.
