[TRANSLATION]
1     … 643
2     [After the consul]ship of the Flavii Zenon and Marcianus
3     … [the] most illustrious, [T]hoth 22, in the 9th indiction.
4     To Aurelia Kyra, the most noble daughter of the one of blessed
5     memory Abraamios, from the (city) of the Hermopolites,
6     from A[urelius] Sarapion son of [Pe]ky[si]os, his mother being Nonna, farmer
7     from [th]e same city. I acknowledge willingly and
8     of my own choice that I have leased from you, for whatever time you wi[s]h,
9     from the crops of the fortunate tenth indiction,
10    th[e three arouras belong]ing to you, in the east
11    [of the city,] to the south (those of) Hatherios son of Brekon, to the north …
12    … of holy Mother Euphemia in the Kleopatris (quarter),
13    to the east the small-plots of Melas, to the west the small-plots of Makarios
14    son of Teukos, for sowing and planting of whatever
15    I may choose; the fixed rent of these per year (being)
16    twelve artabas of grain and of cowpea, five
17    artabas, and of vetch, five artabas, and
18    of lentil, one and a half artabas; which rent
19    I shall measure out and pay to you in the month Epeiph
20    each year without delay, new, clean,
21    sifted, by the Athenian measure,
22    delivered to your house in the same
23    city, by my own animals; the lease is valid
24    and secure, and on being asked the formal question I gave my assent. Aurelius Sarapion son of Pekysios,
25    the aforesaid, have leased as is set forth. Aurelius Theophanes son of Phoibammon
26    from Hermo[u]polis, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
27    Through me, Phoibammon, it was written …
28    Lease of Sara[p]ion from Herm[oupolis,]
