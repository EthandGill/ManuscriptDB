[TRANSLATION]
1     Peisiakles Nikandros to Zenon, greetings. If you are well, [it] would be good;
2     and we ourselves are well. You wrote to us that it would not be possible to recover
3     from the table of the sesame the price. And Diognetus reported to us
4     in what manner you showed zeal for us, so that we might not lose
5     the small change. You then, just as you have also gathered other [things] for us, [also] concerning
6     the remaining small change take thought how it might be recovered, as would be possible at any time.
7     And the house too and the small vineyard, concerning which you write to us, as
8     [the] time might come, dispose [of them], so that we too might have bronze [coins] for
9     expenses.
10    [Fare]well. Year 36, Panemos 26.
11    Year 36, Epagomena 5.
12    Peisiakles Nikandros
13    [concerning] small change.
14    To Zenon
