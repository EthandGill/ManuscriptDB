[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eighth year of the Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus
2     Germanicus Dacicus, Phamenoth on the thirtieth. Payment through the bank of Eudaimon
3     son of Ammonios. Ta[p]euthis daughter of Kastor, with her guardian, her own
4     brother Apollonios son of the same Kastor, (pays) to Papontos the younger son of Herakles
5     in fulfillment of one hundred sixty-six drachmas four obols of silver, the price of the portion belonging to him,
6     one out of nine parts, of what his deceased
7     father Herakles son of Dios, while still living, had sold, of two and a quarter arouras in the Leukopyrgite
8     lower (toparchy), around Poimenon, from the allotment of Xenophantes, in accordance with the acknowledgement of sale registered through the
9     records-office in Hermou polis: the remaining one hundred eleven drachmas one obol of silver,
10    making 111 drachmas 1 obol of silver.
11    I, Papontos the younger son of Herakles, have subscribed to the aforesaid payment.
12    I have received in fulfillment one hundred sixty-six drachmas four obols of silver, the price
13    of the portion belonging to me, one out of nine parts, of what our deceased father Herakles son of Dios, while still living, sold, of two and a quarter arouras in the
14    Leukopyrgite lower (toparchy), around Poimenon, from the allotment of Xenophantes, in accordance with the
15    acknowledgement of sale registered through the records-office in Hermou polis: … drachmas of silver
16    one hundred eleven (drachmas) one obol, making 111 drachmas 1 obol of silver, as is aforesaid. In the eighth year of the Emperor
17    Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, Pharmouthi 1. Ailouras son of Ammonios
18    wrote on his behalf because he writes slowly. I, Papontos the younger son of Herakles, have received the price as is aforesaid.
