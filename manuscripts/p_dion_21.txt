[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Cleopatra and Ptolemy, mother-loving
2     Saviour Gods, the fourth year, under the priest who is in Alexandria of Alexander and the Saviour Gods and the Brother-Gods and the Benefactor-Gods and the Father-loving Gods and the Manifest Gods and the god Eupator and the god Philometor and the god Philopator and the god Euergetes
3     and the mother-loving Saviour Gods, the sacred colt of Isis, the mother
4     of the gods, the great, the wreath-bearer of Queen Cleopatra, goddess Philometor, Saviour, Justice-bringer, Victory-bearer, the prize-bearer of Berenike
5     Euergetis, the light-bearer of Queen Cleopatra, goddess Philometor,
6     Saviour, Justice-bringer, Victory-bearer, the basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphos,
7     the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator, those being in Alexandria, in the month
8     Xandikos, the twenty-fifth, Mecheir the twenty-fifth,
9     in the village of Tenis also called Akoris of the Hermopolite. There lent
10    Ammonios son of Ammonios, a Persian of the epigone, to Dionysios son of Kephalas,
11    a Persian of the epigone, twenty artabas of solid wheat, which he has received in measure from him; and let Dionysios repay the wheat without interest
12    to Ammonios or to those acting for him in the month Loios, which is Pauni, of the
13    fourth year, solid new clean unadulterated wheat, by the measure
14    that is to the bronze-(standard), that of Euphron, and let him deliver it to the
15    harbour at Akoris at his own expense; and if he does not repay
16    as has been written, let Dionysios pay to Ammonios or to those acting for him
17    the value of each artaba, three thousand drachmas of copper, and let the right of execution
18    belong to Ammonios and to those acting for him, for the terms of the contract,
19    both against Dionysios himself and against all his possessions,
20    just as by legal judgment. Let this contract be valid.
21    Witnesses: Apollonios son of Satyros, a Neoteichite; Charinos son of Lysikrates, a Myrinaean; Euphron son of Euphron; Kastor son of Dionysios — the two Persians;
22    Ammonios son of Demetrios, a Macedonian — the five of the
23    katoikoi cavalry of Dryton; Achilleus son of Paniskos, a Macedonian
24    of the squadron of Lochos, of the mercenary cavalry.
25    Keeper of the contract: Apoll[onios].
26    Dionysios son of Kephalas, a Persian of the epigone: I have re[ceived the value]
27    of the twenty artabas of soli[d wheat and] I will do everything [as]
28    is set forth above, and I have given it as valid to Apollonios.
29    Apollonios: I hold it as valid.
30    Year 4, Mecheir 25, in the village of Tenis of the Mochites; registered through Apollonios.
31    In the reign of Cleopatra and Ptolemy, mother-loving Saviour Gods, year 4, Mecheir 25, in the village of
32    Tenis also called Akoris of the Hermopolite: loan of Ammonios to Dionysios, of solid wheat
33    twenty artabas; let Dionysios repay in Pauni of the fourth year
34    by the measure that is to the bronze-(standard), that of Euphron; let him deliver it to the harbour at Akoris;
35    value three thousand; keeper of the contract: Apollonios son of Satyros.
36    Of Ammonios, of Apollonios, of Euphron, of Ammonios
37    of Dionysios, of Charinos, of Kastor, of Achilleus
38    loan of Dionysi[os son of Kephalas]
39    of 20 artabas of wheat
