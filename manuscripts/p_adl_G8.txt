[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the rei]gn of Cleopatra and king Ptolemy surnamed Alexan[der] the son, the Mother-loving gods,
2     [in yea]r 13 which is also 10, in the priesthoods of the priests [and priestesses] and the kanephoros that are in office, in the month Pauni, the 25th, in Pathyris, before Hermias
3     [who is on behalf] of Paniskos agoranomos.
4     Thaibis daughter of Phibis, a Persian woman, about 58 years old, of middle (height), honey-colored, round-faced, straight-nosed, somewhat near-sighted, with as guardian her own kinsman Paous the elder son of Patous son of Phagonis, a Persian, about 35 years old, of middle (height), honey-colored,
5     long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on the left shin, from the date-palm grove belonging to her,
6     of the growing things and all the appurtenances, which is in the part to the east of Pathyris outside
7     the canal, the sixth part falling to Pasemis my younger son, of which the neighbors to the south and east (are)
8     the rest of her palm-grove, to the north (the property of) Gounsis son of Thotomous, to the west a canal, or whoever the neighbors may be on every side;
9     [this] is sold on account of her not having done the things stipulated. Panobchounis son of Nechoutes bought (it), about 45 years old,
10    for one talent of [cop]per, two thousand drachmas. Fore-seller and guarantor of all things concerning this purchase
11    (is) Thaibis the seller, whom Panobchounis the buyer accepted. I, Hermias who is on behalf of Paniskos, have transacted (this).
