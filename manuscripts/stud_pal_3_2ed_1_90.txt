[TRANSLATION]
1     I received and I was paid, I Naraous, ally, son
2     … [of the Arsinoite city, residing in the estate of Centaurus, from you Andr[e]us, [secre]tary of [the] village
3     Tamaue [the] things given yearly to me [from] you by way of sa[la]ry,
4     namely on behalf of the present twelfth indiction gold coins
5     two each at [the rate of] five carats, gold coins 2 each at [the rate of] 5 carats.
6     It was written in the month Phao[phi] 21 of the twelfth indiction through me Phib, notary.
