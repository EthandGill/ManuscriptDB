[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most pious master Flavius
2     Maurikios Tiberios, the eternal Augustus
3     and Imperator, year 4, Pauni 28, in the 4th end (of the) indiction, at Arsinoe.
4     To the Flavii Stephanos, the most magnificent tribune and
5     deputy-landlord, and Neilos, the most distinguished count, full brothers, from the city of the Arsinoites, Aurelius
6     Aanios, son of Isak, a farmer from the same city,
7     from the quarter of Tamia, greeting. I acknowledge that I have leased from
8     your magnificence, from the right belonging to the estate appertaining to her, in the plain of Kolymbos near the suburbs of this city, in the place of the holding called
9     Piaak…ssi, however many arouras there may be, which I also held from before,
10    with all their right, for a period of four years,
11    counted from the crops of the coming, with God's help,
12    sixth indiction inclusive, and that I, the lessee
13    Aannios, will provide to your magnificence, for the fixed rent
14    of them, yearly, six gold solidi less forty
15    six and a half carats — six gold solidi less 46½ carats — to be paid
16    by me yearly in two installments: in the month Pauni
17    three solidi, and in the month Epeiph the other three solidi,
18    without delay. The lease is valid, and on being questioned I gave my assent. Aurelius
19    Aannios, [son] of Isak, a farmer, the aforesaid, have leased the
20    afore[w]ritten arouras and will provide yearly the rent of them,
21    yearly, as af[oresaid]. I, Aurelius Elias, son of Paulos, wrote on his behalf,
22    he being present (but) illiterate.
23    Lease of however many arouras there may be in the plain of Kolymbos by Aurelius Aannios, a farmer,
24    son of Isak, to the Flavii Stephanos, the most magnificent tribune, deputy-landlord …
