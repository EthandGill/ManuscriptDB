[TRANSLATION]
1   † In the reign of our most divine and most pious master, greatest benefactor,
2   Flavius Tiberius Constantinus the eternal Augustus and Imperator,
3   in the 4th year, Phamenoth 25, of the 12th indiction.
4   To the most exalted heirs of the man of glorious memory,
5   Apion who became patrician, landowners also here in the illustrious
6   city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas the servant who interrogates and
7   procures for his own masters, the same all-praiseworthy
8   men, the right of action and liability, (I,) Aurelius Pamouthios lead-worker,
9   son of Georgios of the mother Anniane, originating from the city of the
10   Oxyrhynchites. I acknowledge by voluntary intent, swearing the divine
11   and venerable oath, that I stand surety for and undertake before your
12   exaltedness, through those belonging to it, (for) Aurelius Abr[a]amios
13   son of Herminos of the mother Herais, originating from the estate
14   of Megale Tarouthinou belonging to your exaltedness of the
15   Oxyrhynchite nome, her registered farmer, on condition that he
16   shall continually remain and dwell on his (her?) estate
17   with his dearest ones and wife and cattle and all
18   his baggage, answerable for everything that concerns
19   his person or the status of the registered (farmer),
20   and that he shall in no way abandon the same estate nor indeed
21   remove himself to another place, but also, when he is sought
22   by your exaltedness from me, through those belonging to it,
23   on whatsoever day, for the sake of whatsoever
24   pretext, I will produce and deliver him in a public
25   place, free of any place of refuge and of pretext, where I
26   also received him, in the custody of the same glorious house.
27   Or [if] I do not do this, I acknowledge that I will pay on account of his
28   departure and the delivery not being made by me
29   eight gold solidi, demandable by legal action and force.
30   Valid is the surety, drawn up in single copy, and having been asked I gave my assent.
31   Surety of Pamouthios the lead-worker, son of Georgios, from [the city of the Oxyrhynchites]
32   undertaking for Abraam[io]n son of Herminos fr(om) T[a]r[o]uthi[nou. †]
