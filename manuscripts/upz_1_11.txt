[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy and Queen Cleopatra, his sister,
2     to the gods Philometor, greetings:
3     Ptolemy, son of Glaucus, a Macedonian of those in detention
4     in this year, the thirteenth, in the [sanctuary] near Memphis
5     great Sarapeum. Since I possess a paternal
6     house [near the] village [Psich]is of the Heracleopolite [nome]
7     and this having been torn away and the goods from it
8     having been carried off, being worth twenty talents, by
9     my neighbors, Hesperus and A[ta]ius, his son,
10    and [his] brothers [Polemon] and Paesis,
11    the aforementioned, not content with what they had already transacted, [they also took] the adjacent courtyard and the place of the house
12    [the] empty space, having built [walls] around [it] in the month Mesore
13    of the 20th year, [and they] use [it] as [their] own, treating [it] with contempt
14    because I am unable, having come out of the sacred [place],
15    and having come to the places to lodge
16    a complaint against them. Therefore I beseech you to send [my]
17    petition to Dio[nysius] of the friends and
18    strategus, so that he write to Kudia the strategus
19    to send out to him the aforementioned [men],
20    so that he compel them to deliver the places to Apollonius, my younger brother,
21    and should order them no longer to force their way in, and the price of the goods, the 20 talents,
22    to repay to the aforementioned Apollonius, so that
23    having been brought back to me here by him I may have what
24    is necessary and not be undone by hunger; and concerning
25    the violence which they committed [you should] decide [with some severity],
26    so that I myself, having received a share of the help from you into
27    [the] life [of] support.
28    Farewell.
