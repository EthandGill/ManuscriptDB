[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 13 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus
2     Germanicus Autocrator, Mechei 2, has been written out
3     through Phaesis of Panephrymus, Herieus, on behalf of per capita
4     grain-tax of Nile city, on account drachmas four, making drachmas 4;
5     Phamenoth 6, on account drachmas four, making drachmas 4;
6     Pachon 13, on account drachmas two, making drachmas 2.
