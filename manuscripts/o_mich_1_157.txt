[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 1 of our lord Probus Augustus
2     Aurelius Eudaimon through me Diodorus
3     of the harvest of the census of Aurelian in the treasury of the village of Karanis
4     of the name of Sarapion for the price of wheat
5     artabas 2, drachmas 400, through me Syrus.
