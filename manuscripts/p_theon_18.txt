1     Of the year 22 of Antoninus
2     [Caesar] of the lord
3     [Mesore] of the added days 5
4     From the account of Tiberians
5     Of Julians of Theon
6     And of Theon the also Tryphon
7     To Diogenes, collector of rations
8     Of the month Mesore, drachmas
9     Thirty-two, [they] come to drachmas 32
10    Give the drachmas 32
