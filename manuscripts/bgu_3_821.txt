[TRANSLATION]
1   Greetings, my lord father. Heraiskos
2   greets you. The …(?)
3   went up to the city, so that I might know
4   the latest news. Up to the 17th they have not yet
5   found the one coming in with him; for all
6   have been dispatched. Whenever there is anything newer,
7   I will at once make it known to you; and you too, write
8   to us what you have done. All the works are
9   going well. My mother greets you,
10   and all our (folk). I pray for your
11   health.
