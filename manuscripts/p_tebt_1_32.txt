[TRANSLATION]
1     Aristippos to Eumelos, greeting. Of the letter written to us by Pankrates [the official in charge of the]
2     assignment of the cataphract cavalry-settlers,
3     [the] copy is appended for you. Take care, therefore, that you do not [harass]
4     [As]klepiades with regard to the patrol liturgies. Farewell. Year 36, Pauni …
5     [Pan]krates to Aristippos, greeting. Of the letter which we have written
6     … to the royal scribe we have appended the copy,
7     [that you may] know. Year 36, Phamenoth …
8     … [10.] Of the (letter) from Sosos [and] Aigyptos, of the … men …
9     … [appoint]ed by the corporation [of the Cretans],
10    [written] to us with regard to the matters pertaining to Ask[lepiades],
11    [the copy is] appended, that you may know that he is transferred …
12    [and that it may be done] in accordance with the ordinances.
13    [Sosos] and Aigyptos to Pankrates, greeting. Since it has been ordered by us
14    [that] the cavalry-settlers … the patrols … .
15    Apollodoros of the first friends, the epistates
16    [and] scribe of the cavalry-settlers, has instructed me, out of the men conceded
17    to the corporation of the Cretans, 500 (in number), (concerning) Asklepiades
18    son of Ptolemaios, Macedonian, of the district patrols, on condition that he holds a plot
19    around Kerkeosiris in the division of Polemon, of 24 arouras. You will do well, therefore,
20    to register him and take him into the fifth hipparchy of the hundred-aroura men, and
21    to report to Apollodoros. We have appended also his description
22    and the name of his son.
23    He is about 22 years old, short, honey-coloured, with curling hair …;
24    his son Ptolemaios, five months old …
25    To Eumelos.
