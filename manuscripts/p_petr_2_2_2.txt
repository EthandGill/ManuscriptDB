1     Moschion to Diophanēs greetings. Dorimachus brought me a petition that had been issued [officially]
2     against Dionysius, in which it was written to be concerned that he might obtain justice. Having summoned Dionysius, I read the petition to him and ordered him to do what is just for Dorimachus. But when Dionysius objected, [claiming he] had nothing of the
