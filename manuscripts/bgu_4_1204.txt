[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidor[a] to Asklepiades her brother,
2     greet[ing] an[d g]ood health continually.
3     I have sealed up together for you the letter to Paniskos.
4     So the reply about everything
5     send quic[kly], because the boat
6     is departing. And you, play the man (be diligent),
7     until he arrives, and take care of yourself,
8     [that] you may be well, which indeed is most important.
9     Farewe(ll). Year 3, Phao(phi) 5.
10    Send the sleeping-rug (enkoimetron) of the
11    child.
