[TRANSLATION]
1     [ ] Remainder of the 11th indiction after the great accounting,
2     as follows:
3     administration of the count Papirius:
4     to the elders and to the assistant of Takona, of Alexandria: 9 solidi, 1½ carats;
5     to the stablemen of Takona, of Alexandria: 8 solidi, 23¾ carats;
6     to [J]eremias, assistant of Takona, on behalf of Pamouthios, from the elders, of Alexandria: 7 solidi, 4½ carats;
7     to Apollos, assistant of Spania, of Alexandria: 6 solidi, 12¼ carats;
8     to the people of Sepho, on account of the balance of the price of grain, of Alexandria: 1 solidus, 17 carats;
9     to Pamouthios, elder of Tampeti, on account of his office as elder, of Alexandria: 36 solidi, 6 carats;
10    to [Th]eodoros, riparius, of Alexandria: 25 solidi, 13 carats;
11    to those belonging to the holy church, on account of the office of the logistes, of Alexandria: 26 solidi, 23½ carats;
12    to the assistant of the 4 estates, of Alexandria: 29 solidi, 19½ carats.
13    Total: 152 solidi, 1 carat.
14    Of payments:
15    total, of Alexandria: 152 solidi, 1 carat.
16    to the administrator of Pangouleeion, of Alexandria: 4 solidi, 7½ carats;
17    to the same, on account of the price of grain, of Alexandria: 7 solidi, 15 carats;
18    to the administrator of Thaesis, on account of the balance of freightage, of Alexandria: 7 solidi, 1¾ carats.
19    Total: 19 solidi, ¼ carat.
20    Total, of Alexandria: 19 solidi, ¼ carat.
