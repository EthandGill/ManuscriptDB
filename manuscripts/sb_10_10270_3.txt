[TRANSLATION]
1     To Haremphis, bird-breeder
2     Give for the requisition [to the one who] provided birds, [for] two receipt-tokens
3     eggs similarly, twenty-five
4     totaling 2 receipt-tokens [and] 25 eggs
5     of the 5th year, Mechir, 20
6     from Haremphis, [of the] scribe
