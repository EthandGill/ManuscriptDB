[TRANSLATION]
1     Measure of the treasury of the metropolis [of the] produce [of the] 29th year
2     of Marcus Antoninus Caesar
3     Augustus Epiphi 19 for [the account of] Charakos
4     Psenchonsios Pekysios [pays] wheat one-third one-twelfth, [which] becomes wheat [one-third] one-twelfth,
5     [and] for [the account of] Asklas [the] elder
6     of Ammonios wheat one-third one-eleventh
7     [become] wheat [one-third] one-eleventh, and for [the account of] Psenemsias
8     [Pel]aias wheat one-third [which] becomes wheat [one-third].
9     Ammonios [I] have noted [this].
