1     In the gift of Peleios, unlawfully
2     [it] is shown [to have] been used for
3     brick-making still from
4     third year from the common [public] way
5     six arouras and a quarter aroura, the land that borders the house,
6     in total six artabas; of wheat one artaba and … of wheat three artabas
7     … of wheat forty-two artabas, the total of wheat [is] ten [plus] forty-two artabas
