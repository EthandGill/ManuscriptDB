[TRANSLATION]
1     Iohannes, comes, to Germanos the superintendent.
2     Provide to Iohannes the builder and Paulos the carpenter working on the tank
3     that came to be in the estate of Nomographos in the eleventh indiction by Panhesios the farmer
4     in account of expenses: wheat artabas two, and for the price of meat, thirty liters, six hundred myriads
5     forty eight, and for the price of oil, ten xests, six hundred myriads, and for the price of wine
6     double[d] ten thousand two hundred [...].; the sum is wheat 2 and together of silver myriads.... I noted wheat
7     of year 148 [in month] Epeiph 2, 10th indiction, [can]cellary, artabas two, and myriads two thousand four hundred forty eight only,
8     [the su]m is wheat 2 [from] cancellary and myriads ... [only] to Germanos superintendent of Nomographos.
9     of the tank of Nomographos.
