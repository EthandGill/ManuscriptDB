[TRANSLATION]
1     [To F]lavius [this one], the most blessed grammarian,
2     and teacher of Hellenic free discourse,
3     from Aurelius this one, linen-dealer,
4     son of this one of [the] best memory, [having departed],
5     from the Caesarean metropolis,
6     [and] province.
7     After the consulship of Flavius Opportunus the most distinguished, Thoth twenty-four, [third] indiction.
8     which has also been sold to you by her, the same [woman] and …
9     sister of her for the sale, and since I now
10    [So-and-so] this one disputed against you the purchasers,
11    Flavius this [one] and this [woman] the wife of you concerning the
12    aforementioned house and of the case discussed
13    by many trustworthy men.
