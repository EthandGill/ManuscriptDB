[TRANSLATION]
1     The lady Rhoea paid [the] rent of the two
2     graves/tombs pertaining to the holy great
3     church from Pauni 1, of the 15th indiction, until Pachon
4     [Pachon] 30, [of the] first indiction, of gold [coin] half with [a deduction of] three karats
5     half, of gold [coin] 1/2 with [a deduction of] three karats 1/2 alone. Through me Isaac the rent-collector.
