[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Aurelius Faus]tianus, former magistrate of the city of the Mothites of the Great Oasis,
2     … [ d]efender of the district.
3     [From Aurelius] Pamourios (son of) Psais, from the village of Kellis of the city of the Mothites
4     [(of Mothis). If] for each man the deeds of insolence shall go forward and if the
5     [vigilance of the laws] is not wont to ensue, life would become unlivable for us
6     … the time became (so). Sois then, (son of) Akoutios, comarch (village-head)
7     [of the same vi]llage of Kellis, ever plotting against me daily
8     … to me at every turn, with the visiting soldiers [and off]icials, the exactors, reveling against [my] wif[e and] attacking me. For yesterday, in my ab[s]ence,
9     … having broken down the door with an axe, having entered together with the son of Psena[mounis] the carpenter from Pmoun Pamo, being neither a public servant nor him-
10    … happening to be my [vil]lager, having come upon my wife
11    [with the] club [whi]ch [he ha]d he beat her down with blo[ws,] as the bl[ows appe]ar upon her [b]ody, as if they were not subject to laws.
12    [Such t]hen being the intent of the aforesaid, both the comar[ch and t]he son of Psenamoun[i]s, not being able
13    [to keep qui]et, I submit to your equity these documents,
14    [requesting tha]t these very things be reported to the manliness of my lord
15    [the most disting]uished governor Valerius Victori[ni]anus
16    [so that] what was dared may meet with the fit[ting] vindication. Farewell.
17    [Afte]r the consulship of our masters Constantine
18    Augustus for the 6th time and Constantine the most illustrious
19    Caesar for the 1st time, Tybi 11. Aurelius Pamouris (son of) Psais the aforesaid
20    have submitted (this). I, Aurelius Phibion, wrote on his behalf as he does not know letters,
21    former magistrate of the city of Hermes (Hermopolis).
