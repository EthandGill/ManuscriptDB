[TRANSLATION]
1     I received the letters of your excellence and I wish her/it
2     before dawn to overtake the city carrying [it] and the [things] of the lord
3     Christodōros the singularis before all things and concerning which I said to you
4     to bring into account of the friend. But by all means intercept me before dawn.
5     Give to the … Apollōs [village headman] …
