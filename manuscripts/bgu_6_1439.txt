[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 9, Mesore 3, has measured into [the treasury]
2     of Apollonos city into the same year, below Poeris,
3     Poherios of Arthote,
4     of wheat 4, [they] become wheat 4. Kastor.
5     Patous has measured, according as is set forth below, of wheat 4, [they] become wheat 4.
6     Of year 9, Mesore 3.
