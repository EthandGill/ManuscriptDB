[TRANSLATION]
1     Kametis, collector of money-taxes,
2     to Pachnoumis (son of) Paouphtheus
3     (son of) Paous. I have received for the assessment
4     of the watchtower (skopelos) of Charax, year 19, base drachmas four, 1 obol, 1 chalkous,
5     total base drachmas 4, 1 obol, 1 chalkous. Year 19 of Hadrian
6     the lord, Epeiph 30. I, K(ametis), have signed.
