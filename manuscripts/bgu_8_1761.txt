[TRANSLATION]
1     [Protarchos to Seleukos,] greeting. Of the memorandum given by Heroïdes
2     [a copy is appended. If, then,] it be as is alleged, the woman accused of the …
3     [of the patrimonial property of Heroïdes,] eject, and lend support to Heroïdes. …
4     Farewell. Year 2, Xanthikos 11, (which is) Mecheir 11.
5     [To Protarchos, from Heroïdes son of …]mon the dietarch. My aforewritten father …
6     [of the catoecic cavalry in the Herakleopolite,] having died in the places indicated,
7     [of all the things by my father] left, belonging to me, she, having scorned …
8     … having drawn in as helpers some of those from the village Tokoöis of the same nome,
9     [some of those dwelling in the same (village),] she has trespassed in the present year upon the … left
10    [to me around …, the arouras …;] and she has also forced her way into the house left along with (them) in the same village
11    [and has carried off something of the things in it.] Therefore, being moved to come before you, I request, if
12    [it seem good, that you direct a letter to be written to Seleukos] the kinsman and strategos of the nome, before all things
13    [that there be held for me the] produce [of the … arouras,] and that the accused woman be sent off to you under guard,
14    [before she dare to commit yet more violence,] so that she may fall under the deserved judgment of the things she has done,
15    [because] … her against the house. The value of these things is 200 silver drachmas, and from there
16    [to lend support to me] … holding fast to the patrimonial property, that I may be helped. Farewell.
17    Let notice be given. Year 2, Phamenoth 13.
