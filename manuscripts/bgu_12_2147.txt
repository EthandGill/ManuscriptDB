[TRANSLATION]
1     [After] the consulship of Flavius Bibianus the most illustrious and of the one to be de…signated, Phaophi 10, of the 3rd indiction.
2     [To Flavius] John son of Taurinus, enrolled in the military rank in
3     the Thebaid, from Aurelius Victor son of Bekis, his mother being Pia,
4     from [the village] of Terton Psake of the Hermopolite nome. I acknowledge
5     of my own free will and choice that I have leased from you for a six-year
6     period, from the crops of the prosperous fourth indiction,
7     the six arouras belonging to you in two places, of which the (arouras)
8     in the one place around the tax-collectorate of my village (number)
9     four ar[ouras], and in the other place around the tax-collectorate
10    [of the village of Leu]kos Pyrgos [the] remaining two arouras, for the sowing
11    [of whatever] crops [I may] choose, the fixed rent for these each year (being)
12    [of wheat-and-barley] at the half (rate), thirty-six artabas, which
13    [rent] I shall measure out to you in the month Epeiph each year without delay,
14    [new,] clean, sifted, by the Athenian measure,
15    [at the] threshing-floors of the same village; the lease is binding,
16    [and secure, and] on being questioned I have so acknowledged. Aurelius Victor son of Bekis, the aforesaid,
17    [have] leased as aforesaid. Aurelius Phoibammon son of Hermodoros,
18    [from Hermopolis,] having been asked, wrote for him because he does not know letters.
19    … It was written through me, Phoibammon.
20    Lease of Vi[ct]or son of Bekis [from the village of Terton Psake] of the Hermopolite.
