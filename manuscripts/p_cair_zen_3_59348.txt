[TRANSLATION]
1     Callicrates
2     to Zeno, greetings.
3     I received the
4     letter from you
5     and those written to Apaturion.
6     I myself then was not able to manage except that to that person
7     it has been written, and to the one
8     from you [carry]ing
9     as it has been given [them] sealed
10    and I have ordered him to carry back to Apaturion. So that you might know
11    I have written to you.
12    Farewell. Year 2
13    Athyr 24.
14    To Zeno.
