[TRANSLATION]
1     I will do these things. letters, as many as I have sent,
2     attempt with my men
3     to send through
4     swiftly. I learned
5     from Magnus, that to me
6     papers twenty you have sent; not yet
7     and them I have received.
8     greet the friends [and] [your] people,
9     [as many as] are ours. [be well]
