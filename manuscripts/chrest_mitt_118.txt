[TRANSLATION]
1     To Lucretius Cerialis, strategos
2     of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite (nome),
3     from Teseaphis the younger, son of Teseaphis
4     son of Panephremmis, a priest, from the village
5     of Soknopaiu Nesos of the same division.
6     On the 27th of the past month Tybi
7     of the present 10th [year] of Imperator Caesar Nerva
8     Tra[ja]n A[ugustus G]ermanicus Dacicus,
9     [my w]ife Segathes,
10    daughter of Stotoetis, died, I having a store-room
11    secured in the house of her father,
12    in which all the things … which are
13    1,585 drachmas of silver and … drachmas of bronze-coinage and a garment
14    of linen and two silver finger-rings of priestly (type) and three tin cups
15    and a bronze [cup] and … and … among ornaments … and
16    14 staters [and] a coloured garment
17    [and all the] other (things) that were in the store-[room]
18    … …
19    … of the brother …
20    …s … son of Apygchis and his wife Thenapygchis, who is the sister of my wife, having gathered at the same place — I having from long ago … a key of the store-room of my own, without
21    my knowledge … … my
22    the store-room, through the locksmith of the village,
23    and having plundered whatever I have in the store-room and
24    the things lying beside (it). Therefore I request that they be brought before you
25    so that I may obtain justice from you.
26    I, Teseaphis, have submitted the complaint
27    as is set out above. Year 10 of Imperator Caesar Nerva
28    Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus,
29    Mecheir 4.
