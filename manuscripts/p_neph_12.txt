[TRANSLATION]
1     To my beloved father Apa Petechon, the father of the monastery, Serapion,
2     your … in the Lord, greeting. First of all I greet you warmly, most honored
3     [father.] I greet my father Nepheros the presbyter, that you may attend
4     … to me in your holy prayers; and the other Nepheros the presbyter — I greet you warmly. I salute Euphelis; I salute Jacob; I salute Besas the monk.
5     I greet K…lol and Kasis his brother. I salute Poutis and his son;
6     I salute Aeion; I salute Arion; I salute Heraklas; I salute Amer; I salute
7     P…choleukis; I salute Hor the brother of Amer; I salute Apa Nilos; I salute Apoutis
8     together with his brother Syros; I salute Hereus; I salute Keimai and his brother Paris;
9     I salute Papeis; I salute Papnoutis the brother of Anoubion; I salute Herakles; I salute
10    Hermeinos the reader; I salute Hor pa Tahmouro; I salute Lotos; I greet
11    Aleitis; I salute Apa Horion. I write to you, Apa Nepheros: when I left you, thanks be to God,
12    I came to Ombos; I greet you warmly. Seek out Paul the sailor from Toeto to receive from him the cloak and two tunics and three sickles, and take them from him. For he has sons
13    two, Hatres and Pkylis; but do not neglect it. I salute Apa Ota and Kasis. I salute Paesis
14    and I salute Peter and Horion. I salute all those in the monastery, small and great; I salute Serapion … Tahmourou; I salute Kapiton; I salute you all,
15    my brothers, that you may pray for me. I write to you, Nepheros, that you may take care of the little … of
16    land, as I am coming; but do not neglect it, most honored father, but do not neglect … … …
17    Take the grain, as I am coming; place [it in] the cemetery, as I am coming … … Nepheros,
18    as I am coming, most honored — [I pray for] your good health … father. For [many years] …
19    Deliver to Nepheros the presbyter from Serapion the monk, to the village of Moue.
