1     Year 7, Tybi 16: there has been paid to the
2     bank in Diospolis Magna, (for) the linen-tax
3     of the seventh year: Apollonios, six talents,
4     total 6 talents.
5     Pharmouthi 2, another six, total 6 talents.
6     (the) 26th, five talents, total 5 talents.
7     total 17 talents.
8     17 talents 2580(?).
