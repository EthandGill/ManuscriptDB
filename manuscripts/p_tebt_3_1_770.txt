1     [To king Ptolemaios,] greeting, Asklepiades son of Harthoutes, an Arsinoite, [of those] dwelling in Krokodilon polis of the Arsinoite nome. I have sent,
2     [O king, the one who] will deliver to you a petition, Apollonios, an agent
3     … the suit which I brought against Theroun
4     [son of Nechathymis, of an agree]ment for 1500 drachmas, and we were referred to the
5     … to be introduced again before the chrematistai; but the pet[ition] having been [sent] to Apollonios who is in charge of the
6     … a … on … they demanded. But I, because I am troubled, [cannot] sail down, and I wish to appoint
7     [in my own stead] the said Apollonios son of Asklepiades
8     … . I beg you therefore, O king, to order
9     … present … together … [to Apo]llonios … of the record-office
10    [so that he may accept] Apollonios, who will carry through the (matters) concerning
11    [the appointment] … to be sent. For this
12    [being done, I shall obtain] the benevolence from you fully accomplished. Farewell.
13    [I, Asklepiades, have] appointed (him). Year 12, as the revenues (are reckoned) year 13,
14    … Pachon 21. Apollonios delivered the letter
15    on the 27th of the same month.
16    … having examined whether he declares any land or
17    [has other property, make] it clear to us.
18    … is declared to have leased 15, ¼.
