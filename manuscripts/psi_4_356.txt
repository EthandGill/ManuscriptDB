[TRANSLATION]
1     Nicanor [to] Zeno, greetings. You wrote to me concerning the grain that no one had measured [it] out. [Well] it has been measured [and] still [lies] on the threshing-floor, barley [amounting to] 500 artabae; for thus you permitted me
2     instead of the 400 artabae which I measured [out], to measure [out] so much. You would do well then
3     if you would order [them] to summon Panesis the steward and Anosius and the grain-collector,
4     and if it appears that we have measured [it] in, [you should] order [them] to hand over to Pasion, my [representative], the written agreement. And you wrote to me concerning fodder, [asking] that it be purchased [in exchange] for grain.
5     No one then in the places [is] selling [it] for grain, but [only] for money; and [the price] has been raised,
6     on account of those around Hermolaos buying [it] together for the royal [treasury].
7     Farewell. Year 33, Choiach 21st.
8     Year 33, Choiach 23rd. Nicanor
