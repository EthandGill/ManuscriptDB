[TRANSLATION]
1     Tiberias to Maximos
2     to my lord and brother, very many [greetings]
3     to be well. First of [all] I pray you to be in good health. How, brother,
4     send me the half-artaba
5     of grain. If no one wishes to carry [it] away,
6     [send] to me eight drachmas so that I may buy it,
7     or pay him eight drachmas. Only [send]
8     to me provisions quickly. And send me the marsippias. You
9     [will do] well if you make lagana,
10    and send it to me for the Isieia.
11    absolutely nothing from yourself
12    [you] know. I will make the gourds. Greet the
13    all who love you.
14    Fare well. 9.
