[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 2 of Autocrator
2     Caesar Marcus Aurelius
3     Antoninus Pious
4     Fortunate Augustus, Choiak,
5     26. Of [the] Aurelians Allatos
6     and of Philadelphia Tryphon
7     and of the associates, sitologoi of the village
8     Boubastus. We have measured [out]
9     from the harvests of the same year
10    in the treasury of the aforesaid village on behalf of public [levies]
11    of it to Sotas, [son] of Apunchus, wheat, five artabas,
12    they become wheat, five artabas.
