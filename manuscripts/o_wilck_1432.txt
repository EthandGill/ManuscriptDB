[TRANSLATION]
1     Ailourion, collector of the money-tax of Charax,
2     (to) Dioskourides (son of) Psansnos
3     (son of) Phthouminis, [his mother being] Sen..... I have, for
4     land-survey tax of the date-groves, impure thirty-four drachma[s]
5     5 obols, total 34 dr. 5 obols. Year 4 of Antoninus
6     Caesar the lord, Hathyr 1.
7     Ailourion (son of) Mencheus, I have signed.
8     Paid (in full).
