[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Dorotheos, the most-esteemed and most-respectable scholastic, son of the [one] of best memory
2     Theodore Phybis, legal-expert and administrator. You-have-given the owed [amount] from custom
3     from your respectability, public gold to Ciriaca for public-taxes of [the] tenth indiction,
4     that is, [you have given] two gold coins, and for [its] security I-have-made
5     the receipt wholly-written by my-hand. It-was-written Thoth 8, 11th indiction
