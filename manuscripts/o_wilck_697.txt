[TRANSLATION]
1     Pharmouthi 25 of the 24th year, in the name of Herieus
2     (son of) Phaeris the elder (son of) Panames, for the price/tax of wine
3     and dates of the produce of year 23, four drachmas,
4     total drachmas 4. I, Be( ), have signed. Another (payment), Pachon 7,
5     likewise four drachmas, 4 obols, total <drachmas> 4, 4 obols.
6     Be( ): four, 4 obols,
7     total drachmas 4, 4 obols.
