[TRANSLATION]
1     94
2     To Apion, royal scribe of the Prosopite,
3     from Pantbeus son of Phibis.
4     I register, according to what was ordered by Calvisius
5     Statianus, the most illustrious
6     prefect,
7     for the house-by-house census taking place for good ends, the property belonging to me in the village of
8     Thelbonthon Siphtha:
9     a house and vacant plots of Pantbeus
10    son of Phibis the younger, son of Pnepheros,
11    in which:
12    Pantbeus son of Phibis the younger,
13    son of Pnepheros, mother Thasachmounis
14    daughter of Pnepheronphis, lame, aged 69, scar
15    on the left cheek,
16    Taapollos daughter of Orsenoupis,
17    mother Taaronnesis daughter of Anl(?), aged 52,
18    Isidoros son of Pantbeus,
19    son from both parents, aged 3, without distinguishing mark,
20    Pkouthis son of Pantbeus,
21    the brother, mother Thaesis daughter of Perpheis, aged 35,
22    Thermouthis daughter of Nemesas
23    son of Tithoes, mother Tatithoes, the wife, aged 16,
24    Phibis the brother, mother Taapollos daughter of
25    Orsenoupis, aged 21, without distinguishing mark,
26    Thermouthis the sister and wife
27    of the same parents, aged 13,
28    Taaronnesis the sister
29    of the same parents, aged 24.
30    In the 14th year of the Imperator Caesar
31    Marcus Aurelius
32    Antoninus Augustus
33    Armeniacus Medicus
34    Parthicus Germanicus
35    Maximus, Epeiph 25.
36    Pantbeus son of Phibis, the aforesaid,
37    have submitted (this) as is set out. Petos son of Petsiris wrote
38    on his behalf, he not knowing
39    letters.
40    Through Harpok(ration?), of column 42
