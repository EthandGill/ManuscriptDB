[TRANSLATION]
1     In the [consul]ship of F[lavius] Valerius …
2     [A]urelius Pinoution son of Ei…nios …
3     … eight … with a scar on the forefinger of the left hand … ik …
4     of [Se]ntappouo of the Hermopolite nome, to Flavius Taurinos
5     son of [Plousam]mon, biarch of the numerus of the Moors …, greeting.
6     I [ack]nowledge that I have from you and owe you, as capital, of gold, imperial coins, approved, of full weight, eight in number
7     [and a half, total: of gold] coins 8½, which I will repay to you whenever you may
8     [wish,] on condition that I provide you, for the account of profit, for each
9     coin per year one artaba of grain, the time being reckoned from the present month Choiak of the current 1st indiction
10    until the repayment of the capital, it being intact and free of risk,
11    which I will also repay to you together with the
12    accumulated profit, the right of execution belonging to you against both me and out of
13    all my property just as from a judgment; the document is valid, which I have also issued to you for security …
14    I, Aurelius Pinoution son of Ei…nios, the aforesaid, have received and … the
15    eight and a half gold coins, and I will repay with the profit
16    as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Gennadios son of Dionysios, having been requested, wrote on behalf of him
17    … being present and not knowing letters.
18    Through me, [Pho]ibammon, it was written.
