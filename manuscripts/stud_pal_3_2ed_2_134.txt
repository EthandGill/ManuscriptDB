1     I-owe I Argon son of-Laize silversmith
2     of-you Noaimek of-coins Nobaritans of-gold
3     coins eleven, become of-gold coins 11 only,
4     and these to-furnish to-you, whenever you-should-wish[-],

6     of-Dioskoros to-be null and weak, unless
