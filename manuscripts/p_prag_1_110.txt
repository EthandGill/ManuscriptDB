1     Monimos to Heroneinius
2     to the most beloved greetings.
3     I received and today through Ammonius the donkey-driver
4     other single-owner plots twenty
5     four, [they] become single-owner plots 24,
6     into [account] of the winnowing, of which the account
7     I will give. To fare well
8     you I [pray], most beloved.
