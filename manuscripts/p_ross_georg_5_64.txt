[TRANSLATION]
1     Given to the lord Athanasios the domestic through Stephen, measures 36
2     I have inside in the great granary of Meliton, measures [33]
3     From which thus:
4     I have to Sebennytos to my account measures 3, remaining measures 30
5     And likewise I put from the cellar inward into the great granary, measures 125
6     And likewise by order of the lord, grain, measures 46
7     And likewise through my boy Lochios from Phentimin, measures 11
8     There becomes together the sought [items] into the great
9     granary, measures 482
10    From the whole I have to the wine-press measures 4, remaining measures 484
11    And likewise I have measures 2, remaining measures 486, and likewise I have to
12    the account of Simos to Sebennytos measures 11, and [likewise] given to Martereia on behalf of
13    debt through Christopher, measures 36, remaining measures 489
14    Phamenos 18, I have to Sebennytos measures 1, [remaining] [measures] 488
