[TRANSLATION]
1   (2nd hand?) Sheet (kollema).
2   (1st hand) To Protarchos,
3   from Gaius Julius Philios and from Marcus Munatius Epina…,
4   a Persian of the epigone, and his wife Isidora daughter of Areios, a Persian woman,
5   with her guardian, her husband. Concerning the matters at issue: Gaius Jul(ius)
6   Philios agrees that he has received, from both Marcus and Isidora, thr[ough]
7   the money-changing bank of Kastor — toward what Marcus and Isidora took fr(om)
8   him, together with Sextus
9   Popillius Sarapion, who has died: two loans,
10   under agreements made through the same tribunal in the (…) year
11   of Caesar — one, in Mecheir, of four hund[red] drachmas,
12   and the other, in Phamenoth, of one hundred and fif(ty) drachmas,
13   toward the total of 550 drachmas — on account, of silver:
14   two hundred and twenty-two (drachmas); and that there has been full payment
15   also of the interest on both loans, up to Phamenoth
16   of the present 17th year of Caesar, from hand to hand out of the house — so that there remain
17   owed to him, Gaius Julius Philios, by —
18   Marcus and Isidora, of silver, three hun-
19   dred and twenty-eight drachmas; which Philios, on recovering, also
20   […] … …
21   within the time he has allotted to them — of [two] mo[nths]
22   from Pharmouthi of the same year — is to make a discharge of the aforesaid
23   two loan-agreements, and to give back to
24   Isidora — or to whichever of them pays — what he received from her as security in pledge:
25   both a copy of an agreement and a will
26   concerning the slave Zosimos who belongs to her;
27   and if the debtors, Marc(us)
28   and Isidora, do not pay back, when the two months have passed, the 328 silver drachmas,
29   then the right of execution for this too belongs to Gaius Julius
30   Philios: against both of the two — being mutual sureties for the payment — and against one, and against whichever
31   of them he may choose,
32   and against the slave Zosimos and against her other belongings,
33   in accordance with the aforesaid agreements; and if it should hap-
34   pen that the slave run away or even suffer something human (i.e. die),
35   even so the debts are to be free of all
36   risk; and invalid are whatever
37   pleas of trust they may bring forward — [al]l of them — (and) every protection. Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 12.
