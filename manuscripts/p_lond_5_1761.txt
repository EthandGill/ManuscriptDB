[TRANSLATION]
1     [Re]ceipt: the measure subjoined below …
2     and with the one-percent dues and all charges, through the most magnificent comes Pa[l]os son of Ammonios,
3     as follows:
4     Holding of Taurinos son of Epiphanios, from 114 artabas: [57] artabas.
5     Holding of Ammonios, (one) of the tribunes, from 14½ artabas: 7¼ artabas.
6     Holding of Theodotos, from 70 artabas: 35 artabas.
7     Holding of the same, for the vicesima, from 3½ artabas: 1½¼ artaba.
8     Holding of the same, from 26½⅓ (1/12) artabas: 13⅓ (1/8) artabas.
9     Holding of Hyperechia, from 80 artabas: 40 artabas.
10    Holding of Aurelia the most illustrious, from 29½⅓ (1/24) artabas: 14½⅓ (1/12) (1/48) artabas.
11    Holding of Kallinikos son of Zenodotos, from 24¼ artabas: 12 (1/8) artabas.
12    Holding of Artemidora the most illustrious, from 34 artabas: 17 artabas.
13    Holding of the same, for the vicesima, from 1½ artaba: ½¼ artaba.
14    Holding of Sekountilla, [from 19] (1/4) (1/24) [artabas]: 9½ (1/8) (1/48) [artabas].
15    For the divine mon[ks, fr]om 2 artabas: 1 artaba.
16    Holding of Dabrotheos the most magnificent, from 8¼ artabas: 4 (1/8) artabas.
17    Holding of Theodotos, from 5 artabas: 2½ artabas.
18    Of Ioannes son of Hermes, advocate, from 18 artabas: 9 artabas.
19    Acquisition of Hadrianos the illustris, for the lands of Thynis, from 8 artabas: 4 artabas.
20    Acquisition of Eudokia, for Artabour, from 1 artaba: ½ artaba.
21    Acquisition of Hadrianos the illustris, for the same, from ⅓ artaba: (1/6) artaba.
22    [Total:] 230⅓ (1/24) artabas.
23    … of grain, with the f[ar]es … sar prosth, and all charges,
24    through the most magnificent comes Palos son of Ammonios.
