1     Sempronius to Satornila his mother
2     and lady, very many greetings.
3     Before all things I pray that you are in good health, together also with my swee[te]st brothers; and at the same time
4     I make your obeisance daily before the lord Serapis. Having found
5     the one coming to you, sailing up, [I was eager]
6     by a letter to greet you. (You are) asked, my lady, to write to me
7     without hesitation about your safety, so that I may live more free from care. About
8     my garments I will [writ]e to you another time. …
9     … of my friend …

14    a[nd Sa]tor[ni]los, Celer … I will write to you about him and not …
15    … the m[a]n … may learn
16    …
17    … to me something … why do you greatly burden (yourself) under … . May you receive Kopres and Niki… …
18    … . And Herminos …
19    …
20    … came to pass. I pray that you are in good health.
21    Sempronius to Maxim[us his] brother,
22    very many greetings. Before all things
23    I pray that you are in good health. I received your
24    little letter through which you write to me that you have sent me two letters. Know, brother, that
25    I received only one. You write to me
26    about Lobotos that … it is not
27    easy to give everyone what is pleasing.
28    Because of the trouble (I caused?) you … do not
29    blame me as one being neglectful. …
30    On the one hand I stood by reason of my own pressure of business,
31    but all the things that you told me I have fulfilled,
32    and rather quickly through … it is being sent to you. I greet
33    your wife and your children.
34    Farewell to me, [brother].
35    The barygaudes-cloak that you make for me, for which you have made … having purple bands sewn on it for the collar and for the sleeves;
36    plain … let it be … the things for the loins …
37    [Deliver to Maximus my brother, from S]empronius.
