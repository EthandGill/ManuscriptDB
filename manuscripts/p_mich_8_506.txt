[TRANSLATION]
1   [Vale]rianus to Sokrates,
2   greetings.
3   Necessarily come down quick-
4   ly here, since my friend Au-
5   liatos has already promised me, on my coming
6   down, to settle the matter for you,
7   (so) that I may request
8   it. So see that you do not delay, lest
9   suddenly Julius sail away.
10   You will oblige him too, since
11   he is a stranger. There greets
12   you your daughter.
13   (2nd hand) Farewell.
14   [… to So]krates.
