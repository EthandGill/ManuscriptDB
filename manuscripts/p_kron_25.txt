[TRANSLATION]
1     To Asklepiades son of Asklepiad[e]s,
2     from Kronion son of Cheos, of those from
3     the village of Tebtynis. I happen to have leased from you, around Kerkesis, of an allotment twelve and a half arouras
4     and of arable land twelve and a half
5     arouras, and to have sown the twelve and a half arouras of the allotment,
6     with wheat, and the ten [ ] arouras and two and a half arouras with flax-stalk,
7     and to have paid for the arable land eighty drachmas of silver;
8     all (this) in the present thirteenth year of Tra[i]an
9     Caesar the lord, a break having occurred in the adjoining embankment-canal,
10    in the same present thirteenth year, through
11    the negligence of Marion the sower, the said
12    arouras and arable land having become flooded
13    and not being able to be drained off until
14    now, I submit to you this memorandum, so that
15    you may know that I have voluntarily withdrawn from the cultivation and from the works which I accomplished from the present
16    13th year, having fully paid the dues up to the past
17    12th year and of the 12th year itself the rents and taxes,
18    but you holding on and leasing it out to others.
19    Kronion, about 48 years old, with a scar on the t[hird] finger.
20    Year thirteen of Imperator C[ae]s[a]r Ner[va]
21    Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, Phamenoth
22    21.
