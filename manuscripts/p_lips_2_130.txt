[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year 2 of Tiberius Caesar.
2     Issued, Pachon 10.
3     In the second year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, in the month Panemos, the tenth (= Pachon the tenth)
4     in Tebtynis of the division of Polemon
5     of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge,
6     Marsisouchos
7     son of Marepsemis, a Persian of the epigone, about fifty years old, with a scar on his right thumb, and
8     his wife Tamarres daughter of Maron, about thirty years old, with a scar on her left eyebrow, a Persian woman, with
9     her guardian, the
10    aforewritten husband — sureties for one another for
11    payment — that they have received from Kolleutis daughter of Psoiphis,
12    about twenty-two years old, with a scar on her right
13    ankle,
14    with her guardian, her father Psoiphis son of Pnebtynis, about forty years old, with a scar
15    on his forehead, on the left side,
16    the (sum) of silver, coined money, capital
17    of twenty-four drachmas, and in place of the interest on these
18    they have consented to Kolleutis dwelling, until
19    the month Pauni the sixth of the second year
20    of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
21    in the half-share of the common and undivided house
22    in Tebtynis. And she shall use this house in common
23    with all its appurtenances, until, after the time, they shall repay the
24    silver. Subscriber of
25    the acknowledging party: Patynis son of Phalaous, about
26    thirty-five years old, with a scar on his left
27    sole,
28    and of Kolleutis: Marsisouchos son of Marepsemis, about 30 years old.
29    Kolleuchis daughter of Psoiphis: "It has been made over to me." Marsisouchos son of Marepsemis wrote on her behalf.
30    Marsisouchos son of Marepsemis.
31    Marsisouchos son of Marepsemis, a Persian of the epigone,
32    and his wife Tamares daughter of Maron, a Persian woman, with her guardian, her aforewritten husband — sureties for one another for payment — we acknowledge that we have received from Kolleuthis
33    daughter of Psoiphis, with her guardian
34    [Psoiphis,] her father, of silver,
35    [coined money, capital,] twenty-four [drachmas], and in place of the interest, that [Kolleutis] dwell
36    and those (acting) for her, until
37    [Pauni the sixth of] the present second
38    [year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus]
39    [in the half] share of the common and
40    [undivided] house in Tebtynis, and
41    [after the] time we shall repay the (sum of) coined silver, twenty-[four] drachmas. (Someone) wrote on their behalf,
42    [Patynis son of Phalaous,] because they do not [know letters.]
43    of-residence- (?)
44    of drachmas 24 …
45    of Kolleutis daughter of Psoiphis.
