[TRANSLATION]
1     From Lucius Faustinus.
2     Know that our most illustrious hegemon has graciously agreed to
3     transport us the stones
4     from Satali. I have written
5     therefore to you so that no one of those chosen [for] this [goes] to the
6     hegemon. Let [him] know
7     and also Antoninus.
8     Pirmouthi one
