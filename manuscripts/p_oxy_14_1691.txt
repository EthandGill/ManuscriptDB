1     Ailia Herodiana leased and as she is designated
2     To Aurelii Diogenes son of Diogenes from the sacred
3     Assembly and to Philonikos son of Theon
4     Both from the illustrious and most illustrious Oxyrhynchites
5     City, for only the present 8th year and 7th year
6     From those belonging to her around [the] village
7     Senokomein, [of] arouras six from measurement
8     Arouras three communal toward the co-sister of her sister, so as to harvest with linen-stalk, of transport and taxes
9     Per aroura of drachm[as] two-thousand five-hundred, total drachmas [2500], free from all
10    Risk, of the land public taxes being due to the landowner holding the
11    [Fruits until] the amounts owed she receives back.
12    [Being confirmed] of the lease
13    [Let return the lessees the] [amounts ow]ed [in the month] …
