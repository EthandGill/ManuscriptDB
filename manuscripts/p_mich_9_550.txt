1     In the second year of Imperator
2     Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus
3     Germanicus, in the month Xandikos 21, Mecheir 21,
4     in Karanis of the division of Herakleides of the
5     Arsinoite nome. Thatres
6     daughter of Psenamounis, about thirty years old, without distinguishing mark,
7     with as guardian her full brother by the same father and mother, Pakysis, about twenty-
8     five years old, with a scar below the left knee, acknowledges to Pnepheros
9     son of Satabous, about thirty years old, with a scar
10    on the left forearm, on the underside, that she has sold to him,
11    to Pnepheros, the Theban mill belonging to her, Thatres,
12    together with the table and the handle,
13    such as it is, not to be returned, and that
14    Thatres has received from Pnepheros the
15    agreed-upon price of twenty-eight drachmas of silver,
16    paid on the spot, hand to hand, out of the house, and that she, Thatres, and her assigns guarantee it
17    to Pnepheros and his assigns with every
18    guarantee. Subscriber: Heron son of Satyros, about 29 years old, with a scar in the middle of the forehead.
19    I, Thatres daughter of Psenamounis, with as guardian
20    my full brother by the same father and mother, Pakysis,
21    acknowledge that I have sold to Pnepheros son of Satabous
22    the Theban mill belonging to me, together with
23    the table and the handle, such as it is, not to be returned, and I have received
24    the agreed-upon price of twenty[-eight]
25    drachmas of silver, hand to hand, ou[t of the house. Heron son of Satyros]
26    wrote on [their] behalf, [since they do not know letters].
