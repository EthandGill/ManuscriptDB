1     … [from Soknopaiou Ne]sos of the [A]rsi[noite nome] …
2     putting forward … and … [two]
3     on behalf of a son, and one on behalf of [brothers], because their own [proofs] had been presented to the strate[g]os in accordance with what had been ordered, and a letter of Herakleides, strategos of the Arsinoite, of the Herakleides
4     division, having been read, word for word thus: "Herakleides, strategos of the Arsinoite, of the Herakleides division, to Flavi[us] Melas, the most excellent high-priest, greeting:
5     the undersigned priests of the five-tribe (priesthood) of the greatest god Soknopaios and of the temple-sharing gods, of the reputable temple of the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, submitted to me a petition wishing to circumcise in priestly fashion,
6     some their own sons, others kinsmen on the mothers' side of the undersigned, and they presented copies of the house-by-house census-returns of the 16th year
7     of the deified Hadrian, examined from the local library,
8     through which it is shown that their parents were registered as being of priestly
9     descent, and likewise copies of the house-by-house census-returns of the 9th year of Antoninus
10    Caesar the lord, through which it is shown that the parents of the
