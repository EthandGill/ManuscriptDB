[TRANSLATION]
1   In the second year of Antoninus
2   and Verus, the lords
3   Augusti, Epeiph 12. Paid,
4   through Satabous and his fellow collectors of Soknopaiou
5   Nesos, by Panephremmis son of Stotoetis son of
6   Panephremmis, mother Tanephremmis,
7   for the dike-tax of the first year:
8   six drachmas four obols — total 6 dr. 4 ob. ½ ob.; additional charges,
9   receipt-fee.
