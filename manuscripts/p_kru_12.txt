[TRANSLATION]
1     In the na[me of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity,]
2     of the Father and Son and Holy Spirit. Written in the month Choiak [12, indiction 2,]
3     under our master Argama son of Erd, most glorious amir of the city of Rmo[nt].
4     Chael, administrator of the fortress of the Memnonia, (and) the elders.
5     I, [Pa]thermouthios, the son of the late Konstantine, the man of the fortress
6     (of) Djeme of the nome of the city of Rmont, will provide afterwards a subscriber to subscribe for me below this written deed of sale,
7     which is written, which is set down, and is not to transgress against it and not to annul
8     it and not to do away with it by means of the laws; and moreover (acting)
9     to confirm it through other trustworthy witnesses for me who shall witness to it for me, according to my own request and of my own will and with my readiness, I write to you, Aron son of Senouthios, the man of this same fortress (of) Djeme
10    of the nome of this city of Rmont, greeting: that I, this same one, acknowledge — I,
11    Patermouthios the son of the late Konstantine — by a written deed of sale, written, drawn up, in a single matter, this for which we exchanged our agreement, willing and persuaded, without any deceit
12    or fear or violence or fraud or extortion or circumvention,
13    but with my own free choice, I swearing by God
14    the Almighty and by the salvation of the rulers who govern us:
15    I sell to you, you Aron son of Senouthios, the buyer,
16    and that which I formerly wrote from now unto eternity, at every time
17    to come hereafter, my share of these (rooms?) which are upon the courtyard, the house of Saneth,
18    these which are set down in the fortress (of) Djeme, that is, the four houses of mine, those (same ones),
19    these which came to me from my fathers, from their foundations up to their tops, with their utensils that pertain to them, that is, these are the boundaries of those houses:
20    these are: south, Lasan (and) Paman; west, Palou (and) Pei[bt, Pau]los; north,
21    the gate of the Authentes; and the courtyard, outside the bounds of those houses,
22    these are them. The price (is) this for which we agreed, this gold solidus, one gold-coin (νόμισμα),
23    of the standard of this same fortress (of) Djeme; the price of this sort came to my hand
24    from your hand, you Aron son of Senouthios the buyer, so that
25    from now unto eternity you may come in and become the lord
26    of these four houses, to beget them for you (as your own), to administer it, to adorn them, to give them as a dowry, to bestow them as a gift,
27    to cede them, to leave them to your children and to your children's children,
28    to use them in every manner that is yours and (in your) possession unto eternity
29    at any time. And it is not permitted to me to bring suit against you, neither I nor
30    brother nor sister nor son nor son nor heir nor
31    descendant of descendant, neither in a tribunal nor outside a tribunal nor in a city nor in a district nor outside the bounds of the district of those (places). I am quit
32    of any (claim). And the second one (who comes forward), let him be a stranger to the holy creed
33    of the Christians, whom the Father and the Son serve
34    and the Holy Spirit; and afterwards let him pay the reckoning of a fine, the authority that shall rule over us at that time (requiring it),
35    twelve gold solidi; and after the payment of this fine, the one who shall do a deed of this sort
36    (must) still abide by it, and let him be made to come
37    in and confirm this written deed of sale, this
38    which I have established for you and for those who come and follow you.
39    For your security I have established this written deed of sale,
40    firm (and) valid in every place; and afterwards I summoned trustworthy witnesses, and they witnessed to it
41    for me; they read it to me, and I heard it, and I gave order, and they wrote it
42    according to the procedure of the laws.
43    I, Psere the son of the late Petros, by the spirit of God
44    presbyter and the superior of the holy church of Djeme, give witness,
45    at the request of Patermouthios.
46    I, Demetros (son) of the late Leontios, give witness.
47    I, Nohe the son of Ieremias, the presbyter and
48    the superior of the holy church
49    of Djeme, give witness, at the request.
50    I, Samuel the son of the late Enoch, give witness,
51    at the request of Patermouthios the son of Konstantine.
52    I, Kollouthos the son of the late Iohannes, give witness, at the request of the one who established it.
53    I, Chmtsneu, the least presbyter and superior,
54    the son of Senouthios, of the holy church
55    of Djeme, I wrote it with my hand, at the request of Patermouthios with … Written in the month Choiak … [in]diction the second.
