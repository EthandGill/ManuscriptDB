[TRANSLATION]
1     … (him) being maintained for the festival (days)
2     [by me, Kolluthos, and] (that) I am [not] able to
3     [draw] this (man) [away before the comple]tion of the same one year
4     … on any day whatsoever, without ill-treatment
5     [and (apart from)] the holy Lord's day and the great feasts; but if it should
6     [happen] that I draw him away from you, or that he by himself
7     [withdraw] before the completion of the same one year,
8     [I agree] to provide to you, by way of penalty, nine carats of gold
9     , together also with (the repayment of) whatever I may be found to have received from you of the wage of silver,
10    without any dispute or delay; and for this
11    security I have made for you this agreement, it being valid
12    and secure, and on being formally questioned about everything I acknowledged (it), having pledged
13    to you for this all the things now belonging and to belong to me.
14    I, Aurelius Kolluthos, son of Alos, tow-worker (stippourgos), the aforewritten,
15    (this) is satisfactory to me in all respects as is aforesaid. I, Aurelius Victor son of Petros, from Antinoopolis,
16    being requested, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate. I, Aurelius
17    Ioannes son of Georgios, from Antinoopolis, bear witness to the agreement, having heard (it) from
18    Kolluthos who made it. I, Dorotheos son of Ioannes, notary, from Antinoopolis, bear witness
19    to the present agreement, having heard (it) from him who made it.
20    [Through] me, Aur[elius V]ictor, contract-writer (symbolaiographos), it was written.
21    … Victor …
