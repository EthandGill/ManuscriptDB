r.3    δὸς Ἰωσῆφ καὶ Δίῳ οἴνου μάριον
r.4    ἓν γίνεται οἴνου μάριον α καὶ διʼ υἱοῦ Ἀκώου

[TRANSLATION]
1     to the beloved Palamon
2     John your father greets
3     give to Joseph and to Dios one metretes of wine
4     one becomes wine metretes one, and through the son of Akous
5     tiphakia twenty becomes tophakia twenty
6     I John have signed; and
7     through the son of Hatrous of silvery-oil
8     one sextarius becomes sextarius one
9     be well greatly, 9th indiction
