[TRANSLATION]
1     in the name of the [god,] [philanthr]opic,
2     merciful. ...
3     There is no god except the god alone.
4     Muhammad [is the] apostle of god.
5     Abdelaziz, son
6     of Maruan, counselor. 8th indiction.
7     with god. Flavius Atias, son of Godeos, to you who dwell at Caucoi, to the mountain of the Memnonians.
8     since I have examined the matter of those sought [from you]
9     from a register and wished to demand from you such a register,
10    just as also the other monasteries give their registers because
11    you were ungrateful in the time of the rebellion, you sent ahead to me a sigil
12    of Woeith, who once governed the upper country, containing that you remain
13    in your place, performing your registers, and having confirmed
14    the same sigil, I have used the present sigil, through which I permit you
15    fearlessly to remain in stability in your place and to have cause for not
16    allowing me to pass through you in any way; you, however, guarding
17    your peaceful condition and performing your register,
18    which you neglected, as was said, in the time of the rebellion; and to make [it]
19    clear, I have used the present sigil, having affixed to it also
20    my seal. It was written in the month of Phaophi, the 20th, of the 11th indiction.
21    Greek.
