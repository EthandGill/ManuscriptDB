[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 18th year of Lucius Septimius Severus Pertinax
2     and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus and Publius Septimius
3     Geta, the Pious Augusti. Syros son of Heron and Pinis son of Eudaimon
4     and Didas son of Karas and partners, granary-officials (sitologoi) of the village of Euhemeria.
5     There were measured out to us on the 30th of the month Pachon
6     of the present 18th year, from the produce of the same year, by the
7     public smoothed measure, to Aeious son of Pounas, of public wheat eighty-one artabas, two-thirds and a twenty-fourth; total: 81 5/6 1/24 artabas.
8     The same man, Pauni 9, of public (wheat), another twenty-two artabas of wheat; total: 22 artabas.
9     The same man, Pauni 22, of public (wheat), another forty wheat artabas and a twenty-fourth; total: 40 1/24 artabas. The same man, Pauni 29,
10    of public wheat, two artabas, a third and a twenty-fourth; total: 2 1/3 1/24 artabas.
11    The same man, Epeiph 10, of public (wheat), another twenty-five artabas of wheat;
12    total: 25 artabas. Total of the receipt: 171 ⅛ wheat artabas. Epeiph 24,
13    another seventeen artabas of wheat, a quarter and a twenty-fourth; total: 17 ¼ 1/24 artabas. And another, likewise, of wheat,
14    five artabas; total: 5 artabas. Mesore 10, another four artabas of wheat; total: 4 artabas. Another six artabas of wheat for freightage; total: 6 artabas.
15    Total altogether: 203 1/3 1/12 wheat artabas. Thoth 1 …; likewise another, of public
16    wheat, fifteen artabas and a third; total: 15 1/3 artabas.
17    Phaophi 9, the same man, another four artabas of wheat;
18    total: 4 wheat artabas. Total altogether: 222 ¾ wheat artabas. Thoth 5,
19    eight artabas of barley. Syros son of Heron and Pinis son of Eudaimon, through Amous
20    also called Zenon, the assistant: we have measured out as stated above.
