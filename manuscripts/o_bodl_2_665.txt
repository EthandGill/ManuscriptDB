[TRANSLATION]
1     Imouthes and associates [collectors of monetary taxes] of the metropolis to Abotos and
2     to Phthuminis Harbechios and Harbechei
3     son. We have received for the bath-tax of Charakos year 17 filthy one obol three and a half
4     and for the share of withdrawn money eight obols.
5     Year 17 of Trajan the lord
6     Mesore 12. I have registered.
