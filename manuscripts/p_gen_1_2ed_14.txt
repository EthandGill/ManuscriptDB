[TRANSLATION]
1     To my good master after God, the all-praiseworthy and God-protected Kyros,
2     from Eulogetos your servant.
3     Repeatedly, good master, I sought to approach (you), and fear
4     hindered me. But having heard that the God-protected Kyris ordered that everyone in need
5     should make use of an approach, by which I beg and entreat to obtain mercy together with my
6     lowly children on account of my great straits. For I have nothing else
7     to sell, as the Lord knows, but I have also received affection through … ; for I sought also to
8     receive the freight-charges of the little boat from the sailor for my expense and, as the same sailor said,
9     the count Herakles withheld them. And God is my witness, God-protected
10    master, repeatedly I was in danger of fleeing into the diocese on account of (people) not despising me
11    concerning the glorious affairs, but also … the thorny goods (?) I loaded into the same
12    little boat, bought from a merchant for about nine solidi, and I have the receipts
13    for them; for all the rest was sent to me from Alexandria, and I still owe
14    a portion there; and I owe here too rents and expenses, and I am buying the very boat
15    itself, since my poor beasts too have been carried off. And on every side I am in straits.
16    Lend therefore on my behalf also, master, to the benevolent God; for he who shows mercy [to the poor],
17    as you know, lends to God. As God knows, master, I sought to send my fellow-servant and [her child]
18    to the lady Joannia to my other daughter.
19    And having nothing that I can give them, I did not do this. Through a good
20    judgment, the God-protected Kyris is able both to have mercy on me and to order …
21    the little boat, if it is pleasing to your good soul, and that I may also from the …
22    find some little relief of my debts. As Kyris therefore is good …
23    forgive me, who am in great poverty … neither …
24    as the Lord God holds free of blame the all-praiseworthy and God-protected …
25    and may he take thought for me with my children and other strangers. I pray …
26    master, that being customarily deemed worthy of support …
27    to the rewarding God for the salvation and well-being and … [of the]
28    all-praiseworthy and God-protected lords and of (us) sinners …
