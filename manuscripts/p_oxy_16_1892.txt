1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious
2     master, the greatest benefactor, Fl[avius] [Tiberius]
3     Constantine the eternal A[ugustus]
4     and Emperor, in the 7th year, in the consulship of the [same]

6     To the reverend Epimachos, overseer [of the]
7     divine house, son of Justus of blessed [memory,]
8     from this splendid city of the Oxy[rhynchites,]
9     (from) Areotes, presbyter of the holy ch[urch,]
10    son of Phib, his mother being Erebekka, originating
11    from the estate of Apel of the Oxyrhynchite [nome,]
12    greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from [your]
13    reverence on loan, by hand, from the house,
14    for my necessary needs, three gold solidi
15    reckoned by the private standard,
16    that is 3 gold solidi by the private standard; and these I acknow[ledge]
17    that I will furnish to your reverence in [three]
18    installments thus: in the month of Ha[thy]r
19    of the present year 258 = 227, of the [present]
20    fifteenth indiction, one gold solidus,
21    and in the month Hathyr of the coming first
22    indiction, another one gold solidus,
23    and in the month Hathyr of the [coming] second
24    indiction, the remaining [one] solidus,
25    without deduction; and if I [act in bad faith] concerning the
26    repayment of the first [or second] or third installment,
27    your reverence is to take possession
28    and enjoy that which belongs to me, my own privately-owned
29    inherited half-aroura, situated
30    in the waterwheel called Kylles … ,
