[TRANSLATION]
1     To the Good Demon and Polemon librarians
2     from Petenephotes Sarapas son of Sarapas
3     mother Taos of the Pathyrite, from the city of Oxyrhynchus. I register additionally what has come to me from the name of my mother Taos of the Pathyrite
4     of the Pathyrite mother Arseite of Seleucus from
5     the same city, having died intestate
6     grandsons from other children not having before me the elder and [to] my brothers of the same mother
7     Diogas and Sarapas and Diogenidas, the four only children and heirs, share two from parts five which had
8     the mother in village Takolicheili from bare ground, a brick-house one. Whom indeed brick-house
9     the mother did not register, and divided to her
10    this [the] father of hers Pathres of the Pathyrite of Amullos mother Taurios from Takolicheili
11    from bare ground of his through the lying
12    to her towards the father of us Sarapas son of Sarapas of Pyros mother Diogenidas
13    from the same city of the marriage contract,
14    having come into being through a record office upper toparchy on the
15    appended of the sixteenth year god
16    Trajan, on which lawful and irrevocable being he died Pathres nothing having acted against the division. The very same brick-house one I declare to be, from which registered
17    the Pathres as Athres of Atrenos and paternal
18    according to mother of Horus for the month Pharmouth of the nineteenth year god Hadrian before Saras of Thonis of the Pathyrite of the also Amullos from Takolicheili brick-houses two, as the registration of him contains. And I swear the
19    Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus
20    sacred pious true to be the forewritten. Of the eleventh year Emperor Caesar
21    Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus
22    sacred pious of month Hadrian five.
