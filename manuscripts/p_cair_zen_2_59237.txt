1     Dionysodorus to Zenon
2     greetings. You would do well
3     [having purchased] for me
4     a metretes of honey, one,
5     just as [and] to Nikon
6     you purchase, and as to this one
7     you might send, and
8     to me this having done.
9     And the price to whom [is necessary]
10    to repay write to me.
11    Fare well.

13    Of year 33, Pharmouthi 11 [Dionysodorus]
14    concerning honey.
