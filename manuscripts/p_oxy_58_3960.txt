[TRANSLATION]
1     To the glorious house of the late Apion of illustrious memory, situated
2     also in this city of the Oxyrhynchites. Account of receipts and expenditures
3     made through me, John, wine-steward, of the payment of the 9th indiction, of the year 297 and 266. Of receipts thus:
4     … of those from Leonides and Limenias, from 7022 jars of wine: jars of wine 6200
5     … of wine: jars 544
6     … of wine: jars 350
7     …es, of wine: jars 400
8     … of wine: jars 2280
9     … of (the) Estate (Chorion), of wine: jars 1236
10    …ios, of wine: jars 2(000)
11    …ios, of wine: jars 1(000)
12    and the (jars) demanded from those of Episemos on account of the Persians, of wine: jars 498
13    those purchased from those of Peleas for 25 solidi, at 18 jars of wine per solidus, makes of wine: jars 450
14    those purchased from those of Apollon for another 36 solidi, at 24 jars of wine per solidus, makes of wine: jars 864
15    those purchased from those of Pousempoys for solidi, at … jars per solidus
16    makes of wine: jars 800, of which there was given back to them in vinegar
17    jars 117, remaining of wine: jars 683
18    makes, of the total intake of the wine-steward of the 9th indiction payment,
19    of wine jars: 3 myriads and 3298 (= 33,298)
20    of which there was expended in the same 9th indiction
21    to the holy churches and hostels and martyr-shrines of the city and
22    in the fields by way of offering, in accordance with the schedule (brevium) that is in the cartulary,
23    in the 9th indiction, with vinegar, of wine: jars 8458
24    to the pious monasteries and to the widows and the infirm by way of piety, of wine: jars 576
25    to the office-attendants (taxeotai) for the customary (gratuities) by usage in the 9th indiction, of wine: jars 366
26    to the loader 100 jars and to the superintendent (meizoteros) 150 jars, with freight of the barge-men 42 jars and to others 260 jars, of wine: jars 322
27    those distributed (as rations) to the Persians in accordance with the register that is in the cartulary, of wine: jars 7281
28    to the free attendants (paramonarioi) for wages of the 9th indiction, of wine: jars 8906
29    to the Egyptian se[rvant-boys] for wages of the 9th i[ndiction], of wi[ne ja]rs … 348
30    and there remained outstanding (in arrears) at the following places in the same 9th indiction
31    thus:
32    with those from Leonides and Limenias, of wine: jars 1166
33    with those from Notinou Chorion, of wine: jars 40
34    with those from Adaios, of wine: jars 225
35    with those from Partheniados, of wine: jars 153
36    with those from Orthonios, of wine: jars 514
37    with those from Kotyleion, from 12 solidi, by private standard,
38    for 600 jars of wine, of which there was given to the tribune
39    for the ration (roga) of the Persians, by private standard, 7⅓ solidi
