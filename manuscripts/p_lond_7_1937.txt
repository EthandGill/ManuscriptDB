[TRANSLATION]
1     Nikon to Zenon [greetings]. And many times indeed we wrote to you about both the spelt and the
2     barley that it is [unsold, but] nothing have either you or Apollonius written back to us.
3     Still therefore even now [it would be good if you] would write to us about these things. Know that the
4     barley we find [at artabas 3 3] for the tetradrachm, and the spelt at artabas 5. Apollonius has ordered
5     to sell into the Ch[oiak at about] the artaba [at] five obols, so that the difference beside a stater [is one]
6     obol.
7     Farewell. Year 28, Mechir 8.
8     Year 28, Dystrus 11. [To Zenon].
9     Nikon [concerning] spelt
10    [and] barley unsold.
