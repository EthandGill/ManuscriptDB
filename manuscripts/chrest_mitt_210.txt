[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the keepers of the property-]registers of the Hermopolite (nome),
2     [from Aurelius Tithoeti]on also called Sarapammon, son of Hermeinos,
3     [a Hermopolite registered] in the quarter of the western fort. I register for the present 4th [year of Marcus A]ur[elius]
4     [Antoninus Caesar the] lord, with the consent of Aurelia Kollauchis daughter of Thaesis, granddaughter of Kollouchis, [from the village of Moir]on of the Upper Koussite (toparchy), with as guardian Aurelius Sar[as son of Hor]eis,
5     [who was requested by her] and permitted by Aurelius Kastor also called Th[a]umastos, the serving exegete and councillor of the same city of [Hermes], the right of what he has mortgaged to me by contract [in] the same 4th year
6     [and month], around the same Moiron, from the catoecic allotments of Dionysios and Diokles,
7     [of three arouras] being in two beds, of which of one bed (there are) two arouras, of the second bed [one] aroura,
8     [against which] he owes me, according to the same contract, of capital and drachma-interest, eight hundred [forty] drachmas, for repayment in Thoth of the coming 5th year, on the other terms which the contract contains, [of which the] copy I have submitted to you in an extraordinary (filing). On account of which I report and swear the
9     fortune of [Marcus Aurelius An]toninus Caesar the lord that this is so. Year 4
10    [of Imperator Ca]esar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Pius Felix
11    [Augustus] Hadrianus, 19. I, Aurelius Tithoetion also called Sarapammon, have submitted (this) and sworn
12    [the oath]. I, Aurelia Kollauchis daughter of Thaesis, consent. I, Aurelius Saras son of Horeis, the appointed guardian, have been registered as her authority and have written on her behalf, she not knowing
13    [letters].
