1     To Aurelia Diogenida, also called Turbaina, most honored,
2     from Aurelius Pagenus
3     guardian of property
4     Senepta, greetings.
5     I request [that it] be given to brick-makers for [payment of] extraordinary [expenses]
6     of the molded brick
7     to Cornelius and Lau and Phant
8     [in] account of extraordinary [expenses],
9     of which I will give account: wheat
10    artabas three, lentils artaba 1½,
11    of vinegar karmaia three, of oil
12    radish-oil hemina 12.
13    Year 11 and Year 10 and Year 3, Phamenoth
14    25.
