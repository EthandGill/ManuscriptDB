[TRANSLATION]
1     Year sixteen of Lucius Septimius
2     Severus Sebos Pertinax and Marcus
3     Aurelius Antoninus Sebos Sebastoi and Publius
4     Septimius Geta Caesar Augustus, Mesore 9, by count of Epeiph.
5     Abous Satab[ou]tos wrote out for the Anthian property
6     tax of year 16 of Soknopaiou Nesou four hundred drachmas, totaling drachmas 400
7     and on the 10th the same [person] wrote out for the Anthian property tax of year
8     16 of Soknopaiou Nesou one hundred drachmas, totaling drachmas 100, symbolic 1
9     and on the 11th the same [person] wrote out for the same property of the village
10    … t[ax] of year 16 of Soknopaiou Nesou one hundred drachmas, totaling drachmas 100.
