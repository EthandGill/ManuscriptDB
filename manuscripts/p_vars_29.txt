[TRANSLATION]
1     from
2     [as] it seemed for the things discussed between us has come about, and I ask therefore of you
3     [your] [honor] to await still until the tomorrow, until [we] receive the thing made
4     [for] benefit. For they chose to send to them tomorrow … camels the
5     [bearing] fifty-four artabas of wheat. Receiving therefore these ascend
6     [the] estate herds, until when again under your… I am, and I will make recompense; for indeed
7     [the] ones come-together under all there have sworn an oath upon sacred things, and one might say to guard
8     [the] baskets and not allow to be broken anything of them, until we are satisfied.
