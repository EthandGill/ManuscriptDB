[TRANSLATION]
1     I, P… [?] and Palaeus son of Pekusius, farmer from the estate Tapourshire of the Heracleopolite nome, received from you Chrystos, the honorable administrator of the renowned house of Apiôn the most illustrious proconsul
2     from Heracles city for the wage of irrigation which I perform of the vineyards under your honor
3     from the estate Tapourshire, division of Paomis, Kelebes vineworker, at the completion of the present eleventh indiction in full
4     Another one gold arsatikon coin; there is one gold arsatikon coin. It was written in the month Mesore
5     of the epagomenal days 3, 11 indiction, through me Pseëius
