1     Thonis to Syras and Kallinikos, my lords the children, in God the Lord,
2     very many greetings.
3     I make your obeisance every day before the Lord God. I want you to know that I was detained by the
4     Oxyrhynchites and they made me give a bond in writing to come down with your brothers by the twentieth of Phamenoth, and I am very much hemmed in lest I be unable to come down by the appointed date, because the
5     husband of your sister Theodora died, and I am
6     in dispute with his brother concerning the affairs of her daughter. You will do well, then, my lady daughter, to inquire diligently what the others too who have given bonds in writing intend to do. But do not be negligent.
7     For I expect to depart by the second
