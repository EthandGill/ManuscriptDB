[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: . . . Caesar . . . Augustus Imperator, son of the divine Augustus, that I have indeed honestly and truthfully submitted the above (return?) of . . . women . . . who live with me . . . , to which everyone’s . . . is annexed, and that no one else, neither Roman nor freedman nor Alexandrian nor alien nor anyone else at all, lives with me except for the above-written. If I observe the oath, may it be well with me, but if I swear falsely, the reverse. Year 20 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Phamenoth 11. (N. Gonis)
