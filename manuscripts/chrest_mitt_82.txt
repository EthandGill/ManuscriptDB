[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a record of proceedings.
2     Year 7 of the deified Trajan, Pauni. Akylas,
3     the appointed judge-mediator of Apollonios
4     son of Herakleios against Dionysios son of Apollonios:
5     from what I have heard, I have given my decision: that 11 arouras were bought
6     from Claudia Eudaimonis, and 11 arouras were bought
7     from Demetrios, and 24 arouras from Hermaios, and 4 arouras
8     from Herodion and Soterichos and Theon, and 14½ arouras
9     from Claudia Eudaimonis, and 11 arouras from the same (woman), and
10    13½ arouras from Demetria, and 1 ⅛ 1/16 arouras from Menelaos, and 11½ arouras from Origenes; which Apollonios, having purchased — the father of Dionysios and grandfather of Apollonios —
11    made the purchases in the name of his son Dionysios,
12    after the time of the agreement made between him and Isidora also called Tatreiphis
13    with Laodike daughter of Apollonios, in the
14    7th year of Domitian; they appear to be
15    held in common by Dionysios son of Apollonios
16    and Apollonios son of Herakleios. Likewise
17    the slaves (lit. bodies) Harmatys, Ortygas, Horisas,
18    Euemeros, Heraklas, Hermaios, purchased in the same manner, appear to be common to them; likewise a talent
19    that was lent from the (funds) of Apollonios
20    the grandfather, used for the
21    name of Dionysios, owed by Hermaios the younger,
22    appears to be common to the same persons.
23    For concerning the revenues of the maternal
24    estate that came down to Apollonios son of Herakleios,
25    I was unable to establish anything, because
26    it is agreed by both parties that the
27    pleadings concerning them are not (to be heard) here.
28    [I, Phibis] son of Kastor, have submitted (this) and
29    have sworn the oath according to the law.
30    I, (son) of Apygchis, wrote on behalf of him,
31    since he writes slowly. I, Phibis
32    son of Kastor, requested him.
