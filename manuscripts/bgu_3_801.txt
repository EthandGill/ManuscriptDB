[TRANSLATION]
1   To Nilos, (her) brother,
2   from Tasucharion.
3   Very grievously did I
4   hear (the news) from Taonnophris.
5   Bear it nobly,
6   brother, for the sake of
7   your children; and if not,
8   (remember) that my children are
9   abroad. Ptolemais
10   and Sarapion and I
11   myself were going up (to you).
12   Receive from
13   the one delivering to you this
14   letter
15   sweetmeats, to the
16   number of one hundred (and) six-
17   ty, and ten pine-cones
18   for the offering.
19   [...]
20   Deliver to Nilos son of Tasal☓os, from Tasucharion (his) sister.
