1     Measurement of treasury of metropolis, product of year twenty-seven
2     of Commodus Caesar the lord
3     Payni twenty-six, on behalf of Notus by name Horus
4     the younger Pakoibios, wheat artabai two, total wheat artabai two.
5     Amonios has marked [it].
6     and by name Pete Petach Akourios
7     wheat artabai two, total wheat artabai two. Amonios has marked [it].
