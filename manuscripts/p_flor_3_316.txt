1     [Year fifteen] of emperor Caesar
2     …, month of Augustus Thoth, in Philadelphia of the Herakleid division of the Arsinoit nome.
3     [loaned] Cho… she and [P]etron[i]lla, through a manager
4     Lucius Herennius Heron [aged] fifty-eight years, unmarked, to Pasion son of Rhodon, freedman
5     of Pasion, aged fifty years, marked with [a scar on the]
6     [right eye], and to Phasit son of Sokmenios, aged

8     [drachmae] hundred sixty through hand, of interest
9     [drachmic of the] min[a] per month; and itself [the] capital
10    [and wheat artabs] fifteen by measure of [the]
11    [of Petro]nilla let them repay, those [having borrowed] to [the] [one having loaned] Petronilla or [the ones beside her]
12    [in month] of the present [year 15] [of Antoninus]
