[TRANSLATION]
1     Eudaimon son of Eudaimon, from the city of Oxyrhynchos, leased to Dionysios son of Dionysios, his mother being
2     Thaesis, from the (district) around Pela of Lenon, a Persian
3     of the epigone, for six years from the coming twelfth
4     year of Hadrian Caesar the lord, the arouras belonging
5     to him around Paeimis, ten and a half and a third,
6     no re-survey being made, so that each year
7     the one half he is to sow with wheat, and the other half
8     to plant with green crops for fodder, at a fixed rent for all the arouras each year of ninety-one artabas of wheat, free of all
9     risk; and if any (land) becomes unwatered, allowance will be made to the lessee, the annual public dues of the land
10    being the responsibility of the lessor, who is also to be master of the crops until
11    he recovers what is his own. And, the lease being guaranteed, the lessee is to pay to the
12    lessor the annual fixed rent, always
13    in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor of Lenon, wheat that is new,
14    pure, unadulterated, without barley, sifted, as measured into the public granary
15    by the half-artaba measure of Diogenes son of Alexandros
16    from Pela; or let him forfeit whatever he may still owe with an increase of one half, and let the lessor have the right of execution both against the lessee and against all his property, it not being permitted to the lessor to … an over-payment (?) nor
17    to sublet to others nor to farm it himself within
18    the period. The lease is valid. Year 11 of the Emperor
19    Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Mesore 22.
20    I, Dionysios son of Dionysios, have leased …
21    …
