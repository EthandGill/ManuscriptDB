[TRANSLATION]
1     By authority of Aurelius Tiberius, the most excellent juridicus (dikaiodotes).
2     All who have grain in the city and in the nome, let them register it, so that the city too may have provisions and the
3     public needs may be met, until tomorrow, which is
4     Phamenoth 22, this being without penalty to them, for one shall receive
5     the price which our most illustrious prefect fixed, of 6 denarii,
6     knowing that if anyone be found not having registered, not
7     only the wheat but also the house where it is found shall be confiscated
8     to the most sacred treasury. Year 3, Phamenoth 21.
9     Copy of the registration.
10    To Aurelius Tiberius, the most excellent juridicus, from Calpurnia Heracleia also called Eudamia
11    daughter of Calpurnius Theon, of those maintained in the Museum and as he was styled,
12    through Aurelius Pekyllos also called Theon, ex-gymnasiarch, ex-prytanis, councillor
13    of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, and Chairemon also called Demet… and as he is styled, guardians: I register, in accordance with
14    the orders given by you, the wheat which I have under (the charge of) agents,
15    namely in my possession at Souis 23 artabas, and at that of Dositheos 245 artabas …
16    and at the Iseion of Tryphon 220 artabas, and at Thmoinepsobthis 460 artabas, and at Lile 280 artabas
17    [a]nd at that of Satyros [820 artabas] …
18    and from the aforesaid (amounts) monthly allowances are given to agents and
19    to managers [and] cultivators and slave-boys and monthly hired-men,
20    and at that of Satyros … exchanged, further from the past month
21    Mecheir to Kopreus and … pos to the cooks of the city, because by reason of age
22    the oil had spoiled: 287 artabas.
23    Year 3, Phamenoth 22.
