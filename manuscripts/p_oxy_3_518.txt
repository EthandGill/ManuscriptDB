[TRANSLATION]
1   Measured into the public (granary), of wheat of the produce of the past
2   19th year of the Aurelii Antoninus
3   and Commodus, Caesars the
4   lords, through the sitologoi of the western toparchy, district of Enteleu:
5   to Sarapion son of Charisios, on deposit, artabas 4,
6   total 4 art. of wheat. I, Diogenes, sitologos, have signed for the 4 art. of wheat.
