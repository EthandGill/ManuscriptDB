r.1    τῷ κυρίῳ Κολλούθῳ Ἱερακίονος Θεόφιλος νοτάριος [ὑπὲρ]
r.2    τῆς θεοσεβεστάτης κυρίας Θεοδώρας. ἐδεξάμην παρ[ὰ σοῦ]

[TRANSLATION]
1     To the lord Kollouthos (son) of Hierakion, Theophilus notary [on behalf of]
2     the most pious lady Theodora. I received from [you]
3     according to the command of my lady [artabae of] wheat
4     thirty of dry [grain], there become thirty artabae of wheat alone. It was written in the month of Phamenoth
5     10th of the 12th indiction. Theophilus is surety for me.
6     small label … wheat … thirty artal[bae] of wheat.
