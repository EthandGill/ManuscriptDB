[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 12 of our lord Gallien[us Aug]ustus, Epeiph 27.
2     Aurelius Agath[os] Daimon, having served as cosmete, councillor, decaprotus of the 2nd toparchy of Polemon's division
3     I have measured on behalf of levied tax in the treasur[y] of Teptuneis from the produce of
4     the same year by public measure stroked [with a rod imposed] of name Plutio from Kerkesouchi on behalf of public [matters] of the same village wheat artabas
5     three and one-half and one-quarter, barley three [and one-]twelfth, [they] total wheat artabas 3 and one-half and one-quarter, barley artabas 3 and one-twelfth,
6     and of silver drachmas 5. Aurelius Agath[os] Daimon, having served as cosmete, councillor, I have marked the [wheat]
7     artabas three [and one-half] and one-quarter barley 3 and one-twelfth.
