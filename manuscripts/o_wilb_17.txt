[TRANSLATION]
1     To Erophimis, collector of silver [tax]
2     To Paoukales, Pachnumius
3     [Son] of Paouphtheiotus. I have received for [the] dike-tax and [the] bath-tax
4     Of Charakas, 15th year, dirty drachmas seven, obols 4½
5     It amounts to dirty drachmas 7, obols 4½. Year 16 of Hadrian
6     The master, Hathur 1.
