1   (2nd hand?) Sheet (kollema).
2   (1st hand) To Protarchos,
3   from Philammon son of Ammonios and f(rom) Deme(trios) son of Sarap(ion), of the Philome(torean) deme.
4   Concerning the matters agr[eed,] De(metrios) [conce]des
5   that neither they nor any other on their behalf will proceed against
6   Philammo(n) over what they have paid through the bank
7   toward the price of papyrus rolls in the 13th (year) of Caesar, in the Epagomenai — Demetrios one hundred drachmas through the
8   mo(ney-changing) bank of Dionysios, and Patron through the s(ame)
9   bank in the same (year) 200 (drachmas), making …
10   three hundred drach[mas] …; because, over the three hundred drachmas all told, Demetrios
11   has been satisfied by Philammo(n) and has rece(ived) from him, from
12   hand to hand out of the hou(se), what was mu-
13   tually agreed; the said bank-payments are forthwith void; and he will also fur-
14   nish
15   Patron not proceeding against the same Philammo(n) about the two hundred silver
16   (drachmas), [nor]
17   about anything else at all, written or unwritten, up to the pres(ent) day,
18   and no
19   right of approach is left to Deme(trios) against Philammo(n) about anything, like-
20   wise written or unwritten, from
21   the same day; and that, should anyone proc[eed] against Philammo(n) about the
22   same, Demetrios himself will make him desi[st]
23   at once at his own exp(ense), and will pay him whatever be exacted from or los[t] by Philammon on
24   account of this, with ha(lf again), and the damages and ex[penses,] as if b[y judg(ment),]
25   [or —] the agr(eed terms) remaining nonetheless valid —
26   (he is liable) for the appoin(ted) fi(ne).
