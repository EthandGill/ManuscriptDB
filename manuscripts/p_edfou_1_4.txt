[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving Trinity, of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Epeiph, at the beginning of the 1st indiction.
2     The Aurelii Dios and Isakos and Mousaios and Eudoxias and Athanasias, full
3     brothers (and sisters), on the father's side of John also called … pouki, on the mother's side of … ora, originating from
4     Apollonos Ano, to the Aureliae Thekl[a] … [daugh]ter of Abraamios son of Enoch, on the mother's side of Taam
5     Kotsi, and to Maria also called Tloisa, and to Maria the sister, on the father's side of Danielios
6     on the mother's side of Tsiphous, and to S … daughter of Isakios, on the mother's side of Sophia, from the same
7     Apollonos, greeting. We acknowledge that we have had and borrowed fr[om yo]u
8     today, for our own necessary need, of gold solid[i two]
9     and a third by the standard of Apollonos, that is, gold solidi 2 1/3 only; and these, with God, we are r[eady]
10    to provide to you in seven instalments thus: at a third of a solidus
11    each instalment per year, free of interest, in the month Thoth, as is fit[ti]ng,
12    without any contradiction whatsoever and postponement, through everyone …
13    presenting to you this …
14    security: Dios and Isakos and Mousaios and Eudoxias and A[tha]nasias, solidi 2 1/3 in
15    7 instalments, free of interest from each instalment.
