[TRANSLATION]
1     to Numenios the one over the endowed property from Peteimouthos … [of those from Sebennytus].
2     Ptolemy the chief-bodyguard [and general and having ordered concerning]
3     the farmers so that they not be wronged nor be deceived [I am being wronged by Onnophris who having seized]
4     the sixth of Sebennytus. for you said to me …
5     to harvest so that also the late fruit [was dried up] …
6     to separate the sixth until I should exact [something else] …
7     I ask therefore you, having sent for Onnophris …
8     and the price of the pottery. fare [well].
