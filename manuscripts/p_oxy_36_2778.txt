[TRANSLATION]
1     The farmers of Aspheos
2     have grain ready. The
3     own donkey-drivers the bricks
4     were unwilling to carry
5     saying [that] custom is [that] barley
6     is given to the donkeys. [They are] completing [up] to the 19th [day] myriads
7     of [grain in] C. If you wish through donkey-drivers
8     of the village [to carry] part of the three
9     myriads, or indeed all three to be carried to the [door of] Neophytos for 28 drachmas [it] will be carried. A branch of thorns for two machines: if you wish [it] to be cut, since
10    two new choinikides [measures] lie [ready]. The vegetables of the 13th Erytheos
11    and [of] Lyson's [garden] for tomorrow, which is the 14th, [is] ready to be delivered. The quota of 20 artabas has been completed.
