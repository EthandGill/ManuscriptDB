[TRANSLATION]
1     In the seventh year of Ner[o] Claudius Caesar
2     Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Phamenoth
3     15, on the Augustan day, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites of the Thebaid. Thermouthion
4     daughter of Achilleus, of those from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     with her guardian, the husband of her mother
6     Ploutarche, Diogenes son of Pasion,
7     acknowledges to her former husband Dioskous son of Pausiris,
8     of those from the same city, in the street, that she has received
9     from him, of Augustan and Ptolemaic
10    silver coinage, two hundred drachmas of principal,
11    which Dioskous acknowledged he had received from
12    Thermouthion, from the price of which the same Thermouthion, with her guardian Dioskous, sold a part
13    of a house in the Hippodrome quarter, according to which the same Dioskous and Thermouthion referred to one another
14    through the record-office in the city of the Oxyrhynchites
15    in the month Pharmouthi of the sixth year of Nero
16    Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
17    an agreement concerning the same two hundred drachmas;
18    and that Thermouthion shall make no claim, nor shall claim, nor shall proceed against Dioskous, nor shall any other on
19    her behalf, against Dioskous nor those acting through him, neither
20    concerning these matters nor about anything whatsoever
21    up to the present day, or, apart from
22    the future proceeding being invalid, Thermouthion or the one proceeding on her behalf shall pay to Dioskous
23    or those acting through him, for each proceeding, both the
24    damage and as penalty one hundred silver drachmas, and to the public treasury
25    an equal sum, and nonetheless the contract shall be valid. In the
26    seventh year of [Ne]ro Claudius Caesar
27    Augustus G[er]manicus Imperator, Phamenoth fifteenth, on the Augustan day, through Epimachos
28    the agoranomos, it has been transacted.
