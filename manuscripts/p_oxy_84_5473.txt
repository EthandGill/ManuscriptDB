[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master Flavius Justinianus
2     the eternal Augustus and Emperor, in year 34, for those in the 19th (year) after the
3     consulship of Flavius Basileios the most distinguished, Tybi 10, indiction 9, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites.
4     To Flavius Apion the all-praiseworthy and most eminent ex-consul
5     ordinarius, landholder here too in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,
6     through Menas the servant, who asks the formal question and secures for his own
7     master, the same all-praiseworthy man, the action and liability,
8     Phoibammon also called Pateus, the most admirable steward of your
9     eminence, son of the late Isak, originating from the same (city) of the Oxyrhynchites,
10    subscribing below in his own hand, greeting. I acknowledge
11    of my own free will and by voluntary choice, without deceit and fear and force
12    and compulsion and any fraud, that I have contracted with your eminence
13    through those belonging to her, from the money-taxes or gold-taxes of the present
14    ninth indiction, of the crops and revenues of the with-God tenth indiction,
15    on the condition that I fulfil the office of steward or receiver
16    for her in the administration of the property of Pakerky together with the outlying places attached to it,
17    all belonging to your eminence,
18    and, according to what is furnished to me as due for exaction from her reverend
19    chartularies, I acknowledge to turn the recovery against the liable
20    farmers, both proprietary and any outlying
21    persons whatsoever, in accordance with my receipts issued by me
22    to all the liable farmers, both proprietary and any
23    outlying persons whatsoever, and the recovery which I effect concerning the collection; and if it happen that a deficit arise in only the same property,
24    I acknowledge to make this good, and your eminence to credit
25    this to herself in my accounts, in accordance with my receipts
26    issued to all the liable farmers. And all the outlying (revenues)
27    I acknowledge to receive and exact and bring in to your
28    [emin]ence in accordance with your estate-owned demand-note …
29    [t]his e … [ha]v[i]ng [pledged to the right of this]
30    [c]ontr[a]ct all my property both present and fu[ture, specifically]
31    [and gen]erally, by way of pledge and by right of mortgage. [Valid is the contract,]
32    [in duplicate] written and having been formally questioned I acknowledged. [Phoibammon]
33    also called Pateus, so[n] o[f the la]te Isak, the aforementioned, have m[ade]
34    th[i]s contract of the office of steward, of the administra[tion]
35    [of the propert]y of Pakerky together with its outlying pl[aces, according to the]
36    [c]ontent contained in it, and eve[rything] agrees with me [as set out above,]
37    [and having sub]scribed with my hand I released it.
38    Through me Damianos the notary it was compl[eted,]
39    the contract of Phoibammon also called Pateus son of Isak
40    from the (city) of the Oxyrhynchites, of the administration of the property of Pakerky
41    together with its outlying (places).
