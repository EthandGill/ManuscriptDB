[TRANSLATION]
1     Imouthes and partners [collectors of money taxes] to Heraklates of Bassos. We have received for surveying
2     of Charakos, year 17, soiled drachma one, obol one and a half; [makes] soiled drachma one, obol one and a half, and in the name of Pseneriaos
3     of Heraklates and [his] brothers, similarly soiled obols five; [makes] soiled five-obol-[piece], and in the name of
4     Tamenrosios of Pechytus and partners, similarly soiled drachma one
5     [makes] soiled drachma one. Year 18 of Trajan Aristus our lord
6     Choiak 13. A has noted [this].
