[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of … [merciful] and philanthropic [one]
2     There is no god if not god
3     Abd Elmalik pious
4     Councillor
5     [In the name] of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit of the holy and life-giving [one]
6     And consubstantial in the monad of the trinity, written month of Choiak eighth [of the] eighth indiction
7     Signature of [..] venerable of the Lord … this agreement of those who understand … who …
8     [Those who do not understand] …that the notary signs for them … of the public account, that is
9     … Lord, [..], the councillor, and through the most glorious Lord, the Lord
10    [Basil] the illustrious and pagarch of the venerable Lord and his settlements and his fields all, and through the notable apa Kosma, his person […] and we agree
11    … [..] the venerable who is to sign on his behalf the agreement …
12    … [..] command us … in trustworthy manner to the place of standing …
13    [Phoib]ammon, son of the blessed Jeremiah, this […] of the Nile […]
14    … The one incumbent who is to stand in the service of the Lord, to stand […] … to the venerable [one]
15    Agreement made by Phoibammon [son] of Jeremiah on behalf of the community of the village of Aphro[dito]
