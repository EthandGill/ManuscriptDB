1     In the thirteenth year of Imperator Caesar
2     Trajan Hadrian Augustus, in the month
3     Neos Sebastos, on the 3rd, in K[aranis in t]he He[ra]kleides di[v]ision of the Arsinoite [nome].
4     Hatres son of Papeis, a Pers[ian of the de]scent, about fif[t]y-two years old, with a scar on the mi[d]dle of his forehead, acknowledges to Pasion son of Chairemon,
5     ab[o]ut twenty-nine years old, with a scar on his left wrist,
6     that he has received from him forthwith by hand
7     from the household one hundred silver drachmas
8     and fif[teen] artabas of wheat
9     … … and …
10    … the repayment shall be made by Hatres
11    [to Pas]ion in the month Pauni of the
12    present thirteenth year of Hadrian
13    Caesar the lord; and if [he does] not repay,
14    Hatres con[s]ents that (Pasion) cultivate and take the produce
15    [from] (the land) which Hatres cultivates around Patsontis,
16    of public soil, three arouras, in the
17    … instead of the interest [an]d the one-and-a-half (penalty)
18    and the public dues [up]on them, until he repays the silver and wheat, the right of execution belonging
19    to Pasion from the acknowledging party and from all his property
20    as if by a legal decision. The subscribers:
21    I, Hatres son of Papeis, a Persian of the de[s]cent, acknowledge that I have received from Pa[sio]n son of Chairemon by h[and] from the hous[ehold one hundred silver dr]achmas [and fifteen artabas of wheat]
22    [fif]te[en] … [of which]
23    [I will make the re]pay[ment in the month Pau]ni of [the present thirteenth year]
24    [of Hadr]i[a]n …
25    Ac[knowledgment] of Hatres son of Papeis toward Pasion.
