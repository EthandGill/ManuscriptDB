[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of] our masters Constantius Augustus
2     [for the 2nd time and Con]stans Augustus for the 1st time.
3     To A[ur]elius Eulogios also called Euphronios, ex-magistrate, defensor of the Hermopolite,
4     from Aurelius Leontios son of Leontios, painter, from the same city.
5     There devolved upon my mother Eudaimonis house-properties and small plots of land from the inheritance of her maternal uncle
6     Pinoution, who died childless and intestate, upon her
7     alone, according to the laws, as heir; but a certain Ammonios,
8     son of Kastor son of Apis, not S…nos, from the village of Pentaleos, being cultivator of the arouras, neither provided me rents nor again
9     withdrew from the occupation of the house-properties, with what intent I do not know, as against a woman, from afar
10    … ē … soio … ach … to him
11    … his village-bred willfulness.
12    [Therefore I submit these] documents, asking that there be brought before you
13    [my lord the def]ensor the registered Ammonios … to do one or other of two things …
14    … him, unreasonably being … …
15    … and the rents from the time when [Pinoution] died … …eros Pinoution down to the present and …
16    … the house-properties and the farm … of the property left behind … May you prosper. In the aforesaid consulship
17    … [Leonti]os the aforesaid.
