[TRANSLATION]
1     Taria to my brother Apollo, greetings.
2     It troubles me to hand myself over to Eisadeni, as you handed over your house.
3     To Tapsai I complained that you did not send me small change. Ten times I sent to you, and just now I sent through Dioscoros
4     who is handing over to you the letter
5     the other five-hundred drachmas. You did not leave them. Arrange [it].
6     Give me wages ... you have wages
7     and give from you to Empui and Aunat ...
8     from your [maidservant] Tapsai
9     [until] today ... of Pauni
10    I pray you fare well greatly
11    Hand over to Apollonius from Tarias
