[TRANSLATION]
1     Send the salt-fish to me more quickly.
2     You know that it is winter and there is a need
3     of them; therefore do not neglect; and if [any]
4     you have a need of the things here, inform [me]
5     by letter so that I send [it]
6     to you, knowing that I do [it] willingly; or you will give [it] to my brother. Of this
7     to him I have commanded not to neglect. And you
8     the man owing me, since it was not right to name him
9     by letter, and you yourself so [much]
10    do [it], for I know how you love me, regarding troubling him, since that one is negligent. If
11    anything happens, write back to me. I greet
12    the daughter and the son. Origen and Pasis greet you
13    and Protus. Farewell.
14    Deliver [this] to Nemesianus.
