[TRANSLATION]
1     Account of wheat of [the village] Takkona of the embole of the 13th indiction.
2     Receipts of clean wheat, artabas: 10,010.
3     and [for] the barley-tax of them wheat, artabas 925.
4     and for the larger wheat, artabas 50.
5     and for the wheat-measurer wheat, artabas 92.
6     Receipts of the 13th indiction of unclean wheat to the chancellery, artabas: 10,000 and 1,077.
7     Of which were given to the pulse-dealer unclean wheat, artabas 74,
8     [Similarly] were given to the pulse-dealer for the boat unclean wheat, artabas 1,701 and 3/4,
9     [Similarly] [were given] to the pulse-dealer by means of a pittakion
10    of the secretary of the village Sephtha, unclean wheat, artabas 142,
11    [and] by means of the [stable-hands] of Takkona, unclean wheat, artabas 111.
