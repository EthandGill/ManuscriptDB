[TRANSLATION]
1     (In the priesthood) of the Philopatores, (in the athlophorate) of Berenike Euergetis,
2     (Eirene daughter of Metrophanes being) athlophoros, (in the kanephorate of Arsinoe)
3     Philadelphos (of) Iamneia daughter of [Perigenes],
4     Daisios the fifteenth, [in Hiera Nesos]
5     of the Saviour Gods, of the Arsinoite [nome].
6     Donomazis son of Taotis … [of the]
7     descent acknowledges that he has received from Histiaios …
8     of the fixed-pay cleruchs of Maraios …
9     … ndeus …
10    into the [name] of the king …
11    of Histiaios in the …
12    Donomazis brought a charge against Histiaios …
13    … are …
14    … of the …
15    Histiaios attempted …
16    three hundred and thirty [to] Donomazis …
17    upon … for what Donomazis expended for Theodotos …
18    and that Histiaios receives the account from Donomazis …
19    of the three hundred and thirty drachmas [and that it shall not be lawful]
20    for Donomazis nor for Histiaios concerning the moneys [against one another]
21    to proceed; and if one proceeds … [and if anyone]
22    breaks the contract and does not abide by [the agreement]
23    let his proceeding against him be void and let him further [pay]
24    the one bringing suit …
25    Dionysos … and none the less let the [contract be valid].
26    Witnesses: Stratonides the Chalcedonian,
27    Philokydes the Amphipolitan, Nikolaos …
28    the three of the fixed-pay cleruchs of Maraios,
29    Apollonios the Ainian, Ptolemaios M…,
30    Apollonios the Herakleote, the three of the hundred-aroura men of Hippokrates of the second hipparchy; keeper of the contract, Apollonios.
31    This copy was entered, brought up by Histiaios in Krokodilon polis,
32    year 13, Pauni the fifteenth,
33    because the keeper of the contract was performing a public service (liturgy)
34    in Alabastron polis.
