[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the most illustrious and the one to be disclosed, Thoth 17, indiction 12.
2     … to a biarch among the most noble and sanctified Moors [established] over the Hermopolite [district], from Samuel [son] of Jacob, mother … [J]ewish in religion from the same Hermopolite [district].
3     [I wish voluntarily and willingly] to lease from you [for] whatever [you] wish
4     [time, reckoned from the] present month Thoth of the current twelfth indiction
5     [your two-thirds share] of a complete house, of such kind as there is arrangement
6     … [court] and well and all right[s] and utilit[ies] situated in the same Herm[opolis, located on the] region Phrourion on the east toward
7     [our use and habitation], [the] rent of the same two-thirds share of the whole
8     [for a year, of] silver tal[ents] [eight] thousand, which rent I shall pay
9     [to you at the] end of each year without delay. And whenever you wish to have [it], I shall hand over to you the same two-thirds share with doors and locks as I received [it]. The leas[ing is valid and] firm, and being asked I have acknowledged. Aurelius Samuel [son] of Jacob, the aforementioned one,
10    [I have leased] as [stated]. Aureli[us] Ammonios [son] of Sarapion, being asked, I testify to the lease having heard
11    [from the] one making [it]. Aurelius Phoibammon [son] of Pinotios, being asked, I testify to the lease
12    [having heard] from the one making [it].
13    Through me Aphouts [it] was written through Phoibammon [as] helper.
