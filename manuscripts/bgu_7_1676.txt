[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapodorus to Phaneon his brother greetings.
2     I wish you to know that we went to the [centurion] and
3     he threw us into mediation
4     to be judged with a sword-bearer. And of the
5     mediators having heard from your opponents that the whole matter toward you
6     was directed, I gave a pledge to the sword-bearer until the twenty-third, until I send to you. You will therefore do well by receiving my letters and going up by the appointed day of the twenty-third. But if
7     you wish not to go up, they will send a soldier with me to you
8     and we give other supplies.
9     I pray you are well.
10    Give to Phaneon from Sarapodorus
11    ... kratous.
