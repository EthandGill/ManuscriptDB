[TRANSLATION]
1     with my signature as [is set forth] …
2     of the indiction. Flavius Colluthus, [son] of Hierakion …
3     of artabae seven hundred pure with transport-costs and I will record into the great document; [agrees with me] [all things]
4     as is set forth. Aurelius Phoibammon of Epanakios, scribe, I testify to the reliable one, having heard from
5     the one who deposited [it]. Aurelia Ann[a]
6     daughter of Cyrus from Hermupolis, having been paid by you, the wisest scholar Origen of the great marketplace, from the gold [that was given]
7     to the most magnificent count Zacharias by you on behalf of the valuation of the matters of the blessed Colluthus of Hierakion I [give]
8     and I cede to you this his sig[nature] together with the rights and claims belonging to it. Isaac, [son of her]
9     having been deemed worthy I signed on behalf of her, not knowing letters, and I agree, having assented with my mother concerning all [things]
10    contained in the same signature as is set forth. Aur[elius] Collou[thus] son of Theophilus, brother of Isaac, [I ass]ent, having agreed with my mother concerning all things contained in her signature as is set forth.
