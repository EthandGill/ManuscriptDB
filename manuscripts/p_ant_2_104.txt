[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Athanasius son the most brilliant and wisest advocate of the tax [office of Theb]ai[d]
2     from Aurelius Taurinus son of Serenus farmer from the named settlement of Perseans … phi
3     of those from village Tertamsake of the Hermopolitic nome, dwelling now here [in the city of Antinoopolis]
4     city. I myself the same Taurinus farmer through this written sec[urity] acknowledge [that I owe]
5     and owe [in gold ker]ats six by weight, there are gold kerats 6, and these I acknowledge to furnish to your brilliance [whenever] you [may wish, without] any objection. And for your security I have made this document for you
6     [with my signature] as aforesaid of Aurelius Taurinus son of Serenus farmer
7     … [assents] to me all things as aforesaid
