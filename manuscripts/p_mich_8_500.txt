[TRANSLATION]
1   Roullios to [Julius A]pollinarius, (his) brother,
2   greetings.
3   I marvel how you did not send us a little letter
4   through which you might make clear to us whether, according to
5   our prayers, you were preserved, and what you are doing now,
6   so that we may know how your little affairs are go-
7   ing. The white wool-wood(?), as, being present, I
8   asked you, send to Menon. If they were
9   soft, he said, being present with you, let it be sent from Rome.
10   I will bring (it) up, if you wish to send (it) up […]
11   […] a copy I sent
12   off to you, lest it go astray; bring(?)
13   often to Sempronius, and […]
14   we are distracted […] about military affairs […]
15   […] is troubling, lest […]
16   […] and those able […]
17   (2nd hand) I wish you health, brother,
18   […]
19   Sitelkas greets you, and his children
20   by name.
