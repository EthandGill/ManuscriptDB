[TRANSLATION]
1   To Philiskos, contractor for the weavers,
2   from Sarapion son of Sarapion.
3   My slave Apollophanes,
4   a weaver, registered
5   in the quarter of Tegmouthis,
6   died abroad
7   in the present 7th year of Nero
8   Claudius Caesar Augustus Germani[cus]
9   Imperator. Therefore I request
10   that he be registered
11   in the list of the de-
12   ceased; and I swear by
13   Nero Claudius Caesar
14   Augustus Germanicus Imperator
15   that this is true.
16   Year 7 of Nero Claudius
17   Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
18   Mecheir 27, dies Augusta.
19   I, Philiskos, have signed.
20   Year 7 of Nero Claudius
21   [Ca]esar Augustus
22   [Ger]manicus
23   [Impe]rato[r],
24   [Me]cheir 27, [dies Augusta.]
