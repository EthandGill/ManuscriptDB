1     Panameus the elder [son] of Hermias has recorded
2     for poll-tax, year 2: ten drachmas, and for bath-tax: one drachma, 1½ obol[s]
3     total: 11 drachmas, 1½ obol[s], and these [being] recorded additionally. Year 2 of Nero
4     Claudius Caesar Augustus
5     Germanicus, Emperor
6     month Germaniceius 24. Cephalus, I have noted.
