[TRANSLATION]
1     [From a] bank, Payni twenty-two and nine, year of Augustus eight, drachmas one,
2     Thrasymachus, [son] of Melanos, from Maronia,
3     to Patynos, [son] of Eurimon, ...
4     Patynios, greetings. You have paid off
5     the two-drachma [payment] of Soukhos the god,
6     great, great [god], [payment] for which you purchased houses
7     and two courtyards and a courtyard in two [sections]
8     [under] seals on the amphodon Phremeios, from
9     Demetrios of Apollonios
10    of Demetrios. Year eight
11    of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
12    Germanicus, Emperor,
13    Payni seven. Thrasymachus
14    [son] of Melanos, Maronian, I have signed.
