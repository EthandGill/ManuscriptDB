[TRANSLATION]
1     Germanus to Silvanus greetings. Having been asked [that] you send a potsherd into the
2     [investigation], they say, concerning the
3     Ammonian, concerning [his] safety
4     of him; do not then act otherwise since
5     we are anxious about him; send [it]
6     on tomorrow, the 27th of the month, and if not
7     we send there so that we might hear …
8     … of him; for Aphros says to us that [he will] die; bring from him a bundle
9     … and one of rope-garlic
10    and one for Antoninus and one
11    for Ammonian.
12    Farewell.
