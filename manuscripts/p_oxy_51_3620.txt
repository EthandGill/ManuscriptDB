[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of our l]ords Constantine
2     [Augustu]s for the 7th time and Constantius the most illustrious
3     [C]aesar for the 1st time, Mecheir 8.
4     [To the Aureli]i Aphthonios and Timotheos, both night-[strateg]oi of the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     [from A]urelius Thonios son of Onnophris, from the same
6     … cit[y.] On the day of yesterday, while [my] w[ife] was at home, a certain Tapesis,
7     [together with] her own household-slave Viktora, in the evening
8     hours, dwelling among those far away from our house,
9     coming upon (her), inflicted unspeakable insults on my wif[e,] so that not only her clothing was
10    torn but also her bod[y], (the women) having been brought to bear
11    … … her, against my wife
12    … … of gold. Since therefore
13    … the submission of a petition
14    I make, ask[ing] … that a midwife instructed by you come and note the
15    condition of her and report in writing, [and]
16    the report having been made and the
17    outrage having become known, that they provide securities, so that, if anything should happen to my wife, the proper vengeance may be done before
18    the undefiled tribunal of my lord the most eminent
19    prefect of Egypt, Tiberius Flavius Laetus.
