[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelia Taesis, [daughter] of Asclepiad, mother Sarapous, from Memphis city, confessed [that she has]
2     from Aurelia [Thae]isarion, [daughter] of Komon, from the village of Karanides
3     silver principal drachmas ten-thousand eight-thousand
4     being talents three, which the Aurelia further confessed
5     Taesis [that she would] expend for the debt of the aforesaid
6     her father Asclepiad and she is compelled
7     the confessing [woman] to remain with the Aur[e]lia
8     Thaesarion working, which she knows both leather-work
9     works and household [work] instead of the interest on the principal.
10    But if she wishes to depart, even so she is compelled
11    to repay the aforesaid silver talents three without delay. And in the enforcement
12    there shall be for Aurelia Thaesarion the recoveries both from
13    the confessing Aurelia Taesis or from all that belongs to her just as from a judgment. But this document [is] simple
14    written, let it be valid in all respects and being questioned she confessed. Aurelia Taesis, I have the aforesaid silver talents three and I will remain for service of the [trade] and other
15    household [works]. But if I depart, I will repay the aforesaid money as has been set forth and being questioned I confessed.
16    Aurelius Horion, [son] of Soterichus, from ward
17    [Phre]mei I wrote on behalf of her, [she being] illiterate.
18    [Of year] ... of our lord Probus Augustus, Tybi 15.
