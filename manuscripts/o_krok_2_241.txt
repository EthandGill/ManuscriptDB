[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollos to Priscus [the] brother greetings
2     and always [to be well]. I sent to you
3     two measures of [barley] through Satornilus
4     [and] not you sent to me the statement; I bought them for one drachma and two obols; bring a bundle
5     of [cabbage] from Pebotus; greet
6     Apolinaris [and] Maxim[us] Onnophris;
7     Manneius greets you greatly.
8     Farewell, master.
