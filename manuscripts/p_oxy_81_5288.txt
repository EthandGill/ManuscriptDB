[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of the most divine and most pious
2     our master, greatest benefactor Flavius
3     Justinus, the eternal Augustus and emperor,
4     in the 5th year, in the consulship of their own serenity
5     for the 2nd time, Epeiph 1, in the 3rd indiction.
6     To the most noble Georgia, daughter of the (man) of
7     splendid memory, from this
8     splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, Aurelius
9     Phoi[b]ammon, gardener, son of Anoup, his mother being
10    Ste[pha]nous, from the hamlet of Amoule of the Oxyrhynchite
11    nome, greeting. I acknowledge, through this my
12    written surety, that I have contracted
13    with your nobility, on condition that I
14    supply and give to her vegetables
15    for four months, that is, from the fifteenth of the past month Pachon
16    of the present third indiction until the fifteenth
17    of the month [Th]oth of the fourth indiction,
18    and that I receive from your nobility
19    a third of a solidus on account of the price
20    of the same vegetables for the same
21    four months; and I acknowledge to give to her
22    on the first day, vegetables, bundles
23    nine, and one roll (of greens?), and on the second day
24    bundles of vegetables ten, until the completion of the
25    same four months, without postponement.
26    Binding is the contract, written singly, and on being questioned I acknowledged.
27    I, Phoibammon son of Anoup, the aforewritten, it satisfies
28    me in all things as aforesaid. I, Serenus, wrote on behalf of him, being illiterate.
29    Through me, John the priest, it was completed.
30    Contract of Phoibammon, gardener, son of Anoup, from the hamlet of Amoule.
