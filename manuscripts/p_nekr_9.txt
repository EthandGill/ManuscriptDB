[TRANSLATION]
1     Psennesis son of Horos, son of Senamounis, his mother being Klaudia, undertaker [from Kysis], to Polydeukes also called Mersis, freedman of Petechon, and Petosiris, both sons of Petechon, undertaker from Hibis, greeting. I acknowledge that I have ceded to you, from now [until]
2     forever, the half-share of the service and funerary care of the persons about to
3     die in the village of Pmounesis of Kysis and dwelling in the same estates, of which
4     persons concerning her we render to you, and that for her service you receive,
5     it having come down to me [from] the inheritance of Horos my father, for the price which I have had from you today through hand in full, of three hundred silver drachmas, total 300 drachmas. With which I am satisfied and persuaded, and I will guarantee to you with every guarantee against everyone who shall come against it; and whenever
6     you wish, I will register for you through a public deed this sale, written simple. Valid
7     let it be and firm as if deposited in a public archive, and being formally questioned I acknowledged.
8     In the 1st year of Imperator Caesar Marcus Julius Philippus Pius [Felix]
9     Augustus, Epeiph 4.
10    Aurelius Psenamounis also called Ammon, I have read it over.
11    Psennesis son of Horos, the aforesaid, I have sold and received the price as aforesaid.
12    I wrote on his behalf, Aurelius Basileides also called Sarapiodoros, having been requested, who also wrote the body of the text.
13    Aurelius Apion son of Sarapion, I bear witness.
14    Aurelius Dionysides, [I bear witness.]
