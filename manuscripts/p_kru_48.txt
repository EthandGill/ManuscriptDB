[TRANSLATION]
1     The house of Daniel of Pkouk, with their courts … o …
2     the west, the public street, and the place where the door [of the masters] opens for it, since they took the dining-hall (symposion) which is in …
3     which is in the granary of the house of the tower, and the other dining-hall
4     which is above it; yet however … [o]pen … the door
5     of the dining-hall which is in the courtyard, toward the threshing-floor, so that we may give four more
6     tremisses upon the house which is on the street of Koulol, with the share
7     of the land which is below the house of Anatolios son of Samuel,
8     that is, two tremisses to me, I Leontios,
9     and to my brothers a tremis to Paulos, a tremis
10    to Markos — these are the share of Thekla and
11    Arsenios her brother, which they took for their own account alone.
12    From now on, then, to you, you Thekla and Arsenios,
13    you shall come in and take possession, and you shall become
14    the lord, with the share of the places which we demarcate to
15    you, from its foundations up, to beget them for you, or to beget again
16    them for you, to administer them, to manage
17    them, to adorn them, to dwell
18    in them, to apply them to the necessity, to donate them,
19    to cede them, to make them an exchange,
20    to give them forth, to grant them, to give them away,
21    to leave them to your sons
22    and your daughters, to make them your heirs,
23    to use them in every manner that pleases you,
24    according to every possession and tenancy forever,
25    properly and without hindrance, and it is not permitted
26    to us at any time to sue you
27    or your sons or your heirs;
28    neither we nor our sons nor our daughters
29    nor our heirs shall come forth against you,
30    at the hand of any authority, in a tribunal
31    or outside a tribunal, or in the district
32    or outside the district, concerning this settlement,
33    or a part of it as a whole. At whatever time anyone shall come
34    forth against you ever, you and those to whom we first wrote,
35    and he shall come forth at any time, suing you
36    at the hand of any authority or power, opposing
37    you, neither you nor your sons and
38    your daughters and your heirs and your fields
39    and your fields of fields, concerning the share of the places
40    which we explained to you, from above,
41    or that he utter any word at all
42    at the hand of any authority, or through an official
43    or an ecclesiastic — first he shall not, that man,
44    profit in anything; primarily he shall be a stranger to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
45    and afterwards he shall pay to the account of a fine thirty-three holokottinos of gold of the authority which exists
46    now, apart from the penalty which our law
47    determines against the one who shall dare and transgress.
48    And on top of all these he shall come in and confirm
49    and establish, with every matter that is written in it,
50    that we have established for you this pure settlement (dialysis),
51    firm and valid in every place where they shall produce
52    it, and they shall question (us) and we shall acknowledge,
53    and after these things, it was read to us; we heard it;
54    we approved it; we declared, "It stands in this manner."
55    We have confirmed it with a witness and a confirmation;
56    we have let it go forth in every regular form.
57    We, Paulos, with Markos, with Leontios, with Ananias, with Charis, with Nemphe, those who have first written, from above,
58    we agree to this settlement, with every matter that is written in it.
59    I, Markos, the son of Papnouthios, the least deacon
60    of the catholic church of Djeme, acting [as proxy],
61    they asked me; I have written on their behalf, that they do not know how to write.
62    I, Iohannes, the son of Kyriakos, give witness.
63    I, Matthaios, the son of Israel, give witness.
64    Iohannes, the son of Kyriakos: I have written on his behalf, that he does not know how to write.
65    I, Petros, the son of the blessed Andreas, give witness.
66    I, Demetrios, the son of Leontios, give witness.
67    I, Isak, the son of Kostantine, give witness.
68    Through me, Aristophanes son of Iohannes, it was drawn up.
