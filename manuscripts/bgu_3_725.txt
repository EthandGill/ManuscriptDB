[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and master
2     Jesus Christ our God and Savior,
3     in the reign of our most pious
4     master Flavius Heraclius the eternal
5     Augustus and Imperator, in year 8, Epeiph 27,
6     at the beginning of the 7th indiction, in Arsinoe.
7     [Au]relius Sambas, poultry-keeper,
8     [son] of Kollouthos, and I his
9     wife Eupr[axi]a, daughter of Petros,
10    serving-maid of Kyrillos the most glorious
11    general, from the city of the Arsinoites,
12    from the quarter of Apsis,
13    to the most esteemed apa Ol,
14    [eunuch] of the same most glorious
15    man, greeting. We acknowledge that of mutual liability
16    we have received from you by hand,
17    [for] our own need, of gold
18    three solidi less twenty-
19    three and a quarter carats — of gold three solidi less twenty-three and a quarter carats —
20    in capital; and the repayment of these
21    we shall make to you on the thirtieth of the
22    coming month Mesore of the present
23    seventh indiction, without interest, beyond doubt.
24    And I shall give you also three large
25    fowls, and having been formally questioned we acknowledged (it). Aurelius Sambas
26    and I his wife Eupraxia, the aforesaid: it is satisfactory to us
27    as is set forth.
28    Through me, Petros. Through me, Petros.
29    Of Sambas the poultry-keeper … of Eupraxia the serving-maid, of gold three solidi less twenty-three and a quarter carats, to the
30    most esteemed apa Ol, eunuch.
