[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulships of our lords the Emperor Diocletianus
2     Augustus for the sixth time and Constantius the most illustrious Caesar for the second time.
3     To the Aurelii Dionysius also called Apollonius and Demetrianus son of Plution,
4     both former gymnasiarchs and councillors of the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     the most estimable night-strategoi.
6     From Aurelia Tapammon daughter of Thonios, her mother being Allous, from the same
7     city, transacting business without a guardian by right of children, through the foster-
8     father Aurelius Sarapiades son of Didymus also called Hierax, from the same city.
9     By succession from my parents I possess, together with my sister Dioskouriaine, a slave named Sarmates, house-born from the slave-woman Thaesis,
10    and from the time of the death of my parents this man supplies rents
11    to us. But now, I do not know from what motive he has been stirred, he tears himself away and
12    is unwilling to remain in our service, nor again to give rents.
13    What he is aiming at I do not understand. Whence, not bearing the
14    servant's insolence, I submit these documents in writing, petitioning and
15    requesting that through your care the slave-boy be compelled to pay back the
16    rents owed by him and be ordered so that
17    he may remain in our service.
18    Year 12 and year 11 of our lords the Emperors Diocletianus
19    and Maximianus Augusti and year 4 of Constantius and Maximianus
20    the most illustrious Caesars, Pauni 19.
21    I, Aurelia Tapammon, have submitted it through me, Aurelius Sarapiades. I, Aurelius Ischyrion, wrote on her behalf.
