[TRANSLATION]
1     Theon [Sa]mba, to the oil-maker. Twenty sextarii of oil
2     supply [to the] buc[cellarii], being twenty
3     in Phaneis and in [S]trato(n)'s for twenty days
4     oil, sextarii 20 only. Mechir 13, [indiction] 9. I have noted oil, sextarii twenty only
