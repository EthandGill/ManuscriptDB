[TRANSLATION]
1     Silver drachmas 354, 1 obol.
2     To Deios and Sabinos, public bankers, the undersigned public cattle-breeders of the village of Oxyrhyncha, through Ptolemaios who was appointed: We receive from you the amounts assigned to us by Ptolemaios, royal scribe of the Polemon division, who is also acting as deputy and managing the affairs of the strategia of the
3     Themistos and Polemon divisions, for the transport-charges of barley which we were shown to have brought down from the granaries of the Polemon division to the
4     harbours, the processing thereof having been performed, of the produce of the 17th year, of barley
5     artabas 73 ⅙ ⅛, at the rate of 480 drachmas per 100 artabas, the silver amounting to three hundred fifty-four drachmas, 1 obol, total drachmas 354, 1 obol, in accordance with the
6     order. They are: Theon son of Ptollion, barley artabas 9 ½ ⅓ 1/24; Onnophris son of Mendes, artabas 4 ½ 1/12; Heron son of Mendes and Theon his brother, artabas 3 ½ ⅛; Theon
7     son of Anchophis, artabas 4 1/6; Laros son of Petermouthes, artabas 4 1/6 1/24; Horos son of Peneoueris, artabas 2 1/6; Sarapion son of Patron, artabas 4 ⅓ 1/24; Ptolemaios son of Mendes, artabas 4 1/6 1/24;
8     Thonis son of Heron, artabas 3 1/6 ⅛; Petsiris son of Pekysis, artabas 4 ½ ⅓ 1/12; Mendes his brother, artabas 5 1/12; Petsiris son of Heron, of an artaba ⅓; Papontos son of Petemounis, artabas 16 ½ 1/12;
9     Peebaus son of Ammonios, artabas 5 1/6 1/24; Palbauis son of Satabous, of an artaba ⅓ 1/12; Theon son of Theon,
10    of an artaba ¼. Total the aforesaid. In the 18th year of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus
11    Augustus Pius, Mecheir 25. I, Ptolemaios, appointed by the
12    aforewritten public cattle-breeders, receive from the public bankers the silver
13    three hundred fifty-four drachmas, 1 obol, total drachmas 354, 1 obol, as is set forth above.
