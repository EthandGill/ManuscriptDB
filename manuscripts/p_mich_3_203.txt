[TRANSLATION]
1     Saturnilos to Aphrodous his mother, very many greetings. Before
2     all things I pray for your health and good fortune. I want you to know
3     that I have sent you three letters in this month. The provisions which you sent me through Iullios I received in full, and
4     through the slave-boy of Iulios a basket of olives. I want you to know
5     that another child has been born to me, a male, whose name is Agathos
6     Daimon, the gods willing. If I find an opportunity of getting work, I shall come with letters to you. I want you to know that
7     behold, three months (have passed) since I was transferred to Pselkis, and I have not yet found
8     an opportunity of coming to you. I was afraid to come just now, since they say the prefect is on the route, lest he take the letters from me
9     and send me off to the standards and I incur expense in vain.
10    But this I want you to know, that if another two-month period passes
11    and I do not come to you by the month Hathyr, I have another eighteen months sitting in the outposts until I come into Pselkis and come to you. All who come will testify to you how every day I seek to come. If you wish to see me
12    a little, I (wish to see you) greatly, and I pray every day to the gods how
13    they may quickly grant a prosperous journey for the coming. Everything in the army
14    happens with an opportunity. If I get the opportunity, I am coming to you. As for my children's piglets, rear them for me, so that if (I) arrive (he) may find my children. For your next good fortunes you will do well to send to Ioulas the son of Iulios whatever you can of provisions, and let him be as my son, as you love me, and I love my children
15    (love them). If his brother is at leisure, send him to me quickly,
16    so that I may send you my children through him and their mother.
17    Send me besides an extra jar of olives for my friend; do not do otherwise.
18    You know that whatever you give to Iulios he brings to me, which he also promised me.
19    Whatever he does, write to me. Greet Sokmenion and his children and
20    the … and Sabinos and Thaisas and her children and the
21    brothers and Tabenka my sister and her husband
22    and her sons-in-law. And if she has had a child, write to me. Greet Tasokmenis my lady sister and Sambas and Soueris and
23    her children and Samboun and all the kinsfolk and friends
24    by name. Gemella greets you all, and Didymarin and
25    the newborn Agathos Daimon and Epiktetos. Greet Gemellos
26    and … the wife of …lianos. And it was no great matter … . I pray for the health of you all.
27    Year … of Trajan the Best Caesar the lord. Phaophi 25.
28    To Karanis, to (my) mother Aphrodous … n … utos
29    from Saturnilos the soldier …
