[TRANSLATION]
1     … …
2     care and good cultivation and diligence, on condition [that your Reverence receive half]
3     part of all the produce, and from the common jar seventy knidia of wine at your
4     bronze (standard); and indeed that I, together with the water-supplier, receive the other half part in return for which we perform
5     our labours. And I will dig the same plot a fourth (i.e. four times?) each year; and I will give each year at the time of vintage
6     a young pig worth six thousand talents of silver, and a platter full of various
7     foods, that is, worth six thousand talents of silver, and five litras of relish-fish, and of [radish] oil
8     one xestes, and fifty pairs of soft loaves, and one double (-measure) of old wine, and of dried grapes
9     a twelfth of an artaba, without fail. The lease is valid and secure, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged it. Aurelius Serenos
10    son of Sois, the aforesaid, have leased as set forth above. Aurelius Taurinos son of Kyros, from Hermoupolis, being requested, wrote on his behalf, the letters
11    he not knowing. Phoibammon son of Stephanos, adik(?), from Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) fro[m] the contracting party.
12    Aurelius Phoibammon son of Isakios, from Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the contracting party.
13    Through me, Phoibammon, it was written.
