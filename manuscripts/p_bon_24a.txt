1     In the nineteenth year of the Emperor Caes[ar Tra]ianus Hadrianus Augustus, in the m[onth X]anthikos, on the sixteenth, Mecheir 16, in Tebtynis
2     of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. Te[ph]orsa[i]s daughter of Pausis son of Papn[e]btyn[i]s, about fifty years old, a lentil-mark by the left cheek, with as guardian her husband Herakleides son of Akousilaos, about fifty-seven years old, without distinguishing mark, acknowledges (to)
3     Al[y]te daughter of [Be]llis son of Or[s]eus, about fifty-[eig]ht years old, with as guardian her own son Belleus, of unknown father, about
4     twenty… years old, a scar on the left eyebrow, that she Alyte has sold according to this agreement, from now for all time,
5     that which belongs to her, Te]phorsais, in the [aforewritten vil]lage of Tebtynis, an inheritance of her deceased [full-]
6     blood brother Ep[im]achos, who died childless and intestate, and which Epim[ach]os himself bought through the record-office here in the
7     second year of the deified N[erva], in the [month] Phamenoth, on the ninth, from P[ete]souchos son of Pausis son of [P]ausis — a place, a workshop for wool-carding (?), of whatever measures and cubit-dimensions it is, with its existing foundations and walls and arrangement and all
