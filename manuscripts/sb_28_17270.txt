1     Imouthes, tax collector of silver
2     Psenkhousei of Petemenephis. I received on behalf of the dike-tax

4     total drachmai 7 [and] five obols and two chalkoi. Of year 20 of Trajan the lord
5     Mechir 9. … I have noted [this].
