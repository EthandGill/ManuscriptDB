[TRANSLATION]
1     Nikandros [and] Peisikles to Zenon greetings. If you are well, it would be good; and we too are healthy. And before we wrote to you giving thanks
2     that you have managed concerning the bronze money owed to us, and now
3     you will do well before Diotimos sails up, having taken care that into the
4     owing bronze money [he] brings whatever at any time seems good to you to be proper.
5     And concerning also the house and the small vineyard which you wrote to us about, you will do well
6     having disposed [of it] as quickly [as possible], so that this too not fail us.
7     Farewell. Year 36, Loios 7.
8     To Zenon.
