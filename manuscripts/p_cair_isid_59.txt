[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Ph…a…m…n, collectors of the produce
2     of Kerkesoucha (of) Karanis and of the boundary-district of Karanis, to Aurelius Heras, son of Ptolemaios,
3     and in the name of Taesis, daughter of Kopres, greeting. We have received
4     from you for freights of sea-going ships
5     one talent of silver and seven hundred drachmas, total 1 talent 700 drachmas.
6     In the consulship of Caecinius Sabinus and Vettius
7     Rufinus, the most splendid, Tybi 30.
8     The Aurelii Ptollas and Palemon and Neon and Apollonios,
9     all grain-collectors of the part of Ptolemais (of) Karanis,
10    through those from Karanis, to Aurelia Kyrillous, daughter of Kopres, greeting.
11    You measured out to us in the granary of the same village by the public
12    smoothed measure, for the produce of the 3rd indiction, of wheat with …
13    seven and a quarter artabas, total 7 1/4 artabas, and of barley two and one-sixth artabas, 2 1/6 artabas,
14    and you paid us for the conversion-to-silver of the 3rd indiction, of silver
15    two talents and three thousand drachmas, [total] 2 talents 3000 drachmas.
16    [In the consulship] of Caecinius Sabinus (and) Vettius [Rufinus, the most splendid],
17    [Pharmo]uthi 19. I, Ision, wrote on [their behalf, they being illiterate].
18    The Aurelii Eudaimon and Antiourios and Aphrodisios and Ptollas [and An]tonius … grain-collectors of the part of Ptolemais (of) Karanis
19    to Aurelia Taesis, daughter of Kopres, greeting. We have received from you for the price of wheat and barley for the produce of the 6th indiction
20    of silver one talent and five thousand
21    six hundred drachmas, total 1 talent 5600 drachmas, only.
22    In the consulship of our lords Licinius Augustus for the 5th time
23    and Crispus the most noble Caesar for the 1st time, Epeiph 15.
24    Heras, son of Ptolemaios, [and] Taesis, daughter of Kopres, paid into the account
25    of freights of goods and of lime, through Apphous and Hatres, [collectors]
26    of Karanis and the boundary-district, for the produce of the 6th indiction,
27    two thousand drachmas. I, Apphous, [have] signed; total 2000 drachmas.
28    In the consulship of our lords [Lic]inius Augustus for the 5th time and
29    Crisp[us the] most noble [Caes]ar for the 1st time, Epeiph 23.
30    Heras, son of Ptolemaios, and Taesis, daughter of [Kopres], paid
31    for assessment and for primipilus and for ship-rigging [of a ship]
32    through Isidoros, son of Pelenios, and Path…
33    to Pateis, collectors of the boundary-district [of Kara]nis, for the produce of the 5th indiction, [five thousand] three hundred drachmas, total 5300 drachmas.
34    I, Aurelius Pelenis, son of Kastor, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
35    Taesis, daughter of Kopres, paid for the conversion-to-silver …
36    through … -los and Kasianos and Harpalos, grain-collectors of Kerkesoucha, village of Karanis, three talents of silver, total 3 talents.
