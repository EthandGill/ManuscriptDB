[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of the letter from Semtheus, topogrammateus of the district about Phebichis:
2     to the royal cultivators from the place and of the separated revenues, for loans for the sowing of the 32nd year, on account of wheat, Arp(…),
3     [of lentils 60,] of barley 75, there have been given from the arrears recorded in the
4     places, of wheat 230, of lentils 38 1/3 1/12, of barley [71 ½], and from Bion the agent of
5     Haryotes, of wheat 225; total, 455 wheat artabas, of lentils 38 1/3 1/12, of barley 71 ½.
6     From those remaining, of wheat 730, of lentils 21 ½ 1/12, of barley 3 [½] …
7     in the place, toward those marked by the antigrapheus, of wheat
8     78 1/3 1/12 artabas, of barley 3 ½, and from the Middle toparchy there have been measured out
9     of wheat 113, and from those which Haryotes undertook to exact from those in
10    the district About Tilothis, of the issues … Tothoes the topogrammateus …
11    … from Leonidas the granary-officer, of wheat 400, and from the Arsinoite
12    of lentils 21 ½ 1/12; total of wheat 591 1/3 1/12, of lentils 21 ½ 1/12, of barley [3 ½],
13    to be left over of wheat 140 ½ 1/12, and the amounts for the completion of what is assigned
14    to the place, of wheat 1180 140 ½ 1/12, of lentils 10; total of wheat 1220 ½ 1/12.
15    I report so that, if it seem good, instruction may be given to Theris the kinsman and strategos
16    to give order to measure out, from what is from the Arsinoite, the aforesaid
17    1220 ½ 1/12 wheat artabas, so that, the land not running past
18    the most favourable season, no shortfall may befall the royal (cultivators). …
19    To Herakleodoros, in the 32nd year, Hathyr 29.
