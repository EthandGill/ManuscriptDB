[TRANSLATION]
1     In the third year of Imperator Caesar Vespasian Augustus,
2     in the month of Caesareus, the 25th, in Theadelphia of the Themistes division of the
3     Arsinoite nome. Thermoutharion daughter [of Sarapion] leased,
4     being about twenty-five years old, with a scar on the tip of her nose, with her guardian, her own
5     husband Didymos son of Ptolemaios, being about thirty-[five] years old,
6     with a scar on his left knee, to Soterichos son of Lykos, a Persian of the epigone,
7     being about twenty-five years old, with a scar on the little finger of his left hand,
8     the vineyard belonging to her around the village, a trellised vine-plantation of half an aroura or however much it may be. This lease
9     (is) for three years from the coming fourth year, on the terms that a third part
10    (goes) to the lessee, of the crops and produce coming out of the vineyard
11    each year, free from public dues and
12    every kind (of charge), the whole cost of the wine-making being deducted in common, both
13    …
14    to abandon the lease before the appointed time; but let him also dig the
15    vineyard each year with two diggings, one a winter one
16    of four palms clean, the other a summer one of a single stroke; and let him make
17    the pruning of the vine moderate and proper, not cutting long.
18    And for the earth-carrying and manuring that shall take place each year,
19    Thermoutharion shall furnish the donkeys, and the lessee
20    shall dig them in and scatter (the manure); and let him each year fashion anew, from fresh (reed), eight
21    five-poled trellis-beds, and the propping of the rest, with
22    Thermoutharion furnishing reed and ropes; and let the
23    lessee carry out the yearly irrigation from the new moon of Pharmouthi
24    until the coming foot-watering, one (day) out of
25    two days, and let him bear the cost, providing the bank-worker, all the fruit-trees in the vineyard being
26    the lessee's, from which let him give each year a thousand apples and,
27    in the first year of the lease, one-fifteenth (measure) of thyme,
28    and for the remaining two years, each year double the thyme, by measure
29    of the dromos, four-choinix. And after the time let the lessee hand over the vineyard clean of rush,
30    grass, and all reed-thicket, and the espaliers standing (in order). And if
31    the lessee transgresses any of these things, let him pay both the
32    damages and the expenses double, and a fine of five talents of bronze, and none the less (fulfil the lease), on which terms Thermoutharion shall also guarantee the lease and (keep it) free from public dues; let the contract be valid.
33    I, Soterichos son of Lykos, a Persian of the epigone,
34    have leased the vineyard …
35    on all the aforesaid terms. Nikandros son of Isidoros wrote on behalf of
36    him because he does not know letters. I, Thermoutharion daughter of Sarapion, with
37    my guardian my husband Didymos, have leased
38    to him [the vineyard] on the [aforesaid terms]
39    as aforesaid. He wrote on behalf of them …
40    son of Phanias, they not knowing letters. There has been entered
41    a copy of the lease, in year 3 of Imperator [Vespasian] Augustus,
42    in the month of Caesareus, the 25th.
