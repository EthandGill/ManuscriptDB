[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God, Korra son of Szerich, counselor, to you, those from the 5 Pediads
2     Of the village Aphroditopolis. Receive from your village-chief iron ore, of the public dirty [ore]
3     50 pounds, fifty, and make clean, 33⅓ pounds, thirty-three, one-third
4     Being delivered, and these deliver to Abdelale son of Abi Achim, by [account of] [fruits] [and]
5     Small-ship workers on the present indiction 8, but corvée of indiction 9, and if you give wages, pro[vide] for
6     Of 50 pounds of i[ro]n [o]re by [account of] purification, [coins] 1⅓, one-third only. It was written in month Thoth 6, indiction 8
7     Totaling of dirty iron, 50 pounds, into purification, 33⅓ pounds, coin 1⅓
