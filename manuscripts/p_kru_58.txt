[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the ho]ly and life-giving consubstantial Trinity, the Fa[ther] …
2     … [the] city of Erm[o]nt; I write to …
3     … [this] same fortress (of) Dje[me] …
4     … that: since I borrowed (?) …
5     the necessary use, now I acknowledge that I owe them
6     to you, purely and absolutely, and I am ready to account
7     for these two solidi of this sort to you, from this day,
8     which is the 16th of this month Mesore, up to the completion of forty days,
9     without any ambiguity; and I leave with you my house,
10    the new one entire, this which is in the upper (part) of the well, that same house, it adjoining
11    the house of my father (the) son of Martyrios, so that you may become
12    lord of that same house, from its foundations up to its tops, with
13    the other utensils that pertain to it, until I account
14    to you for these two solidi up to the term which I appointed.
15    Indeed, however, if the term should pass and I (have) not given it
16    to you, you (being) the one assigned the (?) of my house which is upon the well, according as I have shown it forth to you above, and you shall dwell in it, and give it
17    for a dowry, give it away, do with it (in) every manner that is yours,
18    according to every possession and lordship and tenure in perpetuity,
19    because its price has come to my (hand) from your hand, according as I formerly
20    said above. I swear by God the Almighty and the salvation of the rulers who rule over us, now, by
21    the command of God, that I shall keep (faith) with you according to the power
22    of this security. For your security I established it, firm (and) val[id]
23    in every place where they shall declare it; and let them question me,
24    I acknowledging.
25    I, Chmtsneu, the least presbyter (and) superior,
26    the son of Senouthios, of the holy church (of) Djeme,
27    give witness, at the request of Isak son of Abraham.
28    I, Phoibammon the son of the late Athanasios, give witness.
29    I, Kosma the son of the late Pesenth, the least
30    presbyter of (the) holy Maria, give witness, at the request of this Isak.
31    I, Prase the son of (the) blessed Iohannes, give witness, at the request (of) … .
32    Drawn up through [me], A[risto]phanes son of Iohannes.
33    In the name of God, the first: this is the creed
34    of Petros the son of Isak, sworn to him by Senouthios
35    the presbyter, concerning the two solidi
36    and the four (?) for the work, of this (deed) of Isak son of Abraham
37    which is in the lane of the well and his (?),
38    and the (?) which is in your (hand) among them for ever, valid for you.
