[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 14, Phaophi 14, in Ptolemais Euergetis. Maron son of Dionysios leased to Ptolemaios son of Apollonios, a Persian of the epigone,
2     his own (holding) in three parcels for 3 years … he shall give one talent of copper, not to be repaid.
3     In the reign of Ptolemaios also called Alexandros, the god Philometor, and of queen Berenike
4     the goddess Philadelphos, in the fourteenth year, under the priest of Alexandros and of the others
5     as recorded in Alexandria, in the month Apellaios (= Phaophi),
6     in Ptolemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome. Maron son of Dionysios,
7     Macedonian, of the katoikoi cavalry, leased to Ptolemaios also called Petesouchos son of Apollonios,
8     a Persian of the epigone, his own allotment lying in three parcels around Kerkeusiris in the division of Polemon, of which the neighbours of the first parcel are: to the south, Mestasytmis's
9     royal land, with a ridge lying between; to the north, Apollonios's allotment; to the west and east
10    [a canal]; and of the second: to the south, royal land with a watercourse lying between; to the north, Apollonios's
11    [allotment; to the west] … [to the] east a canal, with pasture-lands lying between; and of the third:
12    [to the south] … [to the] north the allotment of Polemon the younger; to the west a ridge; to the east
13    … an allotment. This lease is for three years from the present
14    [14th year, the total rent] each year being a fixed rate of eighty artabas of wheat … [without seed], free of all risk and not subject to deduction
15    [for any damage except that arising from flood]ing or from lack of water, whatever may occur on this land
16    [until the thirtieth of Hathyr; and if it (flooding) occurs,] let it be reckoned to Maron's account from the rents
17    [in proportion to the lack of water,] each; and if the land above or below is flooded
18    … the rents … in full. And all the works of the land
19    [and] the requisite dyke-work and irrigation Ptolemaios shall carry out each year, [and] he shall fallow each year the third part of the land with grass or vetch or fenugreek,
20    [and] it shall [not] be permitted to him to abandon the lease before the time, and let him give to him
21    [Maron in] the fifteenth year, for the works of the land, not to be repaid, of copper
22    [one talent]. And when the time has elapsed let him hand over what he also received, cleared
23    [of rush, reed,] dog's-tooth grass, and other rubbish, and let him pay the yearly rents
24    [to Maron] … [in the month of P]auni in the village indicated where Maron
25    [directs] … and an account by the measure that is at the dromos
26    [of the Soucheion in it. And if he does not pay or transgress the contract in any other point, let Ptolemaios forfeit to Maron] … part of the land
