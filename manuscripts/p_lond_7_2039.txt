[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greetings, Menandros of the epigonē. I am [being wronged] by Dionysodoros of the allotment-holders. For having requested
2     an empty place from Petosiris the station-keeper I built
3     for myself a house. When Dionysodoros came and demanded
4     in place of rent 2 drachmas per year, as long as I was in the fields with Ambilaeus
5     ... him the allotment, [was building] for me the [house]
6     around, [having condemned] me to the [wilderness], beating he drives me out. When I [resisted], not having anyone to stand by me, I built [another] house [for myself],
7     he himself drives me out, beating [and from] this [claiming] that from Zenon he [has] the
8     all the place, in the trial ... [appearing] against me before Nestos. I therefore beseech
9     you, if it seems good to you, to hear me [so that] I may not be oppressed [by] Dionysodoros.
10    [Farewell].
