[TRANSLATION]
1     Phoibammon, [count].
2     [Provide] to the most noble soldiers of the cavalry who came here with the most magnificent ruler John from […soldiers…] 25
3     [our] part of soldiers, and from the parts of the other three, to 6 soldiers […] bread 24 pounds; it amounts to 24 pounds of bread only.
4     [Year …] 134, Thoth 28, of indiction 12.
