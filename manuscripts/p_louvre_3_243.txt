[TRANSLATION]
1     … 2 … [fro]m [the]
2     your brilliance having been reckoned to him, having taken she will give to David the boy,
3     in order that he might not hold it and it might go into another dispute and be found damaged.
4     once indeed in his hand of the elder I have the register and the matter; into the your
5     brilliance having come I left it to take back. this then [by all means] will give David
6     [t]o the boy.
