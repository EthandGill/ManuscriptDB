[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters
2     Constantius Augustus for the 10th time and Julian
3     the most illustrious Caesar for the 3rd time,
4     Choiak 23.
5     To Aurelius Syros and Techosis and
6     Asyne, the three children of their father Dionysios,
7     from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
8     from Aurelius Kopreus son of Syros,
9     his mother being Sarapias, from the same city.
10    I willingly undertake to lease for one year from the 1st
11    of the following month Tubi of the present
12    37th year and 6th year, in the same city,
13    in the Apollonios quarter, the
14    places allotted by you
15    of the house, into the … of …
16    … places in accordance with the
17    division made in writing,
18    on the rights which it contains, because of the (division) between
19    you and the mother … … ,
20    and I will pay you as rent
21    of the same places of yours and of another part of the courtyard outside the house, for the period of one year, of silver of the Augusti
22    coinage, six thousand talents, that is 6,000 talents.
23    The undertaking being guaranteed to me,
24    I shall of necessity pay the rent in two half-yearly installments of one half, and I shall use the places for the period without hindrance, after which
25    I shall hand them over as I received them, the right of execution
26    being yours against me as is fitting.
27    The undertaking is valid, written in duplicate, and on being [asked the formal question] I [ack]nowledged it.
