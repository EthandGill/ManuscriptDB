[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Olybrius the most glorious, Epeiph 25, in the sixth indiction,
2     Flavius Samuelis son of Kolluthos, his mother being Kyra, soldier of the unit
3     of the Ptolemais nome, from the village of Tanyaithis in the nome of Apollonos
4     Polis Mikra, to Aurelius Phoibammon son of Triadelphos, from the village
5     of Aphrodite in the Antaiopolite nome, greeting. All the previous handwritten documents being valid …
6     … having been placed for security
7     by me and by my daughters, for the credit of each handwritten document everywhere produced, by way of mortgage …
8     upon the farm I hold under you, I acknowledge moreover that I have now received and
9     had measured out to me by you, for my own pressing need again, of grain as principal,
10    of pure grain eighteen artabas, total 18 artabas of grain; and these I hold
11    with me, again upon that same farm of mine held under you called
12    Pmounlakon, by way of an advance, together with the other things given to me [by you],
13    both gold and produce included in the earlier … document …
14    and whenever, on your departing from the same place, I shall repay these
15    as I received them, by your measure; and that I being unable to expel [you]
16    from the same place before their repayment together with the other debts,
17    without any dispute or postponement set down in writing. I have made this for you [for] security upon my subscription; and being formally questioned … Flavius Samuel son of Koloth(os),
18    soldier, the aforesaid, owe and am indebted for the grain,
19    eighteen artabas, and everything agrees with me
20    as aforesaid. … on … Psyrou, presbyter …
21    … Flavius … son of Iosephios … [I bear witness] to the debt.
