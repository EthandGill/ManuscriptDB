[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our [lord]
2     Flavius Justinus, on Pharmouthi 11 of the p[resent]
3     eleventh indiction in Herakle[opolis].
4     Aurelia Maria, daughter of Menas, mother
5     Taratios, from Herakleapolis,
6     to the honored Epimachos, son of the late
7     blessed memory Chrestos, from
8     the same city, greetings. I agree that I have rented
9     from your honor
10    from the [property] belonging to him in the
11    same Herakleapolis in an alley
12    Terowianos, from a stable-structure
13    [of] the whole [room] open
14    to the east in the first storey,
15    one place open to
16    the south,
17    and in the second storey one place
18    open to the south and one cellar and bakery ovens
19    two and all [necessary facilities] for as long as
20    [time] [you] wish from the new moon
21    of the month Pharmouthi of the present
22    eleventh indiction, for rent per year
23    of gold keratia seven, totaling gold keratia 7.
24    The lease is valid and upon being asked, I agreed.
25    Maria, daughter of Menas, the aforementioned,
26    have made this
27    lease as aforesaid.
28    Aurelius Theopilos, son of Ioannes, from Herakleapolis,
29    I wrote on behalf of her, [she] being illiterate.
30    Through me, Maximinos.
31    Lease of Maria, daughter of Menas, from Herakleapolis, of 2 places, for rent of gold keratia 7.
