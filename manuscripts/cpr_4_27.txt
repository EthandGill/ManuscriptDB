[TRANSLATION]
1     … I, Sarra, the daughter of the late Theophilos,
2     her mother being Tihere, while Phoibammon the son of Psate consents with me in every
3     matter written in this sale, and afterwards they have a notary-scribe (hypographeus) subscribe to this sale,
4     an Egyptian (deed), without transgressing it and without infringing it, but rather
5     … being firm, and without setting it aside, by the deed of Paulos the son
6     [of the late An]anias, the man of this same village, the nome, of this same city,
7     [Hermonthis, that we have wille]d and [have] been persuaded, without any guile or fear or oppression
8     [or deceit or] surprise or fraud, there being no necessity
9     upon us, but that we have come in a faith that is sound and with a pure heart
10    and a reckoning unhesitating, being persuaded in every good intention,
11    rather having sworn by God the Almighty and by the salvation of our lords
12    of the world, the worshippers of God and the doers of the good — that I give to you
13    from this day, and we have written from now for time
14    everlasting, that there comes after me, of (the property) beyond three (parts), in the whole house
15    that belongs to me in the fortress (kastron) of Djeme, this which is at Temenoute,
16    this which came to me by the succession of my late father
17    Theophilos, he too having received it by lawful succession of his fathers … the smallest right of succession falling to me in that whole house
18    is not to be given to you, by any small succession, whether by thought or in writing
19    or unwritten, the boundary of that whole house being these, lying thus,
20    in this way according to its ancient neighbors, which are its boundaries: the south,
21    the public street; the north, Symeon the cattleman; the west, again Symeon
22    the cattleman; the east, the place of dwelling of Thekla Traht — these are its boundaries on every side. This is the price for which we have agreed and which we have come to terms upon with
23    one another, which is these two holokottinoi of gold of obryzon, according as our most honored Zacharias and Aaron commanded, with your father and the great men; and again that I give to you from my share in the small house, from the ground up to its top, with all its appurtenances, so that to you,
24    you Paulos, the buyer and the one for whom I first wrote it, you shall draw to (this) written sale and take possession and become lord and have ownership of the three-part of that whole house, with my share in the small house,
25    which I have begotten for you, and you administer them and build upon them,
26    and administer them and manage them and adorn them and donate them and cede them and exchange
