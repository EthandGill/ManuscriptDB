[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Sarapodoros, curator of the Oxyrhynchite,
2     from the Aurelii Kyrilla and Martha, both daughters of their father Kastor, from the same
3     city. Gemellos is the son of Rhodon, who was our uncle on our father's side,
4     who, when about to die, … the property left by him under the
5     authority of a certain Ammonios, a monk, who happened to be his uncle on his mother's side,
6     enjoining us not to be troublesome to him. But it came about that the same
7     Ammonios, being a monk, departed from among men not long afterwards.
8     And the property of the aforesaid Gemellos has passed to us, who are
9     of his paternal stock. Since then neither did Ammonios make a will
10    nor did he appoint heirs, but being a monk he ended his life,
11    yet a certain Ammon holds by force this property which does not belong to him, he not being an heir,
12    on this account we petition your diligence to order the
13    same Ammon to present himself and be compelled, he not being a son of Ammonios
14    [nor an heir], to give back to us the property of Gemellos, to us who are
15    [of his pater]nal stock, so that we may, through your help, suffer
16    no [defrauding].
17    [We, the Aure]liae Kyrilla and Martha, have submitted (this). I, Aurelius Theodoros son of Thonios,
18    [wrote] on their behalf, they not knowing letters.
