[TRANSLATION]
1     of my property …
2     be it resolved. And I wish this also, that if it should happen through negligence that [my heirs]
3     being daughters do not pay in good faith, or their heirs,
4     the offering predetermined by me to the holy monastery, both of wheat and of wine
5     … of the vintaged, I order the most reverent priest of the same
6     monastery and the most reverent monks in it to exact these, willing and unwilling,
7     for ever, so that the things of the holy offering may proceed unaccusably for ever,
8     to await the departed. And this is the will itself: let all envy be far off, and
9     wicked deceit, which I wish to prevail and hold sway everywhere, which
10    will I dictated and arranged to be written and subscribed by the
11    following subscribing witnesses, seven in number, whom witnesses I summoned, and read out to them the validity of the will and persuaded
12    them, according to the laws, to subscribe. And all the rest shall be disinherited, because
13    it has thus seemed rightly and justly to me. The will shall be valid and
14    secure, written in duplicate of like form upon my signature; and having been questioned about these
15    all things I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Panchab son of Panouphios, the aforesaid, made this
16    will, persuaded concerning all the things written herein, as
17    I also subscribed with my own hand. I, Psenthaesis son of Ollos, priest, bear witness to the will,
18    having heard from the testator. I, Aurelius Victor son of Syros, bear witness to the will, having heard from the testator.
19    I, Aurelius Abraamios … bear witness to the will, having heard from the testator. I, Aurelius K…
