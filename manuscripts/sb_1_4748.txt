1     [In the name of the holy and consubstantial Trinity and of our mistress]

5     Sym[m]achos, son of the blessed Menas, merchant [from]
6     [Arsino]it[an] city from the quarter of Alypios …
7     [writing below] in his own hand to Aurelius Tribunus
