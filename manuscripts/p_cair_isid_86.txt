[TRANSLATION]
1     came forward and agreed Aurelia Aeis of Paesios [of mother]
2     Talas from [village] Karaneis to have sold to [Aurelius]
3     Isidoros of [Ptolemaios] from the same [village] a female donkey
4     a colt unbred [white], of the agreed price [toward]
5     each other of silver talents two and drachmas three thousand
6     in full [through] hand, the warranty following
7     to her from everything that will come or [will be seized].
8     The donkey [from here] received the one having bought [this one] such worn-out. The sale is valid, and
9     [Questioned], she agreed. Aurelia Aeis have sold
10    the filly [donkey] and I have the price as aforesaid.
11    Aurelius [Paesis] of Ision I wrote for her [unlettered] [being].
12    in the consulship of our lords Valerius Licinianus
13    Licinnius Augustus and Flavius Valerius Constantine
14    son of the [Augusti].
