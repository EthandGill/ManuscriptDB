[TRANSLATION]
1     To Marti…, steward of Flavia Epimache and of the former (property) of Julia Kallinis,
2     from Didymos the builder. Account of work done in part at the brickyard
3     called Kallon's, in continuation of the brick transported and built with from the brickyard of Sarapion, who was formerly in charge of the works. It is as follows:
4     Epeiph 26, there was moved from the brickyard
5     and built with, of brick 2200.
6     29, there was transported and built with, of brick 2200.
7     Mesore 1, there was transported and built with, of brick 2200.
8     Of the intercalary (days), 1, there was transported and placed in the
9     work of the strapwork, [othe]r brick, 1600.
10    2, there was transported also other [b]rick, 1000.
11    Total, all told, of the brick transported,
12    myriads 4, 4600, at 16 drachmas for the transport of the myriad,
13    total 68 drachmas 23 obols. And there was transported the brick lying in the work, 2600, need having arisen through the donkey-drivers, into the lentil-shop.
14    And there was built with, of brick myriads 4, 2000, at (the rate) for the myriad,
15    with other service and clay-making, of 40 drachmas,
16    total 168 drachmas; there is, all told, of the transport and of the
17    building, 236 drachmas 23 obols. For these
18    there were sent 200 drachmas, remaining owed 36 drachmas 23 obols.
19    In the 12th year of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar
20    the lord, Mecheir.
