1     [In year 1 of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius An]toninus Augustus,
2     [Mesore] … on the …th, [through the office at] … Damarion acknowledges,
3     … his [mother] being Thaesis, [that he has sold to Akchophis] … son of Panephremis
4     [of those from Soknop]aiou Nesos [of the Arsinoite a female slave whose] name is Tapsais …
5     … a tympanum-player, [whom he himself purchased] in the twelfth
6     [year of Hadri]an, in Pharmouthi, from Apenchis … [and] the acknowledging party [Damarion has received] from Akchophis the agreed price [of silver] of the imperial
7     [coinage,] thirteen hundred drachmas; that Akchophis shall therefore hold and own [the afore]written and sold [slave to him] together with offspring
8     [from her] and shall transmit and [administer concerning her in whatever] manner
9     [he may choose]. And Damarion shall guarantee [the] sold slave against
10    [all persons with every] guarantee, this one being such, [free of any claim,] except sacred disease
11    [and seizure for debt] … of the aforesaid
12    [slave] … I, [Damarion] the aforewritten, have sold [to Akchophis the slave Tapsais and have received of the price] thirteen hundred drachmas of silver
13    [and I shall guarantee as aforesaid.] … I wrote on his behalf, as he does
14    [not know letters]. … as aforesaid.
