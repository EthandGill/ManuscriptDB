[TRANSLATION]
1     Zoilos son of Aphynchis and Pasis son of Diogenes and Psois son of Harpsoiphis, all from Mouchinaroou of the Thmoisepho toparchy, the three
2     Persians of the epigone,
3     to Horion son of Kallinikos, of the Sosikosmian (tribe) and the Althaieus (deme), greeting.
4     We acknowledge that we have received
5     and had measured out to us from
6     you of barley artabas
7     nine, total of principal
8     nine artabas of barley,
9     for which we shall pay you as increment (interest)
10    at the third, three artabas,
11    total of principal with increment twelve artabas,
12    which also we shall repay to you
13    without delay and [without] subterfuge in the month Pauni of the
14    present second year
15    of the Aurelii Antoninus
16    and Verus, the lords
17    Augusti, at the threshing-floor of the same
18    Mouchinaroou, and we shall deliver to you at Seneta,
19    as also it has been measured out,
20    new, clean barley,
21    unadulterated, free of clods, sifted and well
22    trodden, without darnel
23    and chaff, by the four-choinix measure of Horos son of Aphynchis from
24    the same Mouchinaroou,
25    those on behalf of the aforewritten Horion
26    doing the measuring to themselves. But if
27    we do not repay as it is written, we shall pay you
28    the aforesaid
29    principal together with the increments to be incurred with the half-again, and the right of execution shall be yours
30    both against us and out of
31    all our property,
32    (we) all being mutually surety for the payment
33    and against whichever one of us you choose.
34    The bond of hand is valid,
35    written in duplicate, of the same type,
36    valid wherever produced
37    and by anyone producing it on your behalf. In the 2nd year of Imperator
38    Caesar Marcus Aurelius
39    Antoninus Augustus
40    and of Imperator Caesar
41    Lucius Aurelius [Verus]
42    Augustus … 9
43    I, Zoilos son of Aphynchis, have received
44    together with the others of barley
45    nine artabas and we shall repay [to you] with increment of
46    three artabas, total twelve artabas,
47    on mutual surety as aforesaid. I, Didymos also called
48    Zoilos son of Didymos, wrote on his behalf
49    as he does not know letters. Pasis
50    son of Diogenes and Psois son of Harpsois
51    have jointly received and shall jointly repay as aforesaid. I, Herakles
52    son of Achilleus, wrote on
53    their behalf as they do not know letters. The date, the same.
