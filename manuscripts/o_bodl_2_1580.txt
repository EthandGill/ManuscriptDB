[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis of produce, 11th year of our lords Emperors
2     Severus and Antoninus the Pious, Augustae
3     Payni 23rd, on behalf of Notus, [in the] name of Sarapammon
4     Of Didymus, wheat, three artabas [and] a half [and] one-twelfth
5     Make wheat artabas 3 ½ 1/12. Serenus [I] have certified [it], wheat artabas three [and] a half [and] one-twelfth, make wheat artabas 3 ½ 1/12.
6     Another on the same day, likewise, wheat 1/24 of an artaba. Serenus [I] have certified [it].
