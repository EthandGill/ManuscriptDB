1     ⳨ (chmg)
2     After the consulate of [Flavi]us Basileios
3     the most glorious, [in the twenty]-fourth [year],
4     on the new moon of Mesore, [of the four]teenth [indiction.]
5     Aurelius Psais, [from] his father Hieremias,
6     from his mother Mari[a], a potter
7     from the village of Pte… of the Antaiop[o]lite
8     nome, n[o]w dwelling here at the [vil]lage
9     of Aphrod[i]te aitoi … [, t]o the [he]irs
10    that is, all the [s]ons [and d]aughters of Helene
11    daughter of Romanos, of Victor … and Mariam
12    her full sister, owners
13    of the same village of Aphrodite, greeting.
14    I have leased from you, willingly and
15    persuaded, for the whole time
16    of my life, reckoned
17    from today, the above-written
18    day, which is the new moon in the month of Mesore
19    of the present fourteenth indiction,
20    for the whole subsequent time of the same
21    life of mine, your ancestral
22    third part, whole, of the whole
23    potter's pottery-works, situated
24    to the south of the village of Aphrodite,
25    near the holy place of Abba Michaelios,
26    which is in your ancestral homestead,
27    next to the pottery-works of the holy monastery
28    of Abba Sourous your forebear,
29    it being held in common with it on the northern
30    part, that is two-thirds, to complete
31    the whole pottery-works, inclining toward the west,
32    together with [your] third share of the
33    cistern there and [a]ll the
34    rights and appurtenances in it, that is
35    the two long buildings that are near
36    the (church of) holy Michaelios, and the long building
37    that is in front of the workshop, inclining
38    toward the north, together with a third
39    share of the kiln and the pitch-kiln; on condition that I
40    have this under me for my own work, and
41    give to you each year on account of the fixed
42    rent, suitable empty vessels without pitch,
43    two thousand four hundred. Makes empty vessels 2,400,
44    at the (proper) time each year without dispute.
45    The lease shall be valid and secure; and having been formally questioned
46    I acknowledged. [I, Aureliu]s Psais son of Hieremias, the aforesaid
47    potter, have leased as set forth. Aurelius
48    Horouonchis son of Menas, having been requested, wrote on his behalf
49    the documents, he being illiterate. [A]urelius Kallinikos
50    son of Hermauos, I bear witness as set forth. Aurelius
51    John son of Kyriakos, I bear witness to the lease
52    as set forth.
53    Through me, Kyros the notary, it was written.
