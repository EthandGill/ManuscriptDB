[TRANSLATION]
1     Nicolaus to Horos greetings. You will do [well] having measured
2     … artabas
3     … I will reckon concerning the full [measure], and to Theucles indeed I sent to you the fifty-three artabas of wheat,
4     which it was necessary for you to measure [as] barley
5     and spelt in the presence of Scorpion and Diodorus. measure it out to him, you
6     then thus do and do not do otherwise. be well. Year 6 Choiach.
7     To Horos Paos
