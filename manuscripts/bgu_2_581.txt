[TRANSLATION]
1     … u
2     [To the Protarch,] strategos [of the Arsinoit]e [of the] Herakleides division.
3     [Ga]ius Longinus [Priscus] discharged
4     from military service, [owning land] in the village of Karanida,
5     I swear the customary oath of the Romans
6     willingly and of my own accord to become surety for Sokrates, son of Gaius Valerius surnamed Chairemonianos [from] the district of Thesmophoreion [of residence and appearance], whom I will also present
7     whenever [he] is [sought]. But if I do not present [him], I myself will deliver the [penalties] against [him]
8     [being demanded], or I will be liable to the oath.
9     And the aforementioned Gaius Longinus Priscus
10    Lucius Octavius Longus identifies [him]
11    discharged from military service. It was written
12    through Herakleides the notary, with Ptolemaios the assistant attending.
13    In the 18th year of the Imperator C[ai]sar Trajan
14    [Ha]drian Augustus, Athyr 14.
