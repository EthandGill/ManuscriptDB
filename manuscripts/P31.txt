[TRANSLATION]
FOLIO Folio 1 Verso — Romans 12:3-8
12:3   …to think more highly than one ought to think, but to think so as to be of sound mind, to each as God has apportioned a measure of faith.
12:4   For just as in one body we have many members, and all the members do not have the same function,
12:5   so we, the many, are one body in Christ, and individually members one of another.
12:6   And having gifts that differ according to the grace given to us: whether prophecy, according to the proportion of faith;
12:7   or service, in the serving; or the one who teaches, in the teaching;
12:8   or the one who exhorts, in the exhortation; the one who shares, in sincerity; the one who leads, in diligence; the one who shows mercy, in cheerfulness.
