[TRANSLATION]
1     … written … [t]o (you) who are also my cell-mate …
2     … of the (?), especially since death is the lot of all, and worn down by the failing of my body,
3     [lest] suddenly and contrary to expectation I should depart this life here, the future being uncertain.
4     Wherefore I have hastened to this written, irrevocable, final testamentary deed of security
5     having the legal force derived from the laws, brought forward everywhere and made public, through which
6     I agree, willing and persuaded, apart from all deceit and fear and force and fraud and
7     any compulsion and all lawful objection and circumvention and every kind of contrivance,
8     without any dissension whatsoever and ill-will, but of my own free intention and self-chosen
9     purpose and of my voluntary will, with right understanding, secure faith, in all fullest
10    mastery and absolute authority, I have committed to you, persuaded with all my choice, living, understanding,
11    being sound of mind, having my reason sound, and bearing my judgment with great exactness,
12    walking upon the earth and going forth into the marketplace — which final little testament I dictated, on the one hand,
13    in the tongue of the Egyptians, but in Greek words and phrases I ordered it to be written, according to what is divinely
14    set forth by the laws well and piously established. May it be, then, that I live and am in health
15    and enjoy all my modest (possessions). But when — which I pray against — I suffer some human (fate)
16    and dissolve this life, I wish and command, after my falling-asleep,
17    that you, the aforementioned Victor, the most reverent presbyter and my disciple,
18    enter into the modest property of every kind that shall be left by me, and inherit
19    me, both movable and immovable and self-moving (livestock), in every form and kind and quality and quantity,
20    both in gold and silver and garments and copper-ware and clothing and documents and house-plots
21    and bare sites and courtyards and all (things) whatsoever, from the precious kind to the least, and (a) plethron of land
22    and a single assarion and an obol, and any chance vessel of earthenware and wood and stone, with respect to
23    that same modest substance of every kind that shall be left by me, whether (it be) from the inheritance
24    of those of mine who are departed, or whether from my own (acquisitions) and labors and from purchase and gift
25    and any other kind of contrivance, in writing or without writing; but, moreover, also the holy little place under me
26    of the holy victorious martyr abba Phoibammon, situated upon the aforementioned
27    divine mountain of the Memnonia — likewise, the uninterrupted mastery I have committed to you, with its
28    venerable substance, from the cheap kind to the costly and the charcoal (?); upon condition that you, the aforementioned
29    Victor, the most God-loving presbyter and monk, my disciple, after my falling-asleep, straightway and immediately enter into the modest substance that shall be left by me,
30    to hold sway and be lord and master over all the (things) that shall be left by me
31    of every kind of property, from the small kind to the least, and (a) plethron of land and a single assarion and an obol
32    and any chance vessel of earthenware and wood and stone; still, moreover, also the sacred oratory
33    with also its venerable substance, from the cheap kind to the costly, just as the one before
34    explained, which (it is for) one alone to acquire, administer, manage, adorn, dwell in, build up, possess,
35    lease out, sell, cede, exchange, present, give, give away, and do everything
36    concerning them validly and unhinderedly, and from them to make expenditure for the administration of the
37    aforesaid sacred place and (for) the provisioning of the poor who pass by, because it has so seemed good to me
38    and been approved by me and (because) I have come, giving thanks, to the present fullest testamentary
39    deed of security; none, then, of those of my kindred who are next of kin, or any other person of my (line), neither according to
40    father nor according to mother, nor of brothers nor of nephews, nor of relatives, nor of cousins, nor any other person whatsoever
41    of any human (kind) being ever able, at (any) season or time, to proceed against you or your heirs
42    or successors or holders-in-possession or any other (person) belonging to you, nor to bring or to make charge in a tribunal
43    or outside a tribunal or under local or extraterritorial law, small or great, nor to complain before friends,
44    nor to make an approach against you or those after you, ever, to a magistrate or judge, nor through an outcry
45    in the most holy church or in praetoria, nor to use any manner (of device), nor to ask (for) a divine and pragmatic
46    rescript against this testament or a part of it (or) the whole, nor indeed to transgress it
47    in any way at all; wherefore, in all (this), it is determined and has seemed good to my good purpose that [you] both receive
48    and give on my behalf, if I should be found possessing or owing or being owed, and the whole
49    receipt-and-repayment to make on my behalf. But if anyone attempting, ever at (any) season, to oppose
50    this uncircumscribed testament — upon which (condition) that one who does any such thing, ever at (any) season,
51    shall in the first place be liable to the divine and most dread oath and to the danger and charge hanging over (one) for perjury,
52    and be subject to the fixed penalty against those who dare to transgress
53    divine and royal oaths, and incur, by way of fine and (for) transgression, six ounces of gold,
54    that is, 6 ounces of gold, exacted by deed (and) by force from the substance of the one rising up and bringing suit; thereupon
55    of necessity (he is) to abide by all the (things) written in this inviolable testament. I wish and
56    command, after my departure from life, the laying-out of my body and my holy offerings
57    and love-feasts and the notable days of (my) death to be carried out by your forethought according to the local
58    law and according to my view and supposition. And I further agree that, if there should appear another document, earlier or later, opposing this testament, upon which (condition) that the same document be stale
59    and powerless, brought forward anywhere; but this testament having the legal
60    force derived from the laws — and, for the establishment and security of all that has been agreed by me, I have sworn
61    by the holy and consubstantial Trinity and by the habit (monastic garb) clothed about me, in nothing to transgress
62    nor to disturb all the aforewritten (terms), but that they be unshaken and unbroken in perpetuity, through the
63    present testament, from which let deceit, envy, (and) the evil one
64    be away (and) depart, which (testament) I have made for you for
65    security, being valid and secure, brought forward everywhere and read out before every
66    rule and authority and power, lawful, strong, upon the subscription of the one subscribing on my behalf and of
67    the witnesses witnessing next (below), trustworthy witnesses, by my commission and request; and, being formally questioned in
68    everything (after it had been) interpreted to me through the Egyptian speech by the notary next (below), (it) being pleasing
69    to me, just as I narrated (it) by my own mouth — that these (things) thus rightly stand, to give, to do, to keep, to be content, to abide (by them)
70    I agreed and released (the deed). It being clear that, since I declared that gold and silver — I swear by the
71    faith of the Christians, I neither have gold nor silver, nor have I acquired (any) since I was born;
72    I do not have, neither within nor without, even to one trimesion, and concerning this I declared; nor did I leave (any)
73    at the time of my death; and whatever I acquired, I gave it to the account of the poor, except as has been said;
