[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greetings: Apollōnios of the [from] ... [I am wronged] by Dositheos, of the
2     from the same village. For having used from me [baskets] ... [and] copper drachmas 4,
3     being demanded of by me repeatedly he does not return ... to trouble me. I beseech
4     then you, King, if it seems good to you, to order Diophán[es the strategó] to wr[ite] Agathoklēs the
5     overseer, so that he [compels] Dositheos to retur[n to me what is owed]. And this having been done, I shall be, through you, King, the common [savior of all, having obtained justice]. Farewell.
6     Agathoklēs. Having looked into, take care so that [he obtains justice. Year 1, Gorpiaeus 28, Tubi 13].
7     Year 1, Gorpiaeus 28, Tubi 13.
8     Apollōnios against Dositheos
9     concerning copper and baskets.
