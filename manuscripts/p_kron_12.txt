[TRANSLATION]
1     In the nineteenth year of Imperator Caesar
2     Trajan Hadrian Augustus, in the month Kaisareios,
3     Mesore 28, in Tebtynis of the division of Polemon of the
4     Arsinoite nome. Aphrodisia also called Ptolemais, daughter of Isidoros, [about] twenty-five [years old], without distinguishing mark, acknowledges,
5     with her guardian, her [brother Menelaos also called …]eios,
6     about thirty [years old, with a scar …] to [Kroni]on
7     son of Cheos, about [seventy-five years old], with a scar on the little finger of the left hand, that she has received from him,
8     from Kronion, twenty artabas of wheat which Kronion owed
9     in an unwritten agreement [to Helene daughter of Hero]des, mother
10    [of Herakleides son of Didymos the Younger son of Hero]des, who had served as kosmetes, now deceased, husband
11    of Aphrodisia, [owed to her] through
12    a contract in the nineteenth year of the deified
13    Trajan, on the twenty-fifth of Epeiph, through the same
14    record-office, which she also handed over to him for cancellation, from those debts owed to Herakleides, assigned by him [to Aphrodisia] according to a will
15    in the past eighteenth year, on Epeiph
16    the nineteenth, through the record-office of Tebtynis, and that Aphrodisia shall not proceed against Kronion concerning
17    these matters, nor concerning any other debt of Herakleides,
18    nor concerning any other matter whatsoever, up to
19    now … Aphrodisia not being prejudiced concerning the things which she has received, as stated above.
20    I, Menelaos also called …[ei]os, am registered as guardian of my sister, she receiving in full, on all the aforesaid terms, the
21    twenty artabas of wheat, as is stated above.
22    I, Aphrodisia, have received the
23    twenty artabas of wheat. Kronion: the receipt has been made out to me.
24    Apollonios wrote on their behalf, since they do not know letters. It has been registered
25    through Lourios also called Apollonios, the keeper of the record-office
26    of Tebtynis and Kerkesoucha [Orous].
