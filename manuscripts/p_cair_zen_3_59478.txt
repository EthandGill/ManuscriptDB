[TRANSLATION]
1     Nicolaus to Ptolemaeus
2     greeting. If you yourself are well
3     and the other things for you are according to your wish,
4     may it be as I wish; and be healthy yourself. I have sent to you
5     the one carrying Polemon
6     [a] piglet of thirty days,
7     and bronze drachmas 2. You then well
8     will do taking care of yourself
9     until I come; I will be present
10    moreover quickly.
11    Of Stesilaos
12    [a] piglet.
13    To Ptolemaeus.
