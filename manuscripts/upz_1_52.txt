[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapion of the successors and sub-administrator,
2     from Ptolemy the Macedonian who is in [confinement]
3     in the great Serapeum near Memphis, already eleven
4     years. Remember, I gave to you the sealed [petition] from the king concerning the twins, and you said
5     to me before Serapis: since you are in confinement, who then
6     will handle these matters? I presented to you Apollonius
7     my younger brother. We have no helper
8     except you and Serapis. The twins are being wronged by those
9     in charge of the temple ration. From year 18
10    from Mechir they have not received. From year 19 from Mechir through Mesore
11    and the five added days they have not received except only half
12    of the oil-workers' ration. From the present year 20 from
13    Thoth 1 through Choiak 9 they have not received
14    except only half; from Choiak 10 through today
15    they have not received bread. They are selling their emmer-wheat
16    because of the high price of grain, from thirty minas
17    per artaba [for] 300 drachmas, and the twins from hunger
18    are wasting away. The king has paid the ration, [but] it is plundered by those in charge
19    of the priests. We ask you, if it seems good to you, to recall the supervisor of the priests
20    Psintaes and to compel
21    to repay to us what is owed
22    of year 18 and year 19. And to you Serapis
23    grants you favor and form toward the
24    king.
25    To Sarapion of the successors and sub-administrator,
26    from Ptolemy the Macedonian
