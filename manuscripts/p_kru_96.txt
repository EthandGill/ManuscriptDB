[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God the Almighty.
2     Written in the month of Mesore 26, of the 13th indiction.
3     I, Phoibamon son of Athanasios, this one who gives
4     a subscriber to subscribe on his behalf, an[d]
5     requesting trustworthy witnesses to testify
6     to this deed of gift, inviolable and indissoluble and not to be undone by them, by the laws
7     which are established; I write to the right
8     of the holy martyr, the saint Phoibammon
9     of the mountain of Djeme, through you, Sourous
10    the most reverend deacon, and through everyone who shall administer after you in this single monastery of the saint Phoibammon —
11    namely: since the law of God commands
12    and exhorts everyone, that each one
13    do the good and the thing that is good which is in his hand,
14    with that which is his own; when God the merciful, in His mercy,
15    commanded that this child of mine be begotten for me,
16    I made remembrance of my sins; I decreed it,
17    namely that if he live I shall give him to the monastery of Apa Phoibammon, for the salvation
18    of my soul. When the child became small (still young) and prospered, I wished to transgress
19    the vow, this one which I had set up with God
20    and with His saint. After these things, the small child fell into a great illness
21    which was very severe, and his face was distorted, and
22    we continued, abiding in a great grief on account of the small child, and we were stung whenever we saw all the small children safe and
23    being a comfort to their parents. We took
24    counsel, I and his mother, that perhaps
25    God and His saint do this to us because we have transgressed the agreements
26    which we had set up with him. We took counsel,
27    we with one another, that: let us rise up,
28    let us take the small child, let us go to the holy monastery, let us entreat the holy martyr that: forgive us
29    the audacity which we did; perhaps he shall entreat God and he shall give
30    the healing of the small child. We took, then,
31    the small child; we brought him in to the holy monastery; we continued entreating
32    God and His saint, the holy
33    Phoibamon, weeping and entreating the martyr, that: forgive us
34    the sin which we did; and we continued partaking
35    of the holy mysteries, with
36    the small child; and at the completion of one month
37    of days, the one who had heard the entreaty of the blessed (mother) of the mother of Samuel
38    the prophet hearkened to us also;
39    he granted the healing of the small child,
40    and we went to our house, giving glory to God. We considered, namely that this child
41    small was reckoned among the dead before he obtained the healing; but now he has been saved.
42    Let him become a servant to the holy monastery, the place where he obtained the healing. When we came, then, today, we considered, namely: lest, when our village seek to cast trouble upon the small child, I make haste, I draw up this deed of gift, I give it to our father the bishop and the steward, that he may place it in the library of the holy monastery, so that, if they hinder the small child from being a servant of the holy monastery, they shall produce it
43    if they question him; they shall withdraw from the great judgment. Whoever shall dare, among the Christians, to seek out this small
44    child from the holy monastery forever, that one shall draw upon himself the judgment of the Lord
45    through the mouth of the hierophant Moses, on account of those who shall hinder the vow
46    of the Lord; and again, if this small child
47    wish not to be a servant of the holy monastery — all that he shall beget by his handiwork he shall give
48    in to the holy monastery,
49    according to how he shall divide it with the steward for the time being, it being secured for the right of the holy monastery. I drew up this deed of gift, valid and having force in every place where they shall produce it; I read it to me
50    through the notary; he did these things; I confirmed it
51    with a subscriber and trustworthy witnesses; I issued it according to the procedure of the laws. I, Patlole
52    son of the blessed Abraham, in the hamlet And, give witness.
53    I, Johannes Sanagap, in the hamlet And, give witness.
54    I, Senouthios Joannake, in Hermont, give witness.
55    Through me, Elisaios, least presbyter, from Hermonthis,
56    it was done.
