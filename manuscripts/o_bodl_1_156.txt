[TRANSLATION]
1     Of year 21, Mesore 26, [he] has measured
2     into the great storehouse in the city of Zeus
3     for place 19 of year [by] Indos
4     [by] Abiestus: wheat one and one-half quarter
5     total of wheat 1, ½, ¼. Panas.
6     Paniskos: I have measured as stated
7     of wheat one, ½, ¼; becomes wheat ½, 1, ¼.
