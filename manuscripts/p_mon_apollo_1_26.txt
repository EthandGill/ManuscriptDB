[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the holy Apa Apollo, concerning the judgment of the monastery [through the] father Apa George the archimandrite and the father of the place …
2     We did not find for us monasteries that they yield that which belongs in the fields of the village …
3     We did not exchange with him and men that [they are] that they remain, we shall complete
4     We shall work them under him of the place and our fatherhood for the sake of the fruit
5     That comes [in] the year of the god 868 of the indiction and he said to us and
6     [For the sake of] … [the] days all of our life upon us …
7     [... and ... 2] … of them that they be first we shall be ready
8     The lease of the [... ...] and [the ... 2] …
9     From the monastery that they give that which belongs
10    In the village … [they] … 2 gold coins from carats 22 ½ from the
11    village of Horus
