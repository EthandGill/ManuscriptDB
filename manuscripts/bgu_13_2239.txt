1     To the strategus of Soknepaiou Nesos at Se[ta]boutis
2     of the Herakleid division, from Panephrommiοs son of [P]aous, senior priest
3     [of those] from the same village; on the night leading to the fourth of the month of Neou
4     Augustus, some having attacked in a bandit-like manner in the fourth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus
5     into my existing d[i]pyrgyion in the same village, they having also removed an upper thur[i]da, gotten inside, carried off one by one of the things written below; therefore I submit to you this petition in order that
