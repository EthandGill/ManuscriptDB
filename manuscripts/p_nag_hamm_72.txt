1     To Sansnotis and Psatos
2     to monks Proter[ia] greetings
3     If it is possible among you
4     to search for a little
5     chaff for the service of my animals
6     because they are lacking, and I cannot find [any] to buy here. When then you have found,
7     send [it] to me for the
8     price, [namely] how much the
9     wagon of chaff, and in order that
