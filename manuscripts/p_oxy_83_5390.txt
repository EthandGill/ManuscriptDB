[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] 7th year [of Maurice Ti]berius the eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the consulship of the same
2     [most pious] our master, 6th year, Phaophi 24, seventh indiction.
3     [To the] most magnificent successors of Apion of most renowned memory, who was
4     [protopatri]cian, landowners also here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas
5     [the servant, who] asks and procures for his own masters the same most laudable
6     [persons] the action and obligation, Aurelius Paneuous of the elders, son
7     [of the blessed Apo]llo, subscribing below with my own hand, originating from the village
8     … [of the Oxy]rhynchite nome, subject to the pagarchy of your Magnificence, chief villager,
9     [greeting.] I acknowledge, of my own free will and by voluntary choice, swearing
10    [the divine and] august oath, that I stand surety and undertake before
11    [your] Magnificence, through those belonging to it, for Aurelius Menas son of
12    …, whose mother is Sophia, originating from the village of Mouchis of the Arsinoite nome,
13    [whom, when he is] sought against me by your Magnificence
14    [through those belonging to it] … .
