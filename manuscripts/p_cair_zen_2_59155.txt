[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Zenon, greetings. The king has ordered us [to]
2     double-sow the land. So when you have completely threshed [out] the spring wheat,
3     immediately water the land by hand; but if it is not possible,
4     [set up] scoop-wheels, more [of them], thus water [it]; not [for] more [than]
5     five days hold [back] the water, and having cooled [it] immediately
6     sow the three-month wheat. And write also to us
7     when you are able to harvest the wheat.
8     Farewell. Year 30, Dios 13, Athur 3.
9     To Zenon.
10    … [of] seed.
