[TRANSLATION]
1     Let your paternal piety be asked [to send] the papyrus of the most god-loving abb[a]
2     Of Constantine to send to me through the most reverent abba Senouthes descending; for this [reason]
3     I sent the letter-carrier boy, master
4     [To the master] of me, all things, [to the] most god-loving holiest father abba Taurinus bishop; John son
