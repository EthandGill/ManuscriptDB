[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 30 …, Epeiph 7
2     [as if you should] read the letter
3     [appoint] over us
4     Dionysios of the resident cavalry
5     so that for what [he] has been charged
6     by Petesirios [who is] from
7     Kallikles of the chief bodyguards
8     might be judged. Until this [time]
9     and his crops
10    let [them] be preserved until
11    something from us is announced.
