[TRANSLATION]
1     [...] [drachmas] hundred
2     [fifty] [...] [for] months
3     [...] [from the present] month
4     Of interest [of] one drachma per mina each month, and [I will] necessarily repay
5     Those around Onesimus to Ischyrion the principal amount set forth and the
6     Interest in the month Pauni of the present
7     Year without fail, it coming to him
8     Of the execution both from the one acknowledging [the debt]
9     And from all the property belonging to him
10    Just as from legal judgment. Onesimus son of Amerimnus: I have the drachmas hundred fifty of the principal of the loan
11    And I will repay [...] the interest as
12    Is set forth. Isidorus son of Heron wrote on behalf of him [who is] illiterate.
13    Sathro confirmed. Onesimus, cook.
