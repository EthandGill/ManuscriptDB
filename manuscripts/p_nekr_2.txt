[TRANSLATION]
1     … [To Aurelia Seno]siris, with her guardian Psenamounis son of Apollonios, her father, undertaker (female), from the same (place), greeting.
2     I acknowledge that I will not proceed against nor bring claim against you concerning the (rights?) sold by my deceased father Ophellios son of Psais, of ten days
3     from the hunting-place in
4     the places of Psenkeme, the whole
5     hunting-place being in common with Aurelius Ploutogenes, whose mother is Aurelia Theodora, for the remaining …
6     … days, the neighbors being: to the south, the water-reservoir of Paapis son of Philosarapis,
7     to the north, another hunting-place, but (that I will)
8     leave to you the aforesaid ten days
9     secure from everything and to have
10    no claim against you concerning this, because
11    my father has received the
12    price in full. This document, written in duplicate, was written on behalf of the aforesaid
13    Aurelius Psais. Let it be valid and
14    as if recorded in a public archive
15    and, having been formally questioned, I acknowledged.
16    Year 3 of Imperator Caesar
17    Marcus Antonius Gordianus
18    Pius Fortunate Augustus.
19    Aurelius Psais, the aforesaid,
20    I have deposited the aforesaid (document), on all the terms
21    it contains. I wrote on his behalf,
22    Aurelius Basileides, having been asked.
23    Aurelius Ploution, I witness.
24    Aurelius Sarapion, I witness.
