[TRANSLATION]
1     Zenodoros to Zenon greetings. If you are well, it would be good; and we are also healthy
2     ourselves. Know that Dionysios my brother has won the contest in Sacred Island
3     of the Ptolemaian [games]. I have written to you so that you might know. We have received
4     also the garment which you sent, and you will oblige me by sending the other [one] now;
5     let it be thicker than this and soft in the wool, so that Dionysios my brother may have [it] for the Arsinoia [festival].
6     Farewell. Year 35, Loios 8.
7     [to the one from] Apollonios
8     [to] [Philadelph]ia
9     To Zenon.
