[TRANSLATION]
1     Petenephotes … to Sarapion
2     to the most honored, greetings. Since they say you have our letters
3     from Egypt, you will do well, if
4     you have my letters, send to me, for they are necessary.
5     They are. Greet the
6     all those who love you. Farewell.
