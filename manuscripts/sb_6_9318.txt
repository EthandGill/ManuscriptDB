1     Sarapias Plenios, [son] of Pamonthus, of Amphiomis, mother's daughter Plenios [daughter] of Sarapion; lived 11 years;
2     and Psenmot Papashemis, [son] of Sabinus, likewise having lived 11 years; they received burial in year 17, Athur 30.
3     Sarapias Plenios, [son] of Pamonthus, of Amphiomis; lived 11 years;
4     and Psenmot Papashemis, [son] of Sabinus, having lived the same years;
5     Year 17, Athur 30.
6     Pebotos, [son] of Krathes, temple-warden of the great Sarapis, [aged] about 73 years.
7     [Son] of Senemphiomis and Kalasiries [son] of Herieus, 51 years, 1 month, 21 days.
8     daughter of Herieus Pamonthus. Krates [son] of Pebotos.
9     [son] of Krathes Psenmothus, the same [as] Pebotos [son] of Krathes, temple-warden of the great
10    Sarapis, 17 years, 8 months, 17 days.
