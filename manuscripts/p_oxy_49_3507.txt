[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings, my lord brother Sarapodoros;
2     I, Apollonios, address you.
3     I sent you the letter of Andromachos
4     so that you may deliver (it) and send us the reply
5     quickly. His request
6     is about the decree of the undertaking of the gymnasiarchy being kept for him;
7     and if it is possible
8     for those extra days which last year he was compelled to perform to be reckoned to him for [the] yearly (ones), do not hesitate. But if not, even (let it be) from now on
9     for the decree to be kept. For this
10    is also most just. Achilleus, concerning
11    the wine, neither did anything
12    nor demanded (anything). He seemed to be vexed at this. The price of wine
13    here is altogether in decline;
14    for it stands at as little as 1,900 drachmas, so that,
15    if it is possible for you to ask Alexandros
16    the Akoreite, so that
17    through his boat he may … (arrange) for it to be brought to me, and whatever you find
18    for what you write about, do (it). The Antinoite contractors, however, ordered me that,
19    either we buy your (goods) at the same
20    price, or we carry them off to your brother. I have delivered out the carpets. For the shorthand-writer did not give me a carpet. He promised,
21    however, to give me 9 drachmas … of new.
22    You ought, however, to know that
23    you must come forward on account of the appeal before the appointed term, according to
24    the ordinance. There is here an official of the patrimonial
25    procuratorship
26    seeking a fine for appeals,
27    who also (came) to us on account of the matter then with the patrimonial (procurator) about a bond of 6 talents, and up to now
28    we have done nothing yet with him; and if anything newer is done, it will be disclosed to you.
29    They greet
30    you, our little children and their mother and all our people. I pray for your health, my lord.
31    To Sarapodoros, exegetes,
32    from Apollonios.
