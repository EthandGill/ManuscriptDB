[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Didymos] also called Lurius son of Lysimachos
2     [from Kr]onion son of Pakebkis, one of those
3     [from the village of] Tebtynis, a Persian of the epigone.
4     [I wish] to lease from you for six years
5     [from the coming] eighteenth year
6     [of Hadrian Caesar] the lord the arouras belonging
7     [to you near Teb]tynis in what is called the western
8     [plain], of catoecic holding, three arouras, common,
9     … called of …neus, the
10    [first] and third and fifth year
11    [of the lease] for sowing of fodder-grass
12    for browsing [of cattle] and folding, as rent each year
13    [on the] three arouras, of silver drachmas
14    … four; and the second and fourth
15    [and sixth] year for sowing of wheat, the rent over the
16    [three-year period] each year, together with which I shall receive as wheat-seed
17    [three artabas], the whole each year of the three arouras
18    [being thirty artabas of wheat] by the four-choinix measure of the granary of Tebtynis, and of presents over the
19    [three-year period] each year one hen worth two drachmas,
20    [all being] free of deduction and risk; and I performing the
21    [yearly] works that are fitting and the rents I will pay [each] year in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor, and the
22    [rent in Phamenoth], the public freight-charges
23    [of hastening] being my responsibility, and the
24    [conveyance of the] rents yours; [and after] the
25    [term I will hand] over [the] arouras clean [of]
26    [couch-grass] and all rubbish except reed and rush. And you shall guarantee to me the lease
27    [and clean of] public dues … . Didymos
28    [also called Lurius son of Lysim]achos: I have leased as aforesaid.
29    [Year 17 of Imperator] Trajan Hadrian Augustus.
