[TRANSLATION]
1     and … former gymnasiarch, exegetes of the
2     city of the Oxyrhynchites, and however he is styled, greeting.
3     I acknowledge that I owe you, on account of the rents
4     of the lands of yours which I farmed near Kerkethyris, of wheat by your measure, for each ten [arouras]
5     forty-two artabas and a quarter, two choinikes, and of vegetable-seed likewise
6     by the oil-making measure of Kerkethyris, two and a half artabas, which I shall of necessity
7     repay you, the wheat in three years and the vegetable-seed in two years: in the
8     present 3rd year, fourteen artabas of wheat, four choinikes, and of vegetable-seed
9     one artaba and a quarter, and in the coming
10    4th year likewise fourteen artabas of wheat,
11    four choinikes, and of vegetable-seed
12    the remaining one artaba and a quarter, and
13    in the 5th year likewise the remaining of the wheat,
14    fourteen artabas, four choinikes,
15    in all cases without interest. And if I do not
16    repay at the stated due dates,
17    I shall pay you interest at one and a half times, the execution being made against you both from me and
18    from all my possessions of every kind.
19    The bond is valid, written in duplicate,
20    produced anywhere and by anyone who produces it on your behalf. And concerning
21    these things being rightly and properly done, on being formally questioned by you I gave my assent. Year 3
22    of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius
23    Severus Alexander Pius Felix
24    Augustus, Phamenoth 8. I, Aurelius Patas
25    son of Papontos, owe the forty-two artabas of wheat and a quarter, two choinikes, and of vegetable-seed
26    two and a half artabas, and I shall repay at the stated due dates, and on being formally questioned
27    I gave my assent. I, Aurelius Diogenes also called
28    Peison son of Kallinikos, wrote on
29    his behalf, as he does not know letters.
30    In the present 3rd year, at the threshing-floor of the hamlet …, new wheat, clean, unadulterated,
31    free of lumps, free of barley, sifted, as measured for the public treasury, the measuring being done by
32    your agents. And if I do not repay
33    as I have written, I shall pay you interest at one and a half times, the execution being made against you
34    both from me and from all my possessions of every kind.
35    The bond is valid,
36    written in single copy, produced anywhere and by anyone who produces it on your behalf. And concerning these things being rightly
37    and properly done, on being formally questioned I gave my assent. Year 3 of Imperator Caesar
38    Marcus Aurelius Severus Alexander
39    Pius Felix Augustus,
40    Hathyr 15. I, Aurelius Papontos
41    son of Ouestinos, have received the four and a half artabas of wheat, and I shall repay as aforesaid, and on being formally questioned
42    I gave my assent. I, Aurelius Diogenes
43    also called Peison, wrote on his behalf,
44    as he does not know letters.
