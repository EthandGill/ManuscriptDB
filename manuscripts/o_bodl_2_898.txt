[TRANSLATION]
1     Psensenkamētes, collector of the silver tax of the five-village district
2     To Petearouēreis, son of Senpekysius, of Panakhatos.
3     I received on account of land-tax of year 19 three drachmas one obol
4     It becomes drachmas 3 obol, which is also two five-obols ½, copper coins 2. Year 20 of Hadrian Caesar
5     Of the lord, Mecheir 25.
