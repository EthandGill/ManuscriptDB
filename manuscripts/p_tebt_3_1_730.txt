[TRANSLATION]
1     year 4, Athur 6.
2     to Osoroeris the royal secretary. on the 5th of the present month making an inspection …
3     the fields around the village I found an effusion of blood
4     , but I learn from those of the village
5     Theodotus son of Dositheos having gone out regarding these [matters]
6     no longer to return. I report.
7     to Lykophron the village scribe the same [report]. but [something] was added: I have written …
8     but also to Osoroeris the royal secretary concerning the same [matters].
