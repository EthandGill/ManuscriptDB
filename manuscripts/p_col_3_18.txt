1     ... to Panakestor, greetings. I request you to help [me].

3     [and] ... Arsinoe Philadelphus and your divine spirit, certainly not
4     ... I have been led away. This is a lie, and you yourself know it.

11    ... [I beseech therefore] you and I supplicate you to write to Zoilus [about me] ... her ... [new] ... ... ...
