[TRANSLATION]
1     There acknowledge to one another the Aurelii: Modestos also called
2     Olympos, son of Patas, his mother being Heraklous,
3     from the city of Oxyrhynchus, tax-collector together with others
4     of the grain dues of the metropolis of the upper
5     toparchy of the region of Sko, and the one of the partners,
6     Melas son of Melas, and Nepotianos son of Theon,
7     both from the same city; Modestos
8     also called Olympos (acknowledges) that he has assigned to those about Melas the portion belonging to him of the said
9     collectorship, on condition that they make the registration and the collection of the amounts to be assessed
10    by the royal scribe of the nome upon
11    the same collectorship of grain dues
12    for the reckoning of the past 4th year, and that they obey the decisions made and submit summary accounts, because they have received in full
13    the sums agreed upon for both salary and price of papyrus and other expenses, three hundred fifty drachmas of silver
14    by hand, the portion of the destitute and those hard to collect [being] toward the common account of [the principals; and those about] Melas (acknowledge) that they are content upon
15    these [things and will do each thing] as has been shown above, [so that] also unhinder]ed shall become the
16    [Modestos also called Olym]pos concerning the [portion belonging to him of the said] collectorship.
