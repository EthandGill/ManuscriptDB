1     Copy. From receipts: Sarapas son of Sarapas, of the weavers of Philadelphia, Tybi 7, 16 drachmas;
2     Pharmouthi 24 drachmas; Thoth 12, 36 drachmas; total 76.
3     Pacheiris son of Nemesas, 3rd year of Hadrian, Loios (?), 24 drachmas; Tybi 7, 16 drachmas; Phaophi of the 3rd year, 24 drachmas;
4     total 64.
5     Apis son of Apis, Pharmouthi 28, 36 drachmas, and of the 3rd year Phaophi, 24 drachmas; Tybi 12, 16 drachmas; total 76.
6     Peleeus son of Ision, Tybi 7, 16 drachmas; Mecheir 9, 16 drachmas; Phamenoth 28, 12 drachmas;
7     Pauni 17, 32 drachmas; total 76.
8     Harpaesis son of Onnophris, Phamenoth 23, 32 drachmas, and Pauni 17, 16 drachmas;
9     Thoth 12 of the 3rd year, 28 drachmas; total 76.
10    Herakles son of Sambas, Tybi 7, 16 drachmas.
11    Herakles son of Herakleios, Pharmouthi 27, 16 drachmas; Phaophi, 28 drachmas; Pauni 9, 20 drachmas;
12    Mesore, 12 drachmas; total 76.
13    Herakleides son of Horigenes, 140 drachmas; Phaophi 23, 40 drachmas; of the 3rd year Tybi 2, 16 drachmas; total 76.
14    Nemesas son of Onnophris, Pauni 13, 16 drachmas; Tybi 17, 12 drachmas;
15    Epeiph 2, 16 drachmas; Mesore 23, 12 drachmas; Phamenoth 13, 20 drachmas; total 76.
16    Apis son of Onnophris, Tybi 12, 16 drachmas, and Phamenoth 23, 24 drachmas, and
17    Pharmouthi 28, 16 drachmas; of the 3rd year Phaophi 27, 20 drachmas; total 76.
18    Nepheros son of Onnophris, Mecheir 24, 16 drachmas; Phamenoth 13, 24 drachmas;
19    Pauni 10, 24 drachmas; Mesore 23, 12 drachmas; total 76.
20    Peterpsis son of Herakleios, Hathyr 28, into the count of Hadrian, 8 drachmas.
21    Nepheros, 76 drachmas.
22    Petearpochras, 76 drachmas.
23    Didymos, 76 drachmas.
