1     In the reign of Ptolemy [also called Alexander, the god]
2     Philometor, and of Berenice [the goddess Philadelphos, in the six]teenth year, and the other matters of the public (formulae) [as is written in Alexandria],
3     in the month Apellaios … [Phaophi] …
4     in the city of Oxyrhynchus in the [Thebaid; there lent]
5     Simaristos son of Demetrios [also called …]os, a Laconian, and Didymos a Macedonian, to Demetrios [a Persian of the]
6     epigone …
7     of Aphrodite, of Berenice …
8     from the …
9     of the common funds, [of copper coin]
10    fifty[-three] talents [and … drachmas]
11    a thousand, at interest [of two drachmas per mina]
12    each, every [month. And let]
13    Demetrios [repay] to the lenders [or to the man]
14    who shall be appointed in charge of the common treasury, the fifty-three [talents and …] thousand [drachmas]
15    of copper and the interest in the [ ] …
16    month of the sixteenth year. And [if he does not]
17    repay as has been written, [let]
18    [Deme]trios [pay] to the lenders [or to the]
19    man who shall be appointed in charge of the common treasury, the loan at one and a half (times),
20    and the interest at the simple (rate) both of the contracted and of the overrun time. Surety, for all the things in the contract,
21    for its full payment, of Demetrios himself:
22    his [wife] Asklepias daughter of Sosibios, a Persian woman, with the same guardian. And let the right of execution belong
23    to the lenders or to the man who shall be appointed in charge of the common treasury
24    against both of them and against whichever (of them) he may choose
25    and against all their possessions
26    just as in consequence of a legal judgment. The contract is valid.
27    Simaristos, about 69 years old, of middle height,
28    a scar in the middle of the forehead.
29    Didymos, about 59 years old, larger than middle (height),
30    sleek-skinned, a scar under the left jaw.
31    Demetrios, about 33 years old, larger than [middle (height)],
32    with an ankle-bone (mark), a scar on the right eyebrow, another
33    under the same.
