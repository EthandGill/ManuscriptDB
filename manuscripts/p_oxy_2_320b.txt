[TRANSLATION]
1   Year fifth of Nero Cla[ud]ius Caesar Augustus Germanic[u]s
2   Imperator, Paun[i] (2nd hand) 25 (1st hand), in the city of O[xy]rhynchus of the Thebaïd.
3   Tryphaina daughter of [.]orion, of those from the city of Oxyrhynchus, lent —
4   with (her) guardian, the husband of her sister Tryphaina's daughter
5   Isidora, Dionysios son of Ammonios — to Tryphon and
6   Onnophris, both sons of Dionysios, Persians of the epigone,
7   and to [Tr]yphon's wife Sareus daughter of Apion, a Per[sian wom]an,
8   with (her) guardian, her aforewritten husband Tryphon — to all,
9   of those from the same city, in the street, of silver of Augustan
10   and Ptolemaic coinage, three hundred and
11   fourteen drachmas of prin[ci]pal, to which nothing at all has been added;
12   and let the borrowers repay to Tryphaina the
13   four hundred and fourteen silver dra[ch]mas on the thirtieth
14   o[f the] Neos [S]ebastos [m]onth of the coming sixth year
15   of Nero [Claudiu]s C[ae]s[ar] Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
16   without [any post]pon[e]ment. And if they do not repay as
17   is writte[n, let the] borrowers pay to Tryphai[n]a
18   th[e] pr[incipal with hal]f as much again, and for the time elapsed
19   th[e] appropri[ate intere]st, being sureties for one another for repayment,
20   a[nd let the] right of execution belong to Tryphaina, both from the borrowers
21   and from whomever she choo[ses, and] from all their possessions,
22   just as by legal judgement. The contract is valid.
23   (3rd hand) Year fifth of Nero Claudius Caesar
24   Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Pauni the [fifth]
25   and twentieth (25th). Through Apollonios, formerly transacting (as agent) of Secundus,
26   appointed by the associate agorano(moi), it has been transacted.
