[TRANSLATION]
1     Titus Flavius Sarapion to Publius
2     Aelius Apollonios, greeting. I acknowledge that I will be
3     your partner in the half share of the farming
4     of the present seventeenth year of Hadrian Caesar
5     the lord, of (the lands) which you took up from the state from
6     the unproductive land in the Upper and Lower Peri-polin, of which, on the one hand, from the
7     Xenon allotment near Tertembeuthis, down toward
8     Sarapieion, of sixty-three and a half arouras,
9     excepting six arouras which you leased to others,
10    with which I shall have no concern, so that the lands common
11    to me and you, as aforesaid, are 57½ arouras; and near Temseu,
12    from the (allotment) of Mikylos, thirty-seven arouras, making in all 94½ arouras,
13    … which you took up from the state, on condition that I pay half of the rent that is or shall be charged to you, while I, equally with you, (provide) all
14    the expenses and seed and labors and all the rest unhindered, for the half share.
15    The partnership is valid. Year 17 of Imperator
16    Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, in the month Hadrianos,
17    8. I, Hermaios son of Didymos, wrote on his behalf, as he does not know
18    letters, the account remaining for you concerning what
19    I, Sarapion, owe you according to the handwritten note,
20    as it contains. I, Hermaios son of Didymos, likewise wrote
21    on his behalf this stipulation too, as he does not know letters.
