[TRANSLATION]
1     Of grain (matters).
2     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Berenike, the Benefactor Gods, in the eighth year, when the priest of Alexander and of the
3     Sibling Gods and of the Benefactor Gods and of the Father-loving Gods was Ptolemy son of Ptolemy son of Stasikrates, when the basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphos was Arsinoe daughter of Sosibios, in the month
4     Artemisios, on the sixteenth, in Sephtha of the Oxyrhynchite (nome).
5     Amyntas son of Menedemos, a Cyrenaean of the epigone, lent to Kallippos, a Cyrenaean of the (troop) of Philon, a private individual,
6     eighty artabas of olyra-grain … …
7     The eighty artabas of olyra-grain let Kallippos repay to Amyntas in the month Peritios of the eighth year, grain
8     pure and unadulterated, by the measure, the chous, the …
9     by just measure, and let him deliver the grain
10    to Sephtha, wherever Amyntas may direct, at his own expense;
11    and if he does not repay as is written, let Kallippos pay to Amyntas, as the price of each artaba of olyra-grain,
12    … drachmas of silver, and let the right of execution belong to Amyntas
13    and to another acting for [him against] Kallippos, according to the decree. And let this contract be valid wherever it is produced.
14    Witnesses: … Drakon a Thracian, the two of the (troop)
15    of Philon, private individuals; O… son of Asklepiades a Milesian;
16    Bakchios son of Chrysippos … Apollodoros …
17    … of Megara, Tochlas son of Xenarchos, Kard… the five
18    [of the epigone] …
