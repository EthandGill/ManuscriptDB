[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Domitius Modestus, the most illustrious
2     prefect of the sacred praetorium, and of Flavius Arintheus, the
3     most illustrious general (stratelates).
4     To the Aurelii Doro[theos son of Si]l[vanus] and Ar[t]emi[doros]
5     son of Herminos, councillors and riparii of the Hermopolite,
6     [from Aurelius] Silvanus son of Pachymis, his mother Tbekis, from the village
7     … of the same nome, peace-officer (eirenarch). I deliver up and
8     report the persons registered below as dyke-cleaners (ekboleis)
9     of the embankments for the happily incoming 17th, that is 2nd
10    indiction, of the rising of the crops of the fortunate 18th, that is
11    3rd indiction, being competent and suitable and fit
12    for the task entrusted to them, at the peril of me
13    the peace-officer. They are:
14    Hatres son of Horos
15    Hatres son of Koll[outhos]
16    whom I guarantee and will produce as fulfilling
17    the task entrusted to them soundly and
18    faithfully, so that in nothing may there be blame. But if
19    any of them should fall short and I do not produce (him),
20    [I myself] will undertake the account on his behalf;
21    and on being formally questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius Silvanus the aforesaid
22    peace-officer, I have submitted (it). I, Aurelius Ammonios, wrote
23    on his behalf, as he does not know [letters].
24    Through me, Eponychos, it was written.
