[TRANSLATION]
1     … [and these things I am ready to throw down] [to you] [whenever you wish] … … without any
2     [objection] or [sophistry], the deed being to you
3     from … just as from judgment,
4     [this written document] of the debt binding and firm from …
5     … based on the signature of the one signing on my behalf, Aurelius [Pachom]saites, and having been questioned, I acknowledged [it].
6     Aurelius [K]olobus Paketus, the aforesaid vinedresser, I owe to you and am indebted
7     … … [for] the account of the advance, the aforesaid gold
8     [coins], two, and I will repay whenever you wish, and I have acknowledged as aforesaid.
9     Aurelius Aphthonios Pachomsaktes, physician from the same city, wrote
10    on his behalf being present and having said (he) does not know letters.
