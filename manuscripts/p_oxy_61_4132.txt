[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus
2     Christ, our God and Savior.
3     In the reign of our most divine and
4     most pious master, greatest
5     benefactor, Flavius Heraclius the
6     eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the year
7     9, Pachon 7, of the 7th indiction.
8     Aurelius Ieremias, son of Ioseph,
9     his mother Thaesis, from the hamlet
10    of Pangouleeion of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
11    with my surety Stephanos
12    the steward, son of the blessed
13    Menas, to Aurelius Epiphanios
14    the horse-doctor, son of Phoibammon,
15    from the city of the Oxyrhynchites. I acknowledge
16    that I have received from you here
17    already eight gold solidi
18    by the private standard
19    toward the price agreed on with me,
20    in full, for wine, of eight hundred
21    five-xestes-measure jars,
22    at the vintage of the month Mesore
23    of the present [7th indiction, of wine]
24    of the eighth [collection] …
25    of the holy … or [also I]
26    his surety, from my own resources,
27    (am) to give on his behalf, if the principal
28    (debtor) should default,
29    undertaking the good quality of the wine until
30    the month Tybi. And I will also give the
31    expenses of the vintage: of old
32    wine eight jars and
33    eight new-must (jars) and one piglet,
34    of the price of four carats.
35    And I acknowledge that I shall deliver these to you
36    up to the government canal.
37    The bond, written in single copy, is binding, and on being formally questioned
38    I gave my assent. Aurelius Ieremias son of Ioseph — this bond is satisfactory to me
39    as aforesaid. I, Markos, wrote on behalf of
40    him, he being illiterate.
41    Bond of Ieremias son of Ioseph from Pang[oul]eeion, with the surety Stephanos the steward, of 8 gold solidi
42    by the private standard, for 800 five-xestes-measure jars of wine.
