[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Apia the
2     [sister] greetings.
3     "[To the one delivering] to you the letter, to the Theba[n], [give] 24 drachmas owed to him, having received one vine basket…. let them deliver"
4     the boat to the harbor-keeper
5     with two oars and pole
6     and sail. I held one Theban on [the] dike, to whom you will send
7     bread, 4 drachmas, and to us copper.
8     I greet Seren the brother
9     and Ptolemaeus. About the epistrategos, report to me. Be strong
10    to you I pray, sister.
11    "[Carry from] the Theban a sack. Whom you urge quickly"
12    to come, since he is going to [remain] on the dike. And he says
13    to know and fish to hunt.
