[TRANSLATION]
1     [unfermented or rancid from the] same wine until [the month] Tybi of the same indiction,
2     [I confess that I will exchange with you in good] wine [without] delay and without any
3     [dispute and] trial and justice, and [I am] ready to return to you
4     [the] … [ened] wine from your property, [there] being execution for you
5     [from me and from [all] my property just as from judgment; the
6     [agreement is authoritative and] firm, and when asked I confessed. Aurelius Pames son of Dionysius, the aforesaid, I have been satisfied
7     [of the full price] of the wine skins two hundred twenty, and I will return at the time
8     [as stated. Aurelius] [Phoib]ammon son of Kollouthos from Hermupolis, being asked, I wrote on behalf of him letters [not knowing how].
9     … [son] of Taurinus from Hermupolis, I attest to the agreement and the [giving of the gold of the] price of the wine. Aurelius Victor son of Horus from Hermupolis, I attest to the agreement
10    [and the giving of the gold] of the price of the wine.
11    … by me, Ioannes, [it] was written.
