1     Year five of Autokrator Caesar
2     Domitian Augustus German[icus], month
3     of Caesareum, 21st until … and Eutyches
4     grain-measurers of Theogonidas and other villages of Polemon's district

10    and to Lysimachos of Didymus, through Didymus brother, one-artaba-holder, resident
11    of Kerkesephis, wheat four artabas one-half one-eighth, amounts to wheat artabas 4 1/2 1/8,
12    and to Kronion of Heracles, through his daughter, one-artaba-holder, resident
13    of Kerkesephis, wheat two artabas one-half one-third one-twelfth, amounts to wheat artabas 2 1/2 1/3 1/12,
14    the same [land] of grape-land of Kerkes, wheat three artabas one-eighth, amounts to wheat artabas 3 1/8,
15    amounts to the total wheat artabas 21 1/2 1/3 and the measured-alongside amounts and additional
16    transport-tax of Kerkes and Kerkesephis. Eutyches, I have signed.
