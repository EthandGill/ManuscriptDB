[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 15th year, which is also the 12th, Hathyr 24, in Pathyris, before Hermias
2     the deputy of Paniskos the agoranomos.
3     Horos son of Paous, Persian of the epigone, acknowledges
4     that he has settled with Petearsemtheus and Petesouchos
5     and Psennesis and Phagonis, the four (sons) of
6     Panobchounis son of Tareesis, son of Patous, son of Paous, the mother of the aforewritten
7     father of Horos, (acknowledges) that he holds (it) and will not bring claim
8     concerning the debt of Egyptian and Greek contracts which Paous
9     the father of Horos paid off, and the same Horos, on
10    the account of Tareesis, his mother, to the
11    creditors Chairemon and Esthladas and
12    Pnephis, his wife and sister; and that Horos will not proceed, nor any other of those on his side,
13    against Petearsemtheus and his brothers,
14    nor against any other of those on their side. And if
15    he does, let the proceeding of the one who proceeds be invalid,
16    and let the one who proceeds pay in addition
17    forthwith as a penalty 5 talents of copper and to the
18    kings as sacred (fines) 100 drachmas of stamped silver, and none the less let it be obligatory for them to act
19    according to [the] things aforewritten.
20    Which (took place), their mother Threris
21    daughter of Paos giving her consent, (all parties) approving the things aforewritten.
22    I, Hermias, the deputy of Paniskos, have transacted (it).
23    Release of Petearsemtheus and the
24    brothers.
