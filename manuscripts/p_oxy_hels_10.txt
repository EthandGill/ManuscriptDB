[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the first roll, sheet 36.
2     To Sambas, strategos,
3     from Pausas son of Hephaistas.
4     These are those residing in the paternal house belonging to me
5     in the village of Sinaru of the lower
6     toparchy: Pausas son of Hephaistas,
7     of the mother Heracleia, aged about … years, a scar on the left jaw; Hephaistas his son, of the mother Soërous, aged … years;
8     Hermogenes his brother, of the same mother, aged … years;
9     Amois another, of the same mother, aged … years; Ar…
10    his brother, of the same mother, aged … years; Hermogenes
11    surnamed Aoros, one year old; …
12    of the women, Soërous daughter of Hermogenes,
13    of the mother Ploutarche, aged … years; …
14    I, Pausas son of Hephaistas, the aforewritten, swear
15    by Tiberius Caesar Augustus Imperator,
16    son of the deified Augustus, that in soundness
17    and in truth I have submitted the register
18    of those dwelling with me, and that neither
19    a stranger nor a Roman nor an Egyptian
20    nor an Alexandrian nor anyone else dwells
21    with me apart from the aforewritten;
22    if I swear truly may it be well with me, but if I swear falsely the opposite. The 20th year of Tiberius Caesar
23    Augustus, Phamenoth …
24    I, Pausas son of Hephaistas, have submitted the register
25    and have sworn the oath. Di…
26    … I wrote on his behalf because he
27    does not know letters. …
28    … of the lower toparchy, Sinaru.
