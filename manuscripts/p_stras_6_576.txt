[TRANSLATION]
1     To Hermeias his brother, Hierax, greeting.
2     I considered it necessary to write to
3     you concerning the seven arurae of Claudianus,
4     because … Horas (?) has been … Now therefore,
5     if Silvanus is with you, help
6     … to make (arrangements) for these. For in grain
7     … [j]ust as you too, and for this reason … in hay
8     … of other days it is possible … the sowing-of-fodder. For after
9     this … happen to us. Help therefore, near them. Send me
10    word about them for the depositing of fodder-supplies. Now the money that is with me I have received back. Only two talents remain.
11    I need therefore another ten talents. Do not
12    forget, having heard. For if God grant … , these are nothing,
13    silver … five of fodder-supplies being
14    sold … into which I spent
15    silver … so that by common decision
16    it may come about … you, that if … you wish
17    from the … we shall sell
18    the Nile-reeds (stubble) … the rent
19    Orion … of the estate we may spend at Aioun
20    and concerning the sale, that you are furnishing him a cloak in the market … for (I go?) to the weaver to purchase (it) for him. For I found a tunic, the price twelve talents. It presses us
21    to have (it) made rather than to buy (one) at
22    such a price. Do everything therefore. Send
23    me by someone the … since I did not find … For with difficulty I found silver to have made the equipment of the ploughs,
24    six minae of iron, each mina at 5,000 (?) drachmas, and I also bought the wooden parts for three talents. Do not be neglectful concerning the … I pray for your health, brother.
25    Deliver to Hermeias my brother, from Hierax.
