[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 25, Mecheir 11.
2     From Kottes Agathos Daemon, son of Asklepiades, from the village quarter of Apollonios of Hierakion.
3     From which I pledged as security through you exchanged building plots for
4     money principal and interest together [of] 4 talents by Sever[a] also [called]
5     Apollonaria of Suchos of Arabiōn, from the same village quarter, having died,
6     to the heir, son Diodoros of Isarion, who is also [called] Diodoros of Doriōn,
7     minor, through guardian Apollonios Agathos Daemon of Asklepiades, I wish to release
8     to Diodoros the building plots upon the aforementioned village quarter of Apollonios of Hierakion,
9     which are two-thirds [part] of a house and whole bare ground, wax-seal having come to be,
10    [having received from h]im as the money owed to me 300 drachmae being
11    from [the price] [of building plots sold] to Areia of Demetrios, civic, upon
12    of the [aforementioned] [village quarter] …
13    from Diod[oros of Isarion of] also [called] Diodoros of Doriōn, minor
14    … [of Apollō]nios Agathos Daemon of Asklepiades
