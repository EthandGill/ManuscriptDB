[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Antoni[us] ... [of the] bake[ries] of Memphis through Irenae[us] ...
2     To the Aurelii, village-headmen of the village Theadelphia,
3     Greetings.
4     I have received from you [the] wages
5     of the one taking for you [the] portion of the laborer
6     of the same bake[ries] of Memphis
7     for eight months, that is
8     from Pachon until Choiak of the [present] consulship, in full; and being questioned I acknowledged.
9     [of the] consulship of our lord[s]
10    Crispus and Constantine
11    of the most illustrious Caesars
12    the 3rd [indiction], Choiak 28. Aurelius
13    Antoni[us] through me Irenaeus
14    my brother, I have received the wages in full as [aforesaid].
15    Aurelius Ammonius, [son] of Seranus, wrote [this] on behalf of him [who is] illiterate.
