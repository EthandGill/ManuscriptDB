[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Julius Ausonius to Aetius and
2     Dioscorus, chief magistrates of the Oxyrhynchites, greetings.
3     without troubling those who have renounced a non-business life,
4     but also they have an order of my consecration; them,
5     the business-men and those suborned, having demanded all
6     of whom the names you sent out, restore the gold and
7     the silver. For it is just having benefited, he now
8     at least should receive what he previously spent. Farewell.
9     To Aetius and Dioscorus, chief magistrates of the Oxyrhynchit[es].
