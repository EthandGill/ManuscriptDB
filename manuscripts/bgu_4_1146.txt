[TRANSLATION]
1   To Achaios, the (official) in charge of the tribunal in the court,
2   from Sposos son of Lyppos, and from Arabion son of Didymos, and Dionysodoro[s]
3   son of Heraklides, and Apollonios son of Eirenaios, and Heraklides son of Apollonios,
4   and Sarapion son of Dorion, and Heraklides son of Hermoneikos, and A[… son of]
5   Soterichos, and Ammonios son of Hermias — the eight Persians of the ep[igone. Con-]
6   cerning the security set to be completed, those around Ara-
7   bion agree to repay to Sposos the price which they owe him
8   for what they have bought from him, … of [s]ilver, [Ptol]em[ai]c, one talent
9   and three thousand six [hundred and eighty] drachmas, inter[est-free, in payments]
10   of equal shares, [four], giving on Ha[thyr the fif]th of [the present twel-]
11   fth [y]ear the two thousand four hundred and twenty silver drach[m]as,
12   on Tybi the fifth likewise two thousand four hundred and twenty drachmas, on Me-
13   cheir likewise on the fifth two thousand four hundred and twenty, and the remaining
14   [of the w]hole principal on Phamenoth the fifth of the same twelfth year,
15   [two thousand] four hundred and twenty [drachm]as, making no payment short —
16   [doi]ng these things without lawsuit and any dispute;
17   and if not, they are liable to seizure, or the one of them who is to be present,
18   [and to be h]eld until they pay — at whatever payment they fall short — at on-
19   [ce] the aforesaid principal, the one talent of silver, three
20   [thousand] six hundred and eighty (drachmas), or what is owed, with half as much again,
21   [and for the] elapsed time the interest according to the diagramma, at two dr(achmas),
22   [the right of execu]tion belonging to Sposos, both from them themselves,
23   [being mutual suret]ies for the payment, and from one and from whichever of them he chooses,
24   [and from] all their possessions, just as by legal judgement, vo-
25   [id being (the pledges)] and whatever pledges of trust they may bring forward, (and) every protection.
26   Year 12 of Caesar, Phaophi 22.
