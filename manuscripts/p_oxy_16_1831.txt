[TRANSLATION]
1     From
2     to the superior of village Takonas. Some have reported to me that the shepherds of
3     your village had [a fight] against our village of Tholtheos
4     and [carrying off] of our sheep into your land, and I sent
5     [to enter] my field-guards, [and] they have given a report. Be willing
6     therefore to order our field-guards and the shepherds of your village
7     … not to create hostility toward us … everyone ought not … hostility
8     to raise. For indeed we ought not to raise disputes [between both groups], nor indeed do we have a matter, with the permission of our village-elders, in order to [order] [you]
9     our [officials] from small to [great] so that they not raise battle against themselves …
10    nor a helper of ours … our village-elder. Be [willing] … now these things
11    to settle so that his scheme may not remain unsown.
12    Deliver … to Papas, [superior] of village Takonas
13    from Abba [Nakios], superior of Tholtheos.
