# HGV-EN: To Theon alias -on, royal scribe of the Arsinoite (nome), division of Themistos, from Apollos son of E-, one of those from the village of Dionysias. The husband of my sister ..., Protas son of Heron grandson of Protas, mother ..., who, being over-age (and?) removed (?) from the poll-tax in the village of Philoteris of the same division, died in the month of Choiak of the present 2nd year of Imperator Caesar Traianus Hadrianus Augustus. Therefore I request that he may be listed in the list of the deceased.' To the village scribe: if it is really so, do what is appropriate. Year 2 of Hadrianus the lord, Choiak 15. ...
