[TRANSLATION]
1     … [To Theophanes], strategos or rather exactor of the Hermopolite,
2     [from] …nos and Horos and Anoubis, the three sons of their father Dios, from the village of Tertomepa [of the] same nome. In the present year, most excellent strategos, we leased from Adelphios,
3     [gymnasi]arch, councillor of the same city, two vineyard plots belonging to him together with five orchards near the village of Sarapieion, for one year only of the 4th indiction,
4     on condition that, in return for which we ourselves perform the manual vine-dressing work and the other service,
5     we receive from the produce that comes in a fourth part, in accordance with the lease that was made; therefore, trusting in the agreements made in writing, the
6     cutting of the [orchards] we have done, and we have done the reed-work of the vine
7     except for a quite small spot altogether. But we do not know how, with nothing having happened, this same Adelphios, having forgetfully held to the pledges, sent his own steward Kopreas
8     into the village to hold us back while we were in the reed-work, who also drove us out
9     from the care of the properties, whence, every pretext and dispute
10    being removed, we submit to your care these documents, being ourselves quite poor
11    and, so to speak, in need of our very sustenance, asking
12    you to order the wages to be paid to us for the labors which we have done up to that on which
13    we are completing … and the lease which we made, in order that we, having recovered
14    these, may be able to deposit them toward the things which we … in the …
