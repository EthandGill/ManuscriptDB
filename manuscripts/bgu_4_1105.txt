1   To Protarchos.
2   From Tryphaine daughter of Dioskourid[es].
3   [There agr]eed ...
4   [...] the parents ...
5   ... of Tryphaine, that they should give
6   me out (in marriage) to him, he taking charge (of me); and
7   of (the things) brought to him,
8   a completed agreement
9   through your court. She entered
10   into the marriage as a household (member?), and
11   (brought) on my account as dowry
12   clothing worth forty silver
13   drachmas and, in coined silver,
14   twenty drachmas. [But] the accused
15   Asklepiades, since
16   through our life together he behaved insolently,
17   having on no (provocation) misused
18   the aforesaid (dowry), (treats) me with ill-usage
19   and insults me and, laying his ha-
20   nds upon me, treats me as not e-
21   ven a bought slave (would be). [Therefore,] being dest-
22   itute, (I send) my father Diosku-
23   rides to submit this peti-
24   tion, and, for the departure (divorce),
25   to complete it, (I ask you) to send
26   to him one of the officers of the
27   court, who shall carry out for me the
28   departure (divorce) as is proper, and
29   shall deliver to Asklepiades
30   a copy of this peti-
31   tion, so that ... he may either
32   submit to judgment or pay me the
33   sixty silver drachmas; and also ...
34   ... to Di[os-]
35   kourides, for the ... of the
36   ... expense, which in part
37   (amount to) 66 silver drachmas; and with you (helping) us,
38   and for the damages and expen-
39   ses incurred,
40   (you) having dealt with him as a hater of wrong, so that I may
