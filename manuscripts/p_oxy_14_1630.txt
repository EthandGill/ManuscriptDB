1     [I leased from Ptol]emaeus also called Astoparison and Ammonius also called Heraclius
2     [from the corn-bearing land]s around the Oasis belonging to Claudia Isidora
3     [arouras] … [, which they hold in] lease of it, and having made the sowing for the present year of the
4     [stated lands] and having assisted the local farmers with both the seed
5     [and the expenses,] casting their eyes on my works, Hermogenes son of Petenephotes and
6     [Isidorus] … [, w]hom I, according to (what is) humane, for the service of the affairs
7     [benefited, suppl]ying them with their needs, offered, through the tender which they submitted,
8     [other annual dr]achmas two hundred — one of them, Isidorus, having used a fictitious
9     [name] … of descent; whose tender having accepted, and wishing neither to have
10    [nor to give trou]ble, I asked to receive back from them the advance which they had
11    [from me] and other expenses; an account having been rendered between us
12    [and] certain men of distinction by a confrontation made before the strategus
13    [of the nome,] amounting to three talents and 400 drachmas. But these men dealing unfairly with me and not even toward the
14    … and not having dealt unfairly with the advance which I had made,
15    [the tribunal] having met (them?), I offer against their tender other annual drachmas, 1,552,
16    … of the (sum) toward the taxes …
17    [to make] up an annual talent and [drachmas] 3,000, of the (sums) declared through the same tender
18    [as] fixed (rents) on … [and] at the time, observed by the (terms) of their same tender and by the stipulations
19    being kept …
20    [all.] And I, Aurelius Sarapodorus, being present, stand surety for the aforesaid Heron
21    [for] both the payment of the rents and the care of the works, and having been asked the formal question
22    [we] gave our consent. The acceptance is valid. The 5th year, Tybi 30.
