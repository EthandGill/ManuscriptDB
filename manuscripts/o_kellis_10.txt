[TRANSLATION]
1     1st year, Mechir 27. They wrote off for a two-drachma [amount] Tithoes of Psaites, Tithoeus
2     and Plutogenes, brother, for the 1st year and of the past 1st year
3     of silver drachmas 9, tetrobol [and] 3 copper. I wrote, Aurelius Honoratianus, who also
4     Hierax, having been in charge [as] decaprōtus, through Aurelius Sarapion
5     of Hierax, [having been] in charge [as] decaprōtus. Aurelius Androtimos
6     who also Kan[dia]nus, having been in charge [as] decaprōtus, have marked [my seal].
