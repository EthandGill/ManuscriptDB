[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship] of our lords Constantine, Augustus, the 5th year, and of Licinius the most illustrious...
2     Caesar the first time.
3     [Aurelia A]pollonia, daughter [of] Philon, through guardians, to my own son-in-law Valerius...
4     ... official of the office of the prefect of Herculia Egypt, greetings; since from childhood, father...
5     [and mother, being] deprived, in [orphan]hood having been left, certain ones of my defenseless age ... having inserted themselves into the affairs of our parents, some of these [having] embezzled, some also [having] carried off, so that from this I have no benefit...
6     [of nothing of the] things [left by our parents], in order that I [also] enjoy the paternal [affairs], confident [that] you, being my son-in-law, [will show] all faith and goodwill [towards] me, for this reason I commend you according to these my letters of recommendation [on behalf of] me, so that as if I were present, you may have right to approach the most illustrious our prefect...
7     [to Valerius] Ziper, and [to] set forth [to him] all the rights belonging to me, in order that I obtain...
8     [of my rights] and [to] recover from those holding the affairs of my parents [and to] have benefit [of them. Valid are the] letters [of the] recommendation written simply, and [being] questioned [I agreed.]
9     [of the aforesaid consulship] ...
10    [Aurelia Apollonia] [I issued] the recommendation on all the matters set forth, and [being questioned I agreed.]
