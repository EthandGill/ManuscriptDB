1     Patkous also called Sarapous, daughter of Dionysios son of Harpokration …
2     of the city of (Oxy)rynchus, with her guardian whom I have chosen ka…eotide…
3     of the … strategos of the nome by Apollonios, the incumbent exegetes, councillor of the same city, according to the appended request of Dionysios … son of …tion, his mother being Areia, from the same city, to Theon also called Ammonios, son of …, having been gymnasiarch, councillor of the same city, through Herakleides the scribe, greeting. I acknowledge, according to the report of Beitharion also called Demetrianos and of Ptolemaios
4     engaged in the contract of the money-changers' (bank) in the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     that I have leased to you for five years from the coming twentieth
6     year the six arouras belonging to me near Senepsau, from the (twelve) held in common
7     with my brother Proklos, of twelve arouras, so as to sow and
8     plant with whatever crops you choose, except woad and reed-fodder, at a fixed rent each year of two hundred silver drachmas, and the (rents) altogether for the whole five years, one thousand silver drachmas, that I have here received from you through
9     the same engaged (bankers), the bank in full, the public dues of the land each year being your responsibility, you Theon also called Ammonios. And if any …
10    becomes unwatered, allowance will be made to you in the following years for the Nile-flooded (land), and of necessity I will guarantee to you the lease-receipt for the five-year period
11    with every guarantee, or I will pay you double the money I received, the right of execution belonging to you
12    against both me and against all my property, you having the authority from
13    now, whenever you choose, through yourself to register through the property-record-office the right of this lease-receipt, which is valid. Year 19 of the Emperor
14    Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Britannicus
15    Maximus Pius Augustus, Pachon 8. And the copy of the request is (as follows).
