[TRANSLATION]
1     To Monimus Gemellus, royal scribe of the Heracleides division of Arsinoe,
2     from Ammonius Onnophrio[s]
3     priest of Soknobra[esae]us,
4     great [and] great god of the village of Bacchias.
5     I have filed with you a record
6     of priests and management
7     of the aforesaid temple
8     of the present 15th year.
9     [It] was filed with Gemellus, the royal scribe,
10    year 15, Mesore, [the] 5th [of the intercalary days].
