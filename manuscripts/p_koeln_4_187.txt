[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign] of Ptolemy and Cleopatra, the children of Ptolemy a[nd]
2     [Cleopat]ra, the Gods Manifest, in the thirty-fifth year, when the priest
3     … nios, son of Zoilos, was priest of Alexander and of the Saviour Gods and of the
4     [Brother] Gods and of the Benefactor Gods and of the Father-loving Gods and of the
5     [Manifest] Gods and of the God Eupator a[nd of the Mother-loving] Gods, when Cleopatra, daughter of Isokrates, was prize-bearer of Be[renik]e the Benefactress, and Arsinoe, daughter of Anaxandros, was basket-bearer of Arsinoe
6     [Phila]delphos, when Ergonoe, daughter of Anaxandros, was priestess of Arsinoe Ph[ilopator]
7     [Apoll]onia, daughter of Isokrates, in the month of Audnaios, Epeiph the twentieth,
8     [in Hera]kleopolis above Memphis, when Artemon was agoranomos.
9     [A]pollophanes, son of Exakon, an Althaian, a hipparch over men,
10    about fifteen years old, honey-coloured, with straight hair, long-faced, [the]
11    left eye damaged, with his guardian Archinos, son of Archinos, a Cyrenean, of those of Dionysios and the sons, catoec cavalrymen,
12    about thirty-seven years old, taller than middling, honey-coloured, straight-haired,
13    long-faced, with a scar on his right eyebrow and under his left eye,
14    bought from Ploutarchos, son of Euboulos, a Thracian, of those of Dionysios
15    [a]nd the sons, catoec cavalrymen, about thirty years old, of middle (height),
16    [r]eddish, straight-haired, long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on his right eyebrow at the tip, a Syrian slave, home-born, whose name is Karpos, about
17    [t]en years old, dark-skinned, round-faced, snub-nosed, gently … of bronze … [both the]
18    [se]ller and his brother … mon, son of Euboulos …
19    [of th]ose of Ariston and Kallikles … tes, about for[ty] years old, of middle (height), honey-coloured, straight-haired, long-faced, straight-nosed, [with ear-lobes]
20    [of the] ears attached. Offering for sale … the seller
21    [the] buyer having accepted.
