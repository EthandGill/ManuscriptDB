[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sa[turn]ilus and Apynchus, the lao[graphs] [of Tebtynis]
2     [from] Kroniaene of Herakl[aeus] ... mother Thea ...
3     ... from the village of Tebtynis [with] the authority of the brother Herakl[aeus].
4     There belongs to me in the village [a portion]
5     of a house and an inner court and an outer court in which I register
6     [into] the [register] of the completed ninth year
7     of Antoninus Caesar [the] lord
8     according to household registration; I am moreover:
9     Kroniaene, of thirty-eight years, unmarked
10    and my daughter Hero
11    of no-father [status], of thirteen years, unmarked. [Therefore] [I] submit.
12    [In] year 10 of Antoninus Caes[ar]
13    the lord, Epeiph 10.
