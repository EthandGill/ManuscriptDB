[TRANSLATION]
1     The eunuchs who will bring the Bible, go!
2     Outside to the place that will happen
3     Take the oath of witnesses [and]
4     The testimony and their witness and a herd of hundred of Papa
5     The ox and six [σίππιον/horses] but
6     They shall give these to you-all who will-do
7     But certainly they shall give these and what of-the need that will-be concerning Apollo does
8     That I might say
