[TRANSLATION]
1     Concerning the dispatch of sailors and craftsmen and the expenses of the naval expedition (kursos) of Egypt, 9th indiction. (Arabic: to the master of Ishquh concerning the sailors of the ferry and the craftsmen, that he send them.)
2     In the name of God, Korra ben [S]ze[rich, coun]se[ll]or,
3     to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito. We give thanks
4     to God, and after these (things): there reached you the orders(-documents)
5     of the assessment of the [s]ailors and craftsmen and of the expense
6     both of them and of that of the fighters of the naval expedition (kursos) of Egypt of the
7     [ninth] indiction …
8     not … to them … [receiving, then, the present letter,]
9     send off, with first priority, with all dispatch,
10    the expenses assessed through your administration, before
11    the water of the canal of Alexandria recedes and you are compelled
12    to pay the transport-charges of the same expenses as far as Alexandria itself;
13    moreover, also (send) the sailors (who are) strong and experienced. For we have charged
14    the present envoy not to give you any relief until you send off
15    in full whatever there is through your administration. Furthermore,
16    let there not arise an obstacle through you, and from the wheat and the bread
17    do not fall short of anything at all whatsoever, nor exact from the
18    localities any commutation-into-money for them at all, but only the goods themselves.
19    But of the remaining expenses, whatever kind of good there is among them
20    on hand with those of the localities, send (it) immediately; and whatever
21    one i[s] unable to give in kind, accomplishing the commutation-into-money
22    of it according to the assessed valuation set down in our
23    orders(-documents), send (it) off to us through a faithful man of yours,
24    one who must deliver (it) to us, not giving to the receiving
25    agents any such expenses, any kind of commutation-into-money;
26    and do not be negligent about quickly sending both the sailors and craftsmen
27    and the expenses, lest you give a pretext against yourself. Written in the month Mecheir 5 of the 8th indiction.
28    Korra ben Szerich, counse[ll]or, to [Basi]l[e]i[os, admini]strator of the [vill]a[ge of A]phr[od]ito.
29    [In the month] Mecheir 15 of the 8th indiction, brought through Saeid the courier, concerning the expense of the fighters and sailors of the naval expedition (kursos) of Egypt of the 9th indiction.
