[TRANSLATION]
1     To Poseidonios, archi[soma]tophylax [commander of the guard] and
2     to the strategos [general]
3     from Ptolemaios, son of Amadocos, a Thracian.
4     My mother Asklepias, living
5     with a certain Isidoros, one of those from Pitos, according to which [agreement] she laid down
6     to herself a written agreement of acknowledgment, through which
7     [they] mutually acknowledge among other things that [he is] to have
8     from her the dowry which she had brought
9     of bronze, two talents, and [they agree] about [his providing] to her
10    [a wedding gift] in a year; up to which time
11    [they are] to live together as husband and wife,
12    [with his wife] ruling jointly of the existing property,
13    but if he does not do as has been written,
14    he is to repay her the dowry at once
15    with the half-interest, and in the meantime
16    after Asklepias has passed away
17    and of those things of hers coming to me
18    and after these things both of Isidoros,
19    [there] came a certain Antibios and Isidoros,
20    and Eudemos, to the things left behind
21    [as] property, and making counter-claims they hold [them]
22    not having sent back the dowry. I therefore request,
23    if it seems [good], [you] to order, having summoned
24    the aforementioned [men] to be forced
25    to do justice for me both for these matters and
26    [for those matters] which I happen to have lent to him
27    on interest, copper drachmas [number] and interest [number],
28    for which [matters] he had also acknowledged [this debt] under Nikanor
29    the overseer of the village. Should this come to pass
30    I would be furnished with justice.
31    Farewell.
