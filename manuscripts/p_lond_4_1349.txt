[TRANSLATION]
1     [Being] by the grace of God and [showing themselves]
2     most excellent; and all recognized [that there ought to be accomplished]
3     through them the schedule of the public gold-taxes, [and that there should be nothing]
4     in arrears …
5     through which they were being harmed by certain persons …
6     Begin, then, in the name of God and of his help and of good,
7     the accomplishment of the public gold-taxes [with]
8     fairness and considerate guidance. For to everyone who strives
9     toward order and correction, as is fitting for him, God
10    also lends strength; and, as you gather the sum of gold, you are obliged
11    to show yourself competent before us. Send this in the treasury-bag,
12    striving toward this clearly and pressingly.
13    For, since the people of the district have just now completed the sowing, [so that]
14    they will more easily pay up the
15    (taxes) through them, and with God's help,
16    the time has come for the pay of the Magaritai of the Fossaton (army)
17    and of the raiding-parties, and their pay from the public funds of the seventh
18    indiction is being given. Henceforth do not neglect to send
19    the sum that is gathered from the people of your administration.
20    For the useful servant gathers, without scorn,
21    the due of the Commander of the Faithful with good governance
22    and good will, losing or cancelling nothing. We intend
23    therefore to learn, by God's command, how you conduct yourself in your
24    works, and let nothing whatever escape our notice. For we wish
25    to find you among those who have goodness and goodwill; and if
26    we find you in these (qualities) we shall benefit you, rewarding you
27    according to your works; but if (we find you) acting otherwise, we shall render you
28    likewise, bringing you down and showing you
29    more common than all. But nevertheless we hope in God
30    that you will be found among the true and genuine servants
31    who are eager to please us and who guard
32    the things entrusted to them. For you shall know that the first
33    and highest chapter of all the works of the servant
34    is the accomplishment of the public (taxes): to have it in mind
35    laboriously and ceaselessly until he completes such
36    accomplishment with the cooperation of God. Guard, through your
37    administration, against the fugitives taking refuge in it
38    and against the evil servants and rogues, neither doing nor
39    permitting any deed to be done among them that angers God
40    nor indeed us. Written in the month Tybi 19, of the 8th indiction.
