[TRANSLATION]
1     It was received, year 3, Pachon …
2     Polemon to Menchis, his brother, greetings. We have received the [things] from you written to us and
3     concerning which things you signified to send farmers
4     we are without need. But of Asclepiades
5     having commanded the affairs and having brought [them]
6     outside of the [underlying matters], other artabas of wheat [numeral]
7     apart from money we have resolved to hasten.
8     And concerning which things you signify about village scribes
9     scarcely will they be separated until [the date]. But you
10    will do rightly the message, not diminishing [it] compared to the first so that we may present [ourselves] well, and in the other [matters] having graciously arranged the matters of introduction. Taking care and
11    also of yourself [that you] remain in good health,
12    Farewell. Year 3, Pachon 19.
13    To Menchis.
