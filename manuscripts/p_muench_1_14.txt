[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the lord and master Jesus Christ our God and Saviour,
2     in the reign of our most divine and most pious master Flavius
3     Mauricius Tiberius the eternal Augustus Imperator, the twelfth year,
4     and in the consulship of his Serenity, the eleventh year,
5     Mecheir 21 of the 12th indiction, in Syene.
6     This agreement of settlement they make and execute,
7     being abolitive of every complaint and action and liability and
8     accusation and inquiry and dispute, on the one part
9     Flavius Ioannes son of Iakob, from his mother
10    Tapia, soldier of the regiment of Syene, through the one called Pserre
11    sailor of the same Syene, who also guarantees and undertakes for
12    the will of the said Ioannes in all the matters contained
13    and to be declared hereafter in this settlement; and on the other part
14    Patermouthis son of Menas, sailor from the same [Syene], acknowledging the following
15    as is set out below. Patermouthis the aforewritten brought suit
16    against Ioannes the aforesaid, that, contrary to all reason of justice, he made appeal
17    against him to Kallinikos the most valiant vicar of Hermonthis,
18    filling the place of Ammonianos the most magnificent
19    deputy, and on account of his accusation he was fined seven
20    solidi, and this although he himself held written settlements made
21    by the said Ioannes to himself, Patermouthis, and to Kako his wife,
22    sister also of the said Ioannes, through which he acknowledged that he was released
23    concerning the inheritance of his father, and that no longer should he have any claim
24    against the said Patermouthis nor his wife on account of any
25    matter pertaining to their late father. But Ioannes, pleading in defence against the suits brought against him by Patermouthis
26    the aforewritten, said that such proceeding was made
27    by him against Patermouthis as having hindered his mother from releasing him
28    in regard to the written decision given to him by Paeion the most learned
29    grammarian on account of the matter of her house. And, many
30    things having been said and counter-said between one another, it seemed good, by common
31    consent, that they should resort to arbitration before Sereu the most reverend
32    presbyter of the holy church of Omboi, found by chance in this
33    (city) of the Syenites. And indeed, coming before his Piety, each party
34    laid before him his own pleas, who, having heard the matters
35    disputed between them, and having gone through also the settlements produced
36    by Patermouthis, made to him and to Kako his wife
37    by Ioannes the aforesaid, and moreover having looked at the decision
38    given to Ioannes and his mother by the same most learned grammarian
39    on account of the matter of the house, through which he decided and resolved
40    that there should be given to Ioannes by his mother four solidi, and Ioannes himself
41    persisting and saying that, having been hindered by Patermouthis
42    the said from receiving from his mother the same four solidi,
43    he accused him, the aforesaid most God-loving
44    arbitrator resolved and decided that, before any (other) account, Ioannes the aforesaid should pay
45    to Patermouthis five gold solidi for the penalty which he said had been given
46    by him to the vicar of Hermonthis; and that Ioannes himself should have recourse against Tapia
47    for the mediation made to him and to his mother
48    herself; and that thus there should be made by both parties general settlements
49    toward each other. These things having been thus decided by Sereu the aforewritten
50    most God-loving presbyter, being by themselves they consented
51    to one another amicably, that there should be reckoned to Patermouthis on behalf of Ioannes
52    the four solidi, which it was determined he should receive from Tapia his mother,
53    mother-in-law of the same Patermouthis; and that he should receive from the same Ioannes
54    another one solidus to complete the five solidi for the
55    mediation of the same most God-loving presbyter. These things having thus
56    come about and (the parties) being reconciled to one another, accordingly they fittingly
57    came to this written
58    agreement of settlement, through which they acknowledge, by voluntary intent and without guile,
59    by sincere purpose and pure mind and unaltered judgment, apart from all guile
60    and force and deceit and compulsion and seizure and flattery and ill-will
61    and any defect and every base intent: Ioannes on the one hand,
62    through Pserre his aforewritten surety and accepter of his will,
63    (acknowledges) that he no longer has any claim against Patermouthis nor against Kako his
64    wife nor against Tapia the mother of himself and of Kako, nor against the heirs
65    of them or successors or assigns, neither now nor at any time in any
66    way or pretext or method or sophistry; nor moreover that he is able to
67    move arbitration against Patermouthis and Kako his wife and Tapia the aforesaid,
68    nor to stir up any inquiry of them at any time or season,
69    neither in local nor in foreign courts nor outside the courts, neither through himself
70    nor through an agent or substituted person, nor through divine rescripts, concerning any matter
71    small or great pertaining to the inheritance of his parents, because he has been released
72    by the judgment and mediation of the aforesaid arbitrator according to the manner before related.
73    And if he should attempt still to proceed against them in any way whatsoever at any
74    time or season, he shall gain nothing by the attempt, but shall incur
75    by way of penalty and breach twelve gold solidi, at the risk of Pserre the one
76    who guaranteed him through this written agreement, by deed and force
77    being demanded both from the surety himself and from all his property and
78    future property, generally and specifically. And Patermouthis himself also acknowledges that
79    he does not bring suit against the aforesaid Ioannes or his heirs on account of the loss
80    inflicted on him from the accusation of Ioannes the aforesaid, nor concerning any
81    matter, nor does he move arbitration against him in local courts and
82    foreign or outside the courts, neither through himself nor through his wife nor through
83    Tapia his aforewritten mother-in-law and mother of Ioannes, nor through the heirs
84    of them, nor through an agent or substituted person and divine notification,
85    because he has been released toward Ioannes the aforewritten by the judgment and mediation
86    of Sereu the aforesaid most God-loving arbitrator, and (because) there has been quenched between them
87    every seed of suit and action and liability and accusation and inquiry and
88    dispute. And if the same Patermouthis or any other in the person
89    of him should attempt to accuse or assail Ioannes the aforesaid, he shall be subject to the aforementioned
