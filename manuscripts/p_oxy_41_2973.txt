[TRANSLATION]
1     To Isidoros, priest and chief-justice (archidikastes) and in charge of the supervision of the chrematistai and of the
2     other courts,
3     [fr]om Athenarous daughter of Eunomos son of … from the village of Toka of the middle toparchy of the Oxyrhynchite nome, with her guardian her son Hermaios son of Petseiris, of those from the metropolis of the same Oxyrhynchite nome; and from Horion son of Orsenouphis son of … from the same metropolis. Horion agrees that he has leased from Athenarous for a period of
4     two years from the present seventh year of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajan
5     Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, two sowings and crops of the (land) belonging to the other
6     son of hers and uterine brother of Hermaios, Synistor son of Synistor, around
7     the aforementioned village of Toka, from the holding of Kallimachos together with that of Bakchios,
8     under one seal, one and three-quarters arouras of grain-land, the established rent each year being six artabas of wheat, and the total for the two years
9     twelve artabas of wheat, which Athenarous has received forthwith from Horion; and further
10    the same Athenarous has received from the same Horion through hand also, of the wood cut from the one and three-quarters aroura, namely castor-oil (rikinos) wood, the price, the agreed forty-eight drachmas of silver, the public dues
11    on the one and three-quarters aroura paid each year, all
12    both grain and money dues being borne by Horion, of which the receipts for the
13    two years Horion shall hand over to Athenarous in the month Kaisareios of the coming
14    eighth year of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus.
15    On these terms Horion himself is to hold to the lease for the period, providing for himself seeds and yokes and all the other things pertaining to the cultivation, performing all the proper works on the soil as is incumbent, and when the time
16    is completed to hand over the one and three-quarters aroura sown with grain and clear of all … reed-grass, and Athenarous (is to provide) it to him guaranteed and
17    inalienable, providing the lease for the period, or to pay to Horion
18    both the damages and expenses and other things, as a penalty one hundred drachmas of silver, just as
19    by legal action, Horion not being prejudiced concerning the things he asserts are owed to him
20    by the deceased Synistor, father of Synistor, by public deed, of two hundred sixty drachmas of silver, on all the conditions which it contains,
21    none the less the agreements being valid. We request (registration).
22    Dorion: it has been registered.
23    Year 7 of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus
24    Dacicus, the 27th of the month Sebastos, on the Sebaste day.
25    … year 7 …
