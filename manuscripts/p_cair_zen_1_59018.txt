[TRANSLATION]
1     [Alex]andros to Oruai, greetings. I received the letter from you,
2     [in which you] wrote [to me] both the [letter] from Zenon to Ieddon
3     [so that], if he does not give the money to Straton who came from Zenon, we display pledges of his to him. I then
4     happened to be [ill] from an illness, and I sent along [with] Straton
5     [from] us a young man and I wrote a letter to Ieddon. Having arrived
6     [they] said to me that no attention was paid to my letter, but to them
7     [they put] hands [on them] and threw [them] out of the village. I have written to you therefore.
8     Farewell. Year 27, [intercalary] Peritios 20.
9     To Oruai.
