[TRANSLATION]
1     [Amyntas to Zenon, greeting.] To Demetrios [from He]rakleia we lent little cups. And now,
2     since he is in prison, and his
3     belongings are being sold off, we have written to Apollonios, of which the copy is appended below for you too. You will do well, then,
4     to consider with Artemidoros the
5     physician whether it seems good to return to him
6     the letter or to let him lament. For that
7     the little silver vessels
8     listed below are ours,
9     of the cooler and the little cooler and one of the Antipatrid vessels there is a record even with the king
10    in the letter from Hikesios and in the
11    customs-house a registration, and of the rest
12    with us … is on hand.
13    But if it does not seem good to you to return it,
14    yet caring for Demetrios you will gratify
15    me; for now we learn that he is
16    being slighted.
17    Farewell.
18    To Apollonios. Demetrios, the one in the Prosopite nome appointed by you as checking-clerk, because he was traveling from home
19    not as one staying, and moreover was robbed
20    by Lysimachos the brigand of his travel-money and his slave-boy, as he was being detained
21    by you, asked us to advance him a little money, so that he might be furnished
22    with necessaries. Money, then, we did not have
23    to advance him, but little cups we lent
24    which you had deposited with Tryphera from outside, and a standing little cooler, holding a little more than a chous,
25    whose weight from the record with us …
26    and a cooler and two odd Antipatrid vessels and a cup
27    … You would do well
28    to act and have forgiveness for us;
29    for we would not
30    have expected
31    in so short a
32    time that he would come to ruin.
33    Farewell.
34    Amyntas concerning Demetrios.
35    [Year] 28, Peritios 22,
36    [in] Boubastos.
37    To Zenon.
