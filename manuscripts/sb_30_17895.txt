[TRANSLATION]
1     Philammon to Ptolemaeus greetings. [There] has fallen upon me Samoun from Dionysias
2     a beer maker, who shares with Heliodorus [the brewing] of the village, [that] you have held together and
3     [that] you have embezzled, demanding also [the things in] Heliodorus. I wondered therefore, [if] these things are true,
4     for which reason I also thought [it] fitting to inquire [of] you. You will do well [therefore] the one on the one hand
5     [the] man having remained already how … [the] things concerning the [brewing] not to fall through, but to what
6     [him] [you] have wronged, having made clear.
7     Farewell. Year 10, Pachon 16
8     To Ptolemaeus
