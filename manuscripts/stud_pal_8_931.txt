[TRANSLATION]
1     To Kyriakos, oil-seller: provide to Pesaou
2     a sailor, on account of the need of the great ships, according to the order of the god-fearing
3     Kyros, oil, xests 40 forty. It was written in the month Epeiph, 1,
4     indiction 5. Through me Pettarios, deacon and notary, it was completed.
