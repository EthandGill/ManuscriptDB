[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings, my lord Apion, Philosarapis
2     You I greet, praying you to be saved
3     With your whole household and to fare well.
4     That not only we remember you but
5     Also themselves our ancestral gods, this
6     Is clear to all; for all our generation in our hearts carries you, remembering your good intention. Concerning the things from homeland needed by you, my lord, write to me willingly having, for your commands most gladly
7     Having as gifts I shall receive. I greet the most worthy gymnesiarch Horion. I pray you to be strong,
8     My lord, kind and most noble
9     Apion, throughout life faring well
10    With whom gladly you live.
11    To Apion gymnesiarch having served as strategus of Antaioupolis
12    [From] Philosarapis in office
13    [Hierop]oiou of Antaioupolis.
