[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Gerontios also called Apphous, pertractator of the Heptanomia,
2     from the Aurelii Seuthes son of Heras and Isidoros son of Ptolemaios,
3     both from the village of Karanis, collectors of chaff for the 19th year
4     and the 7th year and the 5th year and the 3rd year of Karanis and of the boundary-survey district of Karanis.
5     Since you require the account of the receiving carried out by us
6     and of the handing over, from the produce of the 19th year and the 7th year [and] the 5th year and the 3rd year,
7     of necessity, having entered the matter item by item, we submit (it) to you that you may know,
8     and we swear the August oath of our masters
9     Constantine and Licinius, the unconquered emperors,
10    that the registered (statements) are true and that nothing has been falsified, or else
11    may we be liable to the divine oath. It is as follows:
12    of chaff there are gathered, of the village-property
13    of Karanis: of royal (land), with the additional assessment, arouras 714 1/2 1/4 1/32
14    of private (land) likewise, arouras 483 1/2 1/8 1/16 1/64
15    they come to: arouras 1198 1/4 1/8 1/16 1/32 1/64
16    of the boundary-survey district of Karanis: of royal (land) likewise, arouras 1250 1/4
17    of private (land) likewise, arouras 1735 1/4
18    they come to: arouras 2985 1/2
19    they come, together of the two parts: of royal (land), arouras 1965 1/32
20    of private (land) likewise, arouras 2218 1/2 1/4 1/8 1/16 1/64
21    they come to: arouras 4183 1/2 1/4 1/8 1/16 1/32 1/64; they come to: of litrai, myriads 10, 4600
22    of which we have handed over to the overseers of Akanthon
23    of chaff, litrai myriads 4, 5700
24    thus, item by item:
25    in the month of December, in the consulship of Maximinus for the 2nd time
26    to Gerontios son of Demetrios, receiver of Akanthon, litrai myriads 4, 5700
27    of which the copies of the receipts (are):
28    Aurelius Gerontios son of Demetrios, receiver of chaff,
29    to Seuthes and Isidoros, collectors of chaff of Karanis, greeting. I received from you, on behalf of the produce of
30    the 19th year, of chaff litras 4800.
31    In the consulship of Maximinus for the 2nd time, Choiak 7. Gerontios, I have signed.
32    And likewise, Choiak 7, through the same, of chaff litras 2850.
33    Gerontios, I have signed.
34    And likewise, through the collector on the same day, of chaff litras 4800.
35    Gerontios, I have signed.
36    And likewise, through the same collectors, Choiak 9, of chaff litras 5800.
37    Gerontios, I have signed.
38    And likewise, Choiak 9, through the same collectors, of chaff litras 1650.
39    Gerontios, I have signed.
40    And likewise, Choiak 11, through the same collectors, of chaff litras 8400.
41    Gerontios, I have signed.
42    And likewise, Choiak 13, through the same collectors, of chaff litras 2700.
43    Gerontios, I have signed.
44    And likewise, Choiak 14, through the same, litras 1650.
45    Gerontios, I have signed.
46    And likewise, Choiak 15, through the same, of chaff litras 4200.
47    Gerontios, I have signed.
48    And likewise, Choiak 22, through the same collectors,
49    38 baskets, litras 5700.
50    Gerontios, I have signed.
51    And likewise, Choiak 25, of chaff litras 3150.
52    Gerontios, I have signed.
53    They come, of the expenditure, the aforesaid, of chaff litrai myriads 4, 5700.
54    Remaining: of chaff litrai myriads 5, 8900
55    which were brought in in the time after these (transactions).
56    In the consulship of R[u]fius Volusianus and Petronius Annian[u]s
57    the most illustrious, Mesore 7.
58    Aurelius Seuthes and Eisidoros, we have submitted (it). Aurelius Dioskorion, I wrote on their behalf,
