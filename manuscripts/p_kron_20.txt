1     Copy of an agreement. In the tenth year of Antoninus Caesar the
2     lord, Hathyr …, [in Te]ptynis of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges
3     Tephorsais, daughter of K[r]o[nio]n son of Cheos, of those from Tebtynis, aged …, with a scar at the forearm …,
4     with as guardian her brother and also husband Har[p]aesis, aged 50, with a scar on the left thumb,
5     to Thaesis daughter of Papon[to]s, aged 40, with a scar on the left hand, with as guardian her son Heron son of …,
6     son of …, aged 40, unmarked, that she (Tephorsais) has received from her, Thaesis, on the spot, from
7     hand (to hand), the loan of a capital sum of silver of two hund[r]ed dr[a]chmas, apart from the other (sums) whic[h]
8     [Te]phorsais owes her according to the [agree]ment of deposit made in the
9     present 10th yea[r] of Antonin[us] the lord, Thoth 6, of three hundred drachmas of silver,
10    making in all five hundred dra[chmas]; and as they have agreed with one another, in place of interest Tephorsais grants
11    to Thaes[is] … if she chooses, to enjoy the produce [for]
12    her own (benefit), from the present tenth year, of two and a half arouras of catoecic allotment
13    and … arouras of the [as]sessed-share of the village from the part to the
14    north of the allotment of Horytynes, on the east, of the nine arouras belonging to Tepho[r]sais around Tebt[ynis], of catoecic allot[ment] together with assessed-share,
15    of 7½ arouras, the (total) being sixteen and a half [arouras together], in place of interest, from the
16    present [tenth yea]r, Ha[th]yr, of the afore[me]ntioned five hundred drachmas,
17    the public-taxes [o]n the two and a half arouras [aga]inst Thaesis
18    [b]eing [all] (the responsibility) of T[e]phorsais and those acting through her; (granting) to Thaesis
19    and those acti[ng] through her the produce on the aforesaid terms and from all
20    the pre[sent] year … … … of the produce. [She] will guarantee (it),
21    as is af[oresaid], [Tephor]sais to Thaesis; she shall exact the aforesaid
22    [five hund]red drachmas through the (terms) mutually agreed upon in these matters, and (she shall guarantee) the
23    watering-cha[nnel] of the aforesaid two and a half arouras … from the allotment to the east. Subscriber: …rio
24    son of Kronion, aged about [ … years] …
