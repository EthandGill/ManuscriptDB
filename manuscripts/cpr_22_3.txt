[TRANSLATION]
1     I have sent the present letter of mine to you going forth with God
2     toward you of the pagarchy, wishing to visit certain villages.
3     Therefore, may not again my departure from the city now mock you …
4     … from the public funds just as the past journey …
5     because I went toward the south into the village Isio Kato concerning the fugitives of the pagarchy,
6     and you neglected and did not complete public business except for the next requisition. But
7     from today divide the amount of the public levies and rows
8     until Mesore 1st so that the amount of each village [be] completed daily
9     until the same deadline, since you have no other single indication except this. Month of Epiphi …
10    … [to] Sabinus … Menas
