[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon greetings, Pais. I [up]sailed in the boat and we are troubled by
2     the one conducting the twelve-drachma [tax], and not yet we ... remember what you wrote. To [Artemidorus] and ... [brought] to Memphis wheat artabas 500.
3     from Tafis, and he gave me 8 drachmas, which I spent on the boat. And the sailors do not care to sail on the third share. Ask yourself then how they sail to the monopoly, and you will find sailing on a half-[share].
4     If therefore it seems good to you, write [to us] [to build] the boat; for the occasion is pressing
5     and the shipbuilders [are] unemployed. [There] will be work; for now
6     [the boat] being old, no one approaches; [and], if you wish, you will rent out
7     it. And I [undertake], on condition that [it] will be registered for the monopoly, I will assign
8     to you 800 drachmas. Write then to me ... [, so that] not being third I do not sit
9     in the boat. [Taking] nothing, we do not have the necessities.
10    Farewell.
11    Year 38, Phamenoth 20. To Zenon.
12    Pais the helmsman.
