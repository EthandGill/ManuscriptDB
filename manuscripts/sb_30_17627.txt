[TRANSLATION]
1     Procles to Asclepiadis the administrator
2     very many greetings
3     [Isidorus], who delivers to you this letter, is
4     my [from the] household, and he is from the village of Psophis
5     of the Memphis district; but by those from Tryphon the
6     strategus, having come into custody, he signed a document concerning the sowing around the village of Philadelphia from the Libya revenue of five and a half arouras.
7     I ask therefore you, dearest, having examined whether about
8     the Psophitic district a census is being made, to cooperate with him so that
9     [undisturbed] he will be regarding which the document
10    [having been given] to him and for the future to have the man established.
11    Farewell. Year 35 of Caesar, Mecheir 22.
12    I ask [you] [administrator] for my [register] to make
13    [everything] for Isidorus; for I am concerned about him.
