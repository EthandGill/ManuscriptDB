[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of villages, harvest of the 14th year of Antoninus
2     Caesar the lord, Epeiph 1
3     on behalf of Agoras, name Phatrêous, similarly Psenasoukhos
4     wheat, two artabas one-half, totals wheat artabas 2 1/2. Pete, scribe, I have noted.
5     Another, Pauni 24, on behalf of Agoras, name of the same, wheat of an artaba, one-half
6     totals wheat of an artaba 1/2. Pete, scribe, I have noted.
