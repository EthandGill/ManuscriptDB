[TRANSLATION]
1     … [saying to m]y face through his own
2     nose wishing [to] free me from life, and if [I] had not received help from Pamoun my fellow-villager
3     long ago [it] would have reached my soul. for this reason not bearing the petitions I submit to your care
4     these very things testifying and asking [that] they be brought before you and the matters following the laws against them be done, and [that] the pasture and dominion of my distinguished bare place be guarded for me. farewell.
5     after the consulship of our masters Gratian
6     the fifth and Theodosius the first of the eternal Augustuses, Pachon 10.
7     Aurelia Eirene submitted [this]. Aurelius Heraklides
8     of Apollonios wrote on behalf of her [being] illiterate.
