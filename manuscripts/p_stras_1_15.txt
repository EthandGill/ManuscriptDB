r.2    τῷ εὐλαβεστάτῳ Ἀνουβίωνι πρεσβυτέρῳ. ἐπληρώθην παρὰ τῆς σῆς εὐλαβείας ὑπὲρ ἐνοικίου ἐπαύλεως

r.6    μεθʼ ὑπογραφῆς ἐμῆς ὡς πρόκειται. ἐγράφη ἐν μηνὶ Ἐπεὶφ ιγ — ἐνάτης ἰνδικτίονος, θ ἰνδικτίονος.

[TRANSLATION]
1     from Theophilos sub-deacon and rent-collector.
2     To the most devout Anubion presbyter. I have been paid by your devoutness for the rent of the farmstead
3     of Symphonia the monastic [woman] for the eighth indiction, that is gold carats thirteen and a half, and of a reduction
4     by order of the same Symphonia the monastic [woman] gold carats four and a half, [thus] the whole [is]
5     gold carats eighteen, and for your security I have made for you this payment receipt
6     with my signature as aforesaid. It was written in the month Epeiph 13 — [indiction] ninth, 9 indiction.
7     Theophilos [son] of Isaak sub-deacon I agree with the receipt as aforesaid.
