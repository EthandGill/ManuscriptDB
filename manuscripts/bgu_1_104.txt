# HGV-EN: To the sitologoi of Karanis. I received under declaration (with a written sworn statement) from the seed grain of year 22 of Antoninus Caesar, the lord. (m. 2) 5th cleruchy . At Karanis for 10 1/8 1/16 1/32 arouras of royal (land), at Karanis for 2 1/16 1/32 1/64 arouras on the Senecan (estate) , on the Camelian (estate) for 3 3/4 1/8 1/64 arouras.
