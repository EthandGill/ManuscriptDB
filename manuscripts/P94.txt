[TRANSLATION]
FOLIO Folio 1 Recto — Romans 6:9-13
6:9    …death no longer has dominion over him.
6:10   For [in] that he died, he died to sin once for all; but [in] that he lives, he lives to God.
6:11   So also you: consider yourselves to be dead to sin but living to God in Christ Jesus.
6:12   Therefore do not let sin reign in your mortal body, so as to obey its desires;
6:13   nor present your members …

FOLIO Folio 1 Verso — Romans 6:18-22
6:19   …present your members [as] slaves to righteousness, for sanctification.
6:20   For when you were slaves of sin, you were free with respect to righteousness.
6:21   What fruit then did you have at that time, in the things of which you are now ashamed? For the end of those things is death.
6:22   But now, having been freed from sin and enslaved to God, you have your fruit for sanctification, and the end, eternal life.
