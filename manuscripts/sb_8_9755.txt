[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius [Appa] Kyros the most magnificent pagarch of the northern section of this
2     city of Heracles through you Cosmas the admired chartoularios of his, Apa Iouseph Aliarenios son
3     of Kaithe and Ambros son of Eta and Mousaphe [son] of Salheis and Saeis son of Amir Amphou
4     … [magaritai] … [we acknowledge] …
5     … nine beings to-you[r] … [to appoint]
6     … au … [mul]es [various ninety] six
7     [mul]es various ninety-six and [toward] your-own security we have made for you the present receipt
8     [h]aving called and Gabalan the emir of this city of Heracles his seal to place
9     [upon] it; written [in] the month [Me]cheir 22 of the present fifteenth indiction; moreover we have
10    the aforementioned m[agaritai] from the northern part of Heracleopolite other various mules three, comes-to mules 3, altogether all mules ninety-nine
11    ninety nine in [month Epip]hi 25 of-the fifteenth indiction
12    receipt of the magaritai receiving [them].
