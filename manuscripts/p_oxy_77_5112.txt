[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Onetus to Aurelius Phanius
2     to the most esteemed, greetings.
3     Immediately upon receiving your letter I went to Diogenes the merchant
4     of seeds and learned [that] he
5     is not residing at the present [time].
6     And at the same time as you departed he sent to
7     the house of Sillarien seeking you on account of the things which
8     you entrusted to him to buy seeds for you,
9     taking leave on account of the fact that up to now
10    he is on [his] bed. And having gone
11    to him I found him very ill. Therefore write to me what seems good to you, so that thus I may act here. But also the tax-collectors
12    troubled us greatly.
13    I wish [you] to be well, my lord.
14    … from Onetus
