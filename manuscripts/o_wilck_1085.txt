[TRANSLATION]
1     Mnesarchos and Herm[ophilos], who have farmed
2     the contract of the pot-makers and wool-gatherers
3     for the 33rd year, to Petearoeris and Aristodemos,
4     greeting. We have from you the accruing
5     tax of Choiak.
