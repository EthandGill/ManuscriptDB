[TRANSLATION]
1     … [of the] consulate of [the] Flavii Caesarius and Atticus of the …
2     most illustrious.
3     To Aurelius Callinicus, [son] of Hermes, councillor of Hermoupolis …
4     the most illustrious …
5     from Aurelius Taurinus, [son] of Pathermouthis, from the same city. I wish willingly and of my own choice to lease from you for whatever time you wish, from [the] produce …
6     of the fortunate [entering] twelfth indiction …
7     [lands] belonging to you around the district of White Tower …
8     [ar]ours, four [arouras], under cultivation, [which] will appear from measurement, … [belonging] to you from exchange of De[metrius, of his] brother, for sowing …
9     [which] [if] I choose, [at] fixed rent of wheat and vetches …
10    [of] twenty artabas, which rent I will measure to you in the month of Epiphi of each year, clean, new …
11    sifted, by Athenian measure, at the threshing floor of the same village, all public taxes [being owed] to you the landholder.
12    The lease is valid. And when questioned I agreed. Thoth 5.
13    I, Aurelius Taurinus, [son of] Pathermouthis, the aforementioned, have leased as stated. Aurelius Hermogenes, [son of] Besarion …
14    from Hermoupolis, wrote on [his] behalf, [he] not knowing [letters].
15    Through me, Hermogenes, [it] was written.
