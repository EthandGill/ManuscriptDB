[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Chairemon son of Ptolemaios, whose mother is Taesis, from the village of Karanis, to the daughters of my brother Kopres, the Aureliae
2     Taesion and Kyrillous, of their mother Sarapias,
3     minors, through their guardian, their maternal
4     uncle Aurelius Ammonios son of Isidoros, from the
5     same village, greetings.
6     Since I held and cultivated, for a thirteen-year
7     period after the death of your father Kopres,
8     the half share belonging to you of two grain-bearing
9     arouras about the village of Karanis, held in common with me as to
10    the remaining half share, being on the west of the olive-grove
11    of Harpalos, without rent, in return for the dues paid
12    on their behalf, both public taxes and annonae — these (arouras), since
13    you too wish to hold and cultivate for the
14    same equal period, henceforth I make to you the cession
15    of the cultivation, from the present
16    2nd year for the same equal thirteen years, of the half
17    share of the 2 arouras falling to me, without rent, just as I too cultivated (it),
18    you, however, paying the public taxes and
19    the annonae yearly; and, the period being completed, each of us is to control and
20    be master of the one aroura falling to him,
21    and to sell and to lease as he may choose. And, on being formally questioned, I acknowledged. In the 2nd year of our lord Domitianus Augustus,
22    Hathyr 24. Aurelius Chairemon son of Ptolemaios,
23    I have made the cession of the half share falling to me, as stated above.
