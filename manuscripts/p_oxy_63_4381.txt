[TRANSLATION]
1     It was read out from the record. From the Flavii Pelion, ducenarius, and Gunthus …, of the [unit]
2     of Moorish Scutarii stationed in Lykopolis under Paulus,
3     praepositus. Your authority is accustomed to help all, my lord duke, but especially
4     us soldiers who even after our terms of service
5     remain (here). Now our affair stands in this manner. Certain
6     violent and insolent men, heedless of the laws, while we were occupied with military service, have committed no ordinary violence
7     against our people
8     in the (territory) of the Oxyrhynchites. For though we possess neither estates nor plots of land
9     nor even camels, they contrived — we, as we said before, being absent — on account of the chrysargyron (gold-and-silver tax)
10    to extort from those belonging to us that which we have [never at any time]
11    been required to pay on account of this pretext. And we possess absolutely no place
12    in the aforesaid city where, in the manner of a camel-stable, for our own use
13    we have (one). Since therefore we are apprehensive
14    lest those belonging to us suffer the same thing again at the hands of those entrusted with
15    the exaction, we take refuge with your greatness, requesting and
16    beseeching that you may deign
17    to order, through the foresight of Krescention who is set over the peace, and
18    of Theodoulos, councillor of the same city, that those who administer the charge of the said
19    chrysargyron henceforth cease from the unreasonable exaction which they make against our people,
20    so that we may be able blamelessly to serve in our military service, in order that, obtaining this,
21    we may be able to render the greatest thanks to your illustrious fortune, my lord duke.
22    Order of my lord the duke.
