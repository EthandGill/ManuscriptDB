[TRANSLATION]
1     To the grain-recorders of the village of Karanidis, greetings.
2     I have received from you as a loan
3     of seeds of the present 23rd year
4     from the harvest of the past 22nd year
5     from the cleruch [land] of Ammon son of Harpokrates
6     of Karanidis [from] public [land], wheat, 3½ artabae.
7     Chairemon son of Pamounes wrote on behalf of [him] who cannot write.
8     Kastor, agoranomos
9     I have noted, artabae three
10    [and] half.
11    Year 23 of Hadrian, [the] 30th.
