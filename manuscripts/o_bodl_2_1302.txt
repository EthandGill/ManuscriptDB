[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the treasury of villages for the crop of the sixteenth year of Hadrian
2     of the lord Epeiph six of the name Pikotes
3     Fahirios of Pikotes for Agorae of wheat artabae two and one-half and one-third
4     and one-twenty-fourth, (total) of wheat artabae two and one-half and one-third and one-twenty-fourth. Ammonios has signed.
