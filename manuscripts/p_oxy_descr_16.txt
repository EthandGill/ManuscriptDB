[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master,
2     the greatest benefactor Flavius Tiberius Constantinus,
3     the eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the 8th year, in the consulship
4     of the same our most pious master, in the 4th year, Phaophi 11,
5     in the first indiction.
6     To the most exalted heirs of Apion, of glorious
7     memory, the late patrician, landowners
8     also here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas
9     the household servant who acts as agent and provides
10    for his own masters, the same all-praiseworthy
11    men, the prosecution and liability, Aurelius
12    Balthelomaios son of Isak, his mother being Tsaeie, originating
13    from the hamlet of Ktesis in the district of Paeimis, an estate
14    of your exaltedness in the Oxyrhynchite nome,
15    a registered cultivator of it, greeting. A need
16    having now arisen for the landholding waterwheel under my charge
17    called that of Akanthon, which draws water
18    for arable land, of one great workman, I went up
19    to the city and requested your exaltedness,
20    through the officials belonging to it, that they might order
21    the same great workman to be provided to me.
22    And immediately, your exaltedness taking forethought for the
23    [organization of her own affairs, your exaltedness]
24    autograph subscription of Balthelomaios. …
