[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklas and Soter, collectors of silver of the metropolis
2     To Eponychus of Herakleous to Apathes. we received
3     for tax of [illegible], of Charakos, 21 year, dirty drachmas four
4     it becomes dirty drachmas 4. Year 21 of Hadrian the lord, Tybi 3.
5     other [entry], Payni 4, of the name of the same, similarly for tax of [illegible], dirty drachmas
6     four triobol, it becomes drachmas 4 triobol. Drachmas 8 triobol
7     Soter has been marked, drachmas 8 triobol.
