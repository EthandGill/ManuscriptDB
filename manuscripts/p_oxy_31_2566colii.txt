[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Ai…los, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite …
2     from Aurelia Apollonarion, daughter of Zoilos,
3     who served as gymnasiarch of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, acting without a guardian by
4     the right of children according to Roman custom, with as her assistant Aurelius Diogenes son of Sarapammon, former exegetes, councillor
5     of the same city, through Aurelius Horion, scribe of Apollonarion.
6     In accordance with the letter of Papirius Sabinus, the most excellent,
7     delivered to you here today, having learned that the property of Claudia Isidora also called Apia, with the treasury,
8     has been (registered), we declare ourselves to owe
9     to the account of Isidora, according to a deed of hand, in the current
10    year, Phamenoth 12, of wheat artabas 300 by the gauge-measure
11    of the estate, which I received on condition of repaying, from the crops of the same
12    current year, in the month Epeiph, the of the capital
13    three hundred artabas, and on account of interest at a fourth, artabas
14    seventy-five. And we request that this our
15    declaration be made known to whom it concerns. Year 4, Pauni 19.
16    Aurelia Apollonarion, daughter of Zoilos, and Aurelius Diogenes son of Sarapammon,
17    through me Aurelius Horion, scribe of the same Apollonarion,
18    have submitted (this).
