[TRANSLATION]
1     Sabinus I registered in the 3rd year of Trajan Caesar
2     Phaophi 19.
3     To Socrates, former gymnasiarch, and Antipater, keeper of records
4     of property holdings of the Arsinoite.
5     From Heron son of Heron son of Heron
6     of those from the village of Narmouthis of the
7     Polemon division. Except that which
8     I previously registered through you I additionally register at the present time, and what came to me
9     after [the death of] my previously registered
10    father Heron son of Heron son of
11    Orseutes from the same village registered through you [intestate,]
12    [upon his] death, [I register] a seventh part
13    of a house and courtyard in the aforementioned village of Narmouthis, free from debt [and] mortgage
14    [and] every lien. Therefore I submit this additional registration.
