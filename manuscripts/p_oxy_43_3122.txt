[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our lords Licinius Augustus for the 6th time and Licinius
2     the most illustrious Caesar for the 2nd time, in the year of the consuls to be designated for the 2nd time.
3     To Valens, of the protectores, and …
4     The Aurelii Panotbeus son of Orsenouphis, his mother T[a]as, and Aphynchis son of Aphynchios, his mother Thaesis,
5     both from the village of Posompous of the 5th pagus of the Oxyrhynchite nome, and Kopreus son of Diosk[o]ros, his mother Tengogis, residing in the same village Posomp[o]us, greeting.
6     We acknowledge, swearing the august divine oath of our lords
7     the invincible kings, that we will perform the cultivation: I Panotbeus
8     of seven and a half arouras which fall to our wife Thermouthis and to
9     her sister Thaisous, and we, the party of Aphynchis and Kopreus, for our part likewise to hold to the cultivation of another seven and a half arouras,
10    these together being fifteen arouras previously cultivated by Cornelius
11    the father of those concerning Thermoution and Thai[so]us, on the rent paid in advance,
12    without fail, so that we be blamed in [nothi]ng, or else we shall be liable
13    [] … and Kopreus
14    … the previously-cultivat[ed] arouras
15    … [hav]ing been questioned we ack[nowledged. Aurelius ] … [ I wrote on behalf of th]em who do not know letters.
