[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Paniskos also called Longus, [strategos] of the Hermopolite,
2     to the keepers of the property-registry of the same nome … greeting.
3     Of the nomination given to me by the comarchs [of the village] of Timonthis,
4     [presenting] those nominated through it for the three-donkey transport-service,
5     a copy is sent to you, so that you may know and may take precaution for the security of the most sacred treasury.
6     I pray for your health.
7     Year 12 of our lord [Gallienus Augustus], Mesore …
8     To Flavius Paniskos also called Longus, strategos of the Hermopolite,
9     from Aurelii Kollouthos son of Kollouthos, whose mother is Teeus, and Paesis son of Silvanus, whose mother is
10    Helene, both comarchs of the village of Timonthis. For the three-donkey transport-service,
11    for one year from the present month Epeiph of the current 12th year of our
12    lord Gallienus Augustus, we give the undersigned, who are well-off
13    and suitable, at the risk of ourselves and of those from the village and of all the residents, on mutual surety. And they are: Aurelii
14    Hatres son of Psois, whose mother is Teeus, about 45 years old, and Leon son of Inarous, whose mother is Helene, about 30 years old,
15    the two for one place (share), having means of 2,000 drachmas.
16    Pekysis son of Hermaios, whose mother is Tereus, about 45 years old, and Lytis … [whose mother is] …itis
17    about … years old, the two for one place, having means [of] 2,000 [drachmas].
18    Panesneus son of Emouis, [whose mother is] …e…itis, about 30 years old, Kastor son of Paesis, whose mother is Teeus,
19    about 30 years old, the two for one place, having means of 2,000 drachmas.
20    Pkylis son of Koulos, whose mother is …os, about 38 years old, and Peues son of Petbeous, whose mother is Kolleuchis,
21    about 50 years old, the two for one place, having means of 2,000 drachmas.
22    Pimouis son of Hermaios, whose mother is …, about 50 years old, and Panesneus son of Kollouthos, whose mother is Helene, about … years old, the two for one place, having means of 2,000 drachmas.
23    Hermes son of Silvanus, whose mother is … , and Leonides
24    son of Epeis, whose mother is … , [about] 45 years old, the two for one place
25    having means of 2,000 drachmas.
26    Farewell.
27    Year 12 of Imperator Caesar Publius Licinius Gallienus
28    Germanicus [Maximus Pius] Felix Augustus, Mesore …
