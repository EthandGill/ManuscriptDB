[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy from a copy of sheets 69, 70, from
2     the public library, of the 16th year of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
3     of a house-by-house census taken in the Prosopite nome, vol. 2, of Patermouthis son of Anikos … farmer, brother … in which:
4     Pathermouthis [son of An]ikos son of Pathermouthis,
5     his mother being Thaseis, a quarry-worker, 47 years old.
6     Thaneutis daughter of Tithoenathymis son of Herpaesis,
7     his wife. Thaesis the daughter.
8     Anikos the brother of the same parents.
9     Thenthnoupis son of Anikos son of Pathermouthis,
10    his mother being Thaseis, Herpaesis, 45 years old.
11    Demetrous daughter of Soterichos, his wife, mother being Thamistis.
12    Thamistis the daughter, … years old.
13    Anikos the brother of the same parents, a minor, 6 years old.
14    Herpaesis son of Anikos son of Pathermouthis,
15    … his mother being Thaseis, Herpaesis, 42 years old.
16    …esis daughter of Horos, his wife, mother being Tertia daughter of Capito, 29 years old.
17    [An]ikos a son of both parents, 20 years old.
18    Thaseis the sister of the same parents, 8 years old.
19    Tertia another sister, 4 years old.
20    Pantbeus son of Anikos son of Pathermouthis, his mother being
21    Thaseis, Herpaesis, 38 years old.
22    Thaesis the wife, her mother being Thaubastis daughter of Piesieus, 21 years old.
23    [An]ikos a son of both parents, a minor, 4 years old.
24    Likewise of the 9th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord, from the same library,
25    of sheet 5 … vol., Chentmouphis son of Anikos son of Pathermouthis,
26    his mother being Thaseis, Herpaesis, a quarry-worker, 52 years old. Anikos the son,
27    his mother being Demetrous daughter of Soterichos, 20 years old. Thamistis the sister
28    of the same parents, 24 years old. Anikos son of Chenthnouphis to Tamystha my sister by the same mother, of unknown father, greeting. I have handed over to you the aforesaid copies of the census-returns, of which I will show the equivalents in the registry, whenever there is need for proof that I am
29    your brother by the same mother. Year 24 of Antoninus the lord,
30    [Ph]amenoth 28.
31    Anikos son of Kenthnoupis.
