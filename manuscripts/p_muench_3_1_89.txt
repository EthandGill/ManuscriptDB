[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters Constantius
2     Augustus for the 2nd time and Constans
3     Augustus for the 1st time,
4     To Aurelii Ammonios and Demetria, children of Aniketos,
5     from the most illustrious city of Hermopolis,
6     from Aurelii Paulos son of Elias and Polis
7     his brother, from the same city. We wish
8     willingly and of our own free choice to lease
9     from you, for one year only, of the crops
10    of the fortunate 14th indiction, the (arouras) belonging to you around Thynis from the allotment that is through them,
11    of the allotment, five arouras for the sowing of flax-stalk
12    and lotus-fodder, at the half, of rent
13    of the flax-stalk, for each aroura
14    one and two-thirds hundredweight of undressed tow,
15    and of that in fodder we shall furnish you the half of all the fodder produced, in place of
16    the rent, which tow we shall pay to you
17    in the month Phaophi of the same year, without delay, in to[w] suitable and satisfactory,
18    by the public weight, the (land) in seed being according to land-survey as shall appear, the public
19    taxes and all the other charges
20    being upon you the landholders. The lease is valid, and being formally questioned we agreed.
21    [In the consulship af]ore[sai]d, Thoth 30. Aurelius
22    … [ has le]ased(?) … Aureli(us)
