[TRANSLATION]
1     Ischyra to Bellicus
2     to my lord, very many greetings;
3     before all things I pray
4     (that) you are healthy; I beseech
5     you, lord, to care for the
6     your ward Zosime,
7     and my lady; you know that
8     I commanded you to send whetstones; not yet
9     they have been made
10    the stone-workers;
11    if they make (them)
12    immediately to you
13    I will send.
14    Farewell.
