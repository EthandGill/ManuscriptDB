[TRANSLATION]
1     [I] have for the price of wine [from] Theodotus one talent. Eukar[pos] has one talent [ ],
2     I have from Zo... two talents,
3     I have from Artemon and Seôs
4     two talents, Pamneius's son has them for
5     the pitch.
6     [He] has Krino from Thaêsios drachmas Γωξ and says [he] gave to Kolloutes of [pitch] drachmas Βρν, amounts to one talent.
7     [He] has from [...epta...]uchios one talent,
8     [He] has from Herakleides drachmas Γυ and a garment,
9     I have drachmas Βσ,
10    and from Pyros [he] has two portions one talent Γρο,
11    of pitch drachmas 30, to Harontôtes drachmas 20, to Krino drachmas 100, for castor-oil
12    drachmas 100, to Axeites for grape-harvest drachmas Β, amounts to two talents,
13    I have from Peto[siri]us Remxinaüius
14    drachmas Α.
