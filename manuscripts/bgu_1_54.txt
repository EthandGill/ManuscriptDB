[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Harphaesis, village scribe of Karanis from Ammonios, son of Harpagathes, the son of Heras, mother Segath[is], from the village of Karanis. I register myself and my (family) for the house-by-house registration of the past 23rd year of the God Antoninus, for Thaisarion’s house and courtyard in the village, which I live in. I am A[mmon]ios the above mentioned, 36 years old without a distinguishing mark and (I register) my wife Thaisarion, daughter of Anton[i]u[s], 29 years old without a distinguishing mark and Seutharion daughter of both of us, 9 years old without a distinguishing mark. There belongs to my wife Thaisarion, the aforementioned, a house and courtyard in the village. Therefore I submit (this registration). Year 1 of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, and Verus Caesar, the lord, Epeiph 13.
