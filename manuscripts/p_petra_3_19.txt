[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most reverend, for the time being, receivers of gold of this
2     metropolis and of the districts of Augustopolis belonging to it through the
3     group. We, Flavii Leontios son of Oualens, town-councillor, and Theodoros
4     son of Obodianos, communicate the following:
5     A vineyard property belongs to me, Theodoros, from my paternal inheritance,
6     situated in Baith el Alkeb, at the
7     uninhabited hamlet called Mal Ouda, registered in the public
8     codex of the city of the Augustopolitans
9     through the most blessed Horion son of Leontios, who became grandfather on the father's side
10    of me, Leontios, on behalf of Magdios son of Euzoïos,
11    who had been his house-slave. The tax-payments, however, of this property,
12    amounting for a free iugerum
13    to the half, third, eighth, sixtieth, were paid both by me, Theodoros, and by the most blessed
14    Obodianos son of Obodianos,
15    my father, both to the most blessed Oualens, father of me, Leontios, and
16    to me, Leontios, having held the right
17    of the tax-payment on behalf of the said property up to the
18    past second indiction, concerning which I,
19    Leontios, have no claim against Theodoros the most estimable, because so much
20    has been paid as has been said.
21    Both of us, then, at the present having agreed together that it is well that I,
22    Theodoros,
23    should have the tax-payments of the said vineyard property … through the
24    public documents, I communicate
25    to you, the most reverend Basileios, and to the most reverend receivers for the time being,
26    to charge my account
27    and person severally out of the group of Augustopolis … on behalf of
28    a vineyard iugerum, the half, third, eighth,
29    [sixtieth] …
