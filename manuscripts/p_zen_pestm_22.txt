1     Ammonius to Zenon greetings. We have sent to Apollonius
2     the garlands of the [pomegranates] concerning which he wrote to us
3     to prepare so for the king. You would do well then, having taken care,
4     that with speed they be received from [the] t … hn
5     [the estate] of Apollonius that it is distant. And the red-dye[d...] know [that] having been purchased together as much as he said he needed of the from-you talents three from [drachmas] twenty-five the talent, it was being sold
6     and at drachmas thirty.
7     In order then that you know, I have written to you. And Charmos the boy indeed I will send to you
8     with speed, having dealt with all matters. You would do me a favor, having taken thought how
9     the remaining letters the from us [are] delivered to Apollon[ios] opportunely and that
10    he write back to us concerning each matter, having taken thought.
11    Farewell. Year twenty-eight, Tybi seven.
12    To Zenon.
13    Of Ammonius. [concerning] garlands.
14    Year twenty-eight, Audnaios twenty-eight,
15    in the harbor of Berenice.
