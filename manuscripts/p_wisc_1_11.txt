[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name] of [the Lord and Master]
2     [Je]sus Christ [our] God and
3     our Saviour, Epeiph 8, of the indiction
4     the fourth, at the beginning, God willing, of the fifth.
5     I, Makarios, son of the late
6     Herminus, from the estate of
7     Great Tarouthinos of the Oxyrhynchite
8     nome, subscribing below in my own
9     hand, to you Severos, carpenter, son
10    of Sergios, from this city of the Oxyrhynchites,
11    greeting. I acknowledge
12    that I have received from you, here and now,
13    already, for my pressing need,
14    one gold solidus
15    at twenty-two and a half keratia,
16    makes 1 gold solidus at 22½ keratia,
17    and in return for it I acknowledge
18    that I will provide you with wine, in four-xestes
19    jars, fifty-
20    five, at the vintage of the
21    month Mesore of the present
22    fourth indiction, of the wine-flow
23    of the fifth, God-willing, tax-cycle,
24    being bound as to the good quality
25    of the wine until the whole of the month Tybi.
26    I have sworn by God
27    the Almighty and the safety
28    of those who rule over us. Valid is the note,
29    written singly, and having been formally questioned, I gave my assent.
30    I, Makarios, son of the late Herminus, (it) agrees with me as is aforesaid.
31    From Makarios, son of the late Herminus, from the estate of Great
32    Tarouthinos of the Oxyrhynchite nome, one gold solidus at
33    22½ keratia.
