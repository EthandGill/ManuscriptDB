[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakles and Ptollas,
2     both sons of Ptolemaios,
3     of those from the city of Oxyrhynchus,
4     Persians of the epigone, to Diogas son of Herakles, greeting. We have from you, by hand from the house, of silver imperial coinage drachmas twelve as capital; and in place of the
5     interest on these we have leased the use
6     of a weaver's loom that we have,
7     of three cubits less two palms, with two cross-beams, two loom-feet, one weft-beam, for
8     six months from the present day, after which we shall also repay to you the
9     twelve drachmas of silver
10    without postponement. And if
11    we do not repay, as written, we shall pay you
12    these with the half-as-much-again and
13    interest. And upon the repayment of the silver you shall hand over
14    to us the loom in sound condition
15    except for wear, we being mutually sureties for the payment,
16    the right of execution belonging to you
17    against both of us and against whichever of us you choose and against all our property.
18    The deed of hand is valid wherever
19    produced. Year 2
20    of the Emperor Caesar
21    Domitian Augustus,
22    in the month Neos Sebastos 19.
23    Herakles son of Ptolemaios,
