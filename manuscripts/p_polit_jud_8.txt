[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 37, Mecheir 20 … we have arranged to write
2     to the Jews in Temei …,
3     and if not, to send within days …
4     To the magistrates for the year 37 of the association in Herakleopolis
5     of the Jews (politeuma) at Herakleopolis
6     from Theodotos son of Theodotos,
7     a Jew of those in the Oxyrhynchite,
8     … in the …
9     in the village Tehis of the same nome.
10    In the year 33, when I lent
11    to Plousia daughter of Apollodoros, a Jewess,
12    known also as Gargarissa, and
13    to her son Dorotheos also called
14    Zenon, a Jew, known also as
15    a Persian of the epigone, twelve talents of copper,
16    at interest of two drachmas, on the security
17    of a vineyard of 1½ aruras near Palosis
18    in the Oxyrhynchite, which I had made over to Philista my wife, I renewed (the loan),
19    having received no wine in the year 34,
20    but in the year 35, in Mesore, when I had made the additional payment, I gained possession
21    of the property, and in Epeiph, when the Jews took it over …,
22    among whom also … magistrates, and from them,
23    when I appealed, they settled, in … on condition that
24    they should pay these same … in two years,
25    of which in Mesore of the year 36, six talents, and
26    in Mesore of the year 37, six talents, and the interest,
27    and they bring at once … from the produce
28    of wine …, when they had paid to me
29    three measures of wine and 1 talent 4,700 (drachmas) of copper, but had not paid back the rest, I request, if it seem good, to order
30    to write to the Jews in Tnehis to compel
31    them to do me justice or to send
32    them before you with attendants, that I may obtain
33    justice. Farewell.
34    Year 37, Mecheir 22. Theodotos
35    against Plousia and Dorotheos.
