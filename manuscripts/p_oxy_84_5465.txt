1     Total of receipts of the banker of the 9th indiction,
2     with the price of wheat, as follows:
3     Of Alexandria, solidi: 2 myriads, 2996, 1 1/4 keration

5     so that remaining, solidi: 2 myriads, 1995, 20 1/2 keratia

11    [so that remaining], solidi: 1 myriad, 3410, 15 1/2 keratia
12    … 9 … 9 for revenues and to the magistrate, of solidi: 1 myriad, 3454
13    … 43 solidi, 8 1/2 keratia
14    … lightened from the account of the 9th indiction as follows:
15    …ou, 6 solidi, 8 1/4 keratia
16    [for] expenditure of the younger ones, 18 1/2 keratia
17    [and] for the Brumalia, 3 solidi, 1 keration, total 10 solidi, [3 1/2 1/4] keratia, of which
18    [for] the grammarians, 3 solidi, 19 keratia, to the freed(man), 6 keratia,
19    to Kollouthos the pedagogue, 17 1/2 keratia, for redemption, 3 solidi, 1 keration,
