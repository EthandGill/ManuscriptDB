[TRANSLATION]
1     Hierokles to Zenon, greetings. Apollodoros, who is delivering to you the letter[s, is one] of our
2     friends. He is sailing therefore to Herakleopolis. We have sent word to him to bring [to us]
3     garments and tunics. You therefore would do well having introduced him … [to …] and to Lysimachos
4     so that they might write [him] a letter to the guard-post above so that they might treat him [with] good will. And … to Simachos the tax-collector introduce [him]. I have written moreover
5     also to the strategos in Herakleopolis concerning him so that [all necessary things]
6     may come to pass for him.
7     Hierokles concerning Apollodoros to Nikanor.
8     Year 29, Xandikos 3, in Memphis.
