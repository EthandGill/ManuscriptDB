[TRANSLATION]
1     … [an]d our lord(?)
2     … [the public administration (demosios logos)], that is the all-praiseworthy lord Abdella [son of Abdelmelek, through y]ou the most glorious
3     lord, the lord Epimachos, the illustrious and pagarch [of this] one and only [district], all of them, and his hamlets
4     and his plains, greeting. Since the … today, which is this:
5     the twenty-seventh of the month Pash[ons] … the seventh indiction; behold, the persons
6     whom they (i.e. you) caused to be presented in the district of Psoi through Abdella son of Shourae
7     the Saracen and the overseer (epikeimenos) of [that pl]ace, came to us, those persons
8     [with] their women and their [children], according as they are shown below.
9     This guarantee-agreement (engyetike homologia): we shall guard them, we shall give them rest
10    according as you commanded us, at all times, your lordship
11    seeking after their persons from us; we shall be (in) readiness; we shall hand
12    them over to you, without account, without dispute, without delay; but if not,
13    we will pay (and bear) every fine which your lordship shall determine upon [us].
14    Therefore, to confirm it, your lordship, we have made this guarantee-agreement for you,
15    binding ourselves with all our substance until we keep watch and guard (them),
16    according to the force of this guarantee-agreement. We swear by the name of God the Almighty (Pantokrator)
17    and the welfare of those who rule over us, that we will keep watch, according as we wrote first
18    when we were asked, we acknowledged (it).
19    A list, thus:
20    Apollo son of Andreas, and Stephanous his wife, and Andreas his son, and Erebekka his daughter — total 4 names
21    Apollo son of Hermaos, and Mariam his wife, and Symeon his son — total 3 names
22    Philotheos son of Onnophrios, and Theodora his wife, and Tekysis his daughter, and Kollouthos his son, and Antherias his daughter — total 5 names
23    Menas son of Hieremias, and Rachel his wife, and Stephanous and Marthas his daughters — total 4 names
24    Tekrombias, and Antonios his son, Staurophane his daughter — total 3 names
25    Elisabeth wife of Apa Kyrios, and Philotheos her son, and Maria her daughter — total 3 names
26    I, Apollo son of the blessed Psoi, and Theodosios son of the blessed Senoute, we agree
27    to this guarantee-agreement according as it is written. I, Apollo, wrote on behalf of Theodosios, because he does not understand (letters)
28    and on my own behalf. I, Ezekias son of the blessed Chamoul, agree to this agreement
29    according to its force. I, Apa Kyros son of the blessed Andreas, bear witness to this agreement
30    according to its force. I, Athanase son of the blessed Makare, bear witness to this agreement, according as I heard from the one who drew it up. I, David son of Georgios, bear witness to this agreement
31    according to its force. I, Enoch son of the blessed Vik[tor, bear witness to this agreement]
32    according to its force. I, Phoibammon son of the [blessed] …
33    Through me, Theodoros, with God notary, it was wri[tten].
