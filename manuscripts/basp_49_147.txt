# HGV-EN: Psimanobet son of Kyrios has given through his heirs into the account of the annona for the kanon of the eleventh indiction three (and) a half (and) a third (and) a twelfth artabas of grain, equals 3 1/2, 1/3, 1/12 art. of gr(ain) only for the kanon.
