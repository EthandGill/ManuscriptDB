10    [total] 5 [ounces of gold]; and I agree, at my own risk and from the resource of [my] property, generally and particularly, the aforesaid [aroura]
11    [to guard] and to restore to you free (?) and unimperilled [and without] account … or wherever you wish, in the same village [of Aphrodite],
12    [and until] I do this, you have authority, both you and the aforementioned (man),
13    … to take control of all my property …
14    [under every] magistracy and authority, until the aforesaid [price]
15    I repay to you [twofold]; and moreover, that you, the said Phoibammon, hold the possession and ownership, or …,
16    [un-outraged] and unmolested by anyone, this property of mine of every kind being liable, concerning which I have made this security [with]
