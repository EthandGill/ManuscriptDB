[TRANSLATION]
1   Year 48, Mecheir 15. There has been paid into the
2   bank at Hermonthis, at which Ammonios (presides): of a penalty —
3   Lysimachos son of Lysimachos, that he do not make use of the
4   equivalent written earlier(?) —
5   three thousand — total 3000, and the appropriate dues.
6   Ammonios banker.
