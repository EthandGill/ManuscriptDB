1     [In the consulship of Publius Metilius Nepos for the 2nd time and Marcus Annius Libo, on the nones of Apri][l, and by the count of the new province of Arabia in the twenty-third year, in the month Xandi]kos on the fif[teenth, in Maoza near Zoara, Judah son of E]lea[z]ar al[so called]
2     Chthousion, gave out [his own daughter Sela]mpsione, a virgin, [to Judah son]
3     of Hananiah son of Somalas called [Kimber—both from the village of Engada in J]udae[a, here] residing—to be Selampsione a wife
4     wedded to Judah Kimber for the partnership of marriage according to the laws, bringing to him
5     on account of dowry feminine adornment in silver and gold and clothing valued by mutual (agreement), as both say, to be worth
6     two hundred denarii of silver, which valuation the husband
7     Judah Kimber acknowledged to have received from the same Selampsis his wife, hand to hand, forthwith, from Judah her father; and (he acknowledged) that he owes to the same Selampsione his wife together (with it) another three hundred
8     denarii, which he agreed to give her toward all the (items) of her aforewritten dowry, on account of her dowry, in accordance with the manner
9     of her nourishment and clothing and (that) of the future children, by Greek law, upon the
10    pledge and risk of the same Judah Kimber and all his belongings, both those he has in his same homeland and here, and whatever he may acquire,
11    fully and entirely, with authority, in whatever manner Selampsione his wife may choose, or whoever
12    acting through her or on her behalf may choose to make the exaction.
13    And Judah called Kimber shall exchange [for] his [wif]e Selampsione [thi]s
14    contract in secured [silv]er as is fitting, whenever she demands it of him, at his own expenses, in nothing gainsaying. 16    may make the exaction. In good faith the formal question was asked and it was acknowledged in return that these things
