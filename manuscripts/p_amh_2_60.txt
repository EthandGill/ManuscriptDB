1     [Of year …] Epeiph 7. Sochotes the grain-collector acknowledges
2     [of villages of Philopator] and Soknepaiu Nesos, workshops to have been measured
3     [in the] [Sok]nepaiu Nesos for them for the [lease] of the same year
4     [from Marrei]us, son of Sisouchus, and the priest[s] of Soknepaius, great god,
5     [of wheat, artabas] 41 and one-half quarter; total wheat artabas 41 1/2 1/4
6     … ros, the copy-maker from the royal scribe, [I] have together measured
7     [of wheat, artabas] 41 and one-half quarter; total wheat artabas 41 1/2 1/4
8     … [I] have measured for wage [of] wheat, artabas 23 and one-half third twelfth; total wheat artabas 23 1/2 1/3 1/12
9     … [ro]s, the copy-maker from the royal scribe, [I] have together measured wheat 23 and one-half third twelfth; total 23 1/2 1/3 1/12
