[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Payment of artabia-tax for the 15th year of Trajan our lord, from the katoikoi of Kerkesephis. Hermes, son of Didymos, togethr with the (artabae) chargeable to Ptolemas, son of Didas, through Herakleia, his daughter: 6 1/2 1/12 artabae of wheat, extra 1 1/8 artaba of wheat, requisition 2/3, extra 1/8, total 8 1/2 artabae of wheat, one-percent-tax 1/8, total 8 1/2 1/8 artabae of wheat, which have also been measured to the sitologoi Gemeinos and Kronion. 15th year, Epeiph 8th. From the Greeks. And in the month Kaisareios 27th. From Kerkesis. From Ptolemas, son of Didas, vine-dresser, 2 1/6 1/24 artabae of wheat, extra 1/2 1/24, one-percent-tax 1/24, requisition from the village 1/2 1/8, extra 1/12, total 3 1/3 1/24 artabae of wheat, charge for carriage 1/24, total 3 1/3 1/12 artabae of wheat, which have also been measured to Gemeinos, Akoutas, Ptollas, son of Petermouthion, and Zoilos, son of Didymos, sitologoi at Tebtunis.
