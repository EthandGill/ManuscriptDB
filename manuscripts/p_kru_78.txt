[TRANSLATION]
1     This deed of donation (doreastikon), inviolable, …
2     eternal, not to be undone, forever. [I have established]
3     it with a pure heart …
4     secured, and an aim (skopos) …
5     a reckoning (logismos) not to be moved. I, John,
6     son of Victor, the man of the fortress (kastron) [of Djeme],
7     that on this present day, which is the month of Phaophi …
8     with God, the indiction and that itself …
9     in succession, and at the carrying through, I give
10    and I donate (it) into the holy place (topos)
11    of Djeme, through its current steward (oikonomos),
12    those who shall receive its divine stewardship (oikonomia), for [Victor]
13    my legitimate (gnesios) son, this one whom I begot like
14    every man; and after three years
15    from when I begot him, he fell into a great [sickness];
16    therefore, when he lingered in the sickness, [with no]
17    healing coming to him, I expected (prosdokein) …
18    I vowed him into the holy place (topos) … [of Apa]
19    Phoibammon of the mountain of Djeme, saying
20    that, "If God of the holy Apa Phoibammon
21    shall grant (charizein) him healing, I will donate (dorizein) him
22    into it; he shall be contributing (syntelein) into
23    it for his whole life, one solidus (holokottinos)
24    per year forever." The possession (nome), then, and
25    the lordship (despoteia) of Victor my son, which I first
26    wrote, I donate it into the holy place (topos)
27    which I first wrote of, releasing it from this day, then, that I first wrote …
28    to the right of the holy place (topos), the holy
29    Apa Phoibammon of the mountain of Djeme, through
30    its current steward (oikonomos), those who shall receive
31    its divine stewardship (oikonomia), so that Victor my son
32    shall be under the subjection (hypotage) of the holy place (topos)
33    forever; he shall be giving [one solidus (holokottinos)]
34    of full weight per year, without any deduction …
35    except for this one nomisma of good weight, since he is disobedient (?)
36    to find it; and there is no power in me, nor (in)
37    my heirs (kleronomoi) or kinsman (syngenes) who is mine,
38    or man of my village, or any other man whatever,
39    to overturn (anatrepein) this donation (doreastike) or to dissolve (katalyein)
40    it, or to set up a counsel of ingratitude (acharistia)
41    against it at any season (kairos) or time (chronos),
42    or that we should say that "we wish to undo it,"
43    or that we should take heed concerning this written deed …
44    or that we should give another voice (decision) opposing it; these are
45    invalid (akyros); or that we should give against its force in any
46    method (methodos), whether in the court of law (dikasterion) or
47    outside the court of law, or in the ecclesiastical
48    help (boetheia), or through … laios … of (?)
49    or through an authority that is exalted; but I acknowledge,
50    I and those who come after me, forever, that we shall not drive off (aposobein) the holy place (topos), the holy
51    Apa Phoibammon of Djeme, concerning the purification (katharopoiesis)
52    of my son Victor, whom I donate (dorizein)
53    into it. But if it shall seem good [to us], whether [I myself]
54    or any other man whatever, in whatsoever manner, to transgress (parabainein) …
55    all these things which I have said in this written deed
56    of donation (doreastikon), upon (?) me, or whoever shall dare,
57    he shall do this — first indeed, that those men
58    shall benefit nothing, but he shall become
59    bound to the charge (enklema) and to the danger (kindynos)
60    of the divine oath (horkos); and afterwards he shall become
61    a stranger to God the Almighty;
62    he shall become subject to the great curse which [the Lord]
63    spoke in the Deuteronomion of Moses,
64    that "He dared a thing of this kind." But whoever shall keep
65    this deed of donation (doreastikon) of the holy place (topos),
66    may God and the holy Apa Phoibammon
67    keep him and his whole house, securing
68    the right of the monastery (monasterion) of Apa
69    Phoibammon of the mountain of Djeme
70    and the current steward (oikonomos), [those]
71    who shall receive the divine stewardship (oikonomia) of the monastery (monasterion).
72    I established this written deed of donation (doreastikon),
73    I consented (stoichein) to it, and I gave a witness [to it].
74    I, John son of Victor, the man of the fortress (kastron) of Djeme,
75    consent (stoichein) to this deed of donation (doreastikon) in the manner that it is written.
76    Pamprepios son of Chael, I bear witness …
77    Thomas son of … I bear witness …
78    Menas son of the blessed John, I bear witness.
