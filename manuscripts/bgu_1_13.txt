[TRANSLATION]
1    The Aurelii Apollos son of Sarapion also called Syrion, and Heras
2    son of Ammonas, both from the district of the Chenebritai of the Mareotis, to Aurelius
3    Antinoos, primipilarius of the prefect of Egypt, greetings.
4    We acknowledge that we have sold to you a male Arabian camel,
5    full-grown in body, for a price of silver, of the Augusti's coinage,
6    of sixteen talents and three thousand drachmas, which we have here received
7    from you in full, from hand to hand, out of your house; and we have
8    handed over to you the camel, unbranded, healthy and unharmed,
9    so that from now on you, having taken it over, may have authority
10   to sell to others, and to manage and dispose of it as you
11   choose; against which we will not proceed — neither we the sellers
12   nor anyone else on our behalf, in any way; and whoever
13   proceeds against it or lays claim to the sold
14   camel or a part of it — him we ourselves will drive off and
15   defend (the sale) at once, at our own expense,
16   as if by legal judgment. And this agreement was written, single, upon
17   our subscription, and let it be valid; and on being formally questioned we gave
18   our assent. The fifth year of our lord Imperator
19   Diocletian and the fourth year of Imperator Caesar Marcus
20   Aurelius Valerius Maximianus, the Augusti, Mesore the fifth.
21   (2nd hand) I, Aurelius Ammonis from the district of the Netheitai, having been asked on
22   behalf of them, who do not know letters, wrote on their behalf.
23   Sale of a camel, through Phekoulos.
