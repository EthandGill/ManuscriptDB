[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fifth year of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, in the month [of Mesore, the 4th,]
3     the 4th of Kaisareios, in Karanis of the Herakleides division of the [Arsinoite nome].
4     There acknowledge to one another Petesouchos son of Kameis, about [fifty] years old,
5     … with a scar on the shin, and Kames his son, about 24 years old, with a scar …
6     on the left, that the two of them have divided between themselves from the [now, for as long as]
7     the cultivation remains theirs, time (of years), and that there have been allotted by lot [and chosen] to Petesouchos for the part falling to [him]
8     of Karanis, of royal shore-land, five arouras, and likewise [of Ptolemais]
9     in the place called Isth…, one aroura, and around Pats[ontis]
10    of royal land, formerly Panas', a quarter, an eighth,
11    a sixteenth, a sixty-fourth (of an aroura); and to Kames the son like[wise]
12    for the part also falling to him, of Karanis, of royal shore-land, together [with the]
13    leased (land), five arouras; of Ptolemais, of revenue-land, … arouras, [a half,] a thirty-second, [a sixty]-fourth; and around Pats[ontis, of royal land]
14    formerly Panas', a quarter, an [eighth, a sixteenth,]
15    a sixty-fourth; and on the whole of the aroura-acreage …
16    and that each of them shall hold fast (the lands) which he has chosen …
17    and pay all the public (dues) on behalf of them …
18    … …
19    Kameis son of Petesouchos … (the lands) divided …
20    to my father and …
21    arouras 5, and around Ptolemais, of revenue (land) …
22    … arouras 2, 16, 64 …
23    Kames [son of Petesouchos] …
