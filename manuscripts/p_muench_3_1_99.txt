2     To Aurelius Hermodoros son of Ammonios, councillor of Hermou polis the most illustrious, superintendent of grain of Alexandria,
3     of the levy of the fourth indiction.
4     [From] the Aurelii Pisimios son of Alexandros, subscribing below, from the Monastery of Chaireos, shipmaster-pilot, and Pathermouthios son of Paneus,
5     [from the] same Monastery of Chaireos, subscribing slowly, shipmaster-pilot, and Paulos son of Papnouthios, his mother being
6     …os, about 56 years old, with a scar on the shin of the left foot, from most illustrious Alexandria, shipmaster-pilot, and
7     … son of Dorotheos, his mother being Dorothea, about 40 years old, with a scar on the index finger of the right hand, from most illustrious
8     Alexandria, pilot of a boat of the universal church of the same Alexandria, and Artemidoros son of Panetbeus,
9     his mother being Didyme, shipmaster-pilot, from the Monastery of Chaireos. We all acknowledge, being mutually liable, swearing
