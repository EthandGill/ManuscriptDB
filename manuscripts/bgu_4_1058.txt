[TRANSLATION]
1   (1st hand) To Protarchos,
2   (2nd hand) from Sillis son of [Pto]lemaios, of the Philometorian
3   deme, and from his mother Philotera daughter of
4   Theodoros, Persian, with as guardian her hus-
5   band P[tolemaios] son of Sillis, of the Philometo-
6   rian deme. Concerning the matters agreed, Philo-
7   tera consents to furnish her own slave-
8   woman Zosime for a period of two years from Phar-
9   mouthi of the current 17th year of Caesar, nurs-
10   ing and suckling, in Philotera's own keeping,
11   the child Sillis has handed over to her — a foundling
12   slave infant at the breast, a girl
13   whose name is Agalmation — at the wage fixed
14   for the milk and the nursing, for each
15   month, with oil and bread-rations, 12 silver drachmas;
16   and the assembled principal of the two years' nurs-
17   ing-wages, the 288 silver drachmas, forthwith
18   has Philotera received from Sillis,
19   from hand to hand out of the house. And if it happen that the said
20   child Agalmation suffer the human lot (die)
21   within the two years, Philotera is bound,
22   taking up another child, to fur-
23   nish her slave nursing it and to present
24   it in full for whatever child she takes up, for the whole two
25   years, receiving nothing at all more, since she has under-
26   taken to nurse against death; and from
27   now on to furnish the slave-woman exer-
28   cising the proper care both of herself and of the
29   child, not spoiling her milk,
30   nor sleeping with a man, nor conceiving,
31   nor taking on yet another child to suckle; and what-
32   ever she receives or is entrusted with of his belongings, to
33   keep safe and to return whenever it is de-
34   manded, or to pay the value of each item — except mani-
35   fest loss, on which, if it be also made evident,
36   she shall be released; and not to quit the
37   nursing within the period. And if she trans-
38   gresses any of this, she is to pay back what she has received
39   of the principal of the nursing-wages, with half again,
40   and the damages and expenses, and a fur-
41   ther penalty of 500 silver drachmas, and the appointed
42   fine, the right of execution belonging to Sil-
43   lis both upon Philotera herself and upon
44   all her property, as if by legal
45   judgment; void being also whatever
46   pleas she may bring forward, every shel-
47   ter; and the concession that Philotera has brought
48   up to Sillis concerning the foster
49   slave-child is to remain valid and as-
50   sured. We request (registration).
51   Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 4.
