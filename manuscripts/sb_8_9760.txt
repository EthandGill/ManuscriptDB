[TRANSLATION]
1     With [God], Hebraeim son [of] Iaeia from Narouti, [property] of Macarius from the laurel-grove
2     Aperetus, city of Arsinoe; it has fallen to you to give
3     for sailors' wages, indiction 6, of barges, [for] course, in number of coinage 1/2 24 48, one half
4     twenty-fourth [and] forty-eighth only; it was written in the month Phamenoth, 10, indiction 5
