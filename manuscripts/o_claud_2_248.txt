[TRANSLATION]
1     Petenephotes to Valerius
2     to the brother many greetings.
3     Receive from Maronnates
4     a camel-driver a bundle of saddle-bags, where saddle-[b]ags
5     four and a tablet
6     written [for] Dioscorotis, and a price from the saddle-bag [charcoal(?)] eight. Take [up]
7     [for] yourself them and you shall give
8     to Phthautis the tablet
9     and the saddle-[b]ags
10    so that he might give them into house. Farewell.
11    You shall give to Kolophonios [charcoal(?)] two.
