1     ch …
2     Year 29, Daisios 6, Egypt[ians]
3     and Pachons 16. [is registered]
4     Nicaios son of Charixenos, Achaian
5     of the second generation to possess
6     for him in Heraclea p[o]lis inside the wall toward
7     the goose-herding areas in the
8     sacred land, a garden of arouras
9     of three and a fourth
10    eighth, as the royal secretaries report. For this I pay the tenth to the Heracleion. I value the produce
