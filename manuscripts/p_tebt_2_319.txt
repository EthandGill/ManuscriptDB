[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Maximus also called Hermaiskos, priest, chief-justice (archidikastes)
2     and in charge of the supervision of the chrematistai and the other courts,
3     from Marcus Aurelius Herodes also called Lurius, Antinoite, and from Marcus
4     Aurelius Sarapammon, and howsoever he is styled. Since there came, both to
5     Aurelius Sarapammon and to Lurius, by succession of inheritance,
6     grain-bearing lands about the village of Kerkeusiris of the Arsinoite nome of the
7     division of Polemon, in two parcels, seven arouras, and about the village of Teptynis of the same division, in a place called Karion, likewise 7 arouras, and about the village of Kerkesis, 1 aroura, in all
8     15 arouras, which up to now were undivided, and it has now seemed good to them
9     to divide these so that each of them may recognize
10    his own share, they now agree, both Aurelius Lurius
11    and Aurelius [Sa]rapammon, that they have divided the aforesaid
12    arouras and that of the 7 arouras about the village of Kerkeusiris each of them has obtained 3½ arouras, and of the 7 arouras about the village of Teptynis each of them has obtained (3½) arouras, and of
13    the (aroura) about the village of Kerkesis each of them has obtained
14    the half share of it, so that from now each of them
15    shall hold and own the share fallen to him, of 7½ arouras,
16    and shall administer and carry out concerning them whatever each may choose; and
17    that Aurelius Sarapammon shall have authority over his own property that is about the village of Theogonis, of the 6 arouras, 3 arouras,
18    because Lurius has sold his own share,
19    the half, but they have left in common the
20    existing 2 arouras that are about the village of Teptynis in a place called Pagagoros
21    until they decide something concerning these,
22    there remaining to them a reckoning concerning whatever other property of the inheritance
23    they may find. And Aurelius Lurius shall not proceed against Aurelius Sarapammon concerning the dwelling in the metropolis, because he has sold his own share, the half; and the rest.
24    Year 5 of the Philippi, Mecheir 9.
