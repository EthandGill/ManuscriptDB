[TRANSLATION]
1   Aphodite to Arsinoes her lady, many greetings.
2   I make obeisance for you before the gods here
3   each day, praying for your health.
4   I at once restored to Mamertinus’ (wife)
5   what you sent over, and Sere-
6   nion received the little roll, sealed. Do not think, lad[y,]
7   that I have neglected your orders. Euphrosyne,
8   after cutting out the little leather garment,
9   pressed it on Isidoros. And you will receive by Arteus
10   both the hand-cloth and … wraps and fo[ur(?)]
11   Puteolan (vessels) and one fig-basket and 5 reeds
12   of warp-thread — it was agreed that these be sent you
13   out of her wages. I have received the
14   little cage from Didymos. We are slow in
15   sending you letters because we have no one
16   … (to carry them). Anbrosia greets you and […]
17   …[. a]nd Athenodoros and Thermouthis and her house-
18   hold, and all your friends greet
19   those who love you.
20   Deliver — from Apodite, to the lady.
