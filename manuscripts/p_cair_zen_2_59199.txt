[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Zen[on, greetings]. Regarding those from
2     the [Arsinoi]te beer-maker[s] ... ...
3     [und]ertakes the beer-seller [the] [in] Philadelphia[...]
4     a fixed allocation to be given into the royal [treasury] per day of barley twelve artabas.
5     Having therefore written an agreement with him and [a written agreement]
6     having received, hand over to him the workshop,
7     appoint also a trustworthy agent
8     who will oversee the
9     work. And the [one] now making beer
10    compel [him] to do what is just for the times
11    for which he has conducted business.
12    Farewell. Year 31, intercalary Peritius, 28th, Phamenoth 6th
13    To Zenon.
14    of the [beer-maker]
15    Paitus
