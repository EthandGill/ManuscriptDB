[TRANSLATION]
1     9. Timasikrates and another [to Alexandros, a written affidavit].
2     In the tenth year, in the month Apellaios, Phaophi the ninth, [in Herakleopolis above Memphis]:
3     We swear by King Ptolemaios and Queen Kleopatra also called Tryphaina, the Father-loving Gods
4     and Brother-loving, and their ancestors, and Sarapis [and Isis and all the other gods]
5     both gods and goddesses, all of them—
6     Timasikrates son of Timasikrates, a Thessalian, of the (men) of Kainon, a kleruch, [and his son]
7     Timasikrates, successor to the paternal kleros, to Alexandros [son of so-and-so] … of the
8     katoikoi cavalry: that we will abide by and perform for you and those [from you all the terms according to the]
9     deed of agreement, which we have deposited for you together with this affidavit [through the same record-office],
10    according to which we have agreed to consent to the change of name made for you in the official deeds, of (the lands) which my father Timasikrates ceded [to you from his own kleros which is]
11    around the village of … of two arouras, of which both the [surveying and the neighbours are shown]
12    through the deed of agreement, in return for which my father has made use [for both the crown-taxes]
13    and the other royal (dues) of the kleros … my own … my father … .
14    The receipts of the conveyances and measurements accordingly …
15    … and we will furnish to you and [those from you the arouras] free
16    [from all royal] and private debts and [all crowns and]
17    [all surtaxes] from the earlier times up to [the times of the change of name]
18    [and from all] cessions and all administration [for all time]
19    … without lawsuit or trial and …
20    … … having … for you …
21    … … nor … similarly …
22    … [nothing] contrary …
23    … the [deed] transgressing …
24    … and if we transgress anything or …
25    [of the things written in this affidavit], let the cession be valid according to
26    [the things administered through the deed of agreement] and [let the right of execution belong to you and]
27    [those from you for the penalties against us] accordingly [to the deed; but let me myself be]
28    [liable to the oath whenever I fail to keep it well].
29    … of the katoikoi cavalry …
30    … I have sworn the aforewritten oath.
