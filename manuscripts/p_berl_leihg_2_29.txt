1     To Isidoros, village-scribe of the village of Lagis,
2     from the undersigned men, public and domain-land
3     farmers of the same village. In accordance with the orders given
4     by the most illustrious prefect Annius Syriacus,
5     we register the unirrigated and (manually) watered
6     arouras around the village of Lagis in the present 4th year
7     of Antoninus and Verus the lords, the Augusti: of unirrigated
8     crown land on the reserve-list, two hundred two and a half, a quarter, an eighth arouras; of (manually) watered [crown] land, one hundred
9     twenty-two, a quarter, an eighth, a 1/64th arouras,
10    of the Pallantian estate, [formerly of Eros and] Charmos, of unirrigated (land),
11    ten and a half, an eighth, a 1/32nd arouras,
12    of (manually) watered (land) likewise, [forty], an eighth (and)
13    a 1/16th arouras; of the Lourian [estate], of unirrigated (land), one aroura,
