[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapiōn to Eutychidēs
2     to his son, greetings.
3     Let it be of concern to you about the wood [cut from] the southern cut [strip] and the other cut [strip] and the barley and the
4     fodder in Psōthi and
5     the wheat and how [there should be] scattered dung from the cut [strip]. I sent you 3 ceramic [vessels].
6     These then sell [for] 7 drachmas [and] three obols.
7     But if your brother comes
8     let him remain in the village until
9     I come to him. Inquire then
10    of the god [whe]n we are able to sow in Psōthi.
11    Be well, Eutychidēs, my child.
12    To Eutychidēs [my so]n
