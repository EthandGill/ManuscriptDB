[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Herakles son of Panouris, acknowledge that I have sold to the woman living with me by written contract, Beris daughter of Petearpsenesis, from the palm-garden belonging to me, two and a quarter arouras out of three and three-quarters [or however many] there may be, in one parcel, held in common and undivided, in which there is a cistern
2     and other plants and all the appurtenances, in the sixth-tax category, around the village of Ares of the division of Polemon, of which (whole) the neighbors are:
3     on the south, a common entrance and exit; on the north, a public
4     embankment and a canal through which the gar[den] is watered;
5     on the west, a public embankment and a canal; on the east, the places of the sons of Philon and, for some part, a public road. And I have received from [you the] price,
6     the agreed (sum), all of it in full, forthwith from hand to hand out of the house, and I shall guarantee the sale with every guarantee, free from all public debts and all assessments and apomoira (one-sixth tax) and eparourion and geometria (land-survey tax) and naubia and all the other things demanded, from former times up to the present sixth year and that very present sixth year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus
7     Germanicus Imperator, and free from private (charges) and every claim for all time; on condition that there remain valid for Besimas son of Apion the advance-lease which he made for him for the same period through the same advance-lease, valid as aforesaid. There wrote on his behalf
8     Heron son of Diodoros, because he does not know letters. I, Beris daughter of Peterpsenesis, with my father Peterpsenesis son of Herakleides as guardian: the sale has come to me as aforesaid. Pompeius son of Patron also called Primus wrote on their behalf, because they do not know letters.
