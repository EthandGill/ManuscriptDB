[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God: Alaarath
2     [son of Abd] to those from the region of Nestanon of the Elousan land of Gaza. Provide
3     [quickly] … [son] … ba of the Bani Klear with companions Gerrakou
4     … t for two months Saraein Rabe wheat modii ninety
5     [oil sextarii] ninety. It was written in the month of February indiction 5, according to the Arabs year 56,
6     [by the hand of St]ephan of Maioumiton. In total: wheat modii 90, oil sextarii 90 …
7     [In total] wheat modii 90, oil sextarii 90 …
