[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Marcus Annius] Syriacus
2     [prefect of] Egypt
3     [from] … son of Pethyllos from the
4     [metropolis of the] Arsinoite. Of your
5     [benefaction toward all] having myself too been benefited and ever obtained it, lord, to your benefaction of necessity I have taken refuge at this time, the practors of the money-taxes making a demand
6     [by a list], the practors of the money-taxes
7     [of the village of Karanis] of the Herakleides Division,
8     [beyond] the age of 77 years, and moreover a cubit …
9     … to perform service
10    [contrary to] my old age being demanded, of this list … through all your
11    [commands], simply the service …
12    … to him who also the eyes
13    [is weak], and moreover that this man does not keep oath well. Since
14    [governor] lord, those of this age
15    [ever by] you the benefactor according to your
16    inborn piety and from the
17    liturgies are released, I deem worthy, if to your
18    Fortune it seems good, to command that I be released
19    [from] this list, so that I may be able
20    [as] an old man from your benefaction
21    [for the] remaining time of life to live
22    [and so that] I may be benefited by you.
23    Farewell.
24    Through the record-office for the money-taxes
25    of the shore-land, Kastor son of Ptolemaios for the 2nd year
26    at one drachma each are set down: 2 drachmas.
