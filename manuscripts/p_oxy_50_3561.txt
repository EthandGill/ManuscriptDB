1     To Veget, general of the Arsinoit[e] Heracl[eides]
2     through Zoilos, royal scribe [diadochos], and those under the general[cy]
3     of the same district
4     [from] Dionysius Eudaemon of the …
5     [...]los Sosikosmeios also called Althaieus.
6     yesterday, which is the 12th of the present month
7     came upon me some [in a] bandit-like manner
8     sword-bearers with [the] village of Sacred Nikolaos before the tower, and [with] many me
9     blows they beat [so] that [I] ran danger to [my] life. Not only this but also
10    all that I had, clothes and money
11    and the gold ring which I had
12    they carried off. Therefore according to necessity
13    [I] appeal to you and request [to be entered] in regist[ration] with you this [matter]
