[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 12 of the Emperor Caesar Titus
2     Aelius Hadrian Antoninus Augustus
3     Eusebous, Pachon 21, reckoning of Pachon. [He] paid
4     Sotas son of Didymus of Pathermouthis, mother Sota
5     for the poll-tax of the twelfth year [of the] Treasuries
6     twenty drachmas, making 20, with the addition of bronze obols ten.
