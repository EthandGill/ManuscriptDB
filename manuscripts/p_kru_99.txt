[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity,
2     the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Written in the month Phaophi 18, in the 4th indiction, in the year 164.
3     I, Thomas son of Basilios the presbyter,
4     the man of Dekadritou in the land of Shmin (Panopolis), writing, giving, with afterwards
5     subscribers to subscribe for me to this deed of gift,
6     which is binding and confirming, in one matter; I write, willing and
7     being persuaded, apart from any fraud or fear or violence or deceit or seizure
8     or imposition (subscription?), there being no compulsion laid upon me, but out of my will,
9     of (my) heart, I write, donating Sabine and Iob my beloved sons
10    into the holy monastery of Saint Abba Phoibammon
11    of the mountain of Djeme, all these days of their life, that they may be slaves into
12    his holy monastery forever, at (all) times, that they may do as any man
13    who is a votary into all the monasteries; if they wish
14    to dwell at the holy monastery and serve it according to
15    the manner that the provost commands them, well; if they wish again
16    to dwell outside, they shall give their public-tax into the monastery,
17    and they shall pour them out for the lamp-oil of the offering and the lamp of this altar through you, Sourous the deacon and the provost
18    of the holy monastery of Saint Abba Phoibammon
19    of the mountain of Djeme; you shall be the lord of my beloved sons Sabine
20    and Iob, all these days of their life, that no man
21    shall be able to be their lord, except this holy place, although
22    the divine law commands in its imperial ordinance that it is lawful
23    for each one to make himself lord of that which is his. I, for my part,
24    have joined myself to the procedure of the laws; I will and I command
25    that the holy monastery of Saint Phoibammon
26    of the mountain of Djeme be lord of Sabine and Iob my beloved sons,
27    all these days of their life. You, Sourous the deacon
28    and the provost, you shall be lord over my children and (over) any
29    steward who comes after you within this monastery,
30    after today and after other times. The one who shall wish to come and bring suit with this holy place concerning these little children, neither I nor brother
31    nor sister nor relative nor relative of relative nor man of the village nor
32    a man at all, being one differing from (?) me — whether in a court or outside a court or in a praetorium or outside
33    the praetorium or through an archontic (authority) or through an ecclesiastical (one) —
34    those of that kind shall benefit nothing; first he shall be
35    a stranger to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit; afterwards
36    he shall pay to the account of the fine sixty holokottina of gold,
37    nomisma 60, apart from the penalty which our law appoints upon
38    all these; afterwards the saint Abba Phoibammon,
39    the honored martyr, this one whom Christ loves, shall take vengeance
40    on him at the judgment-seat that is in fear of God Almighty,
41    and condemn him in this world. To this assurance then of the God-bearing, victory-bearing saint Abba Phoibammon of the mountain of Djeme, I drew up this deed of gift, firm, having power in any place where they shall produce it, and they shall question me, my acknowledging.
42    I, Thomas son of Sabine, in Diktatripou in
43    the land of Shmin, consent to this deed of gift according to the manner that I drew it up.
44    Of Thomas son of Sabine … of Tatripis, Sabine and Iob. Phaophi 18.
