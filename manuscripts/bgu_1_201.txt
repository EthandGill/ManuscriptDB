# HGV-EN: From the seed reserves of year 17 of Antoninus Caesar, the lord. State lands of Karanis, exploited through the tenant farmers of Philopator. Apynchis, son of Pasoknopaios and Onnophris, his brother and his associates through Heron, father unknown, mother Papeteiris. At Karanis 6 1/16 1/64 arouras of royal (land). I, Syros, son of Onnophris, wrote this.
