1     To Vedius Faustus, the most mighty epistrategos,
2     from Gaius Julius Niger, veteran, of Oseirantinoe,

4     vindication. Outrage I have suffered by an Egyptian man
5     [Isidorus, A]chillas, scribe [of the] inspectors of recorded produce
6     [of the] village Karanidus of the [Heracleide] division of the Arsinoite nome
7     working with him in this Didymus, an assistant, no ordinary [one].
