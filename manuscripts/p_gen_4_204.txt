1     Account of what we owe
2     regarding the village of Phanu
3     thus:
4     for the distribution of [the] month Tybi coins 38 1/3
5     for the secretariat of John [the] notary coins 12
6     for the common [fund] of [the] village coins 2 1/2
7     through Aiolios and Phib coins 12 and of grain artabas 2
8     it amounts to coins 65.
