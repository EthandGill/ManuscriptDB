[TRANSLATION]
1     I received and I was paid, I Pettairus, with god, notary, from you sir Neilus
2     Goldsmith, for the tax of my property which you hold, [for the] produce of the fifth indiction
3     Which is one gold coin and two-thirds impure; [the] total [is] one and two-thirds coin impure. It was written in [the] month
4     Thoth, indiction of the same, fifth.
