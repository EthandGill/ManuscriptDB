1     Philotas to Zenon greetings. Crotos requested us to write to you concerning the
2     bodies which have withdrawn from [the harbor of the] Gazaeans. For it happened that I had been entrusted by both Apollophanes and himself [that] I [should arrange for them the matters] concerning the customs officials
3     I should manage jointly and the speed … [to] them [I should send]. Having gone therefore
4     to Heroides the customs official, I found Apollophanes having made a concession
5     to him of taxes drachmas π in the name of Apollonius. This [then] purchase I rejected, but another one I make in the name of Apollophanes and from [the] conceded
6     taxes I deducted drachmas μ and I established the bodies at the [harbor] and we brought
7     the bodies to Heracleides and we handed over to Apollophanes and [he] said he himself would guard,
8     but he did not allow us to have troubles, we being willing to [preserve] jointly.
9     I have written therefore to you
10    so that you might know. You would do a favor for me concerning which I gave you the memorandum, having purchased and sent to me.
11    And you write to us concerning whatever you might wish; for we will do it for [you] willingly.
12    [Farewell]. Year 28, Panemos 27.
13    Philotas. To Zenon.
