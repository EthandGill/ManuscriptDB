[TRANSLATION]
1     … [and] their plains (?), greeting. We acknowledge that, today, on this day
2     [that is the …] day [of] the month Pauni of the year, this seventh indiction,
3     … [and] Thekla his wife and Bes his [son] and Rachel his daughter,
4     … these, whom they handed over (?) in …
5     … to flee (?) in the district of Soi through Kaeis son of Abdella
6     … [tho]se came to us, asking us to fulfil …
7     … [a]nd we shall give them rest according to the way you commanded us
8     [at any time when] your [lor]dship shall seek their persons from us,
9     [we are] ready to hand them over to you, without reckoning,
10    [without dispute, without delay] forever; but if not, we are liable for every penalty
11    [that your lordship may] determine against us. For your security, then, we have drawn up
12    [and given] this surety-acknowledgment, being liable to you with all our substance
13    [in full according to] the force of this surety-acknowledgment, [swearing]
14    [by the name of God] Almighty and by the salvation of the lords who rule (?),
15    [we will guard, we will watch over], in the way that we first wrote (it); they questioned us, we acknowledged.
16    [A record, thus] …
17    … [and Rach]el his wife and Bes his son and Rachel his daughter, making four names.
18    [I, Apollos son of the] blessed Pshoi, and Theodosius son of the blessed
19    [Shenoute, we consent to this] acknowledgment in the way it is written. I, Apollos, wrote [for Theodosius]
20    … [since he does] not understand (letters); and on my own behalf, I, Hezekias son of the blessed [Tsamoul, I consent to this] acknowledgment according to its force.
21    [I, Athanasius son of the blessed] Makare, am witness to this acknowledgment, as I heard from the one who drew it up.
22    [I, Apa Kyros son of the] blessed Andreas, am witness to this acknowledgment [according to its force].
23    [I, David son of George], am witness to this acknowledgment according to [its force].
24    [I, Enoch son of] the blessed Victor, am witness to this acknowledgment according to [its force].
25    [Through me], Theodorus, notary with God's help, it was written.
