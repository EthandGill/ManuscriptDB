1     Heroides Zenichetes Zen[on greetings. Of a certain one of those dwell]ing in Ibion has been taken away
2     The son by Andron, the [guard, the one delivering to you] the letter, is brother of the detained. You will do well, therefore, [especially having ordered] to release the young man from the
3     prison, if not [having given bail for him] … [illegible] so that may not happen to him
4     to be destroyed in the prison[. Write and you, if ] … of those with us, as we [willingly]
5     do for you.
6     [Farewell.]
7     Zen[on].
