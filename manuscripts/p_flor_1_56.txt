1     … [To Aurelius Paulinus, strategos] of the [Hermo]polite (nome) …
2     from Aurelia Aretous [also called Heronous], daughter of Heron, a townswoman, with Aurelius Athenodoros Herodianus assisting her (?). Of the [chr]ematismos-memorandum of (formal) entry which I procured from the record-office, I have appended a copy, and I ask [that you instruct one of the attendants about you so that it may be communic]ated to those in p…, [so that within] the appointed ten days they may withdraw. Year 13
3     of Imperator Caesar Marcus A[urel]ius [Se]verus Alexander, Pi[ous, Fort]unate, Augustus … [Aur]elius Isid[o]ros also called Thermouthion, the priest and chief-judge,
4     to the strategos of the Hermopolite (nome), greeting. Let a copy of the completed (formal) entry be communicated [as] is appended below. Farewell. Year 13 of Aure[lius Severus] Alexan[der] Caesar the lord, Phaophi 24. I, Aurelius Harpokration, have noted (it).
5     The petition of Aurelia Aretous also called Heronous, daughter of Heron son of Anton[as], a townswoman, having been read, and the [registr]ation indicated [through it] having been subscribed and presented, and Aurelius Theon, who is said to have been dispatched, having subscribed that he had been dispatched for the completion of the chrematismos, we have determined that there be [writt]en and se[n]t a copy of the petition to the strategos of the Hermopolite (nome), so that if Aurelia Aretous also called Heronous, being present before [hi]m [and being satisf]ied with these (terms), should also tender [with a lawful guard]ian [the] proper bond concerning the (matters) indicated through the petition being true and that nothing contrary has been arranged, then, having instructed those who are in the registered (properties) to withdraw from them within ten days,
6     after that the (matters) of the (formal) entry be completed in the manner that befits, in accordance with the [pre]scribed (rules). [To Mevius] Honoratianus. [Aur]elia Aretous also called Heronous, daughter of Heron son of Antonas,
7     a townswoman, I have been registered according to the prescribed (rules), [from] a pledging and adjudication according to an agreement completed in the present 13th year … [for]merly of the debtor Aurelius Kastor
8     son of Pauseiris son of Heron, from the village of Moirai of the Hermopolite nome, both the mortgaged … according to the schedule of the public bank in Koussai of the same Hermopolite nome, in the (year) after the deified
9     Antoninus the Great's reign, in the 4th year, in the month Hadrianos, belonging to him in the upper Koussite (district) around Moirai, from the [k]leros of Therous … [twenty arouras] and a quarter, and, in order not to press hard,
10    that which I additionally indicated of the same debtor of mine, likewise the half-share of a house and of the eastward forecourt and appurtenances and [appliances] … of a garden in the western parts of the same
11    village of Moirai, against the (sums) owed [to] me by the same debtor of mine according to the same indicated schedule, of capital and interest, of seven hundred and thirty-two silver drachmas, against the … [ca]pital
12    and the per-drachma interest thereof and against the dues and collector's-fees and the other expenses, on the other (terms) which the agreement of registration contains, through which also the neighbours were declared; and wishing the (matters) of the (formal) entry to be completed, I beg at the assize-session that it be determined to write to the strategos of the Hermopolite (nome) to install me into the registered (properties) as is set forth above, all (of them), and to support me in the holding and ownership and produce-collection of the (things) that [remain]
13    and in whatever other (matters) of his I additionally require concerning these, in accordance with the prescribed (rules), without my being diminished, and in whatever other rights I have, as is fitting. Year 13 of Aurelius [Severus] Alexand[er],
14    Pious, Fortunate, Augustus, Phao[ph]i 3, an Augustan (day). I, Aurelia Aretous also called Her[o]nous, daughter of Heron, have submitted (this). I, Aurelius A[th]enodoros son of Herodes, wrote for her, since she does not know letters. I, Aurelius Herminos, attendant,
15    communicated (it) to Aurelius Achillamm[on son of Herm]esion, farmer, in person as is fitting and (as) cohabitant. Year 13, Epeiph 9. I, Aurelius Pamounis son of Kastor, mother Tauris, aged about 9, having a scar on the left shin, a min[or, h]eir of the aforesaid debtor, received an equal copy of this in person as is fitting, an account being reserved to me against the aforesaid Aretous concerning the rights of every kind which I have in whatever way.
16    Year [1]3, Epeiph 10. I, Aurelius S[i]lvanus son of [Her]minos, wrote for him, who declares he does not know letters. I, Aurelius Herminos, attendant, communicated (it) [to Au]relius Achillammon son of Hermesion, farmer, the cohabitant, and to Aurelius
17    Pamoun[e]s son of Kasto[r, mother Ta]uris, a mi[nor, hei]r of the debtor indicated through him, in person as is fitting. Year 13, Epeiph 13.
