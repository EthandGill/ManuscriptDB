1     Year 21, Mecheir 3. From Tybi 24 until Mecheir 3, Kephalon the charioteer,
2     for 2 charioteers and for 6 others and 10 grooms, total 18.
3     Year 21, Mecheir 3. Kephalon the charioteer acknowledges that he has received
4     from Charmos, agent of Asklepiades, oikonomos of the Arsinoite
5     nome, in Ptolemais which is at the harbour, for the two charioteers
6     who were left behind in Ptolemais, from the 25th of
7     Tybi until Mecheir 3, for each per day of loaves
8     of fine flour 1½ choinix, total 3 choinikes, of wine 4 kotylai, total 8 kotylai, of oil ¼ kotyle, total ½ kotyle,
9     and for 9 days of loaves ½ artaba (and) 7 choinikes, of wine 6 choes, of oil 4½ kotylai,
10    and for the grooms … 4, for each of coarse (wholemeal) loaves
11    2 choinikes, total 8 choinikes, of oil for each ⅛, total ½ kotyle, and for 9 days
12    1½ artaba (and) ¼ (and) 2 choinikes, of oil 4½ kotylai; and for a horse being troubled,
13    which was bathed and bled, from the 26th of Tybi
14    until the 30th, per day of wine 1½ kotyle, of oil 1½ kotyle,
15    and for 5 days of oil 7½ kotylai, of wine 7½ kotylai; and for
16    a burning-lamp per day ½ kotyle of castor-oil, 2½ kotylai …
17    (a horse) being troubled, from the 1st of Mecheir until the 3rd, per
18    day of oil 1 kotyle, total 3 kotylai, of wine 1 kotyle, total 3 kotylai,
19    and for those who arrived with Artemon,
20    6 charioteers, for each of fine-flour loaves 1 choinix, total 6 choinikes,
