[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: (m. 4 ?) Signed. (?) (m. 1) [Sokn]opaiou (Nesos), 7 camel, 2 foals. [To Apollinarios], strategos and to Sarapion, [royal scribe] of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division. from P[an]ephremmis, son of Horos, the son of St[ot]oetis, from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos. Of the 9 camels, which I registered in the past 5th year, I have sold 2 camels in the present month of [Mechei]r to Pamies, son of Stotoet[is], from the same village , I register the remaining seven camels for the present [6th] year of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, along with the two foals from their lineage. (m. 2) 7 camels and 2 foals were declared at the office of the strategos. Year 6 of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, Mecheir 5. (m. 3) 7 camels and [2 foals] were declared at the office of the royal scribe. – – –
