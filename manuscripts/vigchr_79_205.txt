[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: … in Diospolis, so that s/he bear witness to your love in God. Greet in the Lord our most longed-for daughter Marciana together with all those in our house, beloved and cared-for by God, to which (house?) the Lord will repay as befits their labors. I am amazed that, although I have written to you many times, you did not deem it worthy to write me a letter of greeting. Let me have this benefit from you in the Lord and be healthy for my sake in the Lord! … to Ploution, priest and confessor, (from) Lucius.
