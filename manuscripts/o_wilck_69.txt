[TRANSLATION]
1   Harpaesis son of Phenophis, Thrakidas, paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the sixth year
3   of Trajan Caesar the lord,
4   on account eight drachmas — total 8 dr., Epeiph 18.
5   The same, the remaining nine drachmas — total 9 dr.
6   I, Hermogenes, the collector, wrote (this),
7   Mesore 26.
