[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 3 of Tiberius [Caes]ar A[u]gustus, of the month Seb[ast]o[s] …
2     A sale and deed of cession of a house and of all that goes with it in Tebtynis
3     of the division of Polemon, of which the measurements are: from south to north, 8½ cubits; from west to east, 13 cubits;
4     neighbours: on the south, a hall of the buyer Tatithinx and of her son; on the north and
5     on the west, a common entrance and exit; on the east, a royal street. And he has received the
6     agreed price, all of it, in full, and he guarantees with every
7     guarantee for all time. This (sale) Pateinis
8     the third, son of Tagonsis, his mother being Taorseus, about 25 years old, a scar on the right ankle, makes
9     to Tatithinx daughter of Kronides, her mother being Taarmiysis, about 37 years old, marked
10    on the right eye. And there consents also the wife of Pateinis,
11    Tenaus daughter of Orseus, her mother being Araisis, about 24 years old, a scar in the middle of the nose,
12    to the aforesaid sale, and (agrees) not to proceed against (it) for all time.
13    Subscribers of the acknowledging parties: Lysimachos son of Lysimachos, about 22 years old, a scar on the face on the left,
14    and for Tatithinx, Diodoros son of Psenobastis, [about] 27 years old, a scar on the first finger of the left hand.
15    The same Tatithinx daughter of Kronides, about 37 years old, marked
16    on the right eye, with her guardian (being) her kinsman Harmiysis son of Harmiysis, [about] 40 years old, a scar on the left wrist, lent to the same Pateinis the third, son of Tagonsis,
17    a Persian of the epigone, about 25 years old, a scar on the right ankle, and to his wife
18    Tenaus daughter of Orseus, a Persian woman, about 24 years old, a scar in the middle of the nose,
19    with her guardian (being) her aforesaid husband, the two being sureties for one another
20    for repayment, of stamped silver, as principal, a hundred and
21    sixty-eight drachmas, at interest of 1 drachma on the mina per month
22    until repayment, in Mesore of the present 3rd year of Tiberius Caesar
23    Augustus. The same subscribers.
24    There acknowledge Patynis son of Auneus, about 60 years old, a scar on the face on the right, and his son
25    Aunes, about 20 years old, a scar in the middle of the face, the two being Persians of the epigone, sureties for one another, to Patron son of Heron
26    son of Gaias, about 26 years old, a scar on the left cheek, that they have from him, of silver,
27    stamped, 40 drachmas; and in place of these and of what
28    Aunes shall receive monthly as wages from Patron, in the month Sebastos
29    and Phaophi, in all, 22 drachmas of silver, and from the month Neos Sebastos
30    until Mesore 22 of the 3rd year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, per month
31    10 drachmas of silver, (and) Aunes shall provide
32    himself remaining with Patron from the aforesaid time
33    until Mesore 22 of the same year, treading clay in Patron's
34    pottery and [doing] all that is commanded, not being absent for a day, but for whatever days he does not remain; the (cost) of the public dues on behalf of Aunes and the necessities and clothing being (the responsibility) of the acknowledging parties,
35    and after the time the acknowledging parties shall be released from the aforesaid remaining (in service).
36    Subscriber of the acknowledging parties: Lysimachos son of Lysimachos, 22 years old, a scar on the face on the left.
37    Sambathion son of Sambathion, about 46 years old, a scar on the right shin,
38    lent to Lakianis son of Lakianis surnamed Skapaneus, a Persian of the epigone,
39    about 2… years old, a scar on the left knee, of stamped silver,
40    as principal, a hundred drachmas, at interest of 1 drachma on the mina per month
41    until repayment, in Mecheir of the 3rd year of Tib[e]rius Caesar
42    Augus[t]us. [Su]bscriber of the ac[k]nowledging party: Ly[s]ima[chos son of L]ysimachos,
43    [ab]out [22 years old, a scar on the face on the left].
