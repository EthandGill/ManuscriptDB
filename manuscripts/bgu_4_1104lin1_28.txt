[TRANSLATION]
1     Sheet (kollema).
2     To P[rotarch]os.
3     [Fro]m Dionysarion daughter of Protarchos, with her guardian her brother
4     Protarchos, and from Hermione daughter of Hermios, a citizen,
5     [with as g]uardian the son of (her) brother, Hermias, likewise. Dionysarion
6     consents that the (claim) which the deceased son of Hermione,
7     Hermias son of Hermios, brought through the surety of Hermione herself, through the same tribunal in the 21st year of Caesar, in (the month) Phaophi, be void,
8     namely the agreement on account of her deceased
9     son; and that [D]ionysarion herself has received back from Hermione
10    by hand, from the house, that which she had brought to Hermias through the surety
11    of Hermione as dowry: clothing worth two hundred and forty silver drachmas, and earrings and a finger-ring str(…) and
12    one hundred silver drachmas; and that the … [agree]ment be void
13    together with all the things indicated through it; and that Dionysarion
14    shall not proceed, nor anyone on her behalf, against Hermione, nor against the things left behind by the deceased Hermias,
15    neither concerning dowry or cohabitation,
16    nor concerning anything else at all, written or unwritten,
17    from the times before up to the present day. And since Dionysarion is also pregnant,
18    she shall not proceed even concerning the childbirth, on account of her having become compliant concerning these matters; and it is permitted to her … to expose her own infant and to join herself [to another]
19    man; and apart from the agreed terms being valid, [that she be liable], (she) transgressing,
20    to the damages and the appointed penalty.
21    We request (this). Farewell.
22    Year 22 of Caesar, [P]ach[on] …
