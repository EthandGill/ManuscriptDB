[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the fifteenth year of the Emperor Caesar] Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, in the month Hadrianos, 2…, in Hermopolis the Great. Hermione daughter of Eudaimon son of Sarapion, a townswoman, about 17 years old, with her guardian, her own father Eudaimon son of Sarapion son of Dionysios, of the Sosikosmian tribe also called Althaieus, about 65 years old, with an injured left eye, lent to Demetria also called Tereus, daughter of Horion son of Eudaimon, her mother being Ptolemas daughter of Hermokles, a Hermopolitan registered through property-records at the quarter of the West Fort and through public records at the quarter
2     [of the East Fort, about … years old,] with a scar … on the left, with her guardian, her own husband Phibion son of Pathotes, about 36 years old, with a mole
3     on his forehead on the left; two thousand drachmas of imperial silver coin as principal, which the borrower has received from the lender through the overseers of the bank, at interest of four obols in silver per mina each month from the month Hadrianos of the present year for sixty months, the interest amounting to eight hundred drachmas, all on mortgage of the property belonging to the borrower out of the allotment of Hippostratos, in the Tekerkethotis district of the lower Leukopyrgite, of
4     [cleruchic land, two and a half and a third arourae,] which the borrower shall also hand over in the field as sowable and complete by the right of cleruchy, by the measuring-cord, clear of royal land and of every kind whatsoever, of which the neighbours, as the borrower dictated, are: to the south, Sarammon son of Achilles; to the north and west, Horigenes; to the east
5     … [and the loan, the] two thousand eight hundred drachmas of silver of the principal and interest, let the borrower repay to the lender or to those acting for her on the thirtieth of the month Hathyr of the twentieth year of Antoninus Caesar the lord, at once, in approved current acceptable silver. But if she does not repay when
6     [the appointed time] has come, [let it at once be lawful for the] lender or those acting for her, without any need of renewal or of a summons-document or of anything else whatsoever, to have the mortgaged arourae re-registered through the land-allotment records, as is proper, and for her and those acting for her to possess them absolutely in place of what is owed, and to enter into them and enjoy their fruits and lease them out and carry off the yearly produce and all surplus from them for their own, and further to use and administer concerning them in whatever way they may choose forever, no plea or pretext being left afterward for the borrower or anyone on her behalf concerning this. Let the borrower guarantee this mortgage with every guarantee against both public and private claims and against all whatsoever, which it shall not be lawful for her to sell nor to mortgage to others nor to devise any other fraud concerning it
7     [contrary] to these terms in any way, or let whatever is done contrary to these things be void. And if any risk arise concerning this mortgage in whole or in part in whatever way, let the borrower repay to the lender or those acting for her all that is owed and, for the time overrun beyond the appointed term,
8     [the interest of] four obols in silver per mina each month, and whatever the lender may be required to pay on behalf of this mortgage as taxes, and whatever expenses she may incur likewise with interest, each at once, the right of execution belonging to her or those acting for her against both the borrower and out of this mortgage and out of all her other property, as by legal judgment. The contract is valid, and the borrower had it drawn up on the instruction of the property-record-keepers. I, Demetria also called Tereus, daughter of Horion, with my guardian, my own husband Phibion son of Pathotes,
9     [have borrowed from] Hermione daughter of Eudaimon the two thousand drachmas of silver of the principal, which I have received from her through the aforesaid bank, at interest of four obols in silver per mina each month from the month Hadrianos of the present year for sixty months, amounting for the
10    [interest to] eight hundred [drachmas], all on mortgage of the property belonging to me out of the allotment of Hippostratos in the Tekerkethotis district of the lower Leukopyrgite, of cleruchic land free from claim, two and a half and a third arourae, which I shall also hand over in the field as sowable and complete by the right of cleruchy, by the measuring-cord, clear of royal land and
11    [of every kind whatsoever, on the] aforesaid neighbourings. And this loan, two thousand eight hundred drachmas of silver of principal and interest, I shall repay on the thirtieth of the month Hathyr of the twentieth year of Antoninus Caesar the lord, and I shall guarantee this mortgage, and I consent to all the aforewritten terms. I, Phibion
12    [son of Pathotes,] am registered as guardian of my wife and wrote on her behalf, as she does not know letters. I, Hermione daughter of Eudaimon, a townswoman, consent. I, Eudaimon son of Sarapion son of Dionysios, of the Sosikosmian tribe also called Althaieus, am registered as guardian of my daughter and wrote on her behalf, as she does not know letters.
13    [Through Eudai]mon son of Hermaios it was transacted before Ammonion son of Herodes and his colleagues, the agoranomoi.
