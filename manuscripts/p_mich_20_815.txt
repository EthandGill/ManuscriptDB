[TRANSLATION]
1     To Eusebios and to Sarapion son of Plo[utarchos and Hierax son of Serenos, councillors of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, supervisors of the grain of Alexandria,]
2     from Aurelius Paesis son of Horos from the [same Oxyrhynchite nome, steersman of the boat of Diogenes son of Diogenes, from]
3     the logistai, with a guarantor for the delivery of the cargo [and for bringing forward receipt-letters, namely Aurelius Diogenes]
4     son of Diogenes the aforesaid, [and] himself [landowner of the same boat. We acknowledge, swearing]
5     the august divine oath of our masters [Valentinian and Valens, the eternal Augusti,]
6     that I, the steersman, have received from the [persons registered below and have shipped into the aforesaid boat]
7     from the produce of the fortunate 9th new indiction [new clean wheat, and free from every]
8     liability, by the public measure, by [the measurement that was ordered, in all 618 artabas, and to transport the cargo] to most illustrious Alexandria, and I will deliver [it to the public granaries there full in number, and the]
9     receipt-letters for the delivery of these I will bring forward; and I, the guarantor, of voluntary and free will (undertake)
10    to guarantee the same steersman, as delivering t[his cargo of grain and bringing forward the receipt in the name]
11    of you the supervisors, or that I the guarantor must of necessity undergo all that is sought against him concerning this cargo, so that in [no respect be blamed, or be liable to the divine oath.]
12    Aurelius Diogenes son of Diogenes, I g[uarantee the aforesaid steersman Paesis as de]livering the cargo of grain and [bringing forward the receipt as is set forth.]
13    Aurelius Paesis son of Horos, I received and ship[ped the cargo of clean wheat, six hundred eigh]teen artabas, total 618 artabas, and I will deliver as is set forth. Aure[lius] … [wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters.]
14    [Through me] … [it was written.]
