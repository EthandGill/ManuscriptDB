[TRANSLATION]
1   [From] Chairemon son of Zopyrion, and fr(om) Artemidoros son of Pathres,
2   P[ers]ian
3   [of the e]pigone, an[d] his wife Hermione daughter of Chairemon, Persian,
4   [wit]h as guardian her (husband) Artemidoros. Since Cha[i]remon, being asked
5   [by] Artemidoros himself and Hermione, has joined them in taking up
6   two [n]ames(?) ... from Artemidoros son of Herakleides, the eranarch,
7   [to] complete 23 talents 1000(?) bronze, [accord]ing to the agreement made
8   on the same day through the same tribunal - they agree:
9   [Art]emidoros and Hermione, that Chairemon has received nothing at all
10   [fr]om the aforesaid principal, but that they have used it entirely
11   [for] their own (purposes), which they will also repay to Artemidoros son of Herakleides
12   in certain fixed installments according to the
13   [...] agreement, and will release him from the agreement, and
14   from now on will keep him undisturbed and free from exaction in
15   [ev]ery way concerning these matters; and that they will do this without
16   [a]ny dispute; or else that they shall be liable to seizure and held until
17   they pay
18   [wh]atever they may come to owe on the two names, with half again, at once,
19   [and] the proper inte[r]est and the damages, the right of execution belonging to
20   Chairemon
21   [b]oth from the two, being mutual sureties for payment, and from one and from whichever of
22   [t]hem he chooses, and from all their belongings, as if by judg(ment),
23   [v]oid being <whatever pleas they bring forward, every shelter,>
24   Year 12 of Caesar, Choiak 18.
25   To be corrected(?).
