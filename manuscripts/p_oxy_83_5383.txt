1     [In the reign of our most divine and most pious master,] greatest benefactor, Flavius [T]iberius
2     [Mauricius the eternal Augustus and Imperator, in the 2nd year,] after the consulship of him of divine
3     [end, our late master Tiberius Con]stantine, in the 6th year, Thoth 26, of the 2nd indiction.
4     To [the most magnificent heirs of the man of glorious] memory Apion, who became
5     protopatricius, land[holders] [also here in the] splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas,
6     house-servant who formally questions [and provides] on behalf of [his own] masters, the same
7     all-praiseworthy men, [the (right of) action and] obligation, the Aurelii Ph[oibam]mon and Enoch,
8     full brothers, [sons] of … whose mother was Rachel, originating from the farmstead
9     of Aktouarios of the Ox[yrhynchite] [nome, with] guarantors who also stand surety for them
10    both for the delivery [and] repay[ment] of the tenancy of the following declared
11    and leased half [part] of a machine (water-wheel) and its rent — of our Ioseph
12    steward, son of Ioannes, [from the farmstea]d of Piaa, and of Biktor son of Pekysios, steward,
13    whose mother was Tekrampe, from [the farmstead of Phat]emet of the same nome, of the three estates belonging
14    to her, registered tenant far[mers] of hers, [greeting]. Willingly we undertake to lease,
15    we the lessees Phoi[bammo]n and Enoch, from the present year 260 = 229
16    of the present second [indiction], and of the sowing and gathering of crops of the God-willing
17    third assessment, from the (property) belonging to your Magnificence, from the machine called
18    Aplekouch of the East, the half part with the half part of the cistern in it
19    and the water-supply and turning-gear and machine-implements and plants of all kinds
20    and every other right of it and however many arouras there may be, in accordance with the landholder's
21    exaction-list, for the sowing of whatever crops we may choose; and we shall pay to your
22    Magnificence, as rent for these, yearly both in grain and in gold
