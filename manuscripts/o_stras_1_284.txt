1     Antiochos Kassian and Arrius Puplian
2     and Julius Maximus, and those with [them], overseers
3     of the sacred gate of Syene, through Pha[nof]is, assistant. Soter has written
4     P... on behalf of the poll-tax, 15th year
5     drachmas seventeen, half-obol, of prison-guard service, half-obol, [total drachmas 17, obol], and on behalf of division, head-tax of those having withdrawn from the 14th year, one drachma
6     [obol], two-chalkoi, [total] one drachma, obol, two chalkoi. Of year 15 of Hadrian, our lord
7     Pauni, 7th [day].
