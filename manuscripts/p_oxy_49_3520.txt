[TRANSLATION]
1     Nemesianus to Severus, greetings.
2     Deliver to Ammonas who farms in [a] village
3     Sespha wine [of the] vintage [of the] fifth year [of the] Libyan estate [of the] wine-press
4     of four vessels twenty-six and double-vessels sixteen
5     [things] agreed upon with him, of the vessels
6     as [each] one drachmas 16. Of the double-vessels as [each]
7     one drachmas 24; of all these the collected value
8     in drachmas eight hundred [he] transferred to the of the property
9     agent. Farewell and deliver the of the wine
10    vessels twenty [and] double-vessels sixteen,
11    [they] amount to vessels 26 [and] double-vessels 16. Year 7 Pachon 21.
