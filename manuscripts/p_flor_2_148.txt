[TRANSLATION]
1     From Alypios.
2     It is now the time for the oversight of the works. Therefore you must be careful, whoever
3     have irrigated fields, [since] the land has already been baked and [being] capable of receiving water, before [the] cutting, to dig out everything that is needed for
4     [ensuring] that not a single place of the fields be lacking in vines. And [similarly] the level [lands], before the cutting, dig them out and do not cut before
5     unless the fields that are lacking in vines are filled [in].
6     But if anyone disobeys these instructions of mine, it will not be profitable for him. And collect vine-shoots, both Theban and white, [of length] not less than [a] palm
7     [in width], and if [they are] larger or longer, do so as you see fit. And the [newly] cut plants
8     immediately [must] be placed in water so that [they] not dry out, knowing that at each
9     oversight of the planted areas, I note down the location of each manager and property so that if anyone introduces [something] contrary to my
10    instructions, or [something] instead of Theban or white vine-shoot, [whether] a garden plant or some other cutting,
11    [he] will suffer a penalty which he will not expect. I pray for your health
12    greatly. I have noted [this].
13    To Heroneinos, manager at Thraso. Year 13, Tybi.
14    From Alypios to Heroneinos, manager at Thraso.
