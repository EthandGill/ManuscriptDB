[TRANSLATION]
1     From the records of Theon, having been strategos of Prosopites
2     year 20 [of the god] Hadrian, Epeiph 24.
3     Naarotes, called Monthaurios, and Peeotes approached
4     both of mother Taphesieous [before] the present Aronneses Pandbeos
5     and Imouthes Horos, the strategos to Aronneses: What were you saying concerning these? [He] said:
6     Dining with me they withdrew from the symposium at night and returning said they had cut the vines of Imouthes, those around Naarotes saying they were being falsely accused by h[im], the strategos: Then you did not come to an understanding with him?
7     [They] said: Yes, yet he has heard nothing of such a sort from us, the strategos:
8     If you had a clean conscience, [why], when sought out for the inquiry
9     [of] the matter, did you not appear [until] [you were] reported beforehand? [They] said: We were on for[eign]
10    [we] were working on an estate, the strategos: and the reason for which you harmed the estate Aronneses has stated. And allowing them to be struck, he said: Confess the truth. [They] persisting in denying
11    having cut vines, the strategos: Where is the chief-shepherd? [It being] pointed out
12    [that] his brother Imouthes was present, for he was ailing, the s[trategos]
13    to Im[out]hes: [Is it] sufficient for you [that] these two [are] giving [it] so that [they go] to the most powerful
14    epistrategos to go [when] he is deliberating concerning the matter.
