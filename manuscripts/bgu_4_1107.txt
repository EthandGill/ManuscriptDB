[TRANSLATION]
1   Sheet (kollema).
2   To Protarchos,
3   from Isidora daughter of Kom[…], with as guardian her [brother Eutychides]
4   son of Kom[…], and from Didyme daughter of Apol[lonios, Per]sian, [with as guardian]
5   her brother Ischyrion son of Apollon[ios. Concerning the matters agreed,]
6   [Didy]me [consents] to nurse and suckle, outside, at her own home [in the city, with her own]
7   milk, pure and unspoiled, for a per[iod of sixteen mon]ths from Phar-
8   mouthi of the current seventeenth <year> [of Caesar,] the slave
9   infant of hers, a foundling at the br[east, … whose name is …,] that [Isidora has gi]ven over to her,
10   receiving from Isidora herself as wa[ge for the milk and the nursing, for each]
11   month, ten silver drachmas and two kotylai of oil; with which, kept
12   in good [or]der, she is to exercise the care both of herself and of the child,
13   not spoiling her milk, nor sleeping with a man, nor conceiving, [nor]
14   suckling [another] child besides; and whatever she receives or is entrusted with of its things, to keep
15   these safe and return them whenever demanded, or to pay the [value of each, except]
16   manifest loss, on which, having also been made evident, [she shall be re]leased. [And forthwith]
17   Didyme has received from Isidora, from hand to hand out of the house, <30 drachmas and> the oil of the first
18   three months, Pharmouthi and Pachon and Pauni; and (she is) not to quit t[he nursing within]
19   the period; and if she transgresses anything, she is to pay back both what [she has received of the nursing-wages and]
20   whatever she may receive in addition, with half again, and the damages and ex[pen]ses, and to pay further
21   [drachmas]
22   five hundred and the appointed [fine], the right of execution belonging to Isidora
23   both from Didyme herself and [from] all her belongings, as if by legal judg[ment;]
24   void being also whatever pleas she may bring forward, every [shelter;]
25   and, she performing each thing, that Isidora shall furnish her
26   the monthly nursing-wages as aforesaid for the remaining [months,]
27   thirteen, and not take the child away within the period, [or herself]
28   pay the same penalty. And Didyme shall come over to [Isidora]
29   each month, always every four days, bringing [the child too, for it to be]
30   inspected by her. We request (registration).
31   (2nd hand) I, Isidora, agree according to what is written above.
32   I, Eutychides, am registered as guardian of my sister <and have written>
33   on her behalf, as she does not know letters.
34   (3rd hand) I, Didyme, agree according to what is wr[itten above.]
35   I, Ischyrion, am registered as my sis-
36   ter’s guardian and have writ[ten on her be-]
37   half, as she does not know letters.
38   (1st hand) Of Isido(ra). Year 17 of Caesar.
