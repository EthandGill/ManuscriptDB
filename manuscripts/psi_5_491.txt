1     Agriophon to Zenon greetings. I have written out [to you a copy of the letter from Epharmotos to us]

3     And learning what it is that Apollonius copies … … is at a loss for … similar … writes … …

7     of him just as even until now. Farewell. Year 28, Dystros …

11    in response to the letters from us; for again Hippionikos has written similar things … [pre]viously [he] sent when Athenados came to us.
