[TRANSLATION]
1     Ch(rist)-m-g 99.
2     In the consulship of Flavius Anthemius the most glorious, Thoth 20, 9th indiction.
3     … in Hermoupolis the most illustrious,
4     … from the (nome) of the Hermopolitans,
5     from Aurelius …p son of Horos, … orchard-keeper, [from]
6     the village of Temseu Skordon of the Hermopolite nome. I acknowledge
7     voluntarily and of my own free will that I have leased from
8     [your] nobility for a ten-year period reckoned from
9     the crops of the fortunate tenth indiction the portion of the orchard that belongs
10    to your nobility, the part chosen and falling to it,
11    of whatever condition it is, together with all the plants and palm-trees and
12    everything appertaining to that same part of the orchard falling to you,
13    by right situated in the tax-district of the
14    same village of Temseu, and I have leased also the falling
15    to you portion of the palm-trees which are in the dry ground of the place
16    and of those which are under me the orchard-keeper — as rent for this
17    orchard of yours and of the palm-trees, per year, of jujubes
18    one-and-a-third artabas by the Athenian measure, which rent I will pay
19    and measure out in the month Phamenoth of each year, that is, for
20    this rent, eight carats, without delay. The lease is valid
21    and secure, and, having been formally questioned, I acknowledged it. Aurelius …p son of Horos, the aforesaid, have leased as aforesaid.
22    Aurelius Isakios son of Herminos, from Hermoupolis, having been asked, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters.
23    …os, deacon of the holy monastery, I bear witness to the lease, having heard from the
24    contracting party. Aurelius Phoibammon son of John, I bear witne[ss]
25    to the lease, having heard from the contracting party.
26    Aur[el]ius Phoibammon son of Lamachos, from Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lease,
27    having heard from the contracting party.
28    Written through me, Pinoution.
