[TRANSLATION]
1     Eutychides to Sarapion his father greetings.
2     Before all things I greet you and I thank you because you revealed to me the
3     health of you. Concerning the barley in Thallous be of good cheer, for I have sold [it]. Concerning the pottery
4     of yours I write to you in order that you know that we are not neglecting [it];
5     [we purchased] for you fragrant pottery vessels one hundred. The green [vessels] of Poes we managed, for having come there ... gave to us expense not little and as
6     was necessary ... and after much labor
7     we forced him to resist the
8     of these [agricultural lands] on the former
9     export-tax as you wrote concerning the order. Concerning
10    moreover Hyphantos formerly or write to me through days there I send. Concerning the green [vessels]
11    not yet nothing. I ask you to write to me
12    concerning your health. The wheat does not proceed if not from drachmas seven.
13    Fare well and having been exhorted
14    continually write to us concerning your welfare.
15    To Sarapion the father
