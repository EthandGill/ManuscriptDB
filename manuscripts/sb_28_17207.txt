[TRANSLATION]
1     [Ameneus to Pesourei] [greetings; which] Phanesis, village secretary [of Thmoinausireas of the Peran], [wrote] to us, the [copy of the letter] we have appended below so that you may know.
2     [Farewell]. Year 33 Pharmouthi 16.
3     [Phanesis village secretary of Thmoinausireas] and of the contiguous villages to Ameneus greetings;
4     [on the 16th of the present month, having arrested] the [from] the village guards Horion of the from
5     [the same village farmers, was brought to Hera]kleous city toward Komanos the overseer of the
6     [guards; and after these things on the same day] appearing in the same village Agathinos
7     [and Philammon the ones from Dionysios] [with] others making a sealing-inspection of [the] house of Ababikios
8     [of the house in which the aforementioned Horion had been] and Petesouchos of the royal farmers
9     [of the same]... [without] our knowledge or to take any of the [ones] from us.
10    [And the particulars of what was seized]... te: a salted goose 1 pillows 2
11    ... Year 33 Pharmouthi 16.
