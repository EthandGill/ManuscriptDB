[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos,
2   from Lykaina daughter of Asklepiades, with
3   her guardian, her father Asklepiades
4   son of Lykos, and from Hierax son of Apol[lo]nios
5   and from the woman who stands surety for him for the payment,
6   his mother Didyme daughter of Dionysios, [with]
7   her guardian, (her son) Hierax. They agree — [Ly-]
8   kaina and Hierax — that they have come together with one anoth[er]
9   for a partnership of life, and that Hierax has re-
10   ceived from her, through the surety, Didy-
11   me, a dowry, from hand to hand out of the house, of silver
12   Ptolemaic, one hundred and eigh-
13   ty-four drachmas, together with the value of a bracelet(?)
14   of unstamped (silver), eight drachmas; (and) from now on
15   that he provides Lykaina with the neces-
16   saries, all of them, and clothing, as for a wife
17   wedded, according to the means of his prop-
18   erty, and does not maltreat her,
19   nor eject (her), nor insult (her),
20   nor bring in another woman; or else they are to pay ba[ck] —
21   he and Didyme — at once
22   the aforesaid dowry with
23   half as much again, the right of execution belonging
24   to Lykaina, both from Hierax himself and
25   from the surety Didyme, and from the one whom-
26   ever of them she chooses, and from their prop-
27   erty, all of it, as if by legal
28   judgement; and Lykaina (is) not even for a day
29   to be away from the house without
30   Hierax's consent, nor to ruin the
31   household, nor to consort with another man;
32   or, if she too has done any of these things,
33   (she), on being convicted, is to be deprived of the dowry, apart
34   from the transgressor being liable to the
35   fixed penalty. And concerning the …
36   […] within [five] busi[ness] days
37   [from which] they give notice [to one another …]
