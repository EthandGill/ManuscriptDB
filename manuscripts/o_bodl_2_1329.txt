[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the granary of the metropolis
2     of produce, 22nd year of Hadrian Caesar
3     of the lord, Mesore 5, for unwatered [land],
4     [for] a man named Tiktos, [son of] Pamonthos, Arbechios, of wheat, artabae 6 1/24
5     and for dry [land], likewise [for] a man named the same
6     of wheat, artabae 4 1/24. Total wheat, artabae 1/2. Gaius I have signed.
