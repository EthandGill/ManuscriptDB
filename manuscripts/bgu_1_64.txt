[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Aurelius Dionysios, strategos of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division, from the Aurelii Longinus alias Zosimos, son of Leonides, and Harpalos, son of Sarapion, and Heron, son of Kopres, and Horion, son of Maron, all 4 and the remaining sitologoi of the village of Karanis. Monthly summary for the month of Mecheir of the present (year) 25 from the produce of the past year 24. There are 87 1/2 artabas of wheat, which were measured to us for the above mentioned village in this month, of which for Karanis for the cultivators of state land (there are) 68 artabas of wheat, for the katoikoi 19 1/2 artabas of wheat, = 87 1/2 artabas of wheat for the village. To these are added for an administrative fee as well as other (fees) 2 3/4 artabas of wheat, = 90 1/4 artabas of wheat including an administrative fee. And from the balance of the account of the previous month 3731 artabas of wheat, with the balance of this account 3821 1/4 artabas of wheat Year 25 of Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus Parthicus Maximus B[ritann]ic[us Maximus] Germanicus Maximus Felix Pius Augustus. [ (m. 2) A]urelius Longinus alias Zosimos, son of Leonides , as aforesaid.
