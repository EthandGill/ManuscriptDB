[TRANSLATION]
1     Dios to Eutychides, the most honored, greeting.
2     You know that friendships are shown not by words but by deeds,
3     and especially the friendship toward one another is manifest to both, and to
4     most people, the one (shown) through deeds.
5     And this indeed is noteworthy and
6     dear to God and, to all people,
7     praiseworthy, since indeed it restores
8     intimate friends so as to be loved by one another; for nothing
9     is greater, nor more lovely,
10    nor gentler, nor more delightful than such friendship in human
11    life. I, then, in brief
12    words will tell you what has happened up to now.
13    And I wish you to know
14    that I have never hesitated to write to you … of the day,
15    as far as it is possible to send word.
16    For I have been in many troubles, which I would not dare to
17    tell you frankly, lest I should
18    recount them improperly. But I think you have heard; for these things came faded to most people. And if God wills,
19    he will grant me victory through you, scattering the turmoil around me, and keeping you
20    safe for me, and bringing me into
21    your most beloved city.
22    I pray for your health, dearest one.
23    Phamenoth 16.
24    … to logios(?), from Dios.
