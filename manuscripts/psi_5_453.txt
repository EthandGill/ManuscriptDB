[TRANSLATION]
1     to those appointed over the sacrifices
2     [who have been] chosen
3     from Aurelia Teeiouthus from the village of Theadelphia with the
4     daughter of Tadeus. And always indeed
5     sacrificing and acting piously
6     toward the gods I have continued and
7     now in your presence according to [what was] ordained I poured libation and sacrificed
8     and of the sacrificial victims I tasted and I ask you to mark [this] for me.
9     Be prosperous.
10    Aurelii Serenos and Hermas
11    we saw you sacrificing.
12    Year 1 of the emperor Caesar
13    Gaius Messius Quintus
14    Traianus Decius pious
15    fortunate Augustus, Pauni 20.
