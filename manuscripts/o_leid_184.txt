[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 26, Hathyr 14, [he] erased
2     Petekhnout [son] of Ammonios for [the] land-measure-tax
3     of palm-grove produce year 25 in the name of Ammonios
4     Sentouoris the one-fourth [portion], drachmas
5     two, total drachmas 2, through me Amenothus.
6     On the same day [he] erased for [the] fourth [portion]
7     of palm-grove produce year 25 in the name of Ammonios Sentouoris one drachma [and] three obols
8     [and] half an obol, total one drachma [and] three and a half obols, through me
9     Amenothus [the] assistant.
