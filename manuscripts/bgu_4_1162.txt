[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Achaios] … [from] Sosipatros son of … [and from Aristoklea] … a Persian woman, with her guardian … [and Tryphaina] … a Persian woman, with her guardian
2     … . Aristoklea and Tryphaina agree that they have from
3     [Sosipatros] a loan from hand to hand [out of the house, of Ptolemaic silver,] one thousand and eighty drachmas, interest-free,
4     which they will also repay within ten months, being from …
5     the present fourteenth year [of Caesar],
6     without any postponement; but if not, they shall be liable to arrest and held until they pay [the loan,] the thousand and eighty drachmas of silver, with the half as much again, at once, and for the time overrun
7     the interest of two drachmas (per mina) according to the decree, the
8     execution being made for Sosipatros both from
9     the debtors, who are jointly liable for the payment, [and] from any one of them whom he may choose and from all their [property],
10    as if by legal judgment, they being without authority [and whatever]
11    pledges of good faith they may bring forward being void, and (deprived) of every protection.
12    And this loan is apart from what the same Aristoklea and Tryphaina owe to Sosipatros according to another agreement through the [same]
13    court, namely another three talents of silver.
