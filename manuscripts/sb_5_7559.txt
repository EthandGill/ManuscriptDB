1     Year 3 of Emperor C[aesar] Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Phaoophi … , in Tebtyn[ei of] Polemon's division of the Arsinoeite nome. Thaisas, freed slave of Eutychus [son] of Herakleidus, acknowledges
2     from the village of Tebtyneus, as of sixty years of age, scar on the forearm on the right, with the guardian of [he who] freed her Eutychus [son] of Eutychus who is also Lo[nginus], as of thirty ye[ars] of age, scar on the eyebr[ow]
3     on the left, [that] the acknowledging Thaisas has conceded after her ow[n] [dea]th
4     to those offspring from her Primigenes, freed slave of Ptollarion [son] of Eutyc[hus]
5     and Aphrodite, freed slave of the afore-written Eutychus who is also Longinus
6     to the two jointly equally the remaining portion existing to her Thaisas, half sh[are], non-inherit[ed] from her by [him who] had freed her Eutychus, [of his] half
7     share, which is one-fourth part of a two-story house and all appurtenant [thing]s
8     previously called Narcissos in the aforesaid village Tebtynei, and from the
9     living together the two Primigenes and Aphrodite together with their mother Tha[isas]
