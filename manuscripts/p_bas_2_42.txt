[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 9th year of Lucius Septimius
2     Severus the Pious Pertinax
3     and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
4     the Pious [the] Augustuses and of Publius Septimius
5     Geta Caesar [of] Augustus, Archon,
6     Sotos and associates, grain-measurers of the village
7     of Heraclea, we have measured
8     on the 28th of the month Pachon
9     of the present 9th year, from the harvest of the same year, to
10    Segathin [Ha]rpagathus through Pabutos
11    wheat, seven artabas; [there make] wheat, artabas seven.
12    Castor have measured as stated above.
