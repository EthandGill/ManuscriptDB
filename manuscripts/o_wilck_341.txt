[TRANSLATION]
1   Year 30, Pachon 24. There has been paid into
2   the bank at Diospolis Magna, at which
3   Ptolemaios (presides): from the price of salt,
4   Chares the (official) of the infantry, of copper,
5   three talents, three thousand seven-
6   hundred — total 3 tal. 3700.
7   P(tolemaios) banker.
8   (2nd hand) Kallias scribe. (verso)
9   of Kallias, scribe
10   of the infantry.
