[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consulship of our master] Flavius Anastasius the eternal Augustus, for the 2nd time,
2     [Pha]ophi 4, of the 6th indiction.
3     To Flavius Strategios, the most magnificent and most glorious
4     count of the devoted domestics, son of the most exalted and
5     all-praiseworthy ex-consul Apion, landholder here in the splendid and
6     most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites: Aurelius Joseph, son of Abraamios, his mother being
7     Anna, a registered cultivator originating from the hamlet of Papsau,
8     an estate of your Magnitude in the Oxyrhynchite nome, greeting. A need
9     having now arisen also for the irrigation-machine belonging to your
10    Magnificence on the estate under me, called that of Thryeitis,
11    drawing water for vineyard and for arable land, of one axle, having gone up
12    to the city I requested your Magnificence to order
13    this to be provided to me for the replenishment of the machine's
14    parts; and immediately your glorious Magnitude, taking thought
15    for its own affairs, the said axle, new, suitable, fit for drawing,
16    pleasing, was provided to me through the agent acting here, which (axle) having received on the present day, which is
17    the fourth of the current month Phaophi of the present year 174 = 143 of the
18    present sixth indiction of sowing and of crops, the seventh tax-cycle,
19    I acknowledge that I will perform the water-drawings and water-supplyings blamelessly and the rents
20    in good faith, and obey in all things pertaining to the landholding account. Valid is this bond, written in a single copy,
21    of the 4th indiction, the 6th (indiction).
22    Bond … of a registered cultivator.
