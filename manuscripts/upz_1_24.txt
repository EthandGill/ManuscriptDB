[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapion, one of the successors and sub-administrator,
2     from Ptolemy of those being in military possession
3     in the great Serapieion, this year the eleventh.
4     In the 19th year, Mesore, when you went up and made sacrifice, I gave
5     the one appointed by the king of the
6     twins for what is owed to them of oil and castor-oil
7     petition, and I asked you, in order that [it] be given to them and [of which]
8     times is owed to them and he promised me. For this reason also
9     I introduced to you the one providing for me sustenance,
10    my younger brother, to handle these matters.
11    And you, being piously disposed toward the divine and not wishing to violate any of the things promised in the sanctuary [ ]
12    sent, having appointed Mennides to have written authorization;
13    [instructing him to] examine what is owed to give, on the basis of which it might happen to you
14    not only to remain in what you are, but also to advance to greater things.
15    But when Mennides had written: to the secretaries, having examined to submit, [and these] having submitted
16    [it is] due to give, as much as we also were presenting,
17    Mennides again says [it] must be submitted to you.
18    I ask then you with a request, considering [it proper] to the twins
19    in private to give these things, and observing that he who
20    was providing for me the necessities, [having been] pulled away from the month of Mesore,
21    being occupied with these matters, [I] am not able to accomplish [it], order
22    more zealously to Mennides to deliver both the oil
23    and the castor-oil, in order that no longer [be further] corrupted the
24    boy and I, being in need of the necessities, [be] forced no longer to depend on Mennides and the twins
25    having nothing of what is owed [to] abandon the sanctuary.
26    And to you may it be to prosper with the king all the
27    time. Farewell.
