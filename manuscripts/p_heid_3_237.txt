[TRANSLATION]
1     To C[laudius Alexa]nder, centurion
2     from [Pane]tbheutes, public farmer [from village The]adelphia. The woman living with me ... [from which] also I fathered children, [thinking] otherwise
3     in mind [having become estranged from] the living-together with me, seizing the [opp]ortunity of my [ab]sence left from my house [before] ... months without the so-called
4     [sep]aration ... having carried away both her own and
5     very much of [our] property, among which was a large garment
6     white [unfulled] and a head-cloth Oxyrhynchite
7     [and a] two-sheet [garment cr]osswise and companion tunics
8     two and [up] ... [and] other agricultural tools.
9     and repeatedly when I sent to her [him(?)] wishing to recover [our] property this [woman] without returning [nothing] gave back, although I had provided
10    I to her [the expenses for] of the child maintenance. Since therefore now
11    I have learned [that] a certain Nile [a] Syrian from the same village
12    unlawfully [seized] her and married [her], I submit and [request] [that] she be brought before you, and Nile [as well],
13    before you so that I may be able to obtain legal redress
14    and to recover our property and O let [me] be aided.
15    Farewell.
