[TRANSLATION]
1     Ninnus to Heroninus the most beloved, greetings.
2     You have come into such great misfortune
3     squandering such large sums
4     so that you do not have a single [artaba]
5     of lotus. I have indeed sent you the artaba in this situation. Know
6     that if you do not take care of everything for yourself, you will bring upon yourself great misfortune. And Heras the steward of Myron has accused you as owing from the price of reed still from
7     the month Mecheir one hundred sixty drachmas, making drachmas 160, and by those
8     who sold the reed much troubled. Take care
9     then to send him the drachmas.
10    Year 2, Thoth 16. I pray you to be well
11    most beloved
12    so that you may not also be blamed in this matter
