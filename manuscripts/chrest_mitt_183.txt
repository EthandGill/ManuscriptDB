[TRANSLATION]
1     [To so-and-so son of so-and-so]
2     … occupied with the record-office of … of Soknopaiou Nesos …
3     from Tesenouphis son of Tesenouphis. The record-office having been granted to me, which I previously held, of the village
4     of Soknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleides division,
5     for the present seventh year of Tiberius
6     Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
7     Imperator, I undertake to pay as rent
8     of the whole, together with the due charges and the surcharges and the document-fees, in silver
9     two hundred eighty-
10    eight drachmas, and as libation in Phamenoth jars of wine
11    two, of which I will pay the surcharges monthly
12    in (each) month of the same year, always on the fifth and
13    twentieth, and I will register for you every four months all the official transactions administered through me, in one glued and continuous roll and in a single record, and I will give you the registration
14    of the documents, eight drachmas; and I will give you
15    sufficient surety, if it seem (good) to grant (the office)
16    on all the aforesaid terms. Farewell.
17    … on the aforesaid two hundred
18    eighty-eight silver (drachmas), and the
19    others, you having the power to sublet to others;
20    … at any time. Year 7 of Tiberius
21    Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
22    Imperator, in the month Sebastos, 14.
