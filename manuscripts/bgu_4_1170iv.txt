[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos.
2     From Ammonios son of Amenouthes and from Psenanouphis son of Harpaesis. Psenanouphis agrees that he has ceded to Ammonios the right of exaction of the debts owed to his father Harpaesis son of Anoubion by Ammonous also called Semnouphis, daughter of Petechnouphis, a Persian woman, according to the
3     security drawn up in Hypsele in the Lykopolite (nome) in the 12th year of Caesar, in Phaophi, of 26 drachmas
4     of silver; and that it is permitted to Ammonios
5     … (her being) liable for these and the interest from Hathyr 19 of the year of Caesar,
6     and to hand over and to take in pledge and to … and to carry off what accrues to his own account,
7     and that, if he wishes, he may exact (it) as also Petephnouthis himself, who has received the equal amounts from Ammonios by hand from the house; and that Petephnouthis
8     shall not proceed against the agreement, nor any other
9     person, but shall furnish the loan free of encumbrance, and further shall remove at once at his own expense the one who proceeds (against it), or, apart from the agreed terms being valid, Petephnouthis shall pay out the capital with the interest
10    as if by a legal judgement; and he has handed over to Ammonios
11    also the securities concerning the debt. Year 20 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 22.
