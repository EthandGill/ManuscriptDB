[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fifth year of Imperator Caesar Titus
2     Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius,
3     Thoth 13, from the bank of Dioskoros son of Dioskoros, of the Bithynians.
4     Doras also called Ninnos, son of Doras, to Pammenes son of Mysthes,
5     of Pammenes, from … al…los village, about 53 years old, without distinguishing mark, and to Apynchis
6     son of Panephremmis, of Apynchis, about 37 years old, scar on the little finger of the left hand,
7     and to Satabous son of Stotoetis, of Panommieus, about 39 years old, scar
8     on the left arm, and to Pekysis son of Panephremmis, of Horos,
9     about 27 years old, without distinguishing mark, the three from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, the four (being)
10    Persians of the epigone, sureties for one another for repayment —
11    (acknowledges) that they have from Doras a debt of a principal of silver,
12    five hundred and twenty-four drachmas, total 524 drachmas,
13    at interest of one drachma per mina per month; and they shall repay
14    the principal and the interest on the thirtieth of
15    the present month Sebastos, without delay.
16    And we, Satabous son of Stotoetis and Pakysis son of Panephremmis,
17    the four (of us) sureties for one another for repayment, have
18    and shall repay the (sums) of the loan, a principal of silver,
19    five hundred and twenty-four drachmas, total 524 drachmas,
20    and the (interest) at one drachma (per mina) on the thirtieth
21    of the present month Thoth. I, Pammenes, wrote
22    on behalf of Apynchis and Pakysis the greater part, and on behalf
23    of Satabous, being illiterate, as set forth.
24    I, Apynchis son of Panephremmis, have and shall jointly repay [the (sums)]
25    of the loan, of silver, five hundred
26    and twenty-four drachmas, and the (interest) at one drachma (per mina), as set forth. I, Pakysis son of Panephremmis, have and shall jointly repay, from joint liability, the (sums) of the loan, drachmas
27    five hundred and twenty-four, and the (interest) at one drachma (per mina), as set forth.
