# HGV-EN: Col. I (m. 4 / m. 1) 90 Entered through Tourbon, sheet [[50]] 10 of the poll-tax (list) 5 () 25 () sheet 62 (?) of the list 14 () 25 () (m. 1 / m. 2) To Valerios Longus and Sarapion, scribes of the city, from Heron, son of Pammenes alias Heron, the son of Heron, mother Maronis alias Syra, registered in the quarter of the Arabs. My brother Nemesion, born of the same father and the same mother, subject to poll-tax in the above mentioned quarter of the Arabs, died in the month of Mecheir of the present year 23 of Antoninus Caesar, the lord. Therefore I request that [his name] be included in the list of those, who have died. I, Heron have submitted. (Year) 23 of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, Mecheir 30. (m. 2 / m. 3) Tourbon alias Aios through the scribe the above mentioned Nemesion, if he really died. Col. II (m. 3 / m. 4) list of those, who have died Year 23 [of Antoninus Caesar], the lord
