[TRANSLATION]
1     Pamonthos and Petemenofis, tax collectors of silver tax of Market 2
2     To Senpetechonsei the elder, of Petechonsos, through Senamounios Papustios.
3     We have received for geometric tax of year 5, soiled drachmas 4
4     they make soiled drachmas 4. Year 6 of Antoninus Caesar our lord
5     Thoth 11. Dionysios, I have marked [this], drachmas 4.
6     Panameus, drachmas 4.
