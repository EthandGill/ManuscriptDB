[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been registered through the record-office in the village of Soknopaiou Nesos.
2     (Through) the record-office.
3     In the thirteenth year of the Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian
4     Augustus, in the month Apellaios = Phaophi 7, in Sok[n]opaiou
5     Nesos of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges,
6     Pakysis son of Stotoetis the elder son of Panephri[m]is, priest
7     of the first tribe of the great great god Sok[n]opaios, about 41 years old,
8     with a scar on the left arm, to his own full brother on both father's and mother's side Panephrimis, about 41 years old, with a scar on the little finger
9     of the left hand, that he, the acknowledging party, has received from him
10    forthwith from hand to hand out of the house 120 drachmas of silver, which are the remainder of what his brother Panephrimis owed him according to a contract completed through the
11    same record-office in the past 12th year of Hadrian Caesar
12    the lord, in the month Hathyr, on the twelfth, of 420 drachmas
13    of silver; which contract he has also given back to him
14    for annulment and invalidation, and that the acknowledging party Pakysis, nor those acting for him,
15    shall make no claim against his brother Panephrimis, nor against those acting for him,
16    concerning the things for which he has been paid as stated above, nor concerning any other
17    matter whatsoever, written or unwritten, from the
18    foregoing times until the present day,
19    in no manner, on no pretext whatsoever.
20    Subscribers: of the acknowledging party, Onnoph[ris]
21    son of Onnophris, about seventy-five years old, with a scar
22    on the forehead on the left; of the other, Panephrimis son of St[oto]e[ti]s, ab[out … years old, with a scar ] … on the right …
