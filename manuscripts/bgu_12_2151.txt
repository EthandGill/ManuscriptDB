1     [After the consul]ship of Neos Leo of divine memory, for the 1st time, Phaophi 22, of the 15th indiction;
2     … to the venerable scriniarius of the (office) for the Thebaid,
3     … [of the milita]ry de[partment], from the Aurelii Theodoros son of Joannes, his mother being Sophia, and his full brother Sois, both of the same parents on both sides, farmers
4     from the same city. We acknowledge, being mutually responsible for one another and mutually accepting one another,
5     … of our own free will and choice, that we have leased [from] your Admirableness
6     … for a period of six years [from the crops of] the fortunate incoming first indiction,
7     … [the] eighteen [arouras belonging to your Admirableness, situ]ated in …
8     … of the same city, in [two] localities: in [o]ne locality, by the farm called Pa…am… [aro]uras twelve; [and in] the oth[er] locality
9     to the east of the (one) called … the remaining a[ro]uras …
10    for sowing whatever (crops) we choose, at the rent mutually agreed upon between us,
11    of these (arouras), fixed, per year, of imperial approved gold solidi
12    fourteen in number less twenty-eight carats, by the public standard of the same
13    city, that is, gold solidi 14 less carats 28, which gold rent we will pay from our mutual liability
14    in the month of Phaophi each year, year by year; and we will furnish, in addition to the aforesaid
15    gold rent, four artabas of lentils and sixteen artabas of vetch-chaff.
16    The lease is valid and secure, and on being formally questioned we acknowledged it. The Aurelii Theodoros son of Joannes and his brother
