[TRANSLATION]
1     Syrus the elder to Paueres … Senthiaes … [greetings].
2     [Urgently] properly be diligent with
3     Eudaemon, until he [should] cure
4     the two Kolophonian [things], and make
5     your [portion] in haste, but do not neglect, and prepare four
6     artabas of grain.
7     Syrus the elder, I have noted.
