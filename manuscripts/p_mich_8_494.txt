[TRANSLATION]
1   Didymos to Apollonios, his
2   brother, greetings.
3   If I did not every day
4   expect you, I would assuredly myself, set-
5   ting everything aside, have come to you. So
6   much is it my prayer to greet
7   you. Mystes, (your) brother, I gladly
8   saw, and as far as was possible I treated kindly,
9   from whom also receive two good […].
10   And by all means [...] a
11   jar I will either send or, coming myself,
12   bring; for I am not yet coming, if
13   he still stays with you, to greet you. I re-
14   ceived four bundles of leeks(?).
15   Greet your children, free from the evil eye, and
16   all those who love you.
17   Farewell. …Choiak(?) 22.
18   Deliver to Apollonios ☓ from Didymos (his) brother.
