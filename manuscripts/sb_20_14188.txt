[TRANSLATION]
1     of your healing by the grace of Christ … … …
2     of petitions [they proceed] favorably. Be strong, rejoice, be long-lived, may you endure, being guarded
3     you by the god's providence unto a boast of our
4     nothingness. It was written of the month July 26, indiction 1.
5     the lord abba Samuel, the superintendent, along with your monk and all those in the cell
6     sincerely from us greet and the abba Sophronius the Damascene.
7     the monk of me sincerely you greets. It was written the letter from Mardu of the month July 25, indiction 1.
