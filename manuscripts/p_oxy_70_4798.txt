[TRANSLATION]
1     Of [Apion, former pr]otopatricius, landowners also [here]
2     in the splendid [city of the Oxyrhynchites], through Menas the house-slave who formally questions
3     and secures for his own masters, the same most-praiseworthy
4     persons, the action and liability, Aurelius Apollos, son
5     of Pasoeis, his mother being Tarilla, originating from the hamlet of Kolossos
6     of the Oxyrhynchite nome, belonging to your magnificence,
7     a registered cultivator of hers, greeting. A need having also now
8     arisen for the estate irrigation-machine under me called
9     Parnates, drawing water onto arable land, of one large
10    workman(-wheel), having come up to the city I petitioned your
11    magnificence to order that the same large
12    workman(-wheel) be furnished to me. And immediately your magnificence,
13    taking forethought for the maintenance of her own
14    affairs, furnished this to me through the
15    machine-maker here; a new, suitable water-drawing (device),
16    satisfactory, I have received for the completion of all the
17    machine-parts, on the present day, which i[s]
18    Thoth 29 of the present fifth indiction, of the water-supply of the crops of the
19    sixth collection, with God. And for proof of the receipt, this
20    I have made, the handwritten deed, which being valid, written in single copy — and on being formally questioned I gave my assent. Aurelius Apollos son of Pasoeis
21    it is satisfactory to me as aforesaid. I, Papnouthios, wrote on behalf of him, he being illiterate.
22    Total: one large workman(-wheel) only.
23    [ ] [Handwritten dee]d of Apollos son of Pasoeis, from the hamlet of Kolossos, for the receipt of one large w[ork]man(-wheel).
