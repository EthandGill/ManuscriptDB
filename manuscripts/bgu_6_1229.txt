[TRANSLATION]
1     … of Philadelphos, (in the priesthood) of Demonike daughter of Philon, in the month Thoth:
2     Dionysios son of Apidoros, a Naukratite of the workmen(?), has received
3     from Apollonides, agent of Apollonides the nomarch of the
4     Oxyrhynchite nome, from the formerly … upper toparchy,
5     from the administration formerly belonging to Simaristos, seed
6     for the allotment of Pyrrhias of the (men) of Philagros, for the 30th year, in place of vetch
7     of 10 artabas, 10 (artabas) of barley, and in place of olyra of 15 artabas, 10 (artabas) of barley,
8     making 20 (artabas) of barley, clean grain, unadulterated, by the receiving-measure.
9     In the reign of Ptolemy son of
10    Ptolemy Soter, year 29,
11    in the priesthood of Antiochos son of Kebbas, of Alexander and the Sibling Gods, the basket-bearer
12    of Arsinoe Philadelphos being Demonike daughter
13    of Philon, in the month Thoth: Dionysios
14    son of Apidoros, a Naukratite of the workmen(?), has received from Apollonios, agent of Apollonides the nomarch of the Oxyrhynchite
15    nome, from the administration formerly belonging to Simaristos, seed for the
16    allotment of Pyrrhias of the (men) of Philagros, for the
17    30th year, in place of vetch [of 10 artabas, of barley]
18    10 artabas, and in place of olyra of [15] artabas [of barley, 10 artabas],
19    making 20 (artabas) of barley, [clean] grain,
20    unadulterated, by the receiving-measure.
21    Epainetos Euangeleus, a private person
22    of the (men) of Philagros: Dionysios son of Apidoros instructed me to write a receipt concerning
23    him, that he has received the [twenty artabas]
24    of barley according to
25    what is written above.
