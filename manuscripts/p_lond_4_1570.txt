[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Patermou[the, the receiver (hypodektes)] of Apa [P]in[oution]
2     of Pinoution, write to the public office (demosios logos), through [you],
3     the most glorious lords, the master Basileios the pagarch, that I have paid to you for the public dues, of the first installment (katabole) of the sixth indiction, eight solidi (holokottinoi) according to
4     the value of the days, thus, thus.
5     Tybi 14, through Patermouthios the headman (meizon), 8 solidi; Patermouthios agrees (stoichei), through me Philotheos son of Mena, having been requested, it was written on his behalf.
6     I, Patermouthe, the receiver (hypodektes) of Apa Pinoution,
7     write to the public office (demosios logos), [through] you, the most glorious
8     lords, the master Basileios the pagarch, that I have paid to you, in the second exagion (assay), of the first
9     installment (katabole) of the sixth indiction, six solidi (holokottinoi) according to
10    [the va]lue of the days, thus.
11    Thus, [Mecheir] 9, through Patermouthios the headman (meizon), 5 solidi.
12    …u, Phamenoth …
13    … [that I have paid] to you, in the third exagion (assay), of the first
14    installment (katabole) of the sixth indiction, 2 solidi (holokottinoi)
15    according to the value of the days, thus.
16    Thus, Phamenoth 2, through Patermouthios, 2 solidi.
17    3rd, through the same, 2 solidi.
18    I, Patermouthe, the receiver (hypodektes) of Apa Pinoution,
19    write to the public office (demosios logos), through you, the most glorious
20    lords, the master Basileios, that I have paid
21    to you, for the public dues, of the second installment (katabole)
22    of the sixth indiction, one and a half solidi (holokottinoi) according to
23    the value of the days, thus, thus.
24    Mesore 3, through Horouonchios the accountant (logographos), o…t…s, ½ solidus.
25    Patermouthios agrees (stoichei), through me Philotheos son of Mena, having been requested, it was written on his behalf.
