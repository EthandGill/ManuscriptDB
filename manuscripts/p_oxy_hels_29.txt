[TRANSLATION]
1     They acknowledge to one another, Apollonous
2     daughter of Sarapion, with her guardian, her son
3     Diogenes son of Didymos, and the one who is her other son, and brother of
4     Diogenes, Heras son of the same
5     Didymos, a weaver: Apollonous on the one hand
6     (agrees) to furnish to Heras her own
7     grandson … also called Agelochos,
8     son of Harsiesis, not yet of age,
9     for a period of two years from the fifth
10    of the present month Sebastos,
11    doing all the things enjoined upon him
12    by Heras according to the weaver's
13    craft, which he himself shall teach
14    this (boy) as he knows it, the boy
15    being maintained for the aforesaid
16    time by Apollonous,
17    in return for which Heras shall provide to her
18    each month five drachmas of silver.
19    And the same Apollonous shall clothe the
20    son for the two years, in return for which Heras shall give
21    to her twenty-four drachmas of silver,
22    of which Apollonous acknowledges on the spot
23    to have received from Heras twelve
24    drachmas. And the repayment of the remaining
25    twelve drachmas on account of clothing
26    let
27    Heras make to the mother in the Sebastos
28    month of the coming year. And if
29    the boy be required to pay poll-tax or
30    dyke-tax or pig-tax or the public dues
31    of the craft, it shall fall upon Heras.
32    And however many days the boy plays truant,
33    for the equal number Apollonous shall furnish him after the time, or she shall pay for each day one drachma of silver, and for his being withdrawn before the time a penalty
34    of one hundred drachmas of silver, and to the treasury the equal amount.
35    And if Heras himself also …
36    does not teach the boy, let him pay
37    the equal penalty, without prejudice
38    to Heras and Diogenes in the right
39    which they have between themselves by the agreement
40    on all the terms it contains. The bond is valid.
41    Year 15 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
42    Imperator, month Sebastos, 11. I, Apollonous
43    daughter of Sarapion, will furnish the grandson
44    for the two years [and] will do as is set forth above.
45    Year 15 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
46    Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
47    month Sebastos, 11. I, Diogenes son of Didymos,
48    have been registered as guardian of the mother and have written [on her behalf since she does not know] letters.
