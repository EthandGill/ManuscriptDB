[TRANSLATION]
1     Germyllos to Nikephoros the father
2     and lord, many greetings.
3     ... this one did not send to me
4     the double-bed ... bedding, which you intend
5     to send to me, and [to me] a pillow give, because Lady
6     does not have bedding [but] under papyrus
7     I sleep. Besides then [the] [swiftest]
8     send to me the double-bed and for expense
9     to us bronze. I received moreover from
10    Pabetatos a basket where pairs
11    of loaves twenty and measures two of dates.
