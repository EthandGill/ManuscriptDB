[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eleventh year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
2     Augustus Germanicus Autocrator, to have worked
3     in the new six-gate [building] from the month Payni 15
4     for five days for the embankment-work of the same year
5     of Soknopaiou Nesos.
6     Paneph[re]mmis Papeis Marasoutus
7     Korax I have signed.
