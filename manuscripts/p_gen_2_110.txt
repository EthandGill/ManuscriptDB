r.1    ἔτους λγ Λουκίου Αἰλίου Αὐρηλίου
r.2    Κομμόδου Καίσαρος τοῦ Κυρίου Ἀμμώνιος
r.3    ὁ καὶ Σύρος Ἀμμωνίου καὶ μέτοχος Σαραπάμμων καὶ ὡς χρηματίζει καὶ μέτοχοι
r.4    σιτολόγοι πεδίου Ἔξω Ψεῦρ μεμετρήμεθα μέτρῳ δημοσίῳ

[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 33 of Lucius Aelius Aurelius
2     Commodus Caesar the Lord, Ammonius
3     also-called Syros, [son] of Ammonius, and partner of Sarapammon and as he holds office and partners
4     grain-collectors of the field Outside Pseuri, we have measured [with the] public measure
5     [grain] packed, from produce of the past year 32, for Turbona
6     of Kanores, Hephaistiad, residents, together with additionally-measured wheat, artabas
7     two, there are wheat artabas 2. Serenos grain-collector, I have measured as [stated] above.
