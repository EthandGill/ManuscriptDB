[TRANSLATION]
1     […] and having obtained a response
2     through servant Dionysios from
3     the magistrates, and not having met for the judgment because [of] having sold two of my dependents. I beseech you
4     to examine and give me copies of the decision.
5     And against Philippos, chief village-guard of the Koite, [the matter] concerning the same
6     magistrates: having obtained a response through Dorion, overseer, [he] did not meet because [of] having led away my child unjustly to the prison.
7     [It] was brought to the judges and
8     concerning this [they] posted a decision. I beseech you and concerning this, having examined [it], give
9     me the copy so that I may [receive]
10    concerning both the judgment.
