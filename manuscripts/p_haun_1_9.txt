[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Poseidonios, greetings. There came before us S…
2     Paopios, one of those who took up the royal land around Tebtuni[s],
3     because while he was managing the irrigation works and [watering]
4     [having come], Dioknetos [of the] one hundred arouras from Kerkesouchi[s] [ejected the]
5     [his] farmers and took away what they had, two buckets, because he [would]
6     [no longer] endure the men to come [with] his [water].
7     [If therefore] it seems good to you, you will do well having been concerned …
8     [that] the farmers might not be wronged, but having gone away they will be [with the land].
9     But if not, it will happen … to be broken up [on account of]
10    [he] has taken and … […] for you are not unaware of what is in the
11    Dioknetos [debt] …
12    [Be well.] Year 7, Mesore 26.
13    To Poseidonios.
