[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy.
2     In the fourth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Phamenoth 8, in the city of Oxyrhynchos
3     of the Thebaid. Aristopous … acknowledges
4     to Hermophilos son of Harthoonis and to Didymos son of Sarapion, in the street, that he has ceded to them, in accordance with the matters administered through
5     those of the cavalry office, on the basis of which Aristopous submitted a memorandum to Eudaimon, who had earlier been granted (the post) by his brother Theon the official in charge of the land-allotments, to each in
6     equal share, the land belonging to him near Kerkemounis of the upper
7     toparchy, from the allotment of Philon, of bought-in land at twelve drachmas for the assessment of the catoeci, grain-bearing arable land, of rectangular shape, six arouras, together with the annual
8     produce-rents of these, in accordance with the farmer's lease,
9     [all the public dues being chargeable to] those things ceded
10    … [in accordance with]
11    the orders and instructions issued concerning these matters, in return for which Aristopous has received from those to whom
12    the cession is made a cession-payment toward public expense in urgent times, and that Aristopous shall not act contrary to the contract nor commit any fraud concerning the cession of the land in any way,
13    but shall also guarantee to them, those to whom the cession is made,
14    and to those acting for them, the six arouras throughout
15    as secure, and clear both of public (dues) and
16    of all the other levies of the former
17    times down to the past third year and of that very third year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus.
18    But if Aristopous acts contrary to the contract in any of these matters,
19    let it be void, and let him pay in addition to those to whom he acknowledges (the debt), or to those acting for them, in respect of whatever point he transgresses the contract,
20    both the damage and as a penalty of silver one thousand drachmas
21    and to the treasury an equal sum, and none the less (the cession shall stand).
22    in no way being diminished, those to whom the cession is made, in the guarantee which throughout devolves upon Aristopous, of the other (lands) which were ceded
23    by him … of the allotment, of thirteen arouras
24    … … …
25    Cession of Aristopous
26    to Hermophilos and Dikymos (Didymos).
