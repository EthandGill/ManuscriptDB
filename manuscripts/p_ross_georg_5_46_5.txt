[TRANSLATION]
1     Phaophi 18, grain of the fourth indiction.
2     the lord Phoibammon, chartularius
3     through permit of Helias, deacon and baker
4     to [the] chancellor, artabas fifteen, totaling 15 only. Kosmas
5     through me. Phib wrote. Helias I have signed.
6     Christopher I have signed. Theodore through me, Theodore.
