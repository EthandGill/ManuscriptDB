[TRANSLATION]
1     On the fourteenth, Tybi 14, in Polydeukeia of the division of Themistos of the
2     Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges
3     Panesneus son of Hatreus, a Persian of
4     the epigone, about forty-
5     two years old, with a scar on his left hand, to Aphrodisios
6     son of Philippos, about fifty-
7     four years old, with a scar on his left eyebrow,
8     that he has received from him the price of black bean,
9     new, clean, unadulterated, of artabas
10    four and a half, by the dromos measure
11    of four choinikes, of which Panesneus shall make the repayment to
12    Aphrodisios in the month Sotereios
13    of the present fourteenth
14    year at the village of Sethrenpaei,
15    without any delay or
16    quibbling. And if he does not repay
17    as has been written, let him pay forthwith with the half-as-much-again and interest,
18    the right of execution belonging to Aphrodisios
19    both from Panesneus
20    and from all his property
21    as if by a legal decision, apart from
22    the other things which Panesneus owes
23    to Aphrodisios. The subscriber for
24    Panesneus, Horion son of Dorion,
25    about 31 years old, with a scar in the middle of his forehead. I, Panesneus
26    son of Hatreus, a Persian of the epigone,
27    acknowledge that I have received from
28    Aphrodisios the price of black bean,
29    new, of four and a half artabas by the dromos measure, and I shall repay in the month Soterios at
30    Sethrenpaei.
