1     Kastor, I have signed [this], year 15 of Hadrian,
2     the lord, Mesore 9.
3     To Deios also and Apolloni[os] and Herodes also and
4     Diogenes, former gymnasiarchs, keepers of public property of the Arsinoit[e nome],
5     from Tanephremis, [daughter] of Stotoeitis, [daughter] of
6     Herieus, of those from the village Herakleia,
7     with [her] guardian, [her] kinsman Pekik…tos,
8     [of] Pekik…tos. According to [the orders] of the most excellent governor
9     Flavius Titianus commanded [me] to register
10    the [property] belonging to me, [paternal] fourth part of a house
11    and courtyard and … [of a] cell [in] the aforesaid village
12    [Herakl]ei[a] … I shall show.
