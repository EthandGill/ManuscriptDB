[TRANSLATION]
1     In every way your illustriousness will accomplish
2     and will tear away the cloaks of my brother the
3     most glorious duke from the Saracen, the one being at
4     Singara, which is said from Peloole
5     and will restore them and [will hand over in the]
6     lower Nile, but their equivalent whether from
7     Bou whether from another place [he] will provide to him.
8     For as they said to me, they owed … [?] …
9     to give them. But at any rate straightway [immediately]
10    [he] will do this
11    To the most illustrious of all things, worthy of honor, Senuthius the notary of Athanasius
12    with God, illustrious
