[TRANSLATION]
1     In year two of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator Phaophi …
2     Boutharios [son] of Nikolaus I have repaid the aforementioned loan
3     Toward nullification and invalidation, in no way myself being wronged
4     On behalf [of which] I have two foster-children as is set forth. In year four of Tiberius
5     Claudius Caesar Augustus [Germa]nicus Imperator, in [the] month Kaisarion intercalary [days] five.
