[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Chrestos, strategos of the Ar[sino]ite,
2     from Aurelius Isidoros son of Ptolemaios, from the village of Karanis of the same nome. Often indeed
3     has it been ordered by the laws that no one
4     be unduly oppressed or extorted from; and I myself, accordingly, having been improperly and recklessly
5     demanded for three grammata of gold and eight grammata
6     of unwrought silver by Akotas son of Ptolemaios from the
7     same village, on account of the arouras which I myself always cultivate,
8     formerly of Horion, a former councillor, and pay all the public dues on their behalf
9     and the annonae up to today, though I never
10    leased or cultivated these
11    arouras — as also through the neighboring-landholders and
12    the boundary-surveyor the investigations are made, and lest men
13    be simply treated unlawfully, especially since I
14    have paid, on behalf of the 140 arouras for which I was registered,
15    the gold and unwrought silver and the other things belonging
16    to the treasury — these things Akotas has done not for the profit of the demands
17    but for the overturning of me from my own (property), contrary to the
18    divine laws, not having dreaded the fear of my lord, the most eminent Sossianus Hierokles, and
19    of my lord, the most eminent rationalis Aurelius Sarapion,
20    especially since he is in residence; and if
21    this had succeeded for the senseless men of old, if no one of the
22    moderate (men) had stood by — of necessity I submit to you
23    these things in writing, requesting that he be brought before you and that the
24    effective proofs be made through the boundary-surveyor
25    and the neighboring-landholders, whether I ever cultivated this very
26    land, [so that] the account may [re]main to me …
27    In the consulship of Tati[us Andronicus and Po]mpeius Probus [the most illust]rious
28    pre[fects. Aur]elius Isido[ros have submit]ted (this).
29    Aurelius … [wro]te on his behalf, [he being illiterate].
