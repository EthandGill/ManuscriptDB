[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Abounaios, son of Iamblichos, and his partners occupied with the sales-tax, to Bienchis, son of Pachonpaermon, greetings. I received from you the tax in full for the sixty drachmas of the Kytheria you sold to the skipper: seven drachmas, 3 obols. Year 7 of Traianus Caesar, the lord, on the 11th of Pachon.
