[TRANSLATION]
1     To Quintus Pacillius Euxenus
2     overseer of guards
3     from Arthotes of Marreios
4     of the public farmers from Theadelphia. During the night
5     leading to the 18th of Pharmouthi
6     of year 42 of Caesar, the shepherds of the village having invaded
7     into the royal land cultivated by me
8     grazed [upon] my herbage half-aroura. Therefore I request
9     if it seems [good] to have the accused brought before you for the
10    coming prosecution.
11    Farewell.
