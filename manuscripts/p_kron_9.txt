[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fifteenth year of the Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan
2     Augustus Germanicus Da[cic]us, in the month Neos Sebastos,
3     the 25th, in T[ebtyni]s of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite
4     nome. There acknowledge Orsenouphis son of Patynis son of Mesoeris,
5     about forty-five years old, with a scar on the right forearm, and Kronion
6     son of Cheos son of Harmiysis, about fifty years old, with a scar
7     on the little finger of the left hand, the two of them Persians of the
8     epigone, sureties for one another for repayment, to [Di]dymos
9     son of Didymos son of Maron, about thirty-four years old,
10    with a scar on the right shin, that they have from him, Didymos,
11    a loan of new pure unadulterated wheat, twenty artabas, and
12    of new pure unadulterated barley twenty artabas, all
13    by his own four-choinix measure, of Didymos, and that the acknowledging parties Orsenouphis and Kronion shall of necessity
14    repay to Didymos the twenty artabas of wheat of the loan and the twenty artabas of barley, in the month
15    Pauni of the present fifteenth year of Trajan
16    Caesar the lord, without any delay or quibbling,
17    the right of execution belonging to Didymos both upon the acknowledging parties
18    and upon any one of them, and upon whomever he may choose, and upon all
19    their property. Subscriber: M[a]repsemis son of Pakebkis, about 53 years old, with a scar on the nose on the left.
20    Orsenouphis son of Patounis and Kronion son of Cheos,
21    the two of us mutually liable for repayment, acknowledge
22    that we have from Didymos son of Didymos a loan
23    of new pure unadulterated wheat, [twenty] art[abas],
24    and of barley [twen]ty ar[ta]bas, by the [four-choi]nix measure of his own, the sa[m]e Didymos, which we will [also repa]y in the month Pauni of the prese[nt 15th year]
25    as is aforesaid. Pakeb]kis son of Psoiphis [wrote] on behalf of [the]m, since they do not know letters. Didymos
26    son of Didy[mo]s: the acknowledgment has been made [to] me as is aforesaid.
27    It has been re[gistere]d [through] the registry office of the village of Tebtynis.
