r.2    Ταανουβίωνος ὁμολογῶ πεπρακέναι Διονυσίῳ Πολέμωνος μητρὸς Ταμύσθας τὰ ὑπάρχοντά μοι μέρη δύο ἀπὸ μερῶν πέντε πατρικῆς οἰκίας διστέγου καὶ αὐλῆς καὶ
r.3    τῶν συνκυρόντων πάντων κοινῶν καὶ [ἀ]διαιρέτων ἐν Ταλεί· ὧν γείτονες ὅλης τῆς οἰκίας καὶ αὐλῆς νότου

r.8    εἰδέ[ναι] αὐτὸν γράμματα.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Tamystha [daughter] of Orseus, [mother] Tapettheuts, with the authority of my husband Polemonis [son] of Dionysius, [mother] Taanubios,
2     acknowledge [that I] have sold to Dionysius [son] of Polemonis, mother Tamystha, the property belonging to me, [consisting of] two shares out of five [total] shares of an ancestral two-storied house and courtyard, and
3     all the appurtenances common and undivided in Talei; of which the neighbors of the entire house and courtyard [on the] south
4     and east [are] formerly Nikolaos' courtyard; [on the] north [there is a] common
5     entrance and exit; [on the] west [is] Didymus' brick house.
6     And I have received the price in full and I will guarantee [it] with every
7     guarantee, as set forth. Onnophris [son] of Marsisoukhos wrote for them because they did not know letters. Dionysius [son] of Polemonis, mother Tamystha, the sale has been made to me, as set forth. Patron [son] of Heron wrote for him because he did
8     not know letters.
