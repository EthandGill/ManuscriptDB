[TRANSLATION]
1     … a matter, a lawsuit … the soldiers of the formerly city's unit (arithmos) … and no
2     … fearlessly and unsparingly they hurl themselves, after the manner of barbarians, because they have no occupation of living so as to provide self-sustenance for themselves;
3     … of your Magnificence, in sleeplessness we labor together with the servants who are her (our?) sons of ours, until we may find from sweat and
4     … our handiwork, and going round almost the whole province until we may find the means to live moderately and most scantily; but these men
5     … through and through, and they always practice mob-agitation, procuring for themselves every shiftlessness, fearlessly and idly and without occupation;
6     … to the citizens befell the undertaking of these men; for it has been said before that an idle citizen is a great evil to the city. And since to relate in detail
7     … paper does not contain the partial summary; wherefore let us sing to you something of those evil deeds: first, nothing being at issue,
8     … nnou … they … before the most illustrious Apa Besas the numerarius; and secondly, into a certain house called Psintse, intending to plunder it, they entered, on which terms to their own
9     … and that man … then at dawn confessed to us, approaching, that, having been summoned by them, he was about along with these men to trade and to set fire to my … e … lopo
10    … the house [of the] most illustrious [lord] Eulogios, having burst in(?) they struck also his doorkeeper to death, and him having become so, they handed over to Florentius the most devoted [soldier] … [T]ho[mas] the most illustrious also pagarch, acting deputy
11    of Antaiou(polis), and these men together with the soldiers following them …
12    … and to the … sportul(a) … six solidi they were released, and we have grown weary asking the … o … ton … ko … min … te …
13    … os me em … os te … ka …
