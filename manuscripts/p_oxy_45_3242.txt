[TRANSLATION]
1     To Diophanes, strategos, and to Harpocration, royal scribe,
2     from Dionysia daughter of Sarapias, also called Thamounion, of Antinoe, through Sarapion son of Longinus, of the city of Oxyrhynchus. I register, according to the orders given by Pomponius Faustianus, the most illustrious prefect, the property belonging to me about
3     the village of Sko of the upper toparchy, from the allotment of Apollonius:
4     of old vineyard, in which (are) date-palms and fruit-trees and reed-beds, a two-thirds share of 4 1/2 1/16 1/32 arourae, of which a 1/4 share, from the registered
5     whole 18 1/4 1/8 arourae, in the name of my ancestors,
6     declared through public records in the (times) from the 3rd year of Vespasian
7     and the 1st year of Titus; of orchard likewise to the south of the same
8     vineyard, with waste land, a two-thirds share of 1 1/8 aroura, declared through public records to have been entered in the aforesaid times;
9     of building-plots and waste land and water-wheel-house and a 1/2 share
10    of cisterns, a two-thirds share of 1/4 1/8 aroura, which is the portion falling to me and to my partner, [a 1/4] share, of the 1 1/2 aroura registered through public records in building-plots … the neighbours of the aforesaid being on the south and east myself and my partner, on the north the vineyard of Sarapion son of Andronicus,
11    on the west a vineyard. And likewise I register the property belonging to me,
12    a two-thirds share, about the same Sko, from the allotment of Posidippus:
13    of orchard, 16 … 1/8 arourae. The neighbours on all sides (being) myself
14    and [my] partner … And likewise I register about Monimou … k …
