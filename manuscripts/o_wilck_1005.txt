[TRANSLATION]
1     Measure of the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 6 of our lord Imperator
2     Caesar Marcus Antonius Gordianus Pius Felix,
3     of wheat thirteen and a third and a twelfth, total wheat 13⅓ 1/12, through ..o,
4     another, Mesore 5, likewise, for the South and West, in the name of the same, of wheat five,
5     total wheat 5. I, Aurelius Dioskoros, have signed.
