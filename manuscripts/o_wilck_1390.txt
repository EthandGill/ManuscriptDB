[TRANSLATION]
1     There has paid, Amontas (son of) Mesoueris,
2     for poll-tax of year 5, ten dr., (for) bath-tax 1 dr. 1½ obols,
3     total 11 dr. 1½ obols, and the surcharges of 1½ obols on these.
4     Year 5 of Nero Claudius
5     Caesar Augustus Germanicus
6     Imperator, Phamenoth 21. Bassos, I have signed.
