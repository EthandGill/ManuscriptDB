[TRANSLATION]
1   [To Prot]archos.
2   [Fr]om Eirenaios son of Sotas, and from Artemid[oros]
3   son of Didymos, a Persian of the epigone, and his
4   wife Hermione daughter of Chairemon, a Persian woman, with
5   her husband as guardian. There agree Artemidoros an[d] Her-
6   mione that they have leased from Eirenaios, for a period of sixteen months from
7   Pachon of the present
8   seventeenth year of Caesar, the bakery be-
9   longing to him, an oven-workshop in (quarter) Beta, in the street called
10   Eudaimon's, near the Ryamon(?), from the
11   ...property, together with the [two] ovens in it
12   and the baking-implements, a kneading-board ...,
13   at the fixed rent for each day
14   of two [ob]ols, which they shall pay to Eirenaios da-
15   y by day, free of every deduction
16   and expense of the repairs of the workshop
17   and of beams, cross-pieces, and roof-timbers for the roofing,
18   and also the rent paid for the workshop
19   and the charges for ... the business, all being chargeable to the
20   lessees Artemidoros and Hermione,
21   who from now are to hold to the lease
22   and to take the profits for themselves, they not
23   being permitted to abandon the lease within the
24   period, nor from now on, in perpetuity, to set up against (it)
25   another bakery-workshop (in competition) with the oven-house, within fi-
26   ve plethra all around; and, the period having elapsed, they shall hand
27   over the workshop clean, both of grain and
28   of other refuse, together with the implements belonging to it
29   and the ovens, such as they may be from the use; and also
30   clean likewise of rents and taxes; and if
31   they transgress any of these, they shall be immediate-
32   ly subject to execution and held until they make good,
33   and fulfil, and pay to Eirenaios
34   [what]ever they owe against the daily rent,
35   [with] half as much again, and the value of whatever they fail to hand over, and the
36   [damage] and expenses and a penalty of 500 silver drachmas, an[d]
37   [the fixed] fine, the right of execution being
38   [Eirenaios'], against [them], who are mutually sur-
39   [ety for payment], and against either one, whichever [of them]
40   [he chooses, and against] all their property,
41   [as if by legal judgment], (all pleas) which they may bring being void,
42   [all] pleas, every protection (notwithstanding),
43   Eirenaios nonetheless having the power, if they
44   [breach the contract], to (remove) them with[in]
45   [the period ...]
