[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Sarapias to Ammonios her father and lord, many greetings. I constantly pray that you are well, and I make obeisance on your behalf before Philotera. I left Myos Hormos quickly after giving birth. I have taken nothing from Myos Hormos. Recently I sent from Myos Hormos 4 drachmas in order that you bring me slices. Send me 1 small drinking cup … and send your daughter a small pillow. I have received 2 small loaves of bread. I pray for your health.
