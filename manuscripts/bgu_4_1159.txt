[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos, who is in charge of the tribunal,
2     from Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios, of the Althaian deme,
3     and from Zosimos son of … .
4     [Zosimos agrees] … while Ptolemaios was serving as strategos of the Menelaite nome and …
5     for the carrying out of certain works
6     in respect of the prisoners under guard,
7     having provided a great labour-force,
8     and moreover having been entrusted by …
9     … the same Ptolemaios with the guard-posts of
10    the same nome, and having been occupied from
11    Thoth of the present … year of Caesar until Choiak of the same year,
12    Ptolemaios having been removed
13    from the office of strategos from Choiak …
14    … having discharged …
15    … [and if anyone] should bring a charge against Ptolemaios for the
16    work performed by Zosimos on these matters … and …
17    the labour and, having taken [an examination] of the administration,
18    or of any other thing of which
19    Zosimos himself has been occupied or has performed as a public service in matters relating to the office of strategos …
20    Zosimos (agrees) that I will be … and
21    will pay to Ptolemaios whatever loss or exaction is caused on his account, together with the half as much again,
22    and the damages and expenses and other things
23    as a private debt, one thousand
24    silver drachmas, the right of execution belonging to
25    Ptolemaios … these …
26    both from Zosimos himself and from all his property, as if by legal judgement.
