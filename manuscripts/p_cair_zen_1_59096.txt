[TRANSLATION]
1     Zōilos to Zēnōn [greetings. If Apollōnios] indeed is well and you and those whom you wish,
2     it comes to pass as we [ourselves would wish; adequately and] we ourselves held. And it would be fine if
3     you were to write to [us about Apollōn]ios, if he is celebrating the festival with us,
4     so that we might prepare things for [his arrival] and not suddenly
5     unprepared [we] might be caught.
6     Farewell. Year 29, Mesore 2.
7     Year 29, Gorpiaios 16. Of Zōilos,
8     if Apollōnios will celebrate with him
9     the Arsineia.
10    To Z[ēnōn].
