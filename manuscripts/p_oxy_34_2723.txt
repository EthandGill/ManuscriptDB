[TRANSLATION]
1     From Lucius Calpurnius Gaius, and however he is styled, former eutheniarch and kosmetes, councillor of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians, gymnasiarch, prytanis-in-office of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, and from Aurelia Apollonia also called Harpokratiaine, daughter of Publius, citizen as at Ptolemais in the Thebaid, formerly styled Apollonia
2     also called Harpokratiaine, daughter of Publius, her mother being Apollonia, from the city of Oxyrhynchus, with her guardian given to her according to Roman custom, Marcus Aurelius Ammonios also called Geminas, and however he is styled, and from her son
3     they agree, on the one hand Aurelia Apollonia also called Harpokratiaina,
4     to have conveyed to the minor son of Calpurnius Gaius, Lucius Calpurnius Firmus, the property belonging to her about the village
5     of Souis of the lower toparchy of the Oxyrhynchite nome, from the holding of Aristodemos, a vineyard property and a reed-bed
6     which is in neglect, and the existing house-plots and wine-press and storage-jar and pomace-pressing apparatus and the adjoining water-installations, together with the
7     machine set upon these, fully equipped with all wooden tackle and iron-work, and their appurtenances and all that pertains and belongs, of eleven and a half and a quarter arouras, or however many there may be, together with the date-palms in and about them
8     and all the plants and fruit-trees; of the whole the neighbors (are): on the west a flank-strip, and on the other three winds (the land) of Calpurnius Gaius; and that nothing else … to herself does Aurelia Apollonia also called Harpokratiaina upon the … in the conveyance; and if … anything left over, this too is to belong to the purchaser
9     … of the aforesaid (property) and reed-bed of eleven and a half and a quarter arouras … of silver imperial coinage, drachmas ten thousand (?)
10    … there on the spot [Aurelia] Apollonia also called Harpokratiaina received from Calpurnius [Gaius] … to be conveyed to the same purchaser, his minor son
11    … wherefore, from now on, the same purchaser Calpurnius Firmus [is to hold and] be master, [together with his descendants and those who shall succeed from him], of the [vineyard property conveyed] to him as aforesaid … and, having come of age, [to use] and [manage] … as he may choose; which also [Aurelia Apol]lonia also called Harpokr[atiaina shall guarantee, perpetually], against all (claims), with every guarantee,
12    [and free from royal and imperia]l-estate cultivation of land and …
13    … phane exa …
