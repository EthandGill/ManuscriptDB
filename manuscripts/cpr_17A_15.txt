[TRANSLATION]
1     [Of which petitions I submitted concerning certain malefactors who attempt]ed to encroach upon our land, and (concerning) the subscription which I obtained
2     ordering you, the most excellent exactor,
3     to adjudicate between us and to give the appropriate decision, a copy
4     as the (subscription) appended contains, I submit to you these documents, asking that
5     the aforesaid persons be enjoined these very things, so that they may know and may meet (me) for the arbitration, or through
6     it to oppose (me) until judgment. And it is (thus): "To Valeri[us Victori]anus the most distinguished [gov]ernor, from Ad[el]phios son of Adelph[ios: I have]
7     land, governor, lord, in the fields of the village of Thynis and Sarapieon.
8     But having taken care of it, I also handed it over to sowing. Since, then, Anysios and Phibion and 6 partners from the village of Prere attempt[ed]
9     to encroach upon the land, of which according to all the laws I … (have title)
10    and have possession, I beg your excellence to order whomever
11    you approve to restrain the harassment being done against me.
12    Farewell." In the consulship of our lords Licinnius
13    Augustus for the 6th time and Licinnius the most illustrious Caesar [for the] 2nd time,
14    on the 8th (day) before the Kalends of March. "The exactor will adjudicate between you (and)
15    will pronounce the appropriate decision. Of sheet 61, of volume 1. Thus far."
16    Farewell.
