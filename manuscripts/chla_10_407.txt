[TRANSLATION]
1     … Iulianus the advocate said: … making the pleas on behalf of Romanus the devoted (officer), enrolled in the number of the devoted Transtigritani,
2     … in the month … advancing … against … … having as his aim to attempt from others' (property) to procure for himself things not his own, and this …
3     … the power … being so … of the … prefects, by his very close (kinship), but reckoning the claim of kinship as nothing toward this …
4     … for … there is a number (regiment) … having received … exchanging from the same father … years, and undertaking toward him in all things together with …
5     … while the father of the one defended was still alive, preserving toward him a brotherly disposition, having given him a house belonging to him for his dwelling, he has attempted
6     … contrary to the laws to show toward his (brother's) child none of the good things which his own brother did toward him, but rather such things as lead to the complete overthrow of the one defended.
7     For indeed, having become master of the house, he holds the whole of it, belonging to him by no right, but having come round by inheritance to the one who supplicates your Greatness, by the laws, it being known
8     that, having succeeded his own father in all things, he also acquired this (house) which had come to him. So therefore, that the power of this man may not become stronger than the laws themselves, we beg,
9     let your Greatness command that we be put in possession of the said house, the ownership being once for all ours according to the aforesaid manner of the narration.
10    Flavius Paulus Leontius Marinus Petronius Hesychius, vir clarissimus, comes of the sacred council, former praeses, said twice: the office (officium) shall compel the one accused by you to withdraw from the things not justly belonging to him and to give back the dwelling handed over to him, as you stated. But if he should make objection, it (the office) shall bring him before the court to receive your suits.
11    Iulianus the advocate said: the one defended accuses the responsible (persons) of bad faith, whom he requests to be compelled through the office
12    to come to good faith (?). And they are: Syrianus, former principalis, for five pounds of gold,
13    [and] … so-and-so, and … for 5 pounds of gold; they shall be reminded through the office either to acknowledge what is due outside of trial, or, if they object, to be tried
14    in the court. Flavius Romanus son of Iakob, Fl…, I summon Syrianus, former principalis, for five pounds of gold.
