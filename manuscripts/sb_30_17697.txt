[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the blessed Justus from the city of Oxyrhynchus, greeting[s].
2     I acknowledge to have received from you from this point already for my necessary need gold [no]mismata four from karat[ia]
3     [twenty-t]wo and a half; it comes to gold nomismata 4 from karatia 22 and half,
4     [and for them] I acknowledge to provide to you wheat artabas [eighty] clean, sifted
5     [with just] measure in the month of Pauni of the present [fourteenth] indiction, of crops fifteenth [harvest]. [Valid the document] simply written and questioned I acknowledged.
