[TRANSLATION]
1     Appella to Lucretius
2     to the most honored, greetings.
3     First of all I pray you to be healthy with your unbewitch'd horse. Being asked, brother,
4     you will send to me through Krinolaus
5     or [through] Longinus, the horse to [the] sling
6     so that it may suffice; greet
7     Kasis and Serapion.
