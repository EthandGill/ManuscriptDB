[TRANSLATION]
1     Ischyra to Zosime and Parabotos
2     to both, greetings. First of all the prostration to you
3     I make before Athena. Well
4     you will do [by] writing to me the [things] concerning
5     you; I ask you, lady, keep for me the commands which [I] gave to you
6     [as orders]. Before all
7     greetings, be healthy, be elegant
8     become before all.
9     I sent to you 20 headcloths; receive a lekythos of myrrh and
10    the saffron [things].
11    Be well.
12    Greet all [people].
