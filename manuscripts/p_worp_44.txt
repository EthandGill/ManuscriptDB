[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Psanmous and Petesouchos, tax-collectors of Elephantine. Pelaias, son of Pachompchemis, whose mother is Thatres, paid for the contribution of the riverguard boat-tax and the license-tax of the 21st year on account gross 4 drachmas. The 21st year of Hadrianus Caesar, the lord, on the 6th of Payni. I, Petesouchos, have signed; gross 4 drachmas.
