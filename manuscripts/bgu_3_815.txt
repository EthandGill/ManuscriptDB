1   (1st hand) Before all things I pray
2   for your health. I want you to know:
3   your letter
4   I received. If you see that
5   Socrates, my pro-
6   curator, is causing
7   any trouble about the
8   [...], (2nd hand) make it known to me. I have
9   written to him about you, so that
10   he may attend to you in all things.

14   mother and all those in the house
15   by name.

17   If there is need of anything, I will send
18   (it) to you through Tiberianus.
