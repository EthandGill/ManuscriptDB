[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelii Makarios and Ision
2     … to the and N… greetings; we have been paid by you what you owe us
3     from the account of the scribe
4     in the name of Titouetos
5     and you received from us the securities and we have no claim against you concerning this
6     I, Aurelius Makarios, wrote the whole as aforesaid.
