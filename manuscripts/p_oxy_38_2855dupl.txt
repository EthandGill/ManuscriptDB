[TRANSLATION]
1     To the guild of the poll-tax-registrars of the splendid
2     and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through
3     one of them, Aurelius Diogenes also called Hermeias,
4     of the present 7th year and 6th year,
5     from Aurelius Kornelios son of Isidoros, his mother being
6     Thaesis, from the splendid and most illustrious
7     city of the Oxyrhynchites. I wish for the first time
8     to register with you the son of my friend
9     and the deceased Arianos, a veteran
10    of those honourably discharged,
11    namely Aurelius Arianos, by his mother
12    Dionysia, in the quarter of Pammenes' Garden,
13    being, with respect to the present 7th year and 6th year, 13 years old
14    a twelve-drachma (taxpayer) from the gymnasium. Wherefore I submit the
15    memorandum, requesting that he be enrolled
16    [in]to the class of those of the same age, as
17    is fitting, and I swear the oath customary
18    [for] Romans that I have not lied. Year 7
19    [of the] Emperor Caesar Gaius Aurelius
20    [V]alerius Diocletianus, and year 6
21    [of the] Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
22    [V]alerius Maximianus, Germanici
23    [Ma]ximi, Pii Felices
24    [Au]gusti, Pharmouthi 9.
25    [Aur]elius Kornelios have submitted (this) and
26    [have sw]orn the oath as set forth above.
27    [Pamm]enes' Garden
28    [Areia]nos son of Areianos, 13 years old
29    [Areian]os son of Areianos the veteran, in Tho…, through Kornelios the displayer of busts(?)
30    … in the same village.
