[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Justinianus the most glorious, Mecheir 21, 15th indiction.
2     To [so-and-so, son of] John, most reverend presbyter and superior of the
3     [northern rock] of the Antinoites, place of Apa Sabinus, from Paulus
4     [presbyter and] Hatres, both full brothers by the same father,
5     [son of so-and-so,] mother Thaesia, potters, from a hamlet called
6     …olon of the village of Pesla of the Hermopolite nome. We acknowledge, [under joint surety], that we have received and been paid in full from you the
7     [complete and] worthy price of light (jars), good, new, pitch-coated,
8     [dried,] four hundred large ones, of eight xestai each, each vessel eight xestai,
9     …of which there come light dried large (jars), 400, and these we are ready
10    [under joint surety] to furnish to your reverence in the month Pauni
11    [of the coming] first indiction, without any delay
12    [and dispute] and litigation and deduction of weight and any pretext.
13    But should the appointed term pass, if we do not deliver all the
14    [light-jars, in gold] solidi eight forthwith, all our present and future
15    property being pledged to you for this
16    [document, individually and] collectively, [as if by course of] law. This document is valid and secure, and being formally questioned
17    [we acknowledged it. Paulus] presbyter and Hatres, full brothers,
18    the aforesaid, have received and been paid in full for the light-jars,
19    [new, large,] four hundred, and we shall deliver (them)
20    [under joint surety] within the appointed term as aforesaid. Christodoros,
21    [son of so-and-so,] cleric from Antinoou, having been requested, wrote on their behalf, they
22    [being illiterate.] Kollouthos son of Pekousios, assistant from Antinoou, I witness the document, having heard from those who made the agreement, and I witness
23    [also to the] payment of the gold of the price. Flavius Phibis
24    [son of so-and-so,] …os, soldier, I witness the document,
25    [having heard] from those who made the agreement, I witness to the
26    payment of the gold of the price.
27    [So-and-so,] son of Kollouthos, from Antinoou, I witness the document, [having heard from] those who made the agreement, and I witness also to the payment of the
28    [gold of the] price. …through me…
29    The document of Katshoshou. (Coptic)
