[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the lord and master Jesus
2     Christ our God and savior and
3     of our lady the holy Theotokos
4     and of all the saints, Mesore 26, 14th indiction, in Arsinoe.
5     To Flavius Ioannes the most glorious duke of this
6     province of the Arcadians, through Apphouas the most splendid
7     riparius of this city of the Arsinoites,
8     the guild of the trade of the saddle-makers of this
9     city, through us, Menas the presbyter
10    son of Anastasios, himself also a presbyter, and
11    apa Hol the deacon, son of Phoibammon, himself also
12    a deacon, and Menas son of Georgios, and Theodoros
13    … son of …urgios
14    … l … and
15    … a … we have received, and
16    we have been paid in full by your glory,
17    through the same most splendid riparius, the price
18    … from us on account of the
19    … of … saddle-wood items
20    … on the present fourteenth indiction, in full and in entirety,
21    and for your security we have made this
22    acknowledgment-receipt for you, being valid,
23    and on being formally questioned we agreed. Menas the presbyter — it is satisfactory
24    to me in all things as set out above. Apa Hol the deacon,
25    son of the blessed Phoibammon — it is satisfactory to me
26    in all things as set out above. Amaeios
27    son of Thomas — it is satisfactory to me in all things
28    as set out above. Mark (sign)
29    sign of the same Menas
30    of the same Theodoros.
31    (numerals: 643)
32    A receipt of payment in full of the price … made by the guild of the trade of the saddle-makers to Flavius Ioannes
33    most glorious duke of the province of the Arcadians.
