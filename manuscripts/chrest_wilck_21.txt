[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemais to Zosimos her brother
2     very many greetings. Before all things
3     I pray for your health and I make your obeisance before the lord
4     Sarapis. Know, brother, that I have been assigned by lot to the Boukolia; I could not decline, because I have been well allotted. I made it known to Longinos,
5     that he should prepare everything. From the earth you will have to boil either the bricks
6     or the plaster, so that, in good health, if I come to you,
7     I may find everything ready. You know
8     the necessity of the military service;
9     and if you have helped me in this matter,
10    so that … with him
11    until the preparation is made
12    after the sowing, when you have leisure, come to me to the
13    Boukolia, that is, …
14    When you receive this letter, be with Longinos
15    at Skenai Mandrai to Besarion my friend, as you
16    yourself know him. I have at Alexandria a necessary friend
17    and he will impart everything to us.
18    Do everything to bring you away to Apollon the hunter
19    to my friend at Skenai; therefore then, brother, see that you give instruction to the man. Let Gaia Teseina supply fodder to my friend,
20    for indeed he is going to have need of it.
21    I greet my sister much and her children and Serenos and all who love us.
22    I pray for your good health.
23    Deliver to Zosimos, brother of Ptolemaios the brother.
