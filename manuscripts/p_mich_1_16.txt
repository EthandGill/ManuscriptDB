[TRANSLATION]
1     [Nikon] to Zenon, greetings. Concerning the two rams which you left for warpings, Bia asks for the cords.
2     Write therefore to us whether they must be given. For he said he has need of warp-wool and on account of this is delaying. And I
3     and previously I had written to you about these matters.
4     Farewell. Year 29, Pharmouthi 25.
5     [Nik]on 2 cords, if necessary for Zenon. cords
6     [to be gi]ven to Bia. Year 29, Daisios 11.
