1     to Queen Cleopatra and …
2     Herakleia of Apollonius. In the [first] year proclaimed, in the third year my deceased husband
3     Heliodorus of Sotherichis, according to an Egyptian agreement [for a] nursemaid, silver drachmas 270, [he] exchanged [his] life
4     through which he set up through the office of the record-keepers his will, arranging things concerning himself through this
5     both other things customary [he] indicated, [he] left to me things designated through this and through [the one] from both
6     [my] daughter born, Eisidora, [as] guardian without account, managing each thing as I may choose
7     no one of those from whichever side of the family being nearest permitted to hold [the] guardianship nor to [register/claim], and [he] additionally secured these [matters with a] definite [stipulation] when Eisidora
8     [before me having] life [and] depart, if his brother Alexander
9     [the des]ignated through the same nursemaid dowry [of] silver drachmas 270 within sixty days
10    [not return] to [me] the appointed things belonging to her, which [are:] in the village of Ibion …
11    [of Arsamou] … arouras 25, and in the metropolis inside the new Akorios enclosure a fourth
12    [part of a house and] atrium and courtyard and other of all the accompanying things of all kinds
13    … [and in the] sanctuary in the same city of the god Esep, the greatest, sacred shrine and
14    [of all the accompanying things] a fourth part, and in the Sarapidia in the same city goose-keeper's [place] a fourth
15    [part, to be subject to] these [matters]. If I do not receive [them], I am to be mistress of all things just as [Heliodorus indicated in the] will and in the other designated stipulations [I] agreed […]. [Now] after another time Eisidora changed [her] life,
16    [but he paid] attention [to the designations] in no way. Wherefore wishing through [a petition] to recover [and] to proceed against this [matter], for which [I] also request [you] to order [the]
17    [petition to send to the officials] in charge [of] ch[arg]es and Theon the assessor, so that having [secured her into a settle]ment [up to this time] they hold [her] in arrest, and through the
18    [court officer to deliver to the accused] Alexander the copy
19    [of the petition, so that] … [he] to [do] within the appointed sixty days
20    …, there let him know me to be mistress
21    … [with] nothing more remaining to [the]
22    [Alexander] … of all resources. I request also
23    … to [be done] and this
24    … [with the] completed signature
25    … yielding to others,
26    [which if I prefer. When this happens I will be] benefited. Farewell.
