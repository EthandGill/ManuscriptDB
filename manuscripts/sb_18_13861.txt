[TRANSLATION]
1     Diogenes to Demetrius, greetings. Know that we have been prevented from working
2     by Ptolemy son of Menestheus and Ptolemy
3     son of Heraclides, the ones calculating the subscription
4     the two drachmas to the measurer, and claiming
5     you are charged with pottery
6     seventy drachmas, 140, and of empty things, 10 drachmas, so as to be 150 drachmas.
7     You therefore will do well, taking care that we not be diverted. And concerning vine-shoots
8     will not endure bringing in
9     [the] monthly official, if he does not receive bronze. Farewell.
10    You [therefore] take care that the wood will be bound, and if anywhere
11    is brought up, make clear to me.
12    To [Demetrius]
