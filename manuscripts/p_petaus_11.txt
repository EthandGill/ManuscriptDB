[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonius, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2     from Petaus, village-scribe of Ptolemais Hormou and other villages.
3     In answer to what was enjoined upon you by Appianus, the strategos before you,
4     from a letter written to him by Vessidius
5     Rufinus the most excellent dioiketes, with reference to a petition
6     submitted to him by Aphrodisios son of Souchas, accusing Ischyrion, village-scribe of Tamauis,
7     on the ground that he is a debtor and destitute
8     and illiterate: I declare that Ischyrion goes by the name Ischyras son of Hatres son of
9     Heron, his mother being Taneseus, from Ptolemais Hormou, to whom
10    there belongs what he registered in the present 24th year,
11    in the month Phamenoth 24, through the record-office of acquisitions,
12    which he says came to him from the inheritance
13    of his parents by the right of seniority:
14    one-half share of the below-written (property),
15    of paternal (property) in the village, of a two-thirds share of a
16    quarter share of a vacant plot, so that there falls to him a twelfth share worth 300 drachmas,
17    and likewise a two-thirds share of a quarter share of a vacant plot, so that there falls
18    to him a twelfth share worth 40 drachmas,
19    and a two-thirds part of an eightieth share … of a house and courtyard,
20    so that there falls
21    to him a sixth of a twentieth share worth 600 drachmas,
22    and likewise of another two-thirds part of a half share of a house and courtyard,
23    so that there falls to him a sixth share worth 400 drachmas,
24    and about the village likewise a two-thirds part of a catoecic holding
25    of 1 1/4 arouras, so that there falls to him 3/12 (of an) aroura worth 120 drachmas,
26    and about Tanchoiris likewise a two-thirds part of a catoecic holding of 1/2 1/8 aroura
27    and a quarter share of an assessment of 3 arouras, so that there falls to him … aroura
28    and of an assessment of 1/24 aroura, worth 140 drachmas;
29    of maternal (property) in the same village a third part
30    of a house and courtyard, so that there falls to him a sixth share worth 700 drachmas,
31    and about Tanchoiris of sacred land, of 2/3 1/2 1/4 arouras, so that there falls to him
32    1 1/4 1/8 aroura worth 700 drachmas — total on the whole, of valuation, 3,000 drachmas — on the ground of which
33    he has both been sent to office by lot and been allotted to the village-scribeship,
34    and that he is not illiterate either, but
35    signs (documents) which he submits to the strategos and other
36    records of the village-scribeship.
37    Year 24, Pachon 7.
