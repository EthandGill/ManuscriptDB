[TRANSLATION]
1     [We, Andreas the ma]n of Touïchipr…, the son of the [late (blessed)] … [and Se]uerus the son of the late (blessed)
2     … to write to Apa Koltheos the … that we owe (chreostein) you … six holokottinoi (solidi) of gold … of the city. These then we [hold ready that we may give them] to you as grain (sim) according to the price of an artaba of grain … 2 solidi. These then we hold ready [that we may give them to you with]out dispute (amphiballein); these six holokottinoi (solidi), then, which we have received, we beg (parakalein) you that you release us
3     … so that we give them to you without dispute (amphiballein), [the] price (of) an artaba of grain for each one of them.
4     … [the]se then we hold ready that we may give them to you, our whole substance (hypostasis) being at risk (kindyneuein)
5     … the month Epeph, the second indiction (indiktion)
6     … you for them in this year, namely (the) second indiction; for your security (oath) then we have agreed …
7     … God almighty (pantokrator) in it; the salvation, may it rule (archein) over us according to every season (kairos)
8     … the matter; we agreed to it formerly in this guarantee (asphaleia), that if we wish to transgress (parabainein) you
9     [upon the (condition) that we give to you] … solidi (holokottinoi), without lawsuit (hap), without legal recourse (nomos), without any dispute (amphibolia). It was written in the month Pachon 19, of the second indiction (indiktion).
10    [We, Andreas and Seuerus,] we agree (stoichein) to this security (asphaleia), as it is written above, and we have set the three crosses (stauroi) by our hand, begging (parakalein) witnesses (martyres) [to bear witness] for us. The sign (semeion) of Andreas, the sign (semeion) of Seuerus. I, Hor the least (elachistos) of deacons (diakonoi), the man of Manaete,
11    [I bear witness (to) this] security (asphaleia), [I] having heard from those who drew it up. I, Anoup the priest of Manaeti, the son of the late (blessed)
12    … [I bear witness (to) this] security (asphaleia), having hear[d] from those who drew it up.
13    … the late (blessed) Philippos the man of Manaeti, I bear witness to this security (asphaleia); and I, I wrote (it).
