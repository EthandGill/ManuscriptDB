[TRANSLATION]
1     From Sarapio [the] most illustrious
2     to Colluthus the farmer the [amounts] for Alexander; provide for
3     the bread-baking of his house wheat artabas thirty
4     [it] amounts to wheat artabas 30 only in the Athenian measure Thoth 11, [indiction] 10
5     I have noted wheat artabas thirty by the Athenian measure
