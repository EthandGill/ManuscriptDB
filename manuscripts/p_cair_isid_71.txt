[TRANSLATION]
1     distributions of village of Karanidos, village magistracy [of Isidoros] Pelenios
2     first distribution, talents 105
3     second distribution, talents 40
4     third distribution, talents 23, drachmas 2
5     fourth distribution, wheat, artabas 7
6     fifth distribution, talents 46, drachmas 4,
7     chickpeas, artabas 3, beans, artabas 2, bitter-vetch, artabas 2.
8     As the village magistrates wish, they distribute. Neither [of the] thessalarii or quadr[arii]
9     inquire, because they combine with the praepositus, nor
10    of [a] clerk, nor of allotment, nor of [the] justice do they care, but as
11    they wish, they act.
12    barley, in [the] twelve-unit measure, artabas 92
