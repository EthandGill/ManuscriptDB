[TRANSLATION]
1     How many (plants) Andronikos has sent off,
2     plants, from Hathyr 30
3     up to Choiak 11, in 12 days, the pack-animals
4     which are from us:
5     the first pack-animals, 8,
6     at 8 bundles each; total 64 bundles; in the
7     bundle 50 plants; total 3,200.
8     (On the) 18th, Inaros, 9 pack-animals,
9     at 8 bundles each; total 72 bundles; in the
10    bundle 50 plants; total 3,600.
11    (On the) 21st, our pack-animals,
12    and other bundles which they brought from Mouchis,
13    36, at 50 each; total 1,800.
14    But when Dionysodoros arrived and wished
15    to cut, Pasis son of Paies
16    prevented him
17    from cutting the plants,
18    saying that he had given to Andronikos, so that he might not cut from the
19    vineyard, 4 drachmas;
20    and likewise also of the roses,
21    that he might not pull them out, he gave 4 drachmas;
22    and the promise of the eight
23    drachmas he gave to him,
24    seeing him damaging
25    the vines and being inexperienced
26    in the cutting.
27    And from Mouchis, from the
28    huckster Horos son of Phabis,
29    and one of the pack-animals
30    hired for the plants
31    so as to carry (them), having thrown down on the
32    road the plants, the woman
33    he mounted up; and the month-
34    by-month farmers
35    he sent off to the (estate) of Dikaios,
36    and on the second day they arrived. And likewise the
37    same ones brought up to
38    Philadelphia the woman
39    of him; and he himself, having abandoned (it), went off.
40    And which Dionysodoros brought back, Choiak from the 11th
41    up to the 16th, in 6 days:
42    11th, mules 5, at 10 bundles each, at 50 plants each; total 2,500;
43    16th, mules 5, at 16 each, 50 each; total 3,800.
44    And there is in Pseonnophris 40 bundles,
45    and in Krokodilonpolis 12 bundles; total 52,
46    at 50 plants each; total 2,600.
47    Total of all the plants, 1 myriad (10,000), 2,400 (i.e. 12,400).
48    But of the plants sent off by Andronikos.
49    The debt of Akestor, oxen(?), 1,000.
