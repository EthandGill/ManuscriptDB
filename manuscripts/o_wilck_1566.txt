[TRANSLATION]
1     There has paid, Petemenophis (son of) Pamonthes, for
2     embankment-tax of year 14, six dr. 4 obols, and (for) bath-tax 4½ obols,
3     total 7 dr. 2½ obols, and the surcharges on these. Year 15
4     of Domitian the lord, in the month Sebastos 32.
5     Kephalos, I have signed.
