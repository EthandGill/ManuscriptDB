[TRANSLATION]
1     From the prytanis to Sarapammon, clerk of civic (matters), gr[eetings. Deliver]
2     from the lead with you in place of the price of tin talent-weight [of talents]
3     7 which fit into the joining of the pipes of Caesar's bath[house] …
4     and to Amoites and Nepheros and those with them the lead-workers talent-weight [4 talents]
5     on account/record
6     being credited to me for the advance-loan I made talent-weight [of talents] …
7     I pray for your health, dearest.
8     Year 1, Epiphi 6. And deliver the lead talent-weight 4 tal[ents].
