[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemaios, scribe, to Ptolemao[i]
2     the grain-tax-collector Autodikes, greetings.
3     Measure to Poseidonios of Didymos
4     on behalf of Heraklides of Zenobios
5     the produce-tax of which he has cultivated his
6     plot near [Kerkeesis] of Polemones
7     of wheat, by measure forty-five,
8     totaling wheat, by measure forty-five.
9     Year 1, Pauni 19.
10    of [muddy] grain.
