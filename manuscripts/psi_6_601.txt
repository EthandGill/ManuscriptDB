1     Philokrates to Zenon [greetings]. [It would be well, if you] are well; but we ourselves are strong. Know that we [are being treated] by Apollonios
2     to Diospolis the great... [all] the least things. For he ordered
3     Apollonios... dissolved... but as for us... to Diospolis, so that I might arrange
4     and the prices to have been handed over... [to] send with Apollonios
5     ... us meeting
6     so that if he inspects... to the sent things... to the sizes and the
7     thicknesses... to Lykon... one especially and to
8     bran and to baggage-rent prices... barley... we have from Straton the steward.
9     We took care of them as best we were able, and it happened... through you until Kriton came ordering to set out... alone Apollon[ios] having met us ordered from the
10    golden [coin] coming down... to take/arrange... I (am) so that having arranged speedily I may come... for me... is greater... farewell.
