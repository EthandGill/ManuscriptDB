[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the (boundary) since …
2     … upon that house, this one which … [A]ndre[as] son of Sik … le … oi
3     … upon … which … e
4     … beget … the half [of] the house
5     [th]a[t] (house) [an]d the half [of] the vaulted chamber to the inner part
6     upon it of that house, from its foundations up to its top, with all its appurtenances which pertain
7     to it; the boundaries of that house are these. The sout[h:]
8     I, Takoum, the one writing first; the middle: of the children
9     of Antonios; the east: the courtyard of Epiphanios the brother
10    of our father; the west: the public street; the place
11    where the door of the proprietors opens — its boundaries are these,
12    of the share of the house which belongs to me, of Lbesa (Abesa), I made acquire (?); from now then
13    to you, you Abesa (and) Tamo my sister, who shall come in
14    and shall be lord and shall be master of the half
15    of that whole house, according to the manner that we revealed them
16    out to you in the settlement, according to the agreement which I first uttered above; (whether) to beget it for you (or) to beget it for you again,
17    to administer it, to manage it, to beautify
18    it, to return it, to give it up to the … (?), to donate it,
19    to cede it, to leave it to children, to bestow it
20    on heirs, to use it in any
21    manner that pleases, at any time, the one who shall come out against you ever,
22    neither brother nor sister nor child nor son of one nor
23    son of two nor on (the side of) my father nor on (the side of) my mother, beside us,
24    any authority and power — first he of that kind
25    shall benefit nothing, primarily he being a stranger to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit; afterwards he shall pay to the account of the fine thirty-six (?)
26    holokottina of gold, the authority being to exact (it) upon us
27    at that time; afterwards he shall come in and be bound to this written deed of freedom-from-care and of settlement, secure for you and for those who come after you; afterwards it being firm,
28    having power, in any place where they shall produce it,
29    and they shall question (us), my acknowledging.
30    I, Takoum daughter of Zacharias,
31    the one who wrote it first above, consent. I, Philochristos the least deacon, wrote on her behalf, and I bear witness.
32    I, Aristophanes son of Iohannes, bear witness.
33    I, Zegeel son of the blessed
34    Mathias, bear witness. I, Peshate
35    son of Daniel, bear witness. Thomas son of Victor — I wrote
36    on his behalf because he does not understand (letters) — and I bear witness.
37    I, Nohe the presbyter and superior, bear witness according to the request of Ta[koum].
38    [ ] Through me, Ioanna [s]on of Iohannes from the fortress of Memnonia, it was made.
