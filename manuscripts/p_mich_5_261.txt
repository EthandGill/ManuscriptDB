[TRANSLATION]
1     Roll (tomos).
2     In the twenty-second year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
3     in the month Peritios on the 30th, Choiak 30, in Tebtynis of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. Cheos son of Harmiysis acknowledges,
4     about twenty-five years old, with a scar on the left thumb, to Mieus son of Phomsais, about 40 years old, with a scar on the right shin, that he has sold to him the paternal sacred land belonging to him, three arouras, or however many there may be at most, of which the neighbours are: on the south
5     the allotment of Orsenouphis son of Horouanchios, on the north of Marcus Julius
6     Hyllos the allotment, on the west the so-called bed (channel), on the east formerly the allotment of Psosneus daughter of Tapesouris; and that Cheos has received from Mieus the agreed price
7     in full, complete, hand to hand, out of the house, and that he will guarantee,
8     he, Cheos, and those acting for him, to Mieus and
9     those acting for him, the matters concerning the sale, with every guarantee,
10    free both from all public debts and
11    from all surcharges and dyke-taxes and the artaba-tax
12    and surpluses and the village-impost and
13    all the other things demanded
14    from the times above down to the past twenty-first year and that very past twenty-first year
15    of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, and from private (claims) and
16    every laying-of-claim for all time.
17    And his mother Thaesis daughter of Psosneus consents, about 40 years old, with a scar
18    on the right knee, to the sale and that she will not come against it. Subscribers
19    for the acknowledging parties: Herakles son of Herodes, about 50 years old, with a scar on the right wrist; for the other,
20    Ischyrion son of Patron, about 25 years old, with a scar in the middle of the forehead under the hair.
21    Cheos son of Harmiysis, I acknowledge that I have sold to Mieus son of Pomsais the paternal sacred land belonging to me, three arouras or however much there may be,
22    and all that goes with them, in Tebtynis,
23    of which the neighbours are: on the south the allotment of Orsenouphis son of Anchious,
24    on the north the allotment of Hyllos, on the west the bed (channel), on the east formerly the allotment of Psosneus daughter of Tapesouris. And
25    I have received the price in full and I will guarantee (it) free from all public (dues) and
26    the artaba-tax and the dyke-tax and every kind of (impost)
27    from the times above down to the past twenty-first year and that very
28    twenty-first year of Tiberius Caesar
29    Augustus. My mother Thaesis daughter of Psosneus consents, as is set forth. Orseus wrote on their behalf
30    because they do not know letters.
31    Mieus son of Phomsais: the sale has been made to me as is set forth. Ischyrion son of Patron wrote on his behalf because
32    he does not know letters.
33    Acknowledgement of sacred land of 3 arouras, of Mieus
34    against Cheos.
