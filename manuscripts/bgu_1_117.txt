# HGV-EN: (m. 3) 4 (m. 1) To [Harpokration alias Hi]erax, royal scribe [of the Arsinoites nome, Herakleides division, from D]ioskor[os, son of NN], the son of Herakleid[es, mother NN, registered in the quarter Phremei. There belongs to me in the quarter [– – – a house (?) and] a small courtyard, in which I live and register [myself and] my family for the house-by-house registration of the past 28th year of [Aurelius Commodus (?)] <in the same quarter, in which> I registered <myself in the house-by-house registration of year 14 as well>. And I am [Dioskoros, the above mentioned], farmer, 68 years old, and (I register) his (sc. my) wife Thai[sarion, freedwoman of Thaisarion, daughter of Souchas, son of Mysthes, mother Heraklou[s x years old, and the] children of both of us, our son Horion, farmer, [x years old, and NN] another son, scribe, 17 years old, and [Satorneilos, another] son, mother Sarapous, daughter of Horion, farmer [x years old, and] a daughter Harpokratiaina, mother Thaisarion, the above mentioned [x years old], who is the wife and sister by the same father of Satorneil[os] and the son of both of them Sat[orneilos, x years old][ and the daughter Satornile, 14 years old, and Artemido[ra alias Diosko]rous, another daughter of Dioskoros, the [above mentioned][, mother Thais]arion, 20 years old, and Tasoucharion, another daughter, [x years old, and NN], father unknown, 29 years old, Sarapous, father unknown, 8 years old. Therefore I submit (this registration). [Year 29] of Marcus Aurelius Commodus A[ntoninus Caesar], the lord, Mesore 27.
