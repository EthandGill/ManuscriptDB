1     [Year 44,] Phaophi 15, in Pathyris, in the office of Asklepiades the agoranomos.
2     [There lent] Apollonia daughter of Ptolemaios, a Cyrenaean, with the guardian (who is)
3     [her hus]band Dryton son of Pamphilos, a Cretan, of those of the de[tachment], hipparch over men and of the diadochoi, to Solon
4     [also called Sl]eis son of Horos and to Harmais son of Horos, Persians of the epigone,
5     [and to Tebos] daughter of Phageris, a Persine, with the guardian (who is) the af[orewritten] Sleis her son, one talent of copper
6     [and four thousand drachmas, intere]st-[free] for five mon[ths from Phao]phi until Mecheir 30 of the 44th year; and let
7     [this loan be repa]id by the borrowers to Apollonia
8     [in the month Me]cheir 30; and if they do not repay within the
9     [appoint]ed time, let them forthwith pay
10    [one-and-a-half-fold,] and for the time exceeded, interest
11    [at two-drachm]a (rate) on the mina for each month; sureties
12    [for one anoth]er for the discharge of all (the things) written
13    [throu]gh the loan are the borrowers themselves;
14    [and let the right of exe]cution belong to Apollonia, from the borr[owers] and from each and every one of them, and
15    [from whichever] she may choose, and from all their property,
16    [in al]l (cases), just as by judgment.
17    I, Areios, the (agent) of Asklepiades, have transacted.
18    Loan of Apollonia to
19    Sleis and others, one talent of copper, 4000 (drachmas).
