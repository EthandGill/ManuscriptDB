[TRANSLATION]
1     (the numerical sign) 643
2     In the con[sul]ship of Flavius Olybrios the most glorious, Pauni 18, of the sixth indiction.
3     To the most noble Sibylla and Heraeis, on behalf of the name of Sourous, daughters of the
4     bless[ed mem]ory of Mousaios, through the most admirable Apollos son of Isakios, assistant
5     of the village of [A]ph[ro]dite of the Antaiopolite nome, from Aurelius Biktor son of Papnouthos,
6     his mother being Tsen[bi]k[to]r, originating from the same village of Aphrodite of the same nome, greeting.
7     I acknowledge willingly and of my own choice to have leased from your admirableness
8     for a three-[year] time, reckoned from the produce, with God, of the coming seventh indiction and of it,
9     the [es]tate [bel]onging [to yo]u of the holding called Pia Peto Ouesos, with cis[t]ern and reservoir an[d]
10    plants and [palm]-trees and all rights within and without according to the boundaries, on condition that I
11    farm this, and perform and discharge all the cultivation with my own animals
12    and farm-hands. And on behalf of this I am ready yearly to provide the rent,
13    the (charges) imposed of the public gold-tax and of the grain-transport [of the] embolē and freight,
14    in full and even in drought, and to give to you, apart from the embolē and the gold-tax, toward the completion
15    of the same rents, twenty-eight artabas of wheat by the rent-measure of the most magnificent
16    co[m]es [Am]monios. And I shall pro[vide] also fifty cheeses and four small (jars) of lepsanē
17    [on behalf of the] herd [under me, and ch]ree's worth of gold, one trim[esio]n; and unh[indered]ly I have to take water
18    [from the there] cist[e]rn and reser[voir] for the [s]eparate and adjoining ar[our]a[s in t]he sam[e] place without re[n]t.
19    [The lease shall be v]ali[d] and sec[ure], and being formally questioned I ac[knowledged]. And I have the palm-trees, and do not take
20    [any one. ] Aurelius Papnouthios the aforesaid, I have leased as is se[t f]orth.
21    [ Aurelius A]pollo[s] son of Dio[skor]os, having been requ[ested], I wrote on his behalf, lett[e]rs,
22    [he not knowing them]
23    Through me, Kyros the notary, it was written, God willing.
