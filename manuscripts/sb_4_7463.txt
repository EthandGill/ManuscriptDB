[TRANSLATION]
1     Ph… [about] 40 years, scar on left forearm
2     Ph… Petearpsenesiós, shepherd of sheep and livestock, to those [under] Ammonius the strategos
3     of the Arsinoite [nome]; I swear by Tiberius Claudius
4     Caesar, the Revered, Germanicus
5     Emperor, [that] indeed I do not have with
6     myself Esoúrin, [son] of Nekferót, shepherd from Philadelphia of the
7     Heracleid division, and nothing
8     to have falsified; if I swear rightly by me
9     may it be well, but if I swear falsely, the opposite;
10    Sarapion the scribe wrote for him, he saying not to know letters.
11    Year twelve of Tiberius Claudius
12    Caesar, the Revered, Germanicus
13    Emperor, Choiach ninth and tenth.
