[TRANSLATION]
1     To Valerius Ziper, the most eminent prefect of Herculia of Egypt,
2     from Aurelius Isidoros son of Ptolemaios from the village of Karanis of the Arsino[ite nom]e. In the
3     month Phamenoth of the consulship of Sabinus and Rufinus, my lord prefect, Kastor and his
4     brother Ammonianos from the same village, being in need, were supp[lie]d by me with grain,
5     thirty-two artabas, and eighteen artabas of beans, and they made me bo[nd]s for these on the security of three head of cattle; and on the same day their [fath]er-in-law Tomis
6     placed with me written agreements through which he undertook the guaranty of the cattle, on condition that, if they did not make the delivery of the pulse to me within the month Pauni, I should clearly have the ownership of the cattle,
7     in accordance with the bonds. Since therefore Kastor has departed this life and Ammonianos happens to be in flight,
8     and the aforesaid Tomis and his brother Demetrios, who happens to be the husband of Atolas, the sister of my debtors, have taken possession of everything left by those men — I mean cattle,
9     eight in number, and houses and the rest — having provided me nothing, neither the
10    pulse nor the cattle, though so long a time has passed, and despising me,
11    on this account I have recourse to your manliness, begging and entreating that, if it
12    seem good to you, you order, through whomever you may approve, that my opponents be compelled either to give back the pulse or to make me restitution of the three cattle, in accordance with the bonds, and
13    I shall acknowledge thanks to you. Farewell. Pharmouthi 18.
14    The praepositus of the district, having judged between you, will furnish you the appropriate
15    assistance concerning what is owed. Sheet 68.
