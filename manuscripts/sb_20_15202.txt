[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: … Mecheir … Aurelius Ioannes … of the mother … from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome to Aurelius Kyrillos … from the same village, greetings. I acknowledge that I have received and been paid by you the full price of sweet new wine of one hundred and forty knidia, a total of 140 knidia of sweet new wine, and I am ready to provide them to you at the time of vintage of the God willing … indiction, by the big measurement … in the new empty (containers) … I have drawn up this document for your security with my subscription and of the following witnesses, and having been asked the formal question, I gave my consent. I, Aurelius Ioannes … the aforesaid, agree and shall pay you back as stated above. I, Aurelius Anouphis … witness to the debt having heard it from the one who established it. † I, Aurelius Sorak, son of Kollouthos, witness to the debt having heard it from the one who established it. † I, Aurelius Teag…, witness to the debt having heard it from the one who established it. † Written by me, Abraham … a total of sweet new wine double 9
