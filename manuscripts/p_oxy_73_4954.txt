[TRANSLATION]
1     [G]ermanicus the Emperor
2     in portions of silver, twenty
3     four drachmas, having snatched [them]
4     my garment worth sixteen silver drachmas
5     so as to be forty silver drachmas; therefore I request [you] to obtain satisfaction against him as may seem good to you.
