[TRANSLATION]
1     Between Harsiesis and Arsas, both
2     sons of Petosiris, and Myronous also called Ptolema, through her husband Theon, in the presence of Apollonios son of Ischyrion, former exegetes, presiding pompagogos and superintendent of the garlands,
3     and Phanias son of Saras, presiding high-priest,
4     both councillors of the city of the Oxyrhynchites. Since Myronous has brought a suit against the party of Harsiesis
5     and Arsas before Aurelius Achilleus, presiding archidikastes, concerning the recovery which her father gave
6     of the property belonging to the
7     brother of the party of Harsiesis and Arsas,
8     Thompsemis, who is also the former owner, a reservation having been registered in
9     the recovery to the effect that Myronous, when she became of age,
10    would consent to the recovery, and concerning
11    these matters an inquiry being before the
12    archidikastes; it was resolved by common agreement,
13    in the presence of the registered
14    mediators, so that there should be no lawsuits
15    and they should be released from the affairs, that Myronous, receiving from the party of Harsiesis
16    and Arsas, for the expenses she has incurred, one talent of silver and 5,000 drachmas,
17    should consent to the recovery made to the
18    brother of the party of Harsiesis and Arsas, Thompsemis, [who is] also the former owner,
19    and not bring a charge
20    or proceed against either him
21    or those who have purchased from him, so that the cession to the
22    party of Harsiesis and Arsas should be valid … 18 …
