[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Gerontios, praepositus of the 5th pagus,
2     from Aurelius Isidoros son of Ptolemaios, from the village of [Karani]s.
3     Although I possess very much land and am occupied with farming,
4     and have no quarrel with anyone in the village, but
5     keep to myself, I do not know how it was that, on the preceding
6     day, in the middle hours, while I was in the fields, Kalas son of Onnophris
7     and Seuthes son of Timotheos and Heras son of Seuthes and Paesis son of Apolinarios
8     and Sotas son of Achillas and Serenis son of Ariston, from the same village,
9     having nothing against me, weighed down with much wine and
10    emboldened by the wealth that surrounds them, set upon our house with Arabian (cudgels) and, having broken down the doors
11    and got inside, as many utensils as we had for service
12    they smashed, carrying them off, and whatever they found in the place
13    they took; and had not our people, being women, resorted to shouting, those from
14    the village would have caused the collapse of the place. Perhaps they were even plotting greater things
15    against them. Wherefore, since such great shamelessness of theirs
16    requires legal prosecution from the laws, both the
17    doors and the smashed utensils being plain to see,
18    of necessity I submit this document in writing, asking that they be brought before you
19    so that the consequences in accordance with the laws may be carried out, concerning which
20    I shall keep silence in these matters. Farewell.
21    In the consulship of Sabinus and Rufinus, the most illustrious,
22    Phaophi 27.
