[TRANSLATION]
1     … and the damage and nothing
2     less let this lease be valid. And the lease being secured, let Haryotes pay to Nikon
3     each year the prescribed rents at the threshing-floor in the month Apellaios, in Egyptian (reckoning) Pauni, or
4     at the end of each year or whenever the release of the crops is granted, new wheat, clean and unadulterated
5     … not black wheat, of that which shall be on this land, by the ten-choinix measure
6     in the market in Philadelphia, by measure and just leveling-rod, and for himself … having established …
7     … from his own (means). And if he does not pay it, let Haryotes at once forfeit to Nikon for the
8     wheat, for each artaba which he does not pay, one thousand drachmas of bronze coinage, or the highest current
9     price at Philadelphia. And all the royal taxes, as many as are due on this land, let the … measure out …
10    … and if anything is exacted, let Haryotes, having shown matching receipts, reckon it toward the rents,
11    and of the produce and crops let Nikon be master, or those acting for him, until he recovers what is his own.
12    Witnesses: … krates, son of Bithys, Thracian of the epigone …
13    pal … tou … n … nth … i … okl … es …
14    I, Nikon, acknowledge that I have leased out and have deposited the contract as valid
15    with the contract-keeper Timokrates …
16    a hundred-aroura man, wrote on behalf of Nikon because he says he does not know letters.
17    I, Haryotes, acknowledge that I have taken the lease as it is written above and have deposited the contract as valid with
18    Timokrates. I, Leonides, Thracian of those of Galestes, an eighty-aroura man, wrote on behalf of Haryotes,
19    having been requested by him because he says that he does not know letters.
