[TRANSLATION]
1     Artemidoros to Alexander his brother, greetings
2     and health; I have written to Amarakos the [matters] of Phebiche
3     to transport cargo to Sesftha of the lower toparchy
4     of the Oxyrhynchite, so that the wheat there of my father
5     may be transported, beseeching you not to leave anything out;
6     but if also a royal transport-levy happens to be imposed, it is not necessary to stop;
7     for all the transported [amounts] are 2 artabas;
8     and take care of yourself …
9     be well; in the 4th year, Mesore 16
