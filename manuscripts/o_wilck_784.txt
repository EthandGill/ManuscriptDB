1     Pisais and Psenminis,
2     overseers of the granary of the temples, to Phatres (son of) Philotas,
3     greeting. We have received the bath-tax of year 10 of Domitian
4     the lord, Pauni 30.
5     (2nd hand) Paion son of Paios, tax-farmer of the granary of the temples,
6     to Phatres (son of) Metato( ), greeting. I have received the bath-tax
7     of year 10 of Domitian the lord,
