[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of the same our most pious sovereign, year 8, Hathyr 7,
2     in the 9th indiction.
3     To Flavia Praeiecta and Apion her son, the most magnificent,
4     heirs of Apion of glorious memory, the deceased
5     patrician, landowners here too in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
6     through Menas, their servant, who acts as agent and provides
7     for their own masters, the same all-illustrious men,
8     the right of action and liability — Aurelius Hareotes, son of Mousaios,
9     his mother being Tarmouthis, originating from the hamlet of Psempekle, an estate
10    of your magnificence in the Oxyrhynchite nome, a registered
11    cultivator of it, greeting. Since need has now also arisen for the
12    estate-water-wheel under my charge called Perseboul, which draws water
13    onto arable land, of one axle, having come up to the
14    city, I petitioned your magnificence through those
15    in charge of it, so as to order that the same axle be furnished to me.
16    This having been purchased by me, and the
17    price for it having been reckoned to me in my tax-receipts,
18    a new, serviceable, satisfactory water-drawing one I received
19    for the replacement of the machine's parts on the
20    present day, which is Hathyr 7 of the 9th indiction, for the water-supply
21    of the crops of the, with God, 10th indiction. And as proof of the
22    receipt I have made this written acknowledgement,
23    which, being valid, single, written, and having been formally questioned, I acknowledged.
24    Aurelius Hareotes, son of Mousaios, the aforewritten,
25    have made this written acknowledgement for the one axle,
26    as is set forth above. Anastasios, by the mercy of God deacon and
27    notary, I write for him, he being illiterate.
28    … written acknowledgement of Hareotes son of Mousaios from the hamlet of Psempekle, of one axle.
