[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Heras,
2     Herakleides, greeting.
3     Putting everything aside, send to me this very
4     day Antinous the excellent, then his money, 16 talents 4,000 drachmas, and Porit the carpenter.
5     For his brother has died; and the workmen of the Boubalite (district) and the importers
6     of the pagus and the wool, this very day,
7     as the instructions specify.
8     If you do not send the wool this very day —
9     for they have already made me buy the mina
10    of wool for 3,500 drachmas, and moreover do not make light of it
11    that it is from the price — and if you do not send them to me this
12    very day, by God, I am shut up in confinement
13    in the Hadrianeion; so help (me).
14    Not only (these), but also the men from Paomis
15    whom you went surety for to Kopreus the praepositus of the
16    treasury officials, for the procurator is near at hand.
17    Send a few baskets (?) to the sacred anabolikon (clothing-levy), for Potamon the soldier of the procurator has come up on account of
18    the anabolikon. So you yourself send (it),
19    and even one wicker-basket and a little wool,
20    so that I too may become equal to all,
21    and may not be insulted before everyone.
22    And make Teiras come up to me
23    as the official of the prefect arranged.
24    But see that you do not neglect (it), for if I see
25    one who has neglected … .
26    To my lord brother Heras, Herakleides.
