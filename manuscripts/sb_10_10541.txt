1     Year 16 of Emperor Caesar Nerva
2     Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus.
3     [He/She] worked for 5 days for dike-works of the same year 16
4     in the highland Pauni 19-23 of Tebtunis
5     Zoilos son of Zoilos of Harmysian
6     mother Taorsenoureis.
7     Sokr sower [have] marked.
