[TRANSLATION]
1     To the sister Isidora Patosaret
2     Serenos the wine-pourer: provide
3     To the holy Theonas for offering
4     In the sixth indiction wine doubled, ten
5     Comes to wine doubled 10 only. It was written in the month Thoth 20 through me
6     Of Serenos the wine-pourer.
