[TRANSLATION]
1     in the village of Seryphis …
2     … and a whole dovecote and a house and court with appurtenances and all that belongs, of all of which the neighbours are: on the south, Tasot[as'];
3     on the north, a canal; on the east, a public street; on the west, the empty plots of others. And the price agreed between one another for
4     all the aforesaid, of Imperial silver coinage,
5     twelve thousand drachmas, which are two talents of silver,
6     I have received on the spot, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged that it has been counted out [to me] by you through the hand in full. So that you may hold
7     and own, together with your offspring and your successors, the aforesaid property and building-plots, and
8     use and dispose concerning them as you may choose, and have power
9     to sell to others; which under compulsion I will furnish to you guaranteed for ever against all with every guarantee, and clear from cultivation of royal and domanial land and every category, and fr[om] debt and lien of all kinds, public and private; and the grain-bearing and vine-bearing lands free from labour-service and the dyke-maintenance for water-guarding, and moreover
10    from the public taxes paid on their behalf and from all impositions and apportionments of every kind up to the intercalary days, and the intercalary days themselves of the present 1st year, because the (dues) from the coming 2nd year for these belong to you the buyer, to whom also belong all the taxes from the same coming 2nd year; and the one who shall come
11    or make a claim I will repel at once at my own expense,
12    as in consequence of a legal judgment. The sale is valid, written in triplicate, [which whenever]
13    you choose you shall register through the record-office, not requiring [any further consent of] mine nor [any other approval of mine, because from here] …
