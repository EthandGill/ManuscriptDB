1     There has been measured into the granary, of the produce of year 30 of Commodus
2     Antoninus Caesar the lord,
3     Pauni 18, for Charax, in the name of Senphatres the elder
4     (daughter of) Psentanouris, of wheat a third and a twelfth, total wheat ⅓ 1/12, and in the name
5     of Panameus (son of) Asklas, of wheat 1/8, total wheat 1/8, and in the name
6     of Psansn( ) (son of) Phthouminis, of wheat 1/12,
7     total wheat 1/12. I, Pe..( ), have signed.
