[TRANSLATION]
1     Valerius Petermouthis, soldier, through me Aurelius Sarapion son of Sarapion, of the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, of those from the ephebate,
2     of the sacred victors, to Aurelia Eudaimonis also called Apollonia, matron with the stola,
3     greeting. I acknowledge that I have sold to you henceforth (the loom) which I myself also bought from Aurelius Sarapammon son of Thonis, from the city of Oxyrhynchus, in the current year, in the month
4     Mecheir 8, a Tarsian-weave loom of length ten cubits, breadth six cubits, and all the equipment that is there where it lies, however much there may be,
5     of the price agreed upon by us with one another, of silver of imperial
6     coinage, two talents and one thousand drachmas, which here I received
7     from you in full by [h]and, concerning which counting-out, being formally questioned
8     b[y] you, I acknowledged; which loom and equipment you shall carry
9     henceforth from where it lies and transport wherever you wish without hindrance,
10    and you shall hold and have ownership with (your) offspring and those who shall receive (it) from you, and have the power to use and lease and sell as you choose,
11    an[d] I shall furnish you (it) secure forever against all
12    by every guarantee, and the one who comes against [you] I shall remove forthwith at my own expense, just as by judgement. Valid is the sale,
13    written singly, and being formally questioned I acknowledged. Year 14
14    and year 13 of our lords Diocle[t]ian a[n]d Maximian
15    Augusti and year 6 of our lords Constantius and
16    Maximian the most illustrious Caesars, Epeiph 12,
17    in the consulship of Anicius Faustus and Virius Gallus.
18    Valerius Pathermoutis, through me Sarapion,
19    have sold the loom and all the equipment,
20    and I have received of the price two talents of silver
21    and one thousand drachmas in full, and I shall guarantee as
22    is aforesaid, and being formally questioned I acknowledged.
