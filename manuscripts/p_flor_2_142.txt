[TRANSLATION]
1     From Alup[ius].
2     Since I have a commission
3     concerning a necessary friend, let care become yours
4     [to purchase] two female donkeys
5     tall, unblemished, [having] agreed [on]
6     the fair price so that neither the seller suffer loss nor we suffer greater loss
7     than the price when
8     I myself inspect them immediately
9     [the transaction being] completed to you
10    I wish you to be well.
11    I have signed
12    To Heroninus, manager Thrasô.
13    Year 12, Hathyr 29
