[TRANSLATION]
1     In the nineteenth year of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajanus Optimus
2     Augustus Germanicus Dacicus Parthicus, in the month Kaisareios, the 4th of the intercalary days, in
3     Karanis of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge to one another
4     Pnepheros son of Satabous, about forty-three years old, with a scar on his left forearm on the inner side,
5     and Ptolemaios son of Phanomgeus, about twenty-six years old, with a scar on the third finger of his left hand,
6     that they have divided between themselves from now for all time the cultivation of what they farm: around Patsontis, of royal land ten arouras one-eighth one-thirty-second one-sixty-fourth, and from the Anthiane estate two and a half arouras; and around Karanis, of royal
7     land one aroura one-sixteenth, on the whole of the surveyed area or however much it may be; and from which they have made between themselves, of their own free will, by lot of division, that there has fallen and been chosen
8     to Pnepheros, for the portion falling to him, from the lands around Patsontis
9     six arouras one-eighth one-thirty-second one-sixty-fourth in six parcels, of which there are
10    in the Pentekonta three parcels of three arouras, and in the (parcel) of the Hippeis
11    in the hollow one parcel of one aroura one-eighth one-thirty-second one-sixty-fourth
12    [and under] the new canal one aroura, and in the Tatnoue one aroura; and to
13    [Ptol]emaios, for the portion likewise falling to him around Patsontis of royal land
14    [four arou]ras in three parcels, of which the first is in the Strategikon of arouras
15    [two and] a quarter, and the second in the Geeinoi of one aroura, and the third in the Tatnoue
16    [of an aroura] a half and a quarter; and the two and a half arouras of the Anthiane estate
17    [and the] around Karani[da of royal land] one [aroura] [a six]teenth …
