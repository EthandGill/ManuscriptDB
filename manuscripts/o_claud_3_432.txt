r.1    Φίλητος Ἀμμωνίου Κανωπᾶτι χαίρειν.

r.6    ἐπιφέροντι. ἔτους κβ Αὐτοκράτορος
r.7    Καίσαρος Τραιανοῦ Ἁδριανοῦ

r.9    Φίλητος ἀπέσχον ὡς πρόκιται.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Philetos, [son] of Ammonius, to the Kanopite, greetings.
2     [I acknowledge] that I have borrowed from you a loan
3     [by] hand of silver imperial currency [drachmas] 12, which I will also repay in the coming
4     [garment] month of Tybi of the twenty-second year of Hadrian
5     Caesar, the lord, but if I do not pay well, it is permitted to you to retain the garment given to me, and this document shall be valid for everyone
6     bringing [it]. Year 22 of the Autocrator
7     Caesar Trajan Hadrian
8     [Augustus], Athyr 10.
9     Philetos has received as mentioned above.
