1     To the district-ruler of the district [of Mohr]es
2     From Polydeuk[es son of So]tos of Maron[, m]other Heraklous daughter of Dioskoros [registered] upon the [di]strict of [O]ther Goose-herds. I possess [upon the district] of Mohires a half-share of the paternal half-share of a house in which I register the undersigned inhabitants for [the examination] of the passed [9th year] of Antoninus
3     Caesar the [lord], household registration upon the district of Mohires for which also on the [16th] year [registration] I registered, and they are: Ptolemaeus
4     [son] of Mystos of Pr…os, mother [Theonid]os, sister
5     of the father, age 40…, and his wife Thaisan, also known as Thasion, daughter of Dionysios of Didym[os], mother Thasios, sister of the father, age 48, registered in the 16th year upon the same district of Mohires, and from both a son Satyros
6     registered in subsequently-born [children], age 6, and daughters Thaisarion, age 3, and Thasion, age 1. Polydeukes [son] of Sotos, I have submitted the registration as aforesaid. Year 10 of Antoninus
7     Caesar the lord, Epeiph 13.
