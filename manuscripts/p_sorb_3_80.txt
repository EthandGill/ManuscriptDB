[TRANSLATION]
1     Ruling Ptolemy [son of] Ptolemay Soter, year 38, Pauni 3.
2     [There] swears Agathino [son] of Simono to Aristarcho [the] nomarch [in the name of] King Ptolemay
3     the [one] from King Ptolemy
4     and [from] Queen Berenice [the] Saviors
5     and Arsinoe Philadelphus and the gods
6     [the] Brothers and gods Saviors the …
7     of these parents, [that I] will preside over the gathering of the sesame
8     of the [one] in Sebennytos, and [I] will gather
9     all the produce into the royal [treasury]. [If] truly sworn, [may it be] well [for] me; [if I break the oath,] the opposite.
