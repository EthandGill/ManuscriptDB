[TRANSLATION]
1     [Eudaimonis] to Apollonios the
2     son, very many greetings.
3     I was very glad having heard that you are well
4     and your sister Soeris. From which day
5     you sent me, I looked for the Laconian
6     and I did not find but a rotten Attalian. But don't you realize that you gave a half-liter and 2 drachmas of weight for the mixture
7     the white. So you spend one pound
8     one and a stater of silver.
9     Therefore you will buy and send, [so that] it may be sent to you quickly. And I beg
10    you, remain on yourself, so that you not [sadden] me …. Greet Aline your sister. Soeris thanks you [very] much and wrote me a letter about this.
11    [Heraidos] the little one [greets] you and your mother.
12    Be well, child.
13    Choiak 24.
14    To Apollonios, general of [Apollonopolis].
