[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God the almighty;
2     Alaareth son of Abd to those from [Nestane]
3     of the region of Elou[se] of the land of Gaza. Provide [quickly] …
4     of Bani Saad son of Malech for [two] months [Almuarram] [and Safar]
5     of wheat ninety-six modii, of oil ninety-six measures.
6     It was written in the month of October, indiction 4, according to the Arabs year 55, by the hand
7     of Procopius. Total: wheat modii 96, oil measures 96, 1
8     Total: wheat modii 96, oil measures 96
