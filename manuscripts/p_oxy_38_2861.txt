[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios to Dioscurides
2     to the dearest, greetings.
3     As face-to-face I asked you [earnestly] to [make] the [demand] of the
4     names with you and
5     now I ask you further to apply yourself to the demand especially and of the
6     solid names
7     since we were forced to place all things on a table.
8     I found also Polydas
9     having demanded nothing
10    and necessarily here
11    I have remained.
12    Be well.
13    To Dis[courides from] Apollonios friend.
