1     In the name of the lord and master Jesus Christ

4     [the] holy [ones], year of Diocletian 315, Choiak 2, twelfth indiction.

6     city of Arsinoe, through Petteries, the most splendid
7     your notary, Aurelius Abraamios, owner, [man]
8     of [a pomary], son of Kosmas, with a guarantor and surety for [the]
9     payment of the following being declared my debt [to] Neilios, [son] of Menas

12    from the church [landholdings] arourae in the plain of Kokkumios
13    a pomary [set alongside tree]-bearing, of whatever it is an arouraion
14    with also the half part of [the] pit and [a] machine
15    and machine-station and mechanic [tools and] of the two
16    oxen and the storeroom … of provisions and [of] the arourae
17    of his [being turned off] and of all his [claim]
18    for as long a time [as] you wish from hence and thereafter
19    and [to] provide me [for] the public [duty of] him yearly from the fruits
20    of the together-with-god thirteenth indiction gold coin-pieces eight, two-thirds
21    reckoned, [total] gold coin-pieces 8 [and] two-thirds reckoned; and the … services; and whenever

24    [and] the …
