[TRANSLATION]
1     To Victorinianus the most eminent prefect,
2     from Pasnos son of Besas and Hermodora daughter of Besas also called
3     Dositheus, former magistrate, and the heirs of Tanainis their sister, from the most illustrious city of Panopolis. If for those
4     who have great power there were success in whatever they undertake,
5     and if they were not checked by the pursuit of the laws,
6     the life of ordinary folk would become unlivable. Now the matter is thus: there belongs to us a house and farmstead
7     in the first quarter of the city, in common with the co-owners
8     … according to the shares, and we do not know in what way
9     [the] … sons of … the fisherman, whose names are …
10    [and] Prostates and Marcellus and P…
11    … Pachoumios, son of a fisherman …
12    … having in their hearts neither the fear of the
13    court, but trusting in their multitude of hands as being stronger than the laws …
14    … using no reason at all …
15    … of the house and of the farmstead …
16    … and leaving (things) upon the street. And being ordinary folk
17    [and] unable (to act) against the crowd of young men,
18    of necessity we submit to you these petitions, asking that we be vindicated, so that we may be able to obtain the due
19    vindication. And copies of these we have also submitted
20    to the scribe of the council.
21    In the third consulship of the consuls-to-be, Pachon …
22    I, Pasnos the aforesaid, have submitted (this) as aforesaid; I wrote as
23    is set forth, I, Pasnos.
