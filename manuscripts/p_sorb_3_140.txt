[TRANSLATION]
1     Stasos and Antipatos to Kallifon greetings. We have sold
2     to you a she-ass white and a male foal of hers
3     and we have received the price of it [in] silver drachmas
4     forty. Now this is the she-ass concerning which the
5     petition Stasos submitted to the king
6     … to you; but if we do not guarantee [it] to you, we shall pay [the] price one and a half [fold].
7     … Mecheir 19
