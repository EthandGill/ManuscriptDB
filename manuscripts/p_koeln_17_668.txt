[TRANSLATION]
1     Petalus, secretary of cattle-herders of Nile City, to Asclepiadis
2     to the grain-collector, greetings. Measure out to the sword-bearers of the general wheat
3     artabae two, in your measure becomes in your measure 2. Of the year 9
4     Epiphi 14
5     measure out to the troglodytes wheat, two artabae
6     in your measure, becomes in your measure 2. Of the year 9, Epiphi 14
