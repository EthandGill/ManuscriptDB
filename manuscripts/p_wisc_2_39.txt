[TRANSLATION]
1     [they have] me[asured into] the public [treasury] of produce of fourth year of Hadrian
2     Caesar the lord through Andromachous, grain-measurer, upper toparchia of Monimos
3     Hermas [son] of Taroutillios through Taroutillios father, Apeis [son] of Loukas,
4     … -ol Pathol, Diosko Apol, Apollo [son] of mother Siag artabas
5     [t]welve [and a] half choinikes six, equals artabas 12 [and a] half choinikes 6.
6     [and] through grain-measurer [Si]narus likewise of barley through village artaba
7     one, equals artaba 1.
