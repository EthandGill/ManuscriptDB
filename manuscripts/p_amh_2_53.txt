[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 3, Pachon 3. It has been ordered concerning the bank in Hermontis, at which Ammonios [administers] the tenth [tax] of the encyclios purchase according to the record of Memnon
2     and Hermias the tax-farmers, under which Asenothes the record-keeper registers Senpoheris Onnophrios, the charge of a house and
3     treasury and [a] part of courtyard, which was purchased from Lolutus [son] of Petenephotes, bronze, talents 6, charge [amount].
4     Ammonios, banker.
