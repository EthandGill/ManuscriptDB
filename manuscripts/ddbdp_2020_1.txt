r.1    Ἑρμόφιλος Τιεῦτι χαίρειν.
r.2    ἀπόστειλον ἡμῖν τὴν βύρσαν
r.3    παραδοὺς Ἄβδωι τῶι παρὰ Καλλιδήμου. σύνταξον δ’ Ἐμροῦτι

r.11   σοὶ ὅπως μὴ ἐξ ὑστέρου

r.13   ὃ ἄν σοι ἀποκριθῶσι.
r.14   ἔρρωσο. ἔτους κδ Τυβι ιγ

[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermophilus to Tieutes, greetings.
2     Send to us the hide
3     delivering [it] to Abdus who is with Callidemos. Order Emrutes
4     the mountain Arab and his sons
5     to bring the females into the
6     stable so that they might be bred; if not,
7     that they will pay to me the colts
8     which they must bear;
9     and let [him] witness before
10    Radanus and the others with
11    you so that not later
12    [he] deny [it] and write to me
13    whatever they answer to you.
14    Farewell. Year 24, Tybi 13
