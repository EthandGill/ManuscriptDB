[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Antoninus the deputy, I John the clerk [charge] you for
2     [the] fourth mandate of the tenth indiction for the debtor Callinicus through Phoebaommon the procurator
3     [gold] coin[s] twenty-two standard, amounts to gold coins 22 standard only; Flavius John
4     [the] clerk agrees with me [regarding] the warrant of the coins twenty-two standard as aforesaid,
5     [Pa]chon 27 of the 10th indiction.
6     John the clerk for the fourth mandate, gold coins 22 standard.
