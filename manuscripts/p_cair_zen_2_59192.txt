[TRANSLATION]
1     Plato to Zenon [greeting]. Of Demetrius, the one delivering to you the
2     letter, as it seems, [the father] happens to be making residences in the
3     Arsinoi[te] [no]me. So [al]so the young man wants to [do] [something] there.
4     Having learned that you are [decent], some of the friends entreated me to write
5     [to] you [concerning] him, so that you might place him with you. You will [do well therefore] having thanked us and having cared [for him], so that he might accomplish something whatever you deem suitable
6     to be fitting, and [the rest], taking care of him, if indeed he should be [useful] to you.
7     A sign that I sent to you from Sosus, of chickpeas, kriu, 2 artabas purchased at 5 drachmas each.
8     And I will try also from Naukratis, if [it] be, to purchase for you an additional 20 artabas
9     and myself will bring [it] to you.
10    Farewell.
11    Year 31, month Dios, [day] 12.
12    To Zenon.
