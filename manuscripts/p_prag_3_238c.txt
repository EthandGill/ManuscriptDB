1     Account of receipt and expenditure for Tybi,
2     the current month of the 10th year; and it is (as follows):
3     Of receipt: from the price of wine, 150 monochora,
4     at 18 drachmas each, 2,700 drachmas;
5     and from the price of wheat, 13 artabas, at 12 drachmas each, 156 drachmas.
