1     … [fellow-]relatives of [me ap]pear[ing] having been recorded [as be]ing of priestly

4     Stotoetis and Stotoetis son of Pakysis and Stotoetis son of Stotoetis and Stotoetis son of Onnophre[w]s, of the five elder priests of the five-clan group of

6     of the Arsinoe [district], referring to you lord, having submitted as you ordered, of the submitted registrations and of the release of marriage the copies. Be well I wish you, my lord, be well
7     you I wish, my lord. Year 16 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
8     Hadrian Antoninus Augustus Pious, Pachon 20. After the
9     reading Claudius Agathokleis the high-priest and in charge of the [priests]
