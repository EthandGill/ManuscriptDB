[TRANSLATION]
1     Haruotes to Bethis
2     Greetings. If the men from Horos the
3     [grain-measurer] and Petosirio[s] the oikonomos measure to Herakleides the scribe
4     [so that] to the registered by Someneus into the
5     Oasis men,
6     B the resulting measure for the month
7     of Pauni of the 7th year of wheat artabae 25,
8     approve it
9     with the consumption-measure
10    and make a receipt for him.
11    Farewell, 7th year, Tybi 13.
