[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine master
2     Flavius Justinian, the eternal Augustus,
3     Emperor, in the twenty-first year,
4     after the consulate of Flavius Basilios the most glorious,
5     in the fifth year, on the fourth intercalary day of Mesore,
6     of the eleventh [indiction].
7     Psais, son of Besios on his father's side, of the mother Tasaïs,
8     deacon from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, to the most admirable
9     Dioskoros, son of Apollos, chief man of the village,
10    from the same village, greetings. I acknowledge through this
11    my written security that I owe you
12    and am indebted, on account of the sowing and the
13    irrigation and the rent of [one]
14    aroura, in last year's grain-bearing [plots]
15    east of the road of the [place]
16    called Athanasias, near [fertile]
17    land, on condition that I hold this for [the sowing]
18    of flax, at the sowing, [God willing,]
19    expected, of the twelfth indiction, [and that I provide]
20    you, at the third installment [of the]
21    public tax of the eleventh indiction, [one] gold [solidus]
22    less two carats by the goldsmith's [standard];
23    that is, one gold solidus less two carats by the goldsmith's (standard), without any dispute
24    or postponement, my former handwritten document [being] secure and valid [even now],
25    of the … (concerning) …
26    of the other two arouras in the holding (klēros)
27    of Piahse, in accordance with the same [security].
28    And for your security I have set down [this]
29    security with the signature of the one who subscribes [for me];
30    and having been asked I consented.
31    I, Psais, son of Besios, the aforementioned, agree with me
32    as above. I, Aurelius Promauos, son of Hermauos,
33    having been asked, wrote for him the letters
34    as he does not know (letters). I, Flavius Theoteknos, son of Psais, of the rank of praepositi,
35    am witness to the deed, having heard it from the
36    issuer. I, Aurelius Abraham, son of Victor,
37    am witness to the debt, having heard it from the
38    issuer. I, Aurelius Horouonchios, son of Anouphis,
39    am witness to the debt, having heard it from the [issuer].
40    Written by me, Pilatos, with God's help, nomikos.
41    … [handwritten document] of Psais the deacon, of one gold
42    solidus less two carats, on account of …
43    … eiou.
