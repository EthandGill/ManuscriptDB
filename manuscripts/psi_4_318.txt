[TRANSLATION]
1     To my master, [my] brother
2     Machenas [from] Lathebi[us].
3     Provide to my brother Ammoni[us]
4     from [the things] which you have of mine
5     silver, one ten-thousand [drachmae];
6     [this] amounts to silver, ten-thousand, one. But see that you not
7     neglect [this], my master
8     brother. Phamenoth 28th.
9     I pray that you fare well
10    for many years,
11    [you who are the] image of legitimate [ones].
