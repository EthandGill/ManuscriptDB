[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving, consubstantial Trinity,
2     the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. In the month Thoth 27, of the 2nd indiction,
3     I, Senouthios the son of the blessed Philotheos, the steward of the holy
4     Apa Psate of the mountain of Djeme, write to Komos the son
5     of the blessed Damianos, the man of this one fortress of Djeme of the nome of this one city of Ermonet, that, concerning the matter of the tremision which Tsyros the daughter of Takoum
6     gave to the holy Apa Psate, this which she donated
7     in her testament, that it should be given in
8     to the holy Apa Psate; behold, it has come to my hand
9     through your hand, you, Komos (son of) Pshen-Damianos; from
10    now then, no man (acting) for that holy place, whether I, nor brother, nor child,
11    nor child's child, nor any heir
12    of that place, shall be able to come against you; nor (any)
13    man (acting) for me, ever; it is confirmed for you. I have established this written deed; I consent to it, firm and valid
14    in every place where it shall be produced, and they shall question me, and I shall acknowledge. I, Kosma the son of Mena, I bear witness. I, Isak the son of Kostantine, I have written on his behalf, since he does not know (how). I, Kostantine the son of Isak, I bear witness; Isak: I have written
15    on their behalf, since they do not know (how), and I bear witness.
16    I, Senouthes son of the blessed Chmtsney, the least presbyter, wrote (it).
