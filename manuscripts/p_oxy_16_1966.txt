[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Cethegus the most glorious, Pachon
2     29, of the 13th indiction.
3     To the most admirable Apollos, son of the devoted
4     Joseph, from the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     Aurelius Daniel, son of John, his mother being Heraïs,
6     a huckster from the same city, greeting. Willingly
7     I undertake to lease, from the new-moon of the
8     next month Pauni of the present year 181, 150,
9     of the thirteenth indiction, at the beginning of the fourteenth taxation-period, from the
10    property belonging to your excellency, situated
11    in this city, in the quarter of Pses, und[er the]
12    southern public colonnade, a whole workshop
13    having two cells, one within the other, of which the outer
14    door faces north; and that I will provide, by way of
15    rent, each year, of gold
16    one nomismation less two carats by the private
17    standard, making one solidus of gold less two carats, which rent I will pay yearly, half each six months, an[d]
18    [wh]enever you wish I will hand over [the same]
19    workshop with the un[in]jured occupancy. [The lease is valid,]
20    [le]ase written as a single copy, and on being formally questioned [I acknowledged.]
21    A[urelius Daniel …]
22    [I have made the lease …]
23    Lease of Daniel, huckster, of rent of one gold s[olidus less two carats.]
