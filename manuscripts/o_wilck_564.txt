[TRANSLATION]
1     Kametis, collector of money-taxes,
2     to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis,
3     (whose) mother is Senchesthotis. I have received for the assessment
4     anak( ) of Charax, year 18, one base drachma, 4 obols.
5     Year 18 of Hadrian the lord,
6     Epeiph 1. I, K(ametis), have signed.
