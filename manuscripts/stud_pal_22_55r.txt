[TRANSLATION]
1     To the beneficiary official over the places of the [most-illustrious] [governor],
2     From Pakysis son of Stotoetius [priest of the first] tribe
3     Of god [S]oknopaiou of Nesos. I accuse Pakysis Sataboutos
4     Priest from the same village
5     Having misappropriated silver and
6     Clothing and many vessels (of) Taouetis [S]toetius
7     Of Apaopis from the same village, (she) having died childless and without [having made] a will
8     And without heir, (the property) in no way fitting to him.
9     Wherefore I submit and
10    [I] ask [that he] be brought before
11    You and [that] the appropriate come to pass.
12    Pakysis, aged 56, unmarked.
13    (He) was portrayed, claiming not to know letters, through Zosimus the scribe. Year 8 of Antoninus [and Verus] [of the] lords Augustus [Armenian] Median Parthian
14    [Greatest] Athyr 6.
