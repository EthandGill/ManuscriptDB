1   Year sixteenth of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus
2   Germanicus, mo(nth) Ge[rm]anikos, (vac.) in the city of Ox(yrhynchus) of the Thebaid.
3   Thaesis daughter of Thonis son of Amithonis, mother Sintheus,
4   with (her) guardian, her paternal kinsman Onnophris son of Onnophri[s] son of Pam-
5   menes, mother Taarthonis, acknowledges to her former husband
6   Petosarapis son of Thompekysis son of Sarapion, mother Sintho-
7   nis — all from the city of Oxyrhynchus — in the street, that she has received
8   from him, of silver of Augustan coinage, four hun-
9   dred drachmas of principal, which she had brought to him on her own account as dowry,
10   under the surety-transfer of his mother Sinthonis daughter of Petosarapis son of
11   [.]bi[…], according to a contract of cohabitation, through the agorano-
12   [mei]on in the city of Oxyrhynchus, in the epagomenal days of the fourteen-
13   [th year of Im]perator Caesar Domitian Augustus
14   [Germanicus]; (and) that she has handed over to him the deed thereof on the spot,
15   [crossed out f]or cancellation, on account of the divorce of the marriage
16   [having taken place, and] that she does not and will not bring any charge against him, nor pro-
17   [ceed], either concerning the aforesaid matters or concerning par-
18   [aphernalia …] she has received (them), nor concerning any other
19   [matter] up to the present day. And he himself,
20   [Petosarap]is, acknowledges in the same street that he does not bring a charge
21   [nor will brin]g one, nor will pro[ceed] against Thaes[is]
