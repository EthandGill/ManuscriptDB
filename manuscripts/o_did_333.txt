[TRANSLATION]
1     Cornelius to Antonius, greeting.
2     Already ten times I have told you: if you have anything,
3     come to me, come!
4     For you are not a creditor of mine, [nor] do I
5     owe you anything. But if I wish, I will exact
6     from you for the days which [she] claimed.
7     Julia. For I have arranged
8     the whole household so that [they] might come to
9     [you]. For you were slow. And now, if
10    [not] your own [duties] you perform, and if five
11    [talents] you possess, I will tear [her] away
12    from you. I pray [her] to live.
