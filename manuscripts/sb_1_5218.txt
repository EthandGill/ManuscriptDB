[TRANSLATION]
1     Julius [Asclas] horseman of the ala of the Bucuntians
2     of turma Contilliana to Julius Apolinarios his own brother greetings. To manage for me my property except the date-palms and having authority of all
3     and you write to me a letter and you that something
4     you received by year, in order that you might secure
5     them. Julius Asclas. These things you receive from my mother from
6     the 19th year of Emperor Caesar Titus
7     Aelius Hadrianos Antoninus Augustus
8     Eusebous, Tybi 11th. [If] you do not wish to receive them,
9     wr[ite] to me the equivalent of this.
