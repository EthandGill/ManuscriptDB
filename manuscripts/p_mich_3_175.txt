[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ammonius Paternus, centurion
2     From Melas, son of Horion, from the village of Soknopaiou [Nesos], a priest of the god who is in the
3     village of [god]. There exists to me and my cousins [my] Phanesis and Harpagathos jointly and equally a paternal property according to mother in the
4     same village a bare place enclosed by a wall where [we place down] each year the straw which we have. When therefore one Harpagathos died intestate and his share
5     of him having come down to both of us
6     equally, yesterday, which was the 22nd, I placing down in the place our straw, Phanesis violently and arrogantly
7     coming upon me appropriated my straw, not allowing me in our share to place it, attempting from this to repel
8     me and himself alone to claim what is owed to me
9     not only but also the greatest outrage toward me he produced. Wherefore I ask you to order him to be brought in so that I might be able
10    from [you] fairness to obtain. Farewell.
11    Year 1 of [Publius] Helvius Pertinax Augustus
12    Pharmouthi 23.
