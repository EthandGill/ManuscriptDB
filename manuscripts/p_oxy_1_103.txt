[TRANSLATION]
1   To Aurelius [Th]emistokles also called Dioskouri[des, gym]nasiarch,
2   former prytanis, council(lor) of the [il]lus(trious) and most ill[ustrious city of the Oxyrhynch]ite[s],
3   through Ko[rm]ilios, assistant,
4   from the Aurelii Leonides son of Theon and Di[os]k[o]ros son of Ammonios,
5   both of the same city. Of our own free will we undertake
6   to lease, for the present 11th and ninth year only,
7   of the (land) belonging to you near Ision Panga, in the embankment-district of
8   Nesla, held in common with your brother Leukadios, one aroura
9   for sowing flax-stalk; and, in lieu of rent, to furnish you the land-
10   owner half the share of the flax-stalk arising from
11   the land; and we the lessees, in return for the farming we do
12   and the seed and all the expenses we provide,
13   (to keep) the remaining half share and the whole of the linseed,
14   all free of every risk, the public dues of the land being
15   your charge as landowner, you being master of the crops until you recover
16   what is owing. The undertaking being guaranteed to us, of necessity
17   we shall deliver the half share of the flax-stalk arising,
18   retted at the pool, without delay,
19   at the proper season, you having the right of execution against us,
20   we being mutual sureties for payment as is fitting. The undertaking is
21   valid, and on being questioned we acknowledged it.
22   In the consulship of Caecinius Sabinus and Vettius Rufinus the
23   most illustrious, Phaophi 16.
24   (2nd hand) I, Aurelius Themistokles also called Dioskourides,
25   through me Kormilios assistant, have a copy of this
26   deed. If a higher bid is offered,
27   it shall be accepted.
28   (Lease) of Leonides and Diskoros, flax-stalk-valuers(?).
