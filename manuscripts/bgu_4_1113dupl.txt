[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 16 of Caesar, Tyb[i] … 6.
2     To Protarchos,
3     from L[ucius Pomponiu]s Rufus, son of Lucius, of the Pollian tribe, and from
4     [M]arcus Cottius Atticus. Since the father of Lucius Pomponius,
5     Lucius Pomponius, by the Roman will which he made when dying, left Marcus Cottius Atticus and also Canuleius
6     as guardians of his own son Lucius Pomponius, the aforesaid,
7     and since in the meantime Canuleius [hav]ing renounced the guardianship
8     according to the jointly-sealed diplo[ma, M]ar[cu]s Cottius Atticus acted as guardian of Lucius — now Lucius Pomponius agrees
9     that neither he himself nor any other on his behalf shall proceed
10    against Marcus Cottius Atticus or Canuleius … os…ai him
11    either concerning the guardianship or [the things left by his father Luciu]s
12    Pomponius … in any way whatsoever, nor
13    concerning any other matter whatsoever, of debt or claim or [pay]ments
14    or accounts, or any written or unwritten matter from the
15    former times until the present day, on any pretext whatever, because
16    Lucius Pomponius himself has received from Marcus Cottius
17    Atticus everything that [the fath]er ever had in the dwelling or apart from it;
18    (and he agrees) that the agreed terms are valid, and that any proceeding or
19    accusation contrary to these shall be wholly invalid and inadmissible; and further that he shall in addition pay, for whatever he may accuse Marcus, both the damages and expenses and, as a private debt, one thousand silver drachmas, [and] be liable to the dam[ages]
20    and the penalty laid down concerning the agreements. We request (this). Farewell.
21    Lucius Pomponius Rufus,
22    son of Lucius, of (the tribe) Pollia, I agree according to
23    the aforesaid.
24    Marcus Cottius Atticus, I agree according to the aforesaid.
25    (I agree) concerning nothing else, written or unwritten, from the former times until the present day, or apart from it, that the agreed terms are valid, that he is liable both to the damages [and the] expenses [and to the fixed penalty].
