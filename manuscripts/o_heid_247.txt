[TRANSLATION]
1     Mesore 20 of the 23rd year in [the] name of Inaros
2     of Horos for rent of the Agora drachmas twenty
3     [and] one pentobol; total drachmas 21 pentobols. Paniskos I have signed.
4     [the] 28th in [the] name of Inarotos similarly and
5     of Talous [his] sister for land-tax of the Agora one drachma pentobol
6     total one drachma pentobol. Paniskos I have signed.
