[TRANSLATION]
1     To Kleon the vinedresser, three drachmas [and] two obols
2     To Herakleidis the vinedresser, two drachmas
3     to workers the digging
4     in the sand three, one drachma
5     another, one obol [and] one and a half [obols]; to a brick-carrier, one and a half obols
6     To Hephaestion, four obols; to a bath-house, half an obol
7     to workers three, one drachma; [a] measure of grain, one drachma [and] one and a half obols
