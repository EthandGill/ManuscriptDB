1     Year 6 of our lords
2     Valerianus and Gallienus the Pious
3     Fortunate Augustuses, Phaofi 6. [He] registered
4     Through me Melas, helper: Diogenes son of Tourbon, resident, and [another resident] of Philadelphia [received] drachmas sixteen, [total] drachmas 16 complete.
5     Aurelius Melas, helper of the silver [tax] of the village Philadelphia
6     To Aurelius Agenes, greetings. I have received from
7     you on behalf of fruits of date-palms being in [a] lot … …
8     [In] the name of Serapion, head-of-ten, silver drachmas twenty-four, [total] drachmas 24, which will go into the
9     public silver tax of the [district].
