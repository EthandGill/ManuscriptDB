1     Year 13 of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the lord, Epiphi the 9th, [he] paid to Horus and partners
2     to the tax collectors of silver taxes of Soknopaiou Nesos, Stotoetis daughter of Ankhofis of Panephremmis, mother Stotoetis, dike-tax
3     of the past 12th year, drachmas six, four obols, total: drachmas six, four obols, [and] the appended items
