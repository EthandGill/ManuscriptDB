[TRANSLATION]
1     Year sixteen [of] the [Emperor] [Caesar] Titus [Aelius Hadrian]us
2     Anto[ninus] [Augustus] [pious], Tybi 2, in Tebtunis of the [division of] Polemon
3     of the Arsinoe[ite] nome. [Kronios], son of Orsenouphis of Mieus, [acknowledges], as being about twenty years old, unmarked, [with] his own brother by the same father and same mother
4     [brother] Tephorsaites, as being about twenty-seven years old, scar on [his] right foot, and with the both
5     mother Protarout of Mieus, as being about fifty-eight years old, scar on her right knee,
6     to the two, with the guardian of Protarout's other son—those concerning Kronios
7     brother Sarapammon, as being about twenty-eight years old, scar on [his] forehead, [Kronios] has
8     from Tephorsaites a dowry, being given in marriage to Kronios by her, of silver drachmas twenty, and in parapherna
9     a [t]unic and a white pallium and a bronze basin and a tin oil-flask
10    but from the mother Protarout as a gift from now on inalienably
11    a third part of the slave Serapias belonging to her, and of the descendants that will be born from her from now on, of which the possession and control shall remain with
12    Tephors[aites] [and] [those] from her, and [she] has authority to manage concerning it
13    as she may choose. Let those marrying therefore, both Kronios and
14    [the] Tephorsaites, blamelessly; but in case of separation from each other, let Kronios return
15    the dowry and the parapherna within sixty days and let [her] recover
16    Tephorsaites what was given to her as aforesaid, [the] third part of the slave
17    Serapias, becoming [effective] for Tephorsaites the enforcement [from] both Kronios
18    and from [all] his [possessions] …
