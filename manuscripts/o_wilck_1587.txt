1     Pempsaes, tax-collector of the sacred treasury
2     to Petemenwph, elder, [son of] Pthumwnths, elder, [son of] Pamonth. I received one drachma for the price of wheat, the twenty-fourth [part]; total wheat 24 for 2 of year 16.
