[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign] of our most divine master Flavius Justinus the eternal Augustus
2     [Emperor, in the] twelfth [year], in (the year) following the second consulship of the same most serene
3     [our master, in the tenth year,] and of Flavius Tiberius our most fortunate and most benevolent Caesar
4     [the greatest benefactor,] in the third year, Thoth 10 of the eleventh indiction, in Syene.
5     [Flavius Apa Dios] son of Kyriakos, his mother being Tsachos, soldier of the regiment of Syene, to Aurelius
6     … [son of] …, ship's captain from the same Syene, and to Aurelia Anastasia daughter
7     of … [his mother being] …, from the same (city) of the Syenites, greeting. I acknowledge that I have had and
8     [borrowed from] you today, for my own pressing need, from you (masc.)
9     … [three] solidi [of gold], and from you, Anastasia, one solidus of gold,
10    [by the standard of Syene,] four solidi of approved gold, total 4 solidi of gold by the standard of Syene,
11    [and as a pledge] and security I have mortgaged to you, by way of pledge and
12    [by right of mortgage,] the fourth part belonging to me of the house of my mother,
13    [that is, the …] part from the courtyard, with entrance and exit, and the western
14    [part …] of the dining-room on the second floor, and
15    … on the third floor above the dining-room of Taam, so that all the aforesaid
16    [things may be] under your authority and lordship and ownership
17    [and that you may] use them until the completing (repayment) of the aforewritten
18    [four solidi of approved] gold, at my own risk; and for your
19    [security] I have made for you this mortgage-document, valid
20    [and secure,] producible everywhere, with the signature of him who on my behalf
21    [signs,] and on being formally questioned face to face I agreed and released (it).
22    [I, Flavius Apa Dios] son of Kyriakos, my mother being Tsacho, from Syene, the aforesaid, have made this
23    [security] of four solidi, and all is satisfactory to me as is set forth above. I, Flavius Paulos
24    … [soldier of the] regiment of Syene, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters.
25    [I, Flavius Apa Dios] son of Martius, actuarius of the regiment of Syene, bear witness to the security,
26    [having heard (it)] from Apa Dios who also is the contracting party, who told me (it) and was present in a public [place.] I, Flavius Patermouthis son of Ioannes, soldier of the regiment of Syene, bear witness.
27    [I, Flavius Menas] son of Didymos, soldier of the regiment of Syene, bear witness.
28    [Through me, Mar]kos son of Apa Dios, soldier of the regiment of Syene, it was registered.
