[TRANSLATION]
1     Thoth 15 of the 4th year of the name Mi[usios]
2     Senpsansnôtos, for poll-tax of Charakos of the 3rd year
3     Eight drachmas, total: eight drachmas. Hôros, I have sealed.
4     Another: Phaophi 8, likewise four drachmas, total: four drachmas. Agath[os], I have sealed.
