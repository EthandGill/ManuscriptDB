[TRANSLATION]
1     … o … ph … r …
2     … in the same Hermopolis at the district of Polis sun-facing [in a public street ca]lled of Asclepius according to a partnership of [the] brothers
3     [yours together with rooms] and all appurtenances and cells and outbuildings and
4     wells for use and housing of livestock [of a] rental per year of
5     according to you a sixth part of Augustan silver coinage of eight talents and
6     two thousand drachmas, which rental I will pay to you per year without fail and at the end of the two-year period I will hand over to you the aforementioned [your] sixth
7     part of the aforementioned sun-room together with the affixed doors and keys, as
8     I have received [it], in nothing damaged, except for use. The lease
9     [is] valid and questioned I acknowledged. After the consulship of Flavius Dalmatius brother of the lord
10    [our] Constan[tin]us A[ugustus] and Domitius [Zenophilus of the] most illustrious.
11    Ph … phoi … rent … ph … rion.
