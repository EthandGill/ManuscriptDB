[TRANSLATION]
1     … according to a profitlessness …
2     … your body being at rest … may [you] be the more eased, but … to … [we exhort]
3     God, that he may grant [you] and give [to] you both the healing of your body and the salvation of your soul; [may it be] that we are worthy to hear or to see your salvation … a power … we, through your godliness, have sent … the good thing; we received them … [we] have been satisfied; and with all your blessings we give thanks to you again.
4     Through the great offering, since we are unable to repay you
5     for it; but he whom you sent to him is sufficient to heal your soul and your body, for neither are we worthy
6     of an administration of this kind … according to the manner you commanded, namely that
7     we should send the oil of the holy altar to us, together with a little water. Behold, I have sent them, and I have also sent a little token in the cloth, to Apa Severus, thinking that you had gone down into the town. Keep it very carefully for me, who write these things. I greet you from your head down to your feet, and I greet your beloved daughter Kyrakale, again, and all those of her house.
8     My sinfulness prays that God may bless her
9     together with her whole house. Behold these little crosses I have sent
10    to her children.
11    Farewell, farewell, farewell in the grace of the holy and consubstantial Trinity. Since I wrote this letter, the brethren have been exhorting me, saying: appeal
12    to your godliness, that if there be a little
13    servant, rest yourselves; do love with us, even if
14    it be a burden; add this further good thing upon
15    all your other virtues.
