[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis of produce, 17th year of our lords
2     Severus and Antoninus the Pious, Augustae
3     Epiphi 16th, on behalf of Notus, [in the] name of Phatraeus also [called] Kallisus, [son of] Philotos
4     Through Spotutus Imouth, wheat, five artabas [and] a half, make wheat artabas 5 ½.
5     Another, Epiphi 24th, on behalf of Notus, [in the] name of the same, through the same, wheat, seven artabas [and] two-thirds
6     [And] one-eighth, make wheat artabas 7 ⅔ ⅛, altogether make wheat artabas 13 ¼ 1/24. Harpocras, secretary, [I] have certified [it].
