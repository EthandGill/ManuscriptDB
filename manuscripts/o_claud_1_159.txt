[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysodorus to Dios the master and
2     brother, greetings.
3     You will do well from the presence …
4     to make in the Claudian [establishment] from the private
5     bathhouse and having provided for me
6     two minas of meat and one foot from the
7     first [portion]. And I myself for my part
8     [am] a cook, even if [your] fellow-apprentice
9     [as well].
10    I pray you [to] be well.
