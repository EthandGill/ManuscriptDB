1     Year 13, also 10, Mesore 23
2     Pikos son of Psemminios sold
3     to Totoes son of Zmanreous
4     from grain-land called
5     Ptemen Akoaka
6     from the sixth part, the two-thirds
7     [in] bronze coin [amounting to] four drachmas
8     Hermodotos has approved [the payment].
9     [Reigning were] Cleopatra and Ptolemaios her son called Alexander, [the] gods loving-their-mother, year 13, also 10, [while] the priest who was
10    in Alexandria [of] Alexander and of the gods saviors and of the gods siblings and of the gods benefactors and of the gods loving-their-father and of the gods manifest and of the god good-father
11    and of the god loving-their-mother and of the god new loving-their-father and of the god benefactor and of the gods loving-their-mother, [serving at] the sacred bull of Isis the great mother of gods, the shield-bearing Berenice, benefactress, canephorus of Arsinoe lover-of-sibling, priestess of Arsinoe loving-her-father, [the priests] of those in Alexandria, and in Ptolemais of Thebaida [were] priests and priestesses
12    and canephorus of those being and existing, in the month Mesore, on the 23rd, in Hermouthis of the Pathyrite [district] of Thebaida, [at the time of] Hermodotos [serving as] market-controller.
13    [A] sale. Pikos son of Psemminios, [one] of the pastophori from the Memnonia, as 30 years old, [of] medium build, dark-complexioned, [with] curly [hair], long-faced, with straight lips, [and] multiple scars on [the] face
14    from grain-bearing land in the plain around the Memnonia called Ptemen Akoaka, [his] corresponding undivided share of the sixth part, the two-thirds; neighbors of the whole land: to the south, a lake called Ptemen Anoubios, to the north, a lake called Ptemen Akoaka, to the east Tsatphe, to the west, land [belonging to] Psennesios son of Phagonis the younger, or whoever the neighbors may be in all directions. Totoes son of Zmanreous, [one] of the same pastophori, as 35 years old, [of] medium build, dark-complexioned, round-faced, with straight lips, [and a] scar
15    on [the] right wrist [for] bronze coin [amounting to] four thousand drachmas. The guarantor and warrantor of [the terms] of the sale [was] Pikos the seller, [whom] the buyer accepted
16    Totoes the buyer. Hermodotos has approved [the payment].
17    Year 13, also 10, Mesore 26. [The payment] has been assigned to the bank in Hermouthis
18    where Dionysios [handles] the tenth of the circulating [tax], according to the record of Psenkhonsis the tax-official, under whom
19    Herakleides the scribe registers [the transaction]; the tax [on the] sale of Totoes
20    son of Zmanreous from grain-land in the [territory] around the
21    Memnonia
22    plain[s] in land called Ptemen Akaka
23    of the sixth part, the two-thirds which he bought from
24    Pikos son of Psemminios [for] bronze [amounting to] four drachmas, tax 400.
25    Dionysios the banker.
