[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonides to Zenon greeting. You wrote me to give [instead] of the forty-mina [pieces] of the two coverlets to Criton one talent. And I have prepared for you instead of the forty-mina [pieces] two purple-bordered fifty-mina pieces of finest trimming. And in these two
2     I have received from Criton labor-cost indeed of the mina two and a half obols, these amount to minas 100 drachmas 141 four-obol [piece],
3     and for dyeing of both coverlets drachmas 20 and wage for trimming drachma 1, [all] drachmas 62 four-obol [piece],
4     and I have given him both the coverlets. And for the seven which you wrote me to prepare I have [expenses] until reckoning drachmas 400. It will be then as you sent word. I have also helped him and
5     the [sacred offerings].
6     Farewell. Year 29 Pachon 25.
7     And [for] anything else which you need write [to] us.
8     Apol[loni]des concerning coverlets
9     and sacred [offerings] which he gave to Criton.
10    Year 29 Daisios 23. In Arsinoe.
11    To Zenon
