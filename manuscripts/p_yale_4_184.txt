r.1    ἐμέτρησεν Λαμαζων ἀπὸ κτήματος Παλώσεως ὑπὲρ ἐμβολῆς τῆς ϛ ἰνδικτίονος σίτου ῥυπαροῦ ἀρτάβας

[TRANSLATION]
1     Lamazon measured from the estate of Palosis for the payment of the 6th indiction rough grain, artabae
2     sixty-seven; rough grain artabae amount to 67 with kangella.
3     [It was] written in the month of Mesore in the indiction [5] for the payment of the 6th indiction.
4     Year [numerals], through me Abramios the scribe.
5     Jeremiah the goldsmith paid for the public charges of the apparatus.
