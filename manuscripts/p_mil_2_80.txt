[TRANSLATION]
1     Niceanor to Nem[...]ertē the sister
2     many greetings.
3     Before all things I pray you are well
4     and [I do] your proskynema every day before the lord Sarapis
5     with our innocent children and those loving us.
6     I wish you to know that I wrote to you through
7     Pasion the brother of Dioscoratus
8     that whatever you wish I will do and you have written nothing back to me; write to me quickly.
9     For I have found the one able to me
