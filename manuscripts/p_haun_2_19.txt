[TRANSLATION]
1     You have been negligent not sending the grain promptly, especially since no one is here. For indeed on each [occasion] a man
2     comes to us saying that if indeed [you] send [to] me [I am served], but if I do not receive [it I will go] elsewhere. But see if you are able to jump [?] you … …
3     toward the farmer Arius, since he came saying much that he had ordered me not to go to you nor … …
4     carry [it] away to them. Do not therefore be negligent about this. Furthermore take care because of the wool-clippings and the hemp-fibers. And [they] say
5     the monks that if such are the cheeses, [perhaps] … do not bring [them] to us, for they are still unfinished. And because of the …
6     harvest-products. And if you buy for us, do not be negligent either, and because of the tax-obligation while [the] outside [stand]s
7     the landholder, for you know what kind he is. Send us wood, for indeed the road is clear. And [he] says
8     Arcadia that [you] buy me felt according to the likeness of yours. The cheeses moreover of John those in the storehouse
9     send to him. I pray that you remain well for many years, most honored brother.
