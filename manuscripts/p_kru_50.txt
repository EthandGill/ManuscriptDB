[TRANSLATION]
1     [In] the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Tr[inity],
2     Father and Son and Holy Spirit, in the month Mesore 29, of the seventh indiction.
3     In the reign of Flavius Saal, the most glorious amir, pagarch from Diospolis to Latopolis,
4     I, Anatolios, the son of the late Samuel, my mother being the late
5     Rebekka, of the people of the fortress of Jeme, the nome of the city of Ermont, I provide
6     afterward a subscriber to subscribe on my behalf to this document of indemnity which is set down, not to be transgressed and
7     not to be shaken, out of the laws; and to make it more firmly established
8     through trustworthy witnesses, these who shall bear witness
9     to it concerning me, according to my own request and my warrant.
10    I write to Tsherkah the daughter of the late Georgios, her mother
11    being Abessa, and to Arsenios her brother german, in this same
12    fortress, the nome of this same city, greeting. Namely, since in this present time the most honorable Petros the son of Komes and John the son of Mathias, administrators of our fortress, came out and sued
13    me concerning the matter of the cell that is by my house, this which I redeemed
14    through the holy place of the one who shines forth among the holy ones,
15    the saint Abba Psate of the mountain of our fortress; because we donated it to that holy place through the late Epiphaneios his brother, while he was yet living, you established the matter of dispute with us, wishing to annul the sale
