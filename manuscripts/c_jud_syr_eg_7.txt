[TRANSLATION]
1     Having assembled to Horos Psen […]
2     to the [public servant] until the […] was delivered the
3     [ordered to us each month]
4     wheat to those with us. Through others
5     we have written to you concerning those things which we desire
6     no address having befallen [us]. But taking care of ourselves that you might be well, — we are in Ptolemais.
7     Farewell. Year 16, Thoth 8.
