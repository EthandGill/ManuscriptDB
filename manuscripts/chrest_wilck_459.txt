[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the Antinoites.
2     Copy. From a roll of examinations of [Marcus Petronius]
3     [Honoratus], prefect of Egypt, [whose heading is: the under]signed veterans who served [in cavalry-squadrons]
4     and cohorts and in the Syrian fleet, some [having obtained]
5     with children and grandchildren, others alone, the [Roman]
6     citizenship and the right of intermarriage with the wives [whom they then had,]
7     when the citizenship was given to them, [or, if any be unmarried,]
8     with those whom each may take in the meantime, up to one, [and further]
9     also other veterans, those without bronze tablets, who now [too, themselves having] obtained alone the Roman citizenship, [were examined]
10    by order of Marcus Petronius [Honoratus, prefect]
11    of Egypt, through Magius Sabinus, tribune of the [second legion]
12    Trajana Fortis, from Mecheir 3 [to] 2 of the month Pachon
13    [of the] present 11th year of the Emperor [Caesar Titus Aelius]
14    [Hadrianus] Antoninus Augustus Pius. [And the supporting]
15    [proofs] which were submitted to the aforesaid Sabinus are appended [under each] name. After other matters, of columns …
16    … Sempronius Maximus, wishing [to reside]
17    [for a time] in the Arsinoite nome …
18    … a bronze tablet sealed [from a board]
19    [of bronze that] lies in Rome, through which it was [attested]
