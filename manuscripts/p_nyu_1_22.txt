1     [Aurel]ius [Sara]pion son of Ouenaphris acknowledges,
2     his m[other being] Eirene, [from] the vil[lage of] K[aran]is,
3     [be]ing about thirty years old, with a scar on the lower part of the
4     left hand, with [as guarant]or Aurelius Heron son of Ol(?),
5     from the same vill[age, that he has] received and had measured out to him fr[om An]tonius son of Sa[rapa]mmon
6     from the strategi(?), through Heras the steward,
7     of wheat with a third part five artabas,
8     a third artabas 5⅓, which I shall of necessity
9     repay to you at the appointed time, in the month of Pauni
10    [of] the th[ir]d indiction, [wi]th Heron as guarantor,
11    son of Hol(?), by the just four-choinix measure, the produce being
12    new, clean, unadulterated, satisfactory, without delay; and at the demanding, the execution shall be yours both against me the acknowledging Sarapion and against
13    the property belonging to me, with the same
14    Heron as guarantor, as if [by jud]gement; and being formally questioned I acknowledged.
15    In the consulship of our masters Constantine
16    Augustus for the 8th time and of Constantine the most illustrious
17    Caesar [for the 4th], Thoth 4. Aureliu[s] Sarapio[n]:
18    I have received the f[ive] artabas of wheat
19    and a third, and I shall repay at the appointed time
20    with Heron as guarantor, as is aforesaid.
21    Aurelius Kopres son of Paemos, I wrote
22    on his behalf, he being illiterate.
