[TRANSLATION]
1     to this written deed of sale, indissoluble, eternal,
2     which is written, not to be transgressed and not to be disturbed,
3     not to be (annulled) [throu]gh the laws; and what is more,
4     it is confirmed through trustworthy witnesses, these who shall witness to the sale
5     on our behalf, according to our request, our very own,
6     together with our authorization; we write to the most admirable Solomon the son of the blessed Moses, the smith,
7     the south of this one village, greeting; that, since in this year which exists now, that is the twelfth,
8     we have willed, and we are persuaded, without any deceit,
9     or fear or extortion or fraud, [or] coercion,
10    or circumvention; there is no compulsion existing for us,
11    but rather of our own purpose, our very own, [our own],
12    we acknowledge, swearing by the salvation of the Trinity,
13    the holy, consubstantial, the Father and the Son and
14    the Holy Spirit, the unity that is in a Trinity,
15    we having set our intent upon it; we write to you, to you, Solomon
16    (son of) Moses, our shares of land which came down to us through our fathers, since they came down to them (the fathers) through their fathers,
17    that land which they call the "cabbage-land" of the holy bishop Apa Kirmanos,
18    so that you may command them forever, for all time,
19    coming after you; and these are the boundaries of those lands
20    on its four sides, according to their boundaries, that surround
21    its four sides. The first land and the second, these
22    are their boundaries: on the south, Senouthi the son of Phebamon;
23    on the north, Anatole (the heirs of) Samuel; on the west, Kos the dyer(?)
24    the son of David; on the east, the door of the owners. Behold, these
25    are the boundaries of the one land, on its four sides, outside
26    of it; likewise, on the east the boundaries of the other land,
27    these are: on the west, Iakob the son of Andreas;
28    on the north, Solomon (son of) Moses, the purchaser. Behold, these are the boundaries of the second land, on its four sides; of the other (parcel),
29    rightly there remains behind that which we gave to you.
30    And the price which we agreed upon for it, of the two parts (shares),
31    is three and a tremision of a holokottinos of gold,
32    making … three (and) a third. The price, then, we received it from hand to hand
33    in the measure of the fortress, with the assaying of the great (men)
34    and the testing of Paham and the scales of Marthe;
35    [it is confirm]ed for you, you, Solomon, so that
36    upon you, that you may come in and take hold and command
37    and manage the two living(-plots?)
38    these on the street of the Pailakaine, that you may take them
39    and re-take them up, dwelling in them, disposing of them,
40    leaving them to your children and your heirs, simply to use them in
41    [every] manner that is in your (power); whoever shall dare,
42    as may happen, to come against you concerning the two living(-plots?), shall pay ten holokottinoi
43    and shall not gain anything; and if it should happen
44    that someone dare, whether a child, a brother, or an heir, or any man, acting
45    in our behalf, or a stranger, he shall become liable to the penalty, and he shall not gain anything;
46    I have confirmed it for you against every confirmation in Christ Jesus
47    our Lord, in every place; we consent, and
48    we acknowledge, firm and valid, it was read aloud to us,
49    we heard it, it being complete, against all possession and due order, Jesus Christ.
50    I, Kolodje the daughter of the blessed Paham, I consent to the sale according to the way it is written.
51    I, George the son of the blessed Kosma, Kolodje
52    having requested of me, I wrote on her behalf according to the request.
53    [I, D]aniel the son of Saul, I consent to the sale
54    according to the way it is written. I, David the son of Petros,
55    I have written on his behalf, since he does not know (how) to write.
56    I, Mena the son of Philotheos, I consent to the sale.
57    [I], Psere the son of the blessed [Pe]tros, by the grace of God
58    presbyter and also the abbot of the holy church of Djeme, I bear witness.
59    I, Papnouthios the son of the blessed Petros, the least archdeacon of the holy church of Djeme, I bear witness.
60    I, Phoibamon the son of Stephanos, the least presbyter of (the church of) Apa Biktor, I bear witness.
61    I, George the son of the blessed Kosma, I bear witness.
62    I, David the son of Petros, I bear witness.
63    Pesynthios son of Stephanos, I witness.
64    I, Moses, the least deacon, the son of Senouthios,
65    archpresbyter of the fortress of Djeme, I have written the sale
66    by my hand, according to [the] request of those who established it.
