[TRANSLATION]
1     Aristeas to Demeas, greetings. The letter which you sent I was unable to read because it was rubbed out;
2     but it seemed to me concerning the
3     plot [you had written].
4     I also spoke to you in Ptolemais about [it], and
5     now [too]; what is found will still be less for you. I hear the Libyan has taken a garden lease; you should not therefore have let him [go]. And now, if he approaches or troubles you, strip him. And I will be present
6     and I soon after the festival. But if there are any embankments [that need to] be dug
7     in the plot, dig them.
8     Fare well.
9     To Demeas
