[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 15 which is also 12, Hathyr 25, in Pathyris, in the office o[f] Hermias
2     who acts for Paniskos the agoranomos.
3     Sennesis daughter of Psenthotes, a Persian woman, about 25 years old,
4     less than medium (height), honey-colored, ill-favored, with the guardian her own
5     relative Thotsytes son of Erienoupis, about 40 years old, of medium (height), somewhat ruddy,
6     withdraws from the fourth share, bought from her by Petearsemthis son of Panobchounis, of a planted vineyard and
7     of the trees growing (in it) and a [fifth] share of grain-bearing land
8     in (the part) to the north and south-west of the vineyard, adjoining,
9     in the strip of Pathyris, [belonging to him] and to his brothers undivided,
10    and the deed of sale lodged at the record-office in Pathyris in the 13th
11    which is also the 1st year, Mesore 23; of which the neighbors are: on the s[ou]th the vineyard of Tathotis,
12    on the north the land of Patous son of Horos and his br[others, on the] east the enclosure
13    of the vineyards, on the south-west an embankment — for … 1500(?) talents of copper; and (it is agreed) that neither Sennesis nor anyone else acting for her shall proceed against
14    Petearsemthis nor anyone else acting for him
15    concerning all the matters written above. But if (anyone does), the claim of
16    the one proceeding (against him) shall be void, and the one who proceeds shall pay in addition
17    a penalty on the spot of 10 talents and as sacred
18    (dues) to the sovereigns two hundred drachmas of stamped silver,
19    and nonetheless the agreed terms shall be valid.
20    Hermias, who acts for Paniskos, I have transacted (it).
21    Year 15 which is also 12,
22    Hathyr 25.
23    Sennesis withdraws
24    in favor of Petearsemthis.
