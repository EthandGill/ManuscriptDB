[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 20 and 19 and 12 of our lords
2     Diocletian and Maximian Sebastoi
3     and Constantine and Maximian Caesars
4     Phaophi 25. Isidoros the collector cancelled
5     [the accounts of] Karanida of sacred levy of the 17th year and 16th year
6     and ninth [year] of Kerkesoucha, set apart, talents three, equal talents 3.
7     And on the 26th the same cancelled [the accounts of] Karanida of the same levy
8     of the 17th year and 16th year and ninth year of Kerkesoucha, drachmas five-hundred, equal drachmai 500.
