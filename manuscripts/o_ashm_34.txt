r.2    Παναμεῦτι Ψεναμούνιος Παναμεῦτος. ἔσχομεν

[TRANSLATION]
1     Ailourion and Horos, silver tax collectors of Charakos
2     Panameus [son] of Psenamounis, Panamaeus: we received
3     for watchtowers and others, year 2, soiled drachmas seven
4     obols four; amount to drachmas seven, obols four. Year 2 of Antoninus
5     Caesar the lord, Pauni 18.
6     Ailourion, through Kaleos, I have marked.
