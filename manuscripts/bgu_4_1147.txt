[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos, the (official) in charge of the tribunal,
2   from Dionysios son of Dionysios and from Eirene daughter of
3   Patroklos, a Persian woman, with her guardian, her broth-
4   er Patroklos son of Ammonios. Concerning the
5   matters at issue, Eirene agrees that she has from
6   Dionysios a loan, from hand to hand out of the house, of silv[er]
7   Ptolemaic, six hundred drachmas, at interest
8   of nine obols on each mina each
9   month, which she will also repay in six months
10   from Mecheir of the present seven-
11   teenth year of Caesar, paying the in-
12   terest monthly and punctually, and the prin-
13   cipal in the last month, without
14   [a]ny postponement; or else [she] is
15   [at on]ce liable to seizure and to be held
16   until she pays the loan
17   with half as much again, and the interest at the simple rate,
18   and, for the time elapsed, the inter-
19   est according to the diagramma, at two drachmas,
20   [the right of exec]ution belonging to Dionysios,
21   [both from] Eirene [herself] and from all her
22   [property], as if by legal judgement,
23   [and] further also from the slave-woman belonging to Eirene,
24   Erotion, against whom she has also handed over
25   to Dionysios himself, in pledge, the
26   registration … that she holds,
27   and (she is) until the settlement to furnish her
28   [un-al]ienated and unmortgaged,
29   [and if it ha]pp[ens] that she runs away or even suffers
30   [something hu]man (i.e. dies), even so the loan and
31   [the int]erest are to be free of all
32   risk, void being whatever (pledges) of trust she may bring forward,
33   [all of them, (and) every protection], and …
