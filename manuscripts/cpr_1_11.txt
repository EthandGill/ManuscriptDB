[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twelfth year of the Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus
2     Dacicus, in the month Apellaios 12, Phaophi 12, in Ptolemais Euergetis of the
3     [A]rsinoite nome: Stotoetis, about thirty-
4     five years old, with a scar on the left of his forehead, and Horion, about thirty-two
5     years old, with a scar in the middle of his forehead, both sons of Panephremmis, agree with each other that they have divided between themselves,
6     from now for all time, the existing (share), to each of them a fourth
7     part, common and undivided, of a house in which there is an oil-press, and of a courtyard,
8     and likewise of another house and of all appurtenances, in the village
9     of [S]oknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleides division, and that from (the division) which they have made between themselv[es]
10    by mutual consent, from a lot of division, Stoto[etis] has taken up
11    his allotted fourth part of the house on the east, i[n which there is]
12    an oil-press and a courtyard, and the fourth part of these falling to Horion,
13    so that there belongs to him, Stotoetis, a half part of the house and oil-press and courtyard
14    and appurtenances; and the neighbors of the whole, as by agreement they dictated: on the south
15    the house of Tesenouphis, on the north an entrance and exit, on the west a royal lane, on the east
16    the house of Pisais; and that Horion, he too, has been allotted his allotted fourth part of the house on the west and the fourth part falling to Stotoetis,
17    so that there belongs to him, Horion, a half part of the house on the west;
18    and the neighbors of the whole: on the south the house of Apynchis, on the north a royal lane, on the west an entrance
19    and exit, on the east the house of Stotoetis son of Panephremmis. And the (drachmas)
20    making up (the value), of the portion which Stotoetis has been allotted as is set out, by which (it exceeds) the portion to which Horion has been allotted — two hundred and twenty silver drachmas — that
21    [Horion] has received forthwith from Stotoetis at once, from hand to hand, out of the house. Let
22    [then the] contracting parties and each of them and their agents abide by what has been voluntarily agreed and divided, and let each of them have power and ownership over what he has been allotted, and
23    [to dis]pose concerning them in whatever arrangements he may choose, and that no other shall proceed, nor those acting for him,
24    against the other, nor against those acting for him, concerning anything of what has been divided, nor against a part of them
25    in any manner; and that whatever of the aforesaid the contracting parties contravene, let
26    [the] transgressor pay to the abiding party forthwith the damages and expenses doubled, and as a fine five hundred silver drach[mas],
27    [five] hundred, apart from the aforesaid (terms) being valid. Subscribers: for [Stot]oetis,
28    [Pto]lemaios son of Ptolemaios, about sixty years old, with a scar on the right forearm; and for Horion, Zoilos son of Didymos, about
29    [fi]fty-three years old, with a scar in the middle of the nose. I, Horion son of Panephremmis, agree that I have brought about the division with my brother Stotoetis and I have received the (drachmas) making up (the value), two hundred and twenty silver drachmas, and I have been content
30    [with] the division and I shall not proceed (against it), as is set out. I, Zoilos
31    son of Didymos, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters. I, Stotoetis son of Panephremmis, agree that I have brought about the division with my brother Horion [and] …
32    … [and I shall not] proceed (against it), as is set out. I wrote on his behalf,
33    [Ptolemai]os son of Ptolemaios, he not knowing letters.
34    Registered, Phaophi 12,
35    year 12 of the Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan
36    Augustus Germanicus Dacicus.
37    I, Maron, overseer, have signed (it).
