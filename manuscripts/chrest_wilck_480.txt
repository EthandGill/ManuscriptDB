[TRANSLATION]
1     Apion to Epimachos his father and
2     lord, very many greetings. Before all things I pray that you are in health and that always
3     being strong you may fare well together with my sister
4     and her daughter and my brother.
5     I give thanks to the lord Serapis
6     that, when I was in danger at sea,
7     he saved me at once. When I entered into Misenum, I received as travel-money from Caesar
8     three gold pieces, and it is well with me.
9     I ask you therefore, my lord father,
10    write me a little letter, first
11    about your welfare, secondly about that of my brothers,
12    thirdly, that I may do reverence to your
13    hand, because you trained me well,
14    and from this I hope to advance quickly, the gods willing. Greet
15    Capito many times and my brothers
16    and Serenilla and my friends.
17    I sent you a little portrait of myself through Euktemon. My name is Antonis Maximus. I pray for your health.
18    Century Athenonike.
19    Serenos son of Agathos Daimon greets you, and … son of
20    … rus
21    and Turbon son of Gallonius and D…nas son of … sen…
22    To Philadelphia, to Epimachos, from Apion his son.
23    Deliver to the first cohort of the Apameni of Julianus An…
24    to the libellarius, from Apion, for Epimachos his father.
