1     After the consul[ship] of him of [bl]essed memory,
2     Armatos, Paun[i] 2, in the fortu[na]te first indiction,
3     in the village of Phebichis the Great of the Herakleopolite nome.
4     To Flavius Olympios, the most illustrious count of the
5     divine consistory, landholder in the Herakleopolite (nome),

11    [secur]ity that we have received from your Splendor
12    [th]e price agreed between one another for

14    [to your] splen[dor in the] coming mon[th] Mecheir of the fortunate
15    [second] [indi]ction, of the cultivation of the third indiction, without postponement and
16    [without any contradic]tion, all being pledged for this,
17    [of our proper]ty present and future, by way of security and
