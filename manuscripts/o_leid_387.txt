[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius and associates, tax-collectors of the silver [tax] of the metropolis
2     to Psenamounis elder, [son] of Pesfis
3     [we] received for the head-tax of Ophieus year 13 common
4     drachmas ten [and] tetrobol, [it] becomes drachmas 10 tetrobol …
5     [of the] duplicate two-obol semi-obol, [it] becomes two-obol semi-obol. Year 13 of Trajan
6     [Caesar the] master Phamenoth 27.
7     Apollonius I have signed.
