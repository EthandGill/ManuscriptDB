r.2    μοι τίνι δεῖ ἐπιθεῖναι αὐτό. καὶ περὶ τοῦ στρώματος, δόντες ἀρραβῶνα μικροῦ ἀπεβάλομεν. γράψαντος γὰρ Ζήνωνος Ἰάσονι δοῦναι στρῶμα ἢ δραχμὰς λ, οὐκ ἔδωκεν. γραψάτω οὖν Ἀπολλοδώρωι τῶι ἐπὶ τῶν προβάτων δοῦναι ἔρια δραχμῶν λ, ἐγὼ δὲ ἀπέγνωσα

r.5    κατάγωσιν, καὶ τοῦτο προσδέξωνται. ἐπετίθουν δὲ καὶ Μητροδώρωι τὰ γνάφαλλα, ὁ δʼ οὐκ ἔφη λήψεσθαι. ἐπιμέλου δὲ καὶ σαυτοῦ ἵνα ὑγιαίνηις. ἔρρωσο.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Rhodon to Paramonios, greetings, and to the boys. Three measures of honey have been purchased for you. Write then
2     to me [about] whom it is necessary to put it on. And concerning the mattress, having given a small deposit we lost it. For when Zenon wrote to Jason to give a mattress or thirty drachmas, he did not give [it]. Let [him] write then to Apollodorus, he [who is] over the sheep, to give wool worth thirty drachmas, and I have decided
3     thus, so that there will be a mattress for you; and I will give the expense. And [a sacrificial animal] has been purchased for you as well
4     the victim for the Arsinoia [festival] [at] twelve drachmas. You then entreat Zenon so that, just as his own [things]
5     may be brought down, he will also accept this. And [we] were imposing the fullered goods on Metrodorus as well, but he said he would not accept [them]. Take care also of yourself so that you are well. Farewell.
6     Phamenoth 21.
7     [To the] weaponry-fighter. Paramonios.
