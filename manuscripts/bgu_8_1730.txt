[TRANSLATION]
1     the king and queen having ordered
2     no one of the nomes above Memphis
3     purchasing grain or legumes to bring down into the lower land, but not even to the Thebaid to carry up on any pretext, all untaxed
4     being to Alexandria to [transport],
5     or whoever is caught will be liable to death
6     and whoever wishes to inform concerning
7     those doing contrary to these things
8     to the generals according to the nome, on which condition
9     [they] will receive the third part of the property of the one seized,
10    but if [he] is a slave, [he] will also be free
11    and will receive the sixth [part]
12    Year 3, Phaophi 23
13    Horos, topogrammateus, …, through Onias, secretary
14    I have set forth opposite [the previously] posted ordinance
15    Year 3, Hathyr, …, 5
