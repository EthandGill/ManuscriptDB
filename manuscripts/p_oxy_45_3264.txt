[TRANSLATION]
1     To Hermophilos …
2     from Orse[n]ou[phis] son of Diosko[ro]s and Ps[o]sneus son of Psen[mei]nis and Penn[am]is
3     son of Amenneus, of the elders together with others, of the village of Peenno of the middle toparchy. With reference to the report submitted to Claudius
4     Herakleios the strategos by us and
5     the fellow-elders against Diogenes, embankment-overseer,
6     concerning his having received from fifty-one men 4 drachmas each so as
7     that they should not work on the public embankments their five-naubia (dike-quota),
8     and concerning his having let off
9     nine other men likewise
10    so as not to do the work — you having sought the names of those previously listed, we declared, out of these, names of thirteen men who also in writing addressed the strategos (stating) that they had given nothing, neither to Diogenes nor
11    to his (agents) nor to anyone else for the sake of
12    not working [on the emb]ankments. And you seeking the names of the rest,
13    we declare,
14    swearing by the fortune of the Emperor
15    Titus Caes[a]r Vespa[s]ian
16    Augustus, that we can prove nothing of the things declared through the aforementioned report. If we swear truly, may it be well with us;
17    if falsely, the contrary.
18    In the 3rd year of the Empero[r Titus Caesar]
19    [Ve]spasian[us Augustus ] …
