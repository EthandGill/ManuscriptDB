[TRANSLATION]
1     Origen to Serenus the brother
2     Greetings
3     I want you to know that your obeisance [I make]
4     each day among the [gods] here. You will do well by coming to us toward
5     a few days. For many Oxyrhynchites are here. But I blame you because you have not yet replied to me. Greet the overseer
6     and Sarapammon the brother and the
7     teacher and all those in the house.
8     I wish you to be well, brother.
9     Deliver to Serenus the silversmith
