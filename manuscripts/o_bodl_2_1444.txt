[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of treasury of the metropolis of harvest, 22nd year of Antoninus
2     Caesar of the lord, Mesore 3
3     On behalf of the South, name Zmenimouthes Peteménōphios
4     And Ta… [sister] wheat artaba one-third one-eighth
5     Amounts to wheat artaba one-third one-eighth and name Zmenimouthes
6     Alone through farmer Petechōnsius Nepherous
7     Wheat two one-third one-eighth
8     Total wheat artabae 2 one-third one-eighth, total wheat artabae 2 one-half one-third one-twelfth. Sabinos I have marked.
