[TRANSLATION]
1     In the third [year] of the Emperor Caesar
2     Marcus Aurelius Severus Alexander
3     Pious Fortunate Augustus, Mesore 1.
4     The Aurelii Dioskoros son of Priskos and Horos
5     son of Gemellus and Pekysis son of Mouis and Sarapion … and Neilos son of Pelpes and
6     Pabetas son of Heras and Papontos son of Thias and
7     Ammonios son of Phaidros and Paieous son of Annas
8     and Gemellas son of Antiphilos and Paoubestis
9     son of Apynchis and Mysthes son of Apynchis and
10    Ammonas son of Peteeus and Diogenes
11    son of Panetbeous and Sarapion son of Kronion [and] Apollos son of Mannes and Stratippos … mas and Kopres son of Pasis and Sisois
12    … and Kopres son of Hermas and the rest,
13    public and estate and revenue-land
14    cultivators of the village of Philadelphia: the aforewritten public cultivators
15    of the aforesaid village agreed
16    among themselves with one another, to the effect that all should remain together [and] all have made their joint consent,
17    that all should remain together [and] all have made their joint consent,
18    that all should give, each, twenty drachmas to the
19    persons who became elders of the 4th year,
20    toward the completion of the
21    elders who became thirty men,
22    on condition that they themselves not receive more
23    than the thirty, and that the customary charge
24    of the transport-fee and of the others remain according to custom;
25    and concerning these things being rightly and properly done, having been asked the formal question we acknowledge.
26    I, Dioskoros the notary, wrote the body of the document
27    and on behalf of them, who do not know letters.
28    Pabetas son of Heras, aged 35, a scar below the left knee.
29    [Au]relius Papsois son of Ignatios, I agreed
30    as aforesaid. Aurelius Ammonios, I agreed as aforesaid. Harpokration son of Dioskoros, I agreed as aforesaid.
