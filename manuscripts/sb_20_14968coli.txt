[TRANSLATION]
1     Year first of Au[tocrat]or Titus Flavius
2     Vespasian[us] Caesar. [I] have worked in the Hephaestian canal
3     for five days on behalf of the embankments of the same first [year] of Philadelphia
4     Petesouchos son of Dikranos of Polyandros
5     mother Tanetbeous of Petsiris.
6     Ptolemaeus also called Kil I have sealed.
7     Ch[aerim]on secretary of the general I have sealed.
8     Souchius secretary [of] Petesouchos [son] of Dikranos
9     I have sealed.
