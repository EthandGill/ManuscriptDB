[TRANSLATION]
1     Damis, [the agent] of Themistos, [to] Zeno, greetings. [Concerning the] farmers from Ptolemais
2     [the] farmers Petous and Thotortais, [who] having stolen a cow, removed [it]
3     to Philadelphia. I have sent therefore Eubius the guard. Well
4     then you will do, if you order [him], in case he captures the men
5     and the cow, [to ensure] that no one force him [to release them], but rather [have them] brought before you;
6     and if our [people] say anything more just, [let] it be handed over to them, the
7     cow and the [persons].
8     Farewell. Year 36, Thoth 6th.
9     Year 36, Thoth 11th.
10    Damis, [concerning] cow and [person].
11    To Zeno.
