1     Hermaeus Diogatos tax-collector of six-drachma donkeys
2     of the 11th year of Nero the lord
3     To Metocos son of Straton
4     greetings. I hold from you what you owe
5     according to a handwritten agreement, silver drachmas sixteen,
6     amounting to drachmas 16, of the 12th year of Nero
7     Claudius Caesar Augustus
8     Germanicus Autocrator
9     of the month of Augustus the 6th.
