[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the con]sulship of our lords Constantine
2     Augustus for the 8th time and of Constantine the most noble Caesar for the [4th time],
3     Tybi 17.
4     To Flavius Julianus, logistes of the Oxyrhynchite (nome),
5     from Aurelius Diogenes son of Ammonius, from the same city.
6     It would be superfluous to remind your diligence of the things proclaimed by the authority of the laws, as it clearly orders
7     that, when an appeal has been lodged, no new measures be taken. Since therefore
8     on the present day I was informed that an instruction
9     had been presented to your office by the prytanis in office,
10    by which our name was submitted among those about to enter office,
11    a magistracy also of the exegeteia being attached, named for me,
12    and since I am at once making appeal against the very men who submitted me
13    to the exegeteia, having very many just claims indeed
14    in this matter, in order that these things may not be prejudiced before judgement nor anyone be kept in suspense by the instruction,
15    I request, the judgement concerning everything being once for all reserved
16    to my lord, the most eminent prefect of Egypt
17    Flavius Magnilianus, that the prytanis be …
18    so that he may know, with the result that nothing … of circumvention of my plea
19    be done. …
20    I, Aurelius Diogenes, have submitted (this) …
