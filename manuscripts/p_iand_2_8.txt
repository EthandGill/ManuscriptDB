[TRANSLATION]
1     Ischyrion to Antonin[us] the
2     most honored, greetings.
3     I sent along to you through Heracleides,
4     the brother of Chairemon, [the one] in Bacchiad[es] formerly scribe, of Moithymeites,
5     [from the] wine Cnidian three; dispatch
6     therefore to Moithyme [the one] able from him to receive; for [Dameas] was not able to receive because he did not have
7     [a donkey. and the] remaining [things] and…
8     [if I] find [a] donkey, I will dispatch
9     [through the] donkey-driver carelessly.
10    I pray [that] you remain in good health,
11    dearest, prosper[ing]
12    with all your household.
13    To [Ant]oninus from [Ischyrion].
