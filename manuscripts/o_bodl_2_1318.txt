[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis for the produce of the 18th year
2     Of Hadrian the lord, Epeiph 19, for the 16th year
3     Of Agoras, named Pollias [son] of Pollius
4     Of Gaius, wheat one-eighth artaba, makes wheat 8 artabae. G … has been marked.
