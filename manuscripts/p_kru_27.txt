[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity, the Father
2     and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Written in the month … , indiction … .
3     Under our lord, through Flavius Kyros Kollouthos, ar(chon?), from the three fortresses
4     and of Memnonia.
5     I, Ananias son of the blessed Pses — his mother is Manna —
6     the man of the fortress (kastron) of Djeme, of the nome of the city of Hermonthis,
7     I am to give afterwards a subscriber to subscribe on my behalf, with witnesses
8     to bear witness to this written deed of sale, which is not to be transgressed
9     and not to be shaken aside from the laws.
10    I write to Petros son of Zacharias of Petale, the man of this same fortress, that I, Ananias,
11    son of Pses, the one who writes first, acknowledge to you, you Petros son
12    of Zacharias, who buy and who shall first sign it, according to this sale,
13    this which is written, valid and firm, by a single act
14    through it I have come to it, willing and persuaded, without any
15    deceit or fear or violence or trickery and any compulsion
16    of any sort laid upon me, but in my own free choice
17    of myself, swearing moreover by God the Almighty
18    and by the safety of our lords who rule over us,
19    through the command of God, that I sell to you, you
20    Petros son of Zacharias, this Dalei (?), who buy and
21    whom I have first made clear, many times, in all lordship,
22    every right of the law of a sale that is complete,
23    and I write to you from this present day and from now
24    forever, for all time, forever, who shall come
25    after you, of my whole well (anet) which is in my dwelling of Tsacho,
26    daughter of Jacob the blind man, that well, which is within
27    the street, which is the west of the people of Pshoumare, this one which is below,
28    to the west of the house of Zacharias son of Pses, according to its boundaries which surround it.
29    That well came to me, I Zacharias son of Pses,
30    through my blessed mother Manna, so that you become
31    lord of that well, from its foundations
32    up to its top, with its appurtenances (chresteria) belonging to it,
33    according to its situations, ancient and old, thus
34    holding it, the price which we agreed upon with
35    one another; and our portion has become two (parts), this
36    one solidus (holokottinos) of pure gold, making one nomisma, one pure (obryzon), so that
37    you may come in and take possession and become lord
38    of that whole well, that one which is within the door of my dwelling
39    of Tsacho, daughter of Jacob the blind man, and you become its lord from its foundations up to its top, with all its appurtenances belonging
40    to it, because its price has come to my hand from your hand, from hand
41    to hand, in approved gold and as capital, according to
42    the agreement which I first declared above to assign it
43    to you, with power to assign it to you, to administer it, to manage
44    it, to embellish it, to dwell in it, to give it for the ration (eshkar),
45    to donate it, to cede it, to make it
46    an exchange, to give it away, to make a gift of it, to give it utterly away, to leave it to your sons and your daughters,
47    so that your heirs may receive it, to use it in any
48    manner whatsoever, in every possession and lordship and tenure
49    eternal, at no time shall anyone come out against you ever, neither
50    I, nor brother, nor sister, nor son of one, or son of two, nor on my father's side, shall sue you in a court of law
51    or outside a court of law, or set up any claim (proselusis)
52    against you on the part of any authority whatever. First indeed
53    he shall be alien to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
54    and he shall give to the account of the fine six solidi (holokottinoi)
55    to that authority, and afterwards he shall come in and be bound
56    to this sale, it being valid for you. I have established this deed of sale, secured, in force,
57    in every place where it shall be produced through any authority
58    and power, and they shall question me, that I acknowledge,
59    I Ananias son of Pses, that I consent (stoichein) to this sale.
60    I, Markos son of Papnoute — this person asked me — I wrote
61    on his behalf, because he does not understand (letters), and I am a witness.
62    I, Georgios son of Daniel, I am a witness.
63    I, Faustos son of Andreas, I am a witness.
64    Through me, Aristophanes son of John, from the fortress of Memnonia, I wrote.
