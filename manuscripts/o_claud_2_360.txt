[TRANSLATION]
1     To Caecilius Vindix, centurion of Papeiris
2     Apollinaris, curator of the praesidium of Raima,
3     to his master, greetings.
4     Having received your letters, immediately
5     I sent [them] through Debatus' custody at the same hour. I pray that you are well, progressing
6     to greater things.
7     Mesore 27
