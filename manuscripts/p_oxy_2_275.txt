1   Tryphon son of Dionysios
2   son of Tryphon, mother [Th]amoun[i]s daughter
3   of Onnophris, and Ptolemaio[s] son of Pausirion
4   son of Ptolemaios, mother Ophelous daughter of
5   Theon, a weaver, both of those from Oxy-
6   rhynchus city, acknowledge to one another: Tryphon, that he has given
7   out to Ptolemaios his own son Thoo-
8   nis, mother Saraeus daughter of Apion, not
9   yet of (full) age, for a period of one year
10   from the present day, to serve
11   and do all that is enjoined on him
12   by Ptolemaios, according to the
13   weaving craft in its entirety, <and Ptolemaios (agrees) to teach the boy thoroughly, according to the
14   weaving craft,> just as he himself
15   knows it — the boy being fed and clo-
16   thed for the whole period by
17   his father Tryphon, to whom also belong
18   all the public dues of the boy; on condition that
19   Ptolemaios will give him, each month,
20   on account of maintenance, five drachmas,
21   and at the close of the whole period,
22   on account of clothing, twelve drachmas;
23   it not being permitted to Tryphon to take the
24   boy away from Ptolemaios until the
25   period is completed; and however many days within
26   this (period) he plays truant, for the
27   equal (number) he shall provide him [af]ter the per-
28   iod, or let him [pa]y, for [ea]ch day,
29   one silver [dr]achma; and for taking (him) a-
30   way within the per[iod], a penalty
31   of one hundred drachmas, and to the public (treasury)
32   the same. And if Ptolemaios himself
33   does not teach the boy thoroughly, let him be liable
34   to the equal penal[t]ies. The
35   teaching-contract is valid. Year 13 of Ne[r]o Claudius
36   Caesar Augustus Germanicus
37   Imperator, month Sebastos 21.
38   (2nd hand) I, Ptolemaios [son of Pa]usirion
39   son of Ptolemaios, mother Ophe-
40   lous daughter of Theon, will do each thing
41   in the one year.
42   I, Zoilos son of Horos son of Zoilos, mother
43   Dieus daughter of Sokeus, wrote
44   on his behalf, as he does not know letters.
45   Year thirteenth of Nero Claudius Caesar
46   Augustus Germanicus
47   Imperato[r], mo(nth) Sebastos 21.
