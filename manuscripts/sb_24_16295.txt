[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Agathides, strate]gos, and
2     Cavalry-commander, from Theambesis
3     Daughter of Onnophris, wine-seller. Being wronged by Heliodorus son of Bromius, of those
4     Dwelling in
5     The same Oxyrhyncha of the
6     Polemon's division, I have submitted to you a memorandum
7     Explaining to you [that he is] knowing to me
8     To give me to him by hand
9     Copper drachmas 1464 and wheat artabas 1 1/2 of the 5th year, Tybi [month]
10    On the understanding that he owes me interest on the copper
11    Until Phamenoth 30 of the same year,
12    The time having passed,
13    [He is] being demanded [to pay] by me
14    [But] he has given me [a] gaunake [type of garment]
15    In Pharmouthi of the same year
16    On the understanding that [it] will be brought [to] the 30th
17    [Of] the same m[onth, it being] not worthy
18    [Being] of the aforementioned [sum]
19    [Amount]. [Since] you [having written] to Chaerephanes [the] epistates to send him to you
20    So that he might be compelled, having received the gaunake, to return to me the aforementioned [amounts]
21    As I requested, [he] made no
22    Account of me, and the gaunake is rotting lying [there],
23    I ask you to write to Chaerephanes
24    More solicitously, [and] having pledged him, send [him]
25    To you so that not concerning the
26    Same [matters] will I trouble you, but
27    Through you I might obtain assistance.
28    Farewell.
29    To Chaerephanes.
30    Send to us
31    The [ones] being accused, year 7, Thoth 22
32    To Chaerephanes
33    For reading.
