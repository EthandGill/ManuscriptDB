[TRANSLATION]
1     Ioannes, comes
2     furnish for the funeral of Dorotheos the boy one and a half gold nomismata, [total] one and a half gold nomismata only
3     Year 152 121 Pachon 10, 14th indiction. I noted one and a half gold nomismata only toward Germanus the superintendent of the letter-writer
