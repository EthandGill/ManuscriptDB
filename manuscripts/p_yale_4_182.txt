[TRANSLATION]
1     A memorandum of me Ioannes the scribe through Phoibammon
2     the shipowner. I have received from Joseph the foreman of the estate of Themoin …
3     for the produce of the first indiction of rough grain
4     kangella, one hundred fifty-two artabae only.
5     Pauni 2, year 1 of the indiction, through me Ioannes the scribe.
