[TRANSLATION]
1     Having learned that your Magnificence has reached Cynopolis, I resolved to write and to do obeisance to
2     her honored feet, and then to make known to her in detail the matter
3     concerning the people from Mouchis. By the grace of the Lord Christ we have completed the
4     remainder of the money-taxes of the twelfth indiction and the residue of the grain-shipment of the thirteenth, and of the money-taxes
5     … eleven solidi which were given formerly to the headman of the same village,
6     less two-thirds of a solidus, and of grain thirty-four artabas by the kankellos-measure, and these under our
7     seal we have handed over to the headman of the same village, all the aforesaid eleven
8     solidi. And I came also to Pinyris in order that we might get an answer there and
9     might seal (the documents), and I myself stayed with the headman, and I have spent two days
10    coming up to them, and I got no answer from them, and many are
11    the (debts) owed to us by those people too. And if my lord sees fit to send … or
12    younger men so that they may … and to send (them), that we may be able to be rid
13    of this trouble. For I have spent nineteen days today on this matter; for it was written to me
14    by those with you, my lord, that, while I am getting an answer, we should render at Pinyris — read on the back —
15    to you what is yours, and we too take up what is ours. For before …ing
16    the people of Pinyris do not expect to give anything ever with goodwill. Having written these things
17    I do obeisance to her very much and greet her honored feet.
18    To my lord, in all things most magnificent, worthy of all honor and obeisance, my true brother George, comes and administrator, Victor, the illustris,
19    by God's grace chartularius and your servant.
