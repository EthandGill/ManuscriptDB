[TRANSLATION]
1     Epaphroditos, freedman of Aristokles son of Alexandros,
2     of the people from the city of Oxyrhynchos, a Persian of the epigone,
3     to Theon son of Harpaesis, grandson of Harpaesis, his mother being Tatreiphis
4     daughter of Theon, of the people from the same city, greeting. I acknowledge
5     that I have received from you, from hand to hand out of your house, of imperial silver coinage
6     three hundred and fifty-six drachmas, total of silver 356 drachmas,
7     of principal, to which nothing whatsoever has been added, at interest of a drachma
8     for each mina per month, from the present month
9     Neos Sebastos, which I shall pay you in due order each month;
10    and for the security of these I mortgage to you the twenty-four artabas of Greek natron which I have, and another
11    natron of four talents by weight,
12    stored in a house
13    at the eastern gateway of the house of Taarpaesis, in the quarter of
14    Notou Krepidos, of which by number … was hindered …
15    of which house [of Taar]paesis … the same house having been sealed
16    by me and you; and I shall pay you back the principal on the
17    thirtieth of the month Germanikeios of the present second
18    year of the Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus
19    without any delay; but if I do not pay back as has been written
20    it shall be permitted to you, by yourself, to open the house and sell
21    the natron at its current price [and]
22    to recover the aforesaid principal, the credit
23    being with you, of the … concerning the natron
24    the risk being upon … the
25    rest I shall pay you back without delay or evasion,
26    with the addition of one half plus the interest of the overdue time,
27    the right of execution being yours from me and from all my
28    possessions as if by legal judgement, without prejudice in the execution
29    to the others which I owe you according to two other handwritten bonds upon mortgage
30    of salt-land, nor of the others which I owe you in pledges …
31    The bond is valid wherever produced and for everyone producing it;
32    Year 2 of the Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus,
33    month Neos Sebastos 2. I, Epaphroditos, freedman of Aristokles, have received from hand to hand the
34    three hundred and fifty-six
35    drachmas of silver upon mortgage of twenty-four artabas of Greek natron and four talents by weight,
36    and I shall pay back on the thirtieth of the month Germanikeios of the present
37    [year 2] in due order with the monthly interest, without prejudice to Theon in the execution of the others which I owe him according to two other handwritten bonds upon mortgage of salt-land, nor of the others which
38    I owe in pledges, as is set forth above;
39    Year second of the Emperor Caesar
40    [Nerva] Trajan Augustus Germanicus,
41    Hathyr 2.
42    Year 2, Hathyr 2, bond of Epaphroditos.
