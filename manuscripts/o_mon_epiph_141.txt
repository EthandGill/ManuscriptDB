[TRANSLATION]
1     … this letter (chartes) … if … [your] holy writings … holy father … my least estate …
2     … your salvation through … the name greatly, greatly … to hear it at all times,
3     [every (time)] … but truly they … him, that he reported (it)
4     … they reported some [words] not seemly, that he sent (them)
5     [your] fatherhood knows that I did not report (it),
6     … my words I shall say, that I gave him no pretext (aphorme) [to report] unseemly words, but … only that they … with one another, and [that] peace come into their midst,
7     [not only] … that he did not persuade (him) to receive peace
8     [for him], but also he did the other thing, a great non-existence (i.e. great wrong), in that he reported words
9     [that were] not seemly, in line with the evil counsel
10    of the one who took counsel against him, and again truly
11    I did not set him outside, as if he had reported a word
12    after me; but firstly indeed, that he ordained presbyters for himself alone, and again that he cast aside
13    the great commandment of God,
14    apart from him, which is love toward
15    one's neighbor; and again that what is fitting
16    it is, that he keep himself from reporting
17    words of others, to scandalize (them),
18    because there is no way, moreover, to write much that you know,
19    [as to] the madness of his kind. Therefore concerning the order (keleusis) of your fatherhood that …
20    … [behold] we set him within, [that he] gather (synagein); but it shall not happen
21    … [that] the cleric go within
22    … to gather therein without my appointing him
23    … nor that he make peace with … the presbyters and that they unite with one another … your fatherhood that you … ap-…
