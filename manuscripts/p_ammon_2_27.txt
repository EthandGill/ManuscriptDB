[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Ammon son of Petearbeschinis, advocate (scholastikos), from Panos polis
2     of the Thebaid, to Aurelius Faustinus, fellow-citizen and friend, former magistrate,
3     councillor (bouleutes) of the same city, greeting. Since I happen for the time being to be occupied, having now a most urgent matter in hand
4     and the season of agriculture is also pressing me to set out on a journey abroad, seeing at present that, lest our land, becoming unsown, ensnare me with respect to the public dues (eisphorai),
5     I charge you, according to this my written
6     mandate, to fill (sow) my land for the time being. For I have heard that someone
7     suddenly arriving from outside, in a violent manner or by fraud, is attempting to seize the slaves in Alexandria belonging to
8     my brother Harpokration, as being now masterless and as
9     (if) he died with no heirs. I charge
10    you therefore to instruct my lord the most eminent (vir perfectissimus) prefect of
11    Egypt, Flavius Nestorios, concerning this, namely that there exists a brother
12    of that deceased Harpokration, born of the same father and the same mother, his lawful heir, named Ammon,
13    who also bears the burdens of his few affairs in the Thebaid,
14    and those of (lands) that have become dry, and by the necessity
15    of the laws forcing him into the care of these (matters) on account of the public taxes; and this mandate to you,
16    written by me in my own hand, I deliver, it being valid
17    and secure, so that you may be able, on my behalf, to make a plea in the
18    court lawfully, and either to recover the sla[ves]
19    or to request that they be kept by the providence of the office (taxis), until
20    the court receives the full assurance, with me
21    being present.
