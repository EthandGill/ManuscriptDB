[TRANSLATION]
1   In the consulship of Domitius Modestus, prefect
2   [of the sa]cred praetorium, and of Arinthaeus, the most illustrious
3   count and master of soldiers, Thoth 4.
4   To Aurelius Hermeinos son of Hermeias, councillor of Hermopolis
5   the great and most illustrious,
6   fr[om] Au[r]elius Silvanus son of Hermodoros, councillor of the
7   s[am]e city. I agree, willingly and of my own choice,
8   to have [le]ased fr[om yo]u, for the single coming (and) prosperous
9   sowing of the sixteenth — that is, first — indiction,
10   of the second harvest, the farm-plot belonging to you, of
11   however many arouras, in which (there is) a cistern, and all the equipment, the (land)
12   in the fields of the village of Thrage of the Hermopolite (nome), for sowing
13   and planting with whatever I choose, at a fixed rent
14   agreed between us, for each aroura
15   of those that shall appear by survey at the time, by a just measuring-cord,
16   at the very sowing: of those laid down in fallow-rotation
17   in wheat, twenty arouras, at six artabas
18   of wheat (each); and the remaining ones laid down in pulses,
19   at four artabas of barley (each). Which rent, gathered
20   and shown (to be due), I will pay you and measure out
21   at harvest-time — wheat and barley, new, clean, free of clods,
22   unadulterated, sieved — in the month Epeiph of the same
23   second indiction, by the Athenian measure, at the threshing-floors
24   of the same village, the public dues of all kinds being charged to you,
25   Hermeinos. And if not, it shall not be permitted to you afterwards to lease (it) to another.
26   The lease is valid, and, on being formally questioned, I agreed. Thoth 4.
27   (2nd hand) I, Aur(elius) Silvanus son of Hermodoros, have leased as a-
28   bove, and I will furnish you two baskets
29   of barley-chaff.
