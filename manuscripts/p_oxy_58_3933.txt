[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of the same most pious our
2     master, in the fifth year, Pharmouthi 18, of the 6th indiction.
3     To the most admirable Theodoros, goldsmith,
4     son of the late Serenos, from this
5     city of the Oxyrhynchites, Aurelius Serenos, a worker
6     of the goldsmiths, son of the late Apoll[o ] …
7     from the same city, greeting. I acknowledge
8     [of my own free] will and by deliberate choice
9     that I have agreed with your admirableness
10    [from] the new-moon of the past month
11    Thoth of the present sixth indiction, on condition that
12    I fulfil the whole office of the worker of the
13    goldsmiths with her (your establishment)
14    blamelessly and unhesitatingly and unblamably, and that I receive from her,
15    by way of my wage for the whole year,
16    three gold solidi by the private
17    standard, that is, 3 gold solidi only, and that I am not able
18    to withdraw from your admirableness
19    and from the same service before the end
20    of the said year; and if I do this,
21    on condition that I receive nothing at all
22    out of my whole wage; and if she too
23    casts me out without any cause,
24    on condition that she too be penalized the whole
25    of my wage. And it was also agreed … t …
26    … … that I not …
27    … any work whatever …
28    of our craft, on condition that I perform (it) and that she receive the half
29    … part the wage. Valid is the agreement,
30    [writ]ten in a single (copy), and on being questioned I acknowledged it. Aurel[ius]
31    S[er]enos, a worker of the goldsmiths, son of
32    A[po]llo, the aforesaid, have made
33    [thi]s agreement as aforesaid. Pseeios
34    … I wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
35    And I acknowledge that I have already received from you, from here,
36    my wage in full.
37    Through me, Anastasius, it was completed.
38    Agreement of Serenos, worker of the goldsmiths, son of
39    Apollo, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites.
