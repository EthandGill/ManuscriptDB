[TRANSLATION]
1     Polemon to Polemon, greetings.
2     I have written to Artemidoros, the
3     collector, to hold back [matters] concerning
4     Kerkeosira until he/she arrives to the city, and
5     Aristippos to him/her to acknowledge in return. You therefore do not give
6     backing to anyone, for indeed not
7     do you have today [any] produce.
8     and if he/she provides you trouble
9     go up together with him/her.
10    Farewell. of year 3, Choiak 4.
11    To Polemon.
