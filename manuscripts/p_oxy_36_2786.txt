1     Sarapion to Ioustos, the
2     son, greetings.
3     I sent to you through Papyrios
4     drachmas twenty-four and two obols, which you shall cancel on behalf of
5     Hermes and Achilles
6     and you shall signify to me concerning the
7     having received. Greet the
8     brothers of you, with whom also
9     be well. Pachon, sixth.
10    … to Ioustos the son.
