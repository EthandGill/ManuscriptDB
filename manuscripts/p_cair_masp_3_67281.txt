1     [Flavius] Ioannes [Menas] [J]ustinian[os Demosth]enes Str[aton H]elias Th[eodoros] [Dio]skoros the most-magnificent governor of the Theban province,
2     to Apo[llos] of Isaakios.
3     [those] fulfilling the common of [Aph]rodite [the] village, through Charisios and Bot the advocates, having used petition to the tribunal, requested you [to oversee] the said
