1     … [to] Zenon greetings. The five yoke-pairs we have sent to you.
2     [Well then you will do] care in making [it] so that both the supplies and the
3     [wages that have accrued be given] duly and if they complete the twenty
4     [arurae, release them]. Farewell. Year 34, Phaoph 14.
5     To Zenon.
