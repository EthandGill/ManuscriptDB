1     Polemon to the lesonis of Soknebtynes the great god
2     [Greetings]. You will do well
3     for us three houses and
4     [mattresses] and the other
5     necessary things because of the
6     us to come
7     with entire household, so that
8     it might not happen we having come
9     to be despised
10    Farewell. Year 36, Pharmouthi 11.
