[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: [Year] 5 of Lucius Septimius [Severu]s Pius [Pertin]ax Augustus [date?]. We, Lyncheus, son of Lyka[rion] and associates, sitologoi of the village of Apias, have had measured to us from the produce of the same year with respect to Stotoetis, son of Stotoetis, through Kephalon, son of Apias, for the dues on katoikoi by levelled public measure epaiton fifteen artabas of wheat = 15 artabas of wheat. (m. 2) I, [P]olion, sitologos elect, have had measured to me the said fifteen artabas of wheat.
