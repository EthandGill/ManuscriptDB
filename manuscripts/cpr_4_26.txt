[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial
2     Trinity, of the Father and the Son and the Holy [Spirit].
3     This which I am going to disclose, its position, of old, out
4     to you now, square-built, the west, the catholic
5     church, the east, Stephanos the son of Papnoute, the north, the house of Stephanos Papnouthios, the south, the public road,
6     [with the gate of] the Authentes opening onto it, the north, Stephanos Pa[pnou]thios; and this is the price which we agreed
7     upon with one another, [for] both parts,
8     it is two solidi of gold, of refined gold, of the weight-standard
9     of the fortress of Djeme, making in gold 2 nomismata, two, the full price,
10    it has come to us from you, hand to hand, full, complete,
11    of good value to you, Papnouthios, the son of Stephanos,
12    through hand into hand from the house, of approved gold,
13    and as principal; from now on it belongs to you, you Papnouthios the son of Stephanos, who possess and who I have made known by his name from above many times, according to the agreement which I first declared to you above,
14    to acquire it for you, to acquire it again for you, to administer it,
15    to manage it, to embellish it, to dwell
16    in it, to give it as a gift, to cede it,
17    to make it a thing of exchange, to give it out, to bestow it,
18    to bestow it back, to leave it to your children,
19    to make your heirs inherit it, to use it
20    in every favor that you wish, according to every possession
21    and lordship and holding for ever, with full authority
22    and unhindered ever at any time. He who shall come
23    against you, you the most illustrious Papnouthios the son
24    of Stephanos whom I have first declared from above,
25    neither us, nor brother, nor sister, nor first cousin, nor
26    second cousin, nor relative, nor relative of relative, nor
27    first cousin, nor neighbor, nor on my father's side, nor
28    on my mother's side, that he should bring suit against you in a tribunal
29    or outside a tribunal, or in the district or outside
30    the district, in the praetorium or outside the praetorium, or that I should make any approach against you near us, any authority whatsoever, however exalted
31    or however glorious, or that I should bring charge against you, or that I should not bring charge concerning this sale, this one, or a part of it,
32    at all, that we should move against you; near us any
33    motion of a praetorium, or through an official or an ecclesiastic, first indeed
34    that the same persons help no one, in the first place
35    indeed; he is a stranger to the Father and the Son and
36    the Holy Spirit, and he shall give to the account
37    of the penalty, of the authority that is in force now,
38    twelve solidi of gold, apart from the penalty which the law defines
39    upon him who shall dare and transgress against all these things;
40    and he shall come in and consent to that which is the pure
41    sale, and every matter written in it.
42    For your security, then, I have made it, fixed, having power,
43    and guaranteeing in every place where they shall produce
44    it, and they shall ask what we acknowledge.
45    It was read out to us, we heard it, we confirmed it, with subscribers and a witness, we issued it
46    according to the order of the law.
47    I, Theutorake the son of Ezekiel, bear witness according to the request, Maria, I assent.
48    I, Souai the son of the blessed Philotheos, I assent, a witness
49    according to …
50    I, Dimetriou the son of Pmai, I assent, a witness
51    I, Chamoul the son of the blessed Petrou, I assent, a witness
52    I, Seueros the son of the blessed Petre, I bear witness,
53    I assent, a witness.
54    I, Chael the son of the blessed Abriam,
55    I assent, a witness. I, comes Iohannes
56    wrote on his behalf, that he does not understand, and the subscribing.
