[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 22, [epagomenal] days 5, bronze[...] …
2     … [to the] chief-guard of Muchos from Ar[semtheos] [son] of Teos, wine-merchant. I
3     As I was going up from the vineyard about the tenth hour, certain people rushed [upon] and went [away] having the she-ass and the foal of [hers]
4     which were worth 60 drachmas and leather-bottles 8 drachmas and [wine-]jars 2 which were worth 30 drachmas
5     and a garment which was worth 30 drachmas [and bronze] in a vessel 48 drachmas and 2 obols. Total:
6     bronze 106 drachmas and 2 obols. I have therefore written [to you]
7     [in order] that you might know.
8     Farewell. Year 22, Thoth 1
