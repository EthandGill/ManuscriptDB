[TRANSLATION]
1     We, Shen[oute] (and) John… the sons of [the late]
2     Germanos, and Theytourou the daughter of the late Hemai,
3     and Sanagape the daughter of the late Isaac, her mother being the late Tsone — we all, belonging to the fortress of Je[me],
4     the nome of the city of Ermont, who afterward provide a subscriber to subscribe on our behalf, with other trustworthy witnesses, these who shall bear witness to it,
5     on our behalf, concerning this document of division which serves as a settlement,
6     not to be transgressed and not to be shaken,
7     out of the laws — we write to our brother Stephanos
8     the son of the late Germanos, the man of this same fortress,
9     namely, since we sued one another
10    before Komes the administrator concerning the house of our late
11    father Germanos, this which is on the street of Klol; he commanded certain great men of the fortress
12    to make division upon us; they cast lots, and the lot
13    came up upon you, you Stephanos: you got the central dining-room which is on the courtyard, you got the two-thirds share
14    of the upper hall and the quarter of the dining-room
15    as it is from the southern vault; and the … of the four (parts) of the hall
16    above it belong to the granary (?), with the storehouse for water
17    and the courtyard, becoming common — this is the part
18    of the places that our brother Stephanos got within
19    of our late father Germanos. So that to you, you may become lord of it from now and forever,
20    you and your children and your heirs,
21    nor at any season shall any come out against you ever, whether
22    we ourselves or our children, to sue you. There is no
23    authority that is a stranger to the Father and
24    the Son and the Spirit, and he shall pay ten holokottinoi
25    as a fine to the authority; and afterward he shall come
26    in and assent to this division which we have drawn up, valid and binding in every place where they shall produce it, and they shall ask us. We acknowledge,
27    I, Senouthios the least archpresbyter, and (?)
28    the son of the late Germanos, I consent.
29    I, Theytorou the daughter of Hemai, I consent.
30    I, Ioannake the son of Mas …
31    I, Aron the son of Eli[as], give witness.
32    I, Georgios the son of Kosma, give witness.
33    Through me, Aristophanes son of John, it was made.
34    It is clear that … the last, the door, and the … l
35    descending, which is outside the door of the porch,
36    becomes common. (Greek.)
