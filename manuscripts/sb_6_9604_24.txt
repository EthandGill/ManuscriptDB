r.1    Τιθοητίων καὶ οἱ σὺν αὐτῷ ἐπιτηρηταὶ ἱερᾶς πύλης Σοήνης διὰ Μάρκου Ἀννίου Νεμωνιανοῦ ἀπαιτητοῦ. ἐμέτρησεν

[TRANSLATION]
1     Tithohethyon and those with him, overseers of the sacred gate of Syene, through Marcus Annius Nemonianus debt-collector. He measured
2     through Pachompaonnophris priest
3     on behalf of myrobalan harvest of the third nomes of year 12
4     in the name of Ammon's field
5     from two shares the two-thirds [portion].
6     Of year 13 of Aurelius Antoninus
7     Caesar of the lord Pharmuthi 5.
