[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Athenodoros, of equestrian rank, former exegetes of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians,
2     neokoros of the great Sarapis,
3     from Aurelius Ptolemaios alias Bes­arion, former gymnasiarch, councillor
4     and however I am styled, of the most illustrious Hermoupolis.
5     I wish, of my own free will, to lease from you for
6     only the present 1st year the arouras belonging to you around
7     Tekerkethothis, from the allotment of Klearchos and Daïkrates, eleven arouras, among which is unwatered land, for the sowing of wheat and the laying-down of fodder-crops, as to the half the pond-flooded land, at a rent for each
8     aroura of that in cultivation, as it shall appear from land-survey — of that in wheat, five artabas of wheat,
9     and of that in fodder, likewise, [in silver,] drachmas
10    … which I shall also repay to you in the month of Pharmouthi
11    of the same year without delay, the silver being of good standard, and the wheat new, pure, unadulterated, unsifted-refuse-free, barley-free, sifted, by the Athenian measure, at the threshing-floors of the same Tekerkethothis,
12    all the public dues and the additional assessments
13    on the land being to the account of your estate,
14    and being questioned I acknowledged. Year 1 of Imperator
15    Caesar Marcus Aurelius Claudius
16    Pius Fortunate Augustus, Phaophi 19.
17    I, Aurelius Ptolemaios alias Bes­arion, former gymnasiarch, have leased
18    as aforesaid.
