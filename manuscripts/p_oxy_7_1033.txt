[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: In the 2nd consulship of our sovereign Arcadius, eternal Augustus, and of Flavius Rufinus the most illustrious, Phaophi 21. To Septimius Paulus and Claudius Tatianus, riparii of the Oxyrhynchite nome, from Aurelius Gaius and Aurelius Theon, both night-strategi of the said city. Being entrusted with the care of the peace we are irreproachable in our obedience to public orders, and also intent upon the guardianship of the city. We are often called upon for the production of various persons in accordance with the command of our lords the superior officials, but having no assistance either of public guards or inspectors we often run the risk almost of our lives because these assistants have been taken from us and we go about the city on the watch all alone. Therefore to safeguard ourselves we present this petition requesting either that we should be given the proper assistance of the public guards and the inspectors as aforesaid or that we should have no concern for the guardianship of the city or the production of persons who are wanted, in order that we may not incur risk.

3     To Septimius Paul and Claudius Tatianus, riparii of the Oxyrhynchite [nome],
4     from Aurelius Gaius and Aurelius Theon, both night-strategi of the same
5     city. Being entrusted with the care of the peace and serving irreproachably the public commands, being also devoted
6     both to the guardianship of the city, we are continually compelled
7     on account of the production of various persons in accordance with the command
8     of our superiors, the greater rulers; and not having
9     our assistance—either indeed the public guards and the inspectors—
10    often we have been in danger, almost to say into our souls, because
11    these have been taken from us, and going about
12    the city alone and watching. For this reason, securing ourselves,
13    we present this petition requesting either that the proper
14    assistance to us—both the public guards and the inspectors, as we said before—
15    be given, or that we have freedom from concern concerning both the guardianship
16    of the city and concerning the production of the sought persons, in order that we not become liable to danger.
