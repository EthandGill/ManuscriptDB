1     [Cop]y of a contract-will. In the sec[on]d year of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus
2     [Germanic]us, in the month Peritios 28, Choiak 28, in Ptolemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome. In (the priesthood of) Julia
3     [Augusta]. Sataboutos, son of Tesieous, Persian of the epigone, about 40 years old, of great stature, with a scar on a fin[g]er
4     … [of the] left [h]and, acknowledges to his present and cohabiting wife Taarpagathe, daughter of Tesenouphis, [ab]out 25 years old, with a scar
5     … on the right …, with as guardian her brother by the same father and the same mother Stotoetis, about 33 years old, with a scar on the right shin,
6     [that he has from] her, for her benefit, a dowry [t]hrough [h]and in silver of one hundred drachmas. So let their married life remain
7     [blameless, j]ust as before; but if, a disagreement arising between [t]hem, they should separate from one another, let Sataboutos repay
8     [to Taarpaga]the the dowry [o]f one hundred [si]lver drachmas, in the case of dismissal immediately, but in the case of voluntary [separation] within thirty days from when [ev]er it is demanded back; and if he does not repay, as has been written, the right of execution shall belong to
9     [Taarpaga]the both [agai]nst Sataboutos and against all his property, as if by [l]egal [judgment]. Being present
10    [at the magis]tracy, the mother of Sataboutos, Segathis, daughter of Erieus, about 66 years old, with a scar on the left shin, about … …
11    … on the right, with as guardian her [deceased] [broth]er's son Tesenouphis, son of [Teseno]uphis, about 40 years old, with a scar on the forehead on
12    [the right, agr]ees that it shall be, after her own death, of her afore[writ]ten son Satab[ou]tos
13    … the hundred (drachmas) being [t]hose of Sataboutos and of the same-father … [Taarpag]athou and Tabous
14    … deceased … of the … of the son of the … [same-]father [broth]er …
