[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysios to Hephaestion, his brother, greetings.
2     If all else proceeds well with you as is proper,
3     it would be as I wish; and you yourself are well, and
4     Eudaimonins and the children and Isias and your child
5     and all [those] in the house. Having received your
6     letter from you, in which you made clear [that you had] been saved
7     from great dangers and [that you] are in detention,
8     regarding your being well I gave thanks to the gods,
9     but I wished also that you had come to the
10    city, just as Konon and all the others who have been [seized], so that Isias
11    of your child, having come to the extreme [distress],
12    having saved him in every way, and even such
13    times/seasons having endured, now having seen you [might obtain] some
14    relief. For not at all is it necessary, holding you back strictly, to wait until [we] provide something and bring [it] down, but everyone tries, whenever
15    [he] has been saved from dangers, to come quickly
16    and to embrace [both] the wife and the children
17    and friends. Therefore you would do well, if something
18    more necessary does not constrain you, having made effort
19    to come, and taking care of [your] health,
20    so that you [will] be well, be strong. Year 2, Epeiph 30.
21    To Hephaestion.
