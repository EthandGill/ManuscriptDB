[TRANSLATION]
1     memorandum to Z[enon]
2     to the father from Cleon. What is for me to receive
3     salary of Epeiph:
4     of oil and meat drachmas five,
5     of relish drachmas two [obol-and-a-half], castor-oil obol three and a half, four,
6     of fuller's-earth [obol-and-a-half], seasonings obol one and a half, and to the
7     mother drachmas three [obol-and-a-half] quarter-obol
8     the total becomes drachmas twelve
9     Z[enon].
