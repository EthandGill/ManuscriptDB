[TRANSLATION]
1     (page) 148
2     Flavius [Paniskos also called L]ongus, strategos of the Hermopolite. Of the reports [given to me by the] village-chiefs of [the village of Eko]us and Temenkyrkis, Poi[menon and Sinkatape and] Timonthis, who present [those through them r]egistered [for imprisonment] in the jail, equally with the public (copy) is set forth, so that all may know, both those reported [may keep] the things entrusted to them soundly and faithfully, so as to be blamed in nothing. I have signed.
3     Year 12 of our lord Galli[enus Augustus, P]auni 6.
4     To Flavius Paniskos also called Longus, strate[gos]
5     of the Hermopolite,
6     from the Aurelii Phibis son of Pemsitis, his mother being Tisois, and Taurinos son of Laitos, his mother being
7     Eus, both village-chiefs of the village
8     of Ekous. We give and report
9     the undersigned for imprisonment in the jail in the metropolis,
10    he being of means and suitable, at the risk of us and of all those residing in the same village, by
11    mutual guarantee, for whom we ourselves also stand surety.
12    He is:
13    …s son of Tothes, his mother being Eus,
14    aged about 45, having means of 1,000 drachmas.
15    Farewell.
16    [Year 12 of] Imperator Caesar Publius
17    [Licinius G]allienus Germanicus
18    [the greatest, Pi]ous, Fortunate, Augustus,
19    [Pauni] 6. We the aforesaid village-chiefs
20    [have submitt]ed (it). I, Aurelius Hermesion, wrote (it).
