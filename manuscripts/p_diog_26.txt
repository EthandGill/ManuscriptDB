[TRANSLATION]
1     … of … [each year] …
2     each (year) in the month Pa[uni] by the dromos [measure of four] choi[nikes, the] public [dues, both the artaba-tax and the dyke-tax and the surveying-charges being my responsibility, the lessee, but all the [other] public (dues) being your responsibility, Fabius
3     Diogenes. And I shall perform at my own expense the yearly far[mi]ng work[, all of it]: dyke-work,
4     waterings, under-diggings, weedings, sip[h]on-draini[ngs,] embankings of canals and aqueducts, doing no damage. And [a]fter the [aforesaid] time of the
5     lease I shall hand over the half part from the cutting of grass, and the remaining
6     half part from stubble, as I received (it), I sowing with whatever crops I may choose except safflower. And I shall also sow the two arouras of sacred land which you have in another seal, lying
7     by the publi[c] road, of which I shall pay all the public dues, both grain and money,
8     at my own expense, (these arouras) remaining clear for the rendering to you, Fabius Dioge[ne]s, of the
9     ten artabas of wheat, if it seems good to lease. Lucius Fabius Diogenes:
10    I have leased to Diodoros the aforesaid six arouras, at a rent
11    per year, apart from every reckoning, of ten artabas of wheat by the dromos measure of four choinikes, and on condition that for the same years he sow my two arouras of sacred land, on condition of paying for them the yearly grain (dues) and
12    money dues, all, as aforesaid. In the twenty-second year
13    of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Mesore the twenty-sixth.
