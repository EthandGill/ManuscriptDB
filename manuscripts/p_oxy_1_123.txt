[TRANSLATION]
1   To my lord son Dionysotheon,
2   his father, greeting.
3   There is opportunity now too, with the man going up to you.
4   It became necessary for me to address you.
5   I greatly wonder, my son, that to this day I have not received
6   letters from you informing me about your
7   well-being. Even so, my master, write
8   me back quickly; for I am much distressed because
9   I did not receive letters from you. Go to my brother
10   Theodoros and make him take the trouble
11   to go to Timotheos and pass on to him
12   to get his (affairs) ready, so that he may enter to take up his post. For already
13   those of the other cities have brought in their attestation
14   to their colleagues — they have entered. So, on entering
15   with the robe, let the one coming know it, so that ready
16   he may enter. So let them not be willing, without attestation,
17   to set us against one another, as knowing that the same
18   thing befalls us all. For we have been ordered
19   to enter with the cloaks; whence let the one coming
20   come ready, as meaning to take up his post.
21   I greet my sweetest daughter Makkaria
22   and my mistress your mother and all
23   ours by name. I pray for your health for many
24   years, lord son.
25   Epeiph 22.
26   To my lord son Dionysotheon, Ischyrion the tabularius.
