1     [Year … month … in Herakleopolis above Memphis. Ptolema acknowledges, with her guardian,]
2     [her father Ptolemaios son of …,] of the catoecic cavalry … …

5     Tilothis, sixteen arouras of land, which are: in the allotment of Nikanor eight arouras and …
6     from the land of Polemon and Perigenes on the south, toward the north up to the dry land, and …
7     of the allotment of Alexandros toward the east, to make up the eight arouras, becoming …
8     … in Tilothis, of Philotas's fore-tower, to that same Philoxenos and his descendants for all time, in return for which the
9     aforementioned father of the orphan received (it) for the crowns and the other royal dues of the allotment, having had the use; [and that she will furnish]
10    to Philoxenos the land clear of royal dues, those after the second …, and of other cessions for
11    all time. Concerning which Ptolema also happens to have submitted to Archibios, the one in charge of the assessment of the catoecic cavalry, a memorandum,
12    through which she set forth among other things that the father of the orphan had died and had not been able to lease the land except at very
13    much lower rents, which (?) not to manage for the royal dues of the allotment …
14    … the re-registration and demonstration has been made to Philoxenos of the aforewritten arouras. And that neither will Ptolema
15    nor anyone else on her behalf proceed against Philoxenos nor against those of his (party), concerning which Ptolema acknowledges to have ceded to him the land, nor a part, nor in any other way will set up any claim against Philoxenos or those of his. But the …
16    or any other setting up a claim against these, Ptolema further agrees to remove (him) at her own expense; and she agrees to warrant
17    the aforesaid land with every warranty. But if she does not warrant the land as has been written above, or does not remove (the claimant), or proceeds against (him),

22    the execution being as if by legal judgment, and none the less let the things mutually agreed and arranged be valid. This contract
