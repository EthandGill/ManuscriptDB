r.17   τὸν ἀντιποιούμενον· εἰ δὲ μή, ἐκτίσει ἣν ἐκομίσατο τιμὴν καὶ τὸ βλάβος ὁμοίως· ἐὰν δὲ καὶ ὑπὸ ἱερᾶς νόσου

r.23   Κωζα ἀδελφοῦ αὐτῆς. τούτων ἐγένετο ἀντισύγγραφα δύο ὧν τὸ ἕτερον καταχωρισθήσεται ἐν τοῖς ἐνταῦθα

1     Matthabe-ine Abba of [G]ora, a Markouplolite, sold
2     [a] slave of hers, [h]ome-born, Apsal-man, or if also by some
3     other n[ame] [he] is called, [for] six hundred denarii; purchased Mathaath.
4     Under [the] consuls Fulvius Aemilianus [for the 2nd time] and Naivius
5     Acylinus, [on the] eighth [day] before [the] Ides of Nov[ember], which is
6     [the] month Deios, [the] sixth, of [the] year 561, in Markouple, under
7     [the] designated witnesses. [She] sold also
8     [and] registered Matthabe-ine Abba of Gora Markouplolite, with [her] present and he [Koza] signing on her behalf Aurelius Koza [her] brother, [a] slave of hers [h]ome-born [by] name Apsal-man [of] mother Mathseines, [being] about [how many] years
9     thirteen, [somewhat] more, [?], of middle-hair, straight-haired, with somewhat furrowed brows,
10    [with] pierced ears, goat-eyed, healthy and unscarred; purchased
11    the same slave by purchase and sale Aurelia Mathaath
12    [of] Gomaimou, Markouplolite, [at the] price of silver [in] denarii
13    six hundred, and the price having-received the [woman] who bought [it], the [woman] who sold [it] handed over to her the same
14    slave [for her] to have, to possess, to sell, to manage, to use in whatever
15    [way] [she] wishes; and [she] undertakes that if anyone [should] claim
16    [a claim to] the same slave or [a] part of him, standing, [the woman] who sold [it] will defend [the buyer] in court and will make [it] clear and will discharge
17    the [one] claiming [it]; if but not, [she] will pay [the] price which [she] received and the damage likewise; if also [the slave should be] affected by sacred disease
18    [should be] troubled, the same slave, within [the] nearest six months,
19    [the woman] who sold [it] having returned [the] price which [she] received,
20    [will] take [him] back. [That] these [things] thus well [come] to be
21    and [be] guarded in good faith [the woman] who bought [it] asked,
22    in good faith [the woman] who sold [it] acknowledged, with [the] presence of
23    Koza [her] brother. Of these counter-copies two were made, of which the one will be deposited in the here
24    public archives.
25    [It] was written by Balesos [the] legal [scribe].
26    Aurelius Sopatros [I] witness.
