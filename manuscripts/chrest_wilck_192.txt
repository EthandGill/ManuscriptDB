[TRANSLATION]
1     To Stotoetis son of Apynchis and Stotoetis son of Stotoetis
2     the elder, the two lessees of the farmstead
3     of Pisais of Soknopaios the great god,
4     from Herieus son of Herieus, of those from the village
5     of Soknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleides division,
6     a Persian of the epigone. I wish to lease from you, from those things which you too hold on lease in the farmstead, a granary that is in working order, weatherproof and fitted with doors, in which
7     there is a tower and a courtyard and five storerooms and pits and silos and all the rest of the appurtenances, for one year from the present day, the whole rent being
8     of new, clean, unadulterated wheat, of artabas
9     forty-five by the fourth measure of the god Soknopaios, free from risk
10    and from deduction, the repair of the granary
11    or even the rebuilding being incumbent upon
12    us, those about Stotoetis, while the plastering and the watching of the produce brought in
13    are incumbent upon me, Herieus.
14    And further I shall also provide for us a storeroom in the
15    granary without rent and any charge, and I shall pay back the rent in the month
16    Kaisarios of the present thirteenth year,
17    apart from the other lease-holdings which I have on lease in the farmstead …
18    on lease …
19    that the granary be handed over free from all filth together with the doors and keys set upon it, if it seems good to lease it on the aforesaid terms.
20    Herieus son of Herieus, about 50 years old, with a scar on the little finger of the left hand.
21    Year 13 of Imperator Caesar Domitian
22    Germanicus, of the month Germanikeios, 6.
