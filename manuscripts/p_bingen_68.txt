1     To Marcus Ulpius Herculaneus, who is also Heraclides,
2     priest, exegete, and the Caesareans and the other [magistrates],
3     [from] Pelops, son of Pelops, [of great-grandfather Sebaste]-an, who is also Zeneian, of those of the eighth
4     [year] of [the] god Vespasian, who had reached ephebic age. Of the
5     [son] born to me from Sarapias of the …eios
6     citizen-woman who is domiciled abroad in the [regions]
7     over Memphis, [the] son of Pelops being of [proper] age for the introduction into the ephebes,
8     and wishing to register into the
9     [present] seventeenth year [of the] Emperor
10    [Caesar] Nerva Trajan Augustus
11    [Germanic-]Dacic ephebes, I petition you
12    [to arrange] for those [regarding] this matter …
