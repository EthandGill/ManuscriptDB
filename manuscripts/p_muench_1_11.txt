[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Mauricius Tiberius the eternal
2     Augustus emperor and greatest benefactor, in the fifth year, and in the consulship of the same
3     our master, in the third year, Phaophi the tenth, of the fifth indiction, in Syene.
4     Aurelia Tapia daughter of Tsios, her mother being Mariam, without her guardian husband,
5     transacting business and originating from Syene, providing also the subscriber hereinafter
6     who subscribes on her behalf and the witnesses hereinafter by her appointment
7     attesting in addition to this written and valid sale: to Flavius Kyriakos
8     son of Menas, soldier-cavalryman of the unit of Syene, greeting. I, the aforewritten Tapia, acknowledge through this my written sale, willingly and persuaded,
9     without any deceit and fear and force and trickery and compulsion and fraud
10    and flattery and contrivance and all ill-will and malice and
11    knavery and every defect, but of willing intent and guileless purpose and sincere conscience and right mind, according to this simple
12    written security, that I have sold to you today and have conveyed
13    by the law of sale and for eternal possession and with every most complete right of ownership,
14    the half whole portion of a house belonging to me, of whatever arrangement it is,
15    from foundation to air, of which house the other half portion belongs to Jacob
16    my departed husband; furthermore that I have also sold together to you the (parts) belonging
17    to me, the half portions of all the appurtenances, both of the forecourt and the gateway
18    and the pier and the courts and the oven, altogether, with respect to the formerly made
19    sale in the name of myself and of my reposed husband Jacob, the same house being situated
20    in Syene about the southern part of the fortress and
21    about the lane of the public camel-stable of the transport-service of Philae and of the house
22    of Abraamios son of Pachymios. And the neighbors of the whole house, from which I have sold you that very
23    half portion falling to me, from the cell on the first storey facing north
24    onto the public street, and from the dining-room above it on the second storey facing
25    west onto the breadth of the pier, and from the under-pier (room), that is, "chrere," on the first storey,
26    and from the southern dining-room on the third storey facing north onto the common
27    court, and from the air above it up to the air, and from the great eastern
28    chamber up to the air, and the half of all the appurtenances from foundations
29    up to air: south, the blind and narrow street and the house of Abramios son of Pachymios;
30    nor[t]h, the public street; east, the house of Abraamios son of Pachymios; west,
31    the house of Allamon son of Patechnoumios; whoever the neighbors may be on all sides, everywhere,
32    just as I have also dictated by mouth. And nothing at all has been left to me
33    unsold of the aforementioned half portion of the house and of all the
34    appurtenances from foundations up to air, which came to me from the just
35    purchase from John also called Paptsios and from him by paternal succession
36    according to the force of the old sale, at the price mutually
37    agreed upon and consented to, of a principal of five tested gold solidi
38    by the standard of Syene, total 5 gold solidi by the standard of Syene, which the complete and worthy
39    price I have received then and there from you, Kyriakos the buyer, hand
40    into my hand, out of your house in Syene, in number and weight in full, on the
41    present day. And I warrant to you the sale with every warranty against everyone
42    forever proceeding against you or laying claim. The one proceeding against you
43    or laying claim I myself, the seller, shall remove and clear for you
44    at my own expenses and costs, and (I undertake) the law concerning sale and warranty, so that you, Flavius Kyriakos the buyer, from now on
45    shall own and possess and administer and manage and build
46    and sell and bestow and resell and make exchanges and to children and
47    descendants bequeath and do and transact everything concerning it unhindered
48    and unimpeded, and that I shall not bring a charge against you nor will I bring a charge, nor any other of those
49    from my name, now or at any time in any way
50    on any pretext, and that none of my heirs or children or descendants
51    or those akin to my family on the father's or mother's side shall be able
52    to sue you or bring a charge or stir up an arbitration against you on account of this matter,
53    and that the one who shall dare to bring a charge against you or to lay claim against you or to transgress or to disturb
54    this sale shall in no way profit, but shall furnish, by way of penalty
55    for transgression, the aforewritten price twofold, and shall have no force
56    against the things written in this sale, but in addition to him, unwilling, abiding
57    by it as made in a public archive, by reason that I, no compulsion
58    being laid upon me nor force nor trickery nor any deceit, but because it has so seemed good
59    to me, have sold to you the aforesaid half portion of the house and the
60    half of the appurtenances pertaining to it, and that I have received from you
61    the complete and herein-stated price in five gold solidi by the standard of Syene,
62    and have set out for you this sale for security, it being valid and
63    secure and inviolate and unshaken and lawful, produced everywhere
64    and exhibited, with the subscription of the one subscribing on my behalf
65    at my request; and being formally questioned face to face, I acknowledged and released (it).
66    I, Aurelia Tapia daughter of Tsios, her mother being Mariam, from Syene, the aforesaid, have sold
67    the half whole portion of the house belonging to me, from ground up to air, with all its
68    right, and I have received from you the aforewritten complete price in five solidi
69    by the standard of Syene, and I shall warrant to you this sale with every warranty, and everything agrees to me,
70    the things written, as aforesaid. I, Flavius Theodoros son of Abraamios, soldier of the unit of Syene, having been requested, wrote
71    on her behalf, since she does not know letters. I, Flavius Abraam son of Mousaios, soldier of the legion of Syene,
72    witness the sale, having heard from Tapia, who is also the one who made the deed.
73    I, Flavius Komes Isak, soldier of the unit of Syene, witness the sale, having heard from her who made the deed.
74    I, Isakos son of Taeion, archdeacon of holy Mary of Syene, witness
75    the sale, having heard from her who made the deed.
76    I, Flavius Apa Dios son of Praipositos, soldier of the unit of Syene, witness the sale, having heard from her
77    who made the deed. I, Flavius Kyros, defensor of Apollonopolis, found in Syene, witness this
78    sale, having been asked by her who made the deed.
79    Through me, Markos son of Apa Dios, it was written.
80    Sale of the half portion of a house from foundations up to air, sold by Tapia daughter of Tsios, her mother being Mariam, a widow,
81    from Syene, at the price of 5 gold solidi by the standard of S[yene.]
