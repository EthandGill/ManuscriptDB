r.6    ἀπεσχηκέναι τὸν Ἁρποκρατίωνα παρὰ τοῦ Μαμερτίνου ἀνθʼ ὧν ὀφείλει αὐτῷ κατὰ δισσὸν χ[ει]ρόγραφον γεγονὸς τῷ ιϛ ἔτει τῆς εὐτυχεστάτης ταύτης αὐτοκρατορίας μηνὶ Φαρμοῦθι τὸ καὶ δεδημοσιωμένον ἐν τῷ καταλογείῳ ἐξ οὗ καὶ τὰ ἑξῆς νόμιμα πάντα ἐτελείωσεν μέχρι ἐμβαδείας καὶ αὐτῆς

r.8    καὶ ἀνακομιδῆς τοῦ ἐμβαδ[ε]υθέντος τρίτου μέρους ὑπάρχοντος τῷ αὐτῷ Μαμερτίνῳ οἰκίας

r.16   Μαμερτῖνος Νείλου ἐ[ξ]ωδίασα ὡς πρόκειται

[TRANSLATION]
1     (Day) 9, of Mamertinus [son of Neilos: ]600 drachmas of silver
2     In the twentieth year of Imperator Caesar Marc[us Au]relius Antoninus Parthicus Maximus Britannicus
3     Maximus Pius Augustus, in the month Pane[m]os, [Ph]armouthi [9]; through the bank of Anoubion, son of Ammonios, of (the quarter) Matidios
4     also called Kalliteknion, in Antinoopolis, the exchange-bank: Mamertinus, son of Neilos, son of Isidor[os],
5     Nervianios also called Eirenieus, to Harpokration, son of Antonas, son of Sarapion, of (the quarter) Matidios also called Demetrieus,
6     (acknowledges) that Harpokration has been paid in full by Mamertinus, in return for what he owes him according to a duplicate au[to]graph-bond drawn up in the 16th year of this most fortunate imperial reign in the month Pharmouthi, also registered in the records-office, from which he also completed all the subsequent legal formalities up to and including the entry into possession itself,
7     the entry into possession of a principal of three hundred and thirty silver drachmas and the interest on them and the duties and the lawful expenses, those upon dissolution, at his request, on behalf of the dissolution of every debt of any kind whatsoever
8     and the recovery of the third share entered into possession, belonging to the same Mamertinus, of a house
9     and courtyard and all appurtenances, being in the 2nd letter (district), block 7, northern, of Antinoos: six hundred silver drachmas, total 600 silver drachmas, in return for more being owed, everything toward release and
10    annulment and cancellation of every debt of any kind whatsoever and of all the completed legal formalities,
11    and that Harpokras shall make no claim, nor shall make any claim, neither he nor his agents nor others on
12    his behalf, against Mamertinus nor against his agents, concerning anything at all whatsoever, written
13    or unwritten; and he gave back [t]o him what he held in his keeping from the legal transaction, all the documents
14    toward annulment and ca[nc]ellation, as has been declared above. Harpokration, son of Antonas, of (the quarter) Matidi[os] also called Demetrieus,
15    I have been paid in full by Mamertinus the (sums) of release and recovery of the third share which I had entered into possession, of the aforesaid house and all appurtenances, from which I completed all the legal formalities, upon dissolution: six hundred silver dr[a]chmas, upon all the aforesaid stipulations.
16    I, Mamertinus, son of Neilos, have paid out as aforesaid.
