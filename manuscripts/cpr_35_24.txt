[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the] most excellent council of Hermopolis [the great, ancient, splendid]
2     and most august, through [Aurelius Corellius Alexander, of equestrian rank, retired from military] service, and however he is styled, … [incumbent] prytanis of the same [city] …
3     from Aurelius Anoubion son of Horion, [his mother being] …
4     from the village of Titkoeis at … I wish
5     to lease from the civic account those (lands) which I held before
6     in lease around Titkois from the [allotment] of Nostimos,
7     seventeen arouras for four years from the
8     present 14th year of our lord Gallienus
9     Augustus, for the sowing of wheat and grass each year
10    according to the half, of rent and tax: of the (half)
11    in wheat, of seventy-seven fixed artabas,
12    and of the half sown in grass,
13    a fixed (rent) of three hundred eleven drachmas of silver,
14    which I will pay and measure out each year in [Pauni] and
15    Epeiph, the months: the silver I will pay over into the public (treasury),
16    and of the wheat I will measure out into the public granaries
17    [at the first] measuring, and the measure [from] the land …
18    … into the account of the city
19    … and the rest I will measure out by the receiving measure
20    … by the Athenian measure, the six-part …
21    …, all the public dues of the land [and the] additional assessments being chargeable to the civic account; but if,
22    [which] may it not happen, it become unwatered in part, I will not be held to this contract, the right being reserved
23    [to the] city and the council concerning every claim that they have.
24    And if a higher bid be made each year, it shall be permitted to others to re-lease or also to take it over (themselves); and on being formally questioned I acknowledged it.
25    [Year 14 of Imperator Caesar] Publius Licinius Gallienus
26    [Germanicus Maximus,] Persicus [Maximus,] Pius, [Felix,]
27    [Augustus] … I, Aurelius Anoubion son of Horion,
28    [have leased as set forth] … I wrote on his behalf, he declaring that he is illiterate.
