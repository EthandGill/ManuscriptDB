[TRANSLATION]
1     To the keepers of the property-records of the Hermopolite nome,
2     from the Aurelii Hermeinos also called Moros and
3     Theognostos also called Moros, both sons of Hermaios also called Pathotes son of Achilleus, their mother being
4     Souerous, Hermopolitans registered in the quarter City-East.
5     We declare, for the present 6th year of Marcus Aurelius
6     Severus Alexander Caesar the lord,
7     from the inheritance of our paternal uncle
8     Hermeinos son of Achilleus, registered in the City-East quarter, who died childless and intestate, leaving as heirs
9     us and another brother Isidoros, of the same
10    parents, the only heirs, the share belonging to us,
11    a two-thirds part of a whole house and courtyard
12    and fittings and all appurtenances in Hermopolis in the quarter Phrourion-West.
13    Wherefore we report and swear by the fortune of Marcus
14    Aurelius Severus Alexander Caesar the
15    lord that this is so. In the 6th year of the Emperor
16    Caesar Marcus Aurelius Severus Alexander
17    Pius Felix Augustus, Hathyr 3. Aurelius
18    Artemidoros, councillor, keeper of the property-records, through Aurelius Sarapammon, secretary. The name of your paternal uncle being on record,
19    that nothing at all is registered against him as still owing,
20    and it being uncertain whether his inheritance belongs to you, I have received a copy.
