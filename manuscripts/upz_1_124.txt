1     To Krateros, [one] of the first friends and
2     general,
3     from Isidoros, overseer of the village
4     Tachenepre of Memphis. [Of] the sixth year
5     Phaophi 7, Hermias [son] of Dagouzitos of the
6     same [village] delivered to me a memorandum against Chenephnibios [son] of
7     Herieus and his wife
8     Taarmotis [daughter] of Pasites, concerning the
9     [debt] owed to him according to a contract
10    witnessed by six [witnesses], of wheat arouras six, [they] not repaying these and their
11    one-and-a-half [portion], and he demanded [them], summoned, to be paid to him
12    [the wh]eat with the one-and-a-half [portion].
13    [I then] on the eighth of the same m[onth]
14    su[mmon]ed Chenephnibis
15    [and since] he became unwilling [to] acknowledge [the debt],
16    [I han]ded him over [to Peritas, chief] village-elder; [he handed him over] to Ptolema[ios]
17    [the] village-chief, [with him]
18    Hermias and anoth[er] elder
19    of those from the village until the
20    ju[stice] [was] to-be-done to him. But now
21    since Taarmotis [came] upon me with [entreaties] and I have been charged-in-writing as having-shared-in-the-outrage
22    [against] me, which such thing not hav[ing] happened,
23    [I] request [you to] order [to] give me a written [credential], [so that …] I might defend myself regarding the [matters] against me and be troubled by no one,
24    and to write also to Ammonios the sub[command]er under whom I am [to] appoint before you the
25    Peritas and Ptolemaios and
26    Hermias and the elder of the
27    village, so that [they] might bear-witness
28    to you concerning the [matters] regarding me. When this happens I will have obtained the [aid] from you
29    [support]. Year six, Hathyr 30. Farewell.
