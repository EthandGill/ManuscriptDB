[TRANSLATION]
1   (1st hand) …[… lest]
2   I become a fugitive [from my]
3   own (land), I have taken refuge with you, beg-
4   ging, if it seem good to you, to order
5   a letter written to the strategos of the Themistes and
6   Polemon divisions
7   to release me, that I may be helped
8   by you. Prosper.
9   Zoilos, 42 (years old), (a scar) on a finger of the left hand.
10   Year 14 of Imperator Caesar Titus
11   Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus
12   Pius, Pauni 21.
13   (2nd hand) Year 14, Pauni 21.
14   (3rd hand) No one objecting,
15   petition the strategos.
