[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Year 3, Payni 30. Paid into the bank of Ptolemais Euergetis to Philoxenus the banker as for the king, by Helene, daughter of Aphrodisius, as tax for a cultivated vineyard where the vines grow up the trees, of a half aroura, or as many as there might be, and for the concomitant fifth part of a reservoir and of a stone well and all appurtenances which are in ... near Ibion Eikosipentarouron in the district of Polemon, the adjacent properties being: on the South a vineyard of Senesis, daughter of Zosimus, through which the above-mentioned vineyard is watered, on the North a common entrance and an irrigation channel, on the West a vineyard of Isidora, daughter of Isidorus, on the East a garden with olive-plantation of Herodes, son of Apollonius, being in the middle, the ... belonging to Helene through ..., on the 10 talents for which she stated to have bought it by contract from Zosimus, son of Nikanor and his wife Diodora, daughter of Kallion, one talent in copper against silver, total 1 talent. (stamp) Year 3 of Ptolemaios
