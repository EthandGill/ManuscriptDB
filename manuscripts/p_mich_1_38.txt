1     To
2     Semtheus [and] Apollonios.
3     You must receive, according to the
4     contract which Hed[ylos]
5     the architect made: for the seven-couch room
6     and the exedra, panelled window-shutters
7     with hollow framing,
8     closing inward, 10, [having]
9     fillets, at 2 drachmas each, [total 20 drachmas];
10    and for these, hollow-framed (panels),
11    ten, at 4 drachmas each, total 40 drachmas;
12    and double-faced side-doors, [2,]
13    the height of each 3 cubits, the width
14    corresponding, at 6 drachmas each, total [12] drachmas;
15    and for a doorway with hollow framing,
16    height 6 cubits, width
17    corresponding, 12 drachmas;
18    for the bedroom, lattice windows,
19    [8, height … cubits], width
20    corresponding, [at 2 drachmas each], total 16 drachmas;
21    and for these, hollow-framed (panels),
22    closing inward, at 4 drachmas each, total 32 drachmas;
23    a hollow-framed doorway,
24    height 4 1/3 cubits, width
25    corresponding, 8 drachmas;
26    and for the cellar
27    leading into the exedra,
28    height 3 1/3,
29    width 10 pal[ms], 4 drachmas;
30    single-leaf doors of 3 cubits
31    for the staircase, 4 drachmas;
32    and for the privy
33    likewise, 4 drachmas;
34    for the bath, doors,
35    false double-leaved, having
36    solid jambs, 4,
37    at 4 drachmas each, total 16 drachmas;
38    of glue, 12 minas, 12 drachmas.
39    Total: 180 drachmas.
40    [Toward] this you have:
41    through the bank of Art[emidoros]
42    …
43    Pachon …
44    Pauni 4 …
45    7 …
46    19th, 20 drachmas;
47    27th, through Charmos, 8 drachmas.
48    Total: 155 drachmas.

50    And from Theopompos
51    in Krokodilon polis, 12 drachmas;
52    from Sosos, 10 drachmas,
53    through Ephesos, Mesore 19;
54    for the contract-work
55    of the carpentry,
56    up to account, 40 drachmas …
57    Thoth 30, through the bank, 8 drachmas;
58    ep(?)
59    Phaophi 8, through the bank …
60    To Sempheus, who contracted
61    the exedra by the bath,
