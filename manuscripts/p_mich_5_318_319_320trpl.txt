[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] 4th year of Gaius Caesar, Autocrat, Augustus, [in the month] Pachon, [day] 5.
2     Kleon [son] of Palamedes has divided [his portion] and has allotted [it into] two shares falling to me, which
3     and from [the] reading [of the terms] I am persuaded and I will do each [thing] as set forth. Suchion
4     [son] of Palamedes has co-divided [his portion] and has allotted [it into] the third share falling to me,
5     which and from [the] reading [of the terms] I am persuaded, as set forth. Theudas [son] of Patynion
6     has co-divided [his portion] and has allotted [it into] the eighth share falling to me of the aforesaid building plots and courtyards
7     and all appurtenances, which and from [the] reading [of the terms] I am persuaded and I will do each [thing] as set forth. [Someone] wrote for
8     him: Melantas [son] of Kronion, because he did not know letters.
