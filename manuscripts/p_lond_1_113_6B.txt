[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the lord and master
2     Jesus Christ, our God and
3     Saviour, in the reign
4     of our most pious and most benevolent
5     master Flavius Heraclius,
6     the eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the year 23, Mesore 19, 7th indiction, in Arsinoe.
7     To Flavius Gerontios, the respectable (periblepto)
8     count (komes), son of him of illustrious
9     memory, Kollouthos, from the
10    city of the Arsinoites, Aurelius
11    Gerontios, attendant (perichytes) of the public
12    bath, from the same city,
13    son of Phoibammon, dwelling
14    in the quarter of Mouiarion, greeting. I acknowledge
15    that I have leased from your
16    Splendour, from the property belonging
17    to her in this city
18    and in the same quarter, inside
19    the farmstead there, looking
20    toward the south-west, in a house opened
21    toward the east, in the oven (kliba/bake-house)
22    two rooms (places) opened,
23    one toward the north, the [other]
24    toward the east, with
25    their (?) right (of use), for as long a
26    time as you wish, from the
27    past month of Pauni,
28    at the end of the past sixth indiction,
29    and I shall furnish to her, as rent
30    for them, yearly, of gold
31    a half-quarter nomismation,
32    of gold ½, 4 of a nomismation, as is current — that is, for [the] one room (place),
33    a third of a nomismation, and for
34    the other room (place), a nomismation,
35    a third (and) a twelfth. The lease is valid, and on being formally questioned, I acknowledged (it).
36    Aurelius Gerontios,
37    attendant (perichytes) of the public bath,
38    son of Phoibammon, agrees that
39    everything is as aforesaid.
40    Through me, Justus. (= "by my hand, Justus") Through me, Justus.
41    Lease of two rooms (places) in the quarter of Mouiarion, made by Aurelius Gerontios the attendant (perichytes) for Flavius Gerontios the respectable (periblepton) count (komes).
