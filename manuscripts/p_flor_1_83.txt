1     Aureli[us] Agathos Daemon
2     stra[t]egus of the Oxyrhynchites
3     by order of Flavius
4     Eudaemon of the [most excellent]
5     procurator orders
6     to the soldier actually in the nome on the pretext of sowing to present himself openly, or if he should not do this within the days fixed by him
7     five having been sought
8     he will be sent back
9     to the most excellent procurator Flavius Eudai[mona] …
