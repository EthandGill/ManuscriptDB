[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Cleopatra and Ptolemy, the gods Philometores
2     Soteres, in the sixth year, in the priesthood of the one who is in Alexandria [of Alexander]
3     [and the gods Soteres] and the gods Adelphoi and the gods Euergetai [and the gods Philopatores] and the gods Epiphaneis and the god Eupator and the god Philometor
4     [and the god Philopator] and the god Euergetes and the gods Philometores Soteres; the sacred
5     [colt of Isis] mother of the great gods; the stephanephoros of Queen Cleopatra goddess Philometor Soteira Dikaiosyne Nikephoros; the athlophoros
6     [of Berenike Euergetis]; the phosphoros of Queen Cleopatra goddess Philometor [Soteira] Dikaiosyne Nikephoros; the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos;
7     [the priestess of Arsinoe] Philopator — those who are in Alexandria; in the month
8     [Dystros, on the twenty-seventh], on Tybi the twenty-seventh, in the village of Tenis
9     [also called Akoris of the] Hermopolite (nome). Onias son of Ammonios lent
10    … [a Macedonian] of those carried on the rolls among the cleruchs in Kleopatra (estate),
11    [to Dionysios son of Kephalas], a Persian of the epigone, of solid wheat, artabas
12    [twenty-four, which] he has had measured out from him; and the wheat, interest-free,
13    [let Dionysios repay to Onias] or to his agents in the month Loios, which is
14    [Pauni of the sixth] year — solid wheat, new, pure, unadulterated, by the measure
15    [reckoned against the] bronze (standard), and let him deliver it to the harbor at Akoris
16    [at his own expense]; and if he does not repay according to what is written,
17    [let Dionysios pay] to Onias or to his agents, as the price of each
18    [artaba, of bronze] three thousand drachmas, and for the half the proportionate amount;
19    [and let the right of execution] belong to Onias and to his agents for the terms of the
20    [contract, both against] Dionysios himself and from all his property,
21    [just as by legal judgment]; let this contract be valid.
22    [Witnesses: Dorieus son of] … ou, Hermophilos son of Ammonios, Apollonios
23    son of … the three (being) Persians; Ammonios son of Apollonios, Herakleides son of Achilleus,
24    son of … the three (being) Macedonians, all Kleopatreis.
25    Keeper of the contract: Dorieus.
26    [Dionysios son of Kephalas, a Persian] of the epigone: I have received the price of the
27    [twenty-four artabas] of the solid wheat and I will do everything according to what
28    [is set forth, and I have given] the valid (deed) to Dorieus.
29    [Dorieus: I have the valid (deed)]. Year 6, Tybi 27, in the village of Tenis of the
30    Mochite (district); registered through Apollonios.
31    [Of Onias] of Dorieus of Apollonios of Herakleides
32    [of Dionysios] of Hermophilos of Ammonios …
33    Year 6, Tybi 27: loan of Dionysios
34    son of Kephalas, of 24 artabas of wheat.
