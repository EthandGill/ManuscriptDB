r.1    Καλλικράτης Παπέειτος Πέρσης τῆς ἐπιγονῆς
r.2    καὶ ἡ γυνή μου Τασῶκις Ἁρμιύσιος Περσίνη μετὰ κυρίου ἐμοῦ ἀλλήλων ἔγγυοι εἰς ἔκτισιν

r.6    [καθὼ]ς πρόκιται.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Kallicrates, [son] of Papeietos, Persian of the epigone
2     and my wife Tasokis, [daughter] of Harmyusios, Persian, together with my guardian, [are] sureties for one another to [guarantee] payment
3     we have leased and we will guarantee as
4     aforesaid. Patynieon wrote on their behalf
5     [son] of Diodoros, because they do not know letters. Ptolemaios, [son] of Apianos, I have leased
6     [as] aforesaid.
