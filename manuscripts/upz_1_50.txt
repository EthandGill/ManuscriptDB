[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapion, one of the diadochoi,
2     and subdioiketes, from Thaueis
3     and Taous, twins, who serve in the [sanctuary] near Memphis
4     great Sarapeum. And previously
5     [we] submitted to you a memorandum
6     concerning [our] not having received from the
7     priests what should be given to us annually
8     for the necessary [purposes], emmer grain, since which you also wrote to Menides the epistates [to give to us, but when he instructed Psintais the overseer]
9     of the sacred [place], [he] takes no account,
10    but we in the sacred [place] meanwhile
11    being destroyed by hunger are in danger of
12    abandoning the sacred [place]. Since therefore on the one hand from the
13    royal [treasury] it has been allocated to those supervising the sacred [place] and to us
14    [the emmer] from the 18th through the 19th year,
15    [we] however neither the emmer nor [that which]
16    [should] be given [in place of] it to us from the
17    sacred [place], [the] allowance of the [temple workers]
18    have we received, nor indeed the three daily loaves from the Asclepieum since the [time of] mourning
19    [we] beseech you with supplication, because you do not
20    cease taking care of us,
21    and becoming a helper in this matter, summon Psintais
22    the overseer of the sacred [place] and compel
23    him to give to us immediately what is owed [for] the 18th and 19th years
24    of emmer [a total of 160] artabas; and likewise also
25    what is given
26    from the aforementioned Asclepieum held in arrears since the [time of] mourning,
27    [the] allowance of [daily] loaves [for] three [days]; for if we receive these we will serve
28    [the sanctuary] on your behalf more eagerly in the [sacred place]. Farewell.
