[TRANSLATION]
1   [Claudius Antoninus to the agoranomos, greeting.]
2   ... purchase ...
3   ... of (the daughter of?) Phatres and Apis son of Harpaesis son of A...
4   her mother being Tausorapis daughter of Harthoonis, to the
5   priests of Thoeris and Isis and Sarapis and [the temple-
6   sharing] greatest gods, and of Harthonis and
7   Paeis, being also dressers (stolistai) of the same gods, of which
8   the woman making the disposition happens to be the purchaser from
9   Dionysia also called Taamois daughter of Dionysia
10   (daughter) of Epimachos, in the month Kaisareios of the past
11   ninth year, by the Sarapeion at the (gate) facing the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
12   in the lane of Hermaios, on the north ... Dionysia
13   also called Taamois has released, on the north
14   side of the precinct of Sarapis the greatest god,
15   of a width of half a cubit, of grounds partly
16   walled around, together with the loads (rubble) in them,
17   on condition that the purchasers leave the grounds to the lord
18   Sarapis for the use of the same god, and the re-
19   maining parts to wall around, and that the same grounds
20   they will not make productive, so that they remain ser-
21   vice-grounds of the same god and of the temple, nor indeed shall it be
22   permitted to them to sell to others in any manner,
23   which they bought on these terms from Thermouthion daughter of
24   Dionysios son of Thoonis, her mother being Teseuris daughter
25   of Petosorapis, with as guardian her own husband
26   Kephalon son of Harthoonis son of Euboulos, his mother being
27   Thaesis, all of them being from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
28   for a price of silver 692 drachmas, of copper 51405 talents. Farewell.
29   Year ten of the Emperor Caesar Vespasian
30   Augustus, Choiak 12. (hand 2) Claudius Antoninus, register it.
31   (hand 3) Alexandros and partners to the agoranomoi, greeting. There has been paid
32   on the 13th of Choiak, of the enkyklion-tax, by Harthoonis son of Harthoonis
33   and the priests with him according to the
34   assessment (diagraphe), of copper at the silver rate, 5141 talents. Farewell.
