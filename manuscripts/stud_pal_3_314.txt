[TRANSLATION]
1     from the hospital of the Holy Amba [L]eontios through me Hilari[o]ns
2     of the caretaker to Kolluthos, archisynmachos and implanter. I received and I was paid
3     from you for the improvement of the whole house from Pachon 1, indiction 14
4     until Pachon fifteenth indiction, gold keratia six
5     amounts to gold keratia 6 only and for your security I have made for you the
6     present receipt as aforesaid. Hilario caretaker tallies with me the receipt
