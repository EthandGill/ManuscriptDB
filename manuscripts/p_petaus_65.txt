1     To Apollonios, strategos of the Arsinoite, division of Herakleides
2     division,
3     from Petaus, village-scribe of Ptolemais Hormou and
4     of other villages.
5     In place of Maron son of Sabinos and Ischyrion
6     son of Phaseis and Heron son of Hatres, the three collectors
7     of money-taxes of the village of Ptolemais Hormou, and
8     Heron son of Herakleides and Herakleides son of Zoilos and
9     Potamon son of Sokrates and Paaus son of Sokmas and
10    Ioustos son of Hermeios and Harpokration son of Potamon,
11    the six likewise collectors of money-taxes of the village
12    of Syron, and Phanesis son of Marres and Onnophris
13    son of Petermouthis and Onnophris son of Paabos and
14    Horos son of Onnophris, the four likewise collectors of money-taxes of the village of Kerkesoucha Orous,
15    the thirteen who are nearest (to) completing the
16    fixed three-year term, I give the persons written below, who are well-off and suitable, on the judgment and at the risk of those from the villages and of those
17    farming around them, who also stand surety for them according to custom by mutual guarantee, to be sent for allotment to the most excellent epistrategos.
18    They are, of Ptolemais Hormou:
19    Petheus son of Ischyrion, whose mother is Thaubastis, having means of 700 drachmas
20    Dios son of Papontos, whose mother is Thaubas, likewise 600 drachmas
21    Ischyrion son of Neseus, whose mother is Heros, likewise 700 drachmas
22    Pnepheros son of Tourbon, whose mother is Thaubas, likewise 600 drachmas
23    Ischyrion son of Ischyrion, whose mother is Tasouchas, likewise 700 drachmas
24    Ischyrion of unknown father, whose mother is Tamysthe, likewise 600 drachmas
25    Of the village of Syron, likewise:
26    Papion son of Potamon, whose mother is Tasoucharion, likewise 700 drachmas
27    Ptollas son of Ptolemaios, Kalaouakis, likewise 600 drachmas
28    Tyrannos son of Pathynis, likewise 700 drachmas
29    Potamon son of Ioulios, physician, likewise 600 drachmas
30    Ptolion son of Sokmas, Ansas, likewise 700 drachmas
31    Potamon son of Pathynis, Kanibis, likewise 600 drachmas
32    Marres son of Paaus, son of Platon, likewise 700 drachmas
33    Neilos son of Hatres also called Didymos, (son) of Ptollis, likewise 600 drachmas
34    Sokrates son of Ptolemaios, (son) of Neseus, likewise 700 drachmas
35    Pathynis son of Sokmas, (son) of Sokmas, whose mother is Thermouthis, surnamed Pounanoup, likewise 800 drachmas
36    Pakysis son of Sokmas, (son) of Psonthneus, surnamed Ptioli, likewise 700 drachmas
37    Potamon son of Ptollas, surnamed Tioue, likewise 800 drachmas
38    Of Kerkesoucha Orous, likewise:
39    Petesouchos son of Phanesis, whose mother is Taneseus, likewise 600 drachmas
40    Sarapion son of Horos, whose mother is Charitous, likewise 700 drachmas
