[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum to Zenon [from]
2     [D]emetrius. Know me not [having] a grain ration for a longer time, but I buy grain and whatever
3     vessels I once had as security
4     lie [as security]; for the expense in the city I don't think you
5     to be ignorant, that for more days
6     there having been detained, having borrowed copper, I have sailed away.
7     You will therefore do well, if to you
8     it seems, to settle the pottery's price and repay, and of the grain whatever might seem good to you to be well,
9     in order that we may not appease others
10    while you are well.
11    Farewell.
