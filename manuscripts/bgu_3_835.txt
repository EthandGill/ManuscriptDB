[TRANSLATION]
1   To Aurelius Dionysios, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2   from the Aurelii Longinus also called Zosimos
3   son of Leonides and Harpalos son of Sarapion and Heron
4   son of Kopres, the three, and the remaining grain-officials (sitologoi)
5   of the village of Karanis. Monthly summary in total
6   of the month Choiak of the present 25th year
7   from the produce of the past 24th year:
8   These are the (artabas) measured out to us in the granary of the aforesaid
9   village in this month: of wheat 305 1/2 artabas, of surcharge wheat 1/4 artaba,
10   total with surcharge wheat 305 1/2 3/4 artabas, of which:
11   of Karanis, public (land) wheat 254 artabas, likewise of katoikoi wheat 46 artabas,
12   total for the village 300 artabas; and on behalf of the other villages:
13   of Hiera, katoikoi wheat 2 1/2 artabas, of Kerkesoucha [...] wheat 3 artabas, of surcharge wheat 1/4 artaba,
14   total also on behalf of other villages wheat 5 1/2 artabas, surcharge wheat 1/4 artaba,
15   and these are taken up in addition on behalf of [the philanthropon and] other charges, wheat 9 1/3 1/8 artabas,
16   total with the philanthropon wheat 315 1/4 24th artabas, [and] of the carry-over (eklogos) of the previous
17   month wheat 7100 1/2 artabas; and with the carry-over
18   wheat 7415 1/2 1/24th artabas, out of which there were expended for a loan
19   of seed-grain for the sowing of the (land) which by good fortune was made,
20   wheat 3690 1/4 artabas, of which:
21   of Karanis, seed-grain wheat 2465 artabas, of Kerkesoucha, through those from Karanis,
22   wheat 530, of Ptolemais likewise wheat 515 artabas, of Straton(?) likewise wheat 150 artabas,
23   of Hiera of Severus through Karanis wheat 37 1/4 artabas, total the aforesaid.
24   Remaining for the whole, wheat 3718 6 1/8 artabas.
25   I, Aurelius Longinus also called Zosimos, have submitted (this). I, Aurelius Harpalos
26   son of Sarapion, have co-submitted.
27   Year 25 of Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus Parthicus Maximus,
28   Britannicus Maximus, Germanicus Maximus, Pius Augustus,
29   Tybi 12.
