[TRANSLATION]
1   Theon to Theon his father, greeting.
2   A fine thing you did: you didn’t take me with
3   you to the city! If you won’t take <me> with
4   you to Alexandria, I won’t write you a
5   letter, I won’t speak to you, I won’t wish you health,
6   and then, if you go to Alexandria, I will
7   not take your hand, nor greet you a-
8   gain after that. If you won’t take m[e],
9   that’s what happens. And my mother said to Ar-
10   chelaos: “He drives me mad — take him away!”
11   And a fine thing you did: you sent me gifts —
12   big ones! — husks!(?) They fooled us there
13   on the 12th, the day you sailed. Well then, send for
14   me, I beg you. If you don’t send, I won’t
15   eat, I won’t drink. There!
16   I pray for your health.
17   Tybi 18.
18   Deliver to Theon [fr]om Theonas his son.
