[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 14 which is also 11, Choiak 6, in Hermou polis
2     of the Thebaid. Pasion
3     also called Pasis son of Petenephotes, a Persian,
4     centurion of those of Asklepiades'
5     command, of the Akoritai, lent to Dionysios also called
6     Plenis son of Kephalas, a Persian of the epigone,
7     and to his wife Isidora also called
8     Tasis daughter of Hermophilos also called Pachois, and to
9     the mother of the aforewritten Dionysios,
10    Sarapias also called Senabellis daughter of Heliodoros also
11    called Herieus, both Persian women, with
12    her guardian Dionysios who is also a co-borrower, fifty-[four] artabas of solid wheat,
13    which they have been measured out from him, attached to this
14    deed which is appended, at interest of one-and-a-half,
15    twenty-seven artabas of wheat; and let them repay the wheat
16    and the interest, all the eighty-
17    one artabas of solid wheat,
18    the borrowers, to Pasion or to his agents,
19    in the month Loios, which is P[aun]i of the aforesaid year, without lawsuit or judgment
20    or any malpractice, new solid wheat,
21    pure, unadulterated, by the measure (calibrated) to the bronze (standard), and let them deliver it at the harbor by Akoris
22    at their own expenses, to Pasion
23    or to his agents; and if they do n[ot re]pay according to what is written, let the borrowers pa[y]
24    to Pasion or to his agents the pr[ic]e of each artaba, t[hr]ee thousand bronze drachmas; and let the right of execution belong to Pasion and to his agents
25    for the things in the deed, both from the
26    borrowers themselves, and from one and from whichever
27    of them he may choose, and from all their property,
28    as by judgment; sureties for one another
29    for the wheat or for the aforewritten price, for
30    full payment, the borrowers themselves; let this deed be valid.
31    Loan of Pasion also called Pasis
32    against Dionysios also called Plenis
33    [and others], of solid wheat, 81 artabas.
34    aparchnios (?)
35    Hip[pon]ik(os) (?)
36    lo…archou … m
