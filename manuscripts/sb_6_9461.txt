[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and master Jesus Christ
2     of God and savior of us, reign of the most pious
3     our lord Flavius Heraclius the eternal Augustus
4     and Emperor, year 22, Phaophi 17, 6th indiction in Arsinoe.
5     To the holy church of the city of Arsinoe called
6     of Saint Colluthus through you the steward thereof
7     Phoibammon the eminent, Aurelius Eulogios
8     son of Apollos setting out from the village of Bubastus
9     of the Arsinoite nome, greetings. I acknowledge that I have leased
10    from your splendor the properties belonging
11    to the same holy church in the plain of the same
12    village of Bubastus in a place [called]
13    Thansale in five plots [five arouras]
14    more or less, five arouras more or less [with all]
15    thereof of right, for however long a time
16    your splendor wishes from [the] with-God
17    [enter]ing seventh indiction, providing [to you of the]
18    one having leased Eulogios for [fixed]
19    tribute thereof annually of gold [nomismata]
20    three, as they are customary, of gold nomismata [3 as they are customary],
21    to be paid by me annu[ally] [in two]
22    payments, that is in Mesore[i]
23    month one nomisma and [in the] …
24    [m]onth the other two nomisma[ta] …
25    of another lease of the same [arouras]
26    [not] being put forward except [this]
27    which also being valid and [having been questioned] [I acknowledged].
28    Aurelius Eulogios Apollos, it is consistent with me
29    all the aforementioned, as is set forth.
30    through me Iustus the lawyer
31    lease of five arouras more or less in the plain of the village of Bubastus having been made by Aurelius Eulogios son of Apollos to the [holy]
32    church of Saint Colluthus.
