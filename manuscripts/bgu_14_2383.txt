1     … of Arsinoe daughter of [Sosibios], in the m[onth] Peritios
2     in [Tho]lthis of the Oxyrhynchite nome; he leased for one year, [one] sow[ing and harvest, from the] sowing
3     in the ninth year, of which the crops (fall) into the tenth year — Theochrestos the Thracian, of (the corps of) Phi[lon, a private] person, (leased) to Simias son of Demetrios, an Athenian of the epigone — the remnant of the … allotment …
4     of the whole, which are five arourai or however many may come to be from the survey, with seed, free of risk, at a rent for each aroura of seven and a half artabas of wheat; and let Theochrestos give him [seed for]
5     each aroura one artaba of wheat at the time of need; and let [Sim]ias [pay] the [contrac]ted rents to Theochrestos, when the rest of the grain of the village is measured into the royal (granary) of the tenth
6     year, clean and unadulterated grain produced in the land, by the royal choenix-measure, by just measurement,
7     and let him deliver (it) to the royal granary which is in Tholthis at his own expense; and if …
8     then … …
9     of each [of the] whe[at] … drachmas … and let the right of exaction belong to [Theochrestos, exacting from Simias accor]ding to the ordinance; and let Theochrest[o]s be master of the crops until he [recovers] his own; [and let Theochrestos guarantee to Simias the]
10    land and the crops sown in it according to what he has leased; and if [he does not guarantee according to what is written, let Theochre]stos pay to Simias as penalty [of sil]ver drachmas … hundred; and let this contract [be valid wherever it is produced; witnesses:]
