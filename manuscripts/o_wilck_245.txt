[TRANSLATION]
1   Ulpius Cerealis, lessee of the Sacred Gate
2   of Syene, through Pachnoubis the assistant. Paid:
3   Serapion son of Ammonios, mother Thinpetorz(methes),
4   for the poll-tax of the 1st year: seventeen drachmas (and) an obol — total 17 dr. 1 ob.
5   Year (2nd hand) 1 (1st hand) of Imperator Caesar Marcus
6   Aurelius Antoninus Augustus
7   and Imperator Caesar Lucius
8   Aurelius Verus Augustus,
9   Pachon 27.
