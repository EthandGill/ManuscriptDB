[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidorus to Apollinarius the
2     most honored greetings. Often I have asked you such things not to write to me; but this
3     know that a robber at a philosopher does not lodge; but if to me you
4     renounce friendship, me there is necessity to you to serve. A bundle to me
5     of beets, if to you it be not burdensome,
6     use up. Greet
7     Epimachos.
