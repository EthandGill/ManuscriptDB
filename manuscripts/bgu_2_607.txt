[TRANSLATION]
1     Year three of Imperator Caesar
2     [M]arcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus and
3     of Imperator Caesar Lucius Aurelius
4     [V]erus Augustus, Pauni 28. Through the bank of Sarapion in the broad street of the Gymnasium. Didymos son of Didymos also called Demetrios, the son of Theon,
5     of the Sosikosmian and Althaian (tribe and deme), to Pabous son of Satabous, the son of Harpagathos,
6     mother Tases, about 40 years old, a scar beside the left forearm,
7     and to Pakysis son of Naraus, the son of Pakysis,
8     mother Taoues, about 37 years old, a mole on the brow,
9     to the two of them, illiterate as they say, camel-herders of the village
10    of Soknopaiou Nesos: (acknowledges) that they have received from
11    Didymos, out of the freight-charges owed to them
12    for the wheat they brought down from the granaries of the Herakleides division, those from the month
13    Pachon of the 2nd year up to the month Epeiph of the present
14    3rd year, by advance, Pabous son of Satabous on his account two hundred and ninety-six silver drachmas, and Pekysis son of Naraus
15    likewise on his account six hundred silver drachmas,
16    which they shall also repay to Didymos whenever they collect the freight-charges owed
17    to them from those accustomed to dispatch (them) from the public (treasury); and if
18    they do not repay, Didymos shall have the right of execution against both them and
19    all their property
20    just as in a legal judgment. Subscribers (?): they …
21    P[a]b[ou]s son of [S]atabous and Pakysis
22    son of N[arau]s: we have received from Didymos the aforesaid drachmas, and
23    we shall repay as is set forth above. G… …
24    … I wrote on behalf of them, being illiterate.
