1     Account of the [contributions] of the charitable service (diakonia) of the holy church of God, K …
2     thus:
3     of the village of Tan[iathe]os.
4     From the steward Pt …: 4 solidi less 8 carats.
5     From the steward of [V]iktor: 2 solidi less 4 (carats).
6     From the steward Hosios: 2 solidi less 4, of full weight.
7     From the steward of Saint Michael: 2 solidi less 4 (carats).
8     From the steward of Abba Leon: 2 solidi less 4 (carats).
9     From the steward of Saint Mary: 2 solidi less 4 (carats).
10    From the steward of Saint Theodore: 2 solidi less 4 (carats).
11    From the workmen: 4 solidi less [8 (carats)].
12    From the steward of (the church of) Lea: half a solidus less [1 (carat)].
13    From the steward of Saint Koll[outh]os: 1 solidus less 4 (carats).
14    From the steward of (the church of) Con[stantine]: 2 solidi less 4 (carats).
15    From Peteros: 2 solidi less 4 (carats).
16    From Kyros the presbyter …: 1 solidus less 2 (carats).
17    From the lord …: [2 solidi 11 carats less 3].
18    From various monasteries: 4 solidi less 8 (carats).
19    From Parouph: 2 solidi less 4 (carats).
20    From 2 monasteries, P … om: 2 solidi less 2 (carats).
21    and from Potos the oil-maker of the village, of oil: 156 1/8 sextarii.
22    Total: 34 solidi and 3 carats less 68 carats.
23    …: 5 solidi less 10 (carats).
24    …: 1 solidus less 2 (carats).
25    Altogether the whole of the charitable service amounts to:
26    Taniatheos: 34 solidi less 65 (carats).
27    Nabowo: 26 solidi less 52 (carats).
28    Ibion: 27 solidi 21 carats.
29    Gaia: 6 solidi less 12 (carats).
30    … minptel: 5 solidi less 10 (carats).
31    [Ter]ytheos: 17 solidi less 35 (carats).
32    Paschle: 5 solidi less 10 (carats).
33    … of the city: 11 solidi less 22 carats.
34    Altogether the whole of the [charitable service amounts to] 131 solidi 21 carats less 206 carats.
35    [There amounts] net 123 solidi 7 carats, of full weight by the goldsmith's standard.
36    …es the bishop, the sum collected is in order with me,
37    of the [charitable] service, as set out above.
