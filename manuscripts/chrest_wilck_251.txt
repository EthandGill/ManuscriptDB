[TRANSLATION]
1     … [year] of Antoninus and Verus the
2     [lords Augusti] … now … [from] the Arsinoite, and when they had come forward, Longei[nus the advocate said:] … some are fullers, others dyers by trade, and they pay for the tax
3     of the trade-licence: by the fullers an annual one thousand and ninety-two drachmas, and by the dyers
4     [two hundred and eig]hty-eight, according to the schedule and the custom. But when a certain Maximus was appointed examiner
5     and assessed [them at more] than was due, they petitioned the prefect, and he referred them to Crassus the most excellent
6     [administrat]or, who, having summoned the accountant of the nome, ordered him to examine the twenty-year (record) at hand,
7     [and when] he reported that nothing more than the amount in the schedule had been paid, in accordance with these things he wished th[em to make the re]payment, and thus up to now
8     they have paid. Since, then, now a certain man appointed as overseer
9     [of the tax of the trade]-licence wishes to demand from them more than according to the schedule, we petitioned the strategos, setting (the matter) before (him),
10    … [and sinc]e nothing was done by the [strategos] it was necessary for them to petition you. Protarchos the ad[vocate said:] … according to what seemed good to Cr[assus …] … of inquiry a document concerning this was brought to the most excellent [Liber]alis, and he subscribed not to de[mand (it). Sever]ianus said: in the presence of the accountant, who also petition…d the accountant … [I have re]ad (it).
11    … of the [dyer]s and fullers from the Arsinoite, in the presence of Hermi[as the accountant of the Arsi]noite, [Longeinus the advo]cate said: yesterday those whom I represent petition[ed] y[ou]
12    … [concerning the things for which they a]re improperly demanded [by] the overseer of the trade-licence business, and that to … it had [been bro]ught to the most excellent [Liberalis and that it had been subscribed] not to seek more from them.
13    … you may learn the exact truth. We ask therefore that he being present … [when Hermias had rea]d the document brought to Liberalis, Severianus said:
14    … the … of the overseer to the most excellent epistrategos.
15    … I have read (it).
