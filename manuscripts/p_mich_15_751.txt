[TRANSLATION]
1     Sempronius to Saturnila his mother and lady,
2     very many greetings. Before all else I pray that you are in good health, and at the same time I make your obeisance daily
3     before the lord Sarapis. I am astonished how you did not write to me
4     either through Celer or through Sempronius. For having arrived
5     back from my journey abroad I found them and inquired
6     for what reason they had not brought me a letter. They said it was because of
7     the journey abroad. But on learning about your welfare
8     I became more free from care. Therefore, being asked, my lady,
9     [write to me without delay] about your welfare. I received
10    [a letter] through Sokrates and another short one through An[to]nianus
11    [and a let]ter of Hexas I received, and another I received from Lo[botes. You wrote] to me also about … the … …
12    … you have … there is need, that … you may provide … for nothing … me
13    … … through whom I am [not surprised] at all
14    … so much of you …
15    … according to the …
16    … … for to Sempr[on]ius if …
17    … it is mine to take thought, it belongs to another
18    [to ] … to … before the time such things … …
19    … therefore it is a very small matter. Now I write to you
20    [so that] you may remember it. For now he wrote to you as to a deaf person.
21    [And you] wrote about these things the second letter to us. I write about [these things.] For indeed I wished to do everything
22    and to come to you, first to make obeisance to your good
23    and child-loving character, secondly also about these matters. But not now
24    … I found an opportunity, but being about to travel abroad I wrote
25    to you about these very things. Greet Maximus and his wife and Sempronius and Cyrillus and Saturnilus
26    [and Ge]mellus and Iulius and his people and Helene and
27    [hers] and Skythikos [and] Kopres, Chairemon, Thermouthis and her children. Farewell to me, my lady,
28    always.
29    Deliver to Maximus from Sempronius
30    his brother.
