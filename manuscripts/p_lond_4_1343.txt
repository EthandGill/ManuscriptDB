[TRANSLATION]
1     unless you send out to us, together with the households and possessions
2     of them, making a register of the names and patronymics
3     of the persons being dispatched; and moreover also to which districts of your administration
4     they fled for refuge, and what each one possesses both in property and [in plots of land],
5     writing likewise in the same memorandum those found from the
6     pagarchy who have acted beyond the limit which we set, that is
7     the time and the property of each person, and those passed over
8     in your administration; and, to put it simply, every piece of information and disclosure
9     truthfully and without favor, in the same register. For we have enjoined
10    upon our envoy not to depart from you until you send out
11    completely the last soul of those found in your administration
12    from twenty years (of age) and upward, as has been said, and to bring also the aforenamed
13    register containing, as has been indicated, those dispatched and those
14    passed over in your administration from among them. And let it not become known
15    to us that you have failed (to send) anyone at all of those of the fugitives appointed
16    to be dispatched; since our God knows, if we find
17    anyone after our envoy reaches (you) who is not entered
18    in the register dispatched by you, we shall subject you
19    to the things that oppress you, both to a most heavy fine and to bodily
20    prosecution, punishing also the (people) of the village where such
21    a fugitive is found, with a penalty they cannot bear, ruining
22    both the headman and the administrator and his guards, after which
23    also subjecting them to a bodily prosecution that ought
24    to reach their utmost (limits). For the rest, read the present
25    letter to all those of your administration, permitting (them) to copy out
26    the equivalent (copy) for each village and to have it cast (i.e. proclaimed) in their churches,
27    forewarning them and charging (them) to keep inviolate
28    our injunction and to make known to you whomever they know
29    as fugitives of the aforesaid administration of the Arsinoite (nome),
30    so that we may not find against them any pretext whatsoever
31    and their soul and property become a ransom for them.
32    For with God's help we will not pass over in Egypt
33    a single pagarchy alone, unless we send into it
34    our trustworthy and capable men, who must unfailingly,
35    with all exactness, search out and grope after, for the sake
36    of these same fugitives; and to appoint to be given also to those who inform on
37    anyone hidden of those whom we ordered to be dispatched, over
38    and above what you reckon, by way of informer's reward; and (the envoy) refrains, by what appears,
39    from holding (i.e. keeping concealed) any of them after our envoy reaches (you)
40    here. For he is about to call blessed the dead,
41    in that they do not endure the dreadful things coming upon him,
42    in return for which he disobeyed our command and dared
43    against his own soul. Month Tybi 4, indiction 8.
