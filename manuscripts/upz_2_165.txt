1     Year 28, Mesore 28, it has been registered with the bank in Hermontis, over which Dio[n]ysius [presides]
2     of the twentieth [entry] of the register, according to the registration by Asclepiades the one [concerning] the purchase,
3     under which Ptolemy the copy-clerk signs, [of] the purchase of a bare plot of Amenothou of 10 cubits [and] 7½ cubits
4     from the south of the whole bare plot that is
5     from the south of Zeus' City the Great, of which the boundaries have been made clear through the present contract, which he purchased from Halekios and Lobaitos and
6     Tbaiatos [son] of Herieus and Senerieus of Petenephotou and Herieus of Amenothou and Senosorphibios
7     of Amenothou and Sisoitos of and Herieus of Amenothou in year 28 Pachon 18, [bronze] talents 3; the tax of which exchange [rate] 900 becomes 900.
8     Dionysius the banker.
