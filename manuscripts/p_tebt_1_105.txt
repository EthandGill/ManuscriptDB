1     In the 15th year which is also the 12th, Phaophi 24, in Kerkeosiris of the division of Polemon. There acknowledges Ptolemaios also called
2     Petesouchos, son of Apollonios also called Haryotes, a Persian of the Epigone, to Horion son of Apollonios, a Macedonian of those about the
3     strategos, one of the sword-bearers, that he has leased from him — that which he himself happens to have leased — the catoecic allotment of Maron son of Dionysios, for 5 years from the 15th which is also the 12th year, at a total yearly rent of 120 artabas of wheat each year,
4     free of all deduction for any damage, on condition that he shall clear the waste land in the allotment except that which adjoins the land of Thoonis
5     and Pausiris, at his own cost; toward which Horion shall give 4 talents 3000 (drachmas) of copper,
6     of which Ptolemaios receives at once 2 talents 3000, and the remaining 2 talents in 2 years from the 15th
7     year, yearly in the month Pachon; and each year he shall let half lie fallow, and he shall pay [the future]
8     expenses and collection-charges at the threshing-floor of 3 artabas of wheat; and when the period of the lease has elapsed
9     he shall hand back the land clean of rush, reed, coarse grass, [and every other]
10    weed; and if he chooses to clear the whole land in the 2nd year, let (Horion) give him the 2 talents
11    in the month Pachon of the 15th year. Repayment … dispute …
12    … Contract-keeper: Timostratos.
13    In the reign of Cleopatra the goddess Euergetis and Ptolemaios surnamed Alexander, the Mother-loving gods, in the fif[teen]th year which is also the twelfth, the priest of Alexander and the other (priests) being those written in Alexandria, in the month Xandikos,
14    on the twenty-fourth (= Phaophi the twenty-fourth), in Kerkeosiris of the division of Polemon in the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges Ptolemaio[s]
15    also called Petesouchos, son of Apollonios son of Haryotes, a Persian of the Epigone, to Horion son of Apollonios, a Macedonian of those about the strategos,
16    a sword-bearer, that he has leased from him — that which he himself happens to have leased — of Maron son of Dionysios, around the vil[lage],
17    a catoecic allotment in three parcels, of which the neighbours of the first, lying between a canal, are: on the south the land of Mestasytmis [the great god]
18    and of Horos son of Pekous and of Thoonis and Pausiris royal land, on the north the allotment of Ptolemaios son of Apollonios, [on the west]
19    and east a canal; of the second: on the south and north and west a dyke, on the east the allotment of Apollodoros; of the other [parcel]:
20    on the south the allotment of the aforesaid Apollodoros, on the north and east the allotment of Asklepiades, on the west a dyke. This [lease]
21    is for five years from the fifteenth which is also the twelfth year, at a total yearly rent each year of wheat, artabas
22    one hundred and twenty, apart from seed, free of all risk and free of all deduction for any damage, on condition that
23    Ptolemaios shall clear all the waste land in the allotment except that which adjoins the land of Thoonis and Pausiris, at his own cost,
24    Horion paying to him toward the clearing four talents of copper and three thousand drachmas, not returnable, of which
25    Ptolemaios receives at once two talents and three thousand drachmas, and the remaining two talents he shall receive in two years
26    from the aforesaid fifteenth which is also the twelfth year, yearly in the month Pachon one talent of copper,
27    and Ptolemaios shall each year, from the second year of the lease of the allotment, let half lie fallow with such crop as he chooses,
28    except oil-bearing produce; and he shall pay each year all the future expenses and collection-charges at the threshing-floor of wheat,
29    three artabas, at his own cost. And when the period of the lease has elapsed, let Ptolemaios hand back the land
30    … ploughed and levelled and embanked and clean of rush, reed, coarse grass, and
31    [every other] weed, except the aforesaid waste. And if Ptolemaios chooses to clear the whole land [in]
32    [the sec]ond year, let Horion give him the aforesaid two talents of copper in the month Pachon of the fif[teenth]
33    [which is also the] twelfth year. And let Horion guarantee to Ptolemaios and his agents the lease and the
34    right to gather the produce from the [land] on the terms stated for the contracted period and for as long as is needful for this,
35    [and let] it [not be permitted] to him to lease it to others nor to eject Ptolemaios before the time nor to hinder
36    the men who cultivate the land for him nor those who water the crop each year for the growth; but let him al[so re]pay to Ptolemaios within the appointed time the remaining two talents of copper toward the clearing, as afor[esaid].
37    [And if] he does not guarantee them as has been written, or transgresses in any other point of what is written above, let Horion pay
38    to Ptolemaios a penalty of thirty talents of copper, and for not repaying the copper for the clearing the amount and a hal[f],
39    and the damage, and none the less let the lease be valid; and those who resist the one who forces his way into the land, and (Ptolemaios) him[self]
40    and his agents, shall be free of all penalty and all liability. And with the lease being guaranteed,
