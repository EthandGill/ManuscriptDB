1     In the twelfth year of Imperator [Caesar]
2     Mar[c]us Aurel[i]us An[tonin]us [Aug]ustus
3     Armenia[cu]s Medicu[s Parthi]cus
4     Greatest, Phamenoth 1, in Karan[is of the] Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges
5     Ptolemaios, son of Ptolemaios the son of …, mother …r…
6     …, from the village of Karanis, about … [years old, with a scar] … in the middle, to Pakysis, son of Onnophris the son of Satabous, about 20 years old,
7     with a scar on the left knee, that [the] acknowledging party has received
8     from [P]akysis by hand of silver one hundred fifty-two [drachmas, and by the]
9     six-choinix measure of wheat … artabas, [which he owed]
10    to him according to an acknowledgment [completed in the]
11    5th year, in the month Hadrianos, through the record-office [of the village of Bac]chias, which the acknowledging party Ptolemaios has also given back to Pakysis for annulment [and]
12    cancellation, together with its stipulations …
13    [and] (that he shall) not bring a claim nor proceed (against him), neither [Ptolemaios] nor [those] acting for [him] against P[akysi]s
14    nor against those acting for him, from [the] former times up to the present [day], in any way … [ ]
15    I, Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios, acknowledge that I have received from [Pakysis]
16    the [drachmas] of silver,
17    [one] hundred fifty-[two, and]
18    artabas of wheat … [as]
19    is set forth. I, Pasion son of Petheus,
20    wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
