[TRANSLATION]
1     Concerning date-palm groves.
2     In the name of God. Korra, son of Szerich, councillor,
3     to Basileios, administrator of Aphrodito. We give thanks to God, and after this:
4     it has come to our knowledge that many people are buying in your district
5     date-palm groves at a low price without authorization
6     and our letters. And if this is true, you have despised
7     your own soul and have laid a road against yourself. Receiving, therefore,
8     these present letters, do not allow any person whatsoever
9     to make any purchase of date-palms in your district
10    except by our letters; and whenever we write to you
11    concerning the purchase by any person whatsoever of such
12    date-palm groves, do not allow them to be given except at the rate of fifty
13    cubits to the solidus, entrusting this matter to no one,
14    but you yourself acting as intermediary in person with all exactness,
15    so that, as has been said, nothing be given to anyone in excess of the price
16    which we have fixed for such date-palm groves. Let us not find, therefore,
17    that those in charge of the customs-house of Babylon have found you
18    having released any date-palm grove from your district
19    to any person whatsoever except by our letters,
20    as has often been said, nor indeed that anything has been given above the
21    price which we set out in writing — (lest) you be condemned.
22    Having neglected anything of our command in these matters
23    you will pay for it in body and in purse. Written in the month
24    of Mecheir, 8th indiction.
25    Korra, son of Szerich, councillor, to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito.
26    [month] … [8th indiction. Delivered through …] [concerning] not selling date-palm groves.
