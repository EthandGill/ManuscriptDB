[TRANSLATION]
1   Isidoros to Aurelius his
2   brother, very many greetings.
3   As I told you about the
4   two acacias, that they should
5   give them to us: this very
6   day let them be dug
7   round. Let Phaneias himself
8   make them be dug up.
9   If he won’t, write me
10   so I know. For perhaps
11   tomorrow we are coming
12   to seal. So hurry
13   this, that I may know. As for
14   the bulls — let them
15   work; do not let them
16   be idle at all.
17   The branches — bring them
18   all into the road, so that
19   he may tie them three by three and
20   drag them off. Do it so —
21   it pays. Take no notice
22   of their owners;
23   perhaps I’ll give him nothing —
24   (though) I’m doing a great thing
25   for them. The carpenters —
26   do not let them be idle at all;
27   keep at them. I pray
28   for your health.
