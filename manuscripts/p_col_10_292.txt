[TRANSLATION]
1     Before all things praying on behalf of your [heal]th …
2     and to fare well. Since there came here The…ros the brother …
3     having given and [he] stood up the girl Theodora by name, and
4     he received from me keratia litra fourteen. Similarly I pledged
5     her to the apostasiarius litra keratia eight. [He] received
6     and a sticharon-maphorion and a linen cloth, and these I demand as her guarantor. These things I wrote to you, genuine companion and overseer, toward
7     Sokori the fourth and Alypio the second. Take care as much as [you] labor through. For I know that you are prud[ent].
8     To my master brother Charisios toward Sokori the fourth and Alipios the second
