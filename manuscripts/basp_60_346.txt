[TRANSLATION]
1     [I beseech] your sweetness and the lodging-rent send to me, so that I do not … your wife. But send me one cloth,
2     … [gap] to you. But if you send one [cloth], tell your brother Jordan and [he will] also send this to me, but in any case, so that I may thank you all.
3     Show [moreover] my letter to Peter the comb-maker, so that he also send me one cloth, but by all means tell … [gap] of mine.
4     But the children are well and greet you-all.
5     … [to the] sweetest brothers John and Jordan …
