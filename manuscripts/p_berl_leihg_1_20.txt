[TRANSLATION]
1     … to these … 2 … …
2     … [or however many] they may be, to sow with barley, at a rent (calculated) by surveyed area at the rate, per (aroura), of
3     four artabas of barley; and on the 4th plot,
4     the (aroura) called formerly (that) of Zoilos,
5     one and a quarter arouras to sow with barley,
6     at a rent likewise (calculated) by sur[vey],
7     at the rate of six artabas of barley (per aroura); as a deposit
8     of the lease, four drachmas; everything being free from all
9     risk, the public dues on the land being chargeable to the
10    woman who has leased (it) out, who shall also have ownership of the
11    crops, until she shall have recovered all
12    the aforesaid (dues). The lease being confirmed, let the lessees deliver
13    everything in the month Pauni at the threshing-floors, namely of the 5 1/2 1/4 arouras in the
14    plot (kleros), and of the rest at the threshing-floor of …
15    …, all (the produce) new, clean, unadulterated,
16    free of clods, sifted,
17    and the wheat free of barley, measured as for the public (granary), namely the
18    rents of the 5 1/2 1/4 arouras by the
19    sixth-measure of Chysis, and all the rest by the
20    four-choinix measure of Sinkepha, and
21    let the right of execution belong to the woman who has leased (it) out
22    and to her agents both against the lessees, who are mutually
23    sureties for payment, and against all their
24    property, as if by legal judgment.
25    The lease is valid. Year 13 of Imperator
26    Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus
27    Antoninus Augustus Pius,
28    Ph[ao]phi 14.
