[TRANSLATION]
1     To my master and brother Theophanes, Besodoros
2     very many greetings.
3     There was in truth a certain great longing [for you] lying deep in my mind,
4     my lord brother, which by your appearance in the city on each occasion
5     and by your brilliance used to bring about a filling of pleasure and, for those who wished it, the attaining of it through the sight alone; but now this brief time of your being away abroad has produced in us, who pray to behold the more longed-for sight,
6     (such longing), since even the time spent in closing the eyes,
7     being very brief, appears something long and immeasurable to those who love and long.
8     So then, having suffered this same thing, I pray to be relieved of such
9     desire, from which it will also come about that I shall see you most quickly; for I am so disposed to this that I keep asking on every occasion the strangers sojourning here from everywhere — it is a long task even to tell of it;
10    [for] there is nothing more honourable nor more powerful than a
11    genuine brother, as … this very thing often …
12    … And it is now
13    a care to me and very much to be prayed for to learn exactly the fortune of the affairs that have been successfully accomplished by you, so that
14    I too may rejoice greatly at the things over which I was loudly proclaiming with glory; for I believe
15    exactly and am confident that nothing unaccustomed nor out of place will happen, since God has joined with you for every action, in which justly
16    … to do good according to my own means; for to the good
17    among men [very many] honours are stored up from God. And may it be granted you,
18    my lord brother, having succeeded, to return safely to your homeland;
19    for with pleasure and joy you will come down to us to the homeland; … and greater … whatever to me …
20    … is beheld in life. Then to the most high God
21    I would have a certain great gratitude, if I were able to behold with my eyes the affairs concerning you
22    in person, having set out on the same road with you from the beginning; but now I wish, however it may be, to receive this and to learn the affairs concerning you by hearing more clearly, as quickly as possible
23    and I pray. I greet you many times, brother of my soul
24    in truth, and all those with you by name.
25    May I receive you in good health, master brother,
26    having gloriously obtained the things we wish for.
27    Deliver to my lord brother Theophanes, from Besodoros.
