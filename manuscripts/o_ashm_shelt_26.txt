[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis of the produce of the first year of Aurelius[...]
2     of Antoninus and Verus
3     of the lords Augustuses
4     Epeiph 16, on behalf of Charakos of the name Senapollonias of Panameus through a farmer
5     Phaminios, also called Petechontos, of Pasemis
6     of wheat one-sixth; it becomes wheat 6.
7     Petechespocrates has been registered [with] wheat 6.
