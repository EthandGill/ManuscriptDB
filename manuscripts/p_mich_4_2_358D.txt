1     1 (?) 7 …
2     Castor also called Sabinus, son of Ptolemaeus son of Satabous, his mother being Melitous: poll-tax (?), 4 drachmas.

4     4 drachmas, 14 obols.
5     Of the five-drachma (tax) on donkeys of the 14th year, for the working of the 12th year: 5 drachmas; of additional charges, 2 obols; total 5 drachmas 2 obols.

7     Total for the day: 96 drachmas.
