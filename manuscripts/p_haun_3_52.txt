1     Having packed three hundred eighty-four figs, and now into six baskets
2     I have sent (them) to my God-protected master through the present men. [And the]
3     letter-carrier arrived … sent by my fellow-servants
4     Kalos and Georgios, the distinguished, with letters made
5     to me, inasmuch as the heirs of (the one) among the saints, Isapollon, having stirred up (a matter)
6     against them concerning public (dues) … of Kollouthios,
7     and that they handed over to them an arrears-list concerning 2,623 nomismata, 14¼ owed
8     by them up to the fourth indiction, and that I should supposedly come to Athribis and
9     reach an agreement with Zacharias the glorious illustris; and I do not decline indeed …
10    even if a plague has now fallen upon … the master is dying …
11    but nevertheless, because I have repeatedly dealt with him and he did not attend to the things said by me
12    until last year, and this the master does not, as I think, fail to know.
13    For I and my fellow-servant Theodoros came [to the same place] last year, [and],
14    as I said, he did not attend to the things from us … having heard … all the plague
15    I depart. And presently, whatever our Savior God himself shall grant, it comes to pass.
16    My master has written that one litra of gold be dispatched from the price
17    of wine and … …
18    from the produce of the fifth harvest. But when I wrote to you to negotiate
19    with the agents, supposing you to wish (to buy) from the new produce …
20    I wrote as if, having come … … negotiating … with them, who
21    neither came until now. And the said fellow-servant of mine Theodoros has written
22    both to me and to the gold-collector not to spend anything … of the gold laid up
23    of gold …
24    And I have agreed with Ioannakion the devoted one to take hold of gold here.
25    The (matters) concerning this same (gold) I will report to you, and whether … of this gold laid up
26    is to be sent to you, write me how much … to be sent
27    and forthwith I will dispatch these. For if I depart to Athribis without

29    I wrote …
