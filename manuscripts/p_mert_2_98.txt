[TRANSLATION]
1     … full brothers on the father's side, Dmoueis, of the mother
2     Ast…s, originating from the same village,
3     on condition that they remain without interruption and
4     dwell in the same village together with their dearest relations and
5     possessions and livestock and all their
6     baggage, and in quiet and with the becoming
7     condition, answering for everything that concerns
8     their person, that is the status of a registered tenant,
9     and that, if they or one of them are sought
10    from me by the same Skelos through those who pertain to him
11    on any day whatsoever, for any
12    reason of pretext, I shall produce them or him
13    and hand them over in a public place, apart from any
14    place of refuge or plea, where I have also received them
15    into the custody of the same Skelos; but if I do not do this,
16    I acknowledge that I am liable
17    to answer for everything that, with respect to each
18    one of them, may be demanded. Valid is this guarantee,
19    written in single copy. Having been questioned, I acknowledged. I, Jeremias,
20    son of Pamouthios, agree to the said guarantee,
21    as stated above. I, Georgios, having been requested, wrote on behalf of him, since he is illiterate,
22    being so.
23    Guarantee of Jeremias son of Pamouthios from the village … of …o…reos, who undertakes responsibility for Ammonios and Pamous,
24    full brothers on the father's side, Dmoueis, from the same village.
