[TRANSLATION]
1     Protion to [A]matios
2     to the most beloved, greetings.
3     when you arrived at Tebtunis,
4     you left me no word
5     if there is need of me or else; you know
6     that it is the beginning of the year and it [is] necessary to take reports of the salt-sellers; since not
7     one [of them] enters into the salt
8     and the taxes diminish, in all ways
9     on the one hand [certainly] the fifth [I come towards] you to Talei or to Tebtunis
