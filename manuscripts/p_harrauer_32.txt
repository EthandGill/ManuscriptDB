[TRANSLATION]
1     Phanesis son of Hergeus, whose mother is Thases, and his wife Takollouthos daughter of Petesouchos, whose mother is Tamarreous, and Thases daughter of Phanesis, whose mother is Takollouthos, Thermouthis daughter of Phanesis, [whose mother is Takollouthos] …
2     of the aforewritten persons, I acknowledge that I have sold and have received payment in full for a part of a house and of a vacant plot in Soknopaiou Nesos of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite no[me] …
3     for the child; to the north the brewery of Soknopaios the great god, and a vacant plot of Papos the village-scribe; to the west the house of Satabous son of Satabous [a]nd the house of Panephremmis …
4     of Paous the elder; to the east a vacant plot of Papos the village-scribe; of which also the measurements: south to north so many cubits, west to east cubits … of poured(?) … [and the]
5     other things I shall do just as is aforewritten.
6     In the 23rd year of Caesar, Hathyr 26, it fell into registration through Leonides: deed of sale and cession of part of a single-storey hou[s]e and of part of a vacant plot in [the] Soknop[aiou Nesos]
7     which Phanesis son of Hergeus makes, and his wife Takoullouthos and Thases daughter of Phanesis, whose mother is Takollouthos, and Thermouthis daughter of Phanesis, whose mother is Takollouthos … [of Soknopaios]
8     the great great god, to Herieus the younger, son of Paopis, whose mother is Tesenouphis, and T…ses son(?) of Nestnephis … … …
