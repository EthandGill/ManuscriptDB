[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Soknopaiou (Nesos), 3 camels. to Vegetos alias Sarapion, strategos, and Hermeinos, royal scribe of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division, from Tesenouphis, son of Tesenouphis, the son of Kiobios, from Soknopaiou Nesos. The three camels, which I registered in the past 20th year in the village through my agent Panouphis, son of Tesenouphis, when I was still a minor, I register for the present 21st year of Hadrianus Caesar, the lord, too. (m. 2) Three camels were entered on the register at the office of the strategos. Year 21 of Hadrianus Caesar, the lord, Mecheir 3. Three camels were entered on the register at the office of the royal scribe. Year 21 of Hadrianus, the lord, Mecheir 3. (m. 4) I, ios, have counted 3. (m. 5) I have counted and confirm.
