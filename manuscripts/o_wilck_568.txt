[TRANSLATION]
1     Pamonthes and Tauron, col[lectors]
2     of the assessment for the customs-arrears (telonikon deficit), to Abos the elder, (son of) Petos[iris],
3     (whose) mother is Thenpikos. We have received for the assessment of the customs-arrears, year 18,
4     in small coin, two drachmas, 5 obols, total 2 dr., 5 ob. Year 18
5     of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
6     Tybi 4.
