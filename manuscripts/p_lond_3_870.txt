[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords …
2     Aurelius Kot… …
3     bird-seller from Panospolis the most illustrious, to Aurelia [Apollonia] …
4     from the same city, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from you, by hand, out of the house, as an interest-bearing loan,
5     five gold solidi, total 5 gold solidi, for trading and for payment … which also to you
6     I shall repay whenever I have means, without delay, under compulsion … and
7     I mortgage to you the share belonging to me and falling (to me) of [a half-part of the whole common house]
8     in a blind alley, which I obtained from the aforesaid lot, by [share of the other half-part]
9     of the whole common house held jointly with you the aforesaid Apollonia; neighbours: on the east the part aforesaid (belonging) to you, on the south the house of Tasais from Psonis, on the north …
10    to Ptolemaios, on the west the same blind alley, or whoever the neighbours may be on all sides; and that it shall not be permitted to me from
11    today (to dispose of) the aforesaid share of the half-part of the whole house …
12    to mortgage or to sell or (to make) any other disposition;
13    I shall pay (the debt); and if I do not pay, that you shall be owner of the aforesaid mortgage, and I shall guarantee (it) to you forever with every guarantee against everyone … who shall come against (it) …
14    under compulsion. And this document of the mortgage, single, I have issued to you for security …
15    … on my behalf subscribing, valid and secure as if deposited in a public archive; and on being formally questioned I agreed.
