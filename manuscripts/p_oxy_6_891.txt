[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: In the consulship of Valerius Constantius and Valerius Maximianus, the most renowned Caesars, The most high senate of the illustrious and most illustrious city of Oxyrhynchus through Aurelius Cornelianus ..., prytanis in office, to their dearest Ptoleminus also called Sarmates, exegetes, greeting. An exegetes being required for the ... of Epeiph up to the 17th, it was decided that you should preside, while the expenses should be paid by the whole body of those belonging to the order. This letter is accordingly sent to you, dear friend, for your information, We pray for your health, dear friend.
