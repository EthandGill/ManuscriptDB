[TRANSLATION]
1     To Kyrikas, deacon and oil-seller: provide for the account of construction [of] …
2     new, in accordance with the command of the lord Georgios, Athur the 14th, indiction 6, of oil two xestes
3     two only; it was [written] [in the month] …
4     the 14th, indiction 6, by me Petterios, deacon and notary of him, it was completed.
