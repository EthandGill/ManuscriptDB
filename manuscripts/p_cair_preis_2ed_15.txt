[TRANSLATION]
1     To riparian [owners], that is [dike-maintenance workers of the Hermopo]lite [district]
2     From Aurelius Tothes [son] of Anubian [village-headman]
3     of the settlement of Pallant of the same Hermopolite.
4     Signing below. I acknowledge, swearing the divine
5     and august oath of our all-conquering master
6     our eternal king Augustus, that I have stood surety for
7     the presence and maintenance of Aurelius Pankolauchin
8     [son] of Pollon, to garrison in your ranks
9     to remain, fulfilling the countryside
10    and for the release of the waters of the garrison until the end of the water-guard duty. And if [he] should desert or abandon post, I myself [shall settle] the account on his behalf
11    or be subject to the divine oath
12    and to the risk concerning this, and [when] questioned I acknowledged [this].
13    In the aforementioned consulate, Mesore 27. Aurelius Tothes [stand] surety as stated above.
