[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Arsinoe, gods and siblings, year [third, under priest Archelaus son of Dama Alexander and]
2     gods and siblings, basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphia of Arsinoe [of Polemo[crates, month ] … [, Egyptian calendar ] … [. Agreement of Asclepiades]
3     Magnetos [of] Antiochus, hundred-arourer, [concerning] the farmers. On these terms [Asclepiades] [Magnetos] [agreed the] fruits
4     the fruits in the allotment, all, to Pokaa [son] of Pames and Petobates …
5     and Pasitis [son] of Petotes and Gampi, all Heracleopolitan farmers on condition [they will do all things in the proper times as the agreement]
6     prescribes concerning the rental of the allotment. The fruits having been cleaned [of all the things belonging to the royal treasury,]
7     at the same time let Asclepiades take the two parts, and the third the farmers [shall receive]. And if they do not render the two parts [let them] pay [damages]
8     one-and-a-half-fold. Concerning all the remaining matters, let it be for them toward [one another as the agreement] of the rental [prescribes.]
