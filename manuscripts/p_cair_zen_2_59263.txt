[TRANSLATION]
1     Philon to Zenon, greetings. If you yourself are well and all other things are to your liking,
2     [would that] it might be as I wish; and be well yourself also. Maiandria wrote to me that a cloak
3     her you order to weave. Now indeed it is soft, but when it should be stretched
4     the garment will be for you. And know that Apollonios, having taken charge of all city matters
5     and is auditing Dionysodoros. I have therefore written to you so that you may know.
6     Farewell. Year 34, Mechir 9.
7     Year 30, Mechir 27. Philon
8     of the garment.
9     To Zenon.
