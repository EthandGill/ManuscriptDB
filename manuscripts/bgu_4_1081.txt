[TRANSLATION]
1   Didymo[s] to Hermione his lady, ma[n]y greetings.
2   Finding the opportunity of the man coming to you,
3   I rejoiced, that I might greet you and pray to the
4   gods for you, that they keep you safe; and now you will do well
5   to write us about your health.
6   Your sons greet you; I greet Eudaimon
7   and Souerous and Eudaimonis and Elkike(?).
8   Farewell.
9   To Hermione ✗ from Didymos.
