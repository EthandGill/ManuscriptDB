[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavii Aetius and [Statius] of the most illustrious, Phamenoth 18.
2     To Flavius Tatianus, the most respected city-official and riparius of the city of the Oxyrhynchites
3     From Aurelius Touan, son of Sarapion, from the village of Tanaeis of the same province.
4     On the day before yesterday, in the evening, a gang of criminals attacked the farm under my [control],
5     having beneath them both horses ... and ...,
6     By the negligence of the responsible guards [I say that] a driving-off has occurred of our
7     cattle, and blows having been inflicted on my outstanding
8     Musaeus he was brought to a half-dead state, all his limbs having been broken
9     ... For this reason these petitions I submit to your
10    diligence, asking [that you] designate [a] public [physician and helper]
11    to examine the patient, so that concerning his state
12    a written notification may be made, and the responsible guards
13    of the places, I mean those from Sepha of the village, to be brought up
14    and to be placed in custody, until we recover the stolen
15    livestock and there be a confrontation with those who dared such things
16    before the laws for [defense]. For I shall not hesitate to set in motion
17    courts concerning this.
18    Aurelius Touan, son of Sarapion, [have submitted] this.
