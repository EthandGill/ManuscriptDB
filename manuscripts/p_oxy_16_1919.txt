[TRANSLATION]
1     For the public prison of the city, for the (sums) allotted to the prison of Alexandria, 2 coins 14 ½ carats.
2     To the most magnificent governor and to his most learned assistant, for the expenses of the court of Alexandria, 129 coins 14 carats,
3     and for the provisioning of the local guardsmen, in refined gold 103 coins 9 ¼ carats, and for the expense
4     of them according to custom 68 ½ ¼ carats into 2 coins 7 ½ ¼ carats, that makes by public (standard) 106 coins 6 carats. Of which there was given
5     to the impost-collector, that which was also brought back, 15 coins, remainder 91 coins 6 carats. That makes by public (standard) 91 coins 6 carats.
6     To the gold-dealer and receiver of the canonical public dues, for the account of Barbathios, that which
7     was also given for the account of Theodoros and Ioannes son of Nonnous, by pu[b]lic (standard) 2 coins 2 ½ ¼ carats, and
8     for the account of Barbathios, that which was also given likewise for the public dues of Serenos son of Nonnous, 3 coins, by public (standard) 5 coins 2 ½ ¼ carats.
9     To the same gold-dealer and receiver of the can[onical dues], for the [acc]ount of [Ph]oibammon the village-head, by public (standard) 14 coins 14 carats.
10    To the same gold-dealer and receiver of the ca[nonical dues, for] … eon of the city, by public (standard) 12 coins 20 carats.
11    To Georgios and Iustos, overseers and receivers o[f the canoni]cal public dues, for the account of
12    Philoxenos son of Theodoros, by private (standard) 4 coins less 8 carats.
13    To the most illustrious Agapetos, for the price of 76 ½ artabas of barley, of the [account] of Phoibammon the village-head, of Alexandria 5 coins,
14    for milling-tax that is the horse-mule (?). …
