[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Antonius, the most distinguished [governor]
2     of Egypt Herculeia
3     From Aurelius Malchus, [son] of Ejonsthos, from the borders of Eleutheropolis of the
4     New Arabia. I guaranteed, lord, Saeibas from [t]ents of the
5     outside Gerrhus, with Didymus Perdika from Bubastus, [regarding] money,
6     but as that one was not making the repayment, [it] was held by the
7     Didymus, my wine vessel, which even having remained for a long
8     time in storage into spoilage, after these things turned, and
9     [became] the cause of loss to me. For it happened to be under his seal and he held the key beside himself, and me then being pressed
10    to give back the money and to receive my things, [he] did not
11    consent. Since therefore it is just for me to be recompensed the
12    wine that was lost, I request that you give me a judge against him, whomever your majesty shall approve. Farewell.
13    Aurelius Malchus submitted [this]. Aurelius ... os wrote on his behalf [this] letter, he not knowing [how to write].
