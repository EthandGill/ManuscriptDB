[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God first [in] all deeds, I, Athanasios
2     I greet [you], I inform my beloved son Kosmos, you have greeted these [matters] through Kosmos, for the security of Germanos [and] Paulos
3     [saying] ... ... these things, as God who is in my heart
4     and the heart of the one who has died, saying they shall go with him, all of us
5     saying I deny it regarding him [in] a sheet of one and I
6     the joy of the one I gave to him, I released it out for you
7     the one I gave to him in truth without fraud, out of the price
8     nomismata one and one-half; the one I gave to him on the grain, nomismata 11, and God
9     the one who will assure us that I deny it regarding him [in] a sheet
10    one joy in the lord
11    the greetings of my beloved son Kosmos, by Athanasios
