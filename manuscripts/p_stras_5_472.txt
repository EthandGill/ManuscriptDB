[TRANSLATION]
1     643 (Christian symbol);
2     [After the consulship of] our master Flavius Justinian the eternal Augustus
3     [and Emperor,] the 3rd golden year, Choiak;
4     to the holy catholic church [called] that of Anastasia, which happens to be
5     [in the city of the Hermo]politans, through the most reverend Phoibammon, presbyter
6     and steward of the same holy church, from Aurelius Aphous son of Phoibammon,
7     his mother being Thermouthis, from the same city of the Hermopolitans, and from Aurelius Ioannes
8     son of Pathoous, his mother being … [from the hamlet of T]eyparat, of the fields
9     of the village [of Enseu] of the Hermopolite nome, greeting. We acknowledge, being jointly liable and mutual sureties, willingly and of our own choice, that we have leased
10    [from the] same holy church for a two-year period reckoned
11    from the crops of the coming fourteenth indiction, with God's help,
12    the arable land belonging to it, one aroura, being waterless
13    … two, of which the neighbours are: to the south, small plots of the same most reverend
14    Phoibammon; to the north and east, public plots; to the west, the aroura
15    … estemo … situated in the eastern part of the same
16    [city] under the guard of those from the village of Enseu of the Hermopolite nome,
17    for the sowing and planting of crops of whatever we may choose, the rent of it each year being … artabae of grain … which rent, new, clean, sifted,
18    [we shall pay and] measure out to you in the month Epeiph each year without delay
19    [and we shall deliver this] rent to the granary of the same holy church
20    [in Hermopolis by our own animals. And we shall provide] each year one basket of wheat-chaff and
21    … each year; and in the event of unwatered land, which may it not be, I shall provide
22    the … [part of the aforesaid rent]. The lease is valid and secure and in force.
23    [We, Aurelii Aphous son of Phoibammon and Ioannes] son of Pathoous, the aforesaid, have leased on the basis of joint liability
24    … [I wrote on] their [behalf, as they do not know letters.] I, Aurelius … s son of Petros,
25    [from Hermopolis, bear witness to the lease,] having heard [from] those who made it. I, Isakos son of Ioannes,
26    … [bear witness to the lease,] having heard [from] those who made it. Flavius …
27    … [from Hermopolis, bear witness to the lease, having heard from] those who made it.
