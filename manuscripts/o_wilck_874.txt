1     There has been measured, of the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 6
2     of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
3     the 5 intercalary days, for Charax, in the name of Lolous
4     (son of) Paphis also called Psenchonsis (son of) Phaeris,
5     of wheat ½⅓. I, Horos, have signed, of wheat ½⅓.
