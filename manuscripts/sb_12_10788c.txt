[TRANSLATION]
1     Received in year ten, of month Kaisareios, day one
2     To Sarapion and Theon, the ones toward
3     the record-office
4     from Lucius Pompeius, son of Lucius, of tribe [Pollia], a dischargeable soldier. I register,
5     according to the things by our master
6     Gaius Kaicina Tuscus having ordered, the property belonging to me as far as
7     the present day in
8     the metropolis of Oxyrhynchite at the [amphodus] of Hermaios, paternal fourth part of a house
9     and of an atrium and of a courtyard and of other shared useful-buildings and
10    undivided toward Teserin
11    of Apion and others, in accordance with the rights which I hold. Previously moreover I also registered into the same
12    [record-offic]e.
13    [Sarapion I have been marked, in year ten of Nero Claudius]
14    [Caesar Augustus Germanicus]
15    [Emperor, Kaisareios, day one]
