[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aurelius Pem]es son of Patas, his mother being Tanouphis, fr[om]
2     the village of Kar[an]is, [ab]out 45 years old, with a scar on his right shin[bone], to Ant[on]ios [S]arapamm[o]n, gymnasiarch, councillor,
3     …
4     [ex-strategus(?)] of the city of the Arsi[no]it[e]s, greeting.
5     I acknowledge by this deed that I have received from
6     you, through your hand, ten talents of silver and three thousand drachmas — total: 10 talents, 3,000 drachmas — as a loan for one month from the present day, of which I shall of necessity make repayment
7     to you on the 15th of the coming month Thoth
8     without interest, without delay; but if at the appointed time
9     I do not repay, there shall follow upon me, for the additional time,
10    interest; and upon the demand, you,
11    Sarapammon, shall have the right of execution both from me, the acknowledging party Pemes, and from all my possessions
12    in every respect, as if by judgment. Let this deed,
13    written in a single copy, be valid; and on being questioned I acknowledged.
14    In the consulship of our m[ast]er[s] [our] Constantine
15    Augustus for the 7th time and of Cons[t]antius the most illustrious
16    Caesar for the 1st time, Mesore 20. I, Aurelius Pemes,
17    have received the ten talents of silver
18    and three thousand drachmas, [an]d I shall repa[y]
19    at the appointed time in full, a[s] is set forth.
20    I, Aurelius Serenos son of Heron, wrote
21    on his behalf, as he is illiterate.
22    [Handwritten note of Pemes,] from [Ka]ranis … es
