[TRANSLATION]
1     Soter, collector of money-taxes of Charax, to Amenothes
2     (son of) Harbechis, (whose) mother is Senchesthotis. I have received
3     for the assessment anak( ) of year 22, drachmas
4     four, total drachmas 4. Year 22 of Hadrian
5     Caesar the lord, Pauni 30.
6     I, Ammonios, have signed.
7     (2nd hand) Others, Epeiph 21, for the assessment anak( )
8     of year 22, 2 dr.; and for the watchtower and other (taxes) of the same
9     year 22, 1 dr. 4 obols, total drachmas 3, 4 obols. I, P…, have signed.
10    Others, Mesore 6, for embankment-tax and bath-tax of Charax,
11    year 22, base drachmas three, 3½ obols, 2 chalkoi, total drachmas 3, 3½ obols, 2 chalkoi.
12    I, …, have signed.
