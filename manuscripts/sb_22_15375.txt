[TRANSLATION]
1     I beseech you, Lord, O God of the fathers of us Abraham and Isaac and Jacob and of all the holy ones and of the holy God-bearer
2     Mary. The holy trinity have mercy
3     on the sinful [and] useless servant of [you]. Have mercy, Lord Jesus Christ.
