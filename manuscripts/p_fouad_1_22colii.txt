[TRANSLATION]
1     To Claudius Dionysios, strategos of the Arsinoite (nome), Polemon division,
2     Isidora daughter of Marion son of Marion, a townswoman,
3     with her guardian, her kinsman Herakleides
4     son of Gaius son of Gaius, of the Sosikosmian (tribe) also called
5     Althaian (deme). With regard to the appearance made by me before
6     you against Deios son of Zeuxis,
7     I swear by the Fortune of Imperator Caesar …
8     Trajanus Hadrianus Augustus
9     that I will be present at Alexandria after
10    the harvest of the present ninth year of Hadrianus
11    Caesar the lord, and
12    that I will attend upon the most excellent archidikastes until I prosecute to conclusion the claims I have against
13    Deios, in accordance with the things done
14    before you in the memoranda, or may I be liable to the oath. The written declaration was written through
15    Theogeiton the law-scribe, Ploution the assistant attending, in the
16    ninth year of Imperator Caesar Trajanus
17    Hadrianus Augustus, Epeiph 30. Isidora
18    daughter of Marion, a townswoman, with her guardian, her kinsman
19    Herakleides son of Gaius, of the Sosikosmian (tribe) also called
20    Althaian (deme), have sworn the foregoing oath. I, Herakleides, wrote also on behalf of Isidora, who does not know letters. Ploution
21    the assistant, I registered (it). Year 9 of Imperator Caesar
22    Trajanus Hadrianus Augustus, … 18.
23    … with regard to the … in the registration.
