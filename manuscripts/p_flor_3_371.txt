[TRANSLATION]
1     from Isias to-Agathina greetings.
2     do-not neglect to-send me the tunic of-Apollonius; I-know for that
3     and to-you it-cares about-him as son.
4     and if through the letter-carrier or
5     other secure [one] I-send to-you in
6     haste a-pound into the little-bundle
7     my. This I-am-working-at. do-not neglect Apollonius; you-know
8     for that I-have-taken him being-sick.
9     Greet Thermos I-pray you to-be-well
10    [give word] to-Agathina
11    from Isias.
