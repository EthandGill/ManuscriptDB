[TRANSLATION]
1     To Oueget[es], strategos of Arsinoe[ite] Herakleid district
2     from Ptolemaios Chaire from the metropolis; on the 16th of the current month Mesore
3     Horion Phakos and Diodoros called Panethol, while I was staying [with my friend]
4     came to the estate of Strouth[os] near the village
5     Bakchiad, damaged [it]; … [stole] from it dates two; when I searched for them I found [them] with them in village Nestos and I dragged [them] and placed [them] with the [official] of the
6     village agoranomos. Therefore I present [this] and request
7     when [he] has been registered
8     [that there] remain for me toward them the [right] and [may I be]
9     aided.
10    Year 4 of Antoninus and Verus, the Lords
11    Augustus[es], Mesore 19.
