[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of Nero Claudius Caesar
2     Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Pharmouthi
3     23, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites of the Thebaid. There acknowledge Dioskous son of Pausiris and his wife Thermouthion daughter of Achilleus, with the guardian (the husband) of her mother
4     Ploutarche, Diogenes son of Pasion, all of those from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     in the street, on the one hand Thermouthion that she has given no more to her aforewritten husband Dioskous
6     from the price of that which he sold to Didymos son of Didymos, with the guardian the same Dioskous, of her paternal share of a house in the quarter of the Hippodrome, of Augustan silver
7     and Ptolemaic coinage, two hundred drachmas of capital, on the grounds that the remainder of the price
8     had been paid out by her to her mother Ploutarche daughter of Harpaesis for that which the aforementioned (man) owed her, the father of Thermouthion and (former husband) of Ploutarche
9     who became and died her husband, Achilleus
10    son of Pastous, in accordance with the receipt that was issued to her; and Dioskous on the spot that he has received from
11    Thermouthion the aforesaid two hundred silver
12    drachmas, and (he is) under compulsion to repay to her
13    without interest within sixty days from whenever it is demanded, or there shall be to her the right of execution of these
14    both from him and from all his
15    property as if by a legal decision. Valid is the contract.
16    In the sixth year of Nero Claudius
17    Caesar Augustus Germanicus
18    Imperator, on the third
19    and twentieth of Pharmouthi, through Andromachos,
20    the (colleague) of Chairemon and Sotades,
21    agoranomoi, it has been transacted.
