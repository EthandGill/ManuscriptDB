[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, yield of year 14 of Antoninus Caesar the lord
2     Payni 21, on account of Charakos, in the name of Thaesius Isidoros, grain, half
3     Third, twenty-fourth, total grain [1/2 3/24]. Zenon, scribe, through Philon, I have signed.
