[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of the Flavii Sabinianus and Theodorus, the most illustrious, Mecheir,
2     of the 20th indiction.
3     The Aurelii, Cornelius son of Peuês, his mother being Obi…,
4     and Pinoution son of Lilous, and Sois son of Joannes, from the hamlet
5     of Nomenios in the area of Erôs in the Hermopolite nome,
6     to Aurelius Paulus, steward, from Hermopolis, greeting. We acknowledge that we have been paid by you the full price agreed upon between us
7     [agreed upon] in full,
8     … of eight artabas of barley, total: 8 artabas of barley, which we will measure out to you in the month Epeiph of the
9     incoming first indiction, in new, clean, sieved barley by the Athenian measure at the threshing-floors of the same village,
10    without delay, there being pledged
11    [to you for this] debt all of our
12    [property,] just as
13    [by legal judgment. The document] is valid and secure, and on being formally questioned we acknowledged it. The Aurelii,
14    Cornelius son of Peuês and Sois
15    son of Joannes and Pinoution son of Lilous,
16    the aforesaid, have received and have been paid the matter of the eight (artabas) of barley, and we will repay it at the time as stated above.
17    The Aurelii, Sarapion son of Pekysis and Hôros
18    … and Anouthês son of Pachymios,
19    [the three of us,] bear witness to the document,
20    [having heard it] from those who made it. Aurelius
21    [Pinoution son of Lil]ous, having been requested, wrote on their behalf, as they do not know letters.
22    [Through] me, Pinoution, it was written.
23    The autograph subscription of Cornelius …
