[TRANSLATION]
1     To King [P]tolemaios [and Queen Cl]eopatra his wife, the [Benefactor] gods, greeting,
2     Apollonios also called Pse[mmonthes son of Her]mias
3     also called Petenephot[es, of the] mercenary [cavalrymen from] Diospolis
4     the Great in the Theb[aid]. I am wronged
5     by Psenchonsis and [Chonompreus] the sons
6     of Teephibis, and by Harat[reus and Pech]ytes,
7     the sons of Horos and Men[temes, whose] father I do not know, or if they have any [other] names,
8     choachytai (undertakers) dwe[llin]g in the
9     same city as myself. For (there being) [belonging to me]
10    an ancestral house in the part to the [sou]th toward
11    the west of the said Diospolis,
12    of sixteen cubits, of [whi]ch there fall to me
13    seven cubits because my [father]
14    Petenephot[es was the eld]er son, of which
15    my [ancestors] and my father had ownership for as long a time as they were alive, indisputably, the accused, having entered
16    into the said house and having built for [themselves] dwellings round about,
17    inhabit (it) by force, laying hold of no just claim.
18    And in the 44th year, in the month of Thoth,
19    while they were building by a great gang of hands without builders, and when I had words with them, using no restraint but
20    carried away by insufferable lawlessness,
21    springing out upon me
22    and defiling me, having insulted me, they gave (me) blows.
23    Wherefore, having made my
24    refuge with y[ou], I beg you,
25    the greatest gods and victory-bearing,
26    if it seem good to you, to refer my
27    petition to the chrematistai (judges) from the Panopolite (nome) as far as Sy[e]ne,
28    of whom Ammonios is the introducer, so that, having brought it to trial and having summoned the accused
29    through Antiphanes the garrison-commander, they may investigate,
30    so that, if it be as I set forth, they may judge
31    the said seven cubits of the house to b[e] mine, as indeed
32    they are, and that there be exacted from them for me
33    for the injustice, according to the ordinance,
34    5 talents of br[onz]e. For concerning the insult
35    and blows and (the wrongs) that they have committed
36    against me, I shall afterwards obtain
37    from them by another petition
38    my just due as is fitting. And this being done, I shall have been helped.
39    Farewell.
