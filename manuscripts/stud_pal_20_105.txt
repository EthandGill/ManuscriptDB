[TRANSLATION]
1     D… both from the same [city] greeting
2     I received from you on behalf of the tax which I farmed on
3     [In] the name of Nemesion [son] of Hermeios, having become
4     My father-in-law's, of 15 arouras in the
5     Third new indiction, of wheat artabae ninety; there are of wheat artabae 90 in full through hand and
6     I have given to you this receipt for security and cancellation of your lease
7     Because it has lapsed, which if I find I will return to you for cancellation, which
8     Written in single form is valid and being questioned I acknowledged [it]
