[TRANSLATION]
1     The venerable K … administrator of the canonical matters paid on behalf of [ships] …
2     many-oared boats of the house of Timagenes, fourth and fifth of the indictions
3     of each year in gold by public weight twenty-four nomismata,
4     it totals for the 2 years of [gold] by public weight forty-eight nomismata only.
5     Of year 249 and 218, of month Thoth the 25th, of indiction 6. On behalf of the same fourth and fifth of the indictions.
6     Theodore [administrator] I have marked this [the] certification as is set forth.
7     Certification of the most admirable Theodore [administrator] … 4th [and] 5th of the indictions in gold by official nomismata 48.
