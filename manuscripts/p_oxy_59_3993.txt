[TRANSLATION]
1     Koprys a[n]d Sinthonis to Sarapammon and Syra
2     their most honored sons, greeting.
3     Before all things we pray that you are well
4     and we make your obeisance before
5     your ancestral gods. We received
6     from Trophimos and the priest six empty jars, and
7     we are being dunned for freight from Dikomia 6 drachmas, 6 obols,
8     and for toll 3 drachmas, three obols, and a flask and 4 pairs of
9     sandals, and from the letter-carrier we received your letter and a rag in which are gold leaves, which we gave to Trophimos.
10    You informed us that we should receive from Petosiris the priest 500 drachmas, and these up to now
11    we have not received. For they are not unreasonable. For they said
12    to us that, "We are collecting them." For they said that
13    they are 448 drachmas. If then these are they, inform us. For we are at them (saying) that, if you wish (to receive) the 500 drachmas from us and to give us the receipt. But they say that, "Receive what we mediated in the temple through Trophimos." If we recover (them) again, we will inform you. Inform us if you wish to repay Apphys from what is deposited. It lies (there)
14    until you inform us. You wrote to us,
15    who is the heir of the deceased? We inquired and fou[n]d the one whom he had adopted as son,
16    but the people around Pet[o]siris are the next of kin. Theon the father of Petosiris gave you a jar
17    of meat. For we did not find
18    a reliable person about
19    to convey it to you. For if
20    you write to Theon, inform him that you received
21    the jar of meat.
22    If an inundation occurs,
23    I will send it to you through the
24    customary sailors.
25    Greet our son
26    Paapis and Thaesis our
27    daughter. Serenos
28    your brother greets you, and
29    Dioskoros and Opheline
30    and Ptolemaios and Heras.
31    The little papyri which you sent we gave to Thonis the son
32    of Kephalas. We pray that you
33    are well and faring well.
34    See that you do not neglect what
35    Apphys informed you about.
