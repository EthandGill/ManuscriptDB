[TRANSLATION]
1     2nd year of the Decii.
2     To Aurelius Apianus, ex-exegete, councillor of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians, and however he is styled,
3     from Aurelius Heroninos, manager of Theadelphia.
4     Account of receipt and expenditure for the month Thoth
5     of the present 2nd year. It is as follows:
6     Of receipt: from the price of wine, single-chous jars 200, 1828, four obols,
7     at 9 drachmas 1 obol the (jar), drachmas …
8     and I received from the bank on the 14th, drachmas 64
9     and I received from Horion and A…eianus, tax on sheep, drachmas 400
10    and from Sabinus, lessee of the mill, through Aynes, drachmas 200
11    and from the price of wheat, 13 artabas, at 20 drachmas the artaba, drachmas 260; 2752, four obols
12    total of receipt: drachmas …
13    of which, expenditure: for the allotment of Alabien, 10 arouras
14    throwing up the head-dikes of the allotment and of the embankment
15    labourers 38 at 2 drachmas, drachmas 76
16    throwing up the head-dikes of the ridges, 18 arouras
17    labourers 25 at 2 drachmas, drachmas 50
18    throwing up the head-dikes and embankment of the place
19    of Pitannis, 16 arouras, labourers 35 at 2 drachmas, drachmas 70
20    gathering stubble, labourers 18 at 2 drachmas [drachmas 36]
21    throwing up the head-dikes of the allotment and [of the embankment]
22    labourers 48 at 2 drachmas [drachmas 96]
23    breaking dry-land in the estate of Pouaris
24    labourers 20 at 2 drachmas [drachmas 40]
25    breaking dry-land in the estate of Paniskos, [labourers] …
26    at 2 drachmas [drachmas] …
27    for the digging of stones raised from the well of the estate of Chrysochoos, labourers 104 at 2 drachmas 2 obols [drachmas 237, 5 obols]

29    to me, Heroninos, drachmas 40
