[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelia Tisois also called Eudaimonis, daughter of Hermeinos, through my son Aurelius …
2     also called Diogenes son of Hermes son of Diogenes, who served as kosmetes of Hermoupolis [the great,]
3     ancient and illustrious,
4     from Aurelius Phibas son of P[ato]s son of Hermes from the hamlet of [A]chilleus. I wish to l[ease]
5     from you for 4 years from the coming 2nd year of Marcus Opellius Seve[rus Macrinus]
6     Caesar the lord, the (arouras) belonging to you near Poimenon from the allotment of N[ikos]tra[tos,]
7     in two beds of fixed-rent land, seven arouras, for the sowing [of fodder-crops; and I shall measure out]
8     [i]n the 2nd year fifteen artabas of wheat and o[ne] hundred dr[a]chmas of silver, [and in the (following) year]
9     as fixed rent ten and a half artabas of wheat and eighty drachmas, [and] the remaining
10    [t]wo-year period in the same manner — the wheat new, pure, unadul[terated], unspoiled, [free of bar]ley, sift[ed],
11    by the Athenian measure, your own man cleaning and measuring at the threshing-floors of the aforesa[i]d hamlet;
12    and the silver I shall pay in approved coin and I shall measure out the wheat in the months Pauni and Epeiph, apart from
13    the grain-charges, which properly belong to me the farmer, being deducted from the sa[me]
14    rent; and I shall give as rent-charge one jar of wine, total 1 [jar]. [Year 1 of Imperator Caesar]
15    Marcus Opellius Severus Macrinus Felix Pio[u]s Augus[tus …]
16    I, Phibas son of Patos, have leased as stated above. I, Aurelius Kastor son of Pameous, have countersigned (followed after).
