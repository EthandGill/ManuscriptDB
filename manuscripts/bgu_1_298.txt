[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Asklepiades, royal scribe of the Herakleides division from Dionysios, son of Chairemon, mother Tapheros from the village of Arsinoe. There belongs (to me) in the same village a house a tower, to the east an entrance and an exit and to the south of these 1/2 an old house, which I register for the house-by-house registration of the 14th year. And I am the above mentioned Dionysios, 48 years old, [without a distinguishing mark ?] and (I register) the daughter born to me, Amm[onarion (?)], mother – – –.
