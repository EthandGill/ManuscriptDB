[TRANSLATION]
1     I learned from your servant, my wife, that the most God-loving common
2     master, our father, is a little unwell, and I was worried not moderately, so that I
3     was compelled to send the letter-carrier to your brotherly Piety,
4     so that through him you might signify to me concerning his health, than which nothing is more precious to me. I beseech then
5     her before all things to kiss his precious feet on my behalf, then, as I said before,
6     through the letter-carrier to signify to me his health. And I beseech you to grant me
7     pardon for the fact that until n[ow] I have [no]t [pl]anned to send [Is]idoros to you …
8     for the Go… [the let]ter-carrier knows … by the swimming-place and the very muddy Neis…
9     gi[ve order then to] bring out a small boat so that I may send to you … [I Phoibammon]
10    the least [servant] do obeisance and greet yo[ur brotherly]
11    God-loving[ness, and] I beseech her …
12    … of [o]ur most holy father I do obeisance
13    … my father; and since the comm[on …] …
14    … [an]d not a little and exceedingly was he distur[bed] …
15    … upon the [re]ading of my least letters …
16    … [that I may res]t from anxiety, since as …
17    … fare well in the Lord.
18    To my master, in all things most God-loving brother Emouthios, monk.
