[TRANSLATION]
1     643 (chmg, an isopsephistic invocation)
2     In the reign and consulship of our most divine lord Flavius Justinus the Younger, the
3     eternal August[us, Emperor, the] ninth [y]ear, Phamenoth 15, of the seventh indiction,
4     the Aurelii Victor and Paeion and Tsi[a] wife of Hatres who also consents to her, the
5     three full-blooded siblings from their father Dios also called Pasaraei, from their mother
6     Tlou who also [is persuaded and consents] and confirms all
7     the things acknowledged in this settlement, originating from Syene, who also provide the subscriber following
8     who subscribes on their behalf, and the witnesses following who bear witness to this
9     agreement of settlement, to Aurelius Iakobos our own full brother from the same father
10    from the aforewritten Dios also called Pasaraei and Tlou, originating from the same Syene,
11    greeting. Since, when our remembered father Dios was among the living,
12    he had you, the aforewritten Iakobos, as one grown to manhood and having come to
13    the proper age, working together with him in the nautical craft, and the aforesaid
14    our father Dios having reached the end of life, we took over what was left to our blessed
15    father, through me, the same aforesaid Tlou our mother,
16    and … we made your exclusion (from the estate), saying that there was left to you
17    something of the advance of the same blessed our father, and you, the aforesaid Iakobos, confirmed, saying that you had appropriated nothing of our paternal property to yourself,
18    and the quarrel having become long, we chose the guild of the most devoted
19    first (members) of the unit of Syene, who, hearing the things disputed between us, perceived
20    that you held absolutely nothing of our paternal property, and for confirmation
21    and full release of the quarrel raised against you by us, and the warding off
22    of every wrangling to be raised against you by us, they decided that we should receive
23    a sworn statement that nothing of our paternal property is left to you. These things
24    therefore having been resolved and agreed by us, having received from you the sworn and dread
25    oath upon the divine and undefiled and venerable relics, that you have appropriated to yourself
26    nothing whatsoever of these same our paternal property, we have come to this
27    agreement of settlement, through which we acknowledge, we the aforewritten Victor and
28    Paeion and Tsia, by voluntary intent and self-chosen will and guileless purpose, free of
29    all guile and any fear and force and deceit and compulsion and circumvention, first that we have received
30    and obtained from you the venerable oath upon the holy and blameless relics, that nothing
31    of our father's (property) you hold, down to anything trifling; then we acknowledge that we have no
32    claim against you, the aforesaid Iakobos our brother, nor shall have,
33    concerning the advance left by the remembered Dios our father,
34    nor concerning any matter whatsoever pertaining to our paternal inheritance, small
35    or great, conceived or not conceived, spoken or not spoken, brought into the open
36    or not brought, set down in this settlement or not set down; simply we shall not proceed against
37    you, neither we ourselves, nor our heirs, nor successors, nor assigns, nor
38    shall we bring action against you nor against your heirs or successors or assigns, nor make a charge
39    nor transgress nor disturb this settlement now and at no time
40    in any manner or pretext or method or sophistry, in court nor outside
41    court, because, as we said before, we are released towards you concerning all things, and
42    every seed of suit and action pertaining to this matter, or able to pertain, is extinguished.
43    And in addition to these things, we have sworn the divine and venerable oath both by the almighty God
44    and the victory and piety of the mightiest lords of the world, the Flavii Justinus the Younger and
45    Aelia Sophia, the eternal Augusti, Emperors, that we shall not assail you nor bring action nor
46    make a charge any more, nor transgress nor disturb this settlement. But if we should wish [at] any time ever
47    to bring action against you or proceed against you on account of this matter, first we shall be found liable to the
48    divine oath and to the peril and charge of perjury, then we shall be answerable by way of attempt and [transgression] of [two] gold [ounces], total 2 gold ounces, with also there being no validity against the things
49    arranged in this settlement, which we have made for your security, valid and firm and lawful,
50    [wi]th [the sub]scription of the one subscribing on our behalf and of the witnesses following, and having been asked the formal question
51    we have agreed. And it is clear that we received from you one solidus by the standard of Syene, which
52    our remembered father Dios gave to you for the military service of your son, and we have no claim against you
53    concerning this, because, as we said before, we have received (it) from you; and thus we have agreed. The Aureli[i] Victor and Paeio[n]
54    and Tsia, from our father Dios Pasaroei, from our mother Tlou who is persuaded
55    and consents, the aforesaid, have established this settlement and it is satisfactory to us as set forth. Flavius Iakob son of Basileides, of the actuarii of the unit of Syene,
56    having been requested, I wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate. Flavius Menas
57    son of Didymos, soldier of the unit of Syene, I bear witness. Flavius Apa Dios son of Martios, of the
58    actuarii of the unit of Syene, I bear witness to the agreement, having heard (it) from
59    the ones who made it. Flavius Isak son of Iakobos, soldier of the unit of Syene, I bear witness.
60    Flavius Komes son of Paamios, soldier of the unit of Syene, I bear witness.
61    Through me, Victor son of Petros, it was completed, Phamenoth 15 of the seventh indiction, in Syene.
62    Settlement in the name of Victor and Paeion and Tsia, full-blooded [siblings], with Iakobos.
