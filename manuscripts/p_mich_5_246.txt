[TRANSLATION]
1     Pasipsemis son of Orseus, leader of the association of Harpochrates, about 45 years old, a scar
2     on the middle of the nose; to each, two spices, two wafers, one rich-cake, apart from the eating-portion.
3     2nd. Orseus son of Herakles [also called] Besis, about 39 years old, a scar on the forehead on the left side: ten silver drachmas.
4     3rd. Eutychos son of Soterichos, about 45 years old, a scar on the right shin: twenty-two silver drachmas.
5     4th. Petesouchos son of Herakles son of Besis, about 33 years old, a scar on the left shin: nineteen silver drachmas.
6     5th.
7     last of the table(?) Mese(?)
8     Sylias: twenty-four silver drachmas.
9     2nd. N(?): twenty-two silver drachmas.
10    3rd. Ptolemaios the elder son of Akousilaos, about 45 years old, a scar on the right forearm: twenty silver drachmas.
11    4th. Herodes son of Marion, about 45 years old, a scar on the middle of the forehead: eighteen silver drachmas.
12    5th.
13    last of the couch of Heron: Papnebtynis son of Paanouphis, about 60 years old, a scar on the middle of the nose: sixteen silver drachmas.
14    couch-master K(?): twenty-two silver drachmas.
15    2nd. Narkissos, freedman of the sons of Maron, and Maron son of Herakleides, about 36 years old, a wart on the nose on the right side: twenty-one silver drachmas.
16    3rd. Herodes son of Eutychos, about 46 years old, a scar on the nose below the brow: seventeen drachmas.
17    4th. Serapas son of Ptolemaios, about 36 years old, a scar on the neck on the left side: fourteen silver drachmas, three obols.
18    5th.
19    last couch-master: Pastoous son of Pastoous, about 38 years old, a scar on the left shin: twelve silver drachmas.
20    Narkiss[os] the freedman: I have voted. Sarapion son of Ptolemaios: I have voted.
21    Herodes son of Eutychos: I have voted. Ptolemais the elder son of Akousilaos:
22    I have voted. [Ptol]emais the younger son of Akousilaos:
23    I have v[o]t[ed].
