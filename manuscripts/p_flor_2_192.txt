[TRANSLATION]
1     Eirenaios to Heroninus
2     to the brother, greetings.
3     I received from you
4     from the account of the respectable one for my expenses
5     for which I will give account: wine in single-chous [measures]
6     sixty-six, [they] total sixty-six.
7     [May you] be well—
8     I pray.
9     Year 4, Hathyr 21
