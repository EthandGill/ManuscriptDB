[TRANSLATION]
1     Sheet.
2     To Pr[ota]rchos,
3     from Apollonia daughter of Sambathion, with her guardian, her mother's
4     brother Herakleides son of Herakleides, and from Hermogenes son of Hermogenes, of (the deme) Archegetes. Apollonia and Hermogenes
5     agree that they have separated from one another from the cohabitation established between them
6     by agreement, through the same court, in the 13th year of Caesar, Pharmouthi; and that Apollonia has also received from
7     Hermogenes by hand,
8     out of the household which she received from her parents Sambathion
9     and Eirene for it (i.e. the marriage), according to
10    the agreement, a small dowry
11    of sixty silver drachmas. (They agree) that the agreement of marriage is void from this very moment, and that Apollonia shall not proceed, nor any other on her behalf, against
12    Hermogenes concerning the demand of the small dowry, and that both (shall not proceed) against one another either
13    concerning the cohabitation or
14    concerning anything else whatsoever
15    of the (matters) up to the present
16    day, from which it is also permitted
17    to Apollonia (to live with) another man and to Hermogenes (with) another woman, both being free from liability, or the transgressor shall be liable to the fixed penalty.
18    Year 17 of Caesar, Phamenoth 14.
