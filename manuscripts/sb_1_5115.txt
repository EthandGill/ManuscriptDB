r.2    ἐφʼ ἧς Ἑρμίας, εἰκοστῆς ἐγκυκλίου κατὰ τὴν π[α]ρὰ Σαραπίω[νος] κ[αὶ τῶν] μετόχων

[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 36, Pauni 21 it was ordered upon the bank in Hermontis,
2     on which Hermias, [one receiving] the twentieth as a general tax according to the [of] Serapion [and the] partners
3     of those toward the purchase document, under which Ammonius the counter-scribe signs,
4     of Totoes Pelaios purchase of bare place about cubit one in the southern part of Pathyris,
5     of which the boundaries are set forth through the aforesaid written-agreement, which was purchased
6     from Pelaios [son] of Phibis for bronze drachmas 1, its tax of which change fifty, [they] come [to] 50.
7     Hermias the banker.
