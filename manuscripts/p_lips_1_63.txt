[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consuls]hip [of our lord] Theodo[sius the eter]nal Augustus, for the 2nd time, [and] of Flavius Cynegius the most illustri[ou]s,
2     Pauni 20.
3     [The] guild of the … of the gov[er]nor's [staf]f, through the following subscribers, to Claudius Chous (son of) Besas
4     [council]lor [of the] city of Anti[noo]u, the m[ost] illustrious, gold-collector of the prov[in]ce of the Thebaid, greeting.
5     [S]ince, upon the memoranda enjoined by the autho[rity] of my lord the most illustrious governor
6     [F]lavius Eutolmius Arsenius, from the account of the assessment made by the predecessor Eusebius, on account
7     of the chaff for the soldiers going up to Pentapolis, who are to depart to Africa, who also by a second
8     order were comma[nded] … there was left over forty-three nomismata of the city of the Koptites, accordingly
9     we in[stru]ct you to do these things: that there be credited to the Koptites themselves, for barley of the 15th indiction,
10    [accor]ding to the conversion-rate ordained by the greater aut[ho]r[i]ty of my lords the most illustrious prefects, for the thi[r]ty mod[i]i, one nomisma of pure gold, and for your security
11    this
12    note we have set forth with the subscription of each, and being questioned we acknowledged.
