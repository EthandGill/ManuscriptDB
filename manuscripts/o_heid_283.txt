[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, produce of the 5th year of Antoninus
2     and Verus, of the lords Augusti,
3     Hadrian, 24th, of the 6th year, for Charakos, name Premonsamios Psansnetos, through Phaminios Thaminios, one-third artaba of wheat, total wheat artaba 3.
4     Amonios, I have marked.
5     Another [entry], treasury of the villages of Hadrian,
6     29th, of the 6th year, for Charakos, name
7     of the same, through the same, one-half artaba of wheat,
8     half, total wheat artaba ½.
9     Amenrosis, I have marked.
