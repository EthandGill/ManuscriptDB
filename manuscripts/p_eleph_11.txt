[TRANSLATION]
1     Euphronius to Milon greetings. [Regarding] the [account of the fallen] and measured into the [great] temple in Apollo's city, and we have written repeatedly
2     to send to us from the [first] time until the present
3     having separated by [man] and in which [years] the fallen things have occurred.
4     But you, until now having neglected [already] all having postponed, send to us
5     to Zeus's great city, so that we not be [hindered by these] to send the accounts
6     to the city. Of which if you accomplish [as steward(s)], write to us by hand, so that we might know.
7     Farewell. Year 25, Epiphi 2
8     To Milon.
