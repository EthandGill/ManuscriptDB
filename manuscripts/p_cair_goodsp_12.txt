[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Asklepiades, praepositus
2     of the fifteenth pagus of the Hermopolite nome,
3     from the Aurelii Kastor son of Teukis, overseer (ephoros),
4     and Ionios son of Ammonios and Sarmates son of Silvanus,
5     the two village-chiefs (komarchs), and Cornelius son of Ammonas,
6     surveyor (quadrarius), and Choous son of Herakles,
7     and Silvanus son of Pkylis and Polys son of Silvanus,
8     all from the village of Prektis of the pagus under you,
9     greetings. We acknowledge, swearing
10    the divine and august oath of our masters
11    the Augusti who conquer in all things, that the
12    (assessment) submitted to you man by man by us,
13    the public officials, of the apportionment (merismos) of the 14th indiction,
14    is reliable and that we have no dispute concerning the establishment of the aforesaid apportionment, and that nothing has been falsified,
15    or we shall be liable to the divine oath and to the
16    danger concerning this; and, on being formally questioned, we acknowledged.
17    In the consulship of Septimius Acindynus, the most illustrious
18    prefect of the sacred praetorium, and of Poplonius Proculus,
19    the most illustrious, Mesore 22, we the aforesaid
20    public officials swore the divine oath as stated above.
21    Aurelius Silvanus, I wrote on their behalf,
22    they not knowing letters.
