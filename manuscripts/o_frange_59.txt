[TRANSLATION]
1     the least one
2     He greets the brother of the dead [one] who writes concerning Moses
3     Behold, that I greet you as
4     faithful, and also as brother
5     that you said this: what [kind of] joy [is] to you in a matter of which you guarded the word
6     the word of [which we are] making haste
7     whoever you say to our [brothers]
8     these in a matter of mine
9     to neglect your heart
10    is a sin
11    in the Lord.
