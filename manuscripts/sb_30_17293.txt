[TRANSLATION]
1     …[to the strategos] of [the] Arsinoite of Themistos and Polemon's districts
2     [from Tesenouphes] son of Horos and Harpagathes, son of Satabus, the two
3     [priests] …[of the phyle of So]knopaios of the temple [of those] being in the village Pelusium
4     [today which is] the new moon of the present mon[th] Mesore
5     [of she-camels, young ones] four being in respect to the
6     …[for the ser]vic[e] of the temple, one of them wh[ite]
7     …[was carried]. Therefore we request that [this] be registered
8     [in these documents] in order that there remain for us the account if anything
9     [concerning theft] comes out, in order that we should be benefited by you.
10    [May you prosper.]
11    [T]eseno[u]ph[is] as of years …scar on the left eyebrow
12    [Har]pagathes as of years 55, s[car] [on the] [m]iddle nose
13    [Year] …[of the Emperor] C[a]es[ar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus] …
