[TRANSLATION]
1   Germanos son of Menephron
2   and Phmois son of Phthouminis,
3   tax-farmers of the menders (cobblers) of year 5,
4   (to) Psenmonthes son of Panameus.
5   We have from you the tax
6   up to Mesore 30, the remaining two drachmas
7   — total 2 dr. — in full payment.
8   Year 5 of Domitian the lo[rd …].
