[TRANSLATION]
1   Hermogenes and Germanos and
2   Hierax and Papremith(es), the four collectors of the money-tax of Elephantine.
3   Paid: Menopheilos the elder, son of Orbaei[s],
4   mother Tisatis, for the assessments of the 10th year of Antoninus
5   Caesar the lord: (worn) four drachmas
6   — total 4 dr. Phamenoth 6. I, Hermogenes, have
7   signed off: 4 dr.
