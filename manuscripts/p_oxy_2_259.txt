[TRANSLATION]
1   Copy of a written bon[d].
2   Theon son of Ammo(nios), P[ersian o]f the epigone,
3   to Demetrios, appointed over
4   the prison of Zeus. I swear by Tiberius
5   Caesar Novus Augustus Imperator
6   that within thirty days
7   I will produce the man I have gone bail for
8   with you, from [t]he civic prison,
9   in Pha̅o̅phi of the present year —
10   Sara(pion) son of Sarapio(n), brought in [w]ith respect to a
11   holograph bond for a gold bracelet of two minaiai,
12   of Magianos, on the account of Aline daughter of
13   Dionysios, citizeness, through Billos, assistant of the dioiketes.
14   And if I do not produce (him) within the
15   stated days, I will forfeit the
16   aforesaid two gold mina-
17   iai without delay, having no
18   [r]ight to gain other time,
19   nor to transfer myself to
20   an[o]ther prison. If I keep my oath, may it
21   be well with me; if I [fo]rswear, the contrary.
22   Year 9 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Pach(on) 22.
23   Pick out for Sarapio(n) — on whose account
24   Dionysios came — that it was paid; and about
25   the account of Heliodoros, settle it with him too,
26   and take the silver. We are looking into it
27   on this account. We did not sail up
28   in this boat because it did not draw …
29   or him giving security … me …
30   until I make him himself …; but if [n]ot,
31   he has embarked. Farewell.
32   See me — how our mother
33   [s]laughtered (a victim?) on account of the bond …
34   […]… […] … I have …
35   […] he acts we[ll].
