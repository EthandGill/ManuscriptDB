[TRANSLATION]
1     Recei(ved), through(?) ..., year 7, Phame(noth) 13.
2     (hand 2) Tryphon to Asklepiades his brother, greeting
3     and good health. Having received the letter from you
4     concerning the matters touching those who disclose the
5     property of your fortunate-in-inheritance brother,
6     and our friend who has died, Petechon,
7     I omitted no zeal nor hardship,
8     until I might learn each (detail),
9     Nearchos the boy following up (the matter).
10    Since then, with the gods (helping), nothing has progressed,
11    but the beginning of the matter has come about
12    from (our) own (means), three (drachmas?) of copper having been ex-
13    pended, the matter will be (advanced); so, when
14    you receive the letter, put in (lay claim to) both the orphan
15    and his mother, so that, for the
16    consternation of those who have dared (this), we may have
17    th[e]m ready for the petition, and, for (its)
18    resolution, they may again follow up
19    the (matters) that will be managed by us. And
20    take care of yourself, that you may be well.
21    Farewell. Year 7, Mech(eir) 29. (verso)
22    To (his) brother Asklepiades.
