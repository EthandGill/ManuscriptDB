[TRANSLATION]
1     To Herakleides, one of the [chief] bodyguards, and epistates and (officer) in charge of the revenues
2     of the district Around Thebes,
3     from Petenephotes son of Petenephotes, embalmer (paraschistes) of those from
4     Diospolis the Great. I am being wronged by Amenothes son of Horos,
5     embalmer. For in the 51st year, on Pauni 13, we made between ourselves
6     an agreement through the foreign-notary's office in Diospolis, by which
7     Amenothes acknowledges to me that he withdraws from the (embalming service) of the Memnoneia
8     and of Phmou[e]s [and] Thynis and Tmontnempamenis and Peinpoar and
9     Pa… and Mesthbe, villages of the Libyan (side) of the Pathyrite (nome),
10    and of Poeis and Pei and Pmouchis, other villages of the Libyan (side)
11    of the Koptite (nome), and of (the service) of those in Diospolis … priests of Ammon
12    and of these men's slaves, and of the Ammoneion of Diospolis
13    and of Psameres, and of the embalming-service (paraschisteia) attendance of the resident and dwelling
14    foreigners in these (places);
15    and likewise, I too acknowledging to Amenothes that I withdraw
16    from the (service) of those in Diospolis, except for the priests of Ammon
17    and their slaves, as has been written above, and from (the service) of those of the
18    Kerameia and Thmononkoiphis and Gabai and Proitos-Ibion of the (district) Around
19    Thebes and Poonpoeis of the Koptite (nome) and of the embalming-service attendance of the resident and [dwel]ling foreigners in these (places) likewise;
20    and we both further agree not to attend
21    those from the villages assigned apart to one another, nor those
22    crossing over and working in each one's villages, nor
23    to collect either pulse or wine or anything else in any manner whatever, nor likewise to attend or to collect (from) those dwelling in one another's villages, from the 40th year
24    until (the time) that they emigrate to their own (villages); nor shall it be lawful for me
25    [to attend] the bodies transported from Poenpoeis to Poeis of the Libyan (side) of the
26    Koptite (nome), because there is not in the said village
27    a place-of-the-dead (nekria); and likewise it shall not be lawful for Amenothes to attend
28    the bodies dying from Poonpoeis; and both are to abide
29    in the terms agreed between themselves, and it shall not be lawful
30    for us to act contrary to any of the aforesaid (terms); and if not, the
31    (right of) prosecution shall be void, and the one acting contrary to contract shall pay in addition
32    to the one abiding, in respect of whatever part or kind he transgresses, a penalty
33    forthwith of thirty talents of copper coinage and the damage according to the law, and sacred to the
34    kings, of stamped silver, 300 drachmas, and none the less shall the
35    agreed terms be valid, in accordance with which, of good will, we mutually conceded (them).
36    The agreement being of this manner, Amenothes,
37    having established a beginning of wrongdoing: first, when there died
38    in the Ammoneion a certain son of Pamonthes, and after this, of a certain leather-worker's daughter transported from Latopolis, and from Hermonthis, of two sons of Philokles, one being transported dead into the Ammoneion
39    (words spanning) …
40    and the other dying in the Ammoneion; likewise also, when the son of Sniblais
41    of those from the Kochlax of the Pathyrit[e nome] was transported into the Ammoneion and died in the Ammoneion, (Poeis) being one
42    of the villages assigned to me and it being my due to
43    attend these, Amenothes attended
44    them contrary to what was due; and now also,
45    in the 2nd year, Hathyr: (concerning) Erieus son of
46    Harbekis, who was topogrammateus
47    of Poeis of the Libyan (side) of the Koptite (nome),
48    of whom he (Amenothes) also had the settlement, and all
49    his possessions are (there),
50    and (Erieus) being in a manner (a man) of the others
51    of Poeis and of the other
52    villages assigned to me,
53    and especially of the officials in Poeis, and holding the seniority
54    over the others dwelling in the village
55    and surpassing all — and by some chance he having died in Diospolis,
56    Amenothes, much the more emboldened in his audacity, attended even this one
57    with a violent contest and being carried away by a certain self-willed and rebellious manner; and clothing himself
58    with great
59    authority, thinking above himself, it being lawful for him neither
60    to attend this one nor the [others] aforewritten, nor to lay hold of them, nor of anyone else of those
61    from the villages assigned to me,
62    (attending) these
63    though it was my due, … th … e … enos of my
64    livelihood; and … t … n oppressing (me)
65    having condemned everything of what is right, acting contrary to contract and (against) the terms specified through the
66    agreement, and turning his back on the penalties (fixed) through it, and
67    setting at nought the penalties that follow upon him, as if he were someone in the grip of lawlessness. Therefore I ask that he be summoned and compelled to render me justice
68    and that he be exacted
69    the penalties, and that regarding all matters justice be granted
70    to me. And concerning the damages that will befall
71    me after these things, I will establish
72    my ac[count] against h[im].
73    Farewell.
