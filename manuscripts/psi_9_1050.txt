[TRANSLATION]
1     From A[lu]pius.
2     Give to Sarm[a]tes and Nilus, in place of which
3     they receive monthly for
4     [wages] each one whole artaba of wheat, from the current month Hathyr one and a half artaba, receiving
5     of them what they had previously received
6     silver and oil. I have marked.
7     And give monthly to each
8     of wheat one artaba
9     and half and what they had previously [received] silver and oil.
10    To Heroninus, administrator of Theadelphia
11    Year 10, Hathyr 6.
12    Hathyr 6.
