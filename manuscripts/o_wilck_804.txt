[TRANSLATION]
1     Measure of the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 13
2     of Trajan the lord, Pachon 26, in the name
3     of Hermias (son of) Marius and Apollonios
4     (his) son, of wheat five artabas; and in the name of Dioskoros
5     (son of) Ammonios and Apollodoros (his) brother,
6     of wheat ten artabas, total wheat 15 artabas. I, Pekysis, have signed.
