[TRANSLATION]
1    The fifth year of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
2    Imperator, Pauni (2nd hand) 25 (1st hand), in the city of Oxyrhynchus of the Thebaid.
3    Tryphaina daughter of [...]orion, of those from the city of Oxyrhynchus,
4    with her guardian, the husband of her sister Tryphaina's daughter
5    Isidora — namely Dionysios son of Ammonios — lent to Tryphon and
6    Onnophris, both sons of Dionysios, Persians of the epigone,
7    and to Tryphon's wife Sareus daughter of Apion, a Persian woman,
8    with her guardian, the aforewritten husband Tryphon — to all of them,
9    of those from the same city, in the street: of silver of Augustus'
10   and Ptolemaic coinage, three hundred and
11   fourteen drachmas of capital, to which nothing at all has been added.
12   Let the borrowers repay to Tryphaina the
13   four hundred and fourteen silver drachmas on the thirtieth
14   of the month Neos Sebastos of the incoming sixth year
15   of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
16   without any postponement. And if they do not repay as
17   has been written, let the borrowers pay to Tryphaina
18   the capital with half as much again, and for the time overrun
19   the appropriate interest, being sureties for one another for the payment;
20   and let the right of execution belong to Tryphaina against the borrowers
21   and against whomever she chooses and against all their property,
22   as if by legal judgment. The contract is valid.
23   (3rd hand) The fifth year of Nero Claudius Caesar
24   Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Pauni the twenty-
25   fifth. Through Apollonios, formerly styled Secundus,
26   appointed by the associate agoranomoi, it has been registered.
