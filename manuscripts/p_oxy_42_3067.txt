[TRANSLATION]
1     Achillion to [Hieracapollon] the brother
2     greetings
3     Being about to send for the sister necessarily through those things sent by me for this purpose, I write
4     first indeed greeting you
5     and my sister and the son
6     our Dionysius; then exhorting [you] to write to me both concerning your welfare and what you need from here. For having done this you will be to me
7     [having granted a] favor. For we will seem through
8     [the] letters each other to see.
9     I wish [you] well.
10    To Hieracapollon [brother]
11    from Achillion
12    [strategos] of Marmarica.
