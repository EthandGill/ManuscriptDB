[TRANSLATION]
1     Ioannes [son] of Abraamios from the village of Aphrodite to the brothers Enoch [son] of Hermatos
2     and Phoibammon [son] of Triadelphos and Ioannes [son] of Hermeios and another Ioannes [son] of Promaos and Psan
3     … [first-dwellers] of the same village, greetings; I have received and have been satisfied
4     [from you of the] note of fifty-one [coins] of good weight by balance and nothing [I owe you]
5     [I complain about the afore]said gold nor about [any other matter]
6     [written or unwritten] except for fifty-four gold carats
7     alone by balance, [making] carats 54, and may it not be possible for me ever at any time or in any period to … or contest
8     [about any matter whatsoever written or unwritten, nor regarding any other] … during the intervening time until now except for the fifty-four carats and for your
9     security I have made for you this document with my signature as is set forth. Choiak 24, second indiction.
10    Ioannes the aforesaid accords with me [regarding] the document as is set forth. After the return of the aforesaid
11    54 carats, so that I restore to you your note of fifty-one coins.
12    Pilatos [son] of Apollos, jurist, I witness to the document having heard [it] from Ioannes, the one who also [placed it].
