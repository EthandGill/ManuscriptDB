[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greeting Plaison son of Eudoxos, Theoxenean, of Dionysios […]
2     a petition to Diophanes the strategus through which I clarified other things and so that the grain [would be distributed] …
3     concerning which Diophanes wrote to Denias to hold, but when my petition was ratified …
4     to point out Lamedon and Phormiōn acting deceitfully, Diophanes wrote …
5     and when I delivered the letter to Denias and having shown …
6     me Lamedon [against] … having received the grain and with the hundred artabas purification of wheat artabas twelve …
7     contract … demanding, has made no reckoning of me until now. [I beseech you therefore, King,]
8     to order Diophanes [the] strategus to write to Denias the epistates to clarif[y] …
9     the grain as it was written to him; similarly too if Lamedon demanded of me having received the [grain] …
10    to perform the contract for him. For when this has been done, to you, King, having fled I will [obtain justice].
11    To Denias. Clarify for us concerning what [he] writes. Year one, G[orpiaios twenty-eight, Tybi twelve].
12    Year one, Gorpiaios twenty-eight, Tybi twelve.
13    Plaison concerning what [he] demands be written
14    to Denias.
