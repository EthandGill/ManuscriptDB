[TRANSLATION]
1   Lucius Bellenus Gemellus
2   to Sabinus his son, greeting.
3   By all means send Pin-
4   daros the field-guard of
5   Dionys[i]as, or his father,
6   since Hermonax asked me that
7   he might look over his olive-
8   grove at Kerkesoucha, since
9   it is thick with trees, and out of them
10   he wants to cut some trees. So you
11   will do well to send him
12   at once; and on the 18th or 19th
13   send fish to the city for 12 dr.
14   for the fortieth-day feast of the little
15   […] son(?) of Gemella.
