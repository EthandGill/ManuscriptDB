[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Taesis and Allous, both daughters of Kopres, their mother being
2     Sarapias, and Kastor and Chairemon and Klementina,
3     the three children of Ptolemaios, their mother being Taesis, all from the village of
4     Karanis, to one another, greeting. Since there were left to us from
5     the inheritance of our father two millstones (koptourai), and we (women), I
6     Klementina and Kastor, were allotted one millstone, and I
7     and Chairemon and Taesis and Allous (were allotted) the one millstone with you
8     Chairemon, so that each (party) has a half share,
9     we acknowledge, according to this security, that, if anyone
10    comes against this millstone which is with you Chairemon, we the four parties jointly and equally shall remove the one coming against (it) at our own expenses,
11    you, the (party) of Taesis and Allous, having authority to hold and have ownership of the half share falling to you
12    and to manage (it) as you choose. And having been questioned, we agreed.
13    The Aurelias Taesis and Allous, we have agreed to all,
14    and we have received the half share of the millstone as is set out above.
15    I, Aurelius Diodoros, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
16    I, Aurelius Chairem[o]n, have agreed as is set out above. The Aurelii Kastor
17    and Klementina, we have agreed as is set out above. I, Aurelius Polion,
18    wrote on their behalf, they being illit[e]rate.
19    Year 2 of our lord Lucius Domitius
20    Domitianus Augustus, Hathyr 27.
