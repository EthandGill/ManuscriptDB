[TRANSLATION]
1     To Deios and Sabinus, former gymnasiarchs, and partners, public bankers,
2     the undersigned public cattle-breeders of the following villages, through Herakleides their appointed agent: we have received from you the (sums) ordered to be paid to us by Ptolemaios, royal scribe of the division of Polemon,
3     acting as deputy also in the matters of the strategia of the divisions of Themistes and Polemon, for the freight-charges of wheat
4     and barley which we brought down from the granaries of the division of Polemon to the harbors of processing at the
5     (port of) Ptolemaios, of the produce of the 17th year: wheat 91¾(?) artabas, barley 58¼ artabas, at the price per artaba
6     of the wheat, according to what was ordered, at 8 drachmas, the silver drachmas amounting to 735, 4 obols, and of the barley
7     at 480 drachmas per 100 artabas, the silver drachmas amounting to 278, 3 obols, 4 chalkoi, the total being one thousand fourteen silver drachmas, 1 obol, 4 chalkoi. And they are: of Ptolemais of the Bee-keepers: Hatres son of Papontos
8     wheat 3⅙ artabas, barley 2¼ artabas; Theon son of Theon wheat 2 7/12(?) artabas, barley 3 7/12(?) artabas; Demetrios son of Petesouchos
