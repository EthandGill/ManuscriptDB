[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Diocletian Augustus for the 8th time
2     and Maximian Augustus for the 7th time.
3     Aurelius Pasis son of Neilammon, his mother being Tagenes, … from Thraso, [about 60 years old,] with a scar above
4     his right foot, acknowledges to Aurelia [Soumōeis], her mother being Taēsis, from the same village, that he has received
5     from her — he, the acknowledging Pasis — through the hand
6     from the house, in silver, as capital, four
7     thousand three hundred and twenty drachmas, total 4,320 drachmas, as a loan for one month
8     from the present day; which drachmas,
9     four thousand three hundred and twenty, he shall of necessity repay … on the twentieth
10    of the coming month Mesore to Soumōeis without delay; and if by the appointed term he does not repay [or]
11    does not act, he shall pay the interest upon them
12    and there shall accrue to Soumōeis, upon the exaction,
13    the right of execution both against the acknowledging Pasis and against
14    all his property, as by legal judgment. And this
15    handwritten note, drawn up in a single copy, upon the subscription of the one writing on behalf of the acknowledging party, he being illiterate, which shall also be valid wherever produced;
16    and being questioned he acknowledged.
17    In the 19th and 18th and 11th year of our lords Diocletian and Maximian
18    Augusti and Constantius and Maximian the most illustrious
19    Caesars, Epeiph 21. I, Aurelius Pasis son of Neilammon, acknowledge
20    that I have received the four thousand
21    three hundred and twenty drachmas of silver, and I shall repay by the appointed term without delay as aforesaid, and being questioned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius
22    Nemesis, wrote this on his behalf, he being illiterate.
