[TRANSLATION]
1     From
2     my lord brother Aphoutic the guardian at Pela from Phib the ship-owner.
3     Provide to my lord Martyrios the assistant [with] dirty grain [in] artabas
4     forty, dirty grain [in] artabas 40, only. Pachon 29, [of the] 9th year. Similarly for Anoup
5     the scribe of Tholthes [dirty] grain [in] artabas twenty-four, which makes altogether dirty grain artabas 64 only.
6     Through me Phib [it] is correct.
7     Receipts of Phib for Menas the sailor which were also given to Martyrios the assistant
8     artabas 64.
