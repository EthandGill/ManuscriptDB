[TRANSLATION]
1     … seim …
2     … in the city …
3     … registered …
4     [Ath]enaieus also called Erichthonios, that to Alexander son of Isidoros son of Alexander, Matidios also called Kalliteknios, [the said] —
5     [Al]exander has sold to Cornelius, from now for ever,
6     the property belonging to him by right of a cession which was made
7     to him in the present month Pharmouthi of the current year
8     according to a bank-transfer of the same bank, from the children of Chairemon son of Ammonios Sebasteios also called Herakleios, deceased: a half-share of a two-storey house with a ground floor and
9     a courtyard and appurtenances and all the things pertaining, in the 4th grammat-
10    block 7 of the Antinoite (city), the neighbours of the whole house being: on the south a public street
11    through which is the entrance and exit, on the north (the house) of Tithoetion and others, on the east (that) of Cornelius and others, on the west another three-storey house of the seller, or whoever the neighbours may be on all sides,
12    and that the seller has received from the buyer the
13    price agreed between them of silver,
14    six hundred drachmas, total of silver 600 drachmas; that therefore the
15    buyer Cornelius shall hold and own the half-share of the house
16    and courtyard and all appurtenances, and shall administer and dispose
17    concerning it as they may choose, without hindrance, the guarantee
18    being continually maintained for every warranty, devolving upon the seller Alexander and those acting for him; and that every person who shall proceed on this account in whatever manner he shall remove him forthwith at his own expense, or shall pay
19    to the buyer and those acting for him double the price and
20    to the public treasury an equal sum and the damages and the expenses,
21    apart from the sale remaining valid. Alexander son of Isidoros,
22    Matidios also called Kalliteknios, I have sold to Cornelius the property
23    belonging to me, the half-share of the aforesaid two-storey house and courtyard
24    and all appurtenances, and I have received the price of six hundred drachmas of silver, and I will guarantee with every warranty
25    as is stated above. Cornelius, I have bought as is stated above.
