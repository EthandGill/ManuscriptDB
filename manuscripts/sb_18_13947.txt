[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our master Flavius Anastasius, eternal [Augustus], the 3rd, and Benantius, the most brilliant, Phaophi 3, indiction 1.
2     [Aurelius] Pelagius [son] of Daueit, mother Thecla, from the village
3     Nesmimeis of the [Oxyrhynchite] nome, to Aurelius Victor, son
4     [of] Phoibammon … of the most magnificent count
5     [Strategi]us from the city [of the Oxyrhynchites] greetings. I acknowledge that I have received from you the mutually agreed price, pleasing to me, in full, wheat, artabas
6     thirty-one [and barley] artabas twenty, totaling wheat artabas 31 and
7     barley artabas 20, which [are] new products, clean, [winnowed]
8     by the just tenth measure, I will necessarily repay to you
9     in the month Pauni of the present year 184 [or 153] from new
10    crops of the second distribution, with god's help, without delay, your collection being made
11    both from me and from all my possessions.
12    The document [is] authoritative, written simply, and being questioned I acknowledged [it].
13    Aurelius Pelagius son of Daueit, the aforementioned, I have received the price
14    in full, wheat artabas thirty-one and barley artabas twenty, and I will repay at the appointed time and everything aforementioned agrees with me as stated. Aurelius Samuel
15    son of Serenous, deemed worthy, wrote on behalf of him letters
16    [him] not knowing.
17    Of Pelagius, son of David, from village Nesmimeis, wheat artabas 31 and barley artabas 20.
