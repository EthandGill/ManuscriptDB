[TRANSLATION]
1     Account of gold given to the banker on behalf of the stables
2     of Takona, the 9th and 10th indictions, through the following persons,
3     thus:
4     from Stateuos chartulary, on behalf of those from Pleein, Pauni of the 9th indiction, 33 solidi of Alexandria, … carats [,]
5     from the weighers of Kynon, Epeiph of the indiction
6     9, of Alexandria, 33 solidi, 13 carats,
7     from Theodoros logistes, on behalf of the price of wine of Kynon, of Alexandria, 29 solidi, 6 carats,
8     from the weighers of Kynon likewise, Pachon of the 9th indiction, of Alexandria, 17 solidi, 20 carats … [,]
9     from those from Sepho, on behalf of Kynon, of Alexandria, 50 solidi, 6½ carats …
10    from Kalos chartulary, through the seal of Ioannes Kilitoch, of Alexandria, 66 solidi, 1 carat …
11    from the weighers of Kynon likewise, of Alexandria, 18 solidi,
12    from those from Pleein, Pharmouthi 16 of the 9th indiction, of Alexandria, 17 solidi, 20 carats,
13    from the same, Pharmouthi 27 of the 10th indiction, of Alexandria, 11 solidi, 14 carats,
14    from Philinos stableman, Mecheir … 3 of the 9th indiction, in counted coins 44 solidi, by tale, into Alexandria, 35 solidi, 15 carats …
15    and for the 10th indiction, thus:
16    that given by those from Sepho, of Alexandria, 50 solidi, 6½ carats,
17    that given by those from Pleein in two receipts, of Alexandria, 19 solidi, 11 carats,
18    likewise there was reckoned to him, of Alexandria, 29 solidi, 11½ carats,
19    likewise there was reckoned to him, of Alexandria, 16 solidi, 7 carats.
20    There come, also for the 10th, of Alexandria, 125 solidi, 12 carats.
21    Together the sum reckoned for the 2 years, of Alexandria, 284 solidi, … car[ats] … [,]
22    so as remainder with the banker, of Alexandria, 29 solidi, half a carat,
23    a[nd] for the 11th indiction … [that] given to the banker, thus:
