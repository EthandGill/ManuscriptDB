1     To [V]aleria Thais, also called Titania, through
2     of the husband Aurelius Aune Sarap[p]atos and as he is styled by
3     [Aur]elius A[…]oulos Nile of mother
4     …atos [from] village Philadelphia.
5     [I w]ish to lease from you
6     [the] holdings of yours around the village Ta[ni]s in the place called Thaneplor[o]is, [a] lot
7     [k]atoik[i]kos [aro]uras twelve upon
8     [a period of years] … pi …
9     … [of the public] [all grain and monetary] being towards you [the land]holder and after the time I will deliver the lot as also I have received [it]. The lease is valid.
10    and when questioned I agreed.
11    Valeria Thais, she also called Titania
12    I have leased as aforesaid.
13    In year 5 of our lords Valerianus
14    [and Gallienus] the Augustuses, Mesore 30.
