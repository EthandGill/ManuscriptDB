[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 2 of Gaius Pescennius
2     Niger [of the] Augustus, Mecheir 11
3     Saprion to Or[se]nouphis, son of Horus, farmer, greetings.
4     I have received from you for a single bundle of fodder
5     of the same year on account of silver, drachmas
6     four, making four drachmas, Pharmouthi 6, twenty-eight obols,
7     making 28 obols, Pachon 10, twenty-eight obols, making 28 obols,
8     Pauni 7, four drachmas, making 4, year 3
9     Thoth 6, for the past year 2, of silver, four drachmas, making four drachmas.
