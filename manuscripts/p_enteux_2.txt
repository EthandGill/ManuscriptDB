[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting, Harmeusis, wool-merchant, one of those dwelling in Krokodilon polis.
2     I am wronged by Seos, a Jew, one of those dwelling in Alabanthis. For when he had sold me of bastard wool
3     118 fleeces at 4 drachmas, five obols, 2 chalkoi (each), which I had bought up for Amyntas so as to … from the royal allotment, and when he had received from me an earnest-money of 76 drachmas, on condition that the remainder … I shall pay him
4     when I shear the wool, Seos, coming upon … and … and has sheared (them)
5     and has carried (them) off to himself, and when the wool is demanded back from me by him, [he does not give it back. I beg] therefore
6     you, king, if it seem good to you, to give order to Diophanes the strategos that [he write] … [to the epistates] to send Seos before Diophanes the strategos, so that, if it appear …
7     the wool and that he has received earnest-money, he may compel him to give back to me the wool. For [this being done,]
8     I both shall not be wronged, and the dues [owed] at Alexandria … [Amyn]tas, for whom I bought it up, and a larger cargo, but having taken refuge with you, king, [the common]
9     savior of all, we shall obtain justice.
10    Year 4, Dios 3, Phamenoth 27.
11    Harmiysis, wool-merchant, against
12    Seos, concerning wool.
