1     [To our lord a]nd brother
2     … [Apa] Paieous, Pennes
3     [in the Lord, very man]y greetings.
4     [Before all things] I p[ra]y to the Lord God
5     … having brought, with
6     … to him who has all things … [the brot]hers laboring together
7     … the disposition which to a brother
8     … you especially, that with God
9     … of one another the

13    to remember one anoth[er] in the Lord Christ for
14    the health of both parties; and doing this,
15    we shall be called Christians in Christ. And this
16    I hold in my breast, that even if we are far
17    from one another in the faith of the ever-remembered God
18    and our savior … we … and upon him
19    is our salvation, by the power of Christ, since we are with those
20    who do his great power and who have kept the (good) name. I greet you
21    much, and al[l] the [bro]th[e]rs by name who are with
22    you; and the blessed Paulos greets you and the

24    greet you, along also with all the
25    brothers with you. And your love has appeared
26    to all, the (love) that is proclaimed abroad, especially that borne witness to us by Psais and Harpokras, fishermen,
27    so that it is reckoned together with the other (acts of) love.
28    And doing this, we shall be called fellow-anointed-ones (of Christ).
