r.2    παράσχου τῷ ἐνώδια φέροντι ἀργυρίου μυ[ριάδας δηναρίων]

[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Neilos the perfume-merchant, Didymos [assistant greets].
2     Provide to the one carrying incense silver [myriads of denarii].
3     Two hundred only becomes 200 myriads of denarii. I have noted [myriads of denarii].
4     Myriads 200 of denarii. … Pachon 30.
