[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis of the produce, 4th year of Antoni[nus]
2     Caesar of the lord, Epeiph 14
3     on behalf of Charax, in the name of Psaitos, likewise of Stratos, of wheat in artabae 9
4     and on behalf of Nesoi, in the name of the same, of wheat in artabae 4, [it] totals of wheat in artabae
5     thirteen. Horos, I have marked. and on behalf of Agores
6     in the name of Gaios Ioulios Clement, through Phthuminios
7     Dioskouridou, of wheat in artabae two [and] half, [it] totals of wheat in artabae 2 [half]. Horos, I have marked.
8     and [on] behalf of Charax, in the name of Dioskouridou and of Psansnutos
9     Phthuminios, of wheat in artabae four [and] half
10    fourth, [it] totals of wheat in artabae 4 [half] 4th. Horos, I have marked.
