1     In the reign of our most divine [and most] pious [master Flavius Justinian the eternal Augustus]

10    Eminence, on account of … the yield of win[e] of the present third [indiction] …
11    to her, of wine two hundred and fift[y-n]i[ne] doubles. We have received [from those be]lo[nging to]
12    your Emin[ence of go]l[d] o[n]e nom[isma, and we acknowledge to provide] to her on account of this
13    other se[venty jars] of wine [ca … ] [making together of wine] jars 329,
