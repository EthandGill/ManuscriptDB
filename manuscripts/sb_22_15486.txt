[TRANSLATION]
1     Year twelve of Emperor [Caesar]
2     Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Choiak 20
3     They wrote out to Areius and associates … [hand-labor tax]
4     Panephremis, [son] of Pausires, of Panephremis, mother Tanomgeys, from Soknopaiou
5     Nesos, for the hand-labor tax of the same 12 year, on account
6     Of silver, drachmas twenty, become drachmas 20, and Pausires, [son]
7     Of mother Tanephremis, likewise the of silver drachmas twenty
8     Become drachmas 20, and Herieu[s], another son, likewise
9     On account of silver drachmas twenty become drachmas 20, and Pharmuoth
10    27, through Harpocration, likewise [the] hand-labor tax [the] aforementioned names … [of silver] drachmas ninety …
