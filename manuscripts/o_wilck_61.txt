[TRANSLATION]
1   Petroszmethis son of Pelaios Paiai,
2   for the poll-tax of the fourth year
3   of Trajan the lord,
4   on account eight drachmas — total 8 dr.
5   Likewise four drachmas — total 4 dr.
6   The same, the remaining 5 drachmas.
7   I, Hermogenes, the collector,
8   wrote (this).
