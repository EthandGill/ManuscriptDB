1     To Ailius Numisianus, strategus of Arsino[e, division of] Themistos
2     and Polemon divisions
3     from Sotos, [son] of Heron; on the 20th of
4     the present month of Thoth, Pt[…]
5     [a certain person,] son of Psenobastios of Adetos, [about …] years old, elder of the village of Phylakite [Isla]nd
6     arrogantly [having happened upon it] according to my
7     absence came …
8     took a tunic …
9     and having come [and having] caught [him, angered] by the arrogance [toward him]
10    [I set] the village archaephodos
11    [the one] holding [it] and until now [a certain person]
12    … with himself; therefore, lord, no[t being able] to rest, I ask, [if it seems good to you]
13    [it seems], for [him] to be brought before you …
