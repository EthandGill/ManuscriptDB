[TRANSLATION]
1     Pechytes [son] of Ammonius to Psenseine,
2     greetings.
3     See how you are able to come down, so that you might sail with me to Coptos, since I have written to [the people] of Apollonius about the corpses from Coptos, so that you might come down with me and we might receive the corpses which are there
4     [remain] laid out, twelve. The islanders
5     from Syene have departed …
6     saying to them: if you do not [come down to He]ro, [they] give [them], if you do not wish to [come] down, send your brother, [so that]
7     he might come down, before the [corpses] …
8     so that we might sail to the [open space/place …]
9     Farewell. Epip[h] 4.
