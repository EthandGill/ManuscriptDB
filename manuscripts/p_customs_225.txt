1     has been assessed through the gate of the Island of Soknopaiou
2     the hundredth and fiftieth [tax]: Papyris exporting
3     on one camel oil, metretes
4     four, and on another [camel] wheat artabas six duty drachmas
5     twenty-two obols. Year 20 of Antoninus
6     Caesar the lord, Athur [month], [the] 20th
