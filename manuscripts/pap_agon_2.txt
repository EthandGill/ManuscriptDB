[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy
2     Of Oxyrhynch[i]t[es of] [the] bright and most-bright city
3     The most-powerful council [through] Aurelius Euporus, also called Agathos Daemon, having been cosmete, exegete, record-keeper of [the] most-bright city of the Alexandrians, having presided
4     and as he is styled, councilor, chief-magistrate of the prytany,
5     to the keepers of property-acquisitions, most-beloved, greetings;
6     rightful matters he set before us, hanging from himself
7     of universal laws, Aurelius Apollodidymus of Plutio[n], concerning [being enrolled]
8     him into the assembly of the sacred synod,
9     and according to the customary things, having worshipped
10    the divine things even more these [matters] to him we confirmed. Since then we judged it to be fitting that these [matters] be openly reported to you, in order that you know the exemption subsisting to him from the
11    laws, and the proper provision you will make in his name; it is instructed to you, most-beloved.
12    I pray for you to be strong, most-beloved.
13    Year 5 of our lord Aurelianus
14    Augustus, Mecheir.
