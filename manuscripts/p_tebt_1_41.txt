[TRANSLATION]
1     To Kronios, chief of police of Kerkeosiris,
2     from Harmiysis the komarch and the
3     royal cultivators of the same place. Since rather frequently Marreis the topogrammateus,
4     together with several others armed with swords,
5     comes to the village
6     and, with every kind of harassment,
7     makes continual seizures of some of us and of other
8     women, extorting them, taking no account of the pledges we hold from Lysanias
9     the kinsman and strategos,
10    and, in consequence of such things, many of us
11    being in fear have withdrawn to the neighbouring villages.
12    On which account, being hindered in the
13    payment of what is owed both toward
14    the rent and the crowns (dues),
15    therefore we submit to you this memorandum
16    so that you may refer it to those whom it concerns,
17    in order that, Marreis being restrained
18    and made to pay the extortions,
19    he may meet with the fitting rebuke,
20    and that we ourselves, being unmolested,
21    may be able to hold to the
22    payment, and that nothing may be lost
23    to the king.
24    Farewell.
25    Since therefore the accused Marres, besides
26    the extortion, harasses the cultivators
27    so that they cannot attend to the payment of
