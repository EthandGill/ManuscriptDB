[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Stephanos, [son of] Elusinium,
2     administrator of the 9th indiction, from the city of Antaios,
3     to Aurelius Hermeias, [son of] Sarapammon, and associates,
4     distribu[tors] of Philon, 9th indiction, greetings.
5     I have brought and delivered to you for
6     the well-being of the very noble soldiers
7     situated with you, from the delegation canon of the ninth indiction,
8     wine two-thousand xestae, which amount to wine xestae 2000, and I have given you this receipt for security and having been questioned [I] acknowledged.
9     In the consulship of the lords Gratian the 5th
10    and Theodosius the 1st, of the eternal Augusti,
11    Choiak 30. Aurelius Stephanos, I gave the receipt as set forth
12    … this receipt [was] … in
13    in the city of Antaios.
