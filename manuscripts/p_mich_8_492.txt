[TRANSLATION]
1   N[...]nas to Thaisarion, very many greetings.
2   Before all things I
3   pray for your health, and I make your obeisance every
4   hour before the lord Sarapis. I want you to know, sister,
5   that I did not write a letter to Koprous, for I gave her
6   everything by word of mouth. When she went up, the matter had not yet reached an end,
7   but now I thank the lord Sarapis: everything has turned out well.
8   And the letters have not gone out; I am awaiting them. Know that
9   our brother Ptolemaios has sold all his (property),
10   the share falling to him from the eight arouras near the village
11   of Sebennytos, to Philoxas the cavalryman; and in the place Pammebis and in
12   the place Penes and in the place Tbynis and in the place of Kollauthis
13   and in the place Psens and in the place Pilipites; and the share falling
14   to him of the palm-grove in the place Okes — all these have been
15   sold to Heras your son. And indeed, had I not been in
16   Alexandria, he was about to sell everything — yours and mine and our mother's.
17   I thank Ptolemaios, the husband of the daughter
18   of Heuremon; through him everything has been done. He lent to me as
19   to a brother. If Sarapis allows and I get the letters, I have caught up with
20   you. I have told Koprous all these things by word of mouth, so that she
21   (...)
22   may tell you. If you learn that the village-scribe has put me into a (liturgical) service,
23   make known to me into what kind of service he has put me. And if anyone has
24   wronged you, make known to me through the letter his name.
25   Greet my brother Ptolemaios warmly, and Diodora
26   and Longinus and [...] and Gemella and my [m]other
27   warmly. (2nd hand) [I pray for your health.]
28   (2nd hand) [To Thaisarion] daughter of Xistos(?), from
29   [N..nas her] brother.
