[TRANSLATION]
1     To the masters of earth and sea and of every nation of mankind, the Emperors Caesars [Fl]avius V[alerius Constantinus and Valerius Licinnianus Licinius,]
2     the fortunate, invincible Augusti, and to Flavius V[alerius] Crispus and Val[erius] Constantinus Lic[inn]ius and F[lavius Claudius Constantinus, the most illustrious Caesars,]
3     a petition and supplication
4     from Aurelia Isidora daughter of Pales, from the village of …echonsis of the L…polite nome. (It belongs to) your … [most divine Emperors]
5     and most illustrious Caesars to benefit all … the inhabited world, so that we who are in th[is] world …
6     in your eternal power; and my case is th[u]s: long ago our father purchased … [and the]
7     other taxes on their behalf we paid as, for each single arura, half-third artaba of wheat … we are [compell]ed to furnish for the same aruras two artabas for the one arura; and they did not desist … [for each] arura three artabas, so that from the vi[olence] … I became sold-off-from-my-livelihood, and having need to obtain help …
8     and I made my refuge, through this my petition, to your sacred [tribunals] of you our benefactors …
9     for the pious contributions and for the sea-taxes on behalf of my property, to be urged on …
10    (that the assessment) follow the former rule (kanon), and that I, the old and wretched woman, be not held down for those very things … [of Pale]s, which I also help to pay. Prosper, most divine Emperors and most illustrious [Caesars] …
11    the world. I, Aurelia Isidora, dispatched (this) … son of Sarapion, wrote on her behalf, as she does not know letters.
