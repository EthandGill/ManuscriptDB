[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollophanes [the] and Sarapammon, strategos of the Arsinoite, of Heracleides
2     [district]
3     From Herieus Tatat from [v]illage of Soknopaiou Nesos. After I [completed] the requisite works until the sowing of grain
4     of fields being about [the] same village
5     in the place called Toa in the present
6     year Amounis Kiale and [Ho]ros Tathmios and
7     Panephremis called Moros
8     and Petronius, the four, and those with them
9     from the same village, having come against the
10    sown by me fields, as aforementioned
11    from above [they] split [them], so that from this no small damage to me resulted. Therefore
12    According to necessity I present this very [thing], [making it] clear, in order for this little book to be in [the] record, for [the] claim [to] remain to me
13    against them [regarding this]. [Farewell]
14    Year 17 of Lucius Septimius Severus Pious Pertinax and Marcus
15    Aurelius Antoninus Pious Augustuses and Publius Septimius Geta Caesar Augustus, Tybi 28.
