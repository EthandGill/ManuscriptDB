[TRANSLATION]
1     Chairemon to [Apollonios his] dearest friend, greeting.
2     You do nothing foreign … to your kindliness toward me, dearest, … not having forgotten the
3     … toward you, brother, having been further urged on through … in the little works, since, as you see, they are warm
4     and … of the twenty thousand reeds,
5     together with which … and I wrote to you also before, what
6     securities I have after the harvest and as a gift;
7     for through your forethought it is going to turn out rightly, and
8     concerning the rush hurry also and inform me, and concerning
9     Plousios and the others, how he is disposed; and the gods willing I shall by all means greet you after the Soucheia. And I swear to you by the Dioskouroi, with whom he wishes to share
10    and himself … to desire to enjoy your character; nevertheless he will find it perfect, fortune permitting, concerning the common (matter). You write me foolishly that I wish, while you
11    are present, that what is being done should be done, which (you do) not perceiving in your own manner the disposition I have toward you; therefore, brother, as
12    you may approve, let it be done, not so that for the sake of the least
13    the rest should be lost, and let the work of the vines of my own
14    be done genuinely, in proportion, for Apollonios. And you will
15    do well, most honored one, having sent me through Sabinus
16    twenty sealed drachmas, since I have urgent need of them for expense; and inform me
17    for how much the hay is being sold for cutting, that I may know.
18    And if it be no burden to you, lend me a little donkey for a three-chous (load)
19    of wine and the return-freight, that we may have it for the
20    Soucheia, so that in this too through you we may seem to make merry; and I think that in this too you will not grieve me;
21    and by all means on the ninth let it be brought up, so that I may also restore the perfect (one) from the old (stock);
22    and if this year's (wine) also be fragrant, from this year's (stock)
23    and the … and brains
24    concerning the … to me as of my own children … of the wine-jars let it be conveyed
25    and … your Horeion and
26    his wife … the private (things); and if you are able … to do.
27    [I pray] that you be in good health.
28    Lend Sabinus a vessel, into which he is to convey oil to me, and entrust the almonds sealed.
29    To [Apollon]ios … the dearest.
