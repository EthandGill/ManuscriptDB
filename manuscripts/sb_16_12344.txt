[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 11, Phamenoth 29. Ptolemy agrees [that it] has been registered for
2     in the city record-office for the basileus Augchin
3     Marreus, [his] due of additional payment of a third part of a house and rooms which are in
4     Auerei, which [he] pledged to her
5     Achomneuis Petenonterios
6     100 drachmas [were] valued additionally at 60 drachmas; [they] become 160 drachmas, minus 8 chalkoi of exchange [at] half-obol.
