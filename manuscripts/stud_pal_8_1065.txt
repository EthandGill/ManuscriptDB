1     From the count
2     To Kalotyphos, provisioner: Give to Psyros, nurse, for the fruits of the tenth indiction
3     Grain, artabae ten [and] five, at Athens, there is grain [artabae] fifteen, only; Mesore fourteenth, tenth indiction
4     I have noted grain, artabae ten [and] five
5     Order to Psyros, nurse, grain, artabae fifteen, only, tenth indiction
