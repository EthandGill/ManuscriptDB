[TRANSLATION]
1     In the (year) after the consulship of Flavius Theodorichus the most illustrious, Choiak 8, of the 9th indiction.
2     To Aurelia Eucharistia daughter of Hermogenes, the most noble, from the
3     (city) of the Hermopolitans, from [Aurelius] Banos son of John, his mother being Theodora,
4     a farmer from the village of Thynis of the Hermopolite nome.
5     I acknowledge that I have voluntarily and of my own free will leased from you,
6     for only one year, of the crops of the fortunate
7     tenth indiction, the third share belonging to you of two arourai
8     situated in the Half-way (district), in the plain of the same
9     village; the neighbors of these two arourai (being): on the south the public
10    road, on the north the plots of Piarios son of Bekis, on the east the plots of John
11    …, on the west the plots of the heirs of the physician — for the sowing of crops
12    of whichever I may choose, at a rent for the same third share, per year,
13    of twelve and a quarter carats of gold, which gold
14    rent I shall pay you in the month Phaophi each year
15    without delay; the lease is valid and secure, and on being questioned I assented.
16    I, Aurelius Banos son of John, … to the lease as aforesaid. Aurelius Phoibammon
17    … (son) of … genes, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate. Aurelius
18    John son of Palladios, from Hermopolis, witness the lease, having heard (it) from the contracting party. Aurelius Victor son of Kollouthos, from Hermopolis, witness the
19    lease, having heard (it) from the contracting party.
20    Through me, Phoibammon, it was written.
21    Lease of Banos son of John, farmer, from Thynis of the Hermopolite.
