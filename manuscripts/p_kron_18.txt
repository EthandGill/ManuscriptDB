[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Dios I have signed. Year 6 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Tybi 18
2     To Flavius Domitius and Sabinus also
3     to Thracida, having served as gymnasiarch, record-keeper of [acquisitions]
4     from Tephorsaitis of Kronion of
5     Cheos, mother of Thenapyches from the village Teptyneis with authority of the brother
6     [Arphaesis], from which I registered through the
7     [former] matters and with the [deadline], regarding the village [Teptyneis] a residential plot of four arourae and the village surcharge of a [quarter] aroura, I wish voluntarily and
8     [voluntarily] to exchange a residential plot
9     of two arourae [from] the southern part
10    to Didyme son of Heron of Amais for which I owe
11    to her together with my sister Taorsenouphis
12    as security of silver principal
13    three hundred seventy two drachmas
14    and the [interest] according to which, [she is] not to conduct any dealings with me managing anything at all, neither on alienation nor on misappropriation, until I repay what is owed and deliver the [proper] discharge
15    receipt. Therefore I submit the exchange.
