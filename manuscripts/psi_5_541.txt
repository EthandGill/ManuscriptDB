[TRANSLATION]
1     To [king] [great] Ptolemy
2     Greetings, Egypt. Through you [saved]
3     And now I beseech you, if it seems good to you,
4     Order to assign me where to you
5     it appears, in order that being well-regarded and
6     blameless to you being the life I have,
7     you of all the inhabited-world
8     ruling.
9     Fare well.
