[TRANSLATION]
1     Iason to Zenon, greeting. Know that Petobastis, the one in Syron Kome, is not giving
2     the cattle fodder nor the slaves their grain-ration. There came therefore
3     to me Asklepiades and Apollonides
4     and the rest of the shepherds, saying that there is owed to them wages of 6 months and that they cannot
5     [hold] out receiving nothing. So having encouraged them and
6     [given] to each a little, I sent (them) away until
7     [I write] to you about these things. For neither is it
8     at [my] disposal whence I might give, for neither do we receive the
9     [rent] of the bath nor that of the
10    draught-animals. For Glaukias says it does not yield (a profit) for him. And the cheese which you write to us
11    to receive at 10 drachmas the talent —
12    the talent does not come out (at that), but rather at 6 drachmas. And you will do well
13    also to write to Theudoros the moneylender about the
14    bare(-wool) and half-bare and the sweat-cloths.
15    For we deposited them as security in
16    Choiach, for 80 drachmas of silver, the interest of the 4 drachmas being half an obol plus a quarter of an obol.
17    For he has brought them out repeatedly to the
18    market as if about to sell them, rather than recover even the
19    interest itself. He has therefore solemnly protested to me before several persons that he will recover (it). So I have asked him to hold off until the 20th of Pachon.
20    Take thought therefore that it (the security) be sold.
21    Farewell. Year 38, Pachon 4.
22    To Zenon.
