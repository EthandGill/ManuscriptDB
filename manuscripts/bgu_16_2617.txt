1     [Tryphas] to Athenodoros the son
2     [most] greetings and throughout all to prosper
3     [as I pray]; I received what you sent
4     [and I] secured them through [myself] [forty] kormos and one and thirteen ceramic vessels
5     [greet] the ladies of me daughters Tryphas
6     and Artemis; above all take care
7     of [yourself]
8     [so that] [you are well], which is most desired to me before
9     all [the] gods.
