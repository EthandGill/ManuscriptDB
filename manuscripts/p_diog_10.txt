[GREEK]
r.1    Seueri et Marci Aureli Antonini: et Publii Septimi Getae Augustorum, [men]se Phamenoth die vii ἀντίγραφον ὑπογραφῆς· Λούκιος Ἰγνάτιος Ῥου[φῖνος]
r.2    διεθέμην ὡς πρόκειται apertum et recitatum Augusto foro Arsinoitu metropoleos iii Non[as Iunias,]

[TRANSLATION]
1     Severus and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus and Publius Septimius Geta [the] Emperors, [in the] month of Phamenoth day seven, copy of a countersignature: Lucius Ignatius Ruf[inus]
2     [I] distributed as aforesaid; [it was] opened and recited in the public forum of the metropolis of Arsinoe, third Nones of June.
