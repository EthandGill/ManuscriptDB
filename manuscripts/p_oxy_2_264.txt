[TRANSLATION]
1   Ammonios son of Ammonios, to Tryphon son of Dionysios,
2   greeting. I acknowledge that I have sold to you the loom
3   belonging to me, a weaver's loom of three weaver's cubits
4   less two palms, of which (there are) two beams,
5   two loom-feet, [one] heddle-rod, [and that I have] received from yo(u),
6   through the bank of Sarapion son of Lochos at the Sarapeion by the
7   city of Ox[yrhynchus], the
8   price of this, fixed between us, of silver of Augustan and
9   Ptolemaic coinage,
10   twenty drachmas, an[d that] I will guarantee to you the sale with every
11   guaran[tee], or else pay you back the price which I received from you
12   with half as much again, and the damage. The bond is valid.
13   Year 14 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus
14   Germanicus Imperator, mo(nth) Kaisareios 15.
15   (2nd hand) I, Ammonios son of Ammonios, have sold the loom
16   and have received the price, the twenty silver drachma(s),
17   twenty, and I will guarantee as stated above. I, Hera-
18   kleides son of D[ion]ysios, wrote on his behalf, as he does not
19   know letters. Year 14 of Tiberius Claudius
20   Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
21   mo(nth) Kaisareios 15, the Sebaste (day).
22   (3rd hand) Year fourteenth
23   of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
24   Augustus Germanicus
25   Imperator, mo(nth) Kaisareios 15,
26   the Sebaste (day): through the bank of Sar(apion) the payment (diagraphe) was made.
