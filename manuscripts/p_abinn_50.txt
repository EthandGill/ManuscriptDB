[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Ameinaias, praepositus of the camp of Dionysias,
2     from Aurelius Anteus … from the village of Narmouthis.
3     The seizures and acts of greed occurring in these places
4     by the more powerful men your courage is accustomed
5     to check, lord praepositus. Since, lord, I possess
6     six unsown arouras and am not well supplied with grain for their dues, I cultivated arouras in the village of Magais
7     [with] Aion son of Abos from the same village …
8     [the] dues for the arouras which I possess, as I said before … the same Aion, after I had winnowed the threshing-floors,
9     [seiz]ed [my] grain and carried it off to his own (store), and seized our half-share. And not only this, but [I] cultivated other arouras from another
10    name [from] the same village, and I winnowed again
11    … the wondrous Aion seized my grain … lying … in tyrannical fashion … Abos … having the fear
12    of God … nor of you, my lord, but presuming
13    upon my affairs and upon my own ill-fortune; wherefore
14    … with you … o
15    … the … so that, if it seem good to you, you may …
16    … that the aforesaid Aion be brought before you
17    and that you may compel him to give back to me my share
18    and … [and at the s]ame time I may be able to be well supplied
19    from another … [gr]ain for the arouras which I possess.
20    In the consulship of our lords Constantius for the 4th time
21    and Constans for the 3rd time, the Augusti, Mesore 6. …
22    … (illegible) …
