[TRANSLATION]
1     … are …
2     … having spent no small time with my father, of holy memory, the greatest …
3     … he brought to him the very … that man
4     most kindly disposed … remaining …
5     to my master, my father of illustrious memory. And no claim
6     did I ever find, either from my father, nor from
7     another, that as slaves …
8     they had been established by any lawful title whatsoever. From those, then,
9     aforementioned Iakob and Sophia, there were born as children Lia and Rachel
10    and Erebekka. From Lia is born a child Markos and Sophia; but Lia, at the end
11    of her life … died together with her son Markos; and
12    Sophia, the surviving daughter of that departed Lia, from whom is born
13    Ioannes and Isak and Iakob and others — should anyone, dragging her together with her children
14    into slavery, they will face an answer standing before the tribunal of the Almighty.
15    And so much concerning this. Since, then, of those aforementioned
16    Iakob and Sophia, as has been shown above, were born Rachel and Erebekka
17    and Lia, and Rachel chose the solitary life, while Lia bore Sophia, and
18    Erebekka (bore) Eulogia and Martha; but Eulogia obtained for herself the ascetic
19    life, she too, and she remains living in my house. And so that not in some time
20    by someone, as is likely, of those succeeding to my property, she might outlive into old age
21    my most learned heir, and for a very long time of these
22    the divine power will guard him enjoying his household goods, so that, then, she may not be troubled
23    as being slaves; for this reason I have come to the present written
24    agreement, through which I both consider her and accurately know that she is not at all
25    a slave, nor was she born of a slave-woman, in the manner I have narrated above;
26    and if, as is likely, someone of those afterward should attempt to trouble her, from this
27    written agreement, to which I have also caused to consent this most learned man,
28    with God my heir, through which I urge her from now
29    already to conduct the affairs of free persons, and to do what is needful. For I have heard
30    her at the present time speaking and being questioned
31    about a certain capital sum belonging to her, as: "I am not established as free." And
32    for this reason, fearing the judgment of God, and considering the loving-kindness
33    of the Saviour, having groaned, I have come to the present
34    written agreement, through which written agreement I have urged
35    her to know that she is free. And if, as is likely,
36    someone should attempt to call her a slave, let her become,
37    through the present written agreement, free for the
38    future, troubled by no one nor by the most eloquent
39    and most beloved [my heir, who] also consented according to my command
40    and direction to this written agreement. For the future, then,
41    let no one regard with suspicion nor consider that anyone can call her
42    a slave, but let her be, as has been shown above, free, no one
43    being able to harm the freedom given to her from above
