[TRANSLATION]
1     To Deios and Sabinus, who has served as gymnasiarch, and partners, public bankers.
2     We the undersigned cattle-breeders of the village of Mouchis, through
3     Didymos the appointed representative, have received from you the (sums) sent to us by Ptolemaios
4     basilikos grammateus of the Polemon division, deputizing also for the affairs of the
5     strategia of the Themistos and Polemon divisions, for freight-charges
6     of the wheat which we brought down from the granaries of Polemon to the harbors,
7     for the carriage of the produce of the 17th year, 52 artabas of wheat, 3, 12, at, for each artaba,
8     8 drachmas, the (sums) amounting to 419 silver drachmas, 2 obols. And they are:
9     Panesneus son of Pallas, 7 ½ artabas of wheat, 8.
10    Papontôs and Heras, 4 ½ artabas of wheat, 4.
11    Tephorsais and Pachnoubis, 3 artabas of wheat, 6.
12    Pachnoubis son of Pachnoubis, 3 ⅔ artabas of wheat, 8.
13    Leon son of Leon, 3 artabas of wheat.
14    …ionnis son of Nephesnous, 3 ⅔ artabas of wheat, 8.
15    Pansneus son of Pallas, 5 ⅔ artabas of wheat, 8.
16    Pachnoubis son of Orsenouphis, 5 ½ artabas of wheat.
17    Orsenouphis son of Orsenouphis, 4 ½ artabas of wheat.
18    Heron son of Heron, 10 ½ artabas of wheat. Total the aforesaid.
19    I, Didymos the secretary, have received the aforesaid 419 drachmas, 2 obols.
20    Year 18 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
21    Mecheir 30.
