[TRANSLATION]
1     To Demetrios, strategos of the [Oxyryg]chite,
2     from Tasionys also called Dionysia,
3     daughter of Dionysios, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites, an[d]
4     from Ammonios son of Paseis, from the Small
5     Oasis, and (for) Tas[io]nys with (her) guardian
6     Demetrios son of Theon son of Theon, from
7     the city of the Oxyrhynchites. There belongs to each of us in the Metroon quarter
8     a house adjoining that of [eac]h (other); and there has
9     in our neighbourhood Philon son of Dionysios
10    and Apia daughter of Pheremphis, together with the brothers Dionytas and Tapontos and Arsous,
11    children of Sarapion, and Demetrous daughter of Dionysios and Sarapion son of S[a]rapion, a house,
12    which through its long-standing age
13    is in danger of collapsing; wherefore,
14    fearing lest some danger
15    befall us and our (people),
16    especially as the same house leans
17    into the cou[r]tyard of each (of us), we request
18    that a copy of this
19    memorandum be served on them through an attendant, so that, having a written notice, they may secure their own house or know that they will be
20    liable for all
21    the danger that may ensue and
22    for the consequent injuries.
23    Year 5 of Imperator Caesar Traja[nus]
24    Hadrianus Augustus, Mecheir 1.
25    I, Dionysios also called Saras, attendant, served (it) on Apia daughter of Pheremphis
26    and on her brother Dionyt[a]s through her, and on Philon son of Dionysios
27    and on Tapontos and Arsous and
28    Demetrous and Sarapion,
29    in person. In the fifth year
30    of Imperator Caesar Trajanus
31    Hadrianus Augustus, Mecheir
32    on the new moon (first day).
