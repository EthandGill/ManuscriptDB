1     Marcus Aurelius Asclepiades Herminus
2     of the century of Alexander, to Petronius the quartermaster
3     greetings. I received from you wine, one marked vessel
4     one, of denarii two, drachmas two. Marc[us]
5     Aurelius Horion Sarapion, arms-custodian, I wrote on his behalf, not knowing
6     letters, Year 20, Mesore 12
