[TRANSLATION]
1     To Eurylochos, kinsman and strategos and
2     superintendent of the revenues,
3     from Tasemthis, wife of Horos, brother
4     of Herieus the wing-bearing priest of the temple of Herakles, the greatest god, in Tilothis.
5     My parents,
6     having come from Poenamis to the temple in Tilothis and to the
7     pastophorion of the aforesaid (priests), my (husband) Horos became entangled with me
8     out of long association. My father, having deposited the property in his (estate) and a dowry
9     for me through his brothers, both Herieus and Semtheus
10    (sons of) Paabis, contributing 25 talents of copper coinage, he wrote
11    me down as an Egyptian nursling, by whom I have also borne
12    four children, of whom one survives.
13    The brothers brought up to him a division of the dwellings.
14    My husband departed this life
15    while so disposed. Up to now they have compelled
16    me to make the expenses for the funerals upon him,
17    while carrying off all the (property) of the child;
18    they provide nothing for the necessities. Therefore I ask,
19    being a widow, that justice be granted to me and to the orphan
20    little child, (the husband) not having abided by the terms on which
21    the priests and Apion the former gymnasiarch passed judgement,
22    if it seem (good) to take thought for us, that we may be helped.
23    Farewell.
