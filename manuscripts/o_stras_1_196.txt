r.3    ἔσχον ὑπὲρ λαογραφίας Ἀγορᾶς βορρᾶ κ ἔτους ῥυπαρὰς δραχμὰς ὀκτώ γίνονται δραχμαὶ η.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Imouthes, collector of money taxes
2     Psenchoonseis, Petemneophios, of mother Tapouherios.
3     I have received for poll tax of the northern agora twentieth year soiled drachmas eight; total drachmas 8.
4     Year 20 of Trajan the lord Mecheir 1. …
5     other Phamenoth 19 soiled drachmas four
6     total drachmas 4. I have marked.
