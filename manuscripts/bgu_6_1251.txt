[TRANSLATION]
1     To Theodore, overseer of Philadelphia, from Nicagoras
2     of Bithys, a hundred-aroura holder, on the 16th
3     of [Pha]rmouthi of the 26th year
4     … Petosough[us] [and] … mophn … os of the[...]
5     of Pantaleon, having observed the one beside me
6     a guard having been withdrawn
7     into a more distant field, having released their own
8     cattle, bovine, together with other livestock of which I do not know the owners, into the land which I have leased
9     from Simias of Ptolem[ais], divided and trampled down five transport-loads of fodder
10    which [amount] to three copper drachmai
