[TRANSLATION]
1     To Eudoros, strategos of the merides of Themistos and Polemon of the Arsinoite,
2     from Onnophris son of Orsenouphis and Panesneus
3     son of Orseus and Paches son of Chnoubis, the three of them, and the rest of the
4     elders also administering the affairs of the village-scribeship of the village of Mouchis:
5     a list of elders and archephodoi and other public functionaries
6     for the coming 22nd year of Antoninus Caesar the lord.
7     They are as follows:
8     Elders:
9     Sansneus son of Aboblis, the son of Bellis, mother Tapeteeus,
10    from the village, having property worth 500 drachmas;
11    Chrysas, elder, son of Haryotes, mother Tapeteeus, having property worth 400 drachmas;
12    Bysbeitis son of Orsenouphis, the son of Chrysas, cook, [having] property worth 400 drachmas;
13    Ph… son of Phasis, the son of Phas…, called the weaver, [having property] worth 400 drachmas;
14    … son of Paches, the son of Paches, … [having] property worth 500 drachmas;
15    … son of Chnoubis, [having] property worth 400 drachmas;
16    … worth 400 drachmas;
17    … mother …
18    … [having] property [worth … drachmas] …
19    … son of Chnoubis, the son of Bel[lis], mother Taar, having [property worth] 400 drachmas;
20    … Chnoubis son of Paches, the son of Papontos, mother Tasoucharion, having property worth 400 drachmas.
21    Archephodoi:
22    Panesneus son of Paches, the son of Maron, mother Tamystha.
23    Sambas son of Chrysas, the son of Sambas, mother Aphrodous.
24    Guards:
25    Orsenouphis son of Pachnoubis, the son of Orsenouphis, mother Sambous.
26    Thrakidas son of Kameis, the son of Paches, mother Thenamounis.
