[TRANSLATION]
1     There was paid out, of wheat of the produce of the past 8th year
2     of the Aurelii Antoninus
3     and Verus, the lords Augusti,
4     from the amounts credited on account of the toparchy, (to) Philotera
5     daughter of Stephanos, from an advance, at Pela, being reckoned together,
6     one and a half and a quarter artaba, four choinikes,
7     total 1 1/2 1/4 artaba, 4 choinikes.
8     There was paid out, of wheat of the produce of the past 8th year
9     of the Aurelii Antoninus and Verus,
10    the lords Augusti, from an advance …
11    credited to the grain-collectors of Senao, (to) Philotera daughter of Stephanos, 4 choinikes, total 4 choinikes.
12    There was paid out, of wheat of the produce of the past 13th year of Aurelius
13    Antoninus Caesar the lord,
14    through the grain-collectors of the western toparchy at Pela, from the deposit of Diog… and … ,
15    (to) Philotera daughter of Stephanos, at Pela, 3 artabas, total 3 artabas.
16    I, Apion, have signed.
17    There was paid out, of wheat of the produce of the past 14th year of Aurelius
18    Antoninus, through the grain-collectors of the middle toparchy of Nemera, from the deposit of Herais daughter of Ischyrion,
19    (to) Philotera daughter of Stephanos, at Pela, three artabas, total 3 artabas.
20    I, Hermias, have signed.
21    And from an advance credited to the grain-collectors at Pela, the same woman, at Pela, 4 artabas 9 choinikes.
22    Of the 13th year likewise 4 artabas 9 choinikes, total 4 1/2 artabas 7 choinikes. I, Apion, have signed.
23    There was paid out, of wheat of the produce of the 9th year of Marcus Aurelius
24    Antoninus Augustus, through the grain-collectors of the districts of Pela,
25    from the deposit of Petosiris son of Petosiris, (to) Philotera
26    daughter of Stephanos and Aphrodite the mother, at Pela,
27    1 1/2 1/4 artaba 2 choinikes, total 1 1/2 1/4 artaba 2 choinikes. I, Zoilos, assistant, have signed.
28    There was paid out, of wheat of the produce of the tenth year of Aurelius
29    Antoninus Caesar the lord,
30    through the grain-collectors of the eastern toparchy of Pakerke, from the deposit of Diog… her mother being
31    Thaesis, (to) Philotera daughter of Stephanos and
32    Aphrodite the mother, at Pela, one and a half and a quarter artaba, 2 choinikes,
33    total 1 1/2 1/4 artaba 2 choinikes. I, Chairemon, have signed.
34    There was paid out, of wheat of the produce of the past 11th year of Aurelius
35    Antoninus Caesar the lord, through the grain-collectors
36    of the upper toparchy of the districts of Enteios, from the deposit of Didymos, (to) Philotera
37    daughter of Stephanos and Aphrodite the mother, at Pela, 1 1/2 1/4 artaba 2 choinikes,
38    total, from the deposit, 1 1/2 1/4 artaba 2 choinikes.
39    There was paid out, of wheat of the produce of the past 12th year
40    of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar
41    the lord, through the grain-collectors of the upper toparchy of the districts of Enteios, (to) Philotera daughter of Stephanos
42    and Aphrodite the mother, at Pela, one
43    and a half and a quarter artaba, two choinikes,
44    total 1 1/2 1/4 artaba 2 choinikes, from the deposit of Ammonios
