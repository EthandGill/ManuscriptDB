1     … many …
2     … [Diosko]urides [my] brother …
3     … those who received these things
4     [and] having promised to provide (them), with much entreaty, they agreed to take 40 drachmas and to present … of Dioskourides a papyrus on which were inscribed both the Latin texts and their translation. And again
5     coming to them they said it was better
6     that the … had been deposited …
7     … and to them, when they had considered … …
8     … he reckoned rather to help provide …
9     … to give the 40 drachmas and to send them off;
10    and yet do you suppose he received nothing
11    [or] that he was negligent? These then are the things that were done.
12    He promised, when he went out to the
13    matters entrusted to him by the council, on his being here to communicate them
14    to Didymos and to learn from him the things after these and what was done concerning the release;
15    and Kriton son of Epimachos, having brought me these
16    letters of the one who requested, the money having been sent off by you. Learning this, my brother
17    [Dios]kourides caused him to be restored,
18    and indeed it is just … to take the lead
19    … having registered and … which also he [will send to] you. And I, if from the assistants of Didymos
20    the guards of him, I find out anything further,
21    [I will write] to you. I greet by name your unharmed
22    children, with whom I pray that you be in health in the lord
23    God.
24    … to …onarion, wife of Aurelius Apollonios …
25    from Aurelius Artemidoros.
