[TRANSLATION]
1     To Euphranor: to see to it
2     that he shall obtain his rights.
3     Year 33, Payni 13.
4     To Boethos, kinsman and epistrategos,
5     from E—, son of Demetrios, a Macedonian, of the soldiers stationed in the fort at Hiera Nesos.
6     Having therefore been wronged by
7     Ptolemaios, son of Dioskourides, formerly one of the
8     mounted scouts, but now one of the katoikoi cavalry: in the 32nd year,
9     while he was in charge of the garrison-command of the aforementioned
10    fort, having been appointed by
11    Polemaios the former strategos, the accused Ptolemaios, carried away by his insolence,
12    handed me over to Telephos, one of
13    my fellow-soldiers, and
14    shut me up; and this same Ptolemaios and a certain Zoilos in his service,
15    who is one of the mounted scouts,
16    and Hyrkanos, one of the sheep-dealers from Alilas, having passed over into Tertonpetemoun to the courtyard belonging
17    to me, drove off my 8 fleece-bearing sheep, which they made off with, worth 3 talents of bronze.
18    Wherefore I request, if it seems good, that you give order
19    to write to whom it concerns to send Ptolemaios up before you, and, if
20    matters are as I write, to compel him
21    to give me back the sheep
22    or their value. And this being done, I shall obtain the help
23    from you.
24    Farewell.
25    To Hipponikos: to bring (him) before (me).
26    Year 33, Payni 14.
27    To Euphranor.
