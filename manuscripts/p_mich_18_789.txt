1     [Kal]purnia and [Apia also called Theodora], both
2     [daugh]ters of Gai… …
3     … of [m]other Theodora, from the city of [Oxyrhyn]chos,
4     [each] with as guardian her husband — Kalpurnia with Apion son of Apollonios son of Sarapion, of mother Saraeus, by adoption (son) of Diogenes son of Sarapion, from the same city, and Apia also
5     called Theodora with Chosion son of …on also called Chosion, of mother Thaisous, from the same city — to our maternal uncle Dioskourides son of Zoilos son of Dioskourides, of the Hadrianic

9     Sarapeion in the same city, greeting. Since by the (will) which made
10    your father, and our grandfather on the maternal side,
11    Zoilos son of Dioskourides son of Hermes, of mother Theodora,

13    record-office in the same city, in the 27th year, in (the month) Tybi,
