[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Apollonios the most honored, greeting.
2     [All the weapons] for the sake of which Hermias came, having bought them,
3     [I have sent to you] through him, glad that according to
4     [your prayers] they were found genuine and very worthy, so that they were also
5     admired [by all] who saw them.
6     [For] the breastplate, being of fine orichalc and
7     [in its weave] very fine and, what is more important, very light, so that the wearer does not tire of it,
8     [was bought], many noteworthy [friends] of mine having come together
9     [to me], for 360 silver drachmas instead of a higher (price), as will also be apparent to you.
10    And the so-called Italian [sa]mseira (sword)
11    … [of … ] like[wise instead of a high]er (price), 80 drachmas, and the
12    half-pound of purple instead of 264 [drachmas, for] 252 [drachmas],
13    [a]nd the two matia of spices, 80 drachmas. For a belt genuine for the present was not found, but neither did I think it right to buy one liable to be rejected. The bronze little ass (statuette), if it had been for sale
14    for 24 drachmas, I would have sent it to you then; but if you wish
15    it to be bought for 40 drachmas, as much as the craftsman, when asked, promises to give, let me know.
16    The 24 drachmas, however, which you had given for this, I have sent [to you,]
17    although Dionysios the silversmith detained
18    a whole 40 drachmas of mine as a pledge for the wooden case with figures (?) sent to you; which, if it seems good to you, my lord, you will send back to me, so that having paid I may be able to recover my own; for easily one like it can be made at your place.
19    Concerning whatever else you wish,
20    write to me, who will gladly fulfill (it). The unwrought silver [now is]
21    [362 drachmas]; for, as you know, in Koptos every day the prices differ.
22    I pray for your health, my lord. Pauni 25.
