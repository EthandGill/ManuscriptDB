[TRANSLATION]
1     Silvanus to Silvanus the
2     brother, greetings. Know, brother, [that we have arrived]
3     us to Dry Sea
4     and (we) found Crispos the centurion
5     and Kelas was still saying
6     that we drag the Louther.
7     Therefore then, brother,
8     [a record] you will write of the
9     workers until I come.
10    Be well. Choiak 29.
