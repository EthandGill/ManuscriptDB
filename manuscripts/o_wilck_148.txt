[TRANSLATION]
1   [Arrio]s Puplianus and those with him,
2   overseers of the Sacred Gate of Syene.
3   Paid: Atpes son of Papremmei-
4   thos, mother Senbampoueris, for the poll-tax
5   of the thirteenth year of Hadrian
6   Caesar the lord: seventeen
7   drachmas (and) a half-obol, (and) ½ obol (keepers' fee) — total 17 dr. 1 ob.,
8   Epeiph 5.
9   (2nd hand) I, Arrius Puplianus, have signed off: seventeen
10   drachmas (and) an obol.
