[TRANSLATION]
1     … [to the] dearest
2     greetings.
3     I want you to know that
4     I went from Paseutha
5     to the brother of Horion
6     on account of the tau[r]ine, and
7     [he] agreed to give to him, and
8     the workers in readiness
9     I prepared. I went also to Potamon on account of the
10    other taurine, and [he] said
11    saying: the master of the taurine went up into the city
12    and the yoke-driver cannot bear to go without him.
13    to another taurine but I conferred, and [he] did not agree
14    to work in agreement, but
15    per day, as many days as [he] spends, requesting [twelve] drachmas each day. Fare well.
