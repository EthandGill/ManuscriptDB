[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Pkoui-shere the son of Pshe…ne, and Bane
2     the son of Phoibammon, and Ion the son of Taurine, and Petre
3     the son of Phlo, and Mone the son of Paule, and Loulou the son of Apollo, and Pamon the son of Phoibammon, and Psha
4     the son of Herakleides, and George the son of Phoibammon,
5     and Phib the son of Herakleides, and Ioannes the son of Taurine,
6     and Hor the son of Mena, and Enoch the son of Biktor,
7     and Shinoute the son of Anoup, the men of Pousire in the district
8     of Shmoun, the city — we write to our lord, the master
9     the official Kosroi, that we are in debt, and your lordship,
10    we have received, we have been paid in full from him, twenty-three holokottinoi (solidi)
11    by the weight of gold,
12    of full weight, from your hand, for the dung-cakes that we sold to you
13    out, according to twenty-five and five bundles, for the holokottinos
14    of full weight … being twenty-five litrai for the bundle,
15    they make loads 1,980 from 25 litrai; these then we are ready
16    to give to your lordship by the will of God
17    by the 23rd of Hathor, the month of this year,
18    this being of the fourteenth indiction, we swearing
19    by God and by the safety of the king of kings
20    that we cannot transgress against you according to the force of this security.
21    We confirm to you then; we have drawn up this security for you,
22    we have set the cross upon it with our hand. Written, Hathyr 12,
23    indiction 14. Sign of Pkoui-shere the … Sign of Ion … Sign of Bane
24    Sign of Petre, Phlo. Sign of Mone, Paule. Sign of Loulou, Apollo. Sign of Pamon, Phoibammon
25    Sign of Psha, Herakleides. Sign of George, Phoibammon. Sign of Phib, Herakleides. Sign of Ioannes, Taurine
26    Sign of Hor, Mena. Sign of Enoch, Biktor. Sign of Senouthios, Anoup
27    I, Apollo the teacher of the village — they summoned me, I wrote on their behalf,
28    for they do not know how to write.
