[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 8 Phaōphi 30. [He/she] acknowledges
2     Herakleides ship-owner
3     of Agathokleia route 2
4     to have been put [aboard] from Ara... chētou Pailōiou steward
5     so as into Alexandria
6     into the royal [treasury] from the
7     products of the 8th year
8     from treasury ... [to] be carried
9     of clean sifted wheat artabae thousand two hundred fifty [units]
10    and [there is] nothing to complain about.
