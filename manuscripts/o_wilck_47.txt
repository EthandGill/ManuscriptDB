1   Pateoris son of Pateoris, linen-seller, paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the first year of Nerva
3   the lord: seventeen silver drachmas
4   total 17 dr. I, Hermogenes son of Ammonas, wrote (this).
5   Year 2 of Nerva Caesar.
