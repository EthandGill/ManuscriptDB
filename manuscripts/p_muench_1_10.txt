[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Mauricius
2     Tiberius the eternal Augustus, Imperator and greatest
3     benefactor, in the fourth year, and in the consulship of the same our master
4     in the second year, Mecheir 3, of the fourth indiction, in Syene.
5     Aurelia Tapia, daughter of Tsios, her mother being Mariam, a widow,
6     originating from Syene, to Flavius Patermouthios son of
7     Menas, soldier of the unit of Elephantine, a sailor by occupation, greeting.
8     I brought entreaties to you that you should sign in your name with Eusebios son of Parouchios for two and a third gold solidi on my behalf, and, yielding to my request, this
9     you did. In accordance with this I acknowledge that I assign and have assigned to you the third part of mine from the deeds which are owed to us in the lower regions by the Machbar,
10    so that you may have and hold the third part belonging to me
11    from the same securities, and give and receive on my behalf,
12    and for your security I have made this present security,
13    which shall be valid and secure and unbreakable and unshakable,
14    produced everywhere, with the subscription of the one who subscribes on my behalf,
15    and, having been formally questioned face to face,
16    I acknowledged and released. Aurelia Tapia daughter of
17    Tsios, the aforesaid, I made the deed of security
18    of the two and a third solidi, and all things satisfy me
19    as set forth. Flavius Paaeion son of Jacob,
20    soldier of the unit of Syene, having been asked, I wrote on her
21    behalf, she being illiterate.
22    Flavius John son of Dios, of the actuaries of the unit of Syene, I witness.
23    Flavius Paul son of Paam, soldier of the unit of Syene, I witness.
24    Flavius Kolouthos son of Victor, soldier of the unit of Syene, I witness.
25    Through me, Marcus son of Apa Dios, it was written.
26    Security made by Tapia daughter of Tsios with Patermouthis.
