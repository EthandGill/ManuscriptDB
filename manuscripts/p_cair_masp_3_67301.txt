[TRANSLATION]
1     ⳨ (chmg)
2     1 nomismation less 1 keration, 4 artabas
3     In the consulate of the Flavii Orestes and
4     Lampadios the most glorious,
5     on the twenty-seventh of Mesore, of the ninth indiction.
6     To Apollos, the most esteemed chief-villager
7     of the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome,
8     son of Dioskoros of blessed memory,
9     Aurelius Apollos son of Abraamios, of the mother
10    Therakleia, dwelling in the southern plain of the
11    village of Tanyaithis of the Apollonopolite
12    lesser nome, greeting. I acknowledge, willingly and persuaded,
13    that I have leased from your esteemedness
14    for a six-year period, reckoned from the crops
15    of the (year) that, by God's favor, will be the tenth indiction
16    and (from) it and the following time, the arouras
17    belonging to her, as many as there are, situated on
18    the holding called Phenis in the plain
19    of th[e same] village of Aphrodite, called Psalek,
20    of waterless land, for cultivation and for the sowing
21    of whatever crops I wish, at a fixed
22    rent, on condition that I cultivate these and
23    sow them and perform all the agricultural
24    labor with my own animals
25    and at my own expenses, and provide to your
26    esteemedness, on account of the fixed rent for these,
27    in full and (even) in case of drought, which may not be, each
28    year from the crops of the same tenth indiction and
29    (from) it, for the same six years, of gold one
30    nomismation less one keration, and of clean and rentable arakos (vetch)
31    four artabas, and on account of the completion
32    of the rents of the past years
33    up to the ninth indiction, ten artabas of arakos and
34    two keratia of gold to the goldsmith, makes together of gold 1 nomismation less 1 keration, and 2 keratia
35    and 14 artabas of arakos, without dispute. The lease is valid and secure, and having been formally questioned I acknowledged.
36    I, Aurelius Apollos son of Abraamios, the
37    aforesaid, agree to it as set forth.
38    [Aureli]us Enoch son of Jeremias, having been requested, [wr]ote on his behalf [the documents], he being illiterate.
39    Written through Victor the notary.
40    … of Apollos, owner …
