[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 41st year, Pharmouth 21. [someone] sold
2     Pachnoubis, [son] of Torsnoutos, Persian
3     the land belonging to him within
4     the precinct of Pathyres, of which the measure [is] 16 arouras
5     of which the neighboring [plots] have been shown
6     through the contract which he purchased
7     Thrasoon and Areios and Ptolemaios
8     and Asklepiades [for] bronze [of] four drachmas
