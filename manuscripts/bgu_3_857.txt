[TRANSLATION]
1   Gaius Aruntius Posidonios,
2   to Stotoetis son of Apynchis son [of Ors]enouphis,
3   greeting. I acknowled[g]e that I have from you
4   an interest-bearing loan, of silver principal,
5   three hundred drachmas — total 300 dr. —
6   apart from the others which I owe yo[u]
7   under a security of loans,
8   of silver principal, one thousand four
9   hundred fifty-two (drachmas), three obols,
10   — total 1452 — which I will also repay you
11   in the month Epeiph of the present
12   sixteenth year of Trajan Caesar the
13   lord. The bond, as I wrote (it, is valid).
14   Year 16 of Imperator Caesar
15   Ner[v]a Trajan Augustus Germ[ani]cus
16   Da[ci]cus, Tyb[i] 11.
