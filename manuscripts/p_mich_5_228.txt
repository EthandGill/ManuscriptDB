[TRANSLATION]
1     about 26 years old, a scar on the neck on the left side
2     and on the left forearm.
3     The woman Tanouris daughter of Heronas against Bentetis
4     son of Bentetis.
5     From Thouonis son of Akousilaos, of those from Areos Kome, on the
6     26th of the present month Neos Sebastos of the 8th year of Tiberius.
7     To Apollonios, strategos of the Arsinoite (nome),
8     from Thouonis son of Akousilaos, of those from Areos Kome [of] the Polemon division, on the 26th
9     of the present month Neos Sebastos of the
10    8th year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus
11    Germanicus Imperator. While I was reckoning accounts with Bentetis son of Bentetis, a sheep-and-cattle breeder, of those from Oxyrhyncha of the same
12    division, concerning the wages and grain-measures which he owes me — this man, then, not wishing
13    to repay but also to deceive (me), committed an outrage
14    against me and against my wife Tanouris daughter of Heronas in the said Areos
15    Kome. And further he gave to my wife Tanouris
16    quite unsparingly full blows upon whatever parts of the body chanced (to be near), she being pregnant, so that as a result the
17    infant was aborted from her dead, so that she is bedridden and in danger of her life.
18    Therefore I ask that you write to the elders of Oxyrhyncha to send the accused before you for the future proceeding.
19    Farewell.
20    In the eighth year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
21    Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
22    month Neos Sebastos 26.
23    Thouonis, about 26 years old, a scar on the neck on the left side and on the left forearm.
