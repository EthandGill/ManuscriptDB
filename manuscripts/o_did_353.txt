[TRANSLATION]
1     Herenus to Leibon his brother, very many greetings. I give you much thanks [before] the god. I know how you love me. Do not therefore, brother, be negligent toward me but receive
2     the goat-hair cloak from Longinus and the sixty drachmas and give the goat-hair cloak and
3     dye it red. Give it to Chresimus the [slave] of Dexter the muleteer. Do not therefore be negligent if he gives [it] to you. But if not, write to me and I will speak
4     to the centurion, saying this: receive [him/it] into my house. If I have wronged … I said to him I killed … my table has reviled him … three staters of him I have given as interest.
