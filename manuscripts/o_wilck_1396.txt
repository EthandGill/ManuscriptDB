[TRANSLATION]
1     There have paid, the heirs of Haronnophris (son of) Pechois, for year 13,
2     for naubia (dyke-tax) of year 12, of (the bank of) Charax, one dr. ½-obol,
3     total 1 dr. ½-obol and the surcharges on these. Year 13 of Nero
4     the lord, in the month Tybi 23.
5     Herakleides.
