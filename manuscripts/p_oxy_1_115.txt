[TRANSLATION]
1   Eirene to Taonnophris and Philon,
2   take heart.
3   I was as grieved and wept over
4   the blessed one as I wept
5   for Didymas, and everything that was fit-
6   ting I did, and so did all
7   my people — Epaphrodeitos and Thermou-
8   thion and Philion and Apollonios
9   and Plantas. But still, there is no-
10   thing anyone can do in the face of such things.
11   So comfort one another.
12   Fare well. Hathyr 30.
13   To Taonnophris and Philon.
