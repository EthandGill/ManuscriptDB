[TRANSLATION]
1     Andronichos to Dionysius the
2     [brother], greetings.
3     Having come to me, the brother of the one who took the bronze asked me that
4     Allow me until Dionysius arrives, and whatever he has
5     he will exchange to him, if also to you
6     it be pleasant.
7     I pray you to be well, brother.
