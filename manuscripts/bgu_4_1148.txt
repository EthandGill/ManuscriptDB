[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos.
2   From Apollonia daughter of Dionysios, with her guardian Deme-
3   trios son of Horion, of the Althaieus deme, and from Taphasies daughter of
4   Sarapion, of Rhakotis, with her guardian, her sister's
5   son (the son) of Nitoitis, Hermias son of Psammetichos.
6   Concerning the matters in dispute, Taphasies agrees that she has re-
7   ceived back from Apollonia, by hand from the house, (the sums) which
8   the deceased husband of Taphasies, Dionysios son of Dionysios, had lent both to Apollonia herself and also to her deceased
9   husband Isidoros son of Menelaos -
10   by two agreements
11   through the same court: the one in the 4th year of Caesar, Phao[phi],
12   150 silver drachmas; and by the other, in the
13   seventh year of Caesar, Hathyr, 8[7]2 silver drachmas -
14   of both loans, together with the interest for the
15   elapsed time; because, Dionysios having
16   died, Taphasies entered into possession (of the security)
17   of his (estate), in accordance with the [laws] of the land, since she ho-
18   lds the Egyptian deed of Dionysios; and that
19   the two agreements are forthwith void; and that
20   Taphasies shall not proceed, nor anyone on her behalf,
21   against Apollonia, nor against the (property) left by Isidoros,
22   neither concerning Apollonia(?) nor concerning any other
23   debt or claim at all, written or unwritten, from for-
24   mer times up to the present day; and whoever does proceed against her
25   on account
26   of these (matters), in the name of Dionysios, she (Taphasies) shall, from the same,
27   remove (the claimant) at once at her own expense; and if she transgresses any of these,
28   apart from
29   the agreed terms remaining valid, she shall also be liable [to the damages and expenses] and
30   to the fixed penalty. And that
31   Apollonia too has recovered from Taphasies (the documents) which ... gave ... according
32   (to) both she herself and Isidoros, under the agreements -
33   (namely) documents: Isidoros, a copy of a declaration concerning
34   a half-share of a small house situated in the ...; [and]
35   Apollonia, a deed of purchase for a certain part, and other [deeds]
36   of prior title, concerning the house belonging to her
37   in the same quarter; and all the documents ...
38   Year 17 of Caesar, Pha[r]mouthi 8.
