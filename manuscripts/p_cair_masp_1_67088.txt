[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the first day, which is the fourteenth
2     … of the present month Phamenoth of the now beginning
3     eleventh indiction, there approached me, as I was lamenting, Stephanous,
4     [most noble daughter] of Abraamios, one of the assistants of the village of Aphrodite, wife
5     … of Psates son of Hieremias, lector of the holy catholic southern church of the same village,
6     … to her said husband Psates concerning arouras of the said Psates
7     [son of Hieremias] which had been ceded by him to Dioskoros, the most admirable landholder from the same village,
8     … of the taxes. And straightway I brought forward the same Psates, who owed (them),
9     … he ceded his arouras, he said before me
10    [and before Artem]idoros, the imperial (official), administrator of the village, and before the others subscribed below,
11    [other] witnesses, that: … they saw (the lands) not bearing their public (tax), and especially
12    … and … I was compelled to cede it on account of the taxes
13    … if she, wishing something … the said arouras written for her by him on behalf of
14    [her] dowry at the hour of marriage … I compelled him to subscribe other arouras for her
15    … he, having learned this, as he said, that these had not yet been subscribed to her
16    … and in these matters I have set down this testimony
17    [as is stated above. Flavius Ar]temidoros the imperial (official), [the testimony agrees with me] as stated above; Pekysis son of David, elder, the testimony agrees with me as stated above; … son of Hermaos, landholder, the testimony agrees with me as stated above.
18    … [Isa]ak son of Kollouthos, [having been asked,] I wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters
19    … (name) son of Ioannes, landholder [from the same] village, the testimony agrees with me as stated above; Senouthios son of Apollos
20    [having been asked,] I wrote on [his] behalf, [since he does not know letters]
