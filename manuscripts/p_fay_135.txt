[TRANSLATION]
1   Agathos to Naph his father, gr(eeting).
2   Since the season of the har-
3   vest calls, …[……]
4   to yourself the deb[t(?)] …
5   to send off, lest I think fit
6   to send soldiers after
7   you and you be locked up until
8   you pay in full. Rather, make
9   haste to pay in full,
10   so that friendship may abide be-
11   tween us; and let our
12   brother pass word to Gerontios
13   about the monies you received and
14   gave to him. For you owe
15   also from last year one and a
16   half artabas of vegetable-seed.
17   I pray for your health.
