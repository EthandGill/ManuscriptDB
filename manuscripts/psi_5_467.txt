[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavi[us Iulianus] ... [of the praefecture] Augustamnica[n] ...
2     from Aurelia Thatre[tus] ...
3     from the illustrious and most illustrious [city of the Oxyrhynchites]
4     city. Willingly [I undertake to] rent for one year [from]
5     the present month Phaophi [of the current] year 37, [a certain] place
6     formerly of Ammonius the wine-seller
7     in the quarter [of] and
8     I will pay for the annual rent in silver [talent]s eight hundred. And when confirmed moreover
9     to me the undertaking [necessarily]
10    I will pay the rent
11    without complaint, and after the time I will hand over [to you the] same [place] as
12    and I received [it]. Valid is the undertaking
13    and when asked I acknowledged [it].
