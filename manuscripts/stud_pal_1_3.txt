1     [After the] consulship of [Fl]avius Aetius and Studius of the
2     [most-illustrious (ones)], Mechir 19th, 8th indiction.
3     Aurelius [Hypatius], scribe of Apollo's city,
4     to Aurelia [so-and-so from] Antinoopolis city. According to the
5     [today] day which is Mechir nineteenth [of] the eighth indiction I crossed the
