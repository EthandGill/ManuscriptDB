[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of the Flavii Pousaios and Ioannes the
2     most illustrious, Phaophi 13, 7th indiction.
3     Aurelius Kolloutho[s] son of Anouthios, greengrocer of the
4     city of the Oxyrhynchites, to Aurelius Papnouthios son of
5     Mat…ios, monk from the village of Kirka of the upper Theodosiopolite nome, residing here in the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
6     greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from you as a loan,
7     from hand to hand out of your house, for my own and pressing need,
8     one single gold solidus, of standard weight, approved, which makes
9     1 gold solidus of capital, which, being free from all risk,
10    of necessity I shall repay to you on the twenty-fifth
11    of the month Phamenoth of the present year 145 114
12    of the seventh indiction, wi[thou]t delay and without any
13    dispute or lawsuit or trial, the right of
14    exaction belonging to you both against me and from all
15    that I have. Valid is the note, written in single copy,
16    and having been formally questioned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Kolloutho son
17    of Anouthios the aforesaid, have received as a loan the one gold solidus
18    of capital and I shall repay at the appointed time as is set out above,
19    and having been formally questioned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Phoibammon son of Apphous, having been asked,
20    wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters.
21    Through me, through me the assistant.
22    Note of Kollouthos from the (city) of the Oxyrhynchites, 1 gold solidus.
