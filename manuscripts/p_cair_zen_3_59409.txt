[TRANSLATION]
1     Zenon, greetings from Botryis.
2     Since you summoned
3     me, I ask, if it also seems to you and you have need of us,
4     inform me
5     in what position I will be;
6     but if you do not have need,
7     so that I may run back to
8     what is assigned.
9     Farewell.
