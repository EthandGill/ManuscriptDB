[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aurel]ii Heron son of Ptolemaios with a surety for repayment, his full brother Aurelius
2     [Isidor]os, and Apphys son of Pannes with a surety likewise for repayment, Aurelius
3     … son of Apion, and Ammonas son of Papees with a surety likewise for repayment,
4     [Aureli]us Palemon son of Palinis, and Pannous son of Timoth[eos] with a surety likewise
5     [for repay]ment of Aurelius Heraklios … the four, from the village of Karanis, grain-collectors
6     of the same village of the produce [of the f]ourth indiction, to the Aurelii Ptolemaios
7     son of Ptollarion and Apolin[arios] son of Paeianos, both from the same village
8     of Karanis, receivers of the harbor of Leukogeion, greeting.
9     We acknowledge by this handwritten document that we have received from you on loan
10    of [whea]t four hundred and seventeen artabas, of wheat 417 artabas, by the public modius-measure,
11    which four hundred and seventeen artabas of wheat in full we shall repay under mutual surety [on the next] 28th of the present month Phaophi in
12    [the] harbor of Le[ukogeion without] any delay; and upon the demand
13    let [the right of execution belong] to you against us [and] against our [aforesaid] sureties
14    [and] against whichever of us [you choose, and] against all our belongings as by legal judgment;
15    [and on being] question[ed] we acknowledged it. Aurelii Apphys and the surety
16    … is and Heron [and the] surety Isidoros and Ammonas and the surety Palemon,
17    [we acknow]ledge that we have received together with our aforesaid partner Pannous the
18    [of wheat] four hundred and seventeen artabas, and we shall repay under mutual surety
19    [at the] appointed time as set forth above. I, Apphys son of Pannes, wrote also on behalf of the others
20    [who are illiter]ate. Aurelii Pannes with a surety Heraklios, I acknowledge that I have received together with my aforesaid
21    partners the four hundred and seventeen artabas of wheat, and we shall repay under mutual surety at the appointed time [as] set forth. I, Aurelius Ision son of Paesis, wrote on behalf
22    [of them, being illit]erate.
23    [In the consulship of Caecinius Sa]binus and Vettius Rufinus, the most illustrious, Phaophi 22.
