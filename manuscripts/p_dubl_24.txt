[TRANSLATION]
1     To brother Matthia son of Phoibammon from Maria daughter of Na[ar]aos Pachos, both
2     from the Leukogion estate of the Herakleopolis nome, greeting. I acknowledge the matters arranged below.
3     Since a dispute having come about between us concerning the arrears of the blessed
4     George son of Naraos Pachos and many matters having been raised regarding these, finally
5     it seemed good through the mediation of Naraos son of Senouthios that with God it be settled
6     to me the document of eleven coins except for two installments, I am ready
7     to deliver to you the solemn oath that I have hidden nothing from you of the same goods
8     of the blessed George, the brother of us. For this reason alone have I made for you this
9     the freedom from care as is set forth. It was written in the month Mesore twenty-three, indiction four, Aurelia Maria, daughter
10    of Pachos, this agrees with me. I, Anoup who is also Pouonsis, wrote on her behalf and witness
11    through me Eugenios through me A…ios son of Victor it was written
12    security of Maria daughter of Pachos to Matthia of Phoibammon
