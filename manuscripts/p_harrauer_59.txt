[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name [of the holy and consubstantial]
2     Trinity [and of our lady the]
3     Mother of God a[nd of all the saints.]
4     In the reign [of our master Flavius]
5     Phocas the et[ernal Augustus, year 2,]
6     Phamenoth … [, 7th indiction, in the city of the Arsinoites.]
7     Aurelius Ph[ib son of Gerontios from]
8     the hamlet of Me[liton, the northern, of the]
9     Arsinoite, [to Flavius Georgios son of]
10    the blessed P… [, councillor,]
11    fro[m t]h[e] Ar[sinoites' city, greeting.]
12    [I acknowledge that I have lea]sed from you
13    [the (plot) belonging to you in the p]lain of our hamlet, in the place called
14    Phani, a vineyard plot, made up and [n]ewly-planted, of however many
15    arouras it is, together with palms
16    and all its right, for as long
17    a time as you wish, from the present month
18    Phamenoth of the present seventh indiction, I having received for the work
19    one mattock and one wood-cutting tool,
20    receiving from you by way of
21    provision-allowance, until it be harvested, for each
22    one aroura a third of a gold solidus,
23    and six artabas of grain, to the aroura 1 (a third) of a solidus
24    as is customary, and 6 artabas of grain, to take, after the
25    cultivation of the palms, you
26    two parts and I one part, and
27    when, with God, the plot is harvested,
28    you to take from the flow three parts
29    and I one part of the wine,
30    I providing the empty (vessels) of my
31    part. And I will give for the year
32    six cheeses, of which I have received henceforth
33    already one artaba two-thirds of grain,
34    and I to take at the festival of wine
35    one kouri, and I will give at the vintage one piglet
36    of two carats and one kouri of must wine,
37    and the sieve; and on being formally questioned I acknowledged.
38    Phib son of Gerontios; it is agreeable to me as is set forth above …
39    Lease of a vineyard plot in the plain of the hamlet of Meliton, the northern, by Aurelius Phib son of Gerontios to Flavius Georgios, councillor.
