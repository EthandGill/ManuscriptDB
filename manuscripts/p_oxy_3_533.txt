[TRANSLATION]
1   Apion to Apion his son and Horion his dearest, very many greetings.
2   Before all I pray that you are in health, with your children and wives. All that
3   I wrote in the other letter — so as not to write the same again — I write to Horion too. I sent
4   to you
5   by Eutyches of Ision Tryphonos 3 notices-of-claim, 2 against the farmers of Maxi-
6   mus, the other against Diogenes the (son) of Belee.; publish them at once before
7   Phaoph[i, s]o they do not run past their term. Others were sent up to Panechotes the lawyer, from whom
8   you will fetch them — and give him 64 dr. Sell the hay-seed, and ask
9   O.ph.les whether he has need of that from Tampitei. My revenues collected
10   through the farmers: either let them be reckoned at the treasury on deposit, or let them be in safe keeping
11   with the farmers, so that, the gods willing, if they are released we have no entangle-
12   ment with our adversary — or let the risk of them rest on the farmers. The
13   house of T..bios do not lease to anyone, except perhaps to a woman meaning to live
14   in it; … for it is […] to expose such a house to young
15   men — so we get no vexations and no ill-will. Tell Zoilos the farmer from Sento:
16   “See to the bronze according to the agreements.” Tell the twins too:
17   “See
18   to the cash,” and likewise Apollonios and Dionysios; if you are able, send
19   to Pa[ke]rke-East, to Pausiris the donkey-driver: “Just as you arranged with
20   me, give the jars of wine — and let them keep their pledged word.” Take over from
21   Harthonis the priest the 20 artabas of wheat, and from Zoilos the farmer of Sento the 5 artabas of wheat he has
22   borrowed from me. Look up in the strategos’ account-office the letter of the dioiketes
23   written in the month Thoth about names being sent in, in my stead, for the allotment of the
24   collector-
25   ship. Tell Serenos at the camel-yard: “See to the bronze.” Tell Hermias
26   the scribe of the money-collectors of Ision Panga: “Transfer the wheat you owe me, or
27   whatever
28   you approve.” Let Herakleides son of Hermaiskos repay the 6 artabas of wheat on deposit. Tell Dio-
29   nysios son of Epimachos, former chief priest, that I petitioned the dioiketes about the revenue,
30   that it be credited against the debt of Sarapion son of Phanias. Greet Statia my daughter
31   and Herakleides and Apion my sons. Greet little Serenos and Kopreus
32   and all ours by name. Amarantos and Zmaragdos greet you.
33   I pray for your health.
34   Deliver to Apion my son and Horion.
