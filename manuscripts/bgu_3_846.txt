[TRANSLATION]
1   Antonis Longos to Nilous
2   his mother, very many greetings. And con-
3   tinually I pray for your health. Your obei-
4   sance I make every day before
5   the lord Sarapis. I want you to know,
6   that I had no [hope] that you would go up to the metro-
7   polis; for this reason neither did I come into the ci-
8   ty. I was ashamed(?) to come to Karanis,
9   because I go about in filthy state. I wrote you that I am naked.
10   I beg you, mother, be reconciled to me. For the
11   rest, I know what I have brought upon myself. I have been chas-
12   tised as is fitting. I know that I have sinned.
13   I heard from … my … the one who found you
14   in the Arsinoite (nome), and unseasonably he related everything to you.
15   Do you not know that I would rather be maimed
16   than know that I still owe a man an obol(?)
