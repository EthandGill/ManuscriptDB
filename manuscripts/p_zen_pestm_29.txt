[TRANSLATION]
1     Athenodotus to Zenon, greetings. Do not marvel that we did not encounter [Zoilus].
2     For having made an appointment with him as seemed fit to him, and having agreed with us [to meet]
3     concerning the dismissal, he put off [matters] until you sailed away. He forced therefore us
4     to sail up to the Lake. On account of which we were not able [to sail down together] with you.
5     I beseech you therefore, requesting for Dionysodorus's sake and for the kinsman who [introduced],
6     and for our own sake—perhaps also we might be useful to you [having been saved]
7     to write an honorable letter to Zoilus, in order that he might release us; for we are older
8     and helpless. But Philiscus will report to you the matters concerning us, whenever it seems to you
9     to inform him most quickly.
10    Be well. Year 28, Tybi … .
11    To Zenon.
12    Of Athenodotus. Year 28
13    Of Audaeus, 30,
14    In the harbor of Berenice.
