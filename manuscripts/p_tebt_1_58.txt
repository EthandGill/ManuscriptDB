[TRANSLATION]
1     Having therefore attended
2     together with the royal scribe (basilikos grammateus)
3     in the (place) of Magas until
4     the 26th, and in no way
5     have we been summoned.
6     And we have found the man
7     who submitted the memorandum.
8     It is Akousilaos,
9     the son of Paos the
10    Theban surveyor (geometres).
11    On the 24th, then, I went
12    to the record-office (hypomnematographeion) and, having given 200 (drachmas) of copper,
13    I made inquiry;
14    I found the memorandum
15    submitted by
16    Mestasytmis, son of
17    Paos, an agent (pragmatikos),
18    who undertook
19    to add, from produce of every
20    kind, 400 of wheat. And since
21    he has not been present at the
22    audit of the 5th year and
23    of the 6th year, this man, then,
24    observing me as
25    attending daily, as they have been afraid(?). We have decided
26    to draw out the submitted memorandum, lest at some point
27    we be storm-tossed (harassed) at the audit. We have promised
28    to the record-clerk (hypomnematographos) 12 drachmas of silver,
29    and he has not yet consented,
30    so that, with the (help of the) gods, we may hit our mark with him. The rest
31    (is) overleaf.
32    The apportionment has been drawn up
33    of the 1 1/2 myriads (15,000), and they have given (it)
34    to Melas, and they have imposed
35    upon us of wheat 73 5/6, among whom
36    are the village-scribes throughout
37    the nome. On the 25th, then, there have appealed
38    the 13 village-scribes who had sailed down in (the month) Mecheir to the
39    finance-minister (dioiketes), and he has subscribed (an order)
40    to release them,
41    but the remaining village-scribes
42    to exact the 1 5/9 myriads.
43    Having written so that you may know, you too
44    be free from anxiety.
45    Again I additionally enjoin upon you to attend
46    and to further encourage Nikon
47    concerning the collection. And if
48    you need to meet with
49    Aniketos, meet (with him).
50    With the (help of the) gods, nearly
51    will the audit be (finished) by
52    the 30th of Pachon.
53    Farewell. Year 6, Pachon 26.
54    Take care of those
55    at home.
