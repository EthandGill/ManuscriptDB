1     To my lord brother
2     Isokrates, Chrestos, [the] overseer of wine
3     [of the] 6th indiction. Give to Isak Abraam of wine
4     twenty xestes, [let it] become wine [twenty] xestes 20. I have written
5     I the same, by me Philammon [my] son
