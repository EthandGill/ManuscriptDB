[TRANSLATION]
1     Chairas to Dionysios the
2     lord father greetings.
3     I considered it [necessary] to [show]
4     to you the things concerning Serenus accomplished. On the one hand then
5     the accurate Rhodope will show to you. We have entreated
6     these things to him and finally he agreed
7     and released us knowing the things after [that],
8     however not yet into reconciliations to go he was willing, nor concerning a [contract] matter
9     [he has made], waiting for you. On account of this therefore we entreat you
10    [that] you would [advise] him concerning these things. For from you rather than from us
11    [he will listen].
12    to be strong I [pray] for you.
13    To Dionysios father gymnasiarch.
