[TRANSLATION]
1     Artemidoros to Zenon greetings. The works in this [matter]
2     in the middle are being lost, so that they be benefited; for me
3     for sufficient is an obol per day e … ek … ue …
4     … . For they are in wine and in prostitutes always; and
5     the other day in the surrender of the works, wanting to do something more for you, the three, perceiving [this], made me drunk
6     and led me into them. I beseech you then, if it seems good to you,
7     help me, so that it may not happen again to me to be made drunk going toward the works. Prosper.
8     Year 32, Phamenoth 22.
9     Artemidoros [concerning] this [matter]
