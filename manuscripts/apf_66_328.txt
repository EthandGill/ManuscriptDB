[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Twenty-first year of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Augustus Armeniacus Medicus Parthicus Germanicus Sarmaticus Maximus, 18th of the month of Phamenoth, before those administering the writing offices of both the metropolis and villages of the three divisions of the Arsinoite nome, in the marketplace of Kerkesoucha. Pnepheros, son of Pnepheros, about 45 years old, without a distinguishing mark, from Kerkesoucha, (acknowledges) to Melas, son of Horion, that he has sold to him a … donkey [not to be returned. He has received the price] of two hundred, …ty four silver drachmas. [Guaranteed.]
