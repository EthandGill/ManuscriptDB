1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis of produce, 5th year of Lucius
2     Septimius Severus the Pious
3     Pertinax Caesar our lord
4     Payni 23rd, on behalf of Charas, [in the] name of Harpokration [son] of Dioscorus, wheat, three
5     [And] a half [and] one-twelfth
6     Make wheat artabas 3 ½ 1/12. Kle [I] have certified [it].
