[TRANSLATION]
1     [for sowing and deposition] of [fruit]s of whatever
2     I choose [tax of the] same two arouras for each
3     [year] from each aroura barley-wheat according to the half
4     of artabae [six], which tax indeed new clean sifted
5     by standard Athenian measure [I will] deliver and I will measure
6     in the Epeiph month year by year without delay [being established] it
7     the tax into your house in [He]rmu city with my own animals
8     and [with] expenses without delay. The lease [is] binding and valid
9     and when asked I agreed. Aurelius Kolouthos [son of Hermi]nou the aforesaid
10    [I] have leased as aforesaid. Aurelius Ioan[nes] of Kallinikos from Hermu city
11    having been deemed worthy I wrote on his behalf - he being illiterate.
12    Aurelius Ioannes son of Papnouthios from Hermu city I testify
13    to the lease having heard from the one who set it forth.
14    I testify to the lease having heard from the one who set it forth.
15    through me Kallinikos [it was written] through George [my] brother, Lord help [me].
