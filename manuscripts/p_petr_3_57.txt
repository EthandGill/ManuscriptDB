[TRANSLATION]
1     Choiak 24
2     To Python: of the memorandum given to us by Mara[ios]
3     of Ptolemaios of hundred arouras, [lies] to you the copy:
4     Since then, having purchased through us in the 3rd year,
5     In Epeiph 10, a house of Theotimos, son of Euphronos, a Thracian of eighty arouras, and a courtyard and the appurtenances
6     being in Euergetis, sold in pledge which
7     [he] pledged [to] Philippos, son of Peukolaos, the
8     [one] having received the accruing apomorira
9     to Philadelphos and the Philopatores gods
10    of the places around Philadelpheia and Bubastis for the 2nd year
11    of the debt in copper against silver: 1 talent, 16 drachmas, 1½ obol, on condition that
12    [he] shall assign immediately in copper against silver 1 talent, 16 drachmas, 1½ obol
13    [has recorded] to the royal bank in Crocodopolis, upon which Kydronax
14    of year Thoth 12, both the 1 talent, 16 drachmas, 1½ obol and the accruing to the encyclios 325 five-obol pieces and the other things due
15    [let be confirmed] the purchase as is customary
