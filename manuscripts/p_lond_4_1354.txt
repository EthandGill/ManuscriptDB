[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the (revenues?) of the dist[rict] …
2     … the grain and bread that have been allotted
3     … on account of the expense of the expedition (kursos), as has been said
4     … which you have loaded onto boats, arrange to have delivered
5     … but if anyone of those of your administration
6     [appears to have given to any] of your subordinates any monetization whatsoever
7     of [such an expense] beyond the valuation which we have fixed
8     in our orders (entagia), refund this to them in full
9     and do not require from those of the villages the grain and bread from
10    now until the time of the harvest, by God's command. And when they begin
11    the harvest, require of them in full whatever they still owe of the same grain
12    and bread, and send (it) to Tamiathis, not falling short of one artaba
13    even by one. And follow without deviation our injunction in
14    all these (matters) — not entrusting (it) to anyone, but carrying (it) out through yourself. For behold,
15    we have written to the apostolos (envoy) sent by us to you
16    concerning such expense, to bring to us a register
17    containing what you loaded and sent through each carrier (pistikos),
18    both of the expense and of its monetization. Moreover, let it not be made known
19    to us that any monetization of expense was effected by those of your administration
20    at all, below the valuation which we have laid down, as has been said,
21    in our orders (entagia). And the pulse and the vinegar-oil, at once,
22    not waiting even for a moment, send to Tamiathis, and do not
23    be negligent. Written in the month Phamenoth 29, of the 8th indiction.
