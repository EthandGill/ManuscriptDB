[TRANSLATION]
1     In the [rei]gn of King Pt[ol]emy, surnamed Alexander, the god Philometor, and of Queen Berenike his sister, the Philometor gods, in the [14th] year,
2     [under the priests] and priestesses and the kanephoros (basket-bearer) then in office, in the month Phaophi, the 10th, in Pathyris, before Hermias
3     [the agent] of Paniskos the agoranomos. Kolluthes, son of Phageris, a Persian, about 35 years old, of medium height, honey-colored, with straight hair,
4     [long]-faced, straight-nosed, [with] his left ear pierced, sold the unsold portion left to him of grain-bearing mainland (epeiros) land,
5     of two [parcels (seal-plots)] in the plain about Pathyris, [belonging to him] and his brothers undivided, out of (a total of) seven arouras —
6     three arouras: the neighbors of the one (parcel) are, on the south, the land of Patephis; on the north, the land of P[ak]oibis through the
7     sons of Phibis; on the east, the [royal] road; on the west, the land of Psemmonthes son of Panechates and his partners; and of the other (parcel),
8     [on the south,] the land of Pakoibis; [on the north,] the land of Tathotis; on the east, the royal road; on the west, the land of Poregeb[thi]s and Paos; or whoever
9     may be the neighbors of the [two] parcels. Ho[ros] son of Nechous, about 50 years old, bought (it) for three [tal]ents of bronze (and) two thousand drachmas
10    . The fore-seller (propoletes) and warrantor of all the matters of this purchase is Kolluthes, the seller, [whom] Horos the buyer accepted.
11    I, [Hermi]as, the agent of Paniskos, have registered (this).
