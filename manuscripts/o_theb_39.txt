1     Psansnos, tax-collector of silver revenues of Memnon, through
2     Phmois, scribe, to Petearouḗrei Asklâtos
3     through Premtotos. I received for capitation-tax, year 10, four drachmas, which is also 3 tetrobols and a half-obol. Year 11
4     of Hadrian Caesar the lord, Hathur 4th.
5     Choiak 9, capitation-tax, four drachmas, which is also 3 tetrobols and a half-obol.
