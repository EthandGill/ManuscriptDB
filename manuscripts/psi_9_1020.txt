[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 8, Athyr 16, under Apollonios of the market-administration
2     of the Memnoneia of the Pathyrite [nome].
3     There leased Pikos [son of] Psemminios and Tateathyris
4     [daughter of] Psemminios Persine with the guardian of herself
5     brother Pikotes the aforementioned from the property
6     to them in the northern part of the Memnoneia at the border Aphrodisieiou [works] of purity-days and all
7     remaining and forthcoming benevolent [works] and the
8     falling to them share of those brought-in for
9     the 8th year from Athyr until Mesore 30 of the same year
10    of the month in bronze drachmas 100. It shall not be permitted to those about
11    Pikos [to rent] to others within the time
12    nor to Imouthes to be short in days within the time; but he shall hand over the of the days
13    ten and whenever he chooses to rent [it].
14    Apollonios has made [this] contract.
