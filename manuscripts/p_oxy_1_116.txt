[TRANSLATION]
1   Eirene to Taonnophris and Philon.
2   I have given Kalokairos, on the account
3   of Dionysios, 340 dr., since he wrote me
4   to give him as much as he
5   should want. So, being so good,
6   give them to Parammon our work-
7   man, and if he still has
8   need, supply him with as much as he
9   wants, and quickly send him
10   off. I sent you in my clothes-
11   basket a measure of Ombite
12   date(s) and twenty-five pomegranates,
13   by Kalokairos, seal(ed). Be so
14   good as to send me back in
15   it cleaned stuff worth two drachmas, since
16   I have urgent need of it.
17   I sent you by the same Kalo-
18   kairo(s) a b[o]x of smooth fine
19   grapes and a basket of fine
20   dates, seal(ed).
21   Farewell. Hathyr 30.
22   To Taonnophris and Philon.
