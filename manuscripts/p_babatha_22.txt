[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fourteenth year of Imperator T[ra]jan Hadrian
2     Caesar Augustus, in the consulship of Marcus Flavius Aper an[d]
3     Quintus Fabius Catullinus, three days before the Id[es of Septemb]er,
4     according to the reckoning of the new province of A[r]ab[i]a [in the twent[y]-fif[th] year]
5     on the twenty-fourth of Gorpiaios, i[n Maoza in the district of Zoo][r]a, Babatha daughter of Simon to Simon son of Je[sus] [son of Ananias], [both]
6     [dwelling in] Maoz[a, gr]e[e]ting. I acknowl[edge that I have sold] to you the cr[op] of a palm[-grove of gar]dens … e … of [Jud]as son of Chthousion my husband who has died i[n Ma]oza, which I hold in lieu of my dowry and debt, called gannath
7     Pherora and gannath Nikarchos and the thi[rd (part) o]f (that) of Molchaios,
8     for the present year, you paying me for the aforewritten year, into the same gardens, of pressed dates first and second quality, forty-two talents, you setting
9     them up at my house by the standard of Maoz[a] at the season of the dryings; and further also of syrup and naaron, you transporting them to my house
10    two koroi (and) five sata by the measure of Maoza. But if you do not pay me the aforewritten [da]te(-crop), all according as has been written above, you shall give me for each single talent two denarii
11    and of syrup and naaron one melaina (jar); I keeping clear
12    for you the aforewritten gardens from everyone laying claim. And if anyone lays claim to you of the purchase, and not
13    standing surety I (do not) make (it) lawfully yours according as has been written above, I shall be owing you in lieu of your toils and expenses
14    of silver twenty denarii, in nothing gainsaying. And whatever
15    m[ay be left over into the] aforewr[itten gardens, of dat]e(-crop) of Ju[da]s … mon … you shall recei[ve for yourself in l]ieu of
16    your toils and exp[en]ses. [Th]rough h[er guardi]an and
17    subscriber Ioanes son of Machchoutha of the sa[m]e Maoza[s], her faith
18    having been formally questioned and acknowledged in return. Subscription: and further.
19    It was written through Germanus the librarius (notary).
