[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelia Apia, daughter of Septimius Serenus, former exegete,
2     former prytanis of the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
3     from Claudianus, potter of wine-[jars] of (his) own jar-clay,
4     (acting through?) Eudaimon, gymnasiarch, councillor of the same city.
5     I willingly undertake to lease for one more year, from
6     Thoth 1 of the present 3rd year, your quarter share of the
7     pottery of wine-jars that is at the place called Metrikon around Sennis,
8     and of the kiln and vaults in it,
9     and of all its appurtenances, on condition that I mould for you
10    and fire and pitch, from the winter moulding,
11    Oxyrhynchite [four-choes] jars so-called, to the number
12    of four thousand, double-jars one hundred, two-choes (jars) fifteen,
13    you providing [m]e at the same pottery, of stocks,
14    the required ear[th, black-ear]th, porous earth, sandy earth,
15    and [wa]ters for the cistern, and for the firing and smoking of the jars [the re]quired fuel, and
16    for the pitching of the [sam]e jars and double-jars
17    and two-choes (jars), of pitch by we[ight], by [the m]easure of Aline, twelve solid talents,
18    of which half Troadesian, half Siretic,
19    as wage of the moulding and firing and pitching, [at the rate]
20    [o]f thirty-six drach[mas per] hundred jars; and the [drachm]as of the wages [amounting] to one thousand four hundred forty [I will receiv]e … one hundred …
21    … into the …
22    … of the … [dra]ch[mas] … forty,
23    [and I will rec]eive as extras one jar of vinegar, of lentils … art[ab… ; and the undertaking being guaranteed to me, I will make
24    [the moulding an]d firing and pitching of the aforesaid jars
25    and double-jars and two-choes (jars), and I will deliver these in the month Epeiph
26    of the present year at the cooling-floors of the pottery, from the winter moulding, well fired and pitched
27    from rim to base, satisfactory, not leaking, apart from those needing repair and damaged ones. And I acknowledge that I owe, from the moulding of the elapsed
28    [2nd year, jars] two hundred fifty, double-jars fifty,
29    … of Siretic pitch, one bare talent, and …
