[TRANSLATION]
1     ... To Zenon, greetings. You wrote to me concerning the two cattle and [the calf to return]
2     ... to him. But the farmers make difficulty, claiming that they had bought [them] from ...
3     ... by what means the money was given, in order that we might not appear to [use] force ...
4     ... we have sent Nechthemis and Tetoramous of the ... [in order that they may] stand before them on your behalf, those [decided] about these matters. [You will do well ...] ...
5     ... write to me to whom it is necessary [that it] be returned. For the [cattle] and the [calf we hold until]
6     [you send word] about them. For indeed the men from the village [testify] ...
7     ... [about] the Isiaeum, and the woman he had sold [when he was away].
8     [Farewell.]
9     To Zenon.
