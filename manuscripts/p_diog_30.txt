1     [Year] … of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius
2     Severus Alexander Pius Felix Augustus,
3     Pharmouthi … u …
4     … And the Aurelii acknowledge: Aionis son of Pasis,
5     on the mother's side A …, about thirty-two years old, with a scar on his chin, [and] … s son of Sabinus, on the mother's side De…, about … -eight years old, with a scar on his right eyebrow,
6     both from the village of Philadelphia, to Aurelius Ptolemaios … of … City …,
7     that the acknowledging parties have had measured out to them from him, on a joint-liability basis, [as a loan] in kind, five artabas of wheat, which
8     they shall of necessity repay in full, likewise on a joint-liability basis, in the month Pauni of the present year, in the
9     aforesaid village, by the four-choinix dromos-measure; and if within the appointed time they do not repay
10    the five artabas of wheat together with the half-as-much-again (penalty),
11    or even the highest price of wheat that shall prevail,
12    without delay, the right of execution belonging to Aurelius Ptolemaios both against them and against any one and
13    against whichever of them he chooses, and from all their property,
14    just as by legal judgment. And concerning whether these things
15    have been thus rightly and well done, having been asked the formal question, they assented …
16    … I wrote on their behalf, since they say they do not
