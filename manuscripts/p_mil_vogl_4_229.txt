[TRANSLATION]
1     To Tiberius Claudius Serenus,
2     priest, chief-justice (archidikastes),
3     from Diogenous son of Chration, of those from
4     the village of Teptynis of the Arsinoite. Being in dispute
5     with my wife, who is also my sister, I was robbed
6     of the things in my house, carried off by a band of robbers;
7     after these things … … …
8     … … … Ptolema … … …
9     her own brother … … … … …
10    of the house, she had another minor son; and when I came up
11    from Alexandria, my aforewritten daughter
12    Ptolema, having co-operated with her mother, denied …
13    the things which I had deposited with her; and likewise also her mother
14    the debts and property which I hold in her name and
15    other things she attempted to defraud (me of); and when
16    Ptolema asked that her marriage be dissolved, in order to
17    carry off my goods and squander them, I came before
18    Aelius Numisianus, strategos of the Arsinoite, before whom
19    some things were agreed; whence, having authority over my daughter
20    according to the laws, I request you, lord, if it seems good to you, to write
21    to the same strategos [Ael]ius Numisianus
22    to make, before the dissolution of the marriage, the examination
23    of the remaining matters; and to the former
24    epistrategos Julius Petronianus through
25    a petition I appealed, who subscribed (instructing) the same strategos
26    to put an end to the matter; since therefore, the
27    adver[saries, hav]ing learned of this, came down before [y]ou, thinking
28    they are abl[e to d]efraud (me), I report [t]o you so that you may know [and of necessit]y I request you to [wri]te to the [strategos]
29    so that [I may be hel]ped.
