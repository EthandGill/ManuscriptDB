[TRANSLATION]
1     Abba Kyros to you [the people] of the village
2     Poinami: provide on account of the work of the carabans
3     [in] indiction 12, one carpenter, one with his expenses
4     [for] 3 months: of grain 3 artabas, of legumes 1/2 artaba
5     1/2 of oil 3 sextarii
6     of vinegar 3 sextarii, salt cakes 3
7     only. [On the] 25th of the month Choiak, [in] indiction twelfth
8     Poinami
