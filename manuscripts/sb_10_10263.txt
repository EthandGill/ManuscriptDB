1     leased Sarapion, he-who-is-also Achilles, son of Zoilus, and Sarubus, son of Serenos,
2     both, from the city of Oxyrhynchus, to Greek Pheibastos, mother Thaesis, from village Paeimes, for
3     two years from the present 13th year, the lands [belong]ing to them around Paemes, two arourae
4     a quarter, so that the tenant
5     might sow each year whatever he might choose
6     apart from fodder-tax and draft-animal-tax, the rent
7     assigned per year of wheat, artabae
8     fourteen, free from all risk, all risk, of the land per year
9     of public-duties being [the] responsibility of the landlords, whom he shall also control of the produce
10    until the annually owed [amounts] are delivered. But if someone in the entering year becomes rainless [i.e., without rain]
11    it shall be allowed the tenant; when the lease is confirmed, let [him] deliver to the landlords the rent per year in Payni month at the threshing-floor of Paem[es] fresh wheat, pure, unadulterated
12    without-embolus, free from barley, sifted
13    as for the public being measured, with a [measure]
14    tetra[choin]ic of the Treasur[y or]
15    with the receipt-measure of the landlords
16    being measured, and everything without excep[tion].
17    and if the tenant [owes any] additional [amount]
18    let [him] pa[y with one-and-a-half], execution becoming from him and from the…
19    all his existing property. Valid
