1   [… a scar on the right] eyebr[ow; and for Orseus, Didymos son of Kronion,]
2   ag(ed) 34 […]
3   (2nd hand) I, Pakebkis son of P[……, have leased, for only the]
4   twelfth ye[ar, the five arouras of royal land]
5   in two [parcels, of which the first parcel is four] arouras
6   in the field [called] of [Laar]chos, and of the second
7   parcel the remaining [o]ne arour[a] in the Sixth fie[ld],
8   at a total rent, apart from seed, of thirty-
9   seven and a half artabas of wheat by the four-choi[n]ix measure
10   of the god’s granary, as aforesaid. His son Psenkebkis wrote
11   on his behalf because he does not know letters.

13   five arouras of royal land for only the twelf-
14   th year, at a total rent of thirty-sev-
15   en and a half artabas of wheat, the carriage-charges of the
16   delivery falling on me, as afore(said). Didym(os) son of
17   Kronio(n) wrote on his beh(alf), as he does not know letters.
18   Registered through the record-office at
19   Tebtunis.
20   Lease, of Pa[kebki]s
21   to Or[s]eus, of 5 (arouras).
