[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings, Kopres,
2     from Julianus.
3     Send me Bauthlan with the donkeys
4     and the female [of] Koryphus so that [he] may go to the rope-works of the
5     vineyards. If
6     therefore you have [something],
7     give them to him and
8     be concerned about
9     the cargo. In the meantime I am coming down.
10    Farewell.
