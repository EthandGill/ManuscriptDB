[TRANSLATION]
1     … the most reverend presbyter, Aurelius Belisarios [son]
2     … archisymmachos of the glorious house of Strategios the all-praiseworthy
3     [consul], from the same city, greeting. I acknowledge that I have leased
4     [from] you, from the property belonging to the same holy church,
5     [in a] field called Pteuntēena, in an allotment called
6     …ēni, three arouras, in a place of an allotment called
7     …ou, six arouras, total 9 arouras, more or less, with all their right
8     [for as long a time] as you wish, from the crops of the coming seventh indiction
9     [and of itself], and that I the lessee provide, for the fixed
10    [rent of them], yearly in wheat by the just measure artabas
11    [twenty-two] and a half, and of gold thirty-one and a half carats,
12    [total] in wheat 22 and a half artabas [and in gold] 31 and a half carats, in the month …
13    [The lease] is valid, [and on being formally questioned] I gave my assent. Aurelius [Belisarios]
14    [son of] … [archisymmachos], everything aforesaid is satisfactory to me [as set forth above.]
15    … having been [requested], I wrote on his [behalf, since he is illiterate.]
16    [Lease of] 9 [arouras] more or less in a field called Pteuntēena [under Aurelius Belisarios]
17    to the holy [church] …
