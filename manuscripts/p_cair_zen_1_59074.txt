[TRANSLATION]
1     Lycurgus to Z[enon greetings. If you are strong], it would be well; and I too am healthy. We have written to you
2     and [before] concerning [the silver-work, so that you, having] received the small change from Agathocles,
3     might give [it] to him. If indeed you have done it, it would be well; but if not, give [it] to Hermias, [the one] delivering to you the letter, [so that he might receive from him t]he small change.
4     Farewell.
5     Lycurgus concerning silver-work,
6     so that it be given to Hermias. Year 29, Artemisium 16,
7     in Alexandria.
8     [To Zenon.]
