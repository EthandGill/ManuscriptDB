1     Flavius Domitius [As]klepiades, the most illustrious
2     count and flavialis and governor,
3     to the exactors and presidents of Hermopolis. In what
4     danger you stand, what destruction you will experience —
5     not you alone but also your entire council — the trial of events will show.
6     For the most noble and most devoted soldiers
7     you have, for no short time, on account of the rations owed,
8     contrived to make defaulters
9     for a whole three-year period, and, having been ordered
10    to send the provisions, you sent out unprovided the …
11    having nothing in their hands, employing … reports and trifling with the governor's
12    [office], and up to now not submitting these debts for collection, thinking by these means
13    to [escape] the danger hanging over you.
14    … to these men stationed near the [barbarian frontier] in desert places
15    [in] the same des[ert places] …
16    … to buy …
17    and what sort is the …
18    … of the year … [of the governor's]
19    office a judge …
20    since … against you …
21    on behalf of … for the future
22    to make of their own safety … of … the establishment of [all] the debts owed
23    by us, and having set them right, send them off
24    … [to those] places. For if … over the very smallest amounts there shall be sent
25    those who will exact from you yourselves on equal terms
26    … the receipt of chaff, and standing
27    before the highest authority you will be subjected to the supreme penalty.
28    What things are owed to be sent forth
29    to your subordinate account, the schedules make clear [to you].
