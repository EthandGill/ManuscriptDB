[TRANSLATION]
1     Dikaios to Damis, greetings. The land
2     all clean and that sown last year, which you said has been measured for the cavalry, having watered,
3     sow [it]. For if [it] be royal
4     [it is], I will arrange its tax; but if
5     [it] be of the cleruchs, and this [one]
