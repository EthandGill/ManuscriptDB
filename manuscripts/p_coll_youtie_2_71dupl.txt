1     Aur[eli]us [Hi]erax [also] called [Eustephios], councillor of the most illustrious city of the Al[exandrian]s …
2     … e … nypo … n … [through]
3     me, his father, A[urelius Eudaimon, son of Sere]nos, councillor of the same (city), having been instructed, to Aure]lia Artemidora daughter of Besarion, mo[ther being Eudaimo]nis, and however you are styled, from P[anos polis, greeting.]
4     I acknowledge that I have sold [to you, in the] 2nd [quarter, in the]
5     same Panos polis, the [belonging] to me [tenth]
6     [thi]rtieth share of a common house, with the appurtenant utility-[room] … o … ros klе … y …
7     … sen to Harsytos io … a …
8     of … against the fifth year, of chests fr[om] Harsytos,
9     of which the neighbours are declared through the title-deeds concerning it,
10    and likewise in the same Panos polis one aroura in the plain of Marion, on the west, of the daughter of Dioskoros Kalykas, purchased by me as dry land and planted at
11    my own expense, of which also the neighbours through the
12    title-deeds concerning it are declared, and the aforesaid I had
13    by purchase-right in the [5th] year of Probus Augustus, Pachon 3,
14    from Philapollon son of Philapollon, through Aurelius Didymos in most illustrious Alexandria, through Aurelius Isidoros
15    public notary. I have sold to you by purchase and sale at the price
16    agreed upon with you for each, of silver
17    of Ptolemaic old coinage two talents, which I have received
18    from you through me, the aforesaid father Aurelius Eudaimon,
19    in full by hand, so that from now you may own what has been sold to you and have power over it and administer it as you
20    may choose, collecting the rents and paying the public dues, and I will guarantee them to you with every guarantee at every
21    time against everyone who shall come forward. And this document
22    of sale, written in duplicate in my own hand, I have issued to you, and let it be valid and secure as if registered in a public office, and whenever you wish I will register it for you by public transaction, and being formally questioned whether these things have thus
23    rightly and properly been done, I acknowledged.
24    Year 6 of our lord Probus Augustus, Pharmouthi.
25    Aurelius [Hierax also called] Eustephios, councillor of the most illustrious (city).
