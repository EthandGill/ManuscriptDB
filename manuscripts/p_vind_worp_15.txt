1     One great sheep I slew [and sent] so that my mistress salt it with wine as she wishe …
2     And write to me, if well I … the eldest of the two children. If and he came
3     my brother, write to me. [Damianus] the son of Kyrision revealed to me concerning the house.
4     He knows the steward and if the matter [pleases] him, he will give the money. He went away
5     And my brother and he knew the matter. If the house is good and it pleases him beside …
6     the matter, and as I said, the abba Damianus will give the money until …
7     Restore into the hospice … of Phoibammōn
