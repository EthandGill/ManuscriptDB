1     To Aurelia Teieous also called Thermoutharion,
2     transacting business without a guardian by the right of children,
3     through her husband Zotikos, and to Aurelius (?),
4     from Aurelius Pamounis son of Philosarapis,
5     from the village of Teptynis, manager
6     of Ptolemaios son of Eupompos. I wish to lease from you the three cows
7     belonging to you, full-grown (?), three in number,
8     for only the under-ploughing of the present 8th year and 7th year
9     of the arouras belonging to
10    Ptolemaios in the same village,
11    I giving for the rent green fodder, well … , suitable,
12    … and nine artabas of wheat
13    … (it is permitted) for me to abandon the lease of the … and …
14    and to the … and … toward me the
15    lessee; and after the under-ploughing
16    I shall hand back the three cows sound and well-fed
17    just as I also received them; and the green fodder I shall hand over in the month Tybi, and the nine artabas of wheat
18    in the month Pauni, by the just four-choinix measure, if it seems good to lease. I, Aurelia Teeieous also called Termoutharion, have agreed,
19    Aurelia Teeieous also called Termoutharion,
20    I have leased out the cows as aforesaid. Aurelius
21    Zotikes, I wrote on her behalf, since she is illiterate.
22    In the 8th and [7th] year of our lords Diocletianus
23    and Maximianus Augusti, Phaophi 2.
