[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of the queen and king Ptolemy, gods Philometores Soteres, year 4,
2     under the priest of king Ptolemy god Philometor Soter (and) Alexander and of the Soter gods
3     and of the Sibling gods and of the Benefactor gods and of the Father-loving gods and of the Manifest gods and of the god
4     Eupator and of the god Philometor and of the god Philopator the Younger and of the god Euergetes and of the
5     Philometores Soteres gods; under the sacred colt of Isis the great, mother of the gods, the athlophoros of Berenike
6     Euergetis, the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos, the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator, those in office
7     in Alexandria; and in Ptolemais of the Thebaid under the priests and priestesses and kanephoros in office in Ptolemais of the Thebaid, in the month Mecheir 27, in Pathyris,
8     under Heliodoros, agoranomos. Willingly they made a written contract.
9     Totoes son of Pelaios, a Persian of the epigone, about [55] years old, somewhat short, honey-colored, with curly hair,
10    long-faced, straight-nosed, acknowledges that he has divided the landed property belonging to him among his own
11    sons: from the grain-bearing land belonging to him and his brothers in the plain about Pathyris,
12    a twelfth [undivided] share of 80 [arou]ras, whose neighbors are: to the south, a royal embankment; to the north, a road;
13    [to the east, a road; to the west, the] channel of Telon — and from the share falling to him of another
14    [sealed-plot called] (the land) of Patephis, 36 arouras, the falling-share being 3 arouras, neighbors of the whole [land]:
15    to the south, land of Harmonthes; to the north, land of Kollouthos; to the east, an embankment;
16    to the west, land of Panobchounis and others, or whoever the neighbors may be on all sides —
17    to Panobchounis his elder son, two shares;
18    to Taeloloutis and Siepmoutis, daughters of Totoes, his own daughters,
19    one share of the two sealed-plots; and from the bare plot
20    which is in the middle strip of land of Pathyris, which is two and a half cubits
21    of solid (measure), whose neighbors are: to the south, the house of Horos; to the north, the house of Psennesis;
22    to the east, the house of Pates son of Thotortaios; to the west, the broad street of the gods —
23    of which (it is agreed) that there is to be, in the northern part of the bare plot, to Pateis his
24    own son, half a cubit; to Panobchounis the elder son
25    two cubits of solid (measure); and from the house built within the fortified place
26    to Taeloloutis and Siepmoutis the half, of which the other half belongs
27    to Panobchounis; and (it is agreed) that neither shall proceed against the other concerning the
28    shares granted to each; but if not, let the proceeding of the one
29    proceeding (against) be void, and let the one who transgresses the contract pay in addition
30    [to the kings] 500 drachmas [of silver], and nonetheless let the
31    [matters agreed] remain valid.
