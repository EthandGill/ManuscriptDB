[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King P]tolemy, greetings. Theodotus. I am wronged [by a certain Nikias], of those dwelling in Bubastis,
2     [of the H]erakleid division. For having borrowed from me [a donkey for] drachmas 40, in the month Phaoph of the 26th year,
3     and I having sailed up to the Hermopolite [nome] concerning … having been away until now, when I arrived
4     and demanded from him the donkey which he borrowed from [me, he does] not return it to me, nor does he bring the
5     donkey forward. This he does, despising me, contrary to […]. I beseech you therefore, King, if it seems good to you, [order Diophanès the] general to write to Meleager
6     the overseer to send Nikias [before him] … concerning which I appeal to you through this
7     [p]etition, so that through you, King, I might obtain justice. [Farewell.]
8     To Meleager: above all reconcile them; but if not, [send so that] [according to the la]ws they may obtain justice.
9     [Year 1, Gorpiaeus 28, Tybi] 13.
10    Year 1, Gorpiaeus 28, Tybi 13.
11    Theodotus against Nikias, concerning
12    a donkey.
