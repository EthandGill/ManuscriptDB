1     Year 7, [Pauni] 3. Demodichos [I have]
2     from the shepherds of [Mouches]
3     of wool … [so that] to be into the 6th year
4     two talents, ten minas, one, one-half, one-third, one-twelfth, it totals [two talents, eleven minas, one-half, one-third, one-twelfth]
5     Onnophris, the one from Achoapis, of the
6     topogrammateus, acknowledges [to have been] given through me [from]
7     of the wool of Mouches [two] talents,
8     minas ten, one, one-half, one-third, one-twelfth, it totals [two talents, eleven minas, one-half, one-third, one-twelfth]
9     11. one talent, twenty-five minas, two-thirds, it totals one talent, twenty-five minas [two-thirds]
10    26. thirty minas, one, one-half, one-third, one-twelfth, it totals thirty-one, one-half, one-third, one-twelfth
