[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelii [Sarapion] of [Leonides]
2     and Aion of Polydeuces, both
3     [demanding] formulation and all kinds of products of the [settlement] Kalu
4     produce of the 7th [indiction]
5     to Aurelius Isidore of Ptolemaeus
6     greetings.
7     [we received] from you straw pounds
8     fifty, making 50 pounds, and for formulation and primipilus and transport of goods and lime and sea transport for
9     the 7th [indiction] of the [settlement] [Kalu]
10    in silver 1400 drachmae, making 1400 drachmae, and for conversion to cash
11    of meat and straw 300 drachmae,
12    making 300 drachmae, and of meat [pounds] two, making 2 pounds.
13    in the consulate of our lords Constantine
14    Augustus the 5th and of Licinius the most illustrious Caesar the 1st, Choiak 5. Isias
15    I wrote the whole.
