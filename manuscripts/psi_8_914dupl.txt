[TRANSLATION]
1     Onnophris, son of Psenkebkis, [and] mother Tamareous, I acknowledge [that] I have sold to Psoiphi, [son] of Psoiphi, mother Tetoseiris, according to this sale.
2     from the present
3     day, for all time, the belongings of mine and [those] accruing to me, two parts from five parts, which are from two paternal shares of mine,
4     of house-plots, which is a house adjoined together and adjacent court-yards and old places
5     and gatehouse and upper rooms and all the appurtenances in [Teb]tynis near the sacred temple of Isis, [held] in common and undivided both with my paternal brothers and Psoiphi Marepkamis and Nebtynis [son] of Pso[iphi].
6     Whose neighbors [of] the old adjoined house: on the south [are the] houses of the sons of Psoiphi Thothis, on the north [is] the aforementioned Psoiphi Marepkamis' house,
7     on the west [are] the aforesaid and adjacent places, on the east [are] Lysimachos [son] of Lysimachos' places. And neighbors
8     and of the adjacent [court-yards]
9     and places, on the south and north and west [are] royal streets, on the east [is] the aforementioned old house and on some part
10    of the aforementioned Psoiphi Marepk[amis]
11    house and court, and likewise on some part of the aforementioned sons of Psoiphi Thothis' house and court and places. And I have received from Psoiphi the agreed price in full by hand from [his] house immediately, and I will guarantee the sale with every guarantee both against public and private
12    and all encumbrance for all time. And my wife Tetoseiris, [daughter] of Psoiphi, mother Thaubastes, consents to this sale and [will] not come forth against [it] for all time, nor lodge complaint, nor [shall] anyone on her behalf, as aforesaid. Dionysios, [son] of Maron, wrote on their behalf because [they do] not
13    know letters. Psyphos, [son] of Psyphios, [his] mother, the sale to me has come to pass as aforesaid.
