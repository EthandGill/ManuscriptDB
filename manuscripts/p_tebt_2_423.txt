[TRANSLATION]
1   To Aurelius Nemesion, manager, greeting.
2   I wrote to you on another occasion too, reminding you
3   about the work, and I learned that you had received
4   the chickling-vetch from Amatios. So now,
5   as was arranged, let the fodder-vetch go to the (estate of) Tbe-
6   klytis, both the green vetch and the
7   vetch by itself, for seed. Concerning the oxen,
8   do not be careless, and do not overwork them,
9   for you yourself know that (one) is undernourished. And
10   indeed I instructed you, as I was going up,
11   that you should go to Nemera in the Oxyrhynchite,
12   to look for hay; and not yet
13   have you told me about this, so that I am in dis-
14   tress at present. If you find
15   buyers for the surplus donkeys, dis-
16   pose of up to three. I wrote to you,
17   then, that you should send the animals (to be) under fodder
18   to Heron; so if you have not received the sheep
19   for folding, either (keep them) with you, and you
20   hand over the animals. If the farmers come to
21   you wanting seed,
22   let me know quic[kly] so that I may send
23   them some measures (of it) from somewhere. And I want you to
24   know that I have bought the two [ar]ouras of (the land of) Rhegnu-
25   ti..., of Labeon;
26   so go there for the working of them,
27   and dismiss the (former) farmer. See that
28   ... of the flax-stalk,
29   ...deliver [to the] farmstead, and
30   lodge (there) until you receive the price;
31   and [concerning] the tasks I [ga]ve you for the
32   farmstead, [let it be your care] to take them up. If
33   Strabon(?) is doing well, by all means
34   buy from him a fine tunic
35   worth 40 drachmas.
36   I pray for your health.
37   (Address:) Deliver ... to N[em]esion,
38   manager.
39   (Docket:) Year 24, of the reckoning of Mecheir. There paid
40   ...nis(?), [for] the single-bundle (hay-tax) and
41   other kinds, eight drachmas.
