[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving
2     and consubstantial Trinity, the Father and the Son
3     and the Holy Spirit, in the reign of our most divine
4     master Flavius Heraclius the eternal
5     Augustus Emperor, the sixth year,
6     Pauni the second, of the fifth indiction,
7     Aurelius Isakos son of Loulous,
8     slow at writing, a farmer
9     from the city of the Hermopolites, to Flavius
10    Biktor the most esteemed son
11    of the late Kyros from the
12    same city, greeting. I acknowledge
13    that I have leased to you for only one
14    year, that is upon the crops of the
15    sixth indiction, God willing, one aroura around … e
16    … sixty … with all the
17    rights … on condition that I sow [thi]s
18    with fodder-grass and take the
19    green and dry hay of it down
20    to its very root, I being ready
21    to water it a fourth (time)
22    by means of my own animals and their fodder
23    and the wage of the herdsmen, and
24    to guard its hay
25    until its removal.
26    For I acknowledge that I have been paid in full from now
27    by you for the rental-fee of it
28    in advance; and if I should transgress the
29    present agreements or hinder you
30    from taking from me its
31    dry hay, I will provide you for the
32    price three gold solidi
33    at my own risk and resource of
34    my whole property of every kind.
35    The present lease-receipt is valid and
36    secure, and on being questioned I acknowledged.
37    Aurelius Isakos son of Loulous
38    [the ][a]foresaid have made the lease-receipt
39    as set forth. Aurelius …
40    from Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lease-receipt, having heard
41    from the maker. Theodorakios son of Damaskios
42    from Hermoupolis, I bear witness to the lease-receipt
43    having heard from the maker.
44    Through me Isakos, with God, the notary, it was written.
45    Lease-receipt of one aroura … in fodder, of the 6th indiction, made by Isakos son of Loulous, farmer from Hermoupolis.
