[TRANSLATION]
1     From the examination of ephebes, 9th year
2     of the gods, Aurelius Antoninus
3     and Verus. Among others,
4     whose fathers [are] of the tribe Oseriantis. Among other[s], Gaius Julius Longinus [son] of Gaius Julius
5     Nigerus, mother Ptolemais,
6     [of] 14 years, 7 months, 1 day. Witnesses:
7     Gaius Valerius Serenus
8     Nerouianus, also called Hetiaeans,
9     Gaius Julius Priscus
10    Paulinius, also called Isidius,
11    Lucius Aquilius Ptolemaeus
12    Oseriantis, also called Cleitorius.
13    Copy of signature of Lucceius Opellianus, the epistrategos
14    being as follows: [it] was received.
15    Ammonius, councillor, keeper of records of the city, [I] have noted.
