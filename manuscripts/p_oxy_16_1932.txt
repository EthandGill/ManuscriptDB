r.3    [τοῦ ἀγορασθ]έντος εἰς τροφὰς τῶν γε[ουχ]ι[κῶν ὄνων λόγῳ τ]ῆ[ς] ἕκτης ἰνδικτίονος ἀπὸ καρπῶν ἑβδόμης
r.4    ἐ[π]ι[νεμήσεως] παράσχου αὐτῷ κεράτι[α δεκατρία παρ]ὰ κεράτια δύο ἰδιωτικῷ πρὸς συμπλήρωσιν τῆς τιμῆς, ἅπερ τὰ εἰρημένα
r.5    κεράτια δεκατρία παρὰ κεράτια δύο ἰδιωτικῷ λογιζόμενα σοὶ μόνα. δέξαι δὲ παρʼ αὐτοῦ τὴν
r.6    ὁμολογίαν ἣν ἐποίησεν περὶ τῆς τιμῆς τὰ δὲ τρία νομίσματα εὔσταθμα τὰ δοθέντα αὐτῷ διὰ Φιλοξένου

[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the] three coins, less twelve carats, by private weight, which Philoxenos the banker gave
2     [to Apollonius] from the estate of Lucius, for the price of the green fodder of two-eighths of arurae
3     [which was purchased] for the feeding of the agricultural donkeys, according to [the account of] the sixth indiction, from the harvest of the seventh
4     [pasturing] provide to him [thirteen carats, less] two carats by private weight, for the completion of the price, which aforementioned
5     carats, thirteen, less two carats, by private weight, being reckoned to you alone. Receive from him the
6     agreement which he made concerning the price, and the three coins of good weight which were given to him through Philoxenos
7     these demand from the one who farmed the three arurae from Pekytis, from the property of Phoibammon
8     also called Belaus, from Anta, so that these you might pay over to the aforementioned Philoxenos, who also gave
9     [the same] three coins to the aforementioned Apollon[ios].
10    Give to the most admirable Apollinarios beside …
