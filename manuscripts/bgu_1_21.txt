[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Asklepiades, praepositus of the 15th pagus
2     of the Hermopolite nome,
3     from the Aurelii: Horos son of Ammonianos, overseer,
4     and Horos son of Kastor and Cornelius son of Paesis, comarchs,
5     and Herakles son of Pagenes, quadrarius, all of them from the village
6     of Prektis of the pagus under you, greeting. We acknowledge,
7     swearing the divine and august oath of
8     our masters the Augusti who conquer in all things,
9     that there has been apportioned and exacted in our village
10    from the men registered below the
11    apportionments registered below for each month,
12    and that it has been expended thus, as is set out below, and
13    that nothing has been falsely stated, or we shall be liable to the divine
14    oath and to the peril concerning this; and on being formally questioned we gave our assent. In the consulship
15    of Septimius Acindynus, the most illustrious prefect of the sacred
16    praetorium, and of Poplonius Proculus, the most illustrious. Mesore 20.
17    We the aforesaid have submitted the accounts as set forth above.
18    I, Aurelius Silvanus, wrote on their behalf, they being unlettered.
19    Account of receipt and of expenditure
20    made through us, the 2 comarchs Horos and Cornelius:
21    in the month of Pachon, men 125½, from 25 talents, talents 3137½;
22    and in the month of Pauni, men 100, from 15 talents, talents 1500;
23    and in the month of Epeiph, men 100, from 12 talents, talents 1200;
24    and in the month of Mesore, men 100, from 15 talents, talents 1500;
25    total together … receipts, talents [7]337½;
26    of which, of expenditure:
27    for the price of wine, 720 sextarii, from 3 talents, talents 2160;
28    for the price of chaff, 1800 pounds, talents 144;
29    to the recruits in Hermopolis, for the price of a donkey
30    for … days, wine 1 knidion, to the salt-sellers, talents 34;
31    to the same, for expenses, talents 30;
32    hire of a camel for archer's drug (poison), talents 300;
33    to Apollonios, official, for a comedy(?), talents 24;
34    to Neilos, fuller, for a comedy(?) of impositions of tunics, talents 63;
35    to Anoubion, exactor from Antinoopolis,
36    for a comedy(?) on account of … 24, from 15 talents, talents 360;
37    to the praepositus of the Moors, for the price of fodder, talents 63;
38    talents 3175(?);
39    … to the assistant of the exactor, for the … …
40    for the price of vinegar, 13th indiction, 12 sextarii, from 1 talent 20 drachmas …
41    hire of 3 donkeys for the governor going to the Thebaid, talents …
42    [to the recruits] for expenses of 2 days, wine 2 knidia …
43    to Paoues, guard of Olympiodoros, for wages …
44    to Sarapion, beneficiarius, of clothing, for a comedy(?), talents …
45    to the same, wine 2 spathia, from 20 talents, talents 40, to the salt-sellers, talents 2 …
46    expenditure for a cavalry detachment, talents 12 …
47    to Besarion, chief assistant of the exactor, talents 12 …
48    to the centurion of the catholicus, talents …
49    to the tribune, talents …
50    and for the apportionment of the recruits, the sums demanded by the praepositus of the legion, talents 15;
51    to the soldier of the legion, talents 3 …
52    … of Herminos, helmsman, talents 3 …
53    wine for compulsory transport (angareia), talents 3 …
54    Hermogenes, beneficiarius, exacting of the impositions
55    of tunics, for a comedy(?), talents 36 …
