[TRANSLATION]
1     There leased Straton son of Heliodoros, of those
2     from the city of Oxyrhynchus, to Pachnoubis son of Totoes son of Tr[y]phon, of those from the village of Tanais,
3     a Persian of the epigone, for four years, four floodings,
4     from the present twelfth
5     year of Imperator Caesar Nerva
6     Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus,
7     the arouras belonging to him around the [s]ame Tanais from the lot of Nikaios,
8     twenty-four, so as to sow each year the one half with wheat and to plant the other half with bitter-vetch fodder,
9     at a fixed rent each year of, for the
10    present year, of wheat
11    one hundred thirty-nine artabas, and for the other three years each year a fixed rent
12    of wheat one hundred thirty-
13    four artabas, and each year half of the
14    straw that comes out, all free of risk each year
15    from every risk, the public dues on the land each year
16    being upon Straton, who also
17    is owner of the crops each year until
18    he recovers the yearly fixed rent.
19    And, the lease being guaranteed, let the lessee repay to the lessor
20    [the] yearly fixed rent always in the month Pauni
21    at the threshing-floor of T[anai]s, new wheat clean unadulterated free of barley
22    sifted as for the public treasury, measured
23    by the fourth receiving-measure of Straton himself,
24    the measurement being made by whomever Straton chooses.
25    Of necessity the lessee shall hand over the land
26    cleared of rushes, and the half part from fallow
27    in bitter-vetch fodder from the cutting. And if any part becomes unwatered, it shall be allowed to the lessee in proportion to the
28    fixed rent. And let the right of execution belong to Straton both against the lesse[e] and against all his possessions as if by [legal judgment].
29    The lease is valid. Year twelve of Imperator Caesar
30    Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, Phaophi 15.
31    I, Pachnoubis son of Totoes son of Tryphon, have leased
32    [the] twenty-four arouras for the four
33    [yea]rs at a fixed rent each year of, for the
34    [pre]sent year, of wheat one hundred
35    thi[r]ty-nine, and for the [o]ther years
36    three, each year of [w]heat one hundr[e]d
37    thirty-four, and of the straw coming out
38    each year half, and I will repay
39    everything each year and [w]ill do the yearly rush-clearing, and I will hand over in the last
40    year the half part of the aforesaid arouras from fallow, cleared
41    of bitter-vetch and fodder. And I will hand them over cleared of rushes at my own expense. And
42    I will repay and do each thing as is set out above.
43    I, Ploutarchos son of Pachnoubis, wrote on behalf of my father, he not knowing letters. Year
44    twelve of Imperator Caesar Nerva
45    Trajan Augustus Ger[ma]nicus Dacicus,
46    Phaophi 15.
