1     [Syros] to Heroneinos
2     to the dearest, greetings
3     the camels that have come down, fill with dates and send back
4     to the city, having declared the number to be delivered to Tryphon
5     the helper, of which an account
6     [will give]
7     but if you do not [have] them all
8     have the cargo in readiness
9     let it be filled with wood
