1     himself, concerning the previously
2     delivered grain to him, and
3     of wheat, 55 talents, 4 (measures)
4     and in the matters recorded below, not to
5     be carried over into the 11th year, which …
6     it will be necessary for him to deliver
7     at the same time, according as
8     they agreed with him.
9     To Herieus son of Phatres, through Phegonis the beekeeper, m(easure), 4 talents (measures), of which the third 4 (measures), makes 9
10    To Tanouphis son of Latanis, 4 (measures), of which the half 1 (and) 3 kotylai (?)
11    To Palti son of Panas, 4 (measures), of which the half 1 (and) 3 kotylai (?)
12    To Tenouches, through Thotroïsis, the
13    psellinia (?), 3 (measures), in return for which 4 talents, half
14    To Tsemminis, the wife of Portis,
15    through Psenamounis son of Ptoïs,
16    which she was to give in the 9th year, 7, half
17    and in the 11th year she was assigned to give … talents
18    To Pedon son of Phatres, through Herieus, 4 (measures), of which the half 1 [(and) 3 kotylai]
19    and Herieus has undertaken it for us;
20    To Tiuris the wife of Herieus, through
21    Pchemterheus, from the 10th year 9, makes 1, half
22    To Portis son of Anaxagoras, from the 10th year, 4 (measures)
23    To Psemminis son of Eiris, through Petegnes
24    his son, in the 11th year, m(easure), 4
25    makes of the capital 30 talents, half, of interest 10, half, makes 41
26    so that there is on sale for the 11th year
27    m(easure) 204 talents, of barley 13, half, of arakos 1200, of millet [504]
28    and we owe him the price of sheep,
29    and through Petoëris it is given on a cloak
30    for the 11th year, m(easure) 6 talents, in m(easure) 80, half, 3, makes 9.
