[TRANSLATION]
1     [From] Didymos son of Heron, son of Diodoros, his mother being …anion,
2     [and] his wife Iseis daughter of Diodoros, son of Diodoros, her mother being
3     …nion, with me, Didymos, as her guardian, both being of those from
4     the metropolis registered in the quarter of Arab. Concerning the son born of one another (i.e. their common son)
5     Anoubas, who is entering on his fourteenth year in the present 5th year of Antoninus
6     Caesar the lord and is liable to undergo status-scrutiny, we have appended the proofs.
7     I, Didymos, registered myself in the 7th year of the deified Trajan and the 2nd year
8     and the 16th year of the deified Hadrian in the aforesaid quarter
9     of Arab, having co-registered, and my wife Iseis in the
10    2nd year and the 16th year registration, and the one undergoing scrutiny, the son born of one another,
11    [A]noubas, in the 16th year of the deified Hadrian registration; and I too, Iseis,
12    registered myself in the 7th year of the deified Trajan house-by-house census at
13    Tameia, my mother having registered in the quarter of Boutaphion,
14    wherefore we submit (this declaration). Apollonios, former exegetes and former gymnasiarch, through Didas the scribe: I recognize Anoubas
15    son of Didymos, son of Heron, his mother being Iseis. Year 5 of Antoninus
16    Caesar the lord, month Hadrianus 24. I have signed.
