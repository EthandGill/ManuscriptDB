[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Hier[ax], strategos [of the Arsinoite nome], Herakleides division from Ha[tres, son of Sa]tabous, the son of Pan[ephr]emm[is], mother [Se]gathis, from the village of Sokno[pa]iou Nes[os]. I register myself and [my family] for the house-by-house registration of the past [23rd year of the God] Aelius Antoninus, in the house, in which I live, which is in the [village] and is inherited from my mother. And I am the above mentioned Hatr[es, 25 years old], without a distinguishing mark and (I register) my wife [Isa]r[io]n, father unknown, mother [Tanephrem]mis, daughter of P[an]om[i]e[u]s, [13] years old, [without a distinguishing mark]. There belong to her in the village [two] houses and her grandmother’s quarter (inheritance). And (I register) Tap[etsir]is, daughter of Stotoetis, the son of Panephre[m]mis, mother Tapetsiris, 12 years old, without a distinguishing mark. There belongs to Tape[ts]iris two houses and a courtyard and her grandfather’s quarter and here grandmother’s quarter. Therefore I submit this registration. Year 1 of Imperator [Ca]esar Marcus Aurelius A[nt]oninus Augustus [and] Imperator Caesar Lucius Aure[l]ius Verus Augustus, Mesore 4.
