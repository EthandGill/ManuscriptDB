1     To Julius Petronianus the most excellent epistrategos,
2     from Pabous son of Stotoetis son of Panomieus,
3     priest from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos of the
4     Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome, of the Arabotoxotes gate of the same Soknopaiou Nesos.
5     … n accus… [bu]t seeing the fisc
6     being defrauded by Polydeukes, who for a four-year
7     period now, contrary to what is forbidden, has been supervising the aforesaid gate, and by
8     [Harpag]ath[os the] desert-guard, I submitted
9     to the [overseers of the nomarchy] a [c]opy of the autograph
10    registers of Harpagathes that I held, of those (goods) imported through the gate
11    [a]nd exported, [re]questing that the examination of them
12    be [made so as] to recogn[i]ze whether their tolls had been added
13    to the imperial account. And
14    when Polydeukes had recognized (this), having come upon me
15    with others whose names I do not know, with very many
16    blows he assaulted me, and not satisfied,
17    he brought against me a certain Heraklas of the estate sword-bearers, and both of them by force
18    having carried me off brought me into the office
19    of the procurator of the estates and made me,
20    … being k…aios, to be scourged in order that I should hand over to th[em] the register of [Harp]agathes, which
21    became evident to the overseers of the nomarchy and to the beneficiarius who was then over the district
22    … [whence] of necessity I submit (this)
23    and req[ue]st, [i]f it se[e]m good to you, [my lo]rd, to send both
24    Polydeukes and Harpa[gathes before] th[e]
25    most excellent procurator of the lord Caesar, so that
26    I may be able, having made the demonstration before him,
27    to obtain also the benefaction from you. Farewell.
