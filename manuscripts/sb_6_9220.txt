[TRANSLATION]
1     Theocles to Zenon, greetings. You would do well by receiving us [and directing us] to the tax collector Zenon of the κίκιος,
2     namely to be able to give the sixty measures which are owed toward the
3     payment slip, and if not, [at least the] half, thirty [measures, similarly], on the condition [that we receive the payment slip] from Apollonius.
4     Of the quantity, as much as we obtain, or we will pay the price; for we are [being] burdened by the
5     linen workers, not as is proper, [having] accounts rendered against us. [But] we sent previously about this
6     further to Alexandria to Apollonius,
7     requesting, along with the sixty measures
8     which are owed, other two hundred. And I am convinced that speedily the payment slip will come to us.
9     Therefore, foreseeing the safe reception [being certain], I ask you to provide us this need.
10    Farewell. Year 32, Thoth 26.
11    Year 32, Thoth 27. Theocles
12    receive κίκιος. Zenon
13    … κίκιος.
14    [Theocles to] Zenon, greetings. I received the letter in which also you subscribed [the message from the so-and-so about the]
15    [tax collector] Zenon. Since therefore [the linen workers] with difficulty [thus encounter us], [I ask you to] postpone the days, the matter [not being in our control, but in the command of the administrator].
16    [For] we have written to him letter[s] [about the payment slip of the κίκιος, the things] owed [sixty]
17    [measures we request] and [two] hundred [others we] further request. For we have also written to Diotimus about [the requested ...]. But I am convinced that from one of them [the order will come to us on] a certain day. [You would do well therefore, for me]
18    [to request] at least the necessary [amount] given to the linen workers, so that we have [support] until [the letters come from them]
19    [the] letter[s], having received [until] Phaophi, [thirtieth,] [an] order to deliver us or the [price to arrange immediately]
20    [of only] ten measures.

22    [Year 32, Phaophi] 5. Theocles
23    [concerning] [linen workers] to Zenon.
