1     [Gaius Julius Philios to Gaius Julius Eros greeting.] I acknowledge [that] my slave Narcissus has been given to you …
2     … [for a time of one] year from Pharmouti of the present seventeenth year of Caesar, so that he may learn to play [with magadis-pieces] … [four] [arranged and accompanyings two, syringe-] pieces and aulete-service five and the attachments, accompany[ings two, kithara-pieces Sarapiaca accompany][ings two and accompaniments two, in Carian and] [under]perforated accompanyings two, Egyptian teteis accompanyings two, Phrygian-auletes [services] two, in [the left] str[okes]
3     [two; of the wage of the agreed-upon silver Ptolemaic] of one hundred drachmas; to which also you have from me immediately [the] half [of the silver dra]chmas fifty through [the]
4     [of Castor's money-changer's table; but the remain]ing of the silver drachmas fifty I will repay to you after [six] months from … [of the] sa[me] month …
5     … [all the aulos-pipes for the] practices and the exhibitions I myself the master will provide to Narcissus … … seven days
6     … [for the whole time with] me feeding and clothing him; and if he should behave improperly or fall ill, I will provide back to you him remain[ing] after the time [for the] equal days, and doing as an apprentice all things ordered of the art, and I will not separate him from you [within] the time; [regarding] the designated
7     [art the apprentice will be examined by fellow-craft]smen three, whom in common we choose of the same kind having learned the proposed art completely; but if anything

9     Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouti.
