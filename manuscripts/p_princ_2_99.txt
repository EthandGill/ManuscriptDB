[TRANSLATION]
1     from the peace-commissioner Ptolemy
2     to the village-heads of village Philadelphia. At this hour go up to the
3     city because of Piarios the ally. But if they do not send allies
4     against you and [even if] you are not troubled in any way, [still] go up to the city.
5     to the overseer of peace. [Do] not be troubled about you and let [him] stand
6     the chief herald all [things] … concerning the piglet. …
7     to fare well [you]
8     I pray.
