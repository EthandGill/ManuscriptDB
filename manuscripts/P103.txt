FOLIO Recto
13:55 …the carpenter's son? Is not his mother called Mary, and his brothers James and Joses and Simon and Judas?
13:56 And his sisters, are they not all with us? Where then did this man get all these things?

14:3  …[on account of Herodias] the wife of Philip his brother. For John was saying to him:
14:4  "It is not lawful for you to have her."
14:5  And [though] wanting to kill him, he feared the crowd, because they held him as a prophet.
