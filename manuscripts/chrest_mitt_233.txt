[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 6, Mesore 29, in Pathyris, under Ammonios
2     agoranomos. Panobchounis of Totoeus settled
3     a purchase of bare land which is in [the] from the south
4     region of Pathyris, 2 solid cubits, which he mortgaged to Patoutos of Pelaios and to Bokenoupis of Patoutos according to a written agreement
5     of purchase, in trust, before the archive in Pathyris
6     under Heliodoros, agoranomos, in [the] fifth year
7     Mesore 27, of copper, 1 talent, 1000 drachmas, who also being present
8     Patoutos and Bokenoupis, before the archive, acknowledged
9     to have received and not to make claims concerning
10    written through the purchase of all things
11    in no way. Ammonios [has officially acted].
12    settlement of Panobchounis
