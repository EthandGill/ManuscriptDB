[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Zakaon son of Satabous, his mother being Thermoutharion, about 62 years old,
2     with a scar on the right shin, and Ammonios
3     son of Allion, his mother being Thyne, about 40 years old, with a scar on a finger of the left hand, both from the village
4     of Theadelphia, acknowledge that they have received from Flavius Arion, of the praepositi, from hand to hand, in silver
5     of the Augusti's coinage, as capital, talents
6     seventy-eight, as an interest-bearing loan
7     for three months from the current month
8     Thoth, [of which] without delay they will make the repayment within the month [of Choiak]; and if the
9     repayment they do not make by the [appointed time],
10    the interest agreed between them shall continue to accrue, and, when the demand is made, the right of exaction shall be both from us and from
11    all our property, [as if] by
12    legal judgment; this security …
13    which is valid for everyone producing it; [and having been formally questioned]
14    [we ac]knowledged it.
15    In the consulship [of Ianuarinus and] Vet[tius]
16    Iustus, the most illustrious, Thoth 14.
17    We, the Aurelii Sakaon and Ammonios,
18    [have received the] talents of silver,
19    [seventy-eight, and we will repay]
20    [by the appointed time as aforesaid.] …
21    Autograph subscription of Sakaon from the village of Theadelphia …
