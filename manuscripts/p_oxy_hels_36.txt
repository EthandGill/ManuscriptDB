[TRANSLATION]
1     [Dionysia, daughter of Diogenes, Persian of the]
2     [descent, with as my guardian my own]
3     [husband Sarapion son of Soptas,]
4     [to Demetrius, son of Sarapas by adoption, all]
5     [of the people from the city of Oxyrhynchus, greeting.]
6     [I acknowledge that I have received from you, from hand to hand, in]
7     [loan, of silver Imperial coinage eight hundred drachmas of principal,]
8     [to which] nothing at all has been added, at interest
9     of one drachma per mina per month, from this present month Thoth and from Thoth itself,
10    and I will pay you the interest month by month, the trust being reposed in you concerning the matters about which,
11    if I do not bring you documents, and the
12    principal on the thirtieth of Mesore of the coming ninth year of Antoninus and Verus
13    the lords Emperors, without delay. And if I do not pay, I will forfeit it with the addition of one-half, together with the interest at a drachma for the time overrun, the
14    right of execution belonging to you both against me and against
15    all my property, it being lawful for you, Demetrius, of yourself from now, whenever you choose, to attach through
16    the registry office of acquisitions
17    my name, until you recover the principal and the interest. Valid is this note. Year 8
18    of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
19    Antoninus Augustus, victor over Armenia, Media,
20    and Parthia the greatest, and of the Emperor Caesar
21    Lucius Aurelius Verus Augustus, victor over Armenia,
22    Media, Parthia the greatest, Thoth 28. I, Dionysia daughter of Diogenes, have (received) from Demetrius
23    the eight hundred drachmas of principal at interest of one drachma per mina each month from the
24    present month Thoth and from Thoth itself,
25    and I will pay the interest month by month
26    and the principal on the thirtieth of Mesore of the coming ninth year, it being lawful for you to attach
27    my name until you recover it, as aforesaid. I, Heracles son of Heraclius, my mother being Dionysia, wrote on her behalf, since she does not know letters. I, Sarapion son of Soptas, have been registered
28    as of my
29    wife the guardian.
30    I, Demetrius, son of Sarapas by adoption,
31    have lent as aforesaid. Year
32    eighth of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
33    Antoninus Augustus, victor over Armenia, Media,
34    Parthia the greatest, and of the Emperor Caesar
35    Lucius Aurelius Verus Augustus, victor over Armenia,
36    Media, Parthia the greatest, Thoth 28.
