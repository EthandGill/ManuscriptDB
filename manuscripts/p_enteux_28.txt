[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting from Sosigenes, of the people of Theogonis. I am wronged by Hippoitas, of the people of [Berenikis]
2     Thesmophorou. For in the 4th year, Tybi 18, when a certain Demetrios, a Syrian, who happened to be staying …, had laid an information
3     for 154 drachmas 3 obols, and when he had come to Berenikis and been found by Hippoitas the aforesaid, because
4     he was then one of the police, the latter, seizing him, exacted from what had been informed about … [a cup]
5     of bronze and 12 drachmas of copper. And when Demetrios was caught and examined by Herakleides the …,
6     he confessed that he had given to Hippoitas both the cup and the 12 drachmas; whereupon Herakleides ordered him to give back both the cup and the 12 drachmas. The cup he has given back, but the 12 drachmas he has not given back …
7     I beg you therefore, O king, to command Diophanes the strategos, since Hippoitas is no longer …, [to write]
8     to Mikion the epistates to send him before Diophanes and, if it is as I write, to compel him to give back to me the 12 drachmas. For this being done, through you, O king, I shall obtain justice.
9     Farewell.
10    [To Mikion. Having investigated, take care that] he obtains his rights. Year 4, Dios 3, Phamenoth 27.
11    Year 4, Dios 3, Phamenoth 27.
12    Sosigenes against Hippoitas
13    concerning 12 drachmas.
