[TRANSLATION]
1   Herakleides to Hatres
2   his dear(est), greeting.
3   You ought, even without my
4   having written you by Saetos,
5   to have sent up the 20 dr., knowing
6   that I transferred them on the spot
7   to my partners;
8   but you have waited all
9   this time without pay-
10   ing. So, of necessity, to the man
11   who hands you this let-
12   ter, at once
13   pay it, so that you make
14   me too untroubled.
15   See then that you do not do otherwise
16   and make me come
17   to you to have it out
18   with you. For in Pao-
19   mis that time I found you, and
20   though I wished to treat you
21   kindly, you did not
22   stay — by a bad
23   conscience held
24   fast.
