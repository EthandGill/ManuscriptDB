[TRANSLATION]
1   Papirius Apollinarius
2   to Claudius Tiberianus,
3   his most honoured (friend), greetings.
4   Before everything I pray for your
5   health; and I too am well.
6   I make your obeisance
7   every day before
8   the lord Sarapis. You wrote
9   to me saying that Gaius has sold
10   something. So I ask you, brother,
11   to learn what he has sold, and
12   write back to me. And concerning
13   Sextus I heard that he has died.
14   Find out, then, who is his
15   heir and when
16   his will is to be opened.
17   Greet Zotike and Ke-
18   phalon and all his (folk)
19   [and] Ptolemaios. There greet
20   [you] Papiria and Hermes
21   [and] Menouthos and Serapias.
22   To Claudius Tiberianus, veteran, from Pa[pirius Apollinarius].
