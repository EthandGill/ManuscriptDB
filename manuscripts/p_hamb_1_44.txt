[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakleidion [and] Deios and partners, tax-collectors
2     of the silver tax of the village Psenyreus through
3     Herakleidos, manager, to Iulios
4     Serenus from the decadarchs, greetings. I received
5     from you for the tax of year 23, silver
6     twelve drachmas, [which] are 12 drachmas, which I shall record
7     against the public bank and bring to you
8     the public receipt.
9     Year 24 of Marcus Aurelius
10    Severus Antoninus
11    Parthicus Maximus Britannicus
12    Maximus Germanicus Maximus
13    Pius Augustus, Choiac 21.
