r.4    οὐκ ἔλαβα τὸν στατῆρα
r.5    τοῦ μύρου· οὔ μοι εἴρηκε Παράβολος ἀγoράσαι σοι . μὴ οὖν αὐτὰς ἀφῇς ποιεῖν μάχας

[TRANSLATION]
1     Ischyra to P[ar]abolus greetings. I want you to know that Demetrius
2     and Zosima made a quarrel,
3     saying the [woman] of Demetrius that
4     I did not receive the stater
5     of the myrrh; Parabol[us] did not tell me to buy [it] for you. Do not then allow them to make quarrels
6     but [do] what seems [best] to you.
7     Fare well.
