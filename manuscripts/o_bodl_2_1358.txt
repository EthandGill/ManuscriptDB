[TRANSLATION]
1     has been measured into treasury of metropolis, produce of 5th year
2     of Antoninus Caesar [the] lord
3     Pauni 13, name Thaubasthios Papystios
4     on behalf of Agoras, wheat artaba 1/24, and name Senpetechōnsios the elder
5     Petechōnsios and Pamōnthes of Xenōn, on behalf of Agoras
6     wheat artaba 1/12, total: wheat 8 artabas
7     total: wheat 8 artabas. Pthouminis I have signed, wheat 8 artabas.
