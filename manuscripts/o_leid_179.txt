1     He wrote out: Phenoupis Pachompaouonnophrius
2     for the artisan tax of linen-workers pastophori of the fourth year
3     of Vespasian the lord, Mesore
4     30, in silver twelve drachmas, total: 12.
5     Melanthis son of Hermodotos wrote [this].
