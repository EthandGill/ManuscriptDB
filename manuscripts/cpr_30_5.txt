[TRANSLATION]
1     [I] wrote to you move from [the] ninth hour yesterday
2     [that] you be found in [Sinkere] at midnight
3     [and] having passed the things there today about a delay
4     [of the sun] I did not find you. I made clear to you again
5     that you come near to me to Hermoupolis and
6     until [the] sixth hour you did not come. Understand therefore
7     that I speak to you. But prepare!
8     to be loaded the ships sent to you
9     from the illustrated grain and do not send baked bricks
10    [until] when I speak to you nor indeed [should] you release
11    the beams were released in hour six
12    to the worthy of all honor Senouthios, notary, and [secretary]
13    [Taurinos]
