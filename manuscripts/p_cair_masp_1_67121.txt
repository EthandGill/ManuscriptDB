[TRANSLATION]
1     In the [reign] and consulship of our most divine master
2     [Flavius Justin] the eternal Augustus Emperor, in the year
3     eighth, on Thoth the eighteenth [of the] present seventh indiction.
4     Aurelius Isakos son of Ioannes, his mother being Kyra, physician, from the village
5     of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, to Aurelia Tetrompia daughter
6     [of Ioannes], now my wife, [from] the same village, greeting. Since
7     I, Isakos, [took] you into the partnership of marriage upon good hopes of life
8     and for the begetting of children, [thinking] to bring my life
9     [to a peaceful] (close); but now, an evil demon having begrudged
10    [our] cohabitation, we have been parted from one another, so that
11    each man and each woman of us may be joined to another marriage
12    and another spouse, or go up into a monastery or [choose] the solitary [life],
13    with no one hindering, because [we] have been separated, neither
14    to bring suit against one another, [nor] concerning the goods brought into the
15    [marriage], [nor] concerning [the dowry], nor concerning the betrothal-gifts that were assigned,
16    … [nor] concerning expenditures at the time of marriage, nor
17    in a lawcourt or outside a lawcourt; for this reason I have come [to]
18    this dissolution, on account of which I acknowledge that I have no claim
19    against [you] concerning any matter whatsoever. The dissolution shall be valid
20    and [secure]. And being formally questioned I assented to the dissolution. It being clear that the formerly executed written
21    agreements made by me are valid and secure,
22    and (so too) the written … made for me by [you]
23    on account of the betrothal-gifts, according to its force. And upon all these I have acknowledged.
24    I, Aurelius Isakios son of Ioannes, the aforesaid, have set down this
25    dissolution, and everything agrees with me as [stated above].
26    [Aurelius] Komasios son of Phoibammon, I bear witness to this
27    dissolution, having heard it from the maker. Psei… son of Abraamios,
28    I bear witness as stated above. Aurelius Phib son of Isakios, I bear witness to this
29    dissolution, having heard it from the maker.
30    Written through me, Anouphios, with God, notary …
31    A deed of repudiation made by Isakos son of Ioannes, physician, against Tetrompia, [his] wife.
