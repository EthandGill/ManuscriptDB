[TRANSLATION]
1     To the keepers of the property-records of the [Hermopolite nome],
2     from Thatres daughter of Inarous … her mother being Che…, granddaughter of Aphynchis, a Hermopolite woman registered
3     in the quarter of Phrourion East, with my guardian, my own husband
4     Pemous son of Phaneos. I sell to Hermaios son of Achilleus, grandson of
5     Apollonios also called Harpaesion, his mother being Sarapias
6     daughter of Kyon, a Hermopolite registered in the quarter of the City-East,
7     a minor, with his guardian, his kinsman Sarapion son of Achilleus,
8     from what I hold in the property-register, in the village of Magdola
9     of Mire of the Lower Peri-polin (district), in the parts to the south-east,
10    formerly belonging to Penaus son of Menches, a third part
11    of a vacant plot and an entrance and exit, and formerly belonging to Didymos
12    son of Dionysios, a vacant plot, of whatever measurement it may be
13    of those which were brought together into one by re-measurement or …
14    … three and … …
15    … of six hundred drachmas of silver …
16    … together. I request that an order be sent to the secretary of the city
17    to register it under my name; and I swear by the fortune of the Emperor
18    Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus
19    [Dacicus] that the aforesaid [places] belong to me free
20    from every encumbrance up to now. If I swear truly may it be well
21    with me, but if I swear falsely the opposite. In the ninth year of the Emperor
22    [Caesar] Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus
23    [Dacicus], Mesore 21. Thatres,
24    … [with my guardian] my husband Pemous son of Phaneos, have submitted (this).
25    … …
