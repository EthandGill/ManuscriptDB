[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been registered.
2     To Aurelius Sarapion also called Apollonianus, former gymnasiarch, councillor of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, through
3     Aurelius Horion, secretary,
4     and to Aurelia Ptolema also called Magna
5     through her husband Aurelius Aristion
6     son of Diogenes, former agoranomos
7     of the same city,
8     from Aurelius Patas son of Patas,
9     his mother being Thaësis, from the hamlet of Episemos. Of my own free will I undertake to lease for only
10    the present 6th year, from the property belonging to you, according as each one holds his
11    share, near the village of Sko, from the
12    holding of Theodorus and Aulaeus,
13    of forty-four arouras,
14    out of seventy
15    six, after which I leased in wheat
16    a half share, and now the remaining
17    half share that has become green-crops,
18    which is twenty-two arouras, the rent
19    per aroura being … drachmas
20    … ty, free of all
21    risk, and for the irrigations
22    I shall use the waterwheel without rent,
23    the public dues of the land being
24    your responsibility, you being owners of the crops until you recover the rent.
25    The undertaking being confirmed to me,
26    I shall pay the rent in the month
27    Pauni of the present year without delay, the said former lease remaining valid, the execution being made against both me and from
28    all my possessions. The undertaking is valid, and on being formally questioned I gave my assent. Year 6 of Imperator Caesar
29    Marcus Aurelius Severus Alexander
30    Pius Felix Augustus, Choiak 1.
31    I, Aurelius Patas son of Patas,
32    have leased on all the aforesaid terms. I, Aurelius Antoninus
33    son of Pausanias, wrote on his behalf
34    as he does not know letters.
