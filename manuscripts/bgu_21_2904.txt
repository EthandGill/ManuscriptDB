[TRANSLATION]
1     [the man of] the lord Severus — behold, he sent [him] required to go with the letters to his brother and to release the goods
2     [of your brotherhood] … For the rest, for God's sake, in every way you will act and you will release her goods immediately through this man and […]
3     … with the letters; and there is no need [for] the faithful ones at all to go to … [?] … but to Babylon correctly. For in order that [he] may learn that Rhoas, the most renowned
4     [emir] … came to Antinoopolis disgracefully. For the rest, as has been said, there is no need [for] the faithful ones at all to go there, but there is need in every way
5     [to release] the goods today if [he] has everything; you will do and write to me so that I may find to write to Antinoopolis.
