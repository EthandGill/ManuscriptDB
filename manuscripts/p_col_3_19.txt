[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon, greetings, Ktesias. You wrote me so that I give to Aristeas …
2     To Amyntos' wife for the Thesmophoria, choes two. Because Aristeas does not reside, I have given [it] to no one. But I heard that he would be present in a few days.
3     Farewell. Year 29, Dios 2.
4     Year 29. Ktesias.
5     Amyn[tos'] cho[e]s two. To Zenon
