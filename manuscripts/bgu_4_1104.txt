[TRANSLATION]
1   (Sheet.)
2   To P[rotarch]os.
3   [Fro]m Dionysarion daughter of Protarchos, with her guardian, her brother
4   Protarchos, and from Hermione daughter of Hermias, a citizen,
5   [with her g]uardian, her brother's son Hermias, likewise (a citizen). There agrees
6   Dionysarion that void is (the agreement) which the deceased
7   son of this Hermione, Hermias son of Hermias, had brought in, through the surety of Hermi-
8   one, through the same court, in the 21st year of Caesar, Phaophi -
9   the agreement made on account of her deceased
10   son; and that Dionysarion has received back from Hermione,
11   by hand from the house, the dowry which she had brought to Hermias through the surety
12   of Hermione - a dowry of clothing worth two hundred and for-
13   ty silver drachmas, and earrings and a ring ..., and
14   one hundred silver drachmas; and that void is the ... [agree]ment
15   together with all that is indicated by it; and that
16   Dionysarion shall not proceed, nor anyone on her behalf, against Her-
17   mione, nor against the (property) left by the deceased Hermias,
18   neither concerning the dowry nor the cohabitation (marriage),
19   nor concerning anything else at all, written or unwritten,
20   from former times up to the present
21   day. And since Dionysarion is also pregnant,
22   she shall not proceed even concerning the childbirth, since concerning
23   these matters she has been satisfied; and that it is permitted to her ... her-
24   self to expose the infant and to join [another]
25   man; and, apart from the agreed terms remaining valid, [she shall be liable,]
26   if she transgresses, both to the damages and to the fix-
27   ed penalty. We request (registration). Farewell.
28   Year 22 of Caesar, [P]ach[on ..].
29   (2nd hand, docket) ... of those from the Herakleopolite (nome) above Memphis,
30   ...leased ... grain-bearing land, under seals ..., village Machor, from the holding of Zoilos:
31   Atilius ...;
32   Titus Aufidius and Egnatius Festius, of the Quirina (tribe),
33   soldiers of the 22nd legion, 3rd cohort - five hundred drachmas; A...
34   ...doros son of The...; ... of Ptolemaic (silver), interest at two drachmas (per mina?).
