[TRANSLATION]
1     [Antimene]s to Zenon, greetings. If you have been strong, it would be well; and I too am healthy. In the be[ginning indeed]
2     [because the] of Herakleid[o]u son Zenon many times us to have misled and because [Dorida]
3     [to have fallen ill] and not to be able to sail, we were reluctant to write to you, but now know [with Aris][ton we] together-sent us her in the of Zenon ship, and [to Zenon]
4     [we ordered] the whole care of her to do, and we ourselves too agree[d with her]
5     [as much as she asked] us for the voyage.
6     Fare well. Year 28, Hyperberetaio[u] …
7     to Zenon the
8     from Apollonios.
