[TRANSLATION]
1   Through Sarapion. Paid: Harpaesis
2   son of Phenophis, for the trade-tax of the 20th year,
3   which is also the 1st year, of Hadrian Caesar
4   the lord: eight drachmas
5   — total 8 dr. Year 2 of Hadrian the lord,
6   Mecheir 24.
7   (2nd hand) Phamenoth 5, another
8   four drachmas
9   — total 4 dr.
