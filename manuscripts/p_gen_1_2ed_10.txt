[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aurelius] Didymus, veteran, son of Ischyrion,
2     [son of one who had been] a veteran, landholder in the village of Phebeichis of the 11th pagus
3     [of the] Herakleopolite nome above Memphis, to Aurelius Paulus,
4     [veteran,] son of Klemes, landholder in the same
5     [village] of Phebeichis, greeting.
6     [I have leased] to you, from the property belonging to me in the same
7     [Phebei]chis, in the eastern parts of the village,
8     [out of a] three-storey [house] together with a forecourt, held in common with
9     the heirs of Petechon, who had been a councillor of the city of the Naukratites, and the heirs of Anthestios, who had been a soldier,
10    the fourth part that falls to me, for a period of two years, from the
11    [present] month Mesore of the present eighteenth year,
12    at the rent fixed and agreed upon between one another, each year, of one thousand two hundred silver drachmas, the rent being paid each year without falsification
13    [and without] delay. And this lease, written in duplicate
14    and folded at our subscription, let each
15    [hold] a single copy for security, which is valid; and let it [abide] as set forth above, and on being asked the formal question I assented.
16    [In the consulship of] Caecinius Sabinus and Vettius Rufinus,
17    [the most] illustrious men, Mesore 15.
18    [Aureli]us Didymus, veteran, son of Ischyrion,
19    [I have leased] as is set forth above.
