[TRANSLATION]
1     From Kalpurnius Horion of
2     the excellent record-keeper.
3     Sarapion, gymnasium-head, has been sent
4     to gather together with you for the sent-down market to the most noble
5     soldiers according to the command of my lord governor
6     in pigs, as many as you may provide according to the designated amount for each village. These, having gathered them immediately from those having [them], convey with the masters [to be] weighed together with the just [sum], which you will receive from me for the value at
7     [joint valuation], knowing that if any slowness [should occur] concerning this, as [if] having waylaid the military
8     market, you will be sent for the magnitude of it to be punished.
9     to the village-headmen and public [officials] of the
10    [under]written villages.
