[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 12, Tybi 4. [Revenue] has fallen upon the [customs station] in great Diospolis
2     of the circle-tax, for which Hermocles the one conducting [business],
3     to the king from Thoteus of Psemminis and Nikon, who also [is] Petechonis of Athenion, the tax arising into the
4     circle-tax revenue of [arurae] eleven [and a] half eighth, which is
5     in Pesthenmenwfe of the Pathyrite, which [he] purchases from
6     Nikon of the aforementioned according to an Egyptian contract
7     of sale, which was written to him in Tybi of the 12th year,
8     the tax from both [is] eight diobols [and a] dichalkon,
9     gift [of] three obols, bronze-fee [of] four obols.
10    Hermocles, [agent] of Numenius.
