[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy. To Ptolemy, royal secretary, and
2     Apollophanes and Diogenes, topographers
3     and village secretaries:
4     [From Pe]toseirios, son of Petoseirios,
5     [of the] [from Oxy]rhynchus city [district],
6     … as. desiring
7     [from the pre]sent eleventh year of Nero
8     Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
9     Emperor, [I request to register] my son Petechon, not yet being of age, [to be registered]
10    in the same district Egzoeou, so that [he may] learn the bronze-working
11    craft under teacher Heracleides
12    Petosrapis, bronze-worker of the same district,
13    [I request that] this [boy] be registered
14    in the rank of similar [apprentices], as is fitting.
15    Signature of [the] cop[y]. Alexander, secretary, I have signed.
16    Year 11 of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
17    Germanicus Emperor, Mecheir 30.
18    [Another copy]. Apollophanes I have signed. Year 11 of Nero
19    Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
20    Emperor, Mecheir 30.
21    [Another copy]. Diogenes I have signed. Year 11 of Nero Claudius
22    Caesar Augustus Germanicus Emperor
23    Mecheir 30.
24    Petoseiris, son of Petoseirios, I have brought forward
25    the aforementioned copy of the
26    petition, and it is with me
27    the original. Sarapion, [son] of Achilles
28    of Achilles, mother Thaisout,
29    daughter of Demetrius, I wrote on his behalf, [he] not knowing letters. Year
30    twelfth of Nero Claudius
31    Caesar Augustus Germanicus
32    Emperor, Pharmouthi … [Sebast]e.
