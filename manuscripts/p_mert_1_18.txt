[TRANSLATION]
1     … of this let a copy be issued.
2     In the first year of the Emperor Caesar
3     Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus
4     and the Emperor Caesar Lucius
5     Aurelius Verus Augustus, in the month Kaisareios
6     14, in the city of Oxyrhynchos of the Thebaid.
7     Sarapion and Eudaimon acknowledge,
8     both sons of Sarapion son of Ammonios,
9     their mother being Nikarion, and their cousin
10    Ammonios son of Pausanias son of Ammonios,
11    his mother being Emsiouesis, the three having been gymnasiarchs of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, the other two of them, Sarapion
12    and Eudaimon, having been high-priests
13    of the lord Augusti and of all the gods,
14    (acknowledge) to Sarapion, freedman
15    of Sarapion son of Sarapion son of Phanias
16    by adoption son of Kleouchares, from the same city, in the street: that, the acknowledging parties not being able at present to sail down to Alexandria because of the
17    now-approaching harvest and other distractions of life,
18    on this account they have appointed
19    according to this agreement the
20    aforewritten Sarapion, freedman of Sarapion, to sail down to Alexandria and to petition the most illustrious prefect Volusius Maecianus, or also
21    whatever other judge he himself may approve,
22    concerning the matter brought before the same
23    most illustrious prefect at the recently held assize of the
24    nome, the kind … …
