[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Marcianus the most illustrious and of him who shall be declared, Pachon 14, of the 11th indiction beginning the 12th, in the city of Oxyrhynchos.
2     The community of those from the village of Takona of the Oxyrhynchite nome, through us Iohannes son of Patbos and Nilas son of Serenus,
3     [un]dertaking also the consent of the other fellow-villagers, to Aurelius Anouphis son of Theodoros, through you the
4     [father] of him, Th[eodo]ros so[n] of Megalos also called Didymos, from the Ox[yrhynch]ite, n[omikarios], greeting. Of the rotation
5     … so that we should name the nomik[arios acco]rding to the proportion falling to [o]ur village,
6     [be]coming as one and holding to one [o]pinion, we have named you the aforewritten Anouphis, through
7     your father Theodoros, for the twelfth and thirteenth of the indictions, to the same office and
8     charge of the nomi[ka]rios, on condition that you remain and submit to the same charge of the nomikarios and
9     in no way be lacking; and it was agreed and resolved that we, each year, as said,
10    for the twelfth and thirteenth of the indictions provide to you, on account of wages, apart from the so-called auxiliary-aid, twenty-six gold solidi, you nevertheless answering to the public authorities and whatever is
11    incumbent on the nomik[ar]ios to do, and to render every response on our behalf according to the part falling
12    to us, and in no way be lacking. And for your security we have made you this
13    nomination, which being valid was written singly, and on being questioned we acknowledged. The Aurelii Iohannes
14    son of Patbos and Neilas son of Serenus, the aforewritten, undertaking also th[e] consent of t[he ot]her fellow-villagers,
15    have made the nomination of the nomikarios, and all the things written agree with us
16    as stated above. I, Aurelius Germanos son of Iohannes, having been requested, wrote on their behalf, they not knowing letters.
17    Nomination of the nomikarios by those from the village of Takona.
