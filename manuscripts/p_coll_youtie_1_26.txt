[TRANSLATION]
1     To Pantonymos, strategos of the Apollonopolite (nome) of the Seven Villages,
2     from the undersigned cultivators. We additionally require seed-loans from the produce of the 19th year, for the sowing of the present
3     20th year of Antoninus Caesar the lord, for the royal land which we cultivate,
4     not less than 2 artabas, near the scribal-office of the metropolis. These are the names:
5     The sons of Peteesis: Pachoumis son of Peteesis the elder, and Psenpachoumis son of Psennesis
6     son of Pachoumis, each at 1/3 1/12 of an aroura ¼, of wheat artaba ¼. Kollouthos son of Pachoumis son of Peteesis, through
7     his sons, each at 1/3 1/12 of an aroura ¼ 1/64, of wheat artaba ¼ 1/48. Pseathyris son of Peteesis the elder,
8     son of Pachoumis, through the heirs, each at 1/3 1/12 of an aroura ½ 1/8 1/64, of wheat artaba ½ 1/8 1/48. Psenanou(phis)
9     son of Pachoumis son of Pa…and the sons of Phatres his brother, each at 1/3 1/12 of an aroura ½, of wheat artaba ½.
10    The sons of Alitaios son of Peteesis, each at 1/3 1/12 of an aroura ½ 1/8, of wheat artaba ½ 1/8. Phatres and Onnophris, sons of Pachoumis son of Pachombekis, each at 1/3 1/12 of an aroura ½ 1/8, of wheat artaba ½ 1/8. The sons of Sarapas
11    son of Heraklon, each at 1/3 1/12 of an aroura ¼, of wheat artaba ¼. Pachoumis also called Orsenouphis son of Pseathyris
12    each at 2/12 of an aroura ½, of wheat artaba ½. Senpichis son of Petosiris also called Psenmou(this), each at 1/3 1/12
13    of an aroura ¼, of wheat artaba ¼. The sons of Onno(phris?) son of Sisois, each at 1/3 1/12 of an aroura 1/8 1/32, of wheat artaba 1/8 1/24. Peteesis
14    son of Pacho…the younger, and Peteesipos his brother, each at 1/3 1/12 of an aroura ¼ 1/16 1/64, of wheat artaba 1/3 1/12 1/48.
15    The sum total comes to: arouras 5 1/64, of wheat artabas 5 1/48, on condition that we shall measure out the equal amounts from the new
16    produce together with the rents of the land, with all the (charges) that will be additionally registered.
