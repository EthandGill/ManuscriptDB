[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dionysodoros, strategos,
2     from Setabous son of Ergeus the younger, of those from Soknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleides division.
3     I lent in service to Harpagathes son of Panephrommis, of those from the same village, by a contract of loan of silver, 325 drachmas
4     at the agreed interest, Harpagathes having acknowledged according to the contract of the loan that he would not
5     lay hands on the things given to him from the temple, the … mortgaged and benevolent
6     allowances, unless he should first pay back to me the loan and the interest. But in the present 1st year
7     of Tiberius Caesar, Harpagathes, with all litigiousness, being unwilling to abide by the
8     just terms determined according to the contract of the loan, but attempting to snatch away the things falling
9     to him from the temple, the mortgaged and benevolent (allowances), approached the strategos demanding that I be deprived; and I demand, having subjoined the copy of the contract of the loan, if it seem good, that you order it to be written to the leader of the priests
10    of the village and to the other chief men, as is fitting, to pay back to me the things owed by
11    Harpagathes out of the share of the communal benevolences falling to him, so that I may be benefited by you. Farewell.
12    Year forty-one of the rule of Caesar, son of the god, month Phamenoth …, from Satabous son of Ergeus the younger, of those from the island, of the priests of Soknopaios, to Harpagathes son of Panephrommis …
