[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the lord [a]nd master Jesus
2     Christ our God and Sa[v]iour,
3     in the reign of our most divine master
4     Flavius Mauricius Tiberius, the eternal Augustus
5     Emperor, in the twentieth year, Pachon the twenty-eighth,
6     at the beginning of the sixth indiction, in Hermopolis of the Thebaid.
7     The Aurelii John son of Phoibammon, by
8     his mother Stephanous, and Menas and Psas,
9     my sons, conch-workers, from the city of the Hermopolites,
10    to Kyros the most God-loving presbyter and master,
11    tow-worker, son of Victor, from the same
12    city, greeting. We acknowledge, indivisibly,
13    that we have had and received from him by way of
14    advance for our conch-working
15    craft five gold solidi, less
16    six carats each, by the standard of Hermopolis,
17    total 5 gold solidi less 30 carats, on condition that we remain
18    with you and attend at your workshop
19    and work for you at our conch-working
20    craft at the task of the present sixth indiction,
21    from the beginning of the craft until its conclusion,
22    and that we work conch for you from two hundred
23    twenty-five small bundles for each
24    one solidus less six carats, we however
25    providing the tools for these, without any
26    delay or dispute or trial or lawsuit,
27    and that we cannot withdraw from working
28    for you until the conclusion of your craft; and if we
29    do this we shall pay, by way of penalty,
30    for the one solidus less six carats, six
31    carats, in addition to the repayment of the same advance,
32    receiving however from you on one day
33    per week two folles for each
34    small bundle, at our own risk and from the resource of
35    our entire substance. The present bond
36    is valid and secure, and on being formally questioned we acknowledged it.
37    The Aurelii John son of Phoibammon and Menas
38    and Psas, my sons, the aforesaid, have made this
39    bond as is set forth above. I, Aurelius John son of Theodoros,
40    from Hermopolis, having been requested, wrote on their behalf, they being unlettered.
41    Kolluthos son of Victor, with God.
42    … [ as is set fo]rth above …
43    Acknowledgement of advance of the 6th indiction, of 5 gold solidi less 30 carats, made on behalf of John son of Phoibammon and Menas a[nd Psas]
44    from Hermopolis.
