[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Pale and Atammon and their partners,
2     receivers of the harbor of Leukogion,
3     to Aurelius Pelenios and Selpous
4     and Isidoros and Heron and Ammonas
5     from the village of Karanis, greeting.
6     We have received from you at the same
7     harbor of Leukogion, on account of the sowing of the twentieth year, of wheat with the one-hundredths,
8     ten — one hundred and thirty
9     five — artabas, 135 artabas only. No other
10    receipt being produced. Aurelius
11    Atammon, I wrote the whole.
12    In the consulship of our masters,
13    our Constantine
14    and Licinius, the Augusti,
15    for the 2nd time, Hathyr 8.
16    In the consulship of our masters Constantine Augustus for the 5th time
17    and Licinius the most illustrious Caesar for the 1st time,
18    Choiak 24. Aurelius Harpokras and Sarapion
19    and Apollonios and Syrion, collectors of the city,
20    to Aurelius Heron and Isidoros, greeting.
21    We have received from you for the sowing
22    of the treasury lands, of the eighth indiction, of wheat
23    six and a half artabas, 6½ artabas only. And
24    on the 25th likewise the same men, for the same
25    indiction, of the sowing of the treasury lands,
26    of wheat half an artaba, ½ artaba only, no other receipt for seven artabas of wheat being produced.
27    Sarapion, I have signed.
28    Receipt for grain of the sowing of the treasury lands. …
