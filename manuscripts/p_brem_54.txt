[TRANSLATION]
1     Castor to Apollonius the
2     brother, greetings.
3     As soon as I went up from
4     the harvest I approached the
5     general and reminded him concerning our request
6     and gratitude to him. For he did
7     although not having the custom [regarding] the
8     others to do the same. But I submitted to [you] also the copy of the memorandum,
9     so that you might know. All is well with our common
10    children with the
11    mothers. Greet all the friends. I pray that you fare well,
12    lord brother.
13    Farewell.
14    To Apollonius the brother
