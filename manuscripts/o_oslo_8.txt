[TRANSLATION]
1     19 year of the census of Hadrian, has written out
2     Apollos of Aretion to Didymos
3     And partners, collectors of money-taxes of village Teptyneis
4     Of the census of the same year, on account
5     Drachmas twenty-eight, making drachmas 28.
