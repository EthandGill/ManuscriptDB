1     I Frange
2     greeting embracing
3     of my beloved of brother
4     David and the widow I embrace also
5     [those who are] my sons I make haste
6     for them that they did not hear a word of these
7     there the one I went out through [your] your mother I embraced until the day
8     of the hours into the vessel
9     of the oil I took you [for them]
10    behold out of that you take him to me
11    I embrace
12    to the heart
