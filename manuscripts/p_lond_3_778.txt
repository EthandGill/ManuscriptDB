[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign and consulship of our most divine and most pious master
2     Flavius Justinus the eternal [Au]gustus and Imperator, year 3, Mesore 4
3     of the … indiction, in the 2nd reckoning (?), in the city of Oxyrhynchus.
4     To Flavius Apio[n the] all-praiseworthy and most magnificent, of the ex-consuls
5     ordinary and patrician, landlord here also in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites
6     through Menas his servant, who asks (the formal question) and provides for his own
7     master, the same all-praiseworthy man, the right of action and surety: Georgios
8     deacon, son of Apa Horos who was himself also a deacon, of (his) mother Sara, hailing
9     from the estate of Meskanounis of the Oxyrhynchite nome belonging to your
10    magnificence, a registered tenant-farmer of it (the estate), greeting. I acknowledge by willing intention
11    and self-chosen purpose, swearing the divine and revered oath, that I stand surety
12    and undertake (the responsibility) towards your magnificence through those properly belonging to it,
13    (for) Aurelius Onnophris, son of Solomon, of (his) mother …, hailing from the hamlet
14    of Megalon Chorion (Great Estate) of the same nome, himself too a registered field-guard of it (the estate),
15    on condition that he shall continuously [rem]ain and pass his time in the same estate (of yours)
16    with all quietness and fitting conduct, and answering for
17    [all matters concerning] the person of him, that is, the status [of the] registered tenant.
18    A surety made by Georgios deacon, son of Apa Horos.
