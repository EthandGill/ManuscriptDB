[TRANSLATION]
1     In the seventeenth year of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, in the month Xandikos, Mecheir 9, in Karanis of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome. Valeria Diodora, daughter of Gaius Valerius Heraklianos, about 60 years old, with a scar on her right calf and a mole beside her nose on the left, with her guardian her own husband Limnaios son of Petheus, son of Hatres, his mother being Sambous, from the village of Karanis, about 68 years old, with a scar on his lower lip and on his left shin, acknowledges to Gaius [Julius]
2     Niger, discharged from cavalry service from the veteran Gallic squadron, about 47 years old, with a scar on his forehead on the left, that she, the acknowledging Valeria Diodora, has sold to him, so that to him and his descendants and those who shall take over from them it belongs by transfer of title from now
3     [for the] whole time, the house belonging to her, her maternal inheritance from Menoukia Thermoutharion, in the village of Karanis, and two courtyards, of which the neighbors, as they dictated by agreement, are: on the south, of the acknowledging Valeria Diodora a dovecote and over a certain part an entrance
4     [and exit; on the n]orth, the house of Petheus son of Heras and of Heras son of Petheus; on the west, the house of the same acknowledging Valeria Diodora; on the east, the plots of Petheus son of Heras and over a certain part an entrance and exit; on the existing entrances and exits and foundations and walls and the other rights with which they have been held until now, all of them; and that the acknowledging Valeria Diodora has received from Julius Niger the whole agreed price in full, eight hundred drachmas of silver, immediately, hand to hand, out of the house; and that she shall guarantee, both she the acknowledging Valeria Diodora and her heirs to Julius Niger and his heirs, the house sold to him as aforesaid and the two courtyards with every guarantee, which she shall furnish free from claim and unpledged and unmortgaged
5     [and clear] from all public debts from the former times until the present day, and from private claims and every dispute for all time, and that no one shall hinder Julius Niger nor his heirs
6     [in being mas]ters of what has been sold to him as aforesaid, entering and going out and dwelling and lodging others and carrying the rents from it to their own profit and selling and mortgaging to others and managing concerning it
7     [in whatever] manner they choose, without hindrance; and that against these things neither the acknowledging Valeria Diodora nor her heirs shall make complaint nor shall complain nor proceed in any way; but if she should proceed or complain or fail to guarantee, the
8     [proceeding or] complaint shall be void, and moreover she shall pay in addition both the price she received doubled and the taxes and the expenses doubled and as a penalty four hundred drachmas of silver and to the treasury an equal sum, apart from the aforesaid and agreed terms remaining valid; and whenever Julius Niger wishes, he shall register the aforesaid house and two courtyards through the property-record office from a copy of this agreement, because for the present he has not wished to. Subscriber [ ]. Valeria Diodora
9     [daughter of G]aius Valerius Heraklianos, with my guardian the same husband Limnaios son of Petheus, son of Hatres, his mother being Sambous, from the village of Karanis, I acknowledge that I have sold to Gaius Julius Niger, veteran, the house belonging to me, my maternal inheritance from Minoukia Thermoutharion, in the village of Karanis, and two courtyards, of which the neighbors are stated above, and I have received from Julius Niger the whole agreed price
10    [in full], eight hundred drachmas of silver, immediately, hand to hand, out of the house, and I shall guarantee to Julius Niger the aforesaid
11    house and two courtyards with every guarantee as aforesaid. Limnaios son of Petheus
12    [I wrote] on behalf of my wife since she writes slowly. Valeria Diodora daughter of Gaius Valerius Heraklianos, I acknowledge that I have sold to Julius Niger the aforesaid house and two courtyards, and I have received the price, eight hundred drachmas of silver, and I shall guarantee as aforesaid. Gaius Julius Niger, discharged from cavalry
13    [serv]ice from the veteran Gallic squadron, the agreement has been made out to me as aforesaid.
14    It has been registered through the record-office of Karanis.
