r.2    ὑπὲρ χωματικοῦ Χάρακος α ἔτους ἓξ τετρώβολον βαλανευτικοῦ ὀβολοὺς δ 𐅵 γίνονται ζ ὀβολοὶ β 𐅵 καὶ προσδιαγραφόμενα.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been charged: Onnophris of Kemoitos
2     for the dyke-tax of Charakos, 1st year: six [drachmas and a] four-obol piece, bathing-tax: four-and-a-half obols, totaling seven drachmas [and] two-and-a-half obols, and are additionally charged.
3     Of the 2nd year of Servius Galba Emperor
4     of the month Neos Sebastos 3. Ptolemaios [I] have signed.
