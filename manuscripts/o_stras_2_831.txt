[TRANSLATION]
1     Phthoum and associates, tax-collectors of Ophieion
2     to Psenemoneis, [son] of Psenchonsios. We have received for
3     the poll-tax, seven worn drachmas, eight,
4     making drachmas eight. Of the year seven of Hadrian Caesar
5     our lord, [month of] Phamenoth nine.
6     Apion, [I] have sealed [this]. Other [receipts]: Pharmouthi twenty-seven, for the poll-tax
7     drachmas two [and] a tetrobol, making drachmas two [and] a tetrobol, and for the bath-tax
8     of Notus, drachma one [and] a half-obol, making for the bath-tax drachma one [and] a half-obol.
9     Of the year seven of Hadrian our lord, [month of] Pharmouthi twenty-seven.
