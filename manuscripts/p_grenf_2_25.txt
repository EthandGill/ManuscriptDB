[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 14 which is also 11, Mesore 29, in Pathyris, under Hermias
2     who is in the place of Paniskos the agoranomos.
3     An agreement which, voluntarily consenting, they made between
4     themselves, by which agrees
5     Nechthanoupis son of Patseous, a Persian, about 50 years old, that he has ceded to Petearsemtheus son of Panobchounis and to his
6     brothers Petesouchos and Phagonis and Psennesis,
7     from the parcel of grain-bearing land in the plain about Pathyris
8     in the part toward the north, which he bought from
9     Paous son of Horos in the 11th which is also the 8th year, one
10    and three-quarters arouras, according to an Egyptian deed of sale which he made through Espnouthis, an Egyptian sole-scribe of Crocodilopolis, to Tnepheros the wife of Nechthanoupis
11    in exchange for the one and three-quarters arouras ceded in the
12    upper parcel by you; and that neither Nechthanoupis nor anyone else
13    of those acting for him shall proceed against Petearsemtheus
14    and his brothers nor anyone else of those acting for them concerning the things ceded above. And if (he does),
15    let the proceeding of him who comes against (them) be invalid, and
16    in addition let the one who has come against (them) forthwith forfeit as penalty ten talents of copper and, sacred to the sovereigns, two hundred drachmas of stamped silver, and none the less
17    let it be compulsory for them to act according to [the] things written above.
18    Hermias who is in the place of Paniskos, I have transacted (this).
