[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Olympodoros also called Asynkritios, praepositus
2     of the first pagus of the Hermopolite nome,
3     from Aurelius Apollon son of Horion, registrar (gnoster) of the village
4     of Pois of the same pagus, of the first indiction. Since you are inquiring who might be the cultivators of the unattached holdings in the same indiction
5     so that through them the contribution may be made of the public
6     dues, necessarily having compiled (it) by register
7     with all truth, I submit (it) to your care,
8     swearing the divine and venerable oath
9     of our masters the Augusti who conquer all things,
10    that those registered below are cultivators and that nothing
11    is being charged against anyone; or, if I have lied, I shall be liable
12    to the divine oath and to the peril concerning this; and on being formally questioned I acknowledged. It is as follows:
13    Names: the heirs of Pinoution, of wine 234 xestai
14    of chaff, 400 litrai, through Isidoros son of Sarapion from
15    the Hermopolite nome, being …
16    … on, of chaff 480 litrai, through
17    Athenodoros and Horion, landholders
18    … of Olympios son of Alexandros, of chaff 120 litrai
19    … unsown (?)
20    after the consulship of the Flavii Amantius and Albinus, the most illustrious, Pachon 10. Aurelius Apollon …
21    … swore the divine oath as is set forth.
