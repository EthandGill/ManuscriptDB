[TRANSLATION]
1     Syros to Heroninos
2     the dearest, greetings.
3     Give to Pala, manager of Philoteridos, for the expenses beside him, of which
4     he will give account, wine from
5     the readier [wines]
6     single-measures fifty
7     taking from him
8     records of the receipt, and the
9     transportation of them
10    let it come about from the animals beside you. Be well, you I wish
11    dearest
12    [Year] 3, Choiach 1
