[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 28, Epeiph 26: there has measured in,
2     for the crown(-tax) of the katoikoi: Harmais (son of) Paniskos
3     and (his) brothers, of wheat five and a half, a third,
4     a twelfth, total 5½ ⅓ 1/12; Mesore 16, five and a half;
5     year 29, Thoth 24, two and a half, a third; Phaophi 24,
6     two, a third; (the) epagomenal (days), 1½ ⅓ 1/12,
7     total 19½ ⅓ 1/12.
