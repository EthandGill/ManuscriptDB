[TRANSLATION]
1     … [not] be exempt, because the land
2     of Apollonius we farm. Well then
3     you would do having written to Bubalos and Spendates concerning the farmer, so that he might be released until the tax collectors
4     arrive, so that the land might [be grazed]. And I will be present with speed to you. And I have also subscribed of the letter from Apollonius
5     the copies. Apollonius to Thrasion Paramonios greetings. The
6     farmers those in Tapteia do not [trouble] concerning the salt tax.
7     Be well.
8     Year 30, Peritios
9     Intercalary, Mecheir 23.
10    And also Patroklos brings two wild birds,
11    Eggs, geese 6.
12    [Zenon].
