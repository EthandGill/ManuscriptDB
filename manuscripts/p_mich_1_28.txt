[TRANSLATION]
1     Sosos to Zenon, greeting. I received
2     the letter from you, in which you wrote to me
3     to leave behind, from what is in the
4     boat, 100 artabas of wheat, and to dispose of the rest
5     for as high a price as we can. Now concerning
6     the leaving behind of the grain, know
7     that it is no longer possible. For, not knowing that
8     you would have need, we sold off all the
9     wheat that was at the harbour by the city of Aphrodite,
10    through Ptolemaios, the agent of
11    Archibiades the overseer, at 7 (drachmas) of gold,
12    241 artabas, and we gave in addition to the buyers,
13    in place of the cost incurred,
14    3 artabas to the 100. And Charmos reported to me
15    to give to Pyrrhichos money for hides, so that
16    he might buy from Herakleopolis. Know therefore
17    that there is not with us so much money
18    as to be enough to make up for Pyrrhichos. For there is still owing in Ptolemais, from the
19    price of the grain — concerning which I am also conveying a letter to you
20    of receipt, so as to collect it on the 10th
21    of Mecheir — 288 drachmas, and with me there are
22    400 drachmas. You would do well, then, to write to me
23    from where he must take (money) to add to it,
24    and whether we should send Styrax along too,
25    so that we may keep track of the prices;
26    for we know him to be trustworthy
27    in such transactions. We have brought down also all the little (jars of) wine that there are
28    in Ptolemais. Send me word from where one must
29    take grain and how much to give to Ammonios
30    the bread-maker, so that fine flour may be prepared.
31    We have also given to Pyrrhichos for travelling-expenses 10 drachmas.
32    Farewell. Year 29, Mecheir 5.
33    Year 29, Peritios intercalary 6. To Zenon.
34    From Sosos, [whether he should send]
35    Styrax along with Pyrrhichos.
