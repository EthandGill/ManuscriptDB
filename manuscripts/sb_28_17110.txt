[TRANSLATION]
1     Poseidonios to Kalpournios the
2     brother, greetings. Before
3     all things I pray that you
4     are well, and the obeisance
5     of you I make before the gods here. I wish you to know, brother, that because I have nothing for
6     expense, I have not come to
7     you; I shall come at an opportune time; for I long to see you.
8     Do not be slow to write to me
9     concerning the safety
10    of yours. I greet
11    those who love
12    you all.
13    Fare well.
