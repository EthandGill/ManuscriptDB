1     For the dromos, 625 artabas, those in copper-rate — how many at 28?

3     onto the [28] of the difference; they come to 1 myriad, 7500; of these
4     the hundredth comes to 175; add these to the 6[25] artabas
5     they come to, in copper-rate, [800 artabas]; it has been shown.

7     these multiply by the 28 of the difference
8     they come to 175; add these to the [625 artabas, they come to 800]
9     and thus it has been shown.
