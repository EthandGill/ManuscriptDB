[TRANSLATION]
1     To Kallistratus, strategos of Arsinoe [Polemon's] meris,
2     [From] Sabinus also called Ninnus, of Ptolemaeus.
3     A dispute [having arisen] with me and my family members and
4     Posus and [Cronion] having become guardians
5     of the [possessions] of Patron Geminus
6     having died, and of guardians and slaves
7     of Paulinus, a [coastguard], under Gellius Bassus,
8     the most honored epistrategos, and the
9     matter having been referred to the mightiest hegemon Petronius Mamertinus,
10    I request through one [of the] servants with you to deliver a copy of this decision-document equally to
11    both Posus and Cronion, in order that they may know they ought
12    to appear before the most sacred [seat where] the [examination] of the nome or [judgment] may be conducted towards the
13    [of the] [matter] for determination. Heracles having been assigned to
14    the lot of servants I delivered the aforementioned [matter] [adjudged] to me [being present with] Sabinus in the presence as is fitting.
15    Year 19 of Hadrian Caesar the lord, Mecheir 3.
