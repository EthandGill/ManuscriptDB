[TRANSLATION]
1     643 (chi-mu-gamma).
2     [In the reign of our most divine] master Flavius Justinian, the eternal Augustus, [Im]perator,
3     [in the twenty-ninth year], af[t]er the consulship of Flavius B[a]silius the most glorious, the thirteenth year,
4     [Pa]chon, on the eighth, at the beginning of the fourth indiction.
5     [To Fla]vius Dioskoros, son of Apollos son of Dioskoros of blessed memory, the most admir[able],
6     [fro]m the village of A[ph]rodite of the Ant[aio]polite nome — I, Aurelius Georgios, son of Psaios son of Pha[ro]ous,
7     [my mo]th[er being Th]eonoe, from the village of Psinabla of the Pano[p]olite nome, being a shepherd,
8     [being one of the h]eirs of Psaios son of Besios from the same [vi]llage of Psinabla, greeting. I acknowledge willingly and of my own accord
9     that I [have le]ased from your admirableness for a one-year period, the crops [n]ow being of the coming fourth indiction, with God, the patrimonial flocks belonging to you, of the
10    [who]le farmstead of however many arourai it is, with all their rights, called Piah Pall …
11    [of the holding of Aka]nthon of the late Elias, ex-councillor, and of the whole farmstead called … me
12    … of it, of the arourai according to the boundaries of the heirs of (the man) of glorious memory, Ko…tos,
13    … [s]ituated in the southern plain of the same village of Aphrodite, called of Hierados of Ananeias,
14    … the shepherd, on the south of Psilampon — on condition that I am to have under me the aforesaid flocks of the whole
15    arourai of the two farmsteads with all their rights according to the boundaries, for pasture
16    and for grazing of my herds, and to give you on account of rent, that is, hire, of the annual pasturage,
17    eighteen keratia of gold by the gold-weighing standard of the same village of Aphrodite. The lease-acknowledgment shall be valid
18    [and] secure, on the signature of him who signs on my behalf and of the following customary witnesses;
19    … and on all (points), being formally questioned, I [a]cknowledged. I, Aurelius Georgios son of Psaios, shepherd
20    [from] Psinab[la, the] aforesaid, have lea[s]ed as is set forth. I, Dioskoros son of Josephios, presbyter,
21    [having been asked], wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters. I, Aureliu[s] Phoibam[m]on
22    A … bear witness to the lease, having heard from the contracting party.
23    Written by me, Hermauos the notary.
