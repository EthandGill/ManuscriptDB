[TRANSLATION]
1   To Iulius Iulianus, the most excellent
2   epistrategos,
3   from Senphibis daughter of Thortaios, with her guardian,
4   her son Psais the elder, son of Lemos,
5   from the village of Ibion Nemna of the
6   Lower Apollonopolite district.
7   I bought, my lord, a long time ago,
8   from Apollonios and Didymos,
9   both sons of Origenes, from Lyco-
10   polis, five and a quarter arouras
11   of grain-land in the holding called Diagraphe,
12   in the fields of the village of Krekis in the
13   Antaiopolite nome. But a certain Artemi-
14   doros, a clerk of the village-scribe
15   of Krekis, I know not how,
16   registered me for more than
17   my holding, by a whole aroura
18   and more, each year; and from this
19   I suffer no small loss.
20   Therefore, since the man's
21   pressure upon me is compelling, and I am in danger
22   of abandoning my own (land),
23   I take refuge with you, my lord and
24   helper of all, and I request, if it
25   seem good to you, that you order the
26   strategos of the nome to see that the proper re-
27   gistration of the arouras I hold, according to the
28   title-deeds I possess, be
29   made, and that I be not wrongly
30   registered by the village-
31   scribe; for last year too, besides
32   these, he wrongly registered me for other (land),
33   so that I may obtain redress.
34   Senphibis daughter of Thortaios,
35   with my guardian Psais the elder, son of Lamos,
36   have submitted (this).
37   (Endorsement:) by hand.
38   (Of the) Antaiopolite (nome).
39   She complains against the clerk of the village-scribe
40   concerning a wrongful registration;
41   she requests that the proper
42   registration be made.
43   (Subscription:) nothing standing in the way.
