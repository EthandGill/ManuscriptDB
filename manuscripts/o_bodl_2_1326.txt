[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis for the produce of the 22nd year
2     Of Hadrian Caesar the lord, Pauni 24
3     For Agoras, named Pekysios [son] of Psenmonthus
4     Wheat three artabae, make wheat 3 artabae. Gaius has been marked.
