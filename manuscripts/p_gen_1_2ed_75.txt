[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapammon to Nepotion
2     to [the] father, greetings very much.
3     I sent to you Drakon and Melan, hired-workers of my lord Ambrosios, for they are cobblers,
4     so that you might work as a cobbler with them
5     until they purchase horns of thorn-plants.
6     You will therefore do well to watch over them, so that
7     no one might do to them
8     harm, and provide them a place where they might stay.
9     I pray that you are in good health,
10    father, [for] many
11    years.
12    To Aurelius Nepotion from Sarapammon
