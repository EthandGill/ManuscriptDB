[TRANSLATION]
1     Theon to Sambas the oil-maker: two and a half
2     xestai of oil provide to the 9 stone-carriers
3     and one carpenter, for 4 days, departing
4     to (the) Skelos to take up the doors
5     and load the slabs, of oil, 2½ xestai only.
6     Epagomenal (days), 14th indiction. (hand 2) † I have signed for two
7     and a half xestai of oil only †. (verso)
8     † Epagomenal (days), 14th indiction.
