# HGV-EN: ‘To . . . and his associates appointed to receive and forward the accounts concerning the procurator of the privy purse, from Psenouris, collector of money-taxes in the village of Tamauonesus in the Memphite nome. I report to you that nothing has been paid to me on behalf of the revenues of the privy purse from Thoth to Epeiph inclusive, eleven months, of the . . . year of Antoninus Caesar the lord, so that the return has been presented to Archibius also called Theon, eclogistes of the Memphite nome, and I have obtained the corresponding receipt.’ Date.
