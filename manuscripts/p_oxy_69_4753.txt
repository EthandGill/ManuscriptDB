[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Antonius Marcellinus and Petronius
2     Probinus, the most illustrious, Phaophi 22.
3     To Gaius Julius Leukadios son of Herakleianus, through
4     Flavius Eusebius, of the curators, guardian,
5     from the Aurelii Horos son of Panechotes and Papontheus son of …ios, both from the farmstead of Pankyleus. Willingly we undertake to lease for the present [36th] and 18th and 9th year only, from
6     the property belonging to you around Kerkemounis, from the
7     allotment of Philon four arouras, and from the allotment of Stra…on
8     … other four arouras
9     and a half, together 8½ arouras, so as … with wheat (and) grass
10    on the half-share, and to pay you as rent for the wheat half in wheat forty-four artabas, and for the
11    grass half, for each aroura, at two artabas of barley, and the throwing-up of the required earthworks (naubia) I shall perform. The rent is free of all risk, the public dues of the land being (chargeable) to you the landholder, who is master of the crops until you receive the rent. And, the lease being confirmed to me, of necessity I shall pay the rent from the new produce,
12    the wheat unmixed with barley and the barley sifted, by the tenth measure of the estate, four choinikes being measured out in addition for each artaba, in the month Epeiph of the same year without delay,
13    you having the right of execution against me as is fitting.
14    The lease is valid, and, on being formally questioned, I gave my assent.
15    I, Aurelius Horos son of Panechotes, have leased the land and shall pay all that is owed in full as aforesaid. I, Aurelius
16    Fronton, wrote on his behalf, he saying
17    that he does not know letters.
