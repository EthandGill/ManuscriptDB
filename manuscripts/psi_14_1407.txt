[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 29th year of Marcus Aurelius
2     Commodus Antoninus Caesar
3     the lord, Epeiph 28. Harpalos [son] of Orseus
4     and partners, grain-collectors of village Talei, we have measured out from
5     the products of the same year to Chairemon [son] of Petsitheos through Protarches
6     resident-holders, artabae 3 and 1/6, with allowance for measure of artabe 1/2 and 24 [of] grape refuse, artabe 1/3, with allowance for measure
7     of artabe 1/8, [making] artabae 4 and 1/2. Of Theogonidos, the same through the
8     same resident-holders, artabae 2 and 1/8, with allowance for measure of artabe 1/3 and 24, [making] artabae 2 and 1/2.
9     [Making] of the total wheat by public measure, sifted, …
10    artabae seven, [making] artabae 7.
