1     [Tanommieus daughter of Hergeus, her mother being …eus, I acknowledge that I have sold] to Satabous and Tesenouphis, both daughters of Hergeus, their mother being Tan[ephry]mis, the three-storeyed house belonging to me and all the appurtenances in Soknopaiou Nesos of the Herakleides division, of which the neighbours of the whole house are: on the south and east a royal street, on the north the house of Paesis son of Horos which his children control, on the west the house of Thases daughter of Horos; and I have received the whole agreed price
