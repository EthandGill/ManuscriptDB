[TRANSLATION]
1     of the sitologos of the middle toparchy
2     … of the … places, the (grain) ordered by you and by Horion, royal
3     scribe, from the aforesaid
4     granary [to the harbor] of Terythis on the great river, by the consignment-order of the most excellent procurator of Neapolis, of wheat
5     [of the produce of the past] 18th [year,] pure
6     [unadulterated, free of clods,] free of barley, unbeaten, sifted, by the public half-artaba measure, by the measuring ordered, the officials in charge of the embarkation following along,
7     all those appointed, together with the one-percent
8     and the half-artaba surcharge, 2662 artabas
9     total … two thousand six hundred sixty-two artabas, which I will also bring down to Alexandria and deliver into the
10    administration at Neapolis soundly and
11    faithfully, free of all maritime fraud.
12    Valid is the receipt, written in triplicate, to you the
13    strategos in duplicate, and to the sitologos a single copy.
14    Year 19 of the Imperators Caesars Lucius
15    Septimius Severus Pertinax, conqueror of Arabia,
16    conqueror of Adiabene, greatest conqueror of Parthia, of Britain
17    greatest, and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
18    and Publius Septimius Geta, of Britain,
19    greatest, Pious Augusti, Mecheir.
20    [Posid]onios also called Triadelphos, shipmaster
21    of the administration of Neapolis, I have embarked the
22    wheat, with the half-artaba and one-percent surcharge,
23    two thousand six hundred sixty-two artabas
24    [total artabas] 2662, as set forth above.
