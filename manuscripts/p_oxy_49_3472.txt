[TRANSLATION]
1     Aeli[us Aphro]disios the strategos and Serenos also called
2     Sar[apion] the royal scribe of the Oxyrhynchite, to Ammonios also called Pets… [son of Am]monios son of Pekysis and partners, greeting.
3     Clau[dius] Justus the most excellent, in charge of the private account (idios logos), in the assize which he held of the nome in the past 11th year of Antoninus
4     Caesar the lord, when a case had been brought concerning those within the precincts
5     of the temples possessing building-plots, having dealt (with it) for all, declared thus: As many as joined those who submitted the valuation under Tuscus can duly lay claim
6     to the places. But as many as, being summoned, did not submit (their declaration), if even
7     now within two months they do not bring (it forward), let them be subject to the
8     things prescribed. These things, therefore, having been communicated by the other
9     of us [as is f]itting and (with) Herakleides the scribe of the city recently
10    … the list, man by man, of those now in possession of
11    building-plots within the precincts of the temples of the metropolis,
12    according to necessity it is enjoined upon you in writing, in order that within
13    the [appoin]ted term you may set out the things sought, on account of which you were declared to be in possession of building-plots within the precinct [of Ath]ena, in the quarter of Thoereion-Thenepmoi.
14    … I, Sarapammon the scribe, brought (it forward).
15    [In the 12th year of the Empe]ror Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus
16    [Augustus P]i[o]us, Mesore, of the intercalary (days), 5.
