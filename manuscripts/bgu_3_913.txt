1     In the consulship of Nummius Albinus and Fulvius Aemilianus, before (hand 2) the Ides of June,
2     (hand 1) in the high-priesthood of Neikostratos also called Stratonides, (son of) Xanthippos twice, month Xandikos 13, in Myra:
3     ...the high-priest (registered) those who have subscribed and sealed onto this diploma [...]
4     ...through the record-offices of the city of Myra, the sale of the female slave Prosdokia, that [took]
5     [place in Myra] under Neikostratos also called Stratonides, (son of) Xanthippos (grandson of) Xanthippos twice, high-priest of the Augusti,
6     [according to] the laws and edicts concerning the alienation of persons [...]
7     ...to Simon (son of) Demosthenes, a Pataran, her own slave-girl, na[med Prosdokia,
8     or if by any other]
9     [name she is called], by race Lycian, being about twenty years old, for whom also the (warranties) under the law toward the pro[...]
10    [apart from the] causes registered in the laws and ordinances, (at) a price of sil[ver]
11    [denarii ...], received in full, the right of action concerning the guarantee belonging
12    [to the one who has received] her, against the property of the seller, at double [...]
13    [...on behalf] of Gemella I subscribed; I, Simon, subscribed; Hilaros [...]
14    ...I acknowledged ... the slave-woman. Xandikos 12. Hermagoras, guardian, [...] I subscribed.
