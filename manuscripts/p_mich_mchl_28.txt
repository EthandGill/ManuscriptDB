[TRANSLATION]
1     Ninnas to Silvanus, the most honored, greeting.
2     I wrote you this letter at the harbor. None of
3     your servants has given me anything of what is in your charge, out of
4     what is owed by them, but they have put me off for a third day. Especially
5     Eutyches, even with insolence, so that I had
6     to speak to him, when he was giving to Turbon 3 artabas of vegetable(s) and 2 artabas of wheat
7     and to Pielis 6 artabas of wheat.
8     And his harvest(?) Apollos
9     an[d] Sarapion took for the debt.
10    And he declared nothing to me except
11    abusing and giving orders
12    to me. And they did none of the tasks you ordered them, nor did they slacken; for since from now on the seasonal tasks are being done, which you yourself will inspect when you come and it will be attested to you. Nemesas brought me on account
13    of an old account today
14    forty drachmas and says, "I will see to the rest." You will write how I am to send you
15    the rents, if I cannot send them. For I myself know
16    in what trouble I am. Greet Crispina and Diogenas and Sabina
17    and Strongylos. They greet you,
18    all those in the household, and the rest
19    of the sweet-oil they have sold.
20    I pray for your health.
21    Tybi 3.
22    To Hestia, 2 artabas of black olives of Bourios, and
23    to little Manos, 1 1/2 artabas of sweet-oil.
24    To Julius Silvanus at Diospolis, from Ninnas.
