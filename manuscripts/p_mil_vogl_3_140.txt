[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ptollarion, former gymnasiarch, son of Ptollarion, former gymnasiarch,
2     from Orsenouphis son of Komon
3     son of Herakles, from the village of
4     Kerkesoucha Orous. I wish
5     to lease from you for four years from the coming 17th year
6     of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar
7     the lord, from the (lands) belonging to you near the village of Ares
8     of the holding, three and a half arouras in one parcel; in the first and
9     third year for a sowing of wheat, at a rent for the two-year period, per year,
10    [together with what] I shall receive of seed, [three and a half] artabas of wheat,
11    in all per year [of wheat]
12    fifty-[two] artabas
13    and a half, by your own four-choinix measure; and in the second and fourth year for a sowing
14    of grass, sowing it for yourself
15    as fodder for sheep,
16    all (the produce) being free of deduction and risk; and I will perform the yearly works of the arouras, all that are fitting, at my own expense
17    at the proper seasons, and the
18    rents I will pay yearly in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor, new
19    pure unadulterated, the measuring being done by
20    one of your men, the (charges) being upon me
21    for the freight-charges of the wheat-sowing and the supervision,
22    but those of the carriage of the rents upon you; and after the
23    term I will deliver the arouras clear of rushes, dog's-tooth grass, all coarse grass, except reed, and you shall guarantee to me the
24    lease and (that it is) clear of
25    public dues, if it seems good
26    [to lease].
