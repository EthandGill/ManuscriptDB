1     Before all else I bow down greatly and greet
2     your footprints; may your brotherhood deign to allow
3     the lord Uranius in the great house
4     for a few days; and write to me concerning your condition
5     [of you].
6     to my brother Menas the [care-keeper] Papnouthis notary brother
