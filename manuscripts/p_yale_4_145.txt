[TRANSLATION]
1     To the mayor; may he obtain what is just.
2     Or [let] the accused be established. Year 33, Pauni 16.
3     To Boethos, kinsman of the king
4     and strategos
5     From Herges of Gortommanes, from the village Tintereis. Since I possess
6     an ancestral house in the
7     aforementioned village, which
8     and [they] share in according to the
9     half: Harmyuis and Demarchus and certain others from the same village,
10    and the aforementioned [ones] taking advantage and unwilling to act justly but rather forcing me because they are more [numerous] and I am not able
11    [to seek refuge with] other [ones] …
12    Year 33, Pauni 15, to Euphanor
13    Herges of the
14    Gortommanes
