[TRANSLATION]
1   [To Protarch]os,
2   [from Sa]rap[i]on son of Diodoros, of the Althaian (deme), [and from Pylai]menes son of
3   [Akr]atos (slave) of Caesar — present and conse[nting to this agree]ment being
4   [Gaius Julius Her]mias …, (agent) of the master Akratos (slave) of Caesar, Hermias having transacted
5   (as) son of Priscus. Concerning the matters at iss-
6   ue, Pylaimenes agrees — having become [compliant] in all
7   by Sarapion, and having received [from him, from hand to hand out of] the house, the fixed
8   cession-price — that he has ceded [to him the (properties) belonging to him]
9   in the village of Hypsele of the Lykopoli[te (nome) of the Thebaid, from t]he toward-
10   the-eas[t part, plo]ts [in which (are) a house and] a public bath,
11   [formerly (of) Diog]enes son of Isidoros, of which [the neighbo]urs (are): on the south, the avenue
12   of the temple of Chnoubis, the greatest god; on the nort[h, the] sheep-folds of
13   Amenothes; on the east, a water-channel; on the west, a [com]mon entrance and exit
14   of the plots; and that from now Sarapion holds and ow[n]s
15   the (properties) ce[de]d to him, and enters upon (them) [and bui]lds upon and
16   leases out [and] cedes to others and uses [and] manages and carries out
17   concerning them whatever he [wi]shes; and that there is no proceeding for Pylaimene(s)
18   nor for another on [his behalf] against Sarapion, the ce[d]ed (party), nor
19   agai[nst]
20   a part of them, nor against the proceeds from them, from no one
21   at all, written or unwritten, in any way; and that the guarantee
22   follows for him throughout from all, and that he shall ward off the
23   one who proceeds, at his own expense, and shall comply also with whatever
24   other (dispositions) Sarapion may choose concerning the same (properties), through the
25   record-offices at the place, taking nothing further, the ex-
26   penses being charged to Sarapion; or else, apart from the things agreed being
27   valid, that he pay back — both what Pylaimene(s) received as cession-price
28   with half
29   [as much again,] and the damages and expenses, and another 1,000 silver (drachmas), and the fix[ed]
30   penalty, just as by legal judgement; and Sarapion has recovered
31   from Pylaimenes the securities which he held against the aforesaid (matters),
32   in no way … Sarapion being … in the proceeding against Gaius
33   Hermias for the guarantee of what he has ceded …
34   according to the agreement, a plot of two arouras, and of what he owes him …
35   Year 26 of Caesar, Mecheir 20.
