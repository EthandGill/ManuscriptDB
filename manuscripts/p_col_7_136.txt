1     In the year 12 and 11 of our lords Diocletian
2     and Maximian, the Augusti, and the 4th of Constantius
3     and Maximian, the most noble Caesars, the Augusti,
4     Epeiph 8. There paid: Sarapion son of Onnophris, and
5     Chairemon son of Sarapion, and Chairemon son of Kopres,
6     through Isidoros, freight for goods and pickled provisions and the price of wine, for the 5th surtax,
7     one thousand six hundred drachmas, 1600 drachmas.
8     We, the Aurelii Pelenis and Isidoros, collectors, have signed.
9     We, the Aurelii Ptollas and Pankratis, collectors,
10    have signed.
11    In the year 12 and 11 and 4 of our lords Diocletian
12    and Maximian, the A[ugusti], and (of) Constantius and
13    Maximian, the most noble Caesars, Mesore 11.
14    We, the [Aureli]i Paesis [and L]eonides and Syrion and Isidoros,
15    [the 4] grain-receivers of the village of Karanis, there was measured out
16    [to us] in the granary of the same village by the public
17    levelled measure … of Karanis
18    … twenty …
19    … t…
20    We, the Aurelii Paesis and Leonides and Sy[rion and Isidoros, have signed.]
21    I, Paesis, wrote on behalf of them, being illiterate.
22    In the year 12 and 11 a[nd] 4 of our lords
23    Diocletian and M[axi]mian, the Augusti,
24    and (of) Constantius [an]d Maximian, the
25    most noble Caesars, Mesore 11.
26    We, the Aurelii Paesis [and] Leonides and Isidoros
27    and Syrion, the 4 grain-receivers of the village
28    of Karanis, there was measured out to us in the granary of the same
29    village by the public levelled measure, for the current
30    12th year and 11th year and 4th year, on behalf of the village of Karanis, in the name of
31    Chairemon son of Sarapion, through Isidoros,
32    of clean wheat twenty artabas, total 20 artabas; and on behalf of
33    the name of Ptolemaios son of Chairemon, through the heirs of Kopres,
34    of clean wheat five artabas, total 5 artabas; and for boundary-surveying,
35    in the name of Kopres, of clean wheat one artaba; and on behalf of
36    the name of Sarapion son of Onnophris, through Isidoros, of clean
37    wheat eleven artabas, total 11 artabas;
38    and for the after-sowing, of clean wheat
39    twenty and a half artabas, total 20½ artabas.
40    Total of the receipt: wheat, 57½ artabas.
41    We, the Aurelii Paesis and Leonides
42    and Syrion and Isidoros, have signed.
43    I, Paesis, wrote on behalf of the partners,
44    they being illiterate.
45    The same Isidoros measured out, in the name of
46    Sarapion son of Onnophris, of clean wheat
47    three artabas, total 3 artabas.
48    And in the 14th year and 13th year and 6th year, Mecheir 30, there was paid the price of wheat of the produce of the 12th year and 11th year and 4th year,
49    for the after-sowing of boundary-surveying, drachmas
50    seven hundred and twenty, total 720 drachmas, and of these
51    the obols and the bank-charges. We, the Aurelii
52    Paesis and the partners, have signed.
