[TRANSLATION]
1     Petosiris to Didyme, many greetings.
2     The purple dye which you sent us
3     has not been made. Send us …
4     … a hoop, ten bundles, to Daphne, as
5     the color of your maphortion (mantle).
6     The maphortion which you have, sell for 48 drachmas;
7     but if you do not get the price, do not
8     sell it, but see to the
9     40 drachmas and send to us
10    quickly. Inquire about the safety of the son Neilous, and if
11    it is necessary, write to us concerning him. Demand from Teeus the wife of Herakleides son of Sarammon 40 drachmas for freight.
12    Thaesis to Didyme her daughter, many
13    greetings. I handed over to Herakleides Selmon and to Kapeidolis 7½ metretai of wine, and the remaining small change I have settled with them together with the price
14    of 18½ metretai, as of the metretes
15    one at 64 drachmas. And your wife wrote to me
16    in your name, and I have sold the wine at double [what] I bought it for, and I have marked down … drachmas. Soeis
17    troubles me about the artaba
18    of grain. And so now send to me
19    a jar of oil for Herakleides
20    Selmon, and one little basket. I greet you warmly and all
21    your household. Eseis greets you warmly, and Theonilla and Neilous.
22    Didyme. The son-in-law of Menas gave me nothing.
23    We pray for your health, …
24    From Didyme, from Thaesis
25    and Petosir[i]os.
