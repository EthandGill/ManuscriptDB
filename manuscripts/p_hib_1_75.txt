[TRANSLATION]
1     Theodorus to the guards in Talai, greetings. Petosires the toparch and Peteimouthes the topostrategist have written to us [that they] sold to Philammon
2     from the Philoxenos allotment near Talai
3     of forage land arourae 3[...]. Having received
4     therefore the village scribe, measure [it] out
5     to him; more do not grant, just as
6     the [matter] concerning [you] will be.
7     Farewell. Year 15, Tybi 2.
