[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Justinian the eternal
2     Augustus Imperator, the twenty-seventh year, after the consulship of Flavius Basilius
3     the most glorious, in the year, on Hathyr the ninth of the second indiction.
4     [To the heirs] of the learned memory of the deceased Philippos, from
5     …on, from the Hermopolite, from Aurelius Ioannes son of Ionios, mother
6     …, slowly subscribing, a cultivator, originating from the village
7     … of the Hermopolite nome. I acknowledge willingly and of my own accord
8     [that I have leased] [from] your learnedness for a ten-year period
9     [reckon]ed from the crops of the third indiction coming in, with God,
10    [and itself] the six waterless arouras belonging to you …
11    [situated] in the plain of the [aforesaid village of the Hermo]p[olite] [nome, in] the place Ara…en. …
12    … ent …
13    [of gold] seven [solidi] less [forty]-two [carats by the standard]
14    [public (weight)] of the same [city, total] seven gold solidi [less] forty-two carats, which gold
15    [solidi] I shall pay to your learnedness in the month Phaophi in full
16    [and] without delay, all my belongings being pledged to your instruction for this lease [and] payment and repayment …
17    [and] of my future possessions, generally and specifically, by way of security
18    [and] of mortgage [by right, just as] [from a legal judgment], and on being formally questioned I gave my assent …
