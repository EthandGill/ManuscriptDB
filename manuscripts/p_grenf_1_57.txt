[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the consulship of Flavius Basilius,] in the twentieth year, Thoth 19, 10th indiction,
2     … to Zacharias, members of the city council from the city of the Hermopolites … …enes son of Georgios from the village of Elias of the Hermopolite [nome]
3     I acknowledge that I have willingly and of my own free choice leased from you for three
4     years reckoned from the harvests of the coming, God willing, eleventh indiction
5     and itself … an allotment of farmland of the sons of Belas, that is to say,
6     of arouras … of an allotment and of a wooden water-wheel fully equipped with all
7     equipment … of Theon, lying in the plain di… for sowing and planting of crops whichever I choose, at a rent of
8     … one hundred twenty artabas by the Athenian measure
9     … of four, and for the unirrigated land, may it not be so, I shall provide
10    … which rent new, clean, sifted, by the measure
11    … I shall render and measure out to you in the month Epeiph and into your granary
12    … I have the irrigation, a two-thirds share from
13    … to … in winter on the second of the month
14    … without reproach. And I shall provide by way of customary dues according to the
15    … the time of the lease as I received it.
16    … farmer of Paulos son of Phibis, the aforesaid, I have leased as set forth above.
17    … I wrote on his behalf as he does not know letters, Hell…
18    … I bear witness to the lease, having heard it from the one making the contract.
19    … I bear witness to the lease, having heard it from the one making the contract.
20    Through me … notary, through Victor his son …
