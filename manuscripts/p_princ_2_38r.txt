[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aurelia Se]renilla surnamed Demetria, (daughter) of Philippianus also called Kopreus, former town-councillor of Hermopolis the great, ancient and most [illustrious], with as guardian Aurelius Herm[in]os also called Achilleus, (son) of Eudaimon, euthenarch of the same Hermo-
2     polis, and as curator Aurelius Valerius Longus,
3     [veter]an from the same [Herm]opolis, made a will and dictated it to be written. Aurelia Asklatarion also called Koprilla, my mother,
4     let her be my [heir]. And let my sons be disinherited. Let her come forward to my inheritance within 100 days, my appointed (days), when she learns of it and is able to testify that she is my heir. To Aurelius Achilleus also called Herminos, kosmetes, my spouse,
5     I [leave] near Ibion Peteaphthi, of the machimon assessments, from the Naube allotment, of which I hold in one plot arouras
6     … six … five arouras; and around the [same] village, of the life-partnership (?) of Aline, of which I hold three arouras;
7     … curator … of which I dwell (in), and to the curator over [me] … …
8     … nothing shall be sought … …
9     … [of] the same city to be given … …
10    … one, a quarter … …
