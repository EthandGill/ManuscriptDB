[TRANSLATION]
1     Psenchonsis, son of Psansnotes, has paid for dike-tax
2     year 12, six drachmas of four-obol [coins]; totaling six drachmas four-obol, and for bath-tax four-and-a-half obols
3     totals [drachmas] 7, obols 2½ and these further-registered
4     year 13 of Tiberius Claudius Caesar
5     Augustus, Germanicus, Autocrat
6     of month Neos Sebastos, [day] 22. Bassus I have marked.
