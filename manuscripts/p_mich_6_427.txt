[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a cession. In the 18th year of the deified Hadrian, the 4th intercalary day.
2     Gaius Julius Gemellus, one of the discharged
3     soldiers, about 55 years old, with a scar in the middle of his forehead, acknowledges to Chairemon
4     son of Horos, a minor, through his guardian Petsoraipis
5     son of Pnepheros, about 62 years old, with a scar on the right wrist, that there has been ceded to him according to this agreement,
6     from now for all time, the
7     half-share also ceded to him of an olive-grove in
8     the sixth-tax-collected category, of 1/4 1/8 1/16 1/32 aroura, or however much
9     it may be, near Karanis; and in the village of Karanis
10    a whole half-share of a house and courtyard and all appurtenances in one parcel, on
11    the existing neighbours of the whole, and the boundaries and
12    entrances and exits and watering-places and outflows
13    and all the other rights — in return for which the acknowledging party Gaius Julius Gemellus received from Petsoraipis, from the account of the minor Chairemon,
14    for the olive-grove 100 drachmas, and for the half
15    of the house-plots 100 drachmas, the total being 200 drachmas,
16    and that he will not proceed against them in any way
17    whatsoever, and that he, Julius Gemellus, and his heirs will guarantee to Chairemon
18    and his heirs the ceded property as aforesaid
19    with every guarantee, free from all public dues
20    and every kind, up to the past 17th year
21    and that very 17th year of Hadrian Caesar the
22    lord, and free from private claims and every encumbrance for all time. And whenever
23    Chairemon wishes, he shall register the ceded property from a copy of this agreement
24    without requiring the presence of the acknowledging party.
25    And if he transgresses anything, let him pay as penalty
26    100 drachmas and to the treasury an equal sum.
