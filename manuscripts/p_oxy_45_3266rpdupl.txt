[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] consulate of Flavius Philicianus and Fabius Titianus,
2     the most illustrious, Mesore 20.
3     Aurelius Eulogios, [son] of Leonteus, mother Eusebia, from
4     the illustrious and most illustrious Oxyrhynchus city, to Flavius Annianus, first-actor, through Aurelius Heraclius, business-agent, greeting. I acknowledge [I] have received from you as an account
5     of business by hand from your house silver of Augusteans,
6     coinage five hundred talents, talents 500, of principal,
7     on [the understanding] that I [in place of the share of profit from these which you choose] will pay you each month
8     [from] the following month Thoth of the entering year 32, 22, 14, 5, 3,
9     [of silv]er talents ten, and the aforementioned prin[cipal] being [un]imperiled of all peril and un[accounted] of all accountability I will necessarily return
10    [to you in the month of] Phamenoth of the same entering year
11    without delay. But if not, I will pay you for the time exceeding [the deadline] each month silver talents sixteen and drachmas four-thousand
12    until [the] return of the principal, taking place
13    for you of the execution both from me and from all my possessions. Valid the hand-written document
14    written in [duplicate] [and] being questioned I acknowledged.
