r.1    ὑπατείας τῶν κυρίων ἡμῶν Κωνσταντίου
r.2    καὶ Μαξιμιανοῦ Σεβαστῶν ϛ
r.3    συνέθετο Αὐρήλιος Εὐπορᾶς Αὑνῆ μητρὸς Ταήσεως ἀπὸ κώμης Ἰβιῶνος Εἰκοσιπενταρουρῶν Αὐρηλίῳ Πεηοῦτι Πτολεμαίου ἀπὸ κώμης Καρανίδος σακ κοφόρῳ

[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Constantius
2     and Maximianus Augustuses [date]
3     Aurelius Euporas of Ahne, mother Taesis, from the village Ibieons of Twenty-five-arouras agreed with Aurelius Pehouti of Ptolemaios from the village Karanidos, sack-carrier
4     [concerning] the work on [the condition that] Euporas arrive in Alexandria and [fulfill] the [work] in place of Pehouti
5     to fulfill by the transport of the public
6     wheat according to the order of the governor for a period of two months from tomorrow's day
