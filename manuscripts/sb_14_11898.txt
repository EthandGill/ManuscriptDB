[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion to Diogenes the [brother]
2     greetings
3     Of those from the assembly, if not
4     Diogenes and Theonion, there is not
5     here anyone to whom I have spoken about
6     Tetradymis; and they say: whatever you wish you
7     do. Therefore if it is possible for you
8     on the third to come up, perhaps and
9     others from them might happen to be there, it would be
10    good; but if not, make clear what you want
11    Farewell, brother
12    Epeiph 30
