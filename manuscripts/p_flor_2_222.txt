[TRANSLATION]
1     Horion to Heroninus, the dearest, greetings.
2     If you are well, [dearest], from the
3     [things] [transferred] to me from the city into
4     the expenditures with me of my care
5     of my wine-measures, transfer to Logineios
6     to the friend, wine, eight measures becomes eight measures
7     of which I also received the price here
8     from him for my expenditures
9     of the care in necessity. certainly
10    therefore transfer them to him today. the medicine brought down
11    by Ake, your donkey-driver, to the bull with me, certainly send to me
12    so that the bull may also be treated.
13    I pray you to be well, [dearest].
14    Year 3, Epeiph 7
