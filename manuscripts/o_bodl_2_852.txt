[TRANSLATION]
1     Psansnos and Kasianos, tax collectors of silver-tax
2     to Psae, who is also Horos, [of] Pikotes. we received on behalf of
3     watchtowers and guards, [of the] Northern markets, 20th year, base [coins], drachmas
4     three, 5 obols, 2 chalkoi, [which] amounts to base 3 drachmas, 5 obols, 2 chalkoi. Year 20
5     of Hadrian Caesar, the lord
6     Mecheir 15.
7     Psansnos, I have signed, 3 drachmas, 5 obols, 2 chalkoi.
