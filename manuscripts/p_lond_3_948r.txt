[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Herakles son of Dioskoros, from Antaiopolis, master
2     of his own boat of a capacity of two hundred and fifty artabas, unmarked, has freighted (it) out to Aurelius Areios
3     son of Herakleides, councillor of the city of the Arsinoites, for a lading of vegetable-seed of two hundred and fifty artabas, on condition that it be loaded from the harbour of Alsos of the metropolis
4     as far as the harbour of the Oxyrhynchite; of freight of the agreed silver
5     one hundred drachmas, clear of all (charges), of which from here he has received forty drachmas of silver,
6     and the remaining sixty drachmas he shall receive together with the delivery; which cargo he shall deliver safe and undamaged from any maritime mischief, he receiving for the lading two days from the 25th, and likewise for his voyage upstream in the Oxyrhynchite
7     four days, after which, if he be detained, the master shall receive daily
8     sixteen drachmas, the master furnishing for himself the sufficient sailors and
9     the whole equipment of the boat; and he shall likewise receive, for a libation in the Oxyrhynchite, a jar of wine. The freight-contract is valid. Aurelius Herakles: I have freighted (it) out and
10    I have received on account the forty drachmas as is set forth.
11    In the 3rd year of Imperator Caesar Gaius Julius Verus Maximinus Pius Felix Augustus
12    and of Gaius Julius Verus Maximus the most sacred Caesar, Augustus, son of the Augustus, Phaophi 22.
