[TRANSLATION]
1     were distributed wheat produce of year 15 that has passed
2     Aurelius Antoninus Caesar
3     of the lord through grain-collectors [of] Libos toparchy of Seryphe[s], to Diogenides Sarapa[tos]
4     through Horos Sarata[s] farmer of Seryphe[s]
5     artabas sixteen, they become artabas 16, from the plot
6     of Didymos Saras of Hekatones.
7     Theon, who is also Hermias, assistant, [I] have marked.
8     and through grain-collectors of the same from the plot of Horos
9     Sarata[s], to D[io]genides Sarapa[ta]s
10    artabas seven, they become [artabas] 7 … [I] have marked.
