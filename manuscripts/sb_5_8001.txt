[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonius strategus of the seven-village [district] of Apollonopolis
2     from Psenanoufios Artepotes of those from
3     Tanuyaetheos: having a matter [against] Pachomios
4     Papaikios of those from Teruteos of the same
5     nome concerning a debt of money
6     drachmas 66, I request [that you] order him through one of those about you
7     [my] servants to come to the [hearing] which will be for [the purpose of] good
8     [conduct by] Aterius Nepos
9     the most excellent governor.
