[TRANSLATION]
1     Andromachos to Archebis, greeting. Of the dispatch addressed to Leonidas the grain-storehouse keeper (sitologos)
2     a copy is appended below.
3     Far[ewell. Year 4], Tybi 27.
4     To Leonidas. Of the dispatch from Ptolemaios who [is acting as deputy]
5     in the matters of the administration (dioikesis), [a copy is appended below.]
6     Following it accordingly, load up — Arche]bis the royal scribe joining in writing —
7     onto the boat indicated, sent up from the city by Dionysios and Heroïdes those
8     in charge of the freightage, and assigned by dispatch-order (apostolos) to the Memphite nome for the taking-up of w[heat] from the produce
9     of year 3, the vessel of Agathokles of [cap]acity 2500 (artabas), of wheat pure, unadulterated,
10    sifted, by the receiving measure that is to the bronze (standard),
11    and by a just leveling-rod, the guard-soldiers having first been put aboard, those allotted who most especially have trust, on whom too the sealed sample
12    shall be placed in earthen, unbaked
13    vessels, and they shall convey it; and from the one in charge of the grain of the levy (exairesis)
14    taking the appropriate dispatches, and the shipmaster
15    a written bond of the royal oath about restoring to the
16    city the cargo unharmed, having committed no fraud in the
17    passage, together with the opinion of Pankrates and Demetrio[s those concerned with the]
18    administration, the sword-bearers and the marines [— artabas of wheat 2500].
19    There come to wheat artabas 2500; and make out receipts (symbola) and counter-receipts (antisymbola) with him [as is fitting].
20    Ptolemaios to Andromachos the s[trategos],
21    greeting and good health. Onto the (boat) sent up f[rom the]
22    city by Dion[ysios]
23    and Heroïdes those in charge of the freightage and
24    assigned to the Memphite nome for the taking-up of wheat, the vessel of Agathokles, load up
25    from the produce of year 3 the (artabas) of the capacity,
26    of wheat pure, unadulterated, sifted,
27    by the measure that is to the bronze (standard), artabas 2500; there come to artabas 2500.
28    And that it may be fitted out quickly, and that the guard-soldiers
29    holding the sealed sam[ples]
30    in earthen vessels may be put aboard to s[ail] to the city, and that from [the]
31    guards they may bring back the counter-dispatches, take forethought that not carelessly
32    and that a receipt and a counter-receipt be made out
33    with the shipmaster, as is fitting. And take care of yourself,
34    that you may be in good health.
35    Far[ewell.] Year 4, Ty[bi] …
