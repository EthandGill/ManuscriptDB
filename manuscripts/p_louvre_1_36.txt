[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 30 of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus
2     Caesar the lord, Pachon 17. [They] were registered with the nome-governor
3     of the Arsinite [nome] through Fl[avius] … [?] … assistant, of the diploma of fishermen from the territory of Soknopaios Island, for
4     year 28: drachmae sixteen, obols sixteen, which makes 16 obols 16, and
5     for year 29, likewise: drachmae sixteen, obols sixteen,
6     which makes drachmae 16, obols 16.
