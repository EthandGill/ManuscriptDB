1     These are the public revenues completed by me Taurinus of Marcianus from Hermopolis: coins 6, keratia 3, δ
2     Thus
3     For the apportionment of 1 thousand [000,000]: coins
4     For gold at 1½ standard: coins 2, keratia 12, δ
5     [and] for 500 thousands: coins 3, keratia 15
6     For 6 keratia: coins 0, keratia 15
7     For the document: coins 3
8     I Taurinus of Marcianus have submitted the present record of the public revenue completed by me, acknowledg[ing] before and
9     God the Almighty and the welfare of our masters the Amirates, as if there appear[ed] anything
10    having omitted in such record to provide in double quantity and to be subject [me]
11    to the charge of perjury
12    The declaration of Taur[inus] of Marcianus
