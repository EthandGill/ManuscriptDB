[TRANSLATION]
1     To P[ro]tarchos
2     [fro]m a…ra son of Theon son of A…sa… [and]
3     [fro]m Aristonikos son of Aisa…, [a Persian]
4     [of the] descent. Concerning the (matters) at issu[e]
5     Aristonikos consents that he has borrowed from Th[eo]n by hand from the household [of silv]er, Ptolemaic, 100 drachmas, and that, in[stead of the]
6     [intere]st on these and of what Theon shall pay in addition
7     [to] hi[m] each month
8     of three [drach]mas, three obols, under compul[sio]n he shall let Th[eo]n a[nd] [those (acting) on hi]s [behalf,] for a period of twelve months, from
9     [Pha]menoth of the present 17th [year of Caesar — (I) ]Aristonikos — dwell in [the] [house belong]ing to himself and to his sister
10    [Philo]tera, in the tenement (synoikia) which is
11    in the Delta (quarter), which is shut off all round, and using the front-chamber (prostas) belonging to it
12    and the conveniences of the tenement
13    in common — all the repairs of the house and of the front-chamber, as often as need be, being
14    chargeable to Aristonikos — whom Theon shall not eject,
15    nor (shall) those (acting) on his behalf (eject)
16    within the period; and that, this (period) having ela[ps]ed, he shall repay the 100 silver drachmas without interest, without any delay; or, in whatever (way) he transgresses, he shall be forthwith subject to seizure and to detention [un]til he pays out both the loan [with]
17    one-and-a-half (penalty), and, for the overdue tim[e],
18    the interest according to the regulation (diagramma),
19    of two drachmas (per mina), the right of execution accruing
20    [to Theo]n both from Aristonikos himself
21    and from all his property,
22    as if by a legal decision, (all) pledges being void
23    and all guarantees and all protection whatever which he may bring forward; and Theon has
24    the right of dwelling in the house and the
25    front-chamber, and further also of the storeroom (tamieion)
26    in them; and that Theon
27    shall give in addition to Aristonikos the
28    three drachmas, three obols per month for the twelve months; and
29    having dwelt (there) for these (months), having recovered the 100 silver drachmas without interest, he shall make a release
30    of this agreement and a cession from the dwellings, and shall hand them over clean of refuse, together with the fixtures, just as he received them; or else he himself shall be liable to the appointed fine.
