[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 19, Pachon 5. It has been fixed upon the bank in Hermontis on which Callias [holds the position] of the twentieth [indiction] according to the description from Asclepiadus concerning the purchase under which Zminis, [the one] of Pakoibios [son] of Dionysius, the royal secretary, signs
2     [Property] of Kalibis the elder of Harapathos, grain-bearing land the half of 9.5 arouras and of the adjacent fallow and of the house in it built according to half of the one [existing] in the sacred revenue of Aphrodite, whose boundaries have been declared through the preceding [document]
3     of the contract, which he purchased from Siepmoutus son of Harommoutus, [for] 4.2 talents of bronze, the tax of which exchange [is] thousand three hundred, amounting to 108. Callias.
