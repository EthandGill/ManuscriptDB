1   68
2   To Chaireas, strategos,
3   from Herakleios son of
4   Apion, and Naris son
5   of Kollouthos, eld-
6   er. We register
7   for the present 12th year
8   of Tiberius Caesar Augustus
9   the sheep belonging to us,
10   six each,
11   sheep 12, which will graze
12   together with the follow-
13   ing lambs, around Pela in the
14   western toparchy
15   and throughout the whole nome,
16   mixed in with those
17   of Dionysios son of Hippalos,
18   through the shepherd of this man,
19   (his) son Straton the youn-
20   ger, registered for the poll-tax
21   in the same Pela;
22   for which we will also pay the prop-
23   er tax. Fare[w]ell.
24   I, Sara(pion) toparch, have signed: sheep
25   twelve, total 12.
26   Year 12 of Tiberius Caesar
27   Augustus, Mech(eir) 5.
