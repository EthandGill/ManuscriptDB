[TRANSLATION]
1     Eudaemon to Didymus, manager, very many greetings.
2     You will do well having taken care, as you are accustomed, of my accountant and [of] my
3     wife through
4     Pathbeeus, [son] of Horus, Peenno,
5     fourteen cotylae
6     and you will have the half either
7     generally or in silver.
8     If you do this, you will be to me
9     [one] who has done a great favor.
10    Farewell. Mesore eight
