[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Nemesion also called Dionysios, royal
2     scribe of the Oxyrhynchite,
3     from Aurelius Asklas son of Charmion from Oxyrhynchus
4     city, residing in … eilo …
5     I request that an order be issued for seed-loans
6     from wheat of the produce of the past 4th year for the
7     sowing of the present 5th year, for which I farm around
8     the hamlet of Monimos from the allotment of Ptolemaios, all…
9     lisarchenap… tho… of Sarapammon and …
10    th…os of Psamek… royal land … pip…
11    … nopanti, artabas 3 … arouras thirteen and a quarter, of which the neighbours are: on the south and east (the land) of Aurelius
12    Dionysotheon, former gymnasiarch, on the north
13    of Aurelius Diogenes son of Sarapion, former gymnasiarch, on the west royal land, of wheat artabas 10. Which, having cleansed of barley and darnel, I shall sow
14    into the land soundly and faithfully, those appointed for this and the others to whom it pertains following along, and from the new crop I shall render
15    the same amounts together with the appurtenances, along with the genuine
16    payments of the land for the present
17    year, by the public measure and by the artaba-measurement that was ordered. And I swear by the fortune of Marcus Aurelius
18    Severus Alexander Pius Felix
19    Augustus that I have not lied. In the 5th year
20    of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
21    Severus Alexander Pius Felix
22    Augustus.
23    Of royal land.
