[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: We, Petronios, employee of the imperial estate, son of Apollo, and Kyrapanton, his wife, to you, Menas, archisymmachos of the divine house. We acknowledge that we owe you on account of arrears of clothing brought by us for Tebio, your wife, one onyx-coloured cape, decorated with wheels, made of achae-cloth, and one dress, and we shall deliver them to you in the month of Mesore of the present fourth indiction with- out delay. Written on the 18th of the month of Tybi, indiction 4. (2nd hand) Written through me, Ioseph.
