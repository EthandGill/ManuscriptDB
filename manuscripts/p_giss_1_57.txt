[TRANSLATION]
1     From
2     The most reverent Atta Kolluthos the elder who is in the mountain of Oxyrhynchus made to me letters with manifest power
3     Containing which your brotherhood will learn, with a meeting taking place with God toward his reverence of your virtue
4     And I [replied] to his godliness through Makarios our ally having been sent among the Oxyrhynchites with the
5     Servants of the most esteemed Sarapion the scholastikos. You will therefore deign to work carefully and to cause to be delivered
6     The letters and to receive a favorable reply and to be copied appropriately and, if necessary, to take the security toward the
7     Things written to him by me and again to give and further apart from your wisdom letters to be sent to me so that I
8     Might arrange toward the things being written by her and command and order if necessary those of whom there is need here. Do not delay,
9     Master.
10    Give to my master, the most wondrous in all things and [virtuous] brother Phobammon, helper abaktis
11    From Apphout.
