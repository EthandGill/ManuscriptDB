[TRANSLATION]
1   Longinus Crispus and the other over-
2   seers of the Sacred Gate of Syene. Paid:
3   Petorzmethis the elder, son of Poueris, mother
4   Tisatis, for the poll-tax of the 12th year of Hadrian the lord:
5   seventeen drachmas (and) a half-obol, (and) ½ obol (keepers' fee) — total 17 dr. 1 ob. Year 12 of Hadrian
6   the lord, Mesore 8.
7   (2nd hand) I, Longinus, have signed off: seventeen (drachmas) (and) an obol.
