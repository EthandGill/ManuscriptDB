[TRANSLATION]
1     Paous and Petearouheris, tax-collectors of silver-taxes
2     Apol…r…s, scribe, to Horus of Sachomnes, greetings.
3     We received from you [for] poll-tax of the 13th year through
4     Paous [twenty] drachmas, totaling drachmas 20, and through Petearouheris
5     for dike-tax and others of the [same] 13th year ten drachmas three obols,
6     totaling drachmas 10 three obols, and for apportionment of river-guard-tax one drachma three obols,
7     totaling one drachma three obols, totaling of the whole receipt drachmas thirty [two],
8     totaling drachmas 32, the previous receipt being void, through
9     Paous drachmas 20, and through Petearouheris drachmas 12. Year 14
10    of the Emperor [Caesar] Trajan Hadrian Augustus
11    Hathyr 15.
