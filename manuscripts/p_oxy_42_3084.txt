[TRANSLATION]
1     Heracleius to Themistocles the
2     most esteemed, greetings.
3     The most powerful governor is said to be in
4     the Heracleopolite to have arrived
5     on the 24th in the evening. Which, so that you know,
6     my lord, I write to you. I pray for your health,
7     my lord, with the whole household prospering.
8     To Themistocles the steward.
