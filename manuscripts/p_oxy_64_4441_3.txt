[TRANSLATION]
1     [108]
2     To Valerius Ammoni[anus also called] Gerontios, logistes of the Oxyrhynchite,
3     from the guild of the following appointed stone-quarrymen and stone-masons and carpenters and others and of the elders of eac[h cra]ft, through those
4     signing below. We were instructed [by] your care, so as
5     to inspect the buildings throughout the whole city that all belong to it,
6     and moreover also whatever else there is of the most ancient city that is collapsing, having been shattered by force and [t]ime, so that safety may be secured for the houses of the city. Whence, having gone round meanwhile the public works and other buildings that are in bad condition, lying beside works, together with … experienced men, we report that the following registered places need the necessary repair.
7     In year 10 and 8 of our lo[rds Constanti]ne and Licinius the Augusti, in
8     the consulship of Caeci[nius Sabinus and Vettius] Rufinus the most distinguished, Tybi …
9     Aurelii Melas son of … and Demetri[o]s son of Thonios have jointly submitted, reporting a[s] set out above. The same De[metrios]
10    wrote on behalf of the other who does not know letters. Aurelius Choous
11    son of Pausirion, I have submitted, reporting as set out above. Aurelii
12    E … -ios and Thonios son of Dionysios and Sakaon son of Horos
13    and … -ios and Dioskoros son of Serenos, carpenters, have sub[mitted,] reporting as set out above. Aurelius
14    … son of Didymos, from the Oxyrhynchite, wrote on behalf of them, being present but not knowing letters.
15    Aurelius Aphynchis son of Herakle- … stone-mason, I have submitted, reporting
16    [a]s set out above. Aurelius Artemidoros son of Herakleos, stone-mason, I have submitted, reporting as set out above. Aurelius …
17    … stone-mason, I have submitted, reporting as set out above.
