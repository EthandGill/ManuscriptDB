213   the surveyor: I measured the aroura and the share. I, Aurelius Anaios the surveyor, joined in measuring.
214   I, Aurelius Apollonios the surveyor, joined in measuring.
215   I, Aurelius Abous the boundary-surveyor, pointed out the aforesaid aroura and the share and omitted nothing, and Aas and Olkanol the village-head were present at the measuring. I, Aunas the secretary, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
