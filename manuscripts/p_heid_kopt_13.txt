[TRANSLATION]
1     First and the worship upon the holy of your divinity and my lordship lady and your mother Mary
2     While the Theotokos bears [the] heavenly things, that she will become for you for [an] intercessor, she interceding with him before her son
3     And the mercy which the all-lordship is—that he might be for you helper and your salvation in all things according to the day
4     Therefore when your divinity commands, your worshiper the deacon father Shenute, I have sent to you
5     Regarding this, she will give voice that when you command God on account of his name for you, he stands for you
6     And he will help for you with your entire house, he will grant the intercession to the lordship lady, since they came [to]
7     Because she knows a [great] sorrow, this hope for God that you will remove the sorrow from us, and the intercession is joy in the lordship Master
