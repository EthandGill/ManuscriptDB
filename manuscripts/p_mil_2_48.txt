[TRANSLATION]
1     … a … n … Mechei[r] …
2     [of the prese]nt year 225 = 194, [of the] present twelfth indiction, and everything
3     … and we entrust … in this
4     … and to the borders belonging to the same Oxyrhynchos, for the service
5     [of] the merchants and agents, to fulfil, apart from the service of the
6     perfumery, unflaggingly, blamelessly, without deserting my post, so that no blame
7     or negligence or any censure comes to be about me, and that I receive
8     from you, by way of wage for the whole three-year period, together with my food
9     and the household-keep and all other expenses, of gold solidi
10    fifty by the standard of Alexandria, in full, total of gold solidi 50 of Alexandria, in full,
11    and that I seek nothing else against your admirability, but all the things
12    entrusted to me by you I restore to you in full,
13    in good faith, blamelessly, hiding nothing whatever
14    from you, nor indeed withdrawing from you before the end of the same period.
15    And if I do this, I agree to give you by way of penalty of gold solidi
16    thirty-six, and if you too cast me out without any cause and censure,
17    you are liable to the equal penalty. The contract of hire is valid, written in duplicate, and on being questioned I assented.
18    … to Ezekias from the upper region of the Kynopolites, now residing
19    … [of wa]ge for three years from Mecheir … 1, of the indiction 12, of gold solidi 50 by the standard of Alexandria, in full.
