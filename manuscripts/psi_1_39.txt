[TRANSLATION]
1     Marcus A… …os… oi
2     to the Hermopolitan, greetings.
3     I acknowledge that I have sold to you
4     which I also happen to have bought a [horse], a female
5     from Dioscorus, this very one, such a one
6     defective, and I hold the
7     agreed-upon
8     price of silver in drachmas
9     one hundred eighty-eight, amounting to drachmas 188. Of year 11
10    of Antoninus Caesar of the
11    lord, Phamenoth 1.
