[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] fourth year of Emperor [Caesar Gaius Aurelius]
2     Valerius Diocletian, and in the third year of Emperor
3     Caesar Marcus Aurelius Valerius Maximian, Pious
4     Fortunate Augustuses, month of Dystrus, Tybi 21, in Ptolemais
5     the Well-benefactress of the Arsinoite district. [He] agrees Aurelius Aphonis
6     [son] of Eisoukhas, mother Amitis, from village Philadelphia of the Heracleid
7     division, as if forty years, scar on right shin. Aurelius Apei
8     [son of] Demetrius, through Aurelius Theon, business agent, to have the one
9     agreeing from Apei through Theon through hand
10    silver capital one talent, and necessarily the one
11    agreeing shall repay to Apei through Theon the aforesaid [ca]pital on the 20th of the entering [month] Mechir of the present [4th year and 3rd year] without postponement; but if on the appointed deadline the [re]payment not make the one agreeing, there shall follow for him the monthly per mina according to laws the interest, and when the demand is made to Apei through the
12    Theon [of the pr]actice [from both] the [one] agreeing [Aphonis and]
13    from all his [existing properties just as] from judgment
14    or also from market; and being asked I agreed.
15    Aurelius Aphonis [son of] Eisoukhas, I agree that I have received the
16    one of [the] silver one talent. I shall repay on the
17    deadline as [is] provided. Aurelius Dioscorus Aune …
18    from the am[phi]od of Linypheia I wrote on behalf of him, illiterate.
