[TRANSLATION]
1     From
2     if [your] disposition may deign customarily, being willing to love, to bring together the
3     [noble] Paul sent from me to purchase wine for me, and do not allow the ship to be burdened with [wine] at the customs. For being confident in your virtue and
4     in the [between] friendship I have written. For the holy God knows not for the sake of protection
5     [I] have written, but to me the wine is brought. And the things sent from me
6     very humble [things], deign to receive as many. [the] [most dignified]
7     free woman and your most dear children—from me to greet, deign,
8     and in which there is need of the things here, deign to command
9     Give to the master, in all things most wonderful, virtuous brother … ad … to friend
10    from Constantine[.]
