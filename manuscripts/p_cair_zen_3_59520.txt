[TRANSLATION]
1     Pharates the beekeeper to Zenon greetings. I am wronged by Maieus the beekeeper. Quarrel …
2     for having arisen between me and him he made an outcry to [you]
3     having anticipated me and [you] gave him a letter of
4     Pauni 9 to Numenios in the city of Akanthos to [hear] us. We were judged therefore there both on Numenios
5     and on the elders. But he did not remain … in that judgment, but said [he would] be judged before
6     Zenon. But I … out of which I will benefit you share [it] with Limnaios. But he led me away into the prison, so that I not meet
7     you … the swarms of Isis in Sarytes in the Aphroditopolite [nome]. Still [then] and now I am ready
8     [guaran]tors having established to be judged with him. I beseech [you] therefore and I supplicate, if it also seems [to you], not to overlook me
9     being destroyed unjustly in the prison. Know me being under arrest [for] 22 days,
10    when it is the season for the bees to transfer into the pastures …
11    Be fortunate!
