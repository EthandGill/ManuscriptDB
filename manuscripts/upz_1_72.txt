[TRANSLATION]
1     Myroullus and Chalbas
2     Arabs to Dakoutis
3     to the brother greetings.
4     having heard in Poiei
5     the things concerning you that have happened concerning the [man] who toward you
6     having caused annoyance we came to
7     the Serapeum wishing to meet with you.
8     but having heard in the
9     great Serapeum
10    you being, they said, [in] Sachmi of the Letopolite [territory], you would do well then to come to us to Poiei,
11    because we are about to sail to the king [so that] we deliver a petition concerning
12    you to the king.
13    Farewell. Year 29
14    Mesore 26.
15    To Dakoutis.
