[TRANSLATION]
1     Maimachos to Zenon greetings. You wrote to me to give to Kallippos
2     and Amortaios the pastures in the Isieion. I have done this therefore as you
3     instructed me, and the whole matter … [they] from you,
4     I will do everything necessary as if you [were present] and ordering. Moreover you will
5     do well as you promised me, having settled [me] with Diotimos and having spoken
6     about me, so that I might be well cared for by him. But I swear to you by the king's daemon
7     and [by the daemon] of Arsinoe that I have neither now nor before spoken anything inappropriate about the man
8     to anyone nor against [those] with him, but being concerned
9     I continue and I am well-liked by all those with him [as] many as respect him.
10    Moreover, I have also written him a letter; deliver it therefore to him yourself. And you also do not
11    abandon us. If ... of the king and Apollonius' fortune
12    willing, if it seems better to you to remain in the city from what we hear,
13    take care [for] Demetrios, so that [he] might provide you with needs in the city
14    with you; for he will be blameless for you. But if you also need one of the other sons
15    I will also send these to you; for they will serve you just like
16    [to] a brother blamelessly. And if it seems good to you, write
17    to Aristandros about me, so that he [may not] be ignorant, but [might] care for us.
18    Farewell. Year 35, Phaophi 14
19    To Zenon
20    To Aristandros. If you are well, it would be good; and we too are well.
21    You will do well toward Maimachos the nomarch [by]
22    assisting
23    and helping [him]
24    For this [being] done [it] has been ordained [that he] will be more useful
25    [he] will be more eager and to you more useful.
