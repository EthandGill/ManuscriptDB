1     To Apollonius, dekaprōtos of the middle toparchy, wheat [at] 100 artabas.
2     To Andromachus, another
3     dekaprōtos of the same toparchy, [wheat at] 318½ artabas [and] 2 choinikes.
4     To Alexander, former
5     dekaprōtos of the same
6     toparchy, [wheat at] 61 artabas.
