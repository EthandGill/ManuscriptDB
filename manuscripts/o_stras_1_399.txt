[TRANSLATION]
1     measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, grain of year 14 of the lords Emperors
2     Severus and Antoninus, pious Augustuses
3     Hathyr 25 of year 15, on behalf of Charax in the name of the heirs of Psais Onnophris, wheat: half, third, twelfth, comes to wheat 1/2 3/12. … I have marked wheat 1/2 3/12.
