[TRANSLATION]
1     Sotas [son] of Theogitus through the [agency of] father
2     Theogitus, to Sotatis [son] of Ptolemaeus
3     of Mysthos, greetings; I have received from you
4     from what you owe to the aforementioned
5     son of mine Sotatis according to [the] public
6     monetary obligation completed through
7     office of village Bubastus
8     on account of silver drachmai
9     one hundred four, total drachmai 104, not reduced for other things you owe him according as your documents show; year
10    third and twentieth of Aurelius
11    Commodus Antoninus Augustus, Pharmuthi, fifth
