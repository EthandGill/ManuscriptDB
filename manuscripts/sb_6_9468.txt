[TRANSLATION]
1     From Alypius.
2     Since the peace-official went up,
3     Castor, and was shaken not [little],
4     by my brother Maximus,
5     on account of you, ensure [to send back] the men [with] the [owed] money,
6     entirely before the [monthly].
7     For I, having labored much, [deemed worthy]
8     him to postpone [the day], providing me a great favor; either then let them go up or they will blame themselves.
9     I wish you to be well.
10    [The] Heronei[nos] the manager, Thraso.
11    [Of the year] [1]4 Kh[oia]k 10.
