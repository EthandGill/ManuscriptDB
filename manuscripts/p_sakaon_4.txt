1     Man-by-man (register) of village-holdings
2     of the village of Theadelphia, for the tenth
3     indiction. It is as follows:
4     Paneous, priest: of royal arable land, arouras 41 1/8 1/16;
5     of privately-owned arable land, arouras 6 1/4 1/16.
6     Pasis son of Neilammon: of privately-owned arable land, arouras 3 1/8.
7     Sakaon son of Satabous: of royal arable land, arouras 19 1/8;
8     of privately-owned arable land, [arouras …].
9     Psaon son of Horion: of royal arable land, arouras 33 1/4 1/8;
10    of privately-owned arable land, arouras 23 1/2 1/8 1/16 1/32.
11    Of royal unsown land, arouras 2 1/2 1/16 1/64.
12    (Query) Aeias daughter of Hermas, wife of Horion: of royal arable land, arouras 8 1/4.
13    Horion surnamed Kanol son of Heron: of royal unsown land, arouras 12 1/32.
14    Allion son of Satabous: of royal arable land, arouras 6 1/12;
15    of privately-owned arable land, arouras 6 1/2 1/16 1/32.
16    Esouris son of Horion: of royal … [arouras] 10 1/4.
17    … arouras 16 1/2 1/8 1/32.
18    Areion son of Dioskoros: of royal arable land, arouras 5 1/16;
19    of privately-owned arable land, arouras 21 1/32.
20    Of royal unsown land, arouras 2 1/16.
21    Of privately-owned unsown land, aroura 1 1/2 1/8 1/16 1/32.
22    Atiaina daughter of Dioskoros: of privately-owned arable land, arouras 44 1/4 1/8;
23    of privately-owned unsown land, arouras 3 1/8 1/32.
24    Sakaon son of Pemoutis: of privately-owned arable land, aroura 1 1/4 1/12.
25    Amis son of Paesis: of royal arable land, aroura 1 1/4 1/8.
26    In the consulship of Nepotianus and Facundus the
27    most illustrious, Choiak 19. …
28    … of Theadelphia
29    … two hundred and ninety-[two] talents, 292 talents;
30    … talents
31    one hundred and thirty, total 130 talents;
32    … total 14 talents.
