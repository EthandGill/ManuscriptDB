[TRANSLATION]
1     To Joa[nn]es Epagathos, my own (friend), very [many greetings];
2     you did not do well, having altered everything
3     and transgressed your agreement, the one
4     which acknowledged me to be [entitled]
5     to 20 drachmas and the interest; let me hold the cap[ita]l. [I]
6     marvel how you have changed your good faith.
7     Do not, then, compel me;
8     I wish to act, and on the spot
9     to wear you down, nor (to send) a little letter … the interest; this is a sign
10    of this ingratitude. And you shall say also to Taesis,
11    that you do not yet wish to embrace Epagathos
12    as a free man. Let Peripheragathos
13    not be negligent, if he can, to bring
14    to us both a son and … …
15    Know to make hope discov[erable].
16    Come with Taesis without vexation, [if]
17    he comes up. Do not neglect the other things … to bring to me; know …
18    to pay in 1 drachma, and Anthousa obols … [as also]
19    Taesis; know that I have not received
20    from Prosdokimos the copper-money.
21    Bring to us 20, and not 1. Pheragathos salutes you
22    and Taesis and Lysis,
23    Anthousa salutes you. Salute
24    Philadelphos and all his people.
25    Farewell; before all things take care of yourself, that you may be in health.
26    [In year] 7 of the Emperor Caesar Domitian
27    Augustus Germanicus, Choiak 19.
28    [T]o Ptolemais of Hermeios;
29    … to the … -guard, so as [to convey] to Epag[athos].
