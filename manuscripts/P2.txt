FOLIO Recto — John 12:12-15
12:12   (heading: "Gospel according to John") On the next day, the great crowd that had come to the feast, hearing that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem,
12:13   took the palm branches and went out to meet him, and were crying out: "Hosanna! Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord — and the King of Israel!"
12:14   And Jesus, having found a young donkey, sat on it, just as it is written:
12:15   "Fear not, daughter of Zion; behold, your King comes, seated on a donkey's colt."
