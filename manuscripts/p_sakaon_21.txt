1     Aurelius Neileus, councillor, receiver
2     of barley for the city,
3     to Aurelii Soulpous and Heron and
4     [their] partners, sitologoi of Theadelphia, through Arion
5     son of Dioskoros, greeting.
6     I have received from you, on behalf of the 7th indiction,
7     of the villagers of Theadelphia, of clean barley
8     ten and a half artabae, total 10½ artabae, only.
9     In the consulship of our masters Constantine Augustus for the 5th time
10    and Licinius the most illustrious Caesar for the 1st time, Tybi 3.
11    I, Neileus, have signed.
12    Aurelii Harpokration and Sarapammon
13    and Apollonios and Syrion, receivers of the harbor
14    of the city, to Aurelii Heron and Aeil and to the partners, sitologoi of the village of Theadelphia, greeting.
15    We have received from you at the same
16    harbor, on behalf of the produce of the 7th indiction,
17    of the villagers of the same village, by the modius measure,
18    leveled off, one hundred and eleven artabae of wheat, only.
19    In the consulship of our masters Constantine
20    Augustus for the 6th time and Constantine the most illustrious
21    Caesar for the 1st time, Mecheir 14. I, Harpokration, have signed. I, Sarapammon, have signed.

23    same others fifty-one artabae of wheat, total 51 artabae, only.
