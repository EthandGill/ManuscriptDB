[TRANSLATION]
1     Our father died, [leaving behind] various properties, movable and immovable. Since therefore heirs were appointed of the things left behind by this my very
2     father — and these, having led me by persuasion to be content with
3     one and a half arourae, ownerless, from the
4     inheritance left behind, and nothing
5     else; and I myself, being a maiden, on account of this frail
6     condition, yielded to their entreaty; but they appear to have been paid off in full with gold and
7     silver and slaves and cattle. And I do not know how,
8     after eighteen years, one of our brothers, called Kyros,
9     being a soldier of the same unit, tears away the one and a half arourae given to me.
10    And thus he took his stand and reaped it as his own gain on the threshing-floor in the back-country toward the townsfolk, [as]
11    for his own profit. Would that you might leave me, the wretched woman in need, my
12    daily livelihood. For this reason I approach, begging and beseeching
13    that you may regard the misery concerning the poor woman; that you may command the same Kyros to be summoned and [to withdraw] henceforth, or rather indeed to give the portion falling to me,
14    [to give.]
15    [I pray for] your [health], lord.
16    This was done in the presence
17    of Erymanthios, flavialis.
