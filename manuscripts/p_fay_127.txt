[TRANSLATION]
1   Taorsenuphis to Ision her mother,
2   many greetings.
3   Before all I pray that you are in
4   health, and I make obeisance for you be-
5   fore the lord Sarapis. You will do
6   well to give the share falling to you
7   of the fruit of the vineyard
8   to your sister, with the
9   grapes. I sent you 3 [pai]rs of
10   bowls — for you 1, and for Petesouchos 1,
11   and for the in-laws of your sister
12   1, and a little cup for Theo-
13   nas the little one, and another for the daugh-
14   ter of your sister; and if you get
15   lentils, send me some by Katoi-
16   tos.
17   From Taorsenuphis, to (her) mother.
