1     From Menicius Serapion and the
2     other overseers of Exopyle
3     and of Echanodemos of Psi. I have registered
4     to you an account of those from 6 until 10

6     the lord, concerning nothing
7     has accrued from the aforementioned [supervi]sion, and I received
8     the equal receipt[s]. Meni[cius]
9     Serapion I have presented, [as]
