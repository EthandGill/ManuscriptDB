1     In the consulship of our lords Constantine Augustus for the 6th time and Constantine the most noble Caesar [for the 1st time], Phamenoth 18.
2     Valerius Aeion, centurion of the vexillation of the cavalry
3     promoti of the Second Legion Trajana, those under Decentius the praepositus, stationed at
4     in the village of Asphynis in the Latopolite nome, to Aurelii Heras and Abouthios and Hatres and Thaesis and
5     Heroïs, my five brothers and sisters of the same parents, on the father's side Attitius, on the mother's side Timarete, [from the village of] … ikos of the
6     Arsinoite nome, on behalf of five shares; and to Aureliae Senpachoumis,
7     daughter of Bekios Lameieis the elder, on the mother's side Ta the younger, my wife, and to … aeris my daughter, on the mother's side the aforesaid Senpachoumis, the
8     two of them, on behalf of two shares, from the same and aforesaid village of Asphynis of the same Latopolite nome … seven, being of sound mind and reason and
9     understanding what I say, indeed also … as long as I am alive
10    and in health, I have control of my below-written property; but if anything human should befall me in the most grievous illness that has overtaken
11    me, that you, the aforesaid
12    seven heirs, inherit everything that shall be left by me, the … household effects. And it is, item by item, as follows: in gold:
13    of gold solidi, two in deposit, and with Skeuas through a bond two, and with Kephalon likewise [through] a bond two,
