# HGV-EN: Year 22 of Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus Parthicus Maximus Britannicus Maximus <Germanicus Maximus> Pius Augustus, on the first of the month of Tybi. Paid by Aurelius Didymos, Alexandrian to Aurelius Apion and however he is styled, nomarch of the Arsinoite nome, through Neilammon, pragmateutes of the toll-gate of Philadelphia, for the sealing of one calf, which is to be sacrificed in the village.
