[TRANSLATION]
1     Callistratus to Hierax the
2     brother, greetings. I found for you grain [costing] twenty-four obols. [If] you want [it], [I] will send
3     to you, and a half-measure
4     of salt [costing] three obols I bought for you from a soldier.
5     If therefore you want, write
6     so that to you I will send [them].
7     Farewell.
