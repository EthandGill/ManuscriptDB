# ── TRANSLATION ──────────────────────────────────────────────

[TRANSLATION]

1:1   The book of the genealogy of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham.
1:2   Abraham begat Isaac, Isaac begat Jacob, Jacob begat Judah and his brothers,
1:3   Judah begat Phares and Zare from Thamar, Phares begat Esrom, Esrom begat Aram,
1:4   Aram begat Aminadab, Aminadab begat Naason, Naason begat Salmon,
1:5   Salmon begat Boes from Rachab, Boes begat Iobed from Routh, Iobed begat Jesse,
1:6   Jesse begat David the king. David begat Solomon from the wife of Oureiou,
1:7   Solomon begat Roboam, Roboam begat Abeia, Abeia begat Asaph,
1:8   Asaph begat Iosaphat, Iosaphat begat Ioram, Ioram begat Ozeian,
1:9   Ozeias begat — [lines 1:9b–11 not preserved]
1:12  After the deportation to Babylon, Iechonias begat Salathiel, Salathiel begat Zorobabel,
1:13  Zorobabel begat — [lines 1:13b not preserved]
1:14  Sadok begat Achim, Achim begat Elioud,
1:15  Elioud begat Eleazar, Eleazar begat Maththan, Maththan begat Iakob,
1:16  Iakob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, from whom was born Jesus who is called Christ.
1:17  All the generations from Abraham to David are 14, and from David to the deportation to Babylon 14, and from the deportation to Babylon to the Christ 14.
1:18  Now the birth of Jesus Christ was thus: when his mother Mary was betrothed to Joseph, before they came together she was found with child from the Holy Spirit.
1:19  Joseph her husband, being just and not wishing to expose her, resolved to divorce her quietly.
1:20  While he was pondering these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying: Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife; for what was conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit.
