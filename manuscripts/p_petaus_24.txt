[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollotas, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2     to the village-scribe of the places subject to him.
3     A copy of a petition presented to me by Harmiysis is sent to you through Euporos, the assistant,
4     in order that, at your own risk, going to the property indicated, in the presence of all the
5     neighbouring landholders and of whatever others is necessary,
6     in accordance with the securities produced before you and the books in the registry,
7     you may deliver to each his own, no public or private (interest) being damaged,
8     the responsibility resting upon you if anything is not
9     done properly. I have signed.
10    [In the … year] of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar
11    the lord … …
12    To Apollotas, strategos of [the Arsinoite,] the Herakleides division,
13    from Harmiysis son of Apynchis and P…
14    son of Psonsneus, both public farmers
15    of the village of Syron. Of the four arourae which we farm around the village of public land …
16    … … … …
17    of Neseus … and Sokma…
18    … boundary … whence we present (this) and request that instruction be sent to the
19    village-scribe of the village to go to
20    the places with those who are needed and, having measured out the whole assessment, to
21    restore to each his own, so that we may be able
22    to discharge the taxes on the arourae without reproach.
23    I, Euporos, the assistant, have delivered (this).
