1     Φ … ι of year 19
2     of year 19 of Antoninus [Caesar]
3     of the lord, Pauni 22.
4     Agathus having been called and not having obeyed, after other matters
5     Liberalis said: the [epicephalia] was remitted to him: the
6     [appearing] to be [debts], the [hand-worthy],
7     which is of the work, [is separated], [towards] other [assigned]. And he subscribed
8     If he does not use the work,
9     let [it] not be [driven].
10    I portrayed
11    Eutychus.
12    of year 19 of Antoninus.
