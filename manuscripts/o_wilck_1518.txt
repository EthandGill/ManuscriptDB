[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 33, Hathyr 18: there has been paid to
2     the bank in Hermonthis, of which Ammonios (is in charge),
3     (for) the apomoira of year 32: Psemonthes (son of) Diphilos,
4     from the counter-payment, of equivalent copper, two hundred
5     thirty-five, total 235.
6     600(?). Ammonios, banker.
