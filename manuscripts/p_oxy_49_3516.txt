[TRANSLATION]
1     Nemesianus to Severus greetings.
2     Give to Ammonios, manager of certain parts of Souos, for the payments
3     of the vintagers and of the water-carriers of the month Pachon, and for the other
4     of the management expenses wheat of [year] 4 artabas four and of wine of [year]
5     6 [year] of Libyan estate press 3 from the [items] left … jars 46 of which the prices were sent [to] Sarapias the clerk to record, of which for the wheat as [at] one [drachma] drachmas 16
6     Pachon 30
7     and of the wine as of the jar one drachma, drachmas 12. Farewell, and give the
8     wheat artabas four and wine jars forty-six, [which] make wheat artabas 46 jars 4. [Year] 7 Pachon 30.
