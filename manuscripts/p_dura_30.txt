[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the imposed (charges).
2     In the consulship of Urius Lupus and Marius Maximus, on the Kalends of October, of the month
3     Hyperberetaios on the new-moon day, in Katna, at the winter-quarters of the Twelfth Cohort
4     … of the Palestinian … Severiana Alexandriana.
5     Good fortune.
6     They made acknowledgment and drew up a contract with one another on the present day,
7     Aurelius Alexandros, a soldier of the aforesaid cohort, of the century of Papios,
8     and Aurelia Markelleina daughter of Markelleinos, dwelling in Katna, there being present with her … nes, her mother, and Agrippeinos, her brother — namely, that
9     Markelleina has given herself out of widowhood for a marriage-
10    union with Alexandros and to remain together with one another for all
11    time … bringing as a dowry … from her property
12    … in garments and effects appraised, by suitable men as arbitrators,
13    the kinds written below: a white pallium, empty (plain), one hundred [and twenty]-
14    five denarii; another white pallium, new, fifty denarii; a tunic
15    … empty, of forty denarii; a dalmatic of natural color, new,
16    thirty [denarii]; a scarlet dalmatic and a purple maphorion,
17    new, seventy-five denarii; another white dalmatic, new,
18    fifty denarii; a … purple, new, denarii
19    twenty-five; Median garments and cross-bordered pieces, new, fifty denarii;
20    earthenware vessels and bronze utensils, twenty-five denarii; earrings and finger-rings
21    … fifty denarii … the things brought by her (totaling)
22    five hundred [and sixty]-five denarii of silver, which … Alexandros
23    acknowledged that he had received and held … the kinds brought to his own (account) and that he consents to the things that have come to him and … one hundred
24    seventy-five denarii he owes … as a free dowry of silver, seven hundred fifty denarii, an agreement having been made between them
25    [that if] Alexandros wishes to remove Markelleina … on account of …
26    … ser … ou … be … u …
27    … and the property … ou …
28    … en … ō … ekro … the … ia
29    … th … nei … [he shall repay what] he received, denarii … r … between them
30    it was agreed; they happened; if anyone … each of the two esche-ti …
31    Faustinus Avianus, veteran, having been asked, wrote
32    [on behalf of Aurelia] Markelleina daughter of Markelleinos, she being illiterate
33    and consenting [in accordance with the] aforesaid (terms).
34    [Aurelius] Alexandros: I consent [in accordance with the] aforesaid (terms) …
