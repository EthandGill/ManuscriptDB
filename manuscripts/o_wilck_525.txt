[TRANSLATION]
1   Apollodoros, collector, (to) Amenothes son of Harbechis.
2   He received, for poll-tax and bath-tax of the Charax (quarter), year 5, in base (coin), twelve drachmas
3   — total, base, 12 dr.; and in the name of Pamonthes (his) brother,
4   likewise for poll-tax and bath-tax of year 5, in base (coin), 12 dr. — total, base, 12 dr.
5   Year 5 of Hadrian Caesar
6   the lord, Phamenoth 30. Pek( ).
