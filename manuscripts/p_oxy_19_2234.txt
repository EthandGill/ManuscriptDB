[TRANSLATION]
1     To Quintus Caius Passer, centurion,
2     from Hermon son of Demetrios. There belongs to me, about the village of Tei in the Thmoisepho toparchy of the
3     Oxyrhynchite, a grandfather's and father's allotment called Dryos, in which is a public dyke and a cistern in the midst of our (lands), and naubia
4     and other (lands), for which I pay all the appropriate public dues; but being violently oppressed and plundered by the fishermen Pausis and
5     Papsious and his brother and Kaleous and
6     Melas and Attinos and Pasois and those
7     with them, who are not few. Having brought in also Titius a soldier with many
8     nets and scraping-tools, having come upon
9     our cistern with drag-ropes, they fished and hauled up fish worth, all told,
10    one talent of silver; and when I remonstrated
11    with [them], they threatened me as if intending
12    to do away with(?) me. Since they oppress me in many ways,
13    making my recourse to you
14    I request, if it seem good, that the accused be brought before you, so that they may pay me the
15    value of the fish, as aforesaid, and in future abstain from our (property), that I may be
16    benefited. Farewell.
17    Year 17 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Pachon 17.
