[TRANSLATION]
1     The shepherd of Zachaios, the one called by Philippos, [since] you entreated me, I gave the woman to you out this day and tomorrow, the excellence of children in the heart and their oath to me … he took
2     … their word of our Lord therefore may they come to the earth and
3     they may judge, go forth being sent and come to her to turn
4     to them, a joy in the Lord.
