1     Aurelius Sarapion son of Asklepios, singularis of the prefect of Egypt,
2     [being] in military service, Syros also
3     called Sarapion, his mother being …aliane also called Amnessous,
4     daughter of Serenos, … leased …
5     … let the lessee use the lease-receipt for the leased property
6     without hindrance for the period …
7     to sow grain-crops and to plant trees
8     wherever the lessee chooses, free of woad and of … and, at the end of the period, let him hand over
9     the building-plots clean from manure
10    and all filth, and the
11    existing doors and keys, and the land [clean]
12    from public dues and all taxes and impositions of every kind.
13    The lease-receipt is valid, and being formally questioned by one another they gave their assent.
