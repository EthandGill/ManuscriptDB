[TRANSLATION]
1     [C]opy of record [of the year] 6 of Hadri[a]n Caes[ar] [of the lord]. To Naomsat of Tapiesis of Horus
2     Julius Quadr[at]us: [he] asked how long ago [did he] build? Hierax: three years ago. Julius Quadratus, chiliarch: you ought to have [prevented him] then
3     when he was building, but in all this time you did not approach neither the strategos nor anyone else. Excessively even now you bring a charge, and
4     this [is] against the law of the Egyptians
