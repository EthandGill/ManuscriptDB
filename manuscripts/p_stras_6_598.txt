[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the most … Thoth 13, 5th indiction.
2     … [to the most] noble (lady) from the (city) of the Hermopolites,
3     from … [his mother being] Kyrilla, and Phoibammon son of Ales, his mother being
4     … [both from the village of Ens]eu, farmers. We acknowledge, being mutually sureties
5     [and mutual guarantors, voluntarily and] of our own choice, that we have leased from your nobility,
6     [for … time, reckoned] from the crops of the fortunate coming sixth
7     [indiction and it (itself), your share] of sixteen and a half and a quarter arouras, with the (share) falling (to us) … [in common with] your brothers,
8     [on account of the remaining part …, making up] the sixteen and a half and a quarter
9     [arouras …], that is, from the northern
10    [edge …] … the old boundary,
11    … [under the guard]ianship of those from the village of Enseu
12    [of the Hermopolite nome, for sowing and planting of] crops of whatever we may choose,
13    [as rent for these each year, for the flooded (land), of] wheat-and-barley, half and half,
14    [of … artabas … and for the unflooded (land), which may it not be,] the half of the aforesaid
15    [rent …] with a rent of vegetable-seed
16    … the cutting of an aroura, a sixth
17    … [which rent, new, clean,] sifted,
18    … [we shall pay and measure] out by the Athenian measure at the time
19    [of harvest of each year …, in the month] Epeiph, and we shall restore (it) with animals …
