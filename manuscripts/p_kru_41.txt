[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving
2     consubstantial Trinity, Father and Son
3     and Holy Spirit. It was written in the month Pachon 4, of the 2nd indiction,
4     under our master, through Flavius Kometos
5     son of Chael, administrator of the fortress of Memnoneion.
6     I, Epiphaneios the son of the late
7     Pcher, the man of the fortress of Djeme, the nome of the city of Ermont, subscribing
8     with my own hands, and
9     calling upon some other trustworthy witnesses,
10    these who shall bear witness to it for me,
11    to this written deed of division, which is a settlement, undisturbable, and
12    unannullable, and unshakable
13    out of the laws, I write to one
14    son of Seueros, my genuine brother,
15    the man of this same fortress, Djeme, the nome of this same city, greetings: since in this present time
16    which lies before us now, the 2nd indiction, we sued
17    [one an]other before the most glorious comes
18    the administrator, concerning the whole inheritance
19    of our late father Pcher, in houses, in
20    courtyards, in any garment, which pertain
21    to his inheritance, that we should divide it
22    among us, so that whatever falls to each one he may take
23    and become its master and carry it to his own account
24    alone. After the division, then, of the pleadings which we made with one another
25    before his most glorious self, the comes the administrator,
26    he ordered us, according to the manner in which we convinced one another, we … free men in the fortress,
27    he cast them into the houses and the other rooms, they mediated them
28    upon us, according to the manner in which it pleased us.
29    [Of] the two shares he cast a lot
30    up; the lot came up upon you,
31    you, my brother Souai the son
32    [of Seu]eros, the one which I first wrote out:
33    you have set up the new house, to … the … the disturbance (?)
34    of Souai of Philotheos, so that you may become
35    its master from its foundations to its air,
36    with the other outbuildings which pertain
37    to it on your part, with your sons and your daughters and those who come after you, according to
38    its boundaries which surround it, which are these: on the south
39    the room, the north, [the] court, the blind one, inward;
40    the east the public road, and the place
41    where the door of the owners opens onto it;
42    the west the house of the brethren of my father — these are the boundaries
43    of that whole house on every side.
44    And again you have set up … it … a (?)
45    inward toward it, and the … workshop of … (?) the hir-hir (court) upon it inward toward it according to these boundaries which surround it — these are your shares
46    which you set up, you Souai the son
47    of Seueros, in the houses and the other rooms.
48    From now then to you, the one which I first
49    wrote out, you shall come in and possess,
50    and become master of the half-share of the abode of my life (?),
51    of the dux, this which is upon … (?)
52    of stone, from its foundations to its air,
53    of his fourth, the half part, according to
54    the division which I first declared
55    above, to beget them to you, to beget them to you again, to administer them
56    to administer them, to embellish
57    them, to dwell in them, to give them
58    to the pawn, to bestow them, to give them away, to leave them to your children [and] your heirs,
59    to use them in every fashion
60    which pleases you, in every possession
61    at any time. Whoever shall come out against you ever, neither I,
62    nor a brother, nor a sister, nor
63    any single one, nor a second time, nor
64    on behalf of my father and my mother, shall sue you in a court of law
65    or outside a court of law, in the district,
66    or outside the district, nor make accusation
67    against you, nor make accusation first;
68    that one shall profit nothing,
69    being first of all alien to the Father and the Son and the Spirit,
70    and he shall give to the account of the penalty
71    twenty-four holokottinoi of gold
72    of the authority which now exists,
73    apart from the punishment which our law
74    appoints upon the one who shall dare to transgress against all these,
75    and he shall come in and confirm
76    this settlement, it being valid for you, with
77    a deed, undisputable in every place
78    where they shall manifest it through
79    any magistracy or authority, and they shall question me,
80    that I agree.
81    I, Epiphani, son of the late Pcher, agree
82    to this settlement according to the manner in which it stands.
83    I, Stratke the son of Helias, bear witness according to the request of Pephane. I, Leontios his son, wrote for him, since he does not know how to write.
84    I, Makari the son of the late Kosma, bear witness. I, Pabiktor the son
85    of the late Panachore, he asked me, I wrote for him, and I bear witness.
86    I, Kollouthos son of Iohannes, bear witness
87    according to the request of Epephanios.
88    I, Kostantine the son of Pkire, bear witness.
89    Through me, Aristophanes son of Iohannes, it was done.
