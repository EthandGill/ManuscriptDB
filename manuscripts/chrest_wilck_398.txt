[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of an edict. Serenos, royal
2     scribe of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite,
3     deputizing for the affairs of the strategia.
4     For the assessing of the property among the unsold goods,
5     in place of Gaius Julius son of Ptollis, and Amarantos
6     son of Hestiaios, and Heron called
7     Eudaimon, and Diodoros son of Theogeiton,
8     the four who have completed the appointed
9     time, and Anoubion son of Heron and Demetrios
10    son of Souchammon, the two declared to have died, the undersigned have been nominated by the scribes of the city as
11    well-off and suitable for whom there are public duties. They are commanded
12    to take up the task entrusted to them
13    soundly and faithfully so as in nothing
14    to be blamed. I have signed. Year 9 of Aurelius
15    Antoninus Caesar the lord, Armeniacus
16    Medicus Parthicus Maximus, Mesore 17.
17    They are: Gaius Julius Apolinarios, landholding
18    in Karanis, having a resource of 4,000 drachmas.
19    Mysthes son of Cornelius, landholding in Ptolemais
20    Nea, having a resource of 1 talent.
21    Antonius Heraklianos, landholding in the (estate)
22    of Nestos, likewise 4,000 drachmas.
23    Gaius Julius Satorneilos, landholding in Tanis,
24    having a resource of 4,000 drachmas.
25    Ptolemaios, also styled nomographos, of Pharbaitha,
26    having a resource of 4,000 drachmas.
27    Pasion son of Petermouthis son of Petermouthis,
28    from Hellenion, having a resource of 4,000 drachmas.
29    Heron the assistant, having been assigned … , I filed it, Mesore 20.
