[TRANSLATION]
1     … [in the city of the Oxyrhynchites. To the exactor's] office [of the divi]sion an[d house] of the [i]llustrious
2     memory of Timagenes, through Philoxenos the assistant
3     of the same exactor's office, Flavius Prosdokios, centenarius
4     of the unit of the most noble and most devoted
5     lion-clibanarii, son of Satornilos of most excellent memory,
6     subscribing below, through those (officials) here of the same
7     Oxyrhynchite (city). Relieve from the public
8     tax-rolls in your keeping, from the name of Ioulianos
9     the ship-captain, son of Martyrios, from the fourteenth indiction, the taxes of eighteen arouras
10    of private category, and bring over and
11    register (them) under my designation,
12    I paying the taxes of the eighteen
13    arouras, as has been said, of private
14    category, from the same fourteenth
15    indiction and for the time thereafter, for as long as
16    the contract given to me by the same
17    Ioulianos of the personal-security mortgage subsists.
18    And for the security of the public treasury I have made these orders of the registration with my subscription, as is set out above. I, Aurelius
19    Philoxenos, assistant of the exactor's office of the same house of Timagenes,
20    have received the authentic order.
21    [Order of registration] of the lord Prosdok[i]os. Valid.
22    of private (land), 18 arouras.
