[TRANSLATION]
1     Record of treasury of metropolis of produce, 22nd year of Antoninus Caesar of the lord
2     Pauni 25th, for Markets, in the name of Hermes the greatest god, through Patreios
3     similarly [of] Psenasouchos, which through Petehnumios
4     Pamonth of wheat, artabas thirteen [and] 1/6, becomes wheat artabas 13 [and] 1/6.
5     Sabinos has marked.
