1     To Ammonios strategos of Arsinoite
2     of the Heracleidan division
3     from Peteureos of Peteureos the elder
4     of priests of Soknobraisios
5     of god great great
6     of the village Bakkhiados.
7     I have registered to you
8     a register of priests and
9     of the management of the aforesaid temple of the
10    present 29th year
11    [M]arcus Aurelius
12    [C]ommodus Antoninus
13    Caesar of the lord
