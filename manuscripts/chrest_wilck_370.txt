[TRANSLATION]
1     To Marcus Antonius Pallas,
2     from Menodoros also called Ammonios son of Dioskoros, and Eu[da]imon son of Kynos, and Hermaios
3     and Metokos, both sons of Eudemos, and Di[o]s son of Heron, [and D]idymos son of Amphion. We, the six,
4     Herm[opo]lites, wish willingly to lease, for two years from the present
5     6th year of Hadri[an Caesar] the lord, from the lands belonging to you in the Lower Leukopyrgite around Panna… from the holding of Pona…us also called Glaukos, 111¾ 1/16 arouras previously cultivated by Hermesion son of Hermaios, of which … aroura… of barley fixed rent of six and a quarter artabas, and of the other arouras the ones lying fallow for the sowing of wheat, for wheat, by the public receiving-measure, five artabas
6     and the remaining ones for the sowing of vetch and of fodder, for the feeding of sheep and their bedding, 12 arouras, and for
7     the feeding of cattle 13 arouras, and for cutting the remaining ones at a rent for each aroura of 16 silver drachmas
8     for the donkey-transport-charge of the barley at the rate of 1½ per 100 artabas, to which we add for the surcharge of wheat, by the public [receiving-measure], five artabas; and the rents we shall pay back yearly in Pauni and
9     Epeiph, by joint surety, the wheat into the public granaries, and the silver and the barley to the stewards of the estate, and we shall give yearly, by way of libation-offering, of silver
10    twelve drachmas. Year six of Imperator Caesar [Traja]nus Ha[d]rianus Augustus, Phaophi 9.
