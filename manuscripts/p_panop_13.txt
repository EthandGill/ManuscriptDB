[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelia Theodora of Paniscus from the city of Panopolis
2     from Aurelius Psaitus son of Horus from the village of Psone
3     [of] Panopolis. I have rented from [you] a part of a house for a three-year period, a reception-room and courtyard in Nous street, and I will pay you for the rent of each year in silver
4     of Augustuses currency, talents twenty
5     five, totaling twenty-five talents only, and after the [stated] time I will return to you [the property] … as I received it, and when questioned I acknowledged [this].
6     in the consulship of our masters
7     Constantius [for] the second [time] and Constantine
8     [for] the first [time] of the eternal Augustuses, Choiak 26.
