[TRANSLATION]
1     […] Sophia with god, of patrician rank, acting and on behalf of my glorious son Olympios the notary, provide [money] to Pamoun the builder and younger, [?], and Sambat, [collectively] three
2     departing into Syria in order that [they] burn raw bricks for the need of the suburb there, on account of expenditures, other days, 10 myriads, 400, and on account of workers
3     myriads 180, together myriads 600, myriads six-hundred thirty [?] Phamenoth 16, ninth indiction
