[TRANSLATION]
1   Psensentithoes son of Pibouchis paid,
2   for poll-tax of the South-and-West (quarter), year 7, 16 dr. Year 7
3   of Vespasian the lord, Phamenoth 17.
4   (2nd hand) Likewise Pachon 27, 4 dr.; likewise Epeiph 2,
5   for b( ) of year 7, 2 dr.; likewise for u…, of year 7, 1 dr. 2 ob.,
6   likewise Mesore 29, 3 dr. 1 ob., which are 2 dr. 5½ ob.
7   (3rd hand) Likewise (in) year 7, Pachon 27, for the guards' pay
8   of the South-and-West (quarter), year 7, 1 dr. 2½ ob.
