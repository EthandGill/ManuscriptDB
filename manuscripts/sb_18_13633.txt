[TRANSLATION]
1     Barakia the king of the nation of the Belhmuwe who rebel against us - they whom they call him as Christian - Sophia
2     That I command that it came to pass in the commerce alongside the fathers - those whom all men were in it when it came to pass [...]
3     As a free man it is not permitted men to lead away them
4     Forever because they placed throne upon my protection Charakhên the king [...]
5     I myself have commanded that it came to pass in the commerce in honor
6     [In]asmuch as it is not permitted men to hinder them and I stand by the charter that I commanded to Agathon the scribe he wrote the charter
7     Barakia basilisk witnesses, Tata phylarch witnesses, Eisoit sub-tyrant witnesses
8     Eutieka witnesses, Prekam witnesses, Hatika witnesses, Laize witnesses, Kaet witnesses
9     Nouphika phylarch witnesses. Written in the month Phamênôth 15, indiction 2
10    Through me Agathon the scribe I wrote according to the command of the
11    Most glorious Barakia, the basilisk
