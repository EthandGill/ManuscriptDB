[TRANSLATION]
1     [ruling Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and] queen Cleopatra the
2     sister and queen Cleopatra the wife of the gods Benefactors
3     in year 38, Mecheir 1st, was measured a plot in the city being built
4     Euergetes by Boethos the kinsman and military overseer and general
5     of the Thebaid and founder through Paniskos the inspector of plots and recorder
6     for Tanoupis, son of Theopios in the four-sided shopkeeper's lodging not
7     assessed in area nor to the circular tax nor anything else
8     simply, but to have possession properly the property as privately owned. and it will be permitted for her
9     to leave to descendants and to the same husband and to those nearest in kinship or whomever she should
10    choose of those in the city borne in military service these similarly nothing assessed
11    neither of those taking over nor of those yielding according to no
12    device following those ceded by the founder
13    of solid cubits two, there come cubits 2, of which measures to south upon north cubits 20,
14    west upon east cubits 10; neighbors to the south Petosirious a goldworker,
15    north the remaining places, east [a] stoa, west [a] street of cubits 10.
16    Paniskos have measured as indeed is set forth Tanoupis, son of Theopios
17    in the shopkeeper's in the four-square market cubits 2, there come cubits 2.
18    in year 38, Mecheir 1st.
19    has been registered.
20    in year 41, Pauni, 3rd.
