1     Asklas and Soter, tax collectors of silver of Charax
2     To Charon and Pamouthes of Charon. We received
3     For watchtowers and guards of Charax of the 21st year, small change, one drachma, two-obol [piece]
4     Total: small change, one drachma, two-obol [piece]. Of the 21st year of Hadrian
5     The master, Tybi 19.
