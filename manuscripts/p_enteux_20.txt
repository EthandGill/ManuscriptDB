[TRANSLATION]
1     To King [Ptolemy, greetings], [Crateia, of those] from the island of Alexandria. I am being wronged by
2     Philippus [and Dionysius. For my] brother Apollodotus being fellow-members with them
3     … with Maron, being one a priest, and the other
4     head of thiasus [, having died], of [Apollodotus], and besides neither having buried nor
5     [following him according to] [the thiasus] law, nor the burial-rights that are his
6     [not paid back. I beseech] you, king, if it seems good to you, to order Diophanes the strateg[us] to compel to give back to me the burial-fund. If this [having come to pass], I shall be
7     through you, king, I have [obtained justice]. Farewell.
8     … [having examined] the thiasus law, compel [the just things] to [be done].
9     … [if] anything they speak against, send them to us.
10    Year 1, Gorpiaeus 28, Tybi 12.
11    Year 1, Gorpia[us 28, Tybi 12].
12    Crateia to Phil[ippus and]
13    Dionysius concerning [burial-fund].
