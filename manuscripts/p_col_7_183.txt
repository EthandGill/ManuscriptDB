[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Domitius Modestus the most splendid prefect of the sacred praetorium and Flavius
2     Arinthaeus the most splendid count and master of the foot force, Hathur 27.
3     [She] agrees, Aurelia Tetoueis [daughter] of Atres, [of the] mother
4     Tapaetus, from the village Karani[dos]
5     to Flavius Ouenafrius, soldier of the legion
6     First Maximianensis stationed in Oxyrhynchus, to have from him [the one] agreeing
7     Tetoueis, [to provide] the agreed price of wheat
8     pure, six artabas, and [of] pure barley
9     six artabas, and [of] pure vegetable-seed
10    three artabas, which were [necessary]
11    [She] will deliver to Ouenafrius in the month Pauni of the second indiction by the [city] measure of four-choinikes,
12    and the [vegetable] by the [twelve-measure], and concerning recovery there is to be for Ouenafrius the enforcement
13    from both the agreeing Tetoueis and from the
14    things belonging to her, all of them as if from a judgment, and having been questioned she agreed.
15    Handwriting [of] Tetoueis Atres from Karanidos: [of wheat artabas 6 and of barley artabas 6] and of vegetables artabas 3.
