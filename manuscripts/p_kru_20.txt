1     In the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, the consubstantial Trinity, and the power and the strength and
2     the dispensation of the lords (who are) kings,
3     these who rule over all the earth
4     by the command of God the Almighty, on this
5     day, which is the 20th of this month
6     [Meso]re, of the twelfth year (of) the indiction,
7     before the most illustrious and honored
8     Komos, the son of the late Chael,
9     the administrator of the fortress (of) Djeme,
10    the nome of the city of Ermanth (Hermonthis): I,
11    Stephanos the son of the late Germanos, the man of the fortress (of) Djeme
12    and this same nome, will provide
13    afterwards the subscription of the one who shall subscribe for me, with trustworthy witnesses worthy to be believed,
14    those who shall witness
15    to this written deed of sale, according to
16    my own request and
17    my own will, willing and
18    persuaded, without any deceit or fear or extortion or any compulsion
19    against me, but of my free choice,
20    my own, and my own will,
21    not to transgress against it and
22    not to alter it and
23    not to annul it and not to do away with it through the laws; and moreover
24    to confirm it through witnesses
25    who are trustworthy, worthy to be believed, those who shall witness to this written deed of sale: I write to you, you Sinthios
26    the son of the late Senouthios, the man of this same fortress (of) Djeme, of this same nome, willing and persuaded,
27    without any deceit or fear
28    or extortion or any compulsion upon me,
29    but of my free choice,
30    my own, and my own will,
31    rather I swearing by the name of God
32    the Almighty and by the salvation of the rulers, these who rule over us,
33    by the command of God,
34    I sell to you the four (parts)
35    of the houses, this which is set down in the lane
36    of Koulol, this which came down to me from
37    my blessed father Germanos —
38    these are the shares of (the) places that I cause to come within it, the four (parts)
39    of the storehouse and the four (parts) of the upper banquet-room of the storehouse and the four (parts)
40    in the upper hall of the banquet-room,
41    and two of the chamber in the upper hall,
42    and the other chamber in the upper hall.
43    Yours it is, you Sinthios, with the banquet-room which is on the (?) below, of the new exedra of hymai — these are
44    all your parts which came upon you
45    within that same house, these which I have given over to you, you Sinthios,
46    the son of the late Senouthios, the buyer,
47    by the will of God; and the price
48    on which we agreed with one another (is) four gold solidi.
49    They came to (my) hand, hand to hand; from now, then, (it is) to you, you Sinthios, the son
50    of the late Senouthios, that you shall command
51    the four (parts) of all those houses,
52    this which I have given over to you, in every lordship of mastery
53    of the laws, so that no man on my father's (side) nor on my mother's (side)
54    may bring suit against you concerning it for ever and ever, neither I nor
55    brother nor son nor sister nor
56    son's son nor one nor
57    two nor descendant nor descendant
58    of descendant nor any man at all for ever, nor (in) going to a tribunal
59    nor outside a tribunal, in
60    city, in district, in praetorium.
61    The one who shall dare, bringing suit against you, shall give twelve
62    gold solidi (as) a fine
63    to the ruler of that time;
64    but you shall command it,
65    you building it, you demolishing it,
66    you re-building it to the height
67    up to the air, you bestowing it (as a gift), you giving it away as a gift, you keeping it for yourself.
68    But you shall command it for yourself
69    and your children and your children's children and all who come after you;
70    and the one who shall dare, bringing suit
71    against you concerning it, shall become
72    a stranger to the Father and the Son
73    and the Holy Spirit; and let him confirm the validity of this sale
74    and every matter that is written in it, in addition to
75    all these things. I swear by the name
76    of God the Almighty and
77    the salvation of the rulers (who are) kings,
78    these who rule over us, by
79    God, not to deceive (regarding) all that I have said in this sale and every matter
80    that is written in it. For your security, then,
81    I have established this sale, firm (and) valid
82    in every place where they shall declare
83    it, through any rule or any authority that has lordship
84    over it; they questioned me; I acknowledge as is set forth above.
85    I, Komos the son of (the) blessed Iohannes, give witness,
86    at the request of Stephanos. I, Kosma,
87    the son of (the) blessed Ioseph, give witness, at
88    the request (of) Stephanos.
89    I, Pesinthios the son of the late Ioseph, give
90    witness, at the request (of) Stephanos.
91    I, Dioskoros the son of (the) blessed Kosma,
92    give witness. I, Peseos the son of the late (…), I wrote for him because
93    he does not see (read), and I give witness.
94    I, Petros the son of [A]ntone, give witness,
95    at the request of Stephanos.
96    I, Stephanos the son of the late Germanos, consent.
97    I, Markos the son of the late Anastasios,
98    have established this sale with my hand, at the request of Stephanos the son of Germanos, and I give witness.
99    I, Staphare the son of the late Ioseph, give witness,
100   at the request (of) Stephanos.
101   I, Makare (son of) Georgios, give witness; I (witness to) the sale, at the request of Stephanos.
102   I, Papas the son of the late Zekiel,
