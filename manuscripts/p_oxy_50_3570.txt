[TRANSLATION]
1     To my [brother A]pollonius,
2     Besarion [greets you].
3     The colleague of the one having retired
4     from the office of vice-prefect, along with a certain official,
5     coming to us said that the beasts of
6     the prince of the governor are here
7     in number fifteen, to which they need hay of five mandakia,
8     and letters concerning the provision [of]
9     hay having been carried to Philiarchos the
10    strategos being present, written by the
11    prince. In order therefore that you take care of
12    its being sent here [with] fodder I write to you. But immediately do [this]
13    to be sent so that they may not on account of this matter seem to find fault with [us] [for] something.
14    Fare well.
