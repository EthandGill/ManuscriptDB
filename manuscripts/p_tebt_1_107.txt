[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemaios son of Meniskos to Megches greetings. I acknowledge
2     to have leased to you the existing [ones] in the
3     allotment of mine pasture arouras ten
4     [sown] for the 6th year of produce [of each] aroura of wheat five, so as to be
5     of wheat artabas fifty, and I agree to the lease and I will guarantee [to you]
6     and to whomever you lease.
7     Farewell. Year 6 [Hathyr] …
