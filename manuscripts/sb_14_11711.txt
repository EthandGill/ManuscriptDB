[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelia D[emetr]ia, also [called Ammonia], and as she is styled, from
2     Hermopolis, the most [illustrious] city
3     from Aur[elius Sar]apion, [patron]ius residing
4     in the village [of] Magdal[a] of Herdsmen of the Hermopolite [division].
5     I acknowledge [that I have received] from you for lease for a four-year period, [reckon]ed from the present month Mecheir
6     5 of the new indiction, the produce of the fortunate 6th new, that is,
7     of the 20th indiction, [the property] belonging to you near the same village
8     Magdala of Herdsmen, an olive-and-date-palm-garden which I described for
9     the rent agreed between us: in the first year, for black olives, [one and] one-half artabae, and
10    date-palms, of date-pits and dried [fruit], equally, one artaba
11    and one basket [nine-ply] and another five-ply,
12    and for the ensuing [three-year period, each] year, oil, two artabae
13    and date-palms [dried and pi]ckled, one artaba, and silver
14    of the Augustan [coin], six talents, and the same baskets, two,
15    which aforementioned [I will] repay in due time, each one
16    as stated [without hesitation] and the requisite land-service
17    I will perform from what is there
18    …; [and at] the end of the time I will return to you the same
19    garden [in the best] care, clean of debris and
20    every filth, [and the plant]s flourishing and the walls standing firm, as I received them; and the silver—all public taxes being the responsibility of you, the landholder. The lease is binding, and when questioned I agreed.
21    in the consulship of P[appius P]acatianus, the most illustrious prefect of
22    the sacred [praetorium], and Mecillius Hilarius, the most illustrious
23    [Mecheir] … [Aurelius Sar]apion, the aforementioned, [I have] leased as stated.
24    … [I wrote on his behalf] letters, not knowing.
