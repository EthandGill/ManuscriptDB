[TRANSLATION]
1     To Marcus Anthestius Gemellus, of the
2     discharged cavalrymen,
3     from Horos son of Horos, from the village of
4     Bakchias, a Persian of the epigone.
5     I wish to lease from you for
6     four years from the present
7     sixth year of Hadrian [Caesar the lord]
8     the [arouras belonging] to you [around the same]
9     village, five arouras [or however many there may be]
10    in [one] parcel, at a total rent
11    per year, together with which I [shall receive] as seed
12    five artabas of barley — twenty-five artabas of barley … by the
13    six-choinix measure of the village interpreter — and I shall perform the yearly tasks and [so]wings
14    and clearings and conduit-clearings,
15    sowing for the first three years as
16    I may choose, but in the fourth (year) with grass
17    for cutting dry; and the yearly
18    rents I shall pay you in the month Pauni
19    at the threshing-floor of the arouras, new, clean,
20    unadulterated, the yearly public transport-charges
21    being upon me, Horos; and after the period I shall hand over the arouras after the cutting of the grass, clear of r[ush], reed,
22    couch-grass and all rank growth, if it seem good to grant on the af[oresai]d (terms) an[d]
23    free from public (charges). I, Marcus Anth[e]sti[u]s
24    Gemellus, have leased as aforesaid. In the sixth year of Imperator Caesar
25    Trajan Hadrian Au[gustus], in the month
26    Mesore, on the intercalary day … .
