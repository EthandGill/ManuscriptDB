[TRANSLATION]
1     To Calpurnius Concessus, the most excellent epistrategos,
2     from Gemellus also called Horion, son of Gaius Apollinarius, of Antinoopolis.
3     Of the petition which I submitted to the most illustrious prefect Aemilius Saturninus,
4     and of the sacred subscription of his which I obtained, I have appended a copy. I request, if it
5     seem good to your Fortune, that you write to the centurion stationed in the Arsinoite nome to send
6     my adversary to your court and to hear me against him, so that I may obtain
7     justice. It is as follows:
8     To Quintus Aemilius Saturninus, prefect of Egypt,
9     from Gemellus also called Horion, son of Gaius Apollinarius, of Antinoopolis, and however I am styled. I appeal, lord, against Castor, agent of the village of Karanis in the
10    division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nome. This man, despising me as infirm —
11    for being one-eyed I do not see even with the eye that seems to see, so that in both eyes
12    I am plainly weak — extorted from me, and previously, having insulted me
13    publicly along with my mother, after beating her with very many blows
14    and with an axe smashing all four of my doors, so that our whole
15    house became wide open and easy of access to every malefactor — these things
16    being smashed and carried off when we owe [nothing]
17    to the treasury, whence he did not even dare to produce a receipt [lest he be]
18    convicted through it [of doing wrong] or of having extorted. Therefore, since the Savior has ordained that [those who are wronged]
19    may approach you fearlessly to obtain justice, I request [from you, lord,]
20    to be heard and to obtain redress so that I may be benefited, and that the a[dversary be se]nt by your authority to your court. I, Gemellus, [have submitted it].
21    I, Germanus, wrote on his behalf. Year 6, Mesore 18. To the most exce[llent epistra]tegos appeal in regard to his side, since it will not be wanting; deli[ver].
22    I, Gemellus also called Horion, have submitted it as aforesaid. …
23    I, Panebtichis, wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters.
24    Year 6, of the intercalary days, 3rd.
25    To the one now present in the locality
26    appeal. Deliver.
