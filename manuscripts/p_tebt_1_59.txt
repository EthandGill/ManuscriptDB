[TRANSLATION]
1     Poseidonios to the priests in Teptynei
2     greetings and be well; [may I] be healthy
3     also myself. For having arrived
4     in the city of Sokonopai
5     and Oipai [coming] from you
6     and showing [the kind of] which you have
7     toward us from of old, [a] paternal
8     friendship—in whatever [matters] if you should need
9     of me, commanding me, [I will be] all the more ready because of [my] reverence from of old
10    and [my] reverence for the sacred [shrine],
11    Farewell. Year 16, Phaophi
12    9.
13    From
14    Poseidonios
15    to the priests in Te[p]tynei.
