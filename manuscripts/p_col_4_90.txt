[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum of Zenodoros. …
2     … of the slaves who were formerly … …
3     … of those at Philadelphia … at the treasury … …
4     … with the royal scribe and the village-scribe …
5     of iron and of the rest of the things being brought in
6     to Philadelphia, it happened that 15 mattocks were missing. But Eukles the steward forces me
7     to make good only (that). And there is owed to me in addition
8     wages of the 2nd year, of 4 months, and of the
9     3rd year, of 3 months, total 7 months, at 5 drachmas per month, total 35 drachmas,
10    and clothing-allowance for 3 years, at 20 drachmas per year, total 60 drachmas,
11    total 95 drachmas, apart from the oil-allowance. I ask therefore
12    of you that both the mattocks, since I alone am being prosecuted, be restored, and that I myself be not harassed by the collector, that you give order to Hermaphilos
13    after examining the wages owed in addition
14    and the clothing-allowance, that they be paid, so that nothing be discrepant with respect to the treasury accounts.
15    And the amount owed in addition is as Apollonios
16    the scribe rendered it.
17    From Mecheir to Pachons of the 2nd year, wages 20 drachmas of copper
18    and oil-allowance of 4 months, 8 kotylai at 2 obols, 2 (drachmas) 4 obols
19    and of the 3rd year, from Hathyr to Tybi, of 3 months, wages 15 drachmas
20    and oil-allowance, 6 kotylai at 2 obols, 2 drachmas
21    and clothing-allowance of the 2nd year, 20 drachmas of copper
22    and of the 3rd year likewise, 20 drachmas of copper.
23    Total: 79 drachmas 4 obols. The capital total comes to 40 at 2, 80 drachmas.
24    … to Apollonios.
