[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy. Heliodoros, strategos of the Arsinoite, of the divisions of Themistes and Polemon, to Dionysios
2     royal scribe, also deputizing in the affairs of the strategia,
3     his dearest friend, greeting.
4     If you were present when the most illustrious prefect threatened
5     the strategoi concerning the transport of grain,
6     so that the most numerous cattle working in the Arsinoite from the other nomes should be sent, they proceeded against those who insolently, after the
7     prefect's command, had withdrawn — the cattle-keepers.
8     And since the most excellent epistrategos had written to you that no one was to withdraw from those here before they received from me
9     a letter ab[ou]t the grain-transport having been completed,
10    and had added that there be sent up to him
11    under guard those daring to commit any such insolence — the need being altogether of all-surpassing urgency, so that also through letters often
12    in general and in person the prefect had threatened the strategoi — since therefore, although there are only
13    here, from the nome under you, donkeys numbering 411,
14    the greater part have withdrawn, so that there remain here
15    left to this day 156, with whom the men of standing appointed over them, not daring to withdraw, have stayed — necessarily having made clear to you the things commanded by the most illustrious prefect Flavius
16    Titianus, I write, so that in safety,
17    as also the most excellent epistrategos wrote to you, you may send up
18    to him those who withdrew, and the equal
19    number of the donkeys, with trustworthy cattle-keepers able to remain, you may dispatch, while the river is still
20    able to be navigated for the transport to take place,
21    the water already receding, and the need pressing that the conveyance of the grain take place most speedily.
22    Those reported by the men of standing to have departed, on account of whom
23    I have sent to you some of them: Did(ymos?) Apol(lonios?) also called Sekoundos and Phil(?) also called
24    Phil(?) Dion(?) and Ptol(emaios?) Apol(lonios?) also called Pete(?). I pray for your health, dearest friend. Year 5,
25    Pharmouthi 24.
26    These are those who have departed:
27    Sinkepha, 25;
28    Pakerke, 22;
29    Phoboou, 8;
30    Tychin Nekotin, 5;
31    Nomou epoikion, 3;
32    Toka Peeno, 5;
33    Teeos, 18;
34    Psobtheos kato, 5;
35    Takona, 9;
36    Chyseos, donkeys 6;
37    Serypheos, 23;
38    Opheos, 6;
39    of the hamlet of Sarapion son of Chairemon, 5;
40    Senepta, 45;
41    Nemeron, 18;
42    Paloseos, 23;
43    Sinary, 12;
44    Psobtheos, 9;
45    Total: donkeys 255.
