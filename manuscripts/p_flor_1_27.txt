[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelia Theodosia, [daughter of] Apion,
2     to the heirs of Timothy, greetings.
3     I have been paid by you the tax
4     of the seven arouras, from which
5     your father
6     cultivated, [both] the grain-tax and barley-tax
7     in full, and I have no
8     charge against you
9     for the 12th indiction, Thot 15. The receipt
10    [is] valid. And [when] questioned
11    I confessed. Aurelia Theodosia,
12    [daughter of] Apion, I issued the receipt
13    and it is my own handwriting.
