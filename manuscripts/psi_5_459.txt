[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonios son of Apollonios, tax-farmer of the scales of the metropolis and nomes and other goods,
2     from Theon son of Theon son of Horus
3     from the village of Karanidos. Having been approved to me from the present
4     month of Choiach until the month of Kaisareia
5     [epagomenal] fifth of the present [fifth] year of the Emperor
6     Caesar Vespasianus Augustus,
7     to sell wool densely and
8     by weight [in the] aforesaid village I undertake to render tax
9     altogether of silver drachmas
10    sixty, which [and] I will repay [whenever] if he wishes, if it should appear to be approved upon the aforementioned.
11    Apollonios son of Apollonios I have approved as aforesaid. Year five
12    of the Emperor Caesar [Vespa]sianus
13    Augustus, Choiach …
