[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a loan with mortgage of a seventh
2     [part] of the house and court in [Soknopaios] Island of the Herakleid division.
3     Panephrumis of Erieus, Persian of the epigone, to have the loan from Stotoetios
4     of Tesenouphis the elder, by hand from the house, of marked [silver], [capital] coinage, sixty-four drachmas four-obols, at interest of one drachma
5     per mina each month, which also I will repay in the month Tybi of the coming
6     tenth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus
7     as is set forth. Thaes of Nestnephis, Persian woman, with [her] guardian, the aforementioned, her her husband Panephrumis
8     I agree upon all the [aforementioned] as is set forth; wrote on behalf of
9     them Panephrumis Papaios on account of
10    not knowing their letters.
11    Copy of a seal
12    Of year 9 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus
13    Mecheir 9. It has been registered through [the] in the
14    [Soknopaios] Island office.
