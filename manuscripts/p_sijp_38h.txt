[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis, [the] produce of year 5
2     of Antoninus Caesar
3     of the lord, Mesore 5, by name
4     Petemeno[phius] Picos and [his] brother
5     for Charakos, year 19, of wheat, [the] twelfth [part], 48, and
6     by name Paminius Picos, similarly
7     year 19, of wheat, [the] twelfth [part], 48, and
8     by name of the same Paminius, for Agoron
9     of wheat 24, becomes wheat 4, Phthominnis [I] have marked.
