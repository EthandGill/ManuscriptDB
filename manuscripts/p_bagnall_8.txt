[TRANSLATION]
1     A copy of a Roman letter and decision
2     translated as well as possible. Pomponius Faustian[us]
3     To Prob[us] his own, greetings. [I] brought [an action] against Julius Serenus
4     soldier of the same cohort and Crepereius Don[atus]
5     soldier of the same cohort because [they] abandoned their fellow-soldiers, [them] few [in number]
6     barbarians, powerless and unarmed, to be subjected; therefore, together with my judgment I ordered that [it] be copied in the precincts
7     of the mines to be posted so that it may be a reminder to the remaining fellow-soldiers.
8     I thought [it good] for you to do this.
