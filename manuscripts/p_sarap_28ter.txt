[TRANSLATION]
1     To Tryph[aina …] daughter (?) of [Plou]tarchos,
2     from Heli[odor]os son of Hermaios and Mireous son of Panechotes, both farmers [of those] from the village of Magdola Mire. We wish of our own free will
3     to lease from you for two years from the present 17th year of Hadrian
4     Caesar the lord, the plots belonging to you in the district Around-the-City below, near Psobthonpilalis, from the fixed-rent allotments of Dionysios and …okios,
5     thirteen catoecic arouras, so that each year we sow the half with wheat,
6     at a rent for each aroura of five and a half artabas of wheat, and the other
7     half sow with barley and lentil, at a rent for each aroura, of lentil and barley
8     according to the half, of five and a half artabas. And the rents gathered together year by year
9     we shall measure out to you by mutual guarantee according to the half in the months [of P]auni and
10    Epeiph, new, pure, unadulterated, unspoiled, and the wheat free of barley, all sifted, by the measure of Athenaios of one-sixth, your own man measuring at the threshing-floors of the village of Magdola Mire; and we shall give you each year for the rent-charge four drachmas, and to the servants likewise each year one-sixth (?) of wheat, the
11    yearly public dues all being upon you the landholders.
12    And of our own accord we shall hand over the half of the land after fallow(-ground) for lentil. Year 17
13    of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Phaophi 22. I, Tryphaina daughter of Nearchos, have leased out as stated above. Hermaios also called Phibion,
14    son of O…ios, have been registered as guardian of my mother, and I wrote on her behalf as she does not know letters.
15    I, Ptolemaios son of Ploutarchos, have been registered as guardian of my wife,
16    and I wrote on her behalf as she does not know letters.
