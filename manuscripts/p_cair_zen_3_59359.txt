[TRANSLATION]
1     Antipater to Zeno
2     greetings. the small letter
3     concerning the garden well
4     you will do having taken from
5     Sostratus, so that we might be able having exchanged [with] bronze to suffice for the
6     [gathering] of the
7     poppy. For then
8     with those around Menelaus no longer we exchanged on account of having suddenly emigrated.
9     I know moreover also the things for which we sailed down
10    being reasonable; not
11    however at least he accomplished
12    to bring to end
13    on account of the current preoccupations [of] Zenodorus; but we are convinced will be in Memphis. Farewell. year 5,
