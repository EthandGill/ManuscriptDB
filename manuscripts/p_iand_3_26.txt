1     [To so-and-so] …
2     [of the Themist]es division,
3     from [He]ras son [of Petesouchos,]
4     a Pe[rsia]n of the epigone. I wish to lease, of the [Kamelian estate,]
5     … and being …
6     [of the] aforesaid …
7     … of arouras …
8     …
9     of the canal called, for [the] grazing and pasturing of sheep of the produce of the present
10    second year of the Emperor
11    Caesar Nerva Tra[i]an [Augustus]
12    Germanicus, at a total rent of one hundred and twenty silver drachmas, free from risk and
13    not subject to deduction; and the rent
14    I shall pay in four installments, monthly, the due portion in equal parts,
15    until the month Choiak of the same
16    second year of Trajan Caesar
17    the lord, on condition that they (the sheep) shall graze, and I shall go out until the new moon of the month Tybi
18    of the same second [year,]
19    if it seems good to lease to me
20    on the aforesaid terms, not to be re-let to others and not subject to additional cultivation … .
21    I, Heras son of Petesouchos, have submitted
22    the lease-offer at the aforesaid rent
23    of 120 silver drachmas for the pasturing of sheep, on condition that they shall graze and I shall go out …
24    … [until the new] moon
25    [of] the month Tybi of the 2nd year.
26    I wrote on his behalf …
27    as he does not know letters. In the second year of the Emperor
28    Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus
29    Germanicus, in the month Sebastos, the 14th.
