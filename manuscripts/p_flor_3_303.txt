1     From
2     To the most magnificent and most renowned Mela, contractor, from Apa Nilus, [greetings].
3     [I beseech] your excellence [in order that] you might purchase the five thousand light-measures [having placed]
4     earnest money or to Machelos or to Kroust. Do not therefore neglect concerning this, [master].
5     And send to me six gold pieces, since they are needed here. And I sent the
6     my worker, the one also bearing the letters. And I coming with god through
7     the light-measures, a sample, I-give of-them. Therefore may-your-magnificence deign to-send to-me
8     one artaba of beans. Much much much I-greet your
9     excellence and all the ones dwelling in the bountiful your house, from small
10    until great, the by-name. And I-greet the sweetest my sons Phoeammona
11    and Phib and Minan.
