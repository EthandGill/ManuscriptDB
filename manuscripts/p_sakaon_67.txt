[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Sakaon son of Satabous, of those from the village of Theadelphia,
2     from Aurelia Kyrillous, of those from the Hermouthiake quarter. I wish
3     to lease from you the grain-bearing arurae which belong to you around the same village
4     of Theadelphia, twenty arurae or however many there may be, for sowing only,
5     for the present 16th year and 14th year and 6th year, of the tenth indiction, on a partnership basis, for a half share to me the lessee of the crops
6     to be produced in the year, without rent and without payment in kind, the advance
7     of the seed being furnished by you the owner
8     … from the common threshing-floor first of all, and further also
9     I shall take over sufficient yokes of oxen and of donkeys, four-footed beasts, from the watering until the harvest and the harvest itself, and it shall not be permitted
10    to me within the yearly period to abandon the lease in
11    any manner, and under compulsion I shall carry out the year's works on the arurae,
12    all such as are fitting, at my own expense at the proper seasons, doing
13    not a single (bit of) damage, all the public dues, and annonae and impositions of every kind, being (chargeable) to you the owner; and after the yearly period I shall hand back the arurae just as I also received (them).
14    In the consulship of our lords Licinius Augustus for the 6th time and
15    Licinius the most illustrious Caesar for the 2nd time, … .
