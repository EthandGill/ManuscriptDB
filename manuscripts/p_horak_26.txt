[TRANSLATION]
1     Tharsyno to Agathoklees
2     greetings. I have sent you
3     Glaukon and also the
4     letter carrying to you, so that he might bring us the fennel.
5     You therefore will do well
6     having taken care so that he might receive [it] useful and according to the
7     measure [he] not be cheated.
8     Farewell. In the 25th year, Epiphi 26
9     To Agathoklees.
