[TRANSLATION]
1     Amyntas to Zenōn greetings. Know that the cook, whom you bought, …
2     having taken eighty copper drachmae, so that [for fodder] to the horses, he has run away, but met some around Athlibis, which also is [with] the Cappadocians [those there having] a station. You would do well then announcing to all the [slaves/men] and writing to
3     whomever you think it useful to be, in order that, as those from us may seize him, they may help towards [having him] stationed to [you].
4     Be well. Year 28 …
5     To Zenōn.
6     Year 28, Dystrus 8, in Mendēs.
7     Amyntas about the cook
8     of the runaway.
