[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the most eminent duke: the recruits from Egypt and the Thebaid,
2     … he (Licinius?) has deigned to order, for those of Iovius Licinius the most august Emperor,
3     … has come to the knowledge of their heavenly foresight, that for each of the recruits
4     … he has deigned to order that … of money and a military garment be given … having, in my devotion, revered the copy of the divine law, the … in the edict …
5     … that on this account they should not be troubled concerning either the money or the clothing for all the time
6     … indeed to have obtained a share of the military
7     rank; it seemed good therefore, so as to be pleasing to the emperor …
8     … to make (this known) to the soldiers and to the rest of the provincials … therefore …
9     … of our lords the invincible emperors I hastened to make it plain to them, both to those in military service … of the province, that they might know; and I say from this point … that which comes from the proclamation … that from now on the recruits should be entirely exempt from the payment of money or clothing
10    … freedom (from this) for all those enlisting after the publication of the edict, of these things
11    … in my devotion I grant … the perils … of whoever shall make the attempt …
12    … striving after (my) benevolence, so that by their zeal for the treasury they may …
13    … to be. (Original in) Latin.
