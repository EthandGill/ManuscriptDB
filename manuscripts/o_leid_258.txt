1     Copy of receipt which I previously issued. Tybi 19 of the 2nd year, name Psensenmouthos Paesios, for distributions
2     of wheat, [of the] artaba [a] third, eighth; [it] makes wheat 3, 8.

4     of wheat, [of the] artaba 12; [it] makes wheat 12. I, Aurelius Amonios, have signed off.
