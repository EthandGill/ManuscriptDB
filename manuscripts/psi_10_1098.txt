3     [The reign of Cleopatra goddess, father-loving, in her first year, during the priesthood of Alexander and the]
4     [other officials registered] in Alexandria, in the month of Hyperberetaius, the 29th,
5     in Mesore, the 29th, in Tebtūn of the district of Polemon of the Arsinoit
6     nome. Ariston son of Pyrrhos of those through Chomania settler-cavalry
7     [to] Sokrates son of Pto[lem]y and Phanesis son of Palus, the two Persians of the successor-class, from his holding of land, arouras 3, around the aforem[entioned] [village Tebtūn],
8     whose neighbors: south, a canal, north, Ptolemy's holding, [west] a canal, [east]
9     a watchtower … ium holding. This lease [is] for one year [for] the second year,
10    as total rent of wheat, measures 27, without [seed], safe from all danger, and not liable for any damage except drought or excessive rain,
11    which, if [it] occurs on this land until Hathur the 30th, and if it should occur
12    as liable [he shall be] from the aforesaid rents according to the proportion of [the] drought
13    for each. And Ariston has received from the aforementioned [amount] from the rents
14    of wheat [measures] 15. And let Ariston guarantee [the lease] to the lessees
15    this lease for the written period, free from both royal
16    and private debts of every kind. If he does not guarantee as has been written,
17    let Ariston pay to the lessees what he has from them with [one and a] half and as penalty twenty talents of copper and damages. [The lease] having been guaranteed,
18    [and] nothing having been written contrary to the lease, let they
19    the lessees pay to Ariston the remaining [part] of the rents in the month of Pauni
20    of the se[cond] year, having brought [it] to their own expense to the
21    aforem[entioned] [vi]llage Tebtūn where if Ariston directs, wheat

24    price of this in the [market] of the same village. If the lesse[es] should do anything on account of this land or on account of Ariston himself for the royal [or] private or some other contribution, let them present a [receipt], and let it be deducted from the aforesaid rents.
