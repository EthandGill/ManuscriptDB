[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Sar]apion also called … son of …et… and … who have served as exegetes, keepers of the property-records of the [Arsinoite],
2     from Aphrodisia, a minor, registered in the quarter of the Storehouses,
3     … with as guardian and trustee, according to the laws, her father Sarapion, son of Aphr[odisios],
4     [son of] Posidonios. The mortgage and intermediary-security which I hold through you in the
5     [metro]polis, in the quarter of Horion Hierakiou, of a new house in which …
6     [from the] part of it towards the west, a small house; and in the same quarter, of a house and courtyard
7     [and] of another small house in the part of it towards the north; and an intermediary-security about the village of Alabanthis, of a catoecic allotment of three arouras, against a silver capital of one talent,
8     [of] the debtor Alexandra, daughter of Ischyrion, son of Ischyrion, An…s, a freedwoman,
9     registered through the former keepers of the property-records — (this) I wish to release to Philoumene, daughter of Apollonios,
10    son
11    [of Pt]ollarion, from the quarter of Phaneseion, the purchaser of other (securities) and of the aforesaid
12    pledges, on condition that the capital and interest according to the mortgage be repaid
13    [thr]ough the heir by succession of the debtor, her own son Ammonios
14    [also] called Serenos, she receiving from her the aforesaid
15    [tal]ent of the capital, it being permitted on the present day (to convert it) into another mortgage
16    [and inter]mediary-security in the quarter of the Bithynians of the Other Places, of a new house and open court
17    [and] courtyard, formerly a two-towered house, and of an open court and courtyard and a vacant
18    [pl]ot, and about the village of Tebetny, of a half share of a catoecic allotment
19    [of] sixteen and two-thirds arouras. I, Ptolemaios also called Chre…, secretary of k…l…ou
20    … secretary, have registered (it). [Year] … of Aurelius Commodus Caesar the lord,
21    Tybi 10.
22    Registered through me, A[p]oll…, (year) 14.
