[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 762, [Absalmias] Abidrodakos sold the slave-woman belonging to him, named Immadabu, purchased with silver; [Sams]a[i]os purchased …
2     [Te]iatos of Abourenoi, for 700 denarii.
3     Under the consulship of our lords the Emperors Caesars Traianus Decius Augustus for the third time and Decius and Quintus his sons …
4     of the same pious unconquered Augustuses' rule, but according to …
5     the previous reckoning, year 762, month of Audnaios 27, in the village of Behpouraia, in the presence of the aforementioned witnesses. [There] was sold …
6     [by] Absalmias Abidrodakos of [the] village Beathagaes of the Abourene territory of Theganabos, the slave-woman belonging to him …
7     purchased with silver, named Immadabu, or also if she is called or shall be called by any other name, by descent of Nesibe territory, being as [if] of thirteen years …
8     somewhat more, but less, light-complexioned, round-faced, well-browed, fine-eyed …
9     with well-proportioned nostrils, and having been purchased by him according to the description which was exhibited …
10    hav[ing been] made in Septimia colony, the metropolis of Nesibe, on the …
11    fifth before the Kalends of May, by Septimius Satornillus Proclus …
12    of Nesibe, which former document as well the seller gave …
13    to the purchaser for security […] from guardianship having been given to the same Satornillus […]; purchased the same slave-woman [was] Samsaios Teiatos of the village Banathsamson of the Abourene [region]
14    for a price of silver [of] 700 denarii, having received which price the seller from the purchaser handed over to him the same slave-woman to have her and to acquire, to use, to sell, to manage in any manner he should prefer, and the seller promised that if anyone should make a legal claim …
15    or make a charge concerning the slave-woman sold or any part of her, he himself …
16    standing [by his obligation] will defend in law and will clear title and will hand over to the purchaser; but if he should not clear title, he will pay back the price he received [and] the resulting damage …this sale to be and thus valid …
17    for all time; in good faith questioned Samsaios the purchaser, in good faith consenting agreed Absalmias Abidrodakos the seller.
18    … I wrote [on behalf of him, not know]ing …
19    [the letters.]
