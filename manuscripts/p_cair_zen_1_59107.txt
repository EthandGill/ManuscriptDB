[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios to Panakestor, greetings. The [ship] still sooner
2     we would have sent to you, but we see a [great expense] will be
3     to the sailors. If indeed you are able to some of those in Kerke to give,
4     who work and will [feed] the [sailors], whenever then to you
5     need you have of the ship [they] will suffice, write to us; for we will send to you.
6     If not, I do not see how we are able the sitting sailors the greatest time the wages
7     throughout to give.
8     Be well. Year 29, Hyperberetaios 23, Thoth 24.
9     Year 29, Phaophi 2.
10    Apollonios
11    plantations.
12    To Panakestor.
