[TRANSLATION]
1     … of -mon-
2     [Sheet] 57.
3     … Chairemon, scribe of the grain-collectors, and
4     Sotas the guard. As you have requested, Chairemon
5     the scribe of the grain-collectors and Sotas the guard, whom
6     you write have absconded, I have ordered them to be publicly proscribed, so that within twenty days, having appeared on the spot, of (their) accounts
7     they may render (account). But if they delay, having seized their property
8     together with the revenues,
9     declare it to me. I wish you good health.
10    Year 20, Pharmouthi 6.
11    Through the first (memorandum):
12    Sotas son of Ptolemaios, letter-carrier, having taken over Chairemon, scribe of the grain-collectors
13    of the village of Theadelphia, so as to exact
14    from him whatever he had plundered from the public
15    granary out of an empty allocation, became invisible (absconded)
16    together with Chairemon; whence of necessity, lord, I report to you, so that, if it seem good to your fortune, you may order
17    them to be proscribed and to meet with the due correction, so that the rest too may not abscond.
18    Year 20, Pharmouthi 18.
19    Sheet 58. Through another (memorandum):
20    Chairemon, scribe of the grain-collectors of the village
21    of Theadelphia: being required (to render) certain capital sums sought regarding his administration, having been handed over to Sotas
22    son of Ptolemaios the guard and having absconded with him,
23    was proscribed on the spot together with Sotas. But since
24    up to now not even one of them has appeared, I beg, lord, to order them to be proscribed also before you,
25    so that others, in imitation of them,
26    may not do the same and through this the collection of public (revenues)
27    be hindered.
28    Year 20, Pharmouthi 22.
29    For as long as I was in the strategia, they were not seen.
30    So that what follows upon the writings might be done, I handed (it) over to Herakleides, the strategos appointed after me.
31    From the record-office of Sounei, year 10, Tybi. Chairemon to Phasis son of Chairemon,
32    his mother being Thaisas, aged 26 years. From the memorandum of the father.
33    Marcus Petronius Mamertinus, prefect of Egypt, declares:
34    the below-written grain-collectors—by edict—the scribe of the grain-collectors and the guard, as Vegetus also called
35    Sarapion, the strategos of the Themistos division, says have absconded, I order, within twenty days, having appeared on the spot, to render account, or to know that,
36    being sought out, they will meet with the due correction.
37    They are: Chairemon, scribe of the grain-collectors of the village of Theadelphia,
38    handed over to Sotas son of Ptolemaios the guard on account of capital sums
39    pertaining to the administration; Sotas the guard, the one who took over
40    Chairemon. Year 20, Pharmouthi 6.
