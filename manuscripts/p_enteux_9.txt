[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting, Apollonios son of Leptines, of those dwelling in Krokodilon polis
2     of the Arsinoite nome. I am wronged by Thaisithis, my father's sister. For although I own
3     an ancestral house in Pharbaitha of the Herakleides meris, and Thaisithis dwells in the aforesaid house,
4     and my father having died in the 23rd year, and I having need of the house, in order that by selling it
5     I might bury my father, and when I instructed Thaisithis to withdraw from the house, she did not heed,
6     despising me because I am an orphan. Therefore I beg you, king, to instruct Diophanes the strategos
7     to write to Mnaseas the epistates in Pharbaitha to send Thaisithis to him and, if what I write is true,
8     … to compel her to withdraw from the house, since, having received a dowry from my father, she does not submit … nor …
9     … and to have her pay me the rent from the 23rd year, from the time my father died,
10    … each (year) 5 drachmas, which comes to … For if this happens, through you, king,
11    the common savior of all, [I shall obtain justice.] Farewell.
12    To Mnaseas. By all means reconcile them; but if not, send (them) …
13    Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 29.
14    Apollonios son of Leptines against
15    Thaisithis concerning a house and rent.
