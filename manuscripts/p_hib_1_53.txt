1     [Demopho]n to Ptolemaios, gree[t]ing. I have sen[t you] the report of the first ten-day period
2     of Hathyr; try therefore to take security safely, since the reckoning
3     will be against you. Farewell. Year 39, Hathyr 16.
4     Tho[lthis:] Theodoros son of Kallikrates, from the relinquished aroura, 5 1/8, 8 drachmas, 3 1/4 obols;
5     Petesis the shepherd and Paues the oil-seller, 3 1/2 1/8 arouras, 6 drachmas, a quarter of an obol;
6     Harmiysis son of Panesis and Pasis son of Teos, 3 arouras, 5 drachmas;
7     Paues son of Phibis, 1/4, three obols; Pasiaimous, 1/4 1/8, two obols;
8     Horos son of Oimas, 1/4 aroura, 4 drachmas; total 16 1/4 1/8 arouras, 24 drachmas, 2 1/2 obols.
