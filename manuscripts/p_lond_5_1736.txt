[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign [of] our most divine and most serene and God-crowned
2     master Flavius Heraclius the eternal Augustus
3     Emperor, in the first year, Phamenoth 1, of the 14th indiction.
4     Flavius Patermouthis son of Menas, soldier of the regiment of Elephantine, sailor
5     from Syene, [and] his wife Aurelia Kako
6     daughter of Iakob, to Aurelius Ioannes
7     son of Pityron, sailor from the same Syene, greeting.
8     We acknowledge, under joint liability, through this our written security, that we have had and
9     borrowed from you today, for our pressing
10    need, four solidi of gold by the standard of Syene,
11    total 4 solidi of gold by the standard of Syene, which we are ready
12    to furnish to you whenever you wish, that is,
13    each year, one tremis of gold,
14    one, by the standard of Syene, from the aforewritten day, at our own
15    risk and from the resource of our substance, individually and jointly, by the law of pledge,
16    without any dispute; and for your security we have therefore made this joint security, valid and secure, producible everywhere,
17    with the signature of him who on our behalf signs, and on being formally questioned we agreed and released (it). I, Flavius Pathermouthis son of Menas,
18    soldier of the regiment in Elephantine, and his wife
19    Aurelia Kakou daughter of Iakob, the aforesaid, the security is satisfactory to us,
20    of the four solidi by the standard of Syene,
21    as is set forth above. I, Flavius Dios son of Basileides, one of
22    the actuarii of the regiment of Syene, having been requested, wrote
23    on their behalf, since they do not know letters.
24    Stra…psos, deacon, bear witness.
25    Flavius Ioannes Kaeitos, soldier of the regiment
26    of Syene, bear witness. Flavius Biktor
27    son of Ioannes, soldier of the regiment of Syene,
28    bear witness.
29    Through …
30    Security of Patermouthis son of Menas and Kakou his wife … of 4 solidi by the standard of Syene.
