[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ]
2     [of God and our Savior and of the Lady] ours, of the holy Mother of God
3     and all [the saints], Tybi the 24th, of the 14th indiction, in the city of Arsinoe.
4     To Flavius Athanasius [the] most learned justice-administrator
5     of [this city of Arsinoe] Theodore
6     [caretaker] priest, son of the blessed
7     Simeon, [from] the same city, from [the quarter]
8     of Therapeia, greetings. I acknowledge with willing [mind]
9     [to have hired] myself to your wisdom
10    [performing] for her all [the things] [commanded]
11    to me by her as work both in the city and
12    in fields properly and without reproach
13    and without contempt and without murmuring
14    for one year reckoned from
15    the new moon of the present month of Tybi of
16    the present fourteenth indiction
17    and to receive from her as my wages
18    for the same one year [of] grain artabas
19    nine and one [gold] coin
20    containing twenty-three keratia
21    But from the same wages, I received from her
22    from now already the stated one coin,
23    and to receive both the customary perquisites
24    of both harvest-time and feast-times and, if before
25    the [completion] of the year
26    I seek to withdraw from the service,
27    so that I return to you whatever I held
28    from her on account of [wages] and unpaid
29    I depart. The [hire-agreement] is valid and when questioned I acknowledged.
30    Theodore [caretaker] priest son of the blessed Simeon
31    the aforesaid [agrees] with me the hire-agreement as stated.
32    through me Justin the notary.
33    [Hire-agreement] [made] by Theodore caretaker priest son of Simeon to
34    Flavius Athanasius the most learned justice-administrator.
