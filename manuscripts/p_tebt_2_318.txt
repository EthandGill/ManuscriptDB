[TRANSLATION]
1     … ed … [registered in the quarter]
2     [of the Ma]cedonians, with my guardian, my maternal uncle … o …
3     son of Souchas. Since I lent, according to two public agreements
4     made through the record-office of Teptynis in the second year of Antoninus and Verus
5     [the] lords Augusti, by the one, in the month Mecheir on the fourth, to Serenus [and]
6     Didymos, both sons of Heron son of Souchas, whose mother is Sarapous, freedwoman of Thenzoila, of the quarter of Syriake, of a principal of silver five hundred
7     and sixty drachmas, by which agreement they declared that they would preserve
8     for me unalienated and unencumbered, until the repayment of the principal, the two-thirds share belonging to them in common of a katoikic
9     allotment of three and a half arouras in two parcels around the village of Teptynis, and by the other, in the month Pachon on the new-moon, to the aforesaid
10    Serenus alone, another (sum) of silver principal, drachmas
11    … hundred, by which he too declared that he would preserve for me unalienated and unencumbered the two-thirds share belonging to him out of the
12    [northern] part of six and a quarter arouras, being a third part out of [the]
13    [northern] part of eighteen and a half arouras around the same
14    village of Teptynis in the so-called estate of Stasikrates; and fearing lest I should be unaware, according to the right belonging to me,
15    of dispositions concerning the aforesaid arouras, I submit (this) so that the
16    [registration may be] made … of the class of encumbrances of those in
17    [the village of Tebtynis]; and if it should appear that they (the arouras) belong to another or have been previously encumbered through the record-office, there shall be no impediment from this registration, in accordance with the copies I have deposited
18    [of the] dispositions. … I, the scribe, have registered it.
19    Year six of Antoninus and
20    [Verus the lords Au]gusti, Mecheir 1.
