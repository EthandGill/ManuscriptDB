[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Mandrobes. Hierax, your agent, having met up with me, gave me what you had written on behalf of the potamophylakes and the others. To the potamophylakes of the Ptolemaikos (river?), therefore, I have written to send the rear-guard up to Hiera (Nesos) and to hand it over to those from the Troite nome, and to the others (I have written) so that they may be with you at Chiê of the Kynopolite nome. Therefore, you will do well to please ensure that the aforementioned potamophylakes are of good courage with respect to their labors, and whenever (?) they may be at the [ --- ] places, [12] they may ceaselessly do (?)…. ... (J. Bauschatz, Law and Enforcement, p. 290)
