[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: ‘Maron to Zenon, greeting. Please assign me, if you think fit, 2½ more drachmae for my salary … to provide for the cost of grinding and for buying water…. But do just as you think right yourself. Many persons are occupied … those engaged in guarding a salary of 15 drachmae and …artabas of wheat. (Addressed) To Zenon. (Docketed) Maron about his salary. Year 29, Daesius 14, in Alexandria.’
