[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius
2     Basileios the most glorious,
3     in the twenty-fourth year,
4     on the 24th of Epeiph, of the fourteenth (indiction).
5     To the heirs of Apollos
6     son of Dioskoros (grandson of) Psimanobet,
7     through you the most admirable
8     Dioskoros his son,
9     from Aurelius Psempnouthios
10    also called Lilos, son of Hermauos
11    (grandson of) Psekes, his mother being
12    Tsone, machine-operator,
13    from the village of Aphrodite,
14    greeting. I have leased from you,
15    for whatever time you wish,
16    reckoned
17    from the produce of the present
18    fourteenth indiction,
19    both of it and of the time following,
20    the plots of land belonging to you
21    of the hollow of the channel
22    which is south of your ancestral
23    farmstead, situated
24    south of the village, north
25    of the orchard called Pkamet,
26    from the corner of the farmstead
27    of Ptolemaios, southward throughout
28    up to the wall of the same
29    garden, eastward throughout
30    up to the corner of the farmstead
31    of Apa Kyros (son of) Besarion, your
32    forefather; on condition that I lay these (lands) down
33    in whatever crops I wish,
34    with my own hands and
35    expenses, and give to you
36    the rent at a fixed rate
37    in full and even in drought, which may it not be,
38    yearly one artaba of clean wheat,
39    by your rent-measure,
40    without dispute. The lease
41    shall be valid and secure; and concerning everything
42    being formally questioned I agreed, with
43    the subscription of the one who on my behalf
44    subscribes. Aurelius Psempnouthios
45    also called Lilos, son of Hermauos (grandson of) Psikes, the aforewritten,
46    have leased as is set forth above. Aurelius Hermeias son of Biktor
47    wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters, having been requested.
48    Aurelius Ioannes son of Kyriakos, I bear witness to the
49    lease, having heard from the one making the agreement.
50    Through me, Kyros the notary, it was written.
51    Lease made by Psempnouthios [also called Lilo]s
