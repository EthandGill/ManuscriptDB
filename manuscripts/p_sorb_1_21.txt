[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemy son of Ptolemy Soter reigning, year 34
2     Mesore 8, in the city of Crocodiles
3     of the Arsinoite. Demeas agrees,
4     the one of Philiskos, to hold from Archibios and Orseos [and] Phamenis
5     those conducting business with wool,
6     so that to Apollodoros obligation
7     of [Syrian] copper drachmas 580.
8     Ptolemy reigning,
9     son of Ptolemy Soter,
10    year 34, Mesore 8, in the city of Crocodiles of the Arsinoite.
11    [agrees] Demeas, the one of Philiskos, to hold [from] Archibios and
12    Orseos and Phamenis, of whom
13    the wools being conducted in business
14    [so that] to [Apollodoros] obligation of [Syrian] copper drachmas five hundred eighty.
