[TRANSLATION]
1     Demetria, also called Taseus, [daughter] of Apollonides, with my lord my son Areios Nearchus, also called Megchaeus
2     to Anubion, [son] of Sarapion, farmer
3     greetings. I have measured from you all the produce which you cultivated for me
4     of the arouras, produce of the past ninth year of Hadrian
5     Caesar, the lord, and I charge you with nothing
6     [for] anything whatsoever, the
7     public [dues] being [owed] to me, the
8     resident. Year ten of the Emperor
9     Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus
10    Thoth 13. Areios, [son] of Nearchus, [have] signed [as] guardian of my mother, and I wrote on behalf of her [not] knowing letters.
