[TRANSLATION]
1     To the lord brother Haruotes
2     Olbius greetings. Give to the son
3     Psaitis of Maximinus small baskets
4     selected, twenty, since we want them for his vineyard.
5     And after this leave with you other
6     baskets thirty-five for the
7     stone and the other things provide to the
8     [ones] from Tapaios and Psenmau
