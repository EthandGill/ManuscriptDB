[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of treasury of metropolis produce seventeenth year of Antoninus
2     Caesar the lord Pauni twenty-ninth for the South in-name-of heirs
3     of-Imouthos similarly through farmers Fahirios Pikotis and associates
4     who through farmer Pamonthos elder of-Inarotos wheat five become wheat artabae five. Menodoros [I] have marked.
