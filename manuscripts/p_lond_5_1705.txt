# HGV-EN: [...] Aurelii Besarion, son of Dioskoros, son of Psimanobet, and Victor, son of Sansneous and mother Maria, farmer, from the village of Aphrodite in the Antaiopolite nome, greetings. We agree with shared will and without deceptive intention to work with each other for the set worth of harvest of two years’ period of our god willing oncoming twelfth indiction and current, at the field of the holy new church, leased by me, Besarion, so that we can cultivate it with our own animals, and for Besarion to gain third part [...] [...] of Besarion and Victor [...] (translation by Law in Social Networks project)
