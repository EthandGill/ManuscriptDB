1     After the consulate of Flavius Basilius the most illustrious, Pauni 7 of the 8th indiction.
2     Aurelii Cheidis of Phoibammon, the following subscriptor, and his mother Sophia of Taurinus
3     of the mother Cyrillus, both from Hermoupolis, to Aurelia
4     Nemesilla of Phoibammon, the most noble, from the same city, greetings; being valid
5     and valid our prior mortgage, the one being with you, the one having its own strength
6     and power, having on which [contains] all the provisions until repayment
7     of the debt in it and cancellation of this, and now we agree, being joint obligors
8     [and] [mutual sureties] [to have] [and] to have borrowed from you for our own and necessary
9     need, through hand from [your] house, principal gold coins imperial
10    [assayed] four short twenty-four karats by public weight of the same
11    [city], which amounts to gold coins 4 short 24 karats, which to you we shall repay from our joint obligation whenever
12    [should you wish], indisputably and unambiguously and without excuse, and not the us
13    … [recognize] to you above the advantage of the same
14    of Cheidis of Phoibammon and of the [mother Sophia] …
