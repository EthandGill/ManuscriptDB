[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 16 Epeiph 21 [has] fallen into a box
2     the contract in Auhero of the Arsinoit nome
3     through Philemon of Aratos' [share], half [a] house and courtyard and [a] third and 15 of fish-salting: [from] great Achomnus Pasitus [to] Achomnus the small [his] brother
