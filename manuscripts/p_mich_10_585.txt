[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus Germanicus,
2     in the month Dystros, on the twenty-sixth, Tybi the twenty-sixth, in Bakchias of the
3     division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledges …,
4     a Persian of the epigone, about fifty years old, with a scar in the middle of his forehead,
5     to Hermas son of Ptolemaios, about fifty-two years old, with a scar in the middle of his forehead,
6     that he has received from him forthwith, hand to hand, from the house, in coined silver
7     sixty drachmas, and that in place of the interest on these
8     the acknowledging party has granted Hermas and those
9     with him and whomever he may wish to dwell, for one year from the aforesaid
10    time, in the paternal portion falling to him, the acknowledging party,
11    the twenty-seventh share, held in common and undivided with Hermas and
12    others, of building-sites and all the appurtenances
13    in the aforementioned village, and that the acknowledging party
14    and those with him shall guarantee to Hermas and those with him the matters concerning this
15    tenancy with every guarantee, against the one bringing claim against it
16    and for whatever time he must dwell there, and let the acknowledging party provide
17    that no one shall hinder Hermas or those with him from dwelling
18    in the designated property, or from lodging others, and collecting the rents,
19    and using all the facilities of these premises
20    without hindrance. And after the time let the acknowledging party repay
21    to Hermas the sixty drachmas of silver, the right of execution
22    belonging to Hermas both against the acknowledging party and against all his property,
23    just as by a legal judgment. The subscriber of the acknowledging party
24    … and of the other, who knows letters.
25    … son of …, a Persian of the epigone, acknowledge that I have received
26    from Hermas son of Ptolemaios forthwith, hand to hand, from the house,
27    in coined silver sixty drachmas, and that in place of the interest on these
28    I the acknowledging party shall grant Hermas and
29    those with him and whomever he may wish to dwell, for one year
30    from the aforesaid time, in the portion falling to me,
31    the paternal twenty-seventh share, held in common and undivided
32    with Hermas and others, of building-sites and of
33    all the appurtenances in the aforementioned village,
34    and I shall guarantee the matters concerning the tenancy with every guarantee
35    both against the one bringing claim against it and for whatever time he must
36    dwell there, and I shall repay the silver
37    sixty drachmas as set out above. He wrote on his behalf.
