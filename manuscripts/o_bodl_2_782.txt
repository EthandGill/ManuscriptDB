[TRANSLATION]
1     Porieus and associates, collectors of the distribution of customs of market 2 in Pekysis
2     of Pamonthes Petechonsis. We received for the distribution, year 22
3     four drachmas, amounting to drachmas 4. Year 23 of Antoninus
4     Caesar, our lord, Phaophi 2. Porieus by means of Oros his son [has signed]. Isidoros, I have acknowledged.
5     Another [receipt], [month] Hadrian 27, in the name of the same, likewise two drachmas, amounting to drachmas 2. Porieus, I have acknowledged.
6     Isidoros, I have acknowledged
7     Another [receipt], [month] Pharmouthi 7, in the name of the same, likewise one drachma, obols 6 1/2,
8     amounting to drachma 1, obols 6 1/2. Porieus, I have acknowledged.
