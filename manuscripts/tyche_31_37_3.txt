1     Konnes to Ariston, greetings. Concerning the matters about which you [have written to me concerning] the wood(s), I went to the Arsinoite [and] asked where [it is] … and with difficulty it was indicated to me that he was in the lower places; … I found him and
2     I negotiated with him concerning the wood(s) … by letter to
3     Kerkesephi and I found … [I] returned to Samareia,
4     concerning the wood(s) … [lest it] not be carried away … and I found the wood(s)
5     in the same [S]amareia, bundles 15 … … I went to Samareia,
6     [the men] from Lochos … [Samar]eia arrived, so that not
7     [I] be able to move anything … [for the men from]
8     Lochos [to] go. Therefore I write to you so that you know. [Farewell. ] … [of the year] 42 Hathyr 20.
