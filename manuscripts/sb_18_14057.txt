[TRANSLATION]
1     Claudius Macareus, guardian of Calpurnius
2     Reginnianus of consular rank, to Reginnianus, business agent of the same, [sends] greetings.
3     [I] have sent [word] to you concerning the [land being far]med by the good Theon, since also
4     [he] wished to receive letters from you.
5     [And now I writ]e to you so that you may preserve him
6     [remaining] under the boundaries which you established for him.
7     [I pray that] you fare well
8     I pray, most honored [one].
9     [Of the year] … Choiach
10    To Reginn[ianus]
11    From Claudius Macareus.
