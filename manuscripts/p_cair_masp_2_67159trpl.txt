[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign and consulship of our most divine lord Flavius
2     Justinus the Eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the fourth year,
3     on the twentieth of Choiak, of the second indiction. In the most splendid city of Antinoopolis.
4     This they place and make with one another, the counterpart
5     common and twofold agreement, on the following declared terms,
6     by all the stipulations it contains: on the one part Aurelius
7     Daniel, son of Iosephios, of mother Thekla, a fine-work
8     carpenter by trade, hailing from this fair city of the Antinoites,
9     and on the other part Aurelius Victor, son of Philemmon, of mother
10    Maria, also belonging to the same trade, from this
11    city of the Antinoites, mutually declaring to one another the things set out below,
12    greeting. We acknowledge by common consent and guileless purpose, that we work together with one another in our carpentry of fine-work
13    craft, through this our written agreement, without any
14    guile and fear and force and deceit and compulsion and all extortion and circumvention, according to the laws, that we
15    the aforewritten men, the carpenters, are ready, from today and
16    the aforesaid day, which is the twentieth of the month Choiak,
17    a day of the present second indiction, until the completion
18    of one single year's time, this same time being reckoned
19    from the aforementioned day, on condition that we, apart from all
20    fraud, blamelessly and unaccusably, agree
21    with one another and toil together and breathe together for all the fitting
22    works of our craft, with all submission and obedience
23    from one another toward one another in all good and beneficial works and
24    words, blamelessly and uncontemptibly, apart from all
25    fraud and grumbling and postponement and delay
26    of works throughout, in all things enjoined upon us or also commanded by whatsoever person by way of handicraft
27    order, so that we do and accomplish all things with sound
28    faith, and restore to the owners without harm, and the
29    wages sufficiently receive and divide between ourselves
30    these in common, on a half share of the division, apart from all theft and
31    defection; none of us being able nor going to be able
32    to withdraw from the work falling to us
33    at whatsoever time or season or day, and to postpone and stretch out pretexts and to despise the work
34    and if anyone should withdraw beyond these arranged terms, to transgress them, and not justly to follow the coming work each
35    day, God willing, apart from illness, the transgressing party shall pay for negligence four gold coins. And if he should withdraw from the work before the completion of the year, the same penalty
36    he shall incur, apart from judgment and trial and any whatsoever pretext-finding
37    and exception of law, we being liable to one another for this and for all
38    the aforewritten things, with all our property now belonging to us and to be ours,
39    movable and immovable and self-moving, by way of pledge
40    and by right of mortgage, for the fulfilment of all things up to the same one
41    year. And upon all the aforewritten terms, having been questioned
42    by one another and having questioned one another, we have agreed to bring it to fulfilment.
