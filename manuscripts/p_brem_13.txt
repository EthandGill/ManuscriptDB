[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermaios to Apollonios the
2     most honored, greetings.
3     They say Petemenekysin the boundary-marker has gone up
4     to you and we are sitting idle
5     waiting for him in [remeasuring] of couch-fields of 11 arouras and toward
6     the bordering account of the metropolis. An inspection of the [account] of year 15 and year 17 of the metropolis and its scribe and the boundary-marker is sought. If it seems good to you, lord, send them at once to us
7     sitting in the field. The gods
8     being willing, tomorrow we
9     will greet you. May you be well,
10    I pray, my lord.
11    To Apollonios, strategos.
