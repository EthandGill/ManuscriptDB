[TRANSLATION]
1     Gaius Antonius to Longinus
2     Crispus the brother greetings;
3     I have sent you your cloak through
4     Doros the horseman and three loaves of bread,
5     and you did not write back to me. If you received [it],
6     write back to me. And you sent
7     me a letter which I was not able to understand, and if you have heard about your successors most accurately, write
8     to me. Therefore do not do otherwise, my brother. Greet those there
9     all by name.
10    Farewell. [11.]
