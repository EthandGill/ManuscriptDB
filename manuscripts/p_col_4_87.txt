[TRANSLATION]
1     To Eukles. Having drawn up the grain account of the lease of the 3rd year
2     from the abatement, according to the comparison made
3     in Memphis, we find over-measured
4     for wheat, artabas 3[00]…
5     and the remainder of the price of the…
6     standing to the credit of the bank…
7     of the price of the 2(?) which were measured out, the whole in copper, 5[00]
8     and likewise also in the individual (account)
9     the same truly standing. For since Aristandros owed
10    formerly from the land-survey, for wheat, 750, now
11    he over-measures from the abatement, artabas 600. Therefore you will do well above all by sending
12    Herakleides the cultivator, if indeed he has the opportunity, but if
13    not, let him instruct Herakleides the grain-collector, or let him send Chairigenes, so that, if it is
14    possible, he may decline…the present…
15    that we set down the individual reckoning
16    so that the over-measured (amount) which (is owed) by the cultivators
17    …[the]
18    they may give in the produce of the 2nd year
19    is acceptable. If the account is set down still giving differences,
20    it is troublesome. For I have not met with these sacred scribes
21    and those with Alexandros.
22    For above all the grain accounts ought to have been drawn up there with you, so that, if anything
23    you approve as better, we may act so.
24    But the Syrian slaves whom you write in the report of the persons, that some are at the
25    vineyard-work, and others at the cultivation,
26    I do not think you are unaware that they have been carried off.
