1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, produce of the 14th year of Antoninus
2     Caesar the lord, Pauni 16, for the account of Agorē
3     [the] name of Phathreōs, similarly Psenasouchios
4     heap of wheat, measures two twelfths, total heap of wheat measures

6     of wheat, measure one sixth, total of wheat, measure one sixth, total to the same
