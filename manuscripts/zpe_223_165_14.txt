1     In the name of the lord and master [Je]sus Christ the God and savior of us, of the reign
2     of the most divine our master Flaviius Maurikio[s] New Tiberius of the eternal Augustus
3     Imperator year twentieth Mesore [new moon], sixth indiction.
4     To Aurelia Anastasia daughter of ... coming from this [town] of Hermopolites
5     for two-thirds share of the triclinium and for [the] whole exedra and Pourou[?] Kallinikos for the third share of the tri[clinium]
6     from George [son] of Paul bucellarious of the most noble from consular
7     the residence having in this [H]ermupolis. I acknowledge willingly and
8     willingly-chose to have rented from [you] for as much time [that] I hold reckoning
9     from new-moon of the present month Mesore of the present sixth indiction
10    the existing to you triclinium in the [second] story turning toward south and exedra one
11    turning toward north onto the plank with taking share of the well and of [the] court
12    and of the roof and of all right from house situated upon the Hermopolites in street of the archangel
13    Gabriel … e …
14    rental made from George of Paul bucellarious …
