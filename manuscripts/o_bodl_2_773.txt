[TRANSLATION]
1     Paniskos and associates, collectors of a share of customs
2     in the name of Pekysios [son] of Psenmōnthos
3     we received for the same share [a] copper drachma
4     one, five obols, [it] amounts to drachma one five-obol.
5     In the year 2 of Antoninus Caesar the lord
6     Phamenōth 12.
7     Phaminnis, I have noted [it], drachma one five-obol.
