# HGV-EN: ... for the ... according to a public ... to appear (before a magistrate) instead of me ... Ptolemaios from a petition ... and his note of hand ... to have against him ... you ... interest ... he has ... upon subscription ... and his note of hand ... When I myself arrived in Arsinoe, I received from you the money and his note of hand.
