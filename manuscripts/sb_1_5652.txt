r.1    … υνθθ τῆς ἁγίας Μαρίας τῆς αὐτῆς νομισμάτια μζ 𐅵.

r.3    ἀπὸ ἀννθθ τῆς ἁγίας Μαρ[ίας] [ἔ]λ[αχεν][ ὑμῖν ὑπὲρ ][δη]μ[οσ]ί[ων]

r.6    γίνεται νομισμάτια μζ 𐅵.

[TRANSLATION]
1     … [of/from] the holy Mary [of the same], coins 47 1/2.
2     In the name of the God. Korra son of Szerich, counselor to you
3     from …[of] the holy Mary [he assigned [to you for] public [taxes]
4     indiction 6, according to Arabs year 88, in coin numerals 47 1/2, forty
5     [seven] and half alone. Written in month Thoth, indiction 8th.
6     There become coins 47 1/2.
