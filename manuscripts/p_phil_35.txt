[TRANSLATION]
1     Longinus to Hostillius his brother,
2     greeting. Before all things I pray that you
3     are in health, along with my (children) safe from the evil eye,
4     my little ones. I am amazed how, though you are prospering (lit. walking straight), and though I sent even a little letter through Valerianus the Nestopoikite, a trustworthy man, I do not believe that he did not deliver to you the little letter, when I pursued (him) as far as
5     Helikon; and through the (man) of
6     Marcia I sent you the pound (of something), finding him at the gate,
7     he says he keeps watch at the gate at Philadelpheia
8     to keep guard, and not even a little letter
9     did you send me, nor any
10    word. You will do well
11    to send (word) about your welfare,
12    than which I have nothing greater, unless you do not care
13    about me; but I do care about
14    you, although you (are) in the parts around the city (that are) crowded outside the gates,
15    perhaps something plotting may befall you, lest somehow abuse
16    come upon me, so that you may know where
17    I am and to what place I am (going); but for once you do not care about me.
18    You will do well, then, to hasten
19    to come up to me, having finished (things) [qu]ickly. And I wish, through some trustworthy person, to [se]nd you the money (bronze), as
20    I wrote to you before. May you be in good health
21    for [ma]ny (years); I greet by name
22    all the men and all the women, my father, Diodora … ikario …
