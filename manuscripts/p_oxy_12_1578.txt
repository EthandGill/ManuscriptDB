[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Sarapammon, procurator of Claudia Isidora,
2     from Aurelius Areios, steward of Tholthis [Thmoisepho.]
3     I request that there be ordered, for wages
4     of the present month Hathyr, 1[26] drachmas,
5     and for purchase of grass-seed
6     and the sowing of the grain-lands, and
7     for the sealing of the imperial wine, 341 drachmas,
8     and the (sums) spent by me in excess-expenditure, according to the accounts of Phaophi, 53 drachmas.
9     They make of the request 52[.] drachmas …
10    And reckon in return, for the price of 3 jars,
11    of the property of Aurelia Athenais, of the produce of the 2nd year,
12    at the rate of the 1st (year), of 9 drachmas 1 obol, 27 drachmas 3 obols.
13    Year 4, Hathyr 27. I, Aurelius Areios, have presented (this).
14    I, Aurelius Sarapammon also called Dionysius, have noted the (drachmas) of the request,
15    520 drachmas, in which are deducted 27 drachmas 3 obols, concerning which I have written to Asclepiades the cashier.
16    To Aurelius Sarapammon, procurator of Claudia
17    Isidora, the most estimable,
18    from Aurelius Areios, steward of Tholthis Thmoisepho.
19    I request that there be ordered, for wages
20    of the present month Choiak, 128 drachmas,
21    and for the works coming on, 40 drachmas;
22    they make of the request 168 drachmas.
23    Year 4, Choiak 20. I, Aurelius Areios, have presented (this).
24    I, Aurelius Sarapammon also called Dionysius, have noted the (drachmas) of the request,
25    one hundred sixty-eight, concerning which
26    I have written to Asclepiades the cashier.
27    Pay out to Didymianus also called Philantinoos, towards which he conveyed,
28    a supplement of silver, of 2 talents, after which
29    he received 1 talent, others in part on account, 2000 drachmas.
