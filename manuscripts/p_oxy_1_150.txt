[TRANSLATION]
1     Was given through Phoibammon, wine-servant, to the 14 buccellarii of Herakleous and Koma, having come here for the sake of
2     The battle of Kosma from the greater [authority], by account of expenditure Phaophi 10, indiction 9, wine, three and one-half knidions; comes to wine
3     Knidions three and one-half only.
4     Year 267 and 236, month Phaophi 10, indiction 9. Comes to wine knidions three and one-half.
