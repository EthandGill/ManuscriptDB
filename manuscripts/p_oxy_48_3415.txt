[TRANSLATION]
1     Diogenes to Dorotheos and
2     Papnoubis, aides.
3     Eudaimon, our aide,
4     I sent so that you might complete the twenty four
5     coins,
6     the twenty from the account
7     of the gold-silver, and the four
8     from the account of Constantinople; not only but also
9     the remaining [amounts] of the 3rd and 4th
10    indictions of both the grain-collectors and village-chiefs. I pray you be well.
