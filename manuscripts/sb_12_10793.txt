[TRANSLATION]
1     Harpochration son of Horion to Dorion [son] of Heras, money-changer, greetings. Pay
2     Amphon and Galatas, tavern-keepers, for garments for Dionysios [son] of Serapion
3     540 silver drachmas; [total] 540 drachmas
4     Year 4 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Pharmouthi 18
5     Amphon [said: I] received as ordered, and I transferred to Onesikrates the banker of Pterophoros silver
6     540 drachmas, totaling 540 drachmas. Year 4 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Pharmouthi 18
7     Galatas, register as aforesaid. Year 4 of Tiberius
8     Caesar Augustus, Pharmouthi 18
