1     [In the name] of the [l]ord and master Jesus Christ the god
2     [and savior] of us. [In the reign] of our most divine lord
3     [Flavius Maur]icius Tiberius eternal Augustus, year 12
4     [month] Thoth 23, 13th indiction in Arsinoe.
5     [Aurelius P]etrus son of Epimachos, fish-cook from
6     [the Arsinoit]e city, from the district of Theon, to Aurelius Paul
7     [son of so-and-so] … and likewise fish-cook from the same city, greetings.
8     Since I had you in my workshop in the manner of a wage-worker
9     and [after] you departed from your stay with me,
10    I moved [a claim] against you concerning a theft as having occurred to me from you.
11    And when many things were moved between us, finally, me having become weak
12    it se[emed] to [us] with worthy men so as to take
13    [from you] for complete release [of gold]
14    [coins] … [becomes gold coins] … [having] been satisfied by you
15    [from now on, I ordered you] the present written agreement
16    [simple, having been written] by myself to take, through which I acknowledge
17    [not to bring charges against you and to have] no claim to me
18    [nor anyone on my behalf concerning this] nor concerning anything else whatsoever
19    [matter written or un]written in total because in all
20    [to have been released and dis]solved in relation to you. But if henceforth
21    [I proceed against you in any] way, [let me] be liable
22    [to the appointed fine] of two coins. The agreement [is] binding.
23    [And when asked I acknowledged.] [Aurelius Petrus, son] of Epi[m]achos, the aforesaid
24    … Aurelius Justus of Petrus
