[TRANSLATION]
1     A measurement to the treasury of the villages, of produce [of]
2     year 21 of Antoninus Caesar
3     our lord, Payni 18, for Charakos
4     in the name of Petechon of Pikotos, mother Senchonsios, wheat, one-half
5     one-twelfth, becomes wheat 1/2 1/12. Amonius marked.
