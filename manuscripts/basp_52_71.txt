[TRANSLATION]
1     I greet you and worship and [worship] the footprints of your sandals and all
2     [of those] being with you according to name.
3     Vouchsafe therefore to receive the rule [canon] from
4     the letter-carrier and this necessarily
5     [do] write, your piety, that the blessed father
6     Isaac perished from the sixth [day] of this month;
7     because of this he was not able to place loss
8     and [to] meet your messenger, master.
9     Give to my beloved brother Kyros from Kyriakos [the] brother.
