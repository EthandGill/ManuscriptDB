[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Philoxenos to Apollonios the strategist
2     of the Heptakomy, greetings.
3     I do not think you are ignorant that Malchos is mine,
4     so that thus you hold yourself toward him as toward
5     my own part. Why do I write more to you?
6     [You] know indeed my disposition. I wish you to be well.
7     To Apollonios the strategist
8     from Flavius Philoxenos
9     epistrategos of the Thebaid
