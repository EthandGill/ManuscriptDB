[TRANSLATION]
1     ... to Apo[llon]ios, greetings. [It would go] w[ell if you] are in good health; we too are in good health. Know
2     me not ... . I thought indeed to write first to you rather than to oppose, so that ... . You will do well then,
3     if it also seems [good] to you, having written to me to come to you, so that I might give you an account concerning all things how each thing stands; for I [did not think it] right to make [my] residence without you.
4     Do not then overlook us [having been] ruined, but come to [our] aid. For you took thought
5     [that] we share in honor, but this one has clothed me with the greatest dishonor.
6     Concerning a dispute indeed I do not speak, [in] as much as [the amount that] I unjustly brought in against [my] will [having been] treated contrary to law.
7     Farewell. Year 28, Dystros 18th [day].
8     To Apol[lonios].
