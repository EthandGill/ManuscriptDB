[TRANSLATION]
1     Scrutinized, year 18.
2     To Calpurnius Horion, the most excellent
3     recorder, councillor of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians,
4     from Aurelius Melas son of Heraclides,
5     grandson of Ammonius also called Diogenes, his mother being Thaesis, from the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites.
6     Having presented myself and been scrutinized
7     in the quarter of Myrobalanus and entered in the documents submitted, person by person, to the sacred
8     corn-dole by the phylarch, but when in the examination being assigned I was not able the proofs of my descent
9     to furnish; necessarily now furnishing the token of the scrutiny and of my descent
10    the proofs, I submit these
11    documents, and I myself request also
12    to share in the corn largess,
13    having been scrutinized in the 15th year according to the decisions of the most excellent council, on equal terms
14    with those like me. Farewell.
15    Year 1 of the Emperor Caesar
16    Lucius Domitius Aurelianus
17    Pius Felix Augustus, and of Julius
18    Aurelius Septimius Vaballathus
19    Athenodorus, the most illustrious king,
20    consul, emperor, general of the Romans.
21    … I, Aurelius Melas son of Heraclides, have submitted (this). I, Aurelius
22    … wrote on his behalf, since he
23    does not know letters.
