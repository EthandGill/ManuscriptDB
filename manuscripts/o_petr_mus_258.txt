[TRANSLATION]
1     Thoteus, collector of the division of customs-remainder of Charakos, to Kleopatra Bassus
2     of Dekmos, through Theon of Bassus and Heraklas the brother
3     [I/we] have received for the division in year 12, seven drachmas, [which] makes 7 drachmas, year 13 of Antoninus
4     Caesar the lord, Mesore 30.
