[TRANSLATION]
1     [Af]ter the consul[ship of Flavius Theodorus]
2     the most illust[rious][ , Epeiph ] …
3     To the Aurelias Theodor[a and]
4     Tauris, from their father Silvanos,
5     [fr]om the illustrious and most illustrious
6     [O]xyrhynchite city, ascetic nuns,
7     from Aurelius Iose son of Iudas, a Jew,
8     from the same city. Voluntarily
9     I undertake to lease from the new-moon
10    of the next month Mesore of the present
11    year 76, with the beginning of the four[teenth]
12    indiction, from the property
13    belonging to you in the same Oxyrhynchite city
14    in the quarter of the Cavalry Camp,
15    one ground-floor room, an exedra, an[d]
16    [t]h[e] one vault in the cellar,
17    [with] all appurtenances, and I will pay
18    [for] their rent yearly
19    twelve hundred myriads of silver,
20    making 1200 myriads of denarii; and of necessity I will pay the rent
21    by half-year, the half, without fail,
22    and whenever you wish I will hand over the
23    same premises clean as also I received them.
24    The lease is valid, written in duplicate, and having been questioned I acknowledged.
25    I, Aurelius Iose son of Iudas, the aforesaid,
26    have leased the dining-hall and will pay the rent as aforesaid. Aurelius Elias
27    son of Opebaios, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
