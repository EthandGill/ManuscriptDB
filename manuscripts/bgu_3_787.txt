[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Year 5 of Gaius Caesar Imperator Augustus, 26th of the month of Soter. I, Heraklas, son of phros, have had measured to me from the produce of year 4 for the dues on (land) at one artaba of Karanis: Chairemon, son of Sokrates by levelled public measure epaiton twenty nine artabas of wheat, for the tax of a twentieth one and a half; the same person for the dues on idioktetos (land) six 1/4, for the tax of a twentieth 1/4 = for the receipt 37 artabas of wheat.
