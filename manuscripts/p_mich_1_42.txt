[TRANSLATION]
1     Criton to Zenon greeting. Since Polemon the one from our [place]
2     he is transferring his mother's sister to [Philadelphia]
3     he has thought fit for me to write [to you] [so that they] buy jointly for him through you
4     100 artabae of wheat, so that he might [have] it [for] the support of the household
5     you will oblige me by buying jointly for him as [most profitably]
6     whenever you buy jointly
7     send word to us and to Polemon
8     so that we know and, having received the price from him, send it to you
9     Farewell. Year 33, Pachon 14
10    To Zenon in Philadelphia.
11    Criton.
12    Of the joint purchase
13    100 artabae of wheat.
