# HGV-EN: Hermas, secretary of Antonius Dius, to the sitologi of the district of Sincepha, greetings. Transfer, from the amount you hold on deposit in your granary, for Antonius Dius, from the wheat-crop of the current 6th year of Hadrianus Caesar the lord, to Diogenes alias Theon, son of Theon, or to whomever he chooses, one hundred artabas, total 100 art. Year 6 of Hadrianus the lord, Mesore epagomenal 5. (N. Gonis)
