[TRANSLATION]
1     Petearouēris and Pamōnthes, collectors of money-taxes of Charakos,
2     to Paoukalē Pachnumius. We received for the assignment
3     of the 7th year [in] dirty drachmas four, amounting to dirty drachmas 4. Year 8
4     of Antoninus Caesar our lord, Hadrian 19.
5     Pamōnthes, I have signed.
6     another 23, likewise in the name of the same, [in] dirty drachmas two and three-obol, amounting to drachmas 2 and three-obol.
