10    in the twenty-second year, [when Alexik]rates son of Theoge[nes was priest of Alexander]
11    and the Brother-and-Sister Gods and the [Benefactor Gods, the basket-bearer] of Arsinoe Phil[adelphos being Berenike]
12    daughter of Kallianax, in the month [of Xandikos, on the eleventh], in Krokodilon polis [of the Arsinoite]
13    nome, the following dispositions were made, being of sound mind [and understanding, by …]s son of Philon, Sol[ean of the epigone,]
14    aged about sixty, of middle height, injured in the left eye,

16    mortal and end my life, [I leave] all my possessions [to my]
17    wife Artemid[ora] … [and] to the children [born of her,]

19    possessions … [on condition that she] provide to the [children]
