[TRANSLATION]
1     Amyntas to Zenon
2     greetings. We have given
3     to Agathus the steersman
4     as you wrote to us
5     silver drachmas fifty, and the wine
6     and the honey because of Aristea
7     and [his] not being in town and you [all]
8     at the same time [to] exp[ect] not yet
9     to you we have sent. And whenever
10    we perceive whether you arrive or
11    not, we will send [it] to you.
12    Farewell. Of year 28,
13    Hathyr 8.
