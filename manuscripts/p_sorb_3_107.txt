[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greetings. Petous of Thoteias, priest of the crocodiles from Muche of the Polemon district. [Concerning] the sacred [things] dedicated upon the place to the crocodile-burial [ground] which I have been in charge of in the aforementioned village, [a plot] of land of 5 arouras from which the
2     sacrifices and the libations on behalf of you [I] perform, and I having controlled
3     the sacred [place] and the 5 arouras … [being repeatedly demanded] dragged [until] now being in difficulty, Amenaeus [of] the [same] village [proceeding] makes himself owner of certain portions of the land as if [belonging] to him. Since then there is a sacred month,
4     I ask you [and] beseech [you], King, to order Diophanes the strategos to write
5     to Demetrius the overseer [regarding] the produce of the sacred land to the
6     crocodile-burial [ground] having brought to the sacred [place] to seal up until
7     the sacred month to pass and for me to be judged with respect to [the] Amenaeus, so that through you,
8     King, I may obtain justice.
9     Farewell.
10    To Demetrius: … if anyone opposes, send them to us.
11    year 3 Peritios 1 Phauni 4.
12    [P]etous to Demetrius
