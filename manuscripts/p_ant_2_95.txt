[TRANSLATION]
1     On another occasion too I wrote to your brotherly lordship through the assistant of the
2     deacons concerning her expense. And I urge her to write to me continually
3     (of) her health … … I urge her that, if
4     with God [your brotherly lordship] does not come up, she write to me her power of attorney [so that]
5     I may find (means) to lay claim to the remaining affairs left over to us, and that
6     your house not be closed. And let her write to me what she wishes regarding her account and regarding the account
7     of her slaves, for information, and I will send to her, so that she not be compelled to borrow from elsewhere
8     and to give double for single. And I urge her to write to the common
9     mistress, your mother, that she come up, since, by the Lord God, in Oxyrhynchus
10    she is from the time your illustrious brotherhood went down to Alexandria, weeping
11    and afflicted and distressed, as I said before, from the time he departed from her.
12    By every means therefore … to come up, your illustrious brotherhood,
13    meanwhile to make for me the power of attorney, so that I may find (means), readily, with God working together
14    and with the help of the lord Kosmas the embolator, to take care of the remaining affairs left over to us. These things I write, doing much obeisance and
15    greeting the footsteps of your illustrious brotherhood until (we) meet, lord.
