[TRANSLATION]
1     To Gaius Tyrranius,
2     from Teseus and Erieus the leaders and the rest of the priests of Soknopaios the great great god, of the temple that is in the village of Soknopaiou Nesos in the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite: of the public (dues) annually registered on behalf of the temple, and of the other (expenses)
3     spent on the vestings and the monthly
4     processions performed on behalf of the god Caesar and the incense-offerings and burnings of the lamps and the other expenses that occur … to us as we go to … preserving … of all the festivals
5     and processions, so that the village … to be … advantageous to us
6     to remain over, but that the shore from which they draw water is distant … a schoinion, and at certain seasons of the waters into the village … being carried … through a four-schoinion (stretch); and in the summer, annually, when all the people from … [our] village
7     withdraw to gain a livelihood … us the pr[iests by] necessity it happens that we remain in the village for the watching … of the temple and the
8     processions and the others, with no other benefit … to us, except the giving from those in charge of the … processions
9     and the maintenance, from which the public (dues) … …
10    …and it happens … and
11    those borrowing from … certain … the ancient custom
12    to renew … [wherefore we ask] you, the [savior of all and prot]ector …
