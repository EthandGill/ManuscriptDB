[TRANSLATION]
1   […] provide what there is […]
2   We have come out from the inspection.
3   And we sent to you by the sword-
4   bearer Daphnos, who has a …
5   … and letters, and by an-
6   other sword-bearer, Hermon,
7   to the temple of Leto one let-
8   ter. So do not be negligent
9   about anything. As for the date-
10   palm: old fruit we found none, neither
11   in the Diopolite nor in the Ombite nome,
12   and the new is still in hand. For I am per-
13   suaded that meanwhile Ptolemaios, having sent word,
14   will be charged to convey two artabas
15   of loaves
16   and a half-basket of pigeons
17   and a flagon of pickled fish
18   and a phagros-fish kept whole; in
19   the flagon, number 40 — total 40; in the half-
20   basket, pigeons, number 35, and one
21   basket-phagros.
22   And wash yourself, that you keep healthy. So I urge you, carefully, regarding the …
23   … and toward Ptolemaios. Year 3 of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
24   Epeiph 12.
25   [To N.N.,] former strategos of Peri Thebas.
