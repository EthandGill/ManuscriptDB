[TRANSLATION]
1   † Account of gold found in the keeping of the blessed Paulos, former headman, of Ophis,
2   and given to the
3   his named heirs, in the month Epeiph 27,
4   13th indiction, out of 360 solidi, as follows:
5   through Serenos, son of the same Paulos, 159 solidi 20 carats,
6   and for their weight-allowance, 6 solidi 4 carats,
7   total 166 solidi, Alexandrian standard.
8   Through Phoibammon the goldsmith, on behalf of his wife Herais, 107 solidi 16 carats,
9   and for their weight-allowance, 4 solidi 8 carats,
10   total 112 solidi, Alexandrian standard.
11   Through Makarios the headman, on behalf of his wife Sophia, 78 solidi 20 carats,
12   and for their weight-allowance, 3 solidi 4 carats,
13   total 82 solidi, Alexandrian standard.
14   Grand total 360 solidi, Alexandrian standard. †
