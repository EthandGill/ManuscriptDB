[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidorus to Hermaisca the most honorable, greetings.
2     the contractors approached seeking units
3     for the need of the pillar. According to necessity
4     I write to you, brother, so that you might use [it] from Hieronymus
5     and with you bring [it] until the need is completed.
6     I pray you are well, brother.
