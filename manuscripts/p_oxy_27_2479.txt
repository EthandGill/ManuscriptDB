[TRANSLATION]
1     To my good benefactor and master, a petition and supplication
2     from Pieous, your slave, from the hamlet of Kineas.
3     The love of the poor and love of Christ of your all-glorious lordship,
4     having gone forth over all the earth, has caused many to flee to it for refuge,
5     who on coming obtain justice and all mercy.
6     Whence I too, the pitiable one, come, weeping and explaining concerning myself
7     that I happen to be a registered farmer of your all-glorious lordship,
8     and that three years ago my beasts died and I withdrew
9     from my farming, as has been said, three years ago, and I remained abroad.
10    And for the same three-year period, remaining abroad, in order that I might find
11    … sustenance and be sustained, I, with my children,
12    … of the holding.
13    I beseech and supplicate your all-glorious lordship
14    that it order me to enter into the holding and that I not be dunned
15    for that which I did not sow, so that hereafter I may be able to work
16    in the same holding and to nourish my wretched children. And I will send up
17    to the Lord Christ my prayers for the life of your all-glorious
18    lordship. For I am unable, master, to pay for that which
19    I do not sow. For there are those who do sow and are able to pay.
20    And this too I explain, that when the administrator came into the holding he
21    plundered all my poor goods on account of this same
22    cause. And I beseech to obtain your mercy. For I call to witness
23    the Lord God that I have not even the broken morsel, that is, the dry
24    crust, to eat with my children, in the face of
25    this present scarcity. And on this account I have fled for refuge to it,
26    so that I may be numbered among all those benefited
27    by it, most all-glorious benefactor, master, lord.
