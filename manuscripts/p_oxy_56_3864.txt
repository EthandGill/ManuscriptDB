[TRANSLATION]
1     643 (= the numerical symbol for "God is my helper")
2     To my master, truly in all things
3     most honored to me, father Dorotheos,
4     Apa[mmo]n, in the Lord God, greeting.
5     Before [a]ll things I pray to the all-merciful
6     God concerning your well-being, that my letter may find you
7     in health and good cheer. Then know that, through the
8     grace of the Savior and the prayers of the
9     righteous, we arrived at Chaireou after seventeen days, and we suffered nothing bad
10    nor did we give any toll (commodum) along the river,
11    except only three hundred myriads … pumou
12    eiameri, and at the mouth of the canal we gave another one thousand two hundred myriads, total 1,200 myriads of denarii,
13    total together 1,500 myriads of denarii. And the general (stratelates) came in
14    to me and did nothing bad, nor does he have soldiers at all. Since I was delayed on the road,
15    for this reason I did not overtake Alphios nor Makarios. By the grace of God I completed the unloading of the sacks, by the love of our martyrs. Still there is great
16    inactivity (no trade) in Alexandria. Do not therefore wish
17    to take a surcharge (epitheke) from anyone, upon (the understanding that) I am dissolving (settling) these things and going up to Oxyrhynchos.
18    By God, …s… Heraiskos sent Theodo[ros his] assistant
19    to Caesarea with [the] sacks
20    of his again, and Sera…
21    Dorotheos … the brother(s) …
22    to Caesarea with his sacks. Behold, I have written to you beforehand. Do not therefore wish
23    to take a surcharge and make me ashamed in Alexandria.
24    If God [wills] and a transaction takes place, I will not neglect (it), I will send you a gold coin. For already
25    … Theophilos before unloading and gave up beforehand … the
26    receipt through our brother P… . Many greetings to you
27    and to you (pl.) by name in your house. I pray for your health
28    for many years. Pauni 29.
29    Deliver …
