[TRANSLATION]
1     Measure into the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 19 of Antoninus
2     Caesar the lord, Pauni 2, for
3     Charax, in the name of Senkametis (child of) Petemenophis, through the cultivator
4     Psenamounis (son of) Paous, of wheat two and a half
5     and a quarter, total wheat 2¾. I, Horos, have signed.
