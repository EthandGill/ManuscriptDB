[TRANSLATION]
1     The great couch of the blessed Dioskoros with its table, declare [this], since I wish to take it and not
2     not permit anyone to take it, but if it has been appraised, write to me how it was appraised and by whom, that I might write to you
3     to give the gold. Immediately therefore write me this reply. And a small vessel you shall manage whether by tax or of the
4     price to Pkoungthes. And a letter the governor of honey made to his wife in order that she give us two
5     jars of honey. This I have sent to you, provide it and receive the two jars of honey
6     to the most illustrious in all respects worthy of honor Zacharias the cadastral-keeper Hypatius
