[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Bakchon, the dioiketes,] from Amenneus
2     [son of Horos, and On]nophris son of Teos,
3     [embalmers] from Tanis in the
4     Arsinoite nome. We denounce
5     Abykis son of Horos, and Kelechon son of Pasis,
6     and Pasis son of Psenophmous, embalmers
7     [from the village of] Ph[iladel]pheia of the same nome,
8     that, an ordinance being in force that the
9     … of those who acted recklessly,
10    the property of [these apostates] be taken up [into] the treasury; and since Psenophmous
11    son of Paos [had been] among the apostates,
12    to whom there was no relative, but there belonged to him both landed property and other things,
13    and likewise also an embalmer's prerogative
14    of the embalming-establishments [in Ph]iladelpheia — these things
15    having been reported and it having been written that
16    his property be sold into the treasury, there was sold
17    the house belonging to him in the
18    [aforementioned] village, which
19    [Hakoris] son of Paches, a royal cultivator of those
20    [from the same] village, purchased and holds
21    [until now]; but the other things belonging to him and the
22    aforesaid prerogative, those about Abykis,
23    against whom I make this manifest, distributed among themselves
24    contrary [to what is] proper, not paying the price
25    as was customary. And there arises from the prerogative
26    each year 4000 copper drachmas, which they carry off
27    from the 2nd year until now, 19 years,
28    which amounts to 12 talents 4000 copper drachmas. So that nothing may be left out
29    of what pertains to the treasury, we request, if it seems good to you, to write to Hephaistion the epimeletes
30    to dispatch the aforementioned men [before you],
31    so that … as … the aforesaid amount of the copper may be exacted
32    into the treasury … … as is fitting …
33    … not to permit anyone to lay
34    hands upon them until the settlement concerning these matters
35    take place. Farewell.
