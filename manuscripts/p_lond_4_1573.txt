[TRANSLATION]
1     … the [public] account from you [by you (?)],
2     our illustrious one and the pagarch of the village of Tjkoou and after (it) the hamlet …
3     greeting. We acknowledge that, since we summon (?) you, the [most glorious] …
4     the son of the blessed Philotheos, our man, so that what is yours may come to be …
5     of the (people of) Bel(je), that he may collect our public dues and our (military) services (andrismos) and the expense …
6     of (the people of) Belje, for this year, this eighth indiction; what is yours will come [to be, if you (?)]
7     collect our exchange (katallage) …
8     one and the same; and again we acknowledge that everything …
9     upon us, we hand it over within into our house; for we summon (?) you, your [lordship] …
10    you (?) have done it for us as a head, the [well-off] one being for the [poor] one …
11    securely, your lordship; we have drawn up this acknowledgment …
12    against him, and we take the risk of the whole of what is ours, the capital sum …
13    and that which does not move (immovable property) — we lay it as a pledge of account against it, until we make …
14    … [we sw]ear by the name of God the Almighty and your (?) salvation …
15    we will keep guard according to the power of this acknowledgment, of our own free will (?); we acknowledge: [ ]
16    I, Psige the son of the blessed Victor, the man of Pachoune, I agree [to this parakletic]
17    acknowledgment according to his power. I, Theodoros the son of the blessed [John, I agree]
18    to this parakletic acknowledgment according to its power. I, Geo[rge the son of Dione]
19    the man of Pachoune, I agree to this parakletic acknowledgment according to [its power] …
20    I, Apa Kire the son of the blessed Kosma, the man of Pachaune, I agree to this parakletic [acknowledgment]
21    according to its power. I, Shinoute the son of Kolthe, the man of Pachaune, I agree to this parakletic [acknowledgment according to its power] …
22    I, Theodoros the son of the blessed Philotheos, the man of Pachoun, I agree …
23    according to its power. I, Theodoros the son of the blessed John, who requested [me, I wrote]
24    on his (behalf), since he does not understand. I, Theudose the son of the blessed Shinoute …
25    acknowledgment according to its power. I, Theodoros the son of the blessed John, who [requested me, I wrote]
26    on his (behalf). [I, Panesneu the son of] the blessed (Makare) according to its power. I, Apollo the son of Pshoi — he told it to me, I wrote …
27    I, Ezekias the son of the blessed [Cha]moul, I bear witness (to) this parakletic (acknowledgment) …
28    I, Andreas the presbyter and the abbot, I bear witness (to) this parakletic (acknowledgment) …
29    I, Philotheos the son of Makare, I bear witness to this parakletic acknowledgment …
30    I, Pahom the presbyter, I bear witness to this security (asphaleia) according to its power.
31    Assessment of 12 names:
32    Through Psige (son of) Victor: 1 name; through Theodoros (son of) John: 1 name.
33    Through George (son of) Dione: 1 name; through Apa Kyros (son of) Kosmas: 1 name.
34    Through Senouthios (son of) Kollouthos: 1 name; through Theodoros (son of) Philotheos: 1 name.
35    Through Theodosios (son of) Senouthios: 1 name; through Panisneu (son of) Makarios: 1 name.
36    Through me, Apollo (son of) Psoios, having been requested, it was written.
