[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Helio[doro]u[s] [very much]
2     greetings and always to [be in good health].
3     Asclepiades, the one delivering the letter, is our brother and [kinsman].
4     and in a certain way Ptollion's of …
5     boy's father. Therefore I urge young
6     brother, in whatever if he should have need of you …
7     to work together. So the man …
8     practical; in whatever if you should be useful to him and he himself will repay [favor],
9     but beside all things favor of the body
10    being concerned, so that you may [be in good health].
11    Fare well. Year twenty-five, Hathyr …
12    Heliodorus.
