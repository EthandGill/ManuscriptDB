[TRANSLATION]
1     Diodorus and associates, collectors of the crown-tax of the village of Theogonides, to Aion Apynchis, greetings. We received from you on behalf of the crown-tax the 22nd
2     year of enumeration: Hathyr drachmas 4, Tybi drachmas 4, Mecheir obols 16, Pharmouthi obols 12, Pauni obols 16, Mesore
3     obols 16, Phaophi obols 12.
