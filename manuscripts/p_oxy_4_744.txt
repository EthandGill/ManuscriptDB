[TRANSLATION]
1     Hilarian to Alist the sister, to Beroit my lady, and to Apollonarius, very many greetings. Know that we are even now in [Alexandria]; do not worry if at all they enter - I will remain in [Alexandria].
2     I ask and urge you [to take care of] the child, and if we immediately receive the wage, I will send to you up. If
3     Many of many, if you give birth—if it is male, let [it]; if it is female, cast [it] out.
4     You have said to Aphrodisias that don't me …
5     forget? How am I able to forget you? I ask you therefore that you not worry.
6     Year 29 of Caesar, Pauni 23.
7     Hilarian to Alist, deliver.
