[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelia Sarapous, daughter of Theon son of Theon, who had been exegetes of the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
2     acting without a guardian by the right of children,
3     from the Aurelii …, Sarapammon, his mother being Taaphynchis, and his son Hiereus, his mother being Taaphynchis, both from Nomou Epoikiou. Of our own free will we undertake to lease from y[ou for a period] of two years from the 1st of the present month Hathyr of the present 5th year all the hand-labour vine-tending works
4     and the irrigation of the vineyard property belonging to you near Senepta, called Thaaut, of six arouras or however many there may be, and of the adjoining reed-bed of whatever area it is in arouras, [which]
5     works are, of the vine: the stripping of the reed, … its collection and transport, the (proper) wood-cutting,
6     the gathering and binding of bundles and transport, the sweeping of leaves and the throwing-out beyond the moulded (banks), the digging, the trenching, the layering of as many layers as are needed; and we shall stand in the reed-bed
7     before the donkeys carrying earth, so as to (place) where the earth must be thrown, as we shall need of these things; the second cutting of the reed, the reed-working, the hoeing, the shoot-trimming, the Pharmouthi-work, the spreading (of vines), the taking-up, the leaf-pickings that are required, the irrigation and continual weeding, and also all the other seasonal works of the vine; and of the reed-bed the irrigation and
8     the trenching and the scattering of manure; and we shall make for the press-vat and the trough the required repairs, and for the apparatus the … (kyltides), and the rolling of the empty vessels holding the year's wine from the place whence it is transported;
9     and we shall make the rolling of the empty (vessels) from the place whence it is transported; which, when they have received the wine, we shall store
10    in the sun-house and we shall seal them and stir them and watch over (them) as long as it
11    lies there; and furthermore the other one of us, Hiereus, shall sleep continually in the farmstead,
12    at a wage for all the works of the vine, per aroura, of three hundred [six]ty drachmas of silver; and the wages collected we shall receive each year (thus): Hathyr–Choiak for the stripping of the reed, two hundred drachmas; Tybi for the wood-cutting, four hundred drachmas; Mecheir for the reed-working, four hundred drachmas; Phamenoth, one hundred eighty drachmas; Pharmouthi, two hundred forty drachmas; Pachon, one hundred drachmas; Pauni for the leaf-picking, two hundred forty drachmas; Epeiph and Mesore, and at the year's end Thoth and Phaophi, each year the proportionate one hundred drachmas per month;
13    and we shall receive each year for the wood-cutting one keramion of vinegar, and at the time of the harvest, so that we may not be left behind in the irrigation and the leaf-picking, each year, of whe[at]
14    three artabas, and at the vintage each year two keramia of new wine; and … we undertake
15    to contract for half the share of the fruit of the date-palms in the property at the established rent and extras each year; and furthermore to lease, for the present year only,
16    from the lands belonging to you near the same Senepta, from the Dionysophan… (holding)
17    two arouras of the allotment so as to sow one aroura with wheat and one aroura with barley, at a rent for the (one)
18    in wheat of four artabas of wheat, and for the (one) in barley of five artabas of barley, free of all risk,
19    of all risk, the public taxes of the land being the responsibility of you the landowner,
20    who shall also be owner of the crops until you receive the (sums) owed each year; and the undertaking
21    being guaranteed to us, we shall of necessity perform of the vine and reed-bed the seasonal
22    works all, as has been shown above, and of the date-palms and plants the watering
23    and all the care, your (agents) attending all the works so that the vine is not damaged; and we shall deliver the rents of the plots in the present year on the threshing-floor of the place, new produce, pure, unadulterated, free of clods, sifted,
24    the wheat free of barley as measured into the public (granary), the barley well trodden, free of darnel and ear-stubble, by the receiving-measure of you the landowner, your (agents) measuring and measuring in addition two choinikes to each artaba; and the (rent) of the date-palms,
25    the rent that shall appear and the extras, year by year each year, Thoth and Phaophi, without delay; and at the end of the period we shall deliver the vine and reed-bed
26    cared for in the works, the vine clean of rush and of all rank weed, the moulded (vine) … (covered), and the plants living and flourishing; and we shall pay, for whatever work we do not complete or for a plant withered through negligence,
27    the damage twofold, the right of execution being yours against us, being mutually sureties for the payment, and against whichever of us you choose, and out of all our possessions. Valid (is)
28    the undertaking, and on being questioned we acknowledged. Year 5
29    of the Imperatores Caesares Publius Licinius [Vale]r[i]anus and P[ublius] …
30    … .
