[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings in the Lord God, the Savior, friend Pascentia
2     Through Theon
3     There has come to you the
4     most divine and comforting word:
5     May your mind not willingly turn away,
6     Nor overlook
7     the petition of the poor,
8     those in necessity
9     Therefore the old woman having come
10    asked for letters
11    to your God-inspired judgment, having explained that I am wronged, and my son
12    is being wronged
13    Know therefore with certainty
14    whether he has some legal justification, and come together piously
