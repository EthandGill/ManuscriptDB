[TRANSLATION]
1     Stephanos servant of the Theotokos
2     to Ioannes, grain official of Ptolemais.
3     Provide to Ioannes son of Danielios
4     in place of what was given by him for feeding
5     of oxen ploughing for the
6     cultivated by me arouras according to sowing
7     12 of vetch artabas four; amount of vetch artabas 4.
8     Tybi 1 in[diction] 12 amount of vetch
9     artabas four
10    month of Tybi 1 in[diction] 12.
11    Tybi 1 from the most pious lord Stephanos of vetch artabas 4 only.
