[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aelius Herakleitos, assistant of Aelius Eglektos
2     procurator of the lord Caesar,
3     from Ptolemaios son of Diodoros also called Dioskoros,
4     lessee of the marsh of the village of Theadelphia. Whereas
5     the customary waters, which ought to come down
6     into the aforesaid marsh until it is filled, up to
7     now it has not received (them), but is in danger of drying up; wherefore I request you to send to the beach-guard
8     a le[tt]er (to say) that, if any negligence occur with the wat[e]rs and it be not filled as is customary, not [on]ly in the following year does damage ensue, for, the
9     [marsh being lea]sed, but for three years it becomes
10    [unproductive]. I request therefore, l[or]d, to make (it) plain to the beach-guar[d, so that], if through negligence any damage occur,
11    … [that] I [may hav]e recourse against him before the most excellen[t proc]urator. Year 8, Hathyr 7.
12    [ ] … . Aelius Herakleitos to Apollonios the beach-guard
13    [the] most dear, gr[eetin]g. I did not expect that concerning the
14    [mar]sh too I would be petitioned, that you should furnish it
15    [the su]fficient waters, as its lessee
16    [Ptole]maios has requested. For it was fitting
17    [for] your [c]are to attend to this too before anyone petitioned (you).
18    But if s[o]meth[ing] u[p to] now has been an impediment
19    [or someth]ing else of the (things) that seem more pressing to you, it provided a postpon[emen]t in the filling of the marsh; but
20    [also now], brother, take thought, so that the customary waters
21    [to it] be furnished. I pray for your health, most dear.
22    [Year] 8 of Antoninus, Hathyr 7.
