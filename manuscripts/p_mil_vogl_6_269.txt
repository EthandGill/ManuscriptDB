[TRANSLATION]
1     To Philotera daughter of Ptolemaios, citizen, without [a guardian],
2     from Auneus son of …ion and Heron
3     son of Amatios, the two, from the village of Tebtynis [of the] Polemon [divi]sion, Persians of the epigone. We wish to lease from
4     you, on mutual surety, for two years from the
5     coming ninth year of Hadrianus Caesar
6     the lord, from those which you also have in lease,
7     thirty-and-a-quarter arourae, or as many as there may
8     be, in which …ph…, the rent of the whole yearly, without seed, of barley
9     one hundred and seventy-two
10    [and] of bundles(?) yearly … one artaba
11    and two cocks worth two
12    drachmas each, without deduction and free of risk;
13    and we shall do the yearly agricultural
14    works of the arourae, all that is fitting, from our
15    own resources at the proper seasons, doing no
16    damage; sowing yearly the
17    half part with whatever we choose, except
18    … , and the other half with barley;
19    and we shall give back the yearly rents in the month Pauni by the six-choinix measure,
20    your own, the public transport-costs being upon
21    us the lessees; and when the period is completed we shall hand back the arourae clean of dog's-tooth grass and all undergrowth; if it seems good to grant (the lease). The arourae are of those
22    around Tebtynis, of the estate of the Alexandrians, formerly
23    of Queen Cleopatra the [sis]ter.
24    Philotera daughter of Ptolemaios, without a guardian,
25    have granted (it) as is set forth. Year …
26    of Imperator Caesar [Trajan]
27    [Ha]drianus Augustus …
