1     [Herakles, about … years old,] … [a scar on the neck on the right. Chair]emon, about 21 years old, a scar on the little fin[ger of the] left [hand]. Pharmouthi 9. Subscribers: Kollouthos, about 28 years old, a scar on the right wrist; Ptolem[aios, about 26 years old,]
2     a scar on the left forearm.
3     In the [nine]teenth year of Tiberius Caesar A[ugustus, of the month Daisios, on the n]inth, Pharmouthi 9, in Tebtynis of the division of Po[le]mon of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowled[ges Herakles son of Chairemo]n also called Mieus, son of Onnophris, a Mace[donian]
4     [of] the cataecic cavalry, about … years old, a scar on the neck on the right, to Chairemon also called
5     Onnoph[r]is, son of Horion, about twenty-one years old, a scar on the little finger of the left hand, that he has ceded to him,
6     to Onnophr[is], so that for him and his descendants and those associated with him for transfer-of-registration, from the present day
7     for all time, from the cataecic allotment belonging to him near Tebtynis, five arouras by the 96-cubit measuring-cord, in two parcels, which are of the neglected (lands) from the unproductive register; of which the neighbours of the first
8     parcel, which is of three arouras in the so-called (lands) of Ammeginoi, are: on the south, the allotment of Herakleides son of Chairemon; on the east, the allotment of the same Herakleides; on the north, waste-land; on the west, the allotment of Kronides son of Haryotes; and the neighbours also of the
9     second parcel, which is of the remaining two arouras in the so-called Kanabis, are: on the south, the allotment of the sons of Chairemon;
10    on the north, the allotment of Auneus son of Aphrodisios; on the we[s]t, the allotment of Akousilaos and Seras; on the east, the allotment of Orseus son of Orseus, the fuller; on behalf of which (lands) Herakleios shall also carry out the appropriate [official acts] of the transfer-of-registration to Onnophris
11    and of the cession, through the cataecic accounting-office, and (that) Herakles has received from the buyer the whole established sum, the silver cession-payment as principal, and (that) he, Herakles, and those associated with him shall guarantee
12    to Onnophris and those associated with him the matters concerning this cession with every guarantee, namely from all public debts and from all imposts and from every kind (of charge) from the times above
13    up to the past eighteenth year and the eighteenth year itself of Tiberius
14    Caesar Augustus, and from private (claims) and every encroachment for ever, for all time.
15    Subscribers: of the acknowledging party, Kollouthos son of Mieus, about twenty-eight years old, a scar on the right wrist; and of the other, Ptolemaios the younger, son of Akousilaos, about 26 years old, a scar on the right forearm.
16    Herakles son of Chairemon also called Mieus, son of Onnophris, a Macedonian of the cataecic cavalry, I acknowledge
17    that I have ceded to Chairemon also called Onnophris, son of Horion, so that for him, Chairemon, and his offspring
18    and those associated with him, for transfer-of-registration, from the present day for all time,
19    from the cataecic allotment belonging to me near Tebtynis, five arouras by the 96-cubit measuring-cord, in two parcels, which have been purchased from the unproductive register; of which the neighbours of the first parcel,
20    which is of three arouras in the (lands) of Ammeginoi, are: on the south, the allotment of Herakleides son of Chairemon; on the eas[t],
21    the allotment of the same Herakleides; on the north, waste-land; on the west, the allotment of Kronides son of Haryotes; and the neighbours of the second
22    parcel, which is of the remaining two arouras in the so-called Kanabis, are: on the south, the allotment of the sons of Chairemon,
23    on the north, the allotment of Auneus son of Aphrodisios; on the west, the allotment of Akousilaos and Seras; on the east, the allotment of Orseus
24    the fuller; on behalf of which (lands) I shall also carry out the appropriate (acts) of the transfer-of-registration to Chairemon
25    [a]nd of the cession, through the cataecic accounting-office; and I have received from him, Chairemon, the established silver capital, and I shall guarantee with every guarantee, from all public (charges) from the
26    [above tim]es up to the past eighteenth year and the eighteenth
