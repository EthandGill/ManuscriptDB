[TRANSLATION]
1     Since according to the order of the most glorious emir [it seemed] …
2     to demand the levy of Hermopolis …
3     and I realized lest they perhaps be afraid …
4     and flee, behold, I made edicts and [sent] …
5     to you; send to all the villages of your jurisdiction …
6     and to the settlements so that they find …
7     whether a merchant of the Serapeum or a pagan …
8     going up or going down, let them seize …
9     and bring [them] to me. Thus make such a watch …
10    from the river as far as the mountain and in the …
11    ferries so that no one pass by your jurisdiction …
12    and be at risk. Behold, I have written to you beforehand …
13    [this] was released on Pharmouthi, [day], at the hour …
14    [let it be delivered to] [Seno]uthios, notary, by Athanasius the illustrious …
