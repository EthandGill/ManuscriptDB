[TRANSLATION]
1     [Has given] Kyros, the most admirable administrator of the house of Theon, to Antiochos
2     [and] Philippus, with god, [who are] tax collectors, for customary assessments
3     [of] the present third distribution of gold, nomismata seven less
4     keratia seventeen on the private scale, there result nomismata 7 less keratia 17 on the private [scale]. In Pharmouthi 15
5     indiction third.
6     Receipt of the customary [fees] of the tax collector for customary [fees] of indiction 3, of gold, nomismata 7 less 17½ on the private scale
