[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: From Theano, daughter of Petesouchos, from the village of Karanis, with her guardian K[a]mbukis, son of Aunes. According to the orders of the Prefect Annius Syr[i]acus, vir clarissimus, I register the 1/3, 1/6 aroura of a katoikic allotment belonging (to me) near the village of Karanis, but registered in the name of Zoidous, daughter of Petesuchos, as being uninundated for the present year 3 of Antoninus and Verus, the lords Augusti. Therefore I submit (this registration). I, K[am]bukis, the aforesaid, about 26 years old.
