[TRANSLATION]
1     Lease of a vineyard property of three arourae of the estate πματινπε … made before Abraham the vine-worker
2     In the name of the holy and life-giving and consubstantial trinity
3     of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, written on the [following] fifth [day]
4     of the thirteenth indiction.
5     To Flavius Demetrius the most magnificent count and
6     governing this [territory] of the Hermupolitans, son of the [one]
7     of magnificent memory John, from Aurelius Abraham
8     son of [Kal]linikos from [the] mother Theodotos the vine-worker from
9     the same city: I acknowledge willingly and freely
10    to have leased from you for a three-year period reckoned
11    from the crops of the forthcoming with god fourteenth indiction
12    the three arurae belonging to you in a vineyard more
13    [rather] less [in quantity], together with diverse plants and palms and a cistern
14    intact and with a [threshing]-floor and a cattle-stall and a wooden
15    instrument furnished with all equipment and a wine-press and
16    a sun-drying floor and a tower and entrance and exit and every
17    [right] appertaining [to] the property arranged in your estate called
18    πματινπεηρε of the fields of the village of Thynis and under its
19    protection of the Hermopolite nome, for manual
20    and vine-working my labor and care
21    and cultivation and [reed/grain]-stoking and digging around
22    a fifth [share] each year blamelessly and respectfully
23    on the condition that you the land-owners receive the half-share of all
24    of the wine that will be produced from them and of the fruit of all sorts
25    of plants and palms, and specially before the division
26    from the common jar by reason of first-pressing wine measures
27    one hundred, and I together with the farmer hold the other half
28    share in return for which we perform labors; I shall moreover provide each year
29    by custom a young pig valued at three keratia
30    from thirty-six loaves and of old wine vessels
31    three large and one tray full of various
32    foods having one hundred cheese [wheels] and one hundred pairs of bread
33    and one sextarius of radish-oil and [I] must not be able
34    to withdraw from this vine-working task before
35    the completion of the aforesaid three-year period;
36    for I renounce every legal assistance that aids me
37    concerning this; this present lease is valid and firm
38    and when questioned I acknowledged; Aurelius Abramis
39    son of [Kallini]kos the aforesaid, have leased as [set forth above].
40    Aur[elius A]thanasios son of Petros from Hermopolis, deemed worthy, wrote [this].
