1     … it has already become known … concerning the supervision of the …
2     when you (?) took office from the eighth of the month. And since you remember … xa … all
3     from me on account of Christodoros the surveyor, and request that you demand from him the
4     coin which he is accustomed to give to the pagarchs, and demand also from Mouses, the (man) from among

9     of John … r … os …. It is the expected one to come and to administer the
