[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklepiades [to Spinthere]
2     greetings. … having taken the …
3     the one from Moithymae the
4     tax he says having pledged to him so that [he] might preserve [it] and render [it] to the king under the steward
5     and the guard-commander, to us
6     but give nothing. But it is necessary for him to render for Pachon
7     and Payni in bronze drachmas 80 and Epeiph and Mesore drachmas 80
8     and Thoth and Phaophi drachmas 130,
9     so that the whole is drachmas 370.
10    You will therefore do well having written
11    to Spinth[ere].
