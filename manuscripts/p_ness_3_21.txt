[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of] our most divine and most pious master Flavius Justinianus,
2     [benefactor] and Imperator Augustus, in the 35th year, in the consulship the one after
3     [that] of Flavius Basilius, the most glorious, in the four hundred and fifty-
4     [seventh] year (457) of the era, of the tenth indiction, in the month Panemos,
5     … in the village of Nessana, in the territory of the city of Elousa.
6     [With all] good fortune, Flavius Sergios, son of Elias son of Themoobd, made a division
7     [a soldier of] the camp here, between Elias and Alolkaios and Zacharias
8     [and St]ephanos, his own children — Elias being born
9     [from the moth]er Maria daughter of Themoobd, his blessed former
10    [wife, and Alo]lkaios and Zacharias and Stephanos being born
11    [from the] mother Moleche daughter of Abraham, his most noble second
12    [wife], Sergios himself making the agreements on behalf of Alolkaios
13    [and Zacharias] and Stephanos, his younger children,
14    [at his own] risk, they being now under age, concerning the things
15    [being divided], the aforesaid devoted Sergios making an eternal division between his said children, from a fourth part of the buildings and the sown fields
16    [and] … of household goods and chattels belonging to him and pertaining to him from his own substance. So a casting of lots [and]
17    a throwing of dice having taken place between them in the presence of [their friends]
18    [and] relatives present with them, and having bound one another with oaths [into the holy] Trinity and the imperial safety to abide [by this division] and to disturb nothing of the things contained in it,
19    [neither in] a court nor outside it; and if any of them should attempt
20    [to trans]gress, the transgressing party shall give to the abiding party [as a matter]
21    [of penalty] and transgression, by agreement and formal question,
22    [six gold] solidi, 6 gold pieces, besides that even after the payment of the penalty
23    this division [shall be] valid and secure and strong and lawful and …
24    … this division with whatever force
25    [whole]. There fell then to Elias, the most admirable, for his own share,
26    [in place of] the fourth part falling to him, from the [price]
27    [of the dwellings] seven solidi less seven carats by the [standard]
28    [of Gaza], gold solidus 7 less 7 carats. Further there fell to him, Elias, for his own [share]
29    [a fourth] part also of the sown fields, the fitting [portion] lying in Abiathalba, the one below the [heirs]
30    … of Alpheios the blessed, with all its right, of which the neighbours on the east are Amrou … elou the devoted, on the [west]
31    … of Stephanos son of Zonainos son of Mal…, on the south of the [heirs]
32    … of Alpheios the blessed, [on the north] … [Further]
33    [there fe]ll to him, Elias …
