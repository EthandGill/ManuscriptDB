[TRANSLATION]
1     To those ruling in year 33
2     from Ptolemaia. Having given
3     to me Tetwytis cap-bearer
4     of the from the harbor wool [measure] 1
5     so as to spin and from the
6     agreed-upon wage
7     having collected the half, now
8     and [she] not enduring to
9     return the work, but
10    having wandered off, I request
11    having recalled her
12    to compel [her] having collected
13    the remainder of the wage to return to me so that I might thus obtain
14    justice.
15    [Farewell.]
