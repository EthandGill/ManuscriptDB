r.6    Ἑρμόδωρος συνέσχον.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermodorus son of Soter and Aelius Didymus
2     Tax collectors of silver of Elephantine through me on behalf of Didymus.
3     Biengchis, son of Biengchis, mother Thinbiengchis, wrote for
4     Distributions of river-guard [revenues], station [expenses], watchtowers
5     And other expenses of the 19th year, drachmas ten, four-obol [piece], total: drachmas ten, four-obol [piece]. Of the 19th year, Pauni 16.
6     Hermodorus, I received [in full].
