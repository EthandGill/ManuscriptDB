[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Son(?) and
2     of the ho]ly [Spirit]. In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Phocas the eternal Augustus
3     [and Emperor, in the year] … on the nineteenth (day), of the eighth indiction,
4     [to so-and-so residing in this city of the Her]mopolites, son of Phoibammon of illustrious memory, hailing from the
5     … of the brothers, sons of Origenes, cultivators from the hamlet of Monai, under our own guardianship,
6     … and of our own free choice, to have leased from your Splendor for a three-year period, reckoned
7     from … indiction, and it, the (land) sold to you by the most illustrious comes Isidoros,
8     … of three …, whose neighbors are: on the [south] the plots of the most magnificent comes Menas; on the north a public road;
9     … [Ia]cob son of Iannakios [from] Antinoou; on the west a vineyard belonging to Iakybios the comes;
10    … and by every right, under the guardianship of those from the village of Tertonkano
11    [of the Hermopolite nome], (for) a deposit [of the produce] which we shall choose, [as rent] of the sown land per year
12    … to bring to you … n
13    … in the month Ep[ei]ph each year without pretext,
14    … [of] the rent … l … on … t … dimon … etero
15    … to receive half the part of everything that shall accrue,
16    … [to re]ceive the other half in return for the labors that we perform
17    … the custom. The lease is valid and secure, and, being formally questioned (we have agreed).
18    …os Leontios son of Ioannes, from Hermopolis, having been requested, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
19    … [having heard] from those making (the agreement). Christodoros son of Abous.
20    … [A]urelius Victor from Hermopolis.
