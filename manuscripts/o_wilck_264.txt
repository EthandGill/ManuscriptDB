1   Stephanos and his associates, overseers
2   of the sacred gate of Syene, through Serenos assistant. Paid:
3   Paou( ) son of Patsibtis, mother Taleilaueis,
4   for [poll]-tax of year 11, seventeen drachmas one obol — 17 dr. 1 ob.
5   Year 11 of Aureli[us Antonin]us
6   Caesar the lord, Mesore 19.
