[TRANSLATION]
1     Valerius Heron also called Sarapion,
2     logistes of the Oxyrhynchite,
3     to Aurelius Seuthes also called Horion,
4     who served as logistes of the same city,
5     his dearest, greeting.
6     My lord the most eminent prefect
7     of Egypt, Aelius Hyginus, to
8     ate … enenka (?)
9     concerning the (sums) [ow]ed to the public account of the city by way of moneys
10    by Achilleus and Bassos and Arsinoe their sister and the accruing additional charges, having explained the misfortune that befell them, ordered those who lent at their own
11    valuation, whomsoever I should find liable on this account,
12    to be pursued for the repayment, in order that nothing be lost to the city. So that you may know and
13    may conform to the orders,
14    I write to you, dearest, the (documents) by his Greatness
15    … having been appended. I pray for your health.
16    Copy:
17    Aelius Hyginus, to the logistes of the Oxyrhynchite, greeting.
18    It is not by disregard of letters that one should overlook
19    what is advantageous to the city; nor, if it has happened, as you write, that those who borrowed
20    the civic moneys have made room for the most sacred treasury, does this suffice for
21    your security, since it is clear that the
22    logistes who made the loan, or the magistrates,
23    are liable for the repayment of the moneys, who indeed, having made the lending-out of the moneys at their own valuation,
24    ought to have foreseen the outcome. Therefore, following the form given, see to it that you pursue all
25    those whom you should find liable on this
26    account; and if any wish to make use of a counter-plea, let them
27    be sent to the office.
28    I pray for your health.
