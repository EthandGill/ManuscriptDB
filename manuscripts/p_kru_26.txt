[TRANSLATION]
1     [the] men of this [same fortress of Djeme] …
2     Antonios, those who came against us on behalf of our blessed
3     fathers … after the partition, then, of the legal claim which we made with one another, we cast lots
4     up; the lot came up upon you. You,
5     Tagape, the daughter of Eutychios, the dining-hall (symposion) which is in the granary came to your share. I, for my part, Zenon,
6     I took the dining-hall (symposion) which is at the threshing-floor; you gave two more
7     tremisses to us upon the dining-hall which is at the threshing-floor. I,
8     Zenon, the one who first wrote, and I acknowledge
9     that I have nothing reasonable with you
10    concerning the dining-hall which is in the granary, neither I,
11    nor a brother, nor a sister, nor son of one, nor any
12    person who shall act on my behalf, (saying) that I have received
13    and have been paid into my hand for the dining-hall
14    which is at the granary. At whatever time anyone shall come forth
15    against you and sue me — at the hand of any authority,
16    exalted or in glory — first he shall be a stranger
17    to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
18    and he shall pay ten holokottinos to the authority which exists
19    now; and afterwards he shall come in and confirm
20    this sale. For its security, then, I have established it,
21    and I agree to it.
22    I, Papas, son of the blessed Anastasi …
23    give witness. I, Enoch, son of Ello, have written on his behalf, that
24    I (write) and give witness. I, Kostantinos,
25    son of the blessed Isak, give witness. I, Enoch,
26    have written on his behalf, that he does not know how to write.
27    Iohannes asked me; I have written on his behalf, according to his request.
28    Through me, Aristophanes son of Iohannes, it was drawn up.
