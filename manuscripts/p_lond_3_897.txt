[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the poll-tax; for at that time I was reduced (in status) by the previous village-scribe. He indeed lied at that time, but now you
2     write the truth; and how can I come to the Arsinoite (nome),
3     especially with the season of the grain-collection now upon (us),
4     so that, having come, immediately again harassment should befall me; but if
5     I am able to take shelter or to come under some protection, this
6     write to me. You swore through your letter that, you having come,
7     no harassment shall be yours, and most gladly I will leave all (else)
8     so that I may not abandon the friendship towards you. But if you have not
9     found some way, write to me, so that I may not again sail up (on) this same journey; for I have decided, by the
10    gods, to stay in Alexandria. For I believe that
11    I shall be able (to manage); however I do not wish (it), but (only) under necessity.
12    But concerning all matters, write me an answer at once; for I do not
13    sail up to the Arsinoite (nome) unless I receive a letter from you.
14    And if you send me letters, you will send them to the sweet-shop of Theon at the bath of Charidemos, and in the
15    workshop he (the bearer) will find Deios the (son) of Syros, and he himself will
16    deliver (it) to me, or (send it) by way of Herakleidion the (son) of Abas. And about other
17    necessary pending matters I wished to write to you, by the gods.
18    But I did not write through the letter, knowing within myself that you have already
19    [t]aken (the matter) in hand in the affair. And I beg you not to
20    deceive me through the letter, and, having come, immediately
21    … so that …
22    I may sail down again, but to me [at once]
23    write the truth. For I know within myself [that]
24    you care much about me, and you will care
25    for me as for your own child.
26    Farewell.
27    Greet little Aphrodous and
28    all those in the house. Farewell.
29    Year 3 of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus
30    Germanicus, Pharmouthi 3.
