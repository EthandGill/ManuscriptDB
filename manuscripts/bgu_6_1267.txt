[TRANSLATION]
1     Five arouras …
2     in [Ta]kona … [and the rents]
3     contracted [let Poseidonios and Karneades repay]
4     to Neoptolem[os in the month of] … [of the] twentieth year, grain [pure and unadulterated, that which is produced]
5     on the land, by mea[sure, by the chous-measure of Neoptolemos, by a just measurement],
6     and let them carry off the [rents to the property of Neoptolemos]
7     at their own expense, and let them give also the fourth part of the chaff, and this too [let them carry off to the] property of Neoptolemos at their own expense … and … [of] Neoptolemos. And if they do not repay the grain or the chaff according to the
8     written terms, let them pay as the value of each artaba
9     of the wheat four drachmas, and of the chaff ten [dra]chmas, and let the right of execution belong to Neoptolemos against [Po]seidonios and Karneades, exacting according to the edict. And let him be master of the crops until he recovers the contracted rent. And let Neoptolemos guarantee
10    to Poseidonios and Karneades the land and the produced
11    crops according to what he has leased; and if he does not guarantee, let him pay as penalty five hundred drachmas of silver, unless
12    some royal [impediment] arises. And let this contract
13    [be valid wherever it is produced. W]itnesses.
14    Farewell.
