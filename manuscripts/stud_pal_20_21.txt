1     To Aurelia [S]yra also called Eisarion surnamed Severa
2     through [Aur]elius Apion councilor of the city of the Arsinoites
3     from Marcus Aurel[ius Se]renus broker from the ward
4     of Apol[lonius] Hierar[cus; I wish to] lease from you the [property] belonging to you near the village K[erke]souch
5     of the Heracleides district at a place called Pitan a date palm grove with underlying sowing in which are sown date palms [of] six arouras for one year, [with] the date [palms] from those overlying the current [third]
6     [and] twenty-third year falling into the next year
7     but the [things] sown [from] the next fourth [and] twenty-third
8     year [as] yield [of the] sown wheat in artabas
9     ten … , but as tax/rent of the date palms in silver
10    sixty drachmas and [as] exceptional [tribute] dried date-palm wood single-trunk [bundles] of three artabas and [of] wood … twenty-two [uncounted and risk-free; and I am to perform all the] work [whatever] is proper from my own [means]
11    [at the] fitting [times, doing] no [damage]: the
12    tax I will pay in the month of Hathor similarly also the exceptional [tribute] in the property; but the yield in the month of Pauni
13    [on the] threshing floor measured by the running [measure] four-choinix of the
14    public [standards] all simply being towards
15    you Aurelia [Sy]ra also called Eisarion surnamed Severa; and after the [duration] I will hand over the sown [produce] from joint-harvest,
16    but the date palms covered with [fruit]
17    just as I received [them]. Aurelius Serenus
18    I have leased as afore[said].
19    year 23 of Marcus Aurelius Severus [A]ntoninus
20    Parth[ian] Great[est] Brit[tanic] Greatest
21    German[ic] Greatest Pious Augustus Pauni
