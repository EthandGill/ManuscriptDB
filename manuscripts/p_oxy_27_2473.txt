[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Leonides, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite,
2     from the Aurelii Ptollion son of Amois son of
3     Sarapion, his mother being Didyme, and Apion
4     also called Ammonios, son of Apion surnamed
5     Pauseirion, his mother being Ouariane, both from the city of Oxyrhynchus, kosmetai,
6     councillors of the same city. In accordance with an agreement completed through the record office in the past year 8, in the month Hathyr, we bought in equal share from Aurelius Theon,
7     gymnasiarch, councillor of the same
8     city, son of Aristion son of Teiron, former exegete of the same city, his mother being
9     Sarapous also called Demetria, from
10    the same city, the (lands) that belonged to him
11    about Antipera Pela, of which from the allotment of Rhodon and Agasikrates, grain-bearing,
12    of single-artaba (rate) eleven and a half and an eighth arouras, and of double-artaba (rate) two and a half, a quarter, an eighth, a thirty-second arouras,
13    the whole being fourteen and a half and a thirty-second arouras,
14    under one view, having paid him the
15    price of these in full upon the
16    rights and all the conditions declared
17    through the sale, as the agreement
18    contains; the certification of which, referred to
19    the keepers of the property-records, having affixed below to these documents, we present, asking you to write
20    to the same keepers of the property-records
21    to make the requisite registration. Year 9
22    of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Severus
23    Alexander Pius Felix Augustus,
24    Thoth 14. We, the Aurelii Ptollion and Ap[ion]
25    also called Ammonios, both kosmetai, th[rough]
26    Apion also called Ammonios, have presented (this).
