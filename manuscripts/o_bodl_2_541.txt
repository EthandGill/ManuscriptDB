[TRANSLATION]
1     Ailourios and Horos, collectors of money-taxes
2     of Charakos to Psenchos Amenothus Harondotos. We received on account of poll-tax and bath-tax
3     of the 2nd year dirty drachmas eight, [they] total drachmas 8
4     Of the year 2 of Antoninus the Lord
5     Phamenoth 3. Horos [I] have signed-off drachmas 8
6     Ailourios through Kaleous [I] have signed-off drachmas 8
