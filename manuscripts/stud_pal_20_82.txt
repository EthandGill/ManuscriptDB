1     Heronikos to Maskulenos
2     [his] son, greetings.
3     You rather than I from the
4     city having come, know the
5     trouble of the porter-workers, and would that from yesterday
6     you had driven them into the city.
7     As for what you wrote to me, from here it will be sent
8     to the city. [and] not even one hour
9     one therefore [should you] wait
10    knowing the necessity the
11    concerning them, especially [while] the lord
12    my [official] [is] expected to be present
13    being about to [visit] the city.
14    [to be] well I pray you
15    for many years.
