[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eighth year of Imperator Caesar Tra[ja]n
2     Augustus, in the month Artemisios, on the twenty-first day, = Phamenoth the twenty-first, in Soknopaiou Nesos of the
3     Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome.
4     Onnophris son of Harpaesis, a Persian of the epigone, about twenty-five years old, with a scar on the left eyebrow, acknowledges
5     to Horos the younger, son of Tesenouphis, about twenty-
6     eight years old, with a scar on the forehead on the left, that he,
7     the acknowledging party, has from him, in stamped silver coinage, as principal, from hand to hand out of the house, two hundred [drach]mas
8     as a deposit, free of all risk
9     and not subject to any deduction,
10    which the acknowledging party shall [also re]pay [to] Horos
11    whenever Horos may choose, without any postponement or pretext. And if he does not repay,
12    the acknowledging party shall forfeit to Horos the deposit
13    doubled, according to the law of deposits,
14    both from the acknowledging party and from whatever (assets) he may choose and from all his belongings,
15    [the right of exaction] belonging to Horos as if by legal judgment.
16    [The subscribers]: for the acknowledging party, Soterichos
17    … about thirty years old, with a scar on the forehead …; and for the other, Tr[y]phon son of Chairemon
