1     Nikias, overseer
2     of the new vineyard-land on the Island,
3     (to) Psenthotes (son of) Psansnos,
4     through Panameus
5     (son of) Dioskourides. I have
6     from you, for the price of wine of the produce of
7     year 14, impure four drachmas,
8     four obols, [total 4 dr. 4 obols. Year 15(?)]
9     of Hadrian Caesar [the lord],
