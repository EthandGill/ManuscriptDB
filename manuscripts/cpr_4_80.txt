[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Apa Kyri, son of the blessed Theodore, the man of Shmoun (Hermopolis) the city, with my mother, write
2     to Psah son of Apollo, that: I rejoice, I acknowledge, I owe you, purely and absolutely, one trimesion of gold, reckoned from today, which is the 10th of Parmehotp, the month of this year, the fourteenth, until the 22nd of the month of this year, the fifteenth, of the indiction; this, for which I asked you, you gave to me out of my need and my necessity, and I have laid down certain articles which are mine with you until I shall repay it to you, which are these:
3     a strange (foreign) curtain, and a second-quality cloak, and a small mat, and a … and a measure of …; these I am ready to repay to you by the 22nd
4     of the month, the principal of the trimesion to you until the discharge of the deadline, which I wrote first.
5     You are lord of these articles, to give them for your trimesion, and I cannot bring suit against you concerning them
6     forever. If I wish to do this, I am to give ten holokottinoi, without judgment, without law, without any
7     dispute. I swear by God the Almighty and by the welfare of those who rule over us
8     according to the season, that I will keep it for you according to her ability. I, Apa Kyri, the one who wrote first, consent.
9     I, John, the least presbyter, am witness to this security, just as
10    it is written. I, the god(?), the least of presbyters, the man of the place of Nonna, am witness
11    to this security, which I heard from the one who drew it up.
