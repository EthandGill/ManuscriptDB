[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dionysios of [Deios] having been strategos of the city
2     to the son, neocoros of the great Sarapis, of those in the Museum
3     being fed with tax-exemption, priest, chief-judge, and also for the superintendence of the money-dealers and the other courts
4     from [Petronia] Sarapiad [with the] authority of the brother Gaius [Petronius] Marcellus and from Gaius Julius Apollinarius, soldier of the first cohort of the Apanenoi [centurion]ship of [Julian]. (he) agrees
5     Gaius Julius [Apollinarios] [to have received] from
6     Petronia [Sarapiad] a [harmless] deposit
7     of all [risk] [female] garments appraised at silver drachmae three-hundred and golden ornaments in forms together thirty quarters
8     two which he will guard by himself and restore
9     to her whenever she demands [them] without delay or (he) will pay according to the law of deposits, having accrued to Petronia Sarapiad the enforcement from the
10    of Gaius Julius Apollinarius existing all things just-[as] from judgment. We request. Of the eighth year of Emperor
11    Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrian [Anto]ninus Sebastus
12    Pious, Phaophi [6]
