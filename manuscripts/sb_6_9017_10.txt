1     Rufus to Silvanus greetings.
2     I sent you the oil. If
3     you have need of anything, send [word]
4     [to] me; if you wish write a letter to Coptos,
5     since there is the one traveling. Greet the
6     friends all. Say
7     to those around Sartorios if
8     you [all] have need of anything,
9     write.
