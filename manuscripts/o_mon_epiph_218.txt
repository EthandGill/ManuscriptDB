1     First now of our word
2     (The) least, hear that your understanding (that) you shall heed, Apa
3     Victor and Apa Jeremiah with us, concerning the brother Andrew, into God, he set him to the place which he took him
4     Do the spirit, therefore, you shall come
5     Upon you, stand your community
6     Do the spirit, therefore, from your deliberation … in one zeal, a word in the Lord
7     Through the sisters
