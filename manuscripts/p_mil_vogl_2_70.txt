[TRANSLATION]
1     Heraclides to Turbo[n] greeting.
2     If … [falls] before you,
3     receive [the] wheat from him and the
4     swine and the ploughs and the yoke-oxen and all [such] as he has tools;
5     having taken the wheat from him,
6     take [charge of] that young man,
7     and give him from the wheat artabas four
8     … [towards?] of Marou to Tali to Titon
9     so that he may work the reed-field of Chr[ys]ermes, drachmas 120. Go then there so that
10    you may measure the workers. Make
11    the animals work on the …
12    for three days.
13    Farewell.
