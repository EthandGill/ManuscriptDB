[TRANSLATION]
1     Aristeas and Pausanias to Demeas, greetings. For the present it was not
2     possible to travel along with the boy carrying the letters, because of my
3     being troubled and Pausanias being occupied with necessary business, [b]ut with Mesore
4     beginning we shall attempt to come. And toward you we shall have no disagreement.
5     We have written to you and to Theocles, son of Theodore, to give fifty artabas of wheat. When you measure, then take a receipt from him [of the] measurement.
6     Farewell.
7     To Demeas.
8     Aristeas.
