[TRANSLATION]
1   Copy. To Theon, chief-justice (archidikastes) and in charge of the superintendence of the chrematistai and of the
2   other tribunals,
3   from Ammonarion daughter of Ammonios son of Dionysios, as she is registered in Ptolemais Hermiou,
4   a citizen, and from her
5   daughter Ophelous daughter of Herakles, of those from Oxyrhynchos city, with the guardian of the
6   two women,
7   the full brother by the same mother of [Ammon]arion, Besarion son of Heras, as he is registered in the same Ptolemais,
8   a registered citizen,
9   [and from Ga]ius Cl[audi]us(?) Antiphanes son of Ammonios, of those [from] the same Oxyrhynchos city. We agree
10   [with one anoth]er on these terms, that Ammonarion and Ophelous, having become satisfied
11   and having received in full
12   [from Ant]iphanes by hand, from the house, the capital sum which they were also persuaded (to accept), Ammonarion on the one hand
13   in return for what she contri-
14   [buted t]o the brother of Antiphanes's father, who became husband of herself and father of Ophelous,
15   and (now) deceased, Herakles son of Antiphanes, of those from the same Oxyrhynchos city, as a dowry
16   in value, [of sil]ver eight hundred drachmas, according to the agreement completed through the daybook (ephemeris)
17   in the times before, and Ophelous on the other hand herself also cedes to Antiphanes the share belonging to her
18   of all the things left behind by her deceased father Herakles, and that the
19   declared agreement of the marriage is void, and that no right of recourse is left to Ammonarion or to Ophelous
20   nor to anyone else
21   on their behalf against Antiphanes, nor upon the things left behind by Herakles,
22   to Ammonarion neither concerning the discharged dowry, to Ophelous neither concerning the
23   [ce]ded (shares) as aforesaid, and to both concerning absolutely nothing else, written
24   or unwritten matter, from the times above up to the present day, or that the
25   future right of recourse shall be void and inadmissible. And in the aforesaid there is no registration of persons (somatismos);
26   we request as is fitting. Year 4 of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, in the month Neroneios
27   Sebastos, the 3rd. Copy. Apollonios. It has been registered. (ms)
28   (hand 2) Without prejudice to Antiphanes in the [...] of the [share of the courtyard which he bought]
29   from him, in accordance with the [conveyance made] to him.
