[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eleventh year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus emperor, Mecheir
2     on the sixth (day), which (is) sacred, in Euergetis which (is) above Memphis, under agoranomos Harbes, Pasion Harthonios purchased
3     (one) from Oxyrhynchus city of the Thebaid, about 52 years old, medium complexion sweet-looking, long-faced, with a scar on the left wrist, from
4     Metrophanes son of Heras, from the village Taamechis of Koites which (is) above Memphis
5     (of the) Herakleopolite (district), about 58 years old, medium complexion sweet-looking, long-faced, with a scar on the left forearm, in (the) street Euormon, whom Metrophanes said to belong to him, (a) household slave whose name (is) Demetr[ou], about 25 years old, medium complexion
