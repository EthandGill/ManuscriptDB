[TRANSLATION]
1     [he] has measured into the treasury of the metropolis
2     and below [the] toparchia, from [the] produce of [the] 18th year
3     of Antoninus the lord
4     on behalf of the metropolis, Psenosyris son of Senpsenos, Peb, priest, [of] grain, artabas 93, 8.
5     Pachumis son [of the same], likewise, [of] grain, artabas 93 [8]
6     of the 18th year, Pauni 29.
