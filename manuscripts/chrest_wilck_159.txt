[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermias to Demetrios, greeting. A copy of the financial authorization from Hermonax, one of those ranking equally
2     with the Kinsmen and a sub-administrator, is appended below. Following therefore what is indicated through it,
3     authorize payment, conforming to what is signified through it,
4     pay out from the bank at Pathyris, with Phibis the royal scribe countersigning, to those in charge of the aforesaid
5     vessels, of bronze thirty-four talents each, making in total … 34 talents, and make a receipt and
6     a counter-receipt as is fitting.
7     Farewell. Year 9, Pachon 16.
8     Hermonax to Hermias, greeting. A copy of the financial authorization from Ptolemaios the Kinsman and
9     administrator is appended below. Following therefore
10    what is indicated through it, authorize payment out of the Latopolite nome
11    in accordance with what has been arranged, with Phibis the royal
12    scribe also countersigning, to those in charge of the two vessels of Pamphilos, each per month 8 talents 3,000,
13    of wheat 25 artabas, the amount due for 4 months being 34 talents, of wheat reckoned 100 artabas, making in total … 34 talents, of wheat reckoned
14    100 artabas, and
15    make a receipt and a counter-receipt as is fitting.
16    Year 9, Pharmouthi 5.
17    Ptolemaios to Hermonax, greeting. To those in charge of the two vessels sailing together
18    with Pamphilos, the man appointed by us over the speeding-on of the wheat, authorize payment per month, for as long a
19    time as he is engaged in the aforesaid task, to each per month 8 talents 3,000, of wheat 25 artabas,
20    making in total … 8 talents 3,000, of wheat 25.
21    Year 9, Choiak 24.
22    Authorize payment of bronze thirty-four talents, making 34 talents. Year 9, Pachon 16
23    Phibis. Authorize payment of bronze thirty-four talents, making 34 talents.
24    Year 9, Pachon 16.
25    To Demetrios.
