[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord Sarapion
2     Koprias to fare well
3     As you instructed me, the care proper to you has been given concerning the wines.
4     To be unconcerned then about this
5     matter you ought. The brother
6     Apollinarius I entrust to you
7     through your [goodness], whom not even you yourself
8     are unaware of. You will therefore devote yourself
9     to whatever he might need of you. To him you will do well
10    by converting to my necessary need of new silver
11    [four] [talents]. But do not waylay your Koprias. And you yourself know that I am not insensible.
12    I [pray] [you] [to fare well].
13    … Koprias arch[…]
