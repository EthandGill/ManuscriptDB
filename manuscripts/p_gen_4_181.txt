[TRANSLATION]
1     … [of the] two southern dining-rooms built by the aforesaid Pseeios [the presbyter at his own expense,]
2     and indeed it pleased them, on a compromise with a penalty, to be content and to abide [by the judgment given]
3     to them by our humility. And we the aforesaid [common arbitrators] chosen … of the two aforesaid dining-rooms built by [the aforesaid Pseeios the presbyter] at his own
4     expense, and according to what seemed just to us, which you stated, we agreed [that, the two] dining-rooms having been [jointly] valued
5     at an expenditure of nine nomismata, so that therefore the aforesaid Apollonios [the deacon makes himself] liable
6     to pay back to the aforementioned Pseeios the presbyter, toward the completion [and] fulfillment [of the] … from the
7     sums expended by the aforesaid Pseeios the presbyter for the building of the [aforesaid] two dining-rooms,
8     that is, four and a half gold nomismata, 4½ gold nomismata, plain, [as is] aforesaid, apart also from [his share] of the expenditure of the stable which is in front of their aforesaid house, but … renounces
9     the aforementioned Apollonios the deacon entering into co-residence from [the aforesaid] two [dining]-rooms until he pays back to the aforesaid Pseeios the presbyter the aforesaid [four and a] half nomismata,
10    apart also from his share of the expenditure from the aforesaid stable as has been said. I, Pseeios, being present, have submitted this.
11    I, Apollonios, being present, have submitted this. I, John, notary, [witness to the settlement.]
12    Through me, Apa Jeremias.
