[TRANSLATION]
1     Just as also at another time I appealed to the God-protecting
2     you by means of my pitiful letters on behalf of the
3     [letter-bearer] reader and your mercy took hold of
4     him; and days ago my brother was seized
5     his and the donkey and they were sent to Babylon
6     and I was not willing to trouble you and yesterday again
7     his other brother was seized and I think
8     master, as you teach, they ought not to have sent the
9     two together and to have left his beasts. I beseech
10    therefore you God-protecting ones to order this one to be released
11    until the other comes; he is ready to come down
12    and to make his exchange. The feet of the
13    God-protecting ones of you I kiss until sight
14    … all things worthy of honor … you … to the notary and chartulary
15    George
