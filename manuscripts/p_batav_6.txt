[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 8, Mecheir 2. There sold
2     Harkonnesis son of Phigeris a fourth
3     share of 2 plots of dry land
4     in the Latopolite (nome). There bought
5     Naomsesis son of Spemminis,
6     for 18 (artabas) of wheat.
7     In the reign of Queen [Kleopatra] an[d] of King Ptolemaios, the Gods Philometores Soteres,
8     year 8, in the priesthood of the king Ptolemaios, the god Philometor Soter, of Alexander
9     and of the Soter Gods and of the Sibling Gods and of the Benefactor Gods and of the Father-loving Gods
10    and of the Manifest Gods and of the god Eupator and of the god [Phi]lometor and of the god Philopator
11    Neos and of the god Euergetes and of the Gods Philometores Soteres, of the sacred colt of Isis the great
12    mother of the gods, of the prize-bearer of Berenike Euergetis, of the basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphos,
13    of the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator, those being in Alexandria;
14    and in Ptolemais of the Thebaid, in the priesthood of the priests and [pr]iestesses an[d] basket-bearer being
15    and existing; in the month Phaophi 22, in Pathyris before Sosos the agoranomos.
16    There sold Harkonnesis son of Phigeris, a Persian of the mercenary cavalry,
17    about 50 years old, tall, dark-skinned, long-faced, straight-nosed,
18    from the dry grain-bearing land belonging to him
19    in the lower toparchy of the Latopolite (nome), of the fourth
20    share, two plots, one of 8 arouras, neighbours: on the south
21    the land of Pasemis, on the north the hollow of Tpachis, on the east the land of the
22    ibis-keepers, on the west the land of Pasemis, with a hollow between; of another [of] two arouras [neighbours]:
23    on the south a dyke, on the north the land of Patsantis, on the east the branch of the
24    dyke, on the west a hollow; or whoever the neighbours may be on all sides.
25    There bought Naomsesis son of Spemminis, a Persian woman, [for] 13½ artabas of wheat.
26    Guarantor: Harkonnesis the seller, whom accepted
27    Naomsesis the buyer.
28    Ammonios, the (representative) from Sosos, have transacted (the business).
29    Year 8, Phaophi 22. There has been paid to the bank a[t Pa]thyris, over which is Patseous,
30    a tenth deposit of the sales-tax: Naomsesis son of Spemminis, of the purchase of a fourth
31    share of grain-bearing land in the lower toparchy of the Latopolite (nome), 2 plots,
32    one of 8 arouras, the other [of] 2 arouras, which he bought from Harkonnesis
33    son of Phigeris, for 13½ artabas of wheat, total 2 talents 4,200 drachmas of copper, the tax in copper 1,620 drachmas,
34    the exchange-fee of it, 1,950 drachmas of copper.
35    Patseous the banker.
