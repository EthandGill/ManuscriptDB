1     Report
2     To Aurelius Ammonius, the most distinguished prefect [of Egypt] …
3     Valerius Sarapdorus, agent. …
4     Diogenes from the Oxyrhynchite [city] having approached your Greatness …
5     [he having] been declared and confirmed [that] some plot of land [was] ceded …
6     belonging to the Antinoeans, being near the settlement of Sarapion [of Chairémon] …
7     of the Oxyrhynchite under various farmers being …
8     [it] being demanded from him of the land which he said [was] ceded …
9     my mediocrity according to the most just [nature] of the request …
10    having come to me then upon the places and the [documents] given by [Diogenes to your Greatness]
11    of documents presented to me I summoned …
12    of the settlement of Sarapion of Chairémon [submitted to] the petition that took place before your Greatness …
13    to be known through what you ordered from the petition of Diogenes …
14    his request [to] provide care for me; but they asserted … [to have] met with your, my lord, court …
15    report of an agent to the governor
