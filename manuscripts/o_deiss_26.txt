r.2    Πικῶτι νεωτέρῳ Ἀπολλοδώρου.
r.3    ἔσχον ὑπὲρ λαογραφίας [καὶ βαλανευτικοῦ] Ἀγορᾶς βορρᾶ β ἔτους ῥυπαρὰς δραχμὰς

[TRANSLATION]
1     Chesphmo[i]s, tax-collector of the silver-tax of the metropolis
2     to Pikotis the younger, [son] of Apollodoros.
3     I have received for the poll-tax [and bathhouse-tax] of the North Agora [month] 2nd year dirty drachmas
4     twelve; totaling dirty drachmas 12. Of the 2nd year
5     of Hadrian the lord
6     Pharmouthi 19. Phthouninis [I] have signed.
