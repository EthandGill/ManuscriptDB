[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemaios
2     to Ptolles, greetings.
3     The letters concerning those against
4     Diphilos
5     I have withheld
6     lest ever you deemed fit,
7     but if still even now you have not deemed fit, having learned precisely where
8     he says he found
9     them, the silver fittings, clearly
10    write; for it is not
11    [proper] to write carelessly
12    concerning such a kind.
13    Farewell.
