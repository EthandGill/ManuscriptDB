[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidoros to Tausenoufis the most honored, very many greetings.
2     Before all things I pray that you are well.
3     and I make [your] obeisance
4     each day before [the]
5     Lord Sarapis. I was very grieved concerning your wife as concerning Germanos my [brother].
6     But take heart and courageously
7     bear; for [death is] of all things. Let [your] children be well.
8     [My] mother greets you
9     and my brothers. You will receive
10    from the one giving you the [epistle] hundred nuts.
11    Reply to me concerning your well-being. I greet your children.
12    I pray that you fare well.
13    [To] Bacchias, to Valeria from Isidoros.
