[TRANSLATION]
1     [Anubian] to Sarapion the
2     [father], greetings.
3     [After] the dismissal of those [having cooled], I [went] to Terenepà and
4     [after] [many] labors I asked Panergy without any [document]
5     eighty drachmas which via Ammonius
6     I sent to you. So as you wrote to me previously, the pool to cool I make;
7     for the field, if it does not find the adequate water, is about to be harmed. You write
8     therefore to me which of-the-two threshing-floor first do-you-wish to-tread in-order-that after the cooling I-tread. You-will-send then the
9     trap and if you are able, a worker
10    one in-order-that more-securely we-tread.
11    The dekania of the oxen drachmas three
12    is apart-from no worker. You-will-send me breads and relish.
13    [Be strong] to me, father.
