[TRANSLATION]
1     John, Theodore, Menas, Narses, Chnoubammon, Horion, Hephaistos the most glorious quaestor, duke and augustalis of the province of the Thebans.
2     The time having come, God willing, in which it is necessary that care be taken of the inescapable grain-shipment of the fortunate eighth
3     … … [in]diction, with all
4     sloth declined, hasten to send this off to the great city of the Alexandrians
5     [wit]h all fear (i.e. diligence), loading onto small boats the required measure straightway
6     [and] at once according to the appended notice, at the risk of the office (taxis); for it is on account of this reason
7     that it has been sent off from the office. Farewell.
8     Farewell.
9     (column-mark) chi
10    It is necessary for you, in imitation of the just-past
11    seventh indiction, the appended
12    measure of grain on account of the embolē-shipment of the eighth indiction
13    to bring in and load onto ships, and
14    the president and the supervisor (epimeletes)
15    and the sailors together also with the ships
16    laden with the produce
17    to send into the office for the
18    customary transaction of the records,
19    so that after this there may be sent
20    the same measure to the great city
21    Alexandria.
22    of grain, by the kankellos (measure), 4480 artabas
23    as follows:
24    to those from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite (nome)
