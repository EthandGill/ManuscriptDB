[TRANSLATION]
1     From a summary of letters of the epistrategos, year 22 of the god Hadrian
2     Augustus, sheet 41.
3     Concerning sending to him the accusers of Kephalas the village-scribe,
4     sheet 41.
5     Through the first (letter): Send to me at once the accusers of Kephalas also called Maximus the village-scribe, so that concerning the things which
6     before the most excellent governor Avidius Heliodorus they undertook
7     to prove, they may demonstrate. And send with them also whatever other things
8     they may have need of for the proofs. And let them come at once
9     so that they may catch me still in the Herakleopolite and
10    I may be able, being here, to make the examination; and when
11    the case itself is spoken (heard), be present yourself also.
12    I wish you health.
13    Year 22, Athyr 12.
14    Through the second (letter):
15    The accusers of Kephalas also called Maximus, village-scribe of Philadelphia,
16    and Kephalas his kinsman, and Serapion surnamed
17    Deios, both those who are accusers and if any others wish to accuse, send at once to me.
18    I wish you health.
19    Year 22, Athyr 13.
