[TRANSLATION]
1     … [this time] that we are in, the 7th indiction, … God
2     the Good cast a great sickness upon my beloved son Kollouthos,
3     according to the merit of our sins. I went into (the church of) the God-bearer and
4     the victorious, truly mighty, holy Apa Phoibammon the martyr,
5     and the unconquerable in heart; I besought him, saying: "If you [heal]
6     my son Kollouthos for me, I shall make him a slave unto him for the remainder of his days, that he may serve him as a slave to (the monastery) of Apas [always],
7     [and] serve the holy monastery through you, Kyriakos, the most pious monk and presbyter, with all the stewards who shall come after you, that no man,
8     neither I nor brother nor sister nor relative nor relative [of relative]
9     nor stranger nor house-member, shall bring suit against the holy place
10    concerning Kollouthos my son, neither in a court of law nor outside
11    a court of law, whether in a city, whether in a district, or outside the laws of the districts, or through (any) authority of magistrates or ecclesiastical (authority),
12    that he should oppose this deed of donation, this which I have drawn up to the God-bearer
13    and the victorious, truly mighty, holy Apa Phoibammon, [on behalf of my beloved]
14    son Kollouthos, so that he may come to be serving him in the remainder
15    of his whole life — inasmuch as the divine and royal law commands in this way, in their royal order, that it is permitted
16    to each one to make lord of what is his own. I myself too,
17    [according to] the procedure of the laws, I wish and I command that the holy place, to which I have donated [him],
18    in many instances may become his (the boy's) lord, so that no man may command him,
19    except the holy place. Whoever shall dare, now, to bring suit against the holy place concerning my beloved son, whom I have donated,
20    against it, neither I nor brother nor sister nor stranger
21    nor relative — secondly, indeed, he shall become estranged from the Father
22    and the Son and the Holy Spirit, and he shall take the portion [of Judas]
23    the Iscariot, and the holy place shall punish him,
24    and he shall take vengeance (?) at the tribunal which is full of fear of the Lord God
25    of the universe. I came, therefore, to this donation willing and persuaded,
26    without any deceit or fear or violence or deception or any circumvention or aggrandizement, no single necessity being laid upon me,
27    but rather out of my own free choice, my own, with no evil intent or malice, but in all good intent.
28    I swear, moreover, by God the Almighty and the safety
29    of our God-given kings who rule over us now, through the
30    ordinance of God, that I shall not dare, nor shall I be able
31    to bring suit against the holy place forever, through the most God-loving
32    presbyter Kyriakos the monk and the hegoumenos
33    of the holy place of the holy Apa Phoibammon of the mountain of the fortress
34    of Djeme, with all the stewards who come after you. I have drawn up the donation, it being settled, having power, and guaranteed in every place
35    where it shall be produced, and they shall ask me what I acknowledged.
36    They read it to me, I heard it, I confirmed it by a subscriber and witnesses, we released it. Those, indeed, who know how to write, wrote
37    in their own writing; those who do not know how to write among us,
38    they besought a subscriber, he subscribed
39    on their behalf, we released it according to the procedure of the laws.
40    We, Paam son of Theodore, with Makare
41    son of Georgios, with Sikiel (and) Dios, we agree to this charter
42    according to the way it is written.
43    I, Daniel son of Pikos, agree.
44    Makare — I wrote on his behalf.
45    We, Seueros son of the blessed Enoch, with Pcher
46    his brother, with Chael son of Senouthios, with Kosma
47    son of Mena, we agree to this charter
48    according to the way it is written.
