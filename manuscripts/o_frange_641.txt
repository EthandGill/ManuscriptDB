1     in first indeed, I send your brother-hood and I send to the presbyter making the spirit
2     of your sending because of the becoming of your saying [the] answer these, he said
3     [he] took him saying: I come to him concerning the sending, a sign and
4     indeed to the presbyter, he took men, we stay under you of your understanding, you write
5     [the] subscription to the Khome
6     I said him: this is the Khome which Father Dios bore
7     his witness to the church, we lifted up the count
8     of the Rachel, his wife
9     the chapter of these
10    all, making the spirit of your greeting in me, joy
