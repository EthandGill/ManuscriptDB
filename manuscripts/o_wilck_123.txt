[TRANSLATION]
1   Sempronius Celer and those with him,
2   overseers of the Sacred Gate of Syene,
3   through Sarapammon. Paid:
4   Pachnoumis son of Phanophis,
5   mother Tachomtbekis, for the poll-tax
6   of the seventh year
7   of Hadrian Caesar the lord:
8   four drachmas — total 4 dr.,
9   Pachon 5.
10   In (month) 6(?), another thirteen drachmas (and) a half-obol,
11   (and) ½ obol (keepers' fee) — total 13 dr. 1 ob.; grand total 17 dr. 1 ob.
