[TRANSLATION]
1     To Loukios Diogenes and however he is styled, from Amoitas son of Dionysios
2     from the city of the Oxyrhynchites, a Persian of
3     the epigone. I undertake to cultivate the land for four years, four flood-(crops),
4     from the present 15th year
5     of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
6     from the Crown-lands registered to him
7     near Sinkepha
8     from the holding of Dedys, six arouras
9     and a half and a quarter, so as for these
10    in the 15th year and the 17th year to sow and plant with whatever crops you choose, apart from customs-dues sowings, at a fixed rent each year
11    of vegetable-seed of ten artabas, and
12    in the 16th year and the last, the two years, to sow with wheat, at a fixed rent each year of forty artabas of wheat,
13    of libation each year one keramion of wine,
14    one which shall be … in common, free from risk the yearly fixed (rents) from all
15    risk, the public dues of the land being the responsibility of the lessor,
16    who also is to have ownership of the crops
17    until he recovers the yearly fixed (rents). And the lease-application being confirmed,
18    let the lessee pay the yearly fixed (rents) always in the month Pauni,
19    the vegetable-seed at the threshing-floors of Sinkepha, new, pure, unadulterated,
20    free of clods, sifted, by the
21    four-choinix measure of Longinus, the receivable (measure), those of his party making
22    the measurement, and the wheat of the coming year, year by year, let him measure
23    into the public granary of Sinkepha
24    and let him render the deposit of these pure
25    from all expense. And let the right of execution belong
26    to the lessor against both the lessee
27    and all his property
28    as if by legal decision. And if any (land) becomes unwatered
29    in the following years, allowance will be made
30    to the lessee proportionately. Valid is
31    the lease-application. Year 15 of the Emperor Caesar
32    Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus
33    Pius, Phaophi 16. I, Amoitas
34    son of Dionysios, undertake
35    to cultivate the land for the
36    four years and I will pay
37    the stated (rents) each year as aforesaid.
38    I, Dionysios … toto, wrote
39    on his behalf since he does not know
40    letters. The date is the same.
