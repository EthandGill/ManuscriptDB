[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Dalmatius, brother of our master
2     Constantine Augustus, and of Domitius Zenophilus,
3     the most splendid, Pharmouthi 14.
4     Flavius Septimius Serenius Apollonius, most eminent, of procuratorial rank, through me Epagathos, his own (agent),
5     to the Aurelii Palex son of Parammon and of mother Thaesis, and to (his) wife Sinthonis daughter of Thonios and of mother Sarapias, both from
6     the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites.
7     I acknowledge that I have received and have had counted out from you
8     the three hundred talents of silver provided to you by me, in accordance with the document which you executed for me concerning them,
9     and si[nce] this has been mislaid an[d lo]st and not found, for the (purpose of) restitution
10    to you, henceforth (I am) to make no claim against you concerning this
11    nor to bring a claim nor to proceed in any way whatsoever
12    in any manner on any pretext whatsoever; and that the same
13    document made concerning them is void and invalid and inadmissible
14    for me and for all those who
15    on my behalf produce (it). Valid (are) the documents of the acknowledgment and release and receipt, written singly, and questioned I have agree[d].
16    Flavius Apollonius, through [m]e Epagathos, his own (agent), I have received the three hundred
17    tale[n]ts of silver, and I make no claim nor will I claim, and (I agree that) void
18    is the document concerning th[em]
19    as is set out. Aurelius Papnoutis
20    son of Choous, I wrote on his behalf, he not k[n]owing letters.
21    Through me Sotas it was written.
