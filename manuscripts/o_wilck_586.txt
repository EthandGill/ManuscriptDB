r.1    Αἰλουρίων καὶ Σωτὴ(ρ) πράκ(τορες) ἀργ(υρικῆς) Χά(ρακος)
r.2    Ἀμενώθῃ Ἁρβῆχις μη(τρὸς) Σενχεσθώ(του).

[TRANSLATION]
1     Ailourion and Soter, collectors of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis, (whose) mother is Senchesthotis.
3     We have received for embankment-tax and bath-tax of year 22, which is also year 1, base drachmas 4, total drachmas 4.
4     Year 2 of Titus Aelius Antoninus Caesar
5     the lord, Thoth 4. (2nd hand) I, Ailourion, have signed, drachmas 4.
6     (3rd hand) I, Soter, have signed, drachmas 4.
