[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 8 of Emperor Caesar
2     Domitian Augustus Germanicus
3     of month Domitian
4     Hathur 10. Orsenouphis [son] of Hephaistios
5     and associates, given [to] you the grain-tax [of] Philadelphia
6     we have measured from the product of [the] seventh year
7     of Hephaistiad, inhabitants Thermion Herakleid, through Heras the farmer, by measure
8     public xystos, of wheat, two eighths
9     making wheat artabas 2 eighths and the additionally-measured [amounts].
10    Heras [son] of Chairemon I have marked wheat artabas 2 eighths.
11    Andros [son] of Besatos I have marked wheat artabas 2 eighths.
