1     There was paid through us, Claudius Per-
2     naios and partners, collectors of money-taxes of the village of Apollo(nos), of year 6,
3     for the tax on shipwrights, in the name of Pasmet( )
4     (son of) Pasmetp( ), twenty drachmas, <total> drachmas 20.
5     Year 6, Mesore 6.
6     And likewise in the name of Petosiris (son of) Paneb-
7     chounis, for the tax on shipwrights, eight drachmas,
8     drachmas 8, Mesore 6.
9     Through Herakleides.

11    on shipwrights, in the name of Pasmetp( ) and
12    Petosiris (son of) Panebchounis,
