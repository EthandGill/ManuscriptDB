[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
2     the consubstantial Trinity, and the power and the establishment and the dispensation of our God-given kings,
3     these who hold sway over the whole earth through the ordinance of God the Almighty.
4     On this day, which is the eighteenth day of this month Paoni, of this year, the eighth indiction, before
5     the most illustrious and honored Petros, son of the blessed komes, and Ioannes (and) Mathias, the dioiketai,
6     in the fortress of Djeme, in the nome of the city of Hermonthis.
7     I, Pesynthios son of the blessed Paulos, the man of
8     the fortress of Djeme in the nome of the city of Hermonthis,
9     I (declare), after the subscription of him who shall subscribe for me, and the trustworthy witnesses worthy to be believed, those who shall bear witness to this written deed of sale, since I wish and I am persuaded, without
10    any deceit or fear or violence, not to transgress
11    it, and not to shake it, but rather that it shall come to be firmly established, and not to set it aside, I,
12    moreover, establishing it through some trustworthy witnesses, those who shall bear witness to this written deed of sale, according to my own request, with my willingness of heart — I write to Hemai and Senouthi the presbyter,
13    sons of the blessed Germanos, men of the fortress of Djeme in the nome of the city of Hermonthis.
14    I wish and I am persuaded, without any deceit or fear
15    or violence or deception or any circumvention
16    and aggrandizement, but I came of my own persuasion, my own,
17    rather, swearing by the name of God the Almighty and the safety of our God-given kings who hold sway
18    over us through God, I sell to you, you (being) Hemai
19    and Senouthios the presbyter, sons of the blessed
20    Germanos, men of the fortress of Djeme, with every lordship,
21    a lordly sale, with a lordship
22    that cannot hinder you and cannot impede you,
23    namely the share of my whole house, which lies outside the eastern (part?) of the great bishop, the holy Apa Patermouthios of the fortress
24    of Djeme, according to its ancient placements and according to its four
25    corners which surround it: north, the daughter of Peiephte, the house;
26    west, the holy Apa Patermouthios; south, the public street;
27    and east, ancient (boundary), where the door opens onto it. The price,
28    moreover, of the share of the house is: 3¾ gold solidi and a tremissis,
29    its other share of the house being for you, you (being) Hemai and Senouthios,
30    sons of the blessed Germanos — that is,
31    you have authority over that whole house from now on.
32    Moreover, concerning yourselves, you shall have authority over the share of the house which I have given
33    over to you, since you have paid the price to me by your own hand, and the other share of the house is yours, you having authority over it forever,
34    at all times — yourselves and your children and the children
35    of your children and all who shall come after you, because
36    that land I acquired with your father Germanos before
37    their time, those who passed away. Not any right
38    is left to me in that land; I have given it over to you,
39    yourselves (being) Hemai and Senouthios, sons of the blessed
40    Germanos, that you may have authority over it as a lordly matter, that you may build upon it, that you may raise it up on high to the air, that you may pull it down, that you may turn it back up to the air, so that no man
41    on my father's side nor on my mother's side may come out against you forever,
42    forever and ever, neither I nor child nor brother nor sister nor (one born) of one (parent) nor (one born) of two, nor on my kindred's side nor outside my kindred, nor any man at all,
43    on my behalf, who shall bring suit against you, you (being) Hemai
44    and Senouthi, sons of the blessed Germanos, concerning the matter
45    of the share of the land which I have given over, I having taken its price
46    in a court of law or outside a court of law in
47    a city, in a district, in any place — whoever shall dare
48    to come out against you and bring suit against you concerning the matter
49    of the share of the land which I have given over to you — he shall give
50    four ounces of gold as a fine to the ruler
51    who collects at that time; but you shall (have it),
52    yourselves and your children and the children of your children and all who shall come after you. Firstly,
53    indeed, that that one shall gain no profit at all, but rather he shall become estranged from the Father and the Son and the Spirit
54    the Holy, and he shall be subject to the force of this sale and the security and the fine and everything written in it.
55    Over all these things, moreover, we swear by the name of God
56    the Almighty and the safety of our God-given
57    kings, not to deceive (about) what I have said above many
58    times concerning the sale. For your assurance, therefore, I have drawn up this sale
59    for you, that it may come to be settled and lordly, guaranteed
60    in every place where it shall be produced, through
61    any magistracy and any authority that has lordship.
62    After these things we confirm the force of this sale and every
63    matter written in it, as is stated above.
64    I, Pesonthios son of the blessed Paulos, who first
65    wrote above, I agree with the sale and every matter written
66    in it. I, Pcher son of the blessed Epiphanios, wrote
67    on his behalf, because he does not know how to write, and I give witness.
68    I, Demetrios son of the blessed Leontios, give witness. I, Sanagape son
69    of the blessed Mena, give witness. I, Demetrios,
70    wrote on his behalf, because he does not know (how to write).
71    I, Psere son of the blessed Petros, in the love
72    of God, the presbyter and the hegoumenos of the holy church
73    of Djeme, I give witness according to the request of Pesenthios.
74    I, Georgios son of the blessed Daniel.
75    I, Aroou son of the blessed Daniel, with Patermoute
76    son of the blessed Konstantinos, we give witness.
77    Ioannes son of Lazaros, I wrote on their behalf.
78    I, Nohe the presbyter and the hegoumenos,
79    I give witness according to the request of him who drew it up. I, Phelotheos son
80    of the blessed Souloumon, I give witness.
81    Nohe asked me; I wrote on his behalf.
82    I, David son of the blessed Biktor, I give witness.
83    Ioannes son of Lazaros, I wrote on his behalf.
