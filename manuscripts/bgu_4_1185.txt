[TRANSLATION]
1   The king having ordered:
2   we [...] the katoikic cavalry who have kept
3   the goodwill toward affairs [from their fore]fathers and in the
4   [...] times pressing with necessity, having contributed both the grain and
5   the [gold] crowns without hindrance,
6   [to be released] together with their wives and children and [...]
7   [...] of errors, sins, condemnations,
8   [...] of sheltering charges, of all accusations, of registrations,
9   of penalties, of overcharges, of fines, of surcharges
10   [...] of the 19th year, of all royal debts
11   [...] those brought to account for any of these,
12   [...] from the 16th(?) year; and that there remain to them the kleroi which
13   they have held, and to their descendants, together with the billet-quarters,
14   [... free from sycophantic charge and] unac-
15   [cused and unimpeachable, free of every charge being]
16   royal (property). And if any of them should die
17   intestate, the kleroi of these are to pass to
18   those nearest in kin, just as is the case also among the Arsino-
19   ites. And the vineyards and orchards
20   of which they have paid into the survey of the 21st year as poorly-
21   irrigated because it is very high-lying, and into vine-
22   and orchard-planting, and other things only [...]-
23   ing, immunities of money- and grain-dues, these also being appor-
24   tioned. And of dedications to the gods and of leases
25   also for the future let no one make demand nor be
26   summoned; from no one shall claims of possession be established
27   in accordance with those from comparison(?), and to other matters we have
28   made concession through others [...] both the privileges and the customs
29   no one [...]
