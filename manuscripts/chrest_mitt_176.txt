[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a sa[l]e. In the 7th year of Imperator Caesar
2     Marcus Aure[li]us Antoninus Augustus
3     and of Imperat[o]r Caesar Lucius Aurelius
4     Verus Augustus, Phaophi 14, through the
5     bank of Dioxenos also called Sarapion, of the portico of
6     Athena. Tanephremmis daughter of Stotoetis
7     so[n] of Harpagathes, and her daughters,
8     Taouetis and Thases and Erieus, the three
9     daughters of Harpagathes son of Satabous, from the village of
10    Soknopaiou Nesos, each with her guardian, namely
11    Tanephremmis [a]nd Taouetis (with) Stotoetis the brother of Tan[ephr]emmis, and Th[as]es (with) Tha[ses]
12    the husband of Pakysis son of Satabous, and (with the guardian) [of]
13    Erieus, Pabous son of Satabous — the four (women), to the son of Thanephremmis, and
14    (Pabous) being the brother by the same father and same mother of those around Tauetis, namely Pabou[s] … ol … ai
15    … that Pabous [has sol]d to those around
16    Tanephremmis the existing to him thir[d] part, common with them
17    according to the remaining two-thirds part, of two
18    fem[al]e camels, one of them
19    white-coloured, [and the]
20    other of black colour, these
21    being such, unrejectable, which also the (women) aroun[d] Tanephremm[is recei]ved, and
22    Pabous has (received) the agreed price, four [hund]red drachmas of silver, total 400 drachmas, and he guarantees with every guara[ntee].
