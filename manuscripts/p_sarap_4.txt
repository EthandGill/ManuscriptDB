[TRANSLATION]
1     To Philon, strategos of Hermopolis
2     From Anubion, son of Sarapion of Hermopolis, being registered
3     On the ward of Polis, to the southwest. I registered in the year that had passed
4     Year 13 of Hadrian Caesar, the lord, for
5     The tax-administration of Around-the-city below: sheep with lambs 400, goats with kids
6     19, of which perished goats 6 sheep 121 and I sold to Selene daughter of Achilles, paying the pasturage-right
7     For the Around-the-city below sheep 47; which remain, which also
8     I register for the present year 14 of Hadrian Caesar, the lord
9     Of the [lord], sheep two hundred thirty-two, goats
10    [Thirteen], lambs one hundred thirty-two, kids 6, bed of whole place Around-the-city [below] …
