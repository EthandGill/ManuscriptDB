[TRANSLATION]
1     With God Flavius Atias son of Goedu [he signs] … Zacharias the son John
2     the merchant the man declares that he has bound me to the tenth indiction [of one whole-grain]
3     and three-month [payment] two be what you gave to [the …] in nine days [for] you …
4     it comes to be numbered coins one [and a half] only. In the month Tybi 29 of indiction 9.
5     it comes to be numbered coins one [and a half].
