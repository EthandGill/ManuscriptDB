r.3    ἡμῶν ἀναξίων ὄντων ὑμῶν. μαθόντες δὲ ἐκ τῶν γραμμάτων ὑμῶν τὴν ὑγίειαν ὑμῶν καὶ τὴν

[TRANSLATION]
1     Before all things I write, worshipping and greeting the precious footprints of your
2     paternal holiness, and praying for it to be in health for the longest time, praying fervently
3     for us unworthy [as] we are of you. Having learned from your letters your health and the
4     condition, we were filled with joy because peacefully you found a good place and the healing
5     of you, and we do not cease praying night and day on behalf of your salvation until God
6     brings you here and we may in person worship the feet of you. For indeed also if
7     you are in foreign [lands], double reward gives God the master to you. Many [times] worships you Iouak
8     the doorkeeper, your servant. The holy trinity.
9     To our master, the most holy of all and most pious spiritual father, abba Peter, bishop
10    Abramios, apa Menas, and
11    Kalapesios, your servants.
