[TRANSLATION]
1     To Theon, strategos of Arsinoe [of the] divisions of Themistes and Polemon
2     and to Artemidoros, royal scribe of the division of Themistes
3     and to Hera, village scribe of Theadelphia
4     from Apollo and Heron the elder and Heron
5     the younger, the three [sons] of Apollo and Socrates
6     registered in the quarter of the Sacred Gate. [We] own
7     to us in the aforesaid village Theadelphia a paternal half-share of houses
8     and of [a courtyard] and a courtyard, in which no one dwells
9     [and we register] no one in the of the past
10    [9th year of Antoninus Caesar our lord according to] house registration. Wherefore we submit [this].
