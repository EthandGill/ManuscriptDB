[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios to Apollodotus greetings. If some of those exporting
2     the grain from Syria write out for you either the prices or the shipping charge,
3     receive from them through the bank and give
4     to us double sealed tokens, writing both the name
5     of the one who paid and the amount of the money, and if
6     [they] pay on behalf of another.
7     Farewell. Year 25, Artemisios 12.
8     of grain prices
9     To Apollodotus.
