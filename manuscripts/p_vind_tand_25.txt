1     Satabous son of Stotoetis, his mother being Taonnophris, I agree that I have sold to Stotoetis son of Satabous,
2     his mother being Thaesis, the tenth part belonging to me of a maternal single-story house
3     and of a courtyard on the western side, and of a vacant plot, in two lots, in common and [u]ndivided,
4     [and of all] the appurtenances, in Sokno[p]aiou Nesos of the [Herakl]eides division; of which the neighbors of the first lot (are): on the south the house of Pisais [and] his brothers, and the entrance and exit of the buyer Stotoetis; on the north the house and courtyard of Thaubastis; on the west and east the royal street; of which the neighbors of the
5     [other] lot (are): on the south a dung-heap of the village; on the north the oil-press of Kalatyth[is] son of Onnophris; on the west
6     the house and courtyard of Taaipis; on the east the royal
7     street. And I have received the whole agreed price in full, and I will guarantee (it) with every
8     guarantee for all time; and the other things
9     [I will d]o as is written above. Satabous son of St[o]t[o]etis wrote on his behalf, [because] he does not know letters.
10    Stotoetis son of Satabous, his mother being Thaesis:
11    [I have bou]ght as is set forth. Sale and cession of the tenth part of a maternal house,
12    single-story, and of a courtyard on the western side, and of the tenth part
13    [of another] vacant plot, in two lots, in common and undivided, and of all the ap[purt]enances,
14    in Soknopaiou Nesos of the He[rakleido]s division,
15    [of which the neigh]bors, (are those) which are set forth; and I have received the price … [ i]n full, which Satabous son of Stotoetis makes.
16    Taonnophris, about 23 years old, with a scar on a finger …
17    … of the left hand, to Stotoe[tis son of S]atabous, his mothe[r] being Thae[si]s, about 45 years old, with a scar on both knees. The undersigner of the
18    seller (is) Sotas son of Stotoetis,
19    [about ] … years old, with a scar on the right forearm. Year twelve of Ti[ber]i[u]s Claudius Caesar

21    [record-office in So]knopaiou Nesos.
