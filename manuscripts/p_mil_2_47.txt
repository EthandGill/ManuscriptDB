[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Paoutes, superintendent of musicians
2     from Aurelius Melanos, bandit-catcher
3     leading the association of the village of Thraso.
4     I [wish] to take in the aforesaid association music
5     all for seven days [from] the
6     eighteenth of Epiphi
7     month according to the ancients, I providing
8     her a wage in silver drachmas forty,
9     drachmas forty, and for
10    gratuity of all the days
11    of wheat artabae three, of loaves
12    [pairs] twenty … baker
