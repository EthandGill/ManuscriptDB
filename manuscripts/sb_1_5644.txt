[TRANSLATION]
1     [Settl]ement of Pakaunos: coins 498; of grain, artabae one hundred [twenty] eight and one-half [of a twelfth].
2     In the name of God. Korra, son of Szerich, advisor to you (plural) from
3     the [village of Aphro]dito [. He allotted to] you [on behalf of] p[ublic ...]
4     indiction 6, according to the Arab year 88, counted coins 498, four [hundred] ninety
5     [e]ight; and for additional grain, artabae 128 and one-half [of a twelfth], specifically one hundred twenty eight
6     and one-half of a twelfth only. Written in the month of Thoth, indiction eighth.
7     Total: coins 498; of grain, artabae 128 and one-half of a twelfth.
