1     Year 463, month Daisios 13, in Bephoureia village; [sold by Aulaias of Abdialaus …]
2     of village Banasamsow, dwelling there, a slave named Ouardanaia[n, called Diana;] purchased by Abisauta of Abidiardu[s for] five hundred [drachmas].
3     [Under the consuls of our lords …] the Emperor Gaius Uiuius Trebonios …
4     [Gallus and Gaius Uiuius] Aphinios Ueldumianus Uo[lo]u[s]ianus Caesar …
5     [Pious Fortunate Sebastoi] year 463, month Daisios, thirteenth, …
6     [in Bephoureia village, on …] Sunday, before the underwritten witnesses.
7     [sold by Aurelius] Aulaias of Abdialaus of village Bonasamsow of the …
8     [Abureene,] [dwelling] now in Bephoureia village, the one who belonged to him …
9     [a silver-purchased slave] named Ouardanaia[n, called Diana …]
10    [or also if she is called by any other name] or will be called, of Ortheite race, being as (it were) … years …
11    … light-skinned, round-faced, having good eyes,
12    … [having been] purchased by him from Aurelia Uictorinas of Anthi…, dwelling in Nesibeina, with Aurelius Oua… [son] of the first Parthic legion, centurion, primipilus, her husband, present, according to [registration which he showed] made in Nesibeina, in good health, untouched;
13    [purchased the same slave by] Abisautas of Abidiardus of Phourane for the price …
14    [of silver drachmas] five hundred fifty, and having received the price of her, the seller from the buyer [gave …] over …
15    [to him the same slave] to have and to possess securely and firmly; and …
16    [the seller …] that if anyone claims the aforesaid [sold slave or any …] part of her, he, the seller [standing], will defend […, make …] free [and] release the claimant; if not, he will pay …
17    [the price which] he received immediately doubled; and thus …
18    [the slave remains for the purchaser;] if moreover she is afflicted by sacred disease within the [nearest …] six months, having returned the price, [he] will take [her …] back;
19    [for these things] to be done [well and guarded], …
20    [faith having been required and …] sworn mutually between them accordingly.
21    … [I wrote] for Aulaia of Abdialaus the aforementioned, illiterate, acknowledging [to have sold the aforementioned slave named Ouardanaia[n and to have received] the …
22    [price: drachmas five] hundred fifty accordingly with the aforementioned.
23    … [Metolbhe]sumenos, librarian …
