1     Tenth year of Trajan Caesar the lord, Phamenoth the 30th.
2     When it was read concerning the expenditure for the newly being constructed
3     bathhouse and the plaza, sixteen talents, [and] when [it was] said
4     by Heraclides the general that other things in between [have been] expe[nded], Vibius
5     Maximus: it was assessed to the city from Theon [fif]ty talents
6     and from those of the gymnasium-director other [amounts], I think twenty. From those [assessed] to the city [let them be restored]. Her[a]klides: from what and
7     what property? Vibius Maxim[us]: you have [i]t in my memoranda.
8     A copy of a letter.
9     [Sulpici]us Similis to Heracleides, general of Hermoupolis, greetings. Herodes of Dionysius approached me
10    saying the ones appointed with him as [m]anagers of the bathhouse [in]valid
11    [and] not measuring up to the [c]are. [If] therefore they are invalid you will se[nd] me
12    of other overseers the names, and concerning those of the [c]ity's revenues [hav]ing [examin]ed you will report m[e]. [Fare]w[ell I p]ray. Of the tenth [year] of the Imperator Caesar N[er]va
