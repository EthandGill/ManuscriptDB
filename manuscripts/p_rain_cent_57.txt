[TRANSLATION]
1     … giste in the villages,
2     … of the priests, [to whom] it is permitted
3     and [they] are able according to the [custom]
4     piously to perform the mysteries,
5     so that nothing more be demanded
6     from those well-situated than is customary.
7     Let [him] come to me and render an account to me
8     Let [him] give. Year 9 of Tiberius Claudius
9     Caesar Augustus Germanicus
10    Emperor, Phamenoth 8, [on] Sebastis.
