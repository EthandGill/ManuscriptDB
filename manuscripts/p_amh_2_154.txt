[TRANSLATION]
1     Your distinguished genuine friendship knows that it has been resolved that the portion of the pious
2     hospital be set apart for the [distributi]ons, and that no one pass over (it) through its hamlets. Since at present
3     there came here the people from Piahoth the hamlet, saying that they had been assessed (with) a donkey and barley and fodder, and
4     the matter seemed to me credible, but I beseech you, distinguished sirs, to give order [that] no one [pas]s [over]
5     through them. But if they were assessed by my master and brother the most magnificent chartularius, or by the common master
6     the most glorious illustrious one, let your distinguished friend[ship] again command [to be] written to me, so that I may beseech them,
7     since unexpectedly I do not wish to bring up a matter to them, unless I ascertain exactly from you about
8     each matter. For it has been resolved, as has been said, that the portion of the pious hospital be set ap[ar]t
9     for each distribution that shall take place, and I will apportion it to the hamlets that are able
10    to provide each thing that is sought.
11    To my master and brother in all things distinguished, worthy of all honor and obeisance, genuine lord Senouthios the executor, Komitas …
