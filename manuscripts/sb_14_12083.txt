[TRANSLATION]
1     … militi… to Horeion the
2     dearest, greetings.
3     [The] bale which you said had been given to Orneithas the camel-[driver], if it has not already been convey[ed] here, you, going up
4     [here] make haste to have it convey[ed], since as you know the ne[ed] for it is pressing. See then
5     [that you] do not neglect [it]. I pray you farewell, dearest.
