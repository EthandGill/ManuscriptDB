[TRANSLATION]
1     Marcus Aurelius Horus also called Pkoilis and Marcus Aurelius
2     Plenis Plenius, the two chaff-tax collectors of the Memnoneia, we have received
3     on behalf of the name Pasemius Patsebthius, measure 24, and the name of Pasemius
4     Athreous Patsebthius, measure 6, 24, and the name of Pasemius
5     Patsebthius, measure 24, making measure a quarter of twenty-four. Of [the] 23rd year, Epeiph 16th.
6     Marcus Aurelius Horus also called Pkoilis, I have authenticated.
7     Marcus Aurelius Plenis Plenius through his father, I have authenticated.
