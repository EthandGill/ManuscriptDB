[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 14 of the [and] 11, Pharmouthi 5, [it] has been appointed to the bank in Hermouthis, over which Dionysios [is banker], of the tenth encyclion, by copy of Psenkhonsis
2     of the tax-collector, under which Herakleidis the counter-signer writes, [it is the] tax of the purchase of Osorer son of Horus, [a] house built
3     and furnished-with-doors in the southern part near the Memnonia, [at] sixth part,
4     which he purchased from Snakomneus son of Hapkhras, [of] copper drachmas 3, tax …
5     Dionysios the banker.
