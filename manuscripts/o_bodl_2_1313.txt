[TRANSLATION]
1     Treasury measurement of the metropolis, produce, year 18 of Hadrian
2     the lord, Pauni 14, on behalf of Charako, name
3     of Bassus, Psenamunios, through Petechonsius Apion
4     become of wheat artabae: ten, three, half, fourth
5     become of wheat artabae 13 and 1/2 and 1/4, and on behalf of year 16, Charako, name
6     of the same likewise of wheat artabae 24, become of wheat artabae 13 and 3/8. G[aius] … I have signed.
