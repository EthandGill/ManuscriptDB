[TRANSLATION]
1     To the lords of earth and sea and of every race and kind of men Fl[avii] Theodosius and Valentinian, the [eternal] Aug[usti]
2     A petition and supplication from Appion, bishop of the region of Syene and Ke… S[y]ene and Elephantine province of your Upper Thebes.
3     Your phil[anthrop]y is accustomed to extend a right hand to all who are in need, [wh]ence I too, having clearly learned this, [come] upon these petitions
4     [I] have come, the matter being in these circumstances. In the midst of the wicked barbar[ians] toge[ther] with my churches happening [between] the Blemmyans
5     and Ann[u]badans many [incursions] from them as [from] the unseen [we] en[dure with] no soldier presiding over our
6     places. Out of this, the churches under me being humbled and not cap[able] either themselves to aid those taking refuge in them
7     I fall prostrate, rolling before your divine and immaculate footstep[s, so] as [to] be deign[ed to] decree [guard]ing [those under me]
8     holy churches by the soldiers among us [and] to obey [themselves to] me [and to] be subject concerning all things, just as those at Philae thus
9     so-called garrison of your Upper Thebes [are stationed], soldier[s] serve the holy [churches] at Philae of God
10    churches. For thus shall we be ab[le] fearlessly [to live] … [to] follow laws …
11    most severe defined against those breaking … [the [divine]ly by you decreed, of all
12    plundering by the opposing party [having] happened or being about to [happen] …, divine your …
13    special grace concerning this going to [towards] the most magnificent and renowned count and duke
14    of the Thebaïc frontier; and receiving this the customary prayers I will send up to God on behalf of your eternal [ru]le
15    forever.
