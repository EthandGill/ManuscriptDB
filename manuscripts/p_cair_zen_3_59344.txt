1     … greetings. You will do well by writing to Jason to pro[vide] us [the station-house of Theo]pompos so that we [may] have [a place] to dwell in;
2     for the [place] in which we dwell [was] Phileos', [he] who was formerly secretary of
3     the Arsinoite [nome]; for the property has been [con]veyed by the dioiketes
4     and those [from] him are ordering us [to] vacate. But if it is not possible
5     [for] this [to] take [place, take a] letter from Sosstratos to the
6     [officials] under him so that [it] be shown to us, his dwelling.
7     Farewell. Year 1, Mecheir 11.
