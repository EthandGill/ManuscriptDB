[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklepiades at the same place
2     to oil-maker Diogenes, banker, greetings.
3     I acknowledge that I have borrowed money through you
4     from the [in the] city of Zeus, the great city
5     a basket of foreign oil, measures 80
6     of the measure, drachmas 10, of [bronze] drachmas 800, [they] total 800.
7     Farewell. Year 36, Mesore 19.
8     Year 36, Mesore, counterpart [receipt]
9     Of Asklepiades, to be distributed, basket of oil, bronze 800.
