[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zoi[lus an]d Tryphōn and to those
2     with him [designated] for receipt and transport of books being sent into Alexandria
3     to the logistēs of the nome
4     from ... tiōnios and Arsythmēos
5     and [of the oth]er tax-scribes of village Theadelpheia
6     we assigned to you man by man
7     a list of those registered to us
8     for the (census) of the 23rd year of Aelius
9     Antoninus (taken) house by house. the compiled (list) follows those delivered
10    to us as sheets Zōilos ...
