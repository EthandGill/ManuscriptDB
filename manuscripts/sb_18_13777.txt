[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Basileus, the most illustrious
2     Choiach 20, of the fifth indiction
3     Aurelia Taam, also called Kale, daughter of Jacob
4     of mother Tsabine, from the village of Trakatanes of the
5     Coptic nome, to Aurelius Daniēlius, son of Saïtos
6     from Memnonium of the Hermontite nome, greetings.
7     Since I deposited with you a silver maniak in exchange for
8     manifest gold and [promised you would give] interest on those [funds], but now
9     and I was unable to redeem this from you; when you sought from me the completion of the interest, I considered
10    [that] it would be more beneficial to me rather to make use of a renunciation for the same
11    maniak, through which I acknowledge that I surrender to you the said maniak and I give you authority to use this and to sell [it] to others and to pledge and to give away and to do everything [concerning] it in whatever way you might choose, [in a master-like manner, unhindered]
12    [and] unobstructed; but if at any time I should make a claim or
13    ... something against you or your heirs, I myself or [my heirs] or whoever else [on my behalf] concerning whatever matter pertaining to this transaction, I shall pay to you
14    [by way of] penalty twelve gold coins [in potency] and [in deed to be demanded], and so that nothing shall be valid against the written [terms].
15    [This renun]ciation shall be valid and firm, presented everywhere with
16    [the signature] of the one signing on my behalf, and when questioned I acknowledged [this]. Aurelia Taam, also called Kale, daughter of Jacob, of mother
17    [Tsabine], the aforementioned, I placed [my] acknowledgment, that is, renunciation,
18    [and] all the written [matters] agree with me as set forth. Flavius Musaeus
19    [son of Mus]aeus, scribe of the village of Trakatanes, [being] honored, I wrote
20    [on behalf of] her being present and saying to me, [she] not knowing letters,
21    he who also signed [physically].
22    Paulus, [son] of Isaakios, subdeacon of the holy apa Psatē, I witness [for] the scribe, having heard from the one who placed [the deed].
23    Flavius Eulogios, [son] of Eupraxios, from the city of Diocletianopolis, having been requested
24    I wrote on behalf of her, saying to me and being present, [she] not knowing letters.
25    Jacob Daniel Saïtos
