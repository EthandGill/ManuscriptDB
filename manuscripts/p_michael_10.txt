[TRANSLATION]
1     Pichaeus [son] of Myius, elder, …
2     of the first year to Theon [son] of Leonides, greetings;
3     I have received from you for the arrears of dyke-work
4     of the first year of the [Emperor] Caesar
5     Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus,
6     silver [of] two and one-half drachmas; [they] total silver [of] two and one-half drachmas.
7     Year second [of the] Emperor Caesar
8     Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus,
9     Choiak 16. Horion [son] of Hierax wrote for
10    him because he does not know letters.
