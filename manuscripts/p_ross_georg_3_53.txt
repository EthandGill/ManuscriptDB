[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the Lord] and Master Jesus Christ, God
2     [and our Saviour, and of our Lady]
3     [the holy] Mother of God and ever-virgin Mary
4     [and of all t]he saints. Year 391 of Diocletian,
5     … 22, of the second indiction, in Arsinoe.
6     To Fla[vius Stephanos the] most glorious general of this
7     city of the Arsin[o]ites, Aurelius Neilammon, the head-man
8     of the fishermen, son of John, from the village of Kainon of the Arsinoite
9     nome, greeting. I acknowledge that I have and owe you and am indebted (to you)
10    purely and absolutely, of fish, strings
11    two hundred and twenty — of fish, 220 strings — and
12    these I will render to you whenever you wish,
13    without doubt, at my own risk
14    and (that) of [al]l the fishermen under me; and, having been questioned, [I acknowledged. Aurelius]
15    Neilammon, it is agreeable to me as is set forth.
16    John, deacon and collector, son of Stephanos, I witness this deed
17    as is set forth. Georgios, banker, son of John, I witness
18    this deed as is set forth.
19    Through me, Kosmas son of Phib, it was signed; 343 (?)
20    [Deed concerning fish], 220 strings, made by Neilammon, head-man of the fishermen of the estate of Kainon, to Flavius Stephanos the most glorious general.
