[TRANSLATION]
1     To the lady, the sister Atienateia, Didyme and the sisters in the Lord, greetings.
2     First it was necessary to address you,
3     praying for you to be well among us.
4     Write to us, my lady, concerning
5     your health and the things you need—
6     having authority over your orders. If you have received your provisions, let us know. [There]
7     has been left behind with us from the
8     money of your provisions, as I suppose, denarii A[t...].
9     The Canopic [items], having been received from them, will be sent to you.
10    Greet the blessed lady sister Asou
11    and her mother and
12    … [?] the
13    to my lady sister Atienateia, Didyme, with the [sisters].
