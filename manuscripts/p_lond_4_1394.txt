1     In the name of [God. Qurra son of Sharik, governor (symboulos),]
2     [to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito. We give thanks]
3     to God; and after that: [Often we appear to have had recourse to letters]
4     with you [concerning] the realization of the gold
5     public-taxes of the seventh in[d]iction; and up to t[h]is [day]
6     you have not been roused to this, nor indeed have you sent anything at all,
7     but you have been negligent and contemptuous, and the time has come
8     for the Maghāritai (Saracens) of the Army (Fossaton) to receive their
9     pay-rations and to set out on the campaigns (kursa). On receiving
10    therefore the present letter, at once, with all dispatch,
11    send to us whatever gold you have collected
12    from the same public-taxes, with a man of yours who is
13    obliged to deposit this in the treasury (sakella).
14    For we have enjoined the present apostolos (envoy) [that he] not be moved
15    from you until you send whatever you have realized; and strive
16    energetically for the realization of the remaining amount of such
17    gold public-taxes, with good management, sending
18    whatever you realize of them in installments, without delay, so that
19    we may not be compelled to send …

21    through your administration t… [with]
22    fairness and management …
23    or of unjust (men); and you shall be kno[wing that the first]
24    work of the subordinate-official [is the realization of the public-taxes, and]
25    let there not be through you any …
26    impediment, or of the giving …
27    through you the amount, and you fall into da[nger. Written in the month] …
28    Qurra son of Sh[arik, governor, to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito]
