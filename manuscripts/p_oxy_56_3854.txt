[TRANSLATION]
1     Plolus to Hor, camel-driver, Syrian, greeting.
2     As I agreed with you [for] old wine
3     measures ten on behalf of a litter and for me
4     measures twelve to bring them to Oxyrhynchus so that you sell the twelve measures at the current value in Oxyrhynchus and bring me the silver. Do not then be careless, but coming bring me the value…
5     …, since I have need of the money.
6     I wish you well.
7     To Hor, camel-driver, Syrian, from Plolus
8     potter
9     To Hor, camel-driver, Syrian, from Plolus
