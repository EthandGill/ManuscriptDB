[TRANSLATION]
1     without any [deceit and] fear and force and fraud and
2     compulsion and any seizure whatsoever and circumvention
3     of any kind, that I am ready to undertake the management
4     of your affairs which are situated in the plain
5     of [the same village] and of Phthla of the same nome for
6     the time [of my] life and of yours and until the lawful
7     age of [my three] full brothers, and
8     to exercise every care and improvement and good husbandry
9     over them blamelessly and without neglect, first of all
10    to pay [every] year the public dues for these toward the
11    assessment levied upon them in accordance with the public
12    register, and after the discharge of public dues of every kind
13    for these paid by me each year, all the income found
14    from the produce of every kind of these your same estates
15    [I shall pay in to your] fatherhood and restore it
16    in full [without loss], apart from all fraud and
17    default, and render the accounts concerning these things
18    to you, both of the income and the expenditure of these
19    each year, and to take an oath to you in the holy houses of God
20    [that I have concealed nothing] of these, nor indeed
21    into my own house … have I appropriated even one mation,
22    and that I am not able to withdraw from concord with my
23    full brothers and my mother until
24    their lawful age, as has been said, nor to
25    despise these things [but to] remain as in one identity … …
26    in one good life and dwelling, both if I marry and if
27    I do not marry; and for fuller security
28    I have sworn by the holy Trinity and by the victory and continuance
29    of our triumphant master Flavius Justinus the eternal
30    Augustus, emperor, that I shall abide by all the things written
31    and in no way transgress. But if ever at some occasion
32    or time … I transgress the things written and
33    do anything contrary to [this] agreement,
34    I shall be [liable] to the charge of perjury and to the legal penalty
35    against those who transgress sworn
36    covenants. [And for] all the things written and toward
37    each chapter of them and for your security
38    I have made for you this agreement, which is valid and
39    secure, [everywhere] produced, with the signature
40    of myself and of witnesses, and on being formally questioned I have agreed.
