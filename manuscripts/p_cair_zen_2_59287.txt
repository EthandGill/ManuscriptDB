r.2    ῥυπαρά τε καὶ τραχέα· διὸ ἀπεστάλκαμέν σοι κομίζοντα Ἡρακλείδην. εἰσὶν δὲ

[TRANSLATION]
1     Zenon to Isiggas greetings. The Arabian wools received from you were entirely
2     dirty and rough; for this reason we have sent to you Heraclides carrying [them]. And there are
3     fleeces 16, of which the weight [is] minas 18 [and] drachmas 4.
4     Farewell. Year 35, Tybi 14.
5     To Isiggas.
