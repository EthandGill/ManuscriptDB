# tertius: lacuna-check failed lines 6,7,11
1     To Annikios Petronianos, dekatrarch
2     From Horos, son of Satabos, from the village Sokn[o]p[a]ios island, registered on an outpost of Pisai. The possessions
3     to those under my guardianship being administered
4     my cousins, young, of the allotment
5     four arouras around the village H[erakle]ian I leased to Leontas, slave
6     of Neilos, having served as gymnasiarch,
7     and indeed, having recognized that they unjustly had been troubled by Horos Stotoetis and
8     Satyros Chairemon and Sotos the brother and Horos Panoupes,
9     but Leontas at present is not seen,
10    openly according to necessity I came forward,
11    and I demand they be brought to account
12    they being liable for those things which they unjustly carried out.
13    Horos, 46 years old, scar on a small finger of the left hand.
14    Year 11 of Antonin[os] Caesar the lord, Pharmouthi 26.
