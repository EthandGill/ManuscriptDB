12    still disputing … [Del]matios also called Valentinus said
13    [that, being in] minority before the [attainment] of the lawful age of twenty-
14    five years, he had made the sale and was circumvented; for the price,
15    he said, was not paid to him worthy of the property, but less.
16    Pleading against this, Valentinus said that a sale is not overturned
17    on account of a lesser price, for such things always happen
18    in sales, either buying for a little more or a little less
19    or selling; Delmatios also called Valentinus said against these things that, in the case of those who are
20    of full age, this is so; but he himself made, while in minority at that time, the
21    contract of sale and was circumvented; against this Valentinus said
22    that, most of all, he did not even make the contract as a minor at that time,
23    but he also asserted of himself that he was then of full age; and if one should grant,
24    by way of concession, that this is so, yet — having passed the full and lawful
25    age by much — by his own account he kept silent over the sale and
26    did not bring complaint about it; and by this account, there is barred
27    to him every plea against him concerning minority
28    or concerning a comparable price. These things having been said by them to one another,
29    it came about that an arbitration was held by peaceable, good
30    intermediary men, before whom, these matters and many others having been raised, and the matter being about
31    to be brought into court, afterward it pleased the
32    intermediaries, and it seemed good to the parties, for a complete release and settlement
33    of the matter, that there be given to Delmatios also called Valentinus, by Valentinus,
34    a further six gold solidi, 6 gold solidi, in addition to those already given long ago
35    at the time of the sale that took place; and on these (terms) that they be released
36    and that Delmatios also called Valentinus himself have no further claim
37    against Valentinus concerning the same fifth part of the same
38    property, or a part of it, or a right to it, which are also included
39    in the sale made by him, Delmatios also called Valentinus,
40    to Valentinus, nor concerning the price as being less
41    than the worth, nor concerning circumvention as having been circumvented himself.

43    to bring a claim against Delmatios also called Valentinus as having received from him
44    afterward, after the sale, the aforesaid six solidi, because by way
