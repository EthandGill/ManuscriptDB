[TRANSLATION]
1     Heraklides the elder
2     To Heraklides greetings. I acknowledge
3     to have from you artabas of wheat
4     two, third, twelfth,
5     which I [will repay] you from new until
6     Pachon the 1st of the same 7th year.
7     [Heraklides] wrote on behalf of him
8     because he claimed that he
9     does not know letters.
10    Year 7, Athyr the 28th.
