[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our master Flavius Anastasius, the eternal
2     Augustus, Imperator, for the third time, Choiak the fourth, of the first indiction,
3     in Hermopolis of the Thebaid.
4     We have made between ourselves this written and sworn
5     agreement of the division, written in duplicate, identical,
6     so that there should be in the possession of each party one authentic (copy)
7     for security. On the one side Aurelius Victor son of Taurinos,
8     subscribing below, assistant, from the same (city) of the Hermopolites,
9     and on the other side Flavius Menas son of Phoibammon,
10    himself also subscribing below, enrolled in the number
11    of the most noble Moors stationed at the (city) of the Hermopolites,
12    greeting: we acknowledge to one another the following appended matters, having acquired a clear share both of a vineyard plot, that is, of the western
13    part of the same vineyard, that is, from the lane of the Persian (quarter) westward as far as the western
14    wall of the same vineyard, and of my sowable arouras, to the north;
15    of sowable land, of however many arouras it is, I, the aforewritten
16    Menas, soldier, (have?) a quarter and a twentieth share of these, both of the same
17    vineyard and of the same sowable land, devolving
18    … and by right.
