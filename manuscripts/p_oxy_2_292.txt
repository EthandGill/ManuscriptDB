[TRANSLATION]
1   Theon to Tyrannos the most honored,
2   very many greetings.
3   Herakleides, who delivers this
4   letter to you, is my brother;
5   wherefore I entreat you with all your pow-
6   er to hold him as one recommend-
7   ed. And I have asked also Hermias
8   my brother in writing to tell [you]
9   about him. And you will do me the greatest favor
10   if he wins your notice.
11   Before all I pray that you have health,
12   unharmed by the evil eye, faring
13   excellently. Farewell.
14   To Tyrannos, dioiketes.
