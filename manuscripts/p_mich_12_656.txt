[TRANSLATION]
1     Nemesion to Tryphon
2     to the brother, greetings.
3     I ask you to go to
4     the rulers and show
5     to them that Papis, on account of
6     the chief-scimitar-bearer [makes] me unsettled by [journeys],
7     harassing me. For which reason they ought to inform the general. I owe nothing, neither
8     exposure nor [underlying] debt.
9     If you do not have him beaten, I shall not stand firm.
10    Farewell.
