[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: (m. 2) Soknopaiou (Nesos), 1 camel, 1 foal. (m. 1) to Hierax, strategos, and Timagenes, royal scribe of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division, from Stotoetis, son of Horos, the son of Stotoetis, from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos. Of the 2 fully grown camels, which I registered in the past 23rd year in the village, I have sold in the same 23rd year 1 male camel to Harpokration, son of Sarapion, of the Sosicosmian tribe and the Althaean deme, The remaining 1 camel I register for the present 24th year of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, and the 1 foal, born from the lineage, still suckling. (m. 3) 1 camel, 1 foal were registered at the office of the strategos. Mecheir 3. (m. 4) 1 camel, 1 foal were registered at the office of the royal scribe. Mecheir 3. (m. 5) It has been tallied at the office of the strategos in corroboration. (m. 6) It has been tallied at the office of the royal scribe in corroboration. (m. 7) It has been tallied at the office of the [– – – in corroboration]. Verso (m. 6?) Theon, son of Souchas, from Phremei.
