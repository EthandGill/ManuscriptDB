1     [P]ashemis of Petechon ... to Amotos greetings. I have from
2     you the export-duty and the surcharge
3     of my irrigated land. If any[one]
4     comes regarding royal or private [matters], I will dispossess him.

6     [having been] deemed worthy by him because of the
7     [fact that] he claims not to know
8     letters.
