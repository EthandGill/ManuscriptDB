1     In the name of the …
2     [We,] Athanasios son of [the late Isaac],
3     and [Ezek]ias [the son of the late] Tzamoul, the headm[en] …
4     [of the] plain [of the village] of Tjkoou, write to [our lord]
5     the all-praiseworthy Korra, the most eminent counsellor (symboulos),
6     the most glorious lord, the master Basil[ios] …
7     [the] village Nouot of Tjko[ou] …
8     greeting. We acknowledge that … …
9     timber only, and …
10    … them, twenty-eight …
11    … any man at all of this village, whether he is able to say that we gave anything to you
12    … the matter of the strangers of this kind, nor that … to you, nor is it your business
13    nor is it your servant's business, nor any servant's at all, small or great,
14    … this amount of such a kind. But if any of you should presume, after any time, to bring suit
15    [against you, whether we] ourselves, or our sons, or our brothers, or any person of ours, or any
16    [man] at all, whether claiming against us or apart from us — whoever shall presume,
17    … this pretext, namely the matter of the strangers, whether (claiming) that he gave anything
18    [apart from] the public (tax) and the matter of the assessment-order and the three gold solidi
19    according to his (boats?) that went to Africa … whether before our lord the all-praiseworthy counsellor
20    or before any authority whatever, of the north or the south, (saying) that we gave anything contrary to what we previously declared
21    … (we agree) to be liable to whatever fine they shall impose on him,
22    … we will pay it, apart from any prosecution they shall bring against us, securely, then,
23    [for the] public account, through your lordship, we have drawn up this acknowledgment for you, agreeing
24    [to it, and] to be answerable to you, together with all that is ours, for this matter, swearing by the name
25    [of the] God almighty and by the well-being of the lords who rule, that we will keep
26    [and] guard (it) according to what we previously wrote, and they questioned us and we acknowledged it.
27    And I acknowledge again, I, Athanasios son of Isaac, who wrote first, that I will not pay
28    … the public (tax) to you, on account of the hamlet of the (people) round-about that is assigned to it, beyond the public (taxes)
29    … the timber and the matter of the assessment-order to our lord the all-praiseworthy counsellor
30    … my part of the largess [and the matter] of Africa toward the three gold solidi
31    … according to his (boats), nor that I gave to you, nor did any person of mine give,
32    [nor] is it your servant's (business), small or great, in whatever
33    manner; and that I gave nothing to you for the matter of the stranger that is laid upon him.
34    … the (people) round-about, that we shall be unable to presume to say that I gave to you, neither I
35    [nor] any person of mine at all, that I gave you nothing beyond the public (tax)
36    … the timber and the matter of the assessment-order [and] the matter of Africa only.
37    [And if I should] presume, may it come about that I am liable to any fine and any prosecution
38    [that] they shall impose upon us, and we will be answerable for it, that I gave nothing
39    … [beyond what] I wrote first; I, Phi[lotheos the] son of the late Menas, who wrote first, agree to this acknowledgment according to its force, and acknowledge that I paid nothing in whatever manner beyond
40    [the public taxes] and the matter of the assessment-order of [our lord] the counsellor and the three gold solidi of largess
41    according to his (boats), since he went to Africa, and that I gave nothing for the matter of the strangers which we administered
42    … a part, twenty-eight gold solidi, which were given in common: we, Athanasios son of the late
43    [Isaac, and] Isaac son of the late Tzamoul, who wrote first, agree to this acknowledgment
44    according to its force, and acknowledge that we paid nothing in whatever manner
45    [beyond the] public (taxes) and the matter of the assessment-order of our lord the counsellor and the three [gold] solidi of largess according to his (boats), since he went to Africa, and that I gave nothing
46    [for the] matter of the strangers which we administered, [beyond my] part, twenty-eight gold solidi which we gave in common: I, Ezekias [son of the late] Tzamoul, who wrote first, since Athanasios told me to write on his behalf because he cannot read, and I have written on my own behalf as well.
47    [I,] Andreas, presbyter and abbot, bear witness to this acknowledgment according to its force.
48    [I,] …ros, son of the late Athanasios, the least of presbyters, bear witness to this acknowledgment
49    [according to] its force; I, Pakoui son of …, bear witness to this acknowledgment according to its force.
50    [Zacha]rias (and) John, with God presbyter, bear [witness] to this acknowledgment according to its force.
