[TRANSLATION]
1     Horos, collector of the money-taxes of Charax, (to) Tsonesontis (daughter) of Kolamaus,
2     through Senmouthes (son of) Didymos. I have, for land-survey tax of year 3, impure sixteen drachmas,
3     total 16 dr. Year 4 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
4     Hathyr 26. Heraklas, I have signed.
5     Others, Hadrianos 11, in the name of the same (woman), for land-survey tax of year 3, impure eleven, total 11 dr.
