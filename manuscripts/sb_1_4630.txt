[TRANSLATION]
1     Asinnius Secundus to Apollonius
2     to his brother, greeting.
3     Until now we have awaited you and
4     having received your letters, through which you excused yourself on account of foot pain,
5     nevertheless we were not persuaded, because it will seem to you to abandon us on account of
6     the affection toward your brother Theon, but since you to us
7     today did not come, tomorrow we necessarily will embrace [you], for
8     we are very troubled until we learn how your foot is. From the
9     provisions prepared for us for the [dinner] use freely, brother, the
10    portions of yours: half a roasted fish
11    and [two] birds and two doves.
12    I pray that you are well,
13    most honored brother
14    and lord.
