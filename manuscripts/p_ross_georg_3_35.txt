r.6    [στοιχεῖ μοι ἡ ἀ]ποχὴ ὡς πρόκειται.

[TRANSLATION]
1     [Flavius Pa]ulus, advocate of Antaeus, to the most admirable first-village-headmen
2     of the village of Aphrodite. I have received and been satisfied by you of the customarily given
3     one gold coin at two carats by the gold-working weight, the honored ordinarius
4     on behalf of me from my customary canon of the second indiction and for your security
5     I have [made] this receipt for you as stated. Flavius Paulus, advocate, the aforesaid
6     [the receipt accords with] me as stated.
