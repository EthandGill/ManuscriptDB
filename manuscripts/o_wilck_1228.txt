[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 12, Hathyr 14: there has been paid to the bank in
2     Diospolis Magna, of which Antigenes (is in charge),
3     (the) third (part) of the dovecote (tax) of year 12: Patron and
4     Eudios, through Alexandros son of
5     Pinyris son of Plato,
6     of (the) dovecote, in copper, whose exchange is eight hundred (dr.),
7     total, whose exchange 800. Athenion.
8     (2nd hand) Herakleides: there has been paid, of copper whose [exchange], eight
9     hundred drachmas, total of copper whose exchange 800.
10    Year 12, Hathyr 14.
