r.3    εὐθέως ἐπειδὴ ἔχω αὐτὸν ἐν τῷ ἀλλοτρίῳ παραπεφυλαγμένον· ἤδη γὰρ

r.6    δοῦλος

[TRANSLATION]
1     Having found one fine catfish from the canal, I sent (it) to my
2     master; but write to me the reply concerning the lord Anastasius
3     immediately, since I have him guarded in a foreign place; for already
4     he himself also wrote to my master yesterday, master.
5     With God, to my [master], the most magnificent count [Stephen] Puis, your
6     servant
