[TRANSLATION]
1     To Tiberius Claudius Herodes, strategos,
2     from Theon and Aristandros the elder
3     and Aristandros the younger, the three sons of Aristandros, of those from the city of Oxyrhynchos. Leonides son of Ptolemaios, Althaieus (by deme), who is now at Alexandria, holding our paternal mortgage in the region of the village of Senepta of the middle toparchy,
4     [fr]om the allotment of Damon, forty-
5     [s]ix arouras, in accordance with what was written by him to
6     our father Arist[a]ndros concerning this (land),
7     in a bond which was also mediated by
8     [S]arapion son of Philiskos, and having enjoyed the fruits of it for a sufficient time, so that not only was he
9     [f]rom the produce they yielded paid all that was owed by our father,
10    [but] also still owing us not a little besides
11    … upon the reversal,
12    but also … ,
13    he dared, contrary to what is proper, without [our] [consent]
14    to alienate at Alexandria the
15    mortgage to Philostratos son of Zoilos, to whom, when he had duly
16    taken it over, we delivered a notice of protest through
17    you, concerning his having bought (it) improperly. In addition
18    to these things, the same Leonides, forgetting
19    the accountability that follows upon him, procured from the record-office a memorandum
20    against the local praktor of foreign claims (xenika)
21    as though other sums were owed to him by
22    our father, whereas on the contrary he rather owes us besides, as aforesaid; and he delivered to us a copy of this through
23    his son Apion, on the fourteenth
24    of the present month Epeiph. Wherefore, of necessity coming forward, we request that the memorandum be kept on file, and that
25    a copy of it be delivered through an assistant both to
26    Apion, to the account of his father Leonides,
27    and to the praktor of foreign claims, so that Apion
28    may know that all that has been done by his father Leonides to
29    our injury is invalid and
30    remains (so) for us against Leonides, as regards the claim which we have against him concerning these matters; and that the
31    praktor of foreign claims may take no action against us on the basis of the aforesaid memorandum until trial. For of all the rights belonging
32    to us we hold fast and
33    will hold fast. Theon son of Onnophris.
34    I, the assistant, have delivered a copy of the aforesaid
35    [memo]r[andum] [a co]py to the praktor of foreign cl[aims] … and to Apion in person.
36    [Year ] … [of the Empe]ror Caesar Vespasian
37    … [, ][in the mo]nth Epeiph 14.
