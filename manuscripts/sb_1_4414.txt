[TRANSLATION]
1     In year seven of Emperor Caesar Titus
2     Aelius Hadrian Antoninus Augustus
3     [Pious], of the month Apellaios Phaophi 12. In Karanidis
4     [of the Heraklei]de division of the Arsinoe nome. Acknowledges
5     [Heron without-father of mother] Isidora farmer of 23 klerouchi[es]
6     [as of years] twenty, a scar on the right eye, to the one in the same
7     [kleros] farmer Apollos of Limnaios of Onnophres as of years 30,
8     [a scar] on the right eyebrow to have handed over to him the acknowledging
9     [Hero]n from now for as long as the farming remains to him the time
10    [the] portion falling to him half of which he farms of lands
11    [with] the aforementioned Apollos concerning on the one hand Psenarpsenesis
12    [Ka]meliane estate of arouras five and one-half, and
13    [like]wise Maikenaitiane aroura half quarter and Kerkesoukhon revenue arouras two in place of those regarding [them] measured and written for the public
14    [public] all and of the others of the following, [if] anyone of them transgresses shall give as penalty silver drachmas
15    [a hundred] and to the public the equal [amount]. Undersigned Heron
16    [without-]father of mother Isidora I acknowledge having handed over
17    to Apollos from now for as long as the farming remains to me the time.
18    … los of Erieus I wrote on behalf of him not knowing letters.
19    [has been] registered through the office of Karanidis.
