[TRANSLATION]
1     … the Trinity,
2     consubstantial, and the life-giver; on this day, which is
3     the eighth of the month Parmhotp of the year,
4     [this] thirteenth indiction,
5     [before] … son
6     [of the blessed B]asile, and
7     [Petros the s]on of the blessed
8     Komos, who rule over the fortress of Djeme, while [our] Lord Jesus Christ is [king]
9     over all the world:
10    I, Thanasios, the son
11    of the blessed Peloustre — his mother was There — in the fortress of Djeme, the nome
12    of the city of Hermonth, I write,
13    giving the subscription of the one who shall subscribe on my behalf, with trustworthy witnesses, those
14    who shall testify on my behalf,
15    below, according to my request,
16    my own, of myself; I write
17    to my true brother in this same fortress of Djeme, the same nome of Hermonth; I write
18    to you, apart from any deceit or fear
19    or violence or compulsion
20    upon me, but in my own purpose, of myself, that I give forth to you my share in
21    my plot of land, which is the third part,
22    this which is set on the south,
23    this which came upon me on behalf of my blessed mother There,
24    according to its boundaries which surround it,
25    from the ground up to its appurtenances, in its quarter, on the south being Jeremias the … the north, Anken,
26    on the east the street leading in
27    to the house of Iohannes; on the west,
28    the street leading in, this, where the door of the masters opens onto it; these are
29    its boundaries which surround it, from
30    the ground up to its square (?), surrounding it; and you,
31    Enoch, you shall come in,
32    and you shall be lord, and you shall be lord, and you shall take possession in
33    every lordship over my share of my plot of land, which is the third part
34    of that plot,
35    to build it, to refound it,
36    to demolish it up, to exchange
37    it, to … it for you; in short,
38    every matter that you wish concerning it,
39    so that no man
40    shall be able to come forth against you
41    concerning it ever, whether I, or
42    a son of mine, or a daughter of mine,
43    or a brother, or a sister, or
44    son of one, or son of two, or
45    a field, or a field of fields, who is an heir to mine, or in my family,
46    or outside my family;
47    in short, the one who shall dare
48    at any time ever to come forth against [you] …
49    even through any [other] man
50    acting on my behalf — first
51    that man shall not profit in anything,
52    but he shall be subject to the penalties which our just law determines, which are
53    these: two ounces of gold
54    to the ruler who exacts in
55    that time; and afterwards
56    he shall come in and confirm the validity
57    of [this sale], because you have given
58    its price to me, hand to hand,
59    which is these five tremisses
60    of gold, refined, full
61    by the measure of the fortress, complete.
62    The Almighty, and the salvation
63    of our masters who rule
64    over us now, that I not transgress
65    this written deed of sale,
66    through any authority or power
67    whatsoever, and they shall question me, and I shall acknowledge; this sale is confirmed for you, firm and valid in every place
68    where they shall take it, through
69    any authority or any power,
70    as it stands above. I, Athanasios,
71    the one who first wrote, from above, agree
72    to this sale, with every matter that is written
73    in it, as it stands above.
74    I, Komos, the son of the blessed Hatre,
75    give witness. I, Anchen, the son of Keorke,
76    give witness. I, Komes, [have written] on their behalf, that
77    they do not know how to write. I, Zebeteos, the son
78    of Keorge, give witness. I, Komes, have written
79    on his behalf, that he does not know (how to write) …
80    I, Stephanos, the least archpriest
81    of the holy church of the holy Apa
82    Isidoros, [give] witness, according to the request of Athanasios.
