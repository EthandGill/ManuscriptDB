[TRANSLATION]
1     Vaberius Mundus to Apollonius
2     to the strategos, greetings.
3     Oulpion Malchos, beneficiary
4     [of Ramm]ius the most excellent governor
5     of us, having charge of the
6     [territory] under you, a very good man I commend to you.
7     I hope also that he will give [such] effort, presenting [himself to] you, [as] I have assured. And to him too I wrote,
8     in order that, [if] you should bind him to yourself,
9     [more eagerly] he may offer himself [to you]. I pray you [to fare well], most honored brother.
10    Farewell.
