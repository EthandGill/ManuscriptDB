[TRANSLATION]
1     Maron to Zenon, greetings. If you are well and the rest [is] for you according to wish,
2     it would be as I wish; and be well yourself too. Apollonius writes to me in the memorandum
3     [that] the building [is] under Diodorus, but the [matters] concerning the land [are] under those with Damis. Indeed
4     the building is not yet completed, but the grain-bringing and wood-cutting and sesame[-cultivation]
5     and roasting and castor-bean planting. All these things are expended through Damis and Etearchus
6     and Sostrates the brother of them, and [they] seal the daily expenditure;
7     Diodorus meanwhile makes excuses [concerning] the works not being completed; nonetheless
8     the daily expenditure is also recorded. The other [matters] here will report to you
9     Jason and Panakestor himself, as soon as he might arrive [while] being well.
10    Farewell. Year 29, Pachon 14.
11    Maron. Concerning Diodorus [about] the building
12    and Damis concerning the land.
13    Year 29, Daisios 14, in Alexand[ria].
14    To Zenon
