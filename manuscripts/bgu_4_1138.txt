[TRANSLATION]
1   To [L]ucius _(?)_
2   [f]rom Neikanor son of Sositheos, of those from Elbonthis. From the
3   [co]unter-suit that arose for me against Ischyrion, the proxy-
4   bearer (?) ... ...o( ) epistr[ategos] and
5   you gave us as judge Kaasios, and from the things shown
6   by me before Casius, in the presence also of Ischyrion,
7   and again before Casius, when he (Ischyrion) became a denier
8   on the ground that he had not handed over Papias,
9   for the sake of the extortion it seemed good to Casius, being there,
10   to summon Chairemon the jailer of Sais
11   and (to make) his examination for the conviction of Ischyrion, who did wrong;
12   the jailer Chairemon testified concerning Papias
13   having been handed over to him against the 1600 drachmas of silver,
14   and after these things, when Ischyrion came, (he said) that he had said
15   to him: ‘...leia( ) pleeo( ) ...p...isai tou...( ) I shall re-
16   [lea]se Papias from the prison’ .... I ask you,
17   the savior of all and protector, if it seems good to you, to or-
18   der Casius to be brought befo[re y]ou and, having taken from him
19   the one who has departed, to compel [I]schyrion to repay
20   to me the 1600 silver drachmas of the extortion and to rebuke him fittingly,
21   so that I may be protected.
