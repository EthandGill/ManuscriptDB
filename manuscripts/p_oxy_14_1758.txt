[TRANSLATION]
1     Diogenis to Didyma the
2     Most honored, very much greetings.
3     Before all I pray you to be healthy with the blessed
4     Your children, and the obeisance of you I make before the
5     Great Sarapis praying for you the most beaut[iful] things for your whole house. You will do well having secured
6     The earth which Hatres my farmer excavated from the
7     Grounds of mine, and do not allow
8     To be built upon until
9     I come. Let it be a concern for you
10    And also concerning which others I have
11    With you pending [to] withhold, so that I not be easily despised through the [neglect] of mine. I embrace [all]
12    Whom you happily have …
13    Give [regards] to Didym[a].
