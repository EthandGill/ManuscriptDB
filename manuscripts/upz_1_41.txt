[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy and Queen Cleopatra, his sister,
2     to the gods Philometor, greetings: Thaueis and Taous, twins,
3     who serve in the great Sarapeum at Memphis.
4     And indeed at the time, O mighty king, having been preserved according to justice
5     from the outer regions, you went up to the sacred [place] to sacrifice,
6     we approached you bringing forward [the fact that] we should not receive what is due
7     us to be given: one measure of sesame oil, one measure of castor
8     oil, and we asked [you] to order Sarapion
9     the subdioiketes to give [them] to us. You, through the piety which you have
10    toward the divine and [your] kindness toward all people,
11    gave orders through which you made
12    for our petition a written order to give to us.
13    Then indeed through you, gracious gods,
14    we received the two measures that were owed us,
15    which were from the 19th year. But what should be given to us for the 20th
16    and 21st year
17    oil and castor those in charge of these things have not distributed,
18    and although you have shown the greatest care for us
19    [in this matter]. We beseech you, O mightiest gods,
20    to send our petition to Dionysius of the
21    friends and strategus, so that he write to Apollonius the
22    epistates now to give to us and for the future
23    to maintain in order, so that having everything that comes next we may perform
24    the libations to the mightiest god Sarapis and the other [things]
25    that are customary we may perform on behalf of both you and your
26    children. May you attain all that
27    you undertake.
28    Farewell.
