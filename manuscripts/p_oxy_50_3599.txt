[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Ap[ollonius the most illustrious] …
2     Choiak 13.
3     To Flavius Dorotheos the reverend, town-councillor of the
4     city of Upper Kynopolis, son of Ioseph of excellent
5     memory, (from) the Aurelii Timagenes son of Philoxenos,
6     his mother being Anthousa, and Mouses son of Phoibammon, his mother being
7     Thekla, fuller, from the splendid and most splendid
8     city of the Oxyrhynchites, greeting. We acknowledge that we have received from you by mutual surety
9     on loan, by hand, from your house, for our own
10    and pressing need, in gold, one plain, approved,
11    full-weight solidus, total one gold solidus of principal,
12    which one gold solidus, being free from
13    every risk, we shall of necessity repay by mutual surety
14    at the dissolution (i.e. close) of the holy Easter of the month Pharmouthi of the
15    present year 137 = 106 of the current fourteenth indiction, without delay and without any
16    dispute, the right of execution being yours
17    against us, being mutually responsible [for] the repayment, and against whichever
18    of us you choose, and against all our
19    property. Valid is the bond, written singly,
20    and on being questioned we acknowledged. The Aurelii
21    Timagenes son of Ph[i]loxenos, his mother being Anthousa, and Mouses
22    son of Phoibammon, his mother being Thekla, fuller,
23    the aforesaid, we have received on loan the
24    one gold solidus of principal, an[d] we shall repay by mutual surety
25    at the appointed time as stated [above. A]urelius Philoxenos son of Iohannes,
26    having been requested, wrote on their [behalf, they being pre]sent, they not knowing letters.
27    … …
28    (Bond) of Timagenes son of Philoxenos, dyer, and Mouses son of Phoibammon
29    … from the Ox(yrhynchite) …, one gold solidus.
