[TRANSLATION]
1     There leased Aurelius Sarapion
2     also called Dionysotheon, former gymnasiarch, councillor of the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
3     to Aurelius Cornelius
4     son of Kollouthos, his mother Tanesneo[s],
5     from the hamlet of Plerous, for
6     the present 2nd year only, the five arouras belonging to him around the village of Thosbis, previously cultivated by the same lessee,
7     so as to sow with wheat, at a fixed rent of twenty-one artabas of wheat,
8     free of all risk,
9     the public dues on the land being
10    upon the landholder, who is owner
11    of the crops until he recovers the
12    wheat. And, the lease being guaranteed,
13    let the lessee measure out into the public granary at his own expense and with all charges, of which let him give a clean receipt free from all,
14    by the first measurement of the present
15    year, without postponement, the exaction being made
16    both from the lessee and
17    from all his possessions.
18    The lease is valid, and on being questioned
19    [the l]essee assen[te]d. Year 2 of Imperator
20    [Cae]sar Gaius Messius Quin[tus]
21    [Tra]ianus Decius Pius [F]e[lix]
22    [an]d [Q]uintus Herennius E[tr]us[cus]
23    Messius Decius and Gaius
24    Valens Hostilianus
25    Messius Quintus, the
26    most august Caesars
27    Augusti, Phaophi 14.
