[TRANSLATION]
1     Menas son of Eudaimon, and (each other) acknowledge to one another,
2     mother Apia, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
3     residing in the village of Psobthis of the
4     eastern toparchy, collector of money
5     village revenues of the aforesaid Psobthis of the places, and Theon son of Horion
6     (son) of Ammonios, mother Tanachotis,
7     from the same city of the Oxyrhynchites, the one,
8     Menas, (acknowledges) to have contracted with Theon the
9     secretaryship of the aforesaid collectorship and the collection of all that
10    is set forth by the royal scribe of the nome upon the same collectorship
11    of money village genuine revenues
12    from the reckoning of the present month
13    Tybi until the reckoning of the month Choiak of the
14    coming 20th year, on condition that the same Theon
15    shall make the collection and the settling-up of the
16    [villag]e genuine revenues, being subordinate … each month … in the collection
17    and delivering to the strategos of the nome the monthly account. And further also receipts
18    shall be issued to those who pay, the insolvent
19    and hard-to-collect being reported by
20    Theon to the village-scribe of the places, the pay agreed upon between
21    one another, together with the price of papyrus and writing-fees
22    of the books of the collectorship and the registration of these with the
23    accountant of the nome and any others to whom it pertains, being five hundred and twenty drachmas of silver, of which
24    Theon has received here forty drachmas. And the remaining four hundred [ei]ghty drachmas the same Theon shall receive from the sa[m]e Menas at the
25    appointed terms: Pharmouthi 16, one hundred
26    drachmas; Epeiph, one hundred and eighty drachmas;
27    and at the year's end, Phaophi, the remaining
28    two hundred drachmas. And Theon (agrees) to consent to these things and to do each thing as above declared, it being permitted to none of the acknowledging parties to transgress the af[oresaid. Val]id (is)
29    th[e agree]ment. Year 19 of [the Emperor]s
30    Caes[ars Lucius Septimius Severus]
31    Per[tinax Arabicus Adiabenicus]
32    Par[thicus Britannicus the Greatest]
33    and Marcus [Aurelius Antoninus]
