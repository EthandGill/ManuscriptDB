[TRANSLATION]
1     I beseech your most glorious and
2     all-wise patronage to order [that there] be given
3     to George the honored chartulary the coins
4     fifteen Alexandrian [coins] of my wa[ge]
5     of the twelfth distribution, since
6     [I] ordered him [to do] something in Oxyrhynchus, so that
7     and for this [reason] I may thank her. But, as
8     has been said, habitually granting me the greatest [favors]
9     [may] provide these to him, writing me continually
10    her health and about the matters to her
11    seeming appropriate here, [you] being eager
12    to bring to completion all that is commanded to me by her.
13    … to master Thomas sy[mmachus]
14    [let this] be handed over to master Marinus the most glorious
15    for the sake of the 15 coins for wages of indiction 12.
