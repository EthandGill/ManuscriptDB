[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonius, village scribe of Theadelphia
2     from Pnepheros of Phanemies, public farmer
3     of age 63, dark-skinned, round-faced, scar on right eye. I am registering
4     myself for the 11th year of Caesar
5     paying the tax-contribution,
6     but in Esenremphieis
7     I reside. Wherefore I present this petition, so that
8     it may be registered.
9     Year 11 of Caesar, Mechir 3.
10    It was presented year 11 of Caesar, Pachon 2.
