[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Diocletian Augustus for the 5th time and Maximian Augustus for the 4th time.
2     Aurelius Castor also called Eudaimon, councillor of the most splendid city of Antinoöpolis, with the co-warrantor and surety who also guarantees the sale and confirms the written stipulations, Aurelius Hermapollon also called Dioscoros son of Didymos, of Hermopolis, registered in the Fort quarter
3     of the east, I have followed this payment-order and have received from Aurelia Kyrillous daughter of Hierax, from the same city of Hermes, registered
4     in the quarter
5     of the same, transacting business without a guardian by right of children, her husband Aurelius Hermeias also called Kronios, former exegetes,
6     food-controller,
7     councillor, appointed gymnasiarch, of the same city of Hermes, being party to her, for the price at which I sold to her a slave-girl owned (by me), by name Stephane
8     surnamed Stephanous, or by whatever name she is called or shall be called, Cretan by race, about 20 years old, straight-nosed, having a scar above the ankle of the right foot, whom I handed over to her such as she is, not subject to return, free of the sacred disease and of liability to claim, in accordance with the agreement of sale to be registered through the record-office in Hermopolis, of new silver of the Augusti's coinage, fifteen talents,
9     total 15 silver talents in full, and having been asked I acknowledged. Year 10 of the Emperor Caesar Gaius Aurelius Valerius Diocletianus
10    and year 9 of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Valerius Maximianus, Germanici Maximi, Sarmatici Maximi, and year 2
11    of Flavius Valerius Constantius and Galerius Valerius Maximianus, the most illustrious Caesars, Pii Felices Augusti, Thoth 13.
12    Aurelius Castor also called Eudaimon, councillor, I have sold the aforesaid slave-girl by name Stephane and I have received of the
13    price fifteen talents of silver coin in full as is stated above. Aurelius Hermapollon
14    also called Dioscoros son of Didymos, I act as surety and co-warrant and having been asked I acknowledged.
