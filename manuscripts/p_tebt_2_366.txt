[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 28 of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
2     Antoninus Caesar our lord, Pauni 30.
3     Ponnis [son] of Zoilus and Eutychus from the Komarites and associates of the village of Tebtynis, we have measured [grain] from the produce of the same year [with] the public dry measure
4     for Eutychus [son] of Hermes, [a] settler, of wheat, artabas 14 and 1/8, and
5     Taarmiyses [son] of Pakebkis, [a] settler, of wheat, artabas 6 and 1/8, the same
6     Hieras, [a] settler, of wheat, artabas 4 and 1/24,
7     and Epiphi 9, for Cronus [son] of Heracleios, [a] resident
8     of wheat, artabas 7 and 1/12, the same [person], a guardian, of wheat, artaba 1 and 1/4 and 1/24, and
9     for Sarapammon [son] of Eutychus, formerly [called after] Eirene
10    [son] of Areius, [a] resident, of wheat, artabas 6 and 1/8. In total of the settlement together with [those] additionally measured, of wheat, artabas 39 and 1/2 and 3/8.
