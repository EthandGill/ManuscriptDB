[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the] lord and master Jesus Christ
2     [our God and Savior], in the reign of
3     [our most pious and benevolent] master and greatest
4     [benefactor Flavius] Heraclius the eternal Augustus and Emperor,
5     in the twenty-[fourth year], Tybi …, of the seventh indiction, in Arsinoe.
6     [Aurelius Joha]nnes son of Gerontios, from the hamlet
7     … [of the Ar]sinoite nome, to the most illustrious
8     [Zacharias the] administrator of this same property of mine, son
9     [of the blessed A]pphous, from the city of the Arsinoites, [greeting.]
10    [I acknowledge] of my own free will, without deceit and force
11    [and trickery] and compulsion, that I have received and have been paid in full
12    [from] your illustriousness all the things given
13    [by] me at the time when you took over
14    [the administration] of this same property, in full
15    [and in] entirety, and for the future I have no claim,
16    [neither I nor] my heirs nor any other person on
17    [my] behalf, against the same most illustrious Zacharias or against
18    [his succe]ssors concerning any other cause whatsoever
19    [or any] matter, written or unwritten, the whole of it.
20    [And for your] security I have made for you this
21    release, which is valid, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged it.
22    [Aurelius Joh]annes son of Gerontios, all the
23    [things] written above are agreeable to me as stated above.
24    [ ] [by me, Justus son of Pa]nuphis, it was signed; by me, Justus the notary.
