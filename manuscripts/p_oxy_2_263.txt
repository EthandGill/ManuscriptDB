1   To the agoranomoi …, fr[om]
2   Bakche daughter of Hermon, a citizen-woman, with (her) guardian
3   Diognetos son of Dionysios, of the Epiphanean (deme).
4   I swear by Imperator Caesar Vespasia[n]
5   Augustus that I have sold to Heliodora,
6   daughter of Heliodora, with (her) guardian, her husband
7   Apollonios son of Dionysios son of Dionysios
8   also called Didymos, the slave-girl belonging to me,
9   Sarapous, about eight years old, free from any claim,
10   except for the sacred disease (epilepsy) and seizure(-claim),
11   (and that she) is mine and is neither pledged
12   nor alienated to others
13   in any way; and that I have received
14   the price, of silver, drachmas
15   six hundred and forty, and that I will guaran-
16   tee (it). If I keep my oath, may it be well with me; if I
17   [fo]rswear, the opposite. I, Diogne-
18   tos son of Dionysios, of the E[p]iphanean (deme), have been registered
19   as her [g]uardian and have written
20   on her behalf, as she does not know letter[s].
21   Year nine of Imperator Caesar
22   Vespasian Augustus, Pharmo[uthi]
