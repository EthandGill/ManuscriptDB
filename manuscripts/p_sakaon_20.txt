[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Euporas [councillor], receiver of grain
2     bakery of those stationed here
3     most noble soldiers under Dion
4     chief butler through me [Aurelius] Neileus, councillor
5     to Aurelius Selpout and Sakaon and the
6     colleagues, grain-collectors of the village Theadelphia
7     greetings.
8     I received from you for the sixth indiction
9     by measured modius, swept, of pure wheat with accompanying [items] 10
10    artabas forty-three, total artabas 43.
11    In the consulship of our masters Constantine
12    Augustus [for] the fifth [time] and Licinius [for]
13    the most illustrious Caesar [for] the first [time], Tybi 24.
14    Neileus, I have signed.
