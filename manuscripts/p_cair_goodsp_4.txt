[TRANSLATION]
1     Pancrates to Philo[xeneus]
2     greetings. If you are well and
3     things are going well for you according to reason,
4     that would be as we wish, and
5     we ourselves are in good health.
6     Concerning the things which we wished
7     we sent to you
8     Glaucias, being our
9     own [man] who will confer with you.
10    You will do well, therefore, listening
11    to him and showing [him] concerning the things he has come about,
12    but especially taking care of yourself so that you be well.
13    Be well. Year 29, Phamenoth 8.
14    To Philoxeneus.
