[TRANSLATION]
1     Diomedes to Serenus, the most honored
2     son, greetings.
3     the from you going to be to us
4     care accurately I knew and from Heliodorus likewise I learned and from your letters, which having trusted and to the artisan's promise Cretan I suppose to have obtained,
5     and thus on the work to appear I pray,
6     if indeed from the much working the fibers more became than the pure.
7     to fare well you I pray and to write
8     about which if you may wish. Demetrios greets you and all my [people] and the friends and
9     acquaintances. Fare well to me,
10    most honored one.
