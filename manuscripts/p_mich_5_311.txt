[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twentieth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
2     in the month Hyperberetaios 4, Mesore intercalary 4, in Talei of the Polemon division of the Arsinoite nome. There leased
3     Horos the elder son of Apollonios, a Persian of the
4     epigone, about 35 years old, with a scar on his right knee,
5     to Ptolemaios son of Apion, about 26 years old, with a scar in the middle of his forehead,
6     from those which Horos himself also farms around Theogonis of public soils of land, five arouras in one parcel, in the so-called schoinion of Meleleus of ninety-six cubits, for sowing of hay, with the roots, and
7     cutting [and] drying, for the coming twenty-first year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
8     Ptolemaios furnishing for himself
9     the seed, but the lessor making
10    the irrigations and embankment-works and the care of the green (crops), for
11    which Horos has also received the price
12    forthwith as a pre-payment through hand from house, and Horos guarantees the
13    lease from public and private (claims) and every kind. Subscriber for the lessor, Orseous son of Papontos, about 22 years old, with a scar on his right shin.
14    Horos the elder son of Apollonios, a Persian
15    [of the e]pigone, I acknowledge that I have leased
16    to Pt[olemaios] son of Apion, from those which I farm around
17    Theogonis of public soils of land,
18    five arouras in one parcel, in the
19    so-called schoinion of Meleleus
20    of ninety-six cubits, for sowing of ha[y], with the roots, and cutting
21    (and) drying, for the coming twenty-first year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
22    and I have received the price as a pre-payment
23    through hand from house, and I guarantee with every guarantee from public (claims) as stated above.
24    I wrote on his behalf Orseus son of Papontos
25    because he does not know letters.
26    Ptolemaios son of Apion, the lease has come to me
27    as stated above. Year 20 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
28    Mesore intercalary 4. Registered through Hermias at the record-office
29    of Talei and other villages.
30    Horos, about 35 years old, with a scar on his right knee.
31    Subscriber Orseous, about 22 years old, with a scar on his right shin.
32    Lease of 5 arouras of Ptolemaios son of Apion
33    against Horos
