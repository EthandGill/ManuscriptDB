r.2    Ψάις Πετοσίριος Ψενααμούνιος ἀργυρίου δραχμὰς β ὀβολοὺς ἡμιωβέλιον χαλκοῦς δ.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 2: he registered on account of the tax of two drachmae.
2     Psais son of Petosiros son of Senamonios: silver two drachmae, half-obol, four chalkoi.
3     Aurelius Hierax, also called Serapion, ten-man magistrate, wrote [this].
4     Gaius Julius Serapion, ten-man magistrate: I have affixed my seal.
