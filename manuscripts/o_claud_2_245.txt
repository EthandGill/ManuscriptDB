[TRANSLATION]
1     Peteseneph[otis to Valerius]
2     to the brother [many greetings. Do]
3     well, brother, if [the caravan comes]
4     the caravan this night, send to me
5     three pairs of loaves; since I do not have bread and when the caravan comes I will send them to you. [I greet]
6     Kolophon. Do well, brother, when the caravan comes, ask for the bronze of the vessels
7     and send to me the other
8     drachmas 9 in order that you might be paid that which
9     you gave to me. Farewell.
