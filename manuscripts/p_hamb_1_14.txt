[TRANSLATION]
1     To Didas, former exegetes, and Mystes, former kosmetes, both councillors, keepers of the property-records of the Arsinoite (nome),
2     from Heraïs, freedwoman of Apollonios son of Ptolemaios Traianios also called Ktesios, of those in the city of Antinoöpolis,
3     of the sacred victors and tax-exempt, registered in the Hermouthiake quarter, with as guardian her husband Sarapion also called Demetrios son of Didas son of Ischyrion, of the Sosikosmios tribe [also called]
4     Pelieus. The two-thirds share, common and undivided, of a two-towered house — which I registered through the former record-keeper in the metropolis [in the] quarter of the Topoi of Dionysios —
5     being in mortgage [through] you, …, also called Serenos, former chief-priest, councillor of the city of the Arsinoites, son of Sarapion, former exegetes of the same city, from the quarter of [Dionysios]
6     Topoi, for a principal of one thousand
7     five hundred drachmas of silver and other (drachmas) on a plain loan,
8     three hundred drachmas at drachma-interest: I wish to alienate to Sarapion also called Ammonios son of Sarapion [also called]
9     Demetrios, former eutheniarch, … … …
10    at a price of two thousand drachmas of silver. And of the price
11    of two thousand drachmas of silver, that I have left with Sarapion also called Ammonios one thousand eight hundred drachmas, on condition that he repay to the aforesaid creditor
12    the principal owed on the mortgage and the plain loan, and the interest.
13    I, Heraïs, have submitted the alienation of the two-thirds share of the house at the Topoi of Dionysios, two-towered, at a price of two thousand drachmas of silver, from which I have left with the purchaser one thousand eight hundred drachmas
14    going toward the repayment as is set forth. I, Sarapion, wrote also on behalf of my wife who does not [know letters].
