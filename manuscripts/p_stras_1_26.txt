[TRANSLATION]
1     to my master [and] father Hatres
2     elder Heraclammun
3     the most brilliant.
4     make haste to provide Hermes,
5     my [servant] carrying the two
6     coins, which you owe
7     me, and after the return of these from Phibammun [son] of Papnouthis
8     receive the sale, for the
9     time limit of the deposit
10    has been set. therefore do not be negligent, for the matter does not admit of delay.
11    to fare well [you]
12    I pray [for you] many
13    times.
