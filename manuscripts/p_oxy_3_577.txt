[TRANSLATION]
1     Paternal part
2     Of Pammenus of Paradise
3     I[n the city of Oxyrhynchus of the Theba]is before the ago[ranomoi] …
4     [They] purchased … from Sara[piw]n … in the street a two-thirds part … and from there Sarapion is to receive … the amounts agreed upon for the price … of silver 200 drachmas of full weight, 15 talents of bronze; [he] sells and guarantees Sarapion [the] two-thirds part … which he will also provide clear from all registration and from royal and crown farming and every kind throughout in the same street.
