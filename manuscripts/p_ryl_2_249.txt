[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 52, Pachon 21. [It] has been assigned to the bank in the city of Crocodiles, on which Apollonis [is in charge of] the tenth, a circulating [tax], according to the
2     declaration of Paniskos and Kephalon, tax-collectors, under which Polydeukes the registrar signs
3     Nechouthes, who is also Eunomos, [son] of Patseous, of a house [having been] built and fitted with doors and roofed
4     above the gate, which Patseous his father set [there], [valued at] 2 drachmas of copper, of which the exchange [is] 200
