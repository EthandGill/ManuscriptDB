[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus … [Didymos son of Hermaios acknowledges,]
2     registered in the quarter of the City of Apeliotes, being about 41 years old … out of … [with his guardian]
3     her own brother Tothoes, of the same parents, being about (… ) years old … [that the acknowledging party has sold] … [arourai] …
4     a quarter and a sixty-fourth, which he will also hand over in the field, sown and full …
5     of three (?), as the seller dictated, on the south the heirs of Theon son of Theon … [and the price agreed between one another]
6     agreed upon, of silver of the imperial coinage, one thousand drachmas … [the seller has received] … [and that the ownership of the arourai]
7     of the quarter and sixty-fourth, the ownership and possession (shall belong) to the buyer [and] those acting for [her] …
8     the produce each year and all that shall accrue, from now on for all time …
9     of the guardian (matters) being against the seller, but those from the present sixth year …
10    to the seller, who shall also not bring suit nor any other on his behalf against the buyer …
11    anyone proceeding against the buyer or those acting for her, both the damages [and] expenses …
12    the (arourai) belonging to me from the … of the cleruchy of Aristodoros (?) around … a [sixty-]fourth, being in common and undivided arourai … (?)
13    I, Didymos son of Hermaios, have sold and received the price and …
14    and share … agora(nomos)
