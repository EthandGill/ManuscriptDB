# HGV-EN: ‘To Gerontius, strategus of the Arsinoite nome from Annious, senator, overseer of the wine supply. I hereby admit swearing by the fortune of our lords the Emperors and Augusti that I have just now collected from the undermentioned from the crop of the - year 17,816 Italian sextarii and from residents in the villages 13,170 Italian sextarii, the which I will distribute in full…. I take the oath and in reply to the formal question give my assent. (In detail: names).’
