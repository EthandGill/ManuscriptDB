1     Measure into the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 15 of Hadrian
2     Caesar the lord, Mesore 21,
3     in the name of Senmouthis (child of) Pikos

5     one and a twelfth,
6     total wheat 1 1/12. I, Pikos, have signed.
