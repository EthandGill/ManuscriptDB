[TRANSLATION]
1     Heron to Pollarion the
2     dearest, greetings.
3     your lack of zeal I did not
4     now learn, but also from
5     that time, from which you were saying to me to send the wood. But also
6     now try, lord,
7     to dispatch to me the
8     radishes and the geese.
9     Force Amatis to send also the wood.
10    But if you do not find six of those
11    of the radishes, show me
12    better. I am concerned for your
13    unbegrudged [bond].
14    Be well.
15    To Pollarion
