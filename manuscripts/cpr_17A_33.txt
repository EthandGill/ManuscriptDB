[TRANSLATION]
1     [From Aurelius Lysymachus and Choutos and] … [and] … [of the]
2     [all from village Prektes of the same district, being magistrates of the same village, we] give [and declare those written] below [working with] [us who will do all the things toward]
3     the village sought public orders
4     and collection of the public taxes, being suitable and fit for each need at our risk we who have made their nomination. And they are:
5     Polyus son of Silvanus through me Lysymachus
6     Phobis son of Herakles through me Choutos
7     Kastor son of Polydeukes through me …
8     Sarmates son of Silvanus through [me] …
9     whom we vouch for [and will present blamelessly]
10    with us obeying [all the things sought toward the village] orders [requisitions. But if any of] them [desert and we do not present,]
11    [we] ourselves [will bear the] account [on his behalf]
12    [will guarantee and when questioned we have agreed.]
