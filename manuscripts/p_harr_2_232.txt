r.1    παρὰ Πλουτάρχου Φιλαντινόῳ προνοητῇ χαίρειν.
r.2    δὸς σιτο[λό]γοις Τεξεὶ ὑπὲ[ρ] ἀσπόρου πυρ[οῦ ἀρτάβας]

[TRANSLATION]
1     From Plutarch to Philantinous the administrator greetings.
2     Give to the grain collectors Texi for unsown wheat [artabae]
3     eight, [making] artabae 8. I have noted [this].
4     year 20 and 12 Epei[ph] 14.
5     dispatch of grain of year 20 [and] year 12, Thoth, for expenses of year 21 and year 13.
