[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name] of God: Abdella the amir to the greaters of Psophthes. (You) wished to sell
2     …to [s]on Asla of the tribe Kouta, three coins, hay each of two arouras and stable
3     [And exp]ense of three pallicari, each per month, bread one artaba. Choiak 30, indiction 1
4     Via the village coins from Sop[h]thes 3 and pallicari 3
