[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Pasion, son of Amonianos, [from] the
2     Arsinoite city, to Aurelius Isas
3     and Iekone from the Aphroditopolite
4     city, greetings.
5     I acknowledge having leased to you the
6     arouras belonging to me, two arouras 2
7     in a lot called Africanus, rent of each aroura two artabae 2, I the one who leased reaping with the rent,
8     this lease, valid,
9     being everywhere enforceable
10    and being questioned I acknowledged.
11    in the consulship of our lords
12    Constantine and Licinius
13    Augustuses the 3rd, Hathyr 30.
14    Aurelius Pasion, I have leased as stated above. Aurelius
15    Amonius wrote on his behalf.
