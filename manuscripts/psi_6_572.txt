[TRANSLATION]
1     [Pataikion] [to Zenon] [greetings].
2     I have sent you Harmyris carrying the documents, five drachmas [and] five obol.
3     and concerning the garment
4     Porphyrion showed [it] to me,
5     and to others I showed [it] to experienced [people]. They say [it] is worthy to be
6     sixty drachmas; so much also Porphyrion said to be sold.
7     I have therefore written to you in order that you might know.
8     Be well. Choiach 1.
9     Of year 35, Choiach 30. Paitaikion to Zenon.
