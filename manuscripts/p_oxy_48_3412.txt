[TRANSLATION]
1     Horion to Dorotheos, aide
2     greetings.
3     I sent to you Gerontios, a soldier
4     and Demetrios, ally of the strategos
5     so that you might compel the accountable ones
6     to supply them with wax, pounds 10, of the wax […]
7     [...] of Alexandria. But see
8     that you do not detain them for one hour.
9     and send the money through Kopreos
10    on today. Farewell.
