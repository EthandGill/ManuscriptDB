r.2    παράσχου τοῖς κυρίοις μοῦ σπεκουλάτορσι Ἀμμωνίῳ καὶ Αθ …

[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Artemidorus, [Hori]on greets [you].
2     Provide for my lords the soldiers Ammonius and Ath[...]
3     [of] meat, two hundred pounds, totaling 200 pounds only.
4     [In the] year 62, Hathyr 18, the same Horion [I] have noted meat, 200 pounds only.
