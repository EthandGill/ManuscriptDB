[TRANSLATION]
1     Onnophris [and] Pbichis to Agathonikos [and Epimachos], greeting. Of the memorandum from Dionysios son of Kephalas, one of the
2     royal cultivators from the village of Tenis in the Mochite (district), a copy is appended below.
3     Since, then, the most favorable season for sowing has come, do not arrest him, but leave him undisturbed
4     in accordance with the orders given concerning these matters, until he has finished with the sowing.
5     Farewell. Year 10, Thoth 25.
6     To Onnophris [and] Pbichis, royal scribes, from Dionysios son of Kephalas, royal cultivator, one of those from the village of Tenis also called Akoris in the Mochite (district).
7     On account of the reasons that will be shown in the matter — since I, [and] my mother Senabollous, drew up
8     for Admetos also called Chesthotes, one of those from the same village, a contract of loan through the record-office,
9     of 150 artabas of wheat, in the 9th year; and not only that, but I also deposited with him in trust, against certain bare
10    plots which I hold, a contract of mortgage; the accused, having become master of the transactions,
11    brought none of the matters in dispute between me and him to completion, on account of which no small loss
12    has resulted to me through him; and now, taking up an unjust stance, seeing that I am preoccupied with the
13    sowing of the land I cultivate, making raids against me, he does not allow me
14    to attend to the farming, contrary to the orders given many times concerning us cultivators; wherefore, the land being in danger of going out of cultivation, I have been compelled, being at present unable
15    to litigate with him concerning the transactions, to make my recourse to you. I request,
16    if it seems good, that you arrange to write to Agathonikos [and] Epimachos, the collectors of the alien-tax, that if
17    the accused attempts to give surety for me to them, they not arrest me until, from the
18    sowing, I have come and established with him the account concerning everything; and this
19    being done, none of the things useful to the king will be lost, and I shall be assisted.
20    Farewell.
21    To Agathonikos.
22    To Epimachos.
