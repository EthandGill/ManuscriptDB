[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To Aurelius Julius Maximus, decurio, from Isidora, daughter of Asklas from the village of Karanis. On 12th of the present month Pachon Ptolemaios, son of Ischyras, came up to the house of myself and my brothers, who serve in the army, and stole everything I have, at the value of not less than four hundred silver drachmas. Therefore I submit (this petition) and request that he be brought before you and that I may obtain justice from you. Farewell. Year [x – – –].
