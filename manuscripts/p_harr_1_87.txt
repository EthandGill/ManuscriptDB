[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Flavius Anatholius the most illustrious, Epeiph 3.
2     Aurelius Penes son of Aputius, mother Arous, from the village
3     Sepho of the Oxyrhynchite nome, to Aurelius Haryotes
4     son of Horion from the estate of Hiera in Upper Cynopolite, greetings.
5     I acknowledge that I have from you with me and owe to you
