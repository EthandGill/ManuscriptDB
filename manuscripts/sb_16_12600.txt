[TRANSLATION]
1     In the ninth year of Imperator Caesar
2     Domitianus Augustus Germanicus, Choiak
3     30 according to account 21. [it] has been written through Tryphon … [the] Satabus Orsenouphis
4     of Satabo[s], mother Tasiaipia, [for] earthwork operations
5     of Soknopiaios Island, eighth year, of silver drachmas six [and a] four-obol piece,
6     [they] total drachmas 6 [and a] four-obol piece, inscribed additionally and symbolically.
