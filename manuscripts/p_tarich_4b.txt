[TRANSLATION]
1     To the chrematistai (judges) of whom
2     Melas is the introducer, from
3     Am[e]n[neus and O]nnophris son of Teos, embalmers from Tanis. We submitted to you a memorandum
4     on Thoth 15 against [Abykis]
5     son of Horos, and Kele[chon]
6     and Pasis son of Ps[en]emous, embalmers [from Phila]delphia, against whom we have already been bringing charges for 5 months
7     concerning royal (revenues) which they hold, pretending
8     and in what manner they defrauded Drimylos, so that
9     it was written that we be carried off
10    (and) led away. He wrote to Kleon
11    the chief-of-police
12    of Tanis, and we were handed over
13    to the police for arrest, we having
14    the letter from Protarchos
15    and the (letter)
16    of Bakchon the dioiketes,
17    they wishing to take away
18    from us the letters,
19    and concerning all
20    who came to meet us …
21    not abiding by the
22    orders (given) by the king, because they possess
23    gold [and si]lver. We ask you
24    to write to Iason the epistates to send to you Kleon
25    the chief-of-police [to whom]
26    we were handed over, and the
27    police to whom he handed us over, whose names (are)
28    Eserches, Eu[po]ros, Onnophris,
29    Herak[l]eides the desert-[guard],
30    who [is a witness, so that] they may be questioned [about these matters,]
31    in order that, if we prove our case,
32    they may meet with what they must meet with,
33    having disregarded the
34    king's orders,
35    having detained us
36    while our lawsuit is pending
37    before the chrematistai.
38    Farewell.
