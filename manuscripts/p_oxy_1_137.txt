[TRANSLATION]
1   † In the reign of our most divine and most pious lord Fl(avius) Tib[er]ius
2   Maurice, the eternal Augustus and Imperator, year 3, after the
3   consulship of our lord of divine memory, Tiberius
4   Constantine, year 6, Tybi 15, indiction 2.
5   To the most magnificent heirs of him of glorious memory,
6   Apion, former chief patrician, landholders also here
7   in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas the servant,
8   who puts the question and secures for his own masters, the
9   same all-praiseworthy men, the right of action and obligation —
10   Aurelius Ptollion, son of Anouthios, his mother being Nonna, hailing
11   from the hamlet of Ambious of the Oxyrhynchite nome, belonging
12   to your magnificence, a registered tenant-farmer of it, gr[ee]ting.
13   A need having now arisen for the landlord’s water-wheel under my charge,
14   called (the wheel) of the plot of Anianos, drawing water onto arable land,
15   for one axle, going up to the city I asked
16   your magnificence to order the said axle
17   to be furnished me; and at once your magnificence, taking
18   thought for the maintenance of its own affairs,
19   credited me its price in my accounts — a new, serviceable,
20   satisfactory pumping-axle. I received (it) toward the completion of all
21   the machine’s parts on this present day, which is Tybi
22   the fifteenth of the present second indiction, for the water-supply of the crops of the
23   (by God’s help) third tax-period, the said axle to serve the irrigations
24   for a seven-year period; and the old one having been given to the doorkeeper. The bond is valid,
25   written in single copy, and on question I acknowledged it. I, Ptollion son of Anouthios — this bond is agreeable to me
26   as aforesaid. I, Papnouthios, wrote on behalf of (him) being illiterate. † Total:
27   one axle only. † † †
28   † Bond of Ptollion son of Anouthios, from the hamlet of Ambious,
29   for receipt of one axle. †
