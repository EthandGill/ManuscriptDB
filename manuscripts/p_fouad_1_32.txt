[TRANSLATION]
1     To Isidoros, strategos,
2     from Theon son of Pausirion, also
3     called Germanios, son of Isas, his mother being Tausarapis also called Seraous, from the city of Oxyrhyncha. A…arion, freedwoman of Sarapion son of Apollonios, her mother being Heraklous, from
4     the same city, when dying
5     deposited with me, for issue (ekdosimon), (the will) which she made through the local record-office (agoranomeion) in the
6     month Germanikeios of the past thirteenth year of Aurelius
7     Antoninus Caesar the lord,
8     to be opened after her death; whence, bringing this forward and
9     presenting from those who witnessed the will as witnesses Dionysios also called Amois, son of Herakleides son of Dionysios also called Amois, his mother being Sarapous,
10    and Diogenes son of Apollophanes son of
11    Diogenes, and Sarapion son of Chairemon son of Sosibios, and Agathos Daimon, freedman of Herakleides, and Sarapion also called Dorion, the
12    four from the same city,
13    therefore, in accordance with what was ordered,
14    having paid to Horos also called Horion and to
15    Demetrios the appointed twelve drachmas for the dissolution of the will, I request that the dissolution of the will
16    take place. Year 14 of Imperat[or]
17    Caesar Marcus Aurelius
18    Antoninus Augustus, Armeniacus
19    Medicus Parthicus Germanicus
20    maximus, Pauni 14.
21    I, Theon son of Pausirion,
22    have submitted (this).
23    Pauni 18 … chuiou
