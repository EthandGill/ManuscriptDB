[TRANSLATION]
1     [Thrasyllus] ... of the later generation [lent to Ptolemaios the] ... [Thracian] and
2     [Apollodotus the] ... [to the] [two] of the
3     [later generation] ... [of copper drachmae] ... two hundred
4     [Let them repay this loan from the month of] Egyptian
5     Hathyr until Tybi of the 16th year, each month the amount due.
6     Sureties for payment toward each other [are] Ptolemaios and Apollodotus aforesaid. Keeper of contracts: Stratokles
7     son of Artemidorus, Herakleiaus, of those not yet enrolled, Alexandrian of the later generation.
8     Thrasyllus was about 30 years old, of good stature, honey-complexioned, long-faced
9     scar on the left eyebrow at the tip.
10    Ptolemaios was about 40 years old, of medium height, dark-complexioned, round-faced.
11    Apollodotus was about 40 years old, of medium height, honey-complexioned, round-faced
12    limping on the right leg.
13    Stratokles was about 32 years old, of medium height, honey-complexioned, long-faced
14    aquiline, right ear pierced.
15    Written in the 16th year, month of Artemisios.
