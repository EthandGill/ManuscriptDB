1     [I] Frange
2     [he greets] and he rejoices at [his] beloved sons
3     and [he] greets Apa Victor and his wife
4     a greeting in the Lord, the holy one
5     Trinity
6     take all things to [you]
7     I received the trimesion and
8     an excellent one being strong, well
9     I came forth swiftly
10    swift from the beginning, to us I received the letter
