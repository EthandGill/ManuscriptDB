[TRANSLATION]
1     To Cyriacus the deacon and oil-seller: furnish to the cattle-driver of [the] village Sinto
2     for the wage of indiction 6, oil sextarii 12 twelve only. Written in month Mechir 24, indiction 6, by me
3     Pettarius deacon and notary it was completed
