[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Kopres son of Paulos and Ioulios son of Severinus and their associates,
2     receivers of the city's grain,
3     to Aurelius Atisios son of Leonides, kephalaiotes (chief collector) of Karanis, greeting.
4     We have received from you, for the twelfth indiction,
5     of the villagers, of clean wheat, eight artabas, a twelfth,
6     that is 8, 1/12 artabas only. I, Kopres, have signed. I, Ploution, have signed.
7     In the consulship of Septimius Acindynus, the most illustrious prefect
8     of the sacred praetorium, and of Popollonius Proclus, the most illustrious,
9     Phamenoth 25.
10    And on the 27th likewise, the same Atisios son of Leonides, for the twelfth indiction,
11    of the villagers of Karanis, one artaba of wheat, a sixth,
12    that is 1, 1/6 artaba of grain only. I, Kopres, have signed.
13    I, Ploution, have signed.
14    And of the roster (pittakion) of Panas …
15    The Aurelii Kopres son of Paulos and Ioulios son of Severinus and associates,
16    receivers of the city's grain,
17    to Aurelius Antiourios son of Abous, kephalaiotes of Karanis, greeting.
18    We have received from you, for the twelfth indiction,
19    of the villagers, of clean wheat, five artabas, two-thirds,
20    that is 5, 2/3 artabas. And through Chairas, another two artabas, a half, a third,
21    that is 2, 1/2, 1/3 artabas only. I, Kopres, have signed. I, Ploution, have signed.
22    In the consulship of Septimius Acindynus, the most illustrious prefect
23    of the sacred praetorium, and of Popollonius Proclus, the most illustrious,
24    Phamenoth 25.
25    On the same day, through Kastor son of Aritios, kephalaiotes of Karanis,
26    for the twelfth indiction, of the villagers, of clean wheat, five artabas, that is 5 artabas only. I, Kopres, have signed.
27    And on the 26th, through Antiourios son of Abous, kephalaiotes of Karanis, for
28    the twelfth indiction, of the villagers, of clean wheat,
29    one artaba, a half, a third, that is 1, 1/2, 1/3 artaba only. I, Anaios, have signed.
30    I, Ploution, have signed.
31    And on Pharmouthi 1, through Kastor, kephalaiotes of Karanis, for the twelfth
32    indiction, of the villagers, of clean wheat, of an artaba a half,
33    a twelfth, that is of an artaba 1/2, 1/12 only. I, Kopres, have signed.
34    I, Ploution, have signed.
