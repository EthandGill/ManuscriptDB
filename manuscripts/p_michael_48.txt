[TRANSLATION]
1     Chmg (= "God help us").
2     In the reign and consulship of our most divine master
3     Flavius Justin the eternal Augustus, Emperor, in the seventh year,
4     Phamenoth, on the twenty-second day, of the fifth indiction.
5     The Aurelii Anouphis son of Palos, his mother being Maria,
6     and Pakouis son of Jeremias, his mother being Thaesia, shepherds
7     from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome,
8     to the Aurelii Phoibammon, son
9     of Triadelphos, the most admirable, and
10    Victor, son of Kollouthos, taxpayers
11    from the same village of Aphrodite: we acknowledge
12    willingly and being persuaded that we have leased
13    from you for a yearly term
14    of crops, with God's help, of the sixth indiction, the
15    grazing-stock belonging to you, of the whole
16    of your property situated in the eastern
17    plain of the same [village], called Kratistos,
18    in the decania of Phoibammon son of Psakaes, with
19    all its right, up to the
20    old boundaries round about, on condition that we
21    have it under us for grazing and for
22    pasturing of our flocks;
23    and we are ready to make …
24    twenty-five nights with our
25    sheep, in return for the rent of the same
26    grazing-stock, without dispute. The lease
27    shall be valid and secure, and being formally questioned I acknowledged it.
28    We, the Aurelii Anouphis son of Palos and Pakouis son of Jeremias,
29    the aforesaid, have leased as set forth above. Aurelius
30    Horouongkis son of Menas, having been requested, wrote on their behalf,
31    they not knowing letters. [Au]relius Christodoros
32    son of Ponnis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard
33    it from the contracting parties.
34    Through me, Kyros, notary, it was written.
