1     To my lord brother
2     Papnouthios, Dorotheos greets [you].
3     I sent by the guard
4     gold coins four and of silver
5     unit one, of denarii ten-thousands one ten-thousand, and
6     [from] Alexander [are] denarii ten-thousands five.
7     I pray [for you] to fare well
8     for many times.
