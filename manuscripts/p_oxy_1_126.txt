1   † In the reign of our most divine and most pious master, greatest benefactor, Fl(avius)
2   Justinus
3   the eternal Augustus and Imperator, year 7, in the consulship of their Serenity for the second time,
4   Pachon 15, of the fifth indiction, (year) 247/216. †
5   To the office of the exactor of the division and house of Theon of revered memory, through you,
6   Kyros
7   the reverend administrator of this new city of Justinus, (from) Fl(avia) S[t]ephanous,
8   daughter of the most wise advocate Joh'n, with the consent of Markos my most learned
9   husband, from the same city. May your reverence be willing, from the public folded registers laid up
10   with you, to burden my own name and to lighten the name of my own
11   (...) 
12   most wise father John, each year, from the embole and the gold-dues of the
13   sixth indiction now coming, with God's help, both of it and for all the time hereafter,
14   namely for the embole sixty-three artabas of grain by the canon, together with the freight charges thereof
15   to Alexandria and transport and all kinds of expenses, and on behalf of the canonical dues
16   the amounts also paid to the official gold-buyer of the time, twenty-two carats of gold
17   by public weight, and on behalf of the arca-dues the amounts also paid to the
18   arcarius of the time, that is the embolator, twenty-two and a half carats of gold, refined, equalling for the public account
19   twenty-four carats. For these things it has been decided that we should pay on behalf of the immovable properties handed over
20   to me, the same Stephanous, the aforesaid (properties) in the fields,
21   each year; and so that your reverence may know it and
22   for the security of the public account, we have made the present order of
23   registration with our subscription, as is set forth. † (hand 2) † Fl(avia) Stephanous,

25   aforewritten annual payment of the public (dues) is agreeable to me, as is set forth. †
