[TRANSLATION]
1     [Faustus the] and [Am]phiōn [of Ptolemaios] … o…imou to the grain-collectors of the eastern toparchy
2     Taampemou territory, greetings. Settle [accounts] concerning what you have of mine as [a matter of] wheat of the product of year 17
3     of Hadrian Caesar the Lord
4     to Harpaesis son of Harpaisis, Sesphtha, artabas
5     half, which makes artabas ½. Year 18 of Hadrian
6     Caesar the Lord, Phaophi 10.
