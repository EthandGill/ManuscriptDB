[TRANSLATION]
1     In the thirteenth year of Nero
2     Claudius Caesar Augustus
3     Germanicus Imperator, in the month
4     Xandikos = Mecheir, on the twenty-first, in Tebtynis of the division of Polemon
5     of the Arsinoite nome.
6     Thommous also called Protous,
7     (daughter) of Petesouchos, about thirty-
8     four years old, with a scar on the right hand on the outside,
9     with her guardian, her own relative Orseus son of Pakebkis,
10    about thirty-two years old, with a scar
11    on the left hand under the thumb, acknowledges to her
12    own full brother (by the same father and mother) Sambas,
13    about thirty-seven years old, with a scar
14    at the tip of the right eyebrow, that she, the acknowledging Thommous also called Protous, cedes to Sambas all the property whatsoever that is to be left by the father of them both, Petesouchos son of Apollonios, and that neither she the acknowledger nor her (heirs) shall proceed against
15    Sambas concerning anything
16    of the (property) to be left by the aforesaid father Petesouchos.
17    The under-writers of the acknowledger and of her guardian who was registered (as such): Lysas son of Didymos, about 47 years old, with a scar on the left foot,
18    and Theon son of Heron, 59 years old, with a scar on the right knee.
19    I, Thommous also called Protous, (daughter) of Petesouchos, with
20    my guardian, my relative Orseus son of Pakebkis, acknowledge that I cede to my brother
21    Sambas all the property whatsoever that is to be left by the father of us both, Petesouchos son of Apollonios, and that I shall not proceed concerning anything in any manner, as
22    is set out above. Lysas son of Didymos wrote on their behalf because they do not know letters.
23    [Sam]bas son of Petesouchos: [the acknowledgement has been made to me.]
