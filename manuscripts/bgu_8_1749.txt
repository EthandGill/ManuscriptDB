[TRANSLATION]
1     [Dionysios to Paniskos, greeting. Copies of the official communications addressed to Herakleides the sitologos]
2     [and to Phamenes the banker are appended below.]
3     [Farewell. Year 18, Mesore] …
4     [To Herakleides. A copy of the communication from Athenaios, the kinsman and dioiketes, is appended below. Having followed therefore what is indicated through it,]
5     [measure out,] with [Paniskos] the royal [scribe] joining in writing …
6     to the men indicated as assigned to the nome, the Thebans,
7     [five-aroura men, 478(?), at once,] the portions to each, of wheat, 2(?), of wheat for consumption, 816,
8     making, of wheat for consumption, 816; and make receipts and counter-receipts with them as is fitting.
9     Year 18, Mesore 5.
10    Athenaios to Dionysios, greeting. To those assigned to you in Pharsesis(?),
11    the five-aroura Thebans, 478 men, advance the due reckoning for Mesore,
12    to each, of copper, 3 drachmas, of wheat for consumption, 2 artabas, the sums collected being, of copper, 204 talents,
13    of wheat for consumption, 816; and from the 19th year let the grain-allowances be set down for them for a ten-month period.
14    Year 18, Mesore 5.
15    To Phamenes the banker. Make a payment likewise of copper, two hundred
16    four talents, making, of copper, 204 talents.
17    To the scribes … copper …
18    To Herakleodoros it was written. Year 18, Mesore 14.
19    Year 18, Mesore 14.
