1     After the consulship of Flavius Log[ginus] the most renowned, Pachon 28, at the end of the tenth indiction, in the city of Arsinoe.
2     To Flavius E[ustochius] the most magnificent and most illustrious count of the sanctified domestics and first of the city of Arsinoe, Aurelius Sambas
3     son
4     of blessed Nile broker from the same city from the district of Fish-sellers: I confess the matters set forth; having come forward I have implored your magnificence
5     so that you entrust to Aurelius Peter son of Theodotus grain-measurer from this very city of Arsinoe to receive the public grain of the village of Twenty-five-arouras of the
6     Theodo[sio]city parish of the embo[lē] of the blessed eleventh indiction or also of others which your magnitude may entrust me, I myself Sambas [having written]
7     him and
8     agreeing [to receive] and having yielded [to] my pleas you have done this, he sought through a written agreement the security
9     to receive from me concerning the return of which [the aforementioned] Peter receives
10    public product or also other: according to this I confess willingly, taking oath god all-powerful and the piety and victory of the
11    fair-victorious and
12    immortal [he]ad to guarantee and to have undertaken at risk of me and of my substance the aforementioned Peter repaying your magnificence or also the
13    ones by her entrusted in good time that is in the season of the embolē or also whenever your magnitude may wish to receive all which appears
14    to have been received
15    [measure of public grain or] also other on the village of [Twenty-]five-arouras according to the written-records from him or also orders: if but [not] this he do
16    [failing] something into the return of the same measure, to the point that I the guarantor of this be liable to make the responses to your magnificence
17    [of what is] failing into the completion of which appears to have been received as stated measure whether public grain whether other with also the trouble-rubbed-on it
18    … harm or loss, for this reason being subject to your magnificence and to the public account for this [of] all mine having and having-to-have
19    [specially and] generally held as pledge by right and by hypothecation as if from judgment; binding the agreement and when questioned I confessed. Aurelius Sambas
