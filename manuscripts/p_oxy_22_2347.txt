[TRANSLATION]
1     … to the logistai [of the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchi]tes,
2     [fr]om [Aureli]us Zeuxios son of [Sara]pion, from [the village of Pela of the Oxyrhynchi]te nome. I acknowledge, swearing the
3     venerable [divin]e oath of [our sov]ereign Ju[lia]n, eternal
4     Aug[u]stus, that I willingly and of my own accord stand surety for
5     Aurelius Horopt[o]leeis fr[om] the most splendid city of the Antinoites, residing in the same village of Pela, he having been entrusted by you the landowner with having the ship which you own, a Greek phikopedalon-vessel of a capacity of
6     two thousand artabas, more or less, on condition that he himself sails in the same ship, exercising the steering of it,
7     and that whatever cargoes of public produce he receives, in accordance with what is delivered by him [according to]
8     the bond, he shall convey down to the most splendid Alexandria, or wherever it be ordered, and deliver
9     in full, and of their delivery shall bring back the documents from the auspicious sixth new
10    indiction, so that no complaint arise to you concerning them; and whenever you choose, that he shall deliver
11    the same ship together with its tackle as he received it, apart from an act of God; or that I the surety must of necessity undergo all that is sought from him concerning this, or else I shall be liable
12    to the divine oath.
13    I, Aurelius Zeuxios son of Sarapion, have sworn the divine oath, standing surety for Horoptoleeis
14    as is set forth above, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged. I, Au[re]lius At tion son of Eupothios, wrote for him,
15    he not knowing [let]ters.
16    Through me, Harpalos, it was written.
