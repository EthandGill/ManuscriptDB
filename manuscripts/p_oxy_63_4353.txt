[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords
2     the Emperors Diocletian for the 9th time
3     and Maximian for the 8th time, Augusti. …
4     Aurelius Heraklas, son of Zoilas, his mother being Taaphynchis, from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     a carpet-weaver by trade,
6     to Aurelius Kephalon, son of Ammonios …
7     from the same city, overseer of a workshop of the same trade, greeting.
8     I acknowledge that I have received from you as an advance
9     two talents of silver, that is 2 talents, on condition that I
10    work with you in the workshop which you have,
11    a carpet-weaving one, in the same city, receiving from you daily as wages
12    one hundred and twenty drachmas of silver, and that it not be permitted to me to abandon the workshop. But if
13    I do abandon it, I will forthwith repay to you the
14    aforesaid two talents of silver
15    without interest and without any delay
16    or pretext, you having the right of execution
17    against me and against all my property. The advance-contract (cheirograph), being valid, single, written out, is to hold good, produced anywhere and by anyone acting on
18    your behalf, and having been formally questioned I gave my assent.
19    Year 20 and year 12 of our lords Diocletian and
20    Maximian, Augusti, and of Constantius and Maximian
21    the most illustrious Caesars, Pharmouthi 19.
22    I, Aurelius Heraklas, have received the two talents of the advance and will repay as aforesaid, and having been formally questioned I gave my assent. I, Aurelius Agathos Daimon, son of Dionysios, wrote on his behalf, being requested,
23    since he stated that he does not know letters.
