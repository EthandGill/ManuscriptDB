1     To Dionysios [royal scribe of the Arsinoite, of the Polemon] division, also acting as deputy
2     [in the affairs of the strategia,]
3     from Harpaes[is son of Kronio]n from the village of Teptynis [of the same divisio]n. I lea[se]d
4     long ago from A[sklepiades son of A]sklepiades, an Antinoite, near the village [of Kerkesis of the sa]me division, twenty-five [and a half] grain-bearing
5     arou[ra]s, among which there are also of marshland arou[ras]
6     ten and a half, of which I [paid] in full to Asklepiades himself the rents up to the 9th year and including the 9th year;
7     [in full I paid] to him Asklepia[des]; but in the past 10th year,
8     when I had done [the work and sown the] arouras
9     [with wheat to the] full, [a breach having occurred] from the
10    Kerkesi[s marsh, the grain-bearing arouras went] under water
11    together with [the other arouras,] and it became evident to all [that this had happened while Asklepiades] was staying [in Anti]noou(polis),
12    I myself [having paid the public taxes an]d measured out the
13    [grain-tax and naubion-tax for the] arouras; but in the present 11th year, late,
14    when the wat[er of the marsh] had run off, I completed the works due in [the arouras] twelve and a [half] that had been [so]wn,
15    an[d also in] all the [arouras] of the marshland, in the [1]1th year,
16    up … [because] the water ran off late, the sown crops [became] of poor growth, [wherefore] I submit this memorandum
17    and I ask[k that a copy] be conveyed to him through an attendant
18    to Asklepiades, that he may know that I renounce the crops sown in the
19    arouras and the works of them which I completed
20    and that no account [concerning the]m is left over, nor concerning the
21    rents of the present year, but nor concerning any other
22    matter whatsoever, [Asklepiades] himself [t]aking back the arouras from the present [11th year, re]measuring out the
23    public taxes due for them, there remaining due to me, against [those which] I measured out into the wheat account, … artabas [of] wheat [in] thr[ee] in[stall]ments; I am content with this declaration.
24    Harpaesis, [about] 52 years old, with a scar on the left thumb;
25    Posid(onios?), notary, registered.
