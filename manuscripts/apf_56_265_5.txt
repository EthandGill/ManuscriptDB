1     Psansnos son of Petehsius has recorded on behalf of the poll-tax,
2     year four, ten drachmas, amount to drachmas 10; of bath-tax one drachma and half an obol,

4     Caesar Augustus Germanicus
5     Autocrator, Mesore 22. Pikos of Kephalos.
