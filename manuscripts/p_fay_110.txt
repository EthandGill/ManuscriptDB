[TRANSLATION]
1   Lucius Bellienus Gemellus
2   to Epagathos his own, greeting.
3   You will do well, on receiving my
4   letter, to have
5   the manure in it carted out,
6   so that what you call a store-chamber
7   be made a depot; and dig deep round the oil-
8   press on the outside,
9   so that the oil-press is not
10   easy to climb into; and sort the manure
11   off to the dung-heap; and let them
12   flood all our allot-
13   ments, that the sheep may bed there,
14   and let them give the olive-groves their second
15   watering; and cross over to
16   Diony[s]ias and find out whether the olive-
17   grove has been watered twice and forked
18   over; and if at all not, let it be watered
19   and … safely forked
20   over, lest … them fall through; and
21   having paid(?) … [an]d Psellos, the sitologoi,
22   …[… and] Chairas the scribe of the
23   farmers, and Heraklas 90 dr. and interest,
24   and Cha[ira]s the former tax-collector 24 dr.,
25   and Didas …, the price of barley, 240 dr. and interest,
26   and Heron the former foreman two years’ interest,
27   120 dr. And let the carpenters hang
28   the doors; and I am sending you the
29   ropes. Make the arms of the oil-press
30   double, and those of the de-
31   pots single. Farewell.
32   Year 14 of Imperator Caesar Domitian
33   Augustus Germanicus, month Germanikos 14.
34   So do not do otherwise.
35   Deliver to Epagathos from Lucius Bellienus Gemellus.
