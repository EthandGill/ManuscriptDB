[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 27, Phamenoth 11: there has measured in
2     into the granary in Diospolis Magna,
3     (of) year 27: Sambataios (son of) Abielos
4     (in) Poanemoun, of barley
5     fifteen ⅓ 1/12, total barley 15 ⅓ 1/12.
6     Ambryon.
7     (2nd hand) Apollonios: I have received by measure, of barley fifteen
8     ⅓ 1/12, total barley 15 ⅓ 1/12.
