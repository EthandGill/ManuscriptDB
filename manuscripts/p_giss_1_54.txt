[TRANSLATION]
1     To my most honored lord brother Olympiodoros and
2     to Hermaeion, Kyros greeting in the Lord.
3     I am passing by; having found (a chance) for you through brother Kandisanos, I hastened to greet your goodness, that being in good health and good cheer you may receive my letter. I heard that you, Hermaeion, were named by the prospective president as distributor of
4     Syene, and if indeed you have learned that your nomination was confirmed,
5     come quickly to the place, so that you may overtake the boats
6     of the new levy. For many (people) have had grain and barley assessed into those parts from Antinoou as far as the city of Diocletian,
7     and many laden boats have passed by. Hasten
8     therefore with your associates to overtake (them),
9     so that those of the (rival) distributors may not
10    seize your profit. And help in every way, most honored
11    Olympiodoros, fixing the public grain of our estate,
12    so that the household may not be harmed. And I have sent you the receipts
13    [thr]ough Peinouthion the coppersmith and Matinos,
14    for the supplies [o]f the new indiction, so that you may obtain
15    the rec[ei]pts for them. And deign for God's sake to attend to your
16    lord [As]ynkretos. I greet you all by name.
17    I greet my father Apa Laurinos an[d] Apa Par…u. I pray
18    that you be in good health for many years in the Lord.
19    I gree[t my] lord … b … gi … with his children by name
20    and concerning his health. I greet … to the children Manth and Paul…n.

22    … …ro … l … aner … u and Kyros the deacon.
