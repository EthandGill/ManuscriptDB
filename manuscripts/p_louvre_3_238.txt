[TRANSLATION]
1     And finding the present opportunity, I have written to yo[ur] …
2     the matters concerning your health, since [I am] con[cerned] …
3     not holding (back?). And behold, until … day …
4     awaiting the boats of the win[e-merchants,] which, God commandi[ng,] …
5     in Alexandria. For the rest, pray for me … the Lord, and if anything … wr[ite] …
6     … the same lord Antonis and … for … prepared also the lord
7     [to] write to him concerning this; but what I wro[t]e to you in the previous letters I did not send.
8     Be willing, then, to send (it) down promptly, and concerning Leontakios as [I] wrote to you.
9     And if it pleases you, greet warmly from me your uncle and his whole household
10    and the lord Stephanos and the lady Christodora and the lady Antonia.
11    Go up and reverence the feet of the lady and of the lady, with blessing,
12    as from me. May the Lord of glory grant me also to embrace you face to face,
13    in health; and on each occasion let me know of your health. And if
14    we should be delayed, slaughter the piglet and do not wait for me; for, as I think,
15    we have something to do. And if you also have need of barley-fodder for it, tell the lord Anto[nios]
16    and he will provide (it) for you, and when I afterward partake of it I will reckon it to his account. In the month Phaophi 1.
17    … [to] my [most lon]ged-[for] sister Gen nadia, (from) Joa nnakios.
