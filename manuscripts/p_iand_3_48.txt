[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] reign of our most divine and most pious
2     [and] greatest, benefactor master, Flavius
3     [Tiberi]us Constantine, the eternal Augustus
4     [and] Emperor, in the 8th year, in the consulship of the same most pious
5     [our] master, in the 4th year, Pauni 4, of the 15th indiction.
6     [To the] most exalted heirs of the one
7     [of] glorious memory, Apion, the late patrician,
8     [landowne]rs also here in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
9     through Menas, household-servant, who acts as surety and provides
10    to his own masters, the same all-praiseworthy
11    men, the right of action and liability: I, Aurelius Ptolion,
12    son of Anouthios, my mother being Nonna, having my origin
13    from the hamlet of Ambious, an estate of your
14    exaltedness, of the Oxyrhynchite nome, a registered
15    cultivator of it, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from
16    your exaltedness, through those belonging to her,
17    by way of an advance, here and now, [of] gold solidi
18    two, by the private standard, total: 2 gold solidi by the private standard, which, being free of risk
19    and free from every risk, I shall of necessity
20    repay to her, through those belonging to her,
21    whenever you may wish, without delay,
22    at the risk of my belongings, which are made liable
23    for this. The deed, written singly, is valid, and having been questioned I acknowledged it.
24    I, Aurelius Ptollion, son of Anouthios,
25    the aforewritten, have made this
26    deed of the two solidi of the
27    advance, as set forth above. I, Anastasios,
28    wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
29    Deed of Ptollion son of Anouthios, [from] the hamlet [of Ambious, by way] of an advance,
30    of gold [solidi] 2 by the private [s]tandard.
