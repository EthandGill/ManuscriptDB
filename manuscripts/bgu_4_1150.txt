[TRANSLATION]
1   [To Protarchos,]
2   [fr]om [Ar]temis daughter of …, a citizen-woman, with (her) guardian, her husband Demetrios
3   [son of] Althaios; and from Protarchos son of Zenon, and this man's
4   wife
5   [Opora] daughter of Protarchos, with (her) guardian, her husband. Artemis agrees that she has received
6   fr[om] Protarchos and Opora, from hand to hand out of the house, what she had lent them,
7   by agreement through the sa(me)
8   tri[bunal] in year 11 of Caesar, Mesore: of silver, one thousand drachmas, and the [in]terest of these
9   for the overdue time; and that the loan-agreement is void,
10   (and that) Ar-
11   temis shall not proceed, nor another on her behalf, against Protarchos and Opora,
12   neither concerning the same (matters) nor concerning anything else at all, written or unwritten,
13   of (matters) up to the present
14   day, or else be liable to the fixed penalty, apart from the things agreed being valid. And Opora
15   has recovered from Artemis the (securities) which she had given her in pledge — two first-fruits(?) — according to (the agreement)
16   of year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 12.
17   To Protarchos,
18   from Dios son of Aristonikos, of the Althaian (deme), and from Apollonios son of Xenon,
19   a Persian of the epigone, and this man's wife Ptolema daughter of Kerkion,
20   a Persian woman, with (her) guardian, her husband. Apollonios and Ptolema agree that they have
21   from Dios
22   a loan, from hand to hand out of the house, of Ptolemaic silver, [drachmas] fifty-
23   two, interest-free, which they will also repay
24   in . months from Pharmouthi of the present year 17 of Caesar, without any postponement, or else they are
25   at once liable to seizure and (held) until they pay the loan with half as much again; and for the overdue time
26   the interest according to the diagramma, at two drachmas, the right of execution belonging to Dios,
27   both from them, being
28   mutual sureties for the payment, and from one and from whichever of them he chooses, and from their possessions,
29   all of them, just as by legal judgement, void being
30   (the pledges) and [whatever] pledges of trust [they may bring forward] — all of them — (and) every protection. We request (it).
31   Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 12.
