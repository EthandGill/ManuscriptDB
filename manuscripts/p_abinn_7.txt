[TRANSLATION]
1     [To] the beloved and truly brother Abinnaeus,
2     [A]pa Mis, greeting in the Lord.
3     [We] thank God for your soundness
4     and your zeal, that the fear may abound
5     of God in you, for through the fear all things come to be.
6     … therefore we write to you on account of your good
7     conduct, that your love may abound, and
8     … truly, Abinnaeus, in every way. We write to you, we love the desire of your soul; for I wish to meet you,
9     since necessity came upon me and I made haste, I came;
10    pray. I made inquiry therefore about you and I heard that to the
11    parts of Andromachis, and again I heard that
12    he went up to the lower regions, and on account of the needs which I wrote to you that on account of these things, Apa Ision; do not be neglectful now.
13    I gave therefore to Maximus four blades to be given to you,
14    and two to Plas and one to Keron, and you did not find (them) since Hermas (went) to Dionysias, unless he gave you
15    others. For just now we are looking to send them, and
16    if he does any customary (favor), write to me, and on account of the
17    little things … do them be done, and send to me all things. For I know that if you give them the authority … come to be. Do not be neglectful to make happen [what] we write to you, all, as also … …
18    [I salute t]he children and all your people.
19    To Abinnaeus, from Apa Mios.
