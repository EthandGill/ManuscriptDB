[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Nearkhides, Ammonios [sends] greetings.
2     Order [that it] be provided
3     to Hermes grain, artabas
4     ten, and to Syros, artabas
5     five, [total] together artabas 15.
6     Ammonios, I have signed.
7     Farewell perpetually.
