[TRANSLATION]
1     Let [your] piety learn th[at all] the farmers of the village were found saying
2     that: "We have our woad (isatis); we do not pay one keration into our public (treasury), unless
3     the monopolist comes and takes the same woad of ours." And those not having woad have given
4     a sufficient guarantor, that those having woad over (and above) pay on behalf of those not
5     having (it). Be willing to go up to the landlord (geouchos) and to beseech him, that he may send
6     the same monopolist here first. And concerning the grain, I sold not one
7     solidus' (worth), God the only one knows. And those owing the remainder of the grain did not
8     wish to give the price of the same grain, [s]aying that: "Unless you take the same grain."
9     But write to me what ought to be done. For I demanded six nomismata and have given
10    them on account of an advance. And Petros and Menas the servant-boys are holding from me …
11    And you have their pittakion, of the one (and a) third nomismation. And you ha[ve ] … [through]
12    Apa Sirios the soldier (symmachos), of the one nomism[a]tion, that of the same Apa Si[rios ] …
13    one nomisma. And greatly I salute you.
14    Deliver to our most God-fearing lord Martyrios, presbyter,
15    … from Esaeios, deacon
16    … a pittakion of grain and of gold on behalf of the village of Senyris
17    … 1st indiction.
