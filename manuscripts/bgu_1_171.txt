[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: To the sitologoi of the village of Karanis, greetings. I have received from you as a loan of seed grain of the present year 20 from the harvest of the past year 19 from the 83 cleruchy, Aphrodeisios, son of Herakleides, at Kerkesoucha for 8 arouras of royal (land) 8 artabas of wheat. (Deletion) I, Aphrodeisios, have received the seed reserves, as mentioned above. I, Horion alias Chairemon, wrote this on his behalf, because he is illiterate. Year 20 Hathyr 26. (m. 2) I, Horigenes, have signed for eight artabas of wheat.
