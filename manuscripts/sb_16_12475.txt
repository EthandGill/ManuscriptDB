1     The letter[s] … about …
2     Of the ship[s] … [to] be carried.
3     And instead of the matte[r] … and … sailing.
4     That send to me the … for it was reported to me those from the harbor.
5     That according to our[s] demonstration ... since already I have received.
6     These. And send [to] me [these] to the secretaries [who] are.
7     There and [they] seek the [linen cloth] of mine and the head-cloth and send to me.
8     From the ships … [at] here and send.
9     To me ships [for] transport of artabae of grain one ten-thousand five-thousand. Do not then make delay and abandon me here cast away.
10    And if this is the matter of which he mocked me and sent me.
11    Here and if you have need of answer from here without hesitation.
12    Write to me and I will fulfill your command. And introduction indeed.
13    And end of the letter making the declared.
14    From me obeisance of your fraternal disposition.
