[TRANSLATION]
1     to the fixed interest one and [each]
2     of Pathyris. But this loan
3     let Nechuthes, [who has borrowed], repay
4     to those who have lent Erianoupi and
5     the guild-members, in the month of Pachon of the same
6     14th year, new clean and unadulterated from every [source], and restored into [a] house
7     to them at their own expense,
8     in the measure that he also received. But if
9     he does not repay in the appointed time, or does not do according to what has been written, let him pay
10    in the following month immediately
11    the of barley artabs seven-and-a-half, or the
12    [value] which will be in the market price. But the
13    recovery shall be for Erianoupi and the guild-members from [the property] of Nechuthes and from [the]
14    all his belongings, collecting them as-if from a legal judgment.
15    Hermias from Paniakos has transacted [this].
