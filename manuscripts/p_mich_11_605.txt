[TRANSLATION]
1     … [a sc]ar on … to Hermas son of Ptolemaios,
2     … that Katoites and Petesouchos have received
3     from [th]e Hermas, of stamped silver of the Augusti' coinage, as principal, drach[mas]
4     sixty, on the spot, hand to hand, out of the house; and in place of the interest on these
5     the acknowledging parties have agreed that Hermas shall dwell, both he and those
6     from him and whomever he wishes, in what belongs to them, the acknowledging parties,
7     in the aforesaid village, in the paternal ninth and twenty-seventh share, held in common and undivided with Hermas himself, of the remaining shares of the building-plots and of all that goes with them; and that the acknowledging parties shall guarantee, and
8     those from them, to Hermas [a]nd to those from him, the matters concerning this habitation
9     with every guarantee, and shall provid[e] them clear of every debt, public
10    and private, and (warrant) that no one shall lay claim to them or to any part, nor obstruct [ ]
11    and if Hermas shall appear to have spent expenditure on repair or rebuilding, and
12    the acknowledging parties wish to repay, let them repay together with the aforesaid silver as well,
13    the trust resting upon Hermas himself. Subscriber for the acknowledging parties: Heliodoros son of Heliodoros, about 44 years old, scar on the right cheek.
14    Katoites and Petesouchos, both sons of Horos, and their mother … ios
15    daughter of Katoites, with her guardian, the husband of her daughter, Soueris,
16    Apollonios son of Apollon[i]os: we acknowledge that we have received from Hermas
17    the sixt[y] drachmas of silver, hand to hand, and in place of the interest on these
18    we agree that he shall dwell in the share falling to us,
19    the ninth and twenty-seventh share of building-plot sites,
20    and we shall guarantee it; [and i]f he shall appear to have spent expenditure on repa[i]r [or] rebui[ldi]ng, we shall re[pa]y, the trust resting upon him,
21    as is set forth above. [Heliodoros] son of Heliodoros [wro]te on their behalf,
22    they not knowing letters.
23    Hermas son of Ptolemai[o]s: the receipt has been made [out to me] as is set forth above.
24    It has been entered through the record-office in Bakchias.
25    Acknowledgement of Katoites and others to Hermas
26    of habitation.
