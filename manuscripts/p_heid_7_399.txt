[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twelfth year of the Emperor Caesar
2     Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus
3     Pius, in the month [Xandikos =] Mecheir 9, in Karanis of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite
4     nome. Marcus Sempronius Gemellus acknowledges,
5     designated gymnasiarch, about 47 years old, with a scar on his eyebrow
6     on the left, to Herieus … son of Herieus, his mother being
7     Tanomgeus … from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos of the Arsinoite nome, through Harmachis … about 43 years old
8     … with a scar below the left calf, that he (the acknowledger) has received
9     from him, the acknowledging party, of olive oil,
10    new, pure, nineteen measures and choes
11    nine, by the measure of the same Sempronius, which he owed
12    him according to a completed acknowledgement of loan in the past eleventh year of Antoninus
13    Caesar the lord, in the month Gorpiaios = Epeiph, through the record-office of Hiera Nesos, which he (Sempronius) also gave back to him
14    for annulment and cancellation, and that Sempronius shall not proceed
15    against Harmacheus nor against
16    those who act for him concerning these matters in any way. Signature:
17    I, Sempronius Gemellus, have received the nineteen measures of olive oil owed to me
18    [and nine choes].
