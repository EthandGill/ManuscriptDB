[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign] of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Arsinoe the Sibling Gods, in the twenty-four[th] year, the priest being Archetos son of Iasios, of Alexander and the Sibling Gods and the
2     [Benefactor Gods]; the basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphos being Timonassa daughter of Zoilos; in the month Dios, in Hiera
3     [Nesos of the Savior] Gods of the Arsinoite nome. Ptolemaios son of Mnesios, of those of
4     [Ptolema]ios son of Eteoneus, of the second cavalry-command, a hundred-aroura holder,
5     acknowledges [to Petosiris] son of Searmotis, toparch of the (lands) allotted to him of the Herakleid[es divisio]n, and to Horos the royal secretary, that he has sown in his own
6     [allotment ar]ound Hiera Nesos of the Savior Gods, with summer sesame, [eighty] arouras
7     for the twenty-fifth year, and that he has received from the
8     [bank in Croco]diles city the operating-cost for each aroura,
9     [of silver, dra]chmas two, so as to be one hundred sixty drachmas, and
10    [that he will measur]e out all the sesame produced for him into the royal (treasury) in the
11    [twenty-]fifth year and that he will pay the operating-cost to the king
12    [through the sa]me bank in the twenty-fifth year;
13    [and if he does not] pay, let him forfeit on the spot, and let the right of execution belong
14    [to them] and to anyone else who produces the contract, out of all the property [of Pto]lemaios and of his surety as for [roya]l (debts). [The suret]y of Ptolemaios for payment of the operating-cost (is) Paneuis son of Pais.
15    … . And let this contract be valid. Witnesses: Antigenes
16    [the Thracian, Hermo]n the Persian, the two of (those of) Ptolemaios son of Eteoneus; Sostratos the Thracian, Dies
17    …nes the Achaean, the three of (those of) Hippokrates—the five being of the second cavalry-command,
18    [the hund]red-aroura holders; Polemon son of Menelaos, a Macedonian of the epigone.
19    [Of Ptolemaios] (the share of) Petosiris, of Antigenes … …
20    of Hermon, of Dies,
21    [of Paneuis,] of Horos, of Sostratos, of Polemon.
