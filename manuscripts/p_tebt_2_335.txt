[TRANSLATION]
1     Phienous, wife of Apollonios, councillor of A[ntinoopolis,]
2     petitions:
3     A farmstead (epoikion) of mine, Ammonas, belongs to me in the Alexandrian (district) … given in do[wr]y [by] …
4     my father Hermaiskos according to public deeds, [of which]
5     I have always held the ownership; and before Kritonios I set forth [the]
6     right of my ownership, and to Myron he gave judgment …
7     he adjudged another (woman?) to remain in the possession … the …
8     final decision (being deferred) until the examination of the account-books of Her[maiskos, fea]ring lest perchance there be found in them some seizure [on account of the]
9     connection of the farmstead with Hermaiskos. But Herma[iskos]
10    has been found and examined; let the one who found (it) report t[o you, and]
11    I ask, since indeed I am justly in possession and enjoy the produce …
12    an[d] that the official (officialis) of the staff cease trou[bling] me.
13    For he troubles me as though you had ordered (him) to administer the … [,]
14    but if you thought fit to order anything of the sort in fact, we ask …
15    that you hold off until we lay before you the just claims …
16    The same Apollonios petitions: a lodging-house (xenidion) so[ld]
17    to me for dwelling by Flavius Philadelphos in the g[reat]
18    city of Hermou (Hermoupolis) belongs to me. There, accordingly, it has come to pass …
