[TRANSLATION]
1     Heraclides to Turbo
2     greetings
3     I wonder how you did not take care of Talea's machine,
4     and without writing to me; for I learned that you had likewise taken care of Eibion; in any case
5     then, if you wish anything, act,
6     so that the machine may pump
7     and you will give four drachmas to Hermes, so that
8     the division may be made of the
9     arourai; for I already obtained a farmer for my portion; concerning the goose-feeder today I learned, and until now he has not come up; but you neglected, not having written to me about
10    [this]. Let Sarapia receive
11    for you the female ass-keeper, and receive from her the woolen cloak; in any case
12    [come] up to me, since
13    there is a necessary need of [you]
14    for the sake of [Sabinus]
15    I wish you to be well
