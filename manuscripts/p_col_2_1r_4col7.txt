[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sabinus, [former gymnasiarch, and] the associate public bankers.
2     The undersigned public cattle-breeders
3     of the village of Ibion Argaiou, through Mysthes
4     deputed, acknowledge that we have received
5     from you the (sums) directed by Ptolemaios,
6     royal scribe of the Arsinoite, Polemon division, acting in succession
7     also for the matters of the strategia of the two divisions,
8     for the transport-charge of wheat which we were [declared]
9     to have brought down from the granaries of the [Pol]emon
10    division, the conveyance being made under him, of the produce
11    of the 17th year, of wheat, 14 artabas, at eight drachmas each artaba, the
12    sum amounting to one hundred [twelve] drachmas. Total: 112 drachmas. And we are:
13    Herodes son of Dioskoros, of wheat, 3 artabas …
14    Orseus and Sotas, of wheat, 3 1/2 1/3 1/24 artabas.
15    E…oros, also called Sotas, of wheat, … 1/24 of an artaba.
16    Horos son of Apollonios, of wheat, … artaba(s)
17    Nephertos son of Apynchis, [of wheat,] 1/2 1/3 1/12 [of an artaba.]
18    Horos son of Apollonios, of wheat, … artabas; total, the foregoing.
19    Mysthes, deputed by the aforewritten
20    cattle-breeders, I have received the foregoing one hundred
21    twelve silver drachmas, total: 112 drachmas, as set forth above. Year 18
22    of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
23    Hadrianus the lord Augustus Pius. Phamenoth 2.
