[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Phainos, freedman of the Emperor, assistant
2     of Aurelius Agathonikos
3     the most excellent administrator
4     from Aurelius Pasis, Pasis, and Pnephairotos
5     Samba and Pasis Hatres and the rest for the completion of 22 men desert-guards of the Philadelphia gate. [Custom] being, lord, from [you] to [receive]
6     to us through the parasi…on from the taxes
7     of the desert-guard service of the present gate on behalf of the poll-tax
8     of ours per year in silver drachmas one thousand one hundred sixty we request, and [concerning] the third
9     of the year the following [to come to pass]. May you prosper.
10    Aurelius Pasis as of years 28, unmarked
11    Pnepherōs as of years …, scar on finger
12    small of the right hand
13    Pasis as of years 25, scar on the forehead middle
14    of year 5
15    Phamenōth 28
