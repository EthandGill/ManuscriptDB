4     tax 43 drachmas 4 obols ½-obol 2 chalki, additional-charges 2 drachmas 4 obols 2 chalki, total 46 drachmas 3 obols.

6     tax 25 drachmas ½-obol 2 chalki, additional-charges 1 drachma 3 obols ½-obol 2 chalki, total 26 drachmas 4 obols ½-obol.
7     Of the Antonian estate: sheep 6,
8     tax 30 drachmas, additional-charges 1 drachma 5 obols ½-obol 2 chalki, total 31 drachmas 5 obols ½-obol 2 chalki.
9     Of Jucundus and Chresimus: sheep 1,
10    tax 6 drachmas ½-obol, additional-charges 2 obols ½-obol, total 6 drachmas 3 obols.
11    Total of these: sheep 32, goat 1, in silver 111 drachmas 4 obols 2 chalki.
12    And under the care of al… of Augustus:
13    Of the Marcus estate: sheep 19, tax 139 drachmas,
14    additional-charges 9 drachmas ½-obol, total 148 drachmas ½-obol.
15    Of the Pallantian estate: sheep 1, goat 1,
16    tax 11 drachmas an obol, additional-charges 4 obols ½-obol, total 11 drachmas 5 obols ½-obol.
17    Total also of these: sheep 20, goat 1, in silver 160 drachmas.
18    Total of the … : sheep 52, goats 2,
