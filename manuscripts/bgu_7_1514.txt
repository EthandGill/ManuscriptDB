[TRANSLATION]
1     I hold from Nechtheraus
2     tiles one-hundred thirty-six, other tiles I hold sixteen,
3     other tiles I hold one-hundred thirty-one,
4     other I hold Pachon nineteen tiles fifteen
5     [the total is] tiles one-hundred thirty-eight.
6     Nechtheraus has to Plenei drachmas one for
7     barley, other drachmas Nechtheraus
8     [his] brother has drachmas two, tiles I hold eleven, other I hold
9     tiles one-hundred five, other tiles I hold ten, other I hold
10    Pauni nine tiles four [total] tiles one-hundred forty.
11    other Nechtheraus has drachmas eight-hundred,
12    he holds drachmas six-hundred, other he holds drachmas six-hundred
13    [the total is] drachmas two.
