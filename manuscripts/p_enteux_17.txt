[TRANSLATION]
1     [To King Ptolemy] greetings, Menelaus, [son] of Paraibaitus, Cyrenean of the next generation, of [those] from Sacred Island
2     … of the Heracleides division. In year 4, Phaofi 16, Lysanias has died
3     … baitus of my father, [my] younger brother; but to him neither wife nor
4     … of the things abandoned [as] possessions it befalls me to inherit.
5     [I beseech you then, O] king, to order Diophaneus the strategos, having registered me with him and …
6     … the inheritance, to write to Hipparchos the overseer, if any of the
7     … [something] and are willing to give back to me through him [in] writing
8     … [something] through a rescript, but those disobeying to send
9     … so that they may be compelled to do what is just for me. For if this happens, I shall obtain, O king,
10    [my] worth. Farewell.
11    Menelaus, about 30 years old, medium-build, honey-colored, long-faced, straight-nosed, [with a] scar on the right cheekbone,
12    … has been registered with us the inheritance
13    [through] … Year 4, Daisios 27, Athyr 29.
