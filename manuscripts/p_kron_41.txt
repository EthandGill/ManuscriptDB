[TRANSLATION]
1     To Asklepiades son of Asklepiades, his mother being Eirene daughter of Herakleides,
2     from Harpaesis son of Kronion and Sasopis
3     son of another Kronion, both from the village of Tebtynis,
4     Persians of the epigone. We wish to lease from you
5     on joint security, for full payment, the lands belonging to you around Kerkesis of the
6     division of Polemon, of an allotment, twenty-five and a half arouras, or however many there may be, among which are twelve arouras
7     of pasture, for six years from the present fourth year
8     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, the rent each year
9     in the first and third and fifth year of the
10    lease, each year without seed-corn, in which I shall sow with arouras
11    of wheat, sixty artabas of new wheat, clean, unadulterated, unmixed with barley, by the granary-measure of Loupos of the
12    aforesaid village of Tebtynis, and … ; and in the
13    second and fourth and sixth year of the lease,
14    on condition that we measure out each year the artaba-tax of all the
15    twenty-five and a half arouras
16    to the public treasury, of which we shall also deliver the receipt
17    each year, and (giving) each year, over the six years, gifts in kind:
18    two measures of cumin and two cocks, each worth
19    four drachmas, all without deduction and
20    without risk, it being forbidden to us, the first party, within the
21    period to abandon the lease, but we shall perform
22    from our own resources the appropriate works each year, all the dyke-works,
23    irrigations, hoeings, double-hoeings, throwing up of canals and water-channels,
24    all at the proper times, doing no damage,
25    and the aforesaid rents we shall pay back in the month Pauni in Tebtynis,
26    and after the period we shall hand over (the land) free from grazing-damage of
27    sheep and from bedding-down. If it seems good to lease it.
28    We shall deliver also, for the wheat-bearing land for the three years,
29    the freight-charges due from you, Asklepiades, to the public treasury,
30    the naubion-charges being our responsibility, we the lessees.
31    Harpaesis, about 45 years old, with a scar on the left thumb.
32    Sasopis, about 30 years old, with a scar on the right knee.
33    Year 4 of Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus
34    Antoninus Augustus Pius, Thoth 7.
