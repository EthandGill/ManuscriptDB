[TRANSLATION]
1     Dios the scribe, I have marked in the 15th year, Mesore.
2     To Deios, who is also [Apollonius and]
3     Herod who is also Diogenes,
4     having served as gymnasiarchs, keepers of records of Arsinoe properties,
5     from Alexion of Pneph[erotis]
6     of Dionysius, of those from village [of]
7     Theadelpheia. According to the [orders] from the
8     most excellent [governor Flavius Titianus]
9     ordered, I declare
10    the property of mine existing [upon] the
11    village, half portion of a house
12    and courtyard. If indeed something concerning it
13    I [previous]ly managed
14    I will show to exist. Therefore I submit
15    the [registration].
