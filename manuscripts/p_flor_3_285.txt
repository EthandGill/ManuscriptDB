[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine lord Flavius Justinian the eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the twenty-sixth year, after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most glorious, in the year
2     the eleventh, Epeiph 19 of the first indiction.
3     To the right of the holy diakonia called of the Oasites, possessing in the mountain
4     of the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, through the most pious Danielios
5     son of Timotheos, monk and superior, Aurelius Kyriakos son of Hermauos, of the mother
6     Elisabet, oil-maker from the same village, greeting. I acknowledge willingly and irrevocably
7     that I have leased from you for a two-year period reckoned from the past
8     month Pharmouthi of the past fifteenth indiction the
9     [your] … third part of the oil-press situated in the same village
10    [of Aphrodite] on the street of Isis t … mountos, and the share belonging to you of the well there
11    [and your whole] men's-hall facing south on the second storey with an upper room
12    [and] all the appurtenances of the aforesaid oil-press facing west with all the equipment
