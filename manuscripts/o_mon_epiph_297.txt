[TRANSLATION]
1     Concerning the love, when you go down there, I have come to you,
2     behold, it is well, [for] one does not speak [the word] of men except their father and mother, and you [too] are loved
3     [and] they do speak [the] word of men [that] he speaks
4     seeing [that] you are of [such] character
5     [and] they will guard the things that are [to come], not having entered in to you
6     [and] I shall guard you upon the (word) of us
7     I have heard [it],
8     [for] many days [have] you [not] eaten [anything],
9     Concerning the love [and] the word of the brothers, then, [for] entirely I have heard [that] you safeguard their heart,
10    and indeed I will make rest [of] heart with you [as] the bishop, [proceeding] unto death
11    nor [does] the word of worldly men [apply].
