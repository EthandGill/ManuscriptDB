1     I have from you drachmas 25. Of these:
2     3 rental donkeys 14 at two-obols [each] becomes drachmas 4 four-obol
3     4 donkeys rental 14 at two-obols [each] becomes drachmas 4 four-obol
4     5 donkeys rental 14 at two-obols [each] becomes drachmas 4 four-obol
5     6 donkeys rental 13 at two-obols [each] becomes drachmas 4 two-obol
6     7 donkeys rental 13 at two-obols [each] becomes drachmas 4 two-obol

8     Account from Demetrius
9     Of brick(s) which he brought.
