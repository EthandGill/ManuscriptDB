[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios to Zenon, greetings. And [while] being in the land, […]
2     suddenly fodder will fail us for the horses, and now […]
3     I have written to you about these matters. So that … may not come to the horses […]
4     the necessaries, you would do well to have cared [for] the quickest [means] …
5     For it is not easy to buy in the city because the old [fodder] …
6     and the new has become scarce; if not, we would do this […]
7     not even for one day fodder being available. If therefore you do not quickly examine […]
8     to trouble the horses.
9     [Farewell.] …
10    Apollonios to Zenon.
11    Concerning fodder for the
12    horses. Year 29, Daisios 28,
13    in Arsinoe of Dion.
