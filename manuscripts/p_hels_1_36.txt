[TRANSLATION]
1     It was received in year 23, Thoth 14
2     To Alexander the steward
3     from Heraclides son of Heraclides, the one who undertook
4     together with partners the wine-tax privilege
5     of wine and the sixth [part] of the
6     orchards and the third [part] of the
7     bath-houses; since [he] pledges as security in a deposit
8     for me for the stated purchases of Tneferences
9     Marraios as mortgage for a house
10    and a courtyard in which grain [is]
11    in Heracleoupolis for 30 talents,
12    I request [you] to write to whom it is fitting
13    to bring to you the
14    security [agreement].
