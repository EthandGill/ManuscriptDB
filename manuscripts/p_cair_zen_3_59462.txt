1     Criton to Zeno, greetings. Well [would you do] having made a turn of attention on behalf of me, so that not still
2     a longer time I might be ruined. … [promised?] for me for the twentieth [day] to manage nothing has he done,
3     but also while I was sleeping in the field [the] sow at night from the courtyard he threw out [though] she was pregnant
4     and my wife he was summoning out saying [he would] beat [her] and me he was challenging beforehand thinking
5     inside me sleeping. Having come [and I] from the field reported to me the wife the things that happened
6     all. But I of these things nothing [revealed] until the days which he requested pass by;
7     and the sow no longer brought I into the [courtyard].
8     I beseech therefore you by the brothers and the king
9     to manage the affairs concerning me, so that no longer [these] things might happen nor still a longer time I might be maltreated by drunkenness.
10    I swear moreover to you by the king's divinity [and] Berenice [the] queen, so that not you might think insolently me
11    [to be] putting under oath him, [that I did] not receive any brick from him. You therefore take great care
12    on behalf of these [matters].
13    Farewell.
14    To Zeno.
