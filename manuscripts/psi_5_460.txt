[TRANSLATION]
1     From the embankment-inspector
2     through the assistant, to the village-heads
3     of [the district] of Twenty-five-arouras. The men
4     whose names before this [time]
5     the shore-warden already [knows], [you should make] ready
6     having [prepared them], hand them over to those
7     in charge, in order that, upon my arrival
8     tomorrow, no delay be made, but straightaway [they may make] the
9     [departure], [and] proceed to the
10    work of embankments and canals.
11    [This has been] noted.
