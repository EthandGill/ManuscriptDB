[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year twelfth of the Autocrat[or]
2     Caesar Trajan Hadrian
3     Augustus, [month of] Epeiph, 5. Sarapion
4     and [his] partners, sitologi [of the second] toparchy
5     of those around Tebtynis, have measured
6     from the produce of the same year
7     for Harpaesis Kronion of Tebtynis of [the] public wheat [by] public measure
8     sieve-adjusted, [the] measure [of] six artabas
9     [and one] quarter [and one] twenty-fourth, [excluding] carrier Talei [of] twenty
10    four. [There] comes to be wheat artabas 30 and 4 and 24.
