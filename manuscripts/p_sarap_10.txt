[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymus son of Demetrius to Anubian Sarapion's greetings; I have sold to you
2     oxen two, one indeed dark-colored, the other indeed nursing female heifer reddish-colored
3     and I have received from you the agreed-upon price
4     in hand of silver drachmas two-hundred
5     totaling silver drachmas, these same such
6     unchallenged. Year 9 of the Emperor
7     Caesar Trajan Hadrian
8     Augustus Phaophi 17
