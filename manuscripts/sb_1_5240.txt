1     [Copy] from a copy. [A case] about Satabus son of Erigeus the younger from Soknopaiou
2     [Island], who purchased in the 41st year a masterless house from the prophet, [and] acquired in the 43rd year of Caesar other empty masterless plots. The same [one] introduced
3     Nestnephus. [But] it being shown [that] the presbyters of the priests had written [a document] concerning
4     the [old] economy to them [having been] not brought forward, the plots appeared to them [as] not
5     being [of] the prophet, [the case] was tried in the second year by Seppius Rufus. The defendant
6     having been appointed, [to provide] proof either of the old [documents] or of whatever he might produce, to submit to
7     [they] ordered. And the same [requirement applied] to the presbyters submitting; [I] postponed into decision
8     [Lokretios]. [But when] it was shown [that] the prophet still now to keep the sold
9     [[plots] acquired by] him to be in his possession both by his father and [by his] ancestors, which were
10    [his paternal real property, of which] he still now holds a tower and a watchtower and an empty plot and [other appurtenances], and
11    [the complainant Nestneph]us setting forth the whole [extent] of those plots,
12    [in which is the tower] and the watchtower [and] the plots sold by the prophet [to be] a certain ancient archae[type, and to come under] his own claim, for which reason it was placed under examination,
13    [because the presbyters] did not agree in the same [way], but concerning only the sold
14    [[empty] plots [they] had writt[en], masterless ones to appear, [the case] was concluded:
15    [let [it] be demanded] for confirmation of [empty] pl[ots]
16    of Satabus [Erigeus], drachmas 500.
17    Copy of a contract. In the year 4 of Tiberi[us] Caesar
18    Augustus, Phaophi 26.
19    Satabus Erigeus has written
20    the younger from Soknopaiou Island, his own account, 3rd year
21    drachmas five-hundred, total drachmas 500.
