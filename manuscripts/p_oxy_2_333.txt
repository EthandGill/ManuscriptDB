1     Zenon to the agoranomoi, greetings. Record the purchase of Athena[i]s, also Tekosia, the
2     …os of Chairemon, of those from the city Oxyrhynchus
3     of [the] existing [thing] to the one selling in [the] [same] city [in a] quarter

5     from Heron of Heliod[orus] of Herakleios
6     of those from the same city, price in silver of 700 drachmae of copper
7     talents 52 1/3. Farewell. Year 8 of [the] [Emperor] Caesar
8     Domitian Augustus Germanicus, of the month Kaisareus
9     epagomenal 1st.
10    Zenon, decide.
11    Cancellation on the 1st of the epagomenal of a circular [order] in copper
12    against silver, 5 talents Af.
