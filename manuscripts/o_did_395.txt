[TRANSLATION]
1     sends to me saying that you are useless
2     always; to no one am I useless
3     instead of you. We will see who of us
4     is useless.
5     You will say to Marcus
6     coming
7     I will carry to you the [?] of the pig-cricket, and if you need copper you will receive from him drachmas twenty.
