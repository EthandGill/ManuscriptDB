[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios son of Apollonios son of Diogenes, from the city of Oxyrhynchos, leased to Thotes son of Zoilos son of Thoteus, from the formerly-Sarapion-son-of-Chairemon hamlet near Senepsaou, for four years from
2     the coming twelfth year, the eight arouras which he holds near Kerkethonis, on condition that in the first and third years, year by year, he sow them green at a fixed rent year by year of twenty-four artabas of wheat,
3     and in the second and last years, year by year, he sow them with wheat at a fixed rent
4     year by year of eighty artabas of wheat, and for the four-year period, year by year,
5     of extra-payments for libation eight drachmas, all free of every
6     risk, the year-by-year working of the dikes and water-guarding being the responsibility of the lessee Thoteus. And if any land becomes unflooded,
7     allowance will be made to the lessee, the year-by-year public taxes
8     on the land being the responsibility of the lessor. And the lessee acknowledges that he has from the lessor an advance of one hundred drachmas, which he will also repay to him in the month Phamenoth of the coming twelfth year, free of every
9     risk. And let the lessor have ownership of the crops until
10    he recovers his own (dues). And the lease being secured, let the lessee pay back to the lessor the advance
11    [without delay, and the] year-by-year fixed rent in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor …
12    … [wheat] new, pure, unadulterated, [free of barley, sifted, as measured into the public granary] …
