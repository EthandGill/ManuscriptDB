[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Hyginus, the most eminent prefect of Egyp[t],
2     from the community of those from the village of Philadelphia, of the 8th pagus of the Arsinoite,
3     through us, Heron and Sakaon and Kanaout. These three are those
4     set over (matters) in the village, prefect (and) lord, we who pay taxes
5     on behalf of the whole village, of 500 arouras, even though they are not irrigated, and of the poll-tax together with fiscal men, twenty-five, so that as a result the village comes utterly
6     [into] extreme straits. For a sea[rc]h we set out (after) the
7     fellow-villagers into the Oxyrhynchite, and we [ov]ertook, in the farmstead of Eulogios,
8     … uonei in the pagus of Serenus the p[raepositus], six men in all — Dionysios and
9     [So]uchidas and Hor and Ammon and Pol and Sabinus with their families; and the landholder of these, Eulogios, together with Parion the vine-dresser and Sarapion the cultivator
10    did [n]ot even permit us to approach the door of the farmstead, with insults. And moreover we also
11    [f]ound in the Kynopolite nome three other fellow-villagers among all (of them), Gerontios
12    and Pathoous and Heron, holding whole (holdings) of royal land [100] and more. Therefore we beg your authority — we being moderate and solitary men — to command
13    the one entrusted with peace-keeping to hand over to us the fellow-villagers with their families, so that we may be able, through this
14    consolation, to stand fast in the village and forever render thanks to your illustrious fortune. We shall
15    acknowledge (them). Fare well.
16    We, Aurelii Heron and Sakaon and … anaout, have submitted (this).
17    I, Aurelius Eulogios, wrote on behalf of them, they not knowing letters.
