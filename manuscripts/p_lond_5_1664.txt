[TRANSLATION]
1     [Have given] those from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolis district through [the most admirable John, subrecipient]
2     [Of the same] village for the account of canonical and gold titles [first] [payment]
3     Of the third indiction gold nomismata one hundred thirty-four and one-half of good weight simple, it amounts to gold nomismata 134 1/2
4     Of good weight simple, and for your security and of the public account I have made this order with
5     My signature as set forth. Heliodorus, ethnic goldsmith of the prefecture
6     Of the Thebaid I have made the [order] of the nomismata one hundred thirty
7     Four and one-half full simple as set forth.
