[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Abinnaeus, prefect of the wing of soldiers of the fort
2     of the village of Dionysias in the Arsinoite nome, from Aurelius
3     Sakaon son of Satabous, from the village of Theadelphia in the same
4     nome. I was suffering no small violence at the hands of Heron son of Sakaon
5     from the same village. When the praepositus of the pagus, Ision,
6     ordered the grain to be paid, the portion falling
7     upon each captarius, and I at once …
8     having paid up the portion of what fell to me … I do not know
9     by what reasoning and in brigand-like fashion the same man, contrary to justice, attacked the sheep and seized sheep,
10    unshorn, eighty-two in number. For this very reason
11    I ask and beg your benevolence that
12    you seize this man and compel him to restore to me the things wickedly plundered,
13    and then that the things written by me
14    you refer to the knowledge of my lord the duke, for it is his
15    to punish those who dare such things, and having obtained this
16    I shall acknowledge gratitude to you, lord. Farewell.
17    I, Aurelius Sakaon, have submitted (this). Aurelius Demetrios
18    wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
19    In the consulship of our lords Constantius for the 3rd time
20    and Constans for the 2nd time, Augusti, Pharmouthi 3.
