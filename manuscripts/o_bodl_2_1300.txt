[TRANSLATION]
1     It has been measured into the treasury of the metropolis, grain of [the] 16th year of Hadrian

4     through Phthuminios [son of] Dioscurides, for Charas,
5     wheat artabas four, one-third, one-twelfth; there are wheat artabas 4 [and] 1/3 [and] 1/12. Pikas wheat artabas 4 [and] 1/3 [and] 1/12.
