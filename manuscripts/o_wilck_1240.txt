[TRANSLATION]
1     Harbechis (son of) Pamonthes has paid,
2     (whose) mother is Senaryothis, for the embankment-tax of Charax
3     of year 4, 6 dr. 4 obols, and of others, 3½ obols, total 7 dr. 2½ obols, and surcharges.
4     Year 4 of Domitian the lord,
5     Epeiph 30 (and) 3(?). I, Elias, have signed.
