[TRANSLATION]
1     To Heron, the royal scribe of Phthemouthis …
2     Sarapion of Potamon, [having served as high priest] [of the]
3     Tauaeitae city, to the most honored [greetings]
4     Since my father Potamon [was permitted by]
5     Magnus the most mighty to lead [to a vineyard]
6     in the nome of Phthemouthis six arouras and these …
7     he restored in only four arouras, and [when you were asking] me whether at another time they were in a vineyard [I said]
8     these had been in a vineyard still [from god]
9     Trajan's time and for proof [I] set before you the things written below
