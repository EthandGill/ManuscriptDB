[TRANSLATION]
1     To Boethos, [kinsman] and epistrategos and strategos of the
2     [Thebaid, from Thortaios and Senn]ouphis his wife, of those from [Pathyris].
3     [We are being wronged by Patous son of] Psenmonthes and his sons
4     [Nebchounis and Orseus and Patseto]us and the wife of these, [Kalibis, of those from] the same city. For though we held a
5     [copy of an Egyptian concession] translated into Greek as far as possible.
6     A copy of another concession. [In the year] 34, Pharmouthi. In the reign of Ptolemy Euergetes
7     son of Ptolemy and Kleopatra, the manifest gods, and of queen Kleopatra
8     the sister, and of queen Kleopatra the wife, goddess Euergetis, and the rest in common:
9     Thus says the Greek woman Ammonia daughter of Ptolemy, also called Senminis, and the woman
10    Apollonia, all (of them), also called Senmonthis, and Herakleia daughter of Ptolemy, also called Senapathis,
11    and Herais daughter of Ptolemy, also called Tiesris — the four daughters of Ptolemy speaking with one
12    voice to the scribe Nechouthes son of Patous, of the mother, the nurse-woman
13    Tamenotes, and to the woman Sennouphis daughter of Patous, of the mother, the nurse-
14    woman Tamenotes: … we petitioned against you
15    concerning the 35 arouras … they come to 34½ ¼ 1/8 1/16 1/32 arouras, they come to 35 arouras again,
16    and of the broken land adjoining, which the contracted lessee Hermokrates son of
17    Apollonios, also called Panas, son of Nechthyris, made — a contract of purchase to the salaried
18    Ptolemy son of Hermokrates, also called Pamenotes, son of Panas, our father,
19    against them in the 20th year, Epeiph, in the reign of the ever-living one … [and] you have produced contracts of purchase and of cession against them, which
20    the salaried man of the company of Nikanor, Proitos son of Sosikrates, made for the woman Tamenotes
21    daughter of Panas, your mother, in the 21st year, Pharmouthi 15, and [of others] which she made against them, written in the 19th year, Choiak, in the reign
22    of the ever-living one. And there is nothing whatsoever at all between us and you concerning the
23    [aforementioned] arouras; but if at a later time we petition against you,
24    [we shall pay you] … and to the royal treasury one thousand drachmas of silver.
