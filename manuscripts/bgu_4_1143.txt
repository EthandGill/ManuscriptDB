[TRANSLATION]
1     To Achaios.
2     From Montanos son of Diogenes, exegete, and from Papiskos son of Papos, a Persian of the epigone. Concerning the security that has been established,
3     so that it may be completed: Papiskos agrees, as to what he still owes against the agreement which he submitted to Montanos through the
4     same court in the times before,
5     of light (jars), double measure, six thousand jars
6     ⟨…⟩ — the remaining one thousand jars
7     he will pay to him by the fifth of Hathyr
8     of the coming thirteenth year
9     of Caesar, at the property belonging to Diogenes,
10    … in the so-called … of the …
11    … oared(?) and strained and re-strained and burnt with the proper firing,
12    … and neither … nor … these things he will do without
13    judgement and any dispute; and if
14    he transgress any of these things, he is to be
15    liable to seizure and held in custody until he
16    pays the price of whatever he does not deliver
17    — of a jar — the established sum of silver
18    drachmas … and other, as a penalty,
19    one thousand drachmas of silver, and the damages
20    and expenses of the exaction being made for Montanos or for Diogenes
21    both from Papiskos himself and from all his
22    possessions, as if by a legal judgement,
23    those being void, and whatever pledges
24    he may bring forward, and all protection; and the
25    agreement which the same Papiskos has submitted
26    to Montanos …
