1     To Flavius Sarapammon and Serenus
2     the honorable proximi of this Oxyrhynch
3     from Philoxenus, a carpenter, from the same [city]
4     ... ε ... a certain woman setting forth from this
5     [of the city], being a neighbor to me,
6     ... ς for the sake of me spending time
7     in the marketplace, she entered into my
8     room with men with her
9     disagreeing [and] she attacked my
10    ... unbearable outrages
11    ... η of all
12    [of the] [neighbors] of mine testifying.
13    For this reason I beseech your
14    prompt attention to command her to be brought
15    and hear us and the matter [being presented to your prompt attention] to be brought forward.
