[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of the treasury of the metropolis, produce of the 24th year of Marcus Aurelius Severus
2     Antoninus Caesar of the lord, Thoth of the 25th year, for
3     receipts of the 24th year, for Charax, [of the] name of Sarapion [of] Porieutos, of wheat,
4     [one-half], 12, [they] become of wheat, [one-half] artabae, 12, and [of the] name of the same [person], for South, of wheat,
5     quarter, [they] become of wheat, [one-]quarter artaba, [they] become together of wheat, [one-half] [three-quarters] artabae. A [has] marked.
