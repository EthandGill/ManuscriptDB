[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the rei]gn of Ptolemy and Cleopatra [also called]
2     [T]rypha[e]na, the gods Philop[a]tores Philadelphoi, in the [9th] ye[ar,]
3     and as for the rest of the common (formulae), as is written at Alexandria, [in the month]
4     Apell[a]ios and (=) Phaophi 15, at Oxyrhynchos in the
5     Thebaid. Sarapion son of Apollonios, a Mace[donian]
6     of the katoikoi cavalry, leased to Apollonios also called Pan…
7     son of Ischyrion also called Nechthenibis, a Persian of the epigone,
8     [the] two (being) of the street of Cleopatra Aphrodite, from his own allotment,
9     fifteen arouras which he holds near Sepho, for the ninth year,
10    so that, of the arouras in the allotment of Diocles, three
11    he shall sow with wheat; and of the twelve arouras in (the allotment) of Philon,
12    the one half with barley and the other half with lentils, at a rent for each aroura, by survey, of the (land) given for
13    sowing with cereals, of four artabas. And Apollonios acknowledges
14    that he has from Sarapion, for seed
15    and for the other expense of the sowing, free of interest,
16    [of barle]y seven and a half artabas, and of lentils
17    [seven and a] half artabas, and for the cutting of reeds on the land, non-returnable,
18    [of copper one thou]sand five hundred drachmas, free from risk
19    [and from deduction] from all risk. And concerning the rents,
20    apart from the taxes, if anything be exacted of Apollonios to the royal treasury
21    or for any[thing else in whatso]ever manner on behalf of Sarapion
22    or [of the land, let it be deducted from the re]nts; and let Sarapion [be lord of the crops until he recovers the rents]
23    and
24    the 9th year. Lease …
