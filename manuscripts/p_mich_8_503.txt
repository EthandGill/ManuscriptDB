[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion to Antonius Minor, his dearest, greeting.
2     When I was sailing down to Alexandria, I made it my urgent concern to come
3     to the lodging-house of the people from your estate, the one in Memphis, so that
4     from there I might write to you, and I found no one going to you. But having sailed up with the prefect and met Marcus
5     my friend, I sought you, in case you had been there, and not finding you
6     I made inquiries about your welfare, and having learned
7     that you were faring well, I went up rejoicing. So I write to you now,
8     most honored one, first of all greeting you and
9     my lord Ammonianus and all your household,
10    then earnestly asking that, by all means, through Morion's own men,
11    you lend me three full-grown oxen, fine and large in body,
12    [that will provide rest]. For I have need of them for this. So
13    I urge that you make haste to accomplish this for me, in order that
14    you may also make known to me their price, and from here
15    I will send it to you. For to Morion's own man, who was pressed
16    to depart, I could not give the price, since it was uncertain
17    … it was bought. I pray for your health, my lord,
18    together with my lord Ammonianus and
19    all your household.
20    Send me the oxen by all means, since I have need of them
21    for the estates. And if you have an occasion to come
22    to Alexandria, come to me there by all means, even now
23    as I am going to come there, with the gods' help.
24    … (illegible) …
25    To Antonius Minor, who has become royal scribe of the Diopolite (nome), the great.
