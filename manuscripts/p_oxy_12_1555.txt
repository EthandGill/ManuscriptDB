[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Ptolemaios the
2     and Nemesianus, strategos of Oxyrhyncha
3     Aurelius Philantinoos
4     also called Moros son of Besarion
5     and as I am [registered].
6     I swear by the fortune of our lords
7     Macrianus and Quietus
8     the Augustii, willingly and voluntarily
9     to guarantee Aurelius
10    Serenus son of Kastor
11    mother Thermouthis from the city of Oxyrhyncha,
12    whom I will [present to you]
