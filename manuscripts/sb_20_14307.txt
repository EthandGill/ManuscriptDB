[TRANSLATION]
1     Year twenty-first of Autocrator
2     Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus.
3     [Someone] worked for embankment works for the 20th year
4     from 48, 21st year, from Athyr 7 to 11
5     in the embankment of the grove of Kerkesis of Tebtyneos
6     Kronion [and] Onnophres son of Apollonius
7     [of] mother Thenapygches.
8     Heron, I have signed.
