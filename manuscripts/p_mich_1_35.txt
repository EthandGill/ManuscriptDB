[TRANSLATION]
1     Isingas to Zenon, greetings. Concerning the silver which I pledged
2     (to) Nuraios, which is 80 drachmas, [he does] not [wish to] return to me. You would do well therefore, having written to Panakestor [or Artemidoros] in order that [it] may be collected and to me
3     be returned.
4     Farewell. Year 31, Choiak 19.
5     Year 31, Choiak 19. To Zenon.
6     Isingas (against) Nuraios, 80 drachmas.
