[TRANSLATION]
1     Liberalius to Kronion greetings.
2     I sent on to you the donkey …
3     with the cattle. [it] stands until …
4     which I arrive, and the vineyard plots [let them be] carried [to] Teptunin. Receive beside …
5     of Bellas eight drachmas and give …
6     to Isido[r]; [he] also has of mine other [drachmas] …
7     eight drachmas and [let them be] carried [and] 20 loads of reeds into the half fourth. Say to Protis, if I do not arrive until [the fourth day of] Egyptian [months] toward …
8     you, make use of the beside my treasurer …
9     40 drachmas and send [them] upon rope-fiber.
10    Say nothing to her unless on the fourth [day].
