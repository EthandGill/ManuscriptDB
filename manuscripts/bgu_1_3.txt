[TRANSLATION]
1    ✠ In the name of the holy and consubstantial Trinity
2    and of our Lady the God-bearer (Theotokos)
3    and of all the saints. In the reign of our most pious
4    lord Flavius Phocas, the eternal Augustus,
5    the third year after the consulship of the same — year
6    two — Pauni 13, at the close of the eighth indiction, in Arsinoe.
7    (2nd hand) Aurelius Menas, son of Elias, rent-
8    collector of the estate of Theodosakios the most
9    glorious general (stratēlatēs), from the city of the Arsinoites,
10   from the Lower quarter, to Aurelius
11   Kyrikos, pork-butcher, son of Menas, from the
12   same city, greetings. I acknowledge, of my own free will, that I have
13   agreed with you that, if at any
14   time I seek to expel you from
15   the dwelling under you, which belongs to Theodo-
16   sakios the most glorious general,
17   you being unwilling to depart from it,
18   then I am to provide you the expenses laid out
19   by you for the construction of the farmstead
20   there; but if of your own accord you wish
21   to withdraw from the same place,
22   you are to take nothing for those same
23   expenses incurred by
24   you in the same farmstead. This agreement is valid,
25   and on being formally questioned I gave my assent. ✠ (3rd hand) Menas the rent-
26   collector, the aforesaid; the agreement is [valid?] for me
27   as stated above. Elias (son of) John,
28   I wrote on his behalf.
29   (4th hand) ✠ through me, Elias.
30   ✠ Agreement made by Aurelius Menas the rent-
31   collector, son of Elias, with Aurelius Kyrikos
32   the pork-butcher. ✠
