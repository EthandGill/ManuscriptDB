[TRANSLATION]
1   Ammonios to Sabinus, his most honoured (friend),
2   very many greetings.
3   Apollinarius wrote to me that he had been allotted
4   to Valerius Pius and had become his secutor (attendant),
5   and at the same time making clear that Valerius should write
6   to him (Pius), thanking him and welcoming
7   the goodwill and right hand shown toward him,
8   so that, wherever he goes, he may have him with him-
9   self. Therefore I urge you, brother, in my stead
10   once more to act and to prompt
11   Valerius to write to Pius, persuaded
12   by my pledge and right hand, so that on every side
13   you may be not only a helper of the matter
14   but also a good steersman, gratifying
15   a son to (his) father. And if somehow you should bind(?)
16   [...], it being the (right) hour to sail down, make (it) known
17   to me, and I will come to you more quickly. Indeed
18   I am persuaded that you will not hesitate at all in the
19   aforesaid (matters). Before all things I pray for your
20   health.
21   Farewell to me, most honoured Sabinus.
22   To Julius Sabinus, standard-bearer ☓, from Ammonios (his) friend.
