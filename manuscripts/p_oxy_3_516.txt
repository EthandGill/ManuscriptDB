[TRANSLATION]
1   Dionysios son of Faustus also called
2   Amphion, of the sacred victors
3   and former exegetes of the city of the Oxy(rhynchites),
4   through Horion (his) secretary,
5   to the sitologoi of the middle toparchy, district of Kerkeurosis,
6   greeting. Pay out
7   from what you hold on deposit of mine,
8   of wheat of the produce of the past 23rd year
9   of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
10   to Apion son of Apion, artabas
11   twenty-five and a half, nine choin(ikes),
12   total 25 1/2 art., 9 choin. Year 24 of Antoninus
13   Caesar the lord, Hathyr 21.
14   I, Apion son of Apion, presented (it).
