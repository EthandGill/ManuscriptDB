[GREEK]

r.2   διὰ Παχομπετενεφώτ(ου) βουηθ . διέγρ(αψεν)
r.3   Μενόφιλος μίζω(ν) Ὀρβάι τος μητ(ρὸς) Τισᾶτις

r.7   Παῦνι ιη. Ἀμμώνιος σεσημ(είωμαι).

[TRANSLATION]
1   Ammonios and Sok(rates), the two collectors of the money-tax of Elephantine,
2   through Pachompetenephotes the assistant. Paid:
3   Menophilos the elder, son of Orbaeis, mother Tisatis,
4   and Harpaesis his son, for the assessments
5   of the 23rd year, each man 1 drachma (and) a half-obol. Year 23
6   of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
7   Pauni 18. I, Ammonios, have signed off.
