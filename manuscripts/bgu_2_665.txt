[TRANSLATION]
1     … (he) with his own people speaking about her, saying that
2     "Behold, I have convinced him, and he has taken her up,
3     he was eager to go up, so that he might entreat you
4     to take care of her." But he does not have a good opportunity, for the office of the arabarch has come to an end and he is about to wind up the
5     things left to him; to such an extent was he about
6     to send his brother on a fair voyage, so that he might
7     entreat you, since he himself is occupied. And I said to him,
8     "I will write to my father, first about her childbirth, the more pressing matter, and then also
9     about the reconciliation." Therefore I entreat you, father,
10    first to embark with her at the very end of Mecheir or the middle of Phamenoth, so that
11    she may arrive here in time to give birth, which is necessary, and
12    because of your salary; for Theon enjoined, when reading off, that he receives the very first (share), and it is necessary
13    that he be present. Everything has been prepared for her
14    for the lying-in sufficiently; and they ask too, sir,
15    his mother, that she may be delivered and …
16    … I entreat you, father, that out of her you … her …
17    … the little one … and the elder woman, the one of
18    Herakl… …
19    and not … …
20    and … …
21    … and he is coming. Greet Cornelia and Severus
22    and Eision and [So]terichos
23    and Dioskoros. I pray for
24    [your] health, O father.
25    Year 13, Tybi 20.
26    I was distressed concerning
27    the horse. Do not think
28    that I neglect to write
29    to you; we have no one through whom
30    we may send.
