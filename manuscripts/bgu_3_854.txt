[TRANSLATION]
1   [Thases, daughter of Panephremmis, my mother being] Thermouthis: I acknowledge [that I have sold] to Herieus, daughter of Sambathion, her mother being Thases, the two-storey house belonging to me with all its appurtenances,
2   [all of them, at Soknopaiou Neso]s in the Herak[leides division of the Arsinoite no]me,
3   the neighbors of the whole house being: on the south and west the royal road, on the north the house of Tesenouphis,
4   [over which his children hold control,] on the east the [house] of Herieus daughter of Sambathion;
5   and I have received at once, from hand to hand out of the house, the whole agreed price
6   [in full, and I will guarantee it] with every guarantee from the present day for all time, and I will do the rest as written above, and I have in-
7   [structed the witnesses to wri]te, and that the deed be drawn up by the clerk at the record-office. Papais son of Pa..ses wrote on her behalf because she does not know
8   [letters. I, Herieus daughter of Samba]tion, my mother being Thases, have bought as aforesaid. Leontas son of Eirenaios wrote on her behalf because she does not
9   [know letters. Sale and ces]sion of a two-storey house [and] all its appurtenances at Soknopaiou Nesos in the Herakleides division, the neighbors of the whole
10   [house being: on the south and west the roy]al [road], on the north [the house of Tesenouphis, over which] his children hold control, on the east the house of Herieus daughter of Sambathion; and she has the price and gives guarantee — done by Thases
11   [daughter of Panephremmis, mother Thermo]uthis, aged about 3(?), a mole on the left of her forehead; and Herieus daughter of Sambathion, mother Thases, aged about 38, likewise a mole on the left of her forehead. Subscriber:
12   […] … and the other (signatory): Leontas son of Eirenaios, aged about 20, without distinguishing mark. In the fifth year of Tiberius Claudius
13   [Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Pa]chon 20. Registered through the record-office at Soknopaiou Nesos.
