r.6    καθὼς καὶ πολλάκις ὑμῖν ἔγραψα, τὸν ἰχθὺν καὶ τὰς περιστερὰς ἀνενέγ και καὶ δέλφακας μικρὰς δ καὶ εἴ τι ἄλλο ἐ[φ]εύρηται χρησιμεῦον πρὸς τὴν ἡμέραν.
r.7    ἐρρῶσθαι ὑμᾶς εὔχομαι.

[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Pallates and [Pene]phis, [our] dearest, greetings.
2     … the measuring proceeds well; if it should be fine, if not postpone
3     [until] either I or the strategus arrives there. For the strategus promised this.
4     [post]pone until I depart; but if you get ahead and remove the grain
5     [re]port to me so that I may have the strategus write to the grain-measurers. Permit Aphynchius to deposit his female slaves in the portico, and from the night of the 27th,
6     just as I have also written to you many times, [bring up] the fish and the pigeons and the small plates and 4, and if anything else [is] found useful for the day.
7     I pray that you are in good health.
