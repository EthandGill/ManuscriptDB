[TRANSLATION]
1     From part of a decision of the god Hadrian.
2     In the 16th(?) year of Hadrian Caesar the lord, Hathyr 17, Apollonides
3     against Claudius Antoninus. Among other things, Aelius Hadrianus (said):
4     "Even earlier I declared to you that my decision helps you,
5     and I think that Philotera, being a most excellent (woman) and, to the
6     best (of my judgement), known to me, will do you no wrong, and especially
7     since she knows that unjust possession has no force; but you, concerning matters
8     that are not in dispute, wish to trouble me, having the agent of the creditor who will restore the persons to you."
9     Flavius Iuncinus (said): "In accordance with the decisions of our lord
10    (Apollonides) shall recover the persons (slaves) and the works,
11    (namely) Apollonides, from Antoninus the heir."
12    Julius Theon, of the sacred victors and exempt (persons), neokoros of the great Sarapis, former archidikastes,
13    priest and hypomnematographos: rising to (form) a council and taking counsel with those present,
14    he dictated a decision, which was also read out word for word as follows: "… from the sales and from the
15    address of the architects and from
16    the personal inspection which I made, the house
17    belongs to Ptolema. For concerning the possession we
18    need seek nothing, being bound to revere the decisions, read out, of the god Trajan and of our lord
19    Hadrian Caesar Augustus."
