[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion to Dionysius, greetings.
2     For just as indeed [they] have written to you by hand
3     the porters and the handlers
4     to me … lighten the wheat. Well then
5     you will do by ordering [them] to hand over to me the 40 artabas on behalf of
6     which [they] have written to you by hand
7     as that [already] to us it is being given.
8     If indeed I have not [received] them I have mixed them with you. And you yourself those which if
9     … clarify [them] to me.
10    Farewell. Of the 29th year, Epeiph 27.
