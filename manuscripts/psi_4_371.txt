[TRANSLATION]
1     Zenon to Laboetes greetings. Measure out for Perdikkas
2     grain so that Patroklos [has] a grain-distribution from the month Pachon of year 36
3     until Epeiph [for] 3 months, of the month wheat artaba 1½, making wheat artabas 4½, of which medical [allowance]
4     wheat artaba ½, remaining wheat artabas 4
5     To Eutychides [for] the month[s] Pauni and Epeiph wheat artabas 3
6     To Ptolemaios the scribe similarly wheat artabas 4
7     To Ballion similarly of the month wheat artabas 1½, making wheat artabas 3
8     To Eutychos similarly wheat artabas 3
9     To Choerine the weaver similarly wheat artabas 3
10    To Horos the superintendent of building works similarly wheat artabas 3, of which medical [allowance] ½, remaining wheat artabas 2½
11    To Alkimos the vineyard-worker who presided over the thirty-aroura [plots] and the
12    Glaucos and [Lysimachos'] newly-planted vineyards of Epeiph wheat artabas 2
13    and the measurements [for] each [worker] wheat artabas 1½, making wheat artabas 15, of which
14    [shortage/remaining] ... [from] idleness wheat artaba ½ [and] ¼, remaining wheats artabas 14 [and] ¼
15    To Noumenios the horse-keeper of Pauni and Epeiph wheats artabas 3, of which
16    medical [allowance] wheat artaba ½, remaining wheats artabas 2½
17    To Stephanos similarly wheat artabas 3, of which medical [allowance] wheat artaba ½, remaining wheats artabas 2½
18    To Heliodoros of Epeiph wheat artaba 1½
19    To Aristomachos similarly wheat artaba 1½
20    To those under Hegessilaos the horse-keepers Apollonios [and] Horos
21    of Epeiph each wheat artaba 1, making wheat artabas 2
22    To Pare of Epeiph wheat artaba 1½
23    [in total] wheat artabas 50 [and] ¼
24    Farewell. Year 36, Epeiph 2.
25    To Laboetes
