[TRANSLATION]
1   Cornelius to Hierax his sweetest son,
2   greeting.
3   We all greet you warmly, all those at home, and
4   (we greet) all those with you. About the man you write
5   me of so often: take no notice of him
6   until I come to you, with good fortune, with Ves-
7   tinus and the donkeys. For if the gods will,
8   I shall come to you quickly after the month Mecheir,
9   since I have pressing business in hand. See that you
10   quarrel with none of the people in the house, but
11   attend only to your books, studying,
12   and from them you will have profit. Receive by On-
13   nophras the white clothes that can
14   be worn with the purple cloaks,
15   the others you will wear with the myrtle-colored ones.
16   By Anoubas I will send you both money and
17   monthly supplies and the other pair, the scarlet ones.
18   You won us over with the little fish; their
19   price too I will send you by Anoubas — still,
20   until Anoubas comes to you, pay out from your own
21   bronze your allowance and your people’s,
22   until I send it. For the month Tybi there is:
23   for you, what you like; for Phronimos 16 dr.; for Abask(antos’) people
24   and for Myron 9 dr.; for Sekoundos 12 dr. Send
25   Phronimos to Asklepiades in my
26   name, and let him take from him the an-
27   swer to the letter I wrote him, and send it.
28   Tell me what you want. Farewell, my child.
29   Tybi 16.
30   To Hierax my [s]on, from Cornelius his father.
