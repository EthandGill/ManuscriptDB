1     … …
2     … having sailed to you; and concerning the price … …
3     … a [t]alent is sold here at 48 drachmas and …
4     … …has been transported(?) to Oxyrhyncha, another 20 drachmas, so that
5     [the tal]ent comes to 68 drachmas in all; you will say, whence is your exact information.
6     … Heraklas the secretary of Sirion, called Ischas, … for trade thus, 75 talents. If
7     … Dionysios having brought your loan(?), go to
8     … Ischas and buy; and concerning your outstanding matter
9     … I (am) with Diogas daily, and the advocate
10    … [the] memorandum, God willing, in the two days … . But Diogas says that I am spending another mina
11    … of the agreement, but I said that it was not on these terms
12    [that I sailed] down, and he said that Spartakos told me here
13    … lies … of Ammonios, they are assigned to the 6th
14    with much entreaty that they give me the small change.
15    And concerning the fodder of the little pigs, do not neglect to buy for me, as I also asked you in person. And the lord
16    Caesar entered on the 30th at the 2nd hour, first into the camp,
17    … into the Sarapeion, from the Sarapeion to the hippodrome … . And the records of the prices you will learn when I sail up … (greet?) you all. Year 3 of the Emperor Caesar
18    … Vespasian Augustus, of the month Germanikeios, 4.
