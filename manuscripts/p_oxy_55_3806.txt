[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings and good health.
2     … …onios sailing up, I deemed it necessary to [greet] you through writing and to ask you to write
3     to me about whatever you wish. For most gladly I will do. The
4     your brother every day I sit beside,
5     does not wish to send you letters. The sample of the
6     [iris-dyed] show to Philous and write to me if it pleases her or not. But I am more convinced it will please.
7     For all work I gave except also foreign
8     sample to have given to the dyer, and nevertheless more beautiful
9     this came out. The other things [of yourself] take care so that you are well.
10    and write to me responses to the first. Be well to me
11    in spirit. Farewell.
12    Year 1 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus. Pachon 26.
13    … [scribe] of Korragos.
