[TRANSLATION]
1     To Valerius Ziper, the most eminent prefect
2     of Herculian Egypt,
3     from Aurelius Aithiopas son of Kanis, a minor, with his guardian Sakaon, a paternal relative,
4     from the village of Theadelphia of the Arsinoite. The wronged
5     orphans, my lord prefect, your greatness is accustomed to defend.
6     I myself, then, being left an orphan, deprived of both my parents,
7     am wronged in no small measure by the sister of my grandfather on my father's side, Annous, whom it were rather her duty to restrain others should they commit injustice against me;
8     for she, having cast her eye on the choice livestock of my patrimony, now for the first time
9     is coming against me, plainly reckoning as a windfall the death of my father, against whom, while he lived, she made no complaint nor stirred anything concerning
10    whatever it might be, having already come to a great old age; for she appears to have lived
11    beyond sixty years, during which she kept the peace up to the present. Wherefore —
12    it being clear that it is superfluous now to bring up matters she did not press, neither against
13    my grandfather, whose sister she is, nor against my father — I beg of your
14    virtue to order, if it seem good to you, that the woman's insolence be cut off
15    by whatever means you approve — for indeed my father completed three grain-collectorships, in respect of which
16    he is a debtor — and, obtaining this, I shall acknowledge my thanks to you forever. Farewell.
17    I, Aurelius Aithiopas, have submitted this through myself, my guardian. I, Aurelius Agathos, wrote on his behalf,
18    as he does not know letters. Pauni 16.
19    The one who is in possession, the logistes will
20    protect from violence. Sheet 65
21    of roll 2.
