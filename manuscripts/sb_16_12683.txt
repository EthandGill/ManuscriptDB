1     of the year 33 of Lucius Aelius Aurelius [Commodus] Caesar's
2     of the lord Athyr 1. Ammonios also [Syros] of Ammonios
3     and … -ana and as I conduct business and associates grain collectors of the field Outside Pseur we have measured by measure
