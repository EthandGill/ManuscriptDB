r.1    Ἀπολλώνιος Ἀμεννεῖ χαίρειν. τὸ προσάγγελμα τοῦ μεμετρημένου σίτου ἐξαποστελοῦμεν τῆι κα. σύμμειξον οὖν ἡμῖν

[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios to Ameneus, greetings. The report of the measured grain we send on the 21st. Mix/combine then for us
2     to the city of Crocodilopolis on this very day bringing the record
3     of that conveyed through you to the royal treasury up to the … so that
4     this [grain] be as much as possible, take care lest carelessly. For those who in any way have been negligent in these matters we shall bring charges
5     as having wronged regarding business accounts. So that not through you be held
6     the report to be sent—let this concern you
7     Farewell. Year 8, Pachon 16
8     to the toparch Ameneus, regarding the report
