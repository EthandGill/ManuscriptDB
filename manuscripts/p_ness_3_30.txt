[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the n]ame of our Lord and Master Jesus Christ, [Son] of God and our Saviour, in the reign of our most divine and most pious Master Flavius Mauricius Tiberius, faithful in Christ, [the gentle, the greatest benefactor, Emperor Augustus, in the fifteen]th [year], after the consulship of the same our most pious Emperor, in the twelfth year, on the Ides of September, the twenty-sixth day of the month Gorpiaios, in the times of the fifteenth indiction, [in the village of Nessana of the territory of the city of Elousa.] …
2     he has used the public notice in the public place of this village. Zonainos, most God-beloved presbyter, and Georgios, most admirable goldsmith, both brothers of the same parentage … [together]
3     also with Elias, their most admirable brother of the same parentage, now residing in the great city of the Alexandrians, in accordance with the things enjoined upon them by him with regard to … [the]
4     succession of Sara, daughter of Victor, their most blessed kinswoman; and they say that a little while ago Sara daughter of Victor, the aforesaid most blessed cousin, being about to …
5     of which she made a will before the witnesses approved by the law, by which she resolved that we and Elias our most admirable brother [should be her] heirs … [those who according to the]
6     laws are called after us to her succession, willing us to be her heirs, unless we should be content with her estate by some legacy … us …
7     we reckoned from afar to provide in advance for such an estate. Since therefore Elias our most admirable brother, being of the same mind …
8     … all … both the collectors of the public dues and those after us according to kinship …
9     … of the village … and Elias our most admirable brother of the estate, that is …
10    … of what was left behind, we and Elias our most admirable brother having given license …
11    … in whatever manner they wish, a valid agreement being preserved for us upon the …
12    … of the city of the Elousenians, we adjured by the Holy Trinity and by the salvation of …
13    … the customary days …
