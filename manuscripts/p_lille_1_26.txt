[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to his father greetings. You wrote to me concerning the land for sesame,
2     and especially concerning that in Patōn; this indeed is completely abandoned and the village desolate on account of not having been rained upon for a long time; but the remaining l[and],
3     [I wond]er if you do not follow all things according to what is in the description [of the]
4     [for the] fifteenth year seed to have been leased to the farmers; and from the earlier part
5     [make clear] as much as you wish and in which villages it will be possible for us to take and transact [matters]
6     [the] supply from the royal [treasury], but concerning this part it will be necessary to defer
7     [until] we learn about what has been settled in the nome.
8     Farewell. Year 14, Tybi 7.
9     To Apollonius.
