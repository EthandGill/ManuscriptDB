[TRANSLATION]
1     We make known to your fatherly holiness that on the twenty-seventh of
2     the present month Petros the Pentapolite, having arrived at the holy monastery
3     of Aphroditous from Antinoou, delivered to the most God-loving abba
4     Nonnas a letter of Menas son of Arsas, who met him at Pabeeit,
5     this letter containing (the message): "do (me) the kindness of telling the most God-loving
6     abba Andreas that I am going ahead and going up to Antinoou, and all the
7     latest news that I learn I will write to you." For your holiness knows the
8     ways of the man, and unless we write — especially since it grieved us not moderately,
9     the hindrance of his journey up; for if he finds boldness with the most extraordinary general and consul, he cannot keep quiet, but will even raise dust
10    entirely against the monasteries and against each one of us. And not very
11    many days ago we had written to the most God-fearing abba Menas, the (son) of the steward,
12    to receive a letter from the most illustrious lord count Tzanke to the
13    [mo]st mag[ni]ficent Petechon, reminding him about the things wickedly done
14    [by ] … [ w]ith Pelagios the mag[ister] in Linou polis in the year before last,
15    so that he might not find boldness; and [up to] now we have not even received an answer
16    about this. So let your [fath]erly God-love be entreated to bear in mind
17    about this, in addition to (the matter) that by your zeal the unholy man be removed from
18    our monasteries. Writing these things, we do much reverence and salute
19    your fatherly holiness, embracing it in the union of the Spirit.
20    union.
21    To our master, who is truly in all things most God-loving and most holy father abba Georgios, superior of the monastery of Metanoia,
22    Ioannes, the pitiable, your worshipper.
