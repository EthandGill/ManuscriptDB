[TRANSLATION]
1     Isidoros, tenant of a house
2     of the temple-keepers of the great god
3     Sarapis, to Ptollarion, former gymnasiarch
4     Greetings. I have received from you from which
5     you have produce-rents of the present
6     fourteenth year
7     of Antoninus [Caesar], the lord
8     which you have of arouras around the village
9     Tebtunis and the Herakleides settlement
10    on account of the aforesaid temple wheat artabas
11    Sixty; there are wheat artabas 60. Year 14
12    of Antoninus Caesar, the lord,
13    Mesore 14.
