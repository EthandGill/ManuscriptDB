[TRANSLATION]
1     During the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and of the son Ptolemy, year
2     twenty-second, during the priesthood of Pelops of
3     Alexander, Ale[x]andros, and of the Brother Gods,
4     [can]ephor of Arsinoe Philad[el]phus, M[n]esistratus daughter of Tisarch, month Xandic[us], of the Egyptians month Mech[ir], fourteenth,
5     in Muchinaron of the Oxyrhynchite (nome). Sureties:
6     Timocles son of Simon, Thracian, of the younger generation,
7     Mnasson Sim[on] Thracian of the younger generation, Hegem[on] … imon Cr[ete]an of the younger generation, on the condition that [they hand over him] in [Heracl]es city upon
8     Crisiop[pus] … of the [s]t[r]ateg[u]s, until judgment concerning
9     a suit for which Apollonius had guaranteed him,
10    according to written [agreement] for the principal three hundred drachmas and interest drachmas
11    one hundred. But if they do not hand over according to
12    the written terms, they shall pay back the four hundred drachmas and the ten-part [surcharge] [and]
13    the [accruing charges], and the enforcement/recovery [shall be to Apol]loni[us]
14    or to another of [Crisiop]pus [or of] the tax-collector serving according to the [decree].
