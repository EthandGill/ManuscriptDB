[TRANSLATION]
1     27.
2     Copy.
3     To Aurelius Ploution, secretary of the grain-dole,
4     from Aurelius Agathos Daimon son of Areios,
5     grandson of Sarapion, his mother being Senpsois,
6     from the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites.
7     Being an Oxyrhynchite and having been scrutinized
8     in the quarter of the Dromos of Thoeris, and having reached, in the present 2nd year, the age of 20 years, by lot I introduce myself in place of Antiochos
9     son of Antiochos also called Diogenes, son of Antiochos,
10    his mother being Theodora, who was of the [same]
11    quarter, deceased; and I request,
12    showing myself to be a citizen,
13    to share in the distribution of the grain-dole.
14    Year [2 of Imperator]
15    Caesar Marcus Aurelius Claudius
16    Pius Felix Augustus,
17    Thoth 27. I, Aurelius Agathos Daimon son of Areios, have submitted (this). I, Aurelius
18    Apollonios, wrote on his behalf
19    as he does not know letters.
20    I, Aurelius Serenos, scrutineer: this is the one scrutinized,
21    who, having been proclaimed, answered.
22    I, Aurelius Didymos, former phylarch: it is so,
23    and he is himself the aforesaid, who, having been proclaimed, answered.
24    I, Aurelius Theon, guarantor: this is the one examined,
25    whom I also recognize.
26    I, Aurelius Saras also called Isidoros, guarantor: this is the one
27    examined, whom I also recognize.
28    Registered in the 2nd year, Phaophi.
29    Of the Dromos of Thoeris.
