[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our master Theodosius the
2     eternal Augustus for the 2nd time and of Flavius Cynegius the most illustrious.
3     To Aurelius Zenodotos son of …pios, ex-magistrate of Hermopolis
4     the most illustrious, riparius of the Hermopolite nome,
5     from Flavius Isidoros, of the beneficiarii of the office of the governorship
6     of the Thebaid. Having indeed already on a previous occasion, and often before, made complaint
7     to you, I accused Jonas son of Euos and Hatres son of Herminos, shepherds
8     from the village of Telbonthis, malefactors, as having let loose
9     their flocks of … not once, not twice,
10    not three times, into our crops … them … di…nanto. But since no correction has been made against them, on this account again on the day before yesterday,
11    which is Pachon … ōd … ou …
12    customarily the … ō … oi … ch …
13    into our irrigation-machine they let loose (the flocks) to drink …
14    their own, in my own fields, they laid claim …
15    our shepherd … bios by name, … from …
16    and they, stripping (him) at once, with cudgels … n …
17    tearing apart the garment lying upon [him], they took it away,
18    then they cut him down with blows, both upon his legs and
19    upon the other limbs of his body, leaving him half-dead, as is also evident from what was reported by
20    those who inspected the wounds, his own
21    survival being uncertain. On this account also now I submit to your Sagacity
22    these my petitions (libelli), bearing witness and requesting that they
23    be kept in safety until the fortunate arrival of my lord
24    the magistrate; for I am most ready to prove, in his pure
25    court, these men to be acknowledged robbers and (men) who have driven off animals
26    many times, and to show that Jonas, the aforesaid, is a murderer.
27    After the aforesaid (consulship), Pachon 10. I, Flavius Isidoros the aforesaid, have submitted (this).
28    Petition (libellos) of Isidoros, of the beneficiarii.
