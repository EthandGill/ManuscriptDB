[TRANSLATION]
1     To Antonius Sarapammon, strategos of the Arsinoite nome,
2     from the Aurelii: Ptoleminos son of Euporos and Sarapion his son, and Pasis son of Nilammon, and Eleias son of Euporos, and Selpous son of Sarapion,
3     and Alypios son of Heron and his brother, and Melas son of Herakleides, and Sakaon son of Satabous, and Arion son of Dioskoros, and Hatres son of Panēous
4     the priest, and Soul son of Horion, and Aunēs son of Satabous, and Ammonios son of Ellious, and Paesios son of Satabous, and Kelētous son of Kaēt, all of them
5     from the village of Theadelphia, with sureties for the payment of the repayment being the Aurelii Arion son of Dioskoros and Sakaon son of Satabous, the aforesaid.
6     In accordance with the order of the most eminent rationalis Arrius Diotimos, we acknowledge, swearing by the Fortune of our lords the Emperors
7     Augusti, that there has been measured out to each of us the stated measure of seed, both of wheat and of barley, by
8     the public measure, for the coming sowing, as follows:
9     Ptoleminos and Sarapion his son, six artabas of wheat, 6 artabas, and three artabas of barley, 3 artabas; Pasis, of wheat
10    four artabas, 4 artabas;
11    Eleias, four artabas of wheat, 4 artabas; Selpous, six artabas of wheat, 6 artabas, three artabas of barley, 3 artabas; Alypios and
12    his brother, six artabas of wheat, [6] artabas;
13    Melas, six artabas of wheat, 6 artabas; Sakaon, six artabas of wheat, 6 artabas, three artabas of barley, 3 artabas; Arion, of wheat
14    two artabas, 2 artabas; Hatres, of wheat
15    six artabas, 6 artabas; Soul, six artabas of wheat, 6 artabas; Aunēs, four artabas of wheat, 4 artabas; Ammonios, three artabas of wheat,
16    3 artabas, and of barley
17    one and a half artabas, 1½ artabas; Paēsis, three artabas of wheat, 3 artabas, one and a half artabas of barley, 1½ artabas; Kelētēs,
18    two artabas of wheat, 2 artabas. Total:
19    [64 artabas of wheat] and 12 artabas of barley … of our village … which we shall also repay at the time
20    [of the harvest] with the half-again (extra), just as each of us has received. …
21    In the consulship of Rufius Volusianus and Petronius Annianus, the most illustrious, Choiak 6. We, the Aurelii Ptoleminos and Sarapion and Pasis
22    and Elias and Selpous and the rest, have received the 64 artabas of wheat and 12 artabas of barley, and we shall repay at the time
23    of the harvest with the
24    half-again, as aforesaid; and being questioned we acknowledged. I, Aurelius Klematios son of Hermias, from the village of Philagris, wrote on their behalf
25    as they are illiterate. We, the Aurelii Arion and Sakaon, stand surety, as aforesaid, for the payment of the repayment of the wheat and the barley with the
26    half-again, as aforesaid. I, Aurelius …s, wrote on behalf of the illiterate.
