[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy, undefiled and consubstantial
2     Trinity, Father and Son and Holy Spiri[t][.]
3     In the reign of our most divine and most pious
4     master, the greatest benefactor Flavius Phocas the etern[al]
5     Augustus and Emperor, year 4, Mecheir
6     28, of the ninth indiction.
7     To the reverend Sergios, chartulary and
8     riparius of our glorious house,
9     son of Victo[r] of blessed memory,
10    who was a nomicarius, from this
11    Oxyrhynchite city. Aurelius
12    Pamouthios the light-pottery maker,
13    son of Apollo, his mother Anna, from
14    the hamlet of Palitios of the Oxyrhynchite n[o]me,
15    acknowledge that I have received from your
16    reverence, here and now, three
17    solidi of gold by the private standard, for the price agreed on with me,
18    in full, of one thousand new estate light-vessels
19    and six large vessels
20    and six two-measure jars, making 3 solidi of gold by the private standard, for
21    new estate light-vessels 1000 and large vessel[s]
22    6 and two-measure jars 6, which new light-vessels,
23    serviceable, coated with pitch
24    from bottom to rim, of winter
25    moulding, sound and faultless,
26    I acknowledge to provide to your
27    reverence in the month Mesore of the
28    present ninth indiction, for
29    the storing of wine of the discharge of the, with God,
30    tenth collection, without fail.
31    The document is valid, written singly, and having been questioned I acknowledged.
32    Aurelius Pamouthios son of Apollo,
33    this document satisfies me as aforesaid.
34    I, Papnouthios, wrote on his behalf, he being
35    illiterate.
36    through me …
37    Document of Pamouthios the light-pottery maker, son of Apollo, from the ha[m]let of Palitios: 3 solidi of gold by the private standard; of new estate light-vessels
38    1000 and vessels 6 and two-measure jars 6.
