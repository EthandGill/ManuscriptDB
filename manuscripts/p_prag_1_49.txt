[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master
2     Jesus Christ, our God and Savior,
3     and of our Lady
4     the holy Mother of God, and of all
5     the saints. Thoth 24, 2nd indiction, in Arsinoe.
6     The Aurelii: Ioannes, scribe of the late Zacharias,
7     from the city of the Arsinoites,
8     from the quarter of the Bithynians of Ision,
9     signing below in his own hand,
10    and Paulos son of Petros and
11    Pekysios son of Apollos,
12    from the hamlet of [Tham]batoris
13    of the Arsinoite nome, greeting.
14    We agree to have our case judged before
15    one another before the Aurelii
16    Aiouk and Ferma, full
17    brothers, from the hamlet
18    of Belos, before the arbitrator of the same
19    nome, and that we shall abide by
20    and remain in the judgment given
21    to us by them. But if any
22    of us does not abide by their decision,
23    the one not abiding is liable
24    to pay to the illustrious
25    praetorian treasury as a penalty
26    three gold solidi
27    of refined gold, total 3 gold solidi of refined gold. The compromissum is valid, and being formally questioned we so acknowledged.
28    Ioannes, it satisfies me as stated above.
29    The Aurelii Paulos son of Petros
30    and Pekysios son of Apollos,
31    the aforewritten, this
32    compromissum satisfies as stated above.
33    Through me, Elias …
34    Compromissum made between Ioannes the scribe and Paulos and Pekysios.
