1     Didymus to Hieros the net-fisher, greetings.
2     Provide for the price of daggers
3     of denarii myriads three hundred fifty,
4     [they] make denarii myriads 350, only. I pray [that] you be well
5     year 47, [indiction] 16, Pauni 25.
