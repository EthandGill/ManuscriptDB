# HGV-EN: Year five of Imperator [Caesar] Lucius Sep[timius Severus Pius] Pertinax [Augustus Arabicus Adiabenicus] in the month of Gemanikeios, Pa[chon x, in Pto]lemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome. Hatr[es], son of Aph[rodisios], mother Nekpheraus, from [the village of Ka]ranis, about 40 years old, with a scar on his knee acknowledges [to Chai]ras, son of Pasi[on], from the [metropo]lis, about [x] years old [– – – with a scar on his left – – –] that he, the acknowledger (Hatres), has sold to him a male, mouse-coloured donkey foal on the spot, just as it is, unreturnable, and that [he] has received it, and that Hatres has received the price of three hundred silver drachmas from hand to hand in full. And he will guarantee against every claimant.
