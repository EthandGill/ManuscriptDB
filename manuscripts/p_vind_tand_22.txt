[TRANSLATION]
1     In the tenth year of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
2     Germanicus Imperator, in the month Kaisareios, the eighth, in
3     Nilopolis of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite
4     nom[e]. There acknowledges Horos the younger son of Tesenouphis, a Persian of the epigone, about twenty years old, with a scar on the left wrist, on the inner side, to Harpalos son of Satyros, sub-agent,
5     [about] thirty-seven years old, with a scar on the right shin,
6     that he, the acknowledging party, has from him,
7     from the public administration which he administers, of silver
8     of the Augusti a[n]d of Ptolemaic coinage, a principal of two hundred and eight drachmas, by hand
9     from the house, which the acknowledging party shall also repay on the
10    twenty-fifth of the present mo[nt]h Kaisareios of the tenth year of Nero
11    Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
12    Imperator, the right of execution belonging to
13    Harpalos both against the acknowledging party and against
14    all his property in every way,
15    as if by (a judgment of) a court. The subscribers are their own.
16    Harpalos son of Satyros: the acknowledgment has been made to me as
17    stated above. In the tenth year of N[e]ro Claudius
18    [Cae]sar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
19    in the month Kaisareios, the 8th, Augustus (day). It has been registered through the record-office in Nilopolis.
20    H[arp]alos son of Satyros: I have received in full the aforesaid
21    two hundred and eight dr[a]ch[m]as of silver, total 208 drachmas. In the eleventh year
22    of Nero Cl[a]ud[ius] Caesar Au[g]ustus Germanicus Imperator,
23    in the mon[t]h Neroneios Augustus, the third.
