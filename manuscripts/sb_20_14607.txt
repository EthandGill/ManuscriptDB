[TRANSLATION]
1     Paul the elder and receiver of the city
2     to [the] brother Abramis the exactor: provide
3     from the produce which you received from the greater [ones] Pinarch[...]
4     and various persons from P[o]chis
5     to twelve cavalry-men serving the same Pinarch[...]
6     for their wage of four months from Pharmouthi [30] through
7     Epeiph 30, each of grain six artabas, making of grain artabas
8     seventy-two, making of grain seventy-two artabas only.
9     Epeiph 28, of the 2nd indiction.
10    Epeiph 28, of the 2nd indiction, of grain seventy-two artabas according to contract of the children of the estate of Pinarch[...], wage of four months.
