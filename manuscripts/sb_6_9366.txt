[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 46 Choiak 14 in Pathyrus under Heliodorus
2     agoranomus. Heracleides [son] of Theodore, a Macedonian,
3     of the settler cavalry, [lent to] Patetes [son] of Kalios
4     [a] Persian of the metoic class [the following]: wheat artabas 36 and bronze talent 1
5     interest-free; and this loan he shall repay
6     the borrower to Heracleides in the month Pharmouthi
7     of year 46 wheat new pure unadulterated deposited at [his] house at
8     his own expense; and if [the borrower] should not repay
9     in the appointed time, [he shall] pay forthwith one and a half times interest: the wheat [at] the price [that] will be in the market, the bronze
10    of the overdue time the according to
11    the ordinance two-drachma interests.
12    To Heliodorus …
13    Copy of a loan
14    to be given to the [one] in Pathyrus
15    [the] overseer.
