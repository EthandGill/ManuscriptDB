[TRANSLATION]
1     [Aristander] to Zenon, greetings. You ought, since you contributed
2     [to us], Heracleides the grain-buyer to have sent. Nevertheless still
3     indeed and now do this carefully; for Agenor us troubles
4     causes. Know also about the sesame matters us
5     having involved, that the from others brought-up tallies by you
6     are the most of those owing grain prices. Therefore I have written
7     to you so that you might know. But Heracleides, night and day treating
8     send, in order that we might not be blamed. Farewell. Year 36, Thoth 15.
9     [to Philadelphia].
10    Aristander concerning Heracleides the grain-buyer.
11    To Zenon.
