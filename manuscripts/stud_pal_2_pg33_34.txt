[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Aurelia D]emetria who is also [Ammonia] daughter [of Polydeukes] who had served as gymnasium-leader become
2     [councilor of the] great and most splendid Hermopolis; from Aurelius
3     [Pasion] son of Hermophilus from the same city working as farmer in village
4     [Magdalis] of the Cowherds; I wish willingly and of my own accord to lease [from you] for a time of two years the land belonging to you around Kamphor
5     [village of the] plain called Piebellenposi arourae forty-four for the deposit of wheat
6     [and chaff] of which the one in wheat [half] each wheat artabs five and of the other half the one in hay each
7     [arour]a each lentil [artab] one the gathered things onto the same
8     [wheat] artabs one hundred and lentil[s artabs] twenty-two which I shall measure to you in Pauni and
9     [Ep]eiph month of the fortunate which will be of the 17th indiction, namely 3rd indiction
10    [without] delay, wheat and lentils new clean unadulterated unbroken
11    [sifted], the grain-tax measured in the measure of the holding
12    [and] the lentils measured in Athenian measure of the public all and of distributions
13    [all] of all things toward you and [the] landowner, separated the chaff toward me the
14    [farm]er; the lease valid and when asked I agreed; consulship of Flavius
15    [Ian]uarinus and Uet tius Justus of the most splendid Mesore in the epagomenal first
16    Aurelius Pasion son of Hermophilus I have leased as aforesaid.
