[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twenty-second year of Imperator
2     Caesar [Trajan] Hadrian Augustus, Phaophi 9, in Nilopolis of the Herakleides division
3     of the Arsinoite nome. Panephremmis son of Horos, about 77 years old, with a scar on his left forearm, acknowledges to Stotoetis son of Pakysis, about 48 years old, with a scar on the lower abdomen on the left,
4     that he, the acknowledging party, has received from him forthwith, by hand, out of the house, of silver
5     two hundred and forty-eight drachmas, which he owed
6     to him according to an agreement completed through the
7     same record-office in the 21st year of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
8     in the month Phamenoth 26, of which Pa[neph]r[emmi]s produced the copy … and gave back to him the copy for cancellation and
9     annulment, and that the acknowledging party makes no
10    claim concerning the things he has received, as is aforesaid, nor concerning any other matter whatsoever, written or unwritten, from the times before
11    until the present day, in no manner, by no pretext whatsoever. The undersigning party is Eirenais son of Harphaesis, about 50 years old, with a scar on the middle of his nose. I, Panephremmis son of Horos, acknowledge that I have received
