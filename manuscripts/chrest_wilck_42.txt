[TRANSLATION]
1     … I happen to be about [to] … for … there … … …
2     … to decide by a verdict, thus: Antonius Gregorius the most eminent governor of the Thebaid; Paris the exactor
3     … the things declared. Since the present instruction was conveyed to me through an [at]tendant, the aforesaid governor's deci[sion] being inserted, … to abide by the things ordained; for this reason I too, not
4     being able to keep quiet at their vain petition,
5     and being emboldened [by the subscription] given to me by the most eminent governor Antonius Gregorius from my report directing that they
6     be compelled by
7     the praepositus of the pagus to keep [to the cus]tomary labour, being to this effect: "Before the 12th of the Kalends of December the praepositus of the pagus
8     to keep to the customary cultivation
9     will compel your fa[rmer]s, [an account] being kept among them, if they should have any reasonable plea (kollema 158, volume 1)" — necessarily, that the estate may not be turned to waste,
10    the danger of which (falls) upon the lessees, if they should neglect its good cultivation, I submit these documents — annulling their vain instructions and requesting —
11    that these be sent back to them in turn …, so that they may now take up the cultivation of the estate or evidently appear before you, pleading against me, in accordance with the governor's decision, the rights I have being preserved to me. Farewell.
12    In the consulship of Ru[fi]us Volusianus and Petronius Annianus the most illustrious, Phamenoth 23. Aurelius Silvanus the attendant: I made (it) manifest to the praepositus,
13    Phamenoth 23.
14    [Au]reli[a] … I have submitted (it).
