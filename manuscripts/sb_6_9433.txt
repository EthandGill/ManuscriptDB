[TRANSLATION]
1     Year three of the Emperor
2     Caesar Marcus Aurelius
3     Antoninus Augustus and Emperor
4     [Caesar] Lucius Aurelius Verus
5     [Augustus] Mesore 13. Pasion
6     Aphrod and the partners, grain-receivers of Ptolemais the New, have measured
7     from the produce of the same year, wheat, by public measure
8     to the portico, on request, to Socrates of Sarapion
9     … of the public, [grain] from Karanidis, wheat, twenty-nine artabae,
10    make wheat artabae twenty-nine, of Ptolemais public, likewise wheat artabae four, wheat artabae four,
11    make wheat artabae thirty-three.
