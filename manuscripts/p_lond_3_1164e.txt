[TRANSLATION]
1     12th (of the month); of Serenos son of Eud[ai]mon; of silver, 1700 drachmas
2     In the twentieth year of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aur[eli]us An[ton]inus Parthicus Maximus Britannicus Maximus Pius
3     Augustus, in the month Panemos (= Pharmouthi) on the twelfth. Through the exchange-bank of Anoubion son of Ammonios son of Matidios also called Kallitekni[o]s
4     in Antinoopolis. Serenos son of Eudaimon son of Asklas, Paulinios also called Homognios, (acknowledges) to Alexander son of Isidoros son of Alexander
5     son of Matidios also called Kallateknios, that Alexander has sold to Serenos from now for all time the shares belonging to him
6     of two houses which are in Antinoopolis, which came to him by [ri]ght of [c]ession made according to the bank-register of the same [b]ank
7     in the present year in the month Pharmouthi, from the children of Chairemon son of [Amm]onios Sebastios also called Heraklios: of one house, three-storeyed,
8     with a ground floor and all appurtenances, in the letter-D block, lot 7, a third share according to (his) participation in the remaining two-thirds share of Serapammon; and of another
9     house, single-storeyed, with courtyard and all appurtenances, in the letter-G [bl]ock, lot 8, a fourth share according to (his) participation in the remaining shares of the
10    same Serapammon; the neighbors of the two whole houses: of the one in the letter-D block, lot 7, to the south a public street through which (are) entrance and exit,
11    to the north formerly (the property) of Serapas, to the east (that) of Cornelius and others, to the west a narrow street; and of the one in the letter-G block, lot 8, to the south (that) of Chairemonis and
12    others, to the north (that) of Agathos Daimon and others, to the east a public street through which (are) entrance and exit, to the west (that) of the heirs of Didymos, or whoever they may be, the neighbors on every side; and that the seller has received from the buyer the price agreed between them, one thousand silver drachmas
13    seven hundred, they come to 1700 silver drachmas; that Serenos therefore and his (heirs) shall hold and own from now for all time the shares sold to him
14    of the aforesaid houses as has been shown above, and shall administer and execute concerning them as they may choose, without impediment, the guarantee
15    being always followed up for every guarantee against the seller Alexander and his (heirs); and (the seller) shall remove every person who shall come against these for this reason
16    in whatever manner, removing him forthwith at his own expenses; and further shall pay to the buyer or his (heirs) double the
17    price, and to the public treasury the equal (sum), and the damages and the expenses, without prejudice to this sale remaining valid; and he gave to him the copy of the cession in his possession, so that his rights might remain to him. Alexander son of Isidoros, Mat[i]dios also called Kallateknios,
18    I have sold to Serenos the shares belonging to me and aforesaid of the two aforesaid houses and all appurtenances as has been shown above, and I have received the price, one thousand seven hundred silver drachmas
19    and I will guarantee with every guarantee and I consent to all as aforesaid. Serenos son of Eudaimon son of Asklas, Paulinios also called Homognios, I have bought and paid out the price as aforesaid
