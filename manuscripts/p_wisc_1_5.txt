[TRANSLATION]
1     Glaukias son of Pekysis, his mother being
2     Dieus, from Oxyrhynchos city, residing in Sento, leased to Achillas son of Harpaesis, his mother being Tsenosiris, from the same
3     city, for one year from the new moon of the current month Thoth, the slave-woman belonging to him, Tapontos,
4     for work at the weaver's trade,
5     whom the lessor will provide to him
6     neither absent by day nor sleeping away
7     from him, she being fed
8     and supplied with the other necessaries by Achillas, but clothed by the master himself,
9     for a wage for the whole year of silver
10    four hundred and twenty drachmas,
11    which — the lease being confirmed — let Achillas pay to Glaukias month by month, the sum due at the end
12    of the month, without delay. The slave-woman
13    Tapontos shall be idle, on account
14    of festivals, in the month Tybi four days,
15    and likewise in Pachon four days, being on the same reckoning eight days,
16    for which nothing shall be deducted from the wages; but for those (days) on which she is idle beyond (this),
17    whether on account of festivals or illness or some other necessary need of the master, the
18    wage for those days shall be deducted [by] the month.
19    And if ever by night the master needs her for bread-making,
20    he shall send for her, nothing on
21    this account being deducted from the wages.
22    Valid is the lease. Year 26 of the Emperor
23    [C]aesar Marcus Aurelius Commodus
24    Antoninus Augustus Pius Armeniacus
25    Medicus Parthicus Sarmaticus Germanicus
26    [Bri]tannicus Maximus, Thoth 14.
