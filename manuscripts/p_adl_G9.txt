[TRANSLATION]
1     Year [13 which is also] 10, Mesore 21.
2     Haketephnachthes has sold half
3     of a house and courtyard.
4     Senenouphis bought (it),
5     and Sempelaias (also),
6     of bronze 3000 (drachmas).
7     [Of Senenouphis]
8     (daughter) of Phibis,
9     of Sempelaias
10    of the women.
11    In the reign of Kleopatra and king Ptolemy surnamed Alexander, gods
12    Philometores, year 13 which is also 10, in (the term of) the priests and priestesses and basket-bearer (kanephoros) who are in office, in the month
13    Mesore 21, in Pathyris, before Hermias the (agent) of Paniskos the agoranomos.
14    Haketephnachthes son of Psemmonthes, goldsmith, of those from Latopolis, about 50 years old, of good stature,
15    honey-skinned, straight-haired, bald-fronted, broad-faced, straight-nosed, has sold from the house belonging to him,
16    built and vaulted, on the west side of it, and from the adjoining courtyard, the half of these, (the house) being
17    on the west side of Pathyris; neighbors of the whole house and the courtyard: on the south and
18    on the west the house of Pelaios son of Znebonychos, on the north the house of Pates, on the east a royal street,
19    or whoever the neighbors may be on all sides. Senenouphis daughter of Phibis, Persian, bought (it), and Sempelaias
20    daughter of Pmois, the daughter of this woman, each of them according to the half of the half of what was sold, of bronze 3000 drachmas. The two of them shall open the door into the royal street on the east. Fore-seller and guarantor (is) Haketephnachthes the seller, whom Senenouphis accepted
21    and Sempelaias, the (women) who bought.
22    Hermias: I have transacted (this).
23    Year 13 [which is also] 10, Mesore 30, there have been assessed for the [transfer-tax (enkyklion)]
24    deposit until the registration (diagraphe), Sene[nouphis daughter of Phibis]
25    and Senpelaias (daughter) of Phmois, for the purchased
26    house, built and vaulted, and for the
27    adjoining courtyard, the half, [which] they bought from
28    Hak[e]tepnachthes son of Psemmonthes,
29    of bronze 3000, tax 300.
30    Paniskos the banker, 360.
