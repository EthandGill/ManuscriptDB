[TRANSLATION]
1     In the fourteenth year of Aurelius Antoninus Caesar the lord, Tybi 12:
2     Stotetis son of Stotoetis, grandson of Hieropaous, his mother being Stotoetis, paid to Horos and his colleagues, collectors of money-taxes of Soknopaiou Nesos,
3     for the syntaximon of the same year, eight drachmas, making 8 drachmas; Mecheir 7, twelve drachmas, making 12 drachmas;
4     Mecheir 29, four drachmas, making 4 drachmas; Pharmouthi 5, eight drachmas, making 8 drachmas; Pachon 7, eight drachmas, making 8 drachmas; Pauni 7, four drachmas, making 4 drachmas; Epeiph 6, a half-obol, 2 coppers; for "double" taxes and river-guard, one drachma, four obols, 2 coppers, making 1 drachma, four obols, a half-obol, 2 coppers; for prison-guards, two obols, a half-obol, 2 coppers; for the wage of the tower-guards, one drachma, making 1 drachma; Mesore 7, for the levy for (wild) beasts, three obols, 2 coppers; for the wage of the tower-guards, seven drachmas, making 7 drachmas; for the additional levy on the destitute of the past 12th year, three drachmas, an obol, making 3 drachmas, an obol; in the 15th year, Thoth 9, for "double" taxes and river-guard, two obols, 2 coppers; for the wage of the tower-guards, one drachma, three obols, making 1 drachma, [three obols]; for the additional levy on the destitute of the 14th year, one drachma, four obols, 2 coppers,
5     making 1 drachma, four obols, 2 coppers; Phaophi 3, for the additional levy on the destitute of the past 13th year, four drachmas, making 4 drachmas; Hathyr 9, likewise eight drachmas, making 8 drachmas;
6     in the 15th year, Thoth 9, likewise the same man, for dyke-taxes of the past 13th year, six drachmas, four obols; of additional charges, one drachma, 2 coppers, making 7 drachmas, four obols, 2 coppers.
