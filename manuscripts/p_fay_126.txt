[TRANSLATION]
1   Dioxenos to Sarapion his fa-
2   ther, greeting. As I was walking about
3   with father I heard a friend of
4   my father’s talking about you — that
5   he has sent to your mother-in-law be-
6   cause of the property, since the bounds are about
7   to be fixed. So come up quickly, for
8   it presses. I greet Thermouthas and
9   Isidoros and his sister
10   Helene and Tepsois and her child —
11   may the evil eye not touch it — and all the house-
12   hold. Farewell. Pauni 30.
13   From Dioxenos; deliver ✗ to Sarapion.
