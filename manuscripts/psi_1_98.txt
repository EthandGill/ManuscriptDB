[TRANSLATION]
1     deign [...] to arrange for the man so that deign [us] to be found together
2     for us [...] then coming bring so that we may not be found again
3     in great[...] [...] sufficient for we have suffered yesterday, as not knows your all-virtuous friendship, but
4     not may we fail [of] the request; for it has been written in one book that the one showing mercy to the poor
5     [to] God [lends].
