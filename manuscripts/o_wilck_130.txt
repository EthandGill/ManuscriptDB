[TRANSLATION]
1   Flavius Eutyches, freedman of Flavius Pansa,
2   lessee of the Sacred Gate of Syene, through Sarapion
3   the assistant. Paid: Harpaesis son of Phanophis,
4   mother Tachomtbekis, for the poll-tax of the eighth year
5   of Hadrian Caesar the lord, in part: seven-
6   teen drachmas (and) a half-obol, (and) ½ obol (keepers' fee) — total 17 dr. 1 ob. Year 9 of Hadrian
7   the lord, Choiak 2.
