[TRANSLATION]
1     From Thomas, landholder
2     To John, the administrator. Since Jacob says he has been wronged
3     by Daniel, make him meet with him for arbitration,
4     and enforce the results of arbitration. But if it is not troublesome for you, and now
5     deign to [look after] as far as these matters. Farewell.
