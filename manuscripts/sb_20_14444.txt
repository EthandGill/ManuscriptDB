r.1    ἐν ὀνόματι τοῦ θεοῦ·
r.2    Κόρρα υἱὸς Σζερὶχ σύμβουλος [ὑ]μῖν Πέτρῳ Γεωργίου χρυσουποδέκτῃ ἀπὸ

[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the god;
2     Korra, son of Szerich, counselor, to you Peter, son of George, gold-receiver, from
3     the city of Herakleous; it was ordered through you from what was found against you according to the penalty
4     coins 6, six only; it was written in month Thoth 9, indiction 8 eighth, year 91 [and] 8, becomes coins 6.
