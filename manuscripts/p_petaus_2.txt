[TRANSLATION]
1     To Hermophilos, royal scribe of the Arsinοitic [Heracleide] portion
2     from Apykhēs, of no father on the mother's side
3     [of mother] Tapholēmeōs and of the
4     same-mothered sister Tapāsitos being his [wife] and woman
5     from the village P[t]olemaidos Hormu, but of
6     Tapāsitos [wi]th her guardian Apykhēs. We register the daughter born to us from each other
7     Taēsin and being towards the present 25th year of age eight years old. Therefore we submit the
8     [memorandum] of birth.
9     to-the-village-scribe of Ptolemaidos Hormu. The matters concerning the
10    similar [items] following [we] fulfill as towards you of the matter
11    and risk being if anything not properly comes to pass.
12    [Year] 25 of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
13    Antoninus [August]us Pius, Mechir 20
