[TRANSLATION]
1     [And Joannes also] called Kattas, [residing in the monastery]
2     [of Pampa]nes, but now [found in Syene, who also undertakes]
3     [the guardianship] and good faith of [the minor Tsia, the sister of the aforewritten] Tsone, of the same [parents], who also [provide] the subscriber subjoined below subscribing on their behalf, [and the witnesses subjoined below bearing witness] to this valid sale to Aurelius Patermouthios son of Menas and to his most well-disposed spouse Aurelia Kako,
4     daughter of Iakobos, from her mother Tapia, originating from
5     the same Syene, greeting. We, the aforewritten,
6     Tsone and Joannes, on behalf of the name of Tsia, who is also called Tsere
7     by reason of the youthfulness of her age, acknowledge through this
8     our written contract of sale, willingly and being persuaded,
9     without any deceit and fear and force and trickery and compulsion
10    and constraint and any kind of bad intent and malice
11    and every defect and base purpose, and
12    every lawful circumvention; and at the same time swearing by the holy
13    and consubstantial Trinity and the victory and permanence of our most serene
14    and triumphant masters, the Flavii Tiberius and Aelia
15    Anastasia, the eternal Augusti and Emperors,
16    that we have sold to you today and conveyed by law
17    of sale and eternal possession and every most complete
18    right of ownership, the share of a house belonging to us, from the
19    house called (that) of Patechnoumios our great-grandfather,
20    in which same share of a house, on the first floor, one cell,
21    one in the gateway, facing north, and the upper
22    dining-room above it, facing north, toward the passage of
23    the pier; on the second floor, also the small chamber above
24    the courtyard, facing east, toward the small courtyard;
25    and our third share from the other small chamber
26    above the couch-room of Iakobos son of Pasarais, of you indeed the father
27    of Kako and of you indeed Pathermouthios the father-in-law; and the third share
28    of all the appurtenances pertaining (to it), both the forecourt
29    and the gateway and the pier and the half-oven, according to the possession
30    and ownership prevailing up to now. Neighbors of the
31    whole house, from which we have sold to you this our
32    share, are as follows: on the south, the house of Dios son of Kelol; on the north, the public
33    street into which the main door opens; on the west, the house of Dios
34    son of Takares; on the east, the house of Pateroous; or whoever the neighbors may be
35    on every side, on a square and four-sided (plot), as
36    we, the sellers, have dictated, by mouth, for a price
37    agreed upon and approved between us,
38    upon inviolable terms, of ten gold
39    nomismatia by the standard of Syene — total, 10 gold nomismatia by the standard of Syene — which
40    full enclosed price we, having received in full there from you
41    the buyers, hand to hand, in number and
42    in full weight; and we shall guarantee to you the sale
43    with every guarantee for all time and against everything that shall come upon you, at our risk and from the resource of our
44    metric substance, which is also bound for the warding off
45    and clearing of this our present
46    valid sale; and on the condition that you, from now on, of this same
47    third share have authority and ownership and (may) sell and resell and dwell and inhabit and build and build upon, acquire, use in every way pleasing to you
48    without hindrance and without impediment, none of (those) in our name or related by descent being able
49    at any time or season ever to assail you or to move arbitration
50    against you, because we have received and have been paid the enclosed price. But if — which may not be — we should be weak concerning the warding off and clearing of this
51    our valid sale, on the condition that we acknowledge
52    the enclosed price double, together also with the
53    requirement that the present valid sale stand firm,
54    which we have set down for you, being valid and firm and unbreakable
55    and unshakable and lawful, produced everywhere,
56    with the subscription of the one subscribing on our behalf and of the
57    witnesses subjoined below; and concerning all the aforewritten matters,
58    being formally questioned, we acknowledged that it is so, and we released (the deed). Aurelia Tsone daughter of
59    Rachel, from (her) father
60    Apa Dios, originating from Syene, together also with her lord
61    and husband Aurelius Isakios son of Konstantios who also consents
62    and approves with her; and Joannes also called Kattas,
63    residing in the monastery of Pampanes, but now in Syene
64    found, who also undertakes the guardianship and good faith of the minor
65    Tsere, sister of the aforementioned Tsone, of the same parents,
66    have sold to you today the share of a house belonging to us, from the house
67    formerly of Patechnoumios our great-grandfather, and we have received the full
68    price in ten gold nomismatia, and all the written terms are in order for us as is aforesaid. Joannes son of Abraamios, the least presbyter of the holy
69    church of God of Syene, having been requested, wrote on their behalf in
70    a public place, they not knowing letters.
71    Flavius Diops son of Alexandros, [soldier] of the unit of Syene, I bear witness.
72    Flavius Hatres son of Petros, soldier of the legion of Syene and of the vicars, I bear witness.
73    Flavius Joseph son of Hermeias, soldier [of the unit] of Syene, I bear witness.
74    Flavius Hatre son of Markos, augustalis of the unit of Syene, I bear witness.
75    Flavius Makarios son of Ak…, soldier [of the unit] of Syene, I bear witness to the sale, having heard (it) from those who set it down.
76    Flavius Mousaios son of Joannes … [of the unit] of Syene, I bear witness.
77    Through me, Theophilos the least deacon, it was drawn up.
