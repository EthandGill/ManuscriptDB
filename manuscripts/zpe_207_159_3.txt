[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: … A copy of the application which had been given to us by Tapaosiris, daughter of Paosiris, son of Onnophris, is appended. Therefore, if the property declared through it is clear, send us a written report. Year 3 of Hadrianus Caesar, the lord, Phamenoth 20. Ptolemaios, bibliophylax, I have signed. I have signed through Harsythes. To Didymos and Ptolemaios the bibliophylakes from Tapaosiris, daughter of Paosiris, son of Onnophris, her mother being Senpsenosiris alias Syra, daughter of Dionysodoros, from Sesphtha, with her guardian Didymos, son of Onnophris, grandson of Akoris. I declare for the first time that which I have had ceded to me from Iseis, daughter of Thonis, son of Thoni…, her mother being Ammonous, daughter of Dionysios, from Sesphtha, without her having declared the property, in accordance with an agreement through the grapheion of Choinothmis of the Herakleopolite nome in Thoth of the fourteenth year of the divine Traianos, a copy of which I have submitted to you on a separate sheet, in Sesphtha from the allotment of Boethos, son of Boethos, one aroura from the two (?) arouras of catoecic land held jointly with her brother Petsiris, and I swear by Imperator Caesar Trajanus Hadrianus Augustus that [the above mentioned] is true …
