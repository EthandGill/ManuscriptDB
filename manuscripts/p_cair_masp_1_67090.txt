[TRANSLATION]
1     From Victor with God commentariensis to the brothers John the helper and abba Enoch and
2     Papnouthios and Phoibammon proto-village-chiefs and all the community and shepherds and chief officers of shepherds of the village
3     of Aphrodite: you have the word from me regarding all persons and matters, so that I might encounter
4     you, and [that you] might be found unharmed and uninjured for me. Victor with God commentariensis, the word accords with me
5     as aforesaid. It was written in Phaophi 26, indiction 14
