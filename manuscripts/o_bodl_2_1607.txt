1     Measurement of treasury of villages, produce of year 2 of Marcus Opellius
2     Severus Macrinus and Antoninus Diadumenianus
3     Caesars, the lords. Epeiph 3, for Charax district
4     Gaius Julius Didymus: wheat, artabae eight and a quarter, 24, 48,
5     [they] total wheat artabae 8, quarter, 24, 48. Aurelius Tith I have signed.
