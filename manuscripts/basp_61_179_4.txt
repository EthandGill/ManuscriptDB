# HGV-EN: In the third year of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, on the twenty-first of Thoth, in Theadelphia of the Themistos division of the Arsinoite nome. Dionysios son of Soterichos, aged about sixty, with a scar on the little finger of his left hand, and his sons Soterichos, aged about thirty, with a scar on the left shin, and Dioskoros, aged about twenty-five, with a scar on the left eyebrow, the three of them Persians of the Epigone, mutual sureties for full repayment, acknowledge to Aphrodisios son of Philippos, Antinoite, aged about thirty-six, with a scar on [the right side] of his lower lip...
