1     Sarapion, son of Hierax, leased
2     from the city of Oxyrhynchus to Diogenes
3     son of Horus from Serypheia for the present fourth year from the lands registered to him concerning
4     Muchinaxap, two-thirds of an aroura
5     so that [he] might sow with wheat, the assigned rent of the same present
6     year being wheat, artabae six and one-half
7     quarter of choinikes two, free from all risk, of the
8     land's public-duties and assessments
9     being [the] responsibility of the landlor[d]:
10    whom he shall also control of the produce
11    until the assigned rent is delivered.
12    when the lease is confirmed, let the tenant deliver the assigned rent in Payni month of the same present [year]
13    at the threshing-floor of Serypheia, [fresh] whe[at]
14    pure, unadulterated, free from barley, sif[ted] as for the [public treasury]
15    being measured with a measure of te[trachoin]ic, receipt-measure [of the]
16    landlord, from [him]
17    the measuring being done [by]…:
18    and whatever additional [amount he owes, let him] pa[y back]
19    with one-and-a-half, and the execu[tion shall be]
20    from him and from all his [existing] property. [Valid is the leas]e. Year 4 of Emperor [Caesar Mark]
21    Aurelius Antoninus, Augu[stus and]
22    Emperor Caesar Lucius [Aurelius]
23    Verus, Augustus, Thoth 16. D[iogenes]
24    son of Horus, I have leased the [land]
25    for the present year, [the] assigned [rent]
26    of the same present year
27    being wheat, artabae six and one-half
28    quarter of choinikes two, and
29    I will deliver as stated. Plutio[n], he-who-is-also Eutyches, freedman of Sarapion and Diogenes, wrote in behalf of
30    him not knowing letters.
