[TRANSLATION]
1     Inares, watchman of Tilothees, to Ptolemaios
2     [message] from Hour, the topogrammateus, greetings
3     I have received from you Peteun, village secretary of Tagchae, whom I shall also return
4     to Dionysion, the royal secretary
5     by the [blank] of Mechir, or I shall pay
6     all [things] that will be claimed against him
7     Farewell. Year 23, Mechir …
8     Surety of Petotes, village secretary
