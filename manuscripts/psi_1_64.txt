[TRANSLATION]
1     Thais [daughter] of Tarouthi[nos] … Hermogenes swears by Osiris and
2     Isis and [Horus] [and] Zeus and all the other gods and
3     all [if indeed for as long as] you live [I shall] remain together with you
4     [living together with you as] legitimate wife, neither separate [from the bed]
5     [nor] temporary away from your house, and [I shall] be well-disposed
6     [to] you and [love you], overlooking nothing of yours. And since
7     … [gold] namely quarters ten in copper
8     [tal]ents [three into] silver account drachmas thirty-six in co[pper talents] three and a garment of five talents,
9     a tunic [of drachmas] four thousand six hundred, and
10    a loan … [copper] talents five; if in nothing wronged … [I shall not] depart from you, having abandoned [you]
11    you, … I will return everything, having taken nothing for myself; [but] [as for] the loan of copper talents five
12    [I shall] record [the] loan, and to no one shall I grant [it] by any subterfuge; but if other gold ornaments after those aforesaid … you place upon me, I shall not depart [with] them
13    having [them], [but] I will return [them] to you, having taken nothing for myself; and to no one
14    else of mankind shall I associate in a womanly manner
15    except you, nor shall I make against you drugs [or] love-charms nor
16    maleficium, neither in drinks nor in foods, nor shall I conspire with anyone who will do [it] by any subterfuge.
17    Year 2, Choiak.
18    I, Thais, have sworn the aforesaid
19    oath [and] I will do as is set forth.
