[TRANSLATION]
1     For good fortune.
2     In the twentieth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, on the third of the month Hyperberetaios, the third intercalary day of Mesore,
3     in Talei of the Polemon division of the Arsinoite nome. Herakleides son of Isidoros, about 40 years old, a scar
4     below the left cheek, lessee of the estate of Marcus Aponius Saturninus and Tiberius
5     Claudius Barbillus around Theogonis, leased to Diodoros son of Euphranor, about 50 years old,
6     a scar on the left cheek, and to Patynis the younger son of Philon, about 40 years old, a scar in the middle of the forehead under the hair, the two
7     Persians of the epigone, mutual sureties for payment, the
8     working bath in the village of Theogonis belonging to the same estate, in which there are three lead boilers, and the
9     straw-store adjoining the bath
10    in which is straw. This lease is for three years from
11    the coming twenty-first year of Tiberius Caesar
12    Augustus, on condition that they heat the bath each month,
13    one (day) on, one off, the same lessees,
14    and on the festivals, the lessees furnishing for themselves sufficient straw and bath-attendants
15    and the other appropriate things; at a rent of all new
16    clean unadulterated wheat, two hundred and sixty-five artabas, by the measure of the same bath, free of de[duction]
17    and free of risk. And the said rent
18    let the lessees pay from the month Pauni
19    to Mesore of the same year, monthly the amount due, in the village; and the lessor
20    Herakleides shall have from the lessees, as exempt and discharged from the bath-tax,
21    ten men; and after the period they shall hand over the bath as they themselves received it, and the boilers
22    sound; and if any damage results to the boilers through the fault of the lessees,
23    the repair of these
24    shall be the responsibility of the lessees, the lessor Herakleides furnishing the lead
25    … [it shall] be the responsibility of Hera[kleides ] … u …
26    … of the bath, which they shall also re[pa]y together with the rent itself as principal, and the same lessees shall ha[nd over] the straw-store with the straw in it, as they also received it, on condition that
27    there be returned to the same lessees two exempt donkeys. Herakleides guarantees the lease against public (claims). The subscriber of the lessees,
28    Dios son of Dios, about 45 years old, a scar on the forehead …
29    Diodoros son of Euphranor [a]nd P[a]t[y]nis …
30    … en …
31    … Herakle[ides the lessee ] …
32    from [t]h[e A]ponius and …
33    … in the 20th year of Tiberius Caesar A[ugustus]
34    Mesore, 3rd intercalary day, registered through Hermias at the record-office of Talei and of other villages.
35    [Ac]ceptance of the lease of a bath, of Diodoros and anothe[r]
36    with Herakleides.
