# HGV-EN: From Achilles, vir egregius. At once send up, through the soldier despatched, Paphib son of Preiteles with his son Amaeis, both dwelling in the Small Tarouthinou; Aurelius Plutarchus is the plaintiff. (2nd hand) I have signed (it). (1st hand) To the comarchs and the demosioi of Bercy. (M. Cottier)
