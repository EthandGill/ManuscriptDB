[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 22, Pharmouthi 5.
2     The first dream, which Nekthonbes saw concerning the twins and myself:
3     I saw Apollonios, [and he] comes toward me. He says: Greetings, Nekthembes, well. The second:
4     [Egyptian] Pafere and … Pauni in the Bubasteum … in the house
5     of Ammon … [Egyptian]. The third:
6     I saw Ptolemaios holding a sword in his hand,
7     going through the street, [and he] strikes a door and it opens, [and he] carried into [his] hands
8     [the person, he] wanting to strike him. I say: By no means do [this] or
9     [you] will lose your son. The Lord does not destroy his [own] son.
10    A woman sitting on a mat, [and she] had a child on the mat
11    and another opposite her on another mat. I say to her
12    that your mat produces [vegetables and] cabbage. [She was] sitting
13    and not moving. Pharmouthi 24. The dream, which Nektombes saw concerning the detention of the twins and myself, [a dream] that I am suffering misfortune
14    and [concerning] the house. [He] thought [he heard] a man say to me: Bring the skin of your foot and
15    I will give you the skin of my foot. I say: I do not wish. The second:
16    I thought [I saw] a house being demolished [while someone was] beautifying it. The third: I thought
17    [I was chasing] a dove, and she escaped from my hand and I run after her
18    saying: I will not let her escape. I catch her and put her
19    into [my] left hand and I held her and [I was] pressing [her], lest she escape. The fourth:
20    I thought [I saw] two women sitting with a man, caressing him and
21    swearing thus: by Isis the holy [one].
