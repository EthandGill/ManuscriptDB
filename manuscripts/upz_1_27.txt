[TRANSLATION]
1     Theon to Dionysius, greetings. Measure according to the judgment of those accustomed
2     according to the official opinion from Mennides the overseer
3     to Demetrius, son of Sosus, the Cretan, of the former
4     of Eumelos, [established] by the twins through which they submitted a petition to the king so that to them the due
5     [to] the 18th year sesame oil one metretes, castor oil one metretes
6     [and to the] 19th year sesame oil one metretes, [castor] oil one metretes, and altogether of sesame oil [two metretes]
7     castor oil two metretes, [making] sesame oil two metretes
8     and castor oil two metretes, in exchange for which sesame oil
9     one metretes, [total] three metretes, and receipts make for the
10    recipients.
11    Theon. Proceeding accordingly, sesame oil three metretes, [making] sesame oil three metretes.
12    [castor oil two metretes, making] sesame [oil two metretes and cas]tor [oil two metretes], in exchange for which sesame
13    [oil one metretes, totaling three metretes, and] receipts [make for] the [recipients].
