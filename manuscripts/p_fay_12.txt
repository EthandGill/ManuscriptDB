[TRANSLATION]
1     To queen Kleopatra, goddess Euergetis, and to king Ptolemy also
2     called Alexandros, god Philometor, greeting,
3     Theotimos son of Phileas, Mysian of the fourth hipparchy, holder of a hundred arourae,
4     of those from Theadelphia of the Themistos division of the Arsinoite (nome).
5     In the month Epeiph of the 13th which is also the 10th year, Diokles son of Alexandros,
6     a Persian of the epigone, of those from the same (village), conducting himself not in the best (manner), having committed wrongs against me yet again and others
7     besides, I lodged against him concerning these matters
8     the customary reports. But not being ashamed of this,
9     but rather taking as accomplice Ammonios son of Ammonios,
10    a Persian of the epigone, of those from the same (village), and on the … of
11    Mecheir of the present year, having come upon the house belonging to me in the village and having called me out …
12    together with these (men) and others present besides, by the same (men)
13    not undeservedly being most grievously abused, as a result of the consultation
14    which had been arranged against them … …
15    but they even led (me) away with hubris and blows, and having shut me up in the house of a certain Amenneus they stripped off the himation I was wearing, and they went off with it, leaving (me) naked. Then thus, with a garment …
16    … by my acquaintances, thus having gone out
17    and having paid down, through Ptolemaios the banker, to Melis
18    the tavern-keeper, with whom they had deposited the himation as a pledge
19    for (a debt of) profligacy, 2,700 bronze drachmae, I recovered it. Concerning
20    the wrongs unjustly committed against me, having resolved
21    to proceed, I beg (you) to send my petition
22    to the chrematistai assigned to the katoikia,
23    whose introducer is Dioskourides, so that having dealt with
24    it and having summoned both Diokles and Ammonios
25    through T… the collector, they may adjudge that there be exacted from me (i.e. paid to me)
26    each (?) … being liable for the unjust abduction
27    100 silver drachmae, and for the hubris 420 bronze (drachmae), and the 2,700 of bronze,
28    a fitting … decision being made concerning them.
29    And these things being done, I shall have received redress.
30    Farewell.
