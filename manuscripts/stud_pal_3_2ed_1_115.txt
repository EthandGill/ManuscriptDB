[TRANSLATION]
1     [we owe] from mutual obligation, Menas, also called Phansemenneh, son of Ioannakios, and his son Paul, innkeepers
2     [from the] city of the Arsinoites, from the quarter of Nymphaion, to you, Apa Julios, papyrus-seller, son of George, from the same city
3     [clearly] and precisely, gold coins two and one-half, gold coins, 2 and one-half in number, and their repayment and complete
4     [fulfil]ment we shall make for you in installments, that is, on the eighth of the incoming month Mecheir of the present
5     [first] indiction, one coin, and on the twentieth of the next-coming month Phamenoth of the same first indiction, another
6     [coin,] one, and on the [tw]ent[ie]th [of Pha]rmouthi month of the aforesaid first indiction, the other half-coin
7     […] thanksgiving. It was written in the month Tybi, 15, first indiction. Christoph[o]ros, notary
8     […] Theod[o]ros, scribe, son of the most blessed Mousaios, I witness this written document as [it] is [afore]stated.
9     […] through me Kosmas, with God, deacon, notary.
