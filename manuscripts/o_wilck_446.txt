1   Pachoites son of Ammonios paid, for poll-tax
2   of the Opheion(?) (quarter), year 10, ten drachmas — total 10 dr. — and on these
3   surcharges. Year 10 of Vespasian
4   the lord, Mecheir 15(?).
5   Bassos, I have signed.
