[TRANSLATION]
1     [A petition] to Colluthus, village-scribe of [Ammoniada], from the [farmers from] Ammoniada, whose [grain] [was] measured to the foot-soldiers.
2     We are wronged [by] Nestus and Torrimuus
3     and Zenon[os], [being] judges themselves, and
4     our land [they] have taken away. For [with] the foot-soldiers [being] willing to give to us
5     the land [to the] farmers who had sown, [so that] we not be expelled, being farmers — [Nestus] and Torrimmas [having] obstructed,
6     and Zenon [have] seized the land.
7     Since our land has been [taken away],
8     we request [that the] grain be given to us … arourae according to the
9     [comparison]. [We have] written to you so that
10    [you know] that we [shall] depart [being wronged], and Philiscus [having] written [to] the oikonomos [to] review concerning us.
11    Year 33, Choiak 21.
