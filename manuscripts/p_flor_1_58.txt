[TRANSLATION]
1     … To Hierakion, the most excellent epistrategos,
2     [from Aurelia] Aretous also called Heronous, daughter of Heron, a townswoman, there standing with her Aurelius Athenodoros son of Herodianus. My petition, master, requesting from you redress, has a brief exposition. There came to me by inheritance
3     [from the afore]said and deceased father of mine, according to the will which he left, in the village-clerkship of Moirai of the great
4     [Hermopolite] nome, grain-bearing lands amounting to twenty arourae and more, and … I held control, gathering in the rents
5     … which I paid over in the manner which, as I said before, my father also (did) … But in the present year a certain Horion, having come upon these (lands),
6     … surnamed Pemous, and his son Heron … having come upon (them) unreasonably, contrary to all law and contrary to [all] authority and contrary to my judgment and consent … by claim (of right) or by local force, my own (men),
7     … who were long ago farmers of the lands, they expelled … and, the most unjust thing of all, not (even sowing) the whole
8     [lands] but having sown a part, they have left the rest unsown. And not only these things, but also, having come upon my courtyard, [they found] a water-machine of mine which I have for the irrigation of the crops, and this too, without any pretext, they have stolen; and in addition to these things they dared [many] other things. For after I had measured out all the dues owed for the past year, finding a mound of grain of mine
9     … [which] I had for the burial of myself and of my (people) and of the … they pledged (?) … they all behaved insolently against me … [despising] me as a weak woman … my master, of so many and such great audacities … against me, I beg you, being moved, to proceed and to hear me against my adversaries, and to order the strategos
10    [to summon] them before your court to give account of the things
11    of which they were accused, so that I may be able, through your proceeding,
12    … to obtain redress, and the rents owed of the lands and the machine together with the
13    … to recover, from which also the dues owed and incumbent on the most sacred treasury will be preserved, and … me …
14    Fare well.
