[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermeas strategos of Oxyrhynchus
2     [to] the mighty council through the presiding prytanis Eutropius, [to the] dearest, greetings.
3     In accordance with [those things] commanded by the excellence
4     of my lord [the] most distinguished ruler
5     Sabinianus, [since] there is need of [an] overseer
6     of meat [of] 300 pounds, being transported to the
7     Nicopolis for those under Valerianus the praepositus now lodging there, in order that you know and having now chosen this [person] make [it] clear to me. I pray [that] you be well, brother.
8     to the [ones who] will be designated consuls, [for the] third [year],
9     Mesore 24.
10    Gerontius the assistant of the strategos brought [this].
11    Mesore 24.
