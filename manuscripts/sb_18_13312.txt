[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolem[aeus, greet]ings, Petesouchos son of Petoutus and Marres called Great of Petobastis, [both of those from the C]rocodile city. Since we have a linen-weaving workshop
2     [and byssus-weaving workshop in the aforementioned] city, which has deteriorated, which we wish, having torn down [to establish new li]nen-weaving workshops two, so that we might be able the aforesaid
3     [revenues from lin]en-cloth to deliver to the royal treasury; we ask you beseech[ing, King, to command] Diophanès the stratégos to write to those to whom it belongs, having come [to inspect and if] it is true, to give us the authority to tear down
4     [the old and have new. For] if this happens, we shall be, King, having obtained the benef[icence from you.] Farewell.
