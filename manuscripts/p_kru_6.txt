[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity,
2     the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Written in the month Mesore 21, in the 11th indiction,
3     under our lord Flavius Komes son of the blessed Chael, administrator
4     of the fortress of Memnonia. I, Senouthios son of the blessed
5     Enoch, the man of the fortress of Djeme of the nome of the city
6     of Hermonthis, giving, with afterwards the subscription of the one who shall subscribe for me and faithful witnesses worthy to be trusted to believe,
7     to them to bear witness to it, for me, according to my own request,
8     of my own self and my authorization, I write, apart from any
9     fraud or fear or violence or deceit or seizure
10    or circumvention, there being no single compulsion laid upon me,
11    but out of my heart's will and my free choice,
12    I write, giving to you, out (from me), to you Aron son of the blessed
13    Senouthios, my share of (the) courtyard in my dwelling of the man of Pshomare,
14    this one which came to me from my blessed parents, this one, now,
15    which I shall reveal, its boundaries, out to you, according to its position(s)
16    ancient. The west: the house of Petros (son of) Zacharias;
17    the east: the wall (?) blind (closed) toward your house; the middle: David son of Kyriakos;
18    the south: the public wall and the door of the proprietors. The price is this which we agreed upon together, and it pleased us, of the two shares,
19    these two trimesia of gold, making 3⅔ obryzon by the standard of the fortress
20    of Memnonia, the full price, [it] came into my hand, hand to hand, of fine gold.
21    From now then to you, you Aron son of Senouthios, who exist
22    and the one which we first named above, you shall come in
23    by command and you shall be the lord of my share in that dwelling,
24    which is the four(th) part, from now forever, at all times,
25    upon you and (upon) all who come after you, after you. The one who shall dare and come out against you,
26    whether I myself or a child if there be one, or a brother if there be one, or on (the side of) my father
27    or on (the side of) my mother — first they of that kind shall benefit nothing,
28    but he shall be a stranger to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit;
29    afterwards they shall exact (it) from him, and he shall be bound to this document, apart from
30    the penalty and the fine which they shall appoint upon him, to pay them
31    to your assurance. Therefore we drew up this sale for you, secure, having power, in
32    any place where they shall bring it out, and they shall question me, my acknowledging as is stated above.
33    I, Anastase son of Philothis, bear witness. I, Leontios son of the blessed
34    Aristophane, wrote on his behalf, and I bear witness.
35    Through me, Souai son of the blessed Philotheos, it was written.
36    Senouthios son of Enoch.
37    My share in the courtyard which is on the wall, of the man of Pshomare;
38    their boundaries: the west: the house of Petrou (Petros) (son of) Zacharias; the east: the wall inward;
39    the middle: the house of David (and) Philippos; the south: the wall inward, and the door of the proprie[tors]. The price: of nomismata 3⅔.
