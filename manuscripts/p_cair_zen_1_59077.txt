[TRANSLATION]
1     Krotos to Zenon greetings. Know that Alexis has done nothing [concerning]
2     the slave girl, whom we pledged from those who sailed [regarding] the oil [transaction],
3     toward their judgment [against them], for he neither returns to us the money nor the
4     slave girl. Similarly also [the matter] against Theron the sailor from the barge he [leaves unsettled],
5     for he released him from prison, and in general he is not visible [/present].
6     Farewell. Year 29, Xandicus 5.
7     [Krotos] concerning the slave girl whom [he pledged] from those who sailed
8     [concerning the] oil [transaction], and Theron the
9     [from the] barge. Year 29, Daisios
10    … [in] Alexandria.
11    To Zenon.
