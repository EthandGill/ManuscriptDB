[TRANSLATION]
1     Nikias (son of) Epikrates, overseer of the new vineyard-land
2     on the Island, (to) Psenthotes (son of) Psansnos, through
3     Panemeus (son of) Dioskourides, greeting. I have
4     from you, for the price of wine of the produce of year 13
5     of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
6     impure twelve drachmas, total impure drachmas
7     12. Year 14
8     of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
9     Choiak 8.
