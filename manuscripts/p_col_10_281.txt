[TRANSLATION]
1     The Aurelii Hol son of Kopres, of the mother Apilla, from the village
2     of Philadelphia, komarch (village-headman) of the present 3rd year and 2nd year,
3     and Cornelius son of Posis, of the mother Iouliane, from the same
4     village, to each other, greeting. Since I happen to [be] komarch of the same village, hereby we have agreed
5     with each other that I, Hol, shall nominate you, Cornelius, to the komarchy of the same village for the coming year, you completing the appointed one-year period; and, I Cornelius having likewise fulfilled (it), I too shall nominate
6     your own full brother Dioskoros for the following year, he too completing the same
7     one-year period; likewise that I, Hol, have given surety … …
8     the brother [Dio]skoros for [the s]am[e time]
9     just as also it shall not be permitted to either of us [to transgress what is in]
10    the written agreement[s]
11    by force; but if either should [wrong] the other [con]cerning this, the transgressor shall pay to the one abiding (by it), [on account of]
12    a penalty and outrage, of silver [drachmas]
13    two thousand, because on these terms they [have] agreed [with]
14    each other, and having been formally questioned [we acknowledged].
15    Aurelius Hol, aged about 30, with a scar
16    above the right eyebrow.
17    In the 3rd year of our lord D[i]ocle[tian and the 2nd year]
18    of our lord [Ma]x[i]mian, the Augus[ti] …
