[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Valerius Licinnianus Licinnius Augustus and Flavius Valerius Constantinus, son of the Augusti,
2     on the 18th day before the Kalends of July, that is, Pauni 20 …
3     To Ulpius Alexandros, censitor of the Heptanomia,
4     from Aurelius Isidoros son of Ptolemaios, his mother being Heroïs, from the village of Karanis, of the 5th pagus of the Arsinoite nome.
5     In accordance with the divine command of our masters Marcus Aurelius Valerius Maximianus the elder Augustus and Galerius
6     Valerius Maximianus and Valerius Licinnianus Licinnius, the Augusti, and Galerius Valerius Maximinus and Flavius Valerius
7     Constantinus, sons of the Augusti, the most illustrious Caesars, I register before you, the censitor sent down
8     by their heavenly fortune,
9     as liable to tax myself, Isidoros, 40 years old …
10    as exempt my son Peëoun, 3 years old,
11    and I dwell in my own house in the same village, no one having dwelt with me for a long time at all,
12    and I swear by all the gods and by the fortune and victory of our masters the unconquered emperors that I have concealed no one. I, Aurelius Isidoros, swore by all the gods and by the divine oath that I have made the declaration truly, and I have submitted it.
13    I, Aurelius Isidoros son of Heras, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
14    There results: liable to tax, one;
15    exempt, one.
