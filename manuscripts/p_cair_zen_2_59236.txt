[TRANSLATION]
1     To Diotimus the administrator, greetings, Neoptolemos, Macedonian of those allotment-holders in Philadelphia. My father Stratipos is being wronged by Theokles, who governed the Aphroditopolite nome, and Petosiri, the royal secretary. For they make an assessment
2     on the vineyards, receiving the produce from three years, [they] registered one-third, but for my father from two years
3     they have made the assessment, saying it is newly planted. I ask you therefore, if it seems good to you, to look into these things, and if these are true,
4     since for the others too they have made the assessment from three years, to give me an order to Hermolaus and Petosiri so that from three years
5     they make the assessment also for my father, whether they wish to make the beginning from the twenty-ninth year or from the thirtieth year, for we have already made wine from it four years, and to receive for him the money fallen due on account from
6     the wine merchants of wine which they took from the vineyard, so that through you he may obtain justice.
7     Farewell.
8     Neoptolemos' petition to Diotimus concerning
9     a vineyard.
