[TRANSLATION]
1     Grata to Sukcessios the brother, very many greetings.
2     I informed Artemia concerning the 140 skins
3     and she answered [that she had] neither received nor seen them. I sent Geta to the
4     session-keeper and he said [he had] not received [them].
5     But he called Alexander, the formerly
6     curator, and he said he had received [them]
7     and [that he had] handed them over to Kastorates again.
8     [Arti]mas, seeing what you sent, grumbled
9     [say]ing, woe is me, to provide [for] the…
10    [I] do not have. Greet Sabeinos the
11    [bro]ther of mine and everyone.
12    [I] wish you to be well.
