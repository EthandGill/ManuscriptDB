[TRANSLATION]
1     Alkimos
2     chief of the guards
3     Persians of the after-born
4     We acknowledge that we have received through you Alkimos son of Harphasios on the 27th of Phamenoth of the 23rd year, and [on the] days on which you order us to present the aforesaid
5     Alk[imos] within five days, having collected …, but if we do not present [him] to you, it shall be allowed you to deliver us over in place of him and
6     a penalty into the royal treasury of four drachmas from the thirtieth, and none the less let this hand be authoritative everywhere being carried forward
