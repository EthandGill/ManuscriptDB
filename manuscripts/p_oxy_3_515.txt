[TRANSLATION]
1   Philiskos and Herodes, keepers of the public accounts, to Chairemon and Papontos, sitologoi
2   of the district of Monimos, and to Herakleides, sitologos of the district of Singkepha, and to Dionysios, former (sitologos) of the district of Mem(?), greeting.
3   There was registered with us, through Apollonios the scribe, on the 30th of Phamenoth of the 18th year
4   of Hadrian Caesar the lord, for each granary, by individual, the completion-record
5   of wheat of the produce of the 17th year; and Chairemon and Papontos of the district of Monimos, and Herakleides
6   of the district of Singk(epha): [the] monthly account, under the heading of Mecheir; and Chairemon
7   and Papontos alone, of the district of Monimos, also the monthly account of Phamenoth and the supplementary record
8   by individual, of receipts. I, Theon, scribe, have signed.
