[TRANSLATION]
1     4th year of [T]iberius Caesar Augustus, Mesore, 5th of the intercalary days. To be issued.
2     Orseus son of Pat[yn]is, his mother being Tasokis: the aforesaid sale of a ninth share of a vacant plot has been made out to me as is set forth. Diodoros son of Herakleios also wrote on his behalf because he does not know
3     letters. Chaireis son of Apollonios, his mother being Thenobestis, and my wife Taagoreis daughter of Papnebtynis, her mother being Heraëis, acknowledge — I Chaireis on the one hand, that I have sold, according to Egyptian contracts, to Orseus son of Patynis, his mother being Tasoukis, the ninth share belonging to me of a walled-around vacant plot and of all that goes with it, held in common and undivided, in Talei of the Polemon division, of which the neighbours of the whole vacant plot (are): on the south, a common entrance and exit; on the north, of Papnebtynis son of Phasis
4     a house and courtyard; on the west, a plot of Kollouthos son of Patynis; on the east, a royal street. And I have received the whole agreed price in full, and I will guara[nt]ee (it)
5     with every guarantee for all time. And I, [Ta]agoreis the aforesaid wife of Chaireis, consent to the sale and (agree) not to bring claim for
6     all [tim]e, and we will do the rest a[s] is set forth. I, Chaireis, have written also on behalf of my wife, she not knowing letters.
7     Chaireis, 33 years old, scar on the left shin.
8     Orseus, 40 years old, scar on the third finger of the left hand.
9     Taakiaris, 30 years old, scar on the right forearm.
