[TRANSLATION]
1   Thermoutas to Apolinarios, (her) brother,
2   greetings.
3   I want you to know: do not let it concern
4   you about the grain crops; I found
5   a farmer who will harvest them; but
6   who is to give the seed? And concerning the
7   reserve(?) of your (affairs): your sis-
8   ter received six artabas apart from seed.
9   Write to Koupaneous
10   about the house; for Taesis
11   says: 'I am not paying rent;
12   for he owes me money in place of the rents.'
13   And further, blame your sister
14   about the tax, that you do not de-
15   mand from the woman remaining in
16   your house the rent —
17   so that we too may find
18   a reasonable (way) to speak to her. Write
19   a letter to Koupaneous,
20   instructing (him), so that he may find a reason-
21   able (way) to speak to her. Warm-
22   ly does Koupaneous greet you,
23   Zoidas (and) Valeria greet (you),
24   greet Serenos; Heroïs greets
25   you, and Demetrous, and
26   all who love you greet you.
27   [I pray] for your health.
28   And if it seems good to you, send me a blank papyrus sheet, so that we may find (means) to write a letter.
29   Hermias will bring you the … about Kastor, about the others(?)
30   and [...]
31   Deliver to Apolinarios ☓ from Thermoutas (his) sister.
