[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy
2     and Cleopatra, the Manifest Gods, year 9,
3     when Apollodorus son of Zenon was priest
4     of Alexander and of the Savior Gods and of the Brother
5     Gods and of the Benefactor Gods and of the
6     Father-loving Gods and of the Manifest Gods and
7     of the Mother-loving Gods; when the athlophoros of Berenice
8     Euergetis was Kleainete daughter of Komanos;
9     when the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphus was Sarapias daughter of Apollonius; when the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator was Eirene daughter of Ptolemaeus;
10    in the month Apellaios 5, Pauni 5, in
11    Philadelphia of the Arsinoite nome. Theocles son of Eubulides, a Macedonian
12    of the epigone, lent to Aristocles son of Heracleides, a Corinthian of the epigone, 3 talents of bright bronze coinage
13    and 780 drachmas, for 13 months from the aforewritten date, at interest of 2 drachmas to the mina each month
14    each. Let Aristocles repay to Theocles the
15    aforewritten loan, the 3 talents and the 780 of bronze, and the
16    interest accruing on them, in the month
17    Apellaios, Egyptian (month) Pauni, of the 10th year. But if he
18    does not repay, let him pay back the loan at one-and-a-half times,
19    and the interest single; and let the right of execution belong to Theocles, exacting from Aristocles or his surety
20    and from either one of them, whomever he chooses, and from both, as if from a (judicial) decision. Surety of Aristocles
21    for the aforewritten loan of the 3 talents and 780 drachmas
22    of bronze, for payment in full: Megisto daughter of Nicostratus, a Persian woman,
23    with her guardian, her own husband Aristocles the aforewritten. The contract is valid everywhere it may be produced.
24    Theocles, about … years old, [a sca]r in the middle of the forehead, long-faced, a scar on the lower lip on the right.
25    Aristocles, about … years old, [a scar] in the middle of the forehead, round-faced, somewhat bald.
26    Megisto, about 23 years old, of middle height, fair-skinned, with somewhat grey eyes.
27    Eubulides son of Theocles, holder of seven arouras,
28    of the village of Philadelphia. Ktesicles, the debtor …
29    son of Heracleides son of Aristocles
30    the Corinthian …
31    and his surety, Megisto
32    daughter of Nicostratus, a Persian woman, of the village of
33    Philadelphia: 2 talents, 780.
