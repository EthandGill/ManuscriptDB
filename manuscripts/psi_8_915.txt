[TRANSLATION]
1     [So-and-so son of so-and-so] ... s, mother Thermuthis, I acknowledge having sold to Narcissus, freedman, sons of Maron of...
2     [so-and-so and Maron son of] Heracleides, according to this sale, from the present day for all time, the half-share belonging to me of a small two-story house, and it is from the northern part and from the [southern part a court]yard together; of which the measurements of the half-share of the house and courtyard, south to north...
3     [cubits] ... [digits] six, west to east cubits seventeen, and on all of the measurements...
4     [or whatever they may be; of which] neighbors to the south Onnophrius son of Hatres, the shared half-share of the house, to the north...
5     ... [west] sons of Patunios, reed-plaiter, places, to the east, royal street; and I have received...
6     [the agreed price immediate]ly through hand from home, and I will guarantee the sale with every guarantee from [all encumbrances and all] claims for all time. And my wife Thaesis consents...
7     [of so-and-so and our] son Pakebkis, and the [wives] of my deceased sons Chaiemon and...
8     [Onnophrius, women], of Chaiemon [namely] Thaesis Kalatutis, and of Onnophrius Taorseus Patunios...
9     [to this sale and will] not [ap]peal for all time, nor [claim] nor [let another on their behalf as is set forth]. Harmyses son of Orseus wrote on their behalf because they do not know letters.
10    [Narcissus, freedman, s]on of Maron and Maron son of Heracleides, the sale has come to me as is set forth.
