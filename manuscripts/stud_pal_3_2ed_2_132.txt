[TRANSLATION]
1     I, Charachen, king of the Blemmyes
2     [am] writing to [my] children Charachen [and] Charapatcour
3     and Charakhiet, so that I command and [it] has been given
4     the administration of the island called Tanare,
5     and no one is ordered to hinder you. But if
6     the Romans are ungrateful [and] do not provide custom,
7     the phylarch will not be hindered nor the sub-tyrant
8     [from] seizing [the] Romans, until the customs are paid
9     of my island. Charachen, king.
10    Laize, domesticus, [I] bear witness.
11    Tioutikna, domesticus, [I] bear witness.
12    Through me Sansnotos. [It] was written in the month Phaophi [on the] 24th, [of the] indiction 1.
