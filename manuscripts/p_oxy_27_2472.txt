[TRANSLATION]
1     80
2     To Sarapion, strategos of the Lykopolite and of Hypsele,
3     from Horoleon son of Eudaimon the elder and Harmiysis son of Patomtous
4     and Panesneus son of Phmouis, supervisors of the tax of interpretation(?)
5     of the present 4th year of Trajan Hadrian Caesar the lord,
6     the tax-farmers of the 3rd year of Trajan
7     Hadrian Caesar the lord assisting. Account of the five-day period of (receipts) accruing from Thoth 16 to 20 of the same Thoth. (On the) 16th nothing
8     accrued. (On the) 17th: Psois son of Panemgeus from the village of Skinep[oeos]
9     75 artabas of lentil; from the same village in part out of small (sums)
10    99. Akanthon son of Apollonios, shipper from Lykopolis, loaded
11    from the storehouse of …on in the village of Poeis 600 artabas of wheat, and from
12    Apollonios son of Herakleios son of Didymarchos in the village …
13    Mouchenpamounis 200 artabas of wheat. Total of wheat 8.. artabas; (on the) 18th Horion son of Apollo…
14    from Lykopolis, shipper, loaded from the storehouse of Apollonios son of Kollouthos in the village
15    Tasrei 5. artabas of wheat; (on the) 19th, 20th nothing accrued. Total altogether …
16    850 artabas of wheat and 75 artabas of lentil, total … 850, of pure silver … 110 …
17    We swear by the fortune of the Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian
18    Augustus that we have submitted, honestly and truthfully, the
19    foregoing account. Year 4 of the Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian
20    Augustus, Thoth 20. I, Horoleon son of Eudaimon, have submitted.
21    I, Panesneus son of Phmouis, have submitted. I, Harmiysis son of Patomtous, have submitted. I, Apollonios son of Kollouthos, have assisted.
22    e … edole … to T… the pilot, friend
