[TRANSLATION]
1     α …
2     To Sarapiōn [greetings]. I acknowledge [to have received] from you
3     in the city of Oxyrynchus by hand from [house] of silver
4     of Augustus and Ptolemaic [coinage], drachmae
5     two hundred; there are of silver drachmae two hundred as principal, to which nothing at all is attached, which also I shall repay to you
6     on the [thirtieth] of Mesore of the present twentieth
7     year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus without any delay. But if I do not repay as it has been written, I shall pay to you the aforementioned principal with a half surcharge,
8     and for the time having passed the due
9     interest, the recovery being to you from both me and from
10    all my property just as from judgment.
11    Valid is the hand everywhere applicable and to everyone who applies it. Year 20 of Tiberius Caesar
12    Augustus, Mecheir 26.
