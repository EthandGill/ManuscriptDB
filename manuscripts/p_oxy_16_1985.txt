[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the year] after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most illustrious, Phaophi 11, of the 7th indiction.
2     To Flavius Apion the most magnificent ordinary consul, landowner
3     [and] here also in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas the house-servant
4     who acts as questioner and provides for his own master, the
5     same all-praiseworthy man, the right of action and obligation: Aurelius
6     Pamouthios, manager, son of Andreas, his mother being Sophia,
7     originating from the hamlet of Mikra Tholthis, an estate
8     of your Magnificence, of the Oxyrhynchite nome, a registered
9     cultivator, greeting. Since a need has now arisen for the
10    landowner's irrigation-machine under me, called Para Potamon, which draws water
11    onto arable land — of one sinorganon and one hilasterion
12    and one wheel of the cylinder-type — having come up to the city
13    I petitioned your Magnificence to order that there be provided to me
14    those same machine-implements. And immediately your glorious
15    house, taking forethought for the maintenance of its own
16    affairs, having had these brought down to me in the fields out of the
17    acanthus-groves which are on that same estate of mine,
18    new, serviceable, satisfactory water-drawing implements, I received,
19    for the replenishment of all the machine-implements,
20    on this present day, which is Phaophi the eleventh
21    of the present year 220, 189, of the current seventh indiction,
22    for the water-supply of the crops of the eighth, with God's help, assessment.
23    And for proof of the receipt I have made this
24    bond, which being authoritative, written in a single copy, and having been formally questioned I acknowledged.
25    Aurelius Pamouthios son of Andreas, the aforewritten,
26    have made this bond, having received
27    the machine-implements: one sinorganon and one hilasterion
28    and one wheel of the cylinder-type, according to the aforesaid
29    manner, and everything agrees with me as set forth above.
30    Theodoros son of the blessed Phib, having been asked, wrote on his behalf,
31    he being illiterate.
32    [Bond of Pamouthios son of Andreas from the hamlet of Mikra Th]olthis, for the receipt of one sinorganon and a hilasterion,
33    one, and one wheel of the cylinder-type.
