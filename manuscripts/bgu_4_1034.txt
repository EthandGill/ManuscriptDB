[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a property-declaration
2     To Alexander, also [called] Harpech[...], who has served as gymnasiarch and [...]
3     has served as exegete [and] keeper of property-records of the Arsinoite [nome].
4     from Tyrannos, also [called] Achilles [...] also [called] Althaios
5     and of his full-sister Di[dym]aria
6     a citizen of both: Sotos Tyrannos son of Eudaimonos,
7     and Didumaria with her guardian Tyrannos.
8     We declare jointly and equally concerning the village of Kerkesouchoi of [the He]rakleidos division
9     a vineyard, having been harvested, lying fallow, of arouras 2 1/6, which has come to us
10    from the inheritance of our mother Heraid [daughter] of Achilles son of [...]
11    a citizen who has died, leaving us as sole children and heirs, [assets] arranged according-to
12    the deposit of her purchases from Dei[os]
13    of Chaireos of Di[os]koros.
14    [year] 5, Tybi 9
15    through Demetrios [of] the money-changers
