[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Horion and as I am styled
2     to [Aurelius] Pareitus through Isidora
3     wife, greeting;
4     after all account having been settled,
5     the reckoned ones to your [son]
6     being in orphan condition, you appeared to owe
7     for the periods of the cultivation
8     of your husband only wheat artabas
9     seven, [making] artabas 7, toward my share and the tax
10    of the having-passed year [of which] you cultivated my arourae together with your son-in-law Petechon according to the
11    share falling to you.
12    Year 9 and 7, Mecheir 13.
13    Horion I have signed.
