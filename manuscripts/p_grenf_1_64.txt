[TRANSLATION]
1     Many times according to the command of their authority [I] have written the matter to the
2     most illustrious Theophanes to take [possession] of the Antinoites, and he was not willing to do this
3     to do. Of the soldiers then [being] sent, I do not find [myself able] to bring [forward] to their
4     authority both concerning the aforementioned most illustrious Theophanes and concerning the
5     release of the one [who] is in custody, [a] farmer, and with god I find opportunity
6     I bring forward to their authority and I hope in god that each [matter] is able
7     to advance.
8     To my master, in all things most magnificent and illustrious Anatolius, count, 643.
