[TRANSLATION]
1     To Diodotus, priest, chief-judge and in charge of the care of the money-changers and of the other [judgment] chambers
2     from Aphrodeisius of Didymus of Heron, from the Arsinoeite
3     being absent, on behalf of [whom he] asks to complete the registration, Apollonides of Melanos, resident from [the] Syoe of the Arsinoeite, and from
4     Titus S[a]lonius Symmachus and Ti[tus] Julius [Ze]non.
5     The S[a]lonius Sym[machus] agrees to have sold with confirmation of Julius Zenon to [A]phrodeisius through [the] Apollonides the slave-girl Ligurian now
6     named Neike, by birth Pontian, as of years
7     thirteen, from the import, as simple property, being free from sacred disease and touch, tested according to what is prescribed, at the price of silver drachmas two thousand six hundred, which also Salonius Symmachus has received
8     from [the] Apollonides, both by hand and through Tiberius
9     Julius Sarapions through the attending money-changers being [from the] account of Aphrodeisius, which even from now
10    through [the A]pollonides having received the slave-girl Ligurian [to be master of her] remains, as in a contest [they] were separated; we request
11    [through the agency of Julius Sarapions presenting].
12    [Year] … [of Autokrator] C[aesar Marcus Aur]elius Commodus
13    [Antoninus Pious] Fortun[ate Aug]ustus Armenian
14    [Median] Parthian Sarmatian [German] Maximum Britannic
15    [month Choi]ach 2.
16    Poseidonius, secretary, under [the Ju]lius Sarapions, I registered.
