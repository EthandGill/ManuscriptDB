[TRANSLATION]
1     Remainder of grain of the following administrators and others of the 7th indiction:
2     Administration of the count Anoup, thus:
3     With the heirs of Anoup, administrator of Leonides, of foul grain, by the kankellon (measure), 89 1/4 artabas, 7 choinikes, into private (account) 7 1/3 1/12 1/48 1/96 solidi less 29½.
4     With those from Euangelion, of foul grain, by the kankellon (measure), 806 artabas, into private (account) 67 1/6 solidi less 265½ 1/4.
5     With …on, administrator of Spania, of foul grain, by the kankellon (measure), 4[2] 1/4 artabas, 3 choinikes, into private (account) 3½ 1/48 1/96 solidi less 14.
6     Administration of the [most magnificent] count Andronikos, deputy-landholder:
7     [With] [P]apnouthios, administrator of Tarouthinos, of foul grain, by the kankellon (measure), [28]6 1/4 artabas, 6 choinikes, into pri[vate (account)] 23½ 1/3 1/48 1/96 [less] 95 1/4.
8     With those from Tarouthinos, of foul grain, by the kankellon (measure), 97[0]2(?) artabas, 5 choinikes, into private (account) 81 1/96 [less] 324.
9     Administration of the count Georgios:
10    [With] Phib, administrator of Pangouleeon, of foul grain, [by the kankellon (measure)] 206½ artabas, 6 choinikes [into solidi] 16½ 1/3 1/12 … less 67,
11    of which was given to the banker 16 solidi less 6[4] … 192 artabas, remaining 14½ artabas, 6 choinikes.
