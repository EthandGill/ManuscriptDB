[TRANSLATION]
1     Let your sisterly disposition vouchsafe to send me through the letter-carrier the bowl, since a need of it arises tomorrow;
2     For I heard that my lord the ruler is going up. And after the
3     visit of him again if you need you [may] receive; for you know that [nothing] else
4     I do not have; for if I had, I would not have sought, as also you understand.
