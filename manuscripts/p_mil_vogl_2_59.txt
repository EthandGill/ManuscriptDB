[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptollarion to Kronion
2     greetings.
3     to [the] [one giving] to you the
4     [little letter] of mine measure, [from which] I myself use
5     grain [artabas], in the measure of dromoi
6     of wheat artabas six; but Nemesion the brother
7     having taken and himself seal [all]; but [make] them to be [sent forward] through those from the village;
8     [since] a [necessary] friend
9     to me concerning this imposed,
10    to neglect not I [was able].
11    to be well I pray for you.
12    To Kronion, steward of Ptollarion.
