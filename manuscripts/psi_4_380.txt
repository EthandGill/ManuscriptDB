[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermas to Zenon greetings. Know that Menodorus … when and the
2     instructions from you lost in Taskry of the Memphis district. Until
3     indeed he was expected, [we] were obtaining precedence, fear underlying that he will bring something
4     firm from you; but when he came and [they] perceived that he had brought nothing, [they] set upon
5     us the people and placed hands against the shepherds, not allowing [them] to herd in
6     the reeds, from which the huts were already lifted two months ago, saying that
7     it is prior to herd our oxen in the reeds than the goats. You
8     then will do well having made attention about these things, and about the goatherd be concerned how [he might] be examined before the manager and the generals. There are
9     for the ones dressed in [clothes] of the elders. And of the guards saying to
10    the elder that this one is the one who dressed, the elder having come
11    into the prison and having seen the young man [said] he had not seen him. Do not suppose [that] we are eager about the body but how
12    the same not happen to us in vain. Well you would do quickly sending him away; for I am troubled having received the blows.
13    Be strong. Month of Pharmuth 27.
14    Year 37, Pachon 4.
15    Hermas, letter of Menodorus.
16    To Zenon.
