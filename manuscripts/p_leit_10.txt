1     To Aurelius Besa [son] of Orphanos and Hieracapollon
2     [son] of Hierax and Hieraphpollon [son] of Dion
3     to rulers [and] councillors of Panopolis from Aurelius
4     Theotimus and Theon from the same city;
5     [we were] instructed yesterday by January the perfect
6     concerning mining works so that you are putting us forward unjustly into [the position of] livestock-owner,
