[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Peter the contractor Theodosios the baker. I reckon for you on account of the fourth
2     edict of the ninth indiction through Viktoros Phoibammon the pig-herder gold coin
3     one minus carats four, [become gold coin one minus carats four] only. Theodosios the baker agrees
4     the order of the one [coin minus carats] [four] as previously stated, Thoth two
5     nine of indiction. … api… basi.
6     Order of Theodosios the baker for the fourth edict of indiction nine through Viktoros Phoibammon coin one minus carats four.
