[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapous son of Dionysius through Sarapion [son of] Theon, [his] husband, to the grain-buyers
2     of Sko, places, greetings. Set out what remaining [amounts] you hold of mine
3     in the [category] of wheat grain of the past 15th year of Aurelius
4     Antoninus Caesar the lord
5     [from] Taapyche [of] Nemesatos through Psoites Sambath
6     … sefo artabas four [and] half [and] quarter measures four, [they] amount to artabas 4½ measures 4. 16th year
7     [of Aur]elius Antoninus the lord, Tybi 14
