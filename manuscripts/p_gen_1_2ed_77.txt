[TRANSLATION]
1     paid down year 19, Epeiph 10, into the [account] of Valerius Celearius
2     also of Philoxenos, account, through Apeis, manager,
3     Tesenuphis, wine-seller of Philadelphia, from the price
4     of wine, product of year 18, of silver drachmas
5     three hundred forty four, of change,
6     drachmas fifty two, making altogether drachmas 400.
