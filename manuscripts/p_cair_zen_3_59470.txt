[TRANSLATION]
1     Molo[ssus] to Zenon greetings. After writing to you the first [letter, which Horus our representative sent to you]
2     Theogenes came to Mendes on the 23rd of Me[chir] [bringing the things you arranged]
3     to him sealed bundles of flax-reed, three; they are sold at …
4     And when asked whether the merchants are able to dispose of ten thousand one bundles, they said [they are able. As much therefore as to you]
5     is profitable, send as quickly as possible the one who will dispose [of them]; order [him to take care]
6     that [they be sold] as profitably as possible. Write also to Promethion the ban[ker so that when in the region]
7     [he] may take care [together with me]. But concerning the papyri we ourselves also wrote to you [that] …,
8     and having met Criton at the Delta, I have told [him] to report to you. Having learned [therefore from him]
9     write me what I ought to do, so that it may happen thus. Also send word to me about yoursel[f whether you are well].
10    Farewell. Year 3 …
11    Molossos.
12    To Zenon.
