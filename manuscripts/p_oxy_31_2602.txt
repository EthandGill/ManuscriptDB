[TRANSLATION]
1     To [my] lord brother Herakleios,
2     [Agathos] greeting.
3     Having been deemed worthy, my lord [brother], to contribute to our sister
4     Theodora if anything she should request of you. For you yourself know, lord,
5     brother, that wife of
6     our brother [she is]. Help therefore her if anything she should request of you; for I know your earnestness. Rather let [them] be provided to her
7     the things of your good intention.
8     I send greetings from myself
9     my lord sons and
10    their lady mother.
11    I wish you to be well
12    for many times,
13    my lord brother.
14    To my lord brother Herakleios, Agathos
