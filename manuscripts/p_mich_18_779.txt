[TRANSLATION]
1     From Ornumene beside [Petemin]ius
2     of those from Muchos of Polemon's
3     division; I delivered to Athenodorus
4     the dioiketes according to Pythion the
5     [oikono]m[o]s managing the same division
6     concerning drachmas 4 which he transacted with me
7     and illogical …
8     toward talents 5 Βτ and the [memorandum] having been sent up to you
9     you wrote to Philon to restore
10    him to you.
11    Since then the man
12    avoids promising me what is right,
13    I request therefore you, if it seems to you, to write
14    to me an order to the guards so that whoever is found may be restored to you; but if not,
15    … to lock his house.
