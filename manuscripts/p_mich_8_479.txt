[TRANSLATION]
1   Claudius Terentianus to Claudius Tiberianus,
2   his father and lord, very many greetings.
3   Before all things I pray for your health
4   and good fortune, which is my wish. I marvel
5   how, having sailed up, you did not write back
6   to me about your welfare, but up to to-
7   day I am anxious, because you left me
8   being unwell. So you will do well
9   to write back to me more quickly about your
10   welfare. You will deliver to Tabatheus the let-
11   ter, the one from the dioiketes to the stra-
12   tegos. And let him do everything to deliver
13   it more quickly to the strategos, so that, in response to
14   what has been written to him, he too may quickly write
15   back. I too was about to sail up, but
16   I have not yet sorted out my pending affairs
17   up to today. And if you learn about Nemesi-
18   anus, where he is staying, write back to me, since
19   we are anxious about him too. There greet
20   you my (folk). Greet all those who love
21   you by name.
22   (2nd hand) I pray for your
23   health.
24   To Claudius Tiberianus, the most honoured, from Cla[udius]
25   Terentianus.
