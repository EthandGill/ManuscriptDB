[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios.
2     of the aforementioned memorandum having been submitted to Sarapion...
3     [to those] of the successors and sub-administrator from Ptolemaios...
4     of the one having been in charge of the twins in the great Serapieion...
5     concerning what is owed to them from the royal [treasury] annually...
6     sesame oil and castor oil [with] an authorization bearing [the name of] Memnides.
7     [Having] examined what ought to be given, [and] from you: to the scribes. [Having] examined [and] to have brought [it] forth, and through this...
8     [it] having been signified [that] a petition was submitted to the king...
9     concerning these [matters], [we,] having taken up the copy of this...
10    together with the rescript having been made regarding it, we submitted...
11    and Dorios the copyist, having taken [it], brought forth a copy...
12    of which a referral has been made to Sarapion, through which it is signified that in...
13    the 6th year from Phamenoth, [the] month in which the mourning occurred...
14    until Mesore, [it is claimed that] they were given [the portions] falling to the six-month period [of] oil...
15    six choes and castor oil six choes, [while those] pertaining to the 18th year [of] oil...
16    one metretes and castor oil one metretes, [with] the proper [authorization] having been issued...
17    rescript and those [for the 19th year] having fallen through, [they] were not advanced...
18    because [they] had become a year overdue. [But those pertaining] to the 19th year, examining...
19    we find [they] have not been authorized. Since then [it] has been written...
20    [by] the dioiketes: to give the half [of] the amounts owed to the temples...
21    and the king, regarding the petition having been submitted, has ordered:...
22    even if they received [it] previously, [to] give [it] now, and Sarapion too...
23    the sub-administrator has issued [an order] to us through the aforementioned memorandum: [Having] examined what ought to be given, we report.
24    [Year 20], Hathyr 13. [It was] signed: [to] advance accordingly.
