[TRANSLATION]
1     Petearoueris and Pamonthes, collectors of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis. We have received for poll-tax and bath-tax
3     of year 8, base drachmas twelve, total base drachmas 12.
4     Year 8 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
5     Phamenoth 5. I, Pao( ), have signed.
