[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixteenth year of Imperator Caesar Trajanus
2     Hadrianus Augustus, in the month Sebastos, the 8th, in Karanis of the
3     division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nome. Thasos acknowledges,
4     daughter of Petheus son of Pasoknopaios, about fifty years old, with a scar
5     on the left hand, with her guardian, her husband Petesouchos son of
6     Petaus, about sixty years old, with a scar on the left eyebrow — to Petheus son of Pnepheros, about thirty years old, with a scar on the left knee,
7     that she has from him on the spot, by hand from the house, one hundred [drachmas] of silver
8     as a loan, at interest of a drachma per mina each month,
9     and besides also twelve and a sixth artabas of wheat by the six-choinix measure
10    of the interpreter of Karanis, the repayment of which let
11    the acknowledging woman make to Petheus in the month Pauni of the present
12    sixteenth year of Hadrianus Caesar the lord. And if she does
13    not repay, let her pay back on the spot with the half as much again
14    and with interest,
15    the right of execution belonging to Petheus both against the acknowledging woman
16    and against all her possessions, as if by legal judgment;
17    until she repays, it shall not be permitted to Thasos to sell nor
18    to mortgage nor otherwise to dispose of the fifth part belonging
19    to her of a house and courtyard in the village, of which
20    the neighbours are: to the south the house of Mysthes son of Melanion, to the north
21    the house of Bekeno…pso, to the west an entrance and exit, to the east
22    the [royal] street. Subscriber: Heron son of Satyros.
23    I, Thasos daughter of Petheus son of Pasoknopaios, with my guardian
24    my husband Petesouchos son of Petaus, acknowledge that I have
25    from Petheus son of Pnepheros one hundred drachmas of silver
26    … and twelve and a sixth artabas of wheat,
27    [which] I shall repay in the month Pauni of the present 16th year, and
28    I hold as security the fifth part belonging to her
29    of a house and courtyard in the same village.
30    [Heron] son of Satyros wrote for her, she not knowing letters.
31    Registered through the record-office in Karanis.
