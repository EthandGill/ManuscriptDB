[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos,
2   from Stephanos (freedman) of Caesar and from Diodoros
3   son of Diodoros. Stephanos concedes that he has re-
4   ceived from Diodor[o]s, through the money-changing
5   bank of Helenos, the loans he lent him
6   according to the agreements made through the same tribunal,
7   two, in the 8th (year) of Caesar: one in Pha-
8   ophi, 300 silver dr., the other in Phamen[oth],
9   200 silver dr.; and, of both loans, the
10   interest of the overdue time; and that
11   the said agreements are void, together with the
12   bank-payments made toward each of them
13   through Tet... his own ... and Demetrios’ money-changing
14   bank; and that Stepha-
15   nos will not proceed, nor any other on his behalf, against Diodo-
16   ros, neither about the same nor about any-
17   thing else at all, written or unwritten, fr[om] times
18   [pa]st up to the present
19   day; or - the agreed terms not being valid -
20   he is liable both for the damages an[d the] ap-
21   pointed fine. And D[io]do[ros] too concedes
22   that he has recovered from Stephanos what he [ga]ve
23   him according to the agreement of the month Phaophi -
24   the documents ..., all of them, [and a co]-
25   py of the agreement concerning the sl[ave,]
26   such (documents) as he handed over.
