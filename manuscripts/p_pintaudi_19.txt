18    conduct. And if he is sought

23    city, apart from every place of refuge,

25    I acknowledge, for his absconding, of gold eight solidi (nomismatia), of the weight …
26    [ ] [Surety of B]artholomaios son of the late Menas, from Aktouarion, standing guarantor [for Bartholomaios]
