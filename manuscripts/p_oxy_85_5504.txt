[TRANSLATION]
1     Claudius Herakleidēs, supervisor of gubernatorial
2     letters and other [matters], to Sarapion, also called Apollonianos,
3     formerly inspector of the Oasis of seven nomes, to-the
4     most dear, greetings.
5     The letter which you sent to Maikios Laitos, the
6     most illustrious governor, concerning what you found
7     from the inspection that took place, I have delivered.
8     I pray you are well, most dear.
9     Year 10 of the Emperors Caesars Lucius Septimius Severus
10    Pious Pertinax Arabicus Adiabenichus Parthicus
11    M[aximu]s and M[arc]us Aurel[i]us Antoninus the Pious Augustuses,
12    Pachon 15.
