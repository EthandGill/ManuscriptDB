[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord father
2     Apeitheon, Tauris
3     greeting.
4     Send us
5     two combs of weavers' (make)
6     and two ounces of storax, but also two large combs
7     for the head.
8     Just as you told me,
9     I am sending
10    you such things to the farmstead; (you too) send.
11    The purple (cloth) which you said
12    that I am buying, buy.
13    Tell, then, the sister
14    of the wife of Dioscorus
15    to say to Didyme that, just as
16    you said, the di-nut(?) (basket)
17    if you are making, make them;
18    if you are not making (them), (send) the purple (cloth)
19    and the tow of my father.
20    I greet Eisthyr
21    and your sister Sousanna. So, just as you said,
22    Kyra, that "I am sending you sabakathia" — send (them), and I am sending
23    you those of the Egyptians. I greet you, Kyra, and your
24    sister and the sister
25    of your mother.
26    To my lord brother Theodorus, Taueris, very many greetings. Buy me, my lord brother, three sabakathia
27    and the boot … which you mentioned, and three pairs of sandals (solia) for the baths; the soft half-pound (cloth) which I gave you for use,
28    take (it), make it into a face-cloth, or bring its price; send me two large combs. Just as
29    you said that … I am buying, when you come out, remember the oath; the small lampstand which (is)
30    in the sack, give it to no one. Bring it when you come. The soft half-pound (cloth) … …
31    into the cabin(?). The son Heraclianus said that, the two talents, if it seems good to you, … buy purple (cloth).
32    I greet you Herakl…n and your spouse. I greet Theod…u. Come quickly that we may see you.
