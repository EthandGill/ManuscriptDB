[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Limenius [and Catlinus] the most illustrious, Mecheir 11. There acknowledge to one another the Aurelii Ph[il]adelphos and [Eustorgion, both] daughters of Helis, sisters of the same womb, from Alexandria,
2     dwelling in the village of Phi[ladelphia o]f the Arsinoite nome, with Eustorgion being supported and with the consent to this division of her husband Aurelius Koueres son of Apollos, from the same village
3     of Philadelphia, greeting. Since we have divided among ourselves our paternal building-plot in the same Philadelphia, and there fell to you, Eustorgion, by just and unshakeable lot, according to
4     the share belonging to you, a fifth part, the donkey-stable toward the middle and to the south of the house, being unroofed; and we have agreed with one another within six months — that is, in the month Epeiph of the auspicious
5     incoming ninth new indiction — to roof in common the aforesaid courtyard of the donkeys,
6     I Philadelphos giving four parts of the expenses and you Eustorgion one part. If then our sister Nonna shall yield up … … herself … … and …
7     but if not, I will give entrance only as far as the principal gateway, falling to the share into which you were allotted,
8     into the space between the part of the stable and my own exedra. And it shall not be permitted to any of us to transgress any of the
9     written terms; and the one who proceeds against it shall pay to the other, on account of evasion and malice, three hundred myriads of silver denarii. And if I do not [pay you, I will let] you remain until the appointed term, as you remain,
10    unhindered. The agreement is valid, and being [asked the formal question] we acknowledged. The Aurelii Philadelphos and Eustorgion
11    and Koueres consent to the written terms as set forth above. Aurelius Sarapion son of Julianus,
12    from Alexandria, dwelling [in the sa]me village, who also made the document, wrote on their behalf, since they do not know letters, in the [presence of wit]nesses Herennius son of Harpokration and Longinas … …, from the [sa]me village. …
