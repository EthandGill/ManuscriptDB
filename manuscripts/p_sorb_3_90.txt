[TRANSLATION]
1     ... to Aristarchus, greetings. We encountered Ktesiakl[es]
2     [c]oncerning the people in Sebennytos concerning the three-obol [matter].
3     [He] said therefore it was not possible to record the 40 drachmas to the royal [account], but [they] must fall into Hermaphilus's
4     account, and you do not wish to contend
5     about this. And he requests [you] to write to Komon
6     receiving the 40 drachmas, since the people also produce this small coin for plowing. Concerning
7     the remaining [matters] however they may be administered we will send [word] to you.
8     Farewell.
9     To Aristarchus.
