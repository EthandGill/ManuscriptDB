[TRANSLATION]
1     6
2     In the sixteenth year of the Emperor Caesar Tr[a]ianus
3     Hadrianus Augustus, in the month Hadrianos, the 25th, in Theadelph[ia]
4     of the division of Themistes of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge,
5     Akous son of Hermaion, about twenty years old, with a scar …
6     and Taorsenouphis daughter of Chonses, about forty years old,
7     with a scar below the right knee, with her guardian, the aforewritten Akou[s],
8     to Didymion son of Soterichos, about thirty-six years old, with a scar
9     on the little finger of the left hand, that they have ceded
10    to him, from the present sixteenth year, the cultivation of (the lands) which the deceased father of Ak[ous],
11    Hermaion … bou, cultivated around the [sam]e Th[e]a[de]lphia, of royal land, altogether six arouras in (separate) parcels,
12    … an … an … et …
13    … a of it … ou … a …
14    ten … the remaining three arouras … a … in place of the
15    yearly rents payable to the treasury and the additional measures
16    and all the other taxes and all
17    expenses, from the same sixteenth year of Hadri[anu]s the l[o]rd, on behalf of the same arouras, the sowing being fr[om the incom]ing seventeenth year, Didymion receiving the [seed on behalf o]f the arouras from the public granary
18    from t[he sa]m[e in]com[ing seven]teenth year; and (they warrant them) fre[e] from all public (charges) up to the pas[t]
19    fifteenth year and the fifteenth [year] itself.
20    We, Akous son of Hermaion [and Taorsenouphis daughter of Chonses, acknowled]ge that we have ceded to Didymion the [cultiv]ation of the aforesaid royal land, altogether six arouras, fro[m] the sixteenth year, o[n the]
21    aforesaid (terms). Dorion son of Dorio[n] wrote on their behalf,
22    they not knowing letters. It has been registered through the record-office in Theadelpheia.
