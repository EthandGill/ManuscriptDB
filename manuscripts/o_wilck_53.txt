[GREEK]

r.2   α (ἔτους) Τραειανοῦ Καίσαρος τοῦ κυρίου
r.3   ἐπὶ λόγ(ου) (δραχμὰς) τέσσαρας (γίνονται) (δραχμαὶ) δ. Παοῖνι η ἄλλας (δραχμὰς) δ.

[TRANSLATION]
1   Patroszmethis son of Pelaios Paiai paid, for the poll-tax
2   of the 1st year of Trajan Caesar the lord,
3   on account four drachmas — total 4 dr. Pauni 8, another 4 drachmas.
4   Likewise I have another 7 drachmas. The same, the remaining 2 drachmas.
5   I, Hermogenes son of Ammonas, the collector, wrote (this), Epeiph 30.
