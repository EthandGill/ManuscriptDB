[TRANSLATION]
1     Menes to Zenon greetings. ... those having led into the
2     Amyntas' Donax and the [from Zen]odoros ... for was about to be led away already.
3     We seized and also ... [a] little child and he is inside.
4     We sent to you Donax [carrying the] arrived portions,
5     from Megakleos the over ... calf, from Satyrion
6     of the from Cyprus oikonomos leg [calf, from ] ... ratos of the over the
7     mandates side ...
8     ... Be well. Year 29, Daisios 20.
9     Menes concerning Donax and Zenodoros to Zenon.
10    child, and concerning portions.
11    Year 29, Daisios 20, in Arsinoe.
