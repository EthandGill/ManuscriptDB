[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius [to] those in Polemon's division
2     to the overseers and the others assigned to business, greetings. The distributed according to village
3     myrrh, let no one sell more than
4     a mina of silver forty drachmas, in bronze talents three, two, and to these a transport charge at the talent sixty drachmas; and these record until third
5     of Pharmouti to the official sent for this purpose
6     tax-collector. And let the following proclamation
7     be published and through the village-scribe's
8     judgment, who [also] with you under the order signs; or that he who does contrary to these will blame [himself]. We have sent
9     and for this purpose also the blade-bearers.
10    Farewell. Year 6, Pharmouti 2.
11    the ... from those village overseers
12    and of the others myrrh let not record more
13    than a mina of silver forty drachmas, in bronze talents three, two drachmas, and
14    transport charge at the talent sixty drachmas, or that contrary
15    to these doing will blame himself.
