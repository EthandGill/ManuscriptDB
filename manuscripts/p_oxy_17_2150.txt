r.1    Δίδυμος Ἀπολλωνίῳ τῷ ἀδ[ελ]φῷ
r.2    πολλὰ χαίρειν.
r.3    πρὸ μὲν πάντων σε πολλὰ προσαγορεύω

[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymos to Apollonios his br[other]
2     much greeting.
3     Before all else I greet you greatly
4     and your children undamaged by evil eye. And another time indeed
5     I wrote to you about the purple cloth which you sent me through the son of Apollonios, and now to you
6     I write so that you might reply back to me how much
7     for each [one] and how you wish it to be made,
8     in order that very quickly it might be made for you.
9     I pray that you are well.
10    Deliver to the house of Sarmates, [who was] prytanis, from Didymos.
