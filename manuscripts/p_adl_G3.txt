[TRANSLATION]
1     In the year [6],
2     Hathyr 16, there sold,
3     Pasemis son of Nechoutes,
4     the share belonging to him
5     of mainland called (the land) of Telon,
6     the half, and of a palm-grove
7     the same share.
8     Horos bought it,
9     son of Nechoutes, for 2 talents of copper, [drachmas] 2,500.
10    In the reign of Cleopatra and King Ptolemy, the Mother-loving Saviour Gods,
11    in the year 6, in the priesthood of King Ptolemy the god Philometor Soter Alexander, and of the Saviour
12    Gods and of the Brother Gods and of the Benefactor Gods and of the Father-loving Gods an[d] of the
13    Manifest Gods and of the God Eupator and of the God Philometor and of the God Philopator the Younger
14    and of the God Euergetes and of the Mother-loving Saviour Gods; of the sacred foal of Isis the great,
15    mother of the gods; the athlophoros of Berenice E[u]ergetis, [the kanephor]os of Arsinoe [Philadelphos,]
16    the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator, those being [and existing] in Alexa[ndria,]
17    and in Ptolemais of the Thebaid, in the priesthood of priests and priestesses and kanephoros, those being
18    and existing in Ptolemais; in the month Hathyr 16, in Pathyris, before Heliodoros the agoranomos.
19    Pasemis son of Nechoutes, a Persian of the epigone, about 30 years old, somewhat short, honey-coloured,
20    with somewhat curly hair, broad face, straight nose, sold — from the share of land belonging to him,
21    of corn-bearing (land) in the plain about Pathyris, the half; neighbours: on the south, the land of Patseous,
22    on the north, the land of Patous son of Pelaios; on the east, the wall of the vineyards; on the west,
23    the dry land of Patous son of Phibis, or whoever they may be — and from the palm-grove the half,
24    neighbours: on the south, (the land) of Sathon; on the north, of Nechoutes son of Gounsis; on the east, the river; on the west, a canal, or whoever the
25    neighbours may be; in which the brothers have a share undivided. Horos son of Nechoutes bought (it) for 2 talents of copper, 2,500 (drachmas). The fore-seller
26    and guarant[or of the] sale (is)
27    the vendor, [whom] Horos
28    the buyer accept[ed].
29    Ammonios, the (agent) [of] Heliodoros, have transacted (it).
30    In the year 6, Hathyr 30, there has been paid to the bank in Krokodilon [polis, over which]
31    Apollonios (presides), the tenth, the tax of transfer, according to the …
32    of the tax-farmer's declaration, beneath which there subscribes …
33    the controller; Horos son of Nechoutes, of what he [bought]
34    in the plain about Pathyris, of mainland [and palm-grove from]
35    Pasemis son of Nechoutes, for 2 talents of copper [, 2,500 (drachmas); tax 1,450].
36    Apollonios, banker.
