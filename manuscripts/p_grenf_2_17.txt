[TRANSLATION]
1     In year 34, Tu[by] 9. Patous Patouts
2     [to] Takmeiti Patouts greetings. I acknowledge
3     [that I] have from you an iron cone as a pledge, on condition that if you demand [it] from me and [if I do] not
4     repay [it] to you [I] shall pay to you 1 talent 2 [obol] of bronze
5     [as the] price of the aforementioned cone.
6     [The] guarantor [is] Thaesis the elder, [daughter] of Portitus
7     of all the aforementioned matters. [It was] written [by]
8     Drytion [son] of Pamphilus on behalf of them because [they] claim
9     [that] they do not know letters.
10    Year 34, Tyby 9.
