[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Soknopaiou Nesos, 2 camels, 2 foals. To Apollinaris, strategos, and Sarapion, royal scribe of the Arsinoite nome, Herakleides division, from Horos, son of Horos, the son of Harpagathes, from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos. Of the 4 camels, which I registered in the past year near the village, I have [sold] in the past year to Dionysios, son of Sotas, from the quarter of the Hawk shrine of Horion, 1 camel, and to Stotoetis, son of Stotoetis, the son of Stotoetis, in the present month, 1 camel, the remaining two camels and the two foals born to their lineage, I register in the [village] for the present 4th year of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, too. (m. 2) 2 camels, 2 foals have been registered at the office of the strategos. Year 4 of Antoninus Caesar, the lord, [Mech]e[i]r [x]. (m. 3?) [2 camels,] 2 foals [have been registered at the office of the royal scribe.] – – –
