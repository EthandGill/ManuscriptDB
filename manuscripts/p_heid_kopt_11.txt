1     … With God, I greet the welfare of your honored godliness in the Lord, and
2     [the] welfare of your whole house. May the Lord Jesus Christ our God keep
3     [you] from every evil … and may he bless you and [your house and]
4     [those] who belong to you. After this, [I] received your letters and read them, [knowing what] you wrote to me, and I grieved [in heart] greatly when you informed [me] [that] …
5     […]…said it… while the Lord [our] God the Almighty [sends his] help to us with you in haste, and may he prepare your … life and your children, concerning the way you said that your …
6     [ … ] upon you, and you do the work upon it, if you know that …
7     [the] matter … from you, so then do not take anything upon you …
8     … and concerning the place where you dwell …
9     [Con]cerning the way you said, "I come southward," you know that [you have not] hired yourself anywhere today, on account of what …
10    the men being naked of sheep and the days …
11    Know that I desire your recommendation, and my heart grieves for you.
12    And concerning the way you said, "I shall find a man and you go to him,"
13    finally I exert my power and inquire about the matter; if I find it, I send to you, while the Lord goes with you and grants his blessing
14    and his peace upon you and your whole house, and may he save you.
15    To the man Boon, farewell, in the power of the holy Trinity. [ ] month Pharmouthi 1.
16    And if you should find rushes or a little flax, buy the small one;
17    give it out. Simply buy a quantity at all; in this way you will get the price for it.
18    To the all most God-beloved, Christ-loving, spiritual child, lord Apa
19    Joul Koui, the most glorious arch… [ ]
