1     To the magistrates,
2     from Dorotheos, one of those of the politeuma.
3     In the 32nd year, when Seuthes, the brother of my wife,
4     had fallen sick at my house
5     and was gravely afflicted, I looked after
6     him for a considerable time out of my own means,
7     spending a great deal; and after this,
8     having learned that his daughter
9     Philippa had been cast off in the …,
10    I sent for her too, and
11    while both of them were living with me,
12    Seuthes … handed over to me Philippa besides, so that she might be with me,
13    both as a favour for what I had spent on both of them, and secondly as a favour for the
14    … toward the woman …,
15    but Jonas, since his wife
16    had drawn Philippa away and
17    had set her over to her other sister
18    at Paanamei, I request,
19    if it seem good, that having summoned
20    her, you compel her to render me
21    what is just. Farewell.
22    Year 36, Pachon 22: we gave orders that notice be served.
23    And on the 24th, of the magistrates: that it be brought forward on the 21st of Payni.
