[TRANSLATION]
1   Lucius Bell[e]nus Gemellus
2   to [Sa]binus his son, greeting.
3   Aunes the donkey-driver has bought
4   a rotten bundle of hay
5   at 12 dr. — a little bundle,
6   rotten hay, the whole of it
7   broken up, like refuse.
8   Sabinus, the (son) of Psellos, the
9   one from Psinachis who was with you,
10   has brought to the city a let-
11   ter of the prefect to Diony-
12   sios the strategos, to hear
13   his case … violence done me(?)
14   … him … until he
15   writes the …(?) of the hay
16   for the sowing. The bank-receipt
17   of the hay — where have you put it?
18   And its loan of the
19   mina — where is the document? Send
20   the key and tell me
21   where it lies, that I may fetch them out,
22   so that if I am going to reckon with him
23   I have them. So do not do
24   otherwise. Take care of yourself.
25   Greet Epagathos and
26   those who love us in
27   truth. Farewell. Choiak 12.
28   For the Saturnalia send
29   ten cocks from the market,
30   and for the birthday of Gemell[a]
31   send fish and …
32   and bread, 1 (artaba of wheat).
33   Send the beasts to cart dung at the vegetable-plot of Psinachis, and the dung-carts, since Pasis is bawling
34   lest it turn to crumbs because of the water; and let them bring hay for it. At once send the
35   beasts.
36   Deliver to Sabinus my son, f(rom) L[ucius]
37   Be[l]lenus Geme[ll]us.
