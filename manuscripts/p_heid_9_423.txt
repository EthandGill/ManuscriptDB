[TRANSLATION]
1     Stotoetis, village-secretary of Tebetnu, to Dionysios, greeting. Of the
2     report given to me by Theophilos the Thracian, of the (troop) of Philotas, the copy is appended below for you
3     so that you may know. Farewell. Year 23, Pachon 9.
4     Write to those whom it concerns and to the king.
5     To Stotoetis, village-secretary of Tebetnu, from Theophilos the Thracian, of the (troop) of Philotas, of (the district) Across-the-river,
6     a private individual. On the 3rd of Pachon of the 23rd year, certain men, coming upon the lodging belonging to me
7     situated in Tebetnu, perceiving that I was in the temple of Zeus together with my wife
8     and the queen. Year 23, Pachon 9.
9     and the slave-girl, and having forced the bolt of the lower door of the stairway
10    of the tower, about the eighth hour, departed having (taken) of my goods the things written below. Therefore I ask
11    that this be made clear about these things to those whom it concerns.
12    Year 23, Pachon 5.
13    a new man's cloak worth 1 (talent), a half-worn tun[ic worth] 1, a half-worn chlamys worth 1,
14    a woman's fringed cloak worth 3, an ear[ring] of four-quarters, of a talent 1600, a necklace worth 1,
15    a head-cloak 4, two bags full of wool [of 1 talent], gold warp-threads 4 (worth) 1,
16    a basin 2, a drinking-cup 2, a cup, a little globe 1 … of …seos the scribe, a cloak
17    to be given out (for appraisal) — the lords will assess it — and a royal (tax) … come to 4 talents 5600.
18    To Symmachos, Stotoetes village-secretary of Tebetnu [has appended for u]s a copy of the report which he received from Theophilos [the Thracian, of the (troop) of Phil]otas, [a private individual, through which] he set forth that certain men, coming upon
19    on the 3rd of Pachon, the lodging belonging to him in the village, departed having
20    the things written below, to (the value of) 4 talents 5600 of bronze. You will do well to give orders that provision be made … in every way
21    so that, being sought out, they may obtain what is fitting …
22    To Kydias the strategos …
23    To the kings.
24    Year 23, Pachon 9. Stotoetis the (secretary) of Tebetnu,
25    village-secretary, concerning booty among the (goods) of Theophilos
26    to (the value of) 4 talents 5600 of bronze.
27    To Dionysios.
