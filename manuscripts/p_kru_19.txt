[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and
2     life-giving, consubstantial Trinity, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit.
3     Written in the month Hathyr 8, of the first indiction.
4     I, Hello the son of the blessed David, the man of the fortress of Djeme, of the nome
5     of the city Hermont, write
6     to my beloved children Mena
7     and his sister, my legitimate children,
8     that, since in this year
9     which we are in, the tenth indiction,
10    God brought a great straitness upon me together with your mother,
11    I sold the house of your blessed mother Sarra
12    to Kosma the son of Ioseph, I received its price from him,
13    that is, the five holokottinoi; we sealed (it)
14    on the street of Proude, according to
15    the way I declared its position
16    of ancient (status) to them in
17    their sale. But now
18    I acknowledge, I, Hello,
19    he who first wrote to you,
20    you, my beloved children
21    Mena and his sister, the whole house
22    which he (it) possessed together with your mother
23    within, the holy Phoibamon,
24    in exchange for what I gave
25    to Kosma the son of Ioseph, this which now I shall declare its position of ancient (status)
26    to you, now, four-square: the south, the Israel(?)
27    of Paulos the brother of my blessed
28    fathers, the north, Martha the widow,
29    and the place where the door of the owners opens, and the public street; the east, Mathousalla
30    the son of the blessed Helias;
31    the west, the public street
32    inward to the whole boundary of the house
33    of that (mother); these are mine, in any place. From now then to you,
34    my beloved children, that you may come
35    in and take hold and become the lord of that whole house, so that I may not be able,
36    neither I nor son nor
37    brother nor sister nor near-kin
38    nor near-kin of near-kin, nor
39    stranger nor man of my house,
40    to bring suit against you concerning that whole house
41    forever; and if
42    he should bring suit against you in a tribunal, or outside a tribunal, whether in a city or
43    in a district, or outside our nomes (or) our districts, and he should move
44    against you at all concerning the whole house
45    which I first declared,
46    from above you shall beget it
47    for yourselves, and you shall re-beget it
48    for yourselves, and you shall administer it,
49    and you shall manage it,
50    and you shall embellish it,
51    and you shall take it back unto (yourselves),
52    and you shall grant it,
53    and you shall make a counter-grant of it,
54    and you shall leave it to your children,
55    and you shall bequeath it to your heirs, simply you shall use it in every manner
56    that is in your (power); for no man
57    shall at all bring suit against you concerning it
58    forever, against your separation.
59    Therefore I have established this written deed
60    of sale for you, firm,
61    valid, and guaranteed in every place where it shall be produced,
62    and they shall question me, and I shall acknowledge, it was read aloud to me, I wrote
63    it, I confirmed it with a subscriber and witness;
64    I have set it forth according
65    to the due order of the laws.
66    The son of the blessed Pisate
67    bears witness. Petros
68    the least presbyter, I have written
69    on his behalf, since he does not know (how) to
70    write, and I bear witness.
71    I, David the son
72    of Karakos, I bear witness.
73    Petros: I have written on his behalf.
74    §m1 Through me, David son of Psate, it was written.
75    And David, who write(s), to Mena and his sis[ter],
76    my children, … of the house of your mother, which I gave to Kos[ma]
77    the son of Ioseph, on the condition that the house is mine,
78    which I possessed together with your mother
79    by the holy Apa Phoibamon, I shall give it …
80    to you, concerning the house which I gave.
