1     Year 2 Athyr [30, drachmas] 20.
2     Year 2 Athyr 30. It fell
3     upon the accounting office in Phibis
4     of the Kytos [district] to Pason the banker and Stotoetes the tester, from Arendotos, [son] of Taembeous
5     from Talae the brewer into the
6     Athyr [of] bronze at [place] 24 [drachmas] twenty, which total 20.
7     [in the presence] of Dorion.
