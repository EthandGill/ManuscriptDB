[TRANSLATION]
1     Panameus and partners, collectors of the apportionment
2     of Charax, (to) Theon (son of) Psenamounis.
3     We have, for the apportionment of the arrears of the tax-farms of year 7,
4     two drachmas 5 obols, total 2 dr. 5 obols. Year 8
5     of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
6     Epeiph 26.
7     (2nd hand) Others, Mesore 13: Kleopas
8     (son of) Bassos, and in the name of Tach( ) (son of) Herakleides
9     and in the name of Herakla[s] [(son of) Bassos],
10    we have, for the apportion[ment].
