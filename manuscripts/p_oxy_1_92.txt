[TRANSLATION]
1   † To the lord Timotheos, agent
2   of the [granaries of] the citizens: Phoibammon the weigh-
3   er(?). [Sup]ply to Theodoros ...
4   [from] the produce of the twelfth indiction,
5   by the cancellus-measure, two and one-sixth artabas of grain only,
6   grain 2 1/6 art. by the cancellus, Mesore 1,
7   12th indiction. †
8   From Eutrygios to Dioskoros the assistant, greeting:
9   give to Gorgonios the water-organ player, by order, of grain
10   two artabas.
11   Year 38=7, Tybi 21. I have signed.
12   † John, comes, to Phoibammon, [wine-]steward:
13   supply to the men from Sepho who brought (it), 2 double-(measures of) cakes; to Didymos the carpenter of Alexandria, 1 double; and to the men from Kesmouchis who brought (it),
14   2 double-(measures of) cakes; to Megas of the stationarii, 1 double; to the fishermen of Kesmouchis, through Kaleonistos, 1 double; to the doorkeeper of (the church of) Saint
15   John, through Phib the attendant, 1 double; to the villagers of (the shrine of) the Martyr, through Phib the attendant, 1 double; to the field-guards of the great
16   constabulary who guard the opposite bank, 6 doubles; to the village workmen, 2 doubles; total of the whole ordered: eighteen doubles.
17   Year 180=149, Choiak 22=12th indiction. Total of the whole: 18 doubles only. † I have signed: eighteen doubles of wine, total 18 doubles of wine, for Phoibammon the wine-steward.
18   In the month Choiak 22, 12th indiction.
19   From Aphthonios to Ophellios Ochis(?), greeting:
20   supply, for the service of the landlord's household, ten jars of new wine, and
21   to Amethystos the horse-doctor, by order, one jar of new wine; total 11 jars.
22   Year 31=21=13=4=2(?), Phaophi 18.
