[TRANSLATION]
1     of the dry land; neighbours: on the south [reckoned-out land] formerly [belonging to Sen]eca, on the north the mound, on the west
2     [and] on the east the boundary of the same dry land,
3     near Ares village of Patre Upper.
4     In the 9th year …
5     To Apion, royal scribe of the Hermopolite nome,
6     from Ploutas [son of] Demetrios and Sokrates [son of] Apollonios
7     and Herodes the elder, son of Didymos, and Achilleus son of Panther, those
8     appointed by you for the inspection
9     of the locality of Patre Upper, [of the] reckoned-out dry land,
10    the matters having been jointly inspected with us by those under
11    …-nos, strategos of the same nome, those of
12    [Pa]tre Upper Hermaios [son of] … and of Patre Lower
13    [the] public inspector Anoubion
14    [son of] Theon following along, and the
15    [village] village-scribes
16    marking the boundaries in accordance with the
17    … which … from the village-scribes themselves
18    of the reports;
19    the land of Isasos that had been ceded
20    [for] the resumption of dry land, 112 (arouras):
21    [in 2 plots], of the 1st plot arouras 32, likewise neighbours: on the south the mound,
22    [on the no]rth the reckoned-out land, on the east formerly belonging to Seneca,
23    [on the west] the boundary of the dry land;
24    [of the 2nd] plot arouras 80, likewise neighbours: on the south the reckoned-out land
25    formerly belonging to Seneca, on the north the mound, on the west and on the east
26    [the] boundary of the dry land.
