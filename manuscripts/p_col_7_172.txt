[TRANSLATION]
1     … to the most eminent prefect,
2     … greeting.
3     [From Aur]elius Val[erius] antiourios, from the village of Kar[anis]
4     [of the Arsi]noite nome. I and my brothers pay tax
5     [on behalf of ar]ouras eighty, of which half is sown,
6     [but] the remaining half part is uncultivated.
7     Since … the land being remeasured, the surveyor of the
8     places, Palemon, out of malice, all the
9     arouras submitted as sowable, and completely (what were) moderate (lands) …
10    … since, then, there is also an irrigation channel through which
11    our small plots are watered, the same surveyor
12    Palemon submitted them in the category of sowable
13    land, although everyone is watering from it and not even the
14    … of those sown; I beseech your Excellency,
15    since, when Porphyrios the geometer also did the remeasuring, he acted justly and registered the sowable
16    as sowable, but the dry sand-covered land
17    he registered as [dry] land, whereas Palemon the surveyor
18    submitted all the arouras as sowable,
19    and since Tapaeis daughter of Heras, my kinswoman, made use of evasion,
20    possessing twenty-three arouras which, being the finest, they referred up to her, having u … des two … sia …
