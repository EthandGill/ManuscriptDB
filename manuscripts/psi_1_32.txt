[TRANSLATION]
1     Pneperós [son] of Hatrés, mother Tanaraútos, and Thenammenus [son] of Pareítios, mother Thenbotátos, the two from the village of Ankyrónon, [and] Naáti [son] of Pareítios, mother …
2     … from the same [place], greetings. We have leased to you for a period of four years from the present 16th year of Severus and Antoninus and Geta
3     Caesars, the lords, [the land] existing to us
4     around the same Ankyrónon from the south
5     of the village, previously of our parents,
6     a quarter-aroura, on condition that you work this [land] with all [labor] and care blamelessly in place of the rent
7     [of the] four-year period, and after the four-year time [you are to have half of the] remaining [products] from it
8     [of all products for an]other four-year period, and we [the landowners are to] have the remaining half, the
9     [all expen]ses being incurred by you
10    of [Na]ás; and it is not permitted to us to dismiss you
11    from the [lea]se, nor must you, Naás, abandon the lease, all the public charges of the land being owed by us the landowners.
12    The lease is valid.
13    Year 16 of the emperors Caesars Lucius Septimius
14    Severus pious Pertinax Arabian Adiabene[n]
15    Parthian greatest and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
16    pious and Publius Septimius Geta Caesar
17    Augustuses, Phamenoth 28. Thenammenus
18    I have leased as is set forth. Acting for Horion
19    I wrote [on behalf of] her letters, she not knowing [letters].
