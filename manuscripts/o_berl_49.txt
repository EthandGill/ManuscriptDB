[TRANSLATION]
1     Praxous of Petechontos, donkey-driver
2     of the road of Philon, to Serapammon, greetings.
3     I have received from you from the completion
4     of the hundred drachmas drachmas forty-eight, and it is
5     my own handwriting.
6     Year 2, Phaophi 6.
