[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelia Herakleidiaine, matrona stolata, daughter
2     of Herakleides, former councillor of the most illustrious
3     city of the Alexandrians,
4     from the Aurelii Melas son of Melas, his mother being Athenarous, and Zoilion son of Dionysios, his mother being Sarapias,
5     both from the city of Oxyrhynchos. We willingly undertake to lease, for only the present
6     14th year, from the (lands) belonging (to you) around Antipera Pela,
7     out of the allotment (kleros) of Dionysios son of Soleus, of twenty-six arouras,
8     and out of the allotment of Diokles, of ten arouras, an eighth, a thirty-second, being under one view (i.e. in a single plot), the (arouras)
9     from the hill-land, north-west toward the east, twenty arouras
10    or however many there may be, more or less,
11    for the laying-down (sowing) of flax-stalk, at a rent per aroura
12    according to survey of three hundred silver drachmas
13    and, for the whole, of pure soft tow (styppeion)
14    of the weight of one talent and a half, on condition that we,
15    the lessees, may have, free of rent, for
16    the soaking of the flax-stalk which you have around the same Antipera Pela, near the cistern
17    of Diogenis, the pool that is inside the whirlpool (charybdis)
18    of Pasineikos, from the month Pachon. The rent is to be free
19    of all risk, the public taxes on the land being your responsibility, the landowner, you being mistress of the crops until you recover what is owed; and the undertaking being guaranteed to us,
20    we shall pay the rent in these instalments: on Tybi 10, one thousand drachmas, and from Pachon until
21    Mesore, and (in) Mesore itself, of the present 14th year, of the months, each, and likewise (on the) 10th
22    the share falling to them (?), a fourth part, and the tow
23    in the same month Mesore, satisfactory (and) full by weight, the right of execution being yours against us, by mutual surety, and from all our property.
24    The undertaking is valid, and on formal question we have given our assent. Year 14 of the Emperor Caesar Publius Licinius
25    Gallienus, Germanicus Maximus, Persicus Maximus,
26    Pius, Fortunate, Augustus, Thoth 1 …
27    I, Aurelia Herakleidiaina, have received
28    a copy of this.
