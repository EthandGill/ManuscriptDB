1     With good fortune.
2     For gains, Hermes, Aphrodite.
3     For good.
4     [C]opy of a memorandum (of proceedings).
5     … [of the] division of Polemon …
6     before Andromachos, strategos of the Arsinoite, division of Polemon; year 11, Tybi …
7     from a report (laid before) Flavius Titianus, the most excellent prefect:
8     of Demetrios, freedman of Herakleides, against Paulinus
9     son of Patron, of the ex-gymnasiarchs. Ammonios,
10    advocate, on behalf of Demetrios: through which (report) his client submitted two charges that he laid in the report against the opponent, the one concerning a trust of building-plots, the other
11    concerning two thousand drachmas, which he deposited with Geminus,
12    brother of Paulinus. So the matter concerning the building-plots is for the present postponed, but the two thousand drachmas
13    of Geminus, who is deceased, he claims from Paulinus,
14    who is administering the property left by that man, to recover. How Geminus came to have the money I will state. The client, being a member of his household, and sometimes also his steward, having deposited with him the two thousand drachmas,
15    had the documents of the deposit drawn up in the name of a friend
16    of his own, a certain Atrenos. So he claims, producing
17    the documents of Geminus made out to Atrenos, to recover the money
18    … (illegible) … Demetrios submitted
19    a report. Palamedes, advocate, on behalf of Paulinus:
20    the client could indeed have parried the question concerning the two thousand
21    drachmas, for he is not the guardian
22    of his brother's sons. But in order that you may recognize the opponent's
23    malpractice, he is ready to … to make defense to both the charges in the
24    report. Now the first charge, as he himself stated, is as follows: that before the former strategos
25    Claudius Dionysios there had been a judgment against Paulinus concerning
26    a trust of building-plots, he has alleged, and he added that judgment was given that Paulinus should restore to him the trust. But now, knowing that he will be condemned, he declines the trial concerning these matters.
27    For Paulinus affirms that no judgment at all was given against him
28    before Dionysios concerning a trust of building-plots, but against other persons. Demetrios: concerning [this I do not now wish to speak.]
29    Palamedes: through the report he laid the charge; if he does not wish to speak about this, this very point at least must be answered to me. Was judgment given
30    against Paulinus concerning building-plots? The strategos to Demetrios: what (judgment was given) against
31    Paulinus? Demetrios: against Paulinus I was not given judgment
32    concerning the building-plots; but, as I said before, I do not now speak about this. But
33    the money I claim to recover. Palamedes: already in the
34    first (charge) Demetrios admitted that he had misled the most excellent
35    prefect, but also in the matter concerning the money still more
36    will you condemn him. And it is necessary also concerning the persons to say this much in advance, that Geminus was a most respectable man, but the opponent is destitute,
37    having no resource to point to from which the money
38    he could have had. And the fact that he himself has admitted
39    that he was a member of the household, and sometimes also a steward, of
40    Geminus, especially … bears upon the present
41    question; for from this he is proved to be one serving for wages, in want of necessities, and he is suspect
42    lest perhaps, frequenting our man's house, he stole
43    the bond, which, while that man was alive and able to
44    teach more exactly how it was lost, he did not bring forward.
45    But when that man died he goes after the money. To these I add the greatest of all and that which fully convicts the wrongdoing of Demetrios; for I say that he does not even
46    know the Atrenos, in whose name he said the bond was drawn up,
47    nor can he answer who he is or from where …
