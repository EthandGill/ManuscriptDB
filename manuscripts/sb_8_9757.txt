r.5    ἀπὸ καταβολῆς μηνὸς Παχὼν θ ἰνδικτίονος ια διὰ Γεωργίου ὑποδιοικητοῦ Πέτρου πολιτευομένου
r.6    ἀρίθμια νομίσματα κε, εἰκοσιπέντε μόνα.

[TRANSLATION]
1     This is the [amount] of gold deposited for public gold-tax of the ninth indiction of the village of Magdal[on]
2     from the payment [made] in the month Pachon of the ninth indiction, the eleventh, through George, sub-administrator of Peter, the administrator
3     numbered coins twenty-five, twenty-five only
4     This is the [amount] of gold deposited for public gold-tax of the … indiction of the village of Magdolon
5     from the payment [made] in the month Pachon of the ninth indiction, the eleventh, through George, sub-administrator of Peter, the administrator
6     numbered coins twenty-five, twenty-five only.
