1     [Year] 10 of Trajan Hadrian Caesar the lord
2     month Caesareum 29 in Karanidi.
3     [Tapethes daughter of] Pasoknopaeus
4     of Didymion [acknowledges] to Sambathius
5     [daughter of] Herates with [her] lord husband Pekmetus [son of] Phaesis [a] interest-bearing loan of silver drachmas 372 and instead of the
6     [interest] of these [she] agrees to profit from
7     the Sambathian [field] near Psenarpsene
8     [an] olive-grove of 4 arouras until which [she] repays the
9     aforesaid principal.
10    [And] through the earlier written [contract] which was made
11    by Komonos [son of] Pasoknopaeus to Tapethes
12    [daughter of] Pasoknopaeus year 7 [of] Trajan Hadrian
13    Caesar the lord Tybi 4.
