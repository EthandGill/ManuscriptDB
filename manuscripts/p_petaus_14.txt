[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonius, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2     from Petaus, village-scribe of Syron and
3     other villages.
4     With reference to the promise given to you by
5     Tasokmetis daughter of Pathynis, with her guardian,
6     her husband Pholemis son of Sokmetis, from the village of
7     Syron, wishing to purchase from those put up for sale
8     held over by the administration, formerly of Pathynis the younger
9     son of Sokmas grandson of Marreis, in the village, a third part
10    of a house — in return for which a whole new house — and another half share
11    of another house and courtyard in a second place, of which house
12    the neighbours are: to the south an entrance and exit; to the north an entrance and exit; to the east
13    the house of Pathynis son of Pathynis and partners;
14    to the west the house of Tasokmenis daughter of Pathynis; and
15    of the half share of the house and courtyard the neighbours of the whole are:
16    to the south the house of Pathynis son of Melanchoras and partners;
17    to the north the courtyard of Tompsais and the house of Pathynis;
18    to the east the house of Sokmenis son of Pathynis;
19    to the west the avenue of the temple of Sokmetis the great god; and,
20    formerly of Pathynis son of Sokmetis, who became
21    grain-collector, around the village, of a holding of 5½ arouras,
22    total 5½ arouras, neighbours to the south Soeris son of Sokmetis and
23    partners, corn-lands; to the north the corn-lands of Kiales;
24    to the east a travelled dike; to the west of Sokmas son of Socrates
25    and partners, public land; the price of the
26    houses being 200 silver drachmas and of the corn-lands likewise 200 drachmas,
27    the total of the ratification being 400 drachmas, and of the accompanying charges;
28    I declare, considered through inspection of assessment,
29    entirely ownerless and unsold, not worth being put up at auction above the 400 drachmas,
30    from which nothing accrues to the current
31    five-year period, because they are unproductive.
32    Year 25 of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
33    Antoninus Caesar the lord.
