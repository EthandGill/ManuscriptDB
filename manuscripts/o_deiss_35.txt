[TRANSLATION]
1     Sailes son of Hermius has inscribed for the embankment-tax
2     [of the] 2nd year, drachmas 6 tetrobol; and for the bathhouse-tax tetrobol half-obol; totaling drachmas 6 diobol half-obol,
3     additionally registered diobol. [Of the] 3rd year of Tiberius Claudius
4     Caesar Augustus Germanicus
5     Autocrator New Augustus [month] 13, Kephalos
6     Petemenophes Pikotis.
