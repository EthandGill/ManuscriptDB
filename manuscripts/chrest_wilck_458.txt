[TRANSLATION]
1     From a roll of examinations made by Gaius Avidius Heliodorus, former prefect, of which the heading is: the undersigned
2     veterans who served in cavalry squadrons and in cohorts and in two fleets, the Misenate and the Syrian,
3     having obtained, together with their children and descendants, the Roman citizenship and the right of intermarriage with the women
4     whom they then had when the citizenship was given to them, or, if any were unmarried, with those whom they might marry afterwards,
5     each up to one (wife); and further, other veterans, those without bronze (tablets), and other veterans
6     who themselves also obtained the Roman citizenship alone; likewise also Romans and freedmen and slaves and others appeared for examination before Gaius Avidius Heliodorus,
7     prefect of Egypt, from the 15th day before the Kalends of March, which is Mecheir 21, until the 17th (day before)
8     the Kalends of June, which is the 21st of the month [Pachon] of the present 3rd year of Imperator
9     [Caesar] Titus Aelius [Hadrianus] Antoninus Augustus Pius. And the title-deeds which they presented
10    … to …ion, [tribune] of the legion II Trajana Fortis, lie beside each name.
11    After other matters, of column 8 … wishing to reside temporarily for a season
12    [in the Arsinoite nome] … 9 …
13    A copy of the examination …
14    of a veteran and …
15    of his daughter
