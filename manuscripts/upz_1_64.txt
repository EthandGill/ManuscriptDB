r.4    τὰ κατὰ τοὺς ἀλάστορας τοὺς διηλμένους ἐκ τῆς φυλακῆς, προσδιεσάφεις δὲ καὶ τὸν ἀδελφόν σου Σαραπίωνα ἀδικεῖσθαι ὑπὸ Ἁ[ρ]παήσιος
r.5    φυλακίτου. μεταπεμψάμενος οὖν αὐτὸν καὶ τὸν φυλακίτην
r.6    ἐπετίμων αὐτῶι καὶ ὁ ἀδελφός σου ἀνθωμολογεῖτο μὴ ἠδικῆσθαι ὑπʼ αὐτοῦ, καὶ παρεκάλεσα αὐτὸν ἔρχεσθαι, περὶ ὧν ἂν βούληται. χαριεῖ δὲ
r.7    συμπαραστὰς ἡμῖν ἐν τοῖς λοιποῖς καὶ παρατηρήσας τοὺς ἀλάστορας,

r.11   ἔσει μεγάλως κεχαρισμένος. χαρίζοι[ο] δʼ ἂν καὶ τοῦ σώματος

[TRANSLATION]
1     Barcaeus [the] and Ammonius to Apollonius the brother, greetings.
2     If you are in good health and other things are according to reason, the proper thing would be, and we ourselves
3     are in good health. We owe you very great thanks for having shown us
4     the matters concerning the fugitives who have escaped from the guard, and you have further clarified to us that your brother Sarapion is being wronged by Ha[r]paesis,
5     the guard. Having sent for him and the guard,
6     I rebuked him, and your brother acknowledged not having been wronged by him, and I exhorted him to come concerning whatever he might wish. It will be gracious if
7     standing beside us in the rest and watching the fugitives,
8     and if they dare and come down outside the asylum, inform me,
9     so that having arrived, being with you, I might accomplish something, and for you
10    there is a crown of bronze of three talents, as if you being concerned with a private matter, and for us
11    you will be greatly obliged. If you would [grant] also concerning your body's
12    attending to it, so that you might be in good health. Farewell. Year 26, Phaophi 4.
13    To Apollonius.
