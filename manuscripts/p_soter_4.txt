[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sambas son of Mysthes,
2     from Soterichos son of Lykos, a Persian of
3     the epigone. I wish to lease from you, as to the half share, joint and undivided,
4     of the fruit-produce of the palm-grove belonging to you near Theadelphia, of one and a half arouras or however many there may be, for six years, six crops,
5     from those of the present seventh year of Imperator
6     Caesar Domitian Augustus Germanicus that are to come,
7     the crops falling due in alternate years; and the ground for the same six years from the
8     present seventh year for the sowing of whatever
9     we may choose except wheat and safflower,
10    the rent for the whole of the half falling
11    to me being per year fifty
12    drachmas of silver, and as extra payments per year an artaba-basket and another (basket) of rubbed-off white-leaf ware, five-plaited, of the removable kind,
13    you Sambas having per year, over the whole
14    palm-grove, of the fruit-bearing palms,
15    by selection, four (palms), and out of the common (share) of the
16    whole palm-produce likewise
17    per year of dry dates, by selection, two artabas by the dromos-measure; and
18    I, Soterichos, alone shall perform
19    the clearings of the whole palm-grove
20    and the trenchings and the pruning-downs and the fork-cleanings, and both of us, I Soterichos
21    and you Sabinus, (shall perform) for the ground per year the
22    under-diggings and the cuttings and per month three waterings by the bucket-machine, and
23    we shall give for the raising (of the banks) of the reservoir
24    each of us per year two workmen. And the
25    rent I shall repay in two equal
26    installments in alternate years, in Phaophi and in the month of Neos
27    Sebastos, on which (terms) both of us likewise, I and
28    you … …
29    … of the sediment in the palm-grove being cleared …
30    within the enclosure, the part contained where
31    you have a spade and … …
32    you shall have around … cord …
33    … of the cleaning of the palm-grove,
34    the whole, both the palm-branches and the leaves and
35    the palm-fibre being mine, Soterichos', out of which
36    you shall have for your own burning … if it seem good
37    to lease (it) to me on these [terms] …
