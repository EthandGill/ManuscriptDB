[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy life-giving consubstantial Trinity, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. In the month Phamenoth 12, of the 2nd indiction.
2     With God. I, Theodoros the priest, son of Aaron, write on this day, forever, every time, to you, you Petros the superior of holy Apa Phoibamon of the mountain of Djeme, while the lord Chael son of Psmo is dioiketes of the fortress of Djeme, through the will of God the Almighty. I, Theodoros, write on this day, donating Sonchem my son,
3     whom I begot of my members with his mother Maria; I donate him and I give him as a votive offering into the holy place of holy Apa Phoibamon of the mountain of Djeme, all the days of his life, so that he shall be
4     a slave of his master all the days of his life, being a servant to it, and that he become one giving his labor to it
5     all the days of his life; because God brought a sickness upon him according to his will from his childhood,
6     I took him up, I brought him to the place of holy Apa Phoibamon, I left him there, entreating for him; he entreated God,
7     and he bestowed his welfare upon him. I spoke in my own conscience, with his mother Maria, that we should donate him as a gift into the holy place of holy Apa Phoibamon, so that he should become a slave to it forever,
8     so that he shall give his whole labor toward which God shall appoint him, into the holy martyr-shrine; and
9     any man who shall dare to bring suit, or to come forth against this place, bringing suit concerning Sonchem my son, or who shall dare at all
10    against this affair of this kind, before an authority, or in a village, or in a city, or in a praetorium, or outside a praetorium—first, may that man profit nothing, but he shall become a stranger to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, and he shall receive the portion of Judas,
11    and he shall receive the portion of Ananias with Sapphira his wife; and the God of holy Apa Phoibamon the martyr
12    shall take vengeance on him, because he dared at all (and) opposed the holy place. For the securing then, I, Theodo(ros), with Maria his wife,
13    have established this deed of donation for you, you most reverend Petros, the superior of holy Apa Phoibamon; I gave a witness
14    to it, and we assent to it. I, Papas son of [Ste]phanos, am witness. I, Ymai son of Zael, am witness.
15    I, Paule son of Kanah, of Romoou, am witness. I, Souai son of Georgios, of Timeshor, am witness.
16    Through me, Eiob Alex(ander), it was written.
