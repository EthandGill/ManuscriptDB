r.2    [ὑποδεκτῶν ] … καὶ Ὀλυμπίου λαμπροτάτου Στέφανος καὶ Ἀδέλφιος ὀπτίον[ες τοῦ ἐνταῦθα ἐν]

r.5    [τῆς Ἀρκάδων] ἐπαρχίας ὑπὲρ πρώτης καὶ δευτέρας καὶ τρίτης τετραμήνο[υ τῆς ἑνδεκάτης]

r.8    [ὑπογραφῆς τοῦ προειρη]μένου ὀπτίονος ἐν μηνὶ Φαρμοῦθι ἰνδικτίονος εὐτ[υχοῦς δεκάτης].

r.11   [τοῦ ] … ιάθιος ἔγραψα ὑπὲρ αὐτοῦ παρόντος γράμματα μὴ εἰδότος.

[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the most illustrious Flavius Al]upios, son of Heron, overseer of the city of Herakles, [10th] indiction, through
2     [those receiving] … and Olympios the most illustrious, Stephanos and Adelphios, optiones [of those here in]
3     [the passage that were] numbered among the most noble and sanctified Leonto[klibanarii, greetings].
4     [We have received as an account] [foster-children to] our contubernales according to the order [of the most illustrious commander]
5     [of the Arkades] province for the first and second and third four-month [period of the eleventh]
6     [indiction], grain, artabae 2,370, two thousand three hundred seventy
7     [and for your safety] and [of] the public account I have made for you this single copy [with]
8     [signature of the afore]said optio in the month of Pharmouthi of the fortunate [tenth] indiction.
9     And we pray [that] you be well [for] many [times/years].
10    [I, Flavius Adelphios], this single copy agrees with me, as has been written before. Flavius Phoei[bammon, son]
11    [of] … iathios wrote on his behalf while present, not knowing letters.
