[TRANSLATION]
1     Memorandum to Athe[na]goras
2     from Theodor[us the architect]
3     of the Arsino[ite nome].
4     From when I have been assigned [to the wo]rks
5     architectural neither wages/salary
6     has been given to us neither grain-measure from year 36, but [those] serving us do not remain unless [we] su[pply]
7     them. In order therefore not to be aban[don]ed by them and through
8     this of the works something be delayed, well you will do
9     writing to give to us the wages and the grain-measure.
10    We will provide the supplies without blame to both the administrator and
11    you. But it was given to Kleon in Alexandria [hegemonic wages] drachmae 300 and grain
12    in accordance, but in the Arsinoite
13    for servants drachmae 300 of wheat artabae 12½
14    of barley artabae 25.
15    Good fortune.
