[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the n]ame of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, of the holy and consubstantial Trinity.
2     [In the month] of Choiak, on the new moon, of the first indiction, in Hermopolis of the Thebaid.
3     [Aurelius] Isakos, son of Phoibammon, by his mother Thaesia, a farmer from the hamlet
4     called Psahbeou, of the fields of the village of Tertembythis of the Hermopolite nome,
5     to Flavius Theodoros, the most splendid son of Magisteros of splendid memory, from
6     the (city) of the Hermopolites, greeting. I acknowledge that I have had and received from you,
7     by way of advance, for the irrigation of your estate called Pmag Kas, of the fields
8     of the same village of Tertembythis of the Hermopolite nome, six gold solidi
9     at twenty-three carats to the solidus, by the standard of Alexandria, making six gold solidi at 23 carats,
10    which I acknowledge I will provide to you, being about to withdraw from the farming of this estate,
11    in gold as I received from you, without any postponement and
12    dispute and judgment and lawsuit, at my own risk and from the resource of my entire
13    property. The present bond is valid and secure. And, having been asked the formal question,
14    I gave my acknowledgment. I, Aurelius Isakos, son of Phoibammon, the aforesaid, have made this bond
15    as is stated above. I, Aurelius Georgios, son of the late Hermaion, from Hermopolis, having been requested,
16    wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters. I, Theodoros, least deacon, son of Taurinos, from Hermopolis,
17    bear witness to the bond, having heard from the one making it. I, Victor son of Justus, from Hermopolis, bear witness
18    to the present bond, having been requested by the one making it.
19    Written through me, Leontios, with God, the contract-notary.
