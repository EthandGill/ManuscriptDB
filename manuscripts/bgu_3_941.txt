[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Valens for the 5th time and
2     Valentinian the younger, the eternal Augusti,
3     Hathyr [..], in Herakleopolis [...]
4     Aurelius ...yses (son of) Kastalios, his mother being Sar[apias(?)], dwel-
5     ling in Herakleopolis, a wool-worker (lanarius), to A[urelius ...]
6     (son of) Apollonios, from the same ci[ty, a crafts-]
7     man ... [greeting.]
8     Since before this I deposited with you [...]
9     two genuine plain gold coins, total
10    2 coins, and these you furnished to the [...]
11    Apollonios at my order, of which also
12    I received a written note from him, accordingly
13    I acknowledge from now on to have no claim
14    against you, nor to proceed against you nor to sue (you)
15    on the pretext of these, and that this
16    quittance shall be for your security. (verso)
17    Quittance(?) to(?) the account of Kastalios the wool-worker (lanarius).
