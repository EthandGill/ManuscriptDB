[TRANSLATION]
1     Philammon to Pate, [son] of Tsenutos,
2     and to Pachrates and those with them,
3     soldiers, greetings and health.
4     Since the father has written [us] to associate together,
5     leading the Crocodilopolis [garrison] and
6     you together, you will do well and
7     [act] gratefully, [being] prepared
8     as together with us you might march out,
9     But if you do not have a ship, embark together with us into the Roman [settlement]
10    and having care for yourselves in order that you be healthy,
11    Fare well. Year 16, Choiach 28.
12    To the commanders
13    Pate and
14    Pachrates
