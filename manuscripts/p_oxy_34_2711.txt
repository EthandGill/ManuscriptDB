[TRANSLATION]
1     To Statilius Ammianus, the most distinguished prefect of Egypt,
2     from Aurelius Horion Aniketos [having] been agonothete, gymnasiarch, councillor of the illustrious city of Oxyrhynchus; [preserving the] naturally owed goodwill, most illustrious [ruler,] [toward] [the] sons of my cousin Aniketos also called Remmius, Aureliuses A... and Remmion
3     and Aurelia Eudaimonida, very much [destitute] [abandoned]
4     I make the petition in which [might become] [united with] the [most necessary matter] occurring, and
5     your fortune; for according to the 14th year of the divine Galienus' [reign], of their father by fortune
6     [having relied on], I still remained upon myself like [the mother] [of them], Aurelia
7     Thaes[i]us in widowhood [being], and since [now] …
