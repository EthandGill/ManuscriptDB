[TRANSLATION]
1     Ailurion and Horos, tax collectors of silver
2     [To] Charakos, [son] of Pankametes, [grandson of] Panameus, Pankametios.
3     we have received for poll tax and bathing tax, year 2, bronze
4     drachmas four, which total drachmas 4.
5     Year 2 of Antoninus Caesar
6     of the lord, Pharmouthi 28.
7     Ailurion, through Kaleos, assistant, [I] have signed, drachmas 4.
