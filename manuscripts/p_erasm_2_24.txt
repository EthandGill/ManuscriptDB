1     [from the]
2     concerning the Oxyrhynchus workshop [of wheat artabas thousand three-hun]dred,
3     there are wheat [artabas 100] and token and counter-token
4     to make toward them as is fitting and make entries if
5     the commander of the guard shall mount.
6     Farewell. Year 29, Pauni 23.
7     Theodore: make entries according to
8     what lies before: wheat artabas thousand three-hundred,
9     there are wheat artabas 100.
10    [Year] [29] Pauni 23.
11    artabas 300
12    artabas 200 there are 100
13    of pure wheat [800]
