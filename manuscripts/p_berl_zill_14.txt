[TRANSLATION]
1     I have now received the honored letter of your fatherly and holy lordship and
2     of your honored and genuine brotherly love-of-God, and I was filled with joy on learning
3     from it of your condition in the Lord and the health of all the brethren, … our concern. And each day I await the most holy abba
4     Peter the bishop … those from the city of the Oxyrhynchites and
5     with them, when he comes, if I have a measure of life … from …
6     to him … and through Komas and Pacheus I sent …
7     for this, that they might be sent to you from the (things) there … therefore
8     to receive them, until I am deemed worthy to venerate your most holy feet;
9     but I beseech you to pray for my humility, that
10    the Lord God may prosper my ways and may move the common
11    master to release me with love and not … the rest
12    to hinder them; for he seeks this one thing, and if I have
13    a measure of life I cannot but go out, but with love I wish
14    that, after so many years which I served him, I may not grieve him,
15    but I have hopes in God and in your prayers, that God will satisfy him and he will release me with goodwill. And very much in the Lord
16    I venerate and embrace genuinely and happily the
17    holiness of my master, and you, the most God-beloved and my genuine brother Phoibammon, … and this they do, my most holy
18    brother the syncellus, and all from me … let him deem worthy the most God-fearing abba Marinus and the other presbyters by name, and the most God-beloved deacons, and the brother
19    Kiamoul and all the brethren by name. And may our lord master
20    deem me worthy soon to embrace you all. The most God-beloved
21    brother Menas with me venerates your footprints … the brethren,
22    that … some gift from my most God-beloved brother,
23    … of the house of the mother … quickly … white …
24    … I bring it out, coming, if I live.
