[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Atias with God dux through Menas the notary to you Senuthius
2     of Dioscorus concerning Qusi Apollo; these are the things owed to be assessed
3     by you lands concerning produce … of indiction Zacharias Psatiou three arouras
4     alone of the lot Psanakelelel three arouras.
5     total for Qusi Apollo three arouras, three.
