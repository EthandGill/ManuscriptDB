[TRANSLATION]
1   Peteuris son of Peteuris, linen-seller, paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the seventh year of Trajan
3   Caesar the lord: seventeen silver drach-
4   mas — total 17 dr., and for the statue, three obols.
5   I, Hermogenes son of Ammonas, the collector,
6   wrote (this). Year 8 of Trajan
7   the lord, Hathyr 2.
