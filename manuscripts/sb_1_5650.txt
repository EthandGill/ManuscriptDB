r.3    ἀπὸ μοναστηρίου ἁγίας Μαρ[ίας κώ]μ[ης Ἀφ]ρ[οδ]ι[τώ. ἔ]λ[αχεν ὑ]μ[ῖν ὑπὲρ δημοσίων]

r.5    [καὶ] ὑπὲρ ἐμβολῆς σίτου ἀρτάβας πη, ὀγδοήκοντα ὀκτὼ μόνα. ἐγράφη μηνὸς Θώθ,

[TRANSLATION]
1     [of the mon]astery of the holy Mary numismata 98, [of] wheat artabae 88.
2     In the name of God. Corra son of Szerich counselor to you
3     from the monastery of the holy Mary [of the village of Aphr]odito. [He has received for you for public [taxes]]
4     of indiction 6, according to Arabs year 88, numismata 98, ninety-eight,
5     [and] for the augmentation of wheat artabae 88, eighty-eight only. [It] was written in the month of Thoth,
6     of [the] eighth indiction.
7     [it] amounts to numismata 98, [of] wheat artabae 88.
