[TRANSLATION]
1     Amyntas to Criton greetings. Charminos, who is delivering the letter to you, is Heracles[id-]
2     son of the one from the sacred prōireus, and he has been sent both by the father and by the others [who have]
3     the gift-benefice holders, accompanying [him]. You would oblige me by both being zealous that he obtain [it] as quickly as possible and if [a ship moors] putting him on board; for he is inexperienced; but if not, at least taking the sealed packet from him
4     sealed and sending [it] to us, so that we may give [it] back to them.
5     Farewell.
6     About Charminos from Amyntas. To Criton.
7     Year 28, Peritius 5,
8     in the harbor of Berenice.
