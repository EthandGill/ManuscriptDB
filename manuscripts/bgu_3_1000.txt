[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemaios surnamed Alexandros and Berenike his sister, the Mother-loving gods,
2     year 16, the priests and priestesses and the kanephoros being those in office, in the month Mesore 5, at Pathyris, before Hermias
3     the deputy of Paniskos the agoranomos. A sale. There sold Panobchounis, son of Paous, a Persian of the epigone, about 60 years old,
4     middle (height), honey-skinned, with straight hair, bald-fronted, long-faced, straight-nosed, from the land belonging to him and his brothers,
5     grain-bearing dry-land, undivided, in the plain north of Pathyris, the seventh share falling to him; the neighbours
6     of the whole land: to the south [the land] of Patous, son of Psemmonthes; to the north the land of Phamounis; to the east a hollow canal called Pla…
7     of Pathyris; to the west the mountain (desert), or whoever the neighbours may be all around. There bought Eunous, son of Asklepiades, a Persian, about 45 years old, middle (height), honey-skinned,
8     with straight hair, [long]-faced, straight-nosed, for two thousand and two hundred bronze drachmas. The fore-seller and guarantor of all matters concerning this [s]ale (is) Panobchounis the seller, whom Eunous the buyer accepted.
9     I, Hermias, the deputy of Paniskos, have transacted (this).
10    Year 16, Mesore 30. There has been paid to the bank in Krokodilon Polis at which
11    Pankrates (is banker), of the tenth (tax), the enkyklion (sales-tax), according to the assessment of Apollonios the tax-farmer, by Eunous,
12    son of Asklepiades, for a seventh of the arouras of land, of which the
13    boundaries have been shown through the aforesaid contract,
14    which he bought from Panebchounis, son of Paous, for 2,2[00] bronze drachmas;
15    tax 300, total 300.
16    Pankrates.
