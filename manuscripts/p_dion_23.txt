[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 10, in the month of Pe[rit]ios 2, Choiach 2,
2     in Hermopolis of the The[b]aid. There lent
3     Adymos son of Ptolemaio[s], a Macedonian of the
4     cleruchs enrolled under Cleopatra,
5     to Dionysios son of Kephalas, a Persian of the epigone, and to his mother Sarapias
6     daughter of Heliodoros, a Persian woman, with her guardian, her own
7     son Dionysios, the aforesaid, of solid wheat [artabas] 150, which have been measured out to them by him, at interest of half-as-much-again,
8     in wheat artab[as 75; and the] whe[at and] the
9     inter[est, the whole of solid wheat 225 artabas, let the borrowers pay back to Adymos]
10    or to tho[se from him in the month of Loios]
11    which is Pa[uni of the same year, wheat]
12    ne[w] clea[n unadulterated, by the measure that goes]
13    to the bronze (standard), and [let them deli]ver [it]
14    to the harbor by Akor[is], at their
15    own expenses; and if [they do n]ot pay back
16    according to [what] is written, let the
17    borrowers pay to Adymos or to those from
18    him as the price of each artaba of bronze 3,000 drachmas; and let
19    the right of execution belong to Adymos and to those from him,
20    for the things according to the contract, both upon the
21    borrowers, both upon one and upon
22    both and upon whichever of
23    them he may choose, and upon all their
24    property, as by legal judg[ment;] sureties
25    for one another for the wheat or the written price
26    toward payment are the very persons who are also the borrowers; let this contract
27    be valid.
28    Loan of Adymos to D[ionysios]
29    and to Sarapias [of solid wheat, 225 artabas].
