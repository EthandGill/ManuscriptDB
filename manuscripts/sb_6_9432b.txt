[TRANSLATION]
1     to the grain-collectors of the village of Karanidis, greetings. I have received from you
2     as a loan of seeds of the present 23rd year
3     from the produce of the past 22nd year, 6 cleruchies of Sarapion: from Phasei of Karanidis, 3 arouras, of wheat 3 artabas; from Ptolemais, 3 arouras
4     of wheat 3 artabas; from Kerkesouch, 1 aroura, of wheat 1 artaba; totaling 7 arouras of wheat, 7 artabas.
5     Kastor of Petheus wrote on behalf of
6     him [illiterate]. Kastor, agoranomist, I have marked artabas seven.
7     year 23 of Hadrian, 30th.
