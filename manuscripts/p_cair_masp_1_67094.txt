[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine lord [Flavius Justinian the eternal Augustus]
2     and [E]mperor, in the twenty-sixth year, [after the consulate of Flavius Basileios the most glorious,]
3     in the twelfth year, on Pharm[outhi] … [of the first indiction] …
4     To Flavius Julian the most magnificent pagarch of the Antaiopolite (nome),
5     through Flavius Dioskoros son of Apollos and A[po]llos son of John, most esteemed chief-villagers
6     of the village of Aphrodite of the same Antaiopolite nome, from the undersigned below,
7     acknowledging the matters set forth, each being bound by his own signature,
8     greeting. We acknowledge voluntarily and of our own free will, swearing by both the almighty
9     God, and by the victory and salvation [of] our triumphant lord Flavius Justinian
10    the eternal Augustus and Emperor, that we guarantee and have taken upon ourselves to your
11    magnificence, for his presence and appearance, Enoch son of [Joh]n, the most pious lector,
12    that he shall remain and be found in the same village of Aphrodite without any
13    contempt whatsoever or shameful deed; and whenever he should be sought by you
14    and not be found, we his guarantors are ready to give and to pay
15    to your magnificence on account of his absence, of gold
16    six solidi; makes 6 gold solidi; and that he shall in no way absent himself, or else we shall be liable to the divine
17    oath and to the danger attached to this matter. And having been formally questioned on all points
18    we have acknowledged. Peter son of Apoll[os], the subdeacon of the holy place of Apa Horouonchios the martyr,
19    I guarantee the aforesaid Enoch for [two] gold nomismatia. And the Aurelii Ph[i]b son of Araous, the caunace-weaver …
20    I guarantee [the aforesaid Enoch for] two gold nomismatia, and John son of Kyriakos for [one] gold nomismation,
21    and John son of Psenthaesios for one gold nomismation, together six solidi; makes 6 solidi. And we swore the divine
22    oath as set forth. Aurelius Apollos son of Isakios, having been requested, wrote on their behalf the documents
23    [they being illiterate]. Victor son of Promauos, presbyter, I bear witness to the surety
24    [having heard] from those who made it. Moses son of Phoibammon, deacon, I bear witness [to the]
25    surety, having heard from those who made it.
26    Through me, Pilate [the notary], it was written …
