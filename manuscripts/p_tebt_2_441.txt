1   In the eleventh year of Imp[erator Caesar]
2   Domiti[a]nus Augustus Germanicus, [.......]
3   in Tebty̆nis of the Polemon divis[ion of the]
4   Arsinoite nome. [Psoiphis]
5   son of [Pa]opis, about twenty-six years old, with a scar [on the]
6   first [finger] of the right hand, agrees with Eutychos [......],
7   about twenty-five years old, with a scar on the little finger
8   of the left hand, that he has leased to
9   him, Eutychos, for the coming twelf[th] year
10   of Imperator Caesar Domitianus Augustus
11   Germanicus, out of the (lands) which Psoiphis farms around Tebtynis
12   of public lands, those in a single seal-plot
13   in the so-called Forty-Aroura(-field),
14   two and a half arouras of land, or however many there may be,
15   adjoining on the south side
16   (the land of) Marepsemis son of Psoiphis, for the sowing of grass and
17   cutting and drying, the supply of the seed
18   and the cutting of the grass being
19   (the responsibility of) Eutychos, while all the agricultural
20   [work]s and transport-costs are [to be borne by]
21   [Psoiph]i[s, who also] is to ha[v]e from Eu[tychos]

23   [by ha]nd out of the house, and that [Psoiphis] shall guarantee
24   to Eutychos the said [arouras],
25   the t[wo] and a half, with every guar[antee ...]
