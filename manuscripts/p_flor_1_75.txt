[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the consul]ship of our lords Gratian for the 5th time and Theodosius for the 1st,
2     [th]e eternal A[u]gusti,
3     [to the Aureli]i Dioskoros son of Ammonion and Philammon son of Hermes,
4     [councillors of Her]mou polis the most illustrious, superintendents of the grain of Alexandria
5     [of the canon of the] present ninth indiction, of the northern division,
6     [from] Aurelius Chairemon, through me his father Phoibammon,
7     … [m]other Thatres, aged about 60, having a scar on the middle finger of the left
8     [hand, fr]om the village of Kirka, ship's-captain of a private vessel.
9     [I acknowledge, sw]earing by the divine and heavenly fortune of our all-
10    [conquerin]g lords Gratian and Valentinian and
11    [Theodosius] the eternal Augusti, that I have received now also
12    [from the nome] of the Hermopolite and have loaded into my vessel
13    [from the cano]n of the same ninth indiction, of pure grain,
14    [one thousand] and sixteen [artabas], in addition to the others which I received, of pure grain,
15    [four hundred] and forty-seven [artabas], so as to be all together
16    [of pure] grain one thousand four hundred and sixty-three artabas,
17    [making of grain] 1463 [artabas], which I shall carry off to the most illustrious Alexa[ndria] and shall deliver in the gr[an]aries of the New City and
18    [of the] delivery I shall bring in your name the customary recei[pt do]cuments from the annonarch in full. And on the spot
19    [I have been pai]d the freight-charges and the cumuli and the porter[age] wages, so that nothing be sought from you concerning thi[s grai]n on any matter whatsoever, and that I have not in any respect lied [or may I b]e liable to the divine oath and to the danger concerning this.
20    [And on being questioned] I acknowledged. Phaophi 14. I, Aurelius Chairemon, through me his father
21    [Phoibam]mon, received all together the one thousand
22    [four hund]red and sixty-three artabas of grain and shall carry off and deliver as
23    [aforesaid] and I have also released this acknowledgment. I, Aurelius Serenos,
24    … [fr]om Kirka, ship's-captain, wrote on his behalf,
25    [being prese]nt, since he does not know letters.
26    It was written through me, Eponychos, the bond of the acknowledgment.
