1     The expense for Alexandria:
2     from the old [pay]ments:
3     Petoseiris, [son of] Teos, forty-four drachmas
4     and to the young men, Mars of Ephalis, one hundred
5     Harbekis, [son of] Haremphois, ten
6     sailing-fare for the ship, four hundred forty
7     carriage [cost] for wood, Petous, one, [worth] four-obols
8     Petron and Paapis so as to guard and [stat]ion
9     the young men, twenty, four-obol[-worth]
10    of wine in the guard-post and
11    to those with th[em], twelve cotyles, at two-obols each, come to four drachmas
12    and to the elders, ten drachmas
13    and beer to them, two drachmas [and] one obol
14    inspectors of ships, wine, sixteen cotyles, come to six drachmas
15    to the [price]-holders, eleven cotyles, at two [and] four-obols each, come to …
16    pure bread, eighteen, four-obol[-worth]
17    carriage [cost] for the bread to the Hellenic [place], one
18    Pedaos [of] iron, ten, at four-obols each, [by] weight twenty, come to three [drachmas and] four-obol[-worth]
19    labor-cost for them, four
20    a box/measure of bread, two, two-obol[-worth]
21    those who carried [them]:
22    Semthous and Petous, eight
23    Kakis, [from] artabas, two, come to fifteen
24    labor-cost for the bread, three, two-obol[-worth]
25    price of sesame, two, come to [amount]
26    Agathokles loan, sixty
27    from Apollonius [son of] Timotheus, sixty
28    from Harmaios [son of] Pausios, [amount], one-and-a-half obol, [one-obol portion]
29    and from him loan, one hundred
30    from the treasury of the temple, twenty-one
31    and of silver stater, come to sixteen, five-obol[-worth], one-and-a-half obol
32    Tybi, seventh, in Memphis, ninety-eight
33    eleventh in Memphis again, sixty-four, one-and-a-half obol
34    thirteenth in Memphis, one hundred sixty
35    against Pokas [son of] Harphoitos, one obol
36    and Heraklides, [price]-holder, three
37    and he has of price [owed] from:
38    Petoseiris [son of] Ergemonius, two, three-obol[-worth]
39    a measures, two, come to two obol one …
40    I gave to Bienkhis [son of] Harendotos
41    to Alexandria, Mechir, ninth, [in] drachmas, one hundred
