[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Horus son of Pausiris through Ammonius, (his) friend, to Apollo— and Harpaesis and their associate sitologi of the upper toparchy, district of Monimou, greetings. Transfer the (artabas) that you hold on deposit for me, from the wheat-crop of the 19th year of Hadrianus Caesar the lord, to Psammis son of Psammis, (for his account) at Mermertha, twenty (and) one-quarter artabas, at the hamlet of Istrou, six (and) one-half (and) one-quarter artabas (and) seven choenices, grand total twenty-seven artabas (and) seven choenices of wheat. (N. Gonis)
