[TRANSLATION]
1     Dorion
2     not having-been-able you to-meet through the
3     holding you arrogance and fear,
4     which indeed from-beginning I-was-praying to-all
5     the gods for-the greater things you to-advance,
6     I-have-proceeded through petition to-you to-bring, in-order-that, if it-appears,
7     you-may-order to-write Ptolemy
8     the scribe of-the farmers
9     to-receive from me instead-of wheat barley artabas 25
10    through [the] holding-me in the
11    year distress. This and having-done you-will-be
12    having-gratified gods both and men.
13    To Ptolemy. When the use is-fulfilled,
14    take-care so-that we-may-receive.
15    Year 34, Epeiph 4.
