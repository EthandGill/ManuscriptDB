[TRANSLATION]
1     … for 110 artabas, 11 solidi, 12½ carats, ¼;
2     through Tzittas Prau, for the appointment of a guardian, 6 solidi of Alexandria;
3     through the headman Saso Kato, for the appointment of the 13th indiction, through Prau, 18 solidi, 18 carats of Alexandria;
4     for the price of wheat sold to the people of Saso Kato, from 438 artabas,
5     at 13 artabas to the solidus less 6, 33⅔ solidi less 201; to Alexandria-standard, 25 solidi, 6½ carats;
6     total: 50 solidi, ½ carat;
7     and for the price of wine, 120 solidi of Alexandria;
8     and for the field-guards of Saso Kato, 14 solidi, 15 carats of Alexandria;
9     and for the expenses of the share of the 13th indiction, 22 solidi;
10    total: 156 solidi, 15 carats.
11    Total in Alexandrian solidi: 1160 solidi, 18 carats.
12    Of which, paid out:
13    for the guardian, 6 solidi of Alexandria;
14    for the eirenarchs, 6 solidi of Alexandria;
15    for Tpakekem, 37 solidi of Alexandria;
16    for the cession, 15 solidi of Alexandria;
17    and in expenses, 38 solidi of Alexandria; total altogether, 102 solidi.
18    To the most illustrious lord Victor, administrator, 791 solidi, 5 carats of Alexandria;
19    likewise in the month Choiak, 4 solidi, 47 solidi, 11 carats of Alexandria; total, 838 solidi, 16 carats;
20    altogether, 140 solidi, 16 carats; of Kintos in tow, 72 solidi; total, 1012 solidi, 16 carats.
