[TRANSLATION]
1     Sempronius to Saturnila, his mother
2     and lady, greetings most.
3     Before all things I pray that you are well, together with also
4     my unenvied brothers; at the same time I make obeisance to you daily to the lord Serapis. So many letters I sent to you and not one did you write back to me, so many [ships]
5     having sailed in. When urged, my lady, [I beg you]
6     to write me without delay about your safety, so that I too may live more free from care. For this is my constant desire. I greet Maximus and his wife and Saturnilo and Gemellus and Helen and hers. Convey
7     to her that I received a letter
8     from Cappadocia. I greet Julius and his [people] by name and Scythicus and Thermuthin and
9     her children. Gemellus greets you.
10    Farewell to me, my lady, constantly.
11    Sempronius to Maximus, his brother,
12    greetings most. Before all things I pray
13    that you are well. I learned that you serve
14    our lady mother heavily. When urged, sweetest brother, in no way grieve her. But if any of the brothers contradicts her, you ought to strike them. For already father ought to be called [upon]. I know
15    that without my letters you are able
16    to please her. But do not take hard my letters admonishing you. For we ought to revere the one who bore us as
17    god, especially being such a good one. I have written you this, brother, knowing the sweetness of [the]
18    dear parents. You will do well to write me about [the]
19    safety of your [people]. Farewell to me, brother.
20    Give [word] to Maximus from Sempronius,
21    brother.
