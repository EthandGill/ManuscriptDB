[TRANSLATION]
1     Faustus son of Priscus Caesar, to Akousilaos, sitologos of Lysimachis, the two of them, greeting. Measure out from the produce of the 40th year of Caesar to the public cultivators
2     for the royal and sacred and other land which they cultivate, wheat, in accordance with the samples sent and sealed by Oiax, strategos of the division, [and] by Asklepiades the royal scribe,
3     the toparch and the village-scribe of the village and the others customary (officials) attending together,
4     for loans toward the seeding of the 41st year of Caesar, having received from them the appropriate written undertaking concerning (their pledge) to flood and sow all the lands about the [village]
5     at the proper season with wheat and the fitting and … kinds (of seed), and to leave no aroura desolate
6     or unwatered or over-flooded … of land in whatever way not sown through negligence … rents and dues from their own (means),
7     and that they will lay into the ground both seed that is pure and … soundly … and to no one anything … debts …
8     nor (will) he carry off (anything) for himself, in accordance with what has been [enjoined] by the governor concerning them … all …
9     for the rents and dues of the same 41st year of Caesar, free of every charge, of first-quality Syrian wheat two hundred and [eight]y-three artabas and
10    two hundred artabas of Syrian wheat, total of first-quality Syrian wheat 483 artabas, of which receive also the appropriate receipt …
11    [Year] 41 of Caesar, Hathyr 23.
