[TRANSLATION]
1     To king Ptolemy, greeting from Nikaia daughter of Nikias, a Persian woman. My husband Pausa[nias died] in
2     the 23rd year, leaving a will of that same year, in the month Panemos … [, by which] he leaves … naion the
3     his own son as my guardian. It has happened, then, that this one too [died, in year] 4, in the month
4     Daisios, and in the Egyptian (calendar) Hathyr, and that I have no kinsman who shall be registered as my [guardian. So that ] not on
5     this account the things left to me by my husband should be ruined, [since I have] no guardian
6     with whom I may make the dispositions concerning these things, I beg you, king, to com[mand Diophan]es the strategos that there be given to me as guardian Demetrios the Thracian, of the hipparchy of Ptolemy son of Et[eoneus of the] … hekatontarouros, to whom also
7     Pausanias gave my sister in marriage, and that the strategos make a written record concerning these things, so that
8     it may be available to me in an official document; and since, being both rather elderly and having become weak, I am not able
9     to come to Krokodilon polis, I have sent Demetrios the aforesaid to present the petition,
10    (and I ask you) to write to Diophanes (and for him to write) to Dioskourides the epistates, after he has had a portrait made of me and of the
11    guardian whom I request, to register (it) for Diophanes. For when these things have been done, I shall have obtained, king,
12    of your benevolence. Farewell.
13    To Dioskourides. Taking some of the elders of the village, go in to Nikaia and if
14    … of the … explain to us the portraits.
15    Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 29.
16    Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 29.
17    Nikaia daughter of Nikias, a Persian woman,
18    concerning the matters of her petition.
