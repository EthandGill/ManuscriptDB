[TRANSLATION]
1   Pechoites son of Ammonios paid,
2   for poll-tax of the Opheion(?) (quarter), year 1, seven drachmas three obols,
3   total 7 dr. 3 ob., and surcharges on these. Year 1
4   of Domitian the lord,
5   Mecheir 32(?). Bassos, I have signed.
6   (2nd hand) others, Pharmouthi 32(?), two drachmas three obols
7   total 2 dr. 3 ob. Apol( ) son of Apion, assistant,
8   I have signed.
