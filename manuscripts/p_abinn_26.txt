[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord Abinnaeus
2     Agathos.
3     On a former occasion too I wrote to
4     your kindness, master,
5     that I am being detained by Julianus the official
6     on account of other documents,
7     and since it was reported to me that Uranius … to the camp
8     did not store up the annona-supplies,
9     I made the exactor write to
10    him that he should receive (the supplies) from those
11    from Taurinus, and that you should not
12    release this man until I have got rid of the
13    official and come to the camp
14    and render to him my
15    account; for so my lord the dux commanded, that all the
16    annona-supplies of the year be stored up
17    at the camp by request of the
18    actuarii of the upper Thebaid,
19    and he commanded that after the storing-up of the grain an official be sent
20    and inspect together the
21    misplaced wheat-and-barley. For this reason I hastened to write to your kindness, master, that you may bring it about that the whole of the grain and barley be stored up, and that no
22    blame follow
23    a[bout thi]s any longer. If it
24    seems good to you, send to the city the recruit (tiro) now enrolled from Dionysias, Apollon, on account of the sportulae, since I am detained by this official on account (of it). I pray for your health, lord pa[tron].
25    To my lord the praepositus Abinnaeus
26    Agathos, actuarius.
