1     [To Aurelius] Asklepiades, former recorder, succeeding to

7     … of rents and dues and account of extraordinary charges
8     … and wine, and not having made the payment

13    … the cultivation of these, but the lord being not to be carried off

17    … to depart, owing not a little, but indeed

21    … of the existing (properties), in what manner he ought to do it
