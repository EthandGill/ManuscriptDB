[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 15 of Emperor Caesar Trajan [Hadrian] Augustus, Pachon 21, in the praetorium of Syene
2     of the Thebaid. They acknowledge to each other Asklepias daughter of Dada with her guardian Pindarus son of Harsunios heir of her own husband... of Valens soldier having died from troop 2
3     of Thracians division Cartiliana and Aemillius... soldier of the same troop absent through an agent
4     Julius Apollinaris soldier armory-keeper [of the] ala Gallica and Kassianus Gemellus curator of the division
5     Cartiliana creditors of the same Valens having agreed among themselves on the
6     found belongings camp-properties all of Valens being gathered
7     together and being sold and from these deducted the [water/forage] tax and the legacy-tax
8     of Pamonios steward and what he advanced and apart Asklepias [of] drachmae one hundred forty four, to take [each] from the remaining surplus the one
9     Asklepias one share toward the account of inheritance and funeral of the body of Valens, but
10    Aemillius toward what is owed to him two shares and Kassianus the remaining share fourth toward what is also owed to him. [But if] some other creditor comes or if [P]amonios steward forces the legacy-tax, [the three] will repay according to what each has received. But if the portions distributed exceed more drachmae four hundred, the four [hundred] shall [be] on the aforementioned [agreement], but the sixty separately
11    shall go to Aemillius. But [if] even more than the sixty, to the three it shall be on
12    the same four shares as they [have received]. But the loans cancelled shall be repaid to Asklepias. In triplicate it was written and [each received] one. Asklepias daughter of Dada
13    I agreed [to what is set out before] [and I received] one share of the inheritance and I will do... ...
