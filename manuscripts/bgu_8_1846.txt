[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sotel[is], kinsman and general and
2     over the revenues
3     from those from village Tou dwelling [there]. from above up to the
4     of you [in office], having not completed grain-tax
5     more than [by] silver drachmae 100 and the guest-tax,
6     but now being demanded by Agathokleia [payment of] silver drachmae 150 and staters 75
7     you [as] judge having determined precisely
8     we were demanded [to pay] silver drachmae 300, to which each [person]
9     contributes silver drachmae 6, being demanded by the
10    same [persons] silver drachmae 360 [for] tax and being weakened and if no one [helps] from you
11    [who was appointed for aid], assistance [comes to pass]
