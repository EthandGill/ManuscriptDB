[TRANSLATION]
1   Tiberius Julius Longinus
2   and Valerius son of Socrates
3   and those with them, overseers of the Sacred
4   Gate of Syene, with Herakleides
5   and Isidoros the lessees concurring, through Patachemis
6   the assistant. Paid: Kasis son of Patepos,
7   mother Thimeaus, (for) the trade-tax of the 6th year:
8   twelve drachmas — total 12 dr. Year 7
9   of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
10   Hathyr 3. (2nd hand) On the 9th the same paid for trade-tax
11   eight drachmas (and) two obols — total 8 dr. 2 ob.
