1     … [of the reign of]
2     our most divine and most serene master Flavius Mauricius Tiberi[us the eternal Augustus,]
3     Emperor and greatest Benefactor, in the sixteenth year, and in the consulsh[ip of the same most pious]
4     our master, the fifteenth year, Pharm[o]uthi 28, of the second indiction.
5     Aurelius Psais, whose father is Ioannes and whose mother is Stephanous, with God a tenant fr[om the village of Aphrodite]
6     of the Antaiopolite nome, to Aurelius Theodoros, son of the most blessed Mousaios …
7     landholder from the same village of Aphrodite, greeting. I agree, willingly and of my own [free will, to have leased]
8     from you for a four-year term, reckoned from the crops of the coming, with God, [third indiction]
9     and it itself and the time thereafter, the (property) belonging to you and which came to you by lawful inheri[tance from]
10    your most blessed father Mousaios, your whole share out of the whole estate [called]
11    Perion, together with the cistern round about it and reservoir and lodge and tower and hou[se with wooden]
12    stair and palm-trees and vegetable plots and pastures and other various pla[nts, fruit-bearing]
13    and unfruitful, and all things existing and pertaining and belonging to the same estate, with the rights
14    inside and outside, up to its old boundaries all around, according to the force of the written testament
15    and division of your parents, situated in the eastern plain of [the same] village of Aphrodite
16    in the holding of Psioul; and moreover I lease from you also, of the other arourai purchased b[y you]
17    in the same estate of Perion; on condition that I have this under me for cultivation and sow[ing] of whatever
18    crops I wish, with my own animals and cultivators and seed-throwers and expen[se]s,
19    (and) in full I shall bring to completion the whole holding, and (I agree) to give you, on account of the fixed rent of it,
20    each year, of pure and marketable wheat, five artabas, [total artabas] 5, per each aroura, according to the re-measurement
21    of the public measuring-cord by the measure of Ammonios the count; but on condition that you have authority to take each year
22    two arourai, one in rich land and one in wheat-bearing plot, one from the [sou]th [o]f the cistern,
23    and the other from the east of the embankment, from the south of the great channel, by the same cor[d];
24    and to manure one aroura with various dungings each year according to what was agreed, without dispute;
25    and to do the whole agricultural work of them, done by me, and to water them
26    diligently each year; and (I agree) to give you three gold solidi of capital, each less
27    one keration, by the goldsmith's weight of the same village, at one solidus for each in[s]talment
28    of the public taxes each year, provided however that I be relieved and have authority to take, for th[e] aforesaid
29    gold and the two arourai, another ten arourai by the same cord from the aforesaid your arourai
30    of the same your share of the estate of Perion each year according to what was agreed; and in the case of unwatered land — which may not be —
31    according to the terms of the aforesaid eastern plain, to act with [ea]ch other
32    without dispute; and I shall carry out the conveyance of the auspicious shipment (embole) of the aforesaid your share of the estate
33    up to the river-harbor, that is, six camel-loads, both of your rent and the produce of the aforesaid
34    two your arourai, and the chaff, up to your house; and I shall grind for you forty artabas of wheat with my own
35    animals and cultivators, and give you at one carting one bundle of ears of grain, moreover each year; and I shall give you e[ach] year cheeses,
36    dried estate-cheeses, seventy-five, and (fish-)brine, six jars, and three festive extra deliveries
37    with cheese-loaves and vegetables; and of the aforesaid half of the aforesaid pasture … of the estate of Perion
38    I am to have each year, apart from your landlord's share, and of the half of the fruits of the same palm-trees,
39    I am to have again, apart from your landlord's share; and on condition that I raise up for you a small thorn-thicket from the
40    same embankment, from the sycamore southward, up to the boundary, diligently accord[in]g to what was agreed,
41    without dispute. This present written lease-agreement shall be valid [a]nd secure,
42    produced everywhere; and, questioned on all points, I agreed. Aurelius Psais son of Ioannes,
43    the aforesaid, have leased as aforesaid. I, Aurelius Ioannes son of Victor, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he not
44    knowing [letters]. Aurelius Phoibammon son of Io[annes, I bear w]itness to this lease, having heard it from the party who made it.
45    Aurelius Makarios son of Christophoros, I bear witness to this lease as aforesaid. Aurelius
46    Ioannes son of Loukanos, I bear witness. I have leased as aforesaid.
47    Through me, Philotheos, with God tabellio (notary), it was written, it was written, it was written.
