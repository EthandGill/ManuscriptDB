[TRANSLATION]
1   (Sheet.)
2   To Pr[otarch]os.
3   [From] G[aius] ..., and from Papos and Ptolemaios, both sons of ...:
4   concerning the matters in dispute we agree on these terms, namely: since
5   Papos and Ptolemaios have taken over from Gaius the planting (and) bringing-up (of vines) ...
6   for a period of three years from the present 17th year of Caesar, (for) two arouras which are among the
7   vineyard-properties belonging to him near the village of Taphilis of the ...,
8   one in the plot marked 'Muses', the other in the plot marked ...,
9   ...; at the fixed wage for the two arouras, of four hundred and
10   fifty Ptolemaic silver drachmas, of which they (acknowledge) having here and now received from
11   Gaius, by hand from the house, 200 silver drachmas, and the remaining 250 dr.
12   they will receive [in] two equal installments: (in) Mecheir of the coming
13   18th year of Caesar, 125 dr., and the second, at the bringing-up (of the vines), the remaining
14   125 drachmas; and that from now Papos and Ptolemaios will carry out
15   ... vine ... wine ...
16   the planting of the two arouras, setting the vine-shoot in ... aroura(s)
17   ... having the proper depth,
18   (providing) reed,
19   digging,
20   and watering duly at the proper seasons, Gaius pro-
21   viding them with the vine-shoot at the trench, and stakes and reed-grass and the reed,
22   in sufficient quantity,
23   and the wat[er] at the standing(?); they not being permitted to abandon the under-
24   taking within the period;
25   and if any of the vines should happen to fail, they shall re-
26   plant them at their own expense, within the same year, no worse (than before);
27   and at the completion of this (period) they shall hand over
28   the two arouras fully planted and flourishing and brought up,
29   to the same standard; and if they transgress anything, they shall be immediately
30   subject to execution and held until they repay both what they have of the wage and
31   whatever portion they have received, with half as much again, and whatever they damage, twofold,
32   and the damages and expenses and a penalty of 500 silver drachmas and the fixed fine, the right of execution being
33   Gaius', against both, who are mutually surety for payment, and against either one, whichever he chooses, and
34   against all their property, as if by legal judgment, (all) pleas which they may bring being void,
35   and every protection (notwithstanding); Gaius nonetheless having the power, should they
36   breach the contract by withdrawing from the undertaking within the period, to (remove them) and to others
37   re-let it, and to exact whatever shortfall results from the re-letting;
38   and, they performing each duty accordingly, Gaius too is to provide them
39   the undertaking for the period, and to pay over the balance of the wage
40   in the stated installments, or himself pay the same penalty, there remain-
41   ing to Gaius his recourse against the same Papos and Ptolemaios
42   concerning the leases of the aforesaid properties and whatever they owe.
43   Year 17 of Caesar, Pharmouthi 5.
