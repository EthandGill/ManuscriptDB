[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Zenon, greetings, you did rightly
2     in arresting the beer-seller's treasurer.
3     And we sent to you also Ame[n]nea [the]
4     beer-brewer so that concerning what you wrote he can accuse
5     his treasurer [and] convict [him] before Peton
6     the financial official. Set both
7     before Peton. For if it appears to be true
8     [that] Amenneus has said what you wrote to us
9     [he will] be hanged.
10    Farewell. Year 31, Dystrou 23, Phamenoth 30.
11    Year 31, Pharmouthi 1.
12    Apollonius concerning the
13    beer-brewer Amenneus.
14    To Zenon.
15    [Of] Amenneus.
