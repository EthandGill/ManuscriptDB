[TRANSLATION]
1     It was registered.
2     To the Aurelii Theon, ex-agoranomos, and Dioskourides also called Ptolemaios,
3     both councillors, keepers of the records,
4     from Aurelia Th[ermou]thion,
5     styled, her mother being
6     Tapiomis, from Oxyrhynchus
7     city, with her assistant Aurelius Serenos surnamed Leonides,
8     from the same city.
9     Of which (succession) I have made (entry) before
10    Domittius Philippus, prefect of the night-watch, general, concerning the possession
11    of the Roman inheritance of my daughter Techosis daughter of Ploutarchos
12    from the same city
13    who has died childless and intestate
14    after the death of her
15    father, the inheritance falling to me
16    according to the laws as heir, a copy translated into Greek letters as far as possible, bringing it to you in two
17    copies, I submit the memorandum in order
18    that the necessary registration may be made, and I swear
19    the customary oath for Romans
20    that I have not lied. [Year] 4
21    of the Emperor Caesar
22    Marcus Antonius Gordianus
23    Pius Felix Augustus,
24    Tybi 6. I, Aurelia Thermouthion, have submitted (this)
25    and have sworn the oath.
26    Aurelius Serenos surnamed Leonides
27    have assisted her and
28    have written on her behalf, she not
29    knowing letters.
30    Aurelius Dioskourides,
31    keeper of the records, have signed.
