[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelii Psais (son of) Pek[ysis] (son of) Palitous and Tatoup his [ow]n wife, both from [the vil]lage of Kell[e]s
2     of the Mothite nome, residing in the hamlet of E… [ to Aur]elius Tithoes (son of) Petesis, carpe[n]ter from the same village of the same [no]me, greeting. We acknowledg[e] that we have sold to you and registered, from
3     now for all time, the female slave belonging to us, a foundling, reared by me, the
4     aforesaid wife, with my own milk, at the price mutually agre[e]d between us
5     of two simple newly-struck imperial solidi, that is 2 solidi, which we have receiv[e]d from you through hand in full, on which terms we are content [with all that is written,] so that from now you the buyer have power
6     and lordship and ownership over the slave sold to you, and have authority to administer and manage
7     concerning her in whatever manner you choose, the guarantee following to us the sellers throughout
8     against everyone who shall come forward or [lay claim]. The sale is valid, written in duplicate, under the signature of the
9     one signing on our behalf; let it be secure an[d law]ful, produced everywhere, and having been formally questioned we ackn[owledge]d.
10    In the consulship of Mamertinus and Nevitta the most splendid, Thoth by the Greek (reckoning).
11    Aurelius Psais (son of) Pekysis and Tatoup the wife, the aforesaid, have sold the aforesaid slave …
12    and have received the price in two gold solidi and we will guarantee to you with every guarantee as stated above
13    and having been formally questioned we acknowledged. I wrote on their behalf as they do not know letters, Aureliu[s Ti]motheos
14    (son of) Harpokration, former magistrate, having been requested.
15    Aurelius Demosthenes (son of) Pol[y]krates from the village of Kellis, I bear witness.
16    [Aureli]us Horion (son of) Timotheos from the village of Kellis, I bea[r witness.]
