[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of the Flavii Eucherius and Syagrius the most illustrious prefect of the sacred praetorium, Phamenoth 1.
2     Aurelius Kephalon son of Theodoros, his mother being Sophia, deacon
3     of the catholic church, from the village of Tamoro of the
4     Herakleopolite nome, to Aurelius Leontios … the trade, from the city of Herakleous, greeting.
5     I acknowledge that I have received from you, for my own and
6     necessary need, from hand to hand out of your house, of gold
7     plain two-figured, five solidi, total 5 solidi
8     of capital, and on condition that I provide to you in return, by way of interest, each month five bundles of five-mina tow, and
9     from the present month of Phamenoth of the current
10    year of the indiction, I pledge to you as security
11    an uncoined cup and a … of … pounds …
12    which I shall repay to you …
13    of every kind of every sort. The valid chirograph,
14    written singly, and being formally questioned I acknowledged.
15    Aurelius Kephalon the aforesaid, I have received
16    from you in gold two-figured the gold solidi
17    five, 5 solidi, and on condition that I provide to you
18    in return, by way of interest, each month
19    five bundles of five-mina tow,
20    and I pledge now to you an uncoined cup and …
21    … [Kephalo]n son of Theodoros of Tammoro, 5 gold solidi.
