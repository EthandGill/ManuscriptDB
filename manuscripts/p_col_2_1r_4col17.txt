[TRANSLATION]
1     [To Sab]inus and Deios and partners, public bankers.
2     We the undersigned 7 men, watchtower-guards
3     from Ptolemais Hormou, have received in full
4     from you the sums ordered to us for
5     wages of the month Mecheir of the present
6     [18th] year of Antoninus Caesar the lord: to five men [each] 40 drachmas, 200 drachmas; and to two men each
7     [24] drachmas, 48 drachmas, so that the sum total is 248 drachmas of silver, total 248 drachmas.
8     They are as set out below: Eiou…m
9     son of Eio…m, of the first watchtower, aged 43, scar on the left knee: 40 drachmas.
10    Horeus called Oseus, son of Heron son of Patynis, of the [second] watchtower, aged 36, scar on the middle of the left hand: 24 drachmas.
11    Heron son of Z…os son of Karax, of the third watchtower,
12    aged …, scar on the left knee: 40 drachmas.
13    Heron son of …, of the fourth watchtower, aged 33, scar on the middle of the left hand: 40 drachmas.
14    Heron son of …, of the fourth watchtower, aged 32, scar on the left hand: 40 drachmas.
15    Tryphon son of M…nos, of the fifth watchtower, aged 40, scar on the middle of the left hand: 40 drachmas.
16    Onnophris son of Onnophris, of the fifth watchtower, aged 41,
17    scar on the middle of the hand: 24 drachmas. There result the aforesaid (sums).
18    Written through Herakleides, document-writer, they saying they do not know letters.
19    Year 18 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
20    Phamenoth 4.
