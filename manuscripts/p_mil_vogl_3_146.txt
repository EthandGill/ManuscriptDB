[TRANSLATION]
1     Geminus son of Patron to Harpocrtion of Marepshemi greets. I acknowledge receipt
2     from you which you owed me and to my brother Paulinus according to public
3     requisitions two, which I also agree
4     to be invalid, of silver thirty
5     eight. I will moreover present brother
6     my Paulin[us] handing over to you a receipt through public requisition
7     and I claim nothing against you concerning nothing
8     of any matter up to [now].
