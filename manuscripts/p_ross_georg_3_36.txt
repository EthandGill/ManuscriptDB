[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Belisarius the most glorious,
2     Phaophi 10, first indiction.
3     To the most admirable Apollos son of Dioskoros
4     son of Psimanobet, tax-collector, from the village
5     of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome,
6     from Aurelius Psaios son of Phounsis son of Salatokos
7     from the same village of the same nome, greeting.
8     I acknowledge willingly and of my own free choice
9     that I have leased from your excellency
10    for a five-year term reckoned
11    from the crops, God willing, of the coming second in[diction]
12    and itself, the arourae belonging to you
13    called (those) of Kasillas
14    of the allotment of Thinestaue, unwatered,
15    to the west of the road, to the north the
16    arourae of my own, on condition that I furnish
17    for those (arourae) which I shall be able to cultivate [at a fixed rent]
18    in full even in a year of no inundation, for each
19    aroura, by measurement with the rope,
20    two artabae of grain [by your delivery measure]
21    without dispute. The lease is valid and secure,
22    and being formally questioned I acknowledged it. Aurelius Psaios son of Phounsis,
23    the aforesaid, have leased as aforesaid. Flavius Theoteknos
24    son of Psaios, former deputy-praepositus, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
25    Through me, Kyros the notary, it was written, by God's will.
26    Lease of Psaios, arourae of Kasillas.
