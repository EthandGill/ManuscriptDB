[TRANSLATION]
1     thr …
2     to establish [for] you [sureties]
3     of remaining. If it seems good to you
4     to release him, I will receive him for remaining.
5     And the earlier thing I
6     stood surety for him;
7     you blamed me for nothing
8     and him again. Do not
9     suppose that I have gone to the royal [matters]. I am not foreign/alienated
10    to you; for because of you
11    I do, so that he may farm
12    again.
13    Farewell.
