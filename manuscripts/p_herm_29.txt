[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign and consulship o]f our most divine master Flavius Mauricius
2     [Tiberius the eternal Aug]ustus, Emperor, in the fourth year, on the second of Mesore.
3     [These mutual] copies of the agreement of dissolution, that is, of repudiation,
4     [are made and dra]wn up toward one another, so that there be on each side an authentic [single (copy) for sec]urity: on the one side Aurelius Ioustos son of Sampsichos
5     … …ron the storekeeper, and on the other side Aurelia
6     … …phre, whose mother is Erebekka, Samaritans by religion,
7     [both from t]he (city) of the Hermopolitans, greeting. They acknowledge to one another the following:
8     Since formerly we were joined to one another in lawful marriage and partnership of life
9     and for the sake of begetting children, according to (the custom) among men, upon good hopes,
10    and today, we know not whence, out of some wicked influence, we resolved
11    to part from one another, accordingly acknowledging that neither has any claim
12    nor shall have henceforth against one another concerning any matter whatsoever,
13    small or great, belonging to the marriage between us, neither concerning dowry
14    nor concerning bridal-gifts nor concerning wedding-expenses nor concerning any action or
15    liability or legal grievance of complaint, but that it be permitted to each party to enter another
16    marriage from now on, that is, from this present day, just as
17    is written; and it has been resolved that Eudokia our daughter
18    our … the … …
19    … [of Io]ustos son of Sampsichos the storekeeper.
