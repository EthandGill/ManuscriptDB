[TRANSLATION]
1     To Demetrius [the also] Menander, royal scribe of Arsinoe, of Polemon's division,
2     administering [and] the [matters] according to the strategos of the same division
3     from Kronion the Chean from Tebtyn[is].
4     I leased from Irene, daughter of Herakleidou,
5     near the village Kerkeysis of an allotment of arouras
6     twelve [and a half and] ctamian arouras 12 1/2; having been done by me of all the arouras
7     the agricultural [works] and sowing, it happened [that]
8     [from] flooding [of the waters] of the thicket
9     by water the arouras c[a]me to be
10    in the preceding month Phamenoth,
11    for this I ask the equivalent through a [servant]
12    to be transferred [to Irene], so that she may know I am not being retained [from the] present tenth year
13    the [ex]port [duties], being [satisfied] with me by this [settlement].
