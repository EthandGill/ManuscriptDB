1     In the consulship [of our lords the Impe]rators
2     Diocletian [for the 8th time] [and Maximian for the] [7th time], Augusti.
3     Au[r]elius He[ras son of Paapis, his mother being Tamou]nis, from the village
4     of Taampemo[u] [of the toparchy toward the east] of the Oxyrhynchite nome, komarch [of the present] 19th and 18th
5     and 11th year, to Aurelius Euporion son of Aphynchis, from the
6     splendid and most [splendid] city of the Oxyrhynchites, victor in the sacred games, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from you as a loan
7     from hand to hand, out of your house, of imperial silver coinage
8     three talents and two thousand one hundred and sixty drachmas,
9     total 3 talents, 2,160 drachmas, which I shall of necessity repay to you by the 30th of the
10    month Pharmouthi of the present 19th and 18th and 11th year,
11    without any delay or subterfuge; but if
12    not, I shall pay you for the time overrun the interest
13    fixed at each mina for each month,
14    you having the right of execution both against me and out of
15    all my property. The bond of hand is valid,
16    written in duplicate, valid wherever produced and by any-
17    one producing it on your behalf, and, on being formally questioned by you,
18    I gave my assent. In the 19th year and the 18th year of our lords
19    Diocletian and Maximian Augusti and the 11th year of our lords
20    Constantius and Maximian the
21    most illustrious Caesars, Phamenoth 3.
22    I, Aurelius Heras son of Paapis, have received as a loan
23    the three talents of silver and
24    two thousand one hundred and sixty drachmas and I shall repay
25    as aforesaid, and, on being formally questioned, I gave my assent.
26    I, Aurelius Serenus, wrote on his behalf as he does not know
