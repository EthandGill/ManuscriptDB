[TRANSLATION]
1     The [renewal] that is owed of his son
2     To [A]pollo, [namely] the logos of you
3     Come, gather all the people
4     And you shall cease until you grow old, from the beginning forward
5     That we shall speak with you
6     Thoth and 6th indiction, of Musaeus I wrote
7     Daniel subscribed
