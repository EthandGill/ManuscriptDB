1     To King Ptolemy greetings: Apollodōtos, of those dwelling in Alabanthis. I am wronged by
2     Mnaséos, who [administers] the outer lands. For me owing nothing to the King, nor
3     being registered [with] him, [he] impounds me and seizes my goose-herd. I beseech then you,
4     [K]ing, [if] it seems [good] to you, [to order] Diophanes the strategó to write Herodōtos the overseer, if I appear to owe nothing nor be registered with Mnaséas, not to allow Mnaséas to impound m[e nor seize] my property. For this being done, I shall be through you, King, of justice hav[ing obtained].
5     Farewell.
6     To Herodō[tos. ] ... Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13.
7     Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13.
8     Apollodōtos against Mnaséas
9     concerning impounding.
