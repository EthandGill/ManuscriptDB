[TRANSLATION]
1     May there be great years.
2     I beseech the ageless [father] of us and at this very hour send to me
3     the boat so that we might transport the grain of Agathinous, and I will send
4     some ally to Syncypha on account of the people and the camels.
5     Be so kind to learn from the most-pious bishop on account of
6     the passage-money of the camels. [By no means] be negligent of this—care for it!
7     Many things moreover I [greet] your piety, lord master.
8     Give to the most-cautious and [most-pious] lord Pamouthius
