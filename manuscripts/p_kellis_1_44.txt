[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Pekysis son of Psai[s] son of Pamouris, his moth[er being T]apollos,
2     from the village of Kelle of the city of the Mothites of the Great Oasis, to Aurelius Antoninus from [t]he same
3     village of the same nome, dwelling in the village of Aphrodite
4     of the Antaiopolite (nome), greeting. I acknowledge that I have had
5     and borrowed from you, for my own and necessary
6     nee[d], one approved gold solidus of capital,
7     makes 1 gold solidus, and I will furnish [to] you, by way of interest
8     on this, each month, six hundred talents of silver, makes 600 talents, the time being reckoned from
9     the present month, Pachon 1, of the [p]resent
10    elev[en]th indiction, until the repay[m]ent
11    of th[e] capital, without any dispute and any delay, all my property of every kind being held by you, until I pay you in full.
12    Thi[s] valid bo[n]d I have issued [to y]ou for your security, with the subscription of the one [su]bscribing on my behalf,
13    and on being formally questioned I gave my assent.
14    After the consulship of Eucherius and Syagrius [t]he most illustrious.
15    Pachon 1.
16    I, Aurelius Pekysis son of Psais, the aforesaid, have had
17    the one gold solidus of capital and
18    will rep[a]y (it) with the interest as is [s]et forth above. [A]urelius
19    Peb[os] son of Tithoes, from the same village, dwelling
20    in the vil[la]ge of Aphrodite, having been requested, wrote on his behalf,
21    he not knowing letters, the one who also wrote the bo[dy (of the document)].
22    Bond of Pekysis son of Psais, gold solidus 1.
