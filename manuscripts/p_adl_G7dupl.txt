[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a deed of sale.
2     In the reign of Kleopatra and King Ptolem[y, surnamed Alexander,]
3     the son, the Philometor Soter gods, in the 13th year [which is also the 10th, under the priests and priestesses]
4     and the kanephoros then in office, in the month Pauni, [the 18th, in Pathyris, before Hermias the agent of]
5     P[an]iskos the agoranomos. Thaibis, daughter of Ph[i]bis, a Persian woman, [about 58 years old, of medium height,]
6     honey-colored, long-faced, straight-nosed, somewhat short-sighted (?), with her guardian, [her own brother]
7     Paous the elder, son of Patous son of Phagonis, a Persian, about 35 years old, [of medium height, honey-colored,]
8     somewhat stooped, long-faced, straight-nosed, with no distinguishing mark, sold, out of the [land] belonging
9     to her, a date-palm grove of growing (trees) which is in the part [to the east]
10    of Pathyris, the sixth share falling to Pasemis son of Nechous her [own son,]
11    whose neighbors are: on the south, the portion of the date-grove of Paner[e]us; on the north [and east,]
12    (the land) of Horos son of Nechous; on the west, a canal; or whoever may be the neighbors on [all sides. There bought (it)]
13    Phibis and Horos, the two sons of Nechous, the sons of Tha[ibi]s daughter of Ph[ibis, an equal]
14    third share, for two ta[lents] of bronze (and) four thousand drachmas. [The fore-seller]
15    [and warrantor of all the matters of this purchase is Thaibis, the seller,]
16    [whom Phibis and Horos the buyers accepted. I, Hermias, the agent of Paniskos, have registered (this).]
