1     Aurelius Eusebiu[s] of Plotion, mother Theonille[s], from the
2     bright and most-bright city of the Oxyrhynchites, to Aurelius Thonios of Eros, from the
3     same city, [sacred-victor], most-victorious, paradoxical, greetings.
4     I acknowledge [that] I have received from you hence a full price of raw linen-reeds, six bales, which linen-reed[s]
5     [I will] necessarily repay [to] you clean in [the] fields in [the] Pauni month
6     of the present ninth seventh year without all delay
7     and [without] excuse-finding, if [it is] not [so], [I will] pay [to] you for the exceeded
8     time the [price] which will-be at the then time
9     double, as it is-set, the enforcement being [made to] you
10    from both me and from all my existing [property].
11    [The] hand-written-document [is] valid, written in-duplicate, adduced everywhere, on-your-behalf to all, and questioned by-you, I-acknowledged.
12    in [the times] after [the] consulship of Rufius Volusianus and Petronius
13    Annianus of the most-brilliant, Mechir 5.
