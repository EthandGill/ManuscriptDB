[TRANSLATION]
1     Fourth year, Pachon twenty-sixth. (He) dismissed
2     Tryphon and his associate, [tax-collector]
3     of the silver-tax of the village Apias, Stotouetis
4     of Stotouetos through Kephalon,
5     [the] arithmetician of [the] residents, ten drachmas
6     eight, (they) make eighteen drachmas.
