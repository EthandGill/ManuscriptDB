[TRANSLATION]
1   To Protarchos, the (official) in charge of the tribunal,
2   from Apollonios son of Heraklides and from Am-
3   monios son of Anoubion, a Persian of the epigone. Am-
4   monios agrees that he has from Apollonios a loan,
5   from hand to hand out of the house, of Ptolemaic silver, drachmas
6   sixty-four, interest-free, which he will also repay in
7   three months from the month Pharmouthi of the present
8   seventeenth year of Caesar, without any postponement;
9   or else he is at once liable to seizure and to be held
10   until he pays the [princi]pal with half as much again, and, for
11   the time elapsed, the interest according to the diagramma,
12   at two drachmas (per mina), the right of execution belonging to Apo[llonios]
13   both from Ammonios himself and from all his property,
14   as if by legal judgement, void being whatever pledges of trust he may bring forward,
15   (and) every protection. This loan is apart from
16   the others which the same Ammonios, together with his mo-
17   ther, owes to Apollonios under another agreement,
18   of one hundred drachmas.
