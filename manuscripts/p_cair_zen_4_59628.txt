1     To Zenon greetings ... [not to have] sheep nor lamb
2     myself too from my own [property] ... . Now and concerning the
3     green [figs] [am being unjustly accused] ... [he] sells to others, claiming [that] you [must]
4     [the price to] fall ... [4] drachmas per aroura, but now he reports drachmas 6. I ... [ask] of you, praying [for] the

7     not more than four obols ...
