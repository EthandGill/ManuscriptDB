[TRANSLATION]
1     Menches to Herodes his brother, greeting
2     and good health. Know that Ammonios,
3     who is from us(?), being in the office of Amenneus
4     [the royal] scribe, has written to us about the detention of
5     Aroteios son of Petearphreus
6     [by] the royal scribe on account of the level-measuring of the village and
7     the survey by rope, and to me too he has written to join
8     [with them]. I have requested him to hold off for me until the 21st.
9     Whenever, then, you receive this letter, until
10    … the … Aroteios … and …
11    … the counter-plea, taking care of your own other affairs
12    that you may be well. Farewell. Year 52, Mesore 18.
13    Menches to Ammonios his brother,
14    many greetings. I received the little letter written by you
15    through which you explained both the other matters and about the … Give a delay to Dionysios on account of
16    the level-measuring and that (he is) to join
17    at daybreak, since you too are not unaware of the
18    occupation in which we are, and because we are in the custody
19    of the strategos; and having summoned
20    Dionysios to hold off until I get free from the
21    occupation on the 21st. I accept the things
22    from you very much, and I am most grateful that instruction was given
23    to Athemmeus the … to come up. And you too, about whatever
24    you wish, explain; and in other respects you will do a favor by taking care of your own affairs.
