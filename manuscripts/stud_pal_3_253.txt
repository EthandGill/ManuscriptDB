[TRANSLATION]
1     Flavius Pettarius with God, village-chief, to you Melisippus
2     Provide to Georgius a portion from the Bubastite wine, nettles ninety
3     It was written in month Phaophi, 10th, indiction 12, by me [p.e.] notary, it is verified to me
