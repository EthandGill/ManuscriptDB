1   Kametis son of Petearpres,
2   (grand)son of Amphiomis, paid, for poll-tax of the South-and-West (quarter),

5   Likewise Pachon 1, 8 dr.; likewise

7   2 dr. 3 ob., which net 2 dr. 1½ ob. 1 chalkous.
