[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and most pious master Flavius Justinianus, the eternal emperor Augustus, in the twenty-third year after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most glorious, the 9th (year), on the fifteenth day before the Kalends of October, in the year of the province four-hundred and forty-fourth, in the month
2     Gorpiaios, on the thirtieth, of the times of the thirteenth indiction, in the metropolis Petra of Palaestina Tertia Salutaris. … [and to those at the] time
3     being tax-receivers, I enjoin the subjoined things: since … it was ceded …
4     in the place Mal el-Amra, that is, Mal el-… one farm-land … of mine … …
5     … of the coming eighth indiction, per year the … tax-quota of the same irrigated estate …
6     … it to have been paid down by him up to the present … of the twelfth indiction, as has been said … the tax-quota …
7     … and … according to the … per iugerum … between us both … …
8     as is proper. For this reason the present notice has been made to your reverence … …
9     of the patrimony, one and one-ninth iugerum, and this to burden the account and person of the said most esteemed Theodorus son of Obodianus from the present … [for the security of you both]
10    and of the public account and of ourselves too, having acknowledged to ratify this th…
11    … r … ga … ous … b … of all things as is aforewritten accordingly. Theodorus …
