1     To the [agoranomoi] …
2     Ktesidemos of Sotades, [servant], [reported]
3     to you has arranged the of the nome
4     strategist Apollonios to hand over
5     to Harpokrates of Pokoutes which he had brought forward
6     to be placed through you in the seventeenth
7     year, month Tybi, a will, of Harpokrates requesting. Of the year nineteenth
8     of Emperor [Caesar] Nerva Trajan of Aristus
9     Augustus [Germanicus] Dacicus [Parthicus]
10    Epeiph …. the aforementioned will
11    the aforementioned Harpokrates received through me.
12    the same time.
