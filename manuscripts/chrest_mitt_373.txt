[TRANSLATION]
1     A copy of the letter of the lord, translated as far as possible,
2     which (was issued) in the (consulship) of Traianus Hadrianus Augustus for the 3rd time
3     (and) of Publius Dassumius Rusticus,
4     the consuls; it was posted up in Alexandria in the camp
5     of the winter-quarters of the Third Cyrenaican legion
6     and of the Twenty-Second Deiotarian legion,
7     on the day before the Nones of August, which is Mesore
8     11, in the headquarters (principia).
9     I know, my Rammius, that those whom their
10    parents took up at the time of (their) military service have been barred from succession to the
11    paternal property,
12    and this did not seem to be harsh,
13    they having done the contrary of their military
14    discipline. But most gladly do
15    I myself put forward the grounds by which
16    that which was set more strictly by the Emperors before me
17    I interpret more humanely. Therefore, in whatever
18    manner those taken up at the time of military service are not lawful heirs of their own fathers,
19    nevertheless I judge that possession of the property
20    from that part of the edict which is also given to the kinsmen by descent,
21    they too can claim,
22    I judge. This grant of mine
23    you will need to make well-known both to my soldiers and to the veterans, not in order that I seem to them
24    to make account (of it), but that they may make use of it,
25    if they be ignorant (of it).
