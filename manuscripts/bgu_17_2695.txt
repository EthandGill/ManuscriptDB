[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving Trinity, Father and Son.
2     In the reign of our most divine master Flavius Phocas
3     the eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the sixth year,
4     Pharmouthi the sixteenth, of the eleventh indiction.
5     Aurelius Thomas, son of Pamounios,
6     of (his) mother Tsi, a vinedresser,
7     from the village of P[lethm]is of the Hermopolite nome,
8     to Aurelius … son of Phoibammos
9     from the (city) of the Hermopolitans, greeting.
10    I acknowledge that I have received and been paid in full
11    from you the complete and worthy price of eighty
12    measures of [wine], [total: wine] measures [80],
13    which I will furnish to you in the month [M]esore
14    of the twelfth [indiction], with God's help, in new wine,
15    of the finest and most pleasing, by the m[easure] of the [pl]a[ce ] …
16    … os … administration …
17    of the farmers; and if there be found vinegar
18    or unfinished or sour (wine) among them
19    up to the month Tybi of the same indiction,
20    I acknowledge that I will exchange for you in place of them
21    good wine without delay; and if I do not
22    give you the same wine within the same
23    term, I will furnish to you, for the
24    price of them, two gold solidi, at my
25    and my property's (risk). The present deed is valid and
26    secure, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged it. Aurelius Thomas son of Pamounios
27    the aforesaid, have made this deed as aforesaid. Api(?)
28    Pha Xi(?), I wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate. Aurelius Taurinos
29    son of Phoibammon, from Hermou polis, I bear witness to the deed
30    having heard (it) from the contractor. Pharesmaneios
31    son of Isakios, from Hermou polis, I bear witness to the deed
32    having heard (it) from the contractor.
33    Through me, Isakos, with God's help, notary, it was written, 16.
34    Bond for 80 measures of wine made [by] Thomas son of Pamounios, vinedres[ser], from the village of Plethmis of the Hermopolite (nome).
