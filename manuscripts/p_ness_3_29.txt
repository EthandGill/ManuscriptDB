[TRANSLATION]
1     [Of the reign of our most-divine and most-pious master] Flavius M[a]uric[ius T]iberius faithful [in] Christ, greatest benefactor, peaceful Emperor Augustus, of year ninth
2     [After the consulship of the same most-pious] our king, in year sixth, ten days before the calends of January, of year four-hundred eighty-fifth
3     [Of indiction ninth, of month Audnaeus, on the seventh] in the city Elousa. This testimony having the force of second and third command, to be sent Flavius
4     … [consecrated soldier of the fort] of Nessanoi, present now, having come to George Ianetos, consecrated soldier of the same fort, having set out from there
5     … and [he/she] says … those well-minded toward the gracious to incline and from themselves with the blessed to follow and …
6     … to turn aside and other things to think and neither into blame themselves to bring; since therefore differently
