1   Of the granary of Theadelphia,
2   produce of year 5, through the beasts
3   of the metropolis:
4   Sodike, shepherd,
5   through Ammonios, donkey-driver:
6   4 sacks. Year 6, Mecheir 16.
7   And on the 19th likewise an-
8   other 2 sacks — total 6 sacks.
9   Year 6, Mecheir.
