[TRANSLATION]
1     Theodore to Sabinus the
2     most honored brother, greetings; before
3     all things I pray you be strong and
4     be healthy. as just now I did not know
5     that you are in Didymos; if but not
6     each day to you I sent vegetables. Receive from the donkey-driver Claudius a bundle
7     of cabbages, stalks nine, fine ones,
8     and lettuces eight. Fare well.
