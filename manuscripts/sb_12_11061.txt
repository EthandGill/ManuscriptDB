[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign] of Ptolemy, [son] of Ptolem[y] and Berenice, the gods Benefactors, in the fourth year
2     [under the priesthood of] Demetrius [son] of Apollon, [the] second year of Alexander and the gods [Siblings and gods] Benefactors, [and] canephora of Arsinoe Philadelphus [being] Nymphaida of the
3     [Nymphion's] [the] second year, in the month [of Per]itius in Tholthis of the Oxyrhynchite nome.
4     [He leased for one year the] sowing [and harvest] [one from the sowing] in the fifth [year, of which the fru]its [go into the sixth year]—Theophilus, [a Macedonian, thirty-arou]ra plot-holder [of the lands in the …] to [Ari]stolochos [son] of Str[ateios, a Thra]cian of the epigone, [his own] plot whole, unsown, without risk except unwatered as rent-payment of wheat [fifty] artabas. If the land [becomes] unwatered, may Theophilus make allowance to Aristotlochos in proportion regarding the rent of the unwatered land. And the rent-payments writt[en] down shall Aristotlochos pay to Theophilus in the month Dystros of the sixth year [wheat] clean and unc[orrected], measured with just measure in just measuring, and [transport [it to Tholthis] where [Theophilus] arran[ges]
5     [at his own] expense. If [Aristotlochos does] not [pay according to what is written], let him pay to Theophilus the price of the ar[ta]ba, [each of the wheat, drachmas ten, and]
6     let the collection be for Theophilus from Aristotlochos, collecting according to the order…. And Theophilus [shall be responsible for] the fruits until [his own] share [is] received. And Theophilus shall guarantee to Aristotlochos the plot [and the sown] fruits in it as regarding what he has leased. If [he does not guarantee]
7     [according] to the written [terms], let Theophilus pay to Aristotlochos as penalty [silver] five hundred drachmas, unless some royal obstacle occurs.
8     And this written agreement shall be valid wherever it is presented. Witnesses: Phi[lippos]
9     Cyrenaean, Kallis Cyrenaean, Polyainos Cyrenaean, these [are] private [citizens], Hip[ponikos] Hesperian of the decanry, the four [of the soldiers] of Philon, Kalli[anax]
10    [son] of Diocles, Cyrenaean, Ziboites [son] of Nikostratos, Barcaean, the two of the epigone
11    of Theophilus
12    [Aristotlochos] of Philippos, Polyainos, K[allianax]
13    Kallios [son] of Hipponikos [Ziboites]
