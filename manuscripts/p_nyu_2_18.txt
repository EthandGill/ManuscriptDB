1     Proklos to Tryphon [the] dearest
2     [many] greetings.
3     Isidoros the one handing you the letter is
4     of me from the house, and he is by birth from [the] village [Psoph]theus of-the Memphite and the [census]
5     there he performs and the other duties. But by the [... gap ...]
6     from you detained he was forced to sell a document for [the purpose of] sowing concerning village Philad[elph]ia from [the] Libyan revenue five arouras [with] five
7     [and one] half. I ask [therefore] you and concerning my [register],
8     if any remembrance is at you of my friendship,
9     untroubled him keep and take thought
10    so-that it will be given-back to-him the document. This then having done you-will-be to-me favored.
11    Farewell. Year 35 of-Caesar Mechir 25
