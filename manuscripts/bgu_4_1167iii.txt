[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Pr]otarchos,
2     from Herodes son of Zela…eides, an Alexandrian, and from
3     Drakon son of Paeis, a Persian of the epigone. Drakon agrees
4     that he has from Herodes … through his hand from the house … drachmas
5     one hundred and twenty without interest, which [also he will] pay back within seven months
6     from Phamenoth of the present 18th year of Caesar, on condition that, from the
7     same month Phamenoth, within two months Drakon shall of necessity give back to Herodes,
8     as security for the loan, a security upon
9     his father Paeis, who has died, against the grain-bearing land that has come to him
10    around the village Machor of the Herakleopolite nome above Memphis,
11    3 arouras from the allotment of Hieroxenos, in no way at all … deferring, but he shall do these things without delay.
12    And if, when the two months have passed, Drakon does not give back
13    to Herodes the security,
14    he shall be at once subject to seizure and held until he pays off the loan, not having waited for
15    the time allotted to him …
16    to be re-lent, and for the time overrun
17    the [interest] of two drachmas according to the edict, the right of execution
18    belonging to Herodes both out of Drakon himself and out of
19    all his belongings just as if by a legal decision, and further also [out of the]
20    three arouras, against which it shall not be permitted
21    to Drakon either to carry out or [to register]
22    or to alienate them until Herodes recovers
23    his own, to whom it shall also be permitted …
24    and to act [and] to enter upon the three [arouras]
25    and to administer concerning them as he may wish.
26    Herodes son of Zela…dos.
27    Drakon son of Paeis.
28    Loan of 120 drachmas without interest for seven months
29    from Phamenoth, and to have it back.
30    Security for him
31    against the … belonging to him
32    3 arouras around the village Machor
33    from the allotment of Hieroxenos.
34    of the allotment.
