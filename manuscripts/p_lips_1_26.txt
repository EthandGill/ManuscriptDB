1     … Néar[chos] …

4     of this we held in common; but it seemed good to us today, now having become of [ag]e, to make a division of these

13    The agreement, that is, the divi[sion], (is) valid, which we have also issued in two copies so that there be a single (copy) with each party; and, being formally questioned,
14    we gave assent. In the consulship aforewritten, Me[so]r[e] 23. Aurelius Nearchos the aforesaid agrees [wi]th me and I assent to a[ll] th[e aforewritten (terms) as is set forth above].
