[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Zenon] greetings, Horos, [son of] Petaarmotos. I measured out eight artabas of sesame to Apollonios, the one in charge of oil, on behalf of Theotes, oil-seller of Sosphtes, so that he might give me
2     oil, as the king prescribes. Now Leon, the [one] from
3     Hermolaos did not accept [it] from me, but is recording [it] in the green fodder areas where your sheep have pastured—5/4 arouras at three
4     artabas per aroura; they come to 15 1/2 1/4 artabas of wheat;
5     the ten [arouras] with the hundred artabas [yield] 1 1/2
6     12 [artabas]; and the seed 5/4 [artabas];
7     in total they come to 22 1/2 1/12 artabas of wheat.
8     You would do well to write to Leon about these matters,
9     as it seems [right] to you.
10    Farewell.
11    Year 36, Epeiph 21.
12    It was signed to Leon
13    Year 36, Mesore 1.
