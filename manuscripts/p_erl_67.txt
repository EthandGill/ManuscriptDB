[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ our God
2     and Savior, after the consulship of our master Flavius
3     Maurikios Tiberius, year 8, Thoth 19, at the beginning of the fortunate tenth indiction,
4     at Herakleopolis, to Flavius Philoxenos the most magnificent comes, administrator
5     of the glorious house of Sophia the most exalted patrician lady, son
6     of Petronios of illustrious memory, from Herakleopolis,
7     (we), Aurelii Antonios son of Tourbon and his son Petros,
8     both stablemen from the village of Kaine of the Herakleopolite
9     nome, greeting. We acknowledge, by mutual surety, of our own free will,
10    through this our written security, that we
11    have received from your magnificence, from hand to hand, out of her house,
12    for our own and necessary needs, of gold
13    six solidi less thirty-six keratia of capital,
14    that is, of gold 6 solidi less 36 keratia, which under compulsion we shall repay to you
15    and shall pay her in full in one payment, in good faith,
16    in the month Pharmouthi of the fortunate tenth indiction, without interest,
17    without delay and without any dispute. The bond (is)
18    valid, and on being formally questioned we so acknowledged. (We,) Aurelii Antonios
19    and his son Petros, stablemen from Kaine, the aforesaid,
20    the bond is agreeable to us as aforesaid. (I,) Aurelios Kollouthos
21    son of Ioannes, from Herakleopolis, wrote on behalf of them, being illiterate.
22    (I,) Apa Sion, by God comes and chartularius, bear witness
23    to this same bond, having heard (it) from the contracting parties, as aforesaid.
24    (I,) Georgios, by God riparius of the glorious house of Praiekta
25    the most exalted lady of consular rank, bear witness
26    to this bond, having heard (it) from the contracting parties,
27    as aforesaid. (I,) Flavius Kosmas, by God kour (= curialis?), son
28    of Apoi of blessed memory, from the (city) of the Herakleopolitans,
29    bear witness to this bond, having heard (it) from the contracting parties, as aforesaid.
30    (Christian symbol) by me Menas (Coptic subscription) by me Menas (sign).
