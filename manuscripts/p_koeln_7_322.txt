1     In Herakleopolis. Aurelius Ioannes,
2     a farmer, son of Menas, fr[om the village] …
3     of the Herakleopolite nome, to Aurelius Gerontios, son of Antinoos, from the same village …
4     in pure and undisputed (terms) to ow[e] … fifty solidi of refined [gold]
5     … and for security … t … mati … e … d … k … .
6     … n … k … from the … of [A]urelius Amon[ios] … [in a place]
7     [c]alled Tarot, six arouras, with their proportionate share of the cistern there and water-supply
8     [a]nd of the water-machine and machine-stand and mechanical implements and tower and every right belonging to them
9     [altoge]ther, neighbours on the south the public road, on the north the canal there, on the east the [pl]ot … th … ras
10    so that you, the said Gerontios, and your heirs and all manner of successors may [have] such
11    immovable property [with] also every right of them according to the aforementioned description, [and] to have authority
12    over the plots, to sow, to enjoy the fruits and to draw revenue annually, if you should wish, the rent
13    being reckoned and lightened, on behalf of the produce of the plots, in place of
14    the benevolence (i.e. interest) of the same debt; and that neither I
15    nor my heirs may detach such immovable property, or a part of them, or a right, from you
16    or from your heirs until the completion of the same debt and the full payment of the same debt
17    … e … thenton …
18    … e by the debt, just as you received from me; and being questioned I agreed [and] I released.
