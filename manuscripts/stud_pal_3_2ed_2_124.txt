[TRANSLATION]
1     I Nonnus, son of Neilos, grain-measurer from the city of Arsinoe, owe to you, most admirable Philoxenos, overseer of work
2     of sack-carriers of the same city, grain [four] artabas cancellum—[there are] artabas 4 cancellum—and these I will repay to you from my wage of the contribution to the public granaries of this city of the crops of the 13th indiction without ambiguity. It was written in the month Phamenoth 17 of the present 12th indiction. Through me Menas the notary.
