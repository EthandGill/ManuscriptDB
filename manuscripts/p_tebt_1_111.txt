[TRANSLATION]
1     Harbechis of Erges to Apollonios
2     and to Heraklides the grain-collectors
3     into the workshop concerning Theogonides.
4     I acknowledge to have from you
5     from the administration of debt
6     wheat artabas fifteen,
7     total wheat artabas 15, in the measure of the hexachoinos
8     of Kerkeoseira, which I shall measure out to you
9     until Pauni 30, but if not
10    I shall measure and [the] one-and-a-half.
11    Year 54, Pauni 18.
