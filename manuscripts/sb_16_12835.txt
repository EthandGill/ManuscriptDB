[TRANSLATION]
1     … [he was] compelled to provide a handwritten document
2     [for the] sowing [around] the village Philadelphia
3     [from the Li]ouan revenue, five and a half arouras;
4     I therefore ask you, having ascertained whether these things are thus concerning
5     [him] to be from Memphis, [I request you] to write to Tryphon to provide him without hindrance, and to return to him the handwritten document which [he] received from him; do everything, brother, so that he might acknowledge [it] to me more quickly,
6     [and you] also permit [us] concerning whatever [matters] you may wish.
7     [Fare] well. Year 35 of Caesar, Mecheir 22
