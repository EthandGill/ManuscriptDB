[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemais to Abous, her most honored
2     father, very many greetings.
3     I received from Kastor the veteran
4     garments to the number of eight, as I instructed you face to face concerning the woman. You will do well to send across
5     to her the linen dalmatic which you have, and if anything else is being carried off for them
6     according to custom, both in whatever time our lord the Emperor goes forth for good things, an[d] …
7     … the … and the malabath[ra]
8     a … im … Kastor did not give m[e] nor the good … eri … . So do not, attending to my mother, be negligent, even if
9     she herself does not wish (it). Do you take care, as
10    is fitting and at once, if I write to you and it is possible for you, that with her
11    you come down to me; but if you
12    have no leisure, you will send her across through
13    my mother or [the broth]er of her or of the mother, or as you wish,
14    and convey them to Memphis
15    and give their fare and their expenses, and the cover (blanket)
16    send across to me likewise. Greet
17    my br[oth]ers and your wife and all our people
18    by name. Greet Maron
19    and tell him that I delivered the petition, but the
20    subscription has not yet come out; so if it does not come out, again
21    I will resubmit (it). I pray for your health. So make known to me what you did concerning it,
22    sin[ce] it was disclosed to you that I was to go to Pelusion with the governor; I did not go forth.
23    To Abous the veteran, from Ptolemaios his son.
