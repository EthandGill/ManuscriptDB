[TRANSLATION]
1     [ after the con]sulship of Flavius Olybrius the most glorious
2     … of the sixth indiction.
3     Flavius Samuel son of Kolloutos, his mother being Kyra, soldier
4     of the unit of the Ptolemaid nome, from the village of Tanyaithis, of the nome
5     of Apollonopolis Mikra, to Aurelius Phoibammon son of Triadelphos,
6     from the village of Aphrodite of the Antaiopolite nome, greeting. Whereas
7     all my earlier handwritten bonds remain valid, in accordance with the credit of each bond, just as all the stipulations, I acknowledge also now that I have received and
8     have been paid in full by you, as full price of clean wheat in the grain, artabas
9     thirty, total 30 artabas of wheat, and I am ready to repay these to you in the
10    time of the harvest, which by God's help is the coming seventh indiction, by the measure
11    of Victor son of Paulos, actuary of the village of Thmonachthe. But if I do not meet
12    in due time the aforesaid measure as is written above, on condition that these (artabas) be also
13    over and above my aforesaid farm-land (held) by you, together with the other earlier
14    mortgaged properties for use as security, by way of pledge, until the repayment of the aforesaid measure
15    without any dispute. This document I have made for you as security, upon my signature
16    and on being formally questioned. Flavius Samuel, soldier, the aforesaid, agrees with me, and I will repay you in due time as is set forth.
17    Aurelius Phoibammon son of Hermauos, I bear witness to the contract, having heard it
18    from the maker. Phoibammon son of Psaios, elder, I bear witness to the contract,
19    having heard it from the maker.
20    Written by me, Abraamios son of Apollos …
