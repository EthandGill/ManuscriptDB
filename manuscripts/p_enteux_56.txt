[TRANSLATION]
1     ... having rented our land, 5 arouras from
2     ... in the 4th year according to the agreement that is in place
3     ... Pelusian of the previously written partition. Since then
4     ... [K]ing, to order Diophanes the strategó to write
5     ... [agre]ement ... rental through Menandros
6     ... Farewell.
7     [To Menandros. Especially resolve their dispute; if not, send to us from the 10th of] Choiach so that for
8     the proper court they may be judged.
