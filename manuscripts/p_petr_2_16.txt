[TRANSLATION]
1     … send … now … [come] …
2     I will try then, having both [things], [to] be present. [It is] necessary then …
3     [To] remain both on account of knowledge and on account of [the business] with Diogenes …
4     [They] were given; [and] lay exposed already then into [hand(s)], and the entrance-fee …
5     In Dionysodorus' … concerning which [I] arranged and not … [being brought]; Philonides [the son], to whom [it] is [to enter toward the] king …
6     [A] noteworthy precious-stone; [it] is a sea-urchin-stone and other gift[s] … [of] Arsinoe-[type], and business concerning the house-lot has been settled …
7     And of the twentieth year, and of the twentieth [he] undertakes what he received … concerning everything …
8     [… remaining]. We will deposit moreover also this [so that nothing] troubles the house-lot …
9     And Polycrates, what [became] to-him from Philonides until now … [in] Mecheir 3
10    [Something] small he additionally owes; but concerning the twentieth to you [to be] present [I wrote]
11    [It] will be a sum from the Arsinoe-[revenues]. However surely we will inquire more precisely. [I wrote] therefore
12    To you so-that you might know. In this way then understand [more] vigor[ously] … [to] manage [all things]
13    With you being present.
