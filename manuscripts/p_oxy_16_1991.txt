[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord
2     and Master Jesus Christ
3     our God and Savior,
4     in the consulship of [the same] our most pious
5     master, year [1]9, Thoth 21,
6     of the 5th indiction.
7     To Flavius Strategius, the all-praiseworthy and most exalted consul,
8     landholder also here in the splendid
9     city of the Oxyrhynchites, through you, Flavius
10    Dorotheus, the admirable
11    count and his administrator: Aurelius
12    Georgius, son of Menas, his mother being
13    Manna, from the hamlet of
14    Chineoreon belonging to your
15    Exaltedness, of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
16    a registered cultivator of it,
17    greeting. A need having now also
18    [arisen for the landholding under my charge]
19    for an irrigation-machine called … chate,
20    drawing water onto arable land
21    of a single defective water-wheel, having gone up
22    to the city I petitioned
23    your Exaltedness to order
24    that this be furnished to me. And
25    immediately, taking forethought for the
26    organization of her own affairs,
27    this (wheel) having been purchased by me
28    in the fields, and the price for it
29    being reckoned to me against monetary (dues)
30    of the fifth indiction, a new serviceable
31    water-drawing (wheel), satisfactory, I received
32    on the present day, which is
33    Thoth 21 of the present fifth
34    indiction, for the water-supply of the crops of the sixth indiction.
35    This bond, written in single copy, is authoritative as set forth above.
36    I, Georgius son of Menas, assent
37    as set forth above. I, Serenus, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate,
38    being so.
39    A bond made by Georgius son of Menas from the hamlet of
40    Chineoreon … of one defective water-wheel.
