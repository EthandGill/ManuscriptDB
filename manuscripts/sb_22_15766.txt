[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 25, Phaophi 11
2     To the strategus of the Arsinoite nome and to the epistates of the guards
3     and to the archphylakites by division and to the epistates by village
4     and to the toparchs and topogrammateis
5     and to the komarchs and komogrammateis
6     and to the phylakites, greetings. Senoukhin,
7     whom we have appointed displaying the order to copy the fortieth
8     of the reed-fields and …
9     …ing of those dwelling in Mukhis
10    of shepherds and [he] shall select
11    the debts in them, the [debts]
12    for the royal bank. In whatever of you
13    [he] has need of the things pertaining to these matters,
14    you will do well by assisting
15    readily.
