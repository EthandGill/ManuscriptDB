[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakleides to Horion the
2     father, greeting.
3     Just as I [asked] you face-to-face …
4     for the child, seven hundred drachmas as of the greatest necessity,
5     and a pair of Italian lamps
6     up to weight of ninety minas, I [beseech],
7     and now; let [these] necessary things be a care to you [and ent]irely to entrust to me concerning [which]
8     I am in need here before you, as
9     you [will do] it gladly.
10    I wish you to be strong.
11    Of the year … of Caesar the lord
12    [Pha]ophi seventeen.
