[TRANSLATION]
1     You sent to me
2     a letter and not
3     did I find not even one
4     character, but
5     soaked the
6     letter.
7     Being asked please do not neglect
8     concerning the herb-work,
9     as I have written to you.
10    And I grant to you,
11    if it should seem good to you,
12    as you said, to sell
13    the wheat at six drachmas for the fourth year
14    you have drachmas 6.
