[TRANSLATION]
1     Zoilus, scribe of farmers, to Akoussilas, grain-collector, greetings. Measure Herak Psen into which [he farms] [royal land]
2     of wheat two and a half, totaling wheat 2½, year 20, Athyr 15.
3     Marres, village-scribe, measure the [aforementioned] wh[eat]
4     artabas two and a half, year totaling artabas 2½, year 20, Athyr 15
5     Onnophris, leader of farmers, authorize artabas 2½, year totaling artabas 2½.
6     year 20, Athyr 15.
