1     In the consulship of our masters Constantius Augustus for the 6th time and Constantius
2     the most noble Caesar for the 2nd time, Choiak 16.
3     Aurelia Ammonia also called Zois, daughter of Hermon, from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites,
4     acting by the right of children, to my children the Aurelii
5     Paulos and Dionysios, both by my deceased
6     husband Her…s, greeting. Since you, Dionysios, from long ago, from the
7     death of your father, remained with me your mother, working
8     the trade of the fullers, and not abandoning me your mother, being grateful, I acknowledge that I have made over in writing to you both, my children,
9     in common, in equal share, all that I provided and acquired
10    from my own labours — fulling-implements and pebbles and the other things belonging to this trade — so that they shall be under me for as long as I survive;
