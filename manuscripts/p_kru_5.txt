[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving consubstantial Trinity, the Father
2     and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Written in the month Phamenoth 28, indiction one.
3     We, Victor and Pelotare, the sons of the blessed Shenoute, the people of the fortress (kastron) of Djeme, of the nome of the city of Hermonthis, we are to give afterwards a subscriber to subscribe on our behalf, with trustworthy witnesses to testify to this written deed of sale,
4     which is not to be transgressed and not to be shaken aside from the laws;
5     and moreover to confirm it through other trustworthy witnesses, those who shall testify to it on our behalf, according to our own request and
6     our commission, greeting: that we acknowledge, we Victor
7     and Pelotare, the sons of the blessed Shenoute, those who first wrote to you, above, you Aaron son of the blessed Shenoute, the man of this same fortress of Djeme, who buy, and the one to whom we have first written, above,
8     according to this sale, this one which is written, valid and firm, by a single act
