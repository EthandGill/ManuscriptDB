[TRANSLATION]
1     4
2     17th year our father died
3     the common portion and the laws I gave to my brother so that he might diminish it until the 29th year; I did not know what he did, much
4     which I suffered from Sokonopios
5     after our father died
6     and the prophet, as is customary, announced the order
7     of my father, that
8     [he] owes [to] be sold. My brother did not tell me to notify [anyone] with the prophet nor indeed did the prophet.
