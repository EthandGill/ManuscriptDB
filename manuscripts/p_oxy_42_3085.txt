[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermias to Sarapion the
2     dearest, greetings.
3     Having irrationally departed, not well
4     you acted, having the price of the piglet in
5     drachmas sixty. You will do well then, having exchanged with Alexander
6     the one who gave you the letter, from whom the same
7     I already received. Do not then
8     make me …
9     irrationally, so that I do not even demand the expenses from you.
10    [Fare] well.
