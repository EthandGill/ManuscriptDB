[TRANSLATION]
1     … him through them …
2     … to him through the powers; this is the word, the …
3     … which he established beneath, and he …
4     … is in the grasp of his living godhead, that he might create every nature
5     of those that are seen and of those that are not seen by us, through your only-begotten
6     Son and the Holy Spirit, and everyone who has given their direction toward you
7     and clings to you below their hands; guard them in the ways of righteousness, save them and deliver them out of every … and of the (powers) of … the demon and the whole power of the devil; open their eyes that they may know the mystery of your law, fill (them) with the gifts of your Holy Spirit, watch
8     over them against the assault; from now make them stand against the evil one, adorning them
9     in the heavenly world, and make them spiritual, worthy of your incorruptible inheritance which shall be in Christ Jesus our Lord, through the intercessions
10    … and your Spirit, you being for us a boast
11    … our worth before the resurrection unto the Lord
12    … he … and he makes the remembrance of our wretchedness
13    he watching over you for us … a multitude of years, because we have no boast in them
14    nor any consolation … as your Spirit is with us, we worship
15    at the footstool of your holy feet, that you may make the remembrance of our wretchedness
16    in the (deeds of) might to your holy hands; your servants who are with us, the mother of Sosinne
17    and Kapai and his little brother, worship at the message of your feet
18    the holy; we are with them, hoping for salvation through your holy prayers
19    we worship at your holy feet.
