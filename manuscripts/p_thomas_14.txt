[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymus to Hatres the most [honored]
2     heartiest greetings.
3     (While) you writing I told you to rent
4     the house. You did not rent. But annoyed because of the objection,
5     on this account I withdrew. But Panbaulis having lifted the threshold of the
6     door of Maros's courtyard, declared that if is in the house,
7     and the door today is open.
8     But Antinos the of Heraiskus has secured the house and there stays [inside]. Do everything then so as to [write] or
9     [go] up concerning the house or the courtyard
10    [because] I write to you today the third
11    letter concerning this. My all greet you by [name]
12    to Hatres the wine-seller from H[orion …]
