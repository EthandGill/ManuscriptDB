[TRANSLATION]
1     of year 30 of Marcus Aurelius Commodus Antoninus
2     Augustus, Thoth 1. [it] was cancelled through Sotos and [his] associate tax-collectors
3     of silver of the village of Soknopaeus Island, Stotoetis Pabous, on behalf of completion-tax
4     of camels of the past 29th year, drachmas twenty, with additional one obol and half-obol
5     total drachmas twenty-one, obol and half-obol. Hathyr 23, on behalf of the 29th year, other drachmas ten, with additional [amount], total drachmas ten.
