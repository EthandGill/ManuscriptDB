[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most excellent council of Hermopolis the
2     great, ancient, most venerable and
3     most splendid, through Aurelius Corellius Alexandros, who has served as eutheniarch, gymnasiarch, councillor, neokoros of the
4     great Sarapis here, of equestrian rank, retired from military service, prytanis in office,
5     from Aurelius Tourbon also called Ammonios,
6     son of Ammonios son of Tourbon, and however I am styled, oil-pourer of the gymnasium here, according to
7     the things commanded through records
8     by the most excellent former epistrategos
9     Aurelius Tiron, concerning my reporting to the most excellent council whenever an anointing-festival takes place; since therefore on the third of the present month Thoth an anointing-festival took place
10    the heirs of Herakleios also called Heron being gymnasiarchs, and on the eighth, Theogition also called Dionysammon being gymnasiarch,
11    of Theogition also called Dionysammon,
12    and on the tenth, the heirs of Hermophilos also called
13    Isidoros being gymnasiarchs, and on the eleventh, Sarapion … son of …on being gymnasiarch, and on the …teenth (day), being gymnasiarch
14    … son of … ammon, and
15    … and on the twentieth, … son of Kopreus being gymnasiarch
16    and … on the … twentieth, Eudaimon son of
17    also called Hermeinos being gymnasiarch, and … and on the twentieth, being gymnasiarch
18    … son of Aquilinus, likewise also on the thirtieth
19    of the same month Thoth an anointing-festival took place
20    … being gymnasiarchs …
21    … instead of the one to be designated
22    in accordance with the record made by Tiberinus …
