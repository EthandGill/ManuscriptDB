[TRANSLATION]
1     Demetrius, councillor, overseer of the sacred [temple] of Hermes
2     of Memphis, to Sakaos and [the] joint village headmen
3     of the village of Theadelphia, greetings.
4     I have received from you the portion chosen by you of
5     the half of the laborer from the tenth [day]
6     of Thoth until the tenth of Choiak, [for] months
7     three, and moreover also the provisions
8     of the donkey [for] months three, from the tenth of Mesore until the tenth of Hathur, [in] full.
9     Demetrius, [I] have signed —.
10    to the coming consuls, the 4th [day], Thoth, the 10th.
