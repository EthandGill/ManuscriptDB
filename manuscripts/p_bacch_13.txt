[TRANSLATION]
1     To Canopus, also [Asclepiad]
2     Royal scribe of Arsinoe, Herakleides district
3     Succeeding also the generalship
4     Of the same district
5     From Sisoitus, [son] of Orsenouphis, and of the
6     Rest of the priests of god Soknobraisios, great great noted
7     Village Bacchias.
8     [We] registered for you
9     A list of priests and
10    Of the management of the present
11    Twelfth year
12    [It] was registered to the royal scribe succeeding and the generalship
13    12th year, Mesore 5.
