[TRANSLATION]
1     To the beloved father apa Papnoutios
2     Ausonius.
3     Having remembered the commands of your
4     piety I sent-for Horos the
5     from Philonikos and I reminded him,
6     but Gallus I deemed [worthy] in all things to remind me
7     so that in the [possible] things the from-me earnestness
8     I might show; [so that] also concerning this [matter] and concerning all
9     you might command I ask [you] the great matters. Remember then
10    me in your holy prayers. [Being] strong, you
11    and praying for us,
12    God preserve, beloved father.
13    To the beloved father apa Papnoutios
14    Ausonius.
