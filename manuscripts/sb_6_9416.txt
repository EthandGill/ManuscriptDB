[TRANSLATION]
1     In year 4, Phamenoth 10, [it] has fallen
2     Upon the great table in the city of Zeus
3     Through Theon, handler of myrrh, fourth
4     For the 4th year, Glaukos Psenchonsios
5     One drachma [and] four obols; Chairemon.
