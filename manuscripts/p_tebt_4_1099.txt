1     M[egchis], komogrammateius of Kerkeosires of Polemon's district, to Horus
2     greetings. When I was in Ptolemais [Eu]ergetes regarding the [submission] of the [demanded] accounts, [it happened to us that] the royal farmers from the village
3     had withdrawn to the sanctuary at Narmouthis. I thought it good [to] report [this] to you so that you might know.
4     Farewell. Year 4 Phaophi 20.
