[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of the Flavii Zeno and Marcian the most illustrious,
2     Phaophi 22, of the 8th indiction.
3     To Flavia Kyria the most illustrious and most noble landowner
4     in the splendid and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     the Aurelii Pasoerios son of Kornelios and Ioannes son of Phoibammon,
6     registered cultivators from the hamlet of Chairas, an estate of
7     your Excellency, of the same nome: since a need has now
8     arisen for the landowner's irrigation-machine under us
9     called Pso, which draws water onto arable land, of one
10    axle, having come up to the city we petitioned
11    your Illustriousness that this be provided,
12    and immediately your Nobility provided to us
13    that same new satisfactory water-drawing axle
14    for the replenishment of the machine-implements,
15    which, having received on this present day
16    which is Phaophi the second and twentieth of the eighth
17    indiction, of the cultivation of the ninth assessment,
18    we further agree, by mutual surety, to make the water-drawings
19    and the water-supplies blamelessly. The bond is
20    authoritative, written in a single copy, and on being formally questioned we acknowledged. The Aurelii Pasoerios
21    son of Kornelios and Ioannes son of Phoibammon, we have made the
22    bond, having received the new axle, and everything agrees
23    with us as set forth above. Aurelius Sarapammon son of Theon,
24    I wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
25    Bond for one axle …
