[TRANSLATION]
1     Diogenes, also called Sarapion, [son] of Sarapion of Diogenes
2     mother Didyme daughter of Euphran[or], of those from Oxyrhynchus city, Persian of the [later] generation, [greets] Ammonius
3     [son] of Sarapion of Ammonius, of those from the same
4     city, greetings. I acknowledge having received and measured out beside you grain artabas one hundred sevent[y], [these are] of grain artabas one hundred seventy principal,
5     which I will measure on your behalf into the public [storehouse] and will return to you the land-tax on this pure from all expenses in the month Pauni of the present sev[enth] year of Trajan Caesar the lord, without
6     all delay; but if I do not measure out
7     I will pay with interest [at a half again], the recovery being yours
8     from both me and from all my possessions just as from a judgment, you not being diminished in what I owed to the brother
9     your the one who has departed Pausanias in the recovery.
10    valid [is] the autograph of me Diogenes also called Sarapion
11    hand everywhere being attached, and to everyone bringing [it]. [Year] seventh of Emperor Caesar
12    Nerva Trajan Augustalis Germanicus Dacicus
13    Hathyr 29.
14    Written agreement of Diogenes also called Sarapion the exegete
15    of grain artabas 170
