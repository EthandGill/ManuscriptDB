[TRANSLATION]
1     They wrote [on] Phamenoth 14 of the 4th year
2     heirs of Paniskos, son of Ammonios
3     on behalf of sacred tax [assessed] and other collections of the 4th year
4     drachmas four, total drachmas 4. Aurelius Dorion I have signed.
