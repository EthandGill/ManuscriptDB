[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymus, son of Longinus, [to] Amath[is], son of Sokmenis,
2     and to Pekysis, son of Heron, and to the remaining elders
3     of the village of Philadelphia; I swear by the [fortune of the] lords
4     Emperors Se[verus] and Antoninus
5     and Geta, their fortune, [that] these sheep
6     [are] not these 100, but [those] of Mystes the exegetes,
7     concerning which you wish [to make] demand [on] me
8     for account of weighing. If moreover I am found
9     selling wool, let me be bound by the oath. Nilus, [his] son
10    … having served as exegetes of the Arsinite [people],
11    [city], I wrote on behalf of Didymus [his] whole body.
