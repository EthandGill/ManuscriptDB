[TRANSLATION]
1   Dorion, former exegetes, and however
2   I am styled, to Apion also called Dionysios, former gym-
3   nasiarch and however you are styled, greeting. I have
4   bought from you the green crops you hold on lease
5   within the embankment-district called
6   Sampsouchinos, for 1720 dr., of which I have trans-
7   ferred to you 600 dr., and the remaining 1120 dr. I will trans-
8   fer to the agents of the heirs
9   of Aurelius Antiochos, in accordance with the
10   lease made to you, and their receipt
11   I will deliver to you. Year 13 of Aurelius
12   Antoninus Caesar the lord,
13   Phamenoth 12.
