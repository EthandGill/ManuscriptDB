1     … the public counting-house
2     [of Hermoupolis, for] every gold title, one and a half carats of gold,
3     [and for the embolê of grain] each year, half an artaba; total: half an artaba of grain and one [and a half] carats of gold,
4     [of this tax, for] the annona-dues and gold-dues of every kind of these
5     titles, to be discharged by you each year for ever; and that it be permitted
6     to you for ever to own this aroura and to be master of it and to administer [and]
7     to manage and to possess and to acquire, or also to lease for tribute and to rent out and … [to give as a gift]
8     and to alter and to dispose of and to exchange and to bequeath to whatever persons
9     you wish, without hindrance and without impediment and without challenge, under compulsion by no one
10    at all of those of whatever degree of our kinship or strangers who may appear,
11    of which then the possession and the use and the ownership and the usufruct
12    and its revenue of every kind we have handed over to you to receive just now
13    for ever, completely, not subject beforehand to any debt whatever, private
14    or public, nor through any charge or claim or surety, but unimpaired
15    and clear and profitable, free of any danger whatever, except indeed
16    the annual provision made by you of the aforesaid tax now assessed upon you
17    by us, namely of half only of an artaba [of grain]
18    of pure fiscal register, with its freight-charges and its hundredths and twentieths and additional charges of every kind,
19    and one and a half carats of gold for the annona-dues and gold-titles of every kind,
20    you, the aforesaid Victor, who have purchased it, beginning [for]
21    the said aroura to deposit the whole [tax-payment] from the produce of the
22    fourth indiction entering in good fortune, both of it and of the time following, and [after you]
23    your heirs for ever, and to discharge it without fail
24    yearly for this to the public counting-house of the same Hermoupolis the [by] you
25    [now] made sole tax-payment of the same single aroura, it not being liable
26    to any other tax-payment whatever or addition of public dues, but freed
27    from all participation of fellow-slaves' liturgy, of any other on behalf of our possessions,
28    even though, of the public payments of it for the past time
29    up to the present third indiction and itself, it belongs to us, the
30    ones selling this to you, and to our heirs for ever, for which we gathered in its fruits. And we are ready, both we and
31    our heirs of every kind, [to turn away] every man who shall come against
32    you and your heirs, in whatever manner and time and place,
33    in whatever court or also outside court, small or great, local
34    or foreign, on account of this, to drive off [and]
35    to turn away from you and your heirs,
36    at our own expenses and costs; and we shall guarantee to you with every guarantee
37    and warranting against everyone coming against you or laying claim
38    at any season, under compulsion. And if we care less for the guaranteeing, [triple]
39    we shall pay back the valuation, with also the surplus on account of the penalty of the transgression,
40    of the spent agreed price, and after these things the
41    [present sale] is to be valid as it is and secure and strong, [everywhere]
42    presented [as in] a public archive subscribed, and …
43    we have issued it to you [and pledged], for the guarantee of this sale
44    and warranting, all the goods now ours and to be ours. [And]
45    upon all the things written, being questioned, we have agreed that these things are so, [to give,]
