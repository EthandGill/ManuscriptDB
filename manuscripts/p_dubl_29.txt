[TRANSLATION]
1     [we have been satisfied we] Aioulus son of Pekusios
2     [and] … son of Panaues, chief servant, from you abba
3     … iou the most God-fearing elder and leader
4     [of the blessed monas]tery of abba Isaac the according-to-custom
5     [given] from the same blessed monastery on account
6     [as] wage of the blessed Anoup having been [a] cubicle-keeper
7     from Hiera, that is [I speak] for the present first indiction
8     [of] gold coin [the] half as it is accounted,
9     [of] gold coin 1/2 as it is accounted. It was written in [the] month Mesore [the] eighth of the same
10    first indiction. Through me Iustus notary it was subscribed.
