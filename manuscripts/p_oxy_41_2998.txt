1     Aurelius Zōilos, [son] of Theon, gymnasiarch,
2     councillor of the illustrious and most illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, and as I am styled, to Aurelius Kopre, [son] of Pastoites, [his] mother Helen[e], from the same city, greetings.
3     I have sold to you a male camel of mine,
4     dark-colored, having a mark
5     on the right side of the neck,
6     [marked] and on the jaw 7, [for] a price
7     [of the thing] agreed [upon between]
8     each other, of silver imperial coinage, two talents, which right there
9     I received from you by hand from
10    [in] full. The same camel
11    from this point you have received from me
12    in the same city, this one being such
13    unencumbered and uncontested
