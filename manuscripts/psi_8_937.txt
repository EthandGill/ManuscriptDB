1     The holy catholic new church through me Iōsēphios, presbyter and administrator, to Apollōs Dioskoros [greetings].

3     with god coming first indiction, gold coins two minus karats four; [they] total gold coins 2 minus karats [4];
4     and you have paid into the public [treasury] on the third payment of the public canon of the fourteenth [indiction],
