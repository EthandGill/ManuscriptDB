[TRANSLATION]
1     Pasis to Zenon greetings. ... [concerning] the vetch. Having given therefore to Apollodotus the letter ... [to Heraclea] city before ... [to do]; and until now not ... [illegible] ... and to Timothy we gave
2     the letter to him and he wrote back to you. And the 2 artabas from Maimakhos
3     I have and the [one] from you has been conveyed to you. And if the remaining [ones] do not send, write
4     to us how we might buy together; for we have found being sold here. This [letter]
5     and this letter I wrote to you in Philadelphia. We caught you having sailed down. And seeing the land [fleeing beneath] ... [we] bought together
6     as much as the land needed. We have therefore written to you in order that you might know.
7     Farewell. Year 35. Phaophi 16.
8     To Zenon.
