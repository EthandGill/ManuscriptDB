[TRANSLATION]
1     of the city at the district Poimenike
2     until the repayment and full completion
3     of the same eight coins and the
4     difference of them. Valid [is] the security-document
5     [written as] a simple document [and when] asked [under oath] I acknowledged [it].
6     Menas, lead-worker, son of the blessed
7     Pabbaous, the aforementioned one, have written
8     this document according to the foregoing
9     account as it is set forth.
