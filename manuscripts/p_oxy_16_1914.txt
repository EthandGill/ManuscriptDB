[TRANSLATION]
1     [They make] of expenditures of the 5th indiction: of wheat by the kankellos 1330 1/2 1/4 artabas 6 choenices, and of barley 30 artabas, and of nomismata 46, less 1/2 1/3, at 12 (carats), 96 (carats), at 66 1/4, 84 1/4, into
2     [nomismata 2 1/2,] less 1/4, 96, the net nomismata 44 1/6. Against which, of receipts of the same 5th indiction: of wheat by the kankellos 1342 artabas 8 choenices, and
3     [with the surcharge, nomismata] 284 1/2 [1/3], 24, 96, and without surcharge nomismata 105 1/2 1/3, less 180, and of barley by measure 30 artabas.
4     So that there remain of wheat by the kankellos 11 1/4 artabas 2 choenices and with surcharge nomismata 240 5/6 24 96 and without surcharge no[mismata] 105 1/2 1/3, less 180.
5     They make of wheat by the kankellos 11 1/4 ar[tabas] 2 cho[enices], the impure (sum) from the hundredths being 6 1/2 artabas 1/2 of an artaba 8 choenices, they make of impure wheat by the kankellos 12 artabas, the same also sold to the most illustrious
6     Eustathius the loading-officer and given to Andronikos the sailor, and the price was deposited with the
7     most illustrious Anastasius the banker. And there remain of monetary (dues) with surcharge nomismata 240 5/6 24 96 and without surcharge nomismata 105 1/2 1/3, less 180.
8     Of which there was deposited with the most illustrious Anastasius the banker in various payments thus:
9     in the month Choiak 26 of the 5th indiction, with surcharge nomismata 118, less 583 carats; Pharmouthi 12 of the 5th indiction, with surcharge nomismata 112, less 660;

12    Tybi 17 of the 5th indiction, through those from Sepho, without surcharge nomismata 18, less 74 1/4; Pharmouthi 18 of the 5th indiction, without surcharge nomismata 16, less 24;
13    to the same banker, without surcharge nomismata 11; Phaophi of the 6th indiction, for the 5th, without surcharge nomismata 3, 48 96.
