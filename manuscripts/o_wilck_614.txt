[TRANSLATION]
1     Pamonthes and Postumus, collectors of money-taxes
2     of Charax, to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis, (whose) mother
3     is Senchesthotis. We have received for the assessment anak( )
4     of year 6, base drachmas seven, 2 obols, total drachmas 7, 2 obols.
5     Year 6 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
6     Epeiph 17. I, Postumus, through Karo( ) the assistant, have signed.
