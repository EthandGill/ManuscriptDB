[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our masters Honorius for the 9th time
2     and Theodosius for the 4th time, the eternal Augusti, Thoth 30.
3     To the heirs of Phoibammon of illustrious memory,
4     former duces, landholders in the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     from Aurelius Helios son of Kollouthos, from the hamlet of Gnapheus of the same nome.
6     Of my own free will I undertake to lease, from the present year
7     89/58 until the sowing of the twelfth indiction, from the property belonging to you in the fields of the village of Plelo, of the holding of Paeianos
8     called Al… , a fully equipped waterwheel, of arouras
9     thirty and one half and one quarter, so as to sow in wheat arouras
10    fifteen, at a fixed rent of one hundred and twenty artabas of grain,
11    and in barley five arouras, the rent of these being forty artabas of barley,
12    and in vegetables two arouras, the rent of these
13    being six and one half artabas of vegetables and four choinikes, and
14    of the remainder, in grass, eight and one half and one quarter arouras — you to have
15    in grass four and one quarter and one eighth arouras —
16    the rent being free of all risk, the public taxes
17    being chargeable to you the landholders, but it being incumbent on me to provide
18    the rent by the receiving measure at the proper season
19    without delay, and to perform the raising of the embankment-dues. The lease is valid, and on being formally questioned I gave my assent. Aurelius
20    Helias son of Kollouthos, I have leased and I shall pay the rent
21    as set out above. I, Aurelius … son of …, wrote on his behalf
22    since he does not know letters.
23    … …
24    Lease of a waterwheel of the holding of Phaeianos, to Helias son of Kollouthos, from Gnapheus.
25    … …
