[TRANSLATION]
1     … [In the] consulship of Flavius [Philoxenus the most illustrious, Hathyr 14, 4th indiction.]
2     [To] Flavius Strategios [the most glorious and most magnificent ex-consul]
3     [and] general, landowner [also here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites,]
4     [through] Menas his servant, who [also asks and procures for his own]
5     master the same most magnificent [man the action and obligation,]
6     Aurelius Anoup, son of Diogenes, whose mother is … [originating]
7     from the hamlet of Metrodoros of the Oxyrhynchite nome, an estate [of your]
8     Magnificence, a registered farmer, greeting. A need having arisen [now also]
9     for my landlord's water-machine under my charge, called
10    Kaine, drawing water for the vineyard and for arable land, of [one]
11    axle, having gone up to the city I asked your [Magnificence]
12    to order this to be provided to me. And immediately your
13    Magnificence, taking thought for the maintenance
14    of her own affairs, ordered this to be given to me
15    from the new ones brought from the great city
16    of Alexandria, which new, suitable, water-drawing,
17    satisfactory one I received for the completion of all the
18    mechanical implements on the present day, which is
19    Hathyr the fourteenth of the current year 202=171 of the
20    [present] fourth indiction, [of the water-supply.]
21    The handwriting of Anoup son of Diogenes, from the hamlet of Metrodoros, [for the receipt of one axle.]
