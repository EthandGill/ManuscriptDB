[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Hymerios to his own Pitheron, greetings.
2     provide to my lords those concerning
3     Herodian [and] the provincial [officers] of wine
4     doubles ten [spathes] ten of honey
5     one knidion, equals 10 doubles 10 [spathes] of honey knidion [1].
6     [Epiphi] 20 of the magistracy of Iachios.
