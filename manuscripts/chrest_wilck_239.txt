[TRANSLATION]
1     Papontos the elder, son of Auneis son of Auneis,
2     his mother being Tapontos, … years old, concerning whom the keepers of the archives reported thus: we declare that there is registered through the property-register of the
3     village of Paponton, Auneis son of Auneis, as purchaser, having in the return, in the
4     … year of Domitian, in the village of Kerkesoucha Orous a house and courtyard and all the appurtenances; and the village-scribe declared likewise that the aforementioned property does not belong to the above-written man,
5     but to one of the same name, Papontos the younger, son of Auneis son of Auneis, his mother being Tapontos.
6     We declare that the village-scribe of Kerkesoucha Orous ought, concerning
7     this matter, to make report, because the property there has been declared.
8     Herakleides son of Kronion son of Kronion, his mother being Taorsis, 6… years old,
9     concerning whom the keepers of the archives declared thus: we declare that there is registered
10    through the property-register of the village, Herakles son of Kronion son of
11    Kronion, as purchaser, having in the return in the 12th year of Domitian,
12    … a portion of vineyard land of 2 arouras, concerning which the village-scribe likewise declared that the registered property does not belong
13    to the above-written man, but to one of the same name, his mother being S…
14    We declare the aforesaid: a portion of vineyard land of 2 arouras does not belong to the registered man,
15    but to one of the same name, his mother being S…
