[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: He[liod]oros, son of Didymos, the son of Hephaistion, of the Sosicosmian tribe and of the Althaean deme, to Stotoetis, son of Stotoetis, greetings. I acknowledge that I have sold to you a white male camel, which has not shed its first teeth, branded on its right haunch with a Sigma and a distinguishing mark on the same haunch and on the right of its neck a Sigma and on its chest a burn mark and I have received from you the price agreed upon of six hundred and eighty silver drachmas, (=) 680 drachmas, and I shall guarantee (the sale) to you with every guarantee. Year 23 of Imperator Caesar Tit[us]Ael[ius Hadri]anus An[toninus] Augustus Pius .
