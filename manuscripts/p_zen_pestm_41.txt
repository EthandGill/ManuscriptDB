r.1    Παῧς Ζήνωνι χαίρειν. σύ μʼ

[TRANSLATION]
1     Paous to Zenon greetings. You me
2     raised in Egypt, you are able
3     me also here to raise. You said to me
4     to go into the accounting office and to seek Heliodorus. This man is able
5     me to set as equipment-keeper
6     Amynandros is trireme-commander of the
7     nine-oared [vessel]. You then to me send
8     [so] that there may be to me [the advantage of] speed, so that
9     not another may pass me by
10    Fare[well]
11    Paous
