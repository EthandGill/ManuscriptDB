1     to those who have received the sixth of the donkeys
2     of the Hermopolite from Sarapion of Eutuchides
3     of Hermopolis. I register for the present
4     fourth year of Hadrian Caesar the lord which I have
5     donkeys male foals two not working
6     for hire but for private [need]. year fourth of Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus
7     Phaophi 5
8     Dionysius tax collector sixth of donkeys of the fourth year of Hadrian Caesar
