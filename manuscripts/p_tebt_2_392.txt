1     [In the nine]teenth [year] of Emperor
2     [Caesar T]rajan Hadrian Augustus
3     … [in T]ebtynis of the division of Polemon
4     [of the Arsinoi]te nome. Kronion acknowledges,
5     [son of Kronion,] son of Heron, aged about twenty
6     … [with a sca]r on the right shin,
7     [to his] full sister by the same father and mother,
8     [Eud]aimo[nis] also called Politte, aged
9     [about for]ty-six, with a scar on the right knee,
10    [with as guardia]n her own son Eu[t]ychos,
11    [son of Horion,] aged about twenty, with a scar on his
12    [left] hand, that Kronion is [u]nder obligation
13    [to keep] his sister Eudaimonis
14    [also called Poli]tte unmolested and
15    [free from exaction] and undisturbed
16    in every way in respect of the things for which
17    [her husband writes,] and (Kronion) being also brother of Horion
18    [who] has died, to Tao[t]ion, daughter of Herakleides,
19    [according to] an acknowledgment of deposit (parathēkē) in the
20    [thi]rd year of Hadrian Caesar the lord,
21    on Me[s]ore the new-moon, through the record-office here,
22    of five quarters of assayed gold and of [silv]er
23    struck and of other things which the acknowledgment
24    contains, because Kronion had received these for his own (use), while Horion,
25    having received absolutely nothing, was written in with them for the sake of accommodation. Kronion, son of Kronion: I acknowledge that I will keep my sister Eudaimonis, th[e one] also called Politta,
26    unmolested and free from exaction and undisturbed in every way in respect of
27    the things for which our brother Horion writes to Taotion, daughter of Herakleides, according to a deposit, on the terms whi[ch]
28    it contains, because I had received (them) for my
29    own (use), while Horion, having received absolutely nothing, was written in with them for the sake of accommodation, as is set forth above. There wrote on hi[s be]half
30    Sagathes, son of Areios, since he does not know le[tters.]
31    [It has been re]gistered thr[ough th]e record-off[ic]e in Tebtynis.
