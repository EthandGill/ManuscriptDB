[TRANSLATION]
1     In the 17th year, Thoth 28.
2     Petesouchos
3     son of Phibis, and his brothers, sold
4     the half belonging to them
5     of the dovecote which is
6     in the northern part
7     of Pathyris, for 3 (talents) of copper. Horos
8     son of Nechoutes, bought it.
9     In the reign of Cleopatra and king Ptolemy who is called Alexander,
10    the son of the Philometor gods, in the 17th year, Thoth 28, in Pathyris, under Ammonios who is
11    deputy of Paniskos, agoranomos of the upper toparchy. Petesouchos son of Phibis sold,
12    a Persian of the epigone, about 30 years old, of large stature, honey-skinned, slightly curly-haired, long-faced, straight-[nosed],
13    and Panebchounis his brother and Horos his brother, about 20 years old, with as guardian their own brother
14    Petesouchos the aforesaid and joint-vendor, and Thaibis his sister and Sennesis his s[ister],
15    the two of them daughters of Phibis, Persian women, their mother Thausiris, a Pe[rsian woman], joining in authorizing the sale,
16    with as guardian Panebchounis, out of the dovecote belonging to them which is
17    in the northern part of Pathyris, the half; the neighbours of the whole dove[cote being]:
18    to the south the hou[se] of Horos son of Nechoutes, to the north and west the ro[ya]l street, to the [eas]t the house
19    of Phibis [son of Ne]choutes, or whoever the [neighbours may be on all] sides. Hor[os son of Ne]choutes bought it,
20    a Per[s]ian [of the epi]gone, about [43 years old, of middle height, honey]-skinned, [slightly curly-haired,] long-faced, straight-[nosed, for] copper
21    three thousand drachmas. The vendors' agents and guarantors of [all things pertaining to this sale]
22    are Petesouchos and his brothers the vendors, whom [Horos the buyer accepted].
23    I, Ammo[nios,] deputy of Panisk[os, have transacted the deed].
