[TRANSLATION]
1   Has paid: Amenothes son of Pikos, for poll-tax
2   of year 4, ten drachmas, bath-tax one, an obol, a half-obol — total 11 (dr.) 1½ ob. — and surcharges at an obol (and) half-obol.
3   Year 4 of Gaius Caesar Imperator
4   Augustus, month Dios …, the Sebaste day.
5   Petemenophis(?) son of Pikos.
