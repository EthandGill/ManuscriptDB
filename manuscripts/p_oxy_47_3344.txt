1     To Didymos, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite.
2     I, Onnophris son of Patsibthis, my mother being
3     Sintheus, residing in the
4     hamlet of Lenon, appointed by
5     the village-scribe of the locality to the guardianship of the laws (nomophylakia), swear by the fortune of the lords
6     the Emperors Severus and Antoninus
7     and of Geta Caesar, that I shall straightway
8     undertake the aforesaid
9     duty and shall carry it out, being present and attending diligently [so as]
10    to be blamed in nothing; or may I be liable to the oath. And I have provided
11    as my own surety
12    Pauseiris son of Paesis, his mother being
13    Thaesis, residing
14    in the same hamlet, present and consenting. Year 16
15    of the Emperors Caesars Lucius
16    Septimius Severus Pius
17    Pertinax Arabicus Adiabenicus
18    Parthicus Maximus and Marcus
19    [A]urel[i]us Antoninus

26    as aforesaid. I, Pt[o]lemaios
