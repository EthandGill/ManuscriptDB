[TRANSLATION]
1     In the month Sebastos 19, he owes 5 obols.
2     In the fifth year of Tiberi[us Clau]dius Caesa[r Augustus]
3     Germanicus Imperator, in the month Sebastos on the nine[teen]th, in Theadelphia of the Themistos division of the A[rsino]ite nome. There leased Herakleia daughter of Chares, aged about
4     twenty-two, scar on the left cheek, with [the]
5     guardian, her own husband Apollonios son of Isidoros, [aged about th]irty-three, scar on the left eyebrow, to Aunes son of Harthotes,
6     a Persian of the epigone, aged about twenty-five, scar under
7     the left eyebrow, the catoecic holding belonging to her around Theadelphia of six arouras or however many
8     they may be in a single parcel. This (is) the lease: for four years
9     from the present fifth year of Tiberius Claudius
10    Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, the rent
11    for the first year for each aroura, together with that which the
12    lessee shall receive, of one artaba of wheat, of five artabas
13    of wheat, and for the remaining three years each year for each
14    aroura, together with which the lessee shall receive of seed of one artaba of wheat, of six artabas of wheat, and
15    of extra dues each year one artaba of bread, but all
16    the rents each year by the dromos four-choinix measure of the granary of Eisis, free of all deduction
17    and exempt from all risk, from every risk. But the works,
18    all of them, of the holding — the dyke-works and the irrigation-works
19    and the weeding and the other agricultural services,
20    all of them — let the lessee perform each year at his own expense,
21    and he shall leave fallow each year the half part of the holding
22    in fodder or vetch for the grazing and folding of sheep. Let it not therefore be permitted to the lessee within the
23    time to abandon the lease [nor to sub-lease to anyone] else, but the
24    yearly rents let the lessee repay to Herakleia
25    in [the month Pauni in the village], and after the aforesaid time
26    let the lessee hand over to Herakleia the holding
27    clean of rush, reed, couch-grass and all moisture.
28    But if the lessee transgresses any of these, let him pay both the
29    damages and the expenses double and a penalty of 5 [talents] of copper, the
30    right of execution belonging to Herakleia against the lessee, and let him guarantee the lease also from public (claims). The contract is valid.
31    I, Herakleia daughter of Chares, with my guardian, my husband
32    Apollonios son of Isidoros, have leased (it) as [is set fort]h. I, Apollonios, have written on behalf of
33    my wife, [she not knowing] letters.
34    I, Aunes son of Har[thot]es, a Persian of the epigone, have leased
35    from Herakleia the aforewritten holding of six arouras or however many
36    [they may be around Theadel]phia for 4 years from the present year,
37    the rent for the first year for each aroura, together with that which I shall receive, of one artaba of wheat, of five artabas of wheat, and for the remaining
38    three years each year for each aroura, together with which I shall receive
39    of one artaba of wheat, of six artabas of wheat, all by the
40    d[romos four]-cho[inix measure of the granary] [of E]isis [and of extra dues]
41    one artaba of bread …
42    A[u]nes the Per[sian o]f the epigone, of 5 talents.
