[TRANSLATION]
1     [I received and I was pai]d, I Theophania, daughter of the blessed Apollos, without a husband, [acting as]
2     [from the Arsinoite ci]ty from [the] district of Alypius from you Victor, the most admirable surveyor, son of Andrew [the] secretary
3     … [the price] of the … [sold] to you by me of garments, that is: one foreign caracallion,
4     … three linen cloths, men's embroidered [items] four, under-tunics five, shrouds tw[o], vestments
5     … , woolen [items] two, one shirt and one linen cloth, one censer in full and wholly,
6     [gold coins five] tarnished, gold [coins] 5 tarnished, and for [the] rest I have no account, neither I nor my heirs [have any claim] against you concerning th[ese] … of them the total. It was written in the month Athur 5 of the second indiction. Through me Phib, notary.
