[TRANSLATION]
1     Ariston to Zenon, greetings. We have sent [to you a certain person]
2     who will carry what we purchased from the …
3     into these things, you will do well assisting [him] …
4     If anything should happen to you of the matters concerning us …
5     at the same time and carefully.
6     To Zenon.
