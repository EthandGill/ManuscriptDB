1     … Hier[ax] ordered by you
2     from those around Lampon to receive accounts
3     [which] they owe of the [elapsed] and of the
4     present rental account …
5     Hierax the dagger-bearer
6     likewise from the dike-overseers an account of five-days.
7     I pray for you to be well
8     my lord.
9     [To Apollonius] the strategos [of the Apollonopolite] [Seven]-villages.
