[TRANSLATION]
1     The most honored servant signified nothing at all to me [on behalf] of your village, and
2     behold the great proprietors of the city, namely the property of the most [glorious] [from] prefects Julianus,
3     contributing to the village levy has already filled the measure [of it] [and] behold has done to me
4     no care concerning them. So that you might know that if not [through the] tomorrow you deliver to me
5     the measure of both the civic and the village [from] in full, [not] [you will have] [to give thanks] to the
6     matter, behold then, I have written testifying [to you]. [At the same time] but Apollos understood, since necessarily I want to speak to
7     [to] the most esteemed Dioskor[os] and Apollon[ios] proto-village-headmen of Menas.
