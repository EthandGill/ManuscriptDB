[TRANSLATION]
1     To my master, as truly in all respects to me most wondrous and virtuous landholder Leon of Sion, greetings.
2     Kopreus the cobbler and Maximos, having set out
3     from Sinaru of the village, having stirred up a fight
4     against each other in the middle of the village between
5     me and the liturgists, on account of hides
6     that were stolen, and many having been stripped bare
7     and naked in body each fell upon
8     the other; and if divine providence had not helped and the help of the liturgists, they [would have] each [other …]
9     to destroy, and the entire village, [our] own, was desolated
10    by them. Your [excellence], therefore, may [you condescend] to send [here two] companions …
