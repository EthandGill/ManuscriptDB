[TRANSLATION]
1     Germanus to Apollonius the
2     most honored, greetings.
3     I sent to you through Apollonius the
4     small silver drachmas twenty
5     along with which I gave to you in Lycon city
6     drachmas one hundred. You will do well then, brother, having sent to me through him or through whom you might wish akouchous
7     fifteen. And I also urge you to entrust to me, concerning what
8     you wish, as most pleasantly I will accomplish.
9     I pray for your well-being,
10    most honored my brother.
11    Farewell.
12    Hathyr 12
13    To Apollonius the strateg[os] of Apollonopolis from Germa[nus]
