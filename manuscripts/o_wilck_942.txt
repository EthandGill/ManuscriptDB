[TRANSLATION]
1     Measure of the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 18
2     of the Aurelii Antoninus
3     and Commodus, Caesars,
4     the lords, Pauni 18, for Charax, in the name
5     of Horos, likewise (son of) Pikos, of wheat two, total wheat 2.
6     I, Amonios, have signed.
7     Another, Mesore 4, likewise, of wheat two-thirds,
8     total wheat ⅔. I, Amonios, have signed.
