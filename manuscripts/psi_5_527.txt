[TRANSLATION]
1     A petition to Zenon from Glaukios. I had 12 donkey-saddles,
2     and 16 girdles, and 30 cloths.
3     Of these, 2 pack-animals with 2 saddles were sent to Philines,
4     and 6 cloths and 2 girdles.
5     And to Numenios the groom [were sent] 2 saddles,
6     and 2 girdles and 4 cloths; of which
7     the saddles remain, but the cloths have worn out.
8     And a saddle was given to Dorion from Memphis through Theopompos, and 3 cloths; of which
9     2 were brought back to me along with the saddle.
10    And 5 pack-animals were given to the hunters; of which
11    3 cloths are unaccounted for.
12    There are 14 cloths [accounted for], remaining 16.
13    But the saddles and girdles remain.
