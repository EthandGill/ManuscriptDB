[TRANSLATION]
1     Maron and Marres who have undertaken [the portrait-painting]
2     and the gold-working of the Arsinoite [nome] into the 23rd year
3     [Pha]trei and Psenobaste, greetings. We have permitted
4     you to work the portrait-painting craft
5     and the gold-working of the sacred and the
6     funerary faces of the nome. Similarly indeed
7     and it will be permitted to you to travel through the nome
8     [you] being assigned each month to the
9     royal bank from the month of Thoth until Mesore
10    each month of bronze/copper ... one hundred; [it] amounts to 100
11    of the 12th month drachmas [As] and of the intercalary days 5 the [amount] falling [due].
12    Whichever month you do not pay, you will owe the half-additional [penalty]
13    immediately. And it will be permitted to you to buy
14    wherever you may choose.
15    Maron Iranouphos
