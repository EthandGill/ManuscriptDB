[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Year 11 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, Phamenoth 26. Pakysis, son of Pakysis, the son of Herieus, from Soknopaiou Nesos, paid to Aelius Eutyches alias Kleon, nomarch, through Claudius Tyrannos, twenty four drachmas twelve obols for the tax on a calf, which is to be sacrificed for Soknopaios on the same day in Soknopaiou Nesos.
