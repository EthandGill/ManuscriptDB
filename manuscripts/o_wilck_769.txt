[TRANSLATION]
1     Bakchios, the agent of Eponychos, to Sosibios
2     (son of) Harbechis, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from you what you owed
3     to Eponychos himself (son of) Phatres, in the name of Epikrates
4     son of Ammonios, according to the bank-order, of pure wheat,
5     three artabas, total wheat 3 artabas, in accordance with the bank-order; and I make no
6     claim against you. Year 1 of Gaius Caesar Germanicus,
7     the New Augustus, Imperator, Pauni 28.
