[TRANSLATION]
1     [Extract from the] library of acquisitions of the Oxyrhynchite,
2     [of agoranomic transactions,] of the middle toparchy. In the fourth year
3     [of Imperator Caesar] Domitian Augustus Germanicus, Mecheir 2,
4     [in the village of Nemera. Panemgeus] son of Petseirion son of Kouos, [lent],
5     [his mother being …], from the village of Sestha of the lower toparchy,
6     [residing in] the aforesaid village of Nemera, to his own son
7     [Apollonios, whose mother was] …onios daughter of Apollonios, a Persian of the epigone,
8     [of imperial] silver coinage five hundred drachmas,
9     [total 500 drachmas of principal,] to which nothing at all has been added, which the [aforesaid A]pollonios is to repay to his father Panemgeus on the 30th of Phamenoth
10    [month …] of Imperator Caesar Domitian Augustus Germanicus, without
11    [any delay. But if] he does not repay as it is written, let Apollonios pay to his father Panemgeus the aforesaid
12    [principal with the half-again] together with the interest due for the time overrun, he having the right of execution both from the borrower [and from] all his property, as by legal judgment.
13    … Panemgeus, about 70 years old, with a scar on the middle of the nose. Apollos
14    … [with a scar on the] left shin. The writer, Le…s, about 25 years old, without distinguishing marks.
