r.1    ἔτους ε Μεχεὶρ κϛ. τέτακται ἐπὶ τὴν ἐν Ἑρμώνθει τράπεζαν ἐφʼ ἧς διονύσιος δεκάτης ἐγκυκλίου ὠνῆς κατὰ διαγραφὴν

r.5    Διονύσιος τραπεζίτης.

[TRANSLATION]
1     In year 5, Mechir 26, [it] has been arranged at the bank in Hermionthis on which Dionysius [handles] the tithe of the circulating purchase according to the written agreement
2     Psenchonsius, under which Asclepiades the notary signs, Panas son of Pechytus, and Harsiesis and Horus, brothers
3     of a cleared house of which the walls [remain] and entrance and exit, which they bought from Pabourius
4     and Taphiomis of Psenchonsius and Petobasius son of Psenenuphis, copper one talent, tax 20
5     Dionysius, banker
