# HGV-EN: Panouphis, son of Tesenouphis, to Taouetis, daughter of Satabous, mother Segathis, from the village of Soknopaiou Nesos, greetings. I acknowledge that I have sold to you two female camels, both of them branded on the right haunch, and I have received the price agreed upon of a thousand three hundred and forty silver drachmas, = 1340 drachmas, on the spot, just as they are, unreturnable. Year 13 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, M[es]ore 4.
