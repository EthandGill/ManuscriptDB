[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] reign [of] our master [Flavius] Justinian the eternal and Emperor Augustus, in the twelfth year, in the consulship of Flavius Ioannes the most glorious, five days before the Ides of August, in the year of the province
2     [the four hundred and thirty-third, in the month of Loos] on the twenty-first, in the times of the first indiction, in the metropolis [of Petra] of the Salutaris Tertia Palaestina … … …
3     … appointed of this … … [to] you the most reverend Euthenios son of Dousarios, town-councillor and receiver of the same … and to the … most reverend
4     receivers I enjoin the things appended: since … … between me and the most illustrious Panolbios … … I retained a two-thirds portion of these his belongings … …
5     in accordance with the memoranda which passed between me and his illustriousness, it was resolved that I, in those same memoranda, of all the public dues owed and those falling due,
6     if it should so happen, upon these of surcharges, from the now past tenth and eleventh and twelfth and fifteenth and the present first of the indictions, the portion
7     two-thirds of these should pay to the public account; and moreover that I should also contribute, from the second indiction and for all the ensuing time, of all the contribution due through him, a two-thirds portion.
8     I enjoin upon your reverence and upon the from-time-to-time most reverend receivers, through this my injunction, to relieve indeed the person and the account of the said most illustrious Panolbios
9     of a two-thirds portion of the sums owed through his … on behalf of the said indictions and the surcharges that shall befall upon these, if it should so happen; but to burden my per[son and the ac]count with the same two-thirds portion, because already the most illustrious Panolbios has paid in full the (dues) for the thi[rd] and tenth [and] fourteenth of the now past indictions,
10    the public [dues. I enjo]in upon [yo]ur reverence [and] the from-time-to-time [most] reverend [rece]ivers to relieve indeed … the person and the account of the same most illustrious Panolbios of a two-thirds portion of the (dues) through the
11    … of the … that is … sixty-eight and thirteen … free … and of the patrimony ten iugera; and to burden my per[so]n and the
12    [account] … … [from the] coming to enter se[cond] indiction …
