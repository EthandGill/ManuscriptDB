[TRANSLATION]
1     [Concerning items] of the courtyard of the Amir al-Mu'minin.
2     [In the n]ame of God. Qurra ben Sharik, governor (symboulos),
3     to [Basil]eios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito.
4     We give thanks to God, and after these things: we, having looked into
5     [the i]tems distributed through your administration, on account of the
6     [courtyard] being built of the Amir al-Mu'minin by Yazid b[en] Andala,
7     [have found] your administration to be in arrears as to the things in the appended
8     [list. Receiving] therefore the present letter, send off
9     [with all] dispatch the things left over through your administration,
10    [not with]holding any of them at all, nor indeed sending any of the
11    [palm-]beams except such as serve for roofing of large
12    [buildings], from ten cubits and upward. And you shall know that, if you
13    [do not send] any of such palm-beams as serve … … …
14    … as much as we have written to you in the present
15    [letter], they will not accept this from you, nor this
16    do we [give as a pretext] to you. For indeed you have no excuse whatsoever
17    [concerning the] palm-beams. Do not therefore need other letters from us
18    [in writing] on this account, if you have sense and there is
19    [in you] anything good. Written, month Mesore 26, indiction 9.
20    … [In] indiction 8, split palm-beams 12, palm-leaves 1000.
21    … in indiction 9, split palm-beams 9, 1500.
22    Total: split palm-beams 21, palm-leaves 2500.
23    [Qurra] son of Sharik, governor, to Basileios, administrator of the village of Aphrodito.
24    [Month] Thoth 9, indiction 9: brought through Abu Sufyan the courier, concerning the items left over of the courtyard [of the Amir al-Mu'minin].
