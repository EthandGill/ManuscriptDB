[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonios the oikonomos, from Petous son of Marreis, oil-seller
2     of Oxyrhyncha. On the 28th of Tybi of the 10th year … as the night was …,
3     Athenodoros … and Philon son of Alexandros and Theodotos
4     son of Sopatros, the three prisoners, having taken over …
5     in the villages of Oxyrhyncha all the prisoners, gathered together to the number of 30 persons into the village, and having come into the marketplace, having
6     oil … and safflower oil …
7     to 3 choes of castor oil … 2 … to 2 choes, they were selling to those in the village
8     … and … cotylae and … …
9     … (sent) you concerning these things they sent along together
10    … of these places well …
11    … we came against the
12    … our castor oil, and …
13    … to the policeman
14    … (illegible) …
15    … (illegible) …
16    they removed … but of the … … [the]
17    he brought up the three prisoners before you, the oil having already been carried off.
18    Therefore you will do well to write to Metrodoros the oikonomos, so that it may become evident
19    to him that the prisoners, buying the oil and
20    the castor oil from those who had plundered and worked up the oil-cargoes,
21    that these men, by the … release of the Greek oil, are selling, plying a trade.
22    Therefore, unless some attention is given concerning these matters,
23    and such men plying this trade are stopped, it will come about
24    so that even the oil-cargoes for the 10th year are plundered.
25    … Farewell.
26    To Metrodoros. Of the memorandum given to me by Petous
27    son of Manreus, oil-seller, I have appended for you the copies. Year 10, Tybi 28.
