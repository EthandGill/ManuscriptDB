[TRANSLATION]
1   e [...] la.
2   Diogenes, who with others is overseer
3   of the sale-tax (enkyklion) for the 21st year, to Herodes
4   and his colleagues, public bankers,
5   greeting. Receive
6   from Chairemoni-
7   das son of Apollonios son of
8   Herakleides, his mother being
9   Ammonarion, from O-
10   xyrhynchos city, the tax on the slave
11   Ploution, whom he
12   bought from As-
13   klepiades also called
14   Apion son of Apion
15   son of Asklepiades,
16   his mother being Askletarion
17   daughter of Theon, from the same
18   city, through the record-office in the same
19   city, the agoranomeion,
20   in the present month Hathyr,
21   52 drachmas. Year 21
22   of Marcus Aurelius
23   Commodus Antoninus
24   Caesar the lord,
25   Hathyr 9. Diogenes,
26   who with others is in charge of the sale-tax (enkyklion):
27   receive the 52 drachmas.
