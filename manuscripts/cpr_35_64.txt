[TRANSLATION]
1     … presented by Horion, trumpeter …
2     …nion also called Antimachos, exegete …
3     … of the most illustrious city of the Alexandrians …
4     … councillor, chief Hellenodikes of the first
5     Olympiad, concerning the sacred eiselastic
6     ecumenical musical gymnastic Olympic contest first held …
7     … to Hermopolis the great, ancient and most august and
8     most illustrious and to its magistrates, council and people, greeting.
9     Since Marcus Aurelius Horion, gloriously and virtuously …
10    having won the contest of the trumpeters and from evening continuously
11    to the end of the first and of the second night having held out, according to the
12    law of the contestants, and having bound on himself the crown of the first
13    Olympiad of the contest granted to us by the divine munificence of
14    our lord Gallienus Augustus, proclaimed our lord Gallienus Augustus and Cornelia Salonina Augusta, in accordance with
15    what has been ordained, to fulfill all the things owed for the Olympic victory,
16    we send word to you. I pray for your health, dearest brothers …
17    Year 15 of the Emperor Caesar Publius Licinius Gallienus Germanicus Maximus Persicus
18    Maximus Pius Felix Augustus, Pauni 5.
