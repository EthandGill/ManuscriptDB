1     from the village [of Enseu of the Hermopolite nome. I acknowledge,]
2     I the aforewritten Menas, farmer, [voluntarily]
3     and of my own free will, that I have leased [from you]
4     for a five-year period, reckoned [from the crops of the]
5     coming twelfth indiction, with God's help, [the … belonging]
6     to you, one and a half and an eighth aroura …
7     more or less, lying waterless [in a place]
8     called G…xia in the fields of the village of … ,
9     for sowing and planting of whatever crops [I may choose],
10    each aroura by survey, at a re[nt each]
11    year, for the well-watered (land) of so many artab[as] of wheat … [and for the unwatered land]
12    — which may it not be — of a third part of the aforesaid [rent, which]
13    rent, new, clean, sifted, [by the]
14    Athenian [measure] I will pay and measure out [to you in the month] … ,
15    and I will deliver the same rent [to your house]
16    in Hermopolis with my own animals [and at my own expense]
17    at the time of the payment of the public [taxes; and] …
18    one (jar?) worth one thousand talents of silver …
19    and twenty pleasing cheeses and …
20    and two baskets of wheat-chaff. [The lease is valid]
21    and secure, and having been questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius M[enas] …
22    have leased as set forth above. Aurelius Tho[mas] … [having been requested]
23    wrote on his behalf, since he does not know letters. … …
24    from Hermopolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) fr[om] [the contracting party.] …
25    from Her[m]opolis, I bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) f[rom] [the contracting party.] …
26    son of Ammonios from Hermopolis, I bear witness to the lease, having h[eard] (it) [from the contracting party.]
27    Through me, Menas, notary. Cho[iak] …
28    … Apa Menas, farmer, from the village of Enseu of the Hermopolite (nome).
