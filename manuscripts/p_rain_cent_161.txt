[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord, most honored brother
2     Pharion, Martyrios, goldsmith, greeting.
3     Having returned from Alexandria
4     I found the anklets of your free-woman (wife)
5     with me, and my brother says that
6     you are detaining me on account of a loss. For I examined
7     the anklets. I found them having no
8     millet-grains (settings) and (they) have great
9     wear. Learn from your free-woman
10    whether ever, since I made them for you, …
11    another made (them), or made them in the …
12    village, or made them through another goldsmith. …
13    For indeed I did much work for you. Thanks …
14    to God … not even …
15    For behold, and …
16    I was not able to … them. If you know
17    that we have come to a dispute, send (them)
18    and I will hand over to you your unworked (metal) itself.
19    For behold, also the bracelets of your free-woman,
20    behold, they are not yet worn. For they are new. Deign to renew them, that you may know
21    our goodwill. By God, if you give me
22    a solidus, I will not part with the ounce at (a rate of) a carat.
23    [For indeed] I did much work for your brother,
24    … not even by one
25    [carat] apart from their absence (loss of weight). Learn again
26    from the goldsmiths of Herakleopolis what is
27    the absence (loss of weight). Do not wish to entangle us.
28    The bracelets which I have, I cannot finish them,
29    fearing lest I suffer the same things. But if you wish,
30    let me finish the works done through you readily — make it clear to me,
31    that I may finish and send to you. And with God,
32    if you come, you have authority over me
33    to bring me into the holy (places). I pray for your health
34    and that you keep faith with me for many years, my lord brother.
35    As you wish.
36    To my lord, most honored brother Ph… .
