1     Year 31, Pharmouthi 23, has measured into the treasury
2     of Apollo's city for the same year, of Apollonopolite, Haremsynis
3     of Pharatos wheat, one and one-half artaba; wheat comes to one and one-half artaba. Apollonios
4     Euagoras has measured, year 31, of Apollonopolite, Haremsynis
5     of Pharatos wheat one and one-half artaba; wheat comes to one and one-half artaba, year 31, Pharmouthi 23
