1     [Documents of Demetria, also called Thermutharios, of Dionysios, also called] Sabinos [Onesicrat]ous of the tribe Oseirantin[ean, Antino]idan, together [with Marcus] … of the tribe Sab[ine], with Apollonios, also called Philanti[nous, of the tribe] Ailia … to be read [as set forth below]:
