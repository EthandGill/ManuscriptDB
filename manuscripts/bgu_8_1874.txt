[TRANSLATION]
1     ... to Herakleios the brother
2     [greetings] and always to be well,
3     [and I myself] am well writing
4     [to all] our friends [it seemed] necessary
5     [I considered] and to address you,
6     [so that] whatever in general you prefer [you might] signify.
7     [Nothing] for I will omit of the things pertaining to your
8     use appropriate, ... and of all things
9     [most especially] I beseech [you] to take care of your body. ...
10    Farewell, in year 12, Pachon 13
11    the affairs concerning Hermias, the father of Meneaos
12    [now he] happened upon the [remedy] which had fitted [him], whence
13    ... [being pleased] and knowing that you will be pleased
14    I have signified. Be well.
15    to Herakleios
