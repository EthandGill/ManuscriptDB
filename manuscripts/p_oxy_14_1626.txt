[TRANSLATION]
1     There mutually acknowledge the Aurelii: Alois son of Choous
2     and Herakles son of Pous, and all the partners, all of them
3     decani, from the village of Paneuei, with a surety for
4     the payment of the wages that may appear, (and) Ptolemaios
5     son of Ptolemaios, headman of the same village, and Aurelius
6     Herakleides son of Skylakios, supervisor of animals
7     dispatched [to] Babylon for
8     the divine visitation — the decani (acknowledging) that they have contracted with
9     the supervisor for one place of one staff-bearer
10    of the same animals, from the eighth of the present month
11    Pauni, the supervisor receiving from the
12    decani, for wages, daily
13    two thousand drachmas; and that from now on the supervisor acknowledges
14    that he has received from the decani, for wages
15    of two months from the same eighth, of silver
16    twenty talents; and the (sums) that may appear, until the completion of the supervision, he shall receive from the
17    same decani; and that I, Ptolemaios, shall provide
18    the salaries that may appear, so as to keep (them) free from molestation and from harassment and from loss concerning matters
19    pertaining to the same staff-bearing service. Valid is the contrac[t], written in duplicate, and on being questioned they acknowledged.
20    In the consul[ship of Pau]linus and Iulianus, the most illustrious, Pauni 1.
21    We, Au[relii Aló]is and Herakles, [con]sent to all
22    the aforesaid, and on being questioned we acknowledged. I, Aurelius Ptolemaios,
23    sta[nd surety] as is set forth, having written also on behalf of them, since they do not know letters.
