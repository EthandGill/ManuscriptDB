1     Apollodoros, collector, to Pansnos
2     (son of) Horos. I have received for watchtower and bath-tax
3     of Charax of year 4, base drachmas three, 4½ obols.
4     Year 4 of Hadrian Caesar
5     the lord, Hathyr 23.
6     I, Porieuthes, have signed.
