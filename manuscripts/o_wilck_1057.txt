[TRANSLATION]
1     Imouthes and partners, tax-farmers of the cart-tax
2     of year 19 of Trajan
3     the lord, to Petoubastis
4     likewise, greeting. We have received from you
5     for the cart-tax of your cart
6     until Mesore 30. Year 19
7     of Trajan the lord,
8     Pauni 29. (2nd hand) I, Mesoueris,
9     have signed.
