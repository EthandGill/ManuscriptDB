[TRANSLATION]
1     From Alupius.
2     Since I sent a carpenter
3     Mareinos so that he may cut
4     timber for the [mechanical things] of the
5     Pak and of the parts here, see to it for the
6     [cattle] [fodder] to provide and for the bull-drivers,
7     but also for the carpenter
8     the customary [expense]. To be well I pray you.
9     I have marked.
10    To Heroninos the steward, Thraso.
11    Year 14, Tybi 13.
