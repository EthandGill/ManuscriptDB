[TRANSLATION]
1     In [Berenike] which is by the Red Sea, in the 7th year of the sacred Nero Claudius Caesar
2     Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Mesore 2. Horos son of Komaros, grandson of Germyllos,
3     to Nektharaus son of Haryothes, greeting. I acknowledge that I have sold to you and have received
4     from you here, by hand, out of the house, as the price of the donkey belonging to me with its pack-saddle, a white male, such as it is, free of defect, one hundred and sixty drachmas of Augustan silver and Ptolemaic coinage — total 160 in silver; and if anyone shall bring a claim against you, or proceed against you, or another on my behalf against you or against your agents, neither I myself nor my agents in any way concerning the aforesaid
5     donkey, or its price, or a part of it, then the one who proceeds against you I myself
6     will of necessity remove at my own expense, or I will pay back the price which I received from you, at once, without delay, and to the public treasury
7     an equal sum; and whatever you may be exacted on this account, or any expenses you may incur. Let this agreement be valid for the sale and lawful everywhere,
8     wherever it is produced, as if registered in a public office. Horos son of Komaros,
9     grandson of Germalos, have sold and I have received the price, one hundred and sixty
10    silver drachmas, 160 drachmas, the price of the male donkey belonging to me,
11    as is set forth above. Year 7 of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
12    …rion … year 7 of the sacred Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
13    Germanicus Imperator, Mesore 2.
14    (illegible)
