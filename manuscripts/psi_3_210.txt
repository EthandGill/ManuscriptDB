[TRANSLATION]
1     the guardian; but
2     if we live tomorrow it is
3     in as much without harm and
4     stronger [it] becomes.
5     if should come the fair
6     and good O[ua]lerianus into
7     the hous[e of yours, report]
8     to me, so that I might become to
9     you and through much him
10    by sight I might receive again.
11    the sweetest children with
12    the mother and all the yours
13    from us greet.
14    to be strong you I wish.
