[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: There have been given for the name of Hermodoros … through the heirs of Mousaios from Nagogis, for the account of the embole of the canon of the second indiction, seven artabas of clean wheat …, total 7 artabas. The chit of Pharesmanios of the said 2nd indiction is void and without effect. I, Phoibammon, diastoleus, agree.
