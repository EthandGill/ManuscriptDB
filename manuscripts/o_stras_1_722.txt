[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 16, Pauni 4, in the place Neop, gathering leaves
2     men 14 from 1 copper, amount to drachmas 12
3     and at the same place, winnowing: 1 man, 1 drachma 1 obol, comes to 1 drachma 1 obol.
4     5, in the same place, gathering leaves
5     [men] 21 from 1 copper, amount to drachmas 18.
6     [and towards] the same place
7     [winnow]ing: 1 man, 1 drachma 1 obol,
8     [comes to 1 drachma] 1 obol.
