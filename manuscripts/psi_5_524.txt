[TRANSLATION]
1     Sostratos to Zenon Xenophon, greetings. The [letter] from Sosibius
2     concerning Ammonia, the letter, since Hermon has not delivered [it]
3     to Zenodorus, so that [as quickly as possible you will] receive the letter
4     having composed [it] concisely, send [it] via Prothymios to us,
5     so that it may be delivered to him here.
6     And write also to the bee-keepers so that whatever they say they were wronged about, let them send [someone] having
7     [recourse] toward us.
8     Farewell. Year 7, Pauni 14.
9     To Xenophon
