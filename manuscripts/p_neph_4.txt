[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most honored, beloved Nepheros,
2     Paulos, greeting in God.
3     First of all I pray, night
4     and day, to be deemed worthy by your
5     piety and to be commanded in whatever
6     your kindness might wish, while I am at
7     Alexandria, knowing it to be a profit
8     for my soul to be remembered in your prayers of your
9     hope toward God. Wherefore I urge that you remember me
10    in your prayers, and my children
11    also, on account of our sojourning abroad,
12    that God may grant us our end among
13    our own people. You deigned to instruct me, having received
14    the three little bundles, to dispose of them
15    and to send oil for your need. They were sold, then,
16    for fourteen myriads.
17    And the jar of oil was bought
18    for sixty myriads. With God's help,
19    if it is brought to me, I will send again;
20    for I receive a favor
21    whenever you deign to accept from me
22    anything at all. And I ask that, if it is no burden,
23    you take the sixteen artabas of grain
24    from Pa[p]nutios son of Horion
25    and make [loav]es … e …
26    And with God's help, if loaves are made, send us a full mat-basket. I pray for your [health,]
27    O man [of God.]
28    To my master, beloved Nepheros,
29    Paulos.
30    If anyone among those who have sheep can find sheepskins with you,
31    I have need of three or four.
