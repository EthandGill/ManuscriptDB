[TRANSLATION]
1     … registered in [the city] of Hermopolis in the [quarter of the For]t
2     [of the East-wind], a fruit-tax-contractor serving the city in the
3     conveyance of all the seasonal kinds of fruit-produce brought in for its need, without fail
4     and without interruption; whom I will also produce whenever he may be sought, performing the aforesaid services.
5     But if I do not produce him, I myself will undertake
6     the account on his behalf, or I shall be liable to the divine oath and to the
7     danger concerning this matter; and on being formally questioned I assented.
8     In the 14th year of our lords Constantius and
9     Maximian Augusti and the 2nd year of our [lords]
10    [Sev]erus and Maximin[us the most illustrious Caesars], Pauni 2 …
11    … of the most illustrious Caesars, fortune; willingly and of my own accord I have stood surety [for A]urelius Achilleus son of Achilleus, his mother
12    being Tabinis, from [the city of] Hermopolis, registered in the same quarter
13    of the Fort of the East-wind, a fruit-tax-contractor serving the city
14    in the conveyance of the seasonal kinds of fruit-produce,
15    all those brought in for its need without fail and without interruption; whom I will also produce whenever he may
16    be sought, performing the aforesaid services.
17    But if I do not produce him, I myself will undertake the account on his behalf,
18    or I shall be liable to the divine oath and to the danger concerning this matter;
19    and on being formally questioned I assented. In the 14th year of our [l]ords
20    [Constanti]us and Maximian [Augusti] …
