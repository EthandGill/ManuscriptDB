r.1    Παυλῖνα Τίτῳ τῷ ἀδελφῷ

r.3    γινώσκειν σε θέλω, ἄδελφε,

[TRANSLATION]
1     Paulina to Titus the brother
2     and lord, heartiest greeting.
3     I want you to know, brother,
4     that Aponius my husband gives me troubles through the knowing
5     that I have no one. But
6     I beg you, as you have told me
7     … I have written back to you,
8     therefore [you] will come quickly to me if …
9     [he] kills me saying: "Go out from me." So therefore I beg [you] to remember me and having come [to] avenge
10    me or to free me from him,
11    … I fear lest he kill me as
12    he threatens me.
13    [Farewell].
