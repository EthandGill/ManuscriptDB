1     In the name of the lord and master Jesus Chris[t our God and savior, in the reign of]

4     also here in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, through you Flavius …
5     vice-landholder, in the presence of Menas the servant who asks [and confers upon his]

9     swearing the divine and revered oath, to give surety and to undertake [before your]
10    glorious Eminence (for) Aurelius Papnouthios son of Pa…no…iou…en…e… and Arothio[n so]n of …,
11    both goldsmiths originating from the village of Seryphis of the Oxyrhynchite nome, under the pagarchy [held]

19    of the [afore]wr[itt]en Papnouthios and Arothios according to the above narr[ative, and] [all the things written agree with me as is set forth].
