[TRANSLATION]
1     Tarem… [to] Chairemon, my father, [many] greetings. And before all things
2     I pray [that] you are well and I am well
3     with my [household] and your obeisance
4     I make before the [gods] here; he has come to regret what he has done; not to him alone did this happen; I thank indeed
5     the gods [that] his mind [and] his health
6     he has recovered again; do not torment yourself
7     to go up; for the torment is not small; if
8     you find an opportunity, taking Perseon with
9     him sail back; I greet my sisters and their children and the
10    husbands and my brother; [and]
11    greets [you] Panisneús everyone by name and Ancharenis, my son, greets you.
12    give … to the village of the Arsi[noite] …
13    of the daughter.
