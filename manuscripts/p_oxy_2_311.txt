[TRANSLATION]
1   [Yea]r nine of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
2   [Pauni 2.] Paid through Diogenes the banker, poll-tax of the Hippodrome (quarter):
3   [Try]phon son of Dionysios, the same, twelve drachmas, total 12 dr.;
4   [and on the] 29th of Pauni, pig-tax, the same,
5   [t]wo [drachmas] 1 1/2 obols, total 2 dr. 1 1/2 ob.; [and on the 4th of]
6   [Mesore,] embankment-tax, six drachmas 4 obols, total 6 dr. 4 ob.
