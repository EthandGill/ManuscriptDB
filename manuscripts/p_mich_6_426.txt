[TRANSLATION]
1     To Arrius Victor, the most excellent epistrategos,
2     from Gemellus also called Horion, son of Gaius Apolinarius Niger, weak in eyesight, holding land in the village of Karanis in the Herakleides division.
3     Of the just judgment which reaches all men, greatest of procurators, I too, suffering violence,
4     beg to obtain from you what is just. It is as follows:
5     the elders of the same village of Karanis of the same Herakleides division,
6     being utterly without fear and using their own violence and arrogance, since (someone) was nominated as their partner under the name "Horos son of Apolinarius," a non-existent name which I do not even know — so I do not know it; for he neither
7     showed that (I am) maimed or injured or any other of
8     my distinguishing marks, nor indeed is it possible for me to be registered otherwise, since I am a citizen of Antinoopolis, nor again to perform liturgies except only in the city of Antinoopolis. And besides,
9     by the council of the city that was formed I was permitted to wear the chlamys. Because, not only
10    am I one-eyed, but also with the eye that is reckoned
11    to be a second one I do not see, since a white film has spread over its pupil and I am injured in my sight; on account of this I petitioned
12    also our most excellent prefect, and obtained from him a sacred
13    letter, of which a copy (follows): Alphenus Apolinarius
14    to the strategos [of the Herakleides] division, greeting …
