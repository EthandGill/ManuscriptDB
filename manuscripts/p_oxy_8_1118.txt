# HGV-EN: ... I beg you to write to the strategus of the Small Oasis to present to ... and Chenenoubis, through the one of them who may be found, a copy of this memorandum, in order that he may yet pay to me the debt and the interest due in addition and extras, or else that they may know that I shall enter on the mortgaged property and shall occupy and exercise ownership over it and alienate it and do with it whatever I choose, without any claim being left to them or to any one else for the future in any respect ...
