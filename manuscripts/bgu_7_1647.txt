[TRANSLATION]
1     Her[akl]eides of Horigatas from [the village of Philadelphia], of the division of Herakleides of the nome [of Arsinoe]
2     To Mar[c V]alerius Anta, greetings. I acknowledge that I have delivered to [you] my son Chairemon for [years] four [in] the great camp, so that [he]
3     [may serve] you in that which you have ready-sale …
4     … per [year] from the month of Phaophi of the present
5     [Fourteenth] year of Hadrian Caes[ar]
6     of the [master], monthly wage of ten drachmas, which
7     and you shall make good every month according to thirty days to the [son]
8     my Chairemon. [And] it shall not be possible for me to [withdraw] [him] [within] the appointed time or to give [him] to [another] [workshop]. I have received the wage of Phaophi,
9     You, Anta, feeding him, and [me], the father
10    [c]lothing [him]. Year 14 of Imperator Caesar
11    Tra[i]an Hadrian Sebastus, Phaophi 1.
