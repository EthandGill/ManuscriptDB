[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapion, one of the successors and sub-administrator
2     from Thautus and Thauetis, twins of those
3     serving in the great [sanctuary] toward Memphis
4     of Sarapis. In many ways
5     you having aided [us] through the piety which you hold toward the divine,
6     and some of our [matters] having received [them]
7     into the provision coming to us, but some still through [matters] of those subordinate being delayed,
8     and a report having been brought to you
9     by Mennides the overseer
10    concerning the sesame oil due to us
11    for year 20, toward what you inscribed
12    thus; so that the secretaries from us not know the things being reported in the accounts,
13    unless Mennides make [them] clear.
14    To bring forward therefore those who wrote
15    the report, secretaries, such being
16    and the [one] sent by us
17    boy Apollonius who also is established with you
18    by us being in the sacred [place]
19    inactive and the management concerning these
20    being unable to bring into … [lacuna]
21    having reminded you opportunely, but nothing else
22    accruing to us through the
23    secretaries'
24    misconduct except that of being destroyed,
25    which indeed not moderate to be,
26    such service providing
27    to be delayed in time, we ask you
28    still and now a principal [matter]
29    to impose on those concerning us,
30    in whatever manner occurs to you,
31    so that concerning the same
32    we do not trouble you.
33    Farewell.
