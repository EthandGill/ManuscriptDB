[TRANSLATION]
1     to my lord, my son Aniketos,
2     Demetrius, greetings.
3     Do everything, help [me]—
4     do not make the bread-cakes.
5     For since we have found
6     [bread-cakes] made here
7     [in that manner], therefore
8     do not neglect
9     and make them [yourself].
10    Help us then therefore, come out to us,
