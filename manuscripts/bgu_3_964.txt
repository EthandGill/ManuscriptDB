[TRANSLATION]
1     (Chi-Rho) Theon to Sambas the oil-maker: provide six xestai of oil to Leontios, ...( ), the sawyer (prister)
2     building with hard slabs, for [maintenance of] 2 months, through Andreas the chief (kephalaiotes), ...set-over,
3     of oil, 6 xestai only. Thoth 28, 2nd indiction. (hand 2) I have signed for six xestai of oil only †. (hand 1) Through me, Pousi.
