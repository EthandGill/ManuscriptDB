[TRANSLATION]
1     To brother Peter, farmer of the city of Hermes, Theologius the least presbyter, I have received and [was paid] … or … ou
2     Of the land-tax for the past fourteenth and fifteenth of the two indictions, that is two coins minus
3     Karats twelve for the same two indictions, [it] makes gold coins 2 minus 12, and for your safety I have made [this] present receipt
4     Of the same two indictions, wholly in my hand; Theologius the least presbyter I agree to the receipt as [is] set forth
