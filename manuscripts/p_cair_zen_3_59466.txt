[TRANSLATION]
1     To [Zen]on, greetings, Meious.
2     … me because we are not judging [against St]achy; you asked me …
3     [in that] city to judge. But surely [not] justly did you set [it]; for we will stand under it …
4     …; but where he too, a foreigner like we, let him be judged, whether he wishes [in] Memphis, [or] in Heracles' city; for thus it is just. You would [do] well thus arranging [it].
5     [And] Pasis, having heard that he must …
6     [be judged] in Crocodile-city withdrew [to] the altar of the king.
7     Prosper.
