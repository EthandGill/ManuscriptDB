[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of our most divine] and most pious master Flavius Justinian, the eternal
2     [Augustus and Imperator, year 20, in the] 5th (year) after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most illustrious,
3     Tybi 27, indiction ten, in the city of the Oxyrhynchites.
4     [To the most admirable] Christophoros, son of the blessed Menas, from the illustrious (city) of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     [the Aurelii Joh]n son of Phib, his mother being Thekla, and Phoibammon son of Phib, another,
6     [his mother being] …, carpenters by trade, originating from the hamlet of Neophytos of Antiochos
7     [of the Oxyrhynchite nome, greeting.] We acknowledge that we have received from your admirability the
8     [price of the two door-leaves], one large and one small, which were made for it (the house)
9     … of various woods, by means of your nails, in the present
10    [tenth indiction, of gold] four solidi weighed by the private standard,
11    … [I,] John, for my large door-leaf, solidi
12    [three, and I, Phoibammon,] for the small door-leaf the other one solidus, total of gold 4 solidi by the private (standard)
13    … that we have no claim, nor shall have, against your reverence
14    … [concerning the] said two door-leaves or any balance of them. And for security
15    [of it we have made] this receipt of the same four solidi,
16    [which being valid, written in a single copy, and being asked the formal question, we] have consented. The Aurelii
17    [John] … [and Phoibammon] … [the afore]written.
