[TRANSLATION]
1     … to Simplicius the most illustrious [governor],
2     [from Victor, quaestionarius of] your office, my lord, of those who … against the laws …
3     … into the service of the court, and who toward this rebel against so great an authority; for I, Victor, the title …
4     … having been sent by the office of the commentarienses to make the production of certain persons of the Hermopolites,
5     … by Kyros the night-strategos of the aforesaid city, so that I, residing in a foreign land, his attack …
6     … to compel (them) to hand over the persons summoned before me, he regarded less your greatness and the office …
7     … and he inflicted on me and my brother continual insults and blows with his hands, mocking the orders given by [your excellence]
8     … to those summoned; I therefore, having suffered such other things and having no help, called to witness the many bystanders …
9     … the violence done to me … serving your excellence, beseeching (you) to order the same office to send help to the (city) of the Herm[opolites],
10    … (against) the one who dared these things against me, and his father and his brother and those able to testify, my lord governor.
11    … [they are:] …erios, archephodos (chief of police);
12    …erios, donkey-driver;
13    [in the consulship of the Flavii Caesarius and Atticus] the most illustrious;
14    … Victor, quaestionarius … my lord.
