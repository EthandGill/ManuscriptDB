[TRANSLATION]
1     To John the baker, [the coin] which was also given to him for [the items] from Pthochis, coins 2, less 9½,
2     for the price of ten shoots, coin 1, less 5,
3     to the camel-driver in the month of Pauni, 12th indiction, coin 1,
4     to Levis the perfume-seller for the price of Spani oil, 33 xestae, coin 1, less 5,
5     to Pamouthis the pig-cook for the slaughter-fee, coins 2,
6     likewise to the cowherd who trod the grain of the tenth [month], coin 1,
7     to Sergios, horse-keeper, for the price of fodder given to the horse, coin 1, less 6,
8     to Makarios the ally(?) for the wage of the 12th indiction, [the coin] which was also given for … …, coin 1,
9     likewise to John the baker for the [items] from Pthochis
10    instead of fodder-seed for the property of Tapi[…], coin 1
