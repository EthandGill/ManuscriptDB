[TRANSLATION]
1   ⳨ Also at another time I wrote to you to send to Narmouthis and receive
2   the two gold pieces from the deacon. If you have done this, write me
3   here. So please send to Phoibammon
4   and receive grain from him, and learn what is received from him.
5   And inform the lord Elias my brother, as I informed Petros,
6   about the matter of which he spoke to me; and he has informed me —
7   he has reviewed the expenses of the petition. Write me and
8   I act. Greet warmly your lady mother
9   and Elias and Romanos my brothers and all those in your
10   bountiful house, and the lord Euphrantios and his sweetest
11   children. Do not neglect it, then; we trust in the Master of
12   all that with the new governor we are coming out there.
