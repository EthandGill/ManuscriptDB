[TRANSLATION]
1   Anti[och]os Ka[sian]us and Arrius Puplianus and Julius
2   Maximus and those with them, overseers of the Sacred Gate of Syene, through
3   (NN), mother Thinzmethis, for the poll-tax of the 15th year of Hadrian the lord:
4   seventeen drachmas (and) a half-obol, (and) ½ obol (keepers' fee) — total 17 dr. 1 ob.; and for the assessment of the head-
5   tax [of those who had withdrawn, of the 14th year, one drachma (and) an obol (and)] 2 chalkoi(?). Year 16.
