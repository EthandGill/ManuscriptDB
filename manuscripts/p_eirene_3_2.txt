[TRANSLATION]
1     on Hathur 10 ...
2     for this reason I have sent you the acknowledgment through Nemesates of Mysthos
3     in return for which to Eika through whom Herakles the merchant gave me copper. You will send
4     both you too copper; we do not have
5     vinegar nor bitter vetch. You will send
6     therefore to us, everything I have received from Didymos
7     my friend a tunic and a palliolion.
8     Farewell
9     Year 7 of Emperor Caesar
10    Domitian Augustus Germanicus
11    month Hathur 25
