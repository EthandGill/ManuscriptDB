[TRANSLATION]
1     Maïmachos to Zenon, greetings. I have endorsed to you the [copy] from Philiskos, in which
2     and the one from Theophilos has been endorsed, the copy of the letter. Let the allotments therefore be given to them as they write.
3     Farewell. Year 34, Choiach 26.
4     Philiskos to Maïmachos, greetings. I have endorsed to you the copy of the letter from Theophilos. Let the allotments therefore be given as he writes.
5     Year 34, Choiach 26.
6     Theophilos to Philiskos, greetings. Tlepolemos has written to us that [the king] has ordered
7     those who have sworn an oath in other places to restore [them]. Let [the allotments] be given
8     therefore the allotments to those written below. Year 34, Apellaeus 19.
9     Of Philinos: Artemidorus the Ephesian chilliarch, Theophilos the Persian taxis-commander,
10    Lysias the Mytilenaean general; of Chrysermos: Artemidorus the Selgian
11    chilliarch.
