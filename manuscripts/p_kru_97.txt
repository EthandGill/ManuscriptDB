[TRANSLATION]
1     To the place of Apa Phoibam[mon]:
2     God hearkened to our entreaty; he gave us Pesynthios
3     when he had advanced (in age) a little; the (Enemy) hated every good thing (and) cast it into our heart, that we should turn
4     backward in the vow. Furthermore, God
5     sought to turn us back to it once more; he gave teaching
6     to us in this manner: the devil struck that same son of ours
7     into the fire; he was burnt, except that we made remembrance of the holy place.
8     We entreated him concerning ourselves; he was consumed (sick);
9     and when we turned to him, he granted him
10    to us alive, according to the way he said: "The entreaty of the righteous is mighty, and it works" — furthermore,
11    by our own will, we donate the small child to his holy place,
12    according to the way (it was) formerly (decreed), that he should become
13    a servant in the holy place, in every servitude for ever, (at) any time, in the manner of a slave bought for money, that he might be under the authority of the monastery
14    of that same one for ever, for our soul, we
15    his parents and he too, the small child;
16    and that no man at all (shall be able)
17    to dare to say: "I shall take him out
18    from his place," whether it be us his parents, or whether any man at all who acts (in) the person — and the one who shall dare [(to do) this]:
19    firstly, that this matter — he being a stranger
20    to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, the Trinity inseparable in the age,
21    he and the one who comes after him; and afterwards, that he profit nothing of any good thing in
22    the world; and the one who shall keep (faith) to the document, may God and the holy Apa Phoibammon bless him in every place where he shall go, with his cattle and
23    everything that he has, from least
24    to greatest; and afterwards, that this deed of donation become firm and valid in every place where they shall declare it, because
25    we have vowed it, it being among the perils
26    of this sort; and we ourselves confirm (it); we swear by God that we have brought nothing (away), except all that came to be, and that I made void any authority whatsoever, by the name of God the Almighty — the deed of donation passing
27    to his hand; if it (?) — they do not set it up (establish it)
28    in the place of the holy Apa Phoibammon, may God bless it
29    with his sons and the one who has (it),
30    that he may not permit (any) man
31    to take Pesynthios away from this holy place,
32    because the sickness which seized him made him weak — that is, we prayed
33    to the holy martyr Apa Phoibammon
34    (to) save him for us; if not, he would have died. For this (reason),
35    the righteous (one) it is who urges us to watch over
36    the power of the deed of donation, it being secure and standing firm in truth
37    and faith, firm and valid,
38    without turning back in it, that God may question (men) in his great tribunal
39    which is full of fear, and where there is no denial before it, except the great sentence
40    which cuts off (all appeal) concerning this. The one who shall set up the document,
41    may the God of the holy Apa Phoibammon set him up, with
42    his sons and his cattle, and may he take it (the child) graciously on the day of the true judgment
43    of God; and that we have given our son
44    into the holy monastery,
45    the most God-honoring abba Kolouthe
46    being bishop of the city of Rmont
47    and its district, (and) Sourous the deacon (being) provost of that same holy place, (and) Psmo (being) ruler
48    over the fortress (of) Djeme. To secure,
49    then, the legal right of Apa Phoibammon,
50    we established this deed of donation,
51    firm and valid in every place
52    where they shall take it to him, before any authority;
53    and let them question (us), we acknowledging.
54    And we asked some other trustworthy men, (and) they witnessed to it.
55    We, Aron and Jelkt his wife,
56    those who formerly wrote (it), consent.
57    I, Pahom the son of Pesynthe,
58    consent to this.
59    I, Georgi the son of (the) blessed Souai, give (witness).
60    I, Iohannes the son of the late
61    Papa, by my (?), the Sinaite (?), give witness.
62    I, Ioseph the son of Elias, the man of the village (?) of this Senai (?), give witness.
63    … pesat(?) son of (?) sanakape, I wrote (it).
