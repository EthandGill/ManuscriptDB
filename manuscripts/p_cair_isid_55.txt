1     Aurelii Didymos and Moros, [son] of Isidoros
2     collectors of shipping fees, types, [for] year, of village of Karanidos
3     and boundary-marking, to Aurelius Kyrillout[o]s Kopre, greetings.
4     We have received from you for [the] shipping fees, types,
5     for the same year, and [for] silver
6     six hundred drachmas, total drachmas 600.

8     Annianus, the most illustrious, Mesore 27.

10    of village of Karanidos and boundary-marking, to Aurelius Kyrillout[o]s Kopre, greetings.
11    We have received from you for [the] 2nd indiction of meat, eleven pounds, one-quarter, total pounds 11, 1/4.
12    In the consulship of Rufius Volusianus and Petronius Annianus, the most illustrious, Hathur 22.
