[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters Valens for the 6th time and
2     Valentinian for the 2nd time, the eternal Augusti, Hathyr 14,
3     to Flavius Vitalianus, holding land in the Arsinoite,
4     from Flavius Aigyptos Cometianus, now dwelling in the same Arsinoite. I acknowledge
5     that I have leased from you, which you also hold under lease
6     from the name of Nonnos the councillor, in the field
7     of the village of Hiera Nesos, land, namely forty arouras by the measurement of the rope, for
8     the single happily-coming eighth
9     new indiction's sowing, with me giving
10    as rent for each single aroura
11    three artabas of grain and a half-artaba of barley
12    without deduction and without risk, I the lessee performing the works of the
13    arouras through laborers, all that is fitting, at my own expense
14    in the proper season, doing no damage,
15    all the public taxes
16    being upon you the owner.
17    The lease is valid, and on being questioned I acknowledged it.
18    I, Flavius Aigyptos the aforesaid, have leased from Flavius Vitalianus
19    the afore-written arouras, and it is agreed with me as stated above.
20    Through me …
21    Lease of 40 arouras around the village of Hiera Nesos, of rent [thr]ee gr[ain artabas,] of barley [half an artaba.]
