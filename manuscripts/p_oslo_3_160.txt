[TRANSLATION]
1     To Nikanor greetings. I have labored
2     writing to you, and you did not write back to me. If the [pig-]herder does not wish to come down with the
3     little pigs, you, having departed, the
4     best of all, male, castrated, having selected, having placed on your donkey,
5     send to me through the night, since
6     haste is, or the cook Nemesian send
7     with him of necessity; the
8     shepherd, however, send also [h]im willing
