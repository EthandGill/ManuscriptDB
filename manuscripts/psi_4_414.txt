[TRANSLATION]
1     To Zenon, greetings, Menon
2     vine-dresser. I am owed
3     for Hathyr, three drachmas for the
4     wages. You would do well then to order [it] to be given to me.
5     For you see that I do not have,
6     as the others [do], neither vegetables
7     nor anything else, but [I look] to the
8     wages [for support].
9     Farewell.
