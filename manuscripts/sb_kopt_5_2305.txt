# HGV-EN: Be so kind and persuade (?) brother Kyrikos and send him to [me (? us)], ere I come. And (be so kind as to) write to him, in my name and yours, that he observe the promise, according to the force of the promise that he did give us. Fail not therefore to send him, for he shall not disappoint us. "Give it to Apa Enoch and Apa John, from Samuel.
