1     … [you will] do [well] having received
2     letter[s] from him to
3     Dionysius about their being handed over to us. It was indeed
4     then through summer [it would be] most cheap;
5     but in the present the profitable and just price is at
6     four m[nas] is at five-artaba … [measure] sacked.
7     You will [therefore] send us a letter to him, so that receiving the price he might hand over the fodder to us. Double
8     thymiatheric garum with
9     gods coming [you] bring
10    to me, if it were possible. But if
11    it was troublesome to you, perhaps
12    Sotos, being persuaded,
13    by you, [might] bring [me] a ceramic vessel.
14    [The] children greet you.
15    I pray [that] you are in good health.
