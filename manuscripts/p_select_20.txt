[TRANSLATION]
1     … son of Andreas, his mother being
2     Elisabet, and Pamouthios son of Onnophrios, his mother being
3     Thekla, originating from the hamlet of Neophytos of Banos
4     of the Oxyrhynchite nome, belonging to your gloriousness,
5     registered farmers of it, greeting. Since a need has now again
6     arisen for the estate irrigation-machine under us
7     called that of the south, which draws water into the vineyard
8     and into irrigable land, we came up on account of one small part
9     to the city and petitioned your gloriousness
10    to order the same small part
11    to be provided to us; and at once your gloriousness,
12    taking care of the maintenance of her own
13    affairs, provided this to us through
14    Pateuo, the machinist here, a new
15    suitable serviceable water-drawing device. We received it
16    for the completion of all the mechanical
17    apparatus on the present day, which is Choiak
18    the second of the present eleventh indiction, of the water-supply
19    for crops of the twelfth, with God's help, distribution;
20    and as proof of the receipt we have
21    made this deed, which, being valid
22    written singly, and having been questioned, we acknowledged; the [common one of the]
23    aforewritten names [is content with us,]
24    this deed as is stated above. I, Pa[pnouthios,]
25    wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
26    [Deed of receipt] … son of Andreas and Pamouthios son of Onnophrios, originating from the hamlet of Neophytos …
27    of Banos of the Oxyrhynchite nome, of the receipt of one small part.
