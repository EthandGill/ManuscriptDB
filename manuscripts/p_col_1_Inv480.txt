[TRANSLATION]
1     From the edict concerning the (tax on) slaves:
2     the one farming the tax on slaves
3     and the controller shall exact, of the slaves
4     whose sales are registered before the agoranomoi,
5     of the price at which they are registered, in silver,
6     from the seller, together with the hundredth previously collected for the endowment of Dikaiarchos,
7     of the mina, 9 drachmas 2 1/2 obols, and from the buyer, 8 drachmas 2 [1/2] obols,
8     so that there comes to be, of the mina, 17 drachmas 5 obols; [and] to the city, fore-sale-tax from the seller of the slave, 4 drachmas 1 obol.
9     And if anyone buys on condition that he pay all the dues,
10    they shall exact of the mina 20 drachmas 1 obol, and to the city, of the slave, 4 drachmas [1 obol.]
11    And if anyone secures (it) by over-bidding or counter-bidding,
12    he shall pay in addition to the city another fore-sale-tax.
13    Of those sold through the collector of (taxes on) foreigners,
14    the buyers shall be charged, of the mina, 19 drachmas, and
15    a herald's-fee, a hundredth, 1 drachma, and for the endowment
16    a registry-fee of the slave, 1 drachma.
17    Of those sold against royal (debts), the
18    buyers shall be charged, of the mina, 16 drachmas 5 obols, and a herald's-fee, a hundredth, 1 drachma, and a registry-fee for the endowment of Dikaiarchos,
19    of the slave, 1 drachma.
20    And of the mortgaged slaves, as many as, being free, themselves … the debt, they shall exact from the lender, [of the mina] 5 drachmas 1 obol, and from the borrower, 5 drachmas 1 obol,
21    [so that there is for the] mina 10 drachmas 2 obols, and a registry-fee of the slave, 1 drachma.
