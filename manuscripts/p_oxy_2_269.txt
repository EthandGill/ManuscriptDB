[TRANSLATION]
1   Copy. Dioskoros son of Zenodoros, Persian of the epigone, to Tryphon
2   son of Dionysios, greeting. I acknowledge that I have from you, at the Sarapeion near the city of
3   the Oxyrhynchi, through the bank of Archibios son of Archibios, fifty-two
4   drachmas of Imperial silver coin as principal,
5   to which nothing at all has been added; which I will also repay you on the thirtieth
6   of the month Kaisareios of the present 3rd year of Nero Claudius
7   Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator, without any
8   postponement. And if I do not repay as written, I will forfeit to you
9   the aforesaid principal increased by half, and for the time
10   overdue the proper interest, you having the right of execution
11   upon me and upon all my property,
12   as if by legal judgment. This bond is valid wherever produced
13   and for whoever produces it. Year 3 of Nero Claudius Caesar
14   Augustus Germanicus Imperator, month Germanikeios 18, dies Augusta.
15   Copy of the subscription. I, Dioskoros son of Zenodoros, have the fifty-two
16   drachmas of silver as principal, and I will repay
17   as stated above. I, Zoilos son of Horos, wrote for him since he does not know
18   letters. Year 3 of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus
19   Imperator, month Germanikeios 18, dies Augusta.
20   Copy of the bank-note. Year 3 of Nero Claudius Caesar Augustus
21   Germanicus Imperator, month Germanikeios 18, dies Augusta.
22   Through Theon son of Syros, agent of Archibios the banker, the payment was made.
23   (2nd hand) Tryphon to Ammonas
24   the tall, his dearest,
25   greeting. If you can,
26   — I beg you — press
27   Dioskoros and exact from
28   him the
29   bond; and
30   if he gives you the money,
31   give him a receipt,
32   and if you find someone trust-
33   worthy, give him the money
34   to bring to me.
35   Greet all your
36   people. Farewell.
