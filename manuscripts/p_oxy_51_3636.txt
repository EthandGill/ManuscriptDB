[TRANSLATION]
1     And from the account of the boat-yards (platypegia), the assessment of the same 9th (indiction): 1,090 solidi
2     of the Kynopolite 62 1/2 1/3 solidi, of the Oxyrhynchite 298 solidi, of the Herakleopolite 251 1/6 solidi, of the Nilopolite 170 5/6 solidi, of the Aphroditopolite 72 solidi, of the Memphite 37 5/6 solidi, of the Let[opolite] 7 1/2 solidi, of the Arsinoite 163 solidi, of the Theodosiopolite 13 1/6 solidi.
3     There is paid 14 solidi.
4     Of which there was sent to the great city Alexandria through various
5     escorts, together with what is paid in it, 1,031 5/6 solidi
6     thus:
7     Mesore … through Ptolemaios the escort, 612 solidi
8     Thoth 5, through Ioannes the escort, 80 solidi
9     Phaophi 5, through Makarios the escort, 77 1/2 1/3 solidi
10    Hathyr 19, through Ionas the escort, 150 solidi
11    Choiak 2 … through Phoibammon the escort, 25 1/2 1/3 solidi, and there is paid 14 solidi
12    Mesore 11, through Ionas the escort, 30 solidi; Phaophi, through Ionas, 42 solidi
13    remaining toward the assessment, 58 1/3 solidi
14    thus:
15    [Of M]emphite 37 5/6 solidi, of Letopolite 7 1/2 solidi
16    of Theodosiopolite 13 1/6 solidi
17    [And from] the account of donkeys and oxen and the wage of bakers of the great city Alexandria
18    [of the same] 9th (indiction), the assessment 110 1/2 1/12 solidi and 20 units of silver and 4,500 myriads of denarii
19    thus:
20    … of donkeys and oxen 49 1/3 solidi, wage of bakers 11 units. It was sent in full.
21    … of donkeys and oxen 61 1/4 solidi, wage of bakers … 4,500 (myriads). It was sent in full.
