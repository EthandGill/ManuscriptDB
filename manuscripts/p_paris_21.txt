[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving Trinity, Father and Son and Holy
2     Spirit, in the reign of our most serene master Flavius Heraclius,
3     the eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the sixth year, Pauni 9, of the 5th indiction.
4     Aurelia Pyras, whose father is Besas and mother Maria, — and on behalf of
5     me and of my sister Rhose, to act in her stead —,
6     and Antheria, whose father is Senouthes and mother Kyra,
7     from the village of Thinis of the Thinite nome, providing on their own behalf
8     a subscriber and witnesses to this everlasting sale, those
9     who subscribe it below, we set down this
10    deed of conveyance as a simple written security, to abide by
11    this lawful sale and not to transgress it
12    at all: to Aurelius Pachymios son of Psatos, purple-dealer,
13    from the city of Panopolis, now by the providence of God residing
14    in the same village of Thinis, greeting. We acknowledge, by our own
15    voluntary purpose and free will and guileless conscience and
16    unalterable reckoning and unrepentant resolve, with firm
17    indeed and upright intent, in every fullest right of ownership
18    and every authority and everlasting possession,
19    that we have sold and conveyed in good faith to you
20    this day, being persuaded, the house belonging to us
21    entire with all its rights, situated in the same village of Thinis on a street
22    called that of the Guardhouse, which came to us
23    by inheritance and succession, in accordance with the rights and
24    securities we hold over it, on all sides of the aforesaid
25    house thus: to the south the house of Musaios son of Psaros, presbyter, into which the door has been opened;
26    to the north a public street; to the west the house of Monathios son of Pamis;
27    to the east the house of Pebos son of Horos and the house of Pachymios the purchaser,
28    or whoever the neighbours may be on every side, on all sides, from the ground up to
29    the air; and to the effect that you, Pachymis the purchaser, are to be master of these
30    and to have control and to hold the whole ownership and to improve
31    and to build and to sell and to resell if you should wish,
32    to leave them to heirs, successors, and holders in succession,
33    and to make a gift of them, and … concerning … [if]
34    you should wish, for all time; the price agreed between us
35    and mutually satisfactory, at our
36    valuation, being thirteen carats of gold, that is 13½ carats of gold, which price
37    in full we, the vendors Pyras and
38    Antheria, have received from you the purchaser Pachymios, from hand to
39    hands out of the house, in full number, wherewith we are satisfied,
40    the guarantee of these things being made by us and by
41    our heirs, successors, and holders in succession, according to every guarantee,
42    against everyone who shall come forward or lay claim, whether over our
43    name or over a private debt or a public liability or any
44    other whatsoever, at every time, of necessity. But if it should happen,
45    which may it not be, that any eviction or dispute arise concerning it
46    or concerning a part of it, so that we the vendors and our heirs
47    and successors and holders in succession, at our own expense, shall remove and drive off
48    everyone who comes forward or lays claim or would contest the aforesaid,
49    the assessed value being paid twofold, and all the expenses and the expenditures
50    laid out both for the improvement of the building of this house and
51    for a lawsuit, if any such … should happen to arise, likewise twofold, … and
52    we shall give as a fine six gold solidi. And to all the foregoing terms,
53    on being formally questioned, we have consented. We Aurelia Pyras and Antheria
54    the aforesaid have set down this sale willingly and being persuaded, and everything is agreeable
55    to us as aforesaid. I Elias son of Besas, presbyter from Thinis, wrote on her behalf.
56    I Musaios son of Papnouthios, presbyter from Thinis, witness the sale, having been requested by
57    those who set it down.
58    Written through me, Paulos.
