[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twenty-second year of Imperator [Caesar Marcus Aurelius Severus Antoninus]
2     Parthicus Maximus, Britannicus Maximus, [Germanicus Maximus, Pius Augustus,] (in the month) Hadrianos,
3     in Ptolemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite [nome. Isidora daughter of Ptolemaios, from the metro]polis of the Arsinoite nome, from the quarter [of the Sacred Gate, with my lord, my husband Marcus]
4     Lucretius Diogenes of Antinoopolis, and [as he is styled. A donation inter vivos having been made by me, I have also assigned] to my son Isidoros, one [day before my death, whatever I have of fields]
5     and house-plots and gold articles [and garments and …] … [whatever I have by contract]
6     toward my husband Lucretius [Diogenes, apart from my two slave-children and] of silver five hundred drachmas; [for these I bestow on my husband Lucretius Diogenes; I do not]
7     wish him to exact [my two slave-children and the five hundred drachmas, because I have] already bestowed them; and I have [gold ornaments, one pair of earrings of two minas, and a pair of bracelets]
8     [one] of three minas, and likewise [other gold articles of five quarters, and my garments and the bronze vessels.]
9     [All these] I have assigned to my son; [and I have assigned the pair of my earrings to be sold and]
10    [to be spent] on my burial; and my mother Harpokratiaina and my husband
11    the father of my son, Lucretius [Diogenes, to take care of my son and to provide to the nurse] on his behalf the nursing-fees from [all my (property); and I have assigned all these things in the presence]
12    of Sarapammon son of Valerius, the contractor, [with six witnesses, of the village of Philadelphia, and of my mother] Harpokratiaina and of my sister … [and of Aurelius Ignatius Apollinarios]
13    and of my aforewritten lord and husband [Lucretius Diogenes and of Marcus Aurelius Sere]nus. The donation is valid. I, Isidora daughter of Ptolema[ios, have assigned to my son the aforesaid things through] the aforesaid intermediaries as [aforesaid. Marcus Lucretius]
14    Diogenes the husband and lord, wrote on behalf of [my wife Isi]dora, being illiterate, and I was present at the [donation. Sarapammon]
15    contractor of the village of Philadelphia, wrote the [donation]
16    as aforesaid.
