[TRANSLATION]
1     In the ninth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, in the month Neos Sebastos, on the third,
2     in Philadelphia of the division of Herakleides of the Arsinoite nome.
3     There leased Castor son of Kallistratos, about fifty-nine years old,
4     a scar on the left elbow, to Menon son of Kallistratos, a Persian of the epigone, about twenty-nine years old, a scar on the right knee, the (allotment) belonging
5     to him about the marsh near Philadelphia, a catoecic allotment
6     of four and a half and a quarter arouras, by the ninety-six-cubit measuring-rope, [for] thr[ee] years from the present ninth year [of Tiberius]
7     Caesar Augustus, at a rent — together with what the les[see] shall receive
8     from Castor each year for seed — of wheat, four
9     and a half and a quarter artabas in all: the first year, fifty-three artabas of wheat; the second year, of wheat
10    forty-three artabas; and the last year, twenty-five artabas of wheat
11    , all by the quarter-measure, and from the produce and as gift (dues), each [yea]r
12    one artaba of bread; and all (this) free of all risk, from every risk;
13    and let the lessee perform all the labours and all the other agricultural service at the fitting season, doing no damage, and let him water the allotment and sow it: for the firs[t]
14    two [year]s each year with wheat, but in the last year with grass for consumption and
15    bedding of sheep or cattle; and let it not be permitted to him to abandon the lease within the term, nor to Castor to (lease) it to others
16    by sub-leasing nor to cultivate it himself, and let him pay both the other (dues) and the specified yearly
17    rents to Castor or his agents always in the month Pauni
18    in the aforewritten v[illage of Phil]adelphia, paying new wheat
19    pure from all (admixture) and unadulterated by the specified measure ti…
20    … [but if] he does not (pay), let him pay to Castor at once the price
21    [of each] artab[a] which he owes of the rents, of silver
22    [a hund]red [d]rachmas, or the highest price that shall be in the market,
