[TRANSLATION]
1     Hail. I agree [that] I have received …
2     Under me your property called … [principal]
3     Of gold coins five lacking six karats each by [public weight]
4     Of the same city, [they] total gold coins 5 lacking six karats, which I shall furnish to you when …
5     I cease from the irrigation of the same vineyard parcel in gold [coinage]
6     As I received [it], without any delay and objection and judgment
7     And legal proceedings, [with] all my [property] being pledged to you for this debt
8     [And] things that will belong to me [as if] from judgment. This document
9     [Is] valid and confirmed, and being questioned I agreed. Aurelius … k … [the aforesaid]
10    I placed this document as aforesaid. Aurelius Jordanes …
11    Of Hermes city, having been called upon, I wrote the letters for him not knowing [how to write]. Aurelius
12    Phoibammon of Hermes city I witness the document having heard [it] from the one who placed [it].
13    Aurelius Georgis of Achilles from Hermes city I witness the document having heard [it] from the one who placed [it].
14    Through me Victor with God notary.
