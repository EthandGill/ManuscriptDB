1     Aurelius Ptolemins son of Souchidas through his son Palios from Ptolemais Hormou, public channels boatman
2     of the city of Arsinoe to Aurelius Valerius son of Antiour from the village Karanidos of the Arsinoe nome.
3     I acknowledge to have been fully paid by you the [boat-transportations] of the canal of Ptolemais Hormou five, boats 5,
4     of the third indiction, and I gave to you the receipt
