[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Tyrannos of Didymos, chief guard,
2     to Aurelius Apynchis, greetings.
3     I acknowledge to have sold to you a donkey
4     female, black, one, of the price
5     of the agreed-upon [silver]
6     drachmas, four hundred, which
7     I have received and I will guarantee to you
8     from everything of [that which will come upon]
9     this sale. Year 2
10    of the Emperor Caesar Marcus
11    Opellius Severus Macrinus
12    Pious Fortunate Augustus,
13    Thoth, eighteenth.
