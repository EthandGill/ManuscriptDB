1     [In the third year of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus and of Imperator Caesar L]ucius Aure[lius] Verus Augustus,
2     … eipos Her[od] … and Tyranno …

10    [those (arourai) through the allotments of the settlers] … [the] whole capital price for [transfer] of the things transferred … [silver drachmas] … [forthwith by] hand, and that he shall guarantee the …
11    … of the catoecic [al]lotment, two arourai, with every
12    [guarantee] … [an]d against all charges and every kind (of claim) [from the previous times up to the past second year] and of that same second year, and from private (claims) [and every claim for the whole time, and that no one shall hinder Helene nor those who through her] hold possession of these and the produce from them,
13    [carrying off the proceeds and acting as broker and transferring to others and administering concerning] them as she may choose. And if he does not make good the guarantee … [the capital sum with the addition of a half and the damages and the ex]penses double and as a fine of silver
