1     [In the consulate of Rufius Volusianus] and Petr[o]nius Annianus, the most illustrious, Thoth 20. A rental agreement
2     … of the illustrious [city] of Thmouis. To Aurelius Panthir, son of Hermi[as] …
3     … guardian with [the] assent of your father, according to Aurelius Italus …
4     … [from the vill]age of Ibion of the pagus of Mendesia, greetings. I have rented to you … [from the] present 3rd indiction, grain-producing arourae eighteen and a half or
5     [however many as they may be] … of the field called Parompakote, on condition that you [do]
6     … and all the produce that comes from them you will carry away
7     … [render]al each year for each [ar]oura four artabas of wheat, amounting [to each year seventy]-four artabas of wheat, which you [w]ill pay to me annually with [public] measure … [full], and I will give you a receipt. From this moreover I have received from you wheat
8     [four artabas per aroura] … [for] each of mine as advance payment on the rental for the present 3rd indiction
