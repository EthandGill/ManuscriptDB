[TRANSLATION]
1     Palechēmis son of Psenthotes, silver collector
2     [of] the Hermonthi[te nome], to Piaut Epōnychus, greetings
3     I have from you for [the] 13th year [of the] dyke tax 4 drachmas
4     [and of the] bath [fee] 2 drachmas, [making] 6 drachmas
5     I have written about it to Arch Ammōnius
6     [In the] year 15 of Tiberius Caesar
7     Augustus, Pauni 17
