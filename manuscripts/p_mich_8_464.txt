[TRANSLATION]
1   Apollonous to Terentianus, (her)
2   brother, greetings
3   and continual health. I want you to know
4   that, as I wrote to you before about
5   [the] things belonging to me — well then,
6   [...] that the
7   [re]nts [and the s]eed will all
8   come in [entirely. And do n]ot grieve about the
9   children; they are doing w[el]l and
10   attend [sch]ool. And concerning
11   your lands, I have remitted to your
12   brother two artabas from the
13   rents. So I am receiving
14   from him eight artabas of wheat and
15   six artabas of vegetable-seed. And do not
16   grieve about us, and take care of yourself.
17   I learned from Thermouthas that
18   you provided yourself a pair of sword-belts(?),
19   and I rejoiced greatly. And concerning the olive-
20   groves, they are bearing well up to today.
21   And, the gods willing, if he is able,
22   he will visit us. And I wish you
23   health; and your children greet you,
24   and all your (folk). Farewell.
25   Year 2 of Imperator Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus
26   Germanicus, Phamenoth 20.
27   Deliver to Julius Terentianus ☓, soldier.
