[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of our master Flavius Zeno
2     the eternal Augustus for the 3rd time, Thoth 18, of the 5th indiction.
3     To Flavius Ioannes son of Taurinos, scriniarius of the mili[ta]ry
4     office, from Hermoupolis the most illustrious,
5     from Aurelius Phoibammon son of Thourbon, his mother being Helene,
6     from the village of Ptemenkyrkis of the Shepherds of the
7     Hermoupolite nome, greeting. I wish, willingly
8     an[d] of my own free choice, to lease from you for a three-year period the two arouras belonging to you
9     in the plain of the same village, total 2 arouras,
10    for sowing and planting whatever I may choose, at a fixed
11    rent mutually agreed upon, per year, of two gold solidi by the standard
12    of Hermoupolis less six carats, that is, 2 gold solidi
13    by the standard of Hermoupolis less 6 carats, which rent
14    I shall pay you at the close, in the month Mesore,
15    each year without fail; and after the completion
16    of the period of the lease I shall hand over to you two arouras as I received them. The lease is valid and secure,
17    and being formally questioned I gave my consent. Aurelius Phoibammon son of Thourbon,
18    the aforesaid, have leased as is aforesaid. Aurelius
19    Horos son of Pkylios, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he being present and telling me, as he does not know letters.
20    And through me the document was written.
21    Lease of Phoibammon son of Thourbon, 2 arouras.
