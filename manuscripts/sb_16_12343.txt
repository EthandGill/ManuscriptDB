[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year 10, T]ybi 10. Ptolemaeus agrees [that it] has been registered
2     at the [office] in the city
3     record office for the king
4     Augchis of Marres, fee
5     of renewal of a part
6     of a house and rooms
7     which are in Auhere,
8     which he/she pledged with Achommneuis Petenonterios, drachmae 60 and interest of the
9     9th year and of the 10th year drachmae 40,
10    totaling drachmae 100, copper 2, of change obol 1½.
