[TRANSLATION]
1     (cross)
2     In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ, our God
3     and Saviour, and of our Lady, the holy
4     Mother of God, and of all the saints, in the year of Diocletian 379,
5     Ep[ei]ph the twenty-second, of the sixth indiction, in Arsinoë.
6     [To the most holy] and God-honoured abba Peter, bishop of this
7     city of [Ars]inoë, Aurelius Sarapion, son
8     of Victor, your cultivator of the village of Kaminon of the Arsinoite
9     nome … inasmuch as I was convicted of having, in robber-like fashion,
10    ta[ken] from the barley(-crop) of the arouras belonging to you and (cultivated) under me,
11    which a[re in] the plain o[f] the same village, of the crops of the sixth indiction,
12    and this having become known to your Holiness, she was greatly indignant
13    against me about this; but after much entreaty was brought to bear
14    by me and by other persons who entreated her
15    on my behalf, she had mercy on me and pardoned me
16    this once for this reason, (and) she required to receive from me
17    this written security of entreaty, through which
18    I acknowledge, of my own free will, swearing by God Almighty,
19    [that I prom]ise t[o] your Excellency
20    that if in future I am convicted, secretly or openly,
21    of committing another theft of any kind of produce of her (lands)
22    on the aroura, then I shall first provide to her
23    [as] a fine, on account of such a conviction, … gold pieces
24    … [and then] … that I be handed over to the glorious praetorium
25    … to be (beaten) on the back with 27 blows. Having been questioned, I agreed. Sarapion …
26    [son of the bl]essed John, I bear witness to this oath
27    [a]s aforesaid. Kos[mas] son of Marnitas, I bear witness to this oath
28    as aforesaid. …sis son of the blessed Kyriakos, I bear witness …
29    … through me, Phibachy…
30    … the oath.
