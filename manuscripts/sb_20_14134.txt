[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 15 of the Emperor Ca[esar]
2     Trajan Augustus, Thoth 3. [He] paid Herie[utes]
3     to the tax-collector of metropolitan [taxes] of the Syrian district, Dei[os]
4     of Deios son of Didymus, mother Thaisatos [for the poll-tax]
5     of the fourteenth year, twenty drachmas,
6     making 20 drachmas, with the addition of bronze obols ten.
