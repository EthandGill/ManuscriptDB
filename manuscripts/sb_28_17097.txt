[TRANSLATION]
1     Herennius to Lucretius his brother
2     very many greetings;
3     your obeisance
4     I perform before Philotera.
5     being asked, brother,
6     see if any of the friends has there
7     hay for me
8     to advance until it shall be brought to me and immediately I shall repay, since my horse
9     for five days does not eat
10    its food.
11    greet Himeros.
