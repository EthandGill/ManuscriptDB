1     In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ, God and Savior of us.
2     [In the reign] of the most divine and most pious our Master, greatest benefactor,
3     Flavius Mauritius, New Tiberius, the eternal
4     Augustus and Emperor, year twelve,
5     [of the consulship] of the same most pious our Master, year 11, Thoth 30, indiction 12.
6     To Flavius Apion, the all-honored and most eminent, from [the] consuls,
7     [holding land and here in the splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites through Mena], [a] servant
