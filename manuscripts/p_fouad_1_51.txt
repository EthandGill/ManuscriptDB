5     on the left side under the hair, both sons of Zeuxis son of
6     Deios, Sosikosmioi also called Althaieis,
7     mutually bound for the payment, to Hippal[os] son of Sarapas son of Areios,
8     ag[ed] about forty-five, having a scar on the second finger
9     of the left hand, that they have from him, Hippalos, at once from hand to hand out of his house an interest-bearing loan of a capital of four hundred
