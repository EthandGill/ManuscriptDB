[TRANSLATION]
1     … aroura 75 … by night …
2     of Pharmouthi of the present (month/year) …
3     of Claudius Caesar Augustus Ge[rmanicus]
4     Imperator, certain men in the manner of brigands
5     dug through the courtyard of our donkeys'
6     [pen], and getting inside drove off [from] me
7     two full-grown white donkeys, valued at
8     290 drachmas; and concerning these I submitted to the chief-constable of [Karanis], Pankrates, a memorandum, and together with him, having loaded another donkey with water and provisions,
9     and another, we set out, tracking the culprits, from where we found the route
10    to have gone into the region of Bacchias
11    and back again to the desert. But the chief-constable of Bacchias, Pasion, and those at the gate hindered us, when we were already about to apprehend the culprits in their presence; they detained me
12    and the chief-constable of Karanis, and the
13    waterskins they broke and the … having torn away, they kept us in custody for three days
14    until we were unable to seize the culprits.
15    Then, having carried off from us two pack-saddles and the
16    loaves and a sheepskin and two fodder-bags, and they assaulted me with blows, and saying so, the village-scribe and [the]
17    elders compelled us to be released. I request to be given assistance
18    by you.
