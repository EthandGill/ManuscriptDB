[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of our most divine and pious lord, greatest benefactor Flavius Tiberius Maurice of eternal [memory]
2     Augustus and Autocrator, year 3, of the consulship of the same most pious lord of ours, year 1, Athur the 24th, indiction three.
3     To the most excellent heirs of the late-of-glorious-memory Apion, having-been proto-patrimonial-official and land-owning
4     and here in the brilliant city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas, slave of the one asking and providing
5     to their own masters, the same most esteemed men, the claim and liability, Anesiosis presbyter
6     son of Anup, mother Tabes, and Aurelius Anup, steward, [son] of Anesiosis another, mother Tapaes
7     starting from the estate of Great Moches, and George son of John from the estate of Eutychias
8     registered colonists of it. We agree willingly and with voluntary choice, swearing
9     the divine and venerable oath, to guarantee and undertake before your transcendence
10    we Anesiosis presbyter and Anup steward, [guarantee] Praon son of Philoxenos from the same Moches, and I George
11    Cyriacus son of Victor, mother Kyria, from the same estate Eutychias of the same nome
12    another estate of your transcendence, registered colonists of it, on the condition that them
13    without ceasing to remain and to dwell in the same estate and in no way them
14    to abandon, nor indeed to be transferred to another place, but even if sought after
15    them to us from your transcendence through those belonging to it on whatever day and for whatever reason, these we will deliver and hand over
16    without any place of refuge, where them also we have received in keeping
17    of your glorious house; but if we do not do this, we agree to provide to your
18    [transcendence] …
19    guarantee having been made by Anesiosis presbyter son of Anup [and Aurelius Anup steward son of Anesiosis from the estate of Great Moches]
20    and George son of John from [the estate] [of Eutychias, undertaking Praon son of Philoxenos]
21    from the same Eutychias and Cyriacus [son of Victor from the same Moches ?.]
