[TRANSLATION]
1     … of me and Teos Pasetos the [swineherd] has … having the 15 piglet-rents
2     [has come] to you Al…
3     because his sacred animals are about to be tallied. Send him therefore [to] me as
4     [quickly] as possible, and I guarantee to you concerning him both the piglet-rents and the taxes.
5     [Horos] …tonos the chief guard of Crocodilopolis detained me for 3 days while bringing the sacred animals to you.
6     from these, 3 of the [best] fell away. And I also reported [this] to Kleotes
7     to the chief craftsman. I have sent to you
8     the swineherds leading the sacred animals
9     [Teos] Papios, Sontos of Horos, Onnophris
10    Paa… [, Psi]nanophis Tamautus, Sontos
11    Pasitus.
12    Farewell. Year 32, Epheiph 5.
