1     In the name of the Merciful and …
2     There is no god except …
3     Muhammad (is) the apostle …
4     Abd-Allah son of al-Walid …
5     the Amir al-Muminin …
6     In the name of the Father and of the Son and …
7     in the unity of the Trinity …
8     The whole community of the men of the village of Djkow …
9     this agreement, you who write to …
10    write concerning them, that we write to the public [office, that is, the all-praised lord]
11    Korra the most eminent, through you, from you, the most glorious lord, [the lord Basile]
12    illustrious, and the pagarch of this [single] village [of Djkow] …
13    to us, that you may settle me in the place to present …
14    we have come to entreat you, that you …
15    a sigillion for him, namely Onnophrios son of …
16    that he may dwell in his place (and) pay his …
17    on his behalf; and we have come …
18    that the disturbance may arise concerning this matter …
19    the authority to define …
20    this agreement to you, that we are liable [with all our substance] …
21    until we act according to the manner in which we first [said, swearing by the name of God]
22    and the welfare of the lords who rule over us …
23    [Agreement made by the community of the village of A]phrodito on account of the sigillia given for Onnophrios son of Paeiane from the same village,
24    who is in the place Psinemoun of the pagarchy of Antaios and Apollon, to the public office, that is, our master
25    Flavius Basileios the most glorious pagarch.
