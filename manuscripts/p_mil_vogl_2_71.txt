[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year] … of Imperator Caesar [Marcus Aurelius A]ntoninus Augustus, Armeniacus, Medicus, Parthicus,
2     [Germanicus Maxi]mus, in the month Kaisareios Mesore [on the ninth, in Ptolem]ais Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome. Ammonas acknowledges
3     … [from] the village of Teptynis, about … [tw]o years (old), with a scar on the little finger of the left hand, to the woman present with and living with
4     [him without written contract], Tephorsais also called E[udaimonis, daughter of Heron] son of Pasion, from the same village, about twenty-one years old,
5     [unmarked, with] the guardian whom she has voluntarily chosen [through Sabinus the priest,] exegete, according to the official memorandum sent by him, of which a copy [is appended,] Ploution son of Ptolemaios, about [forty years old, with a scar on] the left shin, that the acknowledging party Ammonas has from
6     [Tephorsais also called] Eudaimonis as a contribution [the dowry of] tested gold, by the Arsinoite standard, two quarter-(staters), which are a pair of earrings [and] a white garment, valued at forty drachmas. So let the married couple live together blamelessly [as also before], with Ammonas providing [for her all the things necessary] and clothing and the other things which befit a married woman,
7     [according to his means, and] Tephorsais also called E[udaimonis providing herself] blameless and unaccusable in the cohabitation.
8     [But if, a difference] arising, they separate from one [another, let A]mmonas give back to Tephorsais also called Eudaimonis the aforesaid dowry,
9     [the gold,] two quarter-(staters) in the same trinket and … [the] garments with what is owed, or their aforesaid valuation,
10    [in the case of repudiation,] at once, but in the case of voluntary [separation, within] sixty days, the right of execution being granted to Tephorsais also called Eudaimoni[s, both from] Ammonas and from [all his property], as by judgment. There has been born to them from one another a daughter [Eudaimonis, not] registered until [now. The subscriber] for Ammonas is Harpokration son of Nemesas son of Leonides, about 40 years old, with a scar on the right shin.
11    [Of the memorandum the] copy is: [Sabinus, priest, exegete] and chief-prytanis, to those handling the archives, greeting. Of the petition given [to me] by Tephor[sais also called Eudaimonis] daughter of Heron, a copy is sent to you through Ptolemaios, attendant of the office,
12    [so that] … [you may] jointly transact [for her, making the contribution] with the guardian whom she has voluntarily chosen through me, Ploution son of Ptolemaios, about forty years old. [Year] … [of Antoni]nus Caesar the lord, Pauni 30. To Sabinus the priest, exegete, from
13    [Tephorsais also called Eudaimo]nis, daughter of Heron [son of Pasion, from] the village of Teptynis of the Polemon division, with the guardian whom I voluntarily [choose through you Ploution son of Ptolemaios, from the city of the Arsinoites]. Wishing to make a contribution to my husband, who has long been living with me, Ammonas, … [from the same] village, of tested gold two quarter-(staters) in trinkets and garments valued [at forty drachmas, and to register on my own account] the daughter Eudaimonis born to us from one another, I am hindered
14    [not having the man who is to be registered as my guardian, because there is not] to me a father nor a father's father nor brothers nor sons. Wherefore
15    [choosing through you the aforewritten Ploution, I submit and] request that the order be sent to those handling the archives to jointly transact
16    [for me with the same Ploution, as I make the contribution, so that I may be] favored. Farewell. Ptolemaios, prytanic and exegetic attendant, have transferred (it). [Ammonas …] … [I acknowledge] to have from Tephorsais also called Eudaimonis, by hand,
17    [of tested gold two quarter-(staters) in trinkets and garments valued at] forty drachmas, and at the separation
18    I shall give back [the aforesaid dowry as is set forth. Ha]rpokration son of Nemesas wrote on behalf of him, being illiterate.
19    [I, Tephorsais also called Eudaimonis, with the guardian whom I have voluntarily] chosen [as] is set forth, Ploution son of Ptolemaios, have contributed [the aforesaid trinkets and garments and shall register the daughter Eudaimonis born] from one another, as is set forth. Ploution
20    [son of Ptolemaios wrote on her behalf, she being illiterate. Year] … [Mesore] 9.
