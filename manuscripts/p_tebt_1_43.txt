[TRANSLATION]
1     [To king Ptolem]y and queen Cleopatra the sister and queen
2     [Cleopatra] the wife, benefactor gods, greeting.
3     [Menches], village-scribe of Kerkeosiris in the division of Polemon of the
4     Arsinoite (nome), and his brother Po[le]mon. On the 17th of Hathyr of the present
5     53rd year, when it fell out to us that a certain Asklepiades, one of the men of Amin[ias]
6     the superintendent of the guards of the same nome, was arriving
7     at the village and in accordance with the … we came out to meet (him)
8     together with the village-headman (komarch) of the village [and] some of the elders of the
9     cultivators and with Demetrios who administers the affairs of the superintendency
10    and chief-guardship of the same (village) and with others, having no
11    … we welcomed him, and he laid hands upon us,
12    and likewise upon Demetrios and one of the cultivators
13    Marreis son of Petos, alleging that an information had been laid against us
14    and against Maron son of Diodoros and Petesouchos son of … and
15    Hermon son of …, of the same (village), and Arte[mido]ros the village-scribe of [Ibion] of the Twenty-five-aroura (holders), a report by Haryotes son of
16    Harsigesis, of the men from Krokodilon polis (Crocodilopolis), to the effect that
17    they had dined with him in a certain tavern in the village
18    and that he himself had been done away with by poison, by which we also, having been brought before
19    Aminias on the 19th of the same month, after examination, in the presence also of
20    Amenneus the royal scribe, were immediately released because we were shown to be
21    guiltless. Wherefore, foreseeing that we might sometime, concerning the same matters,
22    [by certain of those who conduct themselves not in the best] way [be harassed nor] … in the … of those denounced
23    by us in certain matters (against those) who have acted in ignorance in the matters
24    that pertain to you, be falsely accused, and (that) having been extorted from
25    we have been led to make our refuge with you,
26    we beg (you), greatest victorious gods, having apportioned also
27    to us of the protection which you have toward all,
28    since after examination in a session, in the presence
29    also of the royal scribe, we have been shown to be
30    guiltless, if it seem good, to order to send
31    our petition to Apollonios the kinsman
32    and strategos, who, being obedient to your
33    will, provides that no one shall bring a countersuit …
34    for the sake of false accusation and extortion, so that
35    it may be permitted to no one hereafter concerning the same matters
36    to harass us nor to distract (us) on
37    any pretext, so that Menches
38    may unhindered fulfil for you the (duties) of his service, we having obtained your
39    protection for the whole of (our) life.
40    Farewell.
41    (Docket) To Apollonios. If it is as he alleges, let provision be made
42    that he shall not be harassed. Year 53, Pachon 4.
