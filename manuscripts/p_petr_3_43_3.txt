[TRANSLATION]
1     [Harmachoron]. You wrote me not to withdraw the [crew] from Philoteridos until the works
2     (are completed). Know therefore the works which [they had] completed and other cords...
3     Because of Theodoros demanding that I wait [until P]auni 10. But still you [do not] know...
4     I spoke with you concerning [sesame] and castor [that] is at hand. [And] the crew does not work until the completed works are measured [to them]. Well would
5     you do when you send the measurers so that they [will measure] so that not [improper]
6     something they may do. For you know what kinds of things are among the crews if they are idle.
7     Further, Megas' crew has departed [and] the remaining ones here so that no one
8     is here except us; and the crew complains, saying they are being wronged
9     [here] already 10 months; and they endure this because the triemarch is not present with them.
10    Farewell. Year 7, Pauni 9.
