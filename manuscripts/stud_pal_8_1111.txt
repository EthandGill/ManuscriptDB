[TRANSLATION]
1     Theodosius with God, general, to Tzittas the most illustrious administrator and receiver of gold. Provide from
2     the revenues of the sixth indiction to Cyrus, the most illustrious universal count, and to Zacharias, the illustrious grain official
3     for the freight of the [shipment] of barley of the fifth indiction from nomismata and keratia fourteen quarter
4     of Alexandria nomismata five keratia fourteen quarter of Alexandria it amounts to nomismata five
5     keratia fourteen quarter of Alexandria only in Tybi eighth of the same sixth indiction I have marked
6     the gold nomismata five keratia fourteen quarter of Alexandria
