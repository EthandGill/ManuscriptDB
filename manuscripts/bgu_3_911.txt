[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the] fourth [yea]r of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, in the month
2     [Hyperb]eretaios, on the eighteenth, Mesore 18, in Sosknopaiou Nesos of the He[r]akleides division of the Arsin[o]ite n[o]me. There lent Horos son of Harpagathes, about
3     forty years old, with a scar on the middle of the forehead, to Panephrommis son of Erieus,
4     a Persian of the epigone, [about] sixty [years old], with a scar on the forehead
5     on the left, and to his wife Thermouthis daughter of Satabous, a Persine, about 35 years old, somewhat short-sighted, with a scar on the left shin, with the guardian, the aforewritten
6     husband, sureties for one another for repayment, that they have from h[im]
7     by [hand] from the house, in silver, of stamped capital, of approved coin, eighty-four drachmas,
8     at interest at one drachma per mina each month. And this loan of the eighty-four silver drachmas let the borrowers repay to Horos or to his (agents) in the month Pharmouthi of the coming [fifth year of Ti]berius Caesar
9     Augus[tus] … [Pa]nephrommis an[d]
10    his wi[fe; and if they do not rep]ay as is afore[said], let them pay back [the loan at half-as-much-]again, and the interest [single, the right of exaction]
11    [belonging to] Horos and to his [(agents), both from the]
12    borrowers an[d from the property]
13    of [them all, as if by legal judgment.]
