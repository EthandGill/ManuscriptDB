[TRANSLATION]
1     Amyntas to Zenon, greetings. The hollow-ware maker came to us [requesting me to write]
2     to you so that he might not be wronged. You would do well then letting him off if he has not [done something serious],
3     and for the future he says he will be well-ordered; for we rebuked him and [we since]
4     he runs about and does not work. And he requests to be left in Memphis to work. If therefore [nothing]
5     else Apollonius orders, you would do well granting him [permission].
6     Farewell. [Year] …
7     To Zenon.
