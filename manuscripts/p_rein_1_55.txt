[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonios [Heron]
2     to the father of him, [greetings]
3     And when in the city I ordered you and through another [epistle]
4     so that if Dioskoros the donkey-driver should come to you, fill
5     him with wine which you have of mine,
6     but do not send him back
7     empty, just as you ordered me regarding Kalantos
8     I wish you to be well, father
9     Year 1, Phaophi 28
