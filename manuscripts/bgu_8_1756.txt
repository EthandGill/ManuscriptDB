[TRANSLATION]
1     [Hephaistion to Paniskos, greeting and good] health. Of the memorandum from Komanos and
2     [Andromachos, the hipparchs in the nome], cavalry settlers, [through which they declare] that the so-called writ of summons has been brought against them, a copy lies below. If indeed
3     [it be true, render] them undisturbed, until they have finished with the [gathering] of the crops. And take care of yourself.
4     Farewell. Year 23, Panemos 13, Pachon 13.
5     [To Hephaistion, kinsman and] dioiketes and (officer) for the special account and the deputies,
6     [from Komanos son of] …ippos and Andromachos son of Andromachos, hipparchs over men, of those in the
7     [Herakleopolite, cavalry settlers]. While we were occupied with the gathering of the crops which we cultivate
8     [near] … land, Ktesippos, one of those
9     [who conduct themselves not in] the best way, wishing to slander and extort from us, has procured a writ from the registry-office
10    [of summons], wishing to drag us away at the most critical season at hand, contrary to
11    … the things [ordered by you] and the providence which you exercise that no one of the cultivators be disturbed,
12    … therefore, most august dioiketes, not … (failing) to give and
13    [to receive justice] … we ask, if it seem fit,
14    … of the nome … us
15    … and that he announce
16    (the matter) of Komanos and Andromachos.
