[TRANSLATION]
1     To Flavius Sisi[nni]us the most distinguished catholicus,
2     from Aureliu[s Ammon] son of Petearbeschinis, advocate, from Panopolis
3     of the Thebaid. Since Eugeneios the mem[or]arius and I, Ammon — friends having come to be between us at the city of the Panopolites, namely Pa[n]isk[o]s the ex-juridicus
4     and Apollon the poet and another, Horion — we have jointly made a written agreement concerning slaves now in Alexandria, this splendid city,
5     left by Harpokration my brother; and in this agreement
6     I do not know why I was induced to accept such a fixed term as this,
7     namely that within twenty days from the twenty-seventh of Hathyr of the past month I should
8     arrive in Alexandria, so that an end might be put to the matter; but having come
9     here now, two days ago, I did not meet with the aforementioned Eugeneios
10    nor have I been able to find him, and on this account today I publicly testify — which is
11    the thirteenth of Choiak, four days before the (end of the) fixed term — securing (myself)
12    and requesting that this securing-statement of mine be laid up in the record-office, until that man
13    arrives, so that it may not be possible for him to put forward any excuse; and these things put forward
14    by me I request to be reported to your greatness, master, through the city's
15    tabularius this very day.
16    In the consulship of Flavius Philippus the most splendid prefect of the sacred praetorium, and
17    of Flavius Salia the most splendid master of cavalry, Choiak the thirteenth.
18    I, Aurelius Ammon, have put forward (this) as stated above.
