1     And from Harmais, holder of seven arouras, from the price of wheat, 5 artabas,
2     at 1,000, 5, 4 (each), total 4,500, of which:
3     26th: to Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios, price of a sack, 1,500;
4     to Kos, for silver, 4 drachmas, 1,840; total 3,340;
5     remaining …
6     From Cholos, wheat 1 and 1/2, lentils 3, of which:
7     to Ammonios son of Lakon, lentils 1, remaining 2,
8     and from the wheat, on the 25th, for bread, 1 artaba.
9     26th: to Mikion, for the 18th, bread 1/2,
10    and from the barley in the Soucheion, 1/2,
11    and from the allotment of Nikanor, through Harbechis, hay 20,
12    from Petosiris son of Petesouchos, for freight, 250;
13    27th: from the same likewise 240, total 490,
14    total with expense 1,230, 1,720, of which:
15    expense through Hermias, of bread … 1 choinix, 80,
16    figs 20, to Harloloutis 200, cheese 40,
17    total 340, remaining 1,380.
18    In return for which was found 1,430.
