[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakleides to Horion, his dearest [friend]
2     greetings.
3     All those sending letters concerning the
4     camels are sending [them]. He held back those with
5     the camels. For after we filled the
6     camels with bones, then the letter was brought [up]. For I was able to buy … … …
7     of the measure [at] 112 drachmas. For indeed foreigners have come
8     seeking oil. You ought, when sending lentils
9     to us and wheat, to send knowing that we do not have [enough] to provide. But if you are not willing now to send,
10    it is able to proceed with [the] stater's measure
11    of one and a half. If now you send in advance,
12    send five artabas of wheat. The lentils
13    are sold at two measures of stater.
14    I received through Phatreous 196 drachmas and
15    of lentils four artabas and … [illegible] obol-valued
16    14 and at two-obols each… and the fish. Bring from Suher[us] a small basket
17    in which there is one measure of dizypha, 30 pomegranates,
18    and 15 cucumber-fruits. Show me from how much
19    the artaba of pressed olive oil you wish to sell to me. For everyone was seeking wheat and
20    lentils in advance so that they might prepare [them]. Bring from the same small basket in which there are two measures of green date-palm and the two choes [of] six
21    measures of old wine with much
22    effort if I find [it], and 12 artabas of bones.
23    Greet Helen, Thahesion and the children, Ptolemaios. Farewell. … [of] Apianus
24    quickly. I did not find the wool-wood tunic as I wanted. But if you wish
25    to have [it] woven
26    for you here, send warp-threads and the measures. Take care of the wheat being sent to
27    Psobthis
28    and of Chairatos' wheat. Do not neglect concerning all [things]. Bring from Phatreous [a basket of] Chairatos
29    in which there are three measures of dates and 50 pomegranates and a note. See then that you do not neglect it.
