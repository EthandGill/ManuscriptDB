[TRANSLATION]
1     Kottarios and Apion, collectors,
2     to Pachnoumis (son of) Paouphtheus
3     and to Paoukales his son: we have received for
4     the assessment for brick(-making), per name, in small coin
5     three and a half obols. Year 19 of Hadrian
6     Caesar the lord, Mesore 5.
7     (2nd hand) I, Apion, have signed.
