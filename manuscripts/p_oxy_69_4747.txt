[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Diocletian
2     Augustus for the 6th time and Constantius Caesar for the 2nd time.
3     Aurelia Ammonilla, daughter of Dionysios son of Dionysios who had served as gymnasiarch and become a councillor of the
4     splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, has leased to Aurelius
5     Paesis son of Heraklas, mother Taamois, from the village of Kerkethyris, for a period of two years from the present
6     13th year and 12th year and 5th year, the (arouras) belonging to her near Seryphis,
7     the six and one-eighth arouras previously farmed by me,
8     so as to sow and plant each year with wheat (and) grass on
9     a half(-share) basis, at a fixed rent per year of twenty artabas of wheat, free of every risk, the
10    public taxes on the land being borne by you the landowner, you having control of the crops until you recover what is owed. The lease being guaranteed, the lessee shall pay the annual fixed rent in the month Pauni
11    at the threshing-floor of the village, wheat new, pure,
12    unadulterated, free of clods, free of barley, sifted, by the receiving-measure of you the landowner, with two choinikes added per each artaba, the
13    right of execution being against the lessee as is fitting. The lease is valid, concerning which, having formally questioned one another,
14    they acknowledged to one another. Year 13 and 12 of our lords
15    [Diocleti]an and Maximian the Aug[us]ti
16    and 5 of our lords Constan[ti]us and
17    Maximian the most illustrious Caesars Augusti,
18    Phaophi 29. I, Aurelius Paesis, have leased the
19    land and shall pay the rent and the produce-rents as
20    aforesaid, and having been formally questioned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Sarapammon, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
