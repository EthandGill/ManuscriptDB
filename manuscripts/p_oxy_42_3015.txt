[TRANSLATION]
1     … mono … … it is best that they
2     [adminis]ter justice acc[or]ding to the laws of the Egyptians
3     [und]er which it is permitted also to alter dispositions. The deceased disposed (his estate) rightly.
4     Year 12 of the deified Trajan, Pachon 13. Areios and Sarapion,
5     both sons of Ptolemaios, against Athenodoros and
6     Apollonios; from what was said: Sulpicius
7     Similis, having inquired of Artemidoros the expounder of the laws concerning the matter
8     and having conferred with the assessors, said: An Egyp[t]ian had the power to dispose (of his property) as he wishes.
9     [Year] … of the deified Tra[i]an, Tybi 20, in the cases concerning Tryphon
10    [agains]t Did … after o[ther matters]: Sul[pici]us Similis,
11    [having con]ferr[ed with th]e asse[ssors] and having consu[lt]ed
12    [Ar]temidoros the jurist, sa[id: it is sa]id … one …
13    … neither the wife, with whom the father of the bridegroom
14    agreed on something rather novel, nor her sons survive,
15    by whom the terms of the agreement could be enforced,
16    this document is already void. But the law, as it is said, gives power to the man who has executed an outright sale and held it (in trust) for his children of their own property, to choose one of them and make him heir. Therefore,
17    since the second security lapsed, the right reverted to the former one. He was permitted to dispose as he wished,
18    leaving as heirs his children for whom he made the outright sale.
