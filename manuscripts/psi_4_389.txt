1     When Ptolemy son of Ptolemy [and Arsinoe, brother-and-sister gods, year 5, under] the priest Aristoboul[us son of Diodotus]...
2     [of] Alexander, and of brother-and-sister gods, and of benefactor gods, [kanephorus of Arsinoe Philadelphia, of Iamn]eia of the Hyperbassant...
3     Egyptians, Epeiph, in Philadelphia of the Arsinites. Zenon [son of Agreophon, of Caunus], a sojourner, lent to Nikandros [of so-and-so]...
4     Apies, silver, drachmas 150. This is the silver which Nikandros agreed [to have from him] in place of the 1460...
5     drachmas which [he owed before],
6     towards which also [there] were given to Epiodonos, the royal collector, [silver of] 595 drachmas. [Let him repay] of this loan, [each month]...
7     the installment of silver, 25 drachmas, beginning from the month Epeiph of the 5th year. [But if he does not repay according to what is written], let him pay [one-and-a-half] immediately, and execution shall be for Zenon according to the laws [and the decree. Surety for Nikandros] of the loan for [payment is so-and-so]...
8     Sisinous, Persina, with the authority of Nikandros son of Aristombrotos. ... on behalf of Zenon, executing [according to these terms]...
