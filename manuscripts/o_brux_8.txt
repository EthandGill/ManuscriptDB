[TRANSLATION]
1     A measurement into the treasury of the metropolis, produce of year 16
2     of Trajan Caesar the lord, Epeiph 9, in the name of Onnophrios
3     Psenminio, wheat artabas 6, and in the name of Phaphis, Phaphis, wheat artabas one-third
4     becomes wheat artabas 3. Gaius I have signed.
5     Germanos [son of] Horion, tax-collector of grain of the metropolis
6     [to Onnophres] Psenminio. I have received wheat artabas one-quarter.
