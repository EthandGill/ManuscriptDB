[TRANSLATION]
1     To Mamertinus also called Herakleides and
2     to his sister Besous, this (woman)
3     acting through the aforewritten Herakleides,
4     from D[io]nysios, son of Heron, from the village
5     of Kerkesoucha Orous of the division of Polemon. I wish to lease from you
6     the (property) belonging to you near the village of Teptynis,
7     a two-thirds share of a catoecic allotment
8     of five arouras, held in common and undivided
9     with your other sister Isidora [al]so called Didyme, for four years from the present 19th year: in the
10    first and third year for the sowing
11    of hay, at a rent per year, without seed, of twenty-eight silver drachmas; and in the second and fourth year for the sowing of barley,
12    at a rent each year, together with the three artabas of barley seed which I shall receive,
13    of a third of the whole of barley, of artabas
14    twenty-four, without deduction and free of risk. And I shall perform the yearly work,
15    all that is fitting, at my own (expense), doing
16    no damage; and I shall pay the rent
17    in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor of the allotment
18    by your own four-choinix measure, the
19    measuring being done through one of your (people); and the (money-)rent in the month Phamenoth, the yearly cost of transport being
20    upon me
21    for the pressing (of work), but the conveyance of the
22    rents-in-kind upon you. And after the time
23    I shall hand over the arouras clean from
24    rushes, couch-grass, and all rubbish, if it seem good to lease (them).
25    Dionysios, aged about 43, with a scar on the right wrist.
26    Year 19 of Lucius Septimius Severus
27    and Marcus Aurelius Antoninus and
28    Publius Septimius Geta, Pious Augusti,
29    Thoth 5.
