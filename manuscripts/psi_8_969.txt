[TRANSLATION]
1     Achilles [to] Seleucus greetings and good health.
2     We have appointed [Arch]edemon of the private [matters]
3     with Diocles toward the superintendence of the according-to
4     [the] law all things. in which if you should be needed, [he] cooperates more zealously, in order that for us
5     [nothing] deficient may come to be. and of yourself take care, friend.
6     … be strong. of year 1, Epei[ph] 5.
7     … to Seleucus.
