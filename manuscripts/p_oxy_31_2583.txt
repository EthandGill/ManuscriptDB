[TRANSLATION]
1     [They] acknowledge to one another, Diophantos son of Herakleios
2     [son of Dio]phantos, and Horion by adoption (son) of the same Herakleios,
3     [both] from the city of Oxyrhynchus, that they have divided
4     among themselves equally the things left to them
5     by Herakleios — furniture and utensils and household stock and wheat and other pulse, and the donkeys which they have, likewise equally, two donkeys — and that each
6     of them has received there into his own … which has also been allotted to the half share belonging to them, namely from the two donkeys one donkey.
7     And that neither of them shall make claim against the other concerning these things, and Diophantos not even concerning the
8     advance made for the common (farms) of himself and Herakleios,
9     namely all the farms, and the
10    common balance-account which Herakleios held, because he has received from Horion the share falling to him; and both shall make no claim concerning
11    [an]y other matter, written or unwritten,
12    … of those belonging to them or to Herakleios
13    … from the times above up to
14    [the pres]ent day, on no pretext whatsoever.
15    … all the sheep and the fodder that is gathered in the fodder-store, and the cultivation
16    … of the time of the lease which
17    [Herakleios lea]sed about Schoibis of the allotment
18    [in accord]ance with the lease, belongs to Diophantos
19    … a half and a quarter share, and to Horion
20    … …
