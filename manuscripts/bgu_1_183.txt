[TRANSLATION]
1     … in the [fourth year] of Imperator [Caesar] Domitian Augustus Germanicus, in the month Germanikeios, on the new moon, in Pachon
2     [on the new moon], [in] [Sokn]opaiou Nesos in [the Herakleides division o]f the Arsinoite nome.
3     [Horos] son of [Tese]nouphis son of [T]es[enouphis, a Persian of] the descent, about forty-three years old, with a scar on his left calf, acknowledges to his own full-sister by the same father
4     [and the same mother], and she is the woman who was previously and is now living with him, Erieas, about thirty-five years old, with a scar,
5     [with as guardian] her son Pekysis son [of Horos, about] twenty-two years old, with a scar on his left shin, that he, the acknowledging party, has received from her forthwith by
6     [hand from the household, settled] upon herself, a dowry of silver, capital sum, twenty drachmas. Let the married couple therefore live together with one another as they also lived together before marriage, [Horos] supplying [all that is needful] and the clothing [and all the other things which] are [fitting] for a wedded wife according to the means of his livelihood, whether at home or abroad, throughout the times of their cohabitation, blamelessly; and if it should turn out (that they separate), let Horos the acknowledging party repay to Erieas the dowry within thirty days from whenever it is demanded; and if he does not repay it,
7     … the right of execution belonging to Erieas [both from] the acknowledging party and from all his property, as if by a legal decision, this [contract] remaining valid in its place, indissoluble. And present at the magistracy is the mother of the married couple, Satabous, daughter of Horos son of Haryotes, about sixty-five years old,
8     [with a lentil-mark above on] the left, with as guardian Stotoetis son of Erieus, the son of her sister Tanephrimmis, about forty-eight years old, with a scar,
9     [on the right eyebrow, who con]sents that after her own death (she grants) to her own children Stotoetis and Horos and to her daughters Soeris and Erieas, and to the children of her deceased
10    [other] son Tesenouphis, (namely) Stotoetis and Horos — to her son Stotoetis she grants, after her own death, a house and courtyard on the
11    [southern side and] a fourth part of another house and courtyard, formerly Psentapiamis's, and a fifteenth part of another house and courtyard formerly Patoulos's; [and likewise] [also] the same Satabous grants, after her own death, to the aforewritten other son Horos and to her daughter Erieas, jointly in equal shares,
12    [a half] part of another new house and courtyard belonging to her on the southern side, with all that pertains to it; and to the same chil[dren Horos and Erieas] Satabous grants a fourth part of the aforementioned house and courtyard formerly Psentapiamis's; and to Horos alone she grants
13    … [garments(?)] … two … and to the two sons Stotoetis and Horos she grants af[ter] her own death a vacant plot within the enclosure of the sanctuary of Soknopaios
14    [the great god], and the same Satabous grants to the two sons St[oto]etis and Horos a Theban millstone, and whatever movable goods she may at any time leave behind,
15    [implements and] all [house]hold furnishings, while she has charge of and … them; and to her daughter Erieas Satabous grants the women's adornment about herself
16    [and to (her) daughter So]e[r]is likewise Satabous grants, after her own death, the remaining half part of the aforewritten formerly
17    [Psentapiamis house] and courtyard; and to the children of her deceased other son Tesenouphis, (namely) Stotoetis and Horos, to each
18    [in addition] eight silver [dr]achmas, on condition that the two … Stotoetis and Horos shall pay (the debt) which Satabous owes by hand without bond, of silver
19    eight hundred drachmas; and that the burial and funeral due to Satabous belong to them jointly in equal shares without reckoning, for as long a time as
20    Satabous lives, she having the authority over all her own (property) to sell, mortgage, dispose of to whomever she wishes without hindrance. The subscribers: for the acknowledging party, Horos son of Stotoetis, about 24 years old, with a scar on the middle of his forehead; and for Erieas and her guardian, Leonides
