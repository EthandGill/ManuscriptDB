[TRANSLATION]
1     2. Polis … of 2080 drachmas.
2     In the twentieth year of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Parthicus
3     Maximus Britannicus Maximus Pius Augustus, in the month Panemos (=) Pharmouthi 4,
4     through the exchange-bank of Anoubion son of Ammonios, of the Matidian (tribe) also called Kallitekneios, in Antinoopolis.
5     Polis son of Kastor son of Dioskoros, his mother being Theus, from the hamlet of Chairephanes
6     of the [Hermopolite] nome, (acknowledges) to Eudaimon son of Ammonios son of Ammonous, of the Sabinian (tribe) also called Trophonieus, about 49 years old, with a scar on his left knee, that Eudaimon has received from Polis, according to this banker's-deed, the (money) which he had lent him in the present year, on Phamenoth 9 in Memphis, according to the deed of the bank of Bernikianos son of Methysion in the same Memphis, of silver
7     principal two thousand eighty drachmas — total of silver 2080 drachmas — and as for the drachma-interest on these up to the present day, Eudaimon declares that he has received it from him in parts, by hand, all of it, in full settlement;
8     and that neither Eudaimon nor his agents nor others on his behalf make or shall make any claim against Polis or his agents, either concerning the aforesaid principal or concerning [its interest] or concerning anything else whatsoever in the whole, written or unwritten, up to the present day, because he has received everything in full;
9     and that … the aforesaid banker's-deed of the loan is to be void for him and for everyone who produces it, of which
10    the original he gave back to him likewise for cancellation and annulment. Eudaimon son of Ammonios son of Ammonous: I have received the two thousand eighty drachmas and make no claim, as aforesaid … the one also called Aurion son of Herminos son of Euporos, of the Matidian (tribe) also called Demetrieus, [have] read out to Eudaimon the deed of the settlement as it contains. Neilos son of Kephalion, of the Matidian (tribe) also called Markianios:
11    I witness the [settlement]. Apollon son of Apion, of the Sabinian (tribe) also called Gamelieus, I witness. Didymos son of Hephaistion, of the Athenaian (tribe) also called Salaminios, I witness. Theon also called Tiron, son of Harpokration, of the Traianian (tribe) also called Ktesios, I witness. Polis son of Kastor: I have paid out as aforesaid. Hermias
12    son of Hermias, of the Sabinian (tribe) also called Gamelieus, I wrote on his behalf since he does not know letters.
