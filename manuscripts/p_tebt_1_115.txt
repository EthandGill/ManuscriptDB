[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 2, account with Horos.
2     From 17 arourai, of which:
3     fallow-land 8 at (a rent of) 8½ each make of wheat 68;
4     stubble-land 5 at 5 each make of wheat 25;
5     the rent-total comes to of wheat 93.
6     Payni 7, to measure into the royal (granary) 61,
7     and to Heliodoros of wheat 30, to the porters of wheat 3, which is of wheat 33;
8     for entertainment, to Artemidoros for sale, of wheat ½;
9     the whole comes to of wheat 91⅔.
10    Sokeabonthis, of the god's land, fallow 4;
11    of lentils 2 arourai at 5 each make of lentils 10;
12    of fenugreek 2 at 5 each make of fenugreek 10. This makes the account of year 2.
13    Likewise the account of year 3. From 17 arourai, of which:
14    of cowpea 7, remaining sown with wheat 10, of which:
15    fallow 4 at 8½ each make of wheat 34,
16    and stubble-land 6 at 5 each make of wheat 30,
17    the rent-total comes to of wheat 64.
18    Pachon 23, to measure into the royal (granary)
19    of wheat 54, likewise of wheat 2, make of wheat 56,
20    to the pastophoroi of wheat 3, to the priest of Soknebtynis ½, to Hermouthis 6,
21    make of wheat 3⅚, make 59⅚,
22    to the porters of wheat ⅚, to the officials of wheat ⅚, make of wheat 1⅔,
23    the whole comes to of wheat 61⅓, the resulting balance of wheat 4, so that 61⅔.
24    Soukeabonthis, of the god's land, 4.
25    Remaining of wheat 3¾, of cowpea 17.
26    Year 4. 7 at 8½ each, artabas 59½,
27    4 at 6 each, artabas 24,
28    make of wheat 83½, of which for loan 15,
29    to the king 50, make 65, remaining 18½.
30    To Imouthes the pastophoros 3,
31    to Soknebtynis 2½,
32    to the priest of Tebtynis ½,
33    make 6, make 71, remaining 12½.
34    And stubble-land 6, of which:
35    of arakos (vetch) 2 at 5 (make) 10,
36    of lentils 4 at 5 (make) 20,
37    make 30, of which of arakos 10, of lentils 20,
38    make of wheat 12½, of arakos 10, of lentils 20,
39    make 42½.
