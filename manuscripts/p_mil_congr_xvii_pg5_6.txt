1     To Pankrates, chief bodyguard and (officer) in charge of the syntaxis,

3     of the 3rd hipparchy, holder of a hundred arouras, and from Herakleides son of Ariston, Thracian,
4     of the same hipparchy, an orphan, with the guardian
5     who is his by deed of cohabitation, his own mother Thais daughter of Apollonios. Since doubt is raised
6     by the agents of the aforesaid orphan and of his mother [concerning]
7     the boundaries of (the allotment) which still earlier
8     the aforesaid Antimachos happens to have had assigned, an allotment of 40 arouras around
9     Kerkesoucha and the village of Ares of the division of Polemon,
10    in exchange for which the father of Herakleides,
