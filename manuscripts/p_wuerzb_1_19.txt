[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving Trinity, of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, it was written on the seventh of Phamenoth
2     of the present tenth indiction, in Hermopolis of the Thebaid.
3     To the public account, through you Flavius Magistor, the most illustrious assistant of the accounting-office and diastoleus
4     of the division of Ammonios and Anatolios and Germanos and Taurinos son of Kallinikos of blessed memory,
5     from the said (city) of the Hermopolites, Flavius Christodoros, son of Aphous of most excellent memory, from the same
6     city, greeting. May your illustriousness be pleased, receiving this my instruction, to relieve the community of the village
7     of Tlethmis, in the public registers under you, from the public (dues) rendered to you by them on behalf
8     of the twenty-four arouras of sowable land sold to me by him, more or less, with all their
9     right, lying in the plain of their village of Tlethmis, of clean grain with freights and hundredth-taxes
10    and all kinds of expenses, eight and two-thirds artabas and fourteen keratia of gold, according to the following apportionment,
11    thus: through Ieremias son of Psas, one and one-third artabas of grain and three keratia, in the name of the acquisition (title) of
12    Heraklammon and Phoibammon, through the assistant of the accounting-office, and through Lolous the interpreter, of grain
13    one and one-sixth artaba and one and a half keratia, in the name of the acquisition of the same (persons), through the same assistant of the accounting-office,
14    likewise, [through] the heirs of … -ske Pees… -s, three artabas of grain and four and a half keratia, in the name of the acquisition
15    … of the illustris, through the same accounting-office, and through Menas son of Ioannes, two and two-thirds artabas of grain, in the name
16    [of Asyncritios] son of Philammon, through the same accounting-office; likewise, through the same Menas son of Ioannes, four keratia, in the name
