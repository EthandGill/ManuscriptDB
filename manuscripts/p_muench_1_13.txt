r.63   καὶ ἐμμεῖναι τούτῳ τῷ ἀπαραβάτῳ καὶ ἀσαλεύτῳ ἐγγράφῳ ὠνιακῷ συμβολαίῳ μετὰ τοῦ μηδὲν ἰσχύειν

r.67   ἑξῆς μαρτύρων μαρτυρούντων κατʼ ἐπιτροπὴν ἡμῶν καὶ ἀξίωσιν καὶ ἐπερωτηθεῖσαι κατὰ πρόσωπον

r.70   οἱ μεθʼ ἡμᾶς εἰς ἅπαντα, ἀναγνωσθέντα καὶ ἑρμηνευθέντα ἡμῖν κατὰ τὴν αἰγυπτιακὴν γλῶτταν

r.74   τὴν τελείαν τὴν ἐγκειμένην τιμὴν ἐν νομισματίοις ἑνὶ τρίτῳ ζυγῷ Συήνης

[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the lord and master Jesus Christ our God and savior, in the reign of] the most divine and [most pious]
2     [our master Flavius Mauricius Tiberius the eternal Augustus,] Imperator and greatest benefactor, in the
3     twelfth year and the tenth year of the consulship of his serenity, Tybi the twenty-third of the twelfth
4     indiction, in Syene. To the Aurelias Tso[ne] and [Tseur]e her sister, [full sisters from their] father
5     Apa Dios, originating [from Sy]ene, [furnishing also the subscriber below who signs on their behalf]
6     and the witnesses below who, at their commission, bear additional witness to this written and valid and
7     inviolable contract of sale [to Flavius Patermouth]ios son of Menas, soldier of the unit of Elephantine,
8     and to his wife Aurelia [Kako daughter of Iakobos] son of Pasarais, both originating
9     from the same [Syene], greeting. We the aforesaid Tsone and Tseure her sister acknowledge
10    through this our written deed of sale, having come forward of our own resolve
11    with willing mind and self-chosen will and guileless purpose and unchangeable intent and unaltered
12    reasoning and sincere conscience, with upright understanding, free of all guile and fear and force and deceit
13    and necessity and constraint and flattery and any sort of fraud whatsoever, according to this simple written
14    deed of sale, but of our own resolve, while also swearing the most awful and
15    venerable oath both of God the almighty and of the victory and permanence of our most pious masters
16    of the world, the Flavii Mauricius Tiberius and Aelia Constantina the eternal Augusti and
17    Imperators and greatest benefactors, that we have sold to you today, the aforesaid Patermouthios and
18    his wife Aurelia Kako, and have conveyed by law of sale and eternal possession and with every
19    fullest right of ownership, from now for the perpetual and ever after whole continuous time,
20    the existing and devolving paternal half-share belonging to us of a courtyard, ruinous and Libyan,
21    being single-storied and roofless, lying in the quarter of the Syenites near the southern part of the
22    fortress, on the lane of the camp and of the Tripourine, and having come to us from lawful
23    inheritance of our father at rest Apa Dios, he too having received it from succession of his parents.
24    Once and for all, nothing at all has been left to us unsold of the aforesaid half-share
25    of the courtyard, of which the other half-share of the courtyard belongs to you, Patermouthios and your wife,
26    by right of a deed of gift from John also called Paptsios, in accordance with the previous
27    purchase-deed and the possession and ownership which is under us. The neighbours of the whole courtyard, from which we have sold you
28    the half-share of the ruinous courtyard, are: south, the public wall of the mountain; north, a public lane;
29    west, a public lane; east, the common wall of the house of Paeion son of Apa Dios the sailor; and the authentic
30    door opens to the north onto the public street; or whoever may be the neighbours all round on every side, foursquare
31    and four-sided, just as we the aforesaid Tsone and Tseure her sister
32    have dictated by word of mouth, we the sellers and conveyors, for the price mutually agreed and pleasing
33    or rather given between us both and at an unalterable reckoning of gold, principal of one and a third gold solidi
34    by the standard of Syene, making 1 1/3 gold solidi by the standard of Syene, which full and worthy price, on the spot, we the aforesaid
35    sellers have received from you, Patermouthios and his wife Kako, the buyers,
36    on the present day from hand to hand to us out of your house, in number and weight in full, after which we will not
37    proceed against it, nor any other person in our name at any time ever, but we approve and will guarantee to you
38    and to those after you, both we and those after us,
39    this deed of sale, with every guarantee, throughout, against
40    everyone who shall come against you or lay claim, at any time or season, with every guarantee and clearance at our own expenses and costs, according to the law concerning sale and guarantee, on condition that
41    you, the aforesaid Patermouthis and his wife Kako, the purchasers of the aforesaid
42    half-share of the courtyard from our paternal Apa Dios, now roofless and uncovered, from foundations
43    to air, with every right of it, immediately and forthwith with speed shall take hold of and own and
44    be master of the aforesaid whole courtyard of ours, or rather the half-share of the same courtyard, with every right
45    of it, to administer and manage and rebuild to height and dwell in for yourselves and lease out and make
46    exchanges and sell and resell and reshape and give as gift and proceed and cede and bestow
47    and assign to children and descendants and leave to heirs [and] successors, to acquire, to use in every way
48    pleasing to you and in every lawful way, and to do everything that the laws permit to
49    independent owners and all that is permitted to undisputed owners, with every fullest right of ownership,
50    according to all power and ownership and eternal possession, validly and unhindered, of the same
51    half-share of our courtyard, while we the aforesaid Tsone and Tseure her sister, the sellers,
52    or no children or descendants or strangers or kin, on the father's or mother's side, being able nor
53    being about to be able to proceed against you or your children or descendants or successors or holders, or to sue or
54    accuse or bring accusation on account of this sale, or to transgress or disturb this our
55    sale, now and at no time or season in any way on any pretext whatsoever, because, as we have said, we have received from you the full aforesaid price in one and a third solidi
56    by the standard of Syene. And the one coming against you or attempting to lay claim, on account of this half-
57    share of our courtyard from foundations to air, we the aforesaid sellers shall remove and
58    defend and drive off and clear at our own expenses and costs and those of
59    our heirs. And if we should be unable to make the defense and the clearance on account of this
60    sale, on which we are to give as fine of penalty and transgression six gold solidi by the standard of
61    Syene, exacted in fact and force from our property, and with nothing availing against the written terms
62    as aforesaid, and the damages and costs and losses; and even so it being constraining to acquiesce
63    and abide by this inviolable and unshaken written deed of sale, with nothing availing
64    against the written terms as aforesaid. And for your security we have made this present
65    sale, valid and firm and unbreakable and unshaken and lawful, produced and published everywhere
66    before every authority and power in every place or time, with the subscription of the one signing on our behalf and of the
67    witnesses below witnessing at our commission and request; and being formally questioned face
