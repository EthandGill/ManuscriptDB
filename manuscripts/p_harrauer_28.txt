[TRANSLATION]
1     Greetings. …
2     [something] indeed …
3     [something] arouras [and] …
4     itself [unfinished] …
5     not to have us the [seeds]. We therefore request
6     you, if it seems good to you,
7     having given orders to write
8     to Charition the grain-tax collector to give us the
9     proper seeds
10    of the lentils and of the beans
11    quickly, in order that the season for threshing might not pass by. We have therefore written
12    to you, in order that you may know.
13    Be well. Year 3, Thoth 20.
