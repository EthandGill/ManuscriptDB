[TRANSLATION]
1     To Achaios, the one in charge of the court in the courtyard,
2     from Euangelos son of Archones, of the Philometorian deme, [and from Apollonios son of]
3     Sarapion … of Alexandria …
4     apart from the court …
5     … of the capital, having brought forward the remaining [of the] established
6     cession-money, of the capital in silver, three hundred drachmas, by the ninth of Phamenoth
7     of the present 12th year of Caesar, he (Apollonios) will cede either to him or to whomever he may
8     direct, through the accounting-office of the colonnade, the property belonging to him in the
9     Beta (quarter), in the square colonnade, in the inner circle, a goldsmith's little workshop, together with the structures built for this purpose,
10    with the trestles … and as one goes down toward the west, of which the
11    neighbours are: on the south Eirenaios, on the north Apollophanes, on the west a road, on the east Sosibios — the expenses of the cession being borne by the cessionary himself, and upon
12    these terms Apollonios shall furnish the cession
13    guaranteed and unimpaired and clear of rents up to
14    the present month Mecheir of the 12th year of Caesar, and from private claims
15    forever, and (he shall) drive off at once at his own expense everyone coming against it or laying claim to it,
16    he, Apollonios, [at his own expense],
17    and it shall not be permitted to Apollonios to say: "I have not yet received (payment)," but
18    neither shall he himself acquire nor make use of the law or custom concerning such matters; and if he transgresses any of these, apart from
19    the agreed terms being valid, he shall further pay in addition
20    what he received into the capital with the half as much again, at once, and other (sums)
21    as a private debt, five hundred drachmas of silver, and the damages and
22    expenses and the fixed penalty, the execution being made
23    for Euangelos both from Apollonios himself and from all his property, as if by legal judgment; and if Apollonios in any way delays in the matters concerning the cession,
24    it shall be permitted to Euangelos, having deposited at a lawful bank in the name of Apollonios the remaining 300 drachmas of silver,
25    the risk concerning the bank being borne by Apollonios himself,
26    and having transferred a copy of this agreement to
27    the accounting-office of the colonnade, to make the cession to himself or to whomever he may choose
28    out of the name of Apollonios, and
29    of those to be dispatched together without portraits, not requiring his presence, the expenses for the transfer
30    being borne by Apollonios himself, and if
31    while for his part Apollonios is ready to make the cession accordingly, Euangelos does not pay him in addition
32    within the appointed time the remaining 300 drachmas of silver, [he shall pay]
33    these with the half as much again, and for the time overrun
34    interest of two drachmas on each mina for each month;
35    and … we request.
36    To be settled. Year 12 of Caesar, Mecheir 8.
