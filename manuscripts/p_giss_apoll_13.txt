[TRANSLATION]
1     [to Apollonius] the master very much
2     greetings.
3     Before all things I greet you, master,
4     and I pray always concerning your health.
5     I was anxious, lord, not moderately, so that I might hear
6     that you were idle, but thanks to all the gods
7     that they guard you blameless. I beseech you, lord, if it seems good to you, and send to us; but [if] not, we die
8     that we do not see you daily. Would that
9     if we could fly and come and worship you; we are anxious for not [seeing] you. Therefore be reconciled to us [and send] to us. Farewell, lord …
10    and all we have [well].
11    On the 24th.
12    To Apollonius, the strategist.
