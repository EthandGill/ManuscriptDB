[TRANSLATION]
1     … sin, to donate the small piece of land in
2     to the holy place of Apa Phoibammon, on account of
3     our need and the need of the poor of our fortress (kastron),
4     this (land) which is called "the field of Pko
5     of Pemhit of the rope-binding of Rmoou" — these, then,
6     the neighbours of that land according to its situations
7     which lie there now: the east of it is the field of Sakau,
8     the west of it goes up to the road of the king; the north of it is the fields of Rmoou; the south
9     of it goes south to the dyke, up to the road of the king. These, then, are the neighbours of that land
10    according to the ancient (boundaries). Now we acknowledge, we
11    the whole community of the people of the fortress of Djeme,
12    those who will subscribe and those who will not subscribe
13    under it, under this deed of donation, that we will not transgress
14    in any of all the things written in it, whether we ourselves or
15    our children or those who shall come after us; and whoever shall transgress in any of all the things written in this deed of donation among the generations that shall come after us
16    shall give twelve solidi (holokottinoi); the very man, after that, shall come to the tribunal (bema)
17    and we shall go to law with him over what he has done; and
18    the (place) of the holy Apa Phoibammon shall go to law with him; and every soul that shall eat
19    from the service (diakonia) of the holy Apa Phoibammon
20    shall go to law with him; and we set aside every authority
21    and every power, while the document
22    shall be valid in his hand, whether lashane (village-head) or
23    administrator (dioiketes) or authority, the deed of donation being
24    valid in his hand, so as not to transgress in any
25    of all the things written in it, whether a small one or
26    a great one; and after that he shall come to the tribunal (bema), so that we may go to law.
