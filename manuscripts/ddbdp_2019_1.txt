[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Harpokras son of Sarapion also called Theon,
2     son of Thonis, his mother being Thaisous, from the city of Oxyrhynchos,
3     to Lucius Calpurnius Firmus and however he is styled,
4     former kosmetes of the most illustrious city
5     of the Alexandrians, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from you the price of wine, the produce of the present second year, in Oxyrhynchite jars
6     of the so-called four-choes measure, sixty-two, total sixty-two, which I shall of necessity repay
7     [to you from what I hold around] Tein of the vineyard estate
8     … [together with] the vintage of the present year,
9     [in the measure by which the whole produce] is drawn off, into which also
10    [I shall supply the empty vessels, and] all of it without delay. But if not, I shall pay you the price that
11    [shall be current at that time], double, [the right of execution belonging to you against] me [and out of all my] possessions …
