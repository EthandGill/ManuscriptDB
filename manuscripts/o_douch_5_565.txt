[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Anumeris
2     Peteuris overseer of fortresses
3     Of Hibeos thirteenth indiction greetings.
4     Give to me and Theonoe grain
5     Italian modii half and barley
6     Italian modii one, becomes grain-barley modius one [and half].
7     I have signed, I the same Peteuris
8     [Overseer] thirteenth indiction.
