[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Father and of the Son
2     and of the [holy] Spirit, of the reign
3     of our most serene lord Flavius Phocas
4     the eternal Augustus and Autocrator
5     fifth year, Athyr 3rd, eleventh indiction.
6     Aurelia Ioanna [daughter] of Kallinicus, mother
7     Eugeneia, from the Panopolitan [nome]
8     to Aurelia Maria, my same-blood
9     [sister] from the same Panopolitan [nome].
10    I acknowledge that I have received from you
11    for my own and necessary need
12    two-thirds of a gold coin
13    having fifteen and one-half carats
14    by the Alexandrian scale,
15    making two-thirds of a gold coin having [fifteen and one-half carats], and this readily
16    I have ready at my own risk and
17    from the resources of all my assets
18    to provide to you, whenever you wish
19    and demand from me, without any
20    objection and all delay.
21    And for security and payment
22    of the same debt I have pledged
23    to you according to the law of mortgage
24    [a share belonging to me, one-third part]
25    of the shared [property] with you according to the
26    [remaining] part, two-thirds of a house
27    [whole] with all of its
28    [rights] situated on this
29    Panopolitan street
30    of the Holy great church
31    adjoined to the house of the
32    heirs of the blessed
33    Timothy, with you having
34    the use of this and
35    habitation in place of the comfort of
36    the same debt, and it is not allowed
37    to me nor to my heirs
38    the same one-third part of the house or any part
39    of it to pledge again or to sell
40    or to make any other disposition
41    against it until payment and
42    full satisfaction of the same debt
43    at my own risk, and when questioned I acknowledged it. Aurelia Ioanna aforesaid
44    all is correct to me, as stated above. I wrote
45    on her behalf Flavius Theodorus
46    Senoutos from Panos.
47    It was written by me, Dius, notary.
48    Mortgage of one-third part of a house … of Aurelia Ioanna …
