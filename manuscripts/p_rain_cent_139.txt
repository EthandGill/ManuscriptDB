[TRANSLATION]
1     Paul presbyter deputy of the city to the brother John collector of Chembabi: provide to Pheiammon the presbyter
2     and to him on whom food is incumbent for his rations of the present fifth indiction to the cancellary of Alexandria vegetables three artabae, amounting to three artabae of vegetables only
3     Thoth sixth of the fifth indiction
