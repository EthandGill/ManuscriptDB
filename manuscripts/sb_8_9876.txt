[TRANSLATION]
1     Of the consulship of [our] lord Flavius Justinianus the eternal Augustus
2     the fourth, Epeiph twenty-second, end of the present twelfth indiction in Herakles
3     city. To the most holy and god-loving abba Paul bishop
4     of the holy [catho]lic [church] this of the Herakleopolite
5     metropolis, Aurelia Manna daughter of John, mother
6     Ania, and N[ymph daughter] of Apollo, mother Thamoulis, with my guarantor I Helia, [s]on of Pa[n]peius, deacon of the same holy church,
7     all from the same city greetings. We have leased to your god-loving care
8     according to law of lease from the existing to us
9     in the fields of the village Noheris of the Herakleopolite nome, plot
10    Tarettary, arouras four, becoming arouras four for sowing fodder
11    into the [sp]or[ing of the] f[o]rtunate fourteenth indiction. And there
12    into the hands [you gave] a[ll] these rents in full as
13    between us has been agreed, us the [le]asing out
14    acknowledging these all public payments.
15    The lease valid, and being questioned we acknowledged. Aurelias Manna
16    daught[er] of John, and Nymph, daughter of Apollo, the aforementioned, with my guarantor I Helia son of Panpeius deacon,
17    we have made this lease as aforesaid.
18    Aurelius [Chre]stus of John from Herakles city wrote
19    for them not having learning.
20    Three hundred forty-three
21    through me Mena.
22    Through me Mena it was completed.
