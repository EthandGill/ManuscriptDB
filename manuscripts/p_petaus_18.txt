[TRANSLATION]
1     Hermophilos, royal secretary of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome, to the village-secretary of Ptolemais
2     Hormou, greeting. Of the instruction sent to me by the strategos of the division,
3     Apollonios, in the name of Ischyrion also called Artemas son of Sabinos,
4     who wishes to purchase from uncultivated vineyard land on the reserve-list about the aforesaid village, for the planting
5     of a garden vegetable-plot, 16 1/32 of an aroura, the copy is sent, so that, having gone to the
6     place and acted accordingly, you may declare to me, since the responsibility and risk
7     rests with you if anything be done improperly. I pray for your health. Through Aviola also
8     called Dionysodoros, assistant.
9     Year 25 of Aurelius Commodus Antoninus Caesar the lord, Pachon 20.
10    Apollonios, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome, to Hermophilos,
11    royal secretary of the same division, dearest one, greeting.
12    Of the petitions given to me by certain persons wishing
13    to purchase, some from the administration of the special account (idios logos), others from
14    the reserve-list, the copies of the properties declared through them are sent to you, dearest one, so that you may know and fulfil your own parts.
15    I pray for your health, dearest one.
16    Year 25 of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Commodus
17    Antoninus Augustus Pius Armeniacus Medicus Parthicus
18    Germanicus Sarmaticus Britannicus Maximus, Pachon 20.
19    And they are five in number.
20    To Apollonios, strategos of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
21    from Ischyrion also called Artemas son of Sabinos, of those [from the village]
22    of Ptolemais Hormou. I wish to purchase from uncultivated vineyard land
23    on the reserve-list about the aforesaid village, from the added portion
24    of a breach (from a canal-cutting), for the planting of a garden vegetable-plot,
25    16 1/32 of an aroura; neighbors on the south and west, a canal-cutting, [on the] north, the canal called
26    Of Prote, on the east, the vineyard of me, Ischyrion; at a price of 52 drachmas per aroura and
27    the additional charges, on condition that, having been shown (the land), I shall pay the price and the additional charges to
28    the public bank. I shall have for the working-up and reclamation a tax-exemption of 3 [years], after which I shall pay the paradisiac
29    dues on the like (plots). And there shall remain to me and to my offspring and to those who succeed from me the possession and ownership of this, inalienable, for
30    ever, and it shall be guaranteed to me free from all public dues
31    and every kind (of charge) up to the time of the showing (of the land),
32    if it seem good for the order to be given.
