[TRANSLATION]
1     To Protarchos,
2     from Dionysia, daughter of Achilleus, a citizen, with her guardian Eudemos
3     and from Hierax, son of Tithoes, and Papos, son of Andronikos, both Persians of the epigone.
4     Concerning the matters about which we were at variance, we make agreement with one another on these terms: since Hierax and Papos have leased from Dionysia for three years from Thoth of the present
5     twenty-sixth year of Caesar the property belonging to her and to her minor son
6     Achilleus, son of Ptolemaios, around Arsinoides also called Eurylochou in the so-called Kolpos, which
7     Hierax, son of Hierax, formerly held, within the existing boundaries, which adjoins the so-called Batheia
8     and the places called those of the Dioiketes and the Pisat and Emouei and, in part, Telkaror,
9     a papyrus marsh, at a fixed rent each year, free of all charge and expense, of five thousand drachmas of Ptolemaic silver, on which condition they shall pay these to Dionysia each year, giving from Thoth to Mecheir
10    per month two hundred and fifty drachmas of silver, and from Phamenoth to Mesore
11    per each month five hundred and eighty-three drachmas, two obols of silver,
12    on these terms that they hold fast to the lease for the term, it not being permitted
13    them to give to the laborers who work the lease more than is given in the Kolpos for the works, and they shall pay also extra each year
14    one hundred six-armful loads of papyrus, and they shall work the leased property, the parts that
15    are suitable by cultivating them, and the rest with fitting goodwill and not litigiously …
16    … nor speak falsely nor cut heavily nor in part …
17    work nor sell rope-stuff nor sublet to others nor pasture in the marsh cattle, neither their own nor others', and whatever they may seize
18    they shall give up at their own expense; and they shall pay the marsh in the proper seasons, and further also dig out and clear the streams by themselves and the side channels
19    and shift them and provide them navigable such as they received also at their own expense; and if it happen to them …  an incursion to occur or
20    a flood or a cutting of timber or a cause against the city or from the public
21    of taxes … or a stoppage or against … or from a god something happen concerning the
22    lease as a cause, none of these shall be against Dionysia, and it shall not be permitted to the
23    lessees to abandon the lease within the term; but if they transgress any
24    of these things, they shall be at once liable to seizure and held
25    until they fulfill and pay to Dionysia whatever they owe toward the taxes with the addition of one half, and for each load which they do not deliver the price current at the time, and as penalty another thousand drachmas of silver,
26    and it shall be permitted to Dionysia, since they violate the contract, to eject them within
27    the term and sublet to others and exact the future surplus profit
28    upon the re-leasing, the exaction being made for Dionysia from the
29    lessees and from any one of them whom she chooses and from the property
30    of them all, just as by legal judgment, any pledges that they bring forward being void,
31    of all kinds, and all protection; and as they fulfill according to
32    the aforesaid terms, Dionysia too shall furnish them the lease
33    undisturbed for the term and allow them to carry off the produce of it to their
34    own use, and not eject them within the term nor exact anything more,
35    or else herself pay both the damages and expenses and the equal
36    penalty just as by legal judgment, apart from the agreements being valid; and the
37    hundred loads the lessees shall deliver at the harbor of Anolmethios. We request (this).
