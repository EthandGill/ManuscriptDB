[TRANSLATION]
1     [to (you)] originating from this
2     fair city of the Antinoites, from me,
3     Peret, son of Iouab, from his mother
4     Rhosyne, Hebrew by nation, but residing here in the same city, greeting.
5     I, the aforewritten Peret,
6     a Jew, acknowledge through this my written
7     contract of lease, willingly and of my own free choice, to have leased from your
8     [Ex]cellency the workshop belonging to you and pertaining to you,
9     an [ent]ire salt-relish (workshop),
10    once so, but now to become a dyeing-workshop,
11    situated on the southern
12    public market, below
13    your residential house, which is
14    in the same southern quarter of this
15    city of the Antinoites, on condition that I
16    have it under my control for dwelling and use
17    for whatever time you wish,
18    reckoned from the new moon of the
19    coming month Epeiph, beginning of the third
20    indiction, both itself and the following time, yearly,
21    of the cell, that is the workshop, in whatever condition it is,
22    with all its rights, per each
23    month, one and one-half and one-quarter keration of gold,
24    without any dishonesty or delay, [at my own risk and from the resource] of all my property …
25    … [unt]il the time of repay… …
