[TRANSLATION]
1     [They] have given those from the village Aphrodite of the Antaiopolis [nome], through John the receiver of the same village,
2     at account of the honorable commentariensis, the customary gift given according to custom of the second canon
3     of the second indiction, that is gold coins two minus four karats, [to the] goldsmith of the same village,
4     comes to gold coins 2 minus 4 karats of gold. and for your security and of the state we have made this receipt
5     under our signature as aforesaid. Flavius Phoebammon son of Asclas singularis and collector of this account,
6     [it] accords to me, the receipt as aforesaid. Flavius Phoebammon son of Victor singularis and collector of [this] account,
7     [it] accords to me, the receipt as aforesaid
8     Receipt of the commentariensis canon of the second indiction of gold [comes to coins 2 minus karats] 4 of gold
