[TRANSLATION]
1     Aur[eli]us Sarapion son of Herme[ios], councillor of the
2     splen[di]d city of Antinoou, to Aurelius Isidoros
3     son of Ptolemaios, from the village of Ka[r]anis, greeting.
4     I acknowledge, through this deed of security, to have received back
5     from you that which our manager Aurelius Chairemon
6     supplied to you without a written deed, still from before,
7     of silver, of Imperial coinage, thirty talents — they come to: talents 30 — in full, concerning which I have no cl[a]im
8     against you nor will I proceed against you, neither I, Sarapion, nor
9     Chairemon, nor those acting through me or through us, because with
10    good faith you have restored these (to me). And when(ever)
11    at any time you suffer trouble or any demand from anyone con[cerning the af]oresaid this silver, I myself,
12    Sarapion, will at once remove (it) at my own expense, so that in every way the security and freedom from care may be furnished to you by me. And having been formally questioned, I acknowledged.
13    In the con[s]ulship of our masters Constantine and
14    Licinius the Augusti for the 2nd time, Pachon 9.
15    Aurelius Sarapion son of Hermeios, the aforesaid councillor,
16    I have received back from Isidoros that which our manager Cheremon supplied [to him] without a written deed, of silver, thirty talents in full, concerning which I have no claim
17    against him, but also the one who proceeds against (him) I will remove
18    as is set forth above. And having been formally questioned, I acknowledged.
19    Aurelius Chairemon, manager of Sarapion, I consent to this
20    receipt of the aforesaid silver, of the thirty talents, as is set forth above,
21    and having been formally questioned, I acknowledged.
