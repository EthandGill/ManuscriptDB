[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Mas[ter] Jesus
2     Christ our God and Savior
3     and of our Lady the holy Mother of God
4     and of all the saints, Mesore 10, 4th indiction, at Arsinoe.
5     To Flavius Ioannes, the most magnificent p[agarch]
6     of this city of the Arsinoites, Aurel[i]us
7     Ioannes, son of Menas, from the village of Eleusis
8     of the Arsinoite nome, greeting. I acknowledge that of (my) own free
9     will I am ready, by the seventeenth
10    of the present month Mesore of the current
11    fourth indiction, to make and prepare the …
12    in my own holding, on account of the
13    Saracens, one coverlet and three mattresses
14    by the measure of the same Saracens.
15    But if I do not do these things by the said term,
16    I am bound to provide to your magnificence,
17    from my own house and from my own resources,
18    for the coverlet, gold solidi
19    six, and for each mattress, gold
20    solidi three. The acknowledgement is valid,
21    and on being questioned I gave my assent. Aurelius Ioannes,
22    son [of Menas], all is agreeable to me as [aforesaid],
23    [through] me, Ioustos.
24    Acknowledgement made by Ioannes, [s]on of Menas, to Flavius Ioannes the most magnificent p[agarch] …
