[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year] 34, Choiak … 1, in Syene of the Thebaid, before Aristodemos the agoranomos.
2     Isias also called Sennesis, daughter of Patepnebteus, an aphontissa, with her guardian, her own
3     husband Neoptolemos son of Neoptolemos, a Cyrenaean of those of Ptolemaios and of the Sons,
4     acknowledges to Esaroeris son of Petosiris, the unsalaried (?) aphontes, that she has settled with him and with Tagos,
5     his wife, concerning the petition which she lodged against them in the 34th year before the
6     judges in Ptolemais of the Thebaid, of whom Philinos is introducer, through which she charged them
7     with outrage and blows, which she assessed at two talents of bronze and one hundred drachmas of silver,
8     and that she will not proceed against them concerning the aforewritten matters, nor concerning the things
9     disclosed [through the] petition, in any way whatever; and if she does proceed,
10    [or anyone] else on her behalf, the proceeding shall be void for them, and Isias shall pay in addition
11    [to Esaroeris and to Tagos] a penalty of [five talents of bronze, and as sacred dues to the kings]
12    [of coined silver] … drachmas …, [apart from the] aforewritten things being valid.
13    [It shall be so.] … It has been transacted [through Aristodemos the agoranomos].
