[TRANSLATION]
1     … of the elders
2     [of the village] of Soknopaiou Nesos.
3     [We register] according to the [things] commanded by Tinneus Demetrius
4     [the] most illustrious hegemon, the according-to
5     times, exports of the village
6     written down from the seashore, arurae sixty-nine [and] sixteen
7     not [having been] watered [by rain] toward the present
8     30th year of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
9     Antoninus Caesar the lord.
10    therefore I submit.
11    Heras the village-scribe received the equal [amount] for examination
12    of [the] year 30 of Marcus Aurelius Commodus
13    Antoninus Caesar the lord, Epiphi 18.
