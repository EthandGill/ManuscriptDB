1     … to [Arte]midoros [s]tr[at]e[gos]
2     [of the Ono]uphite (nome) [ … ]
3     [the peti]tion given [to m]e by Dosas son of Stephanos and those
4     [with hi]m, the craftsmen, having appended it, I have annotated; but I mar[vel] how, the matter having been decided both by Maximus
5     [the mo]st excellent and by [m]e, it is still being harassed. For to have written [with regard to what] he inquired, that (persons) be left over, if any who are well-off wish to withdraw from the trade, for these
6     not to go forward (as exempt) — it is [clea]r that I ordered (this) with regard to those having greater means. For since Maximus called
7     to farming those who had acquired private land from (the census of) a talent, but released together with the others the weavers, it is clear that
8     not, if any weaver had acquired a talent, will he be led into a liturgy contrary to the decision of Maximus,
9     but if anyone, having acquired still greater means, takes refuge
10    under the shelter of the trade, it seems to me that the assessment suffices
