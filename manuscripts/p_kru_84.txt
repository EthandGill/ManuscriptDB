[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. It was written in the month Paone 1,
2     [the] 8th [indiction], before the most illustrious, honored lord Psmotos the dioiketes, from the fortress of Memnonia.
3     We, Senouthios son of the blessed (one), with Martha his wife, in the fortress of Djeme of the nome
4     of the city of Hermonthis: that, since in this time, that we are in now, God,
5     he of great wonders, commanded (and) gave us a male child; we named him,
6     namely Pesynthios; God brought a great sickness upon him; after God brought upon him
7     a great chastisement, in return (for which?) we resolved upon this written deed of donation
8     of love unto the prize-winner and the honored martyr, the holy
9     Apa Phoibammon, this (saint) who is situated in the mountain of Djeme, through you, you (being)
10    the most pious and the superior, Apa Sourous the monk, who is set in charge
11    over that place, so that he may come to be confirming, ministering, without any deceit
12    or fear or violence or deception or circumvention, no single necessity
13    being laid upon us; we swear, moreover, by God the Almighty and the safety of our God-given kings who rule over us through the ordinance of God: I write to the prize-winner, the holy martyr, through you, you (being) Sourous the deacon and the superior of that monastery, with those who shall come after you forever, so that the holy martyr Apa
14    Phoibammon may become lord over our beloved son Pesynthios, both over me and over those who come
15    after me forever — because, after God brought the sickness upon him, we took him up,
16    we brought him into the holy martyrium, we set him before the sanctuary, we besought
17    the angel of the holy altar that his mercy might overtake him, that he might beseech Christ Jesus
18    over him, that he might grant to him healing; after these things, the holy martyr had compassion on him,
19    he besought God, he showed mercy to him through the great wonder; in return (for which) we resolved
20    upon this written deed of donation of love, so that he may become a servant
21    unto the holy place and the lamp in the place forever, so that no
22    man may come out against the monastery concerning my beloved son Pesynthios, neither we nor child
23    nor daughter, neither in the praetorium nor outside the praetorium.
24    But if anyone, moreover, shall dare, after these written deeds of love — firstly,
25    indeed, that that one shall profit nothing, but he shall come to be under the chastisement
26    of the just (judge?) who is blessed, and the prize-winner shall exact vengeance from him,
27    it being secured for the holy monastery, the holy martyr,
28    Apa Phoibammon, this (saint) who is situated in the mountain of Djeme. We have drawn up this written deed of donation of love, and we agree to it,
29    it being confirmed, settled, having power, guaranteed before any
30    authority, whether in a city, whether in the fortress, whether in a court of law
31    or outside a court of law, whether before any authority however exalted
32    and glorious, and they shall ask me what I acknowledged. They read it
33    to us, we heard it, we set up witnesses to it, we agreed to it,
34    we released it as (a deed) of love, according to the procedure of the laws.
