[TRANSLATION]
1     To Dion, strategos of the Arsinoite, division of Herakleides.
2     From the weavers of the village of Philadelphia. Money was recently sent to us from the public (treasury)
3     so that we might make public clothing, and we were praying, we who are of
4     the trade, having been reduced to a few, to complete what was commanded.
5     But since even of these four were assigned to a river-voyage, and these, on account of the
6     [ser]vice, were taken away from us and to
7     Alexandria they departed, and still
8     more fewer we became, of necessity we have fled to you for refuge and we ask, if it seem good to you, to grant to allow
9     us, those who still remain, of the
10    craft, to be undisturbed from other
11    services, so that we may complete the assigned clothing and hand it over, and
12    especially because there is imposed upon us the issue of another public garment, which again we
13    shall be required to complete, so that we may have been dealt with kindly.
14    We were in number twelve men, of whom the four were taken away, so that we remaining are eight men.
15    Year 3 of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus
16    Antoninus Augustus Pius, in the month Hadrianos 20.
