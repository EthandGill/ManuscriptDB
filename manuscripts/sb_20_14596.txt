1     Arsiesis and Phamoubis to Ares
2     Eponychus, greeting. We have from you for [the] guest-friendship tax [for] Pachon [and] Pauni of [the] 4th year drachmas 4.
3     [In the] year 4 of Nero [of] the lord, [month] Pauni 4.
4     If but you [should] be transferred, having worked, from [the] Pachon
5     [onwards] you will repay [the] tax.
