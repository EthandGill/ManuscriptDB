[TRANSLATION]
1     Aur[e]lius Longinas son of Alypios, of the mother Zoes,
2     from [the v]illage of Philadelphia of the Arsinoite nome,
3     to Aur[e]lius Sarapammon son of Paesis, from the same
4     village of Philadelphia of the same nome, gree[ting].
5     I wish to lease from yo[u t]he (lands) belonging to you
6     around the same village of Philadelphia, and part
7     of Tan[is], twenty-two grain-bearing arouras, 22 arouras. And
8     it is, by location: [in] the place called Pia[b]aly,
9     of private (land), twelve arouras, 12 arouras, and in the place called Sa, of private (land), five arouras, 5 arouras, and in the place Psaei
10    called, of private (land), two arouras, 2 arouras, and in the place
11    called Zepsei, of private (land), o[n]e aroura, 1 aroura, and in the place called Theme, of private (land), two arouras, 2 arouras, for a period of [f]ive years from the prosperous, coming
12    fifth indiction, the public (taxes) and provision-levies (annonae)
13    and all kinds of impositions and the work of the embankments
14    (being) upon you the landholder; in the presence of Aion son of Gerontios
15    and … and after the period I shall hand over to you
16    the arouras (cleared) of stubble, and it shall not be permitted to me to abandon
17    the lease in any way. The lease (is) valid
18    and secu[re], and on being formally questioned I acknowledged.
19    In the consulsh[ip] …
20    Lease of Lon[gi]nas son of Alyp[io]s, from Philadelphia, of 22 arouras.
