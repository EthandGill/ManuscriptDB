[TRANSLATION]
1   ko(llema) (document-sheet)
2   To Protarchos,
3   from Nikanor(os) son of Isidoros, of the Philometoreios (deme), and from
4   Eros son of Ptolemaios. Concerning the matters in dispute,
5   Nikanor agrees that he has received back from Eros from hand
6   to hand, out of the house, upon release, also of his wife ....
7   Tyche daughter of Dionysios, and further of Chrysogonos son of Isidoros, who is deceased — which (sums)
8   he had lent
9   to them ac[cording to an agreement] through the same court, in the four-
10   teenth [(year) of Caesar,] in Epeiph, of silver, drachmas
11   one hundred ...... and the interest, and that the
12   agree(ment) of the loan is void, and that Nikanor shall not proceed,
13   nor anyone else on his behalf, against Eros
14   through (the matter of) Tyche, neither concerning the [s]ame matters nor concerning
15   any other thing whatsoever, of any debt or claim,
16   written or unwritten, from the times above until the
17   present day; and that Nikanor shall not proceed
18   even against the property left behind by Chrysogonos
19   concerning ...n of the loan,
20   the same, and its interest; and, apart from the things agreed being
21   valid, further that he, if he transgresses
22   these terms, shall be liable both for the damages and for the fixed penalty.
23   (Year) 17 of Caesar, Pharmo(uthi) 1.
