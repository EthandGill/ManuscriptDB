[TRANSLATION]
1     To the Philometor Saviors, greeting,
2     Demetrios …
3     a settler of the infantry, of those dwelling in Euhemeria of the Themistes meris. In the month Choiak
4     of the 50th year I lent to Theotimos son of Phileas,
5     a Persian of the epigone, of those dwelling in
6     Theadelphia of the same meris of the Arsinoite,
7     7½ artabas of wheat, and in the month Mecheir of the same year another 45 artabas, and likewise also in the
8     month Phaophi of the 51st year another 25 artabas,
9     the whole being 77½ artabas of wheat, according to three contracts,
10    through one of them 7½, through another 45, through which it was stipulated, among other things, that he should make
11    to me the repayment of the aforesaid 77½ artabas at the times
12    fixed in the contracts, or pay
13    me for each artaba 3 bronze drachmas. These things being so,
14    and the times of repayment having passed and many others having elapsed in addition,
15    the accused, though repeatedly dunned,
16    does not consent to repay willingly, turning his back on what is right and on the agreements. Therefore, having made my recourse to you, I beg that you send my petition to the local chrematistae, whose introducer is Dositheos, so that, bringing it to settlement and summoning Theon through Demetrios the collector, they may judge that he pay to me
17    the fixed price of the
18    artaba, 403 (drachmas), the bronze sum amounting to 38 talents, 4500 drachmas, in accordance with the
19    contracts. And these things being done
20    I shall have been helped.
21    Farewell.
