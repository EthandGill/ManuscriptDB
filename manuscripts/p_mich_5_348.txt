[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twelfth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, of the month
2     Panemos on the sixth and twentieth, Pachon 26, in Tebtynis
3     of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge Chairemon son of Horos, about twenty-nine years old,
4     a scar on the forehead on the left, and Heroides son of Heroides,
5     about for[t]y-five years old, a scar on the right forearm,
6     and Patynis son of Marepsemis, about thirty years old, a scar under the left eyebrow, to Papnebtynis son of Petesis,
7     a Persian of the epigone, about for[t]y-five years old,
8     a scar on the right shin, that they have given to him
9     a share of one quarter part of what they have
10    on lease from Chairemon son of Penkis,
11    of cataecic allotment near the village, an allotment of
12    fo[r]ty arouras or however many they may be, for four years from the
13    present twelfth year of Tiberius Caesar Augustus,
14    the rent being fixed by the lease per year
15    together with the seed which Chairemon will furnish, of seed-wheat thirty-five artabas and
16    of barley five artabas, of the whole wheat one hundred ninety-seven and a half artabas
17    and of barley fifty artabas and, of shoots,
18    of bread two artabas and, of additional receipt, five drachmas of silver, and (that) he shall take care, both himself and I myself, of what he owes
19    on a loan of wheat, twenty-three artabas,
20    and (that) he shall of necessity comply with the (terms) of the lease
21    and it shall not be permitted to him to abandon the share.
22    Subscriber of Papnebtynis: Eutychos son of Maron,
23    about 60 years old, a scar on the chin [on] the left. Chairemon son of Horos and
24    Herodes son of Herodes and Patynis son of Marepsemis, we acknowledge that we have granted the
25    share as is aforesaid. Herodes,
26    I have written on their behalf. Chairemon son of Horos,
27    I have given orders to write. Papnebtynis son of Petesis: it has been made to me as is aforesaid, and the rest accordingly.
28    Eutychos son of Maron wrote on his behalf because he does not know
29    letters. A copy of the share-agreement has been registered.
30    Year 12 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Pachon 26.
31    A copy of the acknowledgment of Papnebtynis
32    to Cha[i]remon and others.
