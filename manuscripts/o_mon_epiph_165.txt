[TRANSLATION]
1     [I am] the spirit whom you entreat, the lord of the fathers, he has sent a letter
2     Of the lord Helias, concerning the children
3     Of Abraham, and you entreat the sisters, the bishops, that they guard the letter for him, beseeching him
4     Concerning the misery of the orphans, and concerning my unworthiness, apart from …
5     Of your lordship: you know that they … my son, my daughter, and
6     The remaining [things] would carry them to the public [place], and …
7     Of the children of the poor man, so that he might accomplish [something] … not …
8     To entreat them before [the] god that it was pitiful, they …
9     … that they might rest here when god shows grace to you, I am the spirit
10    Of your entreaty, that they entreat the presbyter Marcus, that they guard him in the compassion of the two, the lord
11    Apa John
12    His witness
