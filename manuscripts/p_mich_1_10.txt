[TRANSLATION]
1     Antimenes to Zenon, greeting. If you are well, it would be good; [I too was in good health.]
2     I have appended for you a [copy] of the letter that came to [me] from Sosipatros, so that, knowing (it), you may enter it in account to Apollonios …
3     no travel-allowance was provided to them, nor …
4     by the storm they were carried down to Arsinoe …
5     [Farewell. Year] 28, Peritios 28.
6     Sosipatros to Antimenes, greeting. If you are in good health [in body and] the rest is with you
7     according to your wish, it would be well; we ourselves are also in good health. Having come to us,
8     Ariston and his sister announced that they had been treated with much care by [you in] all respects. You do well, then,
9     in being not unfriendly toward us; for [we too] will try to take all care concerning the matters about which you are zealous and write to us. [Kn]ow that, having been
10    carried down by the storms to Patara, and from there [having hired] a boat,
11    they sailed across to us to Arsinoe. The fare was settled … 35 drachmas. So I have written to you that you may know.
12    [Farewell. Year 28], Apellaios 26.
13    Antimenes concerning Doris, to Zenon.
14    and a copy of the letter from Antipatros
15    of the letter. Year 28, Dystros
16    17, at Mend[es].
