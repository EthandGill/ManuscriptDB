[TRANSLATION]
1     to my lord brother Origenes
2     Apolinarios.
3     I sent through Paul
4     a basket of cheeses, so that
5     provide it to Theoctistus, the collector of the annona
6     and ask him,
7     because he has need of it
8     for [field], for two other days, and write to me
9     about this …
10    to Nikon, in order that each day he goes into the
11    [pasture]. I pray you remain well
12    for many times.
