[TRANSLATION]
1   To Gaius Tyrranius,
2   from Spintheros son of Harpaesis and his wife Apollonia daughter of Lykos, both
3   present, from the city of the Wolves (Lykopolis) of the Thebaid. We present (the matter of) our daughter
4   Tathreiphis.
5   On account of the everyday distress that gripped us, we made
6   to Parthon, slave of Chretos, a contract of service (paramone),
7   and further also the wet-nursing of the child Tathreiphis born of us to each other,
8   which (child) Parthos, having renamed her, gave her the name Prima,
9   and in the meantime, we having brought in security, we were released by
10   Parthon according to the
11   guarantee made through the registry concerning the release both of the paramone-service
12   and of the nursing of the named child; but in the month Epeiph
13   of the 25th (year) of Caesar, Paris, slave of (the household) of Chretos -
14   having perceived Parthon to be in the regions abroad, dared,
15   though we had done no wrong, the household-arrangement
16   to deprive (us of), and to lead away our daughter Tathreiphis and the (girl) Prima
17   and to keep (them) with himself in prison [for] 5 months. Therefore we ask [you],
18   the savior and protector of all, making the struggle on behalf of (our own) flesh,
19   if it appears (good), to order that the aforewritten
20   Paris be produced and be compelled to bring back our daughter, [so th]at
21   we may recover her and [may be be]nefited.
