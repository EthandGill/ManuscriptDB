[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most-honored Phoibammon assistant and first-villagers Koba: Flavianus the scholasticus.
2     Would you be willing to receive for the one evening John also Pet… the most-esteemed
3     scholasticus with god going up to Oxy[rrhynchus]. [Would you] provide also a-little fodder
4     for his six animals the one evening. [May it] be [so].
5     six-hundred forty-three
6     Deliver to the most-honored Phoibammon assistant and first-villagers Kob[a] from Flavianus scholast[icus].
