[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Apollonius, royal
2     scribe [of] the Lykopolites
3     from Aurelius Atres, [son of] Panehytos
4     and [his] colleagues, elders taking over the village-secretaryship
5     of the places around Tertegchon;
6     we report finding nothing worthy to report beside the
7     account and [the high priest]'s oversight of the
8     present month of the present 13th year
9     and [that] none of the priests or [priesthood] has abandoned
10    the religious [duties] …
11    of the 13th year of Emperor [Caesar]
12    Marcus Aurelius Severus [Alexand]er, pious and fortunate, Aug[ustus of the month Epeiph, 30th]. Aurelius Atres, Pan[ehytos, and]
13    colleagues, elders taking over the [village-secretaryship]
14    [of] the places around Tertenchon[tos, we have submitted].
15    Through me, Aurelius Heraclius, [it was written].
