[TRANSLATION]
1     to the grain-collectors of the village of Karanidis, greetings.
2     I received from you as a loan
3     of seeds of the present 23rd year
4     from the produce of the past 22nd year
5     75 of the cleruchic [land] of Kerkesouchis of the public Petalian [lands]
6     of Pyrrhos of 3½ arouras of wheat 3½ artabas.
7     Ailianus wrote on his behalf [because he is] illiterate.
8     of the year 23 of Hadrian, 30th [day].
9     Kastor agoranomos, I have noted [it]
10    artabas three and a half.
