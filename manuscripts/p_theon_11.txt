1     Panechotes, protector of the Panektyres in [Ibion], pr...ia[s]
2     To Matreas, the most honor[ed],
3     Greetings.
4     If it seems good to you, order to be given for the
5     things having been made by me,
6     expenditures, [of] which
7     I will give an account,
8     of silver drachmas
9     three hundred; there are silver drachmas 300.
