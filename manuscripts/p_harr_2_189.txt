1     Year 9 of Emperor Caesar Titus
2     Aelius Hadrian Antoninus Augustus
3     the Pious, Pauni 25, of the census of Pachon. He/she wrote [this] down
4     Pasion son of Dioscorus, slave of Laberia
5     mother of Alexandra, for the poll-tax of the ninth year
6     in Phremei twenty drachmas, which total twenty, with additional charge of copper ten obols.
