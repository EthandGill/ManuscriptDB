[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysius to Apollon[i]us
2     to the brother, very many greetings and health. Above all I watch over you greatly.
3     We greatly suffer
4     and we do not sleep night and day we go around. We ask
5     those coming about
6     your salvation.
7     I swear by the gods
8     all, if not through the matters of Theon Petraeus
9     to Apollonius of Theon …
