[TRANSLATION]
1     Has been recorded Paesis, [son] of Phthomonthos, of Harpmoites, for laography of [Memnoneians]
2     year 15, eight drachmas. Year 15 of Domitian Caesar the lo[rd]
3     Phamenoth 25. Similarly Pharmouthi 27, four drachmas. Similarly Pachon 15
4     four drachmas. Similarly [on] 24, two bakery [charges], one and a half obols. Similarly Epeiph 23, four drachmas.
5     Similarly year 16, Phaophi 6, embankment tax, four drachmas, [which] equal three drachmas [and] four and a half obols. Similarly year 1
6     Choiak 3, embankment tax, two drachmas [and] one and a half obols, [which] equal two obols [and] one and a half chalkoi. D, I have verified.
