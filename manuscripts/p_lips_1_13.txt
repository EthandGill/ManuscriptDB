1     [After the consul]ship of Va[lentinian] Augustus and F[lavius]
2     [Valens] Augus[tus].
3     …os Ph[ilammon] son of Her[mei]os … of Her[mo]polis

5     [to Aur]elius Okeanios son of Dioskourides, town-councillor

7     [and] borrowed from you, for my own and

10    [well]-stamped, double-stamped, two — making gold coins, 2. On condition that,
11    these being intact and at no risk,
12    [I shall give] to you each month, on account of fixed interest, a third of a gold gramma,
13    [the] time being reckoned from the following month Hathyr
14    [of the] present 8th year, of the indiction, without fail
15    [un]til the repayment of the aforesaid two coins, which also I shall repay to you whenever you

17    [the] right of execution belonging to you both from me and from

19    [doc]ument is valid, and on being formally questioned I gave my acknowledgment. Phaophi 25.
