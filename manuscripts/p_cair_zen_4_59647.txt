[TRANSLATION]
1     … more
2     … of the same …
3     because we have neither a place
4     in which we may do business
5     nor have been supplied with papyrus rolls
6     sufficiently, we ask you that,
7     so that we may not be far separated from you because of your
8     ignorance, you order both brick to be brought and timber
9     and reed, so that a little dwelling may be built for us
10    for daily use …
11    …
12    nor may we fall behind
13    in any of the useful matters to be accomplished, and that we be able to summon
14    those who have managed
15    [and to inquire] about the things which
16    we [may wish], and [not] miss hearing any of the [royal] (orders).
17    [And as for the] rest, you would do well to send us 100 papyrus rolls, of which the
18    price we shall charge to
19    the account of Apollonios. And once the papyrus rolls
20    and the place are given to us, we are able
21    not only of the papyrus rolls
22    to bring in the price
23    from the proceeds drawn
24    … of Apollonios's
25    … accounts. For there is no small quantity of silver, and likewise
26    also of grain.
27    … and to yourself, I do not think
28    you are unaware, but we will make it
29    plain to you, if you are willing
30    to give yourself to us
31    for our conducting business properly;
32    and what we ask of you then
33    is to provide for us, for the under-secretaries, three (of them), to one 5 drachmas
34    and 1½ artabas of wheat, and to the
35    two at 3 drachmas each and 1½ artabas of wheat,
36    which comes to, for the year, 122 drachmas
37    and 54 artabas of wheat,
38    and to add for us ourselves for the present, until
39    we meet with Apollonios,
40    of grain per month
41    3 artabas of wheat, and for
42    the salary 10 drachmas.
43    Farewell.
