[TRANSLATION]
1     Of Pekysios, protocomete of Fort
2     of Memnonia, ninth indiction. To Joseph truly
3     I permit you to sow and farm the fields of Ghedaia,
4     fallow, of-Pouar-property, for sowing
5     of ninth crops; of fruits [ten?], which is the third part your
6     from the same fields, and to give the third part your
7     from the pactus; this is, of gold the sixth of the
8     coinage one. Phaophi 19, ninth indiction, in imitation
9     of Paul, Pouar, Pekysios protocomete,
10    it is approved by me. .
