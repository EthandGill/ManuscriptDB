[TRANSLATION]
1     … to …lios also called Alexandros, chief prophet [of the most il]lustrious
2     [city of the] Alexandrians, prophet of Hermopolis the great,
3     [ancient and s]plendid and most august, and (holder) of other offices, and however he is styled,
4     [from Anou]bion son of Pathotes son of Sois, priest of the temple of Thotoperios
5     [at the treasu]ry of the oil-workers of the Hermopolite, registered in the quarter of Phr[our][i]on Apeliotou (East-Fort).
6     Since the most excellent chief-priest Gessius Serenus and the most eminent
7     governor Mussius Aemilianus ordered that all who are consecrated
8     should submit each year the list both of ourselves
9     and of the under-age children, together with the list of the sacred objects handled
10    and of the revenues — doing accordingly with these, I submit to you the
11    list of the objects handled for the present 7th year of our lords the
12    Valerians and Gallienus Augusti, so that nothing may escape notice.
13    It is as follows:
14    [A shrine of Tho]toperios, gilded, having oars silver-plated … one, of bronze, of two cubits;
15    … one, of bronze, of one and a half cubits;
16    … one, of bronze likewise;
17    … one, of bronze likewise;
18    … one, of iron likewise.
19    [And I swear by] the fortune of our lords the Valerians and Gallienus Augusti
20    [that I have left nothing] out. Year 7 of the Emperors Caesars
21    [Publius Licin]nius Valerianus and Publius Licinnius Valerianus Gallienus
22    [Germanici Maxi]mi Pii Felices, and Publius Cornelius Saloninus
23    [Valerianus the] most illustrious Caesar, the Augusti, Phaophi.
