[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our master Maximinus [the most illustrious] C[aesar].
2     Copy.
3     From Aurelia Tapais daughter of Pat[ermou]thios, [her mother being Taisas, from the village of Phila]delphia of the
4     Heraklide[s di]vision: I bought, by [public] cont[ract executed] here in the Arsinoite (nome) on the present day, in the same village of Phila[delphia], a [sm]all single-storey little house and a courtyard, at the price of
5     one talent of silver and nine hundred drach[mas], from the Aurelii Gounthos
6     and Atari, (the latter) acting [wi]thout a guardian by the right of children, and … [with the guardian, her husbund,] Aurelius
7     Nemesion son [of] Iulius, his mother being Theodora, and (from) Taues and Sambathous, the two minors, with
8     the guardian, [their] aforementioned brother Aurelius Gounthos, the five children of Papnouthios, their mother being Tanilla, all from the village of A… [of the Herakleides division, not (yet) re]gistered; ther[efore I sub]mit (this) [so that the entry may be ma]de in accordance [with that which I deposited]
9     by a copy of [the contract of sale; for whenever I make the registration of it I shall demonstrate that it exists and is]
10    cl[ea]r, [held by no one; but if it should appear to belong to another, or to be previously held, the little house]
11    through the re[cord-office shall be no impediment from this entry.]
