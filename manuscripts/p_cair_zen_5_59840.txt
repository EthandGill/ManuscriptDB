[TRANSLATION]
1     Horos to Psasuti greetings.
2     displeasure has come to me
3     toward Demetrius. For Artemidoros having written about the building
4     of which was the groom, he did not say
5     therefore himself come
6     Zenon send to [Artemidoros] so that [brick]
7     be given and a builder build a manger, I have
8     for the building with
9     abusive language, and so that not,
10    if the groom should arrive with a horse, again
11    [be cast out].
12    … Psasuti.
