[TRANSLATION]
1     [NN to NN], greeting.
2     [We have leased fr]om you, on joint surety, from the
3     [prop]erty belonging to you around the plain of Lesser Sobthis,
4     in a circuit of the estate of Arrianos, of your arouras
5     [as many] as there may be, for the sowing of chickling-vetch (arakos), for the sowing
6     [of the pres]ent year 10 and 7 and 2 (=) 14th indiction,
7     [free of deduction and] free of all risk, for each
8     [arour]a that has been irrigated, by remeasurement,
9     [at] two artabas of clean chickling-vetch (per aroura), 2 art. arakos,
10    [whi]ch chickling-vetch, when collected, we shall pay over.
