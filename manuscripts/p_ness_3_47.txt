1     Before all-things I-write, I-proskyne, and I-greet [yo]u, the [one] of-me

3     and for these [to] make a-gift to-the man of-me Anakla
4     because [he] received them and pray on-behalf-of me through the Lord
5     to-master of-me [all] things most-God-beloved and of-all h[onor] and veneration [worthy] genuine fri[end] Stephanos of-Viktorious
6     deacon
7     Patrikios leading
8     I-received the letters of-the of-me master and friend
9     through Ptolemy together-with also fish-sauce 16 pounds which I-weighed them in-the-presence-of Ptolemy […] until
