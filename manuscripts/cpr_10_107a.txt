[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Olybrius
2     and Probinus, the most illustrious, Mesore 1.
3     The Aurelii: Pounab son of Phronton,
4     his mother being Thaesis, and Psais his brother,
5     of the Herakleopolite, with a guarantor of the
6     payment of the declared debt,
7     Aurelius Papnouthios son of Isidoros,
8     his mother being Theonilla, dwelling in the same Ther,
9     to Aurelius Nemesianos son of Hippios,
10    landholder from Herakleous polis, greeting.
11    We acknowledge …
12    four, making of vegetable-seed 4 artabas by the oil-makers' measure,
13    and the vegetable(-seed) we shall of necessity pay back
14    to you at the threshing-floor of the fortunate 11th …
15    which (price) the agreed price
16    in full, here, together with this transaction,
17    we have received in advance and counted out from you in full without delay, the right of execution being yours
18    against both of us, or the one, or against all our property of every kind, of every
19    sort, exacting as if by legal judgment. The transaction is valid, and being formally questioned we acknowledged it. The Aurelii: Pounab
20    son of Phronton, and Psais his brother, the aforesaid, have contracted for the four artabas of vegetable-seed, making of vegetable-seed 4 artabas, and we shall pay back to you in full as aforesaid. Aurelius
21    Papnouthios son of Isidoros (am guarantor) for the repayment of the vegetable(-seed) as aforesaid. Aurelius
22    Achillys (wrote) for Nemesianos, I wrote on his behalf
23    since he is illiterate.
24    … contract … Pounab son of Phronton and Psais his brother, with the guarantor Papnouthios … 11th …
