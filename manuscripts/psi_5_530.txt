[TRANSLATION]
1     Philoxenus to Zenon
2     greetings. Give the mill to Python, so that
3     he may carry [it] across to us. Well then
4     you would do a beast of burden
5     [having-given] if there is [one] available, so that he may carry [it] away. But take care so that we also may bring [back] the oar.
6     Farewell.
