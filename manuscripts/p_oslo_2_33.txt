1     Ptolemaios son of Hermias, a Persian
2     of the epigone, I have leased from Limmnaios son of Limmnaios (the arouras) which
3     he himself also cultivates in the estate of Marcus A…neius Saturnilus,
4     of the holding, [fourteen] arouras, for the present sixteenth year of Tiberius
5     Caesar Augustus, only for [s]
6     owing of chickling-vetch and as fodder for sheep and as a sheepfold and
7     to sow over (with) vegetable-seed, on (a basis of) parts
8     five: of Limmnaios, parts
9     three; the remaining two parts (being) mine,
10    Ptolemaios's. And the
11    carriage-charges and threshing-charges and winnowing-charges (to come) out of my, Ptolemaios's, two parts; the remaining parts
12    three [being Limmn]ai[os's,] who takes them over, as is set forth above. And all the remaining agricultural works of the holding, and the irrigations and weedings and siphon-watering,
13    being (the responsibility) of me, Ptolemaios, without (provision of) seed; and the price to be given for the
14    chickling-vetch being mine alone,
15    Ptolemaios's; and the rents and expenses of the estate
16    being (the responsibility) of Limmnaios; and I will do [everything]
17    just as is set forth above.
18    Year 16 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Phaophi 9.
19    Year twenty-one of Tiberius Caesar
20    Year …

24    a soldier of the legion
25    Limnaios son of Limnaios, to his father
26    very many greetings.
27    Year twenty-one of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, in the month Apellaios,
