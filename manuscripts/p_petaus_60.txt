[TRANSLATION]
1     To Apollonius, strategos of the Herakleides
2     division of the Arsinoite (nome),
3     from Petaus, village-scribe of Syron and
4     other villages.
5     In place of Ptolemaios son of Pathynis and
6     Paaus son of Paaus and Kilikas son of Esouris, collectors of grain-dues through the public farmers of the village of
7     Syron, and in place of Ptolemaios son of Sokrates and
8     Agathos Daimon son of Deios, likewise collec[tor]s
9     of grain-dues through the katoikoi of the same village, [a]nd in place of
10    Petesouchos son of Phanesis and Sarapion son of Horos,
11    likewise collectors of grain-dues through the public farmers
12    of the village of Kerkesoucha Orous, and in place of Peter[m]outhis
13    son of Paaus, likewise collector of grain-dues through
14    the katoikoi of the same village, the eight (who) are
15    nearest completing the (their) term, I give (nominate)
16    the below-written, being well-off and suitable,
17    with the assent and surety of all those from the
18    villages and those farming around them,
19    all of them, on joint-security, to be sent
20    into the lot (allotment) to the most excellent epistrategos.
21    And they are: of the village of Syron, through the public (farmers):
22    Paaus son of Sokmas, his mother being Heras, having means of 800 drachmas
23    Peiion son of Apollonios, his mother being Thermouthis, likewise 800 drachmas
24    Ptolemaios son of Sokrates, surnamed Selpis, likewise 700 drachmas
25    Psonthneus son of Paaus, his mother being Tasouchis, likewise 700 drachmas
26    Pathynis son of Belekennis, his mother being Thermouthis, likewise 800 drachmas
27    Paaus son of Kammatais, surnamed Papkiep, likewise 700 drachmas
28    and through the katoikoi likewise:
29    Kollouthos son of Heras Phelix, likewise 800 drachmas
30    Sokmas son of Psonthneus, surnamed Taa, likewise 700 drachmas
31    Apynchis son of Pathynis Kalatiots, likewise 800 drachmas
32    Miusis son of Mouis, surnamed Kats, likewise 700 drachmas
33    of Kerkesoucha Orous, likewise through the public (farmers):
34    Paaus, fatherless, his mother being Thermouthis, likewise 800 drachmas
35    Anchorimph[is] son of Phaseis, his mother being Thaesis, likewise 700 drachmas
36    Phaseis son of Phanesis, his mother being Taonnophris, likewise 700 drachmas
37    Orsenouphis son of Phanesis, his mother being Tenaus, likewise 800 drachmas
38    and through the katoikoi likewise:
39    Horos son of Haronnophris, his mother being Thaesis, likewise 700 drachmas
40    Onnophris son of [O]nnophris, surnamed Hasie, likewise 800 drachmas
41    I, Petaus, village-scribe, have submitted (this).
42    Year 25 of Mar[cu]s Aurelius Commodus
43    Antoninus Augustus Pius,
44    Pauni 1.
