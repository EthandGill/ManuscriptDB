[TRANSLATION]
1     There has paid, Onnophris (son of) Horos, for poll-tax
2     of year 5, ten drachmas, (for) bath-tax one dr. 1½ obols,
3     total 11 dr. 1½ obols, and surcharges of 1½ obols. Year 5 of Tiberius Claudius
4     Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
5     Phamenoth 21. (2nd hand) Epikrates (son of) Pikos.
