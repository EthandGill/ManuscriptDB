[TRANSLATION]
1     Diogenes and Euporos and Mystes and
2     Marion and Sarapion and Sabinos and Herakleides
3     and Harpokration and Heliodoros, the nine
4     scribes, to Marion and Herodes, scribes of the metropolis,
5     greeting. Since we agreed with you,
6     apart from the house-by-house registration, to compile only copies of the poll-tax lists
7     man by man, and accounts of the catoeci and
8     reports of minors, and apart from
9     the summary, and now too we have agreed
10    to compile only copies of the poll-tax lists
11    in summary form for the same 18th year of Hadrianus
12    Caesar the lord, you providing
13    however many accounts of the 17th year we may need for the compilation,
14    on condition that you, having taken of the aforesaid books only the copies,
15    shall no longer for any reason whatsoever detain you,
16    and that the transcription of the fair copies and
17    the price of papyrus and the registration
18    shall fall to you, the party of Marion;
19    and from the outset we have also received the
20    salary agreed upon for the work
21    in summary, as stated above, there remaining
22    valid the mutual contracts which we hold of one another. This contract we have issued in duplicate. I, Diogenes, wrote
23    the document. I, Euporos, agreed
24    as stated above. I, Sabinos, agreed
25    as stated above. I, Marion, agreed
26    as stated above.
27    I, Mysthes, agreed as stated above.
28    I, Herakleides, agreed as stated above.
29    I, Sarapion, agreed as stated above.
30    I, Harpokration, agreed as stated above.
31    I, Heliodoros, agreed as stated above.
32    … of Hadrianus the lord, Mecheir 10.
