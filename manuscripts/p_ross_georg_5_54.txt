[TRANSLATION]
1     Eleutheria daughter of Pekysis: there was confiscated new wine; there were entered as arrears of principal … drachmas …
2     of interest until confiscation, 209 drachmas, half-obol, 2 chalkoi; uncollectable.
3     Eleutheria of the same (father): there was confiscated wine; there were entered as arrears of principal 4 drachmas, 4 obols, 2 chalkoi;
4     [of interest] until confiscation, … 31 drachmas, 5 obols, 2 chalkoi; uncollectable.
5     Sarapias daughter of Sarapion, town-woman, through her brother Chares, lending in addition …
6     formerly of Apion son of Apollonios, without heir, the co-sharing heirs having died …
7     there were entered as arrears of principal, 5 obols, 1 chalkous … of interest until confiscation, 46 drachmas, 2 chalkoi; uncollectable.
8     Of Dionysios son of Kames: there was confiscated wine of a certain Kames; there were entered as arrears of principal 11 drachmas, 5 obols, 1 chalkous;
9     of interest until confiscation, 422 drachmas, half-obol, 2 chalkoi; uncollectable.
10    Sarapammon son of Herakleides son of Dionysios and of Soerous, fatherless, mother
11    Charis daughter of Chairemon, from the quarter of Moeris, having been confiscated
12    Pachon 1 … formerly of Orsenouphis son of Sansneus son of Sokmeneus:
13    ½ share of the vineyard on the Crown (land), 7 arourae, the remaining … ½;
14    and another remaining aroura, 1 (and) 1/4, out of arourae 2 (and) 1/4; there were entered as arrears of principal 1 obol, half-obol, 1 chalkous;
15    of interest until confiscation … 4 obols, 3 chalkoi …
16    Deios.
17    Of Horos and Kopres, sons of Souchammon son of Lysimachos, from the quarter of the Macedonians,
18    formerly of Orsenoupis son of Sansneus son of Sokmeneus, half share of the vineyard, 1 aroura, ½, of a price
19    of 547 drachmas, half-obol, 2 chalkoi; of interest until confiscation, 1,385 drachmas, … half-obol, 2 chalkoi;
20    … among the uncollectable, because it is on dry (uncultivated) land and the aforewritten man has died without heir.
