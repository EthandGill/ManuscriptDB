[TRANSLATION]
1     Demetrius, toparch and seed-distributor
2     to Dioscurides and the partners, grain-collectors of Koma, greetings.
3     Measure Alexas of Ammou for seed loans of the for the
4     28 year of Caesar, seed [for] which the village-scribe of Kreke has marked
5     to cultivate him near Krekin, sacred land of Ammon and Chonsis, great gods, from
6     Amphiou 5, of Ammon from Kastor's …, Th…
7     of the great gods from […] 5, they total 20; of royal land from Demetrius
8     of Kreke 5 ½ 4, Alla 9 ½, Dion 14 ½ 4, they total 30, they total 50,
9     of wheat artabas fifty, [which] total wheat artabas 50, and receive
10    from him the proper written documents.
11    Year 28 of Caesar, Hathur 27.
12    Measure the aforesaid artabas of wheat
13    artabas fifty, [which are] wheat artabas 50.
14    Year 28 of Caesar, Hathur 28.
