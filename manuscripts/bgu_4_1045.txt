1     Copy of a contract.
2     In the eighteenth year [of Imperat]or Caesar Titus Aelius
3     Ha[drian]us Anto[n]in[us Augu]st[us] Pius, in the mon[th] of Apel[laios 1],
4     Phaophi 1, through the grapheion of Ala[b]anthis of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite (nome).
5     Hermes, freedman of Skylax son of Skylax, registered in the quarter of …ounos…onos, about forty-seven years old, [witho]ut a distinguishing mark, acknowledges to the woman [previously] and now living with him without written contract, [I]sidora daughter of S[atyros, about] forty-five years old,
6     [witho]ut a distinguishing mark, with [her guard]ian, [her kinsman] Eudaimon son of Didymos,
7     about twenty-four years old, [with a scar] on the middle of his face, that he has from her, in her own (right), already long ago the dowry, of approved gold, of quarters
8     four … gold of two denarii an[d] a ring … and of silver, of drachmas
9     one hundred, being (so). Garments at a joint valuation and among the parapherna: an Aph[r]odite … all of bronze, an unguent-box,
10    … a box … it. Let them therefore live together with one another, both He[rmes and Isidora, blame]lessly, just as before, he supplying [all the necessary] things and the clothing and the oth[er things which are fitting for] a wedded wife, according to (his) ability,
11    and she, [Isidora] … and presenting herself blameless
12    … [and if], a difference arising between them, they should separate from one another, [Hermes is to re]pay to Isidora the dow[r]y … in the same ornaments … [up]on the dismissal immediately, but upon a voluntary separation within … days from when[ever]
13    [it] should be demanded, the right of execution belonging [to Isidora] both from [Hermes]
14    and from all the (property) belonging
15    to him, just as
16    by legal judgement. Subscriber
17    for He[r]mes, Herakleides
18    son of Didy[m]os, about 57 years old, with a scar under his left eyebrow. I, Hermes, freedman of Skylax son of
19    Skylax, acknowledge that I have
20    from the woman previously and now living with me without written contract, Isidora, in her own (right), already long ago the
21    dowry, of approved gold, four quarters, an[d] of silver one hundred drachmas, being (so),
22    garments at a joint valuation, and the
23    parapherna, all of which
24    he will re[pay] upon the difference that shall arise
25    for him, as set forth above. I wrote
26    on his behalf, he n[ot] knowing letters,
27    Herakleides son of Didy[mo]s. I, Isidora daughter of Satyros, with my guardian, my kinsman
28    Eudaimon son of Didymos (acknowledge this).
29    It has been registered through the grapheion
30    of Alabanthis.
