[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name] of the Lord and Master
2     [Jesus Christ our] God and Savior, and
3     [of our Lady] the holy Theotokos, and
4     [of all the saints. Ep]eiph 14, of the tenth indiction, in Arsinoe.
5     [To Flavius Joh]n the most glorious illustris
6     [and pagarch] of this city of the Arsinoites,
7     [Aureliu]s Phoibammon, headman, son of Menas
8     [from the villag]e of Boubastos of the Arsinoite nome, greeting.
9     [I acknowledge,] of my own free will, swearing by God
10    Almighty, that I have agreed with your
11    [glor]iousness for the grain of the prosperous shipment
12    of the present tenth indiction, that I am ready
13    … in full and without deficiency …
14    and to pay it in full to it (the office). And if I do not do
15    this, but something of it remain over, that I am liable
16    to furnish to it, for each measure remaining,
17    one gold solidus — gold solidus 1. The
18    acknowledgment is valid, and on being formally questioned I assented. Phoibammon
19    headman, son of Menas: it all stands good for me as aforesaid.
20    … [of Aurelius Phoib]ammon, [head]man, son of Menas, from the village of Boubastos of the Arsinoite nome, to Flavius Joh[n]
21    the most glorious illustris and pagarch. Through me, Kalomenas son of … notary, with God.
