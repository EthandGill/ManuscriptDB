1     With God, Rasdzid son of Chaled, to you John, Elias, Menas the elder from the Laura of Venetus of the city of Herakleous, [it] has been assigned for you
2     for the public [tax] and other canon of the 15th indiction year [?] … as it has been noted thus
3     public land, property, currency 3 [and] 24, [i.e.] 3 [and] 24ths
4     of which paid through 3 [installments] of currency 3
5     remaining [amount] … of currency 24
6     and for the additional payment in account of grain 4 artabas
7     from the account of the …
8     remaining grain 4 artabas in the month of Hathyr 2 of indiction 2
9     total [of] currency 3 [and] 24 [and] grain 4 artabas
