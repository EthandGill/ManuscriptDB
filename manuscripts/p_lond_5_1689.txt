[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the consulship of] Flavius Olybrius the most glorious,
2     [Pa]uni 19, of the sixth indiction.
3     [To Flavius Panolbios the most] illustrious and reverend, town-councillor
4     [of Antaiopolis, so]n of the magnificent memory
5     [of Ioannes the] former town-councillor, Aurelius Isakos, son of his father
6     [Psenthaesio]s, of his mother Elisabet, a shepherd, from the village
7     [of Aphrodit]e of the Antaiopolite nome, greeting. I acknowledge
8     [voluntar]ily and of my own free will that I have leased from your
9     [illustri]ousness, for the time during which I cultivate, the farm
10    [of the most est]eemed Megalos, son of the blessed
11    [and most illustri]ous memory of Dios, namely the
12    [seven arouras] of unwatered land lying in the plain of the village
13    [of Phthla, in the] holding of Piase, called Pia Sarapammonos,
14    … of the aforesaid estate, on condition that I
15    [plant these] with whatever crops I wish, at my own
16    [expenses, an]d that I pay the rent from the produce of the
17    [fortunately coming] seventh indiction, with God, and of the following time,
18    … on a fixed rent, in full and free of risk of flood-failure — which may it not be —
19    [for the same se]ven arouras, ten artabas of wheat and
20    [ten artabas of barley] by your delivery-measure, indisputably.
21    [The lease is] valid and secure, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged. [Aurelius Isa]
22    [k] the aforesaid, I have leased as is stated above. Thomas
23    … the elder, from the village of Thmonechthe, having been requested,
24    [I wrote on] his behalf, since he does not know letters.
25    … through me Psatos it was written … .
