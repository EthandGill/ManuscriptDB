[TRANSLATION]
1   Phanophis, collector of the money-taxes of Elephantine. Paid: Petorz(methis) and Petorz(methis)
2   the younger(?),
3   for the price of the state palm(-dates), of the produce of the 18th year, in the name of Thinzmethes son of Petorzmethes,
4   5 dr.; and for the 16th year, the fifth (instalment), 1 dr.; and for the 1st(?), ... of the Hadrianeion, 4 ob.
5   Year 19 of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Hathyr 10.
