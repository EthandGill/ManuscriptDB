[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting, from Theroys and Teos. We are being wronged by Temsois and Senemenopis
2     and Teteim… and Herieus and the rest of the fellow-thiasos-women from Kerkethoeris, of the division of Polemon. For Soeris, my sister,
3     the wife of the aforesaid Teos, being a fellow-thiasos-member with the aforementioned women and
4     holding the priesthood of the thiasos for four years, it has come about that she has died.
5     …, and since she had no next-of-kin but us, the aforementioned women, when demanded for
6     the burial-money, do not pay it. We therefore beg you, O king, to order Diophanes the strategos to write to Ptolemy the epistates, that, if even now they remain willing toward us,
7     they pay the burial-money; but if not, to send them up before Diophanes so that they may be compelled to pay us,
8     so that through you, O king, I may obtain justice.
9     Farewell.
10    To Ptolemy. Best of all, settle them; but if not, send them to us by the tenth of Choiach
11    so that they may be adjudged before the proper court. Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 29.
12    Year 4, Daisios 27, Hathyr 29.
13    Theroys and Teos against Temsois
14    and the fellow-thiasos-women concerning
15    burial-money.
