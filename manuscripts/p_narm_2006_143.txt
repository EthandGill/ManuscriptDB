[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Didymos, lessee
2     of the shore, to Aurelius Sarapiom, priest of the village Narmouthis, greetings.
3     I have received from you the rent
4     Mesore, the drachmas twenty-eight
5     and poultry two and eggs twenty,
6     [being] complete for the 5th year, and I have no complaint against you, and being questioned I agreed.
7     Year 5 of our lord
8     Marcus Aurelius
9     Probus Augu[stus]
10    Pauni 5.
