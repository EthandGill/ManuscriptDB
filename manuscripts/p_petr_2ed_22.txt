1     [In the reign of Ptolemy son] of Ptolemy and Arsinoe, the
2     [Brother-and-Sister Gods, in the thir]teenth [year], when S[osib]ios son of
3     [Dioskourides was priest of Alexander and] the Brother-and-Sister Gods and the Benefactor Gods,
4     [and Berenike] daughter of Ptolemy [was kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos,]
5     [in the month …, in Krokodilo]n polis of the Arsinoite nome, thus
6     [disposed, being sound of mind and understanding, …] Oitaios, of the (troop) of Lichas, commander of five hundred,
7     [cleruch, aged about …,] balding, hook-nosed, having a scar:
8     … [may] it [be] for me while in health to manage my own affairs.
9     [But if I suffer anything mortal, I] leave all my property
10    [and the billet which I hold from the roya]l (allotment) in Kaminoi of the Arsinoite
11    [nome and …] and the contracts to my own wife
12    [Anaxila …;] and if Anaxila suffers anything mortal, let it be (the property)
13    of those [left] by me, of Kosmelas my daughter.
