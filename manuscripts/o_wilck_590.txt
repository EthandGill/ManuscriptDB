[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklas and partners, collectors of the assessment
2     for the customs-arrears (telonikon deficit) of year 1, to Amenothes
3     (son of) Harbechis. We have received for the assessment, drachmas
4     four, total drachmas 4. Year 2 of Antoninus
5     Caesar the lord, Phamenoth 29.
6     (2nd hand) I, Peteuris …, have signed.
7     (1st hand) Others, Pharmouthi 16, in the name of the same,
8     drachmas two, four obols, total drachmas 2, 4 obols.
9     I, Asklas, have signed. (3rd hand) I, …bthis, have signed.
