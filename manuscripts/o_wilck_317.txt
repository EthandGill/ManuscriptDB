1   Year 30, Phaophi 20. There has been paid into the
2   bank at Diospolis Magna, at which Ptolemaios (presides):
3   Apollonios, two hundred (drachmas) of copper — total 200.
4   P(tolemaios) banker. (verso)
5   (2nd hand) of the son of Sikon,
6   …a.cho( ) to the account.
