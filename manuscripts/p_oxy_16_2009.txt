[TRANSLATION]
1     To the honored Anastasius, administrator of the house of [someone] of the august memory Theon, Theodosius
2     assistant of the delegation, I have received from you for the custom of my scribe's canon of distribution
3     with god, [of] the sixth distribution, of gold coins four beside keratia sixteen
4     there are gold coins 4 beside 16, and toward your security the present I have made receipt
5     written [in] month Thoth, indiction sixth
