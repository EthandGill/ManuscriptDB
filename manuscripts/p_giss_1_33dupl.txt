[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Apollonios also called Eudaimon, son of Demetrios son of Eudaimon,
2     Hermopolite registered in the City quarter (of the) East, have followed up this banker's payment-order (diagraphe)
3     and have received from my nephew Aurelius Tithoetion also called
4     Sarapammon, son of Hermeinos also called Achilleus, registered in the Fort quarter (of the) West, for the release
5     of what his deceased mother Eudaimonis
6     also called Phintys owed me a[ccording to] a banker's payment-order, in the 29th year of the deified Commodus,
7     in the month Mecheir, 1 … of a [pri]ncipal of one talent of silver, the interest at
8     three obols, of bare silver, one thousand two hundred drachmas,
9     that is 1200 drachmas, after which I have received them part by part by hand, and after which
10    I have shown favor [and] I will not proceed in any wa[y]
11    for the dissolution of the same payment-order and of the terms (set) through it
12    and of the pledge which I produced in the past year, and we make no
13    claim against one another, the agreement remaining valid which both I and
14    his mother concluded by a consenting document [in] the same 29th year, Tybi, as
15    it contains. Year 5 of the Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius
16    An[t]oni[n]us Pius Felix and Marcus Aurelius
17    Al[e]xan[d]er Caesar Augusti, Tybi 9. Aurelius Apollonios [also] called Eudaim[o]n son of Demetrios, I have received and have released and make no
18    claim, as is aforesaid. Aurelius Tithoetion also called Sarapammon have paid out, as is aforesaid.
