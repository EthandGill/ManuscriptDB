1     [In the consulship] of Flavius Philoxenos the most glorious, Pharmouthi twenty-third, of the [third indi]ction.
2     … (daughter) of her mother Eudoxia, transacting without a guardian-husband, originating from the (city) of the Hermopolites, to Au[reli]us …-oulios
3     … [the most admi]rable, from the sa[me] (city) of the Hermopol[i]tes, greeting. I acknowledge to your admirableness the following appended (matters). … of Mari[a]
4     [In a vineyard one aroura and of so]wable land one and a half arouras I have sold to Georgia the most noble daughter of the most admirable …m…oros, from the
5     [same (city) of the Hermopolites] … and a worthy price for these she has paid to me, in accordance with the force of the written (deed) made for my security
6     … entreaties and exhortations I have brought to you, (asking) that I undertake, in the (deed) made by me in the name of the [aforesa]id
7     [Georgia] … in the sale concerning the same aroura in a vineyard [and] of one and a half arouras of sowable land; and your [admir]ableness having yielded
8     [to my] … [en]treaties, this on the one hand consented to do, but [requested] from me concerning this to receive a written se[c]urity. On this account therefore
9     [by my free will, with]out fraud and fear and force [and] deceit and [comp]ulsion and every circumvention, I have come to this with you, a subscribed
10    [and hortatory acknowledge]ment, by which I acknowledge, swearing by God Almighty and the piety and victory of our master Flavius
11    [Justinus the eternal Augustus the empe]ror, that I am ready at my own risk and (that) of my entire substance of every kind, both movable and immovable
12    [and self-moving] … … and heirs and successors and assignees, the non-impeachment to you and
13    [to your heirs and successors and as]signees … … and the non-harassment and the inviolability and the freedom-from-reminder … concerning this
14    … [from] now for the[ wh]ole [coming] time, and not to allow your admirableness to suffer any kind of damage,
15    [whether in a court or even out]side a court, [on behalf of me] concerning this. And I acknowledge to ward off you at whatever occasion [or t]ime or manner
16    … everyone … … coming against [or] disputing with you concerning this, and (to keep you) clear and undisturbed
17    … for the approaching time to safeguard for you, with all the things belonging to your excellence being pledged for this hortatory
18    [acknowledgment, all the prop]erties belonging to me and that shall belong to me, both movable and immovable and self-moving, as many as I have and whatever I may
19    … both spec[ifically and gener]ally, by way of pledge a[nd] by right of [mort]gage, as if from a legal judgment. The document of this
20    [hortatory acknowledgment is valid] and concerning all the aforementioned, being formally questioned by [him] that these things are so to do, to keep, to guard, I [ac]knowledged.
21    … the hortatory acknowledgment having pledged as aforesaid. I, Isakos son of Kyros, wrote on her behalf, she not knowing letters. … Petros

23    [hortatory acknowledgment, having heard from her who made] it. … I bear witness to the hortatory acknowledgment, having heard from her who made it.
