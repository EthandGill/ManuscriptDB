[TRANSLATION]
1   Petroszmethis son of Pelaios [Paiai] paid,
2   for the poll-tax of the eighth year of Tr[ajan]
3   Caesar the lord: [seventeen] silver drachmas
4   total 17 dr., with those he received through Bienchis. [Hermogenes]
5   son of Ammonas, the collector, wrote (this),
