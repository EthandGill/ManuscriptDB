1     … [from west] of-this whole six arourae being in engine called of-the Monastery
2     [together with the belonging] to-them part from the cistern of-the same engine and water-source
3     [and circulation-area and m]echanic[al] instruments and plants of-all-kinds and all other rights
4     [of-them upon the] … of-the leasing-of-these, and I-will-complete to-the your magnificence
5     [through the proper-to-it] for tax of-these according-to each year both-on Nile-watered
6     [and rainless following] the agricultural of-you demandable, which tax from-all risk
7     [risk-free necessarily] I-will-pay-back to-the your magnificence through the proper-to-it in the
8     [proper] [time] [unpost]ponably, making also the deferral of-the river-boat-taxes; the-matter the management of-the
9     [aforementioned all] matters unwounded unharmed I-will-keep and I-will-hand-over to-the your magnificence
10    [whenever it-please] as indeed I-received. Valid the lease simple written and having-been-questioned I-agreed
11    [Aurelius] … s of-Phobammon the aforementioned I-have-made this the
12    [lease and I-will-pay] the aforementioned tax each-year as-aforesaid. Anastasius
