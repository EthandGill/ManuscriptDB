[TRANSLATION]
1     To Paniskos. Concerning the memorandum given to us by Pinuris son of Estphenios and Berenebthis son of Esphénios and Psintaes son of Estphenios, likewise
2     and of the … I have placed at your disposal the copy. Since then … [to] arrange the remaining three [tax-]returns which will [be made by] us,
3     right away, vacating to the inn-keeper
4     of the three tax-returns, which is drachmas 600, and the undersigned farmers gave us a receipt … I have sent you the copies;
5     Having received then from him according to the written specification, bring [it] in the ledger according to the written specification.
6     Year 25, Hathyr 4
7     To Paniskos
8     Apollonopolite.
