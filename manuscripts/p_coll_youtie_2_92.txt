[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign and consulship of our most divine master
2     Flavius Justinus the eternal Augustus, Emperor, in the
3     fourth year, the twentieth of Pachon, the beginning of the third indiction, in Antinoopolis the most illustrious.
4     To Flavius Helladios, the most illustrious scriniarius of the
5     illustrious ducal staff in charge of the Thebaid, son of the man of best
6     memory, originating from the
7     fair city of the Panopolites, from me, Martha being present, daughter of Menas
8     by her mother Thekla, a fish-seller, herself also originating
9     from Antinoopolis, transacting business without a lord-husband, providing
10    a subscriber on her own behalf and the witnesses subscribing below
11    to the present deed of pledge upon the conditions agreed
12    hereafter set forth, with all the stipulations it contains, greeting. My father
13    having fallen into the utmost poverty, and he prospered … u … e …
14    … this long ago being before he had completed his own life of service,
15    Menas by name, surnamed Perichytos, and out of necessity having pledged
16    then my younger orphan sister called Prokla to the
17    most illustrious lord Nonnos for one gold solidus, in accordance with the
18    deed of pledge made by him at that time to the most illustrious man,
19    and after his decease, having meanwhile saved up in these (matters) from my own
20    handiwork the half of the aforesaid one solidus, I gave it to the
21    said man, wishing to redeem my distressed
22    sister—I, the aforewritten Martha—and not being able to complete the
23    making-up of this (sum), but having had the good fortune that your illustriousness was
24    moved by love toward God, I offered her entreaties that this remainder
25    be lent out for the redemption of the same orphan sister,
26    accordingly I, the same Martha, acknowledge that through your hand into my hands
27    I have received and have been lent by you, together with what was made for the security,
28    nine carats of gold by the public standard of Antinoopolis, total 9 carats of gold by the public standard of Antinoopolis, and for the establishing
29    of the faith and security and repayment, clean and unalloyed,
30    I pledge to you from now already and have pledged, by way of surety and pledge
31    by right, my said sister Prokla, being fifteen years old more
32    or less, on condition that she be in your house, serving in all
33    your slave-services and needs without leaving, freely and
34    submissively and obediently, doing all things honestly without any
35    flight and theft and roguery of any kind whatever, at my own risk and means
36    in all these matters until the discharge of the aforesaid debt of the nine carats of gold,
37    she being however fed and clothed, that she be with you
38    inviolably until the repayment of these (sums); and for your security I have made
39    this friendly bond upon all the things written herein, and I have issued it, and on being formally questioned I assented.
40    Aurelia Martha daughter of Menas son of Perichytos, the aforewritten, I have established this pledge of Prokla my
41    sister in return for the nine carats of gold, and I will repay [as] aforesaid. Aurelius Victor son of Joannes from
42    Antinoopolis wrote
43    on her behalf, she not knowing letters readily. Aurelius Kollouthos son of Kallinikos, by God's help notary, I witness
44    to the pledge, having heard
45    from her who established it.
