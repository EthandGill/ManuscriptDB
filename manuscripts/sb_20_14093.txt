1     I have received and I have been satisfied, I Philemon son of Samba from [the] village
2     of Bousiris of the Arsinoite nome from you Paul son of Menas
3     from [the] village of Tebetnu of the same nome the green [fodder] the
4     of [the] Saracens and [the] sheaf of hay of [the] fifteenth indiction
5     [in] full and so that I might carry the grass
