1     The eleventh year of Imperator
2     Caesar Vespasianus Augustus, in the month
3     Neos Sebastos 7, i[n] Euhemeria of the The[mis]tos division of the A[r]sino[ite] nom[e].
4     There acknowledges Maron the elder,
5     son of Onnophris, aged about forty, having a scar on the forehead, to his own full brother (by the same father) Onnophris, aged about
