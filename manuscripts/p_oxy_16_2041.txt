[TRANSLATION]
1     Account of stones measured out in the building of the holy Philoxenos through Phileas the stone-cutter, Tybi 14 of the 13th indiction, as follows:
2     … of the course of the 2nd … 5 … stones 50, others 35, other stones of the course of Thomas 187, total stones …, of the capitals 2057,
3     others 45, total stones 95, others stones 346, others 328, others 42, total stones 192, others of the 5th course stones 960, others 12, total stones 262.
4     In the 1st gateway, of the course stones 29, stones 79, others of the 5th course stones 395, others stones 12, stones 62, others of the 2nd course stones 124, others stones 57, stones 107,
5     others 24, total stones 74, others stones 74, others 23, total stones 73, others stones 73, others 57, total stones 507, others stones 72, others
6     24, total stones 974.
7     Capitals 88, likewise 25 capitals 32, total capitals 120. And of bases 120, and for the vaultings of the arches 18 workmen.
8     Measures of the moldings of the arches 19, at 7 cubits each, 133 cubits, and of the moldings of the doors 8 at 4 cubits, 32 cubits.
9     The work to Victor the builder, 125 marks; of the marks, 40 to a solidus, 1 1/2 solidus with the expense, 4 2/3 1/48 solidi.
