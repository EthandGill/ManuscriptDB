[TRANSLATION]
1     Agrees [Flavius Sabinus, soldier] being recorded [in] the forts of Nar[mouth] of those under Salouition
2     [prapositus] from [protectors]. I agree
3     to have sold to [Aurelius] Capito, primipilaris
4     of the village of Dionysias the one belonging to me [mare]
5     swallow-colored, prime, of the price [agreed upon] between us, of silver [one] hundred thirty talents, full of imperial coinage
6     by hand and I guarantee with full guarantee; and the mare
7     from here the purchaser received it as such. The sale is valid, and being questioned I agreed.
8     after the consulship of [Constantine] and Maximus of the
9     most illustrious prefects, Tybi 26.
10    Flavius Sabinus, soldier, I have sold the
11    mare of mine and I received the full price
12    as is stated above. Flavius Aunes, soldier
13    of the same forts I wrote on behalf of him being illiterate.
