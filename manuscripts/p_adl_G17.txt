[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy called Alexander and Berenike his sister, the Mother-loving gods,
2     in the fifteenth year, in the priesthoods of the priests and priestesses and kanephoros that are in office, in the month Pachon, on the 26th,
3     in Krokodilopolis, before Paniskos agoranomos of the upper toparchy of the Pathyrites.
4     Nechoutes son of Sekous, a Persian of the epigone, about 72 years old, of medium height, dark-skinned, with straight hair, long-faced, straight-nosed,
5     weak in the eyes, has sold from the dry farmland belonging to him in the upper plain of Krokodilopolis,
6     grain-bearing, of twenty-one arouras, the (parcel) in So-roe of five and a quarter arouras, of which from south to north (it measures) one and a quarter schoinia,
7     and from east to west six hundred and three schoinia. Neighbors: on the south the land of Hermias son of S…os,
8     on the north the land of Schotes son of Tryphon, on the east the rest of the land, on the west the road leading to the Kroneion, or whoever may be the neighbors
9     on all sides. Horos son of Nechoutes, a Persian of the epigone of those from Pathyris, about 50 years old, of medium height, honey-skinned, with straight hair, slightly bald in front, long-faced, straight-nosed, his left ear pierced, has bought it for five talents of copper. The fore-seller and guarantor of all matters concerning this sale (is)
10    Nechoutes the seller, whom Horos the buyer has accepted. I, Paniskos, have transacted (it).
