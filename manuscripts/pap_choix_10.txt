1     Year two of Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
2     Antoninus Augustus and Emperor Caesar
3     Lucius Aurelius Verus Augustus Epeiph in Tebtynis
4     of the Ptolemy-region of the Arsinoite nome. Admits Herous
5     of Heron of Apollonios as of age fifty-eight, mark
6     on the shin left, with guardian of the son Orsenouphes
7     of Sarapionis, as of age twenty-eight, mark on the hand left,
8     to Thaisata as of age thirty-eight, mark on the foot left,
9     with guardian of herself, patron Gemellus, freedman
10    of Maro Lucius, as of age forty-eight, [un]marked,
11    [to have] from her Herous dowry upon her daughter
12    [Sarap]ous of Heron marrying [to her own] Herous son
13    [Ecys], of silver drachmae one hundred, being the price of a linen garment
14    [of li]nen, single-tunic, and in parapherna without
15    assessment, earrings of gold, a pair of quarters two-and-a-half,
16    [and] [a] necklace of quarters ten and a hand-ornament of quarters two [and] [rings] two of quarters three and
17    silver collars two · of weight unmarked drachmae twenty-four, and tunic and pallium of dyed color and a pitcher
18    and a bronze vessel and a chest and a wooden chair and
19    [a] woven basket. Let them live together then with each other, the ones marrying blamelessly, both Ecysis and Sarapous, Herous [providing] to them according to [her] ability. But upon [the separation] of each other, let Herous [give] to Sarapous the
20    [dowry and the par]apherna within [days thir]ty, right [being] both from Herous and from [all the property belonging] to her. Herous of Heron with guardian of the
21    [son of Orsenouphes] I have from Thasata dowry upon Sarapous of silver drachmae one hundred in combined valuation of garments
22    [and the parapherna which] [I] shall give as is aforesaid. [Someone] wrote on behalf of them
