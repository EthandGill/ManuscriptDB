[TRANSLATION]
1     To Pete[us] Phomnaïsios
2     from [Cro]nion, son of Cronion, son of Teô[tos], from a village
3     of Teptyneis. I chance willingly
4     and [of my own accord] to have withdrawn
5     from [the] [pre]sent year
6     [agriculture] of the things existing
7     to you [regarding] [the vil]lage Kerkêsin [formerly] Asc[lepiad]ou allotment
8     of arouras ten two halves and
9     [c]tamia of arouras [t]en two halves,
10    [to have been sown] wheat and forage,
11    but you holding back and to others
12    [sub-leasing] from the present
13    year; if [it] appears [to release].
