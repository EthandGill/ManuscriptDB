1     [In the office of the] prytanis of the Oxyrhynchite [city]
2     [From] Aurelius Tyrannos Ptolemaios
3     and Apeitos [son of] Ptollion, both
4     lessees of the bath formerly
5     of Arrios Apolinarios, but now of the city. We request [that order] be sent from the
6     city's account on behalf of rents
7     which service we perform for the present month Epeiph, the appointed days in this month
8     according to our appointment drachmas
9     [five hundred], and we request that you send orders
10    to you the treasurer of the civic
11    funds the disbursement
12    [for us] to make [the aforementioned] five hundred drachmas
13    [Year] … of Emperor Caesar Marcus
14    [of Aurelius] Severus Alexander
15    [the Pious] fortunate [Augustus, in Epeiph] …
