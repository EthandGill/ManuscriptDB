[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lords brothers Victor and Maximus and
2     to another Victor and to Theonas, Victor and Kollouthos,
3     headmen of the governor's many-oared (vessel)
4     under Apion the chief-helmsman, greeting.
5     You did not act well, nor was it without danger for you,
6     in having detain[ed] our oarsman Sennouthes
7     son of Victor, a Hermopolite, on account of a public service
8     not pertaining to him. For since his father
9     had been performing this service for a long time on the same
10    many-oared (vessel) and supplying wages on his own
11    account and rendering service to us by his paternal
12    succession, there belongs to us an oarsman of the same many-oared (vessel),
13    the one detained by you. Wherefore, knowing the
14    impropriety that has occurred, hasten to release him and to keep (him)
15    for so great a magisterial service, if indeed you wish
16    to avert great enmity. But if you persist
17    in the same harassment, you will have cause to repent, so as both
18    to experience judicial indignation, and none the
19    less to incur a penalty. Be willing then to release
20    the detained one from all harassment, knowing
21    the consequence; for if you are persuaded by these
22    our letters, we think that we are able
23    [also our]selves to be of use to you in the office for whatever you may
24    [wis]h locally. I pray that you be in good health
25    for many
26    years.
27    Deliver to Victor and Maximus and another Victor and Theonas …
28    … and (from) Victor and Kollouthos, headmen.
