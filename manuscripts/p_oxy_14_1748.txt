1     Account of donkey-drivers from Pakerke as through …
2     [as] is shown.
3     From Pakerke donkeys 181 at the rate of one [at] 2 and 3/4 drachmas drachmas 456 and 3 obols,
4     from which are deducted for fodder 37 from
5     the river until the threshing floor conveyed at the rate of one
6     [at] 4/3 drachmas drachmas 21 and 1 obol, remaining drachmas 434 and 2 obols
7     of which [they] received drachmas 100, [and] other drachmas 100, [and] other drachmas 200, making drachmas 400, remaining drachmas 34 and 2 obols.
8     And through donkey-driver Plelo donkeys 57, of which
9     the wage through the accounts of Severus [was] disbursed.
10    And through [donkey-driver] Syrians donkeys 255,
11    of [which the wage] was received by Amo the farmer
12    [from] [which he] owed [as] tax.
