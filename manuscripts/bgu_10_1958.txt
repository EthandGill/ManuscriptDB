[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of Ptolemy son of Pt]olemy and Berenike, the Benefactor gods, [in the seventh year, in the priesthood] of Agathokles son of Agathokles, of Alexander [and the Sibling gods] and the Benefactor gods and the [Father-loving] gods, [the kanephoros of Ars]inoe Philadelphos being Phileso daughter of Demetrios, in the m[onth Peritios, i]n Tholthis of the Oxyrhynchite nome. Zopy[rion the Thracian], of (the troop of) Philon, a private individual, acknowledges that he has from Aristolochos son of Stratios, a Thracian of the epigone, and from Straton the Macedonian, a thirty-aroura cleruch of those not yet under a commander, one hundred and fifty artabas of wheat [for] the rent of the cleruchy of the eighth year, according to the contract
2     [of the lease] which is deposited with the contract-keeper Kallikrates
3     the Hesperite, of (the troop of) Philon, a private individual, by which Zopyrion leased (it) to Stratios son of Aristolochos and father of Straton. And let Zopyrion accept for Aristolochos and Straton this grain
4     toward the rent of the ninth year. But if he does not accept it, and instead Aristolochos and Straton are exacted this grain by
5     Zopyrion in any manner whatsoever, or some royal hindrance arises [so that] it is not accepted from Aristolochos and Straton, this grain
6     on behalf of Zopyrion, let Zopyrion pay to Aristolochos and
7     [Straton the price] of each artaba of the wheat, whether [they are exacted or it is not accepted from them, ten drachmas]; and let the right of execution
8     belong to Aristolochos and Straton against Zopyrion, exacting in accordance with the ordinance. And let this contract be valid wherever it is produced. Witnesses.
