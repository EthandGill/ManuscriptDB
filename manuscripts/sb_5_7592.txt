[TRANSLATION]
1     Valerius and the other tax-farmers of the sacred
2     gate of Syene through Sarapion.
3     has recorded Petetensetis who is also
4     Pneb… of Pasenios [the] poll-tax of [the] third
5     year of Hadrian Caesar the lord
6     drachmas two, total drachmas 2, and for [the] fourth year
7     of Hadrian Caesar the lord on account
8     drachmas six, total drachmas 6. Year 5 of Hadrian
9     the lord, Epeiph 23.
