[TRANSLATION]
1     [Horos to Ar]machis greetings. Of the letter from Dionysios of [Theogenes]
2     [carefully] written to us, [a copy] of the letter lies before you. Present now to the grain-receivers for the transport to the harbors
3     [transport] of the grain and all things in the places under you of lay nature
4     [and if necessary], leading pack-animals, and make clear to us
5     [in what way this] happens, so that we might know. But nonetheless also yourself
6     [zealously] show yourself in these matters, so that no excuse might be [left] for them.
7     Farewell, of the [eighth] year, Choiak 28.
8     [Dionysios to Horos] greetings. Of the letter to Herakleides, [a copy lies before you]. Whenever [the] grain [is landed]
