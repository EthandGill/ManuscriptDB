[TRANSLATION]
1     Democrates to Zenon greetings. The tent-pieces of the [tent] I have found eaten-away,
2     and I saw in the place of Deme[...]
3     and beside Helen willow[-wood]
4     straight and suitable. Well then you will do, having requested the
5     Helen to provide 2 four-cubit [pieces] for posts and
6     2, 3 and 1/2 cubits for
7     the diaton, straight and having thickness, and having given [them] to Cleitories so that he may send [them] to me,
8     and taking the price from him, and a young-goat skin one for a [semi-]wine-skin. And you write to us
9     whatever you might have need of.
10    Farewell.
11    Year 4, Tybi 15.
12    To Zenon
13    to Phila[delphia].
