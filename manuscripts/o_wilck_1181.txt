[TRANSLATION]
1     [Psentph]ous the elder: 1½⅓ 1/12 (artabas) of wheat.
2     [Psen]tphous the younger: ½ artaba of wheat.
3     / Permamis: ½ artaba of wheat.
4     / Sekes (son of) Pikos, ⅔: ⅓ 1/12 artaba of wheat.
5     / Horos (son of) Patapes: ⅔ artaba of wheat.
6     / Psenamounis (his) son: ⅓ 1/12 artaba of wheat.
7     (the) 6th: / Psentphous the elder: 1 1/12 artaba of wheat;
8     / Permamis: ½ artaba of wheat;
9     / Sekes (son of) Pikos, ⅔: 1 1/12 artaba of wheat;
10    / Horos (son of) Patapes: ⅔ artaba of wheat;
11    / Psenamounis (his) son: ⅓ 1/12 artaba of wheat.
