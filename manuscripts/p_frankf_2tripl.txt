1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Berenike,
2     gods Euergetai, in the eighth year, in the priesthood of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy son of Stasikrates, of Alexander and the gods Soteres and
3     the gods Adelphoi and the gods Euergetai and the gods Philopatores, Arsinoe Philadelphos being kanephoros, (namely) Arsinoe daughter of Sosibios,
4     in the month Peritios, at Tholthis of the Oxyrhynchite (nome). There leased, for
5     one year, one sowing and harvest, from the sowing
6     in the ninth year of which the fruits (fall) in the tenth year, Eupolis
7     the Athenian, of those of Philon, private citizen, to Alexandros son of Krates,
8     a Cyrenean of the epigone, and to Horos son of P…os,
9     an Oxyrhynchite, temple-slave of Thoeris, the half of the
10    fallow land in [his own] allotment, which are 22
11    arourae, with seed, free of risk, at a rent of 126 artabas of wheat; and let Eupolis give them seed
12    for each aroura one artaba of wheat at the time of need. And if
13    Eupolis finds [any] land more than the twenty[-two arourae]
14    [by survey], and (Alexandros and Horos take) a part of this, [let there be assigned to Eupolis]
15    … rent for each aroura [eight artabas of wheat]
16    [with seed … and let] the rents written (in the contract)
17    [be paid by Alexandros and Horos] to Eupolis in the month Dystros of the
18    [tenth] year, grain [pure] and unadulterated, that which is produced on
19    [the] land, by the chous measure, by the just measurement of Eupolis, and
20    [let them] convey (it) to the (premises) of Eupolis at their own expense; and if
21    [they do not] pay according to the things written, let them pay
22    to Eupolis the price of each artaba of the wheat, ten drachmae. And let the stubble be Eupolis's; and the chaff,
23    Eupolis having threshed (it), let him have (it); and let the right of execution belong to Eupolis against Alexandros [and Horos], exacting according to the ordinance. And let
24    [Eupolis] be master [of the crops] until he recovers what is his. And [let Eupolis] guarantee [to Alexandros] and Horos the [land and] the crops [sown in it according to] what he leased; and if he does not guarantee [according to the things written], let [Eupolis] pay to Alexandros [and Horos a penalty of] 1,000 silver [drachmae, unless] some
25    [royal hindrance occurs. And let this contract be] valid [wherever]
26    [it is produced; witnesses.]
