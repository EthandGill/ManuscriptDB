[TRANSLATION]
1     Poseidonios to Sarapion, greeting. Of the letter written by [Galestes]
2     we have appended for you the copy, so that you may follow up on the proceedings held
3     before the same man concerning the matters in which he has declared that Haronnophris the
4     [sitologos] is in dispute with Dorion, ship-master [of Ptolemaios' kerkouros] of capacity 7, over the fact that with a larger
5     [measure] the loading [of the wheat had taken] place. Farewell. Year …, Hathyr 2.
6     [Ga]l[e]stes to Poseidonios, greeting. Of the letter to [Ha]ronnophris the sitologos
7     we have appended a copy, so that you may follow up. … Year … 10 … .
8     To [Haronn]ophris: since you were in dispute with Do[rion, ship-master of] Ptolemaios' kerkouros of capacity 7, over the fact that with a larger meas[ure] the loading of the [whe]at had taken place,
9     having summoned Dorion, in the presence also of Psammati[chos] your representative,
10    we made the in[spection] of these things on the 1… of the current month,
11    and on the one hand, comparing the measure [with] the bronze (standard) in Memphis, we found
12    it to be larger by 1 artaba in the 100 artabas of wheat; and Dori[on] …, brought forward that he had taken up
13    5,281½ artabas, so that there were being added … 100 …
14    … the 5,334 4 artabas which you had written, in regard to which also of Psammatichos …
15    … that Dor[i]on had not taken up more than the 5,281½ artabas and the … n …
16    … from the inspection made through us, 52½¼ artabas. But of the …
17    he having given a hand-written bond by the royal oath, we thought it necessary to make clear,
18    so that you may be brought into agreement with what has been administered. …
19    To Sarapio[n]
20    … [f]rom Po[s]eidoni[os]
