[TRANSLATION]
1     … Translation of a will.
2     … Gaius Julius Diogenes, a veteran,
3     [made a will. Let my female slaves be free,] Julia (and) Apollonarion, aged over
4     [thirty and aged over thirty], and let them be my heirs
5     [in equal share. And let them come forward to my inheritance whenever] they are able to attest themselves
6     [to be my heirs, each for an equal share, without] evil fraud, in the Arsinoite nome … …tolemas according to the will;
7     [and to so-and-so] … [I give and leave] six arouras from the eastern quarter,
8     [lying …] … of the east, a quarter-aroura of reed-land, and
9     … other six arouras of grain-land, and what I have given,
10    [everything …] my slave-woman Euterpe, Julia Apollonarion … and after my death I give … my slave-woman by the name of Syra … to the aforewritten, for good fortune, to Julia
11    [Isarous, my natural daughter,] from my freedwoman Julia Primilla
12    [I leave] … ten arouras of grain-land and, around the village of Philadelphia [of the same nome, in addition, two arouras] of palm-grove, lying to the east … they shall hand over to them … my heirs …
13    [without delay] … and a third part of my tower on the … [side, lying, and a part of my] paternal house. And if I shall be found to owe anything [by way of pledge and by right of mortgage], from the property of Julia Isarous and Julius
14    [Diogenes] … I wish it to be repaid, and it is … to others
15    … of mine, and let the orphans repay. And if anything human
16    [should befall the aforewritten] … having begotten [no] children, I wish his part to pass to
17    [so-and-so; but if he leaves children,] to them. I wish Julia Isarous and Julius Diogenes
18    [to feed and clothe for life their] mother Julia Primilla, my
19    [freedwoman. And I wish myself to be carried out and] laid out at the wish of my daughters,
20    [and I wish my heirs] to spend on the care of my body not less
21    [than … drachmas. From this] will let evil fraud be absent. The household (of slaves)
22    [and the property, the will having been made,] Gaius Numissius Crispus, a veteran, purchased
23    [for one sestertius coin.]
