r.1    ἔχω ἐγὼ Σουλιην υἱὸς Ουανακτικουτα [παρὰ] σοῦ Φαντ

[TRANSLATION]
1     I Suliēs son of Ouanaktikout have [from] you Phant[illegible]
2     son of Kirbeetak, priest, 13 golden coins
3     and 32 hundredths, totaling 13 golden coins and 32 hundredths only
4     and these I am ready with god to provide to you if my business affairs prosper; but if I do not do this
5     you order my young slaves called Tōdetes of the
6     [female slave] her child until completion of the stated
7     debt and it is agreed to me as aforesaid
8     [written] through me Agathos, Mecheir 23, indiction 13
