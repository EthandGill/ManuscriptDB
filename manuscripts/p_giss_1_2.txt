[TRANSLATION]
1     In the reign of Ptolemy son of Ptolemy and Cleopatra, gods Epiphaneis,
2     in the eighth [year], the priest being Herakleodoros son of Apollophanes, of Alexander and the gods
3     Soteres and the gods Adelphoi and the gods Euergetai and the gods Philopatores and
4     the gods Epiphaneis and the gods Philometores, the athlophoros of Berenike Euergetis being Sarapias daughter of Apollonios, the kanephoros of Arsinoe Philadelphos being Aristokleia daughter of
5     Demetrios, the priestess of Arsinoe Philopator being Eirene daughter of Ptolemy, in the month
6     Peritios on the seventeenth, Mesore the seventeenth, in Krokodilon polis
7     of the Arsinoite nome. Good fortune. Olympias daughter of Dionysios,
8     a Macedonian woman, gave herself, with her guardian, her own father Dionysios the Macedonian of the second hipparchy, a hundred-aroura holder, to Antaios the Athenian, of the troop of Kineas of the second hipparchy, a hundred-aroura holder, [to be] his wedded wife, bringing as a dowry
9     a contribution to the bronze account of ninety-five talents
10    and a slave-girl, [her] maidservant, whose name is Stolis, and her infant child still at the breast, whose name is A …, at five talents of bronze, so that the whole is [one hundred] talents of bronze. And let [Ol]ympias be with Antaios, obedient to him as it is fitting for a wife to be to her husband, having joint authority with him
11    in common of the property; and let Antaios provide for Olympias the necessaries and the furniture and the clothing
12    and the other things as [befits a wedded wife],
13    whether at home or ab[road, according to the means of] the property; and let it not be permitted
14    to him [to bring in any other] woman against [Oly]mpias, nor to have a concubine or a boy-favourite, [nor to beget children fr]om another woman while Olympias lives, nor [to dwell in another house] of which Olympias [shall not have] mastery, nor to cast her out nor to insul[t nor to maltreat her], nor to alienate
15    any of the property to the wrong of Olympia[s]. And if he be shown to do any of these things, or [the furniture or the clothing or] the other things he does not provide for her as
16    [is written, let Antaios pay to Olympias forthwith] the dowry
17    [increased by half. By the same terms let it not be permitted to Olympias to be away at night]
18    [or absent by day from the house of Antaios without] the consent [of Antaios.]
19    Apollonios the Macedonian, of those
20    of Kineas of the second hipparchy, hundred-aroura holder. Witnesses:
21    Philios the Macedonian, Demokratides
22    the Thessalian, the two of those of Kineas
23    of the second hipparchy,
24    Diogenes the Cyrenaean, of those
25    of Diodoros of the first hipparchy, the three
26    hundred-aroura holders; Menophil[o]s
27    the Macedonian, of those formerly of
28    Aristonikos, on fixed pay,
29    Alexandros son of Horion, a Cretan,
30    Sarapion son of Zopyros,
31    a Persian — the two of the epigone.
