1     In the year fifth of the Emperor Caesar Tr[ajan Hadrian]
2     Augustus, in the month Daisios 3, Pharmouthi 3, in K[aranis of the Herakleides]
3     division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledg[e Ptolemaios son of Harpo]kration, about sixty-three years old, scar … [and the]
4     full sister of this man, on the father's and mother's side, [Didymarion, about]
5     seventy-two years old, scar on the seco[n]d finger [of the left hand],
6     with her guardian Ptolemaios, to Paouetis, [fatherless, his mother being Tane]phremmis, about sixty years old, scar by the th[umb of the right hand],
7     that they have received from him forthwith from hand to hand from the hou[se of silver drachmas]
8     one hundred twenty, which are the (sum) falling to them, Pt[olemaios and Didymarion],
9     the half part, of two hundred for[ty] drachmas of silver, [which there owed]
10    Paouetis to their deceased [father Harpokration son of Sambas]
11    according to an acknowledgment of loan, which [he made with him through the same record-office, and (they acknowledge) that they will not]

14    neither concerning that which they have received as is s[et forth, of silver drachmas one hundred twenty,]
15    nor concerning any debt written [nor unwritten from former times]
16    in any way. Under-signer [of the acknowledging parties (is) Ptolemaios the aforesaid].
17    Ptolemaios son of Harpoc[ration and his sister]
18    Didymarin, with (her) guard[ian Ptolemaios, acknowledge that we have re]ceived from Paouetis the [silver drachmas]
19    one hundred twenty, which are the (sum) fal[ling to us, the hal]f part of two hundred for[ty] drachmas,
20    which he owed to our deceased [father Harpo]kration son of Sambas according to an acknowled[gment of loan, which]
21    we also declare to have lapsed, [and that we will not pro]ceed concerning anything writte[n] [nor unwri]tten as is set forth. I, Ptole[maios, wrote]

24    He wrote … not knowing letters.
