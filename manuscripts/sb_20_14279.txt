[TRANSLATION]
1     Psimuras to Chairemon [his] bro[ther gre]etings.
2     Receive through Nikephoros the don[key-driver seal]ed-keeper a basket of grapes which [for] me for th[e] need to carry.
3     Tell me whether I received what I sent to you through my donkey-driver and what I sent to you through Anubas the camel-driver. Greet
4     Tete ... the very best and her mother and all your [people]. Concerning what
5     you need tell me. The children greet you.
6     To be strong [you] I pray, Mecheir 29.
7     To Chairemon from Psimuras.
