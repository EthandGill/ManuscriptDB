1     From John, with God, expelleutus of Hermopolis, to Cosmas, the most esteemed, from the same city.
2     I received and [was fulfilled] from you on behalf of public grain at the place of deposit
3     of the tenth indiction, grain six and a third by the dochimic measure, come to grain artabae 6⅓
4     only. Of Flavius John, expelleutus of Hermopolis, the [receipt] agrees as stated above. Through me, Phoibammon
5     collector of the grain at the place, the [receipt] agrees of the tenth indiction as stated above
6     [From] John, with God, expelleutus of Hermopolis, to Athanasius, the most esteemed, from the same city.
7     I received and [was fulfilled]
8     from you on behalf of public grain at the place of deposit
9     of the tenth indiction, grain three and a third by the dochimic measure, come to grain artabae 3⅓ only.
10    Of Flavius John, expelleutus of Hermopolis, the [receipt] agrees as stated above. Through me, Phoibammon
11    collector of the grain at the place, the [receipt] agrees of the tenth indiction as stated above
12    [Receipt] of grain at the place of the tenth indiction of grain [artabae] 6⅓ and of grain artabae 3⅓
