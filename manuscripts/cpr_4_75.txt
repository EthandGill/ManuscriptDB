[TRANSLATION]
1     Victor the least of [the] deacon, the brother of the blessed ... the man deceased
2     to me [belongs] the son Apa Isaac, the brother of the blessed, the rock, the man deceased, also
3     [that] you owe me purely and completely a whole measure of gold
4     of four shekels; becomes shekels twenty-three only. This therefore I am ready, I give [it] to you
5     in the grace of God, within [the month ...], the twelfth [of the] indiction, this [document]
6     third indiction, without mistake, without law, without doubt, without ambiguity, as he
7     [knows], whoever to the house is subject to you, when I fulfill you [regarding] the security
8     [of the] security was written [on the] seventh of Phaophi, indiction 3. Victor the least of deacon, the brother of the blessed
9     Apollo ... I have signed the security ... those who gave it
