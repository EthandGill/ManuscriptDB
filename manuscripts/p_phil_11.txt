[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of an acknowledgement. In the 3rd year of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius
2     Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, in the month Gorpiaios = Epeiph 30,
3     in Berenikis Aigialou of the Themistos division of the Arsinoite nome.
4     Gaius Anthistius Numisianus, discharged from military service, aged …, acknowledges
5     to Anthistia Kronous, aged about 35, without distinguishing mark, with as guardian Gaius Julius Magnus, aged 35, with a scar
6     on the forehead, that he, the acknowledging party, will bring no charge against Kronous nor against her agents
7     concerning the things which Kronous bought from the acknowledging party's brother
8     Gaius Anthestius Valens, while he was living, according to written deeds — one
9     having been made in the 4th year of the deified Hadrianus, Phaophi, of a catoecic allotment
10    of five arourae around the village of Hephaistias of the Herakleides division,
11    and another written deed having been made in the 21st year of the deified Hadrianus, in the month Epeiph,
12    of a half share of a vacant plot in the village of Philadelpheia — because Gaius Anthistius
13    Valens received back their prices, as the
14    written deeds contain, and (that) the acknowledging party will not proceed,
15    nor his agents, either concerning the aforewritten things nor concerning
16    anything else at all, in any manner whatsoever;
17    (and) that Anthistia Kronous shall therefore own and control the aforesaid things
18    and register them through the record-office of acquisitions from a copy
19    of this acknowledgement, and manage them as she wishes … ,
20    and that she herself, Anthistia Kronous, shall not proceed against the acknowledging party
21    concerning anything at all of the aforewritten things in any manner whatsoever.
22    Copy of a supplementary document of the allotment-registers. Aonios
23    and his fellow overseers of the allotment-registers
24    [of the] Arsinoite, in the 1st year of Antoninus Caesar
25    [the] lord, to Anthistia Kronous
26    [gree]ting. You paid in full for the things which were ceded to you
27    [ac]cording to a written deed made public
28    through the registry-office by
29    your former husband
30    Gaius Anthistius Valens,
31    around Hephaistias, of grain-land, 5 arourae,
32    one hundred and six drachmae of silver, total 106 drachmae.
33    In the 5th year of the Emperor Caesar
34    Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus
35    Augustus Pius, Tubi 5.
36    I, Agathos, secretary, have signed.
