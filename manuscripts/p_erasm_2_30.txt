[TRANSLATION]
1     Sarapion to Sarapion, greetings.
2     Load [it] so that into Alexandria
3     into the royal [treasury] from the
4     crops of the 30th year
5     into [the] barge of-the-queen, of which the tenant-farmer [is]
6     Nephorites, upon which [is] Polystratos
7     artabas, one thousand two hundred, [making] 1200.
8     Fare well. Of the 30th year, Payni 24.
9     to Sarapion.
