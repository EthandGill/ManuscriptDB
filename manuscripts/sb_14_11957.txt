[TRANSLATION]
1     The most illustrious Phonē, king of the Blemmyes,
2     to Aburni, king of the Nubadae and Naqade and Mousa, [his] sons.
3     [I greet you] much, [and] first [of all] the kingdom with all [who are] of the land, and the safety of your people,
4     [which is] very first, in God I pray. Now I write to you through your excellence, since you wrote to me that: great [is the] man who is
5     [great in his lineage]. Just as also you, [so] also [do] I; I have a son, and [so] also I. I have a son Breeiteck and the brothers Eieneae and other
6     [many domains]. But do not think [that] I am not most noble. For indeed all these things that we became, no one sees except God. And the [son]
7     Breeiteck and the bro[ther]s Eieneae wished to pursue the death of Eieneae. And after these [things] your ambassador
8     met me here and I called [for] Breeiteck and the brothers Eieneae and restrained them. No one can ever make war
9     [without] command of me. But perhaps your men do not obey you, but do you hear the words of the men? Just as
10    you wrote to me that: I wish, having concord between one another, [let us] share my cattle with your cattle, pasturing with one another
11    and the sheep; [this] I very much liked. If you wish, [both] I and you [shall] remain well in our house. For indeed first Silko conquered
12    and took Talmis; today you conquered and took Talmin. First Silko took and prevented our lands; today [indeed]
13    you conquered and took Talmin. First Silko said that: Give me sheep and cattle and camels, sufficient, so that [the] lands [be res]tored
14    of you, and I gave all of them and he mocked and prevented us. And I wrote to Eieneae because of peace and sent the ambassadors
15    with oaths and he mocked and killed a phylarch and sub-tyrant and took the prophets in the place of Phontauon.
16    But it is permitted for war to happen, but not [being] permitted he mocked the men and killed the men with oaths. For indeed
17    because of the things done by Silko which Eieneae mocked, for this reason I was grieved and came down and waged war. For indeed the words of Silko
18    and Eieneae have passed. Perhaps the fortune of Eieneae and Silko, [can] we [achieve it] with one another? For now [both] I and you, as brother and as greater, [we] shall make
19    a good time with one another. Withdraw from our land and send the gods to the temple, so that I and you might make [a] good
20    time. And you made known to me concerning coins and sheep and camels. These we found [and] sent to you. Withdraw from the
21    land of mine and give us our own and the gods. And I sent you the [goods] of my hand. If you wish, give me our lands
22    and the gods, [and] I have with you good peace. But know that if you prevent us [from having] our lands and the gods, we are not able to remain
23    [except] to die all. For indeed [it is] not fought over because of your lands; because of our lands [it is] fought over. I write to you again that Pacheniōs came
24    and devastated Danant. And withdraw from our land. For indeed Koei came into [the area] beyond Tabales and made the altars
25    and conquered Kabantia. And we shall not allow our lands, all the kings, to be conquered. And I did not wish to hold your lands,
26    because it is not good. When your ambassador met me, I appointed Scarou's son Aeinēm to [make haste] so that [my lands] be given to him
27    [my lands]. When the ambassador came, I trusted you and sent the brother Eieneae. Behold, I swore that: Restore to me our lands,
28    [and] [we] shall make peace unto the ages. And indeed I have given my oaths to the brother Eieneae, so that you [might] provide to him my lands. And I,
29    Breeiteck, phylarch, greet much my lord, brother Aburni, king of the Nubadae, [with] the gods of Talmis,
30    that, if you [res]tore [our] lands, we shall not wage war with one another anymore unto
31    the ages, unless [I] guard the peace well.
32    And I, Phonē, king, sent to you a camel, one. [It] is [a] greeting-gift. I write to you that
33    I and Breei[tec]k … the… … [I] withdrew [from/after] our lands … came … [of this] … .
34    And concerning Iasateck, do not let him sleep, but do not listen to the words of those fleeing.
