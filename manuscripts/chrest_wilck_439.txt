[TRANSLATION]
1     Lucius Aemilius Rectus says:
2     Let no one be permitted to impress those on the land
3     nor to demand provisions or something else as a gift without
4     [my] diploma; but [to receive] [each] of the
5     [having] my diploma the sufficient provisions
6     price being paid for them. But if anyone
7     is reported either of the soldiers or of the sword-bearers
8     or whoever of the subordinates [on the public]
9     purposes, having acted [contrary to the] my [decree] or having used force against someone of those from the land or having unlawfully exacted money,
10    I will use the utmost punishment against this person
11    in the second year of Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus Imperator
12    Germanicus the fourth
