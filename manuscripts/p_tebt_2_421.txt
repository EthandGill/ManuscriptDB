[TRANSLATION]
1   Apion to Didymos, greeting. Putting off
2   everything, at once, the moment
3   you receive this letter of mine,
4   come to me, since your sister
5   is ailing. And her white
6   tunic, the one you have — bring
7   it when you come; but the turquoise one
8   do n[ot] bring — rather, if you want to sell
9   it, sell it; if you want to leave it
10   to yo[ur] daughter, leave it. But do not neg-
11   lect her at all, and do not trouble your
12   w[if]e or the children; and when you come,
13   come to Theogenis.
14   I pray for your health.
