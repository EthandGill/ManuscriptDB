[TRANSLATION]
1     [Year … of Imperator Caesar]
2     Nerva Tra[jan]us Au[gustus Germanicus,]
3     in the month Dystros, the 4th, Tybi the 4th, [in] Theadelphe[ia] of the
4     Themistos division of the Arsinoite nome. There acknowledge,
5     Ares son of Hermias, from the metropolis, as he states,
6     registered in the quarter of the Bithynians and
7     other places, a Persian of the epigone, [aged about] thirty-
8     two, with a scar on his right forearm, and his wife Thermouthas
9     daughter of Leontas, a Persian woman, aged about thirty-two, with a scar by her left
10    knee, with as guardian her aforewritten
11    husband, being sureties for one another for repayment to Lucius
12    Bellenus Gemellus through Epagathos, aged about 35,
13    with a scar on his right shin — that they have from him forthwith, by hand, out of the house, of silver
14    twenty drachmas, and that, in lieu of the interest,
15    Ares shall remain (in service) for one year from the aforewritten
16    day, pasturing the pigs which Lucius has,
17    driving them to the proper roads and pastures and doing all that is fitting, as a herdsman,
18    the said Areios receiving each month
19    for the whole reckoning, by way of wages, of silver
20    twenty drachmas, it not being permitted to him to depart within the period. And if he should depart, let him pay
21    for each day on which he should leave the pigs
22    two drachmas, and the silver with the half as much again.
23    And after the period let him repay the aforesaid twenty drachmas of silver. And if before
24    the period he should depart, he shall repay the silver double,
25    the right of execution belonging to Lucius both against the parties acknowledging and
26    against all their property, as if by legal judgment. Subscriber
27    for the parties acknowledging, Heron son of Herakleides, aged about 53, from Theadelphia.
28    I, Ares son of Hermias, a Persian of the epigone, and my wife
29    Thermouthas daughter of Leontas, a Persian woman, with me as guardian, being sureties for one another for repayment, acknowledge that we have from Lucius Bellenus
30    Gemellus, through Epagathos, of silver, twenty drachmas
31    upon the (condition of) the remaining (in service) of the one of us,
32    Areios, for one year, pasturing the pigs which
33    Lucius has, he receiving
34    each month by way of wages for the whole reckoning twenty drachmas of silver, and after the period we will repay the silver. And if before the
35    period I should depart, we will repay the silver double, as is set forth above. There wrote on their behalf
36    Heron son of Herakleides, since they do not know letters.
37    Registered through the record-office in Theadelphia.
38    Service (paramone) of Areios … to the wife, of 20 drachmas.
