[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Longinus the most illustrious for the 2nd time,
2     Phamenoth 2, of the 15th indiction, in Oxyrhynchos.
3     To Fl[avius] Iohannes the reverend, town-councillor,
4     son of Martyrius of pious memory,
5     landowner here in the Oxyrhynchite, (from) Aurelius
6     Panechotes son of Mouses, his mother being Anna,
7     from the hamlet of Hipponamos, an estate of
8     your nobility, of the same nome, a registered tenant,
9     greeting. I acknowledge that I have received from you forthwith
10    the price mutually agreed
11    and pleasing to me, in full, of wine, of measures, by the estate measure, sixty-
12    three, total of wine measures 63, which wine, from the vat,
13    from unadulterated must, you supplying the
14    empty vessels, I shall of necessity render to your nobility
15    in the month Mesore of the present year 168 = 137
16    from the flow of wine of the first, God willing,
17    indiction, without delay, the right of exaction being yours
18    against me and against all my
19    property. Valid is the deed, written singly,
20    and on being questioned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius
21    Panechosis son of Mouses, the aforesaid,
22    have received the price in full, of wine, of measures
23    sixty-three, and I shall render (it) at the appointed time, and all is agreeable to me as stated above. I, Aurelius
24    Papnouthios son of Phoibammon, having been requested, wrote
25    on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
26    Through me … …
27    Deed of Panechotes son of Mouses from
28    the hamlet of Hipponamos, of wine, 63 measures.
