[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Petermouthes, chief farmer
2     of Aurelius Paniscus the elder, to Aurelius
3     Psenpanasouthes, poultry-raiser, greetings
4     Give to Horion, agric[ola] … on account of custom until
5     settlement of account: chickens four, hens two, total hens 2, year 7, Epeiph 11
6     likewise also to the grain-collectors and sealers
7     one hen, eggs ten, total hen 1, eggs 10
8     Aurelius Mikka wrote on his behalf
