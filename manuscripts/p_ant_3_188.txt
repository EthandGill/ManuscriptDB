[TRANSLATION]
1     [your] genuine [glo]riousness … … …
2     [Th]eodosios the most glorious, from Eudaimon's (estate), among which he also found a letter t[o] yo[u]
3     the most glorious, of Droserios the most renowned, of consular rank and curator of the divine
4     house of Placidia — which letter, [whi]ch I placed within the letters of your
5     gloriousness, he gave to me as one that ought to be sent through me to your genuineness.
6     And concerning the agreement, according to what was written to you by me through the said Abraamios,
7     if it is acceptable to you, take care that your intention be written briefly
8     here, before, as is likely, the gold is sent by that good man.
9     I do not know whence you came to know him. For if the proposal pleases you and you wish
10    you yourselves to receive such an agreement, and if he sends the gold, I hope that your order
11    is carried out. I, however, master, in the meantime am held back here, partly because of the
12    works … I plunged into disputes … … … … and …
13    driven to complete desolation, partly also because of my waiting here until the hour
14    for my own people from Eudaimon's (estate). For when I was held back here, it was prudent
15    to write to them to come, so that we might not be apart from one another. But I beseech you, the most glorious,
16    as usual to show your genuineness — or to speak more truly, also for your own soul's sake —
17    and to take in hand the affairs of the property of the pious hospice belonging to me,
18    that is to say of Gregorios the most God-loving, and especially today, at such a time, and a change
19    of affairs having occurred, and by the favour of God you being able toward each of the
20    rulers of the affairs of your province. The Lord of glory will grant me
21    your genuine gloriousness prospering for ever.
22    [ ] to our master …
23    a separate letter of the augustalis, and another letter of a different person, of Drakon, by God's grace chartularius,
24    brought through the magistrianus on account of the third part of the public (dues) of the reply
25    Epeiph 6, indiction 15
