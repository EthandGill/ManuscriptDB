[TRANSLATION]
1     In year 17 of Lucius Septimius Severus
2     the Pious Pertinax and of Marcus Aurelius
3     Antoninus the Pious August[us] and of Publius
4     Septimius Geta Caesar August, Mechir 9
5     [registration] of Tybi has recorded Abous Sataboutos … [year 16]
6     of Soknopaeus Island drachmas four hundred, total drachmas 400; and on the 11[th]
7     has recorded the same [person, poll-tax of] year 16 of Soknopaeus Island drachmas two hundred
8     [and] fifty, total drachmas 250; and on the 12th
9     [registration] of Mechir has recorded the same [person, poll-tax of] year 16 of Soknopaeus Island drachmas fifty, total drachmas 50.
