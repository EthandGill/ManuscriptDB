[TRANSLATION]
1     It was given through Belisarius the banker to the most esteemed Pettarius the notary entering into Oxyrhyncha through
2     the 8 cap[it]… nnidou of Caesarion of the present twelfth indiction for the apportionment of gold [currency]
3     [keratia nine] [as] is set forth. It amounts to gold keratia nine toward Pauni of the present year.
4     It amounts to keratia for which is aforesaid.
5     of gold [keratia nine toward] Pauni.
