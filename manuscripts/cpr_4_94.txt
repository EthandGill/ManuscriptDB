1     Behold the writing of Paul, his greeting to you, joy
2     For since your request of others that you took, ready, these I give, ready what is for you until today, month of Athyr
3     When you give to you a measure of whole grain
4     From which I wrote this security for you and you
5     Not he of Athyr … [written by] Thon.
