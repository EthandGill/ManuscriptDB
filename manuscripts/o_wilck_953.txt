1     Measure of the granary of the metropolis, of the produce of year 25 of Commodus
2     Antoninus Caesar the lord,
3     Pauni 5, for the South, in the name of Pamphilos
4     (son of) Pamonthes, through the cultivator Herieus (son of) Esminis
5     and partners, of wheat one and a half, total wheat 1½.
6     I, Amonios, have signed.
