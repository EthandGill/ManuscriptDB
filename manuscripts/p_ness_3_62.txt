r.1    ἐν ὀνόματι τοῦ θεοῦ· Αλααρεθ βεν Αβδ τοῖς ἀπὸ
r.2    Νεστάνων κλίματος Ἐλούσης χώρας Γάζης. παράσχετε ταχέως

[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God: Alaarath son of Abd to those from
2     the region of Nestanon of the Elousan land of Gaza. Provide quickly
3     Amor son of Kais of the Bani Saad son of Malech for two months [Saual]
4     and Dulkada wheat modii three hundred ten, oil sextarii
5     three hundred ten. It was written in the month of October indiction 4, according to the Arabs year 55,
6     by the hand of Theodore John in total: wheat modii [uncertain amount], oil sextarii [uncertain amount].
7     In total: wheat modii [uncertain amount], oil sextarii [uncertain amount].
