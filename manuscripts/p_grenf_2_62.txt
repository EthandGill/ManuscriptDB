[TRANSLATION]
1     To Krenoleiios Quintillianos centurion
2     Demetrios son of Satyros, in place of Syros mother
3     Diodora from [the] district of [the] Bithynians others
4     places. I swear by [the fortune of] Lucius Septimius
5     Severus Pertinax and Marcus Aurelius
6     Antoninus and Publius Septimius
7     Britannicus Maximus Pious
8     Sebastoi fortune willingly and of my own accord to guarantee Pasin son of Apollonios, mother Isitos, [for] security and presentation, whom
9     and I shall present whenever [he] is required.
10    And if I do not present [him], I myself shall submit [myself] to [what] is required against him, or [I] be liable to the [oath]. It was written with [Heracli]des the sword-bearer following.
11    Demetrios as years 28 scar on forehead.
12    Year 19 of Lucius Septimius Severus Pertinax and
13    Marcus Aurelius Antoninus and Publius
14    Septimius Geta Britannicus Maximus Pious
15    Sebastoi, Pharmouthi
