[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymos, collector of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Amenothes (son of) Harbechis. I have received for the assessment
3     anak( ) of year 10, base, 3 obols, total base, 3 obols; and in the name of Harbechis
4     (his) son, likewise for the assessment anak( ), base, 3 obols, total base, 3 obols; and in the name
5     of Cholmeus, another son, likewise for the assessment anak( ) of year 10,
6     base, 3 obols, total base, 3 obols. Year 10 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
7     Pauni 17. I, Pha( ), have signed.
