[TRANSLATION]
1     In the twenty-first year of the Emperor
2     Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus
3     Mesore 27 in [T]ebtuni of the division of Polemon of the Arsinoite nome. Heron acknowledges
4     son of Suchos, of about thirty-two years, with a scar
5     on the left hand; Pakebkis son of Marepshemis
6     of about twenty-eight years, with a scar by the shin [d]exion, to have received from him
7     of Pakebkis [di]a hand of silver drachmas one hundred thirty-two [and] the
8     interest which Pakebkis owed to
9     Heron according to an agreement of use
10    [having been completed] [d]ia the office of Theogonidos village in the present still month of Phamenoth on the fourteenth, which also
11    [he] [has] given [it] back to him for [annulment] and [cancellation] and [Heron] not to proceed
12    [the] Heron [against the] Pakebkis [concerning]
13    [what he has received according to what is set forth above] …
