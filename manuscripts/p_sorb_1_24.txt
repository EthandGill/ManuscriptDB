[TRANSLATION]
1     Diogenes to Thrasymedes, greetings. Order [someone] to measure out to Horus of Skosothis
2     for which he farms near Oxyrhyncha a loan
3     for repayment which he shall repay from the new [harvest] at the same time
4     with the exports in the 36th year barley-wheat
5     [of] 50 artabas
6     Farewell. Year 35, Phaophi 22.
