1     The holy church of God of Hermupolis through me Colluthus
2     the advocate of the division of the Hermopolite to Eusebius the farmer

5     [amounting to karats 23] of Alexandria. Without deduction. Written Mesore
6     3, indiction 12. Colluthus the advocate agrees to me.
