1     Year 7, Epeiph 5. (A) report to Nikaner
2     to the guard of Athribis
3     and to those with him
4     (to the) guards. (Me) being
5     I in Alexandria, having come [some] into
6     my allotment they cut [down] tamarisk wood
7     30, of these 20
8     at drachmas 3 (they) become drachmas 60
9     and the ten at drachmas 2
10    (they) become drachmas 20, (they) become drachmas 80.
