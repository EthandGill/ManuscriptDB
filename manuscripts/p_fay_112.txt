[TRANSLATION]
1   Lucius Bellenus Gemellus to Epagathos
2   his own, greeting. You will do well to push on the dig-
3   ging of the olive-groves and the loosening-round
4   and double-hoeing of the olive-groves, and [the] fal-
5   lows hoe round and double-hoe, press-
6   ing the ox-driver
7   so that each
8   day he renders his work; and do not glue the tally
9   of the ox-teams to the …(?). The strip at
10   Apias to this day you have not reaped but have ne-
11   glected, and so far you have reaped the half
12   of it, waiting on the meas-
13   urer Zoilos — and do not let him put
14   you to shame: you have left it unreaped to this day.
15   Therefore I blame you greatly. Find
16   out whether the olive-grove of Dionysias has been dug;
17   if not, push on its digging
18   in two days; for it pays that it
19   be dug …(?). Let them not hurry
20   to thresh …(?) and the Sentheus plot
21   until I write. Do not crush all the threshing-floors
22   for the present. So do not do otherwise.
23   Farewell. Greet Heron and Orsenouphis
24   and all those at home. Year 2 of Imperator
25   Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus,
26   Pachon 26.
