[TRANSLATION]
1     … he brought forward … terrible … to the [sol]diers for the food-rations and … epi … e … [not]
2     he said he would embark with them unless within two years they all completed (it) or gathered together the price quickly. When such things had happened, he set out as to the (matter) aforesaid.
3     But after these things, being summoned by him on the following (day) so that he might know what they were accomplishing and set the multitude against them, they were no longer seen. Wherefore also
4     immediately, going around the plains and driving together the cattle and the sheep, he brought (them) up to the village, which he also handed over to the
5     guards, of which the particulars are: sheep with lambs 580, oxen with bulls and
6     heifers 67, and these he shut up in the temple,
7     but he himself ran off into the neighboring villages, leaving behind
8     Dionysios as his deputy. And after these things he returned,
9     and the banker having arrived on the 8th, the copper in question was paid out to Poseidonios, the (agent) of Di[o]nysios the banker,
10    the amount specified for each name.
11    But up to the (time of) writing (this) he is still in the village. And those who purified themselves have not even touched the earth; , . But he by every means,
12    (were purified?) …
13    persisting, tries to persuade them. Wherefore I have written to you so that you may know.
