[TRANSLATION]
1     To Gemeinius Valerianus, the most excellent procurator
2     through Aurelius Herakleides and
3     Chæremon, both councillors of the most illustrious city of the
4     Alexandrians, the most worthy
5     from Aurelius Ptolla, administrator
6     of Phobôos. I request [that you] write
7     and now for cutting …
8     and [c]ollection of fodder of the self-sown
9     lands from an allotment …
10    other drachmae seven hundred, [total] 700 drachmae,
11    of which I will give account.
12    Year 4, Pharmouthi.
13    Aurelius Theon, I have noted [this].
