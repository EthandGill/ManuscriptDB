[TRANSLATION]
1     In the tenth year, in the month Apellaios = Ph[aophi, on the twenty-fou]rth, in Herakleopolis the (city) above Memphi[s. We swear by King Ptolemaios and Queen Cleopatra also called]
2     Tryphaina, the Father-loving and Brother-loving gods, and the Father-loving goddess, and the Saviour god, and the [Benefactor goddess and the Benefactor god, and the New Father-loving god, and]
3     the Eupator god, and the Mother-loving god, and the Manifest gods (Epiphaneis), and the Benefactor gods (Euergetai), and the [Brother gods, and the Saviour gods, and Sarapis, and Isis, and]
4     all the other gods and goddesses,
5     (I) Polemon son of Eumelos, a Macedonian of the katoikoi cavalry, and (his) son and successor [to the paternal allotment, so-and-so, to so-and-so of the]
6     katoikoi cavalry, (swear) that we consent to the transfer (metepigraphe) that has been made to you from the al[lotment] …
7     around the village of Sobthis of the (district) About-the-City, of one hundred arouras, from whichever (part) you choose … … [and that the other consents to the cession (parachoresis) and to all that is set forth through the]
8     contract together with this handwritten document (cheirographia), through the same agoranomeion. In return for which [acts of service you performed] … [and toward the crown imposed]
9     upon the katoikoi in the nome by the kings and t[he other royal (dues) of the allotment.]
10    Which (arouras) also we shall provide to you free from every royal and priva[te debt and from all crowns and assessments, all those from the]
11    earlier periods up to Mesore of the tenth year; and from other [cessions and from every administration for all time, guaranteeing with every guarantee]
12    without trial and lawsuit and proceeding [before an]y mag[istr]ate … [And if we transgress any of the (terms) through the contract]
13    or of those [w]ritten in this handwritten document, [let the cession remain valid] [according to what has been transacted], [but let us ourselves be liable to the oath, whenever we are au]dited before whatever tribunal you choose …
14    Year 10, Phaophi 24. Of Pole[mon] [and so-and-so:] [handwritten document to so-and-so.]
