[TRANSLATION]
1   To the registrar of the Oxyrhynchit[e nome],
2   Epimachos son of Pausiris son [of P]tole[mai(os)],
3   his mother being Herakleia daughter of Epimach[os],
4   of the village of Psobthis
5   of the lower toparchy. I swear
6   by Nero Claudius Caesar Aug[ustus]
7   Germanicus Imperator that no
8   collection has been made
9   by me in the same village
10   on any account whatsoever,
11   nor indeed that from now on I will be set over
12   the village; or may I be liable to the oath.
13   Year 13 of Nero Claudius Caesar
14   Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
15   month Sebastos 22.
