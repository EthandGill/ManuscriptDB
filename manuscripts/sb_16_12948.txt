[TRANSLATION]
1     [Consulship] of Flavii Zeno and Postumianos the most illustrious.
2     Phaophi 12, second indiction.
3     To Claudius Colluthus of Dorotheos, venerable and prominent.
4     Of the most illustrious city of Antinoe, from [Aurelius] Apalos.
5     Of Colluthus mother[...], Autos from the Sacred Island.
6     Of the Hermopolite [nome], leaseholder. I confess willingly.
7     And willingly to have leased from [your] excellence.
8     For a two-year period [reckoned] from the sowing of crops.
9     Of the fortunate third indiction, the existing field to you.
10    A vineyard with living plants, of arurae of which there are.
11    With [water-works] [three] [and] wooden [instruments] [fitted-out] by [your] landholder … [plants].
12    All and the [cells] of the [field].
13    Of the Sacred Island.
14    [Vineyard-work].
