[TRANSLATION]
1     To Deios and Sabinos, former gymnasiarch, and partners, public bankers.
2     We the undersigned public and private cattle-raisers of the following villages, through Chrysas
3     who was appointed (representative), have received from you the (sums) instructed to be issued to us by Ptolemaios, royal scribe of the Polemon division, also discharging
4     the strategia of the two divisions, which (sums) we asked to be issued to us for
5     transport-charges of wheat and barley, which we brought down from the granaries of the Polemon division
6     to the harbors, the work having been done
7     under the strategos Demetrios, of the produce
8     of the 17th year, of 49½⅓ (and a) 12th artabas of wheat, at 8 drachmas for each artaba: the
9     409 drachmas, two obols, thereby gathered; of barley 1½⅓ artaba, at 480 drachmas per 100 artabas,
10    the 8 drachmas, four obols, thereby [gathered]; the (sum) on
11    the same in total, 408 drachmas of silver. And it is as follows: of the public (cattle-raisers) of Moucheos.
12    Pach[noubis] son of Pachnoubis, 5⅙ artabas of wheat;
13    of the public (cattle-raisers) of Ares. Ares son of Orsenoupis, 1½⅓ (and a) 12th artaba of wheat;
14    of Ke…kom. Onnophris son of Herakles, 1½¼ artaba of wheat;
15    Panouris son of Panouris, 1⅓ (and a) 12th artaba of wheat; Harmiysis
16    son of Hatres, 2½⅓ (and a) 24th artabas of wheat; Orsenoupis son of Titheous, 1⅔⅛ artaba of wheat;
17    of the public (cattle-raisers) of Kerkesoucha. Auleris, fatherless, 1⅙ (and a) 24th artaba of wheat;
18    Ares son of …eos, 6½⅓ (and a) 24th artabas of wheat, of barley a 24th of an artaba;
19    Pa…ris also called Panesneus son of Petesouchos,
20    3½⅓ [(and a) 24th] artabas of wheat, of barley a 24th of an artaba; Ptolemaios son of Papontos,
21    ⅔ of an artaba of wheat, a third of an artaba of barley; Aurelius, fatherless, from Ares,
22    5[½] artabas of wheat, a 24th of an artaba of barley; Onnophris son of Herakl…, 3½⅓ (and a) 24th artabas of wheat,
23    a 24th of an artaba of barley. Of the private (cattle-raisers) likewise. Diogenes son of Theon,
24    13 artabas of wheat. I, Chrysas, having been appointed (representative), receive
25    the four hundred and eight drachmas of silver. Total: 408 drachmas, as stated above.
26    Year 18 of Antoninus Caesar the lord,
27    Mecheir 29.
