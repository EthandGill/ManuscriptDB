[TRANSLATION]
1     Tabetheus to Claudius Tiberianus her brother, very many greetings.
2     Before all things I pray that you are in good health
3     and I make your obeisance before the lord Souchos.
4     I was glad how you sent me my son so that I might do obeisance to him,
5     but you did not … him to a man. For Satorneilos has not found the things that I have made for him. We have bought three minas of flax
6     and I sent them. Do not blame me if you did not provide them
7     to Metellus the soldier. I want you to write about a friend; perhaps
8     provide for him. I was much grieved. I was able to send you the little garment this year; I did not send it
9     because of last year, but I sent it and we have sold them to Kabis
10    the deacon. Going down from Tonis from our home, having entered into the lodging of Satorneilos, having seen our affairs
11    unharmed (untouched by the evil eye), I was not pleased that my son
12    himself trusted Menas. And having all but killed him, he said
13    to me not to be distressed; I said to Satorneilos that I did not sleep for worrying. [As] you damaged me by twelve hundred drachmas, may they perish, the offscouring of my son.
14    And I went down to Alexandria with my son.
15    For this reason madness has seized him, since he was not content to eat the monthly provisions together with the things from him.
16    [If] god wills and you receive the monthly provisions which I have made for you,
17    do not … them. And you too. Concerning last year's things, I have not
18    made … he made last year. I have left them
19    in Alexandria until the second sailing. And
20    he has been ill. I was afflicted, grieved by him; I rejoiced exceedingly
21    that he lived. We pressed him, urging him that
22    he taste of Alexandria, and he says to us that I do not want.
23    We give thanks to the gods that he is like you,
24    no one is able to mock him. Greet
25    all those from you by name. How much I was damaged last year through
26    Satorneilos, neither did he owe these things nor did I, but in all I was damaged. Farewell.
27    Deliver … to … Tiberianus, from Tabetheus.
