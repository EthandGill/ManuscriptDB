1   […] 4(?) at Karanis of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite nome,
2   […]u, about thirty-eight years old, with a scar on the first finger of (his) hand,
3   […to those acting for th]em, those who shall take it over, for re-registration, from the present day onto the
4   […] of katoikic [land, ar]ouras six, or however many there may be, in one [s]eal (parcel), and the adjoining (properties);
5   […on the w]est a canal; on the east, formerly [the] allotment of [Ta]mystha daughter of Herakleides, and assess-
6   [ed …] of the re-registration and cession-disposition, as is fitting, [and] that he receives —
7   […the pr]incipal in full, at once, from hand to hand out of the house — and [that he will guarantee]
8   […the ce]ded allotment, as is stated above, with every guarantee: free, on the one hand,
9   [of …] the [art]aba-tax and the dyke-tax (naubion) and the arithmetic(-tax) and every kind (of due) from the preceding (years),
10   […and from priva]te (debts) and every claim, for [a]ll time, and that none (is) liable,
11   […] of a part, nor [will (anyone) proceed] in any [w]ay, neither […]…
