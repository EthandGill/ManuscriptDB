[TRANSLATION]
1     H[ai]l, I[s]chyrion.
2     [convey] through the split ones
3     about Herakleon of changes
4     40 drachmas so that you separate the rural rue with the date-palms, but especially with Meikrat[o]s's date-palm and the one from the east of the cabin [of the…]
5     from neighbors of the courtyard. But try to obtain
6     a yoke of oxen for [watering]
7     the estate or two yokes, since
8     and I myself reach you on the 13th. So that therefore also
9     you attend carefully
