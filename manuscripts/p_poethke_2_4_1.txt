1     Year fourteen of the Imperator
2     Caesar Traianus Hadrianus Augustus
3     in the month of Daisios Pharmouthi on the ninth in Theadelphia
4     of the Themistos division of the Arsinoite nome.
5     [There] agree Straton son of Heron registered
6     at the amphodos of Apollonios Parembolē as [being] of [the] age
7     twenty-seven, [with a] scar on the right instep, and Sabouris
8     [son] of Pappos from the aforementioned village as [being] of [the] age
9     forty-two, [with] a lentil-shaped mark beside [the] nostril
10    on the right, the two Persians [being] of [the] offspring of each other
11    sureties for [the] satisfaction [of] Athenarios the elder
12    [of] Herodes as [being] of [the] age [fifty-]three, unmarked, with [the] guardian [who is] the cousin on [the] paternal side
