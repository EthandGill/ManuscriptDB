1     Nikon to Zenon gr[eetings. Sen]d someone who will receive the fodder which is being produced for us from the
2     farmers and remind Apolloniu[s concerning] these things, so that he either send someone or authorize provisions so that we
3     hire one. [For we ourselves are engaged with the gath]ering of grain and with [fragmentary text] …
4     we will not be able [fragmentary]. And concerning us no concern did you show so that provisions [come]
5     be added to us and grain-r[ation, although Apolloniu]s having been reminded of us and having ordered a memorandum to be giv[en him].
6     You would do well [not neglecting] us, but caring so that everything turn out better and now having reminded
7     Apollonius concerning [us. For we have] no one else to remind concerning such things.
8     Farewell. Year 28, Mecheir 8.
