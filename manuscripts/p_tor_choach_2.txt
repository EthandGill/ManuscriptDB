[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 52, Pachon 15; it has been appointed [to] the bank in Hermouthis, over which Ammonios, of the tenth of the circuit
2     According to the assignment of P[tolema]io[s] the tax-collector, under which Ammonios the counter-scribe signs, Osoreris of Horos
3     Of bare ground of 10 cubits in Pakenei, which [he] purchased from Imouthes of Thotsuton, of copper talents 2; end As.
