[TRANSLATION]
1     Demetrios to Kriton, greeting.
2     Of the administration that has taken place,
3     Jason brings the copies,
4     of the portion of Polemon,
5     apart from the thousand-aroura vineyard
6     around Phylakitike Nesos,
7     of silver, 1 talent 2000 drachmas,
8     to Orses and Hegemon.
9     And the thousand-aroura we let
10    at the lower (figure) of 1000 drachmas,
11    for which it will be necessary trustworthy guards
12    to be sent by us; total: 1 talent 3000.
13    Letous Polis: previously it
14    was 2000, but now, to Theophilos
15    and Petalon, 2600.
16    Teschis: previously
17    it produced from self-cultivation 1000,
18    but now, to Theodoros and
19    Dorion and Petesouchos, 1700.
20    Kerkesoucha and Sebennytos:
21    previously it produced
22    from self-cultivation 1700,
23    but now, to Antipatros
24    and Hegemon and Ammonios
25    and Eudemos the grain-collector, 3000.
26    Of the (division) of Themistos, unadministered,
27    and in the (charge of) the royal scribe,
28    the brother of Petosiris, 3000.
29    Previously they were 2500.
30    Until writing to you I have not granted it,
31    but so that they may not do us some harm
32    I think fit to grant it to him;
33    and he brings also my account, complete,
34    for last year up to now.
35    And of all these the
36    security I hold.
37    The rest which must be administered in addition:
38    Hephaistias, Karanis,
39    Bakchias, Tanis,
40    the things around Philadelpheia,
41    Pharbaithos, the hamlet
42    of Metrodoros.
43    The total of the things
44    administered comes to: 3 talents 1500.
45    Farewell. Year 5, Pauni 6.
