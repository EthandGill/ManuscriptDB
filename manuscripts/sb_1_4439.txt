[TRANSLATION]
1     to those over the sacrifices
2     appointed
3     by Aurelius Horion
4     Kiale from village
5     Apiados remaining
6     in village Theadelpheia.
7     ever sacrificing to the gods I persisted and now before present you according to the commands I sacrificed and poured and of the victims I tasted and I ask you
8     to make a note. fare well.
9     Aurelii Serenos and Hermes
10    we saw you sacrificing.
11    Hermes I have marked [this].
12    year 1 Emperor Caesar
13    Gaius Messius Quintus
14    Traianus Decius Pious
15    Fortunate Augustus, Pauni.
