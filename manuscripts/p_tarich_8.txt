[TRANSLATION]
1     To the chrematistae who judge the royal and revenue cases,
2     of whom Dionysios is introducer, from Onnophris son of Teos,
3     embalmer of those from Tanis of the division of Herakleides, who jointly presented (the case) to Amenneus
4     son of Horos, concerning the fact that we submitted
5     a memorandum to Apollonios the dioiketes … against Abychis son of Horos and Kechonsis son of Pasis and Pasis son of Psenemous,
6     embalmers of those from Philadelphia,
7     (stating) that there ought to be taken up into the royal [prerogative]
8     [what] was in the embalming-establishments in Philadelphia of Psenephmous son of Paos, [who fell in the]
9     disturbance and who was engaged in business in Phila[delphia];
10    and on the 2nd of Hathyr of the 21st year …
11    … of me against you by Poseidonios
12    the chief guard, inasmuch as … and, when I had given a written undertaking that I would be present according to the
13    … appointed appearance. Since therefore the …
14    … have been released, because they are
15    … according to the letter of Par…nides
16    until the 30th of Hathyr, the aforesaid persons … enjoy the produce
17    of the produce of the prerogative contrary to what is due, which it was necessary
18    for them to have paid into the royal treasury. I request therefore
19    that you instruct Dionysios the introducer, so that
20    he may write to Marres the prophet of Souchos to give security for the accruing produce of the aforesaid [prerogative]
21    until you make the investigation concerning these matters.
22    And when this has been done, nothing useful to the royal treasury will be omitted.
23    Farewell.

25    and …
26    …
27    that it be done.
