1     Onnophis to Zenon
2     Greetings. I received the from you
3     letter and I went down with Eutychides to the hunt and we were not able to send more except
4     moray eels thirty and
5     larger ones twenty, but it will be a care to me
6     of each day
7     to send the
8     [things befalling].
9     the [but] [letter]
10    which you wrote to Ammonios, he disobeyed it.
