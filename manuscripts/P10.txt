[TRANSLATION]
FOLIO Folio 1 Recto — Romans 1:1-7
1:1    Paul, a servant of Christ Jesus, a called apostle, set apart for the gospel of God,
1:2    which he promised beforehand through his prophets in holy Scriptures,
1:3    concerning his Son, who was born of the seed of David according to the flesh,
1:4    who was designated Son of God in power according to the Spirit of holiness by the resurrection of the dead — Jesus Christ our Lord,
1:5    through whom we received grace and apostleship for the obedience of faith among all the nations, for the sake of the name of Jesus Christ;
1:7    to all those who are in Rome, beloved of God, called saints: grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Christ Jesus.
