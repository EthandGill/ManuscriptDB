1     To Anubion [strategos of the] Arsinoite [district of Themistos and] Polemon's divisions
2     from the village secretaries of both divisions.
3     Concerning a letter written by the most excellent epistrategos
4     Arrius Victor, a copy of which is [as follows]:
5     Arrius Victor to the strategoi of the Arsinoite [district] greetings.
6     How much in each village
7     [distribution] [has been made] of cattle and calves
8     and goats and [swine] and hay
9     and whatever else the land produces among them;
10    further also ... and how much wine, how much
11    has been distributed [and] how many [tens of thousands] have been collected
12    into ... into [the] annona for
13    our lords forthwith make known to me
14    adding to the documents also what was expended
15    in silver ... in advance from the most sacred
16    treasury into how much and what kinds it was distributed.
17    For just as I think care [has been taken]
18    [the local authorities] in having provided the supplies
19    [to] the most noble soldiers, so also
20    [care] should be taken of them, it is necessary
21    ...
22    ... others ... meat of ...
