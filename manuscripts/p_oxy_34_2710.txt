# HGV-EN: (From: edition.) … page … roll one. Translation of the Latin. To Lucius Mussius Aemilianus the most illustrious prefect of Egypt, from Aurelia Heras, daughter of Castor and Syra, from the city of the Oxyrhynchi. I beg you, lord, to give me, as guardian to be registered in accordance with the lex Iulia Titia and the senatusconsultum, Aurelius Chaeremon, son of Diogenes, who was chief-priest of the same city. Given in the consulship of our lords the emperors Macrianus, for the second time, and Quietus, for the second (?) time. First year; Pachon 22nd.
