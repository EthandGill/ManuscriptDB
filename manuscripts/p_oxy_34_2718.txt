[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the] consulship of the Flavii Constantinus and Rufus, the most
2     [illustri]ous; Pauni 11.
3     … To [L]eukadios, the reverend councilman of the splendid
4     [and most] illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites: the Aurelii Abraham
5     son of M[arty]rius and Serenus son of Phoibammon and Apphous son of Didymus
6     a[nd Jo]annes son of Cornelius and Samuel son of Phoibammon, all
7     iron-and-bronze-smiths from the same city: the other, earlier bond of ours being authoritative
8     and secure
9     according to its credit, we acknowledge on the basis of joint liability
10    that we have now also received and ta[k]en in from your Virtue, for
11    the price of nails and other supplies given for the public
12    bath that has been built, God willing, two solidi of gold; and for
13    the security of the receipt of the same two solidi
14    we have made this bond too, it being authoritative, and on being formally questioned
15    we gave our assent. The Aurelii Abraham son of Martyrius and Serenus son of Phoibammon a[nd A]pphous son of Didymus and Joannes son of Cornelius and Samuel
16    son of Phoibammon, the aforesaid, have made the bond, having received
17    now also the … of the work of the public bath, and all the things written agree with us
18    as set forth above. I, Aurelius He[r]minus son of Alypius, wrote
19    on their behalf, present (but) not knowing letters.
20    Through me … [the no]tary it was written.
21    [A bond of the Aurelii Abraha]m[i]us and Seren[us] and Apphous and Joannes and Samu[e]lius,
22    iron-and-bronze-smiths, from (the city) of the Oxyrhynchites, of gold
23    two solidi.
