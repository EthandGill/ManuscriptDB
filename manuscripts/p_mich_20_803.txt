[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Gratian, the most illustrious son of our master Valentinian,
2     the eternal Augustus, and of Dagalaiphus the most renowned, Hathyr 29.
3     To the Aurelii Agathinos son of Didymos and Jeremias son of Achillion and Petros
4     son of Agathinos and … on and Sarapion son of Plutarchos, all the councillors of the
5     [city of the Oxyrhynchites], superintendents of the grain of Alexandria,
6     from A[urelius Olympios] son of Pinouthios, captain of the boat of Theogonios, citizen.
7     I acknowledge, [swearing the august] divine oath of our masters Valentinian and [Valens the e]ternal Augusti, … (that it has been) brought down from the (city) of the Oxyrhynchites
8     according to the bonds which I made out … of the prosperous tenth new indiction, of new wheat, pure, free of barley and unadulterated and not having one grain of barley
9     but being most pure, and through barley-picking the barley found in the
10    cargo has been picked out, in all 632 artabas, [and (I will) hand over the load] into the imperial granaries at Alexandria and [receive] receipts from the office of my lord
11    the most eminent prefect of the annona; [and if any disturb]ance should arise on the pretext
12    of barley or of any other foulness, because the grain was beforehand [pure] and exactly picked grain by grain and made free of barley as I received it, clearly I shall be liable for the loss that befalls, [and] I have not [at all] had cause to complain against anyone about this, so that I be in no way blamed, or else may we be liable to the divine oath.
13    Aurelius Olympios son of Pinouthios, I swore the divine oath and received the barley-picking as aforesaid.
14    Aurelius Thonis, I wrote on his behalf, he writing slowly, with me subscribing.
15    Olympios, I have noted it.
16    Conjoined (sheets) …
17    of [Al]exandria …
18    in the month of Novemb[er] …
19    barley-picking.
