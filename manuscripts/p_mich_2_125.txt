[TRANSLATION]
1     Of expense for the metropolis through Kronion:
2     to Heronas, 6 drachmas;
3     to Ptoll(is?), 8 drachmas;
4     … to (so-and-so), [12] [drachmas];
5     of unwritten (contracts), 8 drachmas;
6     … in Tebtynis likewise:
7     to Heronas from
8     Philoxas through Kronion, 40 drachmas,
9     and through Eutychas, 20 drachmas.
10    On the 14th [of the] same [month], an agreement of Orseus and another with Ptollis and another for a sale of looms; 20 obols.
11    On the 17th, a lease of Leonides with Mysthas and another of pastures on account; 4 drachmas.
12    A bond of Kastor; 6 obols of the tavern-keeper.
13    A claim of Orsenouphis and others against Epainetos; 4 drachmas.
14    Month Sebastos, 8th:
15    an agreement of remaining-in-service of Herakleides son of Pateroous with Stottoues son of Petearpsenesis; 6 obols.
16    On the 9th, an agreement of Ptolemaios son of Diodoros and his wife [with] Herodion son of Lysimachos for a deposit of 4,000 drachmas; 4 obols.
17    An agreement of … and her husband Kollouthos with Orseas; 4 drachmas.
18    On the 10th, an agreement of a receipt of rent of Soueris with Kronion for 3½ arouras; 10 obols.
19    An agreement of Kronion with Horion for a sale of sheep; 6 drachmas, of which he owes 2 drachmas.
20    On the 19th, an agreement of Papontos with Didyme for a dowry of 302 drachmas; 6 obols.
21    An agreement of Phemnasis with Mene[ma]chos for a dowry of 28 drachmas; 6 obols.
22    A bond of Paas and others to [O]uenoustos son of Primigenis concerning a co[p]y of a letter; 10 obols.
23    An[other ][bo]nd [t]o the same man, of Harpheesis and others, concerning the measured
24    [grain] to Apion the [grain]-collector; 10 obols.
25    [Another bond ][of Pap]nebtynis and others to the same man concerning … to [guard] foo[d. 3 obols.] …
26    [A memorandum of Onnophris [aga]inst Sisenxis of a receipt of dowry [of 16 drachmas. 7 obols.]
