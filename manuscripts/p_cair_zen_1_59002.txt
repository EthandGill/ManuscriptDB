1     Apollonios to-Zenon greetings. We-have-sent [to-you]
2     Nikomachus and Zoilos. Introduce then them …
3     into the light-ship or the cabin so-that they-sail and the maintenance-allowance which also to-the
4     remainder is-set-out and these [men] also receive. They-have advance-[payment]
5     from-us each in silver drachmas twenty.
