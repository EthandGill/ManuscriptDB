[TRANSLATION]
1     Demetras to Publius
2     to the decurion, greetings.
3     Know [that] Nemonin, the water-laborer
4     has not obeyed the water-works task.
5     Therefore I have written to you so that you might know.
6     Farewell.
7     Small baskets and large baskets which were brought from Raeima have not been placed in the
8     water-works, but they say
9     that they have brought them to Claudianum. Order them to be brought [in] any case.
