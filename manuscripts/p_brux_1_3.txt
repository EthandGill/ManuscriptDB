[TRANSLATION]
1     92
2     To Apion, royal scribe of the Prosopite (nome),
3     from Therothbechis and Therobasthis, both daughters of Peteesis
4     also called Athas son of Hephaistion, from Thelbonthon.
5     We register, according to the things ordered by Calvisius Stat[ianus] the most illustrious
6     prefect, for
7     the house-by-house census, made for good ends,
8     the things belonging to us
9     in the village of Thelbonthon Siphtha:
10    a house and bare plots of Peteesis
11    also called Athas son of Hephaistion, his mother being Taartysis
12    daughter of Pnepheros; and, from
13    the adjoining house, bare plots
14    formerly of Thompaus son of Pnephero…
15    son of Phalous, in which (are):
16    Athas son of Peteesis also called
17    Athas son of Hephaistion, his mother being
18    Taartysis daughter of Pnepheros, aged 56,
19    by right of residence (paramone),
20    Therothbechis the sister, of the same
21    parents, aged 44,
22    Therobasthis, another sister, of the same
23    parents, aged 48,
24    Areios, his mother Thermouthion,
25    a slave aged 28, without distinguishing marks,
26    Pathermouthis, his mother
27    Thaubarous, a slave
28    of the same (owners), aged 18,
29    and a house
30    and a bare plot of Pnepheros
31    son of Tithoes and of Peteesis also called Athas son of Hephaistion, now being
32    a bare plot, and to the north
33    of this another bare plot
34    of 700 cubits.
35    Year 14 of the Emperor Caesar
36    Marcus Aurelius Antoninus
37    Augustus, Armeniacus,
38    Medicus, Parthicus,
39    Germanicus Maximus,
40    Epeiph 25.
41    We, Therothbechis and Therobasthis,
42    the aforewritten, have submitted (this) as
43    set forth above. I, Petos son of Petsiris, wrote on their behalf since they do not
44    know letters.
45    Through Harpok…, page 42.
