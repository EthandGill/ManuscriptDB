[TRANSLATION]
1     To Andromachos, strategos of the A[r]sinoite Polem[on]'s division
2     from Akous son of Herakleous of the village
3     Teptyneos. On the new moon of the present
4     month of Thoth, Orsenoufis [and] Pouheris, both of Mieutes, and Theon and Sarapas, both of Chaire[s and] Pouheris of Ptolliōn and Marō[n]
5     Orseos and Ors[eu]s himself Prōt[a] came arrogantly into the house which I have in the village … in the gateway, and [conversing] with them
6     against them [they] inflicted [on] me blows against
7     every [limb] of [the] body [and they] carried off as many as
8     a tunic and white [garment] [and] mantle …
9     white [garment] and scissors and beer [and] a weight
10    of salt and oth[er] things which at [the] present time I do not know.
11    And I stationed [the] village [headman]
12    and Heracles [son of Heraclei]des. I request that they be brought
13    before you.
