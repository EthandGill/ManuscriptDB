[TRANSLATION]
1     ⳨ (chmg)
2     In the reign and consulate of our most divine
3     lord Flavius Justin, the eternal Augustus,
4     Emperor, in the third year, on the nineteenth of Phamenoth, of the first indiction. In the most splendid city of Antinoou,
5     Aurelius Martinos son of Sabinianos, from his mother
6     Martha, slave of the glorious house of the all-praiseworthy
7     Athanasios the patrician, having become castrensis to John
8     the most glorious alabarch, exercising the office over this
9     city of Antinoou, to [A]urelius Kollouthos son of Victor, poulterer,
10    from the same city, greeting. I, the aforewritten
11    Martinos, castrensis, acknowledge through this my written
12    security, that is, cancellation, that henceforth no one
13    shall have nor have any claim against you and against your
14    heirs, ever, concerning your note (pittakion)
15    of the two solidi which were lent
16    to you by me, because I have now been paid these by you
17    together with this deed of dissolution made to you by me.
18    And because the said note of yours has been lost
19    among my papers,
20    not having found it to return it to you, or because of the urgency
21    to make a search for it; on this account I further promise hereafter to search whether I may anywhere find it and
22    return it to you, that is, that this very (note) is to be void
23    and invalid wherever it be produced against [you]
24    by any person whatsoever [from] my side or that of another
25    having superior force, because I have already received [and] been paid
26    by you now the said two solidi together, [less twelve] keratia,
27    by the standard of Antinoou. And for your security I have made this
28    cancellation, [it being] valid and s[ecure as set forth,] with
29    the signature of the one signing on my behalf and bearing witness; having been questioned I acknowledged.
