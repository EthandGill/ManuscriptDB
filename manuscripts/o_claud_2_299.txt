[TRANSLATION]
1     Serapion to Serapion the
2     father [send] much greeting. Before all
3     I pray for your health. I was compelled through a small letter to greet [you].
4     You will do well if the papyrus
5     [you buy], having gone to Didymos
6     the teacher, and having given bronze,
7     so that he might copy for me a prose
8     text. For the rest then do not do otherwise.
