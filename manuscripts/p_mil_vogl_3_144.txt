1     To Heron son of Amatios, former exegetes,
2     [f]rom Turbon son of Kronion from the village of Teptynis. I wish to lease from you for four years from the
3     present 7th year the half share belonging to you
4     of a holding of 5 arouras around the village of Teptynis, called "Of Pelbonis," for the first and third
5     [yea]r of the lease for the sowing of bar[ley],
6     of rent each year, without seed-loan, forty-[fiv]e artabas of barley, and for the second
7     and fourth year for the sowing of fodder for cutting, of rent each year, without seed-loan, sixty drachmas of silver, all free of deduction and exempt from risk,
8     performing the yearly wor[ks] as many as are fitting from
9     [m]y own (means) at the proper [seasons], and the [yearly]
10    rents I shall repay in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor, new, clean, unadulterated, by the measure of Didym[e]
11    of Tebtynis, but the rent in Phamenoth,
12    the cost of the freight-charges, the speeding-up, and the donkey-driving being upon me; [but of the]
13    convey[ance] of the rents (the cost being) up[on you; and]
14    af[ter the] time I shall hand over the
15    arou[r]as cle[an] of r[u]sh, reed, couch-grass, and all moisture.
16    If it seems good, lease (it). I, Heron,
17    former exegetes, have leased (it) on the
18    rent of forty-five artabas of wheat
