[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord, most honoured and truly
2     most longed-for brother Psais,
3     Pamouris, in God, greeting.
4     First of all, we salute much your Reverence, I
5     and my wife and the sons by name, being well meanwhile by the providence of God. Salute for us
6     the lord brother Theognostos
7     and the son Andreas. Concerning
8     your arrival to us,
9     most honoured one, day by day we have been expecting you for a long time, ever since you wrote. And, by God, on
10    your account I have remained here, not having gone off to Antinoou for a necessary need with the brother Pekysios. But behold, he has often sent for me there, but
11    since I am expecting you, I did not go. This very thing I also wrote to him, that I am expecting the brother with
12    the children here. Therefore do not neglect to come. Bring me with you a small axe and a bronze pan
13    for baking. Salute for me
14    each one by name. I have been amazed how, when so many have come to us, not even
15    if of a letter did you deem us worthy through so long a time,
16    but indeed I too had decided
17    not to write, and yet I was not able to bear it, especially since
18    Philammon is here; or do you not know that we thirst for your letters? Salute for me Kapiton and Psais
19    son of Tryphanes, with wives and children. Salute much for me
20    the mother Maria and the little
21    Tsempnouthes. Send me, I beg, the girl. I will give the
22    freight-charge, and every year
23    I will grant you wool for a cloak as
24    her wage. Farewell to me, lord.
25    I beg, receive from Kame the ten staters
26    of warp-thread, and give them to Psais, son of Tryphanes. I am amazed, mother Maria, how you did not write
27    to me that I received the hanging-cloth from Psais; receive this other one from Philammon and a little pot of fish.
28    I beg, mother Maria, send me the pan and the iron ring, because I want to put them on the loom.
29    I, Maria, salute my lady the mother
30    and the sister with children. There salute you
31    also my children. Farewell to me.
