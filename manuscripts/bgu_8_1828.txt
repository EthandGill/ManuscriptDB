[TRANSLATION]
1     To Seleucus [his] kinsman and gener[al]
2     and [the one] over the revenues
3     from Ptolemaeus [called] Epinicus
4     prison-keeper of the prison [being] in Phnebiae. Having farmed [land]
5     in the present thirtieth [year] and first year
6     from the formerly Apikcius revenue near Phnebiae in the formerly Agelaus allotment [of land] nine arouras
7     [as] tax that was fixed for each
8     aroura of lentil [of] five artabas from Soteles
9     I have proceeded to present the petition. I request that if
10    [it] seems [right] to order [you] to write to Heracleides the district-ruler having received the aforesaid amount of the taxes not to trouble me,
11    so that I may have obtained [the benefit] of the help from you
12    assistance.
13    Farewell.
