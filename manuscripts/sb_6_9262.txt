[TRANSLATION]
1     With God, Maamed son of Abulkasem to you
2     Damianus deacon from the village of Leukogion. [He] has obtained through you for a written order
3     of indiction 11, amount of currency 3, third only. It was written in the month Thoth 11 of indiction [13].
4     amounts to currency 3
