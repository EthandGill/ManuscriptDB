[TRANSLATION]
1     Measurement of treasury of the metropolis, produce of year 1 of Marcus Aurelius
2     Severus Alexander Caesar, the lord
3     Mesore 20, for Charax district, Esminus [son] of Herieus: wheat, [one] artabe [and a] half [and an] eighth,
4     and for Nesos district, of the same wheat, [one-half of an] artabe, and for Notus district, of the same
5     wheat, [one-sixth of an] artabe, [and] 24, [they] total wheat artabae 2 [and] 1/3. Aurelius Phil I have signed.
