[TRANSLATION]
1     … [indiction] in the city of Arsinoe.
2     To the most holy [presbyter and scribe and overseer of the city of Arsinoe Aurelius] son
3     of Kainos and Damianus son of Abraamios and Menas son
4     of Nei[los and] Apollos son of Amaios from the village
5     of Philoxenos of the Arsinoe nome greetings. We acknowledge
6     from mutual-surety willingly in mind to guarantee and
7     to undertake before your holiness Aurelius Ammona son of Asagon from the same
8     village, whom also we guarantee ready us
9     to have prepared him to be cherished by the
10    [his] wife Mary and to care for her as fitting [it is] for free women henceforth and
11    evident. But if we shall not do this, it is necessary for us to be
12    [to] lead him and hand him over in your jail
13    from where we also received this one. But if we do not
14    hand over, as said, the one sought, we are liable to make defenses on behalf of him to her concerning
15    [all] of the things sought from us by her
16    as much as his. The guarantee is valid and having been questioned we acknowledged. Aurelius John son of the blessed Kalos I witness to this
17    guarantee, [as set forth]. Georgios scribe son of Menas I witness to this
18    [gua]rantee, as [set forth].
19    by me Kallinicos. by me Kallinicos.
