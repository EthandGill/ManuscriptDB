r.2    περὶ τοῦ στιππύου τὴν μὲν τιμὴν
r.3    ὑπέστη Αἰσχύλος θ δραχμὰς τοῦ ταλάντου,

r.5    ἐξ ὅσου ἂν ἀποβῆ[ι], ἐκ τοσούτου τὴν

r.8    ἔρρωσο.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Demetrius to Zenon greetings.
2     Concerning the horse the price
3     Aischylos undertook 9 drachmas per talent,
4     and that it must be combed, and
5     from however much it turns out, from that much the
6     price the aforesaid to be fixed. But the horse is in the open,
7     lying in the Pataician [area], and I don't have the one who would guard it.
8     Farewell.
