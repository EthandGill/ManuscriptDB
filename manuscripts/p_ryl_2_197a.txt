# HGV-EN: ‘Paid at the custom-house of Socnopaei Nesus for the tax of 1/100 and 1/50 by Sarapion, exporting on one camel six artabae of vegetable-seed paying five drachmae, and on one camel and two donkeys twelve artabae of wheat paying three drachmae. The 3rd year of Antoninus and Verus the lords, Hathur 4.’
