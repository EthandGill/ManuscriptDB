1     Measurement of treasury of villages of produce of year 30 of [Commodus] Antoninus
2     Caesar the Lord, Hadrian 3 of year 31
3     by name of Horos the younger Psenchonsis of Panechatos
4     for arrears of year 27 of metropolis
5     grain, two artabas, it becomes grain artabas 2. Horos, I have signed.
