[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysius to Heracleides the
2     most honored, greetings.
3     Having learned from the letter which you wrote to me
4     that you are well, I was pleased, brother.
5     And as for the ship to you, as you wished,
6     I shall consider. It is good that you are here
7     having come with me to stay until
8     we find the small boat.
9     I pray that you be well,
10    most honored brother.
11    To Heracleides, scribe of the strategos of the Oasis of Thebes
