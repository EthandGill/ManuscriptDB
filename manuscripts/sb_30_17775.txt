[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God. To you, greater ones of the estate of Mouchis:
2     Reckon to Chael the bread-maker for the payment of Thoth 14, indiction 11,
3     through Chael the notary, monetary unit one, one only. It was written in the month of Thoth 14, indiction 11.
4     [It] makes one
