[TRANSLATION]
1     To Onesimos also called Herakleides, son of Ptolemos who served as high-priest,
2     from Kronion son of Didas, grandson of Dioskoros, from
3     the Syrian quarter, and Petesouchos son of Petesouchos, grandson of Petosiris, the two of them. We wish to lease from you under mutual surety for six years
4     from the coming sixth year of Antoninus
5     and Verus, the lords Augusti, around the village of Tebtynis, in the so-called marsh,
6     next to the existing flooded (land) of the holding, three arouras: for the first
7     and third and fifth year, for the sowing of barley,
8     at a rent, together with the seed-grain we shall receive,
9     of barley two artabas, in all per year of barley thirty artabas by the measure of Didyme;
10    and for the second and fourth and sixth
11    year, for the sowing of whatever we may choose, except
12    wheat and barley and safflower, at a rent
13    in all per year of vegetable-seed two artabas by the oil measure … and of shoots(?)
14    per year one cock worth four drachmas,
15    all without deduction and free of risk; and we shall perform the yearly
16    work of the arouras, all that is fitting,
17    at our own expense, at the proper seasons, doing
18    no damage; and the rents
19    we shall pay each year in the month Pauni
20    at the threshing-floor, new, clean, unadulterated, the measuring being done by one of your (men),
21    the yearly
22    transport-charges of the speeding-up being ours, but the conveyance of the rents being yours; and
23    after the time we shall hand over
24    the arouras clean of rush, reed, dog's-tooth grass, all rubbish, except
25    … being by the flooded embankment, if it seems good to lease. Onesimos also called Herakleides, the aforesaid, have received a copy of this. Year five
26    of Antoninus and Verus, the
27    lords Augusti, Mesore 2.
28    Memorandum to Onesimos, of [arouras] 3
29    in the marsh, for 6 years.
