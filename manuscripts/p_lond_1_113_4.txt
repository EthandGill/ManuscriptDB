[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ
2     our God and Saviour, in the reign of our most pious
3     master Flavius Maurice Tiberius the eternal Augustus,
4     in the 13th year, Pauni 14, at the end of the thirteenth indiction, in Arsinoe.
5     The Aurelii Joannes also called Phtharouaoumai son of Pamoun
6     and Kastous son of Philoxenos, farmers from the city of the Arsinoites,
7     from the quarter of Moeris, to the most admirable Phoibammon, public tabularius of this city of the Arsinoites, son of the blessed
8     Menas, greeting. We acknowledge, by mutual surety, that we have leased
9     [fr]om you those (arouras) which you hold on rent (pakton) from the most God-loving Leontios
10    the protopresbyter, your father-in-law, in the field of Soulis
11    of the suburbs [o]f the city, thirty arouras, 30, more or less,
12    arouras 30, [m]ore or less, with all their rights, for as long a time
13    as [you] wish, from the crops of the coming fifteenth indiction,
14    and of the crops accruing from them yearly: from
15    the [p]ulse there shall fall to you, Phoibammon, three parts, and to us
16    one part; and of the hay you shall have five parts and we one part;
17    and of necessity we shall perform all the works toward the cultivation of the
18    same arouras, blamelessly and without neglect,
19    and we shall also do the binding of the hay; but of the cutting of
20    the same hay and (the pasturing) of all kinds of quadrupeds and the advance of seed
21    these pertain to you, the seed and the cleansings (?) and the folding (?) of the sheep being reserved at the time of sowing, together with the wage for threshing
22    from the common (account); and we shall let loose into the green crop three goats, and we shall furnish you one hundred fifty satisfactory cheeses and two satisfactory kids
23    and the service of the milk, that is, one jar with little cheeses,
24    at … of milk-bread (?) of one (?) milk, from the feast of the month Tybi
25    until the fourth of the month Pharmouthi, without fail. The lease is valid,
26    and on being [formally questioned] we acknowledged it. The Aurelii Joannes son of Pamoun and
27    Kastous [s]o[n of Philoxeno]s, the aforesaid farmers, have leased the aforewritten
28    arouras
29    [and] we shall do the labors as is aforesaid. I, Aurelius Elias son of Paulos, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
30    [Through me, Sansneus] esemiote … through me, Sansneus …
31    Lease of 30 arouras, more or less, in the field of Soulis, by the Aurelii Joannes and Kastous
32    in the field of Soulis, by the Aurelii Joannes and Kastous, farmers, to [the most] adm[irable] Phoibammon, public [t]abular[i]us.
