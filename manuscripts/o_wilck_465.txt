1   Kametis son of Kame[tis, grandson of Petearpres,] paid,

4   Likewise Pachon 10, 4 dr.; likewise Epeiph 27, 8 dr.;
5   likewise (in) year 6, Phaophi 26, dike-tax 2 dr. 3 ob., (= ) 2 (dr.) 1½ ob. 2 chalkoi.
