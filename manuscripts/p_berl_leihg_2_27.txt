[TRANSLATION]
1     From Mystharion son of Herakleides and Pnepheros son of Satabous and Mysthes son of Alexion and Chairemon son of Eudaimon
2     and Chairas son of Apollonios and Zoilos son of Apion and Harpalos
3     son of Didymos and Markos son of Sarapion and Heron son of Ptollas
4     and Petermouthis son of Chares and Harpalos son of Mysthes; the eleven
5     elders of the district of the village of Theadelphia, to Heron and
6     his associates, the grain-collectors (sitologoi) of the same village, greeting. We have from
7     you others, besides those which you have previously sent to us
8     before the (official) entry, for loans of seed-corn of the
9     present 8th year of Antoninus and Verus the lords,
10    the Augusti, victors over Armenia, Media and Parthia, the Greatest,
11    for the sowing from the produce of the past 7th year,
12    others on account of wheat, six hundred artabas, and of barley
13    thirty artabas, all of which we shall also reckon up
14    out of what is to be dispatched; and the per-head (share) of what
15    is being distributed is to be received by the one appointed by us,
16    as you permitted, Panesneus son of Horos.
17    Mystharion son of Herakleides, I have agreed. Chairemon son of Eudaimon, I have agreed.
18    Pnephros, about 38 years old, without distinguishing mark. Chairas, about 35 years old, scar on the left eyebrow.
19    Zoilos, about 40 years old, scar on the left cheek. Harpalos, about 32 years old, scar on the left hand.
20    Markos, about 30 years old, scar on the right shin. Heron, about 40 years old, scar
21    on the right knee. Petermouthis, about 30 years old, scar on the little finger of the left hand.
22    The seven (have agreed) through Aphrodnomikos, declaring that they do not know letters.
23    Year 8 of Antoninus and Verus the lords, the Augusti,
24    victors over Armenia, Media and Parthia,
25    the Greatest, Hadrianos (= month) 22.
