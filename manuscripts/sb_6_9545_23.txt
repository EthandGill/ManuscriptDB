1     Ulpius Keralis, lessee of the sacred gate
2     of Syene, through Antonius Iulatus the assistant.
3     Thinzmethis son of Pachompaonnophris has paid off on account of the geometric tax
4     of vineyards, produce of [the] third year
5     five drachmas [and] two obols, amounting to five drachmas [and] two obols.
6     Of year four of Aurelius Antoninus
7     and Verus Caesars the
8     lords, Phaophi, the nineteenth.
