# HGV-EN: Is[i]doros, collector of money taxes of Karanis, to Dion, assistant, greetings. I have received from you for the payment of the reckoning for Phaophi one talent of silver and five hundred drachmas, = 1 talent, 500 drachmas, which I shall transfer to the state bank. Year 15 of [An]to[ni]nus Caesar, the lord, Hathyr [x]. And Hadrianos 14 for the payment of the month Hadrianos likewise five hundred drachmas, [=] 500 drachmas.
