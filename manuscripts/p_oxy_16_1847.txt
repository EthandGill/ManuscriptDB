[TRANSLATION]
1     I [came] to the son of Kollouthos the most reverent deacon and he agreed to the agreement
2     the one now sent … e … s and having acted well withdrew. Therefore prepare him
3     your genuine brotherhood [so] that the letter be placed in the middle hand according to what was arranged
4     and some whole [items?] to be given to the woman, so that from them she may write. And concerning servants
5     it was decided that she take them without accounting. Prepare therefore him to do this.
6     To master [of] all, most brilliant one, worthy of honor, genuine brother George, chartoularius and administrator Victor with God, subdelegated.
