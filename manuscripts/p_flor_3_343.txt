r.9    καὶ ὑπερθέσεως καὶ [δίκης ] …

[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most esteemed brother Sarapdorus the singularis Andreas the most distinguished
2     and holding office of the Hermopolitans. I have and owe to your virtue
3     for pure and complete response in gold
4     coins thirty well-weighed by public balance;
5     there results in gold coins thirty well-weighed; and these I am ready to pay out
6     to your virtue within [thirty days counted]
7     from today [the day which] is Mesore the third
8     of the present [tenth indiction, without any objection]
9     and postponement and [dispute] ...
