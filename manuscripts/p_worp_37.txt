[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the Lord and Master Jesus Christ our G]od and Saviour, in the month
2     Phaophi 7, 9th indiction, in the city of Herakles.
3     [The Aurelii Phib son of the mother …]dos a[nd] Ioseph son of Ioannes, whose mother is Anna, and
4     [Petros son of the mother …,] originating from the village of Phnebi of the Herakleopolite nome,
5     … [to the most admirable land-surveyor, son] of Mataeios who was himself also a land-surveyor,
6     … [we acknowl]edge through this our written deed of security
7     [that we have leased from your admirableness, from the property belon]ging [to her] i[n] the field[s] of the same village
8     … of the h]oldi[ng] ten aroura[s], total 10 arouras,
9     … with cistern [and] water-supp[ly an]d machi[ne] a[n]d machine-[st]and and tower,
10    … neighbo[urs] on the [so]uth Th…eou, on the north the pub[lic] road, on the east Aioulios,
11    … [on the wes]t the canal there, for whatever time your admirableness wishes,
12    [from the crops of the coming, with God, ten]th indiction, and that we the lessees Phib and Ioseph provide
13    [and Petros] … each year, for both Nile-flooded and unflooded (land), that is, of fixed gold rent
14    [four arsatic solidi], total 4 arsatic gold solidi, which fixed rent we shall provide each
15    [year] … [for both Nile-fl]ooded and unflooded (land), in the month Pauni, from the threshing-floor of the same tenth indiction;
16    [ … ] … and to you each year, by way of customary due, at the festival of the month Tybi,
17    [ … ] … twenty …lera and one two-sack (bag) of soft loaves at twenty-five to the pair,
18    … one satisfactory piglet at two gold keratia, and a two-sack (bag) of soft loaves,
19    [one, at twenty-fi]ve to the pair. And that we the lessees be ready (to perform) every careful tending
20    [ … for bo]th the arouras and the embankments and the cistern, it being understood that we
21    [the lessees Phib and Io]seph and Petros provide the mechanical implements of the same machine
22    … to [pla]nt [i]n them vines, on condition that we render to you for each
23    [aroura] … of wine one hundred double five-xestes (measures), total for each aroura wine 100 double five-xestes,
24    … [their] tillage unharmed and undamaged as also we received (it). The lease
25    [is valid and firm, and having been formally questioned we acknowledged. The] aforewritten men, it is agreeable to us as is set out above. I, Ioustos son of Petros, wrote on their behalf, since they are illiterate.
26    [ … son from Hera]kles-city, I bear witness to this lease, having been asked by the parties making it.
27    [ … son of …]thion, from Herakles-city, I bear witness to this lease, having been asked by the parties making it; I subscribed.
28    [ … so]n of Serenos of blessed memory, from Herakles-city, I bear witness to this lease,
29    [having been asked by the parties making it.]
30    … through me, Theodoros.
