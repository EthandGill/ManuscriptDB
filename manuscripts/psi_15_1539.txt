r.2    τῶι ἀδελφῶι χαίρειν καὶ

r.4    τῶν βασιλικῶν γραμματέων καὶ ὑπὲρ τῶν κατ’ αὐτῶν

r.6    ποιήσεις συντάξας ἐν οἷς
r.7    ἐάν σου χρείαν ἔχηι συνεργῆσαι α[ὐ]τῶι ὡς ἐν ἅπασι

r.11   ἔρρωσο.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysios to Athenodoros
2     to the brother, greetings and
3     be well; Thoonas the village-scribe of Thosbe has been with
4     the royal secretaries and concerning those [matters] according to them
5     [has been] capable; well then
6     you will do well, having arranged [matters] in which
7     if he should have need of your help to work with him as in all [respects]
8     [he will be established] undisturbed;
9     but listen to him and be attentive in [the] things [that] have happened,
10    and care for yourself so that you are well.
11    Farewell.
