[TRANSLATION]
1     In the sixth year of the Emperor Caesar Nerva
2     Trajan Augustus Germanicus Dacicus, in the month
3     Kaisareios, on the fourteenth, in Theadelphia
4     of the division of Themistos of the Arsinoite nome. Herodes son of [Le]ontios, about sixty-four years old, with a scar on his left shin, acknowledges
5     to Lykos, about thirty-five years old, with a scar
6     on his left thumb, and to Chares, about thirty years old, [with a scar]
7     on the little finger of his left hand, and to Deios,
8     about twenty-five years old, with a scar on his left shin,
9     the three (being sons) of Soterichos, and to their mother [Thaisas] daughter of Chares, about fifty years old, with a scar in the middle of her forehead, with her guardian
10    one of her aforewritten sons,
11    Lykos, that he has received from them thirty
12    drachmas of silver, which the deceased father of
13    those about Lykos, Soterichos son of Lykos, while still
14    living, owed to Herodes according to the acknowledgement drawn up through the
15    same records-office, completed in the fifteenth year
16    of Domi[tia]n; [and] likewise that the same Herodes has received
17    from the a[forewritten] the portion falling to him
18    of all things which the same Soterichos owed to Herodes together with others under written securities, and
19    that neither Herodes nor those acting for him make any claim against those about
20    Lykos nor against those acting for them, concerning the things he has received back,
21    as is aforesaid, in any manner. This deed is valid.
22    I, Herodes son of L[eo]ntios, acknowledge that I have received from those about Lykos and from their
23    mother Th[a]isas thirty drachmas of silver,
24    [which the father of those about] Lykos
25    [Soterichos] owed [me] according to the acknowledgement, and the portion [falling to him] of what he owed together with others under written securities,
26    [and that I make no] claim against them, as is [afore]said. [Lyko]s and the aforewritten brothers: the receipt [has been made out to us] as is aforesaid. He wrote on behalf of them
27    … [because they do not] know letters. It has been registered through the
28    [records-office in Thea]delphia.
29    Acknowledgement of Herodes to Lykos
30    and the brothers,
31    of a receipt.
