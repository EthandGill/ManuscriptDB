[TRANSLATION]
1     Alexas of Pathotes, mother Terautes, Hermopolite
2     [registered at the] garrison of the Libyan as of thirty-five years
3     scar on [the] left shin. To Eu[t]ychides of Sarapion
4     Greetings. I acknowledge having received from you by hand for [advance] principal silver drachmas
5     twenty-four of silver, drachmas 24, on condition that I will provide
6     [myself] for thirty-one [days] in the
7     of the present sixth year of Hadrian Caesar of the
8     [lord] … [being provided] instead of the
9     [aforementioned] drachmas twenty-four
10    because on [these] matters [having agreed together]. [Year]
11    sixth of Autocrator Caesar Trajan
12    Hadrian Augustus, Thoth 11. Horion of Hermias
13    I wrote on behalf of him not knowing letters.
