# HGV-EN: The tenth year of the Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius. Work has been done for the embankment works of the same tenth year for two days in accordance with the command on Mesore 7 and 8 at the Psinali...dyke at Theadelphia by Dius, son of Heraclides, son of Lycus, his mother being Tasooukis. Signed by me, Pretis.
