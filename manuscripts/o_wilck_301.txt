[TRANSLATION]
1   Pkylis son of Prompiouthes and Petorz(methes)
2   son of Hermion, through Apollotas and Hatres,
3   collectors of grain-taxes of Syene. Measured out: Pe-
4   torzmethes son of Petorz(methes), of Bouk,
5   for the shepherds' assessment(?): of wheat, artabas
6   six — total 6 art. of wheat — of the receipts of year 13.
7   Year 13 …, Pachon 22.
8   (2nd hand) I, Hatres, have signed.
