1     Epoeris to Demetrios her son, greeting.
2     Before all things I pray that you are well. Do not
3     think that I have neglected you; for
4     every ten days each time I take an oracle about you. Having found then that the critical things are not opportune for you in this
5     three-month period, for this reason I did not write to you. You wrote to me about the matter of the priest; your brother conferred with him about this
6     and exaggerated it greatly, so that

12    fabricated … upon them
13    all … and I took an oracle that … must be completed
14    … but he himself says
15    … when … not …
16    … new things toward them
17    these new things toward them
18    … your matter … for … …
19    that I give … first
20    and to choose either to remain or for you to journey to Rome; if then
21    you sail up … when are you going to come up? But those with whom you eat and drink, guard yourself from them. They greet
22    you, all those in the house; greet
23    all those in the house. (I pray that) you keep well.
