[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklas and Soter, collectors of money-taxes,
2     to Pikos (son of) Pamonthes (son of) Pikos, (whose) mother
3     is Tasemis. We have received for the assessment anak( ) of Charax, year 21,
4     base drachmas four, total base drachmas 4; and for
5     the assessment for hunting-spears of Charax, year 21, base,
6     {drach.} 4 obols, total base drachmas 4, 4 obols. Year 22 of Hadri[an]
7     [the lor]d, Thoth 28.
8     I, Decius, have signed, drachmas 4, 4 obols.
