1     From Alupius.
2     Hand over to Horrei[on]
3     Supervisor of Euhemeria
4     For the expenditures beside him
5     Of which he will account for wine
6     Monochora one hundred from
7     [The] estate of Elpidephorus

10    The wine monochora
11    One hundred as [is] laid out;
12    To Heroninus, supervisor of Thraso.
