[TRANSLATION]
1     To Heraklianos: colophonia (resin) 37, from the Nephytion (estate);
2     likewise from the estate of Pikeraios, colophonia 5;
3     of wine 3, of vinegar 2, to make up the 5;
4     likewise I have sold a double-jar (diplokeramion) for 8 drachmas;
5     likewise I gave to Horion the clerk a double-jar;
6     […] .. 1 of colophonion.
