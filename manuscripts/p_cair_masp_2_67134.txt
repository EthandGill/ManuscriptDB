1     [Flavii] … [and] Theodosios, sons of Kyros of illustrious memory, former town-councillor,
2     [through me, Victor son of Psaios, through Flavius Victor] …, most reverend presbyter and steward, to the heirs of Apollos son of Dioskoros, through Dioskoros
3     [his son, and Phoibammon his son-in-law, greeting.] I have received from you for the rent of the arouras under you
4     [in various places in the plain of the village of] Phthla, of the holding of Piase, of the fiscal register of the eleventh indiction, thus: for
5     [the holding of Apa] Onnophrios, of wheat-and-barley by half, twenty-one and a third artabas with the additional charges,
6     [and outside] the dyke … [for that under] the said Dioskoros, of wheat-and-barley by half, [artabas]
7     two and two-thirds, and [for that] under Phoibammon the said, inside the dyke, in the place Pirion …
8     of wheat-and-barley by half, five and a third artabas; total together 29 1/3 artabas with the additional charges. And for your security I have issued
9     [this receipt] with my subscription as set forth above. The aforewritten landholders, through me Victor the presbyter
10    [it satisfies] me, the receipt, as set forth above. It was written …

12    of the holding of Apa Onnophrios, inside [the dyke] …
