2     and the gods Soteres and the gods Adelphoi and the gods Euergetai and the gods Ph[i]lopatores and the gods Ep[iph]a[n]e[i]s a[nd] the god Eupator and the god Philomet[o]r and the god[s] Euergetai
3     in Herakleopolis the one above Memphis. [K]assandra daughter of Demokrates, a citizen, with the guardian
4     her own brother [Dio]skourides, commander of men and garrison-commander, leased to Horos son of Horos, garde[ner]
5     of those from Tentyris, the garden belonging to her about the same Tintyris
6     for two years from the (lease) into the twenty-sixth year, at a rent per year of three thousand drachmas of bronze coinage, and per year one Syrian palm-(branch) and another Egyptian one.

8     contracted time with every guarantee; and let Horos withdraw from all the things in the garden [in] the best (condition).

10    or to her agents always each year.
