[TRANSLATION]
1     Menon to Zenon greetings. You will do well …
2     so that I may obtain what Apollonius ordered you to writ[e off for me] …
3     but if it is not possible, write to me, so that I not …
4     appear heavily-occupied, but be released …
5     Menon concerning [one thousand] drachmas
6     which Apollonius has arranged
7     to write off for him. Year 29,
8     To Zenon.
