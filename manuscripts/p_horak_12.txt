[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Pinutios of Didymos, former council member of the Kussites,
2     [distributor] of types of annona of the mines of Maximianoupolis, ninth indiction, to Aurelius:
3     [you brought] from the [great] [Hermopolite] [and you gave over to me] in the same city of Maximianus for the provisions of workers
4     [and craftsmen] from the ninth new [delegation],
5     [namely of the 23 indic]tion, oil, xestai five hundred,
6     [there are of oil xestai] five hundred only, and I issued to you this [receipt for security] and [when asked] I [agreed].
7     [In the year of the consula]te [of Julius Constantius], patrician, brother of
8     our lord Constantine Augustus, and
9     Rufius Albinus, of the most illustrious:
10    Aurelius Pinutios, the aforementioned, I issued the receipt
11    of oil, xestai five hundred, as aforesaid. By me, Plenius.
