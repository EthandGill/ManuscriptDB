# HGV-EN: Agathus Daemon son of Dius through Sarapion . . . , to the sitologi of Monimou, greetings. Transfer, from the amount you hold on deposit for Agathus Daemon, from the wheat-crop of the nineteenth year of Hadrianus Caesar the lord, to Dionysius son of Philiscus . . . , sixty artabas, total 60 art. Year twentieth of Imperator Caesar Traianus Hadrianus Augustus, Thoth 15. (N. Gonis)
