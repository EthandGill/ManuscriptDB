[TRANSLATION]
1     Petition to Zenon from Artemidoros
2     Son of Theodore.
3     [His] father deposited
4     To Pantheros two mills,
5     Which also were in operation; the building
6     On which they were placed—
7     We fixed the rent to Pantheros, likewise also to Semphthes
8     Who now has purchased
9     The house. Having then removed the mills, both Pantheros
10    And Somphthous, me wishing them
11    To remove, do not release [them]. Well then
12    You will do [well] [by] constraining them to return. For because the
13    Father to be absent
14    Us they have scorned.
