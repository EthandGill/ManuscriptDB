r.12   δοῦλος.

[TRANSLATION]
1     And now finding the opportunity of the letter-carrying groom I deemed it necessary
2     and to write, [honoring] the precious feet of your renowned protection after god
3     greeting [her], I beseech her to write to me the things concerning the with god most timely
4     health of her, since, as god knows, in great care I am having become aware
5     that to Koma and to another place [she] has departed. Twenty-eight bundles
6     of olive shoots according to the season I sent for the account of this blessed
7     table, and I beseech her to write back to me concerning the return of these.
8     But just as also in other letters I have written to her [that] two men of Krommyda
9     brought a horse to Kynon and it is with Theodoros the greater one the
10    there because [he] is not able as yet this [horse] to bring it here.
11    to my master, all things worthy of honor, genuine, after god e… brother Sarmates, deputy [prefect], Neilos your
12    slave.
