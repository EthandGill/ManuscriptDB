[TRANSLATION]
1     [H]ave given those from the village of Aphrodite through John, most wonderful deputy, the customary [payments]
2     According to custom of those given by you to the current tribune Antaios, concerning the tax-list of the fourth indiction,
3     gold [nomm]ismata two minus four carats by the goldsmith's weight; [totaling] gold nommismata 2 minus 4 carats by goldsmith's
4     [weight. And for your security] I have made the receipt according to custom as aforesaid.
5     The appointed tribune through me Victor domestic [official] confirms to me the receipt as aforesaid.
6     Pharmouthi 16, indiction 3.
