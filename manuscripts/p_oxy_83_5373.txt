[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of our most divine] and most pious [master] Flavius Justinian the eternal Augustus and Emperor, [in the 26th year, in the] 11th year after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most illustrious, 1st intercalary day, 15th indiction.
2     [To Flavius Apion] the most laudable and most magnificent ex-consul ordinary,
3     [landowner also] here in the illustrious city of the Oxyrhynchites, through Menas, servant, who also asks
4     [and] procures for his own master the same most laudable man the action and obligation,
5     [the Aurelii] Pamoun son of Aleous, whose mother is Helene, and Jonas the scribe, son of Danielios,
6     [whose mother is] …eie, and Horos son of Joseph, and Pamouthios son of Phoibammon, all
7     [originating] from the village of Sepho of the Oxyrhynchite nome, subject to the pagarchy under
8     [your] Magnificence, greeting. We acknowledge, of our own free will, swearing by God the
9     [Almighty] and the piety and victory of the ever-victorious fortune, that we stand surety and
10    [undertake] before your Magnificence for the Aurelii Aleous son of Anoup and Souroun
11    [son of] …los, originating from the same village, so that they, without ceasing,
12    [shall remain] in the same village and shall in no way absent themselves nor indeed
13    [remove themselves] to another place, but also, they being sought by you from us on whatever
14    [day,] for whatever cause of pretext, that we shall bring these men forward and shall deliver them
15    [publicly] to this city, apart from the holy precincts and divine sanctuaries
16    [and every] place of refuge, where we also received them, into the public
17    [prison] of this city; or if we do not do this, that we shall be liable to answer for all
18    [things] sought against them. Valid is the surety, written in a single copy, and being formally questioned
19    [we acknow]ledged. The Aurelii Pamoun the elder, son of Aleous, and Jonas the scribe,
20    [son of Da]niel, and Horos son of Joseph, and Pamouthios son of Phoibammon, the aforesaid, have made this surety, undertaking for the aforesaid
21    Aleous and Souroun, upon all the risks set forth above in this surety,
