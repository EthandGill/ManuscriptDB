[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of God, I [Peter], son of Jacob, and Moses, son of Shnoubios, from the village of Kalahas, in the Taso...
2     [...]he[...] … and … on account of the [Abdella], the city of this, that on account of our need [we] [received] your five coins...
3     ... [Peter] indeed two [artabae] of whole-grain, indeed one [artaba] toward five artabae of whole-grain...
4     ... [artabae] two are for Moses, indeed of whole-grain, one indeed one [artaba] toward … [artabae] two...
5     of whole-grain, two indeed one three toward five artabae of whole-grain which six artabae are...
6     These therefore [are] ready, which God wishes, when the time the lord [becomes] this lamp, this...
7     ... of the fruit of the fifth indiction, the measure of this [with] loss-of-clarity [and] ambiguity, and I [am] the month...
8     [The ... month], son of Zacharias, the lord of [his] testimony [of] measure of this [and] [it happened]...
9     the measure of this I measure of this of that which he gives [to] ... God, Almighty, with the which...
10    those who rule, in the month Phamenoth 25, of [the] indiction 4, and I Stephen, this witness, and I Theodore...
11    son, the blessed Severi, this witness...
12    [It was written] … [Aioumte] … I testify...
