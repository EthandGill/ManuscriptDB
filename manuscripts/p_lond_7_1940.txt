[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 28. Account from Demetrios the assistant.
2     I have from Zenon in Bubastos,
3     through Pelops the dispatch-officer, for the
4     freight-charges, 441 drachmas of silver, an obol, a half-obol, a quarter-obol,
5     and from Aristeas, through a letter of
6     Zenon, of which we have made a receipt,
7     410 drachmas of bronze, four obols.
8     Total: 852 drachmas, a half-obol.
9     From this there has been spent on the freight-charges
10    of the grain shipped from the Kolchonouphite (district),
11    by the receiving-measure, 3,017 artabas of wheat, at 22 drachmas per 100 artabas,
12    total 663 drachmas, three obols, a half-obol. The half of this,
13    331 drachmas of silver, five obols, a quarter-obol, and 331 drachmas of bronze, five obols, a quarter-obol.
14    And the freight-charge of the barley, by the
15    receiving-measure, of 826 ½ 4 artabas,
16    at 16 drachmas, four obols per 100 artabas, total 137 drachmas, four obols, a half-obol, a quarter-obol.
17    The half of this, 68 drachmas of silver, five obols, a quarter-obol of bronze,
18    and 68 drachmas of bronze, five obols, a quarter-obol of bronze.
19    Total, the whole: 400 drachmas of silver, four obols, a half-obol of bronze,
20    400 drachmas of bronze, four obols, a half-obol of bronze.
21    The remainder, I have a surplus of 40 drachmas of silver, three obols, of bronze,
22    and 9 drachmas of bronze, five obols, a quarter-obol, of bronze.
23    And we have, also for the handling-charge and the porterage,
24    from Zenon in Bubastos, 104 drachmas of silver.
25    From this there has been spent:
26    handling-charge to the ship-masters,
27    at 2 drachmas per 100 artabas, of 3,017 artabas,
28    and of barley, 826 ½ 4 artabas, at 2 drachmas per 100 artabas,
29    total 77 drachmas, three obols, a quarter-obol; the half of silver, 38 drachmas, four obols, a half-obol of bronze,
30    and of bronze, 38 (drachmas), four obols, a half-obol of bronze.
31    To the pack-animals that brought down
32    to the river, of 3,017 artabas of wheat,
33    at 2 drachmas, four obols per 100 artabas, and after deducting
34    the sixth, two obols, a quarter-obol of bronze, remainder 2 drachmas, an obol, a half-obol of bronze.
35    Total, of bronze, 68 (drachmas), three obols.
36    And of barley, 826 ½ 4 artabas, at 1 drachma, three obols per 100 artabas,
37    after deducting the sixth,
38    total, of bronze, 12 drachmas, an obol, a half-obol, a quarter-obol.
39    I have a surplus of 65 drachmas of silver, an obol, a quarter-obol, of bronze,
40    and of bronze I have overspent 119 drachmas, three obols, a quarter-obol, of bronze.
41    I have from Aristeas, through the bank,
42    for the grain brought down from the
43    Gynaikopolite (district), from the village of Syron,
44    for the freight-charges and expenses,
45    44 drachmas of silver and 45 drachmas of bronze;
46    others, 48 of silver; others, 20 drachmas.
47    Total: 112 drachmas of silver, 45 drachmas of bronze;
48    the whole, 157.
49    From this there has been spent on freight-charges,
50    through Attinas the (agent) of Demetrios,
51    of 740 artabas, at 12 drachmas per 100 artabas,
52    total 89 drachmas; of this, 44 of silver,
53    and 45 of bronze.
54    And through Petechon the steersman
55    and the handlers there were bought
56    papyri for thatching for the houseboat,
57    at a price of 11 drachmas of bronze; 20 reed-mats
58    at two obols each, 6 drachmas, four obols. And to those who went
59    for the reed-mats, for ferry-boats, an expense of two obols,
60    and for a box for sealing up the half-artaba which we brought up to the sitologos, 1 drachma, two obols.
61    And to the ship-master (in the service) of Amyntas,
62    the hired one, and [to those] who sailed with (him)
63    to the Prosopite (district), what he took in advance
64    for the sailors, which must be exacted
65    because the grain was not shipped,
66    7 drachmas of bronze.
67    Total: 44 drachmas of silver,
68    71 drachmas, two obols, of bronze.
69    I have a surplus of 68 drachmas of silver.
70    I overspend 26 drachmas, two obols, of bronze.
71    The whole that I still owe: 173 drachmas of silver, four obols, a half-obol.
72    For this Artemidoros was appointed
73    to recover from Theudoros 12 drachmas of silver,
74    remainder 161 drachmas, four obols, a half-obol.
75    And of bronze I overspend 134 drachmas, three obols, a half-obol, a quarter-obol, of bronze.
76    which Demetrios brought down,
77    barley,
78    872 ½ artabas.
