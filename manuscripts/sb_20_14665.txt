[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 16 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus
2     Tybi 30. [It] was written off through Antinous and Antiochus
3     Of the bankers, head-tax of year 13, Hermaios, Zoilos the Syrian, twelve drachmas, [total] 12. Pig-tax, similarly year 13, the same [person], two drachmas one obol half-obol, [total] 2 obols 1 1/2.
4     Land-tax, similarly year 13, the same [person], six drachmas four-obol, [total] 6 four-obol.
5     Year 14, head-tax, the same [person] in sum, twelve drachmas, [total] 12.
6     Year 14, pig-tax, the same [person], two drachmas one obol half-obol, [total] 2 obols 1 1/2.
7     Year 14, land-tax, the same [person], six drachmas four-obol, [total] 6 four-obol.
