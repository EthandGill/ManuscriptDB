[TRANSLATION]
1   [To Pro]tarch(os),
2   from Arsinoe daughter of Ammo(nios), citizeness, with as guardian her son Niko-
3   [d]emos son of Dionysio(s), of the Maro(nean) deme, and f(rom) Thermion daughter of Hermio(s), with as guardian
4   [A]mmo(nios) son of Psammis, of the Eusebe(ian) deme. Arsino(e) concedes that she has received
5   from Thermion, from hand to hand out of the hou(se), what she lent her by agree(ment)
6   through the registry in the 15th (year) of Caesar, Thoth: three hundred
7   silver drachmas; [and] that the agree(ment) of the loan is void, together with the
8   (clause of) paramone of her son Paron
9   signified along with it; and that no right of approach is left to
10   Arsino(e), nor to <any other> on her behalf, against Thermion — neither
11   about the same matters
12   nor about anything else at all, written or unwritten, up to the pres(ent)
13   day; and that whoever transgresses this is liable
14   <both for the damages and for the appointed fine>, the terms remaining nonetheless valid.
15   Year 16 of Caesar, Pacho(n) 21.
16   … in the times of Caesar, concerning her own slave child [Agal]mat[ion, so a]s
17   to be nursed by the same Martha; and that no (right of approach)
18   is left [to] Marion, nor to any other on her behalf, against Souerou(s) —
19   neither upon the aforesaid agree(ment), which likewise is void, nor upon
20   anything else at all, written or unwrit(ten),
21   [from times past up to] the pres(ent) day; and that Martha (shall have none)
22   against Marion, neither
23   [about the] nursing-wages of the time between, nor against the little slave
24   child,
25   [and shall not pro]ceed (against them) in any way; and that whoever does proceed against
26   Souero(us), or con-
27   travenes the contract, shall desist at once at his own exp(ense) and — the terms
28   remaining nonetheless valid —
29   the transgressor is moreover liable for the dam(ages) and the <appointed> fi(ne). We req(uest registration).
30   Year 16 of Caesar, Pacho(n) 21.
