[TRANSLATION]
1     leased Aurelius Theon, son of Theon, [an] Antinoan, cosmetes, councillor of the Oxyrhynchite
2     city, to Aurelius Dioscorus, son of Eudaimon, mother
3     Didyme, and to Ammon, [son] of Serenes, [mother] Apoll[onia], both from the same city, [for] a period five years
4     from the new moon of the month of Pharmouthi
5     of the present 3rd year, the pigeon house on his estate near the Dositheus hamlet, containing
6     birds, so that those who have leased these
7     shall provide care, collecting the necessary food, receiving from the landowner each month food of lentils one artaba and wheat [one] artaba, with them paying to the landowner in
8     the first year instead of tax the half part
9     of the manure to be produced and pairs of young pigeons six, Pachon [Pauni] each month three pairs, in the remaining four years similarly the [landowner] shall have instead of tax per year the two-thirds part of the manure to be produced, and pairs of young pigeons in the second year eleven,
10    in the [third] year sixteen pairs, in the fourth
11    twenty-one pairs, in the final year twenty-six pairs; second year Pachon five pairs, Pauni Epeiph each month three pairs; third year Pachon Pauni [each] month five pairs, Epeiph six pairs; fourth
12    year Pachon pau[ni] Epeiph each month
13    five pairs, Mesore six [pairs], and in the fifth year
14    Pachon ten pairs, Pauni Epeiph each month five pairs, Mesore the remaining six pairs. The lease being confirmed, the leased [ones] shall perforce make the care of the pigeon house and shall return [each], as is set out [above], and after the
15    time they shall hand over [the] pigeon house to be made, not being allowed to the landowner within the time
16    to [reject] the leased [ones]. Valid is the lease [and] having questioned each other they acknowledged.
17    In the 3rd year of the autocrators Caesars Publius Licinius
18    O[ual]e[r]ianus and Publius Licinius Valerianus
19    Ga[llien]us, Pious, Fortunate, Augustuses, Pharmouthi 3rd.
20    Aurelius Dioscorus and Ammon, we have leased on all the aforementioned.
21    Aurelius Dioscorus I wrote for him not knowing
22    letters.
