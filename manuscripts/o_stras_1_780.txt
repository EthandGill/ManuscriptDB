[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of assessment through the village in Papa
2     of the Libyan, of the Coptite, Apo[…], exchange table.
3     Year 15 of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, Epeiph
4     … Horos, son of Psenemin, Pabella
5     To Erthe, Pikot, tax-collector of Memnoneia
6     for poll-tax and embankments, year 15
7     in the name of Ero[…] and … silver, drachmas
8     sixteen, amount to drachmas 16.
