[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Maecius N[e]mes[i]anus, tax-collector, acting in succession to the strategos of the Hermopolite nome,
2     and to the Aurelii: Ara … o, councillor, former ex[eg]etes, prytanis in office of Hermoupolis the great, ancient,
3     splendid and most august, and to He[rm]einos, councillor, former agoranomos of the same city, the decemprimi (chief ten)
4     of the toparchy of Patemis Upper, fr[om] Aurelius Apollodoros son of Sabeinos, beneficiarius
5     of the prefect of Egypt. When petitions had been [submi]tted by me to Claudius Marcellus the most eminent
6     catholicus and to Marcius Salut[ar]ius the most excellent procurator of the Emperors, and of the
7     subscription which I obtained, wishing to purchase, according to the things ordered by them, from the public domain,
8     from the unproductive reserve registered for [s]ale at the single price of twenty drachmas (per aroura),
9     in the village-clerk's [distri]ct of Alaba[st]r[in]e, from the holdings of Ademas and Apollonios, 12 arouras,
10    upon the boundaries declared [through the petiti]ons, of which this is a copy:
11    To Claudius Marcell[us the most eminen]t catholicus and to Marcius Salutarius the
12    mo[st excellent procurator] of the Emp[erors], from Aurelius Apollodoros son of Sabeinos, beneficiarius of the pr[ef]e[ct] of Egyp[t]. I wish to purchase, according to the things ordered, at the single
13    price of twenty drach[m]a[s, in the vi]llage-clerk's [di]strict of Alabastrine of the Hermopolite
14    nome, from the holdings of Ademas and Apollonios, 12 arouras; neighbours: to the south bare
15    land, formerly vine, [previously an o]liveyard, and on part toward the east a garden; to the north, toward the west a garden and private grounds, and toward the
16    east private grounds; to the east an old cistern and the dry land of Isidora
17    daughter of Chairemon; to the west an ol[d la]ne, or whoever the neighbours may be on every side all around — which, when confirmed, I will pay over [to t]he public bank in Hermoupolis; and if
18    I am not confirmed, I shall not be held liable by this request. Farewell.
19    Year 3, Pauni 13. I, Aurelius Apollodoros
20    son of Sabeinos, have submitted (this), and it was subscribed for me thus:
21    Let the decemprimi of the toparchy together with the strategos
22    make the delivery to you. Let it be posted up.
23    Sheet 33 of roll 1. Posted up in Hermoupolis.
24    Wherefore I request that the delivery be made to me
25    by you, as you have ordered, the price being paid by me into the
26    local public bank. Farewell (pl.).
27    Year 3 of the Imperator Caesar Marcus Julius
28    Philippus Pius Felix, and of Marcus
29    Julius Philippus, most noble and most illustrious
30    Caesar, the Augusti. I, Aurelius Apollodoros
31    son of Sabeinos, have submitted (this).
