[TRANSLATION]
1     Herakleitos to Zenon, greeting. [If you yourself are in good he]alth and the rest of your affairs are
2     according to plan, it would be as we wish; [and we ourselves are in good health,] and we continually
3     make mention of you on every occasion. [Concerning the little horse], we asked Nikanor to
4     exchange it for us. Since, then, he did not concede, [we were forced] to buy a horse for 800 drachmas and brand it.
5     The horse from you has perished and … And Apollophanes too came to Syria,
6     and we, traveling abroad to Massyas, [met] him at Sidon, and he reported to us
7     that Krotos is at Joppa, wishing to send off slaves and bedding-cloths.
8     He said then that he could no longer sail across; for he had been ordered by Dionysodoros
9     to bring his baggage to [He]rakleia. And he reported to us
10    also about those at home, that they are [all] well. And Menekles too, the one at Tyre,
11    said that, having himself brought certain slaves and freight from Gaza to Tyre,
12    he transferred (them) to Tyre, not having declared (them) to the tax-collectors nor having
13    an export-permit for the slaves, and that those who noticed (it) confiscated (them) from him; so, on his arrival,
14    Apollophanes (went) to Menekles and said that the slaves and the freight were yours;
15    wherefore Menekles also laid hold of him (released them?). I have written to you, then, so that you may instruct Apollophanes
16    to register nothing in your name, unless it seems to you to be useful. And know
17    that we too have suffered much hardship and were barely settled in the month of Daisios
18    of the 29th year. For Nikanor has treated us as one might treat an enemy.
19    You would do well also yourself to take care of yourself that you may stay healthy.
20    Farewell.
21    Of Herakleitos. To Zenon.
