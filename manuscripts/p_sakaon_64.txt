[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our lords Severus Augustus [and Maximinus]
2     the Caesars, for the first time.
3     Aurelius Sakaon son of Satabous, his mother being Thermouthion, from the village of
4     Theadelphia in the division of Themistes, about 50 years old, with a scar by his shin
5     … of the left (leg), to Aurelius Koyle son of Theon … from the village of
6     Phne …, greeting. I acknowledge that I have received [from you]
7     from hand to hand three talents of silver and three thousand drachmas, total 3 talents 3000 drachmas,
8     as a loan for one month from the present day,
9     and I shall of necessity make repayment to you of the aforesaid
10    capital on the seventh of the coming month of Pachon,
11    without delay; and if I do not repay by the appointed time,
12    I shall pay you, for the time that overruns, the interest agreed between one another; and upon the demand
13    let execution be made from me and from all my property, as if by legal judgment; and having been formally questioned I gave my assent.
14    In the 15th and 3rd and 1st year of our lords Maximianus and Severus
15    the Augusti and Maximinus and Constantinus the
16    most illustrious Caesars, Pharmouthi 8. I, Aurelius Sakaon
17    son of Setabous, acknowledge that I have received from hand to hand the three talents of silver and three thousand drachmas and I shall repay as is stated above.
18    I, Aurelius Serenus son of Sotas, wrote on his behalf, he being illiterate.
19    Transacted through me, … (son of) …dos, notary.
20    Chirograph of Zakaon from the village of Theadelphia, receipt for 3 talents 3000 drachmas.
