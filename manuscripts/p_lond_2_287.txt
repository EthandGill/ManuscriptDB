[TRANSLATION]
1     In the ninth year of Imperator Caesar
2     Domitianus Augustus Germanicus, in the month
3     Artemisios, the 28th, Phamenoth the 28th, in Nilopolis of the Herakleides division of the Arsinoite
4     nome; there acknowledge [Pa]pos, about fifty-five [years old], a scar on the middle of the forehead, and
5     Mystharion, about thirty-two [years old, a scar]
6     on the first finger of the left hand, [both]
7     sons of Alkimos, to Satabous son of Stotoetis …
8     a priest of the great great god Soknopaios, about forty years old, a scar on the forehead on the left. The acknowledging parties [have received from him]
9     forthwith [by hand]
10    five hundred drachmas of silver …
11    which are the price of green fodder, arouras …
12    of ninety-six schoinia … which we cultivate around … of Psenyris … and the guarantee shall be effected through …
13    of your sowing of the present ninth [year]
14    of Imperator Caesar Domitianus Augustus
15    Germanicus, and that, [if] they do not proceed, the acknowledging parties against Satabous nor
16    those acting for him, concerning the price which they have received,
17    as is stated above, nor concerning any other matter whatsoever, from the [former] times until the present day in any manner. The subscribers: for
18    the acknowledging parties, Chaireon son of Ammonios, about 56 years old, a scar on the right shin; for the other, Ploutarchos son of Ploutarchos, about … years old, [a scar] on the right shin. Papos and Mystharion, both sons of Alkimos, we acknowledge that we have received from Satabous
19    son of Stotoetis the price of green fodder …
20    … of oxen … arouras …
21    which are five hundred drachmas of silver …
22    of the sowing … and we bring
23    no claim, as … Chaireon son of Ammonios wrote on their behalf, since they do not know letters.
