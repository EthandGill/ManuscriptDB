[TRANSLATION]
1     Agathokles to Demetrius greetings. You have not done well
2     until now still having the threshing floor uncleaned.
3     For the water through the fallow lands being watered
4     by it, has made the grain rain-soaked
5     so as to endanger the exports. Having learned
6     and [that] you are in Alexandria, I have made a decision
7     to the accountant so that through me it may be threshed and
8     I may recover what is mine. Still then even now take care that not … [it] whole be submerged. Make clear to me what is before you
9     so that I may not be in suspense in [my] mind. But if not, I will use [the decision].
10    Farewell.
11    To Demetrius
