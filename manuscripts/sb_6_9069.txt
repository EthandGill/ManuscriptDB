1     [A]urelius Horion, I have marked [it] as 2nd year, Phaophi, 3rd day.
2     [to the] Aurelius Hermaiscos, [who has been] agoranomos, and Isid[orus, also]
3     ..., councillor, record-keeper of properties of the Arsin[oite]
4     [from] Aurelia Casiane, also [called] Thermouthári[on, daughter]
5     [of Aurelius] Deius, Herakleides, son of Ammon[ios, from the neighborhood]
6     [of] Apolló[nios], of Hierakeios, with the guardian of the aforementioned
7     [her] father [himself], Aurelius Deius, from the same neighborhood.
8     [the land] belonging to me near the village of Kerkes[ouch][a of the Herakleide] division, toward the village [of Phanesi,] fallow land in residential [order]
9     [an aroura] one and a half and a quarter, common from arouras [three]
10    [being] also from arouras seven apographed by me I submit, granting [to Aurelius] Demetrius, [son of] Ptolemaios, [son of] Didym[us, from]
11    [the neighborhood] of Bithynians, of Isios, and of Good Dae[mon]
12    [of no father, of] mother Eudaimonis, freedwoman of Ant[onius]
13    [and] as he registers with the two in common, from equal [granting] in silver drachmas six hundred[...]
14    [Aurelia Casiane, also] called Thermoúthári[on, with]
15    [the] guar[dian] of my father, Aurelius D[eius]
16    [I have] present[ed], granting [the land] near [the village]
17    [of Kerkes]ouch toward the village of Phanesi, fallow
18    [aroura] one and a half and a quarter, [granting]
19    [of silver] drachmas six hundred as is set [forth].
20    [Aurelius] Deius I wrote on behalf of the daughter [of me]
21    [who is] illiterate, [not] knowing letters.
