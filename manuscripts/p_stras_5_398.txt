[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the consulship] of Flavius Basilius the most glorious, in the
2     [twelfth] year, Phaophi the seventh, of the second indiction.
3     [To Aurelius …] son of Kollouthos, from the (city) of the Hermoupolites,
4     [from Aurelius Herm]…s son of Theodosios, his mother being Tsouris, a farmer,
5     [from the same] city. I, the aforewritten
6     [Herm… son of Theodosios,] willingly and of my own choice, acknowledge that I have leased
7     [from you, for as long a] time as you hold the mortgage
8     [which was made to] you by me, reckoning from the crops
9     [of the, God willing, coming] third indiction and that (indiction) itself,
10    [the aroura mortgaged to] you by him, one and three-quarters (of an aroura),
11    … with all their right, situated …
12    [in a village called] Thrake of the Hermoupolite nome …
13    [for the sowing and] laying-down of whatever crops I may choose, [at a rent] …
14    … of the same one [and three-quarter aroura] …
15    … pure …
16    … to you in each …
17    … fourteen (artabas), by the Athenian measure,
18    … of the year, without delay, until [the redemption of this my] mortgage. The lease being valid [and]
19    [secure, and being questioned] I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Herm…
20    [son of Theodosios, the] aforewritten, have leased
21    [as aforesaid. I, Aurelius …]yrinos son of Isakios, from Hermoupolis, having been requested, wrote
22    [on his behalf,] he not knowing letters. I, Aurelius Theodoros … r…ros,
23    [from Hermoupolis,] bear witness to the lease, having heard (it) from the one making it.
24    [I, Aurelius Theodo]sios son of Petros, from Hermoupolis, bear witness to the lease, having heard (it)
25    [from the one making it.] I, Flavius …retas son of Victor, of the soldiers, from Hermoupolis, bear witness to the lease,
26    [having heard (it) from the one making it.]
27    [Through] me, Menas, notary; Phaophi 7, indiction 2, it was written.
