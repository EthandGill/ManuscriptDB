1     Artemidoros to Zeno[n] greetings. If you are well, [it would be] good; and I am well too. [He] wrote:
2     Kleitorios [said] that Petōs the swineherd has withdrawn, having left behind 7 pigs and piglets …
3     and he had for us … pigs [in] full obligation, and [he] owed [piglets] to the ta[x] …
4     You would do well therefore, being diligent so that the man [might be] sought, where he withdrew,
5     so that so many pigs might not be lost to us, and [also] the sureties whoever have guaranteed for him,
6     so that either having been captured [they might be] led away or released on surety until
7     we might obtain justice.
8     And we have also received the young pig that you sent to us.
9     Be well. Year 36, Loion … 5.
10    [Year] 36, Thoth 15.
11    Artemidoros.
12    To Zenon.
