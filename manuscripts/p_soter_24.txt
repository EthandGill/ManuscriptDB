[TRANSLATION]
1     Copy of a bank-payment order from the bank near the
2     Hermaion, that of Theon. In the tenth year of Imperator
3     Caesar Nerva Trajan Augustus Germanicus
4     Dacicus, in the month Sebastos, the 21st. Thaisas daughter of Chares,
5     with as guardian her son Lykos son of Soterichos,
6     to Isidora daughter of Herakleides, with as guardian her son Hel[iodo]r[os]
7     son of Maron: (acknowledges) that she (Isidora) has received from Thais[a]s
8     the (sums) agreed upon that she should receive, in return for what
9     was owed to her together with (by) her late and
10    deceased husband, who was the father of Lykos,
11    Soterichos, according to written securities, which also
12    Isidora has returned to her for annulment [an]d
13    cancellation — amounting in all to two hundred drachmas
14    of silver and one hundred and twenty
15    nine artabas of wheat, after which Isidora of her own free will granted as a favour
16    to her (Thaisas) the remaining agreed-upon
17    three hundred drachmas of silver, total 300 drachmas; and Isidora makes
18    no claim at all against Thaisas nor against Soterichos nor
19    against those on their side concerning anything whatsoever, written or unwritten, up to the present day.
20    I, Isidora daughter of Herakleides, with as guardian my [s]on
21    Heliodoros son of Maron, have received from
22    Thaisas the (sums) agreed that I should receive, in return for what was owed to me
23    together with (by) her late and deceased husband Soterichos, according to written
24    securities which I also have returned to her for
25    cancellation — amounting in all to two hundred drachmas of silver
26    and one hu[ndred]
27    and twenty nine artabas of wheat, after which I of my own free will granted as a favour the remaining agreed-upon
28    three hundred drachmas of silver, total 300 drachmas,
29    and I make no claim against her nor against her children
30    concerning anything whatsoever up to the
31    present day, as stated above.
32    I, Heliodoros, wrote also on behalf of my mother,
33    as she does not know letters.
