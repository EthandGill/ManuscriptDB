1     Account from Aischines
2     to Zenon of the barley of 10 artabas. Of [two] days
3     1½-artaba portions [and] four measures, and lunch on the third
4     half six measures,
5     and to the mule 3½-artabas, and ½-artaba,
6     when the pale horse remained
7     here, went away having [it] Aristomachos; and the remaining [amount] and from the third I spend for the two horses
8     dinner.
9     And of the provisions I took for the horse-handlers 6 drachmas, to Aristomachos 2 drachmas
10    to Haryotes 2 drachmas and Dionysius
11    1 drachma [and] 3 obols; the remainder becomes 4-obol
12    of this Dorian
13    the one tending the colt
