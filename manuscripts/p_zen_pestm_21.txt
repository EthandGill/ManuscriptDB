r.1    μαρτυρεῖ Ἀντιπάτρωι Εὐφρόνιος Ἀπολλωνίδου Ἀμμωνιεὺς ὡς ἐτῶν λε εὐμεγέθης εὔρωστος κλαστὸς μελίχρους τετανὸς οὐλὴ ὀφρύων δεξιᾶι.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Euphronios son of Apollonides of Ammoneia testifies for Antipatros as [being] thirty-five years old, well-built, robust, dark, honey-complexioned, scar-marked, [with a] scar on the right eyebrow.
2     I dwell in Philadelphia of the Arsinoite.
3     In the second year, in the month of Panem, [I] being and Nikon and certain others in the barbershop of Eudoxus, [having] arrived
4     Antipatros and Simon, whom I testify, into this barbershop requested Nikon to hand over to them their son Theodosius. Nikon, the one being tried against Antipatros, did not say that he had received the boy from them nor [that he had it]
5     by any discovery.
6     Of Euphronios.
7     Year 2, Gorpiaios 26. [It] is brought
8     for Antipatros against [his] opponent
9     Nikon.
