[TRANSLATION]
1   Seventeenth year of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus
2   Antoninus S[eb]astos Pius, Phaophi 25. Paid to Theogi(ton) and partners,
3   collec(tors) of money(-taxes) of Soknop(aiou Nesos): Stotoe(tis) son of Apynch(is) son of Stot(oetis),
4   (mother) Tekiasis, syntaximon-tax of the same (year),
5   12 silver drachmas, total 12 dr.; on Hadrianos 29, another 4 silver dr., total 4 dr.; on Tybi 26, another 4 silver dr., total 4 dr.;
6   Phamenoth 29, another 4 silver dr., total 4 dr.; Pharmo(uthi) 30, another 4 silver dr., total 4 dr.; Pacho(n) 29,
7   guard-pay, 2 silver dr. 5 ob., total 2 dr. 5 ob.; mag(dola-tax) 5½ ob.; Pauni 26, another 8 silver dr., total 8 dr.; Epeiph 28, river-guard(-dues) 3 ob.,
8   prison-guard(-dues) 1½ ob., dou(ble-tax) 2½ ob., wild-beast(-tax) 1 ob. 1½ ob. ¼-ob.; for the 18th (year), Thoth 27, of the 17th (year) apportion(ment) on the destitute, 4 silver dr., total 4 dr.,
9   and 18th (year), Phaoph(i) 15, through Harp(…) of the additional-levy of the seventeenth (year), 12 dr., total 12 dr.; Hathyr 27, additional-levy of the 17th (year), 12 dr., total [1]2 dr.,
10   on Hadrianos 27, guard-tax of the destitute of the 17th (year), 3 dr. 5 ob., total 3 dr. 5 ob.
11   17th (year), Mecheir 30, paid to Theogi(ton) for the past sixteenth (year), apportion(ment) on the destitute, 8 silver dr., total 8 dr.,
12   Pauni 26, for the past sixteenth (year), 4 silver dr., total 4 dr.; Mesore, Epagomenai 1, for the past
13   sixteenth (year), 8 silver dr., total 8 dr.
