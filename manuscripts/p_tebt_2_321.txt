[TRANSLATION]
1     [A] copy of a census [registration].
2     [To] Maximus also called Nearcus, strategos, and Heracleides, royal
3     [secretary] of Arsinoe, [of] Heracleides' [division], and [to] Sabinus and Antoninus
4     [secretaries] of the metropolis and to the neighborhood-chief and [census]-recorder
5     [of the] neighborhood of Linypheia
6     from Herata [daughter] of Lysimachos [son] of Herodion, mother of Tamystha
7     [being] registered in the neighborhood of Apollonius, Hieracius, with [the] authority of [the] relative
8     Heracleides [son] of Patron. I possess in the neighborhood of Moiris
9     two-thirds of a house and courtyard and land in which I register the underwritten inhabitants for the past 9th year of Antoninus Caesar our lord according to [the] house [census]
10    [census] [in the] neighborhood of Linypheia in which [also in the 16th year of the god Hadrian census I was registered.]
