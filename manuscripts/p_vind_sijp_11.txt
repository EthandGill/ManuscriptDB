[TRANSLATION]
1     [After the ]consulship of Fla[v]iu[s] Sporakios the most magnificent
2     [an]d most val[ian]t, an[d] of the one to be [de]clared from Italy, Mecheir 23, of the 6th indiction.
3     To Flavius Kallinikos son of Helladios, honorable councillor of Hermou polis
4     the most illustrious, from the Aurelii: Apollon son of Mimos, whose mother is
5     Tasalies, and Isak son of Hatres, whose mother is Thermouthia, and
6     Bictor son of Pebes, whose mother is Thatres, and Ioannes son of Hatres,
7     whose mother is Thermouthia, and Bictor son of Kyros, whose mother is Keleuchis, all
8     of them from the village of Monoï, residing on the
9     estate at the place called Arauous, to the east
10    of the canal, near the same hamlet, near the collectorate
11    of the same village of Monoï of the same Hermopolite nome, greeting.
12    We acknowledge, willingly and of our own accord,
13    that we have leased from your prudence, for whatever time you wish, the estate belonging to you, the place called Arauous
14    to the east of the canal, on condition that we furnish
15    [by way] of rent, per year, six thousand talents of silver, as each one's own share, that is 6,000 talents,
16    so that (they) are together, of talents,
17    three [myri]ads — total, myriads 3 — and we are ready
18    [to s]erve your virtue for your necessary needs.
19    The lease being valid and secure, and being formally questioned we acknowledged (it). We, the Aurelii Apollon and Isak
20    and Bictor and Ioannes and the other Bictor, the aforesaid,
21    have leased as stated above. I, Flavius Dionysios son of Eulogios
22    from … eios, having been requested, wrote on behalf of them, they being present
23    and telling me that they do not know letters.
24    And through me, Dionysios, it was written …
25    [L]ease of the estate of Ar[a]uous, of the 6th indiction.
