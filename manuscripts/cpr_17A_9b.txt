[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters Constantine Augustus
2     for the 6th time and Constantine the most illustrious Caesar for the 1st time.
3     To Sostratos Aelianus, strategos and also exactor of the Hermopolite nome,
4     from Aurelius Adelphios son of Adelphios, gymnasiarch, councillor
5     of Hermopolis the most illustrious. I possess imperial-estate land around Tarouthis, which has come to me by succession from my father, which I have cultivated from long ago and now too cultivated, having made very many expenditures so that
6     I might be able readily to discharge the pious
7     contributions. So when the season called for the
8     gathering-in of the crops, I went there for
9     the collection of these, but Sois and Ibois and many others, their associates, from the same Tarouthis,
10    using village-arrogance, came upon and hindered the crops, and in order that no confusion
11    arise concerning the same (crops), I submit to your diligence
12    these documents, requesting that the violence be checked and that I be allowed to gather in the crops which I myself
13    planted on my own land. But if they think they have some just
14    claim to this land, let them appear
15    before the prefectorial court.
16    In the aforesaid consulship, Pharmouthi … 2.
17    B…nios
18    Sois son of Hasies
19    Peues son of Pabytis
20    Bois son of Artemidoros
21    Pollon son of Keraponous
22    Dioskoros son of Pabytis
23    Pachas son of Phibis
24    Hasies son of Sois
