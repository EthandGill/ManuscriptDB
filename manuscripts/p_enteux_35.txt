[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greetings, Diocles. [I am wronged by Sew]s. For having also previously entered
2     a petition to Diophanes the strategos, through which I revealed [that] Sews owing me according to an Egyptian contract wine measures 35, though asked repeatedly would not repay to me, and Diophanes having written to Polycrates to examine, Sews appearing in the 25th year, Epeiph 27, made an agreement to repay to me
3     wine measures 30, from the aforementioned day in 30 days. I beseech you therefore, king, to order
4     Diophanes the strategos to write to Polycrates the overseer, if the [things] through the petition [are] true,
5     to exact from him the 30 measures of wine or the [existing] price in the market. For when this [happens],
6     [I shall be] through you, king, having obtained justice. Prosper.
7     To Polycrates. If … to him from the … [do] what is just.
8     [Year 25, Loios] 26, Ch[oach] [1]3.
9     Year 25, Loios 26, Ch[o]iach 13.
10    Dio[c]les against Sews
11    Concerning a debt of wine.
