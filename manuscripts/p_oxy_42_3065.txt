[TRANSLATION]
1     Areios to his parents Agrippina and Kornelios,
2     many greetings.
3     Before all things I pray to God concerning your safety
4     and that of my brother Stephanos. I have already written you many letters, making clear that I am about to sail and to be saved from the city; but perhaps … concerning the things
5     that have happened here. Such things have happened as never from
6     the beginning of time have happened. Now it is cannibalism and not war … of the city found out now
7     … … to(?) Epeiph. She will make clear
8     to you, Auxanousa, the day on which I shall sail up and the name of the one through whom, if she sends to you, the delicacies which I left behind. They are:
9     two hundred nuts and two hundred dried figs and two writing-styluses.
10    You will receive these together with a letter … therefore, my lady
11    mother … rejoice that I am outside the
12    city; for I wished to come to you, but the
13    … of the city did not permit me. But if we live … embrace my brother
14    Stephanos and … your … Embrace by name
15    all those who love you, many greetings.
16    I pray that you keep well for many years,
17    … and in concord.
18    … Epeiph 5
19    Deliver … to the perfume-seller so that he may deliver it to Agrippina
20    from Areios her son.
21    Deliver … to … the perfume-seller so that he may deliver it
22    to Agrippina the mother, from Areios.
