[TRANSLATION]
1     [of] Maria, Barsimis of the Salamati number of the armored, through me the
2     father and holding him under my hand according to the Romans' law[s],
3     Barsimios of Bassus decadarch of the same cohort, I have sold to Aurelius
4     Apollonius optio the slave-girl Nike surnamed
5     Metathene, by race Arab, [for] simple payment and being free of sacred
6     disease [and] [infection], for the price of silver denarii two thousand two hundred [?]
7     fifty Ptolemaic, and I have received the [p]rice [from him]
8     and I have provided the securities, and being asked I acknowledged [that which is]
9     aforesaid. Rufus [?] optio of the number of the Salamati foreign [?]
10    I wrote on his behalf letters [he] not knowing, his hand [of him]
11    being held, and I guarantee the slave-girl Nike surnamed
12    Saprikin, and [being] asked I acknowledged as aforesaid.
13    Barsimios Bassus decadarch I have sold and I have received the price and I acknowledged as aforesaid.
