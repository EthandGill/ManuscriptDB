[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of the most divine] and most pious our master Flavius Justinian
2     [the eternal Augustus] and Imperator, in the twenty-seventh year,
3     [after the consulship of B]asilius the most illustrious, Thoth 14, of the second indiction, in Arsinoe.
4     [To Flavii Apion the gen]eral and Strategios the [il]lustrious, the most glorious sons of
5     [Theodoros of revered mem]ory, landowners at the city of the Arsinoites, Aureli[us Aioulios son of …,] his mother being Tareu, of long-shaped (face), small-eyed, from your
6     [hamlet (epoikion) of] …eosphyllou near the suburbs of this city, [greeting.] I ac[knowledge that of my own free will I have lea]sed from your gloriousness, of the right of
7     [the property belonging to it,] in the field of your hamlet at the place Geerog, a small orchard
8     … one small [fruit-bearing (plot)] of however many arouras they may be, of whatever arouras they may be, together with
9     … of them, with all their right, for as long a time as
10    [your glo]riousness [may wish,] from the produce of the fortunate coming third indiction
11    [I, the] lessee Aioulios, [paying] on account of a fixed rent (apotaktos) for them
12    [yearly] one solidus of gold less seven carats, one gold solidus less 7 carats by the standard weight,
13    … which rent I shall pay each year to your gloriousness
14    … and to Amaeios your assistant (symmachos), on account of his fee, one solidus.
15    … [The lease] is valid, (secured) on all my property, and on being formally questioned I acknowledged (it).
16    [Aurelius Aioulios …] the aforesaid, have leased the aforewritten (property) from Mamon and Kyn… [a re]nt yearly as is set forth above. I, Aurelius Neilos son of Paulos, wrote on his behalf, he being prese[nt and] illiterate.
17    Through me, Phoibammon. ✝ ✝ ✝ (?) it was transacted.
18    Through me, Phoibammon.
