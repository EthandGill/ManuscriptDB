[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy and [Queen] Cleopatra
2     his sister, the brother-loving gods, greetings.
3     Ptolemy, [son] of Glaucus, Macedonian, who is in
4     confinement in the great [sanctuary] near Memp[his]
5     of Sarapis, [the sanctuary] of Astarte, from the 10th year.
6     When you ascended to the Serapeum in the 24th year
7     on Thoth 2nd I petitioned you through the window because
8     I was not able to come down before [you], both on behalf of
9     Apollonius my younger brother, so that
10    he might be enrolled in the military and on behalf of
11    the Asta[rteion], in which I am in confinement from the
12    aforementioned time, requesting that
13    no one of the priests and shrine-bearers
14    [nor] anyone [forcibly] seize [me] as collateral
15    [nor] treat me with outrage because those in the
16    [sanctuary] are wicked and are besieging me on account of
17    [my] being Greek, so that I might have help and
18    a patron. But now the aforementioned [man]
19    Apollonius has been posted to the station in Memp[his]
20    first, but by the attendants is being dragged into public services
21    and is not permitted to be with me, for which reason,
22    King, I petitioned you. For those besieging
23    me, whenever they learn that he has been assigned
24    to public service, at that point forcibly
25    besiege [me]. For I was treated with outrage in the 25th year
26    by more than one, so that even through the window
27    I was stoned, for which reason, when ascended
28    Poseidonius the chief-of-the-bodyguard
29    and general and when I petitioned him,
30    on the 10th of Pharmouthi of that same year,
31    he summoned them and rebuked them.
32    Therefore I beseech, Sun-king, do not ov[erlook] me in confinement
33    [being], but if it seems good,
34    pr[o]order [him] to write
35    to Poseidonius the
36    chief-of-the-bodyguard
37    and general
38    to make him exempt from service,
39    do so, so that he might be with me.
40    And for you both Isis and
41    Sarapis, the greatest
42    of the gods, may grant
43    [to you] all
44    the land which the Sun
45    oversees and to your children
46    always.
47    Farewell.
