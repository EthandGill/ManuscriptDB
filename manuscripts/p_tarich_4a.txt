[TRANSLATION]
1     To the chrematistae of whom
2     Melas is the introducer, from
3     Amenneus and Onnophris,
4     son of Teos, embalmers
5     from Tanis. We submitted
6     to you a petition on Thoth 15
7     against Abykis, son of Horos,
8     and Kelechonsis and
9     Pasis, son of Psenemous,
10    embalmers from Philadelphia, against whom we have already been bringing charges
11    for five months concerning the royal (dues) which
12    they hold, alleging (a claim); and how
13    they defrauded Drimylos
14    so that it was written that we be led off (to prison). He wrote to Kleon
15    the chief of police
16    of Tanis, and we were handed over to the guards
17    for arrest, we having
18    the letter from Protarchos
19    and the (letter) of
20    Bakchon the dioiketes,
21    they wishing to take away from us
22    the letters, and concerning
23    all that had happened … not abiding by the ordinances of the king,
24    because they have
25    gold and silver.
26    We request that a letter be written by us
27    to whom it is fitting, to send off
28    to you Kleon the
29    chief of police to whom we were handed over, and the guards
30    to whom he handed us over, whose
31    names are Eserches,
32    Euporos, Onnophris,
33    (and to send) Herakleides the desert-guard
34    who is a witness, so that they may be questioned about these matters,
35    in order that we may lay information and
36    they may obtain what they must obtain,
37    they having disregarded the
38    ordinances of the king,
39    detaining us though a trial is available to us
40    before the chrematistae.
41    Farewell.
