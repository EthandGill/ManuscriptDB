[TRANSLATION]
1     Kalokaipos to Euphrosyne
2     [his] sister, greetings.
3     Having been asked, sister, if I
4     wish to do you any favor, take care [to find out]
5     what Ales, my wife, is doing. Even if
6     she has not written to you, you ought to write to me of your own accord as to your brother. Not because I care about her, but if anything I have is with her.
7     And from her not writing to me, I am getting other ideas about her. Greet Thaison, the
8     sister, and tell her, if [anyone]
9     has need here, [write] to me.
10    [I pray that] you fare well.
11    Deliver to Euphrosyne, [my] sister
12    [Dioscor]atus.
