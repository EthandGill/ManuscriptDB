[TRANSLATION]
1     Dionysius to Theon, the most dear [friend],
2     many greetings and be well continuously. As you reminded me about buying relish, I gave my friend [money] to buy pigs [for] sixty drachmas. [He] gave [it]
3     to [the one from] you. And he himself also bought
4     pigs [for] thirty drachmas. And a basket to him I have given. See [that]
5     he does not deceive you, as [has been] commonly
6     bought and [it has been] deceived [in the accounting]. He himself buying took only
7     thirty pigs, but you [took] sixty. And send me beans instead of lentils, artabas
8     five. The other matters care for yourself so that you be well. Greet all
9     your [people], Heracles [son] of Petalus.
10    Farewell. [In the] 30th year of Caesar, Mesore 30.
11    and the basket send to me through
12    Androu[s].
13    Give [this] to Theonas.
