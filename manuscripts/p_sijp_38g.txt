1     It has been measured into the treasury of the metr[opolis] [of the product]
2     of the 18th year of Hadrian Caesar the lord
3     Epeiph 19 for the 16th year of the Agorae in the name
4     of Tikotes, Pikotes, mother Touorios
5     wheat 1 sixth, the total is wheat 1 sixth. Gaius I have signed.
