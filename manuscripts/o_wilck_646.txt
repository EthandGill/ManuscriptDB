1     Thoteus, collector of the assessment for the customs-arrears of Charax,
2     to Psenamounis (son of) Pamonthes (son of) Petenobtous.
3     I have received for the assessment of year 12, three drachmas, four
4     obols, total drachmas 3, 4 obols. Year 16 of Antoninus
5     Caesar the lord, Mecheir 14.
