[TRANSLATION]
1     5
2     ep …
3     To Ptolemaios the oikonomos,
4     from Seneses daughter of Menelaos, of the women dwelling in Oxyrhyncha of the Pole[m]on division.
5     While I was living with Didymos
6     son of Peteimouthes, one of those from the same
7     village, according to an Egyptian marriage-contract, a maintenance-(bride) for silver,
8     of gold … 1 according to the
9     laws of the country, and against these
10    also my maintenance being pledged (upon) all his belongings,
11    among which also a house
12    in the aforewritten village,
13    the accused, wishing to
14    defraud me, so long as, approaching one and every one
15    of those from the same village,
16    he wished to alienate it (the house),
17    but these not consenting
18    on account of my not joining in the authorization, after this he contrived
19    to give (it) as a surety
20    on behalf of Herakleides the tax-farmer
21    to the royal (treasury), and by this
22    he thinks to shut me out of my rights.
23    Wherefore I ask you, being a woman in need
24    and helpless, not to overlook me,
25    being defrauded of the (property) pledged
26    for the dowry through the
27    rascality of the accused, but, if
28    it seem good, to order that it be written to Pt[ole]maios the overseer not to a[c]cept the house of Didymos the [de]clared (man) as a sure[ty.]
29    And this having been [d]on[e, I shall obtain the]
30    help from you.
31    [Farewell.]
32    Year 5, Pauni 14.
