[TRANSLATION]
1     In (the times) after the consulship of Flavius Eusebius the most illustrious for the 2nd time, Choiak 2, of the 4th indiction, in Oxyrhynchus.
2     To Flavius Apphous the most illustrious tribune, son of Eulogius of most excellent memory,
3     from the splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites, Aurelius Theon son of John,
4     his mother being Heracleia, pastry-cook, from the same city, greeting. I acknowledge, through this
5     written security of mine, that I have received from you in loan by hand
6     from your house, for my own a[nd] necessary need, of gold plain
7     imperial approved solidi, two in number, that is, of gold 2 solidi, on condition that I provide and
8     give to your nobility for the interest of these, up to the next stated
9     term, the lawful one-percent interest for them without delay,
10    which interest and the principal itself I shall of necessity repay to your excellence,
11    one [g]old piece in the month of Tybi of the current [yea]r 172/141, of the
12    [pre]s[ent] f[ou]rth indiction with the inte[res]t, and the other [in t]he
13    H[a]thyr m[onth of the c]omin[g] year [173]/142, of the fifth indiction, (which is) [wi]th God, with the inter[es]t, without delay and without any dispute and lawsuit
14    and trial, [and] it shall not be [perm]itted, when each term falls due,
15    to po[stpo]ne concerning the repayment, nor to demand another respite,
16    nor to claim to have paid out of the same debt without written
17    receipts and a joint quittance, or before the recovery of this my
18    bond, but the recovery shall be yours both from me and from
19    all my property. Valid is the bond,
20    written singly, and being formally questioned I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Theon son of John, pastry-cook, the aforewritten, have received the t[wo] gold solidi of principal and I shall repay at the terms with the interest, and everything written agrees with me
21    as is aforesaid, and being formally questioned I acknowled[g]ed and released. Apphouas, reader,
22    son of Philoxenos, having been requested, wrote on his behalf, he not kn[ow]ing letters.
23    Through me Thot it was written, Choiak 12, of the 4th indiction …
