[TRANSLATION]
1     Five grades: 11 drachmas and four-obols; for prison-keeping, one obol and 2 chalkoi; the total is 11 drachmas and five-obols [and 2 chalkoi].
2     Of the brick-making division, 15th year, Pammenus of Paradise, grade 1, Hermaios, grade 1, Street of Thoe[ris] …
3     Bank, 15th year, Phaophi, two-obols, …
4     Of the pig of Sarapates, slave of Apollonius Claudius Deme[trius registe]red on the Street of Thoeris, 1 drachma, four-obols, half-obol; of those additionally registered, one obol, half-obol.
