[TRANSLATION]
1     [It] has been measured into the public treasury of [the] administration of Sebaemphis on [the] 10th Mesore, product
2     of the present 4th year of Marcus
3     Aurelius Antoninus Caesar
4     of the Lord, Hierax, also [called] Harpocration
5     of barley, artabas six, making of barley artabas 6, through me
6     Aurelius Harpocration, assistant.
