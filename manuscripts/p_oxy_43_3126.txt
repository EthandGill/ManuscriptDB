[TRANSLATION]
1     from Aureli[us Kastor son of Paramm]on from the Amm[oniac]
2     Oasis, residing [in the] splendid and most splendid city of the Oxyrhynchites.
3     I bought from Thonios son of Paantas from the same city of the Oxyrhynchites the property belonging to him in the same city,
4     in the Hermaion quarter, a fourth part of a house and of all
5     that appertains to it; but of the
6     co-owners of the house Taorion and Koprias controlled this part. Unable to bear it, I referred the matter to the prefectural
7     authority, requesting that a division of the homestead be made. These (officials), having been instructed, the orders and the courts
8     before your equity having combined toward me, this
9     part has been confirmed to me upon the memoranda transacted, in accordance with the right of the sale made to me.
10    And since it is necessary, according to what has been ordered by the
11    higher authority, to make the division of the homestead too, so that each, having his own (share), may enjoy it,
12    on account of this I have now also hastened, appending the things which I referred
13    together with what was written back to me, requesting to submit the documents,
14    asking that the foremen of the builders be instructed so that they may know
15    and be compelled to come to the division of the same house
16    according to what has been ordered concerning this.
17    In the consulship of Flavius Ianuarinus and Vettius Iustus, the most splendid, Mesore 26.
18    I, Aurelius Kastor, have submitted (this). I, Aurelius …-es
19    son of Didymos, [wrote] on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
