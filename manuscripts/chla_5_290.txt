# HGV-EN: To Mevius Honoratianus, prefect [of Egypt, from Aurelia Diodora. I ask you, my lord, to appoint to me] as guardian, in accordance with the [Julian and Titian] law [and by decree of the senate, Aurelius Theon. Dated] on the XII of the Kalends of December, in the consulship of our lord Verus [Maximinus Augustus and Pupienus Africanus].
