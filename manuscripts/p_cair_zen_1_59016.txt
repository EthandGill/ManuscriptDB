[TRANSLATION]
1     Demetrios to Zenon, greetings. In Tyre I bought some items
2     and spent my traveling allowance. You would do well therefore to give to Nikadas
3     who returned the letters to you, 150 drachmas. But Charmos has already gotten ahead of me
4     by departing first, since I would have sent him with him. But see to it that you also send Nikadas to Beirut in safety.
5     Write to me also where I should send the money.
6     Farewell. Year 27, Apellaios 13.
7     From Demetrios
8     in Cyprus, secretary, silver 150 drachmas.
9     To Zenon.
