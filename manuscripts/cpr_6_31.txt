[TRANSLATION]
1     Hyperech[i]us to Apollonius,
2     to the paralemptae, greetings.
3     [Concerning] the threshing of the fodder that was sown by Anicetus—
4     if the three wagons have ceased, let two of them rest from the vetch that is near Apenatae for threshing, and the
5     other one the fodder that was assigned for the food of the cattle
6     with you from the purchased fodder from those from [T]emseus the Moors let it be assigned so that he might carry this into the place of [threshing-floor] that is from the south of the farmstead of Sinarchebae and send cattle there from the farmstead
7     with you and [you all thresh] this. The matters of Peslas
8     …
9     it will be able to be carried into the fodder storage with you. But already do this and Pinoutius the
10    overseer and Silvanus the cowherd with yourself bring them up to the city so that I might learn if each of these matters
11    has happened. I wish you
12    well.
13    To Apollonius in Peslas.
