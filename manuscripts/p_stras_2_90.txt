[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 16, Hathyr 2.
2     Sale. Thotortaios
3     son of Pates, of
4     a built house,
5     in which is a vaulted room, and
6     half of the courtyard, and
7     door-fittings, bought
8     Petearsemtheus
9     son of Panobchounis
10    for [drachmas of copper] 2(000).
11    In the reign of Ptolemy called Alexander and of Berenike his sister, gods
12    Philometores, year 16, in the (priesthood of) the priests and priestesses and canephore who are in office, in the month
13    Hathyr 2, in Pathyris, before Hermias, deputy of Paniskos, agoranomos.
14    Thotortaios son of Pates, Persian of the epigone, aged about 55, of medium height, honey-colored, straight-haired, with receding hair,
15    long-faced, straight-nosed, with a scar on the chin, sold from the built house belonging to him and to his brother, in which is a vaulted room, and the adjoining courtyard together with the door-fittings, the (property) being in the middle division
16    of Pathyris, the share falling to him, one half, of solid cubits two out of four cubits, in which
17    Psenmenches his brother has a share, the other half; the neighbors of the whole house: on the south the house of Panobchounis
18    son of Totoes; on the north the house of the sons of Kanopos; on the east a street; on the west the house of Panobchounis son of Totoes and
19    Pates his brother, or whoever the neighbors may be on all sides. There bought Petearsemtheus son of Panobchounis, aged about 40,
20    for two thousand drachmas of copper. Guarantor of everything relating to this sale is Thotortaios the seller, whom Petearsemtheus the buyer accepted.
21    I, Hermias, deputy of Paniskos, have transacted (this).
