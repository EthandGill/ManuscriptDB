1     Of the 11th pagus of Selilais, through Tiberios and Hatres and
2     Silvanos and Patos and the associate grain-collectors,
3     to Aurelia Charite daughter of Amazonios, greeting. We have received
4     from you, for the account of the cash-conversion of patrimonial (land), of the produce of the 16th, that is 2nd indiction, and
5     for the produce of the 15th indiction, that is 1st indiction,
6     of the cash-conversion of the assessment, of silver, talents
7     seventeen, drachmas four thousand
8     makes 17 talents, 4000 drachmas. Pharmouthi 19. We the aforesaid grain-collectors
9     have received as stated above. I, Aurelius Serenos, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
10    Flavius Besodoros, to Charite daughter of Amazonios, greeting.
11    I have been paid in full by you the portion of green hay falling to you
12    for the fifth indiction, in full. The receipt
13    is valid. Mecheir 25. I, Flavius Besodoros, have signed.
14    Of the 8th pagus. From Charite daughter of Amazonios, of chaff of the assessment
15    of the 15th indiction, 1280 pounds, for patrimonial (land)
16    110 pounds, makes together one thousand three hundred
17    ninety pounds. Epeiph 15. You delivered through Pinoution son of Pisis 390 pounds; for the grain-collector on the spot, 1000 pounds.
18    I, Sarapammon, have signed.
19    Aurelius Polis son of Silvanos, registrar of the village
20    of Sinape, to Charite daughter of Amazonios, greeting.
21    I have received from you and delivered to Mare… son of Silvanos the overseer, of barley for the
22    … [indic]tion, seven artabas of barley, and
23    … of him the authentic receipt
24    of the same seven artabas, and he placed (his subscription) of the
25    handwriting. Epeiph 18. I, Polis the registrar, have signed.
26    Grain-collectors of the 14th and 15th indiction, through Asklas
27    and Laeonios and associates. We have received from
28    you, for chaff of the same indiction, forty-one
29    pounds, and for freight of grain according to
30    the portion falling to you of the 15th indiction, and
31    of hemp according to the portion falling to you. Epeiph 30. Polis the reg[istrar, have signed].
32    Requisitioners of the annona of the produce of the third [indiction]
33    through Ammonios son of Pane…iterios and Tyrann[os] …
