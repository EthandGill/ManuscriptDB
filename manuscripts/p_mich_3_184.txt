[TRANSLATION]
1     To Ha[ryotes, son of Alexas],
2     from Horos, son of Horos, son of Horos, one of those from the village
3     of Bakchias, a Persian of the epigone. I wish to lease from you for four years, for four sowings, from the present fifth year of Hadrian
4     Caesar the lord, the (lands) belonging to you around the
5     aforewritten village of Bakchias, of the holding,
6     four and a half arouras, or however many they may be, in a single seal-plot, at a total rent each year, together with the seed which I shall receive
7     from you of four and a half artabas of wheat, of twenty artabas of wheat by the six-choinix
8     receiving-measure of Alexas your brother, free of deduction
9     and of risk. And I will perform each year all the farm-tasks, as many as are fitting, each from my own (means)
10    at the proper season, doing no damage; and the
11    yearly rents I will pay in the month of Pauni in the
12    village, new, pure, unadulterated, barley-free, and after the
13    term I will hand over the arouras clean from
14    rushes, reeds, dog's-tooth grass, all coarse sedge, and
15    the half part rested under hay, and the
16    remaining half from the stubble, the cost of transport
17    of the (harvest) speeding being borne by me, Horos,
18    if it seems good to lease on the [a]foresaid terms.
19    Haryotes, aged about 59, with a scar on the ankle-bone of the left foot.
20    In the fifth year of Imperator Caesar Trajan
21    Hadrian Augustus, Tybi 22.
