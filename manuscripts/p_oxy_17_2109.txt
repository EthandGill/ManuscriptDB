[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Dioscurides
2     also called Sabinus, ex-gymnasiarch, councillor and however
3     I am styled, prytanis in office
4     of the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
5     also administering the civic affairs.
6     Of the offer made
7     for the civic place of the Capitolium indicated through it, under the eastern colonnade, for the opening of
8     a tavern, the equivalent (notice) is publicly
9     posted that all may know,
10    and that those wishing to give better
11    offers may come forward,
12    the city's claim being preserved concerning all the various
13    rights it has. I have signed.
14    Year 1 of our lords Macrianus
15    and Quietus, Augusti, Tybi 30.
16    To Aurelius Dioscurides also called
17    Sabinus, ex-gymnasiarch,
18    prytanis in office of the city of the Oxyrhynchites, administering
19    also the civic affairs,
20    from Aurelius Horion, son of Kolluthos, his mother being Tereus,
21    from the city of Oxyrhynchus. Willingly I undertake to lease
22    in the Capitolium of the city, under the eastern
23    colonnade, a workshop for
24    the opening of a tavern, for one year from the new moon of
25    the next month Mecheir of the present year 1, at a rent of eight drachmas each month.
26    The undertaking being guaranteed to me,
27    I shall use the workshop with its entrance and exit for the period
28    without hindrance, and I shall pay the
29    rent each month on the thirtieth
30    without delay, and at the end of the
31    period I shall hand over the place
32    clean of dung and all filth, and whatever
33    doors and keys I receive, or I shall pay
34    the proper price of whatever I do not hand over,
35    the right of execution being
36    as is fitting. The undertaking is authoritative, concerning
37    which, having been formally questioned, I gave my assent.
38    But if I am not confirmed, I shall not be held bound by this promise.
39    Year 1 of the Emperors Caesars
40    Titus Fulvius Junius Macrianus
41    and Titus Fulvius Junius Quietus,
42    Pious, Fortunate, Augusti,
43    Tybi 28. I, Aurelius Horion son of Kolluthos, have submitted (this), undertaking to lease, and I shall pay
44    the rent as set forth above.
45    I, Aurelius Didymus, wrote on his behalf, he not knowing letters.
