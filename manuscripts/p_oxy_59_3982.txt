[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Sarapion also called Serenus, strategos [of the Oxyrhynchite nome,]
2     from the Aurelii Harpaesis son of Pathatres and Ps[enamounis]
3     son of Amatokos, both komarchs of the village of Phobo[ou]
4     for the present [y]ear. We have received an[d have been paid out by] Aurelius Aphthonios, banker of public
5     monies, from an order (epistalma) …
6     … which we requested to be ordered
7     to be [d]i[sbursed to us] for the price of (the things) which we furn[ished]
8     to the public treasury, of tow (stippion), from the account of purch[ase]
9     through Ariston the supervisor, for (delivery) to Alexan[dria, year]
10    20: of 6 centenaria, 5⅓ minas, 6 talents (and) 533 drachmas, of which the 6½ per cent (surcharge) makes [2,375 drachmas;]
11    the remaining 5 talents (and) 4,158 drachmas; of year 7 (and) year 5 likewise, of cen[tenaria 6,]
12    minas 5⅓, 6 talents (and) 533 drachmas, of which the 6½ per cent (surcharge) makes 2,375 drachmas, the re[maining 5 talents,]
13    4,158 drachmas; together 11 talents (and) 2,316 drachmas in full. [Valid]
14    is the receipt, and on being questioned we acknowledged (it).
15    In the consulship of Rufius Volusianus and Petronius
16    Annianus, the most illustrious, Pachon 10.
17    We, the Aurelii Harpaesis and Psanamounis, have been pai[d]
18    the silver in full as is aforesaid. I, Aurelius Agathos, wrote on their behalf as they do not know letters.
19    To Aurelius Sarapion also called Serenus, strategos of the Oxyrhynchite nome,
20    from the Aurelii Theon
