[TRANSLATION]
1   Has paid: Phenoupis son of Pachompaouonnophris,
2   for the trade-tax of the linen-weavers (and) pastophoroi, fourth year
3   of Vespasian the lord, Mesore
4   30: twelve silver drachmas — total 12.
5   I, Melanthis son of Hermodotos, wrote (this).
