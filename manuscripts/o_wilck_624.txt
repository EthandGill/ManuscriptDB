[TRANSLATION]
1     Heron and partners, collectors of the assessment
2     of the tax of Charax: Amenothes (son of) Harbechis
3     (son of) Amenothes. We have received for the assessment of year 8,
4     three drachmas, 3½ obols, total drachmas 3, 3½ obols.
5     Year 9 of Antoninus Caesar
6     the lord, Hathyr 14.
7     (2nd hand) I, Petemenophis, have signed.
