[TRANSLATION]
1     Pompeius Pto[l]emaios, having served as gymnasiarch, to Dorion, greetings.
2     I have a claim against you for the harvest-tax which you owe
3     of the past fourteenth
4     year, [in] which you farm arouras around Theadelphia
5     for account in wheat 130 artabas, which amount to artabas 130, me not being defrauded
6     for the remaining harvest-tax, accordingly
7     the report. Year 15 of Antoninus Caesar
8     of the lord Hadrian, 19
