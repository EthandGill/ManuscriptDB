[TRANSLATION]
1     Aurelius Akylas also called Sara[p]… [chief-]priest of the illustrious city of the Antinoites and as I am styled,
2     to Marcus Aurelius Loukammon … [Hadria]nios also called Olympios and to Gaius Julius …, [through Marcus]
3     Aurelius Serenos and as he is styled, [from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,]
4     both sureties of A[urelius Demetrius] …
5     wrestler, greeting. Since the man who was made surety by you, [Aurelius] Demetrius, contracted with [my] son [Aure]lius Nikantinoos
6     while competing in the [boys' wrestlers']
7     contest, to fall three times and to yield, receiving through you three thousand eight hundred drachmas of old silver coinage, free of risk, on condition that in the event
8     — which may it not be — that this man yields and is not deficient,
9     the crown be dedicated, that he shall not proceed against him
10    concerning these matters; and if Demetrius himself also transgresses any of the things written and agreed
11    with respect to my same son, he shall likewise pay to my same
12    son, of necessity, on account of contumely, of old
13    silver coinage three talents, without any delay or pretext, according to the law of suretyship, because
14    on these terms we made the agreements. The agreement is valid, written in duplicate, in two copies, of which I have one
15    and you one, and being asked the formal question I gave my consent. Year 14
16    of Imperator Caesar Publius Licinius Gallienus
17    Germanicus Maximus, Persicus Maximus, Pius, Felix,
18    Augustus, Mecheir 29.
