[TRANSLATION]
1     Apion to Trypho in Hibae
2     to the most beloved, greetings.
3     Half of the glass vessels
4     brought down to you with
5     cargo by divine providence
6     having received this, send back my letters, and
7     bread, concerning which I have also written to you before. I pray that you are well,
8     most beloved.
9     Year 8, Mecheir 4.
