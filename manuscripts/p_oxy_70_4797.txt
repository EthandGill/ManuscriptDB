[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the same all-praiseworthy men, the (right of) action and]
2     obligation, Aurelia Tarilla daughter of Andreas, her mother being
3     Nonna, originating from the farmstead of Tryne, an estate
4     of your Magnificence, of the Oxyrhynchite nome, a registered
5     tenant-farmer of it, greeting. A need having now arisen also for
6     the estate-machine (water-wheel) belonging to me called That of the Southern
7     Field, drawing water for arable land, of one axle,
8     having gone up to the city, I petitioned your Magnificence
9     through the persons proper to it, so as to order to be furnished
10    to me the same axle. And straightway, taking forethought
11    for the maintenance of her own affairs, this having been bought
12    by me in the fields, and its price reckoned to me
13    in my tax-receipts, a new, suitable, water-lifting
14    (device), satisfactory, I received for the completion of all the
15    machine-implements on this present day, which is
16    Phaophi the tenth of the present second indiction, of the water-supply
17    of the crops of the God-willing third assessment. And as proof of the receipt I have made this bond of hand, which, being valid,
18    written singly and, on being questioned, I gave my assent. I, Aurelia Tarilla,
19    the aforewritten, am satisfied as aforesaid. I, Anastasios, wrote on her behalf as she is illiterate.
20    [Bond of hand of Aurelia] [Tarill]a daughter of Andreas from the farmstead of Tryne, of one axle.
