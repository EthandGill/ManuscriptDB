[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of our masters Theodosus the 12th and
2     [Valen]tinianus the 2nd of the eternal Augusts
3     to Flavius Taurinus, soldier of Plousammon,
4     [from the numerus of Ma]uron of Lyconopolis,
5     from Aurelius P[a]chumi[o]s son of Onnophrius, mother Martyria, from the village
6     of Temenkyrkes, Poimenon of the Hermopolitan nome.
7     I wish willingly and of my own choice to rent
8     from [you for] a three-[year] period, reckoning from the crops
9     of the for[tu]nately entering eleventh indiction,
10    the two arourae of the praktoria, existing to you,
11    of the [same] village, in the field Pari, situated from the south,
12    of my land arourae, six of those exchanged,
13    … I exchanged ours.
