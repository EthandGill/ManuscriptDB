1     From Aelia[nus]
2     To Apollonius the receiver. Give to Isidorus the assistant so that to collectors and others, whose account he will give
3     Of wine measure-skins twelve. There come to be of wine measure-skins twelve from drachmas 255. Do not use …

5     9 indiction, Pharmuthi 15
