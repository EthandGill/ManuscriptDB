1     [Dionysios to Paniskos, greeting. A copy of the document concerning Herakleides the granary-keeper (sitologos) is appended below. Farewell. Year 18] …
2     [To Herakleides.]
3     A copy of the document of Athenaios the kinsman [and dioiketes is appended below. Having followed it, therefore, measure out, Paniskos also writing jointly,]
4     Paniskos the royal scribe, to the … [of the liturgists assigned]
5     to the dioiketes from the crews of the seagoing [fleet, the maintenance due to them for the (period) from … of the 18th year until … of the 19th year, for the eleven-month period,]
6     for the month, 424 artabas of wheat … [for the eleven-month period, the (totals) amounting to 4 myriads, 6,740 artabas of wheat] …
7     and make out vouchers and counter-vouchers for them as is fitting.
8     Athenaios to Dionysios, greeting. … [of the liturgists assigned to me from the seagoing]
9     fleet of the crews, of which the ship-by-ship (list) [is appended below,] … [there is due to them from … of the 18th year until … ]
10    of the 19th year, for the eleven-month period, for the month, on the expenditure-account, 424 artabas of wheat … [the (totals) amounting to 4 myriads, 6,700 artabas of wheat] …
11    of the undecked (ships): of the trierarch Charisandragathos [and Eunomos, Naukratites] …
12    of Sarapion and Menodotos, Kanopites … [of the crews of the double-oared (ships)] …

15    of Noumenios 150, of Athenagoras 128, of Aphrodisios and others 148, of Artemon 70 …

18    of Dionysios and Mozos 44, of Krateros and Nikolaos 40, of Protarchos and Philoxenos 3 …
