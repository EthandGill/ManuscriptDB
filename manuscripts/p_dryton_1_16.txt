[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 39, Choiach 12, in Pathyris, before Sarapion the agoranomos. There lent:
2     Apollonia, daughter of Ptolemaios, son of Hermokrates,
3     a Cyrenaean, with the guardian, her own
4     husband Dryton, son of Pamphilos, a Cretan
5     of those of the supplementary force, a hipparch
6     over men, and her successors, to Apollonios
7     son of Apollodotos also called Psennesis, son of Harsiesis,
8     a Persian of the descents, and to Herais, daughter of Ptolemaios also called Tisris, daughter of Paous, a Persian,
9     with the guardian, her own husband Apollonios the aforewritten, of wheat artabas
10    thirty-five, interest-free. This loan
11    let the borrowers repay
12    to Apollonia in the month Pachon [o]f the 39th year,
13    new, clean, unadulterated wheat, re[st]ored to the house to her at their [ow]n
14    expenses, by the measure with which they also received it,
15    by the 29-choinix measure. And if they do not repay within the
16    appointed time, let them pay
17    forthwith one-and-a-half times the price that will obtain
18    in the market. The borrowers are
19    sureties of one another for the payment of all
20    the things through the loan. And let the right of execution
21    belong to Apolloni[a] from both
22    of the borrowers and from each and every one
23    of them, and from whichever she chooses, and from
24    all their possessions, as if from a judgment.
25    Areios, the (agent) from Sarapion: I have transacted it.
26    Repayment
27    in the 39th year.
28    Year 39, Choiach 12: loan of Apollonia
29    toward Psennesis, of wheat 35 artabas
30    and Tisris his wife.
