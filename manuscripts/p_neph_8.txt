[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord, beloved father
2     and brother Nepheros, Paulos.
3     I have now received thanks too, because you deigned
4     to write to us. Know now that
5     Gennadios has sold the six bundles for
6     ninety-four myriads, with which he bought in exchange of oil five xestai for twelve
7     myriads, and of liquid pitch an anemourion (jar)
8     for ten myriads, and of iron one block
9     of three minas for twenty myriads, so that
10    the whole comes to ninety myriads,
11    and there remain over a further four myriads.
12    Therefore I considered it necessary to write to you first of all, so that you may remember us
13    in your prayers, and then to make known
14    to you these very things. And just as through your letter
15    you made known to us about Horion, know
16    that … in the
17    camp … to him to hand over to me the
18    silver money, and so far he has given nothing.
19    But if it seems good to you, write to her about this, since she promised to give, so that, having received from
20    your … letters she may be ashamed and may provide
21    … , concerning which through letters
22    we may make known. We greet
23    all our beloved brothers,
24    … first … Ammonios …
25    … our [Hor]ion and our [moth]er
26    [Tienor] and …
27    But if Papnouthis neglects the grain … , let him know that [he will be compelled]
28    to take an oath about this, lest he receive him in the assembly.
29    To my lord, beloved father [Nepheros],
30    [Paulos.]
