r.3    φροντιστὰς καὶ τοὺς ναύτας καὶ συμμάχους τοὺς ἐπὶ τῇ ἁλωνίᾳ καὶ τοὺς λοιποὺς κεφαλαιώτας

[TRANSLATION]
1     To the most honored Heracleidas, assistant of Great Sobthes, Flavianus the scholastic.
2     immediately and at that hour, receiving the letters, brought forth and the
3     overseers and the sailors and allies those on the threshing-floor and the remaining principals
4     as [I] said, at one time, since their need is necessarily.
