[TRANSLATION]
1     There leased out the Aurelii [Se]renos
2     also called Sarapion [and Api]on
3     both (sons) of Agathei[nos] from the city of Ox[y]r[h]ynchus, to the Aurel[ii] Antonios son of P[l]aton, mother Tap…allis, and Panechotes son of Panechotes, mother Tauris, daughter of the priest,
4     both from [the villa]ge of Merm[e]rtha, for a period of two years
5     from the present [14th year], the (lands) belonging to them in the area [of Mer]mertha
6     in two holdings, [five] ar[urae,]
7     so that the les[se]es
8     may sow and plant with whatever (crops) they
9     choose, except woad and pasturage, at a fixed yearly rent
10    of ten artabas of wheat
11    and ten artaba[s] of lentil,
12    free of all risk, the public (dues) of the land being yearly [upon] the
13    landlords, who are masters of the
14    crops until they recover the
15    debts owed. And if any (land), which may not be,
16    becomes unwatered, it shall be allowed for to the lesse[e]s. And the lease being guaranteed,
17    let the lessees pay back the wheat and the lentil
18    new, clean, unadulterated, free of clods,
19    sifted, the
20    wheat measured as for the public (granary), and the lentil free of weeds, on the threshing-floor of
21    Mermertha, jointly measuring with a just measure, the right of execution being upon both the lessees, who are mutually surety,
22    just as is set forth, concerning which, having formally questioned one another,
23    they acknowledged to each other.
24    In the 14th year of the Imperato[r] Caesar
25    Publius Licinius Gal[l]ienus
26    Germanicus Maximu[s], Persi[cus]
27    Maximus, Pius Fe[l]ix
28    Augustus, Thoth 27.
29    We, the Aurelii Antoni[os] and Panechotes, have leased
30    the land and we shall pay back the rents [in] mutual surety as is set forth, and being formally questioned we acknowledged. Aureli[u]s
31    Chaire[m]on, I wrote on their behalf
32    as they do not [k]now letters.
33    Lease of [fi]ve arurae of (land) of Merm[ertha].
