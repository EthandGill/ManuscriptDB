[TRANSLATION]
1     In Alexandria.
2     Copies of rescripts posted up in the portico
3     of the gymnasium in the 8th year, Phamenoth 18. The Emperor Caesar Lucius
4     Septimius Severus Pius Pertinax, Arabicus, Adiabenicus, Parthicus Maximus,
5     Augustus, and the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus,
6     to Ulpius Heraklanos also called Kallinikos:
7     Having granted time for the donation, we have remitted the penalties imposed on Alexandrians or Egyptians.
8     To Artemidoros also called Achilleus:
9     Having agreed to what has been decided, you slowly
10    blame the resolutions.
11    To the Aurelii Artemidoros and Anoubion and others:
12    Abide by what has been decided.
13    To Kil…dis also called Midas, through his son Philokrates:
14    Just as you unjustly demand that the sale of the mortgaged properties be annulled, so to recover for yourself
15    the possession of properties held without agreement
16    by force, the governor of the province will give order.
17    To …thalge, son of Ambrelos, through his son Abdomanches:
18    Women are not prevented from borrowing money and from
19    discharging debts on behalf of others.
20    On the 19th, likewise it was posted up in the same portico:
21    To Apollon, son of Harnektotes, and others:
22    The decisions concerning the inspections have in common provided
23    forethought for the Egyptians.
24    To Aurelius Sarapion:
25    That the priesthoods should pass down by succession from the maternal line
26    we formerly forbade.
27    To Proklos, son of Apollonios:
28    The written heirs, even if the wills
29    are said to have been forged, it is not
30    just to be cast out of possession. The
31    officials charged with the trials will take care to summon
32    those who are to be called to account, if indeed the matter is in
33    the category of judicial inquiries.
34    To Kronios, son of Herakleides:
35    The temporary illnesses of citizens do not
36    release them from public services; and even those weak in body
37    perform liturgies, if they are able to suffice
38    for the care of their own affairs.
39    On the 20th, likewise:
40    To Dioskoros, son of Hephaistion, and Pieses, son of Osiris,
41    and others:
42    We forbade you to pay money in place of wheat.
43    To Isidoros, son of Deios:
44    The things ventured by Komon
45    Fulvius Plautianus, the most excellent prefect
46    of the camps and our kinsman,
47    will investigate. But against Apion the tax-farmer, if he does not
48    share in the charges against Komon, you shall have the
49    governor of the province as judge.
50    To Isidoros also called Herakleides, through Apollonios:
51    Having renounced your paternal inheritance and
52    having no relief from your age under the
53    law of sales, since you say the property has been confiscated, comply.
54    To …elatheos, son of Kairenos:
55    If you take guardians for the orphans
56    from outside the appointed order, a judge will be given concerning the properties against those
57    holding them.
