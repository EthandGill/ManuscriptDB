[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord and most longed-for father Dios[kurides]
2     [wishing him] to prosper
3     I wished indeed to make a journey to distant places,
4     as indeed many times, making obeisance to you through letters I have made clear, but you, [master], have shown yourself in no way helping me in this; [you] know
5     for, and even if I do not make clear, [you] will know from the account of both the income
6     and of the expense, that nothing was left over, but [I] also had to take
7     for bare expense, necessity compelled us, as the
8     written expense will teach you. Therefore if you would wish to despise a few
9     legumes, so that by selling these I might find [money], how [can] I travel? Obviously send [it]. For there is a companion with whom I am able to go on foot, and if the
10    sea season passes me by, [I will travel] by donkey. I beg, do not neglect to inform me, so that I might stop thus worrying and grieving, and if those around Philapollon, and if they are accustomed to [matters concerning] the
11    headtax, [they] give; but if not, learn, if you are able to persuade
12    the partners, so that they give a few expenses; and I will see [the result].
13    Remember too for me what we suffered at [the hands] of the abominable and false-tyrant, and using such great speed send with the few
14    expenses the notification of what is being done with the aforementioned [person]
15    …; for it is not reasonable for me to delay
16    … [all the concern of the] admiralty, and afterwards
17    … fortune having caught [together]; and from where
18    … [illegible] … of the departure
19    … [ascent/return] none
20    … [holds/contains except] your letter
21    … I fail to …
22    … [and of those] with you; therefore
23    … [make haste]
24    … [illegible] … whatever
25    But I did not find Alexander, [the one] from the notaries, in Egypt …
26    my letter. [Please] inform me about everything.
