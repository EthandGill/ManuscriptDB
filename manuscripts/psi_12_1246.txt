[TRANSLATION]
1     … so that … . For it is necessary
2     of [friends] and always and likewise and everywhere to care. And my brother
3     cooperating with him [in this], that you will receive [him] favorably in
4     all [things] I have been persuaded. And to him too I have instructed,
5     [dearest one], that things beloved by me be greeted to you as a friend. I pray [you]
6     to be in good health by all the gods, brother, always.
7     … to [Apollonianus], strategus of Hermopolis
