[TRANSLATION]
1     To Sarapionis of the diadochi and vice-administrator,
2     from Ptolemaios the Macedonian who is
3     in confinement in the great Sarapieion
4     this year 11 and of the twins. Having petitioned
5     you previously still concerning those owing
6     towards the provision of year 18 from the Sarapieion
7     emmer wheat, 160 artabas, from which given [for] 6 months,
8     but to the 19th for the six-month [period] and then to the 20th year nothing having received, you ordered
9     according to your piety towards the divine [person] to give,
10    whence also to write to Menydes the overseer
11    to Psintaes to give both the aforementioned, and likewise also that becoming
12    from the Asclepieum, and for the rest to be orderly, [so]
13    that we may complete [it] in accordance with those [governing] such
14    [matters]. Since then not even thus [until] now
15    has the giving back happened for us, both the king
16    many times through petition we have troubled,
17    and also the feritob and you, having presented to you
18    the boy from us in the sacred [place],
19    we ask you and having worked together with us in these things to order more carefully [to Psintaes] to give us the aforementioned, so that
20    we may be able to complete the services in the sacred [place],
21    and to recall Amosis who is succeeding him,
22    so that it may be without excuse. Fare well.
