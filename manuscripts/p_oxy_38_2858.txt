[TRANSLATION]
1     To Theon, scribe of the city,
2     from Petesouchos son of Chairemon son of Petesouchos, his mother being Tausiris, and from his wife Bereis also called Demetria, daughter of Apollonios son of Theoxenos, her mother being Bereis, being the full sister of her husband Apollonios, with her guardian her full brother
3     Agenor also called Theoxenos, and from Tauris daughter of Pausirion son of Demas, her mother being Taarmiysis, with her guardian her father Pausirion son of Demas, his mother being Er…os, all from the city of the Oxyrhynchites. We wish
4     from now to register, in respect of the (property) belonging to Tauris in the quarter of the Metroon,
5     formerly of Diogenes son of Diogenes son of Ischyrion,
6     a third part of a house, the son of Petesouchos and Bereis, Theon,
7     being in the present 11th year four years (old),
8     a twelve-drachma payer, from the gymnasium. Therefore we submit
9     the memorandum as is fitting. Year 11
10    of the Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius
11    Antoninus Augustus Armeniacus
12    Medicus Parthicus Maximus,
13    Mesore 30. I, Petesouchos son of Chairemon, have submitted
14    the registration of my son Theon as aforesaid. I, Beris also called Demetria, daughter of Apollonios, have jointly submitted the
15    registration of my son Theon as aforesaid. I, Agenor also called
16    Theoxenos, have been registered as guardian
17    of my full sister and have written on her behalf
18    since she does not know letters. I, Tauris
19    daughter of Pausirion, have jointly submitted. I, Pausirion son of Demas, have been registered as guardian
20    of my daughter and
21    have written on her behalf since she does not
22    know letters.
