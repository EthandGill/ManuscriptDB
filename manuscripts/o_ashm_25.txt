1     Phaminis [son] of Psenchoinsios has written [the account]
2     for the census tax of the 5th year, ten drachmas; bathing tax, one drachma [and] 1/12 obol

4     1/12 of an obol. Year 5 of Nero
5     Claudius Caesar
6     Augustus, Germanicus, Imperator, Epeiph
7     17. Bassos [has] acknowledged.
