[TRANSLATION]
1     Athenodoros to [Soteriches] the brother
2     greetings [and] to be well; according to Apollonios
3     the [patron's] word, I have hired ships
4     if four hundred artabae [be profitable] to you
5     … [ready] being; if you receive the letter
6     [immediately] report to me; for the ships
7     [on] the third [expect]; and [yourself] be concerned
8     [so that] you may be well
9     be strong
10    year 21 of Caesar, Epeiph 2
