[TRANSLATION]
1     Ptolemais to Ammonios
2     greetings. You will do well by informing me whether you have received from Nicagore or from Isidore either grain or a pledge, whether also Diodore [gave] you
3     the letters or not; if
4     nothing of these things comes to pass, even if you go
5     to … fourth or send [word] from there about
6     all things to me [as] report
7     inform, and what places have stood ready for sowing. Do not therefore be negligent [in doing] all these things …
8     to Ammonios
