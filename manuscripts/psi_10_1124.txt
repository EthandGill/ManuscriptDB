[TRANSLATION]
1     To Tawti, [son] of Ptolemaeus, through [his] son
2     Apollonius of Lourios, who is also [called] Apollonius,
3     from [Dios]corus, [son] of Castor of Ailoura,
4     from the district of Tameia of Persians of [Persian] descent.
5     [I] wish to lease from you for [a period of]
6     two years from the incoming fourteenth
7     year of Imperator Caesar Titus Aelius
8     Hadrian Antoninus Augustus, pious,
9     the half portion, [which is] jointly and indivisibly,
10    of the [lands] belonging to you near T[e]ptunis, [a] plot of ten arouras or however many they may be, called Ilatos: the first year
11    for sowing barley, [the] yield of which [shall be] entirely
12    together with which I will receive seed-barley of five artabas [as part] of all the barley artabas
13    ninety, all [measured by] the four-choinix measure of the Teptunian treasury; the second
14    year for sowing chopped forage for drying
15    [as the] rent, aspermi, of silver of eighty drachmas and plants for the Eiselian [purpose] per year
16    of one bird [worth] two drachmas, all [to be] without deduction and without risk; and I will perform the necessary work that is required and the
17    yields I will give in the month Payni on the [threshing] floor,
18    new, clean, unadulterated; [and] the rent in Phamenoth,
19    the annual transport-expenses [being] at my expense with zealous care, but the conveying of the yields [being] to you.
20    And after the time is completed I will hand over the arouras
21    clean of reed, [of] cane, [and] wild-grass, [being rid] of
22    all. And you will guarantee to me the lease both clear
23    of public [charges] if it appears …
24    Dioscorus, being of age 22, [with a] scar
25    on [his] small finger of [his] left hand
26    of the year 13 of Antoninus Kairos, lord,
27    Mesore 20.
28    Copy of Dioscorus [lease] of five arouras, Ilatos,
29    for two years.
