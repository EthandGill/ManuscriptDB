[TRANSLATION]
1     After the consulship of Flavius Iordanes and Severus, the most illustrious, Phamenoth 28,
2     of the 9th indiction.
3     To the Aurelii Phoibammon son of Basileides and his wife Taurine,
4     from the Hermopolite (city), from Aurelius Eleutherios son of Taurinos, with Eirene
5     daughter of Magiros, from the same city: I acknowledge willingly and of my own free choice
6     that I have leased from you, for whatever time you wish, reckoned [from]
7     the new moon of the coming month Pharmouthi of the present ninth indiction,
8     the property allotted to you by Eudaimonis daughter of Hermeinos, monk,
9     and Pniamias son of Heraklammon — a two-thirds share of a whole house,
10    entire, together with the floors and all the appurtenances, of whatever
11    condition it is, and a [third] part of the cistern there situated in
12    Hermopolis,
13    in the quarter of Phrourion of the East, you allowing me your concurrence
14    for my dwelling and use, at a rent per month of silver
15    one hundred talents, which rent I shall pay to you at the end of each
16    month without delay; and whenever you wish to have it, I shall hand over [to you]
17    the same two-thirds share of the house with its doors and keys, [as] I received it.
18    The lease is valid, and on being formally questioned I gave my assent. Aurelius Eleutherios son of Taurinos:
19    I have leased it,
20    as aforesaid. Aurelius Phoibammon son of Ioannes, from Hermopolis, having been requested, I wrote [on his behalf]
21    the writing,
22    he being illiterate. Aurelius Phoibammon son of Basileides, [I bear witness].
23    Aurelius Kollouthos son of Epimeles, from Hermopolis, I bear witness to the lease.
24    Written through me, Ioannes.
