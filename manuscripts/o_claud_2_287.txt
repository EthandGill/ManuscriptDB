[TRANSLATION]
1     … [Send us] a hard-work[er to repair for us] the mill since it was destroyed and we are not able to grind.
2     and we are suffering for bread.
3     Having been entreated then, lord, send yourself with the courier so that we might be able through you and your kindness loaves
4     to eat.
5     Farewell, lord.
