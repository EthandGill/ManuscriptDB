[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy, greeting, from Philon the Arcadian, pentekontarch of the corps, cleruch. I am being wronged
2     by Apollonios son of Ainios, of the second hipparchy, holder of a hundred arouras, and by Philotis his mother, who are on the Sacred Island of the Saviour Gods. For having borrowed from me a hundred and fifty drachmas of bronze from hand to hand so as to buy cuirasses and a gauntlet for Apollonios, …
3     though demanded many times by me, they did not pay me back, alleging that they were short of money; and I, on account of being close [to them], in those
4     circumstances put up with it until [now]. I therefore beg you, O king, if it please you, to order Diophanes the strategos to write to Menellas the epistates to send Apollonios and Philotis up before him, and, if they acknowledge having received from me the hundred and fifty drachmas, to compel Apollonios and
5     [Philotis] to pay me back; but if … Apollonios [and] Philotis …
6     of which I … [having received] from me the hundred and fifty [drachmas] … the cuirasses
7     and the gauntlet … Apollonios …, out of the things charged against them … and that they be released. For these things being done, I shall, through you, O king, … have obtained.
8     Farewell.
9     To Menellas. Best of all, settle them; but if not, [send them to us so that we may investigate].
10    Year 25, Loios 26, [Choiach 13].
11    [Year 25, Loios 26, Choiach 13].
12    The Arcadian against Apollonios
13    and Philotis the mother
14    concerning a loan of 150 drachmas.
