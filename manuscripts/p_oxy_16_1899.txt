[TRANSLATION]
1     In (the year) after the consulship of him of divine lot,
2     New Leo, for the 1st time, Pachon 13.
3     To Flavius Alexandros the most magnificent and
4     most glorious strategos, through you, Flavius Ioseph, most illustrious administrator of the affairs of the same most glorious man situated in the Oxyrhynchite (nome),
5     (from) Aurelius Symphonias son of Abraamios, from the hamlet of
6     Piaa of the same most glorious man, of the same nome.
7     A need having now arisen for the proprietary irrigation-machine under me, of the estate, called
8     (the one) drawing (water) into vineyard and into arable land, of one wheel — having come up to the city I requested that
9     this be provided to me, and at once your
10    Splendor provided me with the same wheel, new,
11    suitable, for drawing water, through Phoibammon the carpenter, which, having received
12    on this present day, which is the thirteenth of Pachon, of the cultivation of the fifteenth indiction,
13    I acknowledge that the water-drawings and water-supplyings
14    I shall fulfill blamelessly, and (that I shall) be honest about the rents and obey in all that pertains.
15    Valid is the handwritten document, written in single copy, and on being questioned
16    I acknowledged. I, Aurelius Symphonias son of Abraamios, the aforesaid,
17    have made this handwritten document, and all the things written agree with me
18    as is set forth. I, Papnouthis son of Ioannes,
19    having been requested, wrote on his behalf, present, since he does not know letters.
20    Handwritten document of Symph[onias son of] Abraa[mios], from Piaa, for receipt of [one] wheel.
