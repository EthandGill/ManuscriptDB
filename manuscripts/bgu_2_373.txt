[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Aurelios Heliodoros, son of Didymos, mother Alille, from the quarter of the Bithynians and other areas, acknowledges that he has sold to Aurelios Hatres, son of Aboch, mother Thermouthis, from the village of Arsinoe, a small, black donkey foal for the prices agreed upon between them of [x] silver drachmas, which the seller has received in full from hand to hand, on the spot, just as it is, unreturnable. The sale is normative and in answer to the formal question I gave my assent. (m. 2 ?) Year 15 and 14 and 7 of our lords Diocletianus and Maximianus, Augusti, and Constantius and Maximianus, nobilissimi Caesares, Thoth 9. (m. 3) I, Aurelios Kollas, have sold and received the price in full, as aforesaid. I, Aurelios Heron, wrote on his behalf, because he is illiterate.
