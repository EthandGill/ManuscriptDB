[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 8, Mecheir 2, under Sosius as market-master.
2     Harkonesis, son of Phigeria, a Persian of the
3     mercenary horsemen, acknowledges to have from Naomsesis
4     of Spemminia, for the ransom of the fourth share
5     of mainland grain-bearing land in the lower toparchy of Latopolis, which she purchased
6     from the royal treasury in the 5th year toward the [ekphoria] of hers, and having received the customary written agreement
7     to have conceded to her his own d
8     share, and being present at the record office acknowledged in writing to have received in full and not to make claim
9     against Naomsesis nor any other person whatsoever concerning the
10    fourth share of the land; but if not, let the procedure for the one advancing a claim be void,
11    and let him pay a penalty immediately
12    of bronze talents 5 and to the king[s] sacred of silver
13    of marked drachmae 100, and nothing less [necessary] for him [it shall be] to do according to the things written above.
14    Ammonius, the one by Sosius, has authenticated.
