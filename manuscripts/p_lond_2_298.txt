[TRANSLATION]
1     In the eighth year of Imperator Caesar Trajan
2     Hadrian Augustus, in the month Gorpiaios 5 [Epeiph 5],
3     in Ptolemais Euergetis of the Arsinoite [nome].
4     Primos son of Sambas son of Primos, a [Persian] of the epigone, about fifty-five years old, with a scar on his left temple, acknowledges to Herakleides son of Triadelphos son of Anoubion, of the Nilanabation tribe and Althaian deme, about twenty-
5     five years old, with a scar on his right wrist, that he, the acknowledging party Primos, has received from him, through the bank of Dionysios also called Chairemon at the Sacred Gate, two thousand drachmas of silver as a deposit, free of all
6     risk and exempt from all deduction; and that the
7     … of the deposit, two thousand drachmas of silver, the acknowledging party [Primos] shall of necessity repay to Herakleides whenever Herakleides himself may choose, without lawsuit or judgment or any delay or pretext; and if he does not repay as
8     written, let him pay back to Herakleides the deposit double, according to the law of deposits, the right of execution belonging to Herakleides both upon
9     Primos and upon all his property,
10    as in execution of a judgment. Subscriber for Primos, who writes slowly: Didas son of Ptolemaios, about thirty-seven years old, with a scar on his right cheek.
11    Year 8 of Imperator Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, (registered at the) records-office.
