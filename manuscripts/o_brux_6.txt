[TRANSLATION]
1     Phatres [son] of Senchensos sitologos of priestly [portions]
2     of year 19 of Caesar to Arthotes son of Petemenwphis
3     greetings. You have measured into the treasury for Nesos
4     of cavalry wheat artabas two hundred [and] ten, there are wheat artabas 210.
5     of year 19 of Caesar Epeiph 11. Apollonius
6     [son] of Echaretus I have followed the wheat artabas of two hundred
7     [and] ten, there are wheat artabas 210. of year 19 of Caesar Epeiph 1.
