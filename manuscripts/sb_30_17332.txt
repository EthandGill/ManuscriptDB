1     [Reigning] Ptolemy of Ptolemy and Cleopatra of the gods Epi[phanes, year] eight, for the priest being
2     [in Alexandria of Alexander and of the gods Saviors and of the gods Siblings and] of the gods Benefactors and of the gods Philopatours
3     [and of the gods Epiphanes and of the gods Philomaters, prize-bearer of Berenice Benefactress], basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphia, priestess
4     [of Arsinoe Philopator being in Alexandria, but in Ptolemais] of the Thebaid, for priests of Ptolemy indeed
5     [of Soter, Ptolemy and of god Epiphanes and Eucharistos] … of [and] king Ptolemy and Cleopatra
6     [of the mother goddess Epiphanes, basket-bearer of Arsinoe Philadelphia being] in Ptolemais, month of Thoth fifth
7     [in Zeus city the great of the Thebaid upon] … [of the toward the] agoranomy of Perithebas and Pathurites.
8     [lent Sosistratos] … of the ranks of foot-soldiers paid-fixed, as of years
9     … [having a] hooked nose, projecting ears, scar on [the] right wrist,
10    [of wheat artabas one hundred] … [from] the aforementioned time to nine months,
11    [to Druton of Pamphilos Philoterios, as of years] … [middle complexioned light] complexioned stiff long-faced having a straight nose hook-nosed, scar beside eyebrow left edge, and] … [of] Pamphilos to Sostratus [as of years twenty two good-sized dark-complexioned
12    … [hollow] cheek-boned. And the loan of wheat let [them] repay
13    [the ones having been loaned in month Pauni new wheat risk-free from] every risk [in the measure which they also received and let them restore] to [his] house with their own ex]penditures; and if the ones around Druton do not restore the wheat artabas in the determined time, or do] not [do it exactly] as [it] has been written or contrary to the [made agreement they measure or] … [they pledge], [let] the ones around Druton repay the one hundred
14    [artabas one-and-a-half times or the price to be in the market] and the [collection] shall be [to] Sosistratos from one and each
15    [them and from all the property belonging to them and no] less. This agreement shall be valid wherever it is brought.
16    … [of] Ammonius, secretary.
