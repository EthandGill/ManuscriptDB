[TRANSLATION]
1     Pa[n]ouris to Maximos
2     To the lord and brother, very many greetings. If not through me into the pra[i]sidion no one hired the girl. And I
3     Served the lord of her so that he might receive
4     Six staters. And he has wronged me
5     As if not a human being. I carry her away
6     The whole time toward the curator and
7     Eats and drinks, so that Ariston might say to her
8     That: go away, sleep with Panourios. I do my things to her by force.
9     Loves the Daka [and] says to Ariston that: give to the Daki and receive bronze. On account of this
10    Has wronged me.
11    To Maximos … might pass …
12    … I come …
