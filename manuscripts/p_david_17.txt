[TRANSLATION]
1     in the consulship of Flavius Kethegus the most illustrious, Hathyr 2, 13th indiction
2     to Flavius Victor, venerable night-commander
3     of the southern part of the Hermopolite [nome] from Aurelius Josephius
4     Phibammon's farmer from the same city.
5     [if] the correction of the laws did not proceed against those
6     [attempt]ing to do absurdly, all would fall into madness
7     [and into] other absurdities would run. Since therefore
8     [Dion]ysius a certain painter his profession dared against all laws with blows [to strike] me
9     [down] so as also to appear the blows laid upon [me]
10    [across all of] my body, and moreover
11    [and] to send his own son into my dwelling at sun[set] to rob … … all of the
12    [grain] of the sowing of my field,
13    [for which reason] … [making] your …
14    petition of Josephius [son] of Phibammon
