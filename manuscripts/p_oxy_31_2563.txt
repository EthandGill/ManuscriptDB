[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aquilius Capitolinus
2     the most excellent epistrategos,
3     from Sarapion son of Hierax, from the city of the Oxyrhynchites,
4     overseer
5     of the tribes of the Sarapeion and of the
6     other temples of the
7     city. I petitioned
8     you, lord, through a petition,
9     you requesting and you promised to send up
10    the petitions, and up to now it has not been delivered to me,
11    concerning both the assault which was done to me by Ploution son of Dionysodoros and
12    his son-in-law. And I also petitioned Herakleides
13    the royal scribe, also deputizing in the affairs of
14    the strategia, and he compelled one of the attendants about him,
15    Thrasyllos, to inspect the
16    condition concerning me concerning
17    the assault that was done to me,
18    and a report of a physician followed concerning the wounds which I then
19    had. Not only
20    me but also my son
21    and his nurse Heras he maltreated them with blows on account
22    of an inheritance, the … [fur]nishings which her brother
23    Sarapion left to Heras after
24    his death, and the document was made public through the chief-justice so that she should be heir.
25    Thus then, behaving so insolently, being a villager,
26    he attacked us. To you
27    I took refuge, the savior of all
28    and benefactor,
29    that you hear me against him.
30    No violence takes place
31    in the most fortunate (times)
32    of our lord Aurelius
33    A[ntoninu]s Caesar the
34    … e epistra(tegos?)
35    … nmen
36    … ema
