[TRANSLATION]
# HGV-EN: Year 2 of Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Pius Felix Augustus, Mecheir 9, in the market place of Kerkesoucha. Aurelius Serenos, son of Karpinos from the village of Arsinoe, about 45 years old, with a scar on his right wrist, has sold a male, white, fully grown donkey for 500 drachmas to Aurelius Sabinos.
