[TRANSLATION]
1     to my [lord] brother Colluthus
2     Parit greetings.
3     The thirteen xestai of honey which you have for me, give to
4     our brother Ammonis,
5     to the principal of the overseer,
6     a clay vessel. But see that you do not neglect, lord brother. Find
7     therefore for him the finest.
8     for you to be well
9     I pray many
10    times.
11    … to my lord brother [Colluthus] …
