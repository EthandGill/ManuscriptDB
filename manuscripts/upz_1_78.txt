[TRANSLATION]
1     [P]tolemaeus to Damoxenes [greetings.]
2     Year 22, Tybi 12, toward the 13th, I dreamed [myself in Memphis]
3     To go from the west until the [east]
4     And I fall upon chaff, [but] [a] [man]
5     From the west near me falls
6     And he, and as if closed
7     Were my eyes, and suddenly I open
8     My eyes, and I see for you the twins
9     In the school of Tothetes. [They] called.
10    They said: see, be not faint-hearted. Tothes, weary, having found
11    The way to me, because I have changed the bed
12    Of mine. I heard Tothes saying: go straight,
13    Why do you say these things? I will set [up] the twins
14    Upon you. I see for you him setting
15    Them and I went before them,
16    Until I overtake them, and I come into the street
17    With them. I said to them that still a short time
18    I have in Hathur, and in the morning the 8th month [will] be. Straight away I saw
19    One of them going toward the wall into a dark
20    Place and she sits down urinating. I saw straight away that [one] of them
21    Has squatted down. I said to Harmaeus to [haste] [come] to him.
22    And other things I saw many, and again I asked the
23    Sarapis and Isis, saying: come to me, goddess of goddesses,
24    Becoming gracious, hear me, pity the twins,
25    You showed twins. But release me, behold, having gray hair,
26    But I know that in a short time I will stop, but these
27    Are women. If they should be defiled, [they will not] ever become
28    Pure ever. On the 14th I dreamed myself in Alexandria myself to be upon a great tower. I had
29    A beautiful face, I had, and I did not wish to anyone
30    To show my face because of its beauty
31    To be, and an old woman sat near me, and a crowd from the north
32    And from the east. [They cry] that [a man became] gray[-haired]
33    With gray hair, and [he] says to me: stay a short time and I will lead you
34    To the daemon Kneph, in order that [you] worship [him].
35    And I dreamed myself to say to an old man: father, do you not see
36    This vision which I have beheld? I [narrated] to him. He gave
37    Me two reeds. Having looked [quick]ly I saw Kneph.
38    Rejoice, all those with me: [remis]sion to me happens quickly.
39    But I have beheld. More beautiful are these. [It is known] that it lies for me
40    It is set before me to have safe [harbor] the twins.
41    I am cautious, but nothing. These [judge] until Phamenoth.
42    Henceforth [it] is besought, [twins,] [the] [Isis]
43    Know that I am departing. Ammon comes to me,
44    Gave me the way, and [the] [shrine] opened before me.
45    [Fare well].
