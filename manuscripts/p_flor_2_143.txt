[TRANSLATION]
1     From Alypios.
2     From the more suitable wine
3     manage wine [of] monochora
4     fifty for the expenses with you
5     works, that is manure-making and
6     sand-digging of the properties
7     of which you will give account, reckoning at the monochoros
8     at fourteen drachmas;
9     it comes to fifty monochora at fourteen drachmas.
10    I have noted [this,] and manage the [account] of the wine
11    [of] fifty monochora as
12    the monochoros at fourteen drachmas.
13    To Heroneinos, manager at Thraso.
14    Year 12, Hathyr 2.
