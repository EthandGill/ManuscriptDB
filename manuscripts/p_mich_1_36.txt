[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollonius to Zenon greeting. [You] wrote
2     to us that Pais the beer-maker [undertook] eleven [artabas]
3     per day [he] claims to have agreed
4     with us. [He] lied therefore to you; for [he] undertook twelve
5     artabas and in addition to these I sent
6     to you. Detain therefore him until
7     we arrive and order
8     [him] to be recorded the beer-shop [and]
9     to attend to all [matters] according to the
10    workshop.
11    Farewell. Year 31, Dystrus [7, Phamen]oth 14.
12    To Zen[on].
