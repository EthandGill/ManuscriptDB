[TRANSLATION]
1     Philocrates to Zenon greetings. Much thanks to the gods, if you yourself are well and the rest
2     is according to your mind. I have sent to you the oil-seller Paias, about … of whom Hermolaus also reminded you.
3     You would do well then arranging to give to him. And I myself would have come
4     to you, but I am occupied. Write to us always whatever you have need of and do not forget us. Farewell.
5     To Philocrates. Patis [did not arrive], but [went] into the city of Crocodiles. They say
6     To Zenon.
