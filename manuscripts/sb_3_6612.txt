[TRANSLATION]
1     And the copy [is]:
2     in the consulship of our lords Valentinian and Valens
3     eternal Augustuses, Phaophi 25th.
4     Aurelius Polybios, son of Melanos, mother Taor, from the hamlet of Dioscurias
5     near the village of Sinkifas, 2nd pagusby, of the Oxyrhynchite nome
6     to Aurelius Heracleides, son of Dioscurides, having served as prytanis, councillor of the illustrious and most illustrious
7     [Oxy]rhynchite city, greetings. I acknowledge to have bought from you
8     from now on for ever the lands belonging to you in the plains
9     of the same village of Sinkifas, of the soil called Ankelylius
10    [forty-nine] private arouras formerly [of] Kastor
11    [Behes], from the hamlet of Eirene near the village of Berku of the great
12    Hermopolite nome, and in another place near the same village
13    [Sinkifas, royal] twelve arouras or however many there were formerly
14    [of] Seuthos, nicknamed Phambet-be, and of Artemis, a woman from
15    the Oxyrhynchite city, on which they have as boundaries and
16    locations by field. Therefore from now I hold and am master [with descendants] from the tenth indiction [and this] tenth indiction.
17    Valid the counterpurchase written in simple form, and having been questioned I [acknowledged]. Phaophi 25th.
18    Aurelius Polybios Melanos, the aforementioned, I have bought
19    the aforementioned [forty-nine] private arouras and [royal]
20    twelve arouras and I will complete all public [obligations] due on them from the
21    fortunate tenth indiction, as is set out above. Aurelius
22    Apollo, son of Ioannes, I wrote the document for him not knowing letters.
