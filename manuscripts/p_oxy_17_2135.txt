[TRANSLATION]
1     ... to [Sarap]ion [the] also called Apoll[on]i[an]us
2     who [was] gymnasiarch of the same city
3     greetings. Since indeed I have discussed with you in
4     the city's business, I acknowledge that in no way are you being wronged regarding what we owe
5     you, both I and my brother. Of the year
6     eighth and twentieth of Aurelius Commodus
7     Anto[nin]us Caesar the lord, Pharmouthi
8     the twenty-first.
9     Apollonius and Epimachos both of Agathinus
10    having been exegete through Heracles private to Sarapion the also
11    called Apollonianus who [was gymnasiarch] greetings. Since even now
12    we have discussed with you through the same Heracles in the
13    city's business, we acknowledge that in no way are you
14    being wronged regarding what we owe you. Year twenty-nine of Aurelius
15    Commodus Antoninus Caesar the lord, Athyr eighteen.
