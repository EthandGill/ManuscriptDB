1     [After the consulate of Flavius Basilius the all-renowned] Phaophi 14th of the second [indiction]
2     [ to the property of the holy ] ... [church] of the Hermopolitans called the Resurrection
3     [through ] ... [most pious presbyter] and steward of the same holy church
4     [From ] ... farmer [from] village Thrace of the Hermopolite nome
5     [I acknowledge willingly and of my own accord to have leased] from the same holy church for
6     ... [a time] reckoned [from harvests of the] prosperous coming third indiction
7     [the property belonging to] the property of the same holy church, sown land, two arouras
