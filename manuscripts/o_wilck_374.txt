1   Has paid: (the son) of Herieus son of Paapis, for poll-tax
2   of year 21, ten drachmas and bath-tax one, an obol, a half-obol,
3   total 11 dr. 1½ ob., and the surcharges
4   at an obol (and) half-obol.
5   Year 21 of Tiberius Caesar
