[TRANSLATION]
1     From
2     and when your excellence was present, he was entreated by me concerning the six gold pieces of the condemnation
3     and he promised to do what was possible so that he might accomplish these [things] for me, now again I present the same
4     entreaties so that you might deem [him] worthy, together with my lord, the most magnificent count Strategios,

6     so that [they] might cease from the harassment against me.
