[TRANSLATION]
1     Apollodotus to Zenon greetings. If you yourself [are in good health and all other things are] as you wish
2     it is, it would be as we [wish]; we are in good health [and ourselves]. …
3     Your father and your [brothers] met in Caunus … …
4     we did [this] for them and we entreated [them] that also in the future […]
5     they might meet with us. You would do well also [by writing] to them …
6     to speak about whatever they might need and toward us [having written something] …
7     doing [this] we would gratify you. For now in all …
8     from you we wondered what the cause is.
9     Be well. Year 28, Peri[tius] 10.
10    Apollodotus, the beneficent one
11    concerning the father. Year 28, Dystrus 24,
12    in Memphis.
13    To Zenon.
