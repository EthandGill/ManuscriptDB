[TRANSLATION]
1     … of Tothes …
2     … (son) of Apollonius, harvesters
3     of those from Hermopolis. We wish, of our own free will,
4     … from you for harvesting, which
5     wheat-crops you have in the district Around the City
6     lower, one hundred and thirty arouras, at a wage
7     for each aroura of one and a half
8     and a twelfth artabas of wheat; and on the spot we have received from you as an advance
9     forty drachmas of silver, which we shall repay
10    when we receive the wheat … pure (unadulterated),
11    by the sixth-measure of Athenaeus at Hermopolis.
12    And of necessity we shall furnish you with harvesters,
13    ten, you providing another three for the completion of thirteen harvesters. And we shall begin
14    from the thirtieth of the present month Pharmouthi
15    and we shall not withdraw …
16    being sureties for one another; and we shall harvest for you
17    without wage for one day, you providing us with
18    water and bindings and the man who gathers up the sheaves,
19    the choice being ours whether to have the crops measured out again or to receive, in respect of
20    the aforesaid arouras, the fixed wages;
21    and you shall give us one artaba of lentils and you shall furnish the … and you shall give us
22    two sheaves; and the wages you shall repay us in the (month)
23    Epeiph of the present year. Year 9 of Imperator
24    Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Pharmouthi 17.
25    I, Eutychides son of Sarapion, have leased the arouras to be harvested from
26    Pharmouthi 26 and … as is stated above … and we have received
27    the aforesaid forty drachmas.
