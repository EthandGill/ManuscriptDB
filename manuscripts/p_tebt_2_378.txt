[TRANSLATION]
1     [To the Aur]elii Sarap[a]mmon, a minor, and Hera[kleia] transacting business without a guardian, both children of [Her]on from …, and Sarapammon
2     [being with the guar]dianship of his sister Herakleia the aforesaid,
3     [from Au]relius D[e]m[etrio]s and howsoever he is styled. I wish to lease from you the (property) belonging to you around the village of
4     Theogenis, a half share of nine grain-bearing arouras in
5     one parcel, formerly belonging to Heron, for a period of
6     four years from the sowing of the present 13th year, at a rent
7     for the half share of the 9 arouras each year, without seed being supplied, of twelve artabas of wheat; and hence I have received from you toward the restoration of the works of the arouras, which were handed over by Heron in a semi-dry state, which
8     you received from him for the restoration of the arouras,
9     three hundred silver drachmas, on condition that I hand them over clean.
10    And it shall not be permitted to me within the period
11    to abandon the lease in any way, but
12    under compulsion I shall carry out all the yearly works,
13    embanking, watering, hoeings, double-diggings of the canals and water-channels, throwings-up of the embankment-works, buildings, weedings, siphon-drainings, and the
14    other things as are fitting, from my own (means) at the proper seasons, doing
15    no harm, all the public dues being on
16    you the owners. And the yearly rent I shall pay
17    in the month Pauni at the threshing-floor, by the four-choinix racing-measure,
18    and after the period I shall hand over the arouras clean of rush, reed, dog's-tooth grass, and all filth,
19    but if I do not hand (them) over I shall pay back the three hundred drachmas which I received,
20    (the arouras being) not-to-be-subleased and not-to-be-worked-by-another for the
21    aforesaid period, and, having been questioned, I have agreed.
22    Aurelius Demetrios: I have leased the arouras
23    on condition that I hand them over restored, I having received on behalf of them the three hundred drachmas,
24    and, having been questioned, I have agreed. Aurelius Arsinoos
25    wrote on behalf of him, he being illiterate.
26    Year 13 of our lord Gallienus Augustus,
27    Phaophi 21.
