[TRANSLATION]
1     Petemouheres and Pamonthes, collectors of silver revenues of Charakos
2     To Petemenouphis, likewise of Onnophres. We received for laography and
3     bathing [tax] of the 8th year [outstanding] drachmas eight, making [outstanding] drachmas 8.
4     Of the 8th year of Antoninus Caesar our Lord
5     Phamouthi the 12th. Pamonthes, I have signed.
6     Another: Parmouthi the 15th [for] the name of the same [person] for laography, likewise [outstanding] drachmas 4,
7     making [outstanding] drachmas 4. Petemouheres, I have signed.
