[TRANSLATION]
1   Zenon son of Herakleides, lessee
2   of the Sacred Gate of Syene, through Pa-
3   chompsachis the assistant. Paid:
4   Patesibdis son of Petorzmethes,
5   mother Thinpelaias, for the trade-tax
6   of the 9th year of Antoninus Caesar
7   the lord: twenty drachmas (worn)
8   (and) two obols. Epeiph 21.
