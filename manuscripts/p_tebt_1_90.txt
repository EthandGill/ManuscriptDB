[TRANSLATION]
1     To Orsenouphis, chief-guard, of which there is an account:
2     Hermias …kos 1½, 3, of threshing 6, total 2.
3     The same, the wheat instead of barley.
4     Petesouchos son of Pebouris, of threshing 3.
5     Total for the day, 4½, 3, 12.
6     (Day) 21. Ambesis son of Kalatytis 1½, of threshing 6, makes 1 ⅔.
7     Ptolemaios son of [Har]palos, of threshing 6.
8     Makes for the day 1½, 3.
9     (Day) 22. Salmon son of Kokky, ½, 3, of threshing 6, makes 1.
10    Thoteus son of Harbechis 2½, 3, of threshing 6, total 3.
11    Pathebis son of Pasos 3, 3, of threshing 6, total 3½.
12    Son of Charis, the Ibionite, instead of kidney-bean 1 ⅔.
13    Mestas son of Melanippos 2.
14    And on behalf of the cultivators, Bithys son of Phalakros
15    by the dromos measure 5½, 3.
16    Ptolemaios son of Herakleos, of threshing 3.
17    Total for the day 17, 3.
18    (Day) 23. Petosiris son of Anempeus ½, 12, of threshing 6, makes ½, 4.
19    Saras son of Herakleos 1, 6, of threshing 6, makes 1, 3.
20    Maron son of Bithys 2½, of threshing 6, total 2 ⅔.
21    Total for the day 4½, 3, 12.
22    (Day) 28. Didymos son of Soterichos 2, 3, of threshing 6, total 2½.
23    Akousilaos son of Apollonios (son of) Polemon, ½, 3, of threshing 6, total 1.
24    (Day) 28: ⅔.
25    Harmiysis the cobbler ½, of threshing 6, makes ⅔.
26    Total for the day 4, 6.
27    (Day) 29. Koitix son of Berenikis 1 ⅔.
28    Apollonios son of Nikon 1, of threshing 6, makes 1, 6.
29    Total for the day 3½, 3.
30    (Day) 30. Apollonios son of Pinous 2½, of threshing 6, total 2 ⅔.
31    Diokles son of Ptolemaios (son of) Oxinas, 1, 3, of threshing 6, makes [1½.]
32    Harmiysis the cobbler ½, 3, of threshing 6, makes 1.
33    The same, instead of barley ½, 12.
34    Petesouchos son of Okouis (son of) Meniskos, 3.
35    Total for the day 6, 12.
36    Epeiph 1. Apollonios son of Talis 1, 3, of threshing 6, makes 1 [½.]
37    Petesouchos son of Teephme 1½, of threshing 6, makes 1 [⅔.]
38    Horos son of Skordeus, of the holding of Pro(-), 3.
39    The same, instead of bean 1.
40    Poseidonios the collector 1, 3, of threshing 6, [makes 1½.]
41    Patynis has (it).
42    Kollouthes the storehouse-keeper, of threshing [½.]
43    Total for the day 9, 6.
44    (Day) 4. Kronides son of Kotys ½, 3, of threshing 6, [makes 1.]
45    Selepinis and his partners, of the holding of Pro(-), 3, 6, of threshing 6, [total 3, 3.]
46    The same Selepinis, of the holding of Konon, ½, 3, of threshing [6, makes 1.]
47    (Day) 23: 4.
48    … of the holding of the queen, of wheat 10.
