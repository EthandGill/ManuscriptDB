1     From Apaioulios son of Pchip, for public taxes and the diagraphon and other (dues) of the thirteenth indiction, in the month Pauni 17, through Apaioulios the presbyter, of reckoned coin 3, one third only.
2     Damianos … another: Epeiph 27, of reckoned coin 6, one sixth only. The same, another: Mesore 3, of reckoned coin 6, one sixth only. The same, another: Mesore 19, through Apaioulios the presbyter,
3     of reckoned coin 12, one twelfth only. The same, another: Phaophi 8, through the same, of reckoned coin 6, one sixth only. The same, another: Phaophi 13, through the same, of reckoned coin 6, one sixth only. The same,
4     [another:] Tybi 6, through the same, of reckoned coin 3, one third only. The same, another: Mecheir 6, through the same, of reckoned coin 3, one third only. The same, another: Pharmouthi 3, through the same,
5     of gold coin 1¼, one and a quarter only. The same.
6     From Apaioulios son of Pchip, for public taxes and the diagraphon and other (dues) of the fourteenth indiction, in the month Pauni 14, through Apaioulios the presbyter, of reckoned coin 6, one sixth only. Damianos.
7     Another: Tybi 21, through the same, of reckoned coin 6, one sixth only. The same, another: Mesore 9, through the same, of reckoned coin 3, one third only. The same, another: Thoth 8, through Apaioulios the presbyter,
8     of reckoned coin 3, one third only. Georgios the deacon … Another: Phaophi 12, through Apaioulios the presbyter, of reckoned coin 6, one sixth only. Georgios the deacon … .
9     From Sergios son of Antonios the younger and … lph, for public taxes and the diagraphon and other (dues) of the canon of the 14th indiction, in the month Mecheir 19, through Apaioulios the presbyter, once for all, of reckoned coin ¾, one half and a quarter only.
10    From the said Sergios son of Antonios the younger.
