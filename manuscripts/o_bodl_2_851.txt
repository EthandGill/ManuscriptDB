[TRANSLATION]
1     Asklas and Soter, tax collectors of silver-tax, Charakos
2     to Eponycho, who is also Apathe
3     of Herakleous. we received on behalf of watchtowers and others, 20th year, Charakos,
4     three drachmas, 5 obols, 2 chalkoi, [which] amounts to 3 drachmas, 5 obols, 2 chalkoi.
5     Year 20 of Hadrian, the lord, Tybi 11.
6     Asklas, I have signed, 3 drachmas, 5 obols, 2 chalkoi.
7     Soter, I have signed, 3 drachmas, five-obol piece, 2 chalkoi.
