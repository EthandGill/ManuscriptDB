[TRANSLATION]
1     … [something] was offered up. And if you command and it pleases my master, send me two centenaria
2     [of fuel-wood; and after] these things, Lord Menasius said to me to write to my master concerning the hundred centenaria of fuel-wood
3     so that you send to him briefly, and indeed so that you send by his word a little fuel-wood; and I beseech, command
4     to send by his word a [little], since therefore he has come into the matter of the workers. Through my humble letter the feet of my master
5     I kiss …
