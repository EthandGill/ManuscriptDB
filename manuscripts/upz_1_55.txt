[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 18 of the twins, account of the loaves. They receive from Thoth 1 through Mechir 7.
2     From [the 8th] of Mechir through Mesore 30 and the 5 epagomenal days they have not received
3     of spelt loaves 8 artabas per month, [there] come to be 56 artabas. Year 19, Thoth 1 through Mechir 30
4     they receive. From Phamenoth 1 through Mesore 30 and epagomenal days 5
5     they have not received except the half, Pauni pairs 30, the epagomenal days 5 they have not received, [there come to be of spelt] artabas 35 and 1/4, choinices 2
6     [There] come to be of year 18 and year 19 [the] debt of the loaves … artabas 92, choinices 2
7     and first greetings toward the
8     greeting, ei ei ei
