[TRANSLATION]
1     To King Ptolemy greetings, Petesouchos. I am being wronged by Pombtus of those from Mouches. For [there being] to me
2     a bare plot in the aforementioned village, having come the accused one builds the plot, having judged me that I am older
3     and not to be strong with my own resources, but him to be strong. And I [preventing] him and to the builders having refused, I
4     paid no account, disregarding both and the [law. I beseech] therefore you, king, to order Diophanes the general
5     to write to Demetrius the overseer to send [Pombtus] and the builders to Diophanes so that, if
6     what I write be true, not to permit him to rob [the plot] from me nor to build, and the builders to obtain the
7     proper [penalty] as Diophanes might judge. For [having happened, to] you having taken refuge, king, I will obtain justice.
8     Farewell.
9     To Demetrius: best indeed [settle] them, but if not, send so that
10    [before] the judges of the people they might be decided.
11    Year 3, Artemisium …, Tho[th] 7
12    Petesouchos to Demetrius
13    against Pombtus
