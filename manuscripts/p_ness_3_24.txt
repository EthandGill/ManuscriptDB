[TRANSLATION]
1     In the [reign] of our most divine and most pious master Flavius Justinus, faithful in Christ, the mild, the greatest, benefactor and emperor, Augustus, in the fourth year, and after the second consulship of the same most pious our
2     [king], in the four hundred sixty-fourth year of the eras, of the third indiction, on the tenth of the month Apellaios, in the village of Nessana, of the territory of the city of Elousa. We, Flavius Abraamios and Abu Zonainos, brothers, sons of the late
3     [Sadallos] son of Oualens, … recruits of the camp here, and Thoamos son of Aouidos son of Ammonios, himself also a soldier of the same camp, to the most esteemed
4     and most reverend Georgios son of Stephanos, recorder of the said
5     city [of Elousa], greeting. May your esteemed self deign, on receiving this our injunction, to relieve us, the party of Abraamios and Abu Zonainos, from our group — that is, our paternal
6     [own] person — of five kabiaiai from the present and aforewritten third indiction, both itself and for all time hereafter, by right of the parcel ceded by us,
7     [arable land] with all its rights, to the aforesaid devoted Flavius Thoamos who has joined us in this injunction, (the parcel) lying to the south of this village and around it, in a place called Alagrad,
8     the nearer (one), of which the neighbours to east and north are Ablos son of Darebos, to west Zenon son of Persanos, our devoted (kinsman), on the divine south (side) fallow land, or if there are any other neighbours; (and) that I, Thoamos, be charged with — that is,
9     [to inscribe] to my group the said five kabiaiai from the same third indiction, both itself and for all time hereafter, on account of the parcel ceded to me,
10    [ar]able land with [all] its right, in the place called Alagrad, the nearer (one), from the aforesaid devoted Abraamios and Abu Zonainos, in accordance with the
11    written cession set forth to me by them, the public account being in no way harmed or diminished; for if a person should be in want, which may not happen, the original
12    … the public … and for the security and information of you and of the public account we have made this injunction to you, which when signed shall be valid and secure
13    … upon this your … We, Flavioi Abraamios son of Sadallos and Abu Zoninos, brother, have given the injunction, being relieved of the said five kabiaiai
14    … and of my brother … In my presence, Flavius Oualens son of Olesos, Abu Zoneinnos, the brother, gave instruction
15    … to the brother of Abramios, …
