[TRANSLATION]
1     Aristobulus and partners have received chaff
2     of the metropolis of year 4 of Antoninus Caesar, our lord
3     Herieus Petechespochratous. You delivered into the
4     camp at Coptos one load of chaff
5     half, total load of chaff 1 ½ for the inland metropolis
6     of the produce of year 4 of Antoninus Caesar, our lord, year 6
7     of Antoninus Caesar, our lord, Athur 4.
8     Psenamounis, I have been sealed.
