[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and life-giving and consubstantial
2     Trinity, of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. It was written
3     on Hathyr the fourteenth, in the tenth indiction, in Hermopolis of the Thebaid.
4     To Apa Apollonios, most reverent lector,
5     son of Theodorakios of God-loving memory, presbyter,
6     from the city of Hermopolis, from Aurelius Eusebios son of Zacharias, farmer,
7     from the same city: I acknowledge willingly and of my own free choice
8     that I have leased from you for a three-year period reckoned
9     from the crops of the eleventh indiction coming in, with God,
10    and thereafter likewise, the two arouras belonging to you
11    of sowable land, more or less, together with
12    their share of the filled-in cistern
13    and of the entrance and exit and every right,
14    situated in the farmstead called Psansoouhe
15    in the eastern part of Hermopolis, under the guard of the people from the village of Tobthis and of Pkerdon
16    of the Hermopolite nome, whose neighbors are: to the south, the lands
17    of Saint Phoibammon of the marketplace; to the north, the lands
18    of Athanasios the notary; to the east, the lands of the
19    heirs of John Psanaph; to the west, the lands
20    of the son of Mshous; or whoever the neighbors may be on every side
21    in every direction, for sowing and planting whatever crops
22    I may choose, the rent of these per year being wheat
23    of five artabas and barley of five and a half
24    artabas by the receiving-measure of Justus the farmer, son of
25    Leptias — totaling 5 artabas of wheat and 5½ artabas of barley by the receiving-measure of Justus the farmer — which
26    rent, new, good, pure, sifted,
27    I shall pay you in the month Epeiph each year without delay, and I shall deliver the same to your house
28    in Hermopolis, at my own expense for animals and men
29    and outlays. The present lease is valid and
30    secure, and on being questioned I gave my assent. Sign of Eusebios.
31    I, Aurelius Eusebios son of Zacharias, from Hermopolis,
32    the aforesaid, have leased as aforesaid. Gregorios,
33    humble lector, son of Athanasios, having been requested, wrote
34    on his behalf, as he does not know letters. I, Zacharias son of the
35    late Dorotheos, from Hermopolis, bear witness
36    to the lease, having been asked by the contracting party. I, Victor,
37    son of the late Aelianus, from Hermopolis, bear witness to the present
38    lease, having been asked by the contracting party.
39    Through me, Athanasios son of Victor, with God, notary, it was written.
40    … of rent of 5 artabas of wheat and 5½ artabas of barley by the receiving-measure, made by
41    Eusebios …
