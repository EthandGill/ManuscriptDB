[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the …] and tenth [year] of Imperator [Marcus Aurelius Antoninus] Augustus and Lucius Aurelius [Commodus,]
2     [Augusti, Armeniaci,] Medici, Parthici, Germanici,
3     [Sarmatici, greatest, in the month] of Hadrianos, the 22nd, in Ptolemais
4     [Euergetis of the Arsinoite nome.] Asklepiades son of An…
5     … [alias] Dionysieus, of those in Antinoopolis
6     … [being about] forty-nine years old, with a scar above
7     … acknowledges — of those being freed — to Sarapion, being about … years old, [with a scar] on the right knee, that he, the acknowledging Asklepiades, according to the same acknowledgment, has released
8     [Sarapion] … on the present day, under Zeus, Earth, Sun,
9     [and henceforth is content that] Asklepiades … in respect of the future … shall pay the tax for the freeing and proclamation, (and) that neither Asklepiades nor those
10    [who act for him lay claim] nor shall lay claim against Sarapion nor
11    [against those who act for him concerning any] of the things pertaining to the slavery, nor
12    [concerning anything whatsoever up to] the present day in any way; [but if he should proceed or] make claim, the attack and claim … (shall be void), (and) that he shall proceed against those on the side of
13    … the penalties and damages, and further also …
14    … [the damages and] the expenses double, and a fine
15    [of … drachmas of silver] and to the treasury the same amount, (the terms being) valid.
16    … [witness] of the freeing, Apollonios
17    … [from] the quarter of Hermouthiake, being about 38 years old,
18    … with a scar on the forehead.
