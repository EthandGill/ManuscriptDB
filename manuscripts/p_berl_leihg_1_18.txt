[TRANSLATION]
1     To the village-scribe of the village of Lagis
2     from Heron son of Hermanoubion son of Heron, of the Theometorion
3     also called Eusebeion, of those from the sacred synod also of Dionysos,
4     of the sacred victors and the exempt, and from his sister Hermitarion
5     also called Sarapias, a citizen-woman, they being absent, through the man
6     appointed by them according to the deed of appointment of Heron son of Ammonios son of Sotes, from the quarter of Syriake, completed through the prytaneis in Alexandria in the past month Tybi. The
7     property belonging to us jointly in equal shares around the aforesaid
8     village of Lagis: of olive-grove, in the catoecic category, in one parcel
9     ten arouras out of thirteen arouras called those of Postimius, and in
10    another parcel another four arouras out of seven arouras called those of Menas,
11    and in another parcel two arouras called those of the temple of Zeus, and in another
12    parcel another two arouras, half, quarter, eighth, out of three arouras
13    called those of the gourd-garden, and in another parcel another aroura,
14    one, and belonging to Heron alone one aroura, half,
15    quarter, out of two arouras called those of the temple of Zeus — the same
16    21 arouras, half, eighth, or however many there may be, we wish to convert to grain-land
17    from the present 3rd year of Antoninus and Verus, the lords
18    Augusti, on account of their having become poorly productive. Wherefore we submit (this)
19    so that the consequent action may be taken as in like cases.
20    Year 3 of Antoninus and Verus, the lords Augusti, Mecheir 30.
21    Isidoros, village-scribe, I have received the copy of this. Year 3 of Imperator Caesar
22    Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus and of Imperator Caesar Lucius
23    Aurelius Verus Augustus, Phamenoth the twenty-sixth.
