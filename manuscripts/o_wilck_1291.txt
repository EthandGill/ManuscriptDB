[TRANSLATION]
1     Didymos, collector of money-taxes of Charax,
2     to Petepsais [(son of) Porieuthes]. We have received for
3     the doubles of year 11, one base drachma, total base drachma 1. Year 11
4     of Antoninus Caesar the lord, Mecheir 11.
5     I, Didymos, through Phatres the scribe, have signed.
