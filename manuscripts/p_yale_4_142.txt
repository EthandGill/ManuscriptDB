[TRANSLATION]
1     To Boethus, kinsman and epistrategos
2     from Hembetepnachthis son of Horus, undertaker.
3     I have a share of a paternal house
4     in the village of B[usiris and this house having been de]molished during [the times of disorder]
5     Thnepher[os] Sa[...] [sharing] in it
6     [having failed with regard to] everything that was [fine]
7     not being satisfied [...] [with] shared [property] with [my]
8     husband ... [of] salt-fish makers
9     who are in the same B[usiris], having rebuilt the stated house, she dwells in [it]
10    with [what belongs] to her contrary to what is fitting. Therefore
11    compelled to make an appeal to you, the one appointed by
12    the king and the queens to dispense
13    justice and to support all [in their rights],
14    I ask, if it seems [right], having divided [and assigned] to me also of which you have
15    jurisdiction, [to] order [her] who is accused to be established before you, even as I [write to the] king, and [to compel]
16    [her to yield] my prior ownership of the half of [the house to be] compelled
17    to be confirmed [and] shown to me. Concerning the other [matters]
18    I will later present toward them the
19    appropriate case. [Farewell].
20    To Euphranor: if [she] does not do what is
21    just immediately, place [her in your custody]
22    Year [33], P[ayni 1]4.
23    To Ktesippus:
24    according to the note below
25    Year 33, Payni 14.
26    Year 33, Payni 14
27    Hembetepnachthis to Euphranor
28    [son] of Horus
