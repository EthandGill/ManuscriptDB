[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 24, Pauni 17: there measured into the (granary) in
2     Diospolis Magna, the granary, for the locality, of year 24,
3     Dositheos son of Pyrrhos, of wheat twenty-seven
4     and a quarter, total wheat 27¼; of barley 14½ 1/12, total barley 14½ 1/12.
5     Others, of wheat twenty-three and ½, total wheat 23½. Ambryon.
6     Ptolemaios: I have received by measure, of wheat twenty-seven and a quarter,
7     of barley fourteen and ½ 1/12, total wheat 27¼, barley 14½ 1/12,
8     others, of wheat twenty-three and a half, total wheat 50½¼,
9     of barley 14½ 1/12.
