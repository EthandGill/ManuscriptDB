[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 7 Choiak 4. Asclepiadis, wife Patrophila,
2     son Apollophanes as of 15 years, Apollodorus as of 13 years,
3     Artemidorus as of 10 years, Ptolemy as of 5 years, nurse
4     Kosmia, farmers on wages Chazaros, Ragesobaal
5     Ieab, Kraters, Sitalkes, Natanbaal, shepherd
6     Potamon, cowherd Horus total bodies 15.
7     I register the grain belonging to me for the needs of wheat
8     barley artabas 9, emmer artabas 15, for the needs of 4 pack animals the
9     serving [animals] and 6 yoke of oxen barley-wheat
10    artabas 6, hulled wheat mixed with barley artabas 12. are held moreover with me both myself and my partners' barley-wheat
11    artabas 30 for the export taxes, which we owe of Scamander's allotment … from the threshing-floor
12    together with the chaff artabas 15, pulses of beans artabas 23, of chickpeas artabas 35, of lentils artabas 7 and in deposit with Panhesius of lentils artabas 3, of beans artabas 15, and in the in Bubastis royal treasury cleansing of the grain
13    of the exported grain barley-wheat artabas 168, from which [the] wheat was sifted.
