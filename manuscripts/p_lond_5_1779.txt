[TRANSLATION]
1     [He/She] has given and [it] was paid through Phaueistos Ar[…] for the tax
2     of the productive arouras of the thirteenth indiction, that is, wheat, artabae four by [the Dochic measure]; come [to be] of wheat artabae 4 by [the Dochic measure]
3     only. [It] was written Epeiph 11, indiction 13. Kollouthos, [son] of Isaakios, confirms
4     to me the receipt. Through Phoibammon, [the] hired [scribe]
5     I undersigned.
