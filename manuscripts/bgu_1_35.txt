[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurel[i]us Didymus, strategus of Arsinoite,
2     [Heracleides'] division,
3     from Aurelius Stotoetes
4     of Erieios, [from] the village of Soknopaiou
5     Nesos. [From] the third [of the] present month Phaophi, my ox
6     [grazing] in the beach-area, and
7     my cowherd, rising at dawn, found it [murdered] by someone I know not. Wherefore
8     I submit these documents
9     and I request that it be registered, so that there remains for me the
10    claim against [the] ones who will be revealed
11    guilty. Farewell.
12    Year 2 of the Emperor Caesar Marcus
13    Aurelius Severus Alexander,
14    Pious Eu[ty]chous Augustus, Phaophi 10.
