5     … and attempt to snatch the remedies of the incorruptible most pure magistrates, and dare to furnish [in the courts false testim]onies [on their behalf], asking that … [be appropriated] to them the things that are not under them nor under their possession

7     … through … they ordained that the one who is in truth lord and master should make use of counter-petition libelli and give notice
8     [to the courts through the hands of the riparii] or of the defensores and public officials and [the collectors] of public taxes and those subserving
