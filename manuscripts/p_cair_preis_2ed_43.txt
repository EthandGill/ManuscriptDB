[TRANSLATION]
1     Issued document, of four drachmas four obols.
2     In the fifth year of Nero Claudius Caesar
3     Augustus Germanicus Imperator, Epeiph
4     … 9, in the city of Oxyrhynchus of the Thebaid.
5     Antiphanes son of Heraklas, of those from Oxyrhynchus
6     city, acknowledges to Tryphon son of Dionysios, of those from the same city, in the public street, that he has received from him
7     of Augustan and Ptolemaic silver coinage
8     one hundred and sixty drachmas of principal,
9     [which] he lent him [by contract] completed
10    [through the] record-office in the city of Oxyrhynchus
11    [in the] past month Pauni of the present
12    fifth year of Nero Claudius
13    Caesar Augustus Germanicus Imperator,
14    and that neither Antiphanes nor any other on his behalf shall proceed against, nor accuse, nor make any claim
15    against Tryphon nor his heirs concerning anything whatsoever up to the present day;
16    or, apart from the future proceeding being null
17    and void, Antiphanes or the one
18    proceeding on his behalf shall pay to Tryphon
19    or his heirs, at each proceeding, both the
20    damage and a penalty of one hundred silver drachmas, and to the public treasury
21    the same, and none the less without diminishing
22    Tryphon in the consequent
23    guarantee to Antiphanes, of the house which he bought
24    from him, in accordance with the
25    deed of registration made to him. The contract is valid.
