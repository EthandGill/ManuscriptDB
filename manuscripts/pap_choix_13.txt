[TRANSLATION]
1     Senpicos to Melanos his son very many [greetings].
2     I want you to know that your brother [Psenth]othes is assigned a voyage of grain [to Ale]xandria and has signed [and already]
3     receives the grain. Accordingly if [he] departs, I am not able to come to [you].
4     For I make bread for him and prepare
5     him. I greet Tape and
6     Apollinaris and [you all care for]
7     her because of [the] scorpion. Julius greets you.
8     If your brother departs,
9     I dye the wool and carry [it].
10    Farewell.
