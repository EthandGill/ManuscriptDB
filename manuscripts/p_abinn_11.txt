[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Abinnaeus
2     Louppicinus.
3     First of all I pray to divine providence that with good cheer to you
4     my letters may be delivered to you.
5     It has become necessary for me to disclose
6     to your virtue the things written
7     by the official of my lord the duke
8     Flavius Filicissimus so that
9     immediately upon receiving my letters
10    you may cause them to respond
11    at Leukokios as the letters contain. And concerning whatever you wish
12    to command me willingly being [well-disposed] toward
13    to my lord brother [Abinnaeus]
14    [Louppicinus]
