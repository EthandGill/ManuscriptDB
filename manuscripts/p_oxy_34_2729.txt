[TRANSLATION]
1     To my lord brother Akyleos, Dioskourides
2     greeting in the lord.
3     Before all things I pray to the Lord God
4     concerning your wholeness; I wonder how
5     up to today you have sent me no letter; whether bad or good news, give two silver (coins)
6     and write to me; convey then, through the boat
7     of Theodoros our bishop, through Elias
8     the sailor, one sambathon-jar holding 2700 myriads of silver,
9     likewise convey through the boat of Abanos, his own boat, another double knidion-jar, one of small change, holding
10    another 4 myriads; together the two vessels make …
11    1, and 700 myriads; likewise convey through the same boat of Abanos 2 centenaria of iron, so far holding the 2 portions, 48. Divide then, you
12    and my brother Dioskoros, and again I send
13    you other things; for their price today is 200 myriads of talents.
14    Nor is there any (left); in the night I put them into the
15    boat; likewise I dispatched to you, through the boat of Origenes
16    son of Heras, of wine, one omphakera (jar) of the first quality, so that
17    you may buy 25 omphakerai so far; for I have already paid him in jars as much as of their conveyance itself; give him nothing then; if you convey these, write to me; for I heard that
18    you and Ioannes have established (as) heads (of the office)
19    so then buy together, you and Dioskoros,
20    goods and send to me quickly.
21    But do not be negligent, my lord brother; together buy good goods
22    and send me goods, that is country pounds or pounds … or shell-pieces or
23    bronzeware or blades or, in a word, do not abandon me; idle
24    I sit alone; for I am in our storehouse; for I cannot
25    be moved from my storehouse; all the things wanted have been bought dear.
26    Now they are going round to the storehouses; the (price of the) pound of the … of silver ounces
27    25, the country (one) of the 13 ounces of silver 20, the shell-piece of the
28    1 … 38 myriads, the … 3 myriads …, the blade 50 myriads, and the bronzeware
29    of the 1 … of myriads of talents … and 100 talents; everything wanted has been done; send me
30    a downstream boat and I send you small change; the small coin today is 730 myriads; if it seems good to you, send me a blade of garum and one keramion of salt-fish; I greet warmly
31    Helene and Choous with their mother Hanou, and tell my brother Amys
32    that you should not think that I am your enemy; if you wish to send authority …;
33    I pray for your health.
34    Deliver to Akyleos, from Dioskourides his brother.
