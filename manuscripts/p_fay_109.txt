[TRANSLATION]
1   Pisais to Herakles, greeting. Whenever in need you wish
2   to use anything of mine, I do not at once hold you back; and now,
3   I beg you, the three staters that Seleukos told
4   you to give me — give them now to Kleon, reck-
5   oning that you are lending them to me, even if you must put your cloak
6   in pledge, because I have settled the account with
7   (my) father and he has struck me off the books, and I want to take
8   a receipt. For Seleukos has knocked them out of my hands here,
9   saying that you have entrusted them to himself.
10   And now, I beg you, reckoning that you are lending me
11   [them], do not hold Kleon up, and join
12   [wi]th [K]leon and ask Saras for the 12 dr.
13   [So do not do oth]erwise.
14   Year 20, Pa(uni) 25.
15   To Herakleros(?)
