1     There bought [J]ulius Demetrios, formerly a soldier of the 3rd Augustan cohort, dwelling in Rakoukaitha, from Otarnaios son of Abadabos of the village of Sacharedaaouarae, the land belonging to him lying within the boundaries of the same village, which he had bought from Habbeibas, his fellow-villager,
2     i[n the pl]ace called Zairadasacharae, of whatever measure it is, called Karkapha, for 175 denarii.
3     In the consulship of Nummius Albinus and Laelius Maximus, on the seventh day before the Kalends of June,
4     before the men appointed and sealed (as witnesses), in Sachare, at the winter-quarters
5     [of] the 3rd Augustan cohort of Thracians. There bought Julius Demetrios, formerly a so[l]dier of the
6     [aforewritten] cohort, dwelling in Rakoukaitha, from Otarnaios son of Abdabos of the village of Sach[a]redaaouarae, the land belonging to him lying within the boundaries of the same
7     village, of whatever measure it is, which he had bought from Habbeibas son of Bozanos, his fellow-villag[e]r, in Zairadasacharae, in the place called Karkaphtha, with entran[ce]
8     and exit and the trees in it, both fruit-bearing and unfruitfu[l], and every right of th[e]
9     sam[e] l[a]nd, at a price of one hundred and seventy-fi[ve]
10    silver denarii, which price the seller has received from the buyer; and the
11    land he gave to him, for him to have lawfully and securely for all time, to possess, use, sell, manage in whatever way he chooses; the neighbors of the same
12    land being: from the east the water-channel and the Aboura river; west the vineyard of the buyer; south Abdelath; north (the land) of the buyer himself
13    an[d Abdela]th; and if there is or has been any oversight in the boundary-descriptions, there shall be no dispute; and there were before this in the same land six hundred vine-stocks.
14    The seller furnishing to the buyer the same purchase free from claim
15    a[n]d unencumbered and undisputed against any dispute, an[d] cl[e]an from anyone making a counter-claim; but if he do not furnish (it) ac[cordingly,]
16    and if anyone, having intervened, evict the same purchase or par[t] of i[t, the sell]er shall l[i]tig[a]te and clea[r] (the title) for the buyer; and if not, he shall pay o[ut to him the]
17    pr[i]ce double and the damage likewise; the buyer rendering al[l the]
18    (charges) falling upon the same land, on account of the imperial (treasury) and the village-service.
19    This sale being valid wherever it is produced. In good faith asked the formal questi[on]
20    the buye[r,] an[d] in good faith acknowledged Otarnaios the sel[l]er.
21    I, Aurelius Salmames, a vete[r]an, having been asked, wro[te on behalf of Otarnaios son of Aba]dabos, being illiterate, who acknowled[ges that he has sold ]the land [belonging to him] and has received de[narii one hu]nd[red and seven]ty-fi[v]e [and that he con]sents according to the aforewritten.
