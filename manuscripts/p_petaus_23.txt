[TRANSLATION]
1     [I have transmitted (this).] Gemellos, assistant, Phamenoth …
2     To Apollonios, strategos of the Arsinoite, division of Herakleides
3     division,
4     from Alexandros son of Sarapion
5     son of Eirenaios, a minor, through his guardian Herakleides son of Kallistratos …
6     I wish to purchase from the dry
7     land in reserve, in which vines … , for
8     the planting of vegetable gardening (?) according to the schedule, near the village of Pharbaitha, one aroura …, of which
9     the neighbours on the south and north and west are private plots
10    on the east dry land, at the fixed price
11    [of each aroura at … drachmas,]
12    and of these accompanying drachmas
13    on condition that, being assigned it, I shall pay
14    the price and the accompanying (charges) to the
15    public bank. And there shall remain to me and
16    to my descendants and to those who succeed from me the possession of this
17    and inalienable ownership for the
18    [perpetual] time, and it shall be guaranteed
19    [to me clear from all public (charges) and] every kind (of due) up to the time of the [assignment.]
20    [But I shall have, for the working and reclamation,] exemption for 3 years,
21    after which I shall pay the (charges) upon the like (plots)
22    the dues, if it appears fit to make the assignment.
23    I, Alexandros, a minor, through my guardian
24    the same Herakleides, have submitted (this).
25    Year 2 … [of Marcus] Aurelius Commodus Antoninus
26    Au[gustus Pi]ous, Phamenoth 15.
