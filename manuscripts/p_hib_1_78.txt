[TRANSLATION]
1     Nikias to Argaiōs greetings. [Many] times I have written to you concerning
2     Zōilos and Praximahos when
3     a public service falls [upon them] to release them, and you have never obeyed us. Yet now
4     [let it] be careful for you to release them from the now [service] to Alabastron city
5     because [the service] not to fall upon them …
6     now to serve, and if
7     from the Oxyrhynchite [they] select [him], release Zōilos
8     but if from the Koite [they] select [him], release Praximahos; but if you are not able to release [them], write
9     to me, and how […] he will not receive
10    the letter from Dōriōnos without
11    me so-that through me the command to the people
12    might be given.
13    Farewell.
14    [Year] […] …
15    Year 4, concerning Zōi[los].
