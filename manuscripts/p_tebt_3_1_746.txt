[TRANSLATION]
1     Agathon to Patron, greeting. Of the letter from Menodo[ros]
2     I have subjoined for you the copies, that
3     you may know. Farewell. Year 4, Pharmouthi 8.
4     Menodoros to Agathon, greeting. Of the letter which we have written to the collectors according to the toparchy, we have subjoined for you the copies, so that, knowing (them), you may instruct those
5     appointed by you according to locality to release, from
6     the produce, the (amounts) measured out to the
7     grain-collectors for the debts subjoined in them,
8     but to hold together the remaining grain, just as we have written to you
9     also in the previous letters.
10    Year 4, Pharmouthi 6.
11    To Theophilos. Since the reception of the royal
12    plots has begun and the grain-collectors have been appointed for you,
13    I supposed it well to write to you that, of the
14    cavalry-plots, reserving the sufficient grain
15    [for] the money-debts owed of the earlier
16    times [and for] the money-revenue …
17    until [we report] to Phanias concerning [these matters],
18    from … accruing from the fitting
19    half-plots into the royal treasury, you should measure out to the grain-collectors the grain-debts subjoined in them,
20    and likewise also the (dues) paid, both the medical-tax
21    and the crown of the ten-day (period) and from the whole
22    plot and the guard-tax of the plots. And if anything
23    be left over from these half-plots, let it be a care
24    to you that this be kept, as well as the
25    half-plot due to the cavalryman, until the counting
26    and until we write to you what must be done,
27    but the purchased (grain), of which they hold in advance the price …
28    … as we previously
29    instructed you. And if any of the cavalrymen
30    have given a written undertaking to the epistatai concerning the
31    produce, hold back the crown-(dues) due
32    of the 4th year, of the amount falling short,
33    [until] … to report to Phanias.
34    … Farewell.
35    [The] same (letter) to Harpalos, Antiphanes, Argaios.
36    Year 4, Pharmouthi 8, concerning
37    the measuring-out of the half-plots
38    … and …
39    To Patron.
