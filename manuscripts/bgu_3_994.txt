[TRANSLATION]
1     Year 4, Mesore 11. There sold,
2     Tathotis, cubits of solid (ground),
3     5, of which to Taeloloues 3,
4     to Petearsemtheus 2, total 5,
5     of copper, 5,000.
6     In the reign of Cleopatra and King Ptolemy, the Mother-loving, Saviour Gods,
7     year 4, the priest of King Ptolemy, the god Mother-loving, Saviour, being Alexandros, and
8     of the Saviour Gods and the Brother Gods and the Benefactor Gods and the Father-loving Gods and
9     the Manifest Gods and the god Eupator and the god Mother-loving and the god the New Father-loving and
10    the god Benefactor and the Mother-loving Saviour Gods; the temple-attendant of great Isis,
11    mother of the gods; the prize-bearer of Berenike the Benefactress; the basket-bearer of Arsinoe the Brother-loving;
12    the priestess of Arsinoe the Father-loving — these being at Alexandria;
13    and at Ptolemais of the Thebaid, the priests and priestesses and basket-bearer being those who are
14    and who exist at Ptolemais, in the month Pauni 11, at Pathyris, Heliodoros being agoranomos.
15    There sold Tathotis daughter of Phibis, a Persian, about 50 years old, of medium (stature), honey-coloured, long-faced,
16    straight-nosed, with her guardian, her own elder son Patseous, the son of [Pa]tes, a Persian
17    of the epigone, from the vacant plot belonging to her which is in the western
18    part of Pathyris, 5 cubits of solid (ground), in the northern
19    part; the neighbours of these: on the south, the vacant plot of the same (woman),
20    on the north, the house of Sennesis; on the east, a road; on the west, the vacant plot
21    of the temple, or whoever the neighbours may be. There bought Taeloloues son of Totoes
22    and Petearsemtheus son of Panobchounis, of which to Taeloloues 3,
23    to Petearsemtheus two cubits, total 5 cubits, of copper 5,000. The fore-seller and warrantor is Tathotis the seller, whom Taeloloues and Petearsemtheus the buyers accepted.
24    I, Ammonios, the (agent) of Sosos, have transacted (the business).
25    Year 4, Mesore 30. There has been paid into the bank in Krokodilon Polis, over which Apollonios (presides), [the deposit]
26    of the ten-percent transfer-tax, according to the assessment from Paniskos the tax-farmer, under which there [signs]
27    Apollonios the counter-signer: Taeloloues son of Totoes and Petearsemtheus son of Panobchou[nis],
28    of a vacant plot of 5 cubits at Pathyris, of which to Taeloloues [3] cubits [and] Petearsemtheus 2 cubits,
29    which they bought from Tathotis daughter of Phibis, of copper 5,000, tax 500.
30    Apollonios the banker, 600.
