# HGV-EN: From the day-book of Apion, collector of money-taxes at Talao. For naubion and the tax of 1/6 and freight by water for the present 4th year 22 dr. 1/2 ob., for land-tax 109 dr. 3 chalci, for a libation to Dionysus(?) 8 dr. 4 ob. 1 chal. Total 139 dr. 5 ob. Paid 139 dr. 6 ob. Total one hundred and thirty-nine dr. 6 obols. The 4th year, Pauni 5.
