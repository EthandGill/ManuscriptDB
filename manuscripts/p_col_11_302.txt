r.1    καταλαβὼν τὴν Ἀλεξανδρέων ἔγνων τὸν εὐλαβέστατον ἐπίσκοπον ἀποπλεῦσαι

[TRANSLATION]
1     Having arrived at Alexandria, I learned the most reverent bishop sailed away
2     to Pelusium; accordingly, your magnificence, being customarily well-regarded and knowing
3     the power of the order, will you order him to be reminded with the
4     others toward the … from Thebaid to find the matter
5     ready, so that I too will customarily give thanks to your magnificence
6     and the consecrated men will likewise proclaim [your]
7     greatness.
