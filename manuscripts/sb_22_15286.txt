1     [Aurelius An]tás, [son of Th]eon, acknowledges
2     mother Tekiasis, from the village of Andromachis, aged twenty-one, with a scar on the ankle of the left foot, carpenter, to Fl[avius]
3     Timotheos, soldier of the fort[s]
4     of Dionysias, [that he] has from him
5     the acknowledged Antán
6     altogether, which you also measured,
7     for necessary use of me wheat
8     six artabae, amounting to six artabae, just as they were in the village Pisaei
9     as also I will provide them
10    in the same village Pisaei
11    whose repayment … [illegible]
12    I will make necessary for you
13    in the month [Pauni] with measure
14    tetrachoinikal of the fortunate seventh indiction
15    without delay, and upon
16    the demand being made, the execution [shall be] to Timotheos from the acknowledging Antán and from all my property, just as [from] right [/judgment]
17    and having been questioned, I acknowledged.
18    in the consulship of Flavius Claudius and
19    Flavius Nevietta of the most illustrious [of the equestrian] rank.
20    Aurelius Antás the aforementioned
21    acknowledge [having received] the [artabae] of wheat
22    [acceptable] artabae six and I will repay as aforementioned. Aurelius Kopres
23    [son] of Heron [wrote] on behalf of him
24    [being] illiterate.
