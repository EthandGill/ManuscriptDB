[TRANSLATION]
1     The priests of Aphrodite to Apollonius, [the dioiketes], greetings. Just as the king
2     has written to you to give for the burial [of Hesis] myrrh, one hundred talents,
3     you would do well to order [it to be given. For] you are not unaware that Hesis
4     into the nome if we do not have ready [the things that are n]eeded, whatever have need
5     for the burial, on account of the same day …. Know moreover that Hesis is Hesis;
6     may she give you favor towards [the king]. Farewell. Year 28, Hathur 15.
