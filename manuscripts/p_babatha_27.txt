[TRANSLATION]
1     In the consulship of Gaius Serrius Augurinus and Publius Trebius [Serg]i[anu]s, [be]fore
2     the fourteenth of the Kalends of Septem[b]er, and according to the reckoning of the new pro[v]ince of Arabia in the twenty-seventh year, in the month Gorpiaios, on the firs[t, i]n Maoza
3     in the district of Zoora. Babatha daughter of Simon, with her [guardian] present and
4     subscribing on her behalf, Babelis son of Manaem, both of the same Maoza, to Simon the hunchback son of John son of Egla, [of th]e sam[e] Maoza, greeting. You having been appointed second guardian … b[y the council of the Petr]aeans of Jesus son of Jesus the orphan, my s[on,] I have rec[eiv]ed fr[o]m y[o]u on account [of main]tenance and clothing of the same Jesus my s[on] six denarii of silver fr[o]m the month
5     Panemos the first of the same twenty-seventh year up to Gorpi[aio]s the thirtieth, three complete months.
6     Interpretation (of) Babatha daughter of Simon: I have received from Simon the hunchback son of John,
7     guardian of Jesus my son, on accou[n]t of maintenance and clothing of him,
8     six denarii of silver from the month Panemos the first up to Gorpiaios the thirtieth, of the
9     twenty-seventh year, which are [complete] three months. [Through] her [guard]ian Babelis son of Manaem.
10    I, Germanos son of Judah, wrote (this).
