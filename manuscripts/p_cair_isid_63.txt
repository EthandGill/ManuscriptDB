[TRANSLATION]
1     To Aurelius Gordianus, beneficiarius on station,
2     from Aurelia Taesis daughter of Kopres, from the village
3     of Karanis. Having suffered violence and an un[law]ful assault, … of our (property) … by my
4     paternal uncle Chairemon
5     from the same village of K[a]ranis, unable to bear which I have come to the redress of the laws. Left an infant by my parents,
6     I remained with my maternal uncle Ammonios,
7     being maintained (by him), my paternal (property) having been plundered and held fast by the
8     aforesaid Chairemon, on which account, while still being of tender age, I kept quiet,
9     but by the providence of God, having come of age, I was constrained to approach the same
10    and aforesaid Chairemon concerning the paternal (property) which he holds fast of mine. And once and a second time
11    when I had appealed to the beneficiarius entrusted with the
12    decarchy, he also ordered him to give back what he holds in (his) bosom, but he did not [give it back].
13    Yesterday, which was the 24th, his daughters,
14    Kyrilloun and Tasoucharion and Taesis and T…,
15    and Thatres their mother, … …
16    having beaten me with [blow]s, tearing out my hair …
17    and tearing my [cl]othing, threw me on the ground
18    (and) left (me), in the presence of Hol and Kasios, public (servants) of the
19    same village, who dragged me away from the [wom]en
