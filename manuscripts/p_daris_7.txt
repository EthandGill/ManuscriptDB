[TRANSLATION]
1     When Stephanos, the teacher of dancing-and-wrestling, had come in and was saying that he had been paid his wages by the other magistrates in office, only the high-priest not having given (his share), they cried out: "His wages, high-priest! The garland is not free of charge!" Dionysodoros, the high-priest in office, said: "He will testify for you." They cried out through the middle …: "Let him serve as market-overseer; there is no need of the office." When the high-priest had risen and through the spectators
2     had come to the middle of the theater, they cried out: "Is[idoros,]
3     violence is free speech! What is this grudging of the peace? The (one) of the re[su]rrection … you have him! Strategos, (the city) being at peace … the city, do not divide (it)!"
4     "What is this grudging of the peace? Let a charge of impiety be registered for [an]other day! The lord[s]
5     the high-priest abandoned"; while the lords were sitting in session
6     the same man rose: "There is no need of the office!"
7     "(He claims) a privilege for himself from us, the same man, by way of spectacle."
8     After other things spoken: the prytanis said: "…
9     … to the most excellent council, with the lawful …
10    … to consider his case; but the wages of Stephanos
11    shall by all means be exacted by me and by the strategos."
12    "Okeanos, prytanis! Worthy council of the lords!
13    Pure, faithful Isidoros! Noble magistrates!"
