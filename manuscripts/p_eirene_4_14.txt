[TRANSLATION]
1     Heraiscas and Melas to Cheleius, greetings.
2     You do well, not hesitating concerning the important matters
3     us. For you made us more cheerful, [having sent] us these your letters.
4     They said … the village-heads to say that we paid Isidorus pa … drachmas t, but the remainder to the former—
5     [in the] month of Pauni, drachmas [s] and of Castor which …
6     us drachmas [one hundred eight]. Securely then have [the] threshing-floors
7     of Amesysus, and make clear to us the quan[tity]
8     of the ar … n … osia …
9     And indeed we are pressing upon them p …
10    Give to Cheleius from the ten-first [magistrates]
