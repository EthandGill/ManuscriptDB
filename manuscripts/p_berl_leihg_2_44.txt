[TRANSLATION]
1     The undersigned men, public and domain-land and revenue-land
2     farmers of the village of Theadelphia and of part of the fields of other
3     villages, in the most fortunate times of the greatest Emperor
4     Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Augustus
5     Pius, and toward the setting right of the rents falling due both from us and
6     from our fellow-farmers,
7     these being no small grain-rents, both of crops and money-taxes,
8     we request and we abide by the custom, prevailing from still earlier and
9     up to now, established by our ancestral
10    god Souchos Pnepheros, in
11    his festival days of the present 21st year
12    of Antoninus Caesar the lord, designating both the men
13    set in charge in the village for the
14    occurring (festival) presences, and as helpers of both us and
15    our fellow-farmers, and (we do) everything so that not one whit less
16    accrue to the public treasury, but rather that the imperial
17    accounts increase.
18    Herakleides, about 40 years old, scar on the right ankle.
19    Horion, about 35 years old, scar in the middle of the face.
20    Aretion, about 35 years old, scar on a finger of the left hand.
21    Heron, about 50 years old, scar on the forehead.
22    Panouphis, about 50 years old, scar on a finger of the left hand.
23    Papeis, about 38 years old, scar on a finger of the right hand.
24    Heron the other, about 35 years old, scar on a finger of the left hand.
25    Harsytheus, about 32 years old, scar on a finger of the left hand.
26    Apion, about 39 years old, scar on a finger of the left hand.
27    Isidoros son of Nikandros: I abide by all the foregoing,
28    as set forth above.
