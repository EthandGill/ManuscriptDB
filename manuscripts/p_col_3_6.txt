[TRANSLATION]
1     Simale, mother of Herophantos, to Zenon, greeting. Having heard that my little boy had been ill-treated, and rather severely, I came to you, and on arriving I wished to meet with you concerning
2     these same matters. But since Olympichos prevented me from seeing you, I was carried in to
3     the child as best I then could, and I found him in a most pitiable condition and already [ ]; it was enough for me, seeing him, to be grieved. But Olympichos, coming up, said that he would
4     beat him until he made him rotten — or had already done so — though he was already nearly grown up. I therefore beg you
5     and entreat you to give attention to these matters and to report to Apollonios in what
6     manner my child has been continually outraged by Olympichos, so that he has even become partly responsible for his illness. For I, besides not having received anything for a year now
7     except the mina and three artabas of grain in the month of Dystros, in which Herophantos comes
8     to you. The little boy himself reports to me Apollonios' goodwill and your own, which you continue to have toward him. I therefore request you, and at the same time beg, that if Apollonios has assigned any provision-allowance to be paid to him, it be paid to me. And know that, as soon as
9     God releases him, I will bring him to you, so that I may also see you concerning the rest. As for the rest,
10    inquire of the man bringing you the letter; for he is no stranger to us.
11    Farewell.
12    From Simale, the mother of Herophantos.
13    Year 28, Peritios 6.
14    In Berenike's Harbor.
15    To Zenon.
