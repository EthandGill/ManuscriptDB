[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the reign of our most divine] a[n]d most pious master Fl[avi]us Just[ini]a[n], the e[t]e[r]n[a]l A[ugus]tus
2     [and Empe]ror, year 25, in the 9th (year) after the consulship of Flavius Basilius the most illustrious, Me[s]ore 14
3     of the 14th indiction, beginning of the 15th, in the city of Oxyrhynchus.
4     [To the] most admirable Menas, son of the blessed Praous, overseer of the holy catholic church
5     [o]f the city of the Oxyrhynchites: the Aurelii Phoibammon and Elias, full brothers, sons of (their) father
6     [Anou]p, their mother being Tekrampe, and Pantaros, son of Phib, his mother being Rachel, the three originating from the estate
7     [of Lithi]ne of the Oxyrhynchite nome belonging to the same holy catholic church, greeting.
8     [We ackno]wledge by willing intention and free choice, swearing by God Almighty
9     [and] the piety of the victorious fortune (of the emperor), that we stand surety for and undertake before your Admirableness
10    [the Aure]lius Onnophris, son of Pamouthius the late deacon, his mother being Thekla, himself also originating
11    [from the sa]me estate, and before this remaining with her in the rank of a freedman-slave, on condition that
12    we cause him to give to your Admirableness the remainder of her
13    gold which was stolen by him in your house a few days ago, and whenever he is required of us by your Admirableness on any day on account of this matter, we shall bring and hand him over
14    publicly in this city, outside holy precincts and divine images
15    and every place of refuge, where also we received him in the prison of the
16    hospital of the same holy church, without any account being given to him
17    or to be given, for this one matter only; or, if we do not do this, we acknowledge,
18    on the basis of joint liability, we his sureties, that we are answerable in all things required against him
19    [to res]pond, having pledged for the right of this surety
20    [a]ll our property, present and future, specifically and generally, by way of pledge and
21    [mort]gage by right. The surety, written in single copy, is authoritative, and being formally questioned we gave our assent.
22    [Au]relii Phoibammon and Elias, full brothers, sons of (their) father Anoup, and Pantaros,
23    [son of Ph]ib, the aforewritten, have made this surety, undertaking for the same Onnophris
24    [on al]l the matters mentioned above in this surety, at the risk of ourselves and our
25    [substan]c[e, and] all things agree with us as set forth above, and being formally questioned we gave our assent and released (it). I, Apollos
26    [son of Apanakios,] having been requested, wrote on their behalf, they being illiterate.
27    Through me, Victor, it was completed … 14th (year), indiction 14.
