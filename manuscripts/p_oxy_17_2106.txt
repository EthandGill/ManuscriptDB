[TRANSLATION]
1     … to the magistrates and council and
2     … to the … curator of the Oxyrhynchite, greeting.
3     The divine and [august fortune of] our masters the Emperors and [the Caesars] has commanded, by divine letters sent to
4     me, that gold be bought up from the province. Let it be your concern, since the matter admits
5     of no postponement; for it has been ordered that within the 4th
6     of the month Thoth all the gold be delivered at Nicomedia, within 30 days, that is, within the
7     5th of Mesore, and in this, showing your
8     devotion, to exact 38 pounds only
9     from each according to his ability and to bring them to the splendid city of the Alexandrians, namely with you the curator and one of you who holds the
10    first rank in the magistracy conveying these, those contributing to you in this levy being both those who
11    deal in the substance of gold and those who
12    are the most powerful, while the
13    foreigners are not troubled, unless indeed they established their household there and have not yet entered the citizenship and happen to be well-off, ten myriads of course being reckoned for each pound to those
14    who supply it by the most sacred treasury. And so that
15    on the spot the value of these may readily
16    be paid down to them, I have written to my lord
17    and brother the most distinguished rationalis, that he himself
18    may order the procurators to do this.
19    I pray for your good health. After which, Latin.
