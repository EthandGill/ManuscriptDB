r.1    Νεμεσιανὸς Σεουήρῳ χαίρειν.

r.10   ἔτους ζ Παχὼν λ.

[TRANSLATION]
1     Nemesianus to Severus, greetings.
2     Give to Silvanus the manager for the account of works,
3     of the month Pachon of wine, product of the 6th year of the estate
4     of Colluthus, the wine-press, 2 jars 16, and of wheat [product]
5     of the 5th year, 12 artabas, of which the prices Serapiades [sent],
6     the handler is to record the wine
7     as of the jar 1 drachmas 12, and of the wheat as of the artaba 1 drachmas 16.
8     Be well and give the wheat artabas 12,
9     and wine jars 16; total artabas 12 jars 16.
10    Year 7 Pachon 30.
