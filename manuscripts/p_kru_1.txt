[TRANSLATION]
1     In the name of the holy and
2     life-giving, consubstantial Trinity,
3     the Father and the Son and the
4     Holy Spirit. Written in the month
5     Phamenoth 1, of the 3rd indiction.
6     To the most illustrious
7     and honourable count Pshen-Chael,
8     the administrator of the fortress of Djeme.
9     We, Pesate the son of the blessed
10    Philotheos, acting as representative(s)
11    for all our brethren according to their names,
12    together with Stephanos the son of Damianos, the man of the fortress of Djeme
13    in the nome of the city Hermonthis,
14    we give, and afterwards a subscriber
15    to subscribe on our behalf
16    to this written deed of sale,
17    so that it is not transgressed and not disturbed by the laws, but rather
18    that it is confirmed through trustworthy witnesses, these
19    who shall witness to it on our behalf
20    according to our request, our very own,
21    … if not, then through our authority,
22    we write to you, to you, on our behalf:
23    the son of the blessed Senouthios,
24    he who exists, according to the sale
25    which is written and confirmed by us.
26    I have set my intent upon it, willing(ly)
27    and persuaded, without any deceit or fear or extortion
28    or fraud or imposition
29    or registration; there is no compulsion laid upon us at all, but rather
30    with every good and fine
31    purpose, we swearing afterwards by the holy, consubstantial Trinity, the Father and the Son
32    and the salvation of our lords the kings, those who rule over us through
33    the command of God
34    the Almighty, we give
35    to you, to you, on our behalf, O son
36    of the blessed Senouthios,
37    he who exists, from now until
38    ever, and for every time
39    forever, henceforth, to you and after you
40    the whole estate (chorema), the field-(parcels?) which belong to us in
41    the fortress of Djeme, on the street that is called "of the Pailakine,"
42    this whose every boundary I shall make known,
43    according to its ancient positions, which are these: the north, the land
44    which you took, you, on our behalf,
45    from the heirs
46    of the holy Abba Patermouthios,
47    the south side, that of Pahor the son of Pesate, the east the street of Belle, on the south,
48    and the place where the door of the owner opens, the west, that of Herhire,
49    on the south. Behold, these are its boundaries,
50    of every kind. And the price which we agreed upon with one another, and
51    it pleased us, of the two parts (shares),
52    namely two holokottinoi
53    of gold, total: 2 gold solidi, pure,
54    full standard of the measure of the fortress of Djeme, the complete
55    price, came into our hands
56    from your hands, paid in full and complete
57    in the measure of the fortress of Djeme.
58    So therefore upon you, you, on our behalf, O son of Senouthios,
59    he who exists, you shall come in
60    and take hold and become the lord of all that whole estate, you and your children and those who come after you, according to the apportionment which we have set out to declare,
61    from above, to beget it for you for re-begetting it
62    for you, to administer it, to manage
63    it, to embellish it,
64    to dispose of it, to exchange it,
65    to take it back unto (yourself), to do with it every manner that is in your (power), once
66    and simply; for no person
67    shall be able to come against you ever,
68    neither brother nor sister nor
69    son nor near-kin of near-kin; and whoever shall come
70    against you, in the first place,
71    that person shall gain nothing,
72    but rather he shall be(come) a stranger
73    to the holy oath of the Christians,
74    the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit;
75    and he shall pay the penalty of ten holokottinoi of gold,
76    he shall come in and approach
77    this sale which we have established firm for you; we have established this purchase, authentic, firm, valid in
78    every place where it shall be produced,
79    whenever they shall question, and we shall acknowledge; it was read aloud to us,
80    we heard it, we consented to it,
81    we confirmed it; subscriber
82    and witness; we have set it
83    forth, law and due order.
84    I, Pesate the son of Phib,
85    … Phar … he … am …
86    consenting to the sale according to her capacity. I, Souai,
87    the son of Petros, they having requested of me,
88    I have writ[ten] on their behalf, since they do not know
89    how to write.
90    I, Iohannes the son …
91    I, Souai the son of Petros, they having requested of me, I have written
92    on their behalf, since they do not know how to write.
93    I, Petre the son …
94    Damnos and Kostatinos the son of Pakire, I bear witness.
