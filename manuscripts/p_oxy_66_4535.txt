1     In the name of the Lord and Master
2     Jesus Christ, our God and Savior.
3     In the reign of our most divine and
4     most pious master, greatest benefact[o]r
5     Flavius Mauricius Novus Tiberius the
6     eternal Augustus and Emperor, in the year
7     18, in the consulship of the same our most pious
8     master, in the year 17, T[yb]i 18, in the third
9     indiction.
10    To Flavius Apollos the respectable
11    comes of the divine consistory
12    and administrator of the all-praiseworthy
13    Strategios the consul, son of Phoibammon
14    of illustrious memory,
15    landholder here in the illustrious
16    city of the Oxyrhynchites. Aurelius Elias
17    son of apa Nakios, his mother being Isis,
18    originating fr[om the ham]let of Sikeon
19    B … kar … of the Ox[yrhyn]chite nome,
20    registered fa[rme]r of your
21    lordship, ackno[w]led[ge that I owe] to it
22    and [ow]e [purely and]
23    fu[lly, on account of pu]blic
24    go[ld-taxes] … as well
25    … and of the first
26    past indictions,
27    of gold six nomism[a]tia, less
28    twen[t]y-fo[u]r keratia
29    by the private standard, making of gold 6 nomismatia less 24 keratia
30    by the private standard, and these I acknowledge to provide
31    to your lor[ds]hip whenever it should wish
32    without delay, at the risk of the property
33    belonging to me being liable
34    for this. Valid is the note, drawn up singly and
35    having been formally questioned I acknowledged. Aurelius Elias son of
36    apa Nakios, [the] aforementioned,
37    have made this note of the
38    six nomismatia less twenty-four keratia
39    as is set out above. Papnouthios, I wrote
40    on his behalf, he being illiterate.
41    Note of Elias son of [apa Naki]o[s from the hamlet of] Sikeon B … kar,
42    of gold 6 nomismatia less k[er]atia [24] p[rivate standard].
