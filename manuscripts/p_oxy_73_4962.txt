[TRANSLATION]
1     Ammonius to Diodorus his brother
2     much joy.
3     Your brother has been appointed village-scribe of S…. Since formerly
4     we served you without sloth, I think
5     and now [this will] please the brother; so that
6     therefore before I am drawn aside into another matter, make this clear to me.
7     I wish you to be well. Mesore 18.
8     Deliver to Diodorus from Ammonius [friend]
