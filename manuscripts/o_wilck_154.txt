[TRANSLATION]
1   Antiochos Kasianus and those with him, overseers
2   of the Sacred Gate of Syene, through Phanophis the assistant. Paid: Petorzmethis
3   son of Harpaesis, mother Markia, for the poll-tax of the sixteenth
4   year of Hadrian the lord: seventeen drachmas (and) a half-obol, (and) ½ obol (keepers' fee) — total 17 dr. 1 ob.,
5   and for the assessment of the head-tax of the men who had withdrawn, of the 15th year, one drach[ma (and) an obol (and) 3 chalkoi].
6   [Year 16 of Hadr]ian the lord, Mesore 21.
