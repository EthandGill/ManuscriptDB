r.1    Πορεγέβθις καὶ Ἱέραξ πράκτορες ἀργυρικῶν Μεμνονείων διὰ Πορεγέβθιος
r.2    Πετεχώνσει νεωτέρῳ Ψενμώνθου Σαχομνέως. ἔσχομεν ὑπὲρ λαογραφίας κβ ἔτους δραχμὰς ιβ. ἔτους κβ

[TRANSLATION]
1     Poregbethis and Hierax, tax-collectors of silver of Memnonia through Poregbethis
2     To Petechonsei the younger, [son] of Psenmonthos, of Sachomnaios. We received on behalf of the poll-tax, 22nd year, drachmas 12. 22nd year
3     Of Hadrian Caesar the lord, Pauni 24th. Likewise Epeiph 24th
4     Through Psensen helper, drachmas 4. Likewise, 2nd year of Antoninus Caesar
5     The lord, Phaophi 5, on behalf of the poll-tax, 1st year, drachmas eight, total drachmas 8.
