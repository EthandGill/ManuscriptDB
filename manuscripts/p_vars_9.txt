1     In the year twenty-third of the Emperor
2     Caesar the lord, Mecheir 27, [he/she] cancelled
3     Mechautis [son of] Serapion and Sōteriōs, tax-collectors of Kerkesephis
4     Herious [son of] Akeios, villager of the already-passed [year]
5     twenty-second year, copper 300
6     additionally assessed 35, exchange 10. Of the twenty-first year likewise
7     copper 300, additionally assessed 35, exchange 10, apart from division
8     Of Kriou drachma one [and a] half-obol apart from symbolic [fee]
9     Twenty year likewise villager copper 300, additionally assessed 35, exchange 10
10    in good order
