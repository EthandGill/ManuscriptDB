[TRANSLATION]
1     … [to Ze]non greetings. We have ceased cutting the vine on the 28th
2     [and the account I have] written to you of [the amount] spent on the cutting and not [received by me for] an expense, and by name I have written to you the workers
3     … choes drachmas 2 obol, to Panis drachmas 2 diobol coppers 2 and to the son drachmas 2
4     diobol coppers 2, to Hora triobol, Penemas
5     … The two vineyards have been cut for you [with] few coppers.
6     Farewell. Year 35, Tybi 11.
7     To Zenon.
