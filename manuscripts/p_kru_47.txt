[TRANSLATION]
1     [In the name of the Fa]ther and the Son and the Holy Spirit. [Written in the month] …
2     [ ] I, Daniel son of the blessed Esidoros, of [the] fortress
3     of Djeme of the nome of the city of Hermont, asking afterward
4     for a subscriber to subscribe for me, with other witnesses who are trustworthy to witness to this document
5     of an equal-form division which is written, not transgressing
6     it and not shaking it
7     through the laws. I write to Takoum daughter
8     of the blessed Zacharia, of this same fortress of Djeme
9     of the nome of the city [of Hermont] …
10    the person of our lord the most renowned amir … sale
11    of the house and that courtyard, since the decision came about
12    in this way according to how we persuaded one another, so that you become lord of the share of the house and the share of the courtyard,
13    you Takoum, whom I first named,
14    and that I become lord of the whole other share of the house and that courtyard,
15    from their foundations to their walls; and behold, the share
16    of the price of the house and the courtyard came to my hand from [you] …
17    so that I take that share of the house, of the north, I,
18    Daniel, the one writing first, with the share of the courtyard that is below
19    it, in every place I shall wish, and that courtyard,
20    so that I take the share of that house, you Takoum,
21    of the south; so that I take the share of the courtyard, if they assess together the portion that is to the north and that of the south, if I leave the lord … the price …
22    … the receiving … and the price in this way
23    … [wi]th that courtyard. Now the south
24    of the children of the blessed Antonios, and the door of the chiefs;
25    the north [Kos]tantinos; the east the heirs of Epiphaneios;
26    the west the courtyard belonging to us of the two portions; and
27    the street above. These are their borders, on every side, going around
28    from their foundations to their walls, with their appurtenances
29    that pertain to them. From now then you, Takoum
30    daughter of Zacharia, whom I have first named,
31    many times, may come in to take hold,
32    … and be lord of the share of the courtyard
33    and the whole share of the house, according to the way that I first
34    declared it … [so that] you acquire it,
35    again acquire them, administer them,
36    manage them, beautify them,
37    dwell in it, make it for security, donate
38    it, cede them, make them an exchange,
39    give them out, grant them, bestow
40    them, leave them to your children, give them
41    to your heirs, use them in any
42    fashion that pleases you …
43    mastery … …
44    that whoever shall come forth against you ever, neither I [nor]
45    brother nor son nor daughter nor [any] man at all shall bring suit
46    … …
47    against you concerning the share of the house and the whole courtyard, according to the way
48    I gave it, on the asking … according to the way
49    … in a court or outside
50    a court, concerning the half portion of the house
51    and the courtyard. First, may that one
52    profit nothing primarily; he shall be a stranger
53    to the Father and the Son and the [Hol]y Spirit,
54    and he shall give to the account a penalty to the authority
55    that exists now, thirty-six gold solidi,
56    and afterward he shall come in and oppose this division
57    that is written. It being secured for you, I have established it, secured, having force
58    in every place where it shall be produced, that they not interrogate me, what I acknowledge—plainly concerning the sale
59    which she drew up for me with her brethren; if I bring it forth against you
60    it shall become invalid, because you fulfilled
61    for me the whole share of that sale, which is these
62    six solidi—plainly that I shall wall the door
63    against this portion of the south, and the street of the south.
64    I, Johannes, the least presbyter,
65    am witness. I, Phil[…]s,
66    the least deacon, am witness.
67    I, Isak son of Kostantine,
68    am witness.
69    I, Phibamon, the least presbyter, wrote for him, since
70    he does not understand how to write, and I am witness.
71    Through me, Aristophanes son of Johannes, it was made.
