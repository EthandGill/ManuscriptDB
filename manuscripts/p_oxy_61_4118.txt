[TRANSLATION]
1     From Pekyllus
2     to the village-headmen of Dositheus.
3     Many functionaries sought [a]re being stolen by you
4     and for the honor of the village
5     still I bear. Take care then
6     and deliver the sought-one to the [headman]
7     of Psoptha, lest you bear the public service. But
8     also the others take care [to deliver]
9     to deliver. For as I said before, to the epistrategos they have not come for the honor of the village, not of you.
10    I have noted [this].
