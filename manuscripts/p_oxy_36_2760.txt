[TRANSLATION]
1     To Titius [Taeus] Sanctus, prefect of Egypt
2     From [Dionysius] Amyntianus, discharged from the [cavalry] of the Apriana squadron, by order of Pactumaeus Magnus [the]
3     [one who was] leader and of the most excellent camp-commander Cominius [Atticus], I conveyed [them] here from the [Oxyrhynchite]
4     nome under transmission from the strategos [for] needs of the Second legion
5     Traiana Ischua, linen-garments by number
6     [seven hundred] seventy-five
7     [Since] now already forty days and [toward] here I have been detained and not yet the receipt
8     of them has occurred, so that I also have been abandoned
9     and those with me of provisions, I entreat
10    you, if it seems good to you by fortune, to order
11    that when you examine to receive [these] in order that I may be able also, of the
12    season of sowing having come,
13    to [the] [own] to depart in order that I may be [benefited]. Fare well. Dionysius
14    [Amyntianus] [I] have submitted [this]
