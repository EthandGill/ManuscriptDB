[TRANSLATION]
1     Marcus Aurelius Isidoros, optio, to Asklep[iades]
2     and Pambekis, receivers of grain, greeting.
3     Deliver to Peteesis the soldier, on my behalf,
4     of wheat, an artaba and a sixth, total wheat one and two-thirds of an artaba,
5     until the reckoning of the account. Year 22, Phamenoth 10.
6     Bekis the younger: of grain instead of barley,
7     6 (artabas),
8     I have signed.
