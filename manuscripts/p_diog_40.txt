[TRANSLATION]
1     In year 25 of Marcus Aurelius
2     Severus Antoninus
3     Parthicus Maximus [Britannicus]
4     Maximus Germanicus
5     Maximus Pious Augustus,
6     Hadrian 1. He wrote off through Aurelius
7     Pasiphemis of Seneithus and
8     of the remaining money tax-collectors of the village
9     of Taneis of Isas of Ionos on behalf of the arithmeticus
10    of inhabitants of the 24th year four drachmas, amounting to 4 drachmas.
11    Similarly, other eight drachmas,
12    amounting to 8 drachmas, not having made use of another
13    token.
