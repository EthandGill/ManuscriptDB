1     From Ischyrion.
2     Hand over to Horion the steward
3     through Epagathos the wine-seller [wine]
4     one-chous measures one hundred and A … a …
5     Similarly to the wine-seller other things of wine
6     one-chous measures one hundred from [properties]
7     of Leukos and of Kolokyńthas
8     it comes to in wine in number one-chous measures [two hundred]
9     of the price of them [being contained]
10    into the apportioning of the village of [Dionysias]
11    as of the one-chous measure of drachmas fourteen.
12    I have noted wine one-chous measures two hundred
13    as stated above.
14    [Heroneios] the steward of Theadelpheia.
15    first of the year, Tybi, first
