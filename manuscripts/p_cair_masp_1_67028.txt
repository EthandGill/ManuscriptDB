[TRANSLATION]
1     … Dioskoros approached us, saying that his own father, in the first instance,
2     … from … this …
3     came to a second marriage, and that what had passed to the former (wife)
4     he assigned to the second (wife); and that he departed from among men so that
5     the children from her (the former) were both deprived and those things were squandered,
6     and that our suppliant is on every side bereft, [so that]
7     he stands in need of help from us and from the laws. We decree therefore
8     that your Excellency bring them forward and investigate the matters [concerning] this,
9     and if it should appear in truth that the things [belonging to our suppliant's]
10    mother were thus carried off, to exact this
11    and restore it to the petitioner, … as to the remaining property
12    which passed to the deceased, the law of Leo [of] the [divine]
13    memory to preserve for the petitioner, so that the second
14    wife shall not take more than is fitting for one of the children from the former marriage.
15    And since he says that lands also passed over to his [own]
16    father, his aunt having written them as a gift, and that
17    those from the second marriage hold as a gift to themselves, having brought it about
18    that a second (gift) be made for them, we decree
19    that your Excellency also investigate the matters concerning this, and if you find
20    them to be thus, do not give heed to second gifts upon property
21    written as alienated, since not even by our
22    laws are such transactions valid;
23    the law of Leo holds thus: whatever the former
24    wife was destined to gain, the husband dying before her, so much also
25    shall the second wife (gain), the husband [dying first] …
